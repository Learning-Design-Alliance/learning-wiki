# ExploreSEL Framework — Habits of Mind

## Description
The Habits of Mind are a set of thinking dispositions at the core of social, emotional, and cognitive behaviors.  They are displayed by intelligent people in response to problems, dilemmas, and enigmas. The Habits of Mind were developed by Dr. Arthur L. Costa and Dr. Bena Kallick, co-founders of the Institute for Habits of Mind. The Institute's mission is to transform schools into learning communities where thinking and Habits of Mind are taught, practiced, valued, and infused into the culture. Based on research into and descriptions of the characteristics of effective thinkers and high performers, the
framework focuses on the patterns of thought and behavior individuals must draw upon in order to effectively navigate complex and uncertain situations.
