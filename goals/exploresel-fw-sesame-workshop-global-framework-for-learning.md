## Description
The Sesame Workshop Global Framework for Learning is an early childhood development and education framework developed by Sesame Workshop, a non-profit educational media company that uses multicultural characters, settings, and Muppets to foster a love of learning and promote social, emotional, and cognitive development, content area knowledge, and creative expression in children. The framework outlines key areas of development and specific learning objectives for children ages 2-5 and is designed to serve as a guide for creating and adapting Sesame Street content and curricula for children around the world.
