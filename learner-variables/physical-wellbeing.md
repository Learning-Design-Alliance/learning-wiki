## Description
Physical well-being covers a learner's overall physical health: nutrition, regular physical activity and fitness, and good sleep. The LVN's case for it is that the benefits reach beyond the body: learners in good health benefit in cognitive skills, focus and mood, which can support academic performance, and in the early years it frames the same idea as physical fitness, with regular exercise strengthening cognitive skills. Exercise is described as particularly helpful for learners with ADHD. Physical activity tends to fall during adolescence, and for adults, education can itself improve health as health literacy grows and learners become better able to advocate for their needs. Body mass index is the one measure the LVN lists. It overlaps with [sleep](sleep.md), which is one of its components, and with [attention](attention.md) and [executive function](executive-function.md), which are among the outcomes exercise is linked to.

## Implications

### Context
- Breaks for movement, physical activity and access to food during the day bear on learners' focus.
- Hunger, poor nutrition and limited safe places to play are circumstances to plan around, not traits of learners.
- Health literacy is itself a learning goal for adults.

### Target Learners
- Children and adolescents, including those whose activity drops in adolescence.
- Learners with ADHD, for whom exercise may support focus.
- Adults managing their own or their family's health.

### Target Learning Objectives
- Attention, executive function and memory during learning.
- Mood and emotional well-being.
- Attendance and academic achievement; health literacy in adults.

## Measures
- **Body Mass Index (BMI)**: a measure of body fat calculated from height and weight.

## Related Learner Variables
- [Adverse Experiences](adverse-experiences.md): trauma has long-term costs for physical health.
- [Affect Regulation](affect-regulation.md): physical activity goes with less depression and anxiety.
- [Attention](attention.md): regular physical activity can improve attention.
- [Executive Function](executive-function.md): exercise can improve inhibition and cognitive flexibility.
- [Sleep](sleep.md): enough good sleep is part of physical health.
- [Working Memory](working-memory.md): nutrition and exercise are associated with better short-term and working memory.
- [Access](access.md): hearing loss can bring fatigue, and vision loss can make exercise harder.
- [Safety](safety.md): unsafe neighbourhoods limit outdoor play, and bullying goes with poorer health.
- [Socioeconomic Status](socioeconomic-status.md): hunger and limited food options in low-income circumstances harm health.
- [Social Supports](social-supports.md): support goes with better health, possibly by easing stress.
- [Long-Term Memory](long-term-memory.md): exercise before learning can help encoding.
- [Academic and Critical Literacy](academic-and-critical-literacy.md): health goes with achievement and attendance, and health literacy with adults' health.
