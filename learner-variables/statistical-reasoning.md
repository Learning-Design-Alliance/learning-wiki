## Description
Statistical reasoning is thinking about uncertainty and building mental models of real-world phenomena from data. The Learner Variability Navigator describes it as a cycle: learners pose a question about data, decide what data they need, gather it, organise, analyse and display it, and then describe what it shows and draw inferences to answer the question. It is assessed with questionnaires on understanding of variation (in chance settings such as dice, and in data and graphs) that pair multiple-choice items with written explanations, and with the data-analysis and probability subtests of diagnostic batteries.

LVN follows it from grade 3 to grade 10. It builds on [measurement](measurement.md) and [proportional reasoning](proportional-reasoning.md) and connects to [algebraic thinking](algebraic-thinking.md) through functions that describe data. Understanding of ideas like the average can grow when learners meet data that contradicts what they expected.

## Implications

### Context
- Investigations in which learners pose their own questions and collect their own data follow the full statistical cycle.
- Data that contradicts learners' expectations can prompt them to revise their understanding of ideas like the average.
- Stereotype cues about who is good at mathematics can depress performance.

### Target Learners
- Upper-primary and secondary learners working with data, graphs and chance.
- Learners with weaker listening or reading comprehension, for language-heavy data tasks.

### Target Learning Objectives
- Understanding of variation, averages and probability.
- Reading, making and drawing inferences from graphs and data displays.

## Measures
- **Statistical Variation Measure** (Watson et al., 2003): Questionnaire examining student understanding of statistical variation, including understanding of basic chance measurement and graph reading, variation in a chance setting (e.g., results of throwing a die), and variation in data and graphs. Students respond to multiple choice questions but also have space to explain their responses which allows for a more comprehensive measure of their understanding.
- **KeyMath-3 Diagnostic Assessment** (Connolly, 2007): In the Data Analysis/Probability subtest, students interpret tables and tally charts, and estimate probability.

## Related Learner Variables
- [Executive Function](executive-function.md): noticing a gap between an expected and an actual value, a cognitive-flexibility demand, can deepen understanding of the average.
- [Algebraic Thinking](algebraic-thinking.md): functions such as a linear relationship are used to describe data.
- [Stereotype Threat](stereotype-threat.md): stereotype cues can lower early-adolescent girls' performance on statistical reasoning, especially among girls who value mathematics.
- [Communication Skills](communication-skills.md): listening comprehension predicts performance on data-analysis and probability tasks.
