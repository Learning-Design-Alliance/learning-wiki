#### A {: #letter-a }

* [Academic and Critical Literacy](academic-and-critical-literacy.md) - A learner's command of the ways of reading, writing and reasoning used in academic disciplines and real-world domains, including questioning a text's purpose and bias.
* [Academic Anxiety](academic-anxiety.md) - Anxiety tied to learning and assessment in a subject, such as mathematics anxiety, test anxiety and foreign-language anxiety.
* [Academic Self-Concept](academic-self-concept.md) - A learner's perception of their own ability in an academic domain, such as how good they think they are at mathematics or at reading.
* [Access](access.md) - Whether a learner can perceive and operate the material at all — device, bandwidth, sensory and motor.
* [Achievement Emotions](achievement-emotions.md) - Emotions tied to learning activities and their outcomes, such as enjoyment, boredom, frustration, pride, shame and hope.
* [Achievement Goal Orientation](achievement-goal-orientation.md) - The purposes a learner pursues in achievement settings: to master the material (mastery goals) or to demonstrate competence relative to others (performance goals), each in an approach or avoidance form.
* [Adverse Experiences](adverse-experiences.md) - Events that can cause trauma, such as abuse, neglect, witnessing violence or instability at home, and the chronic stress they leave behind.
* [Affect Regulation](affect-regulation.md) - What a learner does with frustration, anxiety and failure while the work is still in front of them.
* [Age and Developmental Stage](age-and-developmental-stage.md) - A learner's age, grade level or stage of development, from early childhood to adulthood, as it bears on what instruction suits them.
* [Algebraic Thinking](algebraic-thinking.md) - A learner's ability to generalise, represent, justify and reason about mathematical structures and relationships, from patterns in arithmetic to equations and functions.
* [Alphabet Knowledge and Print Awareness](alphabet-knowledge-and-print-awareness.md) - A young learner's knowledge of letter names, shapes and sounds, and their understanding that print carries meaning and works in particular ways on the page.
* [Arithmetic Fact Retrieval](arithmetic-fact-retrieval.md) - A learner's ability to recall basic number combinations, such as single-digit sums and products, quickly and accurately from memory.
* [Attention](attention.md) - How long a learner can sustain focus on one thing, and how easily something else takes it away.
* [Auditory Processing](auditory-processing.md) - How the brain makes sense of the sounds that reach it through hearing: locating, distinguishing and interpreting sounds, especially speech.
* [Autism Spectrum Disorder](autism-spectrum-disorder.md) - Autism, a lifelong developmental difference in social communication and in patterns of behaviour, interests and sensory processing, recorded in research by clinical diagnosis or school category; it varies widely between people and is distinct from intellectual ability.

#### B {: #letter-b }

* [Belonging](belonging.md) - Whether a learner expects to be treated as a full participant in this setting, and how much of their attention that question consumes.

#### C {: #letter-c }

* [Civic Mindedness](civic-mindedness.md) - A learner's sense of themselves as a contributor to their community: attending to others' needs, understanding how civic systems work and affect people, and acting with others toward shared goals.
* [Collaboration](collaboration.md) - A learner's ability to work effectively and respectfully with others toward a shared goal: expressing and listening to ideas, compromising and sharing responsibility for the work.
* [Communication Skills](communication-skills.md) - A learner's ability to express thoughts, ideas and feelings and to understand others, through speech, sign, writing, gesture, drawing or technology, including talking about subject matter such as mathematics.
* [Creativity](creativity.md) - A learner's ability to generate novel and useful ideas, usually measured as divergent thinking: the fluency, flexibility, originality and elaboration of ideas produced for an open-ended problem.
* [Critical Thinking](critical-thinking.md) - A learner's ability and disposition to evaluate information and arguments, weigh evidence and perspectives, and reason to a judgement or decision.

#### D {: #letter-d }

* [Decoding](decoding.md) - A learner's ability to turn written words into spoken words and meanings, by applying letter-sound knowledge to unfamiliar words and by recognising familiar words at sight.
* [Digital Literacy](digital-literacy.md) - Whether a learner can operate the interface the instruction is delivered through, independently of whether they can reach it.
* [Domain Context](domain-context.md) - The sector, role or setting a scenario has to be placed in before a learner recognises it as their own.

#### E {: #letter-e }

* [Emotional Intelligence](emotional-intelligence.md) - A learner's ability to perceive, understand, use and manage emotions in themselves and others.
* [English-Learner Status](english-learner-status.md) - Whether a learner is still acquiring the language of instruction (an English learner, multilingual learner or second-language learner), as classified by a school system or measured by proficiency tests.
* [Epistemic Beliefs](epistemic-beliefs.md) - A learner's beliefs about the nature of knowledge and knowing: whether knowledge is certain or tentative, simple or complex, handed down by authority or built from evidence.
* [Executive Function](executive-function.md) - The set of control processes, inhibition, working-memory updating and cognitive flexibility, that let a learner direct attention and behaviour toward a goal.

#### G {: #letter-g }

* [Gender](gender.md) - A learner's gender as recorded in research data, used to describe differences in participation, attitudes and outcomes and to test whether an instructional effect differs between groups.
* [General Cognitive Ability](general-cognitive-ability.md) - A learner's general reasoning and problem-solving capacity, measured by intelligence or general ability tests, as it bears on learning and on how instruction works.
* [Geometric Reasoning](geometric-reasoning.md) - A learner's ability to define, analyse and argue about shapes and spatial relationships, up to writing formal geometric proofs.

#### H {: #letter-h }

* [Home Learning Environment](home-learning-environment.md) - The learning opportunities, materials, practices and attitudes a learner's home, family and community provide outside formal instruction.

#### I {: #letter-i }

* [Identity](identity.md) - A learner's developing sense of who they are, including the social identities, such as race, ethnicity, gender, culture, language and disability, that shape how they meet the world.
* [Inferencing](inferencing.md) - A reader's ability to fill in what a text leaves unsaid by connecting it with background knowledge, experience and other parts of the text.
* [Interest](interest.md) - A learner's engagement with and liking for a topic, whether a lasting personal interest or interest sparked in the moment by the situation.

#### L {: #letter-l }

* [Learning Disabilities and Developmental Differences](learning-disabilities.md) - Identified learning, developmental and intellectual disabilities (dyslexia and other specific learning disabilities, autism, intellectual disability), and the special educational needs that follow from them.
* [Learning-Style Preferences](learning-style-preferences.md) - A learner's stated preference for receiving information in one form (such as visual, auditory or kinaesthetic), and the claim that matching instruction to it improves learning.
* [Long-Term Memory](long-term-memory.md) - The memory system that stores knowledge, skills and experiences over hours to decades so they can be retrieved and used later.

#### M {: #letter-m }

* [Mathematical Flexibility](mathematical-flexibility.md) - A learner's ability to hold and move between several representations of numbers and several strategies, choosing the one that suits the problem.
* [Measurement](measurement.md) - A learner's understanding of measuring: picking out an attribute such as length, area or time and quantifying it by comparison with a unit of the same kind.
* [Metacognition](metacognition.md) - A learner's awareness of their own thinking and their ability to monitor and steer it: planning an approach, checking understanding and changing strategy when something is not working.
* [Mindset](mindset.md) - A learner's belief about whether ability or intelligence is fixed or can grow with effort and good strategies (implicit theories of intelligence).
* [Morphological Awareness](morphological-awareness.md) - A learner's awareness of the meaningful parts of words (roots, prefixes and suffixes) and ability to use them to read, understand, spell and build longer words.
* [Motivation](motivation.md) - Whether a learner is willing to spend effort here, and what makes the spending feel worth it.

#### N {: #letter-n }

* [Need for Cognition](need-for-cognition.md) - A learner's tendency to engage in and enjoy effortful thinking, such as complex problem solving and abstract reasoning.
* [Number Sense](number-sense.md) - A learner's understanding of numerical magnitudes and the relations among numbers, shown in tasks such as comparing two quantities or placing a number on a number line.

#### O {: #letter-o }

* [Operations and Place Value](operations-and-place-value.md) - A learner's procedural and conceptual understanding of arithmetic operations and of the base-ten place-value system that written numbers rely on.
* [Oral Language](oral-language.md) - A learner's spoken language skills, including vocabulary, grammar, listening comprehension and the ability to tell an organised story.

#### P {: #letter-p }

* [Personality Traits](personality-traits.md) - A learner's characteristic patterns of thinking, feeling and behaving, usually described by the Big Five: conscientiousness, openness, agreeableness, extraversion and neuroticism.
* [Phonological Awareness](phonological-awareness.md) - A learner's ability to notice and work with the sounds of spoken language (rhymes, syllables and individual phonemes) and to process those sounds quickly when reading and spelling.
* [Physical Well-Being](physical-wellbeing.md) - A learner's physical health and fitness, including nutrition, regular exercise and adequate sleep, as it bears on cognition, mood and learning.
* [Prior Knowledge](prior-knowledge.md) - A learner's existing domain knowledge in the subject area before instruction begins.
* [Problem Solving](problem-solving.md) - A learner's ability to work through unfamiliar challenges by setting goals and bringing knowledge, resources and creative thinking to bear on a solution.
* [Processing Speed](processing-speed.md) - The rate at which a learner takes in information, makes sense of it and produces an appropriate response.
* [Proportional Reasoning](proportional-reasoning.md) - A learner's understanding of ratios and the relationships between them, as expressed in fractions, percentages and rates.

#### R {: #letter-r }

* [Race and Ethnicity](race-and-ethnicity.md) - A learner's racial or ethnic group as recorded in research and administrative data, used to describe gaps in opportunity and outcomes and to test whether an instructional effect differs across groups.
* [Reading and Language](reading-and-language.md) - What a learner can read fluently, and whether the language of instruction is the one they think in.
* [Reading Fluency](reading-fluency.md) - A learner's ability to read connected text accurately, at a good pace and smoothly, silently or aloud, so that attention is free for comprehension.

#### S {: #letter-s }

* [Safety](safety.md) - Being, and feeling, physically and psychologically secure at home, at school or work, and in the neighbourhood and wider community.
* [Self-Efficacy](self-efficacy.md) - A learner's belief in their own ability to carry out the actions needed to succeed at a particular task or in a particular subject.
* [Self-Regulation](self-regulation.md) - Whether a learner can plan, monitor and adjust their own work without the structure being supplied.
* [Sensory Integration](sensory-integration.md) - The brain's ability to take in, organise and combine information from several senses and turn it into fitting physical and emotional responses.
* [Sleep](sleep.md) - How much, how regularly and how well a learner sleeps, the period in which the body and brain rest, recover and consolidate memories.
* [Social Awareness and Relationship Skills](social-awareness-and-relationship-skills.md) - A learner's ability to understand others' perspectives and feelings and to build and keep positive relationships with peers, teachers and colleagues.
* [Social Supports](social-supports.md) - The network of people, such as family, friends, teachers and community, that a learner has and believes they could turn to for help.
* [Socioeconomic Status](socioeconomic-status.md) - A learner's family income, wealth, parental education and occupation, and the resources and constraints that come with them.
* [Spatial Ability](spatial-ability.md) - The ability to generate, retain, retrieve and transform visual and spatial images, such as mentally rotating an object or relating shapes to one another.
* [Statistical Reasoning](statistical-reasoning.md) - A learner's ability to reason about data and uncertainty: posing questions, collecting and organising data, and describing and drawing inferences from it.
* [Stereotype Threat](stereotype-threat.md) - The risk that a learner performs below their actual capability when a negative stereotype about a group they belong to is made salient in an evaluative setting.
* [Syntax](syntax.md) - A learner's grasp of how sentences are built: how word order, sentence structure and punctuation carry meaning, in reading, speech and writing.

#### T {: #letter-t }

* [Time and Continuity](time-and-continuity.md) - How much uninterrupted time a learner actually gets, and whether progress survives the gap until the next session.

#### V {: #letter-v }

* [Visual Processing](visual-processing.md) - The cognitive work of making sense of what the eyes take in: recognising, ordering and interpreting visual information such as letters, numbers, shapes and diagrams.
* [Vocabulary](vocabulary.md) - The words a learner knows and can use, receptively and expressively, including the academic and subject-specific words that school texts depend on.

#### W {: #letter-w }

* [Working Memory](working-memory.md) - How much a learner can hold and manipulate at once before the material itself becomes the obstacle.
* [Writing Skills](writing-skills.md) - A learner's ability to write, from the foundational skills of handwriting, spelling and conventions to composing texts in a genre and arguing from evidence.
