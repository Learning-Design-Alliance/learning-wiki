# Problem Solving

> **Learner Variable** · [All learner variables](index.md)

## Description
Problem solving is the ability to work through a challenge that has no ready answer: defining the goal, drawing on relevant [prior knowledge](prior-knowledge.md), resources and skills, generating options and choosing among them. The LVN record (written for adult learners) treats it broadly, covering everyday, career and academic problems and conflicts, and includes both critical and creative thinking within it. It can be measured with scenario-based tasks in which learners analyse realistic problems from home, family and work life.

For adults, problem solving bears on navigating personal, workplace and educational challenges. The account links it to fluid reasoning (relational reasoning is described as a key part of it), to working and short-term memory, to executive function, and to the decline of episodic memory in older adults, which can make recall harder during problem solving. Much of the connected material concerns creativity within problem solving, so the page overlaps with [creativity](creativity.md); it also overlaps with [general cognitive ability](general-cognitive-ability.md) and [critical thinking](critical-thinking.md). Domain-specific problem solving in mathematics is covered by the mathematics learner-variable pages.

## Implications

### Context
- Matters for designs built around realistic or ill-structured problems, especially in adult, workplace and community education.
- Intrinsic interest in a topic and confidence in one's creativity are described as supporting creative problem solving.
- Collaborative settings may support creative problem solving when group members are inclusive.

### Target Learners
- Adult learners facing everyday, career and academic problems.
- Older adults, whose metacognitive strategies and memory affect decision making and problem solving.

### Target Learning Objectives
- Solving realistic, everyday and workplace problems.
- Creative and flexible solutions.
- Decision making.

## Measures
- **Everyday Problem Solving Inventory** (Cornelius & Capsi, 1987): task-based assessment in which learners analyse 48 real-life scenarios about family, home, conflict and managing information.

## Related Learner Variables
- [General Cognitive Ability](general-cognitive-ability.md): relational reasoning, part of fluid intelligence, is described as a key component of problem solving.
- [Prior Knowledge](prior-knowledge.md): knowledge of the relevant domain supports creative solutions.
- [Executive Function](executive-function.md): inhibition and cognitive flexibility are linked to better creative problem solving.
- [Working Memory](working-memory.md): greater short-term and working memory capacity goes with better problem solving and creative thinking.
- [Metacognition](metacognition.md): metacognitive training has been reported to strengthen older adults' decision making.
- [Motivation](motivation.md): intrinsic motivation and interest in a topic are described as driving creativity in problem solving.
- [Self-Efficacy](self-efficacy.md): confidence in one's own creativity can encourage positive attitudes to creative tasks.
- [Social Awareness and Relationship Skills](social-awareness-and-relationship-skills.md): inclusive social skills in a group may enhance creative problem solving.
- [Communication Skills](communication-skills.md): oral communication projects such as debates are linked to self-reported gains in critical thinking.
- [Long-Term Memory](long-term-memory.md): declining episodic memory in older adults can make problem solving harder.
- [Sleep](sleep.md): sleep may help problem solving through insight.
- [Digital Literacy](digital-literacy.md): judging online sources for bias and truthfulness is a problem-solving skill tied to digital literacy.
