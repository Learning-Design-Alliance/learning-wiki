# Five-category characterization of formative assessment effects using benchmark effect size and statistical significance

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For studies meeting WWC standards, the review characterized findings "according to a benchmark effect size, statistical significance, and the direction of effect into one of five mutually exclusive categories": statistically significant positive, substantively important positive, indeterminate, statistically significant negative, and substantively important negative. Figure B.3 sets criteria, e.g., a substantively important positive effect requires an estimated effect greater than .25 but not statistically significant, and an indeterminate effect falls between .25 and -.25 and is not statistically significant.
