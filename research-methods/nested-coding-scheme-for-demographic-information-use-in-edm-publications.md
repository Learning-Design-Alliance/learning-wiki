# Nested coding scheme for demographic information use in EDM publications

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes a nested coding scheme for how demographic information appears in research: publications are first coded as applicable or not applicable, then as analyzed, merely reported, or not reported, and analyzed papers are subdivided by whether demographics were investigated, used as features in models, or used for testing and validation. The article states that "a publication could potentially use the information in more than one type of analysis (e.g., both investigated and features in models)". Six demographic categories (age, education level, gender, race, SES, geographic location) were coded within this scheme.
