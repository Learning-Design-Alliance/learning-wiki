# Strigel and Pouezevara framework of mobile learning configurations, extended with access/affordability and mathematical pedagogy spectrums

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article uses the Strigel and Pouezevara (2012) framework of "Variations on Mobile Learning Configurations", which attends to three spectrums: a learning spectrum from formal to informal, a kinetic spectrum from stationary to mobile, and a collaborative spectrum from individual to group work. The authors situate the service as informal but school-supported, mobile, and near the individual end. They argue two dimensions are missing: an access and affordability spectrum (from free public access and free broadband data to BYOD and private data contracts) and a mathematical pedagogy spectrum attending to knowledge, attitudes and beliefs about mathematics and how it is taught.
