# Consistency plots

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A consistency plot is a 2-d grid representing two separate ways of analyzing student responses, with each student's change shown as a circle-line-triangle response pair and identical initial/final responses shown as squares. The article introduces it as "a kind of 2-d escalator diagram" extending Kanim's escalator diagrams, applied to solution method and correctness of student answers on nearly identical midterm and final exam questions in intermediate mechanics.
