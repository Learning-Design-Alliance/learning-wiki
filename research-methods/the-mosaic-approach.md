## Description
The mosaic approach, developed by Peter Moss and Alison Clark, creates environments reflecting children's perspectives of their educational institutions by combining information "obtained from different methods and resources". It uses visual, physical, and oral tools such as camera use, album-map creation, and in-school tours alongside observations and interviews. It proceeds in three stages: collecting information from different sources, sharing and interpreting that information with children and teachers, and deciding with children what can change or stay the same and making adjustments.
