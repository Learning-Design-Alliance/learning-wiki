## Description
The study uses a self-controlled before-and-after design where "the first 6-hour session in the initial semester served as the control condition (conventional teaching), while the subsequent 6-hour session in the following semester served as the experimental condition (debriefing teaching)." This design accounts for baseline differences within the same cohort but is "inherently susceptible to threats to internal validity, including maturation effects, history effects, and testing effects."
