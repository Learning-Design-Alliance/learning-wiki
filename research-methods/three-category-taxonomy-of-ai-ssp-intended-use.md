# Three-category taxonomy of AI-SSP intended use

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The review organizes the 20 unique AI-SSP systems by their primary intended use into three categories: educational tools for deliberate practice, hybrid training-and-assessment tools, and systems focused explicitly on assessment. It reports that "The largest group, 9 (45%) of 20 systems, framed AI-SSPs primarily as deliberate practice or educational tools", that six (30%) were hybrid training and assessment tools combining SP interaction with structured feedback and scoring, and that the remaining five systems focused explicitly on assessment. The taxonomy shows that even systems with scoring components have most often been introduced as formative learning environments, with an increasing subset emphasizing automated scoring and performance analytics.
