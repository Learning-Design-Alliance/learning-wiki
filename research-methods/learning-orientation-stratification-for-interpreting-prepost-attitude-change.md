## Description
The article proposes that learning-orientation analysis offers "a potential metric to interpret pre-post performance measures for these courses", for example whether measurements on an instrument like the CLASS are negative or positive. Because Framework-oriented students gained expert-like attitudes while others declined, orientation distributions may help designers of IPLS courses that include both health science and life science majors interpret attitude outcomes.
