# Precursor question relation

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The precursor question relation is the article's formal account of empirical relationships between test items: question A is a precursor of B when f(A,B)=P(B=1|A=0)/P(B=1|A=1) falls below a threshold, an "adaptation of the relative risk index to the context of a multiple-choice test" with Cohen's d gauging strength. The threshold was derived from Sawilowsky's effect-size categories, with f(A,B) <0.485 chosen, corresponding to a large effect size. Unlike local dependence, the relation is not symmetric, and unlike Doignon and Falmagne's surmise relations it is not transitive; the authors position it as a concrete implementation of surmise relations "based primarily on operational needs rather than on theoretical considerations".
