## Description
The article identifies the DINA model and the DINO model as "two typical theoretical diagnostic classification models". Each TDCM makes different assumptions about the relationship between a student's response pattern and attribute profile. In this article they serve as the theoretical models combined with ANNs in the semi-supervised framework.
