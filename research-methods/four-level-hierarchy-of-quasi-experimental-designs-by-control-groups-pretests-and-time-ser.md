# Four-level hierarchy of quasi-experimental designs by control groups, pretests, and time series

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article organizes non-randomized educational designs into a four-level hierarchy attributed to Cook, Campbell, and Shadish: "A. Design with no control groups. B. Design with control groups but no pretest. C. Design with control groups and pretest. D. Design for time series data," with D the best. Each level is instantiated with educational examples (one-group posttest, posttest-only nonequivalent groups, pretest-posttest nonequivalent groups, interrupted time series) and a DAG exposing its backdoor paths and internal-validity threats.
