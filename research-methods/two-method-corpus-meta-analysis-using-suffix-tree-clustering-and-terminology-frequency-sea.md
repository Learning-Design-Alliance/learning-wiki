# Two-method corpus meta-analysis using suffix-tree clustering and terminology frequency search

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article meta-analyzes Physical Review Physics Education Research with two complementary approaches: Suffix Tree Clustering, "an algorithm for automatic document classification" applied to 342 abstracts via the Carrot2 implementation, and case-insensitive full-text search of the journal converted to ASCII. Clustering objectively identifies widespread concepts and phrase cluster labels but may miss goal-relevant topics; terminology search is sensitive to minor ideas but requires researcher-chosen strings and suffers from synonymy and homography.
