# Ten experience criteria for analyzing positive learning experiences

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article applies ten criteria of "positive experiences" developed by Jantzen et al. (2011) to analyze students' emotional responses to learning designs: Interactive, Near, Intimate, Authentic, Unique, Involving, Lively, Learning, Understanding, and Interesting. The criteria cover psychological aspects ("whether the design is involving, relevant, interesting, and provide learning and understanding") and structural aspects ("whether the design is interactive, authentic, original, spontaneous and persuasive"). They were used to develop the semantic differential questionnaire and to guide walk-along and observation data collection.
