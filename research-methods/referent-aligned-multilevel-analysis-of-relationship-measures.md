# Referent-aligned multilevel analysis of relationship measures

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because relationship items differ in their referents — dyadic ("I like my teacher"), plural ("this teacher likes us"), or averaged ("I like my teachers") — the referent should determine the level at which analysis takes place. The review states that "Where referents are plural or averaged, analyses should be multilevel to account for shared group-level variance and ensure appropriate interpretation". This matters because implications for practice and intervention differ depending on whether individual relationships, multiple relationships, or classroom culture is being addressed.
