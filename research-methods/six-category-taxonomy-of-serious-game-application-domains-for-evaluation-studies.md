## Description
The review classifies the application domains of evaluated serious games into six categories: health & wellness, culture, professional learning & training, social, support, and education. Each category is defined by the purpose of the games it contains, e.g. health & wellness "includes serious games developed to improve people's quality life regarding their health." The taxonomy underpins the review's answer to RQ1 and its distribution analysis of evaluated games per domain.
