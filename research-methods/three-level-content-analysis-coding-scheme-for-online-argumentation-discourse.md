# Three-level content analysis coding scheme for online argumentation discourse

## Description
The article developed and released a qualitative coding scheme for analyzing online discussion transcripts, applied to 272 postings in Atlas-Ti with open, selective, and axial coding and inter-rater reliability of .90. Level 1 conventional analysis produced 84 codes; Level 2 directed analysis organized them into five boldface categories: "(i) position on dilemma; (ii) support of position on dilemma; (iii) perspective of dilemma evaluation; (iv) nature of dilemma evaluation; and (v) nature of response to others." Appendix C is presented to allow other researchers to use a similar coding scheme.
