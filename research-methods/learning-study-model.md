## Description
The Learning Study is "a fusion between lesson study and design experiment" in which teachers and a researcher jointly plan a lesson around a chosen learning object, implement it with one pupil group, and analyze pre-test, post-test and delayed post-test results together with video-recorded lessons. The knowledge gained provides the basis for a revised lesson in a new group of pupils, forming micro-cycles of three lessons. In this study six teachers ran three Learning Study cycles over three semesters, with 54, 64 and 48 participating pupils respectively.
