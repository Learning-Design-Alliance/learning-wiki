## Description
The definition‐based approach explicitly identifies a sub‐sample of resilient students and compares them to a group of non‐resilient peers facing similar socio‐economic constraints, in contrast to variable‐centered or model‐based approaches that conceptualize resilience as a continuous or latent construct. The article states that "by directly contrasting students exposed to comparable levels of socioeconomic risk but exhibiting divergent academic outcomes, the definition‐based approach enables theoretically grounded and empirically transparent comparisons of cognitive and socio‐emotional characteristics." In this study, both the academic achievement index and the family SES index were divided into tertiles, yielding nine SES × achievement profiles.
