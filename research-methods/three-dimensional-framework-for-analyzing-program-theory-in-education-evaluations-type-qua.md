# Three-dimensional framework for analyzing program theory in education evaluations (type, quality, and use)

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A coding framework, summarized in Figure 1 and drawing on theory-based evaluation principles and the National Research Council committee's mathematics-specific criteria, that "combined perspectives from both “method-oriented” evaluation (as emphasized by WWC) and “theory-driven” evaluation." It codes each evaluation report on three dimensions: the type of program theory articulated (none, sub-theoretical implementation description, or theoretical programmatic causal-chain mapping), the quality of articulation (entirely implicit, limited sources, or multiple resources including research literature), and how theory is used in research questions, construct measurement (outcomes and fidelity), and analysis (whether mechanisms are explained). The review applied it to 36 WWC-qualified mathematics program evaluation reports.
