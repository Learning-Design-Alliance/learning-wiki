## Description
The notice describes Webb's monograph as organizing the current status of general science instruction around enumerated aspects of the field: "(1) the subject matter of general science; (2) the acceptability of general science topics; (3) size of topics; (4) distribution of the sciences," together with correlations between the sciences, adaptability of general science, analysis of marks, and analysis of the complete reaction of children to science. It functions as a taxonomy for quantitative textbook analysis in this domain.
