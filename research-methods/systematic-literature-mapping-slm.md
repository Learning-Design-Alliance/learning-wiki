> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The study applies systematic literature mapping (SLM) following PRISMA 2020 guidelines, defined as providing "a comprehensive overview of the available literature within a defined time frame, identifying trends, methodological approaches, and research gaps". SLM complements systematic literature review by offering "a preliminary exploratory analysis" before more restrictive SLR criteria. The method uses ten Kitchenham-based quality criteria on a Likert-type scale with a minimum inclusion threshold of 7 out of 10 points, applied by five independent reviewers with consensus-based discrepancy resolution.
