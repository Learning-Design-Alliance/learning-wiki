# Four-code scheme for coding sources of measurement uncertainty by measurement versus physical model and limitations versus principles

## Description
The article develops a coding scheme that classifies student statements about sources of measurement uncertainty along two axes: measurement model versus physical model, and limitations versus principles. As printed, the codes "were Measure- ment model Limitations (ML), Measurement model Princi- ples (MP), Physical model Limitations (PL), and Physical model Principles (PP)", chosen to align with the Modeling Framework for Experimental Physics. ML covers mistakes by experimenters or devices, MP covers devices not being infinitely precise, PL covers invalid assumptions or unaccounted variables, and PP covers variability inherent to the physical model such as the uncertainty principle. Responses were also coded for uncertainty type: precision, accuracy (data-versus-data or data-versus-theory), or generic.
