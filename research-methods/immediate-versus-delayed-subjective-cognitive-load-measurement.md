# Immediate-versus-delayed subjective cognitive load measurement

## Description
When using subjective rating scales to measure cognitive load, researchers should not assume that the average of ratings given after each task equals a single rating given after the whole learning phase. Across two experiments, "the delayed ratings of both effort and difﬁculty were signiﬁcantly higher than the average of the six ratings made during problem solving", while affective ratings showed no such difference. The choice of measurement point therefore changes what the rating indicates and should be reported and justified.
