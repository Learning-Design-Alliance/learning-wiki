## Description
Differential Item Functioning (DIF) is a psychometric framework for evaluating item-level bias that "takes into account differences at every level of candidate ability". It compares two or more groups of interest on each test question across the whole ability curve, allowing detection of no bias, "uniform differences (group A outperforms group B across the ability curve)", non-uniform differences where the gap varies, and crossing differences where the direction reverses across the curve. The article uses logistic-regression DIF with Wald tests, and uses DIF to flag questions for expert review, revision or removal in a high-stakes postgraduate examination.
