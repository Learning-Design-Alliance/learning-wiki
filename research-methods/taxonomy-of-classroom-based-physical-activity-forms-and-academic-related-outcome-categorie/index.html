Taxonomy of classroom-based physical activity forms and academic-related outcome categories¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review organises classroom-based physical activity into three forms — active breaks, curriculum-focussed active breaks, and physically active lessons — defined as "physical activity carried out during regular class time", distinct from recess or lunch breaks. Academic-related outcomes are grouped into three categories: classroom behaviour (on-task and off-task behaviour), cognitive function (e.g. executive function), and academic achievement (standardised tests, progress monitoring tools, grades, perceived competence). This taxonomy structures the review's search, data extraction, meta-analysis grouping and conclusions.

Claims¶

  • Classroom-based physical activity improves on-task behaviour and reduces off-task behaviour in primary school children (pooled SMD = 0.60) [+M]
  • Classroom-based physical activity improves academic achievement when progress monitoring tools are the outcome measure (pooled SMD = 1.03) [+M]
  • Classroom-based physical activity showed no statistically significant pooled effect on cognitive function in primary school children (SMD = 0.33, CI crossing zero) [~M]
