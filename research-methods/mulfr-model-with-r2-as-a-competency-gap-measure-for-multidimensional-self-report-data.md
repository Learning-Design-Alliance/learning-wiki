# MULFR model with R² as a competency gap measure for multidimensional self-report data

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The multidimensional unreplicated linear functional relationship (MULFR) model compares two sets of p-dimensional variables—graduate competencies and industry expectations—both measured with error via questionnaires, yielding a single coefficient of determination. The article states "1−Rp2 measure the competency gap reflected on the fresh graduated quantity surveyors," with criteria adapted from Cohen classifying gaps as large, medium, small, or none. This study is presented as the first application of MULFR to competency gap analysis.
