## Description
The article uses the learning curve as its central organizing measure for judging surgical training approaches: it "applies the learning curves as a measure to demonstrate their teaching ability" and compares simulation types by analyzing their learning curves. Learning curves track how operative performance (e.g., operating time, conversions, complications) changes with accumulated operative experience, allowing identification of case thresholds at which performance stabilizes. The review surmises the applicability of different surgical simulation types by comparing and analyzing learning curves across procedures such as laparoscopic hemihepatectomy, pancreatoduodenectomy, and robotic pancreatoduodenectomy.
