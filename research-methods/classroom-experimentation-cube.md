# Classroom Experimentation Cube

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article defines a framework for categorising and comparing educational experiments along three primary axes: iterativity, scale, and technological readiness. The axes form a cube whose nodes are eight distinct experimental types; the root node is the traditional static classroom void of direct experimentation, and each axis 'adds' new capabilities to the learning environment. The authors use the cube to locate teacher-led classroom experimentation, large RCTs, big-data RCTs, expensive RCTs and large-scale A/B tests, arguing that the combination of rapid teacher-led experimentation with large-scale rigour is newly enabled by technology.
