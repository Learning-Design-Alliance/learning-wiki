One-shot GPT-4o-mini codebook-based annotation of LLM math-tutor dialogue¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 3 studies (1 causal, 1 associational, 1 design), q2 · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article reproduces an 82-code educational codebook organised into 7 major categories (MPS, MKT, Cognition, Metacognition, PIU, Student Intent Utterance, Interaction/Discourse) by inlining it into a single GPT-4o-mini prompt at temperature 0: "we reproduce an 82-code educational codebook on119,009tutor sentences with a one-shot structural classifier". Labels cover 3,500 scenarios across Base Greedy, Aligned GRPO, and Base TEI@4 cells, with no human coding.

Claims¶

  • GRPO pedagogical alignment of a reasoning LLM lowers actual student learning while raising judge-rated helpfulness (a multi-dimensional alignment tax) [+M]
  • On codes requiring recognition of pedagogical intent not explicitly stated, LLMs over-apply and the blind verifier rejects their application in favor of human coding [~W]
  • Cosine similarity has opposing effects on LLM coding accuracy: lower similarity within codebooks helps, higher similarity within excerpts helps [~W]
