# Minute-by-minute whole-classroom observation protocol

## Description
The authors developed a classroom observation approach that uses time sampling to observe all children minute by minute, replacing the prior focal-child approach in which each child was observed only 2-6 minutes. The protocol "documents student behavior (off-task, disruptive, aggressive and social isolation), classroom context (instructional format, subject area), overall level of classroom behavior (a classroom scan every 5 minutes) and teachers' instructional and classroom management style (punitive, neutral, positive)". It was used in the GBG professional development trial in Houston.
