# Rule-based computational gaming detection in intelligent tutoring system log data

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A computational detector that automatically labels tutor–student turn pairs in ITS log data as gamed or not. The authors hand-analyzed a log fragment to identify gaming patterns, then "encoded these patterns into a computational rule-based gaming detector that could automatically label the data", hand-checking output on a new fragment. It classifies five short-duration turn pairs as gaming: skipping hints, copying a bottom-out hint, guessing, and lack of planning, with a time threshold per pair.
