Research Methods¶

Methods for studying learning and evaluating education that are specific to it or especially useful in it. General social-science methods are not listed.

A¶

  • A CAT-sensitive process for evaluating content alignment - The article outlines a process for evaluating content alignment that is sensitive to the particular ways computerized adaptive tests can diverge from their intended content domain.
  • A four-type hierarchy of evidence for judging educational technology effectiveness - The guide presents an organizing framework of four evidence types that educators encounter when evaluating product effectiveness claims.
  • Adapted conjecture map framework for learning scientists and computer scientists - An adaptation of Sandoval's conjecture mapping tool, created by Chang and Roschelle, that supports interdisciplinary design-based research teams in connecting learning theory to technical design.
  • Adaptive G-UKT - Adaptive G-UKT is a knowledge tracing framework that models each learner's evolving latent state as a Gaussian distribution rather than a point vector, tracking \"semantic activation levels and estimation confidence through diagonal covariance\".
  • Adaptive Self-Referenced Testing (ASRT) - ASRT is a proposed framework, arising from the project's finding that norm-referenced and criterion-referenced approaches lack a common structure, for measuring individual improvement in achievement during instruction.
  • Agent-based educational science (AES) - Agent-based educational science (AES) is a research agenda in which educational theories are formalized as interacting agents and environments, enabling in silico experimentation on developmental processes.
  • Agent-based simulation methodology for decomposing teacher quality gap sources - The article introduces \"a novel simulation methodology\" that models teacher workforce dynamics to attribute teacher quality gaps to their underlying processes.
  • AMOVA (Accumulative Manifold Validation Analysis) - AMOVA (Accumulative Manifold Validation Analysis) is a specialized statistical methodology for testing the internal and external validity of uniquely designed psychometric instruments.
  • Assessment metadata as naturally occurring direct SEL measurement - The article proposes that assessment metadata — data about how students behave on a test or survey, including disengagement indicators — can function as naturally occurring, direct measures of social-emotional competencies, offering an alternative to deliberately administered SEL surveys.
  • Attainment-based evaluation of charter schools as a complement to achievement-test-based studies - The report frames its contribution as moving beyond the standard outcome measure in charter school research.

  • Baseline covariate adjustment in education randomized trials - The paper recommends that studies using student achievement outcomes generally aim to collect and use baseline data, because analyses using more than one wave of data typically have much greater statistical power than posttest-only analyses.
  • BASIE - BASIE (BAyeSian Interpretation of Estimates) is a framework for interpreting impact estimates from evaluations.

  • Between-student testing and response-curve method - The between-student method, first used in physics by Sayre and Heckler, gives short conceptual quizzes to different sub-groups of the population in successive weeks, avoiding test-retest effects.

  • Black box analysis of educational processes - The paper's central framework is black box analysis: the examination of the internal character and content of educational processes that lie between documented inputs (instructional formats) and outcomes.
  • Body of work approach to standard setting for alternate assessments - The body of work approach is a standard setting methodology in which \"Reviewers examine all of the data for a student and use this information to place the student in one of the overall performance levels.\" Standard setters receive papers demonstrating the complete range of possible scores from low
  • Bootstrapping adjustment (BA) method for item parameter bias reduction - BA is one of two item parameter adjustment methods proposed in the article, reducing biases in both item difficulty and item discrimination parameters using statistical bootstrapping.
