Research Methods¶

Methods for studying learning and evaluating education that are specific to it or especially useful in it. General social-science methods are not listed.

  • Baseline covariate adjustment in education randomized trials - The paper recommends that studies using student achievement outcomes generally aim to collect and use baseline data, because analyses using more than one wave of data typically have much greater statistical power than posttest-only analyses.
  • Bayesian analysis for complex factorial experiments - The paper recommends that researchers interested in studying complex interventions adopt Bayesian analysis for factorial designs.
  • Causal-chain analysis of built-environment effects - The brief describes \"causal chains\" as a method environmental health researchers developed to evidence how building features affect people where direct causal proof is impractical.
  • College-readiness predictor selection using decision trees, random forests, lasso, and factor analysis - This framework uses data reduction to select, from thousands of measures, the variables that maximize classification accuracy for college enrollment and persistence.
  • Combined regression discontinuity and interrupted time-series design with instrumental variables - When a threshold-based policy changes conditions for non-targeted students too, a standard regression discontinuity cannot estimate the policy effect, only the enrollment effect.
  • Compound Polynomial (CP) growth model - The CP model is a multilevel growth model whose design matrix combines a within-year (fall-to-spring) matrix with a between-year (spring-to-spring) polynomial matrix, so that both kinds of growth are estimated in one model.
  • Correct variance estimation for CACE in clustered education RCTs - The report's methodological contribution is to work out the correct variance formulas for the CACE estimator under clustered designs and to compare them with \"those that are typically used in practice\".
  • Design-based RCT analysis with grouped administrative data - The article recommends analyzing RCTs with grouped administrative data \"to help improve data access,\" using design-based estimators formed as group-level averages.
  • Digital learning platform process-data analysis - The report urges researchers to use the robust log data DLPs generate, such as which tasks students begin, time spent, and detailed progress.
  • Early-warning indicator construction - A Consortium report cited in the paper outlines five characteristics of effective indicators: predictiveness, usability and clarity, real time/right time availability, direct causal linkage to outcomes, and malleability.
  • Embedded experimental designs in digital learning platforms - This pattern describes embedding interventions directly within platform infrastructure—modifying problem formats, feedback types, or visual scaffolds—and observing effects during authentic learning activities.
