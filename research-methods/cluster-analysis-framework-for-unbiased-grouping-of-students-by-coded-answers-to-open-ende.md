# Cluster-analysis framework for unbiased grouping of students by coded answers to open-ended questionnaires

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The article presents a framework in which students' coded answers (an M x N binary matrix of answering strategies) are grouped by "Clustering Analysis methods can be roughly distinguished in Non-Hierarchical (or Centroid-Based), and Hierarchical ones". Similarity is built from a modified Pearson correlation coefficient Rmod and a Euclidean distance d = sqrt(2(1 - Rmod)); k-means partitions are evaluated with the Silhouette Function and a proposed centroid-reliability coefficient, while hierarchical clustering uses linkage methods, the cophenetic correlation coefficient, the Inconsistency Coefficient and the Variation Ratio Criterion, with Variation of Information comparing partitions across methods. The framework aims at unbiased classification without prior knowledge of group form.
