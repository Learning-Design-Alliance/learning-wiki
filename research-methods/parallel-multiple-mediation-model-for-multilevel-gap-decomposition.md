## Description
The article argues the extensions must rest on a parallel multiple mediation model, in which "school segregation and district segregation are correlated but separate processes". In a serial model where district composition mediates through school composition, the within-school district segregation parameter cannot be estimated because the district proportion of minority students is constant within schools. The article shows that where a serial variant is estimable, its decompositions are algebraically equivalent to the parallel-model versions.
