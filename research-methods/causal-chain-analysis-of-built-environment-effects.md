## Description
The brief describes "causal chains" as a method environmental health researchers developed to evidence how building features affect people where direct causal proof is impractical. Rather than showing a maintenance practice directly causes fewer asthma attacks, "researchers can test and evidence that the practice inhibits the growth of mold. Mold, causal studies can show, impacts asthma rates." The brief uses this framework to connect indoor air quality, heat, and lighting to attendance and achievement. It applies the framework to argue that sustainable school design can improve learning and well-being.
