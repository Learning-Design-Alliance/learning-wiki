Late pretest decontamination procedure¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 2 studies (2 theoretical), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size

Description¶

A three-step statistical procedure for removing early treatment effects from contaminated pretests. It entails building a model with the contaminated pretest as outcome and exogenous covariates plus time between school start and baseline testing as predictors, estimating it on control students only, and then "creating predicted pretests for all students using the estimated model but setting the time between school start and pretest date to zero." The article applies it in a preschool cluster RCT and says it can be applied in similar situations with exogenous predictors of the pretest.
