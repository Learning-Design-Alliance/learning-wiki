# Implementation-integrity and cross-context robustness evaluation

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Teams should gather data on implementation integrity and variability across contexts, addressing both whether a solution is implemented as intended and how it performs across a wide range of contexts. They should also "document the factors that support high quality implementation as well as the robustness of a breakthrough solution's impact on learning when implementation quality varies," for example under resource and staffing constraints.
