## Description
The article assembles a coordinated integrative data analysis (IDA): rather than pooling raw data, it estimates the same STARTS model separately on published covariance matrices from 11 longitudinal studies and pools parameter estimates meta-analytically. The studies cover more than 20,000 students, span the middle of primary school to the end of secondary school, and were found via a systematic PsycINFO search plus snowballing. The authors argue this fits "the idea of a coordinated IDA" and exemplifies an underused approach in educational psychology.
