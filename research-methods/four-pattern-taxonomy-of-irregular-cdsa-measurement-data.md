## Description
The article proposes a classification of irregular measurement-value patterns in CDSA log data used to detect clinical skill gaps. Signs per health center "were labeled with one of the four distinct pattern types (i.e., skipper, mono-repeater, multi-repeater, and wrongly evaluated)", each with a level of expression (mild, moderate, or severe) determined by how much statistical parameters (mean, IQR, missing-value frequency, value distribution) deviated from the combined average of all health centers. Skipper means high frequency of not-feasible/missing values; mono- and multi-repeater mean values concentrated around one or 2–3 values with small IQR; wrongly evaluated means high percentages of outlier or implausible values.
