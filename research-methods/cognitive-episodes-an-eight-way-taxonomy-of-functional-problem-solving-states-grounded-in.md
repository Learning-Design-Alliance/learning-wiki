# Cognitive episodes: an eight-way taxonomy of functional problem-solving states grounded in Schoenfeld's Episode Theory

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Cognitive episodes organize LLM reasoning traces into temporally ordered functional problem-solving states. The framework builds on Schoenfeld's Episode Theory, which "models the problem-solving process as a temporally ordered sequence of functional episodes", comprising Read, Analyze, Plan, Implement, Explore, Verify, and Monitor, plus an added Answer episode capturing final answer commitment. In this article the taxonomy supplies a cognitively grounded vocabulary for characterizing how problem-solving burden is distributed across stages of long reasoning traces, enabling interpretable difficulty prediction.
