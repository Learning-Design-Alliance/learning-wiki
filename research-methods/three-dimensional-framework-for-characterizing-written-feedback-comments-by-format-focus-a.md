# Three-dimensional framework for characterizing written feedback comments by format, focus and tone

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article derives a three-dimensional characterization of written feedback comments on students' reflective essays via Multiple Correspondence Analysis of educators' card sorts. The dimensions are "format of the feedback comment, focus of the feedback comment and tone of the feedback comment": question versus statement, descriptive versus deeper reflection content, and positive versus negative tone. The framework is offered as a feasible basis for teacher training on written feedback.
