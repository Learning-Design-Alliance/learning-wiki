## Description
The principle holds that LLMs can make qualitative coding faster and reliable for large datasets in physics education, provided prompts are engineered and hyperparameters optimized, but that reliability must not be delegated to the machine. As the article states, "human-rater oversight is necessary for reliability and ethical rating practices". A small number of human raters can employ and monitor an LLM for analysis of large datasets, and subscription costs of proprietary models are an equity consideration researchers should weigh.
