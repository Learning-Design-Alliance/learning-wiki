# Price et al.'s expert problem-solving decision framework coding method

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A structured list of the decisions experts commonly make when solving problems, organized into categories A through G. Price et al's interviews of science and engineering experts found 'a surprising degree of overlap in the decisions made by experts across various fields', and this study used the framework to code devised plans: 'it is this framework that we base our analysis for this study'. The framework spans 34 decisions, from defining goals, criteria and constraints to maintaining attitude and efficiency, providing a structured approach to assessing problem-solving skills.
