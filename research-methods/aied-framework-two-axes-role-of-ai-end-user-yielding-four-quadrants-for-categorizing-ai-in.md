# AI×Ed framework: two axes (role of AI, end user) yielding four quadrants for categorizing AI-in-education research

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
AI×Ed is a categorization framework for research at the intersection of AI and education. Its vertical axis distinguishes "AI as an applied tool vs. AI as an analogy to HI" (human intelligence), and its horizontal axis distinguishes whether the primary end user is a researcher or a learner, with teachers, practitioners, policymakers, and parents in between. The two axes give rise to four quadrants, each highlighting a particular role AI can play in education, such as learner-facing intelligent tutoring systems (upper right), educational data mining (upper left), computational models of learning (lower left), and children writing AI programs to reflect on their own thinking (lower right). The authors use it to code and compare AIED and IJAIED papers across four decades.
