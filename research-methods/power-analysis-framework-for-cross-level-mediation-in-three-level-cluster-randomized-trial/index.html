Power analysis framework for cross-level mediation in three-level cluster-randomized trials¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article presents a framework in which mediation in a three-level CRT is formulated in a linear structural equation system, and "the mediation effect is the product of the direct effect of the treatment of interest on the mediator (a in expression (1)) by the direct effect of the mediator on the outcome controlling for the treatment (b in expression (2))". The asymptotic variance of the product is estimated with the Delta method following Sobel, and the resulting test statistic is used to estimate the power of a given design given a priori estimates of variance components and path coefficients. The framework is applied to plan studies of whether teacher knowledge mediates professional development effects on student achievement.
