# Social network analysis for e-learning interaction networks

## Description
The review frames social network analysis (SNA) as a methodological approach for describing and analysing network behaviours in e-learning. It notes SNA "has proven extremely powerful at describing and analysing network behaviours in business, economics and medicine, but its application to e-learning has been relatively limited." The review positions SNA as a way to understand and improve e-learning systems, operationalised through measures such as centrality and density applied to learner interaction networks, most often in forum-based one-mode networks.
