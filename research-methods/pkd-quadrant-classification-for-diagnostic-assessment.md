# pK–D̄ quadrant classification for diagnostic assessment

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Items are plotted with percentage correct pK on one axis and scaled randomness D̄ on the other, forming four quadrants. Q3 (low pK, low D̄) signals the class converged on a shared, identifiable error and is the pattern most worth an instructor's attention; Q1 (high randomness) is the null case consistent with undifferentiated guessing. The paper states "The pedagogically consequential contrast is between Q1 and Q3", and Q2 and Q4 are of secondary interest because D̄ adds little there.
