# SNA-based taxonomy of five types of collaborative relationships combining friendship and academic prestige networks

## Description
The article introduces a method that overlays friendship and academic-prestige networks onto the collaboration network to classify each collaborative tie. As the authors state, "we combined friendship, prestige and collaboration networks to identify different types of collaborative relationships among students." The resulting taxonomy (Table III) comprises five types: overall collaboration degree centrality, strong ties (between friends), weak ties (between non-friends), strong ties with academic prestige, and weak ties with academic prestige, each counted as degree centrality per course. This categorization is the article's stated methodological contribution for studying collaboration effectiveness.
