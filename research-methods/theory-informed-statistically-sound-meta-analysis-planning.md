## Description
This principle holds that a meta-analysis in applied linguistics delivers critical information only under three conditions: it must be "well planned," "employs sound statistical methods," and be "based on a thorough understanding of relevant theory." When these hold, the review states the meta-analysis "can provide critical information that informs theory as well as future research, practice, and policy." The article presents this as the general condition for meta-analysis to benefit SLA.
