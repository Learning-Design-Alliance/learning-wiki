# Standardized taxonomy descriptors for CSCL phenomena and analytic approaches

## Description
Because CSCL lacks shared descriptors for phenomena and analytic approaches, the article recommends standardized descriptors within a community taxonomy. These mitigate the jingle-jangle fallacy, using the same terms for different concepts or different terms for the same concept, letting researchers find relevant studies across paradigms based on the phenomenon itself. The taxonomy would also serve authors as a scaffold for articulating study aspects and as a transparent checklist supporting review processes.
