> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The norming approach, employed previously in the course-specific MAP Growth Mathematics norming study (Thum & He, 2019), uses "a multivariate true score model that accounts for the known imprecision of scores from the fall, winter, and spring terms." Observed scores are decomposed into true scores and error scores, with each score's standard error of measurement introduced into the model. True scores are assumed multivariate normal, and the resulting joint distribution of predicted fall, winter, and spring scores provides the basis for achievement norms (marginal distributions) and growth norms (between-term gains).
