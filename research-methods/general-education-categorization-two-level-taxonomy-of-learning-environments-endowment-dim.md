# General education categorization: two-level taxonomy of learning environments, endowment dimensions, and developmental dimensions

## Description
The article constructed a general education categorization from 343,238 Web of Science papers via a pipeline of "literature screening, term extraction, embedding generation, coarse categorization and semantic clustering," refined by two domain experts. The result comprises three main categories—learning environment (14 subcategories), endowment dimensions (13 subcategories), and developmental dimensions (8 subcategories)—used to assign values to student development agents and support empirical findings retrieval.
