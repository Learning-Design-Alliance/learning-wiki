# Ladyshewsky's equality and mutuality indices for describing peer-assisted learning methods

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review reports an earlier attempt by Ladyshewsky to clarify PAL terminology using two common indices: "1) equality (e.g., to which extent learners take direction from each other) and 2) mutuality (e.g., in relation to the learners' discourse)". The authors of the present article judge the framework theoretically sound but of limited practical applicability, because the indices are not quantifiable, overlap, and can make a single programme difficult to categorise.
