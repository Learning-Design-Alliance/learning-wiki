## Description
The study is framed by Kaupapa Māori Research (KMR) methodology, which the article describes as aligning "with a Māori inquiry paradigm and provides the theoretical foundations on which to develop research processes". It operates through principles including tino rangatiratanga (self-determination), ako Māori (culturally preferred pedagogy), and explicit rejection of findings blaming culture or genetics for educational failure, requiring instead that analysis critique structural power imbalances and demand institutional change. Within this methodology the authors developed a 'predictors of academic success' model grouping concepts: demographics, socioeconomic status, academic preparation, transitioning, early academic results, and the tertiary environment (Fig. 1).
