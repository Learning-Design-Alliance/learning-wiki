# dITA (distractor Information Theoretical Analysis)

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
dITA treats randomness in incorrect answers as a measurable signal rather than noise. It computes the Shannon entropy of the observed incorrect-answer distribution and compares it to the entropy expected under equiprobable distractor choice, yielding D′, the effective number of distractors in use, scaled to D̄ = D′/D with practical range 1/D to 1. The closed form makes D′ "the Hill number of order 1 of the incorrect-response distribution alone, with no remaining dependence on pK". Items are then classified on a pK–D̄ quadrant scheme.
