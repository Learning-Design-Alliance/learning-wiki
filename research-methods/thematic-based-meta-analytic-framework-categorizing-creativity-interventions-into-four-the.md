## Description
The article organizes creativity research into four themes: "creative thinking, creative thinking activities, creative thinking techniques and creative writing applications" (CT, CTA, CTT, CWA). Studies are coded in three phases—study identity, study characteristics (course type, grade levels, duration), and study data (sample sizes, means, standard deviations)—then pooled using Hedges' d under fixed-effect and random-effects models with Q-test homogeneity checks and fail-safe number analysis.
