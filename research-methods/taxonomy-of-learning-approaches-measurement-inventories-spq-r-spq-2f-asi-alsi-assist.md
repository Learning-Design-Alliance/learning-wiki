## Description
The review describes the family of questionnaires used to measure learning approaches: Biggs' Study Process Questionnaire (SPQ), whose strategic approach is termed the achieving approach; its revision, the R-SPQ-2F, which "uses fewer items to narrow in on solely the deep and surface approaches"; Entwistle and Ramsden's ASI with meaning, reproducing, achieving, and non-academic orientations; the ALSI, which "splits the strategic approach into organised studying and effort management"; and the ASSIST. The review notes "the ASSIST, alongside the R-SPQ-2F, is the most commonly used inventory for studying learning approaches."
