# Cognitive diagnostic models (CDMs)

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article frames CDMs against unidimensional item response models: unidimensional models "postulate a single underlying proficiency," whereas CDMs "posit multiple, discrete skills or attributes, thus allowing CDMs to provide a finer-grained assessment of examinees' test performance." The Q-matrix is described as a common CDM component for specifying the attributes required for each item.
