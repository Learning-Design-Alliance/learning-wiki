# Reliance calibration as an output-contingent classification of reliance decisions

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review defines reliance calibration as "an item-level, output-contingent classification of whether a reliance decision was appropriate given AI-output quality", distinguishing it from trust (a psychological attitude) and reliance (an observable decision). For mixed-quality outputs, calibration may be judged at claim level or on a graded appropriateness scale, because one response can contain accurate, incomplete, and misleading elements simultaneously. Accepting faulty content constitutes over-reliance; rejecting useful content constitutes under-reliance.
