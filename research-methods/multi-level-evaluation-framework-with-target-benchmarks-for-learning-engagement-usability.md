## Description
The article proposes evaluation across three domains: learning (pre-/post-tests, transfer tasks, sustained STEM interest), engagement and motivation (Bangla-adapted Intrinsic Motivation Inventory, Flow Short Scale, and in-game analytics), and usability (think-aloud trials with 15-20 students per tier, success rates, expert review). Table VII sets targets including "+15% gain" on pre-post tests, "70% mastery" on transfer tasks, "System Usability Scale >70," and "Cost per student-hour <$2 USD."
