## Description
The study adopts critical realism as its theoretical approach, carried out through a realist inquiry approach. As the article states, "Realist inquiry concerns what works for whom, under what conditions, how, and why by focusing on relationships between contexts, mechanisms, and outcomes." Context covers fixed settings like VR equipment and variable human factors such as cultures and trained behaviors; mechanisms operate at a psychological level to explain how and why people respond to interventions; outcomes are the intended and unintended impacts of the intervention. The authors use this framework to structure the discussion of VR's acceptability and applicability for MCI training.
