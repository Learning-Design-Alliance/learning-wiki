# Stakeholder-by-dimension heuristic for navigating AI integration complexity

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report proposes a systems-thinking heuristic to help science education researchers navigate the layered complexity of integrating AI. It is operationalized as a table that juxtaposes stakeholder groups (students, educators, parents, researchers, society, funders) against guiding dimensions: goals, potential uses of AI, research contributions, emergent problems, limitations, and ethical, legal, or cost-related challenges. A six-step process runs from defining the research question through anticipating risks to documenting standards, emphasizing that researchers must account for how AI tools shape both findings and the conditions under which findings become meaningful. Two use cases (a K-12 data analysis tool and scaling responsive teaching in higher education) illustrate its application.
