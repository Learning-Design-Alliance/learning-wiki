## Description
The article frames the Angoff method not as a single technique but as "a flexible family of approaches with distinct outcomes." The family includes Traditional Angoff, Modified Angoff, Angoff (Yes/No), Angoff Group (Yes/No), Three-level Angoff, and Mastery Angoff, each differing in how judges estimate borderline candidate performance. The choice of variant allows for the deliberate setting of more lenient or stringent standards, making variant selection a conscious decision aligned with assessment purpose and context.
