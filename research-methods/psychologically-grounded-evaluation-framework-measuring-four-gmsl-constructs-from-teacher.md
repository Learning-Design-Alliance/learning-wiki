# Psychologically grounded evaluation framework measuring four GMSL constructs from teacher and student surveys

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The paper proposes a survey-based framework for evaluating GMSL that measures the perceived impact of teacher language on students rather than text quality alone. "The surveys include items estimating four constructs given an excerpt of a student-teacher interaction: (1) teacher's per- ceived growth mindset, (2) teacher's promotion of challenge-taking behavior, (3) shame felt by students in the teacher's class, and (4) respect felt by students in the teacher's class." Items are adapted from instruments validated in large-scale growth mindset interventions, with baseline motivational framework items as controls.
