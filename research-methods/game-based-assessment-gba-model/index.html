Game-Based Assessment (GBA) model¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The GBA model is a design strategy developed by the Games, Learning and Society group to capture evidence of player learning during game play rather than treating the game as a black box. It integrates four layers: "the content-model; the game flow design; and the GBA components of the semantic template and learning telemetry." The content model outlines learning goals, game-flow design builds interaction opportunities around them, the semantic template selects which click-stream data may indicate learning, and the learning telemetry layer collects and organizes that data into player profiles for analysis against pre-post measures. It draws on evidence-centered design, stealth assessment, and education data mining.
