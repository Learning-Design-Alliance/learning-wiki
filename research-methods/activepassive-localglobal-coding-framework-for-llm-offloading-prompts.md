# Active/passive local/global coding framework for LLM offloading prompts

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A four-way coding scheme applied to each of 564 learner prompts, capturing whether the learner "remained somewhat active or was passive during a comprehension activity, and if the comprehension within a prompt was targetting local or global level". Active prompts specify learner direction (clarifying gaps, seeking evaluation or structure); passive prompts leave the LLM to act without learner-specified constraints; local refers to micro-level items and global to whole-text or cross-text structure. Reproducibility was checked against three LLM-based labelling agents, reaching an average kappa of 0.64 (passive/global) and 0.61 (local/global).
