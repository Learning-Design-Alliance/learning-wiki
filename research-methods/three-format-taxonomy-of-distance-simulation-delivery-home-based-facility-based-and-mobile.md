# Three-format taxonomy of distance simulation delivery: home-based, facility-based, and mobile

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review classifies distance simulation delivery into three descriptive formats. "Home-based distance simulation" involves trainees using portable simulators at home or other remote locations; "facility-based distance simulation" has trainees physically at a training center receiving instruction remotely via telecommunication; and "mobile simulation" transports a simulator to the learners' location. The review used these categories to group studies and interpret implementation strategies, finding home-based formats in 64 studies, facility-based in 39, and mobile in 1.
