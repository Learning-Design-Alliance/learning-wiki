## Description
Where jurisdictions lack longitudinal achievement data because surveys are costly, intrusive, and suffer attrition, the article recommends creating representative data by linking large-scale administrative databases under clear protocols and validation procedures. Successive cohorts can be continually linked if databases are cyclically re-generated, supporting "sustainable research." The authors "recommend the filling of key voids in sustainable research capacity by creating representative data through linkage protocols and data verification."

- **Fill longitudinal data voids by linking complementary administrative datasets through partnerships rather than new surveys**: Where jurisdictions lack longitudinal achievement data because surveys are costly, intrusive, and suffer attrition, the article recommends creating representative data by linking large-scale administrative databases under clear protocols and validation procedures. Successive cohorts can be continually linked if databases are cyclically re-generated, supporting "sustainable research." The authors "recommend the filling of key voids in sustainable research capacity by creating representative data through linkage protocols and data verification." (Sinclair Jeanne et al. (2023))
- **Cross-agency data linkage of hearing assessment, perinatal, school attendance and NAPLAN administrative datasets**: The study used unit-level linkage of four key administrative datasets (NT Perinatal Data Register, school enrolment and attendance records, NAPLAN results, and the Remote Hearing Assessment database) undertaken by SA NT DataLink using probabilistic linkage and clerical review. The authors state the study "demonstrates the utility of cross-agency data linkage that combines hearing assessment data with other administrative datasets in facilitating comprehensive investigations not possible using stand-alone datasets or survey methods". The linkage enabled adjustment for perinatal, attendance and community-level confounders. (Su Jiunn-Yih et al. (2020))
- **Linked national surgical training databases (ISCP, eLogbook, JCST SIMS, JCIE) as a data source for cohort competency analysis**: This is the set of mandatory UK repositories the study linked by GMC number: the ISCP database (training dates, PBA records), the JCST SIMS database (out-of-programme periods), the Joint Committee on Intercollegiate Examinations database (special interest at examination), and the eLogbook (procedures, urgency, supervision level). The article states "All databases used are mandatory repositories of surgical training data in the UK." Complete training records, reviewed regularly by supervisors, are described as adding reliability to the data. (Elsey EJ et al. (2018))
