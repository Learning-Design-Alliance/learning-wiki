## Description
The study's theoretical framework is Bahr's deconstructive approach, in which students' academic pathways through higher education are examined to understand their dynamic interaction with institutional integration and progress toward academic goals. Transcript data are used to "'deconstruct' the varied steps or stages through which students pass from the point of college entry to a given outcome of interest." By examining academic histories in stages to identify course attempts and outcomes, potential institutional interventions may be identified to improve performance and reduce attrition.
