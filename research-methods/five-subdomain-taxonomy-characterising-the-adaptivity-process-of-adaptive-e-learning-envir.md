# Five-subdomain taxonomy characterising the adaptivity process of adaptive e-learning environments

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review proposes that the adaptivity process of an AEE be characterised by five subdomains that "emerged from the review": "(1) Adaptivity method. (2) Adaptivity goals. (3) Adaptivity timing. (4) Adaptivity factors. (5) Adaptivity types". Method distinguishes designed (expert-based, 'if THIS, then THAT') from algorithmic adaptivity; goals cover effectiveness, efficiency, behaviour change and metacognitive processes; timing distinguishes beginning-only from throughout-training adaptation; factors are the learner data used (scores, actions, response time); types follow Knutov et al (content, navigation, multimedia, presentation, tools). The review applies this taxonomy to all 21 included AEEs.
