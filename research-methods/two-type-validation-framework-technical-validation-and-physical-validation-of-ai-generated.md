# Two-type validation framework: technical validation and physical validation of AI-generated simulations

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article names and describes two complementary validation types for LLM-generated simulations. "Technical validation tests the simulation's behavior under various conditions and assess its response to parameter changes", while "Physical validation compares simulation results to known analytical solutions and verify consistency with established physical laws". The framework is applied to each of the three demonstrated simulations and can be conducted through natural-language dialogue with the model.
