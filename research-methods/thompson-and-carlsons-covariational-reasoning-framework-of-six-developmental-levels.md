# Thompson and Carlson's covariational reasoning framework of six developmental levels

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article adopts the covariational reasoning framework, which describes reasoning as 'progressing through several developmental levels', from no coordination of varying quantities, through pre-coordination, gross coordination, coordination of values, and chunky continuous covariation, to smooth continuous covariation in which both variables vary smoothly and continuously. The researchers used these levels to code the two students' reasoning as they interpreted the height-time graph.

Covariational reasoning is a hierarchy of mental actions from coordinating values and directions of change to reasoning about rates of change; coordinated changes "need not be physically realized but may be imagined".
