# Dohn's three-level framework of competence demands applied to literacy demands

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper uses Dohn's (2009a, b) context-level framework to analyze literacy demands as context-sensitive expectations placed on students at three interacting levels. The "domain-internal level" concerns domain-specific traits, facts, and perspectives; the "activity-internal level" concerns how the activity is performed, such as integrating GenAI inputs into coherent writing; and the "activity-framing level" concerns how the setting norms the activity, such as institutional expectations about individually authored work. The levels are analytical rather than isolated: demands interact with, frame, concretize, interfere with, and contradict each other, producing a unity of demands negotiated in every concrete situation that may be incoherent.
