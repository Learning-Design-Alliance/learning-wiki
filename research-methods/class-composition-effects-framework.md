## Description
The article organizes its inquiry around composition effects, which "occur when individual student characteristics (e.g., individual academic achievement) are combined into a class-level composite score (e.g., mean academic achievement) and that score is related to individual-level outcomes (e.g., academic achievement) beyond student-level characteristic." The framework spans mean-level composition (achievement, interest) and within-class heterogeneity dimensions, tested at the classroom level with individual-level outcomes, and extended to motivational composition and learning-environment mediators. It guides the study's hypotheses about who benefits from which composition.
