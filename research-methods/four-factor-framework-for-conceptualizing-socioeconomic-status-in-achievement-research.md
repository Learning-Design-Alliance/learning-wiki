# Four-factor framework for conceptualizing socioeconomic status in achievement research

## Description
The review's moderator results yield a methodological recommendation: "researchers should consider the following four factors when conceptualizing SES: (a) the unit of analysis for SES data; (b) the type of SES measure; (c) the range of the SES variable; and (d) the source of SES data." Each factor demonstrably shifts the magnitude of the SES-achievement correlation, so measurement choices must be reported and justified. The author also recommends collecting SES information from parents rather than students.
