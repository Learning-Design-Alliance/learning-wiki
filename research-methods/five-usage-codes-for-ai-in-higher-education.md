# Five usage codes for AI in higher education

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This systematic review used grounded coding of 138 articles to let uses of AI in higher education emerge from the data rather than fitting them to pre-existing frameworks. The review reports that "Five usage codes emerged from the data: (1) Assessment/Evaluation, (2) Predicting, (3) AI Assistant, (4) Intelligent Tutoring System (ITS), and (5) Managing Student Learning." Each code has axial codes as subcategories, e.g. automatic assessment, prediction of dropouts, chatbots, adaptive tutoring, and learning analytics.
