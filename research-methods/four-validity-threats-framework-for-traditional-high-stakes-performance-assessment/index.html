Four validity threats framework for traditional high-stakes performance assessment¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review organizes its analysis around four validity threats that traditional high-stakes performance assessments pose: test anxiety among candidates, the restricted linearity resulting from combining standardization with construct-relevant tasks, a lack of assessment authenticity, and inconsistencies between raters in scoring performance. It states that "The first validity threat associated with high-stakes performance assessments is the prevalence of test anxiety among candidates", and evaluates serious-game mechanics by their potential to mediate each threat. The framework structures the review's extraction of game mechanics and its design principles.
