## Description
The article introduces 'confidence judgement', a novel measure defined as "the accuracy of one's own judgement in relation to one's ability at an online or written test", conceptualised as an aspect of metacognition. It is computed as the within-candidate correlation between the log odds of answering each item correctly, predicted by a two-parameter logistic IRT model, and the candidate's own confidence ratings rescaled as within-person z-scores to separate ability from trait confidence. A score of 1 represents perfect judgement, 0 no relationship, and −1 perfect inverse correlation.
