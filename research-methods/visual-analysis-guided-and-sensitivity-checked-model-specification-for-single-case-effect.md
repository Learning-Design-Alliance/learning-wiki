## Description
The article recommends that analysts choose the hierarchical model for single-case data by first using visual inspection of the data series, then conducting preliminary analyses such as information criteria and likelihood ratio tests. It states: "we recommend researchers using visual analysis to guide the initial model specification and then conduct preliminary analyses, including information criteria and likelihood ratio tests, to reach an appropriate model specification." Because different specifications yield different effect size estimates, it also recommends reporting sensitivity analyses across plausible models.

- **Avoid PND and IRD as magnitude estimates in SCD meta-analyses; prefer Tau or NAP among overlap measures**: The article recommends that reviewers selecting quantitative synthesis measures for single-case meta-analyses not use PND or IRD as estimates of effect magnitude, writing "we do not recommend the use of PND or IRD in meta-analytic summaries of SCD interventions as an estimate of magnitude of effect." PND and IRD null values are not zero and their magnitudes vary with the number of baseline observations, design type, session length, and recording system. Tau lacks this sample-size dependence and has an available standard-error formula, so the authors recommend "considering use of Tau, or the directly equivalent NAP measure, instead of other overlap metrics." (Zimmerman et al. (2018))
