## Description
Interaction geography is an analytical approach introduced by Shapiro, Hall, and Owens that connects "novel forms of analyses" to constitute "the key elements of the analytical approach." It extends interaction analysis by including physical movements: through movement analysis researchers "can explore how the syntax of space creates an 'interaction geography' from which visitors' gestures and activities can be analyzed."
