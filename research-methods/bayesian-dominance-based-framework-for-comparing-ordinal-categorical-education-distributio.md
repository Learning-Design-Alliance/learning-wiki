# Bayesian dominance-based framework for comparing ordinal categorical education distributions

## Description
A Bayesian framework for comparing ordinal categorical education distributions: first order stochastic dominance and a restricted two-category form for education levels; generalized Lorenz dominance, the Cowell–Flachaire and Jenkins indices, and a poverty-style headcount index for inequality. Results are posterior densities and dominance probabilities; the article notes "An advantage of the Bayesian approach is that three dominance probabilities can be reported."
