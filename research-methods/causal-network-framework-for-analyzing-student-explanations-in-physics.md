## Description
A proposed analytic framework the article offers for future development: "One plausible candidate for such a framework is the formalism of causal network , which can represent the complete set of causal relations between factors in a physical situation." The rationale is that "the reasoning used for qualitative physics questions is fundamentally causal  rather than explicitly computational," so causal networks may align well with the conceptual reasoning such problems demand. The framework is intended to replace ad-hoc expert comparison of student ideas with canonical physics by systematically representing how student explanations align, and fail to align, with valid causal reasoning.
