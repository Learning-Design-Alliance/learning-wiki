> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The editorial describes Bridges et al.'s methodological lens, Interactional Ethnography (IE), as "a logic-of-inquiry that can be used to examine collaborative processes" as undergraduate students and their facilitator work with multimodal text. Its companion conceptualization, dialogic intervisualizing, is designed to capture "the interplay between the conversation, the broader collaborative problem solving processes, and the evolving artifacts produced during the interaction", drawing on Silverstein's concept of indexical order for how conceptual objects are decontextualized and recontextualized across modalities.
