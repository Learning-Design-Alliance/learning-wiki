# Five-category latent skill taxonomy for science MCQs (S1-S5)

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The taxonomy defines five skill categories calibrated to NEET-type science MCQs: S1 Direct Recall, S2 Conceptual Application, S3 Multi-concept Integration, S4 Quantitative Reasoning, and S5 Elimination and Negation. "Categories are chosen for structural discriminability: each can be identified from the surface form of the question without requiring inference about latent cognitive depth, which maximizes inter-rater reliability." S1-S4 map approximately to revised Bloom levels, while S5 does not appear in standard taxonomies and is introduced to capture format-driven falsification operations; the design follows collapsed Bloom rubrics that achieved higher inter-rater reliability than six-level schemes.
