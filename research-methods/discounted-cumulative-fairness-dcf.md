# Discounted Cumulative Fairness (DCF)

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The survey reviews individual fairness definitions that require similar students to receive similar predictions, including distance-based Lipschitz-condition metrics and ranking-based metrics using NDCG@k and ERR@k. It highlights that "Zhanget al.[62] proposed Discounted Cumulative Fairness (DCF), an individual fairness metric built upon the ranking-based fairness framework and explicitly designed to handle censored educational outcomes", discounting uncertainty from partially observed records so missing data does not skew fairness evaluations.
