# Four-dimension classification framework for fNIRS-enabled game-integrated learning systems

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review develops a classification framework for characterizing fNIRS-enabled GILS research along four dimensions. The article states: "The framework comprises four dimensions: learning platform, device type, outcome measure, and study type, each corresponding to a column in Table 1." Learning platforms are distinguished by pedagogical purpose into serious games, game-based learning, and educational games; device types by interaction fidelity into screen-based, immersive, and specialized; outcomes into brain activation, behavioral, subjective, and learning measures; and studies into comparative versus cognitive-response types. It organizes the review's synthesis of all 20 included studies.
