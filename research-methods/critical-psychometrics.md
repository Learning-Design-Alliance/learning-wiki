## Description
The article proposes "critical psychometrics" as the next stage in psychometric evolution, in which "psychometricians engage critical and interpretivist approaches more fully during scale development to consider power dynamics, gender disparities, and socio‐cultural differences as an integral part of validity and reliability." It builds on QuantCrit, which explores interaction terms for demographic variables so models can predict impacts of sexism and racism in ways that are not merely additive, and on critical realist philosophy-of-science paradigms. Figure 3 depicts historically subjective worldviews as required to support psychometrically sound scale development, with outputs of the scales feeding back into interpretivist approaches.
