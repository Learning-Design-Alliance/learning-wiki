# Digital shadowing as a framework for recording LLM behaviour under controlled conditions

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Digital shadowing is the article's organising methodological framework: unlike digital twins, shadows record system behaviour without feedback loops. The article states that "digital shadows record what a system does across controlled conditions without updating, simulating, or influencing external counterparts." A Math Education Digital Shadow is defined as a structured record of an LLM's mathematics-related psychological profile and problem-solving behaviour under specified constraints. Human shadows simulate synthetic individuals defined by sociodemographic and psychological attributes, while AI assistants respond as themselves.
