# Operationalisation framework for observing adaptive practices in clinical settings

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An observational instrument developed in the study that translates the adaptive expert framework into observable behavioural markers for cognitive ethnography. It defines markers for three constructs: epistemic distance (e.g., "Looking up information, acts of surprise or simply stating 'I do not know'"), self-regulation (e.g., "ordering more tests or seeking help"), and orientation to novel content (e.g., "Seeking learning opportunities"). The analysis then organised observations into taxonomic tables structured by actor, moderator, and consequence, integrating observations of cognitive artifacts such as patient journals and technological aids.
