# Six-element itemization of scaffolding as it appears in written physics exam questions

## Description
The article defines scaffolding for written exam questions via six elements: use of representations and language to bridge expert-novice understanding, reduction of cognitive overhead, insertion of expert knowledge, ordered task decomposition, conceptual prompting, and reduction of degrees of freedom, each itemized into concrete instances (20 items total). The authors state "we deﬁne 6 general ways in which scaﬀolding can occur (elements)" and use the elements as a scoring system assigning each exam question a scaffolding score. Many elements are adapted from Quintana et al.'s scaffolding design framework, with conceptual prompting motivated by work showing students apply concepts when prompted beforehand.
