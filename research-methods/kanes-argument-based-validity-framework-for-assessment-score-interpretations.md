## Description
The article applies Messick construct-validity framework and Kane's argument-based approach to interpret LLM examination scores, examining "evidence concerning scoring, generalization, and extrapolation, together with construct-irrelevant variance and construct underrepresentation". Table 1 maps four Kane inferences to evidence: scoring is supported for recorded answers against the official key; generalization is limited to these items and configurations; extrapolation shows that "Clinical competence and equivalence with human score interpretations are untested"; and the study does not validate clinical decisions or autonomous educational use. This framework structures the article's central caution that high model accuracy does not by itself establish professional competence.
