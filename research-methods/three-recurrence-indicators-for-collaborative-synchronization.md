## Description
The article proposes a system-based index for measuring learners' collaborative processes built on the degree of behavior recurrence of each speaker. It focuses on "three indicators—gaze synchronization, language conformance, and emotional matching through facial expression" to establish an index of synchronization during PCA-mediated collaborative learning. The indicators are quantitative metrics derived from qualitative investigations of joint activity, intended for real-time detection of coordination quality by tutoring systems.
