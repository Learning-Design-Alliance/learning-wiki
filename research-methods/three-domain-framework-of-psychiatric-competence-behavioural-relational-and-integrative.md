# Three-domain framework of psychiatric competence: behavioural, relational and integrative

## Description
The review conceptualises psychiatric competence as comprising three interrelated domains. Behavioural competence refers to observable clinical actions such as information gathering and interview organisation; relational competence encompasses empathy, rapport and therapeutic engagement; and integrative competence involves "the formulation of clinically meaningful understandings through the synthesis of biological, psychological and social information". The review uses this framework deductively to analyse which domains are explicitly represented in published assessment approaches.
