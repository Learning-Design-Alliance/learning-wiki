## Description
The paper redefines the discrimination index, scaling the top-versus-bottom-27% mark difference by each question's maximum marks so one equation covers MCQs, long-answer and experimental items. The authors state it "can handle negative marking in MCQs without any modiﬁcation to equation or the data," and that "wrong answer" and "blank answer" are not treated at par.
