# Taxonomy of four SR research traditions distinguished by core explanatory construct families and Lakatosian hard cores

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Using a Lakatosian framework of research programs, the article categorizes SR research into four traditions, each with a hard core of foundational assertions protected by a belt of auxiliary hypotheses and methods. The article states: "we categorize existing research programs into four broad traditions, each of which is characterized by its core explanatory construct family: (1) Stable Dispositions, (2) Limited Resources, (3) Driving Forces, and (4) Learning Activities." The traditions differ in stability assumptions, methodologies, and temporal levels of analysis.
