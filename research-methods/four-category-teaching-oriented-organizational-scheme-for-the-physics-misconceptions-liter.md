# Four-category teaching-oriented organizational scheme for the physics-misconceptions literature

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article assembles a teaching-oriented overview of selected literature and sorts it into four top-level categories with subcategories: "Sources of misconceptions, Diagnostic methods, Remediation strategies, and Other." The database "contains 122 distinct works," each assigned a primary category by its principal pedagogical contribution, with subcategories such as reflection-based, discussion-based, and experiment-based remediation. It is offered as a guide for instructors to navigate themes, not as evidence of instructional effectiveness.
