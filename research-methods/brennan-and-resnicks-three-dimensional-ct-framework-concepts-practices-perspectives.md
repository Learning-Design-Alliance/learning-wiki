# Brennan and Resnick's three-dimensional CT framework (concepts, practices, perspectives)

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article adopts Brennan and Resnick (2012)'s framework as its theoretical scaffold for understanding CT learning and assessment. It classifies CT into three dimensions: "concepts, practices, and perspectives" — concepts are foundational programming ideas (sequences, loops, conditionals, events), practices are how learners apply concepts in problem-solving (testing, debugging, decomposition), and perspectives address how learners view themselves in relation to computing. The framework integrates cognitive, procedural, and dispositional dimensions and directly informed the coding of the learning-outcome moderator.
