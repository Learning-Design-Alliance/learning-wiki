## Description
The article positions its framework as a reusable analytic instrument beyond its empirical basis. It states the framework "represents a constructive tool for analysing and comparing textbooks" regarding the multiplicity of force definitions, with potential for analysing classroom teaching and student responses about forces. The framework was designed to be as context-free as possible to be applicable in areas of physics besides dynamics.
