# Three-level trace clustering analysis scheme for self-regulated learning in restrictive mastery-based online learning systems

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The scheme analyzes restrictive-system trace data at three consecutive levels of granularity. At the event level, finite mixture modeling of log attempt and study durations separates abnormally short guessing attempts and click-through study events from normal ones. At the module level, hierarchical agglomerative clustering partitions module traces into m-clusters using data-driven, hybrid, or theory-based distance metrics, visualized with process maps. At the sequence level, optimal-matching clustering of m-cluster membership traces reveals how students' strategies change over time. The authors state it was developed because "online learning systems or online learning data sets are oftentimes more restrictive, meaning that they provide fewer event types and less variability in event sequences."
