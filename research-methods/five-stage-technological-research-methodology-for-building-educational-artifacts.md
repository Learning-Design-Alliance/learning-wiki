## Description
The article classifies its study as technological research, which "emprega meios de pesquisa científica (básica ou aplicada) com o propósito de construir um artefato cuja composição e utilidade dela derive", citing Bunge's notion of technology as knowledge relative to the design of artifacts. It prescribes five stages: 1) problem identification, motivation and sensitization; 2) definition of the proposed technology's objectives; 3) design and development of the artifact, including construction mapping, evidence modeling and quality-control calibration; 4) demonstration, implementing the artifact in a controlled environment close to the real one with performance tests; and 5) evaluation of functionality using indicators such as usability, requirement adequacy, precision, viability, completeness, sustainability and safety.
