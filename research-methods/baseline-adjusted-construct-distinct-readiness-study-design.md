## Description
Drawing on Brainerd (1977), the article sets two methodological standards for studies claiming to identify children on the verge of learning: the learning measure "must take into account the child's level of knowledge before training", indexing improvement rather than absolute performance, and the measure used to capture learning should not be the same as the measure used to identify children as transitional, to avoid circularity. The article shows Perry et al. (1988) met both standards, since mismatch counts on the pretest identified transition while posttest problem-solving measured learning.
