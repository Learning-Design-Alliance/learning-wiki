SAVVY content-derived visual and auditory information density metrics¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Grounded in the Limited Capacity Model of Motivated Mediated Message Processing (LC4MP), SAVVY quantifies information density of both visual and auditory channels per slide-transition-induced event segment. Visual density combines edge density of image regions (detected with Mask R-CNN), average normalized IDF surprisal of slide text (detected with DBNet), weighted by region area ratios; auditory density uses the entropy of the narration's word distribution divided by narration duration, plus semantic similarity between slide text and transcript to capture cross-channel redundancy. The authors caution these are "content-derived proxy indicators" describing the presented content itself, not learners' internal cognitive states.
