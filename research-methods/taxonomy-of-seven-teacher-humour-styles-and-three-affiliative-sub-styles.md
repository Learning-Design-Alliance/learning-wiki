# Taxonomy of seven teacher humour styles and three affiliative sub-styles

## Description
The review organises teachers' classroom humour into seven styles drawn from the included literature: course-related, course-unrelated, affiliative, aggressive, self-enhancing, self-defeating, and festive humour. It defines each, e.g. course-related humour is "humour used to explain or display information related to the class", and notes styles overlap and can be positive or negative in nature. Şahin's (2021) three affiliative sub-styles (shy, passive, partial) are included, with classroom observations identifying students as the most common trigger for teachers' humour.
