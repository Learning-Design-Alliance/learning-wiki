# Research-validation framework for classroom observation protocols

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article argues that observation protocols, which are used by faculty rather than answered by students, require a development and validation process substantially different from other RBAIs. Its categories include research into classroom behavior, iterative observations, inter-rater reliability, tested training materials, multi-institution use, and independent peer-reviewed publication. As the article states, "when developing observation protocols, it is vital to ensure that the categories of observation are grounded in real classrooms." Protocols are iteratively developed through classroom use, and it does not make sense to do student interviews for them.
