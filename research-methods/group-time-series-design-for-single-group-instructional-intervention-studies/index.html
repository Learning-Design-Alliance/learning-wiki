Group time series design for single-group instructional intervention studies¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A group time series design involves repeated pretests and posttests on a single group, allowing researchers to track development over multiple measurement points. In this study, the design included two pretests, three treatment sessions (X1-X3), and three posttests across 10 weeks with 20 teaching hours. This design helps establish whether improvement follows the intervention rather than natural maturation, by comparing pretest-to-pretest stability against pretest-to-posttest gains.
