Four-category functional taxonomy of AI tools shaping collaborative learning in higher education¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review organizes AI tools by underlying functionality into four categories: predictive analytics (machine learning models, early warning systems) that predict student success and identify at-risk students; language-based systems (chatbots, large language models, natural language processors) enabling close-in-time interaction and automatic feedback; recommender algorithms personalizing learning through resource proposals and peer matching; and intelligent tutoring and monitoring applications recording emotional states and performance for context-adaptive feedback. This taxonomy structures the review's synthesis of how AI enriches collaborative learning processes and outcomes.
