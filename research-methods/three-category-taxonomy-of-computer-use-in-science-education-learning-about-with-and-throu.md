# Three-category taxonomy of computer use in science education: learning about, with, and through computers

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article adopts a categorization, attributed to Bybee, Poewll, & Trowbridge (2008), in which "the overlapping terms related to the uses of computer and associated technologies in science education are categorized into three": learning about computers (knowledge of computers and their parts), learning with computers (computers as tools for data acquisition, analysis, communication and information retrieval), and learning through computers (computer as an aid for presentations, practice and drill, with immediate feedback and self-paced work). The study itself involves mainly learning through computers as well as learning about computers.
