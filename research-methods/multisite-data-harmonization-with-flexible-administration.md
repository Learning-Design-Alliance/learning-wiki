# Multisite data harmonization with flexible administration

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To balance aggregation needs with local variation in calendars, topics, and classrooms, the article recommends that "researchers standardize data types across sites and centralize data collection infrastructure to the extent possible" while building in participant flexibility. For surveys this means researcher-owned Qualtrics versions, standardized recording templates, or centralized platforms, with flexible administration windows; researchers should determine a priori whether analyses are robust to varying administration conditions.
