## Description
The article proposes analyzing argumentative knowledge construction in CSCL along four process dimensions: participation, epistemic, argumentative, and social mode. The framework spans the whole analysis pipeline, "from sampling and segmentation of the discourse corpora to the analysis of four process dimensions". It is intended to refine process-oriented instructional support such as collaboration scripts by making specific discourse processes measurable.
