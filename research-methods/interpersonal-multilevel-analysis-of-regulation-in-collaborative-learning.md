## Description
The editorial reports that Su et al. advocate studying regulation "using the inter-personal level as the starting point and focusing on the interdependency between individual action and social context", rather than conceptualizing regulation mainly as self-regulation with the individual as unit of analysis. - **Multilevel framework of regulation in collaborative learning across physiological, psychological, and interpersonal levels**: The article frames regulation in collaborative learning as a multifaceted phenomenon that unfolds across multiple systemic levels. Monitoring challenges "can be seen as adjustments on different systemic levels (physiological, psychological, and interpersonal) of a collaborative group." This framework motivates simultaneously measuring interactional indicators (video-coded monitoring) and physiological indicators (EDA arousal and synchrony) to capture how regulation manifests across levels during collaboration. (Haataja et al. (2021))
