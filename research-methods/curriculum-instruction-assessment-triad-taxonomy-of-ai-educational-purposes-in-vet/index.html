Curriculum-Instruction-Assessment triad taxonomy of AI educational purposes in VET¶

Description¶

Building on Wang et al. (2024) and Dai et al. (2025), the review organizes AI applications in VET "into the main categories of the Curriculum-Instruction-Assessment-Triad (Pellegrino, 2012)", plus holistic system-level functions. The curriculum domain covers curriculum creation and analysis; the instruction domain distinguishes seven pedagogical functions (instructional planning, adaptive learning, intelligent tutoring, conversational agents, content curation, instructional creation, authentic simulation); the assessment domain covers intelligent assessment and profiling/prediction; holistic functions include administration and guidance. Table 1 maps each purpose to AI types (ML, NLP, rule-based, computer vision) and example applications.
