# Three-level classification of outcome assessments by alignment with the intervention

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To describe variation in effectiveness, the review classified outcome assessments "into one of three levels of alignment with the intervention": (1) broadly focused standardized tests such as a reading achievement test; (2) narrowly focused standardized tests targeting a subdomain such as passage comprehension; and (3) specialized topic tests developed specifically for an intervention. This classification draws on prior research relating intervention effects to type of outcome assessment, and is used in cross-tabulations with intervention type, student sample, and study context.
