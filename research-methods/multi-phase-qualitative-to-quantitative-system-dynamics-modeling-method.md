# Multi-phase qualitative-to-quantitative system dynamics modeling method

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article develops a multi-phase method moving from semi-structured student interviews through inductive thematic analysis to a runnable system dynamics model. Interview themes become causal loop diagrams and stock-and-flow structures, and coded mention counts become equation weights, "systematically converting qualitative themes into weighted parameters". The method was applied to model engineering student engagement in Learning Studios, bridging qualitative perception and quantitative analysis.
