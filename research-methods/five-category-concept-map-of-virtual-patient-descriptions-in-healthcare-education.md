# Five-category concept map of virtual patient descriptions in healthcare education

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article develops a concept map synthesizing how virtual patients are described in the healthcare education literature. Five main categories were developed inductively-deductively, "stemming from a Simulation-based model developed by Issenberg" and the authors' shared understanding of VPs; the categories represent the life-cycle and environment of a VP with three main actors: the patient, the teacher who creates a VP, and the learner who engages with a VP, plus the VP itself as learning activity and the curriculum as environment. The map visualizes concepts and relations using CMap software and is offered as a dynamic resource for educators and researchers.
