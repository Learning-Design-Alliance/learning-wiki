## Description
The article organizes its comparison of the two assessment methods along four dimensions: ability to measure progression of surgical skills, suitability for understandable feedback, feasibility of acquiring during clinical procedures, and future perspectives. It states APM are "easy to capture in the simulated setting and often good at measuring skills progression but can be difficult to convert to meaningful feedback", while gesture analysis breaks surgery into phases of actions and "can be used to analyze performance patterns throughout a procedure instead of a cumulative score as with the APM". Both methods currently have low clinical feasibility.
