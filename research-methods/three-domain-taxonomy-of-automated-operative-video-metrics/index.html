Three-domain taxonomy of automated operative video metrics¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The review organizes the metrics generated by automated video analysis into three domains: process metrics (for example movement of instruments), outcome metrics (for example intraoperative phase duration), and critical safety elements (for example critical view of safety in laparoscopic cholecystectomy). Process-based metrics "uniformly analysed motion" and were mostly generated by computer vision tracking, while outcome-based metrics were more diverse and largely generated from advanced AI-based analysis. The review uses this classification to describe the full range of metrics across the 13 included studies.
