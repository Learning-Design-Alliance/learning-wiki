# Four lenses on pathways toward inequity in AIEd: system, data, algorithmic, and human–algorithm

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The chapter proposes an organizing framework of four lenses for examining how AIEd systems risk amplifying existing inequities: (1) socio-technical system design, (2) data reflecting historical inequities, (3) underlying algorithms, and (4) interplay between automated and human decision-making. Each lens "dedicat[es] higher resolution to particular components of the overall socio-technical system," and the authors argue that equitable design "will likely require meaningful integration across all four lenses." The framework maps how stakeholder decisions, training data, and algorithmic behavior form bidirectional feedback relationships.
