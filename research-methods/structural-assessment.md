# Structural assessment

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Structural assessment is defined as measuring the learners' knowledge organization and comparing this with a referent structure. It rests on the assumption that knowledge is organized in structures containing important concepts and the relations among them. The article proposes it as an alternative to verbal assessment for serious games, arguing that effective knowledge structures enable faster problem solving and integration of new information.
