## Description
The article proposes "a seven -stage workflow model, grounded in the Learning Analytics Cycle Framework (Clow, 2012), for quantitatively analysing educational video design at scale". The stages span planning and video selection, data collection, dataset construction, exploratory data analysis, model training and validation, insight extraction, and educational design feedback. It is implemented in Python with open-source libraries such as scikit-learn, OpenCV, Whisper and numpy, and is designed to be compatible with various video-hosting platforms and both educational and research contexts.
