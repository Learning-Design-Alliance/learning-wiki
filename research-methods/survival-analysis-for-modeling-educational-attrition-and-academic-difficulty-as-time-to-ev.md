# Survival analysis for modeling educational attrition and academic difficulty as time-to-event outcomes

## Description
The article advances a methodological framework that treats attrition and academic probation as time-to-event outcomes modeled with semi-parametric multivariable Cox proportional hazards models, rather than as dichotomous variables measured at a single point. The authors argue that "using dichotomous outcomes may obscure the complex, multifactorial nature of attrition, which unfolds over time" and that a time-to-event framework "allows risk to vary across the course of training." They apply this framework to a complete cohort of PA students using undergraduate admissions metrics as predictors, presenting results as adjusted hazard ratios with 95% confidence intervals. The framework is guided by the academic integration component of Tinto's Student Integration Theory.
