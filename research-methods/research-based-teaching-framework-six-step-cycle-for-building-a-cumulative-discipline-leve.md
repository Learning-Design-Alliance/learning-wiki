# Research-based teaching framework: six-step cycle for building a cumulative discipline-level knowledge base about student learning

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article outlines a framework used by physics education researchers to build a common disciplinary knowledge base about student learning. It proceeds from "Begin with a demonstrated knowledge gap with respect to a key concept in the field" through designing resources to address the gap, assessing learning, checking robustness across instructors and institutions, and adding to the cumulative knowledge base. The authors argue economists could adopt this model.
