# Multilevel latent transition analysis with a mixture IRT measurement model (multilevel LTA–MixIRTM)

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The multilevel LTA–MixIRTM extends latent transition analysis by incorporating a mixture IRT measurement model into a two-level LTA, so that "change can be accounted for as a transition from one latent class to another and as progress or lack thereof along a latent continuous variable (e.g., ability) within a latent class." It models students nested within teachers, with teacher-level latent classes characterized by different proportions of student-level latent classes, and regresses transition proportions on intervention status at the teacher level. The article presents it as the first model to simultaneously combine a continuous latent variable within a mixture, a latent Markov process for longitudinal data, and a multilevel data structure.
