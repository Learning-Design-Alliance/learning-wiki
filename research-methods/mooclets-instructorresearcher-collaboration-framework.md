# MOOClets instructor–researcher collaboration framework

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper proposes a framework in which "instructors specify what kinds of changes to lessons, exercises, and emails they would be willing to adopt, and what data they will collect and make available." Researchers then "specify or design experiments that compare the effects of different versions on quantifiable outcomes" and "Explore algorithms for maximizing particular outcomes by choosing alternative versions of a MOOClet based on the input variables available." This division of labor preserves instructor editorial control while enabling research within live courses.
