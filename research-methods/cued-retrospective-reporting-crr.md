## Description
Cued retrospective reporting (CRR) is a retrospective think-aloud protocol based on eye movements: subjects verbally report their thoughts after task completion, using their own previously recorded eye-tracking patterns as memory cues. The article argues the method's key advantage is that "it does not interfere with task performance", avoiding divided attention or altered viewing patterns, and notes retrospective reporting is particularly accurate for short tasks of 5-10 seconds, such as interpreting an ECG. In this study the method is applied to a cardiology expert's ECG interpretation to create learning videos for medical students.
