Bespalko's four-level taxonomy of tests for assessing knowledge assimilation¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article adopts V.P. Bespalko's technique, under which, as it prints, "there are tests of four levels": Level 1 tests for identification, distinction and classification of studied objects (recognition with a hint); Level 2 tests of reproduction without hints (substitution, constructive, and standard-task tests); Level 3 tests of non-standard heuristic tasks, including transformation of acquired data and spatial-creative thinking; Level 4 tests of creative abilities and research opportunities with information new to the branch of science. The authors used Levels 1 and 2 as the basis for their created test tasks, checking assimilation at the level of acquaintance with and reproduction of the studied material.
