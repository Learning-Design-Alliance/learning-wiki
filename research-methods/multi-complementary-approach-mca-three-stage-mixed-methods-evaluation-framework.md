## Description
The MCA is the article's own methodological framework for combining quantitative and qualitative evidence under a single roof, grounded in constructivist learning theory and the Gestalt holistic approach. It "consists of three basic stages, which allow access to pre-complementary, post-complementary and complementary information": a literature stage (meta-analysis plus meta-thematic analysis), an application stage (an experimental implementation plus participant interviews), and a synthesis stage combining both. The authors present MCA as distinct from standard mixed-methods approaches because single data sources are deemed insufficient and results are gathered "under a single roof".
