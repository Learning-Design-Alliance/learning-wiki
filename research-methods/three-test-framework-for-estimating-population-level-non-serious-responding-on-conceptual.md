# Three-test framework for estimating population-level non-serious responding on conceptual inventory surveys

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article develops three seriousness tests that estimate 'the percent of students in a sample who did not take that RBAI seriously.' The Pattern Recognition Test searches answer sheets for non-random patterns such as runs of a single letter; the Uncommon Answers Test counts selections of rarely chosen answers on nine identified questions per instrument; the Easy Questions Test flags students who answer all four easiest questions incorrectly. The framework estimates non-seriousness for a population, not individuals.
