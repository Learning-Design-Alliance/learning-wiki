# Smartwatch-based wrist tremor assessment for surgical simulator training

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A wrist-tremor measurement approach in which an Apple Watch worn over the surgical glove records three-axis wrist acceleration at 100 Hz during simulated procedures. Data are band-pass filtered (0.1–20 Hz) and processed in MATLAB to compute frequency-domain features — "peak power", "peak frequency", "total power", and the voluntary-to-bandwidth power ratio — plus root-mean-square jerk as the time-domain feature. The article applies it to assess training effects in neurosurgical simulator training.
