Virtual comparison group construction via database matching¶

Research Method · All research methods Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The VCG approach creates an equivalent virtual comparison for each treatment student: each treatment student was matched with up to 51 real students from NWEA's national MAP test database based on school locale, school-level FRL percentage, test-taking dates, grade level, and pretest scores; aggregated values were treated as a single virtual comparison student. Each virtual student received a comparison criteria level from 0 to 8, with smaller values indicating a closer match. Approximately 89 percent of virtual comparison students were within level 0 to 2.

Accounts¶

  • Virtual comparison group (VCG) construction via NWEA's matching algorithm: The VCG approach creates an equivalent virtual comparison for each treatment student: each treatment student was matched with up to 51 real students from NWEA's national MAP test database based on school locale, school-level FRL percentage, test-taking dates, grade level, and pretest scores; aggregated values were treated as a single virtual comparison student. Each virtual student received a comparison criteria level from 0 to 8, with smaller values indicating a closer match. Approximately 89 percent of virtual comparison students were within level 0 to 2. (Huang et al. (2025))
  • CREDO Virtual Control Record (VCR) methodology: The Virtual Control Record method builds a counterfactual "virtual twin" for each charter student by averaging the subsequent-year test scores of up to seven matched traditional public school students from feeder schools. Matches are made on grade level, gender, race/ethnicity, poverty status, English language learner status, special education status, and prior test scores. In this study, virtual matches were created for 79 percent of tested charter observations in reading and 62 percent in math, and matching was done period by period to meet WWC Version 4.0 baseline equivalence criteria. (Charter School Performance in New Mexico (2019))
  • Matched-comparison evaluation design for the Kauffman School impact study: The evaluation identified a group of students in other Kansas City district and charter schools who had similar demographic characteristics and achievement at the end of 4th grade, then compared their outcomes to Kauffman students in grades 5 through 10. The report describes this as the method "to measure the impact of the Kauffman School on its students." This matched-comparison design supports the reported achievement and attendance impact findings. (Matthew Johnson (2018))
  • Virtual Control Record (VCR) matched-comparison methodology for charter growth evaluation: The analysis evaluates one-year academic growth using state assessment scores, where "1 growth period requires 2 years of data" and four growth periods are possible. Charter students are matched to feeder and TPS students "on all demographics and baseline achievement" via Virtual Control Records, with match rates of 70% in reading and 68% in math. Sample sizes per growth period range from 1,933 to 2,506 students per subject across 2014-15 to 2017-18. (Charter School Performance in Rhode Island (2023))
  • Virtual Control Record (VCR) matched-comparison methodology: CREDO's quasi-experimental design constructs a "virtual twin" for each charter student from records of students in feeder traditional public schools, matching demographic and academic characteristics so the twin differs "only on a single factor: attending a charter school." The VCR produces the expected score the charter student would have realized in TPS. In this study, virtual matches were possible for 87 percent of tested charter observations in reading and 88 percent in math, and matches were newly developed for every growth period to conform to WWC Procedures Handbook Version 4.0 baseline equivalence criteria. (CREDO et al. (2020))
