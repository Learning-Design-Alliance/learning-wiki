# Three-strategy taxonomy for modelling student knowledge in Simulated Students

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review organises knowledge modelling for LLM-based Simulated Students into three strategies. In direct prompt-based simulation, "Knowledge levels, common mistakes, or learning styles are directly defined within the LLM prompt", which is easy and customisable but risks overly idealised behaviour. Knowledge tracing tracks mastery of knowledge components over time with models such as DKT, DKVMN, AKT, or LLM-KT, offering strong prediction and real-time adaptation at the cost of technical complexity and data needs. Knowledge graphs and heuristics define the knowledge state with structured representations and explicit rules, giving high interpretability but requiring extensive manual curation. The review uses this taxonomy to structure its analysis of how simulated agents acquire, forget, and apply knowledge.
