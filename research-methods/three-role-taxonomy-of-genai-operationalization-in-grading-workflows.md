# Three-role taxonomy of GenAI operationalization in grading workflows

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review organizes how GenAI is operationalized in assessment workflows by the role the model plays. In most studies AI acted as "an automatic grader, independently assigning points or grades to student responses with minimal human involvement"; in others it both assigned a score and generated feedback on strengths and weaknesses; and in co-grading it suggested a grade the educator could verify. The taxonomy is paired with a task-type distribution (essays in 16 studies, short written answers in 11) and a prompting characterization: GenAI was typically guided using rubric- or exemplar-based prompts, with 32 studies including a scoring rubric or sample correct answer.
