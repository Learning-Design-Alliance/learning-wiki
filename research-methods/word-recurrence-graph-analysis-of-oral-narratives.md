# Word-recurrence graph analysis of oral narratives

## Description
The article represents transcribed oral narratives as directed graphs in which "each node represents a word, and each directed edge represents the temporal order of consecutive words." Three connectedness attributes are computed: repeated edges (RE), largest connected component (LCC), and largest strongly connected component (LSC), using a 30-word moving window to control verbosity. The graphs "are not based on meaning but on recurrence patterns," which the authors argue lets them "reveal patterns of connectedness from the earliest stages of language acquisition because they do not rely on meaning or linguistic rules." Developmentally, RE decreases while LCC and LSC increase as children advance in schooling.
