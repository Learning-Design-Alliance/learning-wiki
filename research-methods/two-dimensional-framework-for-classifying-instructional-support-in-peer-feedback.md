# Two-dimensional framework for classifying instructional support in peer-feedback

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q4` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes two orthogonal ways to differentiate instructional support in peer-feedback scenarios. The first distinguishes support by the phase in which it is presented — feedback provision vs. feedback reception — within the five-phase peer-feedback process (design, task performance, provision, reception, evaluation). The second distinguishes support by specificity: content-specific support focuses on "the application of specific knowledge in a selected subject area", whereas generic support assists students independently of content, e.g. prompting feed up, feed back, and feed forward. The meta-analysis applies both categorizations to estimate differential effects.
