# Automated multimodal assessment of attention-related processes during learning

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The dissertation organizes automated attention assessment around four data modalities — video, eye tracking, physiology, and interaction log files — from which attention indicators are extracted and combined with machine learning for prediction of attentional states. It positions this as "a more objective, continuous, and scalable assessment than traditional methods such as self-reports or observations". Video yields facial action units, gaze direction, head and body pose; eye tracking yields fixations, saccades, pupillometry, blinks, AOI and vergence; physiology yields EDA, heart rate, temperature and EEG; log files yield keystrokes, mouse tracking and time spent.
