# Four-category taxonomy of classroom management strategy foci: teacher, student behavior, social-emotional development, and teacher–student relationships

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q4` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The meta-analysis organizes classroom management programs by coding each included intervention for the presence or absence of four categories of strategies. As the article states, "Programs were coded for the presence/absence of four categories of strategies: focusing on the teacher, on student behavior, on students’ social-emotional development, and on teacher–student relationships." This taxonomy serves as the organizing framework for the moderator analyses of which strategy foci drive intervention effectiveness in primary education.
