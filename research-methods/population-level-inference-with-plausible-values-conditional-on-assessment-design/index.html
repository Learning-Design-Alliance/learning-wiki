Population-level inference with plausible values conditional on assessment design¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article's central recommendation: with large-scale assessment data, 'inferences are only possible at the population-level—and then only when framed as conditional on the assessment design and assumptions—not at the level of individual students'. Findings can inform system-level policy decisions but not classroom-level instructional decisions about individual students.
