Wilson's four building blocks of measure development¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Wilson's (2005) framework comprises four building blocks: articulating latent constructs in a construct map, designing items as real-world situations where the construct is manifested, defining an outcome space of possible data profiles, and a measurement model translating scored responses to construct locations. The author applies it to a fidelity measure, noting it "treats validity as an ongoing, iterative process of construct conceptualization, operationalization, and measurement modeling." The framework had not previously been used for fidelity measures.
