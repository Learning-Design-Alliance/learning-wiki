# Twelve-component framework for measuring formative-assessment fidelity of implementation

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Applying FOI, the authors propose twelve critical components of formative assessment in two categories: structural (learning objectives, formative assessment prompts, evidence of student understanding, feedback, skills for self-regulated learning, personal pedagogical content knowledge, prior knowledge) and instructional (reveal student understanding, personal pedagogical knowledge and skills, diagnosis of in-progress learning, generate feedback, recognize and respond to feedback). The structural group reflects composition and organization of the intervention; the instructional group reflects "how instructors and students behave and interact with one another." The set is a hypothesis for iterative refinement, not a validated measure.
