# School-level randomization for multilevel intervention evaluations

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that studies measuring impact at multiple levels consider school-level randomization even though it is less efficient for teacher-level effects. Contamination risk and the need to measure student effects drive this: "these concerns, together with the need to measure impact on students, suggest that it may be beneﬁcial for such studies to consider randomization at t he school level". Simulations with contamination showed within-school and completely randomized designs can become less efficient than randomize-by-school.
