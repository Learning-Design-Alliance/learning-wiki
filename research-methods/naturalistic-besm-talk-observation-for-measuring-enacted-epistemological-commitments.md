## Description
Because beliefs surveys are administered at semester boundaries and aggregated, the article recommends BESM talk as an alternative measure accessible during teaching: 'BESM talk, by virtue of being embedded in natural settings, is an alternative measure that is accessible in the moment of teaching.' Classroom video of student discourse suffices, and no researcher need pull students from their settings. The authors contend that looking for and valuing students' BESM talk is valuable for both researchers and instructors interested in whether and how students think like physicists.
