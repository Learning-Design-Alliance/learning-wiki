## Description
Figure 1 of the article presents "the four main types of observed interactions that students exhibited while working in groups": independent work (I), semi-collaborative effort with one passive member (S), fully collaborative effort (C), and a dominant member directing the others (D). Students did not necessarily remain in one form but groups typically used a predominant dynamic. The pattern is used to classify each group's predominant dynamic for analysis.
