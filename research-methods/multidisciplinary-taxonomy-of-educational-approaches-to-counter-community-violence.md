# Multidisciplinary taxonomy of educational approaches to counter community violence

## Description
The review establishes a multidisciplinary taxonomy that organizes the broad range of educational interventions to counter community violence into distinguishable approaches. It was built, in the authors' words, by a "critical analysis of distinctions in perspectives, objectives, and strategies", so approaches are differentiated by what they take the causes of violence to be, what objectives they pursue, and what strategies they deploy. The taxonomy serves as the organizing framework for the paper's coherent overview of current educational responses and for identifying which kinds of approaches are underrepresented.
