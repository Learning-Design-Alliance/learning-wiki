- **Corpus-based keyword detection methodology using Zotero, AntConc, and JASP**: The study employed a corpus-based detection methodology in which article data was collected via the citation manager Zotero, exported as RefWorks Tagged .txt files, and analyzed using the concordancer AntConc for keyword and cluster/n-gram frequency. Statistical analysis was then performed in JASP using contingency tables. The authors state the method "is composed of storing variable data in .txt files and analyzing variables over the concordancer." (Ayan et al. (2017))
- **Five thematic clusters structure the research landscape of simulation-based critical care education**: Keyword cluster analysis of the SBME-in-critical-care literature organises the field into five thematic areas: Simulation and Outcomes in Acute Care; Competence and Curriculum Development; Patient Safety and Teamwork; Skill Acquisition and Clinical Training; and Education, Confidence and Perceptions. The clusters were derived from a co-occurrence network of 70 keywords with at least five occurrences, and each cluster groups keywords reflecting a distinct research emphasis, from "acute care and resuscitation" outcomes to learner "confidence" and "perceptions". (Li Lang et al. (2026))
- **Bibliometric mapping as a data-driven foundation for curriculum innovation and policy development in stomatology education**: The article presents evaluative bibliometrics as a quantitative science that assesses research performance to identify influential works, complementing qualitative literature reviews. It argues that "Bibliometric analysis provides a data-driven foundation for curriculum innovation and policy development by identifying emerging trends, evaluating influential studies, and uncovering knowledge gaps". The framework combines CiteSpace social network analysis (co-citation clustering, burst detection, centrality) with Bibliometrix thematic and historical citation mapping. (Zou Ziang (2025))
