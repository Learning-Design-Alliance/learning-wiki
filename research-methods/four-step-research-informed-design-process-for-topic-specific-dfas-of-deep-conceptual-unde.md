## Description
The article suggests four steps to ensure the validity, relevance, and in-depth informativity of DFAs: Step 1, selection of relevant topics by identifying basic concepts; Step 2, unpacking selected basic concepts into concept elements, representations, language means, and valid indicators; Step 3, optimizing validity and in-depth informativity of DFA items; and Step 4, enhancing in-depth informativity by exploiting additional digital potentials such as log data and speech-to-text.
