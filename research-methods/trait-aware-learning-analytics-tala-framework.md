# Trait-Aware Learning Analytics (TALA) framework

## Description
TALA is a proposed framing of learning analytics in which relatively stable dispositional constructs (e.g., conscientiousness, goal orientation) are used not merely as predictive features but as moderators that explain for whom analytics and interventions work, and as design resources informing pacing, framing, and timing of support. The article states that "TALA models states at the interaction grain and traits at the learner grain (separating within- and between-person variance), and tests preregistered trait× predictor or trait× intervention interactions." The framework is mapped onto the LA cycle stages (learner, data, analytics, intervention) with design decisions at each stage, and illustrated through three case studies.
