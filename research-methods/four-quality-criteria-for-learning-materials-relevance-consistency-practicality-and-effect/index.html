Four quality criteria for learning materials: relevance, consistency, practicality, and effectiveness¶

Research Method · All research methods

Description¶

The article adopts Plomp and Nieven's (2007) four points about the quality of learning materials: "relevance, consistency, practicality, and effectiveness". Relevance relates to the validity of the content; consistency refers to the validity of the construction; practicality is associated with the users of the products, here lecturers and students; and effectiveness refers to the achievement of expected outcomes. The article's preliminary studies addressed relevance, consistency and practicality, finding the products highly relevant, consistent, and practical, while the present study addressed effectiveness.
