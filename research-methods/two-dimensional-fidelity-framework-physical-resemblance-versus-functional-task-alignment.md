# Two-dimensional fidelity framework: physical resemblance versus functional task alignment

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article adopts Hamstra et al.'s stratification of simulation fidelity into two dimensions: "physical resemblance – characterized by the tactile, visual, auditory and olfactory appearance of the simulator relative to the simulation concept in mind" and "functional task alignment – the simulator's functional property in alignment with the learning objectives at hand". This distinction underpins the study's argument that low-fidelity basic manikins can achieve functional alignment (e.g. compression rhythm mastery in CPR) at low cost, while VPS supplies clinical information and haemodynamics that a high-fidelity manikin would otherwise relay.
