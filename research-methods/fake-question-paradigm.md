## Description
The Fake Question Paradigm is an objective behavioral measure of cheating in real exams. "This paradigm involved adding researcher-prepared fill-in-the-blank target questions to the exam questions prepared by the professors"; the target questions appear course-related but test fictional concepts with fake answers unavailable online or in books. The answer key is posted to the class portal after the exam starts, so "any correct answer would indicate cheating and the number of correct answers would measure the extent of cheating." Fill-in-the-blank format minimizes guessing, and different target questions per section prevent answer sharing.
