# Goal-by-Environment framework for classifying simulated student systems

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes organizing the simulated-student design space along two dimensions: behavioral goals (performance, learning, human aspects) and environment (subject domain, learner population, interaction modality). Its purpose is comparability: "two systems can both be called “simulated students” yet be incommensurate", so making the dimensions explicit prevents overclaiming and aligns evaluation with the fidelity notion actually claimed for a given setting.
