## Description
The method of orbits is a visual-analysis technique in which each subject's binary multivariate longitudinal record is represented as a two-dimensional orbit: variables are ordered by frequency of change, answers are swapped rightward at each time step a value changes, and the sequence of states is connected by edges. The article states it is "a simple way of visualizing such data but useful in handling data sets with large number of variables and subjects". The authors apply it to six binary gender and distinction variables over three semesters, with red edges marking moves to an "unfitter" state and green edges to a "fitter" state.
