# Seven-step recursive ANT analysis of dialogic and non-dialogic classroom talk networks

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Applying ANT to classroom talk, the article defines networks of human and non-human actors (texts, boards, instructions) that constitute the socio-material structure of talk. A network is dialogic when no human actor is subordinated: "We call such a network, in which all the human actors have agency, and no actor, human or non-human subordinates them, as dialogic". Conversely, "We define such a network in which some human actors are subordinated as a non-dialogic network". Roles are not predetermined but translated in interactions, and networks can be blackboxed.
