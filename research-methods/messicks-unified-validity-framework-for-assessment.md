# Messick's unified validity framework for assessment

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The commentary grounds its critique in Messick's unified validity framework, which "conceptualizes validity as integrated sources of evidence encompassing construct representation, sources of construct-irrelevant variance and the social consequences of score use." Within this framework, speededness represents a systematic source of construct-irrelevant variance targeting response process and consequential validity, undermining equity without enhancing inference about clinical competence. The framework is used to argue that time limits reduce assessment validity while adding inequity.
