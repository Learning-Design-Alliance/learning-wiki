## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participating teachers, university teachers, and a researcher cooperate in planning, testing, and analyzing lessons
- Teachers identify critical aspects of the learning object to make visible for pupils
#### Constraints
- The article states learning study is not as strict as an experiment and variables are not controlled, so causal conclusions are not possible

### Target Learners
- primary and secondary school pupils
- teachers and student teachers

### Target Learning Objectives
- subject-matter learning (e.g., subtracting fractions from wholes)
- teachers' professional and research knowledge development
