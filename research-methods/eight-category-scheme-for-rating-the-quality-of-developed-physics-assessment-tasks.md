# Eight-category scheme for rating the quality of developed physics assessment tasks

## Description
The study rated each developed task on "speciﬁcity (scale: 0=does not apply; 1=applies), clarity (scale 0/1), correctness (scale 0/1)" — within a set of eight categories that also included not misleading, adequate difficulty, context, relevance to the target concept, and overall quality. Two independent raters rated every task, showing on average "moderate agreement between the two raters (κ = 0.55)", with disagreements resolved through discussion.
