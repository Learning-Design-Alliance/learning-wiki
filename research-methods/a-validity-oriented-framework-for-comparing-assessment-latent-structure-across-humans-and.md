# A validity-oriented framework for comparing assessment latent structure across humans and LLMs

## Description
The article introduces a methodological framework that treats similarity of latent factor structure as a necessary, though not sufficient, condition for transferring score interpretations from humans to LLMs. It combines exploratory factor analysis, factor congruence with Hungarian-algorithm factor matching, and repeated resampling against a human-human baseline. As the authors state, it "introduces a validity-oriented framework for comparing the latent structure of assessments across humans and LLMs", demonstrated through two case studies showing instruments "may capture substantially different constructs in humans and LLMs".
