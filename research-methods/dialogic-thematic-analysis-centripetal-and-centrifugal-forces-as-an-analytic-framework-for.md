# Dialogic thematic analysis: centripetal and centrifugal forces as an analytic framework for meaning-making

## Description
The article develops a dialogic-thematic analysis procedure, grounded in Bakhtinian concepts, for mapping meaning-making in blended collaborative settings. The analyst treats "centripetal forces as convergent and related to the permanence of values, ideologies, standardisation and cultural beliefs" leading to instrumental learning, while "centrifugal forces are divergent producing decentralisation, polyphonies, openness, novelty, high variability in meaning-making, and agency". Transcripts are organised in vertical turn-taking sequences and then in horizontal units of analysis, with a semantic map charting recurrent themes and a re-contextualisation box synthesising results.
