# R-List randomization procedure for oversubscribed pre-k programs

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A randomization mechanism for evaluating a voluntary pre-k program when demand exceeds capacity. Oversubscribed programs submitted applicant lists, which the research team sorted into random order and returned; "program staff were asked to fill their pre-k seats in the order that children appeared on these randomized lists", with unoffered children becoming the waiting-list control group. Across two cohorts this generated 150 randomized applicant lists, of which 111 active R-Lists with 3,131 VPK-eligible children formed the study base.
