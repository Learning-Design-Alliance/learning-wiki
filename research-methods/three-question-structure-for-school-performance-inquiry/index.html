Three-question structure for school performance inquiry¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The framework structures school performance inquiry around three questions: "1. How are students doing? 2. How much does the school contribute to how students are doing? and 3. What is happening in the school?" Each question maps to a complementary grouping of measures—student outcomes, school impacts on outcomes, and processes driving those impacts—so the three together give a rounded picture of school performance.
