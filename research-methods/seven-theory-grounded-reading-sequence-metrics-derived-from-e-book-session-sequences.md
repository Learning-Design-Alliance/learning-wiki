# Seven theory-grounded reading sequence metrics derived from e-book session sequences

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A proposed set of interpretable metrics computed from temporally encoded e-book reading sessions: N_Jumps, N_Stops, N_Responsive, Sequential, Stickiness, Quickness, and Stableness. Sessions are encoded with the method of Miyazaki et al., with consecutive Next/Previous events recoded as jumps and the medium/long interval threshold adjusted to 120 seconds. The metrics are grounded in reading-strategy theory (scanning, skimming, receptive and responsive reading) and learning approaches; the article reports "The selected metrics explained 20.1% of the variance in final grades" without engagement.
