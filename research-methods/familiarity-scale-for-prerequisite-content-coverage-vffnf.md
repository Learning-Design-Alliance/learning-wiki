# Familiarity scale for prerequisite-content coverage (VF/F/NF)

## Description
The familiarity scale, previously developed by Shaffer et al., classifies downstream-course exam questions as very familiar (VF), familiar (F), or not familiar (NF) relative to prerequisite content, enabling "examine the impact of prerequisites on exam performance on a topic-by-topic basis." In this study, four researchers characterized MLab exam questions using MLec lecture slides, and three instructors characterized them for their own sections, yielding six designations; four researchers agreed identically on 78% of questions and three of four on 95%.
