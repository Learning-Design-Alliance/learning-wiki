# Theory-driven framing of non-content instructor talk via immediacy, self-efficacy, and task values

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
This framework organizes non-content instructor talk in undergraduate lab courses by three motivational theoretical frames rather than by the atheoretical, grounded categories of prior work. Content analysis of 182 recordings "identified 14 unique types of instructor talk aligned with our theoretical frames of immediacy, self-efficacy, and task values": talk fostering closeness with the instructor (immediacy), building confidence in scientific abilities (self-efficacy), and promoting sense of worth in the work (task values). The framing is intended to set the stage for testing links between talk and student motivational outcomes.
