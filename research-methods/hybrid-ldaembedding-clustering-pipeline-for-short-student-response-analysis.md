# Hybrid LDA–embedding clustering pipeline for short student-response analysis

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A portable text-analysis pipeline combining Latent Dirichlet Allocation (k=8 topics on unigrams/bigrams) with sentence embeddings from the small multilingual encoder EmbeddingGemma-300M, projected via UMAP (cosine metric) and clustered with HDBSCAN. The article states this combination "leverages the transparency of bag-of-words topics and the semantic richness of small language model (SLM) embeddings", with the SLM layer serving as a robustness check against tokenization artifacts. It is designed for resource-constrained educational settings and Spanish-language (ES-PE/ES-LA) short responses.
