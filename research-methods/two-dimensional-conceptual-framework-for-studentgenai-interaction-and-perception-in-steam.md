## Description
The article proposes a conceptual framework with two dimensions: student–GenAI interaction and students' perceptions of GenAI use. The interaction dimension examines interaction "from three perspectives: the 'Student–GenAI' perspective, the 'Student' perspective, and the 'GenAI' perspective", comprising four sub-dimensions: interaction content, student standpoint, prompt type, and prompt purpose. The perception dimension covers satisfaction, trust level, and GenAI utility. The framework maintains interpretation at the behavioral level because the same interaction behavior may reflect different cognitive processes across contexts.
