## Description
The article argues that inconsistent prior results for expectancy theory stem partly from methodological deviations: between-person designs when Vroom conceived motivation as within-person, researcher-provided rather than participant-generated second-level outcomes, and valence scales with only positive anchors. Assessing motivation–performance links using within-person multilevel modeling, participant-generated outcomes, and bidirectional scales enabled a more appropriate test that revealed the simplified force model's validity.
