## Description
Normalized gain (g) is a change metric that standardizes score gains by dividing the change in scores by the maximum change that could occur, calculated as (posttest mean minus pretest mean) divided by (100% minus pretest mean). Hake adopted this standardizing coefficient because it accounted for the smaller shift in means that could occur in courses with higher pretest means, arguing g was not correlated with pretest mean. Hake used g to define high (g > 0.7), medium (0.7 > g > 0.3), and low (g < 0.3) courses, and it became the most common gain measurement in physics education research and other DBER fields.
