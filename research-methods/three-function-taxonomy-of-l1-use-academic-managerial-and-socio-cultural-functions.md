# Three-function taxonomy of L1 use: academic, managerial, and socio-cultural functions

## Description
The article groups the purposes for which L1 is used or advocated in L2 classrooms into three themes: "academic functions", "managerial functions", and "socio-cultural functions". Academic functions include explaining aspects of the target language, facilitating and checking comprehension, translating, eliciting, and question-and-answer exchanges. Managerial functions include giving instructions, classroom management, feedback, and motivation. Socio-cultural functions include praising, joking, establishing rapport, and socialization. The taxonomy was derived from studies providing categories and classifications of L1 purposes.
