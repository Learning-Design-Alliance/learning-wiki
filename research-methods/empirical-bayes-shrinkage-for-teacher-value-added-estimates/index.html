Empirical Bayes shrinkage for teacher value-added estimates¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 2 studies (2 associational), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Empirical Bayes shrinkage is described as "an approach commonly used in implementing teacher accountability systems" that adjusts value-added estimates. The paper studies how it affects estimates for teachers of students with hard-to-predict achievement levels, finding it increases precision and reduces absolute estimate values without significantly changing extreme-rating probabilities.

Claims¶

  • Stabilization makes larger adjustments to scores from smaller student groups and to more extreme scores, as expected [+W]
  • Empirical Bayes shrinkage increases the precision of value-added estimates and reduces their absolute value for teachers of hard-to-predict students [+W]
  • Teachers of students with hard-to-predict achievement levels tend to have less precise value-added estimates than teachers of other students [~W]
  • Shrinkage has no statistically significant effect on the relative probability that teachers of hard-to-predict students receive extreme value-added ratings [~W]
