# Dimensionless squared jerk (DSJ) motion-tracking method for psychomotor skill assessment

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
DSJ is a normalised smoothness measure of hand movement, "a derivative of acceleration with respect to time and distance while remaining independent of spurious peaks and dimension". In this study a 6-DOF Aurora electromagnetic sensor on a 3D-printed holder tracked the probe while participants performed BPD, AC and FL biometry on a SPACE-FAN ST phantom; DSJ was computed with the Hogan and Sternad formula using translation only. The setup used clinical ultrasound machines, making it reproducible by other groups.
