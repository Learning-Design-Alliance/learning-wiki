# Topping's 17-parameter typology of peer assessment

## Description
The article defines peer assessment as "an arrangement in which individuals consider the amount, level, value, worth, quality, or success of the products or outcomes of learning of peers of similar status", restricted to students of similar degree status in higher education. It then develops a typology of 17 parameters of variation (Table 1), including curriculum area, objectives, formative/summative focus, product, relation to staff assessment, directionality, privacy, contact, year, ability, constellations, place, time, requirement and reward. The typology was developed "from immersion in this literature" and warns that sweeping general conclusions are unlikely to be meaningful.
