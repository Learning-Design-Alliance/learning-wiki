# Semiotic Problem Framing (SPF) framework

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
SPF extends the two-dimensional problem-framing plane of Nguyen et al. and Bing & Redish, defined by physics–mathematics and conceptual–algorithmic axes, by adding a z-axis for semiotic resources. As the article states, "The novelty of the SPF framework lies in the addition of a z -axis, which introduces linguistic and representational semiotic resources". The intersections generate new frames — Pictorial Language, Visual Language, Physical Wording, Conceptual Metaphorical Understanding, Mathematical Wording, Mathematical Meaning, and Phenomenological Language — alongside the original CP, AP, AM, and CM frames. The framework is offered as both an analytical tool for researchers and a didactical instrument for learners and teachers.
