## Description
The article introduces a shared coding rubric applied identically to interaction logs and think-aloud speech. "Dimension 1, operational process category, answered what metacognitive function was observable and assigned planning, monitoring, or regulation. Dimension 2, depth of explicit metacognitive evidence, answered how explicitly and integratively the unit articulated a goal, criterion, procedure, evaluation, or transfer and assigned implicit, awareness, strategic, or reflective." Depth is an ordinal property of the evidence in a semantic unit, not a taxonomy of processes or a measure of learner proficiency, and boundary rules govern ambiguous cases such as criterion-free versus criterion-tied judgments.
