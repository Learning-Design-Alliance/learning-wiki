# Durkin's eight-category scheme for classifying teachers' comprehension-related behavior

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article develops a coding scheme distinguishing comprehension instruction—"Teacher does/says something to help children understand or work out the meaning of more than a single, isolated word"—from seven related categories: application, assignment, help with assignment, review of instruction, preparation for reading, assessment, and prediction. The definition was built after a literature search found no usable definition, and it deliberately excludes single-word work such as phonics. It was applied identically to reading and social studies periods so time use could be compared.
