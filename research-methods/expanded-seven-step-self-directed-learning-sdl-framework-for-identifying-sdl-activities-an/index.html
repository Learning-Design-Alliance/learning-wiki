Expanded seven-step self-directed learning (SDL) framework for identifying SDL activities and assessment methods¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The authors developed an SDL framework from a literature review of accreditation standards and guidelines across health professions education programs. It "comprises seven steps, each step of which involves various components or activities", and was used to develop research objectives, identify SDL components in included studies, and count the number of steps in reported SDL interventions. The authors state these steps "may serve as a framework for identifying SDL activities and/or SDL interventions discussed in research articles and assessment methods employed for studying outcomes of SDL interventions". It was built because there is no agreed-upon standard defining SDL, how it should be taught, or how it should be assessed.
