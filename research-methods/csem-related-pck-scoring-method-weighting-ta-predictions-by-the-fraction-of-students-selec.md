# CSEM-related PCK scoring method weighting TA predictions by the fraction of students selecting each distractor

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article develops a quantitative measure of TA knowledge of student difficulties: for each CSEM item, a TA who selects a particular incorrect answer as the MCI receives a PCK score equal to the fraction of students who selected that choice, summed across items into a CSEM-related PCK score. The weighting was chosen because "the more prevalent a student difficulty is, the more important it is for a TA to be aware of it." The method also handles items with two common alternate conceptions, e.g., Q29 where 26% selected A and 23% selected B.
