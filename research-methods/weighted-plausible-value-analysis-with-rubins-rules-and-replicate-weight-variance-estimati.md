## Description
The article recommends that secondary analysts of PVs weight student observations to restore population representativeness, repeat each analysis separately across all PVs (usually 5 to 10), and aggregate results using Rubin's rules with replicate-weight variance estimation. Without these steps, analysts 'risk underestimating the amount of uncertainty in their findings', which can lead to overstated claims.
