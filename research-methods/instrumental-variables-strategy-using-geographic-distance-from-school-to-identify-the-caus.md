## Description
The article employs a two-stage least squares design in which "the geographical distance that a student lives from school" serves as an exogenous instrument for days present. The first stage regresses attendance on distance and covariates; the second stage regresses achievement on fitted attendance. The design is motivated by time-variant unobservables, such as motivation, that fixed effects and value-added models cannot remove.
