# EVER framework for judging EdTech evidence

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
EVER organizes evidence quality along four aspects. Methodological quality asks whether methods are appropriately executed, described and justified. Outcome strength concerns sizable impact or predictive value, quantified via effect size or sensitivity/specificity. Generalizability is the extension of findings from one sample to the target population, requiring representativeness. Ethics and transparency ensure ethical design, users' well-being, culturally-responsive approaches and transparent data use.
