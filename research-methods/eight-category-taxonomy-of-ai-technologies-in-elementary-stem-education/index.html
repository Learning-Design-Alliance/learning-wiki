Eight-category taxonomy of AI technologies in elementary STEM education¶

Description¶

The review organizes the field into eight AI technology categories identified through preliminary scoping: intelligent tutoring systems and conversational AI, automated assessment and feedback, learning analytics and predictive modeling, computer vision for engagement monitoring, multimodal sensing and biometric analysis, adaptive content generation, educational robots and embodied AI, and extended reality with AI enhancement. Each category is defined by its primary functions, evidence level, and main limitations in Table 1, from "Personalized instruction, adaptive hints, dialogue-based scaffolding" for tutoring systems to "Immersive environments, spatial learning, adaptive simulations" for XR. The taxonomy structures the review's synthesis, distribution analysis, and gap identification.
