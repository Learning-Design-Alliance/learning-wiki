General Diagnostic Model (GDM)¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The General Diagnostic Model (GDM) is a cognitive-diagnosis student model in which learners carry ability vectors with one component per knowledge component and questions carry discrimination vectors and an easiness parameter, combined through a logistic probability of a correct answer. The article states it "choose[s] to calibrate a General Diagnostic Model, because it predicts student performance better than other cognitive diagnosis models", citing its prior work, and assumes the learner's level does not change during the initial question bundle. Estimated ability components can be interpreted as degrees of mastery per knowledge component.
