## Description
The article uses the Consolidated Framework for Implementation Research as an analytical lens for understanding why evidence-based practices such as HBB are difficult to implement in routine care. It states that CFIR "offer[s] a lens through which implementers can identify areas needing attention", covering characteristics of the implemented method, the roles and attributes of targeted individuals, the setting, and the implementation process. In this study, challenges were identified across all CFIR domains and were not isolated to HBB itself, spanning referral systems, working culture, recognition, and psychological and physical safety.
