## Description
The article adapts Scott et al.'s framework, which the authors say defines four classes of communicative approach using two dimensions: authoritative versus dialogic, and non-interactive versus interactive. Authoritative approaches focus participants on one meaning while dialogic approaches are open to many points of view; non-interactive approaches exclude others' contributions while interactive approaches allow multiple people to contribute. The R-PDOT type-of-engagement codes are located within these four classes, distinguishing lecture, independent work, closed questioning, presentations, small group and large group open discussion.
