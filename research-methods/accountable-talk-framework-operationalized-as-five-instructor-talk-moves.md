# Accountable Talk framework operationalized as five instructor talk moves

## Description
The article operationalizes Accountable Talk theory (O'Connor et al., 2015) as five measurable instructor moves: Adding on, Connecting, Eliciting, Probing, and Revoicing. As the article states, "The talk moves we chose are part of the Accountable Talk Framework that encourages instructors to promote student participation in an equitable way". Each move is defined in the annotation guide (e.g., Revoicing restates students' ideas anticipating their intent) and labelled with printed distributions from Adding on at 25% to Connecting at 4%.

Within this, talk moves are linguistic acts intended to facilitate dialogue; the study operationalizes six tutor talk moves (e.g., Keeping Students Together, Restating, Press for Accuracy, Press for Reasoning, Revoicing) and measures them automatically with a fine-tuned RoBERTa model (macro F1 0.765).
