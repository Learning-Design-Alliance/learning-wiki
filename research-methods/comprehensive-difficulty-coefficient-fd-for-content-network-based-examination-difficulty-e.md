# Comprehensive difficulty coefficient (Fd) for content-network-based examination difficulty estimation

## Description
Fd is a proposed metric defined as the product of "average degree, network density, average clustering coefficient, and network transitivity", i.e., Fd = <k> × ρ × T × <c>. It reflects both breadth and depth of questions and originates solely from the content of examination papers, making it independent of the examination creator and respondent. A larger value indicates more nodes, stronger clustering and denser connections, i.e., a more difficult examination.
