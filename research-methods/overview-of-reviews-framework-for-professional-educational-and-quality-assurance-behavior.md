## Description
The article organizes the evidence on changing professional behavior by systematically identifying, appraising, and synthesizing systematic reviews rather than primary studies. It covered "systematic reviews of professional behavior change interventions published between 1966 and 1998" and identified "Forty-one reviews... covering a wide range of interventions and behaviors." This review-of-reviews structure maps which intervention families (passive dissemination, educational outreach, reminders, multifaceted approaches) have what kind of evidence, making the literature accessible to decision makers.
