# Five learner-state features operationalized at the COI level from eye-tracking metrics

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Drawing on a literature review and instructor interviews, the article defines five interpretable learner-state features computed per COI: Attention ("allocating cognitive resources to the current task while suppressing task-irrelevant thoughts"), Cognitive Load, Interest, Preference, and Synchronicity. The first three characterize the learner's internal response to a single concept (intra-concept engagement); the latter two capture the match between learner behavior and instructional design intent and pacing (inter-concept integration). Each is operationalized with specific gaze metrics: mean peak saccade velocity for Attention, baseline-corrected mean pupil diameter for Cognitive Load, blink suppression for Interest, dwell-time surplus for Preference, and with-me-ness-style temporal alignment for Synchronicity.
