# A taxonomy classifying authentic CSCL learning environments into simulation and hybrid environments with five descriptors

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This conceptualization, from Hod and Sagy, categorizes and compares authentic learning scenarios. It builds on a distinction between environments that "match or approximate a learning environment to an authentic culture" and environments where students interact with practitioners in the professional setting itself. The editorial reports it offers a framework for classifying authentic learning environments that can be used to assess existing fieldwork and guide future research.
