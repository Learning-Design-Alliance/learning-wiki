## Description
Critical Quantitative Intersectionality is a framework for applying critical theory and intersectionality, which have been used primarily in qualitative studies, to quantitative research. It reframes questions from ranking the importance of race, gender, or class to examining unique constellations of social location as categories of experience, and calls on researchers to question the models, measures, and analytic practices of quantitative research to better describe the experiences of those who have not been adequately represented. This article applies CQI by using large-scale data and advocative terminology to model intersecting race and gender effects in physics learning.
