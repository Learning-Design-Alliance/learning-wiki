## Description
The article proposes a "topic relation graph descriptive analyses which are based on the topic modeling" to extract core keywords from open-ended survey answers. It forms a bigram-based Latent Dirichlet Allocation model per question, tunes the number of topics (2 to 10, 10 words each) using coherence scores, then builds a graph whose nodes are unique words in the optimized topics and whose edges link the highest-weighted word of a topic to overlapping words in other topics, exposing associated keyword paths and topic centrality.
