# Prado et al.'s six-category classification of special-relativity teaching proposals

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review classifies the didactic proposals in the literature "in terms of the didactic aspect that is mainly emphasized", defining six categories: proposals of conceptual change, proposals of dissemination, animations, visualization without computation, proposals of geometrical nature, and proposals based on the geometry of Minkowskian spacetime. Each category is populated with named proposals, from constructivist models building on classical physics to applet-based Minkowski-diagram implementations, and the classification serves to position the authors' own Minkowskian proposal.
