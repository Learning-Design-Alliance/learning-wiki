# Cognitive Task Analysis

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
CTA is a collection of interview-based qualitative methods for identifying the cognitive processes and skills required to perform complex tasks in demanding, contextually dependent work domains. The article organizes it around three phases: "(i) knowledge elicitation; (ii) data analysis; (iii) knowledge representation" — interviewing or observing experts to break down task steps, eliciting the cognitive processes used during task completion, and mapping knowledge requirements, cues, strategies, and skills into a digestible format that enhances workplace knowledge transfer. It is presented as complementary to existing managerial cognition methods, with a specific focus on the role of context.
