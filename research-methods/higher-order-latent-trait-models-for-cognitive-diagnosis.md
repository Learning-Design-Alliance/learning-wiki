# Higher-order latent trait models for cognitive diagnosis

## Description
The article names and defines a modeling approach in which higher-order latent traits specify the joint distribution of binary attributes in cognitive diagnosis models, replacing the unrestricted latent class joint distribution. It works by treating attributes as "the specific knowledge required for examination performance, and modeling these attributes as arising from a broadly-defined latent trait resembling the theta of item response models." The approach is presented as "based on a plausible model for the relationship between general aptitude and specific knowledge."
