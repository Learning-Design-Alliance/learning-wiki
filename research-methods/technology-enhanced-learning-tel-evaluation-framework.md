# Technology-enhanced learning (TEL) evaluation framework

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The TEL evaluation framework, developed in the context of medical education and built on the CIPP evaluation model and the Kirkpatrick model, guides both development and evaluation activities for e-learning resources. "The TEL-framework outlines seven areas of evaluation activities: conduct needs analysis and environmental scan; document processes, decisions, and final product, test usability, document key events during implementation and final product, assess participant experience and satisfaction, and assess learning outcomes, and estimate cost, reusability, and sustainability." The article used it to structure the co-development and RCT evaluation of the SCI counselling course, alongside CRe-DEPTH reporting.
