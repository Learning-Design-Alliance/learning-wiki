# Three-layer hierarchical framework: Pellegrino's assessment triangle, Webb's Depth of Knowledge, and the TIMSS cognitive domains

## Description
The article organizes its analysis through three complementary frameworks in a hierarchical relationship: Pellegrino et al.'s assessment triangle (cognition, observation, interpretation) as meta-theory, Webb's Depth of Knowledge as taxonomic theory, and the TIMSS framework as the operational layer. The mapping holds that "DoK Level 1 corresponds to the knowing domain in TIMSS, DoK Level 2 to the applying domain, and DoK Levels 3–4 to the reasoning domain." This structure justifies analyzing TIMSS documents as evidence about cognitive structure and predicts that applying functions as a prerequisite foundation for reasoning.
