Framework for determining the quality of rephrased responses (accuracy × responsiveness)¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A two-dimensional framework the authors propose from their evaluation results for judging the quality of rephrased feedback responses. Accuracy measures whether the rephrased response is a better example of the lesson's target practice; responsiveness measures how selectively words are changed while preserving the trainee's original words and ideas. Responses rated 1 or 2 in accuracy are acceptable; the "optimal rephrased responses should be high in both accuracy and responsiveness (i.e., Excellent area in Fig. 6)", while low-accuracy responses (≤ 0) are undesirable regardless of responsiveness.
