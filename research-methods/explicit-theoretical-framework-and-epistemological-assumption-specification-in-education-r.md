## Description
The review recommends that family medicine education research be explicitly theoretically informed, arguing that articulation of underlying assumptions is needed for researchers to critically engage with study findings. The authors state: "research should be explicitly theoretically informed; this can be achieved by engaging scholars with diverse areas of theoretical expertise." The principle addresses the observed lack of stated research paradigms and epistemological assumptions in the field.
