## Description
The review introduces a classification (its Fig. 1) that distinguishes two families of item difficulty prediction approaches. Expert-driven approaches are qualitative, resting on "an expert consensus that exploits their domain knowledge and experience" and on pre-defined features from educational taxonomies or experts' difficulty perceptions. Machine-driven approaches quantify difficulty using statistical or data-driven prediction models to enhance scalability and minimise human intervention. The review uses this taxonomy to organise the field and to motivate its focus on automatic, text-based, a priori prediction.
