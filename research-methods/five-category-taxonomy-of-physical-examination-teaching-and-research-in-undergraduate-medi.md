# Five-category taxonomy of physical examination teaching and research in undergraduate medical education

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The scoping review developed a content categorization of 117 articles using qualitative content analysis following Mayring. Articles were matched into five categories: "teaching methods" (CAT I), "teaching quality" (CAT II), "performance evaluation and assessment formats" (CAT III), "student views" (CAT IV) and "patient/ standardized patient perspective" (CAT V). Categories were derived inductively from the text material through an iterative six-step process, with inter-rater reliability of 0.88 (n = 103). The taxonomy represents the possible dimensions of assessment for physical examination education research.
