# Fleck & Fitzpatrick's five-level scheme of reflective depth

## Description
This taxonomy grades the depth of written reflection in five ordered levels; the article states "The coding scheme includes 5 levels: description, reflective description, dialogical reflection, transformative reflection and critical reflection". Each higher level adds perspective analysis, internal dialogue, plans for next steps, and finally a wider social, political and historical context. PapagAI trains its reflective-level detection model on this scheme and uses the detected level to select feedback prompts that push reflections toward higher levels.
