# Integrated IRT and knowledge graph modeling framework for quantitatively assessing course knowledge-point difficulty, importance, and necessity

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The article proposes combining item response modeling with knowledge graph modeling to quantify properties of knowledge points from student self-rated difficulty data. IRT places person ability (SEL) and item difficulty on the same logit scale, addressing "which KPs demand additional teaching resources", while the KGM built on partial correlations identifies important and necessary points, addressing "which KPs need to be taught first". The article states "The IRT and KG methods utilized in this study offer distinct perspectives for visualizing hierarchical relationships and correlations among the KPs". The framework was validated on real-world teaching data from a physical therapy course.
