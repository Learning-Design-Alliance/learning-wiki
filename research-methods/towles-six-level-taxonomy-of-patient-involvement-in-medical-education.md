# Towle's six-level taxonomy of patient involvement in medical education

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The review adopts Towle's taxonomy, which "delineates a continuum of patient involvement in medical education across six progressive levels." It runs from patients represented abstractly in case studies, through standardized or volunteer patients with scripted roles, patients sharing personal experiences, patient-teachers involved in instruction and evaluation, patients as equal partners in teaching, assessment, and curriculum development, up to sustained educator roles with participation in institutional governance and policy. The review uses it to distinguish genuine co-teaching from simulated-patient roles in its inclusion criteria.
