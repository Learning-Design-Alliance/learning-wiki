# Fitted metric

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The fitted metric is a parametric model with parameters fitted by nonlinear optimisation (performed in Octave) whose objective is to "predict some target, e.g., the outcome of the final exam". Part scores follow a formula with a maximum point value, an attempt-value fraction, and a constant per-part penalty per failed attempt; loop scores distinguish first-try success from review-loop completion. The article stresses that its purpose "is not to grade students in an obscure manner but to extract useful information from the Stateful data in relation to the actual grading of the course": examining optimal parameter values shows which parts and behaviours best predict exam success.
