## Description
The article recommends that researchers "should avoid discarding trials whenever possible", because removing early trials as practice replaces one stationarity assumption with another unprincipled one and, as the results show, is "actually discarding signal, not noise". Instead, time-sensitive analyses of pilot data can identify timescales of change and what amount of practice would leave participants at reasonably stable performance.
