## Description
The article organizes motivation constructs using Graham and Weiner's three basic questions — "Can I do it?", "Do I want to do it?" and "Why am I doing this task?" — mapping them onto competence and control beliefs, feelings-related and importance-related task value, and intrinsic versus extrinsic reasons for studying, plus attitudes toward school subjects including a future-interest subcategory. Figure 1 provides "a visual overview of the motivation categories used in our meta-analysis". The taxonomy structures which motivation measures were coded and how moderator analyses were run per construct.
