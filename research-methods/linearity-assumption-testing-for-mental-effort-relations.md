## Description
The article recommends that researchers check whether relations involving self-rated mental effort are linear before fitting and interpreting linear models, because "predictions based on linear models may be substantially biased for most of the range of mental effort". It demonstrates a pragmatic approach: fit competing models of increasing polynomial complexity (none, linear, quadratic, cubic, more complex) in two-level mixed-effects models and compare them on R2M, AIC and BIC. The response-time finding is presented as "a clear example of why testing the linearity assumption and reporting the core outcomes of that check is important".
