# Scenario study approach for efficiently interrogating causal contextual moderators of behavioral interventions

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
A three-part experimental method the article proposes and illustrates: (a) craft scenarios that vividly represent candidate moderating contexts based on available observational, survey, and experimental evidence; (b) measure a short-term behavioral outcome known to relate to typical intervention outcomes; and (c) randomly assign participants to scenario conditions to assess "the causal effect of the moderating context on the link between the psychological variable typically targeted by interventions and this short-term outcome". It is positioned as a bridge between initial identification of measured moderators and large, costly multisite field experiments.
