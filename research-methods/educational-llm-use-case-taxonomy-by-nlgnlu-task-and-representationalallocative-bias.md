# Educational LLM use-case taxonomy by NLG/NLU task and representational/allocative bias

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review organizes LLM applications in education into two broad use-case types: natural language generation tasks such as creating lesson plans and assessments, and natural language understanding tasks such as scoring essays or detecting confusion. It then maps these onto the responsible-AI distinction between representational and allocative biases, stating that "The potential biases associated with NLG tasks are mostly representational biases" while NLU tasks carry mostly allocative biases involving differential access to resources or opportunities. Applications combining both task types are susceptible to both bias types.
