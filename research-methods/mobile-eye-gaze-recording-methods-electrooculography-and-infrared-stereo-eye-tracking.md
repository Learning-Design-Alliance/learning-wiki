# Mobile eye-gaze recording methods (electrooculography and infrared stereo eye tracking)

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes two promising methods for recording eye gaze in real-life mobile settings. Electrooculography (EOG) measures potential changes as the eye moves as a cornea-retina dipole; with this technique "it's possible to recognize reading and other eye movements in realistic everyday scenarios". The alternative tracks gaze with infrared light and stereo cameras, "provides potentially higher accuracy but requires more processing power", inferring motion from iris shape and local face features.
