# Radial meta-joint display for integrating five analytical levels of student learning ecologies

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The article presents a final meta-joint display, chosen as a radial display, that integrates findings at five distinct levels: LE core components, MMR pillar building sub-components, learner profile solutions from qualitized data, the LE matrix representing four conceptual zones of learning, and traits of a connected academic curriculum. Learner activity was placed at the centre and curriculum traits at the outer border as a mediating factor. The radial form was chosen to represent the ecological, dynamic process of learning across multiple contexts and to link information while avoiding complexity.
