# Microscopic network measures as indicators of sentence-level integration during reading

## Description
The article frames reading networks with sentences as nodes and transitions between sentences as weighted edges, and proposes four microscopic measures of integration. "Strength refers to the number of edges connected to a node" and reflects how much a sentence has been visited; betweenness identifies sentences lying on shortest paths between others; harmonic centrality (a form of closeness centrality) quantifies proximity to all other sentences; and the local clustering coefficient captures interconnection among a sentence's neighbors. The approach aligns with construction-integration accounts of mental representation building.
