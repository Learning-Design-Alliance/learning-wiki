## Description
The article presents Hatton & Smith's (1995) hierarchy of four levels of reflection in pre-service teachers' journal writing as background for measuring reflective thinking. "The lowest level, descriptive writing, is not reflective at all, but involves a pure description of a situation or an issue." Above it, descriptive reflection gives justifications based on personal judgment and experience; dialogue reflection explores and considers differing reasons; and critical reflection considers broader historical, social and political contexts of reasoning.
