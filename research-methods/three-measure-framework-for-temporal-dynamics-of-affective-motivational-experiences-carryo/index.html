Three-measure framework for temporal dynamics of affective-motivational experiences: carryover, instability, and variability¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article organizes the question of whether affective-motivational experiences remain stable or fluctuate using three complementary dynamic measures. It investigates "the extent to which affective-motivational experiences remained stable or fluctuated from one situation to the next, using carryover (autoregressive effects), instability (rMSSD), and variability (within-person SD) as dyamic measures". Carryover captures how strongly a state persists to the next occasion, rMSSD captures moment-to-moment instability, and within-person SD captures overall variability. The framework is applied to medical students' motivation and emotions during exam preparation and analyzed with DSEM in Mplus.
