## Description
The Executive LLM is a single LLM that generates responses for all AI teammates, has access to the pedagogical rubrics, and is prompted to "create situations that will allow the subject to demonstrate their skill, so that their skill level can be quantitatively inferred". The article frames it as analogous to a computerized adaptive test: whereas traditional CAT varies only item difficulty in a narrow domain, the Executive LLM varies the flow of a group conversation — heightening or softening conflicts, creating project-management needs, or eliciting creative ideas — to maximize information about the test-taker while keeping the interaction naturalistic.
