# Three modeling modes framework for analyzing expert covariational reasoning

## Description
The article proposes three modeling modes, larger patterns of reasoning combining the mathematics mental actions and the reasoning devices. Function Knowing applies a compiled model using physically significant points and MMA 1; Function Choosing uses the general trend between physically significant points to choose among compiled models via MMA 2; Function Generating uses neighborhood analysis and MMA 4 to build a curve from slopes at significant points. The authors report that "physics experts were likely to engage in one of three modes when graphically modeling the relationship between two quantities," and describe the modes as a spectrum from applying models to generating models where none exists.
