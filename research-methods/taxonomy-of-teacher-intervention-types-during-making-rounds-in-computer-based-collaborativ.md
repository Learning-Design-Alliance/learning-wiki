# Taxonomy of teacher intervention types during “making rounds” in computer-based collaborative learning

## Description
The paper organizes its analysis into distinct types of teacher intervention during rounds: ratifying pupils' work, reminding pupils of neglected task aspects, making suggestions, maintaining classroom control, and making whole-class announcements. Through "a close analysis of different types of teacher interventions into pupils working in pairs with a storyboarding tool", each type is illustrated with transcribed video fragments. The taxonomy describes what teachers actually do, not what they are supposed to do.
