# Longitudinal multi-task, multi-stage EEG learning-monitoring framework

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The article establishes a framework for continuous neural monitoring across a full semester rather than short isolated tasks. As the authors state, "we established a longitudinal EEG acquisition and analysis framework within an authentic course-based learning scenario, achieving continuous neural monitoring over an entire semester." It combines time-domain amplitude, spectral (relative PSD), connectivity (PLI, entropy-based information transfer), and machine-learning analyses across three tasks and three course stages to characterize evolving brain dynamics during learning.
