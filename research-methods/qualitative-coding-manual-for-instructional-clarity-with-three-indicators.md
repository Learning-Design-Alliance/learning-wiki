# Qualitative coding manual for instructional clarity with three indicators

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A high-inference rating system was developed, blending deductive and inductive approaches, to code lecturers' instructional clarity from video recordings at three-minute intervals synchronized with ESM signals. "The rating system comprised three items: two positive indicators measuring the amount of detail (depth) and variation (width) in the explanation, and one negative indicator measuring the logical consistency of the argumentation." Two independent raters coded 238 observation intervals; an overall composite rating was abandoned due to low variance of the negative indicator.
