Three-domain validity framework for evaluating surgical simulation models¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review applies a three-domain validity framework to classify hernia repair simulation models. As the review defines it, "Face validity was defined as the physical realism of the simulator", construct validity as the ability to differentiate performance by experience level, and content validity as expert-judged suitability as a teaching modality. Each of the 27 included models is tabulated against these three domains plus educational impact and skill transfer.
