# GPA-ranked systematic assignment for covariate-balanced instructional comparison groups

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because students with higher GPAs tend to achieve higher MDC test scores, the authors ranked all 158 consenting students by GPA and alternately assigned odd-ranked students to the BL group and even-ranked students to the VBL group. This systematic procedure controlled the GPA confound that simple randomization may leave unbalanced, while acknowledging that self-reported learning style and numerical competency are unreliable to stratify on.
