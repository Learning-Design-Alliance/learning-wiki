# Four-kind classification of fundamental reasoning elements by origin

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
From their interview analyses the authors classify fundamental reasoning elements by origin: statements taken from an authoritative source such as an instructor or book, commonly made statements applied "as a fact, without structure," generalizations of processes such as "things come to equilibrium" (identified as p-prims), and statements of personal observation. Each kind is used by students as an unquestioned starting point for answering questions or building models.
