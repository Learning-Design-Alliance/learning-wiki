# Refined 16-category system for differentiating and evaluating visual-graphical qubit representations

## Description
The article introduces a refined categorisation system for evaluating visual quantum physics representations for educational purposes. It takes Ainsworth's Design, Functions, and Tasks (DeFT) framework as its conceptual foundation, "extended with relevant aspects of QP representations, including their respective potential risks of inducing misconceptions in learners". Sixteen categories are organised under four axes: design (salience, dimension, understanding difficulties, colour), functions (actions/steps, mathematics, contiguity, overlap/redundancy, complementarity, predictability), tasks/applications (concepts, quantum technologies, phase, amplitude) and cross-concepts (generability, effort in explanation). Experts used the system to profile four qubit representations.
