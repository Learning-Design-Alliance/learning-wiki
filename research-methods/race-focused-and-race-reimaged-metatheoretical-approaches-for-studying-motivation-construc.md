# Race-focused and race-reimaged metatheoretical approaches for studying motivation constructs

## Description
The article distinguishes two metatheoretical approaches for educational research: race-focused approaches, which "center on issues of race and are grounded in racial or cultural theories and constructs" such as racial identity and stereotype threat, and race-reimaged approaches, which reconceptualize traditional motivational constructs by integrating racial theories through critical social-cultural-historical lenses. The authors extend these approaches from single constructs to interactions among multiple interrelated constructs (belonging, emoting, achieving) contextualized in STEM education.
