# Two-category indexing scheme for intercultural science education literature: conceptual work versus practical implementation

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article organizes its review of 2018–2023 publications with an indexing code built following Del Rosario Escobar and Buteler (2018). Studies are classified into "dos tipos: Trabajo conceptual (T) y Puesta en práctica (PP)", with conceptual work subdivided into theoretical reflections and reflections on implementation, and implementation subdivided into boundary objects and implementation experiences. Of the 16 works found, 9 were conceptual and the analysis focuses on categories T2, PP1 and PP2.
