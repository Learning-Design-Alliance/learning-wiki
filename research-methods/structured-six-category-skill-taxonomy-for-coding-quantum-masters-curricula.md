## Description
A predefined skill taxonomy classifying courses by emphasis on core quantum topics, interdisciplinary competencies, and applied workforce skills. Courses were coded on an ordinal 0–2 scale (not present, secondary component, primary focus) across categories spanning quantum theory, hardware, algorithms/software, communication/cryptography, sensing/metrology, plus non-technical communication/project management and career awareness. The study uses it to produce program-level skill profiles and system-level aggregates. The article states the compiled curricular data "were systematically coded using a predefined skill taxonomy to classify courses according to their emphasis on core quantum topics, interdisciplinary competencies, and applied workforce skills."
