# Two-axis content-valence taxonomy for narrative comments on clinical skills

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A content-analysis taxonomy for narrative comments on preclerkship clinical skills with two axes: a content axis of four categories (attitude; knowledge and cognitive processes; clinical reasoning; general), each elaborated with inductively derived subcategories, and a valence axis of positive, negative, and improvement suggestions. The authors "classified units into content (knowledge, skills, attitude, clinical reasoning) and valence (positive, negative, improvement suggestions)" and applied it to 1,314 analysis units from 272 comments.
