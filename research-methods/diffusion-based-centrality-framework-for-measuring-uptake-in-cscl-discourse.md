# Diffusion-based centrality framework for measuring uptake in CSCL discourse

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
A framework representing threaded CSCL discussion as a directed post-reply network in which centrality measures operationalize the uptake of ideas and stimulation of further discussion. It combines diffusion-specific measures — "Maximum Neighborhood Component (MNC) is the cardinality of connected neighboring nodes excluding the target node", coreness ("similar to H-index"), cross clique connectivity, and diffusion degree — with traditional degree, closeness, betweenness and eigenvector centralities, positioning uptake capability as a function of position, connectedness and embeddedness in the network.
