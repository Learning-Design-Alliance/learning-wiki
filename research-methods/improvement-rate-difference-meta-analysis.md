## Description
This element is the meta-analysis itself: the authors synthesized "Twenty-four single-case studies" on aided AAC with individuals with ASD "via an effect size measure, the Improvement Rate Difference (IRD)." The IRD is used to quantify improvement across single-case designs, a literature base the authors describe as dominated by single-case research "with small numbers of participants." The analysis addressed three research questions concerning overall impact, individual targeted behavioral outcomes, and effects of three types of AAC interventions.
