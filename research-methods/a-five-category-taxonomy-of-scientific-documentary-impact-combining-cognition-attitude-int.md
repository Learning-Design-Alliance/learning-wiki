# A five-category taxonomy of scientific documentary impact combining cognition, attitude, interest, impersonal report, and not-applicable codes with three sentiment classes

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article introduces a sentence-level taxonomy for quantifying how scientific documentaries affect audiences, modeled off issue-focused documentary impact categories with updates for scientific documentaries. It codes each sentence into one of five impact categories — "Shift in Cognition (C)", "Attitudes Toward the Film (A)", "Interest with Science Topic (S)", "Impersonal report (I)", and "Not applicable (N)" — crossed with Positive, Neutral, and Negative sentiment. Shift in Cognition captures viewers' "ability to identify terms, features, and concepts, up to and including metacognitive elements," and the scheme is framed as seeking evidence of constructivist learning trends in uncontrolled online comments.
