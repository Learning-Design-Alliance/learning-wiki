# Event-centered phase segmentation for multimodal simulation analysis

## Description
A reusable analytic pattern in which a simulation scenario is divided into three scripted segments so behaviors, regulation strategies and physiological responses can be compared across critical points. The article explains: "We used the patient’s deterioration and scripted events to guide the segmentation of data, for example, we may use the “patient’s” sudden drop in pulse or the anaphylactic shock that tightens the airway to initiate the transition from the ‘‘Beginning’’ to the ‘‘Escalation’’ of a simulation." Segments were chosen for comparable length and to identify the stakes at each point, with the 'Peak' marking the convergence of medical decision-making and execution of patient care.
