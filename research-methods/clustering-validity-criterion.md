# Clustering-validity criterion

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The article proposes treating embedding-based clustering as an object of validation rather than an assumed grading method. Its criterion, illustrated schematically in Fig. 1, is that "if embedding clusters supported grading-related interpretation, sizable clusters would contain responses with similar scores." The relevant construct in grading is not general semantic similarity but rubric-relevant correctness, since two solutions may be textually similar while differing in a crucial sign, state variable, unit, or physical assumption. Mixed-score clusters may still be useful for exploratory organization, whereas large score variation indicates embedding proximity does not preserve the grading-relevant construct.
