# VanderWeele's four-way causal mediation decomposition

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The article applies VanderWeele's four-way decomposition within a counterfactual (potential outcomes) framework to disentangle mediation and interaction. The "TE is the sum of all four components": the Controlled Direct Effect (effect of SES net of mindset), the Reference Interaction (interaction when SES does not affect mindset), the Mediated Interaction (interaction operating through SES-influenced mindset), and the Pure Indirect Effect (SES operating through mindset). The authors argue this avoids overcontrolling bias and misleading coefficient comparisons that plague standard regression approaches in the mindset literature.
