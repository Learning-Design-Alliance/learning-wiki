# Seven-domain taxonomy of standards for evaluating undergraduate e-learning programmes in health professions in LMICs

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review inductively derived a taxonomy of evaluation standards from the eight included articles, organised into domains: curriculum planning, proficiency of the educator, learner proficiency and attitude, infrastructure for learning, support, and evaluation. Each domain carries specific themes, such as course design, educator training, learner satisfaction, IT and network infrastructure, technical support, and monitoring for continuous improvement. The authors state that "Seven domains, each with a specific theme were generated inductively after engaging with the individual themes from each included article."
