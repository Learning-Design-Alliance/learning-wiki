# Definition of generative AI-powered agentic AI via three core characteristics: task autonomy, goal-directed reasoning, and memory- and context-aware interaction

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review defines agentic AI as systems "powered by generative AI, that exhibit goal-directed autonomy": perceiving the environment, reasoning about goals, formulating and executing multi-step plans, adapting behaviour, and coordinating with tools, other agents, or human stakeholders. Three core characteristics distinguish educational agentic systems from general-purpose text generators: task autonomy, goal-directed reasoning and adaptive decision-making, and memory- and context-aware interaction; multi-step planning and multi-agent collaboration may be present but are not required for inclusion.
