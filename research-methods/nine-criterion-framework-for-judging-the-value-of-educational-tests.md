## Description
The article enumerates nine virtues a test may possess to varying degrees, from validity ('Measure what it purports to measure') through reliability, objectivity, scaling, norms, interest to the child, range of abilities registered, diagnostic discrimination between failures, and correlation with intelligence at maximum ability. The framework is offered as 'a convenient assembly of important test virtues' to guide administrators who must choose among many existing tests rather than find one.
