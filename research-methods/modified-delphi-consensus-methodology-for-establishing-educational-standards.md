# Modified Delphi consensus methodology for establishing educational standards

## Description
The article employs a modified Delphi consensus approach, described as a systematic method that has demonstrated robust reliability in establishing educational standards across surgical specialties through structured expert consultation. The protocol uses a sequential multi-phase design: a preliminary competency framework, a first round with categorical maintain/remove/modify assessment, and a second round with binary agree/disagree responses. "Participant selection is one of the main sources of bias, since the technique relies heavily on the expertise of the panel."
