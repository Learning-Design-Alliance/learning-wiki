# Engagement funnel framework

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper adapts Callahan et al.'s (2019) funnel of influences on occupational identity into a narrowed "engagement funnel" of awareness, interest, and engagement, used as the organizing coding scheme for interviews and observations. The authors state they "focused our attention on the earlier stages of influence to create an 'engagement funnel' of awareness, interest, and engagement, where awareness and interest are a more nuanced view on exposure." Movement along the funnel is described as not necessarily linear, with the stages interrelated.

# Engagement funnel framework: awareness, interest, and engagement as stages of influence on occupational identity

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper adapts Callahan et al.'s (2019) funnel of influences on occupational identity into a narrowed "engagement funnel" of awareness, interest, and engagement, used as the organizing coding scheme for interviews and observations. The authors state they "focused our attention on the earlier stages of influence to create an 'engagement funnel' of awareness, interest, and engagement, where awareness and interest are a more nuanced view on exposure." Movement along the funnel is described as not necessarily linear, with the stages interrelated.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Data on student and educator experiences must be coded against the funnel stages to trace transitions
#### Constraints
- The paper states deep levels of participation were not expected within the implementation timeline, so participation stages were excluded

### Target Learners
- historically and systemically excluded (HSE) high school students

### Learning Goals
- career and technical education (CTE) pathway awareness, interest, and engagement; occupational identity development
