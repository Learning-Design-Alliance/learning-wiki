## Description
The editorial describes Tissenbaum, Berland, and Lyons' framework "for recognizing and coding collaboration and divergent learning — Divergent Collaborative Learning Mechanisms (DCLM)", used to analyze museum visitors' interactions with an interactive digital tabletop exhibit. It challenges the assumption that productive collaboration requires shared objectives, showing collaborative learning occurred among visitors "while holding divergent conceptions and pursuing divergent goals."
