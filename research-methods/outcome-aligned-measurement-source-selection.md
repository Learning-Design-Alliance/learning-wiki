## Description
The methodological moderator analyses highlighted "the importance of using methods that center teachers’ and students’ experiences and align with the nature of the focal outcome". Because personal perception is central to psychological experiences of motivation and engagement, survey measures and student respondents may yield stronger correlations for engagement and competence beliefs, while observation and teacher reports may align better with achievement outcomes. Researchers designing studies of classroom structure should match the measurement approach to the outcome's perceptual nature.
