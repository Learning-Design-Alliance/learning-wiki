# Triangulated multi-source assessment of self-regulated reading processes

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because think-aloud protocols face reactivity and veridicality risks, the review recommends that researchers "view think-aloud data as indicators of only a part of self-regulated reading process rather than a full picture of it". One or more other data sources — pre- and post-interviews, questionnaires, observations, on-line computer logs, and eye-movement registration — should be combined with think-aloud reports for a fuller picture. The review also recommends reporting details such as languages used for reading and verbalizing and participant ages.
