# Inductive coding manual characterizing students’ instrument-choice justifications in three categories plus uncodable

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The authors inductively developed, through five rounds of coding and rephrasing, a coding manual for students’ justifications when choosing between measurement instruments. It distinguishes justifications based on "data quality (labeled as data), the experimental process (labeled as exp.), and personal considerations (labeled as pers.)", each with sub-categories (e.g., data.1 precision/accuracy/uncertainty, data.2 avoiding reading mistakes or systematic effects, pers.1 experience, pers.2 ease of use or speed, pers.3 preference or bias, pers.4 fun), plus a category for uncodable responses. Inter-rater reliability on 50 randomly drawn justifications showed "good agreement between the coders, κ = 0.76".
