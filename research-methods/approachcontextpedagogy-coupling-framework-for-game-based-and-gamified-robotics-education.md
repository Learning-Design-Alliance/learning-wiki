# Approach–context–pedagogy coupling framework for game-based and gamified robotics education

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review's organizing framework holds that instructional approach (GBL vs. gamification), learning context (formal vs. informal), and pedagogical model are coupled in robotics education: "GBL more prevalent in informal settings, while gamification dominated formal classrooms" and gamification "favored project-based learning". The framework maps three dominant pedagogies — experiential learning, project-based learning, and constructivist learning — onto approaches and contexts, with formal classrooms leaning toward PBL-driven designs and informal settings favoring experiential approaches with higher proportions of constructivist elements.
