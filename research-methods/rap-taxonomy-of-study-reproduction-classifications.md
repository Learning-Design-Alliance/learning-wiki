# R.A.P. Taxonomy of study reproduction classifications

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The R.A.P. Taxonomy, created by Ihantola et al. and described in this review, classifies study reproduction along three key criteria: researchers, data analysis, and data production. "They consider three key criteria: researchers, data analysis, and data production to identify seven classifications where one or more of the criteria are changed for study reproduction." The seven categories range from Re-Analysis to Reproduction, and by considering the taxonomy researchers can examine the state space of possible study reproductions related to a specific hypothesis.
