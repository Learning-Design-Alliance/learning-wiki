## Description
The article argues that non-adaptive control variants fail to control for expectancy, motivation and demand effects because adaptive trainees are constantly challenged and see their improvement. It recommends controls that "effectively train[] a capacity unrelated to working memory", and implemented adaptive visual search training (Codebreak) as such a control in children for the first time.
