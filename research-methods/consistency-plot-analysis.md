## Description
The article identifies four recurring patterns in consistency plots that carry distinct interpretive meanings: circulation (opposite response pairs between two regions), attractors (regions with net inflow of response pairs), starbursts (regions students leave in all directions), and voids (regions with no responses). The authors state these patterns are "hard to represent in simple tabular form" and each is revealing about student performance, with circulation questioning class-wide improvement claims and starbursts distinguishing stepping stones from confusion.
