# Three-method taxonomy of learning analytics: descriptive, predictive and prescriptive analytics

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The review organises learning analytics methodologies into three types. "Descriptive analytics use data obtained from sources such as course assessments, surveys, student information systems, learning management system activities and forum interactions mainly for reporting purposes." Predictive analytics use similar data to measure onward learning success or failure, while prescriptive analytics deploy algorithms mainly to predict study success and course completion and to recommend immediate interventions. The review uses this taxonomy to characterise the diversification of early trace-data approaches into broader educational applications.
