# Course-unit-granular process mining of educational event logs

## Description
The article recommends that educational process mining of whole-course logs be complemented by finer-grained analysis. The authors report that "Increasing the granularity and producing sub-files by unit made it possible to analyze student behavior more thoroughly and produced sounder models which were easier to interpret." Whole-course models exhibited huge parallelism and were not clearly detailing the route followed by students, whereas per-unit networks were longer, more developed and showed a more structured student workflow that instructors could visualize and interpret.
