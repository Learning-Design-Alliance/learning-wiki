# Three-criterion textbook-review protocol for evaluating the electromagnetic field in introductory physics textbooks

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article designs a textbook-review protocol based on three criteria derived from the epistemological analysis: treatment of Faraday's and Ampère-Maxwell's laws in relation to field sources (Criterion 1), presentation of the generation and propagation of electromagnetic waves in relation to their sources (Criterion 2), and presentation of the electromagnetic field as a single entity across reference systems (Criterion 3). Each criterion is developed through sub-aspects (C1.1–C3.2), and ratings use four categories: "Complies (C)", "Partially complies (PC)", "Does not comply (NC)", and "Does not address the issue (NT)". The analysis covers theoretical explanations plus questions and solved examples, excluding end-of-chapter exercises. Inter-rater agreement was high, with an overall Cohen's kappa of 0.94.
