# Adapted context-based analysis model for instructional design in developing countries

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article presents an adaptation of Tessmer and Richey's (1997) context-based analysis model, which defines context as a "multilevel body of factors in which learning and performance are embedded." The model investigates context across three temporal aspects—orienting, instructional and transfer—at three levels: learner, immediate environment and organization. The adaptation adds new immediate-environment and organizational factors to the orienting context that are specific to developing-country conditions, so that front-end analysis goes beyond traditional needs assessment.
