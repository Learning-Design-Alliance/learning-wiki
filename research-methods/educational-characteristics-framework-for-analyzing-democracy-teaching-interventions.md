## Description
Beyond teaching-method types, the review analyzes interventions through three educational characteristics. As the article prints, "We analyzed what the educational characteristics (i.e., subject matter, classroom interaction, classroom management) of these interventions were and for which democratic competences ... they were effective." This three-part lens describes how the 54 interventions were implemented in classrooms and links implementation features to effectiveness for democratic competences.
