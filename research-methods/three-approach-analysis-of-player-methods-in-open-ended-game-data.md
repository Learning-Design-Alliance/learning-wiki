The article's reusable analytic pattern consists of three complementary lenses: "looking at actions and tool use, play patterns and player types, and tool input patterns". Applied together to one open-ended quest, these lenses identify content-specific gameplay decisions and distinguish players utilizing different methods. The pattern is intended to generalize as guiding questions for learning games designers and data scientists.
