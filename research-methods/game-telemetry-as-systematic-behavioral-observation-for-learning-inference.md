# Game telemetry as systematic behavioral observation for learning inference

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The chapter conceives game telemetry as "the data associated with specific game events, the state of a game, or other parameters of interest," captured to a permanent external store using a predefined record format rather than ad hoc logging. It flows from the behavioral observation tradition, where the two most important properties are "the precise deﬁnition of the behavior to observe" and "the connection between the behavior and a theoretical framework within which the behavior is interpreted." Telemetry is the first element in the chain of reasoning from behavior to inferences about learning.
