# Almeida Bauer Zytkuewisz rubric for characterizing levels of inquiry in undergraduate laboratories

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A rubric that differentiates laboratory activities into six levels (originally five, recently updated) based on six characteristics: problem/question, theory/background, procedure/design, result/analysis, results communication, and conclusions. The level of inquiry is determined by the amount of information provided to students. Level 3 authentic inquiry provides no information for any characteristic and is considered impractical in undergraduate courses due to difficulty, complexity, safety risks, and students' limited technical expertise. This article's activity was designed to align with Level 3.
