Three-part taxonomy of intervention elements: practice, process, and implementation elements¶

Research Method · All research methods

Description¶

The review distinguishes three discrete element types in interventions for children at risk. Practice elements are "specific activities or actions used to evoke or influence an outcome (e.g., positive reinforcement)"; process elements cover delivery forms and contexts such as home visitation; implementation elements are "discrete strategies to facilitate or enable the delivery of practice and process elements" such as ongoing training. The taxonomy organizes the review's coding matrices and frequency analyses of 147 identified elements.
