# Ullmann's eight-category model for reflection detection in reflective writing

## Description
The article synthesizes 24 reflective writing models into a "model for reflection detection" combining depth and breadth qualities. "In total, the model consists of eight categories": the depth dimension Reflection (reflective vs. non-reflective) and seven breadth categories — Experience, Feeling, Belief, Difficulty (critical stance), Perspective, Learning (retrospective outcomes), and Intention (prospective outcomes). The synthesis deliberately used a general model rather than one specific model, so the evaluation covers categories "common to many models" used in manual content analysis of reflective writing.
