# Couplet scoring

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Couplet scoring is a scoring paradigm for content research-based assessment instruments in which the unit of assessment is the couplet, an item scored through the lens of one assessment objective (AO). As the authors put it, "a couplet is an assessment item viewed and scored in light of a particular AO", and multi-AO items have one couplet per AO. Couplet scores are summed and normalized into AO scores (0–1), which are averaged into an overall score that equally weights AOs; AO scores and the overall score are what get reported to instructors and used in research analysis.
