# Six-dimensional agentic capability framework rated on a 1–5 ordinal scale

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review develops a coding rubric rating six agentic capability dimensions — autonomy and proactivity; planning and reasoning depth; memory and context management; tool and environment interaction; multi-agent collaboration; and robustness, explainability and governance — each on "A 1–5 ordinal scale" capturing thresholds from non-agentic to fully agentic behaviour. Activity theory anchors the dimensions educationally (e.g., autonomy maps to the subject, governance to rules), and scaffolding theory supplements interpretation of planning, memory, and governance dimensions.
