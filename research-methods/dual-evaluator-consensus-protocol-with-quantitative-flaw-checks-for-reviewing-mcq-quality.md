# Dual-evaluator consensus protocol with quantitative flaw checks for reviewing MCQ quality

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A reusable review workflow for assessing MCQ item quality: two content-expert evaluators independently rate each item against an evidence-based protocol of 15 item flaws, compare results, discuss disagreements and agree a final evaluation by consensus. Items are tracked with unique identifiers through a synchronised form-based workflow. Programmatically checkable features supplement the qualitative judgements.
