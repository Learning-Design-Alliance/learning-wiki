# Point-like and set-like paradigms of measurement uncertainty reasoning

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The point and set paradigms, "developed by researchers at the University of Cape Town and the University of York", describe "two opposing ideas about uncertainty". A point-like reasoner believes "a single measurement can yield the 'true value'" of a quantity and considers individual measurements independently; a set-like reasoner recognizes no single measurement yields the true value and considers "a distribution of measurements with an associated mean and spread". Set-like reasoning is associated with expert-like understanding, and the paradigms organize PMQ coding of student explanations.
