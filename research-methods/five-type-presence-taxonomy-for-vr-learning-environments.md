# Five-type presence taxonomy for VR learning environments

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review organizes VR presence research around five presence types identified across the included studies: spatial presence, social presence, co-presence, self-presence, and cognitive presence. The abstract states the review provides "a detailed classification and analysis of different types of presence in VR environments, including spatial presence, social presence, co-presence, self-presence, and cognitive presence." Each type is defined by its function: spatial presence concerns perceiving position and spatial relationships, cognitive presence concerns deep thinking and knowledge construction, social presence concerns interaction quality, co-presence concerns shared awareness, and self-presence concerns identity and embodiment. The taxonomy structures the review's data extraction (DE4), its mapping of presence types to task functions, and its design recommendations.
