# The evidence ladder: five levels of learner-related claim strength from component response to transfer

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article organizes evaluation through a five-level evidence ladder ranking the strength of the learner-related claim: E1 component or response evidence, E2 pedagogical quality, E3 downstream task or interaction behavior, E4 measured learning gains with real learners, and E5 transfer or delayed retention. The ladder 'ranks thestrength of the learner-related claim, not methodological quality': a careful E2 study can be better run than a weak E4 one, but E4 licenses a stronger claim. It is used to locate gaps in adaptive tutoring, simulator validity, longitudinal evaluation, and deployment.
