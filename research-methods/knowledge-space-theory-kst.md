# Knowledge Space Theory (KST)

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
ALEKS's domain model is based on Knowledge Space Theory, a combinatoric and probabilistic model of knowledge assessment in which a student's knowledge state is the set of items the student can complete correctly unaided, and a knowledge structure captures prerequisite relationships among items. The outer fringe is defined conceptually as "the set of items the student is ready to learn," tracked over hundreds of discrete items; the article notes the inner and outer fringes are "somewhat related to Vygotsky's (1930–1934/1978) notion of Zone of Proximal Development (ZPD)." The outer fringe serves as a pillar for all three grouping methods.
