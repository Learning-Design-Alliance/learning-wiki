## Description
Thematic progression (TP), first proposed by Daneš (1974), concerns "the way that the texts develop the ideas they present" in Theme-Rheme structure. The study identifies five TP patterns (linear, constant, summative, split Theme, split Rheme) and three types of unmotivated Themes (back, contextual, new), counting connections between contiguous T-units. The framework is used to code 150 essays and quantify how instruction changes learners' information structuring.
