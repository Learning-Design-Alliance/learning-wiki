Three-stage comparative educational experiment (stating–forming–control)¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article presents a reusable experimental design for psychoeducational research in which, as it prints, the comparative experiment "as a rule it includes three stages": a stating stage choosing experimental and control groups and determining the initial level of formation of students' technological training via testing; a forming stage introducing the didactic set into the educational process in the experimental group while the control group uses the traditional technique; and a control stage performing a final check (cutoff test) of the forming stage's results and final confirmation of the research hypothesis. The design rests on comparing control and experimental groups and registering corresponding changes.
