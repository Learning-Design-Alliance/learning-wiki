# Mixed functional data analysis for grade-varying longitudinal predictor effects

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The article applies longitudinal mixed functional data analysis (FDA) to estimate regression effects as functions of grade, using linear splines for fixed and random effect functions. It argues FDA "is not constrained by the parametric assumptions of cross-lagged and related models" and enables simultaneous estimation of overall domain-general and domain-specific effects, grade-by-grade contributions of individual variables, and linear and non-linear across-grade change in these effects on subsequent mathematics achievement.
