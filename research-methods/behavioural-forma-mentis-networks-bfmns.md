# Behavioural forma mentis networks (BFMNs)

## Description
Behavioural forma mentis networks combine free association data with valence ratings in a single network representation. Nodes represent concepts, links encode memory-based free associations, and each concept carries an emotional label. The authors state that "BFMNs offer a quantitative and interpretable framework to study academic anxiety and to distinguish human affective framing from current AI-based simulations." The framework captures not only which concepts are connected but also how they are affectively perceived and clustered, enabling detection of otherwise hidden forms of test anxiety through valence auras and semantic frames.
