# Zoom Lens multilevel collaboration analytics framework

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Zoom Lens framework is a multilevel collaboration analytics framework proposed by Zhou and Cukurova as an extension of the 'from clicks to constructs' framework. It “extended the human-observable behaviours of the previous framework into three different types of behaviours, namely social signals, group interactions and individual engagement”. Group interactions are detected from each individual's social signals, while individual engagement is estimated based on that social signal and the group interaction happening. This study applies Zoom Lens and operationalises it with a rule-based automatic detection of individual engagement from group interactions.
