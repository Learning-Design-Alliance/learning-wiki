# Three types of measures derivable from gameplay telemetry: overall game performance, in-game performance, and in-game strategies

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The chapter proposes that "three types of measures can be derived from gameplay: (a) overall game performance, (b) in-game performance, and (c) in-game strategies," each with certain uses and selected according to the question being asked. In the case study, overall performance was the last level reached, in-game performance included addition errors, level resets, and player deaths, and in-game strategies were derived from sets or sequences of behaviors analyzed with methods such as cluster analysis.
