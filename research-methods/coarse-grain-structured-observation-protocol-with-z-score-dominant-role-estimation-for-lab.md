# Coarse-grain structured observation protocol with z-score dominant-role estimation for lab groups

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A structured observation protocol, adapted from prior work with demonstrated strong inter-rater reliability, in which a researcher recorded, "in two to three minute intervals", whether each student had hands on the experimental equipment, a computer or laptop, or pencil and paper, or was talking to their group, another group, or the instructor. A total of 43 intervals were coded for each student, and each student's dominant activity was identiﬁed as "the activity with their largest z-score compared to the rest of the class", allowing roles to be compared across group types and genders.
