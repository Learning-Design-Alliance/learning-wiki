# Hierarchical logistic regression with average marginal effects as a framework for identifying and tracking STEM awarding gaps

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes an analytical framework in which a multivariate binary logistic model with a random intercept for STEM Subject identifies predictors of first-class degree outcomes while accounting for confounding variables and nested data structures. Average Marginal Effects translate odds ratios into absolute probability differences, which the authors call "an easily interpretable metric for socio-demographic awarding gaps, facilitating clearer communication of results to policymakers". Year-interaction models with year-specific AMEs provide "a method for tracking awarding gaps through time", and the authors hope the approach "can serve as a template for other institutions".
