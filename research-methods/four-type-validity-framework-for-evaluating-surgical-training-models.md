# Four-type validity framework for evaluating surgical training models

## Description
The review organises its evaluation of body donor models around four validity types drawn from simulation literature: "face validity, construct validity, content validity, and predictive validity". Face validity concerns resemblance to a real-life procedure, construct validity the ability to distinguish experience levels, content validity the capacity to enhance knowledge and skills across settings, and predictive validity the translation of improved skills to real-life clinical settings. The review sorts its 55 included studies into groups by which validity type they assess, and uses this framework to diagnose where evidence is strongest (face validity) and weakest (predictive validity).
