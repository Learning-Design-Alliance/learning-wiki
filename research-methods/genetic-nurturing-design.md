## Description
The genetic nurturing design separates, within genotyped parent–offspring trios, polygenic scores based on alleles parents transmitted to their offspring from scores based on non-transmitted alleles. Because non-transmitted alleles cannot affect the child directly, "The effect of the non-transmitted PGS is necessarily mediated by parental phenotypes, insofar as they contribute to the rearing environment of the offspring (genetic nurturing)". Transmitted alleles carry both direct genetic effects and indirect effects mediated by the environment parents create. The article applies this design to educational attainment and ADHD polygenic scores to test environmental transmission of parental characteristics to offspring outcomes.
