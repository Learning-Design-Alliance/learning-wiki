Homogeneous-outcome selection for causal learning-loss analysis¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

This design principle holds that causal estimates of instructional disruptions require tight confinement of the counterfactual trend. The authors achieved this by restricting the outcome to reading fluency, "a readily measurable and well-defined construct", and by noting that "the choice of an ability with a homogeneous development, a narrow definition of the construct reading fluency, and the measurement with a standardized instrument allowed us to control time-dependent trends in our causal analysis." Multiple measurement points additionally separated short-term from long-term effects.
