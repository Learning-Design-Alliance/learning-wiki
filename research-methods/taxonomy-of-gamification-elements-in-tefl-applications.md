## Description
The review organizes the gamified TEFL literature around a taxonomy of game elements, counting the frequency with which each appears across the 33 included studies: "Feedback	23", challenge, points, leaderboard, leveling, time, badge, progress bar, story/narration, digital badges, avatar/character, and competition. This element inventory structures the review's comparative discussion with medical education, where feedback and competitive features are also central.
