## Description
The article uses Biggs' constructive alignment framework to structure a multi-modal curriculum evaluation across three aligned components: intended learning outcomes, teaching and learning activities, and assessment and feedback. The authors state they conducted "a national, multi-modal evaluation of ECG education in Canadian Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons' Emergency Medicine residency programs using Biggs' constructive alignment framework". Each component was assessed with a distinct method: EPA document review, program director survey, and resident survey.

- **Constructive alignment as the evaluative lens for communication skills curricula**: The article frames communication skills curricula through constructive alignment, defined as "aligning teaching methods, as well as assessment, to the intended learning outcomes". In medical education this alignment is meant to ensure students achieve communication outcomes needed for patient-centred care in diverse contexts. The study uses this framework to evaluate whether classroom teaching, clinical practice and OSCE assessment demand and reward the same communication skills, finding that alignment remains an elusive goal in many medical schools.
