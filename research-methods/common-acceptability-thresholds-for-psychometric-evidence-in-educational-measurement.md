# Common acceptability thresholds for psychometric evidence in educational measurement

## Description
The review assembles a framework of psychometric indicators and their common acceptability thresholds in educational research, covering content validity, structural validity, internal consistency, reliability, and criterion validity. For example, it lists "RMSEA < .06; CFI > .95; TLI > .95" for confirmatory factor analysis, internal consistency and test–retest/inter-rater correlations at ≥ .70, diagnostic accuracy at "Sensitivity ≥ .90; Specificity ≥ .70", and criterion correlations at ≥ .60. Tools were rated Acceptable, Mixed, or Low against these thresholds.
