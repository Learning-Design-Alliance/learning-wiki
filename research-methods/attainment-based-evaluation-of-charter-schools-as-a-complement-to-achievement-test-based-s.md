- **Evaluate charter high schools on attainment outcomes such as diploma completion and college attendance, not test scores alone**: The article argues that attainment outcomes should anchor evaluations of charter high schools because of their downstream stakes: "Given the impact of educational attainment on a variety of economic and social outcomes, a positive result could have significant implications for the value of school-choice programs that include charter high schools." The study therefore measures standard diploma receipt and college attendance rather than relying solely on achievement tests. (Kevin Booker et al. (2010))
