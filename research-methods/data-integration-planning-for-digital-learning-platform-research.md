# Data integration planning for digital learning platform research

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report's fourth consideration addresses data availability: DLPs may lack individual demographic data while collecting school-level covariates such as locale type or Title I status. Researchers should "weigh the tradeoffs and level of effort that may be required to access and integrate additional data," possibly through data sharing agreements, embedded surveys, or LMS/SIS roster linking, and may iterate toward richer data across multiple small-scale studies.
