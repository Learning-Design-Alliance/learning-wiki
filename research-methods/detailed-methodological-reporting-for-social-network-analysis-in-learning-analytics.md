## Description
The article recommends that researchers reporting on social network analysis in learning analytics document their operationalization choices, because measurement robustness underpins reproducibility. It states that "the results emphasize the need for researchers who report on SNA to present in detail their methodological choices so that research is better able to be compared, replicated, and ultimately generalized." The article enumerates nine reportable items, from tie definition and weighting through software versions to community-finding parameters.
