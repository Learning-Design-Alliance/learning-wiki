# CAPERS (Community and Participatory Evidence and Research Synthesis)

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
CAPERS (Community and Participatory Evidence and Research Synthesis) is a mixed-methods framework that engages community partners prospectively in research synthesis rather than post hoc. The article describes it as "a novel mixed-methods synthesis framework" that shifts community engagement "from a post hoc interpretive activity/responsibility to a prospectively planned component of the research synthesis design." It applies across synthesis types including meta-analyses, scoping reviews, and qualitative meta-syntheses.
