# Four-function taxonomy of pictures in multimedia testing (decorative, representational, organizational, informational)

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review organizes pictures in test items by their functional relationship to the accompanying text, adapting Levin's MML classification. Decorative pictures "can be loosely associated with the item context but do not display task or solution relevant information"; representational pictures represent part or all of task-relevant text content; organizational pictures support the solution procedure by providing visuospatial structure; and informational pictures "depict information that is essential for the solution of the problem." The review uses this taxonomy to classify 62 studies and proposes simplification and extension in its discussion.
