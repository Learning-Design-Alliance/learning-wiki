# A five-step systematic methodology for building the conceptual evidence base of educational innovations

## Description
The article introduces a structured methodology for evaluating educational innovations' conceptual foundations, developed through practice at the International Centre for EdTech Impact. It proceeds from evidence identification to interpretation and application: "We followed five systematic steps. First, we conducted a rapid literature review, guided by the principle of methodological plurality. Second, we evaluated the identified studies using the criterion of weight of evidence." The sequence was designed to reduce the risk of retrospectively selecting evidence to justify existing product features, and culminates in a theory of change shared with the company to inform its research roadmap.
