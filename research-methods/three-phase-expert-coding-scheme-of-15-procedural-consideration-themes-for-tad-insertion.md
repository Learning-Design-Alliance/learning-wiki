# Three-phase expert coding scheme of 15 procedural-consideration themes for TAD insertion

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A coding framework derived from think-aloud transcripts of three orthodontic/oral maxillofacial surgery consultants, organizing TAD insertion into 15 themes across three phases. Phase 1 ("Preoperative") covers planning, radiograph assessment, TAD length and palpation; Phase 2 covers intraoperative actions such as site marking, angulation and slow insertion plus rationales like anesthesia verification and tactile feedback for resistance; Phase 3 covers postoperative checks of insertion depth and primary stability. The scheme served as the deductive coding frame for analyzing residents' verbalized thought processes.
