# Multilevel mixture modeling with propensity score weights operationalizing VLE usage as latent classes

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
This methodological framework treats VLE usage as a discrete latent variable: latent class analyses at student and classroom levels characterize usage patterns, generalized propensity scores with inverse probability weighting remove selection bias into classes, and Vermunt's three-step method accounts for classification uncertainty. The article states its objective is "to demonstrate a multilevel approach for quasi-experimental evaluation of VLEs which operationalizes treatment statuses as latent classes", extending Bray et al.'s single-level approach so that both students and classrooms are participants of usage groups.
