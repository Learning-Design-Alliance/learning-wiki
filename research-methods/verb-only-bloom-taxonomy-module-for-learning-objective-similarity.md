## Description
The pattern is to compute Bloom's taxonomy similarity as a separate module of the methodology rather than fusing it into the main sentence-similarity pipeline: the authors "implement the Bloom's taxonomy as separate part of methodology and restrain its inﬂuence on the main sentence similarity methodology". This is because a considerable number of course drafts differ significantly from the taxonomy norm, so integrating poorly structured learning outcomes with the primary method would violate its purpose. The verb layer alone, with its hierarchical levels 1 (Remembering) to 6 (Creating), supplies the difficulty-level comparison via the Bloom Index.
