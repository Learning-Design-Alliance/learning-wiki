## Description
The article adopts Holmboe, Yepes, Williams, & Huot's categorization of feedback into four hierarchical groups, ordered from least to most effective: "giving recommendations, enabling learner reaction, asking for self-assessment and agreeing on an action plan". The study used these categories to develop its coding manual for written feedback on Mini-CEX forms, later collapsing categories into a dichotomous general/specific variable. The article notes Holmboe et al found most Mini-CEX feedback fell into the least effective category.
