- **Multimodal writing-to-learn research agenda embedding cognitive process measures in disciplinary context**: The article proposes a research agenda that treats writing as a socio-cognitive phenomenon within a complex understanding of context, blending three research activities: "1) primary trait scoring, 2) revision-based analysis, and 3) key stroke analysis." Each method captures a different facet: primary trait analysis captures the merit of the final document, version control captures revisions between sessions, and keystroke analysis captures revision within a single session. The multimodal triangulation is argued to strengthen conclusions about writing-to-learn within disciplinary contexts. (Hermsen et al. (2006))
