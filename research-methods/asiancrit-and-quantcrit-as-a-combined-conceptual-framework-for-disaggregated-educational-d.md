# AsianCrit and QuantCrit as a combined conceptual framework for disaggregated educational data analysis

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study draws on Asian Critical Theory (AsianCrit), which centers the racialization of Asian students, and Quantitative Critical Race Theory (QuantCrit), which holds that "numbers and quantitative methods are not neutral because they reflect human choices." AsianCrit tenets used include Asianization, strategic (anti)essentialism, and intersectionality; QuantCrit tenets include that numbers are not neutral and categories are neither natural nor inherent. "By integrating AsianCrit and QuantCrit, the study aligns its conceptual commitments with its methodological approach", guiding decisions about data disaggregation, model specification, and interpretation.
