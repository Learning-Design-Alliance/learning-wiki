# The EdTech Evidence Evaluation Routine (EVER)

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
EVER is a simple guide proposed by the authors to evaluate the evidence base of existing EdTech solutions and to help companies grow their products' evidence base. It is a matrix crossing evaluation approaches (conceptual, qualitative, quantitative, validation) with quality assurance aspects, with each cell rated. The authors state it "can be used as a prompt for reflection when evaluating the evidence portfolio of diverse EdTech products, processes and initiatives" and is best used as part of formative evaluations.
