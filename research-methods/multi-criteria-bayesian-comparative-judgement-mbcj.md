# Multi-Criteria Bayesian Comparative Judgement (MBCJ)

## Description
MBCJ extends BCJ to rubric-based assessment where each item is compared independently per learning outcome (LO) with predefined weights. Overall ranks come from either a mixture of LO-specific rank distributions or a weighted mixture of LO-specific preference CDFs, and pair selection uses the sum of LO-specific Beta entropies to pick the most uncertain pair. The article states: "This framework, which we term Multi-Criteria BCJ (MBCJ), enables criterion-specific ranking and uncertainty estimation." Mixture CDFs enable direct computation of overall preference probabilities and full rank distributions, with Monte Carlo sampling used to avoid combinatorial explosion for large item sets.
