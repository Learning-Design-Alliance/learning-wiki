# Multiagent first-person perspective analysis (FPA)

## Description
The article presents multiagent, time- and space-synchronized first-person perspective analysis (FPA) as a method for capturing observer-independent behavioral metrics during emergency simulation training. Wearable smart glasses record first-person video, gaze, and audio from all team members; fixations are mapped to five areas of interest ("patient," "member," "device," "screen," "unknown") and eye-contact events are detected with YOLOv8-based face detection combined with gaze-vector estimation. The authors position FPA-derived indicators as "complementary measures" to the Ottawa GRS Situational Awareness and Communication domains, which involve cognitive and nonverbal elements difficult to evaluate from an external perspective.
