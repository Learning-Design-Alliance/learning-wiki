Expert-panel interpretation of compiled educational evidence¶

Description¶

The article describes an evidence-review approach in which a research organization compiles a large body of studies and convenes expert panels to interpret the data. As printed, "The Center on Reinventing Public Education compiled hundreds of studies and convened panels of experts to interpret what the data show." This pairs a broad compiled evidence base with structured expert judgment to characterize what the evidence indicates about pandemic effects on students.

Empirical methods ground policy deliberations and clarify implications of options, while qualified, trained panelists make the final judgment. The report applies this by pairing the analytic methods of Chapters II–IV with guidelines drawn from standard-setting best practices. (Cook et al. (2012))
