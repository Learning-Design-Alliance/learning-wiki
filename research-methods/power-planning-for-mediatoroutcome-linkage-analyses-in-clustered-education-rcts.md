## Description
Because the article develops power formulas specifically for exploratory analyses linking teacher practice outcomes to student achievement outcomes in clustered school-based RCTs, it implies that researchers should evaluate power for these analyses when designing studies. The simulated power analysis exists to inform such assessments, indicating that typical RCTs may face power limitations for linking impacts on teacher practice and student achievement.
