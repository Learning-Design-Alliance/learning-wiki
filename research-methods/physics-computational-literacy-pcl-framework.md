# Physics Computational Literacy (PCL) framework

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Physics Computational Literacy (PCL), put forward by Odden et al. based on diSessa's broader computational literacy framework, treats computing skills as a kind of literacy akin to reading, writing, or mathematical literacy. It has three aspects: "Material (MCL) How people practically use computing, such as through the act of coding", cognitive (how people understand the computing they are doing and use it to understand physics), and social (how people work together with computing and communicate what they are doing). The authors use PCL as the primary lens for coding interviews and find it "particularly well suited to the task of breaking the experience and process of learning computational physics into manageable pieces".
