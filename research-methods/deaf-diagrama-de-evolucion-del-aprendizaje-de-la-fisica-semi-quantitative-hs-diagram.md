# DEAF (Diagrama de Evolución del Aprendizaje de la Física) semi-quantitative H–S diagram

## Description
The Diagrama de Evolución del Aprendizaje de la Física (DEAF) is a geometric technique that plots normalized performance S on the horizontal axis and homogeneity H on the vertical axis across consecutive applications of a multiple-choice test. The article states that "El DEAF permite estudiar el comportamiento global o particular de un sistema en cuanto al aprendizaje disciplinar de conceptos concretos de física elemental se refiere." The diagram defines zones delimited by points ABCDA, with the semi-rect AC indicating the most efficient learning trajectory toward high performance and high homogeneity.
