# Assessment triangle framework

## Description
The assessment triangle is a framework from the National Research Council for designing and evaluating educational assessments systematically. As the article prints it, "It consists of three connected aspects: 1) cognition: a model or theory of what students should learn; 2) observation: an assessment that elicits evidence of their knowledge and understanding; and 3) interpretation: inferences about what each student knows or understands based on that evidence." The authors used it to structure QISCIT's development, addressing cognition through concept identification and observation and interpretation through item generation.
