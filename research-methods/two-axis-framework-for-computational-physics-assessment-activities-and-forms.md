## Description
The article organizes computational physics assessment along two axes: assessment activities, broad task categories such as exams or assignments, and assessment forms, the focus and manner of assessment. The authors' insight is that "these two aspects of assessment are related but somewhat independent of one another; they can be combined in different ways," so instructors can mix and match them for better coverage.
