# Coherence analysis framework and its six activity-trace measures

## Description
Coherence analysis (CA) is the paper's approach to analyzing learners' problem-solving processes in OELEs by "combining information gleaned from sequences of student actions to produce measures of action coherence." CA assumes that when students encounter information that could improve their solution, they should act on it; edits not justified by recent views are labeled unsupported edits. Six measures are derived from activity traces: edits/annotations per minute, unsupported edit percentage, information viewing percentage, potential generation percentage, used potential percentage, and disengaged percentage.

- **Coherence analysis (CA) as a theory-driven measure of metacognitive strategy use from trace data**: Coherence analysis is a method that evaluates metacognitive strategies by examining the sequential alignment of students' learning actions within online learning contexts. The article states CA "examines how learning activities are sequentially connected in a way that indicates metacognitive strategies such as self-reflection and planning". In this study, the authors use CA to quantify coherent reading (reviewing material after a quiz) and coherent quiz (preparatory engagement before a quiz) as metacognitive strategies, counting time devoted to related content within a five-minute window.
