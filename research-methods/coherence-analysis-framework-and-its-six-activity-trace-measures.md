# Coherence analysis framework and its six activity-trace measures

## Description
Coherence analysis (CA) is the paper's approach to analyzing learners' problem-solving processes in OELEs by "combining information gleaned from sequences of student actions to produce measures of action coherence." CA assumes that when students encounter information that could improve their solution, they should act on it; edits not justified by recent views are labeled unsupported edits. Six measures are derived from activity traces: edits/annotations per minute, unsupported edit percentage, information viewing percentage, potential generation percentage, used potential percentage, and disengaged percentage.
