Precursor question relation

The precursor question relation is the article's formal account of empirical relationships between test items: question A is a precursor of B when f(A,B)=P(B=1|A=0)/P(B=1|A=1) falls below a threshold, an "adaptation of the relative risk index to the context of a multiple-choice test" with Cohen's d
