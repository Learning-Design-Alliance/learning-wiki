# A taxonomy of digital tutoring defined by tutor location, synchronicity, instruction driver, and curriculum location

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article develops a categorization system for digital providers to enable rigorous estimation of OST tutoring program effects. It classifies programs along four dimensions: tutor location (online/phone vs face-to-face), tutor synchronicity (asynchronous vs synchronous), instruction driver (curriculum-based software, tutor with software, or tutor without software), and curriculum location (Internet-accessed, locally installed software, or nondigital resources). The taxonomy was built iteratively from provider applications to the state of Texas and cross-checked with district administrative data, defining "digital" as services in which students directly interact with digital technology at least as sophisticated as an iPod.
