# Scoping review methodology (Arksey and O'Malley framework, advanced by Levac et al.)

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review employed a scoping review approach designed to 'map' literature in a particular area, illuminating key concepts and main sources and types of evidence. The authors used "the methodologically rigorous scoping review approach proposed by Arksey and O'Malley and advanced by Levac, Colquhoun, and O'Brien", involving five stages: identifying research questions, identifying relevant studies, study selection, charting the data, and collating, summarizing and reporting results. Unlike systematic reviews, it does not explicitly aim to assess study quality but can identify research gaps.
