## Description
The review proposes a guiding framework identifying three major design decisions for scholars using spatial skills in mathematics education research: selecting a spatial ability taxonomy, selecting corresponding analytical frameworks, and selecting spatial tasks for assessing spatial performance. The review states this framework "is intended to provide educational researchers and practitioners with a common language and decision-making process for conducting research and instruction that engages learners' spatial abilities," aiming to improve understanding of associative and causal links between spatial and mathematical abilities.
