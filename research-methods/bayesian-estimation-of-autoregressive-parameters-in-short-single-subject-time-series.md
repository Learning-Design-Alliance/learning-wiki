## Description
The authors state a limitation of their classical procedures: single-subject designs are very short time series and this presents a problem in estimating the parameters in the autoregressive process assumed for the errors. They further state that the Bayesian approach described has the potential for overcoming this limitation, positioning the Bayesian estimation route as the response to this boundary condition.
