# A covariate-based IRT framework grounded in generalized linear mixed modeling

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article organizes item response modeling within a framework in which "an IRT framework is described based on generalized linear mixed modeling". The framework has two organizing aspects: the kind of covariates (person, item, or person-by-item mode, external or internal to responses) and the kind of effects they have (fixed or random, and if random, across persons or items). It serves as the organizing structure from which the paper derives the model families it explains with lmer code.
