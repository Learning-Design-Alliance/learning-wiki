# EER contexts of emergence: jumpstart versus shift in direction

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Within the EER framework, a regulatory move emerges in one of two contexts: a jumpstart, when the group is silent or stagnant and someone breaks the silence to speak or act, or a shift in direction, when the group is actively talking or operating a task and someone brings up a different idea or operation. The authors note the two are related but categorize them to better notice and separate the emergence context.
