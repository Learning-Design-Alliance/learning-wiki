# Three Paths of Student-AI Interaction typology

## Description
A typological framework identifying three qualitatively distinct modes of student-AI engagement: Passive Review (student accepts AI output with minimal scrutiny, unidirectional, limited higher-order opportunity), Direct Question (transactional gap-filling with surface evaluation, moderate opportunity), and Strategic Dialogue (dialogic, recursive, critical, metacognitive engagement with AI as a thinking partner, substantial opportunity). The article states "The typology distinguishes three paths—Passive Review, Direct Question, and Strategic Dialogue" and links them to degrees of cognitive and epistemic agency, mapping onto a developmental trajectory of AI literacy from treating AI as authoritative to maintaining epistemic agency. Its core claim is that learning outcomes depend on which path students are on, not whether they use AI.
