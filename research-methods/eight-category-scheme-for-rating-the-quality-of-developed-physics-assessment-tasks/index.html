Eight-category scheme for rating the quality of developed physics assessment tasks

The study rated each developed task on "speciﬁcity (scale: 0=does not apply; 1=applies), clarity (scale 0/1), correctness (scale 0/1)" — within a set of eight categories that also included not misleading, adequate difficulty, context, relevance to the target concept, and overall quality.
