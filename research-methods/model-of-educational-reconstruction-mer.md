## Description
The Model of Educational Reconstruction (MER) is a framework for science education research and development that the Einstein-First project used to structure its three research questions and development cycles. The article states that "The MER connects research on the science content structure to its educational importance, thereby building on empirical studies of student understanding and initial testing of pilot learning resources in authentic classroom settings." Its three strands — research on teaching and learning, clarification of content, and evaluation of learning resources — organised the project's iterative cycle of trials, identification of weaknesses, and revision of lesson plans and teacher training.
