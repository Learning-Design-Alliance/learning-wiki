## Description
The Model of Educational Reconstruction (MER) is a framework for science education research and development that the Einstein-First project used to structure its three research questions and development cycles. The article states that "The MER connects research on the science content structure to its educational importance, thereby building on empirical studies of student understanding and initial testing of pilot learning resources in authentic classroom settings." Its three strands — research on teaching and learning, clarification of content, and evaluation of learning resources — organised the project's iterative cycle of trials, identification of weaknesses, and revision of lesson plans and teacher training.

- **Model of Educational Reconstruction (MER) as the planning framework for a university course**: The Model of Educational Reconstruction is a science-education planning model the authors transferred from high-school to university course design. It has three components: clarification and analysis of science content, research on teaching and learning including learners' perspectives, and design and evaluation of teaching and learning environments. A key idea is that "domain-specific knowledge has to be transformed into knowledge suitable for educational purposes", via elementarization into key ideas and construction of a content structure for instruction. The authors applied it to select mathematical skills, physical contexts, and learner-perspective considerations for the T1 course. (Korner et al. (2023))
