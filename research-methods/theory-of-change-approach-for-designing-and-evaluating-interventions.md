# Theory of Change approach for designing and evaluating interventions

## Description
A Theory of Change approach, initially developed to evaluate complex initiatives and support long-term behaviour change, starts with the end goal and requires identification of intermediate outcomes through backwards mapping that will, over time, lead to that goal. Causal chains are identified linking an intervention with evidence-based steps towards the desired change, produced iteratively as a Theory of Change diagram (Figure 2, from Davenport et al., 2020). The article advocates that all STEM engagement developers adopt this approach to critically consider whether their activities can achieve their intended aims.
