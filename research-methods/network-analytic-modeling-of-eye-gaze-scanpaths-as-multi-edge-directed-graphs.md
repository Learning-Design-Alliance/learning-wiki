## Description
The article frames visual expertise development through network representations of eye-gaze scanpaths: nodes are fixated Areas of Interest and edges are saccadic transitions, modeled as "multi-edge directed graphs weighted by cumulative frequency of eye-gaze movements". Metrics fall into four categories: basic (node and edge counts), centrality, structural, and entropy measures. Applied longitudinally with time series clustering and mixed modeling, the framework links scanpath structure to learning outcomes and, per the authors, offers "lower computational complexity and greater transparency" than deep learning approaches.
