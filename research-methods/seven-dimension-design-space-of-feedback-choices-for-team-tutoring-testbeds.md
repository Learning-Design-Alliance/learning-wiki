## Description
The article organizes feedback design as seven dimensions realizable in the Surveillance testbed: subject (individual vs. team), target (public vs. private), timing (immediate, after, omitted), type (proactive vs. reactive), specificity (generic vs. specific), tone (positive vs. negative), and style (collaborative vs. competitive). "Table 1 illustrates different dimensions of feedback that can be explored." The pattern lets researchers vary feedback systematically, since feedback at too high a frequency could be overwhelming and ignored, while immediate delivery has been reported effective under higher cognitive load.
