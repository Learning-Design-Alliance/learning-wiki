## Description
The article recommends that a framework encompassing multiple levels, functional networks, and their dynamic interactions across time (illustrated by its Fig. 1) be used across studies and disciplines. It states "a common framework is essential in order to allow communication between the experts at each level", enabling researchers to specify which levels and networks their study addresses and take into account constraints and modulatory influences from other levels. It also supports exploratory research by relating findings to specific parts of the multilevel model.
