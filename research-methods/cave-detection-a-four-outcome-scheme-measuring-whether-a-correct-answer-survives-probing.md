# Cave detection: a four-outcome scheme measuring whether a correct answer survives probing

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
Cave detection is the article's proposed measurement framework for robustness of understanding. A cave is "a correct first idea abandoned under a single changed case that adds no new information". For each law the agent records whether the first committed answer was correct and whether it changed under probing, giving four outcomes: held, caved, self-corrected, and misconception survived. A right-or-wrong item scores held and caved identically and self-corrected and misconception-survived identically, so the within-pair difference is lost. The scheme is proposed, not validated.
