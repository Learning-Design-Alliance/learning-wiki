# Citation-clustering bibliometric analysis of academic performance research

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Using CitNetExplorer's clustering function (Van Eck's 2012 formula, resolution value 1.0, minimal group size 500), the article organizes the academic performance literature into nine main research groups. As the article states, "By using the clustering function, we found nine groups related to different areas of research in this field: health, psychology, psychosociology, demography, physical activity, sleep patterns, vision, economy, and delinquency." The groups range in size from 591 to 3223 publications, with group 1 (psychological factors: personality, cognitive ability, self-discipline, motivation, demographics) the largest and group 9 (digital devices and social networks) the smallest. Each group is anchored by a most-cited publication, e.g. Hillman et al. 2008 for physical exercise and Curcio et al. 2006 for sleep.
