# Four-aspect classification of metrics and statistics for measuring the quality of test-based exercises

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The article classifies the metrics and statistics usable to measure the quality of test-based exercises from student performance data according to four aspects: whether the results of the exercise are consistent (CIC via Cronbach's alpha, True Weight), whether undesired situations can be inferred (mean, median, standard deviation, skewness, kurtosis), whether the exercise meets the student's level of knowledge (Ease Index, Item Difficulty), and whether the exercise can determine the student's knowledge level (Error Ratio, Standard Error, Random Guess Score, Discrimination Index, Discrimination Efficiency, Item Discrimination Index). This classification, building on Peña (2014), structures both the tool and the evaluation questions.
