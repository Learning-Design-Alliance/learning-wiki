# Sensitivity analysis across estimators and cluster-level weighting schemes for clustered RCTs

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
Because standard errors from alternative estimators of treatment effects in clustered RCTs can differ, the report's key recommendation is that analysts "test the sensitivity of their impact findings using different estimation methods and cluster-level weighting schemes". This guards policy conclusions against dependence on a single estimator choice.
