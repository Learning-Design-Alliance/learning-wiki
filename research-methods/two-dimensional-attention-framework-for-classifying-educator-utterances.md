# Two-dimensional Attention Framework for classifying educator utterances

## Description
The article develops a two-dimensional Attention Framework that classifies each educator utterance along two axes: the recipient of attention (student A, student B, both students, or an ambiguous recipient) and the nature of attention. The nature dimension captures "three key dimensions of instructional practice: Content-focused instruction (discussing academic material), relationship-building (developing rapport and engagement), and session management (coordinating student behaviors and activities)". The taxonomy synthesizes theoretical frameworks from prior education research on instructional attention with empirical observations from the tutoring dataset, mirroring established classroom observation rubrics, and enables quantifying attention as the total duration of utterances directed to a student.
