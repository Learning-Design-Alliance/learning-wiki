# Biber et al.'s 16-variable feedback coding rubric

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report develops an explicit coding rubric that itemizes all important factors varying across feedback studies, developed inductively by reading a wide sample of studies. It includes 16 variables covering research paradigm, design type, target language and population, writing-task genre and length, and five feedback dimensions — the article states that "feedback can be described with respect to five variables: type, focus, tone, mode, and source" — plus outcome measures. The rubric was applied to categorize all studies in the 306-article corpus and to code the subset entering the statistical meta-analysis, providing the basis for subsequent effect-size comparison.
