# Four-theme framework for GoPC communication training research: delivery, clinician outcomes, patient outcomes, and system outcomes

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The review organizes its synthesis of communication training interventions for Goals of Patient Care conversations into four themes with embedded sub-themes. It states that "From these included studies 4 themes with embedded sub-themes were derived: (a) delivery of training programs, (b) clinician outcomes, (c) patient outcomes, and (d) system outcomes." The framework maps how training is delivered and what levels of outcome it affects, guiding the review's synthesis of uni- and multidisciplinary interventions in acute settings.
