# Cognitive Categorization Model (CCM) of the card-sorting process

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The Cognitive Categorization Model (CCM) is a parsimonious random model of the card-sorting process, 'directly based on the rules of the categorization experiment'. It 'has three parameters': the number of questions being categorized, the average number of categories (generated from a shifted binomial distribution), and the probability of categorizing a card into more than one pile. Three variants of that probability (constant, penalized per split, and dependent on the number of categories) were fit to the human sorters' 3-cycle distributions, and the model's graphs qualitatively match human sorter graphs.
