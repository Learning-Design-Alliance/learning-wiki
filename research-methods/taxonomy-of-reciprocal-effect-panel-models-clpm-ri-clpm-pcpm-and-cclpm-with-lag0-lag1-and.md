# Taxonomy of reciprocal effect panel models: CLPM, RI-CLPM, PCPM, and CCLPM with lag0, lag1, and lag2 effects

## Description
The article's methodological contribution is a taxonomy of structural equation models for directional ordering, extending Muthén and Asparouhov's contemporaneous-effects models with latent measurement factors, lag2 paths, random intercepts, and covariate strategies. "We focus on four basic models (Fig. 2): CLPMs (lag1 effects; no RI; no lag0 effects), RI-CLPMs (with lag1 and RI effects, but no lag0 effects), pure contemporaneous panel models with only contemporaneous effects (PCPM; RI and lag0 effects, but no lag1 cross-paths), and reciprocal models (RI-CCLPM; RI, lag0 and lag1 effects)." Lag0 paths are reciprocal paths within each wave; lag2 paths link non-adjacent waves. The models were fit to five annual waves of PALMA data.
