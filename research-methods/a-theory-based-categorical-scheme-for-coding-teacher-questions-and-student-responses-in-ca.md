# A theory-based categorical scheme for coding teacher questions and student responses in case-based learning

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article presents a hierarchical categorical scheme (Table 1) operationalising teacher questions and student responses in CBL. Teacher questions are coded as didactically irrelevant (organisational, comprehension) or relevant, with relevant questions further coded by type (initial vs. follow-up), openness (open vs. closed) and cognitive level (reproduction vs. elaboration/reasoning). Student responses are coded as organisational statements, questions, "reproductive statements", "Elaboration / Reasoning statements", or non-response. Four raters applied the scheme to video data and reached interrater agreement of Cohen's Kappa = .80 after training.
