## Description
The Future Technology Workshop (FTW) is a structured participatory method in which participants with everyday knowledge of a domain envision and design interactions between current and future technology and activity. It organises this exploration as a four-box grid crossing current/future activity with current/future technology, and runs seven sessions: Imagineering, Modelling, Role-play, Retrofit, Everyday, Futurefit and Requirements. The aim is "to reach an informed understanding of how people might interact with technology in the future (box 4 in Table #2) by exploring the possibilities represented by all four boxes".
