# Course deficit model with equity of parity and equity of fairness definitions for evaluating grading policies

## Description
The article uses the course deficit model, under which "observed equity gaps in a course are the result of the structures of the course rather than an underlying deficiency of one group compared to another", so the solution to gaps is altering course structures such as grading policies rather than student behavior. It pairs this with two equity definitions: equity of parity requires all groups to reach the same average outcome, requiring initially lower-scoring groups to benefit more; equity of fairness requires all groups to receive the same average benefit, which maintains pre-existing performance differences. The study evaluates aggregation methods against both definitions.
