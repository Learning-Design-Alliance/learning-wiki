## Description
The article recommends constraining the feasible option set with three kinds of restrictions: structural constraints excluding genuinely impossible options, normative constraints excluding options ruled out by policy or judgment, and statistical constraints excluding rarely observed options via a propensity-score threshold. The authors argue "an unconstrained ODTR model is not merely impractical here but systematically misleading, in that the largest estimated contrasts arise where the data are thinnest", so "feasibility restrictions are therefore part of a defensible estimation strategy rather than a concession to practicality".
