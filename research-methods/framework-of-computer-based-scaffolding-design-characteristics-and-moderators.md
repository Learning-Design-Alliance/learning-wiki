## Description
The article organizes computer-based scaffolding research along several coded dimensions: context of use (problem-based, case-based, design-based, project-based, inquiry-based, modeling/visualization, problem solving), scaffolding change (none, fading, adding, fading/adding), scaffolding logic (none, performance adapted, self-selected, fixed), scaffolding scope (generic or context-specific), and scaffolding intervention type, where "conceptual scaffolding suggests things to consider when addressing the problem" while strategic, metacognitive, and motivation scaffolding boot strategies, self-evaluation, and motivational variables respectively. This framework guided coding of all 333 outcomes and the meta-analysis's moderator questions.
