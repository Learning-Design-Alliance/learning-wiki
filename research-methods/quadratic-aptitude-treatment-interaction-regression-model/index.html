Quadratic aptitude-treatment interaction regression model¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article introduces a multiple regression model in which the aptitude variable enters as a continuous predictor and visual design is coded with two dummy variables, dumOpt and dumSub, each carrying a quadratic interaction term with ability. The relevant coefficients are 'b 41 and b 42 indicating the specific progression of the regression lines presented in Fig. 1', and the five possible coefficient combinations 'can be summarized by the simple hypothesis "b42 > b41" indicating a higher benefit from the optimized visual design than from the suboptimal visual design in the medium ability range'. This approach detects nonlinear interactions that linear moderation tests would miss.
