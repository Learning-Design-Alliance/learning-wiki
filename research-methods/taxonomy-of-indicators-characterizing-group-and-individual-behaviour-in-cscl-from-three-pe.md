# Taxonomy of indicators characterizing group and individual behaviour in CSCL from three perspectives

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article presents a taxonomy of indicators for characterizing group and individual behaviour in CSCL, based on three perspectives: the group in relation to similar groups, an individual in relation to his/her colleagues, and the group itself. The authors describe this taxonomy as the first step in building a CSCL ontology for analysing collaborative learning tasks. The experience was later shared in the Kaleidoscope NoE, and conclusions on indicators for collaborative processes were summarized in a joint article.
