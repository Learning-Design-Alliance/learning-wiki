# Differential Course Functioning (DCF)

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article introduces differential course functioning (DCF) as "a statistical model to detect and quantify variations in course difficulty related to group-level attributes". DCF fits a Rasch model to dichotomized course grades to estimate student traits and course difficulties, then runs a second logistic regression per course testing whether a group encoding predicts achievement beyond trait and difficulty. "Finding a DCF effect indicates that the course exhibits systematic variations in difficulty between the groups." DCF logits are converted into differences in achievement-grade probability at the average student trait for interpretability.
