# Three-approach taxonomy of emotion theory: basic, multidimensional, and academic emotion theory

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review classifies the dominant taxonomies of emotion into three approaches. Basic emotion theory relies on "a small number of discrete, discontinuous states" with plausible evolutionary facial expressions; multidimensional theories (Plutchik's cone, the Geneva Emotional Wheel, Damasio's primary/secondary scheme) map emotions onto geometric axes; academic emotion theory is Pekrun's control-value schema, distinguished by its separate intellectual history and focus on school learning. The review uses this taxonomy to weigh each approach's value for empirical research on emotion and learning.
