## Description
The article's central design recommendation is that "biases that might have once been reasonably ignorable can pose a real threat in evaluations that are powered to detect small impacts". It recommends strategies researchers can use to avoid or mitigate these biases, covering attrition in RCTs, functional form misspecification in RDDs, and all study designs. The article stresses the problem is surmountable so long as it is not ignored, and that findings should not be read as advising against powering for small impacts.
