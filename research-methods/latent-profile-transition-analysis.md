## Description
Latent profile transition analysis models each learner's knowledge at a measurement point as a latent class defined by a profile of scores on multiple indicators (here misconceptions, everyday conceptions, and scientific concepts), and models learners' transitions between profiles over time. The authors argue it "allowed us to define explicit quantitative criteria for knowledge fragmentation or integration, which can be compared across different studies." It handles multiple measures, models knowledge as a latent variable underlying overt behavior, and can reveal gradual as well as abrupt change, addressing methodological weaknesses of interview-category studies.
