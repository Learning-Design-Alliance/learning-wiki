# Stepped-wedge cluster-randomized trial design

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study's evaluation design is a stepped wedge cluster randomized trial in which seven natural clusters of six to nine SHOs are randomized for the moment of training across eight study periods of seven weeks each. "A stepped wedge trial is a cluster-randomized trial in which all study groups (clusters) receive the intervention by a computer-generated random sequential roll-out of the training sessions over time." Advantages named include estimating effects from within- and between-cluster comparisons and controlling for time effects.
