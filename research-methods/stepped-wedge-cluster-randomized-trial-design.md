# Stepped-wedge cluster-randomized trial design

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study's evaluation design is a stepped wedge cluster randomized trial in which seven natural clusters of six to nine SHOs are randomized for the moment of training across eight study periods of seven weeks each. "A stepped wedge trial is a cluster-randomized trial in which all study groups (clusters) receive the intervention by a computer-generated random sequential roll-out of the training sessions over time." Advantages named include estimating effects from within- and between-cluster comparisons and controlling for time effects.

- **Stepped-wedge randomized controlled trial design: 24 Kenyan clinics randomized to four intervention waves stratified by county and facility size**: The SPEED trial uses a stepped-wedge RCT in which all 24 clinics eventually receive the intervention but are "randomized to when the intervention is introduced." Facilities are stratified by county (Nairobi, Kiambu, Kisumu, Homa Bay) and facility size (high vs. medium volume based on the median AYA enrollment), with six clinics per wave approximately 9 months apart. The pragmatic design was chosen because training occurs at the clinic level, uses routine EMR data for outcomes, and simultaneous implementation would not be feasible; only the biostatistician holds the randomization assignments. (Wilson Kate S. et al. (2017))
