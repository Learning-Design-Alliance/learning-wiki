# Applied Cognitive Task Analysis (ACTA)

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
ACTA is an interview technique designed to identify the key cognitive elements required to perform expert tasks, typically in three phases: developing a task diagram, a knowledge audit interview, and data analysis with knowledge representation. Task Diagrams contain three to seven steps aligned with short-term memory; the Knowledge Audit probes aspects of expertise such as big picture, noticing, job smarts, improvising, self-monitoring, and anomalies. Findings are represented in a Cognitive Demands Table highlighting common challenges, errors, and solutions, which can inform future training and job design. ACTA is streamlined and suited to instances in which time is limited.
