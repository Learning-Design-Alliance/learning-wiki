(Kevenaar et al. (2023))
- **ACE-beta twin models as a design for estimating responsiveness while controlling for unobserved genetic and social confounding**: The ACE-beta twin model is a biometric structural equation approach that decomposes variance into additive genetic (A), shared environmental (C) and non-shared environmental (E) components estimated as latent factors, and additionally estimates phenotypic pathways of interest. The article uses it to estimate the responsiveness of educational expectations to performance "by estimating these confounders as latent variables within the model, providing an direct estimate of social and genetic confounding". It is conceptually comparable to the MZ fixed-effects model but explicitly models the unobserved confounders. (Mirko Ruks (2023))
