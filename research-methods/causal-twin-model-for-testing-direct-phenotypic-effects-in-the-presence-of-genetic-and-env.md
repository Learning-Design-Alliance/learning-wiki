## Description
The article fits a causal twin model, depicted in Fig. 2, that estimates the putatively causal regression coefficients of self-control and grit on school performance while allowing background A, D, or E correlations (dashed double-headed arrows) between the traits. The article explains that "the strongest support for the causal hypothesis would be the finding that the phenotypic regression coefficients ... are significant, while the background correlations are all zero". Because causal effects and confounding are not mutually exclusive, the model fits the phenotypic regression while permitting A, D, or E confounding, with the classical twin design supplying the decomposition into additive genetic, dominance, and environmental components.
