## Description
The article presents a flow-diagram framework for building and evaluating statistical models in physics education research, covering raw data description, data encoding and transformation, train/test splitting, comparison of multiple statistical models, out-of-sample performance evaluation, and explanation of the final model. The authors state they "oﬀer a framework for the PER community that considers data management, model evaluation, and results communication holistically" and that "The framework we present is model agnostic, that is, it will work for traditional linear models, as well as more complex “machine learning” models." It is grounded in the PRPER focused collection on quantitative methods and modern statistics and machine learning practice.
