# Thick description as the analytic basis for understanding transactive discourse

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Stahl draws on Austin and Geertz's notion of thick description: CSCL researchers understand transaction meanings because they share a community with their subjects. He contrasts "a thin description of someone's eye twitching vs. a thick description of someone winking" — a wink is a meaningful transaction requiring subtle cultural knowledge, unlike objective physiological description. Computers cannot formulate or understand thick descriptions of discourse moves.
