# Disaggregated subgroup analysis of instructional effectiveness

## Description
The authors warn that instruction judged effective by peers, pass rates and headline cohort-averaged pre/post gains can hide failing sub-groups: such instruction 'may be far from a panacea for some of the particular sub-groups one would like to target especially'. They demonstrated this by splitting cohorts into pre-instruction FCI quartiles and gender sub-cohorts, exposing that roughly half of female students started in the lowest quartile and mostly stayed there despite substantial cohort-averaged normalised gains.
