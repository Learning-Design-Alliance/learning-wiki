## Description
The article frames learning a successive theory through patterns of change affecting concepts (ontology) and mathematical constructs (representational role): "New formation may entail, e.g., coalescence or differentiation of familiar notion in nonintuitive terms", evolution creates difficulty discriminating old and new versions, and disappearance may deprive students of resources. Change in exemplars is related to knowledge fragmentation. This taxonomy distinguishes successive-theory change from naïve-to-scientific conceptual change.
