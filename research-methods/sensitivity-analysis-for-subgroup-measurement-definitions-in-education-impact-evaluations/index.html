Sensitivity analysis for subgroup measurement definitions in education impact evaluations¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that subgroup measurement decisions in preschool evaluations be deliberate and tested rather than default. The authors "recommend that researchers be clear on which possible special needs subgroup they are interested in studying" and that analytic decisions be guided by hypotheses about why students identified under a definition might experience programs differently. They further advise that "future studies should formally assess the impact of any measurement decisions", both by describing subgroup composition under each definition and by testing estimate sensitivity.
