# Six patterns of theoretical integration in EER research

## Description
The review develops a six-category typology of how deeply EER articles integrate theory, classifying each of the 24 included articles along four dimensions: didactic considerations, implementation of a specific EER, theoretical foundation connected with didactic considerations, and empirical verification of the theoretical foundation. The patterns range from "general statements on theoretical grounding in EERs without connection to didactic considerations" through loose connections, combined theoretical-didactic treatments without a specific EER or without empirical validation, up to "theoretical grounding with empirical validation". Each pattern is labeled with the dominant category of its articles.
