# Selection-adjusted observational comparison of optional instructional components

## Description
Because labs are almost always paired with lecture and students self-select into them, prior small studies comparing pedagogies within labs confound the lab treatment itself. The authors apply a bounding strategy comparing unconditional and conditional correlations across increasingly restrictive regressions with lecture-section fixed effects and student covariates, arguing that if the correlation shrinks as controls are added, "selection bias is inflating the correlation." This design moves observational studies toward more credible causal interpretation.
