# Combined Kirkpatrick–Miller framework for simulation-based training outcomes

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The study uses a conceptual framework combining Kirkpatrick's Learning Evaluation Model with Miller's Clinical Assessment Framework to guide assessment of the MTU training. Kirkpatrick's four levels (reaction, learning, behavior, results) are mapped onto Miller's competence levels (knows, knows how, shows how, does): Kirkpatrick's learning level corresponds to the bottom three Miller levels, and Kirkpatrick's behavior level is closely related to Miller's does. The study examined Kirkpatrick's reaction and learning (knows and shows how), deliberately excluding knows how due to anticipated retention challenges, and excluding behavior, does, and results because the study was limited to an experimental setting.
