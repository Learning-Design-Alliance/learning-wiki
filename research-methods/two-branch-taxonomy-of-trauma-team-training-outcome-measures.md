# Two-branch taxonomy of trauma team training outcome measures

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The review organizes trauma team training evaluation into trained team-related outcomes — team-specific measures such as 'error rates (the appropriate time, the correct order, the correct frequency)' and 'self-reported outcomes such as team performance, collaboration, and knowledge', plus time-related measures such as time to surveys, intubation, CT, and OR — and patient-specific outcome measures of mortality, morbidity, complication rate, and length of stay. It uses this taxonomy to ask whether training effects transfer to patients.
