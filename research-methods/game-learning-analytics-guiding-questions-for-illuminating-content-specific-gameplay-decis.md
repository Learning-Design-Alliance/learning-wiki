## Description
The article proposes that its three approaches — looking at "actions and tool use, play patterns and player types, and tool input patterns" — could ground "a set of more generalized guiding questions in the GLA field that could be adopted by learning games designers and data scientists". The framework organizes analytics around identifying content-specific gameplay decisions, i.e. methods of completing game tasks related to a domain, so that designers and data scientists can convey information about how students think within a game's subject matter.
