# Two kinds of BESM talk coding taxonomy

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article identifies a taxonomy of BESM talk emerging from student utterances in natural settings: 'We identify two basic kinds of BESM talk within the physicist speech genre: “expectations BESM talk”, and “self-eﬃcacy BESM talk”.' Expectations talk encompasses students' expectations about technical and cultural physics practices and has four subcategories — understanding, confusion, spotting inconsistencies, and generalized expectations. Self-efficacy talk positions the student within the field through statements of belief in their own abilities. The categories overlap with CLASS and MPEX dimensions but emerged from discourse rather than survey factor analysis.
