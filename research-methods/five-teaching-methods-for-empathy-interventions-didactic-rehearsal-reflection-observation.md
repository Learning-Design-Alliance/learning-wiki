## Description
The article organizes behavioural empathy interventions around five teaching methods used singly or in combination: "didactic (lecture and reading and/or written information), rehearsal (practicing the empathy skill and/or role-playing as the patient), reflection (reflecting by oneself or in discussion with others), observation (watching enactments pertaining to the skill and/or exposure to a patient's perspective), and feedback (individualized from peer or facilitator)". Each method is mapped to a type of learning (surface, deep, or both) and a theoretical basis, such as cognitive, experiential, transformative, and social learning theories. The network meta-analysis uses combinations of these five methods as its treatment nodes.
