Two-dimensional taxonomy of peer grading systems: evaluation approaches and reviewer weighting strategies¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The review develops a taxonomy organizing peer-grading systems along two orthogonal dimensions: Evaluation Approaches (summative feedback via rating, ranking, and categorization, plus formative feedback) and Reviewer Weighting Strategies (calibration, back-evaluation, consensus, and reputation). It was built bottom-up: two authors independently analyzed all 122 included studies, identified features (11 and 10, with eight overlapping), and refined them through iterative discussion into the consolidated dimensions. The taxonomy serves as "a descriptive framework and a critical lens to assess the strengths and limitations of various peer-grading approaches across multiple factors such as accuracy, graders learning outcomes, engagement, and scalability."
