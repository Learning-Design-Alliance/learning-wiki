# A priori taxonomy of three inference types defined by how interdependent information is distributed between collaborators

## Description
The article distinguishes three types of solution-relevant inferences, defined a priori by the experimental distribution of two interdependent pieces of information in a dyad: "Collaborative inferences" result from unshared, distributed information and "can only be drawn collaboratively"; individual inferences result from unshared information located with the same person; shared inferences result from information known to both partners. The taxonomy allows systematic comparison of inference difficulty, since the distribution is manipulated experimentally.
