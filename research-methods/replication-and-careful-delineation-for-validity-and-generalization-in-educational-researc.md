## Description
The article states that educational research is largely ex post facto and needs to eliminate opinions in favour of facts, since "Educational research needs to eliminate from its studies the opinions to demonstrate the facts if it aspires to be scientific." It also recommends replication as validation, and outlines generalization considerations: generalization cannot seek universal laws, must proceed in parallel between individuals and groups, and requires careful delineation of phenomena, precise measurement and validated theories.
