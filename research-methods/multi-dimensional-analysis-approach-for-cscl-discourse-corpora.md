## Description
The article proposes a multi-dimensional approach to analyzing argumentative knowledge construction in CSCL. It proceeds in stages, covering "sampling and segmentation of the discourse corpora" before applying the analysis of the four process dimensions (participation, epistemic, argumentative, social mode). The approach serves researchers who need to evaluate the effects of instructional supports such as collaboration scripts on discourse processes.
