# Four-domain taxonomy of soft skills teaching research in undergraduate dental education

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article organises the literature on soft skills teaching in undergraduate dental education into a revised coding map with four main domains: dentist-patient relationship, cooperation, life balance and clinical reasoning, each with subcategories such as professional attitude, communication, vulnerability, interprofessionalism, teamwork, leadership, personal confidence, management, managing feelings, critical thinking and key steps. The map was built from predefined frameworks (ModES, WHO, UNESCO, ADEE, Santé Publique France) and iteratively refined against the 317 included articles. The authors state the papers "were classified into four domains (i.e. dentist-patient relationship, cooperation, life balance and clinical reasoning)".
