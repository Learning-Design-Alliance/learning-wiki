# Sequential randomized trials (SRTs)

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
SRTs are trials in which "an individual is randomized multiple times throughout the course of the trial", producing varied treatment sequences that can be compared for sequencing, timing, and personalization questions. The article presents SRTs as exploratory designs whose resulting adaptive intervention can later be confirmed in a simple A/B trial, and distinguishes two common variants: SMARTs, where re-randomization depends on response to prior treatment, and micro-randomized trials for frequently delivered interventions. SRTs differ from adaptive designs and reinforcement learning in that randomization probabilities are fixed in advance.
