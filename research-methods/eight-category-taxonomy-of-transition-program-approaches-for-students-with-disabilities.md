# Eight-category taxonomy of transition program approaches for students with disabilities

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review organizes transition programs into eight mutually exclusive categories, realigned from the 15 NSTTAC predictor categories retained after eliminating exit exam requirements. The categories are: career and technical education; career awareness or development programs; community-based work experience programs; employment while in school; functional life-skills development programs; inclusion in general education; interagency collaboration programs; and student support including parent involvement. This taxonomy structures the review's reporting of evidence by program category and outcome domain.
