## Description
The article adapts Bell and Gilbert's framework of three interrelated development domains — personal, social and professional — to study how physics faculty grow as teachers. Originally built to assess one professional development program, it is extended here: the authors write that they extend the framework by considering the multifaceted, context-laden, trajectories of physics faculty as they develop their teaching practices.
