# Six-criterion operational checklist for classifying a system as agentic

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review operationalizes agentic AI with a checklist of six verifiable criteria covering autonomy, reasoning, memory, planning, goal-directed interaction and related capabilities. A system counts as agentic if it meets at least four criteria: "In this survey, a system is considered agentic if it satisfies at least four of these criteria." The threshold is intended to exclude reactive chatbots while accommodating diverse architectures; the authors give the example of a tutoring system that plans multi-turn lessons, remembers learner progress, triggers prompts autonomously and provides formative feedback.
