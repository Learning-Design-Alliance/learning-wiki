## Description
Instructional Systems Design is a general-purpose task decomposition methodology widely used by the Air Force since 1972, in which duties are broken into tasks, subtasks, and supporting skills and knowledge via expert observation and documentation review. The report states "The output from an ISD analysis closely parallels the condition-action rules discussed earlier as 'how-to-do-it' or procedural knowledge." Its documentation form and flowchart (Table 1, Figure 2) list steps, decisions, and supporting skills and knowledges for tasks such as isolating defective parts in power supply circuits.
