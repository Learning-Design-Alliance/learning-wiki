# Three-category taxonomy of educational research evidence

## Description
The article organizes science education evidence into three research categories with different methodological strengths and different implications for policy. Program-based studies evaluate whole programs with pre-post or business-as-usual designs; controlled studies "alter only one target factor at a time to examine the effectiveness of an instructional procedure over alternatives" using random assignment; correlational studies use large datasets such as TIMSS and PISA to examine associations over large populations. The article argues the three categories yield divergent findings about inquiry-based teaching and that policy should draw on all three.
