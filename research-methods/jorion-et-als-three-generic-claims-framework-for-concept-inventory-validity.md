# Jorion et al.'s three-generic-claims framework for concept-inventory validity

## Description
A validation framework for concept inventories, attributed to Jorion and colleagues, that the authors follow to evaluate the CLCI. It holds that "three generic claims underlie the common uses of CIs in classrooms and educational research": that CI scores indicate overall understanding of all concepts, understanding of specific concepts, and propensity for misconceptions or student errors. The framework prescribes a categorical judgement scheme and assignment rules, with distractor, classical test theory, item response theory, and structural analyses as supporting evidence.
