# Simulated power analysis for clustered education RCTs

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article applies its developed statistical power formulas in a simulated power analysis of clustered school-based RCTs of education interventions. The simulation examines whether typical RCTs have sufficient statistical power for linking impacts on teacher practice and student achievement outcomes, using "ordinary least squares and instrumental variable estimators." The simulation is the empirical demonstration of the formulas' implications for study design.
