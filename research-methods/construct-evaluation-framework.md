## Description
A framework for evaluating the characteristics of psychological constructs prior to automated coding, grounded in educational psychology measurement practices and validity theories (Cronbach & Meehl, 1955; Kane, 2013; Messick, 1995). It comprises three dimensions — "Operational Definition," "Empirical Grounding," and "Conceptual Integrity" — each with sub-dimensions such as clarity, specificity, granularity, concreteness, objectivity, measurability, theoretical coherence, context sensitivity, and sensitivity to individual sources of variability. The authors used it to rate persistence, self-efficacy, interest, perceived challenge, and prior KSAs, finding persistence among the less well-defined constructs.
