# Adapted virtual patient classification framework with seven classes described on two levels: predominant competency and predominant technology

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article adapts Talbot et al.'s seven-class virtual patient taxonomy into a one-dimensional classification with two levels of description. Using a deductive content analysis approach, the authors "decreased the number of levels in Talbot's classification to the two most fundamental: educational ('predominant competency') and technological ('predominant technology')". The seven classes are Case Presentation, Interactive Patient Scenario, VP Game, High Fidelity Software Simulation, Human Standardized Patient, High Fidelity Manikin, and Virtual Standardized Patient, each labelled with its predominant competency (e.g. clinical reasoning, patient communication skills) and predominant technology (e.g. multimedia systems, virtual worlds, conversational characters).
