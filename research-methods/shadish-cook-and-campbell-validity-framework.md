## Description
The paper uses the Shadish, Cook, and Campbell (2002) validity framework to organize how design and analysis complications threaten research conclusions. It distinguishes internal, external, construct, and statistical conclusion validity, applying each to decisions such as network-identified treatment schools, within-pair analysis, IPTW weighting, and missing-data weights. The framework is used "to describe the implications of different complications and decisions during the data collection and analysis phases of the study."
