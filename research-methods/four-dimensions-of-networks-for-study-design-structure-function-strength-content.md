# Four dimensions of networks for study design: structure, function, strength, content

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
As a brainstorming tool for conceptualizing what interests a researcher about a network, the article adopts Perry et al.'s four dimensions: "structure, such as the network's size or patterns of ties", function (type of exchanges or resources available), strength (relative durability or variety in bonds), and content (attitudes and beliefs present in the network). All relate to the dual node-and-link nature of network systems. The article notes many PER analyses foreground structure or function, and suggests strength and content dimensions—such as counts of strong ties or colleagues' views about teaching—could inform retention and departmental reform questions.
