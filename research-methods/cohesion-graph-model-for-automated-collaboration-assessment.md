## Description
The article's first proposed model represents collaboration through "a cohesion graph" over the conversation. It can be perceived as "a longitudinal analysis of the ongoing conversation", accounting for collaboration from a social knowledge-building perspective. The model is one of the two approaches implemented and validated in ReaderBench for automated collaboration assessment in CSCL chats.
