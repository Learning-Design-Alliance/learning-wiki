## Description
The article organises simulation fidelity into three categories, where fidelity refers to "how closely the simulation resembles the actual situation being replicated". Environmental fidelity concerns the situation in which the simulation takes place, equipment fidelity how closely tools resemble clinical practice, and psychological fidelity how faithfully the simulation mimics emotional and behavioural aspects. The article uses this taxonomy to orient novices to simulation terminology.

- **Simulation fidelity framework: technical, environmental and psychological aspects of simulation-based training**: The article uses the framework of "simulation fidelity" to describe SBMT, comprising three aspects. "Equipment fidelity describes the degree to which simulation replicates reality", often focusing on mannequin fidelity (low- versus high-technology). Environmental fidelity describes the extent of environmental reality of the session location, distinguishing simulation centers from in-situ training in the actual clinical environment. Psychological fidelity describes participants' perceived reality, governed by equipment and environmental fidelity, scenario content and team composition. (Stocker M et al. (2017))
