Three-dimensional parental scaffolding framework¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 4 claims (1 for, 3 mixed) · 4 studies (2 associational, 1 causal, 1 theoretical), q1–q3 · 2 of 4 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article organises parental scaffolding during joint problem-solving into three aspects: "cognitive support, emotional support, and contingency". Cognitive support covers parents' provision of metacognitive information such as task management strategies and rationales; emotional support covers encouragement and rejection; contingency is the degree to which parents adjust instructional demand in response to children's ongoing evidence of task understanding, operationalised via the shift rule of raising demand after success and lowering it after failure. The framework structures the study's coding and regression analyses.
