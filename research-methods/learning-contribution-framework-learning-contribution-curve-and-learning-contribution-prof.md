# Learning Contribution Framework (Learning Contribution Curve and Learning Contribution Profile)

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A distributional complement to class-average measures of conceptual learning. Students are ranked by initial achievement; the LCC plots the cumulative fraction of total individual learning gain against the cumulative fraction of students, while the LCP, defined as its slope, expresses local mean contribution relative to the class mean. The article states students are 'ordered from lowest to highest according to pretest performance'.
