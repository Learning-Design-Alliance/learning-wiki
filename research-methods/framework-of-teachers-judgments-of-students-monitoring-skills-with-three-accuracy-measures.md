## Description
The article introduces a framework that integrates teachers' judgments of students' performance, teachers' judgments of students' monitoring judgments, and students' actual performance and monitoring judgments. It defines three measures: 'Teachers' judgment accuracy of students' monitoring judgments is captured with three measures' — SMA-TJ (student monitoring accuracy according to teacher judgments), TJASMJ (teacher judgment accuracy of the student's monitoring judgment), and C-SMA-TJ (correctness of student monitoring accuracy according to teacher judgments). The framework 'applies to every classroom situation, regardless of the proportion of optimists/pessimists/realists' and serves both to conceptualize the constructs and as a guideline for operationalizing them, unlike the earlier categoric approach.
