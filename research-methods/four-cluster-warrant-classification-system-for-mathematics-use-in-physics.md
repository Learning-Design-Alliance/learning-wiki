# Four-cluster warrant classification system for mathematics use in physics

## Description
From an iterative knowledge analysis of about 50 argument snippets, the authors classify students' mathematical justifications into four framing clusters. As they report, "Four main clusters emerged from this data set's examples of physics students' framing of their math use": Calculation, Physical Mapping, Invoking Authority, and Math Consistency. Each cluster is identified primarily by the general class of warrant used (e.g., algorithmic steps yield trustable results; goodness-of-fit between math and physical situation) and secondarily by indicators such as math chaining, demonstrative gesturing, quoting rules, or analogy with another math idea (Table 1).
