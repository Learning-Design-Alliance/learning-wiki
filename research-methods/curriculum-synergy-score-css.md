# Curriculum synergy score (CSS)

## Description
The CSS evaluates whether an algorithmically constructed double-field curriculum achieves deeper integration than the element-wise union of the two single-field curricula. It is the product of the Jensen-Shannon divergence between the union and integrated curriculum vectors, capturing "surprisal" or emergent new information, and a weighted-overlap similarity σ between the two disciplines' optimized course vectors. The article states it "evaluates whether the algorithmically constructed interdisciplinary curriculum Ω (DA ⊗ DB) achieves a deeper level of integration than a simple combination of the two disciplines". Ideal synergy requires a balance between emergent information and decent similarity; too-similar or unrelated pairs both yield CSS ≈ 0.
