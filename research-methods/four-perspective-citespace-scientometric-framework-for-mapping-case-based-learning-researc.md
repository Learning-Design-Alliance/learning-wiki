# Four-perspective CiteSpace scientometric framework for mapping case-based learning research

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A scientometric analysis framework applying CiteSpace software (6.1.6R6) to visualize CBL research in Chinese and international medical education. It operates from four perspectives: "quantity analysis of literature; network analysis of co-occurring authors; network analysis of co-occurring research institutions; keywords clustering and burst analysis." The article uses it to compare Chinese and English corpora and proposes, on that basis, "a novel knowledge-oriented framework" aimed at enhancing effective CBL implementation in medical education.
