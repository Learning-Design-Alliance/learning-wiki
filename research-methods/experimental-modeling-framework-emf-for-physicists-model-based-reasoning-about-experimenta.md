## Description
The Experimental Modeling Framework is the second of two complementary frameworks the authors use to investigate student troubleshooting in upper-division electronics. The article states that "The Experimental Modeling Framework complements this analysis by providing further insight into both the role of model construction in troubleshooting and how students use models to make predictions." In the analyzed episode, applying the EMF places the students' process within a larger cycle of model construction, and the second portion of the episode is described as demonstrative of how students assess proposals for revisions. Detailed EMF results are deferred to a companion paper.
