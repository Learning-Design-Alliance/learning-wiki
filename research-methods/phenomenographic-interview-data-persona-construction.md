# Phenomenographic interview-data persona construction

## Description
A reusable procedure for constructing personas from existing qualitative data, which the authors call 'a new approach': watch a subset of interviews for emergent themes, repeatedly analyze the whole set for variation within each theme, fill a spreadsheet of candidate motives, flesh out sketches with names and short descriptive quotes, and 'revised and validated the set of personas by matching back to the data.' Constant comparison among personas and data keeps them distinct and collectively embodying the phenomenographic results.

- **Personas methodology as a user-centered design approach for professional development resources**: Personas are "person-like constructs created from data of a group of potential users, which are synthesized into archetypes," each built around a common goal and created in sets representing users' most important goals. The article uses personas to design targeted professional development resources for informal physics practitioners, noting benefits including preserving participant confidentiality and representing real users rather than designer assumptions. Users can identify with multiple personas depending on motivations, needs, and context. (Shams El-Adawy et al. (2023))
