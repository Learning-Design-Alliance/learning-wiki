## Description
The article describes a reusable development sequence underpinned by PBPT principles: a team of clinicians, academics, and researchers developed the metric "using an iterative six‐stage process, culminating in a Delphi panel meeting". A scoping review supplied background perspective; forehead-mounted GoPro video recordings of real and simulated procedures were repeatedly and iteratively deconstructed into steps, errors, and sentinel errors; the metric was stress tested for clarity, scorability, and inter-rater reliability; and a Delphi panel verified face and content validity. The authors state this methodology provides a framework for developing validated metrics and performance standards.
