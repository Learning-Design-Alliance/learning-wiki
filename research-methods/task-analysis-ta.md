## Description
Task analysis (TA) is described by the article as "a systematic qualitative and quantitative process to generate information about activities or processes (tasks) that must be implemented to achieve an objective". A task is an action with an identifiable beginning and end, a measurable component of job responsibilities spanning cognitive, manual and affective domains. In this study TA combined a knowledge consensus workshop with a stratified national field survey in which respondents rated the frequency with which they performed each of 233 tasks, using a 5-point scale with subcategories for tasks never performed.
