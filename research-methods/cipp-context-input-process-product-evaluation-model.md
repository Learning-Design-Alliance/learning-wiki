## Description
The study used the Context, Input, Process, Product (CIPP) model as the framework to evaluate the hybrid PA programme. The article states it "provided a framework to establish the effectiveness and/or success of the hybrid model" and that it "particularly focuses on the inputs and process of an innovation". Input activities included additional cameras and microphones, adjusted timetabling for twice-monthly on-campus days, and ongoing communication; Process involved mixed methods data collection; Product compared hybrid and on-campus student outcomes.
