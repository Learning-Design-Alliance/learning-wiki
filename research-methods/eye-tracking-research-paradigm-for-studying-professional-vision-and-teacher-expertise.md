## Description
The article characterizes an emerging research paradigm in which eye-tracking methodologies are used to study professional vision and teacher expertise. It argues the paradigm brings together "different and originally separated strands of research": professional vision in teacher education, expertise structure and development in cognitive science, eye-tracking study of cognitive processes in psychology, and educational effectiveness and instructional quality research. The seven special-issue contributions instantiate this paradigm.
