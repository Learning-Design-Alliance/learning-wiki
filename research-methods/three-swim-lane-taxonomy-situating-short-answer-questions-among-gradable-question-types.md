## Description
The review defines short answer questions within a hierarchical view of question types organized under three swim lanes: depth of learning, question category, and question type. Depth of learning separates recognition from recall questions; question category separates natural language from structured text and speech; question type distinguishes short answers from fill-the-gap and essay questions using the dimensions of length, focus, and openness.
