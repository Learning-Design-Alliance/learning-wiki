Benefit-cost analysis framework for SEL interventions¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article outlines a benefit-cost analysis framework for SEL interventions in which costs and benefits are compared in monetary values to derive a net present value. Costs are estimated with "the ingredients method (Levin & McEwan, 2001)", where each component of the intervention is investigated in detail. Benefits require linking a measured SE competence to a later outcome with a shadow price; the framework explicitly addresses temporal dynamics — the "ratchet effect" (how fast benefits occur) and the "fade-out effect" (how long they last) — and general spill-over effects on the school, which "should be addressed when calculating the benefits of an intervention".
