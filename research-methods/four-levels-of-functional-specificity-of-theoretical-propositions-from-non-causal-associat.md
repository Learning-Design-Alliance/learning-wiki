# Four levels of functional specificity of theoretical propositions, from non-causal association to functionally specified causal effects

## Description
The article proposes that propositions differ in specificity, which determines their falsifiability. Table 1 depicts four levels: non-causal association, weak causal effect, moderate causal effect with specified direction, and strong causal effect with a defined functional form. The author notes that "almost all propositions of the motivation theories addressed are located on Levels 1–3" and argues that moving to Level 4 would make contradictions between theories and between theory and evidence easier to detect.
