## Description
The article proposes "another lens to psychometrics: grading validity," extending psychometric evaluation from test items to the grading process itself when AI assigns the scores. It comprises two components: the rules for grading, instantiated as the prompts given to the AI system, and the grading judgement, meaning the inference and reasoning performance of the AI system. The framework assumes the exam items themselves have established content and construct validity, isolating grading error for study. It is applied to rubric items, where a test taker may or may not receive credit on each item.
