# CLICS framework for translating AI-mediated learning traces into auditable CME/CPD evidence

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
CLICS is a conceptual framework integrating AI-mediated interaction, learning-trace capture, the PIS-7 rubric, human calibration, and credit conversion into a coordinated CME/CPD infrastructure. It makes 3 conceptual shifts: "from time-based toward evidence-informed CME; from AI as a learning assistant alone toward AI-assisted evaluation of learning traces, subject to human calibration and oversight; and from isolated learning activities toward practice-embedded professional learning infrastructure." It is positioned as complementary to, not a replacement for, traditional CME.
