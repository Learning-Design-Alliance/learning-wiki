# Instructor Talk framework: five categories and 17 subcategories of noncontent instructor language

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Instructor Talk is defined as "any language used by an instructor that is not directly related to the concepts under study but instead focuses on creating the learning environment." The framework organizes this noncontent talk into five emergent categories—Building the Instructor/Student Relationship, Establishing Classroom Culture, Explaining Pedagogical Choices, Sharing Personal Experiences, and Unmasking Science—each containing two to five subcategories, for 17 subcategories in total. It was built via grounded theory coding of 666 instructor quotes from 29 class sessions of a cotaught introductory biology course, and the authors propose it as a novel research variable for studying instructor effectiveness, student resistance, stereotype threat, and instructor immediacy.
