## Description
The EER framework categorizes regulation target areas, adopting Sobocinski et al.'s monitoring categories and adding a social category from the authors' own observations. One EER instance can address one or multiple target areas, and Table 1 gives descriptions and inquiry examples for each area. The addition of the social area reflects tensions and interactions between group members observed in the IMPRESS data.
