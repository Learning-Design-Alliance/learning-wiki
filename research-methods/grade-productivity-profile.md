# Grade productivity profile

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The guide introduces a value-added framework in which each school grade's productivity profile presents "three inter-related pieces of information for each school grade." Input status is the previous year's matched test scores, output is end-of-year scores for the same stable students, and the gain is the improvement between them. Profiles are built year by year from 1987 to 1996, smoothed with best-fitting trend lines, and used to read school improvement over time.

## Description

Input status is the previous year's matched test scores, output is end-of-year scores for the same stable students, and the gain is the improvement between them. ## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires matching student scores across two consecutive years in the same school, so grades with few matched students (e.g., second and third grades where early-grade testing is sparse) are less informative
#### Constraints
- Students who move into or out of a school during the academic year do not count in that year's productivity profile

### Target Learners
- Chicago public elementary school students in grades two through eight

### Target Learning Objectives
- Reading and mathematics achievement growth as measured by the Iowa Tests of Basic Skills
