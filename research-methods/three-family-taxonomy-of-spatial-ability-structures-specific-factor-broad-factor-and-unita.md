# Three-family taxonomy of spatial ability structures: specific-factor, broad-factor, and unitary

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review organizes the sprawling spatial ability literature into three general types of taxonomies. Specific-factor structures use factor-analytic methods to identify subcomponents such as spatial orientation, mental rotation, and non-rotational spatial visualization; broad-factor structures use theoretically motivated categories such as large- versus small-scale or the intrinsic-extrinsic by static-dynamic 2x2; unitary structures treat spatial ability as a single factor. The review states these were chosen "to highlight the main sub-factor dissociations in the literature" and to make definitions navigable for non-specialist mathematics education researchers.
