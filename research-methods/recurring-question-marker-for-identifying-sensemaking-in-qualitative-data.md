## Description
The article proposes a research use for the finding: for physics education researchers, "these types of recurring questions could give us a useful marker for analytically identifying sensemaking in qualitative data". The principle is that if students are repeatedly returning to a vexing question while trying to build an explanation, individually or in a group, that recurrence can serve as evidence that those students are in a sensemaking frame. This converts the conversational finding into an operational criterion for analysis.
