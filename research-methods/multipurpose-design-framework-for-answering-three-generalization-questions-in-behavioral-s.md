# Multipurpose design framework for answering three generalization questions in behavioral science experiments

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper develops a 'multipurpose' study design that fuses survey sampling and experimental design results so that a single study can answer three generalization questions: the average effect, its variability, and treatment effect moderation. The article states the design 'requires researchers to develop hypotheses about treatment effect heterogeneity at the outset of the study, to develop methods to measure these moderators, and to use a stratified selection procedure, with strata based on potential moderators.' Power for all three estimands involves trade-offs.
