# Two-level coding framework for observing collaborative regulation as interactional phenomena

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The article treats co-regulation (CoRL) and socially shared regulation (SSRL) as interactional phenomena observable in group discourse, operationalised through a two-level coding framework. At the episode level, regulatory episodes are coded for mode (CoRL, SSRL, or hybrid CoRL&SSRL) and for regulatory processes such as obstacle detection, strategic action direction, and shared affective regulation. At the utterance level, each turn is coded for participatory focus spanning cognitive, metacognitive, task-execution, and socio-emotional subcodes. The framework treats regulation as "distributed and emergent" rather than a static shift in language use.
