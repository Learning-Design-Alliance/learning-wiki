Two-dimensional typology of eWIL: function of technology by degree of technological involvement¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The review develops a typology classifying eWIL experiences along two dimensions: the function of the technology (supporting administrative WIL processes vs. delivering the WIL experience pedagogically) and the degree of technological involvement (low vs. high). Iterative open and pattern coding of 22 studies produced "four broad codes: "Technology-Supported", "Technology-Facilitated", "Technology-Blended" and "Technology-Based"", validated by independent recoding with no inconsistencies between researchers. The typology extends Kaider et al. (2009) by recognizing that technology can deliver, not just support, WIL.
