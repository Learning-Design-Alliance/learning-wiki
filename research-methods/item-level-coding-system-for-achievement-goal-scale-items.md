# Item-level coding system for achievement goal scale items

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article developed a coding system classifying every individual item in each achievement goal scale. Items were first coded for goal-relevant language matching the article's three-part goal definition, then coded as general goal or into component categories such as performance-normative, performance-appearance, performance-evaluative, mastery-potential, mastery-improve, mastery-task, mastery-interest, and mastery-learning, with fear/negative-affect codes for avoidance scales. Three coders "agreed on 81% of the items (␬ ⫽ .79)", with disagreements resolved by consensus. This coding operationalized the item-content moderators tested in the meta-analysis.
