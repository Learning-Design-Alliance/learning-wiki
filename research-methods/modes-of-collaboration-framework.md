## Description
The Modes of Collaboration framework describes student interactions in group work along three independently assessed dimensions: social (consonant vs. dissonant tone), discursive (consensual, responsive, elaborative, or argumentation), and disciplinary content (specific vs. abstract physics content). Data are segmented into episodes defined by shifts along any dimension, and a Mode is the combination of classifications across all three. The authors state that "The Modes of Collaboration framework defines student interactions along three dimensions: social, discursive, and disciplinary content." It was developed from qualitative video analysis of introductory physics group work.
