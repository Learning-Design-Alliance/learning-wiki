# RAISE (Reporting AI Studies in Education)

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
RAISE (Reporting AI Studies in Education) is a structured reporting standard for empirical studies involving AI as part of the educational experience. The article states it "is a structured reporting checklist comprising 30 core items" and that "the 30 items are grouped into ten thematic domains, where each domain contains specific items that ask authors to clarify what was done, why, how, and with what implications." The ten domains span educational justification, AI system specification, AI role and interaction, accessibility and cultural fit, setting and participants, human involvement, study design, ethics, transparency and reproducibility, and limitations. The framework "does not mandate any particular method, model, or ideology; rather, it insists that whatever choices are made, they are made visible."
