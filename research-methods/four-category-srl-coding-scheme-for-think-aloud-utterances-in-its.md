# Four-category SRL coding scheme for think-aloud utterances in ITS

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A coding scheme adapted from Winne and Hadwin's four-stage model, labeling each concatenated utterance for the presence or absence of "1) processing information, 2) planning, 3) enacting, and 4) realizing errors". The categories are coarser-grained than other think-aloud schemes because utterances between problem-solving attempts are short, and they are not mutually exclusive. Codes were synchronized with tutor log timestamps to model the next attempt's correctness.
