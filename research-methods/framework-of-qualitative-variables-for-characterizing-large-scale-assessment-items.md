# Framework of qualitative variables for characterizing large-scale assessment items

## Description
The article builds a set of qualitative variables to characterize multiple-choice items against the exam's stated objectives: the skill assessed, text length as a proxy for reflection time, presence of visual objects, contextualization level, demand for specific disciplinary knowledge, and quantitative character. Contextualization follows Nentwig: an item has high contextualization "se no texto estão presentes as informações relevantes para a sua solução", with pre-text, low, medium and high levels defined by the share of text lines needed. Items are also classified as quantitative, semiquantitative or qualitative. Classifications were made independently by more than one researcher with collective discussion to consensus.
