# Traditional versus construct-driven SJT development approaches

## Description
The article distinguishes two SJT developmental approaches. Traditional SJTs "mimic samples of previous work experience" with expert-evaluated situations and responses scored into a mixture of skills "often not further specified". Construct-driven SJTs follow personality-test procedures: they start from defined constructs, use skill-activating situations, and score each skill separately. The article applies this framework to medical admission via the HAM-SJT (traditional) and CD-SJT (construct-driven, agency and communion).
