# Thinking Through AI framework

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Thinking Through AI framework is a methodological approach integrating cyber-social research with cognitive labs and think-aloud protocols to create continuous feedback loops in AI tool development. It is structured around three core dimensions: students' cognitive engagement with AI, educators' instructional strategies embedding rubrics and knowledge repositories, and the collaborative design of AI tools and learning experiences via Learning Experience Research (LXR). The article states it is 'a methodological approach that integrates principles of cyber-social research with cognitive labs and think-aloud protocols to facilitate continuous feedback loops in the development and implementation of AI-driven educational tools.'
