Co-referencing typology and coding scheme for analyzing collaborative interaction around external representations¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The Evans, Feenstra, Ryon and McNeill article provides analytic tools for collaborative interactions around external representations, honing in on "co-referencing": multiple people referencing the same thing through a deictic word, a pointing gesture, or any verbal, physical, or virtual action referencing something as intended by more than one person. The editorial reports that "a systematic typology of forms and levels of co-reference is sketched and a method of coding co-references in their temporality is proposed and illustrated," enabling both qualitative sense of co-construction and quantitative comparison across cases.
