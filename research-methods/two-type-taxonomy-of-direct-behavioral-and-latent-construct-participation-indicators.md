# Two-type taxonomy of direct behavioral and latent-construct participation indicators

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review synthesizes the varied conceptual indicators used to model individual participation into two general types. The first, direct behavioral indicators, are "represented by log data or specific, countable behaviors reflecting individual active or passive participation," such as counts of utterances, submissions, shared-document additions, and forum posts. The second type comprises latent constructs inferred from individuals' conversational, physical, and textual inputs, classified (drawing on the reviewed theories) into individual cognitive participation, metacognitive participation, and socioemotional participation, plus coordinative participation for logistics management. The review notes the direct indicators "demonstrate deliberate and intentional engagement in group work but without necessarily interacting with other group members."
