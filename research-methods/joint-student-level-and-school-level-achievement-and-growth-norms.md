# Joint student-level and school-level achievement and growth norms

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The 2020 norms treat student-level and school-level norming as a joint enterprise rather than separate products. The report states: "Finally, we have calculated student-level norms and school norms jointly an d include both in this study." Student norms describe individual scores relative to peers, while school norms describe school-level mean performance, and both are derived from the same multilevel modeling of longitudinal test event data, with school intra-class correlations reported alongside.
