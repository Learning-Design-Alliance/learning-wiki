# Five-theme taxonomy of medical students’ reflective writing in e-learning communication training

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes a five-theme taxonomy derived inductively from students’ reflective texts: patient-centred communication, conversation structure, emotions, learning experience, and attitude. Themes 1–3 mirror the course’s three modules and are grouped as recalled course content, while Themes 4 and 5 capture “more critical reflections on students’ own learning and competencies, concrete intentions for their future conversations with patients”. The taxonomy organises what students address when guided prompts ask about awareness, understanding, and transfer.
