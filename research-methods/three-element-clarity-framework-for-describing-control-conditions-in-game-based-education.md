# Three-element clarity framework for describing control conditions in game-based education experiments

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review defines three elements needed to replicate a controlled experiment involving gamification or game-based learning: "type of teaching material (the tools used to transmit course content), teaching method (how is the course taught), and evaluation method (how is the effectiveness of experimental and control methods evaluated)". Each element is scored 'Clear' or 'Unclear' depending on whether the experiment can be replicated with the information presented. The framework is applied to 43 studies to document how completely control conditions are reported.
