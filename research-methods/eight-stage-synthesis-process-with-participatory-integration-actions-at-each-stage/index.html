Eight-stage synthesis process with participatory integration actions at each stage¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article maps community integration onto eight synthesis stages: planning and protocol development, literature search, study identification, coding and data extraction, data analysis, results interpretation, reporting, and dissemination. Each stage carries actionable steps and integration examples, e.g., at coding, "Coding expands beyond effect sizes to include implementation features, participation outcomes, equity relevant variables." The pattern transforms a review "from a retrospective aggregation of effects into a mechanism- and context-informed synthesis."
