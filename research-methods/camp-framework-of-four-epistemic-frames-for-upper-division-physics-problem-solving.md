## Description
The CAMP framework, presented by Chari et al. (2017) and adopted here, characterizes student thinking in upper-division physics problem solving. The article states that "This framework includes four frames: Conceptual Physics (CP), Algorithmic Physics (AP), Conceptual Mathematics (CM), and Algorithmic Mathematics (AM)", reflecting whether students use concepts or algorithms and math or physics knowledge. The authors use it to code the focal student's framing before and after instructor interaction.
