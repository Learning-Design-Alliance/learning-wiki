# Hybrid multi-label supervised-contrastive and multiple-negatives-ranking embedding method for performance-aware submission analytics

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's embedding model is fine-tuned with a hybrid objective combining Multi-Label Supervised Contrastive Loss (MulSupCon), which groups solutions sharing problem-type and correctness labels, with Multiple Negatives Ranking (MNR) loss, a retrieval-oriented ranking objective, weighted by a factor alpha. The article states: "By training our embedding model with this hybrid loss function, the resulting semantic space becomes highly structured along multiple axes simultaneously." This performance-aware space arranges embeddings so geometric structure reflects functional correctness, driving the UMAP instructor analytics. MulSupCon is inspired by the work of Zhang et al. on multi-label supervised contrastive learning.
