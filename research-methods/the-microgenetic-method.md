# The microgenetic method

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The microgenetic method is a research approach for studying cognitive developmental change directly rather than by before-and-after snapshots. The article defines it by three properties: "(a) Observations span the entire period from the beginning of the change to the time at which it reaches a relatively stable state", high density of observations relative to the rate of change, and intensive trial-by-trial analysis to infer the processes giving rise to change. It is presented as suited to revealing qualitative as well as quantitative aspects of change, the conditions under which changes occur, and short-lived transition strategies.
