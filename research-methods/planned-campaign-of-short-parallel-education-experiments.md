# Planned campaign of short, parallel education experiments

## Description
The article recommends that education RCTs be realised as a planned campaign of shorter experiments aiming to reveal smaller effect sizes over large populations, with many experiments running in parallel, because the timeframe for compounding effects aligns with learning as a lifelong activity. Shortening experiments keeps the coefficient of variation stable while high statistical power is maintained by exposing each user to multiple concurrent experiments. A campaign of short-lived experiments delivering modest individual effects can build up over long periods into big impacts for learners and society.
