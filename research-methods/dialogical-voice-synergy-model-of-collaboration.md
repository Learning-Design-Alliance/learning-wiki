# Dialogical voice-synergy model of collaboration

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's second model assesses collaboration "from a dialogical perspective" as the participation of different speakers through their voices. Collaboration is seen as "the intertwining or synergy of voices pertaining to different speakers", enabling "a transversal analysis of subsequent discussion slices". This complements the longitudinal cohesion-graph model by capturing how voices are interwoven across slices of the chat.
