## Description
A pooled analysis of items treats each examination item as its own comparison: correct-answer rates are computed per item with 95% confidence intervals and combined in a random-effects proportional meta-analysis yielding a school-level odds ratio. Here "The intervention group comprised students from the same school as the item author, whereas the control group consisted of other students." The authors present the method as a privacy-preserving complementary benchmarking tool.

- **Pooled analysis of pretested items as a benchmark-assessment comparison method**: A statistical method that compares cohorts on the same pretested assessment items by calculating per-item odds ratios with 95% confidence intervals and performing fixed-effects meta-analyses per content area. The article positions it against existing approaches, noting that "mean scores may not allow for reliable comparisons between scores on different exams because exams may have different levels of difficulty" and that cumulative deviation requires longitudinal appraisal. It provides item-level information on specific knowledge gaps beyond group-level comparisons. (Hamamoto Filho PT et al. (2021))
