## Description
A pooled analysis of items treats each examination item as its own comparison: correct-answer rates are computed per item with 95% confidence intervals and combined in a random-effects proportional meta-analysis yielding a school-level odds ratio. Here "The intervention group comprised students from the same school as the item author, whereas the control group consisted of other students." The authors present the method as a privacy-preserving complementary benchmarking tool.
