# Equity of Parity and Equity of Individuality framework

## Description
The article applies two models of equity to evaluate the CLASP reform. Equity of Parity is "achieved when the average success of individual demographic groups are statistically indistinguishable from the average of all other groups", pairing naturally with a course deficit model. Equity of Individuality "compares each groups' success to the prior success of that group instead of comparing to a specific comparison group", so improvements within each demographic group count even if gaps to other groups remain. The authors use both models across drop, pass, and graduation analyses.
