## Description
The article operationalizes equity in small groups with three complementary measures: total speaking time and its distribution, conversation consistency (dot products of neighboring normalized conversation vectors across time bins), and conversation equality (dot product of the conversation vector with a unit equality vector). The authors state "We use three methods to measure equity" and analyze how effective each is at capturing equity in group discourse. Equality distinguishes whether members participate in the same amount, while equity means each member has affordances to participate.
