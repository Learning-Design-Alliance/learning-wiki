# Messick's seven facets of validity

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper adopts Messick's taxonomy of validity facets to structure validation investigations in epistemic game contexts. It lists "the following seven facets of validity or validation processes": content, substantive, structural, predictive, external, generalizability, and consequential validity, each with a guiding question about what the assessment content represents, which processes respondents engage in, and so on. The authors use this framework to organize the third section of the paper on structuring additional validation research.

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interpretations about learners must be clearly described and organized before the assessment is developed
#### Constraints
- The authors argue a restricted causal view of validity (Borsboom & Mellenbergh) is probably too narrow for epistemic games, which rely on socio-cognitive and socio-cultural theories of learning

### Target Learners
- learners whose game performance is interpreted in assessment narratives

### Target Learning Objectives
- defensible interpretations of learners' developing 21st-century skills and epistemic frames
