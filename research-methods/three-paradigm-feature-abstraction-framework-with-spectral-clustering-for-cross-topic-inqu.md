# Three-paradigm feature-abstraction framework with spectral clustering for cross-topic inquiry-pattern analysis

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The article presents a clustering framework to analyze log data from interactive simulations under three levels of topic abstraction: variable (simulation-specific qualification by independent variable), relationship (qualification by the nature of the variable–outcome relationship, generalizable across isomorphic simulations), and focus (simulation-agnostic qualification by number of involved variables). Sequences are transformed into Markov transition matrices and clustered with spectral clustering, which handles non-convex data. The framework aims to "identify productive inquiry patterns without relying on predefined behavior assumptions" and to offer insight into the trade-offs between topic-agnostic and context-rich representations.
