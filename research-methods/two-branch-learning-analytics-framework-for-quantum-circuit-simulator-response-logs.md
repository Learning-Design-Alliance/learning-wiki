# Two-branch learning analytics framework for quantum circuit simulator response logs

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The framework consists of "two parallel branches operating on the same underlying submission log", sharing source data and preprocessing but differing in unit of analysis. The task-level branch characterises exercises through participation, completion, outcome difficulty, drop-out hazard, first-attempt accuracy, mean valid attempts, sessionised active time, and observed answer-space reporting. The learner-level branch, still research-stage, will produce per-learner indicators grouped into performance, temporal effort, path-related behaviour, and questionnaire-based psychometrics families. It is presented as a reproducible, shareable framework designed to evolve across successive course runs over a three-year horizon.
