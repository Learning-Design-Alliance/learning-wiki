# Principle-to-indicator mapping as an accountability device for AI-enabled pedagogical systems

## Description
The framework translates pedagogical design principles into observable indicators using a compact mapping (Table I) from principle to indicator to observation method: transparency of identity and limits (misinterpretation rate via survey/observation), scaffolding toward humans (transition-to-human prompts and follow-through signals via logs/observation), directional micro-mediations (micro-action formulation and completion via logs/rubrics), prevention of dependency (looping and exclusive-use signals via logs), communicative autonomy support (goal/question articulation quality via rubrics), and data minimization (sensitive-data solicitation frequency, which should be near zero). The mapping is treated as an accountability device making the system's pedagogical function testable through concrete traces, structured judgments, and participant perceptions.
