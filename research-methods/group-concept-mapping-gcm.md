## Description
Group Concept Mapping (GCM) is defined in the article as a "structured methodology for organizing the ideas of a group on any topic of interest and presenting those visually through a series of interrelated maps". It integrates participatory, quantitative and qualitative methods: brainstorming generates statements, participants sort and rate them, and multidimensional scaling with Ward's-method hierarchical cluster analysis yields visual theme maps and Go Zone quadrants. The article uses GCM via the groupwisdom tool to coproduce priorities for inclusive NbSP teaching with 38 stakeholders, arguing it frees participants from groupthink constraints of Delphi approaches.
