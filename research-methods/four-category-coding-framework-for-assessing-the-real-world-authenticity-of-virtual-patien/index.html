Four-category coding framework for assessing the real-world authenticity of virtual patient collections¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article develops a coding framework with four main categories — patient data, patient representation, diagnoses, and setting — to analyze how well a virtual patient collection represents a relevant patient population and how patients are represented as persons. Codes cover sex, age, sexual orientation, BMI, disability, occupation, cultural/migration aspects, substance use, naming, social context, direct speech, emotions, multimedia, ICD-10 diagnoses, onset, setting, and scenario closure. The authors state: "We developed a coding framework to analyze the VP collection that is provided to medical students in the interdisciplinary year at LMU Munich and evaluate (1) its authenticity compared to the German population and (2) how the patient is represented as a person."
