Data integration approach using auxiliary observational model predictions as covariates for RCT estimation¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The data integration approach improves precision of randomized trial effect estimates by fitting a model on a large observational auxiliary sample to predict the outcome, then using its predictions on the experimental sample as a covariate. As the article puts it, "The predictions from this model, applied to the experimental sample, can be a powerful covariate for adjustment." Combined with a sample-splitting estimator for paired trials, the approach is unbiased because auxiliary predictions are pre-treatment covariates. The article also frames auxiliary data as a "sandbox" for flexible exploration without touching experimental outcomes.
