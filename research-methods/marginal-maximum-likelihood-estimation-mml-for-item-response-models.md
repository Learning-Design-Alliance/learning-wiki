# Marginal maximum likelihood estimation (MML) for item response models

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article presents marginal maximum likelihood (MML) estimation as a framework for estimating item parameters in item-response models. Rather than conditioning on or jointly estimating examinee abilities, the approach works "in the marginal distribution, integrating over the distribution of ability". This marginal formulation is the basis for the paper's EM-algorithm computing procedure and its extension to general item-response models.
