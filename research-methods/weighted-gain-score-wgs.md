# Weighted Gain Score (WGS)

## Description
The Weighted Gain Score is a learning gain metric for pretest-posttest designs that applies a weighting coefficient 'pre/µ' to linearly transform the difference between pretest and posttest percentage scores, thereby adjusting for pretest variability. Formally, WGS=(post−pre)×(pre/μ). The article reinterprets µ not solely as the cohort's average pretest score but as an adjustment coefficient that functions as a scaling parameter, where higher values of µ lead to smaller gain estimates and lower values produce larger estimates. The WGS requires substantially lower computational complexity than Rasch-based approaches and can be applied to both small and large datasets.
