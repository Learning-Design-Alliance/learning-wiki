# Taxonomy of PDW payoff schemes: four-cell matrices versus free-choice designs

## Description
The review organizes the heterogeneous payoff systems used in post-decision wagering into two broad classes: four-cell matrices, where a low and a high wager option combine with correct and incorrect responses to yield four possible outcomes, and free-choice designs with more than four cells. It reports that "A total of 78 four cell matrices (70% of all reviewed payoff schemes) were retrieved from the literature" and that "In total, 33 such free choice designs were retrieved from the literature (30% of all reviewed payoff schemes)". Within these classes it further distinguishes no-wager/safe-option variants, always-wager-high matrices, no-loss gambling, adjusted wagering, and designs matching other instruments.
