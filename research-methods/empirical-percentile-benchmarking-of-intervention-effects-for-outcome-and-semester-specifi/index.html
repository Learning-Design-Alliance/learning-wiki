Empirical percentile benchmarking of intervention effects for outcome- and semester-specific RCT planning¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends situating a planned or observed effect within the distribution of true effects from prior community college RCTs, by outcome and semester, instead of relying on Cohen's small/medium/large conventions. For planning, "researchers may want to choose a sample size that will make it possible to detect effects at the lower end of the distribution of effects presented in this paper" for lighter-touch interventions, with larger minimum detectable true effects acceptable for comprehensive interventions. MDTE calculations should be specific to outcome and timing.
