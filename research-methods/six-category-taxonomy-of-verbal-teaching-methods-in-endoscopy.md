# Six-category taxonomy of verbal teaching methods in endoscopy

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article names and substantively describes a classification of verbal teaching in endoscopy derived through Grounded Theory analysis of a 53-page, 12000-word transcript of 33 recorded procedures. The six types are demonstration by the teacher, motor instructions, broad tips/tricks/pointers, verbal feedback, questioning, and non-procedural information. The authors state that "The process of iterative review and repeated testing yielded 6 types of verbal teaching: demonstration by the teacher, motor instructions, broad tips/tricks/pointers, verbal feedback, questioning, and non-procedural information." Table 1 gives example utterances for each category, such as "Good. Well done" for feedback and "What do you think of the stomach lining over there?" for questioning.
