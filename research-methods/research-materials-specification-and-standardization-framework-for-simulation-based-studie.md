## Description
Table 1 of the article classifies the materials developed for research purposes in simulation-based studies. Instruments that assess knowledge treat it as a prerequisite of performance, while competence instruments "operationalize this construct as observable results and processes in simulations", including computer-based tests, coding schemes, and rating scales. The educational intervention is the treatment condition examined, such as instructional support like prompts, and the experimental procedure consists of the measurement procedure and an experimenter's guide.
