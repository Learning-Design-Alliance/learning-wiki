## Description
The article demonstrates an interpretability workflow in which complex ML models are explained post hoc: "we employed explainable AI techniques: we examined feature importance from the ML models and used SHAP values and decision tree visualizations to understand how each predictor influenced the predicted scores." Feature-importance rankings identify the strongest predictors per country, SHAP values quantify each feature's contribution to individual predictions, and tree-surrogate diagrams expose conditional interaction rules. The workflow is presented as balancing predictive accuracy with transparency for educators and policymakers.
