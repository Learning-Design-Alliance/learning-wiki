# Design-based impact estimation

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A framework in which impact estimators are derived from the known features of the experiment rather than from an assumed data-generating model. The report explains that "Design-based estimators are derived from the known features of experiments and are based on the Neyman-Holland-Rubin causal inference model," using the random assignment process to build the estimation model so that potential outcomes are treated as fixed. Compared with model-based methods, these methods "tend to make fewer assumptions about the nature of the data" and explicitly account for known design information.
