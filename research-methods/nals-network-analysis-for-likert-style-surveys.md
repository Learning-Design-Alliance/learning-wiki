# NALS: network analysis for Likert-style surveys

## Description
The NALS methodology "is designed to enable the modeling of survey items as a network built from survey responses", with individual survey items as nodes and edges weighted by the similarity in responses between those nodes. Unlike exploratory factor analysis, it "allows investigation of the multilevel complexities of subclusters within each theme". In this study NALS is combined with fast-greedy clustering, bootstrapping, and centrality measures to compare ASES networks across demographic groups.
