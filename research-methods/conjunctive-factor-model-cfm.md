## Description
The Conjunctive Factor Model is a parametric item-response model developed to test whether skills in student log data act conjunctively, i.e., whether a step requiring multiple skills is harder than steps requiring each skill alone. The article says CFM "can be thought as modeling the conjunctivity as a multiplication of skill parameters" and is a special case of Embretson's multicomponent latent trait model, customized for the high-dimensional feature space of intelligent tutoring systems, where cognitive models contain many more skills than traditional assessments have cognitive attributes.
