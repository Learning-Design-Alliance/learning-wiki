# Propensity score matching for observational educational comparisons

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study's analytic approach uses propensity score methodology (Rosenbaum, 2002) to construct a counterfactual comparison group of promoted students similar to retainees on 43 pre-retention covariates. The authors state: "We use two different propensity score methodological approaches to estimate the average treatment effect on the treated: (1) nearest neighbor (one to one) matching without replacement and (2) caliper matching with replacement." Regression-adjusted matched estimates with probability weights and covariate adjustment are planned, and retained students outside the area of common support are excluded.
