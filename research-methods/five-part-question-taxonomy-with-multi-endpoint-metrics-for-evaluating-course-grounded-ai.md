# Five-part question taxonomy with multi-endpoint metrics for evaluating course-grounded AI tutors

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article extends a prior 30-question generic/specific set into a five-part taxonomy of classroom question types: "(a) single fact lookup, (b) multi hop concept linking, (c) thematic explanation and synthesis, (d) contradictionandambiguityhandling(Houetal.2024),and (e) curriculum update questions probing sensitivity to revisedmaterials." Questions were human-generated with reference answers and word limits and balanced across course topics, yielding 59 retained questions (45 topic-page, 14 cross-page). The taxonomy is paired with a multi-endpoint metric suite — RAGAS core metrics plus claim-citation alignment, blinded LLM-judge synthesis quality, robustness under knowledge shift, and cost — scored separately to avoid winner-take-all conclusions.
