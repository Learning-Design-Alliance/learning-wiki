# Four-area categorical framework for analyzing science-competition assessment items

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The article develops a purpose-built categorical framework for analyzing competition test items, applying qualitative content analysis to IBO theory and practical items. It organizes item characteristics into four areas: "formal characteristics, scientific content and practices, cognitive aspects, and use of representations." Lower-level categories and trait definitions were derived inductively from the analyzed material, while the areas were grounded deductively in prior item-analysis research and educational frameworks such as Bloom's revised taxonomy, NGSS core ideas and scientific practices, and representation-type systems. Coding reached high interrater agreement (average 84% in the final round across N = 79 items).
