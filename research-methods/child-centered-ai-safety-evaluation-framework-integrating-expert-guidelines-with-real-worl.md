# Child-centered AI safety evaluation framework integrating expert guidelines with real-world AI incident data

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article introduces an evaluation framework for child safety in generative AI that combines two resources: expert-proposed child safety guidelines and AI incident databases. It first constructs "a taxonomy of child safety risk categories informed by expert guidance," then leverages incident data to build a synthetic test set reflecting realistic harmful scenarios. The framework is designed to be flexible and extensible so it can respond rapidly to real-world AI incidents, addressing the gap that most existing safety benchmarks focus on general adult user populations.
