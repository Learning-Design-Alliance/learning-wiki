# Coding scheme for observable Direction, Integration, and Evaluation in student–AI interaction traces

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An exploratory multilabel coding scheme applied to student turns in AI-mediated conversations, with the student turn as coding unit interpreted in relation to its local sequence and the whole conversation. Direction is coded when "the learner exercises task-relevant control over the purpose, constraints, procedure, artifact, or trajectory of the activity"; Integration when a subsequent action connects to uptake, application, transformation, or reuse of a prior AI contribution; Evaluation when the learner participates in judging a contribution through inspection, comparison, testing, discrepancy detection, diagnosis, correction, or criterion-based judgment. Provenance is recorded separately for content, criteria, and judgments, and evidence is classified as explicit or conservatively inferable.
