## Description
The article develops an emergent coding scheme categorizing student questionable research practices into subjective interpretation, unjustified interpretation, stating a purpose to (dis-)prove the model, and data manipulation. Table I lists codes such as "Concerning results", "Claim of systematic error", "Doubting statistics", and "Inﬂating uncertainty". Each coded practice was further classified by whether it was oriented toward confirming the model, enabling analysis of confirmatory goals.
