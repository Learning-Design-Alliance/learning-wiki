# Three complementary views of AI-integrated writing: product, process, and interaction

## Description
The article argues that understanding student writing in AI-mediated settings requires three complementary views: the product view characterizes the final artifact, the process view captures how the artifact develops over time, and the interaction view captures how the student engages with the AI during that process. The authors state that "understanding of writing requires considering three complementary views" and argue existing tools treat these capabilities as separate signals rather than complementary views of the same writing activity. DRAFTTRACE operationalizes this framework by capturing and aligning all three.
