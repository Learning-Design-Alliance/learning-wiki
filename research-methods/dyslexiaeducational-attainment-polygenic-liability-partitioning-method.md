# Dyslexia–educational attainment polygenic-liability partitioning method

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The article proposes dissecting the genetic liability for dyslexia into components based on their relationship with educational attainment, using EA as a proxy for school performance that reflects both cognitive abilities and noncognitive influences. Variants are divided into those not associated with EA (DYSnoEA) and those associated with EA, the latter subdivided into concordant (consistent direction of effect in both traits) and discordant (opposite direction) subsets. This approach "allowed us to distinguish dyslexia-specific genetic effects from those shared with general cognitive and non-cognitive skills relevant to academic performance." The framework reveals that the discordant partition accounts for the highest enrichment in dyslexia genetic background despite comprising a small proportion of SNPs.
