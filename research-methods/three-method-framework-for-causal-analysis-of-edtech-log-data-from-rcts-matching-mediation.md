# Three-method framework for causal analysis of EdTech log data from RCTs: matching, mediation, and principal stratification

## Description
The article develops, demonstrates, and contrasts three approaches to the challenges of log-data analysis in EdTech RCTs: confounding of non-randomized implementation, log data existing only in the treatment arm, and complex multi-type data structures. It provides "a detailed demonstration and comparison of matching, mediation analysis, and principal stratiﬁcation on the same dataset," focusing on applicability to implementation data from randomized educational technology experiments. Each method makes different identification assumptions: matching relies on no unmeasured confounding, mediation additionally requires a coherently defined control-arm mediator value, and principal stratification in an RCT relies on randomization rather than confounding assumptions.
