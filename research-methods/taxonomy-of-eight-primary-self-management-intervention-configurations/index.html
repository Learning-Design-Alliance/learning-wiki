Description¶

The review organizes the school-based self-management literature by the specific intervention configuration employed, distinguishing approaches such as prompted self-monitoring, self-monitoring plus reinforcement based on both accuracy and performance, and prompted self-monitoring plus graphing. The authors state that "eight primary approaches to self-management were utilized across more than one study" within the 56 studies reviewed. This taxonomy provides the organizing framework for evaluating component-level effectiveness.
