# Five-characteristic framework of student diversity for evaluating universal SEL interventions

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review operationalizes student diversity for evaluating universal SEL interventions in terms of five demographic characteristics: "gender, race/ethnicity, SES, disability status, and sexual orientation/gender identity." It uses this framework to assess how and when demographic characteristics were reported, whether they were analyzed as moderators of program outcomes, and how differential effects entered generalizability discussions. The framework organizes the review's content analysis of published evaluations.
