# Natures of Negativity in Introductory Physics (NoNIP) framework

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
NoNIP is a taxonomy of the meanings of the negative sign in introductory physics, with three base categories. The Quantity category attaches a sign to a single scalar or vector quantity (with subcategories such as Type, Change, Comparison to reference, Direction from origin), the Relationship category signifies how quantities oppose, are opposite, or relate via relative orientation, and the Operation category covers subtraction uses such as removal and difference. The article presents it as "a framework for categorizing various meanings and interpretations of the negative sign in physics contexts, inspired by established work in algebra contexts from the mathematics education research community."
