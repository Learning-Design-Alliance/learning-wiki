# Learning from Instructor, Concept, and Experience (LfI/LfC/LfE) framework

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The framework decomposes general learning ability into three complementary dimensions grounded in cognitive psychology and educational theory: Learning from Instructor (acquiring knowledge via explicit guidance, mirroring rapid-instruction-to-implementation learning), Learning from Concept (internalizing abstract structures and generalizing to new contexts, grounded in symbolic cognitive theories), and Learning from Experience (adapting through accumulated exploration and feedback, consistent with experiential learning theory). Formally, a learning task is a tuple with input, output, and context spaces, and the context takes a different form per paradigm: instructional signals, static conceptual knowledge, or accumulated interaction trajectories. The article uses it to design targeted experimental paradigms and the LearnArena benchmark.
