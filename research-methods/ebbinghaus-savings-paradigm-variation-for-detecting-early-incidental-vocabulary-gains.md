# Ebbinghaus savings-paradigm variation for detecting early incidental vocabulary gains

## Description
This methodology exposes learners to novel words incidentally, then asks them to learn the words explicitly, with half of the explicit-phase words previously encountered and half completely new. The article states it was "sensitive enough to detect vocabulary acquisition from two exposures to FL words", detecting "traces of word knowledge that had not necessarily reached the threshold for explicit recognition". It is offered as a more sensitive alternative to traditional recognition and recall tests in incidental vocabulary research.
