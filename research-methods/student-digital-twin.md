## Description
A Student Digital Twin is defined as "a continuously evolving model of an individual learner's academic performance, learning behavior, career interests, degree progression, financial circumstances, co-curricular engagement, and workforce preparedness." Its purpose is "to simulate": answering counterfactual questions such as what happens if a gateway course is repeated with tutoring, or if the student changes majors. The paper specifies three design properties: fidelity matched to purpose, calibration of predicted probabilities to observed rates, and validation of simulated causal claims against real outcomes. It cautions the twin "is not the person" and should be a decision aid, not an oracle.
