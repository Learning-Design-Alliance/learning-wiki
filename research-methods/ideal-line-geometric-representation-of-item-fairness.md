# Ideal-line geometric representation of item fairness

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article represents each dichotomously scored item by the two coefficients of a linear regression of proportion-correct on normalized total score, interpreted as coordinates of a point in a plane. Fair items localize near the 'ideal line' passing through (1,0) and (0,1); the article states 'the distance from the ideal line presents the unfairness measure sought for', so the farther an item point lies from the line, the less fair the item. A cutoff value df turns the continuous measure into a definite unfair-item identification.
