# Two complementary misconception-acquisition formalizations: the Novice Student Misconception Model and the Expert Tutor Misconception Model

## Description
The article formalizes misconception acquisition from two perspectives. The Novice Student Misconception Model simulates an individual student holding one specific misconception and must satisfy Misconception Replication on applicable types plus Correct Reasoning Preservation on non-applicable types. The Expert Tutor Misconception Model captures the spectrum of misconceptions a tutor encounters across students, a computational analogue of teachers' Knowledge of Student Misconceptions, and must replicate every training misconception while maintaining Overall Correct Accuracy across all problem types. Both use thresholds where "We setθm =θ c = 90%in all experiments."
