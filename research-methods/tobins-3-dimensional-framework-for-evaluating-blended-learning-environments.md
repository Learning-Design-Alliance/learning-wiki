## Description
The article applies Tobin's 3-dimensional framework for evaluating participants' perception of the web-based environment to interpret the CTBL program's effects. The authors write: "On the basis of Tobin's [61] 3-dimensional framework to evaluate participants' perception of the web-based environment, we evaluated the CTBL program." The emancipatory dimension (the WCP) let participants control pace, place, and depth of learning; the coparticipatory dimension (case-based approach) provided a platform to explore and discuss clinical teaching options with peers and instructors; and the qualia dimension captured participants' enjoyment of the program.

- **Tobin's three-dimensional framework for evaluating perceptions of online learning environments**: The article applies Tobin's (1998) framework of three dimensions for evaluating participants' perception of an online environment: "1) 'emancipatory activities' dimension (the degree of autonomy afforded to the participants in controlling the pace, place and depth of learning)", "co-participatory activities" (collaboration and interaction among participants and instructors), and the "qualia" dimension (satisfaction, enjoyment and frustration). In this study, emancipatory activities were rated highly while co-participatory activities left much to be desired. (Kho Madeleine Huei Tze et al. (2018))
