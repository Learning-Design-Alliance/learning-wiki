# Tobin's 3-dimensional framework for evaluating blended learning environments

## Description
The article applies Tobin's 3-dimensional framework for evaluating participants' perception of the web-based environment to interpret the CTBL program's effects. The authors write: "On the basis of Tobin's [61] 3-dimensional framework to evaluate participants' perception of the web-based environment, we evaluated the CTBL program." The emancipatory dimension (the WCP) let participants control pace, place, and depth of learning; the coparticipatory dimension (case-based approach) provided a platform to explore and discuss clinical teaching options with peers and instructors; and the qualia dimension captured participants' enjoyment of the program.
