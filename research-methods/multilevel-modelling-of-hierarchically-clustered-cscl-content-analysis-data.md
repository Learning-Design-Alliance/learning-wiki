# Multilevel modelling of hierarchically clustered CSCL content-analysis data

## Description
The article presents multilevel modelling as a statistical approach for hierarchically clustered data in collaborative learning research, in which messages are nested within students (and measurement occasions) within groups. It argues that traditional unilevel techniques violate the independence assumption, that "conventional modelling can result in underestimation of standard errors", and that multilevel modelling handles "the hierarchical nesting, the interdependency, and the unit of analysis problem in a natural way". The article demonstrates the framework through three-level and four-level random intercept models with roles, themes, and ASIMeC dimensions as predictors.
