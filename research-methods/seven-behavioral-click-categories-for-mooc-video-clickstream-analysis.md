# Seven behavioral click categories for MOOC video-clickstream analysis

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The report constructs seven semantically meaningful behavioral categories from the most frequent 4-gram click subsequences (top k=100 n-grams) of students' video watching state sequences. Each category "acts like a latent variable, which is difficult to measure from data directly": Rewatch and Clear Concept combine seek-back and scroll-back clicks indicating struggle with content; Skipping and Fast Watching combine seek-forward and ratechange-fast clicks indicating low processing; Slow Watching indicates slowed, high-processing viewing; Checkback Reference captures waves of SeekBw clicks; and Playrate Transition captures waves of ratechange clicks and is treated as neutral. Category importance is quantified via fuzzy string similarity weights between category patterns and the full clickstream.
