Three paradigms of AI integration in education: AI-directed, AI-supported, and AI-empowered¶

Research Method · All research methods

Description¶

The review adopts Ouyang and Jiao's (2021) framework, which "frames the educational role of AI along three paradigms that reflect increasing levels of learner agency": AI-directed (learner-as-receiver, rooted in behaviorism, with AI structuring content and giving prescriptive feedback), AI-supported (learner-as-collaborator, grounded in cognitive and social constructivist theories, with dialogic tutoring and exploratory environments), and AI-empowered (learner-as-leader, aligned with connectivism and complex adaptive systems theory, where learners co-design learning trajectories). The review uses this framework as the coding scheme for RQ3 on theoretical conceptualization of human–AI interaction.
