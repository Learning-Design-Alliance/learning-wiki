Nonlinear mixed-effects Bayesian learning-curve modeling¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A fully disaggregated nonlinear Bayesian mixed-effects model, implemented in the R package brms, estimating each participant's initial accuracy, rate of change (a time constant), and asymptotic accuracy as a continuous function of trial number, with an exponential change curve bounded via logistic-regression link functions. Fixed effects of task order and feedback were included and found not reliable on their own. The authors state it "prevents the conﬂation of components of learning inherent in many other forms of learning estimates".
