## Description
The report organizes the quantitative methods literature into three research topics: subgroup (moderator) analyses using participants' characteristics "measured before the intervention is implemented"; subgroup analyses based on "participants' experiences, mediators, and outcomes measured after program implementation"; and impact estimation when treatment effects vary. This taxonomy structures the report's technical guidance for education impact evaluations.
