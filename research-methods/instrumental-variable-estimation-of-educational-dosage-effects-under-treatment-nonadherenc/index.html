Instrumental-variable estimation of educational dosage effects under treatment nonadherence¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article uses a two-stage least squares instrumental variable approach to address selection bias in estimating effects of Head Start hours. Hours per day offered by Head Start centers serve as instruments for hours per day children actually attend, exploiting variance generated by the HSIS random assignment. Because the instrument is not correlated with family and child characteristics, the second-stage regression yields the estimated effect of hours on child outcomes. Cubic regressions were added to detect non-linear effects.
