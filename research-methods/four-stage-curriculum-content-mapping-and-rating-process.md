# Four-stage curriculum-content mapping and rating process

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a four-stage evaluation process applied to a five-year MBBS curriculum: "i) identifying the learning objectives, ii) mapping them onto the criteria outlined, iii) assessing them against clear objective standards (planned, explicit, universal and quantifiable), and iv) rating data collected". Course content is classified as discrete learning objectives (maximum three resilience outcomes each) or components of structured activities (no limit), and resilience outcomes are logged per learning block and collapsed into parent categories. The authors report the methodology was easy to use and replicate.
