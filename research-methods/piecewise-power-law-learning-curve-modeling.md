# Piecewise power-law learning-curve modeling

## Description
The article proposes that individual learning curves reflect two processes operating at different scales. Locally, within a specific strategy, improvement is "a gradual, smooth improve- ment, with diminishing gains within a specific strategy, which is modeled well as a PL". Globally, learning proceeds as "a discrete sequence of strategy shifts, in which each strategy is better in the long term than the ones preceding it". This framework extends the classic power law of practice by connecting multiple PL pieces without assuming known strategies or fixed components, deriving the piecewise structure directly from large-scale data.
