# Cultural evolutionary framework for analyzing pedagogical change

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A framework, presented with Figure 1, for viewing academia as "a global population of individuals who have pedagogical beliefs and practices that can evolve over time". Cultural variants are analogized to alleles but "need not be discrete or transmitted faithfully", since individuals can invent, repair, and revise what they learn. It is offered not as a new change strategy but as a lens for understanding change at larger scale, developing testable hypotheses, and inspiring future formal modeling.
