## Description
The article adopts Odden and Russ's account of sensemaking as "a dynamic process of building or revising an explanation in order to ‘figure something out’" built from everyday and formal knowledge. From this definition the authors deduce five sensemaking elements: noticing inconsistency, blending everyday and formal knowledge, ascertaining the underlying mechanism, generating and connecting ideas, and seeking coherence. These elements serve as the coding lens for both student and AI solutions to the Gravitron task.
