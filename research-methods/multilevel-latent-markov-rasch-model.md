## Description
The article proposes a multilevel extension of the latent Markov Rasch model in which a discrete latent Markov process represents each student's ability level over time via a Rasch parametrization, and an additional discrete latent variable captures between-cluster heterogeneity. As the article states, the discrete latent variables Uh "have the role of capturing the heterogeneity between clusters in terms of t heir eﬀect on the ability level of each subject." Covariates affect initial and transition probabilities of the latent process through global-logit parametrizations, while item responses depend only on ability and item difficulty. The model is offered as a more flexible alternative to growth models for value-added school assessment.
