## Description
The article proposes categorizing representations by persistence rather than by symbol type. Durable representations, such as diagrams, graphs, written text and written equations, "stay in place and can be easily returned to", while evanescent representations are "temporary and disappear when not in use", such as verbal and facial expressions and gestures. The authors use this scheme to describe the flow of representations during problem solving and how students come to stand fast on a representation.
