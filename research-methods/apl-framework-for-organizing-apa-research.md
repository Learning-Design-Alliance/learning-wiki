# APL framework for organizing APA research

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The APL framework organizes research on animated pedagogical agents around three areas analogous to classroom elements: 'A represents APA characteristics', 'P represents Presentation by the APA', and 'L represents Learners' characteristics'. It is derived from the four fundamental classroom elements (teacher, teaching, students, physical environment), dropping the invariant physical environment. The paper uses it to structure its review of controversial findings and factors in APA-based learning environments.
