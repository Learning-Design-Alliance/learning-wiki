# Three-category taxonomy of collaborative instruction models with distinct causal mechanisms

## Description
The review organizes two-teacher in-class interventions into three categories studied largely separately: co-teaching (general plus special education professionals), teacher assistants/aides (collaboration with adults without formal teaching qualifications), and team teaching (two general education teachers). It "broadly define[s] collaborative instruction as the simultaneous presence of two or more educators/adults working together and sharing responsibilities in instructional and/or behavioral interventions." Mechanisms include improved student-teacher ratio, freed teacher time, scaffolding, feedback, and behavior management.
