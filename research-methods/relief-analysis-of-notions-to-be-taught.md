## Description
The relief is the article's reference mathematical analysis of content: a study crossing epistemico-mathematical, curricular and cognitive aspects (including already-documented difficulties) of the notions to be taught. This crossing yields types of notions, for example notions associated with a new, generalising and unifying formalism far from what students already know, which are assumed particularly difficult to introduce. The article notes that TSD and TAD have studies of the same type through their shared entry via content to be taught.
