## Description
The article's organizing approach is a qualitative meta-synthesis: rather than computing a new pooled estimate, it compares and contrasts the assumptions and findings of existing meta-analyses of PBL. The method treats divergent meta-analytic conclusions as data, explaining apparent contradictions (e.g., retention outcomes) by the type of outcome measured. As the article states, "This study used a qualitative meta-synthesis approach to compare and contrast the assumptions and findings of the meta-analytical research on the effectiveness of PBL."
