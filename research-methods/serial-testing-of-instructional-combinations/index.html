Serial testing of instructional combinations¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article argues that teaching happens in series while methodology tests are typically done in parallel, implicitly assuming no interaction effects. Its replicated 2×2 serial trial found a replicable interaction effect, leading the authors to state that "parallel modes of testing, even in an RCT format, might mislead and that lessons plans in series should be considered for fuller testing".
