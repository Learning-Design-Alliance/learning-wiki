# Checklist-based BPMN modelling with Delphi consensus for procedural skill breakdown

## Description
A methodology in which a procedure is first broken down using activities from psychometrically validated evaluation checklists, represented graphically in BPMN with tasks, sequence flows and gateways, then enriched and validated by a Delphi expert panel. The authors position it as "a simple, without requiring specialized resources, and easy to interpret alternative to those currently in use" for generating procedural curricula. It reuses existing evaluation work and produces a cross-specialty, consensus-based breakdown suitable for preclinical training stages.

- **Start procedural breakdowns from validated checklists to reduce resource demands and expert bias**: The article recommends beginning a procedure breakdown from one or more validated evaluation checklists, because these instruments have "proven useful in abstracting those activities considered critical when evaluating the achievement of a competence". This avoids the hours of interviews, trained personnel, and specialty-dependent bias of classical cognitive task analysis. Checklists alone cannot express decision points or alternative paths, so they serve only as the basis for a fuller BPMN model. (de la Fuente R. et al. (2020))
