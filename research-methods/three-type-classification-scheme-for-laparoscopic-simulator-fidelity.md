# Three-type classification scheme for laparoscopic simulator fidelity

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review proposes a classification scheme ordering laparoscopic simulators from Type 1 (a simple box, e.g. plain cardboard, with or without a mirror and webcam) through Type 2 (a box trainer with camera/laparoscope display and light source, with a 2s variant adding motion sensors) to Type 3 (virtual reality simulators with display-based software and hardware). It was introduced because "there is no standard definition of fidelity for laparoscopic simulation and no accepted classification", and the authors applied it to all reviewed studies so that HF and LF labels could be assigned consistently.
