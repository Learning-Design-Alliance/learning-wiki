# Typologies of replication studies (Lykken's three types; Schmidt's direct vs. conceptual)

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The editorial presents two classifications of replication studies. Lykken (1968) identified three types: literal, operational, and constructive replications. Schmidt (2009) re-categorized them into direct and conceptual replications, "with direct replications being the preferred path by many as they preserve the original instrumentation and approach." ETR&D accepts both direct and conceptual replications.
