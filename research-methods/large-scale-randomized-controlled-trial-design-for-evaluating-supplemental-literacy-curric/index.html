Large-scale randomized controlled trial design for evaluating supplemental literacy curricula¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 3 studies (3 causal), q3 · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The study's organizing method is "a large-scale randomized controlled trial of the effects of four supplemental reading comprehension curricula." Impact analyses are conducted separately by study year, allowing effects to differ across years and across teacher subgroups defined by experience using the intervention. This design supports causal claims about each curriculum's impact on students' reading comprehension scores.

Claims¶

  • Project CRISS, ReadAbout, and Read for Real showed no significant impacts on reading comprehension in the study's first year [+M]
  • ReadAbout had a positive and significant impact in the study's second year among teachers with one year of experience using the intervention [+W]
  • Reading for Knowledge had a statistically significant negative impact on students' reading comprehension scores in the study's first year [+W]
  • Four supplemental reading comprehension curricula had no positive impact on fifth-grade students' test scores, with negative impacts in some cases [+W]
  • After one school year of implementation (first cohort), the curricula produced no statistically significant positive impacts, and four statistically significant negative impacts were observed. [+W]
