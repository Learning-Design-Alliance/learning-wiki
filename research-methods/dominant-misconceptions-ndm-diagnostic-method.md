# Dominant misconceptions (nDM) diagnostic method

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
A diagnostic method for FCI pre-test answers in which each question's largest incorrect response is normalised and questions above a threshold are flagged as dominant misconceptions. The authors apply it to three UJ cohorts, where 'The DMs are normalised by subtracting away the %(correct answer) and a threshold is chosen where anything above this 'value' is deemed a DM.'
