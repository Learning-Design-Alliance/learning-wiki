# Five-category coding rubric for metacognitive sophistication in reflective journals

## Description
A new coding scheme created and validated for unguided and partially guided journals, adapted from the GRF rubric and inspired by Kori's scheme, which "forms a loose hierarchy of metacognitive sophistication." Its five categories are Description, Logic/rationale, Evaluation, Discussion, and Patterns of thought, "ordered in increasing sophistication." The scheme is considerably broader than the GRF rubric to account for the wider range of topics in journals, including discussions of other people's approaches.
