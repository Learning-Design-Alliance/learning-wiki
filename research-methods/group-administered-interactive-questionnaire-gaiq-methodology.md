# Group Administered Interactive Questionnaire (GAIQ) methodology

## Description
The GAIQ is a data-collection methodology the authors developed as a variation of interviews around concrete instructional settings, replacing the individual interview with a written questionnaire and group discussion. It was designed to "Encourage TAs' introspection and articulation about their beliefs" regarding example-solution design, triangulate findings across contexts, minimize researcher bias, and compare results with research-based pedagogies. Respondents judge carefully designed instructional artifacts that activate instructional decision-making in an imaginary classroom setting.
