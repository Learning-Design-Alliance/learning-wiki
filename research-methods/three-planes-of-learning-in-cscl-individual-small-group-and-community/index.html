Three planes of learning in CSCL: individual, small-group, and community¶

Description¶

The article names a tripartite unit-of-analysis framework for CSCL: "analyses of CSCL are often conducted on one of three levels: individual learning, small-group cognition or community knowledge building". The planes are actualized in classroom practice, where individual activities precede small-group work that culminates in whole-class sharing. The framework is grounded in CSCL practice and in Vygotsky's foundational role for intersubjective learning.

  • Three interdependent layers of units of analysis in CSCL: individual, small group, and community: The editorial proposes a conceptual framing of CSCL research units of analysis as three interdependent layers. As printed: "Conceptually, one could frame the different units of analysis in CSCL as three interdependent layers— individual, small group and community — all of which are needed to understand and explain collaborative learning with computational tools." The framing holds that different theoretical orientations (cognitive, socio-cognitive, socio-cultural) direct analytic attention to different layers, and that a full understanding requires all three, including how individual contributions constitute group collaborative efforts. (Sten Ludvigsen (2016))
