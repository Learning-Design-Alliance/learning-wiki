## Description
The review organizes synthesized evidence into four thematic domains: "AI in Clinical Education, Assessment, Simulation and Data-Based Quality Improvement". This taxonomy structures the narrative synthesis, mapping AI tools (ITS, adaptive platforms, NLP, computer vision, learning analytics) to educational functions and outcomes. It provides the organizing axis for the review's tables of applications, principles, competencies, and challenges.
