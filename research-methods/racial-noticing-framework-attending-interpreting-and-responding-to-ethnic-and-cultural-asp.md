## Description
Racial noticing, introduced by Shah and Coles (2020), extends the teacher-noticing framework from mathematics education to teachers' attention and sense-making of ethnic and cultural aspects of teaching and learning. The article states that "Shah and Coles (2020) describe three interrelated, sequential processes of racial noticing: attending, interpreting, and responding." It goes beyond a mere cognitive ability, resting on the assumption that power in an unequal and stratified society is also reflected in classroom activities. The present study operationalized the first two processes via vignette ratings of perceived microaggressions and projected harm to school belonging.
