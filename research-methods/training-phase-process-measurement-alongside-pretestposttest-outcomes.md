## Description
The article argues that effects of game elements can appear during the learning process even when pre-post learning outcomes do not differ, so research should measure performance during training. It states this "underscores the importance of studying the actual learning process when investigating the effects of game elements rather than analyzing learning outcomes alone (i.e., pretest–posttest differences)". In the study, only the training-phase measures revealed the accuracy and speed differences between conditions.
