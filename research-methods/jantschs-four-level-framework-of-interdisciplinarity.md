## Description
The article adopts Jantsch's framework of four levels of interdisciplinarity as an analytical lens: crossdisciplinarity, pluridisciplinarity, interdisciplinarity, and transdisciplinarity. The authors map these levels onto network features: "pluridisciplinarity to be visible in a network as sharp divisions between different subjects, interdisciplinarity to be visible as a focus on general Science-related competencies, while transdiciplinarity would be visible as a mixing between subjects." They use it to categorize the observed teaching.
