Metareview method: systematic review of systematic reviews with AMSTAR quality filtering¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article uses a review-of-reviews design: comprehensive multi-database searching identified 29 systematic reviews of digital asthma self-management interventions, which were then quality-appraised with AMSTAR so that only reviews meeting predetermined standards (at least 50% of quality indicators plus documented quality assessment of included studies) advanced to data extraction. This yielded 10 reviews describing 19 RCTs whose outcomes were synthesized narratively by outcome domain. The authors frame this as an approach that has "proven helpful in synthesizing a broad base of literature in order to identify research gaps and inform future intervention programs".
