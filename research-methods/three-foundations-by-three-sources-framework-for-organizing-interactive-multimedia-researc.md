## Description
The article proposes a matrix framework for organizing research and theory relevant to interactive multimedia design. As printed, "The matrix shown in Table 1 organizes existing research and theory according to foundations--psychological, pedagogical, technological--and basic sources-general, component, primary." Foundations represent fundamental influences on learning-system design, while sources represent how generalizable the principles are, from media-independent psychological theory to capabilities of specific interactive multimedia technologies. The framework is used throughout the article to root each design principle in identifiable empirical and theoretical referents.
