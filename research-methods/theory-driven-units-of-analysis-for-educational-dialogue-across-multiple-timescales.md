# Theory-driven units of analysis for educational dialogue across multiple timescales

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The Baker, Schwarz, and Ludvigsen Squib argues for deep theoretical understanding of discussion and its unit of analysis to inform methods for analyzing dialogue over different timescales, from episodes to societal-scale argumentative knowledge construction. The editors summarize: its aim is "to argue for the necessity for a deep, theoretical understanding of discussion and its unit of analysis." It proceeds in three parts: collective thinking enacted through dialogue, critical considerations of timescales, and how timescales are accounted for in units of analysis applied to platform data. General theories of dialogue, cognition, and collective activity guide chosen units of analysis and explain their deep meaning.
