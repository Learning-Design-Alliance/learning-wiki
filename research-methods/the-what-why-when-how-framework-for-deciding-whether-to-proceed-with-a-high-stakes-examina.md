# The 'what, why, when, how' framework for deciding whether to proceed with a high-stakes examination during a pandemic

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article applies a four-question decision framework, attributed to Fuller et al, for deciding whether and how to run a high-stakes examination during the COVID-19 pandemic. It 'addresses the purpose and necessity of the examination as well as the timing and format of the examination'. The authors work through each question for their gastroenterology licencing examination: what the examination is for, why it must proceed, when to hold it, and how to conduct it while 'adherence to standards and validity of the examination are critically important'.
