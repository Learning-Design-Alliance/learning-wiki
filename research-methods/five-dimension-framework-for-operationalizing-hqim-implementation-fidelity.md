# Five-dimension framework for operationalizing HQIM implementation fidelity

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report defines implementation as "a reduction in the translational difference between the essential, intended design elements of the curriculum and its enactment in classrooms" and operationalizes five dimensions. Dosage captures how much of the official curriculum teachers use; adherence captures following prescribed units, lessons, and sequence; instructional quality captures alignment of instruction with intended interactions; student experience captures students' perceptions of opportunities to learn; and support captures district and school professional learning. The framework treats implementation as contextual, informed by professional learning that shapes teachers' decisions to add, modify, or omit curriculum elements.

## Description
Dimension 5 of the construct map states that "Teachers implement HQIM with integrity while adapting to their students' needs and identities".
