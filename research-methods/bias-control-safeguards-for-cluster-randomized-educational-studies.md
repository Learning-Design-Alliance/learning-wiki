# Bias-control safeguards for cluster-randomized educational studies

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because blinding of participants and lecturers is usually not possible in educational research, the article recommends compensating measures to protect the outcome variable from bias: outcome variables that are as objective as possible and blind assessment, for example evaluation of problem-based learning outcomes by independent people not participating in the study. It also recommends maintaining structural equivalence by creating the same conditions for the groups, such as the same times and seminar rooms for the study arms being compared.
