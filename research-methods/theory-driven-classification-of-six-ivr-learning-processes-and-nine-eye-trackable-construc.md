## Description
The article develops a classification mapping eye-tracking metrics onto learning processes in IVR, grounded in CTML, CATLM, CASTLE, CAMIL, and Cognitive Load Theory. It proposes "a classification of six key learning processes (i.e., cognitive, motivational, affective, social, metacognitive, media-specific) and nine associated constructs (i.e., attention, visual strategy, cognitive load, motivation, emotion, social attention, self-regulation, immersion, usability) measurable through eye tracking in IVR." The media-specific category was added to capture IVR-specific processes such as embodiment, and review literature supplied the construct subcategories used as the coding scheme.
