## Description
The article frames its single-item probe ("How difficult was it to pay attention during the last minute of the lecture?") as a temporally localized reference signal targeting perceived difficulty sustaining attention, explicitly not equivalent to engagement as a whole. The authors write that they use it "as a narrower, engagement-related attentional experience rather than as engagement itself or its direct inverse", recognizing that a learner may remain engaged with demanding material while finding attention hard to sustain.
