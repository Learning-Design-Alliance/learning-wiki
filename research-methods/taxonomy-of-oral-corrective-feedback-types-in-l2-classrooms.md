## Description
The review's organizing taxonomy identifies the various types of oral corrective feedback teachers provide in second language classrooms. As the abstract states, "Various types of oral CF are first identified", establishing a classification of CF moves (such as recasts, prompts, and explicit correction, as discussed in the review) that structures the subsequent synthesis of frequency, preference, and effectiveness research. This taxonomy serves as the framework through which the review compares CF across instructional contexts and studies.
