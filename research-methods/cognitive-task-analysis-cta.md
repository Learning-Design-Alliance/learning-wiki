## Description
The article frames cognitive task analysis (CTA) as a process in which "experts provide complex information to trainees in a logical and simplified manner", making procedural steps easier to visualize and digest. CTA packages technical steps, cognitive decision points, and potential errors and solutions, delivered here through simultaneous written and audiovisual modalities. It has been used to train pilots and in other surgical specialties, and the authors position it as relatively new to orthopedic training.
