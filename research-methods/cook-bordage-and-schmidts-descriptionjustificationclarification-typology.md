## Description
The review adopts Cook, Bordage and Schmidt's threefold classification of research purposes: "description (what was carried out), justification (does it work), and finally, clarification (how does it work)". Description covers perceptions and implementation accounts, justification covers comparative effectiveness studies, and clarification covers theory- or framework-driven studies of how TBL works. The review uses this typology to classify 288 studies and to diagnose the field's lack of progression, inspired by wave analyses of PBL and programmatic assessment research.
