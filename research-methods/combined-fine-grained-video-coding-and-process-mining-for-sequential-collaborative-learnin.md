# Combined fine-grained video coding and process mining for sequential collaborative-learning analysis

## Description
The article introduces a methodological contribution that combines fine-grained video analysis with process mining to study sequential patterns linking challenges and collaborative regulatory activities. Coded event logs were analysed with the BupaR package in R, producing process models in which "boxes represent challenge and regulatory categories, with numbers indicating their frequency of occurrence" and arrows depict sequential transitions. Unlike coarse-grained or aggregated measures, this method captures micro-level events as they occur in real-time collaborative interaction, offering a structured framework for analysing sequential interactional patterns in large-scale educational interaction data.
