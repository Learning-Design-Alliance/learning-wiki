The article identifies automated analysis techniques for detecting learners' internal argumentative scripts during CABLe. "Scheuer et al.   • Automated analysis techniques and tools for diagnosing argumentation during discourse: The article identifies automated analysis techniques for detecting learners' internal argumentative scripts during CABLe. "Scheuer et al. ( 2012) classified available automated analysis techniques to this effect into four categories, namely syntactical anal ysis, problem-specifi c analysis, reasoning analysis, and collaborative filtering analysis." Named educational technologies using AI and language content analysis to analyze student argumentation moves and structures include ARGUNAUT, TagHelper, Rashi, Belvédère, and LARGO; NLP has also been used to code micro-argumentation prior to a learning phase. (Noroozi et al. (2018))
  • Rhetorical move typology of salient sentences for analytical writing analytics: The XIP rhetorical parser detects sentences conveying rhetorical moves in analytical texts, building on Swales' concept of rhetorical moves as communicative functions in scholarly argumentation. AWA labels salient sentence types: Summarizing issues (S), Background knowledge (B), Contrasting ideas (C), Emphasizing important ideas (E), Novel ideas (N), Surprising facts (S), open Questions (Q), and research Trends (T). Detection relies on "syntactically related words and expressions that convey constituent concepts", e.g. a pair conveying 'contrast' plus 'idea/mental operation' signals a Contrasting idea sentence. (Knight et al. (2016))
