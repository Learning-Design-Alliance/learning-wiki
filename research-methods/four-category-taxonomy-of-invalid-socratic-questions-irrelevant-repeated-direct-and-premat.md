# Four-category taxonomy of invalid Socratic questions: irrelevant, repeated, direct, and premature

## Description
The article organizes invalid Socratic questions — the negative samples used for preference optimization — into a four-category taxonomy adopted from Al-Hossami et al.: "invalid Socratic questions fall into the four following categories". Irrelevant questions shift focus from the actual bug; repeated questions have already been asked in previous dialogue turns; direct questions reveal the bug and "do not prompt students to think and may hinder their learning process"; and premature questions prompt code fixes before the bug is identified. The taxonomy drives both the GPT-4 generation prompts and the consistency-checking labels.
