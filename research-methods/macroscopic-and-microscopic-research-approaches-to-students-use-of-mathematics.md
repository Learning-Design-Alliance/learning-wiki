# Macroscopic and microscopic research approaches to students' use of mathematics

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review organizes existing research on students' use of mathematics in upper-division physics into two approaches differing in scale, methods, and goals. As the authors state, "researchers have often taken one of two basic approaches: (1) a macroscopic approach that aims to uncover difficulties that students encounter with particular concepts and tools, and (2) a microscopic approach that is more grounded in learning theory". Macroscopic work typically uses written work and interviews at the whole-class scale; microscopic work uses in situ observations and semi-structured interviews at the single-student or small-group scale. The authors argue connecting the two is necessary for greater impact on student learning.
