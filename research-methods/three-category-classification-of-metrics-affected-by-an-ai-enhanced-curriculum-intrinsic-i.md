# Three-category classification of metrics affected by an AI-enhanced curriculum: intrinsic, implicit, and extrinsic

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The article organizes the performance metrics affected by the AI-enhanced curriculum into three categories. Intrinsic metrics are the preselected learning objectives the tutor directly taught; implicit metrics "despite receiving no direct feedback demonstrated significant change by virtue of their close association with the intrinsic learning objectives"; and extrinsic metrics, such as the rate of healthy tissue removal, have a more complex relationship with the intended objectives that cannot be easily implied. This taxonomy frames how unintended learning outcomes of AI tutoring are analyzed.
