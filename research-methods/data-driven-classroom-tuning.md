## Description
A methodology by which educators 'constantly improve their teaching by identifying teaching methods, instructional tools, and pedagogies that work best in their own classroom and acting upon findings'. It comprises four steps: flexible assessment letting students choose learning activities; acquiring an initial level of preparation (prior grades, pre-test, background data); predictive modelling of exam scores after the course ends; and matching the model's residual, called Added Value, to students' preferred learning activities. The article implements it with symbolic regression and a companion app.
