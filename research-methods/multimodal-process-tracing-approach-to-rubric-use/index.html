Multimodal process-tracing approach to rubric use¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article adopts a methodological framework combining eye-tracking with think-aloud protocols to capture students' real-time engagement with rubrics during academic tasks. Eye-tracking supplies fixation times, number of visits, and gaze transitions between rubric and task stimuli, while think-aloud protocols capture verbalized reasoning and strategies. The authors state that "The integration of these two data sources follows a logic of triangulation common in mixed-methods research", allowing them to identify convergences (prioritization of PL4) and divergences (visual behavior predicting performance while verbal references did not). No prior study had combined both methods to examine how students read and use rubrics.
