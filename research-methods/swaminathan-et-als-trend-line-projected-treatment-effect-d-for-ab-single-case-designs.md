# Swaminathan et al.'s trend-line projected treatment-effect D for AB single-case designs

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes a treatment effect measure for AB single-subject designs based on linear models fitted separately to each phase. The authors state that the effect of the intervention may be defined as the difference between the pints on the trend line in Phase B and the points of the Phase A trend line that is projected into Phase B, calculated at all points in Phase B and averaged to yield D. This yields a single interpretable measure even when both level and trend change across phases.
