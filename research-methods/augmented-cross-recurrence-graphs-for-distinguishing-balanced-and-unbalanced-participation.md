# Augmented cross-recurrence graphs for distinguishing balanced and unbalanced participation

## Description
A new visualization developed by the authors for dual eye-tracking data. The paper states that "we additionally developed a new visualization (augmented cross-recurrence graphs) that allows researchers to distinguish between high JVA groups that have balanced and unbalanced levels of participations." It augments cross-recurrence analysis of gaze data so researchers can see whether similarly high joint visual attention conceals unequal participation between collaborators.
