# KLD and MultiMatch as complementary gaze synchrony measures for lecture-video attention

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The article proposes assessing gaze synchrony with two established measures chosen for a relatively static slide-based video stimulus: Kullback-Leibler divergence between gaze density maps, which captures the spatial distribution of fixations, and the MultiMatch scanpath comparison, which "incorporates the temporal dimension by considering the sequence and a range of other multidimensional gaze properties". The two measures correlated modestly (r = −0.45), suggesting they "provide complementary insights into the nature of gaze behavior". Both showed a stronger connection to self-reported attention than the previously used ISC baseline, likely due to lower time sensitivity.
