# Three-phase instrument-development pattern for competency inventories

## Description
The article develops the SMCI through three sequential phases modeled on Crocker and Algina's 10-step test construction process: Phase 1, Conceptualization and Domain Specifications (literature review, expert panel review, tables of specifications); Phase 2, Item Development (expert review of items, think-aloud sessions, pilot test); and Phase 3, Inventory Testing and Finalization (field test with factor and Rasch analyses, then administration and interpretation guidelines). Think-aloud sessions provided response process validity evidence.

- **Blueprint-driven development of scalable competence measures: content definition, expert item generation, field testing and Rasch calibration**: The article describes a reusable multi-phase design pattern for building scalable therapist-competence measures: define content via a blueprint linking the content to be learned with assessment items, generate plausible-distractor multiple-choice items from typical trainee errors, field-test with a heterogeneous sample, remove misfitting items via Rasch analysis, and equate versions via anchor items. "Blueprints systematically seek to link the definition of the content to be learned with the specific items that are covered by the assessment measure." The authors state the method "may be used to develop and test measures relating to therapist competence in other treatments." (Cooper Z. et al. (2015))
