## Description
The authors analyzed all reports with a qualitative grid following "empirical research methods where research questions are dominant with respect to class analysis". Disciplinary content was rated in bands (no mention to above 66%); pedagogical approach 0-3 (from no mention to "activity sequences and operative proposals oriented to overcome learning difficulties"); transversal elements 0-3 (from no mention to "with resort to transversality to overcome learning problems"); plus material usage counts and 20 exclusive path-quality categories covering learning-problem orientation, interdisciplinarity, methodological rigours and motivation.
