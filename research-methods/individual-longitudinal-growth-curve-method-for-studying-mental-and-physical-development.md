## Description
Baldwin advocates the individualizing method: following the same children consecutively over years with both physical and mental measurements, rather than cross-sectional snapshots. He argues that "The day for the study of physical growth processes by single measurements is over" and that prediction of later status requires identifying a child's growth-curve type, chronological and physiological age. The method underpins all the paper's prediction analyses and his call for intensive consecutive studies of the same individuals across a series of years.
