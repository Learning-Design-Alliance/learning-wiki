# The tutoring loop: a process-centered framework for LLM-based adaptive tutoring

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article introduces a process-centered view that treats learner evidence, learner-state inference (L1), pedagogical action (L2), tutor training (L3a), evaluation (L3b), and learner outcomes as dependent stages of one process. It asks whether evidence crosses the interfaces: 'does the learner-state estimate change the next intervention', whether the intervention changes learner behavior, and whether supporting models are valid. Unlike prior application-based taxonomies, its unit of analysis is the tutoring process itself.
