# Coding scheme for collaborative diagnostic activities across 11 scientific reasoning sub-skills

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Elicitation (asking for evidence pertinent to the diagnosed pupil's scientific reasoning skills), sharing (sharing such evidence), and negotiation (negotiating how consistent or contradictory observations affect the joint diagnosis) were coded from screen recordings at the level of the individual, for each of 11 sub-skills spanning hypothesis formulation, experimentation, and drawing conclusions. Dyad-level coverage variables counted sub-skills for which any participant performed an activity, divided by the maximum score of 11. Inter-coder agreement was acceptable to excellent (e.g., negotiation ICCabsolute = .67 in the learning phase; elicitation ICCabsolute = 1.00).
