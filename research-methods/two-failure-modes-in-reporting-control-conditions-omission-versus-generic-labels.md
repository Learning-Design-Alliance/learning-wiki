# Two failure modes in reporting control conditions: omission versus generic labels

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Among studies lacking clarity in teaching material and method, the review distinguishes two categories: studies that "simply did not mention these two components", and studies that mention them "but resolves them in generic terms (e.g.: defining them as 'standard', 'classical' or 'traditional')". This pattern does not apply to evaluation-method reporting, since even unclear evaluations are mentioned as an essential part of the experimental design.
