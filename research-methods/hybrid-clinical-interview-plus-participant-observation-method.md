# Hybrid clinical-interview-plus-participant-observation method

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's data-collection instrument is a testing method applied to five street-vendor children (four boys and one girl, aged 9-15, mean 11.2, grades one to eight). In the informal test, the researcher posed as a customer and posed purchase questions in the natural working setting, questioning subjects about their solution methods afterward. The authors state the "method can be described as a hybrid between the Piagetian clinical method and participant observation". Formal test items were then built for each subject from the problems that subject had solved informally, presented as dictated operations or word problems with paper and pencil.
