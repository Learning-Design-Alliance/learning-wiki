# Four heuristics for empirically choosing the unit of cognitive analysis (clustering, persistence, resistance, transition)

## Description
The article proposes four heuristics for grounding the choice of cognitive unit in data: clustering (simultaneous activation of multiple elements), persistence (clusters lasting over time), resistance to change (clusters surviving perturbations or bids), and transitions (sudden shifts marking the boundaries of stabilities). The authors stress these are 'not a coding scheme, but rather a guide for empirically grounding the choice of the unit of analysis.' The cognitive unit can be modeled 'at the grain size(s) at which there are observable stabilities and coherent dynamics in cognitive activity.'
