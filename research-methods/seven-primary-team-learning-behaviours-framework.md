## Description
The review adopts the framework of seven primary team learning behaviours: knowledge sharing, constructive conflict, co-construction, boundary crossing, team activity, team reflexivity, and storage and retrieval. These are forms of social interaction between team members while accomplishing work tasks, such as "giving feedback, sharing, storing and retrieving knowledge, jointly reflecting on work strategies, and communicating with individuals outside the team". The seven behaviours served as the categories in the narrative synthesis of the included studies.
