# Centrality measures taxonomy for educational social network analysis

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The article organizes educational social network analysis around centrality measures grouped into three constructs: quantity of interactions (in-degree, out-degree, degree), role in moderating and relaying information (betweenness, information, closeness centrality), and connectedness (eigenvector centrality, eccentricity, clustering coefficient). It notes that "centrality can be calculated in various ways depending on the context" and that each measure captures a distinct aspect of a student's position, from popularity to brokering to social capital. The framework is applied to course-level, thread-level and ego-network analysis of forum interactions.
