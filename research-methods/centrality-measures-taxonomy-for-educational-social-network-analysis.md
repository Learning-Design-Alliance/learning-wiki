# Centrality measures taxonomy for educational social network analysis

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The article organizes educational social network analysis around centrality measures grouped into three constructs: quantity of interactions (in-degree, out-degree, degree), role in moderating and relaying information (betweenness, information, closeness centrality), and connectedness (eigenvector centrality, eccentricity, clustering coefficient). It notes that "centrality can be calculated in various ways depending on the context" and that each measure captures a distinct aspect of a student's position, from popularity to brokering to social capital. The framework is applied to course-level, thread-level and ego-network analysis of forum interactions.

- **Framework of nodal centrality measures for quantifying student connectivity in homework-collaboration networks**: The article presents a set of network-analytic centrality measures for quantifying a student's connection to peers in directed, weighted collaboration networks built from homework help reports. Local measures capture direct connections: "The in-strength, out-strength, net-streng th, in- disparity, and out-disparity are all local centrality meas ures while the harmonic, closeness, and betweenness centraliti es are global measures." In- and out-strength count help received and given; disparities measure non-uniformity of links; closeness and harmonic centralities capture degrees of separation; betweenness captures hub status bridging groups. The framework also introduces weighted reciprocity, not considered in the prior study. (Nathan Crossette et al. (2022))
