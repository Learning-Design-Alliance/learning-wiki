# Three-mode fidelity framework for virtual simulation engagement

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The article defines fidelity as "the learner’s perceptions of how well a simulation represents or reproduces reality" and identifies three modes: physical (properties of the VP and environment, voice-over acting), conceptual or semantic (concepts and the if/then logic linking MI-consistent skills to dialogue opening), and emotional and experiential (learners' emotions and beliefs about the holistic experience). Strategies per mode are tabulated, from photographing a real nurse's office to co-designing content with an HIV/MI expert nurse and establishing a fiction contract.
