> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The article presents a Virtual Patient typology developed from the literature, a review of existing VP systems, results from a student focus-group study, and an international modified-Delphi consensus among VP experts. It "synthesised 19 factors concerning four categories identified as relevant to VP design", giving researchers and designers a common reference point for studies or reports on Virtual Patients. The typology organises VP design attributes from general metadata to technical integration.
