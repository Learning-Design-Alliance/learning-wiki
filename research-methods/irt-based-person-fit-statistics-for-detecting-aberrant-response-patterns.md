## Description
The paper proposes applying Item Response Theory and, within it, Person-Fit Statistics to educational assessment as a lens for detecting GenAI cheating. PFS is "a collection of statistical methods designed to assess the alignment between an individual's test performance and an expected response pattern". Because AI and human intelligence differ architecturally, AI cheating manifests as deviations from expected human response patterns, quantified by non-parametric statistics (G, G*, U3, ZU3) computed per examinee. The framework grounds a learning-analytics approach in established educational measurement theory rather than ad-hoc detection heuristics.
