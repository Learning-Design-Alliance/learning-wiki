# Functional system incorporating tools as the unit of analysis for CSCL microanalysis

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Arnseth and Krange argue, building on Vygotsky and Wertsch, that "the unit of analysis needs to be a functional system incorporating tools." This stance opens up understanding of the multiplicity of activities, in which collaboration between students and computational tools can take multiple paths, and analysts must understand how participants orient themselves through a series of activities and why they choose specific paths. The editors present it as grounding ethnographic description combined with detailed turn-by-turn interaction analysis of upper-secondary students' inquiry into energy phenomena.
