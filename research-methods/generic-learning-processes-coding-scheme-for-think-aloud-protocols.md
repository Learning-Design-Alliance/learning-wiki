# Generic learning processes coding scheme for think-aloud protocols

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A coding scheme used to classify think-aloud utterances into mutually exclusive categories of superficial processes (e.g., reading/watching and filtering, checking solution steps), elaborative processes (developing and testing hypotheses, relating and integrating, giving (self-)explanations), and regulative processes (orienting, planning, monitoring, reflecting). It was piloted and adjusted, and "was constructed to have a generic character allowing the representation of all instructional approaches and applicability to problem-solving domains other than probability theory". Interrater reliability reached .85 (Cohen's kappa) for the three main categories and .74 for subcategories.
