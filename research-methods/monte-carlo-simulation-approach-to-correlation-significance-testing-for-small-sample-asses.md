# Monte Carlo simulation approach to correlation significance testing for small-sample assessment data

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article develops a Monte Carlo method for testing the statistical significance of correlations in assessment data with small samples, where many question-pair correlations risk chance findings. Stochastic models of the student population (multinomial models of answer patterns) are sampled, and p-values estimated for observed correlations against a zero-correlation model. The authors state: "We have also developed and applied Monte Carlo simulation techniques suitable for the analysis of correlations in data with small sample size." They apply it to the RCI's 276 question pairs, using a threshold of p < 1/276.
