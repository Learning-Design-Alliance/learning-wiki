## Description
The article operationalizes proportional reasoning in physics as six subskills: identifying ratio as a useful measure, interpreting a ratio verbally, constructing a ratio from measured values, applying a ratio to determine an unknown amount, translating between representations of proportional relationships, and scaling a proportional relationship. 'We regard fluid and accurate use of all six reasoning modes as evidence of overall competence.' The framework is intended to inform empirical research on student reasoning and the development of instructional strategies.
