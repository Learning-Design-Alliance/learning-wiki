# Percentage of non-overlapping data (PND) with Scruggs and Mastropieri effectiveness cutoffs

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
PND is a non-parametric measure for evaluating single-subject research, calculated by dividing the number of treatment-phase data points exceeding the highest baseline data point by the total treatment data points and multiplying by 100. The article applies Scruggs and Mastropieri (1998) cutoffs: "very effective treatments (over 90 PND), effective treatments (between 70 and 90 PND), questionable effectiveness (between 50 and 70 PND), and ineffective treatments (below 50 PND)." Inter-observer agreement for PND calculations in this meta-analysis was 90.2%.
