# Two-dimensional framework for classifying electronics use in research labs

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article develops a framework for classifying instances of electronics use in research labs along two dimensions. The Type dimension — "referring to the type of electronics content knowledge needed for the work" — comprises Analog, Digital, Programming, Black Box, and Safety categories, with Safety "added during coding as an emergent category." The Interaction dimension — "referring to how researchers interacted with their equipment" — comprises Design, Build, Use, and Fix, with Fix also emergent. Categories are distinct but "not meant to be mutually exclusive."
