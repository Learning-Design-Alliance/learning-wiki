# Three-axis taxonomy of agentic AI in education: pedagogical role, autonomy level and embodiment

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review proposes an organizing taxonomy for educational agentic AI built on three axes. The first axis covers pedagogical roles (tutor, coach/mentor, companion, instructor's assistant, curriculum planner); the second orders systems from reactive to adaptive, proactive and collaborative agents by autonomy; the third distinguishes text-based, graphical/avatar, embodied and multimodal agents. As the authors state, "we propose a taxonomy based on three axes: pedagogical role, autonomy level and embodiment", summarized in their Figure 2.
