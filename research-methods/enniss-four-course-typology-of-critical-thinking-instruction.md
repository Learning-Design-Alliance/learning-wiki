# Ennis's four-course typology of critical-thinking instruction

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article uses Ennis's (1989) typology to classify CT instructional interventions. In "generic courses, CT skills and dispositions are the course objective, with no specific subject matter content", while "content is important in both the infusion and immersion approaches": CT is an explicit objective in infusion but not in immersion. The mixed approach combines a separate general CT thread with subject-specific instruction. The typology is the article's baseline classification scheme for coding intervention type, which this review assesses for instructional efficacy.
