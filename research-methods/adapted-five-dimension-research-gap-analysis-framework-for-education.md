# Adapted five-dimension research gap analysis framework for education

## Description
A taxonomy for structuring research agendas in education, adapted from Miles's (2017) seven-dimension research gap framework by collapsing the Knowledge and Theoretical gaps and the Empirical and Evidence gaps into single dimensions. The article states "The seven-dimension deductive research gap analysis framework proposed by Miles23 was then used as an organising structure" and that the adapted framework yields five dimensions: gaps in existing theory and knowledge, between research and practice, in research methodologies, in empirical testing and evidence verification, and in research with specific populations. It is used to organise a thematic gap analysis of international expert workshop content.
