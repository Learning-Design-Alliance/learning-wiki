# SLATE's four-dimensional theory-grounded evaluation framework (D1–D4)

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
SLATE evaluates generated teaching decks along four complementary, theory-driven dimensions. As the article states, these are "linguistic knowledge validity (D1), cognitive-pedagogical design (D2), visual communication quality (D3), and learning and transfer outcomes (D4)". D1 and D2 are grounded in Cognitive Load Theory and the Cognitive Theory of Multimedia Learning; D3 provides lightweight automatic visual-support diagnostics; D4 measures learner-side outcomes through a pretest–study–posttest–transfer protocol operationalizing near and far transfer.
