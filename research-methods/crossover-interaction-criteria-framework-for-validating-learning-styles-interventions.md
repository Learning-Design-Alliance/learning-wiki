# Crossover-interaction criteria framework for validating learning-styles interventions

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review defines a four-part experimental design that any credible validation of learning-styles-based instruction must satisfy: learners are classified by learning style, randomly assigned to at least two instructional methods, all take the same achievement test, and results must show a crossover interaction such that "the learning method that optimizes test performance of one learning-style group is different than the learning method that optimizes the test performance of a second learning-style group." The framework distinguishes acceptable evidence (Figs. 1A–1C) from interactions that look like crossovers only when method is plotted on the horizontal axis. It also separates the broad learning-styles hypothesis from the specific meshing hypothesis.
