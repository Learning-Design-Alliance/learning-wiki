# Three-genre taxonomy of research on games for learning: value-added, cognitive consequences, and media comparison

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The book organizes all research on games for learning into three research paradigms. The value-added approach compares a base game against the base game plus one added feature; the cognitive consequences approach compares pretest-to-posttest change for extended off-the-shelf game play versus no play; the media comparison approach compares learning from a game versus conventional media on the same material. The book states it "distinguishes among three genres of game research: the value-added approach, the cognitive consequences approach, and the media comparison approach" and systematizes the field by sorting experiments into these categories.
