# TA-calibrated depercolation method for identifying small groups in small, dense contact networks

## Description
The article introduces an analysis procedure for small, dense networks in which "links are removed from weakest to strongest", tracking the steps at which the network breaks into disconnected components (a depercolation tree akin to a dendrogram). The teaching assistant serves as calibration: "groups that are connected at the depercolation step which detaches the TA are classiﬁed as strongly connected". The stated advantage over conventional community detection is that standard methods fail here because links between groups are too numerous, so removing high-betweenness links breaks subgroups rather than revealing them.
