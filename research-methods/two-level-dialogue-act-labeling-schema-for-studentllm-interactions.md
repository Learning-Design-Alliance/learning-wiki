# Two-level dialogue act labeling schema for student–LLM interactions

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors developed a two-leveled dialogue act schema with 8 broad labels and 31 specific labels (Writing, Editing, Contextual Questions, Conceptual Questions, Verification, Context, Off Topic, Misc), built on prior co-programming work and their own systematic review of the conversations. Annotation at scale used a GPT4.1 prompting approach; human validation reached a Cohen's kappa of 0.910 for broad labels and 0.788 for specific labels, and human–LLM agreement on a held-out set was on par with human inter-annotator agreement.

- **Six-category coding framework for student prompts to GenAI (Command vs. Questioning behaviors)**: The study adapts a coding framework from Liu et al. to categorize student prompts into communicative acts, distinguishing Command Behaviors from Questioning Behaviors. Table 1 lists six types: Abstract Generation Command (e.g., "Write an abstract for me"), AI Improvement Command, Meta-Command, Clarification Question, Factual Question, and Challenging Question (e.g., "Are you sure about that percentage?"). Two researchers independently coded a subset, achieving Cohen's κ = 0.75. (Angxuan Chen (2026))
