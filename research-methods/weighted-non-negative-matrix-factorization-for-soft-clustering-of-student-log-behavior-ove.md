# Weighted non-negative matrix factorization for soft clustering of student log behavior over weekly periods

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article uses weighted non-negative matrix factorization (WNMF) as a soft clustering method: features aggregated per student per weekly activity period form a matrix X approximated as UV, where V gives feature importance per cluster and U gives each student-period's membership in each cluster. Missing values (e.g. absent quiz scores) are handled via a binary weight matrix, and clusters are rescaled so rows of V sum to one. The method is applied once over two years of Clio Online data, allowing tracking of behavior at individual and system-wide levels.
