# Two-axis framework placing lab types on continua of discovery and broad relevance

## Description
The article conceptualizes instructional labs along two continua (Fig. 1): discovery, defined by who knows the answer to the investigated question (from known to everyone in the class to unknown to the scientific community), and broad relevance, defined by the lab's relevance to the physics community (from not relevant beyond the classroom to directly relevant to researchers). The framework distinguishes broad relevance from personal relevance, and places traditional labs, SALT, PEPPER, undergraduate research, and CUREs at different positions, enabling component-isolation studies.
