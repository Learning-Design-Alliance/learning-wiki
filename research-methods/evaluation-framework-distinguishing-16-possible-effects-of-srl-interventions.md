# Evaluation framework distinguishing 16 possible effects of SRL interventions

## Description
The article proposes a framework for evaluating interventions supporting self-regulated learning, built on four distinctions: targeted SRL processes versus domain-level learning; current versus future learning; same versus a new learning environment; same versus a different domain. "These four items define a space of 16 possible effects on learning that interventions to support SRL may have." It relates the Help Tutor studies to this space and argues future research should test additional cells, such as effects on future domain-level learning. The authors see measuring SRL both during and after an intervention as key to moving the field forward.
