# Four-type taxonomy of word-problem process models by process order and mental representation

## Description
The article organizes existing cognitive models of word-problem solving along two axes: whether text comprehension and computation occur in a "linear" or "flexible" order, and whether they use "separable or integrated mental representations". This yields four model types (linear-separable, flexible-separable, linear-integrated, flexible-integrated), each predicting distinct patterns of canonical, phase-crossover, and attention-crossover effects on eye-tracking data. The taxonomy is used to judge which model best fits the observed solution process.
