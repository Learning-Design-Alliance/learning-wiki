## Description
The editorial describes Cognitive Work Analysis as a technique drawn from engineering fields that Kim MacKinnon applies to educational research. It is offered as a response to recognized weaknesses of design-based research, including that "there is no specified methodology for analyzing the collaborative student usage of CSCL technologies" and the difficulty of generalizing from single case studies. By characterizing a case study's context, the technique is intended to help judge its broader relevance.
