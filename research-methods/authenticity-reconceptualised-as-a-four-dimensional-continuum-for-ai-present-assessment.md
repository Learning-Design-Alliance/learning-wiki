# Authenticity reconceptualised as a four-dimensional continuum for AI-present assessment

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper reconceptualises authenticity as "a multidimensional continuum rather than a binary", locating it across four intersecting dimensions: task–context alignment with contemporary professional practice, foregrounding of professional judgement and ethics, visibility of process (iteration, critique, rationale), and appropriate use of tools including AI within human decision-making. Different design strategies emphasise different dimensions, and combining strategies strengthens authenticity only when it deepens performance on these dimensions. The appropriate level of authenticity is stage-appropriate across a program of study.
