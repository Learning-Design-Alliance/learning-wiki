# Four-phase development plan for upper-division modeling assessments

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A staged instrument-development plan for creating standardized, scalable assessments of experimental modeling skills in optics and electronics labs. Its phases are to "(i) establish test objectives, (ii) characterize student navigation of lab practicum-style activities, (iii) create a free-response assessment, and (iv) create a validated multiple-response-style assessment." Practicum observations inform free-response questions, and free-response answers inform multiple-response options. This article reports on the first phase. The final instruments are intended to use a Coupled Multiple Response format and centralized data collection and analysis.
