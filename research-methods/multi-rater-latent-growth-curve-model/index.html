Multi-rater latent growth curve model¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article introduces a model for estimating growth from longitudinal data in which multiple raters evaluate a construct such as a student's self-efficacy. The model "removes all but the shared perceptions of raters at a given timepoint (i.e., removes unique rater variance), then adds on a latent growth curve model across timepoints." It was developed because few existing models estimate growth in the presence of multiple raters.
