# Five-component, three-level framework for analysing mathematics teachers' practices

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To analyse teacher practices generally, the framework adds three nested levels of organisation — global (projects), local (the class), micro (automatisms and routines) — to the double didactic and ergonomic approach. Practices are specified through five components: cognitive and mediative components covering observable choices of content and unfoldings, institutional and social components covering constraints, and a personal component. The article notes that in beginning teachers the global and micro levels are not yet developed.
