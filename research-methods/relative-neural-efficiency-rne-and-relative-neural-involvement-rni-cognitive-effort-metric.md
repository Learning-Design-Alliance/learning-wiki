# Relative Neural Efficiency (RNE) and Relative Neural Involvement (RNI) cognitive-effort metrics

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size

## Description
The article applies a cognitive effort framework in which brain activity alone cannot distinguish focus from struggle, so effort is assessed by combining neural activity with performance. Two z-scored metrics are derived: RNE, where "High RNE means good performance with less effort", and RNI, which "shows how engaged or motivated a person is". The study computes these from fNIRS-derived oxygenated hemoglobin and quiz scores in an educational game to detect efficient learning, engagement, and cognitive fatigue.
