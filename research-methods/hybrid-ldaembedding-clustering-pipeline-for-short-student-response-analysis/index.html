Hybrid LDA–embedding clustering pipeline for short student-response analysis

A portable text-analysis pipeline combining Latent Dirichlet Allocation (k=8 topics on unigrams/bigrams) with sentence embeddings from the small multilingual encoder EmbeddingGemma-300M, projected via UMAP (cosine metric) and clustered with HDBSCAN.
