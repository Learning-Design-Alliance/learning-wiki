# Emergent Explicit Regulation (EER) framework

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
EER characterizes moments when a student spontaneously makes a regulatory move to direct or adjust a group activity in response to a challenge or concern. Its elements are a challenge or concern, a context of emergence, explicit externalized expressions, target areas, and effects. The framework captures how regulation develops from implicit monitoring to explicit action, situating itself within socially shared regulation of learning.
