Latent structural readiness space for digital and AI transformation¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The framework trains a variational autoencoder on standardized country-level indicators of autonomy, digital skills and teacher support, projecting them into a two-dimensional latent space interpreted as "a structural readiness space for digital and AI-related transformations in education". Geometric proximity reflects similarity in joint learning environment configurations, and a composite AI Readiness Index is formed as a linear aggregation of the two latent coordinates (α = 0.5). Countries with higher latent readiness tend to show stronger ICT aspiration growth, though the gradient is imperfect.
