# Two-level multisite cluster-randomized trial with within-school teacher randomization

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study evaluated CARE for Teachers using "a two-level (teachers/classrooms, schools) multisite cluster randomized trial design with intervention at level two (teachers) and schools serving as naturally occurring blocks". Teachers were block-randomized within schools by grade after baseline collection, yielding 118 intervention and 106 control teachers. The authors note this design "has been recommended as a strategy to control between-school variability" and requires fewer schools for adequate power.
