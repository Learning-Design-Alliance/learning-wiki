# Three-step structural assessment process: elicitation, representation, evaluation

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article distinguishes three consecutive steps to implement a structural approach to measure and interpret knowledge structures. Knowledge elicitation captures the underlying organization of knowledge through methods such as card sorting or rating relatedness between concept pairs. Knowledge representation transforms ratings into a graphical network. Knowledge evaluation compares the learner's structure with a referent knowledge structure using similarity or coherence measures.
