# Four-dimension taxonomy of teaching quality in medical education

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review proposes that all target parameters used to assess teaching quality fall into four dimensions. The structural dimension covers the physical environment, teaching materials and curriculum design; the process dimension covers teacher-student interaction and learning atmosphere; the teacher dimension covers teaching skills, preparation and perceived enthusiasm; and the outcome dimension covers learning outcome and professional attitudes. The review structures its entire analysis of instruments and confounders around this taxonomy.
