Cautious interpretation of within-domain effects in the classic I/E model¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that researchers avoid interpreting within-domain effects in the classic I/E model as exclusively reflecting social comparisons. The authors state that "labeling the within-domain effects in the classic I/E model as 'social comparison effects', as occasionally done in the literature... should be avoided" because these effects likely also reflect dimensional comparisons, criterion-based comparisons against individual or objective standards, and temporal comparisons. They recommend the more cautious terms "within-domain effects" and "between-domain effects."
