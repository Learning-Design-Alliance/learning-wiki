# Two-dimensional framework classifying digital learning environments by adaptivity and basis in an explicit model of learning

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article frames web-based learning environments along two dimensions: "the extent to which they adaptively respond to student behavior and (2) the extent to which they are based on a careful cognitive model". Static environments deliver fixed-order content with identical feedback; dynamic environments track behavior (errors, error rates, time-on-problem) and use a programmed decision tree to select problem sets and feedback based on estimated skill mastery; cognitively-based dynamic systems additionally encode the cognitive processing needed for skill acquisition and can adapt and fade scaffolds. Examples named include ALEKS, Khan Academy, ActiveMath, and Cognitive Tutor.
