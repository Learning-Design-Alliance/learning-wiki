## Description
The study adopts a qualitative categorization scheme based on the scientific literacy indicators proposed by Sasseron (2018), presented in Table 1, to analyze students' statements during the investigative activity. The indicators "range from the identification of scientific concepts and phenomena to the ability to argue based on evidence and apply knowledge to new contexts" and include sequencing, organization and classification of information, hypothesis formulation and testing, justification, prediction, and explanation. The authors use them to identify levels of students' appropriation of scientific concepts and investigative practice.
