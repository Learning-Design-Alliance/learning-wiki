# Four-category taxonomy of blueprint criteria: disease-, patient-, learner- and encounter-related

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article organizes the variables specified for every VP into four categories. Disease-related criteria cover final diagnoses, key symptoms, disease onset, and scenario closure; patient-related criteria cover demographic and identity characteristics; learner-related criteria cover the learner's role in the scenario; encounter-related criteria cover the consultation setting. The authors grouped criteria "into (i) disease-related, (ii) patient-related, (iii) learner-related, and (iv) encounter-related criteria" and mapped diagnoses to MeSH and national competency frameworks to ease curricular integration. The taxonomy operationalizes situativity theory's claim that clinical reasoning depends on contextual factors related to the patient and the encounter.
