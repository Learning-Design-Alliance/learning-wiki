## Description
The chapter proposes a diagnostic taxonomy of human–AI teaming with five levels: transactional, situational, operational, praxical, and synergistic. Each level 'introduces one additional affordance over the level beneath it', and 'the classification is functional and is intended to be coded from observable behaviour' such as whether the system surfaces state, accepts governance, revises durably from feedback, or co-reasons. Interaction types are querying, monitoring, governing, adapting, and co-reasoning; diagnosis proceeds top-down, classifying a system at the highest threshold it meets.

- **Five levels of teacher-AI teaming: transactional, situational, operational, praxical and synergistic**: The chapter proposes an orthogonal conceptual framework of teacher-AI teaming focused on information flows, decisions and actions shared between teacher and AI, grounded in distributed cognition. The authors state: "we propose (1) transactional, (2) situational, (3) operational, (4) praxical and (5) synergistic levels of teaming, as shown in Fig. 8.1." Levels run from minimal request-response interactions to mutual co-adaptation, and the framework is validated against a systematic review of teacher-facing AI studies. The authors caution the levels "need not always follow a progressive ordered hierarchy" and that improving teaming level does not guarantee improving teaching performance. (Mutlu Cukurova et al.
