## Description
The paper's stated purpose is "to provide cautionary guidance regarding the interpretation of distributions of site-specific impact estimates in multi-site experiments". Because within-site sample sizes in typical multi-site RCTs are small and reliabilities low, visualized distributions of fixed or random effect estimates reflect normal sampling error rather than the true distribution of impacts. Researchers should therefore not read shape features (skewness, outliers, modality) of such plots as features of true site-level impact variation.
