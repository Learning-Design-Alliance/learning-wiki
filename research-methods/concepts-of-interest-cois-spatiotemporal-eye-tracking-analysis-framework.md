# Concepts of Interest (COIs) spatiotemporal eye-tracking analysis framework

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The COI framework redefines the unit of analysis for eye tracking in video learning. Rather than spatial-layout AOIs, a COI is "a concept-indexed spatiotemporal region of the learning material that can serve as a unit for aggregating learners' eye tracking evidence." Each COI has three components: a Concept Identifier linking to a course concept map, an Instructional Episode of aligned video/audio/slide intervals, and Concept-driven AOIs as its visual footprint. Four relation types—sequence, inclusion, association, similarity—organize COIs into a relational network, and a hierarchical COIx–y–z indexing scheme supports multi-granularity analysis.
