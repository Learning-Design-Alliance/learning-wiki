# Graphical-model (DAG) framework for causal inference with time-varying educational treatments and confounder feedback

## Description
The article proposes modeling the education system as time-varying treatments (e.g. solving problems, talking to the TA or counselor), observed and unobserved time-varying confounders, and feedback arrows from treatments back to confounders, quantified with causal DAGs. It argues that "if we control for a sufﬁcient set of confounders and use appropriate inference techniques such as the inverse probability of treatment weighting (IPTW) or g-formula, we can close the backdoor paths and derive the unbiased causal estimate of joint interventions on the outcome." The framework also maps experimental and quasi-experimental designs onto DAGs to expose their backdoor paths and validity threats.
