# Two-minute directional on-/off-topic interaction video-coding procedure

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors developed a coding procedure in which two-minute clips are the unit of analysis, coded in BORIS software. Each interaction is coded once per clip as directional, with an identified source and targets, and classified as on-topic (related to the activity, the program, or STEM education) or off-topic (unrelated to education). A detailed codebook described "what counted as an interaction and how it might vary for hearing and DHH individuals". Inter-rater reliability testing with three coders on seven random clips improved agreement from 88% to 98% after revising code definitions.
