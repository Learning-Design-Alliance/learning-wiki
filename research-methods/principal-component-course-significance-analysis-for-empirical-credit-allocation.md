## Description
The article proposes that because the first principal component's correlations with course means, variances, and sums of correlation coefficients are strong, its per-course contribution quantifies a course's significance. The analysis is based entirely on student grades and is independent from declared curricula, so it offers an empirical complement to top-down credit assignment. The authors state it "can be used for assigning ECTS points to a given course."
