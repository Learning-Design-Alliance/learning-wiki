# Three behavioral process indicators for operationalizing planning in complex problem solving from log data

## Description
The article introduces three behavioral indicators extracted from computer-generated log data to operationalize planning in complex problem solving: "the duration of the longest interval between two successive interactions (duration indicator)", "the delay of the longest interval between two successive interactions from the beginning of the process (delay indicator)", and "the variance of times between each two successive interactions (variance indicator)". These indicators capture planning behavior occurring in the time between interactions, which the authors take to include understanding, representing, formulating, monitoring, and reflecting processes from the PISA framework.
