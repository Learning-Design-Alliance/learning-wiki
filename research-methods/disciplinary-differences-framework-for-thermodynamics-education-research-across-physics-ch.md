## Description
The review organizes thermodynamics education research by topical area, each subdivided into physics, chemistry, biology, engineering, and multidisciplinary literatures, and delineates how the disciplines differ. For example, it notes that "Most introductory chemistry courses, for example, neglect gas-phase reactions and the role of pdV work", while chemistry and biology students encounter an emphasis on constant-pressure processes and a conflation of heat and enthalpy. It uses a student-data code to mark articles reporting data on student understanding.
