# Four-role classification of stakeholders in the LMS implementation process

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article classifies LMS implementation stakeholders by their current role in the process into four groups: "I. Decision-making stakeholders II. End-user stakeholders III. Vendor stakeholders IV. Support stakeholders". Decision-making stakeholders (administration, the CIO, and faculty representatives) decide on and fund the implementation; end users are faculty and students; vendors create or sell the LMS; support stakeholders assist users. The article notes roles overlap, e.g. faculty may be end user, decision maker, and student support simultaneously.
