# OLM log-analysis method for measuring the effectiveness of online learning resources

## Description
The article introduces what it calls a "new method to quickly provide detailed information on the effectiveness of online learning resources" by analyzing and visualizing time-stamped log data from student interaction with an online mastery learning system. Each module assesses students before and after they interact with its learning resources, and the method combines three measurements — assessment outcomes, test-taking effort, and learning effort — into sunburst charts that instructors can read at a glance to identify less effective modules.
