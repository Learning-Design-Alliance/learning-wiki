# Four-way classification of assessment items by passing and failing conditional-probability ratios

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article defines two classifying ratios computed from per-item contingency tables: a passing ratio, Pr(Pass|has item)/Pr(Pass|not has item), and a failing ratio, Pr(Fail|not has item)/Pr(Fail|has item), whose product is the odds ratio. Items are then classified as "Basic" (large failing ratio, foundational), "Advanced" (large passing ratio, more advanced topic), "Core" (both properties), or "Uninformative" (neither property, including items all or no students have). Figure 7 applies this classification to the 182 items in each course and year.
