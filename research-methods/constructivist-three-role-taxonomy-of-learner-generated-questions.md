## Description
The article organises learner-generated questions through a constructivist framework drawing on Piaget and Vygotsky's theories, in which student inquiries are categorised into three roles: "knowledge transmitter, facilitator and co-learner". This taxonomy serves as the class scheme that the NLP models are trained and evaluated against, linking question type to inquiry depth and to personalised learning and adaptive feedback in educational settings.
