## Description
The review inductively clusters the design variables compared across included studies into four main categories: "instructional strategies", "simulation models", "learner engagement", and "assessment models", each with named subdomains (e.g., feedback source, intensity, timing, automation; simulator type, cost, fidelity; stakes and motivation; minimum passing standards and validity). The authors recommend this framework, as presented in their Table 3, as a structure for future comparative efficacy studies of instructional design features.
