# Two-axis taxonomy of e-learning: three types crossed with three multimedia levels

## Description
The article proposes that e-learning interventions should be described along two axes before comparing studies. Type corresponds to the learning method: "1. Presentations (simple e-learning with no interactivity), 2. Scenarios (interactions fx cases that allow the learner to take decisions) or 3. Games/simulations". Level refers to multimedia development, from text and simple interactivities (level 1) up to complex animation and multivariable interaction (level 3). Crossing the axes yields nine categories, which the author argues would make e-learning research comparisons reliable, since material quality and digital setup currently vary so much that efficacy data lack reliability.
