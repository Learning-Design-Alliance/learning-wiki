# WWC effectiveness rating framework for intervention evidence

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The What Works Clearinghouse rates interventions per outcome domain by combining study design quality with the magnitude, statistical significance, and consistency of findings. Ratings range from positive effects to no discernible effects; for example, "Potentially positive effects" requires "At least one study shows a statistically significant or substantively important positive effect" with no overriding contrary evidence. A finding is "substantively important" when it "has an effect size of 0.25 or greater, regardless of statistical significance." Extent of evidence is rated small, medium to large, based on the number of studies, schools, and total sample size.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- At least one study meeting WWC group design standards (with or without reservations) reporting findings in the outcome domain; clustering and multiple-comparison corrections applied where assignment is clustered or outcomes are multiple
#### Constraints
- A rating of potentially positive effects can rest on a single quasi-experimental study whose effect is not statistically significant, provided the effect size is substantively important and no contrary evidence exists

### Target Learners
