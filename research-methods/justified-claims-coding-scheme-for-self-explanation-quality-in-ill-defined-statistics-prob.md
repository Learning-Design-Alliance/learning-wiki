# Justified-claims coding scheme for self-explanation quality in ill-defined statistics problems

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article develops a coding scheme assessing self-explanation quality as the degree to which written problem solutions include correctly applied and justified conceptual information. For each of three problems, three claims (variables and design, statistical analysis, statistical assumptions) were coded as no claim (0), incomplete (0.5), or complete (1), and separately coded for justification (visually, spatially, or verbally linked). Scores were summed to a 0–27 self-explanation quality measure (Cronbach's α = .78), with concept application coded separately to capture the inferences learners made.
