# Four-part uncertainty-quantification methodology for trustworthy machine coding at scale

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article casts machine coding of student text as an experimental measurement practice with associated uncertainties, rather than relying on reliability metrics against human coding. The methodology has four parts: "evaluating a trained model, calculating statistical uncertainty, calculating systematic uncertainty from the trained algorithm, and calculating systematic uncertainty from novel data sources." Each part is demonstrated with survey data and paired with explicit recommendations for researchers.
