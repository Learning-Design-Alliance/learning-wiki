# Seven-theme content analysis framework for e-learning in self-management support training

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review used summative (deductive then latent) content analysis of the ten included studies to organise findings into seven themes: "participants and professions; time; package content; guiding theoretical framework; outcome measures; learning features or formats; and learning barriers". Each theme carries subthemes with counts of studies, e.g. package content subthemes include problem-solving, motivational interviewing, action planning and goal setting, and barriers most commonly cited were time (10 studies) and access (9 studies). The framework maps the current landscape of e-learning for SMS continuing professional development.
