# Four-dimension learning analytics reference model (what, how, why, who)

## Description
The review adopts Chatti et al.'s (2012) reference model in which "LA consists of four dimensions including (1) what (what types of data does the system capture and analyze), (2) how (how does the system perform analytics), (3) why (for what reasons does the system gather and analyze data), and (4) who (who is served by the analytics)". This framework structures the review's coding scheme and four research questions, and underpins the conceptual framework the authors propose to guide implementation of LA for feedback systems in higher education.

(Seyyed Kazem Banihashem et al. (2022))
- **Open learning analytics as an ongoing analytics process stressing diversity across the What-Who-Why-How dimensions**: The article adopts the view, from Chatti et al. (2017), that openness in learning analytics should be interpreted across multiple conceptualizations (open learning, open architecture, open access, open datasets, open learner modeling, and more) and that OLA is "an ongoing analytics process that stresses diversity at the What-Who-Why-How dimensions". It leverages data from diverse sources (What?) to address various objectives (Why?) of multiple stakeholders (Who?) by applying different analytics methods (How?). The review uses these four dimensions as the organizing template for coding 137 tools and comparing five OLA frameworks. (Arham Muslim et al. (2019))
