# Four-category model for categorizing students' physics definitions

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The article adapts Michelini's (2024) categorization model into four conceptual categories for students' definitions: 'Declarative Statements' (static, instructional statements), 'Scientific Definitions' (textbook-style or accurate definitions), 'Recollection of (learning) Experiences' (definitions based on prior knowledge or the activity), and 'Interpretative Statements' (personal interpretations of the physical meaning). Each definition received both a primary and secondary category "to capture the lexical and semantic nuances used by the students". The authors report the model "closely aligns with our findings", with two adjustments made for their high school sample.
