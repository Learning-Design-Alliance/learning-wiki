## Description
The article organizes teamwork into behaviors that regulate team performance and behaviors that keep the team together, following Lewin's locomotion and maintenance processes. Regulation spans behaviors "before/in preparation for team task performance," during execution (communication, coordination, cooperation), and after task completion (reflection, monitoring, adjustment), while a fourth dimension covers interpersonal dynamics such as conflict management and social support. The meta-analysis used these four dimensions to code targeted and measured dimensions of interventions.
