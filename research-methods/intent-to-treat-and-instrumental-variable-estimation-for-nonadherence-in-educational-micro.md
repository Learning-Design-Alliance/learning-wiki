# Intent-to-treat and instrumental-variable estimation for nonadherence in educational micro-randomized trials

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article identifies adherence to prompts as a major methodological challenge in educational micro-randomized trials, since education often targets the effect of a behavior on a different outcome rather than the behavior itself. It recommends estimating the effect of randomized prompts regardless of whether the behavior was performed — analogous to an intent-to-treat analysis — and using instrumental variable estimation to recover the effect of the behavior when adherence is not perfect, noting the applicability of these approaches still remains to be thoroughly investigated.
