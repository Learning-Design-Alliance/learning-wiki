# Taxonomy of local, emergent, and fundamental theories in science education

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Within the structuralist view, the article classifies science education theories by the size of their set of models: "we call theories having a set of models that includes just one model 'local theories,' and theories with a potentially infinite set of models 'fundamental theories'", with finite subsets of another theory called emergent theories. The authors reject the older opposition of local (qualitative) versus fundamental (quantitative) theories, arguing both kinds are needed and local theories can be starting points from which theories become more fundamental as models accumulate. Conceptual change theory is offered as an example of a fundamental theory, with an emergent theory of conceptual change in quantum mechanics learning and a local theory of one student's learning pathway.
