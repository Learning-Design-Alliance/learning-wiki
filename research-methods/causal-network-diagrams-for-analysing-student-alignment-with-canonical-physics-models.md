# Causal network diagrams for analysing student alignment with canonical physics models

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The article introduces causal networks (Bayes nets) as a graphical formalism of nodes and directional links representing causal influences between physical quantities, used to represent canonical physical models rather than student reasoning. It argues this formalism "provides a complete model for evaluating how student reasoning does or does not align with canonical physics", building on diSessa and Sherin's unformalized theory of causal nets.
