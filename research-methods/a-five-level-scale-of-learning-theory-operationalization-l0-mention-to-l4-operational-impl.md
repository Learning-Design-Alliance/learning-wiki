# A five-level scale of learning-theory operationalization (L0 mention to L4 operational implementation)

## Description
To analyze the role of theory in studies of digital educational technologies, the article defines a scale distinguishing ways learning theories influence research, "ranging from simple conceptual mention to their implementation as functional components of educational technologies." The levels are L0 Mention (general reference without influence), L1 Justification, L2 Analytical use, L3 Design principles, and L4 Operational implementation as functional components of the system.
