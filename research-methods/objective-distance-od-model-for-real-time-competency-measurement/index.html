Objective distance (OD) model for real-time competency measurement¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The study adapts Chaichumpa and Temdee's (2018) objective distance model to compute learners' CT competency in real time during iThinkSmart gameplay. As the article states, "OD measures the distance between the expected/satisfactory learner's score ( S i) and the current learner's score ( C i ), mainly used to provide personalized support and intelligent feedback to a learner." The formula uses the satisfactory score, current score, and total score for each instance of a learning object. In this study, the total weighted score and satisfactory score were defined in the system while the player's current score was obtained during gameplay, and control-group Kahoot! data were manually computed with the same formula.
