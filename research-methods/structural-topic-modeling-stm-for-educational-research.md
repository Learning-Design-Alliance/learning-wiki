# Structural topic modeling (STM) for educational research

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article applies a structural topic model (STM) to the abstracts and titles of 2,568 metacognition articles, running 26 candidate models with topic numbers from 5 to 30 and selecting the 19-topic model based on semantic coherence, exclusivity (FREX), and expert verification. The 19 labeled topics include "self-regulated learning", "judgments of learning and memory accuracy", "assessment and feedback", "creative and critical thinking", and "nursing, medical, clinical, and healthcare education". The article states this "marks the inaugural application of topic modeling technology to the field of metacognition". Topics were labeled by two domain experts through a three-step procedure of interpreting discriminating terms, examining representative articles, and unifying labels.
