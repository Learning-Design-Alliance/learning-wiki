# Transfer for Future Learning (TFL) three-phase assessment approach

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
Transfer for Future Learning (TFL) is an assessment approach measuring whether students transfer recently learned concepts to future topics within the same learning progression. Inspired by Preparation for Future Learning and grounded in Evidence-Centered Design, TFL provides instructional support ("bridging") to reduce transfer difficulty, adopting a three-phase protocol of eliciting prior knowledge, bridging instruction, and assessing performance on the target concept. The article developed TFL tasks within Cignition's microtutoring context.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires domain analysis and domain modeling to identify source and target concepts within a learning progression
- Requires a tutor or instructor to deliver bridging instruction
#### Constraints
- Designed for relatively near transfer within a learning progression, not far transfer across domains

### Target Learners
- fifth-grade students struggling with fraction concepts

### Target Learning Objectives
- transfer of fraction understanding to more advanced mathematical topics
