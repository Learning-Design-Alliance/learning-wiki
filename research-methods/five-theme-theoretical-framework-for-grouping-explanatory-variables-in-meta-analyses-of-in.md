# Five-theme theoretical framework for grouping explanatory variables in meta-analyses of intervention trial effect sizes

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review developed a theoretical framework derived from literature with "five overarching themes to group explanatory variables": Intervention, Implementation & fidelity, Theory & evidence, Context, and Evaluation design. Each theme contains coded explanatory variables (e.g. curriculum focus, cost, CPD provision, trial design) used to structure subgroup meta-analyses of the effect sizes reported by the 82 EEF trials. The framework was developed focusing on primary ITT outcomes and adapted for secondary and FSM analyses.
