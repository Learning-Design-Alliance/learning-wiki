# Opportunity–attainment scope model

## Description
The model characterizes curricular supply under three scopes: a floor counting only courses every graduate is required to take, a ceiling counting the entire catalog, and an expected scope weighting each elective course by the probability a graduate completes it under the program's elective model. The difference between floor and ceiling, termed the opportunity–attainment gap, measures how much apparent coverage is guaranteed rather than contingent on elective choice. It corrects the catalog-level over-counting that would otherwise distort supply–demand comparison.
