## Description
The article recommends that intervention research with students with or at-risk of EBD consistently report participant demographics, fidelity of implementation, social validity, and intervention duration. It argues these data are needed "to help identify for whom and under what conditions interventions are most effective" and to ensure practices are acceptable and consistently used. Few studies across the included reviews reported these variables.
