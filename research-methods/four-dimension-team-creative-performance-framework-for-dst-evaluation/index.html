Four-dimension team creative performance framework for DST evaluation¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The study adopted Liu et al.'s (2023) TCP assessment framework comprising four dimensions: "novelty, relevance, user experience, and sustainability." Novelty assesses originality and innovation; relevance focuses on practical applicability; user experience evaluates engagement through authenticity, cultural immersion, and audience connection; sustainability considers long-term viability across environmental, cultural, and economic factors. The framework was operationalized with two to three criteria per dimension on a ten-point scale, weighted 30% each for sustainability and user experience and 20% each for novelty and relevance.
