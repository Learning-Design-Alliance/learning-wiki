# Reliable–Relevant–Automatically-Assessable framework for validating on-demand AI-generated practice problems

## Description
The article operationalizes three criteria for choosing validation metrics for LLM-generated problems: reliability as model–ground truth agreement (an LLM judge reproducing expert labels), relevance as predictiveness of learner choice and preference rather than direct learning efficacy, and automatic assessability as feasibility at scale without human intervention. Metrics are judged by an LLM-as-a-judge mechanism whose goal is to "distinguish viable problems that can be forwarded to the learners from defective problems that should be discarded." Ground truth comes from expert ratings, and relevance is triangulated with exit-survey feedback.
