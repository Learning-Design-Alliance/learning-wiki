# Four AI model families as complementary lenses on educational cognition

## Description
The review organizes AI approaches to modeling educational cognition into four families treated as different lenses rather than a formal taxonomy: neural networks, reinforcement learning agents, cognitive architectures, and large language models. It situates them along two dimensions, learning flexibility and scale, and mechanistic transparency and construct alignment, arguing that "neural networks emphasize learning from experience through gradual parameter change; reinforcement learning formalizes adaptation through feedback and incentives; cognitive architectures enforce psychologically motivated structure and capacity limits; and large language models bring broad, pretrained competence for language-mediated tasks". The families are complementary and increasingly integrable through hybrid approaches.
