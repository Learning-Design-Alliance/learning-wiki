## Description
The review classifies digital learning resources into defined types used to categorize interventions: e-learning, digital assessment, social component, podcasts, blended learning, virtual patient, virtual reality, augmented reality, and serious/educational games. E-learning is defined as a "Web‐based (online) or offline course offering a mixture of text, multimedia, and interactive elements," and blended learning as any DLR combined with face-to-face teaching. The taxonomy structures the review's analysis of which DLR types are used and how effective they are, with e-learning the most-used type (55 of 65 articles).
