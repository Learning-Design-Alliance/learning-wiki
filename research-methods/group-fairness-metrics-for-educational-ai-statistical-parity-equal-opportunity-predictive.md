# Group fairness metrics for educational AI: statistical parity, equal opportunity, predictive parity, predictive equality, and concordance imparity

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The survey formalizes group fairness for educational settings with quantitative metrics that detect, quantify, and mitigate unequal treatment across student populations. For example, "Statistical Parity ensures positive predictive outcomes as ˆY, such as predicted academic success or eligibility for interventions, are proportionally and equitably distributed across protected (a) and non-protected (¯a) student groups". Equal Opportunity requires equal true positive rates, Predictive Parity equal precision, Predictive Equality equal false positive rates, and Concordance Imparity handles censored outcomes via concordance fractions.
