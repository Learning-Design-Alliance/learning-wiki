# Rule-based multimodal engagement detection from gaze and speech in group interactions

## Description
A transparent data-processing pipeline that detects four gaze behaviours and speaking behaviours from video and audio, infers seven group interaction types (IPC, RF, PO, RM, ITC, TO, NC) with a rule-based method and a five-second sliding window, then codes each student per second as “active, semi-active, and passive” using the rules in Table 1. Gaze behaviours were manually annotated by two researchers achieving “0.98 Cohen’s Kappa in a 1000-frame sample”, and speaker diarization used Amazon Transcribe. Normalised code frequencies were clustered with K-means into drivers and passengers.
