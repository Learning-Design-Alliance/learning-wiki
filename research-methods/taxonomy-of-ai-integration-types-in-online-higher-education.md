# Taxonomy of AI integration types in online higher education

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
From 54 articles containing specific examples, the review classifies AI integration into categories: "AI teaching assistance/machine teachers; automated AI monitoring; AI-driven surveillance; algorithms, generative AI (GAI), large language models, machine learning, prediction model performance (i.e., adaptive modeling), and other." Each category is illustrated with cited studies, e.g., remote proctored exams for automated monitoring and Q-Module Bot for large language models. The taxonomy maps the functional purposes AI serves in online teaching and learning.
