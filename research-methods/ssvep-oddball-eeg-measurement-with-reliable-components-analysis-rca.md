# SSVEP oddball EEG measurement with Reliable Components Analysis (RCA)

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A measurement element combining steady-state visual evoked potentials with Reliable Components Analysis (RCA) over a 128-sensor array, recorded in a laboratory inside the school. Word oddballs are presented at 1 Hz within a 3 Hz base stream across three conditions (learned-vs-unlearned low-frequency words, high-frequency words vs pseudowords, medium-frequency words vs pseudowords). The article notes the paradigm elicits much higher signal-to-noise responses than other EEG paradigms, enabling reliable measurement with just 10 trials of 10 s each, and that RCA produces component topographies more closely resembling underlying cortical sources than PCA or Common Spatial Patterns.
