## Description
The article argues that EDM methods which incorporate human effort up front — defining and labeling independent variables that "map to clearly defined constructs" — produce discoveries that are interpretable and actionable for instruction. LFA's human-labeled starting KCs made its circle-area split interpretable as a square-root difficulty, enabling hint, scaffolding, and practice redesigns that purely automated discovery methods have not supported. The authors encourage the field to devote more attention to interpretability and experimental loop-closing.
