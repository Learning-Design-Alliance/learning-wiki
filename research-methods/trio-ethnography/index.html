Trio-ethnography¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Trio-ethnography brings three participants (here two computing educators with contrasting instructional philosophies and one undergraduate student) into reflective conversation as co-inquirers. The article uses it not to compare perspectives but as a process in which "student dialogue as evidence" progressively refines educators' interpretations across three stages: initial educator dialogue, student dialogue, and educator reflective reconstruction. It positions the method as narrowing the gap between observable programming products and actual AI-supported learning processes.
