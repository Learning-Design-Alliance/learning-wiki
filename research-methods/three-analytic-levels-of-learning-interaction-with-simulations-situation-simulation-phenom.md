# Three analytic levels of learning interaction with simulations: situation, simulation, phenomenon

## Description
The article analytically differentiates three levels of learning interaction: the level of the situation (what the learner sees as relevant to the learning setting), the level of the simulation (understandings of how the simulation works and its prominent features), and the level of the phenomenon (conceptual links between simulation and represented phenomenon). These levels are interrelated in experience, but at a given time learners may be focally aware of them in quite different ways, and the levels are used to characterise each learning-focus.
