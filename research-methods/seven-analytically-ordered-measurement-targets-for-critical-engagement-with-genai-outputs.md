# Seven analytically ordered measurement targets for critical engagement with GenAI outputs

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review proposes a construct-validity framework separating seven measurement targets: epistemic evaluation, verification initiation, process quality, success, reliance decisions, immediate task performance, and independent learning. It argues that broad labels such as "critical" or "responsible" AI use "can obscure distinct outcomes" — a learner may check without a strong process, and a correct final answer does not establish retention or unaided performance. The ordering is analytic, not a validated causal sequence.
