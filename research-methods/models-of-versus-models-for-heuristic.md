# Models of versus models for heuristic

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article presents a heuristic contrasting two ways of understanding models in science. Models can be understood in terms of how they represent phenomena in the world (models of), or with respect to the aims and intentions of a cognitive agent (models for). The authors present this contrast as a tool educators can use to notice and interpret how models are positioned in standards, curriculum, and classrooms.
