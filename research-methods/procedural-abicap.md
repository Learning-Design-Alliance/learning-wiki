## Description
Procedural ABICAP represents a domain as a knowledge graph in which each node is a procedural knowledge component with a mastery value, connected by weighted undirected edges whose weights capture the extent of the relationship between two nodes. The graph's density varies by domain: for KCs with no rationale it may be a large collection of nodes with no or few edges, while for domains like algebra equations it can have many edges between related steps. Constructive and interactive learners reinforce edge weights, whereas active and passive learners leave them unchanged.
