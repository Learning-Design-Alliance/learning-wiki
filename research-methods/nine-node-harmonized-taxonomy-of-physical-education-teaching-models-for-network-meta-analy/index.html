Nine-node harmonized taxonomy of physical education teaching models for network meta-analysis¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (2 quant-synthesis), q2–q3 · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review harmonizes study-level intervention labels into a connected treatment network of nine nodes: "Control, Functional games, Game-based, Hybrid TGfU-SE, Linear, Nonlinear, Sport Education, TGfU, and Traditional." Labels were mapped "according to instructional logic rather than name alone" — e.g., classical, technical, and direct-instruction labels were assigned to Traditional or Linear by degree of explicit technical sequencing, and constraint-led approaches to Nonlinear. Control denotes minimal or regular PE; Traditional denotes structured teacher-centered instruction; Cooperative appears only in the broad sensitivity network. The harmonization was intended to preserve interpretability and network connectivity rather than erase pedagogical nuance.

Claims¶

  • In the under-18 primary network, Hybrid TGfU-SE, Nonlinear, and Functional games are the only nodes whose estimates versus Control exclude zero, led by Hybrid TGfU-SE (SMD 1.35) [+W]
  • Traditional instruction is approximately null versus Control in the primary network, and the analysis does not support claims that every active or structured node outperforms Control [+W]
  • In the adolescent subgroup network, Functional games had the highest P-score (0.830; SMD 2.568), but rankings differed by age stratum and inconsistency remained significant [+W]
  • Cooperative learning's top rank in the broad network rests entirely on a single visual-motor integration study and disappears when that study is removed [+W]
  • The treatment ordering is sensitive to outcome definition, participant age, and individual studies, including the reconstructed Orangi effect [+W]
  • Teaching Games for Understanding improves tactical decision-making more than traditional physical education instruction. [+W]
