## Description
Graphical Jogthrough is a modification of the typical Jogthrough method for evaluating user interface design, itself related to Walkthrough techniques. In it, “evaluators' ratings produce evidence in the form of a graph, presenting estimated proportion of users who effectively use the interface versus the time they had to work with it in order to succeed effectiveness.” The article develops the method and applies it to an educational simulation interface.
