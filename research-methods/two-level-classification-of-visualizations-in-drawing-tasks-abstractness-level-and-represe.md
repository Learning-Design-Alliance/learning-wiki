# Two-level classification of visualizations in drawing tasks: abstractness level and representational vs. relational drawing type

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review proposes describing visualizations in learning-by-drawing studies on two levels: the level of visual abstractness (concrete vs. abstract) and the drawing type, distinguishing "visualizations representing tangible real-world objects, labeled as representational images" from "non-tangible concepts or abstract relationships, labeled as relational images," following Hegarty (2011) and Skulmowski (2024). Complex drawings such as multiple representations or animations form an additional category. The authors apply these labels to both provided reference images and learner-generated drawings to make studies comparable.
