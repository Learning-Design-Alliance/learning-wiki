## Description
The review organises the design of intensive longitudinal studies around core methodological parameters: study duration, assessment frequency, sampling scheme (fixed, random, semi-random, event-based), item phrasing and questionnaire density, and data-collection device. It argues these choices "contain, rather implicitly, theoretical assumptions about the psychological processes under investigation and delimit the kinds of inferences that can be drawn from the data", requiring alignment between parameters, research questions, and constructs. For example, low-frequency assessments implicitly assume no meaningful within-day fluctuations.
