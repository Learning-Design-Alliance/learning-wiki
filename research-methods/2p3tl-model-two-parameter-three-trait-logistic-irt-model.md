# 2P3TL model (two-parameter three-trait logistic IRT model)

## Description
The 2P3TL model extends the standard two-parameter logistic IRT model by introducing two learner traits beyond ability: a copying trait χj, which lifts the lower limit of the item characteristic curve so that even low-ability students can appear to solve problems, and a guessing trait γj, which lowers the probability that even high-ability students answer correctly. The article states that "The copying trait χj allows even low-ability students to "solve" problems, as it lifts the lower limit of the item characteristic curve." The guessing trait models random guessing, i.e., inputting answers without truly figuring out the solution, and is explicitly distinguished from the item-level guessing parameter ci of 3PL models.
