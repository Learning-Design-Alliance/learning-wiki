# Contemporaneous (lag0) sensitivity analysis for selecting panel-design time intervals

## Description
Because effect sizes vary with the interval between measurement waves and there is no optimal interval, the article proposes testing lag0 paths within each wave as a practical check on design. "Tests of contemporaneous (lag0) effect in CLPMs are heuristic and offer a practical sensitivity analysis of whether the research design is based on appropriate time intervals." Significant lag0 effects may not be truly instantaneous but may signal that effects occur between waves, motivating shorter intervals; researchers should consider alternative intervals since failure to find lagged effects at one interval does not preclude effects at others.
