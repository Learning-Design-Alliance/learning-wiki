# NTO tutor move taxonomy: four support categories with learning support moves organized along a student-engagement spectrum

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The NTO tutor move taxonomy organizes one-on-one tutoring dialogue into discrete codable instructional actions across tutoring support, learning support, social-emotional and motivational support, and logistical support. Learning support moves are placed on "a spectrum ranging from those designed to elicit active student thinking, explanation, and engagement" down to moves where students are passive recipients of information or worked examples.
