# Hypothetical-population inferential method for estimating activity-based reinstruction effects

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
A sampling-theory method that treats the actual LB students as a random sample from a hypothetical Population 0 instructed by lecture methods, then hypothetically returns that population to its preinstruction state and reinstructs it with AB methods to form Population 1, of which the actual AB students are a sample. Responses are categorized as ww, ww', wr, rw, and rr, and the effect of reinstruction is represented by probability transfers A, B, C, and D satisfying the article's constraint equations. Because "there are more unknowns than independent equations", the method adds the assumption that ww' and rw responses are equivalent, fixing the mean transition probabilities f(ww,wr) = f(rw,rr) and allowing all transfers to be estimated with standard deviations derived in the appendix.
