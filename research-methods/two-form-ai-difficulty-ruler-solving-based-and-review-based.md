# Two-form AI difficulty ruler (solving-based and review-based)

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article distinguishes two forms of the AI ruler. The solving-based ruler has AI read problems, generate code, and submit to the online judge, producing process data such as pass rate, attempts, running time, and error type. The review-based ruler has AI provide structured scores for concept, implementation, debugging, complexity-risk, reading, and overall difficulty without submitting code. The article states the solving-based evidence "constitutes the empirical basis for choosing a unified reviewer in the subsequent review-based extension," while the review-based form is low-cost and scalable but lacks real judging feedback.
