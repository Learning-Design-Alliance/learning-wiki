# Eight-timepoint video annotation method for pure RiSSA and purse-string suture skill assessment

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The article defines eight annotated intraoperative milestones (timepoints A through H) from surgical video, from which two composite metrics are computed: the pure RiSSA interval, which "represents the net duration of the whole RiSSA procedure, excluding specimen extraction", and total purse-string suture time, the sum of proximal and distal suture durations. The framework isolates anastomotic steps from confounding factors such as specimen extraction variability and pelvic dissection complexity, enabling granular, reproducible skill assessment beyond console time.
