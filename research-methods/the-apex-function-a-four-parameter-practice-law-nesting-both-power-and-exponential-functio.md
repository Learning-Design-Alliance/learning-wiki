# The APEX function: a four-parameter practice law nesting both power and exponential functions

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The APEX function is a four-parameter practice function, E(RT_N) = A + B e^(−a'N) N^(−β'), proposed to adjudicate between power and exponential accounts of practice. It "nests (i.e., contains as special cases) both the power and the exponential functions", and its relative learning rate is "the sum of the RLRs for the power and exponential functions", decreasing early in practice but approaching a nonzero asymptote later. A better APEX-than-power fit supports an exponential component; a better APEX-than-exponential fit supports a power component.
