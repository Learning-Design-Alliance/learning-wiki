# Four-quadrant rigor framework combining cognitive demand and content depth

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes a framework for comparing rigor across historical eras rather than defining rigor absolutely. It combines "Cognitive Demand which follows from Wyse and Soneral (2018) and Hess et al. (2009) and content depth" into "a four quadrant matrix". Content depth is described as "generalizations, multiple perspectives and connections between different areas of content knowledge", and cognitive demand as tasks requiring "analysis, synthesis, application and generalization by the student". The framework is explicitly relative and makes no absolute or quantifiable claims.
