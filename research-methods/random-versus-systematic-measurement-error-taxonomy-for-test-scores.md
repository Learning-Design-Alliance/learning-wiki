# Random versus systematic measurement error taxonomy for test scores

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The module organizes sources of score inaccuracy into two classes. Random errors occur for some test takers and not others, or to different degrees, and include guessing, testwiseness, content sampling, fatigue, and subjective scoring; their average across a group should be zero. Systematic errors "affect all examinees to nearly the same extent" and shift all scores up or down without changing their order. The taxonomy explains which errors a reliability coefficient can detect.
