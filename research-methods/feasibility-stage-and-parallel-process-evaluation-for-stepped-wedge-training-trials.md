# Feasibility-stage and parallel process evaluation for stepped-wedge training trials

## Description
The authors recommend that future stepped-wedge trials evaluating training interventions plan for implementation difficulty from the outset. They state that "future SW-RCT studies should consider a feasibility stage and a parallel process evaluation" to explore barriers and facilitators to randomisation and implementation, and that such trials "should allocate resources to enable the measurement of the proportion of staff trained", since intervention penetration could not be assessed in THISTLE.
