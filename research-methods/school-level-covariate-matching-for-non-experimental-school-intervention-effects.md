# School-level covariate matching for non-experimental school intervention effects

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
When selecting non-experimental comparison groups for school-level interventions, researchers should incorporate school-level attributes alongside student characteristics. The paper's two within-study comparisons showed that ignoring school context can bias estimates, and the authors explicitly advise: "go beyond matching only on student level covariates in practice." School-level covariates are readily available from public sources, making this easy to implement.
