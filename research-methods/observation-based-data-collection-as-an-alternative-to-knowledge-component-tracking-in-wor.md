# Observation-based data collection as an alternative to knowledge-component tracking in workplace settings

## Description
The article argues that existing AIED techniques such as knowledge tracing presuppose a decomposition of skills into knowledge components (as in ACT-R and the Cognitive Tutor), which is difficult in workplace-based settings with multi-source data. LiftUpp instead "collects data on performed treatments in terms of what can be observed not what constitutes jumps in learner knowledge, thus bypassing the question of KC deﬁnition", establishing a data-collection approach that can serve as a starting point for alternatives to knowledge tracing, such as deep knowledge tracing.
