# Standardized effect size for single-case designs obtained by dividing D by pooled error standard deviation

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article states that the treatment effect parameter is itself an effect size measure, and that a standardized effect size measure is obtained by dividing by the standard deviation obtained by pooling the error variances in the two phases. The authors claim to derive an approximate distribution of this standardized measure, which is intended to lend itself to meta-analysis of single subject designs.
