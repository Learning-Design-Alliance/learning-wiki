## Description
The article proposes a game design process dedicated to psychological profiling, stating: "Following our framework, we propose a game design process in 9 steps". The steps cover selecting a validated psychological model, setting audience constraints, building an expert validation pipeline, confronting the player model with the psychological model, defining psychometric items inside the gameplay loop, rationalizing content-dimension links, documenting the results interface, running a test-and-analysis protocol, and iterating. Its originality lies in the coexistence of the player model and the psychological model throughout design.
