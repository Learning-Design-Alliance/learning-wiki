# Model-preserving averaging functions for nonlinear forgetting models

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For each nonlinear model there is an averaging operation that preserves the model equation when individual data are aggregated: geometric averaging for the power and exponential models, harmonic averaging for the hyperbolic model, and arithmetic averaging for the linear and logarithmic models, as tabulated for the six forgetting models of Wixted and Ebbesen (1991). The article states that geometric averaging on power- and exponential-generated data "has the property of being a model-preserving averaging function" and thus will not yield spurious results. The Appendix derives the general one-parameter form via a function h such that h-1(y) equals the parameter.
