## Description
One of two procedures developed at the University of Limburg to reflect "the extent to which students are able to identify important learning issues given a particular problem". The production of learning issues was "represented as the percentage of overlap between learning issues raised by students and pre-set faculty objectives for each problem", providing a quantitative index of issue quality per problem.
