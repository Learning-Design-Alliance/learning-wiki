# Framework of reliability and validity evidence categories for classifying RCR outcome-measure validation

## Description
The review develops a coding framework distinguishing forms of reliability evidence (test–retest, parallel forms, split-half, internal consistency via Cronbach's alpha or Kuder–Richardson formulas) and validity evidence (content validity including cognitive interviews and S-CVIs, face validity, criterion validity, construct validity). It extends the framework of Fenn et al. (2020) with additional categories such as "Global Content Validity Indices (S-CVIs)". The framework was applied so that each measure was coded independently by one reviewer and checked by a second, documenting only explicitly reported evidence.
