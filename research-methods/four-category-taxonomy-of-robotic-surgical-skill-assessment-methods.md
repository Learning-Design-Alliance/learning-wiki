# Four-category taxonomy of robotic surgical skill assessment methods

## Description
The review organizes the landscape of robotic surgical technical skill assessment into four categories: "structured rubrics, skill-based metrics, crowd-sourcing techniques, and automated assessment models." Structured rubrics (OSATS, GEARS, GOALS and adaptations) use expert-rated grading categories; skill-based metrics capture completion time, kinematics, completion-based criteria, outcomes, robotic control, and precision errors; crowd-sourcing aggregates nonexpert rubric scores; and automated assessment uses supervised machine learning and deep learning on kinematic and video data. The taxonomy structures the survey of 61 da Vinci-focused studies and grounds the review's trend analysis.
