## Description
The article demonstrates a descriptive practice: representing grade outcomes as cumulative probability curves stratified by demographic group, so that differences at every grade threshold are visible rather than only mean grades. It argues such analyses "may support institutional quality work by providing empirical basis for monitoring patterns and development over time" and respond to calls for transparency in educational data analysis. The approach is presented as assumption-light and reproducible, not as a causal test.
