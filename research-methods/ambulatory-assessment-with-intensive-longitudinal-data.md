## Description
Ambulatory assessment is a measurement approach in which intensive longitudinal data are captured in real-life settings with repeated within-subject assessments of physical activity (e.g., via accelerometry) and cognitive performance (e.g., via a smartphone app) at high measurement density, such as sub-daily or daily. The article proposes it to complement between-subjects analyses with within-subject analyses of "the differences between days with less or more physical activity", and recommends continuous monitoring of activity level and context across intervals of at least 7 days before, during, and after the summer break.
