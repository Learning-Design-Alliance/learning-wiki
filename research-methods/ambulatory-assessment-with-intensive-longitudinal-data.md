## Description
Ambulatory assessment is a measurement approach in which intensive longitudinal data are captured in real-life settings with repeated within-subject assessments of physical activity (e.g., via accelerometry) and cognitive performance (e.g., via a smartphone app) at high measurement density, such as sub-daily or daily. The article proposes it to complement between-subjects analyses with within-subject analyses of "the differences between days with less or more physical activity", and recommends continuous monitoring of activity level and context across intervals of at least 7 days before, during, and after the summer break.

- **Experience sampling method (ESM) for capturing macro-level emotion dynamics in language learning**: ESM, also known as ecological momentary assessment, originated in psychiatry and collects real-time data on participants' behaviours, experiences and psychological states in natural environments. Participants are prompted "multiple times per day over a specified period ranging from days to weeks", via fixed, random, or event-contingent prompts on smartphones or wearable devices. The review proposes ESM to fill the gap of methodologies examining emotion dynamics from a macro, developmental perspective in language studies, illustrating a hypothetical two-week exam-period design with prompts after language activities plus contextual questions.
