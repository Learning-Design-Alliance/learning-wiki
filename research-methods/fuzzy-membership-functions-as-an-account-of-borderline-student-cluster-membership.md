## Description
The article proposes describing educational-data clusters as fuzzy sets whose membership is given by triangular membership functions µρ, µx, µy and µxy, computed from cluster radii and a radius change factor kR. The article states that "Due to fuzzy membership functions, it is possible to correctly describe the membership of a certain object, which is on the border of clusters, to several clusters at the same time." This account supplements crisp K-Means clustering for educational data.
