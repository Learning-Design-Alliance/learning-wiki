## Description
The article applies a two-dimensional external validity model, created by the authors for usability evaluations of health information technology, to educational simulation testing. It holds that "External validity comprises (1) population validity (ie, the extent to which the sample represents the population) and (2) ecological validity (ie, the extent to which the setting matches the real world)." Fidelity on each dimension is set by evaluation objectives, and usability is treated as an emergent property of the whole work system rather than of the interface alone. The team mapped constructs such as learner attributes, clinical environment, technology, and scenario onto their telemedicine use case.
