# Flow map technique

## Description
The flow map technique elicits and quantifies a learner's cognitive structure by recording the student's sequential answers to questions and charting the statements with direct and cross links between them. As the article puts it, "With the flow map, students’ thought patterns are revealed in succession and correlated with each other by cross-links." Four parameters are calculated: size (number of ideas), richness (recurrent linkages), integratedness (richness/(size + richness)), and correctness (misconception rate). In this study flow maps were built from voice-recorded achievement-test responses before and after an 8-week unit, and used to detect misconceptions and track cognitive-structure development.
