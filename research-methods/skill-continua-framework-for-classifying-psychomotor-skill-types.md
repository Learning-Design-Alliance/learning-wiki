## Description
Davis et al. (2000) and Galligan et al. (2000) classify skills on six continua: "Granularity (fine-gross), Environment (open-close), Pace (internally-externally), Continuity (discrete-serial-continuous), Interactivity (individual-coactive-interactive) and Complexity (simple-complex)". The review prefers this one-dimensional framework over Gentile's two-dimensional 16-class taxonomy because its simplicity mitigates interpretation errors that could distort the picture of the ITS landscape, while still providing a good overview of the skill types ITS were developed for.
