# Renaud's didactic model of usability, usefulness and acceptability for evaluating educational tools

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
A 15-criterion evaluation framework, adapted by Renaud from ergonomic psychology and design ergonomics, organized into three dimensions: usability (five indicators A1–A5, e.g. ease of understanding, workload, flexibility), usefulness (six indicators B1–B6, e.g. relevance of objectives, student engagement, observed progress) and acceptability (four indicators C1–C4, e.g. compatibility with ethics and teaching style). The article applies it, via a semi-open-ended questionnaire, to evaluate "trainers’ motivation, engagement, and the model’s relevance within the population of PFA-O simulation trainers". It is framed as part of Design-Oriented Research, with bidirectional instrumentation and instrumentalization between tool and practice.
