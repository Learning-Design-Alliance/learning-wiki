## Description
The article applies Calvani's classification scheme, which formulates "hierarchical relations between macro-structures (didactic architectures) and teaching methods (pedagogical strategies)". It distinguishes a teacher-focused meta-architecture (receptive/transmissive; behavioural/directive-interactive) from a student-focused meta-architecture (situated guided discovery, simulative, collaborative, explorative, metacognitive), each characterised by degree of pre-structuring, interaction and control (Table 1). The survey mapped 14 strategies onto 7 architectures and validated the grouping by factor analysis.
