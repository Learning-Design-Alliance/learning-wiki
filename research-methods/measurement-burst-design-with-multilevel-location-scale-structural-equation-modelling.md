# Measurement burst design with multilevel location-scale structural equation modelling

## Description
The article operationalises clinical performance determinants through a longitudinal measurement burst design combining short-intensive weekly surveys with long-term tracking across placements. The authors state they "utilised a longitudinal measurement burst design, incorporating a hybrid of short-intensive and long-term assessments to examine intra-individual processes over time". Multilevel structural equation modelling with latent variable decomposition partitions variance into between-person, between-placement and within-placement levels, and location-scale modelling captures both mean levels and intra-individual variability of APP scores. This addresses the article's critique that cross-sectional between-person designs mismatch the within-person nature of clinical performance.
