# Power planning for individual-level moderators in cluster-randomized education trials

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's design recommendation for efficacy trials aiming at moderator questions: because the total number of individuals, not clusters, drives power for individual-level moderators, planners should increase students per cluster. As the article states, "if a study is seeking to answer not only what works but also for whom, larger numbers of students per school will be helpful in answering the for whom question." This is feasible because standardized assessment makes added students inexpensive.
