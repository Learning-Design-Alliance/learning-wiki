# Six-type typology of formative assessment interventions based on process leadership

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review refines and updates Black and Wiliam's (1998a) original three broad categories into "six mutually exclusive types base d on who or what primarily leads or takes ownership of the process". The types are student-directed, teacher-directed, computer-directed, teacher/student-directed, teacher/computer-directed, and student/computer-directed, each defined by who appraises or monitors work and who reflects on the assessment information to determine next steps. The typology is used to classify interventions so effectiveness variation can be related to intervention type.
