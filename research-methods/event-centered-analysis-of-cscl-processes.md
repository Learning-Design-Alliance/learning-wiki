# Event-centered analysis of CSCL processes

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Peter Reimann proposes an event-centered approach as an alternative to conventional variable-centered methodologies for analyzing processes in CSCL settings. The editorial reports that "tracking events can be more responsive to changing circumstances than plotting values of presumably fixed variables," and that event-centered analysis can account for richer causality and narrative reporting. It reflects on multiple analytic methods in CSCL at a fundamental conceptual level.
