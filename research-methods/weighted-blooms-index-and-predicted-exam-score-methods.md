# Weighted Bloom's Index and Predicted Exam Score methods

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study introduces two methods for controlling for exam difficulty in longitudinal course comparisons. The Weighted Bloom's Index "summarizes the average Bloom's level of exam questions weighted by the points possible," computed from independent Bloom's ratings by three trained TAs blind to quarter and hypothesis. The Predicted Exam Score uses three experienced peer-TA graders to predict the average points students would receive on each exam question part. The two metrics were strongly and negatively associated (r = −0.72 for the 18 exams), and both indicated that exams in the highly structured quarters were harder.
