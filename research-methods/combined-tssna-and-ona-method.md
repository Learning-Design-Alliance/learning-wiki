# Combined tSSNA and ONA method

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
This analytic method combines temporal socio-semantic network analysis (tSSNA) and ordered network analysis (ONA) to analyze collaborative knowledge creation. tSSNA tracks idea improvement via total degree centrality in moving windows over discourse, segmenting sessions into phases. ONA models ordered relationships between epistemic codes, capturing which actions respond to which. The article states this work "demonstrates the potential of combining tSSNA and ONA for analyzing collaborative KC processes."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Transcribed discourse segmented by turns of talk
- A coding framework (here, shared epistemic agency with seven categories)
- Bilingual analysts when data spans languages
#### Constraints
- Applied to three teams in 30-minute sessions; tSSNA used moving window of four turns, network lifetime of two windows, 287 unique key terms
