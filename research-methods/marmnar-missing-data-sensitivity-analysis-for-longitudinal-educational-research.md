# MAR–MNAR missing-data sensitivity analysis for longitudinal educational research

## Description
The article recommends that researchers gauge the stability of longitudinal growth estimates by comparing techniques assuming MAR against those assuming MNAR. Because the MAR and MNAR assumptions cannot be tested in everyday practice, "one cannot rule out MNAR missingness for a given dataset". While some methodologists suggest sensitivity analysis only under strong suspicion of MNAR, the authors suggest conducting it whenever contextual information or prior knowledge makes MNAR plausible, as when learning strategies are linked to dropout.
