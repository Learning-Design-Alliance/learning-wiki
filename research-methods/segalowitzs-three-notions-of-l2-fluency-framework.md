## Description
The article organizes its measurement of speaking around Segalowitz's distinction among cognitive fluency, utterance fluency and perceived fluency. Cognitive fluency concerns "the speaker’s abilities to efficiently plan and execute his speech", while utterance fluency is measured in a speech sample. Following Skehan and Tavakoli, the study further splits utterance fluency into breakdown, speed and repair fluency and selects measures so these aspects are not confounded.
