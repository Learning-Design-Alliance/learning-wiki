# Gilmore's eight catastrophe flags for detecting developmental transitions

## Description
Gilmore's eight criteria (flags) — bimodality, inaccessibility, sudden jumps, hysteresis, divergence, divergence of linear response, anomalous variance and critical slowing down — are empirical criteria for detecting transitions, illustrated for the cusp model with conservation acquisition. Five flags are present inside the bifurcation set and three can also be present outside it. The article stresses that 'None of the individual flags alone deliver conclusive proof for the presence of a transition, but together they give convincing indications that a transition is present.'
