# Phased or randomized rollout evaluation design

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article argues that simultaneous national or large-scale rollout destroys evaluation credibility: "several programs lack a credible counterfactual, owing to the demand for simultaneous national or large‐scale rollout". It recommends donors and governments weigh the political advantages of universal rollout against phased implementation, which improves management and implementation fidelity. Lottery or randomized selection of school groups into phased entry treatment groups permits rigorous testing of programmatic effects.
