## Description
The article proposes an extension of the Gaussian hidden Markov model that "incorporates parameters representing temporal change in a subtask appearance distribution for each learner," so that writing-process sequence patterns can be estimated from keystroke log data. The model treats the writing process as a sequence of subtasks such as planning, formulation, and revision, and is intended to relieve instructors of analyzing sequence patterns for all learners manually.
