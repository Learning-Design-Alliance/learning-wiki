# Cluster-based productive-persistence discovery method

## Description
The article proposes "a new cluster-based method that combines posttest performance, eﬀort (to quantify persistence), and unsolicited hint usage to discover productive persistence." It defines productive persistence as "persistent (high) eﬀorts that result in higher posttest performance", building on work distinguishing productive persistence from wheel-spinning. Effort is operationalised through two research-based measures: time spent on unsolved (skipped) training problems and the number of restarts on eventually-solved problems. Students are clustered on five features spanning productivity, effort, and unsolicited hint usage.
