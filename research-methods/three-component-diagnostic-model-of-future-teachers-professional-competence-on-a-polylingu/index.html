Three-component diagnostic model of future teachers' professional competence on a polylingual basis¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

According to the developed model, professional competence consists of three components: motivational, cognitive and operational. The motivational component "performs a regulatory function in the structure of formation, expresses a conscious attitude of the student to polylingual approach"; the cognitive component covers pedagogical, special, methodological and polylingual knowledge; the operational component ensures abilities and skills for practical experience. Each component carries a criterion with three indicators (Table 1).

Claims¶

  • Future teachers show low motivation for polylingual subject teaching, with 73.2% saying it is unnecessary and 87.2% not self-learning subjects in a second language [+W]
  • Few future teachers master operational competencies for polylingual teaching, from 17% able to plan to 7% able to analyze and evaluate results [+W]
  • Initial formation levels are predominantly low and medium in both experimental and control groups, with the high level absent in the experimental group [+W]
