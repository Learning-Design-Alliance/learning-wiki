# Reusable and inspectable behavioral annotation components

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends making behavioral annotation components reusable and inspectable: an assertion annotated once in a corpus can inform multiple construct labels, and assertion annotations can train smaller classifiers for later annotation. Sharing "assertion definitions, annotated examples, trained classifiers, and combination rules" reduces the need to develop every measure from the beginning, while each reused measure still requires validation for its intended use.
