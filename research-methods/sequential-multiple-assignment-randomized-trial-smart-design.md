## Description
The sequential multiple assignment randomized trial is a design in which "participants are randomly assigned to intervention components at multiple stages of the intervention," with assignments determined by decision rules based on proximal outcomes. Participants meeting a benchmark are deemed responders and continue unchanged; nonresponders receive preassigned modifications. Head-to-head comparisons across the multistage randomization yield "embedded interventions" whose most promising version can proceed to future research. The article uses it to compare two context-personalization approaches and two nonresponder supports.
