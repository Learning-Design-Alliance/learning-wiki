## Description
The study adopts action research, defined as a kind of study carried out by a practitioner, usually in collaboration with others, concerned with identification and solution of problems in a specific educational setting. The article identifies three defining characteristics: it is carried out by a practitioner, it is collaborative, and it is aimed at changing things. Nunan's five-stage procedure is described, from initiation by a practitioner through collaboration, objective data collection, dissemination, to an ongoing cycle in which the instructor "reflects on, returns to, and extends the initial enquiry".
