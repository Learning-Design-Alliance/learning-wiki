# Six-behaviour spatial pedagogy coding scheme for team teaching

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An observation coding scheme adapted from prior work that integrates spatial pedagogy space types with team-teaching research. "The final coding scheme distinguished six spatial pedagogy behaviours organised into three broader categories": Instruction (Lecturing), Interaction (Ts-S interaction, T-S interaction, T-Ss interaction), and Collaboration (Assisting, T-T interaction). Supervisory and Personal space behaviours were not retained because they typically involve little or no sustained speech. Inter-rater reliability on a pilot session reached a Cohen's Kappa of 0.75.
