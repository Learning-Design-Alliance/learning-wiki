# Quasi-mixed methods: LLM-human workflow for analyzing qualitative reflection data

## Description
The article proposes a "quasi-mixed" method in which "our “quasi-mixed” method applies quantitative and qualitative analysis to the same qualitative dataset", extending NLP-human mixed-methods workflows to an LLM-human workflow. It is operationalized as a six-stage pipeline: data familiarization, manual quote extraction, human and LLM-assisted sentiment labeling, human revision of LLM labels, parallel qualitative and quantitative analyses, and interpretation of qualitative findings considering quantitative results. The LLM is treated as an additional individual rater whose discrepancies inform deeper analysis.
