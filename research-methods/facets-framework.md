# FACETS framework

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The FACETS framework, introduced by Gordon et al. in BEME Guide No. 84, offers a structured approach to describe AI innovations in education across six dimensions: Form of AI, AI Use Case, Context, Education Focus, Technology, and SAMR. The review describes it as acting as a "compass" for reporting AI in education, combining details often reported separately into a single description. It was used as the coding schema in this review to classify each included study and enhance comparability across studies.
