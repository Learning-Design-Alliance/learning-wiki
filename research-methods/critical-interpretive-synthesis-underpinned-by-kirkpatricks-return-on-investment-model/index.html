Critical interpretive synthesis underpinned by Kirkpatrick's return on investment model¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

This is the review's methodological framework: a "Critical interpretive synthesis underpinned by Kirkpatrick's return on investment model" applied to identify features of effective dementia educational programs. The approach integrates interpretive synthesis of heterogeneous studies with Kirkpatrick's evaluation framework, allowing the authors to judge program efficacy across training outcomes for the workforce.
