Three-variable taxonomy (knowledge, attitudes, perceptions) for classifying outcomes of nurses' fever-management education¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review organizes the evaluation of educational programs for nurses around three outcome classes. Knowledge covers "understanding of fever physiology, fever management, and antipyretic drugs or measures"; attitudes cover changes in clinical practice with a febrile child, performance against febrile convulsions, and health education provided to parents; perceptions cover how parents, nursing colleagues, and physicians influence care of a febrile child and how much control professionals have over management. The meta-analysis pools studies separately within these three classes.
