Taxonomy of algebraic thinking operationalizations: global measures versus specific facets¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The review organizes algebra outcome measures into a taxonomy distinguishing global constructs from specific facets. It states that 'some studies focus on conceptual understanding of algebraic concepts, such as the equal sign, negative sign, variables, and exponents; this is often referred to as algebraic feature knowledge.' Other operationalizations include equation encoding, 'their ability to perceptually process and represent equation structures, components, and terms,' and equation-solving accuracy on linear equations. The review argues propensities 'may be differentially related to algebra understanding depending on the algebra outcome measure used.'
