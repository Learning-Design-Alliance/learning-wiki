Four-condition dichotic listening paradigm with mTRF speech-envelope reconstruction¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A child-appropriate EEG paradigm in which participants attend to an English narrative in one ear while ignoring interference in the other across four conditions: Single talker (no interference), English–English, English–Latin, and English–Musical Rain. Backward mTRF decoding models reconstruct attended and unattended speech envelopes from 60-channel EEG, with reconstruction accuracy (Pearson's r) as the index of attentional encoding. The article notes the method "maps all available neural data simultaneously" and is suited to multi-channel EEG and natural speech.

Claims¶

  • Selective attention improves neural encoding of attended speech envelopes in children aged 7–12 [+M]
  • Bilingual children show a flatter pattern of attended-speech encoding across conditions, driven by weaker encoding in low- or no-interference conditions [+W]
  • Monolingual and bilingual children perform the dichotic comprehension task equivalently, with both groups finding English–English interference hardest [+W]
  • Unattended Musical Rain streams were better reconstructed than unattended linguistic distractors in both groups, with stronger MuR encoding in monolinguals [+W]
