## Description
The article organizes computer-supported word reading interventions into four program types: foundational (comprehensive) reading instruction, supplementary alphabetics, reading fluency programs, and remedial reading programs for at-risk students in higher grades. It states "A distinction can be made between programs focusing on basic phonics instruction, supplementary alphabetic skills, reading fluency programs, and remedial reading interventions", and compares these four types on accuracy and speed of processing letters, phonological units, words, pseudowords, passages, and spelling. Comprehensive programs teach grapheme-phoneme correspondences explicitly in incremental sets; supplementary programs add phonological awareness and letter training; fluency programs build speeded word recognition; remedial programs give individualized or small-group tutoring.
