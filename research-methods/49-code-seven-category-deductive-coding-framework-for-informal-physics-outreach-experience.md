## Description
The article's qualitative analysis rests on a deductive coding scheme drawing on Hazari et al.'s physics identity framework (interest and motivation, performance and competence beliefs, recognition, sense of belonging), the Dynamic Systems Model of Role Identity, situated learning theory's legitimate peripheral participation, transformative learning theory, and a list of 21st-century physics career skills. As printed, "49 codes were used in this work, organized into the following seven categories: Identity, Community, Affect and Experience , Disciplinary Skills , Non-disciplinary Skills , Mindset, and Outcomes." Codes were applied by five researchers with inter-rater reliability κ ≥ 0.90, then analyzed via co-occurrence network analysis.
