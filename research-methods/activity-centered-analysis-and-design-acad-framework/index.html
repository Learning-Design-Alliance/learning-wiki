Activity-Centered Analysis and Design (ACAD) framework¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The Activity-Centered Analysis and Design (ACAD) model provides a metatheoretical framework for understanding complex learning situations by focusing on activities at the heart of student perceptions. In the context of DPBL, it categorizes instructional design options into three distinct categories: "social design" (how students engage with tasks individually, in groups, or as teams, including social roles), "epistemic design" (the tasks that students are asked to perform), and "set design" (the digital tools with which students interact). The review used this framework to categorize 453 student quotes and identify key design considerations for educators designing future DPBL interventions.
