# Categorization schema for classroom factors: density dependency and psychosocial vs. environmental

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The framework operationalizes classroom factors along two dimensions: density dependency and type. The framework "operationalizes these factors by introducing dimensions of density dependency (dependent vs. independent) and type (psychosocial vs. environmental), enabling a systematic evaluation of their cumulative and interactive effects on classroom outcomes." Density-dependent factors (e.g., instructional time, student-teacher ratios) change in effect as collective student needs increase, while density-independent factors (e.g., classroom space, teacher training) affect support quality regardless of density. Environmental factors are tangible (space, materials, time), psychosocial factors include efficacy, relationships, and behaviors.
