# Three structural causal models of the self-concept–achievement reciprocal relationship

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The article derives a taxonomy of three structural causal models for the reciprocal effects model: "(a) a model with prior scores predicting post scores; (b) a model with additional lag-2 effects ... also referred to as a full-forward model; and (c) a model with lag-2 effects and additional confounders". Model (a) assumes no hidden confounders or carry-over effects and is judged unrealistic; model (c), including time-invariant and time-varying confounders such as gender or school track, "seems to be most realistic". The taxonomy determines which statistical model can identify causal effects.
