# Nonequivalent control group quasi-experimental design

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article adapts Campbell and Stanley's nonequivalent control group design to standalone library instruction: treatment and control sections are pre-existing course sections rather than individually randomized, and an in-class pretest strengthens evidence that the groups are similar. The librarian retains control of administering and collecting pretests, posttests and surveys within the single session. The authors frame this as a site-specific methodological strategy for evaluating pre-session homework independently of identical in-class instruction.
