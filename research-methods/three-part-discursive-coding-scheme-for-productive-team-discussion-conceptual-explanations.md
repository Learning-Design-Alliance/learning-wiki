# Three-part discursive coding scheme for productive team discussion: conceptual explanations, re-evaluations, and co-construction

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article develops a grounded-theory coding scheme for team discourse built around three phenomena: conceptual explanations, re-evaluations, and co-construction. Conceptual explanations are "stretches of talk in which one or more students in the group produced a hypothesis of physiological functioning that was not simply fact based"; re-evaluation covers disagreement, pointing out overlooked issues, or asking for further explanation; co-construction covers turns in which speakers build on one another to create explanations. The scheme was applied to 12 transcripts of recorded team talk.
