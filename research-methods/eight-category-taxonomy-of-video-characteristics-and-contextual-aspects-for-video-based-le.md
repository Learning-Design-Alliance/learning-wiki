# Eight-category taxonomy of video characteristics and contextual aspects for video-based learning

## Description
The review derives a taxonomy that organizes video characteristics and contextual aspects into eight categories: audio features, visual features, textual features, instructor behavior, learners' activities, interactive features, production style, and instructional design. Each category is populated with sub-categories and example features drawn from the 257 reviewed articles, such as transcripts, metadata, and on-screen text under textual features, and quizzes, annotations, and navigation interface elements under interactive features. The authors state the taxonomy "provides a holistic view and allows to systematically consider the critical components of a learning video" and can support assessment, optimization, and customization of educational video content.
