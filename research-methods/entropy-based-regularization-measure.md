# Entropy-based regularization measure

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article formalizes regularization using Shannon entropy quantities — H(V), H(C), H(V|C), H(C|V), joint entropy H(V,C), and mutual information I(V;C) — as a "principled measure of regularization that is comparable across diﬀerent distributions of variation and stimuli domains." Regularization "is the reduction or elimination of entropy in a data set," occurring by eliminating variants, eliminating conditioning contexts, or increasing conditioning between variants and contexts. It applies this framework to define how much variation each experimental participant regularized.
