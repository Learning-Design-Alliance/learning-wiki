# Bloom, Raudenbush, and Weiss multilevel methodology for estimating variation in program impacts across individuals, subgroups, and sites

## Description
The article applies a new and innovative methodology for estimating sources of variation in program impacts (Bloom, 2012; Bloom, Raudenbush, & Weiss, 2013). It uses a two-level model with a random treatment-effect component across center groups, estimated with new capabilities in the HLM software, and tests cross-site impact variance with a Chi-Square test of the Q statistic used in meta-analysis. Crucially, it distinguishes variation in program effect estimates from variation in true program effects, accounting for estimation error.
