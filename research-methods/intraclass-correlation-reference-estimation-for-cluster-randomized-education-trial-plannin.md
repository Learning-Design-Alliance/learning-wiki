# Intraclass-correlation reference estimation for cluster-randomized education trial planning

## Description
A tabulated compilation of intraclass correlations of academic achievement and related covariate effects drawn from national probability longitudinal surveys (ECLS, Prospects, NELS, LSAY). The article states it "provides a compilation of intraclass correlation values of academic achievement and related covariate effects that could be used for planning group-randomized experiments in education." Estimates were computed via restricted maximum likelihood in Stata's XTMIXED, yielding 416 ICC estimates each with a standard error, organized by grade, domain, covariate set, and school status.
