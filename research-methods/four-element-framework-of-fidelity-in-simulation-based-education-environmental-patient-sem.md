# Four-element framework of fidelity in simulation-based education: environmental, patient, semantical, and phenomenal elements

## Description
The article cites a framework subdividing fidelity in simulation-based education into four elements. The article states "More recently, fidelity in simulation-based education has been subdivided into the following four elements [8]: (1) environmental element - the training facility and equipment used. (2) Patient element - the virtual reality, full body or part-task trainer used to represent the patient. (3) Semantical element - the scenario in which the simulator is used and the associated intention for the user. And (4) phenomenal element - the engagement of the user through their emotional and cognitive experiences while involved in the simulation activity."
