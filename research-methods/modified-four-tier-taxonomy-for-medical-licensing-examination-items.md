## Description
The article introduces a refinement of Japan's traditional three-tier examination taxonomy, retaining Types I (recall) and II (interpretation) but subdividing Type III into IIIa and IIIb. Type IIIa covers "Items in which once the stem is correctly interpreted, the answer can be derived through a simple recall process", addressing a previously undefined region between Types II and III. Type IIIb requires interpreting both stem and answer choices, capturing context-sensitive decision making contingent on severity, comorbidities, or allergies. The framework was aligned with Revised Bloom's Taxonomy and ACGME Internal Medicine Milestones and applied to classify all 2880 sampled NMLE items.
