# Multi-method research framework combining SNA, content analysis and contextual analysis for networked learning

## Description
The article develops a framework in which three methods answer complementary questions about networked learning: social network analysis to find out "who is talking to whom", content analysis of coded learning and tutoring activities to find out "what they are talking about", and contextual analysis (including critical event recall) to find out "why they are talking as they do". The methods triangulate and contextualise findings, using the outcomes of one method to understand the results of the next. Timeline analysis across beginning, middle and end phases adds the dimension of time, which the authors say has been largely ignored in NL research.
