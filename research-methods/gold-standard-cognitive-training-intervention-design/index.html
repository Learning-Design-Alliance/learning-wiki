Gold-standard cognitive training intervention design¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Drawing on recent reviews of cognitive training, the article states that "a gold standard cognitive intervention should (1) employ outcome measures with a clear targeted theoretical construct; (2) minimize potential placebo effects... by including an active control group", (3) randomize participants, and (4) restrict transfer tasks to theoretically related ones. The study applied these by using a single-task active control, randomization, and a factor-analytically grounded transfer battery.
