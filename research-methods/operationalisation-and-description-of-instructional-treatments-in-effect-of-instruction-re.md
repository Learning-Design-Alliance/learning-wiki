## Description
Because active learning is rarely defined and interpreted in many different ways, the article advocates that BER concerning active learning provide a cited definition and that authors define and describe the active-learning strategies used in experimental research. The article states: "we advocate for all BER concerning active learning to provide a cited definition." Providing definitions may aid increased fidelity and reproducibility of experimental outcomes.
