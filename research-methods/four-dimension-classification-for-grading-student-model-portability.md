# Four-dimension classification for grading student model portability

## Description
The article introduces a classification that grades student models in terms of portability. Portability is measured across four dimensions — accessibility, complexity, architecture, and popularity — each composed of factors such as open source status, availability, inputs, types of data, input formats, computational techniques, data mining use, model origin, implementation, modularity, server/client architecture, and re-implementation. Each factor is graded 1 to 4 (low, mid, high restrictions, or no information), dimension grades are normalized sums mapped to 1–4, and the overall grade is the sum of the dimensions, where a grade of 4 represents "the models with least types restrictions we found (Very Low)". The authors intend it "to be used by researchers both as a methodology to measure the portability of a student model and as a guide to find existing reusable models".
