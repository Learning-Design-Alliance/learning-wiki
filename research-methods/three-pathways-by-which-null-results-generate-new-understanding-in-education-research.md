# Three pathways by which null results generate new understanding in education research

## Description
The article proposes a framework of three ways null results contribute to educational knowledge: "by providing existence proofs of the viability of new approaches, by showing how previous results can fail to generalize to new contexts, and by uncovering the necessary conditions for replication." Each pathway is illustrated with a case study from the authors' own PER work: the GPS study, the proportions study, and the diagram-prompting replication. The framework underwrites the article's argument that studies should be judged by their potential for generating understanding rather than by p-values alone.
