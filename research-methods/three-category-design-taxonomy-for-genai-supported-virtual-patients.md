## Description
The review organizes the design choices of GenAI-supported virtual patients into three categories: "The designs of GenAI-supported virtual patients can be classified into three distinct categories: (1) input, (2) output, and (3) avatar." Input modalities were text (11 of 15 studies), voice (5), or hybrid (1); output corresponded closely to input mechanisms; and avatar design distinguished 6 studies using 3D-embodied virtual patients from 9 using nonembodied interfaces.
