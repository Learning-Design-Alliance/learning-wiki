Grey-number method for evaluating mean performance from linguistic grades¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article proposes using grey numbers (GNs), numbers with unknown position within known boundaries represented as closed real intervals, to estimate group mean performance when students are assessed with linguistic grades A–F. Each grade is assigned an interval (e.g. A∈[85,100]), and the grey representative M yields W(M) = (m1+m2)/2 as "a reliable estimation of the student group mean performance". This avoids dilemmas about exact numerical scores and reduces student anxiety, but prevents conventional statistical testing.
