# Five key questions for determining whether CTA methods fit a research study

## Description
The article provides an enumerated set of five considerations for judging whether CTA is appropriate: whether the study concerns a complex workplace environment characterized by uncertainty, whether the research focuses on cognition that is hard to articulate, whether relevant experts can be accessed, whether onto-epistemological assumptions have been considered, and whether findings have potential to impact practice. These are printed as question prompts in Table 1, guiding researchers on when CTA fits versus when alternatives such as the Critical Incident Technique are more appropriate.
