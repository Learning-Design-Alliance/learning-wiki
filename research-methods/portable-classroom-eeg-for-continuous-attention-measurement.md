# Portable classroom EEG for continuous attention measurement

## Description
The article argues that portable EEG provides an objective, real-time index of classroom attention that behavioral observation and self-report cannot supply, because those methods "are each indirect and cannot pick up the dynamic changes in student engagement". In their study, neural measures revealed that looking-away during group work may coincide with attentional engagement, and the authors conclude EEG signals "offer a powerful tool for understanding differences in student cognitive states as a function of classroom instruction that are unobservable from behavior alone".

- **Use portable EEG to assess student attention in classrooms because behavioral observation misses covert attentional processes**: The article argues that portable EEG provides an objective, real-time index of classroom attention that behavioral observation and self-report cannot supply, because those methods "are each indirect and cannot pick up the dynamic changes in student engagement". In their study, neural measures revealed that looking-away during group work may coincide with attentional engagement, and the authors conclude EEG signals "offer a powerful tool for understanding differences in student cognitive states as a function of classroom instruction that are unobservable from behavior alone". (Jennie K. Grammer et al. (2021))
- **Wearable dual-channel EEG headband setup for real-classroom brain recording**: A portable EEG data-collection setup in which students wore "a dual-channel headband with dry electrodes... to record EEG at Fp1 and Fp2 over the forehead at a sampling rate of 250 Hz" during regular Math and Chinese lessons. Headbands were fitted before and removed after each 40-minute session, enabling EEG acquisition from an entire class across a full semester (39 sessions over 4 months). An eye-closed/open validation task confirmed signal quality via the expected alpha-range spectral peak in the eye-closed condition. (Jingjing Chen et al. (2023))
- **Wearable single-channel EEG headband (NeuroSky-based) for continuous classroom attention monitoring**: A single-channel headband (Cusoft, China) with dry electrodes and a NeuroSky EEG biosensor recording at Fpz at 512 Hz, with a reference on the left ear lobe. The biosensor "can calculate every second’s attention level  based on their patented algorithm from EEG signals", with values from 1 to 100 indicating "very distracted" to "very focused". The article uses it because it monitors attention continuously and non-intrusively, and notes its high portability and low cost make findings readily applicable in educational practice. (Jingjing Chen et al. (2022))
