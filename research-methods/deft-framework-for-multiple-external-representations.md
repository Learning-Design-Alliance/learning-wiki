## Description
The article applies the DeFT framework (Designs, Functions, Tasks) for multiple external representations to quantum curriculum development, noting that recent publications "have emphasized the importance of multiple external representations (MERs) within the DeFT framework" for quantum science curriculum development. Per the framework, MERs "serve to provide complementary information, to constrain interpretation, and to construct deeper understanding of a concept." Intro2QC links graphic tables, bit-counting graphics, Bloch spheres, circuit diagrams and simplified state-vector equations as each section's objective requires.
