# General Multi-method Approach to Data-Driven Redesign of tutoring systems

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
A three-step process, introduced by Huang et al. and tested for generality in this article, that "integrates KC model refinement, instructional redesign, and optimization of individualized learning". It addresses misallocated practice across KCs, insufficient scaffolding for hard KCs identified by persistently high learning curves, and recurring errors, combining Difficulty Factors Effect Analysis with educational data mining techniques supported by DataShop. In this study the process was applied to four MathTutor units: learning-curve analysis refined KC models, focused part tasks scaffolded difficult KCs, and an adaptive problem selection algorithm prioritized unmastered KCs.
