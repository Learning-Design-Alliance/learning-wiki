# PARI methodology: a structured dyadic problem-solving interview for cognitive task analysis

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The PARI (Precursor, Action, Result, Interpretation) methodology is a structured, thinking-aloud interview technique for cognitive task analysis of diagnostic problem solving, developed under the Basic Job Skills program. Per the report, "PARI: Precursor, (or reason for Action), Action, Result, and Interpretation (of result) are problem-solving components of interest in diagnostic tasks." Experts work in dyads: one poses a problem to a naive expert who generates a solution step by step while the first supplies results, followed by rehash sessions probing the reasons for decisions. The approach is grounded in studies of over 200 Air Force technicians in aircraft maintenance specialties whose primary task is troubleshooting.
