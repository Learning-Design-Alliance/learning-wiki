Five-level scaffolding scale for measuring the directness of assistance in LLM tutoring responses¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article operationalizes scaffolding as a five-level ordinal scale characterizing LLM responses by the amount of assistance they provide: L0 Minimal, L1 Prompting, L2 Hinting, L3 Explaining, and L4 Solving, where "Higher levels provide more directly usable assistance and leave less of the task for the student to complete independently." The L3/L4 distinction is direct usability: L3 gives an approach the student must still apply, L4 a directly usable task-specific solution. Responses are classified with GPT-4o-mini at temperature 0 given the codebook and preceding context.
