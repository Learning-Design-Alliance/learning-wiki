## Description
The FASI framework holds that assessments of expert-like thinking should probe "many different facets of thinking and learning, rather than a single construct," and must serve instructors by defining aspects instruction can target, within classroom time and attention limits. The NRC's three assessment elements — cognition, observation, and interpretation — map onto expert-novice differences, the survey tasks, and interview-based validation. Group-level results matter more than ranking individuals, and the assessments must remain low stakes.
