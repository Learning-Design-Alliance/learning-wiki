# Pre-impact piloting of intervention delivery and measurement procedures

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report's key conclusions state that the Hampshire Hundreds project "illustrates the importance of careful piloting of an intervention before attempting an impact evaluation". Unanticipated practical barriers, such as schools' insufficient IT for administering the computer-adaptive tests and the onerous testing process, caused delays and attrition that reduced the trial's power. Piloting would surface such delivery and measurement problems before they compromise a randomised evaluation.
