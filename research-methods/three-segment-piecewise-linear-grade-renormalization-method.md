## Description
The article recommends mapping raw recitation grades through three continuous line segments whose break points in the domain subdivide the section into three approximately equal numbers of students, leaving two parameters constrained by the goal-average condition plus one distortion-minimizing condition. The minimax variant "minimizes the maximum change, in absolute value, in a student's grade" and forces the middle slope to 1; the slope variant minimizes the summed squared deviation of slopes from 1, treating f and its inverse on the same footing.
