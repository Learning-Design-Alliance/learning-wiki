# NISPI (Nonverbal Indexes of Students' Physical Interactivity) framework

## Description
The NISPI (Nonverbal Indexes of Students' Physical Interactivity) framework codes students' states in 30-second windows as passive (0), semi-active (1) or active (2) based on hand activity with objects and head direction, then derives group-level indexes for synchrony, individual accountability, equality and intra-individual variability. The article states the coding scheme "is simple enough to be automated with existing learning analytics systems" and comprehensive enough to interpret key CPS aspects. It was applied to high school and university groups in practice-based learning and validated against human observers' CPS rankings.
