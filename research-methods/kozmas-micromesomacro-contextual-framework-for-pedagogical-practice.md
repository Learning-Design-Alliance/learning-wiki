# Kozma's micro–meso–macro contextual framework for pedagogical practice

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The framework adopts Kozma's (2003) three-level account of contexts shaping pedagogical practice. The article states that "RIGID expands the scope of analysis by incorporating Kozma's (2003) framework of three contextual levels that shape pedagogical practice: micro, meso, and macro." The micro level covers classroom factors such as student characteristics and teacher expertise; the meso level covers institutional conditions including organizational goals, resources, and local policies; the macro level covers educational policies and theoretical perspectives. Effective practice emerges when all three interact in a mutually reinforcing way rather than one level unilaterally determining outcomes.
