# Triangulated multi-method needs analysis for learner-centered ESP course design

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The article frames needs analysis (NA) as "an information gathering process" executed to identify learners' reasons for learning a language so they can be taught relevant materials, drawing on West's (1987) delineation of target situation analysis and deficiency analysis. It argues multiple sources and procedures should be used, with triangulation involving "the systematic comparison of interim findings from two or more sources" to validate findings with informants. The study operationalizes this with observations, two interview protocols and a questionnaire, triangulated to validate findings.
