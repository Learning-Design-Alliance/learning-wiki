# Collaboration Literacy Analytics Framework (CLAF)

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The CLAF is a conceptual framework 'that integrates evaluation metrics, indicator interdependencies, and feedback mechanisms.' It 'captures the dynamic relationship between process and outcome measures, connects individual- and group-level indicators, and incorporates review mechanisms as student-driven quality assurance.' The framework organizes learner-valued indicators across process, outcome, and review layers and links real-time feedback for immediate self-regulation with post hoc feedback for longer-term reflection and collaboration literacy development. It is grounded in consolidated collaboration dimensions and the student survey data.
