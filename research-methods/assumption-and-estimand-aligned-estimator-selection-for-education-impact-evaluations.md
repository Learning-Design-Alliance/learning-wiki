## Description
The report's central recommendation is that method choice should be driven by the alignment between an estimator's assumptions and the study's goals rather than by the design-based versus model-based label, since the empirical differences were small. It advises that "researchers should select estimators with assumptions that best suit the goals of their study regardless of whether they use a design- or model- based approach," and that researchers should consider how assumptions affect interpretation. A stated advantage of design-based methods is that their assumptions are explicit and must be specified by the analyst.
