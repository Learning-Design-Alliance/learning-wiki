## Description
The paper argues SEERNet's contribution "could be enhanced by integrating a problem-based approach inspired by Improvement Science—that is, by collaborating with educators to identify the right problem of practice to focus on". Starting from educators' sense of what is important enables context-sensitive, precisely targeted interventions that are practically applicable and sustainable, while still using rigorous comparative testing within the platforms.
