# Three-question structure for school performance inquiry

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The framework structures school performance inquiry around three questions: "1. How are students doing? 2. How much does the school contribute to how students are doing? and 3. What is happening in the school?" Each question maps to a complementary grouping of measures—student outcomes, school impacts on outcomes, and processes driving those impacts—so the three together give a rounded picture of school performance.

## Description
The article presents a framework offering "an improved measurement strategy states and districts can implement to answer three critical questions about school performance": how students are doing, how much the school contributes to how students are doing, and what is happening in the school. It organizes performance measurement into complementary groupings of student outcomes, school impacts on outcomes, and the processes that drive those impacts, so that officials can better identify and understand student and school needs.
