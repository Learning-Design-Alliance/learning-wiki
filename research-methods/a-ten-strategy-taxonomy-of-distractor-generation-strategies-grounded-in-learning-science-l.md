# A ten-strategy taxonomy of distractor-generation strategies grounded in learning-science literature and observed LLM reasoning

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
The article introduces a taxonomy of ten distractor-generation strategies, "grounded in learning-science literature and empirical observations of LLMs’ reasoning traces", spanning task understanding (INTER, CORR), candidate construction (LINK, ERR_DESC, ERR_SIM, INST), quality control and selection (PLAUS, DISCR, CURATE), and cross-cutting reconsideration (RECON). It was built by merging expert-practice strategies from the literature with strategies extracted via manual open coding of LLM traces, and is applied to annotate 885 reasoning traces span by span.
