## Description
The article classifies participants along two axes — creative versus conservative intention, and creative versus conservative effective solution — yielding four profiles: the creatives, the conservatives, the non-intentional creatives and the non-intentional conservatives. Non-intentional creatives intended to repeat their solution but failed and adopted a creative behavior, while non-intentional conservatives include participants whose creative intention collapsed into conservative behavior. This pattern lets an observer diagnose where in a repeated task creative pursuit is lost or accidentally achieved.
