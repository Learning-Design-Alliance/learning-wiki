## Description
The article recommends planning replications inside multi-year research programs rather than as one-off studies: "a logical next step for our research on ROOTS would be to conduct a series of increasingly distal replication studies." It reports grounding its IES-funded ROOTS efficacy project in a systematic replication framework aiming to accumulate converging evidence across four cohorts from two regions, and proposes adding rates of treatment response to the replication conversation. It cautions that statistical power and available resources must be taken into full consideration when planning such series.
