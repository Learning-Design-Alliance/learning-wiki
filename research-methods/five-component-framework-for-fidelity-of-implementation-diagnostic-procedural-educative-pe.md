# Five-component framework for fidelity of implementation: Diagnostic, Procedural, Educative, Pedagogical, and Student Engagement

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Drawing on Century and Cassata's summary of research, the article presents "five components to consider in fidelity of implementation: Diagnostic, Procedural, Educative, Pedagogical, and Student Engagement". Diagnostic components say what the "it" being implemented is; structural-procedural components tell the instructor what to do; structural-educative components state what users need to know; interaction-pedagogical components capture expected instructor actions and interactions; interaction-engagement components delineate expected student actions. The framework is operationalized through a rubric used as the guide for collecting and reporting implementation data.
