# Two-ladder classification of readability: easy-difficult and interesting-dull parameters

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The University of Illinois readability survey organizes readability measurement around two principal parameters. The easy-difficult ladder covers vocabulary difficulty, word length, sentence length and complexity, and parts-of-speech difficulty; the interesting-dull ladder covers references to people, verb-adjective ratios, image-bearing and human-interest words, and early-learned words and localisms. The survey reports that "Readability measures, for the most part, classify writing on two principal parameters", making this the organizing framework of the reviewed literature.
