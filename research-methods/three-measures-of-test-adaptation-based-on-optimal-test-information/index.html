Three measures of test adaptation based on optimal test information¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article introduces a framework of three new measures of test adaptation, each grounded in the amount of information that a test provides to individual examinees. As the abstract states, the measures "are designed to provide theoretical, engineering, and operational views of the amount of adaptation that a particular test provides." The framework extends prior work on quantifying how adaptive a test is.
