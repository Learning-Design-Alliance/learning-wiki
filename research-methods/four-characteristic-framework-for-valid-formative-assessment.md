# Four-characteristic framework for valid formative assessment

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review presents validity as a matter of degree and identifies characteristics that underpin valid formative assessment. It states: "According to Gikandi et al. (2011), four characteristics underpin the validity of formative assessment: (1) authenticity of assessment activities, (2) effective formative feedback, (3) inclusion of multidimensional perspectives, and (4) robust learner support." It further argues that learning analytics strengthens the validity argument by "capturing, analyzing, and reporting rich, multimodal evidence of student learning progress," and cites Divjak et al. (2023) on using analytics to compare ideal learning-objective priorities, assessment weights, and achievement data.
