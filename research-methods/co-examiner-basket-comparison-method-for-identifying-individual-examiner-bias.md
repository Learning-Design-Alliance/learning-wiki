# Co-examiner basket comparison method for identifying individual examiner bias

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
A statistical method in which each examiner's marks are compared with the aggregate 'basket' of marks made by all co-examiners who judged the same candidates at the same stations. Because two examiners see identical candidate performance, differences must reflect examiner variance, giving the method "much greater statistical power, as well much greater face validity". It assesses hawkishness-doveishness, sex bias, ethnic bias and a control odd-even bias, using t-tests with Bonferroni correction.
