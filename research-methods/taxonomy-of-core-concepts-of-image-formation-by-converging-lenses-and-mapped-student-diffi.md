# Taxonomy of core concepts of image formation by converging lenses and mapped student difficulties

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
A taxonomy compiled from prior literature linking core concepts of image formation by converging lenses to documented student difficulties. The article notes "Galili and colleagues [13] (p. 280) introduce five core concepts (CC) about image formation and observation in the context of converging lenses" — object and image points, the optical device, converging and diverging light fluxes, the observer's eye, and light rays as a theoretical tool. Table I maps these and further concepts, such as light properties, human vision, screen placement, and virtual images, to difficulties that form the basis of CLCI items and distractors.
