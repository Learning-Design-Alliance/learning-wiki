## Description
The article organises its validity evaluation around "the framework described in the Standards of Educational and Psychological Testing", examining "four aspects of construct validity": content, response process, internal structure, and consequences of testing. Content was addressed via blueprint alignment and expert scenario review; response process via training and calibration of examiners, SPs and students; internal structure via six psychometric metrics; and consequences via BRM and Angoff standard setting. The framework structures both the methods and the discussion of the virtual OSCE.
