Teacher Team Method for co-taught value-added estimation¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Teacher Team Method is one of three methods the article proposes for estimating teacher value added when students are co-taught. Like the Full Roster Method, it presumes that co-teachers share joint responsibility for the achievement of their shared students. The article explores its properties and compares its empirical estimates with the Full Roster Method, finding the two produce similar results.
