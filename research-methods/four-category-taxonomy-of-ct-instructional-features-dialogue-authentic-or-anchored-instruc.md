# Four-category taxonomy of CT instructional features: dialogue, authentic or anchored instruction, coaching/mentoring, and individual study

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To explain more variability in CT outcomes than Ennis's course typology alone, the authors developed "A new set of four major categories: individual study, dialogue, authentic or anchored instruction, and coaching." Each category is rated 0–3 (not present to strongly present) in each study, with subcategories such as teacher-posed questions, formal debate, Socratic dialogue, case studies, simulations, role-play, and one-on-one mentoring. Individual study was excluded from the main moderator analyses because it rarely distinguished experimental from control conditions. The taxonomy is the review's fine-grained lens on how CT instruction is enacted.
