# Newell-inspired time-scale taxonomy for aligning SRL measurement to theorized process duration

## Description
Adapting Newell's bands taxonomy from cognitive science, the article proposes that SRL phenomena be matched to the time scale at which they are theorized to occur: cognitive-band events over seconds observed with trace methodologies and think-alouds, rational-band events over minutes to hours including strategy changes and motivation fluctuations, and social-band contextual factors enduring over weeks and months. "Combining measures that span more than one band provides a rich account of SRL." Table 1 maps each special-issue study's measures to bands.

- **Align intervention strategies and model specifications with the timescales of the underlying psychological processes**: The article argues that because perceived competence and skills influence each other at different speeds, both intervention design and longitudinal modeling must match the timescales of the processes targeted. The abstract states the findings "underscore the importance of aligning intervention strategies and model specifications with the timescales of the underlying psychological processes". Fast skill-to-self-concept effects call for immediate feedback after performance; slow self-concept-to-skill effects call for sustained motivational support across semesters. (I Feel Competent, Therefore I Am: Self-concept and Skill Interact at Different Speeds)
