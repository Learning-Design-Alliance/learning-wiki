# Joughin's six-dimension taxonomy of oral assessments

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article presents a taxonomy of oral assessments along six dimensions: primary content type, interaction, authenticity, structure, examiners, and orality. It also distinguishes two qualities oral assessments can measure: "the student's oral skills in communication, and the second is the command of the content as demonstrated through the oral medium." The article uses this taxonomy to show oral assessments vary widely in form, from presentation to dialogue, and that educators' design choices along these dimensions determine what the assessment measures.
