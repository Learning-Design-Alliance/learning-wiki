# Response-to-challenge coding scheme adapted from the mindset literature for interview dialogue

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A set of interview codes characterizing how students respond to challenge in college physics, created by "adapting the key elements of response to challenge from the mindset literature: effort, strategy, emotion, and self-capability statements (Dweck, 2000)". A priori codes covered a range of effort (no effort, some effort, high effort), problem-solving/metacognition and new/change problem-solving/metacognition, positive and negative emotions, positive and negative self-capability statements in the present and future, meta-statements linking hard work or strategy-use to success, and deflection statements. Codes were then made more general than the mindset literature's versions due to the complexity and idiosyncrasy of student dialogue in transformed, project-based physics courses.
