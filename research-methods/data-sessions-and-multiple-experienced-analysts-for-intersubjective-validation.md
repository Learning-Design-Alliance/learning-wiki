# Data sessions and multiple experienced analysts for intersubjective validation

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Stahl proposes data sessions—involving multiple analysts reviewing the same data—as a mechanism for guarding against bias and idiosyncrasy in EM case analysis. Because the accountability of discourse makes it intersubjectively understandable, analysts sharing cultural understanding with participants can reach consensus on the meaning displayed. Detailed transcripts are published to allow reader validation.
