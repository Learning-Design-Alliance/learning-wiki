## Description
The article is grounded in a framework (from Reeves et al., 2016) in which "The framework articulates three key PD program outcomes variables: GTA cognition; GTA teaching practice; and undergraduate student outcomes." These outcomes are posited to be linearly and probabilistically related: PD changes GTA cognition, which changes teaching practice, which changes student outcomes. The framework also contains three categories of contextual variables (GTA training design, institutional, and GTA characteristics) and two categories of moderators (implementation variables and GTA characteristics, invoking Dane and Schneider's adherence, exposure, and participant responsiveness concepts). The present study tests the framework's assertion that PD effects vary as a function of contextual factors such as GTA characteristics.
