# Conceptual framework for online learning along three dimensions: replacement vs. enhancement, learning-experience type, and synchronicity

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report develops a conceptual framework to guide its literature search and review, "identifying three key components describing online learning": (a) whether the activity serves as a replacement for or an enhancement to conventional face-to-face instruction, (b) the type of learning experience (expository, active, or interactive), and (c) whether communication is primarily synchronous or asynchronous. Exhibit 1 illustrates uses of online learning for each combination of these dimensions. The dimensions were derived from prior distance-learning meta-analyses.
