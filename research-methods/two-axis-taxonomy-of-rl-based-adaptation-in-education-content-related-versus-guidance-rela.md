# Two-axis taxonomy of RL-based adaptation in education: content-related versus guidance-related

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review organizes RL-based educational applications by the kind of action the RL agent takes, distinguishing two main categories. The content-related category covers instructional sequencing (also called reinforcement scheduling) of learning activities to create personalized learning plans, including selecting content and adjusting pace or difficulty. The guidance-related category covers pedagogical strategies: "selecting the type of activity or hint to provide in response to a given learning task or problem", including hint generation, personalized feedback, and tutorial planning. This extends Doroudi et al.'s instructional-sequencing focus to a more holistic scope.
