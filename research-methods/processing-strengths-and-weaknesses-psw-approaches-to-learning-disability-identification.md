# Processing strengths and weaknesses (PSW) approaches to learning-disability identification

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
PSW methods operationalize a cognitive discrepancy approach to LD identification based on identifying an intraindividual pattern of processing strengths and weaknesses. The XBA approach "utilizes a series of normative comparisons to identify deficient and normal academic and cognitive abilities," requiring an achievement deficit, a normal cognitive profile, and a theoretically related cognitive deficit. The C/DM instead "calculates difference scores across a number of cognitive and achievement tests and compares these difference scores against computed critical values," requiring concordance between achievement and cognitive deficits and discordance with a cognitive strength. Both are aligned with CHC theory in this study.
