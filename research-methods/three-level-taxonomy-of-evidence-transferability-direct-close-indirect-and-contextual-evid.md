## Description
The review organizes its evidence by transferability to Surgical Instrumentation education in Table 6. Direct evidence comes from Surgical Instrumentation students and has "High relevance, low breadth"; close indirect evidence comes from "Medical students, residents, nursing students and surgical trainees" and is moderate in applicability; contextual evidence comes from reviews and implementation studies and is useful for interpreting pedagogy and barriers but not population-specific. The taxonomy's purpose is "to avoid overstating applicability to a professional field with specific intraoperative and technological responsibilities."
