Six-category taxonomy of feedback comparisons in cardiac arrest simulation education¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The review developed six deductive categories for classifying feedback comparisons based on methodological design: "1) human feedback versus a control or as an intervention only, 2) device feedback versus a control or as an intervention only, 3) human versus human feedback, 4) device versus device feedback, 5) human versus device feedback and 6) multiple comparators." This taxonomy organises the diverse feedback literature into comparable groups, enabling thematic synthesis across studies with consistent comparators. The multiple comparator group was excluded from coding due to wide variation in outcomes, which reduced the clarity and usefulness of insights.
