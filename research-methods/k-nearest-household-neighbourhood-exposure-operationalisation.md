# k-nearest-household neighbourhood exposure operationalisation

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A spatial operationalisation used to measure neighbourhood affluence and poverty for each individual and year. Equipop calculates the proportion of the k-nearest neighbours meeting user-set criteria — here, the ratio of poor or affluent households within the 200 nearest households for each year of an individual's life, based on geocoded 100x100m residential locations. Affluence is defined as household income above 150% of the year's median and poverty as below 60%, following the Eurostat at-risk-of-poverty definition. Constraining neighbourhoods to 200 households standardises measures across densely and sparsely populated areas and focuses on people rather than space.
