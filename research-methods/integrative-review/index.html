Description¶

The article itself is an integrative review: it states "This paper presents an integrative review of theory and research on the broad range of educational interventions to counter community violence." Motivated by "alarming rates of community violence and the detrimental impact on youth and communities", the review spans multiple disciplines, critically analyzes distinctions in perspectives, objectives, and strategies, and yields the multidisciplinary taxonomy and the coherent overview of current educational responses that structure the paper's contributions.
