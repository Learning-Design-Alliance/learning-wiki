## Description
Table S1 organizes quantifiable text characteristics by linguistic level: sounds in words (number of phonemes, phonemic Levenshtein distance, internal phonemic predictability), word structure (decoding demand, orthographic Levenshtein distance, syllables, sight words), word meaning (age of acquisition, abstractness, word rareness), within-sentence/syntax (sentence length, grammar link type, distance to verb, link distance), discourse (linear edit distance, word overlap, cohesion triggers, type-token ratio, phrase diversity, edit distance, Cartesian word overlap), text density (information load), and non-compressibility (compression ratio). Each characteristic has a definition, a source, a possible score range with a complexity interpretation, and multiple variable operationalizations. The taxonomy structures which characteristics were tested as predictors of text complexity.
