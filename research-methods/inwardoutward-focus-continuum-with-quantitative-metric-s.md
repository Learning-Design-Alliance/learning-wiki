# Inward/outward focus continuum with quantitative metric s

## Description
The article proposes parameterizing educator motivations for time-intensive PD on a continuum from inward-focused (personal learning goals: new science, skills, like-minded colleagues) to outward-focused (student and other-teacher benefits). Five encoding words (students, teachers, team, colleagues, self) are counted in open-ended survey responses, and a metric s is computed as colleagues-plus-self rates minus students-plus-teachers rates; "The value of s varies between −1 and 1. Values of s < 0 suggest a more outward focus, and s >0 suggests a more inward focus." Team is encoded but excluded from s. The authors offer this as "a new way of parameterizing why educators participate in PD programs that require a considerable investment of time."
