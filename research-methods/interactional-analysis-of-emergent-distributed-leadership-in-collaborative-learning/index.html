Description¶

The editorial argues against folk-theory conceptions that label individuals as leader, follower, lurker or lazy, and against simply assigning a leadership role to one student per group. It states that "roles being conceptualized as group processes, addressing multiple distinct facets of collaboration and being naturally distributed across the group." Leadership is emergent from group interaction and distributed among group members, moving analysis away from the idea that leaders are just born that way.
