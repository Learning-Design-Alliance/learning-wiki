# Six-category taxonomy for characterising GenAI learning activities

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes a taxonomy comprising six categories for characterising GenAI learning activities: Learning Objective (intentionality), Content/GenAI Objects and Processes (what students engage with), Representation Format (media selection), Epistemic Engagement (methodology), Human-GenAI Labour Distribution (role distribution), and Artefacts (material resources). The framework fuses Heimann's German-Scandinavian didactic theory with the ACAD approach to address the postdigital entanglement of humans and technology. As the authors state, "these frameworks complement each other and provide six categories that are important in characterising learning activities across design, presentation and analysis."
