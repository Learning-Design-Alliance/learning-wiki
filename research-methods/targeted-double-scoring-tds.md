# Targeted Double Scoring (TDS)

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)

## Description
TDS is a scoring-assignment methodology in which a first rater scores every performance, and only examinees whose Rater 1 score falls within a Critical Score Range (CSR) near the pass/fail cutoff receive a second rating. The article defines the CSR as "a specific range of scores where the risk of incorrect pass/fail decisions is highest" and selects the optimal CSR by minimizing a statistical-decision-theory loss function combining the probability of a score falling in the CSR with configured costs of false passes (LP), false fails (LF), and extra rating (c). The article positions TDS as a generalizable framework adaptable to different OSCE designs and rubrics rather than as a finding tied to its own dataset.
