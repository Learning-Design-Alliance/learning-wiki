# Hidden Markov Model framework for classifying student enrollment strategies as hidden states

> **Research Method** · [All research methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article introduces a Hidden Markov Model in which the hidden states correspond to three enrollment strategies—full-time enrollment strategy (FES), mixed enrollment strategy (MES), and part-time enrollment strategy (PES)—while the observations are the actualized enrollment in any given semester. The model parameters are estimated using the Baum-Welch algorithm, converging after 20 iterations. The framework distinguishes a student's long-term enrollment strategy from their semester-by-semester enrollment status, addressing the gap that "there is no clear deﬁnition of what it means to be a part-time student or full-time student outside the ephemeral academic designation given each semester."
