# TuRBO (Team Reflection Behavioral Observation) coding framework

## Description
TuRBO is a structured observation framework for in-action team reflection in acute care teams, adapted in this study from Schmutz et al. It categorizes utterances as seeking information (actively inviting input, expressing uncertainty), evaluating information (stating a working hypothesis, recapping, explicitly assessing the situation, reasoning), planning (stating plans and priorities), plus an implementation category the authors added "given the importance of recognition of MH and then also acting to implement appropriate treatment." Two researchers coded six of 22 randomly selected files, resolving discrepancies through social moderation, reaching Cohen's kappa interrater reliability of 0.73 before independently annotating the rest.
