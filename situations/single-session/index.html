Designing for single-session¶

Duration · one sitting · All situations

AI Tutoring¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Short time: one session or a single lesson (from the wording) A research-designed tutor can replace a lesson for an immediate outcome; do not extrapolate to a course AI tutor beat active learning in less time [+M] (one lesson, immediate test)

Always report and interpret effect sizes alongside p values for primary outcomes¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
A small pilot of one course or class, or a single session only (from the wording) Report the effect size, interval and minimum detectable effect; call a non-significant result inconclusive; plan the next test rather than a verdict The evaluation used a school-level cluster-randomized quasi-experiment (N = 50) that was powered only to detect large effects (d = 0.81) [+M] (pre-service teachers; carried to any small pilot)

Assessment for Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The assessment is a one-off entry or placement test (from the wording) Treat it as summative: tell learners beforehand what it covers and what it decides, and gather formative evidence elsewhere; a single sitting offers no revision step Students experience placement as a one-day isolated event with little advance information [-W] (describes experience, no outcome measured)

Building Empathy¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Only one session or a single exercise (from the wording) Use one concrete account, a prompt to take the person's perspective, and a comparison of readings; do not promise lasting attitude change, and re-measure later if you can Building empathy improves intergroup attitudes [~M] (a review of 418 experiments: most test light-touch interventions whose long-term impact is unclear)

Cognitive Flexibility¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Duration single-session Use two or three contrasting cases with a similarities prompt and the principle stated after; test on a new case the same day. If the course allows, revisit the cases later under a new theme. Comparing cases side by side improves learning and transfer by a moderate average amount, though in one algebra study it raised procedural knowledge and flexibility but not conceptual knowledge [+M]: comparison helped in single experiments, more at immediate tests

Coherence Principle¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
A single session, or a fixed short time budget (from the wording) Spend the minutes on the essential explanation and the brief's own check. Do not plan on cutting extras to save time: a combined revision made no reliable difference to time with the lesson Coherence principle [+S]; Revisions made no reliable difference to time [~M]

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Large lecture class, one session at a time (from the wording) Replace part of lecture with questions or problems answered in class that demand the outcome; use some at a higher level than the exam. Active learning [+M] (undergraduate STEM; held across class sizes); HOCS clickers [~M]
Only one session or unit can be changed (from the wording) Audit the whole course, then align the one unit's outcome with an assessed item that exists; do not promise a course-level effect. Untested; from the earlier page ("a single aligned lesson inside a misaligned course changes little")

Principle

Rest untested

Developing Your Cultural Awareness¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
A single session only (one workshop, one in-service hour) (from the wording) Run the scenario step (20–30 minutes), one group comparison of readings (15 minutes), and one written decision change with a named follow-up check (10 minutes); arrange for a peer tally or data review a few weeks later, even by email. Do not claim lasting change from the session Brief empathic-discipline exercise reduced suspensions [~S] (one targeted belief about discipline, middle-school mathematics teachers; carried to other decisions, untested); otherwise untested

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
A single session only (cover teacher, a one-off workshop, a guest) (from the wording) Do not build a plan; state three expectations at the start, greet each learner, use the host's existing routines where you know them, redirect with the least intrusive move, and leave a short note on what happened for the regular teacher Greetings at the door [+M], carried to a single session it did not test; the rest untested

Elaborative Reasoning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
A fixed short time budget, or a single session only (from the wording) Elaborate only the five to ten statements that matter most; plan about 20 minutes for 10 prompts with one modelled answer; do not claim retention from one session Elaborative interrogation increased perceived difficulty and time spent with the lesson [~M]; minutes are an untested proposal

Epistemic Cognition¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Only one session (e.g. a library visit) (from the wording) Teach one move (leave the page and search what others say about the source) rather than a long checklist, and expect modest gains; schedule a follow-up task if at all possible. Checklist-based evaluation [~M]: one-session checklist and networked framework did not differ; gains modest

Error Analysis¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
single-session: one lesson or less Use one comparison set per target misconception with explanation prompts and a short immediate check; do not expect the delayed effect to be shown. erroneous examples [+M] (brief session, immediate outcome)

Evaluating Sources¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Only one session (a library visit, an orientation) (from the wording) Teach one move (leave the page and search the source's name) and one practice round rather than a long checklist; tell the course teacher what to follow up One-session checklist (CRAAP) instruction did no better than the alternatives it was compared with [~M] (first-year business and nursing students; one session of either method, modest gains, no difference)

Formative Assessment¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
A large lecture with a polling system, checking several times within one session (from the wording) Interrupt the lecture with multiple-choice questions, show the class's response distribution, and follow it at once with a related question or a discussion; decide the number of checks locally and record what changed after each Wireless keypads convert a passive audience to active learners [+W] (the authors' account of their own lectures, no comparison); the frequency is an untested proposal

Functional Behavior Assessment¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Only one session (a cover teacher, a one-off workshop) (from the wording) Do not attempt an assessment; use antecedent defaults: greet each learner by name at the door, give instructions one step at a time, show a way to ask for help or take a short break, and pass a note on what happened to the regular teacher Greetings at the door [+M] (middle-school classes, carried to a single session it did not test); the rest untested

Game-based Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time is a single-session Use one short game round, a worked example, a second round and a debrief; do not expect a gain over non-game teaching; if days-weeks are available, spread play over several sessions rather than lengthening one Sessions [+M] (single-session games g = 0.08, not different from non-game conditions; total minutes unrelated to effect); the single-session plan itself is an untested proposal

Modeling¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time is one session only (from the wording) Model one item fully with narration, then a partial model the learners finish, then one unaided item; record what the unaided item shows and do not claim retention Example-based sequences outperform problem-only practice for novices, and fading support is argued to aid transfer [+M] (example-first beat problem-first in one short troubleshooting task, immediate test)

Physical Activity¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
A single session only (from the wording) One bout of about 5 minutes vigorous (or a 10-minute active segment) at the point the group first drifts, with the next task named; observe on-task behavior in the 10 minutes after; claim nothing about learning Dose: Classroom physical activity… [+S] (children). Single-session framing: untested

Practice with Simulated People¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time is short (one session) (from the wording) Run at least one baseline encounter, feedback and debrief, and a repeat; tell learners the gain on the repeat will be larger than on a new scenario Large first-to-second run-through improvement [+M]; repetition did not transfer to a new task [~M]

Pretesting (Prequestions Before Study)¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
A single session (from the wording) Pretest, study, done; expect the effect on a later test of the same content, not a lasting course effect Pre-instruction testing enhances memory for directly tested text content [+M] (immediate and one-week posttests)

Professional Development¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Only a single session is available (an inset day, a one-off workshop) (from the wording) Use it to model and rehearse one narrow practice and to set up a follow-up the school can run without the presenter: one peer observation with a look-for list within two weeks and one team meeting to review the clips. Do not promise changed practice from the session alone. Half of inservice activities lasted four hours or less, mostly talks or brief workshops [~W] (prevalence, not effect); short, fragmented training is reported to hinder a holistic view [-W]; the follow-up is untested

Redundancy Principle¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
A single session (from the wording) Apply steps 2–5 of the default; skip the across-session withdrawal of text Redundancy principle (Mayer 2008) [+M]; the rest is the default above

Reflection¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time available: one session (single-session) or a term-plus course One session: one reflection of three prompts, about five minutes, at the end, ending in one change to try; no portfolio. A term: reflect after each comparable performance, keep the same criterion, and compare across attempts at least once. single session untested, from the earlier page; intervention duration is a moderator in reflection interventions [~M], direction not stated on the claim page

Segmenting Principle¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
A single session only (from the wording) Segment within the session and budget extra minutes for the segmented version Segmentation increases learning time [~M]

Self Affirmation¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
A single session only (one workshop, one exam day) (from the wording) Place it immediately before the evaluation in that session (10 minutes), and claim no lasting effect self-affirmation claim [~S] and Brief expressive writing before an exam can raise test-anxious students' scores, but across the wider literature its effect on test performance is negligible [~S]: one-off writing exercises have small and fragile effects on performance

Social Emotional Learning¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
A single session, assembly or workshop only (from the wording) Do not promise durable change. Teach one skill and leave a routine (a check-in card, a breathing step) the teacher will run daily; or spend the session on the adults' discipline mindset instead. Empathy-building claim [~M]: most prejudice-reduction studies test light-touch interventions with unclear long-term effects; empathic discipline [+M]: a brief teacher exercise reduced suspensions for high-risk groups, with a marginal overall effect in the preregistered replication

Spaced Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
A single workshop or one-off session (from the wording) Add at least one later review: short spaced quizzes after the session, spread over days and weeks rather than given together. A credit workshop followed by spaced quizzes retained more at about five months than massed quizzes or none, and massed quizzes did not differ detectably from none Spaced retrieval after a financial workshop improved retention at five months [+M]; Spaced beat massed retrieval and no practice [+M]

Strengths-based Approach¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time is one session only (from the wording) Give one low-stakes task, then one piece of specific feedback naming what worked and a next target; do not run a mindset or encouragement talk in place of it, and claim nothing about lasting change Growth-mindset interventions have weak average effects on achievement, including a negligible GPA gain (d = 0.11) for lower-achieving US ninth-graders in one preregistered national trial [~S] (a brief online intervention moved ninth graders' GPA by a negligible amount; meta-analyses weak overall); carried from mindset messages to strengths framing

Transfer of Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time is one session only (from the wording) Teach two cases side by side, prompt the comparison, state the principle, and end with one near-transfer item; do not claim far or delayed transfer Analogical Reasoning Improves Transfer [+S] (immediate tests favoured in the meta-analysis)

Wise Feedback Across Racial and Ethnic Difference¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
A single session only (one workshop, one assessed piece, a one-off review) (from the wording) Consistency cannot build; make the assurance concrete by pointing to evidence in the work, and give one revision in the session; claim no lasting effect A brief values-affirmation writing exercise raised African American seventh-graders' grades in two small field experiments, but a large same-district replication found no effect [~M] and Growth-mindset interventions have weak average effects on achievement, including a negligible GPA gain (d = 0.11) for lower-achieving US ninth-graders in one preregistered national trial [~M]: one-off identity-targeted messages are fragile
