Designing for online-self-paced¶

Setting · digital, learner-paced, no live instructor · All situations

152 rows from 146 pages say what to change when the brief's setting is online-self-paced; 99 of them cite a claim. Pages whose rows cite claims come first. Read the basis before relying on a row: many carry a claim to learners or settings it did not test, and say so.

Blended Learning¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The online part is online-self-paced and learners have little experience of it Add planning prompts, a visible schedule with intermediate deadlines and self-monitoring checks; review them at the next in-person session. SRL interventions in online and blended environments [+M], pooled across 15 studies, populations not reported on the claim page
No teacher present between sessions, or a large group (from the wording) Monitor completion each week, contact non-starters within the first week or two, and keep a named person learners can ask. Online continuing-education persistence [~W], fully online adult continuing education, associational; untested for blends

Case-Based Learning¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners work alone (online-self-paced) Put both cases on one screen with a written prompt to describe their similarities Comparing cases side by side improves learning and transfer [+M] (undergraduates comparing two cases on one page)
Learners work through cases self-paced online (online-self-paced), with a mentor available on demand Put the cases side by side with a written comparison prompt; require a short individual written analysis of each new case; have the mentor read a sample and return the principle statement with feedback; add an asynchronous peer exchange on each case; recap the principle in writing; use cases from the learners' own workplaces where possible Comparing cases side by side improves learning and transfer [+M]; Co-regulation shows stronger relationships with the three CoI presences than self-regulation in an online case-based course [~W]; Some Chinese students felt uncomfortable with case-based learning due to lack of structured instruction content [~W]; mentor role from Mentor Support in Self-Paced Online Learning, untested for case work

Competency-Based Learning¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners will set their own pace (online-self-paced, adult or college) Keep checkpoints or deadlines and monitor who stalls; self-paced mastery courses lost more students, and learner control in general showed almost no effect Self-paced mastery programmes reduce course completion [-M]; Learner pacing helped on complex material only [~M]
Online, asynchronous, self-paced, with mentors available (online-self-paced) Have the mentor open with a planning contact in which the learner sets milestones per competency; trigger mentor outreach on a missed milestone or repeated failed attempts, not on calendar dates; plan coaching across terms and measure persistence each term. Follow mentor support in self-paced online learning Coaching raised 12-month retention [+M] but not 24-month degree completion [~M]; planning prompts raised online completion [+M]; commitment devices are mixed [~M]; none tested inside a competency-based programme

Mentor Support in Self-Paced Online Learning¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners new to online or self-paced study, or in their first term (from the wording) Put the planning contact and a personal reply in the first week, before any content, and the first scheduled check-in before the first milestone; consider a brief belonging message in the first contact Orientation-week attrition online is twice that of on-campus first weeks [~M]; after instruction starts, online and on-campus drop rates are about the same [~M]; Planning prompts raised MOOC completion [+M]; brief belonging interventions improved first-year college outcomes [+M] (college entry, not self-paced courses)
Online, self-paced, asynchronous, no class meetings (from the wording) Use a consistent course layout with goals, due dates and feedback turnaround stated, and schedule personal messages rather than waiting for learners to ask; do not count the layout as facilitation Taking a course online reduced college success compared with in person [-M]; A standard template heightened teaching presence in accelerated online courses [+W]; organization rated high while facilitation rated lower [~M]; Personal messages had the largest association with satisfaction [+M]

Simulations & Immersive Virtual Environments¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners study online-self-paced with no lab access, and the goal is conceptual knowledge or inquiry skill Use a guided virtual lab in place of the bench: a goal and data table for each run, a required experiment plan before running, prompts during it, an explain-with-the-simulation-off task after; a mentor or instructor reads each write-up and answers within a stated time; schedule a delayed conceptual check Physical Experience Enhances Science Learning [~M] (physical = virtual for concepts, one undergraduate study); Guidance during inquiry learning improves post-inquiry learning outcomes [+M]; Students perceived online lab exercises as more effective when instructors were present online and used scaffolds and probes [+W]; immediate-only gains (AI-generated and prebuilt simulations … advantage did not persist [~M]); mentor role from Mentor Support in Self-Paced Online Learning, untested for labs
Self-paced online, no lab access, and the competency includes a manipulative or bench skill (from the wording) Do not credit the skill from the virtual lab; use the virtual lab to prepare (parts, steps, predictions) and add a hands-on component (a local or campus lab, a residency, a home kit) assessed on real equipment One RCT found AI-assisted simulation inferior to standardized patient simulation for breast self-examination skills [-M] (carried from a tactile nursing skill); Pretraining improves transfer [+M]; no claim in this wiki compares a virtual and a bench science lab on manipulative skill

AI Tutoring¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Online self-paced or asynchronous, no instructor watching (from the wording) Answer-withholding by default, a hint ladder, logged reliance signals, and a named human handoff with a response time; add reflection prompts before accepting the tutor's answer Reflection prompt cut acceptance of wrong advice [+M]; Human-AI tutoring raised MAP growth over AI alone [+M]

Analogical Reasoning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners work alone (online-self-paced) Show both cases on one screen and ask for a written description of their similarities; reading the same cases separately gave far less transfer Comparing cases side by side improves learning and transfer [+M]

Anchored Instruction¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The anchor is a simulation or video that learners work through alone (online-self-paced) Build prompts and feedback into the environment instead of leaving the anchor open Guidance added to simulation inquiry modestly improves outcomes [+W]

Authentic Assessment¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher present Give the rubric and scored exemplars up front; have learners score an exemplar and see the expert's score before self-assessing; use structured peer review in a timed window; reserve teacher or practitioner judgement for the final product. Peer assessment [+M] (robust to online or offline delivery in the meta-analysis); self-assessment training [~S]; carried to authentic tasks untested

Autonomy¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
An open, self-directed course with little structure (online-self-paced, MOOC-style) Keep the choice of topic or project, but supply a plan, milestones and a few vetted options; teach planning as in Self-Regulated Learning Not all students in connectivist courses could direct their own learning [~M]

Check-ins¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, with no instructor Turn the check-in into a self-monitoring prompt against a stated goal, with a suggested next step for each answer Self-monitoring improves self-regulation [+W]

Chunking¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Recorded lectures for online-self-paced study Split long recordings into several shorter videos, each on one idea Long recorded lectures impeded focus [+W]; Segmenting improves multimedia learning [+M]

Cognitive Activation¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher present Put the prompt in the material with a required typed answer before the model explanation is revealed; the learner then compares the two. Self-explanation prompts improve learning from worked examples [+M] (prompting is tested; the reveal-after-commit step is untested)

Cognitive Flexibility¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting online-self-paced or hypertext Give a guided route through the cases (a theme per pass) rather than free browsing, and build the comparison prompts into the pages. A flexible hypertext course group averaged 76% on achievement tests against 59% for a direct-design group that studied different software, so the contrast confounds design with content [~W]: groups studied different software, so it does not isolate the design; navigation burden is untested, from the theory page

Coherence Principle¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced pages, learner-paced Still cut, but expect a smaller effect; give learners a clear path through essential content so optional extras can be skipped Design principle effects moderated by pacing and complexity [~M]; Hypermedia usability problems [~M] (distraction and overload in learner-controlled hypermedia)

Collaborative Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The setting is online-self-paced or asynchronous online groups Plan the interaction explicitly (shared task, deadlines for responding to each other); do not expect a role or reward scheme alone to raise achievement Structuring interdependence did not change achievement online [~M]; Students rated collaboration worse online [~W]

Combine intentional and incidental vocabulary learning rather than relying on incidental learning alone¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting is online-self-paced, phone-only, or no teacher present Put graded readings or audio with tap-to-see glosses in the app; schedule spaced recall of words the learner taps or saves; after each reading ask for one typed or spoken use of a saved word Listening to vocabulary lessons while physically active… [+M] (ESL students, phone audio added to class teaching); Supplemental computer-based spaced repetition… [+M] (EFL cadets, alongside classroom teaching, not instead of it); in-app glossing carried from Marginal glosses, dictionary use, and repeated occurrence… [+M] (printed text)

Compensate for age-related sensory impairments by combining audio input with visual presentation¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced recordings Give captions or a transcript the learner can switch on, plus pause and replay; no need to force both channels at once System-paced but not learner-paced advantage [~M]; modality effect absent when self-paced [~M]

Constructive Alignment¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting is online-self-paced with no teacher present Build practice with immediate feedback into each module ahead of its assessed task; make criteria and exemplars part of the module; audit automatically scored items for recall-only questions. Practice presence [+M] (computer-based lesson, undergraduates); the audit is untested

Contiguity Principle (Spatial and Temporal)¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Self-paced online course (online-self-paced), learners can pause and scroll Integrate anyway; expect a smaller gain than in system-paced media Design principles matter less when learner-paced [~M] (g = 0.27 vs 0.41)

Debriefing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no facilitator Replace the live debrief with a written self-debrief: three to five specific questions tied to the criteria, then a model performance or expert commentary to compare with, then one change for the next module, shown back at its start Reflective Practice Improves Outcomes When Structured [+M] (classroom engineering course); Metacognitive prompts improve learning [+M] (computer-based environments)

Deliberate Practice¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting is online-self-paced, no coach present Give items with automatic checking or a worked answer to compare; have the learner score against annotated exemplars and keep an error log; require one whole-task submission per session reviewed by a person or a rubric-based check Self-regulated learning models treat self-monitoring as a core process through which learners notice gaps and adjust strategy [+M] (reviews; untested within deliberate practice or online)

Develop Understanding¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher Make every prompt require a typed explanation before the next screen; give worked explanations to compare against; include automatically checked prediction items with feedback; end with an unseen changed item. Practice present in a computer-based lesson [+M] (undergraduates, one lesson, horizon not reported); the explanation-comparison step is untested

Digital Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting is online-self-paced, no teacher present Add the contact task in week 1, a fixed weekly rhythm with a deadline, visible progress against a criterion, and a weekly plan prompt; check logins by day 3 Self-regulated learning interventions have a moderate effect (0.65) on learning outcomes in online and blended environments [+M] (15 studies); a diary alone did not help in one course (A daily learning diary alone (Group D) did not produce statistically significant pre-post gains on any measured outcome in an online mathematics preparation course [~M])

Direct Instruction¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, with no teacher to respond in the moment Use worked examples as the model and build feedback into the guided items; worked examples beat unassisted discovery by more than direct teaching did Worked examples' advantage over unassisted discovery [+M]

Dual Coding¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher present Build a model drawing to compare with and a self-check list into the material; offer a drawing template as optional, and if the platform adds drawing aids (highlighting where to draw, prompts per part), compare them with plain drawing before keeping them Drawing in STEM [+M] (online drawing benefited more from instructor-provided scaffolds than paper-based drawing); against: in one tablet experiment the two supports tested did not beat unsupported drawing, and local support lowered recognition [-M] and raised rated difficulty [-M]

Educators should act as mediators and facilitators of learning rather than content disseminators¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced or web-based course, teacher not present in real time Provide the task, tools and criteria up front; mediate through written questions on submitted work and scheduled feedback rather than live circulation; in a discussion forum, post as a participant and tutor at the edges rather than answer every thread, and post less as groups begin to run the discussion themselves E-teachers reported shifting toward a facilitator role online [+M] (one teacher's interview; describes the shift, does not test its effect); both teachers remained peripherally active and concentrated their contributions on tutoring [+W] (one networked learning community, content and network analysis, no comparison)

Elaboration Theory¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher present Keep the epitome as a persistent map on every page, with the current level highlighted; make each synthesis an activity the system checks (arrange items into the map, mark the new detail), not a passage to read. Learners explicating the organisation [+M] for the synthesis activity (same 20-student high-school experiment; untested online); the persistent map is an untested proposal

Employ flexible error treatment strategies matched to objectives, competence, affect, and correction effectiveness¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Online self-paced course or app (from the wording) Keep practice items and feedback in the lesson rather than cutting them to shorten it; use the correct form with a rule line for novice learners Learners' attitudes were most negative toward the version of a computer-based lesson stripped of practice and other events (lean version most negative [~M], undergraduates, a computer-literacy lesson, attitudes not learning); carried to language apps

Error Analysis¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher present The system must supply the correct solution and the explanation; log error streaks and self-reported confusion, and switch to a worked example after repeated errors on one item type. confusion with prior incorrect trials [~W] (exploratory); the switch rule is an untested proposal

Expanding Social Networks¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting is online-self-paced or learners study alone at home Add a peer group of learners in the same situation with set participation norms (one weekly post, one reply) rather than relying on the tutor relationship alone The student-tutor relationship alone is judged insufficient: peer mixing and virtual communities are needed to counter isolation in online research supervision [+M] (doctoral supervision, consultation study); SPICE learners report that the virtual Center reduced their rural isolation and made learning enjoyable [+W] (family literacy, testimonials)

Experience with Languaging Activities (ELAs) guided by a sequence of experience-based questions¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher Embed the four prompts after each segment; after the learner commits a step 4 answer, show a model answer and ask them to mark what they missed; adapt the stem to the content of the segment. Metacognitive prompts [+S]: in computer-based environments prompts helped, more so with feedback, task-specific and adaptive (meta-analysis read from its abstract)

Experiential Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no facilitator present Replace the live debrief with specific written prompts after each task and a model answer to compare against; let learners retry a changed task with automatic feedback Reflective Practice Improves Outcomes When Structured [+M] for specific written prompts (engineering undergraduates, in a classroom course); untested online

Experiential Learning Cycle¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no facilitator Replace the live debrief with three written prompts after each task and a model answer to compare against; offer an automatically scored changed task as the retry. Structured reflection [+M] for written specific prompts (a classroom engineering course); untested online

Explicit Instruction: Computer Basics¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
No teacher: a self-paced video or online course (from the wording) Show one routine per short video segment, then a practice task on the learner's own device with a check the learner can see (did the email arrive in the test inbox?); include a practice task after every demonstration, never demonstration only Practice: In one experiment with 256 undergraduates, computer-based lesson versions that included practice produced significantly higher posttest scores than versions without practice [+M] (undergraduates in a computer-literacy course, computer-based lesson). Segment length: untested

Explicit Instruction: Math Strategies¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, phone-only, or no teacher present Put the reason beside every worked step; use completion problems that leave the last steps to the learner; check each answer automatically and show the worked solution; give a one-screen plan card the learner can open Fading support promotes the transfer of responsibility from instructor to learner. [+S] (worked-example fading, carried to self-paced mathematics); Sixth graders scored significantly higher on posttests that included the seven-step problem-solving plan than on unformatted posttests [+M] (a plan printed with the problems)

Explicit Instruction: Online Reading Strategies¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting is online-self-paced, no teacher present Build the supports into the course: a goal box that must be filled before links open, a notes panel beside the reading, a prompt at each page change ("does this page answer your question?"), and a "review your notes and revise your summary" step before the answer is submitted Metacognitive prompts improve learning [+S] (computer-based environments); review-notes compliance [+M] (one study, associational)

Explicit Instruction: Vocabulary¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting is online-self-paced, phone-only, or no teacher present Build each word as a short card (meaning, example, picture, audio) with recall prompts scheduled by the app over two to three weeks; put the words into short readings or audio inside the course; ask for one typed or spoken use per word Supplemental computer-based spaced repetition… [+M] (EFL cadets, alongside classroom teaching, not instead of it)

Feedback Loops¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher present Build automatically checked items with a retry on a new item after each piece of information; route a learner who fails twice to a worked example; keep a delayed recheck in a later session Intelligent tutoring systems improve achievement over non-individualised instruction [+S] (intelligent tutoring beat large-group, textbook and other computer instruction, not human tutoring or small groups; gains were smaller on standardized than on locally developed tests)

Formative Assessment¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, with no teacher reading responses Build the checks into the materials with automatic feedback, and give learners a next action for each result Formative-assessment-based learning analytics show positive effects [+W]

Fostering Creative Thinking¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Generative AI or an automated writing-feedback tool is available, or the setting is online-self-paced Require a first list of the learner's own ideas (or a first draft) before the tool is opened; then use the tool to extend or challenge that list; keep the making of the prototype or product with the learner; where variety across the class is the point, look at the spread of the whole class's ideas, not only each learner's best; judge readiness on a later task done without the tool Think first, ChatGPT later group more original on the later unassisted task [+M]; Free ChatGPT use gains vanish when assistance is removed [+M] (university students, one experiment); LLM access boosts individual story creativity while homogenising output across writers [~W] (reported second-hand from one study of writers); Prototype generation is the only GenAI activity associated with perceived loss of creativity or ownership [~W] (design students' survey, self-report; brainstorming with AI was not associated with it); Overreliance on AWE may lower learners' critical thinking and creativity [-W] (a theme in a review, no statistic)

Game-Based Mastery Learning¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher The software runs feedback, retries, checks and review; a weekly lesson target and a soft deadline per level; a streak that survives one missed day Self-paced mastery reduces completion [-M] (college PSI courses, carried to apps); level prompts [+M] (practice volume only)

Goal Setting & Monitoring¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher present Build goal setting and an if-then plan into the first session (a short writing activity); show progress against the goal on every return; do not expect the prompt to help every learner Implementation intentions [~M] (course completion rose for learners in individualist cultures, not collectivist)

Graphic Organizers¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting is online-self-paced, no teacher present Place the organizer next to the text it summarises (not on a separate page); give partial organizers with a reference version revealed after the learner submits; add one explanation question per organizer Adding relevant pictures or graphics to text improves learning from it, with a small pooled effect on reading comprehension, though whether dual coding explains the benefit is disputed [+M] (graphics added to text helped reading comprehension, elementary to adult; carried to organizers, which it did not isolate); the reveal-and-compare step is untested

Guided Practice¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting is online-self-paced, no teacher present Build practice items with automatic checking and feedback after each item into the lesson; do not cut them to save time; add an unaided check item before the next topic In one experiment with 256 undergraduates, computer-based lesson versions that included practice produced significantly higher posttest scores than versions without practice [+M] (undergraduates, one computer-based lesson; horizon not reported)

Handouts/Online Guides/Visual Reading Aids¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting is online-self-paced, or mixed (some learners remote, some in the room, some on phones) Give one single-column online guide that reads on a phone: section headings that match the reading, short prompts, fillable blanks or a form; the same file for everyone, so remote and in-room learners work from one version; ask remote learners to submit one completed section Students in courses with guided note-taking and other active learning activities reported less difficulty with motivation and engagement after the shift online [+W] (focus groups in 28 STEM courses; self-report). Phone format and mixed mode: untested

Holistic Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher present Keep the map on every screen with today's part highlighted; make the closing placement an arrange-or-label activity the system checks; add a redraw-from-memory item from the middle of the course Learner-built organisation [+M] (classroom study, untested online); concept mapping [~S] for the redraw; the persistent map is untested

Inquiry-based Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The inquiry runs in a simulation or online (online-self-paced) Build the guidance into the environment (prompts, annotations, feedback); expect near transfer, and do not expect far transfer from one unit Guidance added to simulation inquiry modestly improves outcomes and inquiry skills [+M]; PAIR-C scaffolding: near-transfer gain, no far transfer [~W]

Interleaving¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners choose their own practice order (online-self-paced, homework, revision) Build the mixing into the materials or the software; do not rely on learners to choose it, since learners in two studies judged blocking more effective Interleaving improves discrimination [+S] (Kornell & Bjork: most learners believed blocking had served them better); Learners judge blocking more effective even when their test performance favours interleaving [~M] (about two thirds of 114 undergraduates); the same misjudgement for spacing is on Spaced Learning

Journaling¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher present Put the prompts in the platform at the end of each module, tied to that module; require a "next step" line; show the learner their last "next step" at the start of the next module; pair the diary with short self-regulation training. training with diary [~M] and diary alone [-M] (prospective university students, online mathematics preparation; no mathematics gain either way)

Keep the learner in control of the information flow with on-demand tools and supports¶

Structured Academic Controversy¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting online-self-paced, or no teacher present Run it asynchronously over days-weeks: positions posted, restatements as replies that the original author must accept, reversal posts, then a joint document. Grade the restatement and the individual position, not post counts. A shared misconception and a caricatured reversal will go unseen, so add a facilitator check before the synthesis. untested proposal
