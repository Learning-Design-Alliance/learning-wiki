# Eight constant forms of data collected within the drawing laboratory

> **Design Method** · [All design methods](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The methodology relies on eight kinds of data, all from events and traces within the drawing lab: dated drawings, time-lapse negatives, recalled lab notes, direct observational descriptions, transcriptions of inquiry sessions, transcriptions of verbalizations while drawing, faithful negatives of completed drawings, and prints of process negatives. The report notes that "The forms of data are relatively simple and constant from one artist to the next and from one time to another." This constant data structure supports comparison across cases.
