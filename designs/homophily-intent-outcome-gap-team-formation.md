# Behavioural gap between equity intent and outcome: homophily in self-selected team formation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article proposes that even in classrooms where instructors and students value equity, behavioural tendencies lead students to form teams with peers of similar socio-economic backgrounds, which it links to "the well-documented behavioural tendency in which individuals, often without conscious intent, associate with others who share similar socio-economic background". This gap between intent and outcome motivates structural supports — quantified equity scoring and balanced partitioning — to translate equity goals into practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
