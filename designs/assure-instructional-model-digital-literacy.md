# ASSURE instructional design model for face-to-face and blended older-adult instruction

## Description
The ASSURE model is the instructional design framework the authors used to structure both workshop modalities. It presents "6 phases: Analyze learners, State objectives, Select instructional methods and materials, Utilize materials, Require learner response, and Evaluation and revision." The authors chose it because it "has the necessary characteristics to implement face-to-face and blended instruction" and can adapt course or lesson design systematically. They credit it with producing adequate structure, attractive text colors and images, quality materials, and clear instructions attentive to older adults' visual, cognitive and emotional characteristics.
