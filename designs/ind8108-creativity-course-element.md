# IND8108 'Creativity in applied sciences and engineering' course with ten pedagogical strategies

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 45-hour elective course at École Polytechnique de Montréal offered to graduate and undergraduate engineering students, incorporating "ten pedagogical strategies, including serious games, an observation book, individual and group projects". In-class activities include warm-up exercises, invited-speaker conferences, lectures/discussions, and small-group presentations of creativity approaches; projects include an individual artistic or personal project, a group engineering project, and a group 'MouseTrap' Rube Goldberg machine project; personal work includes a log book kept across the semester, a forum for sharing creative behaviors, and guided discussion of four scientific texts.
