# IG-IBD Master program three-phase competency-based IUS training curriculum

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (7 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
A standardized three-phase competency-based training curriculum implemented across eight Italian tertiary IBD centers. It comprises an intensive 8-h theoretical module covering "principles of IUS" including bowel wall anatomy, probe technical principles, vascular Doppler parameters, and case-based image interpretation; one week of supervised practical training with 30 supervised scans under expert sonographers; and an independent assessment phase of 99 examinations with blinded expert reassessment. Each trainee was paired with a single fixed expert throughout to ensure consistency in evaluation and feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Expert sonographers with >2000 IUS examinations and a pre-study calibration session to standardize assessment criteria
- Trainees paired with a single fixed expert throughout the study period
#### Constraints
- Designed for trainees with substantial general abdominal ultrasound experience (>200 examinations) but limited IUS exposure (<20 examinations)

### Target Learners
- Gastroenterology trainees with prior abdominal ultrasound experience

### Learning Goals
- Intestinal ultrasound competency for IBD assessment covering basic and advanced parameters
