# Taxonomy of case-study types by who performs the case analysis, with case styles matched to instructional format

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article organizes case-study teaching by who does the case analysis: "individual assignments, lecture, discussion, or small group activities". Case styles are then matched to formats, e.g. public hearing, trial, or Socratic cases fit discussion; journal article, problem-based learning, debate, role playing, or team learning cases fit small groups; storytelling cases fit lecture. The taxonomy is attributed to Herreid (1998) and extended with experimental and numerical-modeling case styles for physics.
