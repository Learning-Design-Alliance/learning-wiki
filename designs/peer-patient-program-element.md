# Peer Patient: a blended peer simulation education program for patient role portrayal

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Peer Patient is a curriculum-embedded peer simulation program developed in 2016–17 "informed by standards of best practice" and guidelines for successful SP programs. It comprises two online modules (6 h and 2 h) covering general portrayal skills and a specific patient role, plus three 3-hour classroom sessions in which student triads rotate through peer-therapist, peer-observer and peer-patient roles across preparation, simulation and SHARP-informed debriefing hours. Three patient roles were developed spanning acute, ambulatory and rehabilitation care.
