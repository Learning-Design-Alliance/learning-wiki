# ORaClES online training resource for recognising dying

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A simple, non-interactive online training resource containing mainly text and diagrams, built from a previous judgement analysis in which expert palliative care doctors weighted prognostic signs and symptoms. As the article states, "The training resource for the intervention group was developed from a group of expert palliative care doctors' weightings of various signs/symptoms to recognise dying." Participants in the intervention arm could re-access it while completing post-intervention vignettes, and spent a median 2.65 min (IQR 1.90; 3.90) reviewing it.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Built from experts' weightings of prognostic signs/symptoms derived from a prior judgement analysis of patient vignettes
#### Constraints
- Very simple, non-interactive, containing mainly text and diagrams; vignettes used in the trial were artificial and simple in format relative to real patients

### Target Learners
- Senior (penultimate or final year) UK medical students

### Learning Goals
- Recognising when a person is dying and estimating probability of death within 72 hours
- Weighting prognostic information similarly to expert palliative care doctors
