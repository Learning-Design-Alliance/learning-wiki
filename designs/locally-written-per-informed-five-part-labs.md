# Locally written PER-informed labs with a five-part structure (Objectives, Overview, Explorations, Investigations, Summary)

## Description
The department developed locally written PER-informed laboratories to replace traditional cookbook labs in the introductory courses. The article states "The locally written PER-informed labs consisted of five parts: Objectives, Overview, Explorations, Investigations and Summary." Explorations use qualitative measurements or thought experiments targeting persistent conceptual difficulties, while Investigations involve quantitative data taking, graphing and interpretation. Labs required no formal write-up but included laboratory homework, and were designed to require interactive engagement during the lab.

## Design Implications

#### Requirements

- The article notes the Investigation section is the part more like a traditional lab
