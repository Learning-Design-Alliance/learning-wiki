# Coordinated course sequence with shared energy vocabulary, representations, and constructivist pedagogy

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's institutional setting is a set of coordinated science content courses for preservice elementary and middle school teachers, one each in physics, geology, biology, and chemistry. The curricula "use similar vocabulary, representations, and modeling tools surrounding energy concepts, and employ a common, constructivist-based pedagogy." Students develop and use energy diagrams throughout the series, giving instructors opportunities to see whether students apply foundational energy concepts in new disciplinary contexts. This coherence made cross-disciplinary learning more visible than in traditional science courses.
