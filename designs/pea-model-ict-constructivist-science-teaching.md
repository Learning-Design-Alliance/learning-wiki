## Description
The PEA model is a triple learning model the study designed based on ICT and constructive learning strategy for teaching science. It depends on three requirements: "Using ICT in a way consistent with the tendencies of the Students and their attitudes", "Constructing educational content based on the constructivist theory of learning", and "Providing a social learning environment". The article presents it as a model to help teachers give students an advanced level of scientific knowledge, and expects it to include teacher and faculty training.
