# Subacromial injection simulation curriculum: lecture, video, and mentored cadaver practice with dyed-injection dissection assessment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A procedural training curriculum in which all participants receive a twenty-minute lecture and a four-minute technique video, after which the intervention group receives "an additional ten minutes of mentored guidance to practice the SAI on a cadaver" under an orthopedic surgery resident guided by a sports medicine specialist. Skill is tested on a cadaveric shoulder with colored latex dye, scored with a 1–25 rubric, and accuracy verified by blinded cadaver dissection one week later.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cadaveric shoulder specimens and orthopedic faculty/resident mentors proficient in injections, with an educator to learner ratio of 1:3
#### Constraints
- The authors note cadaver specimens are often expensive for an institution to purchase and store, and results may differ if performed in another joint than the small subacromial space

### Target Learners
- Internal Medicine and Family Medicine residents and attending physicians

### Learning Goals
- Accurate and sterile subacromial injection technique
