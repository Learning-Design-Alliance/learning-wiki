# Visitor center interpretation built on the intersection of resource stories, visitor interests, and institutional mission

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The planned VLA/ngVLA Visitor Center upgrade uses "modern interpretive methods to explore the intersection of three realms: The stories arising from the resource, visitors' intrinsic interests, and the mission and goals of NRAO." Interpretive goals include making information understandable and relevant for non-scientists without over-simplification, actively including people of diverse backgrounds and cultural voices, and helping visitors feel the program is useful, relevant, and worth supporting. An overarching theme frames the ngVLA as serving humanity's deep curiosity and drive to explore the universe.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Exhibit content that connects resource stories, visitor intrinsic interests, and institutional mission goals
#### Constraints
- Scientific messages must avoid over-simplification to the point of introducing inaccuracies

### Target Learners
- general public visitors
- K-12 students
- educators
- local community residents

### Learning Goals
- basic understanding of radio astronomy and the ngVLA
- appreciation of diverse cultural voices in science
- sense of relevance and support for scientific facilities
