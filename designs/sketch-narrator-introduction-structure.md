# Sketch-based play structure with narrator introductions framing each scene

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The play is organized as a sequence of short sketches, each opened by a narrator who frames the scene before the student-actors perform it, and closed with the actors leaving the stage so attention returns to the narrator. As the article states, "No início de todos os esquetes o narrador faz uma pequena introdução sobre o tema a ser trabalhado pelos estudantes -atores para situar a audiência cronologicamente sobre alguns acontecimentos". The narrator also presents curiosities, the problems and challenges of the period, and provocative questions to awaken and maintain the audience's interest, and the sketch division prevents overloading the audience with excess information.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A narrator role to open and close each sketch
- Division of content into short scenes with few actors each
#### Constraints
- Sketches were designed so concepts are transmitted in an objective and simpler way, avoiding excess information for the audience

### Target Learners
- high-school students
- general public

### Learning Goals
- understanding astronomical concepts and phenomena
- maintaining audience interest in science
