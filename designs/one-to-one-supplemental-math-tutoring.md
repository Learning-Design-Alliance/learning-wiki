# Deliver supplemental math tutoring one-to-one outside ordinary math periods, staffed by teachers or trained paraprofessionals

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Pirate Math delivers tutoring one-to-one, with tutors who may be teachers or paraprofessionals working with students outside of ordinary math periods. This staffing and scheduling model allows struggling third graders to receive intensive individual support without displacing their regular math instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Paraprofessionals or certified teachers as tutors, with training provided through AIR
#### Constraints
- Requires scheduling sessions outside ordinary math periods, three per week over 16 weeks

### Target Learners
- Low-achieving third graders

### Learning Goals
- Math calculations and word problem solving
