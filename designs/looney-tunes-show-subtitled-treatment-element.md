# The Looney Tunes Show animated sitcom treatment with English subtitles

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The main instrument of the study was the English animated sitcom "The Looney Tunes Show", chosen for its popularity and attractiveness to English learners. Twelve episodes were shown during a six-session treatment, played with English subtitles to aid comprehension, with the teacher pausing and replaying each occurrence of a conditional sentence. Shorter segments of each episode were shown rather than full episodes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Played with English subtitles to help learners better understand the content
- Shown in shorter segments to keep focus on the conditional sentences
#### Constraints
- Two students found the characters' fast speech difficult to understand, which the article says motivated the subtitles

### Target Learners
- intermediate EFL learners aged 14-18

### Learning Goals
- learning conditional sentences in context
