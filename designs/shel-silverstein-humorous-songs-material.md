# Shel Silverstein humorous songs as listening-treatment material

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study's treatment material was a set of humorous songs: "the poems written and sung by Shel Silverstein were chosen". Silverstein (1930-1999) was an American poet, singer-songwriter and children's author whose quirky style is described as laid-back and conversational, occasionally employing fancy and jokes. One song, "SICK", is reproduced in Appendix 3 as an example of the material learners heard.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Songs must be humorous and suited to relaxed listening without follow-up questions
#### Constraints
- The authors state the materials should be adapted to non-native learners, in higher quality sound and slower text reading pace

### Target Learners
- intermediate EFL learners aged 15-25

### Learning Goals
- listening comprehension through aural exposure to humorous songs
