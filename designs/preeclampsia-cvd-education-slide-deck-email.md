# Provider education slide deck delivered in person or by email with read-receipt

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention artifact was an educational slide presentation for internal medicine physicians on preeclampsia as a CVD risk factor, released as Additional file 2, plus a copy of the covering email (Additional file 3). The article states that "At clinics where in-person teaching sessions were not possible, the educational slide presentations (Additional file 2) were sent to physicians via email (Additional file 3) with a read-receipt link." The read receipt confirmed providers received the material but could not confirm review of the slides.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A delivery channel that reaches providers; the authors note emailed slide attachments may decrease engagement
#### Constraints
- The read-receipt served only as confirmation of receipt, not of individual participation or review; the intervention produced no significant change in screening documentation

### Target Learners
- Internal medicine primary care physicians at academic-affiliated clinics

### Learning Goals
- Educate providers on the significance of preeclampsia as a risk factor for cardiovascular disease
