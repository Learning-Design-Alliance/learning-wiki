# Quantum Forge: a year-long capstone course partnering student teams with quantum-industry companies on authentic projects

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Quantum Forge (Q-Forge) is an upper-division, two-semester capstone course at CU Boulder in which student teams "partner with a company in the quantum industry to work on an authentic project". The industry partner supplies the project, modest funding, and employee interactions. Weekly class meetings include lectures, skill-development modules, and visits with local quantum companies, alongside roughly three hours of weekly project time. The course's first cohort (2022-2023) worked on optimizing heat exchangers for a dilution refrigerator.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An industry partner willing to supply a project, modest funding, and employee interactions throughout the year
#### Constraints
- The first cohort was small: eight students enrolled in Fall 2022 and five continued in Spring 2023, so all students worked on a single industry project

### Target Learners
- upper-division undergraduate physics majors seeking terminal bachelor's degrees

### Learning Goals
- understanding what comprises the quantum industry
- building skills for the quantum workforce
- building confidence to enter the quantum industry
