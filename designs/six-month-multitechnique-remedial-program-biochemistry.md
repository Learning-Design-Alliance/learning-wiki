# Six-month multi-technique remedial program for underperforming first-year biochemistry students with rubric-based incentives

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A structured remedial program in which the Biochemistry faculty "designed an exclusive teaching methodology for the remedial students": daily one-hour sessions over six months, spanning mind maps, role play, viva, seminars, quizzes, small group discussions, weekly tests, quick revisions, assignments, grade incentives in FA, summarization, open- and closed-book tests, poster making, underlining and highlighting, leader-led group reading, independent study, and solving previous years' papers. Sessions ran in addition to the standard curriculum, mentors were trained beforehand, and modules were "continuously tailored based on the feedback and ongoing evaluation of students by their mentors". Compliance was monitored weekly with rubric scoring and a reward system of added scores and chocolates.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Eligible students scored ≤ 40% in their second FA in theory; informed consent and an orientation session with mentors preceded enrollment
- All mentors underwent training for the structured remedial program before the study began
#### Constraints
- The remediation was confined to the subject of biochemistry and was conducted only among first-year students at a single institution
- Students scoring between 40 and 50% were not included as it would have been challenging to provide individualized attention to a larger group

### Target Learners
- Underperforming first-year medical undergraduate students

### Learning Goals
- Improved formative and summative assessment performance in biochemistry
