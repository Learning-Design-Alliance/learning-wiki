# SRL-enriched online science modules with reflective prompts as instructional and research tools

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Online modules designed by teachers after SRL&T professional development, integrating reflective questions as SRL prompts into pre-existing teaching materials. Modules served as both instruction and data sources: "each module was analysed twice—first, as an indicator of the teachers' SRL&T level, and second, as the source for the students' reflective replies." Examples include Adam's climate change module and Ben's spectrophotometry module delivered via Google Classroom.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers need assessment knowledge to design reflective prompts of graded levels
- Modules were adapted from teachers' pre-existing teaching materials during PD training
#### Constraints
- Reflective prompting analysis is conducted independently of student responses to keep evaluation unbiased

### Target Learners
- 10th-12th grade secondary students in science and chemistry classes

### Learning Goals
- Self-regulated learning skills including goal-setting, help-seeking, elaboration, and reflection
