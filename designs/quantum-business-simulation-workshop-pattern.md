# User-centered quantum computing workshop built as a business simulation game in a fictitious company

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A workshop design in which participants take roles in a fictitious company that is made "quantum-ready", selecting problem scenarios such as quantum-based optimization in production or quantum-secure encryption. The game is carried out without computer support, using pens, paper or tactile materials, with optional programming projects. Keynotes are spontaneously adapted to participants' interests, and working groups of typically 5 to 8 members present solutions at the end. The concept is kept open so it can be adapted to different target groups and companies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trainers need flexibility, a broad interdisciplinary knowledge base spanning quantum technologies, teaching concepts and real business processes, and several trainers covering different specialist areas
#### Constraints
- The one-day format used at IT-Tage 2024 tends to be too limited in duration, especially for participants with little or no prior knowledge

### Target Learners
- IT professionals and managers without prior quantum knowledge

### Learning Goals
- Understanding basics and possible applications of quantum computing
- Identifying industry-relevant quantum use cases
