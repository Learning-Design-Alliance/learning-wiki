# StudentResearcher virtual learning environment for advanced quantum mechanics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
StudentResearcher is a virtual learning environment incorporating "simulations, multiple-choice quizzes, video lectures and gamiﬁcation into a learning path for quantum mechanics at the advanced university level". It contained 192 slides, 7 video lectures, 4 SGE games, 1 quantum physics simulation tool, 24 reshuffling exercises, 162 multiple-choice questions, and 25 Peerwise-authored questions, with points, badges and leaderboards. It was deployed as an extensive supplement to traditional lectures and theoretical exercises in a 7-week graduate course.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students were encouraged, but not forced, to use it as part of their learning trajectory, framed as course material in preparation for an oral exam
#### Constraints
- Use was voluntary, so activity drop-off toward the exam was expected

### Target Learners
- advanced university (4th-year graduate-level) physics students

### Learning Goals
- advanced quantum mechanics, including proofs, change-of-basis calculations and the Stern-Gerlach experiment
