# CHEW implant-insertion training program: 8 days combining classroom-based learning and facility-based practicum

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Uganda Ministry of Health trained community health extension workers to insert Implanon NXT implants and refer clients for removal. The training used a Ministry-developed manual delivered by national-level trainers, and "The training on implant insertion was provided for 8 days, with 4 days of classroom-based learning and 4 days of facility-based practicum." After successful training, CHEWs were accredited and deployed at their supervising health facility, later working independently. This blended classroom-plus-practicum structure is the instructional component the evaluation assesses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training delivered by national-level trainers using a training manual developed by the Uganda Ministry of Health
#### Constraints
- The training focused specifically on implant provision and appropriate referral for implant removal, not the full family planning method mix

### Target Learners
- community health extension workers in Uganda

### Learning Goals
- clinical competency in Implanon NXT insertion
- client counseling and informed choice
- accurate recording of family planning services
