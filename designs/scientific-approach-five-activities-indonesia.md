# The scientific approach (observing, questioning, experimenting, associating, communicating) in Indonesian mathematics education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes the scientific approach mandated in the 2013 Indonesian curriculum as comprising "observing, questioning, experimenting, associating, and communicating", each with indicators such as reading and listening for observing, asking and clarifying for questioning, trying and collecting data for experimenting, processing and analyzing information for associating, and compiling reports for communicating. It served as the control condition against which the problem solving strategy was compared for HOTS outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Implementation follows the Permendikbud (81A) 2013 standard process for basic and secondary education
#### Constraints
- In this study it was outperformed by the problem solving strategy on all four HOTS measures
