# Technology use for four functions in personalized learning: planning, learning, assessment, and recordkeeping

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study frames technology use in learner-centered schools around four functions: "planning, learning, assessment, and recordkeeping". The national survey of ELA teachers measured how extensively teachers used technology for each function and compared high- and low-performing learner-centered schools. Teachers in high-performing schools utilized technology for more functions overall and more frequently for sharing resources, and reported having a more powerful technology system.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A technology system perceived as powerful enough to support the functions, which high-performing schools' teachers reported more often
#### Constraints
- Findings come from ELA teachers in identified learner-centered schools and are associated with standardized test performance rather than experimentally established

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in learner-centered schools
