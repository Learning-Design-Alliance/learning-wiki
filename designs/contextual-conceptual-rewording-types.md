# Two types of word-problem rewording: contextual (situational) and conceptual

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article distinguishes two rewording approaches. In contextual rewording, "the context —or non -mathematical content —of a problem is altered", for example replacing unfamiliar story content with personally familiar content while maintaining problem structure. In conceptual rewording, "textual modifications make semantic relations among key problem elements (e.g., given and unknown sets) more explicit and transparent by, for example, adding or rearranging text". The study built three reworded tests (T1, T2, T3) from these types plus their combination and tested their effects on seventh-grade error rates.
