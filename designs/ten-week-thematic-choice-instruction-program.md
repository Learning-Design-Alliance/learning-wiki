# Ten-week instruction in thematic choice for Chinese college students' academic English writing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An instructional program of ten sessions (about eight hours total, 50 minutes per session) delivered over 10 consecutive weeks during regular English lessons by the researcher, who was also the students' regular classroom teacher. It was delivered only to the experimental group (CE), while the control group (CC) received regular lessons on planning the writing, structuring paragraphs, writing introductions, writing conclusions, and the writing of one essay. The program targeted thematic choice in academic English writing, aiming to help students choose the right kind of information to place in Theme position.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered during regular class hours scheduled for English lessons
- Participants complete pre- and post-instruction writing tasks for effect assessment
#### Constraints
- Delivered only to the experimental group; the study was quasi-experimental because students remained intact in their regular classes due to school policy

### Target Learners
- Fourth-year Chinese college English majors

### Learning Goals
- Making native-speaker-like thematic choices in academic English essays
