# Nearpod-based digital physics lab module on waves and sound

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A digital learning module for the topic "Waves, Sound, and Sound Propagation" built in Nearpod.com as a unified environment for content delivery, interaction, and assessment. It integrates interactive slides, embedded simulations and questions, real-time teacher monitoring and feedback, and student-paced modes; hands-on activities such as a slinky demonstration and rubber-band vibration investigations were paired with digital tasks. The module was presented at GIREP 2023 and MPTL 2023 workshops, and "The developed set of labs in the Nearpod interface is shown in figure 3".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Nearpod's built-in assessment and interaction features; internet access for participants
#### Constraints
- Limited media integration (some content opens in separate windows) and difficulty processing post-lesson data in numerical form

### Target Learners
- secondary-level physics students (ninth grade, ages 14-15)

### Learning Goals
- understanding waves and sound propagation
- formative assessment of physics concepts
- collaborative hands-on investigation
