# Four activity categories scaffold CRA learning from reflection to redesign

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide organizes course activities into four categories that progress from self-examination to system redesign: positionality and reflection; understanding the on-the-ground ecosystem of assessment; critically examining and modifying existing assessment tools or systems; and designing a new reality. This sequence lets students first articulate how their own identities and social positions shape assessment work, then map real assessment ecosystems, critique and adapt existing instruments, and finally design culturally responsive assessments. The categories are presented as a flexible structure instructors adapt to their cohort.
