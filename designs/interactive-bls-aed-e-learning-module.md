# Interactive BLS-AED e-learning module aligned with Swiss Resuscitation Council guidelines

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 30-minute interactive e-learning module developed under Storyline 3 and hosted on a Joomla-based study platform, adapted from a module used with second-year students and designed according to Swiss Resuscitation Council BLS-AED provider guidelines with COVID-19 adaptations. Its goal was to enable students to "(1) identify a cardiorespiratory arrest" through "(5) use an AED", with all subjects "presented in an interactive way". No time limit was set for completion, and 124 participants completed it.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Completion of the precourse questionnaire before access.
- Registration on the study platform; access to the follow-up 1-hour practice session for certification.
#### Constraints
- The article states the module was designed to last 30 minutes with no time limit, and that interactive e-learning modules cannot replace hands-on practice.

### Target Learners
- first-year medical and dental students

### Learning Goals
- recognize out-of-hospital cardiac arrest
- alert professional help
- perform high-quality CPR
- use an AED
