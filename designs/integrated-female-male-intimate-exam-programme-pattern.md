# Integrated same-programme teaching of both female and male intimate examination with combined teaching modalities

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A programme-level design in which female and male genital examination are taught together in one simulated clinical programme, combining preparatory online materials, model practice, teaching associates and clinical tutors. The article notes that "An integrated programme of learning including both male and female genital examination in a simulated educational setting has been rarely reported". Integrating both genders means all students examine a TA of the same and the opposite gender, which the authors say helps minimise any perception that either gender is more important.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to trained teaching associates of both genders
- A supportive, learner-centred simulated clinical environment with expert tutors
#### Constraints
- Most reported studies focus on either male or female anatomy separately; evidence on integrated programmes is sparse

### Target Learners
- senior medical students

### Learning Goals
- intimate examination skills for both female and male anatomy
