# Situated learning lesson set distributing mathematics content across the model's eight components

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A reusable unit-level design in which a mathematics topic sequence is distributed across the situated learning model's components rather than taught as formula presentation and board practice. The article reports: "The lessons were developed on these eight key components of situated leaning model - four each on reflection, and technology; three each on cognitive apprenticeship, articulation of learning skills and collaboration; and two each on stories and multiple practice." Reflection was supported with specific prompts, and multiple practice used linear programmed instruction material.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Lessons in English per the prescribed grade VII syllabus; component-specific supports such as prompts for reflection and programmed material for multiple practice
#### Constraints
- Treatment lasted 25 days at 35 minutes per day, which the author calls a relatively short treatment period

### Target Learners
- grade VII students in English-medium schools

### Learning Goals
- grade VII mathematics content learned with reduced mathematics anxiety
