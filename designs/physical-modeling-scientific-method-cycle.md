# Physical modeling activity structured around the scientific method: observation, hypothesis testing, modeling, forecasting and conclusions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The activity is organized as a miniature scientific investigation: students observe the illumination patterns, build and test hypotheses about the Moon's shape by comparing sphere and disk models, forecast phases, and draw conclusions. The authors state it "incorporates several elements typical of the scientiﬁc method, such as observation, construction, and testing of hypotheses, modeling, forecasting, and establishment of conclusions based on critical thinking." They argue that because "the activity could provide students with an experience similar to that of scientists in their research laboratories", it may promote a more realistic view of science.
