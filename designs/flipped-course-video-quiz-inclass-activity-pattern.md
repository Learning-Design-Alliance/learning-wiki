# Flipped-course pattern: pre-class videos with monitored quiz forms, in-class group activities, and after-class group work

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's experimental course followed a structured flipped pattern: before class, EG students watched 10-15 minute recorded lecture videos in staged steps and completed electronic forms with 3-5 random questions, which served to monitor activity and guide preparation; during class, the FL model was implemented "through group discussion and in-class activities" using varied group-work techniques; after class, students did group work and assignments. The control group received traditional lectures with handed-out materials.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Videos and lessons available on an e-learning portal before the activity
- Electronic forms to monitor and structure pre-class viewing
- Classroom arrangements (chair positioning, data show projection) appropriate for group activity
#### Constraints
- Videos longer than 10 minutes were perceived as time-consuming and slowed progress through the steps

### Target Learners
- undergraduate pre-service teachers

### Learning Goals
- understanding key course concepts
- active participation and collaboration
