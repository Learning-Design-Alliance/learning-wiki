# Lecture-then-hands-on-simulation sequence for complex three-dimensional anatomy

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The instructional pattern tested in this study: both groups receive the same one-hour didactic lecture and Q2, then diverge into a half-hour hands-on simulator session or video review before Q3. The quiz design targeted "the specific learning difficulties reported by medical undergraduates", and equal difficulty was ensured across the three quizzes. The pattern links a preparatory lecture with an interactive practice phase.
