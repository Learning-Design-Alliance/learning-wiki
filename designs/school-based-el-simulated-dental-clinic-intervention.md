# School-based experiential learning intervention: oral health presentation plus simulated dental clinic role play

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A multi-session school-based intervention in which children first receive "a lively 40-min presentation on oral health given by dentists" using slides, cartoon videos and dental models; one month later they take part in a simulated dental clinic in the classroom, demonstrating dental tools and role-playing patient, dentist, nurse and parent in 4-person groups; a month after that they visit the dental clinic for PFS treatment. The trial compared this with a conventional 5-min Tell-Show-Do session at the time of the visit.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Class as the unit of delivery with dentists and dental nurses to run the presentation, tool demonstration and role play; a simulated dental clinic setup in the classroom with a dental bed
#### Constraints
- Evaluated in 7-8-year-old children in Shenzhen primary schools immediately before planned pit and fissure sealant; the authors note different EL activities might be needed to cover other dental procedures than PFS

### Target Learners
- Children aged 7–8 years in primary school, before their first preventive dental visit

### Learning Goals
- Familiarity with dental procedures and tools, dental health knowledge, reduced dental anxiety
