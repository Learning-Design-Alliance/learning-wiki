# One-hour CI-CDSS clinician training combining CI education, workflow guidance, and case-based demonstration

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A one-hour virtual training was held for clinicians and clinic teams in clinics randomized to the CI-CDSS arm before implementation. "The training emphasized CI education as well as appropriate workflow for using the CI-CDSS with demonstration of CI-CDSS features and content through case-based learning." Sessions were led by investigators with neuroscience and geriatric psychiatry expertise plus a senior CDSS program manager, with lunch and one CME credit offered to encourage attendance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivery by experts in CI and in the CDSS system; scheduling with incentives (lunch, CME credit) to secure clinician attendance
#### Constraints
- 60% of CI-CDSS clinic clinicians attended at baseline; attendance varied across the 19 clinics (M = 6 clinicians/clinic, SD = 3)

### Target Learners
- Primary care clinicians, nurses, rooming team members, and clinic managers

### Learning Goals
- CI diagnosis and care knowledge
- Workflow competence with the CI-CDSS
