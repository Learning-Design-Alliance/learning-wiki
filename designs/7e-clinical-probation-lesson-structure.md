# 7E-based clinical probation lesson structure combining case-based exploration, student-designed simulation, weighted evaluation and parallel-medical-record extension

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A recurring unit-level instructional design in which each probation lesson runs through the seven 7E stages: pre-class elicitation materials, engagement with the clinical case, CBL–RISE group case discussion at the explore stage, cross-group comparison and instructor concept formation at the explain stage, student-authored simulation scripts presented in three phases ("preparation, implementation planning and evaluation"), a weighted evaluate stage (theory 60%, skills 40%), and an extend stage of six sessions built around narrative-nursing literature, film, writing and sharing of parallel medical records via PBL. Sessions ran twice weekly for "≥ 120 min each time, and a total of 24 lessons were taught".
