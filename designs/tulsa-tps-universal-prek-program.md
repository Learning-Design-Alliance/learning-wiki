# Tulsa Public Schools pre-K program: a universal, high-quality school-based pre-kindergarten program

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The Tulsa pre-K program is a universal, voluntary school-based pre-kindergarten offered since Oklahoma's 1998 universal access policy. The article describes its quality standards: "Class sizes cannot be greater than 20 children, and child-to-staff ratios cannot exceed 10:1", and lead teachers must hold a B.A. and early childhood certification with public-school pay and benefits. Documented instructional support exceeds other states' school-based pre-K, and Tulsa pre-K teachers devoted more time to literacy, math, and science than counterparts elsewhere. The study evaluates whether its short-term effects persist to third grade for two cohorts.
