# Blended educational programme for GP trainers combining e-learning, interactive meetings, a learning conversation and quality improvement targets

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A two-day blended educational programme for GP trainers, built on the Dutch STI guidelines, that combined a two-hour e-learning package, interactive lectures with casuistry, small-group workshops, a 'learning conversation' with the trainer's own resident, focus groups on barriers and facilitators, and self-formulated quality improvement targets. The article describes "a two days training programme incorporating evidence-based practice guidelines and multiple teaching strategies, including interactive lectures, discussion groups, e-learning and quality improvement targets". Satisfaction with both meetings was rated high, at an overall median of 4 on a 1–5 scale.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- GP trainers prepare by reading the STI guideline and completing the e-learning before the first meeting
- teachers are provided with a comprehensive handbook to ensure consistency in how the programme is taught
#### Constraints
- evaluation was hampered because laboratory-documented HIV testing data could not be retrieved for the majority of GPs in both groups

### Target Learners
- experienced GP trainers (median age 51) in Dutch general practice

### Learning Goals
- knowledge, attitude and skills for diagnosing HIV and other STIs and for proactive, provider-initiated HIV testing
