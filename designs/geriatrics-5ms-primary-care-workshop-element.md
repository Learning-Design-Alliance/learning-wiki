# Geriatrics 5Ms half-day primary care workshop with longitudinal patient case and point-of-care tools

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A half-day (2.5-hour time block, 2 hours 45 minutes class time) workshop piloted with 33 internal medicine residents, built through Kern's six-step curriculum development approach. It comprises five interactive modules—Mobility (falls risk factors), Medications (polypharmacy and deprescribing), Mind (cognitive impairment evaluation), and Multicomplexity and Matters Most (prognostication and cancer screening)—centered on "a longitudinal patient case with unfolding information." Learners receive point-of-care tools including a Geriatrics 5Ms summary and EMR template, a deprescribing algorithm, and access to ePrognosis and the Mini-Cog. Peer-reviewed appendices include slide decks, handouts, a facilitator checklist, and evaluation instruments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Computer and projector, PowerPoint slides (Appendices A–E), whiteboard or poster board with sticky notes and markers, learner handouts, and a lead facilitator checklist; clinics closed to permit attendance
#### Constraints
- Scheduling challenges and time constraints for implementation; blocking clinic patient care sessions "is not feasible at all institutions"; sustainability depends on institutional support and dedicated teaching time

### Target Learners
- Clinical-year resident physicians in primary care settings

### Learning Goals
- Applying the Geriatrics 5Ms framework to older adult care
- Applying a five-step deprescribing plan
- Listing dementia warning signs and diagnostic workup components
- Making patient-centered cancer screening recommendations aligned with goals and prognosis
