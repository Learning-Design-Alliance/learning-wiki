# Neurocognitive-based instructional model (5P): five phases integrating educational neuroscience with research-based learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The 5P model is an innovative instructional model integrating research-based learning and educational neuroscience, with the principle of promoting learner development of attention, working memory, and positive mood. The article states that "the neurocognitive-based model (5P) covers five main phases: persuasion, planning, performance, production, and presentation", in which learners investigate prior knowledge, plan research, collect and analyse data, interpret results, and write and present research reports. It was validated by nine experts and piloted with 34 Grade 9 students before the main study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivery by a teacher trained in the model's five-phase syntax, with a teacher's guide and lesson plans developed and expert-validated in the study
#### Constraints
- Validated and tested with Thai Grade 9 science students on eco-system content over six weeks; the article does not claim generalization beyond this setting

### Target Learners
- Grade 9 secondary science students

### Learning Goals
