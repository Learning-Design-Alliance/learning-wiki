# ChatGPT Prompt Framework for children's art teachers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A ChatGPT prompt framework developed for children's art teachers to structure interactions with GPT-4o during lesson planning. The article states that "frameworks offer a systematic way to construct prompts, ensuring that they are clear, focused, and effective in eliciting the desired response from AI models." The framework outlines four main steps following the logic of teachers' lesson-planning thinking: determining the theme, generating a lesson plan, optimizing design, and filling content. It was tested on a sample lesson plan before the experiment and refined using participant feedback during the study.
