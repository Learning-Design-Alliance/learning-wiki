# Mapping astronomical properties to musical parameters: pitch for planet size, colour for stellar note, magnitude for appearance order, and water covering fraction for filter cut-off

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This pattern maps physical properties of astronomical objects and data onto musical parameters so audiences can hear structure in the data. Smaller rocky planets were given higher pitches and gas giants lower pitches, with the Sun acting as a musical "pedal point"; stellar colour (B−V difference) selected one of five glockenspiel notes, with "The reddest stars are assigned the lowest notes and the bluest stars the highest notes"; brightness determined the order stars sounded; and Earth's water covering fraction by longitude controlled a low-pass filter cut-off frequency.

### Context
#### Requirements
