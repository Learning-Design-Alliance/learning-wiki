# Multi-layered GPT-4o/Claude 3.5 Sonnet OSCE simulation chatbot with automated scoring and text feedback

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A proprietary educational AI chatbot combining GPT-4o and Claude 3.5 Sonnet in a multi-layered architecture, used as the experimental training tool in the pilot RCT. OSCE scenarios were "converted into chart‑style prompts optimized for SP role simulation"; the chatbot acted as a simulated patient, generated natural responses to student inquiries, and after each interview provided "both quantitative scoring and individualized text feedback based on the dialogue". Students practiced dizziness and shoulder pain cases freely without time or attempt restrictions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pre-designed chart-style scenario prompts; a clinical expert supervising sessions; reliable server performance and technical support on standby
#### Constraints
- Students reported inability to implement physical examination, lack of understanding of Korean medicine terminology, absence of real interaction with patients, and scoring speed delay

### Target Learners
- final-year Korean medicine students preparing for OSCE

### Learning Goals
- clinical interview and history taking
- patient education
- clinical reasoning
