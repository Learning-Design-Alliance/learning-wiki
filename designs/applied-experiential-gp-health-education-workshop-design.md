# Applied experiential workshop design combining didactic content, skills practice, feedback, coaching, peer collaboration and authentic field experience

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The workshop combined short didactic presentations on adolescent health topics with training in experiential, learner-centred teaching strategies. "Training techniques employed in the workshop included short didactic presentations, practicing skills, direct feedback, coaching, peer collaboration and small group discussions." Participants then applied workshop learning in an authentic context by collaboratively planning and delivering health education sessions in schools. The design was informed by research cautioning "against a singular focus on facts and knowledge in favour of functional health information" and developing skills that support health.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Informed by contemporary research on effective adolescent health education
- Includes an applied field experience applying lesson plans in an authentic school context
- Content on functional health information, values and beliefs, and health-supporting skills rather than facts alone
#### Constraints
- The article notes pre-post designs are not as strong as having a comparison group
- Barriers to GPs' broader involvement in health promotion remain unexplored at structural and practice levels

### Target Learners
- General practitioners
- General practice registrars
- Medical students

### Learning Goals
- Knowledge of adolescent health issues
- Skills in experiential teaching strategies and lesson planning
- Confidence to engage adolescents in health education
