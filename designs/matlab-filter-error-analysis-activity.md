# MATLAB filter error-analysis and optimization activity

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A MATLAB-based filter implementation activity set after the Fourier-transform sequence: students are told a previous designer filtered a signal to remove noise 'but has not achieved it in an optimal way', and must report the prior designer's analysis, the error made, and the criteria for the optimal solution, 'limited to one and a half hours to solve the problem'.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students enter with solid knowledge of spectral analysis via the Fourier transform; MATLAB tools such as sptool or filter design are available.
#### Constraints
- The article identifies the absence of a meaningful title and the limited time-frame as design limitations that may have hindered comprehension and constrained synthesis and evaluation; it later refers to a nearly two-hour allocation despite stating a one-and-a-half-hour limit.

### Target Learners
- university engineering students in signal processing

### Learning Goals
- filter implementation, spectral analysis, error diagnosis and justification of design criteria
