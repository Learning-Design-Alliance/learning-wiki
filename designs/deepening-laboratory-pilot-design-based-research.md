# Deepening laboratory for motivated and talented students as a pilot toward design-based research

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Within the National Plan for Science Degree (PLS), the authors proposed two optional deepening laboratories, Waves and energy and Sound and surroundings, for selected students, one of several PLS laboratory types alongside laboratories that approach the discipline and develop vocations and self-assessment laboratories. The guidelines orient to science degrees through training, treat "laboratory as a method not as a place", and make the student the main character of learning. Testing these activities in an optional laboratory is presented as the first step toward a design-based research learning path (Hake 2008).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Joint planning by teachers and university, with students meeting for 3 hours almost every month across the last 3 years of high school
#### Constraints
- The laboratories are optional and intended for motivated and talented students; conclusions come from a small pilot with few students

### Target Learners
- Selected third-class Liceo Scientifico students in deepening laboratories

### Learning Goals
- Deepening topics in wave physics through laboratory work
