# 40-day daily diary study of medical students' affective-motivational experiences during exam preparation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
This element is the study design through which the article generates its evidence: "This study examined daily feedback loops and fluctuations in medical students’ affective-motivational experiences during a 40-day exam preparation period." Motivational states were operationalized as cognitive appraisals of daily goals and self-efficacy, and emotional states as the achievement emotions joy, pride, anxiety, anger, and boredom. The repeated daily measures were analyzed with Dynamic Structural Equation Modeling in Mplus to detect feedback loops and stability of experiences within and between learning sessions.
