# Barrows' six essential elements of problem-based learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article presents PBL as a learner-centered instructional approach anchored in concrete problems, citing Barrows' six essential elements: "student-centered", small student groups, tutor as facilitator or guide, authentic problems encountered before preparation, problems as tools for knowledge acquisition and problem-solving skills, and new information acquired through self-directed learning. The article's own clinical PBL model operationalizes these elements in pharmacy rotations.
