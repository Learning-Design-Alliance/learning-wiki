# Small-group seminar series pairing STEM critique with arts, humanities, and social science frameworks

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The QSTC's first instructional activity is a Fiat Lux seminar series, Upending the Hard Sciences, in which "The series course will consist of six consecutive panel discussions followed by Q&A." Each panel explores intersecting frameworks of knowledge and technology production and its linkage to broad social and local community (in)equities. The format uses UCLA's Fiat Lux program, which offers "small group settings to engage in meaningful discussions" between undergraduates and Senate faculty, with one unit of Pass/No Pass academic credit.
