# The Model of Educational Reconstruction structures the clarification of content and investigation of student perspectives in task design

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The article adopts the Model of Educational Reconstruction, whose "core idea is that instructors should follow three steps while designing learning environments": clarifying and analyzing the science content, investigating students' perspectives such as interests and preconceptions, and iteratively integrating both to design and evaluate the learning environment. In the framework, design principles 1 to 3 can be linked to the MER.
