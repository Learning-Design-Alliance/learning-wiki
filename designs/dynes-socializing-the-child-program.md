# Dynes's primary-grade program for developing the historic sense through the immediate social environment

## Description
Dynes's book Socializing the Child proposes beginning history in Grade I rather than relegating it to upper grades, using "play, construction work, pictures and stories" to familiarize children with their immediate social environment, including community workers and public enterprises. Grade II covers primitive life, fire, occupations and existing tribes such as Eskimos and American Indians; Grade III studies home life and customs of Holland, Germany, Japan and France, plus stories of Bible characters, Greek and Roman heroes and Columbus. The reviewer judges the work well planned and valuable for building a foundation for later systematic historical study.
