# Whole-group introduction followed by small-group homogeneous practice of the same skills

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Journeys program organizes daily literacy instruction in two tiers: whole group activities introduce a "big idea" and "essential question" for the day, a teacher read-aloud, and vocabulary, phonics, comprehension, and fluency activities. Small group activities then "follow up these same skills in small, homogeneous groups," so all students meet the same content with differentiated group support.
