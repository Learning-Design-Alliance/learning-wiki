# MBCL deep learning triangle: visible thinking enables timely instructor advice that promotes additional cooperative study toward deep learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors propose an MBCL deep learning model in which students' cooperative study progress is visible in real time on LiveBoard electronic whiteboards, the instructor captures that visible thinking and advises students instantly, and students then engage in additional cooperative study to achieve deep learning in knowledge integration and synthesis. The article states that "Visible thinking has been recognized as one of the critical requirements for effective, scaffolded knowledge integration," positioning the model as coherent with existing findings. The cycle "dynamically integrates students and the instructor toward an instructor‐guided in‐depth learning process" (Figure 7).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A shared, instantly updated electronic whiteboard so student study progress is visible to the instructor in real time
- An instructor who monitors the shared whiteboards and provides timely, insightful advice without giving expected answers
#### Constraints
- Presented as a proposed model in a proof-of-principle study, not independently tested as a mechanism

### Target Learners
- undergraduate biology students in regular college classrooms

### Learning Goals
- knowledge integration
- knowledge synthesis
- deep learning
