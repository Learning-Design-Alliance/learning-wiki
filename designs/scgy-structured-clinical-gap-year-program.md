# Structured clinical gap year (scGY) program: paid 14-month dermatology-practice clinical employment with mentorship and didactics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 14-month paid clinical employment program at a private dermatology practice, launched in 2017, for students aiming at medical, PA, or dental school. "Applicants undergo interviews before joining clinical teams of physicians and physician assistants, where they participate in patient care, surgical assisting, and electronic medical record tasks as paid team members." It is complemented by weekly lectures on high-yield topics, mentorship, research opportunities, and personalized recommendation letters. The article evaluates it via a 44-question alumni survey with a 97% response rate.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Applicants are interviewed before joining clinical teams as paid team members; the program requires direct patient care, surgical assisting, EMR use, structured mentorship, and weekly didactic sessions.
#### Constraints
- All participants worked in a multi-specialty dermatology clinic, which the authors state limited exposure to other areas of medicine and may impact generalizability.

### Target Learners
- pre-medical and pre-professional-healthcare students taking a gap year between college and professional school

### Learning Goals
- career exploration
- clinical competency development
- preparedness for professional school
