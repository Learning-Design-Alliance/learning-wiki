# Six-step webinar session structure: clinical case, individual questions, shared answers with feedback, objectives and content, work documents, and closing questions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes a repeating session structure for its 61 resident webinars: "The presentation of a clinical case by an expert on the clinical topic", followed by open-ended and multiple-choice questions answered individually, feedback on answers, a brief conference on the learning objectives and content, and lectures "complemented with work documents". Time for questions and answers was allotted at the end of the class.
