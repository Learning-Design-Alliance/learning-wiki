# Grenoble-Alpes 4-step flipped-classroom hybrid learning method with a local LMS

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A hybrid learning model used in health studies at Grenoble-Alpes University since 2006, in which "A 4-step learning method was proposed for students": remotely-accessible knowledge capsules, interactive online questions for the lecturer, interactive on-site training and explanation meetings, and participation in training-exam sessions or practice tests with junior tutors. Each theme is split across 4 sequential weeks, and the method is supported by a local LMS offering learning activities and a planner that schedules and provides access to activities at specific times.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A local learning management system providing access to learning activities and a planner scheduling activities at specific times
#### Constraints
- Not all lectures are flipped in later years, so time management is needed to manage both types of lectures; the study's learning situation is described as not generally reproducible

### Target Learners
- first-year health studies (PACES) students
- second-year midwifery, medical, and pharmacy students

### Learning Goals
- deep approach learning linking knowledge, skills, and habits
- preparation for competitive examinations and professional practice
