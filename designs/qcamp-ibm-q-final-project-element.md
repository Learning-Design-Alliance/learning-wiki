# IBM Quantum Composer final project: group research exercise characterizing assigned quantum devices

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The camp culminated in a hands-on research exercise using IBM's cloud-based quantum computers, reflecting the authors' guiding principle that "the best way to learn how a quantum computer works is to actually use one." Participants were introduced to the IBM Quantum Composer, a visual drag-and-drop interface requiring no prerequisite programming experience, then divided into small groups, each assigned one IBM quantum computer. Groups evaluated CNOT error and readout error to predict which computer would perform best, ran five experiments for each single- and multi-qubit circuit, recorded outcomes for statistical analysis, and compared measured error rates to other groups' results and IBM's specified errors. The authors report the main outcome was reinforcement of the scientific method through critical thinking and hands-on experimentation.
