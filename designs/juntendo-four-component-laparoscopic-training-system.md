# Four-component laparoscopic gastrectomy training system (Juntendo)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A structured surgical education system in which trainees "received systematic education about laparoscopic gastrectomy in four components": (1) understanding anatomy and standard procedures via theoretical study and repeated viewing of non-edited operative videos; (2) mastering basic laparoscopic skills using a training box, computer simulator, and hands-on porcine laboratory sessions; (3) gaining intraoperative experience through simple laparoscopic surgeries and serving as scope operator then assistant; and (4) receiving direction from a trainer acting as first assistant during real TLDG. The study evaluated whether this system enabled safe, feasible trainee performance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trainees complete all four components, including basic skills practice and graduated assisting experience, before operating as TLDG surgeon
#### Constraints
- Evaluated retrospectively at a single high-volume centre; trainees were allocated lower-stage patients, which the authors state may have shaped the learning curve

### Target Learners
- trainee gastrointestinal surgeons with at least 7 years of post-graduation surgical experience

### Learning Goals
- performing total laparoscopic distal gastrectomy with Billroth I reconstruction safely and independently
