# Six-step implementation process for an admissions portfolio review system

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The report documents the process the Ross Undergraduate Admissions team used to build and run portfolio review: align the portfolio to central admission processes, establish technological capacity, develop rubrics, build reader capacity, conduct outreach, and iterate. Rubrics align with "skills and mindsets students need to flourish in the Ross program and community—including critical thinking, problem-solving, collaboration, and curiosity." The pattern balances a standard review's efficiency with qualitative portfolio depth inside existing university timelines.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Rubrics with clear criteria and detailed performance indicators for reliable qualitative review
- A technology platform (SlideRoom) to accept and transfer media submissions
- A hired team of 20 to 25 short-term contractual professional readers
#### Constraints
- Implemented within the University of Michigan's existing application deadlines, review timeline, and decision release, avoiding university-wide change

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate BBA applicants

### Learning Goals
- Reliable evaluation of applicants' critical thinking, problem-solving, collaboration, and curiosity
