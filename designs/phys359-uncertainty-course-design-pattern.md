# Upper-level laboratory course design combining small enrollment, extended lab time, precision experiments, a self-paced Python curve-fitting module, and grade weight on uncertainty skills

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The 2022 PHYS 359 honors laboratory combined structural elements the authors link to a demonstrable increase in student understanding of uncertainties: smaller enrollment allowing greater one-on-one interaction with the instructor, more weekly lab time, and experiments requiring a higher degree of precision and attention to uncertainties. An additional module dedicated to curve fitting in a self-paced Python tutorial offered advanced exercises emphasizing graph interpretation and analysis, with an interactive simulation feature and personalized feedback. The assessment scheme allocated 20 percent of the lab report grade to skills such as justified quantitative statistical uncertainties, χ2 and residuals discussed quantitatively.
