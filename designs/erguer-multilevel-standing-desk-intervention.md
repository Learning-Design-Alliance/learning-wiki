# Multi-level classroom standing desk intervention (ERGUER/Portugal): LearnFit standing desks plus parent meetings and teacher training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A 16-week school-based intervention combining physical and social environmental changes. The physical component replaced all traditional seated desks with portable LearnFit Adjustable Standing Desks that enable postural changes and class-based movement. The social component included three parent/guardian meetings and six teacher training sessions, with teacher and parent feedback used to continually modify the intervention and peer-to-peer recommendations such as a U-shaped desk arrangement promoted as best practices.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Parental written informed consent for student participation
- Schoolteacher participation in the six training sessions to adapt to the new classroom reality
#### Constraints
- The intervention only reduced sitting time at school, with no changes in sitting time or physical activity outside school
- Used the least physiologically demanding break, continuously standing, rather than more intensive activity breaks

### Target Learners
- 6th-grade students aged 11–13 years in a public secondary school

### Learning Goals
- Reducing classroom sedentary time as a route to supporting cognitive performance and academic achievement
