# Online SAT preparatory course offered as complimentary field experimental treatment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A commercial online SAT preparation course from a well-known test prep company, including "practice exams, a library of prerecorded videos and instructional content, live online class sessions, analysis of individual performance, and test-taking strategy". It was offered free to eleventh graders in four low-income Los Angeles high schools as the field experiment's investment opportunity; students valued it highly, estimating its cost at $260 on average, and none of the schools had purchased it before.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schools normally purchase subscriptions at a cost of about $200 per student; the offer was complimentary to students in the experiment
#### Constraints
- Prior to the study no students in these schools were using the course, and the company does not offer the software to individuals

### Target Learners
- Eleventh-grade students in low-income, predominantly Hispanic public high schools

### Learning Goals
- SAT preparation and college readiness
