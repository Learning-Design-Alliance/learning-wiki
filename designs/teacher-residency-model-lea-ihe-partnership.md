# Teacher residency model: LEA–IHE partnership integrating coursework with a yearlong clinical placement under an expert mentor teacher

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The teacher residency model is a pathway into teaching developed and operated by a partnership between an LEA and a state-approved preparation program at an IHE. The brief describes it as focusing on rigorous "clinical preparation" whereby "a resident's credentialing coursework is integrated with a yearlong placement in the classroom of an expert mentor teacher," with the resident gradually assuming increased responsibility. Financial supports such as living stipends or tuition reductions are central, lowering barriers for candidates from underrepresented or low-income backgrounds. In California, grants of $20,000 per resident funded 38 LEA–IHE partnerships targeting STEM, special education, and bilingual credential areas.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A partnership between an LEA and an IHE with a state-approved teacher preparation program
- Financial supports (stipends or tuition reductions) so residents can complete a year of intensive clinical placement and coursework
- An expert mentor teacher providing mentorship and regular observations and feedback
#### Constraints
- The brief notes California's grant funding was one-time, and authorizing legislation initially capped administrative funding at 5 percent, so sustainability depends on LEA and IHE cost sharing over time

### Target Learners
- Teacher residency candidates, particularly those in STEM, special education, and bilingual credential areas, and candidates of color or from low-income backgrounds

### Learning Goals
- Rigorous clinical preparation for credentialing, including planning, teaching, and connecting with students and staff
- Gradual assumption of increased classroom responsibility under expert mentorship
