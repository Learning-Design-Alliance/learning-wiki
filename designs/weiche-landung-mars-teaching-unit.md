# Weiche Landung auf dem Mars: a 90-minute physics lesson unit for grades 9-13

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 90-minute teacher's guide ("Handreichung") for grades 9-13, produced as teaching material for the ISS-Horizons mission. Its learning goals state that students "ermitteln eine Bestimmungsgleichung für die Endgeschwindigkeit eines Falls mit Fallschirm", compute parachute descent speeds on Mars and Earth, explain why parachutes alone allow no soft landing, and compute the parachute size needed for a soft Mars landing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Worksheets (available from the Haus der Astronomie website), a pen, a calculator, and optionally a computer/tablet/smartphone with internet access.
#### Constraints
- The calculations rest on stated simplifying assumptions: vertical fall, neglected buoyancy, a massless parachute, constant gravitational acceleration, homogeneous atmospheric density, constant atmospheric temperature, a pure CO2 Mars atmosphere, and ideal-gas behaviour.

### Target Learners
- students in grades 9-13 (Sekundarstufe)

### Learning Goals
- deriving and applying the terminal-velocity equation for fall with air resistance
- explaining why parachutes alone cannot soft-land on Mars
- applying the ideal-gas equation to compute atmospheric density

### Claims
- Mars Parachute Terminal Velocity Six Times Earth [+W]
- Ideal Mars Parachute Diameter 224 M Unrealistic [~W]
