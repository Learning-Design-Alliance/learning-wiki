# DR-TA instructional sequence: predicting, reading, and proving repeated per text segment, followed by fundamental skill training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a two-phase instructional pattern. Phase 1 directs the reading-thinking process through three steps — "Predicting", "Reading", and "Proving" — repeated "with each new segment" of text, with students closing their books for comprehension-check questions such as "Were you correct?". Phase 2 provides fundamental skill training, reexamining story elements to develop word attack, semantic analysis, concept clarification, observation, and reflective abilities.
