# Seven unit-specific custom GPT chatbots for pharmaceutical science units

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Seven custom GPT chatbots (cGPTs) were co-created for six undergraduate units in a Bachelor of Pharmaceutical Science program: Polymer Knowledge, Polymer Socratic, Physiology 1, Physiology 2, Medicinal Chemistry 1, Physical Chemistry 2, and Scientific Inquiry. Purposes ranged from a virtual tutor supporting scientific writing to Socratic chatbots guiding problem-solving and alkene halogenation. By the end of development, "seven distinct yet consistent student-staff-designed cGPT tools had been created, each tailored to its unit's content and learning activities".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Built via the Create GPT function within an OpenAI Plus account, with system prompts and curated knowledge-base files
#### Constraints
- Development took two to six weeks depending on topic, student experience, and available resources; model updates (GPT-5, Study mode) changed cGPT behaviour mid-project

### Target Learners
- undergraduate pharmaceutical science students

### Learning Goals
- discipline-specific problem solving
- scientific writing
- polymer concept understanding
