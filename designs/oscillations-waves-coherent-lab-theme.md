# A coherent oscillations-and-waves theme as the organizing framework of a first-year laboratory course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes organizing the entire first-year laboratory course around a single physical theme so that techniques accumulate coherently. The authors write: "By choosing oscillations and waves as a theme for our first year lab course, we provide students with a coherent context in which to practice basic techniques, use of new instruments, and experimental data treatment." Within this theme, the pendulum builds statistical skills and the mass-spring grounds simple harmonic motion theory with a single degree of freedom.
