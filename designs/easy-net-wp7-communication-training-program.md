# EASY-NET WP7 communication training program for the audit team

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 2-month communication training course for a 10-member clinical audit team, divided into 10 meetings of 2 hours each. The training phase consisted of "4 face-to-face lectures, 3 focus group, and 3 workshops", delivered by three psychologists specialized in communication, with role-playing activities reproducing real-life communication problems. Topics covered included verbal, paraverbal, and nonverbal communication, listening modalities, communication styles, Grice's conversational maxims, dysfunctional modes of communication, and group dynamics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training materials sent to participants by e-mail beforehand so they could familiarize themselves with the topics
- Delivery by psychologists specialized in communication
- Administration of questionnaires before and after to evaluate effectiveness
#### Constraints
- Evaluated so far only on a single 10-member audit team; the authors state the sample size is a limitation

### Target Learners
- audit team professionals: physicians, nurses, statisticians, a clinical risk manager, and an audit specialist

### Learning Goals
- communication skills and styles
- interpersonal and group dynamics
- knowledge of communication in organizational settings
