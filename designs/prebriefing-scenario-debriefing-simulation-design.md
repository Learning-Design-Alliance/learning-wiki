# Three-component simulation design: prebriefing, immersive scenario, and structured debriefing aligned with Healthcare Simulation Standards of Best Practice

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A simulation-based educational design comprising 3 structured components aligned with best practices for simulation-based education: prebriefing, immersive simulation, and debriefing. Prebriefing was an asynchronous session establishing psychological safety, reviewing learning objectives, and outlining expectations. The immersive scenario involved a high-fidelity manikin reproducing respiratory mechanics, hemodynamic variability, and physiological responses. Debriefing was a structured 30-minute session emphasizing reflective practice, emotional processing, recognition of performance gaps, and reinforcement of effective clinical judgment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A high-fidelity manikin capable of reproducing respiratory mechanics, hemodynamic variability, and physiological responses consistent with the patient case.
- Faculty facilitators conducting a structured debriefing designed to meet the Healthcare Simulation Standards of Best Practice.
#### Constraints
- Participation in the simulation was a required component of the course curriculum; completion of the SET-M evaluation was voluntary.

### Target Learners
- Second-year doctoral nurse anesthesia students (SRNAs)

### Learning Goals
- Structured handoff communication
- Clinical decision-making in high-acuity scenarios
- Interprofessional collaboration and leadership
