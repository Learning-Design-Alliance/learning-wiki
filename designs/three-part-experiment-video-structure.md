# Three-part structure for instructional experiment videos: theory introduction, scenario-based procedure, measurement instructions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each of the three videos follows a common internal structure: "a short introductory part with the relevant theory", "the experimental procedure based on a specific scenario for each experiment" and "the appropriate set of instructions to get the necessary measurements". This structure supports both demonstration use and data-collection use, and the videos can be integrated into a lecture, the lab or class activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Video processing to focus on the range of interest (e.g. the 22 min GRAC record was shrunk to a 5.25 min video)
#### Constraints
- Designed for experiments where equipment may be unavailable or measurements time consuming

### Target Learners
- first year undergraduates with a medium background in physics
- secondary school students

### Learning Goals
- pre-lab preparation
- understanding experimental procedure and theory
- data collection and analysis
