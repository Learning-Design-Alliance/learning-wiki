# Provide self-sufficient introductory resources to ease the transition from lectured courses to research

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article argues that the transition from student to researcher often meets difficulty, wasting limited PhD or master's research time, and addresses this with a package aimed to ease the transition from lectured physics courses to research-oriented helium scattering studies. It states the package "does not require the support of the authors, or the faculty members running the programme, to be effective", so students can use it independently.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Resources must be freely available and written to promote intuition early in the study of the relevant physics
#### Constraints
- Elements of the package may find use in other fields, especially those related to atom scattering or surface diffusion, but it is oriented to helium atom scattering

### Target Learners
- new postgraduate research students
- undergraduate interns entering research

### Learning Goals
- accelerating early progress in doctoral or master's research
- understanding the helium-3 spin-echo spectrometer
