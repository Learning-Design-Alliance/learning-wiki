# Coherent whole-course lever configurations: think-first, direct-instruction, and Harkness classrooms

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The paper presents three full configurations of all seven levers drawn from classrooms the author has taught: a think-first classroom aiming at curiosity, perseverance, collaboration, and ownership; a direct-instruction classroom aiming at confidence, fluency, and content mastery; and a Harkness classroom aiming at communication, ownership, and meaning-making. As the paper puts it, "Two teachers may have clearly identifiable settings on all seven levers and still create substantially different learning experiences because they are pursuing different outcomes."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All seven levers must be set coherently in pursuit of a stated aim
#### Constraints
- The configurations are not ranked, are not representative accounts of classroom types, and are not offered as evidence that each achieves its stated aims
