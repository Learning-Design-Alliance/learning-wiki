# Early Algebra Learning Progression (EALP) organized around five big ideas

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Early Algebra Learning Progression is a curricular framework the authors developed from research findings, curricular resources, and standards documents in early algebra. It is "organi]ed aroXnd five ³big ideas´": Generalized Arithmetic; Equations, Expressions, Equality, and Inequality; Functional Thinking; Proportional Reasoning; and Variable. In this study it structured a year-long classroom teaching experiment whose functional-thinking strand is evaluated here.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered through classroom teaching experiment lessons of approximately one hour per week over a school year, with research-based tasks aligned to the progression
#### Constraints
- Evaluated here in a largely white (91%), middle-class southeastern Massachusetts district, so generalization beyond that setting is not established in this article

### Target Learners
- grades 3-5 elementary students

### Learning Goals
- algebraic thinking, functional thinking, covariational reasoning, correspondence rules
