# Bond-energy lesson sequence from definition, visualisation and hands-on experiments to real-life applications

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 design, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The guide organises the topic as a sequence: discussing the definition of bond, introducing a visual kit to visualise the energy associated with bonds, conducting two hands-on experiments where students "see first hand the effects of creating and breaking bonds at the molecular level", then applying the learned concepts to real-life situations such as alcohol evaporation and sport bags. Stimulating questions (Q) run throughout, and optional (EXTRA) material for more advanced discussion can be skipped with GCSE students.
