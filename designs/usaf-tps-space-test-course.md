# USAF TPS Space Test Course (STC)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Space Test Course is the USAF Test Pilot School course that grew out of the Space Test Fundamentals course first executed in January 2021 and expanded into a parallel course alongside the aircraft flight test track. "STC currently provides hands-on training in test fundamentals, systems test, space science application, and advanced space system test and evaluation." Students are selected from a variety of backgrounds and career fields spanning operations, test, engineering, and program management, providing diversity of perspectives, and the course partners academics and test events with flight test students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A cadre of space test instructors and leaders; partnership with the aircraft flight test track for academics and test events
#### Constraints
- STC graduates will be only a fraction of the larger USSF test workforce

### Target Learners
- USAF TPS students from operations, test, engineering, and program management communities

### Learning Goals

- systems test
