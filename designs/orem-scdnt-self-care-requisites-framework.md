# Orem's self-care deficit nursing theory as a framework structuring patient education around universal, developmental, and health deviation self-care requisites

## Description
Orem's self-care deficit nursing theory (SCDNT) is the explanatory framework this article uses to design and justify a nurse-led patient-training program. The theory "considers each individual as a self-care agent with the necessary ability to perform self-care activities individually", and the authors structured the CAD training booklet around the theory's universal, developmental, and health deviation self-care requisites.
