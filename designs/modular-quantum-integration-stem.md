# Integrate quantum computing into existing high-school STEM subjects modularly rather than as a standalone course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper proposes embedding quantum concepts inside subjects students already study, instead of adding a separate quantum course. As the authors state, "Rather than proposing a standalone quantum computing course, we suggest a modular approach" in which "quantum concepts are included in subjects that students are already learning, using hands-on projects and freely available materials." This works within saturated curricula and leverages open resources for global accessibility.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Existing mathematics, physics, and computer science courses with topics that naturally connect to quantum principles
#### Constraints
- Curricula are already saturated, so the approach avoids a standalone subject
- Depends on availability of freely available community-developed materials

### Target Learners
- High-school students

### Learning Goals
- Foundational quantum literacy
- Enriched mathematics, physics, and computer science learning
