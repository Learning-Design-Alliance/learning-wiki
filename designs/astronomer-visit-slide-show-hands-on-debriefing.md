# One-hour astronomer visit structure: slide show, hands-on activity, debriefing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each visit focuses on a few simple concepts only and follows a fixed three-part structure: an introductory 10-minute slide show, 40 minutes of hands-on activity, and a final 10-minute debriefing and questions. The article reports this structure for its Tucson classroom visits, with topics chosen in consultation with the teacher and material drawn from existing resources such as the book 'The Universe at your fingertips' and NASA Education web sites.
