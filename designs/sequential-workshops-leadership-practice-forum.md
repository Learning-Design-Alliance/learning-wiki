# Sequential interactive workshops as forums for practicing leadership behaviors in hierarchical settings

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The study used a series of three interactive workshops, held between 2018 and 2022, as "structured setting[s] where the participants engaged in facilitated learning, reflection, and peer dialogue." In settings with deeply ingrained hierarchies, these workshops "provided a space for inclusive dialogue and skill-building," and active nurse participation demonstrated the potential to broaden leadership across professions. The continuity across workshops enabled longitudinal qualitative data collection on leadership practices.

## Design Implications

#### Requirements

#### Constraints

- multidisciplinary healthcare teams of physicians and nurses in LMIC university hospitals

### Learning Goals
