# Role-playing narrative frames data collection tasks as a fictional space mission

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The activity is a simplified role-playing game in which students join a mission aboard a spacecraft under the leadership of their Captain, the teacher, to start a conversation with aliens. Each desk represents a room on the spaceship where a specific task is performed, and students gather data to answer questions within the narrative. The game-like nature and lack of rigid rules make it intentionally open-ended and flexible to accommodate varying learning approaches and student needs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The teacher acts as facilitator and creates the role-playing story, guiding students through specific tasks to collect data on a chosen topic within the curriculum.
#### Constraints
- The activity is designed for the classroom with the teacher as a guide; engaging parents in after-school homework activities would not be possible in all families.

### Target Learners
- children aged 10-12 in primary school

### Learning Goals
- data production
- understanding the relationship between data and the world
