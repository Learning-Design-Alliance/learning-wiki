# Telling the story as a tool in the mathematical toolbelt

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Redish identifies "telling the story as part of the mathematical toolbelt" developed across his paper series, treating storytelling as a learnable skill alongside the series' other tools. He makes its use explicit by assigning it an icon — a reading terrapin — which he uses "on slides, text, and problem solutions" whenever a story is told to set up, solve, or check a problem. Case studies in the research literature suggest that being explicit about epistemological issues can have a powerful impact, even crossing disciplinary boundaries.
