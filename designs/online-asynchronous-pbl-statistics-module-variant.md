# Asynchronous online PBL variant of the Maastricht MSc Public Health statistics module

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An 8-week online variant of a problem-based statistics module on multilevel analysis of longitudinal data and exploratory factor analysis, taken by 12 part-time students. It included "the asynchronous delivery of video recorded lectures and a series of individual SPSS activities designed to mirror the guided practice given to full-time students within the classroom". Classroom PBL tutorials were replaced by an asynchronous discussion board on Polaris facilitated by students themselves on a revolving basis. Students met face-to-face only in week 1 for orientation and week 8 for the compulsory examination, and accessed materials via Blackboard 8.1 (EleUM).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Video recorded lectures delivered in time and accessible on the learning platform
- Technical support and equipment testing prior to the module (internet speeds, microphone, camera, software)
- An initial face-to-face orientation meeting to foster commitment and cognitive presence
#### Constraints
- Some recorded lectures did not show PowerPoint slides clearly, so students needed printed handouts to see diagrams or equations
- Audio equipment failures occurred in the teaching room, though a separate microphone preserved the online recording

### Target Learners
- Part-time Masters students in public health working in healthcare

### Learning Goals
- Multilevel analysis of longitudinal data and exploratory factor analysis using SPSS
