# Four action lines — think, work, deduce, and present as Newton did — organize the advanced historical project

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This framework organizes an advanced historical-reconstruction project along four action lines: students 1) think as Newton did, by building step by step all his knowledge and reasoning; 2) work as Newton did, by performing the whole series of his original experiments with prisms; 3) deduce as Newton did about the nature of light and colours; and 4) present the results of their activity, including physics demonstrations, to the general public "in order to test abilities in communicating what learned and discovered". The article uses it as the design rationale for the whole project.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A historically rich topic that can be reconstructed from original sources
- Access to laboratory resources for reproducing historical experiments
- Opportunities for students to present to the public
#### Constraints
- The framework was developed for a small group of outstanding students, not standard classrooms

### Target Learners
- outstanding high-school or undergraduate science students

### Learning Goals
- historical scientific reasoning
- experimental practice in optics
- science communication to the public
