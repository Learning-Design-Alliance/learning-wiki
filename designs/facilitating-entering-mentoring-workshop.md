# Facilitating Entering Mentoring train-the-trainer workshop

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The facilitating Entering Mentoring (FT) workshop is an extensive, interactive, multifaceted train-the-trainer workshop that prepares local facilitators to implement research mentor training at their home institutions. Its goals are to "increase facilitator knowledge of an RMT curriculum", "increase facilitator confidence in implementing the curriculum", and "provide a safe environment to practice facilitation of curricular activities". The workshop was refined iteratively across three phases: the RCT, preparing for scale-up, and national scale-up.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Attendees practice facilitating core curricular activities, rotating in the facilitator role, and give and receive peer feedback during the workshop.
#### Constraints
- Formative data from each iteration informed the final length, structure, and content of the FT, with room for flexibility and contextualization.

### Target Learners
- faculty members, staff members, training grant directors, and postdoctoral trainees preparing to facilitate research mentor training

### Learning Goals
- small-group facilitation skills, knowledge of the RMT curriculum, and implementation of research mentor training at home institutions
