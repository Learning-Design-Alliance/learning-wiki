# Example SKIT scenario: traffic accident with suspected traumatic brain injury

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A worked example scenario in Box 1 that concretizes the SKIT method: an adult road-traffic casualty with suspected severe TBI moves from ED triage through resuscitation, head CT imaging, and handoff. "The learner-facing script specifies the case background, role identities, scenario layout, and task flow, embedding core challenges in clinical operations and doctor–patient communication." Roles include ED attending, triage, ED nurse, family communicator, and at-fault driver narrator, with three timed decision checkpoints and structured documentation outputs.
