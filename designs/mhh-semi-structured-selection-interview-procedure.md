# MHH semi-structured selection interview procedure with weighted total score (49% interview, 51% Abitur)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Hannover Medical School selection procedure ranked applicants on a weighted Total score from 0 to 15 points, calculated as 0.49 × Interview grade + 0.51 × Abitur grade, with the Abitur dominant by legal requirement. Two-physician committees evaluated four categories (personal qualifications, professional aspects, extracurricular activities, and a subjective overall impression), each weighted 25% of the interview grade, on a 0–15 scale, agreeing on a common score. Interviews targeted qualities "only inadequately reflected in high school grades or aptitude tests" and lasted at least 20 but no longer than 30 minutes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Committees of two MHH faculty physicians from different sections, with at least one clinically active doctor
- A pre-interview questionnaire providing interviewers information on school subjects, hobbies, vocational training or experiences as foreign exchange students
#### Constraints
- The interviews were not standardized, meaning that no uniform questions were asked to all candidates
- Committee-level common scoring means potential interviewer bias can only be investigated at the commission level, not at the individual level

### Target Learners
- medical school applicants preselected on high school grades

### Learning Goals
- assessment of social competence, societal engagement, motivation to study, and self-reflection
