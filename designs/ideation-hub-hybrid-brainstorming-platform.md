# Ideation Hub online brainstorming platform

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Ideation Hub is an online platform built for this study to run structured hybrid brainstorming sessions. It "provides task instructions for both individual and group phases, enforces time limits, and presents the necessary rules before and during each phase". It delivers condition-specific scaffolds: SCAMPER question prompts with principle descriptions and examples in the experimental conditions, and condition-dependent display or hiding of individually generated ideas during the group phase to manipulate cognitive group awareness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An online platform able to deliver timed phase instructions, display prompts, and control visibility of individually generated ideas across group members
#### Constraints
- The platform was designed specifically for this study and its scaffolds were implemented for a business-idea generation task on sustainable development

### Target Learners
- higher education students, mostly master's level, in agriculture and food sciences programs

### Learning Goals
- generating original business ideas addressing sustainability issues through divergent thinking
