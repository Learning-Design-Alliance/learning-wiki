# HybridLab algorithm-driven hybrid simulation learning method

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The HybridLab method at the Lithuanian University of Health Sciences combines distance learning on an e-learning platform with small-group peer-to-peer simulation training under remote instructor supervision. The article states the method "offers a well-structured and standardized learning pathway, which encompasses studies on an e-learning platform, peer-to-peer hands-on training sessions in the skill lab or simulation classes using carefully elaborated learning algorithms, direct feedback by peers, and assessment by a remotely working instructor". Learners in groups of three use tablets with proprietary educational software, electronic scenarios, checklists and learning algorithms, with 24/7 lab access and video-recorded sessions reviewed remotely by teachers who give feedback by email.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learners must pass an online test of 10 questions with a perfect score before starting practical training
- Training labs with installed cameras and hand-held tablets containing proprietary educational software, electronic scenarios and checklists are needed so training can proceed without a technician or instructor
#### Constraints
- The method was studied with 5th-year medical students and first-year Emergency Medicine and Anesthesiology residents learning endotracheal intubation

### Target Learners
- 5th-year medical students
- first-year residents in Emergency Medicine and Anesthesiology

### Learning Goals
- endotracheal intubation procedural skill
- safe airway management principles
- peer feedback and assessment skills
