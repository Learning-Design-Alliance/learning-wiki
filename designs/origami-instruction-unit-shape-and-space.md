# Four-lesson origami instruction unit on shape and space for preservice teachers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Origami instruction is "a lesson delivery where the teacher leads students to discover or deduce geometric properties, theorem, etc. from a resultant origami figure in the process of folding". In this study the experimental group received four two-hour lessons using six origami models (box, airplane, dart, swan, whale, butterfly), covering plane shapes, triangles, nets of solids, and lesson design, with peer teaching sessions in the final lesson. The control group received conventional poster-sketch and chalkboard instruction with realia but no construction of shapes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A teacher leads folding and unfolding phases with discussion of the shapes formed and their properties
- Time reserved in each lesson for students to summarize geometric vocabulary, concepts and properties
#### Constraints
- The unit was restricted to four lesson slots within a 16-week semester scheme of work

### Target Learners
- first-year preservice teachers at colleges of education

### Learning Goals
- spatial visualization and orientation
- geometric knowledge for teaching shape and space
