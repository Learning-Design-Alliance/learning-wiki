# Generative-model lesson pattern with mind mapping for physics concept lessons

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's lesson pattern pairs the generative model's syntax with mind mapping media: orientation prepares and motivates students, cognitive conflict presents facts and everyday problems that help students recognize misconceptions, disclosure has students express ideas based on prior and new knowledge, construct builds knowledge through experiment activities, application focuses on physics problem solving with feedback, and reflection-evaluation presents feedback to support improved outcomes. The article claims this pattern makes learning more active and connects content to everyday events and phenomena.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher facilitation at each syntax stage, including challenging questions, experiment activities, and feedback
#### Constraints
- Tested only on motion dynamics material in Class XI at one school; the design did not fully control external variables, as the article notes for quasi-experimental designs

### Target Learners
- Class XI senior high school physics students

### Learning Goals
- Meaningful understanding of physics concepts
- Problem-solving skill application in physics
