# 4-hour modular simulation-based obstetric ultrasound training course (U/S Mentor, 123 core tasks, 4 modules)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A short-term simulation training course designed "to enable residents to conduct detailed mid-trimester obstetric ultrasound scans", built from 123 core tasks defined by ISUOG guidelines and organized into 4 task modules (head/face, fetal heart, neck/chest/abdomen, spine/limbs/placenta etc.). Trainees deliberately practice one hour per module on the U/S Mentor simulator in groups of 3–4 with instructor demonstration, on-demand feedback, and real-time 3D anatomical guidance and probe-orientation feedback. It can be introduced before clinical practice or as refresher training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Uniform content across centers based on ISUOG guidelines; same simulator with uniform software versions and identical virtual patient cases; standardized didactic lecture (1 hour) and 30-minute online instructor training; supervised small groups of 3–4 with a standardized checklist covering all core tasks
#### Constraints
- Course focused on basic operational skills and excluded abnormal obstetric scenarios; class hours were allocated based on practicality rather than systematic difficulty assessment; findings limited to skill changes within simulated environment

### Target Learners
- First-year residents without obstetric ultrasound training experience
- Residents with more than one year of hands-on clinical training

### Learning Goals
- Obtaining standardized mid-trimester obstetric ultrasound planes
- Organized scanning thinking and basic probe-handling operational skills
