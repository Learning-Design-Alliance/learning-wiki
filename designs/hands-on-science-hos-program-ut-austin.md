# Hands on Science (HoS) undergraduate inquiry-based science content program for preservice elementary teachers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The HoS program at the University of Texas at Austin is a required four-semester science course sequence for elementary education students, based on the Physics and Everyday Thinking framework and organized around big ideas in Matter and Energy (physics, chemistry and geology, biological systems, astronomy and earth science). Classes use "teacher-directed or guided inquiry" in which students pose and investigate scientific questions by collecting, organizing, and analyzing data, formulating and evaluating explanations, and sharing and justifying them with peers. The instructor does not lecture but probes student knowledge and offers helpful nudges, working with students in small groups and whole-class discussions.
