# Quantum in Pictures course materials: abridged textbook, filmed blackboard lectures and exercises with filmed solutions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The pilot course's materials consisted of "a course textbook, which was a slightly abridged version of the book Quantum in Pictures", covering topics from states, processes and composition through quantum gates, teleportation, entanglement, the uncertainty principle and circuit optimisation. These were supplemented by filmed blackboard lectures covering all relevant material and exercises with filmed solutions. Many concepts were given alternative names that more directly resemble their pictorial incarnations and daily reality, with a translation provided at the end of the book.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students were asked to watch one video lecture per week in preparation for the weekly live tutorials
#### Constraints
- All course components were delivered online with pre-recorded lectures and exercise solutions

### Target Learners
- high school students without advanced mathematical prerequisites

### Learning Goals
- quantum states, processes, gates, circuits, entanglement, measurement and quantum protocols
