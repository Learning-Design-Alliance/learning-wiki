# Event Horizon: portrait installation of cosmic ray scientists holding self-selected meaningful artifacts.

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Event Horizon, by Faisal Abdu'Allah with Maryam Ladoni, consists of photographic prints on canvas showing scientists "holding a self-selected meaningful object or artifact" to reveal something about themselves, including Yodh with a picture of his thesis advisor Fermi, Gaisser with the Victor Hess balloon picture, and Olinto with a thinking ball. ICRC attendees were invited to have their portraits added.
