# Three interchangeable introductions to the redeﬁned SI: constants list, base units in terms of constants, and experiment-based realizations

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article frames three interchangeable textbook introductions to the redefined SI, stating that "these three Proposals are interchangeable in terms of both the eﬀect on the system of units, and on satisfying all the advantages listed above". Proposal A presents the seven deﬁning constants; Proposal B lists the seven traditional base units defined via the constants; Proposal C defines commonly used units via realizing experiments. The pattern gives designers a menu of pedagogical framings that are physically equivalent.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All three framings must remain interchangeable in effect on the system of units and in satisfying the redeﬁnition's advantages
#### Constraints
- A shared introductory paragraph was added to each Proposal for the questionnaire and does not appear in the new SI brochure

### Target Learners
- beginning STEM students in senior high school and early college

### Learning Goals
- introducing the system of units and the redefined SI
