# Standardized simulation session structure of pre-briefing, brief simulation, and structured debriefing using the 4Es technique

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Each clinical scenario followed the same three-session structure: pre-briefing (10 min), simulation (10 min, capped at eight minutes of simulation), and debriefing (20 min), developed in accordance with the Standards of Best Practice for Simulation. The article states the brief encounter "serves primarily as a catalyst for deeper reflection during the 20-minute structured debriefing session, considered the core component of experiential learning." The debriefing used the 4Es technique (events, emotions, empathy, explanations) to promote reflective thinking, and scenarios were conducted in pairs with one student as midwife and one as pregnant woman.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Scenarios standardized for structure, duration, and cognitive/affective/communication demands, validated by six faculty experts using the Clinical Scenario Evaluation Form
#### Constraints
- Simulation limited to a maximum of eight minutes to avoid cognitive overload associated with longer high-intensity simulations

### Target Learners
- final-year midwifery students

### Learning Goals
- empathy
- privacy awareness
- reflective practice
- intrapartum clinical skills
