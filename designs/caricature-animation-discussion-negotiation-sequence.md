# Caricature-animation activity sequence ending in discussion and negotiation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A lesson-level design pattern in which each subject's activities culminate in a humorous caricature animation that students first respond to individually and then discuss. Students "write their first opinions related with this activity before participating to discussion environment", after which they enter environments where opinions conflict (discussion) and combine (negotiation), with an administrator messaging students to move the process along. Introductory animations at the start of each unit aim "to attract the attention of the student and to arouse the interest s of the students."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Discussion and negotiation environments separated from the control condition
- Administrator messages to close each discussion stage
- Time allocation beyond regular course hours for humorous activities
#### Constraints
- Applied in a computer-laboratory setting with one computer per student

### Target Learners
- 7th-grade secondary school students

### Learning Goals
- Science concept understanding and discussion skills
