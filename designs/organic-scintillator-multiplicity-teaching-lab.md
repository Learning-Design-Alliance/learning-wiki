# Advanced teaching laboratory on organic-scintillator multiplicity counting for nuclear nonproliferation assay

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An advanced instructional laboratory in which students "learn the fundamentals of a current International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) nondestructive assay technique." Students set up and calibrate an eight-detector trans-stilbene well counter with CAEN electronics and CoMPASS software, perform pulse-shape discrimination and light-output calibration with a 137Cs source, and apply signal-triggered timing gates to build combined neutron and photon multiplicity distributions for three sealed sources. The lab is "designed for nuclear engineering and physics students interested in organic scintillators, neutron sources, and nonproliferation radiation measurement techniques."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to sealed neutron sources (252Cf, PuBe, AmLi) and a 137Cs calibration source in a licensed source room
- Eight trans-stilbene detectors with PMTs, a CAEN DT5730S digitizer, high-voltage units, and CoMPASS acquisition software
#### Constraints
- Cross talk is acknowledged but "additional simulations outside the scope of this paper are required to fully account for cross talk in organic scintillator systems"
- Measurement times depend on source strength; the weakest source required a 30-minute acquisition

### Target Learners
- nuclear engineering and physics students in an advanced instructional laboratory

### Learning Goals
- operating a complex organic scintillator well counter
- pulse-shape discrimination
- neutron multiplicity counting for nondestructive assay
