# 5P reflection model: an iterative five-stage GenAI-aware reflection model

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The 5P Reflection Model is an iterative reflective model with five stages: Purpose, Process, Product, Pitfalls and Plan, with emotional factors considered across all stages. It synthesises features from experiential, transformative, reflective-practitioner, affective and self-regulated learning theories and incorporates the technological dimensions of GenAI. The article states it is proposed "to manage greater demand for reflection, ii) to address the limitations of the existing reflection models, iii) to consider technological aspects of GenAI."
