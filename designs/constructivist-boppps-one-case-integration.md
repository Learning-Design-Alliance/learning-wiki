# Constructivist, student-centered grounding of BOPPPS and “One Case to the End”

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article situates both pedagogies in constructivist learning theory, whose core philosophy it quotes as "Student-centeredness, fully leveraging students’ subjective initiative, and cultivating their proactivity and consciousness in exploring, discovering, and constructing knowledge." It argues BOPPPS supplies a complete process-oriented instructional structure while “One Case to the End” supplies teacher guidance and clinical context, so the two "effectively leverage their complementary strengths". This rationale frames the study's integrated design.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher training and guidance, since the method's implementation "relies heavily on effective guidance from teachers"
#### Constraints
- Each component alone has stated limitations: BOPPPS lacks stage-level implementation guidance, and the case method depends on sustained teacher facilitation

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students in medical education settings

### Learning Goals
- active knowledge construction
- critical thinking
- professional values
