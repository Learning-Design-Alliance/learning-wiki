# Emergency airway management blended learning course on openlearning.com with videos, manuals, quizzes and social networking discussions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The intervention artifact: course materials consisting of videos, manuals and quizzes made available through the openlearning.com learning management system, with social networking services used to enhance discussions among participants and instructors. Participants were given 12 days (estimated at 40 min per day) to go through the materials. Contents were standardized with the F2FL arm and validated by a modified Delphi expert panel addressing safety gaps identified in NAP4.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor availability to guide online discussions, since participants wanted a more active online facilitator role
#### Constraints
- Cross-contamination between arms remains a risk despite instructions not to share materials

### Target Learners
- junior doctors in emergency departments

### Learning Goals
- emergency airway management knowledge and practical skills
