# Alternating independent-level and instructional-level texts across tutoring lessons

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Within LLI, lessons alternate between "independent level" texts and more challenging "instructional level" texts. The instructional-level texts may also be taken back to students' classrooms or homes, extending the tutoring texts into classroom and home reading.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A supply of leveled texts at both independent and instructional levels for each student
#### Constraints
- Applied within a small-group tutoring model for early-grade struggling readers

### Target Learners
- struggling readers in grades K-2

### Learning Goals
- reading fluency and comprehension at increasing text difficulty
