# An undergraduate-level QIST topic and skill list extracted from industry employer needs, spanning ideal and physical qubits, algorithms, noise, coding, statistics, and research

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article presents an enumerated list of four topics (ideal qubits, physical qubits, quantum algorithms, noise sources) and six skills (coding, statistics and data analysis, troubleshooting, modeling, electronics, conducting research) for undergraduate QIST curricula. It explains: "In their analysis Fox, Zwickl, and Lewandowski [ 7] compiled a list of skills and knowledge sought by industrial employers. That list covers mostly graduate-level hirees. We have extracted undergraduate-level skills and topics for PUIs from it, and we have added prerequisite elements." The grain size is curriculum-wide content selection.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Skills such as coding and statistics can be embedded in courses that are not QIST-specific
- Research experiences may be "research-like" experiences within courses rather than traditional standalone experiences
#### Constraints
