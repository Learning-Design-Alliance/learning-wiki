# Adapted Swedish OMP workshop: a 1.5-hour interprofessional training with role-play, example video and laminated micro-skill cards

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 1.5-hour formal training session for physician and nurse clinical tutors in primary care, including a PowerPoint presentation on OMP key elements, an example video, and a role-play activity practised in pairs without an audience. The five micro-skills were translated into Swedish and adapted: 'Correct mistakes' was replaced with 'feedforward', and 'rule of thumb' was chosen instead of 'general rules' to "incorporate experience-based knowledge". Laminated cards outlining the micro-skills and example questions were provided, with 'teach rule of thumb' placed last to reinforce feedforward.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Ongoing tutorship of medical (year three or higher) or nursing (year two or higher) students at PHC centres with 8000 or more registered patients
- Material adapted for both physicians and nurses
#### Constraints
- Role plays practised in pairs without an audience because acting in front of others was a concern raised in needs assessment interviews

### Target Learners
- Clinical tutors (physicians and nurses) in primary health care

### Learning Goals
- Clinical tutoring skills including feedback, feedforward and eliciting student clinical reasoning
