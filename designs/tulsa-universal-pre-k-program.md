# Tulsa Public Schools universal pre-K program

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
A universal, optional, school-based pre-K program funded by the state of Oklahoma after its 1998 law. Tulsa pre-K teachers "are paid on the same scale as other public-­school teachers, student-­to-­teacher ratios are 10:1, and teachers are required to have a bachelor's de­gree and be early childhood certified." Classrooms are co-located in elementary schools or separate pre-K buildings, pre-K students attend the same number of school days as other public-school students, and about 68 percent of TPS pre-K students attended a full-day program. Any parent could enroll a child.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- State funding under Oklahoma's universal pre-K law
- Teachers with bachelor's degrees and early childhood certification
#### Constraints
- Participation is optional, so parents choose whether to enroll
- All Head Start students attended full day, but only about 68 percent of TPS pre-K students did

### Target Learners
- Four-year-old children in a universal, socioeconomically and racially diverse urban district

### Learning Goals
- School readiness in literacy, math, and prereading, prewriting, and premath skills
