# Profiling game: a gamified app for building profiles of a mystery user from data traces

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The first workshop centered on a gamified profiling app in which "students were guided to analyze various types of everyday data traces to construct and iteratively refine detailed profiles of a mystery social media user." Data traces mirrored those collected by social media services commonly used by children, such as listening to music, watching videos, posting images, and sharing locations. Student pairs worked together as new clues and question prompts were gradually unveiled, and finished profiles were shared on a virtual bulletin board to prompt whole-class discussion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pairing of students for collaborative work; teacher facilitation of question prompts and whole-class discussion via the virtual bulletin board feature
#### Constraints
- The game highlighted differences between human-made and automated profiling techniques, which were further elaborated only in the second workshop

### Target Learners
- 5th and 8th grade students (11-15 years old)

### Learning Goals
- Understanding of data collection, data traces, and profiling; connecting datafication concepts to everyday life
