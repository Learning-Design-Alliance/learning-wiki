# Epistemic agency model predicting that higher-agency assignments produce more disciplinary integration in student writing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes a theoretical model (Fig. 1) linking assignment-level epistemic agency to observable interdisciplinary integration in student writing. The model predicts that 'a post-lab writing assignment with high epistemic agency will exhibit more disciplinary integration,' evidenced by music and physics keywords co-occurring in proximity more frequently than in the activity prompt. Epistemic agency is fostered by assignment features encouraging authentic physics practices and communication to a designated audience.
