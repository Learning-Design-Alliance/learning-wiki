# H.E.S.S. Source of the Month blog as an online astronomy outreach format

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Source of the Month is a blog-like outreach format written by members of the H.E.S.S. collaboration, featuring "an interesting gamma-ray object each month" for the general public. The first blog was published in October 2004 and there have been more than 170 features since, with a hiatus between October 2013 and December 2015. It initiated H.E.S.S.'s online outreach presence.
