# West Point physics department accountability model

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A departmental quality-assurance arrangement described from the second author's teaching at the United States Military Academy: the required two-semester calculus-based physics sequence uses standardized curriculum and objective assessment tools, a new instructor training program stressing course objectives, pedagogy, and assessment, and repeated classroom observations by senior instructors and peers. The authors state "Documented accountability of the department toward instructors and the instructors toward students ensures that Army's requirements are met."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors go through a new instructor training program before teaching the introductory sequence, and each instructor is observed a number of times each semester
#### Constraints
- Context-specific: a required service course sequence backed by a five-year Army service obligation following a taxpayer investment of over $400,000 per graduate

### Target Learners
- West Point cadets taking the required calculus-based physics sequence

### Learning Goals
- specified physics learning objectives and mathematical and scientific maturity of future Army officers
