# Accel Kitchen: a university accelerator-facility outreach workshop for junior and senior high school students

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Accel Kitchen is a workshop series held in December 2018 and July 2019 at the Cyclotron and Radioisotope Center (CYRIC), Tohoku University, in which twelve junior and senior high school students per workshop participated in the ongoing francium production and laser-trapping experiment for two days. The article describes it as "the first opportunity for junior and senior high school students to know about the particle beam experiment in Japan." Participants worked in three groups supported by researchers and undergraduate mentors, and the workshops contributed to the CYRIC Fr experiment, including the first Fr trapping using Group C's optimum magnetic field.
