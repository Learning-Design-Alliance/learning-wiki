# Brain Based Teaching Approach (BBTA) with three instructional techniques

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The Brain Based Teaching Approach is grounded in Caine and Caine's Brain Based Learning Principles and implemented through three instructional methods. The article defines them: "Orchestrated Immersion - makes a learning setting that fully engages students in many learning skills", "Relaxed Alertness - removes fear in the learners while preserving highly stimulating settings", and "Active Processing - lets the learner combine and assume material by actively practicing it". The study operationalized these three techniques in a 12-session EFL intervention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instruction must engage all three techniques: Relaxed Alertness, Orchestrated Immersion, and Active Processing
#### Constraints
- Study applied BBTA to young EFL learners (ages 13-16) in an Iranian language institute over 12 sessions

### Target Learners
- Young EFL learners aged 13-16

### Learning Goals
- Foreign language proficiency
- Self-efficacy
