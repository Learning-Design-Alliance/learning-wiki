# Two-phase session design: playful narrative simulation followed by modular debriefing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The iBuddy session is designed in two phases: (a) a highly engaging narrative-based playful simulation playable with all age groups, and (b) a flexible modular debriefing adjustable to the age group and to questions emerging in each session. The debriefing has two steps: behind-the-scenes modules (what happened, emotions, technical features, data deletion) and real-life modules (how the internet works, personalization, advertising, data value, profiles, terms of use, alternatives), combined usually 3 or 4 at a time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Half of the session time is devoted to debriefing
- Cliffhangers or pointers to the debriefing are integrated during the simulation design
- A presenter leads and an operator prepares safe custom questions
#### Constraints
- The debriefing path depends on participants' questions, age group and available time
- The debriefing is less interactive and engaging than the simulation

### Target Learners
- students aged 11–20 in secondary, high school and university classes

### Learning Goals
- privacy awareness
- understanding of data collection and processing
- uptake of privacy-safe behaviors
