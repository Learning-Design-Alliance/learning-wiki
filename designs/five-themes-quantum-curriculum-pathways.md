# Five thematic pathways for organizing quantum mechanics curriculum content

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The curriculum's roughly 80 articles can be read in a variety of orders; the authors "identified five different pathways associated with different themes or approaches to the material". The themes are "physical", "mathematical", "historical", "informational" and "philosophical", each emphasizing a different aspect of quantum mechanics. Students or instructors choose a theme on the home page, which automatically arranges the articles for that theme in an appropriate order, and each article lists prerequisites and further reading so users can set out their own path.
