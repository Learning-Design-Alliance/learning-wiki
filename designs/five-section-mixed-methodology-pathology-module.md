# Five-section module structure combining expository, case-study, and step-by-step WSI-analysis designs

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The module is organized into five sections, each using a different instructional approach suited to its content. The article states "The learning module is divided into 5 sections: bullous diseases, cutaneous drug reactions, psoriasiform reaction patterns, lichenoid reaction patterns and a general inflammatory skin pathology test." The bullous diseases section uses a classic expository structure (epidemiology, pathogenesis, clinical features, microscopy, immunofluorescence, differential diagnosis, treatment) with tests to consolidate learning; the drug-reaction and psoriasiform sections use case-study methodology; the lichenoid section uses step-by-step WSI analysis with questions, feedback, and progressive disclosure of clinical information until a final diagnosis is reached.
