# LaparoscopyBoxx home trainer with SurgTrac instrument tracking and the LS-CAT self-assessment form

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The study's training and measurement setup combined three components: the low-budget wooden LaparoscopyBoxx box trainer, the SurgTrac tablet software that tracked red and blue instrument markers, and the validated LS-CAT self-assessment form. SurgTrac "measured the following parameters: time of executing a task (in seconds), distance traveled by the left and right marking (in meters)" plus off-screen, speed, acceleration, smoothness and handedness. The LS-CAT rates four sub-tasks on a one-to-four scale, where "a low total LS-CAT score indicated better performance".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A tablet or smartphone camera positioned through the box opening as the laparoscopic screen
- A 5 mm needle holder, grasper and scissors and a suturing pad on an exercise board
#### Constraints
- The authors note objective parameters may not necessarily reflect the quality of the laparoscopic sutures

### Target Learners
- Medical students and junior doctors training at home

### Learning Goals
- Laparoscopic suturing technique with objective performance feedback
