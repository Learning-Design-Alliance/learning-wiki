# Shooting module: homemade coil (solenoid) with relay-selected resistance launching the latch by self-induction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The second module is "the construction of a homemade coil that launches a latch, using concepts of electromagnetic induction, to hit the sphere". It comprises relays, resistors, and a voltage source (typically 12V and 2 A) connected to a solenoid. The force category selects a relay: low force adds a high resistance pot in series, medium a low resistance pot, and high connects the solenoid directly to the source. The current through the solenoid lags the voltage change, so its self-induction pulls the latch; alternatively, relays charge capacitor banks whose discharge feeds the coil.
