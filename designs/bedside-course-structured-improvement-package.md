# Structured improvement package for a bedside course: scripts, lecturer briefing and interactive e-learning cases

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A bundle of course revisions introduced in WS 13/14 in a fourth-year internal medicine bedside course at TU Munich. It included scripts containing "medical history sheets as well as instructions on how to carry out patient interviews", a lecturer briefing in morning meetings based on the scripts, and voluntary e-learning cases for preparation and follow-up. The package was developed from student and lecturer evaluation feedback and was associated with improved self- and external assessments of clinical abilities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Definition of global and specific learning objectives and revision of course content
- A contact person who regularly reminded and helped supervise the courses
#### Constraints
- E-learning cases were recommended only as a voluntary form of preparation and follow-up
- The article states it cannot be conclusively decided whether improved performance was only due to the improvement measures

### Target Learners
- Fourth-year medical students in internal medicine bedside courses

### Learning Goals
- History-taking and physical examination techniques
- Diagnostic and therapeutic clinical reasoning
