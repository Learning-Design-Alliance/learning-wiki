# Author-designed educational games built with three iPad applications (TINY TAP, Quiz Maker, Puzzle Maker) plus a teacher guidebook

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The author designed educational games using iPad applications specialized for designing games in templates, permitting adding cognitive content, images and maps for the (Resources of my Country) unit. The study comprises three applications: "TINY TAP", "Quiz Maker" and "Puzzle Maker". Games were reviewed by specialists and experienced teachers, piloted in another school, and applied by the class teacher with four iPads shared among four student groups.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Games were designed following preparation, design, and production/development stages including task, learners' and content analysis
- A teacher guidebook explaining the teaching method was provided
#### Constraints
- Only four iPads were available; Quiz maker lacks a sharing option so questions were created on one device connected to a data show

### Target Learners
- sixth-grade primary students in Jeddah

### Learning Goals
- social studies knowledge and concepts of the Resources of my Country unit
