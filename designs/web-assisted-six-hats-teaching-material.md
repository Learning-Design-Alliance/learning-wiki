# Web-assisted six thinking hats teaching material for the Human Beings and the Environment unit

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A researcher-developed software package covering ecosystems, biological variety, and environmental problems. The material opens with animations to attract interest, presents lecture content in written and visual formats "prepared according to the dual coding theory", and embeds the six thinking hats after each sub-topic via animation. Students select the hat matching their thinking style and answer multiple-choice questions in an assessment section.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A computer laboratory where each student works on a personal computer equipped with headphones
- The material uploaded to the computers before implementation
#### Constraints
- Designed for the Turkish secondary school 'Human Beings and the Environment' unit content

### Target Learners
- 7th-grade secondary school students

### Learning Goals
- Understanding ecosystems, biological variety and environmental problems
- Multi-directional thinking about science topics
