# Four-phase gamified Medication Contest combining theory, escape room, online quiz and timed clinical simulation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The Medication Contest is a gamified educational innovation structured in four phases: a theoretical session with a practical medication preparation laboratory and an "Online Escape Room designed to simulate clinical scenarios interactively"; an online 30-question contest via a web platform from which the 50 best performers advanced; selection of the top nine students; and a final in-person competition with "advanced clinical simulations" under timed conditions assessing calculation, preparation and administration. The design follows principles of intelligent teaching based on prior knowledge, motivation, practical application and self-monitoring.
