# Jiao Tong Global Virtual Classroom: a specifically updated blended classroom with four large screens, two HD webcams and managed Zoom focus

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course joins the Jiao Tong Global Virtual Classroom Initiative, making it "open to global students in Association of Pacific Rim Universities at the same time with the offline students". The classroom "has been updated specifically", with "the additional use of four large screens and two HD webcams": three 85-inch screens at the back for teachers, a 75-inch front screen for offline students to interact face-to-face with online peers, one webcam auto-tracking the teacher's blackboard writing, and one panoramic webcam. A teaching assistant manages three Zoom accounts, using 'focus for everyone', 'replace focus video' and 'add focus video' to give close-up views of interacting students.
