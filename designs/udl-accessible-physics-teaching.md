# Universal Design for Learning as illustrated by accessible physics teaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article invokes Universal Design for Learning (UDL) as the framing for a key lesson of its experience: accommodations made for a blind student improved the course for everyone. The authors write that "all students benefit when the instructor clarifies concepts in class or use auditory and tactile aids, thus demonstrating one of the tenents of Universal Design for Learning (UDL)". In their account, oral explanation of board content, tactile and 3D graphical aids, and sonified or tangible demonstrations are not disability-only measures but general instructional improvements that sighted students also appreciated enthusiastically.

## Design Implications

### Learning Goals
- conceptual understanding of physics conveyed through multiple auditory, tactile, and visual channels
