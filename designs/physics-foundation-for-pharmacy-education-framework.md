# Physics as a foundational framework for pharmacy education mapping physical principles to pharmaceutical applications

## Description
The review presents physics as "the backbone for numerous applications in pharmacy education, providing fundamental insights into the physical principles governing pharmaceutical systems." It maps specific physics topics onto pharmacy functions: fluid dynamics and Poiseuille's Law to drug dispersion and intravenous delivery; thermodynamics and the Gibbs free energy equation to drug stability and solubility; spectroscopy and the Beer-Lambert law to determining drug concentration; nuclear magnetic resonance and the Larmor frequency to MRI; and quantum mechanics and the Schrödinger equation to molecular docking and drug design.
