# PBL-SP combined teaching design with preclass questions and standardized teacher training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A combined instructional design in which Problem-Based Learning is paired with standardized patients: students receive 6–9 thought-provoking questions one week before class, preview and discuss in groups, then during class the teacher presents key points, demonstrates clinical diagnostics through the SP, and guides discussion of the preclass questions. The article reports it "enhances student engagement, clinical thinking, and overall educational outcomes" in orthopedic residency training. Teachers and SP scripts were standardized across groups.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers trained in designing guiding questions, organizing group discussions, and SP-simulated clinical reasoning
- A unified teaching syllabus to ensure consistency in teaching content
- Trained nonmedical volunteers serving as standardized patients following a unified script
#### Constraints
- Delivered online via Tencent Meeting owing to the COVID-19 pandemic
- Requires more time and effort from students than traditional teaching, per the authors
- Subtle differences in individual teaching styles may still influence students' classroom experiences

### Target Learners
- Orthopedic and traumatology residents in standardized residency training

### Learning Goals
- Clinical reasoning and handling of common orthopedic diseases
- Self-directed learning and preclass preparation
- TCM holistic diagnostic reasoning in orthopedics
