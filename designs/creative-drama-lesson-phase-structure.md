# Creative drama lesson structure: preparation/warm-up, improvisation in groups, and relaxation phases

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's example drama lesson is organized into named phases: "Preparation" (warm-up with music and freeze-animation of communication tools), "Improvisation" (students with the same tools "gather in the same groups" and "create a scene with these tools", guided by open-ended questions), and "Relaxation" (sharing feelings, walking to music, muscle relaxation). Planning accounted for "creative drama components like; preparation, play, improvisation, relaxation" plus drama techniques, dramatic moment, pretending, teacher and student roles, group works and playlike processes.
