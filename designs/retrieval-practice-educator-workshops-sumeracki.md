# Educator workshops on retrieval practice as the authors' science-communication vehicle

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors report that their efforts to increase science communication with educators "have included workshops", through which they engage educators in conversation about implementing retrieval practice. These workshops are the setting in which the three recurring concerns about workload, scope of usefulness, and test anxiety surfaced, and in which the authors attempted to address them by discussing nuances of the retrieval practice literature. The article describes the workshops as the experiential basis for its commentary.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
