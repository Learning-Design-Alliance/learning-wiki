# Flipped Classroom Instruction as a learner-centred model shifting direct instruction out of the group space

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
FCI is described as a learning approach in which "teachers shift direct learning out of the large group learning space and move it to the individual learning space", devoting class time to student-centred, active learning strategies. The article presents it as grounded in Bloom's taxonomy, spanning lower-order and higher-order reasoning, and as compatible with Communicative Language Teaching and task-based language teaching. It is used in this study as the treatment for the experimental writing classes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructional videos or preview materials must be available to learners before class
- Class time must be reallocated to practice, application, and interaction rather than lecture
#### Constraints
- The article notes some researchers have viewed FCI as similar to traditional instruction in terms of learners' achievement and performance

### Target Learners
- higher education EFL students
- sophomore university writing students

### Learning Goals
- EFL writing skills
- learner autonomy and motivation
