# Teach quantum information processing in upper-secondary informatics to present a fundamentally different information carrier and link vector analysis with gate and truth-table notation

> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article argues that the binary discretization of information is less self-evident than today's teaching suggests, and that quantum information lets students encounter a fundamentally different information carrier. It calls QIP "ein gutes Bindeglied zwischen der Vektoranalysis der Oberstufe und der Gatter- und Wahrheitstabellenschreibweise der Informatik," with advantages and disadvantages versus classical bits worked out in a few double periods.
