# Instructor practices as moderators of assessment-mode participation gaps

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article frames student participation in research-based assessments as jointly determined by testing environment and instructor practices, examined through faculty interviews and a randomized testing-condition design. It treats makeup policies, email reminder frequency, and extra credit as the actionable levers that explain why some instructors' online participation rivalled paper rates while others' collapsed. As the paper states, it examines "how testing environments and instructor practices affect participation rates and identify best practices for future use."
