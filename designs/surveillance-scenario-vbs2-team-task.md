# The Surveillance Scenario: a scalable two-person military surveillance team task in VBS2

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The Surveillance Scenario is a team task in Virtual Battlespace 2 (VBS2) in which "The Surveillance Scenario requires a team of two. Each member stands atop a building and is responsible for surveillance of a 180-degree zone." Members scan for enemies, verbally and by keypress alert each other to crossings (Transfer), acknowledge alerts (Acknowledge), and identify transferred enemies (Identify); unannounced identification counts as backup behavior. It follows an Event-Based Approach to Training model, is scalable in difficulty by changing enemy numbers, and was designed as an open-ended research platform agnostic of tutoring approach.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A task requiring tutorable skills and enabling assessment; communication implemented through structured language or keypresses so it can be measured
#### Constraints
- The current tutor "was not developed to use contextual markers to distinguish between true backup behavior and a fortuitous identification"

### Target Learners
- two-person teams in military surveillance training

### Learning Goals
- observation, communication, acknowledgment, identification, and backup behavior
