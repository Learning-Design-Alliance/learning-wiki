# Constructivist nutrition curriculum design: six lessons combining active learning, prior knowledge, group work and socio-cultural connections

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's curriculum comprises "six one-hour lessons" covering food groups, analyzing influences on eating, food labels, health claims, body image and fast food restaurants. Lessons "position students as active learners, have them connect the new content with their prior knowledge, encourage students to work in groups and share their work, discuss, and debate with their peers, and connect new content with students' lives outside of schools." Experienced health educators reviewed each lesson against these constructivist criteria.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Lessons must be reviewed to ensure students are actively learning, that new information connects with prior knowledge, that social interaction is maximized, and that lessons involve students' lives in school, at home and in the community.
