# Sequenced build-up from tuning curve to locking: observing the cavity reflection phase shift before implementing Pound-Drever-Hall feedback

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The experiment is structured as a cumulative sequence: students first observe the oscillator tuning curve, then the cavity resonance and coupling conditions, then the real and imaginary parts of the reflection coefficient via mixing, then modulation sidebands, and finally implement the Pound-Drever-Hall technique. The article states that directly observing the phase shift "reinforces" the basis of the technique, since this observation is difficult in the optical domain due to short wavelengths.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- RF equipment that permits direct observation of the cavity reflection phase shift with an unmodulated source
- An adjustable delay line or phase shifter to set the reference phase for observing Re(Γ) and Im(Γ)
#### Constraints
- In an abbreviated single session the authors omit the investigation of voltage controlled oscillator modulation and the Pound-Drever-Hall error signal, locking instead with the interferometric technique

### Target Learners
- senior undergraduate physics students

### Learning Goals
- understanding the phase change in the cavity reflection coefficient that underlies Pound-Drever-Hall locking
- connecting frequency modulation to sideband generation
