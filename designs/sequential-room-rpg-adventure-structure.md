# Sequential-room adventure structure: tutorial room, concept rooms in progression, and a final debriefing room

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The complete adventure O Efeito FotoNewtoniano is organized as "4 salas sequenciais, sendo a última o encerramento da partida": an introductory narration, challenge rooms where the game actually happens, and a final room where the Master leads closing considerations. The group must solve each room's problem to advance, and "Os conhecimentos adquiridos em cada uma das salas serão de suma importância para o entendimento dos desafios posteriores", so knowledge progresses with the game. Room 1 works wave-particle duality as a tutorial; Room 2 targets that light intensity does not change the energy needed to stop the electron flow; Room 3 targets that light frequency (color) does.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each room's puzzle must be solved before the group can advance to the next room
- A chalkboard in each room carries the instructions for the group
#### Constraints
- The battery answer key per color (1 to 7 batteries from red to violet) and the three key-releasing colors are stipulated by the design group and can be altered by whoever masters the game

### Target Learners
- secondary and higher-education physics students working in small groups

### Learning Goals
- understanding the photoelectric effect: intensity versus frequency dependence of electron emission
- wave-particle duality
