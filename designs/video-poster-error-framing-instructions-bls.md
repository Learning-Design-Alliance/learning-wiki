# Standardized video and poster error-framing instructions embedded in Peyton 4-step BLS training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The intervention consists of short video and poster instructions presented before the independent practice step of a BLS training taught interprofessionally according to the Peyton 4-step approach (Demonstration, Deconstruction, Comprehension, Performance). EM instructions framed errors as "an important part of the learning process"; EA instructions told learners to try to avoid errors and think in advance how to do so. The control group received no error instructions. Practice occurred on ResusciAnne manikins with tutor feedback and a feedback device.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Standardization of the instructions via video and posters installed visibly in the training rooms
#### Constraints
- The study intervention was rather short, and it was not recorded whether or how long participants paid attention to the instructions

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate healthcare students in a mandatory introductory emergency medicine course

### Learning Goals
- correct chest compression depth and rate in basic life support
