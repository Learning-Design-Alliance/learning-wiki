# Contextual and structural approaches as two contrasting methods of vocabulary teaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article contrasts two vocabulary-teaching frameworks. The contextual approach teaches "the meanings of new words by having them used in different contexts surrounding the words", drawing on referent words, synonyms, antonyms, hyponyms and restatement, and distinguishes instructional from natural context. The structural approach "is based on the morphological analyses of the word", breaking words into prefixes, root and suffixes so learners generate and modify words across parts of speech. The experiment operationalized both as validated lesson plans taught by trained teachers.
