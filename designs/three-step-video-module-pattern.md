# Three-step online module pattern: animated theory videos, interactive video practice, gamified assessment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The online module addressed each of its five communication domains through a repeated three-step structure: the article states "Each domain was addressed though a three-step process" — theory acquisition via animated cartoon-style explanatory videos created with Canva, practice through video-based training with interactive videoclips of acted-out doctor-patient conversations whose course could be influenced by mouse click, and a gamified knowledge assessment with a multiple-choice quiz, a video annotation exercise, and a real-time leaderboard intended to foster motivation and friendly competition.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Animated explanatory videos, interactive videoclips, and gamified tasks delivered via the OpenOlat platform
- A completion gating feature to ensure students fully watch the videos
#### Constraints
- Instructions to the video annotation task were reported by students as somewhat unclear, which the authors state might bias quantitative results

### Target Learners
- Second-year medical students

### Learning Goals
- Understanding and application of patient-centered communication techniques (WEMS, NURSE) and communication structure
