# Complementary-mechanism account of AI-assisted PBL-CBL effectiveness: AI standardization plus PBL inquiry plus CBL immersion rationalizes cognitive resource distribution

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes an explanatory account of why the combined model works: "The core advantage of this teaching model lies in the complementary advantages of AI technology combined with PBL-CBL teaching methods." AI provides "standardized clinical simulation scenarios and real-time accurate feedback," PBL contributes problem-oriented inquiry and thinking training, and CBL contributes immersive teaching with real cases. The article further states that the integration "rationalizes cognitive resource distribution during learning," cutting down invalid cognitive interference and concentrating learning resources on absorption of key clinical competencies, while AI-generated virtual patient scenarios allow repeated clinical training that strengthens clinical pattern recognition without the restrictions of limited teaching resources.
