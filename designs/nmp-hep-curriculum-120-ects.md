# NMP-HEP curriculum: 120 ECTS over four semesters combining theory, experimental methods, computing and research training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An English-taught two-year, 120 ECTS curriculum structured over four semesters, integrating "theoretical knowledge, experimental competencies, computational skills, and research experience". Table 1 lists five components: Theoretical Foundations (e.g. Standard Model, BSM), Experimental Methods (Particle Detectors I & II, DAQ I & II), Computing and Data Analysis (Monte Carlo Simulations, Computational HEP), Research Training (Research Practice, Dissertation), and Transferable Skills (Ethics in Research, Programming, VHDL).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivery across four partner universities requires harmonised learning outcomes, assessment procedures and mutual recognition of assessment results
#### Constraints
- National legislation permits up to 60% of course hours and 35% of practical and research activities online, shaping the online/face-to-face balance

### Target Learners
- Master's students in high-energy physics

### Learning Goals
- Advanced particle-physics theory, detector and data-analysis skills, and research experience aligned with contemporary collider physics
