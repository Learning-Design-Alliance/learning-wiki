# An atmospheric-physics course should build professional aptitudes for answering concrete environmental and meteorological questions of engineering practice

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article argues the environmentally empathic engineering professional should be able to estimate the consequences of their work, and that "una materia basada en Física de la Atmósfera deberí a proporcionar aptitudes profesionales para dar respuestas a preguntas" about emissions, particle size distributions, pollutant drag parameters, ENSO-phase effects on open-air works, solar radiation effects on contaminants and tropospheric ozone, solar resource quantification, and solar-activity effects on telecommunications and power distribution.
