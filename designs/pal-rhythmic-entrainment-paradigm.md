# Time-resolved Paired Associative Learning paradigm with rhythmic tone-flash sensory entrainment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A novel PAL paradigm in which each trial begins with 16 transient task-irrelevant tone-flash stimuli presented "brieﬂy (30 ms) and isochronously at a rate of 5 Hz ... or 10 Hz" before a novel speech-print pair, followed by dense sampling of stimulus onset asynchronies (200–980 ms, 20 ms steps) so that frequency-dependent behavioral effects of rhythmic stimulation can be measured. Five pseudo-phones crossed with five Korean characters generated the learning stimuli, learned via feedback over 10 blocks of 80 trials. A null-stimulation control task replaced entrainment with a single salient visual stimulus.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A unimodal familiarization phase ensuring successful recognition (> 70%) of the learning materials before associative learning.
#### Constraints
- Studied with right-handed native Chinese speakers with no Korean experience; the design does not include EEG, which the authors say is needed for fine-grained temporal dynamics.

### Target Learners
- healthy adults learning novel speech-print mappings

### Learning Goals
- paired associative learning of speech sounds and written symbols

### Claims

- [Alpha-rate (10 Hz) but not theta-rate (5 Hz) sensory stimulation improves accuracy of speech-print associative learning compared to a null-stimulation control](../claims/alpha-stim-improves-speech-print-associative-learning.md) [~W]
