# Three-tier system of priority forms of educational work for civil activity formation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article enumerates a system of priority and effective forms of work of a modern higher education institution for forming students' civil activity, organized in three tiers: "search (interview, situations of civil dialogue, business and role-playing games, debate)"; converting and reflexive forms (academy of the young citizen, school of social development, personal growth trainings, school of leadership, youth forums, project work); and practice-oriented forms (patriotic propaganda teams, volunteering). Practical coordination of all these forms was carried out by the department of educational work and employment of the Taganrog institute of A. P. Chekhov.
