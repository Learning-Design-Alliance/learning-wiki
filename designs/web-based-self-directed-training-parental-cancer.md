# Web-based self-directed training on parental cancer with psychoeducational modules, exercises and expert videos, approximately 3 hours

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Intervention 2 of the trial is a self-directed online training developed concordantly with the face-to-face training. The article describes it as 'a self-directed web-based training that provides psychoeducational modules, exercises and questions to examine the individual level of knowledge', including video sequences of experts in parental cancer giving commentaries or case examples. Completion takes approximately 3 hours and module completion rates are used to assess training fidelity.
