# Stepwise LAS training pattern: lecture, pre-learning with videos and simulators, then hands-on live-animal practice

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A lesson-level instructional pattern for laboratory animal skills training in which each session follows a fixed sequence: a faculty lecture with handouts distributed in advance, a preparatory phase with training videos and mouse simulators used at students' discretion, and finally hands-on training with live mice. The article reports that "a stepwise program, including handout distribution, a lecture, pre-learning with alternative educational tools (videos and simulators), and hands-on training with live mice was conducted". The pattern was applied to habituation, restraint, and vaginal smear sampling with one mouse per 2–3 students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Alternative tools must remain available during both the pre-learning period and as review resources after live training
- Teaching assistant support during hands-on sessions
#### Constraints
- Hands-on training using live animals remains essential for mastering psychomotor techniques
- Utility of each tool may vary depending on learner prior experience, technique type, and student numbers

### Target Learners
- novice veterinary students with no prior live-mouse handling experience

### Learning Goals
- basic mouse handling techniques: habituation, restraint, and vaginal smear sampling for estrous cycle determination
