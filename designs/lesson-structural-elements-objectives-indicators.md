# Didactic objectives and performance indicators of the structural elements of the lesson

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
As part of Stage 2, the authors formulated didactic objectives and performance indicators for seven structural elements of the lesson: actualization of previous work, presentation of new information, guided practice, independent practice, self-control and self-assessment, summing up, and homework. Each stage pairs a didactic objective with a performance criterion, such as obtaining objective information on pupils' achievement of the expected results.
