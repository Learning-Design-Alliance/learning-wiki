# K.W.L instructional program and implementation manual for Islamic education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The researcher developed a manual to implement K.W.L in teaching Islamic education, built by content analysis of the two study units, reconstructing the units with the K.W.L strategy drawing on related literature (Atieh 2008, 2009; Mustafa and Muhammad 2016), and checking validity via expert judgment. The achievement test measuring religious concepts was judged valid by 12 experts and showed a KR20 reliability coefficient of 0.918 on a pilot of 40 students.
