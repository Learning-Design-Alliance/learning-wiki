# Internal topocentric perspective pattern: representing the sky as a dome viewed from inside rather than a globe or flat chart

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The dome is designed so celestial objects are "viewed from an internal perspective, under the dome", a topocentric (alt-azimuth) viewpoint that directly corresponds to the three-dimensional perspective of an Earth-based observer. The zenith is directly overhead rather than at the center of a flat drawing. This advantage is absent in globe-based representations, which misleadingly suggest the observer is outside a sphere of stars, and it offers a global view of the firmament in contrast to the fragmented perspectives of mobile applications.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Accurate astronomical data for the chosen location and date; assembly with stars facing inward
#### Constraints
- Highly immersive alternatives such as digital planetariums and VR provide accurate experiences but their high cost and limited availability restrict use in many educational settings

### Target Learners
- students learning observational astronomy
- teachers

### Learning Goals
- understanding the celestial sphere from the observer's viewpoint
- mapping cardinal points and zenith to the horizon
