# Six requirements for designing e-teaching materials for humanities disciplines in future teachers' training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article identifies and rationalizes a set of design requirements for e-teaching materials for humanities disciplines in future teachers' training. As stated, the article "identifies and rationalizes the requirements (integrative framework, gradual development of materials, marked structure, individualization of the teaching process, the strategic character of knowledge assimilation, openness of the system) for the design of e -teaching materials for the humanities disciplines in th e training of future teachers." The requirements respond to the finding that existing e-resources' structure and content "do not always meet the didactic requirements."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Design must follow the six named requirements: integrative framework, gradual development of materials, marked structure, individualization of the teaching process, strategic character of knowledge assimilation, and openness of the system.
#### Constraints
- The requirements were developed for humanities disciplines in the training of future teachers.
