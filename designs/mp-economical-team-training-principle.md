# Adopt mental practice as an economical, sustainable addition to surgical training, including shared mental rehearsal within teams

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article concludes that in designing surgical training, team building and safety procedures, "MP has potential to improve performance and its application is highly economical, sustainable and feasible with current resources", offering an alternative to expensive technology-based simulation. It further proposes that teams can share their practical envisioning derived through MP to improve collective performance, positioning MP as an information-sharing practice proximal to the task and related to shared mental models, anticipatory thinking and situational awareness. Unlike reflective practice's retrospective review, MP privileges cultivating foresight.
