# CASUS-based simulation of internist-radiologist collaboration for assessing information sharing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A computer-based simulation on the case-based learning platform CASUS in which learners, in the role of an internist, scan a patient file, request a radiologic test by filling in a request form choosing among 42 different combinations of methods and body parts, and share patient information or differential diagnoses. Only learners who appropriately justify their request receive the radiologic findings. It separately assesses information sharing (collaborative) and final diagnosis (individual).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A fiction contract and technical familiarization were added to increase perceived control after pilot testing showed low dependability ratings
#### Constraints
- Developed for the specific context of radiologic test requests identified as problematic in interviews with seven practitioners

### Target Learners
- medical students
- internists and residents

### Learning Goals

- diagnostic accuracy
