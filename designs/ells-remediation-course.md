# Essential Lifelong Learning Skills (ELLS) remediation course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The ELLS course is a remediation programme for medical students repeating their first semester, drawing on a blend of constructivist, sociocultural and complexity theories. "The ELLS course involves faculty-facilitated groups of six students meeting throughout a semester to work through a syllabus that integrates learning study skills and critical thinking with the content of basic science course material." The article reports it produced significantly improved long-term outcomes versus historical controls, with outcomes varying by teacher experience.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty-facilitated small stable groups meeting throughout a semester
- a syllabus integrating study skills and critical thinking with basic science content
#### Constraints
- Designed for students who failed and repeated their first semester at one medical school with lower admissions criteria

### Target Learners
- at-risk medical students repeating their first semester

### Learning Goals
- lifelong learning skills
- critical thinking
- self-regulation
- basic science mastery
