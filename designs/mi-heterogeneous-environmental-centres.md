# Multiple Intelligences heterogeneous learning centres for environmental topics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A four-week lesson design in which "the students were appointed to eight multiple intelligences heterogeneous centres" created according to MI principles. Each centre used a modality-matched activity: reading sessions, environmental-problem sounds and an adapted song, documentaries and drawing, games and drama, an environment club with classroom visits and information cards, empathy with pictures, field trips and tree planting, and internet investigation of local environmental change. Students then created projects and rotated through different centres.
