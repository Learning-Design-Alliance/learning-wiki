# Use short, low-cost DVD training to scale up essential breastfeeding support skills among large numbers of maternity staff

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article concludes that a brief video-based training method can address low baseline knowledge of positioning and attachment and hand expression among clinicians, and "may be suited to addressing training needs more widely in China due to its effectiveness on knowledge, and the feasibility and low cost to staff time for large numbers of staff". It recommends this approach where thousands of clinicians need training and conventional courses cannot reach them.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content must target the specific support skills clinicians lack, here positioning and attachment and hand expression
#### Constraints
- Only short-term knowledge and confidence effects were measured; effects on practice and breastfeeding outcomes require further study

### Target Learners
- doctors, nurses and midwives in maternity services in China

### Learning Goals
- knowledge and confidence in teaching mothers positioning and attachment and hand expression
