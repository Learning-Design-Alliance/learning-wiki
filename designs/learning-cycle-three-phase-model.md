# Learning cycle approach: a three-phase student-centered science teaching model

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The learning cycle approach is a student-centered teaching approach based on Piaget's mental development theory and first developed by Karplus, composed of three main sections: exploration, term introduction and concept implementation. In exploration, students collect and record data from observations, measurements and experiments while the teacher takes a passive role; in term introduction students define science concepts in their own words under teacher guidance; in concept implementation students apply the concepts to new situations. The article implements this model in 4th-grade science teaching over 7 weeks and 14 lessons.
