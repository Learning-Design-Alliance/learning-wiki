# Monash Online Learning Hub (MOLH)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Monash Online Learning Hub (MOLH) is a university-wide online orientation resource for students studying in online, blended, off-campus, distance, and flexible modes. Built in Articulate Rise 360 and embedded within the existing on-campus orientation platform (Monash Essentials), it comprises eight sections, including six content sections: Welcome to MOLH, Start Here, Learn Online, Study Effectively, Stay Well & Connected, and Graduation & Beyond. The site aims to consolidate existing resources, reduce duplication, and support student induction, belonging, and well-being.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Developed through a cross-faculty community of practice working group with an appointed Educational Designer leading content development.
#### Constraints
- Course-specific resources were excluded as beyond the project's scope; innovative ideas (e.g., AI and VR features) were set aside for feasibility and cohesion with the on-campus equivalent.

### Target Learners
- Online, blended, off-campus, distance, and flexible-mode university students

### Learning Goals
- Online learning preparation
- Digital literacy
- Academic and well-being support
- Sense of belonging and community
