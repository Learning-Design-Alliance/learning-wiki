# Capillarized training distributed across regional hubs adapting interventions to local needs

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The specialization course was distributed across six regional hubs in Santa Catarina (Florianópolis, Blumenau, Joinville, Criciúma, Chapecó, and Lages), a design the authors call "capillarization." The article states that "The capillarization of training allowed the adaptation of interventions to regional needs, promoting collaborative practices among different health professionals." Each hub's graduates produced interventions responsive to local epidemiological profiles, such as smoking cessation actions in Joinville and oral health monitoring programs in Lages.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multiple regional training hubs with local professional participation
#### Constraints
- Hub representation varied considerably, with Florianópolis at 24.92% and Lages at 6.23% of graduates

### Target Learners
- Health professionals in primary health care across diverse regional settings

### Learning Goals
- Adapting chronic disease interventions to regional needs
- Collaborative practices among different health professionals
