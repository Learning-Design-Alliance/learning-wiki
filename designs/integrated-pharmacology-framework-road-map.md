# Offer the integrated flipped case-based pharmacology design as a transferable framework for programs considering curricular change

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article presents its curricular redesign as a transferable framework: pharmacology content is distributed across organ-system courses over 15 months, delivered through flipped asynchronous videos, and practiced in collaborative case-based sessions with dedicated pharmacology faculty. The authors state, "We hope the framework presented here will serve as a road map to other medical or pharmacy schools, as well as nursing and physician assistant programs considering curricular changes." The design description is paired with item-statistic and satisfaction evidence from their own implementation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A discipline director with expertise in each basic science, plus dedicated pharmacology faculty responsible for all pharmacology content
- Team-written cases with answers to all questions provided in script form for facilitating faculty
#### Constraints
- Developed and evaluated at a single 4-year allopathic medical school
- Classical Test Theory item statistics are sample-dependent, so observed differences may reflect changes in student ability or assessment administration

### Target Learners
- Pre-clerkship medical students
- Medical, pharmacy, nursing, and physician assistant programs considering similar curricular changes

### Learning Goals
- Integration of pharmacology with other basic and clinical sciences
- Preparedness and confidence in pharmacology knowledge and drug-regimen selection
