# Six-step discovery learning cycle mapped onto the scientific approach and three-phase listening

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A lesson-level pattern in which the discovery learning cycle of "stimulation, problem statement, data collection, data processing, verification, and generalization" is sequenced onto the scientific approach processes of observing, asking, collecting information, associating, and communicating, and onto pre-, while-, and post-listening phases. The article reports the "sixth section of the core activities ... proven to improve students' English listening scores" in its discussion of the intervention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A planning phase defining objectives, materials and media, and an assessment phase, per the Kemdikbud implementation phases the article prints
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from a single one-group pretest-posttest class with no control group

### Target Learners
- tenth-grade high school students

### Learning Goals
- English listening comprehension
- social attitudes such as politeness, honesty, discipline, confidence, and responsibility
