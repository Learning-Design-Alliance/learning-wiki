# CineVR trauma training curriculum of 360-degree videos filmed from 6 provider perspectives

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A set of 360-degree video and audio (cineVR) trauma training videos filmed at Zuckerberg San Francisco General Hospital. The team "filmed 20 live and simulated high-level trauma resuscitations in one trauma bay" over 6 months, recording "from 6 different perspectives to capture the experiences of 6 different types of providers" (team leader, nurse, airway provider, attending physician, charting nurse, and patient). "The footage from 8 of these trauma scenarios was used to produce cineVR videos, 4 of which were further edited to include a trauma curriculum for trainees," with teaching-point overlays synchronized to video timing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A HIPAA-compliant production partner with data security infrastructure
- IRB approval and a consent process covering patients, providers, staff, and EMS personnel
- Equipment that does not obstruct patient care, tested iteratively with providers
#### Constraints
- Videos are location-specific: learners at other institutions will not obtain a sense of PREality from them

### Target Learners
- residents
- medical trainees
- trauma team members

### Learning Goals
- teamwork
- management of a trauma patient
- empathizing with the roles and priorities of different team members
- anticipating next steps in care planning
