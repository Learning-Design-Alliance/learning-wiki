# eIAYC 2020: an online alternative to the in-person IAYC with 63 participants and three activity strands

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The eIAYC 2020 was the International Workshop for Astronomy e.V.'s virtual alternative to its cancelled three-week in-person summer camp, running from 16 June until 30 August 2020 with 63 participants overall, roughly the size of a normal in-person camp. It consisted of three types of activities: an engagement series of talks and workshops, small independent research project groups, and a non-astronomical programme of social activities. Participation was restricted to former IAYC participants because the foremost goal was keeping the community engaged with the organisation.
