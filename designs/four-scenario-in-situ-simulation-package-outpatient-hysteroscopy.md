# Four-scenario in-situ simulation training package for outpatient hysteroscopy emergencies

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A training package of four simulation scenarios targeting oversedation, hemorrhage, local anesthetic systemic toxicity, and vasovagal reaction in the outpatient hysteroscopy setting. Each scenario "was designed to resolve within 15 minutes, with up to 45 minutes for debriefing." The package includes appendices with case progression tables, debriefing materials, a CRM primer, and a latent safety threats template.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Minimum of 1 gynecologist and 1 nurse per scenario
- Patient simulator (ACLS manikin), monitors, hysteroscopy tray, venous access supplies, oxygen supplies, and crash cart
#### Constraints
- Requires physical material resources, though low-fidelity alternatives are described
- Small number of roles may restrict participant numbers, though actors and debriefers can be divided

### Target Learners
- Interprofessional outpatient procedural teams including nurses, residents, and practicing gynecologists

### Learning Goals
- Recognize presenting signs and symptoms of hysteroscopy complications
- Initiate management of complications in an outpatient surgical suite
- Apply crisis resource management principles
- Identify latent safety threats
