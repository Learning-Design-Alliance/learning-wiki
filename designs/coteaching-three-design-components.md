# Co-teaching design built on three components: full-semester duration, pre-set course structure, and a collegial cooperative relationship

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's conclusion that its co-teaching design rested on three components: '1) it lasted an entire semester, 2) the course structure was set up in advance by the experienced instructor, and 3) there was a collegial, cooperative relationship between the co-teachers.' The relationship was colleague-to-colleague rather than mentor-mentee, and the authors argue a half-semester probably would not have sufficed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The co-teaching must span an entire semester; the authors argue co-teaching for only the first half probably would not have been enough.
#### Constraints
- Findings come from a single instrumental case study, which the authors caution against generalizing.
