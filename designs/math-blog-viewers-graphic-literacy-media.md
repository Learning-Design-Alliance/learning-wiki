# Mathematics blog viewership graphic as literacy media for statistical interpretation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article illustrates its literacy media with a website display of a mathematics blog's viewership. "The display in figure above describe the numbers of  viewers of mathematic blog per days, per weeks, and per months." Students interpret the histogram and summary figures, e.g., the biggest viewership leap on January 18th 2012 reaching 337 persons, 84 viewers that day, and 32,900 total since establishment.
