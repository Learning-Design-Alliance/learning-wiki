# Combined flipped classroom + PBL session pattern: pre-class videos and materials, pre-quiz, case-based small-group discussion, group presentations, and instructor summary

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
This pattern describes the article's own implementation sequence for a combined flipped classroom + PBL session in an endocrinology internship. Before class, students view instructor-prepared lecture videos and supplementary materials on their own time. In class, students complete a 32-item pre-quiz, hear a brief topic introduction, work through a patient case with questions such as "What is the pathogenesis of hyperthyroidism?", discuss in small groups with instructor guidance, and give group presentations before the instructor summarizes and reviews difficult questions. The session closes with a post-quiz identical to the pre-quiz and an 11-item perception survey.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires instructor-prepared pre-class lecture videos and supplementary materials, and a teaching group of one instructor plus three assistants to supervise group discussion
#### Constraints
- The article states further optimizations are required before the combined model can be widely accepted and implemented in other curricula

### Target Learners
- Fourth-year undergraduate medical students majoring in clinical medicine during endocrinology internship

### Learning Goals
- Apply basic theoretical knowledge of hyperthyroidism to analyze and solve clinical cases
