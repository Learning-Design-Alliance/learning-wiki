# SPECTRA: a district initiative supporting Princeton educators of color through training, relationships, and affirmation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
SPECTRA (Supporting Princeton Educators of Color through Training, Relationships, and Affirmation) is Princeton City Schools' initiative "dedicated to training, development, and affirmation for racially minoritized educators within the district." Developed through a multi-step exploration, ideation, and implementation process with district leaders and teachers, it gave teachers agency in addressing the scarcity of educators of color, including partnering with the Thurgood Marshall Fund to tour and spotlight the district for prospective candidates.
