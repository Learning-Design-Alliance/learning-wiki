# University of Rochester Pre-College Experience in Physics (PREP) summer program

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
PREP is a four-week summer program for 9th and 10th grade Rochester-area high school girls, on the University of Rochester campus since 1994, involving "interactive lecture and discussion learning, 6 hands-on lab activities", four major group projects, a teamwork day, a professional panel, and laboratory visits. It was taught by two female undergraduate science students. Evaluative surveys showed students responded positively to the role models, projects, laboratories, and interactive lecture style, and defended the program's single-sex format.
