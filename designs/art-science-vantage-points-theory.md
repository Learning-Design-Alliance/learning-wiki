## Description
The article frames its art-science collaborations with the belief that "a deeper understanding of something is gained by viewing it from multiple vantage points." Just as scientists look in new ways, the authors argue, "we must try new approaches to connect with diverse groups and reach different audiences." The theory treats artists' community iconography and immersive world-building as alternative lenses on scientific practice.
