# Pair service learning with structured reflection and multi-method pre-post evaluation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article attributes its ability to detect changes in knowledge, self-efficacy, and attitudes to a multi-method assessment strategy with anonymous pre- and post-programme surveys, plus "structured reflection sessions" in which students processed challenges and emotional responses. Instruments underwent expert content validity review (CVI = 0.92) and reliability reporting, and the authors recommend randomized controlled designs with objective performance measures and longitudinal follow-up for future studies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Validated instruments (content validity index ≥ 0.80), anonymous online pre-post administration, and structured reflection sessions integrated into the programme.
#### Constraints
- The single-group pretest-posttest design without a control group limits causal inference; all outcome measures were self-reported.

### Target Learners
- Fourth-year undergraduate dental students

### Learning Goals
- Professional knowledge, self-efficacy, and empathic attitudes toward serving vulnerable populations
