# Three-stage implementation of the model: propaedeutic, activity-based, and outcome-correction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The method of implementing the structural-functional model for teachers' professional development proceeds through three stages. At the propaedeutic stage the authors "defined the targets, objectives, methods of data collection, the evaluation criteria of teachers scientific and methodical work" and provided primary evaluation. At the activity-based stage the model content was introduced and teachers took part in the training program with monitoring. The outcome-correction stage analyzed monitoring data and corrected indicators, program content, organizational forms and monitoring tools.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pedagogical monitoring tools and evaluation criteria defined before the activity-based stage
- Correction of model components based on monitoring data at the outcome-correction stage
#### Constraints
- Applied within a professional training program for teachers working in secondary vocational institutions in the city of Ekaterinburg

### Target Learners
- teachers of secondary vocational institutions

### Learning Goals
- raising the level of knowledge, skills and professionally important qualities in scientific-methodical work
