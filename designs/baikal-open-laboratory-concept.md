# The Baikal Open Laboratory concept: an open-science education and instrument-development testbed for multimessenger astronomy

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Baikal Open Laboratory is a proposed educational and outreach unit focused on multimessenger astronomy and open science, resting on three pillars: development of open training programs, focus on modern IT and open-source solutions, and interaction between different facilities. It exploits the co-location of Irkutsk State University with the TAIGA gamma observatory and the Baikal-GVD neutrino telescope, which the authors call a unique environment "on-site". The neighborhood of experiments measuring different messengers and the large educational center naturally lead to the Baikal Multimessenger concept, a testbed for future multimessenger activity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cooperation with the TAIGA and Baikal-GVD facilities
- informational support from the GRADLCI framework and the astroparticle.online platform
- educational resources at ISU including its international schools
#### Constraints
- Data transfer between experiments can be complicated by data policies established by the different collaborations

### Target Learners
- university students in particle and astroparticle physics
- future experts in big data and deep learning for physics analysis

### Learning Goals
- training experts in astroparticle physics and multimessenger astronomy
- developing methods and techniques for multimessenger astronomy
