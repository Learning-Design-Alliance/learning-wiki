# Large-theater demonstration show format with rotated topics, super-sized demos, and a signature finale

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A reusable event format for physics outreach: a roughly 75-minute show targeted at elementary and middle school children, with topics rotated in and out of the set list on a three-year cycle, classic demonstrations, and a closing bed-of-nails finale. In a thousand-person theater the authors note it is important to "super-size" many of the demos, using a close-up camera for some acts and large student-built projects such as a giant pendulum snake.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A large venue with professional theater technicians, live video and a projection screen; student and volunteer help building equipment
#### Constraints
- Described for a thousand-seat theater context; the initial 175-person lecture-hall version used a simpler template

### Target Learners
- Elementary and middle school-aged children and their families

### Learning Goals
- Inspiring youth with physics through classic demonstrations
