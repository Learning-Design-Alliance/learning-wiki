# Conceptual understanding and procedural fluency as interdependent constructs developing reciprocally

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's framework for how the ROOTS curriculum supports mathematics development holds that focused whole-number content and explicit instructional design jointly impact two knowledge forms. The authors "consider conceptual understanding and procedural fluency as interdependent constructs that develop in tandem and reciprocally," citing Rittle-Johnson, Siegler, and Alibali (2001) and Wu (1999), and hold that these in turn impact overall mathematics achievement. ROOTS instruction is designed to build both forms simultaneously.
