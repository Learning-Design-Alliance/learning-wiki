# Integrative Teaching Consultation (ITC) as a framework combining student evaluations with professional consultation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Integrative Teaching Consultation is a team of pedagogues and psychologists headed by a medical doctor at the Medical Faculty of Heidelberg. Its stated aim is "to combine SETs with counseling and professional feedback in order to enhance the ability of teachers to translate the SET feedback into concrete measures of teaching behavior". The process runs through supported reflection and goal-setting, preparation, teaching observation with pre-defined aspects, and a feedback meeting in which SET results are considered. It draws on evidence on teaching and learning and principles of effective consultative feedback, including active involvement of teachers and teacher self-ratings.
