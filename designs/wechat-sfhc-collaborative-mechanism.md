# WeChat-based school-family-hospital collaborative communication mechanism (SFHC)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The SFHC is a WeChat-based teaching model establishing "an integrated educational community comprising nursing interns, their family members, clinical nurses, and physicians". It constructs "a closed-loop educational system featuring family participation in learning, medical-nursing linkage in guidance, and full-cycle tracking of intern training". Family members upload real case information and raise health questions; a three-person guidance team of a nursing teacher, an internal medicine doctor, and a surgical doctor guides interns to retrieve literature and answer questions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- WeChat groups connecting interns, family members, and a three-person guidance team
- Family members willing to share anonymized real case information and health questions
#### Constraints
- The article notes the intervention period was relatively short (4 months), reflecting short-term effects
- The article notes the single-center design may limit generalizability

### Target Learners
- General surgical nursing interns

### Learning Goals
- Medical curiosity
- Self-directed learning efficacy
- Doctor-patient communication
- Inter-professional learning ability
- Nurse licensure examination preparation
