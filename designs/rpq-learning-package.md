# Reading, Presenting, and Questioning (RPQ) learning package

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
RPQ is a learning package combining a teaching method with modern-physics topics (the Large Hadron Collider at CERN and the Wilkinson Microwave Anisotropic Probe), applied to three physics sections of 91 students. It comprises students' autonomous reading of popular articles and online resources, PowerPoint presentations of learning results, and questioning about unclear elements of reading and peer-presented materials, with sections broken into three teams to encourage discussion. Its rationale draws on "read to learn", "present to learn", and "question to learn" practices.
