# CCT active-learning framework: broadened representational infrastructure and timely feedback loops improving formative assessment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's theoretical framework holds that connected classroom technology supports active learning environments that "broaden the representational infrastructure of the classroom" and provide "timely, targeted, and accurate feedback loops" that improve formative assessment opportunities and ultimately algebra 1 achievement. CCT serves as a mediating tool between mathematical content and classroom culture, transferring responsibility for mathematical thinking to students through public display of tasks, anonymous participation, and simultaneous display of multiple mathematical representations. The framework was used to design the professional development intervention and to interpret the trial's achievement gains.
