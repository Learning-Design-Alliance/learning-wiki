# Two-experiment viewing design measuring form recognition, meaning recall, and meaning recognition

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's empirical apparatus consists of two experiments with Dutch-speaking EFL learners who viewed TV material, with vocabulary learning assessed through three measures. As printed: "Two experiments were conducted with Dutch-speaking EFL learners to measure the effects of viewing TV on form recognition and meaning recall (Experiment 1) and meaning recognition (Experiment 2)." The design pairs viewing of audio-visual input with distinct receptive vocabulary knowledge measures, allowing learning to be detected at different levels of word knowledge.
