# Four-domain structure with descriptive reports and self-assessment fields per domain

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The instrument's first version comprised five assessment chunks: a header with identification data, presentation and usage guidelines, four domains with their items (transversal skills; nursing care; nursing management; education and research), space below each domain for "the evaluator and the student to write a descriptive report, aiming at their self-assessment", and a formula for the final formative assessment. Scores of 0 to 10 are assigned per item, and the summative grade is the simple arithmetic mean of the two formative assessments.
