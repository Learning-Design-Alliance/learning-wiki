# Introduction to Physics Teaching course: paired tutorial design and authentic college recitation teaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A three-credit, three-hours-per-week semester course in which students read and discussed one or two teaching-and-learning articles weekly, then designed and delivered two 25-minute introductory physics tutorials on DC circuits and electromagnetic induction in authentic college recitation classes. Students worked in pairs of their choosing, chose their tutorial focus, set goals and performance targets discussed in class, and iterated tutorial versions with the instructor and TA before testing them on classmates.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each student pair required to conduct two college recitations, motivating the B-average prerequisite in introductory physics
#### Constraints
- Taught twice with a total of 12 students, so all course-effect findings rest on this small cohort

### Target Learners
- science and engineering undergraduates, with two masters students from the school of education

### Learning Goals
- knowledge of pedagogical issues and physics education research
- designing and delivering effective tutorials
