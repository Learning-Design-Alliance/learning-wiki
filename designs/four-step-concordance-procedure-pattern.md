# Four-step concordance procedure with a schema-activating pre-activity

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The CPDL lesson pattern opened with a paired pre-activity in which "the activity aimed to activate the students’ pre -existing knowledge of over/above" by generating and comparing their own sentences, replicating a concordance task on their own output. The corpus work then followed four stages of Sinclair's procedure: "initiate, interpret, consolidate and report followed by a  classroom discussion to help the learners to uncover the contexts for the different uses of the vertical prepositions". The pattern interleaves metalinguistic cognitive explanation with hands-on corpus discovery.
