# 24-week weekly 90-minute active learning health promotion program with group work and self-monitoring

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A weekly 90-minute class for 24 weeks addressing "the role of exercise, diet/nutrition, and cognitive activity in health promotion among older adults". Participants searched assigned topics as homework, discussed ideas in small groups of 4 to 5, planned behavioral changes, and self-monitored physical activity using an accelerometer recorded in a notebook. The median attendance rate was 91.7%.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Small groups of 4 to 5 participants; homework topic search between sessions; accelerometer self-monitoring with notebook records
#### Constraints
- Compared against a didactic health education class covering similar themes; effects assessed only at baseline and week 24

### Target Learners
- older adults aged 65 and older with low health literacy

### Learning Goals
- healthy lifestyle planning, self-management, and behavior change in daily life
