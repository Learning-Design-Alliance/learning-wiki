# Modeling Discourse Management with Think-Pair-Share as a classroom model for guided derivation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article followed "a classroom model called Modeling Discourse Management (MDM)" combined with Think-Pair-Share to structure a whole-class derivation of Stokes' Law. Its defining rule is that "if the class is missing a critical or interesting idea in their small group discussions, teachers should attempt to \"seed\" discussions by asking simpler, related questions." Students worked in groups of four using small whiteboards to record work and share ideas with the class. The model shaped how students identified force dependencies and built a quantitative expression via dimensional analysis.
