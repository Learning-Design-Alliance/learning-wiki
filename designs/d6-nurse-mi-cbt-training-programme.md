# D6 nurse training programme in six MI/CBT skills with workshops, handbook, and monthly supervision

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The D6 training programme prepared primary care nurses in the intervention arm to deliver MI and CBT skills within routine diabetes care. It was developed and delivered by an experienced clinical psychologist using didactic and practicum strategies: "Nurses were trained in six MI/CBT skills: active listening, managing resistance, directing change, supporting self-efficacy, addressing health beliefs, and shaping behaviours." Initial interactive workshops ran over 12 3-hourly sessions with a handbook for ongoing reference, and nurses attended monthly supervision with the trial psychologist plus e-mail support for individual cases.
