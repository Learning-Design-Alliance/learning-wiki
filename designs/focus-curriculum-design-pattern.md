# FOCUS curriculum design: backward design, just-in-time developmental sequencing, and deconstruct/reconstruct

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The FOCUS curriculum pattern coordinates a credit-bearing course scope and sequence built by backward design from state and local objectives, a developmental course whose concepts are sequenced just-in-time to support the credit-bearing content, and a deconstruct/reconstruct approach that replaces memorized, context-bound rules with generalizable conceptual knowledge. The article notes "The notion of "deconstructing" misunderstanding and "reconstructing" conceptual knowledge was prevalent in the developmental mathematics curriculum". The developmental course extends an algebraic core to geometry, measurement, probability, and statistics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Developmental concepts must be sequenced to support upcoming credit-bearing content, e.g., solving systems of equations before matrix methods
#### Constraints
- Some content, such as continuity and the logistic function, exceeded traditional College Algebra expectations

### Target Learners
- Developmental mathematics students co-enrolled in College Algebra

### Learning Goals
- Conceptual understanding of functions and algebra beyond memorized procedures
