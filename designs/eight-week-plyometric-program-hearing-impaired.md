# Eight-week progressive plyometric training program of 20 exercises at 80-100% intensity added after warm-up to volleyball sessions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention was a 30-minute plyometric training block "consisting of 20 different actions" applied after 20-minute warm-up and before volleyball practice, at "80-100% exercise intensity" with rest according to the full rest principle. Table 1 lists the 20 movements (e.g., Jump Squat, Airborne Heisman, Lunge Jump, Monster Truck Tire Leaps). Table 2 progresses the drills from low intensity (weeks 1-2, 2x10, 2x30sec rest) to high intensity (weeks 7-8, 4x10, 5x30sec), 3 days per week for 8 weeks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Applied after warm-up, before volleyball training sessions, with rest according to the full rest principle
#### Constraints
- Tested only with male hearing-impaired volleyball players over 8 weeks at 3 days/90 min weekly volleyball training

### Target Learners
- hearing-impaired volleyball players (male, mean age 19.08±1.62 experimental, 17.75±1.28 control)

### Learning Goals
- static balance performance
- sporting performance in volleyball
