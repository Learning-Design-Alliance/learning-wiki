# Mastering your Fellowship exam-preparation series presenting MCQ, SAQ, critical-reading and OSCE question formats

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A recurring journal section that helps South African family-medicine registrars prepare for the FCFP (SA) Final Part A examination by providing worked examples of every written and clinical question format with model answers available online. The article states the section 'aims to help registrars prepare for the FCFP (SA) Final Part A examination' and 'will provide examples of the question formats encountered in the written exam: Multiple Choice Question (MCQ) in the form of Single Best Answer (SBA – Type A) and Extended Matching Question (EMQ – Type R); Short Answer Question (SAQ), questions based on the Critical Reading of a Journal article (CRJ: evidence-based medicine) and an example of an Objectively Structured Clinical Examination (OSCE) question'. In this edition the formats are instantiated around anaesthesia content.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Questions must be attempted by candidates before model answers are consulted, ideally with peers and supervisors, as the section instructs: 'attempt to answer the questions (either on your own or with peers and supervisors) before finding the model answers online'
#### Constraints
- Content is keyed to the FCFP (SA) examination blueprint and the clinical domains, national unit standards and evidence-based medicine outcomes of that programme, so examples are South African family-medicine specific

### Target Learners
- Family medicine registrars preparing for the FCFP (SA) Final Part A examination

### Learning Goals
- Familiarity with written and clinical examination question formats
- Application of anaesthesia, obstetric safety, clinical governance and evidence-based practice knowledge under exam conditions
