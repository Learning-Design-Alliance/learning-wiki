# Interview protocol on a contestable worked solution with two critical arguments and three-level coding

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study's instrument is an in-depth 45-minute individual interview around a reproduced exercise solution (Encadré 1) on irreversible gas expansion, in which "8 EDs en fin de formation (master MEEF2) ont participé". Coding defines two critical arguments (C1: intensive quantities p and T are not defined during a non-quasistatic transformation; C2: the ideal gas relation pV = NRT is invalid in this case) with three levels each (firm acceptance, firm critique, mitigated position), plus C3 on the teaching-choice of the explanation and detection of critical retreats.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A contestable text that is not a usual explanation, to separate interpretation parameters
#### Constraints
- The investigated explanation was deliberately neither habitual nor simple nor satisfactory, which should discourage choosing it

### Target Learners
- beginning physics teachers at the end of training

### Learning Goals
- critical analysis of a contestable explanation
- pedagogical choice of explanations
