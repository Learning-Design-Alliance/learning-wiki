# Interactive Simulations Instructional Approach (ISIA): a five-step inquiry sequence for conceptual change

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The ISIA is an inquiry-based instructional sequence that uses interactive simulations to support conceptual change in physics, grounded in Posner et al.'s conceptual change theory and Vygotsky's zone of proximal development. "The ISIA includes ﬁve steps": elicitation and clarification, prediction and implications, testing predictions using interactive simulations, elucidation and linking, and metacognitive evaluation and further testing. Each step elicits and challenges existing conceptions, has students test predictions against simulations, and builds metacognitive evaluation, with scaffolds from teachers, peers, worksheets and the simulations themselves.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Scaffolds are essential: the article states scaffolds such as teachers, lab partners, PhET simulations, scientific language and student worksheets support students through the sequence and can be gradually removed
#### Constraints
- Alternative conceptions are resilient, so more than one cycle through steps 2–4 may be required in a single teaching sequence

### Target Learners
- Grade 10 senior secondary physics students

### Learning Goals
- conceptual understanding of physics (forces and motion)
- inquiry process skills
- confidence in learning
