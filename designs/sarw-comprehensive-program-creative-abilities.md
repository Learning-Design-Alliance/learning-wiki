# Comprehensive SA&RW program for continuous formation of creative abilities and research skills

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's student academic and research work (SA&RW) program is "a continuous, step -by-step formation of creative abilities and research skills". It is not developed separately or in parallel with existing specialized courses but as a phased, system-based line of the same study modes throughout the whole period of study, completed with the prevalence of independent (non-standard) research project tasks that grow larger in volume and deeper in content from one complexity level to another. A control system diagnoses the research competency level, records results, and adjusts the upbringing process.
