# SP+CBL integration creates synergistic benefits through contextualised communication training combined with analytical rigour.

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes that integrating SP and CBL demonstrates synergistic benefits: SP's contextualised communication training combined with CBL's analytical rigour creates an immersive learning environment that elevates comprehensive clinical competence. This hybrid model reduces faculty workload through structured protocols and increases learner satisfaction. The framework is grounded in situated learning theory, cognitive load theory, and competency modelling.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained standardised patients who can authentically replicate patient symptoms, medical histories and physical signs
- Clinically relevant cases designed to align with curricular objectives
- Small-group sessions for SP-based interviews, physical examinations and case analyses
#### Constraints
- Time-intensive case preparation
- Operational complexities in implementation

### Target Learners
- Chinese medical students
- Resident physicians

### Learning Goals
- Theoretical knowledge acquisition
- Clinical practice performance
- Teaching satisfaction
