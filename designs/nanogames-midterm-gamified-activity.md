# Nanogames: team-based gamified midterm activity with web-based games and real-time leaderboards

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Nanogames is a midterm assessment component intertwining a traditional examination with interactive gamified elements. After a 25-minute course test of 25 questions, students form teams of 2-3 and compete in "a series of interactive, web-based games hosted on the Educaplay platform", ranging from crosswords to matching games, with real-time leaderboards displaying group scores between activities. The top two teams advance to the Alphabet Game, a quiz-show-style finale worth bonus points (+0.5 for the winning team, +0.25 for runners-up over ten). The whole sequence spans approximately 100 minutes and is orchestrated by educators, who assign student leadership roles.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educator orchestration and facilitation, including assigning leadership roles among students
- Access to the Educaplay platform for hosting the web-based games
#### Constraints
- Bonus points are small (+0.5/+0.25 over ten toward the midterm score), as printed

### Target Learners
- fourth-year biomedical engineering undergraduates

### Learning Goals
- retention and understanding of course content
- collaborative and competitive learning
- teamwork and leadership skills
