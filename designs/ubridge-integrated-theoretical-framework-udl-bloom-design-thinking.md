# Integrated theoretical framework combining UDL, Bloom's Taxonomy, and design thinking for AI-focused faculty development

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The institute design rested on three complementary lenses treated as interconnected rather than separate: Universal Design for Learning as the inclusive framework, Bloom's Taxonomy as a cognitive framework for judging AI's appropriate roles across cognitive levels, and design thinking's five stages (empathize, define, ideate, prototype, test) as the innovation process. The article states the design "emphasized their interconnection through three primary theoretical lenses", using Bloom to help faculty "discern when AI collaboration enhances versus undermines learning objectives across cognitive levels".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty development must address multiple dimensions simultaneously: technical competence, pedagogical innovation, ethical reasoning, disciplinary application, and institutional navigation
#### Constraints
- AI's role in higher-order thinking remains contested territory requiring careful pedagogical consideration, per the article

### Target Learners
- Higher education faculty integrating AI into teaching

### Learning Goals

- Inclusive AI-enhanced course design
