# Low-cost implementation of the RF Pound-Drever-Hall experiment at approximately $7000

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article reports the total cost of implementing the experiment was approximately $7000, with all items bought new except the frequency counter and adjustable delay line, which came from used test-equipment dealers. Many components are generic and "may be available in a standard undergraduate physics laboratory (frequency counter, oscilloscope, and function generator)." Fabrication of the resonator requires access to a lathe, milling machine, and a drill press.
