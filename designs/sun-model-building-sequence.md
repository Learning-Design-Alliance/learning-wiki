# The SUN model-building sequence: analogical model building across abiotic and biotic energy-transfer experiences using a common electron-donor/acceptor language heuristic

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The SUN model-building sequence induces understanding of biological energy transfer through sequentially analogous experiences: a hydrogen-plus-oxygen explosion video (source), a hydrogen fuel cell lab, and enacted models of cellular respiration and photosynthesis (targets). "The SUN model-building sequence was developed to induce understanding of biological energy transfer based on a general model of energy transfer occasioned by moving electrons during thermodynamically favorable or “spontaneous” reactions." Teachers structurally align common relational elements across experiences using an across-domain language heuristic of ultimate electron donor, ultimate electron acceptor, and work done by moving electrons, grounded in standard reduction potentials.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learners must be guided to attend to relationships between elements common to each experience, since base and target experiences may be viewed differently by teacher and learner
#### Constraints
- Energy transfer under reversible conditions was not considered; the ABC chart is used only roughly, not under standard conditions, when explaining biological electron movement

### Target Learners
- high school biology teachers
- their regular biology students

### Learning Goals
- understanding biological energy transfer in cellular respiration and photosynthesis
- fundamental laws of thermodynamics including conservation of matter and energy
