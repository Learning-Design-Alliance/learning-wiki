# Three foundational pillars of LEARN: motivation, scaffolding, and mentoring

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The LEARN framework rests on three foundational pillars that act as enabling conditions: 'Motivation, Scaffolding and Mentoring'. Motivation is linked to dopaminergic reward pathways and self-determination theory's autonomy, competence, and relatedness; scaffolding breaks down tasks, provides feedback, and gradually reduces support; mentoring provides social and emotional support through trust and modelling, activating brain regions involved in social cognition and emotional regulation. Together the pillars underpin engagement, adaptability, and ethical AI literacy.
