# Rotating-role small-group triage simulation with faculty as patient

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A lesson-level design in which each small group works through triage scenarios with students cycling through provider, nurse, and consultant roles while faculty assume the patient role. The small group format with rotating assigned roles "allowed students to engage in both active and observational learning while providing concrete educational experiences from differing perspectives." The faculty-as-patient-and-debriefer role scaffolds students into more challenging performance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A student nurse receives a script with additional information including vital signs, laboratories, and fetal monitoring data; the consultant receives detailed case information
- Faculty function as patient and debriefer
#### Constraints
- Group size and faculty-student ratio shape the design: smaller groups limited interprofessional skill development, while larger groups limited the experiential learning space for some students

### Target Learners
- graduate advanced practice nursing and midwifery students

### Learning Goals
- triage prioritization, delegation, consultation, and interprofessional communication

### Claims
- Ideal Group Size Three Students One Faculty [+M]
