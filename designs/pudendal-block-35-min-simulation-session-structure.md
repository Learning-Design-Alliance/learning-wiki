# 35-minute simulation-based procedural training pattern: pre-evaluation, didactics, task-trainer practice with checklist assessment, and post-evaluation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A reusable session structure for teaching a low-frequency procedural skill: a preintervention survey and knowledge assessment, a didactic lecture covering history, anatomy, indications, procedural steps, and medication dosing and complications, hands-on task-trainer practice scored with a procedural checklist, and an identical post-intervention evaluation plus satisfaction items. The article specifies "Our final 35‐min intervention consists of (1) a 10‐min preintervention survey..." and reports significant pre/post gains with this structure.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A validated procedural checklist for objective assessment (maximum score 48 points)
- A task trainer allowing practice and assessor verification of needle placement
- Standardized answer key and grading system for scoring
#### Constraints
- Developed and refined through pilot testing and a Delphi expert panel recruited via snowball sampling, which the authors note limited the panel's expertise

### Target Learners
- Residents and practicing clinicians in obstetrics, family medicine, and midwifery

### Learning Goals
- Knowledge, comfort, and confidence in identifying candidates, discussing risks and benefits, offering and performing pudendal nerve block, and managing complications
