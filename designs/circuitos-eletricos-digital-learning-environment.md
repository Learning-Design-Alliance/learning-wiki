# Circuitos Elétricos: a 4C/ID-based digital learning environment for 9th-grade electrical circuits

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Circuitos Elétricos is a digital learning environment built with Adobe Flash CS3 Professional, working on MacOS and Windows, structured in three lessons: electric current and conductivity, circuit symbols and representation, and series and parallel circuits. Tasks fade from a modeling example with maximum guidance to completion problems to conventional problems, followed by part-task practice, with supportive information videos, cognitive feedback and corrective feedback messages. The article reports "The digital educational resource was built with Adobe Flash ® CS3 Professional and it works both in MacOS and Windows environments".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Supportive information videos followed multimedia principles: irrelevant sounds, words and images eliminated, simultaneous text and images, and narrated words
#### Constraints
- Procedural information was focused only on corrective feedback, considering the participants' age and the kind of content taught

### Target Learners
- 9th-grade compulsory education physics students

### Learning Goals
- Analysing simple electrical circuits, including series and parallel circuit characteristics
