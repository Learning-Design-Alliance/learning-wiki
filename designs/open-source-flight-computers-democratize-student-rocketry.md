# Open-source flight computers democratize access to aerospace technology and support hands-on learning in student rocketry

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article argues that providing open-source, sophisticated flight computers to student teams lowers barriers to high-quality aerospace technology: "This democratization is crucial for fostering innovation and providing students with the tools they need to excel in aerospace engineering." By integrating such systems into competitions like EuRoC, students gain hands-on experience with telemetry, control, and mission safety that was once the domain of well-funded institutions. The article presents this as an interpretation supported by its field deployments rather than a tested educational outcome.
