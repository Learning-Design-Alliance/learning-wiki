# Interactive OHNS e-learning video resources with layered information, interactive questions and end-of-video quizzes

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A set of interactive e-learning teaching resources designed by the authors to supplement the University of Sydney OHNS curriculum, covering thyroid, rhinological, otological, oral and head & neck clinical examinations in videos lasting approximately 26 minutes. "Layered information and interactive questions were placed at intervals throughout the video resources, where students can decide to interact as they see fit," and a quiz at the end of each video supports self-assessment and knowledge retention. Access was provided through a unique link and login details.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires internet-based delivery with unique links and login details for student access
#### Constraints
- Usage of the resources was not logged due to system limitations, so the study could not quantify how often students used them

### Target Learners
- Final-year medical students

### Learning Goals
- OHNS clinical examination skills and knowledge (thyroid, rhinological, otological, oral, head & neck examinations)
