# The Australia New Zealand Microsurgical Skills Course (ANZMSC)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The ANZMSC is a three-day ophthalmic microsurgical skills course conducted through the University of Sydney at the Sight for Life Foundation Surgical Skills Training Laboratory, running three times a year with twenty students per course. The article describes it as "the first ophthalmic microsurgical skills course in the Southern Hemisphere that has been made mandatory by RANZCO for first year ophthalmology trainees". It comprises eleven practical skills stations covering suturing on skills boards and pig eyes, strabismus surgery on model eyes and cadavers, eyelid repair and minor procedures on cadavers, and phacoemulsification on EyeSi simulators and dry lab sets, supported by pre-course online lectures and live demonstrations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- High tutor-to-student ratio to allow maximal feedback during the course
- Course materials and equipment supplied by SFLSSTL and industry partners
#### Constraints
- Course fee of $2,750 AUD for participants, with reimbursement available in New Zealand but not typically in Australia

### Target Learners
- Pre-vocational junior doctors interested in ophthalmology
- First-year RANZCO ophthalmology trainees

### Learning Goals
- Ophthalmic microsurgical skills including corneal suturing, strabismus surgery, eyelid repair, and phacoemulsification
