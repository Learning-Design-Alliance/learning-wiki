# Ten-week multimodal focused echocardiography training pattern combining didactic, proctored and independent phases

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A training sequence based on American Society of Echocardiography recommendations in which trainees first complete didactic lectures and observation, then a 10-week practical phase of "a minimum of 20 individual scans per trainee with interpretations", moving from six weeks of proctored scanning to three further proctored weeks and finally one week of independent imaging and interpretation. Feedback was immediate during proctored scans.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Didactic component covering ultrasound principles, cardiac anatomy and physiology, machine orientation and scanning windows
- Proctored image acquisition with immediate feedback from an experienced echocardiographer
- Progression to independent imaging and interpretation before evaluation
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only with two residents at a single teaching hospital
- Focused on qualitative assessment of LV and RV structure and function and pericardial effusion; colour flow, doppler and valvular assessment were not included in the evaluation phase

### Target Learners
- internal medicine residents and other non-echocardiographer clinicians in resource-limited settings

### Learning Goals
- acquiring limited echocardiographic windows
- qualitative interpretation of ventricular structure and function
- detecting pericardial effusion
