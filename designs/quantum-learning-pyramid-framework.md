# The Quantum Learning Pyramid: a four-tier framework for quantum computing education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The QLP organizes quantum education into four vertically aligned tiers: phenomenological foundation, computational abstraction, hardware-aware development, and sociotechnical contextualization, supported by cross-cutting mechanisms of adaptive pathways, competency-based assessment, and authentic project integration. The article states that "Movement upward through the pyramid corresponds to increasing mathematical rigour and system level reasoning, while horizontal engagement emphasizes real world application and relevance." It is grounded in constructivist learning theory, threshold concepts, and deliberate practice.
