# UAT's four priorities for connecting isolated PUI researchers and students to a large research project

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article enumerates the priorities it identified to address the challenges of connecting isolated researchers and their students in PUI environments to a large legacy survey: "communicating a basic understanding of radio astronomy and extragalactic astrophysics to students with little or no class preparation," providing computers and travel funds, identifying undergraduate-appropriate research projects, and demonstrating modern collaborative science practices. These priorities structure the program's workshops, funding, and mentoring activities.
