# TCLEBL: an integrated team-, case-, lecture- and evidence-based learning model for medical postgraduate education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
TCLEBL combines four pedagogies the article describes separately: team-based learning's collaborative group work, case-based learning's authentic clinical vignettes, retained lecture components for structured knowledge frameworks, and evidence-based learning built on literature retrieval and critical appraisal. In the studied implementation, students worked in two teams on an ocular toxocariasis case, performed pre-class literature searches for "the latest scientific research, clinical trials, meta-analyses, and systematic evaluations", presented diagnostic and treatment plans in class with teacher guidance, and wrote a review article afterwards. The model aims to "provide a comprehensive, well-rounded teaching experience in medical postgraduate education".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A teacher who instructs both content delivery and evidence-synthesis guidance; pre-selected case records; access to academic databases, medical journals, and specialty guidelines; assessment via theory exams and written literature reviews.
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only in ophthalmology postgraduates at a single institution, on a single disease topic not covered in standard five-year ophthalmology textbooks.

### Target Learners
- medical postgraduates (ophthalmology)

### Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning and diagnostic/treatment planning
- literature searching, critical appraisal, and review writing
- teamwork and self-directed learning
