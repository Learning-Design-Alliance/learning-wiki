# Interprofessional acute-care simulation training for final-year nursing and medical students

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A three-hour high-fidelity simulation training in which four nursing and four medical students work in interprofessional pairs through three deteriorating-patient scenarios using the ABCDE-method, followed by a plenary debriefing focused on interprofessional collaboration and communication. Each interprofessional session is taught by two teachers, one doctor and one nurse. The training was developed based on suggestions by Anderson et al. on optimal IPE simulation design, with transfer to daily clinical practice as a central role.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Two teachers per interprofessional session, one doctor and one nurse
- Small groups with every student an active participant at least once
- Plenary debriefing including all students, focused on interprofessional collaboration and communication
