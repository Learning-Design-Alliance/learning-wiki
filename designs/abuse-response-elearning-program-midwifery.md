# Four-chapter e-learning program on handling high-risk child abuse cases for obstetric midwives and nurses

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A self-paced e-learning program developed by the authors for midwifery continuing education, aligned with Japan's CLoCMiP clinical ladder. "The e-learning program comprised four chapters with several sections composed of short videos (5–10 min) and a quiz with multiple-choice questions with a single correct answer." Learners must answer correctly to proceed, and the program "took approximately 90 min to complete". Chapters cover abuse response and laws, prevention (risk factors, perinatal mental health, domestic violence), cooperation with related organizations, and roleplay.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants complete a pretest, the program, and a post-test online; an online support system was provided to help participants operate the program.
#### Constraints
- The article notes 20% of participants felt the program was too long, and about half of dropouts could not access the program after the pretest.

### Target Learners
- obstetric midwives and nurses working in perinatal care in Japan

### Learning Goals
- knowledge of abuse definitions, laws, and risk factors
- responding to and supporting high-risk abuse cases
- cooperation with related organizations
