# Elaborated standards document and 'Becoming an effective teacher of English' booklet

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Two supporting documents were developed for the interventions. The elaborated standards document made the general and broad competencies in the Indonesian government standards clearer, more specific, and easier to measure by defining the listed reading competencies. The second document, a booklet titled "Becoming an effective teacher of English", discussed the characteristics of effective teaching as defined by the classroom factors in the dynamic model, linking these factors with the specific reading skills in the elaborated standards.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers attended a one-day introductory workshop to discuss the elaborated standards document
#### Constraints
- The article notes the standards documents' broad and global language as a problem the elaboration was meant to address

### Target Learners
- junior secondary teachers and students (ages 12/13 to 14/15) in Indonesian madrasahs

### Learning Goals
- clearer understanding of what to plan, teach, and assess in English reading
- effective teaching of reading competencies
