# Flipgrid-based remote knot-tying exercise with household items and adapted OSATS scoring

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A remote skills-training element in which final-year medical students view a surgeon's video demonstration on Flipgrid, practice one-handed knot-tying at home using "common household items such a string or shoelace on a door handle or the armrest of a chair", record video attempts, and upload them for blinded scoring with an adapted OSATS rubric. The instructor viewed, liked, and briefly commented on each submission within 24–48 hours, but no formal feedback was provided. Flipgrid is described as a free Microsoft video discussion platform facilitating social learning between students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A video demonstration produced and sanctioned by the department, related online content (steps manual, OSATS rubric), and a device capable of recording and uploading video
#### Constraints
- Formal feedback was not provided on attempts; students noted challenges with camera positioning and visibility

### Target Learners
- final-year undergraduate medical students

### Learning Goals
- one-handed surgical knot-tying to a reliable reef knot
