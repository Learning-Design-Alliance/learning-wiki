# Remote flipped Bachelor of Pharmacy program at Monash University Malaysia (COVID-19 adaptation)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 4-year Bachelor of Pharmacy program that ordinarily uses a primarily flipped classroom model, adapted in 2020 for fully remote delivery. Pre-class materials were unchanged, while in-class lectures became "a mixture of asynchronous and synchronous depending on respective units," and workshops, OSCEs, compounding labs, and device teaching moved to Zoom breakout rooms with facilitators, examiners, or demonstrators respectively.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Internet connectivity sufficient for video-conferenced sessions
- Zoom breakout-room infrastructure with facilitators or examiners for interactive activities
#### Constraints
- Specialized activities such as compounding labs and OSCEs were adapted remotely with known validation and fidelity limitations reported by students

### Target Learners
- undergraduate pharmacy students (years 1–4)

### Learning Goals
- pharmacy content knowledge
- communication and counselling skills
- extemporaneous compounding and medical device skills
