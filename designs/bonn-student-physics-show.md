# Bonn student-developed physics show for school classes and the general public

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A two-hour physics show at the University of Bonn, developed and performed entirely by undergraduate physics students for children aged 10 and older and the general public. The article states that "the essential point of this initiative is that the students develop and perform the show themselves; it is entirely in their control." Students choose experiments from the demonstration collection, assisted by Michael Kortmann and the university workshops, and rehearse daily in the last 8-10 days before the first show. Performances fill a 520-people auditorium, with one show for school classes and two weekend shows for the general public each cycle.
