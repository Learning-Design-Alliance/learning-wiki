# Applied behavior analytic instructional techniques embedded across classroom and internship settings

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The PS-ASD model increased the structure and intensity of Project SEARCH learning experiences by ensuring the use of applied behavior analytic techniques throughout classroom instruction and internship supports. Techniques include "scored task analyses for teaching multistep tasks, structured repeated trials for discrete tasks, behavioral rehearsal for specific social skills, visual and self-directed prompting procedures for transitioning, self-management procedures for behavioral challenges, and reinforcement for appropriate behavior," with regular data review, functional behavior assessment, and customized employment to match tasks to intern strengths.
