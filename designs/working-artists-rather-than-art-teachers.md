## Description
The article identifies the AP's distinguishing design feature as its use of "working artists as opposed to art teachers" in school workshops. Artists are "selected through an open competition" and "paid competitively," with the implementing partner's reputation and attractive salaries cited as explaining the capacity to "attract excellent artists to the program." This design choice is presented as the key novelty distinguishing the AP from traditional public school art education.
