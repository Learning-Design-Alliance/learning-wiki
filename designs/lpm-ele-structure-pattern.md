# E-learning environment structured as physiology modules plus pathology areas with model results and virtual cases

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The environment's instructional design combined general and topic-specific components: "The environment comprised a general introduction, modules on basic cardiovascular physiology and the three areas of pathology sub-divided into aetiology, epidemiology, pathophysiology, treatments, interactive model results and virtual clinical cases." Questions were embedded in the text at two levels — on model parameters (e.g. how to model hypertension with the LPM) and on the disease itself (e.g. how a heart failure patient would be treated) — with sequential Back/Proceed navigation and a jump navigation tab.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Embeds assessment questions within the content so the environment includes both interactive and assessment elements
#### Constraints
- Maintaining a structured design became challenging in the final iteration, which contained over 200 pages, though users were not required to interact with each page sequentially

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Learning Goals
- cardiovascular physiology and pathophysiology knowledge; disease classification and severity grading
