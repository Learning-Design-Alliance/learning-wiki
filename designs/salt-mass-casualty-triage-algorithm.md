# SALT Mass Casualty Triage Algorithm as the training content framework

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
SALT (Sort, Assess, Lifesaving interventions, Treatment and/or transport) is a mass casualty triage guideline proposed by the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention to address limited evidence supporting one triage algorithm over another. The study taught the SALT algorithm through an 18-slide lecture created with national trauma instructors and disaster medicine experts, covering the history and purpose of triage, the origin of SALT triage and the SALT triage algorithm. After the 2011 Norway terrorist attack, the Norwegian Directorate of Health proposed a guideline for use in MCIs based on SALT.
