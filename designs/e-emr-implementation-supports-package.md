# E-EMR implementation support package: training, playbook, PLC protocols, and weekly equity-tip emails

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Beyond the core relationship practices, E-EMR includes a set of implementation supports the article describes as "training, a 'playbook' with implementation scripts, structured professional learning community (PLC) protocols, and weekly email prompts". The six-hour initial training covered STR rationale, implicit-bias science, and equitable implementation, and was coded for fidelity (97–100% adherence; 96% behavioral engagement). The monthly PLC protocol guides teachers through six steps including reviewing progress, identifying students in Establish or Restore phases, reflecting on equity by examining how student demographics relate to relationship phase, and setting student-specific goals.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Monthly small-group PLC meetings following the six-step structured protocol
- Weekly email prompts including an equity tip tied to the experiences of students of color
#### Constraints
- Fidelity of the intervention could not be measured when COVID-19 moved schooling to distance settings, so T3 data were excluded from the study

### Target Learners
- Ninth-grade high school teachers

### Learning Goals
- Teacher implementation of equity-explicit relationship-building practices
- Disrupting teacher implicit bias
