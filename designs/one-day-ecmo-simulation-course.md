# One-day ECMO simulation course integrating technical and nontechnical training components

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 1-day simulation course developed by the ECMO Faculty at King's College Hospital with simulation experts, intended to integrate clinical and nonclinical training for ECMO specialists. It comprised purpose-designed precourse material (a 52-page ECMO workbook with 29 self-assessment questions) and six hierarchical sequential training modules: a seminar on simulation principles and human factors, manikin and circuit introduction, a troubleshooting workshop, ECMO scenario training with debriefing, case-based discussion, and an assessment with evaluation questionnaires. Scenarios covered sweep-gas failure, circuit disconnection, line rattling and air in the circuit.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delegates were required to complete the precourse workbook and self-assessment prior to the practical component of the course.
#### Constraints
- The evaluation involved a small number of attendees (14 delegates across three courses), and no long-term clinical outcome data were available.

### Target Learners
- intensive care nurses
- adult intensive care consultants
- ECMO technicians

### Learning Goals
- ECMO circuit troubleshooting
- communication and teamwork in ECMO emergencies
- leadership and delegation during ECMO crises
