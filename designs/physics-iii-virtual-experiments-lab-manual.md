# Physics III virtual lab manual of twelve experiments on fluids, waves, thermodynamics, optics, and modern physics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article presents a lab manual of twelve virtual experiments for introductory physics: "This covers fluids, waves, thermodynamics, optics, interference, photoelectric effect , atomic spectra  and radiation  concepts." The contents list names each experiment, from Archimedes, Pascal, and Bernoulli principles through standing waves, resonance, calorimetry, gas laws, reflection and refraction, mirrors and lenses, double-slit interference, the photoelectric effect, the Bohr model, and radioactive half-life. It is the third series in a set with prior Physics I and Physics II manuals.
