# Implement explicit, intentional, uniform curricula across classrooms with professional development supports to improve and maintain quality

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The district's theory of change was that "implementing explicit, intentional, and uniform curricula across classrooms with professional development supports would improve and maintain the quality of support provided to teachers and optimize resource allocation", for example through streamlining teacher training. The evaluation's moderate-to-large cognitive impacts are the article's evidence that this combination can work at scale in a public district.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- District-chosen, research-based curricula plus coaching and curriculum-specific training; teachers meeting BA and masters-degree requirements.
#### Constraints
- Achieving positive impacts is more challenging at scale than in research demonstration trials, where creators are heavily involved and implementation quality is easier to maintain.

### Target Learners
- prekindergarten children in urban public school districts

### Learning Goals
- school readiness in language, literacy, and mathematics
