# Virtual Immersive Teaching and Learning (VITaL) Laboratory in the Sonoma State University Makerspace

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The VITaL Laboratory is a newly formed space added to the Sonoma State University Makerspace where students from all disciplines use virtual reality head-mounted displays, augmented reality devices, 360-degree cameras, and VR-capable computers as part of their academic experience. Students explore subject material in virtual or augmented reality alongside classroom instruction, using existing applications with faculty-written instructions. The lab also serves as a community space for the student-led VR Club, and its hardware is used by the student team to build and test the electric circuits application.
