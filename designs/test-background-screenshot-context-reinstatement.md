# Test-phase background screenshots reinstating the video setting as a contextual cue

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In Experiment 2, the video's setting was reinstated during assessment: after watching the video, participants worked on the retention and application test "while a screenshot of the authentic or the neutral setting was presented as a background". This manipulation tested whether the setting could act as a contextual cue that further improves test performance, but the article reports no evidence for context effects.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Screenshots of the video's shooting setting must be presented as the background during the retention and application test.
#### Constraints
- The article found no evidence for context effects from this manipulation.
