# Proficiency-based progression simulation training using expert-defined competency thresholds

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
This framework, following guidelines outlined by Gallagher et al., defines competency thresholds by the mean rubric score and completion time of expert performers in a simulation environment. Participants across experience levels are assessed on a validated checklist, and "the mean rubric score and completion time of experts were defined as the competency thresholds for this procedure," enabling standardized proficiency-based progression rather than time-based progression alone.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A validated checklist with expert panel review
- Trained graders to reduce interrater variability
- A simulation model allowing standardized task performance
#### Constraints
- Simulations lack real-world consequences and duress conditions, which may limit external validity

### Target Learners
- medical students
- nurses
- physicians
- EMS providers

### Learning Goals
- achieving defined proficiency in procedural skills
