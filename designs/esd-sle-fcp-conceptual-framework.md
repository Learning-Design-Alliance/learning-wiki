# Conceptual framework linking education for sustainable development (ESD) and sustainable learning in education (SLE) through the flipped classroom

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes a bidirectional conceptual framework in which sustainability and education influence each other's development. SLE refers to "learning that lasts" achieved through well-structured and responsive teaching, while ESD emphasizes humans living in harmony with nature. The FCP is positioned as one possible option for SLE: it offers quality education through flexible teaching, and conversely it can cultivate learners who contribute to SDGs via ESD by embracing sustainability principles. HEIs play an active role in promoting SDG4 through this dual pathway.
