# Editable gamified onboarding pattern: staff-maintained quests keep simulation content current without IT support

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A reusable instructional design in which permanent staff autonomously create and update onboarding quests, rewards, and simulation elements. The article states this "ensures a rapid adaptation to the frequent changes observed in hospital services while preserving the quality and consistency of the induction experience for new employees." Quest tasks span location discovery, agent dialogs, object searches, and quizzes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A quest management application usable by nontechnical staff without IT or 3D specialists
#### Constraints
- Question authoring currently requires writing XML code, which the authors plan to replace with point-and-click drop boxes

### Target Learners
- New hospital employees in high-turnover departments

### Learning Goals
- Rapid familiarization with premises, equipment, and colleagues
