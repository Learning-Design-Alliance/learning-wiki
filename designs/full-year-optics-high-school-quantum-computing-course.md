# A full-year, optics-based high school quantum computing course using VQOL, IBM Quantum Composer, and Google Colab

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes the first full-year high school course on quantum computing that counted toward a graduation requirement, piloted at a Texas public high school from fall 2019 and later offered through the UT OnRamps program. Students used the Virtual Quantum Optics Lab (VQOL) to simulate manipulation of light, IBM's Quantum Composer to implement quantum protocols and algorithms on real and simulated quantum computers, and Google Colab for Python programming. The course integrated inquiry-based experiments, collaboration, and just-in-time skill instruction.

### Target Learners

### Learning Goals
- Quantum information science concepts
- Technical skills in physics, programming, cybersecurity, and mathematics
- Critical thinking and problem solving
