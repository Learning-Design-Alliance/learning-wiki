# Four-step formative assessment cycle: create assignments, assist students through immediate feedback, assess class performance, analyze answers together

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report organizes the intervention around a "4-step formative assessment process (Heritage & Popham, 2013) that is integrated throughout the ASSISTments platform": (1) create assignments aligned with grade-level standards using OERs or Skill Builders; (2) assist students through immediate feedback, hints, and explanations; (3) assess class performance by reviewing reports to identify needs; and (4) analyze answers together by projecting anonymized reports for whole-class discussion of misconceptions. The vPLC embedded this cycle across its session content.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must review performance reports to identify student needs and inform future instruction, and project anonymized reports for whole-class review.
#### Constraints
- The cycle's ultimate goal, as stated, is to improve student math achievement; sessions 2 through 6 each highlighted different report features to build teachers' instructional toolkit over time.

### Target Learners
- Middle school math teachers and their grade 6-8 students

### Learning Goals
- Formative assessment practice and improved student math achievement
