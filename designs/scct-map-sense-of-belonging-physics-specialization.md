# Social Cognitive Career Theory map with an added sense-of-belonging construct

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study's framework is Social Cognitive Career Theory (SCCT), which the authors extend with a sense-of-belonging construct. The map "consists of self-efficacy, outcome expectations, proximal environment influences, interest, and learning experiences", with choice goals and choice actions following along the map; in a low-interest study a choice goal "could be avoiding a particular method". The authors used Lucidchart to build individual and merged graphical maps of each student's influences.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Semi-structured interviews with interest ratings and SCCT-coded follow-up questions
- Diagramming of individual student maps, then merged and organized maps
#### Constraints
- The sense of belonging construct is not part of the original SCCT framework
- The limited sample size limits reaching saturation in data collection and analysis

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics majors

### Learning Goals
- career and interest development in physics method specializations (theory, computation, experiment)
