# A workshop model for continued diversity education of student program organizers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article presents a workshop model giving student leaders of a diversity-oriented physics organization "the background and resources to make informed programmatic decisions about their organization." Each workshop lasts two hours and consists of five parts: pre-workshop homework, an Empathy Activity, a homework debrief, an Application Activity, and post-workshop feedback. Workshops were offered quarterly and framed as an ongoing curriculum rather than a one-time intervention, with successive workshops building on one another and informed by participant feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants must be volunteers who already recognize lack of diversity as a problem and have established trusting relationships through shared organizational work
#### Constraints
- Participation was limited to CU-Prime leaders, excluding faculty and staff, so the model is not necessarily appropriate for settings where students, professors, and staff are co-learners
- Workshop agendas were tailored to a particular student organization and may not be widely applicable beyond the local context

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate students, graduate students, and postdocs leading a student-run diversity organization

### Learning Goals
- Understanding challenges and successes of students from underrepresented groups in physics
- Applying equity learning to organizational decision-making
