## Description
The article's CBT curriculum for primary teachers on brain-based learning management was developed following a curriculum-development sequence: "setting curriculum's objectives and learning objectives, specifying curriculum's contents, ordering contents and specifying duration, selecting experiences, ordering experiences, holding the training, specifying evaluation and evaluation methods." The article reports this sequence was consistent with Taba's (1972) curriculum development procedures.
