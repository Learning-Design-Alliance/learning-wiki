# Deliver supplemental academic services in a structured environment with a low student-to-teacher ratio

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The Wraparound program is delivered in a structured environment with a low student-to-teacher ratio, which the page presents as part of how the program aims to accelerate academic achievement while fostering a supportive learning atmosphere. Staffing criteria by service type (certified instructors, certified counselors, experienced coaches) support this delivery model.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors with state-compliant certification; counselors with certification and/or MSW; coaches with 2 years of K-8 experience
#### Constraints
- No required technology component to the program model or the delivery of services

### Target Learners
- K-12 students receiving supplemental educational services

### Learning Goals
- Accelerated academic achievement in literacy and math
