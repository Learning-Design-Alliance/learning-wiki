# E-learning environment with virtual tours of Gardom's Edge for FE/HE teaching and school virtual field trips

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An e-learning environment envisaged to let FE/HE students and Key Stage 2-4 pupils experience and explore ancient landscapes such as Gardom's Edge. It offers an interactive landscape students can enter at will alongside lectures and before field trips, including a virtual walk through a hollow way or enclosure area to complement map work. The authors write it would be beneficial to have an interactive landscape for students to explore alongside lectures and to prepare for field trips. Curriculum-linked uses include the rock cycle at KS3, citizenship interviews, and a standing stone panoramic tool for Light and Shadow and Seasons.
