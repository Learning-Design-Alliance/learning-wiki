# Three-case taxonomy of pandemic fieldwork innovation: administration, intrapreneurship, and virtual care

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article organizes pandemic-era fieldwork innovations into three cases derived from interview data using Yin's case study methodology. Administration placements involved research projects, policy development, and program or guideline creation without traditional clients; intrapreneurship placements advocated for occupational therapy services in new and non-traditional settings; virtual care placements transitioned traditional in-person settings to virtual service delivery. Of 28 interviews, "11 were categorized as virtual care, eight as intrapreneurship, and eight as administration."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A placement was considered innovative if it did not fit the definition of a traditional fieldwork placement
#### Constraints
- Cases were derived from participants at the University of Toronto catchment area during March 2020 to December 2020; interview data did not completely fit neatly into each case
