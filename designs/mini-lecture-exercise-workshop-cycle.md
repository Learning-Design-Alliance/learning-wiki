# Blended learning workshop structure of short live mini-lectures alternating with bite-size practical exercises

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The 3-hour workshop is broken into intervals of approximately 10 minutes of lecturing to establish context and motivation, including live worked examples, followed by roughly 30 minutes of practical exercises with teaching assistants and peers providing support. This follows evidence that adult students maintain focus for only about 15–20 minutes and that seeing worked examples before attempting exercises helps students tackle future problems. Students are expected to read lecture notes beforehand.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students read the lecture notes before the workshop
- Teaching assistants on hand during exercise periods
- Explicit justification of the approach in the first lecture to reset passive-learning expectations
#### Constraints
- Requires timetabling within a fixed contact-hour budget; the 2011 variant needing an extra 3-hour practical block was unsustainable

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate geoscience students

### Learning Goals
- programming skill development through practice with immediate support
