# WoT4EDP: a five-element Ways of Thinking framework for engineering design-based physics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes "Ways of Thinking for Engineering Design-based Physics (WoT4EDP)" as a structured approach for investigating and facilitating student thinking in undergraduate physics. It "integrates five key elements—design, science, mathematics, metacognitive reflection, and computational thinking—within an undergraduate introductory physics laboratory", with double-headed arrows in Figure 1 illustrating each element's interconnectedness with the other four. The framework was developed from the authors' prior qualitative studies and is grounded in reform documents such as the NRC 2013 report, AAPT laboratory recommendations, and mathematics education reports.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The framework is intended for introductory, calculus-based physics courses that include engineering design-based laboratory experiences
#### Constraints
- The authors state the list of concepts and abilities for each element is non-exhaustive and that educators should retain flexibility in which aspects to emphasize
- The five elements are conceptual constructs rather than fixed, measurable entities

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate engineering and science majors in first-semester calculus-based physics

### Learning Goals
- Integrated STEM problem-solving in engineering design-based physics projects
