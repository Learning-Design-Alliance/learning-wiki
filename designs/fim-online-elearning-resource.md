# Online Clinical Microbiology e-learning resource with glossary, question/answer activities, podcasts with MCQ quizzes, antibiotic content, pathogen index and clinical case scenarios

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An online module hosted on Moodle designed to complement didactic teaching in the Foundations in Microbiology module. It contained "an alphabetical glossary of important terms in Clinical Microbiology and question/answer-type activities on core concepts", podcasts with MCQ-style quizzes, antibiotic activities, and clinical case scenarios for each important genus or family with feedback and unlimited attempts. Each pathogen name was hyper-linked to an alphabetical index summarising epidemiology, virulence, pathogenesis, laboratory diagnosis and antimicrobial management. Students could track their progress and compare it to the class.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students were expected to complete all components of the online content, with expectations outlined in the Marks and Standards document and a face-to-face introductory session
#### Constraints
- There were no academic consequences for not engaging

### Target Learners
- second-year undergraduate medical students

### Learning Goals
- characteristics of medically important bacteria, viruses and fungi
- pathogenicity and spread of infection
- antibiotic chemotherapy and stewardship
- laboratory diagnosis
- infection prevention
