# 72-hour tailored training program combining in-person theoretical sessions with hospital-based simulation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The training program consisted of theoretical and practical components tailored to the 21 identified educational topics. The article describes that "the training consisted of theoretical and practical sessions, which lasted for 72 h in total", with 20 in-person sessions of 3.5 h each followed by simulation-based practical training in the hospital. Nurses had to score above 70 on a final exam to advance; 30 of 35 participants passed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Full-time attendance, a bachelor's degree in nursing, at least 3 years of cardiac surgery ward experience, and a final exam score over 70 to qualify for the performance comparison
#### Constraints
- Five of the 35 case-group nurses failed to pass the training course and did not advance

### Target Learners
- registered nurses with at least 3 years of C-ICU experience

### Learning Goals
- clinical and diagnostic skills for cardiac surgery care, including reducing cardiac surgery complications
