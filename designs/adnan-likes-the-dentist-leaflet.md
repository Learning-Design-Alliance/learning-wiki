# Story-based educational leaflet "Adnan likes the dentist" for children's oral health education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A colorful, attractive printed leaflet in the form of a short story named "Adnan likes the dentist", designed by a graphic designer and used as the educational tool for the Leaflet cluster. It covered "proper brushing technique and frequency", regular use of dental floss, regular dental visits, dental plaque and its implications, nutritional guidelines for minimizing caries risk, and the role of fluoride in caries control. In the trial it produced better knowledge, plaque and gingival outcomes than the E-learning program.
