# Virtual Room of Error design pattern: 360-degree hazard-embedded scene with gamification, shared notebook, and real-time interprofessional teams

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 3 studies (2 review, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A reusable session-level design in which interprofessional teams of about five enter a hazard-embedded virtual patient room simultaneously and operate independently to identify and document patient safety hazards under time pressure. "The room features 13 errors linked to patient safety hazards"; a red-flashing countdown in the final minute heightens stress, and a shared digital notebook with notifications supports real-time collaborative documentation across professional boundaries.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teams must be interprofessionally mixed (automatic or manual assignment via drag-and-drop) and communicate verbally during the session.
- Realistic hazard-embedded scenarios aligned with patient safety competency catalogues.
#### Constraints
- Designed to avoid specialized equipment such as VR/AR; the scene uses 360-degree high-resolution images rather than full immersion.

### Target Learners
- interprofessional teams of healthcare students and professionals

### Learning Goals
- situational awareness
- error detection under time pressure
- interprofessional collaboration
