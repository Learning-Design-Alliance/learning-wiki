# Competency-based feedback block combining standards-based and competency-based grading with student-chosen weightings and a progress bar

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In Block 4, students could indicate one of 11 pre-set competencies (including regional anatomy, embryology, and comradery and collaboration) to be assessed on each session and determined each competency's weight toward their final grade. The article states this "incorporated elements of both standards‐based grading, which focuses on knowledge acquisition, and competency‐based grading, which focuses on the application of that knowledge." A mandatory opening seminar explained the rationale and competencies, and an LMS progress bar tracked completion. Students received dictated individualized feedback and descriptor rankings, with numerical grades hidden until block end.
