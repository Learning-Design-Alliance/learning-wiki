# Give each participant the experimental set-up as a gift so every participant can repeat the experiment after the competition

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Experimental Physics Olympiad hands each participant the physical set-up they worked with, so that learning continues after the competition ends. The organizers state: "Each participant in EPO receives as a gift from the organizers the set-up, which one worked with." This is intended to let even poorly performing participants reach champion level later, and the set-up remains in the participant's school, raising teaching quality beyond the event itself.
