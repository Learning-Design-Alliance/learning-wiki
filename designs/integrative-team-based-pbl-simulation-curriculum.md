# Integrative Emergency Care curriculum combining team-based PBL with emergency-care scenario simulation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
An 18-week, two-credit elective Emergency Care course design in which team-based problem-based learning is combined with emergency-care scenario simulation. Its framework (Table 1) spans analysis and design, development, implementation and application, and evaluation stages. In the simulation room, "there were simulated patients whose changes in blood pressure, pulse, respiration, blood oxygen saturation concentration, and electrocardiogram results the nursing students had to monitor". Two external nursing-professional reviewers confirmed the curriculum's content validity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Curriculum drafted by instructors and reviewed by two external nursing-professional curriculum reviewers
- Scenario cases selected around the top 10 causes of death and accident injuries in Taiwan
- Simulation room with simulated patients displaying physiological parameters
#### Constraints
- Designed and evaluated for senior nursing students who had finished core nursing courses; the authors note students could not be randomly selected

### Target Learners
- senior nursing students at a medical university

### Learning Goals
- core nursing competencies and competence in team-based collaborative emergency care
