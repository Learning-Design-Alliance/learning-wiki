# Pair a shortened strategy intervention with an expanded dependent measure to detect improvement efficiently

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends determining the most efficient intervention design by shortening instruction while extending the outcome measure. "These findings support the use of a shorter intervention (i.e., 11 vs. 16 sessions) coupled with the revised dependent measure." In this study the intervention was reduced from 16 to 11 sessions (8.25 vs. 13.3 hours) while the pre-/posttest prompts were expanded from 25 to 30 errors, and students still improved on more error types than in the prior 16-session study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dependent measure with enough error instances to avoid a ceiling effect and expand the measurable range of performance
- Explicit strategy instruction with mastery criterion maintained despite fewer sessions
#### Constraints
- The shortened intervention still required 8.25 hours and 11 sessions; the authors call for further investigation of even shorter intervention times

### Target Learners
- postsecondary students with intellectual and developmental disabilities

### Learning Goals
- editing skills in written expression
