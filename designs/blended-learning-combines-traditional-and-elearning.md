## Description
The article defines blended learning as an approach that "combines traditional learning with e-learning", integrating electronic and traditional learning in a single model. It describes the blended learning environment as enabling student-teacher, student-content, and student-student interaction through direct dialogue or online communication, overcoming the social isolation of pure e-learning while using multimedia tools such as e-mail, instant messaging, and forums synchronously or asynchronously. In the study it was operationalized as blending traditional classroom instruction with computer- and internet-based learning.
