# Three GPT-4o configurations for generating digital health OSCE stations (standard, personalized, simulated-agents)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article designed and compared three ChatGPT-4o configurations differing in prompt type and provided documents: a standard GPT with "a simple prompt and the reference book for OSCE construction", a personalized GPT adding the UPCité digital health reference book as a knowledge base, and a simulated-agents GPT with "a prompt simulating a fictional multiagent system specialized in OSCE construction". All three received the same 8 digital health topics and 3 competencies per topic, generating 24 French-language OSCE stations each containing learning domains, a case vignette, an observation checklist, and an SP script.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the OSCE Vademecum reference framework and, for configurations 2 and 3, the UPCité digital health reference book as a knowledge base
#### Constraints
- Generated OSCEs were in French and in the digital health domain, which the authors state may limit generalizability to other medical specialties

### Target Learners
- Health professions students (medicine, pharmacy, nursing, rehabilitation, midwifery, dentistry)

### Learning Goals
- Assessment of digital health competencies such as health data, cybersecurity, communication, digital tools, and telehealth
