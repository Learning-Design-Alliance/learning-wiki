# Incorporate needle guides into CVS training to promote safer practice for novice learners

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that training for ultrasound-guided chorionic villus sampling incorporate needle guides, because guide use consistently improved novice performance in transfer tests regardless of the training method used. The authors conclude that "incorporating needle guides into CVS training could promote safer practice for novice learners", particularly as clinical training opportunities decline with the uptake of NIPT.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to ultrasound systems with compatible needle guides (the study used the GE HealthCare C1-5 non-sterile ultrasound needle guide) and simulation models for practice.
#### Constraints
- The benefit was demonstrated in complete ultrasound novices; the authors note the benefit "may be more pronounced in this group than in more experienced practitioners".
- Only the early phase of the learning curve was assessed; longer-term effects of training technique are unclear.

### Target Learners
- Medical students and novice ultrasound practitioners learning invasive fetal procedures

### Learning Goals
- Safe and competent performance of ultrasound-guided chorionic villus sampling
