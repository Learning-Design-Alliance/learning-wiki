# SFA® ability-grouped 90-minute daily reading block with sequenced phonics-to-comprehension instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
SFA® elementary reading programs "combine cooperative-learning strategies with detailed lessons, which incorporate multimedia, puppet skits, and videos to support students' engagement and classroom instruction." Students in prekindergarten through first grade participate in Reading Roots, and students in second grade and above participate in Reading Wings. Students are grouped into reading classes of 15–20 students with others of similar reading ability, regardless of age or grade level, during a daily 90-minute reading period; regrouping by demonstrated improvement lets teachers teach the whole class without smaller within-class groups. Instruction is sequenced across several years, emphasizing phonemic awareness initially and broader reading skills later, with daily tutoring for struggling readers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Certified teachers or paraprofessionals to provide daily tutoring to students who have difficulty reading at the same level as their classmates
- Regrouping structures that allow reading classes of 15–20 students of similar ability across age or grade levels
#### Constraints
- The review focuses on the literacy component implemented within the whole-school reform program, so most students also received other program components

### Target Learners
- beginning readers in grades K–4

### Learning Goals
- phonemic awareness, phonics, listening and speaking vocabulary, reading comprehension, and composition writing
