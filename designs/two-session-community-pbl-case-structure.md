# Two-session community-oriented PBL case structure (core time 1 and core time 2)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A reusable PBL session pattern in which each patient case is divided into two approximately 3-hour sessions per week over 2 consecutive weeks. In core time 1 students receive the patient's history and physical examination findings; in core time 2 investigation findings and treatment plans are discussed. Students work in randomly composed groups of 7-8 with randomly selected, standardized faculty tutors, and the same case scenario is used for all students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Small groups with a tutor given standardized instructions and materials before the sessions to ensure consistency
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness may vary depending on instructors' teaching skills, despite standardized tutor training

### Target Learners
- fourth-year medical students transitioning from preclinical to clinical clerkships

### Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning, community health care understanding, and self-directed learning
