# Explanatory schema relating learning processes activated by creative project-based lessons to effects on students and faculty

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review developed an explanatory framework from content analysis of 17 studies, organizing six themes — enhanced learning, collaborative learning, deep learning, student engagement, student disengagement, and faculty engagement — into "an explanatory schema relating learning processes activated by creative project-based lessons and the effects on students and faculty" (Fig. 2, Table 2). It links student-created models and projects to self-directed learning, cognitive load modulation, and metacognitive behaviours, mediated by mentorship and group learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Creative project-based lessons require student autonomy in design and production, team-based work, and consistent engagement from team members or mentors
#### Constraints
- Challenges include high task demands, cognitive and emotional intensiveness, and mismatch with students' professional identities

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Learning Goals
- preclinical and clinical medicine topics
- collaborative learning
- deep learning
