# TPB-based 12-week active learning health education program for older adults

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 12-week community health education program for older adults structured by the Theory of Planned Behaviour, targeting attitudes (weekly health talks), subjective norms (group discussions and collaborative activities) and perceived behavioural control (self-planning sessions and exploratory homework). "Each 90-minute session offered a structured opportunity for older adults to engage in health lectures (30 min), group discussions (30 min), individual daily planning (20 min), and exploratory learning assignments (10 min)", delivered by providers with nursing and public health backgrounds and covering twelve weekly themes from orientation to summarizing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A trained primary provider and facilitators with nursing and public health backgrounds, pre-program protocol meetings, and collaboration with community stakeholders on local characteristics, needs and resources
#### Constraints
- Evaluated over only three months in a large urban Chinese setting; the control group received pamphlets only, so awareness effects of class participation could not be excluded

### Target Learners
- Community-dwelling adults aged 60 and older

### Learning Goals
- Health literacy: accessing, assessing and utilizing health information; health knowledge, beliefs, behaviours, skills and intentions
