# Web-based education program on schizophrenia and help-seeking for parents of adolescents

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A web-based education program developed by the authors to improve understanding of schizophrenia, including promotion of help-seeking. Its content included "help-seeking, i.e. how to prevent progression and exacerbation of the disorder, signs of progression, and consultation alternatives". It comprised 12 slides with narration, required 13 minutes to complete, and was delivered via the same internet website used for the questionnaire survey. In the study, all 2690 parent participants viewed it between the two questionnaires.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Internet access via the survey website through which the program was delivered
#### Constraints
- The program was evaluated with a one-week post-viewing questionnaire among Japanese parents; the article reports no longer-term follow-up

### Target Learners
- Parents of junior and senior high school students in Japan

### Learning Goals
- Improve understanding of schizophrenia symptoms
- Promote timely medical help-seeking behavior
