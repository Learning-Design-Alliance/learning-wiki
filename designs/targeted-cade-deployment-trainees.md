# Targeted deployment of CADe prioritizing trainees and early-career endoscopists

## Description
The article recommends prioritizing CADe use among trainees and early-career endoscopists, where a clear PDR-5 benefit was observed, rather than deploying it universally. The authors state these results "support a targeted deployment strategy for CADe, prioritizing its use among trainees and early-career endoscopists." The added value for high-volume, experienced endoscopists in real-world practice may be limited.
