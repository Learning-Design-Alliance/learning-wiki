# Masterclass session structure: education phase, training phase, test phase, and discussion of results

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article structures the one-day masterclass into four sequential phases. As printed: 'The Masterclass in Astrophysics lasts one day and is characterized by: an education phase, a training phase, a test phase, and eventually a discussion and comment of the results.' The morning education phase features two lectures on theoretical foundations; the training phase has couples of students characterizing sources under researcher guidance; the final phase includes a debate on communicating results and distribution of a mock scientific paper listing all participants as authors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Senior researcher guiding the day plus tutors (PhD or undergraduate students) each following about four groups to verify each step before the group moves on
#### Constraints
- Designed for a group of about 40 high school students, 16-18 years old, in the Department computer lab

### Target Learners
- high school students aged 16-18

### Learning Goals
- carrying out research studies on galaxies and black holes
- understanding the research process as a continuous evolving process of discoveries
