# The web-quest «Терморегуляція в живій природі» integrates biology and physics learning through QR-coded decoding tasks and Google Forms submission

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a web-quest «Терморегуляція в живій природі» used while studying «Основи термодинаміки» (grade 10). During the web-quest pupils deepen biology and physics knowledge, consolidate knowledge of thermal phenomena and applying it to practical tasks, improve Internet search-and-processing skills and independent work, and learn to rationally manage their time. Within it pupils play the game «Дешифрувальник», decoding a «фізична веселка» with tasks offered by the fictional characters Лірик and Мудрик; they decode coded proverbs, send answers via a form under the QR code, and answer which type of heat exchange the proverbs concern. Answers are submitted via Google Forms embedded in the task page.
