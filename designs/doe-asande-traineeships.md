# DOE-funded AS&E traineeships (ASET, Courant, CAST, VITA)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Four DOE-funded traineeship programs support AS&E graduate students at universities with 5-year grants. Each is "designed to start ~4-6 university graduate students per year in the field with two years of capped funding eligibility," providing a start before students transition to national laboratories to complete MS or PhD thesis research. The programs are ASET (MSU, FY 2017), Courant (Stony Brook and Cornell, FY 2020), CAST (IIT and NIU, FY 2020), and VITA (Old Dominion, Norfolk State, and Hampton Universities, FY 2022). VITA emphasizes underrepresented minority recruitment and MS education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Available and continuous laboratory programs to host students after initial university training
- Sustained funding continuity beyond the 5-year award period
#### Constraints
- Capped student support of $110k is small relative to the estimated $300k-$400k cost to produce a PhD
- Funding awards of 5 years are shorter than the typical time to establish a reputable PhD program
- Traineeship relies on USPAS scholarships (which do not fund travel) for specialized training

### Target Learners
- Graduate students in physics, mechanical engineering, and electrical engineering

### Learning Goals
- Preparation for accelerator science and engineering research careers at national laboratories
