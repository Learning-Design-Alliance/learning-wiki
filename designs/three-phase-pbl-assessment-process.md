# Three-phase assessment process: Pass check, Score determination, and Grading

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The framework organizes evaluation into three sequential phases. The Pass check phase screens for attendance, task engagement, ethical conduct, and team involvement, excluding students with severe deficiencies from ranking. The Score determination phase applies Z-MABAC to rank remaining students across 15 criteria. The Grading phase assigns grades A through D using percentile thresholds from a predefined distribution vector π=[0.20,0.45,0.30,0.05].
