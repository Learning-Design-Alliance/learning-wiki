# Quantum Mechanics for Everyone edX course as field-tested teacher-training material

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes an online edX course, "Quantum Mechanics for Everyone," that incorporates the analyzer-loop and two-slit materials and provides "the background needed for a teacher to develop a conceptual understanding of quantum mechanics." The online class "has had over 45,000 enrollees," and a companion paper reports teaching quantum mechanics to over 28,000 nonscientists. It employs computer-based animations and tutorials that help students grasp complex ideas and explore the systems they work with.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- It cannot be done too quickly; the course runs over a 4-6 week period with 7-10 hours per week of effort
#### Constraints
- The materials are field tested and evidence informed, but not evidence based; education research has not been performed on these ideas yet

### Target Learners
- Nonscientists, teachers, and the quantum-illiterate but well-educated public

### Learning Goals
- Conceptual understanding of quantum mechanics for the second quantum revolution
