# Three-tier train-the-trainer professional development cascade through school networks

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A district-scale professional development delivery pattern in which each school network sends experienced teachers to full-day district-sponsored sessions three to four times per year; these teacher facilitators "replicated these sessions at network Teacher Leader Institutes," and teacher leaders then share the content with colleagues in their own schools. The district considered this three-tier distributed approach the best way to reach its sizeable workforce. The pattern trades reach for control: the district had less assurance that every teacher received the professional development, and participation levels varied widely across schools.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Organized school networks with identified experienced teacher facilitators and time for Teacher Leader Institutes
- Coordination of session content across years
#### Constraints
- The district had less control over making sure each and every teacher received professional development on the new standards

### Target Learners
- All district math teachers, network leaders, and administrators

### Learning Goals
- Building capacity to teach to new, more challenging standards
