# Across-subject online experiment comparing AI-generated and human-made teaching videos

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An online experiment with an "across-subject experimental design" in which 447 participants completed two treatment conditions presenting different mixes of AI-generated and human-made videos, with 1788 video treatments in total. Participants rated learning experiences after each video and took a multiple-choice exam at the end of the session.
