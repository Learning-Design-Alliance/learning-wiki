# Three informatics-in-science-teaching courses: an instrumental tools course, a course on informatics for fundamental-level science teaching, and a course on informatics for biology, physics or chemistry teaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The proposal includes information and communication technology formation aligned with Mercosur official documents: "A estrutura curricular aqui proposta tem três disciplinas sobre uso de informática no ensino de ciências." The first is instrumental, covering tools for editing texts, spreadsheets and presentations and the resources available on the Internet; the second treats possibilities of using informatics in science teaching at the fundamental level; the third treats the use of informatics in teaching biology, physics or chemistry. The proposal also prepares future teachers for computer-controlled experiments, simulation of physical phenomena, and the elaboration and use of hypertexts and other learning objects.
