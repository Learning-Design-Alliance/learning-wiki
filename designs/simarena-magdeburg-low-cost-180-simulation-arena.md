# SimArena Magdeburg (SAM): a low-cost 180° simulation arena for emergency medical simulation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The SimArena Magdeburg (SAM) is a semi-circular 180° curved wooden projection screen on which two synchronized short-throw projectors display panoramic video, with ambient sound, a fan, fog machine and flash strobe to represent emergency scenarios. It was developed at Otto-von-Guericke University Magdeburg for "a realistic, secure, quickly interchangeable, and standardized environment" for emergency medical simulations and exams, and is used regularly in curricular and optional emergency medicine courses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires two synchronized short-throw projectors, NVIDIA Surround synchronization, Immersive Display software, 360° panoramic video footage cropped to 180°, and playback via QuickTime or VLC player.
#### Constraints
- Development quality trade-off: "the quality of the video recordings, video projections and ambient sounds will be lower" than high-cost arenas, and the diameter is slightly smaller with a 180° rather than 270° screen due to spatial conditions.

### Target Learners
- medical students (third to sixth year) in emergency medical education

### Learning Goals
- emergency medical simulation training, lay resuscitation skills, stress resistance under realistic conditions
