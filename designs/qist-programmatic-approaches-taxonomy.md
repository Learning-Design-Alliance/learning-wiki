## Description
The article organizes QIST curricular implementation into eight program forms, divided into those that likely do not need accreditation (modules, new courses, summer institute, minor) and those that likely do (certificate, concentration or track, major, master's program). It states: "Below, we list a variety of solutions for providing QIST education in PUIs. The items are roughly in order of increasing scale of eﬀ ort." Table I summarizes each approach's advantages and disadvantages, e.g. modules have "Little/no insitutional overhead" but the student experience is not formally documented.
