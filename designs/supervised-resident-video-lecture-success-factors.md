# Key success factors for a supervised resident video-lecture curriculum

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's Table 2 enumerates the design characteristics the authors identify as key to their program's success: a comprehensive 2-year surgical curriculum; resident tasks of searching and preparing up-to-date literature reviews; expert supervision including defining learning objectives; an "Interactive presentation with 3–5 questions asked by the presenter to test the knowledge of the audience"; and a 15–20 minute length with a 10-minute additional discussion opened by the expert. Each factor is paired with a stated benefit for residents, such as reliable topic collection, independent literature searching, timely error correction, active audience involvement, and presenting complex topics in a clear time frame.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Definition of a comprehensive surgical curriculum to be delivered over a 2-y period
- Supervision by an established expert covering preparation, presentation, and defining the learning objectives
- Strict quality control of published material, including professional revision before publication
#### Constraints
- Costs of roughly 1000 Euro per published video, with a complete curriculum estimated at 50,000 to 100,000 Euro, must be borne by the institution

### Target Learners
- surgical residents
- resident presenters in academic centers

### Learning Goals
- presentation skills
- literature searching and appraisal
- comprehensive surgical curriculum knowledge
