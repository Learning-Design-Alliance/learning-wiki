# Closer to the Sky: co-produced astronomy courses and de-colonial courseware in the PPG favela

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Closer to the Sky co-produces scientific knowledge between astronomers and artists/educators living in the favela of Cantagalo Pavão e Pavãozinho (PPG), Rio de Janeiro, for children, teenagers and young adults of the community. It results from collaboration between the PPG Astronomical Club and the social project 'Ninho das aguias', where classes and night sky observations are held. The project offers extracurricular courses and cultural experiences, provides positive role models of scientists from afro-descendant backgrounds, uses arts and embodiment to improve the learning experience, and creates novel de-colonial courseware based on contextualised science shared as open educational resources.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Collaboration between astronomers and local artists/educators; in the next year, five students will be trained as astroguides in collaboration with Astronera, benefiting from visits and courses at the local Planetarium and Museum of Astronomy.
#### Constraints
- The article notes that children in favelas attend public schools where, according to SAEB 2021, students do not reach a satisfactory level in Portuguese language (69%) and mathematics (95%), and hardly have access to after-school courses.

### Target Learners
- Children, teenagers and young adults living in the favela of Cantagalo Pavão e Pavãozinho, Rio de Janeiro

### Learning Goals
- Astronomy knowledge
- English language skills
- Strengthened chance of staying in education after secondary school
