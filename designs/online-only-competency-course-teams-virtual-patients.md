# Online-only eight-week competency-based medical course with virtual patient encounters (Microsoft Teams, BYOD)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The studied course translated a compulsory eight-week interdisciplinary training course for N = 193 fifth-year medical students (obstetrics, paediatrics, human genetics among others) to an e-learning-only format with a bring-your-own-device concept. 'Key lectures (groups of 90, 24 hours) and interactive seminars (groups of 15, 34 hours) were all offered as mandatory interactive online seminars via Microsoft Teams.' Students worked weekly on one long case in groups of six per teacher, taking patient histories via Teams, with daily supervision, PICO-questions, and a discharge letter in patient-friendly language.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Virtual patient and teacher encounters only, with students in live camera contact; a well-functioning laptop and a fast, stable internet connection on both sides
#### Constraints
- Individual skills in physical examination and manual skills could potentially not be conveyed in this setting
- Anonymized course evaluation had a low response rate of N = 68 students (35%)

### Target Learners
- 5th-year medical students in an interdisciplinary clinical course

### Learning Goals
- evidence-based case management including data research and reflection, history taking, and medical report writing
