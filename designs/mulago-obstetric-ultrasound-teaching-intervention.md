# Two-hour didactic-plus-hands-on obstetric ultrasound teaching intervention

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A short instructional course combining didactic education with hands-on demonstration, totaling "approximately two hours". Topics included "fundamentals of performing an ultrasound and optimizing an image, fetal presentation, placental position, amniotic fluid index, fetal biometry and biophysical profile". Hands-on skills were taught in small groups of two to three participants at a time using patient volunteers with ongoing second-trimester pregnancies, delivered by a single trainer, with a post-test administered immediately afterward.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A trainer with senior-level experience in basic obstetric ultrasound
- Patient volunteers with ongoing pregnancies for the hands-on demonstration portion
- A pre- and post-test survey to assess knowledge change
#### Constraints
- The overall training period was very short, as the authors state

### Target Learners
- Medical students
- Junior house officers
- Senior house officers

### Learning Goals
- Basic obstetric ultrasound skills: fetal lie and presentation, placental position, amniotic fluid volume assessment, fetal biometry and biophysical profile
