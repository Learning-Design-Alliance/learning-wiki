# Breaking up a traditional lecture with interactive group units plus a voluntary self-study research project

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
This course-level pattern restructures a traditional lecture by replacing three 90-minute lecture units with group work on worksheets and computer exercises, and by adding a voluntary research-proposal sketch in self-study with bonus-point presentations. The article describes that "drei Vorlesungseinheiten durch Gruppenar-beiten mit Arbeitsbl¨attern und Computer¨ubungen er- setzt" were made to deepen and apply method backgrounds, with lecturers acting as learning companions. Evaluation via triangulated questionnaires, observations and exams showed students highly approved and most learning objectives were better supported.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tasks for worksheets and computer exercises must be didactically valuable, which the authors note takes considerable preparation time.
- Ideally one instructor per one to two small groups should be present for in-depth, tailored exchange.
#### Constraints
- Only one of the lecture-relevant methods could be treated within the time frame, limiting transfer to other methods.
- The study's results are not statistically rigorous and refer primarily to the investigated course.

### Target Learners
- first-semester master's students in materials science

### Learning Goals
- deepening and applying lecture content
- understanding methods and their difficulties
- raising interest in research
