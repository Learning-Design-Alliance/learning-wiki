# Framework synthesizing the Campinha-Bacote Model of Cultural Competence with Kern's Six-Step Approach for transborder simulation curriculum adaptation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes a framework for transborder simulation curriculum adaptation by synthesizing two existing models: Campinha-Bacote's cultural competence model, with "five primary constructs: Cultural Desire, Cultural Awareness, Cultural Knowledge, Cultural Skill, and Cultural Encounters", and Kern's Six-Step Approach to curriculum design. Qualitative findings are mapped onto Kern's steps (Fig. 1), locating each of the nine themes at a temporal point in the design process while remaining iterative and recursive throughout.

### Target Learners
- Health professions faculty and simulation staff engaged in transborder curriculum partnerships

### Learning Goals
- Cultural competence in curriculum adaptation
- Successful transfer and adaptation of simulation-based education curricula across cultural contexts
