# Proposed Quantum Computing Concept Inventory

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
A proposed standardised, purely conceptual, multiple-choice-style assessment instrument for quantum computing, modelled on the Force Concept Inventory. It would carry "non-mathematical, jargon-free, conceptual questions that are accessible to students who have yet to start their quantum studies" and allow instructors to measure teaching effectiveness against a widely recognised benchmark comparable between institutions. This paper reports only a preliminary expert-consultation stage toward it.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Significant international buy-in from the quantum computing education community and concepts determined by international leaders across institutions and countries.
#### Constraints
- Development requires rigorous statistical testing in a large quantum computing course and creative jargon-free question design; without a common benchmark, institutions would develop incompatible instruments.

### Target Learners
- primary and high school students in pre-university quantum programs
- university students before quantum mechanics study

### Learning Goals
- conceptual understanding of quantum computing, sensing and communication concepts
