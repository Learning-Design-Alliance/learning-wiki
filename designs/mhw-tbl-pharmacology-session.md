# Meta Horizon Workroom team-based learning session for pharmacology (60-minute virtual application exercise)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A virtual TBL session delivered in the Meta Horizon Workroom with Meta Quest 2 headsets, in which students created avatars and collaborated on a pharmacology case scenario about hypertension and its treatment. The article states: "The TBL session lasted for approximately 60 min, comprising a 10‐min introduction and VR headset training, a 40‐min application exercise and discussion, and a 10‐min post‐session questionnaire." Faculty members were present in the session to facilitate discussion and give immediate feedback, and interactive tools included 3D models and shared whiteboards.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Meta Quest 2 VR headsets and access to the Meta Horizon Workroom platform
- Faculty members present within the MHW session to facilitate discussions and provide immediate feedback
- Individual preparatory reading of assigned materials before the session
- Initial VR headset and MHW training for participants
#### Constraints
- Piloted with seven questionnaire respondents from a cohort of approximately 82 students
- Participants were recruited for prior pharmacology competence and comfort with immersive technology
- Technical issues such as echo and login difficulties occurred during the session

### Target Learners
- Year 4 medical students

### Learning Goals
- Applying pharmacology concepts (hypertension and its treatment) to a clinical case scenario
- Collaboration and communication within team-based learning
