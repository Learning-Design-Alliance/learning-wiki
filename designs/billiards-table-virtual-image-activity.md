# Miniature billiards-table guided-inquiry activity for virtual image formation

## Description
A small, inexpensive billiards table is used in a guided-inquiry activity based on the research-verified Physics by Inquiry text, with billiard balls acting as light. Students locate the image of the 8-ball via the method of parallax, draw the image beside the table, bank the cue ball off the sidewall to hit the 8-ball, mark incident and reflected paths with tape or chalk, and use protractors to show that "the angle of incidence is equal to the angle of reflection". Repeating the procedure from a second cue-ball location guides students to an operational procedure for determining image location via intersecting straight-line motions through the mirror.
