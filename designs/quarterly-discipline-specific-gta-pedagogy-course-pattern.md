# Quarter-long discipline-specific GTA pedagogy course combining weekly active learning, readings, peer sharing, and reflection

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Biology 301 is a required graduate course meeting weekly for 50 minutes across a 10-week quarter, designed around recommendations for teaching PD: discipline specificity, emphasis on student learning, practicing pedagogical skills, active learning, collective participation, and self-reflection. Each week GTAs shared classroom experiences, read a pedagogy paper (topics including classroom discourse, active learning, metacognition, inclusivity, formative assessment, and cooperative learning), discussed it after experiencing an active-learning technique, and developed and presented their own active-learning activities, with biweekly reflections embedded.
