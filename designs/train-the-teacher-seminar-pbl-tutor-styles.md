# Train-the-Teacher seminar preparing PBL tutors for defined facilitative and non-facilitative roles

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 60-minute seminar in which all four tutors refreshed their PBL experience and familiarized themselves with the facilitative and non-facilitative tutoring styles, supervised by an established expert (Master of Medical Education). At its end, "it was checked, if the tutors can correctly act in the different styles using video-analysis". Despite this preparation, tutors reported in interviews that non-facilitative tutoring was difficult to apply and that more practicing of the two styles was needed (n = 18 suggestions).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Supervision by an experienced PBL expert and video-analysis verification of correct style enactment
#### Constraints
- A 60-minute format that tutors still judged insufficient, requesting more practice of the two styles

### Target Learners
- experienced PBL tutors (content experts in endodontics)

### Learning Goals
- enacting defined tutoring styles reliably in PBL sessions
