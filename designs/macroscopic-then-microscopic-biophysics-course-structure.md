# A general education biophysics course structured as two halves: macroscopic scaling topics followed by microscopic cellular topics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course divides a ten-week term into a macroscopic first half focused on how physical forces and biomechanical properties scale with organism size, and a microscopic second half covering cellular and subcellular phenomena. As the article states, "The ten week term is roughly divided into two halves," with the first covering scaling concepts and the second targeting Brownian motion, biomolecules, and self-assembly. The structure serves three goals: understanding how physical principles guide life, improving comprehension of quantitative data and models, and improving understanding of how scientific understanding develops.

### Target Learners

### Learning Goals

- understanding physical principles governing life
- quantitative estimation and graph interpretation
