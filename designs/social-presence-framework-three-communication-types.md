## Description
The article uses a social presence framework comprising three communication types — interpersonal communication, open communication, and cohesive communication — as the design basis for the learning buddy chatbot in Study 2. The framework addresses the online-learning challenges of “the lack of student self-regulation, and the sense of isolation” by structuring chatbot conversation around these three communication dimensions to support learners in fully online activities.
