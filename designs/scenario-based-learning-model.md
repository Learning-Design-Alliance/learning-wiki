# Scenario-based learning model

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The article defines scenario-based learning as "a learning model, based on having the student put him/herself into an actor/actress, and display the goals and behaviors for solving the problems they come across, by plotting the expected goals and behaviors within the framework of a scenario". By bringing true or likewise real-life stories into the classroom, it enables students to think about real events, transfer knowledge to them, and research solutions, so that "students come to structuring their knowledge" (Veznedaroğlu, 2005). In this study it is implemented through weekly online scenarios tied to students' school practice experiences.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Scenarios drawn from true or real-life-like professional stories, presented in an environment where students can respond and discuss solutions
#### Constraints
- In this study scenarios were introduced in the form of texts; the authors note video-based scenarios as a future direction

### Target Learners
- preservice teachers

### Learning Goals
- structuring professional knowledge
- solving profession-specific problems from multiple points of view
