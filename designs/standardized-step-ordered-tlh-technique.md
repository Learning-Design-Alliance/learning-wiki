# Standardized step-ordered total laparoscopic hysterectomy technique

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Since 2008, all TLHs at the institution follow a standardized intrafascial technique in which "the same standard steps are performed in the same order for every TLH." The fixed sequence covers patient positioning and pneumoperitoneum, trocar placement, round ligament and broad ligament dissection, uterine vessel section, colpotomy, transvaginal uterus removal, and x-shaped plus continuous colporrhaphy sutures. The authors report that standardization made surgical outcomes comparable regardless of surgeon and eased resident training by removing exposure to multiple technique variations.
