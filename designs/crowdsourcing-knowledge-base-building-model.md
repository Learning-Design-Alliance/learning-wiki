# Crowdsourcing as a knowledge base building model with three primary design issues

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article organizes crowdsourced content creation using the taxonomy of Saxton et al. (2013), placing ASSISTments' approach in the knowledge base building model, where "information- or knowledge-generation processes are outsourced to community users" with incentives and quality control. It distinguishes crowdsourcing from the 'wisdom of crowds' and separates teachersourcing from learnersourcing. Three issues structure the design space: what is outsourced, crowd collaboration, and managerial control over quality.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Incentive measures and quality control mechanisms to elicit quality knowledge from the crowd
#### Constraints
- Managerial control over contribution quality is described as one of the greater challenges of crowdsourcing in general

### Target Learners
- K-12 students using online learning platforms

### Learning Goals
- Mathematics problem solving supported by elaborated feedback
