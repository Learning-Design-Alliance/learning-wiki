# GenAI agent embedded in VIPE small-group processes as an instructional component

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The element is a generative artificial intelligence-based agent (genAI agent) placed into the small group processes of student teams during a session of a well-established virtual interprofessional education methodology. The article describes it as "this novel genAI tool" whose "integration into each student team was an intentional pedagogical technique". Its purpose is to introduce controversy and counter groupthink in newly formed interprofessional teams engaged in collaborative problem-based learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Integration into each student team's small-group processes during a VIPE problem-based learning session
#### Constraints
- Designed for newly formed student teams that rapidly come together in VIPE sessions

### Target Learners
- Interprofessional health professions students

### Learning Goals
- Collaborative problem-based learning
- Countering groupthink in team decision processes
