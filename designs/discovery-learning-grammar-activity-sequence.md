# Discovery learning activity sequence for English grammar (diagnose, design, interactions, presentation)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes a set of classroom activities designed to implement discovery learning for standard V English grammar: 'Diagnose the problems, Designing the Discovery learning, Face–to- Face interaction, Internet interaction, Team interaction, Team pair Table and Presentation'. The sequence begins by diagnosing learning obstacles from a pre-test, then has pupils discover grammar content through face-to-face, internet and team interaction before presenting results. Planning guidance includes selecting an activity, gathering materials, staying focused, using caution, planning extra time, recording process and results, discussing and reviewing, and trying again.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A researcher-made achievement test to diagnose problems and evaluate grammar skills
- Planning steps including selecting an activity, gathering materials, and recording process and results
#### Constraints
- The study confined the sequence to 60 standard V pupils in one middle school and to the Tamilnadu state board English textbook grammar

### Target Learners
- standard V primary school pupils learning English as a second language

### Learning Goals
- functional English grammar achievement
