# Three-station messenger-structured escape room with coordinate-reveal narrative

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The escape room is organized as three interactive stations, each focused on one cosmic messenger and its detection method, embedded in an immersive astrophysical narrative. Completing each station's challenges reveals part of the celestial coordinates, and the full coordinates let participants identify the event's location in the planetarium software Stellarium, giving the experience what the authors call "a scientifically meaningful outcome." Stations map to real experiments: Pierre Auger Observatory for cosmic rays, MAGIC/CTAO for gamma rays, and KM3NeT for neutrinos.
