# Physics GTA Preparation course: Orientation, Follow-Up Meetings, and out-of-class activities

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A preparation course for first-time physics GTAs at Georgia Tech, structured in three parts: a pre-semester Orientation (about 3/4 of contact hours), Follow-Up Meetings every 2-3 weeks during the semester, and out-of-class activities (workload surveys, classroom observations, mentoring meetings). Between 2013 and 2020, 174 graduate students participated. Topics include policies, teaching physics, classroom management, lab simulation, microteaching, grading, midterm evaluations, teaching videos, and teaching and research. A full set of course materials is available at https://tinyurl.com/ealiceaGTAPD.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dedicated instructor (originally a Super TA, now a non-tenure-track faculty member)
- Departmental administration support and partnership with the Center for Teaching and Learning
- Video recording equipment (approximately $2000) for classroom observations
#### Constraints
- Designed specifically for first-time GTAs in the School of Physics at Georgia Tech; Fall 2020 delivery moved online due to COVID-19

### Target Learners
- first-year Ph.D. students concurrently enrolled in a Graduate Teaching Assistantship

### Learning Goals
- learner-centered teaching practices
- physics pedagogical content knowledge
- grading and classroom management
- transferable professional skills
