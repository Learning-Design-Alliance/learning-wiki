# Seven-hour co-development-based change-agent training for practising occupational therapists

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A continuing-education workshop of seven hours built on a co-development pedagogical approach leveraging participants' prior experience. Its theoretical content covered four themes: "l’analyse du contexte de réalisation du projet de changement, le développement de partenariat, les stratégies de communication et la planification des actions". It was offered on seven days between January 19 and October 19, 2019, to occupational therapists recruited via professional association member lists, and evaluated with a pre-post questionnaire and group interviews.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants needed sufficient French comprehension, the training's language; there were no exclusion criteria
#### Constraints
- The training was not specifically focused on systemic injustices, though it addressed elements relevant to meso- and macro-system action

### Target Learners
- practising occupational therapists in Québec

### Learning Goals
- exercising agency as a change agent
- effective communication
- intentional collaboration
- observation and analysis
- action planning
