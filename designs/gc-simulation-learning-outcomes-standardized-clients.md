# Co-designed simulation learning outcomes and standardized clients for genetic counselor education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A set of six simulation learning outcomes and eight standardized clients built with experienced genetic counselors using co-design principles for a master of genetic counseling program. The outcomes cover "establishing an effective counseling relationship, eliciting information, assessing need, delivering difficult news and helping clients cope with complex emotions, effective communication and facilitating adaptation". Real cases were de-identified into detailed case studies, mapped to learning outcomes and program stages, and sequenced from straightforward to complex across the two-year program.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Standardized clients must be reviewed for accuracy, authenticity, and privacy by the genetic counselors involved in the real cases
- The authors state regular review and update of the standardized clients is vitally important to optimize learning
#### Constraints
- The authors question the extent to which outcomes around communicating effectively and facilitating adaptation can be achieved in a brief simulated interaction
- Further work may be needed to validate each standardized client for each learning outcome

### Target Learners
- first-year master of genetic counseling students (novice student genetic counselors)

### Learning Goals
- establishing an effective counseling relationship
- eliciting information
- assessing need
- delivering difficult news and helping clients cope with complex emotions
- effective communication
- facilitating adaptation
