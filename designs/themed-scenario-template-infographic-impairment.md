# Scenario template organizing patient and caregiver information by themes with infographic impairment indicators

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A reusable scenario design pattern in which information is classified by topics for the patient (e.g. situation summary, self-care impairment, sociodemographic data, personal background) and the caregiver (e.g. availability to provide care, self-efficacy, affective relationship), supplemented by illustrative graphical elements. "The traffic light system was chosen to indicate the level of impairment": green for no impairment, yellow for moderate and red for severe, with three equal icons per concept for students who have difficulty distinguishing colours. The template adopts the ten domains of the self-care dependency evaluation form.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Intentional and systematic planning by the teaching team to identify and classify the information usually provided in scenarios
#### Constraints
- Developed for the specific topic of a person regaining independence and returning home after an acute event, with adaptation to other contexts proposed but not tested

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students in laboratory realistic simulation
