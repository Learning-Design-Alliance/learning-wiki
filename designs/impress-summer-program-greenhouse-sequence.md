# IMPRESS summer program and greenhouse-effect model construction sequence

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
IMPRESS (Integrating Metacognitive Practices and Research to Ensure Student Success) is a two-week bridge program at Rochester Institute of Technology for matriculating first-generation and/or deaf and hard of hearing students, designed to develop metacognitive skills through intensive laboratory experiments, reflective practices, and small-group discussion. Its greenhouse-effect sequence spans Days 2-5: initial model construction, thermal absorption and reflection experiments with model revisions, CO2 absorption testing, and final model construction, with temperature data collected via thermometer probes.
