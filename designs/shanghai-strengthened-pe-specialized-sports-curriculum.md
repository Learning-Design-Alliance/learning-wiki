# Shanghai strengthened PE pilot curriculum: two 90-minute specialized sports sessions replacing four 40-minute general fitness sessions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A school-based PE curriculum reform in one Shanghai high school that, without changing the number of weekly sessions, combined two 40-minute sessions and the intervening break into one 90-minute session and shifted content from general fitness training to specialized sports chosen by students. The article states the program involved "a shift from a 40-minute traditional cardiovascular fitness oriented curriculum to a 90-minute specialized sports training oriented curriculum", with coaching in football, volleyball, badminton, table tennis, tennis and aerobics, basic skills training, fitness work and competitive games.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- PE teachers with relevant expertise coach the specialized sports
- students choose a sport of personal interest and train in it across their high school years
#### Constraints
- evaluated in one high school in Yangpu district, Shanghai; generalizability limited by the authors to China or a similar East Asian setting

### Target Learners
- grade-10 high school students in China facing high academic burden

### Learning Goals
- increase interest and participation in physical activity
- cognitively enrich exercise through specialized sports training
