# Double-dose semester block: Strategic Reading first semester, English 1 second semester

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
TDHS gives ninth graders a double dose of reading: students take double periods of Strategic Reading, 90 minutes daily, in the first semester, and then double periods of English 1 in the second semester. This sequencing concentrates intensive reading instruction before the standard English course. The pattern is the structural backbone of the reading component of the whole-school model.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Scheduling capacity for 90-minute daily double reading periods in the first semester
#### Constraints
- Applies to ninth graders in high-poverty schools as implemented in the qualifying study

### Target Learners
- Ninth graders in high-poverty high schools

### Learning Goals
- Reading comprehension
- Reading fluency
