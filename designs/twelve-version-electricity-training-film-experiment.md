# Twelve-version training-film experiment on elementary electricity

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A factorial set of "Twelve different training-film presentations on elementary electricity" combining four review treatments and three sub-titling treatments, each shown to 3 high school classes for a total of 36 classes and 1263 subjects with pre- and post-tests. The design allowed the study to compare massed versus spaced versus no review and sub-titling levels within one experiment.
