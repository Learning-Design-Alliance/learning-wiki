# Delirium e-learning course for hospital nursing staff

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A four-hour e-learning course on delirium developed by a commercial publisher with a Dutch hospital, reviewed by researchers and the Netherlands Centre of Excellence in Nursing, and consistent with Dutch delirium care guidelines. It "incorporated case studies and short tests for self-assessment to facilitate the learning experience" and covered clinical features, risk factors, diagnostics, prevention and treatment across seven chapters, framed by a baseline and a final knowledge test.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Internet access from any computer, access codes sent by email, and a 3-month access period per hospital
#### Constraints
- The course was based on the 2004 Dutch delirium guideline; a 2013 revision was published, though the authors judge the course still suitable because its topics were retained

### Target Learners
- Nurses, nursing students and healthcare assistants on internal medicine and surgical wards of hospitals

### Learning Goals
- Awareness of delirium and its risks
- Knowledge of delirium care including recognition, risk screening, prevention and treatment
