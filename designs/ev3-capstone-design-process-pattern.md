# Capstone engineering design process with free exploration for novice robotics learners

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The camp's novel design pattern trains students through a complete engineering design process culminating in a capstone project: students imagine and create an architecture of the model, plan, work in teams, divide tasks into sub-tasks, construct, program, and finally test and refine the model. The authors write that the process "also involves free exploration and experimentation (developing divergent thinking) in which the instructor only provides guidance when asked rather than providing direct solutions." This positions the instructor as a facilitator rather than a solution provider during model building.
