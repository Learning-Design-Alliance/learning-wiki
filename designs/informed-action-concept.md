# Informed action concept: understand students via metrics, then take targeted, measurable action

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's organizing concept has two motivations: instructors need to understand their students to find a reason for action, and then need to take action that is as targeted and as measurable as possible, which the authors call informed action. Metrics support understanding; for action, "we found three action categories: Encouragement (i.e. personalized emails), Rewarding (i.e. badges) and Material Improvement (i.e. add reading material, re-record videos)", with Encouragement implemented first via targeted emails to discovered groups.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must first gain enough understanding about individual learner activity, via metrics and clustering, before acting.
#### Constraints
- The concept was implemented and evaluated only for Encouragement actions; Rewarding and Material Improvement were not enabled in the tool.

### Target Learners
- MOOC participants

### Learning Goals
- Improving student success through targeted instructor interventions
