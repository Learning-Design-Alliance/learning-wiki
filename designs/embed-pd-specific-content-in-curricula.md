# Embedding personality disorder-specific content, including crisis de-escalation and engagement strategies, into advanced practitioner curricula is essential for safe and effective practice.

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
This principle holds that advanced practitioner curricula should embed personality disorder-specific content covering diagnostic understanding, crisis de-escalation techniques and strategies for engaging individuals with fluctuating presentations. The study demonstrates the need for personality disorder-specific education to equip future ANPs with the knowledge and skills to manage these challenging cases more effectively and with greater confidence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Formal curriculum content on personality disorder assessment, diagnosis and management
- Coverage of crisis de-escalation techniques and strategies for engaging individuals with fluctuating presentations
#### Constraints
- Findings should be interpreted within the UK healthcare context and Northern Ireland policy environment; service structures and advanced practice roles differ internationally

### Target Learners

### Learning Goals
- mental health assessment skills
- risk management
- building therapeutic relationships
