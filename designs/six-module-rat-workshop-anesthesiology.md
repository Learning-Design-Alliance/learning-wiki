# Six-module, 90-minute in-person residents-as-teachers workshop for anesthesiology residents

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention was "a 90-minute, in-person workshop consisting of six modules that included both a theoretical component and small group workshop scenarios." Modules covered the clerkship curriculum, key teaching behaviors (Cleveland Clinic and De Oliveira tools), Kolb's theory and Miller's Pyramid, psychological safety checklists, setting daily learning goals, and the One-Minute Preceptor model. It was developed with Kern's 6-step approach and delivered by an education faculty member, a clinician-educator, and a resident.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Printed checklists for each module; facilitators rotating among small groups to prompt real-time reflection and structured debriefing
#### Constraints
- The authors state the workshop was practical to implement with minimal resources but was not fully context rich compared with the real OR environment

### Target Learners
- Clinical anesthesia residents from first to third year teaching in the OR

### Learning Goals
- Resident teaching skills, psychological safety practices, goal setting, and clinical teaching techniques
