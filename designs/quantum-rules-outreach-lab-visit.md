# Quantum Rules! outreach lab visit at Leiden University

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The Quantum Rules! lab is a project permanently based at the Faculty of Science, Leiden University, containing around 20 quantum experiments designed according to the Dutch quantum curriculum. Each year around 30 school groups of 6VWO students (17-18 years old) visit for a day. A visit comprises roughly 2 hr 30 min of autonomous pair work on chosen experiments supported by teachers and university students, a 45-minute lunch talk with a 20-minute lecture, and one hour of student presentations of their findings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students work autonomously and choose experiments; there is practical work, classmates, contact with experts, and a focus on understanding quantum physics for final exams.
#### Constraints
- The visit structure varies slightly with group size, school and teacher preference.

### Target Learners
- 6VWO upper secondary students in the Netherlands

### Learning Goals
- understanding quantum physics
- positive attitudes towards quantum science
