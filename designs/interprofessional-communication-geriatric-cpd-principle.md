# Structure geriatric continuing education around interprofessional communication as a cornerstone competency for team-based care

## Description
The article identifies interprofessional communication as one of the most highly ranked transversal skills among DACH HCPs and argues it underpins geriatric team functioning. It states this capacity "seems the key to work in geriatric teams as it builds the cornerstone for goal-oriented care planning, respectful collaboration and sustainable patient security", and concludes that these findings set the demand for continued education based on the concept of interprofessional education (IPE).
