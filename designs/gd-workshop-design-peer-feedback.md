# Group discussion workshop design: experience sharing, role-play, peer feedback, and instructor guidance

## Description
The GD intervention consisted of six two-hour interactive workshop sessions with four participants each. Sessions began with participants sharing patient education experiences, followed by a brief instructor presentation on essential patient education skills and role-playing exercises with observers providing peer feedback. The article states each session "comprised 15 min of scenario role-play by participants, followed by 15 min of group discussion and instructor feedback based on the patient-education feedback checklist", with participants rotating through roles across predesigned scenarios.
