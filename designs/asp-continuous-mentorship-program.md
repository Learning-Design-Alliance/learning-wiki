# ASP continuous mentorship program pairing students with international lecturers beyond the school

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The ASP mentorship program is "a dedicated effort to mentor ASP students in collaboration with their academic advisors" and "runs continuously even when there is no formal school." International lecturers help select and mentor students, and the mentorship helps the organizers track alumni academic development. The conclusion notes it allows African students to stay connected to lecturers from abroad and benefit from active mentorship supplementing academic advisors' efforts.
