# Portuguese Law no. 60/2009 establishing mandatory school-based sexuality education with a teacher-coordinator

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Law no. 60/2009 established the regime for sexuality education in Portuguese school settings, making it "mandatory in the educational projects of school clusters and non-clustered schools", with workload adapted to each educational level and distributed across the school year, under a psychosocial or biopsychosocial model. The review notes it established the figure of the teacher-coordinator responsible for introducing, supervising, and implementing sexuality education, and purposes including equality, protection against sexual abuse, and elimination of sexual discrimination. Ordinance no. 196-A/2010 regulated the contents per study cycle.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A teacher-coordinator appointed by each school responsible for implementation
- A minimum number of hours of sexuality education in each grade
#### Constraints
- In 2012 the non-disciplinary curricular areas were extinguished, which the review calls a significant barrier to implementation

### Target Learners
- students in Portuguese basic and secondary education

### Learning Goals
- informed and responsible sexuality
- equality and elimination of sexual discrimination
- protection against sexual abuse
