# Emporium course design combining computer-assisted instruction, cooperative learning, and immediate feedback

## Description
The article describes the emporium method as combining several instructional components: cooperative learning, online instruction, computer-assisted instruction, and immediate feedback from instructors. Students work through online modules at computer workstations during three weekly hours, plus a fourth hour with a GTA focused on workbook practice problems where students lead problem-solving while the GTA facilitates. The design encourages students to be actively involved in acquiring their knowledge and to work together in peer tutoring, building a community of learners.

#### Constraints
