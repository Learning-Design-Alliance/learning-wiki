# UNSW Bachelor of Quantum Engineering: a four-year degree preserving the core structure of UNSW Bachelor of Engineering programs.

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The world's first undergraduate quantum engineering degree, offered by an Engineering Faculty at UNSW Sydney. Its four-year philosophy intentionally preserves the core structure of other UNSW Bachelor of Engineering programs, with engineering design courses "a common thread throughout the entire degree program" and nearly every course including a hands-on laboratory program scaffolded to theory or adopting problem-based learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The program inherits many courses from a conventional electrical engineering program, so students need the standard electrical engineering foundation in mathematics, circuits and signals.
#### Constraints
- The authors report that "avoiding duplication of material yet allowing for each course to be self-contained is a challenge" across the degree's quantum courses.

### Target Learners
- commencing undergraduate engineering students

### Learning Goals
- professional quantum engineering competence spanning quantum technology and electrical and computer engineering
