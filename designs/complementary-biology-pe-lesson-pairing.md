# Complementary interdisciplinary lesson pairing in which biology explains PE processes and PE visualises biology content

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
In this pattern, two subjects' lessons are designed to complement each other: "the biology lesson supplies explanations for processes occurring in PE, whereas the PE lesson provides a visualisation of the theoretical biology content". The test group received interdisciplinary block-classes of three lessons a week in the fitness studio, combining a short theoretical introduction with a practical phase in which scientific input was applied to physical perceptions, observed and reflected upon. In combination the lessons offer a physical experience and critical reflection on what was learned theoretically and practically.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Both groups must deal with the same content so lessons differ only in teaching method
#### Constraints
- The pattern was studied in human biology topics such as muscle build-up, the cardiovascular system, nutrition and endurance training

### Target Learners
- German grammar school students in fifth and sixth form

### Learning Goals
- Connecting theoretical biology content with practical physical experience
- Applicable knowledge of training and human biology
