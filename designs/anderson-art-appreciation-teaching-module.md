# New teaching module using Anderson's model of art appreciation with three sets of test questions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's performance instrument was a new teaching module using Anderson's model of art appreciation, paired with a written-response instrument of three sets of test questions administered in week 1, week 7 and week 14. Reliability was assessed by test-retest with three expert art-educator raters using Cronbach's alpha, yielding good reliability (.813 to .877) across scales for the tests and (.831 to .877) for studio art production.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Three art-educator raters with field expertise in the subject for scoring
- Test-retest reliability procedures at sufficient time intervals
#### Constraints
- Administered only to a small purposive sample of 33 students in a single unique course

### Target Learners
- third-year undergraduate graphic design and multimedia students

### Learning Goals
- art appreciation knowledge
- cognitive knowledge applied in studio art production
