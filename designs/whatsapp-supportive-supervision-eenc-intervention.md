# Digital EENC skills-maintenance intervention combining repeated self-practise with WhatsApp-based supportive supervision from EENC facilitators

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention pairs a standard one-day refresher EENC coaching with repeated, self-directed, practise sessions by district health care providers, with mobile phone-based supportive supervision from EENC facilitators. Providers practise managing breathing and non-breathing babies with simulation equipment once every two weeks and send videos to a hospital-level WhatsApp group, where facilitators review the videos and share feedback. It was co-developed with Ministry of Health officials, midwives, nurses and paediatricians and pilot-tested in four district hospitals in Lao PDR.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A WhatsApp group for each district hospital connecting providers with EENC facilitators for sharing videos
- Simulation equipment available for practise alongside clinical duties
#### Constraints
- Applicable in other countries with limited resources where there is good mobile network coverage
- Time constraints limited self-practise sessions and confidence in sustaining the intervention was low

### Target Learners
- Newborn care providers at district hospitals in Lao PDR and similar low-resource settings

### Learning Goals
- Maintaining EENC knowledge and skills, including management of breathing and non-breathing babies
