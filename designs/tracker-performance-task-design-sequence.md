# Seven-part performance task design sequence progressing from video analysis to video modeling with mentorship

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article enumerates its own performance task design ideas: flip videos for home preparation, starting with simple classroom activities, a primer scientist activity, an integrative dynamics and kinematics lesson flow progressing from video analysis to video modeling, a motivating performance task scenario, assessment rubrics, and close mentorship. The sequence scaffolds students from tool familiarisation toward independent scientist-like inquiry over 6 to 10 weeks.
