# YouTube-integrated lesson routine for EFL course content

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An instructional routine in which the instructor selects short YouTube videos of approximately 10-15 minutes on the class topic, emails the URL to students two days before class, opens class with discussion and a PowerPoint outline, shows the video on a data show, then re-shows it "with frequent stops for further discussion". Students sometimes watch in pairs or small groups to complete tasks, and as homework each student finds a video of their own to present at the next meeting.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to YouTube and a data show in class; email distribution of video URLs before class; instructor time to pre-select appropriate videos
#### Constraints
- The article notes incorporation of YouTube "is not without its challenges", and cited literature notes poor sound quality, pronunciation and slang in some clips can make them harder to understand than traditional listening platforms

### Target Learners
- Second-year EFL college students in a teacher-education program

### Learning Goals
- Learning the theoretical content of a course on classroom observation, including observation lenses and methods of observing and recording
