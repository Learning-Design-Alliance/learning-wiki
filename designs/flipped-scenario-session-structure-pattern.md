# Flipped classroom session structure with pre-class media, entry quiz, mini-lecture, and small-group clinical scenario problem-solving

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A reusable lesson-level structure for flipped sessions in basic-science medical courses. Before class, students study teacher-prepared videos and pamphlets shared via messaging apps; each session opens with "a quiz of five FCQs to assess students' understanding", followed by a 20-minute mini-lecture, then an 80-minute small-group discussion of a clinical scenario presented via video projector and handouts, with randomly selected groups presenting responses for 20 minutes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must access and study pre-class educational videos and pamphlets shared via Eitaa and Telegram before attending class
- Small groups (five groups of six students) for scenario discussion
- Clinical scenarios with video projector and handouts prepared by the teacher
#### Constraints
- Stated in the context of a gastrointestinal physiology course for medical students at a single university
- Requires teacher time to prepare videos, pamphlets, and scenarios

### Target Learners
- medical students in basic-science (physiology) courses

### Learning Goals
- integration of basic science with clinical application
- deep understanding of course concepts
- teamwork and presentation skills
