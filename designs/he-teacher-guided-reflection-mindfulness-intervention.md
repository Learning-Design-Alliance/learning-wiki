# Guided reflection and mindfulness intervention for higher education teachers (4 group meetings, 2 guided reflection sessions, Moodle self-study)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A professional-development intervention for higher education teachers aiming to "help teachers reflect on their own teaching, offer tools to use LFT methods". Teachers participate in 4 group meetings and 2 individual guided reflection sessions (before and after all group meetings), all conducted online via Zoom, with self-study assignments and mindfulness-based exercises completed independently on Moodle between meetings. The intervention was built from three designed elements: training in LFT methods, mindfulness-based stress reduction, and reflection on teaching practices. Stated learning objectives include teaching interactively while supporting one's own well-being, recognizing elements of good teaching, and becoming aware of one's pedagogical choices.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participation required proficiency in Finnish, willingness to participate in the entire research period, willingness to join group meetings using peer support, and ability to use the Moodmetric smart ring for 2 weeks
#### Constraints
- The intervention was conducted in Finnish, so participants not proficient in Finnish were excluded

### Target Learners
- higher education teachers at universities and universities of applied sciences in Finland

### Learning Goals
- learning-focused teaching methods
- pedagogical awareness
- supporting teacher well-being
