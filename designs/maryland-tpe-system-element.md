# Maryland Teacher and Principal Evaluation (TPE) system

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Maryland's statewide educator evaluation system combines measures of professional practice and student growth. Teacher professional practice uses four Charlotte Danielson Framework domains (planning and preparation, instructional practices, classroom environment, professional responsibilities) broken into 22 components, each domain counting 12.5% of the practice score. Student Learning Objectives are the predominant growth measure. The report states "In the overall rating of teacher effectiveness, both professional practice and student growth are given equal weight."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Local evaluation models must include required criteria such as the 50/50 split between professional practice and student growth, and must be endorsed by the local bargaining unit and approved by the state.
#### Constraints
- No single SLO may count for more than 35% of the total performance score.
