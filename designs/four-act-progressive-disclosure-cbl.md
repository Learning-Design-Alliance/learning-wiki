# 4-act progressive-disclosure case structure aligned with occupational poisoning workflow

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A scripted 4-act CBL model that progressively releases key information with teacher-verified distractor cues. Act 1 focuses on problem framing and initial hypothesis generation; Act 2 on occupational history elicitation and laboratory cues; Act 3 on field investigation and exposure attribution; Act 4 on management and prevention decision-making.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each act provides only the minimum information required for the current task, retaining other cues as to be elicited or verified
#### Constraints
- The intervention's scope is bounded by exploratory reasoning and should not be regarded as a general clinical-reasoning enhancer

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning, exposure assessment, and epidemiologic thinking in occupational medicine
