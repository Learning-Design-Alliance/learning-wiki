# Authentic industry-sponsored capstone project embedded in a two-semester course with skill modules and metacognitive reflection

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This course-level design pairs a year-long authentic project sponsored by a quantum-industry company with weekly class meetings featuring lectures on industry skills (programming, measurement uncertainty, linear regression), four "Essential Skills Modules" on project management, bystander-intervention training, company visits, and two forms of metacognitive reflection (weekly reflection questions and photovoice). The design aims to expose students to career possibilities, build skills authentically, and build networks, giving students experiences "often historically available only through individual research positions or internships."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An industry partner supplying a project, funding, and employee interactions
- lab space students can access at any time
- instructor time for twice-weekly meetings
#### Constraints
- The authors note a capstone project exposes students to only one aspect of a very diverse industry, and mid-year the project goal "was changed dramatically" after mismatched expectations between students and the company

### Target Learners
- upper-division undergraduate physics majors

### Learning Goals
- authentic industry project experience
- project-management and communication skills
- career decision-making about the quantum industry
