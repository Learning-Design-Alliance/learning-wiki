# Quantum in Pictures high-school curriculum: eight weekly diagrammatic episodes from wires-and-boxes to measurement-based quantum computing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The pilot's curriculum is based on the book "Quantum in Pictures" by Coecke and Gogioso, an accessible version of the university textbook "Picturing Quantum Processes". It is delivered as weekly recorded episodes: week 1 introduces "diagrams composed of wires and boxes to describe processes and perform mathematical operations", later weeks cover quantum teleportation, spiders, quantum gates and measurements, measurement-based quantum computing, relativistic causality via Sure-boxes and Maybe-boxes, double wires distinguishing quantum from classical worlds, and the square-popping rule. Participants also received a slimmed-down version of the book as their tutorial text.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Weekly one-hour online episodes plus half-hour self-study before tutorials, accessed via the project portal so all participants receive identical lectures.
#### Constraints
- The article notes the material was a slimmed-down version of the published book, tailored for the high-school trial.

### Target Learners
- high-school students aged 16 to 19

### Learning Goals
- performing quantum calculations by diagrammatic manipulation
- conceptual understanding of quantum information scenarios represented by diagrams
