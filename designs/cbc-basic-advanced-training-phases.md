# CBC structured training pathway divided into basic (pre-clinical) and advanced (clinical) phases

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Colégio Brasileiro de Cirurgiões training pathway is divided into a basic (pre-clinical) phase and an advanced (clinical) phase, ensuring progressive acquisition of theoretical knowledge and technical competencies. The basic phase includes theoretical training (≥30 hours), simulation training (≥20 hours), in-service exposure (≥4 hours), and procedural observation (≥5 robotic procedures). The advanced phase requires assistant participation (≥10 procedures), console surgery under supervision (≥10 procedures), and final evaluation by a qualified tutor.
