# Differential (local, infinitesimal) analysis as the cognitive core of causality and field structure in electromagnetism teaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article organizes its electromagnetism presentation around the cognitive value of differential (local, infinitesimal) analysis of fundamental concepts: field structure, causality, and relativistic field transformations. Building on Einstein's assessment that "The differential law is the only form which completely satisfies the modern physicist's demand for causality", it argues that the global structure of the fields described by integral equations is generated by infinitesimal concatenations characterized by differential equations. The proposed course path tracks the Newton→Maxwell→Einstein development of ideas, with Bohr's observation that differential equations connecting electromagnetic intensities in space and time made a causal analysis in complete analogy to mechanics possible.
