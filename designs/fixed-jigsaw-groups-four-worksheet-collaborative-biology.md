# Semester-long fixed jigsaw-style collaborative groups of four with worksheet activities occupying most class time

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article’s CL implementation placed students in fixed groups of four for the entire semester, using a jigsaw method in which students divide lecture material beforehand, explain different parts to their group, then discuss instructor-provided worksheet activities. "these activities take up almost 70% of the class time for around 45 minutes for each class", with three activities per lecture and intensity increasing "every 2–3 sessions". Activities align partially with upcoming examinations, and the instructor uses remaining time to summarize.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Fixed group membership across the semester to build a motivated, friendly teamwork environment
- Groups of four, which the article cites as more effective for the flow of ideas than bigger groups
- Instructor guidance, feedback quality, and activity quality
- Activities comprising real-life scenarios, fill-in-the-blanks, or true/false questions
#### Constraints
- Recommended when the subject consists of independent portions such as Biology
- Activities are similar but not identical to examination questions

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate introductory biology students in single-gender and mixed-gender classes

### Learning Goals
- Conceptual understanding of basic biology and applying understanding to solve biological questions
