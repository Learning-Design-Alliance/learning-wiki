# Physics agreement pedagogy: extending community agreements to disciplinary content with four key features

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A pedagogy extending community agreements from relational norms to disciplinary content: physical laws and definitions are adopted as negotiable norms structuring in-class activities. The article names "instructor-provided starting points for negotiation, regular student-led revision of the developing theory, official documentation, and continual support for students to make choices" as key features, illustrated in an undergraduate spacetime physics course.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor provides starting points and routinely invites student-led revision of the agreement
#### Constraints
- Scope may need restriction under time and size constraints
- Scope should align with students' content learning goals

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics students
- learners at a higher-access public university

### Learning Goals
- collective theory-building skills
- epistemic agency
- spacetime physics concepts
