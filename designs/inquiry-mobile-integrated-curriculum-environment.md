# Inquiry-based mobile learning environment with integrated curriculum

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A one-class pilot learning environment in a Finnish lower secondary school combining inquiry-based learning with an integrated, multidisciplinary curriculum. Students "received personal tablet computers as their primary tool for school work", containing e-books, e-notebooks, and applications for mind mapping, instant messaging, and image and video editing. Learning projects revolved around one overarching theme covered in different subjects, such as a cross-curricular water-themed project in which students collected and analysed water samples and wrote reports. Teachers had opportunities to participate in professional training courses.
