# One-day build-and-program robotics workshop with a custom visual curriculum

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A workshop format in which student teams construct and program a robot within a single day while a custom curriculum of "presentation slides, instructional photos, and visual aids" guides each step and explains concepts in robotics, coding, bioinspiration, aerodynamics, and engineering. Activities integrate learning objectives with engagement: assembling the blimp, investigating physics topics, working with electronic components, practicing wiring, and programming via Arduino IDE. Pre/post assessments aligned with the curriculum measured learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A custom curriculum with slides, instructional photos, and visual aids aligned to each build step
- Hands-on tasks pairing construction with concept explanations
- Pre- and post-assessments aligned with the workshop curriculum
#### Constraints
- Studied with ten middle school students in a single one-day event; the author notes engagement varied and only half the students were actively engaged throughout

### Target Learners
- middle school students

### Learning Goals
- STEM comprehension across physics, engineering, and computer science
- technical vocabulary and tool familiarity
