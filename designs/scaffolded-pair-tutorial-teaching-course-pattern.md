# Teaching-awareness course pattern: scaffolded peer-pair design and teaching of authentic tutorials with fading support

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A semester-long course pattern in which student pairs design, implement, and assess two instructional tutorials in authentic college recitation classes, with scaffolding from instructor, TA, and peers that gradually decreases. Students choose partners, set goals and performance targets discussed in class, iterate tutorial versions with the instructor and TA, and test materials on fellow classmates before teaching. The course met 3 hours per week for a semester for 3 credits, with weekly readings and discussions of physics education research.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Weekly class meetings with readings and discussion of teaching and learning literature
- Instructor and TA availability for iterative tutorial development and feedback
- Access to authentic recitation classes in which students can teach
#### Constraints
- Tutorial length was limited to 25 minutes (10 and 15 minutes spent on pre-test and post-test respectively)
- All pairs taught on the same broad topics: DC circuits and electromagnetic induction

### Target Learners
- science and engineering undergraduates considering K-12 teaching careers

### Learning Goals
- instructional design skills
- awareness of the intellectual demands of teaching
- confidence in teaching
