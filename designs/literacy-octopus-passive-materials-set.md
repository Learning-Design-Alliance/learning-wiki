# Literacy Octopus passive dissemination materials: CEM booklet, IEE magazine and email summaries, ResearchEd webinar archive, and Teaching How2s subscription

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q4` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Four providers each sent evidence-based literacy materials passively to their 2,500 allocated schools: CEM sent "the evidence-based booklet, Improving Reading: A Guide for Teachers"; IEE sent print issues of Better Evidence-based Education, the fortnightly 'Best Evidence in Brief' email, and access to the Evidence 4 Impact database; ResearchEd/NatCen sent an invitation to view an archived webinar and conference materials; and the Campaign for Learning gave schools a free Teaching How2s subscription with step-by-step visual guides. Materials were sent between spring 2015 and July 2016 with no active support.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Providers only needed to confirm they had sent their materials as planned to headteacher contacts; no active engagement or consent from schools was required
#### Constraints
- The trial did not monitor whether schools engaged with or used the materials; attendance at the ResearchEd conference was not part of this trial

### Target Learners
- Key Stage 2 pupils in English primary schools

### Learning Goals
- Key Stage 2 literacy attainment
