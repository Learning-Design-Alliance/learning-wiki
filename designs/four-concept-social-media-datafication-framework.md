# Four-concept framework of social media datafication: data collection, profiling, engagement, and recommending

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article organizes the teaching of social media mechanisms around four key concepts. "Understanding the basics of datafication and the data-driven mechanisms of social media require familiarity with a number of concepts, of which four were selected for this intervention." Data collection distinguishes data given, data traces, and inferred data; profiling categorizes individuals based on behaviors, interests, and demographics; engagement concerns user interaction with content and its role in the business model; recommending suggests content, friends, and communities via techniques such as collaborative filtering. The framework grounds both the apps' design and the assessment items.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructional designs using this framework must make opaque mechanisms visible, since the extent of data collection, engagement scores, and recommendation mechanisms remain invisible or opaque to users
#### Constraints
- The article notes these mechanisms are naturally opaque to some extent and protected secrets of platform owners, and that many AI systems lack precise and complete scientific representations due to intrinsic and contingent opaque features

### Target Learners
- Children and young people (K-12) learning about social media and AI

### Learning Goals
- Data agency, digital literacy, and understanding how digital services and data-driven platform mechanisms work
