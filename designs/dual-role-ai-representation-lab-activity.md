# Two-session dual-role AI representation activity (teacher role: wave velocity; student role: Ideal Gas Law)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A two-lab activity in a required physical science course for elementary education majors in which participants first created representations without AI and then with Microsoft Co-Pilot Pro. In the teacher role they explained wave velocity to elementary (K-6) students; in the student role they explained the Ideal Gas Law to an English teacher, choosing from a list including "pictures, cartoons, equations, analogies, graphs, animations, tables containing numbers (data), stories, scripts, plays, games, or drawings."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants must be told not to copy and paste questions but to converse in their own words; the student-role session should precede formal instruction on the topic
#### Constraints
- The article describes the activity as very linear and specific, focusing on only two types of audiences

### Target Learners
- elementary education majors in a physical science course

### Learning Goals
- selecting effective representations to communicate physics concepts to different audiences
