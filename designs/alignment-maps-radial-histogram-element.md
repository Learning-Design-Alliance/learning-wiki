# Radial and histogram waveform alignment maps for archaeoastronomy sites

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Students had to understand how archaeoastronomers map sites and potential celestial alignments, including the difference between true north and magnetic north on archaeology site maps. Two types of alignment maps were taught: radial maps and histogram waveform type maps, illustrated with radial and histogram maps of the alignments of churches in Spain (Figure 2 and Figure 3, from Gonzalez-Garcia, Belmonte, and Ferrer 2016). The histogram waveforms capture both the direction of alignment and the number of churches with their axes so aligned.
