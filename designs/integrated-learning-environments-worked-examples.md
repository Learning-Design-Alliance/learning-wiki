# Integrated learning environments combining instruction-oriented and problem-oriented design principles

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The article applies the concept of integrated learning environments (Reinmann & Mandl, 2006), which "combine elem ents from instruction-oriented and problem-oriented learning approaches in an effort to maximize their respective advantages and to minimize their disadvantages". Instruction-oriented principles included elaborative sequencing of increasing complexity and structure-emphasizing example sets; problem-oriented principles included authentic, relevant tasks and multiple perspectives to avoid inert knowledge. The learning environment operationalized this with twelve worked examples based on a real study.
