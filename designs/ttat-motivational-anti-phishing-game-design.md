# Technology Threat Avoidance Theory (TTAT)-derived motivational design model for anti-phishing games

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article grounds its mobile game design in elements of a theoretical model derived from Technology Threat Avoidance Theory, which was formulated through empirical investigation to enhance computer users' phishing threat avoidance behaviour through their motivation. The design incorporates "perceived threat, safeguard effectiveness, safeguard cost, self-efficacy, perceived severity and perceived susceptibility elements into the mobile game design context". The game's objective is to teach users how to identify phishing URLs and to enhance avoidance behaviour through motivation, with a teacher fish providing tips and an AVOID button for rejecting phishing URLs.
