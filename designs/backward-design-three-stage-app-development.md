# Wiggins and McTighe's three-stage backward design framework as a guiding strategy for educational app development

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Backward design is a curriculum design methodology in which learning goals and assessment are established before instruction is planned. McTighe and Wiggins "formalized the backward design process into three consecutive stages": Stage I identify desired results, Stage II determine acceptable evidence, and Stage III plan learning experiences and instruction. The authors applied all three stages to develop the CityHacks app, arguing it ties learning-goal identification, assessment design, and app feature design into one coherent process.
