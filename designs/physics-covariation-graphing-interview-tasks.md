# Animation-based graphing interview tasks adapted from Hobson and Moore (2017), including Going Around Tacoma, Ferris Wheel, Rigamarole, and Drone

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A set of think-aloud interview tasks in which participants relate two quantities in an animated physics scenario and draw a graph. Each question "was designed with an accompanying animation designed to mimic the method used by Hobson and Moore (2017)." Round-one items (e.g., Going Around Tacoma, Ferris Wheel) involve motion-based contexts relating two distances; round-two items (Rigamarole, Drone) use non-motion physics contexts such as gravitational potential versus distance traveled. Participants could play animations at will and move between questions.
