## Description
The IQP course teaches mainly through lab-tutorials modeled on the University of Washington's Tutorials in Introductory Physics, mostly developed at the Maine Physics Education Research Laboratory. Students work in small groups on application-oriented worksheets: "Man arbeitet in kleinen Gruppen (3 bis 4 Personen) an anwendungsorientierten Arbeitsblättern". Each week has three lecture hours for about 50 students and three tutorial hours; mathematics is de-emphasized in favor of qualitative descriptions of the physics.
