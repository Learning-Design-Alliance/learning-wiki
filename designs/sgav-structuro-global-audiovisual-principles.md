# SGAV (structuro-global audiovisual) method principles underlying the audiovisual vocabulary treatment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study draws on the SGAV method, which the article says "concentrates on learning verbal language" and rests on two essential principles. The structuro-global aspect holds that learners need both linguistic elements (vocabulary, grammar) and non-linguistic elements (rhythm, stress, gestures, facial expressions) for learning to occur. The audio-visual aspect seeks "simultaneous presentation of voice and image in order to facilitate the comprehension of message". In this study the first aspect was fulfilled through video pronunciations and the second through written transcripts of those pronunciations, though the author modified SGAV's usual priority of spoken language by using spoken and written language simultaneously.
