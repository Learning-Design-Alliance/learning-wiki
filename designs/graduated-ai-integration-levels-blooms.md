# Graduated levels of AI integration aligned with Bloom's taxonomy and the AI Assessment Scale

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course organized AI use into five levels—"No AI", "AI-Assisted Idea Development", "AI Collaboration", "Full AI Integration", and "Advanced AI Integration"—mapped onto Bloom's Taxonomy levels and AI mastery levels from none/novice to expert (Table 1). Levels ranged "from 'no AI' to 'advanced AI' integration, depending on the level of AI mastery required to complete an assigned task" and align with Perkins et al.'s AI Assessment Scale (2023). Higher levels require deeper ethical understanding and critical evaluation of AI output.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tasks assigned at levels matched to the AI mastery students need; instructor-designed custom AI tools via MindStudio alongside ChatGPT
#### Constraints
- Applied in one 14-week doctoral writing course; alignment described with Perkins et al. (2023) and Bloom (1956) rather than independently validated

### Target Learners
- doctoral students (Doctor of Healthcare Administration)

### Learning Goals
- progressive AI mastery, ethical AI use, academic writing development
