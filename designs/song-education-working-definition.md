# Song education: the melodic singing, appreciation and analysis of English songs within EFL teaching-learning

## Description
The article defines song education as its working definition: "the melodic singing, appreciation and analysis of English songs dur ing the EFL teaching-lear ning process". It rests on the view that musical intelligence (Gardner, 1983) involves sensitivity to rhythm, tone, pitch, timbre and melody, terms that resemble language's phonological features, and on claims that melody and rhythm constitute a common core of the phonetic and musical sound systems. The framework organizes expected gains into phonological, lexico-grammatical, and cultural perspectives.
