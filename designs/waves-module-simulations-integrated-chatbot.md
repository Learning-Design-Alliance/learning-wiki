# Custom-built online waves module with simulations integrated with a chatbot

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 10-page extra-credit online activity on wave concepts, built for a large-enrollment calculus-based physics course for future engineers using the Matter and Interactions textbook. Each content page presented "an interactive simulation and followed by four questions", with a chatbot integrated throughout. The first page collected anticipated chatbot preferences among three options, and the last page collected student ID, a 5-point activity rating, perceived learning, qualitative feedback, and retrospective reflections on content familiarity and chatbot preferences. Estimated completion time was around 30-45 minutes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administered as an extra-credit activity with students informed it was not part of course assessment, to encourage honest participation
#### Constraints
- The module covered waves because the topic was not covered in the host course

### Target Learners
- introductory calculus-based physics students for future engineers

### Learning Goals
- wave concepts not covered in the host course
