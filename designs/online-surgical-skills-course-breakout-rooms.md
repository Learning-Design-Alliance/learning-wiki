# 1-day online surgical skills course with breakout-room teaching at 1:5 demonstrator-to-delegate ratio (Hopin platform)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A fully online Foundation Skills in Surgery Course delivered on a platform "able to simultaneously accommodate a virtual lecture hall and several breakout rooms" (Hopin by Medall). Skills (scrubbing, instruments, suturing and knot tying, then tendon repair and vascular anastomosis) were demonstrated by two demonstrators to the main hall, while breakout-room delegates practised on identical commercially available models under remote supervision and were assessed with detailed feedback. 553 delegates from 20 countries attended; 64 were trained in breakout rooms and 489 in didactic sessions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Breakout-room entry required proof of purchase of the prescribed models and instruments, emailed to delegates 4 weeks in advance
- Demonstrators required at least 5 years since medical school, Membership of the Royal College of Surgeons, teaching/feedback training and a pre-course briefing
#### Constraints
- Retention of skills after online teaching was not assessed
- Translation to clinical practice was not assessed

### Target Learners
- Junior surgical trainees
- Medical students

### Learning Goals
- Basic and advanced surgical skills (suturing, knot tying, tendon repair, vascular anastomosis)
