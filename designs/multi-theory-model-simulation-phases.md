# Simulation structured through the phases of an integrated multi-theory adult learning model (base knowledge, dissonance, refinement, organization, feedback, consolidation)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article organizes the simulation's learning mechanism using the 2013 AMEE Guide multi-theory model of adult learning, which the authors cite as combining conventional learning theories. Learning is structured on base knowledge from readings and didactic sessions; the "dissonance phase of this model [7] is entered when students engage in the simulation experience"; debriefing carries the refinement, organization and feedback phases, and consolidation occurs as learners reflect on the big picture. The authors position their joint-faculty debrief as compatible with this model.
