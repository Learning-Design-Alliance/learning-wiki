# ELC module: a structured experiential learning module for mothers of preterm infants

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The ELC module is the treatment artifact given to the treatment group during phase 2, contrasted with leaflets given to the control group. The article states "ELC learning process gives the mother an experience that is structured by using modules independence of mothers in caring for preterm infants." Learning activities begin with maternal awareness of problems faced by preterm infants, then mothers learn the skills nurses perform, think through problems as they arise, and finally demonstrate mastered skills. Treatment was given "during 2 weeks is calculated from the first day of birth until the age of neonates (28 days)."
