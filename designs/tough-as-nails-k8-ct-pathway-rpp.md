# Tough As Nails: NSF-funded research-practice partnership creating K-8 computational thinking pathways in two Kentucky Appalachia districts

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
Tough As Nails is an NSF-funded research-practice partnership led with Digital Promise and local educators to "create K-8 computational thinking pathways in Pikeville Independent School District and Floyd County Schools." The article presents it as the central vehicle for CS/CT investment in Eastern KY, with cultural responsiveness as an express goal and panelists representing administrators, teachers, parents, and community organizations.
