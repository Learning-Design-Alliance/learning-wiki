# Annotated online repository of cadaveric dissection images for visceral anatomy

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A pool of photographs of body-donor dissections of the head, neck, and thorax, edited in Adobe Photoshop with "arrows and numbers were overlaid on the images and pointed toward various structures". Images were uploaded to the course virtual platform in two files, one with annotations and one without, for self-directed study and self-assessment covering 50% of the practical sessions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Edited high-quality images with overlaid structure annotations, hosted on the course virtual platform
#### Constraints
- Covered only head, neck, and thorax regions; abdomen and pelvis were not included

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate medical students

### Learning Goals
- identification of anatomical structures on cadaveric material
