# The Behrangi Education Management Model is a ten-step learning management framework integrating conceptual indexes from multiple management and teaching models.

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The Education Management Model is described as "in fact learning management" with "special power in absorbing basic and important points of all teaching models." It integrates conceptual indexes from knowledge management, transformational leadership, multi-intelligence, organizational learning, and other management frameworks. It is operationalized through ten steps for joint application by teachers, including student-developed scaffolds, two-person study groups, Jigsaw-style participation, and complementary evaluation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers collaborate in workshop groups and apply the ten steps jointly
- Students develop their own knowledge scaffolds rather than receiving teacher-presented pre-organizers
#### Constraints
- The article recommends starting with "ten percent of reading enthusiast teachers" to empower themselves before directing broader teacher acceptance of changes

### Target Learners
- Second-grade elementary students
- K-12 students across grades and courses

### Learning Goals
- Mathematics learning
- Use of conceptual indexes of course content
- Autonomous learning, self-awareness, critical thought, problem solving
