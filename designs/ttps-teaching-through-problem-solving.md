# Teaching through problem solving (TTPS) as one of three approaches to problem-solving instruction

## Description
The article situates TTPS within Schroeder and Lester's three approaches: teaching about, for, and through problem solving. "Teaching through problem solving (TTPS) involves teaching mathematics concepts through problem-solving contexts, provides opportunities for students to develop higher-level thinking during mathematical problem solving, and takes place in an inquiry-oriented learning environment." TTPS lessons begin with word problems carrying high cognitive demand, and students must make sense of both the situation and the underlying mathematics. In this study TTPS is defined as an integration of instructor, instructional method, tasks, and learning environment.
