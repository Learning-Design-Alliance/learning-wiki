# Suite of mLearning tools structured in the university learning management system

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A suite of mobile learning tools co-designed with nursing students to support B-PAS learning and application, delivered through the university LMS and accessible on mobile devices. It contains the digital simulation with virtual patients, a MOOC, auscultation resources, instruction videos and video lectures, a podcast, communication and documentation guidance, MCQ tests, a written assignment with anonymous peer review, and checklists. The article states "The LMS of the university was used to structure the suite of mLearning tools, which enabled the students to access digital learning resources by using mobile devices."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires the university LMS for structure and mobile access, and log-in details and contact information for the digital simulation and MOOC as described in Table 2
#### Constraints
- The written assignment's peer review element was excluded for first-year students, and content had no fixed order so students could access what interested them

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students in first, second, and third year

### Learning Goals
- basic physical assessment skills
- human bioscience knowledge
- clinical reasoning
- professional communication and documentation
