# Hierarchical assessment system of the flexible CDBRC

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A two-level assessment component of the CDBRC. At the basic level, "the course grades were comprehensively assessed by experimental course performance, literature review, and final exams, which can ease the academic burdens for students". The advanced level is optional and includes research projects, competitions, and research paper publications, allowing top-rated students to pursue deeper research engagement without raising the pass threshold for everyone.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Basic-level assessment must be comprehensive across experimental performance, literature review, and final exams so ordinary students can pass without advanced research output
#### Constraints
- The advanced assessment (projects, competitions, publications) is optional and depends on students voluntarily engaging beyond the compulsory core

### Target Learners
- undergraduate dental students of varying ability levels

### Learning Goals
- basic research skills and knowledge assessment
