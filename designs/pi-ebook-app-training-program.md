# Interactive pressure-injury E-book app training program

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A smartphone E-book app built with SimMAGIC software on the 2014 pressure-ulcer prevention and treatment guidelines, integrating "pictures, audio, videos, film, and animations" plus notes, bookmarks, and preset question prompts. It comprises three sections covering PI definition, etiology and staging; risk assessment, prevention, dressing, nutrition, repositioning and wound care; and interactive learning feedback with self-learning practice in every session. Sessions took 10–15 minutes on average, and participants could read content anytime, anywhere alongside lecture teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants need an iOS or Android smartphone and instruction on downloading and using the app

### Learning Goals
- pressure injury prevention and care knowledge, attitudes, and confidence
