# Four-component 2.5-hour patient education training: teaching, simulated practice, feedback, and discussion

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's intervention is a 2.5-hour session with four components printed in Table 1: teaching (45 min, theory and evidence on patient education with video examples and discussion), simulated practice of patient education (45 min, students in pairs plan and perform assessment, implementation and evaluation with a structured simulated patient), feedback (15 min, structured feedback from physiotherapists and students), and discussion and report (45 min, examining cases and barriers). The authors state the intervention "included instruction on patient education, implementation, evaluation, practical exercises, and a video demonstration".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Both groups completed the same assessment process; the intervention requires an independent blinded examiner for OSCE testing and same-day scheduling to prevent cross-group contamination
#### Constraints
- The intervention was conducted at a single educational institution and may not reflect the diversity of physiotherapy programs worldwide; it is not clear which components had the greatest impact on outcomes

### Target Learners
- final-year physiotherapy students

### Learning Goals
- patient education skills
- communication competencies
- teaching self-efficacy
