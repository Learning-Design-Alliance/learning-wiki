# Three-phase weekly online lab unit: exploration, problem-solving, and revision with discussion board and peer review

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Each weekly unit of the online course is structured in three phases: "exploration, problem solving, and revision". In the exploratory phase students read the textbook with web-based notes, videos, and interactive questions and post an initial discussion-board post; in the problem-solving phase the main interactions occur via a graded discussion board and a peer-review system in which students reply to teammates with questions, comments, compliments and/or connections and give feedback to two other students; teaching assistant and instructor feedback is provided throughout the week. Students submit a first draft individually, then a final lab report with a metacognitive reflection at the end of the unit.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multiple assignments due throughout the week; discussion-board posts and peer feedback are required; TA and instructor feedback time of about 2 hours per week comparable to student lab time
#### Constraints
- Labs are shorter in length than in residential instruction partly because students work mostly individually and receive asynchronous feedback

### Target Learners
- adult online learners, many working full time or with young children at home

### Learning Goals
- lab discussion and collaboration
- peer critique and feedback
- metacognitive reflection on lab work
