# Pilot integrated histology theory-and-practice curriculum at Jinan University School of Medicine

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A pilot curriculum for overseas-enrolled Clinical Medicine students in which histology theory teaching was implemented simultaneously with laboratory demonstration, designed to remove the temporal-spatial barrier of the traditional separated format. The content, textbook, examination papers and graders were identical to the traditional course; only delivery differed. It was taught twice weekly in 120-minute sessions totalling 108 hours (54 lecture, 54 practice).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Morphology laboratories with histology slides, light microscopes, computers with digital panorama imaging system and internet access for every student; more tutors needed to scale up
#### Constraints
- Voluntary participation of 24 of 69 students; laboratory capacity limits hosting; teachers reported needing more preparation time than traditional teaching

### Target Learners
- sophomore overseas Chinese students of Clinical Medicine

### Learning Goals
- comprehension of histological theory and its application during microscope practice
