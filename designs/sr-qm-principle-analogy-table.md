# Structural analogy table: special relativity's light postulate parallels quantum mechanics' equal-frequency spin outcomes

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Table IV of the article organizes the analogy underwriting the principle account: in special relativity the empirical fact is that Alice and Bob both measure c regardless of motion relative to the source, with the consequence that each may claim the other's length and time measurements need correction; in quantum mechanics the empirical fact is that both measure ±1 (in units of ℏ/2) regardless of SG magnet orientation, with the consequence that each may claim the other's individual ±1 values "are erroneous and need to be corrected (averaged)." Both empirical facts follow from NPRF.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Understanding that spatial rotations, not just Lorentz boosts, are part of the Lorentz transformations, so different SG magnet orientations constitute different reference frames
#### Constraints
- The analogy is drawn for spin-1/2 particles in the triplet state per the Mermin device; the singlet state is treated as analogous
