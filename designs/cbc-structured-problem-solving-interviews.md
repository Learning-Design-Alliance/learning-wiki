# CBC structured problem-solving interview sequence across consultation stages

## Description
CBC casework proceeds through "a series of stages comprised of meetings coupled with between-session assessment and intervention supports." The structured interviews are the Needs Identification/Analysis ("Building on Strengths") Interview, the Plan Development and Implementation ("Planning for Success") Interview focused on co-constructing intervention plans, and the Plan Evaluation ("Checking and Reconnecting") Interview evaluating plans and progress. Consultants met with teachers and parents for approximately four sessions over approximately eight weeks, often in small-group format.
