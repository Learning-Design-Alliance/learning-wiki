# Two-tiered alternate reality game narrative structure for framing problem-based course activities

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Door AltRG uses a two-tiered narrative structure in which fictional clients "hired" student teams to complete authentic tasks, while a second tier of game structures including puzzles, codes, and ciphers gates access to scaffolding resources. The Puppet Master character, played by the instructor, "provides soft scaffolding and additional resources for students who may be struggling." Game characters also act as gatekeepers judging solution quality before students advance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A distributed set of free online resources (e.g., web sites, Ning, Moodle, e-mail, YouTube) linked together as the game's resource distribution system
#### Constraints
- Given the complications and expense of designing immersive game worlds, the design embeds game activities across existing media rather than a fully integrated, stand-alone product

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in large introductory courses

### Learning Goals
- critical thinking and problem solving with technology tools
- computer applications skills
