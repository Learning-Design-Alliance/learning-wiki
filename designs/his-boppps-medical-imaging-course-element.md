# HIS-based BOPPPS implementation for the medical imaging experimental course (crossover design, 117 interns)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An instructional implementation in which 117 medical imaging interns at the First Affiliated Hospital of Xinxiang Medical University (2021–2022 academic year) were taught in two groups across two semesters in a crossover design: the first group used the HIS-based BOPPPS model in semester one while the second used traditional teaching, and the models were swapped in semester two "to eliminate potential inter-group difference impacts on the results." The first semester comprised 60 h each of theory and practical classes, the second 52 h each. The same faculty with ≥ 5 years of experience taught both groups from the same textbook.
