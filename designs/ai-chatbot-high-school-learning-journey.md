# Non-linear AI literacy learning journey cycling through Understand, Evaluate, and Use

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report illustrates the framework in action through a high school lesson on AI chatbots, 'following a winding pathway of the three Modes of Engagement.' Students first learn how chatbots work, then responsibly tinker with prompting while noting questions, research those questions with evidence, center human judgment through Socratic seminar discussion, center justice through an Ethics Matrix of stakeholders and values, and optionally design a chatbot for a community problem. The report states 'engagement in AI literacy is non-linear and intertwined, where each mode of engagement builds and strengthens on each other to foster overall AI literacy.'
