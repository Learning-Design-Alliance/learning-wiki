# Model of domain learning as the framework for multidimensional explicit teaching in history

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article underpins its intervention with the model of domain learning, treating explicit teaching as multidimensional: it "focus[s] on strategies and second-order concepts to generate and verbalize causal explanations and epistemological underpinnings connected to causal reasoning in history". The model motivates measuring knowledge, epistemological beliefs, and interest as interrelated outcomes of domain learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Explicit teaching must address multiple dimensions simultaneously: strategies, second-order concepts, and epistemological underpinnings
#### Constraints
- The study applies the framework to causal reasoning in history with 11th-grade students

### Target Learners
- 11th-grade history students

### Learning Goals
- Constructing causal explanations in history
- Second-order historical reasoning
- Epistemological understanding of history
