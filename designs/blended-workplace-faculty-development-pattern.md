# Blended workplace-based faculty development combining online instruction with supervised workplace application and feedback

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The program pattern combines short online workshops with a month of workplace application supported by observation and feedback. Training was "delivered in a blended format (online and workplace) and included feedback": three days of online sessions (9 hours) covering observation, feedback, reflection, and the Proctor model, followed by one month in which faculty applied the skills in clinical rotations while trained observers completed checklists and faculty received feedback at the end of rounds. The design followed the ASSURE model's six steps, from learner analysis through evaluation and revision.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- workplace settings where faculty can apply supervision skills in real clinical rounds
- trained observers and an observation checklist for performance feedback
- coordination with department leadership for orientation and scheduling
#### Constraints
- the article states limited faculty availability and wide geographical distribution of hospitals necessitated online delivery; the short duration was a limitation

### Target Learners
- clinical faculty members (pediatric specialists and superspecialists)

### Learning Goals
- clinical supervision knowledge, attitudes, and performance
