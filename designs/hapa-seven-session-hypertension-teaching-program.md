# Seven-session HAPA-based group teaching program for older adults with hypertension

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A structured teaching program of seven weekly one-hour lecture and group-discussion sessions, each mapped to a HAPA construct: understanding risk, expected outcomes, action self-efficacy, action planning, coping planning, coping self-efficacy, and recovery self-efficacy. Content covered blood-pressure monitoring skills, medication and diet regimens, 30-minute physical activity at least five times per week, weight control, stress management, relaxation and guided imagery. Sessions were delivered at a health center with telephone, texting, and consultation follow-up over two months; content was approved by nursing specialists.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Weekly one-hour group sessions at a health center plus two months of follow-up contacts (telephone, texting, consultations)
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only in literate older adults over 60 with hypertension and no physical or psychological disease history, at a single Iranian center

### Target Learners
- Older people with hypertension attending community health services centers

### Learning Goals
- Hypertension self-care behavior across dietary, medication, food-labeling, and disease-management domains
