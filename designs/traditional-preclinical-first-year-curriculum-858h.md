# Traditional first-year preclinical curriculum: 858 hours of planned teaching, 73% basic biological sciences, no patient contact or individual feedback

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The studied first-year curriculum consisted of 858 hours of planned teaching, of which 73% covered basic biological sciences and anatomy; the remainder covered statistics, ethics, Latin, introduction to philosophy, and a 3-hour introduction to humanistic medicine. "There were no teaching elements dedicated to introductory orientation, patient contact, hospital visits, mentoring, or individual supervision and feedback, and very limited expectations of structured critical reflection." A reformed curriculum was implemented after the study.

## Design Implications
