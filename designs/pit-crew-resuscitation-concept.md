# Pit-crew resuscitation: coordinated, role-assigned team approach to cardiac arrest tasks

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Pit-crew resuscitation is a team-organisation concept for cardiac arrest care in which "clinicians in a team act in a predetermined way to undertake set tasks in a specific order", described as "akin to a formula one motor racing team". In the trial's pit-crew training arm, team members adopted nominated roles associated with specific tasks and were trained to perform tasks in a coordinated manner, with these concepts reinforced in feedback during practice scenarios.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Team members adopt a nominated role associated with specific tasks and are trained to perform tasks in a coordinated manner
#### Constraints
- In this trial pit-crew training produced no measurable benefit over standard training when performance was measured immediately after training in a manikin simulation

### Target Learners
- Registered health professionals with Immediate Life Support or Advanced Life Support certification

### Learning Goals
- Coordinated team deployment of a mechanical chest compression device in cardiac arrest
