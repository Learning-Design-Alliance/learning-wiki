# WHS blends PBIS, RtI, and explicit instruction principles

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
WHS purposefully blends three evidence-based practices: positive behavior interventions and supports (PBIS), response to intervention (RtI), and explicit instruction (EI). PBIS contributes defining expected behaviors, teaching what those behaviors look like, rewarding students, and using continuously collected data. RtI contributes the multi-tiered logic of universal instruction with secondary and tertiary supports. EI contributes careful example selection and sequencing, modeling, adequate pacing, frequent opportunities to respond and practice, and error correction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Effective social skills instruction consists of distinct phases: acquisition, fluency building, and generalization
#### Constraints
- Many social skills curricula focus on skill acquisition but neglect fluency building and generalization

### Target Learners
- K-3 elementary school students

### Learning Goals
- Prosocial classroom behaviors and social skills associated with school success
