# One-day multi-professional obstetrical emergency simulation course with role-retention, small mixed groups, standardized patients and video-supported debriefing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A reusable course design in which mixed teams of midwives and junior and senior obstetricians train six emergency scenarios (shoulder dystocia, postpartal haemorrhage, instrumental delivery, pre-eclampsia/eclampsia, maternal Basic Life Support, neonatal resuscitation) on simple and high-fidelity mannequins. Participants "trained in small groups and retained their normal function" under multi-professional tutor teams, with "scripted role players... as standardized patients at all the training stations". After each scenario a debriefing gave immediate feedback, with video extracts shown where applicable.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multi-professional tutor-teams of experienced obstetricians, anaesthesiologists and neonatologists with special education in leading simulation courses
- Small groups of maximum six participants mixing two midwives, two junior and two senior obstetricians
- Presentation of clinical algorithms and crisis resource management essentials before training
#### Constraints
- Videotaping was done only with participant consent at three of six stations and records erased after the course
- The course was voluntary and its financial costs may have attracted specially interested participants

### Target Learners
- midwives
- junior obstetricians
- senior obstetricians

### Learning Goals
- management of obstetrical emergencies
- team communication
- knowledge of clinical algorithms
- self-confidence in emergencies
