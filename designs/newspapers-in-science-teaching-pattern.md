# Newspapers-in-education science teaching combining daily article reading, biweekly hands-on experiments, and weekly small-group discussion

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A recurring instructional design in which the teacher photocopies daily science articles from a newspaper (here the Mandarin Daily News), runs hands-on experiments based on experiments published in that newspaper, and organizes small-group discussion and sharing. The article states the design used "reading, science experiments and group discussions utilizing newspapers in education" over a 12-week course, aiming to offer inspiring scientific inquiry learning through multiple instructional strategies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A newspaper that regularly publishes science articles suitable for students (the study used the Mandarin Daily News)
- Teacher time to photocopy and integrate daily science articles with the curriculum
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only in a single-group quasi-experiment with 33 sixth graders, without a control group

### Target Learners
- sixth grade elementary school students

### Learning Goals
- science learning attitudes
- science reading attitudes
- attitude toward science
- media literacy
