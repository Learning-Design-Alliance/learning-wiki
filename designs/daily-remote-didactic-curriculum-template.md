# Daily remote didactic curriculum template for residents on remote rotation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A structured daily schedule (Table 1) for residents working remotely during the pandemic. The day opens with a faculty-led teleconference in case presentation format, followed by independent prerecorded webinar viewing, assigned reading on the next day's topic, a chief resident-led question-review teleconference at midday on the earlier faculty topic, and independent completion and review of 50 in-training exam questions. The template operationalizes the department's remote curriculum so that residents not on-site continue didactic education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Residents on the remote team must have access to teleconference technology and prerecorded webinars and assigned reading for independent completion
#### Constraints
- Described in the context of pandemic social distancing for an orthopedic surgery residency

### Target Learners
- orthopedic surgery residents (PGY1-5) on remote rotation

### Learning Goals
- clinical knowledge via case presentations and question review
- in-training exam preparation
- preparation for next day's faculty-led teleconference topic
