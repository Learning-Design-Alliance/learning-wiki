# Information-motivation-behavioral skills (IMB) model as the framework for designing breastfeeding training for NICU mothers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The IMB (information-motivation-behavioral skills) model structures the training content in both intervention arms: it informs mothers about breastfeeding, motivates them toward breastfeeding behaviour, and builds the behavioural skills to acquire and maintain that behaviour. The article states: "Therefore, we designed an education method with video and simulator methods based on the information-motivation-behavioral skills (IMB) model [21]." The same components recur in the methods: "The aim of the trainings was to inform the mothers about breastfeeding (knowledge), to motivate them to establish breastfeeding behaviour with the trainings received (motivation) and to enable them to acquire and maintain breastfeeding behaviour (behavioural skills)."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training content must address all three IMB components: information, motivation, and behavioral skills for breastfeeding
#### Constraints
- The model was applied to postpartum mothers of babies admitted to a neonatal unit who had no early skin-to-skin contact or breastfeeding and who resided in a mother hotel

### Target Learners
- Postpartum mothers whose newborns are hospitalized in a neonatal intensive care unit

### Learning Goals
- Breastfeeding knowledge, motivation, and behavioral skills; breastfeeding self-efficacy and mother–infant attachment
