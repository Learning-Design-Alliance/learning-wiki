# Small-group scaffolding framework for PBL in EFL reading instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article presents a small-group interaction framework (Figure 1) in which PBL small groups are "centered on the problem-solving cycle" across five stages: confronting, examining, re-examining, reviewing the problem, and presenting solutions. Grounded in Vygotsky's social learning theory and group cohesiveness research, the framework assumes learners are supported by teachers and peers, with heterogeneous grouping so proficient learners model successful outcomes. The study applies it in a university EFL reading course.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Groups should be heterogeneous, composed of both proficient and less-proficient learners, formed based on pre-test scores to ensure normal distribution of reading proficiency
#### Constraints
- The pedagogy focuses on small-group scaffolding specifically due to limited conditions in the EFL context, such as limited authentic immersion resources and test-oriented learning goals

### Target Learners
- university EFL students at intermediate English level

### Learning Goals
- English reading comprehension
- reading comprehension strategy use
- active English learning attitudes
