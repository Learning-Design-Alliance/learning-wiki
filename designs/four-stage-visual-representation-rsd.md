# Four-stage progression of visual representation formation using remote sensing means

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For forming geographical representations visually, the article divides the means of remote sensing into four stages: "visual representation of definite geographical objects and phenomena characteristics", then "occurrence of representations about geographical objects and phenomena", then "preservation and reproduction of representations", and finally "regular application of the received ideas into the process of concepts formation". This sequence organizes how images move from presentation to consolidation of concepts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Images should be of high quality and their content consistent with the material studied at the appropriate time of the lesson.
#### Constraints
- The information transmitted by the images should not affect the integrity of the lesson.

### Target Learners
- school geography students

### Learning Goals
- formation and consolidation of geographic concepts from visual representations
