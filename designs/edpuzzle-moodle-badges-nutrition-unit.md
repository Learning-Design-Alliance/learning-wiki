# Flipped nutrition didactic unit with EdPuzzle videos and Moodle digital badges

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's intervention was a 7-session didactic unit on nutritional education and healthy habits delivered through Flipped Classroom and gamification. Teachers uploaded videos the day before class via EdPuzzle, which registers students, tracks who watches, and inserts assessment quizzes; in class, gamification awarded bronze, silver or gold digital badges through the LMS Moodle platform, where contents were sequenced into basic, intermediate and advanced levels, missions and adventures. "Depending on the qualification obtained, the reward was a bronze, silver or gold badge. Obtaining three badges meant that the teaching unit had been passed."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Videos provided to students should not exceed 10 minutes in length, following prior flipped-classroom studies
- Students must be registered on the EdPuzzle platform and complete viewing-check questionnaires
#### Constraints
- Implemented with 202 sixth-grade primary students in four public and state-subsidised schools in Granada, Spain

### Target Learners
- sixth-grade primary education students aged 11–12

### Learning Goals
- nutritional education and healthy habits
- motivation, autonomy and self-regulation towards learning
