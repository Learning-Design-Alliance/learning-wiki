## Description
The article argues that astronomy is an effective holistic educational tool because it embodies 'a unique combination of science, technology and culture', and that in the 21st century it has become 'a gateway to physics, chemistry, biology and mathematics'. UNAWE applies this by encapsulating space-based themes in stories to develop language skills and in problems that develop numerical skills.
