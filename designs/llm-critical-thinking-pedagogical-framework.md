# A design-oriented pedagogical framework for integrating LLMs to scaffold critical thinking in higher education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes a normative, design-oriented framework grounded in Design-Based Research, instructional-design theory, AI ethics and Freirean critical pedagogy. It links three risks of unstructured LLM use to six intellectual processes and eight design principles, arguing that 'the value of generative AI in education is not technical but fundamentally pedagogical'. It positions LLMs as objects of inquiry learners must interrogate rather than as tutors or efficiency supports.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires deliberate instructional sequencing and alignment of principles with the cognitive, metacognitive and epistemic processes they support
#### Constraints
- The authors state the framework 'is not intended as a definitive or universal solution, but as provisional pedagogical orientation that must remain open to critique, adaptation, and contextual interpretation'
