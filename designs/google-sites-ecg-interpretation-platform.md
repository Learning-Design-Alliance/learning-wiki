# Dedicated Google Sites platform for methodical ECG interpretation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A free web-based learning platform built with the Google Sites application, part of the "Google Workspace for Education" suite, accessed via institutional accounts. The site was "structured in a homepage and ECG bank containing 30 ECG tracings accompanied by their corresponding clinical vignettes", with guidance on a recommended three-week work progression, a debriefing method, and a downloadable nine-step ECG interpretation worksheet. Separate Google Sites provided weekly answers to the interpreted tracings. Students interpreted two pre-selected ECGs per day, and weekly debriefing sessions reviewed ten designated tracings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access via institutional accounts of the format firstname.lastname@fmt.utm.tn or firstname.lastname@etudiant.fmt.utm.tn
- Weekly debriefing sessions with a supervising tutor to review and discuss the designated tracings
- Students required to complete the interpretation of at least 24 out of 30 ECG tracings
#### Constraints
- Development of the ECG bank and corresponding answers was time-consuming
- The study was conducted at a single center with a relatively small sample

### Target Learners
- First-year second-cycle medical students (MS3) during a cardiology rotation

### Learning Goals
- Methodical interpretation of electrocardiograms
- Structured application of a nine-step ECG interpretation approach
