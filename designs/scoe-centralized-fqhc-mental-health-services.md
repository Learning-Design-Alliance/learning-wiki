# SCOE centralized school-based mental health services through an FQHC-eligible Medi-Cal billing model

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
SCOE hires clinicians and places them in schools under the Sacramento County Health Center, a Federally Qualified Healthcare Center eligible for Medi-Cal billing. The brief states these clinicians "provide clinical mental health services (Tier 3) to students and support Tier 1 and Tier 2 prevention and early intervention programs and services at school sites." SCOE also manages Medi-Cal billing centrally, which the brief says removes administrative burden from schools and allows centralized data tracking such as numbers of students served.
