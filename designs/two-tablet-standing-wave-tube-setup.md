# Two-tablet experimental setup for visualising standing-wave pressure and air-displacement profiles in a semi-open tube

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A low-cost arrangement using two tablets (or smartphones), a glass tube open at one end and closed at the other, a speaker, a microphone taped to a wooden rod, a tape measure, and small styrofoam balls. One tablet runs the free app SGenerator Lite as a signal generator driving the speaker; the other runs the free app oScope Lite with the microphone, which is moved along the tube to reveal maxima and minima of the pressure-variation profile. Styrofoam balls spaced along the tube visualise air displacement, requiring a speaker of around 10 W. The authors note "ela pode ser reproduzida n˜ao s´o em um laborat´orio, mas tamb´em em sala de aula e outros ambientes".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tablets or smartphones with the required apps (SGenerator Lite and oScope Lite) installed and working; a speaker of reasonable power around 10 W and sufficiently spaced styrofoam balls so their inertia does not hinder their movement.
#### Constraints
- Demonstrated for a semi-open tube; the same experiment could be reproduced in a tube open at both ends but was not done here, and a fully closed tube would require somewhat greater sophistication.

### Target Learners
- mid-level and higher-education physics students

### Learning Goals
- visualising pressure-variation and air-displacement profiles of harmonic modes of stationary sound waves in a tube

- Harmonic Profiles Observed 404 1212 Hz [+M]
