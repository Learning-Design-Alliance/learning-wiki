# Three-question two-part post-lab quiz instrument for circuit understanding

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The assessment instrument used after each of the five lab activities: each quiz "consisted of three two-part "check all that apply" questions", with the second part asking for "an explanation of why the ﬁrst part's answer was chosen" to differentiate correct guesses from correct reasoning. The three questions targeted circuit recall, procedural recall, and an altered procedure in which students determined the function of a slightly different circuit.
