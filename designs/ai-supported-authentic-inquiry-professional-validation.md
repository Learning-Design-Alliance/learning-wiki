# Integrate AI-supported experimental design with professional validation to make authentic inquiry manageable in undergraduate laboratories

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article presents an approach where students use AI to design lab manuals for real-world sample analysis, then implement them experimentally. AI's "rapid response and capacity to produce extensive information from user prompts" helps students narrow broad topics and develop manuals, making Level 3 authentic inquiry more accessible. Independent professional validation ensures safety and feasibility before implementation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- AI tools available to students with institutional guidance on ethical use
- Professional validation team to verify safety and feasibility of student-designed manuals
- Consultation sessions with instructor for safety protocols and practical guidance
- Two draft submissions with instructor feedback before experimental sessions
#### Constraints
- Students need prior hands-on experience with relevant analytical instruments
- Requires staff availability for increased supervision workload
- Students need foundational knowledge to critically evaluate AI-generated content

### Target Learners
- third-year undergraduate analytical chemistry majors

### Learning Goals
- experimental design skills
- critical thinking
- problem-solving
- troubleshooting
- scientific communication
