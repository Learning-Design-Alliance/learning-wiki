# Instructional governance: the designed allocation of authority, agency, accountability, context, and evaluation visibility in AI-mediated teaching interactions

## Description
The article defines instructional governance as "the designed allocation of pedagogical authority, learner agency, human accountability, contextual boundaries, and evaluation visibility within an AI-mediated teaching interaction". It locates governance at the level where educators and students encounter AI: prompts, rubrics, approval gates, interfaces, dashboards, style guides, and course-specific constraints. The construct complements institutional policy by making governance choices visible in concrete instructional artifacts and workflows.
