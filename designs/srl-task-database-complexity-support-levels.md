# Task database with 75 biology problems organized by five complexity levels and three support levels

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The learning environment contained a task database of monohybrid cross problems in biology: "The task database (cf. Kostons et al. 2012) contained 75 problems at ﬁve levels of complexity, with three levels of support within each complexity level". Complexity increased via multiple answers, reasoning type, generations, and unknowns; support faded from 4/5 worked-out steps to none, in line with the completion strategy and the 4C/ID model. Students freely selected eight tasks during the self-regulated learning phase.
