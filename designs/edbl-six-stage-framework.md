## Description
EDBL is a pedagogical approach grounded in problem-based learning and constructivist theories in which students engage in authentic, iterative design challenges to develop scientific understanding and practical skills. The article describes it as typically involving six core stages: "(1) identifying the problem, (2) researching the problem, (3) proposing possible solutions, (4) selecting and developing the best solution, (5) prototyping and testing, and (6) evaluating and redesigning." In science education it fosters systems thinking, collaboration, and creative problem-solving deemed essential for STEM literacy. The study uses EDBL stages as the primary evaluation framework for rating AI-generated lesson plans.
