# School survey profiles comparing a school to demographically similar schools and system trends over time

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report's display format charts a school's score on each measure across five survey years alongside two reference trends. It "compares your school both to other schools that are demographically similar to yours, and to the Chicago public school system as a whole," with a gray box marking the middle two-thirds of schools and a star locating the school's standing. Time-trend data show whether reform efforts are improving, and trend bands distinguish somewhat from substantially above- or below-average reports.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multiple years of survey participation to display trends; at least seven respondents per measure for a valid school reading
#### Constraints
- Star placement carries unavoidable imprecision, so small differences between the school and reference lines should not be over-read; a school's own data may be missing for some years

### Target Learners
- school leadership teams, principals, teachers, and Local School Councils

### Learning Goals
- school self-assessment and improvement planning
