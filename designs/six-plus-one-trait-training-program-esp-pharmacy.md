# Ten-session 6+1 trait training program for ESP pharmacy students

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A researcher-constructed training program based on the 6+1 traits model, aiming to develop ESP university students' critical thinking skills, writing skills, and study skills for lifelong learning such as communication and autonomy. "The program consisted of eight topics that were distributed over ten sessions (six sessions for paragraph writing and four sessions for essay writing." Each session was 105 minutes alongside one semester, applied over 11 weeks during the academic year 2017/2018. Students were evaluated on their contribution and participation in paragraph and essay writing and were allowed to evaluate their own writings.
