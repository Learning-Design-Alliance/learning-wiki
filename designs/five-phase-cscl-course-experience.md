# Five-phase online collaborative learning experience with projects and case studies

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The studied courses embedded "online collaborative working processes using project and case-study based methodologies" across five phases: pre-group identification of classmates, self-formed groups of 4 or 5 members via forums and Google Sheets, intra-group agreements on roles and communication tools, development of collaborative learning activities, and online self-assessment and peer-assessment of process and results. Teachers created virtual spaces, facilitated debates, gave feedback, and assessed group processes and products.
