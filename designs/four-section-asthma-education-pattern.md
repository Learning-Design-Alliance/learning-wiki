# Four-section interactive education pattern: introduction, joint education, patient demonstration, and follow-up sessions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's intervention follows a four-part session structure: "(i) introduction (patient and caregiver); (ii) education (patient and caregiver); (iii) demonstration component (patient); and (iv) follow-up session(s) (both caregiver alone and patient and caregiver together)." Content covers pathophysiology, triggers, attack signs, control, and action plans, followed by a hands-on inhaler demonstration checked with a pulmonary airflow meter. This joint child-plus-caregiver design with a scored skills demonstration and scheduled follow-ups is the reusable lesson/program-level pattern.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An asthma educator to demonstrate metered dose inhaler technique per CDC guidelines and score ten steps, with steps 2–8 required for adequacy
- A pulmonary airflow meter to check the patient's effort
#### Constraints
- Successful MDI transition required successful demonstration of steps 2–8 of the technique checklist

### Target Learners
- Pediatric patients with asthma and their caregivers

### Learning Goals
- Disease knowledge
- Symptom self-management
- Device technique
