# Three-day WHO-based malaria microscopy refresher course with lectures, hands-on sessions and a take-home colour atlas manual

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention was a three-day refresher course based on the WHO training manual, delivered by one technician from the Angola National Malaria Control Programme and one expert microscopist. It "included lectures and practical hands-on sessions" covering malaria disease, epidemiological data, slide cleaning and storage, preparation of thick and thin blood films, Giemsa staining, use and care of the microscope, examination of blood films and correct identification of Plasmodium species. Each participant received a manual with a colour atlas of Plasmodium species for note-taking and later laboratory use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivery by trained facilitators from the national malaria control programme and an expert microscopist
- A manual with a colour atlas of the different Plasmodium species for each participant
#### Constraints
- Course attendance was only 9, 8 and 11 participants across the three laboratories, limited by schedule incompatibility, lack of financial reward and the impossibility of laboratory closure

### Target Learners
- Laboratory technicians in malaria-endemic hospital laboratories

### Learning Goals
- Malaria microscopy diagnosis: blood film preparation, Giemsa staining, microscope use, and Plasmodium species identification
