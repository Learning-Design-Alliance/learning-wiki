# Monash blended EBM program: classroom workshops, YouTube lectures, library guide, and self-directed mobile learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
This is the blended learning intervention evaluated in the randomized trial and its economic analysis. It integrated classroom, online, and mobile components: "The BL approach to teaching EBM integrated (1) classroom activities (lecture/tutorial) with (2) online and (3) mobile learning". Online activities comprised a YouTube channel of 11 lectures averaging 17 minutes and a Monash University library guide indexing databases, textbooks, and guidelines; mobile learning involved self-directed patient interactions on clinical placements. The format kept total face-to-face hours identical to the traditional approach.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Staff time to create the YouTube resources, tailor content, and design activities as a one-off transition investment; the library guide already existed, so only activity design was costed.
#### Constraints
- Mobile learning is completely student self-directed, with no costs factored into the cost model; the article notes wider applicability depends on the type of blended learning utilized, staffing expertise, and educational context.

### Target Learners
- Third-year medical students (undergraduate and postgraduate) entering clinical training

### Learning Goals
- Evidence-based medicine competency: asking clinical questions, acquiring and appraising evidence, and applying it to patients
