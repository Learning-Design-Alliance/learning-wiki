# Scaffolded hackathon structure interweaving tutorials, breakout groups, small group coding challenges, and report-outs

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The hackathon design interwove "technical tutorial-style talks to lay the foundation and situate everyone on a level playing field, breakout groups on related topics, and small group coding challenges". After each discussion or hacking session, a report-out let each participant or group give a short update while facilitators asked follow-up questions, noted synergies, and connected groups to additional resources.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pre-hackathon reading assignments to build common understanding among the group
- Hacking groups of 3-4 tasked with a design challenge, designed to ensure a variety of coding expertise and skills in each, with 2-3 faculty leads floating between groups
#### Constraints
- Year 1 narrowed the scope to using DDM to analyze data to keep the 2-day event achievable

### Target Learners
- participants ranging from high school students to faculty with varied coding experience

### Learning Goals
- algorithm development and data analysis skills
- a common language for discussing material properties and computational approaches
