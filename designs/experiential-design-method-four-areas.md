## Description
The article cites Fazidin Mohd Jabar et al. (2007) describing a virtual reality or game-theory design approach called the "experiential design method" with "4 major areas which are 'Focus', 'Emotion', 'Space/Form' and 'Orientation'". Focus concerns experiencing sense of place, skill or role play; in Virtual Haj it makes users feel as if they are in Mecca performing the pilgrimage; Emotion concentrates on inner feelings and, for Virtual Haj, can elevate faith and devotion. The study applies this framing to justify its pilgrimage simulation.
