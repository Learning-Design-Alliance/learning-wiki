# Five-component training program for medical physicists transitioning to direct patient care

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article presents a training program comprising five sequenced components: a 1-day Clinician-Patient Communication Workshop, two 2-hour Communication Strategies for Radiation Oncology sessions, Simulated Patient Interactions with trained patient actors, Faculty-Observed Patient Consults, and a Case-Based Treatment Toxicity Course. The program "emphasizes experiential, practice‐based learning over didactic presentations" and was designed with the UC San Diego School of Medicine for physicists moving into clinical roles with direct patient contact.
