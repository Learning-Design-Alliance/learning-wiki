# Ten-stage P4C enquiry session structure from stimulus to review

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
SAPERE's P4C session follows ten named stages: getting set, presentation of stimulus, thinking time, question making, question airing, question choosing, first thoughts, building, last thoughts, and review. Pupils and teacher sit in a circle, pupils generate and vote on the dialogue question, and in the building stage "the rule is to justify opinions with reasons" while the teacher models fallibilism. The stages are a flexible guide and need not all occur in one session.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A classroom protocol that must be adhered to, with pupils and teacher seated in a circle facing each other
#### Constraints
- The stages can be used flexibly as the teacher's expertise grows and do not need to be completed all in one session

### Target Learners
- Primary school pupils across all education age ranges

### Learning Goals
- Questioning
- Reasoned argumentation
- Listening to others' views
