# O-AMAS blended course design with five linked stages

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course design pattern arranges a blended course in five parts matching the O-AMAS links: multi-dimensional objectives (macro knowledge/ability/quality goals plus per-lesson micro objectives across Bloom's six bands), activation of students physically, emotionally, and cognitively, diversified teaching methods (LBL, TBL, PBL, CBL), process-plus-final assessment with feedback, and a brief student-led summary using platform-generated hot words or mind maps. The article summarizes the design in Fig. 1 and applies it across pre-class, in-class, and post-class stages.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Online platforms (MOOC, Chaoxing) integrated with offline classroom and clinical internship
- Assessment aligned to both low-order and high-order objectives
#### Constraints
- Designed and evaluated for a single core clinical nursing course; the article notes limitations in number of courses, class hours, and sample size

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students

### Learning Goals
- self-directed learning
- internal medicine nursing theory and practice
- deep learning of key content
