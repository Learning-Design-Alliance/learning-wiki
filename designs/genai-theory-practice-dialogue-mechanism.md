# Theory-practice dialogue mechanism: generative AI bridges the gap between theory instruction and business practice

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The first of the article's three proposed mechanisms for reconfiguring classroom pedagogy with generative AI. As the article states, "The theory-practice dialogue mechanism is designed to bridge the gap between theory and practice." In the strategic management course example, the mechanism uses generative AI to connect theoretical frameworks taught in class with contemporary business practice, supporting the shift from knowledge transmission toward capability development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to generative AI tools in the classroom
- A course with substantial theory content that students must connect to practice
#### Constraints
- Proposed for management education, illustrated with a strategic management course; the article reports no empirical evaluation of the mechanism
