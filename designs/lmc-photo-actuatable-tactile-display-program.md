# First LMC program: refreshable, photo-actuatable tactile displays for the visually impaired

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
As a proof of concept for the LMC approach at the K-12 segment, the paper describes the first LMC program, designed for the dissemination of refreshable, photo-actuatable tactile displays to the visually impaired, serving both lab-to-market and lab-to-classroom initiatives. Through programmatic design via the logic model, the overlap among classroom, market, and laboratory was predicted to nucleate when a technology in developmental phase is deployed in a classroom with high affinity to such technology.
