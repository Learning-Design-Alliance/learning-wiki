# Interactive video-based online learning module on medical communication (OpenOlat web-based training)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A web-based training delivered through the open-source learning management system OpenOlat, developed by the University Hospital Basel medical communication department and the University of Basel. It covered the lecture content in five domains, including the "WEMS" and "NURSE" technique sets and the "book metaphor" for structuring information. Access was via secure password-protected link, with a completion gating feature ensuring videos were fully watched; the module plus knowledge assessment took about 90 minutes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered through a learning management system (OpenOlat) with secure access link and password
- A dedicated time slot and electronic device with headphones for completion in person
#### Constraints
- Designed to comply with the learning objectives of the University of Basel medical curriculum

### Target Learners
- Second-year medical students

### Learning Goals
- Knowledge and application of patient-centered physician-patient communication techniques
