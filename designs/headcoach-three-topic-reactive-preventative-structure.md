# Three-topic sequential structure moving from recognising mental illness, to helping an individual employee, to minimising workplace mental health risks

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
HeadCoach organises its content into three broad topics completed sequentially: Common mental illnesses, How to help an employee you are concerned about, and Minimising mental health risks in the workplace. The article describes this as building confidence across "reactive and preventative managerial strategies", so the sequence moves from understanding mental health problems, to supporting individual employees, to primary-prevention skills for the whole team. Each topic comprises between three and seven 10-min modules featuring text, activities, short videos, and practical exercises.
