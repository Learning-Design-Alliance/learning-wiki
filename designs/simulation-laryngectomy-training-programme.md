# Novel simulation-based training programme for urgent laryngectomy care combining didactic teaching, immersive scenarios, debriefing, skills practice and expert patient session

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A one-day training programme delivered in an NHS medical simulation suite using a modified SimMan mannequin, wearable prosthetics and actors. The article states that practical sessions involved immersive simulation scenarios; debriefing; clinical skills-based sessions, such as practicing stoma care or cleaning a voice prosthesis; and discussion with an expert PWL patient. Taught elements covered anatomical changes, differentiation from tracheostomy, HME use, and Human Factors and crisis resource management skills, facilitated by experienced head and neck cancer-specialising SLTs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivery in a high-fidelity medical simulation room with separate classroom, a SimMan mannequin with front of neck airway, prosthetics, and facilitation by experienced head and neck cancer-specialising SLTs supported by simulation centre staff
#### Constraints
- Each training day was designed for nine participants to ensure fidelity in the simulation scenarios and to support shared, skills-based learning

### Target Learners
- Multidisciplinary healthcare professionals who work with people with laryngectomy or may encounter them out of hours, such as A&E staff and on-call respiratory physiotherapists

### Learning Goals
- Managing urgent laryngectomy care needs including stoma care, voice prosthesis problems and resuscitation of a neck breather
- Human Factors and crisis resource management skills
