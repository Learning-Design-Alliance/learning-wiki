# La-ACES two-semester student team payload program

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A project-based course structure in which recruited university students form teams, receive training in aerospace subjects over one semester, then spend a second semester designing and building a payload that flies on a high-altitude balloon, with minimal help from a supervising instructor. The article describes that "The teams receive training (on various aerospace subjects) over a semester and spend another semester working on a payload that will ﬂy on a high altitude balloon." The design is student-driven, fostering ownership.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- NASA/consortium funding and access to a high-altitude balloon launch facility
- A supervisor, usually an instructor at the students' institution
#### Constraints
- Extreme temperatures of the upper atmosphere are a main technical challenge for payload electronics

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students from Louisiana universities

### Learning Goals
- aerospace subject knowledge
- team-based experimental design and payload engineering
