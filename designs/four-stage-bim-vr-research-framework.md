# Four-stage research framework for BIM+VR gamified learning in architectural and construction education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article organizes the BIM LOD + VR game research into four major stages of work, shown in Figure 12: prototype development (modeling in Revit/Rhinoceros/Unity), precedent exploration, studio setup with practice areas, and gamification with level development mapped to LODs. The authors state the first two stages and a portion of the third are reported in this paper, with a larger scale experiment planned for construction education.
