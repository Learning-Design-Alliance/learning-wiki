# 90-minute 5A smoking cessation counselling training module with parallel online and role-playing variants

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A newly designed one-time 90-minute course implemented in the compulsory module on prevention and health promotion, teaching the 5A's (ask, advise, assess, assist, arrange) brief intervention model. Structure and content were identical across variants, using the same graphics and explanations; the seminar variant used small role-play groups of three students with rotating doctor, patient and observer roles and structured feedback, while the online variant used a 14-minute video with a commenting background voice and Prezi mind-map presentation. All students received a pocket card summarising the 5A's model and the Fagerström test.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Three instructors available for questions and feedback in the seminar variant
- Email support to solve technical problems with the online module
#### Constraints
- Both interventions were designed as a one-time 90-minute course with identical structure and content
- The video example was in English while the role-play and OSCE were in German

### Target Learners
- third-year medical students

### Learning Goals
- smoking cessation counselling skills following the 5A's model
- knowledge of nicotine dependence and therapy options
