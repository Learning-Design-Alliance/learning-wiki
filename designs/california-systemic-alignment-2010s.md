# California's 2010s systemic alignment of standards, curriculum frameworks, instructional materials, assessments, teacher education, equitable funding, and multiple measures accountability

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper presents California's 2010s reforms, enacted while the author was State Board of Education president, as a promising example of systemic standards-based reform. The state "developed alignment among standards, curriculum frameworks, instructional materials, assessments, and teacher education, coupled with a new, more equitable funding system, and a multiple measures accountability system." The paper judges the educator-capacity impact positive but limited, because no infrastructure existed for delivering high-quality professional development intensively across the state.
