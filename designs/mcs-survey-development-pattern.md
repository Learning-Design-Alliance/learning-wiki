# MCS development pattern: free-response reasoning and interviews guide multiple-choice distracter design with faculty review

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's survey-development pattern proceeds from a test blueprint tabulating content scope and cognitive complexity (a simplified Bloom's taxonomy), through free-response questions with student reasoning, to multiple-choice items whose distracters reflect common difficulties. The authors state they "used the most frequent incorrect responses in the free -response questions and interviews as a guide for making the alternative distract er choices", followed by faculty review of wording and student interviews to detect misinterpretation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty consultation on content and performance criteria
- Free-response administration with reasoning before multiple-choice design
- Faculty review of several survey versions for ambiguity and individual student interviews
#### Constraints
- Designed for introductory magnetism content up to Faraday's law

### Target Learners
- introductory physics students

### Learning Goals
- conceptual understanding of magnetism
