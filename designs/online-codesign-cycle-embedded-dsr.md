# Participatory online co-design cycle embedded in design science research

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study embeds participatory design into the design science research methodology as the driver of the design, development, and demonstration phases. The article prints that "the online co-design process cycle involves planning, exploring, designing, discussing, and evaluating stages", which are iterative and strengthen research rigor within DSR. Online co-design was adopted because face-to-face meetings were not feasible during the Covid-19 pandemic, and it engages users and researchers to conceptualize, create, and prototype an artefact through an online environment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Engagement of users and researchers in iterative online stages of planning, exploring, designing, discussing, and evaluating
#### Constraints
- Adopted to suit situations where face-to-face meetings between participants and researchers are not feasible

### Target Learners
- higher education students co-designing educational games

### Learning Goals
- computational thinking knowledge through co-designed mini games
