# Three-stage methodological teaching model for endosuture competence progression (pre-test, concept teaching, training station)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a methodological teaching model in which competence in endosuture is built through three sequential realistic-simulation stages. As printed, "The work was divided in three distinct stages": V.1 evaluates previous proficiency without orientation, V.2 teaches videosurgery concepts (ergonomics, triangulation, ambidexterity, stereotaxy, hapticity, fulcrum effect) with immediate task execution, and V.3 provides preceptor-guided training and a final timed, photographed task. The model was used in the BariUp course to transfer psychomotor concepts to the operating room.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Realistic abdominal cavity simulators with needle holders, suture threads and suture plates
- Theoretical-practical classes covering videosurgery concepts before guided training
- Preceptor orientation during the training station
- Timed and photographed task performance for evaluation across stages
#### Constraints
- Evaluated in a single course with 11 surgeons of varied specialties and experience levels
- Applied to endosuture for Nissen fundoplication in bariatric-surgery training

### Target Learners
- surgical residents
- young surgeons advancing in bariatric videosurgery

### Learning Goals
- laparoscopic endosuture skill
- psychomotor concept absorption
- progression toward proficiency in bariatric procedures
