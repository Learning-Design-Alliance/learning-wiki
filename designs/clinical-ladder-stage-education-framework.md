# Clinical ladder stage framework for tailoring nurses' continuing education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article grounds its needs assessment in nursing career-stage frameworks: Benner's proficiency model proposing five stages from novice to expert, Buchan's clinical ladder for competence and career development, and Jang's four-stage categorization of clinical experience (novice, advanced novice, competent, proficient). The study used these stages to partition focus groups and argues "education should align with their clinical careers, and practice education should match the educational needs of each clinical level."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Nurses must be classifiable into career stages by months of clinical experience (novice entry to 1 year; advanced novice 13–47 months; competent 48–83 months; proficient from 84 months)
#### Constraints
- The article notes a curriculum based on a clinical ladder system was reported effective for satisfaction, motivation, and competence development citing prior work, not tested in this study

### Target Learners
- clinical nurses at tertiary university hospitals across career stages

### Learning Goals
- medication safety competence including attitudes, knowledge, and skills
