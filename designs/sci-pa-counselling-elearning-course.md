# Self-guided e-learning course on SCI physical activity counselling best practices (10 modules plus bonus material)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A self-guided e-learning course co-developed by an international expert panel to familiarize health, fitness and lifestyle professionals with the best practices for SCI physical activity counselling. "information is presented in different ways (e.g., text, short videos, knowledge quizzes, reflection exercises) to create an engaging learning experience." It comprises 10 modules plus two bonus reflection modules, targets the 'remembering' and 'understanding' levels of the revised Bloom's Taxonomy, follows UDL Guidelines, and takes on average about 2.5 hours to complete. The course will be made freely available after the study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Designed for professionals who provide guidance or counselling to adults with SCI on starting, changing and/or maintaining a physically active lifestyle
- Learning objectives: describe and understand the best practices, understand how to use them in conversations, and use SCI-specific physical activity knowledge
#### Constraints
- Some participants experienced technical difficulties, including the system not saving progress between sessions
- Participants suggested adding closed captions and more counselling example videos

### Target Learners
- Health, fitness and lifestyle professionals (counsellors, physiotherapists, occupational and recreation therapists, fitness trainers, SCI peer mentors)

### Learning Goals
- Knowledge of SCI physical activity counselling best practices
- Self-efficacy for using the best practices in conversations with clients with SCI
