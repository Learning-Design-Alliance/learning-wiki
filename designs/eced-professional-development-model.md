# ECED professional development model: institute, site visits, and monthly check-ins across two years

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
ECED's professional development combines an initial institute, recurring on-site visits, and regular remote check-ins. "Both years include: Three-day institute for teachers (introductory/reflection, pace support, strategy and lesson modeling)" and "Four 3-day site visits per year (workshops, model lessons, strategies, individual and group coaching, class visits)", plus "Monthly phone check-ins". The second year of professional development is optional but recommended, making the model a sustained, coaching-based design rather than a one-off training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

- Monthly phone check-ins
