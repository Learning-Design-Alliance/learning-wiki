# A ten-task weekly working-memory strategy sequence embedded in EFL lessons

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The intervention consisted of ten weekly classroom tasks built around the Gathercole and Alloway (2007) strategies plus phonological-loop activities: reducing memory load with scrambled stories, color-coding verbs and nouns, word posters, Socrative and Kahoot quizzes, clustering with graphic organizers, KWL charts, and four listening-based phonological-loop tasks. Each task had printed success criteria. The authors chose these strategies because they "can indeed be incorporated into regular language lessons and do not need major adjustments from the teacher or the classroom setting".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Strategies are applied one per week over a ten-week period within regular English lessons
#### Constraints
- The article does not report which individual strategy contributed most to the effect

### Target Learners
- undergraduate beginner (elementary-level) EFL students

### Learning Goals
- retention and retrieval of English vocabulary
- use of past-tense forms in reading, writing, speaking and listening tasks
