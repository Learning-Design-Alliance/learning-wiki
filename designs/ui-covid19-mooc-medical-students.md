# COVID-19 pandemic response massive open online course for medical students (Universitas Indonesia)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A massive open online course implemented by an Indonesian medical institution to equip medical students with essential biomedical, clinical, and epidemiologic knowledge on COVID-19. The article reports that "the course also provided basic skills trainings including infection prevention and control, mass education, and risk communication through video lessons and webinar sessions". The article cites evidence that MOOC use during the pandemic has been shown beneficial in strengthening healthcare systems, and that online PPE training produced comparable performances to offline instructor-led training.
