# Six-test repeated-measures cognitive battery (UFOV, SOPT, IT, TSP, SART, TMT)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
A battery of six widely used psychometric tests administered in a fixed order (UFOV, SOPT, IT, TSP, SART, TMT) at four time-points, chosen because each measures a process previously shown to be alcohol-sensitive, each is well-known and easily accessible, and each is "suitable for repeated measures designs". The battery could be completed in 40-50 minutes, minimizing variability from rising and falling BACs. TMT forms were symmetrically transformed to remove learning effects across presentations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tasks must be free of strategies producing abrupt outcome changes, or alterable to achieve this, for repeated administration
#### Constraints
- The battery requires approximately 40-50 minutes per test-run, which the authors note constrains testing within a stable BAC window

### Target Learners
- adults aged 18-45 years

### Learning Goals
- measuring dose-related changes across processing speed, working memory, strategic optimization, vigilance, response inhibition, cognitive flexibility and psychomotor function

### Claims

- [Acute alcohol produces a significant main effect on performance on most cognitive tasks, with partial eta-squared effect sizes from 0.07 to 0.37, but not on TSP or SART-OM](../claims/alcohol-main-effect-nonuniform-cognitive-tasks.md) [+W]
- [Baseline intercorrelations among the six tests were small, suggesting the battery covered relatively independent cognitive processes](../claims/baseline-intercorrelations-independent-processes.md) [+W]
- [Only SOPT, IT and SART-CO showed significant impairment at the lowest blood alcohol concentration of 0.048%, with large effect sizes (d = 0.76, 0.65 and 1.06)](../claims/three-tasks-sensitive-bac-0048.md) [+W]
- [TSP (complex spatial problem solving) and SART-OM (vigilance for frequent events) showed no significant alcohol effects, an outcome the authors report as unexpected](../claims/tsp-sart-om-null-alcohol-effects.md) [~W]
- [The authors conclude Inspection Time has strong potential as a single marker test sensitive to the time course of alcohol intoxication](../claims/inspection-time-marker-potential.md) [+W]
- [SART-CO errors rose sharply at BAC 0.048% but changed little thereafter across the 0.048% to 0.10% range, with a suspected ceiling effect](../claims/sart-co-ceiling-low-dose.md) [+W]
