# Test-Diagnose-Remedy cyclic learning model

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The CSTPP is organized around a continuous improvement cycle in which students self-test, receive a diagnosis of subject-specific weaknesses mapped to each item in the question pool, and receive targeted remediation through answer explanations and short teaching videos before retesting. As the article states, "The entire learning process involved an uninterrupted cycle of Test, Diagnose, and Remedy, forming a progressive improvement learning process." Stronger students spend less time in the remedy stage and progress at their own pace, while weaker students build confidence by repeatedly addressing weaknesses across cycles.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- a test item pool in which every question corresponds to a subject-specific weakness
- remedial materials such as teaching videos targeted to those weaknesses
#### Constraints
- the model was studied with junior high school EFL learners over a 3-month period

### Target Learners
- junior high school students

### Learning Goals
- English learning progress customized to individual ability
- remediation of specific learning weaknesses
