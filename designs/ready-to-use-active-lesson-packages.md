# Provide physically active lesson packages as ready-to-use sessions with clear curriculum-fit guidance to maximise teacher implementation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Teachers stressed that future physically active VFT intervention packages must remove preparation burden: almost all interviewees said packages must be ready to use immediately, and many wanted clear guidance on how each session fits curriculum topics. The article presents this as essential for recruitment and fidelity in future intervention work, echoing teachers' consistent reports of lack of time as a barrier to school PA interventions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sessions must be easy to implement and usable straight away, as teachers requested
#### Constraints
- Does not resolve the anticipated resistance of less ICT-confident teachers to the underlying technology

### Target Learners
- primary school pupils (ages 4-11)

### Learning Goals
- increasing physical activity within curriculum teaching
