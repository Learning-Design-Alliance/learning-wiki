# Small-group session structure pairing a computer simulation with a manual of guided physiologic experiments

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The instructional pattern places students in groups of two or three within larger facilitated groups, working through a manual of prepared experiments in an interactive computer program, with facilitator-initiated pauses for review among the larger group. The manual included "instructions on how to use the program, a lexicon of terms and reference ranges, and seven sets of experiments". Piloting with 12 learners led to revisions for readability, blank answer spaces, and an added two-compartment model illustration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A facilitator manual with answers, key teaching points, and common student questions; facilitators prepared via email and a presession virtual briefing
- A 2-hour session with approximately 24 students per facilitator group
#### Constraints
- Some respondents felt overwhelmed by the amount of content relative to the session duration; the ICU simulator portion was left as optional out-of-class work

### Target Learners
- second-year medical students in an integrated organ systems course

### Learning Goals
- causal reasoning about pulmonary gas exchange and prediction and explanation of physiologic variable changes
