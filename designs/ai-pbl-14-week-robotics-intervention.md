# 14-week localized, adaptive AI-PBL intervention for computer robotics programming

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention integrated AI tools into problem-based learning over 14 weeks (42 instructional hours) for first-year computer education students. "Localized" emphasizes grounding projects in authentic, context-specific challenges such as maze navigation, assistive robotics, recycling, and search-and-rescue tasks. "Adaptive" highlights the dynamic role of AI tools—ChatGPT for idea generation, debugging, and decision logic, and Teachable Machine for training classifiers—that provided iterative feedback and cognitive scaffolding. Students designed a line-following robot, a service robot for visually impaired individuals, a recycling-sorting robot, and a search-and-rescue robot, with Arduino kits providing tactile programming experience.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Robotics kits and AI tools (ChatGPT, Teachable Machine) available; students shared in groups because the robotics tools would not be enough for each individual
#### Constraints
- The control group received traditional lecture-based teaching on the same content without AI integration

### Target Learners
- First-year undergraduate students in a Computer Education programme

### Learning Goals
- Computational thinking
- Robotics programming skills
- Machine learning concepts
