# voLeA blended-learning course ÄGF+/CCC+ combining online video-based modules with face-to-face role plays

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
ÄGF+/CCC+ is a blended-learning course developed within voLeA as an extension of a three-session face-to-face communication course at TU Munich. Basic knowledge is imparted online in a virtual learning environment with a study time of approx. 45 minutes; instructional phases are enriched by short illustrative videos and interactive exercises, and students watch positive and negative examples of doctor-patient interaction as a basis for reflection via guiding questions. The subsequent attendance-based part consists of role plays with feedback in small groups meeting three actors (duration approx. 105 min). The project examines whether this combined model achieves additional competence gains over the purely lecture- and role-play-based model.
