# Calibrated hybrid feedback model for EAP writing instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes a hybrid model of feedback provision in which language instructors leverage generative AI to support formative assessment while maintaining pedagogical oversight. It works through calibrated prompts and pedagogical (re-)framing, so that GenAI supplies immediate comments on student writing and the instructor supplies depth and pedagogical value. The model is grounded in the study's finding that GenAI "can supplement human feedback effectively but will not replace it."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Calibrated prompts designed by the instructor
- Instructor pedagogical oversight of the feedback process
#### Constraints
- GenAI supplements rather than replaces human feedback
- Intended for academic writing instruction in EAP contexts

### Target Learners
- first-year EAP students in higher education

### Learning Goals
- academic argumentative writing
- formative feedback on second language writing
