# North Coast Teacher Residency Consortium: a half-time special education residency that lets paraeducators keep their salaries while training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Humboldt County Office of Education and Humboldt State University launched this residency in fall 2019 to recruit existing special education paraeducators across 32 primarily rural districts. "Paraeducator residents keep their jobs during the residency but are guaranteed to spend at least 50% of every day in a class setting working with a special education mentor teacher." Residents earn salaries of $18,127 to $25,635 plus benefits, receive $10,000 tuition and testing-fee coverage, and attend evening courses, with remotely located residents using distance learning tools.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Partnership agreements guaranteeing the 50% clinical fieldwork minimum and release time for program requirements, including site-swap systems; a $9,000 supplemental site budget for mentor stipends and substitutes
#### Constraints
- The program was in its first year, so results were too early to show; half-time placements are a compromise, as full-time paid placements would be ideal

### Target Learners
- Employed special education paraeducators seeking certification

### Learning Goals
- Full special education credential preparation
- Co-planning, curriculum design, and whole-class and small-group teaching with mentor support
