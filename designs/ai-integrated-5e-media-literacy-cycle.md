# AI-integrated 5E learning cycle for critical media literacy in elementary grades

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An 18-hour instructional pattern that maps each phase of the 5E learning cycle (Engage, Explore, Explain, Elaborate, Evaluate) onto specific AI and digital tools, activities, and national curriculum outcomes. The article states it involved "an 18-hour instructional intervention designed using the 5E learning cycle model—Engage, Explore, Explain, Elaborate, Evaluate—integrated with AI-based tools to foster critical media literacy among elementary school students." Tools such as ChatGPT, Perplexity AI, Canva AI, and Grammarly were embedded within pedagogical tasks rather than used as standalone applications, and the sequence was aligned with the Turkish Language and Social Studies curricula.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Alignment with national curriculum outcomes (Turkish Language and Social Studies programs)
- Digital tools selected for pedagogical potency and technological accessibility in low-resource settings
#### Constraints
- The participating school had a socioeconomically disadvantaged profile, shaping tool choices toward accessible options

### Target Learners
- fourth-grade elementary students

### Learning Goals
- critical media literacy (reading and writing)
- digital citizenship
- ethical and reflective media use
