# Open-ended ill-structured capstone design project as the culminating activity in an engineering graphics course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The course culminated in "an open ended, ill structured final design project as a capstone activity" worth 25% of the grade: a robotic arm in solid modeling software capable of picking up a ping pong ball and a pencil. Students worked in teams of two over 4 weeks, dividing arm and gripper components, encouraged to think economically about cost and materials within a given work envelope. Solutions were analyzed for adherence to size and economy constraints and demonstrated via animation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Solid Edge solid modeling software, team pairs, a 4-week design window, and a defined work envelope with size and economy constraints
#### Constraints
- The work envelope was set to given dimensions simulating constraints that often exist in engineering

### Target Learners
- undergraduate mechanical engineering students

### Learning Goals
- open-ended design problem solving with CAD solid modeling
