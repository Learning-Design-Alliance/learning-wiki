# Anatomy of Disciplinary Discernment as a framing for the AHaH labs' learning goals

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article frames its labs' goals using the Anatomy of Disciplinary Discernment of Eriksson et al. (2014), a four-level progression describing how students engage with disciplinary content. The authors write, "The goals of these labs are well-described by the Anatomy of Disciplinary Discernment as laid out by Eriksson et al.", and map students' expected movement from the first level, Disciplinary Identification, to Disciplinary Explanation, potentially Disciplinary Appreciation, and for a few students approaching Disciplinary Evaluation as they grasp how standard calculations are approximations diverging at high redshift.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must have prior familiarity with basic terms such as galaxy and redshift to begin at the identification level, as the article assumes
#### Constraints
- The article presents this framing as an interpretive lens on the labs' goals, not as an evaluated outcome of the study

### Target Learners
- introductory undergraduate astronomy students

### Learning Goals
- progressing from identifying astronomical terms to explaining and appreciating astronomical relations
