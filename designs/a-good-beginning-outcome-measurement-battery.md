# Multi-domain outcome battery: CITO achievement tests, Activ8 accelerometer, executive-function computer tests, health and wellbeing instruments

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The protocol specifies primary outcomes — academic performance via the standardized CITO test battery (arithmetic, orthography, reading comprehension) and proportion of sitting time via the Activ8® Professional 3D accelerometer worn on the upper leg for five consecutive school days — plus secondary outcomes covering executive functioning (N-Back, Tower of London, Fish Flanker, Wisconsin Card Sorting), health (shuttle run, grip strength, vertical jump, BMI, body composition, stool and sleep diaries) and wellbeing (faces scale, KIDSCREEN-52, school-environment satisfaction).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Computer tests are administered individually in a separate room under assessor supervision using Inquisit 4 software; questionnaires are completed in class under teacher supervision; parents help students keep diaries
#### Constraints
- The Activ8® has difficulty distinguishing standing from other movements in complex activities, where standing is often underestimated and detected as walking; the sleep diary and faces scale are not validated for children

### Target Learners
- primary school children aged 8–9

### Learning Goals
- academic performance
- executive functioning (working memory, planning, inhibition, cognitive flexibility)
- health and wellbeing indicators
