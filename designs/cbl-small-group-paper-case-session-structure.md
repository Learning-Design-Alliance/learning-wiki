# CBL session structure in this setting: ~90-minute small-group (15-student) paper-case sessions supplementing didactic lectures

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes the implemented CBL format: each session ran approximately 90 minutes with groups of 15 students facilitated by a physiology faculty member, using paper-based clinical cases designed by faculty with clinical input and “aligned with recently covered lecture content to reinforce conceptual integration rather than clinical decision-making”. CBL served as a supplementary strategy, not a replacement for lectures.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A faculty facilitator per group, oriented to session objectives
- Paper-based cases with clinical input aligned to lecture content
#### Constraints
- Facilitators had no formal CBL certification training
- Sessions did not replace traditional didactic lectures

### Target Learners
- Second-year MBBS students

### Learning Goals
- Conceptual integration of physiology across systems
