# Fabric robot: a human-sized rag doll with an electrical circuit quiz board on its belly

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The fabric robot was the students' central artifact: "a human sized rag doll with an electrical circuit quiz board on its belly", inspired by Brazilian folk culture. Visitors at the science fair answered a mathematics quiz and were invited to explain why the hidden circuit worked the way it did. Students were familiar with pattern making and sewing from a prior Literature exhibition, which supported its construction.
