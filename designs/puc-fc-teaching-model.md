# PUC-FC: flipped classroom combined with postgraduate-undergraduate collaboration

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The PUC-FC model is an advanced flipped-classroom paradigm in which senior postgraduates act as near-peer mentors across all course phases. Its "core concept is to use the clinical experience of senior postgraduates as scaffolding and digital technology as a lever" to address teacher shortage and delayed skill feedback. It constructs a "trinity educational ecology of 'knowledge transmission-skill training-experience inheritance'" through pre-class guidance, in-class one-on-one support and case debates, and post-class feedback reports within 48 hours.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Senior postgraduates with at least two years of clinical and academic experience, trained via a standardized mentoring protocol clarifying responsibilities, feedback standards, and intervention boundaries
#### Constraints
- The article notes total instructional exposure may confound results because the PUC-FC group received additional contact time through weekly online meetings and individualized mentoring

### Target Learners
- undergraduate stomatology students

### Learning Goals
- clinical operational competence
- case analysis and clinical reasoning
- learning satisfaction
