# PBL course structure with weekly problem situations, small-group problem-solving plans, and end-of-week solution presentations

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a reusable weekly PBL cycle for computer courses: the lecturer presents a problem situation at the beginning of class, small groups spend two course hours drafting a problem-solving plan, groups meet during the week to develop solutions, and at week's end each group projects their solutions to other groups and submits a problem report. This cycle was implemented over seven weeks with groups of six or seven members.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A two-hour introductory seminar before the research process to provide the critical structure of course subjects
- Small groups of six or seven members
- Four course hours per week
#### Constraints
- Implemented in a basic computer course covering MS Excel features and functions

### Target Learners
- pre-service primary school teachers in higher education

### Learning Goals
- problem-solving skills with spreadsheet software
- collaborative working skills
