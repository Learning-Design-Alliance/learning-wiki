# Ghana Heart Initiative BLS and ACLS training and assessment package based on AHA 2018 and 2020 guidelines

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A training and assessment package delivered by Code Red, an AHA-certified provider, consisting of 1-day BLS and 3-day ACLS courses using "lectures with PowerPoint presentations, video materials, practical demonstrations, and hands-on exercises." Course materials were provided "at least 4 weeks prior to the training for preparation." Assessment used a standardized 20-item questionnaire (10 BLS, 10 ACLS) plus CPR skills drills immediately after training, with scores ≥90% classified as adequate. The authors state it "created a standard for BLS and ACLS training and assessment that could be used in Ghana and other countries."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- AHA-certified training provider and instructors
- course guide distributed at least 4 weeks before training for preparation
#### Constraints
- immediate post-training assessment may not reflect real-world emergency performance and may introduce social desirability bias (authors' stated limitation)

### Target Learners
- health care professionals in Ghana (physician assistants, nurses, house officers, medical officers, senior medical officers, specialists, trainee specialists)

### Learning Goals
- BLS and ACLS knowledge and skills for cardiac arrest and cardiovascular emergency management
