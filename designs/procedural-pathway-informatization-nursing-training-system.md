# Nursing skills training system combining procedural pathways with information management

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A one-year in-service training system in which standardized operation pathways are built through collective and individual lesson preparation and delivered with information-technology support. Every operation procedure formed "different but regular operation pathways, including quality requirements, pre-operation preparation, operation process, post-operation treatment, and effect evaluation." A learning platform publishes appointment times, records practice via QR-code scans, and hosts recorded teaching videos; detailed scoring of operation assessment standards reduces invigilator subjectivity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A trained nursing skills operation group (15 staff with 10+ years clinical experience) to prepare standardized pathways
- A learning platform supporting appointment scheduling, QR-code practice logging, and video replay
#### Constraints
- Evaluated in a single hospital with a historical (2020) control group rather than random assignment

### Target Learners
- Newly admitted nurses and nurses requiring training within three years of admission

### Learning Goals
- Clinical nursing theoretical knowledge
- Basic and specialized nursing operation skills
- Nurse competency and clinical problem-solving ability
