# Blended-funding classroom alignment: uniform curricula, assessments, quality measures, and ratios across funding streams

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
This pattern, observed in Lindsay Unified School District, aligns preschool classrooms funded through different streams by braiding funding and applying identical standards everywhere. The report states: "All classrooms, regardless of funding stream, use the same curricula, child assessments, and quality measures and have the same teacher-child ratio (one adult for every eight children)." The district blends CSPP, TK, and other funding sources, integrates TK and CSPP staff in professional development, and prioritizes fully inclusive environments where children with disabilities learn alongside peers. A districtwide strategic plan and a district leader with ECE expertise supported this consistency.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A district leader with expertise in ECE
- A districtwide strategic plan
- Ability to braid multiple funding streams
#### Constraints
- Lindsay Unified is a small rural district and the only public preschool provider in the area, serving about 90% of eligible children

### Target Learners
- 3- and 4-year-old children in a rural district
- Children with disabilities in inclusive classrooms

### Learning Goals
- Consistent, developmentally appropriate classroom quality across funding streams
