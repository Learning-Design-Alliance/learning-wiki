# Online learning module (OLM) sequence with assessment-first design on the Obojobo platform

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The OLM sequence is the instructional and measurement artifact of this research program, implemented on the open source Obojobo platform. Each module pairs an assessment component with an instructional component: "Students have 5 attempts on the AC, which contains 1-2 multiple-choice problems, and must make at least one attempt before being allowed to access the IC." In a sequence, the required first attempt on each subsequent module assesses transfer of learning from the previous module's instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must make at least one attempt on the assessment component before being allowed to access the instructional component
- A student must either pass or use up all five attempts on the AC before being allowed to access the next module
#### Constraints
- Accurate transfer measurement holds only for the sub-population of students who did not frequently make brief submissions on their initial attempts; for those who did, the design provides an upper bound on transfer ability

### Target Learners
- introductory-level calculus-based physics students at UCF completing OLM sequences as homework

### Learning Goals
- transfer of physics problem-solving skills to slightly different contexts
- learning to solve specific problem types from online tutorials
