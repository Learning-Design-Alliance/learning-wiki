# Diagnostic computer-based arithmetical puzzle task implemented in Imagine Logo

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The outcome of the bootstrapping process was a diagnostic computer-based task for investigating mathematical thinking, in which pupils solve provided puzzles using provided equalities to transform an arithmetical sum into its answer. The software was programmed in Imagine Logo, which "lends itself to the fast proto-typing of microworlds". Implementation occurred rapidly and little re-programming or debugging has proved necessary despite substantial trialing in a variety of primary schools.
