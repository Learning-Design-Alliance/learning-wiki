# Model program for community care of mentally deficient school children

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Presented in the reviewed book on the basis of the New Haven survey: its results "are used to make specific and valuable recommendations for the local situation and a model program for the community care of mentally deficient school children is presented." Children reported by teachers as mentally deficient were given Doll's short Binet and some other tests.
