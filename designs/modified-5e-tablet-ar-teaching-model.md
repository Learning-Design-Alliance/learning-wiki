# Modified Bybee 5E instructional model adapted for tablet-based AR learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The study's teaching method was "based on a slightly modified version (so as tablets could fit to the process) of Bybee's 5E". Its stages were: a short teacher introduction with engagement and exploration activities; use of the application for exploring and studying the material; application of new knowledge to everyday situations using worksheets; and evaluation by completing exercises in the application. Students worked in pairs with one tablet, were free to discuss and collaborate, and the teacher acted as a facilitator without giving away solutions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students work in pairs with one tablet per pair
- The teacher acts as a facilitator rather than giving solutions
- Enough time for students to work at their own pace (two teaching hours per session)
#### Constraints
- The method was modified from Bybee's 5E, which was not formed with tablets in mind

### Target Learners
- primary school students, sixth grade

### Learning Goals
- understanding the functions of the respiratory and circulatory systems
