# Reflective inquiry as a constructivist, student-centred instructional technique

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Reflective inquiry is defined in the article as "a thinking process through which individuals examine their experiences to better understand the assumptions and implications of events and actions". It combines inquiry activities (investigation, hypothesis formulation, data interpretation) with reflective thinking, which the article says prompts students to relate new knowledge to prior understanding and understand their own thinking. In the study it was operationalised through lesson plans using thinking skills, concept mapping, scaffolding graphic organizers, question prompt tactics and interactive journals.

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires lesson plans built on reflective instructional strategies and teacher training; the study used a one-week intensive training programme for participating teachers
