# Three Python-based Colab laboratory activities on the Fourier transform: audio signals, FT-NMR FID, and FT-IR interferogram

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article introduces a series of three self-contained Jupyter-notebook laboratory activities that guide students from qualitative to practical understanding of the Fourier transform. Activity 1 analyzes musical chords generated in Python, Activity 2 builds and analyzes model FT-NMR and FT-IR datasets, and Activity 3 processes an experimental 2-butanone FID with zero-padding and windowing. The authors state they "present a series of three self-contained, Python-based laboratory activities designed for undergraduates to understand the FT" and apply it to audio signals, an IR interferogram, and an NMR FID. Activities run on Google Colab to avoid local software installation.

### Learning Goals
