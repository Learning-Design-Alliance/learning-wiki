# Model for the development of a reading habit through access, enjoyment, attitude, and motivation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes a step model (Figure 1) describing how a reading habit develops in low literate adults. It begins with a healthy library containing a variety of books with easy access and freedom to select, leading to enjoyment and a sense of accomplishment, then a positive reading attitude, then motivation to read more, culminating in a reading habit, independent readers, and language development. The authors present it as "a model with the steps that favor the development of a reading habit", grounded in their finding that only participants with access to books and encouragement developed habits.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A healthy library containing a variety of books in genre and level, with easy access and freedom to select books
- Enjoyment and a sense of accomplishment during reading, in a low-anxiety situation where readers can choose and abandon books
#### Constraints
- The model is offered for adults with low reading skills in short-duration literacy programs, based on self-reported data

### Target Learners
- low literate adults reading at grade 3-5.9 levels, native and non-native speakers of English

### Learning Goals
- development of a reading habit and positive reading attitude
- becoming independent readers and eventual language development
