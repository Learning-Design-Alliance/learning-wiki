# Interleaved lecture-practice-lecture-practice sessions alternating with practice-lecture-practice according to content

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The integrated histology course replaces separated lecture blocks with sessions in the morphology laboratory where short lecture segments alternate with immediate microscope practice. Teaching patterns "lecture-practice-lecture-practice" and "practice-lecture-practice" were "alternatively employed according to the content", with theory and practice in a 2:1 proportion. For example, muscular tissues began with slide observation to build perceptual knowledge before the lecture, then a second observation round.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sessions held in a laboratory equipped with light microscopes, virtual microscopy, computers with digital panorama imaging system and multimedia teaching system
#### Constraints
- Theory-to-practice time proportion of 2:1 in the integrated course; capacity limited by laboratory size

### Target Learners
- second-year undergraduate medical students

### Learning Goals
- histology: microscopic structure of human tissues and organs
