# Eight-week taught course with weekly video lectures, small-group tutorials and a take-home exam modelled on a postgraduate course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The course structure was based roughly on that of an Oxford University postgraduate course: "an eight-week taught portion, with lectures, tutorials, and weekly exercises, followed by a take-home exam". Students watched one video lecture per week, then attended hour-long tutorial sessions in groups of up to 15 students where a tutor facilitated discussion and exercise work. The exam mirrored an Oxford postgraduate examination, was double-marked with a third marker for deviations over nine points, and gave participants three weeks to complete the take-home assignment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Weekly live tutorials with a tutor facilitating discussion
- Weekly video lectures watched in preparation for tutorials
#### Constraints
- During the pilot, participants had no opportunity to interact with each other outside tutorial sessions due to institutional restrictions on experiments involving minors

### Target Learners
- high school students

### Learning Goals
- advanced quantum theory concepts and problem-solving at postgraduate exam level
