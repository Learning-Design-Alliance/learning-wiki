# Structure the preclinical curriculum with frequent testing, intentional repetition, and explicit exam/study skills instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The second key domain is curriculum structure and content: a two-year preclinical curriculum in which "students are tested every three to four weeks, with intentional repetition to enhance knowledge retention." Exam skills, study skills, and time management are explicitly taught, and students performing poorly early complete required faculty-guided enrichment sessions using active learning strategies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Frequent in-course testing schedule
- Structured faculty-guided enrichment sessions for low performers
#### Constraints
- Reported in the context of one school's integrated and traditional discipline-based course mixture

### Target Learners
- preclinical medical students

### Learning Goals
- knowledge retention
- exam performance
- study and time-management skills
