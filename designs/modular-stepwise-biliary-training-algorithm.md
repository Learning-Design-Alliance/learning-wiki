# Modular stepwise training algorithm for emergency laparoscopic biliary surgery

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a modular training algorithm in which trainees attached to the biliary firm for 6 months progress through stepwise competence in five components: "1) access and pneumoperitoneum, 2) gallbladder dissection, 3) pedicle dissection and visualization of the critical view of safety when feasible and safe, 4) intracorporeal knot tying of the cystic duct and cystic artery, and 5) cholangiography ± proceeding to bile duct exploration", followed by complete cases under supervision. Access was often the last component routinely performed, and senior trainees progressed to transcystic or choledochotomy CBDE.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- On-table consultant supervision for all cases, with the consultant present as a scrubbed supervisor providing continuity in assessment, feedback, and direct observership
- Trainees demonstrate competence in the individual components before performing complete cases
#### Constraints
- Establishing access and pneumoperitoneum was often the last component routinely performed by trainees according to seniority and experience, especially in patients with central abdominal scars

### Target Learners
- Surgical trainees of all levels, from junior first-year registrars to penultimate-year trainees

### Learning Goals
- Advanced laparoscopic skills including access, dissection, intracorporeal tying, cholangiography, and common bile duct exploration
