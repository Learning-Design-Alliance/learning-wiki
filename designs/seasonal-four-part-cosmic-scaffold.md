# Seasonal four-part scaffold: structuring a show as one step outward into the cosmos per Earth season

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The show was divided into four parts aligned to Earth's seasons, each simultaneously tracing a step outward from Earth into the Universe while staying anchored to Appalachian farming rhythms: Summer covered the celestial sphere and zodiac, Fall the Moon, Winter stars and planets, and Spring the Milky Way. The authors report that "the choice of scaffold is foundational to the progression of the story," helping them deconstruct a big topic into achievable goals and providing a metric for evaluating new material.
