# Mother hotel as a supportive setting keeping mothers near their NICU newborns

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The mother hotel (Guest Mother Application) is accommodation established alongside hospitals with NICUs so mothers whose newborns require intensive care can stay near the hospital instead of being separated. The article states "Mothers' Hotels help mothers breastfeed their NICU babies until the babies become mature" and concludes that "staying in a mother hotel is a supportive practice for mothers to breastfeed their babies frequently when appropriate conditions are created and to establish maternal bonding." Most participating mothers stayed 1–2 days (70 of 100 stayed 1 day).

#### Requirements

### Learning Goals
