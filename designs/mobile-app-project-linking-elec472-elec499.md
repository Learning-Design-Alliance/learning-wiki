# Mobile application development project linking an elective course with the capstone course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The multi-course project artifact was a mobile application assignment shared by students enrolled simultaneously in ELEC 472 (Wireless Network and Applications) and ELEC 499 (Senior Design Project II). As printed, "The project was to develop a mobile application that can be used to present the results of their capstone project (ELEC 499) using the concepts students are learning in their ELEC 472 course". Teams of three or four capstone teammates began with a conceptual phase, verified the structure in simulation, and tested it integrated with their capstone prototype; sample outputs included EV-charging booking, drone cleaning, ECG monitoring and crop-health applications.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Detailed project guidelines and a grading rubric structured to fulfil the learning-outcome requirements of both courses, plus dedicated training sessions on the tools
#### Constraints
- Some students were unable to develop their comprehension because they felt the learning was too difficult and needed additional tasks, and some had troubles operating in groups

### Target Learners
- senior undergraduate electrical engineering students in capstone and elective courses

### Learning Goals
- self-learning, real-life problem-solving, teamwork, and applying elective-course concepts to the capstone prototype
