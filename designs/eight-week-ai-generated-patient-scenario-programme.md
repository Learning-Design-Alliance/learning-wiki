# Eight-week AI-generated patient scenario programme for rehabilitation education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (2 review, 1 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes an eight-week educational intervention in which second-year physiotherapy students analysed AI-generated (ChatGPT) patient scenarios and developed treatment plans. The intervention consisted of "weekly 90-minute sessions that alternated between assessment and treatment applications". Four musculoskeletal cases (cervical disc herniation, patellofemoral pain syndrome, carpal tunnel syndrome, total hip replacement) were each addressed over two weeks, alternating case evaluation and treatment planning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- AI-generated scenarios were reviewed by two academics specialising in physiotherapy and rehabilitation before use
#### Constraints
- The scenarios were used exclusively for educational purposes, and the treatment plans were not implemented on real patients

### Target Learners
- Second-year physiotherapy associate degree students

### Learning Goals
- Clinical reasoning and treatment programme development based on anatomical and rehabilitation principles
