# Three progressive thematic inquiry lessons sequenced to develop problem posing ability

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The instructional pattern is a sequence of three problem-based inquiry lessons layered onto regular lessons: "Based on this content, we designed three progressive thematic inquiry lessons; the teaching content and objectives are presented in Table 1". The progression moves from understanding the characteristics of good questions, to constructing logically progressive question chains, to interdisciplinary questioning from scientist, engineer, mathematician, and artist roles in a wind-turbine context. Each lesson targets a distinct problem posing objective and draws on prerequisite knowledge built in preceding regular lessons.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Regular lessons preceding each inquiry lesson to construct prerequisite knowledge for the topic
- A context derived from the scientific content of the preceding regular lessons for each inquiry lesson
#### Constraints
- The pattern was implemented within the Grade 3 'Air' unit in regular classroom teaching in a Chinese primary school

### Target Learners
- Third-grade primary school students

### Learning Goals
- Understanding good questions, building question chains, and developing interdisciplinary and creative problem posing
