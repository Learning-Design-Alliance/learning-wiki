# Four characteristics of a real research experience for young students

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Drawing on literature on undergraduate research (Osborn and Karukstis), the article identifies "the main characteristics of undergraduate research are listed as mentorship, originality, acceptability, and dissemination". It argues that for research with younger students these conditions are expected "to be fulfilled to a lesser degree, but encompassing all four elements makes for a real research experience". The article maps each characteristic onto the high school project: mentoring during the visit, faculty responsibility for originality, quality assurance of acceptability, and dissemination via student report and poster.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All four elements — mentorship, originality, acceptability, dissemination — should be present for the experience to count as real research
#### Constraints
- For younger students the conditions are expected to be fulfilled to a lesser degree than in undergraduate research

### Target Learners
- high school students
- undergraduate students
