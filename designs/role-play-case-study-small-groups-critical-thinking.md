# Use role-play, case study, and small groups to develop critical thinking in teacher education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a structured active-learning sequence combining role-play, case study, and small group work in which students analyze problematic teaching scenarios, plan responses, and rehearse solutions in a safe environment before encountering them in the field. The authors followed Bean's (2011) model where "the teacher presents students with critical thinking problems, gives students supervised practice at addressing them, and coaches their performance."
