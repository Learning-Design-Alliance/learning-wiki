# GPAS MSc in Astrophysics at the University of Split

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 2-year MSc program in Astrophysics offered by the Physics department of the University of Split, Croatia, following the Bologna convention and starting in fall 2008. The article states the degree 'will be given in English, enabling students from other countries to follow the courses in Split increasing their mobility and exchange of experiences'. Courses are divided into required and elective courses, with a thorough astrophysical background supplemented by optional courses in general physics, computer science and humanities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Applicants must have completed undergraduate studies (BSc) in physics or a related subject
- The program must actively attract bright students through outreach, media presence and local and regional scientific events for young people
#### Constraints
- There are currently no professional astronomers at the Department of Physics in Split, so the bulk of astronomy-specific teaching is given by visiting lecturers
- The first cohort commences in fall 2008 when the first Bologna-process students finish their BSc

### Target Learners
- Croatian and international students who completed a BSc in physics or a related subject

### Learning Goals
- comprehensive astrophysics knowledge and skills
- preparation for a scientific career
- research methods through scientific projects
