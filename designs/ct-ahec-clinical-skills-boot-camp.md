# CT AHEC interprofessional clinical skills boot camp

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A clinical skills boot camp within the Connecticut Area Health Education Center interdisciplinary program that trains primary healthcare professionals to serve underserved communities. Training began with "six online self-paced learning modules prior to beginning the school year for most cohorts," followed by "a one-day in-person experiential training session with some lectures" introducing core principles and practices for underserved primary care. Participants were "525 students" from dental, medical, nursing, pharmacy, physician assistant, and social work schools.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Implementing this program requires an online learning platform; teaching materials include slides and videos, which are "available at no cost from CT AHEC".
#### Constraints
- CT AHEC did not collect evaluation data on participants’ areas of discipline.

### Target Learners
- beginning graduate students in dental, medical, nursing, pharmacy, physician assistant, and social work schools

### Learning Goals
- core clinical knowledge and skills for providing primary healthcare to underserved communities
- interprofessional care skills and team-based care
