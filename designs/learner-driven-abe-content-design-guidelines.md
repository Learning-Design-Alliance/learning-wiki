# Learner-driven, situated design guidelines for adult basic education content

## Description
The article organizes prior literature into guidelines for designing and delivering learning content for adult non-literates. Content-design features are multimodality, building on previous knowledge and past experiences, and contextualized learning, where "learning is embedded within an activity, context, and culture" (situated learning theory). Delivery features are facilitated learning, repetition of content, and frequent feedback with positive reinforcement, drawing on Keller's motivational design model (attention, relevance, confidence, satisfaction). The article maps the selected EPM content onto these features.
