# Noether's theorem: every continuous symmetry of the laws of physics corresponds to a conservation law

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Noether's theorem, proved by Emmy (Amalie) Noether in 1905, establishes a deep connection between continuous symmetries of the laws of physics and conservation laws. The article uses it as the theoretical backbone of the proposed module: conservation of momentum, energy, and angular momentum are presented as consequences of spatial translation, time translation, and rotational symmetry respectively. The authors note conservation laws are usually presented as consequences of Newton's laws, but "they emerge from symmetry concepts far deeper than Newton's laws."
