# Guided activity “Journey to a Neutron Star”

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A guided V-SeMo activity in which learners investigate light deflection in the vicinity of a neutron star by constructing geodesics in the equatorial plane of the Schwarzschild spacetime exterior to a non-rotating, spherically symmetric neutron star. The article states: "“Journey to a Neutron Star” lets learners explore the Schwarzschild spacetime exterior to a non-rotating, spherically symmetric neutron star." An information panel embeds tasks in the story of two astronauts, with to-do lists and automatic checking of answers; learners discover that observers can in part see the side of a neutron star facing away from them.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learners should be familiar with geodesics, sector models, construction of geodesics on sector models, and the operation of V-SeMo including the 'Manipulate Geodesics' mode
#### Constraints
- The geodesics constructed are spatial geodesics used in analogy to spacetime null geodesics of light signals; the analogy is well-established but the deflection of these spatial geodesics is smaller, so neutron star parameters are set accordingly

### Target Learners
- secondary school students
- undergraduate university students

### Learning Goals
- understanding gravitational light deflection
- relating local straightness of light rays to global deflection near a massive object
