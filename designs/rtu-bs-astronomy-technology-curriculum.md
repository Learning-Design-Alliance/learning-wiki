# RTU BS Astronomy Technology curriculum: 180 units over four years, about half pure astronomy, with three academic tracks

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A four-year undergraduate degree combining pure astronomical science with engineering technology, offered since 2005 as the first formal astronomy degree program in Philippine education and revised in 2018 with Astronomy Advisory Council support. The program requires "about one hundred eighty (180) units to complete the program in four (4) years, about fifty (50%) percent is pure astronomy". Table 1 lists curricular domains including 75 units of astronomy subjects, 17 units of physics, and 9 units of mathematics, with Astrophysics, Meteorological Sciences, and Space Science Technology as academic tracks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students pursuing the degree should have a sufficient background in science and mathematics
- Passing the comprehensive examination is required to proceed to third year
#### Constraints
- About half of the 180 units are government-mandated general subjects rather than astronomy content

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate students with any suitable background seeking astronomy careers in the Philippines

### Learning Goals
- Astronomical observation and instrumentation
- Astrophysics and space science technology
- Research and scientific paper writing
