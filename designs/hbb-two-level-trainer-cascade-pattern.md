# Two-level master-trainer cascade with small-group hands-on simulation training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's training design pairs experienced central and country master trainers to train facility-level master trainers in a four-day hands-on course, who then deliver three-day facility courses to birth attendants. It used "A maximum ratio of 6 trainees:1 trainer", "Maximal opportunities to practice in trainee groups of 6", and "Before and after formal testing of knowledge and skills". The goal was to train at least one master trainer per facility to minimize the training cascade and roll out training in all facilities simultaneously, with roving monitors observing and providing feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- At least one trained facility-level master trainer per facility
- Neonatal simulators and teaching materials (Learner Workbook, Facilitator Flip Chart, Action Plan poster) for each training group of six
#### Constraints
- Refresher training was restricted to active birth attendants who regularly attended deliveries, based on delivery logs

### Target Learners
- Facility birth attendants in resource-limited settings
- New facility-level master trainers

### Learning Goals
- Neonatal resuscitation skills and knowledge
- Training-delivery skills for master trainers
