# Projects and Practices in Physics (P3): a restructured introductory calculus-based mechanics course with group VPython modeling projects

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
P3 is a newly adopted format of introductory calculus-based mechanics at Michigan State University in which "groups of students work on short modeling projects – which make use of a novel inquiry-based approach – to develop their understanding of both physics content and practice." It builds on the Matter & Interactions curriculum with a computational element using VPython. Students negotiate meaning in small groups, develop a shared vision for their group's approach, and employ science practices to navigate complex physics problems, with computers treated as indispensable modern tools that students must generate meaningful direction for.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Groups of students must leverage their understanding of fundamental physical principles to model real world phenomena computationally, i.e. generate meaningful direction to a computer, since computers do not generate meaningful direction on their own.
