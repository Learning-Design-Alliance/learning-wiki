# Integrated palliative care course combining TBL, flipped classroom, and experiential home visits for fifth-year Thai medical students

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A mandatory fifth-year palliative care course at Prince of Songkla University combining large-group flipped classroom sessions (written assignment and online video submitted before the on-site session) with small-group team-based learning and experiential learning. Teams of five discussed video-based palliative cases with guided questions, and each group performed at least three home visits during a six-week family medicine rotation, presenting case findings to attending specialists.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Completion of pre-class written assignments and online videos before the on-site session
- Home visits during a six-week family medicine rotation with case presentations to attending specialists
#### Constraints
- Evaluation relied on assignments and a final multiple-choice exam; the study measured self-reported knowledge and attitudes rather than observed clinical competence

### Target Learners
- fifth-year medical students

### Learning Goals
- palliative care knowledge
- attitudes toward palliative and end-of-life care
- communication and empathy through patient and family contact
