# Full-day high-fidelity VA-ECMO cannulation simulation program with immersive refractory cardiac arrest scenarios

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A full-day training session at a university simulation center featuring two immersive scenarios of refractory cardiac arrest using a high-fidelity patient simulator with dynamic physiological features, plus hands-on VA-ECMO cannulation, ECMO console management, and transesophageal echocardiography simulation. Between scenarios, participants rotate through three procedural stations: ultrasound-guided cannulation on a CREAPLAST mannequin, TEE training on a US Mentor simulator, and ECMO console training on a portable CardioHelp system. Sessions follow briefing, scenario-based training, procedural workshops, and debriefing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A high-fidelity patient simulator, TEE simulator, ECMO console, and simulation center facilities; prior completion of an e-learning module on VA-ECMO cannulation
#### Constraints
- Resource-intensive full-day format; the trial found no overall performance advantage over a 3-hour procedural workshop

### Target Learners
- Anesthesiology and critical care residents

### Learning Goals
- VA-ECMO cannulation during refractory cardiac arrest
- ECMO console management
- Transesophageal echocardiography for ECMO-related imaging
