# Scripted small-group strategy instruction program for Grade 3 word problems

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The intervention comprised "20scriptedlessons administeredover 8 weeks," each 30 minutes, three times a week, in small groups of two to four children, delivered by six tutors. Each lesson had four phases: warm-up, instruction, guided practice, and independent practice. Treatment fidelity was high, with tutors following strategy steps at a mean of 98% across observed sequences, and interrater agreement exceeding 90% across all observed categories.
