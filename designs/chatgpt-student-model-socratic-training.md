# ChatGPT as a model of a student (tutee) for practising Socratic dialogue in teacher training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes using ChatGPT in the role of a tutee, or model of a student, with whom teachers can engage in Socratic dialogue, offering a "controlled, private and safe environment" as a starting point for pre-service teachers who lack access to students. The two versions can simulate students with different characteristics: "ChatGPT-4 modelling an easily guidable student with relatively solid physics knowledge, while ChatGPT-3.5 is modelling a more stubborn, self-confident and tougher to guide student". The ability to admit mistakes and revisit responses makes current versions suitable as a model of a student prepared to learn from its own mistakes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tasks the chatbot answers incorrectly on the first attempt, or guiding questions to probe correct answers, so there is something to dialogue about
#### Constraints
- A chatbot can, at most, simulate a simplified model of a student and is not a replacement for genuine interactions with human students
- Cases presented are limited to interactions in natural language

### Target Learners
- pre-service physics and mathematics teachers
- in-service physics teachers

### Learning Goals
- Socratic dialogue skills
- active listening
- recognising strengths and weaknesses in student reasoning
