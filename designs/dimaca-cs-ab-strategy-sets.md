# Nine communication strategies and four categories of affective backchannels implemented in DiMaCA

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors selected nine communication strategies from Dörnyei and Scott's taxonomy (including Simplification/Approximation, Code-switching, Asking clarification, Suggesting AP, Asking confirmation, Asking repetition, Expressing NU, Guessing, Repetition) chosen for probable WTC-friendliness and technical feasibility. They also introduce four categories of affective backchannels — Congratulatory, Encouraging, Sympathetic, and Reassuring AB — 'to enable ECAs to explicitly convey thoughtful support to learners by congratulating them, cheering them up, or showing sympathy in accordance with the interaction state'.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- CS selection follows a heuristically predefined order per state; AB selection is stochastic within the triggered category
#### Constraints
- Response reject was excluded because it cannot be considered a WTC-friendly strategy, illustrating the selection criteria applied

### Target Learners
- L2 learners with low willingness to communicate

### Learning Goals
- Overcoming communication pitfalls and reducing anxiety during L2 conversation
