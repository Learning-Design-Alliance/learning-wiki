# MI-based science lessons organise varied activities around all eight intelligence types

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The instructional pattern used in the experimental condition gave students varied activities drawn from the eight intelligence types within their regular science learning environment. As the article describes it, "Students carried out all the activities prepared in the light of eight multiple intelligence types in their learning environment", with lesson plans for the sciences course written in light of this strategy over the 8-week treatment, in contrast to the lecture-and-blackboard traditional approach used in the control group.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- teacher training in multiple intelligences based instruction and intelligence profiles before the treatment
- lesson plans planned in light of the strategy
#### Constraints
- evaluated in one school with two fifth-grade classes taught by the same teacher over 8 weeks

### Target Learners
- fifth-grade elementary students

### Learning Goals
- academic achievement in science
