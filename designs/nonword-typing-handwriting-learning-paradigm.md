# Nonword learning paradigm comparing typing and handwriting with spelling and nonword-image association tests

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study used a paradigm in which participants learned nonwords varying in transcription regularity, each paired with an image, through either typing or handwriting. Learning was tested with spelling and nonword-image association tasks. The article states that "Recorded metrics included accuracy, writing duration during learning and spelling tasks, and reaction time in the nonword-image mapping task." Eighteen Italian middle school students with developmental dyslexia and eighteen age-matched typically developing readers participated.
