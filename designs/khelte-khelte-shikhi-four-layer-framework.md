# A four-layer framework for Minecraft-based learning integrating infrastructure, curriculum, interaction, and institutional support

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes a system architecture of "four interdependent layers" that jointly integrate infrastructure, pedagogy, content, and institutional support. The Institutional & Training Layer handles "Policy alignment, teacher professional development, partnerships, open licensing"; the Interaction & Pedagogy Layer covers inclusive UI/UX, adaptive tutorials, and accessibility tools; the Content & Curriculum Layer provides culturally grounded, curriculum-mapped bilingual worlds; and the Infrastructure Layer handles tiered deployment, hardware provisioning, power, and offline-ready distribution. The authors state each layer "works independently but they are stronger when combined."
