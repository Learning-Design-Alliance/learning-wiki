# Framework for a longitudinal undergraduate medical US curriculum

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The authors propose a possible structure for a longitudinal undergraduate US curriculum, built from their survey results and a literature review. Its core moves are to "Implement US at different stages throughout the curriculum", implement compulsory courses so every student gains basic theoretical and practical skills, add electives as extra practice, and "Gain for longitudinal US course implementation to increase total hands-on scanning time (approximately 20-40 hours in total)". It is a curricular framework with milestones rather than a tested model.
