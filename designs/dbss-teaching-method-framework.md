# Defect-Based Scenario Simulation: error-embedded high-fidelity simulation for specialized skills training

## Description
DBSS is an instructional method that uses simulation technology to create highly realistic, immersive clinical environments in which procedural flaws or latent risks are deliberately embedded. The article defines it as one where "the deliberate embedding of procedural flaws or latent risks within high-fidelity clinical simulations" compels learners to identify, analyze, and resolve problems. It aligns with Kolb's experience–reflection–conceptualization–application cycle and reflective learning theory, moving trainees from passive execution to active thinking. The article notes DBSS currently lacks an independent theoretical framework.
