# Competency-based education model for postgraduate ultrasound training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes a competency-based education model for training physical therapists in ultrasound, in which 'Competency-based education is driven by the 'product' rather than the process', meaning learning outcomes are identified first and the curriculum is built in discrete steps to ensure students achieve the stated competencies. The model rests on a set of fundamental competencies common to all US use plus application-specific competencies for RUSI, diagnostic, interventional or research US, and is intended as a foundation for developing or evaluating US courses for physical therapists.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learners must demonstrate common fundamental competencies followed by application-specific competencies
- Access to postgraduate education is needed because competent US use is not part of an entry-to-practice skill set
#### Constraints
- Content is based on literature review and the authors' extensive training experience rather than a controlled evaluation

### Target Learners
- physical therapists
- practising clinicians seeking postgraduate training

### Learning Goals
- safe and competent conduct and interpretation of ultrasound examinations
