# Web-based 3D game-based simulation platform for vital signs skills

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A game-based simulation application developed with 3D animation technology for teaching blood pressure measurement, radial and apical pulse assessment, body temperature measurement, and respiratory rate counting. Built with Blender, MetaHuman, motion capture, and Unreal Engine 5 on a WordPress web side with a MySQL database, it comprised an educational video module and a game module: "The website had an educational module to watch videos created with 3D animation technology and a game module to reinforce what they had learned by playing games." Embedded multiple-choice questions paused the videos, with applause for correct answers and text explanations for incorrect ones, and responses were stored per user profile.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students accessed the game by registering with corporate email accounts using individual usernames and passwords
- Development required collaboration with a professional software company over approximately three months
#### Constraints
- The study used it as a six-hour supervised computer-laboratory intervention, not as a standalone replacement for laboratory practice

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate nursing students

### Learning Goals
- vital signs measurement skills: blood pressure, pulse, body temperature, respiratory rate
