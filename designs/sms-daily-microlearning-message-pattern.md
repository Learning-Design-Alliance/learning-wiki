# SMS microlearning pattern: one concise daily message pairing a peer-performance statistic with teaching points and curated resource links

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
This pattern delivers brief, focused educational content via one text message per day over a fixed series (13 days in this pilot). Each message "began with the mean performance on the survey of the particular teaching point in question, provided information or management recommendations, and concluded with live links to reference materials and additional reading." Messages were generated from knowledge gaps identified in a formative survey, and the platform tracked link engagement and subscription status. The authors describe the format as low friction, working on any device and easy to consume between tasks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A formative needs assessment to identify local knowledge gaps and inform message topics and sequencing
- A commercial SMS platform with privacy compliance and engagement tracking
#### Constraints
- Results may not generalize to settings without reliable SMS access or to clinicians with different baseline training

### Target Learners
- busy clinicians (residents, fellows, faculty, advanced practice providers) at an academic medical center

### Learning Goals
- foundational competencies in tobacco use disorder management, including pharmacotherapy, counseling approaches, and management in special populations
