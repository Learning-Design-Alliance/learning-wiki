# Nevada eight-step instructional materials adoption step matrix

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Table 2 lays out an eight-step adoption process: issuing a Nevada-specific Request for Quote, publisher submission of digital materials, internal technical review, CARP evaluation and rating, forwarding recommendations to Cabinet, SBE adoption, publication of the SBE-approved list, and debrief/evaluation. Each step names the entity, tasks, outcomes, and a timeframe, from a 30-day submission window through publication no later than April 30.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Publishers must submit materials in digital/online formats within the specified window; publishers of rejected materials may appeal within 30 days of notification
#### Constraints
- The process is specific to Nevada statute and the State Board of Education adoption cycle, running roughly June through June of the following year

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in Nevada public schools

### Learning Goals
- Transparent, predictable statewide adoption of standards-aligned materials
