# Ultrafast melting puzzle as a picosecond/femtosecond course topic

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A course example illustrating the timescale gap between electron and ion dynamics: an ultrashort laser pulse heats electrons within about 100 fs, but electron-to-ion energy transfer requires a few thousand collisions, so thermal melting takes at least a picosecond. Experiments from 1983 onward showed melting within a fraction of a ps at high intensities, resolved as non-thermal melting in which loss of valence electrons weakens chemical bonding and ions fly away before heating. The author notes modern molecular dynamics simulations confirm structural disorder sets in within half a picosecond.
