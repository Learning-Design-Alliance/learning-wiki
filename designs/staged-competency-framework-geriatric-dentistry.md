# A staged competency-based framework for geriatric dental education with 6 core competencies, 34 sub-competencies, and 72 enabling competencies

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review derives a three-level hierarchical competency framework mapping eight curricular themes onto six core competency domains. The article reports that "In total, 6 core competencies, 34 sub-competencies, and 72 enabling competencies, were identified." Core competencies guide program-level planning, sub-competencies inform course or module design, and enabling competencies support specific measurable learning outcomes and assessment criteria across undergraduate and postgraduate levels.
