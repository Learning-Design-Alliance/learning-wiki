# Student Learning Objectives (SLOs) as an evidence-based instructional process

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The report frames SLOs as “an evidence-based instructional process for improving educator effectiveness and student achievement,” and the predominant measure of student growth for teachers and principals within Maryland's TPE system. State models allot 30%-50% of the total evaluation rating to SLOs, with no single SLO counting for more than 35% of the total performance score. The Maryland Teacher and Principal Evaluation Guidebook, Version 3 emphasizes using SLOs as an ongoing, iterative, and collaborative process spanning data review, SLO development, conferences, and integration of results.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Districts must establish processes for setting, reviewing, assessing, and aligning SLOs, provide SLO training per state guidelines, and document a verification process to validate consistency, comparability, quality and rigor of SLOs and evaluation results
#### Constraints
- No single SLO may count for more than 35% of the total performance score
- SLO weighting in overall ratings varies by district under local control

### Learning Goals

- selecting learning content and research-based instructional strategies
- data-driven instructional improvement
