# Challenge-Device-Synthesis (CDS) methodology

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
CDS is a pedagogical methodology for developing critical views on the social impact of AI among AI engineering students, carried out by an interdisciplinary teaching team. Its "methodological body can be divided in 3 parts": challenge identification and adoption, device construction, and development of the synthesis. Students pick a challenge, conceive an AI device prototype, and produce a synthesis scientific paper that is the only evaluated piece of work, assessed on depth of analysis and how genuinely it reflects the device's social transformations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An interdisciplinary teaching team, whose background heterogeneity the article says crucially warrants diversity in approaching the social impact of AI and enriches feedback quality.
#### Constraints
- In the reported academic context the targeted alumni were heavily skilled in computer programming with little to null experience in social and human research, so the challenge list had to guarantee motivation and make the social link evident.

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate students of Artificial Intelligence (6th semester, Degree of Artificial Intelligence, UAB)

### Learning Goals
- Development of critical appraisal of the social impact and social innovation power of AI
- Social innovation competences for future AI engineers
