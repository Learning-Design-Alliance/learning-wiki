# Three-component blended structure for large introductory physics courses: asynchronous lecture, synchronous problem-solving session, and in-person laboratory

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article restructures 4-credit introductory physics classes into three weekly components: "an asynchronous lecture assigned for one hour class period associated with a simple task to complete", a two-hour problem-solving session applying e-lecture concepts to real-world applications, and a two-hour in-person laboratory connecting concepts to hands-on demonstrations. Lectures run online as one large mixed-modality section while labs meet in person in groups of 18. Course materials are organized in unit-wise modules in the LMS, and 98% of surveyed students reported navigating the page easily.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An LMS built with unit-wise modules so all learners can navigate class materials easily
- Zero-cost materials, effective feedback, and one-to-one support provided throughout the semester
#### Constraints
- The structure was implemented in blended mode after covid, with the lecture portion online as one large section and laboratories in person

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in algebra-based gateway physics courses at a four-year senior college

### Learning Goals
- foundational physics content knowledge
- problem-solving and real-world application of physics concepts
- hands-on laboratory experience
