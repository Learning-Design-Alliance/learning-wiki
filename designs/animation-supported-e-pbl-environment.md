# Animation-supported e-PBL environment with Vyond animations, ALMS, and Perculus

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An online problem-based learning environment in which thirty written scenarios from 14 body systems were converted into multimedia animations using "Vyond.com™ web applications", with sound effects from Soundbible.com and voice recordings processed in Audacity, archived as .mp4 videos. Sessions ran live in the Perculus virtual classroom with chatbox, whiteboard, and talking, while the Advancity Learning Management System (ALMS) hosted access links, learning objectives, and recorded sessions for asynchronous sharing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Unlimited Internet access for participating student groups
- Facilitators and students with technical skill in addition to pedagogical skill
#### Constraints
- Technical and infrastructural problems occurred during live e-PBL sessions
- The study group was relatively small and the research time was short

### Target Learners
- Second- and third-year medical undergraduates

### Learning Goals
- Clinical problem-solving and reasoning through PBL scenarios
- Collaborative small-group learning
