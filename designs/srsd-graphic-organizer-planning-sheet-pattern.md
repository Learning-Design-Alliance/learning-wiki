# Schema-driven story planning with a graphic-organizer planning sheet

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A lesson-level design pattern within SRSD for narrative writing: students are first trained to identify the important parts of a story (characters, time, place, trigger, events, denouement, resolution) across texts, then use that knowledge to build a schema-driven planning strategy for their own stories. "Students are taught to structure their planning sheets with a graphic organizer, which is expected to help them generate and organize their ideas during a planning phase to help them write higher-quality stories." The planning sheet offers a series of subgoals and supports retrieving ideas from long-term memory.
