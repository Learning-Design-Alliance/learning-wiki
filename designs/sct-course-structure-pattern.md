# SCT course pattern: case-based group discussion, tooth preparation, group report, question defense and teacher comments around simulated clinical scenarios

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's SCT course is a situational teaching pattern in which "teachers provide background information of typical cases and students solve problems and propose different solutions through discussion and analysis". Students query a teacher role-playing the patient, discuss feasible restoration plans in groups of 8, perform the chosen preparation or restoration on a head model, report, defend their plan under teacher questioning, and handle simulated post-treatment problems. Group work is scored across operating process (20), teeth preparation (30), oral presentation (35) and defense (15), totalling 100 points.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must have completed the theoretical dental prosthetics course and the practical course of dental defect restoration before the SCT class
- Two cases are needed: one for classroom teaching and one for before-and-after effect evaluation
#### Constraints
- The article states the realistic scenario "put forward higher requirements for the preparation of lessons and teaching supplies before class" and that "time control was the key and difficult point in the teaching process"

### Target Learners
- undergraduate dental (stomatatology) students in preclinical prosthodontics training

### Learning Goals
- clinical decision making and treatment plan selection for tooth defect restoration
- dentist-patient communication
- diagnostic reasoning and complication anticipation
