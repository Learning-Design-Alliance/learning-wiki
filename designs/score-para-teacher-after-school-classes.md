# SCORE after-school classes: para-teacher-led remedial literacy and numeracy lessons 5–6 days a week for 3 years

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The main intervention component is "a two and a half hour-long class, to be held five to six times a week before or after school hours and during vacations", run by community-resident para-teachers trained by Effective Intervention with the Ministry of Basic and Secondary Education. Classes cover the first- to third-grade curricula over three academic years, and "The primary aim of an ASC is to ensure mastery of the concepts learned in regular classes during school hours." Each para-teacher teaches a group of 20 to 30 eligible children.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Para-teachers are selected by the implementation team in consultation with community members and trained on pedagogical best practices and on facilitating English and mathematics learning during ASCs
#### Constraints
- The intervention is provided only to children from intervention villages planning to enroll in grade 1 for the first time in the 2015–2016 school year

### Target Learners
- Primary-school-age children entering grade 1 in rural Gambian villages

### Learning Goals
- Literacy (English) and numeracy mastery of grade 1–3 curriculum concepts
