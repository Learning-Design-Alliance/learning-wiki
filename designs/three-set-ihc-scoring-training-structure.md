# Three-set training course structure: demonstration set, pre-test self-assessment, and larger competence test

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The training course organised 33 expert-consensus-scored digital cases into three sets: a demonstration set of 5–10 cases used with trainer guidance, a 5-case pre-test self-assessment followed by discussion of results, and an 18-case competence test used for outcome analysis. Cases were resection samples spanning the full range of PD-L1 expression, and each meeting combined "a lecture session; a review of example PD-L1 IHC images (NSCLC); an initial pre-test self-assessment session, followed by discussion of results; and then self-assessment of a larger competence testing sample set".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cases scored by two expert pathologists with consensus reference scores, verified identical on glass slides and digital images
- Standardised laptops, screens and resolution across sessions
#### Constraints
- All 33 selected cases were resection samples; participant feedback indicated a need for a greater proportion of NSCLC biopsy cases, the sample type most frequently assessed in routine practice

### Target Learners
- board-certified pathologists in regions where PD-L1 testing is performed

### Learning Goals
- reproducible classification of PD-L1 TC expression as high or low/negative at ≥1%, ≥25% and ≥50% cut-offs
