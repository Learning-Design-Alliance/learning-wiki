# Conceptual framework mapping PICUP goals to SNA metrics and expected network structure

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article defines a framework connecting a faculty learning community's goals, the expected community structure if goals are met, and the SNA metrics used to assess them. As printed, it is "The Conceptual Framework mapping three of PICUP's goals to expectations of community structure and metrics from Social Network Analysis to assess whether the goals are being met in the Slack Community." The three goals are lowering barriers to integrating computation, continual community growth, and increased community leaders, mapped to metrics including strength, in/out-strength, weighted reciprocity, closeness centrality, and the weighted global clustering coefficient.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires directed, weighted network representations of community communication data from which the chosen SNA metrics can be computed
#### Constraints
