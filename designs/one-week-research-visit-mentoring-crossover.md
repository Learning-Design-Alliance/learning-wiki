# One-week university research visit with teaching-to-mentoring crossover

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The project follows a structured sequence: a general introduction to research at school, a one-week visit to the research group, months of follow-up work in class, and a final report and poster session. During the visit, mentoring "has the characteristics of teaching in the beginning of the week, but crosses over to mentoring of more independent nature towards the end of the week", and students may choose the project direction after initial results — in this project they chose to calculate the full potential-energy curve.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A faculty mentor working with students on campus and high school teachers working on general areas in school; prior introduction to the project and suggested reading material before the visit
#### Constraints
- The one-week time frame may pose problems for result quality, addressed here by trimmed calculation parameters and later verification by faculty

### Target Learners
- high school students

### Learning Goals
- independent research work
- understanding the essence of a specific research field
