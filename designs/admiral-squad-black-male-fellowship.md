# Admiral Squad: a Black male educator fellowship providing community, visibility, and a district hiring pipeline

## Description
Admiral Squad is Middletown City Schools' initiative "developed to increase the number of Black male educators in Middletown City Schools." It provides recognition of Black male educators' intersectional identities, a professional learning community, and development opportunities, and attends college fairs. The district, which has 32 Black men among over 650 education personnel, has a goal of hiring an additional 25 Black men across its next five hiring cycles. It also spawned "I am a King," an early-grades mentoring program.
