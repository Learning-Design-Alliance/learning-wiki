# Modified Top Notch series with conversations omitted and problem-scenarios added for cognition-based PBL instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The experimental treatment material was the Top Notch series by John Saslow and Alan Ascher, modified by the researcher "to make it compatible with cognition-based teaching approach". The article states "The main difference was omission of conversations and adding problem-scenarios instead." The unmodified series, used in the control group, focuses on communication and contains standardized and natural conversations, grammar, pronunciation, and vocabulary sections.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Problem-scenarios designed so learners cannot decode the problem from its description
- A defined tutorial process, built on Scott's (2004) model, piloted before data collection
#### Constraints
- The article states the teaching process designed for this study is in no way claimed to be perfect and should be enhanced by future researchers

### Target Learners
- intermediate adult EFL learners aged 17-21 at a language institute

### Learning Goals
- speaking proficiency across pronunciation, fluency and coherence, lexical resources, and grammatical accuracy
