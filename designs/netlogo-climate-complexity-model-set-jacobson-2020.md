# NetLogo climate and complex systems model set with student guidebooks and teacher scripts

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study's main learning materials were four weather and climate agent-based models developed in NetLogo by a participating science teacher (Carbon Cycle, Climate with Feedback, Wind and Storm, Climate with Water Feedback), plus four non-climate analog models from the NetLogo Model Library (Wolf-Sheep Predation, Ants Foraging, Birds Flocking, Forest Fire). Supporting materials included an online Student Guidebook with daily instructions, challenge problems, and answer fields, and per-condition Teacher's Guides with instructional scripts that were "virtually identical" across conditions except for which models the teacher referenced.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers received professional development on the PF approach, the content, and the NetLogo models
- Challenge problems requiring climate ideas combined with crosscutting complex systems concepts
#### Constraints
- The climate models were built by the content-expert teacher, though the far transfer advantage held in classes taught by teachers with no involvement in model design

### Target Learners
- ninth-grade science students

### Learning Goals
- climate change concepts
- complex systems principles
- transfer of structural concepts across domains
