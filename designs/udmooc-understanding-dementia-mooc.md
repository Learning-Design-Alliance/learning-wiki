# Understanding Dementia MOOC (UDMOOC)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The UDMOOC is a free, 9-week online course from the Wicking Dementia Research and Education Centre aiming to improve dementia knowledge in a broad international community. It covers "basic neurobiology, dementia pathophysiology, medical management and person-centred care", and its "design includes a conversational framework, video discussions, summaries and transcripts, discussion forums, games and quizzes". It was designed to maximise accessibility for non-traditional adult learners who may have low levels of previous education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Open enrolment with no exclusion criteria; content designed for accessibility for learners with low previous education
#### Constraints
- Completion requires participants to finish the final unit before the post-test survey to be counted in the evaluation

### Target Learners
- Non-traditional adult learners
- informal and professional carers
- general public across 130+ countries

### Learning Goals
- Understanding brain pathology associated with dementia and how it affects the person, to help carers comprehend and respond to behaviour
