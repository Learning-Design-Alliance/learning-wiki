# SkyNET-scape Room: a portable escape room introducing the messengers of the high-energy universe

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
SkyNET-scape Room is an interactive escape room developed within the PRIN 2022 SkyNET project to introduce the public to astroparticle physics. It is described as "an interactive escape room designed to introduce participants to the main messengers of the high-energy universe, namely cosmic rays, gamma rays, and neutrinos." Participants work in small teams solving puzzles and quizzes at three stations, using audio, video, and written materials, with the goal of sparking curiosity, teamwork, and excitement for science.
