# Staged exploratory simulation laboratory moving from familiarisation to theory connection to student-designed verification

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The RTR laboratory activity sequences learning into stages designed to manage cognitive load and encourage exploration: students first familiarise themselves with the interface, then connect selected theories and phenomena of special relativity to their observations, and finally apply their understanding by designing and carrying out an experiment to verify the mathematical formulation of time dilation. Throughout, students work collaboratively in pairs and keep a visual log book connecting abstract theory to visual effects, which is assessed.
