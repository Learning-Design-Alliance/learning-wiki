# Flipped-classroom interprofessional preparation: shared online modules establishing a common EBP baseline before an in-person case-based activity

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The project used a flipped classroom format in which "online learning modules were chosen as the mode of delivery for the EBP content to permit students to access the information at their own rate" and relieve logistical challenges of face-to-face instruction across two institutions. All professions completed the same modules to ensure similar EBP knowledge before a facilitated in-person small-group (approximately five students per group) case-based activity developing an evidence-supported plan of care.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Online modules must be completed before the in-person interprofessional activity
#### Constraints
- Gains measured by the AFT were not retained after the in-person session

### Target Learners
- students from multiple health professions programs across institutions

### Learning Goals
- uniform EBP knowledge baseline
- application of EBP skills to an interprofessional patient care plan
