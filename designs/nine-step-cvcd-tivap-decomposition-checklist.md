# Nine-step standardized decomposition of CVCD TIVAP implantation with a 25-item competency checklist

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A panel of 10 board-certified general surgeons distilled the TIVAP-by-CVCD procedure into nine distinct steps, from identifying surface anatomy through wound closure, and the simulator was designed to let residents practice the techniques in the correct sequence. The same panel developed a 25-item checklist in which "Twenty-one of the 25 items evaluate the technical aspect of the procedure, and 4 items evaluate the decision-making skills involved"; each item is graded pass (done correctly) or fail, and technical competency requires correctly performing all 21 technical items in the correct sequence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
