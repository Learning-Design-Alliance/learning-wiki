# Three-stage real visit sequence: Control Room, Exhibition Centre, Cavern

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Real visits follow a fixed three-stage sequence: visitors begin in the ALICE Control Room, proceed to the ALICE Exhibition Centre, and culminate with a descent into the ALICE Cavern housing the detector. The Exhibition Centre features 'informative displays, detector exhibits, and multimedia presentations that cater to both novices and those with a background in science.'

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the three areas (Control Room, Exhibition Centre, Cavern) and guided tours providing detailed explanations of the detector components and their roles in data collection.
#### Constraints
- The descent into the ALICE Cavern is only possible when the LHC schedule allows underground access.

### Target Learners
- visitors including novices and those with a background in science

### Learning Goals
- appreciating the scale and engineering of the ALICE detector
- understanding the experiment's scientific goals and principles of particle physics
