# Personal and professional development courses with a cumulative skills portfolio

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Three one-credit courses on personal and professional development were created to foster cross-cutting skills. The first focuses on social skills such as communication, kindness, organization and teamwork, plus the place of quantum science in society; the second covers internship environments and employer-expected skills such as autonomy and ethical responsibility; the third encourages students to think about future choices, develop creativity and take their place in the quantum ecosystem. Over three years, students build "a skills portfolio which includes analysis of their competencies", described as a tool for self-knowledge and job-market preparation.
