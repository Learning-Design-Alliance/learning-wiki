# XERTE-built Solar System Educational Resource with interactive multimedia activities

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An Educational Resource on the Solar System was built with XERTE, an open-source authoring tool from the University of Nottingham described as "a flexible template for creating interactive learning objects". It incorporated videos, MP3 sound effects for pictures, click-to-reveal and drag-and-drop activities, and formative exercises (matching, quiz, true/false, MCQ, closed test, classifying) with immediate feedback, guided by Kemp's Instructional Design Model and Gagne's Nine Events of Instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A teacher able to operate the IWB and navigate the resource; the resource was used in 50-minute sessions over three days
#### Constraints
- The study used it with Standard 5 pupils (9-10 years old) in two Mauritian primary schools

### Target Learners
- Standard 5 primary school pupils aged 9-10 in Mauritius

### Learning Goals
- Understanding the Solar System (Earth, Moon and planetary orbits), part of the Standard V Science syllabus
