# Five-week FS-focused task-based course with weekly topic conversations and graded production

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A unit-level instructional pattern in which each week centers on one recorded native-speaker conversation about a conversational topic. Session one builds listening comprehension identically for all learners; session two diverges, with the treatment group mining the transcript for FS and exploring usage; session three centers on production, with students expected to use learned FS while speaking. About half of course time was given to speaking practice, and explicit grammar was not taught at all.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Voluntary class meeting 30 minutes three times weekly over five weeks
- Different instructors per group following agreed-upon lesson plans
- Pretest and posttest dialogic conversations on the same topic
#### Constraints
- Attrition was high: only 19 of 40 initial students completed; two sessions were cancelled due to instructor illness, so students could attend at most eight hours of class

### Target Learners
- mid-intermediate to advanced ESL learners in an intensive university preparation program

### Learning Goals
- spoken fluency in dialogic tasks
- noticing and producing formulaic sequences
