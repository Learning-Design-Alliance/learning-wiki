# Four-hour VR-trainer MIS RYGB training curriculum with e-learning, basic MIS module, and four-step RYGB module

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
A structured simulation curriculum the study developed for medical students: a 2-h e-learning session on MIS RYGB anatomy, illustrations and videos, followed by exercises on a Simbionix LAP Mentor VR trainer covering a basic MIS skill module and a four-step MIS RYGB module, all "under the supervision of trained tutors". Performance was assessed with the BOSATS score, with proficiency defined as ≥ 105/110 points.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tutors previously trained by an experienced board-certified bariatric surgeon
- Participants must have completed a basic MIS training course covering camera guidance, PEG transfer, cutting, intracorporeal knotting, and laparoscopic cholecystectomy
#### Constraints
- The training curriculum was specifically developed for the purpose of the study and was not previously validated by other studies
- VR trainers' visual and haptic feedback remains constrained compared to animal and cadaver models, per the authors

### Target Learners
- medical students with basic MIS skills

### Learning Goals
- performing the individual steps of minimally invasive Roux-en-Y gastric bypass
