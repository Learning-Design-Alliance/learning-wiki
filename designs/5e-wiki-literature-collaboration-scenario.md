# 5E-model-based collaborative Literature scenario with three literary texts on a wiki platform

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The didactic intervention was a training scenario divided into three sections representing three different literary texts, based on the 5E Instructional Model (Bybee, 1997) founded on the constructivism-based Atkin and Karplus (1962) model. Its purpose is to encourage students to enrich their initial thinking with additional knowledge and change misconceptions. The five phases are Engagement, Exploration, Explanation, Elaboration and Evaluation, carried out cooperatively with the experimental group working at networked workstations.

## Design Implications

#### Requirements
- Cooperative teaching method with each group of students having a computer with internet connection in the wiki-supported condition
#### Constraints
- The article notes the actual research time was relatively limited, thirteen hours in total, and the subject matter concerned only three literary texts

### Target Learners
- second-grade General Lyceum students studying Modern Greek Literature

### Learning Goals
