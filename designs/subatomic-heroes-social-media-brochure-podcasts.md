# Student-run social media channels, a layman brochure, and podcast contributions used to convey that studying physics in Siegen is fun and interesting

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To attract students, the program runs Instagram and TikTok channels 'predominantly run by PhD students and post-docs (Instagram for the young generations) and by Bachelor-students (TikTok for the very young generations)'. The channels report on physics and life in academia, broadcast events, discuss physics highlights, and report from international conferences. These are complemented by the brochure 'Elementarteilchenphysik', a layman introduction to elementary particle physics and the Standard Model with an accompanying video, and podcast contributions to 'Strange & Charming' and the University of Siegen science podcast 'Spark!'.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Student and postdoc contributors to run the channels, plus a brochure, an accompanying video, and podcast production within a Collaborative Research Center
#### Constraints
- Channel staffing is differentiated by audience: Instagram for the young generations, TikTok for the very young generations

### Target Learners
- prospective students and young generations considering studying physics

### Learning Goals
- awareness of studying physics in Siegen
- layman understanding of elementary particle physics and the Standard Model
