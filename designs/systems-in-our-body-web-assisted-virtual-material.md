# Gain- and activity-based web-assisted virtual material for the 'Systems in our body' unit

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A web material developed by the researcher on constructivist principles for the 6th-grade unit 'Systems in our body', built with "Adobe Flash CS6 program" and Adobe Dreamweaver CS6. It contains interactive activities for each of the unit's 27 gains, using "semantic feature analysis, conceptual maps, structured grid, association of words, video and animations". Students can use it in class with the teacher or repeat the activities independently.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A computer-equipped environment; the material was designed so education can be carried to any environment outside the classroom that has a computer in it
#### Constraints
- Developed specifically for the 'Systems in our body' unit of the 6th grade Turkish science and technology curriculum

### Target Learners
- 6th grade primary school students

### Learning Goals
- Understanding body systems (respiration, circulation, muscular, skeletal) and the unit's 27 gains
