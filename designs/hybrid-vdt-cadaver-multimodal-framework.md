# Hybrid multimodal anatomy instruction framework combining VDTs with cadaveric dissection

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review synthesizes evidence into a framework in which virtual dissection tables serve as one modality within a multimodal anatomy curriculum that retains cadaveric dissection. The framework is grounded in the finding that "a strong consensus was observed in favor of a hybrid instructional model, combining the visual and interactive advantages of VDTs with realism and tactile experience of cadaveric dissection". It balances the spatial understanding and repeatability of VDTs against the tactile authenticity of cadaveric dissection.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Availability of both VDT technology and cadaveric dissection resources
#### Constraints
- VDTs lack tactile feedback and involve high implementation costs (up to $200,000 USD)

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate medical and healthcare students

### Learning Goals
- Anatomical knowledge, spatial understanding, and clinical reasoning
