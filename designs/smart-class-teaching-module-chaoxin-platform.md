# Smart Class teaching module built on the Chaoxin Online Teaching Information Platform for rehabilitation medical English

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Smart Class teaching module is a blended learning module mixing traditional class teaching with network-based autonomous learning, built through the Chaoxin Online Teaching Information Platform. It supported "pre-class preview", "interactive teaching in class", and "review after class", and let teachers obtain student feedback through the data platform to analyze progress, customize personalized learning plans, and adapt to individual differences. In this study it delivered a rehabilitation medicine English course to first-year physiotherapy students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires modern information technology and network media to build a teaching platform covering teaching resources, real-time classrooms, and interactive communication, as the article describes.
#### Constraints
- The article notes the Smart Class teaching module is not exactly equivalent to the online teaching module, and that it required higher self-discipline in learning for prereview.

### Target Learners
- First-year undergraduate physiotherapy students in China

### Learning Goals
- Medical English: professional terminology, reading of professional references, and clinical case analysis in professional medical English
