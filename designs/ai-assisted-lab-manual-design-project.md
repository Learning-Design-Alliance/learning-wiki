# AI-assisted lab manual design project in analytical chemistry laboratory course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A group-based project embedded in a third-year analytical chemistry laboratory course at Hong Kong Baptist University. Students selected from six broad everyday-life topics (food, cosmetic, water analysis using various instrumental techniques), used AI tools to design a complete lab manual (objectives, background, procedures, data analysis), submitted two drafts, consulted with instructors, implemented their manuals in two 3-hour sessions, and presented results. Manuals were validated by an independent professional team before release to students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to AI chatbots and institutional licensing
- Instructor consultation sessions
- Professional validation team (course instructor, project assistant, Chemical Testing Services representatives)
- Two 3-hour laboratory sessions for implementation
#### Constraints
- Each topic focused on determination of a single analyte to manage complexity
- Group size of 4-5 limited individual participation in all steps
- Course constraints limited the study to a single iteration with small sample

### Target Learners
- third-year undergraduate analytical and testing sciences majors

### Learning Goals
- experimental design
- analytical chemistry practice
- AI literacy
- problem-solving
- scientific communication
