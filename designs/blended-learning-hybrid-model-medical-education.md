## Description
The article describes blended learning as "a hybrid educational model that combines traditional in-person classroom instruction with online digital learning, integrating the best of both methods for a flexible and effective learning experience." It gained significance during the COVID-19 pandemic and has been shown to enhance student engagement, knowledge retention, and academic performance across specialties. In this study, it takes the form of live demonstrations supplemented by QR-code-linked videos.
