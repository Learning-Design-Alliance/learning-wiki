# Three-Coordination training model for residency education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Three-Coordination model is the article's organizing framework for residency training, coordinating three relationships: training bases with medical school education, theoretical learning with clinical practice, and technological empowerment with professional development. Its core component "established bidirectional mapping between theoretical knowledge and clinical application through a progressively challenging three-stage 'theory-virtual-practice' learning pathway." AI technologies (adaptive learning algorithms, NLP case generation, computer vision skill assessment, decision support) were embedded in the third coordination. The authors position it as a competency-based framework aligned with CBME principles.
