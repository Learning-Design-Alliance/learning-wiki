# Reallocate the cost savings from abridged IMNCI training to training more providers and to supervisory visits

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A cost-reinvestment principle drawn from the study's economic findings: because the abridged 7-day IMNCI course cost US $525 per participant against US $825 for the standard course, the authors argue the surplus funds "can be reallocated to train more providers or for supervisory visits for previously trained providers", which they state can further improve the quality of care and sustain the quality of skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An abridged training must be adopted in place of the standard 11-day course so that surplus funds are generated for reallocation.
#### Constraints
- The article notes that lack of long term supervision has been identified as an important obstacle to the proper implementation of IMCI, so reinvestment must include supervision to sustain skills.

### Target Learners
- Healthcare providers at first-level public and private health facilities in Pakistan, including physicians, registered nurses, midwives, health technicians and lady health visitors

### Learning Goals
- IMNCI case-management knowledge and clinical skills for assessing, classifying and treating sick children
