# Self-paced CALL grammar program with audio-visual explanation, exercises, and immediate feedback per section

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's software embodies a repeating lesson-unit design: each grammatical sub-title is divided into components comprising audio-visual explanation and examples, exercises to check understanding, and a Test Yourself section, with "An immediate feedback is received after the completion of each section." Drills support overall understanding and a comprehensive exam drawn from General Secondary Certificate examinations closes the program. Learners may move freely between components at their own pace.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content validated by TEFL and curriculum-design specialists; exercises with model answers and a correction system
#### Constraints
- Designed for one grammatical topic (the passive voice) in the first secondary curriculum

### Target Learners
- secondary EFL pupils

### Learning Goals
- English grammar structures, specifically the passive voice
