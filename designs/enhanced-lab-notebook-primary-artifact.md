# Enhanced laboratory notebook as primary scientific artifact replacing traditional lab reports

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In the transformed course, the laboratory notebook was enhanced and served as "the primary form of com- munication with the faculty," recording activities, measurements, models, and analysis. Students were coached with goals, approach, and exemplar versions, encouraged to print plots and tape them in, and did most analysis and plotting during lab. Traditional lab reports were eliminated to reduce workload, emphasize authentic scientific practice, and focus faculty interactions on formative feedback. The goal was notebooks resembling those of practicing scientists.
