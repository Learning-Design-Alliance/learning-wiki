# KUH structured training pathway for introducing surgeons to robotic liver surgery

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A sequenced training pathway used at Karolinska University Hospital to introduce surgeons to robotic HPB surgery: "The initially introduced KUH surgeons took at least 30 h of simulator training, followed by the Surgeon-Led Procedure Training Series (TR 300, Intuitive Surgical) at the Orsi Academy (Melle, Belgium)." This was followed by case observations at UMCU and multiple rounds of on-site proctoring at KUH. Laparoscopic liver surgery experience of these surgeons was comparable to that of the UMCU surgeons.
