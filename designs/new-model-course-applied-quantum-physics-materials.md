# New Model Course in Applied Quantum Physics curriculum materials

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A set of research-based curriculum materials for an upper-division modern physics/quantum mechanics course for engineers, developed through a cyclical process of research, curriculum development, and evaluation. The materials include small group learning environment worksheets inspired by the University of Washington Tutorials in Introductory Physics, associated conceptual homework problems, applied homework problems, daily essay assignments used in the mode of Just-in-Time-Teaching, and conceptual examination questions. The conductivity unit comprises three Tutorials, three conceptual homework assignments, one applied homework assignment, several essay questions, and an examination question.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tutorials taught in place of one lecture per week; essay assignments connecting classroom physics to the real world and emphasizing the different models used in quantum physics
#### Constraints
- These materials form only a small part of an entire course developed by the authors

### Target Learners
- upper-division engineering students, mostly electrical engineers (~85%)

### Learning Goals
- understanding and applying models of conductivity and other applied quantum physics topics
