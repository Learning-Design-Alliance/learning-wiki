## Description
The Reciprocal Teaching Procedure (RTP), designed by Palincsar and Brown (1984), is an explicit instructional strategy in which teacher and students take turns leading a dialogue about a text while applying four strategies: "generating one's own questions, summarizing parts of the text, clarifying word meanings and confusing text passages, and predicting what might come next in the text". Instruction is scaffolded: the teacher first models the strategies explicitly, then gradually fades involvement as students take turns as dialogue leaders, shifting responsibility to learners. The article applies RTP as the treatment for improving EFL reading comprehension.
