# Teaching and Learning Physics course (cross-listed physics/teacher-education course with community fieldwork)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A 10-week upper-division course composed of "a study of physics content, readings about the teaching and learning of physics, and practical experience teaching physics to less educated students," each roughly one third of the course. It was adopted as an upper-division restricted elective by the physics department, offered in the teacher certification program, and was the first course cross-listed between the physics department and the Teacher Education Program. Students taught at four school and community sites and wrote weekly field-notes.
