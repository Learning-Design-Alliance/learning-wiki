# ACP-SCT program: a three-axis simulation-based communication training program for nurses

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The ACP-SCT program is a three-week, twelve-hour training program for hemodialysis nurses built on three axes: professional knowledge of ACP legislation and advance directives, communication skills practiced through scenario simulation and role play guided by the PREPARED model, and debriefing with weekly reflective journals. The article states "The ACP-SCT program had three main axes", with expert validation yielding an average CVI of 0.89. Sessions ran once weekly for four hours, with role-play turns of approximately 25 to 30 minutes per participant.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered by a facilitator specializing in palliative care with an ACP counselor role, with standardized patients for role-play scenarios
#### Constraints
- Piloted only with nurses due to time, personnel, and resource constraints; the article recommends extending future versions to a variety of healthcare professionals

### Target Learners
- Hemodialysis and nephrology nurses

### Learning Goals
- Knowledge of advance care planning and advance directives
- Communication skills for ACP and end-of-life discussions
