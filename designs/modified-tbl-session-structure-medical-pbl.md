# Modified TBL session structure with pre-reading, IRAT/TRAT readiness assurance, immediate feedback and team problem-solving

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a modified TBL format replacing two PBL iterations in a Year 1 cardiovascular block: compulsory pre-reading, a 20-minute Readiness Assurance process comprising a 10-minute Individual Readiness Assurance Test followed by a 10-minute Team Readiness Assurance Test of 10 single-best-answer MCQs, 20 minutes of immediate feedback with facilitator clarification, and 45 minutes of team problem-solving with five clinical tasks. Peer evaluation was excluded from this modified format. Two PBL groups of ten were combined into a TBL class of twenty split into four teams of five.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Compulsory pre-reading completed before class
- An MCQ test instrument for the readiness assurance process
- A facilitator to release answers and offer clarification
#### Constraints
- Peer evaluation was not part of the modified format
- Designed and customised to run in a single institution

### Target Learners
- first-year graduate-entry medical students

### Learning Goals
- basic science concept application to clinical problems
- team problem solving
- preparation for class
