# KUHT-TV educational television station of the University of Houston and its televised psychology and biology course sections

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
KUHT-TV is the University of Houston's educational TV station, evaluated for instructional effectiveness and audience reaction. The study enrolled "17 subjects were enrolled in a TV-lecture only (correspondence) section, 96 in a traditional campus lecture section, 30 in a section which had TV-lectures plus two campus discussion periods a week" in psychology, and matched traditional versus TV-plus-discussion sections in biology. An audience telephone questionnaire to 384 people measured viewing behavior and program preferences.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Television lecture delivery, with campus discussion periods added in one psychology section and in the biology TV section
#### Constraints
- The traditional psychology class completed the course before the two TV classes began to prevent contamination; there was no preliminary matching or tests for significant basic differences in the psychology course

### Target Learners
- college students in elementary psychology and elementary biology courses
- Houston-wide television audience

### Learning Goals
- college-level elementary psychology and elementary biology achievement
