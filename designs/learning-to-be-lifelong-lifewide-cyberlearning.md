# Learning to Be and Learning to Live: lifelong, lifewide learning as the modus operandi of living

## Description
The article's organizing vision, drawing on UNESCO's 1972 'Learning to Be' treatise, frames learning as spanning the entire spectrum of life rather than age-bounded stages. It defines 'Learning to Live' as 'referring to both learning how to thrive in a new and evolving range of contexts and circumstances, as well as providing the modus operandi for living as learning.' Blended formal and informal environments, passion-based pursuits, mentoring in both directions, and the dissolution of retirement and life-stage boundaries underpin this framework, which the Holodeck is meant to serve.
