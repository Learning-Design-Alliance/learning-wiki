# University of Pisa summer course that accredits the Fermilab internship with 6 ECTS

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Since 2015 the internship is embedded in an ad-hoc summer course of the University of Pisa that gives the training an academic framework. Interns are enrolled for the 9-week duration of the internship, hold a regular position as local students with identification and insurance coverage, and "Upon successful completion of the internship with an accurate final report and an oral interview, they are acknowledged 6 ECTS in their Diploma Supplement." This accreditation formalizes recognition of the informal laboratory training within the students' degree records.
