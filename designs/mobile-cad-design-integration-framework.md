# Conceptual framework integrating mobile information access and CAD ideation to produce creative design products

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study's framework combines Carroll's minimal theory (1995) for the mobile website, Brown and Campione's learning-with-technology theory (1996) for technology integration, and the Creative Product Analysis Matrix (CPAM) of Besemer and Treffinger (1981) for evaluating creativity. It posits two independent variables — use of web mobile for obtaining design information and use of CAD technology in creating new ideas — influencing one outcome, the creative product. CAD integration occurs at the synthesis stage, the simulation stage and before producing the final creative product, with stage products compared across the process.
