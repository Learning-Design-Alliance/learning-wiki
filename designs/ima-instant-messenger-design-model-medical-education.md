# Proposed instant messenger learning design model combining the three WhatsApp strategies with the exploration-enactment-assessment framework

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors propose an instructional design model for instant messenger application learning in medical education, intended to fill the theoretical vacuum they identified in the reviewed studies. It is "drawing from Dabbagh and Bannan-Ritland's exploration-enactment-evaluation learning design framework for online education [50], in turn informed by socio-cultural and constructivist theories (Fig. 2)". The model combines the three usage strategies and is offered as a resource for educators planning IMA use, standing distinct from generic technology-enhanced learning models.
