# Optics/Lasers seven-week final project structure

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In the Optics and Lasers courses at Bethel University, the final 7 weeks of each 14-week course are dedicated to group final projects. Students rank a portfolio of possible projects, instructors assign groups of 2 to 4 students by preference, there are no lab manuals, and projects run in departmental research labs with groups coordinating their own time management and division of labor. Projects culminate in oral presentations and journal-style reports, and the syllabus emphasizes student autonomy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- a portfolio of possible projects ranked by student interest
- a second instructor joining to help mentor student groups
- access to relevant journal articles and prior groups' documentation
#### Constraints
- projects do not take place in a dedicated classroom or during regularly scheduled times of day
- topics are informed by the instructors' research interests and available equipment

### Target Learners
- third- and fourth-year physics and engineering majors in an upper-division optics lab

### Learning Goals
- experimental design competence
- student ownership, engagement, and fire
- technical skills with equipment, apparatus design, and data analysis
