# Constructivist hands-on curriculum combining simple-machine lessons with skill-mapped worksheets, homework and projects

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article defines a curriculum for Grade 1-3 consisting of lessons, worksheets, projects and homework in which students learn levers, wheels and axle, pulley, inclined plane, wedges, screws, gears, cam and ratchet. The learning environment is "primarily based on the constructivist theory of teaching and learning", involving "learning by doing, building structures and artefacts using concrete objects". A topic-to-skill mapping table (Table I) links each topic's worksheets and homework to targeted technical and non-technical skills.
