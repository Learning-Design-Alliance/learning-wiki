# Framework for interprofessional case conferences combining conversation-phase structure with ICF-based action tasks

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article develops a framework for interprofessional case conferences from literature analysis, empirical conversation research, and the ICF. It distinguishes verbal tasks present in every conversation (organising, formulating, coordinating, maintaining relationships, constituting modality, generating reciprocity) from extra-linguistic tasks specific to the case conference type. Conversations consist of "opening, core, and termination phases, which must be actively established by the parties involved". The framework serves as orientation for teaching content and for describing and evaluating communication processes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants must process both the verbal micro-level tasks and the extra-linguistic case-based tasks for the conversation to count as a case conference
#### Constraints
- Developed for the conversation type of the interprofessional case conference in health professions education

### Target Learners
- health professions students in nursing, physiotherapy, occupational therapy, speech therapy and midwifery
