# Blended text-processing intervention pattern: knowledge building, inference practice, summarization with oral feedback, and cross-text integration scaffolded by listening comprehension

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The multicomponent intervention pattern tested in both studies. Study 1 components were "(a) accessing and building background knowledge through explicit instruction and repeated exposure to the target words"; (b) explicit inference-formation practice integrating within-text and text-to-knowledge information; (c) summarization in which students "identify key words and important details in text" and share concise summaries orally with targeted tutor feedback; and (d) answering overarching questions integrating information across a unit's texts. All texts were read aloud and discussed, making students' retrieval and integration processes transparent to the tutor, who redirected incomplete summaries back to the text.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All texts are read orally to students and tutors engage students in text-based discussions with targeted corrective feedback on oral summaries.
#### Constraints
- Both studies were multicomponent, so the relative impact of individual components could not be determined.
