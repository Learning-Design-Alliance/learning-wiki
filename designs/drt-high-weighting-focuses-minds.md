# Weight the DRT highly (70% of course marks) to focus student attention on laboratory notebook quality

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article reports that the DRT's significance for course marks is what motivates students to keep good records: because "it represented a signiﬁcant contribution to their course marks, they were highly motivated to make sure that they kept a good record". The weighting is deliberately kept at 70% to focus students' minds on the importance of laboratory notebooks, even though students wish it were lower.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The DRT must be announced in advance so students know it is coming at the end of term
#### Constraints
- Students report the weighting feels disproportionate; the author acknowledges this objection

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate physics students

### Learning Goals
- motivating sustained good laboratory record-keeping across the term
