# Experiential learning-based Mathematics teaching material for fifth-grade volume of cube and beam

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A student-centered Mathematics teaching material developed on Kolb's four-stage experiential cycle, evaluated in this study against conventional material consisting only of content and questions. The conventional material the schools used was "still abstract, not accompanied by the visualization of the material". The experiential material instead starts from concrete experience with picture illustrations that "can clarify the material content, increase the attractiveness, and reduce the students' boredom", then moves through reflection, conceptualization, and application. It was tested with a 30-question multiple-choice instrument (reliability 0.730 via ITEMAN).
