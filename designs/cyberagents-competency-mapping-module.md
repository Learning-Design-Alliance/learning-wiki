# Competency-mapping module organizing curriculum topics by difficulty, prerequisites, and concept clusters

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The competency-mapping module extracts structured competencies from course materials using a cybersecurity-specific competency schema. The extractor "identifies key entities such as tools, techniques, concepts, attacks, vulnerabilities, and defenses; assigns difficulty levels from Beginner to Expert; and maps instructional relations relevant to gameplay design, including prerequisites, concept clusters, and learning enablers." The resulting competency graph serves as the initialization blueprint for challenge generation, keeping gameplay curriculum-aligned from the outset.
