# Six-module astronomy course structure grounded in project pedagogy and active methodologies

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The course was divided into six modules — Tópicos de Astronomia, Observações do Céu, Práticas astronômicas (building a simple telescope), História Oral, Tópicos em Matemática and Metodologias Ativas — 'a partir de uma proposta fundamentada na pedagogia de projetos e nas metodologias ativas', all structured from the guiding question of what one sees when looking at the sky. Each module pairs astronomy content with a practice: contemplative night observation, instrument building, oral-history cultural rescue, geometric transcription, and project methodology applied to astronomy teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Organized around a guiding question: Você já olhou para o céu hoje? O que você vê quando olha para céu?
#### Constraints
- Half of the 180-hour load is in person with fortnightly meetings, and the observation module depends on night conditions, with participants noting an absence of activities for bad nights.

### Target Learners
- Basic-education professionals, teachers and education technicians in municipal networks

### Learning Goals
- Updated astronomical knowledge to reduce conceptual errors
- Connection with nature and recognition of star groupings
- Application of project methodology to astronomy teaching
