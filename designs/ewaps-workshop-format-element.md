# EWAPS workshop format as a model learner-centred CME event

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The European Workshop of Advanced Plastic Surgery (EWAPS), established in 1986, functions as a self-organised community of practice of plastic surgeons. Its format combines closed invitation-based membership, three-minute presentations with twelve-minute discussions, problem-based thematic axes (Cry for help, My original technique, My worst experience, Ideas and Innovations, Follow up), hands-on skills workshops with live models, and social activities including participants' families.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Invitation-based, restricted membership with defined participation criteria
- A scientific programme counterbalanced by social activities involving all participants and families
#### Constraints
- The element is described from one case; the study's findings from it are limited in scope and self-reported

### Target Learners
- Plastic surgeons and specialists in continuing professional development

### Learning Goals
- Sharing clinical knowledge and experience, solving clinical problems, and acquiring new techniques through peer interaction
