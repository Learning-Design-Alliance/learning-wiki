# Four-stage iterative CPE notebook template scaffolding the transition from homework problem to computational modeling

## Description
The CPE assignment provides students a structured Jupyter Notebook template guiding them through a four-stage iterative process: Problem Statement, First Iteration/Solution, Second Iteration/Solution (critique and refinement), and Conclusion synthesizing findings into a scientific argument. The article states the template scaffolded "the transition from “homework problem” to “computational modeling”" and that the second iteration required students to "critique or improve upon their first model and imple- ment refinement". This iterative structure supports constructionist, project-based learning in which students test hypotheses on the notebook canvas.
