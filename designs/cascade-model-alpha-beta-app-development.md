# Cascade-model development with alpha and beta testing for educational apps

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article developed the study guide using the cascade model, whose stages are "initial analysis, system analysis, design, programming, testing (both alpha and beta), implementation, and modification." Alpha testing gathered ratings from 12 healthcare experts and 5 IT specialists on content, ease of use, and practicality; beta testing gathered usability, convenience, and simplicity ratings from 17 nursing students. The program was deemed suitable by both professionals and learners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Expert alpha testers (clinical and IT) and end-user beta testers before implementation.
#### Constraints
- The reported alpha and beta evaluations were opinion ratings on 5-point items, not learning outcomes.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students (end users)

### Learning Goals
- development of usable, clinically accurate educational applications
