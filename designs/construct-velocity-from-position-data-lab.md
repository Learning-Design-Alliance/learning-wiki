# A lab design in which students construct their own velocity values from position data surfaces the consequences of definitional choices

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
This lesson-level pattern has students collect position data with a motion sensor, export it to a spreadsheet, and compute velocity themselves from the definition of average velocity rather than using instrument-generated values. Because multiple reasonable-looking implementations assign the interval velocity to different points in time, the class splits into groups whose graphs support opposite answers to the energy-loss question. The article states this creates "a rich lab experience ... that results in good student discussions of proper analysis of data, and implementation of models."
