# Discussion group followed by summative OSCE and structured multidisciplinary debriefing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A reusable training sequence in which a formative group discussion is followed by a summative OSCE station and then a four-step debriefing: clarification of objectives with the grading grid, dynamic analysis of actions and emotional states with a psychologist, systematic response to students' free questions, and return of the guide including the S-P-w-ICE-S protocol. "All four steps were designed to improve the students' relational skills." The authors state that in practice "the discussion group would be followed by a formative OSCE and then an active debriefing of the group with the teachers."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An OSCE station validated by the university board, standardized patients, and blinded qualified-physician evaluators
#### Constraints
- The physician-patient relationship "cannot be defined or constrained by strict criteria and guidelines", so the OSCE was chosen as an objective quantitative proxy

### Target Learners
- fifth-year medical students

### Learning Goals
- breaking bad news
- communication skills
- relational skills
