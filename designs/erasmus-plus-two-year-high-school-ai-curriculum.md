# Erasmus+ two-year high school AI curriculum combining AI with Python programming

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Bellas et al. present a European Erasmus+ effort defining an AI curriculum for high school students and teachers with no previous AI knowledge. The commentary describes a 2-year sequence organized around an expanded version of the Five Big Ideas with 8 key ideas, using teaching units implemented as needed and mobile learning to keep costs manageable. It highlights attention to math prerequisites and integration so learners "learn Python and AI at the same time." Initial evaluation via teacher feedback and student technical questionnaires was encouraging.
