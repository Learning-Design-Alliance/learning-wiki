# Physics of Contemporary Challenges sophomore course using real-world topics to attract and retain majors

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
One of two new sophomore-level courses, Physics of Contemporary Challenges prepares students to apply physics concepts and reasoning to sustainable energy, climate change, space exploration, and fundamental physics puzzles. Contemporary challenges determine the narrative flow while physics concepts and reasoning skills are the main substance; mathematically rigorous derivations are only briefly mentioned. By prioritizing the most engaging challenges, the course aims to attract and retain as many potential physics majors as possible.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Taught in winter and spring quarters to reduce transfer-student burden in Fall
- Constructed to teach no content required for Fall or Winter junior-year courses
#### Constraints
- Teaches both juniors and sophomores together

### Target Learners
- sophomore and junior physics majors
- potential physics majors

### Learning Goals
- estimation
- dimensional reasoning
- interpretation of integrals
- physics reasoning on complex problems
