# Transforming End of Life Care (TEoLC): a free 2-day multiprofessional course for non-specialist palliative care providers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The TEoLC course is a free, 2-day course run by an inner-city tertiary hospital since 2012 for "multiprofessional, non-specialist palliative care staff working in local hospitals and the community". It covers last days of life, the AMBER care bundle, Fast Track discharge, symptom management, advance care planning, difficult conversations, spiritual and cultural aspects, and family support. Teaching uses lectures, case-based group work, interactive workshops and Q&A sessions, taught by palliative care nurses, consultants, social workers and a chaplain.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants are non-specialist palliative care staff who choose to attend themselves
#### Constraints
- Content is dynamic, responding to participants' needs, and was developed from national and local policy imperatives and staff's informally expressed needs

### Target Learners
- Non-specialist hospital and community healthcare staff, including qualified nurses, senior nurses and general practitioners

### Learning Goals
- Confidence, competence and knowledge in end-of-life care, including communication and practical procedures such as Fast Track discharge
