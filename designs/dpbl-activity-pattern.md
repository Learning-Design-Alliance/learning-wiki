# D-PBL weekly activity pattern: small-group PBL with branching case decisions and facilitated debate

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
This pattern organizes a PBL module so each week's case is a branching virtual patient worked by a small group of 6-8 learners. The article specifies that D-PBL "involved small-group (6 to 8) learners, face-to-face sessions at the start and end of the week, independent research and study between face-to-face sessions, scaffolding by a case with triggers based around problems, and 1 case worked on per week." Within face-to-face sessions, the group debates alternative paths at decision points, takes a majority-view choice, and deals with the unfolding consequences. The pattern was applied across a 5-week module with cohorts alternating D-PBL and linear cases weekly.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Predefined learning objectives that the branching options map onto, reviewed by the module faculty committee
- A Web-based delivery system supporting dynamic branching and central path logging
#### Constraints
- Designed to differ from linear PBL only in the dynamic case medium and decision debates, to minimize impact on the wider curriculum

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students in small-group PBL tutorials

### Learning Goals
- patient management decision making
- debate and reasoning about clinical consequences
