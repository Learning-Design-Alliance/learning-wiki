# Guided-inquiry tutorial pattern built on documented difficulties with hypothetical-student dialogues and scaffolded superposition scenarios

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The tutorial is a guided-inquiry sequence built directly from research-identified student difficulties. It presents scenarios (two charged spheres, a cylinder plus point charge, parallel sheets), asks students to apply Gauss's law results separately and add fields vectorially, and embeds dialogues: "The tutorial includes multiple conversations between hypothetical students in which one student voices a common difficulty and the other provides a correct explanation". Students evaluate these conversations and apply the ideas across multiple contexts to solidify the concepts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Used in small groups with the instructor or teaching assistant moving around to ensure students engage productively with the guided inquiry-based sequences.
#### Constraints
- Assumes all non-conductors (insulators) are non-polarizable, as stated at the top of the tutorial.

### Target Learners
- calculus-based introductory physics students (mostly first-year engineering, chemistry, mathematics and physics majors)

### Learning Goals
- correctly combining Gauss's law results with vector superposition to find net electric fields
