# A group work-based PBL protocol with checkpoints, e-portfolios, and a final debate across a 13-week semester

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The protocol is a group work-based, learner-centred, problem-oriented teaching design implemented over a single 13-week semester. Course content is divided into modules presented as checkpoints; students form groups of six, analyse assigned real-world problem scenarios, generate hypotheses, pursue self-directed study, and present at each checkpoint, with all work recorded in e-portfolios. The final presentation is followed by an open forum or debate to consolidate learning. Assessment is holistic and performance-based, using e-portfolio rubrics covering content relevance, knowledge integration, and clarity and accuracy of concepts and theories.
