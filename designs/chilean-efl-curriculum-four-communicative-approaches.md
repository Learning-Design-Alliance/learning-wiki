# Chilean national English curriculum combines the Communicative Approach with Natural Approach, Cooperative Learning, Content-Based Instruction and Task-Based Language Teaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review describes the Chilean National Curriculum's English programs as anchored in the Communicative Approach and supplemented by four further approaches promoting communication. It states: "Themain contributions come from: a) Natural Approach; b) CooperativeLearning; c) Content-Based Instruction and d) Task-Based Language teaching". The curriculum emphasizes development of critical and creative thinking, teaching of learning strategies, communicative tasks, connections, culture, the writing process, and ICT and multimodal texts (MINEDUC, 2015), with class activities involving pair work, role play, problem solving and collaborative group projects.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Class objectives address all language components (vocabulary, topics, grammar, strategies, functions) across the four skills of listening, reading, writing, and speaking
#### Constraints
- The pattern is prescribed by the Chilean Ministry of Education curriculum, mandatory from fifth grade (students 10 years old)

### Target Learners
- Chilean students aged 10–18 in mandatory English schooling, plus tertiary learners

### Learning Goals
- Communicative competence: using English to interact and communicate relevant messages in contextualized, meaningful ways
