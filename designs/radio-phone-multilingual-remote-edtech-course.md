# Low-tech radio-and-phone remote EdTech course with multilingual delivery options

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A remote interactive STEM course in Lira District, Uganda, delivered to 2,931 learners via basic keypad phones and radios, using Robocall and SMS alerts, radio lessons, and USSD menus or live call-ins for responses. The course taught learners to build a solar-powered food dehydrator through a 9-step Engineering Design Process, with instruction and assessments in the learner's chosen language and logged phone-based interactions enabling engagement analytics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivery via widely available low-tech tools: basic keypad phones and radios
- A dedicated local radio broadcast with scheduled language-specific lesson times
#### Constraints
- Designed for contexts where access to internet, smartphones, computers, and academic materials is limited

### Target Learners
- Rural learners in Northern Uganda, including primary and secondary school-aged participants

### Learning Goals
- Practical STEM and engineering design skills, culminating in building a solar-powered food dehydrator
