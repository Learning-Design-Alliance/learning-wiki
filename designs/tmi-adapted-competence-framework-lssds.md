# Adaptation of Howard Gardner's Theory of Multiple Intelligences as an eight-competence framework driving LSSDS selection and teaching philosophy

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The organizing committee adopted an adaptation, shown in Fig. 1, of the main aspects in Howard Gardner's Theory of Multiple Intelligences, described as "an adaptation of the eight basic competences adopted by the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development heavily inspired by TMI". Although it emerged from a different context than Data Science, the committee finds these competences vital for defining student selection criteria and for their teaching philosophy and curriculum. Some competences consciously and unconsciously drove selection of LSSDS students.
