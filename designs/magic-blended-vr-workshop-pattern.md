# MAGIC blended workshop: didactic teaching, role-play, VR simulation, and structured debrief

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
MAGIC is a three-hour blended learning workshop combining four sequential components: a didactic ECARE lecture on agitation management, tutor-led role-play of communication and restraint techniques, the VRAM VR simulation, and a tutor-facilitated debrief using the RC22 model. The article describes it as integrating "didactic teaching, role-play, and the Virtual Reality in Agitation Management (VRAM) activity". It was a mandatory psychiatry-curriculum component standardised across medical and nursing tracks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tutor facilitation for role-play and debrief; the RC22 debrief model with stages of reaction, recollection, reflection, analysis, and application
#### Constraints
- Delivered as a mandatory curriculum component; the study could not establish a control group for this reason

### Target Learners
- medical students
- nursing students

### Learning Goals
- confidence in agitation management
- mental health literacy
- reduced stigma
- empathetic care
