# Graded-severity case set: three pelvic trauma cases spanning mild fracture to severe hemorrhagic shock, each requiring different diagnostic and treatment strategies

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The curriculum's core instructional pattern is a set of three initial cases ordered by trauma severity: "We developed 3 initial cases, each representing a different stage of trauma and treatment approach (Table 1)." Case 1 is a pelvic fracture treated with surgery; Case 2 adds mild hemorrhagic shock treated with antihemorrhagic shock therapy plus surgery; Case 3 adds severe hemorrhagic shock requiring CT of pelvis and abdomen. Learning paths follow a flowchart of correct and wrong diagnostic-treatment routes based on clinical treatment guidelines, serving as a framework for evaluating student performance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cases designed by basic and clinical medicine specialists drawing on a literature review, real patient cases, and the course syllabus, with learning paths based on clinical treatment guidelines
#### Constraints
- Cases were developed for severe pelvic trauma in orthopedic surgery education; the article does not claim generalizability beyond this domain

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate and graduate medical students

### Learning Goals
- Progressive mastery of severe pelvic trauma diagnosis and treatment across increasing case severity
