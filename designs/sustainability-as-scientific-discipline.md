## Description
The article frames sustainability not merely as a goal but as "una verdadera disciplina con objetos de estudio y de investigación". Its fundamental topics include migration from non-renewable to renewable energy sources, dynamics of complex systems, emergent behavior, multi-scale processes, and vulnerability and adaptive capacity between systems. A key research aim is understanding how local and regional patterns and processes are influenced by, and feed back on, global-scale processes over longer periods. This framing justifies teaching atmospheric physics as a first step for engineers toward sustainability.
