# Kuhn's seven studio activities organised within the Double Diamond framework

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article organises the architectural design studio around seven fundamental activities from Kuhn (2001): open-ended semester projects, rapid design iterations, formal and informal critiques, consideration of heterogeneous issues, study of precedents with global-detailed oscillation, faculty-guided constraint setting, and use of diverse design media. These are positioned within the Double Diamond, a design process model whose "iterative nature provides clear visual structure that organizes Kuhn’s seven studio activities into a cycle of divergent and convergent" phases of Discover, Define, Develop, and Deliver.
