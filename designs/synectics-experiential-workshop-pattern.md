# Synectics-based one-day experiential workshop for team creative skills

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A one-day workshop pattern using the Synectics creative problem-solving approach: an ice breaker to create a collaborative climate, explanation of Synectics principles, split-group experiential learning exercises, and team practice with creative techniques such as "brainstorming, excursions and metaphors". Experiential learning and reflective practice are used for creative problem-solving, with opportunities for teams to practice newly acquired skills during the workshop itself.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A supportive classroom environment created to inspire creativity, with group roles assigned and guidelines for creative behavioural processes
#### Constraints
- Workshops accommodated a maximum of 20 participants per session

### Target Learners
- higher education students in leadership, entrepreneurship and project-based courses

### Learning Goals
- team creative skills
- novel and adaptive thinking
