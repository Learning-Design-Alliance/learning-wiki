# Tiered training system progressing from admission assessment through graded practice to supervised independent operation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The program's tiered training pattern comprises "a three-level model comprising admission assessment, graded practice, and supervised operation—that ensures skills meet standards before clinical practice". Trainees must pass three simulation assessments (≥90% success, under 5 min, ≤1 complication) before clinical work, then serve as first assistant for 5 supervised intubations, then as main operator after scoring at least 80 on the Operation Score Sheet, with the first three cases supervised by a senior physician.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- High-fidelity simulators (Laerdal SimMan) for monthly scenario drills
- Faculty supervision providing real-time feedback at each stage
- Structured assessment tools such as the Operation Score Sheet
#### Constraints
- The authors note differences exist between simulation and actual clinical practice, which could limit skill transfer

### Target Learners
- standardised training residents in emergency medicine

### Learning Goals
- procedural skill proficiency in endotracheal intubation
- decision-making under pressure
- patient safety during supervised practice
