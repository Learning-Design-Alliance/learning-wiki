# Generative learning model with its staged syntax (orientation, cognitive conflict, disclosure, construct, application, reflection-evaluation)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes the generative learning model as one that actively integrates new material into students' existing knowledge, citing Maknun (2015) that it "memu satkan terhadap integrasi aktif pengetahuan dari pengetahuan awal dan isi pembelajaran". Its syntax proceeds through orientation (activating prior knowledge and motivation), cognitive conflict (challenging questions that expose misconceptions), disclosure (constructing concepts toward scientific conceptions), construct (rebuilding situations and solving ambiguous problems, including experiments), application (problem solving with feedback), and reflection-evaluation (feedback to support learning outcomes). The article maps each stage to a diagnosed learning problem in the introduction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A teacher who prepares students, provides motivation, poses challenging probing questions, guides concept construction, and supplies feedback at each syntax stage
#### Constraints
- The article applies and tests the model only on Class XI motion dynamics (kinetic mechanics, TP 11.4) material at SMAN 1 Painan

### Target Learners
- Class XI (grade 11) senior high school physics students

### Learning Goals
- Physics conceptual understanding of motion dynamics
- Knowledge and skill competencies in physics
