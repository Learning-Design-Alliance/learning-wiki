# Integrated pedagogical proposal for an electromagnetism IIC combining six active strategies with formative assessment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article designed a training proposal for a three-week, 45-hour intensive electromagnetism course, built from a teacher focus group and literature review. It states the proposal "included some of the active methodological strategies described in sections I B and I C, among which the following were selected: interactive lectures, direct teaching, collaborative work, problem-based learning, use of online simulation tools, and formative assessment." The EG followed this proposal while the CG received non-innovated lessons, and an activity log for the 2024 summer course appears in the appendix.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers with previous experience teaching the subject in a regular semester, able to regulate pace from less to more difficult content
#### Constraints
- Designed for a three-week remedial IIC with exclusive student dedication and small cohorts of about 20 students

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate STEM students re-enrolling in a failed high-failure-rate course in an intensive format

### Learning Goals
- Conceptual understanding of electromagnetism (electrostatics, direct current circuits, magnetostatics, electromagnetic induction)
