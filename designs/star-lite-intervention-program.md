# STAR Lite advising intervention program

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
STAR Lite is a one-semester advising intervention for undergraduate students on academic probation, grounded in a strength-based mindset and tailored to individual student needs. Participation requires "meeting with an assigned STAR Lite advisor at least three times during the academic semester." The program uses a cohort model allowing students to work together and share experiences, building peer-to-peer community support guided by the Neighborhood Student Success Model. Students with GPA under 2.0 who are Exploratory Preference (undeclared) majors are invited to participate.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students meet with an assigned advisor at least three times during the semester
- Participation limited to one semester immediately after probation placement
#### Constraints
- Served only Exploratory Preference (undeclared) majors at one Midwestern research university
- Pre-COVID data with all services provided in person

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate students on academic probation with undeclared majors

### Learning Goals
- Return to good academic standing
- Persistence and graduation
