# AGREE II online training interventions: virtual-coach multimedia tutorial and tutorial plus interactive practice exercise with two-stage formative feedback

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Two internet-based training platforms for learning to appraise practice guidelines with the AGREE II. The Tutorial is a seven-minute multimedia presentation with a "virtual coach" followed by the PDF User's Manual. The Tutorial + Practice Exercise adds appraisal of a practice PG with "immediate feedback on how their score compared to the mean of four experts" and two-stage formative feedback when scores fell outside a predefined range, plus a performance summary. The control was the 56-page AGREE II User's Manual PDF. Both interventions are available through the AGREE Enterprise website.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Password-protected web-based delivery platform; participants require little-to-no prior AGREE or AGREE II experience per the study's eligibility criteria
#### Constraints
- Specific reliability and validity testing of the Learner's scale items and subscales was not undertaken; the study population had substantial prior health-methods experience

### Target Learners
- Methodologists, clinicians, policy makers, and trainees involved with practice guidelines

### Learning Goals
- Appraising practice guidelines using the AGREE II's 23 items and six quality domains
