# Six-module academic integrity sequence combining values definitions, scenarios, multimodal tasks, quizzes and an integrative final test

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each of the six AI modules follows a common instructional sequence: definitions of values with interconnection scenarios, scenarios depicting diverse student life, multimodal tasks, module quizzes, and a final integration test. The article lists "Multimodal tasks such as drag and drop, ordering of responses, videos and games" and a final test awarding badges and an e-certificate. This sequence operationalizes East's (2016) recommendations that AI modules be engaging, image-rich, game-based and practice-oriented.
