# Team Members Teaching Design: a team-teaching approach in which each member studies a different part of the subject and every learner also teaches

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
TMTD is one of the team and cooperative teaching methods, resting on two hypotheses: "each member of the team studies a different part of the subject" and "each learner can teach her team members, so each member acts as both a teacher and a learner." The article implements it by dividing patients into teams, redistributing members with identical content across expert groups for discussion, then returning each member to their original team to teach their topic. It is used here to deliver self-care education to MS patients in small groups, which the authors say "can lead to the creation of an active learning environment for all learners."
