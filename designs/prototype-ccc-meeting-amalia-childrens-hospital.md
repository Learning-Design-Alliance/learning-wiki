# Prototype CCC meeting at Amalia Children's Hospital

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A prototype clinical competency committee meeting developed in the prototyping phase of a design-based approach at a Dutch university children's hospital. It combined "previously gathered scores from multiple frontline workplace assessors" with written independent judgments from CCC members, structured discussion led by a neutral chair, and formulation of feedback comprising positive feedback and three points for improvement for each resident. It was evaluated with observations, member interviews and resident questionnaires over two cycles.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- CCC members trained in providing feedback
- Program director briefs members beforehand on the CCC's purpose, assessment forms, and interpretation of EPA levels and CanMEDS competencies
- Residents deliver multiple assessment data prior to the meeting
#### Constraints
- Evaluated in a single paediatrics program with a relatively small sample of CCC members and residents; the authors note future research should focus on CCCs in different contexts

### Target Learners
- paediatrics residents (24 at the time of study)

### Learning Goals
- developmental assessment of clinical competence across CanMEDS competencies and EPAs
