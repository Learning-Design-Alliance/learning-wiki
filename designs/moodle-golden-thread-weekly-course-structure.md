# Moodle 'golden thread' course structure: weekly units of virtual lecture, online exercises, and material organized in small chunks through Moodle's lesson activity

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course was entirely organized via the university's Moodle into weekly units consisting of a virtual lecture, non-mandatory online exercises, and additional online material including videos and lecture notes. Using Moodle's lesson activity, the authors organized all content for each topic "in small chunks connected by a golden thread guiding the students", released after each lecture, sequencing video watching, lecture-note reading, and self-testing tasks while reminding students weekly of contact possibilities.
