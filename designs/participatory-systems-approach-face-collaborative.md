# Participatory systems approach to collaborative design that fosters relationship building and active knowledge acquisition

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief describes a "participatory systems approach in the Collaborative's design and practice" that fostered "relationship building, collaboration, and active knowledge acquisition." It operates through empathy in the design process, inclusion embedded in the infrastructure, and structured leadership development, treating state leaders as active co-participants rather than recipients of technical assistance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Needs-sensing to surface state-specific strengths and leadership opportunities
- Meeting processes centered on building relationships, pooling knowledge, and sharing research
#### Constraints
- Applied to family and community engagement leadership across state education agencies in a four-state region

### Target Learners

### Learning Goals

- Cross-state knowledge sharing
