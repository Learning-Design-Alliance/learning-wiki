# Longitudinal four-timepoint SBML curriculum structure (baseline, asynchronous readiness, in-person mastery, 60-day follow-up)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The ED SLIT LAMP curriculum was organized as "four unique time points (Time 0–3) of intervention staggered over six months": a video-recorded baseline exam (Time 0), an asynchronous learning packet with an independent readiness assessment requiring a minimum 90% score within 30 days (Time 1), an in-person demonstration and rapid cycle deliberate practice session with an ophthalmologist on a standardized patient (Time 2), and a 60-day post-examination survey (Time 3). Assessments were mapped to ACGME EM Milestones Patient Care domain (PC8) levels 1 through 5, and the structure enabled evaluation across all four Kirkpatrick model levels.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Independent readiness assessment passed at a minimum 90% score before advancing to the in-person phase
- Two-hour scheduling intervals per learner at Time 2 to allow for device preparation, re-attempts, debriefing, and survey completion
#### Constraints
- The study over-budgeted a two-hour template for each learner, which drastically limited the number of participants schedulable for the final in-person exam

### Target Learners
- Board-certified attending emergency physicians

### Learning Goals
- Procedural mastery of the comprehensive slit-lamp exam and downstream teaching of junior learners
