# ADDIE-based design and pilot process for digital escape rooms in medical education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study structured escape room development around the ADDIE model (Analyze, Design, Develop, Implement, Evaluate), reporting the Implement phase. Needs analysis came from earlier focus groups; designs were based on committee and clerkship learning outcomes; development used an iterative cycle with a technology firm. The pilot ran in two rounds, with revisions between rounds informed by video, interview, and think-aloud data.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A multidisciplinary team of about 13 people, including clinical teachers, medical educators, educational technology experts, and software company members
#### Constraints
- The authors state the design and implementation process was intensive and resource-demanding, requiring significant coordination across different areas of expertise

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Learning Goals
- applying prior coursework knowledge in a gamified, problem-based format
