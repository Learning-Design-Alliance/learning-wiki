# 4-hour simulation-based CARE protocol handover training workshop

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A 4-h offline communication training delivered in hospital seminar rooms across 15 sessions, capped at 40–42 nurses with a facilitator-to-participant ratio of 1:7. Guided by Madeline Hunter's direct instruction model, it included "video demonstrations … structured role‑plays, case‑based discussions, and guided reflection" built around the CARE protocol (Connect, Ask, Respond, Empathize). Facilitator fidelity to core elements averaged 89% across assessed sessions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- trained facilitators completing a one-hour standardized training program
- low facilitator-to-participant ratio for small-group discussion and personalized feedback
#### Constraints
- evaluated in a single-group quasi-experimental design without a control group

### Target Learners
- qualified, English-proficient nurses in Hong Kong public hospitals

### Learning Goals
- structured handover communication using the CARE protocol
