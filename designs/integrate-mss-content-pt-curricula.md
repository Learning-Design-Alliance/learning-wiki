# Integrate MSS content into physical therapy curricula and provide continuing professional development to close competency gaps

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article concludes that significant gaps in Israeli PTs' MSS competency require educational action: "Integrating MSS content into physical therapy curricula and providing continuing professional development are necessary to enhance competencies." It further recommends equipping PTs with essential tools, clarifying roles, and promoting interprofessional collaboration to optimize MSS management and improve patient outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clear definition of PTs' roles in nutritional management
- Providing PTs with essential tools and collaboration opportunities
#### Constraints
- Nutrition is not formally integrated into the core competencies of Israeli PT education, and implementation of nutritional care varies globally due to legal and systemic constraints

### Target Learners
- Certified physical therapists
- Physical therapy students

### Learning Goals
- MSS knowledge
- Malnutrition and sarcopenia screening and treatment skills
- Interprofessional collaboration in MSS care
