# Six-block pedagogical model of foreign language communicative competence formation through interactive learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article proposes a model of formation of students' foreign language communicative competence consisting of six interconnected blocks: "linguistic, discursive, socio-cultural, strategic, social, sociolinguistic competence". Interactive technologies are assigned to building the linguistic and discursive blocks through nine elaborated task types (questioning, completion, drill, expansion, gap-filling, jigsaw, matching, odd word, prediction). The model was tested on the forming step via an educational-methodical complex on "Speech practice".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Formation must be based on the development of the FLCC components: linguistic, social, sociocultural, strategic, discursive and linguoprofessional
#### Constraints
- Tested at a single institution (Kazan Federal University) with 54 students

### Target Learners
- University students training as teachers of English

### Learning Goals
- Foreign language communicative competence across linguistic, discursive, socio-cultural, strategic, social and sociolinguistic components
