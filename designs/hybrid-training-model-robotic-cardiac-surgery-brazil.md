## Description
The article proposes a hybrid training model integrating two complementary pathways: short-term certification courses (<40 hours) for experienced surgeons and a one-year full-time fellowship for early-career surgeons. The model integrates entry requirements, theoretical instruction, simulation-based training, supervised clinical practice, and structured follow-up. As stated: 'The model integrates two complementary pathways - fellowship and short-term certification - and outlines a stepwise progression including entry requirements, theoretical instruction, simulation-based training, supervised clinical practice, and structured follow-up.'
