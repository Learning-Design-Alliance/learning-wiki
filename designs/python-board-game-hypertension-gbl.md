# Custom Python board game for hypertension pharmacotherapy with three difficulty levels

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The GBL intervention was a board game format consisting of multiple-choice practical questions developed in Python 3.9, presenting questions randomly from a predefined question bank. Students could choose among three difficulty levels: Level 1 involved "the recollection of facts (Knowledge)", Level 2 "comprehending information (Comprehension)", and Level 3 "solving problems related to their clinical practice (Application & Analysis)". Questions and scenarios were standardized with input from three clinical pharmacy specialists.
