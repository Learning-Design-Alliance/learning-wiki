# Daily 10-part lesson plan in three instructional blocks with built-in review

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Wilson Reading System organizes each lesson into "a daily 10-part lesson plan that builds on interaction between the teacher and student," divided into three blocks: parts one through five emphasize word study, parts six through eight emphasize spelling, and parts nine and 10 emphasize fluency and comprehension. The program sequences word-structure instruction from basic word skills to more complex language structure including morphological principles, with reviews reinforcing previous lessons. Lessons run 45–90 minutes in reading classes, small groups, or tutorials.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A Wilson instructors' manual that helps the teacher prepare the daily lesson
- teacher-student interaction as the core of each lesson
#### Constraints
- The intensive model of 60–90 minute instruction in small groups or individually is recommended for the most challenged readers

### Target Learners
- striving readers in grade 2 and above
- special and general education students

### Learning Goals
- decoding and word study
- spelling
- fluency and comprehension
