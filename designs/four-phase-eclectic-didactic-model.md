# Four-phase eclectic didactic model for teaching the English temporal system

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article presents a didactic model that is "a selection of aspects from different methods combined" so that activities reinforce each other, aiming for higher linguistic competence. It consists of four phases adapted from Piaget's learning process and the researcher's own continuation: Reception and Assimilation (comprehension-based exercises), Accommodation (consciousness-raising and translation exercises), Production (reconstructive exercises and free writing), and Self-Assessment (an ongoing journal). The model transforms the grammar lesson into a language lesson integrating all skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Intensive instruction (20-30+ hours/week), large classes around 30 students, low-intermediate learner level, and teachers competent in managing time effectively
#### Constraints
- The teacher need not be highly proficient in speaking the L2 because comprehension questions can be supported by Arabic; the model was designed for and tested with Arabic-speaking learners

### Target Learners
- Arabic-speaking freshman university students at low-intermediate English level

### Learning Goals
- English tense and aspect system (temporal system)
- language awareness
- reading and writing skills
- vocabulary building
