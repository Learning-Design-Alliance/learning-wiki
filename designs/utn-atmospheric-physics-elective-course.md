# Proposed elective course Física de la Atmósfera for engineering degrees at UTN Regional San Nicolás

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A proposed elective undergraduate course for all engineering specialties at Universidad Tecnológica Nacional, Regional San Nicolás, Argentina, to be implemented from 2013 within the institutional project "Forzantes Externos al Planeta y Variabilidad Climática". It is "una materia de tipo optativo para carreras de ingeniería", intended to give solid foundations in atmospheric physics to professionals interested in environmental and sustainability problems. Four interrelated chapters cover the atmosphere and meteorology relevant to contaminant transport, solar energy, pollutant dispersion, and climate.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Vertical articulation with prior basic courses such as Física General (I y II) and Análisis Matemático I y II, and with later subjects such as Higiene y Seguridad, Ingeniería Ambiental, Estadística, Análisis Numérico and Cálculo Avanzado.
#### Constraints
- The proposal is original and the course had not been implemented at the time of writing; practical work should be adapted to the specialties of the students.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate engineering students of all specialties at UTN Regional San Nicolás

### Learning Goals
- understanding atmospheric processes related to local and global climate
- dispersion and transport of air pollutants
- key concepts of solar activity, climate change and climate variability
