# Four-week daily audiovisual-media listening treatment embedded in second-semester French instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The treatment consisted of daily listening activities using online audiovisual media excerpts, taking approximately 10 minutes each across 13 class periods, embedded in the regular course so that instructional time and content were equivalent to the control group and "the only difference was task type." Activities used pre- and postlistening tasks to process aural input from the excerpts, and the same instructor taught both sections to keep teaching style consistent. Students were unaware of the difference in instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Equivalent instructional time and content across conditions
- Same instructor across groups to remove teaching differences as a confound
- Pre- and postlistening activities surrounding the audiovisual excerpts
#### Constraints
- The article notes the short four-week time frame limited learners' contact with the experimental instruction

### Target Learners
- postsecondary second-semester French students
- native English-speaking L2 learners of French

### Learning Goals
- L2 listening comprehension development
