# Akamai Maui Short Course: one-week inquiry-based internship preparation course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The AMSC is the first week of the eight-week Akamai Internship Program, which places Hawaii college students in Maui high-tech internships and aims to "retain and advance students in science and engineering ﬁelds, and to create a local technical workforce". The course contains four sequential full inquiry activities (Camera Obscura and Sun Shadows, Lenses and Refraction, Color and Light, and the Adaptive Optics Demonstrator) plus Communication and Science, Technology and Society strands, tailored to host-site needs, with a printed day-by-day schedule (Table 1).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors trained in inquiry design (via the PDP)
- Host-site employer input to tailor goals
- Low-cost optics materials and an AO workstation
#### Constraints
- Small Maui academic community means some students arrive with prior exposure to the same inquiry activities, creating redundancy

### Target Learners
- freshmen and sophomore community-college students from underrepresented groups in Hawaii

### Learning Goals
- science and engineering process skills, optics content, communication and professional skills for internship success
