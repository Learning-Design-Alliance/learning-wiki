# Methodological system of blended learning in computer science with four components

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article develops a methodological system of blended learning in computer science whose components are "the target one, the content-related one, the procedural component and the assessment and performance one". The target component reflects educational demands and state standards; the content-related component develops information-activity skills and ICT modelling; the procedural component includes the schedule and roadmap activity elements and determines check points; the assessment and performance component uses a module and rating system correlating scores to information and communication competency development level. The system is built on six transformed general didactic principles and realised through learning roadmaps in a subject information environment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A specially designed subject information environment has to be created to provide the multitude of conditions for drawing up learning roadmaps
#### Constraints
- The system was developed and trialled for computer science courses at higher education institutions in Russia and Kazakhstan

### Target Learners
- first-year university students of economics and related directions
- senior pedagogical-university students of physics and mathematics profile

### Learning Goals
- information and communication competency in computer science
- self-regulated learning and self-education skills
