# Web ARCO virtual didactic model of multiple-choice exam with problem-solving heuristic report

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A web-based learning resource mounted on the ARCO platform, an online teaching support channel developed by the CFRD at the University of Concepcion, accessible at www.arco.cfrd.cl. Students perform timed test simulations comparable to official exams; once finished, the solution is released and "a report wi th the correct answers and a problem-solving heuristic, entering previously by the professor" is shown. The platform stores date, time and duration of each attempt, giving teachers diagnostic information about preparation habits.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must access the platform within the 10-15 days before each official test, and serious use was operationalized as spending at least 50% of the available exam time (approx. 55 of 110 minutes) and simulating at least 12 hours before the exam.
#### Constraints
- The heuristic reports must be entered previously by the professor, so content preparation is instructor-dependent.

### Target Learners
- first-year engineering students at University of Concepcion, Chile

### Learning Goals
- preparation for multiple-choice and problem-solving physics exams
- systematic self-directed study and self-assessment of knowledge level
