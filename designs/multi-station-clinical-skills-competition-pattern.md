# Multi-station clinical skills competition pattern combining operative-skill stations, a knowledge quiz, expert refereeing, and digital scoring

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (5 for, 2 mixed) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Guanghua Cup was organized as two sections with seven stations: six operative-skill stations (cavity preparation, scaling and suture, bandaging and suture, impressions and crown preparation, tooth carving, CPR and BP measurement) run simultaneously with teams rotating every 25 minutes, followed by a multiple-choice knowledge competition. Dental experts from participating schools designed the projects and scoring standards, and 2–6 referees per station scored each team with averaged scores. The article presents this as a reusable program design that let students "exhibit their clinical skills in a competitive environment and develop collegiality and teamwork".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dental experts from participating schools design the competition projects and develop the scoring standards, announced in advance
- Referee training before the competition so referees are familiar with rules, procedures, evaluation forms, and scoring standards
#### Constraints
- The article notes the competition was on a smaller scale than the China National Clinical Skills Competition for medical students and did not fully cover all oral dentistry-related knowledge and skills

### Target Learners
- Fifth-year undergraduate dental students in their clinical practice stage

### Learning Goals
- Clinical operative skills across seven dental disciplines
- Professional theoretical knowledge of oral diseases
- First aid ability
- Collegiality and teamwork
