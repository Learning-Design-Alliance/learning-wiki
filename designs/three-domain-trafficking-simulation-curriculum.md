# Three-domain simulation curriculum: communication in simulation, cognitive processing through documentation, and knowledge acquisition through a didactic module

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The M-SIGHT module organizes learning across three sequenced domains within one session. As the article states, "This curriculum teaches students through three domains: patient communication in simulation, cognitive processing through documentation, and knowledge acquisition through a didactic module." Students first interact with a standardized patient and receive SP feedback on communication, then document the encounter in USMLE Step 2 Clinical Skills format, then complete an online didactic module with pre- and post-assessment quizzes. The post-encounter placement of the didactic material lets the module confirm what the SP scenario only suggested.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A standardized patient encounter with feedback focused on communication skills and trust-building
- A documentation task prompting assessment, plan, and reflection on resources and interdisciplinary collaboration
- An online module with pre- and post-assessment quizzes to measure knowledge acquisition
#### Constraints
- The scenario is designed to arouse suspicion rather than require students to confirm trafficking; SPs do not disclose during or after the interview that they are victims

### Target Learners
- third-year medical students

### Learning Goals
- trauma-informed communication skills
- clinical documentation of trafficking red flags
- knowledge of human trafficking definitions, scope, and referral resources
