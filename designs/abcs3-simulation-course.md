# ABCS3: a two-day simulation-based learning course with case-based lectures, an ABCDE hands-on station, and rotating simulation scenarios

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The ABCS3 is a two-day simulation-based learning seminar for undergraduate medical students in Greece, built from "three core components: the case-based lectures, the ABCDE hands-on station, and the simulation-based clinical scenarios". Sixty full participants rotated through ten 30-minute scenarios in groups of six with two instructors, using mannequins, partial task simulators, and standardized patients, while 170 observers watched lectures and streamed scenarios. It was designed by the Scientific Society of Hellenic Medical Students with faculty revision.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors in each scenario room to direct the process, enforce time limits, and structure team roles
- Appropriately designed rooms with the necessary medical equipment for each scenario
#### Constraints
- Only 60 of 230 attendees could take part in the interactive hands-on scenarios; the remaining 170 observed via video live-streaming without interaction

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students (years 1-6) in Greece

### Learning Goals
- clinical knowledge and skills in emergency medicine scenarios
- familiarity with simulation-based learning techniques
