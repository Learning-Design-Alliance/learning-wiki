# NanoTechStart: entrepreneurial startup-pitch project simulating a venture capital session

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
NanoTechStart is a collaborative project activity modeled after the HealthStart MadrI+D program, in which teams of 2-3 students select cutting-edge nanotechnology from an instructor-curated article database and develop a biomedical application proposal. Teams receive guided mentoring and brainstorming, then craft an elevator pitch and a video presentation (limited to 10 minutes) submitted one week before the final session. The culminating NanoTechStart session "simulates a real-world investment pitch environment" before a panel of faculty and industry experts, followed by democratic voting; a student coordinator per team receives a +0.5 grade bonus. The activity is worth 30% of the continuous evaluation grade, with a maximum score of 10.0.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A curated database of nanotechnology articles provided by the instructors
- A panel of faculty members and industry experts for the final session
- A designated platform for uploading video presentations by the deadline
#### Constraints
- Student satisfaction was lower than for Nanogames (76.2% vs 93.7% overall satisfaction, non-significant)

### Target Learners
- fourth-year biomedical engineering undergraduates

### Learning Goals
- applying nanotechnology knowledge to real-world biomedical problems
- entrepreneurial acumen and communication skills
- teamwork and leadership
