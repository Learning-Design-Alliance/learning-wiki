# Paired explicit, intentional curricula with embedded language and social-skills design

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The district's paired curricula are explicit, intentional, domain-targeted preschool curricula. OWL "targets children's early language and literacy skills and includes a social-skills component embedded in each unit", while Building Blocks targets early mathematics, particularly number and simple arithmetic and geometry, measurement, and spatial sense, with patterns, data, and sorting and sequencing woven in and many intentionally child-directed activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Curriculum-specific training before and during the school year plus weekly to biweekly on-site support from an experienced early childhood coach trained in both curricula.
#### Constraints
- Neither curriculum targets children's EF skills directly; the evidence base for Building Blocks is stronger than that for OWL.

### Target Learners
- prekindergarten-aged children (four and five years old)

### Learning Goals
- early language and literacy
- early mathematics
