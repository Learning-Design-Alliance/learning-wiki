# Officina della Luce: a light-themed tinkering implementation project in Bologna primary schools

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A September 2021–September 2022 experimental project involving 13 primary school classrooms and 24 teachers (approximately 300 students) in Bologna, funded by a grant covering materials, teacher time and external facilitators. The Light Play workshop, recontextualised from the Tinkering Studio, was run at least five times per class (10 hours minimum). A three-branch resource library (workshops, hands-on science references, documentation) was published on the INAF platform https://play.inaf.it/officinadellaluce/.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Professional development sessions where teachers personally experience the workshops
- A repository of possible disciplinary connections and documentation tools
- External expert facilitators available to lead or support classroom activities
#### Constraints
- The pandemic compressed the project from eight to four months, and the fishbowl documentation protocol could run only once per teacher team, at the end, making documentation the most fragmented part

### Target Learners
- primary school students in Bologna

### Learning Goals
- engagement with physical phenomena
- integration of tinkering with disciplinary learning
