# GIS-based instruction unit built in ArcGIS 10.0 for the topic 'The Distribution of the Forests and Wildlife in the World'

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An eight-lesson GIS-based instruction unit designed using ArcGIS 10.0 software, delivered in a computer laboratory for eight weeks with one 80-minute lesson weekly, plus two extra lessons introducing the GIS software. The article states the unit "focused on the topic of 'The Distribution of the Forests and Wildlife in the World'". Lesson activities focused on creating data, inserting hyperlinks, tracing forest areas, and comparing and labelling forest areas on world maps, with induction sets, classroom exercises, and question-and-answer segments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a computer laboratory and ArcGIS 10.0 software
- Two extra introductory lessons to familiarise students with the GIS software before content teaching begins
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only with Form 4 (Grade 10) underachieving Geography students in two rural Smart Schools in Sabah

### Target Learners
- Form 4 (Grade 10) underachieving secondary Geography students

### Learning Goals
- Understanding the type and distribution of world forests and wildlife
- Mastery goal orientation toward learning Geography
