# Pilot medical student robotic course combining IS online module, IS-led robot overview, console skills practice, and bedside-assist simulation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A four-component course: a 1-h online module introducing "components of the da Vinci Xi platform, terminology, and basic features"; a 1.5-h IS-led robot overview with docking and instrument-exchange practice; 1.5 h of console skills practice (ring transfer, suturing, SimNow tasks); and a 1-h faculty-led bedside-assist simulation with instrument introduction around an obstacle, instrument exchange, and camera cleaning. Designed for senior students, capped at six per session.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a da Vinci trainer robot and IS trainer
- A surgical faculty member to design and facilitate the simulation exercise
#### Constraints
- The simulation exercise was added as a supplement to the standard IS curriculum
- Access relied on limited loaner-robot availability

### Target Learners
- third- and fourth-year medical students

### Learning Goals
- bedside assist competencies: safe instrument introduction, visuospatial targeting, instrument handling and exchange, communication with the console surgeon
