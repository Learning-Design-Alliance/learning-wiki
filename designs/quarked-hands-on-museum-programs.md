# Hands-on museum programs: How Small is Small and Quarks: Ups, Downs, and the Universe

## Description
Two hands-on education programs at the KU Natural History Museum, taught by a professional museum educator, have reached more than 5,000 youth since 2006. "All programs incorporate demonstrations and hands-on activities." How Small is Small explores size and scale, including why insects as tall as buildings could not exist; Quarks: Ups, Downs, and the Universe introduces the particulate nature of matter, states of matter, atom structure, and quark properties through activities like density experiments and building atom models.
