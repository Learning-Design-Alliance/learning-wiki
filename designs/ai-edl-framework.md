# AI-EDL: a cyclical, theory-grounded framework modeling learning as an iterative feedback loop with Student, AI Agent, and Teacher roles

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The AI-Educational Development Loop is a conceptual framework modeling adaptive, goal-oriented, reflective learning in AI-powered environments. It comprises sequential components — Knowledge Gap, Learning, Trial, Assessment, Reflection, Loop Continuation, Goal Achievement — and is characterized as "Personalized", "Cyclical", "Collaborative", "Technologically enabled", and "Theoretically grounded", aligning Socratic dialogue, Aristotelian ethics, Hull's drive theory, and Skinner's conditioning. A fourteen-phase AI planning logic workflow specifies Teacher, Student, and AI Agent interactions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires coordinated roles among Student, AI Agent, and Teacher, with the teacher preparing materials, questions, answer keys, and a grading rubric as input to the AI agent
#### Constraints
- The framework was implemented and piloted for writing-intensive, feedback-sensitive tasks in teacher education; the authors state further investigation is needed across diverse disciplines, especially more quantitative or collaborative contexts

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and graduate students in special education teacher-preparation courses

### Learning Goals
- reflective practice and revision of written responses to content-specific questions
- self-regulated learning and metacognitive self-assessment
