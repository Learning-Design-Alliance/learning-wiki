# Weekly scaffolded milestones decomposing the capstone thesis into SMART goals across one semester

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The course decomposes the thesis into weekly tasks: literature is collected at four items per week and reviewed one week later, the introduction is divided into weekly elements (aim, background, significance, questions, hypotheses, glossary), and methodology elements are built in weeks 5-6, with data collection, analysis, discussion, and conclusion scheduled across weeks 7-12. The author states this "decomposes a large literature review (collecting and reviewing approximately 40 academic sources) into SMART weekly goals". Weekly submission templates identify the required items.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Weekly submission templates identifying required items
- Laboratory activities covering the predefined processes (e.g., Endnote, literature searching)
#### Constraints
- Literature collection is restricted to academic sources at a fixed pace of four items per week

### Target Learners
- undergraduate capstone students

### Learning Goals
- managing a semester-long research project
- conducting a literature review
- writing a research thesis
