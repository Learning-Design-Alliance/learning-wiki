# Kharkiv quantum education initiatives of 2023-2024: qUA workgroup, US-Ukraine Quantum Forum, and Kharkiv Quantum Seminar series

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Three linked initiatives emerged in 2023 to sustain and internationalize quantum education and research at SPT. In May 2023 a voluntary quantum workgroup (qUA) of the Ukrainian Physical Society was formed by representatives of Kharkiv, Kyiv, Lviv, and Donetsk to promote Ukrainian quantum scientists within the EU Quantum Flagship landscape. The US-Ukraine Quantum Forum (August 28-31, 2023) brought together 32 speakers over four days with more than 50 attending scientists and students. Since October 2023 the regular virtual Kharkiv Quantum Seminar series features mid-career and diaspora speakers plus world-leading invited specialists. The authors write that "we organized the Kharkiv Quantum Seminar series, which are held on a regular basis in the virtual format since October 2023."
