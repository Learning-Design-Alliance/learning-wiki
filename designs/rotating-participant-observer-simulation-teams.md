# Rotating participant-observer student teams with a closing whole-group debriefing in each simulation session

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A session-level design in which each 60-minute simulation session divides eight students into two teams: "four students (Team A) participate in a 15-minute simulation while four students observe (Team B)." Teams switch roles for a second 15-minute scenario, then all eight join a 30-minute debriefing with a faculty expert and debriefer; each student leads three times over 12 weeks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Eight students per session divided into two rotating teams
- A faculty expert and a faculty debriefer for the 30-minute debriefing
- A one-way mirror observation area for the waiting team
#### Constraints
- Each 60-minute session accommodates two 15-minute scenarios plus the 30-minute debriefing

### Target Learners
- Medical students in clinical clerkships

### Learning Goals
- Repeated team-leadership experience
- Learning through observing peers manage scenarios
