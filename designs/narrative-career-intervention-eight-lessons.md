# Eight-lesson group-based narrative career intervention sequence (CIP, collages, lifelines, journals)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A reusable lesson-sequence pattern in which learners complete narrative questions in the Career Interest Profile, make collages depicting how they see themselves, draw lifelines noting significant life events, and keep reflective journals, sharing each in group discussion. The article states the activities were designed so participants could "narrate their life stories; ultimately giving them the opportunity to identify life themes inherent in the story-telling process". Each activity ran for approximately 45 minutes over two months alongside standard lessons.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Group discussion in which learners share responses and the meaning of their artefacts with fellow learners
- Facilitative questions prompting reflection on and elaboration of life events
- Journal entries after each activity
#### Constraints
- The article reports some learners found tasks hard, e.g. "It was hard for me to do the timeline 'cause I find it hard to share my personal experiences"

### Target Learners
- Grade 11 learners in both independent and resource-constrained state schools

### Learning Goals
- Self-knowledge
- Clarified values and interests
- Career decision-making
- Career adaptability
