# Goal-based scenarios in interactive science modules motivate novices through accomplishment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The website’s interactive modules and experiments are organized as goal-based scenarios: users pursue a goal, such as creating a black hole or finding one in the night sky, and each scenario is designed ”to motivate learning and a sense of accomplishment when completed”. The pattern is tailored for novices and children, who ”often prefer interactive learning experiences” that keep them engaged until they achieve some payoff.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each scenario gives the user a concrete goal and a payoff upon completion
#### Constraints
- Tailored for novices and children; experts and adults are served instead by the FAQ encyclopedia and glossary

### Target Learners
- novices and children

### Learning Goals
- motivation and sustained engagement with black hole science
- a sense of accomplishment in science tasks
