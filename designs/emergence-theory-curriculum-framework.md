# Emergence theory as a framework for teaching compromise skills

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Emergence theory holds that complex structures and patterns develop from the self-organization of simple processes: "establishing a few simple rules that every part of a system can follow produces highly complex behaviors and results." The article applies this framework, grounded in Piaget's cognitive development, Skinner's behaviorism, and Vygotsky's social cognition, to design a curriculum teaching complex communication and compromise skills to students with ASD. The theory had not previously been applied to teaching compromise and communication skills.
