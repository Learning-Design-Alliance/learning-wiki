# Progressive video modeling workflow: parabola fit of video data converted into nested-if computational model lines

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A lesson-level design pattern in which students analyze segmented regions of a real video with parabola fits in Tracker's DataTool, then convert the fitted mathematical values into nested if-statement computational model lines, comparing the model view against the real data and the three scientific graphs. The article demonstrates this across three stages: free fall, free fall with upthrust and drag, and bounce up, stating "Tracker's video modeling can afford for both mathematical and computational thinking integrated seamlessly".
