# Daily supplemental small-group early-reading cycle combining repeated reading, phonemic segmentation, and guided writing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The EIR® instructional pattern organizes supplemental early-reading instruction as short daily sessions (15–20 minutes) for the lowest-achieving students, working in groups of five to seven with the classroom teacher. In the studied first-grade implementation, the program ran in three-day cycles: the teacher read a picture book to the whole class, taught intervention students to segment words and blend phonemes, then had students read a story summary with minimal assistance and write one related sentence a day with the teacher's help. Children additionally worked individually (5 minutes) or in pairs (10 minutes) with a trained aide or project assistant, with weekly running records to assess progress.

### Learning Goals
