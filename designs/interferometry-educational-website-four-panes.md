# Educational website on laser interferometry with four integrated panes

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A web-based learning environment for remote teaching of optical measurements using lasers, designed for 4th year undergraduate and 1st year postgraduate students. The site integrates background literature, simulation, data analysis, and remote experiments: 'The website comprises four panes namely a. Background Literature and Introduction to the Subject b. Generation of Optical Images by Numerical Simulation c. Interferometric analysis, Fringe Counting and Fourier Filtering d. Conducting Real Experiments by Mach-Zehnder Interferometer'. It targets learning of interferometry fundamentals and heat-transfer applications through a standard web browser.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An internet-connected computer with a commercially available web browser
- A physical laboratory hosting the lasers, cameras and interferometer for remote access
#### Constraints
- At the reported stage, optical methods considered are those based on refractive index changes in the material medium
- The authors state remote laboratories are intended for use in a limited way, not as a replacement for hands-on laboratories

### Target Learners
- 4th year undergraduate engineering students
- 1st year postgraduate students

### Learning Goals
- Fundamentals of interferometry and its applications
- Optical measurement techniques in engineering heat transfer and fluid flow
