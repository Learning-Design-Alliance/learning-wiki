# Lecture-based adaptation of Tutorials in Introductory Physics with whole-class checkouts and micro-lectures

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An adaptation of Tutorials in Introductory Physics for large-enrollment settings where "students proceeded through TIP materials in groups, but with whole-class 'checkouts' rather than instructor-intensive, individual group checkouts". Short micro-lectures and Peer Instruction activities are interspersed between TIP activities. This adaptation strays in some significant ways from the intentions of the curriculum developers but was necessary for logistical reasons at institutions without graduate teaching assistants.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A SCALE-UP-style classroom with round tables seating six students each, a primary instructor, backup instructors, and at least one undergraduate Learning Assistant
#### Constraints
- This adaptation strays in some significant ways from the intentions of the curriculum developers

### Target Learners
- natural science majors in large-enrollment introductory physics

### Learning Goals
- conceptual understanding of electricity and magnetism through guided group tutorials
