# Eight-step digital storytelling production sequence embedded in a writing course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article prescribes a reusable lesson/unit sequence for DS-integrated writing: writing and rewriting the narrative in three drafts, storyboarding, selecting suitable multimedia elements and accompanying them with scripts, recording the voice, using WeVideo to make digital stories, in-class presentations, sharing via Facebook, and getting feedback on the videos via Facebook. The sequence keeps the process-writing core while adding multimodal layers and authentic audience feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A video-editing tool (WeVideo), storyboard sheets, a Google Drive account for lecturer feedback, and a Facebook account for sharing and peer feedback
#### Constraints
- Both groups spent the same amount of time (almost 42 hours in total) on the project implementation, with weekly time reducing from five hours to 2 or 3 hours toward the term's end

### Target Learners
- intermediate-level EFL English-major students in preparatory programs

### Learning Goals
- narrative paragraph writing
- multimodal composition
