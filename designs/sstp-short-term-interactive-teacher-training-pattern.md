# Short-term teacher training combining information, video examples, exercises, and discussion of easy-to-use techniques

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The SSTP was a six-hour program in four sessions of one and a half hours each, built around presenting information, watching 19 previously recorded real-life classroom videos, doing exercises based on real-life examples, and discussion. It taught five techniques (modeling, rehearsing, prompting, giving cues, rewarding) chosen from observation and interview data about what teachers lacked and wanted. The authors state that "programs similar to the SSTP consisting of many exercises, real- life examples, and visual materials with a short implementation time can be a useful tool for training teachers".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content determined based on the needs, interests, and expectations of teachers
- Techniques that can be easily implemented in a short period of time, demonstrated step-by-step with clear language
#### Constraints
- Effects on student outcomes were not examined in this study
- Observational data were gathered in only one academic class

### Target Learners
- Inclusive grade 1-2 classroom teachers in public elementary schools

### Learning Goals
- Knowledge of teaching social skills
- Use of social skills teaching techniques (modeling, rehearsing, prompting, giving cues, rewarding)
- Expectations regarding social skills of students with special needs
