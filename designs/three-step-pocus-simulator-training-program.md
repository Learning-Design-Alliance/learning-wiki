# Three-step standardized POCUS training program: e-learning, simulator sessions, and blinded practical evaluation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A standardized three-step pathway for teaching the abdominal POCUS procedure to ultrasound-naive medical students: an expert-reviewed e-learning module with theoretical assessment and individualized feedback, then "two simulator-based training sessions", and a blinded practical evaluation with an OSATS grid and checklist. In the pilot, "30 medical students (15 undergraduates and 15 residents) participated in a standardized three-step training program".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires an ultrasonography simulator with standardized exercises, an online curriculum that adhered to the Royal College of Radiologists’ recommendations for ultrasound training, and blinded expert evaluators for the practical assessment
#### Constraints
- In this study, POCUS was treated solely as a procedure, without assessing the student’s clinical reasoning

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students
- family medicine residents

### Learning Goals
- performing an abdominal POCUS procedure
- classifying ultrasound images as normal or pathological
