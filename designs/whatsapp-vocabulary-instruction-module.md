# WhatsApp-based vocabulary instruction module (pre-teaching, teaching, post-teaching phases)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A mobile-based instructional module in which the experimental group installed WhatsApp and "electronically received vocabulary instructions four days a week for four weeks (16 sessions)", with five vocabulary items per session in a shared chat group. Print materials were converted to mobile format, with pictures, videos, definitions, synonyms, antonyms, and drills sent via WhatsApp, and students sent individual answers to the teacher. The module had three phases: pre-teaching, teaching, and post-teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must install WhatsApp on a cell phone, tablet, or laptop and join a shared WhatsApp chat group
- Instructional materials must be converted from print into a mobile-based format
#### Constraints
- The study applied the module to elementary-level, non-English-major junior high school students in Isfahan, Iran

### Target Learners
- junior high school EFL students

### Learning Goals
- English vocabulary knowledge
