# Performance–play–dialog (PPD) design model for game-based learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The PPD design model views learning through the lens of performance, cultivated through play and dialog, mirroring Dewey's action–reflection dialectic. The article states: "In prior work, we proposed the performance –play–dialog (PPD) design model for game-based learning (Chee, 2011)." In the model, play is instantiated by students playing Statecraft X, where learning to be is experienced in the first person through role taking, and dialog is teacher-facilitated classroom conversation prompting reflection and sense making. The model raises the desired outcome standard to VIP: values, identity, and performance.
