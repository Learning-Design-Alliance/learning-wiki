# ELPAS: a case-based interactive e-learning course on patient safety for medical students

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
ELPAS is an online course developed at Freiburg University focusing on "team work, error management, situational awareness, and crisis resource management". Content is delivered in 3 major modules that are linked but can be completed in random order, using a case-based interactive approach in which students work in virtual groups of 6 persons on multiple choice tests, video analysis and case studies. Students need a total study time of 6 to 8 hours and have three months to complete the course on the university's ILIAS learning management system.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
