# Multiple identity vs. single identity priming intervention (adapted Aspects of Identity Questionnaire vs. Collective Self-Esteem Scale)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A brief pre-test identity priming manipulation in which students either reflected on multiple aspects of the self via a 22-item adapted Aspects of Identity Questionnaire covering "personal", "relational", "social", and "collective" aspects rated on a 5-point Likert scale (about 5 min), or reflected on a single cultural identity via a six-item adapted Collective Self-Esteem Scale. A manipulation check showed a significant association between condition and identity self-descriptions, χ²(1) = 22.59, p < 0.001, with coding agreement Cohen's κ = 0.67.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administered before a performance task so that identity salience is active during the test; effectiveness was checked with an open-ended "Who am I?" self-description
#### Constraints
- The authors state the brief questionnaire "might not have led to a sufficiently in‐depth reflection" and may not have contained the identity dimensions most relevant to participants

### Target Learners
- Secondary school students in grades 9 and 10, including students of migrant descent

### Learning Goals
- Maintaining academic test performance in evaluative, stereotype-relevant situations
