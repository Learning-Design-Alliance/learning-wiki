# Six-step constructivist lesson format alternating group exploration, discussion, teacher elaboration and evaluation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article operationalized its constructivist learning environment through a fixed 45-minute lesson format of six steps: revision and grouping into groups of 8-10, group activities with "explorations and sharing of ideas", reconvened group discussion facilitated by the teacher, teacher elaboration correcting "students' misconceptions", further development of major concepts with student questions, and evaluation with chalkboard summary and assignments. The same content was taught in both environments; only the presentation differed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers were personally trained by the researcher for 2 weeks before the study to create the environment and follow the guides
#### Constraints
- The format was applied to junior secondary basic science topics in intact classes in Delta State, Nigeria

### Target Learners
- junior secondary school students in Nigeria

### Learning Goals
- basic science content knowledge (living things, human body systems, changes in matter)
