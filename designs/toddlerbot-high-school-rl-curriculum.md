# ToddlerBot-based humanoid curriculum instantiation for grades 9-12 with eight CS+AI sessions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The curriculum targets grades 9-12 with four students in two pairs, one ToddlerBot per pair, a recommended mentor-to-student ratio of 1:4, and "Eight three-hour computer science and AI (CS+AI) sessions (24 contact hours)" accompanying assembly, electrical checks, and a closing demonstration. ToddlerBot provides open hardware and software with a reported parts cost under USD 6,000. Five intended learning outcomes (LO1-LO5) span safe startup, explaining reward-based learning, simulation-physical comparison, failure diagnosis, and collaboration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A revised prerequisite of running and modifying a short Python program before RL labs
- Shared computers for training runs
- Mentor supervision for all physical trials
#### Constraints
- Additional build, preparation, and training time is not quantified
- If hardware is unavailable, simulation work preserves conceptual tasks but does not substitute for the safe startup outcome

### Target Learners
- High-school students in grades 9-12, paired with one robot per pair

### Learning Goals
- Assembling and safely starting a robot
- Explaining how rewards and trying different actions guide learning
- Comparing simulation with physical measurements
