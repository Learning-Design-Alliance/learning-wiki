# Three-tier secondary robotics programme progressing from robots to solar cars to electronics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Woodlands Ring Secondary structures robotics as a multi-year progression. In Secondary 1 students spend 20 hours designing, building, programming and testing robots in teams; in Secondary 2, over 30 hours, students design a solar energy-powered car; in Secondary 3 students "undertake a more elaborate electronics project" using multimeters and soldering tools to construct sensor circuits, building on prior knowledge and skills.
