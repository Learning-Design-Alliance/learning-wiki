# Standalone web-based ECG tutorial with theory module, feedback-based training, and encyclopedia

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A web-based ECG tutorial developed by cardiologists at Copenhagen University Hospital with the university e-learning unit. It comprised "a theory module concerning the ECG and its components", "a training module with the opportunity to interpret 15 different ECGs (including clinical scenarios) with feedback", and "an ECG encyclopedia for use during the training module". Students worked through it individually and unsupervised in a 5-hour session including pre- and post-tests.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered via the Internet or a local intranet; students worked individually without personal teaching
- Administered in a supervised session to resolve technical issues and record usage
#### Constraints
- In the study, students had access to the tutorial only for a limited time on one occasion and not during the tests

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Learning Goals
- ECG interpretation skills (rhythm, intervals, hypertrophy, axis, ischemia)
