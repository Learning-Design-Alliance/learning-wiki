# Diabetes distress e-learning: a course for diabetes educators

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
An interactive online e-learning course for credentialled diabetes educators covering identification and support of diabetes distress. It "includes learning objectives, videos demonstrating consultation skills, and activities (e.g., opportunities to check understanding, practice skills, self-reflect, and plan actions)", with greater emphasis on skills development than the chapter active control. It was developed using intervention mapping and consists of 7 modules; median modules accessed was 6, with 47% accessing all 7.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Desktop or laptop computer access with an internet connection
- About 7 hours total commitment (42–73 min per module)
#### Constraints
- A few participants experienced platform navigation and technical difficulties, to be resolved in future versions

### Target Learners
- credentialled diabetes educators (nurses, dietitians, pharmacists)

### Learning Goals
- recognising and addressing diabetes distress
- consultation and communication skills
- making mental health referrals
