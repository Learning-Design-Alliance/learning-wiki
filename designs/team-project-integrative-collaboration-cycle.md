# Team project method organisational cycle for integrative collaboration

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes an organisational process cycle for learners' integrative collaboration within technical study subjects: teams analyse members' cooperation in problem solving, develop an action plan, implement the project, present it, discuss it, and finish with reflection as a critical assessment of the team project and self-evaluation. Teacher participation decreases gradually while each learner's learning autonomy increases as collaborative experiences organise the learning process towards mastery.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teams of three to four members formed after a short introductory lecture
- A common objective and tasks assigned by the teacher
#### Constraints
