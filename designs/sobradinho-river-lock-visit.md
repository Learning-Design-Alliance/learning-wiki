# Visit to the Sobradinho dam river lock as a non-formal setting for teaching hydrodynamics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes the Sobradinho dam's lock, whose chamber has "120 m of length and 17 m width", overcoming a gap of "around 32.5 meters" with "ma ximum ﬁlling time of 16 minutes", as a non-formal learning setting. On site, students "can have contact with several devices that could not be viewed in a form al space (classroom)" and discuss operation with an experienced professional. Locks function "as stairs or elevators for ships or boats", with two gates separating the two river levels, filling and emptying usually by gravity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a nearby river lock or comparable hydraulic structure and receptivity of its operating staff
#### Constraints
- The site was 150 km away from Senhor do Bonfim, and the article notes that reaching distant non-formal spaces in the region is a tiring, stressful and expensive activity

### Target Learners
- secondary-level physics students in regions without nearby museums or science centers

### Learning Goals
- hydrodynamic principles of lock operation
- economic importance of river navigation and dams
