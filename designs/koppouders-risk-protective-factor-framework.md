# Risk- and protective-factor-based design framework for the KopOpOuders course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course design is grounded in social learning theory, the theory of developmental psychopathology, and contextual theory, with course components linked to identified risk and protective factors for children of parents with mental illness. The authors chose five focal points: "strengthening parent-child interaction, supporting the unaffected parent, ensuring a support network or trusted person for each child, reinforcing children’s coping and social skills, explaining the parental mental illness to the children." Each focal point was operationally defined into concrete course objectives, such as parents learning to inform children in age-appropriate ways about the illness and absolve them of responsibility.

#### Constraints

### Target Learners

### Learning Goals
