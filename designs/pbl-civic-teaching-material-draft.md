# Draft teaching material of civic education based on PBL with introduction, content and closing parts

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The developed product is a draft of civic education teaching material for fourth-grade elementary students integrating the PBL model. The article states "This teaching material is arranged into three parts; introduction, content, and closing." PBL syntax is integrated via rubrics: Let's Observe, Let's Think, Let's Read, Let's Work, Let's Discuss, Let's Write. After revision, the problem is presented at the beginning of discussion as a medium of delivering the material.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher must understand the teaching material and PBL deeply for more effective learning
- PBL needs to be socialized before learning
#### Constraints
- Teachers were initially not familiar with the material and did not understand PBL deeply
