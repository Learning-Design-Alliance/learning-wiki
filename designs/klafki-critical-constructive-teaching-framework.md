# Klafki's critical-constructive teaching framework grounded the curriculum development for layperson EBM training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The courses were adapted for patient and consumer representatives using the theoretical framework of critical-constructive teaching methods of Klafki, which "focuses on self-determination and participation abilities of students". The framework promotes systematic reflection regarding aims and intentions of instructions as a prerequisite for curriculum development. Combined with adult-learning strategies and cognitive learning and teaching principles, it shaped small-group work built on participants' own experiences as patients or counsellors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Systematic reflection regarding aims and intentions of instruction as a prerequisite for curriculum development
#### Constraints
- Developed for medical laypersons acting as patient or consumer representatives, not for physicians

### Target Learners
- Medical laypersons acting as patient and consumer representatives

### Learning Goals
- Self-determined, participatory acquisition of EBM problem-solving skills
