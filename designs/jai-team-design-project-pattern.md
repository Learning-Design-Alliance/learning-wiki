# Team design project at the end of the first year exposes graduate students to real-life accelerator problems collegially

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The JAI Accelerator Physics course includes a design project that students must complete at the end of the second term of their first year. A team of students is assigned to design an accelerator and its main subsystems with minimal initial input, with projects usually related to actual projects under development at other institutions. Students present their work in an open seminar at the JAI and at CERN. The paper reports the project “has proven to be very successful in that it exposes the students to real-life problems at an early stage in their training and challenges them to work on difficult topics collegially,” with unanimously positive responses from students and scientists.
