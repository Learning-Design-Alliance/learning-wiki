# Longitudinal, vertically aligned research modules embedded across three years of an undergraduate medical programme

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Selective 01–03 modules form a compulsory, community-orientated research strand running through years two to four of the 6-year MBChB programme. The article states these modules are "incremental and vertically aligned to develop students’ research competence longitudinally over a 3-year period". Each year builds on the previous: topic selection and literature review, then protocol, ethics approval, data collection and poster presentation, then an intervention. All students participate rather than a self-selected few.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All students complete each stage in sequence, with supervisor allocation, ethics committee review, and a structured marking rubric for the poster assessment
#### Constraints
- Described and evaluated in a single cohort at one South African medical school; the authors note limited generalisability because of the unique context

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical (MBChB) students in years 2–4

### Learning Goals
- research methodology competence, evidence-based medicine, community-orientated primary care
