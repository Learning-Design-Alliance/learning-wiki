# Forest-themed preschool environmental program integrating environmental ethics across four thematic units with empathy-building techniques

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A semester-long interdisciplinary program (January to June 2002) comprising seventy five designed activities and fifteen emerging activities, organized in four units: Trees, Animals, Risks to the forest, and Protection of the forest. It aims at fostering environmentally friendly attitudes through knowledge acquisition, feelings of care and responsibility, forming values and rules of pro-environmental behaviour, and strengthening environmentally friendly action. Techniques include outdoor experiential games, literature and role-playing, inductive discussion, values-education strategies, and eco-management actions such as cleaning the forest and planting trees.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Children in direct contact with the natural environment
- Techniques promoting feelings, especially empathy
- Children playing an active role in creating behavior rules rather than rules stated as absolute truths
#### Constraints
- Implemented with a single convenience-sample kindergarten class; results not generalisable since the sample does not represent the population as a whole

### Target Learners
- five-year-old kindergartners

### Learning Goals
- environmental values
- empathy for non-human beings
- pro-environmental behavior rules and action
