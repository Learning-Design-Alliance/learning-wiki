# Mathematics learning management model for improving creative thinking (six components grounded in five theoretical bases)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A learning management model for secondary mathematics designed to improve creative thinking, composed of six components: "1) principle, and concerned theoretical concepts, 2) lear ning objectives, 3) learning process, 4) social system, 5) principles of reflection, an d 6) a support system." It integrates constructivist theory, metacognitive strategy, problem solving strategy, inquiry approach, and Gestalt psychology principles, and was judged highly appropriate by expert evaluation before try-out with 9th-grade students over 30 hours of learning on Probability and Statistics content.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Expert evaluation of quality and appropriateness of the model, as done with a rating form in Phase 2
#### Constraints
- Developed and implemented with 9th-grade students in Khonsawan school, Thailand, on the Math 23102 Probability and Statistics content over thirty hours

### Target Learners
- 9th-grade secondary mathematics students in Thailand

### Learning Goals
- Creative thinking (elaboration, flexibility, fluency, originality)
- Mathematics learning achievement in probability and statistics
