# Majorana's autograph lecture notes for his Naples theoretical physics course, written as aids for his students

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Majorana wrote with care a full series of autograph lecture notes for his theoretical physics course, explicitly as aids for his students, possibly intending to develop them into a book for students as he had with his original study notes, the Volumetti. He left them in safekeeping with his favourite pupil Gilda Senatore the day before departing for Palermo. The notes later passed to Carrelli and then to E. Amaldi, lacking six lessons on electrodynamics and special relativity; a complete handwritten copy by Eugenio Moreno, including the relativity part, was found more recently.
