# Three-day AI bootcamp curriculum sequenced from foundations to learning agents to ethics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A comprehensive AI curriculum for teenagers that "balances the foundational concepts with an understanding of the state- of-the-art, organized around three concepts": Day 1 Foundations of AI, Day 2 Learning Agents, and Day 3 AI and Ethics. Delivery combined slides, animated videos, embedded demos, Google Colab notebooks, and quiz questions. The authors report that covering foundational modules first prepared students for more complex topics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Foundational modules covered first and foremost to prepare students for more complex topics
- Leveraging students' high school mathematics background for multi-layer perceptrons and neural networks
#### Constraints
- The four-hours-per-day, three-day length posed challenges to students; a few suggested increasing the number of days and decreasing the hours per day

### Target Learners
- high school students aged 14-19

### Learning Goals
- AI history and rational agents, search and supervised learning, neural networks and computer vision, prompt engineering, AI fairness, bias, and safety
