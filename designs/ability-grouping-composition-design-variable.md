# Ability-grouping composition as an instructional-design variable in collaborative learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The article frames grouping composition (homogeneous vs. heterogeneous) as a manipulable instructional-design variable within collaborative learning. Homogeneous groups can be "divided into high group of homogeneous, middle group of homogeneous, and the lower group of homogeneous", each with distinct psychological dynamics: low and middle homogeneous groups experience low competition and mental pressure but lack high-ability peers to push them upward, while high-ability groups face high competition. Heterogeneous grouping allows upper-ability members to contribute to lower-ability members but may weigh down the upper group. This framework organizes the study's comparison of four compositions.
