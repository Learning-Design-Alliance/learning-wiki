# Intro to Measurement introductory laboratory course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Intro to Measurement is an introductory laboratory course at UC Berkeley designed within the Compass Project, meeting "two hours once weekly for a 14-wee k semester (28 hours total)" with an additional three hours per week of expected out-of-class work. It engaged first-year undergraduates in iterative improvement of experiments and of self through three major activities: a semester-long thermal expansion activity, seven-week independent research projects, and guided weekly reflections. The second implementation enrolled 10 students with two graduate student co-instructors.
