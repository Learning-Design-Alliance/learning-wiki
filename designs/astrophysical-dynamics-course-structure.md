# Advanced research course combining multi-disciplinary lectures, a step-by-step exercise, and parallel expert seminars on hot topics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course design combined a lecture series on astrophysical dynamics covering gas dynamics in context with stellar dynamics and plasma physics, a single extended step-by-step problem on the birth and life of a galaxy, and parallel seminars on hot topics given by experts and open to a wide scientific audience. The author notes the course covered "modern research on astrophysical dynamics, and thus also of interest to" physicists, mathematicians and engineers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Experts willing to give parallel seminars on hot topics
- A public computer lab and multimedia equipment for seminar demonstrations
#### Constraints
- Designed for students with good background in physics and mathematics

### Target Learners
- graduate students
- senior researchers
- motivated undergraduate students

### Learning Goals
- understanding astrophysical dynamics across gas dynamics, stellar dynamics and plasma physics
