# TIDE: Tinkering, Ideas generation, Disciplinary connection, Exploration — a model for integrating tinkering with disciplinary learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
TIDE is a preliminary model combining open tinkering workshops with disciplinary learning. A tinkering workshop triggers student questions, generating a 'cloud of ideas' of possible explorations; the teacher then selects significant questions aligning with children's development and works with disciplinary tools, books and experiments, with the learning objective becoming evident to teacher and learners. The authors present it as a shift from the traditional instructionist trajectory of juxtaposed predefined experiences.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A tinkering workshop centred on a specific material or physical phenomenon so the resulting idea space is rich but relatively compact
- A teacher who, as Ciari states, knows how to choose suitable themes and discard unsuitable ones
- Tinkering must remain an open-ended experience, with the facilitator stance maintained during disciplinary investigations
#### Constraints
- The only risk of this approach is that the two phases become blurred
- The model is situated in a specific context and its validation is preliminary; full validation requires documenting children's ideas over time

### Target Learners
- primary school students

### Learning Goals
- authentic research question formulation
- nature of science understanding
- construction of scientific knowledge
