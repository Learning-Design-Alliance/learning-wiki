# Facebook virtual classroom with four learner-created content assignments

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The treatment was a closed Facebook group acting as a virtual classroom in which groups of four students created and shared content about assigned vocabulary. The wall feature let members "share their presentations, videos, photos and other documents" and comment on each other's posts. Four assignments structured the twelve-week treatment: Facebook presentations, blog e-portfolios, vocabulary exercises with quizzes, and creating or editing Wikipedia, Wikitravel and Wikihow entries.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A researcher-created closed Facebook group enrolling all students
- Groups of four assigned ten vocabularies per week
- An agenda covering the expected 500 vocabularies in three months
#### Constraints
- Membership was initially voluntary; only 16 of 23 students joined at first
- Mother-tongue (Turkish) commenting caused arguments and needed prohibiting

### Target Learners
- adult EFL university preparatory students

### Learning Goals
- English vocabulary knowledge at recognition level
