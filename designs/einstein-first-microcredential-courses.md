# Einstein-First teacher micro-credential courses (MC1 face-to-face and MC2 online)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Two 75-hour professional development micro-credential courses, "Einsteinian physics for primary school teachers" and "Einsteinian physics for secondary school teachers", each consisting of "37.5 contact hours and 37.5 hours of reading and homework", first delivered in January 2022 (MC1) and repeated online in August 2022 (MC2). Topics included photons, phonons, atoms and molecules, curved space, warped time, gravity, and gravitational waves. Learning was distributed between presentations, activities, self-study and preparation of a presentation; MC2 added pre-recorded videos, live tutorials and a two-day hybrid workshop. Assessment used questionnaires and live PPT presentations evaluated by two external markers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivery by presenters involved in creating the Einsteinian physics curriculum
- Activity-based learning with models and toys as the foundational design concept
#### Constraints
- One-day workshops provide only a narrow range of content; the MC courses were seen as offering limited time for reflective practice, which the authors suggest is overcome through repeated classroom implementation

### Target Learners
- Practising primary and secondary teachers, local, regional and international, with or without science backgrounds

### Learning Goals
- Content knowledge in Einsteinian physics
- Confidence and age-appropriate pedagogy for teaching Einstein-First modules
