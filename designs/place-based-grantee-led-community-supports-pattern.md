# Place-based grantee-led implementation of community support programs for children in poverty

## Description
Promise Neighborhoods are implemented locally by lead agencies that receive federal funding and choose which services to offer their community. The pattern pairs flexible, locally selected services with a common cradle-to-career goal; case studies documented "the services they chose to offer, and the early successes and challenges they had in improving the lives of children in their community."
