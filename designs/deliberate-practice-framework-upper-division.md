# Deliberate practice framework for designing active learning tasks in upper-division physics courses

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors adapt deliberate practice from educational psychology as a framework giving specificity to active learning design: "Deliberate practice [13] is the intense explicit practice with timely guiding feedback of the speciﬁc skills that make up expert performa nce in a discipline." Their refinement targets "the speciﬁc cognitive tasks that make up t he desired expertise in physics," identified via cognitive task analysis.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires cognitive task analysis of the relevant expert thinking to identify the component cognitive skills that should be practiced
- Requires selection of tasks that provide students practice at a level of appropriate challenge
#### Constraints
- Developed and tested in a specialized upper-division optics course; the authors argue it could be applied to most upper-division physics courses with a similar mathematical base

### Target Learners
- upper-division undergraduate physics students

### Learning Goals
- expert-like quantitative problem solving
- blending conceptual and quantitative skills
