# Stateful Diesel Exercise (SDE): an adaptive gamified thermodynamics exercise on the Diesel cycle

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 unmarked) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The SDE is a single extensive Stateful exercise describing the thermodynamics of a four-stroke Diesel engine, built from "review loops from the prototypal shapes in Fig. 2" only. Its "overall structure of the exercise is linear, but elaborative feedback in the form of hints help the student forward": on a wrong answer the student enters a review loop of MCQs and then retries a modified main scene with the loop's results available. A "simplistic grading logic" awarded one point per successful transition to the C-scenes, six points in total, shown as a cumulative real-time score.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Elaborative feedback administered after incorrect answers, which the article notes previous studies show is beneficial for learning
#### Constraints
- The article notes the exercise is far too restricted to be regarded as a true e-learning game
- Time-stamp intervals must be interpreted cautiously since presence and focus are unknown

### Target Learners
- first-year university mechanical and civil engineering students in thermodynamics

### Learning Goals
- understanding of the Diesel cycle and thermodynamic processes: adiabatic, isobaric, and isochoric processes, heat, work, and efficiency
