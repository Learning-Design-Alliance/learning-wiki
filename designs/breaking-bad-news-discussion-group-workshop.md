# Multidisciplinary discussion-group workshop on breaking bad news with videos, dialogue and collective guide development

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An active, participatory learning workshop prepared with a clinical psychologist, a social worker, physicians and an expert patient. It "comprised four phases": viewing three videos about breaking bad news, discussion and dialogue after the videos, "development of a guide on good practice in breaking bad news through collective intelligence", and distribution of copies of the S-P-w-ICE-S guidelines. The group comprised a clinical psychologist, a social worker, two physicians and the students.
