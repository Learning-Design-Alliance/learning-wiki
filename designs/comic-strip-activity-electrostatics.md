# Comic strip activity: drawing charge stories across five worksheet sections with individual, small-group, and whole-group sharing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A two-day, open-ended worksheet activity in a summer physics course in which "students were asked to draw comic strips that showed interactions between positive and negative charges as if they were characters in a cartoon series". Five consecutive worksheet sections moved from four-frame stories to single-frame tasks, and "the activity was structured to accommodate individual work" interspersed with small-group and whole-group sharing. Overall, "students spent 80 minutes on this activity" across the two days.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- a worksheet specifying the first scene of each story so students start from a common scenario
#### Constraints
- Sections 3-5 shift to single-frame tasks that "aim to probe students' thinking about electric fields" rather than the electric forces probed in the earlier story sections

### Target Learners
- high school students in an algebra-based physics summer course

### Learning Goals
- expressing ideas about electrostatic phenomena prior to formal instruction
