# LSPPDM six-step pedagogical framework for procedural skills training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The 'Learn, See, Practice, Prove, Do, Maintain' framework is an evidence-based pedagogical framework developed through review and synthesis of the literature for teaching procedural skills. The article describes it as "a step-by-step process consisting of six steps", divided into a cognitive stage (learn, see) and a psychomotor phase (practice, prove, do, maintain), progressing from lectures, demonstrations and video through simulator practice and summative assessment to supervised clinical practice and lifelong maintenance. In this trial it structured a laparoscopic training course for surgical residents.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trainees progress sequentially from 'virtual simulation' to 'live animal' and ultimately to 'clinical practice', with simulator-based assessment passed before operating on patients under supervision
#### Constraints
- The 'maintain' step is carried out throughout the trainee's career, so full-framework effects extend beyond a single course

### Target Learners
- first- and second-year surgical residents

### Learning Goals
- laparoscopic procedural skills
- non-technical surgical skills
