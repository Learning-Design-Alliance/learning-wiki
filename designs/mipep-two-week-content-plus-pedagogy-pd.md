# Two-week intensive summer PD pairing university faculty content instruction with master-teacher pedagogy sessions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
MIPEP is a two-week intensive summer professional development program for in-service Texas high school physics teachers with little or no physics background. Faculty deliver day-long, TEKS-aligned content sessions contributing 66 contact hours, while 'late afternoon and evening sessions led by two master teachers who focus on pedagogy and pedagogical content knowledge' follow each content day, alongside laboratory activities, enrichment activities and community-building.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- In-service teachers assigned to teach physics, selected with little to no physics background (0-2 college-level physics courses)
- Both university physics faculty and experienced master high school physics teachers on the delivery team
#### Constraints
- Content aligned to the Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills (TEKS) state criteria
- Residential two-week summer format supported by campus lodging, meals, travel reimbursement and funder funding

### Target Learners
- in-service high school physics teachers with little or no physics background

### Learning Goals
- physics content knowledge
- research-based instructional strategies
- confidence in teaching physics
