# Educational programs for additive manufacturing should be multidisciplinary

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors draw from their course experience the recommendation that AM education not be confined to mechanical engineering. They state: "We therefore believe that, while rooted in mechanical engineering and materials science, AM education is truly multidisciplinary." Their rationale is that cutting-edge AM spans materials science, machine design, fluid mechanics, heat transfer, imaging, and computation, and that in the capstone project teamwork among students with complementary backgrounds was highly correlated to the sophistication and novelty of their prototypes. Educational programs on AM processes and applications should therefore embrace multidisciplinary context.

## Design Implications
