# Systems Review working agreements that ground teams in an inquiry-based, systems-focused stance

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
R15CC developed Systems Review Working Agreements that "guide all Systems Review projects" and delineate how teams commit to working together on improving the system. The six agreements are: stay focused on the system; prioritize equity of voice (all teach, all learn); keep an open mind with an inquiry focus and give activities a genuine try; experience discomfort and treat struggles as learning opportunities; be present and participate with a right to process; and acknowledge impact and consider intent. Facilitators verbally add italicized elaborations when working with groups.
