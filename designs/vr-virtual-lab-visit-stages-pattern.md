# Virtual visit pattern retracing the stages of an on-site research-laboratory visit

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The activity is a roughly ninety-minute session, deliverable "in a dedicated University room, or directly in the classroom", that mirrors a real lab visit: a slide-based welcome and introduction recalling school topics such as electromagnetism plus newer content (Standard Model, cross section, particle decay, Belle II's goals); a guided entry into the detector with a researcher piloting while the class watches on a large screen; and first-person student exploration with researchers answering questions. The authors state the proposal "retraces all the stages of an on-site visit" and that researchers share emotions and experiences to paint a realistic picture of their working environment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Researchers to carry out the activity and guide the virtual visit, with one researcher per active VR station
#### Constraints
- Only a limited number of students can be in VR at once while others watch classmates, and some participants' role can feel too passive

### Target Learners
- High school class groups

### Learning Goals
- Bringing students closer to scientific research and STEM careers, and visualising physics phenomena otherwise impossible to see
