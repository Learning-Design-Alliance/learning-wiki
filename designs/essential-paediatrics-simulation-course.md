# Essential Paediatrics simulation course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The simulation-based intervention studied: “Essential Paediatrics is a half-day course that runs throughout the academic year” for fifth-year medical students. Each session pairs an interactive human factors session and pre-simulation briefing with simulated paediatric emergencies (sepsis, arrythmia or seizures) managed in pairs, followed by a group debrief covering technical skills, non-technical skills and emotive aspects.
