# Five-step learning sequence from powers of two to the scale of the Universe

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The module follows a five-step progression taught through 13 activities: exponential intuition via powers of two (doubling/halving), extension to extreme numbers, a bridge from powers of two to powers of ten via the approximation 2^10 ~ 10^3, approximation skills with powers of ten arithmetic, and finally placing the entire scale of the Universe on a logarithmic number line. The article states "The 5 conceptual steps are taught through 13 activities listed in Table 2", with each step providing skills for the next.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Activities connect to real-life examples from science and everyday situations, with lesson structure prioritising activities
#### Constraints
- The sequence was trialled as a 6-hour progression in three schools with students aged 7-13

### Target Learners
- primary school students aged 7-13

### Learning Goals
- positioning and reading numbers on the logarithmic number line
- proportionality between pairs of numbers
- mathematical operations with powers of ten
- estimation by rounding to powers of ten
