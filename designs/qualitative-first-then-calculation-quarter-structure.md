# Qualitative-first quarter structure: concept introduction and qualitative discussion in the first 60% of the quarter, calculation practice on all topics in the final 40%

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
This pattern reorganizes a course so that, for each topic, the instructor first introduces ideas and develops qualitative understanding, deferring computationally complicated problem solving. As the article puts it, the treatment "takes steps i) and ii) for each topic and puts them into the first 60% of the school quarter and then puts step iii) in the final 40% of the quarter." Weeks 7-10 were devoted to review and calculations across all topics.
