# Single-session simulation-based ultrasound-guided CVC placement training program for pediatric providers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
An educational resource for pediatric providers responsible for CVC placement (residents, fellows, attendings, APPs) that teaches US-guided CVC placement in a single 2- to 3-hour session with a 1:2 facilitator-to-learner ratio. It was "structured around simulation of CVC placement on a task trainer" and "designed to include multiple active learning components to encourage participant engagement." It uniquely covers pediatric catheter size selection and all three primary sites: femoral, internal jugular, and subclavian veins.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Skilled facilitators including attending physicians, senior fellows, and senior APPs; specialized equipment including task trainers and US machines; prework assignments completed 1 week before the session
#### Constraints
- The evaluation was conducted at a single center with a relatively small number of learners (27); the authors note the resource "depends on skilled facilitators and specialized equipment"

### Target Learners
- PCCM fellows
- PEM fellows
- PCCM advanced practice providers
- pediatric residents and attending physicians

### Learning Goals
- US-guided CVC placement technique
- site and size selection
- sterile technique
- Seldinger technique
