# Organize the event around one overall theme with three lectures, mixing mathematics with real-life problems or geometric ideas

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each SK Day is organized with one overall theme and three lectures around it. The authors report that "mixing mathematics with real life problems or geometric ideas is a great way to get students interested". Themes they have run include Games on Surfaces, Games of Chance, Knots and Graphs, Mathematics in the sea, and Mathematics and Magic, each with three subtopics such as billiards, non-transitive dice, graph colouring, fractal coastlines, and flexagons.
