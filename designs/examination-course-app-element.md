# Course-specific app for anamnesis and physical examination used as blended-learning preparation and review material

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The intervention tool is "an app specifically created for the anamnesis and physical examination course", a smartphone application letting students view learning content organized by organ system, with each organ system's examination divided into inspection, auscultation, palpation and interpretation of unusual findings, and explained with descriptive images. It served as pre- and post-course material replacing paper handouts, and students reported satisfaction with its usability and design. The authors note advantages of offline availability and updatable content.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students needed an "iPhone" or "iPad" to participate, since the app ran only on Apple devices
#### Constraints
- At the time of the study the app did not include android support; the authors state expansion to android devices and addition of interactive content, videos, and regular updates is planned

### Target Learners
- medical students in the clinical phase of study

### Learning Goals
- history taking (anamnesis)
- physical examination techniques by organ system
