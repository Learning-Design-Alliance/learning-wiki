# Prognosis training brochure with epidemiological survival data for the 21 most common oncological diseases

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A printed resource central to the training intervention: "The prognosis training consisted of a brochure designed to support the physicians with the aid of epidemiological data on the 21 most common oncological diseases of the clinic." For each disease it gave median survival overall and for subgroups by age, stage or mutation status, with Kaplan-Meier curves showing absolute and relative survival. Sources included the Munich Tumor Registry, UpToDate and clinical or epidemiological studies. The data served as numerical anchors during conscious prognostication.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to registry or published epidemiological survival data for the relevant diseases
- Subgroup breakdowns (age, stage, mutation status) with corresponding Kaplan-Meier curves
#### Constraints
- Built from the specific clinic's 21 most common diseases, so anchor data are population-specific

### Target Learners
- physicians in training (residents)
- board-certified oncologists

### Learning Goals
- anchored clinical prognostication
- interpretation of survival curves
