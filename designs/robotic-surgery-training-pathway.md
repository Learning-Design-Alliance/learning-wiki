# Sequential proficiency-based pathway for robotic surgical training from observation to independent practice

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review proposes a structured, competency-based training pathway for novice robotic surgeons. It "usually involves sequential progression from observation, case assisting, acquisition of basic robotic skills in the dry and wet lab setting along with achievement of individual and team-based non-technical skills, modular console training under supervision, and finally independent practice". Progression is gated by assessment of competency at every level, with proficiency and safety demonstrated in basic robotic skills and procedural tasks before modular training and sign-off.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assessment of competency at every level before progression
- Demonstration of proficiency and safety in basic robotic skills and procedural tasks prior to independent practice
#### Constraints
- The review notes no metrics such as number of procedures or duration of patient-side assistance are available for the patient-side stage

### Target Learners
- novice robotic surgeons
- surgical residents and trainees

### Learning Goals
- basic robotic console skills
- patient-side assisting skills
- non-technical and team skills
- procedural competence
