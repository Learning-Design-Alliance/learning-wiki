# Signal Corps experimental mobile color television unit

## Description
An experimental production unit built for Army television research on color instruction. The article states: "The Signal Corps operates an experimental mobile color unit which consists of three color cameras and associated equipment mounted in an air-conditioned van." Transistor use makes the cameras lighter than predecessors though bulkier than standard image orthicons. It supported the color-versus-monochrome teaching study at Fort Monmouth and Huntsville.
