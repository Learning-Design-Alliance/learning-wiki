# Directly measured maximal oxygen uptake test protocol for assessing cardiorespiratory fitness in adolescents

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study, `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The study assessed cardiorespiratory fitness with an incremental treadmill test to volitional exhaustion, using a metabolic system with a 4.2-L mixing chamber. Oxygen uptake was recorded at 30-s intervals, and VO2max was defined when two of three criteria were satisfied: attaining a VO2-plateau, attaining a HR >90% of age-predicted maximum, and attaining a respiratory exchange ratio >1.1. The average of the two highest consecutive 30-s VO2 values was recorded as individual VO2max.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- 10-min warm-up with gradient of 1.7% on a motorized treadmill
- Incremental protocol with treadmill-gradient of 5.3% and start velocity of 8 km/h, speed increased 1 km/h each minute until volitional exhaustion
- Full system calibration before testing each group and regular volume calibration with a 3 L syringe
#### Constraints
- Participants were excluded if diagnosed with heart disease, asthma or any disease potentially affecting the cardiorespiratory system
- Participants were asked not to engage in strenuous physical exercise the day before the physical test

### Target Learners
- healthy male high-school students

### Learning Goals
- cardiorespiratory fitness measurement
