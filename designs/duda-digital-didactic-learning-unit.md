# D-UDA: a digital didactic learning unit combining an escape room with multisensory games for fifth-grade mathematics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
D-UDA (unità didattica di apprendimento digitale) is a website-based digital learning unit for fifth-grade mathematics, offered during the first Italian COVID-19 lockdown. The article states that "The D-UDA experience was divided into two parts: the virtual classroom and the virtual archeological site." The first part contains logic puzzles aligned with INVALSI national test frameworks; the second contains multisensory Scratch games, a Domus escape room, and a robot-programming activity in OpenRoberta. It was integrated into the curriculum in agreement with teachers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Built only with freely available technologies such as Scratch and G-Suite to foster the inclusion of children with economic difficulties
- Conducted under supervision of at least one teacher and the experimenter who intervened in case students encountered difficulties
#### Constraints
- Tools used are very basic and not easily usable on all browsers

### Target Learners
- Fifth-grade primary school children, including those with sensory impairments or economic difficulties

### Learning Goals
- Fifth-grade mathematics (numbers; space and figures; relations, data and forecasts)
- Transversal competencies such as cooperation and engagement
