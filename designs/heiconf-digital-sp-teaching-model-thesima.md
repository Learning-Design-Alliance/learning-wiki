# HeiConf-based digital SP teaching model at TheSiMa (Mannheim)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A pandemic-era conversion of SP-based communication teaching at the Mannheim learning environment TheSiMa. SPs sat alone in a hygiene-compliant room in the learning environment and joined lessons digitally via a laptop, while students connected from home using the conference software HeiConf, "which is considered to be secure for data protection reasons". From May to August 2020, 176 teaching units of 45 minutes each were run with 354 students from academic years 3–5 and 18 SPs portraying 15 different complex roles, each unit including a 10–30-minute SP conversation followed by structured feedback from SPs, fellow students and lecturers, with conversations recorded.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- SPs need a separate hygiene-compliant room in the learning environment with a laptop for digital participation
- A data-protection-secure online conference platform (HeiConf) for students to join from home
- Structured feedback from SPs, fellow students and lecturers after each conversation
#### Constraints
- No physical contact between SPs and students
- Non-verbal aspects, group dynamics and atmosphere could not be addressed to the same extent as in classroom teaching

### Target Learners
- Medical students in academic years 3, 4 and 5

### Learning Goals
- Medical conversation techniques such as NURSE and SPIKES
- Doctor–patient communication skills
