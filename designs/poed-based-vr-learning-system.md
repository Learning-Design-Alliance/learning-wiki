# POED-based VR learning system built on UPTALENT SVVR with two-tier diagnostic testing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A spherical video-based virtual reality (SVVR) learning system implemented with UPTALENT for a junior high school natural science course. The article states the system "consisted of a POEE database, a two-tier test database, a learning material database, an SVVR scene database, and a learning process database". Students predict experimental results, watch experiment videos, explain principles via microphone, and take a two-tier test; the system "automatically determined whether the answer was correct or incorrect, and then proceeded with the corresponding instructional action".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher-designed two-tier diagnostic questions and supplementary learning materials stored in the system databases
#### Constraints
- The article notes traditional VR application in basic education remains limited due to high development costs and technical complexity, which SVVR addresses as a more accessible alternative

### Target Learners
- eighth-grade junior high school students

### Learning Goals
- learning the factors affecting chemical reaction rate through inquiry-based VR activities
