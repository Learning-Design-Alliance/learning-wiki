# 360°-VR shoulder dystocia scenario video (2 min 50 s, GoPro Max capture, Quest 2 headset)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An immersive 360°-VR scenario developed to explore all obstetrical maneuvers needed to manage shoulder dystocia, lasting "2 min and 50 s". It was scripted and approved by obstetrics experts, filmed with a GoPro Max 360 camera placed centrally in the delivery room, and viewed on a standalone Meta Quest 2 headset with 4k resolution at a 75-Hz refresh rate, allowing participants to move freely within the room. The script depicts a G1P0 patient with gestational diabetes at 40+2 weeks whose delivery reveals the turtle sign, walking through the HELP-RER maneuvers including the Gaskin and Wood maneuvers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A standalone VR headset such as the Quest 2; no computer connection or additional sensors are needed while still offering wireless connection for a live view
#### Constraints
- The 360° technology is non-interactive, providing immersive experience through non-interactive visual and auditory observations, and the training lacked a haptic modality

### Target Learners
- resident and attending physicians
- midwives
- final-year medical students

### Learning Goals
- recognizing shoulder dystocia and executing the modified ALSO (HELP-RER) maneuver sequence
