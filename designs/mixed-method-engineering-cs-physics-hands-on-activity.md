# Four-hour hands-on mixed-method activity pairing nuclear engineering and computer science with basic physics concepts

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's educational design adopts "a mixed method that is based on applying nuclear engineering concepts and computer science tools to explore in-situ environmental radioactivity", structured as two 4-hour hands-on experiments: an indoor propaedeutic experiment on gamma photons and spectra, and an outdoor multi-measurement survey. Each combines theoretical background, hands-on setup operations, data analysis, and critical synthesis, with students working collaboratively in teams to extract quantitative information.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A relatively accessible and affordable detector (4” × 4” NaI(Tl)), multiplatform software (Android, Windows), and a supporting website with materials for teachers and students
#### Constraints
- Activities are addressed to a group of 5–8 students and were tested with pre-collegiate students and teachers of Italian high schools

### Target Learners
- pre-collegiate students

### Learning Goals
- comprehension of basic physics concepts of environmental radioactivity
- problem-solving attitude including analysis design, critical thinking, communication, and teamwork
