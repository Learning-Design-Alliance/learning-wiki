# Multi-faceted assessment framework weighting individual assignments, group projects, participation, and peer evaluation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course uses a four-component assessment scheme: "Individual Assignments (40% of final grade) consist of nine targeted assignments, one per major topic"; Collaborative Group Projects (40%) involve two team endeavors in consistent groups of four; Active Participation (10%) covers class exercises and discussions; and Peer Collaboration Evaluation (10%) has students assess group members' contributions and communication. The structure applies foundational concepts before building with advanced technologies.
