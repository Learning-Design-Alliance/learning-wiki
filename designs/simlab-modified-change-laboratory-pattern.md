# SimLab: a condensed four-session Change Laboratory pattern combining in-situ simulation with facilitated expansive learning discussions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
SimLab adapts the Change Laboratory formative intervention — typically up to ten-plus sessions — into four sessions designed "for the demands of the general practice workplace". Each session pairs an in-situ simulation or CHAT-guided group interview with facilitation of double stimulation: video footage of the simulation and observations serve as mirror data, while the activity system model, laminated guidelines and prior-session summaries serve as second stimuli. Reflection walls and diaries capture disturbances and aha moments between sessions. The pattern is described as potentially transferable to other clinical settings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires mirror data (ISS video footage, guidelines, prior-session summaries), a researcher interventionist facilitator, and flexible reflection tools between sessions
#### Constraints
- Designed and evaluated in a time-poor general practice setting; sessions ran at lunchtime when the practice was closed

### Target Learners
- interprofessional primary care teams

### Learning Goals
- expansive learning and transformative agency
- team-based emergency care preparedness
