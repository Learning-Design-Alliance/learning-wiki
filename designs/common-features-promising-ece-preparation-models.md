# Six common features of promising early educator preparation models

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Across three case studies targeting different workforce segments, the report identifies shared design features: "Local early educator pipelines and pathways," relationship-building among students, instructors, and staff; "Opportunities for clinical practice in feedback-rich environments"; multifaceted student supports; well-supported and diverse staffs of instructors, advisors, and coaches; and community partnerships and funding to strengthen preparation. The report suggests these features may be developed locally and supported at the state level.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dedicated staff who understand their local context, plus partnerships and funding that provide financial supports, student teaching opportunities, and classroom space
#### Constraints
- The features were observed in three California case study programs and New Jersey's state-level example; the report presents them as promising rather than proven at scale

### Target Learners
- Diverse current and future early educators of children birth to age 5

### Learning Goals
- Effective early childhood teaching practice, credential and degree attainment, and persistence through preparation
