# Physics-as-theater: students act out historical scientific parody to inhabit the roles of past physicists

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In this pattern, a historical scientific text is staged with students playing the roles of the physicists involved, linking names introduced in earlier lectures to a narrative cast. NE290 reserved class time for students to "act out (over zoom) selected passages" from Max Delbrück's 1932 parody The Blegdamsvej Faust, aiming to provide an immersive environment where 21st-century nuclear engineering students could don the mantle of 20th-century physicists. The fable was used throughout the course as an emphasis of the complex bargains inherent to nuclear physicists and as a lens for exploring nuclear history in literature.
