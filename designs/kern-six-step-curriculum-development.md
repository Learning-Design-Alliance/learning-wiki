# Kern's six-step approach to curriculum development as a framework for designing medical education tools

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Kern's six-step approach is a widely used medical education framework comprising problem identification and general needs assessment, targeted needs assessment, formulation of goals and objectives, selection of educational strategies, implementation, and evaluation and feedback. The article used it as the organizing structure for building the MiLeMed curriculum, reporting one results section per step. It grounds design choices in assessed needs before strategy selection.
