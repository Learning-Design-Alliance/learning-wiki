# Five research-based tutorials on Coulomb's law, superposition, symmetry and Gauss's law

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A set of five tutorials for calculus-based introductory physics: the first two cover Coulomb's law, superposition and symmetry for discrete and continuous charge distributions; the third targets distinguishing electric flux from field; the fourth and fifth address symmetry and Gauss's law and revisit superposition. The tutorials "guided students to understand the vector nature of the electric field, learn the superposition principle and recognize the symmetry of the charge distribution," with common misconceptions explicitly elicited via paired student discussions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administered after traditional instruction on the relevant concepts, in groups of two or three students, with pre-/post-tests accompanying each tutorial
#### Constraints
- Complementary to, not replacements for, the University of Washington tutorials on related topics, focusing on symmetry ideas

### Target Learners
- calculus-based introductory physics students

### Learning Goals
- functional understanding of Coulomb's law, superposition, symmetry, electric field vs. flux, and Gauss's law
