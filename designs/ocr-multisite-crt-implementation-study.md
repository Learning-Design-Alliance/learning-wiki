# Multi-site cluster randomized trial with embedded implementation study of OCR at scale

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The evaluation combined a multi-site cluster randomized trial (CRT) and an implementation study across 49 elementary schools (kindergarten through 5th grade) in 7 districts, with approximately 4,500 students and 1,200 teachers per year. Schools were "randomly assigned to treatment (N=25) and comparison (N=24)" blocking at the district level, with districts recruited over a 3-year timeframe. Year 1 focused on kindergarten and third grade cohorts; Year 2 on first and fourth grade cohorts, assessing approximately 50 students per school per cohort in fall and spring. Outcomes were measured with the GRADE and Student Motivation Form, and fidelity was captured through observations, interviews, and surveys.
