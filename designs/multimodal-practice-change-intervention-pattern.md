# Multimodal practice-change intervention combining training, coaching, goal setting, social support and prompts mapped to behaviour change techniques

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The intervention employed "a range of behavioural change techniques as defined by Michie et al." mapped onto different TDF domains, including goal setting, goal review, social support, social comparison, homework tasks, role play, instruction and prompts. Practitioners attended a one-day training session and then received regular coaching and support in a community of practice (lunchtime learning collaborative), with co-designed posters and postcards as prompts. Asking about adversity was adapted to clinical practice to not burden time-limited practitioners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Regular coaching and support in a community of practice following initial training
- Mapped referral pathways so practitioners have services to respond to
#### Constraints
- Single interventions such as education alone are rarely effective in achieving sustained practice change, per the article's background

### Target Learners
- health practitioners
- social care practitioners

### Learning Goals
- directly asking about childhood adversity and responding to identified adversity
