# Gatekeeper training develops individuals' abilities to recognize psychological distress, approach those they are concerned about, and refer them to mental health support services.

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article defines gatekeeper training as "teaching individuals to recognize the signs of psychological distress in others and to be able to approach and talk with those they are concerned about for the purpose of making a referral to mental health support services." The United Nations has recommended it as an important part of an effective suicide-prevention strategy. In this study, faculty, staff, and administrators completed an online simulation teaching them to recognize distress in student veterans, engage them in conversation, and motivate them to seek mental health support services.
