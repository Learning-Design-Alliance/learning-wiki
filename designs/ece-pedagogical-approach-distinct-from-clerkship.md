# Early clinical exposure as a pedagogical approach distinct from traditional clinical training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
ECE is "an approach that integrates clinical experiences into the early years of medical training." The review emphasizes that "ECE is conceptually different from traditional clinical training, since it entails early, often observational exposure aimed at contextualizing theoretical information rather than the organized clerkships of subsequent years." It is not merely an earlier clerkship but a technique meant to integrate theoretical learning with actual exposure, with forms including real patient contact, case-based learning, shadowing, and pre-clerkship education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- supervision by clinicians, faculty members, or residents during exposure activities
#### Constraints
- organization and timing of clinical education vary substantially among nations; ECE modalities include community placements, clinician shadowing, or organized skills workshops

### Target Learners
- medical students in the early (pre-clinical) phase of training

### Learning Goals
- contextualizing theoretical knowledge
- preparation for real-world clinical practice
