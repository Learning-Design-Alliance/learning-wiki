# Modular, adaptable ethics education resources facilitable by non-experts across audiences and course formats

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
QEP designs its materials as modular units adaptable to varied backgrounds, levels, and available course time — from a one-hour lecture to a full-semester course — because a one-size-fits-all model of quantum ethics education can never be truly scalable. Prior instructor interviews found that "instructors requested modular resources that could be incorporated into a lecture or two of a quantum technologi es course coupled with simple instructions for facilitation b y non-experts." The pattern pairs modular content with an interactive, (semi)-non-hierarchical model of learning in which the instructor acts as facilitator and students and instructor challenge one another through discussion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Simple facilitation instructions so non-experts can run the materials; adaptability across backgrounds, levels, and available course time
#### Constraints
- Retrofitting ethics into existing courses is described as one of the easiest near-term ways to scale, implying limits on standalone adoption

### Target Learners
- quantum technologies course students at any level
- instructors without ethics expertise

### Learning Goals
- engagement with quantum ethics within existing quantum courses
