# Peirce's three-category semiotic sign taxonomy (iconic, index, symbol) as a basis for selecting infographic signs

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article applies semiotic theory to educational infographics, stating that "Semiotic signs have been classified by Charles Sanders Peirce; who adopted three main classifications of signs: iconic, index and symbol". Icons resemble the object represented (e.g., desktop screen icons); indexes indicate an object's location or relation without resembling it (e.g., smoke indicating fire); symbols carry information and alerts through a non-resembling signifier-object relationship. The study used these three categories, singly or in mutual links, when selecting semiotic forms for infographic lesson designs.
