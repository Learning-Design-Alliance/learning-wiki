# Ten-week multimodal stimulation programme with three one-hour sessions per week

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The intervention programme is a lesson-level design in which Grade 1 learners participate in a variety of activities specially planned to stimulate the pedagogical factors identified in the literature survey. "The programme was presented over a period of ten (10) weeks at a rate of three (3) one hour sessions per week", held from 9:00-10:00 in the morning. Activities developed skills, knowledge and values and involved learners "both in individual and group capacity", while the rest of the school programme continued as usual for both groups.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A presenter familiar with the programme's construction and with Foundation Phase teacher education
- permission from school authorities and parents
#### Constraints
- Presented in an English-medium school in a medium-sized South African city with a multicultural class

### Target Learners
- Grade 1 learners aged 6-7

### Learning Goals
- Stimulation of perceptual, motor, emotional, language and conceptual development for more effective learning
