# Thermodynamics focus-group session: small-group brainstorming alternated with moderated slide discussion

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An outreach session format positioned by the authors halfway between an experiment and a focus group, between a quiz and a game, to focus on sustainability and the laws of thermodynamics. High-school students are divided into groups of about 5 persons equipped with a post-it block and a pen; groups brainstorm keywords about sustainability, ecology and energy and present them to the audience, while researchers moderate, alternating brainstorming with slides showing plots and diagrams.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A moderator from the research team
- post-it blocks and pens for each group of about 5 students
- slides with plots and diagrams introducing and analyzing the concepts
#### Constraints
- Designed and tested with high-school students and public science-event audiences; survey attendance was voluntary and typically 20/30% of students.

### Target Learners
- high school students
- science-festival and public audiences

### Learning Goals
- thermodynamics principles
- reversibility, efficiency and entropy
- sustainability, waste and energy economics
