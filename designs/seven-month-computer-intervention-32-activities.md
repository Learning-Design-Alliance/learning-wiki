# Seven-month integrated computer intervention with 32 activities using open-ended and close-ended software

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The intervention was a program of 32 computer activities (four software packages, eight activities each) designed by the researcher and implemented with two teachers over seven months. It followed an integration philosophy in which "the computer was 'one more centre' in the preschool class and the extension of curriculum areas was attempted". Software included open-ended programs (Paint, Kid Pix Deluxe 4) for exploring and creating, and close-ended programs (Adiboo, Putt-Putt Joins the Circus) for mouse control and curriculum-linked activities. Preschool children participated twice weekly for 20-30 minutes; preps once weekly for 45 minutes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers were given lesson plans, examples and written and electronic instructions in advance, with regular researcher contact to ensure consistency of delivery
#### Constraints
- Software selection was limited by availability, usability and teachers' prior unfamiliarity with the programs

### Target Learners
- children aged 44-79 months in preschool and prep classes

### Learning Goals
- familiarity with computers as tools integrated across curriculum areas
- mouse control
- curriculum skills such as shapes, letters, numbers and animals
