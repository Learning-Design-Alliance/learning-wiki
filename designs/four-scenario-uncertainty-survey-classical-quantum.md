# Four-scenario uncertainty survey spanning physics paradigm and theoretical expected outcome

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors developed a survey presenting four experimental scenarios — projectile motion, Stern-Gerlach, Brownian motion, and single-slit — that "span the possible combinations of physics paradigm (classical or quantum) and theoretical expected outcome (single value or distribution)." Each scenario included a text description, a diagram, and a histogram of fictitious results, followed by an open prompt to list sources of uncertainty, plus generic questions about uncertainty in classical and quantum mechanics. Responses were coded as limitations or principles using a scheme with Cohen's kappa of 0.85.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Scenario descriptions must be vague enough not to limit student thinking yet specific enough to ensure students understand how the experiment might be conducted
#### Constraints
- Each participant saw only two of the four scenarios to reduce survey completion time

### Target Learners
- Upper-level undergraduate physics students who have taken or are taking quantum mechanics

### Learning Goals
- Identifying sources of measurement uncertainty in classical and quantum experiments
