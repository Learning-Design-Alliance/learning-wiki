# Learning Management Model Based on Constructivist Theory integrating reasoning strategies for critical thinking

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article develops a learning management model grounded in Constructivist Theory, in which students construct knowledge by associating new experience with prior knowledge, and integrates reasoning strategies for enhancing critical thinking into science instruction. It was built through a Research and Development process and structured following Joyce, Weil and Calhoun's model of teaching, including "the basic rationale, approach, and theory", objectives, implementation steps, social system, principle of response, and support system.
