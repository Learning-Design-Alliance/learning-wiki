# Deliver supplemental fraction tutoring in small groups three times weekly for 12 weeks

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q4` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The developer recommends "students use Fraction Face-Off! three times a week for 12 weeks for about 30 minutes per session." This dosage was the schedule under which the program's positive effects were demonstrated: in the contributing study, the intervention was delivered three times per week for 12 weeks between September/October and early February, in small groups at a three-to-one ratio, typically during class time or a pull-out time to supplement the math curriculum.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schools must provide classroom space if the intervention is not implemented in the student's regular classroom, and make copies of materials and manipulatives for all participating students
#### Constraints
- The recommended dosage is three 30- to 35-minute lessons per week; the study delivered sessions during one of three instructional periods of typically 50, 20, or 45 minutes

### Target Learners
- fourth-grade students who have demonstrated difficulty in mathematics

### Learning Goals
- solving fraction problems
- understanding fraction magnitude
