# USAFA Quantitative Reasoning/Evening Tutoring Center

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A faculty-staffed after-hours tutoring center at the United States Air Force Academy offering extra instruction from 1800-2200 hours, Sunday-Thursday, in first- and second-year courses in Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and Engineering Mechanics. It is "staffed by four faculty instructors (one in each discipline)" with three PhDs, one M.S., and a combined 100 years of prior teaching experience, each also teaching one section of a core course. In math, ten weekly hours are course-specific walk-in group sessions and ten hours are individualized sessions booked via an online SharePoint calendar.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Staffing by qualified faculty-level instructors who also teach the courses they tutor
- A scheduling mechanism (online calendar) to allocate individualized sessions on a first-come, first-served basis
#### Constraints
- Maintaining adequate service is challenging at the current staffing level as demand increases

### Target Learners
- first and second year undergraduate cadets in quantitative core courses

### Learning Goals
- quantitative problem solving in mathematics, physics, chemistry, and engineering mechanics core courses
