# Activity 1: managing the future of coasts using the Surging Seas Risk Zone Map

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A problem-based learning activity in which high school students explore sea level rise impacts on New York City using the 'Surging Seas: Risk Zone Map', which "offers the opportunity to explore inundation risk of a sea level rise of up to 30 m across the world's coastlines". Students follow an identify-model-specify-decide sequence: identifying map features, modelling potential impacts, specifying affected areas and communities, and determining adaptation strategies such as relocating people or raising sea defences.
