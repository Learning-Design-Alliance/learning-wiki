# Extracting astrophysical information from simulated gravitational-wave signals activity

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A classroom activity casting students as gravitational-wave astronomers who use scientific theory to infer the physical system behind a detected signal. Students "use simulated signal measurements and relevant equations to determine the chirp masses and astronomical distances of the systems, and experience the scientific process that will be the underpinnings of gravitational-wave astronomy."
