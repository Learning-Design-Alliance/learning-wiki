# O2O/PBL teaching process for nephrology residency training (Ding Talk platform with pre-class materials, in-class problem-based study groups, and post-class live playback)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a blended teaching process combining online and offline components: instructors upload "online teaching materials such as operation and theoretical courses" and "specific clinical problems" for roughly 30 minutes of pre-class self-study; during class, trainees in study groups of 2 to 3 discuss the pre-class problems while Ding Talk live broadcast runs alongside face-to-face teaching on smart electronic screens; after class, live playback is available for repeated viewing. The LBT comparison group received traditional classroom teaching with no pre-class materials, no problem-based discussion, and no live broadcast.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Attending nephrologist instructors with at least 3 years of department experience, regularly trained in the teaching methods
- Smart electronic screens in the classroom and the Ding Talk network platform for material distribution and live broadcasting
- Teaching content formulated according to the residency training teaching syllabus in China
#### Constraints
- The article states the online workload was calibrated so the amount of online work was comparable to the time needed to review course material with the LBT group

### Target Learners
- Internal medicine residency trainees rotating through nephrology

### Learning Goals
- Theoretical knowledge of nephrology
- Practical skills such as urinary catheterization
- Clinical thinking including diagnosis and treatment planning
