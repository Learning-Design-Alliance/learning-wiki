# Interview coverage spanning five question domains from platform experience to belonging

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The protocol organizes its questions into recurring domains: general platform and structure, courseware-specific features, course content, student learning behaviors, and student perceptions and sense of belonging, closing with overall impressions and 0-10 ratings. Each domain pairs experience questions with follow-ups, such as asking what impacts a student's decision whether or not to review topics suggested by Readiness Checks, and ending with recommendation and instructor ratings from 0 to 10.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Questions from each domain should be asked within one interview session
- Follow-up branches are scripted for supportive, neutral, and negative student responses
