# Structured paired journal club activity for undergraduate pharmacy courses

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A structured educational activity in which undergraduate pharmacy students, working in pairs, select and analyze recent research articles relevant to their current pharmacology or hospital pharmacy coursework and present their findings to peers and instructors. The article states "Structured, detailed guidelines and rubrics were provided to ensure consistency in article selection, analysis, and presentations" and that each presentation is "followed by discussions to engage other students and instructors." The activity was evaluated with a validated four-domain survey covering general experience, learning outcomes, perception and attitude, and feedback and future improvements.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students are paired and given structured, detailed guidelines and rubrics for article selection, analysis, and presentations
- Articles selected must be recent research relevant to the coursework of the semester
#### Constraints
- Evaluated with self-reported survey data only; no direct assessment of clinical application skills was performed

### Target Learners
- undergraduate pharmacy students (3rd and 5th year)

### Learning Goals
- critical thinking
- research and literature evaluation skills
- presentation skills
- teamwork and collaboration
