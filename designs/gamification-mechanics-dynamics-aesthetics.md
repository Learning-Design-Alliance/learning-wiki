# Gamification's three fundamental elements: mechanics, dynamics and aesthetics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The article defines gamification in education as using game elements to increase motivation and participation, and names its three fundamental elements: "mechanics (motivating students through points, budgets and rewards), dynamics (engaging users through stories and narratives), and aesthetics (user experiences from applications about being user-friendly and attractive)". It operationalized these in its mobile application: mechanics via green carts at each stage, dynamics via the scenarios, and aesthetics checked in alpha and beta tests.
