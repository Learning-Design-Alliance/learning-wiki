# Information-constrained question formats (#TooCloseToDiagnose and #TooFarToDiagnose) force diagnosis from minimal information

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Two gamified question formats constrain the information available to the learner. In #TooCloseToDiagnose, participants see only "a specific high-power static image of a defining diagnostic feature of an entity", excluding clinical features and low-power view, and must type their answer without multiple choices. In #TooFarToDiagnose, the diagnosis must be reached from only a low-power image. Both formats increase difficulty and force recognition and interpretation of specific microscopic features.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Open-text answer entry to increase difficulty
- Curated images that isolate defining diagnostic features at high or low power
#### Constraints
- These formats were associated with lower correct response rates in the SIAPeC Quiz

### Target Learners
- Pathology residents and trainees

### Learning Goals
- Pattern recognition from limited information
- Diagnostic reasoning under constrained conditions
