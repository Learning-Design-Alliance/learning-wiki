# Three-phase motivational interviewing training program for prison professionals

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 25-hour training program delivered at Topas Prison in three consecutive phases, each combining face-to-face sessions with self-training activities. Phase 1 covers "Theoretical basics of MI*, intrinsic motivation and resolving ambivalence" (8 hours), Phase 2 covers advanced technical competencies and simulation of complex cases (8 hours), and Phase 3 covers constructive feedback, self-assessment and practical application (9 hours), with an evaluation moment after each phase.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Face-to-face sessions combined with self-training activities in each phase
- Practical exercises reflecting day-to-day prison work situations
#### Constraints
- Delivered in a real prison setting where no random control group could be formed due to operational and logistical restrictions

### Target Learners
- prison professionals in specialised inmate healthcare modules (psychologists, lawyers, educators, social workers, security personnel)

### Learning Goals
- competency and adherence to motivational interviewing principles
- reduction of practices incompatible with MI
