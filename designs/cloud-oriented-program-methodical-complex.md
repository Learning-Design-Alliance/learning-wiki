# Cloud-oriented program-methodical complex as a structured set of Sage worksheets, Jupyter notebooks and tex documents

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's central design pattern is a cloud-oriented program-methodical complex built in SageMathCloud as a structured set of Sage worksheets, Jupyter notebooks and/or tex documents (layouts of printed and presentation learning-methodical materials of mathematical content). Such a complex serves as the basis of computer-oriented learning of natural-mathematical and informatics disciplines in the format of an electronic learning course in which management and control of learning are mediated. Its main components are enumerated and illustrated in the article.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- components organized as SageMathCloud projects with replication and backup; each project is saved in three physically separate data-processing centers
#### Constraints
- management and control of learning in this format are mediated rather than direct

### Target Learners
- students of natural-mathematical and informatics disciplines

### Learning Goals
- computer-oriented course-level learning of mathematical and informatics disciplines
