# Lab-to-Market-to-Classroom (LMC) continuum with the theory-experiment-data intersection as the conceptual and content framework of the MGI

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper proposes the LMC continuum as a classroom model in which practical, hands-on content of high student interest is not only delivered but also generated and end-consumer tested, creating a social ecosystem representative of society at large. It positions the intersection of the theory-experiment-data content framework and the lab-market-classroom conceptual framework as the zone where the MGI will unfold. The paper states that the LMC builds on student-centered education and active, project-based learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Topics and systems under study must be real problems and of specific interest to the students
#### Constraints
- Implementation within the K-12 segment is strained by the need for whole school reform under the PBL paradigm

### Target Learners
- K-12 students
- undergraduate students
- graduate students and postdocs

### Learning Goals
- Multidisciplinary competence in materials science combining theory, experiment, and data science
- Twenty-first-century skills and workforce preparation
