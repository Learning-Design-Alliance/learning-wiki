# Pairing each macroscopic thermodynamic result with a microscopic derivation that explains how the process actually happens

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In the proposal, for the explanation of the main concepts such as work or heat, as well as for several thermodynamic processes discussed, a microscopic description is provided alongside the macroscopic treatment. The authors argue that thermodynamic explanations can be unsatisfactory for the student need of understanding, since they often only show how things should or should not be, but not how things actually happen; the microscopic account supplies that mechanism.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The microscopic account must be derivable from the laws of motion, general principles of mechanics and simple probabilistic arguments for it to remain simple and clear for students.
#### Constraints
- The microscopic description definitively requires more details than the macroscopic treatment.

### Target Learners
- high school students

### Learning Goals
- mechanistic understanding of thermodynamic processes
