# Caricature-animation-based online learning environment for 7th-grade Science and Technology

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
An online learning environment built for the "Human and Environment" and "Sun and Space" units of the 7th-grade Science and Technology course, prepared in two versions with and without caricature animations. Its main page holds subject titles and sub-titles, discussion, site map, about and help menus, and "Information, video, application and caricature (caricature anim ation) activities are found under the sub-title of each subject." The system gates progress so students cannot skip activities before completing the current title's activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students receive training in using the system and individual access credentials
- A computer laboratory with internet infrastructure and monitoring software (NetOp School)
#### Constraints
- Content limited to the two named units
- Humor factor limited to two-dimensional cartoon animations

### Target Learners
- 7th-grade secondary school students

### Learning Goals
- Science and Technology unit content: Human and Environment; Sun and Space
