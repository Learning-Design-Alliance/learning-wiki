# Integrate specific AED training into the undergraduate dental curriculum

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article concludes that dental students demonstrate insufficient preparedness for AED use and that "the provision of clearer verbal instructions markedly improves performance." It explicitly states these findings "support the integration of specific training on the use of the semi-automatic external defibrillator into the dental curriculum," noting that European Degree in Dentistry guidelines already require competence in basic life support and defibrillation devices.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured training in medical emergencies and AED use must be incorporated into dental curricula, consistent with European Union recommendations.
#### Constraints
- Optimizing voice instructions should not be regarded as a substitute for comprehensive life-support education.

### Target Learners
- Final-year undergraduate dental students

### Learning Goals
- Correct operation of a semi-automated external defibrillator in a cardiac arrest emergency
