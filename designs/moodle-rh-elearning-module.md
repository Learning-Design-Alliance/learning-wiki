# Moodle-based interactive reproductive health e-learning module with lesson quizzes, pre/post module quizzes and online interaction tools

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
An open-source Moodle course covering the Reproductive Health section of the Public Health curriculum, containing four modules (Measurement of health, Maternal health program, Family planning program, Child health care) with 45 lessons of about one hour each. Each lesson "is followed by a lesson quiz with multiple choice questions (MCQ) and extended matching questions" and the CMS environment offers "student-student as well as student-teacher communication via a chat room (open 24 hours), forum discussions". Material was based on the required textbook and intended learning outcomes, complemented by new visuals, animations and interactive materials; completion averaged five weeks with facilitators spending 20-25 hours weekly in forums.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A 70% passing grade on each lesson quiz is required to move to the next lesson
- Course facilitators (two senior and one junior staff) participating about 20-25 hours a week in forums and answering questions
- Deadlines for completion of each module set by course facilitators
#### Constraints
- Modules were written in English; students requested sections in Arabic
- The university provided no computer and internet connection facilities for students, limiting access for non-participants

### Target Learners
- Fourth-year undergraduate medical students in a large-enrollment Public Health course

### Learning Goals
- Reproductive health knowledge: maternal and child health, the family planning program in Egypt, and indicators to measure health
