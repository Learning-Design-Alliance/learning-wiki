# HFS/curriculum-integrated session pattern: horizontally integrated scenario-based simulation with hands-on practice and debriefing in small groups

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
This pattern combines a horizontally integrated curriculum (merging disciplines within one time frame, here third-year women's health and child health nursing) with high-fidelity simulation using computerized manikins. Students practice paired maternal/newborn scenarios in small groups, followed by structured debriefing. The article's protocol comprised "HFS using birthing manikins, hands-on practice, scenario-based learning" for delivery management, newborn care with newborn manikins, and a 30-minute debriefing to "promote reflection, and improve clinical performance."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must already have been exposed to the topics separately in each course before the integrated session
- Instructors adequately trained in the equipment and HFS manikins
- A prepared protocol covering both the vaginal delivery and newborn care parts
#### Constraints
- Assessed only for two topics (vaginal delivery management and immediate newborn care) in one institution
- Small convenient sample; findings may not generalize to other nursing programs or contexts

### Target Learners
- third-year undergraduate nursing students

### Learning Goals
- nursing management during normal vaginal delivery
- immediate newborn care
- clinical decision-making and procedural skills
