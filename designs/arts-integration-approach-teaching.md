## Description
The article adopts the definition of arts integration as "an approach to teaching in which students construct and demonstrate understanding through an art form," in which students engage in a creative process connecting an art form and another subject area while meeting evolving objectives in both. The article distinguishes three variations of where to use art in school: in the arts curriculum, in the arts-integrated curriculum, and in the arts enhanced curriculum. It applies this framework by integrating fine art content into mathematics lessons in primary school.
