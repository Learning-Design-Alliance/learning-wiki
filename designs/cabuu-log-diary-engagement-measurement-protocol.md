# Cabuu vocabulary app log data and twice-daily diary protocol for measuring observable and subjective engagement

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The measurement setup combines commercial app log data with daily diaries: log timestamps of study and test activities are aggregated into a daily score of "how many vocabulary items were studied or tested by each participant per day," treating engagement as quantity rather than correctness. Children answered single-item diaries before learning (expectancy, value facets, cost facets) and after learning (satisfaction, effort, task delay) on 32 of 36 days, with timestamp checks enforcing temporal order.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Timestamp validation that pre-learning diaries precede first app interaction and post-learning diaries follow app use
#### Constraints
- The score does not differentiate study from test activities and ignores correctness, given varied activity types and no uniform correctness metric

### Target Learners
- fifth graders learning English vocabulary in an informal out-of-school setting

### Learning Goals
- vocabulary learning; situational motivation and engagement measurement
