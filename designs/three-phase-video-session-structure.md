# Three-phase video session: pre-movie, movie, and post-movie activities

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Each treatment session followed a three-part structure for exploiting video clips: "1) pre-movie activities, in which the teacher will activate the participants' schemata by asking relevant questions about the movie topic. 2) Movie activities", in which the movie was watched and learners could take notes, and "3) post-movie activities", in which new vocabulary was introduced and practiced followed by free discussion. The article also recommends clips be short, around 2 or 3 minutes, and clear, self-contained and engaging.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher guidance and well-chosen materials; clips prepared ahead with appropriate pre-viewing, viewing and post-viewing activities
#### Constraints
- Clips should be short (around 2 or 3 minutes) to allow exploitation of their contents in class

### Target Learners
- intermediate adult EFL learners

### Learning Goals
- vocabulary learning from video materials
