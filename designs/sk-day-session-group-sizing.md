# Schedule outreach sessions of 45 minutes with short breaks, small groups of 12-15 students, and one volunteer per 5-6 students

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article prescribes a lesson- and group-level structure for outreach days: after icebreakers, participants are separated into smaller classrooms where "groups of about 12-15 people are best, with one volunteer every 5 or 6 students". After several years of modifying the schedule, the authors found that "sessions of 45 minutes work best, with 5 to 10 minutes breaks in between, and an hour lunch break". The program typically runs Saturdays from 10:30 a.m. to 3 p.m.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Enough volunteers to staff one per 5-6 students
- Multiple classrooms for parallel sessions
#### Constraints
- The 10:30 a.m.-3 p.m. window was chosen to accommodate families coming from outer Chicago; in a smaller city the program could be longer

### Target Learners
- female students aged 7-17 attending university-hosted outreach days

### Learning Goals
- engagement with new mathematics in small-group settings
