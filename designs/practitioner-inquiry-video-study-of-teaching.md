# Use practitioner inquiry with video review to help novice teachers study their own teaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends engaging early program teacher candidates in practitioner inquiry of their own teaching through a Study of Teaching project. Candidates videotape lessons, analyze them, conduct a modified literature review, and synthesize findings in a written paper and class symposium. This process provides explicit opportunities to focus on the tasks and activities of teaching and fosters learning from practice in practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Candidates need access to video technology, a short field practicum placement, and mentored guidance through the inquiry process.
