# Eight-strategy pattern for overcoming ESL pronunciation challenges within the CLT framework

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review proposes a matched set of eight strategies mirroring its eight identified challenges: a pronunciation-oriented curriculum within CLT, inclusion of pronunciation assessment, teacher preparedness through CPD, available pronunciation-integrated training, limited class size with longer class periods, technology-integrated pronunciation practice, availability of resources, and motivation among teachers and students. The review states "Implementing these strategies can significantly enhance the effectiveness of ESL pronunciation teaching in Bangladesh" and that a holistic approach combining them can improve students' communication skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Curriculum integration, regular assessment, targeted teacher training, technology use, and motivation, per the review's holistic approach
#### Constraints
- Strategies are proposed for the Bangladeshi secondary ESL context within the CLT framework

### Target Learners
- Secondary-level ESL learners in Bangladesh

### Learning Goals
- Explicit pronunciation instruction
- pronunciation assessment and feedback
- communicative competence
