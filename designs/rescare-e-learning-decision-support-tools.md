# ResCare e-learning and decision support e-tools for care home staff

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The ResCare intervention is an online individualised intervention for care home staff supporting residents with dementia and challenging behaviour. "The e-learning for staff comprised three interactive multimedia modules with built-in feedback to introduce care home staff to observational skills and a formulaic approach for individualising care planning." Staff entered information on a resident's health and abilities, life history, communication style and others' responses; logic-based algorithms generated bio-psychosocial action plans. A cascaded learning approach used Champions to spread the approach to colleagues.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- IT provision (broadband and computers) in homes
- staff time to complete e-learning and develop action care plans
- a dementia research therapist to assist with training and care planning
#### Constraints
- Take up of the e-learning and e-tools proved hard; the decision support tool needed more staff time than first calculated

### Target Learners
- care home staff, including managers, senior care assistants and care assistants

### Learning Goals
- observational skills and individualised care planning for residents with challenging behaviour
