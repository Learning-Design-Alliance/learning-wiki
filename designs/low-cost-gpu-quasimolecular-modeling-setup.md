# Low-cost GPU and CUDA classroom setup for quasimolecular modeling of a vibrating string

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A low-cost classroom laboratory for the article's vibrating-string modeling experiment: a bungee cord with clamps and pulleys from a hardware store, a Nikon 1 J3 camera filming at 1200 frames per second, and a GeForce GTX 580 graphics card, which 'cost around 500 dollars new, but can be bought used for around 150 dollars.' Appendix A provides the complete C and CUDA code for the GPU implementation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A CUDA-capable GPU, a high-speed camera (the article used 1200 frames per second), and hardware-store equipment to clamp and tension the cord.
#### Constraints
- The cord's stiffness was 'nonlinear and inconsistent so an average value for 𝑘𝑘 was used.'

### Target Learners
- high school and college freshman modeling students
