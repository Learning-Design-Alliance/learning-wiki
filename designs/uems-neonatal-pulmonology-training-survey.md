# Structured online survey of neonatal trainees across UEMS member countries on pulmonology training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A structured online questionnaire distributed via the ESPR newsletter and targeted national neonatology societies from 35 full and associate member countries of the UEMS between December 2023 and June 2024. It collected demographic information, training description, research activity, and self-rated confidence (1–5) and supervision-requirement levels for manual tasks and clinical decision-making, plus readiness to become a consultant on a 1–10 scale. One hundred and eight individual responses from 24 countries were received; 105 were analysed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Distribution via ESPR newsletter and national neonatology societies
- Eligibility restricted to current NST or those who completed training within the last 3 years
#### Constraints
- Risk of selection bias as the survey was not mandatory
- Distribution through the ESPR introduced bias toward trainees from more academically inclined institutions

### Target Learners
- Neonatal sub-specialty trainees
- Junior consultants who completed training within the last 3 years

### Learning Goals
- Assessment of training experiences and confidence in pulmonology-related skills
