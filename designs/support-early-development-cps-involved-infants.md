# Assess and support cognitive stimulation and normative development during infancy for CPS-involved children regardless of placement

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that child welfare agencies attend to early development during the infancy sensitive period whether or not a child is removed. It states its findings "highlight the need for agencies to assess and support cognitive stimulation and normative development in this sensitive period regardless of whether children remain in their homes or enter foster care." It further argues for enhanced efforts to engage vulnerable families in Early Intervention and home visiting services, since few substantiated victims engage such services and children remaining at home showed poorer outcomes.
