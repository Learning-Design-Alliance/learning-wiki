# FRAC Feedback Literacy Script (Filter–Reason–Act–Check)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
FRAC is a four-step feedback literacy script that guides students' processing of external AI feedback: "Filter, identifying the type of problem addressed by the feedback; Reason, judging whether the feedback was valid and relevant to the current task", then Act (implementing adopted feedback as a specific revision) and Check (reviewing whether the revision achieved the intended result). Training lasted approximately 15 to 25 minutes before T1 with brief reviews before T1 and T2; students recorded decision sheets at T1 and T2 as process data, with return rates exceeding 90% per round.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must process AI feedback systematically through the script and complete decision sheets recording how they handled each feedback item
#### Constraints
- In this study it was implemented with undergraduate English majors using Claude Sonnet 4 feedback during T1 and T2 only

### Target Learners
- undergraduate English majors with basic academic writing skills

### Learning Goals
- critical evaluation and effective uptake of AI-generated writing feedback
- deep revision of argumentative essays
