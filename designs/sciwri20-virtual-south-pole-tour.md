# ScienceWriters 2020 virtual South Pole tour presentation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For the virtually organized ScienceWriters 2020 conference, IceCube produced an hour-long presentation with three parts: an introduction to IceCube and its science, a prerecorded tour of the South Pole facilities, and a demo of the augmented reality app ICEcuBEAR. The introduction was a video produced by 2019 PolarTREC educator Jocelyn Argueta from a team-written script. "Over 100 people attended and we received overwhelmingly positive feedback", and the YouTube recording "has been viewed more than 7,500 times".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A host able to engage an audience of scientists and science writers in no more than 15 minutes
- Prerecorded tour footage from South Pole winterovers
- A live AR app demo
#### Constraints
- Designed for a professional audience of scientists, journalists, and science communicators rather than students

### Target Learners
- Science writers, journalists, and science communicators

### Learning Goals
- Understanding of the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and its South Pole science
