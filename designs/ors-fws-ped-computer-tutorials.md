# Brief self-contained computer tutorials on Oral Rehydration Solutions and Fever Without Source

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Two brief computer tutorials were designed for use by medical students in the Pediatric Emergency Department, presenting mainly declarative knowledge. The ORS tutorial (30 screens) targeted conceptual knowledge of osmosis and facilitated diffusion; the FWS tutorial (42 screens) targeted factual knowledge of the diagnostic and treatment approach to febrile children aged birth-36 months. Instructional strategies "included animations, schematic diagrams, interactive exercises and patient presentations but not video". They were installed on a single computer at the nursing station and were independently reviewed by content experts and pilot tested to saturation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Installed on a computer located in the clinical area so the tutorial can be done immediately in the flow of patient care
#### Constraints
- The tutorials were invariant "black boxes" with fixed instructional strategies that may not have been optimal for a given student situation; they were not available to students outside the study

### Target Learners
- third-year medical students on a Pediatric Emergency rotation

### Learning Goals
- physiologic principles of oral rehydration
- diagnostic and treatment approach to fever without source
