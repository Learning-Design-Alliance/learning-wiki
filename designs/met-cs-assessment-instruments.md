# Direct and indirect assessment instruments adopted by the MET CS department

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The department adopted a set of direct assessment deliverables including "Writing samples from term projects", locally designed quizzes and tests, portfolio artifacts, capstone projects, licensure/certification exams, internship reports, and employer ratings of workplace skills. Indirect instruments included an anonymous alumni survey conducted annually, internship supervisor comments, and a pre/post self-rating survey. Accreditation review by an external team was also treated as direct assessment.

## Design Implications

- The first assessment did not focus extensively on indirect measures of student learning
