# Math-enhanced micro-teaching with peer teaching and role-playing as secondary students

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A lesson-level design pattern in which preservice teachers prepare and deliver agricultural lessons incorporating Stone et al.'s (2006) seven components of a math-enhanced lesson to their peers, who role-play as secondary students. A math-enhanced lesson is defined as "an agricultural lesson that incorporates Stone et al.’s (2006) seven components of a math-enhanced lesson". The pattern combines lesson preparation, peer micro-teaching, and observational learning through watching peers teach.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A lecture explaining and demonstrating the seven components of a math-enhanced lesson
- Assignment of specific cross-referenced NCTM sub-standards to each preservice teacher
#### Constraints
- The authors note it is unknown whether the value lies in preparing the lessons, teaching them, participating as students, or a combination of these activities

### Target Learners
- preservice agricultural education teachers in a teaching methods course

### Learning Goals
- mathematics ability
- designing and delivering math-enhanced agricultural lessons
