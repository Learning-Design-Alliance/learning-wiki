# Two-phase script strategy program combining small-group worksheet activities with workplace-based script development and individual and group reflection

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's educational program is a six-month, 48-session clinical training design in which "the method of ‘direct illness script teaching using structured worksheets’ was conducted in two main phases": Phase 1 uses small-group foundational and advanced scenario worksheet development with facilitator feedback, and Phase 2 moves to real-patient script worksheets under tutor supervision plus individual and group reflection using the seven-step REFLECT model. The nursing script worksheet template covers patient history, physical assessment findings, nursing interventions, pharmacological considerations, and potential drug interactions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must have completed theoretical ICU/CCU courses and passed the corresponding exam before entering the intensive-unit clerkship
- Facilitators and clinical tutors are needed to moderate sessions and provide structured feedback
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only in intensive care clerkship settings at one university, without a control group, with short-term assessment only

### Target Learners
- fourth-year nursing students in intensive care clerkships

### Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning
- hypothesis formation
- care planning
- reflective practice
