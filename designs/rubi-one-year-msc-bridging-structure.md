# One-year Masters structure evenly splitting coursework and research with concurrent project design

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The RUBi MSc runs over 11 months with "10 to 13 coursework modules and a research project running partially concurrently". Modules (about 25 contact hours each, delivered in intensive one-week blocks) combine lectures, computer-based tutorials and practicals, while students develop their project proposal alongside first-half coursework. "The coursework and the research project each contribute 50% to an overall mark", with sub-minima per module and thesis. The programme acts as a bridge from many disciplinary legs into bioinformatics PhD study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires teaching and supervision collaboration from multiple departments (Chemistry, Computer Science, Pure and Applied Mathematics, Statistics) alongside the host department.
#### Constraints
- Research time dedicated purely to research is very limited (July-December).

### Target Learners
- MSc students from diverse disciplinary backgrounds

### Learning Goals
- interdisciplinary bioinformatics competence and readiness for PhD research
