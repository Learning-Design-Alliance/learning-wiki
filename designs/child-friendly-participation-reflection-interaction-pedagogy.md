# Child-friendly pedagogy lesson plan based on participation, reflection, and interaction with frequent materials use

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The intervention's instructional design is "A child-friendly pedagogy lesson plan, based on child participation, reflection, and interaction, with frequent use of teaching and learning materials (TLMs)", prepared by the technical team based on the STRIPES intervention. Para-teachers and monitors are trained on pedagogical best practices and on facilitating English and mathematics learning during the classes. The pattern pairs an activity-centered lesson structure with concrete materials that let children practice and apply concepts repeatedly.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Para-teachers and monitors are trained by the Effective Intervention team in partnership with the Naandi Foundation on pedagogical best practices for ASCs
#### Constraints
- The lesson plan is adapted from the STRIPES intervention to the Gambian context

### Target Learners
- Grade 1–3 children in rural Gambian after-school classes

### Learning Goals
- Mastery of concepts learned in regular school classes through participatory, interactive lessons
