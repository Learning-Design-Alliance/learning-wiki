# Course designs that assign students ownership and consensus-based iteration over experiments and projects

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In the Compass fall course, students have ownership over a final research project on a question "personally interesting to them", advised by graduate students, culminating in a community presentation. In the spring course, students "explicitly practice collaboration and iteration through the reﬁnement of a thermal expansion experiment": they build a measurement procedure by consensus, then change apparatus, procedure, or data analysis after the first round and evaluate whether the changes improve results.
