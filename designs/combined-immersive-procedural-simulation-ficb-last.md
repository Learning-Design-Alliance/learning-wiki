# Paired immersive-plus-procedural simulation curriculum linking FICB and LAST

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A one-day curriculum pairing two separate but concurrent one-hour simulations on intertwined topics: a 30-minute immersive LAST case (seizure and cardiac arrest after a FICB, treated with intralipid therapy and supportive care, followed by a toxicologist-informed debrief) and a hands-on FICB procedural simulation using a fascia iliaca manikin, a porcine-tissue hydrodissection model, a standardized procedural checklist, and a maximum-dose calculation handout. The article describes it as "a novel experience with a combination of immersive and procedural simulation."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty with perceived skill and confidence in performing and teaching FICB, including board-certified US faculty and a board-certified toxicologist for the debrief
#### Constraints
- The article notes the simulation may not be worthwhile at other sites if FICB procedures cannot be implemented given accessibility to intralipid emulsion therapy in case of possible LAST

### Target Learners
- Emergency medicine residents (PGY 1–3)

### Learning Goals
- Recognize and manage local anesthetic systemic toxicity
- Perform ultrasound-guided fascia iliaca compartment block
- Calculate maximum local anesthetic dosing
