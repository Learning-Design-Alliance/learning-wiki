# A standalone week-by-week curriculum directory of resilience outcomes

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
As a product of the evaluation, the data sets were collated into "an itemised, standalone curriculum detailing week-by-week activities and experiences that possess elements of resilience building", produced for the entire five-year course. It constitutes a complete directory of resilience outcomes (learning outcomes with a clear resilience component), organised to display different activity types and the learning objectives found within. The authors hope it will prove useful in cataloguing and further enhancing the course by providing enhanced visibility and ease of access.
