# AI-decision checkpoints framework: one explicit AI-adoption decision point per BPM lifecycle phase

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The framework uses the BPM lifecycle to structure explicit, phase-related decisions about AI adoption across identification, discovery, analysis, redesign, implementation, and monitoring. At each checkpoint students "identify AI-candidate subprocesses, establishing the baseline, evaluating expected benefits and risks using the analytical instruments of the corresponding lifecycle phase, and documenting a reasoned decision to adopt, adapt, postpone, or reject specific AI components". It decomposes AI adoption from a monolithic yes/no judgment into locally grounded assessments producing a traceable decision trail.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each checkpoint aligns with one of the six BPM lifecycle phases and uses that phase's analytical instruments
#### Constraints
- The framework does not prescribe a correct answer at any checkpoint; it foregrounds reasoning quality
- individual checkpoints can be weighted or combined without disrupting the logic

### Target Learners
- Master-level Business Information Systems or Computer Science students
- future process developers

### Learning Goals
- reasoning about AI as a first-class design element of end-to-end processes
- justifying AI adoption decisions with phase-appropriate evidence
