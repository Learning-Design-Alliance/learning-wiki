# MOOC 'Postural and Technological Adaptations in Pediatrics' on the MiriadaX platform

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A free x-MOOC hosted on MiriadaX used as an extension activity for occupational therapy and physical therapy students. It comprised "six modules" covering disability concepts and evaluation, postural adaptations for pediatric therapeutic intervention, assistive technology tools, low-cost design and building of an assistive product, and adapted play for people with functional diversity. Each module paired short two-to-three-minute videos with clinical case discussion, analysis and clinical reasoning, and students could participate in discussion forums to increase engagement and interaction.
