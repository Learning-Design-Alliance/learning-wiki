# Blended-learning CPD programme design combining asynchronous video lectures, case-based seminars and online discussions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes the instructional components of the blended-learning Master programme: "Key elements such as asynchronous video lectures facilitated flexible, self‐paced learning. Interactive, case‐based seminars promoted critical thinking and clinical reasoning. Moreover, online discussions supported collaborative knowledge building." The programme also incorporated problem-based learning, self-directed study, explorative learning opportunities and cooperative learning in virtual classrooms.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- User-friendly e-learning software enabling flexible online access for working professionals
#### Constraints
- Online teaching alone cannot fully replace conventional methods; a multi-modal approach blending digital and face-to-face education is needed

### Target Learners
- postgraduate dentists in part-time CPD programmes

### Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning
- collaborative knowledge building
- self-paced theoretical learning
