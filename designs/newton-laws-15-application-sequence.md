# Fifteen-application didactic sequence moving from prior-knowledge questionnaire through expository, simulator, comic/AR and student-produced video moments to a final questionnaire

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The thesis organizes instruction into a numbered sequence of applications: a prior-knowledge questionnaire, presentation of prior conceptions on mass vs weight, a PHET-simulator-mediated lesson, a history-of-physics report, comic/AR-mediated lessons on each of Newton's three laws, moments on resultant, weight, friction and normal forces, a challenge, student-produced videos, and a final learning-verification questionnaire followed by an interest/suggestion questionnaire. Its stated aim is to "desenvolver uma sequência didática , utilizando-se de quadrinhos, Gifs e 3D com programação de vídeos, e realidade aumentada, de modo a abordar as Leis de Newton".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher mediation at each moment; printed magazine plus AR app; PHET simulator for the third application.
#### Constraints
- Designed and applied with one first-year high-school class on Newton's laws.

### Target Learners
- first-year high-school physics students

### Learning Goals
- conceptual understanding of Newton's laws and forces; application to everyday events
