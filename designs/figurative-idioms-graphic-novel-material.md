# Figurative-idioms-embedded graphic novel teaching material

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A teaching artifact built for the experimental group: forty figurative idioms listed by Grant (2007) from MICASE were used in a script that "was converted to a graphic novel with the use of computer software (www.storyboardthat.com)". Each week a required part of the novel including ten idioms was read in class, students guessed meanings, and the teacher checked meanings and gave extra example sentences.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A script embedding the target idioms and conversion software to produce the graphic novel
- Weekly in-class reading with teacher-checked meanings and extra example sentences
#### Constraints
- The scenario was written by the lead researcher and converted by software, so the quality of the scenario and graphics may have affected results

### Target Learners
- first-year English Language Teaching department students

### Learning Goals
- figurative idiom knowledge
- vocabulary learning
