# Two-week mandatory POCUS rotation for first-year emergency medicine residents with ten content classes and bedside learning with real patients

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The curriculum implemented in 2019 at the authors' institution is a mandatory two-week rotation for first-year emergency medicine residents combining a journal club (3 h), an image-review session (3 h), ten 30-minute didactic lectures covering cardiac, lung, abdomen, aorta, DVT, soft tissue/musculoskeletal, ocular, kidney/urinary, OB-GYN and procedural guidance content plus FAST/EFAST protocols, and "bedside ultrasound learning with real patients (18 h)". The study assessed competence outcomes after this integrated training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mandatory inclusion in first-year emergency medicine residency training, ultrasound machines in the emergency department, and access to real patients for the 18-hour bedside component
#### Constraints
- Described and evaluated in a single Thai emergency medicine residency setting; the authors state results "may not be generalizable to other specialties"

### Target Learners
- First-year emergency medicine residents

### Learning Goals
- POCUS image acquisition, image interpretation, and clinical integration of ultrasound findings into diagnosis
