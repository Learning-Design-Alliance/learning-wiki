# Learn it, do it, write it, teach it, recycle it: a peer-instruction research workflow in which trained students write guides and teach peers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The Dead Stars Society co-developed a student-level research workflow centered on peer instruction: the professional researcher teaches a data analysis technique to two or three student researchers, who apply it to real data, write an accessible research guide, and use it to teach other student researchers. The authors summarize this as 'learn it, do it, write it, teach it, recycle it,' leveraging collaborative learning while giving students ownership of their learning, autonomy, and engagement with the project.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The professional researcher first teaches the data analysis technique to two or three student researchers before they write and teach the guide
- The analysis guides serving as the entry point to the project must be accessible at the level of the student researchers
#### Constraints
- Holes in the guide are discovered and filled in by the team and, if needed, the professional researcher via Slack
- The authors state it is very valuable to have person-to-person time at the beginning to get the workflow started, even though with an existing structure the research could be carried out virtually

### Target Learners
- community college undergraduate students early in their careers, some of whom have not taken an introductory astronomy class

### Learning Goals
- observational astronomy data analysis techniques
- transferable coding and data visualization skills
- ownership of learning and research autonomy
