# ECG Online: the University of Ulm e-learning electrocardiogram course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
ECG Online is the e-learning elective at the University of Ulm covering the basics of the resting ECG and its interpretation. It consists of "12 audio visual lessons of about 1 h duration and 2 to 4 online exercises (usually ECG interpretations)" per lesson, with sequential release of lessons after completing exercises and email contact with the instructor. Approximately 160 students participate online per semester versus about 12 in the identical-content face-to-face course.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Technical prerequisites for online participation, which all interviewees reported meeting in their university and private environments
#### Constraints
- Completed lessons remain available indefinitely, which students criticised as lacking an expiry date; about 25% of online enrolels on average take the voluntary final examination

### Target Learners
- medical students in semesters 7 to 10 at the University of Ulm

### Learning Goals
- basics of the resting ECG and interpretation of ECG curves for clinical purposes
