# Learning curve-based individualized training modality for simulator skill acquisition

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article introduces a competency-based training modality in which each trainee's individualized learning curve is generated from their first 16 simulator procedures using a previously validated exponential equation, and training continues until the individualized end point is achieved. The trainees stop when "the following procedure time fell into the 95% CI of the asymptote 3 consecutive times," meaning performance has reached the curve's plateau. This replaces time-based training with skill-acquisition-based training, addressing variable learning pace among trainees.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A simulator that automatically records procedure time and performance scores so individual learning curves can be fitted
- At least 16 initial training procedures to generate the individualized learning curve before end-point assessment
#### Constraints
- Some simulation training may not be construed by a learning curve, per the authors
- Whether the method detects trainees who require more time was not investigated in this study

### Target Learners
- Anesthesiology residents and interns who are novices at FOB intubation

### Learning Goals
- Acquisition of basic flexible optical bronchoscopic intubation skill
