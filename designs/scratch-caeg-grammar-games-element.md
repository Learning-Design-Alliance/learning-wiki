# Scratch-built computer-assisted educational games for 6th-grade Turkish grammar (parts of speech)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A set of computer-assisted educational games designed in Scratch, a programming language the article describes as "suitable for every age group, fun, and attracts users' attention to itself with a user-friendly interface". The games taught parts of speech from the 6th grade Turkish Teaching Program over a 12-week plan, two course hours per week, played on a smart board and on students' tablets, individually and in groups of 5 or 6. Games started with a demo, explained goals and rules, presented a story and scenario, and gave feedback on the gamer's decisions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Games were designed in the frame of previous studies and expert opinions, with feedback from informatics teachers, Turkish teachers, and academicians, and revised accordingly
#### Constraints
- The subject was chosen because the practice time coincided with when parts of speech were scheduled in the curriculum

### Target Learners
- 6th-grade middle school students

### Learning Goals
