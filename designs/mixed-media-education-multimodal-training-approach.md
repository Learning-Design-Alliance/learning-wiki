# Mixed media education as a multimodal training approach combining lectures, videos, role-play, and online supervision

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's intervention embodies a mixed media education approach in which "3 biweekly mixed media education sessions, consisting of lectures, videos, role-play, and feedback" were combined with "15-20 minutes of individual online supervision and feedback sessions following each class." The approach layers didactic content (lectures on infection control principles and bloodborne pathogens), observational modeling (videos demonstrating correct procedures), and experiential practice (small-group role-playing with scripts), supported by an online platform for materials and discussion. The authors frame this combination as effective where self-directed reading alone showed no significant improvement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multiple delivery formats (lecture, video, role-play) prepared by researchers with hospital infection management staff
- An online communication platform for distributing materials and individual follow-up
#### Constraints
- Tested with nursing students at the beginning of clinical training in a single Chinese teaching hospital; the authors note the sample size was small and appropriate for a pilot test

### Target Learners
- nursing students at the beginning of clinical training

### Learning Goals
- knowledge of standard precautions
- attitudes toward standard precautions
- compliance with standard precautions
