# Game-like holographic robot practice loop: modeled pronunciation, spoken answers with feedback, and a reward animation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A reusable lesson-level design in which a holographic virtual teacher models each word's pronunciation, asks children to say it aloud, gives immediate correct/incorrect feedback via speech recognition, and ends each mode with a motivating reward. The authors state: "We added an animation of Arturito singing and dancing at the end of each mode to encourage the children to pay attention and listen to him model the pronunciation of the target words." The goal was a fun, game-based environment encouraging children to work towards a goal without worrying about making mistakes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Speech recognition via the Android Speech API, prerecorded native-speaker pronunciation files, and the holographic stage or smartphone screen for displaying the robot and word images
#### Constraints
- In the study only the random (not adaptive) practice order was used; the holographic version requires the HolograFX stage and a dark background

### Target Learners
- Pre-school children aged 4-5 learning English as a foreign language

### Learning Goals
- Correct pronunciation of basic English vocabulary words
