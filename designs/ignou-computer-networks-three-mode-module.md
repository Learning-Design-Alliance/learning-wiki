# Three-mode self-learning module on Computer Networks with integrated learning activities and the VirtualCampus.org virtual learning environment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study's instructional artifact: the CIC Block 3 Unit 2 module on Computer Networks was restructured into a self-learning module of three small units equivalent to 15 hours of study and delivered in three modes — print with face-to-face support, an interactive multimedia CD, and a web-enabled version "with built – in discussion forum" posted on a specially designed website (VirtualCampus.org). Thirteen learning activities were integrated across the three modes, eight common to all, including pre-test, induction, study guide, self-assessment questions, discussion, chat, summary and post-test.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A personalised web server established for the online students
- Two mentors at the server-end to facilitate chat and group discussions
- Designated telelearning centres for the CD and online groups
#### Constraints
- The online module was tested for five days at two designated telelearning centres with 20 students, 10 at each centre

### Target Learners
- Certificate in Computing students at IGNOU

### Learning Goals
- computer networks content at certificate level
