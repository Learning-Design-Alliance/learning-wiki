# SimMan 3G simulated difficult trauma airway scenario with tongue swelling, cervical collar and ground-level stretcher intubation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A standardized simulated difficult airway scenario built on SimMan 3G high-fidelity manikins, created "by activating the tongue-swelling feature in the SimMan 3G manikins with a standard cervical collar applied for cervical immobilization." Participants intubated the manikin placed on a stretcher on flat ground using a size #7 ETT, mimicking trauma conditions. Time to intubation was measured from picking up the laryngoscope to confirmed tracheal intubation by chest rise, with failure defined as no successful intubation within 90 seconds.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- High-fidelity simulator with tongue-swelling feature
- Familiarization session and training video for participants before testing
#### Constraints
- The article states other difficult airway scenarios such as blood, secretions, anatomic variations or mandibular injuries could not be simulated, and intubating a manikin differs from intubating real patients

### Target Learners
- Medical officers with 2–3 years of postgraduate training

### Learning Goals
- Assessment of difficult-airway intubation proficiency under trauma conditions
