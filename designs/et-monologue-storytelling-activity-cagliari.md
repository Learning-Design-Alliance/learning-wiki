# Storytelling activity: monologue with poems, researcher physics session, and debate on gravitational waves and the Einstein Telescope

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An informal-learning activity designed by the Educational Division of the Physics Department at the University of Cagliari and INFN Cagliari, delivered to 200 students (17–19 years old) from five Sardinian high schools. It used "storytelling and theatrical techniques such as a monologue" set in 2030 with an Einstein Telescope physicist protagonist, including poems in Italian and Sardinian. The session structure was that "the first 20 minutes was dedicated to the monologue", followed by a 20-minute physics explanation and a 20-minute debate.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A researcher-led explanation session and a debate phase follow the performance so students re-elaborate the experienced content
#### Constraints
- The monologue used no mathematics, and some concepts used (e.g. field, wave) were unknown to younger students

### Target Learners
- high-school students aged 17–19 in scientific and humanities lyceums

### Learning Goals
- introduce students to contemporary physics topics (general relativity, black holes, gravitational waves, cosmology) and to research infrastructures
