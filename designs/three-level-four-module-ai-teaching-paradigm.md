# Three-level–four-module AI teaching paradigm operationalizing cognitive-information theory

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A cognitive informatics–driven teaching paradigm integrating a micro-meso-macro three-tier architecture with four closed-loop AI modules (cognitive diagnosis → case generation → explainable guidance → adaptive scheduling). The micro level handles task-level real-time feedback, the meso level course-level scheduling via reinforcement learning, and the macro level longitudinal ability transfer tracking. The article states it 'operationalizes cognitive-information theory' and addresses fragmentation of existing single-module AI educational systems through closed-loop integration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Integration of cognitive diagnosis outputs directly into the RL reward function to achieve 'state-scheduling' synergy
- Unified data flow and feedback mechanism across all three levels with global parameter updates at the end of each weekly cycle
#### Constraints
- Developed and tested specifically for postgraduate medical imaging education

### Target Learners
- Postgraduate medical imaging students

### Learning Goals
- Diagnostic image interpretation accuracy
- Skill transfer across imaging modalities and disease types
- Cognitive load management
