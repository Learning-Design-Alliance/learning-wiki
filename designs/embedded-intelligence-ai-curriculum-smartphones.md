# Embedded Intelligence Approach to AI Education Using Smartphones

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The curriculum centers on programming real-world devices that interact with real environments, using smartphones as the central technological element. The article states that 'Current smartphones have the technological level required for AI teaching in terms of sensors, actuators, computing power and communications.' This approach reduces cost and makes AI education feasible at schools with limited budgets.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must have access to smartphones with adequate sensors, actuators, computing power and communications
#### Constraints
- Relies on Android smartphones; budget limitations may arise if students cannot use their own devices

### Target Learners
- High school students aged 15-18 in scientific programmes

### Learning Goals
- Understanding AI working principles through programming embedded intelligence systems
