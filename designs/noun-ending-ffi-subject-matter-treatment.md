# Noun-ending form-focused instruction embedded in subject-matter teaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An instructional treatment that draws learners' attention to selected French noun endings that reliably predict grammatical gender and provides practice associating endings with gender attribution. It was "implemented in the context of regular subject-matter instruction by three of the four teachers, each with two classes, for approximately 9 hours during a 5-week period," while a comparison teacher taught the same subject matter without FFI. The treatment targets written and oral gender-attribution accuracy in immersion students.
