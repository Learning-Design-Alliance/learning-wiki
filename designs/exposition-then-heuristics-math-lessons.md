## Description
The article states that the experimental teaching approach "was, as a matter of fact, a combination of the expository strategy and problem -solving approach", because facts, rules, and action sequences such as terms, formulas and procedures had to be explained through exposition before the heuristics could be used. This lesson-level pattern pairs direct explanation of foundational content with student problem-solving practice built on heuristic steps.
