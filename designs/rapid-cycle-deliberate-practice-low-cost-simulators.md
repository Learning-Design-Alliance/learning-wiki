# Rapid-cycle deliberate practice on low-cost maternal simulators for emergency protocol proficiency

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The OBLS in-person training incorporates "rapid-cycle deliberate practice utilizing custom-designed, low-cost maternal simulators," enabling participants to develop proficiency in emergency protocols within controlled, high-pressure environments. Small instructor-led groups (4 prehospital, 6 hospital-based participants) practice team communication, leading maternal cardiac arrest, left uterine displacement, resuscitative cesarean delivery, and debriefing for critical events.
