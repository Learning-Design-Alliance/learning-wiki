# Intensive 5-week Paradigm courses with daily 7-hour-per-week scheduling

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The revised junior-year curriculum restructures nine 3-week Paradigm courses into six 5-week courses while maintaining the intensive 7-hour-per-week daily schedule. The article states this schedule allows students to focus intensely on a single topic, fosters cohort building, and supports active engagement. Moving to two 5-week courses per quarter gives students who fall behind a chance to catch up and enables more feedback before final exams, and 3-credit courses simplify teaching loads.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Maintaining the intensive 7-hour-per-week schedule
- Fewer faculty can teach the curriculum, albeit at increased workload per professor
#### Constraints
- Designed within the constraint that transfer students must be able to graduate with two years of upper-division courses

### Target Learners
- junior-year physics majors
- transfer students

### Learning Goals

- cohort building
