# CQS design characteristics: sequenced questions balancing difficulty, context stability, and abstract-concrete mix

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's own design characteristics for sequencing clicker questions: questions were "sequenced to balance difficulties, avoid change of both concept and context between adjacent questions as appropriate to avoid cognitive overload, and include a mix of abstract and concrete questions to help students develop a good grasp of the concepts". Early sections deliberately keep one representation at a time and a familiar two spin-1/2 system before extending to higher-dimensional product spaces.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learning goals established in advance; iterative validation with faculty and student think-aloud interviews
#### Constraints
- Sequencing choices were made to avoid cognitive overload in upper-level QM content

### Target Learners
- upper-level undergraduate physics students

### Learning Goals
- developing a functional grasp of quantum formalism concepts such as coupled and uncoupled representations
