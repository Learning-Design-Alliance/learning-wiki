# Offer teacher professional development free-of-charge, facilitated by an interdisciplinary expert team that explicitly connects astronomy content, pedagogy, and technology

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
SATTAA was designed as a free-of-charge teacher professional development program in which an interdisciplinary team of experts connects astronomy content, pedagogy, and technology explicitly. The authors state that when designing the program "we agreed that the program should be free-of-charge to participating teachers, that it should be facilitated by an interdisciplinary team of experts and connect astronomy content, pedagogy, and technology explicitly", with the goal of improved and inclusive STEM education focused on teachers at schools in need.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The program must be offered free-of-charge to participating teachers.
- Facilitation must be by an interdisciplinary team of experts.
- Astronomy content, pedagogy, and technology must be connected explicitly in the sessions.
#### Constraints
- The authors state equitable access remains flawed: school funding is inequitable, available technologies are disparate, and many teachers work other jobs in summer, so they are seeking funds for teacher stipends.

### Target Learners
- Future and current secondary and elementary school teachers, especially those at schools serving minority and economically disadvantaged learners

### Learning Goals
- Astronomy content knowledge beyond what state science standards require
- Hands-on methods for teaching astronomy topics
- Integration of technology in astronomy instruction
