# Integrated framework linking causal attributions, self-regulation, and achievement motivation as dynamically interconnected processes

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The article integrates Weiner's causal attribution theory, Zimmerman's self-regulated learning theory, and expectancy-value theory into one intervention model. It conceptualizes the constructs as "dynamically interconnected processes in which attributional beliefs shape motivational expectancies, in turn, expectancies guide self-regulatory engagement, and sustained regulation reinforces academic performance". This grounds the study's synergistic intervention targeting all three mechanisms simultaneously rather than in isolation.
