# Holography lesson series structured from scratch holograms through thin-film interference to the Fresnel zone plate

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A series of lessons on holography for secondary level II and basic university courses, developed from the three preliminary studies. It starts from the fact that students connect a three-dimensional structure with the hologram concept but do not relate it to interference effects, so "The creation of simple, freely floating figures with the help of 'scratch holograms' provides a motivating and functional introduction". The transition to physical holograms proceeds via interference at thin layers and the Fresnel zone plate, with Moiree models and computer simulations supporting the phases.
