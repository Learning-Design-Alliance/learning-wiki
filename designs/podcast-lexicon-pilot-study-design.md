# Pilot study design: podcast experimental group vs. control group with VKS test and strategy questionnaire

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The pilot study involved 36 students, of whom 30 were present at both data collections. The design compares an experimental group learning vocabulary with self-made German–Italian audio podcasts against a control group learning without podcasts, with classroom observation of both groups, a written survey on vocabulary learning strategies (adapted from Neveling, 2004, changed to an ordinal scale), and the Vocabulary Knowledge Scale (Paribakht & Wesche, 1997) administered before and after the 4-month period. The VKS asks students to rate 30 Italian words on a scale from I (full unfamiliarity) to V (correct grammatical and semantic use in a sentence), yielding 30–150 points.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Two participating teachers with their pupils; pre- and post-test administration; questionnaire at beginning and end
#### Constraints
- The majority of experimental-group students did not create the audio podcasts, citing lack of time and no grade for the project; two teachers with different teaching styles and schools introduced intervening variables

### Target Learners
- Beginning learners of Italian as a foreign language at school

### Learning Goals
- Italian vocabulary acquisition and vocabulary learning strategies
