# Five-step learning process sequence for creative thinking in mathematics lessons

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The model's learning process is a five-step activity sequence: "1) engagement and connection to prior knowledge, 2) encounter of problem and thoughtful incubation, 3) analyzing alternative an d investigating solutions, 4) applying and modifying thinking pattern, and 5) concluding and evaluating creative thinking." It structures each lesson or unit so students connect prior knowledge, confront open-ended problems with incubation time, investigate alternative solutions, modify their thinking patterns, and conclude and evaluate their creative thinking. The sequence was implemented across 30 hours of 9th-grade mathematics instruction.
