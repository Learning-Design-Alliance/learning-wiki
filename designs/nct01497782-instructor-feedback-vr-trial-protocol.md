# Randomized educational trial protocol NCT01497782 comparing instructor feedback with automated feedback only

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The trial is a CONSORT-following randomized educational trial registered as NCT01497782, conducted in a skills laboratory at a centralized university hospital. "Based on a sample size estimation 98 participants will be randomized to an intervention group or a control group", stratified by sex and computer gaming experience, with central concealed computerized randomization by the Copenhagen Trial Unit. Primary outcomes are time and repetitions to reach the predefined proficiency level.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Medical bachelor degree participants with informed consent; exclusion of prior VR simulation experience, more than 3 independent laparoscopic procedures, or Danish language non-fluency
- Complete case analyses with Holm's procedure to adjust P values for multiplicity, plus prespecified worst-case, strong-bias and mild-bias sensitivity analyses for missing values
#### Constraints
- The protocol is prespecified; no outcome results are reported in this article
- Participants are recruited from special interest groups, which the authors note makes them resemble residents more than the average student

### Target Learners
- Medical students in years 4-6 at Copenhagen University Medical School

### Learning Goals
- Efficient training of complex laparoscopic operational tasks to proficiency on a VR simulator
