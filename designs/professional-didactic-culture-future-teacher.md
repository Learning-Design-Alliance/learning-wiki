# Professional and didactic culture of the future teacher: five features of formation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article defines professional and didactic culture as a prerequisite for pedagogical activity that contributes to the self-development of the teacher's professional and personal qualities. It formulates five features of its formation in future history teachers: constant actualization of a valuable attitude towards pedagogical activity, formation of self-improvement skills, the ability to find innovative solutions to pedagogical situations, intellectually and creatively productive cooperation under a cultural approach, and a holistic person-centered process with individual professional growth trajectories. The framework was taken into account in Stage 3 of the study.
