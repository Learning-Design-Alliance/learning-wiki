# Baseline qualification pathway: accredited residency plus dedicated neurointerventional fellowship of at least one year at a high-volume center

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This element is the consensus baseline training pathway for physicians providing intra-arterial acute stroke treatment. It combines residency training with board eligibility or certification and dedicated interventional neuroradiology training under a neurointerventionalist at a high-volume center; the document states "It is preferred that this is a dedicated time (minimum of one year), which occurs after graduating from residency (i.e., a fellowship)." Physicians lacking adequate residency training must spend at least one additional year in clinical neurosciences and neuroimaging before fellowship. Fellowship training must include stroke-specific skills such as microcatheter navigation and complication avoidance and management.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dedicated training under the direction of a neurointerventionalist at a high-volume center
- Physicians without adequate residency training must spend at least one additional year training in clinical neurosciences and neuroimaging before fellowship
#### Constraints
- National accreditation of the training program is strongly preferred but not required; national standards differ in procedure requirements

### Target Learners
- residents and physicians in radiology, neurology or neurosurgery pursuing neuroendovascular training

### Learning Goals
- obtaining access in challenging anatomy
- microcatheter navigation in the cerebral circulation
- use of stroke-specific devices
- complication avoidance and management
