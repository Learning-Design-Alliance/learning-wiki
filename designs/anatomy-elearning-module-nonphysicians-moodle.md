# Anatomy e-learning module for non-physicians on Moodle with interactive task material

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes educational materials developed for training healthcare workers in anatomy on an e-learning platform on Moodle. The working materials covered macroscopic anatomy topics with 22 questions on the gastrointestinal tract, 19 on the spinal cord and 20 on the cardiovascular system, using task types such as "Labelling of arrows, completing gaps in texts, checking correct answers and labelling of images", provided in reproductive (solutions given) and interactive (solutions worked out) versions. The materials were accompanied by a short evaluation form using a 5-point Likert scale.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Identical content base for interactive and reproductive versions so the two formats can be compared
#### Constraints
- The study version was a time-limited 45-minute intervention; the article notes the option of splitting topics or group work was not explicitly mentioned to participants

### Target Learners
- Graduates of medical vocational colleges in healthcare fields
- Experienced physiotherapists in postgraduate CPD

### Learning Goals
- Macroscopic anatomy knowledge (gastrointestinal tract, spinal cord, cardiovascular system)
