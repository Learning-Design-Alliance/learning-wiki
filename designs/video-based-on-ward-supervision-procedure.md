# Video-based, on-ward supervision session procedure with feedback loop and video review

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A session model in which a final-year student performs a self-chosen routine clinical procedure at a real patient's bedside under supervision while being filmed. Afterwards a standardised feedback loop runs: student self-reflective feedback, patient oral feedback, then supervisor feedback, with the supervisor identifying specific issues to focus on before the student watches the video in a quiet room, mostly with the supervisor present but not commenting. The full session took roughly 60–90 minutes across its parts, e.g. supervision with video-taping of "15–20 min (depending on supervised skill)".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- informed consent from patient and student
- a trained supervisor following a structured feedback manual
- camera placement that does not show neighbouring patients
- exclusion of patients with language difficulties, dementia, or psychological instability
