# Semi-flipped, student-centered introductory quantum computing course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An undergraduate introductory quantum computing course taught with a "student-centered, interactive learning approach in a semi-flipped classroom environment," in which students read ahead and come prepared to discuss, work problems, and answer questions in small groups or as a class. Topics ranged from two-level systems and gates through quantum algorithms, using a quantum text, the online Qiskit textbook, and the IBM quantum platform as the basis for the course.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students are encouraged to read ahead and come to class prepared to discuss, work problems and answer questions
#### Constraints
- Shor's algorithm was not taught due to a lack of time at the end of the semester

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics majors taking an introductory quantum computing course

### Learning Goals
- understanding two-level quantum systems
- applying the basic postulates of quantum mechanics in quantum computing
