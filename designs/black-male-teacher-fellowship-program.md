# Black Male Teacher Fellowship Program

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
One LEA created a fellowship for all of its Black male teachers, administrators, and staff in service of a goal to increase Black men in LEA teaching positions "by 25 in the next 5 years." The program creates "a very authentic safe space" and builds a brotherhood to retain Black males in the profession, meeting four times during the school year for two hours each during the workday. Initial meetings use personal-vulnerability exercises, and the fellowship doubles as a recruitment vehicle through tailgates, elementary classroom appearances, and connections with Black Student Unions and educator preparation programs.
