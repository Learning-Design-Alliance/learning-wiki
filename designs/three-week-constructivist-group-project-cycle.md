# Constructivist group projects structured across three weeks with mixed-ability groups

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In the constructivist-based section, each of three group projects spanned three weeks: week one for contacting group members and submitting a plan, week two for solving and submitting the problem, and week three for presenting findings electronically and discussing other groups' work. Groups of "three to four participants with a mix of high and low achieving students as determined by the pretest" were used, which the article says is supported by constructivist theory because students construct knowledge in relation to life experiences.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A study-group area in the online classroom where groups can discuss assigned problems
- Problems complex enough that groups can transcend what individuals could accomplish
#### Constraints
- Student withdrawal in the first weeks of class made establishing and maintaining groups difficult

### Target Learners
- adult distance learners in developmental mathematics

### Learning Goals
- mathematics content knowledge and collaborative problem solving
