# Stepwise Clinical Competency Development Program (SCCDP) for new nurses

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The SCCDP is a structured clinical education program for newly employed nurses, divided into basic and advanced stages tailored to tenure. Each session combined "theoretical content, practice, and simulation-based education methods", expert-validated with a content validity index of .95. The basic stage ran during the first eight weeks of employment alongside preceptorship with four-hour sessions every two weeks; the advanced stage began four months after joining, with four eight-hour sessions at two-month intervals. Clinical nurse educators delivered sessions and feedback, supported by a Padlet bulletin board, individual interviews, and one-on-one shadowing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered by clinical nurse educators with structured feedback
- Sequential delivery aligned to new nurses' tenure
- Expert-validated curriculum content and materials
#### Constraints
- Evaluated at a single hospital with a convenient sample, so generalization requires caution per the authors

### Target Learners
- New nurses with less than one year of clinical experience

### Learning Goals
- Basic and advanced clinical knowledge
- Clinical performance ability
- Emergency, respiratory, renal, and circulatory nursing competencies
