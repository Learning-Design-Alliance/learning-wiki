# NASA summer program training CPS teachers and students in multiwavelength astronomy

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 2–3 week summer program in which CPS teachers and some of their students were introduced to the multiwavelength lessons and trained in astronomy at various wavelengths. Students conducted research under supervision and presented projects at the end. The article states students "were allowed to keep" the funded computational device after the program, and staff collected devices nightly for charging, which "provided incentive to the students to attend every day."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Grant funding must include teacher stipends, staffing, lunch provision, and a computational device per student
#### Constraints
- Devices were charged overnight on site because classrooms lacked charging stations; participation depended on independent grant funding

### Target Learners
- Chicago Public Schools teachers and their students

### Learning Goals
- astronomical inquiry skills
- pedagogical training for science inquiry
- in-depth content-area knowledge
