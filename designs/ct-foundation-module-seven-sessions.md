# A structured critical thinking foundation module for postgraduate trainees: seven sessions, fifteen teaching hours, validated competencies

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes a structured CT module developed by a five-member core group through a systematic competency-selection process and validated internally by three and externally by five medical education unit members. It comprised "seven sessions which covered all the competencies": thinking routines and styles, rationale behind CT, sequential steps of CT, tools for practicing CT, critical reading/writing/communication/reflection, barriers to CT, and practical application in healthcare scenarios. It was delivered as a 15-hour, 2-day workshop in two batches of 26 participants, using videos, role plays, text reading and analysis, essay writing, matching quizzes, case scenario discussions, and PowerPoint presentations.
