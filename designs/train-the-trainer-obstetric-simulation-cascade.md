# Train-the-trainer model cascading simulation-based obstetric training from master trainers to local facilitators to senior house officers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 2 against) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A cascaded training delivery model in which "training was cascaded down from master trainers to local facilitators, and then to the learners, who were the SHOs." Master trainers certified by institutions such as EuSim or the Center for Medical Simulation delivered a 4-day train-the-trainer program to 14 local facilitators, of whom 12 were certified after an assessment session. Local facilitators then delivered a 1-day (8 h) mono-professional simulation-based training to fixed clusters of 6 to 9 SHOs, with half-day refresher sessions every 7 weeks and an annual refresher for facilitators.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Local facilitators selected for clinical and teaching experience, compensated for lost clinical income, and offered a 1-day annual refresher training
#### Constraints
- Training all obstetric ward staff was not feasible due to financial and logistical challenges, so only SHOs were trained

### Target Learners
- Senior house officers in obstetrics at a national referral hospital in Uganda

### Learning Goals
- Medical-technical skills for obstetric emergencies
- Teamwork skills for obstetric emergency coordination
