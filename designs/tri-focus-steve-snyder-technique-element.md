# Tri Focus Steve Snyder technique (TFSST): a peripheral-reading technique stressing fun and motivation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
TFSST is the comparison technique in this study. The article states it "is a technique that prioritizes reading peripherals. It stresses fun reading activities. There is no direct correction for mistakes, since the main aim is building students' interest and motivation." According to the article, it can be taught to children to develop peripheral vision with tri focus training, and its founder describes it as changing reading from one word at a time to reading more than one word at a time. In this study it served as the control-class treatment for teaching reading comprehension.
