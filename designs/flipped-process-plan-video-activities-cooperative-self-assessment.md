# Flipped classroom process plan combining pre-class video and activities, in-class cooperative learning, and post-class self-assessment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's planned process for the unit: students watch video lectures and use the instructional material before class, then "conduct cooperative learning activities in the classroom" guided by the teacher using the same activities, and after the unit complete web-assisted self-assessment plus a teacher-administered quiz. The material serves as pre-class individual study and as an alternative after-class measurement and evaluation tool.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Theoretical information must be learned before class so classroom time can go to cooperative activities
