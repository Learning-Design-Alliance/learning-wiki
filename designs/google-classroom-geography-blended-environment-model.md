# A model of the information and education environment for blended geography learning based on Google Classroom

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article develops a model (Fig. 1) of an information and education environment in which Google Classroom serves as "an educational interactive tool that allows creating an informatively rich educational environment integrating the Google Docs text editor, Google Drive cloud storage, Gmail and other applications". Content delivery, visualization and control tools (Docs, Drive, Presentations, YouTube, Maps, Earth, Sheets, Forms) support revealing discipline content and monitoring achievement. Interaction among learning subjects occurs both in-class and at a distance via e-mail, e-conferencing and chats.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Integration of Google applications (Docs, Drive, Gmail, Forms, Sheets, etc.) into a single course environment
- Interaction of all learning subjects (student-student, student-group, teacher-student) in both distance and in-class modes
#### Constraints
- The article notes an "inadequate level of readiness for their implementation by university lecturers and students" for modern ICTs and insufficient material and technical support in some classrooms

### Target Learners
- geography students in higher education (Bachelor and Master levels)

### Learning Goals
- professional geographic competencies
- map and cartography skills
- monitoring and control of academic achievements
