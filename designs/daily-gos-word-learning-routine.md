# Daily graphic-organizer word-learning routine combining modeling, guided practice, cooperative construction and display of organizers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The experimental condition followed a recurring lesson routine: (a) modeling graphic organizers, (b) guided practice, (c) reading a selected textbook text, (d) generating comprehension questions, (e) filling in organizers with visuals, (f) using target words in clarifying sentences, (g) discussing word meanings, and (h) "hanging the organizers on classroom walls on which students can see and be proud of their products". Three organizer types were used: concept definition map, circle thinking map and word-questioning strategy, with think-pair-share for partner work.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- One day of professional development for implementing the instructional practices, plus lesson plans aligned to the social studies curriculum scope and sequence
#### Constraints
- Implemented in 40-minute periods, three days a week over approximately 7 weeks in fourth-grade social studies

### Target Learners
- fourth-grade elementary students

### Learning Goals
- word-learning of social studies vocabulary, including definitions, examples-nonexamples, illustrations and sentence usage
