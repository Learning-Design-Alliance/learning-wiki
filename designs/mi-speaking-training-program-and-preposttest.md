# MI-based speaking training program with speaking skills pre-posttest for pre-service EFL teachers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The researcher developed a multiple-intelligences-based training program to enhance the speaking skills of pre-service teachers, delivered through a Student's Workbook with supplementary material on everyday language functions. The accompanying speaking pre-posttest comprised four parts (oral presentations, requests, suggestions, advice), with a written total of 36 points and an oral presentation component of 30 points scored by rubric. The test was jury-validated and achieved inter-rater reliability of 0.911 (Pearson, p ≤ .01) between two independent raters.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Jury review by TEFL and linguistics specialists for test clarity, suitability, and scoring
- Two trained raters scoring with a shared rubric for inter-rater reliability
#### Constraints
- The program targeted four specific speaking subskills only, as delimited in the study's stated limitations

### Target Learners
- Fourth-year pre-service teachers of English (EFL)

### Learning Goals
- Oral presentations, making requests, making suggestions, giving advice
