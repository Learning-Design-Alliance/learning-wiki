# Integrated female–male genital examination teaching programme using trained teaching associates in a simulated clinical setting

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A collaborative programme developed by two university departments and a sexual health service, consisting of an introductory session plus a 3–4 hour practical session in which students practise on models, then each undertake a female and a male genital examination with a trained teaching associate and a clinical tutor. Each practical session "can accommodate up to 16 medical students at a time by running four parallel streams of dedicated clinic rooms". TAs act as real patients and provide patient feedback; tutors guide the examination and give constructive feedback with the TA.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained and paid teaching associates recruited and trained by the sexual health service
- Clinical tutors from multiple disciplines who agreed on intended learning outcomes and session format
- Preparatory online materials and video recordings reviewed before the practical session
- Efficient administration to organise students, TAs, tutors and reserves
#### Constraints
- Organisation of up to 16 students, eight TAs, four tutors and reserves per session is complex
- Quality of experience was dependent on the individual tutor

### Target Learners
- senior (5th-year) medical students

### Learning Goals
- performing female and male genital examinations
- communicating appropriately with patients during intimate examination
