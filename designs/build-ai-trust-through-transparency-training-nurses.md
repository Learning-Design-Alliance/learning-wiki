# Build nurses' AI trust through transparency, clinical examples and training to enhance AI competence

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that hospitals enhance nurses' AI competence by building AI trust through multiple mechanisms. Specifically, "Hospitals can reduce AIU and enhance AIT by ensuring transparency in AI decision‐making, offering clinical examples of AI applications and providing training." This works because trust reduces psychological resistance to AI use and increases perceived competence. Nursing administrators should also involve nurses in AI implementation decisions to foster ownership and trust.
