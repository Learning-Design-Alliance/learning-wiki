# Video as a pedagogical tool providing a virtual entry into classrooms and opportunities for analytic thinking unavailable in live observation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article grounds its work in a framework assuming people learn best when actively engaged in interpreting the world, with video providing "a bridge that connects prior learning to new knowledge". Because video is "not live," it enables one to rewind, re-watch, review, and reflect, providing teachers opportunities for analytic thinking that live classroom observations or clinical interviews cannot. Video offers a shared focal representation for professional development and lets teachers discern what is important and compare with their own lived experiences.
