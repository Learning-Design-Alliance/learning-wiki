# Five-round progressive RCDP scenario structure pairing technical objectives with TeamSTEPPS team-performance objectives

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The IP-STAR scenario was segmented into five structured RCDP rounds, each with predetermined learning objectives: Round 1 recognition of arrest, calling for help, CPR initiation, role allocation and monitor attachment; Round 2 high-quality CPR and bag-mask ventilation; Round 3 epinephrine, rhythm checks and the PALS algorithm; Round 4 airway management and reversible causes; Round 5 post-resuscitation care and SBAR handover. Across all rounds, demonstration of TeamSTEPPS elements was the overarching objective, and each iteration recommenced from Round 1 to reinforce prior learning. Hard stops marked actions that must be performed in the recommended way; soft stops allowed performance variability.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Debrief guidance pre-decided as hard stops and soft stops; progression to the next round only once learners demonstrate reasonable proficiency
#### Constraints
- Each iteration commences from Round 1, so later-round content is practiced only within restarted sequences

### Target Learners
- novice undergraduate medical and nursing learners

### Learning Goals
- technical resuscitation skills
- non-technical team performance behaviors
