# AM innovation project: semester-long team capstone prototyping a novel AM capability

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course capstone asks student teams to conceptualize, justify, design, and prototype a novel contribution to AM technology. The article states: "The capstone of the course is a project whereby students are asked to conceptualize, justify, design, and prototype a novel contribution to AM technology." Teams form in week one, face no topical limits beyond semester feasibility, a $1000 budget, and machine shop access, and meet instructional staff at least weekly. Seven Spring 2014 projects included ice cream printing, molten glass printing, local viscosity control polymer printing, biodegradable composites, in-situ scanning, carbon fiber composites, and embedded electronics. Several projects led to patents, future research, and peer-reviewed publications.
