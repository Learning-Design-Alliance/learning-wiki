# Matched-pairs crossover design pattern: equal content, dosage, order, and activity type across treatment and control units

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
This lesson-and-unit-level design pattern pairs an arts-integrated unit with a conventional unit matched in content, dosage, order of content presentation, and type of instructional activity, so that the only systematic difference is the arts-based versus conventional mode of demonstrating knowledge. Students are randomized to one condition for a first unit and cross over to the alternate condition for a second unit, with classroom pairs matched within sites for time of day and within-teacher effects. The article used this pattern to isolate the effect of arts integration on retention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All teachers deliver both conditions; unit pairs must provide the same science content, dosage, and mode of delivery to assure active learning in both conditions
#### Constraints
- Randomization at the student level with IEP designations checked for even distribution across classes; condition reversal in the second session

### Target Learners
- elementary students in matched classroom pairs

### Learning Goals
- retention of academic content in non-arts subjects
