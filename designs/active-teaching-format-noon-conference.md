# ACTIVE teaching format for resident noon conferences

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A structured conference format the authors built from this study's results to give faculty a way to facilitate active learning with minimal faculty development time and effort. "This ACTIVE format was centered on 3–5 focused learning points, clinical questions, small group break-out discussions, and frequent summarization." It is presented as one example of an easily implemented alternative to comprehensive lecturing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty development or guidelines and training for faculty unfamiliar with facilitating active learning.
#### Constraints
- The article notes structured formats like problem-based and team-based learning require large programmatic change and may not fully suit the residency environment; the ACTIVE format is offered as one example needing further work.

### Target Learners
- internal medicine residents
