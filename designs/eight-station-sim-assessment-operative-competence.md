# Pilot eight-station simulation-based assessment of operative competence for higher specialist general surgery trainees

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A time-limited eight-station OSCE-style assessment built on the OSATS examination format, using bio-hybrid models (e.g. ileostomy reversal, emergency laparotomy) and LapSim virtual-reality stations (laparoscopic appendicectomy, cholecystectomy, right hemi-colectomy vessel ligation). Procedures were selected via a modified CAMES needs-assessment Delphi process. Performance was scored with an equally weighted modified Procedure-Based Assessment checklist plus OSATS tool, with a 30-min briefing and modified Angoff pre-assessment standard setting.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Examiners familiarized with format, tools, and models prior to the assessment
- rater training conducted before the assessment
- pre-assessment standard setting using a modified Angoff method
#### Constraints
- The article states pilot data with a limited number of trainee participants does not yet support implementation in informing high-stakes assessment outcomes
- due to time and feasibility constraints, key component tasks rather than entire procedures were simulated at some stations

### Target Learners
- higher specialist trainees (ST5–ST8) in general surgery

### Learning Goals
- operative competence sufficient to operate safely as an independent practitioner in general surgery
