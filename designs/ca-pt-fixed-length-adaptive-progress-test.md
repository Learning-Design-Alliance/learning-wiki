# Fixed-length computer adaptive progress test (CA-PT) with Rasch-calibrated item bank

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 135-MCQ digital progress test built from a bank of 3400 Rasch-calibrated questions drawn from 30 previous linear tests, delivering "120 calibrated questions, and 15 non-adaptive pretest questions" per student according to the PT blueprint. Six non-adaptive starter questions give a first ability estimate, after which the algorithm adapts item difficulty; navigation is unidirectional and the score is the estimated ability level (theta). It was administered in TestVision in an authentic exam setting.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A calibrated item bank with difficulty and discrimination parameters, blueprint labels for category and discipline, and enemy-item exclusion rules
- A pre-determined item-selection algorithm and digital delivery platform
#### Constraints
- Navigation is only unidirectional, unlike the conventional PT where students could review and change answers
- The question-mark option of the conventional PT is absent

### Target Learners
- medical students across all years of a six-year curriculum

### Learning Goals
- reliable assessment of end-of-curriculum medical functional knowledge at each student's ability level
