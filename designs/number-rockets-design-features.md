# Scripted small-group Tier 2 intervention with mastery-based topic progression, individualized fact practice, and a points-based behavior management system

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The intervention combines several recurring design features: highly prescribed scripted lessons with specified feedback and prompting; mastery criteria that allow skipping additional lessons once the whole group meets criteria; individualized flashcard fact practice whose "difficulty of the flashcards increased with the skill of individual student"; and a positive-reinforcement behavior system awarding points for accomplishment, mastery, and randomly timed engagement checks, with small rewards roughly every two sessions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interventionists able to follow a prescribed lesson script and prepare individualized flashcard decks before each lesson
- A screening procedure to identify at-risk students before assignment to groups
#### Constraints
- Fact practice difficulty is independent of the group's progression through the lessons

### Target Learners
- Grade 1 students at risk for mathematics difficulties in Tier 2 RtI settings

### Learning Goals
- Early mathematics proficiency and addition/subtraction fact fluency
