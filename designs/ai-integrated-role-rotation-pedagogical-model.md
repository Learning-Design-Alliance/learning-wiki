# AI-integrated role-rotation pedagogical model conceptualizing role rotation as a mechanism for structuring critical human-AI interaction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The model combines four interconnected components: a digital educational environment, integration of generative AI tools, case-based learning, and collaborative role-based interaction with role rotation. Grounded in socio-constructivist, reflective, and experiential perspectives, it conceptualizes AI as "a mediating cognitive and reflective tool embedded within case-based learning, role rotation, and collaborative reflection." Its novel contribution is treating role rotation "not merely as a participation strategy but as a pedagogical mechanism for structuring critical human-AI interaction in professional learning," requiring students to reconsider AI-generated outputs from analytical, practical, and reflective positions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students rotate through all four professional roles across iterative learning cycles
- AI use is embedded in case-based tasks with prompts requiring comparison with theory and contextual adaptation
#### Constraints
- Developed and examined in gifted-education coursework for pre-service educational psychologists; findings are context-specific qualitative evidence of engagement, not demonstrated competence gains

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate pre-service educational psychologists

### Learning Goals
- Professional reasoning and case analysis
- Intervention planning and contextual adaptation
- Critical evaluation of AI-generated outputs
- Reflective practice
