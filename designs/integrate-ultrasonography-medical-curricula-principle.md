# Integrate ultrasonography into medical school curricula starting from the first year

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors argue that because evidence-based echography applications in care practices have increased, "It is time to strive for the integration of ultrasonography into the medical school curricula." They recommend beginning as soon as possible, right from the first year of medical education, so that graduates master miniaturised echoscopes as physicians once mastered stethoscopes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Standardised faculty-developed curricula, peer tutoring structures, and equipment including pocket-sized ultrasound machines
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is grounded in the authors' own programme experience rather than a controlled evaluation reported in this article

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate medical students

### Learning Goals
- first-level echography competence for future physicians
