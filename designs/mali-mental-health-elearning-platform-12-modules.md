# Mental health e-learning platform with 12 interactive modules for primary care in Mali

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An e-learning platform comprising an introduction and 11 modules covering disorders and psychosocial support (schizophrenia, depression, bipolar, anxiety, child/adolescent psychiatry, agitation, addictions, psychosocial support), completable at learners' own pace in up to 15 hours. "Each module included animated text and graphics with a voice‐over recording, so that both visual and auditory memories could be leveraged. The modules also included frequent quizzes to make the experience more interactive and engaging." Interactive webinars led by a local psychiatrist complemented the modules, and completion earned a certificate signed by the Dean of FMOS, WASP and UNFM.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Registration and questionnaires must be completed online; modules can be downloaded and completed off-line.
- Validation of specific modules was a prerequisite to attend the corresponding webinars.
#### Constraints
- Difficulty in connectivity, electricity issues and low information technology literacy reported as e-learning downsides in LMICs

### Target Learners
- rural general practitioners and primary healthcare professionals in Mali

### Learning Goals
- diagnosing and managing mental disorders at primary care level, including addictions, child and adolescent disorders and psychosocial support
