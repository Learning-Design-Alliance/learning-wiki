# Particle-lifetime measurement techniques as a course thread from microseconds to yoctoseconds

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A course thread covering how lifetimes of unstable particles and nuclei are measured across dozens of orders of magnitude: direct observation of muon tracks enabled by relativistic time dilation, vertex-detector reconstruction of picosecond-scale charmed and beauty hadron decays, the crystal blocking technique for attosecond nuclear half-lives, and the energy-time uncertainty relation tau = hbar/Gamma for zeptosecond and yoctosecond ranges, exemplified by the tetraneutron width of about 1.75 MeV corresponding to a 0.4 zs lifetime.
