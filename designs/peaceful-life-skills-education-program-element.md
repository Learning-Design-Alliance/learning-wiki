# Peaceful Life Skills Education Program for preschool children

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · no studies recorded yet

## Description
A 12-week peace-education program developed by the author and delivered to 5-year-olds one hour per week, targeting skills of "recognition of emotions, development of self-esteem, communication, empathy, respect for differences, anger control, conflict resolution, cooperation, love of nature, and peace". Sessions used pictures, stories, emotion cards, puppets, drama, games, music, arts and experiments, with narration, discussion, question-and-answer and observation methods. The author prepared the environment weekly, ensured all children participated in small and large groups, and closed each day with assessment and children's comments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- One hour per week for 12 weeks delivered during the whole learning process
- Materials and environment prepared by the instructor before each activity
- Classroom teachers refrain from discussing the applications to maintain external validity
#### Constraints
- Studied only with 5-year-old children at one independent kindergarten in Malatya; the study group was not randomly selected

### Target Learners
- 5-year-old preschool children

### Learning Goals
- social adaptation
- social skills
- empathy
- anger control
- conflict resolution
- cooperation
