# School-within-a-school career academy structure with sustained student-teacher clusters

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
This pattern organizes a small learning community inside a larger high school. "A small learning community is formed within the larger high school by clustering three to five teachers and 50–75 students per grade." Teachers are drawn from academic and career-related disciplines and remain with students from year to year, with one lead teacher administering the academy and liaising with administrators and employer partners. Students take two to four academy courses a year, with block-scheduled academy classes in the morning followed by regular classes in the afternoon.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clustering three to five teachers and 50–75 students per grade
- Teachers remaining with students from year to year
- A lead teacher with responsibility for administration and employer liaison
- Block-scheduled academy-oriented classes
#### Constraints
- The report describes this structure as implemented in the evaluated academies; the comparison group attended regular high school classes, and 7.2% of comparison students enrolled in an academy at some point

### Target Learners
- High school students in grades 9–12 in urban districts

### Learning Goals
- Integrated academic and career-technical learning
- Personalized attention from teachers and peer collaboration
