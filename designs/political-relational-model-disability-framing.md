# Political/relational model of disability as the framing for rehumanizing mathematics professional development

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The study situated disability in a political/relational model influenced by feminist and queer studies, in place of the traditional medical model focused on diagnosing and fixing individual deficits. This model "centers disability as an embodied experience that intersects with social conditions," defining disability in relation to shifting standards of normalcy and highlighting the curative imaginary. The professional development used this framing, alongside the disability rights movement and critical disability texts, to prompt educators to reassess deficit views of disabled mathematics learners.
