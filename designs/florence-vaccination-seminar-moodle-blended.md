# Blended-learning vaccination seminar delivered via Moodle with pre/post questionnaires

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An 8-hour extracurricular seminar over two consecutive days, held by the Section of Hygiene, Preventive Medicine and Public Health, covering the Italian National Vaccination Plan 2017-2019, vaccine safety, and risk communication. The article states "the teaching methodology of the Blended Learning was chosen, which involved alternation of frontal lessons and distance activities", with questionnaires administered through the Moodle learning management system.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Attendance at the lecture was required to complete the post-lecture questionnaire
- Questionnaires were anonymous and administered through the Moodle LMS
#### Constraints
- Optional and non-curricular, so the sample was self-selected and relatively small

### Target Learners
- Medical students in years IV-VI of the Degree Courses in Medicine and Surgery

### Learning Goals
- Knowledge of the Italian immunization schedule and vaccination recommendations
- Risk communication skills regarding vaccines
