# Five-step scenario-ranking activity structure for ethical AI use

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A lesson-level activity of five core elements with optional extensions, typically taking about thirty minutes: understanding the tools, ranking scenarios, identifying key boundaries, connecting to academic integrity frameworks, and class discussion. Students work in small groups to "rank the scenarios from most ethical to least ethical and identify key boundaries," then compare their judgments with the institution's formal definition of academic integrity in whole-class discussion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A set of 10–12 short scenarios involving the use (or non-use) of generative AI in a physics context
- Small-group work followed by instructor-facilitated whole-class discussion
#### Constraints
- Typically takes about thirty minutes but can be extended through further discussion, written reflection, or policy work

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics students

### Learning Goals
- distinguishing AI use that supports learning from AI use that undermines it
- ethical reasoning
- academic integrity awareness
