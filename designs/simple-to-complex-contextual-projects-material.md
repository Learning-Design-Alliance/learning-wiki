# Learning material structured from simple to complex with contextual examples, illustrations, and integrated writing projects

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The material's design pattern arranges content in good order from introduction through main concepts, examples, illustrations and problem examples, moving from easy to difficult texts and finally abstract passages, with contextual examples from daily life serving as models and four projects integrated for guided practice. The article reports that "the projects steps available in the learning material are able to guide the students to learn Bahasa Indonesia" and that contextual examples are used as a model to guide essay writing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Examples integrated from students' daily-life contexts; supporting figures, tables and illustrations relevant to the text
#### Constraints
- Validated for senior high school Bahasa Indonesia under the Indonesian national curriculum 2013

### Target Learners
- First-year senior high school students

### Learning Goals
- Constructing and writing exposition text with a process in academic reports
