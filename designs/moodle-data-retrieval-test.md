# Moodle-based on-line Data Retrieval Test for first-year physics laboratory notebooks

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A Data Retrieval Test is an open-book assessment in which "the notebook is taken into the exam room and questions are asked on its contents". UCL's implementation is an on-line Moodle quiz of multiple choice, select-the-right-word, numerical and written answers, usually 15 to 20 questions with 3 hours allowed, taken under conditions where only the laboratory notebook may be used. Written answers require page numbers so markers can cross-reference the notebook.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must have been trained in laboratory notebook practice, including a practice experiment with a guide-document at the start of first term
- One computer per student in the teaching laboratory
- Twice-per-term one-to-one oral assessments of the notebook to give in-flight corrections
#### Constraints
- Only the laboratory notebook may be used; no pens, pencils, mobile phones or internet resources
- The not-answering-the-question allowance applies only to written answer questions

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate physics students

### Learning Goals
- keeping laboratory notebooks that comply with subject best practice
