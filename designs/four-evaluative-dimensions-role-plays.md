# Four evaluative dimensions for observer reporting in ethics role plays

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Students not playing a role are divided into observer groups that report on four dimensions of the communication process: the normative, performative, informative and responsibility dimension. Reporting on the normative dimension shows whether students "can recognize ethical issues in the discussion as ethical issues"; the performative dimension concerns authenticity, credibility and openness or manipulation; the informative dimension concerns clarity and consistency of information; the responsibility dimension involves a moral judgment on performance and result from the perspective of course-taught moral notions. The dimensions were already developed in the ToFlyOrNotToFly role play.
