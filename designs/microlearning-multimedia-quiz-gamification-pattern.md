# Microlearning lesson design combining chunked content, dual-channel multimedia, concluding quizzes, and gamification

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each app lesson follows a repeating structure: a short chunked microlecture, text combined with relevant visuals, and a closing brief quiz, embedded in a gamified app shell. The article states "each lesson concluded with a brief quiz to promote active engagement with the material", a shift from passive reading to active recall that also lets learners self-monitor progress. Short lessons fit brief intervals in students' daily routines.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Text-based content combined with relevant visual elements; quizzes integrated into each lesson
#### Constraints
- The cumulative cognitive load of frequent interactions over a semester remains insufficiently studied

### Target Learners
- Medical students preparing for clinical skills examinations

### Learning Goals
- Knowledge retention, engagement, and self-directed learning
