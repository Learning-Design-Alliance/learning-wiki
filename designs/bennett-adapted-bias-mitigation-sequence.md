# Adaptation of Bennett's intercultural competency framework sequencing knowledge before bias awareness before skill application

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The intervention's delivery was designed to reflect an adaptation of Bennett's intercultural competency framework. In this model, learners first acquire knowledge and understanding about implicit bias to overcome denial, then develop acceptance of implicit bias's effect on healthcare disparities by recognizing bias within themselves, and are ultimately motivated to acquire and apply effective communication skills where implicit bias is likely to arise. The article treats recognition and self-awareness of bias as the critical first steps in bias mitigation training.
