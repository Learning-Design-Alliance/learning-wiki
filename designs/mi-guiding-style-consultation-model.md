# Consultation model of flexibly moving between directing, following and guiding communication styles

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The intervention development was guided by a consultation model, drawn from the Motivational Interviewing approach, which describes the value of moving flexibly between directing, following and guiding communication styles when talking about behaviour change. A stakeholder reference group considered this model during development, and the training programme emphasises a guiding style when consulting with patients about behaviour change. The model treats targeting as something that happens within the consultation, as the practitioner shifts style and topic according to the needs of the patient, supported by a flexible menu of strategies from which practitioner and patient select a topic according to need.
