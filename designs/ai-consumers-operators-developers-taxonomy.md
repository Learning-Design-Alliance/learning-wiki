# Three-category taxonomy of K-12 AI learners: AI Consumers, AI Operators, and AI Developers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article organizes high school AI learners into three categories drawn from the K-12 AI education research community: "those who will be AI end-users in their everyday lives and careers, those who will need to implement AI algorithms, and those who will research and advance AI algorithms." AI Consumers (all students) need to learn what is possible with AI and its impact; AI Operators need to understand algorithm varieties, strengths and weaknesses to make informed decisions; AI Developers need deep algorithmic understanding including estimating space and time complexity. ARIN-561's design targets appropriate takeaways for each category.
