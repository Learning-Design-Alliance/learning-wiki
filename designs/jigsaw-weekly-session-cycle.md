# Jigsaw weekly session cycle: expert teaching, individual quiz, group mind map, reflective evaluation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each thematic session in both courses follows a repeating cycle that reinforces the cooperative routine. Students first read individually, discuss in expert groups, and teach their piece in base groups; then, as the article describes, "In the following class, students completed an individual formative quiz through Socrative to assess comprehension. Afterwards, they presented group-created mental maps of the assigned pattern category." Sessions conclude with a self- and peer-evaluation form on participation, clarity of explanation, and group coordination, and "Each session followed the same structure, ensuring consistency across topics and reinforcing the cooperative learning routine."
