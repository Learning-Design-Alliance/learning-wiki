# One-day PBL program on AI ethics centered on a clinical video case

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 1-day (approximately 7 hours) mandatory program for fourth-year medical students built on the PBL model, addressing three competency gaps: understanding regulations, discussing ethical issues, and understanding AI applications. It opens with a video case in which "a medical AI consultation app" diagnoses left shoulder pain as muscle pain and an anxious son questions the app's reliability. The schedule pairs a foundational AI lecture with PBL sessions (problem clarification, self-directed learning, 70-minute group work on shared Google slides) and a case-review debriefing lecture, with MCQ and essay assessments at three time points.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Five tutors facilitating with a standardized tutor guide
- Key domestic AI guidelines distributed as group work materials
- Small groups of 7-8 students with shared Google slides for collaborative group work
#### Constraints
- Content and examples mainly focus on generative AI
- Limited to considering the explanation's content and did not provide opportunities for practical application such as role-play
- Evaluated only for short-term effects at a single university in Japan

### Target Learners
- fourth-year undergraduate medical students

### Learning Goals
- understanding legal and ethical issues of AI use in medicine
- explaining AI diagnosis credibility to patients
- understanding AI applications in clinical care
