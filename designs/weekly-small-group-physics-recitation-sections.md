# Weekly small-group recitation sections (~25 students) with collaborative problem solving and PER-based tutorial or ranking-task activities

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The reformed section gave up one of its three weekly lectures so students could attend a weekly recitation of approximately 25 students led by a graduate teaching assistant with an undergraduate learning assistant present. In each session, students "worked in collaborative groups of three to four to solve a single quantitative problem" and also worked through a tutorial or ranking task inspired by physics and astronomy education research curricula, receiving nominal participation credit; no new content was introduced.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A graduate teaching assistant and an undergraduate learning assistant for each session, trained by the course instructor in weekly meetings
- Tutorial or ranking task activities adapted from research-validated PER curricula
#### Constraints
- Sessions introduced no new content
- The recitation replaced one 50-minute lecture per week, keeping total weekly class time identical between the two sections

### Target Learners
- introductory calculus-based physics students

### Learning Goals
- quantitative problem-solving
- conceptual understanding
