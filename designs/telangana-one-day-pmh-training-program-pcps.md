# One-day PMH training program for primary care physicians (four modules with PHQ-9/GAD-7 hands-on practice and case vignettes)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A one-day training program delivered to government-sector primary care physicians across 30 Telangana districts, organized into four modules covering mental well-being, assessment of anxiety and depression, psychological interventions, and safe psychotropic use with referrals. It included "Hands-on training using PHQ-9 and GAD-7 screening tools" and case-based group discussions. Pre and post assessments used multiple-choice questions based on case vignettes developed by two perinatal psychiatrists and validated by three experts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Facilitation by psychiatrists trained in perinatal psychiatry; pre-recorded expert talks to ensure uniformity across groups
#### Constraints
- The program focused on common PMH conditions like anxiety and depression while neglecting other conditions such as OCD, somatization disorder, and PTSD; the assessment had no practical component

### Target Learners
- Primary care physicians / medical officers in government primary care

### Learning Goals
- Identify perinatal risk factors, depression and anxiety symptoms and severity
- Safe psychotropic prescribing in pregnancy and lactation
- Referral decision-making
