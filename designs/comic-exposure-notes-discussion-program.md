# Extra-credit exposure-and-learning program with note-taking and guided discussion

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The study's treatment was worked into normal class structure: students could earn extra credit by reading or watching and summarizing eight comic strips or cartoons (three historical, five Sport Rivalry Man). To foster comprehension and retention, the class first watched one story of each type together and was "led in a brief discussion of rivalry and the stories." Notes required describing characters and scenarios and explaining in their own words what the comics meant.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- students turn in notes on the assigned comics or cartoons
- an in-class viewing and brief discussion to model note-taking and foster retention
#### Constraints
- offered as extra credit within on-campus classes; online classes serving as controls received no exposure

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and graduate students in sport management classes

### Learning Goals
- learning about the rivalry phenomenon
- modeling appropriate behavior toward out-group members
