# Mobile phone-based educational website for the Strategies of Teaching and Learning course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A Blackboard-delivered mobile educational website developed by the researchers after analyzing E-learning instructional design models. The course content was prepared as PowerPoint lectures and split for small screens: "the web's content was split into smaller and shortened pieces to fit the mobile phones' monitors or screens." It offered a Content link plus links for discussions, groups, poster, and tools to encourage student-student and student-instructor interaction, and was compatible with Android and IOS devices used by participants.
