# Four acute stroke nursing care bundles for surveillance at Landspítali University Hospital

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes four distinct nursing care bundles developed for a small stroke unit: thrombolysis and endovascular treatment, ischemic stroke without recent recanalization therapy, risk of malignant edema and suspected elevated intracranial pressure, and intracerebral hemorrhage. The authors state "We identified a need for four distinct care bundles that, while sharing many common care components, differed in important aspects" and that "Each care bundle comprised four to five essential components, with overlapping elements across all pathways". Common components include position and mobility, fever, sugar, swallowing (FeSS), nutritional intake, neurostatus, and vital signs, with a bleeding-risk component for the thrombolysis bundle and shared red flags for notifying the stroke physician.
