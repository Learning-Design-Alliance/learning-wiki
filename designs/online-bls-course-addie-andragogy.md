# Online BLS course built with the ADDIE model, Andragogy and Significant Learning Theory

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A 20-hour online course on adult basic life support developed on the University of São Paulo's Moodle Stoa platform, structured with "the analysis, design, development, implementation, evaluation (ADDIE) model of instructional design, based on Andragogy and the Significant Learning Theory". Objectives followed the Bloom taxonomy; learning objects included virtual classes, interactive exercises, videos, and an infographic. Eight tutor-nurses supported participants in the virtual learning environment, and the course was evaluated by specialists before student access.
