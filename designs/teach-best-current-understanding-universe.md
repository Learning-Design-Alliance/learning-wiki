# Teach students our best current understanding of the universe rather than deferring to an older adequate paradigm

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article argues that we have "an obligation to teach our best understanding of the universe." Just as an architect could not argue that the flat Earth paradigm is good enough, the Newtonian paradigm being 'good enough' does not justify avoiding Einsteinian concepts. The article argues the public fascination with gravitational wave discovery shows demand for understanding, and that modernising the school science curriculum will benefit gender equity in physical science.
