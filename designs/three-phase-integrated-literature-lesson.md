# Three-phase integrated literature lesson: Language, Cultural, and Enrichment Consideration

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's lesson plan teaches literature in three phases. In Language Consideration, students read a literary text at home with a vocabulary list including culturally meaningful items; in Cultural Consideration, the instructor provides background and cultural information in a pre-reading stage; in Enrichment Consideration, the class holds an instructor-facilitated discussion followed by post-reading writing and language activities. The author states these stages "assisted students in acquiring language knowledge effectively and facilitated their cultural competence and interpretative abilities".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A literary text, vocabulary list with cultural items, instructor-provided background information, and facilitated discussion with post-reading activities
#### Constraints
- Evaluated over eight weekly sessions of two and a half hours with Thai English-major undergraduates

### Target Learners
- Thai EFL university students majoring in English

### Learning Goals
- literature knowledge
- critical thinking skills
- positive attitudes toward reading literature
