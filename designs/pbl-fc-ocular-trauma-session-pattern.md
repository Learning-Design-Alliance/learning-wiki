# PBL-FC ocular trauma session pattern: pre-class team inquiry on socially relevant topics, in-class team presentations with Q&A, and instructor-facilitated whole-class discussion

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A lesson-level instructional pattern combining flipped pre-class preparation with problem-based small-group inquiry. One week before class, teams receive texts, PPT slides, e-books, videos and online platform resources and investigate topics "aligned with current social issues in China" such as fireworks-related ocular injuries. In the two 40-min sessions each team delivers a 10-min presentation, a 5-min Q&A follows, and the instructor facilitates a 20-min whole-class discussion integrating clinical knowledge with value-based reflection.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Same textbook, teaching content, instructor and total teaching hours across comparison groups; centralized lesson preparation and a detailed teaching protocol observed for fidelity
- Students organized into small teams with assigned group leaders before class
#### Constraints
- Implemented in the relatively straightforward, socially linked Ocular Trauma module; generalizability to more abstract ophthalmology modules (e.g., glaucoma, retinal diseases) needs further validation per the authors

### Target Learners
- third-year undergraduate clinical medicine students in ophthalmology

### Learning Goals
- self-directed learning ability
- empathy
- theoretical ophthalmology knowledge (ocular trauma)
