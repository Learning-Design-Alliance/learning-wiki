# BOPPPS-CBL clinical clerkship sequence: pre-class videos and pre-assessment, in-class case-based participatory learning, post-assessment and summary

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
This pattern operationalizes BOPPPS combined with CBL across a one-week clinical clerkship. Before class, students preview pre-recorded videos and take a pre-assessment; in class, "The instructor developed a representative SECC case and introduced it to the students", after which each student independently made a diagnosis and treatment plan, gave a 10-min speech, and joined question-driven discussion with the instructor as facilitator. After class, a post-assessment test and summary close the loop, with critical-thinking questions or homework extending learning beyond the classroom.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A representative clinical case, an educational platform for pre-class materials, and an instructor who "primarily served as a facilitator" during discussion
#### Constraints
- Evaluated in a brief one-week pediatric dentistry clerkship focused on severe early childhood caries

### Target Learners
- Fourth-year undergraduate dental students on clinical rotation

### Learning Goals
- Diagnosis, radiographic interpretation, treatment planning, and behavior-management strategies for pediatric dental patients
