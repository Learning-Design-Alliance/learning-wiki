# A three-tiered laddered CBME training model for breast surgery: L1 basic skills, L2 minor procedures, L3 advanced surgery

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article introduces a stepwise, competency-based training model that categorizes breast surgery training into three sequential levels: L1 basic skills (ultrasound-guided biopsy, 1 month), L2 minor procedures (venous access port implantation, 2 months), and L3 advanced surgery (breast-conserving surgery, 3 months). Progression to each subsequent level requires completion of predefined objectives and competency requirements of the preceding stage. The model reorganizes case-driven clinical exposure into sequential, competency-based practice without extending the 6-month rotation period. Ultrasound serves as a bridging technology across all three tiers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Completion of predefined objectives and competency requirements of the preceding stage before progression
- A fixed 6-month rotation period within which training time is redistributed rather than expanded
- Ultrasound as a bridging technology across diagnostic and procedural milestones
#### Constraints
- Implemented within a single tertiary hospital setting
- A 6-month training period inadequate for longitudinal competency assessment
- Absence of individualized adaptation strategies for residents with varying baseline abilities

### Target Learners
- Second- and third-year breast surgery residents in standardized training

### Learning Goals
- Ultrasound-guided biopsy competency
- Venous access port implantation competency
- Breast-conserving surgery competency
- Clinical decision-making and patient management
