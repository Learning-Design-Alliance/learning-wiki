# VSSPlus: a post-reading vocabulary strategy integrating technological tools with the Vocabulary Self-Collection Strategy

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
VSSPlus is described as a post-reading variant of Haggard's Vocabulary Self-Collection Strategy that adds technological tools. Students select words from a text, answer three questions about each word, and use tools such as e-Dictionaries, Wordsift, Thinglink, podcasts and wikis to explore meanings, synonyms and definitions. The article states it "requires from its users to integrate various applications of technological tools during its practice" and that technology should be applied generatively, with the instructor acting as a facilitator of discussion, word selection and team formation.

### Learning Goals
