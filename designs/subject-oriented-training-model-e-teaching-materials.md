# Model of subject-oriented training of future humanities teachers based on e-teaching materials

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article presents a model of subject-oriented training of future humanities teachers built on e-teaching materials, exemplified in the 'Teaching proficiency' course. As printed, it "consists of the subject oriented module, which includes e -textbook (theoretical, practical and diagnosis modules) and methodological, consisting of multi -level integrative tasks, which allow to implement the student-centered approach." The model links objectives, organizational and methodological conditions, content and results, aiming at formation of knowledge and skills on the course and abilities to solve professional and pedagogical goals based on e-teaching materials.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The model requires e-teaching materials whose content is "supported by text, audio, video, graphics, animation, determined according to didactics."
#### Constraints
- The model is exemplified in the 'Teaching proficiency' course for future History and English language teachers at one university.

### Target Learners
- future humanities teachers (History and English language) at university

### Learning Goals
- subject and methodological knowledge and skills in the 'Teaching proficiency' course
- ability to solve professional and pedagogical goals based on e-teaching materials
