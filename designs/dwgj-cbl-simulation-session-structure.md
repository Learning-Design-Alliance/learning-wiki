# Simulation session structure pairing 20-minute hands-on CBL practice with a 20-minute DwGJ roundtable debriefing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A lesson-level simulation design in which resident subgroups of four rotate hands-on and observer roles during a case scenario, then debrief. The article specifies that "Each 20-min hands-on session was followed by a 20-min DwGJ roundtable debriefing", using the Plus/Delta strategy and Advocacy and Inquiry, with a hands-on member recounting the case evolution and the instructor facilitating analysis and offering solutions, at total teaching hours matched to conventional CBL.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- SimMan 3G simulator and case scenarios at elementary, intermediate, and advanced complexity
- An instructor facilitator to identify procedural variations and guide deeper analysis
#### Constraints
- The duration of some debriefing sessions was not strictly controlled, which occasionally limited the time available for trainees to practice specific technical skills

### Target Learners
- first-year resident physicians across internal medicine, surgery, obstetrics and gynecology, pediatrics, emergency medicine, and intensive care medicine

### Learning Goals
- clinical skills procedures
- clinical reasoning
- teamwork and communication
