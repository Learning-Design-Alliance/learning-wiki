# 12-month spiritual care training protocol for oncology nurses

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A hospital-based continuing-education protocol layered on routine nursing education, consisting of "spiritual care training classes for two weeks every six months, two case sharing sessions each month, and one personal growth book discussion session each month." Content included a group pledge and sharing of feelings, empathy training, positive spiritual education fostering personal growth, reflective logs, suicide prevention, end-of-life care, and life-and-death education. It was delivered by an intervention team of palliative nursing education experts and clinical head nurses specializing in spiritual care.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Senior lecturers in clinical spiritual care education and pastoral counselling, a trained team leader, and hospital approval to incorporate training classes into the workday
#### Constraints
- One intervention content element combined "unique elements of Chinese culture," and the authors state the transcultural validity of the intervention requires further verification

### Target Learners
- Registered clinical nurses with at least 5 years of experience in a cancer hospital

### Learning Goals
- Spiritual health and spiritual well-being of nurses
- Spiritual care competency including assessment, implementation, professionalization, personal and team support, and attitudes towards patient spirituality and communication
