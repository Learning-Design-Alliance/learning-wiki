# Focused coverage of a small number of key mathematical ideas with explicit, systematic instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
ELM's instructional design pattern emphasizes depth over breadth: "The curriculum emphasizes a small number of key ideas in math to provide more focused coverage, instead of trying to teach a comprehensive but more surface set of skills." It pairs this focused coverage with "Explicit and systematic instructional design." The pattern structures a full year of 120 45-minute lessons plus daily calendar activities.
