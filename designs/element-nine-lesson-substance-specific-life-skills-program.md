# Nine-lesson substance-specific life skills program for low-achieving eighth graders

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A school-based prevention unit of nine lessons fully compliant with the existing syllabus, designed for low-achieving eighth graders. It consisted of two parts: "an initial drug-specific module concerning the gateway drug cannabis, and a subsequent substance-nonspecific one, targeting self-esteem and resistance self-efficacy (Table 1)". The substance-specific part was taught in three lessons under three different approaches, while the six substance-nonspecific lessons were taught in a student-centered environment using trust games, describing skills and abilities, and anonymous peer evaluation.

### Learning Goals
