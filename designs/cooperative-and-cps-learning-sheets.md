# Cooperative learning sheets and creative problem-solving learning sheets used in web-based cooperative learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The learning sheets 'contained issues for group discussions during the group learning session' and came in two types: 'cooperative learning sheets' used by both EG1 and EG2, and 'creative problem-solving learning sheets' used only by EG1. Through the use of the learning sheets in group discussion, the author states that 'students' spirit of CL and CPS could be cultivated.' Answers to the learning sheets were released after group discussion to familiarize students with the problems.
