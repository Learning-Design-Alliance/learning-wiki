# Detailed co-designed worksheets and inexpensive laboratory materials for introductory measurement and thermodynamics labs

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The pilot's core materials were a detailed worksheet and low-cost equipment. The worksheet "contains simple operative explanations, all formulas, hints for evaluating and reducing uncertainties, spaces where to write measures and calculations", and was "carefully designed jointly with teachers that had a very concrete idea of the needs of their students". Laboratory organization focused on maintaining students engaged on operational aspects through this worksheet, and all materials were "easily available and inexpensive" to encourage the school to become independent. Each student returned a personal worksheet that was assessed by the teacher.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Joint design with the class teachers who know their students' needs
- Assessment of each student's returned personal worksheet by the teacher
#### Constraints
- Special care was needed to ensure safety, e.g. obtaining boiling water directly into the Dewar vessel, for students not well accustomed to correct laboratory behavior

### Target Learners
- First-class upper secondary vocational school students (ages 14-15) with poor motivation for physics

### Learning Goals
- Direct and indirect measurement of mass, volume and density; thermal equilibrium and heat capacity
