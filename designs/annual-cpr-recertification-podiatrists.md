# Require annual rather than biennial CPR re-certification for practising podiatrists

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that New Zealand podiatrists' mandatory CPR re-certification move from a two-year to a one-year cycle, aligning with Australia, the United Kingdom, and resuscitation councils' advice that skills be refreshed annually. It grounds this in the observed knowledge gaps among a cohort with 98% training coverage: "the authors recommend a change from biennial to annual CPR re-certification for podiatrists in New Zealand". The recommendation is the authors' interpretation of survey findings, not a tested intervention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mandated change would require cooperation of the Podiatrists Board of New Zealand, which administers CPD
#### Constraints
- The article notes the change would need to be weighed against increased financial implications of course fees and time off work

### Target Learners
- New Zealand registered podiatrists maintaining an annual practising certificate

### Learning Goals
- Maintaining current CPR knowledge and basic life support skills
