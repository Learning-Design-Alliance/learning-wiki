# Autopsy Learning Module

## Description
The Autopsy Learning Module is a standardized residency resource combining pre-rotation online training with post-autopsy reflective documentation. The online training addresses "the difference between a usual case and coroner case" and communication with the coroner's office, consent conversations, and breaking bad news. After each autopsy, residents complete Autopsy Self-Reflection Forms documenting learning points and what could have been done differently; the forms are placed in the resident's portfolio and evaluated by the program director at year's end to assess practice-based learning and improvement competency.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Residents complete an online training before starting the ICU rotation; the critical care fellow, senior resident, and interns rotating in the ICU are responsible for completing it.
- Residents fill out an Autopsy Self-Reflection Form after completion of the autopsy, and forms are evaluated by the program director at the end of the year.
