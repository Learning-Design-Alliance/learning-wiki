# PALS three-strategy reciprocal tutoring routine: partner reading and retelling, paragraph shrinking, and prediction relay

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A recurring lesson-level structure in which paired students alternate tutor and tutee roles across three timed activities. During "partner reading and retelling, the stronger reader reads for five minutes, while the weaker reader serves as the coach by identifying errors, initiating correction procedures, and awarding points". In paragraph shrinking, readers generate main idea statements in 10 words or less for one point per correct response; in prediction relay, the reader predicts before reading half a page and evaluates the prediction afterward. Roles switch after each timed segment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher-ranked student pairs formed to decrease reading-ability disparity
- Timed segments of approximately five minutes per role, with students switching roles
- A correction procedure the tutor uses to help the reader amend inaccurate main idea statements
#### Constraints
- In paragraph shrinking, the second reader does not read the same material as the first reader

### Target Learners
- Elementary students in pairs of stronger and weaker readers, including English language learners

### Learning Goals
- Reading fluency, sequencing information, generating main idea statements, and generating and evaluating predictions
