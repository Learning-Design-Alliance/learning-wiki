# A three-stage blended chairside teaching model integrating PBL, CBL, and seminar learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes "a novel blended teaching model for chairside teaching of dental undergraduates: Based on the PBL teaching method, guided by specific cases from the CBL methodology, and supplemented by interdisciplinary seminar discussions." It unfolds across three internship phases: early PBL cases with standardized-patient simulation on head models, mid-phase real clinical cases followed by next-day seminar discussion, and a final phase emphasizing comprehensive examination and general-practice-oriented treatment planning. The model aims to move students "from isolated knowledge points to comprehensive understanding and application."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors with profound medical expertise, robust instructional skills, and rich experience, plus time to curate representative PBL and CBL cases and manage seminars
- Students able to prepare cases with literature review; inadequate literature search skills may result in insufficient preparation
#### Constraints
- May be more time-consuming and cumbersome for students balancing internships with exam preparation or job hunting
- May increase the learning burden

### Target Learners
- fourth-year undergraduate dental students in clinical chairside teaching

### Learning Goals
- clinical diagnostic and treatment thinking
- hands-on clinical abilities in Operative Dentistry and Endodontics
- communication and humanistic care skills
