# Madden et al.'s multidisciplinary STEAM undergraduate curriculum with Student-Initiated Integrative Major

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Madden et al. (2013) describe building from scratch a multidisciplinary STEAM undergraduate curriculum including biology, computer science, mathematics, music education, psychology, theatre and visual arts. STEM disciplines aim to develop analytical skills while artistic disciplines aim to develop creativity and divergent thinking. Students personalize their major (Student-Initiated Integrative Major) including courses from at least two disciplines, work in multidisciplinary problem-solving teams on thematic problems, and complete internships in local companies.
