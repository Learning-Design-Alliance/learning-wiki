# Multiliteracies pedagogy organized around four knowledge processes: experiencing, conceptualizing, analyzing, applying

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The article applies the pedagogy of multiliteracies, in which literacy is treated as active meaning-making across multiple modes rather than sentence-bound rules of spelling and grammar. It states that "The pedagogy of Multiliteracies suggests transformative ways of knowing, the four ‘knowledge processes’: ‘experiencing, conceptualizing, analyzing and applying’", and these processes serve as an outline for educators' pedagogical actions. Each folk-tale thematic unit in the program was designed around these four processes, with tasks such as experiencing the known/new, conceptualizing by naming and with theory, analysing functionally and critically, and applying appropriately and creatively.
