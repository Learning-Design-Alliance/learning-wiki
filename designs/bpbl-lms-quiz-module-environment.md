# bPBL e-learning environment of LMS-based self-study modules with embedded short quizzes

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The bPBL e-learning environment was built on the institutional learning management system and consisted of three self-study lecture modules plus short quizzes supplementing usual PBL practice. "Quizzes related to the learning content were inserted after every few pages in a module" to ensure completion; each module took 15 to 20 min, and tutors could check login information and progress status on the LMS. Online literature resources replaced printed versions, and tests, feedback and guidance were also provided online.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students receive LMS information at freshman orientation and routinely use it; tutors were trained in how to use the system
#### Constraints
- Online quizzes were provided only in the bPBL condition; blended materials were opened to original-PBL students only afterwards to keep opportunities equal

### Target Learners
- fourth-year medical students during internal medicine clinical rotations

### Learning Goals
- self-study skills
- knowledge acquisition for tutorial meetings
