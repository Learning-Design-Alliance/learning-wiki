# HSDM pilot online radiographic caries diagnosis module

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 associational), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A pilot five-module online learning program created at Harvard School of Dental Medicine for radiographic caries diagnosis, built from learning objectives of lectures given by HSDM faculty with interactive lessons including matching and multiple-choice questions. It was uploaded to cloud storage and made available to the Class of 2016 (n=31) for practice on computers or tablets at any point in time, with concepts confirmed by faculty. The module was assessed with three clinical FMX cases and a perception survey.

### Learning Goals
