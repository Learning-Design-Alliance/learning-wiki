# Use double-eye (double-check) control in high-risk pediatric medication administration

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article notes that "establishing a clear policy and standard guidelines for the administration of high-risk medications by healthcare institutions and double-eye control of nurses in high-risk medication administration are among the initiatives recommended by WHO." Double checking catches calculation and administration errors before they reach the patient; a web-based system can supply the second check when staffing prevents two-nurse checking.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

#### Constraints
- The article states double-eye control cannot be performed in every pediatric clinic due to working conditions and a lack of medical staff
