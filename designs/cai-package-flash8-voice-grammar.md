# Macromedia Flash 8 tutorial-mode CAI package and 50-item achievement test on Active and Passive Voice

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The investigators developed and standardized a Computer Assisted Instruction package on Tutorial mode using Macromedia Flash 8 software for learning English Grammar – Voice for eighth-standard students, alongside an Achievement Test on Active Voice and Passive Voice. After item validation through a pilot study, 50 items were retained: 25 items converting Active Voice to Passive Voice and 25 converting Passive Voice to Active Voice. The package served as the experimental treatment and the test as the outcome measure.
