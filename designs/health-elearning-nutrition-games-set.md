# Five nutrition education computer games from Taiwan's health e-learning website

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The study used five computer games provided on Taiwan's health e-learning website: 'Little Dietician,' 'Gifts from Heaven,' 'Saving Health Kingdom,' 'Health Superman's Delicacy Island' and 'Nutrition Supplement Battle.' The first two taught students "to correctly classify the six types of food for a balanced diet," while the others addressed snack reduction, one-day diet observation, and nutrient deficiency diseases via "questions, hints and answers."
