## Description
Michigan established the Diverse Educator Advisory Group in 2021 "to provide recommendations for changes to educator workforce policy", with results under review by department staff. The Michigan Department of Education also hired a director of diversity, equity, and inclusion responsible for developing and supporting internal and external efforts and strategies fostering greater fairness and inclusion in the state's public schools.
