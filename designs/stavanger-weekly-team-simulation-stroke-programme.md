# Weekly in situ team simulation training programme for acute stroke thrombolysis at Stavanger University Hospital

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A team simulation training programme introduced in February 2017 as part of a quality improvement project at a Norwegian stroke centre. Sessions were weekly and "focused on improving procedural adherence and team leadership skills for neurology registrars (eg, improving team situational awareness through briefs and summaries, using closed-loop communication)". Attendance dates per registrar were registered from the first session, and attendance prior to a patient's admission defined the exposure in the cohort analysis.
