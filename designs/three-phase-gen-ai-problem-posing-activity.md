# Three-phase Gen AI-assisted problem-posing activity with pre-training, training video, and post-training reflections

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A self-study activity administered as an extra-credit assignment in which "The study design consisted of three phases completed over one week". In Phase 1 students posed a variation of a recitation problem with a Gen AI platform of their choosing and reflected on the interaction with six guided questions; in Phase 2 they watched a 15-minute training video on Gen AI and prompt engineering; in Phase 3 they repeated the task using what they learned and answered additional reflection questions on training and willingness to continue. Students submitted their Gen AI conversations as data.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- access to a Gen AI platform of the student's choosing
- completion of the training video before Phase 3
#### Constraints
- administered as an extra-credit assignment in an online summer course, which the authors note may have skewed participation toward more agreeable views

### Target Learners
- prospective engineering majors in first-semester calculus-based physics

### Learning Goals
- problem posing as a self-study technique
- effective prompting of Gen AI
