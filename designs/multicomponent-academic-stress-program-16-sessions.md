# Multicomponent academic-stress program: psychoeducation, mindfulness training, and biofeedback-assisted mindfulness across 16 sessions in 8 weeks

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A structured intervention for university students consisting of "16 individual sessions during 8 weeks, divided into 3 phases: pre-test evaluation, multicomponent intervention program, and post-test evaluation". The program integrates three components: one asynchronous online psychoeducation session on academic stress (conceptualization, sources, symptoms, risk and protective factors, coping); ten daily online mindfulness sessions with body scan and breathing awareness meditations; and three in-person biofeedback-assisted mindfulness sessions in which participants meditate while watching an HRV-driven video. It was adapted from the Mindfulness Suite by the Biofeedback Federation of Europe, translated into Spanish.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants must be able to attend pre-test and post-test psychophysiological stress-profile sessions and the in-person biofeedback sessions using ProComp5 equipment recording HRV, BVP, breathing rate, and galvanic skin response.
#### Constraints
- Designed and piloted with high-performing scholarship students during the COVID pandemic; the authors state the sample may not be representative of all university students.

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate university students, specifically high-achieving scholarship students (Leaders of Tomorrow)

### Learning Goals
- Academic stress management and self-regulation of psychophysiological stress responses
