# Diagnostic e-module for Symmetry and Gauss's Law with about 650 tagged True/False questions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 3 studies (2 design, 1 associational), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A diagnostic e-module that divides Symmetry and Gauss's Law into concept categories and tests each with randomly drawn True/False questions. The article states "In our diagnostic module there are about 650 questions with at least 50 questions in each concept category", so repeated attempts present fresh question sets and prevent memorization. Questions are presented one at a time with immediate feedback, and a student must answer all seven drawn questions in a category correctly to be diagnosed as understanding it.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each question must be answerable using only the concepts of its one category and tagged to a unique concept category.
- A large question bank per category (at least fifty) so retakes present fresh sets.
#### Constraints
- The module does not directly offer any instruction; it diagnoses deficiencies and directs remediation.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in an Electricity and Magnetism course

### Learning Goals
- conceptual understanding of Symmetry and Gauss's Law
