# Dynamic model of educational effectiveness: eight teacher factors measured on five dimensions, grouped into five developmental stages

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The dynamic model of educational effectiveness defines quality of teaching through eight teacher factors — "orientation, structuring, questioning, teaching-modelling, applications, management of time, teacher role in making the classroom a learning environment, and classroom assessment" — each measured on five dimensions (frequency, focus, stage, quality, differentiation). A longitudinal study grouped the resulting 42 teaching skills into five developmental stages ordered from basic direct-teaching routines to differentiation and the new teaching approach. This article uses those stage groupings as the content and sequencing basis of the DIA professional development programme.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must first be classified into a developmental stage via observation and Rasch analysis before stage-matched training can be delivered
#### Constraints
- The stage grouping was established for achievement in different subjects and for both cognitive and affective outcomes in prior EER studies cited by the article
