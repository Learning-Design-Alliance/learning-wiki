# Generalisation Assessment Game (GAG)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
GAG is a critical-thinking card game for English Language classes. Groups of at least four students receive four cards inscribed ALWAYS TRUE, SOMETIMES TRUE, NEVER TRUE and RARELY TRUE; a volunteer draws statements about ethnic and religious groups from a box, groups raise a card, and must "support their answers with reasons or examples", losing points to groups that debunk their assertions and gaining two points for satisfactory justification. Students then modify over-generalised statements (e.g. "Christians make good parents" became "Some Christians make good parents") and generate their own statements about Nigerian ethnic groups.

### Learning Goals

- querying ethnic and religious generalisations and prejudices
