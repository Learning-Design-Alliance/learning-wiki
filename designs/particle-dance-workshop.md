# Particle Dance workshop: particle physics introduced through dance in the dance studio

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A two-part workshop bringing particle physics into the dance studio for young children. In the first part students learn about fundamental particles and the Higgs boson and connect particles to dance through short choreographic movements; in the second part they learn about interactions and, in teams, produce a choreography of an interaction. The article states the core idea is to "bring particle physics in the dance studio", encouraging informal learning and interdisciplinary connections.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A dance studio, a dancer, a musician, and a physicist facilitating the session
- A final artistic creation: at the end of the workshop the students have a complete artistic creation they could perform and discuss
#### Constraints
- Pilot implementation focused on girls in Key Stage 3, specifically Year 8 (12-13 years old), in the British education system

### Target Learners
- Year 8 students (12-13 years old), Key Stage 3, British education system

### Learning Goals
- Understanding that matter is made of fundamental particles and how they interact
- Building self-confidence in relation to science and research
- Generating lasting interest in science as a creative subject
