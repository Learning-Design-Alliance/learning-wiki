# Two-round simulated pediatric CPA assessment protocol with blinded video evaluation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study's assessment element is a two-round simulation protocol: round 1 comprised three consecutive scenarios (a 5-minute baseline scenario, a 45-minute RCDP or PSD training session, and a 5-minute post-test), and round 2 after a 5-6-week washout was a single retention scenario. Scenarios 1, 3, and 4 were video-recorded and analyzed by four evaluators "blinded to the randomization arm, as well as to the scenario's number and timing", using a pre-established checklist, with compression metrics extracted from the SimPad SkillReporter.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Blinded evaluators and video recording of test scenarios
- Standardized pre-briefing establishing a psychologically safe environment and confidentiality contract
#### Constraints
- Sessions were run in two settings (simulation center and in situ emergency room), which the authors note may have impacted performance

### Target Learners
- first- and second-year pediatric residents

### Learning Goals
- measuring acquisition and retention of pediatric CPR performance
