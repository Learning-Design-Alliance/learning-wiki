# VSP closed-loop competency-development model built on three core mechanisms

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article presents VSP teaching as a mechanism account in which "the VSP system reconstructs the clinical competency development path through 3 core mechanisms": a full lifecycle disease spectrum database, a human-computer interactive consultation environment requiring learners to independently complete history collection, examination selection, and treatment planning, and intelligent evaluation algorithms providing millisecond-level feedback. The authors describe "simulation practice, instant feedback, correction, and improvement" as a closed loop that breaks through the time, space, and ethical limits of traditional bedside teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A human-computer interactive consultation environment in which learners independently complete the entire process of medical history collection, physical examination operations, auxiliary examination selection, and treatment plan formulation
- Intelligent evaluation algorithms providing feedback at operational nodes
#### Constraints
- The authors state that evidence on VSPs' feasibility and effectiveness remains limited
- The trial found no significant between-group difference in differential-diagnosis improvement

### Target Learners
- medical students in clinical internal medicine practice training

### Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning and clinical thinking ability
- independent diagnostic and treatment decision-making
