# Scribbler 3 robot real-time kinematic graphing activity

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A real-time graphing laboratory activity in which students receive a verbal motion description, sketch the corresponding position-time graph, draw it in the s2mmsKinematicGUI software, and send it to a small Scribbler 3 robot that "will move according to the graph you draw". The design goal is instantaneous feedback: students see whether the executed motion matched the graph they imagined. It was tested as a treatment for non-science majors in a two-hour lab session.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A programmable Scribbler 3 robot, the s2mmsKinematicGUI graphing software, and a computer-to-robot connection so a drawn graph can be executed as motion
#### Constraints
- In this study the activity occupied only half an hour and students drew two graphs, which the authors judged too little and too short to affect TUG-K scores

### Target Learners
- non-science majors in a linked lecture-lab conceptual physics course

### Learning Goals
- constructing and interpreting position-time graphs and matching graphs to imagined motion
