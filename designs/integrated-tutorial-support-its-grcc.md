# Integrated Tutorial Support (ITS) program at Grand Rapids Community College

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
ITS is a short-term developmental education intervention combining supplemental instruction with acceleration "in an accelerated format within the first month of class". Students placing in the lowest third in Beginning Algebra are identified by a first-day arithmetic survey, invited to mandatory twice-weekly tutoring in groups of up to three for the first four weeks, and must pass a Final Quiz at 80%. ALEKS-generated worksheets on whole numbers, fractions, decimals, and signed numbers guide instruction, and tutors tailor instruction to individual needs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Attendance is mandatory and students complete worksheets and the Final Quiz within 3 to 4 weeks of the course beginning
- Passing the Final Quiz at 80% is required, with retakes allowed after review
- Select experienced tutors and a single coordinating instructor deliver the support
#### Constraints
- The program has been conducted only in sections of Beginning Algebra taught by a single instructor since Fall 2014
- Logistics required relocating tutoring services from near the classroom, with effects of this change under investigation

### Target Learners
- Community college students placing in the lowest third in Beginning Algebra on the ACCUPLACER exam

### Learning Goals
- Acquisition of prerequisite arithmetic skills (long division, fractions, signed numbers, order of operations)
- Successful completion of Beginning Algebra with a C- or better
