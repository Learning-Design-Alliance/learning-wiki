# Non-linear, case-study-driven course structure with two weekly sessions pairing framework introductions and case analysis

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course runs as weekly pairs of two-hour sessions whose "Class content consists of (1) an introduction of key concepts from each framework and (2) an analysis and discussion of a case study." Early weeks build sociopolitical histories of science and engineering; middle weeks center one critical field per week; final weeks are driven by students' learning desires and chosen case studies, with the course frequently returning to foundational frameworks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Case studies offer dedicated class time for current or historical events involving science and/or technology and can be adapted with each course iteration to keep material topical.
#### Constraints
- The template was created to fit UCLA's quarter system with two classes per week and remains situated within a North American-centric academic calendar.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and graduate STEM students

### Learning Goals
- applying critical theoretical frameworks to real-world technoscientific cases
