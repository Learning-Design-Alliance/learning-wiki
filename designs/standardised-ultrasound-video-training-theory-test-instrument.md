# Standardised ultrasound video training and 54-question online theory test instrument

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study's training and assessment materials: a standardised video training session covering gallbladder, liver, and pancreas normal findings and pathologies, in which "The video training for both groups was identical in duration, spoken text, and displayed captions, differing only in the format of the sonographic findings." The accompanying digital theory test comprised 54 multiple-choice questions (16 gallbladder, 20 liver, 18 pancreas) with four answer options each and a 30-second per-question limit. Materials were developed by an interdisciplinary expert team and pretested by five DEGUM Level II/III experts for content validity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content validity was established via proof-of-concept pretesting by five ultrasound experts certified as DEGUM Level II or III
#### Constraints
- Sonographic findings were sourced and curated from an online teaching platform; training was delivered in plenary format with projector and audio system

### Target Learners
- participants in certified basic ultrasound courses (students, residents, and physicians)

### Learning Goals
- theoretical ultrasound competencies in abdominal sonography covering normal findings and pathologies of the gallbladder, liver, and pancreas
