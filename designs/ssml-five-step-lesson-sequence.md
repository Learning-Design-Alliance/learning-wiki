# Soft skill-based metacognitive learning follows a five-step lesson sequence

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's SSML approach is enacted as a lesson pattern in which "students follow a leraning by five steps are: early discussion; independence; group discussion; group presentation; and reflection and conclusion." Each step pairs metacognitive activity (planning, monitoring, evaluating thinking in problem solving) with the empowerment of named soft-skill values such as confidence, honesty, independence, and cooperation. The pattern was implemented in experimental classes in the quasi-experimental study.
