# RoSTraC: a three-step, proficiency-based surgical residency training curriculum progressing from simulation to laboratory to operating room

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
RoSTraC is a structured curriculum for surgical residents combining "a three-step training curriculum including multimodal learning contents": theory with VR simulation (step 1, about 3 months), laboratory training on training boxes and synthetic biological organ models (step 2, about 3–6 months), and supervised on-patient operating-room practice (step 3, about 6 months). Exercises carry expert-defined benchmark times, and trainees repeat exercises until objectives are met. The pattern operationalizes progression from simple bimanual tasks to complete robotic procedures with graded complexity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Successful completion of VR training is required before step 2; taking over console steps in the operating room should only occur after passing step 2
#### Constraints
- Validated only on DaVinci Xi and Si systems; transferability to other robotic systems "remains unclear and needs to be investigated"

### Target Learners
- surgical residents in visceral and general surgery

### Learning Goals
- robot-assisted surgery basic and complex procedural skills
- safe integration into a robotic surgical team
