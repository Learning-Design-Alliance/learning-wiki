# Semiotic approach to preschool foreign-language vocabulary instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study grounds both instruction types in semiotics, the study of signs and sign processes called semiosis, applied to foreign language learning as educational semiotics. In early language learning, pictures are central because "a picture represents a wide variety of meaning by the use of signs". In the experiment, semiosis was provided by the researcher's verbal and visual cues in the TVI group and by the computer itself in the CAVI group, with both groups studying the same 20 target words over four weeks.
