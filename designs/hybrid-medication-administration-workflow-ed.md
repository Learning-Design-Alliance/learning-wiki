# Hybrid paper-electronic medication administration workflow with two distinct pathways

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a hybrid medication management workflow integrating paper-based and electronic processes, comprising two distinct pathways. In the emergency pathway, verbal orders and emergency carts enable rapid administration with retrospective documentation and inconsistent CDSS use. In the non-emergency pathway, handwritten orders are transcribed into the HIMS with activated CDSS checks for "dose limits, drug–drug interactions, and patient-specific allergy alerts." Each stage represents a potential point of risk.
