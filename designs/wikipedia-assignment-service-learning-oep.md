# Wikipedia writing assignments as a service-learning form of Open Educational Practice (Renewable Assignment)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article frames the Wikipedia writing assignment—students research a topic and expand or create an article—as an Open Educational Practice that contributes to the public domain, called a Renewable Assignment or Non-Disposable Assessment (Wiley & Hilton, 2018). It aligns with service-learning, and the article lists six key elements of service-learning found within Wikipedia assignments, from "contributing to society" to contributions that "can be easily assessed by the community". This framing underpins educators' motivations, particularly the service-learning aspect that motivated both students and instructors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assignments must be connected to the course's objectives and allow for self-reflection, per the article's six service-learning elements
#### Constraints
- The article notes absence of adequate university support and recognition may limit skill development, yielding outcomes comparable to traditional assignments

### Target Learners
- higher education students
- university and college instructors

### Learning Goals
- research, critical thinking, writing, and source citation skills
- digital, information, and academic literacies
