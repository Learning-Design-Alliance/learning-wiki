# Active Learning–GenAI Synergy Framework

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's theoretical contribution positions generative AI tools within Kolb's experiential learning cycle and active learning principles, so that AI functions "as cognitive partners, metacognitive mirrors, and equity tools" rather than as a standalone intervention. The framework structures AI use around weekly experiential cycles (drafting with AI, ethical reflection, peer discussion, revision) and an Ethical Integration Protocol. The authors conclude "the pedagogical framework mediating AI interaction—rather than the technology itself—determines educational outcomes".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sustained engagement over repeated experiential learning cycles; the article reports cognitive partnerships required approximately 6-8 weeks to develop effectively
#### Constraints
- The cultural and institutional specificity of Pakistani higher education may limit generalizability, requiring cross-cultural validation

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate EFL/ESL writing students in resource-constrained higher education

### Learning Goals
- EFL/ESL writing proficiency
- metacognitive awareness and ethical AI use
- digital literacy
