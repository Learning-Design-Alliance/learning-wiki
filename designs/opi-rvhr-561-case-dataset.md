# Eight-year single-surgeon dataset of 561 consecutive robotic ventral hernia repairs (468 analyzed) with OPI and clinical variables

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A retrospective review of prospectively obtained data from 561 consecutive robotic-assisted ventral hernia repairs performed between February 2013 and November 2022 at a high-volume academic medical center; exclusion of concurrent procedures left 468 cases. The cohort comprises 38.7% minor, 45.5% moderate, and 15.8% major complexity hernias under expert consensus criteria, with OPIs represented as "total event counts, durations, rates, and normalized durations" from robotic data streams.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Robotic data streams with surgeon-to-console mapping to attribute OPIs to operators
#### Constraints
- Single surgeon with trainees at one academic center; cases with concurrent procedures outside ventral hernia repair excluded

### Target Learners
- attending surgeons and clinical fellows performing robotic ventral hernia repair

### Learning Goals
- modeling operative performance and case complexity from intraoperative metrics
