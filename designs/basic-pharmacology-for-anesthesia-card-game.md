# Basic Pharmacology for Anesthesia card game

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A tabletop card game developed as a supplementary learning tool for teaching anesthetic drug pharmacology to anesthesiology residents. The deck comprised 55 cards representing 11 anesthetic drugs, with each drug detailed across five cards covering name, dosage, onset and duration, contraindications, side effects, and metabolism or pharmacology. The game objective was for participants to collect a complete set of five cards for a specific anesthetic drug. Content was reviewed and approved by board-certified anesthesiologists.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a deck of 55 cards representing 11 anesthetic drugs
- Designed for 3-8 participants per session
#### Constraints
- Opioids and local anesthetics were excluded from this version
- Drug selection was tailored to commonly available anesthetic drugs used in Thailand and at the study institution

### Target Learners
- First- to third-year anesthesiology residents

### Learning Goals
- Anesthetic drug pharmacology knowledge
