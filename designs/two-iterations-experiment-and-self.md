# Two complementary types of iterative improvement: of experiments and of self

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The course framework centers on two complementary iteration cycles. The first is "iterative improvement of experiments through cycles of modeling systems, designing experiments, analyzing data, and reﬁning models and designs"; the second is "iterative improvement of self through cycles of reﬂecting on progress, soliciting feedba ck, and implementing changes to study habits and habits of mind." The article organizes its curriculum description around these two goals, showing how course activities instantiate each and, in one student case, how the two intertwine.
