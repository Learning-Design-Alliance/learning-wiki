# AIXTRA digital simulated-person training concept via video conferencing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A concept developed by the AIXTRA Competence Center for Training and Patient Safety at RWTH Aachen to enable learning of communication skills with simulated persons digitally when classroom teaching was cancelled in the SARS-CoV-2 pandemic. Digital seminars with the methodology of simulated conversations with SP were conducted via video conferencing software for planned 690 students in the anamnesis course (12 seminars), the psychiatry communication course (20 seminars) and the clinical competence course (14 seminars). The study protocol was approved by the Ethics Committee with EC number 185/20.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Appropriate preparation of cases, SPs and technology; a privacy policy for Zoom use provided to students in advance
#### Constraints
- Participation was voluntary in the summer-semester 2020; recordings were not allowed

### Target Learners
- medical students in semesters 6, 8 and 10 at RWTH Aachen

### Learning Goals
- communication skills, history taking, psychiatric conversations, clinical competence
