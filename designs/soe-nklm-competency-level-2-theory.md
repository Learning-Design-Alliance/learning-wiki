# SOEs as competency-level-2 assessment bridging factual knowledge and performance skills in the NKLM competency model

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article situates the structured oral examination within the NKLM model of four ascending competency depths. It argues that written exams capture level 1 factual knowledge and OSCEs capture level 3 performance skills, while the SOE targets "Handlungs- und Begründungswissen" (applied knowledge and reasoning, level 2), letting students explain facts, place them in clinical-scientific context, and evaluate findings on a data basis. It thereby closes the curricular gap between knowledge reproduction and practical action competence. The assessment theory of constructive alignment is invoked: exams must be interlocked with teaching goals and methods because they steer learning behavior.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Standardized case vignettes, checklists, and defined scoring criteria, plus alignment of the exam with NKLM competency level 2 learning objectives
#### Constraints
- Applies to competency level 2 objectives; unstructured colloquia with mainly factual questions show low reliability and limited content validity per the article

### Target Learners
- medical students (10th semester, ENT block practicum)

### Learning Goals
- applied knowledge and reasoning in ENT diagnostics (audiometry, rhinomanometry)
