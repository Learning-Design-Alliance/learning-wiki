# Four-step continuous assessment cycle: collect, characterize, analyze and plan, implement

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's assessment methodology proceeds in four main steps repeated each term across a four-term assessment cycle: data is collected through direct and indirect means, characterized into quantitative and qualitative types, analyzed to identify correlations and trends with a corrective action plan devised, and implemented. "These steps are repeated in the next iteration", with a complete cycle comprising four terms or two academic years.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assessment data from faculty, enrolled and graduating students, and alumni; a departmental ABET committee to analyze results and devise action plans
#### Constraints
- Activities are carried out four times per assessment cycle, with two terms per academic year excluding the optional summer term

### Target Learners
- undergraduate engineering students in an accredited program

### Learning Goals
- attainment of ABET student outcomes and continuous program improvement
