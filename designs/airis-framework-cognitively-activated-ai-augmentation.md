# AIRIS: a three-phase framework for cognitively activated AI augmentation in physics instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
AIRIS (Activate – Inquire – Reflect with Intelligent Support) is a three-phase instructional framework that structures student activities before, during, and after AI use in science education. It is not tied to a specific tool and focuses on when and how AI enters the learning process. The authors state: "We propose a simple but powerful three- phase instructional framework for AI -augmented science education that structures student activities before, during, and after AI use". It draws on self-regulated learning, cognitive load, multiple representations, and hybrid intelligence research.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires prior direct instruction establishing the conceptual knowledge students need for meaningful Phase 1 predictions
- Requires teachers able to design tasks that resist AI completion and evaluate AI outputs in their domain
#### Constraints
- Presented as a design proposal grounded in established learning science principles, not as an empirically validated intervention
- Designed for inquiry-based instruction and problem-solving settings; does not replace direct instruction

### Target Learners
- introductory physics students

### Learning Goals
- epistemic practices of physics: predicting, evaluating evidence, coordinating representations
