# Nuclear medicine therapy escape room: a team-based, case-puzzle capstone applying clinical reasoning, dose calculation, and safety decision-making

## Description
The nuclear medicine therapy escape room is the program's capstone event, described by the authors as "a first-of-its-kind capstone event in the field of nuclear medicine therapy". Teams solve case-based puzzles requiring "clinical reasoning, dose calculation, radiopharmaceutical selection, and workflow problem-solving" under time pressure in a team-based format. The authors cite reviews and meta-analyses finding educational escape rooms improve cognitive, psychomotor, affective, and nontechnical skills, and report it received the program's strongest satisfaction ratings.
