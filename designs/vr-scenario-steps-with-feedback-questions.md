# VR simulation scenario sequence with guided competency steps and feedback questions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The training follows an enumerated competency sequence: greet the older adult and explain the care, inspect the virtual environment for safety, choose and use proper denture cleaning products, and assist cleaning of natural teeth including moisturizing oral rinses. After the simulation, learners must "correctly answer four questions" covering denture storage, interdental cleaning aids, fluoride toothpaste selection, and dental check-up frequency, and "the learning system would generate feedback to guide the participant to the correct answer."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A VR system able to present a virtual patient scene, accept remote-control step selections, and deliver corrective feedback on incorrect answers
#### Constraints
- Content was based on prior training of dental health students and targeted geriatric oral health care competencies

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students

### Learning Goals
- operational oral health care skills
- geriatric oral health knowledge
