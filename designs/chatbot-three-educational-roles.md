# Three educational roles of chatbots: teaching assistant, personal tutor, and learning partner

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article structures its program around a meta-analytic account that "chatbot programs can serve three roles in education—teaching assistant, personal tutor, and learning partner." As a teaching assistant the chatbot supplies specialized knowledge, visuals, and feedback; as a learning partner it converses like a friend and encourages learners to sustain progress; as a personal tutor it answers questions, guides learning, and offers quizzes, enabling SDL without instructor assistance.
