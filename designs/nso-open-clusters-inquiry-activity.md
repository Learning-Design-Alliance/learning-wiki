# Inquiry-based teacher-free open clusters activity guiding students from photometry to a colour-magnitude diagram and peer discussion

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The NSO hosts an inquiry-based activity, designed by the author, in which students learn about open clusters and HR diagrams and the technique of photometry without a teacher leading each step. The article describes it as "an inquiry-based 'teacher-free' activity for students to learn about open clusters and HR diagrams as well as the technique of photometry". Including screenshots and screencasts, it shows students how to analyse their data with a spreadsheet package such as Excel and upload their results as a CMD, with the aim of encouraging them to discuss their findings with other students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- a choice of datasets (currently 28 B and V image datasets from the FTP) or teacher-arranged observations of a cluster of the student's or teacher's choice
- a spreadsheet package such as Excel for data analysis
- screenshots and screencasts to guide students through analysis and upload
#### Constraints
- the article notes this activity is the first of several projects to be created, with topics such as exoplanets and variable stars still to come

### Target Learners
- school students using the NSO portal

### Learning Goals
- understanding open clusters and HR diagrams
- the technique of photometry
- data analysis and discussion of findings with other students
