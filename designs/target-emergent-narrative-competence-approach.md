# Emergent storytelling with competence-linked narrative cases for complex competence development

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The TARGET project's approach combines emergent narrative with learning-goal guidance: a narrative engine that "supports emergent storytelling to support the development of complex competences" in project management and innovation. An adapted Fabula Model formalises the emerging events, and cases representing situated contexts associated with particular competences are triggered so a learner encounters dramatic points where the associated competences need to be used.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each learning objective must explicitly have one or more associated cases used to inspire story direction
- A formal representation of the emerging events is needed so the engine can analyse story-level goals
#### Constraints
- Applied in the learning domains of project management and innovation within the TARGET project
- Controlling autonomous believable characters is unreliable, since agents may or may not follow suggestions

### Target Learners
- corporate employees developing soft skills through serious games

### Learning Goals
- complex competences such as conflict management, sense-making and time management
