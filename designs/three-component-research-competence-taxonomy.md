# Three-component diagnostic taxonomy of engineering students' research competence

## Description
The article defines research competence as a training outcome and indicator of a student's readiness to conduct research, assessed through three components: motivational (studied by questioning), cognitive (assessed via end-of-course paper test or oral questioning), and operational (assessed by comparative analysis of grades and observation results). Each component is graded high, average, or low with printed level characteristics (Tables 1-3); for example, a high operational-level student can "state the research purpose fully and clearly" and independently develop a testing technique. The taxonomy grounds the experiment's pre/post diagnostics.
