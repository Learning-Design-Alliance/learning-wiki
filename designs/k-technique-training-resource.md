# The "K" technique as a training resource for four-arm robotic platforms

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The "K" technique is a reproducible training methodology for the four-arm robotic platform, developed from the training of more than 400 surgeons. It offers "a simple and reproducible methodology for developing important skills to reduce the learning curve for new robotic surgeries", and serves as a practical tool for dry-lab and virtual reality training environments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Practical training is essential; some skills can be trained in VR but dry-lab practice with a qualified instructor is needed
#### Constraints
- Dry-lab training is limited by availability of dedicated robotic platforms, cost of training arms, and instructor availability

### Target Learners
- robotic surgeons
- surgery trainees

### Learning Goals
- reducing the learning curve
- instrument control
- depth perception

### Claims

- [The K technique exercise stimulates five of six GEARS skill domains](../claims/k-technique-stimulates-five-gears-domains.md) [+W]
