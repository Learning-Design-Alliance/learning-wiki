# Reflective Deliberate Practice (RDP): a combined debriefing framework pairing deliberate practice with reflective debriefing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Reflective Deliberate Practice (RDP) is a proposed synergistic debriefing framework that combines RCDP's focus on the 'what' with TRD's focus on the 'why'. The article proposes it after observing that learners in each arm wanted the alternate approach: TRD learners wanted practice opportunities, while RCDP learners wanted to reflect and challenge assumptions. RDP would let learners "practice in action and reflect; a focus on the 'what' (RCDP) and 'why' (TRD)", with RCDP conducted alongside interval or final cumulative reflective debriefing. It is proposed for future study, not tested in this trial.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires combining RCDP's repetitive practice with immediate feedback and TRD's reflective discussion so learners can both practice in action and reflect
#### Constraints
- Not yet empirically tested; the article states additional research comparing single-methodology debriefing versus RDP is necessary, including effects on retention and clinical application

### Target Learners
- Interprofessional emergency department teams including physicians, fellows, nurses, medical technicians, paramedics, and respiratory therapists

### Learning Goals
- Teamwork skills such as role clarity, closed loop communication, and shared mental model in resuscitation care
