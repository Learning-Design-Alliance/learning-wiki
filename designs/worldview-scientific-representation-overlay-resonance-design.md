# Overlaying the physical world view with the scientific resonance graph across damping runs

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A simulation design merging the world view of many spring-mass systems excited by different driving frequencies with the scientific amplitude-versus-frequency representation, showing different damping runs together "for ease of visual comparison". It also displays the transient instantaneous oscillation of each mass alongside the maximum and steady-state amplitudes, so students can associate the physical oscillations with the resulting resonance graph.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The model must display "the transient instantaneous positions of each mass" together with the maximum red amplitudes, and the theoretical pink amplitudes serve as hints for how long each damping level should run.
#### Constraints
- The design replicates what the authors call "a difficult to setup world view" of many spring-mass systems being excited by different driving frequencies.

### Target Learners
- Singapore-Cambridge GCE Advanced Level physics students
- physics educators

### Learning Goals
- associating physical oscillations with the resonance graph
- comparing steady-state amplitudes across damping levels
