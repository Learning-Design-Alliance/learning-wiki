# Constructivist learning environment for argument structure training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A computerised training intervention conveying conceptual and procedural knowledge about the Toulmin model within a constructivist learning environment using Jonassen's (1999) cognitive modelling approach. It comprised a theoretical introduction with examples and learning goals, explanation prompts, two video tutorials modelling typical and atypical arguments with an expert model, twelve practice texts of varied argument types, and immediate feedback with a progress table. Participants could access theoretical information, tutorials, and notes at all stages.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Immediate feedback on each task
- clear learning goals matching the task
- varied argument types including atypical structures
- accessible supporting information throughout
#### Constraints
- The intervention was tested with psychology students using German texts summarising empirical psychology articles

### Target Learners
- university psychology students

### Learning Goals
- identifying structural components of informal arguments and their relations
