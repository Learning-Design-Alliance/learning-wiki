# Phy Ex course pattern: semester-long open experimental research projects in pairs with theory phase, disputatio and reports

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Phy Ex course, created by Yves Couder at Université Paris VII, has students work in pairs over a 12-week semester on open scientific problems. "Experimental projects in physics at Paris 7 university take 8 hours a week (2 times 4 hours) over the course of a semester (12 weeks). Each group is composed of 30 to 36 students, who work in pairs on 15 to 18 projects." The first 20 hours go to theoretical analysis and reading primary sources, followed by about 18 four-hour experimental periods, a written report, and an oral presentation with practice peer review (disputatio).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Projects must have at least one quantity to measure and one control parameter so an experimental curve can be drawn
- A well-equipped machine shop with a permanent lathe-mill operator and a second technician for materials and computers
- Three or four experienced academics to supervise the projects
- Collective check of prior feasibility of proposed subjects
#### Constraints
- Subjects that are too qualitative or involve fields rather than scalar quantities are rejected

### Target Learners
- Third-year bachelor's physics students

### Learning Goals
- Learning the scientific method through guided research practice
- Designing and building experiments to answer open questions
- Reading scientific papers and practising peer review
