# Quantum engineering bachelor's degree program born out of an engineering school

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An undergraduate quantum engineering degree program that one interviewed educator (Professor Maverick) helped spearhead, created from within an engineering school to prepare more non-physicists for the quantum workforce. It grew from a long-running course that was "basically a course of quantum mechanics for engineers using the modern engineered quantum devices as the model system to teach quantum physics", teaching quantum dots, Cooper pair boxes and SQUIDs instead of harmonic oscillators and the hydrogen atom, later expanded into quantum-specific courses.
