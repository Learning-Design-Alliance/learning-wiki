# Station-rotation format with small-group equipment-based activities

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The SBSM curriculum organizes each of thirty lessons into Warm-up, Workout, and Cool Down sessions, with the Workout at 4-6 stations where student groups rotate through equipment-based physical tasks. Activities are "designed for small spaces" and do not require gyms, fitting inner-city school constraints. Teachers reported the rotating-station format engaged all students simultaneously.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- P.E. equipment including balls, cones, free-weights, jumping ropes, exercise mats, agility ladders, and marking tape
#### Constraints
- Class duration limits rotations to 3-6 per session depending on P.E. class length

### Target Learners
- Adolescent students in grades 7-10 in inner-city schools

### Learning Goals
- Physical fitness
- Confidence in physical activity
