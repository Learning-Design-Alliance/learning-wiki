# DynEd and Imagine Learning computer-assisted English learning software packages

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Two multimedia English-as-a-foreign-language software packages evaluated in Costa Rican primary schools. DynEd uses a "non-linear," blended approach presenting all material only in English (immersion), with worksheets, online teacher training, and placement and mastery tests; Imagine Learning uses a "linear" teaching approach and a "fade" approach explaining early vocabulary and concepts in Spanish, with no supplementary materials or teacher training. Project documentation reports average time spent per week of 67 minutes for DynEd and 127 minutes for Imagine Learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schools in the treatment groups received a laptop and headset for every third grade student alongside the software
#### Constraints
- DynEd presents all its material only in English, while Imagine Learning introduces early concepts in the student’s native language

### Target Learners
- Third-grade primary school students learning English as a foreign language in Costa Rica

### Learning Goals
- English language proficiency in reading, writing, listening and comprehension
