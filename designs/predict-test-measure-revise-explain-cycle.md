# Predict–test–measure–revise–explain learning cycle for simulation-based investigation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each guided investigation follows a recurring cycle in which students commit to a prediction before the simulation reveals the outcome, then test, measure, revise, and explain. "The prediction gives the result something to challenge; the measurement makes the conclusion auditable; and the final explanation requires the student to say why the result occurred." The design is intended to limit passive watching, and predictions are recorded but not graded for correctness so that a wrong prediction can become the most useful part of the activity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Simulation scenarios with prepared scenes, task-specific measurement instruments, and locally saved progress
#### Constraints
- Predictions are deliberately not graded for correctness; the cycle is aimed at introductory astronomy activities

### Target Learners
- introductory astronomy students

### Learning Goals
- forming and testing predictions
- connecting measurements to physical ideas
- controlled experimentation
