# Set of six free online games and simulations mapped to personal finance topics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The experimental group completed a curated set of free online games and one market simulation, selected for "relevancy, level of rigor, time to complete, and ease of use". Role-playing games from the Doorways to Dreams Fund (Farm Blitz, Refund Rush, Celebrity Calamity) reward desired financial behaviors such as confining spending to budgeted limits and saving regularly; Financial Football, Gen I Revolution, and the Wall Street Survivor trading simulation cover money management, consumer credit, financial services, and investing. Each role-playing game took around 30 minutes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Games must cover a wide range of personal finance concepts without difficult finance-course calculations
- Assignments submitted by finishing games online or handing in written learning reports
#### Constraints
- Games were not customized to the course, so not all scenarios could be referenced back to the course textbook
- Only selective portions of Gen I Revolution's 15 missions were assigned due to semester time constraints

### Target Learners
- undergraduate non-business majors in a personal finance course

### Learning Goals
- budgeting and saving
- risk and return
- consumer credit
- financial services
- investments
