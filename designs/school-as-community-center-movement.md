# The school as community center: extending education beyond school age to the entire community

## Description
The article characterizes the community-center school as a sociological movement in which the school plant serves the whole community that supports it. It reads the movement as "extending education far beyond the limits of school age" and as "sending the entire community to school," a vista of possibilities the reporter finds fascinating yet potentially distracting from routine classroom work.
