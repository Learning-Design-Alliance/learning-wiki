# Unscaffolded GenAI use as self-initiated engagement without GenAI-specific guidance, framed by cognitive load theory and the zone of proximal development

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article defines unscaffolded GenAI use as "learners’ self-initiated and self-regulated engagement with GenAI in the absence of GenAI-specific instructional guidance or pre-designed prompting frameworks", drawing on self-regulated learning perspectives. It frames the problem with scaffolding theory and the zone of proximal development, which emphasize structured guidance for novices, and with cognitive load theory, which holds that effective support should reduce extraneous load. The framework motivates examining outcomes when GenAI is permitted but not scaffolded in authentic course contexts.
