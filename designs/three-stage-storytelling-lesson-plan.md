# Three-stage storytelling lesson plan: preteaching, while-teaching, and postteaching stages

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's storytelling treatment was organized as a lesson plan "had three sections: preteaching, while-teaching, and postteaching stages". In the pre-storytelling stage learners received comprehensible input through talk, games, reading and listening and learned key vocabulary via pictures and gestures. In the while-storytelling stage the teacher told the story with PowerPoint visual support and interaction, and in the post-storytelling stage learners played vocabulary games and role-played the story by memorizing dialogues.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher-prepared stories based on the course's units, a PowerPoint visual presentation, and data-show hardware in the classroom
#### Constraints
- Developed and evaluated with young upper-beginner EFL learners in a private language institute in Isfahan, Iran

### Target Learners
- Young EFL learners (upper-beginner, ages 8-14)

### Learning Goals
- English vocabulary knowledge
- listening comprehension and engagement with stories
