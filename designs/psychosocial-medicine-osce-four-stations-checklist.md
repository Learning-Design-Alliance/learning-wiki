# Four-station psychosocial medicine OSCE with global checklists across four professional domains

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A mandatory eighth/ninth-semester OSCE at Heidelberg's Centre for Psychosocial Medicine with "four different stations" covering post-traumatic stress disorder, schizophrenia, borderline personality disorder, and suicidal tendency or dementia. At each station students establish contact and take the history of one standardized patient for nine minutes, assessed by one examiner using tablet-based global checklists scoring relationship with the patient, conversational skills, anamnesis, and psychopathological findings and decision-making, for 25 points per station and 100 total, with 60 needed to pass.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained examiners with tablet-based global checklists at each station
- Standardized patients trained for each disorder scenario
- Part of a four-week mandatory psychosocial module with lectures, seminars, clinical placements and communication training
#### Constraints
- Only four stations could be provided, yielding low internal consistency (Cronbach's alpha = .58), and task specificity may limit generalisability across conditions

### Target Learners
- Clinical-phase medical students in their eighth or ninth semester

### Learning Goals
- Building a relationship with the patient
- Conversational skills
- Structured anamnesis
- Psychopathological findings and decision-making
