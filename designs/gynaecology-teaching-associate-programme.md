# Gynaecology Teaching Associate (GTA) programme: trained expert patients who instruct medical students in pelvic examination

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
GTAs are "expert patients known as Gynaecology Teaching Associates (GTAs), who are recruited and trained to instruct medical students in achieving practical experience" of female pelvic examination. In the trial, GTA teaching was a 2-hour session early in a 5-week O&G placement, with students then examined on GTAs by a blinded assessor. GTAs were paid £30 per hour including travel, and a faculty of eight GTAs was recruited by advertisement and formally interviewed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- GTAs must be recruited (in the trial by poster advertisement and formal interviews) and trained in knowledge, skills and communication by clinical lecturers over multiple supervised sessions
#### Constraints
- GTA teaching is an expensive human resource; costs depend on local pay rates and facilities, and GTA availability is largely after clinic hours because GTAs have their own work commitments

### Target Learners
- final-year undergraduate medical students

### Learning Goals
- competence in gynaecological pelvic examination (speculum and bimanual examination)
