# Guided group reflection using the Mamede and Schmidt deliberate reflection framework

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
GGR is a six-step structured group reflection intervention delivered in 60-minute case-based sessions, following "the deliberate reflection framework described by Mamede and Schmidt". Steps move from initial hypothesis generation through identifying supportive, contradictory and missing findings, generating alternative hypotheses, to prioritising differentials with facilitator feedback. The article uses it as the collaborative comparator to SNAPPS in undergraduate clinical teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained facilitators; each 60-min session facilitated by trained instructors with a 60-min orientation
#### Constraints
- Sessions were conducted in separate medical units to minimise contamination

### Target Learners
- final-year MBBS undergraduate medical students

### Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning
- diagnostic hypothesis generation and revision
