# JBI Evidence Implementation Framework: seven-stage audit-and-feedback approach with GRiP barrier analysis

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The project was based on the JBI Evidence Implementation Framework, "which consists of seven stages, including first, identification of practice area for change; second, engaging change agents; third, assessment of context and readiness to change; fourth, review of practice against evidence-based audit criteria (baseline audit); fifth, implementation of changes to practice; sixth, reassessment of practice (follow-up audit); and seventh, consideration of the sustainability of practice changes." The GRiP matrix was used to identify barriers and match strategies, resources, and outcomes. The framework structured the three-phase project from January to June 2021.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A project team with defined roles and responsibilities, including organizational support from nursing leadership
- Audit criteria derived from evidence summaries and guidelines, measured via documentation review, interviews/surveys, and training lists
#### Constraints
- The article notes audit programs and implementation interventions may need regular updating to align with updated guidelines

### Target Learners
- Practicing nurses in hospital neurology wards

### Learning Goals
- Improve nurses' knowledge, skills, and compliance with evidence-based screening, prevention, and management of poststroke depression
