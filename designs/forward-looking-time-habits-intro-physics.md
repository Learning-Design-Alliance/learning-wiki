# Teach clock-specific time assertions and map-frame specification from the first course to minimize later unlearning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends beginning, in introductory mechanics, the habit of specifying the clock's frame of motion whenever time intervals are discussed, since introductory texts already require specifying a coordinate system for displacements. Such "forward-looking habits" minimize what physics-bound students must later unlearn, like clock-independent use of time, and open a deeper and simpler understanding of space-time even for students with only one physics course. The approach is described as consistent with Edwin Taylor's 1998 AAPT Oersted Medal talk proposal.
