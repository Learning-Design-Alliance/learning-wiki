# Generative AI-powered digital tutor with knowledge graph learning environment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The intervention artifact is a generative AI-powered digital tutor embedded in a knowledge graph–based learning environment for the course Human Anatomy and Histology & Embryology. Its functions were "limited to course-related learning support, including personalized preview tasks before class, real-time question answering during class, and adaptive feedback and customized review plans after class". The knowledge graph represents anatomical structures, histological features, embryological processes, and clinical concepts as nodes linked by hierarchical, structural, functional, or clinical relationships.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students need basic computer literacy: the ability to log into the online learning platform, access course materials, enter questions or responses, and complete online learning activities
#### Constraints
- The study did not systematically audit the factual accuracy of each AI-generated response
- The authors note excessive reliance on AI technologies may potentially reduce students' independent problem-solving abilities

### Target Learners
- First-year undergraduate nursing students

### Learning Goals
- Understanding anatomical structures and their clinical applications
- Knowledge integration and retention in foundational medical courses
