# Student-built WIKI as a legacy environment for reflection, ongoing contact and international scientist links

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Follow-up work for more than a year included a web-based environment (WIKI) developed by the students, which "allowed them to reflect upon their work and use both literacy and IT skills to communicate their findings". The WIKI remained a vital tool for keeping in contact with students and monitoring progress, since the specialist scientists were not based at the school, and enabled international links through which students discussed questions with foreign scientists and became more confident.
