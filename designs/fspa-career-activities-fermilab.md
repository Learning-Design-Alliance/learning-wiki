# Fermilab Student and Postdoc Association career activities as an existing US networking program

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Fermilab Student and Postdoc Association (FSPA) represents the community of early career researchers at Fermilab, "including all students, postdocs, summer students, and interns." Among other activities it organises Career Panel discussions and talks regularly and maintains a linkedin group for networking with alumni, where members can exchange job information, share ideas, and build personal networks. The authors cite it as one of the few existing US efforts, alongside CERN programs, that points toward the need for more organised career networking.
