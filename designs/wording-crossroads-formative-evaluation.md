# Two-phase formative evaluation combining wording and a crossword game (crossroads)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The TLS's formative evaluation consists of two phases. The 'wording' task is a final summary activity in which students choose words to keep track of the contents discussed, promoting interactions, creativity, and construction of conceptual maps. The 'crossroads' task has groups choose words, define them, and play a crossword guessing game, promoting cooperative learning and letting the instructor uncover erroneous conceptions. The authors report these tools "effectively track students' learning processes and can be easily applied in informal contexts (RQ2)".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students working in groups to generate words and definitions; a teacher to collect and present definitions through the crossword game
#### Constraints
- Erroneous conceptions surfaced in crossroads may be dictated by the extreme synthesis imposed by the game

### Target Learners
- upper secondary school students in non-formal physics activities

### Learning Goals
- monitoring students' learning processes
- linking physical knowledge and natural language
