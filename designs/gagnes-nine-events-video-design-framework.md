# Gagné's nine events of instruction as a design framework for interactive educational videos

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article designed its interactive educational video based on Gagné's nine events of instruction: gaining attention, describing the goal, stimulating prior knowledge, presenting material, providing guidance, eliciting performance, providing feedback, assessing performance, and enhancing retention and transfer. The authors state that 'When video materials match these steps, higher levels of achievement in student learning outcomes and learner engagement can be accomplished.' This framework structured the video's development by the course coordinator.

### Context
