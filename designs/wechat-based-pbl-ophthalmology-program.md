# WeChat-based PBL program for ophthalmology (5-week timeline with structured 2-hour facilitated sessions)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A WeChat-based PBL program in which 54 fifth-year medical students in three groups studied primary angle-closure glaucoma over a 5-week timeline of activities (guiding questions, exploring factors, applying concepts to scenarios, presenting solutions, evaluating solutions). "Each WeChat-based PBL session lasted 2 hours and was supported by the platform's features for sharing text, images, voice recordings, videos, and documents." Three clinical PACG vignettes from the hospital's glaucoma database were distributed 1 week before lectures, and instructors followed a structured facilitator's guide with 5 must-cover objectives mapped to the national syllabus.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors follow a structured facilitator's guide, identical across groups, distributed 1 week before the session
- Cases and guiding questions are distributed to students 1 week prior to the lecture
- Each group establishes its own discussion team on the WeChat platform
#### Constraints
- Implemented outside regular class schedules, primarily during evenings and weekends, increasing workload for students and instructors
- Evaluated on a single textbook chapter (primary angle-closure glaucoma) at a single institution

### Target Learners
- fifth-year undergraduate medical students in ophthalmology

### Learning Goals
- diagnosis and treatment of primary angle-closure glaucoma
- clinical reasoning and self-directed learning
- collaboration and communication skills
