# School training for delinquents organized around social values of each curriculum subject

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The outline's school plan rests on the general underlying principle of "emphasis of the social values in each subject of the curriculum," with specific purposes of creating good habits of thought and conduct, permanent wholesome interests, and knowledge for better social functioning. The central idea is social life and its manifold relations, centering about the home for girls and the industries for boys.
