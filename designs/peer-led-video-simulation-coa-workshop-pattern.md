# Peer-led bilingual workshop design combining real-world evaluation videos, simulation, and multidisciplinary case discussion

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A reusable training design in which experienced clinical evaluators (peer faculty drawn from clinical trial networks) co-develop and deliver bilingual workshops featuring "real-world evaluation videos, simulation-based learning, and multidisciplinary case discussions". Video recordings of real patient evaluations across the clinical spectrum let participants observe standardized assessments, analyze item-level performance, discuss scoring discrepancies, and reach facilitated consensus, mirroring clinical trial training practices. The article reports this format supported clarification of key uncertainties and practical knowledge translation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Expert faculty with trial-based COA training and preceptorship experience; recruitment through clinical networks such as RQAS and NMD4C
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only on self-reported short-term familiarity and confidence gains, not objective performance or sustained practice change

### Target Learners
- allied health clinical evaluators (PTs, OTs, nurses) in neuromuscular care

### Learning Goals
- hands-on competence in administering and scoring SMA motor outcome assessments
