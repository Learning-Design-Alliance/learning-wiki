# A skateboarding-based static equilibrium tutorial targeting documented student conceptions about force, torque, and center of mass

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The tutorial is designed to help students refine conceptions documented in the physics education research literature, including the assumptions that forces applied on both sides of a fulcrum must be equal, that a tilted orientation is caused by unbalanced torques or forces, and that the center of mass divides a system into two components with equal mass. The tutorial uses a skateboarding scenario to help students relate static equilibrium concepts to their everyday experiences.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- the tutorial should elicit, confront, and resolve the documented student conceptions about static equilibrium
#### Constraints
- the authors note that future work is needed to validate the tutorial by surveying students' motivations and examining their performance on questions about static equilibrium

### Target Learners
- science and engineering majors in a calculus-based introductory physics course

### Learning Goals
- understanding the relationship of force and torque to a system in static equilibrium and the location of the center of mass
