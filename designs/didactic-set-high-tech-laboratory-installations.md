# Didactic set of high-tech educational laboratory equipment with digital program control for material-processing methods

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A created complex of teachware comprising the course program and lecture course «Bases of Electrophysical and Electrochemical Methods of Processing of Materials», a set of educational and laboratory installations on electrospark, high-frequency electrospark processing, and metal hardening by currents of high frequency that, as the article prints, "was designed and made", plus methodical recommendations for laboratory workshops and educational tests for determining the level of mastering basic concepts. In the experimental groups the classes were given with the application of the problem and research methods, with students conducting educational and research experiments to find optimum processing modes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Integration of the content of technological and general technical disciplines with physics and chemistry
- Methodical recommendations to the laboratory course including tasks explaining production processes from the point of view of Physics and Chemistry
#### Constraints
- The comparison at Tyumen State University notes results were higher where the equipment is better, indicating equipment quality matters

### Target Learners
- students majoring in «Technological education» at teachers training institutes and technical universities

### Learning Goals
- learning the bases of electrophysical and electrochemical methods of processing of materials
- developing practical skills and creative-task abilities
