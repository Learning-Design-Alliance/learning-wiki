# Middle school transition-words writing lesson integrating self-assessment, peer feedback, and personal learning goals

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A middle school lesson vignette in which English learners and native English-speaking students learn to show relationships between ideas by combining sentences with transition words. Students write personal learning goals based on the success criterion, revise drafts with optional partner brainstorming and sentence stems, exchange peer feedback aligned to the success criteria, rate themselves using a rubric, and set a new personal learning goal for the next lesson.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The teacher designed the lesson after reviewing students' writing drafts from a previous day, which demonstrated that students needed support creating coherence and fluidity in their writing.
