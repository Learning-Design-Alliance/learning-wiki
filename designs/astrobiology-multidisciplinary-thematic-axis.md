# Astrobiology as a multidisciplinary thematic axis capable of integrating knowledge fields in science education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article characterises Astrobiology as "uma ciência multidisciplinar" whose concepts and research gaps rest on base sciences such as Astronomy, Cosmology, Geology, Astrochemistry and Astrophysics (Galante et al., 2016). Because understanding it requires traversing the Universe's history from the Big Bang to the emergence of life, the authors use it as a thematic axis that connects natural and human sciences, supporting a less fragmented education and articulating a view of science as open, with many unanswered questions.
