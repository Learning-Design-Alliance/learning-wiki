# Duesseldorf List of Complaints: 123 complaint items linking multidisciplinary diseases to clinical practice training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Duesseldorf Curriculum is built on learning with patient complaint items: "For this educational format, 123 complaint items were defined and their compatibility with diseases from various disciplines was tested." The list was synthesized from the Dundee task list, the German Catalog of Learning Objectives (GK2-2009) and the Dutch Blue Print 2009, revised by directors and lecturers of 44 clinics and institutes, and passed by the Faculty Council. Complaint handling is largely independent of the health care environment, while underlying diseases vary with setting.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The list had to be extensive enough to link to all diseases relevant for medical studies across disciplines, yet limited enough to provide practical use in clinical practice training
#### Constraints
- Reasons for consultation predominantly in outpatient and primary care, such as advisory services, screening and vaccination, were also included

### Target Learners
- medical students in their 3rd, 4th and 5th year of study

### Learning Goals
- clinical thinking and action
- differential diagnostic and therapeutical procedures
- integration of basic-science knowledge into clinical cases
