# Five-step sequential robotic training pathway with benchmark-gated progression

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a structured, sequential training pathway for surgical trainees new to robotic console surgery. It "included sequential steps: online didactic modules, a first-assist course, performance-tracked virtual simulation exercises using the Versius Trainer, basic surgical skills assessment, and supervised training in live operating lists". Progression was contingent on meeting defined objective benchmarks at each stage, and live operating used a modular, step-by-step deconstruction of procedures adapted from laparoscopic training paradigms, with case segmentation based on trainee competence to protect patient safety.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Consultant trainers who have completed their own robotic learning curve and been approved to train trainees
- Access to the robotic platform's e-learning, first assist course and Versius Trainer simulation resources
#### Constraints
- Rolled out at a single centre using a single robotic platform, which the authors state could limit generalisability
- Trainees received no dedicated time off from service; training was embedded in clinical schedules

### Target Learners
- General surgical specialist trainees (ST4–ST8) with no prior robotic console experience

### Learning Goals
- Robotic console surgical competence, including system set-up, instrument and endoscope use, suturing, knot-tying and live procedural skills
