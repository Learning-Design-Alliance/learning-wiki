# Rare Knowledge Mining Methodological Framework (RKMMF) for developing rare-disease clinical training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The RKMMF is a phased methodological framework the article uses to structure development of clinical evaluator training in rare disease care. It proceeds through a preliminary needs assessment — described as "an essential step prior to implementing RKMMF-informed workshops" — followed by knowledge mining with expert content leads, group sharing with medical experts to validate findings, external validation via pre-workshop surveys adapted from the Kirkpatrick model, and knowledge translation with post-workshop evaluation. The article demonstrates it as "a scalable approach for capacity-building in rare disease care".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A needs assessment must be conducted prior to implementing RKMMF-informed workshops to align the intervention with the target audience's real-world challenges
#### Constraints
- Demonstrated in the Canadian SMA clinical context with PTs, OTs and nurses; the article notes some insights were directly related to Canada

### Target Learners
- clinical evaluators: physiotherapists, occupational therapists, and neuromuscular nurses

### Learning Goals
- selecting, administering and interpreting clinical outcome assessments in spinal muscular atrophy
