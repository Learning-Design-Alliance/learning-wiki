# Online booking system for clinical skills SDL laboratories and equipment at ULGEMS

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The University of Limerick Graduate Entry Medical School offered students the option of online booking of clinical skills laboratories and equipment to facilitate SDL, with electronic records of bookings retained. The study used these records to quantify engagement, finding that the most frequently booked skills and equipment were common to both pre-clinical years and were those requiring equipment or simulators.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must have access to bookable clinical skills laboratories and equipment, and booking records must be retained for engagement to be tracked
#### Constraints
- Booking or attending a session does not necessarily mean students actively engaged with the clinical skill, and students were not required to submit logs of SDL activity

### Target Learners
- graduate-entry medical students in pre-clinical years

### Learning Goals
- clinical skills practice
- preparation for OSCE assessments
