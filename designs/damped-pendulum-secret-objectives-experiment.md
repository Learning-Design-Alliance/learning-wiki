# Damped pendulum experiment with hidden Coulomb damping as a concrete secret-objectives implementation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A simple pendulum with a paper cone on the bob is presented as a worked example of embedding secret objectives. The script omits damping sources: attaching the string with 'a messy knot at the attachment to the pivot' introduces dry (Coulomb) damping producing linear amplitude decay, while students may expect only the exponential decay of viscous damping, confronting preconceptions when data disagree with the expected model.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Little, if any, specialised equipment beyond that available in even high-school science departments
- A means of securing the string at the pivot so that friction can be introduced or removed
#### Constraints
- Students with strong preconceptions may ignore unexpected results or attempt to adjust measurement data to fit expectations

### Target Learners
- introductory undergraduate physics students

### Learning Goals
- identifying dominant damping mechanisms
- testing the limits of theoretical models
- observation during experiments
