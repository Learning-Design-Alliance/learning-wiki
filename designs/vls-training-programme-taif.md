# Vocabulary Learning Strategies Training Programme

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 12-week vocabulary learning strategies training programme for EFL students, based on Schmitt's (1997) VLS taxonomy, covering determination, social, memory, cognitive, and metacognitive strategies. The programme was delivered to the experimental group alongside their regular English course.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered alongside a regular EFL course over 12 weeks
#### Constraints
- Studied only with Saudi female EFL students at Taif University

### Target Learners
- EFL female university students

### Learning Goals
- English vocabulary size
- vocabulary learning strategy use
