# CRI:SIS: a three-hour virtual adaptive simulation intervention with four COVID-19 scenarios, prebriefing, and structured debriefing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
CRI:SIS (COVID-19 Responsive Intervention: Systems Improvement Simulations) is a "3-hour virtual training simulation" delivered with Laerdal LLEAP software 1–10 days before a clinical shift. It comprised four scenarios of varying COVID-19 severity (CMO goals-of-care, mild COVID-19 with vaccine hesitancy, moderate COVID-19 using the COVID-19 Severity Index, and severe COVID-19 with ventilator management), each preceded by prebriefing on guideline changes and followed by "a structured debriefing session" led by a clinician trained in debriefing. Critical actions were updated iteratively as institutional COVID-19 guidelines evolved.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained simulation co-investigators board-certified in emergency medicine delivered the scenarios, with the principal investigator present for every scenario to ensure consistency.
- A research team member with emergency medicine clinical expertise and advanced debriefing skills led each debriefing.
#### Constraints
- Only physicians (residents and attendings) were enrolled; the effect on other clinicians was not tested.
- Participants could not be blinded to the intervention.

### Target Learners
- Front-line emergency medicine resident and attending physicians

### Learning Goals
- COVID-19 communication, clinical management, and disposition decisions
- Adoption of rapidly evolving COVID-19 guidelines
