# New BSc program on Cyberphysical nuclear technologies with quantum computing courses at SPT

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
SPT recently introduced a BSc educational program on "Cyberphysical nuclear technologies" that combines classical quantum courses with newly introduced courses on quantum computing, and a Master-level program oriented on quantum computing and quantum technologies is under development. A new course on quantum information theory is delivered jointly by representatives of Taras Shevchenko Kyiv National University and the University of South Carolina. The article states the program "contains classical quantum courses as well as newly introduced ones, such as “Quantum computer basics” and “Quantum algorithms”."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cross-institutional collaboration (Kyiv National University and University of South Carolina) to staff the new quantum information theory course
#### Constraints
- The Master-level program on quantum computing and quantum technologies is still at the development stage

### Target Learners
- BSc students in nuclear and quantum technologies; MSc students in a planned quantum computing program

### Learning Goals
- quantum computing basics, quantum algorithms, quantum information theory
