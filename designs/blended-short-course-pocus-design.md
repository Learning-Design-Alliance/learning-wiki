# Blended short-course design pairing pre-course e-learning with in-person didactics, high-ratio hands-on practice, and simulation-based pathology cases

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The course's design pattern combines flipped e-learning prework with an intensive one-day in-person session. The article states that "To allow adequate hands-on instruction and deliberate practice, we maintained an instructor-to-learner ratio of ≤ 1:5", and that participants practiced on "the portable devices ... that participating EMS agencies planned to adopt", so learners trained on field equipment including knobology, anatomical relationships, and artifacts. Normal anatomy was scanned on standardized patients, while pathology was presented via the SonoSim LiveScan platform.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor-to-learner ratio of ≤ 1:5
- Access to the portable ultrasound devices the learners' agencies use or plan to adopt
- Standardized patients verbally consented and contracted for safety
#### Constraints
- The article notes the study did not directly assess transfer of skills to the clinical environment

### Target Learners
- Practicing prehospital providers (paramedics and flight nurses)

### Learning Goals
- Image acquisition and interpretation for the eFAST examination
- Familiarity and troubleshooting with agency-specific portable ultrasound devices
