# Scientist-narrative lesson design combining oral histories with archival media and participant data

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A lesson-level design pattern in which lesson content is built from scientists' own accounts — interviews, archived oral histories and memoirs — and enriched with NASA media, archival material, and participant-contributed photographs, light curves and spectra. The article reports that "The lives of the various astrophysicists... were all appreciated by the students as much as the science," and that students enjoyed meeting the experts behind the lessons.
