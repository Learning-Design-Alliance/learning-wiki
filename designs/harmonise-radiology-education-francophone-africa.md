# Radiology education in francophone sub-Saharan Africa should be harmonised across faculties to ensure uniform, high-quality training.

## Description
The article argues that harmonising radiology education across francophone sub-Saharan African countries is essential to ensure uniform, high-quality training. This can be achieved by "developing a common educational framework and standardized course materials, defining the minimum competencies to be acquired in radiology at each level of medical education". The authors state it is necessary to "rethink radiology education in most medical schools".
