# Eight-week tier movement cycles with fast-tracking and exit rules aligned to report card periods

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study's RTI protocols allowed movement across tiers every 8 weeks in tandem with report card periods. Students who remained below the 40th percentile on three out of four measures and showed growth slopes below the sample mean moved to a more intensive tier; students successful for two consecutive 8-week periods were exited to a less intensive tier. The design was "unique relative to prior RTI investigations in allowing movement across tiers every 8 weeks ... and in allowing fast tracking to Tier 3 for the most needy students."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Screening and progress-monitoring assessments administered at each 8-week session
- Local-norm 40th-percentile cut points and sample-mean growth slopes as movement criteria
#### Constraints
- Movement criteria were developed and evaluated in a first-year RTI implementation in one district

### Target Learners
- First-grade students in Tier 1, Tier 2, or Tier 3 reading intervention

### Learning Goals
- Timely intensification of support for non-responders and de-escalation for responders
