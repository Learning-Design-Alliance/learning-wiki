# Coordination of Services Teams (COST) link students to mental health supports within a multi-tiered system of supports

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
COST is SCOE's coordination mechanism linking students in Sacramento County schools to mental health supports within a multi-tiered system of supports (MTSS). The brief states that "Rather than relying on direct referrals to clinicians, COST coordinates referrals using systematic and proactive approaches (e.g., universal screening data)." This is intended to ensure students' needs are met through appropriate channels, for example avoiding inappropriate referral to therapy when a classroom-based issue is present.
