# Standardized GUIDE teaching unit with four elements (slides, short video, Colab lab, related papers)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each GUIDE unit is the smallest teaching-ready element, corresponding to one concrete teaching objective, and follows a uniform structure of four elements: slides presenting the core idea, a short video for self-study and review, a runnable Colab lab with an end-to-end workflow, and related papers connecting the unit to research context. The article states "GUIDE uses a uniform unit structure so instructors can teach with low setup cost and students can use it easily." This organization enables consistency for students' learning experience and for instructor reuse and grading.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Slides should introduce the problem, define inputs/outputs, highlight common pitfalls, and include an example
- The lab includes at least one runnable example so students can execute the main steps and see the expected artifacts
#### Constraints
- If Google Colab is not applicable (e.g., special tools, licensing limits, or heavy compute), the unit should provide a runnable script and clear instructions

### Target Learners
- students with diverse backgrounds across EE/CE/CS majors

- one concrete teaching objective per unit in GenAI-driven digital design
