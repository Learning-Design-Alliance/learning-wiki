# Four-hour annual emergency-training module combining a lecture, hands-on equipment stations, and role-play simulation stations

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The KAUFD module is a one-day, four-hour annual design for clinical-year dental students: "a pre-test, a 45-minute lecture, four hands-on stations, six role-play simulation-based medical emergency stations, and a post-test". The lecture covers prevention and preparedness with videos including an error-critique exercise; the hands-on stations cover emergency medication, crash carts, monitors and oxygen equipment, and a BLS refresher; the role-play stations assign students rotating roles (patient, operator, assistants) in six emergency scenarios with instructor-guided checklists until competency is demonstrated.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor-led stations with trained instructors and checklists; role-play scenarios aligned with commonly reported medical emergencies in the literature
#### Constraints
- The article notes the module ran in "a leading university center with extensive resources" and replication in centers without emergency-department support is yet to be seen

### Target Learners
- Clinical-year (Years 4-6) undergraduate dental students

### Learning Goals
- Knowledge, skills, and behavior for prevention, preparedness, and management of medical emergencies in the dental clinic
