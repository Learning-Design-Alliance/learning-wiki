# Real-time visual representation of sensor data in Classical Mechanics formalism as a teaching framework

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's organizing idea is that teaching improves when measured data are displayed in real time through the visual and mathematical formalism of the discipline itself. iMecaProf's "visual representation is built upon the formalism of Classical Mechanics", showing frames, unit vectors, acceleration and velocity vectors, and energy plots live, so abstract formalism is directly tied to a physical object in the student's hand.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Real-time sensor data acquisition (100 times a second) and a display that maps measurements onto the discipline's formal representations
#### Constraints
- Built specifically for Classical Mechanics formalism: frames, vectors, trajectories, Newton's laws and energy

### Target Learners
- First-year university students in Classical Mechanics

### Learning Goals
- Connecting formal representations (frames, vectors, energy) to measured physical motion
