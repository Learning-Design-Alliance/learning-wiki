# Bauman's layered-learning model as a framework for scaffolding didactic teaching with multimedia educational technology

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Bauman's layered-learning model is the theoretical framework the study adopts. It describes "a matrix for scaffolding traditional didactic presentation of course materials with multimedia educational technology", presupposing that traditional didactic techniques retain relevance while digital media make simulation available anytime and anyplace. In this study the model framed Kahoot® quizzes and the whole-slide imaging platform as layers added to lectures, reading, and faculty interaction, shifting faculty from lecturer to guide.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Traditional didactic resources (faculty interaction, readings) remain part of the learning mix alongside the technology layer
#### Constraints
- The model does not replace traditional reading assignments with technology

### Target Learners
- first-year medical and dental students

### Learning Goals
- histology knowledge and engagement
