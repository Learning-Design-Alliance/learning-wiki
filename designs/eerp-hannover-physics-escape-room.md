# The educational Eurogame escape room in physics (eEERP) at the University of Veterinary Medicine Hannover

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A spectral educational escape room in physics covering mechanics, optics, electromagnetism (dc/ac), atomic physics, solid states, thermodynamics and X-ray, built for veterinary medicine students. It contains 10 mandatory sequential physics puzzles (e.g. Stirling engine, train experiment, magnetic dog puzzle) and 16 optional Eurogame puzzles (e.g. dexterity cubes, wood puzzles, knots), scored by a victory point system with a maximum of 166 VP. The article reports that "Statistically, the students earned ≈ 20.1 VP per player visited the eEERP", and that approximately 85% of the participating students were female.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Four kinds of supporting documents: story, rulebook, knowledge transfers, and puzzle instructions
- An inverted (not fully locked) room layout for fire protection, with the starting room unlocked
#### Constraints
- A mark/grade was not given; it was only possible to fail or pass the ERP examination
- Mobile phones were allowed but digital detoxification was strongly urged

### Target Learners
- First-semester veterinary medicine students (Group C) at the University of Veterinary Medicine Hannover Foundation

### Learning Goals
- Basic knowledge of physics across its main disciplines
- Experimental skills and hands-on mentality relevant to veterinary patient examination
