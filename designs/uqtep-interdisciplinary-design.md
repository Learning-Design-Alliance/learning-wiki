> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The article holds that quantum technology is intrinsically interdisciplinary and transcends conventional divisions between physics, computer science, engineering, and mathematics. The program therefore emphasizes interdisciplinary learning and cooperation through a core curriculum spanning multiple fields, elective courses outside the student's primary field, interdisciplinary group projects, and cross-disciplinary second-year research projects.
