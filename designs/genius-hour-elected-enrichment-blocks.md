# Genius Hour: student-elected enrichment blocks in the extended day with showcase events

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Genius Hour is Los Padillas's extended-day enrichment structure in which "Students select an extracurricular activity to focus on daily for 6 weeks," with options ranging "from coding, robotics, and circuits to knitting, soccer, martial arts, and mariachi." All teachers are involved, keeping classes small for individualized attention, and academic skills are woven in informally. Each 6-week cycle ends with a Genius Hour Showcase where parents and caregivers observe what students produced, serving also as family engagement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Extended day schedule with time built in for enrichment
- All teachers involved so classes stay small
#### Constraints
- Devised as part of the school redesign and directly tied to community priorities gathered in visioning sessions

### Target Learners
- Elementary students (PreK–5) in a Title I, predominantly Hispanic dual language school

### Learning Goals
- Student choice and voice
- Academic enrichment
- Relationship-building across grades
- Family engagement
