# Self-contained web-based obesity management learning module for medical students

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A self-contained Articulate-based module presenting current obesity management guidelines, evidence-based recommendations, myth debunking, and practical tips for primary care. It "can be completed in 20–30 minutes" and "can be navigated on demand by the learner," usable as independent study or an in-class exercise, ending with two multiple-choice knowledge questions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A web browser supporting HTML; learners need a basic understanding of obesity as a prerequisite from preclinical curriculum
#### Constraints
- Current guidelines could be updated at any time, so the instructor must ensure the resource is current prior to delivery

### Target Learners
- Medical students in clinical years
- primary care residents
- practicing primary care physicians

### Learning Goals
- Evidence-based obesity management
- debunking weight-loss myths
- applying guidelines in clinical practice
