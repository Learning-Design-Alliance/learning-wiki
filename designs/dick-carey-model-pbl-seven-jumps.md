# Dick and Carey instructional design model combined with the PBL 7-jumps approach for curriculum development

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The project adopted the Dick and Carey model for instructional design, in which "The output from one phase of the model provided the input for the next phase", covering analysis of learning needs, design and development of objectives and assessment instruments, participants' assessment with emphasis on formative evaluation, and evaluation of the whole strategy while being designed. Within courses, PBL tutorial groups follow the structured "7 jumps approach": clarify unfamiliar terms; define problems; brainstorm explanations; arrange tentative solutions and priorities; formulate learning objectives; undertake private study; and share and evaluate results, facilitated by trained tutors.
