# Google Classroom clinical pathology course platform with five themed activity zones

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a Google Classroom platform built for 12 units of a clinical pathology course, where "Each unit included separate 'zones' for Videos, News, Games, Quizzes, and Arena." The Video zone linked to teacher-made or YouTube videos; the News zone linked pathology knowledge to everyday life; the Games zone used Quizlet, Quizizz, and Quizalize with time-based leader boards; the Quizzes zone provided past national exam papers via Google Drive and Google Forms with instant feedback; and the Arena zone supported competition, discussion, and teacher commentary. Students used their own internet-enabled mobile devices to access it anytime.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students need internet-enabled devices and access (free campus/dorm wireless was provided for those without unlimited internet), and time to learn to operate the platform
#### Constraints
- Students faced problems similar to other learning management systems, such as internet speed and coverage, difficulty understanding platform operation, and needing time to practice

### Target Learners
- fourth-grade five-year junior college nursing students in Taiwan

### Learning Goals
- clinical pathology knowledge (microbiology, immunology, bloodwork, system diseases, function tests, tumor markers)
