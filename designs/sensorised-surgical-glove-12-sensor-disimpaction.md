# Low-cost sensorised surgical glove with 12 piezoresistive force sensors for simulated fetal head disimpaction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A sensorised surgical glove developed through engineer–obstetrician collaboration, with "12 sensors placed on numerous points including the fingertips, palm and back of the hand". The piezoresistive sensors (10 mm × 8 mm × 2 mm) are individually calibrated to map voltage changes to force, worn under a third over-glove, and wired to a data acquisition system and laptop. The gloves are low-cost, approximately $1 USD, and were used with a Limbs and Things impacted-fetal-head simulator at a national course.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Worn as three layers (under-glove, sensorised glove, over-glove) with wired connection to a data acquisition system and laptop running custom calibration software
#### Constraints
- Wired design and triple gloving reduced usability; participants reported reduced tactile sensitivity and discomfort from wires, and the force benchmark derives from one expert rather than a validated safety threshold

### Target Learners
- Obstetricians of varying seniority (ST1–2 to consultant)

### Learning Goals
- Safe force application during impacted fetal head disimpaction at caesarean birth
