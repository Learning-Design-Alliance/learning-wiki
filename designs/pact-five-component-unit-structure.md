# PACT five-component unit structure for text-based content instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The PACT program organizes a 10-day content unit around five interrelated components embedded in teachers' content instruction. As the article prints it, "The PACT program consisted of five inter- related components embedded in the teachers' content instruction: comprehension canopy, essential words, knowledge acquisition, team-based learning comprehension checks, and team-based learning knowledge application." The comprehension canopy opens the unit with a video and overarching question reviewed daily; essential words are high-utility concepts introduced day one and reviewed across the unit; teams complete comprehension checks with scratch-off answer sheets for immediate feedback and a day-nine application activity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers were provided with semi-scripted lesson plans, a daily schedule identifying when components were delivered, and all materials needed for implementation.
#### Constraints
- Fidelity observations showed teachers had the most difficulty with the knowledge acquisition and team-based knowledge application components, with more than 50% of observations in those areas rated 2.

### Target Learners
- eighth-grade students in general education U.S. history classes, including students with disabilities

### Learning Goals
- engagement with text-based learning
- social studies content knowledge
- application of content to complex historical questions
