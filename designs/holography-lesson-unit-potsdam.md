# 12-school-hour holography lesson unit with simulation programs in ray and interference optics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A lesson unit of 12 school hours developed at the University of Potsdam to examine model building processes in interference optics. Its basic structure moves from development of spatial images, through interference effects at Fresnel zone plates, to production of transmission holograms and reconstruction of holograms. A focal point is the implementation of simulation programs in ray optics and interference optics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The unit was implemented in general and extension courses of secondary school level II in Brandenburg schools, with pre- and final tests, concept maps and post-lesson interviews.
#### Constraints
- The article reports evaluation of learning gain, motivation, practicability and impact of the simulation programs as ongoing research questions, with a statistical-empirical analysis of the questionnaires still to follow.

### Target Learners
- secondary school level II students in general and extension courses

### Learning Goals
- model building processes in interference optics
- understanding of holography and models of light
