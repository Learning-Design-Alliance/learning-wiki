# Autonomy-supportive teaching unit on heat transfer with choices, rationales, and informational language

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A standardized physics teaching unit on heat transfer delivered either with autonomy support or in the regular teaching style. The autonomy-support version entailed "provision of choices, provision of rationales, and informational language". It was taught by students' own physics teachers in 17 real-life classrooms and evaluated against a control condition for effects on need satisfaction, achievement emotions, and self-regulated learning behaviors.
