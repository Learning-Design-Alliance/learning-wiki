# Educational marble game kit for trilingual vocabulary teaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A physical game board and accessory set developed by the researcher over a ten-year interdisciplinary project supported by Bosch, adapted from children's marble games for language lessons. The game board "consists of a square of 65 x 65 cm" with 24 numbered spots, holes, and four numbered storage areas; play uses "24 numbered stones, four standard sized marbles and a dice", with stones bearing profession pictures inscribed in three languages. It was used with teacher candidates, school students, and university learners of German.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A referee and four players per game, with student sheets, referee sheets, and word lists prepared for the target group
#### Constraints
- Instructions for use "can be modified according to the levels and needs of the target groups"

### Target Learners
- primary and secondary school students
- university students learning German as a second foreign language
- teacher candidates

### Learning Goals
- vocabulary learning in Turkish, English, and German
- communication skills through game dialogue
