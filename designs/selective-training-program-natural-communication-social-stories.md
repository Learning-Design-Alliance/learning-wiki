# Selective training program combining natural communication strategies and social stories

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A researcher-designed teacher-training program in two axes: first training teachers of students with autism to use "some of the techniques found in the natural communication strategies and social stories", then having trained teachers apply a series of sessions with students. It comprised 20 teacher-training sessions over four weeks and 30 application sessions over six weeks, five weekly sessions of 60-90 minutes each.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers trained in the techniques before applying them with students
- Sessions delivered in the students' natural school environment
#### Constraints
- Developed and rated for students with autism at the Institute of Intellectual Education, east of Riyadh

### Target Learners
- students with autism
- teachers of students with autism
