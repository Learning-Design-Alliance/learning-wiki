# Diabetes – a Global Challenge MOOC on Coursera

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 6-week open online course offered three times (spring 2014 – fall 2015) on the prevention and treatment of diabetes and obesity, hosted on Coursera. It is "characterized by instructor-guided lessons combined with tests and assignments, with optional certification", and includes reading materials, video lectures, multiple choice questions, and an online discussion forum. 52,006 participants signed up and 29,469 were active across the three sessions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Internet access for enrollment and participation in the online course room
#### Constraints
- Optional certification is granted only if the learner achieves a certain level ascertained by on-line tests

### Target Learners
- health care professionals
- medical students
- global learners interested in diabetes and obesity

### Learning Goals
- update knowledge of basic and clinical sciences in diabetes and obesity
- improve clinical practice in prevention and treatment of diabetes and obesity
