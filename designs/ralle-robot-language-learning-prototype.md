# RALL-E robot-assisted language learning prototype

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
RALL-E (Robot-Assisted Language Learning in Education) is an Alelo project that migrates agent technology to the Robokind R25 robot, producing a prototype that can engage in conversation in Chinese. The concept is being tested in collaboration with the Virginia Department of Education and the Thomas Jefferson High School for Science and Technology in Alexandria, Virginia. Embedding agent technology into the robot lets learners engage naturally in mixed-initiative conversation more than with a screen-displayed agent; the approach descends from the STEVE work in that agent and learners cohabit a shared world, but the robot enters the learners' real world rather than learners entering the agent's virtual world.
