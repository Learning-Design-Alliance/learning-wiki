# Garbagnatese FGC randomized controlled trial

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
An individual-level RCT, blocking on schools, in a social district of 7 cities in the province of Milan. Of 262 referred at-risk students, 131 were assigned to treatment (84 treated) and 131 to control. Randomization created equivalence on a large subset of observed variables; attrition rates were "negligible and balanced". Outcomes came from pre- and post-intervention questionnaires, school administrative data, and national standardized tests, with ITT and IV-based ATT estimates.
