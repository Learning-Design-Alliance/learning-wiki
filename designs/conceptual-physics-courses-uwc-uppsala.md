# The Conceptual physics courses at UWC and Uppsala: reflection on learning integrated with physics content, everyday contexts and critical thinking

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Conceptual physics courses at the University of the Western Cape and Uppsala University add to the physics content "an additional focus on reflection on learning, and supporting the students in becoming aware of their own learning", integrated tightly with course content through in-class discussion and reflective assignments. They address beliefs about the nature of physics knowledge via puzzling situations with philosophical undertones, and relate physics concepts to everyday, social, historical and environmental contexts to empower critical thinking.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teaching and developing the course rests on a scholarly collegiality among teachers, discussing student learning and drawing on research on students' approaches and conceptions.
#### Constraints
- The courses were conceptual, focusing on concepts rather than mathematical calculations, though the authors state the outlined features are not tied to that nature in particular.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics students in Sweden and South Africa

### Learning Goals
- awareness of one's own learning of physics
- critical thinking about physics-related phenomena beyond academia
