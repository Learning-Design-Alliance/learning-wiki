# Five-stage interactive e-learning platform for point-of-care ultrasound (FAST) training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An interactive e-learning platform for PGY-1 PoCUS training structured in five irreversible stages: "Stage 1 was Pretest Stage with multiple-choice questions", a Teaching Stage for core knowledge, a Self-Learning Stage recognizing at least 20 US images with interactive interpretation, a Self-Testing Stage, and a Case-Simulation Stage for clinical judgments. User activity and time per stage were automatically recorded, and a satisfaction survey preceded logout. Most users finished Stages 1–3 at first access and accessed the platform via desktops or laptops (95% of accesses).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Internet access and unique user authorization to access the platform
- US machines and a hands-on FAST curriculum (30-min didactics and 2-hour hands-on training) alongside the platform
#### Constraints
- The learning process was irreversible among stages and steps within a stage
- In this study only FAST performance was evaluated, at a single academic hospital

### Target Learners
- first postgraduate year (PGY-1) residents, novice sonographers

### Learning Goals
- FAST image acquisition and interpretation
- clinical judgment in point-of-care ultrasound
