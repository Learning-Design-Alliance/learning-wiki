# Laboratorio Pedagogico Didattico TIC: a two-part ICT laboratory for mathematics and physics teacher candidates

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Laboratorio Pedagogico Didattico TIC is a 15-hour (1 CFU) component of the TFA teacher-qualification course for Class A049 Mathematics and Physics at the University of Palermo, introduced by D.M. 487. It was organized, as the authors state, in una prima parte dedicata alle novità nella gestione della Scuola e in una seconda parte dedicata alle tecnologie dell'informazione e della comunicazione nella didattica. Trainees documented administrative ICT, experimented with the Edmodo sharing platform, and worked on interactive whiteboards, real-time sensors, smartphones as lab tools, and modeling and simulation software.
