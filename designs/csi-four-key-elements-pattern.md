# Collaborative Science Inquiry (CSI) with four key elements: Guided Inquiry, Modeling, Collaborative Discussion, and Visualization

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The CSI pedagogy, developed in the WiMVT project at School of Science and Technology Singapore, uses the web as a platform to support students' collaborative inquiry-based learning in science. Figure 1 depicts CSI "with the four key elements of it, i.e., Guided Inquiry, Modeling, Collaborative Discussion, and Visualization." The modelling element aligns with the NRC Framework for K-12 Science Education, which lists modelling and mathematical and computational thinking among essential scientific practices for grades K-12.
