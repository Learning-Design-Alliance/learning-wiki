# Vocab+ vocabulary-added intervention (semantic-relations and morphological-awareness variants)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A supplementary push-in classroom intervention for first-grade English Learners combining explicit vocabulary instruction with phonological awareness and decoding. Words were Tier II words drawn from weekly expository passage read-alouds, introduced with "synonyms, antonyms, pictures, examples and non-examples, and definitional sentences," and revisited across the eight weeks. The SR variant taught grouping, comparing, and semantic feature analysis of word meanings; the MA variant taught roots, inflectional and derivational suffixes, and word formation through activities such as a cubes game.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Small-group instruction by trained researchers within regularly scheduled language arts time
- An average of 15 minutes per day, four days a week, for eight weeks
- Weekly expository passage read-aloud shared across conditions
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only with first-grade, primarily Spanish-speaking English Learners in one Title I school
- Vocabulary outcomes were measured with an experimental true/false sentence test of taught words

### Target Learners
- First-grade English Learners at risk of reading difficulty

### Learning Goals
- Target word vocabulary knowledge
- Phonological awareness and decoding
- Morphological and semantic word-learning strategies
