# Measurement battery pairing standardized child assessments with software-log penetration, observed fidelity, and intention scales

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The protocol assembles a multi-source measurement battery: direct assessment of cognitive ability (DAS-II) and school readiness (Bracken-3), teacher-rated adaptive behavior (ABAS-II) and social skills (PDD-BI), parent-reported SCQ for autism classification confirmation, monthly TeachTown software logs for penetration, developer-conducted fidelity checklists, and validated theory-of-planned-behavior item stems measuring intentions, attitudes, norms, and self-efficacy for each EBP. Assessment teams meet weekly to maintain 90% reliability on direct measures.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Bachelor's level research assistants trained to 90% reliability on each fidelity measure before field observations
#### Constraints
- ADOS-2 is administered to only 20% of the sample for diagnostic confirmation

### Target Learners
- Students with autism aged 5-8 and their K-2 autism support teachers

### Learning Goals
- Cognitive gain, school readiness, adaptive and social skills, and faithful implementation of EBPs
