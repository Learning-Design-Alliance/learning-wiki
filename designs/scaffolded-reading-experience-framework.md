# Scaffolded Reading Experience (SRE) framework

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The SRE framework is "designed to assist students in comprehending individual texts", enabling a teacher to provide scaffolding that increases the likelihood students complete the reading task successfully. It rests on four instructional concepts: scaffolding, the gradual release of responsibility, the zone of proximal development, and the notion of success. It has two major phases: a planning phase, in which teachers consider the text, the purposes for reading, and students' strengths and needs, and an implementation phase of pre-, during-, and post-reading activities. In this study it served as the common instructional container that allowed fair comparison of the two teaching approaches.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers select pre-, during-, and post-reading activities based on consideration of the text, the reading purpose, and the students' strengths and needs
#### Constraints
- In this study the SRE was operationalized with approach-specific purposes, so its comparison value depends on holding text and students constant

### Target Learners
- middle school students reading individual narrative texts

### Learning Goals
- comprehension of individual texts
- response to literature
