# Constructivist, cognitive load, and Self-Determination Theory frameworks explain the Puzzle technique's pedagogical value

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors interpret their findings through three educational theories. The Puzzle technique "aligns with constructivist principles, as students actively build knowledge through collaboration and peer teaching". Dividing complex pharmacological content into smaller segments "helps to reduce cognitive load, enhancing comprehension and retention". The intervention may also engage Self-Determination Theory dimensions by "fostering a sense of autonomy, competence, and social connection", enhancing intrinsic motivation.
