# Seven-activity small-group narrative lesson format with story grammar icons, peer retelling, and embedded vocabulary review

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Each lesson conformed to the same instructional format with seven activities: story reading with icons, vocabulary introduction in story context, story-part naming with Story Gestures, individual retells with progressively removed visual supports while peers played listening games (Story Bingo, Story Cubes, Story Sticks), and vocabulary review with photos or objects. Children heard the story twice and retold it as a group and individually each session.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Presentation book with scripts and illustrations
- icons representing story grammar elements
- photographs or classroom objects for vocabulary contexts
#### Constraints
- Lessons were semimanualized; interventionists were taught to complete each activity's primary objective and differentiate based on children's language and engagement

### Target Learners
- preschool children in small groups of three

### Learning Goals
- narrative retelling
- story grammar knowledge
- targeted vocabulary word knowledge
