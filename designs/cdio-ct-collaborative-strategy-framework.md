# CDIO-CT collaborative strategy framework for complex STEM problems

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A framework combining the conceive-design-implement-operate engineering approach with computational thinking for problem-project-oriented STEM education, in which "the CDIO is about 'how to do', CT is about 'how to think', and the project means 'what to do'". The four CDIO stages are interwoven with the five CT factors (decomposition, abstraction and modeling, logical and algorithmic thinking, debugging, and verification-validation-testing), illustrated through solving the pendulum period and generalized in Figure 12 to arbitrary complex STEM problems in modeling and simulation, where one problem admits multiple ways and each way multiple methods.

## Design Implications

#### Requirements
