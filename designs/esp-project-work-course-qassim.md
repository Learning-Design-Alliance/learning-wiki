# Project-based ESP course with weekly projects and a term dictionary-program project for computing students

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's intervention is a series of weekly projects plus one term project, with titles chosen in light of the needs analysis questionnaire results. Weekly projects covered topics such as parts of a computer, purchasing a computer, and implementation and management of LAN; the term project was designing a dictionary program fed by 2800 vocabulary items collected by eight groups. Co-operative learning techniques, peer and group coaching, individualized learning and activity-based learning were employed throughout.

### Learning Goals
