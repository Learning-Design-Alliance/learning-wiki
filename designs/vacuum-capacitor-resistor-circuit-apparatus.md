# Vacuum capacitor-resistor circuit apparatus for revealing non-exponential decay

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A first-year-style RC circuit revised for accuracy: "The RC circuit shown in fig. 1 consists of a parallel resistor and a vacuum capacitor with C = 2.789 nF (Comet model CFMN-2800BAC/8-DE-G)," energized by a 98 V power supply and disconnected with a mechanical switch. A Keysight 34465A digital voltmeter collected all the data shown, providing the resolution needed to illustrate deviations from single exponential decay.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A digital voltmeter with resolution beyond a typical 8-bit laboratory oscilloscope (a Keysight 34465A was used to collect all the data shown here)
#### Constraints
- A major issue in acquiring data that is precise and valid is associated with the small capacitance values that are found in vacuum or air capacitors

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics laboratory students

### Learning Goals
- collecting valid and repeatable data
- testing scientific models against measurements
