# Four-hour simulation-based ABCDE training course with checklist scoring form and global rating

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A four-hour simulation-based ABCDE training session within a three-week emergency medicine course, consisting of an introduction, a video demonstration, and six megacode scenarios of intended 12-minute duration run in groups of six trainees on a Resusci-Anne mannequin. The "scoring form included an overall global rating (scale 1–5), where a score ≥ 3 indicated acceptable overall performance." The same checklist was used by the facilitator, peer observers, and the two blinded raters.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A facilitator from the Centre for Clinical Education, a Resusci-Anne mannequin, videotaping of scenarios, and the checklist scoring form modelled on the advanced life support Cardiac Arrest Scenario test checklist
#### Constraints
- Scenarios covered medical and surgical conditions but included no trauma cases; detailed assessment results were not provided to trainees after scenarios

### Target Learners
- final-year medical students

### Learning Goals
- systematic assessment of critically ill patients using the ABCDE approach
