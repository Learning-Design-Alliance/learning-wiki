# mDPBL lesson sequence combining multimedia presentation with small-group problem-based work

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The mDPBL pattern organises a unit around multimedia-supported content plus small-group problem-based activity. "Students divided into small groups which consist of four to six members. The topics presented using multimedia learning. The groups presents the work report." The sequence ran three weeks: introduction to the multimedia learning and group design, content explanation and group task, and group presentation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A multimedia learning package for the topic
- Small groups of four to six members
- A group presentation stage
#### Constraints
- Described for Computer Networks at one Indonesian university

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate students in Informatics Engineering and Information Systems

### Learning Goals
- Understanding abstract networking concepts, teamwork and communication
