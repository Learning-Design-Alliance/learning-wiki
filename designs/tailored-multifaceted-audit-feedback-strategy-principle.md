# Use tailored, multifaceted strategies including training and audit-and-feedback to improve evidence-based nursing practice

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article concludes that "This study provides an example of effective strategies to improve evidence-based practice and nursing care quality through tailored, multifaceted strategies for PSD, including training and audit and feedback interventions." Strategies should be selected in response to barriers identified in a baseline audit, spanning system, nurse, and patient levels. The authors state the approach "can be implemented in more hospitals in the future" and recommend continuous nurse training on best practice compliance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A baseline audit to identify compliance gaps and barriers before selecting strategies
- Engagement of stakeholders including nursing leadership for policy and organizational support
#### Constraints
- The article states further testing of this program in more hospitals is needed, and the audit was carried out in only one hospital with a small sample, reducing generalizability

### Target Learners
- Practicing nurses in hospital neurology wards

### Learning Goals
- Improve compliance with evidence-based screening, prevention, and management of poststroke depression
