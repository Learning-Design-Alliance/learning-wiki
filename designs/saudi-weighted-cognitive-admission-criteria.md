# Saudi weighted cognitive admission criteria (30% HSGA, 30% APT, 40% ACT)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The admission weighting system for Saudi colleges of medicine and allied health professions combines three cognitive criteria: "30 % for cumulative high school grade point average at application (HSGA), 30 % for aptitude test (APT) score, and 40 % for achievement test (ACT) score". The APT assesses analytical and deductive skills in verbal and quantitative sections; the ACT assesses comprehension, application and inference in biology, chemistry, physics, English and mathematics. Applicants surpassing the minimum weighting also pass an unweighted face-to-face interview.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Weighting ratio computed as (HSGA × 0.30) + (APT × 0.30) + (ACT × 0.40)
#### Constraints
- The cut-off weighting value varies each year with applicant numbers and university capacity

### Target Learners
- applicants to Saudi colleges of medicine and allied health professions

### Learning Goals
- selection of candidates with sufficient intellectual abilities for allied health degrees
