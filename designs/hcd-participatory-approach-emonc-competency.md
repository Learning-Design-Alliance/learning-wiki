# Human-Centred Design as a participatory approach for identifying competency barriers and co-creating solutions in maternal health

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes HCD as "a participatory approach that values empathy, creativity, and collaboration with end-users when designing practical, contextually appropriate interventions." Techniques included journey mapping, systems mapping, ranking exercises, and low-fidelity prototyping, which surfaced hidden barriers such as workflow inefficiencies, cognitive overload, and emotional stress. The approach enabled providers to move beyond identifying challenges to developing solutions enhancing clinical practice and health system responsiveness.
