# Interactive computational modelling workshop activities in pairs with guided protocols and embedded video support

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The course component consisted of interactive and exploratory workshop activities in which student teams of two, one per computer, worked through PDF protocols with embedded video support, in a Moodle-centred collaborative online context. Students analysed and discussed problems on their own using guidelines from lectures and protocols, were continuously accompanied and helped by teachers, and global class discussions were held to keep pace and clarify doubts.
