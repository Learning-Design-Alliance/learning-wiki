# Three-page Google Classroom course structure (Stream, Students, Information) for blended geography courses

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each blended geography course in Google Classroom is organized into three pages with defined content. The "Stream" page holds practical and laboratory classes, independent work, nomenclature and individual tasks, with attached files and video; the "Students" page shows the class code and attendees with e-mail access; the "Information" page provides "the course tasks and objectives, the classroom, the calendar / schedule of classes", curriculum program, literature, maps and atlases, methodical materials and multimedia resources. The Information page contents may vary with the discipline.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The Information page must contain the electronic resources needed for the tasks provided in Stream
#### Constraints
- The contents of the Information page may vary depending on the specifics of the discipline content

### Target Learners
- geography students in higher education

### Learning Goals
- access to course content and multimedia resources
- organization of in-class and out-of-class work
