# Cyclical four-day instructional cycle: introduce forms, receptive practice, expressive practice, weekly writing or editing measure

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Both intervention conditions used a weekly cycle teaching two dialect forms per week: "an introduction to the dialect forms of the day 1, receptive language activities on day 2 to build a foundation for the new knowledge, and expressive language activities on day 3 to practice using the forms". Day 4 provided a writing or sentence-editing task as a weekly measure. Activities included sentence sorts, cloze sentences, sentence completion, sentence generation, editing, puzzles, memory games, and bingo.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Both receptive and expressive tasks for each form
- A weekly day-4 measure of understanding and usage
#### Constraints
- Implemented over a four-week period in 15–20 minute daily sessions

### Target Learners
- Second through fourth grade students who use Non-mainstream American English

### Learning Goals
- Producing School English grammatical forms (copula, plurals, past tense) in writing
