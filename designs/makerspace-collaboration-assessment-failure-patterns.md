# Strategies of fostering collaboration, building relationships, personalized assessment, and encouraging failure

## Description
Beyond scaffolding, the analysis identified four strategy categories: fostering collaboration through organizational and spatial interventions, building relationships through direct student-teacher engagement and a catalytic role, assessing performance through personalized metrics and learner-defined outcomes rather than fixed standards, and encouraging failure by teaching overcoming fear of failure and the importance of failing fast and early through tinkering.
