# Four interconnected learning objectives for engineering data science: Theory, Methods, Applications, Analysis

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The ecosystem organises teaching around four interconnected objectives. The article states: "We integrate the engineering perspective into teaching DS by pursuing four learning objectives." Theory covers understanding data-generating systems; Methods, selecting appropriate tools for each DS stage; Applications, formulating real-world problems so that a solution is feasible; Analysis, interpreting results to provide explanations and identify shortcomings. Fig. 1 depicts the objectives as mutually supporting.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The article holds that "research, teaching, and practice are heavily intertwined in DS", so the objectives are taught by staff active at the forefront of DS research and practice.
#### Constraints
- The objectives are framed for engineering programmes and are stated to be in line with accreditation criteria such as the UK's Accreditation of Higher Education Programmes (AHEP).

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and postgraduate engineering students
- professionals in continuing education

### Learning Goals
- understanding data-generating systems
- selecting methods for DS stages
- formulating real-world DS problems
- interpreting results and identifying methodological shortcomings
