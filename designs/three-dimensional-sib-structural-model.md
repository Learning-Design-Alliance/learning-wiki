# Three-dimensional structural model of social interaction behaviors (goal, interactive, and task structure)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article adopts a three-dimensional structural model for dissecting social interaction behaviors, "delineating it into goal structure (cooperation versus competition), interactive structure (synchronous versus turn-based), and task structure (interdependent versus independent)". The study fixes goal structure at cooperation and interactive structure at synchronous, and varies the task-structure subdimension by contrasting high and low task interdependence within cooperation key-pressing tasks. The model thus provides the design axis along which the experimental tasks were refined.
