# Networked Faculty Learning Community (NFLC)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes the NFLC as a new configuration of collaborative teacher learning, positioned between a community of practice, a Faculty Online Learning Community and a Faculty Learning Community. It is defined as "an inclusive group of people from different educational institutes, motivated by a shared learning vision, who support and work with each other mainly online". Unlike a FOLC it also meets in person, and unlike a Faculty Learning Community it spans institutions, with the intention to inspire and inform others of their vision.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers from different institutes with a shared learning vision and a shared goal, supported by time (here a grant) to work together
#### Constraints
- The article distinguishes the NFLC from FOLC, COP and FLC definitions; the group in this study met both online and in person across three universities

### Target Learners
- higher education teachers of bachelor's science lab courses

### Learning Goals
- designing and implementing open inquiry in lab courses
- sharing and disseminating course materials
