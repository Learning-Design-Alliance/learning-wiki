# Un ciel de galaxies: a real-time planetarium session built on the Cosmic Flows research catalogue

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
'Un ciel de galaxies' is a session offered for more than ten years at the Vaulx-en-Velin planetarium for adults and adolescents, aiming to initiate the public to the cartography and geography of the large-scale Universe. Since 2012 it uses a galaxy catalogue from the Cosmic Flows project of the Cosmologie observationnelle/Euclid group at IPNL: the authors built a file in Cartesian X Y Z coordinates 'répertoriant près de 10 000 galaxies', generating a 100 Mpc point cloud centred on the Milky Way, projected and manipulated as a 3D object with the Digistar 3 planetarium. Visitors orient their position in the virtual space with a mediator and discover gravitation's role in matter distribution, including the fall of the Local Group toward the Virgo cluster and the possible 'grand attracteur'.
