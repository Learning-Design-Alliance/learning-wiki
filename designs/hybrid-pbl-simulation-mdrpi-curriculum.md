# Eight-session hybrid PBL plus scenario-based simulation curriculum for OR MDRPI prevention

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The program combined a PBL module (three 2-hour sessions using OR-specific case triggers, problem listing, evidence search, group solution planning, and facilitator summaries linked to guidelines) with a simulation module (five weekly scenarios, each 40 minutes of scenario execution followed by 20 minutes of structured debriefing following a Description–Analysis–Application framework). Sessions were delivered face-to-face in small groups of approximately 10 nurses, using real OR devices, standardized checklists, and role-play with defined roles including primary nurse, circulating nurse, and observer. The article states the design "integrated: Problem-based learning, to promote clinical reasoning, guideline-based decision-making, and prevention planning for device-related risks; Scenario-based simulation, to support skills transfer, teamwork, and communication in realistic OR contexts."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A multidisciplinary training team (department head nurse, unit head nurses, senior nurses) delivering sessions with standardized materials and prior facilitator calibration
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only in a single tertiary hospital OR setting with a single-group pretest–posttest design; generalizability is limited

### Target Learners
- Operating room nurses

### Learning Goals
- MDRPI risk identification, prevention planning, skill execution under OR constraints, team communication, and documentation
