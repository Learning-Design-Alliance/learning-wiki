# Integrated AI Triad (IAT) model for teacher-AI cooperative instructional design

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The IAT model, developed by Talebzadeh (2025a), is a conceptual framework structuring the cooperative workflow between teachers and generative AI during the design of learning trajectories. The article states it emphasizes "task authenticity and student cognitive outcomes" and uses it as the baseline structure within which the SDP constraint is later imposed. Pre-service teachers received structured training on formulating prompts aligned with the IAT model before drafting lesson plans.
