# Emotional Literacy-Based Reading Comprehension Strategy (ELBRCS): a combined cognitive-affective reading comprehension strategy

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
ELBRCS is a reading comprehension strategy for narrative texts that combines cognitive and affective strategies, conducted orally and in written form. Cognitive aspects are stimulated by facilitator-guided questions aimed at deep comprehension, while affective aspects are generated by encouraging students to focus on the beauty of plots and share feelings triggered by the text. The article grounds it in Constructivism, stressing students as "meaning builder", and in EQ Theory linking emotion to cognitive meaning making.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A facilitator who asks guided questions and guides students until they can use the strategy on their own
- A safe discussion environment with teachers acting as facilitators
#### Constraints
- Studied with Form Two secondary students reading narrative texts in Malay language as first language in Malaysia

### Target Learners
- Form Two secondary school students (average age 14)

### Learning Goals
- Reading comprehension mastery from literal to inferential, evaluative and appreciative levels
- Emotional literacy and affective response to narrative texts
