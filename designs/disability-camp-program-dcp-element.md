# Disability Camp Program (DCP) based on Paralympic School Day principles

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The DCP is a ten-activity program (theoretical-practical) delivered over 20 consecutive days in a summer sport and leisure camp, based on Paralympic School Day principles. Activities included Human Rights, Information about Paralympic Games, Boccia, Classification, Wheelchair Rugby, Athletics, Accessibility games, Swimming, Wheelchair Basketball, and Drawing. Each activity lasted 35 minutes with children rotating through all ten.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Children enrolled by themselves in activities; program needs to be planned
#### Constraints
- Some activities proved too difficult for children aged 11 years old

### Target Learners
- campers without disabilities (M = 11.25 years old)

### Learning Goals
- awareness and understanding of children with physical disabilities
