# Small-group station rotation for Tier 2 reading intervention

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The program organizes intervention as rotation of small groups of four students through instructional stations. In RISE, "groups of four students rotate through four instruction stations for 45-60 minutes per day for six to eight weeks"; RISE Up uses three stations focused on comprehension strategies on a short text for 45 minutes a day. The rotation distributes explicit, structured instruction across comprehension, word study, phonics, and writing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Groups of four students
- 45-60 minutes per day
- Six to eight weeks of implementation
- Teacher(s) or interventionists staffing the stations
#### Constraints
- RISE coaches provided guidance to modify the program for a 30-minute day timeframe with one instructor to meet the district's intervention model

### Target Learners
- Students in grades 1-5 reading below grade level (Tier 2)

### Learning Goals
- Reading comprehension
- word-solving
- fluency
- writing
