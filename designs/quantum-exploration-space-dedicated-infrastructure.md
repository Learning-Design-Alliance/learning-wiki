# Quantum Exploration Space: dedicated educational lab infrastructure reused across programs

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A reconfigurable educational space at the Institute for Quantum Computing housing dedicated teaching equipment, including NMR spectrometers, NV apparatuses, entangled photon sources, and liquid helium flow cryostats. The authors report that as the program evolved, "we have been able to develop dedicated infrastructure strictly for educational purposes" housed in this environment, allowing lectures and labs in one location and parallel lab setups. The infrastructure is reused for a Master's program, undergraduate lab courses, high-school programs, and teacher workshops.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dedicated purpose-built space and equipment, plus technical staff to build, maintain, and facilitate the labs
#### Constraints
- Early iterations used spaces otherwise used for research, which the article implies limited the program's evolution
