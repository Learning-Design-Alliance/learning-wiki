# Three-pillar partnership design: community and belonging, research opportunities, and quantum community connections

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Center's design rests on three key pillars that align to AIP TEAM-UP's recommendations: 1) building community and fostering a sense of belonging, 2) strengthening relationships internally and with the broader quantum community, and 3) providing funding to support undergraduate, graduate, and faculty research at HBCUs. The design was "created collaboratively with HBCU faculty and IBM Quantum, along with discussions from national labs and other funding agencies," and each pillar has stated measurable ways of being met.
