# Researcher-developed 12-session REBT treatment package for academic procrastination

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A manualized REBT treatment package created by the researchers "to assist in-school adolescents with academic procrastination." It contains "detailed information about the 6 weeks of intensive treatment, including timeline, procedures, objectives, activities, duration, and evaluation processes," delivered as 40-minute sessions twice weekly for 6 weeks (12 sessions total). Sessions progress from familiarization and identifying procrastination factors, through identifying and disputing irrational beliefs and self-talk, to termination. Techniques include cognitive restructuring, rational emotive imagery, role playing, humor, and direct teaching.

## Design Implications

#### Requirements

#### Constraints
- Developed and evaluated specifically with secondary-school adolescents in Udenu Education Zone, Nigeria

### Target Learners
- in-school adolescents in secondary school

### Learning Goals
- reducing academic procrastination
- disputing irrational beliefs about academic tasks
