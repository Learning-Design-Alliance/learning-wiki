# Chapter pedagogical flow: warm-up dialogue eliciting student ideas, text, short interactive task, and activity with real data

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each section of the curriculum follows a repeating four-part flow shown in the article's Figure 1: "Warm-up Dialog: elicit student idea, Text: including Figures and visualizations, Short Interactive Task, Activity with real data." The warm-up dialogues are fictional exchanges between archetypal students using student-derived language gleaned from research, and students choose the speaker they most agree with and explain why. Responses are recorded so that students and the instructor have a running record of how their thinking evolves over time, and chapters close with a Wrapping It Up dataset activity and a graded Mission Report.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A computer learning system that records student responses and gives automatic feedback about the acceptability of answers
#### Constraints
- Dialogues address commonly held misconceptions only when appropriate

### Target Learners
- general-education (GE) undergraduate introductory astronomy students

### Learning Goals
- addressing preexisting concepts and misconceptions while learning cosmological content
