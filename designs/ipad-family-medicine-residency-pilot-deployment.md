# Eight-month iPad deployment pilot with pre-loaded medical apps, data plans and training workshops in a family medicine residency

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A technology-deployment pilot in which 25 participants (20 preceptors, 5 residents) at Pembroke Regional Hospital received 3G-enabled iPads with data plans and pre-loaded medical and general apps such as drug references and calculators. Support included "A half-day educational workshop" on the tablet, installed apps and the Triple C curricular framework, plus two peer-led workshops and periodic coaching meetings over six months. Experience was evaluated at the beginning, at four months and at the end of the study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sufficient evidence from pilot studies to guide adaptation of resources and infrastructure to the needs of teachers and learners
#### Constraints
- Small sample size and recruitment from a single institution in a rural community setting; inability to access EMRs through the iPad

### Target Learners
- Family medicine residents
- Family medicine and specialist preceptors

### Learning Goals
- Accessing the Triple C competency-based curriculum and assessment resources
- Clinical teaching and learning at the point of care
