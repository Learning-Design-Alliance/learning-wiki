# Validated 12-session maths training programme and 30-item achievement test

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The researcher prepared a special research plan in Maths using the established method consolidating process training and task analysis; the programme was made up of 12 training sessions with varied activities taking care of individual differences, teaching methods, technology and computed values. It was validated by specialised arbitrator members at universities. The achievement test covered the statistics and probability unit in 30 paragraphs categorised by Bloom's Knowledge Taxonomy, with a Koder Richardson (K-R20) of 0.81 and a test-retest R of 0.84.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Validation by arbitrator members of the training council at universities, whose comments and suggestions were applied
#### Constraints
- Difficulty factors ranged from 0.37 - 0.74 and differentiation levels from 0.35 - 0.71 as printed in Table 2

### Target Learners
- female students with learning disorders at first High School level in KSA

### Learning Goals
- achievement in the statistics and probability unit of maths
