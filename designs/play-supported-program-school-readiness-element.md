# Play Supported Program for Primary School Readiness of 60-72 Month-Old Disadvantaged Children

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A researcher-developed education program designed "to increase primary school readiness levels of children" in institutional care. Goals and objectives were drawn from the 2006 preschool education program for 36-72-month-olds across cognitive and language, socio-emotional, physical development and self-care skills, with developmentally appropriate activities prepared for each area. It ran for eight months on school days between September and May as three play activities per day, and its draft was analyzed and altered by 5 experts before being given final shape.
