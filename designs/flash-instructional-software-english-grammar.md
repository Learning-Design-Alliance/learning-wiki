# Flash-based instructional software program for teaching English grammar items

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The researcher developed a computer software instructional program in Flash version to teach grammar items including "Lexis formation, Comprehension, Sentence construction and Transformation of sentence". The program "was provided with explanations and ample examples" and was constructed so that "the student can receive feedback for his achievement and he can get his scores when he finishes any exercise". English language specialists' suggestions were incorporated before administration, and by this means its reliability was confirmed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Constructed with the help of programmers and suggestions of English language specialists carried out before its administration
#### Constraints
- Restricted to grammar items like Lexis formation, Comprehension, Construction of sentences and Transformation of sentences

### Target Learners

### Learning Goals
- English grammar: lexis formation, comprehension, sentence construction, transformation of sentences
