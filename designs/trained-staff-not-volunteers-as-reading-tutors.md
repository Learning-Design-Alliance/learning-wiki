# Staff one-to-one tutoring with trained school staff rather than community volunteers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The program specifies that "Classroom teachers, reading teachers, or well-prepared paraprofessionals are needed to serve as tutors," while "Community volunteers are not suggested as tutors." Tutoring quality is treated as depending on staff who receive extensive training, observation, and feedback, so schools implementing one-to-one tutoring should assign trained staff rather than volunteers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to classroom teachers, reading teachers, or well-prepared paraprofessionals to serve as tutors
#### Constraints
- Community volunteers are not suggested as tutors
