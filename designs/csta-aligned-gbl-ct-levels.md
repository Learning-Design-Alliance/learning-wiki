# CSTA-aligned game levels that embed CT facets (decomposition, abstraction, debugging) in crafting and modeling tasks

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Each Factory Craft game level was designed by aligning game objectives, learning objectives, CSTA standards, and CT facets. For example, Lesson 2 asks students to "build various models of contraptions to produce objects and explore ways to build objects more efficiently with minimal waste," requiring data collection, analysis, pattern recognition, and modeling that reflect abstraction. The MFP lets students drag and drop inputs and outputs to construct contraption models used in Minecraft.
