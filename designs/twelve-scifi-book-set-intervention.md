# Set of 12 science-fiction and genre-alternating books used in the experimental intervention

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The intervention material is a set of 12 books read by the experimental group: 9 science-fiction children's books plus 3 non-science-fiction books alternating the genre (Catalhoyuk Stories Series). Books were selected by five criteria including being written by a well-known author, being scientific, being appropriate to the age-group level, being funny and informative, and being recommended by experts in children's literature. Titles include From the Earth to the Moon, Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Sea, Journey to the Center of the Earth, Time Bike, Cipher of Genes, Aliens Are Coming, and Journey to Mars at the Speed of Light.
