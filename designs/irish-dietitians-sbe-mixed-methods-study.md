# National survey and interview study of Irish dietitians' knowledge, attitudes and use of SBE

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
A convergent mixed-methods study combining an anonymous online SurveyMonkey questionnaire (adapted from previously validated Knowledge–Attitudes–Practice instruments, piloted with three dietitians) with 15 semi-structured Zoom interviews analysed thematically in NVivo. The survey captured "participant characteristics, knowledge and familiarity with SBE, attitudes toward SBE, and current use, barriers and facilitators". Code saturation was reached by interview eight, with all 15 completed to support meaning saturation.

#### Requirements

#### Constraints
