# Structural-functional model of managing teacher scientific-methodical work with five components

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article presents a structural-functional model of management of teacher scientific-methodical work grounded in project-target, systemic, activity-based and axiological approaches. As printed, "They are target -based, methodological, content, organizational and procedural, evaluation -resultative (Figure 1)". The target component aims at systematic improvement of teachers' scientific-methodical competence; the content component covers planning, motivation building, organization and control; the evaluation-resultative component has motivation-axiological, cognitive-activity and professionally-reflexive blocks.
