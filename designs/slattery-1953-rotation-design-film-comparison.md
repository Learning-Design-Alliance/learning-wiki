# Rotation-design comparison of sound motion pictures and silent filmstrips in fifth-grade social studies

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
An experiment in which "The experimental population of 422 fifth-grade pupils from nine elementary schools of Rochester, New York, was set up in six divisions according to a rotation design." Divisions were equated on IQ, mental age, and initial test scores, and the rotation involved four sound motion pictures and four silent filmstrips, with participation consisting of reading verbal material aloud and answering script questions.
