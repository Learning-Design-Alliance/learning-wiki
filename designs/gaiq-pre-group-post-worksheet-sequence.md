# GAIQ pre-discussion, group-discussion, post-discussion activity sequence

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The GAIQ unfolds in three stages: an individual pre-stage, a group lesson stage, and an individual post stage. Per Table 1, in the pre stage students "Individually, ... solve target problem ... and answer questions in pre-discussion worksheet"; in the lesson stage "In groups of 3, students answer the same questions in group worksheets then a whole class discussion takes place"; and in the post stage students individually answer the same questions again, with the opportunity to modify previous answers and connect their features to a pre-defined list.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Respondents complete individual worksheets before and after the group discussion
- Group discussion in small groups followed by whole-class sharing
- A pre-defined feature list available at the post stage
