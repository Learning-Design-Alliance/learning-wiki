# Brain teaser game curriculum delivered three days a week for one hour over ten weeks

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's intervention pattern is a structured play-based curriculum: the experiment group "was given the brain teaser curriculum 3 times a week for an hour each time, for 10 weeks". The curriculum comprised named strategy and construction games such as Quoridor, Katamino, Make'n Break, ColourCode, Tangram, Mangala, Jenga and Kapla, implemented by classroom teachers after researcher briefing, with reinforcing statements such as "good job" given to children during activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers briefed about the implementation of activities, with the first activity implemented under researcher supervision
- Reinforcing statements provided to children during activities
#### Constraints
- Tested with six-year-old pre-school children in one kindergarten; no implementations were made on children in the control group

### Target Learners
- pre-school children of age six

### Learning Goals
- attention span and concentration
