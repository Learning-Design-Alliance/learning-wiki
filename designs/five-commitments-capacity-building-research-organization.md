# Five organizational commitments for research that builds district capacity

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
CCSR's three themes for capacity-building research are institutionalized in five commitments that organize how the organization conducts and communicates research: 'developing an extensive data archive on CPs, (2) extensive stakeholder engagement and strong ongoing relationships with the district, (3) conducting scientifically rigorous research while making findings broadly accessible, (4) building knowledge of core problems across time and across studies, and (5) an extensive outreach of providing information to the public.' The pattern treats the how of research—interaction, organization, dissemination—as capacity-building mechanisms in their own right.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An ongoing data-sharing agreement with the district
- Governance structures (steering committee) that institutionalize stakeholder consultation
- High internal standards of quality and review
#### Constraints
- The report notes technical quality can interfere with accessibility, requiring deliberate translation of rigorous analysis into public reports

### Target Learners
- Practitioners, principals, district leaders, and the broader civic reform community in a single urban district

### Learning Goals
- Making research findings usable for school improvement planning and policy dialogue
