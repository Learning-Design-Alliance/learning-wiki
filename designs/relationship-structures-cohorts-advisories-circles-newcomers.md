# Build trusting relationships through stable cohorts, advisories, restorative circles, and peer buddy structures

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The report identifies relationships as the foundation of the school's learning culture, supported by concrete structures: each new student is paired with a buddy from a similar linguistic and cultural background, students move through classes in heterogeneously selected cohorts of about 25, daily advisory periods build social-emotional learning, and restorative justice circles run on a consistent schedule. The report states that "key structures and practices such as peer partnerships and cohorts, student advisories, restorative justice circles, and community walks have earned the school a reputation as 'a safe and caring place.'"
