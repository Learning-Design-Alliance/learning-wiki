# The quantum crucible framework: encoding unfavorable game states in superposition, manipulating them via quantum gates, and measuring to reshape outcome probabilities

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The quantum crucible is a three-stage framework for making quantum physics playable. When a game presents an unfavorable outcome, “favorable and unfavorable possibilities are encoded in a quantum superposition,” the player manipulates the superposition through quantum gate operations (the Gate of Fate), and measurement produces a definitive outcome according to the resulting probabilities. The framework comprises two components: the quantumstance, which establishes the superposition, and the GOF, through which the player manipulates it.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A game situation that can be represented as a superposition of favorable and unfavorable alternatives, with a measurement step that resolves the outcome
#### Constraints
- A skillful sequence of operations can increase the likelihood of a favorable result without necessarily guaranteeing it

### Target Learners
- beginners learning quantum physics
- video game players

### Learning Goals
- quantum intuition about superposition, unitary evolution, quantum gates, and measurement
