# PBLE framing: matching problem types and cognitive scaffolds to learners' needs

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article frames issues uncovered by RPBL in terms of a Problem-based learning environment (PBLE) following Jonassen (2011), in which "There are components and cognitive scaffolds to be matched to learners' needs in solving different kinds of problems". In Engineering Dynamics the author identifies troubleshooting problems, where students verify the correctness or plausibility of a proposed solution, and decision-making problems, where a solution is known to be possible via alternate routes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor assessment of students' solutions to place pupils on their developmental trajectories
#### Constraints
- Differentiated learning apparently contradicts leveraging, a limitation the author says future work should reconcile with teamwork in PBL environments

### Target Learners
- sophomore engineering students solving varied Dynamics problem types

### Learning Goals
- aligning learning objectives with desired outcomes
- developing problem-solving across problem types
