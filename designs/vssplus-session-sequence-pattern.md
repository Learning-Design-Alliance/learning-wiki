# VSSPlus session sequence: word selection with three questions followed by technology-mediated word-study artifacts

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's training sessions follow a repeated design: teams read a text, select an unknown word, ask and answer three questions (where the word is found, what it means, why it is important to learn it), then produce a technology artifact — an e-Dictionary page with audio recording, a Wordsift visual linking synonyms, or a Thinglink image with tagged definitions and a podcast — and present it to other teams. The article describes sessions in which "each team to co-operate together to select from the previously post-reading text another word" and present it via PPT for discussion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Small teams of about 4 students with exchangeable roles
- Reading texts containing unknown vocabulary appropriate to learners' level
- Access to technological tools (wikis, PPT, audio recording, Wordsift, Thinglink, Podbean)
#### Constraints
- Validated in the article for first-level university EFL students in a Vocabulary Building course

### Target Learners
- first-level university EFL students

### Learning Goals
- selecting, defining and using new vocabulary in correct sentences
