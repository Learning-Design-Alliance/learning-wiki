# Multi‐station OSCE design with dual raters, 4‐point rubrics and a piloted rating scale

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The OSCE design used multiple stations combining nursing procedures and written interpretation: 'For the midterm, seven OSCE stations were set, five for nursing procedures and two for written interpretation, each with an equivalency of 10% of students' midterm grade'. The final used ten stations weighted at 20% of the grade. Rubrics used a 4‐point Likert‐type scale, two trained raters scored each station, and a pilot test preceded the examinations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained raters and a piloted rating scale appropriate for assessing the students
- Standardized patients and station equipment set up according to the blueprint
#### Constraints
- The procedural rubrics were adopted from course‐appropriate reference books but were not tested for reliability, which the authors list as a limitation

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students (BSN)

### Learning Goals
- clinical competence in nursing procedures
- communication and patient‐teaching skills under simulated clinical conditions
