# One-semester learning community programs with four components: linked courses and cohorts, faculty collaboration, integrated instructional practices, and student supports

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The intervention evaluated across the six trials was a one-semester learning community built from four components, with variation in emphasis and implementation across sites. The components are linked courses and student cohorts (groups of students enrolling together in two or more courses, often scheduled back-to-back), faculty collaboration (instructors of linked courses planning and running classes together), instructional practices emphasizing integrated instruction and active and collaborative learning such as group work and hands-on assignments, and student supports such as tutors, designated counselors, or supplemental instruction. At Kingsborough some learning communities did not include developmental education classes, while at the other colleges all did.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors of the linked courses collaborated to plan and run their classes
- Teaching methods focused on integrated instruction, emphasizing the interconnections between the linked courses
#### Constraints
- There was variation in the emphasis and implementation of the four components across programs

### Target Learners
- community college students in developmental English or math

### Learning Goals
- credits earned in the targeted subject
- persistence
- degree completion
