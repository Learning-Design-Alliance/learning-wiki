# 16-week outdoor education program for pre-service classroom teachers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A semester-long (16 weeks) environmental education implementation in which pre-service classroom teachers worked in 12 small groups on assigned environmental issues, formulated research questions, searched scientific resources, and shared findings with the lecturer acting as moderator. The program combined indoor active learning with "activities suitable for th e outdoor education model", including field trips to a Bald Ibis reproduction station and Old Halfeti, brainstorming discussions after trips, school visits, and learning "how to plant trees, observe birds, and walk in the open air".
