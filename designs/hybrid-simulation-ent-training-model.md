# Hybrid lecture-plus-simulation training model with objective standardized assessment for ENT emergencies

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's training model combines a 1-hour lecture-based teaching program with PowerPoint, video demonstration and scenario-specific hands-on mannequin training, followed by objective checklist-based assessment in a simulated environment. The authors state that "Simulation training followed by objective assessment improves retention and application" and that the simulation-based hybrid approach improves cognitive, psychomotor skills and confidence. Assessment used a validated questionnaire and expert-validated checklists with three assessors to minimize bias.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A validated questionnaire and checklist for the trained scenarios
- Hands-on simulation training on mannequins for the assigned scenario
- Assessment in a simulated environment under supervision to standardize scoring
#### Constraints
- Assessment was completed within the 15-day posting window; repeat assessment after three months was not possible because participants had postings in other departments
- Participants' prior exposure to scenarios during postings could have biased assessment

### Target Learners
- Medical interns in rotatory ENT postings with no prior exposure to ENT emergencies

### Learning Goals
- Knowledge, confidence and psychomotor skills in managing ENT emergencies and procedures (tracheostomy care, epistaxis management, nasogastric tube insertion)
