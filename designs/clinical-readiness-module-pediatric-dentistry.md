# Clinical Readiness Module: a five-week simulation-based preclinical preparation program for pediatric dentistry

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A five-instructional-week program (delivered across eight calendar weeks) preparing third-year dental students for their pediatric dentistry rotation. Per the article, "The Clinical Readiness Module incorporated multiple learner-centered educational strategies, including simulation-based skill practice on dental models, role-play with standardized patients, clinical scenario analysis, small-group discussions, and instructor-led reflective debriefing". Weeks were structured as pretest OSCE, thematic training sessions, and posttest OSCE, with educational materials distributed two weeks in advance via Google Drive and a WhatsApp group.
