# Teacher-made intercultural evaluation sheet with Likert-scale indicators validated by expert review and Cronbach's alpha

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A teacher-made evaluation sheet analysed the language, functions and cultural dimensions of the materials in phase 1. It "included three mai n dimensions, subdivided into a set of indicators, measured on a Likert scale, rang ing from 1 to 5 points, with 1 being totally disagree and 5 totally agree". Indicators covered intercultural awareness (body language, customs, timetable, gestures) and diversity understanding. Validity was checked by two experts in education and cultural studies; reliability testing "concluded with a 0.75 alp ha, that is 0.15 points above the 0.6 standard".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Expert review of construct validity
- Cronbach's alpha check of internal consistency
#### Constraints
- Used for in-class phase 1 material analysis, not for scoring the phase 2 SL versus class comparison, which used a 1-10 grading scale
