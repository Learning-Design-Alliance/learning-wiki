# Progressive puzzle difficulty aligned to flow to sustain engagement in educational escape rooms

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The escape room was designed to start with simpler puzzles and gradually increase in complexity, aligning with the flow concept in game theory. The article explains that "overly simple puzzles may lead to disengagement, while overly complex puzzles may cause frustration and fatigue"; progressively increasing difficulty maintained participant motivation and engagement throughout the experience. Puzzles served as interactive mechanisms for accessing research data rather than as standalone educational tools.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Puzzle difficulty calibrated through expert feedback and pilot trials to complement educational goals
#### Constraints
- The subjective experience of challenge may vary among individuals, an area the authors say warrants continuous refinement

### Target Learners
- health professions students in team-based games

### Learning Goals
- sustained engagement and motivation during competency assessment
