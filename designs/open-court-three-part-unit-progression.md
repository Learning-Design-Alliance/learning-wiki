# Three-part unit progression: Preparing to Read, Reading and Responding, Language Arts

## Description
Each unit of Open Court Reading© is organized into three parts in a logical progression. Part One, Preparing to Read, addresses phonemic awareness, sounds and letters, phonics, fluency, and word knowledge; Part Two, Reading and Responding, emphasizes reading literature for understanding, comprehension, inquiry, and practical reading applications; Part Three, Language Arts, focuses on writing, spelling, grammar, usage, mechanics, and basic computer skills. The pattern sequences skills instruction before and alongside literature-based application and written language work.
