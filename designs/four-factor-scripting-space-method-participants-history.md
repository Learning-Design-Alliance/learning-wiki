# Four-factor scripting approach for blended learning: space, pedagogical method, participants, and history

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The final article in the issue, by Mar Pérez-Sanagustín, Patricia Santos, Davinia Hernández-Leo, and Josep Blat, proposes and illustrates a scripting approach the editors describe as "focused on four factors: the space, the pedagogical method, the participants, and the history." The emphasis on space relates to a blended learning geography course that uses mobile and other technologies to support teams of students exploring the urban environment in Barcelona.
