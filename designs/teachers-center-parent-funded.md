# Teachers Center (TC): a parent-funded company of experts-in-demand supporting teacher advanced study

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Teachers Center is a special company formed by experts-in-demand, to which tuition accumulated for teachers by parents is paid directly. The author likens a TC to "a medical research group formed by all kinds of experts who unite in effort of helping doctors improve their services to patients." The estimated model TC serves 100 teachers across 50 schools, collects about 1.8 million units a year from parents, may reach 3-5 million with matching funds and donations, employs 18 permanent staff, and serves 15,000 students a year. Experts work on-site or nearby so teachers stay in their schools.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The article states the sustained inflow requires "the inflow proceeds along a clear path from clients to the providers," and that "the transparency of the resource flow must be constantly protected" to prevent bureaucracy and profit seekers from becoming a burden.
- A TC must have "a clear, overarching goal"; its central task is to "build, use, improve, and share the concepts of productive learning and productive teaching."
#### Constraints
- The cost figures are order-of-magnitude estimates assuming Polish averages; the author states "In other countries, and for specific schools and areas that depart from the average values used in the estimate, some rescaling will be needed."

### Target Learners
- teachers in high schools
- experts in academic disciplines

### Learning Goals
- advanced studies and deliberate practice for teachers
- improvement of teaching quality
