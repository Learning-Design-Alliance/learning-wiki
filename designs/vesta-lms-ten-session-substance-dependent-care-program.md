# Vesta LMS e-learning program: ten sessions on clinical management of substance-dependent pregnant women

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention is a four-week e-learning program delivered through Vesta, a learning management system belonging to Shiraz University of Medical Sciences. Content covers "the effects of six types of substances" and labor, postpartum, breastfeeding, and neonatal abstinence syndrome care across ten sessions in Persian, generated under content production standards and nine implementation stages, with pictures, diagrams, clinical videos, patient scenarios, and quizzes. Sessions were released every three days, and gated self-assessment tests of 3–6 questions had to be passed (with two permitted attempts) to progress.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a computer and internet and minimum computer skills to enter the LMS
- Passing each session's self-assessment test to participate in the next session
#### Constraints
- Evaluated for midwives in governmental maternity hospitals with 4 weeks of training and a one-month follow-up

### Target Learners
- Working midwives in governmental maternity hospitals

### Learning Goals
- Knowledge and clinical skill performance in caring for substance-dependent pregnant women during labor and post-partum
