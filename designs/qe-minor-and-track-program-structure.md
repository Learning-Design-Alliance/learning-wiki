# Quantum engineering minor plus quantum track within engineering majors as the program structure for quantum-proficient engineers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes that a complete undergraduate quantum engineering program be built as a minor "accessible to all STEM majors" and a "quantum track directly integrated into individual engineering majors", and that "such programs typically require only three or four newly developed courses" complementing existing classes. This keeps students' broader engineering degree intact while adding quantum specialization, fitting the article's T-shaped engineer goal.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

### Target Learners
- undergraduate engineering majors
- STEM majors at four-year colleges and research universities

### Learning Goals
