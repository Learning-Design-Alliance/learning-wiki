# AI-driven talent cultivation reform framework with five interconnected components

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The article proposes a comprehensive reform framework for computer-related majors grounded in the new engineering philosophy, integrating AI across curriculum, pedagogy, and practical training. As printed, "The framework is structured around five core components: curriculum restructuring, smart teaching integration, interdisciplinary and collaborative talent cultivation, practice-based learning enhancement, and multi-dimensional evaluation." The components form an interconnected system intended to deliver AI-empowered talent cultivation with academic depth and industrial relevance, and the framework was phased in across four undergraduate programs as a replicable model.
