# Take-home artifact pattern: planispheres kept by students, pamphlets left behind

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The program's planisphere activity has students build a two-disc take-home artifact: the first disc shows Western names of celestial objects for finding stars, directions, and time; the second disc shows Boorong names with familiar constellations (Southern Cross, Scorpius, Orion) as half-tone references, permitting students to draw observational correlations between what they already know and what they are learning. Evaluation showed students valued the planispheres and kept these, while the accompanying pamphlet was not ideal as most were left behind, so it was discontinued in favour of a webpage.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Guides demonstrate how to use the completed planisphere and its connection to seasonal cycles using Stellarium
#### Constraints
- Accompanying printed pamphlets were mostly left behind by students and were discontinued

### Target Learners
- Primary school students with no prior Aboriginal astronomy exposure

### Learning Goals
- Learning Boorong names and stories of celestial objects
- Using stars to tell time and find direction
