# Semester research arc: proposal, weekly mentored work, notebook documentation, and public seminar presentation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The FP course models the structure of scientific research within one semester: groups write a proposal outlining the problem, motivation, literature review, materials, budget, and timeline; implement the project including designing and building an experiment, calculations, and data analysis; and finish with a departmental seminar. The article states the course structure is designed to model several aspects of scientific research, "from proposing a project, to carrying out the elements of the proposal, to presenting the results to a wider audience."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A faculty mentor for each small group
- A scheduled weekly two-hour meeting with the mentor plus work between meetings
- Groups have generally been required to keep a scientific notebook
#### Constraints
- Final reporting formats have varied over time, though the oral seminar presentation has always been an important element

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate physics majors

### Learning Goals
- scientific communication
- research methods
- teamwork and project management
