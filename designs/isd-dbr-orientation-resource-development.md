# Instructional systems design (ISD) with design-based research (DBR) for orientation resource development

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article applies an instructional systems design (ISD) model, used in a non-linear fashion across analysis, design, development, and evaluation phases, aligned with a design-based research (DBR) approach advocating collaboration between researchers and practitioners in real-world settings. The authors state the process "advocates for the collaboration between researchers and practitioners in real-world settings, to create contextually-sensitive outcomes and resources". This framework structured the project's four phases of data collection and resource refinement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Phased, iterative development with data collection at each stage, feeding outcomes into subsequent phases.
#### Constraints
- The ISD model was applied non-linearly, reflecting the flexible, context-sensitive demands of the project.

### Target Learners
- University students in online and flexible study modes
