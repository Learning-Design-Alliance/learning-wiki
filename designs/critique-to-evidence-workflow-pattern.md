# Critique-to-evidence workflow: snapshot, independent coding, reconciliation, export

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This pattern connects an evolving design artefact, its critique, an action plan and an inspectable research record. As the report states, "The intended research workflow is to preserve a machine-evidence snapshot, obtain independent human codes, reconcile disagreements and export the reviewed record." Machine-generated cognitive and architectural labels are treated as provisional and are checked against independent human coding before any analytic use, retaining the educator's interpretive role.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

- Independent human coders and a reconciliation step before export
