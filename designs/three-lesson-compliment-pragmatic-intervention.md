# Three-lesson explicit pragmatic intervention sequence on compliment sequences

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention consisted of three classroom lessons of approximately 15 minutes each, totaling 45 minutes across a semester. The first lesson gave an explicit explanation of compliment sequences followed by practice exercises; the second used video clips from the Spanish-speaking world for learners to identify compliments, responses, speaker relationships, and purposes; the third was a condensed, video-supported modification of CARLA's lesson on compliment sequences.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Video clips providing authentic context for compliment sequences
- Explicit explanation of compliment norms in Spanish followed by examples
#### Constraints
- The intervention lasted half of a semester, which the authors list as a limitation

### Target Learners
- heritage learners of Spanish
- beginner L2 Spanish learners

### Learning Goals
- recognizing and responding appropriately to Spanish compliment sequences
