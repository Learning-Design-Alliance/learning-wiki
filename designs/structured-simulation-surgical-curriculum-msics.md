# Structured simulation-based surgical curriculum combining self-study, classroom instruction, dry-lab, simulator practice and live surgery

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The experimental curriculum is a structured, reproducible training program for scleral tunnel construction that sequences self-study using an eBook, instructor-led classroom instruction, hands-on dry-lab activities, training with the HelpMeSee Eye Surgery Simulator, and a live surgery phase. It spanned 6 days (35 h total), of which 80% of the time was dedicated to hands-on training on the simulator. During live surgery, a HelpMeSee surgeon was physically present in the operating room solely as an observer for the first 20 procedures.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trainees allowed time off from clinical responsibilities to attend the simulation-based training phase
- Instructor assessment of competence in individual tasks before progression
#### Constraints
- The article states the curriculum was evaluated in three highly specialized tertiary eye care centres, so the observed effect is not necessarily applicable to trainees across all training institutions

### Target Learners
- novice ophthalmology residents within 3 months of matriculation

### Learning Goals
- safe and efficient scleral tunnel construction in MSICS
