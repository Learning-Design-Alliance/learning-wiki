# Dative alternation training task presented as a simple structural change

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The learning target and task of the experiment: the English dative alternation, an abstract generalization that learners tend to overgeneralize. All subjects received training on the alternation before the feedback manipulation; the abstract states that "All subjects were trained on the alternation, which was presented in terms of a simple structural change." The task served to elicit errors on which the different feedback types were then delivered.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training on the alternation precedes the feedback manipulation for all subjects
#### Constraints
- The structure was presented as a simple structural change rather than in full abstract complexity

### Target Learners
- Adult Spanish-speaking learners of English as a second language

### Learning Goals
- Acquisition of the English dative alternation and its abstract constraints
