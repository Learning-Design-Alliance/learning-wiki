# Four-phase action research cycle for developing mobile medical education: needs assessment, application design, training, and evaluation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study used "an action research approach, divided into four phases: needs assessment, application design, training, and evaluation". Needs assessment gathered faculty and student nominations of diseases; design included content development, expert validation and three-step application design (server, application, management panel); training involved video production, a Telegram learning group and app installation; evaluation combined exam scores and a student questionnaire. The authors frame this as integrating research with the educational situation to improve the education process.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- participation of teaching faculty, instructional specialists, a filming team, and a software development team
- expert validation of recorded content before final production
#### Constraints
- the reported cycle produced content for only 15 diseases given "financial and time constraints"

### Target Learners
- medical interns and trainees

### Learning Goals
- common disease education suitable for virtual and mobile teaching
