# Transfer-first governance framework bounding non-neurosurgeon temporising cranial intervention

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes a conceptual framework (Table 4) mapping each included pathology to a time-criticality level, an intended role for the non-neurosurgeon, minimal prerequisites, and a transfer principle. EDH with deterioration maps to time-critical temporising decompression only when transfer is infeasible; acute SDH is a rare "in extremis" exception; intraparenchymal hemorrhage is primarily educational exposure. The framework's core is that "NEUROSAVE is best understood as a structured educational intervention that prepares non-neurosurgeons to execute strictly bounded temporising steps within an explicit transfer-first system, not to expand the scope of independent operative practice."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Predefined indications, neurosurgical consultation, and audit; checklist, defined instrumentation, supervision plan
#### Constraints
- Any real-world application of ICH exposure should remain highly restricted and protocol-defined; implementation should remain protocol-bound, transfer-first, and embedded within defined consultation and audit processes

### Target Learners
- general and pediatric surgeons in regional hospitals without on-site neurosurgical coverage

### Learning Goals
- executing strictly bounded temporising cranial steps as a bridge to definitive specialist care
