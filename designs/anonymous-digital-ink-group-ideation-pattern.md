# Anonymously displayed digital-ink responses structure multi-step group ideation with critical commentary and a student-constructed metric

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In an upper-level course nurturing creativity in undergraduate and graduate physics students, InkSurvey structures group idea generation in three steps: simultaneous anonymous solicitation of unrestrained ideas and questions; simultaneous solicitation of positive and negative critical comments on the organized ideas; and construction and submission of a metric to choose the final group solution. Displayed responses are "blind to the audience (but not the instructor)", which the authors say addresses evaluation apprehension and encourages participation regardless of gender or race, while instructor-visible identifiers keep students accountable.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students and instructor must all be equipped with pen-enabled devices
- The facilitator must sort ideas and questions into categories in real time
#### Constraints
- Anonymity applies to peers, not the instructor, who retains student identifiers for accountability

### Target Learners
- upper-level undergraduate and graduate physics students

### Learning Goals
- creative idea generation
- metacognition about what students do and do not understand
- critical evaluation of peers' ideas
