# Modern English drama texts (Death of a Salesman and Who's Afraid of Virginia Woolf) as instructional materials

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The treatment materials were two modern English drama texts, Arthur Miller's Death of a Salesman and Albee's Who is Afraid of Virginia Woolf, taught over ten weeks to 18 treatment students. The texts "contain so many short phrases, colloquial words, English idioms, slangs and daily short dialogues which can motivate students to use them in their daily conversations". Students used familiar phrases learned from the texts in short sentences during treatment.

## Design Implications
