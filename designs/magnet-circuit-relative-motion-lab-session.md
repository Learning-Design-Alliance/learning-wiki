# Pre-instruction laboratory session on magnet–circuit relative motion described in the paper

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper describes a laboratory session, held before any electromagnetic induction discourse but after the special relativity lessons, in which students in couples experiment freely with the relative motion of a magnet and a rigid filiform circuit. After about an hour of experimenting, students describe what they saw with a formula, first spontaneously in the magnet reference frame, then in the frame of the moving coil, and the instructor suggests guessing a formula obeying the locality principle.
