# IMB-based e-learning design pattern: cartoons, videos, self-check lists and interactive quizzes organised in five chapters

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The program's instructional design combined several media and interaction formats to deliver an IMB-based intervention: it "provided informed evidence in an interactive, interesting, and easy-to-understand way using cartoons, videos, and interactive quizzes." Cartoons "were developed based on interviews with people who have stayed abroad," a second chapter provided a self-check list of risk factors, and a final chapter contained a test of understanding. Participants' evaluations indicated the illustrations and cartoons were highly appreciated.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content should be kept easy to understand and engaging, using media (cartoons, videos, quizzes) matched to the target behaviour-change constructs
#### Constraints
- The article reports one negative comment that it took too much time to learn the material, and the authors judge the content needs to be reduced slightly

### Target Learners
- adult overseas workers aged 20-44 preparing for long-term assignments abroad

### Learning Goals
- understanding oral diseases and evidence-based prevention
- adopting fluoride toothpaste use and self-care behaviours
