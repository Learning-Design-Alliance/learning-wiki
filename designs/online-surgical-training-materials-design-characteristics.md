# Four design characteristics of online surgical training materials enabling instructor-free flipped learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article enumerates four design points for its online materials: "the training videos and assignments were created with actual surgery in mind", videos let all students learn equally from the instructor's viewpoint, tasks were "created by breaking the task down into elements", and "students could respond on their own when they encountered difficulties". Including failure countermeasures in the materials made flipped learning possible without an instructor, and element breakdown allowed step-by-step mastery.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Materials must include methods for dealing with failures so students can respond on their own
- Tasks must be broken down into elements for step-by-step training
#### Constraints
- The pattern was applied to ligation training in a surgical department; the article hopes to extend it to needle suture and surgical skill training

### Target Learners
- medical students in surgical clerkships

### Learning Goals
- acquisition of basic surgical skills such as ligation
