# School counselors and counselor educators should deliver brief face-to-face ASCA National Model presentations to educate principals about appropriate and inappropriate school counselor activities

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that school counselors and counselor educators use information from the ASCA National Model to create presentations educating principals about the role of school counselors. The study's intervention — a 90-minute face-to-face presentation — produced significant changes in future principals' ratings of inappropriate activities, and the authors state that "a brief face-to-face presentation on the ASCA National Model can change future principals' views regarding the role of school counselors."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to an audience of principals or future principals, such as through Educational Administration classes or principals' meetings
#### Constraints
- The study's sample was small, geographically restricted, and composed of future principals rather than practicing principals

### Target Learners
- Future school principals
- Practicing school principals

### Learning Goals
- Understanding appropriate and inappropriate school counselor activities
- Understanding the ASCA National Model and its recommended allocation of school counselor time
