# College and career pathways framework integrating CTE, core academics, work-based learning, and personalized supports

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The report proposes a comprehensive high school redesign framework in which pathways "integrate CTE and core academic curriculum, combine classroom and work-based learning, and align secondary and postsecondary programs." Its state framework should include four essential components: college preparatory core academics "emphasizing real-world application, project-based learning, and performance assessment"; a sequence of four or more challenging CTE courses aligned to industry standards with dual enrollment and certifications; a continuum of work-based learning from career awareness in grade 9 to internships or school-based enterprise by grade 12; and personalized student supports including counseling, accelerated instruction, and social-emotional learning. The framework is grounded in the MDRC and SRI evidence the report reviews.
