# Mechanical ventilation nursing chatbot educational program for self-directed learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A chatbot educational program built with the LandBot online tool to support self-directed learning of mechanical ventilation nursing after instructor-led instruction. It provides "specialized knowledge on mechanical ventilation nursing and offers relevant visuals, such as photos and videos," engages students in text- or voice-based conversation with encouragement, and offers quizzes and feedback. Its content covers understanding of mechanical ventilation nursing and ventilator settings and modes, followed by case-based alarm scenarios.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Designed to be used after instructor-delivered pre-learning instruction; a 26-minute pre-learning video was provided to establish a common baseline before the SDL phase
#### Constraints
- Learning sessions typically take 10 to 30 minutes and additional learning pauses if students choose not to continue; the study evaluated it with a single university sample and a single post-assessment

### Target Learners
- fourth-year undergraduate nursing students
- novice nurses (stated potential application)

### Learning Goals
- mechanical ventilation nursing knowledge
- clinical reasoning competency
- self-confidence in mechanical ventilation nursing
