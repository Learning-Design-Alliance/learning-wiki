# Work-team examples profiled by combining CFQT qualification profiles with content-map subdomains

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A course-level design pattern extending the CFQT's Part 3 personas: 'examples of real work teams are integrated into the courses to illustrate applicability of students' future work, providing them with real-life scenarios of quantum applications'. Each member is profiled by combining a qualification profile with content-map subdomains — for example, a finance team pairing P6+5.5, P7+5.3/5.6, P5+8.1 and P8+8.2/8.3 roles for quantum risk assessment in volatile Latin American economies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Starts from the framework's existing example personas, built by combining qualification profiles with a selection from the content map
#### Constraints
- The work-team example is illustrative and 'does not necessarily reflect a real-life situation'

### Target Learners
- University students in quantum courses exploring career prospects in the quantum field

### Learning Goals
- Understanding real-world applicability of quantum skills, team collaboration roles, and regional career opportunities
