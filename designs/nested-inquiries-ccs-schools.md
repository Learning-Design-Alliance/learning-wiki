# Nested inquiries: students pursue their own investigations intertwined with partnering scientists' projects in school-based citizen science

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
Nested inquiries are cases where educators and scientists strike a balance between students' own questions and those of partnering professional scientists, offering a solution to the tension among the multiple goals of CCS in schools. The commentary states these cases "offer evidence of students' learning and deep engagement with scientific practices, the nature of science and uncertainty, and using their science for decision-making and action." It extends earlier work on youth working with nested data situated in larger datasets, projects, and communities.
