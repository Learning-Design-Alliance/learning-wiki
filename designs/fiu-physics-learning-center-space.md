# Physics Learning Center at Florida International University

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The PLC is a physical complex comprising a 1200 square foot reformed classroom with five student tables, whiteboards, projectors, and computers; a lounge with kitchen area; and a 12-person conference room. It hosts scheduled Modeling Instruction classes, open labs overseen by CHEPREO Fellows, Society of Physics Students meetings, and abundant informal study, socializing, and cross-grade peer help. The article describes it as "the hub for students to become integrated into the social and academic fabric of the university by participating in a learning community."
