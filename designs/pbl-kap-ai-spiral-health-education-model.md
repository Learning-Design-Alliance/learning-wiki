# PBL-KAP-AI spiral health education model

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The model integrates Problem-Based Learning, the Knowledge–Attitude–Practice framework, and AI-driven dynamic feedback into a closed-loop iterative process of "PBL-guided—KAP-deepened—AI-feedback". Unlike traditional linear health education models, its core is a nonlinear iterative process in which patients undergo multiple educational cycles, each encompassing PBL-guided learning, KAP-based knowledge and belief deepening, and dynamic AI feedback. Iteration initiation is determined by AI's assessment of learning progress, health behavior, and emotional state rather than fixed time points. The model was tested in a randomized trial with breast cancer patients.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires an AI platform analyzing clinical, demographic, and behavioral data to deliver personalized tasks and dynamic feedback
- Requires nurse-facilitated PBL discussions and trained staff with consistency assessments
#### Constraints
- Implementation requires considerable organizational and facilitation capacity and may be constrained by disparities in educational resources and learner preparedness

### Target Learners
- female breast cancer patients in hospital care

### Learning Goals
- health behaviors
- health beliefs
- quality of life
- self-directed learning and self-management
