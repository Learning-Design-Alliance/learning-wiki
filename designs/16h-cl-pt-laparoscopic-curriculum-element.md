# 16-hour combined CL and PT laparoscopic training curriculum with six cognitive and seven practical modules

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (1 for, 4 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A predefined 16-hour curriculum of cognitive learning (CL) and practical training (PT) modules "specifically tailored for MIS training in novices." The CL side comprises six units, including e-learning programs for LCHE, tutor-led video analysis, situation awareness training with pop quiz questions, video-frame annotation in LabelMe, a theoretical knowledge test, and the Touch Surgery mobile serious game. The PT side comprises seven modules, including camera guidance, rubber band tasks, cutting and thread-guiding tasks in a Szabo–Berci–Sackier Box Trainer, VR training, and tissue dissection on ex-porcine liver tissue.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained tutors, previously trained by an experienced board-certified surgeon, supervise all modules and ensure designated training time is accurate
#### Constraints
- The theoretical knowledge test was not performed in Group 2 due to time limitations of designated CL

### Target Learners
- Medical students in their clinical years without MIS experience

### Learning Goals
- Visuospatial and visuomotor cognition, instrument handling, camera guidance, and laparoscopic cholecystectomy procedural skills
