# Schedule hackathons for roughly 2.5 days, since participant creativity and stamina decline after the second full day

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors found that "~2.5 days was optimal to achieve our goals within logistical and budgetary constraints, and ensure participants were productive and engaged the entire time." They scheduled events in summer and kept execution deliberately flexible, defining expectations and goals at kickoff that "served as a North Star for decision making during the hackathon."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schedule events in summer when both students and faculty have more flexibility in their schedules and campus rooms are more accessible
- Define expectations and goals at the kickoff session to guide decision making during the event
#### Constraints
- By the end of the second full day, most participants demonstrated "a decline in creativity and stamina" suggesting further work would likely lead to diminishing returns
- Shorter times would not have allowed the groundwork and build-up to final projects

### Target Learners
- trainees from high school students to postdocs in physics, engineering, biophysics, and statistics

### Learning Goals
- data-driven analysis of complex research data
- team coding and collaboration skills
