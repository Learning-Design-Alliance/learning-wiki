# Sixteen-session three-group learning-strategy training program with TOEFL reading passages

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The study's instructional element is a quasi-experimental training program in which "The participants in all three experimental groups participated in sixteen sessions of strategy training". Ninety upper-intermediate learners, screened as homogeneous from 130 via a placement test, were divided into three classes of 30, each receiving one main strategy from Brown's (2000) taxonomy — metacognitive, cognitive, or social/affective — taught according to Oxford's (1990) training model. Three reading passages from Longman Complete Course for the TOEFL Test with 50 multiple-choice questions served as the outcome measure.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A proficiency/placement test to establish homogeneous groups before treatment
- Reading passages appropriate to participants' proficiency level
#### Constraints
- Quasi-experimental design with three intact experimental classes rather than random assignment

### Target Learners
- upper-intermediate EFL learners aged 16-26

### Learning Goals
- reading comprehension through learning-strategy use
