# Latino/a Affinity Group at Huntley 158: an affinity space fostering belonging and retention of Latino/a teachers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Huntley Community School District 158 initiated a Latino/a Affinity Group "to foster connections and support among Latino/a teachers, creating a sense of belonging and networking opportunities across the district to improve staff retention." Emerging from a Digital Promise-facilitated design studio, it gave educators a platform for personal and professional growth, enabled cross-school collaboration on teaching practices, and supported student-facing events such as a fourth-grade dance performance that centered Latino/a identity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Time and space for members across school locations to meet and compare practices
#### Constraints
- Focused on Latino/a staff in a district with fast-growing Latino/a enrollment that faculty demographics do not match

### Target Learners
- Latino/a teachers and staff

### Learning Goals
- Retention, job satisfaction, belonging, collaborative professional growth
