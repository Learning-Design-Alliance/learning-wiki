# Design physics labs to eliminate algebraic manipulation so students focus exclusively on geometrical reasoning about centripetal acceleration

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends structuring lab activities so that calculation is removed entirely, leaving students to reason geometrically. It argues that "The complete absence of algebraic manipulations obliges students to focus exclusively on the development of their geometrical reasoning abilities", and that freed from calculation time, teachers can let students discover procedural steps themselves. The authors believe this produces favorable conditions for geometric insight into centripetal acceleration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An apparatus and procedure must be arranged so that no numeric calculation is needed, e.g. a 1:1 drawing scale
#### Constraints
- The article presents this as a pedagogical design argument, not a tested comparison of learning outcomes

### Target Learners
- introductory physics students, including algebraically-accomplished students who avoid conceptual engagement

### Learning Goals
- geometrical reasoning about centripetal acceleration as a centrally-pointing vector
