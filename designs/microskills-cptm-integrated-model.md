# Synergistic micro-skills training-CPTM model: video-based reflective skill training embedded in a standardized clinical pathway framework

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The model embeds "micro-skills training’s video-based, feedback-rich skill training within CPTM’s systematic clinical pathway framework, thereby providing both structure and personalized skill development." Students use pathway forms covering admission criteria through follow-up, record skill demonstrations and their own practice on mobile phones, review recordings at least three times with structured reflection forms, and join weekly 30-minute group video review discussions with teacher feedback. The integration aligns recorded skills with the corresponding pathway step.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors trained in video recording protocols, structured feedback, and pathway form use
- Weekly video review discussion sessions
- Clinical pathway teaching forms for core diseases in each subspecialty
#### Constraints
- Video recording was conducted solely in hospital wards and may have been affected by interference or poor quality
- Scalability of the intensive micro-skills training component remains a major challenge for broader adoption
- Unreasonable clinical pathways may compromise teaching quality

### Target Learners
- surgical interns
- medical undergraduates in clinical rotations

### Learning Goals
- basic surgical procedural skills
- clinical reasoning
- theoretical knowledge
- patient communication
