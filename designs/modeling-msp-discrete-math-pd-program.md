# Modeling MSP professional development program for discrete mathematics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A year-long, 135-contact-hour professional development program in which 42 teachers initially participated, built around a models and modeling perspective. It combined "two 40 -hour intensive summer workshops" with "9 additional 8-hour Saturday sessions", emphasized "modeling high cognitive demand tasks in problem -based-learning scenarios", and used simulation tools, probeware, data analysis, and graphical/visualization tools. Teachers organized into cross-school curriculum development teams designed and piloted a fourth-year discrete mathematics course, iterating lessons with videotaped practice and student work samples shared via a project Sharepoint site.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers organized into curriculum development teams tasked with designing fourth-year curriculum structure and tasks for subsequent implementation
#### Constraints
- Retention of participants was an ongoing challenge; of 60 originally identified participants, 43 instructors participated

- Junior-Senior level high school mathematics teachers

### Learning Goals
- Mathematical Knowledge for Teaching in discrete mathematics
- Pedagogical content knowledge in mathematical modeling
