# Narrative medicine education program combining theoretical sessions with clinical practice (parallel charts, diary exchange, reflective writing)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A two-part narrative medicine program for Grade 3 nursing students. The theoretical part comprised "12 weekly sessions that comprised an hour-long class" covering narrative medicine theory, film observation and discussion, and close reading of medical narrative books. The clinical practice part, run by trained nurse mentors in groups of 10, required students to "write the patients’ parallel charts according to the patients’ narratives", exchange diaries with one patient, write two reflective papers, and attend biweekly 2-hour seminars with a final group presentation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A teacher well trained in narrative medicine for the theoretical course
- nurse mentors trained in narrative medicine for the clinical practice component
- completion of two parallel charts, diary exchange with one patient, and two reflective papers
#### Constraints
- In this trial the practice component occurred during the autumn term when students were beginning clinical practice, so it depends on access to patient contact

### Target Learners
- Grade 3 nursing students in China

### Learning Goals
- empathic ability toward patients
- narrative competence
- critical thinking
- humanistic care skills
