# Weekly clinical-rotation PBL cycle combining lecture-based teaching, pharmaceutical care practice, SOAP notes, and case wrap-up

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's PBL model was implemented in 1-day periods each week over 15 weeks at clinical practice sites. Students were randomly assigned to 3 practice sites (internal medicine, oncology, psychiatric wards, community pharmacy), rotated every 3 weeks with a case presentation at the faculty, and performed activities including providing pharmaceutical care, evaluating therapeutic regimens, developing SOAP notes, peer feedback and case wrap-up sessions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clinical practice sites with preceptors and a teacher available as facilitator during rotations
#### Constraints
- Peer feedback process between teacher and students may be limited by limitation of area space in clinical practice sites

### Target Learners
- 5th-year pharmacy students

### Learning Goals
- applying didactic knowledge to direct patient care
- clinical communication
- designing evidence-based treatment and monitoring plans
