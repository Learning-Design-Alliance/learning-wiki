# Effective Presentations courses should be required for graduate students in English for Careers programs

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article argues that a dedicated presentation skills course produces measurable improvements in organization/content, delivery, poise, and visuals, and that "Effective Presentations (CR 752) should be a required course for all graduates studying in the Master Program in English for Careers." The authors view presentation skills as professional competence required by employers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A dedicated course focusing on clear, well-designed, professional business presentations in English
#### Constraints
- Findings are based on a cross-sectional survey of 26 conference attendees at a single Thai university

### Target Learners
- Graduate students in English for Careers programs

### Learning Goals
- English presentation skills
- Professional communication competence
