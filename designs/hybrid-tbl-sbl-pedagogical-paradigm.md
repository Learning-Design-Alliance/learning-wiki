# Hybrid TBL-SBL pedagogical paradigm for complex clinical education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes an integrated pedagogy that sequences team-based learning's knowledge-assurance steps (pre-class preparation, IRAT, TRAT, immediate feedback) with simulation-based learning's phases (pre-briefing, scenario, debriefing). The authors state its "primary contribution is the creation of an innovative hybrid pedagogical paradigm" for enhancing knowledge and confidence in infant dysphagia care, arguing TBL characteristics address shortcomings of SBL identified by Bae et al. at the pre-briefing, scenario and debriefing stages.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires TBL components (micromodule, IRAT, TRAT, feedback) preceding the simulation scenario and a facilitator-led debriefing at closing
#### Constraints
- The study implemented a modified integration rather than a 'pure' integration of the two frameworks
- Evidence comes from a single-group pre-post design without a control group

### Target Learners
- final-year speech-language pathology master's students

### Learning Goals
- assessment and management of infant feeding and swallowing disorders
- collaborative clinical reasoning and shared decision-making
