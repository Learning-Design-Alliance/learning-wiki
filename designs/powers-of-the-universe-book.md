# Powers of the Universe book: a 130-page logarithmic-scale book with one power of ten per page

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A long-term activity in which students create a book representing the entire logarithmic scale of the Universe in SI units. The article describes "the creation of a 130-page book called Powers of the Universe" in which "All numbers in SI units describing the known Universe can be placed in this book, if each page is an increasing power of ten". Students search for sizes of quarks, atoms, viruses, people and the observable Universe, and add speeds, masses, times and counts; it was the most remembered activity (64%) at ten-month retention testing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Extended collaborative team time; the authors suggest it should be a term-long activity
#### Constraints
- Post-test showed 54%, 89% and 80% of students connected page numbers to order of magnitude, and only 23-45% could compare objects by counting pages between them

### Target Learners
- primary school students aged 7-13

### Learning Goals
- developing a logarithmic sense of scale from atoms to the observable Universe
