# Four-component lesson plan representation schema grounded in curriculum studies and differentiated instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
AdaPT decomposes lesson plans into four components: curriculum standards, learning objectives, learning profile analysis, and learning tasks. The design draws on the Encyclopedia of Curriculum Studies' four common features of instructional design models and on Tomlinson's differentiated instruction framework, operationalizing readiness via Vygotsky's zone of proximal development. The authors state they "designed a structured representation that decomposes a lesson plan into four key components." Learning tasks are refined into atomic units with task, expected answer, detailed plan, implementation method, and design intent, enabling reuse and reflection on standards alignment.
