# Train-the-trainers cascade from master trainers to local facilitators to learners

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The training program uses a cascading model in which Dutch master trainers first train local facilitators (gynaecologists selected by the head of department for clinical and teaching experience), who then train the SHOs without supervision. As the article states, "The training will be performed using a train-the-trainers model in which training is cascaded down from master trainer to local facilitators to learners." The four-day course covers simulation-based education principles, scenario practice among facilitators, and a final test day teaching junior house officers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Local facilitators selected based on clinical and teaching experience and paid for the training sessions
- Annual one-day train-the-trainers repetition course provided by the master trainers
#### Constraints
- The article notes a limitation in selecting only gynaecologists as local facilitators and recommends future studies include other obstetric team members as facilitators

### Target Learners
- Local gynaecologist facilitators
- Senior house officers

### Learning Goals
- Delivering simulation-based obstetric emergency training locally
- Sustaining training capacity through annual repetition
