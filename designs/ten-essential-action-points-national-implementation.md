# Ten essential action points for national dissemination and implementation of Helping Babies Survive programs

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An Utstein-style stakeholder meeting formulated 10 essential action points for national dissemination and implementation of the Helping Babies Survive and Helping Mothers Survive programs. The points span establishing a maternal-newborn-child health alliance, forming a working group for advocacy and planning, nation-to-facility training plans, simultaneous provision of adapted materials and equipment, supporting local champions, and setting up "local systems for frequent, brief refresher training, debriefing, and audits."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Country-level alliance with public, private, and nongovernmental partners; a functional working group; a financial plan to implement and sustain the program
#### Constraints
- Achieving impact at the population level requires integration of the curriculum into the regional health system

### Target Learners
- Birth attendants, facilitators, and national health leaders in resource-limited settings

### Learning Goals
- Sustained nationwide coverage of high-quality neonatal resuscitation training
