# Certified Intensive English Program (CIEP) at ELS Language Centers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The CIEP is the core full-time proficiency course at ELS Language Centers Malaysia, structured into 9 levels grouped as Beginning (101–103), Intermediate (104–106), and Advanced (107–109). It runs full-time over 4 weeks per session (20 days), with 28 lessons per week covering "speaking, listening, reading, writing, pronunciation, vocabulary and gramma r". Components include Structure and Speaking Practice, Reading and Writing, Conversation, and E-Learning Language Technology (LTC).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Full-time attendance over a 4-week session (20 days), with 28 lessons per week of classroom instruction
#### Constraints
- Attendance requirements are very stringent: students were required not to miss class for more than 3 days

### Target Learners
- Low-scoring Malaysian Form 4 secondary school students

### Learning Goals
- General English language proficiency across listening, speaking, reading, and writing, plus grammar, spelling, and pronunciation
