# Two-hour Teach-Back training workshop with video, handouts and telephone-simulating role play

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A single 2-h group workshop for 7–8 nurses, run by two facilitators with a collaborative rather than didactic approach. It comprised two modules (introduction to Teach-Back; Teach-Back in practice), a short video of a distracted caller to illustrate a universal precautions approach, two double-sided handouts of strategies and phrases, and role play with nurses' backs to each other to simulate the telephone environment, swapping roles and partners. Nurses "enjoyed the practical application of skill during role play".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Small-group face-to-face format; facilitators experienced in call centre operations and Teach-Back training; evidence-based materials adapted to the workplace
#### Constraints
- Not all calls are amenable to Teach-Back; nurses were told to use professional judgement; one nurse felt the training had too much theory and not enough role playing

### Target Learners
- maternal and child health telenurses

### Learning Goals
- knowledge, skills and beliefs for using Teach-Back in telephone consultations
