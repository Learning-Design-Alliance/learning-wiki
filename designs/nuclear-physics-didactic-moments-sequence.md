# A nuclear-physics teaching sequence for high school built as adaptable didactic moments, not a fixed script

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A sequence of suggested didactic moments for teaching nuclear physics in high school, covering the Rutherford-Geiger-Marsden experiment, proton and neutron structure, nuclear radius, the nuclear force, binding energy, fission and fusion. The authors explicitly design it as raw material rather than a recipe: "um material de car´ater sugestivo e male´avel, adapt´avel `as aspira¸c˜oes, necessidades e circunstˆancias de cada docente e de cada turma". Teachers are expected to adapt the moments to their own class context.
