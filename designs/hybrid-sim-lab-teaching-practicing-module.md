# Hybrid sim-lab teaching-practicing module for ultrasonic supragingival scaling (lecture, demonstration, remote guidance)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An online course delivery system for preclinical ultrasonic supragingival scaling training, structured into "three distinct parts: lecture, demonstration and remote guidance". A live-streamed 15-min lecture with real-time comments and WeChat questions is followed by a 45-min multi-camera phantom-head demonstration and 30 min of remote-guided student practice, with recordings available for replay. The SEEWO interactive recording and broadcasting system captured the sessions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers and assistant teachers undergo comprehensive training sessions to standardise the teaching approach
- Students need access to electronic equipment and a stable internet connection
- Ongoing technical support from personnel due to the complexity of the remote system
#### Constraints
- Tested only in a single course (ultrasonic supragingival scaling)
- Coordinating schedules across 23 medical colleges posed significant challenges
- High demands on network connectivity; disruptions could compromise course quality

### Target Learners
- second-year dental students

### Learning Goals
- ultrasonic supragingival scaling technique
- four-handed dentistry operation
- instrument handling and finger rest positioning
