# Three-facet data scientist competency framework guiding course objectives

## Description
The course organizes its objectives around three facets the article, citing Conway, attributes to a data scientist: math and statistics knowledge, hacking skills, and substantive/business expertise. Math and statistics covers "a toolkit of techniques for running summary, classification, and predictive analytics over (un)structured data"; hacking skills cover open-source programming, visualization, and the analytics task pipeline; substantive expertise covers interpreting results and conveying findings to a layman.
