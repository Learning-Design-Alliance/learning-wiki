# Final group project researching a protein's structure-function relationship with a 3D-printed physical model

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course incorporates a final project in which "students, in groups, research some protein with the goal of explaining the relationship between its structure and function." Students also 3D printed a physical model of their protein and compared its usefulness as a visualization with computational rendering using the widely used PyMol software. The article reports that nearly everyone preferred, overall, the computational illustration, and suggests a greater focus on scientific visualization methods as a possible extension.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to 3D printing and the PyMol software for computational rendering
#### Constraints
- Students nearly universally preferred the computational rendering over the 3D-printed physical model

### Target Learners
- non-science-major undergraduates

### Learning Goals
- understanding protein structure-function relationships
- scientific visualization
