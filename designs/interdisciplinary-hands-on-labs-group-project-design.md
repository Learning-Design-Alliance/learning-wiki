# Interdisciplinary data science course design built on hands-on labs and a mixed-majors semester project

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A course-design pattern for interdisciplinary data science teaching: objectives are pursued through "a series of hands-on laboratory sessions (eight in total) and a semester long group project, rather than individualized written and programming assignments", with labs training tool use and reporting on real data in a collaborative environment, and the project engaging students in defining and exploring a dataset within the business domain it emerged from.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Every laboratory assignment includes the manipulation of real data using R and/or Python, with students working in small groups.
#### Constraints
- Project teams were formed with mixed-majors students, consisting of at least two students from different academic disciplines.

### Target Learners
- Mixed-majors upper-level undergraduate and graduate student groups

### Learning Goals
- Data science tool skills, interpretation and reporting of results on real data, and domain-grounded hypothesis testing
