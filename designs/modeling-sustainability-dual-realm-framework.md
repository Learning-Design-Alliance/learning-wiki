## Description
The review's organizing framework (Figure 1) sets two realms side by side: learning about sustainability, where students gain knowledge of sustainability and its mathematics, and sustainable learning in mathematics education, learning that lasts beyond formal schooling. The framework 'highlights the interconnection between learning about sustainability and sustainable learning in (mathematics) education', with a bridging course symbolized by a red rectangle that 'connects these two domains'.
