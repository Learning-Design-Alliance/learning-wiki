# Four-phase iterative cGPT co-creation process with SPAR reflective logging

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a reusable co-creation pattern with four construction phases: Creation (writing the system prompt and selecting knowledge-base files), Refinement (iterative testing, prompt editing, and SPAR reflections), Consolidation (finalising prompts and merging versions), and Evaluation (scoring responses to ten evaluation questions). Throughout, students wrote SPAR reflections — "Stimulus (Prompt sent to GPT), Processing (GPT's response), Analysis (Evaluation of GPT response based on design principles) and Reflection (Next steps based on GPT response)" — which informed system prompt adjustment until satisfactory accuracy and instruction adherence were reached.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Regular student-academic team meetings (weekly to twice weekly) for testing and refinement, and design principles focused on clear, accurate and consistent responses
#### Constraints
- The overall process from construction to implementation lasted two to six weeks, depending on the cGPT's topic, students' prior experience, and availability of resources such as system prompt templates

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students without extensive prior research experience

### Learning Goals
- AI literacy, teamwork, and project management
- iterative design and evaluative judgment
