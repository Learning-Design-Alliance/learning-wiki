# Ten-day sequenced micro-learning content framework for PIVC placement

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The intervention's educational content followed a day-by-day framework (Table 1) progressing from "PIVC basics" and catheter selection, through placement protocols for different ages, vein selection and localisation, asepsis and patient safety, to "Post-intubation care assessment and complication prevention programme". Each day carried one or two focused topics delivered as short messages, embodying fragmented, spaced delivery aligned with nurses' workflow patterns.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content must be mapped to clinical guidelines and national standards; the study revised items against three Chinese national standards and guidelines
#### Constraints
- Framework was designed for PIVC placement education in Chinese hospital settings

### Target Learners
- Clinical nurses in emergency, medical and surgical wards

### Learning Goals
- PIVC placement knowledge
- aseptic technique and patient safety
- complication prevention
