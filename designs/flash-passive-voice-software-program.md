# MacroMedia Flash software program for teaching the English passive voice

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The researcher developed an instructional program in MacroMedia Flash Professional Version 6 to teach the passive voice to first-secondary pupils. It is organized into sections covering construction, use, agent, present, past and modal verbs in the passive, and problematic issues, each with "Explanation and Examples: presented in an audio-visual manner" plus exercises, drills, and a Test Yourself section. The program provides model answers and "a system for correction" so students receive immediate feedback and scores. Its stated objectives include facilitating understanding, increasing motivation, building self-confidence, and reducing shyness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A computer lab; the program was validated by a committee of ten experienced English teachers, four Ministry of Education supervisors, and two PhD holders in curricula and instruction
#### Constraints
- The program covers only the passive voice for first secondary grade

### Target Learners
- first secondary grade EFL pupils in Jordan, aged 16-17

### Learning Goals
- understanding and producing the English passive voice
