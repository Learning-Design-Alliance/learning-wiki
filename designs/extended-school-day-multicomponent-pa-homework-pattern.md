# Multi-component extended-school-day intervention combining physical activity, homework support with activity breaks, and walking with audiobooks

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The protocol describes a school-based programme delivered during an extended school day, 60 minutes three times per week for six months, with three components: varied physical activities chosen with teachers and students, homework support with 3–5 minute activity breaks every 30 minutes, and walking while listening to audiobooks. Activities are part of the mandatory curriculum and led by school staff so the programme is "integrated and sustainable".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Activities are scheduled within the mandatory school curriculum and led by school staff, so schools must provide facilities and extra staff time
#### Constraints
- Schools are expected to implement the programme using their own resources, including staff time and facilities, which the authors state may lead to challenges

### Target Learners
- adolescents in grade 8 (age 14–15)

### Learning Goals
- mental health (anxiety)
- cognitive function
- academic performance
- physical activity
