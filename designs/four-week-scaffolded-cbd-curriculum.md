# Four-week progressive scaffolded case-based curriculum: foundations, planning, simulation, then evaluation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A lesson-level instructional pattern in which a complex clinical case is taught across four weekly 1.5-hour sessions with staged scaffolding: Week 1 foundational knowledge and problem identification, Week 2 collaborative evidence-based management planning, Week 3 scenario-based simulation with structured role-play, and Week 4 critical analysis, performance evaluation, and structured feedback. Support is systematically reduced as learners demonstrate competency, culminating in autonomous decision-making.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Identical course duration and scheduling across comparison groups
- Instructors trained on course content and learning objectives
- A complex anchor case (here HOCM anesthesia management)
#### Constraints
- The authors note this design compares integrated scaffolded CBD against lecture-based teaching and does not isolate the specific impact of scaffolding within case-based learning

### Target Learners
- medical residents in specialty training

### Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning
- self-directed learning
- knowledge acquisition and retention
