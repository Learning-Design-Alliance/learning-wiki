# Comprehensive teacher-delivered experiential learning oral health education program with manual, seminar training, group projects and extramural visits

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A school-based oral health education program in which teachers, trained in a seminar, implemented a 3-month experiential learning program "according to a preset course using the program's manual". Children worked in groups on oral health projects, made extramural visits (dentist, vet, pharmacy, supermarket), presented outcomes via theatrical play, posters, songs, crafts and role playing, and uploaded project products to the program's web-site. A dentist lecture and demonstration supplemented the classroom work.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers previously trained in experiential learning received a seminar on oral health education, experiential learning methods and use of the program's manual
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness is influenced by the environment and the educator; the authors note differences might have occurred due to the dynamic interaction between the teacher and specific students group

### Target Learners
- 10-year-old primary school children (4th grade)

### Learning Goals
- Oral health knowledge, attitude, behavior, oral hygiene and gingival health
