# Fourteen-week blended course structure pairing weekly sessions with peer-teaching practice and staged assignments

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The teaching methods course was organized as fourteen weeks of three contact hours each, pairing a first session of content (e.g., lesson planning, behavioral objectives, warming-up activities, questioning strategies) with a second session of peer teaching practice, plus weekly assignments such as formulating sample objectives and preparing practice activities. Student evaluation allocated 15 marks to attendance and in-class activities, 20 to the midterm exam, 15 to peer-teaching, and 50 to the final written exam. The course consisted of forty-two hours covering twenty-three items.
