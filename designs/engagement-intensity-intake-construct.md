# Engagement intensity as a pair of complementary intake features (reported use and self-rated familiarity) for learner modeling

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article introduces engagement intensity as a working label for two complementary intake indicators of technology engagement drawn from technology acceptance and AI literacy research: "reported use and self-rated familiarity." It functions as a lightweight learner-modeling signal for intake-level grouping, explicitly scoped to pre-instruction segmentation rather than adaptive sequencing, personalized feedback, or a full intelligent tutoring system. In this framing, behavioral usage frequency is the primary axis and familiarity a secondary, self-perception-based check on perceived readiness.
