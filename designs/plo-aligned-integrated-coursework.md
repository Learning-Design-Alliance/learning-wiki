# Coursework aligned to four Program Learning Outcomes integrated across all courses

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Alder's curriculum is organized around four Program Learning Outcomes: asset-based community and cultural responsiveness; developmentally appropriate, whole child, and inclusive practices; pedagogical content knowledge; and critical, reflective, and research-based orientations to teaching. The report states that "The integration of these PLOs into each course allowed residents to see and experience similar concepts across courses, which helped reinforce learning and encouraged residents to reflect on their clinical experiences in relation to the PLOs throughout the residency year."
