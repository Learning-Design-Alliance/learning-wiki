# The living network of schools owned by teachers and students as a self-organizing educational system

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article names and substantively describes a model educational system in which "Schools are owned and governed by the teachers and students" and members form teams, classes, schools, districts, regions and academies. The structure is explicitly provisional: "The design described here is provided as illustration for the underlying principles and their implications rather than as the ultimate structure." The network is expected to evolve and adapt, with structure changing according to the needs of learning processes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Members identify themselves by contributing to the processes of the system and by using its structures
#### Constraints
- The model structure is arbitrary and not sufficiently studied; the author states one would have to study mathematical models of the ownership structure to make reasonable evaluations

### Target Learners
- students at all levels from nursery to academia
- teachers at all levels

### Learning Goals
- learning how to learn effectively
- learning subject matter by inquiry
