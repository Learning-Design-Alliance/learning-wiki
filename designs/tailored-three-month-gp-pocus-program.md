# Tailored three-month POCUS educational program for office-based GPs

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
A longitudinal educational program for office-based general practitioners consisting of "three teaching seminars over a period of three months, a curriculum of 10 POCUS applications", and an online learning platform providing support before, during and after the seminars. It included 24 hours of on-site hands-on training, 2 hours of webinars and a minimum of 16 hours of guided self-study. Activities were released stepwise, scaffolding learning from image production through scanning healthy individuals to recognizing pathology, with mentors tutoring four GPs each and supervising remotely while GPs implemented ultrasound in everyday clinical practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants needed access to an ultrasound device in their clinic during the study
- Mentors were ultrasound training experts with experience in working in primary care
#### Constraints
- The program is resource demanding, according to the authors
- GPs were restricted from attending other POCUS related courses during the three-month follow-up

### Target Learners
- Office-based general practitioners in Denmark

### Learning Goals
- Point-of-care ultrasound scanning competence across 10 POCUS applications
- Sustainable implementation of POCUS in daily general practice
