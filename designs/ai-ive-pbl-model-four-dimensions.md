# The AI-IVE-PBL model integrates an AI-enabled immersive virtual environment with project-based learning across four dimensions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes a PBL in AI-IVE (AI-IVE-PBL) model that combines the advantages of an AI-enabled immersive virtual environment with the features of project-based learning. The model is "structured around four key dimensions: teachers, students, agents, and teaching phases." It was designed to foster design creativity among vocational students and was implemented in a Public Space Interior Design course.
