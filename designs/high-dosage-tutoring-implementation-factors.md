# High dosage tutoring implementation factor set: frequency and scheduling, group size, qualified personnel, outcome measurement, curriculum, tutor-to-student relationships, and removing barriers to access

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The brief organizes effective high dosage tutoring around a set of implementation factors that "must be considered and included to implement high dosage tutoring effectively." These are "frequency and scheduling, group size, qualified personnel, measurement of outcomes, curriculum, tutor-to-student relationships, and new components like removing barriers to access." The set functions as a design checklist for schools or districts planning tutoring programs for at-risk students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Implementation must attend to frequency and scheduling, group size, qualified personnel, measurement of outcomes, curriculum, and tutor-to-student relationships for the tutoring to work as described.
#### Constraints
- The brief notes that some components, such as removing barriers to access, are new additions to the factor set.

### Target Learners
- Academically at-risk K-12 students

### Learning Goals
- Accelerated academic growth in core academic subjects
