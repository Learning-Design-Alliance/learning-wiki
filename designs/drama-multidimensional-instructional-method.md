# Drama as a multidimensional, student-centered instructional method for communication development

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article frames drama as a modern instructional method that is student-centered, learner-activated, hands-on, and entertaining, drawing on theatre techniques such as role playing, improvisation, role cards, and dramatization. It treats drama as "a mult i-dimensional concept" spanning education, visual arts, and psychological treatment, and argues that through drama activities students achieve thinking, speaking, listening, and narrative skills, with group interaction improving language skills naturally.
