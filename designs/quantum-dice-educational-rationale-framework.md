# Educational rationale combining model-based learning, analogical reasoning, embodied cognition and the spin-first approach

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article grounds the Quantum Dice design in four complementary accounts. The Dice "provide opportunities for Model Based Learning, enabling users to construct, test, and refine mental models of QP phenomena"; they support "explanations through analogical reasoning, where familiar and visible base domains support understanding of abstract concepts"; their haptic nature "aligns with embodied cognition principles, serving as material anchors", potentially reducing cognitive load via multimodal engagement and enabling cognitive offloading; and the design follows the "spin-first approach" that uses simple small quantum systems to emphasise concepts while minimising early mathematics.
