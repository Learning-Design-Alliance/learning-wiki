# Extracted-tooth preclinical endodontic training protocol with standardized radiographic quality assessment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A preclinical course element in which third-year students perform complete root canal treatment on extracted human teeth embedded in plaster or acrylic blocks during three-hour supervised laboratory sessions, using manual step-back instrumentation with 0.02 taper stainless steel K-files and cold lateral condensation with gutta-percha and resin-based sealer. Completed samples are radiographed under standardized conditions and scored 0–2 on length, taper, and homogeneity criteria, with overall adequacy requiring all criteria met in all canals.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor and assistant guidance through each stage, anatomical training before each session, and calibrated blinded examiners for radiographic assessment
#### Constraints
- Two-dimensional periapical radiographs may not fully reflect the three-dimensional quality of fillings; the direct field of view on extracted teeth may limit translation to clinical conditions

### Target Learners
- third-year undergraduate dental students

### Learning Goals
- manual dexterity and technical skills in root canal preparation and obturation
