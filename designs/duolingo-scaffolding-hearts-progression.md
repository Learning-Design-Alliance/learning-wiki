# Scaffolding via level-gated progression and hearts in Duolingo

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes Duolingo's design as scaffolding, in which learners progress into more advanced levels once they show competence at the previous level. A heart system enforces this: users lose hearts for incorrect answers and a session ends when all hearts are lost, so the user "must answer items correctly before they are able to complete the new material." Content is grouped into manageable units, and negative feedback identifies incorrect answers users may learn from.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Recording of incorrect answers to gate advancement to higher levels
#### Constraints
- Average users who lose all hearts end their session for the day; plus users can continue without hearts

### Target Learners
- Self-directed app-based foreign language learners

### Target Goals
- Progressive mastery of vocabulary, phrases, enunciation, and echoing
