# Learn.Plan.Succeed. (LPS) initiative with Individualized Learning Plans

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Through the district's Learn.Plan.Succeed. (LPS) initiative, students track their completion of college and career exploration and preparation milestones via an Individualized Learning Plan (ILP). The report states the district has established software, curricula, and advising frameworks to monitor and support LPS, making it a promising system for organizing college and career advising across students over time. LPS requires students to select one of six postsecondary pathways by grade 12 and provide evidence of their plan to graduate.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Software, curricula, and advising frameworks to monitor and support milestone completion
#### Constraints
- All students must complete the FAFSA and apply to at least one college regardless of pathway selection

### Target Learners
- Students in grades 6 through 12 in Chicago Public Schools

### Learning Goals
- Postsecondary pathway selection
- college and career exploration
