# Three-phase DPBL lesson sequence: introduction and identification, application and solution, evaluation and conclusion

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The DPBL classroom pattern organises a lesson into three phases. In Phase 1 the lecturer introduces the lesson, divides students into small groups, presents theory and the problem, and monitors group discussion. In Phase 2 "The groups define the real world problem and final solution" and search and synthesise related data while the lecturer provides advice. In Phase 3 groups present their work reports and "The lecturer explains and summarises the concepts and then closes the lesson."
