# Inclusivity-driven course design framework built on three guiding principles for general-education science courses

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article presents a course design framework organized around three guiding principles: "Both science content and the human story of understanding the Universe must be addressed throughout the course", respect for diverse perspectives, and many opportunities for students to connect content with their personal experiences and "life files". The principles were woven through classroom norms, lecture content, writing prompts, homework, and quizzes in a pilot general-education astronomy course, and are offered as a framework for other instructors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The article states that to successfully establish a norm, an instructor has to not only state it but also enforce it throughout the semester
#### Constraints
- The course represents a pilot test of these principles at a single institution in one semester

### Target Learners
- non-science-major undergraduates in general-education science courses

### Learning Goals
- connecting science content with students' personal lives and identities
- valuing diverse perspectives and cultural connections to science
