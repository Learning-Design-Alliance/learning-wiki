## Description
The AI Co-Creators project is a student-staff co-creation initiative exploring generative AI in higher education assessments. The article states the project "aimed to promote dialogue between students and staff on the impact of AI with the objective of using AI tools more effectively". Its partnership evaluated ChatGPT-generated output against diverse coursework assessments on an undergraduate medical sciences programme.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dialogue between students and staff on the impact of AI, which the project aimed to promote
- Coursework assessments from which ChatGPT-generated output can be produced and evaluated
#### Constraints
- The reported evaluation concerned ChatGPT-generated output on an undergraduate medical sciences programme

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical sciences students
- academic staff partners

### Learning Goals
- using AI tools more effectively
- evaluating AI-generated output against co-designed assessment criteria
