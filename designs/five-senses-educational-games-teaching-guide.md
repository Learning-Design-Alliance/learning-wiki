# Teaching guide of eight educational games for the five-senses unit

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The researcher developed a teaching guide for the first-grade five-senses unit, containing study plans for educational games across eight lessons. The games — "surprise box, close your eyes, identify sounds and fragrances, say then taste, what’s in the bag, cold and hot, I’m the doctor" — are purposeful activities practiced individually or in groups under pre-set rules to acquire scientific concepts. The guide was reviewed by a jury of six reviewers and revised per their recommendations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Study plans allocating a specific number of classes to each of the eight lessons
- Teacher briefing: the researcher met the science teacher and intensively discussed the guide and how to apply the games
#### Constraints
- Validated for first graders and the five-senses unit only

### Target Learners
