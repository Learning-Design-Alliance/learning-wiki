# A high-school teaching sequence introducing Particle Physics through Quantum Electrodynamics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article introduces a multi-part teaching sequence that brings Particle Physics into the high-school curriculum with emphasis on scientific literacy. It starts from atomic structure and the discovery of the electron, moves through the electromagnetic interaction, electromagnetic waves, wave-particle duality and photons, and culminates in the QED description in which "a interação entre cargas elétricas é mediada pela troca de fótons". The sequence is designed to intersect with content usually taught in high school, so Particle Physics can be included without sacrificing classical physics learning.

### Target Learners
- Brazilian high-school students (ensino médio)

### Learning Goals
- Understanding key concepts of particle physics and quantum electrodynamics
- Scientific literacy
- Physical intuition and argumentative capacity
