# Multimedia-supported web-based interactive student workbook for 4th grade Social Studies

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A web-based, multimedia-supported interactive workbook for the "Power, Management and Society" learning domain, built with Flash CS4, Photoshop CS4, Dreamweaver CS4, AppServ and ActionScript 3.0, AmfPHP, PHP, MySql, AJAX. "This material, used on web, is formed according to three user types": managers design and upload lessons, teachers sign up students, set activities, view answers online and give feedback, and students complete and revise activities and see teacher feedback. It auto-evaluates multiple choice, correct-incorrect and fill-in-the-blanks questions and records student data.
