# Interdisciplinary polarization laboratory sequence combining physics demonstrations, biology (bee vision) and art with everyday materials

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The laboratory is organized as a sequence of interconnected activities: the Dirac three-filter experiment with polarizing filters; sky polarization examined with polariscopes including a model of von Frisch's bee retinula; Tyndall scattering experiments; birefringence art with cellophane, plastic and stressed materials between crossed filters; and optical-activity experiments with sugar solutions. The design uses the fact that beautiful images between crossed polarizers "remind them of those ones of abstract painting or alien landscapes", and teachers discuss with pupils the relation between art and science, for instance how artistic trends depended on science and technology.
