# P-I-T-D-S extended flipped classroom model

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The P-I-T-D-S model is a five-step extended flipped classroom framework for dental preclinical education built around a virtual simulation system. Each letter is an implementing step: 'P' represents patient and practice (case-based teaching with the simulation system); 'I' information and inspiration (pre-class self-learning tasks using textbooks, MOOCs, PubMed, AI); 'T' test and teach (pre-class tests with targeted teaching); 'D' discussion and debate (group case design, presentations, and debate of design schemes); 'S' summary and search (summarizing core content and extended reading on research progress). Its core concept is active learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a virtual simulation training system (OSCRS/Unidraw) plus information technology resources for pre-class self-learning
- Requires teachers to assign pre-class tasks, conduct pre-class tests, organize group discussion and debate, and lead summary sessions
#### Constraints
- The article states the model was verified only with sophomore dental undergraduates in one course at one institution; the authors propose extending it to clinical medical education in the future

### Target Learners
- Second-year dental undergraduates in preclinical bridging courses

### Learning Goals
- Applying theoretical knowledge to clinical diagnosis and treatment
- Clinical reasoning, history taking, and treatment-plan formulation
- Teamwork, communication, and self-directed learning skills
