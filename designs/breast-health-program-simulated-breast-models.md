# 90-minute breast health program with hands-on BSE practice using simulated breast models

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A 90-minute school-based breast health program consisting of a standardized lecture on breast cancer overview, facts, and American Cancer Society detection recommendations, a video on breast health and breast self-awareness, a BSE demonstration, and a hands-on activity in which "the participants in the treatment classrooms engaged in a hands-on activity with simulated breast models" in small groups of four, practicing BSE and identifying embedded lumps. The comparison classrooms received the same lecture and video plus a Wellness Inventory instead of the hands-on activity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A script was utilized to standardize the treatment and comparison groups so every class received exactly the same information and activities
- The researcher delivered all sessions personally to ensure consistency across classrooms and schools
#### Constraints
- The comparison group's extra time was filled with a Wellness Inventory discussion to equalize program time across conditions

### Target Learners
- adolescent females ages 15-18 enrolled in health education, health science, and child development classes

### Learning Goals
- breast health knowledge
- breast self-examination technique
- breast self-awareness
