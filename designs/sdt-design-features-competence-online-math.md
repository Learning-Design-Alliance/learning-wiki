# Design online mathematics courses with self-check buttons, prior knowledge sections, and chunked scaffolding to support competence

## Description
The article recommends three design features for improving competence in online mathematics courses: self-check buttons with informative and correctness feedback, prior knowledge explanations with self-check practice, and content chunking with scaffolding. These features are grounded in both quantitative and qualitative findings and are being integrated into the school's new Algebra 1 course.
