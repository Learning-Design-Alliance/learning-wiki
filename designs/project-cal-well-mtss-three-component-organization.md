# Organizing school mental health work as a multi-tiered system of supports with three Project Cal-Well components

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The district shifted toward "a multi-tiered system of supports (MTSS) approach, allowing it to implement a sustainable school mental health system." Work is organized across three components: Tier 1 universal supports for positive school climate and social-emotional skills; Tier 2 increased availability of and access to school-based mental health services; and Tier 3 partnerships and cross-system collaborations for youth well-being.
