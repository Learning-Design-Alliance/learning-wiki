# Simulation session structure pairing two complication cases with an immediate PEARLS debrief and a reinforcing didactic presentation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The session pattern runs two ~30-minute simulation cases, an immediate ~30-40 minute debrief, and a PowerPoint didactic distributed before or after: "The simulations and debrief required around 2 hours to complete; each case ran around 30 minutes, and the debrief sessions required around 30 minutes." During the debrief "learning objectives were reviewed, and any objectives that had required a prompt or had been missed were discussed in detail," and "The educational PowerPoint was used to augment any missed learning objectives." Critical actions tied to educational objectives were tracked throughout both simulations with prompts embedded so objectives would be completed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- One to two simulation instructors, ideally PEM providers with experience in simulation, to run the scenario and debrief
- A critical actions checklist modeled on institutional procedural sedation standards to track objective completion
#### Constraints
- Cases must be discussed and reviewed amongst the running physicians prior to simulation to help standardize scenarios across institutions
- Debriefing allotted time was around 40 minutes per simulation

### Target Learners
- emergency medicine residents
- PEM fellows
- advanced practice providers

### Learning Goals
- managing sedation complications
- presedation preparation and consent
- dosing of propofol and ketamine
