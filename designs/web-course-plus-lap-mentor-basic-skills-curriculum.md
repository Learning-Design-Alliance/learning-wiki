# Simple laparoscopy curriculum combining a web-based theory course with LAP Mentor virtual-re simulator basic-skills practice

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The intervention element is a two-part curriculum: a web-based theoretical course, Basics in Gynecological Laparoscopy, covering pelvic anatomy, instruments, the laparoscopic unit, energy sources, operative phases, operations and complications with a test, plus practical training on the LAP Mentor virtual reality simulator. "With the simulator, all the nine basic skills tasks were practiced five times each, and the rehearsals were recorded for further evaluation." The whole program was self-guided and available online to every resident in Finland.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Both the web course and the simulator training were required to be completed within a month before the assessment
#### Constraints
- The intervention comprised basic skills tasks only, without procedural modules

### Target Learners
- inexperienced junior residents in Obstetrics and Gynecology

### Learning Goals
- basic laparoscopic skills and theoretical knowledge of gynecological laparoscopy
