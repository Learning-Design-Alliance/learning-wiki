# Modular framework for building an introductory QISE course from interchangeable modules

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article organizes the first QISE course as a set of thirteen modules (Module 0 linear algebra through Module 12 quantum sensing modalities), each labeled E (accessible to any level) or A (advanced). The authors state they "present our recommendations for an introductory QISE course as a set of modules from which a course can be built" and tailored to local needs. Faculty exemplars in the article show different module orderings for first-year, senior, and graduate audiences.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Modules are combined in different sequences depending on program goals and the advanced courses offered beyond the introduction
#### Constraints
- Some modules, such as quantum noise, are labeled advanced and likely inappropriate for introductory students

### Target Learners
- undergraduate STEM students
- engineering majors

### Learning Goals
- foundational quantum information concepts
- quantum awareness for engineering students
