# HOPP (Health Oriented Pedagogical Project) dataset of 1711 Norwegian fifth-year school children

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Health Oriented Pedagogical Project (HOPP) is an ongoing quasi-experimental study (ClinicalTrials.gov NCT02495714) in which 9 Norwegian schools were allocated to intervention (n=7, with 225 minutes per week added physical activity and an activity-based learning component) or usual curriculum (n=2). The analyzed data comprised "outcomes from 1711 year 5 (11- and 12-year-old) children", with National Academic Tests (reading, mathematics, English averaged) and the Norwegian Inventory of Life Quality as outcomes, plus physical activity, fitness, strength, diet, and parental education predictors.
