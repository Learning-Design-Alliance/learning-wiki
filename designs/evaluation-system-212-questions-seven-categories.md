# Evaluation system with 212 questions in 7 categories providing immediate feedback

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The evaluation system provides continuous feedback and was elaborated to ensure instrumental and relational understanding and successful exams. After each lesson there are practical tasks, control questions measuring memorisation of text, definitions and theorems, and test questions measuring understanding. "There are 212 questions separated into 7 categories (dichotomous questions, multiply questions, ordinal questions, relationships analysis questions, matching questions, numerical questions and embedded questions)", with results and right answers given immediately, plus three trial tests after the course.
