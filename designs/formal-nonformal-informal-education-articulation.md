# Articulation of formal, non-formal and informal education distinguished by educational space

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article organizes its case around three educational modalities distinguished by space: school actions are formal, actions outside school are non-formal (museums, cultural centres, NGOs, events like MAES), and informal learning happens in family and friendship settings and through media. The author states that "a diferença entre educação formal, não formal e informal pode ser estabelecida a partir do espaço escolar". The framework is used to show how one school's astronomy project moved students across all three modalities, with formal learning as the base, non-formal settings as the site of research activity, and informal discussion as a consequence.
