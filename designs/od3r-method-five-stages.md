# The OD3R method: a five-stage sequence integrating lecture and laboratory work across cognitive, affective, and psychomotor domains

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The OD3R method is a student-centered instructional method for biochemistry consisting of five stages: orientation to generate motivation, developing/designing the investigation, conducting the investigation, reporting and discussing results, and reflecting on learning. The article states it "consists of 5 stages that integrate learning implementation and laboratory works; integrating assessments on cognitive, affective and psychomotor domains". It was applied to a biochemistry course on qualitative and quantitative analysis of proteins over 2-4 weeks with 2 meetings per week.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Observers trained to use evaluation instruments assess student activities, with each observer assigned to keep track of 10 students
#### Constraints
- The article states that to date there was no method integrating classroom learning and laboratory works in biochemistry learning implementation and assessment in Indonesia, positioning OD3R against cookbook-dominated laboratory practice

### Target Learners
- undergraduate chemistry and chemistry education students

### Learning Goals
- biochemistry concepts (proteins, amino acids, enzymes)
- practical laboratory skills
- scientific attitudes
- laboratory report writing
