# Progressive level difficulty adding charges, motion, rotation and gravity

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The game's level structure increases difficulty by layering molecular-science-relevant complications onto the docking task. 'There are currently 7 levels each with several rounds (or sub-levels)'; the first two levels are tutorial-based with one protein and hidden charges, then charges become visible and candidate counts grow, with the last level offering 4 possible candidates. Later levels add proteins moving on their own and a gravity effect pushing candidates toward the bottom, which the authors say 'reinforce[s] the peculiar physics and stochastic element that can be observed in nature in molecular interactions.'
