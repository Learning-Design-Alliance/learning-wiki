# High-fidelity simulation case embedding a certified medical interpreter in an eclamptic-seizure scenario for formative assessment of interpreter use

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A simulation case in which a 31-year-old Spanish-speaking postpartum woman presents with eclamptic seizures and her Spanish-speaking family member arrives seeking information, so the learner must request and properly use a certified medical interpreter to obtain the history. The article describes it as "a low-stakes, formative simulation assessment tool" with multisource assessment by a faculty observer and the interpreter, checklists, debriefing materials, and appendices including the case script, SP development tool, labs, assessment tools, and debriefing information.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- High-fidelity mannequin, simulation operator, nurse simulated participant, Spanish-speaking actor for the family member, certified Spanish medical interpreter, and faculty evaluator
- Briefing and walk-through training for interpreters, simulated participants, and faculty raters before assessment days
#### Constraints
- Designed for formative, low-stakes purposes; the authors state high-stakes use would require further validation of scoring and standardized evaluator training
- The underlying diagnosis may be too difficult for residents in unrelated specialties

### Target Learners
- Emergency medicine residents (PGY 1-3)

### Learning Goals
- Appropriate use of medical interpreter services
- Recognition and management of postpartum eclampsia
- Communication with a non-English-speaking family member and the health care team
