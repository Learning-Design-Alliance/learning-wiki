# Online primary angle closure education package (1.5-h recorded lecture with MCQs)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An accessible online education package hosted on the Moorfields learning portal, consisting of "a 1.5-h recorded lecture on primary angle closure disease that contained multiple-choice questions and links to relevant publications". It covered glaucoma classification, PACG mechanisms and risk factors, case-finding tests and their accuracy, LACD interpretation and application, evidence-based management, and referral guidelines. Completion was tracked electronically, and it was piloted for clarity by a learning and technology specialist and an optometrist.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered online via a learning portal with electronically tracked completion between assessments
#### Constraints
- Vignettes did not include patient history and cannot represent a dynamic slit-lamp examination; effect on real-world referrals not yet assessed

### Target Learners
- UK community-based optometrists in primary care

### Learning Goals
- Accurate grading and interpretation of limbal anterior chamber depth for primary angle closure case finding and referral
