# Team teaching (TT) technique with role behaviour

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article investigates "a form of practical classes – a social and psychological training that uses the team teaching (TT) technique". "Team teaching is a class that is held by two teachers or a teacher and a student at the same time", whose "main advantage is the addition of diversity and the improvement of effectiveness, since the teachers complement each other". Of the forms shown in Figure 4, "The most effective and efficient form of TT is the second one with role behaviour."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Two teachers, or a teacher and a student, conducting the class simultaneously
- Role behaviour in the most effective form
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness ranking of TT forms is the authors' assertion without printed test statistics

### Target Learners
- high school and university students

### Learning Goals

- intensive interaction and exchange of opinions
