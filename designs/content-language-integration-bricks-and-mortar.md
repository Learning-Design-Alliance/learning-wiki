# Language-embedded, project-based college-preparatory curriculum integrating content and language objectives

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
At Oakland International, all students learn English language skills alongside academic content within college-preparatory courses, using a project-based curriculum centered on students' lives and cultures. Teachers pair a content objective (the "bricks") with a language objective (the "mortar") in every subject, group students heterogeneously across grades and language backgrounds, and end units with performance-based assessments. The report notes that "by integrating English language objectives into each subject, students practice language skills while building and demonstrating their content knowledge."
