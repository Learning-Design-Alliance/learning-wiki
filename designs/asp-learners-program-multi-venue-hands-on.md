# ASP learners' program: rotating multi-strand hands-on physics outreach at distributed school venues

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The ASP learners' program is a week-long outreach design in which selected high schools serve as daily venues and learners congregate for targeted physics engagements. Up to four activity strands run in parallel, with rotations exposing learners to different activities. The report states that "The learners were organized into groups assigned to different activities, and in some cases, rotations were implemented so that learners were exposed to the different sets of activities presented." It drew about 1000 learners from up to forty high schools.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educational materials, most of which were shipped from abroad or brought in by the lecturers
- Venues and participants selected by the Local Organizing Committee with regional and national education authorities
#### Constraints
- Learners must be accessible at schools; the July main-session program reaches fewer learners because most are on school break

### Target Learners
- high school learners in the vicinity of the ASP venue

### Learning Goals
- develop and maintain interest in physics and related applications
