# Teacher guide for teaching sex education concepts via cognitive conflict strategy

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A teacher guide was prepared based on the cognitive conflict strategy for the Family Planning and Reproduction chapter. It includes an introduction, objective, significance, the unit's objective and significance, and a proposed teaching plan, plus an overview of the strategy's role in modifying sex education misconceptions. Each tailored lesson was formed to achieve learning outcomes and "include questions which measure misconceptions, evaluation and homework."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Lessons were reviewed by specialists in Science for accuracy, appropriateness to students' level, and consistency with the cognitive conflict strategy
#### Constraints
- Developed for the Family Planning and Reproduction chapter of the intermediate second-grade Science course in Jeddah

### Learning Goals
