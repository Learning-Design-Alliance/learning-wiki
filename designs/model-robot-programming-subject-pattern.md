# Model Robot Programming subject: constructivist project-based lessons with LEGO NXT robots in small groups

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A semester-long subject in which only about twenty percent of lessons use traditional teacher explanation and presentation; new material is introduced by examples, and students then "solve programming problems and tasks in groups of two-three members". The central activity is a group project to write an algorithm and program and build a robot controlled by that program, with documentation, video and slides presented to classmates and evaluated by teachers, other students and group members.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Real programmable devices (LEGO NXT robots)
- group work of two-three members
- teacher acting as facilitator rather than transferring knowledge
#### Constraints
- Traditional teaching methods are still retained for about twenty percent of the total lessons

### Target Learners
- college IT students who meet programming first at higher education

### Learning Goals
- basic programming structures
- problem solving
- collaborative knowledge building
