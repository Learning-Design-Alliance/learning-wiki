## Description
The course team developed a comprehensive assessment and evaluation system combining process evaluation and final evaluation from online and offline sources. Table 1 prints the weights: question-answer process in class 5%, chapter quiz 5%, topic discussion 10%, group activities 10%, clinical noviceship 10%, and final exam 60%, with forms spanning offline, online, and online + offline. Assessment content covers both low-order goals (knowledge, comprehension) and high-order goals (application, analysis, synthesis, evaluation), and feedback is used to adjust teaching strategies.
