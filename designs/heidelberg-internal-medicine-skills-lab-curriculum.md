# Heidelberg internal medicine skills-lab curriculum with mandatory, voluntary basic, and voluntary advanced session types

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The interdisciplinary longitudinal skills-lab curriculum at Heidelberg embeds internal medicine skills training in the 6th/7th term, using Peyton's Four-Stage Approach, role-play with context-relevant scenarios, and peer and faculty feedback. It comprises three course types: mandatory basic sessions (90 minutes, groups of up to six, 6:1 ratio), voluntary basic sessions whose "learning objectives are derived from the obligatory skills-lab training sessions and are relevant for the OSCE assessment" (5:1 ratio), and voluntary advanced sessions on specific complex topics held by faculty only.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Staff, simulators, infrastructure and consumables; the authors note skills-lab training requires extensive resources
#### Constraints
- Embedded in the obligatory internal medicine rotation at one medical faculty

### Target Learners
- third-year medical students in the internal medicine rotation

### Learning Goals
- clinical-technical skills acquisition, including OSCE-relevant procedural skills
