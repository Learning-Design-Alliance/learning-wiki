# VFT lesson structure alternating location visits with embedded on-the-spot movement and question prompts

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The demonstrated VFTs follow a reusable lesson-level pattern: pupils virtually travel between mapped locations, perform movements simulating actions during travel and at destinations, and answer questions and view media aligned to teaching objectives. Movement is integrated in travel between locations and in videos at locations, with pupils standing throughout the session, so the physical activity is woven into curriculum content rather than appended as a break.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Google Tour Builder or similar mapping software with a plotted journey; content aligned to teaching objectives
#### Constraints
- Grain size is the individual lesson session (around 10 minutes in the demonstrated version)

### Target Learners
- elementary school pupils

### Learning Goals
- curriculum-topic knowledge alongside moderate-to-vigorous movement
