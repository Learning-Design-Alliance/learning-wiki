# Use gamified humanoid-robot sessions to support therapeutic education where clinic barriers limit demonstration-based instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes that an engaging, entertaining technology such as a gamified session with a humanoid robot can support clinical practice for therapeutic education in pediatric asthma. It argues that outpatient education faces barriers including "limited time, followed by poor staff training, inadequate knowledge and above all the lack of demonstration material," and that "new technologies could represent a valid support to health workers in patient/family education."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A humanoid robot platform with a tablet and dialogue capability
- Content designed to catch children's attention and describe procedure steps in an amusing way
#### Constraints
- The proposed effectiveness has not been tested: the planned study was canceled due to the COVID-19 pandemic

### Target Learners
- children aged 6-11 diagnosed with asthma in outpatient care

### Learning Goals
- correct self-administration of inhalation therapy
- improved therapeutic adherence
