# Online competency-based mechanical ventilation course with virtual simulation (Xlung) for internal medicine residents

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
An 8-month online competency-based MV course built on 20 competencies adapted from a recommended MV competency guideline, organized into eight modules. Six modules combined a 1-hour live online meeting — "20 minutes of didactic lecture and 40 minutes for training using a virtual web-based simulator platform (Xlung)" — with self-directed learning material, retention quizzes, and Google Classroom delivery; two modules were exclusively self-directed. Engagement was reinforced through live meetings, an online platform, and group chats, and all residents received 1 month of deliberate simulator practice before the virtual SBA.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the Xlung virtual simulator platform and Google Classroom
- Live sessions limited to 1 hour, scheduled in the early evening to respect duty hours
#### Constraints
- Developed and delivered during the COVID-19 pandemic with all activities online
- Two modules were exclusively self-directed without live simulation

### Target Learners
- Internal medicine residents in a large university hospital residency program

### Learning Goals
- Applying mechanical ventilation knowledge to simulated ICU scenarios across 20 MV competencies
