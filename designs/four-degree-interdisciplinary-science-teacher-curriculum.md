# Progressive four-degree curriculum pattern: three radically interdisciplinary years yield a fundamental-education science-teacher degree, and each additional year of discipline-specific study yields a high-school biology, physics or chemistry teaching degree

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The curriculum is structured as a ladder of degrees: "O diploma de professor de ciências é obtido com a integralização de créditos oferecidos ao longo dos três primeiros anos do curso", and for each subsequent year a high-school teaching degree can be obtained, so that "o curso permite a obtenção de quatro diplomas". The nature-science components are entirely interdisciplinary in the first three years; discipline-specific biology, physics and chemistry courses are offered from the fourth year onward for the respective high-school teacher formation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The science-teacher degree requires the credits of the three initial years plus a curricular internship: "a realização de estágio curricular"
#### Constraints
- High-school teaching degrees require one further year of specific disciplines: "o aluno cursará um ano de disciplinas específicas (biologia, física ou química)"

### Target Learners
- undergraduate science-teacher education students

### Learning Goals
- certification as fundamental-education science teacher and as high-school biology, physics or chemistry teacher
