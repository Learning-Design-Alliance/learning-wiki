# Teacher Training Program for Gifted Education (TTPGE)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 40-hour certificate program for teachers covering “basic pedagogical formation, thinking-critical thinking and creative thinking training for gifted and talented students and the curriculum integration of these trainings.” Its content has three stages — gifted students, creative thinking, and critical thinking — delivered over two weekends by a university professor and an expert, with a booklet and pre-testing at each practice. The aim is to enable teachers to identify gifted children, learn appropriate methods, and prepare enriched and differentiated lesson plans.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- instructors with degrees and field experience in gifted education
- pre-testing of participants to analyze readiness and needs
#### Constraints
- short-time format was chosen so teachers from other cities could attend and nonattendance prevented

### Target Learners
- kindergarten, elementary and secondary school teachers

### Learning Goals
- identifying gifted children
- differentiated and enriched course content
- integrating thinking skills into curriculum
