# Pro-forma combined laboratory manual/notebook (logbook) for inquiry laboratories

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A combined laboratory manual and notebook ('logbook') inspired by scaffolded pro-forma notebooks used at Imperial College London. It contains the printed introductory material and the full derivation and uncertainty propagation formulae, followed by structured pages with headers, empty tables for the first session, and blank pages with explanations and reminders. The authors created it after noting that with traditional scripts "students can spend the entire of the ﬁrst session reading, distilling and even copying out large sections of the script into their laboratory notebooks, which is a complete waste of time". Preparatory activities are incorporated into the logbooks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Derivation and uncertainty formulae included in the introductory section so the method can be inferred
- Completion of preparatory activities confirmed at the start of the session by whole-group discussion
#### Constraints
- Developed for a two-session experiment; the authors note laboratory time was precious with only seven first-year sessions

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate physics students

### Learning Goals
- experimental record keeping
- developing an experimental method
