# Comprehensive principal preparation programs combine recruitment, coherent curriculum, applied learning, mentoring, and cohorts

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The report defines "comprehensive principal preparation programs" as those incorporating features identified in Preparing Leaders for a Changing World: "close partnerships between districts and programs; proactive recruitment into the program; well-supported cohorts and/or networks for collegial learning; a coherent curriculum enacted through applied learning (e.g., internships longer than 20 weeks with a mentor, action research or inquiry projects, field-based projects); and a focus on leading instruction, developing the organization, developing people, and managing change." Studies of such programs examine principals' self-reports of preparation, teachers' views of leadership, and student outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- District–program partnerships
- proactive recruitment
- cohort or network structures
- applied learning including extended internships with mentors
- curriculum focused on instruction, organization, people, and change
#### Constraints
- All studies meeting the review's criteria focused on programs incorporating all or many of these critical features, so findings may not generalize to programs lacking them
