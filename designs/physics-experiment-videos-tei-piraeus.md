# A collection of videos of physics experiments that guide students to measure and analyse data to calculate physical quantities

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article presents three videos (fluid viscosity, sound velocity, gamma-ray attenuation) produced in the physics lab of TEI Piraeus with a common digital camcorder and a free video processor. Each video includes "a short introductory part with the relevant theory", "the experimental procedure based on a specific scenario for each experiment" and "the appropriate set of instructions to get the necessary measurements". They are freely available online and target first-year undergraduates with a medium physics background and secondary school students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Videos were produced with a common digital camcorder, a free video processor and the collaboration of students
#### Constraints
- Only a minority of available experimental-physics videos allow quantitative estimation of physical parameters; these videos were designed to fill that gap

### Target Learners
- first year undergraduates with a medium background in physics
- secondary school students

### Learning Goals
- collect and analyse measurement data
- calculate physical quantities such as viscosity, sound velocity and attenuation coefficient
