# Kern County regional BLA workforce planning pilot

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A regional BLA workforce planning pilot that "brought together county, district, and educator preparation program partners in Kern County for a series of planning workshops in 2025 to examine recruitment and retention data and identify workforce strategies." The pilot is presented alongside the EL-WIN initiative as a scalable, replicable proof point demonstrating how districts, counties, and preparation programs can jointly analyze and coordinate supply and demand to address critical workforce needs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Partnership among county offices, districts, and educator preparation programs; internal collection of workforce data where state systems do not provide it
#### Constraints
- The pilot county office of education collects some data only for some districts, and pilot districts have varied internal collection practices

### Target Learners
- Multilingual Learners in Kern County bilingual instructional programs

### Learning Goals
- expanding access to bilingual instructional programs that improve student achievement
