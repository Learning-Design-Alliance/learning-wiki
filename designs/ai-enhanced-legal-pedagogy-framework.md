# AI-Enhanced Legal Pedagogy Framework integrating constructivism, TAM, and TPACK

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes an integrated framework in which constructivist learning theory, the Technology Acceptance Model, and the TPACK framework are not parallel layers but "constrain and inform one another at specific theoretical junctures". Constructivism governed the staged 16-week intervention design via the ZPD trajectory from other-regulated to self-regulated performance; TAM governed the measurement framework and SEM analysis; TPACK governed instructor preparation and implementation consistency. Cross-constraints include TPACK's Technological Content Knowledge shaping TAM's Perceived Usefulness, and TAM's Behavioral Intention feeding back into constructivist engagement.
