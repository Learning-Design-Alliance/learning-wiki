# Hypothesis-driven physical exam workshop pattern: vignette-based differential diagnosis generation, maneuver selection with justification, and supervised peer practice

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
This lesson-level pattern organizes a clinical-skills workshop around five clinical vignettes. For each vignette, students "worked in pairs to brainstorm differential diagnoses, determine the relevant HDPE maneuvers to perform, and establish a rationale for performing each maneuver," then joined a facilitator-led small-group discussion on diagnostic approach, indicated maneuvers, and expected findings, and finally practiced selected maneuvers in pairs while the facilitator circulated with feedback. A student worksheet structures differential generation and maneuver justification, and a facilitator guide supplies suggested diagnoses, maneuvers, and rationale.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A facilitator preparation session reviewing goals, format, and small-group facilitation
- A student worksheet with differential prompts and maneuver-justification tables
- A facilitator guide with suggested diagnoses, maneuvers, rationale, and timeline
- One facilitator per nine to 10 students for direct observation and feedback
#### Constraints
- Requires facilitator availability and adequate classroom/clinical skills facility space
- Developed at a single institution, which could limit generalizability to institutions with different curricular structures

### Target Learners
- Rising third-year medical students entering clerkships

### Learning Goals
- Generate a differential diagnosis from a case vignette
- Select and justify physical exam maneuvers based on the differential
- Perform focused PE maneuvers with proper technique
