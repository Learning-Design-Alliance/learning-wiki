# Use mHealth tools as a complement to, not a replacement for, traditional mental health counseling

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article argues that mHealth applications should extend rather than substitute for traditional therapy: "mHealth apps, despite their effectiveness, are not replacements but rather complementary to traditional mental health therapies". mHELP embodied this by pairing self-management tools with seamless access to telehealth counseling, extending continuity of care beyond limited therapy sessions and helping students apply skills learned in therapy to daily life.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to appropriate mental health professionals through an integrated telehealth platform
#### Constraints
- There is no strong evidence that mHealth apps are more effective than traditional therapy

### Target Learners
- college students facing barriers to traditional mental health care

### Learning Goals
- sustained stress, anxiety, and depression self-management alongside professional care
