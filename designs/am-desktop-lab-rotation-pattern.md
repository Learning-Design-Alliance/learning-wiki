# Lab rotation pattern: teams complete pre-processing, printing, post-processing, and inspection on desktop machines

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A recurring laboratory session structure in which small student teams operate desktop FDM and SLA printers through a full AM workflow. The article prints the structure directly: "In each lab session, teams of students complete an outlined exercise involving part pre-processing using the machine software, printing (while discussing and observing the machine operation), post-processing, and inspection." Labs sit on shared workbenches with a computer, FDM printer, SLA printer with post-processing setup, 3D scanner, USB microscope, and hand tools, and are paired with lectures on the corresponding process so students relate process capabilities to machine architecture.
