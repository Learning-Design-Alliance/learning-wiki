# Three-step progressive lab session from model RC circuit to single-channel recording

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The laboratory session is divided into three successive steps corresponding to a progressive approach of the topic: Step 1 has students compute and measure capacitive and resistive currents for a model RC circuit; Step 2 has them form a lipid bilayer and measure its capacitance and resistance, showing it is electrically equivalent to an RC circuit; Step 3 has them record gramicidin A unitary currents, observing that certain molecules form pores acting as electrical switches at the molecular level. Each step is framed by a guiding question and explicit learning goals in the student hand-out.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A brief 15-20 minute introductory lecture on membrane electrical properties and voltage clamp; one instructor for two groups of two students is described as an optimal configuration
#### Constraints
- The session typically lasts four hours and requires one bilayer setup per pair of students

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students in basic neuroscience training

### Learning Goals
- understanding the membrane as a leaky capacitor (RC circuit)
- understanding negative feedback in voltage clamp
- observing single-molecule ionic channel function
