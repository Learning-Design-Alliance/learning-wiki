# Fixation-star lexical processing task with noun and verb trials

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A variant of Fernald et al.'s (1998) lexical processing task in which, during the Response phase, 'the two images or videos disappeared, and a centrally positioned yellow star appeared, during which children heard the test query'. The two scenes then reappeared with a 'ding' sound to facilitate coding. The task included 13 trials with 7 nouns and 6 verbs, with noun referents shown as static images and verb referents as dynamic videos of people performing everyday actions. Latency was coded manually from video at 30 frames per second as the time from the onset of the 'ding' to the first look to the target scene. Because children look at the center at word onset, latency can be measured on all trials.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Children must be able to view the two scenes side-by-side and hear the auditory test query; the design requires that children are looking at the center of the screen at word onset so latency of the first saccade toward the target can be measured on all trials.
#### Constraints
- Latencies are measured from the onset of when the scenes reappear after word onset rather than from word onset, so they are 'not directly comparable to those in other studies'.

### Target Learners
- typically developing 36-month-old children

### Learning Goals
- receptive vocabulary and lexical processing speed for nouns and verbs
