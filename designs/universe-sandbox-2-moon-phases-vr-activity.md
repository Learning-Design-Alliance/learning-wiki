# Universe Sandbox 2-based VR activity on Moon phases and eclipses

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A self-developed interactive VR activity built in the Universe Sandbox 2 software package because no package of VR astronomy lessons existed. It models the Sun, Earth and Moon, begins with precursor tasks to acclimate students to the goggles and controllers, and then provides "an interactive scenario that modeled the Sun, Earth and Moon" in which "the student would interact with these objects to perform tasks and answer a series of questions". It was the VR condition in the pilot comparison and was completed in pairs under a 20-minute HMD limit.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Precursor tasks for students to get used to the VR goggles and controllers
- An interactive Sun-Earth-Moon scenario with embedded tasks and questions
- Staffing to work with a few students at a time, in series rather than in parallel
#### Constraints
- The authors note the technology itself might become a distraction for students and inhibit learning of the material
- VR students received an initial standard lecture first because it was unknown whether VR alone would disadvantage them on exams

### Target Learners
- Introductory college astronomy (ASTRO 101) students

### Learning Goals
- Understanding Moon phases and eclipses
