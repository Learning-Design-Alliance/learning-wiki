# Integrated STEM education programme with engineering design activities for 6th-grade science units

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An integrated STEM implementation delivered within the 6th-grade science programme across the units of force and motion, light and sound, electrical conduction, and matter and heat, lasting 22 hours over 5 weeks after a 1-week pilot. The article states that "The students were asked to design proposals for solutions of the problem and to design these solutions, and the designs were transformed into products and the engineering processes of the students were evaluated." A sample activity, 'I design a racing car', followed the engineering design cycle within the velocity topic.
