# Affective-environment approaches: Humanistic teaching and Suggestopedia frame humor and song as anxiety-reducing aids to language learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article situates humor and song within two approaches that prioritize a relaxed classroom atmosphere. Humanistic teaching "tries to secure a positive atmosphere before a learner begins an effort", with eradication of anxiety as a milestone. Suggestopedia uses "a relaxation session for unconscious assimilation of the lesson materials", in which music increases self-esteem and energizes learners. The study's humorous-song treatment is framed as an application of this affective perspective, chosen because the indirect, environment-focused perspective on humor has proven more fruitful in measurable effects.
