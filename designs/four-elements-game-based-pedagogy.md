# Four-element framework of game-based pedagogy: cognitive, behavioral, social, and affective engagement

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article designs its game by observing four elements of game-based pedagogy. "Cognitive engagement encourages students to think analytically, apply their knowledge, and adapt to evolving situations in the game by stimulating cognitive processes through presenting challenging patient safety situations in the surgical unit." Behavioral engagement shapes active participation and problem-solving; social engagement operates through peer interaction, group play, and competition; and the affective element uses curiosity, excitement, points, and competition to make learning enjoyable and motivating.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Game scenarios presenting challenging patient safety situations
- Group play structures enabling peer interaction and competition
- Points and riddles to create curiosity and motivation
#### Constraints
- Described and applied in the context of surgical unit patient safety education

### Target Learners
- Surgical nursing students and nurses

### Learning Goals
- Critical thinking and decision-making about patient safety risks
- Engagement and collaboration in safety training
