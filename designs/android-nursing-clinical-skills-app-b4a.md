# Android nursing clinical-skills training software with nine skills, videos, offline access, and engagement tracking

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A researcher-developed Android application, built with B4A, whose educational content "includes nine nursing clinical skills" presented as text with instructional images and videos. It supports Android versions 5 and above, enables online and offline video downloading, requires registration with a personal password, includes a gallery, a question submission section, a chat-room-like feedback section, search over procedures and medications, and functionality to track user engagement. Content was validated by faculty and IT experts before use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Users must register and create a personal password for login and installation
- Content validity was confirmed through feedback from a panel of experts before the software was designed
#### Constraints
- Supports Android versions 5 and above only

### Target Learners
- 6th-semester nursing students and 8th-semester nursing interns

### Learning Goals
- Nine basic nursing clinical skills, including vital signs, dressing change, serum preparation, medicine drawing, IV insertion, injections, suctioning, heart auscultation, and nasogastric tube insertion
