# Group Dynamics Theory as the framework for classroom-based L2 instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Group Dynamics Theory (GDT) deals with "the processes that occur between members of a group in a learni ng situation" and casts light on learners' behavior in a social system. The article identifies four major components: group cohesiveness, group norms, group leadership style, and group size. It uses GDT as the focal pillar motivating GDOI, arguing pair work and group work create motivation and chances of language production rather than individualistic learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Group cohesiveness is necessary for the development of group dynamics, built from perceived similarity and mutual acceptance
#### Constraints
- The article notes relationships do not always go well and group members may experience conflicts and constant arguments, which can make classes difficult for instructors

### Target Learners
- intermediate EFL learners in language institutes

### Learning Goals
- L2 speaking ability
- willingness to communicate
