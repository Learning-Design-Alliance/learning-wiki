# The Recognising, Diagnosing and Managing Dementia in General Practice workshop

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A tailored continuing-medical-education workshop developed by the Wicking Dementia Research and Education Centre in response to the expressed absence of appropriate dementia-related content in GP registrar training. The article states "The GPR workshop consists of a 3-hour face-to-face presentation delivered by medical educators focusing on (1) recognising and diagnosing dementia and (2) managing dementia in general practice"; supervisors attend a 2-hour modified version designed to help them teach registrars this content. It provides a framework for decision-making, tools and resources, a strong focus on lived experience situating people with dementia and their carers as central, and considers diagnosis and management through a biopsychosocial lens.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered face to face by medical educators at regional training offices, augmenting the apprenticeship-model interaction between supervisors and registrars.
#### Constraints
- There is more in-depth coverage of some aspects of dementia diagnosis and management in the registrars' workshop than in the supervisors' workshop.

### Target Learners
- General practitioner registrars
- General practitioner supervisors

### Learning Goals
- Recognising and diagnosing dementia
- Managing dementia in general practice
- Supporting timely and supportive engagement with diagnosis
