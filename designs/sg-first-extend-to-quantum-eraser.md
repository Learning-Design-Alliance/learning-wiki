# In Stern-Gerlach-first courses, extend the analyzer-loop treatment to delayed-choice quantum-eraser material to reinforce superposition, measurement, and complementarity

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that instructors in the Stern-Gerlach-first paradigm go beyond textbook coverage by adding the delayed-choice quantum-eraser variant early in the course. "Covering this material, allows the instructor to reinforce notions of changes of basis functions, quantum superpositions, quantum measurement, and the complementarity principle as expressed in whether we know “which-way” information or not." It also argues this dispels common misconceptions about when a measurement occurs and when a system is in a superposition of states.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students already have the prerequisite knowledge from current textbook coverage of the Stern-Gerlach experiment
#### Constraints
- The material presumes the Stern-Gerlach-first paradigm; in conventional historical-order courses it would come later in the curriculum
