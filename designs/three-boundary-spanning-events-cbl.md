# Three boundary-spanning events linking field data to community impact (field co-design, collaborative alignment, mutual validation)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The CBL course is structured as three structured occasions traversing institutional interfaces: Week 3 field co-design generating museum digital assets, Week 4 collaborative alignment of proposals with four SDGs (4, 8, 11, 17), and Week 7 mutual validation where "validation criteria are negotiated between stakeholders." The pattern links museum field data through student-created materials to intended community outcomes, followed by co-dissemination via open-source release and student co-authorship.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A community partner selected for geographic proximity, mission alignment, and expressed need
- Boundary-spanning events at planned weeks of the course
#### Constraints
- Validated in one 7-week course with two students and one museum

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate students in community-based learning courses

### Learning Goals
- Participatory design with community stakeholders
- Aligning technical projects with sustainable development goals
