# Example AI project modules: weather forecasting, gene-function prediction, and Supreme Court case clustering

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes three developed modules. A math or environmental science module lets students build a weather forecast model using National Weather Service data (precipitation, wind speed, humidity, UV index), choose variables and algorithms for their prediction target, and optionally host a webapp in HTML and Python. A biology module trains an AI on genomic data to predict gene function from base pair sequence, applying the central dogma, amino acid interactions, and protein structure. A humanities module has students read Supreme Court cases and analyze an AI algorithm clustering the cases by topic against historical events.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to relevant datasets and, for the weather module, optional web hosting; humanities modules use pre-built models rather than model building
#### Constraints
- Humanities modules shift focus from building AI algorithms to exploring results of pre-built models

### Target Learners
- high school students in math, environmental science, biology, and humanities classes

### Learning Goals
- weather and climate topics
- genomics and the central dogma
- historical analysis of Supreme Court cases
- AI fundamentals
