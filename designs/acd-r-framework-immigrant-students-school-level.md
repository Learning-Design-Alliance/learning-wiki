# The ACD-R framework applied to immigrant students at school level

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The article's running head names "THE ACD-R FRAMEWORK AMONG IMMIGRANT STUDENTS", an academic and cultural demands–resources framework in which school-level academic demands (resource shortage, hindered learning), academic resources (student assistance), cultural demands (immigrant socioeconomic disadvantage) and cultural resources (cultural learning, shared cultural values, immigrant language support) are modelled as predictors of immigrant students' motivation (self-efficacy, valuing) and achievement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- School-level PISA indices for each demands/resources factor must be available to instantiate the framework
#### Constraints
- As applied here, the framework operates at the school level among immigrant students using PISA 2018 data

### Target Learners
