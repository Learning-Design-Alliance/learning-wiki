# Knowledge–Learning–Instruction (KLI) framework as an organizing theory for AI instructional design

## Description
The KLI framework connects knowledge components, learning processes, and instructional principles to support robust learning. The article states that "effective instruction requires careful alignment among three core elements": Knowledge Components as the goals of learning, Learning Processes describing how learning occurs, and Instructional Principles as the methods facilitating those processes. The authors use KLI to guide the reasoning of their multi-agent systems, grounding AI instructional design in an evidence-based instructional theory rather than leaving pedagogy to user prompts.
