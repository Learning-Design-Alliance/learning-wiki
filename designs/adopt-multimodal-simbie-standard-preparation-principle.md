# Adopt multimodal TeamSTEPPS-based SimBIE with preparation and co-debriefing as standard preparation for undergraduate health care professionals

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors recommend integrating TeamSTEPPS with medical movies, MOOCs, 3D computer-based or VR-based SimBIE training, and co-debriefing as standard preparation of undergraduate health care professionals: "We recommend adopting these novel instructional designs as standard preparation of undergraduate health care professionals for better clinical practice to improve patient and personnel safety and patient outcomes." The recommendation rests on the study's significant mTPOT gains in the prepared arms versus control.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Preparatory medical movie and MOOC materials with certification quizzes
- 3D computer-based or VR simulation scenarios aligned with TeamSTEPPS objectives
- Structured multiprofessional co-debriefing
#### Constraints
- VR format significantly increases simulator sickness (SSQ Δ=21.02, P=.001), a stated limitation for learners susceptible to cybersickness
- Evidence comes from a single-center quasi-experimental study with stratified convenience sampling rather than random allocation

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate health care professional students in emergency care settings

### Learning Goals
- Interprofessional collaboration and team performance for patient and personnel safety
