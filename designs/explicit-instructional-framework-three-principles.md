# NS1 explicit instructional framework built on three interrelated design principles

## Description
NS1's instructional framework applies explicit instruction principles drawn from meta-analyses of interventions for low achievers and students with mathematics difficulties. The article states that "The explicit instructional framework of NS1 centers on the following three interrelated design principles": instructional scaffolding with progressive fading of supports, opportunities for guided practice with immediate specific academic feedback and review, and differentiated support to learners. The framework aims to present new information in a manner that is unambiguous and easy to understand.
