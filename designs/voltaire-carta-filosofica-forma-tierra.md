# Voltaire's philosophical letter as historical reading on the shape of the Earth

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A primary historical text read aloud to the whole class after students record their own ideas: Voltaire's fourteenth philosophical letter contrasting English Newtonian and French Cartesian views of the universe, including the shape of the Earth ('En París, os figuráis la Tierra hecha como un melón; en Londres, está aplastada por los dos lados'). The article uses it to situate the scientific debate about Earth's figure and to show that the Newtonian model prevailed.
