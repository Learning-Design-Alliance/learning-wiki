## Description
The article distills its interview findings into an organizing framework of three principles: "1. Train a new generation of interdisciplinary scientists"; "2. Implement an innovative and interdisciplinary pedagogical approach"; and "3. Provide a professional, student-centered, training program". These principles guided selection and arrangement of pedagogical activities, course content organization, mandatory internships, and modalities of learning, teaching, supervision and evaluation. The framework links a needs analysis to concrete curriculum structures.
