# The Four Noble Truths (FNT) method: a four-stage Buddhist-principled instruction method comparable to the scientific method

## Description
The FNT method, developed by Saroj Boasri, applies Buddhist principles for instruction with systematic thinking procedures the article describes as "comparable to the scientific method". It has four stages — suffering, cause of suffering, cessation of suffering, and the way to the cessation of suffering — emphasizing problem solving by oneself. In this study it structures environmental education lessons on global warming, with stages operationalized as setting the problem, formulating hypotheses, experimenting/collecting data, and discussing/concluding.
