# Geometer's Sketchpad angle-identification activity sequence for Year Three pupils

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention consisted of six lessons in which Year Three students used two pre-constructed Geometer's Sketchpad activities per angle concept, one on visualizing the angle and one on identifying its properties. The article states the activities were important "for the students to visualize the angles and identify the properties of angles." The control group completed the same six activities without the software. Posttests followed each lesson pair.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An introductory lesson familiarizing students with the Geometer's Sketchpad before instruction, plus pre-constructed activities
#### Constraints
- Pilot work with Year Six pupils showed students "needed more time to get familiar with the software", so the researcher adjusted time and simplified materials for Year 3

### Target Learners
- Year Three primary students (nine years old) learning angles

### Learning Goals
- van Hiele Level 1 (visualizing and recognizing angles) and Level 2 (identifying properties of right, acute, and obtuse angles)
