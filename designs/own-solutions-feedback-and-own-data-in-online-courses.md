# Design online recitations around graded feedback on students' own solutions and online laboratories around students gathering their own data

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article concludes that "graded feedback followed by online discussions turned out to be more eﬀective (in the students' perspective) than handed solutions", and that "students value the possibility of gathering own data" in online physics laboratories. Gathering own data can be accomplished via remote controlled labs or, more easily, videos or simulations of experiments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Making the solution process visible (reconstruction) requires technical resources, e.g. tablet computer with pen input
- Gathering own data from a distance requires remote controlled labs, videos, or simulations from experiments
#### Constraints
- When secondhand data is used, the article advises clarifying its origin, e.g. with a detailed description of the measurement including photographs of the experimental setup

### Target Learners
- university physics students in online recitation and laboratory courses

### Learning Goals
- problem-solving skills supported by feedback on students' own solutions
- experimental skills and content reinforcement through gathering and analyzing own measurement data
