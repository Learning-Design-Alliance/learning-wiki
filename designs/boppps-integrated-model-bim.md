# The BOPPPS-integrated model (BIM): a modular instructional framework integrating PBL, TBL, and CBL into BOPPPS sessions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
BIM is a student-centered instructional model that maps PBL, TBL, and CBL onto the six BOPPPS sessions. As the article states, it "systematically integrates PBL and CBL into bridge-in and summary sessions to contextualize learning and reinforce core concepts; PBL into pre-assessment and post-assessment sessions"; and PBL, TBL, and CBL into participatory learning sessions to promote collaboration, knowledge integration, and practical skill development. It was developed through the ADDIE cycle and implemented in the Water Hygiene chapter, with peer evaluation after group presentations. The framework is motivated by limitations of each method used alone: TBL may diminish individual accountability, PBL might lack comprehensive knowledge integration, and CBL may hinder knowledge transfer due to its context-specific nature.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty with advanced instructional design skills and expertise in student-centered instructional methods
- Additional resources such as case libraries and technological support, plus online teaching platforms for pre- and post-assessment
#### Constraints
- Integrating multiple instructional methods within the BOPPPS framework presents a complex challenge

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate public health students

### Learning Goals
- Information literacy, expressive communication, teamwork, summarization, self-directed learning, objective evaluation
