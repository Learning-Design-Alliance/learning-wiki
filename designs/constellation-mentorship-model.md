# Holistic, non-dyadic constellation mentorship model with mentor nodes, mentors, peers, and mentees

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article presents a holistic, non-dyadic (constellation) mentorship model as a network-based alternative to traditional dyadic mentorship, in which mentees can also be mentors and have peer groups at each level. Its Figure 2 "describes a robust holistic, non-dyadic mentorship model, combining elements from both of the excellent figures presented above," including "mentor nodes", long-term mentor slots emphasizing academic and personal growth, peer slots to reduce othering and increase belonging, and mentee slots to develop leadership and physics identity, all surrounding a central self. Spectrum develops its training materials and mentorship model after this framework.
