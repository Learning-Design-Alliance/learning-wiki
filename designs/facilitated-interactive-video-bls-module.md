# Facilitated interactive video BLS training module (AI-assisted animated video with embedded pauses and real-time instructor feedback)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 40-minute facilitated session built around a 4-minute-45-second interactive animated instructional video covering the adult BLS algorithm, CPR technique, and AED use. The video was produced with an AI-assisted workflow (Google Gemini for scene images, Seedance for animation) under full investigator control, and delivered "with embedded pauses, learner responses, and instructor feedback". It was evaluated as an adjunct to a standardized lecture against simulation-based training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered with real-time instructor feedback during scheduled facilitated sessions
- Content reviewed for guideline alignment by BLS-certified instructors
#### Constraints
- Participants were not given the opportunity to rewatch the video during the study period
- AI tools were used solely for visual asset generation, not educational content

### Target Learners
- fourth-year medical students

### Learning Goals
- adult basic life support knowledge and skills, including CPR and AED use
