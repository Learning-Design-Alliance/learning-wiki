# GPT-4o-simulated beginner-student system for learning by teaching in English vocabulary acquisition

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The proposed system casts GPT-4o as a conversational partner role-playing a beginner English learner. Teaching materials are erroneous English sentences generated by GPT-4o from a keyword prompt; 'the learner corrects English sentences by replacing incorrect words or idioms with correct ones and explains the reasons for these corrections.' The system was 'designed to simulate a beginner in English by instructing GPT-4o through prompts,' generating follow-up questions from the wrong sentence and the learner's explanation in a repeating question-answer loop.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- GPT-4o access with prompts instructing the model to role-play a beginner English learner and to generate erroneous sentences, reasons, and corrected words
- A web application developed with Vite+React and FastAPI through which learners enter explanations and answers
#### Constraints
- The temperature hyperparameter of GPT-4o was set to zero and the prompts did not strongly emphasize adapting to the learner's responses, producing repeated questions
- The maximum number of corrections for incorrect sentences was set to five, and LbT time was set to three minutes

### Target Learners
- English vocabulary learners (university students in the experiment)

### Learning Goals
- Acquisition and retention of English vocabulary and idioms
