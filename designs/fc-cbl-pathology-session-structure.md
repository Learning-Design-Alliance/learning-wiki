# Flipped classroom with case-based learning session structure for undergraduate pathology

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A lesson-level design in which students study preprovided materials (learning purposes, annotated PowerPoints, web-based video lectures, typical case handouts) one week before class, prepare their own PPTs, then in pre-assigned groups of 7–8 present, discuss real clinical cases not disclosed until class, and receive instructor guidance and summary. The article states students "collaborated to take turns interpreting and discussing the real clinical cases proposed by instructors."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Preprovided course materials on the course website one week before class
- Pre-assigned small groups (n = 7–8)
- The same instructor and consistent content across compared formats
#### Constraints
- The article notes FC "consumes generous human, material and financial resources," including more faculty time preparing videos, PPTs and cases; only 2 textbook sections were implemented in this pilot

### Target Learners
- third-year undergraduate medical students majoring in clinical medicine

### Learning Goals
- understanding functional and structural changes of disease
- clinical case analysis and higher-order cognitive abilities
- teamwork
