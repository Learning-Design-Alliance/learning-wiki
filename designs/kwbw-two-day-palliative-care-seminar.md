# Two-day compacted palliative medicine seminar within the KWBW VerbundweiterbildungPLUS post-graduate GP training programme

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A "tightly scheduled comprehensive course in palliative medicine" delivered as eight teaching units of about 45 minutes within a two-day seminar for up to 30 GP trainees, run eight times in 2017. Day one covered palliative care in the domestic environment, organisation of patient care and symptom control (pain, dyspnoea) in interactive seminars; day two covered adjuvant therapeutic strategies and patient decrees. Seminars used case-based discussions with lecturers' and trainees' own cases, and a hidden curriculum aimed to establish an affirmative attitude towards end-of-life care.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Three lecturers each with more than 20 years of GP experience, an additional palliative care qualification, ongoing primary care work and membership of a specialised ambulatory palliative care team
#### Constraints
- The programme could offer each participant only up to 36 hours of seminar per year due to voluntary participation and low financial resources

### Target Learners
- Post-graduate GP trainees (residents) in vocational training in Baden-Württemberg, Germany

### Learning Goals
- Basic knowledge in supporting patients during end-of-life care in general practice, including symptom control, organisation of care, communication, and legal framework conditions
