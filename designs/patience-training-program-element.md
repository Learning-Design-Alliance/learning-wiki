# Patience Training Program (activity-based, 110 activities over 5 weeks)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An activity-based, participatory training program developed from the literature and Patience Scale items, with two objectives covering understanding the value of patience and showing patient behavior in daily life, social relationships, and difficult situations. As the article describes it, "an activity based participatory patience training program was applied with a total of 110 activities for 5 weeks to the experimental group". Each session ended with students sharing impressions and plans to apply outcomes in daily life, with homework application between sessions.
