# Genetics e-learning CPD module on oncogenetics for general practitioners

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 2-hour online continuing professional development module on oncogenetics for primary-care physicians, containing "multimedia presentations, interactive cases with feedback, enabling tools (for referral, family history and online information searches)" and step-by-step clinical practice guides. Its contents were designed to include 10 items prioritized in a multidisciplinary Delphi study on core competencies of health professionals endorsed by the NHG. Participants could freely revisit program sections, and the module was designed to enable completion within 2 hours.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Designed to enable completion within 2 hours
- Content based on a prior multidisciplinary Delphi needs assessment of core competencies endorsed by the NHG
#### Constraints
- Developed for the Dutch general-practice context and the NHG's usual online CPD presentation style

### Target Learners
- General practitioners in family practice

### Learning Goals
- Identify patients with an inherited predisposition to cancer
- Apply oncogenetics guidelines in identifying patients for whom referral is indicated
- Explain the possibilities and limitations of oncogenetic testing
