# Double-slit experiment QuILT: a guided-inquiry tutorial with pre-test, warm-up, simulation-based main tutorial, homework, and post-test

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The DSE QuILT is a Quantum Interactive Learning Tutorial built from a cognitive task analysis of the underlying concepts and common student difficulties. Its "guided approach to learning" sequence is a pre-test, a warm-up reviewing the de Broglie relation and wave nature of particles, a main tutorial, an associated homework component connecting conceptual and mathematical aspects, and an identical post-test. The main tutorial uses "an interactive computer simulation in which students can manipulate the DSE setup and observe the resulting pattern", with students predicting the on-screen pattern, checking predictions with the simulation, and reconciling differences before proceeding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administered in class in small groups with the pre-test before the warm-up and main tutorial, homework finished individually at home, and an identical post-test after all components
#### Constraints
- The version evaluated was administered over a short time frame without additional in-class instruction

### Target Learners
- Upper-level undergraduate quantum mechanics students
- First-year physics graduate students

### Learning Goals
- Understanding wave-particle duality and which-path information in the double-slit experiment
- Understanding how photon wavelength and intensity affect the interference pattern
- Connecting conceptual and mathematical descriptions of the double-slit experiment
