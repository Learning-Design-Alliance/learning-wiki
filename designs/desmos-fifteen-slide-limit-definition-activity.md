# Desmos interactive activity sequence of fifteen slides in three phases for discovering the formal definition of limit

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A web-based dynamic geometry activity set developed by the instructor in Desmos for a Differential Calculus class. "The activities contain fifteen slides" organized into introduction, main, and closure sections: warming up on the informal definition and needed algebra, guided practice choosing an appropriate delta with dynamic graphics whose sliders visualize the implications of the chosen delta, and an appreciation closure. Students worked in groups of three to four, one laptop per group, with group progress displayed to the class for comparison and revision.
