# A 6-10 week personal video-analysis performance task guided by the 8 practices of science

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The pattern is a multi-week performance task in which every Grade 9 student conducts a personal video analysis using Tracker, guided by the 8 practices of science (ask question, use models, plan and carry out investigation, analyse and interpret data, use mathematical and computational thinking, construct explanations, discuss from evidence, communicate information). The authors situate it in "actual students' work" and assess it with rubrics worth 20% of exam score under an authentic scientist scenario.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- access to Tracker and video recording of a moving object
- assessment rubrics communicating expected performance indicators
- weekly consultation time-table with teachers
#### Constraints
- the authors note there was not enough time for teacher professional development on the Tracker Dynamics model builder this year

### Target Learners
- Grade 9 (Secondary 3) physics students in Singapore schools

### Learning Goals
- conducting authentic scientific investigation
- analysing and interpreting kinematics and dynamics data
- constructing explanations of complex motion
