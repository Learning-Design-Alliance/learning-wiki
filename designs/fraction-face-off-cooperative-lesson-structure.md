# Tutor-introduces-then-cooperative-groups lesson structure with a sports theme

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each lesson follows a sequence in which tutors introduce "concepts, skills, problem-solving strategies, and procedures," after which "students work in groups, sharing solutions and helping others." Lessons have a sports theme and "make extensive use of cooperative learning." This structure repeats daily across the 36-day tutoring period.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained tutors following structured manuals with lesson guides and materials
#### Constraints
- Delivered as 35-minute daily small-group sessions

### Target Learners
- at-risk fourth graders in small tutoring groups

### Learning Goals
- fraction concepts, skills, and problem-solving strategies
