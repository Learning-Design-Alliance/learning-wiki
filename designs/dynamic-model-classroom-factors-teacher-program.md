# Dynamic model of educational effectiveness classroom factors as the basis for a teacher improvement program

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The dynamic model of educational effectiveness (Creemers & Kyriakides, 2008) defines classroom factors found to be associated with student outcomes, such as orientation, structuring, modelling, application tasks, questioning, and assessment. In this study, "the classroom factors of the dynamic model were used in developing a teacher improvement program in the second intervention," delivered through a booklet, a one-day workshop, and six half-day monthly meetings. The authors frame this as an integration of Educational Effectiveness Research findings with improvement initiatives.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- EER findings are used to determine the direction of improvement, requiring translation of research factors into concrete program materials and meetings
#### Constraints
- The authors state that developing improvement measures is not as easy as identifying factors that work for education and applying them, and the integration proved complex

### Target Learners
- junior secondary students (ages 12/13 to 14/15) in Indonesian madrasahs

### Learning Goals
- English reading comprehension achievement
- teaching quality in English lessons
