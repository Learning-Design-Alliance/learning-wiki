# Polite informational axiomatic approach: quantum theory axioms emerge from phenomenological analysis of qubit-like quantum objects

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The thesis's central pedagogical approach to quantum physics is a "polite informational axiomatic approach" in which the axioms of quantum theory emerge from the phenomenological analysis of the behaviour of quantum objects representing qubits, such as electron spins and photon polarisation states. It deliberately moves away from historical-origin narratives: "Moving away from a reﬂection on the historical origins of quantum physics, the focus is on the informational aspects of the theory and the concept of qubits." The approach has three pillars — change the question, change the physics, change the language — and treats quantum technologies as a context in which to construct the theory itself.
