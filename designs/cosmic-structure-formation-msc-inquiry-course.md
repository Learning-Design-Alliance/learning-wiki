# Cosmic Structure Formation MSc inquiry course series at ETH Zurich and University of Milan Bicocca

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A series of university MSc courses of about 40 hours taught at ETH Zurich from 2017 to 2019 and at UniMiB in 2020 and 2021, with on average between 8-13 students at ETH and about 20 and 13 students at UniMiB. The course is designed as dialogic experiences mirroring authentic research, including 'generating and refining questions; making and testing assumptions; developing one's own research path; and sharing, explaining and justifying ideas and results with peers'. The content framework covers Galaxy Clusters, the IGM and Cosmic Web, the Early Universe, and Galaxy Formation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Small class sizes and facilitation support to enable dialogic group activities
#### Constraints
- Offered as an optional course; the article notes the small number of MSc students favored the dialogic approach

### Target Learners
- MSc physics students, including Bachelor's and Doctoral students with varied backgrounds

### Learning Goals
- Deriving physical properties of the largest baryonic structures using atomic-scale radiation processes
- understanding radiation as an active agent in cosmic structure formation
