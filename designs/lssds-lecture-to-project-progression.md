# Gradual lecture-to-project progression with faculty mentors on real data problems

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A program-level instructional pattern in which an intensive school moves gradually across three activity types: lectures settle foundational content, hands-on labs "settle the content of the lectures", and the closing days are devoted entirely to faculty-mentored group projects on real data science problems. The article states "the first days being more 'lecture' heavy, and the last days of the school being focused solely on project time". Projects are proposed by core faculty with steps of differing difficulty and a possible open-ended final goal.
