# For emerging programs, build around artifacts with a computation-first backbone paired with a validation-first laboratory rather than an equipment wish list

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
For emerging R1 and R2 programs, the article recommends building around artifacts rather than an equipment wish list: "a computation-first backbone that teaches verification and reproducibility, paired with a validation-first laboratory experience that teaches calibration, normalization, and decision rules". Shared cyberinfrastructure and teaching kits make the model practical when local infrastructure is limited, and shared facilities let students complete design and release artifacts locally, execute fabrication or measurement elsewhere, and finish validation on campus.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A computation-first backbone teaching verification and reproducibility must be paired with a validation-first laboratory experience teaching calibration, normalization, and decision rules.
#### Constraints
- Shared cyberinfrastructure and teaching kits are what make the model practical when local infrastructure is limited.

### Target Learners
- emerging R1 and R2 program faculty
- engineering students without local cleanroom access

### Learning Goals
- verification and reproducibility
- calibration, normalization, and decision rules
