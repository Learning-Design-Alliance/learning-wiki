> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a lesson-level design pattern for implementing reciprocal teaching: a teacher's guide prepared for the experimental group covering three lectures. "The guide included the following:- 1-Behavioural objectives of the lesson. 2-Content 3-Teaching methodologies 4-Teaching media 5-Evaluation". The guide was reviewed by a jury committee of specialists in curricula, instruction and psychology before use, and the course was chosen because it "contains many situations, issues and problems which are suitable to be dealt with using the reciprocal-teaching strategy".
