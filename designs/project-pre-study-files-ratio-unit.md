# Project pre-study files guiding weekly group inquiry in the ratio, proportion and percentage unit

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The researchers prepared five project pre-study files, one for each sub-issue of the unit, containing research questions and thought-provoking router questions. Each file opened with the research issue of the week and closed with an individual 'What I have learned?' section, with group tasks, debate questions and everyday-life connections between. Groups of 4 or 5 students worked through one file per week over 7 weeks, presented projects in the final week, and the teacher guided groups and canalized them to proper resources.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Groups of 4 or 5 students with a named group leader
- Teacher guidance and access to resources such as the library and ICT laboratory
- Class time allocated for completing the files
#### Constraints
- The study is limited to the ratio, proportion and percentage unit in 7th grade
- Some files needed more than one week because of content density

### Target Learners
- 7th grade secondary school mathematics students

### Learning Goals
- ratio, proportion and percentage concepts and problem solving
- collaboration and learning-to-learn skills
