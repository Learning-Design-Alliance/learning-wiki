# Undergraduate astronomy research programs should be sustainable, usable, publishable and curriculum-integrated

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 mixed) · 2 studies (2 review), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review relays Beaky's four broad principles for undergraduate astronomy research with robotic telescopes: "the research must be sustainable", with accumulated experience passed between students before graduation; the technology must be reliable and relatively easy to use; the research should be designed to lead to publishable outcomes even if publication does not occur; and the research needs to be integrated into the curriculum. These principles guide how single-institution robotic observatories are used in undergraduate teaching programs.
