# Small-group independent research projects mentored by leaders and capped with online poster presentations

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The project-group strand replicated the camp's signature independent research work online: leader-provided astronomy topics based on the leaders' own research (instrumentation and machine learning, astronomy for development, exoplanets and eclipsing binaries), with participants working in groups of two to four, some alone, and up to eight per leader over a few weeks. Work culminated in presenting an online poster to other participants. This preserves the in-person camp's project structure in a distributed format.
