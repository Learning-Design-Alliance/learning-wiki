# Cognitive-science framework of creativity linking concept combination and creative processes to trainable fundamental cognitive abilities

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article constructs a model integrating elements of the Cattell-Horn-Carroll (CHC) model with other cognitive abilities known to relate to creativity. It organizes creativity into levels: "Concept combination and cognitive processes (associative thinking, analytical thinking, encoding, and potentiality)" are associated with more fundamental abilities such as associative memory, ideational fluency, figural flexibility and meaningful memory, which can then be targeted by specific pedagogical strategies. This shifts understanding of the creative process from procedural steps to a cognitive level and serves as the design basis for the IND8108 course.
