# Big Ideas in Cosmology online college curriculum

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An online curriculum for college students developed with NASA ROSES funding as part of Fermi E/PO higher education work. It comprises three five-chapter modules — "Our Place in the Universe: Space and Time", "The Darker Side of the Universe: Gravity, Black Holes and Dark Matter", and "Our Evolving Universe: Past, Present and Future" — with the first being field tested. The potential audience is "conservatively estimated at 25,000 students per year", and publisher Kendall Hunt supports market testing and online implementation.
