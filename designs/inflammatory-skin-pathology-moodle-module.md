# Inflammatory skin pathology e-learning module on a Moodle platform for pathology and dermatology interns and residents

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An online learning module built on a Moodle e-learning platform for interns and residents in pathology and dermatology. The article states "The learning module contains text, interactive clinical cases, tests with feedback, whole slides images, images and clinical photographs." It was developed by a pathology resident from Québec over 6 weeks in France and Switzerland under the supervision of two dermatopathologists, and was to become accessible to interns and residents in spring 2014.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Whole-slide imaging, scanning, image storage and online display with virtual slide viewers must be developed and maintained by each participating university or group
#### Constraints
- A major limiting factor to maintain and update the learning material is the amount of resources needed, as the article states

### Target Learners
- interns and residents in pathology and dermatology

### Learning Goals
- inflammatory skin pathology diagnosis, including bullous diseases, cutaneous drug reactions, psoriasiform and lichenoid reaction patterns
