# Student training programme and dedicated ItemManagementSystem platform for generating and reviewing progress-test MCQs

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
To enable students to develop the progress test, the project ran an intensive training programme of "five 2-day workshops, which occurred between February and October 2013", covering competency-based assessment, CanMEDS roles, the progress test concept, the blueprint, quality criteria for MCQs, group review, and working with the IMS. Students then drafted, group-reviewed, revised and re-reviewed MCQs in workshop and self-organized small-group phases, using a separate IMS platform dedicated solely to this progress test. Group reviews with trainers served as a quality assurance measure, and participants received work-study contracts to ensure long-term cooperation.
