# Problem-based learning as a student-centered approach organized around four key principles

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes PBL as a student-centered teaching approach introduced by Barrows and Tamblyn at McMaster University, with "4 key principles of PBL, including contextual learning, information processing, cooperative learning, and self-determination." In radiology it places imaging in a clinical context, with students learning through practice in a tutor-guided small group around organ-system-based scenarios, contrasting with passive lecture-based learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- PBL scenarios based on organ systems suit radiology's subspecialization
- tutor-guided small groups with clinical scenarios
#### Constraints
- In China, application lags behind developed countries due to curricula arrangement, large classes, deficiency of tutors, and high tuition fees

### Target Learners
- medical students in Chinese medical universities or colleges

### Learning Goals
- theoretical radiology knowledge
- clinical film-reading skills
- clinical thinking
- lifelong professional learning
