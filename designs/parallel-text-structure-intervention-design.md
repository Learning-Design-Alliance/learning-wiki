# Parallel single-domain text-structure intervention design with diagrams, text-type vocabulary, summarization, and transfer instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Both interventions taught a domain-specific strategy for constructing text-level representations via close reading, analytical reasoning, and text-structure vocabulary with a domain-specific diagram or equation mapping the text type's structure. Design elements drawn from meta-analyses included two text types, a writing component with graphic organizers, rule-based summarization, text-structure naming, signal words, and a short explicit transfer-instruction unit. The article states the interventions "were designed to maximize parallelisms between the processing of text with and without numbers, while preserving the domain-specificity of each text-processing strategy."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- 45 lessons over 15 weeks, three 30-minute 1:1 lessons per week
- tutors trained to fidelity criteria with weekly coaching
#### Constraints
- Only two text types were included because intervention lasted only 15 weeks; designed for second graders with comorbid difficulty and broadly average intellectual ability

### Target Learners
- second-grade children with comorbid reading comprehension and word-problem solving difficulty

### Learning Goals
- cause-effect and compare-contrast text-structure knowledge in passages and word problems
- text comprehension
- word-problem solving
