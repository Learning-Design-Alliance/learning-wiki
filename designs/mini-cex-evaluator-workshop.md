# Evaluator-training workshop for mini-CEX assessors

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To ensure reliability and validity and optimize educational impact, the hospital selects senior doctors as evaluators and runs a training workshop for them. The article states: "A workshop comprising a pretest, special lecture, video, and group discussion is held for all selected evaluators to introduce the background, principle, concept, purpose, function, and procedure of the mini-CEX." Simulation practice of mini-CEX assessment is conducted at the end of the workshop.
