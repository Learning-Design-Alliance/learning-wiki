# Current Topics in Biophysics (SC/BPHS 2090), a second-year co-taught interdisciplinary course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
SC/BPHS 2090 is a second-year course at York University, co-taught by a physicist and a biologist, serving as a gateway to the biophysics degree program and as an introduction to interdisciplinary science for other majors. Over six years enrollment averaged 18 students (range: 10–24), with about half non-majors (30% life sciences); 50% were biophysics majors. An archived course website with assignments and test questions is available online at www.yorku.ca/planters/BPHS_2090_website/. Assessment comprised term tests (30%), assignments (20%), gists (5% each), and a final exam (40%).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must have taken first-year courses in biology, physics and mathematics.
#### Constraints
- Enrollments were small, averaging 18 students (range 10–24), compared with well over 100 in most second-year science courses at the institution.

### Target Learners
- Second-year biophysics majors (50%) and students from diverse science majors, about 30% life sciences

### Learning Goals
- Analytical thinking integrating physics and biology
- Preparation for upper-level biophysics courses
