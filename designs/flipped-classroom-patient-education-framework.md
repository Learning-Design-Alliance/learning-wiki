# Flipped classroom as a framework for patient education over telemedicine

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article applies the flipped classroom—an approach that transfers didactic content to the learner's home and reserves meeting time for collaborative learning—to patient education in radiation oncology. Its core features are quoted as "content in advance (generally a pre‐recorded lecture), educator awareness of students’ understanding, and higher order learning during class time." Telemedicine is presented as a platform where these features are easily implemented, giving patients time to digest information and formulate questions at their own pace.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Education content delivered in advance of the appointment
- Appointment time repurposed for collaborative, question-based learning
#### Constraints
- Pilot evidence only; the authors call for a randomized controlled trial with a larger sample size

### Target Learners
- adult cancer patients receiving radiation therapy

### Learning Goals
- comprehension of complex treatment information and reduced anxiety
