# Low-cost MISTELS trainer box (30×37×23.5 cm acrylic box with HD webcam)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A trainer box built from folded acrylic plates measuring 30*37*23.5 cm, using a Logitech C920 HD Pro webcam, two LED lamps, a smartphone camera, and VLC 2.2.4 freeware for image streaming. The article reports its cost as "estimated at 500 Tunisian Dinars" (about $165 or £132.33), excluding laparoscopic forceps. Residents performed peg transfer, precision cutting and suture with intra-corporeal knot in it.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A portable computer to obtain images from the webcam, standard laparoscopic instruments (Maryland forceps, a grasper, scissors and a needle holder), and a smartphone camera facing the central opening
#### Constraints
- Two of the five MISTELS tasks (ligating loop and suture with extra-corporeal knot) were not performed due to financial constraints

### Target Learners
- surgical residents in general surgery and paediatric surgery

### Learning Goals
- basic laparoscopic surgical skills of peg transfer, precision cutting and intra-corporeal knot suturing
