# CST lesson sequence: deduce properties and methods from structure, then verify against textbook and pharmacopoeia

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
This pattern operationalizes CST within a course: students are first trained to deduce possible physical and chemical properties from a medicine's elementary structures, then explore possible impurities and analysis methods, summarize methods for identification, impurity test and assay, and verify against the textbook and ChP. Finally, students "draw a frame diagram for each drug to visualize and methodize piecemeal knowledge points", building links between structure, properties and analytical methods.
