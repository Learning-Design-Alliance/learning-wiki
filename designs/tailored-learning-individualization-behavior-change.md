# Tailored learning: individualizing information to improve behavior change in eLearning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article frames KeepCoool within the didactic shift away from one-size-fits-all approaches toward learner-adaptive approaches. "Tailored learning involves the individualization of information for each participant to reach better results regarding the desired behavior change"; it is discussed that individualization increases acceptability of information so that it is more frequently remembered and discussed. KeepCoool applied this via professional-group targeting, feedback tailored to baseline knowledge, and personal address.
