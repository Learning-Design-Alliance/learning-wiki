# EPIC study of Michigan school district responses to the COVID-19 pandemic

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Education Policy Innovation Collaborative (EPIC) at Michigan State University is conducting a study of "Michigan school district responses to the COVID-19 pandemic and efforts to support student learning." The study responds to "the Return to Learn legislation (2020 PA 147, 148, 149)", which tasked the Michigan Department of Education with studying student progress and school effectiveness across instructional modalities. This report is one component of that study, capturing local leaders' perspectives.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The study is anchored to Michigan's Return to Learn legislation (2020 PA 147, 148, 149), which mandated the Michigan Department of Education to study student progress toward learning goals during and beyond the 2020-21 school year
#### Constraints
- The study covers Michigan districts and was conducted during and beyond the 2020-21 school year

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in Michigan school districts

### Learning Goals
- Student progress toward learning goals across in-person, hybrid, and remote instructional modalities
