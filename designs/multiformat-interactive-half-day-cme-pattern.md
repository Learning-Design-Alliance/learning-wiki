# Multiformat interactive half-day CME combining case-based learning, audience response, lectures, hands-on demonstration, discussion groups, and role playing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A reusable instructional design for continuing professional education in which a half-day program layers several active formats around clinical cases. The article states the program "incorporated many of the educational formats recommended by the American College of Chest Physicians, including case-based learning, audience response system, lectures, hands-on demonstrations, discussion groups, and role playing", with short didactic lectures punctuated by audience-response questions and two small-group workshops. The authors attribute the program's 50% effect size partly to this multiple-mode delivery.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content developed by an expert faculty panel against identified knowledge and practice gaps
- Assessment instruments (case-vignette surveys) field-tested for content validity before implementation
#### Constraints
- The authors note participants were a self-selected group of primary care providers with an interest in COPD, so effectiveness may not generalize to all primary care providers
- Impact was measured immediately after the program via case vignettes, not observed practice or patient outcomes

### Target Learners
- Primary care physicians and other clinicians managing chronic disease

### Learning Goals
- Guideline-based diagnosis, staging, and management of COPD
- Accurate office spirometry use and interpretation
- Patient adherence strategies
