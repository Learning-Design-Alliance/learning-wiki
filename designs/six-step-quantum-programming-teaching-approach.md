# Six-step teaching approach for quantum computer programming and quantum software engineering

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes a six-step teaching approach for quantum computer programming and quantum software engineering, developed from experience teaching in two countries. The steps are: "Establishing the Quantum Foundation", quantum software engineering and the quantum software development lifecycle, "Containerization for Quantum Computer Programming Education", "Programming with Qiskit Package", "Programming With PennyLane", and "Programming With Ocean SDK". The authors state "This approach suggests a common pattern that could eventually be generalized."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors need access to quantum frameworks (Qiskit, PennyLane, Ocean SDK) and, for real-device work, QPU accounts, which are "usually QPUs are available free for teaching purposes for the universities" per the article's access discussion
#### Constraints
- The approach is based on the authors' teaching experience and "this proposal is subject to review at regular intervals" because technology evolves rapidly; no empirical evaluation has yet been conducted

### Target Learners
- M.Sc. and Ph.D. level university students in quantum computing courses

### Learning Goals
- Quantum computer programming
- Quantum software engineering
- Practical use of quantum SDKs and real quantum devices
