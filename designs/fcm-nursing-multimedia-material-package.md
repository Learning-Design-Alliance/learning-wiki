# FCM instructional material package: PowerPoint presentations, 2D/3D animations, and laboratory-shot skill videos

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The researchers built a digital teaching package in which "FCM teaching material consists of PowerPoint presentations on four topics, two- and three-dimensional animations and videos of basic nursing skills". Presentations covered vital signs, nutrition, drug management and parenteral drug preparation; skill videos of 5 to 15 minutes were shot in the nursing skills laboratory as practical explanations of all process steps; contents were combined into lesson videos recorded in a virtual studio and shared via a Teaching Management System and WhatsApp one week before each lesson.
