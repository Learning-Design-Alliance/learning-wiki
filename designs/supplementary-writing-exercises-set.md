# Set of more than 60 supplementary writing exercises for well-organized writing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In the experimental group, the same textbooks as the control group were used, but "about more than 60 supplementary exercises was used in order to develop the skills in the field of well-organized writing." The exercises include grouping food words into categories, completing the skeleton of an outline from a passage about insects, and writing a paragraph on a chosen topic followed by partner discussion. They are divided into three main groups: outline, coherence, and unity.
