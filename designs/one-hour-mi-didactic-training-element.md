# One-hour motivational interviewing didactic delivered during a primary care rotation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 1-h didactic on the fundamentals of motivational interviewing, delivered by the staff psychologist and psychology trainees during the first week of an internal medicine primary care clinic rotation. Residents completed a pre-test before the session, a post-test after it, and the same measures approximately 1 month later. The authors call the intervention's brevity "perhaps the most unique aspect of this training", noting previous trainings were at least 2.5 h.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivery within the first week of the primary care rotation by a staff psychologist and psychology trainees
#### Constraints
- Follow-up extended only to 1 month; the authors state future research could evaluate whether the changes are sustained for a period >1 month

### Target Learners
- Postgraduate year 1 or 2 internal medicine residents in supervised practice

### Learning Goals
- Fundamentals of motivational interviewing
- Knowledge, confidence and utilization of MI skills
