# Use a well-designed Tier 1 intervention within MTSS before assigning students to Tier 2 services

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article argues that within multi-tiered systems of support, effective core (Tier 1) classroom instruction should be provided to all students before more intensive Tier 2 and Tier 3 interventions, because "it would be inappropriate to place students in Tier 2 if there is no evidence to suggest they had the opportunity to learn from well-designed Tier 1 instruction." Findings from this and prior SBI studies support using the SBI curriculum preventatively to meet the needs of all students, including those who struggle.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Empirically validated Tier 1 interventions in mathematics
- Teachers with deep understanding of ratios, proportions, and problem solving who can scaffold appropriate strategies
#### Constraints
- For students with MD, the effect on general mathematical problem solving was not statistically significant, and the authors note further investigations must determine whether more intensive instruction would be advantageous

### Target Learners
- seventh-grade students with and without mathematics difficulties in inclusive classrooms

### Learning Goals
- proportional problem solving
- meeting Common Core State Standards in mathematics
