# Four-game vocabulary treatment sequence (hangman, flash card memory game, bingo, odd man out)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The treatment element is a sequence of four group-based vocabulary games adopted from "Games for Young Learners" and practiced in the last 20 minutes of each session: "New vocabulary items in "Happy Earth 1" (Bowler & Parminter, 2002) were taught and practiced via four games with the first group in the last 20 minutes of every session." Each game ran for five of the 20 total sessions, with learners arranged into small groups and the instructor monitoring and eliciting answers and reasons afterwards.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- New vocabulary items from the course book Happy Earth 1 as the target content
- Small-group arrangement and teacher monitoring during and after game play
#### Constraints
- Tested only with elementary-level learners aged 10 to 15 at one language institute

### Target Learners
- young EFL learners at elementary proficiency level (ages 10-15)

### Learning Goals
- foreign language vocabulary learning and retention
