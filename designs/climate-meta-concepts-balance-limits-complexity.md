# Three meta-concepts — balance/imbalance, planetary limits, and complexity — as an organizing structure for climate understanding

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article introduces three cross-cutting meta-concepts introduced via a single framing sentence: "Global Climate is a complex system based on the non-linear interactions of six major subsystems and the sun, that can result in balance/imbalance depending on whether (or not) the earth systems are operating within certain planetary limits or boundaries." The meta-concepts aim to enable holistic rather than piecemeal understanding and to address the barriers of knowledge pollution, transdisciplinarity, onto-epistemology and psychology. They embrace threshold concepts such as uncertainty, the geological perspective, and climate science fundamentals.
