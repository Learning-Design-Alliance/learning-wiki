# Own It, Learn It, Share It framework for student-centered learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Lee and Hannafin's design framework "intersects constructivist, constructionist, and self-determination theories to address student-centered learning." It has three parts: "Own It involves students developing ownership over the process and achieving personally meaningful learning goals," Learn It involves autonomous learning "through metacognitive, procedural, conceptual, and strategic scaffolding," and Share It involves students generating "artifacts aimed at authentic audiences beyond the classroom assessment." The framework also includes "ten discrete design guidelines for the instructional design process."
