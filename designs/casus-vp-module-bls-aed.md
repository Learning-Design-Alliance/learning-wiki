# CASUS virtual patient module of six BLS-AED cases

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
A web-based virtual patient module on the CASUS® platform consisting of six "linear string of pearls" cases of 8 to 17 screen cards with text, images, videos, pop-up comments and formative questions with feedback. Cases were added weekly to match each week's course topic, and the design followed Mayer's multimedia learning principles: "text was broken into small segments (screen-cards), essential concepts were highlighted and the text was illustrated by relevant images and animations". The module covered only topics also presented in lectures and face-to-face classes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students needed individual anonymous accounts on the CASUS platform and prior practice in a Basics of Computer Science course
#### Constraints
- The module contained no topics not presented in the lectures or traditional face-to-face classes

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate medical students

### Learning Goals
- basic life support and automated external defibrillation knowledge and skills
