# Mixed objectivist and constructivist instructional design model for intensive blended courses

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article applies an instructional design model that combines objectivist features (teacher-led face-to-face instruction, defined learning goals, structured lesson plans, assessment aligned with goals) with constructivist features (student-controlled Moodle platforms, group activities for knowledge construction, new media integration, self-assessments). The authors state that "a mixed instructional design model combining both the features of objectivist and constructivist methods of instructing and learning would be a distinctive strategy to be utilized for intensive BL course". The model structured the blended Oriental Music course tested in the experiment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires both objectivist structured instruction and constructivist student-controlled online activities to be combined in one course design
#### Constraints
- Recommended for intensive blended courses where lesson time is limited, per the article's context of music lessons with limited allocated time

### Target Learners
- Senior secondary students (Grade 10 and 11)

### Learning Goals
- Oriental Music competencies at Ordinary Level, including practical singing/playing abilities, music theory fundamentals, creative works, and performing abilities
