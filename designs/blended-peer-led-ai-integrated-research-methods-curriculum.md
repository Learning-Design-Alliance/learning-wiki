# Blended, peer-led, AI-integrated research methods curriculum with eight structured components

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention is an eight-component postgraduate research methods program: flipped self-produced video lectures, gamified weekly group article critiques, in-class study design tasks, CASP peer appraisal rounds, faculty-led roundtable discussions, online expert lectures, supplementary skill activities, and a final proposal with oral defense. The article describes "a blended, student-centered research methods program integrating flipped learning, structured peer critique, active application tasks, and regulated use of AI tools." ChatGPT was permitted to improve clarity of critiques without replacing student reasoning; the control group received traditional lectures with AI prohibited.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty training, ongoing support, and dedicated preparation and feedback time, which the authors state may limit scalability in resource-constrained settings
- Transparent guidelines around AI use and balanced workload
#### Constraints
- Resource-intensive to deliver
- Implemented in a single postgraduate dental program with cohort-based learning and close faculty supervision

### Target Learners
- First-year postgraduate dental students

### Learning Goals
- Research literacy and critical appraisal competence
- Research proposal development and thesis preparedness
