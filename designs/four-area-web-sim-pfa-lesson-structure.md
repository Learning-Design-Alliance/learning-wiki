# Four-area web-based simulation lesson structure: pre-learning, pre-briefing, simulation, debriefing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The program structures online simulation learning into four phases: pre-learning (earthquake and PFA content), pre-briefing (scenario introduction, participation instructions, client information, and five learning goals), simulation (12 video clips and 11 quiz games), and debriefing (self-debriefing written via a menu screen). This mirrors the experiential learning emphasis the article draws from Kolb, in which "learning is a continuous process that combines experience, cognition, perception, and action".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Scenario and quiz content grounded in literature review, needs assessment, and consultation with experts and disaster victims, as done in the development phase.
#### Constraints
- Disaster situations are "difficult to fully reproduce through traditional methods such as role-play or simulation education", so fidelity is bounded by web-based video and quiz formats.

### Target Learners
- nurses

### Learning Goals
- disaster psychological support skills
- problem-solving in disaster scenarios
