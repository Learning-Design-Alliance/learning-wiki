# Project-based learning organized around eight principles with a five-phase project procedure

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes PoBL as implemented through eight principles: "the organization of learning around real world problems; student centered instruction; collaboration; teacher as facilitator; authenticity through the use of authentic materials and audiences; formative assessment; reflection; and the production of authentic artifacts". The experimental group worked through five phases — organizing groups, stating subjects, designing the project, applying it, presenting, and evaluating — over two projects, each completed in six weeks, covering all course concepts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students assigned to mixed-ability groups with explained group-working rules
- Projects based on course concepts, covering systematic investigation of an educational problem and application of instructional design procedures
#### Constraints
- Each project required six weeks of group work, with substantial time spent out of class

### Target Learners
- third-year undergraduate educational technology students

### Learning Goals
- self-directed learning skills
- system-based education course concepts
- instructional design procedures
