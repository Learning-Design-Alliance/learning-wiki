# Prediction–scaffolding/observation–reconciliation cycles with reflection prompts and self-monitoring checkpoints in tutorial design

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Both QuILT versions embed recurring cycles in which students predict outcomes, receive scaffolding (including engagement with a computer simulation), and reconcile discrepancies between predictions and observations. The article states they "have prediction–scaffolding/observation–reconciliation cycles such that students predict outcomes, then scaffolding is provided ... and then they are asked to reconcile discrepancies", alongside reflection prompts, self-monitoring checkpoints, and gradually scaffolded complexity. The hybrid version adds explicit prompts to connect mathematical results to physical meaning.
