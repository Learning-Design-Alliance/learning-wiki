# Three-practice school improvement package promoted by SIG

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The School Improvement Grants program promotes a package of school improvement practices for low-performing schools comprising three components: "using data to inform and differentiate instruction," "expanding technology access for teachers or using computer-assisted instruction," and "providing ongoing professional development in which teachers work collaboratively or that school leaders facilitate." An evaluation brief found over 96 percent of surveyed low-performing schools adopted all three practices, making this a widely implemented improvement pattern in SIG-funded settings.
