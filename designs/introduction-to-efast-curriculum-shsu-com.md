# Two-week Introduction to EFAST curriculum with didactics, standardized-patient skills practice, and OSCE assessment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A required pre-clerkship curriculum for OMS III students in the Clinical Clerkship Preparation course, built from EFAST competencies mapped to institutional and AACOM core competencies. It comprised two 50-minute didactic sessions, a 2-hour hands-on skills session with standardized patients, checkout handheld probes, and an OSCE with faculty and standardized-patient rubrics plus MCQs. Respondents rated quality items highly, with "mean values of 4.41–4.76 on a 5 point Likert scale".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires handheld portable ultrasound machines, standardized patients, and knowledgeable faculty to design and teach the sessions
#### Constraints
- Implementation without these resources may require increased reliance on online learning materials and simulation of cases

### Target Learners
- Third-year osteopathic medical students entering clinical clerkships

### Learning Goals
- Performing and interpreting the EFAST exam, communicating with patients and teams about the exam, and recognizing indications and limitations of POCUS
