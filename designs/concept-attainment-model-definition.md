# Concept attainment model as a learning model designed to help students learn concepts more easily

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The concept attainment model is described as "a model of achievement of learning model that is designed to help students to be more easily learn a concept". It focuses on providing learners with a number of concepts and on how to develop the concept, helping students identify and define concepts through discovery of essential attributes. In this study it was the treatment for the second experimental group. The authors note it requires teachers to support student hypotheses, direct argumentation in testing hypotheses, and focus students on specific examples.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher should have a supportive attitude toward student hypotheses
- Teacher should provide direction for students to provide arguments in testing hypotheses
- Teacher should establish students to focus on specific examples
- Requires samples to be delivered to students with direct student involvement
#### Constraints
- It does not always work with all age groups or topics
- Students may become too verbal and shout out answers

### Target Learners
- 8th grade junior high school students

### Learning Goals
- Concept understanding in physics
- Identifying relevant and irrelevant information
