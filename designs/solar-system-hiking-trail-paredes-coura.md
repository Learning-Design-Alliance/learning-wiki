# Solar System Hiking Trail: an accurate scale model of the Solar System built during AstroCamps

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Solar System Hiking Trail is a community service activity produced by the program: "one of only 16 (in Europe) scales model of the Solar System, that is accurate both in terms of sizes and distances of the objects." It was developed during the first four AstroCamps and officially opened on 13 August 2016. It doubles as a public science-communication artifact and a camp community-service project.
