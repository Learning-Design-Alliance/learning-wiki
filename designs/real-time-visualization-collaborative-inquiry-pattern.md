# Two-workshop collaborative inquiry pattern: hands-on exploration with real-time multi-level visualizations anchored in individual and collective actions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention's instructional pattern combines gamified hands-on activities, paired collaboration, and real-time visualizations on multiple levels. "Concepts related to social media mechanisms were anchored in both individual and collective classroom actions, enabling students to observe how their micro-level interactions—collected as individual data traces—lead to visible patterns and clusters at the macro-level." Each workshop ends with reflective tasks linking the app experience to students' own everyday social media use, culminating in a letter to decision makers aimed at promoting critical consciousness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Newly developed classroom apps with real-time visualization; a researcher- or teacher-led session; paired student devices and a classroom projector
#### Constraints
- The intervention was short, only two sessions, which the authors note when interpreting results

### Target Learners
- K-12 learners (5th and 8th graders in this study)

### Learning Goals
- Conceptual understanding of opaque data-driven mechanisms; data agency; critical consciousness toward platform technologies
