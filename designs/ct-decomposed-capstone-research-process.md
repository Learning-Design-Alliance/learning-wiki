# Structured systematic capstone research project procedure: decomposing the research process into algorithmic CT steps

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article presents a structured systematic process that decomposes the undergraduate capstone research project into algorithmic procedures students step through, inputting a choice or following automation. It sequences research context selection (ranking prior courses), a three-question philosophy logic circuit mapping to qualitative or quantitative research, subject selection across five categories mapped to ten methodologies, fill-in-the-blank topic statements, and research question decomposition into PRQs and SRQs. The author states "a structured problem decomposition approach was taken to divide the process into algorithmic procedures that the student steps through".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires the coordinator to hold a finite knowledge base covering the ten generic topics and the data analysis tools used across them
- Requires weekly submission templates identifying required items and laboratory activities covering the predefined processes
#### Constraints
- The procedure was intended to address inquiry-based research, so the author notes there may be a limitation for PBL capstone courses
- Remote delivery limits the generation of experimental data, with remote simulation and modelling topics more common

### Target Learners
- undergraduate 3rd-year capstone students in an aviation program

### Learning Goals
- conducting independent research
- formulating research questions and methodology
- reporting research findings
