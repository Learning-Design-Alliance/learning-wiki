# Intervention mapping with embedded behaviour change techniques as an e-learning development framework

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The e-learning was developed using intervention mapping, described by the article as "a comprehensive best-practice framework for informing the planning, development and evaluation of an intervention". The framework produced a theory-informed course with behaviour change techniques embedded in activities: video demonstrations ('Credible source', 'Demonstration of the behaviour'), written practice with feedback ('Behavioural practice/rehearsal', 'Feedback on behaviour'), self-reflection ('Valued self-identity'), and action planning. The authors credit these BCT components for improvements seen only in the e-learning group.
