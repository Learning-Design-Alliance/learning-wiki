# Instructor discussion-post subject headings as a personalization lever in learning management systems

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Most learning management systems "offer the ability for the instructor to change the subject heading or title to a post." Instructors can retitle posts using a student's name to signal a class-wide conversation, highlight subtopics, or add humor to entice students to read. The article treats this small, instructor-controlled feature as a valuable personalization of the discussion forum and manipulates it experimentally as personalized versus standard auto-generated reply titles.
