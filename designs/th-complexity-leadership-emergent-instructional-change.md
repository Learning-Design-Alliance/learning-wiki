# Complexity Leadership approach to emergent instructional change

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article frames its institutional change initiative as an emergent, non-top-down change model in which instructors and TAs developed a shared vision for effective teaching while retaining individual agency to become reflective and innovative instructors. It states that "The approach most closely aligns with Borrego and Henderson's "Complexity Leadership" strategy", in which the leader encouraged disruption of the status quo while creating an environment where faculty could create their own changes as an interdependent collective. The initiative drew on literature highlighting the need for sustained efforts to sustain cultural change in instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A change leader who encourages disruption of the status quo while simultaneously creating room for faculty agency
- Sustained effort over multiple terms rather than one-off prescription
