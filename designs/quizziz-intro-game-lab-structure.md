# Laboratory session structure: introductory expectation-probing game, experiments, closing recap

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The laboratory followed a fixed session structure of about one hour. It opened with "an introductory game was proposed using the ”Quizziz” platform to qualitatively measure students’ expectations about phenomena showed during the activity", lasting ten minutes with no evaluation intent. Then the experimental activities ran for 40 minutes, and the session "ended with a general recap on the physics dealt and the results of the introductory game was discussed in the light of phenomena observed".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A quiz platform (Quizziz) for the introductory expectation-probing game and time for a 10-minute game, 40-minute experimental activity and closing recap
#### Constraints
- The game questions "had not any evaluation intent, rather they just measured their feeling or previous knowledge"; the pedagogical approach discussion is left to a future paper

### Target Learners
- high-school students aged 17-19 attending the last three years of Lyceums in Sardinia

### Learning Goals
- probing and discussing students' expectations about wave and particle phenomena
- connecting students' prior predictions with the phenomena observed in the experiments
