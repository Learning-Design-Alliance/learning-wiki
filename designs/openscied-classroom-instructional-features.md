# OpenSciEd classroom-level instructional features: facilitator teacher, student questions, navigation routines, practices, problematization, and assessment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Figure 2 summarizes six distinctive classroom features: the teacher as facilitator who choreographs learning experiences without prescriptive scripts; instruction driven by student questions supported by driving question boards; lesson-to-lesson enactment with "whole class navigation discussions to maintain coherence from the students' perspective"; practice-based activities engaging science and engineering practices; problematization moments that prompt students to identify gaps in understanding; and assessment opportunities such as consensus models and performance tasks applying concepts to new contexts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Materials and teachers must be anticipatory of student questions so activities can respond to them
#### Constraints
- Teachers must promote classroom interactions that engage students deeply with the practices; teacher materials identify what needs to be problematized

### Target Learners
- K-12 science students

### Learning Goals
- figuring out target science ideas through investigation and collaborative sensemaking
