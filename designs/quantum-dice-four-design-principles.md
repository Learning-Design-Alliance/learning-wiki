# Four educational design principles for quantum demonstration material: empirical, haptic, accessible, intuitive

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article enumerates four design principles guiding the Quantum Dice, aligned with recent demonstration-material publications: (i) Empirical — students "formulate hypotheses, perform tests, and refine their understanding based on the observed outcomes"; (ii) Haptic — physical multisensory materials acting as material anchors for collective interaction; (iii) Accessible — usable "in a typical classroom setting" without prior technical knowledge and prioritising cost-effectiveness; (iv) Intuitive — building on familiar contexts so users without physics backgrounds can experience distinctions from classical behaviour.
