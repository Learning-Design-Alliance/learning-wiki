# EAPBD: decontextualized problem-based learning scenarios that isolate specific chess patterns

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
EAPBDs are "situaciones de aprendizaje dise˜nadas en forma de problemas o desaf´ios ajedrec´isticos espec´ificos, presentados deliberadamente fuera del contexto de una partida convencional completa", designed to focus the learner's attention on specific cognitive or conceptual aspects. Each scenario highlights one or more strategic/tactical patterns (e.g. a pin, a queen-and-knight mate, an isolated pawn), so that pattern acquisition happens in a meaningful problem context rather than as an isolated list of themes. The variety of decontextualized problems pushes learners to analyze the essence of a position and transfer known principles to new contexts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A repertoire of problems each designed to highlight specific patterns, presented outside full-game context, integrated with dialectical dialogue and difficulty adaptation
#### Constraints
- The article presents EAPBDs within the Odychess method for chess; transfer to other domains is proposed but not tested in this study

### Target Learners
- secondary students, beginner-intermediate chess players

### Learning Goals
- pattern recognition and structured chess memory
- tactical problem-solving
- transfer of principles to novel positions
