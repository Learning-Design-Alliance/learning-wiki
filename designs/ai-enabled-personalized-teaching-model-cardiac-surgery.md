# AI-enabled personalized teaching model for cardiovascular surgery trainees (intelligent question bank + staged case-based teaching + AI standardized patients)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
An integrated teaching model in which ChatGPT-4o generated practice questions, staged case materials, and AI-SP communication scenarios, with expert review of generated items. It combined "an intelligent question bank, staged case-based teaching, and AI-SP training", delivered over one semester with weekly AI-SP history-taking sessions and instructor feedback on learning data. Items were reviewed by two cardiovascular surgery experts for accuracy, difficulty, and syllabus consistency.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Standardized prompt templates developed before the intervention
- Expert review panel to evaluate AI-generated items
- Instructor training on ChatGPT-4o use and platform-use training for all participants
- Instructor supervision and feedback to correct inaccurate AI-generated responses
#### Constraints
- AI-generated responses were not to be regarded as authoritative clinical guidance
- Findings are based on ChatGPT-4o and may not generalize to other large language models

### Target Learners
- Cardiovascular surgery trainees (registered physicians and nurses in specialized training)

### Learning Goals
- Theoretical knowledge of cardiovascular surgery
- Clinical thinking and critical thinking disposition
- History-taking and doctor–patient communication skills
