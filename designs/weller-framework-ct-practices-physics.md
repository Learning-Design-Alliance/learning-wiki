## Description
The article adopts Weller et al.'s framework, which categorizes discipline-specific computational thinking skills relevant to physics modeling and analysis. The authors structured a series of activities to progressively introduce key CT skills, "such as decomposing, translating physics into code, algorithm building, debugging, applying conditional logic, generating and analyzing data, selecting effective data representations, and working in groups on computational models." The framework guided both the activity design and the assessment rubric.
