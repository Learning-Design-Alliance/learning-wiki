# Epistemic Principle: designing the course around theoretical modelling practices of physicists so students accept quantum description as plausible product of their own inquiry

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes engaging students in theoretical modelling cycles drawing on historically significant epistemic practices of theoretical physicists, such as thought experiments and mathematical modelling in purely theoretical settings. The goal is to help students "accept the quantum description of the world as a plausible product of their own inquiry" and "build theoretical reasoning skills". Practices are listed in Fig. 3 and converted into authentic inquiry activities.
