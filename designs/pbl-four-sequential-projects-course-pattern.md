# PBL-integrated research training course built on four sequential projects with distinct assessments

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The scientific research training course was designed on the project-based learning model and grounded in constructivist theory, integrating scenarios, collaboration, and discussion into task design. It "comprised four sequential projects: the implementation of flipped classroom activities, the completion of a research proposal, peer assessment, and a final reflective journal", completed in a designated order as the course progressed, with a printed timeline of tasks and assessment methods (Fig. 1). Reflective journals were also used as supplementary data in the evaluation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students complete the projects in the designated order as the course progresses
- Teacher reviews project completion and returns work for revisions
#### Constraints
- Implemented in a single postgraduate nursing scientific research training course at one Chinese university

### Target Learners
- postgraduate nursing students

### Learning Goals
- nursing research theoretical knowledge and methods
- research proposal development
- critical thinking and reflection
- academic writing and presentation skills
