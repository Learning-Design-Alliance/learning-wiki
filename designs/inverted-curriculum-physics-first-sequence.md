## Description
The inverted curriculum reverses the traditional biology-chemistry-physics sequence so that physics is taken first and biology last. The article states that rather than the traditional sequence, "the inverted curriculum follows a physics–chemistry–biology sequence", resting on the logic that understanding fundamental biology mechanisms depends on previously mastered physical and chemical concepts. Physics lays conceptual groundwork in matter, motion, forces, energy and waves; chemistry constructs an explicit particle model; biology applies these models to molecular interactions at the cellular level.
