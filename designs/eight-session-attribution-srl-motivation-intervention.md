# Eight-session (16-hour) psychological intervention program for grade 10 students

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A structured training program of "eight sessions (16 h), one session per week" targeting causal attributions, self-regulation, and achievement motivation. Sessions used mini-lectures, case analyses, scenario-based discussions, skill-building workshops on goal setting, time management, and distraction control, and seminars on expectancy–value theory. Training material was adapted from recognized sources, expert-reviewed, and given to each student after pre-testing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivery by trained facilitators with translation support for participants preferring the Kafi noono language
- Expert-reviewed, self-explanatory training material adapted from recognized sources
#### Constraints
- Evaluated in a one-group pre-test-post-test design without random assignment or a control group

### Target Learners
- Grade 10 secondary school students in Kafa Zone, Ethiopia

### Learning Goals
- Adaptive attribution patterns
- Self-regulation skills (goal setting, time management, distraction control)
- Achievement motivation
