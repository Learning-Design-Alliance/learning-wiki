# GRADE: a controlled grid task environment for independently manipulating teacher expertise and teacher-student representational alignment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
GRADE (Grid Manipulation of Representational Alignment and Domain Expertise) is a modular student-teacher cognitive task where stimuli are arranged on a grid and labeled. Teachers know all labels but their expertise is modulated by corrupting labels, while "the arrangement of the stimuli on the grid might vary between the student and teacher, allowing us to manipulate representational alignment." Teachers select labeled examples to reveal; students classify remaining stimuli. It supports any grid-arrangeable stimuli and can be extended to multi-turn interactions.

### Context
#### Requirements

- The reported experiments focus on a one-shot case where the teacher makes one round of selections

### Learning Goals
