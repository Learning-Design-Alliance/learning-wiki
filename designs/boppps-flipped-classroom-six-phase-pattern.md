# BOPPPS combined with flipped classroom: a six-phase closed-loop teaching pattern for clinical practice training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a teaching pattern that "divides the teaching process into six parts: bridge-in, objective/outcome, pre-assessment, participatory learning, post-assessment, and summary". In the experimental group, bridge-in distributed assessment materials two weeks before class with guided group preview; participatory learning combined the flipped classroom format with resident presentations and skill operations as the main focus; post-assessment simulated a clinical practice ability assessment before residents left the department. The authors describe the whole process as "a complete and rigorous closed loop and is highly operable and practical".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers with residency training and examination qualifications guide groups and monitor discussions; residents study independently before classes and reflect after classes with timely feedback on teaching effectiveness.
#### Constraints
- The article states the BOPPPS model "is still being gradually improved in China"; the study applied the pattern only in obstetrics and gynecology standardized training at one base.

### Target Learners
- First-year obstetrics and gynecology residents in standardized training

### Learning Goals
- Clinical practice skills
- Clinical thinking ability
- Self-directed learning
- Doctor-patient communication
