# Scientific thinking and medical research: a three-credit introductory course-based research experience through PBL

## Description
An introductory-level CRE developed as "the first point of entry into research for undergraduate medical students," aiming to expose students to research, provide authentic research experiences, and develop basic research skills. It was a three-credit course meeting twice weekly for 120 minutes across a 15-week semester, with nine groups of four to five students each guided by a professor. Grades combined individual components (attendance, contribution, self- and peer-assessment) and group components (presentation, proposal, report).
