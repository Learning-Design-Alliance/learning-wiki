# Vygotskian zone of proximal development scaffolding combined with preparation for future learning guides QuILT design

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The tutorial's design framework combines Vygotsky's zone of proximal development with the preparation for future learning (PFL) framework. The ZPD is "defined by what a student can do on their own without scaffolding vs. what they can do via instruction using learning tools" that build on prior knowledge. PFL holds that effective instruction should involve both innovation and efficiency dimensions so students transfer learning; in the QuILT, students first answer questions representing the innovation dimension, then receive scaffolding via guided teaching-learning sequences representing the efficiency dimension. Transfer is assessed through near and far transfer questions.
