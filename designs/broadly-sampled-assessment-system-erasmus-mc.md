# Broadly sampled assessment system with algorithm-derived final clerkship grades

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An assessment system implemented at Erasmus MC in 2012 in which six clinically relevant CanMeds competencies are "formally evaluated in five different assessments by at least two assessors on multiple occasions", including a computer-based knowledge assessment. Partial grades are integrated by an algorithm into a final clerkship grade, though supervisors may deviate by a maximum of one full grade point on the 1–10 scale.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multiple evaluation moments, multiple assessors, human and computer assessments, and explicit partial grades integrated by an algorithm
#### Constraints
- Supervisors may deviate by one full grade point, which reintroduces ethnicity-related differences; scheduling is difficult in clerkships of 3 weeks or shorter

### Target Learners
- medical students in the master phase (clerkships)

### Learning Goals
- evaluation of clinical competencies including medical expert, scholar, communicator, health advocate, collaborator and organiser roles
