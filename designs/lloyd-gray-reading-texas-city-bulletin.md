## Description
A University of Texas Bulletin (No. 1853) prepared especially for elementary teachers of Texas, reporting a city school system's program for diagnosing and remediating pupils' reading ability. From available reading tests described in its first chapter, two were chosen and used as described in chapter two; chapters four and five describe the diagnosis and remedial program in detail. The reviewer judges that its "numerous charts and diagrams should make it quite possible for any teacher to make regular use of the procedures devised," and that its literature listing supplements the meagre diagnosis-and-remedy literature.
