# Rotating student-led online journal club with faculty-mentored presenters

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A weekly one-hour online reading group in which the discussion-leader role rotates among students and faculty, each assembling slide presentations on the assigned reading. Student presenters are paired with a faculty mentor "in an arrangement designed to give students a taste of what a one-on-one research experience with the faculty mentor might look like." The first meetings are faculty-led to set templates before students take over, and a lead facilitator handles long-term logistics and student-faculty pairing. Sessions balance presentation and discussion time roughly half and half.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A lead facilitator charged with long-term logistics, speaker coordination, and student-faculty pairing
- Faculty members willing to attend sessions and mentor student presenters
#### Constraints
- Sessions were restricted to one hour and conducted over Zoom; the reported run covered one summer and one fall semester at a single institution

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics majors
- master's students in physics

### Learning Goals
- reading and discussing scientific articles
- presentation and discussion-leadership skills
- exposure to physics research topics and careers
