# A three-section tutorial structure that pairs a standard physics context with a culturally relevant context and revisits the standard questions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The tutorial consists of three sections. The first section uses a modified version of two questions about a two-piece bar to elicit student ideas about static equilibrium. The second and third sections center around a skateboarding trick called the manual, and both sections start with an elicit question analogous to the relevant two-piece bar question. At the end of both sections, students are guided to revisit the two-piece bar questions and resolve inconsistencies.
