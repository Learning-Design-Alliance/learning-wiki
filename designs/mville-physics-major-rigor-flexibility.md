# Physics major structure pairing a rigorous required core with student-controlled pacing and structural options

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The redesigned major requires '54 mandatory credits total, which includes a large 16-credit mathematics co-requisite', a four-course 2000-level intermediate core, two 3000-level special-topic electives, a summer research internship, and a senior research seminar with thesis. Students may take one intermediate core course per semester, choose internship timing, or spread senior research over two semesters; the authors call the resulting program 'one of rigor and ﬂexibility'.
