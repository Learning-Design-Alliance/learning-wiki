# Educational Opportunity Program (EOP) at Stony Brook University

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
EOP is a state-sponsored access-and-support program at Stony Brook serving nearly 600 low-income students and students of color whose high school preparation was incomplete. It provides "a five-week summer 'boot camp' program, personal counseling and academic advising at least three times a semester, and a mandatory study skill workshop" including an academic success contract. The report reports EOP students graduate at higher rates than other Stony Brook students despite entering less prepared.
