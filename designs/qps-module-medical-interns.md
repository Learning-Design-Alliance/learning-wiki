# Structured Quality and Patient Safety (QPS) module for postgraduate medical interns

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A compulsory module in the internship curriculum at a private tertiary care hospital in Pakistan, consisting of "a pre-module online assessment administered through the institutional Virtual Learning Environment, followed by two consecutive in-person teaching days (13 contact hours)". Morning didactic lectures cover quality and patient safety, the science of safety, error disclosure, safety culture, and conflict management, followed by facilitated group-based applied learning including case-based learning on learning from defects, a communication lab, and structured case-based discussions, concluding with a post-module assessment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered as a compulsory component of the internship curriculum with pre- and post-module assessments administered through the institutional Virtual Learning Environment
#### Constraints
- The one-arm quasi-experimental design precluded a concurrent control group and limits causal attribution of observed gains

### Target Learners
- Postgraduate medical interns in a one-year internship programme at a tertiary care hospital

### Learning Goals
- Patient safety knowledge
- Systems thinking
- Knowledge efficacy for safety and quality improvement practices
