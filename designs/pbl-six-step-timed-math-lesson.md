# Six-step timed PBL lesson sequence for mathematics (2 lesson hours)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article prints the PBL steps it used: after a 5-minute explanation of tasks and assessment procedures and group formation, "each group was given problem and for 40 minutes discussing in groups", followed by a 10-minute whole-class discussion integrating new knowledge into the problem context, 30 minutes of group presentations with question and answer, and a 5-minute teacher summary with group and individual tasks. This lesson-level pattern operationalises PBL within a fixed 2-hour lesson block.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- groups of students formed at the start of the lesson
- a problem given to each group
- teacher acts as facilitator rather than dominating the class
#### Constraints
- designed for 2 hours of lessons

### Target Learners
- grade ten senior high school students

### Learning Goals
- mathematics critical thinking ability
