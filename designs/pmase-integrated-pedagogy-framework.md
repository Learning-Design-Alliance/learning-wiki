# P-MASE: an integrated five-component pedagogy (Problem-based, Mobile, Authentic, Social, Experiential) for hospital management education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q4` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
P-MASE is a descriptive acronym for a bundled pedagogy combining problem-based learning, mobile learning, authentic learning, social learning, and experiential learning. It "organizes job-relevant management competencies into authentic, cross-department task chains, supports learning through mobile/digital tools and collaboration, and aligns assessment with management performance evidence collected across tasks." Its novelty lies in integrating what to learn, how to learn, and how to judge performance into one coherent design logic, targeting systems thinking and trade-off reasoning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interdisciplinary teaching teams spanning management, clinical, and informatics expertise
- Digital infrastructure: SuperStar platform, virtual simulation platform, Power BI, AnyLogic modeling, and NLP tools
#### Constraints
- The study evaluated P-MASE as an integrated package and was not designed to estimate component-specific effects or isolate individual components

### Target Learners
- undergraduate Health Administration and Information Resource Management students

### Learning Goals
- hospital management competencies including policy interpretation, data-driven decision making, crisis communication, and systems thinking
