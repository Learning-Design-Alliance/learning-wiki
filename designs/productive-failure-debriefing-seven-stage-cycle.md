# Seven-stage productive-failure-plus-debriefing teaching cycle for clinical skills training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
This pattern organizes a clinical skills course around productive failure's generation-and-exploration and integration-and-consolidation stages combined with the GAS debriefing model's "collection, analysis, and summary" stages, yielding links from "activity theme introduction, group inquiry production process, video production, video reproduction, peer reproduction, self-reproduction, guidance, and evaluation". Learners first attempt complex problems in groups, produce mind maps, flow charts and explanation videos, then identify key failure samples through video review and peer and self debrief before teacher-guided evaluation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers introduce typical cases and learning tasks before class and provide access to data or tools for simulation operation
- Participants are grouped with high inter-group homogeneity and large intra-group difference, with group leaders elected
- Structured video recording requirements must be defined by the teacher
- A three-stage teacher intervention of inspection, questioning, and guidance during debriefing
#### Constraints
- The article applies the pattern to PICC skill training for oncology specialist nurses in a two-month, 68-hour program at a single hospital

### Target Learners
- oncology specialist nurses in post-registration training

### Learning Goals
- PICC insertion and maintenance skills
- critical thinking
- problem-solving ability
- self-efficacy
- learning motivation
