# Printed learning material on exposition text with a process for senior high school Bahasa Indonesia

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A printed learning package for first-year senior high school Bahasa Indonesia consisting of five sub-chapters covering exposition-text theory, contextual examples, supporting figures, tables and illustrations, and four integrated guided projects leading to a formal academic report. The article states the material "has been arranged in good order contained simple to complex examples of exposition text with a process" and was standardized against Indonesian National Education Standards Board criteria before classroom use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Validated by Bahasa Indonesia lecturers and teachers and standardized against BSNP criteria on content, depth, legibility, extension, design and language before classroom use
#### Constraints
- Developed and evaluated for first-year senior high school students majoring in science at one Indonesian state senior high school

### Target Learners
- First-year senior high school (SMA) students majoring in science

### Learning Goals
- Writing exposition text with a process in formal academic reports in Bahasa Indonesia
