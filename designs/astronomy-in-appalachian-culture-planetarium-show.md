# Astronomy in Appalachian Culture: a student-built 45-minute planetarium show linking Appalachian folk science to college astronomy

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A planetarium program developed over an academic year by two junior-year undergraduate researchers with faculty mentorship at East Tennessee State University. The show was "the development of a brand new planetarium show highlighting the connections between Appalachian folk science and the systematic description of Astronomy that is offered within college classrooms." It was presented twice publicly, with audience feedback described as overwhelmingly positive.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires access to a planetarium facility with digital projection and scripting software, plus time for research, scripting, and coding across an academic year
#### Constraints
- The final production used live narration without musical tracks due to technological challenges, limited time, and music licensing complexity

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate research students
- General public audiences
- K-12 and community outreach audiences

### Learning Goals
- Science communication
- Interdisciplinary research skills
- Astronomy content knowledge
- Cultural fluency
