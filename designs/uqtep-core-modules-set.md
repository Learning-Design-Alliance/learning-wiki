# UQTEP compulsory modules: quantum algorithms, semiconductor devices, laboratory and research skills, quantum photonics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The curriculum defines compulsory subjects binding together the hardware and software aspects of quantum technology. Introduction to Quantum Algorithms covers "quantum gates, qubits (quantum bits), and quantum circuits" including Shor's and Grover's algorithms; Semiconductor Devices covers diodes, BJTs, MOSFETs and IC fabrication; Laboratory and Research Skills offers practical lab instruction operating quantum systems; Introduction to Quantum Photonics covers photon generation, control and measurement.
