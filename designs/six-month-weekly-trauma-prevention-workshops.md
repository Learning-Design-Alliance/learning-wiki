# Six-month weekly trauma-prevention workshop model for high school seniors

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's educational model consists of "Weekly educational workshops" held "for six months, explaining different trauma mechanism, traffic accidents, car crush images, pre-hospital care, surgery, recovery and sequelae that remain after injury." Individual surveys on safety-device use and attitudes toward alcohol and driving are administered before and after the workshops to measure behavioral impact. Attendance was reported at 100% over the six months.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Weekly two-hour sessions delivered over six months, with pre- and post-program individual surveys to measure behavioral impact
#### Constraints
- Evaluated in Colombian high school senior students; the authors state a more accurate study using multivariate analysis is needed

### Target Learners
- High school senior adolescents (mean age 17.5) in Colombia

### Learning Goals
- Road safety attitudes and behaviors, including helmet and seat belt use and avoiding driving under the influence of alcohol
