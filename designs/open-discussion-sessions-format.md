# Open Discussion Sessions: weekly extracurricular small-group discussions in a relaxed non-classroom setting

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A reusable lesson/unit-level design in which students meet weekly in a non-classroom setting (a large tent) in small groups of seven, each guided by an instructor, to discuss student-chosen topics. Each 50-minute session follows a set time-plan: a 10-minute instructor introduction, 25 minutes of small-group discussion, and a 15-minute whole-group summary and conclusion session. The article states "Each weekly session lasts for 50 minutes managed smoothly and systematically according to a specific time-plan."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An Activity Hour or equivalent time slot outside regular classes, with instructors available to mentor groups and prepare materials and thought questions beforehand
#### Constraints
- Participants reported one hour per week was not enough and that the format demanded good preparation of proper material and increased instructor workload

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate EFL English language majors

### Learning Goals
- Oral communicative ability in English
- Speaking confidence
- Fluency and coherent expression of opinions
