# Linked-course learning community pattern integrating study strategies with companion course content

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The pattern pairs a content course with a support course that meets twice weekly and uses the same textbook, readings, and materials, so study strategies are applied directly to actual course work. Sessions mix "short lectures, group discussions, small group assignments, and other educational activities," and enrollment is capped at 24 to create a small-class experience within large first-year courses. The article situates this within learning communities' linked-courses practice, which it reports leads to higher student learning outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Concurrent enrollment in both the content course and the IL course
- Use of the same course materials as the target class for direct application
#### Constraints
- Offered only where the course professor is supportive of the program

### Target Learners
- First-year tertiary students in academically-challenging gatekeeper courses

### Learning Goals
- Effective textbook reading and note taking
- Peer collaboration and learning networks
- Application of course content
