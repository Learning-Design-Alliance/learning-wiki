# University-level curricula structured around five focal scientific and technological topics of space science

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The regional-centre project organized education curricula at university level around five focal topics. The article states that "education curricula at the university level were developed for the core disciplines of remote sensing and the geographic information system, satellite communications, satellite meteorology and global climate, space and atmospheric science, and global navigation satellite systems". Each curriculum is published as an open-access UNOSA document, and in 2017 the curricula were supplemented by two open-access books covering full university courses in linear algebra and probability and statistics.
