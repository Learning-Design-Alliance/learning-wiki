# Holography instruction unit with experiments, computer-supported work sessions and a simulation program

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A 12-hour instruction unit on holography for upper-level physics classes, developed at the University of Potsdam, that supplements holographic experiments with computer-supported work sessions and a simulation program, using the program phenOpt and a holography simulation program. It was tested in grade 13 and evaluated with a pre-post-test design including concept maps, knowledge and motivational tests, videotaping and interviews.
