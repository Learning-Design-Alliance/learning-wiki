# Universal mental health screening systems in Cal-Well districts

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A district-level universal screening system in which every Project Cal-Well district in Stanislaus County now administers a mental health screener, such as the Covitality screening tool. Despite pandemic-era implementation hurdles and screener-selection challenges, the districts sustained ongoing screening, and the brief notes these efforts "yielded valuable lessons that have been shared with other districts across the county."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Selection of an appropriate screener and district capacity to administer it
#### Constraints
- Initial implementation hurdles related to the COVID-19 pandemic and screener selection
