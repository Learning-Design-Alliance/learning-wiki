# Learning environments for students on quantum technologies, including quantum cryptography

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The thesis describes learning environments designed for upper secondary students, covering the classical-to-quantum nature of light and quantum technologies such as quantum cryptography (BB84 and BBM92 protocols). The thesis reports: "The use of the developed approach also allowed the creation of learning environments for students that enabled them to grasp the interdisciplinary dimension needed to understand the potential of quantum technologies." These environments connect mathematics, computer science and physics to support students' exploration of quantum physics and its technological applications.
