# Teaching is My Favorite Color: a three-district regional support ecosystem for teachers of color

## Description
Teaching is My Favorite Color was developed by Avonworth, Hampton Township, and South Fayette Township school districts as "an initiative to create a holistic ecosystem of support for teachers of color across the Southwest Pittsburgh region." It had four vital elements: empowerment and amplification of voices, building a sense of belonging, professional development opportunities, and attracting and retaining diverse teachers. Activities included a social hour with a guest speaker and a convening with breakout sessions such as "Having an Affirming Classroom for Students of Color."
