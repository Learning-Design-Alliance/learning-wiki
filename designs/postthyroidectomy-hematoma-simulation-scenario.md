# Postthyroidectomy hematoma simulation scenario for junior otolaryngology residents

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A high-fidelity mannequin (SimMan 3G) simulation in which a 68-year-old male presents with an expanding cervical hematoma 2 hours after total thyroidectomy. Teams of three or four junior residents gather history, prioritize bedside hematoma decompression before operating-room arrangements, and manage confederates playing a ward nurse and an agitated family member. The article reports that "Since 2012, the simulation has been used with a total of 96 residents as part of an annual boot camp." The scenario runs 10 minutes for setup, 15–20 minutes for simulation, and 15 minutes for debriefing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A high-fidelity mannequin such as the SimMan 3G, monitors, airway and tracheostomy equipment, and a low-cost hematoma moulage packet embedded in the mannequin's neck
- Confederates playing a ward nurse and a family member, plus a guided debriefing session with guiding questions
#### Constraints
- Designed for junior residents (PGY 1, PGY 2) in otolaryngology–head and neck surgery or equivalent training levels
- No formal assessment of medical judgments was designed, as the simulation was used in a formative setting

### Target Learners
- Junior residents (PGY 1–2) in otolaryngology–head and neck surgery, anesthesiology, and other surgical specialties

### Learning Goals
- Urgent airway management and bedside hematoma evacuation
- Closed-loop communication and multidisciplinary team coordination in a crisis
