# Blended 'Sports Multimedia Courseware Design' course (4 months, 36 credits, online self-learning plus offline group-learning)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A blended PET course for Chinese university PE students combining an online self-learning section on a Superstar learning platform with an offline seminar-type smart classroom group-learning section. The course "lasted for 4 months, consisting of 36 credits (standard class hours) and 90 min for each class hour", split into 18 online credits followed by 18 offline credits. It comprised six chapters (20 sections), including 'Sports Action Video Material Shooting', and used a triangle three-camera shooting setup with EV screen recording over a unified LAN.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must fulfil the 36 credits; online self-learning is delivered first for 2 months, followed by offline group-learning for 2 months
#### Constraints
- The sequence of online-then-offline delivery was determined by COVID-19 government policy rather than learner preference

### Target Learners
- Chinese university students majoring in physical education

### Learning Goals
- multimedia courseware design skills
- information-based teaching ability
- collecting and processing sports multimedia materials
