# Sixteen-week undergraduate elective course targeting the P3 'QC literate person' profile

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 3 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A sixteen-week elective (four hours per week) that 'shifts the focus more towards QC, offering deeper insights into its applications and challenges', combining CFQT content with a selected guidebook and targeting the P3 profile with the Quantum Computing and Simulations domain. The target persona 'understands the language of quantum computing' and can 'effectively communicate with experts and beginners in the field', performing basic tasks such as quantum programming and algorithms.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prerequisites: Basic Introductory Course and STEM basic courses such as Linear Algebra, Python Programming, Probability and Complex Variables
#### Constraints
- Has prerequisites that non-STEM students typically do not fulfill

### Target Learners
- STEM undergraduates who have completed the introductory course and basic STEM coursework

### Learning Goals
- Describe fundamental quantum computing concepts, perform basic tasks in quantum programming and algorithms, and identify QC's value and industry impacts
