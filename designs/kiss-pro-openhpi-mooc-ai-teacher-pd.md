# KISS-Pro open educational resources: the openHPI MOOC on teacher professional development for AI in schools

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To facilitate broader dissemination of INSIGHT, central elements of the training were transferred into open educational resources within the KISS-Pro project. The massive open online course "Lehrkräfteprofessionalisierung für KI in Schule und Unterricht" on the openHPI platform "provides freely accessible, video-based learning modules and practice-oriented tasks that enable teachers to engage with the content in a flexible, self-paced format." The authors state this format may help reduce practical barriers to participation and support teachers in integrating AI-based technologies, including LLMs, into lesson planning and preparation of teaching materials.
