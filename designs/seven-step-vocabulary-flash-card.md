# Seven-step vocabulary flash card (VFC)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The VFC is a two-sided card the article presents as a new vocabulary learning tool. Per the article, "This seven-step vocabulary flash card consisted of word entity, word pronunciation, different parts of speech, synonyms and antonyms, dictionary example (Oxford Dictionary), learner's example and L1 definitions." The front page carries the L2 word, pronunciation and word forms; the back page carries examples and the L1 translation. Cards were made by the researcher and used across eight sessions with about 7 to 8 new words per session.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Both sides of the flash card should be used, with letters visible and large enough for the whole classroom
#### Constraints
- The flash card used in this research was limited to vocabulary from the students' textbook

### Target Learners
- female Iranian pre-university EFL students aged 18

### Learning Goals
- vocabulary knowledge, including meaning, pronunciation, word forms and spelling
