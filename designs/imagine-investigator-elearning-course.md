# IMAGINE investigator e-learning course: four H5P modules with multimedia, interactive questions and clinical vignettes

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A series of four e-learning modules developed for investigators in the IMAGINE study, "covering four key phases of protocol implementation": study eligibility criteria, outcome assessment, data collection procedures, and data security. Modules were built with H5P software on WordPress, included multimedia, interactive content and multiple-choice questions based on clinical vignettes, had learning objectives written using Bloom's taxonomy, were designed to take less than 30 minutes, and required a 75% pass mark for study participation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Satisfactory completion with a 75% pass mark was mandatory for participation in the IMAGINE study
- A basic level of English language proficiency was required for participation
#### Constraints
- Modules were publicly accessible from a web browser with an embedded Google Translate widget; translations with significant inaccuracies led users to be advised to complete the modules in English

### Target Learners
- medical students in clinical years
- junior doctors
- consultant/attending surgeons participating in a multicentre clinical study

### Learning Goals
- applying study eligibility criteria
- assessing gastrointestinal recovery outcomes
- assigning Clavien-Dindo grades
- secure REDCap data entry
