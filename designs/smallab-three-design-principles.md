# SMALLab mixed-reality design pivots on three principles: face-to-face interaction, distributed thought and action, and immediate consolidation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The SMALLab mixed-reality classroom design, presented by Birchfield and Megowan-Romanowicz, pivots on three principles: direct face-to-face interaction among co-located participants should be cultivated; thought and action should be distributed across multiple participants through an active, generative process unfolding in real time; and immediate spatial and temporal consolidation of emergent conceptual models should follow the active learning process. The editorial states each principle "supports shared knowledge" by providing preconditions and mechanisms for knowledge sharing through structured interaction.
