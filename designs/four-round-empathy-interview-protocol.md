# Four-round semi-structured interview protocol for studying faculty empathy

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study's data-collection instrument: semi-structured interviews with physics faculty across four rounds (summer 2023 to spring 2024), asking faculty to recount noticing student struggles, define empathy, give examples of cognitive and affective empathy received and given, describe communication practices and failures, and reflect on their roles. Interviews ran 'from 30 minutes to 1 hour', were audio-recorded, transcribed with Otter.ai, and returned to participants for member checking.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- participant verification of transcripts through member checking
- constructivist grounded theory analysis with inter-rater reliability coding rounds
#### Constraints
- the protocol did not specifically request affective-empathy examples until the fourth round
- the interviews were personal, which may have made some participants uncomfortable sharing affective examples

### Target Learners
- physics faculty in teaching or tenure-track positions

### Learning Goals
- eliciting faculty accounts of empathy development and empathetic action
