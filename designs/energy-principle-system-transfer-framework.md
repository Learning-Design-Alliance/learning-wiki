# Energy principle framework: system, environment, transfer and transformation as the organizing concepts for introductory energy instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article advocates a modern treatment of energy in introductory mechanics built on the principle of conservation of energy as a fundamental principle of nature, rather than on Newton's laws. Total energy is expressed as Esystem = K + U + Eint, with changes explained by transfers across the system boundary: "its increase or decrease can always be explained in terms of some input or output across the system's boundary". The framework prioritizes the concepts of system, environment, transfer and transformation of energy, from which the mechanical energy conservation theorem is derived only under restricted assumptions (isolated system, all internal forces conservative).
