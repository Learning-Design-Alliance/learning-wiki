# Make humanities-informed STEM courses a required facet of the core curriculum, with continued student support afterward

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend institutionalizing HI-STEM: "Ultimately, we recommend that this course becomes a required facet of the core curriculum for all STEM students in higher education." They condition this on lasting cultural changes in higher education accompanying classroom change, and on students continuing to be supported after the course, proposing a virtual community to keep students connected with each other and with instructors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Success hinges on lasting cultural changes in higher education and beyond, with students continuing to be supported after completing the course.
#### Constraints
- The curriculum is a one-term survey course spending limited time on key texts; the authors recommend expanding it into multiple courses and establishing post-course support networks.

### Target Learners
- all undergraduate and graduate STEM students in higher education

### Learning Goals
- critical understanding of the social, political, and cultural impacts of scientific and technological knowledge
