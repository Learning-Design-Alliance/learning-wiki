# Staged project deliverables with weighted points and iterative faculty feedback across a semester

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course project is structured as three staged deliverables: Part 1, a complete analysis of the data with graphs, tables, and computations; Part 2, a complete intermediate draft including abstract, introduction, method, results, discussion, and bibliography; and Part 3, the final report for a grade. Point weights were tuned to reward early work: "We've settled on 20% of the project points for Part 2" and Part 1 was raised "up to 30% of the total project points in order to encourage better work at this stage, because a good start is key to quality work going forward."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty mentors available and accessible for regular feedback on each stage
#### Constraints
- Groups who procrastinate until the night before deliverables often produce incorrect or incomplete analysis

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate freshmen with weak mathematics and writing skills

### Learning Goals
- Data analysis
- Scientific report writing
- Responding to feedback
