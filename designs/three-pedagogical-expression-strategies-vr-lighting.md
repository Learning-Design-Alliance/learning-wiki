# Three pedagogical expression strategies for VR lighting instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
From analysis of five beginner-level stage lighting courses, the authors identified three recurring strategies for VR implementation. "Spatial Geometry" uses "spatially mapped arrows, text, and lines" to emphasize directional vectors and light propagation. "Contextual Tagging" overlays dynamic labels on fixtures and console panels to link jargon with physical components. "Actionable Demos" provide real-time demonstrations of lighting adjustments to illustrate parameter change effects during live instruction.
