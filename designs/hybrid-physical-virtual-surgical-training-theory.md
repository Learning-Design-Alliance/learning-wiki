# Hybrid physical-virtual training platforms: combining physical realism with virtual measurement, evaluation and AR feedback

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article articulates a hybrid approach to surgical skills training that merges the two dominant platform types. Physical platforms give realistic tools, haptics and environment interaction but no automatic assessment; virtual simulators give objective evaluation and varied scenarios but lack realism and remain expensive. Hybrid platforms are described as "based on a physical device that reproduces the anatomical cavity, allows the use of real surgical tools and presents the capability of measuring and evaluating in real time the execution of the exercises", adding augmented reality as a virtual mentor. EndoTrainer is the article's instantiation of this framework.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Real-time data fusion from tool tracking, computer vision and workspace sensors to link the physical and virtual sides
- Measurement and evaluation capacities, which the article calls "the link between the physical and virtual platforms"
#### Constraints
- Orthopaedic surgery, with rigid anatomical structures, facilitates implementation of this type of simulator; natural-orifice applications are less developed
- Only a very few hybrid platforms for minimally invasive surgery have been validated

### Target Learners
- Surgical trainees learning minimally invasive and natural-orifice endoscopic procedures

### Learning Goals
- Psychomotor surgical skills
- Camera navigation and instrument handling
- Objective assessment of skill evolution
