# Incorporate asymptotic analysis throughout the high school physics curriculum, especially when introducing limits, non-ideal circuits, and model simplification

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors recommend teaching with limiting cases because asymptotic methods "Reveal fundamental system behaviors obscured in exact solutions" and resolve mathematical paradoxes through physical reasoning. They suggest embedding the approach when introducing limits and continuity in mechanics, circuit theory with non-ideal components, and model simplification techniques. The aim is cultivating deeper physical intuition and preparing students for advanced study where asymptotic methods are central.
