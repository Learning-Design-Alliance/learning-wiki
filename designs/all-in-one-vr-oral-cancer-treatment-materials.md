# All-in-one VR educational materials covering oral cancer treatments (surgery, radiotherapy, chemotherapy, molecular-targeted therapy, immunotherapy, tracheostomy care)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes self-developed 3D and 4D VR educational materials containing detailed treatment information for oral cancer, covering surgical preparation, postoperative and home care for tracheostomy, radiotherapy processes, chemotherapy, molecular-targeted therapy, and immunotherapy. Nurses interacted with a VR game in which "the VR app responded to nurses regarding whether their choices were correct." The materials were delivered via headset or smartphone display and accessed by QR code.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A VR headset or smartphone display to deliver the materials

#### Constraints
- Developed specifically for oral cancer treatment content at Taipei Veterans General Hospital

### Target Learners

### Learning Goals
- Treatment decision-related knowledge
- Skills in bedside pre-treatment patient education
