# Implement Red Light, Purple Light as short circle-time sessions twice weekly over eight weeks with trained teachers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In the studies contributing to the WWC report, the program was delivered in low-dosage, repeated circle-time sessions rather than as a separate curriculum block. Teachers implemented sessions "for approximately 20 minutes during circle time," and "The Red Light, Purple Light sessions took place twice a week for 8 weeks." Teachers implementing the sessions received a 3-hour in-person training and a developer-provided kit of materials; comparison teachers received no program training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A half-day (3-hour) in-person training from the developer, plus a training manual and a materials kit.
#### Constraints
- The evidence comes from Head Start centers in the Pacific Northwest with children from families below the poverty threshold; effects on other populations are not established in this report.

### Target Learners
- preschool children in Head Start classrooms

### Learning Goals
- self-regulation skills supporting school readiness
