# TA+CBL teaching-round scheme: theory training, three-step thinking path, group role rotation, and prompted think-aloud reporting

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A multi-step ward-round design in which new nurses receive three 45-minute theory sessions on the TA and CBL methods, train a three-step thinking path (identify the issue and data needs; integrate case data with resources; assess information adequacy), then rotate through roles of case reporter, language prompter, and data recorder in groups of three. During reporting, "when a reporter paused for more than 30 s, a language prompter would use prompts such as 'Go ahead', 'Please express your thoughts verbally'". Rounds span weeks 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, and 11 in six rotation departments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A teaching round research group of instructors with supervisory nurse titles or higher and over 5 years of oncology specialty teaching experience
- Typical cases selected and patient information, teaching objectives, and teaching purposes provided to nurses three days in advance
- Theoretical knowledge training totaling 3 times and three-step thinking path training with instructor demonstration and face-to-face guidance
#### Constraints
- Developed and piloted in a single 3A specialised cancer hospital; the article states the singular focus on a solitary cancer hospital could limit generalizability

### Target Learners
- new nurses with less than two months of clinical nursing experience in a cancer hospital

### Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning and case analysis
- critical thinking
- verbalising thought processes during case reporting
