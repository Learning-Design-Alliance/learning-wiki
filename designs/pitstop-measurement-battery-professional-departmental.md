# Multilevel measurement battery for professional and departmental guideline adherence

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The protocol measures professional-level outcomes with the 14-item QPR knowledge subscale, a six-item Confidence and Beliefs questionnaire, the 25-item SIRI-2 for recognizing appropriate responses to suicidal patients, and video-vignette guideline adherence scored against an expert panel of masters as the reference for excellent adherence. Department-level adherence is assessed one year after intervention by NEDKAD using a 10-question protocol scored by two independent assessors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants provide online informed consent; assessments occur at baseline (T0), immediately after training (T1), and three months later (T2)
#### Constraints
- The NEDKAD approach has not yet been published; the authors aim to further develop and systematize the instrument for the suicide guideline

### Target Learners
- Mental health care professionals in the participating psychiatric departments

### Learning Goals
- Knowledge, confidence, skills and guideline adherence in suicide prevention
