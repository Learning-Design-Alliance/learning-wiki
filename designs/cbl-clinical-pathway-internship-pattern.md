# Eight-step internship pattern combining CBL and clinical pathway: from disease selection through independent learning, pathway-guided patient care, and weekly case presentation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article prescribes a sequenced internship pattern: determine teaching diseases; administer pretests and questionnaires; send representative cases and questions for "independent learning" in advance; teach the clinical pathway forms on day one; follow patients from the teaching clinic through the ward; have students complete history-taking, examination, records, orders, and simulated 3D-laparoscope surgery under supervision; and hold weekly case presentations with teacher scoring and feedback. Students follow patients from outpatient to inpatient across the 4-week internship.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teaching diseases must be chosen according to the internship syllabus as common gynecological diseases
- A teaching secretary sends cases and questions in advance
- Teachers supervise and guide all clinical work students perform
#### Constraints
- Some diseases cannot be treated with clinical pathway therapy, so appropriate teaching patients must be chosen

### Target Learners
- Fourth-year 5-year-program medical students on gynecological internship

### Learning Goals
- Clinical diagnosis and treatment skills
- Clinical thinking
- Doctor–patient communication
- Independent learning
