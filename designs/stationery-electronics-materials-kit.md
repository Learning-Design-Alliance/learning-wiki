# Low-cost stationery electronics materials kit for graphite-trace sensor experiments

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A defined set of everyday materials for replicating the experiments: standard 80 g/cm2 office printer paper as substrate, soft B-type pencils as the graphite source, ruler and scissors, adhesive tape, a coffee mug and kettle for the temperature experiment, and a handheld multimeter (the article used a BOLYFA BF117, noting "any kind of handheld multimeter could be used to reproduce the experiments"). Optional items include a printer for outlines, magnets, and copper/Kapton tape contacts. Supplementary videos demonstrating the working principles are provided.
