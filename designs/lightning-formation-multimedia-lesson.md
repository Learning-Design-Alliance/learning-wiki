## Description
This is the multimedia lesson on lightning formation that Moreno developed for her first major study on multimedia learning with Mayer, published in the Journal of Educational Psychology in 1998. The editorial opens the lesson with the sentence "Cool moist air moves over a warmer surface and becomes heated" and reproduces early words and graphics from it as Fig. 1. Mayer reports that "the lightning passage has been used in more than a dozen published experiments conducted at UCSB and around the world," making it a widely reused stimulus in multimedia learning research.
