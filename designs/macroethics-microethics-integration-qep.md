# Macroethics and microethics as two realms of professional ethics, integrated via individual agency

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article adopts the engineering-ethics distinction in which "Macroethics emphasizes the quantum community's collective responsibility to society" while "microethics focuses on the individual scientist's responsibility to disciplinary no rms and conduct." Historically, ethics education has favored microethics at the expense of macroethics. QEP integrates both throughout its curriculum, using the concept of individual agency to link systemic macroethical issues with the daily decisions a quantum scientist or engineer may make.
