# Preclinical communication skills programme with simulated patient interviews at Istanbul Medipol University

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A three-year vertically structured, obligatory preclinical course within the Basic Clinical Skills corridor, coordinated by the Department of Family Medicine with trainers from basic and clinical sciences and approximately twenty trained simulated patients. First-year students receive "six hours theoretical, four hours drama work for each student, and four hours of simulated patient interviews", learning basic communication principles and history-taking of the chief complaint. Assessment uses written exams, direct observation, and OSCEs; self-assessment was in a pilot stage.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Completion before clinical years; scenarios chosen according to each term's learning objectives and horizontally integrated with the organ-system-based core curriculum
#### Constraints
- Scenario complexity increases across the three years; the study evaluated only the first-year level

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students in the preclinical period

### Learning Goals
- communication skills
- history-taking
- self-awareness
