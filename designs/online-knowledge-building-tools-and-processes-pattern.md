# Online knowledge-building trajectory built on two tools and two processes

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The online TPACK learning trajectory organizes professional learning around two tools and two processes within a social metacognitive constructivist framework. As printed, "Two tools support the online professional development: (1) a community of learners and (2) reflection," supporting social and cognitive presence; the two key processes are shared/individual knowledge development and inquiry learning, in which learners move between group and individual knowledge-building and investigate worthy questions. Reflection happens through reflective essays, peer reviews, and online portfolios.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Establishing a community of learners to support communication and interaction in the asynchronous online environment
- Critical reflection activities such as reflective essays, peer review, and portfolios
#### Constraints
- The primarily asynchronous nature of online learning poses additional challenges for the design of these educational experiences

### Target Learners
- in-service mathematics teachers in graduate online courses

### Learning Goals
- reframing teacher knowledge for teaching mathematics with technologies
- knowledge-building through inquiry and discourse
