# GoodOD guideline and its implemented ontology-design curriculum

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The GoodOD guideline is a concise report on good ontology development combining foundations from philosophical ontology and logics with the representational language OWL DL, rooted in the domain top-level ontology BioTop; it is intended for consultation rather than as beginner training material. The authors implemented it in a curriculum following a six-step approach designed for medical education, using brief hands-on exercises done individually or in small groups, with classroom lectures minimized to short introductory talks. Each instructional unit lasted 105 minutes, with four units per day over five and a half instruction days, corresponding to 22 units, and modules ordered by inter-module dependence and increasing complexity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

#### Constraints

### Target Learners
- students with a domain background in the life sciences and basic knowledge in computer sciences

### Learning Goals
- competence in formal ontology development, including use of top-level ontologies and ontology design patterns
