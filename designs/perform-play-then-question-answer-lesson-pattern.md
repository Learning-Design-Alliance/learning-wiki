# Performing-arts lesson pattern: play performance followed by teacher-led question and answer

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A repeatable lesson structure in which a prepared play related to the lesson topic is performed at the start of class and the regular course teacher then leads discussion. As the article describes it, "In the first 20-30 minutes of each lesson (time varies by subject and play), the plays were performed by the researcher and her team, and in the remaining time, the questions were answered by the social studies teacher of the class". Plays were distributed across the unit's themes (Karagöz and Hacivat, storytelling, puppetry, village play, theater) with prepared questions given to the teacher as material.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Play texts prepared in line with expert opinion for scope validity and subject integrity
- Rehearsals done with a specialist team and teachers informed about application stages
- Advance-prepared questions about the play's subject given to the course teacher as material
#### Constraints
- Performances required the researcher's team to bring equipment to each school about half an hour before lessons
- Plays had to be performed simultaneously in all schools in the same week to avoid conflicts

### Target Learners
- 6th-grade secondary school students

### Learning Goals
- Social studies unit content knowledge and permanence of knowledge
