# Networked 3D insulin-protein CSCL environment with supporting website

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A CSCL environment for gene technology consisting of computer-based 3D models (a research laboratory and a molecular level of the cells where students pick up amino acids and place them in a chain) plus a website presenting the problems and the codontable. "The 3D model was organised for distributed settings, with the participants connected in a local area network (LAN) and supplied with a telecommunication system that allowed them to communicate orally during the whole session." Students and teacher, each in a separate room, shared the 3D models and website as shared objects and were represented by avatars.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A local area network connecting all participants
- A real-time telecommunication system for oral communication
- Basic teacher training using the tools ahead of the educational setting
#### Constraints
- The 3D model comes with taxations, including weaknesses concerning the conceptual representations and a storyline that partly steals focus from the disciplinary problem

### Target Learners
- ninth-grade secondary school students

### Learning Goals
- reading DNA sequences with a codontable
- relating codons to amino acids
- building a model of the insulin-protein
