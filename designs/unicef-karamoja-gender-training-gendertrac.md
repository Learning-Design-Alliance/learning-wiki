# UNICEF/MoES gender-socialization teacher-training program with the GenderTrac SMS platform

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A five-activity teacher professional development program in gender, conflict, and identity, delivered by UNICEF and Uganda's Ministry of Education and Sports in Karamoja, Uganda. It includes a three-day training of trainers, training manuals, teacher training, ongoing support via a mobile SMS platform called GenderTrac, and two refresher trainings. The evaluation compares a complete intervention (training plus SMS) against training only and control.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained CCTs and national-level trainers to deliver the teacher training
- Distribution of training manuals to all trainers and teachers
#### Constraints
- Because of limited resources and capacity, the intervention package reached only 1,000 of approximately 2,000 teachers in the Karamoja region

### Target Learners
- Primary school teachers in Abim, Kaabong, and Napak districts, Karamoja, Uganda

### Learning Goals
- Knowledge, attitudes, and practices related to gender equality and positive gender socialization
