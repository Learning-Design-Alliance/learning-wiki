# “Online–simulation–bedside” three-step teaching sequence for team CPR skills

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A blended training sequence in which interns first learn theory online, then practice team CPR on a high-fidelity simulator in preset scenarios, and finally enter bedside clinical practice. The authors describe it as completing “the transformation from theory to practice,” moving students from “recognition,” “understanding” to “application.” Simulation serves as a bridge connecting theory and bedside teaching in a safe environment before contact with real patients.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A simulation classroom with a high-simulation simulator, prepared items and medicines, video recording equipment, and instructors who preset scenario vital-sign data
- Randomly grouped interns in monthly rotations
#### Constraints
- Team CPR performed by unskilled students on real patients may put patients at risk, motivating the simulation step; the study was conducted in a single teaching hospital

### Target Learners
- emergency and critical care nursing interns

### Learning Goals
- team CPR skills
- teamwork
- clinical thinking
- skill confidence
