# Wise interventions targeting psychosocial barriers: social belonging, values affirmation, and self-transcendent purpose for learning

## Description
The article frames its intervention within "wise interventions," programs "designed to create behavior changes that endure past the end of treatment" that target the attitude, belief, or mindset underlying a maladaptive behavior rather than the behavior itself, triggering a recursive process in which the environment reinforces new beliefs. Three approaches are central: social belonging (normalizing struggle to dissociate social identities from college obstacles), values affirmation (reestablishing self-worth during identity threat), and self-transcendent purpose for learning (connecting learning to helping others and making a difference). The summer nudges combined these to address psychosocial barriers to community college STEM persistence.
