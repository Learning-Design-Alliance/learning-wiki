# Project work with master classes producing socially significant student projects

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Within the interiorization stage, master classes in creation and presentation of projects were organized for the experimental group, ending with discussion of present problems and ways of their decision. The article reports that "28 socially significant proj ects were developed by students" addressing Russian culture and traditions, promotion of a healthy lifestyle, design of the inclusive educational environment, and creation of space promoting formation of civil activity; the prepared projects were submitted to a competition of city youth self-government. More than 50 people from 84 students who passed a master class became active participants of forums, student's self-government, moderators of projects (6 in student council, 16 forum organizers, 34 volunteers).
