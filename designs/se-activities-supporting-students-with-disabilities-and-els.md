# Seven activity types by which SE grantees equip subgrantees to serve students with disabilities and English learners

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
From open-ended survey responses, the report tallies seven activity types grantees used to ensure subgrantees can serve all students: technical assistance, partnering with the SEA, monitoring and audits, training, application requirements, contracted services, and dissemination. It states "Eleven of 19 SE grantees reported providing TA to the subgrantees." Activities were similar for both student populations, with only the topics changing, so results are reported in aggregate.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Under ESSA, SEs are responsible for ensuring charter schools receiving subgrants meet the educational needs of their students, including those with disabilities
#### Constraints
- These numbers were derived from an analysis of responses to an open-ended item, so it is possible this is an under-representation of the SE grantees engaged in each activity

### Target Learners
- Students with disabilities and English learners in charter schools receiving CSP subgrants

### Learning Goals
- Equipping subgrantees to meet the educational needs of students with disabilities and English learners
