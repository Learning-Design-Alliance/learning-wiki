# Two cross-listed NSNT courses taught by multidisciplinary faculty teams

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The project developed and offered two NSNT courses satisfying elective requirements for engineering, chemistry, and physics BS degrees. The first course, "Nanoengineering and Nanoscience", covered "the fundamentals of nanoscience and nanoparticles based on their physical and electronic structures" and was taught by Chemistry and Physics collaborators; the second, "Engineering Applications of Nanoscience & Nanotechnology", covered mechanical, electrical, and chemical properties of nanomaterials, engineering applications, and ethical, social, and environmental impacts, taught by Engineering collaborators. Multidisciplinary delivery is intended to help students understand the multidisciplinary nature of the field.
