# MOOC-HFS complementary integration framework grounded in flipped classroom and deliberate practice

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes that "MOOC and HFS are not isolated educational tools, but strongly complementary." Theoretically, the integrated model "aligns with flipped classroom and blended learning theories: MOOCs support pre-learning and standardization of theoretical knowledge; HFS supports hands-on practice and clinical reasoning." Practically, MOOCs improve consistency and flexibility of theoretical teaching while HFS provides a low-risk, repeatable environment for skill training, forming a reinforcing closed loop for skills-oriented medical education.
