# Elementary On-Track categories combine GPA and attendance thresholds into four support tiers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The playbook presents CPS's Elementary On-Track framework, introduced in 2018 to organize on-track work for younger students. It crosses two thresholds—GPA 3.0 and attendance 90%—yielding four categories: "On-Track: GPA 3.0+, Attendance 90%+"; Academic Support (GPA below 3.0, attendance 90%+); Attendance Support (GPA 3.0+, attendance below 90%); and Intensive Support (both below). Educators use the categories to ask who needs support, what students are experiencing, and whether strategies are working.

### Context

### Learning Goals
