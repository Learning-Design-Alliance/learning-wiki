# USZ surgical resident video-lecture YouTube channel

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A free, expert-curated YouTube channel from University Hospital Zurich that released 120 video presentations between January 2017 and January 2022, viewed more than 619,000 times (mean=5163 views; SD=8773). Residents prepare 20-minute lectures "under the close supervision of a specialized academic expert", recorded and edited by a professional film team, each including "3 to 5 interactive questions" and a final discussion led by the expert. Videos are tagged, quality-checked, and grouped thematically (surgical oncology, general surgery, emergency surgery; upper GI, lower GI, hepato-biliary and pancreatic, endocrine, hernia, transplantation).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Preparation time of at least three weeks per presentation
- Supervision by an established expert covering preparation, presentation, and defining learning objectives
- Professional filming, editing, and a final quality check including accompanying text and 30 content tags per film
#### Constraints
- Technical tips, tricks, and specific surgical steps were beyond the scope of the project
- Older videos are replaced after about 2 years as lectures become repetitive

### Target Learners
- surgical residents
- medical students
- certified surgeons and physicians worldwide

### Learning Goals
- comprehensive general and transplantation surgery curriculum
- current guidelines and latest research findings
- complex surgical conditions
