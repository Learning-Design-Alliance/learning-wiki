# Debriefing with good judgment: a reflective-practice debriefing method using performance gaps to explore learner frames

> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Debriefing with good judgment is a well-established debriefing method in which the debriefer uses observations of performance gaps to explore learner frames and thought processes. It "uses a 3-phase (reaction, analysis, and summary) conversational approach based in reflective practice". In this curriculum it was used in one of each session's two scenarios to focus on mental models, teamwork, and thought processing to fill in performance gaps, and was chosen for its established track record and associated assessment tool.
