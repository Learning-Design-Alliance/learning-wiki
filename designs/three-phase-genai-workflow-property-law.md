# Three-phase instructional workflow with iFlytek Spark in property law sessions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Each class session during the 16-week intervention followed a three-phase workflow (Fig. 2): pre-AI preparation, in which the instructor presents a case scenario and students draft an initial analysis independently; AI interaction, in which students submit to iFlytek Spark, compare with their own draft, engage in iterative dialogue and follow-up probing, and integrate AI feedback; and post-AI reflection, with whole-class discussion evaluating AI versus student analysis and a reflection journal entry documenting the reasoning process. The design operationalizes the constructivist scaffolding rationale of the intervention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students submit only hypothetical case scenarios and avoid personally identifiable information, per the study's data governance measures
#### Constraints
- The workflow was implemented with iFlytek Spark, a Chinese-language commercial platform selected for language appropriateness, ease of use, and compatibility with national data governance norms

### Target Learners
- third-year law students in property law courses

### Learning Goals
- legal reasoning
- iterative analysis refinement
- metacognitive awareness of reasoning processes
