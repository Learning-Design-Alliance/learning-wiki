# Case-based learning (CBL) as a teacher-led, student-oriented model that builds clinical thinking through typical-case discussion

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes CBL as "a case-based, problem-based, student-oriented, and teacher-led teaching model" in which teachers select typical cases and guide students through in-depth analysis and discussion to encourage curiosity and stimulate learning interest. Teachers do not simply transmit knowledge for passive reception; clinical practice is closely integrated into teaching, and students improve clinical thinking through case interpretation. In this study CBL was embedded in both arms: pre-operative and post-operative conditions of typical otoendoscopy cases were discussed to encourage students' subjective initiative so they could master standardised perioperative management.
