# UNAM 18-station summative end-of-career OSCE with four equivalent versions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A summative OSCE administered at UNAM Faculty of Medicine as part of the end-of-career graduating exam: four versions, each with 18 equivalent stations drawn from six areas (pediatrics, obstetrics and gynecology, surgery, internal medicine, emergency medicine, and family medicine), for 72 stations total. The exam "lasted for 2 h, with 6-min stations, and there were two rest stations"; 108 trained clinical teachers served as examiners and 124 trained standardized patients participated. Stations covered history-taking, physical exam, diagnosis and management, imaging/laboratory interpretation, and critical appraisal.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Equivalent stations built from the same blueprint across versions
- Trained examiners and standardized patients
- Random distribution of students across six simultaneous clinical sites
#### Constraints
- Only one examiner per station, preventing separation of station, examiner, and patient effects

### Target Learners
- fifth-year (internship) medical students

### Learning Goals
- clinical competence assessment across six medical knowledge areas
