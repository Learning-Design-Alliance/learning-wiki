# Messages: an interactive two-installation gallery exhibit on the cosmic ray community and science.

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Messages was produced by the IceCube education and outreach team with "local students, internationally accomplished artists, science communicators, and scientists" and displayed at the UW-Madison Memorial Union Gallery from June 14 through August 2, 2019. It consists of two original installations, Event Horizon and Messages from the Horizon, and visitor portraits were added during the show.
