# Stink bomb and chemiluminescence extension with luminol

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An extension activity in which glowing yellow solutions imitating the game item stink bomb are made from everyday products such as energy drinks with B vitamins, milk, vanilla ice cream, caramel, or honey. These solutions can be improved in the chemistry lab by adding table vinegar and hydrogen peroxide, or directly with luminol, to illustrate chemiluminescence.
