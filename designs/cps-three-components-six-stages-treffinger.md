# Treffinger, Isaksen and Dorval's three-component, six-stage Creative Problem Solving (CPS) process

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes the CPS process as a structured creativity-nurturing framework that 'mainly starts with divergent thinking and gradually end with convergent thinking.' The version adopted in this study, proposed by Treffinger, Isaksen and Dorval (1994), comprises three components — understanding the problem, generating ideas, and planning for action — and six stages: Objective-Finding, Data-Finding, Problem-Finding, Idea-Finding, Solution-Finding, and Acceptance-Finding. The author adopted this process to design the content and activities of the experimental teaching for EG1.
