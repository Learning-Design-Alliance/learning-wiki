# Inclusive adapted aquatic exercise class with undergraduate exercise personal assistants

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A socially-inclusive, university-offered adapted aquatic exercise (AE) class designed from SWD focus-group input. Each participant was paired with two to three kinesiology undergraduate exercise personal assistants who received "two weeks of intensive education and training on AE, care, transfers, etc." and earned three experiential academic credits. "Classes were held twice each week on campus, during the regular spring semester, and lasted 50 minutes," with individualized exercise programs created from initial physical assessments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Exercise personal assistants provided by the class, trained in aquatic exercise, transfers, and care
- Initial physical assessments to create individualized exercise programs
- Instructor supervision allowing assistants to adjust programs to participant needs
#### Constraints
- Results may not apply to more diverse campuses, as the intervention took place at a university recognized for its disability services

### Target Learners
- university students with physical disabilities

### Learning Goals
- exercise self-efficacy
- quality of life
- social inclusion
- recreational physical activity participation
