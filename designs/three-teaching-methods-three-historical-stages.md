# Three teaching methods mapping onto three historical stages of education: teaching-centred, information processing, and student-centred

## Description
The article organises mathematics problem-solving instruction into three methods — traditional, heuristic, and active — and states "These three methods correspond with the three historical stag es of education (teaching-centred, information processing and student-centred)." The traditional method has the teacher presenting predefined materials orally while students memorise; the heuristic method teaches named strategies such as drawing a picture, systematic tables, and eliminating inappropriate possibilities; the active method centres on students solving problems themselves with teacher guidance. The framework frames the study's comparison of the three conditions.
