# Puzzle-based board game for surgical patient safety education addressing eight common patient safety risks

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A puzzle board game designed around four elements of game-based pedagogy, played in small groups with rotating structured roles (e.g., scenario reader, solution proposer, checklist verifier), peer evaluation between groups, and facilitator feedback. "The educational puzzles were meticulously designed to address eight common patient safety risks", including the WHO safe surgery checklist stages, retained surgical items, medical gas cylinders, pathology samples, and pressure injuries. Gameplay involved classifying shuffled cards (e.g., into ISBAR handover structure) and completing a matrix game per puzzle step.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A trained facilitator team to monitor group dynamics and provide constructive feedback
- Pre-class briefing materials (patient safety scripts and info-graphics) sent one week before sessions
#### Constraints
- Implemented monthly over four months in surgical nursing education at a single institution

### Target Learners
- Surgical nursing students in six-month surgical unit internships
- Practicing surgical nurses with six or more months of clinical experience

### Learning Goals
- Reasoning skills in managing patient safety incidents
- Performance in applying the WHO surgical safety checklist and related safety practices
