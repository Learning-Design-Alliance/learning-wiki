# Rueß et al.'s classification of research-related teaching as a spectrum from receptive to independent research activity

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article adopts Rueß et al.'s systematization of research-related teaching (forschungsbezogene Lehre), defined following them as "Lehre mit einem Bezug zur Forschung" without valuing the degree of connection. The classification spans students receptively learning research results and methods, applying them, conducting limited research themselves, and engaging with the research process. The article maps its course onto this spectrum: the lecture already covered receptive elements, the interactive units added application and limited research activity, and the proposal sketch added receptive and applied engagement with the research process itself.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Courses should be located on the classification to identify which facets of research-related teaching they cover.
#### Constraints
- The final element — independently carrying out the entire research process — remained uncovered in the lecture; the article suggests a follow-up master's thesis could cover it.

### Target Learners
- university students in research-related courses

### Learning Goals
- understanding the spectrum of research-related teaching formats
- positioning course designs along the research-teaching nexus
