# Manikin-based laryngoscopy training protocol with lecture, demonstration and video instruction for novice intubators

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A simulation-based training protocol for inexperienced anesthesia nurses combining a device-operation video, an anesthesiologist-led lecture and demonstration on a Laerdal Airway Management Trainer manikin, and randomized hands-on practice across devices and neck positions; each participant performed "a total of 18 endotracheal intubations". It was used to measure how quickly novices acquire intubation performance on different laryngoscopes under normal and cervical immobilized conditions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants without previous intubation experience
- Video instruction plus anesthesiologist lecture and demonstration before practice
- Manikin model with randomized scenario sequence
#### Constraints
- The manikin model "does not entirely reflect real-life clinical situations"; airway secretions, other difficult-airway causes, and the quiet operating-room setting limit transfer to real patients

### Target Learners
- novice anesthesia nurses without previous intubation experience

### Learning Goals
- tracheal intubation skill acquisition with direct and videolaryngoscopes
