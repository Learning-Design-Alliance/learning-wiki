# Decision making about the future learning outcome framework with twelve components

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article develops a 'decision making about the future' learning outcome framework informed by decision-making science, AI methodologies, and educational and psychological perspectives. It has twelve components and treats sustainability as dynamic: "AI technology enables sustainability to be treated as a dynamic and evolving concept of sustainability, and learners are able to study the nature and rate of anthropogenic environmental change, identify likely future challenges and opportunities, and make decisions". It serves as a methodological guide for designing AI-supported activities.
