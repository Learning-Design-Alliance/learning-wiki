# Three Grand Challenges framework for FuturICT education: effectiveness, cost, and exemplary interdisciplinary demonstration

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper organises its educational programme around three named Grand Challenges: enabling people to learn orders of magnitude more effectively, enabling people to learn at orders of magnitude less cost, and demonstrating success through exemplary interdisciplinary education in complexity science. The authors interpret 'order of magnitude' as "a factor between two and ten", with a factor of two an 'almost achievable' minimum and ten the aspirational goal. The framework is meant to be tested through exemplar programmes rather than theory alone.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Explicitly defined, measurable learning outcomes for the effectiveness challenge, including knowledge and understanding, cognitive skills, key skills, and practical and professional skills, plus learning time and learner enjoyment and engagement as measures
#### Constraints
- The authors state the ten-fold aspiration "some think unattainable" and that making education twice as effective at half the cost would itself be an historic achievement

### Target Learners
- learners at all educational levels, starting with tertiary and postgraduate students

### Learning Goals
- measurable gains in knowledge, cognitive, key and practical skills; reduced cost of learning; interdisciplinary complex-systems competence
