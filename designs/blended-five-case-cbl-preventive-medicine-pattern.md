# Blended CBL course pattern: five clinically linked preventive-medicine cases taught through paired online self-study and offline case sessions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course organized preventive medicine content into "five distinct case themes" — tobacco control, rational nutrition guidance, infectious disease prevention and control, occupation-related diseases, and public health emergencies — each drawn from "environment, diet, nutrition, lifestyle, and occupational exposure" perspectives and linked to clinically relevant diseases. Each case was split into first and second halves, with 280 minutes of online video self-study, 4 virtual simulation periods, 20 lecture periods, and 20 case-teaching periods. Case writing teams included at least one clinician and peer-reviewed drafts before final evaluation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Case writing teams must include at least one clinician, and groups mutually review and revise one another's drafts before a review team's final evaluation.
#### Constraints
- Case scheduling was fitted to the class schedule of Preventive Medicine at Wenzhou Medical University, and each case occupied 4 periods divided into 2 sessions of 2 periods each.

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate clinical medicine students in a Sino-foreign joint training program

### Learning Goals
- Preventive medicine concepts, knowledge, and skills; integration of clinical and preventive medicine; comprehensive application of knowledge
