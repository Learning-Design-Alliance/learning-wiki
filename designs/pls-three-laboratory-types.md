# Three types of PLS laboratories: vocation-approaching, self-assessment, and deepening laboratories

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes that PLS laboratory activities be organized into three types: "laboratories which approach the discipline and develop vocations", "self-assessment laboratories for improving the standard required by graduate courses", and "deepening laboratory for motivated and talented students". In southern Tuscany these types were realized in practice as curricular classroom laboratories, extracurricular vocation laboratories such as Under the starry sky, and extracurricular deepening laboratories such as light-curve measurement and spectroscopy paths, with laboratories for entire classes or small groups, curricular or extracurricular.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Joint programming activity with teachers
- Students as main actors performing experiments
- Coordination at national level with local referents
#### Constraints
- Some schools lack laboratory facilities or space and resources for student-performed experiments

### Target Learners
- High school students, both entire classes and selected small groups

### Learning Goals
- Developing vocations toward scientific degrees
- Meeting graduate-course standards
- Deepening physics understanding for motivated and talented students
