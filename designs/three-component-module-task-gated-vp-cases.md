# Three-component module structure with task-gated progression for virtual-patient cases

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The concept converts overarching learning goals — defined via the NKLZ and clinical course requirements in line with Constructive Alignment — into modules consisting of case information, additional information and learning tasks. Each section of the case forms a block with a learning task matched to the chosen learning goals, and answering the task gates access to subsequent contents. The narrative runs from the first appointment including medical history to completion of oral rehabilitation. The authors call its clear structure the concept's greatest strength, simplifying use and guiding case creation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Overarching learning goals defined in advance based on NKLZ and clinical course requirements, so each module serves as a template for the respective section
#### Constraints
- Teachers were indispensable as supervision of created content in terms of its accuracy was necessary

### Target Learners
- dental students in preclinical education

### Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning, communicative competences, and dental treatment planning
