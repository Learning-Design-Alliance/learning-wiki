# Teacher-built web-based Physics video channel of 153 videos for N, O and O-level Pure Physics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A YouTube channel created by Mr Oh Ming Yeo of Fuchun Secondary School to support students taking 'N', 'O' Science Physics and 'O' Pure Physics. It contains "a series of 153 web -based Physics videos, which include topical concepts, experimental skills and ‘O’ Level detailed solutions for students to learn anytime". The chapter reports the channel was viewed more than 100,000 times locally and globally within a year and had 300 subscribers, with positive feedback from parents, students and teachers.
