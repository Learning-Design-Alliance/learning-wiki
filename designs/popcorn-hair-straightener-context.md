# Popcorn-with-a-hair-straightener context as an interdisciplinary, multi-sensory phenomenon for inclusive science lessons

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The manual's worked example uses making popcorn with a hair straightener, demonstrated in a TikTok video, as the scientific issue. The topic "is relevant to various disciplines in science education, including biology, chemistry, and physics, and can be approached in an interdisciplinary manner": physics lessons can discuss pressure, temperature and heat transfer, chemistry the molecular structure of starch and its denaturation, biology the structure of corn kernels. Pedagogically, the context "can be experienced through multiple senses and is both interesting and motivating due to its relevance to everyday life."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A phenomenon accessible through multiple senses and relevant to everyday life, so that learners with different prior experiences can engage with the context.
#### Constraints
- Gender may play a role, e.g., when the video with the hair straightener is used; learners in year 7 may be interested in the context only to a limited extent.

### Target Learners
- secondary school science learners (year 7 example)
- learners with diverse sensory and cultural backgrounds

### Learning Goals
- interdisciplinary science content: pressure, temperature and heat transfer; molecular structure of starch and its denaturation; structure of corn kernels
- reasoning about scientific issues
