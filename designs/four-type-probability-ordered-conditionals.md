# Four-type probability-ordered classification of English conditionals

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A lesson-level classification presenting conditionals in order of high-to-low probability of the result-clause event: factual (probability = 100%), predictive (~50%), hypothetical (~10%), and counterfactual (0%). Each type pairs a probability band with its verb-phrase structure, e.g. "If+past perfect→ would have+past participle" for counterfactuals. The article presents this ordering in Table 2 as the core instructional content of the Probability Approach.
