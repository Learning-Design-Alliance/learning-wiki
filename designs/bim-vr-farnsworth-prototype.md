# BIM+VR Farnsworth House gamified precedent-study prototype

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A Unity game-engine prototype in which users explore an architectural precedent (the Farnsworth House) in first-person VR, watch an in-game video narration, practice object interaction, then complete assemblage tasks using LOD 200 LEGO components and LOD 300 precise-geometry components. The model was built in Autodesk Revit, abstract concepts modeled in Rhinoceros, and merged in Unity with the SteamVR plugin. The authors state it "provides a less costly approach and improved accessibility to well-known architectural precedents with a potential for learning and engagement."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- VR hardware and a game-engine environment (Unity with SteamVR plugin); BIM models prepared in Autodesk Revit and Rhinoceros
#### Constraints
- Limitations included difficulty generating custom scripts and retention of BIM geometry data in Unity when converting Revit objects to game objects

### Target Learners
- architecture and construction students
- users without formal architectural or construction education

### Learning Goals
- understanding architectural precedent tectonics and design principles
- building composition and assemblage knowledge
