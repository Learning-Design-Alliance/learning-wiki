# Spaced education model combining the spacing effect and the testing effect

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes spaced education (SE) as "a novel, evidence-based form of online education demonstrated to improve knowledge acquisition in randomized trials". Its classical protocol "involves a spacing effect and testing effect": repeated exposure over time reinforces and consolidates retention, and testing improves learner performance. This study implemented a modified version focused on the spacing effect via email reinforcement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires repeated scheduled exposure of content over a given time period, typically paired with multiple-choice questions
#### Constraints
- This study's variant omitted immediate feedback and regimented question repetition, so it did not use a typical SE protocol

### Target Learners
- medical residents and interns

### Learning Goals
