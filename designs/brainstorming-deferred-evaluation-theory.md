# Brainstorming as idea generation with deferred evaluation for developing creative thinking

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article presents brainstorming as a teaching method in which ideas are generated without immediate evaluation: "brainstorming is a way of bringing ideas without consideration of their evaluation", with evaluation postponed to the end of the session. It is characterized as working on the flow of ideas without criticism, speeding thinking, breaking deadlock, and challenging minds, and as encouraging all participants to contribute to teamwork. The article uses this account to frame brainstorming as a way to develop creative thinking and positive learner participation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Postponement of evaluation to the end of the session rather than abandoning evaluation
#### Constraints
- The article notes brainstorming is not guaranteed to produce new ideas
