# Five-chapter progression from programming-language basics to complex numerical simulations of quantum circuits and interacting particles with spin

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The manual is organized as a deliberate progression: "In ﬁve chapters, this book takes the student all the way to relatively complex numerical simulations of quantum circuits and interacting particles with spin". Chapter 1 introduces the Wolfram language and can be skipped or replaced; chapter 2 connects quantum mechanics to vector/matrix algebra; chapter 3 treats finite-dimensional Hilbert spaces, spin systems and qubits; chapter 4 develops real-space descriptions of particle motion; chapter 5 combines spin and spatial motion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Chapter 1's language introduction is prerequisite material for readers unfamiliar with the Wolfram language, though it may be skipped or replaced by an alternative introduction
#### Constraints
- The methods presented do not pretend to solve large-scale quantum-mechanical problems efficiently; the focus is on developing a descriptive language

### Target Learners
- Master's and PhD students in physics and related fields

### Learning Goals
- expressing and manipulating quantum-mechanical ideas computationally, from basic states and operators to coupled spin-space dynamics
