# The t'-score framework: a continuous, set-paradigm measure of disagreement between two measurements relative to their combined uncertainty

## Description
The t'-score takes the difference between two measurements A and B and normalizes it by the combined uncertainty of the difference, defined as t'=A−B over the square root of δA²+δB². The article states that "the t'-score gives a quantitative measure of how different the two measurements are relative to their uncertainties", similar to sigma levels in particle physics. It replaces dichotomous overlapping-error-bar comparisons with a continuous scale and prescribes follow-up behaviours by score magnitude (Table 1).
