# Action physics: unified introductory instruction from many-paths quantum physics through stationary action mechanics to relativity

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Action physics is a unified first-year university curriculum organised around the principle of stationary action, running from many-paths quantum physics through ray optics, classical mechanics and relativity. Its rationale is "uniting different areas of physics under the stationary action principle, and explaining classical physics in terms of quantum physics".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students are required to take mathematics courses covering: complex numbers, differential equations, and partial differentiation
#### Constraints
- Action physics "does not naturally incorporate friction, a central part of everyday experience"
- The "electromagnetism part of the curriculum is outside the domain of action physics"

### Target Learners
- first-year university physics majors, described as high-achievers at the national level

### Learning Goals
- understanding the stationary action principle
- explaining classical physics in terms of quantum physics
- a unified view of quantum, classical and relativistic physics
