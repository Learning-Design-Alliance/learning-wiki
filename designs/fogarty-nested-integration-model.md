# Fogarty's nested integration model for embedding a second learning target within a single discipline

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The nested model is one of Fogarty's models for integrating curriculum within a single discipline, in which a second set of objectives is nested inside an existing course. The article states that "The nested integration model monitors the training program with three -dimensional glasses and targets multiple dimensions of a course" and that attention is drawn to more than one area at the same time, providing enriched and improved learning paths. The study applied it by nesting intercultural sensitivity development activities inside a scientific research methods course.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A host discipline whose outcomes can accommodate activities serving the nested objective, as the scientific research methods course outcomes accommodated intercultural activities
#### Constraints
- The study applied the model within a single discipline (scientific research methods) in one English teacher education department, and the article limits its conclusions to activities integrable with that course's outcomes

### Target Learners
- second-year pre-service English teachers in higher education

### Learning Goals
- intercultural sensitivity development
- scientific research methods outcomes
