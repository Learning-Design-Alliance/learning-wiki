# Two-year remote UDL-based faculty development programme with hands-on Web 2.0 tool training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A faculty development programme conducted remotely between 2020 and 2022 in which 262 medical faculty members were trained in UDL-aligned Web 2.0 tools (Padlet, Wooclap, Thinglink, Mindomo, Mindmeister, Edpuzzle, Google Forms, Formative, Kahoot). Each four-hour session was repeated 20 times over two years and delivered via the university's distance education platform. Overall satisfaction was moderately high (M = 3.90, SD = 1.1), with highest scores for pace of the training and likelihood of recommending it.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sessions used the university's distance education platform and included interactive videos, collaborative group activities, and real-time Q&A sessions.
#### Constraints
- The programme's impact remained at an early stage: most educators did not use the tools after training, per the authors.

### Target Learners
- medical faculty across basic, clinical, and surgical sciences departments

### Learning Goals
- integrating Web 2.0 tools into flexible and interactive educational environments consistent with UDL principles
