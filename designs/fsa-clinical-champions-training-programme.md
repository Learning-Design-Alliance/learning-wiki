# Family Safety Advocate clinical champions training programme with community of practice

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The hospital's clinical champions pathway: 'The clinical champions programme provided in-depth training in evidence-based, best-practice responding when working with patients experiencing family violence (9+ hours in total)'. It combined staged training (a 3-h internal seminar plus 6.5-h external CRAF3 training in 2018-2019; from 2020 a 1-h self-paced online module and an 8-h live online seminar) with monthly clinical supervision and quarterly network meetings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Further and ongoing resourcing, such as ongoing access to refresher training and a permanently funded family violence team that is available to support clinicians when needed
#### Constraints
- COVID-19 pandemic disruption in 2020 reduced the training provided and the number of attendees at training sessions relative to preceding years

### Target Learners
- Hospital clinical staff, predominantly allied health professionals, with social workers the largest professional group

### Learning Goals
- Risk assessment and management, safety planning, referrals, sensitive practice and responding to disclosures
