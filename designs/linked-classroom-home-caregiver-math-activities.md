# Linked classroom and home caregiver-child math activity model for early mathematics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
This instructional pattern pairs weekly teacher-led small-group classroom math activities with caregiver-child home activities linked to the classroom content. Each classroom activity includes "a core lesson as well as additional activities to tailor the lesson for lower- and higher-performing children" and scaffolding suggestions for common errors. Home packets contain the activity, manipulatives, and a bilingual caregiver letter with a picture strip depicting how to conduct the activity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher training in implementing the curriculum with fidelity and in progress monitoring, plus ongoing in-class coaching
#### Constraints
- Implemented in Head Start and state-funded preschool settings with low-income families; effects on non-mathematics domains were uncertain

### Target Learners
- preschool children ages approximately 3.4 to 4.5 years from low-income families

### Learning Goals
- early mathematics achievement and informal mathematical knowledge
