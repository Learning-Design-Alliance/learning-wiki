# Translational medicine as a bridging framework for integrating simulation and bedside teaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article applies translational medicine (TM), an interdisciplinary "bench to bedside" approach, as the guiding concept for connecting basic medical science with clinical situations in skills training. It argues TM "acts as a bridge for the bi-directional conversion between basic research and clinical practice", and uses this idea to justify tightly alternating simulation scenarios with real clinical practice rather than teaching theory, simulation and bedside work in isolation.
