# Fresno TK–3 Teacher Residency Program

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An 18-month residency (2017–2019) within Fresno Unified's workforce pipeline, run with California State University, Fresno, leading to a Multiple Subject credential and a master's degree in curriculum and instruction. The program focused on "early literacy, developmentally appropriate practices, and social and emotional learning" plus family engagement, with all credential coursework co-taught by district leaders paired with university faculty and quarterly curriculum meetings used to redesign syllabi around a developmental focus. Residents received an $11,000 stipend, clustered school-site placements, and courses held at a school site near their clinical placements.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- District–university co-instruction of all credential coursework
- Federal TQP grant funding for the resident stipend
#### Constraints
- The stipend was not enough to fully cover residents' cost of living; some residents substituted one day a week to supplement income

### Target Learners
- Recent college graduates, career changers, and uncertified educators seeking TK–3 credentials

### Learning Goals
- Developmentally appropriate pedagogy, early literacy, STEM integration, and social-emotional learning for TK–3
