# Interactive, research-inspired learning environments in which students work as teams of scientists do

## Description
The article frames physics learning on the model of scientific research as a dynamic and exploratory process of creation, testing and improvement of mathematical models. It argues that physics may be more successfully taught in interactive and exploratory environments where students, helped by teachers, work as teams of scientists do, promoting knowledge performance and fighting common sense beliefs and incorrect scientific notions, with computation given a central role alongside theory and experiment.
