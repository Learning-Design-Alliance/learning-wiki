# Delrin bilayer chamber with replaceable PTFE film for hands-on bilayer formation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The bilayer chamber consists of two compartments milled in two blocks of polyoxymethylene (Delrin) pressed together by a pair of screws, with a commercially available 50 µm PTFE film sealed with silicon grease between them; a circular 200 µm aperture is etched in the film before insertion. This design choice allows easy replacement of the Teflon film, which is sometimes torn during student manipulations. Each compartment connects to the amplifier through an Ag-AgCl electrode and a salt bridge, and the chamber sits in an aluminium box acting as a Faraday cage to minimize electric noise.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Commercially available PTFE film with an etched 200 µm aperture, sealed with silicon grease; Ag-AgCl electrodes and salt bridges (1 M KCl, 2% Agar)
#### Constraints
- Mechanical vibrations must be damped, which the authors achieve by placing the chamber on a heavy granite slab resting on four tennis balls

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students performing their first electrophysiology laboratory work

### Learning Goals
- forming and monitoring planar lipid bilayers
- measuring bilayer capacitance and resistance
