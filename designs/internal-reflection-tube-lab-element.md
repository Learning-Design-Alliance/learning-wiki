# Internal reflection tube: a no-prior-experience-needed laboratory activity for observing internal light reflections

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The internal reflection tube is "a specially de signed, clear acrylic tube that measures 7.5 cm in diameter and 12.5 cm in height". Students shine a laser beam onto a polished step cut into the upper end and see the beam's path as a spiral inside the tube wall. The article positions it as a corresponding laboratory project requiring no prior experience, paired with the basic transmission system lecture demonstration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An internal reflection tube (available from science suppliers) and a laser beam source
#### Constraints
- Designed as a no-prior-experience-needed laboratory project accompanying the lecture demonstration

### Target Learners
- college students, including those with no prior optics experience

### Learning Goals
- observing internal reflections of light
- building intuition for light guiding in fibers
