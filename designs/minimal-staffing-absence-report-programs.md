# Attendance interventions can be delivered at scale with minimal staffing, training, and technology

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The page specifies that the Absence Reports intervention requires minimal staffing — one district staff member supporting implementation at approximately 30 minutes per week and one district-level data staff member supporting data transfer in under 3 hours total — and that professional development is minimal, with "none required aside from the staffing requirements described above," and no technology required. This shows a low-burden delivery model can accompany a statistically significant attendance effect, making district adoption feasible without extensive training infrastructure.
