# Multimodal rTEE training curriculum with monthly criterion-based proficiency simulator sessions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A pilot curriculum combining clinical introduction (observing clinical rTEEs with supervised probe manipulation), asynchronous web-based didactics, and monthly hands-on sessions on the HeartWorks rTEE high-fidelity simulator. Training was "organized into clinical introduction, synchronous didactics, and simulator training," with proficiency defined as errorless acquisition of eight adequate rTEE views on cue, and training continued for four months even after proficiency was met.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A board-certified rTEE instructor who assesses proficiency monthly via direct observation
- A high-fidelity rTEE simulator and access to clinical rTEE observation for the clinical introduction component
#### Constraints
- The article notes the simulator may be easier than clinical practice due to absence of complications like probe insertion or chest compression motion, and should be viewed as a complementary tool rather than a replacement for hands-on training

### Target Learners
- rTEE-naive emergency physicians proficient in transthoracic echocardiography

### Learning Goals
- Acquisition and retention of eight resuscitative transesophageal echocardiography views, including naming, probe manipulation, and image acquisition
