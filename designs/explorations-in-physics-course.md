# Explorations in Physics introductory course for non-science majors

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Explorations in Physics (EiP) is a concept-oriented introductory physics course for non-science majors based on physics education research, incorporating guided-inquiry techniques with small group projects in a collaborative learning environment. Students spend one-third of the quarter on student-directed projects culminating in a written report and poster presentation, and the remaining two-thirds working through two activity guides. The course draws students from majors including information technology, computer science, and economics, and seeks to instill scientific literacy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Student-directed group projects with joint proposals and experiments
- Activity guides structuring guided-inquiry experiments
#### Constraints
- The division of time between projects and guided inquiry is noted as relevant because the study found differences in student writing between project and other reports

### Target Learners
- non-science majors from a variety of disciplines

### Learning Goals

- evaluating and communicating new information
