# SILS appendectomy resident-training protocol with five designated critical steps

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A training and evaluation protocol in which three PGY III residents, each previously involved in at least 10 SILS procedures and versed in conventional laparoscopic appendectomy, performed SILS appendectomies under a single attending. Execution was evaluated on "5 designated critical steps in the operation": access, dissection, endoloop ligation, retrieval of specimen, and closure. Progressive improvement was judged by successful completion of key steps without attending intervention, with secondary outcomes including complications, operative time, conversion, and hospital stay.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Residents must be well versed in conventional laparoscopic appendectomy and encouraged to practice on a laparoscopic trainer box before learning SILS
#### Constraints
- Evaluation was by a single attending surgeon using descriptive statistics because of the small study set, not objective performance criteria

### Target Learners
- PGY III general surgery residents

### Learning Goals
- mastery of the critical steps of SILS appendectomy
