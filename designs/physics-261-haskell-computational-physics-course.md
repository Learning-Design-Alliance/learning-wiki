# Physics 261: a second-year computational physics elective requiring no prior programming experience

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Introduction to Computational Physics (Physics 261), a second-year one-semester elective at Lebanon Valley College where 'No previous programming experience is expected or assumed.' The course has two parts: learning a subset of Haskell, then using it for Newtonian mechanics and electromagnetic theory; 'most students seem able to become proﬁcient in this subset of Haskell in about seven weeks.'

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prerequisites are 'one year of introductory physics and one semester of calculus.'
#### Constraints
- The Haskell subset taught intentionally avoids explicit recursion and the creation of new algebraic data types.

### Target Learners
- second-year undergraduate physics students

### Learning Goals
- strengthening understanding of basic physics
- learning a subset of Haskell
