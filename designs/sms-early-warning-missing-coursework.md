# SMS early-warning contact immediately upon missing a continuous assessment component

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An early-warning component of the Director of Learning Support role in which the Director "would contact students by SMS immediately upon them missing any continuo us assessment component of the class". Students volunteer their mobile numbers rather than being required to provide them, and SMS is chosen over telephone calls because it gives students a buffer to compose a reasoned response and actually submit the work.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students are asked to volunteer their mobile telephone numbers rather than being required to provide them.
#### Constraints
- The article contrasts this with the previous approach of contacting students about missing work only at the end of the first semester, which could be too late for students who had missed many deadlines.

### Target Learners
- first-year physics and astronomy undergraduates at the University of Glasgow

### Learning Goals
- timely submission of continuous assessment work
- passing level 1 physics
