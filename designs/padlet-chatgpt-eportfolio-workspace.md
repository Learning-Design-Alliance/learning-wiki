# Padlet-plus-ChatGPT digital workspace configuration for e-portfolio speaking tasks

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In the CEA implementation, students used two linked platforms: Padlet as the primary e-portfolio environment for uploading recorded speaking videos, receiving peer comments, and exchanging collaborative feedback, and ChatGPT as a secondary workspace. The article states that "students used ChatGPT as a secondary digital workspace to help them organize the feedback generated by AI, capture their self-reflections using a Google Form, and archive practice transcripts to refer back to when they were working alone." Speaking performances were 3 to 5-minute recorded videos evaluated with rubrics covering complexity, accuracy, and fluency.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to Padlet, ChatGPT (with voice-mode interaction), and a Google Form for self-reflection; lecturer and peer commenting activity on uploaded videos
#### Constraints
- Padlet was chosen for its multimodal, collaborative, socially visible interface; the control group's Google Drive lacked these features, which the authors flag as a possible confound

### Target Learners
- vocational college EFL students

### Learning Goals
- business and professional English speaking tasks
- giving and using feedback
