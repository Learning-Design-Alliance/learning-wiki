# Socrative-supported iRAT and tRAT sequence in the flipped classroom session

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The flipped classroom session used an individual readiness assurance test (iRAT) followed by a team readiness assurance test (tRAT), then PowerPoint-based discussion, concept recall writing, and peer question exchange. The online platform Socrative hosted the tests: "The iRAT and tRAT were conductued in Socrative which is an on line application in which you can conduct quizzes, surveys, team activities, and educational content". Students received individual scores without knowing which questions were correct, then debated issues and taught concepts to one another in teams.
