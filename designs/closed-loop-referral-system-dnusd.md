# Closed-loop referral system linking DNUSD wellness centers with community and county behavioral health providers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
DNUSD established a closed-loop referral system with Project Cal-Well support after earlier referrals to community partners were often lost to waiting lists and lacked follow-up communication. The system "enables community-based service providers to refer students to the district's school-based wellness centers if they cannot meet their needs" and encourages regular communication with the county's Department of Behavioral Health, improving identification of unmet mental health needs in the district.
