# In-situ simulation as a high-fidelity training approach conducted in clinicians' usual working environment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article defines in-situ simulation as "simulated clinical scenarios in participants' usual working environment," offering "a higher level of fidelity compared to other settings." It is described as enhancing knowledge retention, helping learners maintain composure in real encounters, benefiting interdisciplinary teams exposed to ED design and equipment, and identifying systems issues that would otherwise go undiscovered.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires use of the clinical space for the scenario itself, with debriefing possible elsewhere
#### Constraints
- A common barrier is the concern that it contributes to a delay in providing patient care

### Target Learners
- emergency department clinicians
- interdisciplinary team members
- undergraduate and postgraduate medical trainees

### Learning Goals
