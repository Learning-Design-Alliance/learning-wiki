# Brief, structured, unit-based repeated education sessions embedded in clinical workflow

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention pattern consists of "three monthly 50-min education sessions" delivered in the ICU setting, with content covering "MDRO definitions, transmission routes, national IPC guidelines, proper isolation techniques and outbreak case reviews". Delivery used "lecture-based presentations, visual aids and brief scenario-based discussions", with sessions offered twice daily to accommodate rotating shifts. This unit-based, workflow-embedded format achieved 100% attendance and completion across all three sessions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sessions must be brief (50 min) and scheduled around shift work, offered twice daily
- Attendance is mandatory and facilitated by infection control staff with advanced IPC training
#### Constraints
- Evaluated in the surgical and medical ICUs of a single 600-bed general hospital in South Korea

### Target Learners
- ICU nurses with at least 6 months of experience

### Learning Goals
- MDRO infection control knowledge
- adherence to infection prevention and control practices
