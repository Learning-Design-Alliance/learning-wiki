## Description
The activity sequences three steps: qualitative observation of a color gradient across cuvettes, spectral measurement of transmission and absorbance, and plotting absorbance versus concentration with fitted linear models. The article states that 'the sequence of Figures 2-4 allows students to connect qualitative observation (color change) with quantitative evidence (spectral data and fitted plots),' reinforcing movement from observation to analysis and interpretation.
