# Implement AI-assisted PBL within a teacher-led framework with faculty review of AI-generated content

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article implements DeepSeek-assisted PBL inside a teacher-led structure: instructors reviewed AI-generated materials before class, joined discussions, and corrected misleading outputs in real time. Students were required to "critically cross-reference AI-generated information against standard ophthalmology textbooks" rather than treat AI answers as authoritative. The authors argue this mitigates risks from LLM hallucinations in clinically complex fields.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors review AI-generated materials prior to classroom use
- Students receive training in critically evaluating AI-generated information
#### Constraints
- Medical students' skepticism alone is insufficient to guard against AI-generated misinformation, per the article citing prior work

### Target Learners
- fourth-year undergraduate medical students

### Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning in glaucoma
- critical evaluation of AI-generated medical information
