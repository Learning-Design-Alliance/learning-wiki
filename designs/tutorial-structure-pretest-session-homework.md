# Tutorial instructional structure: pretest, group tutorial session, and homework with facilitator support

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The tutorial framework supplements lectures and textbook with three components: "a 10-minute ungraded pretest, a 50-minute tutorial session, and a tutorial homework assignment." Students work in groups of three or four on worksheets guiding qualitative reasoning, while teaching assistants act as facilitators asking questions that help students work through difficulties in their own thinking. The pretest, given after lectures and readings but before the tutorial, helps both student and instructor recognize gaps in understanding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A weekly training session in which tutorial instructors complete the same pretest and tutorials as students and discuss student responses
#### Constraints
- Developed and implemented as a supplement within a standard introductory calculus-based physics course, not a replacement for lecture and textbook

### Target Learners
- introductory calculus-based physics university students

### Learning Goals
- linking mathematical equations and methods to physical concepts
- qualitative reasoning about fundamental physics concepts
