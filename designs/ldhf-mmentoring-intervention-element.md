# Simulation-based LDHF/m-Mentoring training intervention: two 4-day onsite courses, weekly simulation practice, SMS quizzes, and monthly mentor calls

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The intervention arm consists of two 4-day "low-dose" onsite BEmONC training courses repeated after 1 month, with lecture time reduced and hands-on practice increased. Between courses, providers practice via "high-frequency simulation-based practices of 2-3 times weekly" logged by Peer Practice Coordinators. Mobile mentoring consists of weekly SMS reminder messages and quiz questions, plus structured monthly half-hour mentoring calls from a trainer/master mentor for PPCs. MamaNatalie/NeoNatalie manikins are used for simulation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Peer Practice Coordinators trained in session plans, case scenarios and simulator use must log practice sessions
#### Constraints
- Assessment covers normal delivery, AMTSL, neonatal resuscitation, pre-eclampsia/eclampsia case management, and PPH management

### Target Learners
- Entire facility teams of maternal and newborn service providers in the intervention facilities

### Learning Goals
- BEmONC knowledge and skills at 80% or higher competence level
