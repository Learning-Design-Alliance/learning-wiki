# The shoebox study: a two-arm RCT protocol comparing sequential versus simultaneous learning of laparoscopic C-loop suturing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A registered monocentric, two-arm randomized controlled trial protocol in which 54 clinical-year medical students are randomized 1:1 to sequential learning (group 1) or control (group 2). The protocol states: "Group 1 learn the C-loop using a transparent shoebox, thus only learning the psychomotor skills", then transfer to a box trainer with standard laparoscopic view, while group 2 trains solely on the box trainer. The primary outcome is mean total training time per group.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants must be medical students in their clinical years at Heidelberg University with no prior laparoscopic training course of more than 2 hours
#### Constraints
- Results were not yet available at publication; the article reports the protocol only

### Target Learners
- clinical-year medical students learning laparoscopic surgery

### Learning Goals
- laparoscopic suturing and knot tying (C-loop technique)
