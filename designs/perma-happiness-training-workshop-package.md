# Ten-session PERMA-based happiness training workshop for primary school teachers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention is a happiness training workshop "based on the principles of positive psychology and Seligman’s PERMA model", consisting of "10 sessions over 5 weeks", each lasting 2 hours and facilitated by a trained positive psychology coach. Sessions cover positive emotions (gratitude journaling, mindfulness), engagement and flow, positive relationships, meaning, accomplishment and goal-setting, resilience, mindfulness and stress reduction, long-term maintenance via a Happiness Action Plan, and reflection and future planning (Table 1).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A trained positive psychology coach to facilitate sessions
- Sequential delivery across 5 weeks with teachers applying principles in everyday teaching
#### Constraints
- Evaluated in this study only with primary school teachers and without a control group
