# Progressive exchange model: three-stage telecollaborative task sequence

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The virtual exchange model used in EVALUATE was based on the Progressive Exchange Model, which "involves 'three interrelated tasks which move from information exchange to comparing and analysing cultural practices and finally to working on a collaborative product'". This staged structure scaffolds online intercultural collaboration by progressively deepening the level of engagement between partner classes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher trainers from partner institutions co-design a joint curriculum integrated into their respective courses.
#### Constraints
- Tasks must be matched to the key competences and themes identified by public authorities or course objectives.

### Target Learners
- Student teachers in initial teacher education

### Learning Goals
- Intercultural competence
- Digital-pedagogical competence
- Collaborative online learning
