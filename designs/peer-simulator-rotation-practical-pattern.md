# Alternating peer-practice and simulator small-group rotation within a single practical

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The modified 90-minute practical split students into two subgroups: one practiced auscultation and blood pressure measurement on each other in pairs in a room without the simulator, while the other worked with the CPS Harvey in small groups of 4-8; groups then alternated. The session used a flipped-classroom design with case notes and protocols provided in advance for pre-class self-study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Two rooms, one equipped with the simulator
- Pre-class self-study materials distributed in advance
- Demonstrators available to support students
#### Constraints
- Total duration limited to 90 minutes in the pilot

### Target Learners
- Second-year undergraduate medical students

### Learning Goals
- Cardiovascular physical examination skills
- Applying lecture knowledge of cardiovascular physiology in practice
