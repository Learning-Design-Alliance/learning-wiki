# 15-day supervised ultrasound training course for novice AAA screeners

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A short training course delivered by an experienced sonographer that prepared three novices with no imaging experience to perform abdominal aortic ultrasound examinations. It combined a theoretical component (didactic sessions on ultrasonography principles, aortic anatomy, hemodynamics, AAA pathology and epidemiology) with a practical component using a standardized protocol of four anterior-posterior aortic diameter measurements. The trainer gave "performance feedback during approximately 30 supervised sessions as well as feedback based on the stored images collected", and on average each novice practiced 50 ultrasound examinations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- One-to-one and group supervised practice plus self-directed and peer-directed practice
- Performance feedback during approximately 30 supervised sessions and from stored images
#### Constraints
- The novices used a portable laptop-based Terason system with features the article states are equivalent to the standard clinical system

### Target Learners
- novices with tertiary-level health science education but no prior imaging or ultrasound training

### Learning Goals
- performing abdominal aortic ultrasound examinations and detecting AAA to screening standard

### Claims
- [After 15 days of training, three novices' maximal coronal aortic measurements were within 0.5 cm of experienced assessors' in 85-97% of scans](../claims/novices-15-day-training-accurate-coronal-aortic-measurement.md) [+M]
