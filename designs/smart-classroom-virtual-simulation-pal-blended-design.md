# Blended basic-nursing teaching design combining smart classroom, virtual simulation training, and peer-assisted learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An instructional design for basic nursing courses that, in the article's words, "involved combining theoretical teaching with virtual simulation training and PAL; engaging in online and offline hybrid teaching". It comprises preclass microclasses and task sheets on an online platform, in-class Rain Classroom quizzes and interactions, post-theory virtual simulation laboratory practice, paired PAL scoring via QR-coded electronic score sheets, and summary feedback. The article evaluates it against traditional lecture teaching in a quasi-experimental study of 135 nursing undergraduates.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Smart classroom hardware and network: interactive whiteboards and screens, movable desks, Wi-Fi high-density coverage
- Recorded microclasses and clinical cases, an online platform with WeChat groups, and a virtual simulation training system with imported student information
- A PAL scoring sheet with QR codes and the PAL app for paired operation exercises
#### Constraints
- The article notes smart classrooms have high requirements for classroom facilities and network speeds, and that many teaching designs cannot be implemented if these are not met
- The design places a higher burden on teachers

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate nursing students in basic nursing courses

### Learning Goals
- Theoretical and practical nursing knowledge and skills
- Independent learning ability
