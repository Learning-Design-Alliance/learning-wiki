# Three cultural stakes defining digital planetariums as virtual museums of born-digital research data

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article enumerates three cultural stakes that digital planetariums can claim: first, to become true virtual museums collecting astronomical observation data on which science invests research work; second, to show science 'telle qu'elle se fait'; and third, to facilitate visitors' knowledge construction based on ongoing research data. This pattern organizes the planetarium's role around born-digital observation archives, whose storage and conservation keep a trace of a research community's living know-how and enable unusual mediation situations for transmitting emerging knowledge.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to born-digital observation data from research programmes, and real-time 3D visualization technology with human mediation
#### Constraints
- The pattern is argued for the specific case of the Lyon (Vaulx-en-Velin) planetarium and the domain of cosmography

### Target Learners
- general public, including audiences in disadvantaged urban areas

### Learning Goals
- understanding the stakes of modern science and emerging scientific knowledge
