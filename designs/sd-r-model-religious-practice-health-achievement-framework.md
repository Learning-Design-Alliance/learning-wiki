# Study Demands-Resources (SD-R) model as framework for religious practice, healthy lifestyle, and achievement

## Description
The article applies the study demands-resources model, derived from the job demands-resources model, to frame religious practice as an environmental resource that may foster healthy lifestyle behavior as a study resource and academic achievement as a study outcome. It states that "within this SD-R framework, high study resources foster positive outcomes, such as academic performance" and that the model is "explicitly and exclusively validated within the university context." The proposed conceptual framework is presented in Fig. 1. The study's null mediation result did not support the hypothesized pathway.
