# Blended HEMS course structure: pre-course e-learning, scenario-driven group discussion, and immersive simulator sessions in one cycle

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The revised course integrates three components into a cohesive learning cycle: (1) pre-course e-learning covering foundational helicopter knowledge and safety management, (2) extended scenario-driven group discussions in small multidisciplinary groups on challenges such as emergency landings, in-flight fires, and crew resource management, and (3) hands-on MeTra simulator training with scripted emergency scenarios and targeted debriefs. This replaced a two-day classroom-based program and aims to align "online learning, scenario-based discussion, and immersive simulation into a cohesive learning cycle."
