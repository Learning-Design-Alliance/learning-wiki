# OMP-based preceptor education program (PEP) developed through participatory action research

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An eight-week preceptor education program developed and applied with 30 preceptor nurses at a Korean tertiary hospital through participatory action research. It comprised two weeks of developing standardized nursing practice standards (content validity index ≥0.80 required) followed by six weekly application sessions, with daily conferences by education specialist nurses, weekly reflection journals, mini lectures, and facilitator support from four researchers and four education specialist nurses. Quantitative evaluation showed no significant CCCP or GICC-15 change, while qualitative analysis documented growth experiences.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Facilitators (researchers and education specialist nurses) supporting weekly conferences
- Organizational support including a training room and nursing department cooperation
#### Constraints
- Conducted at tertiary general hospitals in one region of Korea; the authors state generalizability is limited and there was no control group

### Target Learners
- preceptor nurses with at least three years of clinical experience

### Learning Goals
- preceptor competency
- communication competence
- evidence-based practice
- quality feedback
- self-reflection
