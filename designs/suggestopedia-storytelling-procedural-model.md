# Suggestopedia-based storytelling teaching model: a four-stage procedural development pattern

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article develops a procedural teaching model — a systematic procedure to organize learning experiences to meet learning objectives — through four stages adapted from Borg and Gall: "1) preliminary research and needs analysis, 2) design and development of the model prototype, 3) limited and general trial, and 4) effectiveness test." The model combines suggestopedia with group work, socio-drama play, discussion to compose stories, and performance, with the teacher acting as facilitator rather than the center of the lesson.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teaching scenarios must be executed by teachers according to the prescribed procedures; the model should be equipped with an assessment rubric to grade students' performance.
#### Constraints
- The model was developed and tested with fifth graders in Salatiga primary schools; the article states the prototype "can be further developed to increase the primary students’ story telling skill in Salatiga."

### Target Learners
- Primary school students (fifth grade) in Salatiga

### Learning Goals
- Bahasa Indonesia storytelling skill, active and creative participation, teamwork, and self-confidence
