# STARLORE permanent wall display at Sir Thomas Brisbane Planetarium features Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander astronomy from three communities

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For the Sir Thomas Brisbane Planetarium, a large permanent wall-display entitled STARLORE was developed featuring Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander astronomy with a focus on the scientific elements of this knowledge. It features examples from three communities showing diversity in geography and representation: Wardaman, Euahlayi, and Meriam. Elders gave permission for their names to be shown even after death, spoke at the launch, and were centered in the media; each section includes a small map of Australia showing each community's location.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Senior Elders or Boards of Elders able to give permission for knowledge to be shared and displayed
#### Constraints
- With very large communities there may not exist a single central body that can give blanket permission; shared knowledge may need to focus on smaller sub-groups such as clan, family, or dialect groups

### Target Learners
- Planetarium visitors in Brisbane, Australia

### Learning Goals
- Learning Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander astronomy and its scientific elements
