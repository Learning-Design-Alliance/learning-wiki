# Fermi problems as a variation of the Socratic method

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article frames Fermi problems conceptually as a variation of the Socratic dialectic method: 'Fermi Pro blems are a variation of the Socratic method.' As in Socratic questioning, the answer must be extracted from the person asked, who has no choice but to ask himself a series of questions that help him reach a solution. The authors state the Socratic method is at the heart of the method of solving Fermi problems and liken it to what instructors do in the classroom.
