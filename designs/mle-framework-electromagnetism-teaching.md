# Teaching electromagnetism within the Maxwell-Lorentz-Einstein (MLE) conceptual framework with the field as a primary theoretical entity

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper proposes presenting electromagnetic phenomena within the conceptual framework of Maxwell-Lorentz-Einstein Electromagnetism instead of the traditional chronological development. Its three cornerstones are that 'The use of the field concept as a primary theoretical entity' comes first, that electromagnetic phenomena be treated within the MLE conceptual domain, and that only local equations can be interpreted causally. A point charge is presented from the beginning as producing, in general, both an electric and a magnetic field, with the Lorentz force acting on charges.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must abandon the chronological development of the matter and accept the field as a primary concept rather than one derived from force
- Some essential microscopic descriptions of matter and currents are needed
#### Constraints
- The proposal is not a ready-to-use recipe; instructors must adapt the formalism to their teaching context
- Students' mathematical and physical background varies widely from country to country

### Target Learners
- upper high school students (Italian scientific Lyceum, last three years)
- students in elementary (intermediate-level) college physics courses

### Learning Goals
- understand electromagnetism within a field-based, relativity-consistent conceptual framework
- distinguish field descriptions from action-at-a-distance force descriptions
