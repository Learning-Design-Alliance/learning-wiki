# SMART VHV Plus Model framework

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The SMART VHV Plus Model is an original framework mapping the acronym to five operational competency domains: S (Screen) = communicable disease surveillance and at-risk group screening; M (Management) = participatory resource management and collaborative problem-solving; A (Application) = digital technology application and health information systems; R (Response) = community-level outbreak response and risk communication; T (Time) = timely, self-reliant community health planning. The designation "Plus" signifies extension beyond foundational Ministry of Public Health VHV standards to incorporate three emerging domains not previously systematised: digital literacy, leadership, and community self-reliance planning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Model development was guided by the Participatory Action-Oriented Research (PAOR: Plan–Act–Observe–Reflect) cycle with 34 stakeholders
#### Constraints
- Findings are limited to VHVs in a single rural village with strong community infrastructure and prior COVID-19 experience

### Target Learners
- Village Health Volunteers in community-based communicable disease prevention and control

### Learning Goals
- communicable disease surveillance, digital reporting, risk communication, leadership, and community health planning
