# Tutorial on the method of images with corresponding pretest and posttest for the upper-level undergraduate electricity and magnetism course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A tutorial on the method of images (MoI), developed and validated together with a corresponding pretest and posttest for the upper-level undergraduate electricity and magnetism course. The article states: "we developed and validated a tutorial along with a pretest and posttest on the MoI." Graduate-student interviews were conducted as part of the tutorial's development and validation process; interviews were organized as unscaffolded and scaffolded pretest, tutorial session, and unscaffolded and scaffolded posttest across three sessions averaging approximately 3 hours per student.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Scaffolding in the scaffolded versions breaks questions into multiple parts to support students in proceeding
#### Constraints
- Undergraduates enrolled in the upper-level course were not interviewed because they had not yet had instruction in the relevant concepts

### Target Learners
- upper-level undergraduate physics students in electricity and magnetism

### Learning Goals
- learning and appropriately applying the method of images to boundary value problems with conductors and charge distributions
