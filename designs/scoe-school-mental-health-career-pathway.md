# SCOE mental health career pathway employing peer specialists, graduate clinical interns, and associate clinicians

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To address a shortage of available mental health clinicians, SCOE built a career pathway using peer specialists, graduate clinical interns, and associate clinicians in paid positions. The brief states these roles "provide career support while establishing a professional pathway for school-based mental health service providers." Internships for undergraduate and graduate students are available, and associate clinicians with graduate degrees can earn clinical hours while employed full-time. Recent partnerships also nurtured the School Based Mental Health and Wellness Path, one of SCOE's Career Pipeline Paths.
