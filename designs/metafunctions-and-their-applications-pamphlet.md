# The training pamphlet 'The Metafunctions and Their Applications'

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The experimental group's instructional material was a pamphlet titled "The Metafunctions and Their Applications", composed of three chapters of up to 60 pages each. Chapter one introduced metafunction and the theoretician; chapter two explained the three metafunctions, of which only the textual metafunction section was taught, divided into subsections on Textual Metafunction and Components, and Cohesion and Coherence; chapter three taught how to write a standard three-paragraph essay in English. Exercises at the end of each chapter and section let participants check their understanding. The control group received a one-chapter pamphlet on writing a three-paragraph essay, similar to the experimental group's third chapter.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sessions were held two sessions per week for a ten-week term, with exercises at the end of each chapter and section
#### Constraints
- Only the textual metafunction section of chapter two was taught, since the study focused on its application

### Target Learners
- adult female intermediate EFL learners aged twenty to thirty-five

### Learning Goals
- writing a standard three-paragraph essay with improved cohesion and coherence
