# RICT case study: pre-service teacher-training course as testbed for the topologies

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The RICT case is an on-campus pre-service teacher-training course, "Educational Resources and ICT for Primary School", at a Spanish university, taught in English to first-year students over 12 weeks (5 h per week) with 6 ECTS credits and an estimated workload of 150 h. The cohort included 50 students organised into eight stable working groups. The course is designed around a formal cooperative learning model with cooperative base groups, weekly trialogical learning tasks, and pre-established scripted roles. Data were collected in the academic year 2021-2022, starting at week 6, over 2 weeks, anonymously.
