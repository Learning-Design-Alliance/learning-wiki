# STARS cluster randomised controlled trial design with economic and process evaluations

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
STARS is a pragmatic cluster randomised controlled trial in which one teacher of children aged 4–9 years from each of 80 primary schools in Devon, Torbay and Plymouth is randomised to the TCM course or to "teach as normal" (control arm). The primary outcome is the total difficulties score from the teacher-completed Strengths and Difficulties Questionnaire at baseline and 9, 18 and 30 months, supplemented by parent SDQs. Secondary measures include academic attainment, children's enjoyment of school, and teacher self-efficacy, burnout and stress, with parallel economic and process evaluations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schools must have at least one single year group class of 15 or more pupils in Reception or Years 1–4; the nominated teacher must have classroom responsibility for a single year group class a minimum of four days per week
#### Constraints
- Staff in schools cannot be blinded to allocation; observers are kept blind but teachers may inadvertently disclose allocation

### Target Learners
- primary school children aged 4–9 years
- their teachers

### Learning Goals
- children's socio-emotional well-being
- academic attainment
- teacher professional self-efficacy and classroom management skills
