# 1-day e-learning programme on perinatal mental health assessment and empathic communication

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
An e-learning course developed by the T. and F. Kitamura Foundation for clinical midwives and other perinatal health professionals, consisting of two sections of "eight 15-min video scripts" each: perinatal mental health assessment (diagnostic criteria, epidemiology, mood, anxiety and psychotic disorders, child abuse, bonding disorders, grief) and empathic communication (interview preparation, active listening, probe techniques, a case of acute insomnia after childbirth, catharsis). In the trial it was delivered face-to-face in about four hours to ensure reliable attendance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered with video scripts covering both knowledge content and role-played empathic communication interviews
#### Constraints
- In the trial the materials were provided in a 1-day seminar; the authors state results may differ if accessed online at participants' convenience

### Target Learners
- midwives
- nurses
- public health nurses with roles in perinatal care

### Learning Goals
- knowledge of perinatal mental health assessment
- empathic communication skills
