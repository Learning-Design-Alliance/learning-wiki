# Combined PBL-CBL model integrating structured clinical cases with inquiry-based problem-solving

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a hybrid pedagogy that "combin[es] the structured clinical context of CBL with the inquiry-based, student-driven nature of PBL". In practice, students study pre-class materials, take a pre-class quiz, analyse a representative clinical case in small groups, present findings, and receive instructor feedback before a post-class quiz. The authors state this integration "appears to strike a balance between instructor facilitation and learner autonomy". It is grounded in adult learning theory and competency-based education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires structured learning materials prepared by the instructor, including lecture videos, literature, and surgical demonstration videos
- Requires adequate instructor facilitation to maintain focus
#### Constraints
- PBL alone can be time-consuming and demanding for residents with intensive clinical schedules
- CBL alone may result in passive learning if discussion is excessively guided

### Target Learners
- Gynecology residents in postgraduate medical education

### Learning Goals
- Clinical reasoning
- Diagnostic reasoning
- Learning initiative and engagement
