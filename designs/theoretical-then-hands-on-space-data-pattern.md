# Two-part outreach structure combining theoretical lectures with hands-on replication of published analyses

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The SSDC outreach program is "split into two parts: one theoretical, in which relevant topic are presented and explained, and one practical, consisting of hands-on activities aimed to replicate scientific analysis of real space data". Short oral presentations (~half an hour each) by researchers introduce the physics and connect it to curricular topics, supported by slides; weeks later students return for a two-day long stage using professional tools on real data. The authors state that achievement of the goals "has been facilitated by the possibility of using the tools soon after the theoretical lectures".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An initial theoretical stage introducing the science topics and tool capabilities before hands-on work
- Access to professional tools and real mission data
#### Constraints
- The standard pathway offers limited time, with 30% of students judging the experience too short

### Target Learners
- upper secondary (high-school) students

### Learning Goals
- understanding how scientific analyses of space data are conducted
- connecting school physics topics to real research practice
