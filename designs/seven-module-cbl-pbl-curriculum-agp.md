# Seven-module CBL-PBL curriculum combining foundational lectures with intensive case-problem sessions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's CBL-PBL curriculum is "structured into seven modules designed to enhance the participants' clinical reasoning and problem-solving skills". The first module provides three one-hour lectures on CBL-PBL pedagogy, mind mapping, and literature retrieval; the following six modules are three-hour sessions with pre-session materials (guidelines, five papers, a 30-minute procedure video), simulated patient encounters, group discussion, group reporting, and instructor review. It operates at the level of a full training course for AGP trainees.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires instructional teams to prepare case materials, guidelines, papers, and procedure videos before each session, and instructors skilled in case design and facilitation
#### Constraints
- Evaluated in a single-centre AGP training context with a small sample; the article notes implementation consistency may vary with individual differences between teachers and students

### Target Learners
- second-year assistant general practitioner trainees in China's 3+2 training program

### Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning and problem-solving skills
- clinical thinking skills including critical, systematic, and evidence-based thinking
