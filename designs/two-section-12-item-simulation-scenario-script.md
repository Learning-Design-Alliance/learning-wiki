# Two-section, 12-item script template structuring planning and development of a high-fidelity simulation scenario

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors drafted the scenario from a script template that organizes the information needed to prepare a simulation. The article reports that "The script built for elaboration of the scenario was defined in two sections and structured in 12 items, which direct planning (seven items) and development of a high-fidelity simulation (five items)", covering "the necessary prior knowledge, learning objective, preparation and development of the simulated activity, theoretical foundation and debriefing".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Creation must follow national and international recommendations on high-fidelity clinical simulation, with content grounded in current literature on the theme and internal evaluation and review by the research team before expert validation.
#### Constraints
- The script template was prepared by the authors and, as a stated limitation, was not previously validated by expert judges.

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate health students and health professionals trained with scenarios built on this template

### Learning Goals
- Structured planning and delivery of high-fidelity clinical simulation scenarios
