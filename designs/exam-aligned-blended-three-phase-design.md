# Examination-aligned blended course design with pre-class, in-class, and post-class online-offline integration

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A course-level design pattern in which online resources (courseware library, micro-lessons, case library, knowledge graph, mind maps, and a 1,000-item exercise bank) are deployed across three phases. One week before class educators upload videos, courseware, case analyses, and practice questions; in class the instructor elucidates critical examination components using knowledge graphs and mind maps with case-based discussion; after class students work replicated examination questions. The design aligns the pathophysiology syllabus with the National Medical Licensing Examination syllabus and integrates actual past examination questions into chapter tests.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A curated online resource system on platforms such as Xuetangyun and Youmuke
- A syllabus revised against the National Medical Licensing Examination outline
- Instructor time to compile past examination questions aligned to knowledge domains
#### Constraints
- Implemented at a single medical college in the subject of pathophysiology; the authors state results may not generalize to other institutions or subjects

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate medical students preparing for the National Medical Licensing Examination

### Learning Goals
- Preparation for licensing examination question formats
- Consolidation of pathophysiology core concepts
