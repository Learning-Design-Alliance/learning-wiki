# 210-minute peer-taught communication skills lab unit on history taking and informed consent

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A mandatory communication training unit within a surgical skills lab for third-year medical students, instructing correct medical history taking and structuring of informed consent discussions. "This unit of 210 min duration is held by peer tutors who have been specifically trained beforehand." It combines theoretical parts, role-playing exercises in a hospital setting, and 360° feedback, and focuses on empathetic behaviour and strategies for taboo-afflicted topics. Quality is maintained via tutor manuals, standardised presentations and mandatory tutor training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Specifically trained peer tutors, tutor manuals, standardised presentations and mandatory regular tutor training to maintain unit quality

- The study notes individual randomisation was not possible due to curricular integration; students were assigned to groups in advance by the Office of the Dean

### Target Learners
- 3rd-year undergraduate medical students

### Learning Goals
