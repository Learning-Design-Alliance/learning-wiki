# BioRobots design conjecture: studying biological organisms supports understanding of structure–function relationships, which supports engineering design and CT practices

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The BioRobots project rests on a design conjecture (Sandoval, 2014) that "studying biological organisms will support student understanding of structure–function relationships," and that using this understanding to design a robot will support student practices in engineering design and computational thinking. Conjectured embodiments and mediating processes follow from this conjecture, and the conjecture map was revised in Y3 to reflect evolving thinking about alignment of science, engineering, and CT practices.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A design task that requires students to create a conceptual model of the animal structure–function relationship by decomposing the animal system and extracting relevant principles
#### Constraints
- The conjecture was tested in 3- to 4-week middle school implementations in science, engineering, enrichment, and after-school settings

### Target Learners
- middle school students in science, engineering, enrichment, and after-school programs

### Learning Goals
- understanding of structure–function relationships in biology and engineering
- engineering design practices
- computational thinking practices such as decomposition, abstraction, and algorithmic thinking
