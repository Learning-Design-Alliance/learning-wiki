# Facilitated CBL virtual course (FCVC): three-day virtual case-based learning sessions with flipped classroom and peer instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The FCVC was an extra-curricular series of webinars in which students in teams of 5–7, each with a trained moderator, discussed clinical scenarios on the Big Blue Button platform. "Each virtual case-based discussion consisted of three phases and was completed in three days": Day 1 case discussion (60–90 min), Day 2 presentations and peer learning (60–120 min), Day 3 conclusion with questions and expert summary (~120 min). Thirty clinical scenarios were designed by basic scientists and clinical educators using David Cohen's twelve tips for writing a discussion case.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained moderators per team, facilitator training via workshop with group work, exercises and role play on facilitation skills
- Clinical scenarios standardized using twelve tips from David Cohen and reviewed by five other teachers
#### Constraints
- Extra-curricular program at a single institution during the COVID-19 pandemic

### Target Learners
- first- and second-year medical students

### Learning Goals
- understanding the importance and application of basic sciences in medicine
- clinical reasoning and teamwork
