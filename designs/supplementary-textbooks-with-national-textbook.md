# Pair the national EFL textbook with supplementary reading materials to improve reading comprehension

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article supports using supplementary textbooks such as Gaj and Khate Sefid alongside the national academic textbook in EFL reading instruction. In the study, experimental classes used selected reading texts from these supplementary books in addition to the normal textbook, while the control class used only the national book. The authors conclude that "using supplementary text books as an instructional tool had a positive impact on reading comprehension of English learners in experimental groups."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learners need orientation in how to use the supplementary texts, as the researcher showed participants the correct deployment of these books before treatment
#### Constraints
- Evidence comes from one quasi-experimental study with female second-grade high school learners in Iran at the intermediate proficiency level

### Target Learners
- Iranian female second-grade high school EFL learners at intermediate proficiency

### Learning Goals
