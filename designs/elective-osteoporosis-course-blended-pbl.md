# Elective Osteoporosis Course: blended e-learning plus PBL case seminars consolidating content dispersed across five mandatory courses

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A two-part elective for fourth-year medical students consolidating osteoporosis content otherwise spread across five mandatory courses. Each sub-course comprised "14 study hours, combining 10 hours of self-directed e-learning via an online platform" with two interactive 2-hour PBL seminars built on clinical cases. Students prepared a assigned case topic, presented reasoning, and received faculty feedback; assessment used a scaled multiple-choice test with a 70% passing threshold. More than 250 students completed the course over its 12 years of existence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Voluntary enrollment as an elective, since the mandatory curriculum could not accommodate it
- An e-learning platform providing a structured knowledge framework before the seminars
- Faculty who guide the case debate and provide feedback aligned with established clinical practice
#### Constraints
- The course consolidates existing mandatory-course content without introducing new material
- Elective status was chosen because mandatory integration into the existing curriculum was not feasible

### Target Learners
- fourth-year medical students

### Learning Goals
- osteoporosis pathophysiology, diagnostics, and pharmacological treatment
- treatment of osteoporotic fractures and rehabilitation
- clinical reasoning and collaborative problem-solving
