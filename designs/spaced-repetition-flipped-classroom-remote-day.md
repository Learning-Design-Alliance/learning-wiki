# Remote didactic day structured around spaced repetition and flipped-classroom preparation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The department's remote curriculum sequences a single topic across a day and into the next: a live faculty teleconference on clinical cases, a break of several hours, a chief resident-led question review on the earlier topic, and independent flipped-classroom preparation (prerecorded webinars and assigned reading plus review questions) for the next day's faculty-led teleconference. The authors state the several-hour break uses "the principle of spaced repetition," and the independent materials prepare residents in advance of the next teleconference.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A faculty member on the remote team to present the live teleconference and a chief resident to lead the question review
- prerecorded webinars and assigned reading available for independent study
#### Constraints
- Implemented during pandemic social distancing; the article cites, rather than tests, the spaced repetition and flipped-classroom literature

### Target Learners
- orthopedic surgery residents on remote rotation

### Learning Goals
- retention of didactic content through spaced review
- preparation before faculty-led sessions
