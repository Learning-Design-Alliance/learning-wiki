# Two-day blended lung POCUS continuing professional development curriculum for primary care physicians

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A curriculum for primary care physicians in a rural, medically underserved region of southern Israel combining remote and in-person components: "recorded lectures, pre-course assessments, hands-on training, post-workshop lectures, and individual practice." Hands-on workshops ran in small groups of up to five PCPs on a simulator and live patients, led by an intensive care physician with 15 years of experience in clinical POCUS.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- PCPs must have access to ultrasound devices in their clinics, as per the study's inclusion criteria
#### Constraints
- The assessments were conducted on healthy models rather than sick patients, which the authors note may not encompass real patient challenges
- Independent practice sessions between the two workshops were not monitored
- Lasting impact was evaluated only at a 10-week follow-up using self-reported data

### Target Learners
- Primary care physicians (specialists/consultants and family medicine residents) with no prior ultrasound training

### Learning Goals
- Acquiring standard lung ultrasound images and interpreting normal versus pathological scans
- Integrating lung POCUS into daily primary care practice
