# School of the day off thematic immersion program in the historical-patriotic direction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A program of thematic immersion realized at the Taganrog institute of A. Chekhov within the historical and patriotic direction of educational work, in which 68 students of the experimental group took part. Its purpose is drawing attention of students to the Russian history and a role of Russia in world historical process and formation of creative civil activity of youth. Three thematic days included a training on acquaintance, self-determination and identification of leaders, reconstruction of a family tree, the game «That? Where? When?» on stories of Russia, game elections, a master class of bases of civil self-defense, a film society meeting and a patriotic flashmob.
