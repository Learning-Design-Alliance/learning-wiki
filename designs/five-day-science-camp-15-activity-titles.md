# A 5-day active learning based science camp curriculum organized around 15 nature-based activity titles

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The camp curriculum was organized under 15 titles: drama in nature, nature-art integration, museum visits, pet care, ecology games, ecological environments and human, mountaineering, herbarium, insect training, the world without human, eco-city, animal products in supermarkets, butterfly collection, field work in Aladaglar, and field work in Cappadocia-Goreme historical national park. Theoretical parts were realized at Niğde University and field works were done in two national parks. A pilot camp was conducted and evaluated before the real camp study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A pilot camp should be conducted and evaluated before implementing the full program
#### Constraints
- The program was implemented over 5 days with 8 trainers in the Cappadocia region

### Target Learners
- Primary school students at sixth, seventh and eighth grades (11-14 ages)

### Learning Goals
- Opinions towards scientific knowledge
- Scientific process skills
- Conceptual knowledge towards environmental problems
