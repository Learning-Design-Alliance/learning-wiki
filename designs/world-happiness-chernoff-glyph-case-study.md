# World Happiness Chernoff face glyphs case study element

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The case study refines data from 160 countries and designs Chernoff face style glyphs using processing.org with the Handy sketch-rendered library, aiming for a design more attractive and artistic than typical distorted-egg Chernoff faces. An alphabetical arrangement was easiest for locating countries and enhanced visual appeal.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The data was refined to 160 countries from the 2024 World Happiness data, supplementing missing fields with equivalent values from previous years and merging the two Congo entries
#### Constraints
- The author found that organising the faces by the Cantril ladder happiness score gives a less interesting data-art piece than an alphabetical arrangement

### Target Learners
- students learning data-mapping and graphics algorithms

### Learning Goals
- multivariate data mapping to glyph glyphs
- data processing with missing values
