# Validated personality trait stories for conveying learner personality at a polarised level

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Short stories about fictional learners, adapted from IPIP-NEO self-report questionnaire items (Goldberg et al. 2006), describe one Big Five trait at a high or low polarised level. They were used instead of simplistic labels "such as 'John is an extrovert'" so participants could identify and empathise with the learner. A validation experiment had participants rate each story with the Mini-Markers scale (Saucier 1994), and stories were adjusted and re-validated as needed.
