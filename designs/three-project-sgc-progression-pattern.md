# A three-project progression (video → channel → website) where students create, review, and reuse peer-generated content about SDG topics.

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a "series of three learning projects whose outcome is content addressing the computer engineering profession and its relationship to SDGs". Students first create a video on a specific SDG, then create derivative videos in a shared channel, and finally build a multilingual website integrating content from previous projects and peers. The design embeds "the exchange of information and the sharing of opinions" through teamwork and progressive content building.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor-provided presentation documents and materials
- Video channel and website hosting infrastructure
- Team-based work with 3–8 members per project
#### Constraints
- SDG-related content is "not assessed in exams or other tests, and is used purely for formative assessment"
- Time-constrained: video and channel projects last 3 weeks each, website project lasts 8 weeks

### Target Learners
- Third-year university Computer Science students

### Learning Goals
- Project management skills
- Understanding of the impact of ICT on Sustainable Development Goals
