# Weekly online-course rhythm: introduction video, three lessons with required and optional readings, ten-question lesson assessments, a forty-question weekly quiz, and a programming assignment in Jupyter notebook templates

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This pattern organizes an online course into a repeated weekly cycle: "each week began with an introduction video" summarizing topics and demonstrating example code, "Each week’s material was organized into three lessons" with required and optional readings, each lesson ending in a "short lesson assessment with ten multiple-choice questions," and a "comprehensive quiz with forty multiple-choice questions" at week's end. Programming assignments in weeks two through fourteen used Jupyter notebook templates, and no midterm or final exam was given. The pattern was used in the fifteen-week Introduction to Data Science course.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered fully online via a course platform (Moodle) plus GitHub-hosted notebooks so materials, communication and submission are all online
- Reading lists and short assessments available for each of the three weekly lessons
#### Constraints
- The authors report there was no midterm or final exam in this course; difficulty and workload consistency across assembled sources was hard to maintain

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate and graduate students with no assumed programming experience across diverse disciplines

### Learning Goals
- Broad exposure to data science topics: data, storage, visualization, probability and statistics, cloud and high-performance computing
