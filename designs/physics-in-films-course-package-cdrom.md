# Planned self-contained Physics in Films course package with textbook and CD-ROM of scene analyses

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors state they are "in the process of preparing a self-contained physical science course, complete with textbook and a CD-ROM with all scene analyses and slides we use, that can be handed to an instructor ready-to-go." The package is intended to make the film-based approach transferable to other instructors without requiring them to develop scene analyses themselves.
