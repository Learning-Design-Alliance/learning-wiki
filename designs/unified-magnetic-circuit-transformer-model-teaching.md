# Unified magnetic-circuit approach for teaching the transformer electrical model

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article introduces a unified teaching approach in which every element of the transformer electrical model — series leakage inductances and the shunt excitation branch — is derived from magnetic-circuit analysis, unlike the common hybrid approach where shunt elements are explained by decomposing the no-load current. The authors state the goal 'to explain a unified approach for describing the electrical model of power transformers in which both series and shunt elements can be described using magnetic circuits.' The approach is based on complex permeability and 'provides direct connections between the real power transformer and corresponding elements in the electrical model.'

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Undergraduate power engineering courses covering transformer electrical models; familiarity with magnetic circuits, Faraday's law and phasor analysis
#### Constraints
- The duality approach based on complex permeability is chosen because it is 'easier to explain to the undergraduate students while it still has acceptable accuracy'; the hysteresis model is valid only for the non-saturated region of the magnetization curve

### Target Learners
- undergraduate power engineering students

### Learning Goals
- understanding the electrical equivalent circuit of power transformers
- connecting model elements to physical transformer phenomena
