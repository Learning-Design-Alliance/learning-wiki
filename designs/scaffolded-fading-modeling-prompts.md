# Scaffolded fading of explicit modeling prompts across a semester of guided labs

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A course-level design pattern in which lab guides early in the semester contain very explicit prompts for each modeling step (comparison, refinement, prediction), and later guides progressively fade this scaffolding to give students more freedom about when and how to model. The authors took "a scaﬀolding approach to developing student expertise" and rewrote all lab guides accordingly. This pattern is intended to move students from prompted engagement toward independent application of modeling practices, supported by preliminary evidence of internalization in later weeks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All lab guides must be rewritten to align with the modeling framework, with explicit prompts in early guides
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness of the fading beyond preliminary observation is a focus of future studies

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics majors in laboratory courses

### Learning Goals
- independent application of modeling to experiments
