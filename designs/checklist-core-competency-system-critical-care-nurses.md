# Checklist-based core competency training and evaluation system for critical care nurses

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A structured training and evaluation system developed "guided by checklist management principles and Kirkpatrick's four-level evaluation model, and developed using the Delphi method." Three rounds of expert consultation with seven experts produced a framework of "one first-level indicator, four second-level indicators, and 11 third-level indicators (Table 1)" covering knowledge, skills, communication, and practice domains. Training and evaluation ran under a group leader responsibility system with ward head nurses, teaching instructors, and quality control officers delivering level-specific assessment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Implementation required expert panels for adaptation, standardized head-nurse training on checklist utilization, and a group leader responsibility system for quality control.
#### Constraints
- Developed and evaluated for critical care nursing at a single institution.

### Target Learners
- critical care nurses (ICU and emergency department registered nurses)

### Learning Goals
- core nursing competencies: knowledge, skills, communication, and practice
- reduction of work-related alienation
