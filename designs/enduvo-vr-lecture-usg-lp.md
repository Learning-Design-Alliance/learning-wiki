# Enduvo-based interactive VR lecture for ultrasound-guided lumbar puncture

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
An interactive VR lecture created with Enduvo software and delivered on Meta Quest 2 headsets linked to gaming laptops. It contained the same outline and assessment questions as a matched slideshow lecture, plus 3D models of an ultrasound probe, lumbar puncture tray components, and an anatomical model with removable layers, allowing users to "move, resize, and view different layers of 3D models". The lecture lasted 32 minutes and 27 seconds.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Meta Quest 2 headsets linked to gaming laptops with compatible graphics cards and Enduvo downloaded
- A reliable Wi-Fi source for connectivity
#### Constraints
- Technological issues arose from internet connectivity problems and a first-time learning curve with the software's controls and interface

### Target Learners
- medical students

### Learning Goals
- background knowledge and technique for performing ultrasound-guided lumbar puncture
