# Metacognition workshop structured around task-tool matching, metacognitive process, and Bloom's taxonomy

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The hourlong Metacognition workshop titled "Strategies to Get the Grade You Want in Bio [Course Number]!" had three main sections: "1) matching the task with the tool, 2) applying the metacognitive process (planning, monitoring, and evaluating) as a way of purposefully learning, and 3) selecting learning strategies based on the depth of learning desired, following Bloom's taxonomy." Active-learning exercises and peer discussion maintained engagement throughout.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A worksheet for notes and major takeaways accompanies the workshop
#### Constraints
- Content was designed for introductory college biology following a first exam

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate introductory biology students

### Learning Goals
- Metacognitive planning, monitoring, and evaluating
- Selecting strategies matched to learning-task depth
