# Networked Improvement Community (NIC) approach as a framework for collective climate action in astronomy

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes structuring a discipline-wide climate change transition using Networked Improvement Communities, a model "originally developed by the Carnegie Foundation for the Advancement of Teaching (CFAT)" with roots in rapid prototyping of engineering design. The authors argue the NIC model "honors the complexity of a problem and empowers people within a community to take collective action." They supply a Driver Diagram (Figure 1) organizing the work into primary drivers (Education and Outreach; Research Practices and Infrastructure), secondary drivers such as Astro 101 and planetaria, and small "change ideas."
