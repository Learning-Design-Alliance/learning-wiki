# Virtual flipped learning (VFL) model: pre-recorded lectures before virtual sessions focused on synthesis, application and clarification

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes VFL as a virtual adaptation of the flipped classroom in which "the teacher provides didactic material as a prerecorded video lecture for students to watch at any time prior to a virtual learning session." Virtual sessions then "focus primarily on synthesis and application, as well as clarification of concepts that are difficult to grasp independently," eliminating the physical classroom while maintaining interaction through internet platforms. The article stresses VFL "does not equate to conventional flipped learning."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Recorded didactic material available to students before the virtual session, and virtual sessions devoted to application and clarification rather than first exposure.
#### Constraints
- The article notes lack of evidence regarding knowledge retention compared with traditional classrooms, and time and commitment needed to create a meaningful educational experience.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate dental students
- health professions students

### Learning Goals
- short-term recall of lecture content
- self-directed learning skills
- active learning in online environments
