# Short daily small-group number relationships activities using rekenrek, software, and ten-frame

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The intervention pattern consisted of three activities introduced in teacher training sessions: "Three ac tivities (rekenrek, Building Block software, ten-frame) were introduced at the training sessions." Teachers delivered them Monday to Thursday for 5 to 10 minutes during the first circle period, with rekenrek twice weekly and the other activities once weekly. Each activity was designed to strengthen children's understanding of number relationships, with quantities starting at 3 to 5 and extending up to 8.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers received two 30-minute training sessions; classrooms were observed at least twice a week to ensure activities were implemented as planned.
#### Constraints
- Computer software was used only once a week, with more hands-on activities on remaining days.

### Target Learners
- preschool children ages 3 to 5 in blended classrooms

### Learning Goals
- subitizing
- parts-whole relationships
- more-and-less relationships
