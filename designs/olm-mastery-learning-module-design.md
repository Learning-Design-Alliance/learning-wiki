# Online learning module (OLM) with embedded pre- and post-learning mastery assessment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An OLM pairs an assessment component with an instructional component: "Each OLM consisted of an instructional component (IC) and an assessment component (AC)". Students must attempt a short 1-2 problem quiz before the instructional resources unlock, may study optional text, figures, and practice problems with immediate feedback, and can retry isomorphic problem sets differing only by surface features, passing on any attempt for full credit. In this study 10 such modules covered mechanical energy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An online platform that logs time-stamped events and can lock the instructional component during assessment attempts; the study hosted modules on the open-source Obojobo platform and assigned them via Canvas
#### Constraints
- Due to platform limitations at the time of the study, students could access the next module after making only the first attempt on the current module's assessment, though they were not informed of this setting

### Target Learners
- college students in calculus-based introductory physics

### Learning Goals
- conceptual understanding of mechanical energy and solving mechanical energy problems
