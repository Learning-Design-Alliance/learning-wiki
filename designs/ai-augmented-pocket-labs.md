# AI-Augmented Pocket Labs: smartphone mini-labs combined with MLLM analysis

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes an approach in which smartphones serve as portable measurement platforms — integrated sensors such as accelerometers, microphones and cameras turn them into portable "Mini-Labs" — and multimodal large language models act as co-investigators that analyze the recorded data. With such AI-Augmented Pocket Labs, learners "can individually explain phenomena scientifically, create and evaluate designs for scientific investigations, and critically interpret scientific data and evidence", competencies the authors map to the PISA 2025 Science Framework. Three example investigations across different sensors and data modalities illustrate the concept.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A smartphone with integrated sensors and an MLLM such as ChatGPT-4o capable of processing images, audio and video; data must be recorded and provided to the model in a usable format
#### Constraints
- Everyday phenomena can be complex and the necessary analytical methods are often not available in school curricula, which is the limitation the MLLM support is meant to overcome

### Target Learners
- secondary school students at levels 1 and 2 in physics classes

### Learning Goals
- scientific inquiry competencies: explaining phenomena, designing investigations, critically interpreting data and evidence
