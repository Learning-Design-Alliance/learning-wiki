# Cluster-randomized pragmatic replication trial design: 2:1 school-level assignment, nested student provider randomization, and mixed-effects analysis

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The protocol describes a two-group parallel cluster-randomized trial in about 30 schools serving students ages 11–14, with schools stratified by subsidized lunch status and randomized to HOPS or TAU/WL at a 2:1 ratio; students in HOPS schools are then randomized 1:1 to a school or research provider. Outcomes (COSS-P/T, HPC, HPQ-T, APS, report-card grades) are assessed at post-treatment and 6- and 12-month follow-ups, with intent-to-treat linear mixed-effects models nesting students within schools and Bonferroni-adjusted significance (.025 per domain). The article states "we adjusted these projections to an average of 4.5 students/school for 30 schools for a total of 135 participants (122 with attrition). This sample size will result in a MDES of 0.48 with adequate power (80%)."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- School consent and a consenting school provider before randomization; caregiver consent and student assent
#### Constraints
- No concealment of treatment assignment, consistent with the pragmatic design; no 6- or 12-month follow-up for TAU/WL students for ethical reasons; no data and safety monitoring committee, consistent with sponsor expectations

### Target Learners
- Students in grades 6–8 in general education with teacher-nominated OTMP deficits

### Learning Goals
- OTMP skills
- homework performance
- academic performance
