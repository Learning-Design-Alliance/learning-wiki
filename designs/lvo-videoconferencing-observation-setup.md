# Internet-based videoconferencing observation setup with remote-controlled pan-tilt-zoom cameras and omnidirectional microphone

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A low-cost, easy-to-operate videoconferencing installation enabling remote, unobtrusive classroom observation. The setup at the Child Study Center included "two remote-controlled cameras capable of panning, tilting, and zooming" and "one omnidirectional microphone," connected via Internet protocol to a campus multimedia classroom where the live feed is projected onto a large screen. Sessions can be recorded for make-up observations and to build a library of video segments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Readily available broadband Internet connections
- Media consent from parents of observed children
- A compatible videoconferencing system in the observing classroom
#### Constraints
- The system supports three additional camera inputs, which the authors suggest could address limited-view complaints
- Sound quality during open-play periods was reported as poor

### Target Learners
- preservice teachers
- pre-professional daycare providers

### Learning Goals
- remote observation of preschool children's developmental behaviors
