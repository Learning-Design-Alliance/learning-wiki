# Three-mode delivery for technique-based multidisciplinary science courses: interactive lectures, hands-on laboratories, and peer-instruction proposal sessions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The proposed course is delivered in three modes. Interactive lectures with a conceptual focus remove confounding details to build understanding of the basic science underlying each technique; accompanying laboratory sessions provide hands-on technical training and data-analysis skills; and peer instruction takes the form of proposal preparation sessions where students draft research plans for individual projects. The author states the course "can be taught in three modes of delivery" combining these components, with working on real-world problems in laboratories developing critical thinking.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- accompanying laboratory sessions with hands-on technical training and data analysis
- individual projects for which students determine appropriate techniques and draft a research plan
#### Constraints
- intended for science majors who have completed the introductory physics and chemistry classes

### Target Learners
- undergraduate science majors

### Learning Goals
- conceptual understanding of the basic science underlying each technique
- technical and data-analysis skills
- critical thinking and identifying effective solutions
