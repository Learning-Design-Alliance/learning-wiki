# Year-long small-group faculty development program in humanistic practice and teaching at eight US medical schools

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A one-year faculty development program designed to enhance humanistic teaching and role modeling, in which participants attended "twice-monthly experiential and reflective learning sessions for one year" at eight medical schools. Site leaders selected promising clinical teachers and practitioners from applicant pools. All 92 graduates were surveyed for this study, with responses from 68 faculty members (74% response rate), forming the data source for the study's motivator and barrier themes.
