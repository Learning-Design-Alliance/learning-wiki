# Exploring 3D Anatomy: a free open online course for three-dimensional spatial awareness

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Exploring 3D Anatomy is an open online course, collaboratively produced at universities in the United Kingdom and South Africa, and available free of charge via Canvas. It is designed to develop three-dimensional spatial awareness remotely, using accessible household objects and art-based learning, and can be described as a small-scale, open, free, formative, instructor-guided, vocational open online course. The external Instructure course achieved 460 users (2023-2026), while 4447 users joined the internal Canvas version since launch (2021-2026).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered via the Instructure Canvas learning management system, with a general Course Information module introducing educators, course aims, required materials, and navigation guidance
#### Constraints
- Available free of charge to institutional and non-institutional users; serves as a supplement to, rather than a replacement for, conventional teaching in areas under-represented in formal curricula

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate medical students
- Novice anatomy learners globally

### Learning Goals
- Three-dimensional spatial awareness
- Understanding of three-dimensional and cross-sectional anatomical concepts
