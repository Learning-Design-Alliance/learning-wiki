# Oriented globe with gnomons and a narrated video as an online resource for discovering time zones and seasons

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The oriented globe, also called self-centered or day-night globe, simulates Earth's orientation in space; any globe becomes oriented when its rotation axis is parallel to the Earth's and the local city sits at its highest point. The Roma Tre team turned the AstroGarden visit into a YouTube video with "detailed, step-by-step instructions" so teachers can replicate the activities autonomously.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A globe whose rotation axis can be arranged parallel to the Earth's, with the local city placed at its highest point, plus a gnomon of suitable length placed on the surface
#### Constraints
- The globe reproduces the Earth's real-time illumination pattern only when placed under sunlight in the oriented configuration

### Target Learners
- general public
- school students of all levels
- pre-service and in-service primary teachers
- kindergarten children

### Learning Goals
- understanding time zones
- understanding seasons and Earth's orientation in space
- relating shadows to the position of the Sun
