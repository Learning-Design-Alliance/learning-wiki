# Web-assisted alternative measurement and assessment material for the Human and Environment Interactions unit

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A website (built with Adobe Flash and Adobe Dreamweaver CS6) containing web-assisted concept maps, structured grids, word association, meaning-analysis tables and objective-based assessment activities. Activities give immediate feedback: a correctly dragged concept is "placed with a warning of “correct”", while incorrect attempts return to their starting place with an "incorrect, try again" warning. Results are reported to students and emailed to the teacher.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Activities must be usable individually before class and by the teacher in class via projector or smart board
- Students answer objective statements as agree/disagree and must answer correctly or complete the linked activity before moving to the next objective
#### Constraints
- Developed for the Human and Environment Interactions unit of 7th-grade science; the author states the material "can be re -organized for any subject of the science class"

### Target Learners
- Secondary school 7th-grade science students (11-12 years old)

### Learning Goals
- Human and Environment Interactions unit objectives on ecosystems and bio-diversity
