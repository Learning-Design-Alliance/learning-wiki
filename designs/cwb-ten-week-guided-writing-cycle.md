# Ten-week progressive online writing-practice cycle with guided question sets, teacher feedback, and revision

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The CWB curriculum organized writing practice into five two-week exercises across ten weeks: one week for students to complete a complete piece of writing and one week for teachers to rate it. Each exercise's first page presents basic guidance and advanced guidance question sets for pre-writing planning; a second page supports translating with tips and reference links; and completion and revision pages support reviewing, with timely teacher scores and suggestions. This progressive cycle gave students a sense of achievement and confidence while making revision expedient.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Suitable question sets designed according to the conditioned writing approach; teacher rating and feedback time built into each cycle
#### Constraints
- Each writing exercise was conducted for two weeks during the ten-week experiment in this study

### Target Learners
- seventh-grade junior high school students

### Learning Goals
- Chinese essay writing performance across argumentation, organization, and language use
