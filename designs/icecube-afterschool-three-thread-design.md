# IceCube AfterSchool three-thread layered learning design

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
IceCube AfterSchool, a ten-week virtual after-school program for high school interns, was "designed to layer three threads of learning: IceCube and neutrino science, programming, and computational thinking". Students wove these threads into a final coded project demonstrating their IceCube science learning. Short 15-20 minute introductions covered data types, loops, operators, and conditionals; code.org activities were assigned for asynchronous practice; and engineering design stages required students to create a coded communication tool for a chosen stakeholder such as peers, family, or teachers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Guest speakers on IceCube science topics
- Asynchronous coding practice activities
- A final project presented to an audience including parents and collaborators
#### Constraints
- Delivered virtually during the pandemic; some students opted for alternatives to code.org, including compiled Python codes, storyboards, and websites

### Target Learners
- High school student interns

### Learning Goals
- IceCube and neutrino science
- Programming
- Computational thinking
