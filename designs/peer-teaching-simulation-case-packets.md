# Peer-teaching simulation case packets with objectives, encounter summary, outcomes checklist, and debriefing questions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A prepared instructional packet given to each student peer teacher to standardize delivery of the simulation case. The article states "Each case had a list of objectives, patient encounter summary, outcomes checklist, questions to facilitate debriefing, instructions on how to use the rhythm generator and a list of resources for the topic." Students were encouraged to augment their knowledge with outside reading, and the same packets were given to physician teachers in the control condition to minimize confounding from multiple instructors.
