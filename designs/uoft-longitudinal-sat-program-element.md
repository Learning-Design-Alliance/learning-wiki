# University of Toronto longitudinal 7-month extracurricular Students as Teachers program for pre-clerkship medical students

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 7-month extracurricular SAT program for second-year medical students consisting of eight 2-h educational modules, five practical teaching sessions, and three independent assignments, spread across the academic year so students, as the article states, must juggle other educational tasks. Practical sessions were a graded experience from peer-to-peer small group teaching to a large-group lecture to an audience of more than 40 students with two clinician educators, all videotaped with peer feedback. Overall mean attendance/completion of program components was 82%.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Certificate of completion required mandatory attendance of the first and last modules, at least 5 of 6 remaining modules, and completion of all teaching sessions and independent assignments
#### Constraints
- Attendance was 82% overall, which the authors attribute to students managing competing academic and extracurricular demands

### Target Learners
- second-year (pre-clerkship) medical students

### Learning Goals
- educational pedagogy and teaching skills
- communication skills
- reflection and learning strategies
