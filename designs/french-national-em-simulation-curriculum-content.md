# National simulation-based curriculum content for French emergency medicine residency (44 priority skills and 15 situations as starting points)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A Delphi-derived curriculum content list specifying which technical skills, non-technical skills, and situations as starting points should be taught through simulation during French emergency medicine residency. The initial questionnaire "included 64 TS, 37 NTS and 103 SSPs," and expert comments "resulted in additional 12 TS, 24 NTS and 6 SSPs." The final content comprises 24 technical skills (e.g., orotracheal intubation, chest compressions), 20 non-technical skills (e.g., leadership, team coordination), and 15 situations as starting points, approved by the CNUMU for distribution to regional directors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A panel of emergency physicians with significant experience in simulation-based education to validate content via the Delphi method
#### Constraints
- Pediatric-related content and ultrasound items were excluded because separate specialized curricula exist
- Developed by French experts for French EM residents and may not be applicable in countries with different healthcare systems

### Target Learners
- Emergency medicine residents in France

### Learning Goals
- Technical skills such as airway management, ventilation, and vascular access
- Non-technical skills such as leadership, teamwork, communication, and crisis resource management
