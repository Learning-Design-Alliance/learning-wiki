# Flipped learning as direct instruction moved to the individual dimension with four pillars

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article presents flipped learning, per the 2014 Flipped Learning Network, as "an educational approach in which direct instruction is transferred from the group learning dimension to individual learning", with the instructor guiding students in applying concepts and engaging creatively with content in a dynamic, interactive environment. It describes four pillars: "flexible environment, learning culture, purposeful content and professional instructor". In this trial the flipped structure meant students read content before class and solved scenario assignments in class.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must read the content before the class and do the assignment in the class, as the article states as the fundamental element of a flipped class
#### Constraints
- In this study the flipped class alone did not significantly outperform lecture on knowledge scores (P = 0.43)

### Target Learners
- undergraduate nursing students

### Learning Goals
- client health assessment knowledge and clinical examination skills
