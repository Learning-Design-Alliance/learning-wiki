# Vermont education program pattern combining education courses, two outside subjects, secondary methods, and an educational thesis

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The program's requirements interleave education study with subject-matter study across the degree. As printed: "The requirements of the department are: psychology; principles of instruction; history of education; one o^her full course in education," plus two courses in each of two subjects given in other departments, two courses in secondary methods, and free electives to reach at least fifteen hours per week. A thesis treating some subject studied in the University from the educational point of view must be accepted by both the subject professor and the dean of the department of education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A thesis treating of some subject studied in the University, from the educational point of view, must be accepted by the professor who teaches the subject and by the dean of the department of education
#### Constraints
- Argumentation and debate are required of all students in the College of Liberal Arts and are excluded from the fifteen-hour weekly total

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate students in the College of Liberal Arts preparing to teach

### Learning Goals
- Teaching a school subject with grounding in psychology, instruction, history of education, and educational philosophy
