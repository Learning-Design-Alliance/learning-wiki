# Three forms of noticing required for learning to facilitate productive discussion

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Building on Gibson's perceptual learning theory, the article proposes three forms of noticing for teachers developing discussion facilitation. "First is increased differentiation with respect to the structure and features of student talk, including both what is present and what is missing." Second, differentiation of the features and dimensions of teachers' response moves; third, identification and refinement of one's values and commitments and of alignment between a discussion and those values. The intervention was designed around these three areas.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires teachers to encounter varied discussion examples that make features of student talk, teacher moves, and values contrasts salient
#### Constraints
- The matching-problem analogy is acknowledged as a simplification; discussion also occurs in social and cultural contexts shaped by values and relational histories the analogy does not capture

### Target Learners
- preservice teachers
- prospective teachers (undergraduates interested in teaching)

### Learning Goals
- noticing features of productive classroom discussion
- differentiating talk moves such as revoicing
- refining values and commitments about discussion

### Claims
- [Preservice teachers became more likely to notice the absence of students building on each other's ideas and marginally more likely to notice open-ended questions](../claims/blair2024-noticing-absences-increase.md) [+M]
- [Student-focused noticing marginally increased (Cohen's d = 0.43) while teacher-focused codes showed no change (d = 0.06)](../claims/blair2024-student-focused-increase-teacher-flat.md) [~M]
- [All participant pairs ranked the fully student-led Dialog E best, and disagreement about the elaborative-revoicing Dialog B reflected differences in values rather than in noticing](../claims/blair2024-ranking-values-vs-noticing.md) [+W] — supports treating values as a distinct third form of noticing

## Patterns Used
- [Contrasting Cases](../strategies/contrasting-cases.md): varied discussion transcripts are set side by side so the three forms of noticing (student talk, teacher moves, values) become perceptually salient
