# vPLC-augmented ASSISTments intervention with four-step formative assessment process

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The intervention combines WPI's ASSISTments online math platform with a virtual professional learning community (vPLC) designed to enhance scalability, reducing travel and scheduling barriers especially for rural districts. Teachers participated in eight vPLC sessions focused on using ASSISTments reports to differentiate instruction, establish routines, and discuss student data. During implementation, teachers followed a four-step formative process: create standards-aligned math assignments, assist students through immediate feedback, assess class performance, and analyze answers together to address common errors and adjust instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher participation in the eight vPLC sessions and implementation of the four-step formative process (Heritage & Popham, 2013) as part of using ASSISTments.
#### Constraints
- The intervention targets grades 6 through 8 math; prior studies showed ASSISTments improves learning when paired with in-person coaching, and the vPLC was added to reduce travel and scheduling barriers.

### Target Learners
- middle school students in grades 6-8
- middle school math teachers, including those in rural districts

### Learning Goals
- middle school mathematics achievement
- teachers' use of ASSISTments reports to differentiate instruction and adjust teaching
