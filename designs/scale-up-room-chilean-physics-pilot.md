# Scale-Up classroom with student-centered layout for active learning lectures

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Purpose-built classrooms of capacity 54 students used for the active learning pilot. The article reports that "Classes are held in Scale-Up rooms [30], build for this project, of capacity 54 students" and that "Their design promotes student-centered learning as the teacher is not the focal point". Features include five projectors, furniture arranged for micro groups of 3 students, wall-covered boards, portable blackboards per group, and easy circulation between desks.

## Design Implications
