# Two-workshop, 40-hour critical thinking faculty development intervention

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A faculty development program of two workshops (total 40 hours of direct contact) held 14 weeks apart, developed by a multidisciplinary team of nursing, basic sciences, and English language educators. It targeted knowledge, skills, and attitude dimensions of critical thinking, using Bloom's taxonomy for writing objectives and generating higher-order questions, plus active strategies such as "group work, games, concept maps, debate, and reflections". Between workshops, participants submitted reflections on applying their learning, and the second workshop critiqued participants' course grids for curriculum alignment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants teach in BSN programs and attend both workshops; between workshops they reflect on and attempt to apply workshop learning in their teaching practice
#### Constraints
- The article notes cultural and institutional barriers such as limited resources and expectations to complete content, which participants initially believed prevented active strategies

### Target Learners
- full-time nursing and non-nursing faculty teaching in BSN programs in Pakistan

### Learning Goals
- educators' ability to ask higher-order questions
- use of active teaching strategies
- facilitation skills for promoting student critical thinking
