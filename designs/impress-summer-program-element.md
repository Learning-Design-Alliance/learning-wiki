# IMPRESS summer program for first-generation and DHH students engaging in authentic science practice

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
IMPRESS (Integrating Metacognitive Practices and Research to Ensure Student Success) is a two-week bridge program for matriculating RIT students who are first generation and/or deaf/hard of hearing. Its objectives are to "engage students in authentic science practice, to facilitate the development of a supportive community, and to help the students reflect on the science and themselves". Students work in small groups of 3-4 and a whole class of 20 through laboratory experiments, reflective practices, and discussion.
