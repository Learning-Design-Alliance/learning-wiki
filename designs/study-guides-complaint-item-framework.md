# Study Guides created by clinical departments to support complaint-based learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Study Guides are discipline-specific learning-support documents developed by Duesseldorf's clinical departments and institutes, accessible via a web-based learning platform by complaint item. "All Study Guides adhere to a conceptual framework: a) learning objectives form the subject's point of view, b) diseases matching the complaints, c) special features in clinical management d) literature recommendations for further reading, e) questions for reflection." The information on special features in clinical management and the reflection questions exceed the contents of textbooks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Guides are created by the clinical departments and institutes that have selected a complaint item as course content
#### Constraints
- The Guides contain chapters with a selection of complaint items or other reasons for consultation relevant to each specific discipline

### Target Learners
- medical students in workplace-based practice blocks

### Learning Goals
- subject-specific understanding of complaint items
- clinical management
- reflective learning
