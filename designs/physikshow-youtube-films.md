# YouTube release of show films reaching a large online audience

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Two weeks before the article's writing, the group began putting the introductory films and films of experiments on YouTube. The most successful film, showing an aluminum foil boat floating in an aquarium filled with sulphur hexafluoride, had "over 290,000 viewings" within the first 3 weeks, making it "the fourth most viewed German science and technology film 'all time'", according to the article. The films extend the show's reach beyond the live auditorium audience.
