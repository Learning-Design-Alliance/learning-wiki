# Third-year undergraduate medical elearning resource (DVD and portal) at Queen's University Belfast

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The studied artifact is a substantial elearning resource embedded in the third year of Queen's University Belfast's five-year medical course, which from 2007 replaced all year-group wide traditional lectures. Delivered through a sophisticated portal and also on DVD (to circumvent temperamental internet access in peripheral hospitals), it was intended to complement geographically dispersed clinical placements, offering 'just in time learning', flexibility to link theory with clinical practice, and revision support. This resource was the focus of the focus-group study of engagement obstacles.
