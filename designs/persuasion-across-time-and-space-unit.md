# Persuasion across Time and Space: five-week CCSS-aligned ELA unit on persuasive text and media messages

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A five-week, Common Core State Standards-aligned English language arts unit on persuasive text and media messages, targeting middle school English learners at an intermediate level of proficiency. It shows teachers how to help students build English language mastery in tandem with conceptual understanding of text, using texts from television commercials to the Gettysburg Address and Civil Rights Movement speeches, with tools such as Aristotle's ethos, pathos, and logos for evaluating arguments. The unit is available online with downloadable teacher and student materials.
