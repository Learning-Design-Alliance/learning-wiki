# QASEE learning model (Questioning, Answering, Sharing, Extending, Evaluating)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
QASEE is a constructivism-based learning model designed to develop pre-service teachers' thinking skills through constructive experiential learning. Its five-phase syntax moves students from individually generating questions about unread material, to independently searching for answers, to group sharing with class presentations, to applying knowledge to new real-world contexts, and finally to independent self-evaluation. The article positions QASEE as extending the RQA model by adding group work, knowledge transfer, and self-reflection phases that RQA lacks.
