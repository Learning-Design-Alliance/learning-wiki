# A design model for flipped learning in higher education that integrates online and face-to-face sessions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article develops a prescriptive instructional design model for flipped learning (FL) that guides designers through analysis, design, development, implementation, and evaluation of both online and face-to-face components. The model treats online lectures and F2F activities as "core complementary constituents of the learning experience as a coherent whole" rather than as supplementary additions. It was validated through expert review, usability testing, and field evaluation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a design team or instructor able to create and curate online video lectures and facilitate interactive F2F sessions
#### Constraints
- Designed for higher education contexts; the authors note that FL design involves "considerable intricacy" and "practically infinite design variability in diverse contexts"

### Target Learners
- higher education students

### Learning Goals
- content mastery
- critical and creative thinking
- problem solving
- communication and presentation skills
