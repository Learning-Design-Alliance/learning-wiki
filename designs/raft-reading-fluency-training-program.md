# RAFT: Reading And Fluency Training based on phoneme awareness, a twelve-week one-to-one tutoring program adapted to Swedish orthography

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
RAFT is a phonics-based intervention program designed for the study and adapted to Swedish orthography, delivered as individual instruction every school day, 40 minutes a day for 12 weeks, for a total of 40 hours by special-education teachers trained within the project. Around "60% of the instruction time was spent on phonemic decoding and phonemic awareness, whereas about 40% was spent on comprehension strategies and fluency training." The program followed a strict daily progression, with fidelity checked by project staff visits and daily teacher reports.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers with a graduate diploma in special education, trained within the project to deliver the program
- Strict daily progression with detailed instructions and fidelity monitoring
#### Constraints
- Designed for Swedish orthography; sight word training was not included as there are few such words in Swedish

### Target Learners
- nine-year-old pupils in grade 3 with reading difficulties

### Learning Goals
- phonemic awareness and decoding
- reading fluency
- reading comprehension
- spelling
