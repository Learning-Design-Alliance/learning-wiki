# Blended task-based Business English programme combining plenary and small-group sessions with Moodle-mediated collaborative tasks and a virtual resource center

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The programme combines one-hour plenary sessions and one-hour small-group sessions (15 students) with two to four hours of computer-mediated work on a Moodle platform offering three spaces: a course space with scenarios and instructions, a class space for submitting productions and receiving feedback, and a VRC containing "contextualised practice exercises and explanations of specific forms". Students in groups of three or four complete six to eight business-oriented collaborative real-world tasks leading to oral and written productions, with corrective feedback helping them notice the gap.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher corrective feedback in the form of advice and suggestions directing students to post-task VRC micro-tasks
- Group work of three or four students on collaborative real-world tasks
#### Constraints
- VRC was underexploited by students, making its effects on language development difficult to assess

### Target Learners
- first-year university LEA (Foreign Languages and International Trade) students, CEFR levels A1 to C2

### Learning Goals
- business English accuracy, fluency, and professional competence in English
