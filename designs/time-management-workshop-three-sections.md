# Time management workshop covering calendars and task lists, removing distractions, and procrastination management

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The second hourlong workshop, "Taking Control of Your Time Management and Procrastination," had three main sections: "1) using calendars and task lists, 2) removing distractions, and 3) implementing procrastination management strategies." It drew on a meta-analysis of procrastination interventions and taught techniques such as the pomodoro technique, daily top-three task prioritization, and countering rationalizations for procrastination.
