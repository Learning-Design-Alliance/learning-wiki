# Culturally tailored character design — linguistic matching and contextual adaptation as the mechanism for relatability and engagement

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's explanatory account for why Super Divya engaged learners is cultural tailoring: the character was created to be culturally applicable to the Bihar context with language and concepts matched to the NMS population. The authors state that "Super Divya utilized culturally competent healthcare delivery with culturally specific concepts, linguistic matching, and adaptation of materials to the context in Bihar, India," drawing on the literature on culturally competent healthcare. Because Divya's experiences mirror the facilitation situations NMS encounter, relatability is posited to add to the module's effectiveness and to sustain engagement in the nurse mentoring program.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Characters, facility layouts, clothing, accents, and language must be modeled after the target learner population so learners recognize their own setting.
#### Constraints
- The authors note limited generalizability of the study due to the specific, tailored use of interactive video modules for NMS and NM in Bihar, while stating that specificity enabled the success of the character Divya.

### Target Learners
- Nurse Mentor Supervisors in Bihar, India
- Nurse Mentors in the AMANAT Jyoti nurse mentoring program

### Learning Goals
- Sustained engagement of simulation educators
- Reinforcement of simulation facilitation skills
