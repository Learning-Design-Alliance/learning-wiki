# SpinFrame apparatus: a 3D-printed frame holding letter-size paper, mounted on a turntable via a 3D-printed fitting

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The SpinFrame is the physical apparatus for the lab: "a 3D-printed rectangular frame mounted on a flat disk, which in turn is mounted onto a turntable" using an additional 3D-printed fitting. Its dimensions admit a standard 8.5 x 11-inch piece of paper on which students draw acceleration vectors and trace radial lines. The 3D files and assembly instructions are released under a CC-BY-NC license.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
