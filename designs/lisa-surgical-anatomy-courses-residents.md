# LISA national surgical anatomy courses for Dutch surgical residents

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Lowlands Institute for Surgical Anatomy (LISA) is a Dutch national institute in which all eight Dutch University Medical Centers cooperate on postgraduate surgical anatomy education. The course module Surgical Anatomy of the Colon and Rectum is given twice a year to second- or third-year residents "to enhance their knowledge of anatomy, aiming for applied surgical anatomy education". The Dutch Association of Surgery made LISA courses obligatory for all surgical residents in the Netherlands in 2004.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teaching is always provided by an anatomist and a surgeon, an involvement the authors consider an essential element of its success
#### Constraints
- The course was hosted in the SkillsLab of the Erasmus University Medical Center, a clinical training center with six laparoscopic working stations, a microsurgical lab, and three dissection rooms

### Target Learners
- second- and third-year surgical residents in general surgery in the Netherlands

### Learning Goals
- applied surgical anatomy knowledge for colorectal surgery
