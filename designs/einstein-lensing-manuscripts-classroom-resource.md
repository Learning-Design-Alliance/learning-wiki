# Einstein's published gravitational-lensing manuscripts as classroom material

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A set of Einstein manuscript calculations (Table I: the Prague Notebook pages of 1912 and 1915, the 1936 Letter to Mandl, the German manuscript and Science publication, and sheets Ms 1–3) held in the Albert Einstein Archives. The article proposes that "various manuscript calculations by Einstein have been published (see Table I) and allow students to explore and reconstruct Einstein's ideas and the concept of gravitational lensing directly from his own publication and scratch notes."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students need familiarity with basic notions of geometrical optics (thin-lens approximation), basic Euclidean geometry and trigonometry with the small-angle approximation, and manipulation of a quadratic equation
#### Constraints
- Most pathways are documented by Einstein's calculations, but his published note gives no derivation pathway

### Target Learners
- twelfth-grade high school physics students

### Learning Goals
- reconstructing the lensing equation and its solutions from historical notes
- exploring the concept of gravitational lensing
