# Fit to Study cluster-randomised trial design: 104 schools, stratified block randomisation, baseline and 12-month assessments

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The trial is a parallel group, superiority cluster-randomised efficacy trial comparing a one-academic-year VPA intervention with a 'PE as usual' control. As printed: "Schools (n = 104) were randomised into an intervention group or control 'PE as usual' control group using stratified block randomisation and an allocation ratio of 1:1", stratified by single-sex versus co-educational status, with randomisation performed by NatCen using a random number generator in STATA. Assessments occur at baseline (end of Year 7) and after 12 months (end of Year 8); the primary outcome is maths attainment via the Progress Test in Maths Level 13, analysed intention-to-treat by the independent evaluator NatCen.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schools must be secondary state or academy schools delivering PE, located in pre-defined local authorities, with Year 7 pupils moving to Year 8 at the start of the intervention
#### Constraints
- Schools were informed of their assigned group only after completing baseline assessments, to reduce risk of bias; the primary maths test is administered post-intervention only to a random selection of half of all form groups per school

### Target Learners
- Year 8 pupils (aged 12-13) in secondary state schools

### Learning Goals
- Maths attainment, cardiorespiratory fitness, cognitive performance, and mental health
