# Rhode Island's new performance framework sets rigorous, multidimensional quality standards across academic achievement, governance, financial stability, and mission alignment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes an enumerated set of quality standards that the new performance framework imposes on existing charter schools. Schools must "demonstrate strong academic gains, measured through standardized assessments" and match or exceed sending district performance; governing boards are accountable for oversight and culture; schools must show long-term financial viability; and they are evaluated on mission fulfillment and service to diverse populations including English learners and students with disabilities. The framework applies these standards from the initial application through renewal, with annual dashboards supporting continuous improvement.

### Context
#### Requirements
