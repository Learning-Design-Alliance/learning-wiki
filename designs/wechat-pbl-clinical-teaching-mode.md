# WeChat–PBL teaching mode: pre-class delivery of clinical case materials and questions to small WeChat groups, in-group discussion, and classroom presentation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a clinical teaching workflow in which WeChat group chat is combined with problem-based learning. The teacher divides students into WeChat groups of 3-4 students, delivers real patient case materials (text, images, video) and questions before class, students research the material individually and discuss within their group, and a representative presents the group's conclusions in class followed by teacher comments. The article calls this 'the teaching mode of WeChat combined with PBL'.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Written patient agreement must be obtained before clinical data, imaging and surgery materials are collected and shared
- Each WeChat group contains 3-4 students, with a teacher delivering materials and questions before class
