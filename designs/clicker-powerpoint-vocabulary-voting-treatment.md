# Clicker-based L2 vocabulary voting treatment via PowerPoint

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An instructional setup in which the teacher projects vocabulary questions created in PowerPoint on a board, students respond by pressing the matching button on hand-held clickers that wirelessly transmit input to a receiver, and statistical results are displayed on the board after polling closes. In this study the Clicker Group voted on questions about 30 low-frequency or off-list words from James and the Giant Peach across five treatments of approximately 30 minutes each over two months, while the Non-Clicker Group answered by hand-raising or orally.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A presentation software such as PowerPoint to create the questions
- Clicker handsets and a receiver to transmit and collect student responses
#### Constraints
- The study used low-frequency or off-list words that students encountered mainly only in the classroom, which the authors say may have limited learnability and motivation

### Target Learners
- Grade 8 secondary ESL learners (age 13-14)

### Learning Goals
- English L2 vocabulary knowledge, assessed through drawing, translation, or explanation
