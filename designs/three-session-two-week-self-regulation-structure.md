# Three-session module structure with two-week intervals mapped to self-regulation phases

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each dietary behavior module follows a reusable session structure: "Each module (i.e. fruit, vegetables, high-energy snacks and fat) consists of three sessions of approximately 30 minutes that can be worked through with a two-week interval between each session (taking six weeks in total)". Session 1 covers the pre-action phase (knowledge, tailored feedback, attitude, self-efficacy, goal and implementation-intention setting); session 2 evaluates goal achievement, formulates coping plans and allows goal adaptation; session 3 addresses long-term maintenance with monitoring, coping plans and maintenance instructions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- E-mail invitations after each two-week action period to prompt return to the intervention
#### Constraints
- The fat module omits the attitude and self-efficacy sections in the first session to limit participant burden because fat-intake assessment is quite long

### Target Learners
- adults working through the modules at home at their own pace

### Learning Goals
- moving from intention formation to goal pursuit, coping with failure and maintenance of dietary change
