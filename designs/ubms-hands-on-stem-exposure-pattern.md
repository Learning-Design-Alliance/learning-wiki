# Hands-on math-science exposure pattern: labs, computer facilities, field sites, and scientist contact

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The UBMS design pattern combines practical STEM settings with expert contact: grantees "provide participants with hands-on experience in laboratories, computer facilities, and at field sites, as well as opportunities to learn from mathematicians and scientists at host institutions." The report presents this as the program's core design for building skills and experiences that prepare disadvantaged students for college success in math and science fields.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Host institutions (colleges and universities) with laboratories, computer facilities, and field sites
- Access to mathematicians and scientists at host institutions
#### Constraints
- Implemented within the federal Upward Bound grant structure for disadvantaged high school students

### Target Learners
- Disadvantaged high school students

### Learning Goals
- Math and science preparation for college
- Career exposure to mathematicians and scientists
