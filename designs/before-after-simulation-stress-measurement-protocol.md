# Before-after simulation stress measurement protocol combining STAI-Y, NASA-TLX, and five-point salivary cortisol sampling

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A measurement protocol for stress in simulation-based training: STAI-Y administered before (T0) and after (T1) sessions, NASA-TLX at the end to assess mental workload, and five saliva samples collected "upon the arrival of the student, 10 min before starting the simulation, and then at 10, 20, and 30 min after the training". Cortisol was assayed in duplicate by enzyme immunoassay and summarized as AUCg, treated as related to total hormonal output, plus a fold-increase ratio. Exclusion criteria (smokers, obese, chronic disease, relevant medication, recent major stress) controlled biological confounds.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants refrained from eating, drinking, smoking, and brushing for at least 30 min before sample collection; samples were centrifuged and stored at -20 °C before duplicate assay
#### Constraints
- Only 77 of 148 subjects (52.02%) correctly collected all salivary samples; AUCg extrapolates between cortisol peaks, so an error margin must be taken into consideration

### Target Learners
- medical students

### Learning Goals
- assessing psychological and biological stress responses during simulation-based clinical training
