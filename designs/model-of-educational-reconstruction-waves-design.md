# Model of Educational Reconstruction as the design framework for identifying disciplinary knots in wave physics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors used the Model of Educational Reconstruction (citing Duit, Komorek, & Wilbers, 1997; Duit, 2007) to guide design of the learning path. According to this framework they "identified some disciplinary knots that arise usually in empirical investigation," which structured the path around waves as functions of several variables, superposition, energy transport, analogy in wave phenomena, and resonance. It functions here as the research-to-design bridge connecting empirical studies of student difficulties to the choice of laboratory activities.
