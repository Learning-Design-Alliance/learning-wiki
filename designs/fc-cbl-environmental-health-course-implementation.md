# FC+CBL implementation of Introduction to Environmental Health Science for MPH at Guilin Medical University

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A course-level implementation combining flipped classroom and case-based learning for a required MPH course. Classic cases in five content areas (environmental pollution, indoor air pollution, occupational poisoning or injury, food poisoning and transgenic food, social factors and health) were "sent to the students through QQ or the Rain class platform before class"; students worked in 3–4-person groups, analysed the case, made slides, and reported in class, followed by joint teacher–student analysis. Assessment combined daily performance (30%) and paper results (70%).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students need pre-class access to cases via an online platform (QQ or Rain class) and time to prepare group presentations
- Cases must be selected to match syllabus and curriculum requirements
#### Constraints
- Implemented with small cohorts (22 students); the authors note fewer MPH students per semester limits generalisation
- Daily-performance assessment differed between experimental and control groups, so grades are not fully comparable

### Target Learners
- Master of Public Health (MPH) graduate students

### Learning Goals
- Theoretical understanding of environment and health
- Independent learning ability
- Application of knowledge and innovative spirit
