# Medical Expertise assessment framework combining case documentation, tutorial presentations and Mini-CEX

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A competency-oriented, workplace-based assessment framework in which students document every case (3–4 cases per week) using predetermined questions and present it to a doctor, assessed by checklist with individual feedback; present cases in twice-weekly tutorials of up to 15 students rated by medical tutors; and pass at least one Mini-CEX per practice block, predominantly performed on the patient. "All of the above listed assessments of point 3) to 5) have to be successfully passed, they sum up to the faculty internal transcript „Medical Expertise"." Students collect these assessments in a portfolio until the Second State Examination (M2).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mini-CEX needs and expectations and rating criteria must be transparent and available for the students; about 80 Mini-CEX developed by the departments are available
#### Constraints
- A Mini-CEX should average about 20-30 minutes, including an oral feedback; some manual skills are performed on a model and complex communicative situations with simulated patients

### Target Learners
- medical students in years 3–5 practice blocks

### Learning Goals
- case presentation and moderation skills
- critical analysis of findings
- clinical-practical skills
- communication skills
