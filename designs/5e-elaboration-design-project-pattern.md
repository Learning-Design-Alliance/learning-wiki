# 5E Elaboration-phase engineering design project applying previously investigated concepts

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
This lesson-level pattern places a design-build-test project in the Elaboration Phase of the BSCS 5E Instructional Model: after teacher-led investigations of heat, temperature, energy, and thermal conductors and insulators, students apply those concepts to design, construct, and test an artifact (here an insulation box keeping sheep organs between 4 °C and 8 °C). The author notes "The activity is that of the Elaboration Phase based on the BSCS 5E Instructional Model" and aligns it to NGSS performance expectation MS-PS3-3. It integrates engaging tasks, cooperative self-selected groups, rubric-based assessment of four practices, and cross-disciplinary extensions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prior exploration of the target concepts through student investigations (adapted from the Thermal Concept Evaluation), a rubric communicated in advance, materials and tools made available, and laboratory safety routines.
#### Constraints
- The activity is relatively expensive: approximately $200 for a class of 30 students; the author suggests model organs, donated materials, and temperature probes to reduce cost and data-collection burden.

### Target Learners
- grade-seven (12- to 13-year-old) middle school students

### Learning Goals
- conceptual understanding of heat, temperature, energy, and thermal insulators and conductors
- planning investigations, analyzing and interpreting data, constructing explanations, and designing solutions
