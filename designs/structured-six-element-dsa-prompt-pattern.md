# Structured six-element prompts that break DSA problems into manageable steps

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's structured prompts decompose DSA problems into manageable parts and guide students step-by-step toward solutions. The final prompt version included six elements: "Problem Understanding", "Key Characteristics", "Common Operations", "Algorithm Description", "Real-World Scenario", and "Code Snippet". Prompts were iteratively refined over three rounds based on student and TA feedback, adding warnings about common pitfalls, and were credited with keeping critical information intact and encouraging critical engagement rather than passive acceptance of AI answers.
