# Interactive Learning Setting Cooperative Model (ILSC/SSIPs) as an instructional framework for mathematical critical thinking

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The ILSC model (also called SSIPs) is an interactive learning model with cooperative settings that emphasizes students constructing knowledge through problem solving in groups. It rests on four principles: "cooperation; freedom of expression; responsibility to yourself and groups; and equality". The model follows five syntax phases: introduction, activity or problem solving, presentation and discussion, closing, and assessment (before and after learning). It is designed to maximize interaction between teachers, students, and material.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must facilitate student interaction with students so that teaching and learning activities can take place properly
- Assessment is done before and after learning
#### Constraints
- The model requires active students and was studied in the context of Aliyah Madrasas (Islamic senior secondary) mathematics classes

### Target Learners
- Grade X (senior secondary) students in Aliyah Madrasas

### Learning Goals
- Mathematical critical thinking ability across five aspects: trigger event, exploration, drawing conclusions, clarification, and resolution
