# Structured low-cost home-based psychomotor training programme guided by occupational therapists

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A home-based exercise programme delivered to first-year dental students over eight weeks, instructed by three occupational therapists in roughly one hour, with daily, weekly and monthly tasks performed with both hands. The article reports that "The training consisted of five days a week (approximately 20–30 min a day), for 8 weeks." Participants received a booklet with pictures or videos, recorded activities by video or photograph sent via WhatsApp, and received a check-in message every two weeks. The programme targeted force control, grip strength, gross coordination, hand-eye coordination, praxias, surface sensitivity and spatial orientation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participant adherence monitored through video/photo records sent via WhatsApp and biweekly researcher messages
#### Constraints

- first-year dental students

### Learning Goals
- manual dexterity
- tactile sensitivity
