# Three key concepts from the epistemological development of Ampère-Maxwell's law frame its undergraduate teaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article derives three key concepts (KC1–KC3) from a historical-epistemological analysis of how classical electromagnetism developed into Ampère-Maxwell's law: KC1, that "Ampère's law is only valid in stationary circuits where the current is closed"; KC2, that charge conservation requires including the displacement current; and KC3, that Ampère-Maxwell's law is general and must include conduction and displacement currents to calculate magnetic field circulation. These concepts "helps us to explicitly define learning goals regarding Ampère-Maxwell's law" (LO1–LO5) and guided the design of the study questionnaire.
