# Deliberate practice as the instructional design basis for haptic simulation training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The school extended its existing deliberate-practice-based simulation approach to haptics training. Deliberate practice is described as "a theory of strategic instructional design for purposeful action to acquire psychomotor skills", requiring explicit learning outcomes, learner focus, consciously assigned task difficulty, structured purposeful repetitiveness, immediate feedback, in-action reflection, evaluation towards mastery learning, and increasing degree of difficulty.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Explicit learning outcomes, structured purposeful repetitiveness, immediate feedback, in-action reflection, evaluation towards mastery learning, and increasing degree of difficulty
#### Constraints
- Opportunity to practise in simulation was limited by complex timetables and increased post-pandemic need

### Target Learners
- Pre-clinical undergraduate dental students in years 2 and 3 BDS

### Learning Goals
- Psychomotor/manual dexterity skills
- Mastery of operative dental procedures
