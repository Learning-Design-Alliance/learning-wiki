# Three-stage professor training cycle for research-based active learning implementation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A weekly preparation cycle in which a coordination team distinct from the teachers designs activities, reviews them with professors and teaching assistants, and the teachers then implement them. The article states that "Implementing active learning strategies in the classroom requires a preparation work that we structured in a three-stage cycle (ﬁg.1 top)", starting two weeks before the semester and repeated each week. It supported a pilot transforming introductory physics lectures for engineering programs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A coordination team distinct from the group of teachers to prepare and review activities
- Professors accompanied by teaching assistants present simultaneously during lectures
- Weekly iteration of the cycle starting two weeks before the semester
