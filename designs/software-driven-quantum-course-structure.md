# Software-driven quantum computing course structure of weekly modules, programming assignments, and a capstone final project

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The course pattern teaches quantum computing through a practical, software-driven sequence: an introductory software-setup and math-prerequisites module, 10 week-long lecture modules each with a 3-hour lecture and assignments, and a final project replacing a final exam. Weekly programming assignments counted for 60% of the grade and the final project for 40% (up to 65% in spring 2023). The author reports this approach is effective for students with software development backgrounds.
