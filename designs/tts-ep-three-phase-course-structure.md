# Three-phase structure of psychodrama-based teacher training: needs evaluation, technique training, effectiveness evaluation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The TTS-EP course follows a three-phase structure within a five-hour intensive session: a one-hour needs-evaluation focus group, a three-hour training phase with three sub-phases (oral presentation of techniques, technique-content association, and experiential technique training supervised by trainers), and a one-hour final focus group evaluating perceived effectiveness. "Implementation of TTS-EP involves three different phases", with Figure 1 illustrating the course structure.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training led by two psychologists specialized in educational psychology and psychodrama
- Volunteer teachers apply techniques in experiential processes supervised by the trainers
#### Constraints
- The training phase allocates 90 minutes to psychodramatic technique training

### Target Learners
- university teachers

### Learning Goals
- applying psychodramatic techniques to specific curricular content
