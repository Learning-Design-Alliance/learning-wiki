# High-fidelity hypovolemic shock simulation scenario with PEARLS pre-briefing and debriefing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A 10-minute simulated hypovolemic shock scenario in a replicated single-patient ICU room, developed using Healthcare Simulation Best Practice Standards, piloted with five third-year nursing students, and reviewed by two simulation experts. The plan specified pre-briefing (10 minutes), scenario (10 minutes) and debriefing (30 minutes), with "Method: PEARLS" debriefing and four scenario objectives including communication, teamwork, patient assessment and hypovolemic shock interventions. Sessions included 4–5 students, each participating once.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Developed using Healthcare Simulation Best Practice Standards and reviewed by two simulation experts
- Piloted prior to the main study for clarity, feasibility, and realism
#### Constraints
- Each student participated only once
- Scheduling prevented interactions between students who had and had not yet completed the simulation

### Target Learners
- Senior undergraduate nursing students

### Learning Goals
- Self-confidence in patient care interventions
- Shock assessment and intervention skills
