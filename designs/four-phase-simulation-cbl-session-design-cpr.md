# Four-phase simulation-with-CBL session design for CPR training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a reusable session structure designed in accordance with the Healthcare Simulation Standards of Best Practice, comprising "pre-simulation preparation", "role allocation and team formation", "scenario facilitation", and "structured debriefing". Clinical decision points are embedded at key procedural steps, roles rotate systematically, and each simulation cycle is followed by peer feedback and facilitator-guided reflection. The article uses this pattern to deliver case-based CPR training to pediatric interns.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

#### Constraints
- The authors note the single-center design restricts generalizability and that simulated scenarios may have introduced participant bias, limiting ecological validity

### Target Learners
- pediatric interns (postgraduate medical trainees)

### Learning Goals
- pediatric CPR clinical skills
- teamwork and role coordination
- clinical reasoning and decision-making
