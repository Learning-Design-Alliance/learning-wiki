# Adopt the combined flipped classroom + PBL approach over traditional lecture-based teaching, accepting the workload trade-off

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
This principle captures the article's own recommendation that "The combination of the flipped classroom and PBL teaching approach could be a better option over the traditional lecture-based classroom in the teaching of hyperthyroidism during endocrinology internship, although it can increase students' workload." Instructors choosing this approach gain higher post-quiz performance and better student-rated motivation, interaction and abilities, but must plan for substantially longer student preparation time (172 ± 33 min vs 108 ± 15 min) and the possible satisfaction cost of that burden.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must have access to personal computers or mobile platforms to view pre-class videos and materials on their own time
#### Constraints
- The article states the combined method "can increase their workload" and that "further optimizations are required" for wide implementation

### Target Learners
- Medical students in endocrinology or similar clinical internships

### Learning Goals
- Improve quiz performance and self-perceived clinical skills such as communication, clinical thinking and teamwork
