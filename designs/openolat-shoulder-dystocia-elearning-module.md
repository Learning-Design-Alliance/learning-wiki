# OpenOLAT-based one-hour e-learning module on shoulder dystocia with five videos, HELPERR scheme, and self-assessment quiz

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The e-learning element was created on the open source management system OpenOLAT, which the article says "offers the possibility to import, create and visualize own learning content as well as to analyze its usage." Learning objectives were collected with the LOOOP platform, and content was conveyed "using five different videos and accompanying texts on definition, diagnosis and manual measures" named after McRoberts, Rubin, Woods and Dudenhausen, including a 3D-animated training program. Content was based on ALSO recommended actions, and a self-assessment quiz followed the course.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Only login data is required for application and the system offers a support area; students were already familiar with OpenOLAT from previous sections of study
#### Constraints
- The article states some participants reported the information in the e-learning was not sufficiently comprehensible

### Target Learners
- final-year medical students
- midwifery trainees

### Learning Goals
- theoretical knowledge of diagnosis and treatment of shoulder dystocia
- NKLM learning objectives for shoulder dystocia
