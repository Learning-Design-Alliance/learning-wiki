# Simulation-debriefing pattern combining lectures, simulated conversations with actors, video-review debriefings, photolanguage, and a follow-up session

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The PERCS-ICU course structure combines multiple learning methods in a fixed sequence: brainstorming, a lecture, three simulations of difficult conversations with trained actors, learner-centered group debriefings with video review and actors' feedback, a photolanguage session before the organ-donation simulation, and a follow-up session where residents recount what they applied in practice. The article states the workshop "was conducted using a combination of learning methods, such as lectures, simulations with trained actors, group debriefings, and a photolanguage session." Photolanguage is described as a structured group activity using black and white photographs to stimulate communication on a topic.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- trained actors with clinical psychology background
- facilitators using a learner-centered debriefing style
- a separate simulation room with closed-circuit television for observation
#### Constraints
- designed for small groups of 10–15 residents
- the follow-up is scheduled 15 days after the workshop

### Target Learners
- postgraduate medical residents

### Learning Goals
- difficult-conversation skills with families
- empathy
- self-reflection and emotional awareness
