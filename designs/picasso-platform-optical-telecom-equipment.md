# PICASSO research platform and its optical telecommunications equipment set used for teaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The PICASSO platform (Innovation and Design Platform for the Analysis and Simulation of Optical Systems) at the University of Burgundy, part of the Solitons, Lasers and Optical Communications team, is specialized in high-speed telecommunications with state-of-the-art lightwave characterization equipment. For the first time, these research facilities were involved in a pedagogic activity for a Master's-level audience outside internships. The equipment set included an external-cavity laser diode at 1550 nm, a 10 GHz lithium niobate phase modulator, photodiodes and oscilloscopes (including a 1 THz optical sampling oscilloscope), two optical spectrum analyzers (0.07 nm and <0.1 pm resolution), a 26 GHz electrical spectrum analyzer, SMF-28 fiber spool, and a programmable Finisar Waveshaper spectral shaper.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cooperation with a research-dedicated platform, given the approximately 500 k€ acquisition budget
- Technical data sheets compiled into a booklet given to students well in advance
#### Constraints
- Interface and technical documentation exclusively in English
- High-end devices (optical sampling oscilloscope, real-time oscilloscope, high-resolution OSA) each costing more than 100 k€

### Target Learners
- Master's-level physics students

### Learning Goals
- hands-on use of standard optical telecommunications equipment
- experimental characterization of optical signals in temporal and spectral domains
