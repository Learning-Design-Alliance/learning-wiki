# Produce MOOC videos with genuine video technology and careful scripting rather than videotaping a classroom course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article argues that MOOC videos should be produced as videos, using green-screen technology with text, equations, images and animations incorporated in editing, and that all material should be carefully scripted and shootings controlled by a fellow teacher. The author states: "our pedagogical project was to be realized by videos that used a genuine video technology (green screen), rather than by the mere videotaping of a classroom course". This workflow, initiated with the teaser video, was kept during the entire course.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Video production requires professional camera and editing support, scripting of all material, and control of shootings by a fellow teacher.
#### Constraints
- Because videos are replayed and viewed non-linearly, even slight imprecisions such as omitted words and half sentences would create terrible confusion, further amplified by multiple viewings.

### Target Learners
- Registered MOOC students in advanced physics

### Learning Goals
- Mastery of statistical mechanics and computational physics content
