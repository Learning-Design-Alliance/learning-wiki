# Clinical Supervision Model (CSM) for teaching practice

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The CSM is a collaborative supervision program involving the teacher trainee, cooperating teacher, and university supervisor, adapted from a model used at a major university in the southeastern United States. The article states that "the CT and US observe and provide systematic feedback to the TT, and opportunities for TT self-reflection in an effort to improve the TTs' performance in the classroom." Stakeholders receive training in observation techniques, feedback language, three-way conferences, data collection, reflective techniques, and action plans, with coordinators maintaining communication to strengthen the triadic relationship.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training for CTs and USs in their roles, observation techniques, feedback language, three-way conferences, and reflective techniques
- Communication between stakeholders maintained through coordinators throughout the teaching practice
#### Constraints
- Evaluated in one large Turkish state university's classroom teacher training program; the article notes results come from "this one teacher education program"

### Target Learners
- preservice teacher trainees (TTs) in teaching practice

### Learning Goals
- improving teaching performance through systematic observation, feedback, and self-reflection
