# Numerical study of the quantum bouncer connecting the shooting method, Airy-function solutions, and experimental neutron gravitational bound states

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Students studied the quantum bouncer potential (infinite wall plus linear gravitational potential) first via the shooting method in L9, using the half-oscillator as a testbed, then revisited it in L12 with exact Airy function solutions. 'The change to dimensionless variables for the neutron-bouncer pr oblem already pro vides insight into the natural length and energy scales of the syste m, allowing for an early comparison to the experimental values' from ILL experiments on neutrons in the Earth's gravitational field. Numerically obtained eigenvalues were compared to exact values, and expectation values ⟨n|z|n⟩ with macroscopic magnitudes (10's of µm) played a role in the experimental identification of the quantum bound states.
