# Short student survey measuring mathematical experiences, sense of belonging, and mathematics self-efficacy

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A student-facing instrument, taking "no longer than 15 minutes to complete," administered at the beginning and end of each school year to triangulate teacher self-reports of implementation. It uses the same construct categories as the teacher survey—coherence, cognitive demand, collaboration and discourse, formative assessment, and differentiation—with items drawn from existing valid and reliable instruments. It includes belonging items, described as a significant predictor of motivation, and "Nine additional survey items were also included to replicate a reliable self-efficacy scale." The team plans exploratory factor analysis and correlation plots to compare teacher and student reports.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires items drawn from existing valid and reliable survey instruments and administration at the beginning and end of each school year
#### Constraints
- The report notes classroom practice may not meet a coherence standard for implementation fidelity if teachers' and students' reports differ, which is why student-facing instruments were created

### Target Learners
- middle grades (6–8) mathematics students

### Learning Goals
- capturing student experience of mathematical opportunities to learn and classroom interactions
