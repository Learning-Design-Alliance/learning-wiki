# Symani Surgical System with VITOM 3D exoscope setup for preclinical microsurgical anastomosis training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A preclinical training configuration in which participants at the Symani surgeon console, seated in "an ergonomically designed chair, in which the participant sits and holds the manipulators", perform microvascular anastomoses on 2 mm PVA vessel models visualized through the VITOM 3D exoscope on a 32" 3D/4K display. Motion scaling was set to x10, and micro-dilator and micro-needle holder suture cut NanoWrist instruments were used, with footswitch control of instrumentation. In the trial, 13 participants each performed nine anastomoses across three sessions, yielding 117 anastomoses for analysis.
