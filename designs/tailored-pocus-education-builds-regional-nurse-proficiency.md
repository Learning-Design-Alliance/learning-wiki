# Provide regional haemodialysis nurses with tailored POCUS education to build proficiency in vascular access assessment and cannulation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's concluding recommendation is that tailored education, not equipment alone, is what enables proficiency: 'The results have demonstrated that when nursing staff are provided with tailored education, they can become proficient in the basic functions of POCUS to assess and cannulate HD vascular access.' The programme combined recorded online modules with face-to-face practical training and sonographer support, delivered on site at three regional units. The authors argue this matters most in regional hospitals with limited access to vascular access specialists.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A dedicated training programme combining online lecture modules with face-to-face practical assessment
- A POCUS machine available at each site
#### Constraints
- Stated for basic POCUS functions in regional haemodialysis units; the article notes no routinely collected vascular access PROMs existed for comparison

### Target Learners
- Haemodialysis nurses in regional units, from trainees to experienced staff

### Learning Goals
- POCUS assessment of vascular access and ultrasound-guided cannulation
