# Adopt minimum training standards and greater curricular alignment across UEG member societies, using the ESBGH Blue Book as a foundation

## Description
The article advocates for minimum training standards and greater curricular alignment across UEG member societies to ensure consistent and high‐quality gastroenterology training in Europe. It states that "The ESBGH Blue Book already provides an excellent foundation" and advocates "a pragmatic, stepwise approach: promoting mutual exchange, encouraging the sharing of best practices, supporting voluntary benchmarking, and strengthening collaboration between national societies, educational experts, and UEG bodies." This principle is grounded in the study's finding of marked heterogeneity across the five analysed domains.
