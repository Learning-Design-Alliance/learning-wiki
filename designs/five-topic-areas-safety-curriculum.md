# Five topic focus areas structure a patient safety and quality curriculum for medical physics residents

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The program organizes its content around five topic focus areas selected from published recommendations (AAPM Report No. 249, CAMPEP standards, TG-100, ASTRO's "Safety is No Accident"): accidents and outcomes, failure mode and effect analysis, incident learning and safety culture, principles in error proofing and quality improvement, and quality audits. Each topic area carries learning objectives and assigned reference readings. The authors note the areas "are very similar to those proposed by Yeung and Greenwalt" and consistent with ACGME and CAMPEP recommendations.
