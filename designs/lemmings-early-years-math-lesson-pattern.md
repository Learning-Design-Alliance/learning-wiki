# Videogame-based lesson plan pattern for early-years mathematics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes a lesson plan pattern using the videogame Lemmings to develop early mathematics. Its general goals include learning "the possibilities offered by the body" and taking first steps toward logic-mathematics skills. Specific goals cover ordering, quantifying and classifying, problem solving, playful computer use, experimenting, and knowing quantity and space concepts, developed through conceptual, procedural and attitudinal contents.
