## Description
The article develops an educational chatbot system for biology learning that lets students "interact with a chatbot out of class anytime and anywhere and further obtain instant learning supports." It was designed to support students learning and reviewing biology knowledge outside class, and was evaluated in a grade 7 quasi-experiment against traditional biology learning.
