# Nineteen expert-ranked innovation learning activities for medical curricula

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A prioritized set of 19 learning activities produced by the NGT process, spanning conducive environments for creative thinking, brainstorming healthcare challenges, group problem-solving, prototyping solutions, peer feedback, reflection sessions, mentor and industry connections, and continued exploration beyond the classroom. The article states the NGT process "highlights 19 learning activities as key components for developing an innovation model," with expert ranking scores from 2 (Disagree) to 5 (Strongly Agree) establishing priority order.

#### Requirements
