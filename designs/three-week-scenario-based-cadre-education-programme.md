# Researcher-developed 3-week scenario-based education programme for health cadres

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A researcher-developed education programme delivered over 3 weeks (one 4-hour day per week) plus a fourth-week evaluation, combining a theory lecture, skills training with measurement equipment, and "scenario-based simulation" with pregnant women as simulated patients. It was grounded in a prior ethnographic study of cultural barriers and family decision-making in the same community, and evaluated via a scenario-based home-visit test scored with a skills checklist by midwife evaluators.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained midwife evaluators with detailed checklists to maintain rating consistency
- Scenario materials and simulated patients drawn from locally observed risk situations
#### Constraints
- Delivered over a brief period of 3 weeks, which the authors state may not have been sufficient to produce sustained improvements in competency

### Target Learners
- Experienced female community health cadres (volunteers) in Banjarnegara, Indonesia

### Learning Goals
- Identifying pregnancy risk factors and danger signs
- Health assessment skills (blood pressure, abdominal palpation, height, weight, BMI)
- Communication with pregnant women and families and referral behaviour
