# Three de novo manuals (tutor, practical activities, participant) supporting the PBL breastfeeding course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
To facilitate the tasks and performance of tutors and participants and make the process transparent, three manuals were developed de novo: a 41-page tutor manual with glossary, objectives, schedule, detailed guides for tutorial groups, practical activities and clinical practice, plus assessment instructions, a communication-skills checklist, a constructive feedback model and three PowerPoint presentations; a 44-page manual for practical activities and supervised clinical practice with cases, checklist instructions and role-play tips; and a 50-page participant manual condensing or expanding content from the other two.
