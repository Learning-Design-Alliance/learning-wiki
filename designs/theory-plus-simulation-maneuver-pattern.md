# Combined theory-plus-simulation maneuver pattern for crisis management training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A course-level design that pairs theoretical sessions with staged practical maneuvers of increasing complexity. The intervention type is described as "Combined intervention (educational-operational)" including "Theoretical training (training workshop based on the crisis management model)" and "Practical simulation (realistic earthquake scenarios with mannequins and actors)," followed by "Team exercise (coordination between personnel in crisis situations)." The simulation stages had "increasing complexity."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Realistic earthquake scenarios with mannequins and trained actors
- Standard educational films and video debriefing
#### Constraints
- The authors note a "simulation-reality gap inherent in training exercises"

### Target Learners
- Prehospital emergency medical personnel

### Learning Goals
- Decision-making under pressure, team coordination, and management of limited resources in disaster response
