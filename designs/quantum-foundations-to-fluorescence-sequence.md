# Quantum-foundations-to-fluorescence instructional sequence

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The module builds fluorescence understanding stepwise: 'to understand how fluorescence works in anthracene we will first consider the structure of the atoms and the building up of the periodic system of the elements', then the chemical bond, electron spin and the exclusion principle, singlet and triplet states in helium, molecular bonding in H2, and finally π-electron transitions in anthracene.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prior chemistry lessons covering the benzene structures (Kekulé and the hexagon-with-circle picture)
- Completion of the preceding de Broglie module
#### Constraints
- Only the π-electrons of anthracene are treated as giving rise to fluorescence and phosphorescence; the low-lying 1s carbon orbitals are left out of the bonding account

### Target Learners
- 17-year-old secondary school students

### Learning Goals
- understand fluorescence and phosphorescence in anthracene
- apply quantum mechanical concepts such as orbitals, spin, and singlet and triplet states
