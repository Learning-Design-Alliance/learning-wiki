# FC-TCLEBL: flipped classroom combined with team-, case-, lecture- and evidence-based learning for ophthalmology teaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
FC-TCLEBL is a self-designed ophthalmology teaching model that combines a flipped classroom with team-, case-, lecture- and evidence-based learning. Students study pre-class materials (PPTs, teaching videos, classic cases) shared via QQ or WeChat, work in four small teams preparing PowerPoint presentations on assigned subtopics of a diabetic retinopathy case, present in class, and the teacher recaps key points and summarizes evidence-based medicine sources. The article reports it aims to "promote student participation and strengthen the students' understanding of medical knowledge, clinical thinking, and scientific research while effectively reducing the students' extra-curricular burden."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students need pre-class access to shared materials and internet resources, WeChat groups for teacher support, and organization into small teams with assigned presentation topics
#### Constraints
- The article's implementation was limited to a single chapter on diabetic retinopathy and did not address how to choose themes or frequency of use
- The study did not compare the effects of FC, TBL and CBL separately

### Target Learners
- eight-year medical program students in ophthalmology

### Learning Goals
- clinical thinking
- scientific research ability
- active learning
- teamwork
- humanistic care
- practical application of clinical knowledge
