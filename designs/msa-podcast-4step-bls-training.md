# Media-supported 4-step approach (MSA): a podcast-based variant of the Peyton-style 4-step resuscitation training method

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The MSA is a newly created variant of the standard 4-step approach for BLS training in which steps 1 (demonstration) and 2 (deconstruction/explanation) are delivered through a standardised self-produced podcast containing video and audio with "all key information about the BLS algorithm and procedural resuscitation skills". Steps 3 and 4 proceed as in the standard method, with all tutorials supervised by certified ERC Advanced Life Support instructors and equal course duration for both groups. The element was evaluated in the reported controlled parallel-group trial on manikins.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Certified Advanced Life Support instructors from the ERC supervise the tutorials in the same manner in both approaches
- Same training time and support for every participant
- A standardised self-produced podcast including all key information about the BLS algorithm and procedural resuscitation skills
#### Constraints
- Evaluated in layperson first-year medical students, not representative of health-care professionals
- Tested in a manikin simulation, which cannot fully mimic an unconscious victim

### Target Learners
- laypersons without previous medical training
- first-year medical students

### Learning Goals
- basic life support algorithm knowledge
- external chest compression skill acquisition and retention
