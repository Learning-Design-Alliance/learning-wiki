# Adapted cardiac rehabilitation training curriculum for home care clinicians built on AACVPR core competencies

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A training curriculum for RNs, PTs, and OTs delivered in two 4-hour sessions led by an exercise physiologist, registered dietician, two nurse practitioners, and a program director. It covers six sections from "Background on Cardiac Rehabilitation" through "Application to Practice", including exercise physiology with the FITT equation and 2-minute walk test, nutrition, risk factor management, behavioral change and motivational interviewing, caregiver engagement, and the teach-back method. Content was adapted from the AACVPR core competencies for the home care setting.
