# Learning-style-matched memorization instruction in piano education

## Description
The article proposes that piano students have different learning styles (visual, audio, kinesthetic) and that memorization instruction should be matched to the student's dominant style. Visual students perceive notes as figures and are good note readers but struggle with memorization; supporting them with visual elements addresses this specific difficulty. The framework draws on the distinction between primary learning areas (playing, reciting, interpretation) and secondary learning areas in piano pedagogy.
