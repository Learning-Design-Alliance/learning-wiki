# Top-Level Structure (TLS) method as a text-structure teaching framework

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The TLS method is a reading and writing strategy based on Meyer's (1975) hierarchy of text content into top, middle, and bottom levels of importance. It teaches students to identify the organizational plan of a text to find main ideas and supporting details. The method "consists of four types of structure, which are listing, problem and solution, cause and effect, and comparison and contrast". Students learn to use signaling words to identify these structures.
