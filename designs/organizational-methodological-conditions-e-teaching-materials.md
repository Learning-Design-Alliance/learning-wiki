# Effective use of e-teaching materials requires attention to students' needs and motivation, cognitive independence, and control and diagnostics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article grounds organizational and methodological conditions for the effective use of e-teaching materials in future teachers' training, naming "students' needs and motivation, cognitive independence, control and diagnostics." In practice this meant identical tests for experimental and control groups introduced at the same stages of studying material, with electronic tests as a module of the e-teaching materials for experimental groups, and multi-level integrative tasks ensuring independent fulfillment of operations with the materials.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assessment must control assimilation of knowledge and skills, with tests applied after studying each topic and identical across groups.
#### Constraints
- The conditions were grounded in the training of future humanities teachers at one university.
