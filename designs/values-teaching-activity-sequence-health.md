# Values-teaching activity sequence for health education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A 9-week instructional pattern in which activities developed according to values teaching methods were applied to teach the value of paying attention to being healthy within the 4th-grade Social Sciences course. The application "covered a total of 27 class hours during 9 weeks" and produced significant attitude and behaviour gains compared to regular instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Activities must be developed in accordance with values teaching methods
- 27 class hours across 9 weeks
#### Constraints
- Tested only with 4th-grade students in two Turkish primary schools

### Target Learners
- 4th grade elementary students

### Learning Goals
- adopting the value of paying attention to being healthy
