# Leipzig proprietary lecture podcast player with chaptering and supplementary materials

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because YouTube only permits "simple lecture recordings" without subdividing videos into chapters or adding supplementary literature such as pdf files, scripts or learning objectives, the Faculty of Medicine has published its lecture podcasts since the 2011/12 winter semester in a proprietary player providing these additional options. The article states this "enables more effective learning". YouTube is instead recommended for augmenting lectures with training videos on diseases and medical skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- In-house hosting infrastructure for the proprietary player rather than third-party platforms.
#### Constraints
- YouTube cannot provide chapter subdivision or supplementary literature attachments for lecture recordings.

### Target Learners
- medical students at Universität Leipzig

### Learning Goals
- accessing lecture recordings with supplementary materials for more effective learning
