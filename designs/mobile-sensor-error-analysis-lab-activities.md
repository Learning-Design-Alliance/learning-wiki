# Mobile-device sensor laboratory activities for teaching error analysis and uncertainties

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A set of laboratory activities in which students record accelerometer time series with apps such as Physics Toolbox Suite, Androsensor and PhyPhox, and analyse mean values, standard deviations, histograms and σ intervals. The article states that "the lengthy and laborious manipulations necessary in traditional approaches based on repetitive measurements, are avoided allowing teaching to focus on the fundamental concepts." Activities span fluctuation recording, sensor resolution, noise intensities, interval counts, optimal measurement number, photograph steadiness and road quality.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Smartphones or tablets with built-in triaxial accelerometers
- A sensor-reading app (Physics Toolbox Suite, Androsensor or PhyPhox) with configurable sampling frequency and delayed execution to avoid touching the device when recording starts
#### Constraints
- Experiments described are based on the z direction perpendicular to the screen for clarity
- In the case of the iPhone the manufacturer does not provide sensor range and resolution information

### Target Learners
- first-year engineering and science students

### Learning Goals
- understanding statistical (type A) errors and uncertainties
- interpreting mean values, standard deviations, histograms and normal distributions
- distinguishing precision from accuracy

### Claims
