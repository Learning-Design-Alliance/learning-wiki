# Undergraduate medical curricula should emphasize STEMI temporality, assistance flows and transfer persistence within the health network

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article concludes that "STEMI approach needs to be improved during medical undergraduate courses, highlighting practical aspects such as temporality for starting treatment and the assistance flows of these patients" within the Brazilian Unified Health System. Because most students remembered antiplatelet agents but a significant proportion did not reinforce immediate transfer, the authors argue education must integrate pharmacological treatment with the urgency network so patients reach reperfusion-capable centers even without available beds.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teaching of the hierarchical network structure of the SUS and regional assistance flows
- Discussion of the Federal Medicine Council "zero vacancy" resolution on urgencies and emergencies
#### Constraints
- The authors note telemedicine support for ECG interpretation will be useless if physicians lack knowledge of how to speed up transfer according to the established assistance flow

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Learning Goals
- time-dependent STEMI treatment
- transfer persistence and network navigation
- patient communication
