# Integrated prevention-intervention framework connecting adaptive survey chatbots to mental health assessment tools

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The dissertation's proposed framework (Objective 3) unifies the preventive layer (TigerGPT plus AURA) with the intervention layer (PsychoGPT and SMMR). "This design allows insights from survey chatbot's adaptive dialogues, such as emerging student concerns or early signs of distress, to flow directly into specialized models like PsychoGPT or SMMR." By aligning both strategies under one system, administrators can enhance campus-wide satisfaction initiatives while identifying and supporting individuals experiencing psychological challenges.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Deployment of both the survey chatbot layer and the mental health assessment models within campus well-being services
#### Constraints
- The intervention tools were tested on public datasets rather than live campus data; the dissertation states they demonstrate capabilities with potential to be adapted for real campus contexts

### Target Learners
- university students, faculty, and staff

### Learning Goals
- campus satisfaction monitoring and early detection of psychological distress
