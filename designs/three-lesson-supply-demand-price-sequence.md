# Three-lesson sequence on how simultaneous changes in supply and demand affect market price

## Description
The instructional element is a series of three lessons through which students were to come to understand "how the simultaneous change in the supply of and the demand for a certain good affects its market price." The article reports that "this study shows how the understanding of the simultaneous change in the supply of and the demand for a certain good affects its market price can be brought about in a powerful way." It was taught by theory-guided teachers and by comparison teachers, with mastery assessed after the series.
