# Anthracene fluorescence module for Route.Net

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A downloadable module for 17-year-old secondary students, published for use in Route.Net 'to understand the detection of cosmic rays with two scintillator plates containing anthracene'. It is described as a typical mix of modern physics and chemistry, sequenced from atomic structure and spin through singlet and triplet states to bonding and fluorescence in anthracene, with exercises and hints.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- It follows the de Broglie module in the Route.Net sequence
- The exercises can be solved with the help of hints at the end of the module
#### Constraints
- Aimed at excellent pupils working on cosmic radiation within the HiSPARC project, as an optional extracurricular module

### Target Learners
- 17-year-old secondary school students in the Netherlands

### Learning Goals
- understand the fluorescence of anthracene
- understand how cosmic rays are detected with scintillator plates
