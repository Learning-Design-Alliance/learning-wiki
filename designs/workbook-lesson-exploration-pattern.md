# Workbook lessons pair guided exercises with self-exploration of program options, theory limitations, and troubleshooting

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Workbook's instructional pattern deliberately avoids the tutorial style of only showing how to run calculations. Its lessons propose exercises whose answers, with in-depth analysis, appear in a final chapter, and students are expected to explore program options and diagnose failures themselves. The authors write that tutorials "typically show how to do calculations without actually having students explore program options, discovering theory limitations, and puzzling out what to do when the program does not work as expected."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Exercises with answers and in-depth analysis provided in a final answers chapter
#### Constraints
- Students are expected to attempt exercises before consulting the answers chapter

### Target Learners
- Masters and advanced undergraduate chemistry students

### Learning Goals
- independent problem-solving with quantum chemistry programs
- understanding theory limitations and program behavior
