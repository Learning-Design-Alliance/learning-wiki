# Training in Teaching Skills: Educational Psychodrama (TTS-EP) teacher training course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
TTS-EP is a five-hour intensive university teacher training course implemented by the University of Seville in nine editions between 2017 and 2019, led by two psychologists specialized in educational psychology and psychodrama. It has three phases: a needs-evaluation focus group, three hours of training in four psychodramatic techniques, and a final effectiveness focus group. The course "is based on the role theory of the psychodramatic approach", training teachers to apply techniques to their own curricular content.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers select content from their own academic curricula for each technique in the technique-content association sub-phase
- Participation is recommended from teachers of a range of disciplines to generate variability in technique use
#### Constraints
- Each course is limited to a maximum of 15 teachers
- Training focuses strictly on educational contents, excluding family events and personal situations of participants

### Target Learners
- university teachers across disciplines

### Learning Goals
- educational use of psychodramatic techniques
- active teaching practices
- teaching effectiveness
