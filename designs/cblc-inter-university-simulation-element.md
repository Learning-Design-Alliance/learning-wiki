# The Case-Based Learning Challenge (CBLC): a digitally mediated, inter-university case-based simulation in three phases

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (3 for, 2 mixed, 2 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The CBLC is a single-session, approximately two-hour synchronous online simulation in which third-year physiotherapy students across 25 Italian universities discuss a complex cardiorespiratory case. It is "divided into three phases: (i) Case Presentation, (ii) Peer Review, (iii) Collaborative Q&A", delivered on Webex Cisco® with GoogleForm® for data collection. Two Challenger Universities present and peer-review case solutions while Observer Universities participate mainly in the moderated Q&A, all exposed to identical educational content.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A multidisciplinary expert panel develops and validates the clinical case, distributed one month in advance for self-directed preparation
- A faculty moderator with physiotherapy and research methodology expertise guides peer review and Q&A
- Institutionally available digital infrastructure (Webex Cisco® and GoogleForm®)
#### Constraints
- Delivered as a voluntary extracurricular activity outside scheduled curricular teaching hours
- Observer students do not contribute to initial case development, an asymmetry the authors note may reduce depth of experiential learning

### Target Learners
- Third-year physiotherapy students

### Learning Goals
- Clinical reasoning
- Clinical decision-making
- Evidence-based practice
- Communication and interprofessional collaboration
