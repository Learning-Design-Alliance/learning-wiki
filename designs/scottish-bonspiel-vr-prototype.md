# Scottish Bonspiel VR: a two-level VR game with an LLM-based cultural heritage ambassador chatbot

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A web-based VR game built with Vite and A-Frame, hosted on Vercel, using an Oculus Quest 2 headset, an OpenLLM API with the GPT-4 model, Whisper speech-to-text, and Azure text-to-speech. Level 1 simulates the Grand Match on a frozen lake; Level 2 moves indoors and includes a Q&A session with either the ChatGPT or predefined chatbot. The chatbot avatar is "Dressed in an outfit inspired by the grand match era" to avoid the uncanny valley effect and enhance historical authenticity. Players aim and shoot a curling stone with a single button, guided by visual cues and head-tracked aiming.

### Context
#### Requirements
- Oculus Quest 2 headset
- robust internet service for the LLM API and speech processing
#### Constraints
- open-ended feedback revealed technical issues such as voice not being recognized correctly and internet connection problems

### Target Learners
- adult students and general audiences interested in cultural heritage

### Learning Goals
- Scottish curling history
- climate change impact on traditional outdoor sports
- modern adaptations of intangible cultural heritage
