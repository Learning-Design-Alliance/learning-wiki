# Three-category classification of quantum computing fields: devices, algorithms, and software/system support

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article organizes quantum computing research into three major categories used to scope the lecture series: "developments of reliable quantum devices, improvements and theoretical analysis for quantum algorithms, and software and system support for quantum computers". The series deliberately concentrates on the third category, which the authors argue is underrepresented in existing education. The classification is used to make clear the topics of the lecture series and to categorize subdivisions of research directions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Used to scope and select topics for a lecture series aimed at the software and system level
#### Constraints
- The article applies the classification to quantum computing research directions, not to other domains

### Target Learners
- practitioners and researchers in quantum computing
- students new to the area
- quantum educators

### Learning Goals
- understanding the structure of the quantum computing research landscape
- identifying software and system level topics within quantum computing
