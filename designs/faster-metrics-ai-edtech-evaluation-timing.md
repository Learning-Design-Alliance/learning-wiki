# Build evaluation frameworks with faster metrics and role-specific data access to time contract decisions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article identifies a timing misalignment: districts must make contract renewal decisions before reliable outcome data arrive, since state test results typically arrive in mid- to late summer after procurement cycles conclude. It recommends that districts "establish comprehensive evaluation frameworks that provide role-specific data access and identify faster, more reliable metrics beyond annual assessments to enable timely decision-making."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Usage dashboards for data-driven decisions
- Adverse incident reporting systems
- Early quality indicators including keyword detection at the input and output layers
#### Constraints
- Annual state assessment data arrives too late for procurement cycles, so faster interim metrics are needed

### Target Learners
- District evaluation and technology staff

### Learning Goals
- Judging AI-enabled edtech effectiveness and informing contract renewal decisions
