# BOPPPS lesson pattern integrating standardized-patient role-play, group-rotated examination, and ECG interpretation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A lesson-level design pattern in which scenario simulation is embedded in the participatory-learning phase: the teacher acts as a standardized patient while one student takes the medical history, the observing group evaluates content, posture, language and doctor-patient communication, then roles rotate for cardiac physical examination and ECG operation, followed by whole-class ECG interpretation and teacher correction. The article reports that "the teacher acts as a standardized patient, and the student from Group A conducts the simulated medical history taking", with groups of 6–8 students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students divided into two rotating groups (6–8 per group)
- Clinical typical cases with targeted case-related questions
- ECG of the standardized patient displayed via slides for group discussion
#### Constraints
- Requires a male teacher or male student as examination subject in the reported implementation

### Target Learners
- undergraduate clinical medicine students in groups of 6–8

### Learning Goals
- medical history taking
- cardiac physical examination technique
- ECG operation and interpretation
- doctor-patient communication
