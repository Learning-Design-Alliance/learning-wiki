# TRIPP framework applied to sequence intervention development studies

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Translating Research into Injury Prevention Practice (TRIPP) framework is a six-stage linear process developed specifically to inform sport injury prevention research. The I-PROTECT project applies it to align its studies with stages: study 1 at stage 5 (understanding the implementation context), study 2 spanning stages 1–5 to produce the model at stage 3 (develop preventive measure), and study 3 at stage 6 (evaluate effectiveness).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Existing epidemiological and risk factor research allowing a project to begin at later stages
#### Constraints
- TRIPP describes a linear six-stage process; the project entered at stage 5 because stages 1–2 evidence already existed

### Target Learners
- researchers developing sports injury prevention interventions

### Learning Goals
- planning research that leads from evidence to implemented prevention practice
