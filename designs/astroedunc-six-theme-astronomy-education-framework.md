# Six-theme framework for examining the goals of introductory astronomy education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The AstroEdUNC meeting report organises community deliberations on the purpose, content, and delivery of astronomy education into six interrelated themes: "(1) Context", "(2) Content", "(3) Skills", "(4) Engagement", "(5) Beyond the Classroom", and "(6) Astronomy Education Research". Each theme was unpacked through participant-voted questions, bringing together perspectives, personal experiences, and resources, and the framework culminates in recommendations for future ASTRO101 development. It functions as a community-generated taxonomy for auditing and redesigning introductory astronomy courses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Convening a diverse community of astronomers, education researchers, and practitioners to vote on and discuss guiding questions per theme
#### Constraints
- Framed for the US context and introductory astronomy (ASTRO101), while noted as equally encompassing higher and lower level courses

### Target Learners
- introductory undergraduate astronomy (ASTRO101) students
- K-12 learners
- non-STEM majors

### Learning Goals
- scientific literacy
- 21st-century skills
- attitudes and self-efficacy toward science
