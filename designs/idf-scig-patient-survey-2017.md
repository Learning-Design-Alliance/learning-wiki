# Immune Deficiency Foundation (IDF) patient survey on SCIG training, infusion characteristics, and patient-reported outcomes

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Immune Deficiency Foundation conducted an online survey of US-based patients with primary immunodeficiency (or their parent/caregiver if under 18) regarding SCIG training and infusion experiences. Using the IDF patient database, 11,232 patients were contacted regarding an unincentivized online survey containing 74 questions, including the TSQM, PROMIS Fatigue SF7a/SF10, and GHP measures. In total, 35.3% (366/1037) of responders were included in the analyses, with adult patients accounting for 89% (n = 326) and parent/caregiver proxy responses for patients under 18 accounting for 11% (n = 40).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Respondents currently receiving SCIG treatment
- Complete and congruent survey responses
#### Constraints
- As a non-probability sample, generalization of results to the larger population should be made with caution
- Responses could not be independently verified with patients' physicians

### Target Learners
- US-based patients with primary immunodeficiency on SCIG treatment
- Parent/caregiver proxy respondents for pediatric patients

### Learning Goals
- Assessing patient-reported outcomes in relation to SCIG training and infusion characteristics
