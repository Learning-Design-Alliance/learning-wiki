# 11-week kindergarten phonological awareness intervention program

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article evaluates an 11-week intervention in "instruction in phonological awareness" provided in low-income, inner-city kindergarten classrooms by kindergarten teachers and their teaching assistants. The program was implemented with 84 treatment children alongside 75 comparison children, and produced significant post-test advantages on phoneme segmentation, letter name and letter sound knowledge, two of three reading measures, and invented spelling.
