# Self-contained asynchronous ultrasound-guided PIV training cart

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A self-contained learning cart deployed in each ICU that let nurses learn ultrasound-guided PIV placement independently. It combined a laptop holding a recorded 17 min 23 s didactic video, Reacts software recording practice on a CAE Blue Phantom simulation limb with paired webcam and ultrasound feeds, an Epiphan video capturer, and an ultrasound device. Recorded performances were reviewed asynchronously by a subject matter expert, who gave in-person formative feedback and judged competence via a global rating assessment covering probe selection, vessel identification, probe orientation, image optimization, and needle-tip tracking.
