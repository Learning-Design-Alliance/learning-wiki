## Description
Olive (Online Learning Institute of Virtual Education) is the virtual learning environment through which the CMC-ELT blended model was delivered at Allama Iqbal Open University. The article states it "has been designed in Moodle (Modular Object-Oriented Dynamic L earning Environment), an op en source" by the Department of Computer Sciences of the University.
