# Quantum Entrepreneurship Lab (QEL) course at TUM

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Quantum Entrepreneurship Lab is "a multi-disciplinary, one semester course at the Technical University of Munich (TUM) within the larger Munich Quantum Valley (MQV) ecosystem". Technical students work in teams on industry-relevant quantum research topics under academic supervision while business students study commercialization; midway, selected business students join technical teams to assess turning scientific concepts into viable business solutions. The course is supported by four institutional pillars: MQV, TUM, TUM Venture Lab Quantum/Semicon, and the student initiative PushQuantum e.V.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An institutional ecosystem providing funding, project ideas, coaches, and industry connections
- Application-based selection of motivated participants, limited to 5 teams of 5 (15 technical and 10 business students)
#### Constraints
- Securing enough coaches with the right expertise is difficult because qualified coaches have demanding schedules and the pool of specialized, industry-minded academics is limited

### Target Learners
- Master's-level technical and business students, typically in their 2nd semester

### Learning Goals
- Bridging academic research and industrial application in quantum technology
- Entrepreneurial and commercialization skills
