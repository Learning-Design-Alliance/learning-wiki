# Training programs should maintain structured teaching and assessment in both ultrasound-guided and landmark-based vascular access techniques

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The article concludes that anesthesia training programs should maintain structured teaching and assessment in both ultrasound-guided and landmark-based techniques to improve preparedness across varied clinical settings. The authors frame landmark skills as "a rescue competency when ultrasound is unavailable, not as a routine alternative" and suggest educators might consider teaching both techniques with structured simulation, exploring a minimum number of landmark-based procedures before or alongside ultrasound training, assessing competency separately for each technique, and including ultrasound-scarce scenarios in simulation.
