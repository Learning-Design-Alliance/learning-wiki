# Comprehensive simulation training combining literature review, instructor video, model preparation, and hands-on practice

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The experimental training protocol layered four components: reading related English literature over approximately 50 days, watching an instructor's video of suprapubic catheter insertion, personally preparing the simulation model, and practicing puncture and catheter insertion with it. The control group received the same protocol "except without hands-on practice using the model". This layered design produced significantly higher performance scores than video and literature alone.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a reusable, low-cost simulation model so every trainee can practice hands-on
- An instructor-recorded video of the procedure and related literature for pre-study
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only with pre-clinical fourth-year medical students who had no prior patient contact or procedural experience

### Target Learners
- fourth-year medical students
- surgical trainees and residents

### Learning Goals
- surgical procedure skills for suprapubic catheter insertion
- procedural understanding and confidence
