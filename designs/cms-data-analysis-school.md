# CMS Data Analysis School: an immersive workshop devoting 90% of time to hands-on analysis work with real published measurements

## Description
The CMS Data Analysis School (CMSDAS), first held in January 2011, is a workshop designed to help CMS physicists learn or deepen CMS analysis skills. Its innovative classes let students, in some cases with zero experience, get hands-on experience with real data physics measurements CMS published just days before and refine them by searching for new processes. "Over 60 students and 60 facilitators took part in the school", with 20% of participants from outside the US, and the school material is maintained as part of the CMS WorkBook.
