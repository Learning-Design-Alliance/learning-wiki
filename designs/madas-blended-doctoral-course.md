# MADAS: a blended doctoral course on meta-analysis of diagnostic accuracy studies delivered across Uganda, South Africa and Sweden

## Description
The Meta-analysis of Diagnostic Accuracy Studies (MADAS) course trained 19 doctoral and pre-doctoral students across three universities in January 2013, using synchronous web conferencing and chat (50% of the course), asynchronous email and Moodle, and geographically co-located local tutors. Students completed five modules, practical exercises with the open-source MetaDisc software, and an individual meta-analysis protocol assessed by multiple-choice exam and draft protocol. The self-directed materials are available through the ARCADE Open Course Repository.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Local facilitators at each remote site to support practical tasks such as literature searches, data extraction and meta-synthesis
- Moodle platform hosting lecture notes, slides, video-recorded lectures and access to MetaDisc
#### Constraints
- Delivered as an elective within the 2013 doctoral programmes of the participating universities
- The full-time first-week schedule limited time for self-directed learning activities

### Target Learners
- doctoral and pre-doctoral students in health sciences and clinical medicine

### Learning Goals
- developing a meta-analysis protocol
- conducting comprehensive literature searches
- managing and analysing data
- preparing a manuscript for publication
