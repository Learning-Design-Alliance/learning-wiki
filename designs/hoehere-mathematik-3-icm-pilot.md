# Higher Mathematics 3 inverted pilot: graphics-tablet recordings in eight 7–18-minute units delivered via unlisted YouTube links on ILIAS

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The pilot inverted the topic of differential equation systems (about three lecture hours) in Higher Mathematics 3, summer semester 2012, with roughly 20 third-semester students. Recordings were made by the lecturer with a graphics tablet combined with a video of the lecturer, using Camtasia Studio, MyPaint and MS OneNote, produced in HD quality. "Die Aufzeichnung des drei Vorlesungsstunden umfassenden Stoffes wurde in acht thematische Einheiten von 7-18 min Länge unterteilt" and provided via a YouTube direct link on the ILIAS learning platform, using the unlisted option so videos were reachable only via the exact link.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A 30-minute training for the lecturer in camera and software operation
- Good audio quality and legible media; freehand drawing without automatic conversion into Bézier curves
- ILIAS course area with keywords, exam-relevance notes, a question forum and an online survey on usage behaviour
#### Constraints
- Videos were not fully published during the test phase (unlisted only); publication for a broad audience is planned after a successful test phase

### Target Learners
- Third-semester engineering students in Higher Mathematics 3

### Learning Goals
- Mastering differential equation systems through application and practice in the in-person phase
