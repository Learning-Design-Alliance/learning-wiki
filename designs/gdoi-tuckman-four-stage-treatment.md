# GDOI treatment structured through Tuckman's four stages of group development

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The treatment followed Tuckman's (1965) four stages of group development: Forming, Storming, Norming, and Performing, which the article says "have a great impact and relevance for the study of the classroom context". Over a 45-day semester with three 90-minute sessions per week, both groups received discussion topics from Four Corners 4; the experimental group worked with as much pair and group interaction as possible while the control group worked mostly individually.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A thirty-day period of time lapse before the post-test, with the semester lasting forty five days and classes meeting three times weekly for 90 minutes
#### Constraints
- The control group had no or little interaction with peers, performing mostly individually

### Target Learners
- intermediate EFL learners aged 18-35 at a language institute

### Learning Goals
- L2 speaking ability
- willingness to communicate
