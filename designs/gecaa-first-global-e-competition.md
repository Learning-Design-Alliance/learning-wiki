# GeCAA: the first Global e-Competition on Astronomy and Astrophysics (2020)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
GeCAA was a fully online international astronomy and astrophysics competition held in September–October 2020 as a replacement for the pandemic-postponed IOAA, organized on eight weeks' notice with no participation fee. It comprised individual rounds in theory, data analysis, and night-sky observation, a two-week international team competition, and online lectures; "In total, 325 students were able to partake in the GeCAA experience."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Questions had to be significantly rephrased to be "answerable as either numerical values, multiple choice, or a single word" to maximise automated marking on the custom-made website.
#### Constraints
- GeCAA followed the IOAA financial model, "with no participation fee for the teams", and team leaders were asked to volunteer to help grade answer scripts.

### Target Learners
- senior secondary-school students competing internationally in astronomy and astrophysics

### Learning Goals
- theory of astronomy and astrophysics
- practical data analysis using real or simulated data
- practical night-sky observation
- collaborative team problem-solving
