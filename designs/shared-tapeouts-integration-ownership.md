# Shared tapeouts need a dedicated integration and tapeout coordination role with centralized ownership

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The article's project-organization lesson states that shared tapeouts need explicit integration ownership beyond subsystem contributors: maintaining the top-level design and repository, tracking verification and dependencies, managing the design freeze, and coordinating the MPW submission demand centralized ownership. The experience highlighted "a dedicated integration and tapeout coordination role", which the article states closely resembles system-level integration in industrial SoC projects and which it found best assigned to an experienced doctoral researcher, postdoctoral researcher, or staff member.
