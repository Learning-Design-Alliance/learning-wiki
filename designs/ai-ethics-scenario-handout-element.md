# Student-facing handout with AI-use scenarios and reflection prompts

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A supplemental student-facing handout that guides students through each stage of the activity, including representative scenarios and reflection prompts. The scenario set presents 10–12 brief scenarios depicting varied uses of generative AI in a physics course, spanning "a range of practices, from ethically commendable uses that support conceptual understanding to clear violations of academic integrity." Sample scenarios and printable classroom materials are available from the author.
