# The Collaborative Project-Based Teaching Model for culturally relevant physics learning through Gudeg

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A five-phase instructional model that uses the Gudeg-making process as the context for physics learning. The phases run from "initialization and contextualization" through exploration and experimentation, analysis and conceptualization, collaborative presentation, and reflection and evaluation. It assigns a "triple learning roles between teachers, students, and Gudeg producers" — the teacher as technical director, the student as consultant learner, and the practitioner as subject matter expert validating that findings match traditional standards. The model rests on Vygotskian socio-cultural theory and treats Gudeg producers as "definitive stakeholders" linked to educational output.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Community practitioners must participate as subject matter experts to validate student findings against traditional standards
- Schools must function within a larger socio-economic network rather than as isolated institutions
#### Constraints
- The model was designed for Indonesian high school physics (Grades 10-12) using Gudeg as the learning context

### Target Learners
- high school physics students (Grades 10-12, Indonesian national curriculum)

### Learning Goals
- physics concepts including density, elasticity, torque, thermodynamics, and heat transfer
- culturally relevant understanding of local heritage
- scientific inquiry and data interpretation skills
