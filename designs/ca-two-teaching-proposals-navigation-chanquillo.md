# Two CA-based teaching proposals: historical oceanic navigation without advanced instruments, and the Chanquillo monumental horizon calendar

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes two teaching proposals designed with the didactic potential of Cultural Astronomy in mind, both intended for secondary Natural Sciences and adaptable to teacher-training institutes. One addresses space and time concepts using historical oceanic navigation without advanced instruments, drawing on ethnoastronomical studies. The other uses an archaeoastronomical approach with the Chanquillo monumental horizon calendar so that students can identify local horizon markers and build their own calendars. Both are detailed in a companion paper (Karaseur et al., BAAA 63, 2021).
