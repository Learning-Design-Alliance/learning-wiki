# Searching for gravitational waves in noisy data activity

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A classroom activity casting students as LIGO data analysts, introducing compact binary coalescence sources and the matched filtering methods used to seek signals in detector noise. The article states students "learn the concept of signal and noise in scientific measurements, data filtering to determine the likelihood of detecting a signal and how LIGO scientists would use these methods to search for gravitational waves."
