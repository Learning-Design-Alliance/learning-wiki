# Scoping review framework mapping nurses' diabetes knowledge, care knowledge, and knowledge-acquisition factors in SSA hospitals

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (2 review), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review organises the literature around three review questions: nurses' knowledge of diabetes, nurses' knowledge of diabetes management or care, and factors hindering or promoting knowledge acquisition. It applied the Arksey and O'Malley scoping methodological framework, which "included the following steps: identifying the research question; searching for relevant studies; selecting studies; charting data; and collating, summarising and reporting the results." Primary outcomes covered knowledge of diabetes types, pathophysiology, diagnostics, medications and management; secondary outcomes covered personal, organisational and support-system barriers and facilitators.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Studies addressing nurses involved in care for adults with diabetes in hospital settings in SSA, published in English from 2000 to 2024.
#### Constraints
- The protocol was not pre-registered; studies were not formally assessed for quality; only English-language studies were included, possibly missing Francophone Africa.

### Target Learners
- Nurses in hospital settings in sub-Saharan Africa

### Learning Goals
- Diabetes knowledge for care and patient education
