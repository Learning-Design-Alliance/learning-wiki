# Universal 3D-printed puncture template with adjustable navigation frame plus BTPS imaging software as a digital puncture-training apparatus

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The training apparatus combines two digital components. First, "universally applicable coplanar templates capable of mass production for puncture practices" with guiding holes spaced at 2.5 mm intervals, mounted on a multi-joint navigation frame adjustable to any angle, guiding the needle toward the imaging target. Second, the BTPS planning system developed by Beihang University provides multimodal image fusion, three-dimensional reconstruction, needle-track planning, and quantitative measurements such as needle depth, angle, and proximity to vital structures. Group 2 trained with both; Group 3 added ChatGPT.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires the template affixed to a versatile navigation frame with articulating joints for angle adjustment, and access to the BTPS planning system with CT/MRI data
#### Constraints
- The templates are standardized tools, not individualized 3D-printed models of specific patients

### Target Learners
- Refresher doctors training in CT-guided puncture procedures

### Learning Goals
- Accurate needle trajectory alignment and mastery of CT-guided puncture techniques
