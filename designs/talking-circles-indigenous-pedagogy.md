# Talking circles: Indigenous pedagogy centring relational listening and reflective witnessing as the foundation for structured sharing in STEM

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article grounds its format in the tradition of talking circles, "a foundational approach to Indigenous pedagogy that centers relational listening, reflective witnessing, and the co-creation of understanding." Learning Together adapts this tradition for STEM communities, applying the logic of a land acknowledgement: local history and current events are relevant to any physics community situated in that place.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- facilitators familiar with the format and committed to the process, though not experts in the session topic
#### Constraints
- The format was drawn from the talking-circles tradition as cited by the authors; the article cites Barkaskas and Gladwin and related sources rather than providing an evaluation of the underlying pedagogy

### Target Learners
- physics and STEM community members learning about local histories and diverse worldviews

### Learning Goals
- relational listening, reflective witnessing, and co-creation of understanding around historical and equity topics
