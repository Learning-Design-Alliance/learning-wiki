# Graasp ILS 'Ecuaciones Diferenciales': a six-scenario inquiry learning space on the simple pendulum

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A free-access virtual inquiry learning space (ILS) built in Graasp for teaching homogeneous second-order differential equations through the simple pendulum, hosted at https://graasp.eu/s/sjcuja. The article states it "posee seis escenarios de aprendizaje y dos criterios de acceso a datos y escenarios de control para estudiante y profesor", organized per Table 1 into orientation (introduction, pretest), conceptualization (content), investigation (learning activity), conclusion (postest) and discussion (conclusions) phases, with a Colorado Boulder pendulum simulator embedded in the content.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Inquiry-oriented implementation in which the teacher guides students through synchronous sessions (web conferences, tutorials and CIPAS) toward the learning objectives
#### Constraints
- Applied specifically in Unit 2, topic 'Ecuaciones Diferenciales Homogéneas de Segundo Orden', as part of a four-week task worth 40 of 100 points

### Target Learners
- fourth-semester UNAD engineering students (industrial, systems, telecommunications, electronics, food, environmental, agroforestry) who have passed differential and integral calculus

### Learning Goals
- solve problem situations involving higher-order differential equations using different mathematical methods
- introduce students to second-order differential equations through the simple pendulum physical model
