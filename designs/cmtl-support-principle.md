## Description
The article argues the model supports higher-order thinking completely because each phase contributes a distinct support: varied contextual problems, analytical and critical questioning, individual and group work, public presentation, and reflection plus testing. It states the model "provides support for the development of higher-order thinking ability completely", with each component mutually supporting the others toward the intended purpose.
