# TNG hands-on observing campaign within the Complementi di Astronomia course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A multi-year component of the Complementi di Astronomia course at the University of Firenze in which students select targets, observe at the 3.58 m Telescopio Nazionale Galileo, and reduce and analyse their own imaging and spectroscopic data of nearby spiral galaxies (z<0.2) using the DOLORES instrument. Students work in "groups of maximum 3 people" and are "required to do most of the steps themselves" at the telescope, including inputting command lines, checking the source in the field-of-view and selecting filters or slits. The data reduction, done in Matlab or Python, represents "the most intensive part of the entire course, namely about 20 hours".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- External funding (a private donation) and direct collaboration with the TNG staff, who reserve a few observing hours each year
- A support astronomer and teachers present to guide students through the observing steps
#### Constraints
- The overall budget required in 2019 was roughly 15,000 euros for 15 participants, which the authors call unaffordable for a typical Italian university study program
- Observing on-site was prohibited during the pandemic, forcing remote observing

### Target Learners
- Bachelor and master students in physics and astronomy

### Learning Goals
- Experimental astronomy techniques: imaging and low-resolution spectroscopy, data reduction and analysis, target selection and telescope operations
