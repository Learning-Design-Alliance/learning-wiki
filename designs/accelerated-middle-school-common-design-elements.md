# Accelerated middle schools share a common design: thematic cross-subject linking, experiential instruction, smaller classes, and added academic and social supports

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Despite varying somewhat in instructional approach, the programs share several common design elements: "Classes are often linked thematically across multiple subjects"; instruction is "more experiential and 'hands on' than is typical in a traditional middle school"; classes are generally smaller than in traditional middle schools; and programs provide additional supports such as tutoring, attendance monitoring, counseling, and family outreach. This pattern describes the program-level configuration the three studied schools shared.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Additional instructional time in core academic subjects, obtained by offering relatively few electives
#### Constraints
- Programs vary somewhat in their approach to instruction; the pattern describes the three studied schools rather than all accelerated middle schools

### Target Learners
- middle school students behind grade level

### Learning Goals
- accelerated coverage of core academic curriculum
- dropout prevention
