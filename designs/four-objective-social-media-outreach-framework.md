# Four-objective framework for research-group social media outreach: outreach, science communication, training, and recruitment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article organizes its initiative around four stated objectives. Outreach aims to spark curiosity and "create a space for authentic engagement with our research"; science communication shares incremental everyday results, not only once-in-a-lifetime discoveries, and leverages evidence that scientists' social media activity increases public trust in science; training builds communication skills; recruitment targets students and graduates at career decision points. The conclusion names these as "four primary objectives".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A platform aligned with the target demographic (Instagram chosen because the target audience is most active there), plus content that combines explanatory videos, personal lab-life material, and showcases of individual scientists
#### Constraints
- Institutional accounts such as those of CERN, DESY or the CMS collaboration focus on press-related content and do not highlight individual groups or members, which the group-level account is designed to fill

### Target Learners
- general public aged 18-34
- prospective interns, thesis students, PhD candidates and postdocs

### Learning Goals
- public engagement with particle physics
- trust in science
- career-boundary awareness of research careers

- [Co-organizing outreach developed the PhD student's proposal writing, media, and social-media communication skills](../claims/outreach-personal-skill-development-phd-student.md) [+W]
