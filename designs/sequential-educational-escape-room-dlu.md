# Sequential educational escape room structure for a digital learning unit

## Description
The unit adopts the escape room model, relying on the EscapED theoretical framework and Nicholson's puzzle-organization typology. The authors state: "We applied the sequential structure, where players must solve the puzzles one at a time, and the final puzzle allows them to win the game." In D-UDA, solving classroom riddles yields a code linking to Scratch games, whose completion yields puzzle pieces composing a feather that unlocks the final Domus escape room. A narrative character, a cycling-traveler archaeologist, links the enigmas.
