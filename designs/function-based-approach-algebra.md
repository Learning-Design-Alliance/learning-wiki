# Function-based approach to teaching algebra

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A reform approach that organizes introductory algebra concepts and skills around functions, their representations, and operations on functions, rather than around symbolic manipulation. The function is "the central concept around which algebra curriculum is meaningfully organized", treated as an underlying theme throughout a course rather than a single chapter, typically explored with graphing calculators linking symbolic, tabular and graphical representations situated in real-world contexts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A tool such as a graphing calculator for students to explore different representations of functions
#### Constraints
- The article cautions the approach does not offer a simpler path for understanding the complexities inherent in algebra than traditional instruction, but offers more opportunities for inquiry-based learning

### Target Learners
- lower secondary / high school algebra students

### Learning Goals
- conceptual understanding of functions, equivalence, and multiple representations of algebraic relationships
