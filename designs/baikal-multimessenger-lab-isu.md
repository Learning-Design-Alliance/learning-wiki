## Description
A laboratory established at Irkutsk State University to support the TAIGA gamma observatory and Baikal-GVD neutrino telescope. Its declared missions are creating a common framework for Baikal-region experiments, integrating the setups into full-stack multi-messenger astronomy, and "Creation of a competitive school for astroparticle physics at ISU". The astroparticle.online resource serves as its education and outreach instrument for attracting and training students.
