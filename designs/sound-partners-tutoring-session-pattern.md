# Short frequent one-to-one tutoring sessions with scripted phonics lessons and mastery checks

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The program's instructional pattern is "30 minute sessions of one-to-one tutoring that take place four days per week throughout one school year." Each session includes from four to eight short activities, with the last 15 minutes allocated for oral reading practice in decodable texts matched to taught phonics skills. Although scripted, instruction can be adjusted to individual student needs, and tests given every 10 lessons gauge student mastery.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tutors trained to choose a reading method (independent reading, partner reading, or echo reading) matching each student's reading skills
#### Constraints
- Tutoring is supplemental; treatment students in the reviewed studies also received typical classroom reading instruction

### Target Learners
- Kindergarten and first-grade students at risk for reading difficulties

### Learning Goals
- Applying word-level phonics skills in oral text reading
- Reading fluency and accuracy
