# Teaching orbital motion through centre of mass, motion equations and reference-frame change with numerical simulation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article introduces a teaching framework built on three concepts: "CM of a system, trajectory of bodies in the CM referential, and absolute trajectory of the same bodies". Bodies' motions are computed from Newton's second law with gravitational forces and solved numerically with the Euler method, then transformed between the CM frame and an inertial frame via simple translation equations. The approach is developed from work with students at the 11th Summer School of Physics of University of Porto for high secondary level students, using solar-system examples with real ephemerides.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Introduces the centre of mass definition, Newton's second law and gravitational force equations before the referential discussion
#### Constraints
- No rotation is introduced in the frame transformation; the change of reference frame is a simple translation of the inertial reference frame

### Target Learners
- high secondary level students

### Learning Goals
- understanding centre of mass of a system
- understanding trajectories in different reference frames
- understanding the Geocentric versus Heliocentric models
