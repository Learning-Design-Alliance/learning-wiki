# Video production as a route to Ausubelian meaningful learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article frames student video production as "a means to acquiring meaningful learning in the sense of Ausubel's theory", in which new information must interact with prior information in the student's cognitive structure. Producing a video on a physics theme forces students to access their cognitive resources frequently, implying a ressignification of concepts and reorganisation of the cognitive structure, especially when working collaboratively. The activity is also described as metacognitive, since students plan and replan actions and try different language forms. Mechanical learning is easier for the teacher to detect at three points: production, the teacher's analysis, and the class discussion.
