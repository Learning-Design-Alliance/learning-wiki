# LPQ lecture practice: pre-study videos with unanswered quizzes plus 2-3 in-lecture polls every 15-20 minutes with live explanations

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
This lesson-level practice sends students pre-recorded videos and slides with quizzes (without answers) before each lecture for pre-study, then, as the article reports, "During the lectures, 2-3 LPQs were conducted every 15-20 minutes followed by live explanations and discussions of the answers". The LPQ questions are fact-based and formally defined with clear answers, rather than open-ended or opinion-based.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A web-based live polling tool and devices for students to respond during lectures
- Pre-recorded videos and slides with the quizzes distributed before each lecture for pre-study
#### Constraints
- Only one polling software (itempool.com) was studied
- Questions were fact-based with clear answers rather than open-ended or opinion-based

### Target Learners
- final-year undergraduate Computer Science students

### Learning Goals
- checking understanding of factual lecture content
- real-time feedback to adjust teaching
- student engagement during lectures
