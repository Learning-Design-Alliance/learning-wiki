# Boltzmann Factor guided-inquiry tutorial

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A guided-inquiry tutorial activity for undergraduate statistical mechanics courses in which students, working in small groups, derive the Boltzmann factor from reservoir multiplicity. The article states it was designed "to lead students through its derivation and encourage deep cognitive con- nections between the physical quantities involved." Sections move from an isolated ideal gas container, to a divided system-reservoir scenario, to a single-particle toy model with discrete energy levels, to the Taylor-series derivation and partition function normalization.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- One 50-minute class period at School 1, plus an additional 20 minutes at School 2; small groups with explicit instructions to discuss responses and reasoning with group mates; instructor and facilitator available.
#### Constraints
- The article reports modifications were needed so students could complete the tutorial during the allowed class time without sacrificing measured effectiveness.

### Target Learners
- upper-division undergraduate physics students in statistical mechanics or thermal physics courses

### Learning Goals
- recognizing when the Boltzmann factor is applicable
- articulating the physical significance of the Boltzmann factor as an expression for multiplicity
- deriving and applying the canonical probability distribution
