# Three-part molecular phylogeny exercise with student handout and instructor guide

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A set of three inquiry activities using real datasets from Homo sapiens and related primates: (1) aligned GULO pseudogene DNA sequences from four species, (2) aligned SCML1 amino acid sequences from five species, and (3) cytogenetic ideograms of chromosomes from four species. The complete student handout is provided as Supplemental Material 1 and the complete instructor guide as Supplemental Material 2. The article reports the exercises "require very little prior student exposure to these topics" and suit a wide range of educational levels.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- One 2.5-h laboratory session in the authors' implementation
- Students working in pairs (normal laboratory groups)
- Optional Internet connection for BLAST searches during the postactivity discussion
#### Constraints
- Species identities are withheld until all activities are complete in the authors' implementation, though the article states this aspect is by no means required

### Target Learners
- introductory biology students at postsecondary and secondary levels

### Learning Goals
- molecular phylogenetics
- comparative genomics
- evolutionary relationships of humans and primates
