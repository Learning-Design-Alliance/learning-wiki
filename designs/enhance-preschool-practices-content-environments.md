# Enhance preschool teacher practices, instructional content, and classroom environments to prepare children for school

## Description
The Early Reading First program embodies the principle that improving preschool quality on three fronts — teacher practices, instructional content, and classroom environments — helps children start school ready. Grants were "designed to enhance teacher practices, instructional content, and classroom environments in preschools to ensure that young children... start school with the skills needed for academic success."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Funding (grants) to preschools to support changes across teacher practices, instructional content, and classroom environments.
#### Constraints
- Evaluated in preschool settings serving young children, especially those from low-income families.

### Target Learners
