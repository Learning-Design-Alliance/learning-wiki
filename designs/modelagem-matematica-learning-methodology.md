# Mathematical modeling as a learning methodology: representing the real through a reality–reflection–action cycle

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article treats mathematical modeling as building and working on models that represent the real: "Esta forma de modelagem consiste na representação do real." Following D'Ambrosio, modeling proceeds through the dynamics of reality–reflection–planned action, so students analyze the reality in which they are immersed while constructing statistical and mathematical knowledge. The thesis uses this modeling as its differentiated methodological proposal for teaching basic Statistics to undergraduates, with everyday problems, software and model creation and simulation.
