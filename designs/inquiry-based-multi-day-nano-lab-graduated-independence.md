# Inquiry-based multi-day laboratory structured around daily scientific questions with graduated student independence

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The lab is designed to support inquiry-based learning by having students discover answers to a series of scientific questions each day while expanding technical capabilities, with support fading across the three days. Initially students are closely monitored while executing new techniques (objectives 1-3); after synthesis they prepare samples per the Student Manual (objectives 4-5); and finally "Students performed all data analysis for subsequent discussion" (objectives 6-7). This graduated release of responsibility pairs hands-on execution with diagnostic and formative assessment throughout.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Seven enumerated learning objectives spanning stoichiometry, chemistry skills, air-sensitive techniques, sample preparation, characterization, data analysis, and communication
- A laboratory assistant available for monitoring and questioning at each step
#### Constraints
- Because the lab introduces new techniques, close monitoring by a laboratory assistant is retained at each step rather than full independent work

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate students from first-year through fourth-year pursuing a range of degree programs

### Learning Goals
- Inquiry-based scientific discovery
- Technical skill development in synthesis and characterization
- Data analysis and scientific communication
