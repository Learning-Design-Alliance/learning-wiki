# SDMA cultivation program: two-week lecture plus Three Talk video-based EOL simulation intervention

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention evaluated in the study was a two-week SDM attitude cultivation program for nurses, comprising weekly sessions of three hours of SDM basic-concept lectures and three hours of video-based simulation teaching. Its video simulations referenced Elwyn et al.'s Three Talk model and were produced in "appropriate" and "inappropriate" versions covering three EOL scenarios: choice talk about receiving or not receiving intensive care, option talk covering pros, cons and patient preferences, and decision talk following the decision. Faculty members with more than five years of EOL clinical experience led all phases.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Videos in paired appropriate/inappropriate versions per scenario; faculty registered nurses with over five years' EOL experience
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only as an add-on to normal practice, with participants at a single hospital where SDM is an accredited skill with on-service training

### Target Learners
- clinical nurses at a medical center in Taiwan

### Learning Goals
- attitudes toward shared decision-making in end-of-life care
- SDM communication with patients and families
