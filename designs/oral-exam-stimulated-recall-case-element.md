# Oral exam video episode with stimulated recall interview as a data source for studying instructor noticing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study draws on video data from tutorial sections and oral exams in an upper-division electromagnetism course, focusing on a 4-minute period of a 37-minute oral exam. The authors state they chose the oral exam because "it has frequent and sustained instructor interaction", and they coded a selected interaction using an iteratively refined codebook, supplementing video with a stimulated recall interview.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Videos were screened for interactions and coded with an iteratively refined codebook developed by two of the authors.
#### Constraints
- Oral exams present the simplest instructor-student system (one instructor, one student, concentrated interaction) that is not present in other classroom interactions.

### Target Learners
- upper-division undergraduate physics students

### Learning Goals
- solving a whiteboard problem on the electric field of an infinite uniformly charged sheet
