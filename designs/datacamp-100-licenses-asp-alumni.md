# DataCamp donated 100 one-year licenses for online data analysis and coding education for ASP alumni

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The online education platform for data analysis, DataCamp, donated one hundred licenses to ASP alumni for one year, starting in March 2021. According to the report, "All the licenses are used to gain valuable experience in data analysis" and coding. The donation was arranged by Dr. David Bertsche, who also managed the ASP subscriptions and exercises. Student feedback on the licenses is referenced in the report's cited program materials.
