# Live-streaming-based remote classroom (LSRC) as a digital-physical hybrid education paradigm

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
LSRC is a digital-physical hybrid education paradigm that combines elements of both digital and physical learning modalities. It uses live-streaming to deliver real-time teaching videos and materials from an urban well-resourced classroom to rural physical classrooms, following a 'Four S' principle: preparing lessons Synchronously, lecturing Synchronously, with Same education material and Same homework. The article examines how this paradigm works in practice, its challenges, and its impacts on the rural education ecosystem.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Local teachers to provide on-site assistance
- Weekly online meetings between remote and local teams for lesson preparation
- ICT infrastructure connecting urban and rural classrooms
#### Constraints
- Interactions between remote teachers and local students are limited to one-way communication
- The one-to-hundreds model makes differentiated adjustment impractical

### Target Learners
- rural K-12 students in remote and under-served areas

### Learning Goals
- access to high-quality educational content from prestigious urban schools
