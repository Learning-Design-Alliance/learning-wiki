# AI-Enhanced Astronomy Lab Manual with three modality components

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A comprehensive methodological guide for the pilot laboratory, comprising an introduction to AI in astronomy, lab objectives, and three interconnected components: evaluating GenAI text platforms, creating AI-generated astronomical imagery, and visualizing dynamic astronomical phenomena via GenAI video tools. The structure "may take the form of a single, integrated assignment with three interconnected components —text, image, and video —or as three discrete tasks, each dedicated to one modality." It includes detailed instructions, links to digital forms for documenting results, and recommended platforms for each modality.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Computer with Internet access
- Lab report template
- Access to multiple GenAI platforms across text, image, and video modalities
#### Constraints
- Two academic hours per session were insufficient for all students to complete all three components

### Target Learners
- undergraduate non-science majors in general astronomy

### Learning Goals
- evaluating GenAI capabilities
- critical analysis of AI outputs
- science communication through multimedia storytelling
- prompt engineering
