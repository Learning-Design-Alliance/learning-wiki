# Online course catalog as a research instrument and scalable intervention medium

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
An online course catalog is a campus-wide platform storing official course information, used here both as a data source and as an intervention delivery medium. The article notes it "can incorporate randomized experiments to identify causal relationships during academic decision making with high statistical power and at a low cost", and that search queries' open-ended nature provides "an authentic representation of the areas that students are interested in". The platform collected 7,500 search queries from over 4,000 participants during the study year.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- University-wide access and log data merged with registrar demographics via hashed student IDs
#### Constraints
- Students consider less than 2% of university courses listed on an online course catalog, per cited prior work
- The topic model is limited in capturing the full range of topics due to its reliance on term co-occurrence

### Target Learners
- undergraduate, graduate, and professional students at a large university

### Learning Goals
- course exploration and interest-driven course selection
