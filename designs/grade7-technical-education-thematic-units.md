# Thematic-unit structure of 7th-grade Technical Education curriculum

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article enumerates the thematic units making up the 7th-grade Technical Education course: "Procedures and tools for processing technical materials; Means of mechanization; Components of residential installation; Wiring works. Electronic components of automation and regulation; Individual work of the pupils." Each unit is analysed with its knowledge and skill requirements, and the final unit has pupils independently draw a construction, select material, follow a technological procedure and choose a surfacing, developing self-reliance and creativity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher supplements the curriculum continuously with new information on the basis of science and technology development
#### Constraints
- As the situation of a respective school is taken into account, only objects made of materials accessible to the teacher at that school may be produced

### Target Learners
- 7th-grade elementary school pupils

### Learning Goals
- Technical knowledge, skills and habits; development of mental and physical capabilities; key competencies for work and extra-work life situations
