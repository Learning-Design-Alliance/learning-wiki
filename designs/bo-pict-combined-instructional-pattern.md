# Combining behavioural objectives with peer instruction and concept tests (BO-PICT)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
BO-PICT integrates Behavioural Objective-Based instruction with Peer Instruction enriched with Concept Tests into a single instructional sequence. The article reports that "E3 that used BO-PICT was superior to the rest of the experimental groups", achieving the highest post-test mean (66.4) and greatest gain (35.3). The pattern combines explicit behavioural objectives with interactive peer discussion and concept-based testing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must be able to state behavioural objectives in lesson notes and facilitate peer instruction with concept tests.
#### Constraints
- Tested only on compound proportion and rates of work in form three secondary mathematics over three weeks.

### Target Learners
- form three secondary school students

### Learning Goals
- achievement in compound proportion and rates of work
