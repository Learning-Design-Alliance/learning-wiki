# OUSD open-access dual enrollment support model with the Point Person role

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Oakland Unified School District's support model for open-access dual enrollment centers on the Point Person: "a designated K–12 staff member in each dual enrollment classroom whose explicit role is to stay in proactive contact with dual enrollment students throughout the semester, track progress in coordination with both high school and college instructors, and reach out before small difficulties become insurmountable ones." It is paired with school-day academic support, navigation assistance, and professional development, and the program reports an average pass rate of 83 percent, reaching 90 to 91 percent in recent semesters.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

### Target Learners
- High school students across a wide range of academic backgrounds, including many from low-income and first-generation backgrounds

### Learning Goals
- Passing dual enrollment courses and navigating college systems and expectations
