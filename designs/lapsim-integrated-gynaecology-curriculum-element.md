# LapSim VRS-integrated laparoscopic gynaecology curriculum with box trainers, workshops and graded modules

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A structured laparoscopic training curriculum piloted at an Australian tertiary teaching hospital, combining didactic teaching on anatomy and safe laparoscopy, two three-hour workshops, and 24-hour access to box trainers and the LapSim VRS. The VRS was "programmed with four modules, each with multiple exercises of graded complexity", covering basic skills, a gamified Precision & Speed exercise, and procedure simulation including tubal occlusion, salpingectomy and myoma suturing, with in-built metrics marking each exercise.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trainees asked to dedicate at least 30 min/week to simulation training; investigators available for supervision and electronic login verification
#### Constraints
- Training was not mandated but encouraged; uptake was poor, with median logged VRS time of 7.9 hours (novice) and 6.0 hours (experienced) over six months

### Target Learners
- Gynaecology trainees at novice and experienced levels

### Learning Goals
- Laparoscopic psychomotor skills including camera navigation, dissection, suturing and salpingectomy procedure steps
