# Conceptual model of inquiry-based instruction as interacting student, teacher, and social-cultural components

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's Figure 1 model represents inquiry-based instruction as a set of interactions: significant student-to-student interaction in which learners draw on each other's experiences, student-to-teacher contact in which the teacher acts as facilitator, and teacher preparation, skill, and knowledge. As the article states, "The model represents the interactions taking place in an inquiry–based classroom." The model guided the study's measurement of the inquiry method's effectiveness for knowledge-based achievement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must facilitate learning, have a strong foundation, and know where to guide students to find required information, though they need not know all potentially correct answers

- The model was used to guide one quasi-experimental study in secondary agriscience classrooms
