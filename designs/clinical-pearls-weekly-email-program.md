# Clinical Pearls weekly email program

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Clinical Pearls are weekly key learning points compiled from the residency's major didactic sessions and emailed to all housestaff. The document is drafted by a resident on a teaching elective, is "typically one to two pages in length, in bullet-point format, and organized by day of week and didactic session". All residents receive the emails regardless of rotation; a pre-study survey found only 22% read them weekly.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a motivated and experienced resident, such as one on a teaching elective, to compile Pearls and organize quizzes
#### Constraints
- Low readership: 22% of residents read the emails weekly and more than 60% never referred back to old emails

### Target Learners
- internal medicine housestaff

### Learning Goals
- reinforcement of key clinical concepts from weekly lectures and conferences
