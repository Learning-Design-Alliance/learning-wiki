# Share-and-credit economic system linking teacher income and student budgets to learning outcomes

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes an economic element in which teachers own shares that cannot be bought but can be earned, bringing dividends, while students own credits earned by passing standard tests, exams and publishing reports. Students buy passes for courses from individual budgets, and school productivity is measured by student test results and sales of team reports. The author states "the standard test results of students need to be accounted for in money" through tables of equivalence between credits and money.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A sophisticated system of collecting payments, share accounts and copyrights with simple, published rules; own accountants who are also teachers
#### Constraints
- In the current situation the full financing scheme is not directly implementable since taxes are paid according to schedules unrelated to how the money will be spent

### Target Learners
- students managing their own education budgets
- teachers earning shares

### Learning Goals
- learning the cost of education and how to avoid waste
- earning credits by demonstrated understanding
