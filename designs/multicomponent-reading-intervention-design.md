# Multi-component reading intervention design combining phonemic decoding, fluency training and comprehension strategies

## Description
The RAFT program was based on three main elements: phonemic awareness/decoding, reading comprehension strategies, and fluency training, integrated in line with the National Reading Panel's recommendation that these aspects be integrated into a complete reading programme. Instruction time was split roughly 60% phonemic decoding and awareness and 40% comprehension strategies and fluency. Comprehension training used teacher-read texts with explicit question types and reflection before, during and after reading.
