# Textbook Termodinamica e teoria microscopica: a five-chapter Italian high-school thermodynamics text with microscopic-first structure

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The educational path is published as an Italian textbook entitled Thermodynamics and Microscopic Theory, intended for high school students, though the authors believe a similar path can be adapted for undergraduate physics courses. After an introduction it contains five chapters, from thermodynamics and kinetic theory through entropy and the second law, ending with a section on past, future and the entropy of the Universe.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Exercises are proposed at the end of each chapter, mainly aiming at testing the level of understanding reached by the students, with ideas and suggestions for their resolution provided at the end of the volume.
#### Constraints
- The textbook is published in Italian.

### Target Learners
- high school students

### Learning Goals
- understanding thermodynamics from a microscopic perspective
