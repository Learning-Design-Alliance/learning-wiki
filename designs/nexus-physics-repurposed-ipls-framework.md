# NEXUS/Physics: a repurposed second-year introductory physics curriculum supporting life science students' interdisciplinary scientific competencies

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
NEXUS/Physics is a redesign of introductory physics for life science majors in which the course "is envisioned as a second year class for life science majors" taken after biology, chemistry, and calculus prerequisites. Its stated primary goals are "to provide support for biology majors for difficult physics concepts that they will encounter in biology and chemistry classes" and "to help students build a sense of relevance." The framework repositions physics to articulate with concurrent and later biology and chemistry courses rather than to prepare students for further physics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prerequisites of two semesters of biology, one semester of general chemistry, and two semesters of calculus or math for biology majors
#### Constraints
- Content is narrowed to topics all biologists study, since the course cannot cover every potential application for sub-populations

### Target Learners
- life science majors
- pre-medical students

### Learning Goals
- interdisciplinary scientific competencies
- scientific reasoning skills
- perceiving the value of physics for biology and chemistry
