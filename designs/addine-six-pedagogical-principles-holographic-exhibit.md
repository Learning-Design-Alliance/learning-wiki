# Six pedagogical principles (after Addine) as the pedagogical foundation for designing the Holographic Exhibit

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article adopts Addine's classification of pedagogical principles, oriented to directing the pedagogical process, as the theoretical basis for the exhibit. The principles include "el carácter científico del proceso pedagógico", the linking of education with life and social context, the unity of instructive, educative and developmental aspects, the unity of the affective and the cognitive, the collective and individual character of education, and the unity of activity, communication and personality. Each principle is mapped onto a design feature of the exhibit, such as interactive demonstrations supporting affective-cognitive unity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The activity must be structured taking into account continuous advances in science and technology, per the principle of the scientific character of the pedagogical process
#### Constraints
- The framework is applied to a university extension activity for high-school students, not to formal classroom instruction

### Target Learners
- high-school (enseñanza media) students

### Learning Goals
- motivation for physics and optics
- understanding of image formation and holography
- group-work skills
