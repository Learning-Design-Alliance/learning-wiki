# Force-Sensing Table Technology (FSTT®) laboratory for teaching and assessing SMT force–time characteristics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The FSTT® is a force-sensing table used with a human analogue mannikin to teach and assess spinal manipulative therapy. The article states "The force-sensing devices provide students with quantifiable measures of their SMT performance by giving visual feedback on the biomechanical aspects of their SMT", including preload forces, peak forces and time to peak force, and that the devices reduce students' variability when delivering SMT. Outcomes were automatically analysed by standardized algorithms, and the FSTT® has demonstrated excellent reliability in measuring SMT force–time characteristics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the FSTT® laboratory; in this study students averaged 58 hours of lab attendance over the prior academic year
#### Constraints
- The article notes students are typically accustomed to real-time visual feedback during training, which is not readily available in real-world practice

### Target Learners
- Chiropractic students learning spinal manipulative therapy

### Learning Goals
- Accurate modulation of SMT preload, peak thrust force and time to peak force
