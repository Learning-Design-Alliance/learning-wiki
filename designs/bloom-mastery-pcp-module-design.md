# Six-module Bloom's taxonomy sequence with Mastery Learning formative-assessment retakes at an 80% threshold

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A six-module instructional sequence organized by the Revised Bloom's taxonomy (Remember, Understand, Apply, Analyze, Evaluate, Create), each module ending in a formative assessment (FA) and a summative assessment (SA). In the Mastery Learning variant, students who "score less than 80% on the FA" "retake the FA after reviewing the instructional material" before continuing to the SA, while control students proceed to the SA regardless of FA score and without feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Modules are completed in order of increasing complexity, students receive feedback on their answers after each FA attempt, and instructional material (text, videos) must be available for review before retaking.
#### Constraints
- ML was incorporated only into the Remember, Understand, Analyze, and Evaluate modules; there are no FA questions for the Apply and Create modules because students are drawing PCPs and learning occurs in an applied setting.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in a data visualization course

### Learning Goals
- developing literacy with Parallel Coordinate Plots across the six cognitive processes of the Revised Bloom's taxonomy
