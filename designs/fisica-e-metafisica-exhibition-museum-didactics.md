# Museum didactics exhibition 'Fisica e Metafisica' linking historical physics instruments to metaphysical art works

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An exhibition of historical instruments from the University of Ferrara's physics collection (November 2015–January 2016), structured in five sections (measures, meteorology, electromagnetism, astronomy, medical apparatus), each paired with metaphysical paintings and period documents. "Dall'osservazione di alcuni particolari presenti nei quadri, con un gioco di corrispondenze, vengono introdotti gli strumenti scientifici"; guided visits for schools added a technological deepening and the human story of the scientists.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A historical scientific instrument collection
- Pairing of instruments with art works and period documents
- Guided visits and interactive physics laboratories for younger visitors
#### Constraints
- Bound to the local history of Ferrara and the Bongiovanni–de Chirico circle

### Target Learners
- Visiting school groups and the general public

### Learning Goals
- Introducing discoveries of modern physics through art-science correspondence
- Approaching science through the human stories of its protagonists
