# Methodic recommendations on English-language material for forming creative independence

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors developed a course and methodic recommendations on material of English for forming students' creative independence, including "test tasks, speech situations, creative tasks for knowledge of lexical, grammatical material". Procedures suggested include analysis, heuristic methods, vision of structure of object, alternative decisions, creation of essentially new ways of solving a problem, and use of elements of game activity, with question-answer work as a form of control. Formation was estimated by performance of creative tasks such as making up dialogues, writing compositions and letters, and translating texts and verses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires creative tasks and exercises expecting individual student work and use of language material for expressing thoughts in dialogues and monologues
#### Constraints
- Developed and tried out on first- and second-year students at Kazan (Volga region) Federal University

### Target Learners
- first- and second-year university students of English

### Learning Goals
- lexical and grammatical knowledge
- creative independent work
- transfer of language skills
