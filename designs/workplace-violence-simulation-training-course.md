# Workplace Violence Simulation Training Course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 3-h course for nursing staff combining classroom explanation, a simulation video of verbal and physical violence, demonstration of communication and breakaway skills, and group simulation exercises with scenario review discussion. The course was developed "based on the systematic reviews and nursing staff focus group interviews", and its teaching video followed Jeffries' simulation design principles of introduction, learning objective, case study, and assessment of countermeasures. Participants worked in four groups of eight to nine persons, and two expert focus group interviews examined content validity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Nursing staff participants in high-risk units such as emergency departments and medical and surgical wards
- Teaching video organized from real situations and expert review of content validity
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only in a single-group pre-/post-test design at one medical center, so sustainability of learning effects could not be measured

### Target Learners
- Generalist nurses in high-risk units of a medical center

### Learning Goals
- Concepts of workplace violence prevention
- Confidence in coping with patient aggression
