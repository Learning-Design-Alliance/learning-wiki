# Three broad groups of required software skills for HEP research software sustainability

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article organizes the software skills a sustainable HEP workforce needs into three broad groups. The first is fundamental and generic software engineering, exemplified by Unix, version control, C++ and continuous integration. The second is knowledge of domain-specific HEP packages and practices, exemplified by the ROOT data format and analysis framework. The third is more advanced specialized knowledge, including parallel programming, machine learning and data science tools, and techniques to maintain software projects at all scales. The curriculum in the paper is structured around these groups, progressing from basic to HEP-specific to advanced material.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The taxonomy is tied to the goal of sustaining the HEP research software ecosystem, which the article says requires a workforce combining HEP domain knowledge and advanced software skills.
#### Constraints
- The skill groups are framed for the high energy physics research software context and its upcoming facilities and upgrades.

### Target Learners

- research software engineers in HEP

### Learning Goals
