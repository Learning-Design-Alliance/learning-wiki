# WeChat public account for English and American literature appreciation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A WeChat public account created to deliver weekly supplementary articles for an English and American literature course at Guangdong University of Foreign Studies. Each of the 12 weekly articles comprises five parts: "lead-in, keywords explanation and translation, writers’ or poets’ information, extended readings and comments on each week’s theme." Content combines verbal, audio, and visual information, with Chinese used as a supplementary language tool for summaries of literary figures.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students need WeChat access and the willingness to read weekly supplementary articles ahead of literature classes
#### Constraints
- The platform is a tool for information dissemination but not for information search, and the current WeChat platform makes it hard to give tasks for students to promote their team work

### Target Learners
- junior undergraduate English majors in an International Business English Program

### Learning Goals
- English and American literature knowledge, literary appreciation, and English competence
