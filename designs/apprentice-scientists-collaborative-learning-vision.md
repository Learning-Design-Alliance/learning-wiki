# Apprentice-scientist vision of technology-dependent collaborative learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The e-Lab rests on a vision in which student research teams investigate real-world problems 'through online projects that are collaborative, student-driven and technology-dependent.' Students are positioned as 'apprentice scientists' who use the same emerging grid infrastructure as their professional partners. The design builds on twelve years of the LInC Program and research from the North Central Regional Educational Laboratory.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The foundation of an e-Lab is the grid infrastructure and virtual data tools and techniques; experiments provide data, analysis software and background material; educators create user-friendly, single-purpose, application-specific web pages on top
#### Constraints
- The project builds on the existing scientist-facing grid architecture and does not attempt to build new grid-based learning services such as the Learning Flow grid services in the EleGI project

### Target Learners
- high school students
- high school science teachers

### Learning Goals
- inquiry-oriented investigation of open scientific questions
- participation in the practices of scientific collaborations
