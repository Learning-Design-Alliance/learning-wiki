# A stepwise eight-step approach to creating educational videos for clinical teaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article summarizes "our approach to creating educational videos" (Fig 1) as a stepwise sequence: choose a durable topic, identify specific learning objectives and a target length, pick software, plan the video (storyboarding, privacy review, equipment testing), consider adding activities, produce (images, webcam use), watch the final product with a reviewer, and make it available to learners. Objectives are limited to "no more than five per video" to reduce cognitive load, and topics unlikely to become obsolete are preferred.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A group of colleagues (and potentially learners) to help in development and peer review
- Checking whether a suitable high-quality video already exists before investing time
#### Constraints
- Topics likely to change rapidly, such as developing guidelines or recent research findings, may not be worth the time investment

### Target Learners
- busy clinical educators creating videos for residents, fellows, and students

### Learning Goals
- efficient production of durable, high-yield educational video content
