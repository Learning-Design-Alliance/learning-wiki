# Four-condition computerized training design for implicit, incidental, rule-search, and instructed L2 rule learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A computerized training apparatus in which 104 adult ESL learners were randomly assigned to implicit, incidental, rule-search, or instructed conditions, then judged novel tokens of easy and hard rule sentence types. Speed and accuracy of these grammaticality judgments served as the dependent measures, allowing comparison of learning conditions across simple and complex rules in a single randomized design.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Random assignment of learners across the four training conditions and computerized presentation of training and judgment trials
#### Constraints
- Studied with adult learners of English as a second language in a computerized laboratory setting

### Target Learners
- Adult learners of English as a second language

### Learning Goals
- Acquiring simple and complex second language grammatical rules under different attentional and instructional conditions
