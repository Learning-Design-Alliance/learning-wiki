# Eight-hour simulation-based EMS POCUS course combining e-learning prework, didactics, hands-on scanning, simulation cases, and a review game

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A one-day, 8-hour in-person course training EMS providers in POCUS fundamentals and the eFAST protocol for blunt trauma. Per the article, "The course design included lectures, standardized patients hands-on scanning, simulation-based ultrasound cases, clinical cases based on real patients, and an end-of-course review game", preceded by assigned e-learning modules (SonoSim) on ultrasound fundamentals and eFAST. The agenda included 2.5 h of lectures, 30 min of interactive cases, 3.5 h of hands-on skills training, and 1 h for assessments. Pathology images were practiced on the SonoSim LiveScan platform, and the course ended with a Kahoot! review game.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An instructor-to-learner ratio of ≤ 1:5 to allow adequate hands-on instruction and deliberate practice
- Pre-course e-learning modules assigned before the course day
- Faculty with extensive POCUS training guiding small-group hands-on practice
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only at Kirkpatrick Levels 1 and 2; behavior and impact levels not assessed
- The article states a one-day program is not sufficient for mastery and recommends additional practice and oversight

### Target Learners
- EMS professionals: paramedics, flight nurses, EMS physicians, military medics

### Learning Goals
- POCUS fundamentals including knobology, image acquisition and interpretation
- eFAST examination across six anatomical regions in trauma patients
