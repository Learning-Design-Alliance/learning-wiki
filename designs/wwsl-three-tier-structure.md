# WWSL three-tier structure: Coordination Center, Research Group Sites, and Experimental Setup Sites with student e-lab-book sites

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The project is organised in tiers: a WWSL Coordination Center supports the official Web site, registers participants, monitors activity, manages study methodology and maintains the FAQ and chat rooms. A Research Group Site registers participants in given lab studies, integrates Student's Sites, and "provides access to experimental data from all Experimental Setups." Each Experimental Setup Site handles data storage, processing and analysis, while students create sites analogous to an electronic lab book for access, control and reporting.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must create their own sites, analogous to an electronic lab book, for access to WWSL resources and report preparation
#### Constraints
- The structure uses existing technology and does not rely on new technical developments at student or research laboratories or on the Internet

### Target Learners
- university students under the instruction of an educator

### Learning Goals
- conducting collaborative experimental research studies remotely
