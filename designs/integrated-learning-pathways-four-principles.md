# Integrated Learning Pathways defined by four principles: competency-based, complementary, cumulative, and consistent

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Learning pathways are system-wide learning progressions that "function according to four principles: They are competency-based, complementary to disciplinary learning, consistent across classrooms, and cumulative from year to year." Competency-based means Portrait Skill Sets are broken into actionable Practices learners demonstrate; complementary means pathways supplement rather than replace existing structures; cumulative means developmentally appropriate progressions articulated as "I can" statements across grade bands; and consistent means practice opportunities are designed into every classroom so attributes are "realistically attainable by each of their students." The design process is intended to be adapted to each district's Portrait.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers, leaders, and experts work together to identify and map integration opportunities; a designated team of district, school, and teacher leaders guides development
#### Constraints
- Each competency-based Practice should not be integrated universally or indiscriminately across grade bands and disciplines; consistency does not mean pathways are blind to context

### Target Learners
- K-12 students across all classrooms and grade bands

### Learning Goals
- Portrait Skill Sets and Practices developed dispositionally across disciplines and grades
