# ECSS-based compressed project lifecycle with milestone reviews tied to assessment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The module structures a full space project development cycle into one term using a simplified version of the ECSS project phasing: the Feasibility, Preliminary Definition and Detailed Definition phases are combined into a single Proposal phase ending in a Preliminary Design Review, followed by a Production & Qualification phase leading to a Flight Readiness Review, and an Operations & Utilisation phase covering flight acceptance, launch, recovery and post-flight analysis. The milestone reviews are tied directly to student assessment, replicating "the major project milestones within the project lifecycle" as formative group assessment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors act as customer and launch authority, reviewing and gating each milestone
- Deadlines must be met to secure a launch at the end of the term
#### Constraints
- The full ECSS scheme is consolidated to fit a single term of just over 3 months

### Target Learners
- postgraduate MSc students in space science and technology

### Learning Goals
- space industry project management practices
- systems engineering
- design review documentation
