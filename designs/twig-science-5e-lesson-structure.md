# 5E instructional model structure (Engage, Explore, Explain, Elaborate, Evaluate) as the organizing lesson pattern

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Twig Science organizes instruction around the 5E model, which the summary spells out as "Engage, Explore, Explain, Elaborate, Evaluate." Within this structure the curriculum places student inquiry at the center, embeds science and engineering practices, and sequences multimedia-rich investigations and collaborative tasks across the phases. Built-in literacy supports are integrated rather than treated separately.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
