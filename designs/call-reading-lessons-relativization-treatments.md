# Computer-assisted reading lessons for relativization instruction (MOG, ROG, and COG treatments)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Ten daily computer-assisted reading lessons delivered the treatments to all three groups in a common format: "a text that appeared on the screen in three sequential reading-skills formats—a skimming format, an in-depth reading format, and a scanning format—followed by the two measures of comprehension". The experimental manipulation occurred during Reading for Understanding: the meaning-oriented group received lexical rephrasings and sentence clarification, the rule-oriented group viewed Animated Grammar decomposition and recombination, and controls viewed only the text sentences. Daily comprehension tests and L1 recall summaries were built in.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Computer laboratory access and daily one-lesson assignments on individual diskettes
- Researcher presence to ensure subjects viewed only assigned lessons
#### Constraints
- Instruction was limited to restrictive OBJECT OF A PREPOSITION relative clauses within three created stories

### Target Learners
- Middle-proficiency intensive English institute students

### Learning Goals
- Comprehension and acquisition of English relative clauses
