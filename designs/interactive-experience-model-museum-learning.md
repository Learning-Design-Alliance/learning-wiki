# Interactive Experience Model as the basis for museum learning programs

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Interactive Experience Model (Falk and Dierking, 1992) holds that the personal, social, and physical intersect, providing a dynamic way for museums to achieve successful learning experiences for school children using their unique attributes outside the school environment. Sydney Observatory adopted this hands-on approach as central to all of its education programs, and the Dreamtime Astronomy program was designed under it with the ambition of achieving a 'minds-on' experience for students through a cultural experience.
