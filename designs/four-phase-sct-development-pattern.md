# Four-phase SCT development pattern: blueprint and construction, faculty feedback, pilot testing, then administration

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article organizes SCT construction as a four-phase process: (1) test blueprint and SCT construction, (2) SCT feedback, (3) SCT pilot testing, and (4) SCT administration and data collection. The blueprint maps curriculum objectives to accreditation standards; faculty review content validity and clarity across two feedback rounds; pilot testing with clinicians meeting reference-panel criteria identifies questions with unanimity (too straightforward) or extreme divergence (too ambiguous) for revision or discard; finally the optimized test is administered and scored.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty content experts for blueprint evaluation and feedback, and a pilot sample meeting reference panel inclusion criteria, are needed before administration.
#### Constraints
- Items with bimodal extreme responses or unanimous panel answers are discarded, so the pattern assumes willingness to eliminate and revise items during optimization.

### Target Learners
- first-year Doctor of Physical Therapy students

### Learning Goals
- valid assessment of clinical reasoning in uncertainty
