# Cadaver-based ETI testing and training protocol with blinded video review

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 unmarked) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
This element is the study's training-and-assessment protocol: fourth-year medical students were stratified by prior ETI experience (<10, 10–24, 25+ attempts), randomized 1:1 to a 30-minute instructor-led session on an unembalmed cadaver or a Laerdal airway management trainer, then tested on a separate unembalmed cadaver using direct laryngoscopy. Two intervention-blinded reviewers assessed video recordings of time to successful ETI and POGO, with reviewer correlation coefficients of 0.99 and 0.98 and no systematic bias on a Bland-Altman plot.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An unembalmed cadaver used as the testing medium for all learners to eliminate between-group differences in airway difficulty
- Blinded video review with student identifying information blurred
#### Constraints
- POGO could not be judged in cases where the camera became obscured; one session's data was lost to camera malfunction

### Target Learners
- fourth-year medical students on EM sub-internship or procedures electives

### Learning Goals
- endotracheal intubation procedural competency
