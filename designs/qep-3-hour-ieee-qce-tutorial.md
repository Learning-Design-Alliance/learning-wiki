## Description
QEP's next major project is a 3-hour tutorial workshop debuting at the IEEE International Conference on Quantum Computing & Engineering in September 2023, its first pilot outside the university setting, aimed at quantum professionals at various experience levels. The article states "The tutorial will expand upon our workshop at Perimeter Institu te," with learning goals including articulating principles of quantum ethics and responsible innovation, reasoning about specific ethical issues, interrogating hype using the Gartner Hype Cycle, analyzing incentive structures and positionality, and applying ethical decision-making tools to realistic dilemmas.
