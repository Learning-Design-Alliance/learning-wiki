# CONSULT-BP: a three-part knowledge, awareness, and practice training intervention targeting implicit bias in clinician-patient communication

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
CONSULT-BP is a multi-component educational intervention for internal medicine, family medicine, and nurse practitioner trainees. Its three core elements are trainee knowledge acquisition about healthcare disparities, implicit bias and racism; trainee awareness of personal bias via Implicit Association Tests with results feedback; and skills practice through four standardized patient encounters focused on hypertension management with patients from vulnerable populations. The article describes it as a multi-component "knowledge, awareness, and practice" training model.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trainees practice with standardized patients recruited and trained from local racial and ethnic communities
- Online e-learning modules on health disparities, implicit bias, and patient-centered communication
#### Constraints
- Developed and evaluated at a single academic safety net hospital system
- Clinical focus limited to hypertension management

### Target Learners
- Internal medicine residents
- Family medicine residents
- Doctor of Nursing Practice nurse practitioner students

### Learning Goals
- Mitigate effects of implicit bias in clinical encounters
- Improve communication skills and hypertension management with minority and low-income patients
