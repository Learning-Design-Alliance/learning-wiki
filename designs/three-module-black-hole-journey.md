# Three sequential journey modules: Finding the Invisible, The Voyage, and Get Up Close

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The core of the website is a journey through ”three sequential, intera ctive modules”: Finding the Invisible, The Voyage, and Get Up Close. Users find black holes in a draggable viewfinder, travel in an animated starship to Cygnus X-1 or the Andromeda galaxy, and orbit a black hole to see phenomena such as gravitational lensing, completing a path to ”a personal encounter with a singularity”.
