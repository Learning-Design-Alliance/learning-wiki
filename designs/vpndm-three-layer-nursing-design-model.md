# Virtual patient Nursing Design Model (vpNDM): a three-layer theory-anchored blueprint for authoring VPs in nursing education

## Description
The vpNDM is a design blueprint grounded in the 3rd-generation nursing process (OPT model) that specifies what elements a VP for nursing education should contain. As the article states, "A virtual patient Nursing Design Model (vpNDM) composed of three layers was developed." Layer 1 covers patient-centric data collection (patient story, interviews, physical examination, observation, measurement scales, records), Layer 2 covers iterative clinical reasoning (framing, cue logic, juxtaposing, testing, judgment), and Layer 3 covers measurable outcomes such as data analysis, documentation, prioritizing interventions, and reflection. It is intended as a guideline for teachers authoring VPs.
