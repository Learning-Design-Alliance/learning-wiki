# Scripted lessons combining role-playing, cooperative learning games, small group activities, and classroom discussion

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The program's lesson design pattern pairs fully scripted lessons with active, participatory methods. As the report describes, "All lessons are scripted and engage students through role-playing and cooperative learning games, small group activities, and classr oom discussions." Cooperative learning activities, role-playing, and skill building methods reinforce positive behaviors and skills and encourage students to apply them in other contexts. Lessons run 30-60 minutes in elementary school and 30-45 minutes in middle school.
