# Validated 3-day RDT training curriculum with 20-step observation checklist for community health workers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A 3-day training curriculum derived from Kenyan Ministry of Health materials plus skills-oriented sessions, delivered by nurses, public health officers, and peer mentors, after which 271 CHWs demonstrated competence in performing and interpreting RDTs. Competency was measured with a standardized observation checklist that "divided the RDT procedure into 20 steps in three domains: safety (five steps), accuracy (seven steps), and procedural (eight steps)". CHWs were also equipped with full testing supplies for independent community use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training culminates in observed demonstration of competence in completing the RDT procedure and interpretation of test results
#### Constraints
- At follow-up, observers interrupted only when one of five safety steps or two accuracy steps were performed incorrectly, since failure on these could compromise patient safety or test results

### Target Learners
- Volunteer community health workers in western Kenya, mean age 43.6 years, 68.9% women, with varying education levels

### Learning Goals
- Safely and accurately conduct malaria RDTs and interpret test results
