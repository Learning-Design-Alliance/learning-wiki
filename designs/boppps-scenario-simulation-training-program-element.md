# BOPPPS-plus-scenario-simulation training program for junior circulating nurses

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A structured in-service training program implemented September–October 2023 in a real operating room at a single hospital, in which "scenario-based simulation incorporated into the participatory learning component" supported training in circulation responsibilities. Five scenario cases were developed from pre-training video review and the five lowest-scoring ability items; each of five groups drew four randomly selected scenarios, with nurses rotating through surgical-team roles while head nurse, teaching leader, and quality-control leader observed, followed by self-assessment, peer review, and three-tiered guided debriefing. Outcomes were assessed with a self-developed ability form, execution checklist, satisfaction questionnaire, and adverse-event counts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained instructional team capable of scenario facilitation and feedback delivery; time investment in the Objective phase so learners internalize objectives and evaluation dimensions
#### Constraints
- High costs of equipment, case development, and instructor training hinder wider adoption; transfer of learning to real high-pressure surgical environments remains uncertain; evaluation of non-technical skills lacks objective criteria

### Target Learners
- Junior operating room circulating nurses (N2 level)

### Learning Goals
- Work quality, execution of circulating responsibilities, patient safety, teamwork, and communication competencies
