# E-learning OHE-PH course for non-dental undergraduates (45-min online module plus online Q&A session)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An e-learning version of the periodontal health education course, built on the online components of the "Preventive Dentistry" textbook, delivered to non-dental students in 2020. It comprised "a total of 45-min of online course time and a 45-min online question and answer session via Tencent Group", covering aetiology, prevention (plaque control), clinical treatment and periodontitis-associated systemic diseases, with "animation and videos instead of text explanations" to enhance understanding. Student attitudes were positive: 75.00% preferred the e-learning course and 98.53% found it helpful.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Internet access and an online platform (Tencent Group) for course delivery and the question-and-answer session
- Educators need to routinely update courses as dentistry knowledge advances
#### Constraints
- Inadequate learning atmosphere and interaction among students and between students and teachers were among the top disadvantages reported
- Universities and hospitals may encounter increased IT infrastructure costs to support e-learning development
- Difficult to ensure consistency of the learning environment for students attending online courses at home during the COVID-19 pandemic

### Target Learners
- Non-dental undergraduate students

### Learning Goals
- Knowledge of periodontal disease aetiology, prevention and treatment
- Self-reported oral care and gingival bleeding treatment behaviours
