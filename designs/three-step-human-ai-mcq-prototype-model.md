# Three-step human–AI co-creation model for generating MCQ prototypes

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A goal-oriented conceptual model in which a teacher and several commonly available AIs co-create MCQ prototypes through three prompts: building a concept map from educational text or learning objectives, formulating real-world questions with correct one-phrase answers, and generating complete MCQs meeting nine quality criteria. Human revision follows each prompt before the next is set, and each prompt is assigned to the AI that performed it best. The article states the model "positions AI as a supportive instrument that enhances the productivity of MCQ developers and reduces educators’ workload, rather than as a substitute for human judgment."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A teacher with subject-matter and pedagogical expertise but no specialized IT competencies, access to several freely available LLM tools, and educational text or learning objectives as input
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness was established under simulated conditions, not yet in teachers' real practice; the authors note real evidence "would be obtained through an empirical study"

### Target Learners
- university students in natural science disciplines (biology, microbiology, biochemistry, immunology)

### Learning Goals
- assessment of higher-order thinking skills and knowledge application in real-world situations
