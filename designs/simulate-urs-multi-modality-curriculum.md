# SIMULATE multi-modality URS simulation curriculum

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The SIMULATE URS curriculum is an expert-developed, Delphi-refined multi-modality training programme for novice urology residents. It "begins with didactic lectures" covering ureteric access, guidewires, lasers, and semi-rigid and flexible ureteroscopy, then continues with tasks and cases on the URO-Mentor VR simulator, bench models (Uro-Scopic Trainer, Advanced Scope Trainer) and fresh frozen cadavers where locally available. It was delivered across five international sessions with local board-certified urologist supervision.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants are novices with 0–10 URS procedures and no prior structured simulation training
- Sessions guided by local board-certified urologists through each curriculum phase
#### Constraints
- FFCs could only be utilised for UK participants (n = 14)
- VR simulation was unavailable for Japanese trainees (n = 5), who were taught these tasks on dry-lab models to compensate

### Target Learners
- Novice urology residents in years 1–4 with 0–10 URS procedures

### Learning Goals
- Semi-rigid and flexible ureterorenoscopy technical skills transferable to the operating room
