# Two-week quantum computing course-chapter within the TCS course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A self-contained course-chapter on quantum computing embedded in the elective Technologies of Computing Systems (TCS) course of the MEEC MSc program at Instituto Superior Técnico, worth 2 ECTS with 56 total work hours (14 contact, 42 autonomous) in two weeks. It covers qubits and quantum states, the Bloch sphere, quantum gates, the Deutsch algorithm, the Quantum Fourier Transform and Shor's algorithm, and superconducting qubit technology. Assessment combines a final written exam (40% of the grade) with two projects (60%), including a circuit demonstration and group discussion. The author concludes the chapter "effectively bridged the gap between classical and quantum computing for engineering students within a short period of two weeks".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- students with background in physics, mathematics, computer engineering and computer science
- supplementary tensor algebra material (Appendix A) since tensor products may not be taught in engineering BSc programs
#### Constraints
- the physics of quantum phenomena and detailed technology cannot be studied in the short period; the chapter focuses on superconducting technology only

### Target Learners
- MSc students in Electrical and Computer Engineering, including Erasmus exchange students

### Learning Goals
- principles and math supporting quantum computing
- analysis of quantum gates and circuits
- derivation of quantum states and data representation
- designing quantum algorithms
