# Course redesign combining blended and flipped learning with ongoing project-based assessment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In a 2019 action-research innovation of the course Methods of Physical Problems Solving, the authors implemented extensive blended and flipped learning. Assessment changed from traditional credit tests to "the outcomes of students' ongoing independent work on assignments and projects," and a new syllabus emphasized physical modeling and visualization strongly augmented by digital technologies, leveraging students' prior knowledge from general and theoretical STEM courses.
