# Extension of Biggs' constructive alignment for embedding GenAI in existing curricula

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article extends Biggs' theory of constructive alignment into the GenAI era, using it as the lens for curriculum integration of generative AI in business higher education. Its theoretical contribution is "demonstrating how GenAI can be embedded into existing pedagogical strategies without requiring full curriculum redesign". On this account, GenAI tools are aligned with intended learning outcomes and existing teaching and assessment strategies, so adoption proceeds by integration into the aligned system rather than by rebuilding the curriculum around the technology.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- GenAI use must be aligned with strategic learning outcomes for adoption to be coherent and sustainable
#### Constraints
- The extension is demonstrated in business higher education contexts at one UK university during first-year implementation

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and postgraduate business higher education students

### Learning Goals
- meaningful integration of GenAI tools into business curricula aligned with learning outcomes
