# Manufacturing-defect investigation as an open-inquiry pattern linking fabrication to circuit concepts

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
After a class creates roughly 30 panels, substantial variation in output becomes visible and students compute statistics for panel voltage, current, and power, then compare expected and outlier panels to diagnose defects such as bridged tabbing wire. The article states that when run as open inquiry, wiring panels in series to power a 12 V fan integrates "closed conducting paths, Kirchhoﬀ's voltage loop rule, and trouble-shooting loose wires with a multimeter." The pattern treats student mistakes as interesting and valuable for learning.
