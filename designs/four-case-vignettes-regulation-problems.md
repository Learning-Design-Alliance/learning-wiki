# Four case vignettes systematically varying motivational and comprehension-related problems

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The study's core instrument is a booklet of four case vignettes describing a study group preparing for an exam, arranged in a 2 × 2 within-subjects design: no problems ("high knowledge and high learning motivation"), only motivational problems, only comprehension-related problems, and both problems ("low knowledge and low learning motivation"). After each vignette participants indicated at three social levels whether they would "do something to ensure a high quality of learning" and wrote down open-ended strategy answers. A soccer-team example vignette familiarized participants with the level distinctions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants able to imagine being a member of the described study group and to distinguish the three social levels of regulation, supported by the example vignette
#### Constraints
- The vignettes describe hypothetical rather than real group learning situations, and did not point to specific group members who might have more motivational or comprehension-related problems than others

### Target Learners
- university students in an introductory Educational Science lecture

### Learning Goals
- reporting conditional strategy knowledge for regulating motivational and comprehension-related problems at self, co and shared levels
