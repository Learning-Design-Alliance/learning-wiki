# Blended e-learning activity structured across eight learning phases

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The study organized its blended e-learning activity based on "the eight learning phases proposed by Baldwin-Evans (2006) and Bielawski and Metcalf (2005)": Prepare me, Tell me, Show me, Let me, Check me, Support me, Coach me, and Connect me. Weekly sessions combined two in-class lecture hours with one e-learning hour, plus after-class discussion board and email support. Paper-based and online practice and tests let students check their learning progress.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A syllabus emailed to students and an orientation introducing blended e-learning before the course
#### Constraints
- The sequence was traditional learning followed by e-learning, with e-learning treated as a supporting tool provided after class

### Target Learners
- 11th-grade vocational high school students

### Learning Goals
- electrical machinery knowledge and skills (transformer unit)
