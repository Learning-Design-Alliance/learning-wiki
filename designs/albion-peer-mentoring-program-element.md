# Albion College low-cost peer-mentoring program (~$1500 per semester) blending mentoring, advising, and active-learning teaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A pilot program run in 2013-14 and 2014-15 and continuing in 2015-16 at Albion College, a private undergraduate-only liberal arts college, combining peer mentoring, intensive advising, and evidence-based teaching practices in introductory physics and calculus. It is "relatively low -cost (~$1500 per semester)", funded from existing grader, tutor, and teaching assistant budget lines in the Department of Physics and the Quantitative Studies Center. Introductory sections average about 25 students and use reading quizzes, share/pair, cooperative learning, board work, flipped classrooms, and technology-based activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Existing grader, tutor, and teaching assistant funds allocated in annual department budgets
- Small class sizes (typically averaging about 25 students) that support active learning techniques
#### Constraints
- Piloted and evaluated at a single small undergraduate-only institution; the article notes effects on mathematics enrollment are more difficult to quantify due to the lack of a single path through the required mathematics courses

### Target Learners
- entering students intending Physics Major, Minor, or Dual Degree Program in Engineering

### Learning Goals
- persistence to graduation and on-time program completion
- success in introductory physics and mathematics courses
