# Four-stage knowledge challenge sequence synchronized with MOOC learning modules

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each KSX knowledge challenge follows a fixed sequence of four stages: proposal (students submit solutions and earn contribution points), offering (approved stocks are sold in a single-day sealed-bid IPO auction), trading (continuous LMSR trading with a five percent-edit-rule allowing shareholders to improve stock content), and evaluation (a judge grades stocks and payoffs are distributed). The article recommends connecting each MOOC learning module with a corresponding challenge so the market progresses with the course, and notes the per-challenge payoff lets students learn trading tactics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A precise task description for each challenge, since sourcing solutions requires it
- Supervisor approval of proposed solutions before they become tradable stocks
#### Constraints
- Each challenge with its set of solutions is treated as a separate market, so challenges are independent of one another

### Target Learners
- Adult MOOC participants in higher education

### Learning Goals
- Iterative improvement of knowledge artifacts
- Peer evaluation and market-based quality signaling
