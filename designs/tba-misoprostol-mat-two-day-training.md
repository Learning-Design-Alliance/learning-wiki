# Two-day TBA/CHW training on misoprostol and a blood measurement tool added to clean delivery kits

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a two-day training delivered to 696 RDRS field staff, 588 of whom were TBAs, in spring 2009. It covered misoprostol function, dosage, timing, side-effects and their management, use of the locally-produced blood measurement mat that "can capture blood loss up to 500 mL", high-risk pregnancy identification, danger signs, referral procedures, stages of labour, newborn resuscitation, and use of the new CDKs. The mat and tablets were then added to existing clean delivery kits at no extra charge to clients.
