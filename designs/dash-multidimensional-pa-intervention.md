# DASH multi-dimensional physical activity intervention (two PE lessons, moving-to-music, in-class breaks, infrastructure adaptation)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A multi-dimensional school-based physical activity program delivered during official school lessons over 20 weeks in three intervention schools. "The physical activity intervention module included four parts: (i) regular physical activity classes including two physical education lessons per week; (ii) one weekly moving-to-music class; (iii) regular in-class activity breaks incorporated into the main school curriculum; and (iv) school infrastructure adaptation." Lessons were co-taught with life orientation teachers and NMU students; some schools also received nutrition or health education components.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivery within official school lessons in collaboration with life orientation teachers; commitment from participating schools
#### Constraints
- Implemented in only three of eight study schools due to financial, logistical, and manpower constraints; teacher compliance and adherence varied considerably

### Target Learners
- Grade four primary school children (aged 8–13) in disadvantaged quintile three schools in Port Elizabeth, South Africa

### Learning Goals
- Selective attention (d2 concentration performance and error percentage) and end-of-year academic performance
