# Triple Helix theory operationalized for teacher education as university-industry-government collaboration

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article applies the Triple Helix model (Etzkowitz and Leydesdorff) to normal university education, reconceptualizing stakeholder relationships so that teacher training institutions, educational technology providers, and educational governance bodies act as collaborative partners. It claims the model "facilitates knowledge transfer across institutional boundaries, enables resource sharing and risk distribution, and creates synergistic innovation cycles". In this study the theory guides the platform's cross-sector data flows, shared governance, and alignment of teacher preparation with national standards.

## Design Implications
