# Culturally relevant, place-based, visually grounded STEM resource design

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This program-level pattern combines three design focuses the article names for authentic astronomy engagement: cultural relevancy, storytelling, and place-based relevancy, expressed through visual language. Place-based education "engages students and communities, breaks down Native students' 'boredom'," and demonstrates tangible local significance of science; the article also cites evidence that children with developed visual perception and representational skills are better with numbers and physical concepts. Resources root themselves in local history and location while making global connections, weaving science, culture, and art together with diligent purposefulness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content tied to local history, landscape, and community
- cultural knowledge honored alongside Western science
- visual artifacts (maps, calendars, paintings) as central teaching tools
#### Constraints
- The article notes spatial thinking is under-recognized, undervalued, underappreciated, and therefore under-instructed, per a National Academy of Sciences report

### Target Learners
- Native American K-12 students
- indigenous communities
- K-12 educators

### Learning Goals
- STEM engagement
- science with tangible local significance
- visual and spatial thinking
