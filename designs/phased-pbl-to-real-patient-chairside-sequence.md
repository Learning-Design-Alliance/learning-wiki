# Phased chairside teaching sequence progressing from simulated PBL cases to real comprehensive patient care

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The chairside teaching process was divided into three stages with a consistent focus across groups: "the initial phase (Weeks 1–2), the mid-phase (Weeks 3–4), and the later phase (Weeks 5–12)." The sequence moves from pre-prepared PBL cases and simulated-head-model practice, through real typical cases with seminar discussion, to comprehensive examination of all oral conditions and systematic treatment planning. This graduated structure scaffolds the transition from preclinical knowledge to independent clinical reasoning within a 12-week internship.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All students independently complete at least one premolar root canal treatment and present a collected complex or interdisciplinary case
#### Constraints
- Designed for a 12-week chairside internship in Operative Dentistry and Endodontics

### Target Learners
- fourth-year undergraduate dental students beginning clinical practice

### Learning Goals
- clinical operation skills
- comprehensive examination and treatment planning
- general practice-oriented clinical mindset
