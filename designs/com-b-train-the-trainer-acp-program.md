# COM-B-driven train-the-trainer ACP program combining a workshop with individualized field coaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The program is a two-component implementation strategy targeting the COM-B behavior-change constructs of capacity, opportunity, and motivation. A 2-day face-to-face ACP workshop uses group lecture, guided presentations of pictorial ACP tools, and "simulation sessions using case scenarios and role-play to help learners to practice ACP conversation in an immersive and engaging environment". It is followed by one-to-one field coaching in which nurses first shadow a certified ACP facilitator, then deliver ACP jointly and independently with telephone follow-up, with extra sessions for complicated cases.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A certified ACP facilitator coach and a designated nurse per home who subsequently coaches other nurses
- Evidence-based teaching strategies such as role-play, case scenarios, and pictorial ACP education material
#### Constraints
- The strategy was designed for the unique contextual factors of residential care homes, and its impact in alternative settings may not be assured
- Evaluation primarily focused on the trainer aspect due to the limited follow-up period

### Target Learners
- Enrolled nurses, registered nurses, health assistants, and social workers in residential care homes

### Learning Goals
- ACP knowledge, skills, and competency
- Capacity, opportunity, and motivation to implement ACP in routine care
