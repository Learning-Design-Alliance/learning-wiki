# Flexible compulsory research course combining compulsory modules with elective and tiered optional components

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The CDBRC is a lesson/program-level design that makes basic research education compulsory while preserving flexibility. Its teaching module combines "compulsory theoretical and experimental courses, elective online courses, flexible mentor-guided forms, and elective extracurricular research activities". Compulsory content stays fixed while elective online courses are regularly updated, and mentor-guided participation is tiered so all students engage in research practice at a level matching their interest and ability. The article reports this design covered all undergraduates without excessive burden over three years.
