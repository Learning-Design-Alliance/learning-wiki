# Four-genre English text set for eliciting varied reading stances

## Description
The study used four English texts: an 88-word poem, a 606-word parody of Little Red Riding Hood, a 53-word joke with word-play, and a 910-word expository article on eating insects. The texts were selected for their range of genres and difficulty and for the likelihood they would elicit story-driven, information-driven, or point-driven reading stances from bilingual readers.
