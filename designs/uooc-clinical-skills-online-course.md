# UOOC online course 'Clinical Skills Training and Practice' with five clinical modules

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The online component of the blended intervention was the UOOC course "Clinical Skills Training and Practice", divided into five clinical modules: internal medicine, surgery, gynecology, pediatrics, and emergency medicine. Each module contains "theoretical lecture videos, standardized operation demonstration videos, PPT resources, as well as supporting exercises and tests", plus a discussion board for teacher-student interaction. Online teaching constituted 25% of total class hours.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the UOOC platform and electronic devices for students
- Teacher presence on the discussion board to answer questions and post case material
#### Constraints
- Online teaching made up only 25% of class hours; the article states online teaching cannot fully substitute offline teaching for highly practical content

### Target Learners
- undergraduate clinical medicine students in their fifth semester

### Learning Goals
- clinical skills proficiency assessed by OSCE
