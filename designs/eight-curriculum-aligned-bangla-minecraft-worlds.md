# Eight curriculum-aligned, Bangla-localized Minecraft worlds mapped to National Curriculum and Textbook Board standards

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Content and Curriculum Layer specifies eight pre-built worlds mapped to "National Curriculum and Textbook Board standards across science, math, social studies, and languages for grades three through twelve." Examples include "Lalbagh Fort with sandstone courtyard, bilingual lecterns" for Mughal architecture and research skills, the Sundarbans ecosystem, States of Matter Lab, Monsoon Flood simulation, Basic Circuits, Soil Erosion, Water Cycle, and Shaheed Minar. "All environments include full Bangla localization, voiced tutorials, and accessible in-world annotations."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Bilingual signboards and voiced tutorials; USB/SD distribution for offline tiers
#### Constraints
- Worlds are proposed designs, not field-tested artifacts; exam-oriented curricula require close alignment with curriculum requirements

### Target Learners
- Bangladeshi students in grades 3-12, including students with limited reading skills supported by audio narration

### Learning Goals
- Subject-specific goals such as phase transitions, ecosystem dynamics, Mughal architecture, electricity, and land conservation
