## Description
The article invokes Issenberg and co-workers' account of the features needed for simulation to transform a principle into a lesson learned. The listed features include "provision of feedback, repetitive practice, curriculum integration, appropriate range of difficulty level, multiple learning strategies, simulation that captures clinical variation, a controlled environment, individualized learning, defined outcomes and simulator validity". The review uses this framework to interpret why simulation improved coronary anastomosis performance and to discuss curriculum design across the included trials.
