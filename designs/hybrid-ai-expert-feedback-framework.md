# Hybrid dual-feedback framework: AI feedback as an auxiliary tool combined with expert judgment for formative assessment of psychomotor clinical skills

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes a hybrid educational model in which AI-generated feedback is combined with expert feedback rather than replacing expert judgment. The authors state that "AI should serve as an auxiliary tool rather than an independent assessor" and that a "dual-feedback approach leverages the scalability and standardization of AI with the contextual richness and adaptability of expert input". Human-in-the-loop frameworks, where AI outputs are reviewed or modified by educators, and explainability mechanisms are identified as ways to improve reliability and learner trust.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Human-in-the-loop review or modification of AI outputs by educators
- Calibration with expert-annotated datasets and incorporation of case variability to improve AI alignment
#### Constraints
- Current text-based LLMs cannot process radiographic data directly, limiting image-dependent parameters such as taper formation and void detection
- AI-generated assessments should be interpreted with caution in radiographically intensive specialties until multimodal models are validated

### Target Learners
- undergraduate dental students in preclinical and clinical endodontic training

### Learning Goals
- root canal shaping skills
- radiographic interpretation
- formative feedback on psychomotor clinical procedures
