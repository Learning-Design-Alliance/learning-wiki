# Escalating role of multimedia across teacher-training stages

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a reusable design in which "The role of multimedia as a means of teaching is expanding at each subsequent stage". Multimedia shifts from visual presentation of material, to learning the technology of creating multimedia tools, to producing creative multimedia projects used in teaching practice. This distribution "allows us to achieve the goal of each stage of the methodology".
