# Iterative lecture content design process: fine-tune, prompt for topic outline, expert mapping to ULOs, then generate and refine slides

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A step-by-step process for generating lecture content, shown in Figure 1. The model is first fine-tuned on custom reference material, then prompted with the course title, description and Unit Learning Outcomes to suggest subject outline topics; an expert maps topics to ULOs, and sub-topics and slides are generated and refined until the expert accepts them. The authors note: "Prompts should be refined to get the quality output."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Course description and reference material as fine-tuning input
- Expert analysis and evaluation at the topic-mapping and slide-review steps
- Iterative prompt refinement until the expert accepts the output
#### Constraints
- The process "currently does not omit the need for expert instructors and thorough content evaluation for correctness"

### Target Learners
- Higher education students

### Learning Goals
- Lecture content aligned with unit learning outcomes
