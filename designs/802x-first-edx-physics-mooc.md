# 8.02x: MITx's first introductory physics MOOC on the edX platform (Spring 2013)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
8.02x is a Massive Open Online Course offered through edX in Spring 2013, centered on recorded video lectures of MIT's Introductory Physics II broken into roughly 5-10 segments with interspersed machine-graded multiple choice questions. Other components include "machine graded weekly homework sets", TEAL simulations with concept questions, videotaped problem solving sessions, an e-Textbook, three midterm exams and a final exam, and a threaded discussion board. Certification is granted for scores greater than 60%.
