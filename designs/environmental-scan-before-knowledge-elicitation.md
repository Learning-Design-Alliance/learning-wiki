# Pair a low-intrusion environmental analysis with knowledge elicitation when studying cognition in busy clinical settings

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends sequencing a minimally intrusive environmental scan before more intrusive knowledge elicitation. "Including at least two data collection methods is unique to our health care-setting adapted CTA framework. We developed this progression to minimize intrusion into the clinical environment." The environmental analysis identifies actors, barriers, facilitators, and policies, and refines interview guides and sampling for the subsequent knowledge elicitation step.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A priori identification of data collection methods and materials during planning
- Iterative refinement of knowledge elicitation materials based on environmental scan findings
