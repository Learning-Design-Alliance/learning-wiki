# AI4CHEM five-theme curriculum progression from chemical data foundations to AI-driven experimentation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The curriculum is organized around five themes in AI for experimental chemistry, presented as a conceptual design (Figure 1): chemical data foundations, machine learning models in chemical context, patterns in chemical space, vision and language intelligence, and AI-driven experimentation. Figure 1 describes "Conceptual design of the AI4CHEM curriculum organized around five themes in AI for experimental chemistry," guiding students from representing and visualizing realistic chemical datasets to building and interpreting predictive models and using AI tools to plan experiments. Each module aligns core AI concepts with corresponding chemistry principles and in-class tutorials (Table 1).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assumes only general chemistry and introductory organic chemistry knowledge, starting from first principles in both coding and machine learning
#### Constraints
- Designed for a one-semester course meeting twice weekly for 80 minutes over approximately 30 class periods

### Target Learners
- upper-level undergraduates and graduate students beginning research in synthetic organic, inorganic, materials chemistry, and biochemistry

### Learning Goals
- connect data-driven methods to wet-lab tasks such as property prediction, reaction optimization, and experiment planning
