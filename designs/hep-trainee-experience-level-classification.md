# Four-level classification of trainee experience for HEP computing training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper proposes that training programmes be organised around "four diﬀerent experience levels" of trainees: Beginners need formal training in modern computing techniques and operating system basics; Intermediates supplement existing experience with modern approaches; Advanced experts stay up-to-date with new developments; and Software Specialists lead development in areas such as DAQ systems, infrastructure, databases and frameworks. The paper states "Each of these groups has very diﬀerent training needs", so programme design must account for audience variation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training programmes must take into account the variation in target audiences, from undergraduates to professors
#### Constraints
- The classification is described as broad and specific to the HEP community's software training context

### Target Learners
- HEP researchers at beginner, intermediate and advanced levels, and software specialists

### Learning Goals
- Modern computing techniques for research software development, including languages, version control and frameworks
