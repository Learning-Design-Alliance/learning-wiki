# CREST: a schoolwide Positive Action-based social and character development program grounded in Cognitive Behavioral Theory and integrated with Belize's HFLE curriculum

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
CREST was a one-year comprehensive schoolwide social and character development program piloted in Belize City primary schools to decrease violence and assist students exposed to violence. It integrated the Positive Action curriculum with the existing Health and Family Life Education curriculum, "providing grounding in a theoretical framework (Cognitive Behavioral Therapy)" and a consistent organizational structure so children could self-reinforce positive behaviors. Positive Action is organized into six units (self-concept; positive actions for body and mind; managing yourself responsibly; treating others the way you like to be treated; telling yourself the truth; improving yourself continually), and the program includes curriculum, school infrastructure elements, and parent and community involvement activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher instruction with a curriculum crosswalk identifying HFLE stories, games, activities and vignettes so additional resources emphasize the six Positive Action units
#### Constraints
- Evaluated as a one-year pilot in Belize City primary schools; the Positive Action curriculum had not previously been used with this population

### Target Learners
- Primary school students in Standards Infant 1 through Standard 6 in Belize City

### Learning Goals
- Positive youth development behaviors
- social-emotional and character development
- reduced violence and negative youth behaviors
