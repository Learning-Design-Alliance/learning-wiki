# Clinic session workflow pairing student IPE teams with faculty supervision and interpreter-supported assessment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The clinic's recurring session structure functions as a reusable instructional pattern: student volunteers "receive an orientation, form an IPE team, complete a patient assessment, then report to the faculty physician, nurse practitioner, and/or dentist." The team identifies a diagnosis and creates a plan of care that includes addressing social determinants of health in collaboration with social service staff and the interpreter. Student leaders from each school manage operations during one-year terms.
