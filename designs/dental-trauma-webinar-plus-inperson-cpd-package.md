# Customized dental traumatology CPD intervention: nine pre-recorded webinars plus a 1-day interactive in-person clinic course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A CPD package built on dentists' expressed needs and known knowledge gaps in dental traumatology. The webinar track comprised "nine webinars lasting between 9 and 20 min each, with a total duration of 2 h and 20 min" covering diagnosis, emergency treatment, and prognosis, available flexibly on a secured platform for three months. The in-person component was a 6-hour clinic-based course combining lectures on team management, trauma registration, radiography, and photography with a 3-hour interactive group discussion of trauma cases for dentists and hygienists. It was based on IADT guidelines.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants in IC2 were expected to view the webinars prior to attending the physical course
- in-person course delivered by specialists with clinical and teaching experience in TDI
#### Constraints
- Control clinics received no intervention during the study period but were offered both formats afterward
- the interactive discussion part was offered only to dentists and dental hygienists

### Target Learners
- general dentists in the Public Dental Service
- dental hygienists
- dental assistants
- clinical managers

### Learning Goals
- diagnosis of traumatic dental injuries
- emergency treatment of TDI
- prognosis of TDI in primary and permanent dentition
