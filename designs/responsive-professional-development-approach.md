# Responsive Professional Development (RPD) as an adaptive facilitation approach

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article characterizes RPD as "an adaptive facilitation approach that bases PD activities on the needs, interests, affects, and backgrounds of teachers." It operates through two core mechanisms: being attentive (creating multiple opportunities for teachers to communicate needs) and being adaptive (tailoring PD activities at micro levels like regrouping and macro levels like co-designing workshop structure). The approach is aligned with design-based or adaptive PD models and aims to minimize learning barriers from differing perspectives by catering to teachers at all career stages. It addresses critiques of traditional CoP frameworks that overfocus on novice development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- PD coordinators must co-design and co-facilitate PD activities with teachers
- Multiple channels for teachers to communicate their needs must exist
#### Constraints
- The article notes this is an exploratory phenomenological study and does not aim to generalize results to all teachers in all responsive PD programs

### Target Learners
- High school physics teachers
- Teachers across career stages (novice, mid-career, veteran)

### Learning Goals
