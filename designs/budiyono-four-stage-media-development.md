# Four-stage development model for producing and validating learning media

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study followed Budiyono's development model, which "consists of four stages, which are the preliminary, product development, product trial on its effectivity, dissemination and product implementation." The preliminary stage used observation sheets, pre-tests, needs analysis questionnaires and interviews; product development added expert validation and individual, limited-scale, and broad-scale trials. This study's analysis ran only up to the product trial phase.
