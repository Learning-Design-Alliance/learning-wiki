# Adjust-Table Basic sit-to-stand desks with lever-operated gas spring height adjustment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The intervention artifact is the Adjust-Table Basic sit-to-stand desk, manufactured by Presikhaaf Schoolmeubelen, provided to every student in the experimental classroom. The article describes desks "operated by a lever, which releases a gas spring lock", allowing low-effort height adjustment between 75 and 120 cm, with desktops of the same dimensions as the regular desks (500 × 700 × 18 mm). Students keep the desks until they go to secondary school.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Desks must be easy to operate by young children; the teacher also received an adult sit-to-stand desk to function as a role model
#### Constraints
- Students are not obligated to stand; for instructions the teacher can order all children to sit for a proper view of the (digital) blackboard

### Target Learners
- primary school children aged 8–9

### Learning Goals
- reducing classroom sitting time while maintaining normal instruction
