# Competency frameworks as descriptions of knowledge, skills and attitudes organized to show progressive development across training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article defines competency frameworks as "descriptions of the knowledge, skills, and attitudes pertaining to each competency expected during the students’ training", organized so that they show "the results of the progressive development of students based on competencies" from university entry to post-graduate training. They describe competencies to be repeatedly demonstrated in clinical environments of different complexity, and in this study they structure the ABC emergency-care competencies expected of nursing students at each level of progression.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Frameworks must be organized to show progressive development of students based on competencies, and competencies must be repeatedly demonstrated during curricular schedules in clinical environments with different levels of complexity.
