# Stokes-analysis polymer lab apparatus: polarized laser, polarizer, polymer sample, analyzer, detector

> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A bench-top optical setup on an optical rail consisting of a vertically oriented linearly polarized laser with λ=633nm, a linear polarizer/analyzer, a polymer film sample (unoriented or oriented), and a light intensity detector. Students align elements using laser reflections at 90 degrees to the optical path, write computer programs for the matrix calculations, and record intensity versus analyzer and sample angle. The article describes the setup as "three elements on an optical rail" for part 1 and four elements for parts 2 and 3.
