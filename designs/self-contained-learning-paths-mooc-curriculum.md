# A modular MOOC curriculum structured as self-contained learning paths lets participants set their own priorities.

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The nine-course quantum curriculum is organized into three pillars (introduction, cryptography, algorithms) and offered as self-contained learning paths following prerequisite arrows, so participants can customize their learning based on interests and prior knowledge. The article states that "a series of self-contained learning paths is offered, so that participants can set their own priorities and not have to deal with topics outside their area of interest". Information on the structure is given in the first video of each course, and repetitions of topics from different perspectives are intentionally included.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Courses must be thematically coordinated so each course can be understood with the knowledge of its predecessors in the path structure.
#### Constraints
- Course learners could only attend subsets in the order of the courses' timeframes; self-learners can choose any order.

### Target Learners
- Adult professionals and lifelong learners without prior knowledge of quantum physics or mathematics

### Learning Goals
- Sound introductory understanding of quantum information, quantum cryptography, and quantum algorithms
