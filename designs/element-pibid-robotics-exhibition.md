# PIBID educational robotics exhibition in Parnaíba public high schools

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A one-day-per-school exhibition of educational robotics run by PIBID undergraduate fellows in public high schools of Parnaíba, Piauí, as part of a Brazilian government teacher-training program. During the event "the following activities were also promoted: demonstration of robotics projects and prototypes, presentation of banners and videos, workshops and competition of combat robots." It aimed at teacher training, improvement of teaching in public schools, and dissemination of science.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All projects were brought into reality under the supervision and guidance of the authors, with financial support of the PIBID fellowship program
#### Constraints
- The Educational Robotics exhibition lasted one day in each school chosen to host the activities

### Target Learners
- high school students in Brazilian public schools
- undergraduate teacher-education (PIBID) fellows

### Learning Goals
- physics concepts through robotics prototypes
- dissemination of science and formation of new scientists and researchers
