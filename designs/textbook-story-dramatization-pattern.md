# Story dramatization pattern: rewriting a textbook story into dialogue scenes taught through role play over multiple sessions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's experimental treatment converted the prescribed textbook story The Boy from the Past into a dramatized script and taught it through role play. "The Boy from the Past was rewritten into thirteen scenes in dialogue format and taught through dramatization in ffteen sessions over a period of nine weeks." Students worked in groups to role play the dramatized scenes, answered questions about them, and produced writing tied to each episode's focus (e.g., thesis statement modeling and paragraph production).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A source story from the students' own textbook
- A teacher trained by the researchers to follow set procedures for each session
#### Constraints
- Studied with male eighth-grade EFL students in one Jordanian school; the observation was unstructured and had no validity or reliability check

### Target Learners
- EFL eighth-grade secondary students

### Learning Goals
- Writing performance across thesis statement, unity, completeness and coherence, wording and cohesion, and organization
