# Set of 10 pictorial stories used as experimental instructional material over two months

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The experimental material consisted of (10) pictorial stories distributed over a period of two months in the first semester, covering topics such as long and short tide characters and the static section. In preparing them, "the elements of movement and suspense, prompt reinforcement, direct feedback, and formative evaluation were taken into consideration", and the stories were reviewed by PhD and MA specialists in Arabic, educational technology, and curriculum.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

- Applied only in the first semester of the 2016/2017 academic year with second-grade classes
