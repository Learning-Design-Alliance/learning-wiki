# Three-condition 20-week classroom PA break intervention (combo, aerobic, cognition)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A feasible and innovative long-term intervention including cognitive and physical demands was developed and evaluated, consisting of two 10-minute daily PA breaks over 20 weeks (200 prescribed breaks), delivered by regular class teachers after a half-day training. Three variants systematically varied cognitive engagement and physical exertion: combo (high both), aerobic (high exertion, low CE), and cognition (high CE, low exertion via fine-motor versions of the same games). Teachers reported implementing on average 145.4 of the 200 breaks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Half-day teacher training prior to the study
- Teacher self-report of implemented breaks and implementation accuracy for compliance monitoring
#### Constraints
- Interventions were limited to 20 weeks to fit an uninterrupted school phase and avoid holiday confounds

### Target Learners
- primary school children aged 7–9 in regular classes

### Learning Goals
- executive functions
- mathematics, spelling and reading achievement
