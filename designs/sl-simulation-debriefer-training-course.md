# Standardized learner (SL) simulation debriefer training course delivered by video conference

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A debriefer training course in which a trainer plays a standardized learner (an ICU nurse in a recorded 5-minute simulation-based healthcare education video) while each trainee practices debriefing, followed by immediate individual trainer feedback. Over 2 weeks there were "five training sessions of 100 minutes each," each targeting a different PEARLS framework element (reactions, descriptions, analysis, summary). Simulations were video recorded for OSAD rating and conducted on Zoom, with waiting trainees preparing and reflecting in a separate breakout room.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trainers acting as SLs prepare scripted responses and response frames based on the PEARLS framework; trainees pre-watch the sample video and prepare a debriefing plan from provided self-learning materials (72 slides).
#### Constraints
- The course was structurally designed only for debriefing and could not train pre-briefing or facilitating; it used one SL and one sample video featuring one learner, and included a small number of participants.

### Target Learners
- Novice physician debriefers who teach medical students or residents

### Learning Goals
- Debriefing skills in simulation-based healthcare education and self-confidence in operating simulation classes
