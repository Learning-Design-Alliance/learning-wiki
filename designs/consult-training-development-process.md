# Four-phase stakeholder-informed development process for researcher training: survey needs, develop content, iteratively pilot, disseminate and evaluate

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The CONSULT Training was developed through a four-phase process: "(1) a stakeholder survey to identify researchers' training needs; (2) development of the CONSULT Training content; (3) iterative piloting and refining of the content; and (4) dissemination of the CONSULT Training and initial evaluation". Development was collaborative, involving a lay advisory group of seven people with lived experience and a researcher advisory group of six, and was underpinned by adult learning theories such as self-directed learning, with a '3i' stakeholder analysis used to identify parties with interest, influence, and impact. Survey findings shaped content, and piloting established acceptability, usability, and accessibility before launch.

#### Requirements

### Target Learners
