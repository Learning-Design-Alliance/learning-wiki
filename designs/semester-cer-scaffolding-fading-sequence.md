# Semester-long fading scaffold sequence for scientific argumentation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course implemented a progressive fading of argumentation scaffolds across the semester: early weeks had students label provided statements as claim, evidence, or reasoning; middle weeks had them write their own CER statements; and "In Weeks 11-15 of the semester students were expected to construct full and complete arguments with minimal scaffolding." By the last five weeks, students were expected to fully construct CER-based arguments, which the study then assessed with LLM feedback. The rubrics were designed to mirror this recitation scaffolding.
