# PBLCCS: a five-step instructional model combining Problem-Based Learning with cognitive conflict strategy

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
PBLCCS is a combined learning model the authors compiled from the steps of Problem-Based Learning and Cognitive Conflict Strategy, grounded in Piaget's constructivist ideas of assimilation and accommodation. It proceeds through "Orientation of the problem (conflict identification)", learning group organization, individual and in-group supervision, work development and presentation, and analysis and evaluation of problem solving. The article used it as the experimental treatment to develop critical thinking and curiosity in a Number Theory course.
