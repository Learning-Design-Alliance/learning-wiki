# Ten-day critical care experiential training course for pharmacy students

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A condensed 10-day critical care training course delivered in waves of six or seven trainees at an 80-bed Egyptian hospital, designed by a clinical pharmacist and supervised by an academic. Each day combined a one-hour lecture with five hours of experiential learning: "participating in ward and critical care rounds with a multidisciplinary team, exploring case-based scenarios, interpreting lab results, dosing, mitigating medication errors and drug interactions". Trainees progressed from observing to performing supervised tasks, and completed daily pre/post knowledge tests plus self-efficacy, self-esteem, and satisfaction questionnaires.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Professional clinical pharmacy preceptors specializing in critical care plus an academic supervisor
- Daily pre- and post-tests and validated questionnaires (knowledge Scale-CVI 0.82, Cronbach's alpha 0.73)
#### Constraints
- Delivered as an elective in a small private non-academic hospital; small groups of six or seven trainees per wave

### Target Learners
- 4th-year B.Pharm undergraduate pharmacy students
- post-graduate professional master's pharmacy students

### Learning Goals
- critical care knowledge across eleven topics
- self-efficacy and self-esteem for critical care practice
- communication and consultation skills
