# Missouri shortage predictor model for the teacher workforce

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In 2014, as part of its teacher equity plan, Missouri developed a shortage predictor model that "identifies where the state is likely to see teacher shortages in the coming years," pinpointing specific content areas and geographic locations most likely to experience shortage. DESE targets districts identified by the model and supports their recruitment of potential teacher candidates at the high school level, while EPPs train those candidates in high-need content areas, ideally sending teachers back to their home districts.
