# Slide-caliper apparatus with rings for in-situ catenoid observation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A low-cost apparatus made from a familiar slide caliper with circular rings attached to its tips, used to form and observe soap-film catenoids. The device "enabled us to measure simultaneously both the distance h between the two rings and the neck radius a". A string attached to the movable part is pulled to increase ring separation while a video camera with motion capture records the film's behaviour from formation to rupture.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Soapy water introduced between the rings at h = 0
- A video camera mounted above the caliper to record the catenoid
#### Constraints
- A precise measurement of separation speed was difficult in this experiment

### Target Learners
- physics students

### Learning Goals
- observing local extrema phenomena
- studying minimal surfaces and dynamical mechanics
