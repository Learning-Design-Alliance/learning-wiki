# Introductory physics for life sciences (IPLS) course emphasizing chemical energy for interdisciplinary coherence

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The data source for the study is an introductory physics course for undergraduate life sciences majors. "This course is unusual in that it emphasizes chemical energy as a way to build interdisciplinary coherence across physics, biology, and chemistry" (Redish et al., 2014; [Dreyfus et al., 2014a](https://doi.org/10.1080/09500693.2015.1025306)). The course used the Lennard-Jones potential frequently to represent energy associated with chemical bonds, providing opportunities to observe reasoning about energy across disciplinary contexts. Video recordings of every class were collected for the first two years, plus semi-structured student interviews.
