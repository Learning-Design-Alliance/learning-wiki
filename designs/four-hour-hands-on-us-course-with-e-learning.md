# Four-hour hands-on ultrasound course with preceding e-learning for prehospital physicians

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A blended training intervention combining an online e-learning program of seven interactive modules (approximately 110 min total) with a four-hour hands-on course. The hands-on course used "a participant-to-instructor ratio of 4: 1" and rotated small groups through four instructor-led stations with healthy volunteers demonstrating 12 simulated case scenarios, with continuous feedback and tips for image optimization from instructors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors with emergency US experience leading small-group stations
- Healthy volunteers portraying case scenarios with pathology displayed by the instructor on an iPad
- An e-learning program completed before the hands-on training
#### Constraints
- The course was implemented as mandatory training for all MECU physicians, which the authors state made a randomized controlled trial not feasible

### Target Learners
- Experienced consultant anaesthesiologists working in prehospital mobile emergency care units

### Learning Goals
- Performing focused point-of-care ultrasound examinations, including systematic scanning, image optimization and identification of pathology
