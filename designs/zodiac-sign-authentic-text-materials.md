# Zodiac-sign authentic reading texts with dictionary lookup and personal description writing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The instructional material evaluated in the study: 12 short printed texts about the 12 zodiac signs, one per session, treated as authentic materials because "everybody has his or her own sign according to their birth month". Learners read each text, looked unknown adjectives up in their dictionaries, and wrote a short description of a classmate or family member born under the same sign using the learned adjectives. The control group instead received dictionary extracts of the same adjectives.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Target adjectives must be unknown to learners beforehand; known words were omitted from treatment and post-test
#### Constraints
- Content is tied to learners' birth months and zodiac signs, a specific authenticity framing

### Target Learners
- Iranian intermediate EFL learners in intact classes

### Learning Goals
- Acquisition of 42 previously unknown adjectives

### Claims

- [Iranian intermediate EFL learners who learned adjectives through authentic tasks outperformed learners who studied dictionary extracts on a vocabulary post-test](../claims/authentic-task-outperformed-dictionary-extract-adjectives.md) [+W]
