# Writing-focused GenAI declaration structure with six activity categories

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A declaration form for writing-focused activities (e.g., a short literature review on technology and society) organised around six activity categories: Planning & Structure, Textual Content Generation, Research & Analysis, Revision & Polish, Visual Content, and Evaluation & Feedback. For each category students (1) state whether GenAI was used (Yes/No), (2) specify the extent of use (Minor, Moderate, Extensive), and (3) provide a short explanation with example prompts. The categories operationalise aspects of the Bouvier et al. taxonomy in an essay-writing context, e.g., "Textual Content Generationaligns withGenerate".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students complete per-category Yes/No use, extent rating, and short explanations with example prompts
#### Constraints
- Categories were derived from a computing-education activity taxonomy; the article notes the boundary of what counts as GenAI use is not always obvious to students

### Target Learners
- Higher education students completing writing-focused assessments such as professional or literature-review style reports

### Learning Goals
- Transparent disclosure of GenAI use in writing tasks
- Distinguishing acceptable assistance (e.g., limited revision support) from outsourcing of core content
