# 22-module social-network microlearning program on cardiac arrhythmias for EMTs

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A microlearning intervention delivering arrhythmia training as short online modules pushed through a social-network group. Content covered identification of cardiac arrhythmias, medications and treatment methods, pre-hospital treatment, and resuscitation protocol, prepared from AHA 2020 and Iranian Emergency Organization protocols and presented as "videos, images, and short texts" following microlearning design principles of conciseness, reduced reading material, more images and graphics, and simplicity. It was compared against webinar and control conditions in the study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content approved by cardiology and emergency medicine specialists
- Delivery via a social-network group with three daily postings
- Participants need access to mobile devices and internet connectivity
#### Constraints
- Effectiveness depends on participants' digital literacy, device access, stable internet, and personal motivation, per the article's stated limitations

### Target Learners
- emergency medical technicians (EMTs) in pre-hospital emergency bases

### Learning Goals
- knowledge of diagnosis and treatment of cardiac arrhythmias
- self-efficacy in pre-hospital arrhythmia management
