# Five-phase instructional model for music-mathematics integrated lessons

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An (2012) proposed an instructional model in which "The model has five phases, with each phase containing varying levels of focus on music and mathematics." Phase one focuses solely on music; phase two shifts focus toward mathematics while music retains more focus; phase three gives music and mathematics equivalent focus during student activity; phase four uses students' music products to design mathematics tasks with greater emphasis on mathematics; phase five focuses on pure mathematics with no music. The model underpinned the study's intervention design.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers need professional development and familiarity with music-math integration; participant teachers received 10 hours of PD seminars and pilot-lesson feedback before implementing the model.
#### Constraints
- The article presents the model as a hypothesis-generating framework tested in one exploratory quasi-experiment; the authors do not suggest their activities are a prototype for all mathematics classroom activities.

### Target Learners
- elementary school students

### Learning Goals
- mathematical modeling, strategy, and application abilities
