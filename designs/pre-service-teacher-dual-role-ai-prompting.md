# Dual-role framing of pre-service teachers as simultaneous learners and future instructors shaping AI prompting behavior

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article frames pre-service teachers as occupying a dual role, straddling "the roles of students and future teachers," which requires adopting both the learner's and the instructor's perspectives. It uses this framing to design two lab sessions in which the same participants prompted AI as teachers (wave velocity to elementary students) and as students (Ideal Gas Law to an English teacher), and to interpret role-dependent prompting differences as differences in agency.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants must actually occupy both roles, e.g., through simultaneous coursework and field experiences as in this study
#### Constraints
- The article notes its activity was linear and specific, focusing on only two types of audiences, and calls for more iterative and longitudinal activities across diverse content areas

### Target Learners
- elementary education majors in physical science courses

### Learning Goals
- choosing effective representations to communicate physics concepts
- AI prompting and questioning practices
