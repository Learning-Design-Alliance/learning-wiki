# Structure EVH training programmes with theoretical knowledge, equipment training and progressive clinical skills, assessed with a validated OSATS

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review argues that EVH training should use a surgical skill curriculum training tool that is "structured, reliable and rigorously validated to be incorporated into an objective clinical assessment". It specifies programme elements: theoretical knowledge reinforcing vein integrity preservation, equipment training to improve hand-eye coordination, and gradual introduction to clinical practical skills. Every step should be evaluated using validated Objective Structured Assessment of Technical Skills (OSATS), which the review states have proven reliability and construct validity for minimally invasive surgical skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Validated OSATS evaluation at every step
- Both theoretical and technical knowledge plus performance evaluation in the curriculum
- Benchmark criteria to allow progression
#### Constraints
- The review notes structured competency-based surgical training tools do not yet exist for EVH and require validation of tasks, experience levels, duration and frequency

### Target Learners
- Novice EVH practitioners in cardiothoracic surgical training

### Learning Goals
- Technical operative skill and preservation of vein conduit quality
