# Analytical approach to strategy training: detailed step-by-step strategy presentation combined with integration of strategies across modules and practice tasks

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article implements and tests an analytical approach to strategy training that "combined a detailed, step-by-step presentation of each strategy with the integration of various strategies across modules and practice tasks." Rather than presenting strategies globally, each strategy is broken down and taught step by step, and learners then practice combining multiple strategies across course modules and tasks. The approach was delivered by course instructors within a digital literacy course.
