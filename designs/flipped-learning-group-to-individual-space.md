# Flipped learning: first contact with new concepts moves from group to individual learning space

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Flipped learning, as defined in Talbert's 2017 book quoted by the report, is "a pedagogical approach in which first contact with new concepts moves from the group learning space to the individual learning space in the form of structured activity", transforming class time into a dynamic, interactive environment where the educator guides students applying concepts. Students first encounter concepts before class using carefully curated sources, such as Nuclear Regulatory Commission videos and regulation pointers for a radiation-protection lesson on agreement states. The report links flipped learning's origins to just-in-time teaching's idea of pre-class work on not-yet-covered topics.
