# Pepper robot interactive module explaining inhalation technique in four steps

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An interactive module implemented on Aldebaran's Pepper robot explains the inhalation procedure to children. The verbal description of steps is accompanied by music, images on the Pepper tablet, and "suitable gestures and expressiveness, to obtain a better engagement and at the same time boost the learning process." A chatbot-based dialogue component manages interaction with children in Italian.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A Pepper robotic platform equipped with a tablet
- A chatbot-based dialogue component to manage interaction with children
#### Constraints
- The interaction is in the Italian language, since the experimentation will be conducted in Italy

### Target Learners
- children aged 6-11 diagnosed with asthma

### Learning Goals
- correct execution of the inhalation technique
