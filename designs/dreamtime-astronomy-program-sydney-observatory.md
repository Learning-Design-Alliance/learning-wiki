# Dreamtime Astronomy program at Sydney Observatory

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Dreamtime Astronomy is a 1.5-hour museum education program incorporating Australian Indigenous culture, astronomy, and Sydney's astronomical history and heritage. It was originally titled Shared Sky and highlights the diverse astronomies of Aboriginal people, exploring how they used stars for navigation, seasonal calendars, food economics, ceremony, and social structure. The program was developed to align with the Australian National Curriculum's cross-curriculum priority on Indigenous histories and culture and to increase education visits to the Observatory.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Developed within the framework of the Australian National Curriculum's cross-curriculum priority on Indigenous histories and culture
#### Constraints
- Initially based on Boorong (Wergaia) content from northwestern Victoria rather than local Sydney Aboriginal astronomy, which teachers criticised

### Target Learners
- Primary school students, Year 2 to Year 4 (aged 7 to 10)

### Learning Goals
- Appreciation of Indigenous culture and Aboriginal astronomical knowledge
- Foundational understanding of how the sky works
