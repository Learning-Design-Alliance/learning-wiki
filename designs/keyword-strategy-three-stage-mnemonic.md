# Keyword strategy: a three-stage mnemonic for learning science concepts

## Description
The keyword strategy is a mnemonic strategy first developed by Atkinson (1975) that consists of "an acoustic bond established between words and the steps of supporting this bond with visuals". Levin (1988) simplified it into three stages: saving (selecting a pronunciation-similar keyword), associating (linking the target word to the keyword via verbal repetition and an image), and retrieval (recalling the keyword and image to derive the target word's meaning). The article applies this strategy to teach ten Turkish science concepts to students with SLD.

#### Requirements
