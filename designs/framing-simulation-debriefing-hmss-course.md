# Three-phase framing–simulation–debriefing structure for an online simulation course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The HMSS course followed "the recommended three-phase approach of framing, simulation, and debriefing" across four weeks online. Week 1 framed the simulation via pre-recorded lectures and a live practice class; weeks 2–3 had three-student groups complete six cases; week 4 held debriefing with peers and teachers in 10 groups with assigned teachers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A framing phase to familiarize students with the cases before simulation begins
- A debriefing phase with peers and teachers after the simulation
#### Constraints
- During the simulation, students did not receive any support from trainers

### Target Learners
- undergraduate health management students working in small groups

### Learning Goals
- acquisition of the complete health management service process for chronic disease cases
