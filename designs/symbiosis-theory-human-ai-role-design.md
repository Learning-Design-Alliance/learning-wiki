# Symbiosis theory as a framework for designing human-machine symbiosis in online collaborative learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Symbiosis theory frames human-GAI collaboration as a mutually beneficial partnership in which learners and AI partners co-develop. The study is "Grounded in symbiosis theory" and uses it to derive a role-based intervention strategy with defined responsibilities and interaction rules. The authors state their findings validated the theory's core value for optimizing human-AI collaboration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interventions derived from the theory must assign clearly defined responsibilities to learners and AI partners
#### Constraints
- Validated in one quasi-experimental study with graduate students; the authors note further exploration is required to achieve effective human-machine symbiosis in online collaborative processes

### Target Learners
- graduate students in online collaborative learning

### Learning Goals
- human-machine co-development
- optimized human-AI collaboration
