# Framework for Simulation-based Dental Education guiding haptics curriculum development

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A framework proposed by Australian researchers to guide and support contemporary dental simulation, influenced by instructional design theory for psychomotor skill acquisition while emphasising collective impact of faculty, curriculum and facilities. The QMUL school applied it to give "a structure to the development of a haptics curriculum", aiming to meet faculty requirements for educational quality improvement and provide students experiences that translate to better education outcomes.

### Learning Goals
