# Biology educational card game for nutrition/digestion and respiration/circulation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A card game in which student-players "form and justify combinations that demonstrate a clear conceptual relationship between the terms on the included cards." The researcher selected 90 terms per topic, each repeated twice for 180 cards per set, with terms printed on four sides of 6.1-cm by 8.6-cm cards and a topic icon at the center. Rules were derived from the Philippine card games tong-its and pusoy-dos, pilot tested, and validated by eight biology teachers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must be able to justify their card combinations as showing a conceptual relationship between terms
- Cards are played in small groups during break times, with score sheets and pictures or videos submitted
#### Constraints
- The game covered only two selected biology topics: nutrition and digestion, and respiration and circulation

### Target Learners
- college biology students at Bulacan State University–Sarmiento Campus

### Learning Goals
- reinforcing biological concepts and terminology in nutrition and digestion and in respiration and circulation
