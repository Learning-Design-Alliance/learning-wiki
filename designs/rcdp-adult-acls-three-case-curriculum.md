# Three-case adult ACLS RCDP simulation curriculum (MedEdPORTAL)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A published curriculum of three in-person simulation cases for adult ACLS training using RCDP, set in a postanesthesia care unit with high-fidelity mannequins. "Scenarios in this curriculum are designed to be delivered in person and include pulseless ventricular tachycardia, unstable supraventricular tachycardia, and unstable sinus bradycardia." Each case includes simulation case files, critical-action flow charts, lab images, and a debriefing form as peer-reviewed appendices. It was designed for junior anesthesiology residents but is intended to generalize to other inpatient settings and specialties caring for adult patients.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- High-fidelity mannequins
- A resuscitation cart, a defibrillator with pads, IV pumps, and other code equipment
- Physician and nonphysician instructors trained in simulation and RCDP
- An attending anesthesiologist as content expert
#### Constraints
- Has only been used at one institution with anesthesiology residents; sample size relatively small

### Target Learners
- junior anesthesiology residents
- other staff trained in ACLS caring for adult patients

### Learning Goals
- effective teamwork and communication
- role designation
- defibrillator operation in manual mode
- team leadership
- applying ACLS algorithms
