# Blended-learning science units for third grade (changes of material; parts and functions of plants)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An instructional package combining classroom teaching with computerized lessons for two third-grade science units, taught over 25 classes in five weeks at five classes per week. Teachers used data shows connected with laptops, and "The lesson also includes worksheets used by the students to provide answers related with the use of the blended learning." The control group studied the same Ministry of Education units through traditional classroom teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers with computer readiness and ICDL and Intel training courses
- Availability of a computer lab, ready computers, and a data show in both schools
#### Constraints
- The study was limited to two schools in Bani Kenana in the second semester of the academic year 2016/2017
- The computerized material was not provided for students taught the ordinary way

### Target Learners
- third-grade male and female students

### Learning Goals
- science achievement in the units and changes of the material and the parts and functions of the plants
