# Multi-modal education framework guided by Social Cognitive Theory and Socio-Ecological Theory

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The MSWeight intervention is "a multi-modal educational intervention (MME) guided by Social Cognitive Theory, and Socio-Ecological Theory", designed to address key theoretical constructs at the individual, interpersonal, and institutional levels hypothesized to influence weight management counseling skills. The web course targets attitudes, the role-play and standardized web-patient encounter provide practice and structured feedback, and preceptor-facilitated clerkship teaching provides observation, instruction, and feedback, together intended to build skills and reinforce confidence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Curriculum elements operating at individual, interpersonal, and institutional levels together, hypothesized to provide a structured foundation for building and practicing skills
#### Constraints
- The framework was hypothesized rather than directly tested; the trial found few MME-versus-TE differences on skills outcomes

### Target Learners
- medical students

### Learning Goals
- weight management counseling skills
- self-efficacy for counseling
