# Omani nation-wide e-lab effectiveness research project design

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A nation-wide research project financed by The Research Council to measure the e-lab's effectiveness on Omani students' acquisition of practical abilities and skills. It adopts "true experimental design with Pre-Post Test Control Group Design", sampling 40% of the first basic education science population across three Omani educational regions, with each field experiment using a different e-lab type (3D, 2D, online). Seven tools are specified, including achievement, science processes and practical skills tests, attitudinal scales, a visual thinking scale and a classroom environment scale.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Equivalency of experimental and control groups on study variables; teacher training on e-lab use and research tools; installed technological infrastructure (e-lab, software, equipment, licenses)
#### Constraints
- Students use computer tablets to connect to the e-lab software and network only under teacher supervision

### Target Learners
- fourth-grade basic education students in Oman

### Learning Goals
- academic achievement
- science process skills
- scientific attitudes
- visual thinking
- practical laboratory skills
