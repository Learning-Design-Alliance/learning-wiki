# Case-based flipped learning combining pre-learning, quizzes, worksheet-guided group discussion, presentations, and reflective journals

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A course-level instructional pattern in which students study pre-learning materials and take quizzes before class, then complete illness script worksheets through collaborative group discussion of authentic clinical cases, present their work, receive feedback, and write reflective journals afterward. The article structures it as "activities before and during the class," with professors developing cases, quizzes, and materials and facilitating discussion. It is offered as a viable design for developing clinical reasoning in preclinical students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pre-learning materials uploaded at least a week before class; orientation for learners and professors; rubrics for journals and tests; professors oriented on flipped structure and facilitator role
#### Constraints
- Students and professors found the 2-hour class time insufficient; twice-weekly sessions were reported as challenging

### Target Learners
- preclinical medical students

### Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning
- collaborative learning
- illness script formation
