# Non-generative AI academic chatbot with TA-supported knowledge base and #quizme practice function

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A text-based chatbot with non-generative AI capability that sent 2-3 scheduled, customized messages per week (about 40 per semester) to students in large online courses. Messages provided "timely reminders about course requirements", customized feedback on individual progress, connections to academic supports, and an additional communication channel. When students texted in, the bot responded from a pre-programmed knowledge base; low-probability matches were flagged to the course TA, whose manual responses updated the knowledge base. A #quizme function in Government let students request multiple-choice practice quizzes before exams.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty buy-in and a course TA embedded in both the course and chatbot administration to maintain the knowledge base and review bot responses daily for quality control
- Students opted in to receiving text-based communication from the university
#### Constraints
- The #quizme function was deployed only in Government, since Microeconomics did not have exams
- Messages required timely gradebook information from the TA to customize outreach to students' performance each week

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate students in large-enrollment, asynchronous online courses at a minority-serving public university

### Learning Goals
- Course engagement and assignment completion
- Course performance and completion
- Connection to academic supports such as tutoring
