# iEN lesson pages combining video clips, avatar explanations, knowledge maps, and e-books

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Lesson pages on the iEN portal package several asynchronous learning resources for each lesson. As the article describes, the page "provides clips to explain experiments, other clips explained the lesson in an avatar video, video clips provided asynchronous teaching, a clip summarizing the lesson in a knowledge map, and an e-book". These pages supported remote teaching during the COVID-19 transition and accompany the textbook units studied.
