# 3D visualization reconstruction plus PBL as a combined teaching model for complex anatomical learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article frames the combined model as addressing two complementary deficits: 3D visualization reconstruction "simulates the three-dimensional shape of the tumor" and allows precise analysis of anatomical relationships through rotation and translation, while PBL is "an interactive, student-led, teacher-supported seminar-based teaching method focused on problem-solving". Together they convert passive lecture-based learning into engaged, spatially grounded problem-solving around real cases.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- 3D reconstruction software and case imaging data
- A problem-based seminar structure with case materials and guiding questions
#### Constraints
- Developed and evaluated only for pheochromocytoma teaching in urology residency; authors call for wider application to other diseases and educational levels with long-term evaluation

### Target Learners
- resident physicians specializing in urology

### Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning, diagnostic and surgical decision-making, anatomical spatial understanding
