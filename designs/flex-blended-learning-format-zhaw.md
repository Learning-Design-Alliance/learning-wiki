# FLEX blended learning study format with halved classroom time and video-based online self-study

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
FLEX is a blended learning study format at the Zurich University of Applied Sciences in which "on- site classroom teaching for the FLEX format was reduced by about half compared to the part -time programme, and that half was replaced with online sessions". FLEX students attend classes approximately every three weeks for two days, with self-study phases between that use Moodle and self-produced learning videos. It offers flexibility in time, place, pace, learning style and learning path, but not assessment or content.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Lecturers receive didactical and technical support for course transition and video production from the Center for Innovative Teaching and Learning
- Regular face-to-face meetings are retained to foster reflection on and consolidation of online-phase content
#### Constraints
- Flexibility is limited to time, place, pace, learning style and learning path; assessment and content are identical to the conventional format
- Students are recommended to do no more than 25 hours of outside work

### Target Learners
- Bachelor's students combining study with work or private responsibilities

### Learning Goals
- First-semester business administration knowledge (Introduction to Business Administration)
