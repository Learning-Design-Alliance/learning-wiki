# Three-day high school youth forum with competence-targeted platforms

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A high school Forum organized at the Taganrog institute of A. Chekhov during the experiment, lasting 3 days and involving 74 students of the institute participating in the experiment in active forms of work. Each day was constructed for acquisition of competences of civil activity through named platforms: a mass-media platform producing the youth newspaper «Grazhdanin» (communication competence), a debate platform (oratorical competence), the training course «My Choice», a propaganda-team competition (health saving competence), a discussion platform on youth policy (political competence), an intellectual game platform (linguistic competence) and a trust training course (tolerant competence). Forum work was based on stated principles including personal orientation, activity of subjects of education, integrity and systemacity, diagnostability, and humanization and selectivity.
