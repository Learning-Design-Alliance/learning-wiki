# Eight different boxed load phase impedances used so every student pair gets different measurement results.

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The laboratory load was changed from a wire-wound rheostat to a typical carbon composition resistor, which reduced the rheostat's large inductance effect on load impedance. The resistor's small size "enabled us to construct eight different load phase impedances that were placed in 3D printed black boxes". The boxes "insure that all students will have different measurement results which among other things would reduce cheating".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A 3D printer for the black boxes and carbon composition resistors as the load element.
#### Constraints
- The carbon composition resistor swap lessened, rather than eliminated, the inductance effect of the original wire-wound rheostat.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate electrical engineering students working in laboratory pairs

### Learning Goals
- performing power factor measurements with each pair obtaining distinct results
