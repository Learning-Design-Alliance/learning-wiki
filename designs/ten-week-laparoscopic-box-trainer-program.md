# Ten-week laparoscopic box trainer simulation program with graded time targets

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A structured laparoscopic simulation curriculum delivered on standard box trainers over ten weekly one-hour weekend sessions facilitated by two to four surgical supervisors. Students received "a laparoscopic program with ten exercises to complete; videos of the exercises to perform; and timed targets to achieve, with competent, advanced and elite levels identified", and were encouraged to reach competent times with mastery before progressing. Peer pair allocations were maintained within groups for the program duration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Weekly face-to-face sessions with surgical supervisor instruction
- Timed targets and exercise videos for each of the ten exercises
#### Constraints
- The article reports attendance declined in the mid portion of the program in both groups

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate medical students with no prior laparoscopic experience

### Learning Goals
- Basic laparoscopic psychomotor skills
