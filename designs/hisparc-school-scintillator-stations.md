# HiSPARC school-based scintillator detection stations

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Detection stations 'in the form of ski.boxes' situated on school roofs let students measure cosmic rays for the practical part of their thesis; the stations contain anthracene whose fluorescence is used for detection. Similar stations are located in the Netherlands, the U.K. and Denmark, and the detectors are partly maintained by the participating schools.
