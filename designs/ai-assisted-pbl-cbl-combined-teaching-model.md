# AI-assisted PBL-CBL combined teaching model integrating pre-class preparation, in-class simulation, and post-class consolidation phases

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a teaching model that "combines AI-assisted PBL with CBL," structured across three phases: pre-class preparation with AI-supported preview tasks, in-class implementation where "teacher-verified simulated patients are generated based on PHN cases" and students conduct simulated diagnosis and treatment in groups with AI evaluation, and post-class summary where groups present learning outcomes reviewed by teachers. The model integrates problem-centered inquiry (PBL) with real-case linkage of theory to practice (CBL), using AI to overcome constraints of time, place, and resources in traditional teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires an AI platform with intelligent Q&A and AI agent modules capable of generating simulated patients and evaluating treatment plans
- Requires teacher guidance and correction of biases in clinical reasoning during in-class discussion
- Requires distribution of learning materials and core questions before class
#### Constraints
- The single-center RCT tested the model over four weeks solely under simulated patient conditions, lacking the complexity found in real-life environments; the short duration failed to reflect sustained effects.

### Target Learners
- Medical interns in standardized clinical teaching

### Learning Goals
- Clinical reasoning ability
- Integration of theoretical knowledge into clinical decision-making
- Clinical skills in diagnosis and treatment planning
