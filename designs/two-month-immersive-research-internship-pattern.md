# Immersive two-month research-internship pattern: students are matched to a real research group's program and integrated into its work

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The program's core instructional pattern is a roughly two-month stay in which the student is embedded in an active research or technical group and works on a real project, from data analysis to detector and accelerator component design. The article states that "The student is integrated as much as possible in his or her research group" and that trainees "participate in group meetings, where they present and discuss their results in a wide professional environment." Programs span physics analysis, electronics, superconducting magnets, and advanced computing, and are matched to each student's skills and interests.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A host research group with a suitable training program and a supervisor willing to mentor the trainee
- A match between candidate profiles and the interests of the groups
#### Constraints
- Placement depends on finding a good fit and work program within a team; only students matching an available training program are selected

### Target Learners
- advanced undergraduate and graduate physics and engineering students

### Learning Goals
- research and technical skills in particle physics, accelerator technology, and scientific computing
