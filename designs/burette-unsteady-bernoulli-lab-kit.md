# Burette-based unsteady Bernoulli lab kit for remote learners

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A lab kit built around a 50 mL EISCO CH0233B acrylic burette with 0.1 mL graduations and PTFE stopcock ($19.75) mounted with a mini jeweler clamp ($5.99), using a free smartphone bubble-level app and stopwatch. Learners fill the burette, measure static column height, and time full draining; repeating the drain at least 12 times enables statistical uncertainty analysis. The kit costs less than $30 in materials, can be mailed home, and is intended as an additional course fee.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- vertical mounting verified within ±0.1° of 0°; caliper measurement of burette and stopcock diameters; 12 repeated drain runs for statistical uncertainty analysis
#### Constraints
- resolving the unsteady component requires stopcock diameter measurement accuracy better than 0.001 mm, which the authors state is future work

### Target Learners
- undergraduate engineering students
- high school physics students

### Learning Goals
- testing the unsteady Bernoulli equation
- experimental uncertainty analysis
