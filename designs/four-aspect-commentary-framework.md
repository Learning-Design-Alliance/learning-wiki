# Four-aspect commentary framework: Impact/Value, Application, Limitations and Constraints, Future Suggestions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The editors established review criteria asking responding authors to structure their commentary pieces around four aspects: 'Impact/Value', 'Application', 'Limitations and Constraints', and 'Future Suggestions'. Each aspect is phrased as a question the respondent answers from their own perspective, e.g., 'What impact or value does the paper have on "Shift to Digital" from your specific perspective?' This pattern turns a review of prior work into practice-oriented dialogue.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Respondent authors identify 2–3 manuscripts of interest and specify the perspective they will focus on
- A defined context of need, here flexible learning, online learning, and learning disruptions with limited resources
#### Constraints
- Essays were 500–1000 words and situated the 17 previously published articles within COVID-19 learning needs

### Target Learners
- educational professionals
- school administrators
- teachers

- applying published research to rapid digital shifts
