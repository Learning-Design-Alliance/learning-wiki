# Four-Stage Progressive teaching system across the 36-month residency

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
This pattern structures the three-year residency into four sequential developmental phases of increasing complexity. As the article states, the "Four-Stage Progressive" teaching system "structured the 36-month training period into four sequential developmental phases: Basic Adaptation Period (1–6 months), Capability Enhancement Period (7–12 months), Specialty Mastery Period (13–24 months), and Comprehensive Integration Period (24–36 months)." Each phase pairs stage-appropriate competencies with targeted technological interventions, from AI-powered virtual cases and 3D anatomical visualization early on to team-based consultation simulations in the final integration period.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Stage-appropriate educational content and assessment (standardized knowledge tests, OSCEs, 360-degree evaluations) tailored to each developmental phase
#### Constraints
- Designed for and evaluated within a three-year obstetrics and gynecology residency cycle

### Target Learners
- obstetrics and gynecology residents in a three-year standardized residency program

### Learning Goals
- sequential development from foundational knowledge and basic procedures to independent practice and interdisciplinary collaboration
