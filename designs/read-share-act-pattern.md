# Read-Share-Act: a three-stage extensive reading pattern of self-selected reading, rotating group sharing, and story performance

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
RSA is a classroom technique integrating extensive reading into an existing reading course through three stages: R (reading a self-selected graded reader outside class), S (sharing the story in three rounds of progressively larger groups with note-taking and journals), and A (acting the nominated story as a play). The article describes it as "a means to perform extensive reading activities". In the study it ran over 15 hours of a 15-week semester with 24 Grade-10 EFL students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students each select a book from a list of graded readers suited to EFL students
- Time allocated within normal reading instruction for sharing rounds and play preparation
#### Constraints
- Time constraints arose because RSA shared hours with teacher-directed intensive reading; a stand-alone extensive reading course is suggested
- Students lacked storytelling skills initially, reading notes aloud instead of telling stories

### Target Learners
- EFL secondary students (Grade 10)

### Learning Goals
- English reading habit
- listening, speaking and writing skills
- confidence expressing ideas in English
