# OTIL lessons delivered through Moodle modules (Forum, Glossary, Chat, Wiki, Quiz)

## Description
The OTIL lessons are online listening lessons implemented on the Moodle learning management platform, with "learning tasks and activities ... embedded in Moodle modules, such as Forum, Glossary, Chat and Quiz." Moodle was chosen after analyzing the technical environment at Tongren University and offers interactive tools supporting synchronous and asynchronous interaction, collaboration, and automatically corrected exercises with immediate feedback.
