# Conceptual framework integrating adult learning theory and constructivist pedagogy through peer learning, AI-assisted feedback, and blended delivery

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study is guided by a framework grounded in adult learning theory and constructivist pedagogy in which three instructional components interact to support research literacy, critical appraisal competence, and engagement. The article states: "The framework posits that three core instructional components—peer learning, AI-assisted feedback, and blended delivery strategies (self-paced videos, in-class tasks)—interact to support students’ development of research literacy, critical appraisal competence, and learning engagement." Peer critique promotes active knowledge construction, AI feedback provides individualized iterative refinement, and blended delivery supports autonomy and flexibility.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Thoughtful integration of the components so peer critique, AI feedback, and blended delivery reinforce one another
#### Constraints
- Hypothesized to produce superior outcomes versus lecture-based instruction; measured performance differences in this pilot did not reach statistical significance

### Target Learners
- Postgraduate health sciences students

### Learning Goals
- Research literacy
- Critical appraisal competence
- Learning engagement
