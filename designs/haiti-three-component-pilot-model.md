# Three-component pilot model combining infrastructure and technology, Creole digital content, and professional development with coaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The pilot model organizes implementation into three components. Infrastructure & Technology includes "Solar power & internet connectivity for rural schools," "Tablets for coaches, teachers & students," and classroom speakers. Digital Content comprises "Standards-aligned, culturally relevant, interactive teaching & learning applications in Haitian Creole." Professional Development includes "Teacher training on pedagogy, content knowledge & technology" and "Ongoing classroom observations & coaching." A Monitoring, Evaluation & Learning strand measures impact using qualitative and quantitative methods.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Local partner capacity for infrastructure management; funding for solar and connectivity expansion
#### Constraints
- 57 of 70 schools used offline resources only; infrastructure expansion is pending funding

### Target Learners
- Early grade primary students and teachers in rural Haitian schools

### Learning Goals
- Blended learning implementation
- improved learning outcomes in Creole science content
