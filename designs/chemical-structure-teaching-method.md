# Chemical Structure Teaching (CST): a structure-first method for pharmaceutical analysis

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
CST is a teaching method for pharmaceutical analysis that centers learning on the chemical structures of medicines. Its key idea rests on the principle that "properties are determined by structures", so identification tests, impurity tests and assays can be inferred by logical deduction from functional groups. The process is designed as "observing structures →speculating properties →determining analysis methods→verifying with textbook and the ChP", linking prior organic and analytical chemistry knowledge to pharmaceutical analysis.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students need prior knowledge in organic chemistry and analytical chemistry to deduce properties and methods from structures
#### Constraints
- The authors position CST for chemistry courses whose knowledge systems are diverse, memorization-based and focused on relationships between chemical structures and properties

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in pharmaceutical engineering, chemistry and pharmacy majors

### Learning Goals
- deducing drug properties and analytical methods from chemical structures
- critical and logical thinking
- independent problem-solving
