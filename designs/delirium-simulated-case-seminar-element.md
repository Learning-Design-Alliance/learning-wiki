# Two-hour delirium seminar combining simulated case presentation with interactive debriefing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A two-hour teaching seminar at a Barcelona university hospital in which 131 physiotherapy and medicine students watched a simulated clinical case of delirium in an older person — either as a live theatrical performance by three actors or as a pre-recorded video of the same case and actors — followed by open questions discussed in groups, teacher feedback via a powerpoint presentation, and pre/post questionnaires on theoretical knowledge (score 0–7) and subjective learning perception (score 0–20). Questionnaires were corrected by geriatrics experts blinded to group assignment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A written case summary given beforehand, agreed correction criteria for theoretical questions, and expert-blinded marking
#### Constraints
- Students were not individually randomised; each programme attended on separate days in reduced groups chosen at random, with programmes kept separate

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physiotherapy and medicine students

### Learning Goals
- theoretical knowledge of delirium: definition, predisposing and precipitating factors, and preventive measures
