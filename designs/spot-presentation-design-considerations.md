# SPOT presentation design considerations for engaging, story-driven slides

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
SPOT presentations follow a recurring design pattern: accurate, engaging, locally tailored content organized as a deliberate story line, with interactive questioning, high-quality videos and simulations, and minimal on-slide text. The article enumerates that "Essential design considerations include the need to: 1. Provide accurate information while creating an engaging and relevant content. 2. Tie all the ideas together by creating a deliberate story line." and that "SPOT presentations have almost no text," with detailed presenter notes in the PowerPoint notes section. Presenters are encouraged to spend more time on areas of special interest to bring out their own personalities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Presentations created by subject matter experts with presenter notes, then learned and practiced by presenters before delivery
- Use of the best available technology to show high-quality videos and model simulations
#### Constraints
- Presentations must be customized to the location and age group of each audience, and presenters must adjust on short notice to available on-site technologies

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in classrooms and community programs

### Learning Goals
- Engagement with current space science topics
- Understanding of complex technical topics at an age-appropriate level
