# Gagne's nine-step model of instructional design applied to teaching procedural skills

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Gagne's model derives from information processing theory, which "tries to explain how information is encoded into memory." It "considers the internal and external conditions of learning to create a practical, stepwise series of instructional events" comprising nine steps: gaining attention, informing learners of objectives, stimulating recall of prior learning, presenting new material, providing learning guidance, learner performance of the procedure, providing feedback, assessing performance, and enhancing retention and transfer. The workshop adopted this model as its organizing instructional framework.
