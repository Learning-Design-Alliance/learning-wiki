# Design Thinking as a three-step framework for postgraduate course development

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Design Thinking is a "creative problem-solving approach used in industry and education to improve user experiences", often framed as stages or mindsets such as empathise, define, ideate, prototype, test. The authors applied it to postgraduate course development by dividing their work into three steps: "understanding our students, defining the problem, and implementing solutions", operationalised as an alumni questionnaire, affinity mapping into problem themes, and trialled interventions in the following academic year.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The approach requires understanding the user before understanding their problems, and understanding the problem before solving it, as the article states.
#### Constraints
- The article applies the framework to a single postgraduate programme; it notes several variant framings of the stages exist across sources.

### Target Learners
- postgraduate space science and technology students (MSc SS&T cohorts)
