# Three-activity self-paced lesson sequence: interactive app practice, written proof exercise, then skill-based competitive game

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The individualized lesson was structured as three sequenced activities through which students progressed at their own pace: solving 10 equations in the Algebra Touch application with interactive tools, a written exercise with the problem distributed as a PDF in iTunesU requiring a full solution with proof in notebooks, and the Orbit Integers game in which up to four students compete on one racetrack. Finishing one activity visibly signalled the transition to the next, making progress easy to monitor.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each student needs a tablet with access to the iTunesU course, and notebooks for the written activity; the teacher sets rules for notebook work at the start.
#### Constraints
- The game component requires an internet connection to place up to four students on one racetrack.

### Target Learners
- 12-13-year-old students in a regular mathematics classroom

### Learning Goals
- practicing simple equations
- developing general math skills needed for solving equations
