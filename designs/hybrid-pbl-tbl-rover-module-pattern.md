# Hybrid PBL+TBL rover-design module pattern for large engineering cohorts

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A module-level design in which students in teams of eight develop a smart rover that accomplishes communications, sensing and imaging tasks within a predefined budget (RMB1000) and a fixed time-frame (17 weeks), with no examination component. The article states it "designed a module that mixes both team-based and project-based learning, which can be effectively delivered to large student cohorts", with interpersonal skills developed as by-products of the activity rather than taught directly.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multiple instructors (three convenors) moderating the 40 teams
- A detailed module handbook with task descriptions, assessment mechanisms, mark schemes and deadlines uploaded to the VLE
- A technology-enabled authentic challenge (the rover project)
#### Constraints
- The article notes managing 320 students divided into 40-50 teams by a single instructor is simply infeasible, so the pattern depends on multiple instructors

### Target Learners
- Third-year undergraduate electronic engineering students

### Learning Goals
- Systems engineering design skills
- Teamwork, project management and communication skills
- Decision making, troubleshooting and planning on ill-defined problems
