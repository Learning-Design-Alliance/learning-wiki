# QLP modular curriculum: six core modules with defined competencies and four specialization pathways

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The curriculum is organized into six core modules — QPhenom, QDesign, QSoftware, QHardware, QSystems, and QSociety — each aligned with QLP tiers and defined by explicit learning competencies and credit values (Table II). The article notes modules "may be offered as stand-alone courses or combined into sequences" and supports four specialization pathways: Quantum Theory, Quantum Engineering, Quantum Software, and Quantum Applications. Stackable micro-credentials such as Quantum Programming Foundations support workforce alignment, and the curriculum is explicitly aligned with the ACM/IEEE CS2023 "AR-Quantum: Quantum Architectures" knowledge unit.
