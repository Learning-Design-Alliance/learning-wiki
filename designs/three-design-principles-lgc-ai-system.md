# Three design principles for an AI system facilitating LGC-based learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
From a literature review on LGC, self-determined learning, and second-language strategies, the authors derived three design principles: learners are "creators capable of generating their own contexts and studying autonomously based on such contexts, through personalized learning assistance and continuous interactions with resources"; the system provides personalized support for determining learning content, plans, and strategies; and an open platform of learning resources with a content curation tool encourages interaction with resources and context expansion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Functionalities include mobile and web-based anytime/anywhere learning, learner-generated content, OERs and online media resources, multisensory strategy choice, multimedia and natural language processing technologies, and an intelligent agent providing guidance without human instructors
#### Constraints
- Principles were derived for English language education in South Korea with secondary students as the priority population

### Target Learners
- Korean secondary-school students

### Learning Goals
- autonomous English learning
- learner-generated content creation
- learning strategy selection
