# Hybrid CBL-SST-simulator session design for preclinical endodontics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A six-week weekly two-hour session structure integrating Case-Based Learning, Scene Simulation Teaching, and dental simulator practice. Each session moves through pre-class online case review, a 15-minute theory review, a 30-minute "Simulated clinical encounter (CBL and SST)", 40 minutes of standardized simulator operation, 20 minutes of feedback and evaluation, and a 15-minute summary ending with a written outpatient medical record assignment. Communication is practiced using the SEGUE framework during role-play.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dental simulators and standardized patient records/role-players
- An online learning platform for pre-class case materials
- Instructors to circulate and guide posture and technique during simulator practice
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only in a pilot with 20 students in one Endodontics module at a single institution

### Target Learners
- fifth-year undergraduate dental students

### Learning Goals
- clinical thinking and differential diagnosis
- doctor-patient communication
- Class II cavity preparation and access opening skills
