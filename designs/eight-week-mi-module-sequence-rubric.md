# Eight-week MI module sequence with assignments and compact assessment rubric

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper provides reusable curriculum artifacts: an eight-week module sequence aligned to the materials discovery workflow, three assignments (descriptor benchmarking, leakage and dataset-shift diagnosis, uncertainty-aware active learning), and a compact 0-2 scale rubric. The rubric translates 'workflow-aligned AI literacy for materials discovery' into observable performance indicators across seven competencies from data provenance to ethics and equity awareness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Deployable within existing materials science curricula as an upper-division elective module, graduate mini-course, workshop, bootcamp sequence, or cross-institution short course
#### Constraints
- Adaptable to local constraints such as credit hours, computing access, student preparation, and available datasets; assignments use public datasets such as Materials Project-derived datasets or OQMD subsets

### Target Learners
- upper-division undergraduates
- graduate students
- workshop and bootcamp participants

### Learning Goals
- documenting data provenance
- justifying representations
- evaluating model validity
- interpreting uncertainty
- connecting AI outputs to scientific judgment
