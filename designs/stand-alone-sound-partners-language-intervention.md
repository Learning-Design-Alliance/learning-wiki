# Stand-alone Tier 2 intervention: Sound Partners plus Bridge of Vocabulary and Language in Motion

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A supplementary Tier 2 intervention combining three externally developed programs: Sound Partners, a scripted program providing "explicit and systematic instruction in phonemic awareness, phonics, spelling, and reading"; Bridge of Vocabulary, a semiscripted vocabulary program using manipulatives and discussion; and Language in Motion, a scripted program building syntax and listening comprehension through science-based manipulatives, interactive stories, and games. It was delivered as a 25–30-minute daily reading component plus a 15-minute daily language component.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interventionists administer skill assessments after every 10 Sound Partners lessons and repeat lessons when skills are not mastered
- Two-day training in late September plus manuals and instructional materials
#### Constraints
- No WWC-rated language intervention programs existed for at-risk K–2 students, so vocabulary and language components were selected on clinical and efficacy evidence rather than WWC ratings

### Target Learners
- K–2 students below the 30th percentile in vocabulary and reading-related skills

### Learning Goals
- Phonemic awareness, phonics, spelling, word reading, vocabulary, syntax, and listening comprehension
