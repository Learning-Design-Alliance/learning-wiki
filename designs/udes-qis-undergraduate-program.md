# Quantum Information Science undergraduate program at Université de Sherbrooke

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A specialized bachelor's degree launched in fall 2022, focused on quantum software development and designed to train quantum software developers in three and a half years. The curriculum combines mathematics, physics and computer science foundations with integrative project courses, professional development courses and three mandatory internships. By fall 2024 the program had "three active cohorts with over 50 enrolled students", and the first cohort had completed first internships at governmental agencies, academic institutions, research institutes, startups and multinational corporations.

## Design Implications
