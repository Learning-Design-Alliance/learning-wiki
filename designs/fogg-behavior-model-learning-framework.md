# Fogg Behavior Model (FBM) as a framework for designing conflict-management e-learning

## Description
The Fogg Behavior Model explains behavior change through three factors — motivation, ability and prompt — with motivation and ability able to "reinforce and compensate for each other". Prompts are delivered as a spark (motivational initiation), a facilitator (emphasizing ease), or a signal (a simple reminder). In education, FBM helps planners "think systematically about motivational elements, simplicity, and strategies used to change student performance". This trial operationalized FBM as motivation (attractive infographics, motivational mini-lectures, evidence-based statistics), ability (animated clips, completed negotiation worksheets), and prompts (polling, immediate feedback, educational SMS on WhatsApp).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learners must have motivation and commitment to learning goals as prerequisites to developing new behaviors
- An e-learning platform and delivery channel (e.g., messaging apps) able to send prompts at specified intervals
#### Constraints
- The article notes not all individuals successfully modify their behavior during the learning process despite motivation

### Target Learners
- second-year (pre-clinical) medical students

### Learning Goals
- positive attitudes toward conflict management in healthcare teams
- conflict management behavior change
