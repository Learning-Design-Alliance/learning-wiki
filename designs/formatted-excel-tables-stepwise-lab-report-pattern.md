# Structured lab workflow: formatted excel tables, step-by-step in-class calculations, and detailed lab reports

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each experiment follows a recurring instructional pattern: formatted excel data tables are provided to students, step-by-step calculations with excel are demonstrated during the live class, and students submit a detailed lab report per experiment in which all excel functions are included below each table. The abstract notes "Formatted excel tables were given to students and step by step calculations with excel were done during the class." Tables scaffold the full analysis chain from raw readings through uncertainty propagation to rounded final results with percent error or percent difference comparisons.
