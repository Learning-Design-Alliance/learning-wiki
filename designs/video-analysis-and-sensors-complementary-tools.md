# Video analysis and smartphone sensors as complementary laboratory tools with distinct measurement strengths

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes that video analysis and smartphone sensors are two complementary technologies for physics laboratories. As a rule, "video analysis provides distances or angular variables, while sensors supplies velocity or acceleration (either linear or angular)". Used together in specific setups they can corroborate the coherence of different measures or obtain different physical quantities, and their comparison in class supports discussion of experimental and numerical pros and cons.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An app is needed to communicate with the smartphone hardware sensors and record readings
- Video analysis requires a digital recording of the experiment and software such as Tracker
#### Constraints
- Physical quantities obtained with numerical schemes from video analysis are considerably noisier than those obtained with sensors
- Measures obtained with the linear acceleration sensor were found to be inaccurate near the point of stable equilibrium

### Target Learners
- high school students
- undergraduate physics laboratory students

### Learning Goals
- understanding physical phenomena through measurement
- understanding experimental and numerical aspects of measurement techniques
