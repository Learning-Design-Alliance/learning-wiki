# PBL + CBL dual-track model for lymphoma subspecialty physician training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes a dual-track teaching model in which PBL drives knowledge acquisition through complex clinical problems while CBL provides authentic cases as a context for application. It states that "The combination of PBL (driving knowledge acquisition) and CBL (providing a context for application) is highly suitable for lymphoma, a specialty defined by fine pathological typing, highly individualized treatment, and reliance on multidisciplinary team (MDT) collaboration". The model aims to enhance independent diagnosis and treatment capability, critical thinking, and evidence-based decision-making.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Carefully designed problem chains and selected real cases aligned with lymphoma training goals
- Facilitators with advanced instructional skills including curriculum design, guided facilitation, and timely effective feedback
#### Constraints
- The model is proposed as a conceptual framework; empirical validation through future controlled implementations is planned but not yet conducted

### Target Learners
- Residents in standardized training in the lymphoma subspecialty of hematology

### Learning Goals
- Independent diagnosis and treatment capability
- Critical thinking
- Evidence-based decision-making
- MDT collaboration
