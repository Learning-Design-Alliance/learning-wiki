# Lecture/Studio (New Studio) course structure: retained lectures with fused studio sessions replacing separate lab and recitation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Lecture/Studio format is a hybrid of the traditional lecture/lab/recitation structure and fully restructured formats such as Workshop Physics, Studio Physics, and SCALE-UP. "The lecture component is retained and meets twice a week for 50 minutes at a time, laboratory and recitation are fused into a single 110-minute studio session that also meets twice a week." Each lecture and its following studio form a tightly linked module; the weekly cycle begins with a Just-in-Time Teaching reading assignment whose feedback shapes the lecture, and studio groups of 3-4 work on PALS activities. Because students are not all in studio simultaneously, existing laboratory rooms suffice, keeping TA needs similar to the prior format.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Studio rooms converted from former introductory physics laboratories, used for multiple sections throughout the week
- Instructors meet weekly with the full instructional team to prepare for upcoming studios
#### Constraints
- Teaching these classes requires more time and effort from faculty than an upper-division course they have taught before; the burden can be reduced by offering a reduced teaching load after several semesters

### Target Learners
- large-enrollment undergraduate introductory physics students, including life-science majors

### Learning Goals
- conceptual development
- problem-solving skills
- taking and analyzing data
