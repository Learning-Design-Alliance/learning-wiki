# Four-module POCUS training programme for haemodialysis nurses (three online lectures plus face-to-face practical assessment)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A blended training programme designed by a Registered Vascular Technologist and Accredited Medical Sonographer: 'The training programme (Table 1) was an interactive four module workshop for clinical staff in the use of ultrasound imaging techniques.' Modules 1–3 were recorded online lectures covering duplex ultrasonography physics, vascular access assessment, and protocol development including image sharing with the metropolitan vascular access team; module 4 was 6 hours of face-to-face practical assessment per site delivered across two visits four months apart by trained HD nurses and a sonographer. A GE Logiq V2 POCUS system was used, exporting images in DICOM and PC formats.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Face-to-face practical training with sonographer support so staff can correlate ultrasound images with vascular access anatomy
- Equipment capable of exporting assessment images (DICOM and PC formats) to communicate with the parent hospital's vascular access team
#### Constraints
- Delivery was delayed by Covid-19 and redeployment of research staff to frontline clinical care roles

### Target Learners
- Registered Nurses and Enrolled Nurses responsible for vascular access cannulation in regional haemodialysis units

### Learning Goals
- Assess arteriovenous fistula and graft vascular access with ultrasound
- Identify vessels, thrombosis, infiltration, stenosis and optimal cannulation sites
- Apply infection control and document and refer POCUS findings
