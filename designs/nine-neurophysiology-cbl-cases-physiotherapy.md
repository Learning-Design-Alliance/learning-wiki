# Nine published clinical cases for CBL in applied neurophysiology

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A curated set of nine clinical cases used as the CBL material in the neuroscience component of applied physiology, each mapped to explicit course objectives. The article states: "Nine cases were selected through online search to match the specific objectives and contents of the neuroscience physiology course." Cases included Brown-Séquard syndrome with Horner's syndrome, diabetic polyneuropathy, thalamic infarction with Cheiro-Oral syndrome, referred pain, pain analgesia, post-COVID Babinski sign, ankle clonus, hung-up knee jerk, and post-COVID facial palsy, each with relevant course objectives and a published reference.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cases reviewed for relevance, comprehensiveness, and potential to stimulate critical thinking and discussion
- Case-related materials and reading resources made available to students in advance on Microsoft Teams for pre-reading
#### Constraints
- Cases were selected to match the specific objectives and contents of the neuroscience physiology course, so the set is tied to that curriculum

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physiotherapy students in a fourth-semester neuroscience course

### Learning Goals
- relating neurophysiology concepts to clinical manifestations
- critical analysis of clinical cases
