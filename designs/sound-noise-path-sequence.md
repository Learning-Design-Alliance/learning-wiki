# Sound-and-noise interdisciplinary path sequence: waves and sound, phonetics, psychoacoustics, noise, signal-to-noise ratio

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A unit-level sequence that starts by introducing waves and characterizing sound as a particular wave, then studies human sound production and phonemes, then how sound is perceived and transduced by the ear, arriving at a shared definition of noise, and ends by extending noise to physical measurements via signal-to-noise ratio. Activities are designed for school and outdoor settings with open-source software, and later issues can be shaped on students' interests such as environment, pollution, health and music.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- careful designing of all activities
- collaboration between physics and science teachers, and with mathematics teachers for harmonic functions and spectral decomposition
#### Constraints
- the pilot showed limits when run as an optional laboratory with activities too distributed in time

### Target Learners
- secondary scientific high-school students

### Learning Goals
- characterizing sound waves, phonemes and noise
- understanding resonance, transduction and signal-to-noise ratio
