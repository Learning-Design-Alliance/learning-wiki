## Description
The article enumerates the course's key features associated with increasing student achievement: "(a) in-depth focus on science content; (b) opportunities for teachers to engage in active learning; (c) coherence and alignment between the teacher curriculum and standards-based student curricula; (d) substantial duration and length of contact time, 24 hours over five days; and (e) a process of collective participation during which teachers engage in professional discourse and critical reflection." The course is built around hands-on science investigations and discussions of narrative teaching cases drawn from actual classroom episodes.
