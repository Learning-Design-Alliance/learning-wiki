# Physics of Music course structured as five guided inquiry labs plus one open-ended project with escalating epistemic agency

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The course uses six two-week laboratory activities: 'The first five activities were structured as guided inquiry labs. The final activity was an open-ended project in which student groups chose a topic to study, specifically designed to promote students' epistemic agency.' Each activity is delivered as a starter Jupyter notebook with instructional text and starter Python code, and each concludes with a Computational Letter submitted through a revise-and-resubmit process with a single-point specifications rubric.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires lab activities that vary in the degree to which they leverage students' existing disciplinary knowledge
#### Constraints
- Designed for a general education physics course tailored to music majors with college algebra prerequisite

### Target Learners

### Learning Goals
- Understanding the nature of sound
- Connecting physics concepts to musical practice
