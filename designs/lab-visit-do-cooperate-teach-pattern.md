# Out-of-school lab sequence combining learning-by-doing, cooperative learning in mixed-school groups, and learning-by-teaching on return to class

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A reusable activity pattern for research-lab visits: students execute the experiment actively (learning-by-doing), groups are often composed of students from different schools so they share knowledge toward the common goal (cooperative learning), and afterwards students re-elaborate and synthesize what they learned for classmates (learning-by-teaching). The article states: "L'approccio utilizzato è il learning-by-doing in quanto lo studente è parte attiva nell'esecuzione dell'esperienza" and "viene adottata la strategia del learning-by-teaching in quanto gli studenti devono relazionare ai pari l'esperimento condotto".
