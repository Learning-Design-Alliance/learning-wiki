# GenAI-integrated professional development program embedding ethical reasoning for EAP educators

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A field-tested professional development program for higher education EAP educators that integrates generative AI training with ethical reasoning. The article states the study "designed, implemented, and evaluated a GenAI-integrated Professional Development program that embeds ethical reasoning within a multicultural Global South institution," engaging 97 educators in quantitative testing and 15 in qualitative interviews. The authors state the model "can be adapted for use in other contexts."

## Design Implications

### Context
