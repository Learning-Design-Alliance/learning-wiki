# Flipped classroom as an inversion of knowledge delivery explained via zone of proximal development and cognitive-process staging

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The article describes the flipped classroom as "a blended learning model that inverts the traditional teaching process" in which knowledge delivery shifts to before class and classroom time is used for interaction, case discussion, and problem-solving. It explains the observed advantages through the "zone of proximal development" theory: lower-order cognitive processes occur before class and higher-order processes (analyzing, synthesizing, evaluating, creating) occur in class, "thereby achieving knowledge internalization and transfer". This is the framework the authors use to interpret their pooled results.
