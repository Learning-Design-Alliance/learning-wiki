# Pairing social-emotional learning with interactive storybook reading and phonological awareness activities in preschool

## Description
REDI's instructional design combines an SEL curriculum with structured early-literacy activities: "an interactive storybook reading and retelling approach, along with phonological awareness training and alphabet center activities." The pattern pairs self-regulation and emotional-control teaching with language and print activities in the same preschool day.
