# Teach physics students with researchers who actively do science themselves

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article presents the core organizing principle of education at SPT: instruction is delivered by working scientists rather than by career lecturers. The authors state that "The nuclear students were taught by professionals – those people from KIPT, who did science themselves. This was and remains the crucial point in arranging the education at SPT by now." This is extended by visiting lecturers, mostly SPT graduates now leading experts abroad, and by department heads who bring the most recent results of their research into teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Lecturers must hold active research positions at research institutes (at SPT, more than 25% of professors are part-time employed at KIPT), so that course content reflects current research activity
#### Constraints
- The model depends on a co-located partner research institute (KIPT) and, since 2022, has been strained by war conditions and destruction of the SPT building

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and graduate physics students

### Learning Goals
- quantum physics and modern physical research competences
