# The OTIL Model: a six-phase, 17-step instructional design model for online task-based interactive listening

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The OTIL Model is a systematic, web-based instructional design model for English listening instruction that combines a task-based approach with interactive online learning. It comprises 6 phases and 17 steps: identify setting, set instructional goals, design lessons, produce the online instructional package, conduct developed lessons, and evaluate. The article states it is "an online interactive listening instruction design with task-based approach" and "a learner-centered instruction design, emphasizing interaction."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Web-based activities such as Forum, Glossary, Chat, Wiki, and Quiz are required so that learner-instructor, learner-learner and learner-content interaction and immediate feedback are supported
#### Constraints
- The model was developed and evaluated for college English listening teaching in China; the experts noted weaknesses in phase names, step explanations and the flow of the model, which were revised

### Target Learners
- second-year non-English major undergraduate EFL learners

### Learning Goals
- English listening comprehension
