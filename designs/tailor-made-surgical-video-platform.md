# Tailor-made interactive surgical video platform for undergraduate online-only surgical education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An interactive online platform developed with the faculty's audiovisual media center to teach operative techniques and skills. Surgical procedures were videorecorded in the operating theater and processed into an interactive video format, organized around representative surgical cases and procedures across seven topic sessions. Students received platform access one week before each online tutorial, followed by a ten-question multiple-choice exam and a 60-minute online tutorial in which three visceral surgeons discussed the exam and watched the video with both groups.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Informed written consent from students and patients whose surgeries were recorded
- Educators trained and assisted by an audiovisual media center
- Videos discussed in detail by three experienced surgeons to ensure quality
#### Constraints
- No preceding peer review process of the videos was implemented
- Offered free of charge only to students of the authors' faculty

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students in surgery courses

### Learning Goals
- theoretical surgical basics
- operative techniques and skills
- case-based comprehension of surgical procedures
