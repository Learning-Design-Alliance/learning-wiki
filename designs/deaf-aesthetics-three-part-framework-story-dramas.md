# Three-part theoretical framework linking deaf ontologies, axiologies, and epistemologies to ocularcentrism, multimodality, and interactivity in story drama pedagogy

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article develops a multi-level theoretical framework in which deaf ontologies, axiologies, and epistemologies inform pedagogy and curriculum through three components of deaf aesthetics. Ocularcentrism affirms learning through vision, interactivity emphasizes moral orientation toward mutual understanding, and multimodality names visual-gestural-tactile pedagogies. The framework organizes the study's coding of story drama sessions: "a multi-level theoretical framework includes deaf ontologies, axiologies, epistemologies, and components of deaf aesthetics".
