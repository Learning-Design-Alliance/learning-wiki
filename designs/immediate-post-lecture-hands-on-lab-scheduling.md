# Immediate post-lecture 75-minute hands-on lab scheduling pattern

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course pairs each weekly topic with a 75-minute hands-on lab section carried out immediately after the topic is discussed in class. The authors state that "Performing a lab immediately after the topic is taught ensures that the information is still fresh in students' minds" and that real-time observation of results gives instant feedback that lets students identify and correct misconceptions. Labs run in a Linux cluster lab with pre-installed Python 3, Qiskit, and Jupyter in a virtual environment.
