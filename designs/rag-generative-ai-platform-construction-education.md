# Retrieval-augmented generative AI learning platform for construction education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A web-based generative AI–supported learning platform developed for construction education that "enables document-grounded interaction with course materials and generates source-linked responses aligned with curated instructional resources." It uses a retrieval-augmented generation architecture combining semantic retrieval with large language model generation to constrain responses to course-specific content. Prior evaluations included expert review of accuracy and relevance, automated grounding assessment, and a formative classroom usability evaluation; the same configuration and knowledge base served both AI conditions in the experiment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A curated course-aligned knowledge base and prior evaluation establishing response reliability and instructional alignment
#### Constraints
- Responses are constrained to course-specific content via the RAG architecture

### Target Learners
- undergraduate construction engineering students

### Learning Goals
- self-directed review of construction course concepts with source-linked explanations
