# Klamen et al.'s online video-based critical clinical competency curriculum for deliberate clinical reasoning practice

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review describes a mediated-experience artifact: an "online video-based critical clinical competency curriculum where medical students practice clinical reasoning deliberately with 144 distinct patient cases." Students stop to think through each new piece of patient information, rule differential diagnoses in and out while explaining justifications, then compare their reasoning with expert panels of different specialties. It is freely available online to any medical learners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Designers must differentiate between what clinical experiences can be mediated online and what cannot, such as night-shift work and interacting with different healthcare professionals.
#### Constraints
- The review notes some clerkship experiences are hard to mediate online; this curriculum addresses cases students cannot possibly see and reasoning practice that busy hospital workflows preclude.

### Target Learners
- third-year medical students in clerkship rotations

### Learning Goals
- deliberate practice of clinical reasoning, differential diagnosis, and treatment planning
