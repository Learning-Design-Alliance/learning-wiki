# Just Dance Now exergame with ClassDojo gamification platform and ad hoc Excel game board as the intervention's technological toolkit

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The intervention's technological toolkit comprised the Just Dance Now exergame (chosen because it can be applied in the facilities of the participating schools and is based on accessible materials: laptop, screen projector, Internet, and smartphone), the ClassDojo virtual platform for contextual gamification with badges and points accumulated and consultable at any time, and an ad hoc digital game board designed in Microsoft Excel where students entered their dance scores. Ten exergame dances were selected from 300 dances on the Just Dance Now web platform based on motor difficulty, cultural variety and adjusting values to Primary Education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Wi-Fi connectivity, facilities, laptop, screen projector, Internet, and smartphone
#### Constraints
- Wi-Fi signal interruptions at all four schools prevented the exergame activity from continuing
- smartphone availability limited devices so some students danced without a phone and could not score points

### Target Learners
- primary school students in Physical Education (grades 5-6, aged 10-12)

### Learning Goals
- dance skills
- motivation toward Physical Education
