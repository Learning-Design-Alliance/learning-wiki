# Dynamic business-simulation mechanism: generative AI shifts simulation-based learning from executing predetermined rules to decision-making under uncertainty

## Description
The third of the article's three proposed mechanisms. The article states that "The dynamic business-simulation mechanism shifts simulation-based learning from the execution of predetermined rules to decision-making under uncertainty." Generative AI dynamically generates simulation scenarios and responses, so students practice decision-making in contexts that resemble real-world complexity rather than following scripted classroom simulations.
