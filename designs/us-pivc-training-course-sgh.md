# US-PIVC training course for foundation doctors at St George's Hospital

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A pilot ultrasound-guided peripheral intravenous cannulation training course consisting of four sessions (n = 16, n = 20, n = 22, n = 25), with each cohort receiving two sessions over a 2-month period from October 2023 to January 2024. Each session combined a 15-minute introductory lecture on basic ultrasound principles with a 45-minute small-group live practice on a model using a Sonon 300c portable handheld ultrasound unit with the Sonon app on an Apple iPad 9. Teaching was delivered by one core anaesthetic trainee and three internal medicine trainees, with a repeat session a month after the first.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Written consent was obtained for all participants of this study
- equipment: portable handheld ultrasound unit and tablet app
- facilitators: one core anaesthetic trainee and three internal medicine trainees
#### Constraints
- The course was piloted at a single large tertiary centre and was not yet incorporated into foundation programme curricula

### Target Learners
- foundation year 1 and year 2 doctors
- trust-grade doctors

### Learning Goals
- ultrasound-guided peripheral intravenous cannulation skill
- confidence with difficult venous access
