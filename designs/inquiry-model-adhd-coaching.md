# Inquiry model of ADHD coaching

## Description
The article describes ADHD coaching as an "inquiry" model that contrasts with didactic academic services such as tutoring or strategy instruction. Rather than telling students what to do, "coaches use speciﬁc types of questions to model effective executive functioning and to elicit students' own ideas as they increase their capacity to clarify, plan, and take action on goals." Coaches' questions promote reflection and more accurate self-awareness, after which coaches hold clients accountable for acting on their plans, so students learn what supports or restricts their goal attainment.
