# Five-phase protocol to develop a theory-based global CPD impact instrument

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article plans a research and development project in five phases. "This research and development project will be carried out in five phases": phase 1 identifies and critically appraises existing instruments from a systematic review; phase 2 builds a new global instrument via a seven-person Development Committee and an e-Delphi process to check face validity, acceptability and utility; phase 3 assesses reliability (test-retest with 50 physicians) and validity (before-and-after study with a randomly selected control group); phase 4 explores acceptability through interviews and focus groups; phase 5 uses consensus-building methods to develop an implementation plan with decision makers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A seven-person Development Committee of researchers, CPD decision makers and an external expert
- An e-Delphi process to verify acceptability and missing items in phase 2
#### Constraints
- The French version is developed first; an English version is created only after the instrument is found valid and reliable, using translation and back-translation methods

### Target Learners
- physicians in Québec attending accredited CPD activities

### Learning Goals
- assessing the impact of CPD activities on clinical practice behaviour
