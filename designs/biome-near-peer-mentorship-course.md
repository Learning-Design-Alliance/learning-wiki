# BIOME near-peer mentorship seminar course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
BIOME is a co-taught, one-unit, pass/no pass weekly 50-minute Fall seminar restricted to first-year biology majors, consisting of "ten, 50-minute meetings where groups of six first-year mentees meet with an upper-division student mentor". Each meeting is built on a short (20–45 minute) reading and reflection assignment; mentors direct table discussion while instructors run class-wide framing. Enrollment was optional (~110–150 per cohort), and mentors received one unit of credit for a parallel weekly training seminar emphasizing facilitation and feedback rather than content tutoring.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Academically successful upper-division biology mentors who complete a 10-week mentorship training seminar
- Weekly reading and reflection assignments to structure mentor-led discussion
#### Constraints
- Enrollment was optional, creating self-selection; the article notes the instrument and program may be uniquely tailored to the academic context of the biology major at this institution

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate biology majors at a large public university

### Learning Goals
- academic soft skills including growth mindset, time management, and reflective and adaptive study skills
