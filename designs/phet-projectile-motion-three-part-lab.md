# PhET projectile-motion lab analyzing three launch configurations and extracting g from graph slopes

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Experiment 5 uses the PhET projectile-motion simulator to analyze three projectile types: horizontal launch from height h, inclined launch from ground level, and inclined launch from height h. Students measure time of flight and range, calculate initial velocity and gravitational acceleration, and compare analytical with graphical results. The objective states "Gravitational acceleration is calculated by using part-c. Analytical results are compared with graphical results." Students also fit range versus angle with a parabola to find the angle for maximum range using -(b/2a).

#### Requirements

### Target Learners

### Learning Goals
- two-dimensional kinematics, decomposition of motion into horizontal and vertical axes, graphical determination of g and optimal launch angle
