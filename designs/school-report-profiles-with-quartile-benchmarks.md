# School report pattern: summary profiles plus detailed scale distributions benchmarked against similar and quartile schools

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report follows a two-part design: Part I gives five profile graphs, one per essential support, offering a quick comparison of the school with all participating schools and with similar schools matched on IGAP scores and school size; Part II details each scale with item-level endorsement percentages, an interpretation table for scale scores, and distributions comparing the school with top and bottom quartile schools. This design is intended to alert schools to possible strengths and weaknesses and guide deeper examination.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Comparison groups of schools similar with respect to IGAP scores and school size, and sufficient respondent counts per scale
- A separate guide on how to read the report and familiarity with the Self-Analysis Guide are recommended before beginning the analysis.
#### Constraints
- The report advises reading a separate guide on how to read the report and the Self-Analysis Guide before beginning analysis
- The report is intended to assist schools in assessing strengths and weaknesses and improvement efforts, not to prescribe actions.

### Target Learners
- school staff and Local School Council members conducting school self-analysis
- school staff and Local School Council members conducting self-analysis

### Learning Goals
- diagnosing school strengths and weaknesses to guide improvement efforts
- school self-assessment and improvement planning
