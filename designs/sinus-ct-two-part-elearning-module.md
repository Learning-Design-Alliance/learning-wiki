# Two-part sinus CT e-learning module (anatomy/imaging basics plus CLOSE criteria introduction)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A purpose-built two-part e-learning module. Part one contained a review of paranasal sinus anatomy, imaging basics, sinus CT anatomy, CT findings of anatomical variants and their clinical relevance, delivered as slides with theoretical content and CT examples. Part two introduced the CLOSE criteria, explaining the structures on CT example images and their clinical relevance. It was sent by e-mail and used without time limit.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A computer with a clinical image viewing program for students to scroll through CT scans in multiple planes
#### Constraints
- Studied only in ten students of one medical faculty with no control group

### Target Learners
- fifth-year medical students without prior radiology training

### Learning Goals
- identify anatomical variants in paranasal sinus CT scans
