# Dialogic teaching theory as scaffolding framework for AI tutors

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors formulate their scaffolding framework upon dialogic teaching theory (Alexander, 2006), in which "the tutor encourages exchange of ideas using follow-up questions, clues, elaborations, or recaps." SingaKids instantiates this by sampling synthetic dialogue from a stronger teacher LLM to train smaller models to provide scaffolded interactions, and by recognizing individual learner characteristics to adjust pedagogical approach dynamically.
