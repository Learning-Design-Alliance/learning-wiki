# NLP- and deep-learning-based educational chatbot for teaching Logo to secondary school learners

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article presents an experimental educational chatbot built on recent Natural Language Processing and deep learning techniques, intended to instruct secondary school learners Logo, an educational programming language. It was "implemented and evaluated in Moroccan public schools" with support from teachers of the Regional Center for Education and Training Professions of Souss Massa. Its intended function is providing replies suited to each learner's specific needs within an e-learning setting.
