# Workplace-based learning program (WBLP) integrating mini-CEX and DOPS with immediate feedback into an internal medicine clerkship

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (4 for, 2 against) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
A workplace-based learning program layered onto a standard 9-week internal medicine clerkship across six subspecialty departments. It features "structured formative assessments every 1–2 weeks using mini-Clinical Evaluation Exercises (mini-CEX) and Direct Observation of Procedural Skills (DOPS), with immediate feedback provided after each assessment". Twelve senior attending supervisors delivered assessments after an 8-hour consensus training workshop covering rating forms, formative intent, and structured feedback models such as ask-tell-ask, with calibration via video-recorded encounters.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained supervisors: an 8-hour consensus training workshop on mini-CEX/DOPS rating forms, formative intent, and high-quality feedback principles
- Regular assessment cadence (every 1–2 weeks) with immediate feedback after each assessment
#### Constraints
- Evaluated in a single-center retrospective cohort of fourth-year internal medicine clerks; the study was not designed to disentangle the effects of individual tools, feedback frequency, or supervisor characteristics

### Target Learners
- Fourth-year undergraduate medical students on internal medicine clerkships

### Learning Goals
- Clinical competence: medical knowledge, history taking, clinical reasoning, physical examination, procedural skills
- Professionalism, communication, and motivation
