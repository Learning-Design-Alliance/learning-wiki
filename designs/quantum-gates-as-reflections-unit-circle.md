# Introduce quantum gates both as reflections on the unit circle and as truth tables on the basis states

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A lesson-level design in which one-qubit gates are interpreted geometrically as Drehspiegelungen (reflection-rotations) of the qubit vector on the unit circle — X about the first angle bisector, Z about the horizontal axis, H about the 22.5° axis — which makes gate reversibility immediately evident. Simultaneously the gates are given as truth tables, so that "Somit kann direkt an Bekanntes aus der (klassischen) Informatik angeschlossen werden."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Truth tables with the basis states are introduced alongside the geometric view to connect to classical informatics.
#### Constraints
- For the teaching unit the X, Z and H gates suffice; the rotation R(θ) is given only for completeness.

### Target Learners
- upper-secondary informatics students

### Learning Goals
- applying simple quantum gates (X, Z, H) to given qubits and understanding gate reversibility
