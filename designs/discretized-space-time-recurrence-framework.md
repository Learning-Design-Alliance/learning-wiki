# Discretized space-time recurrence framework for classical, stochastic and quantum evolution

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's organizing framework formulates classical, stochastic and quantum evolution in a discretized space-time, where 'the evolution equations become recurrence relations that students are ﬁrst required to handle with the use of a spreadsheet'. Given any initial condition, solutions at any future time are computed and graphically represented, and the continuous limit is obtained qualitatively at scales where discretization becomes unnoticeable. The same scheme supports the paper's unified comparison of the three dynamical regimes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students handle the recurrence relations with a spreadsheet; for the stochastic section, previous knowledge of some basic elements of probability theory such as joint and conditional probability and Bayes' theorem
#### Constraints
- The article states a rigorous theory is missing while the value of the time step remains unspecified
- The project uses the simplest form of discretization of differential equations, without entering any stability problem of the numerical computation

### Target Learners
- high school students
- second-year physics and mathematics students in the Didactics of Physics course
- high school science teachers

### Learning Goals
- evolution equations of classical, stochastic and quantum systems
- the distinctive role played by probability in different contexts
