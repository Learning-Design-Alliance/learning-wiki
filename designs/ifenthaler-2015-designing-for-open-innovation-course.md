# Designing for Open Innovation: a multidisciplinary STEAM undergraduate course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Ifenthaler et al. (2015) built the undergraduate course 'Designing for Open Innovation' using STEAM methodologies. It is a multidisciplinary course including topics from Economy, Social Sciences and Environment. Students divided in teams complete different assignments that allow them to identify competences to develop, help them acquire collaboration skills, and facilitate acquisition of defined competences. The course ends with a reflective essay, and learning is achieved at three levels: individual learning, team learning, and learning from each other.
