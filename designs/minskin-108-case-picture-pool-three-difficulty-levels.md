# Pool of 108 example cases at three difficulty levels for picture scoring sessions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study develops a pool of 108 example cases, pictures of skin lesions with known diagnosis supplemented with short histories, divided evenly as "36 cases at a high difficulty level, 36 cases at a medium difficulty level and 36 cases at a low difficulty level". Difficulty levels are defined independently by two Dermatologists, and 36 cases (12 per level) are randomly allocated to each of the three scoring sessions before the study starts to assure equal difficulty. Pre-defined VAS cut-offs per difficulty level allow analysing answers corrected for picture difficulty.
