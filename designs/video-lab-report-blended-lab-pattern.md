# Blended lab course pattern: out-of-classroom video lab reports with peer evaluation replacing traditional labs

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In this blended introductory mechanics course (N=355), traditional lectures were largely replaced with online videos while retaining some formal lecturing, and laboratory exercises moved out of the classroom: students performed authentic observational activities, analyzed and modeled them with Tracker and VPython, and produced "a five-minute video lab report in the style of a short colloquium talk" uploaded to YouTube, followed by anonymous peer evaluation. The course aimed to develop understanding of physics as applicable to everyday experience and practice of scientific communication.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Two weeks per lab for activity, report preparation, and upload
- Instructional videos on preparing and evaluating video lab reports
- Peer evaluation software developed by the Georgia Tech Physics MOOC VIP team
#### Constraints
- Unlike a fully flipped course, some formal instructor lecturing was retained

### Target Learners
- large-enrollment introductory calculus-based mechanics undergraduates

### Learning Goals
- computational modeling
- real-world physics practice
- scientific communication
