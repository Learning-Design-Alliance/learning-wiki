# Effective literature search training course for medical postgraduate students

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 4-month course comprising 12 training sessions covering familiarity with medical resources and databases, search strategy design and conduct, and applying search and meta search engines, presented by a highly qualified teacher. The instrument was validated by five faculty members with Cronbach's α of 0.9 and test-retest reliability of 0.9. Assessment included researcher-made questionnaires on a 5-point Likert scale and a practice test evaluating competency in designing search strategy, formulating search questions, finding keywords, applying Boolean logic, and using search functions such as filtering.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered by a highly qualified teacher across 12 sessions within a 4-month course
- Assessed with validated instruments (content and face validity confirmed by five faculty members; Cronbach's α 0.9; test-retest 0.9)
#### Constraints
- Studied among postgraduate students at Tabriz University of Medical Sciences; authors note limited participant numbers and self-reported behavior changes as limitations

### Target Learners
- Medical postgraduate students (MSc and PhD)

### Learning Goals
- Information literacy skills including search strategy design, database familiarity, and use of search and meta search engines
