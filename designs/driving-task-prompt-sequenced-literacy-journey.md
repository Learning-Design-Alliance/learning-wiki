# Sequenced literacy units organized around a Driving Task Prompt moving through comprehension, knowledge building, genre study, synthesis, and writing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This pattern organizes curriculum units and modules around a Driving Task Prompt within a disciplinary-literacy framework. Students explore complex, diverse texts through a sequenced instructional journey that includes comprehension, knowledge building, genre study, synthesis, and writing. The design purposefully builds knowledge and supports text-based analysis and communication across genres and formats.
