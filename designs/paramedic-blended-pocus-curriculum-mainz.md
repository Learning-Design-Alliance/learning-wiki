# Tailored 25-hour blended-learning POCUS curriculum for paramedics (webinars, e-learning, on-site hands-on phase, iPOCUS mnemonic)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A target group-specific point-of-care ultrasound curriculum for German paramedics comprising 25 hours across a digital preparation phase, an on-site phase, and a digital follow-up phase. Key didactic elements were "the use of blended learning", "the integration of case-based learning using five case scenarios", and "the development and use of a structured approach to an ultrasound examination with the help of the mnemonic “iPOCUS”" (Indication, Positioning, Orientation, Correction, Ultrasound examination, Save & Speak). The preparation phase included 70 short videos and 42 single-choice questions; the on-site phase used one tutor per four participants with pre-defined checklists and at least 84 minutes of personal practice time per participant. Learning objectives were defined by emergency physicians, paramedics, educators, and ultrasound experts based on the DEGUM basic emergency sonography curriculum.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tutors and lecturers with practical and teaching experience in ultrasound diagnostics who received additional training in preparation
- Small-group workstation instruction (one tutor per four participants) with pre-defined checklists
#### Constraints
- Content was tailored to paramedics' limited prior knowledge of ultrasound-related basic principles
- The modified RUSH protocol excluded the echocardiographic sections

### Target Learners
- qualified paramedics
- emergency medical technicians

### Learning Goals
- theoretical POCUS competence (anatomy, sonography basics, orientation, structure and pathology detection)
- practical skills in focused organ examinations, the modified RUSH protocol, and ultrasound-guided peripheral intravenous access
