# Custom-configured pedagogical AI chatbot for physics problem solving (OpenAI o3-mini via Fobizz)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study's chatbot condition used OpenAI's o3-mini model accessed via the Fobizz platform, with pedagogical behavior shaped through an iterative prompt-refinement cycle. Each cycle evaluated responses to simulated student inputs against three criteria: "its ability to accurately assess a student's current understanding", posing guiding reflective questions rather than direct corrections, and a "build on what's right" principle of affirming correct reasoning before prompting reconsideration of flawed parts. The final system prompt instructed the chatbot to act as a supportive tutor guiding students toward independent problem solving and conceptual understanding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pedagogical behavior depends on meticulous prompt engineering through iterative refinement cycles evaluated against expert-tutoring criteria.
#### Constraints
- The chatbot's practical effectiveness is dependent on the quality of its pedagogical design and underlying model; the authors note current AI tutors are not yet a perfect substitute for an expert human tutor.

### Target Learners
- ninth-grade physics students

### Learning Goals
- independent problem solving
- conceptual understanding of buoyancy
