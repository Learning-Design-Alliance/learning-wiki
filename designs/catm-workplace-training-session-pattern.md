# Short, focused workplace training session combining reception and selection concept-attainment activities

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The training sessions lasted between two and two-and-a-half hours, held during working hours, and combined both CATM paradigms: trainees worked individually with picture-based PowerPoint presentations for the reception strategy and in groups of 3-5 with printed pictures and information cards for the selection strategy. Materials were prepared in English and Norwegian and used interchangeably according to participants' needs. After concept attainment, trainees were guided through a discourse relating the concept to their work.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Materials in the learners' languages, used interchangeably according to need
- baseline pre-test to establish trainees' prior knowledge
#### Constraints
- Sessions had to fit within time agreed with supervisors because workers are time-constrained during working hours

### Target Learners
- cleaning workers with primary education or less
- workers of minority background with language challenges

### Learning Goals
- chemicals information and health hazard knowledge
- applying hazard concepts to actual work tasks
