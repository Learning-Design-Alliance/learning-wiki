# Text-based patient safety e-module developed for the comparison study

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An e-learning module developed for this study covering the same patient safety topics as the game's video lectures (communication, focus under stress, teamwork) plus stress management, offered as structured written text with limited interaction and text-based breathing exercises. It had no replacement for the patient missions, since interactively treating patients was not possible in this format. Estimated study time was 1-2 hours.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- No replacement for the game's patient missions existed in this format, which the authors identify as a confounding limitation of the comparison
#### Constraints
- The e-module was evaluated as boring with too little interaction by students; it lacked patient missions aimed at patient safety awareness and effective communication

### Target Learners
- fourth-year undergraduate medical students

### Learning Goals
- knowledge on patient safety issues
- stress management
