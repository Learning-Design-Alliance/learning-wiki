# PEOE-scaffolded spectrometry demonstration series with pre-demo, demonstration, and post-demo questionnaires

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A demonstration-series design wrapping each of four spectrometry demonstrations in predict–explain–observe–explain cycles, with a 'set of 5 questionnaires' spanning pre-demo, demonstration, and post-demo stages 'to stimulate their thinking and deepen their understanding of the demonstrations'. The goal is student-centric, investigative hands-on learning supplementing the existing modern physics syllabus.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sufficient intellectual scaffolding before the series, which the article says 'is important for students to grasp the key learning objectives'; beneficial concepts include diffraction gratings, the Bohr model, discrete emission spectra, blackbody radiation, and Heisenberg's uncertainty principles
- 'A DIY spectrometer and a smartphone are required for all demonstrations in this series'
