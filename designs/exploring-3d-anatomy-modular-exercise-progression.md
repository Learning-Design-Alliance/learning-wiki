# Modular exercise sequence progressing from cognitive visualization to combined haptic-visual drawing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course is structured in distinct modular exercises that learners move through in sequence, beginning with the Astronaut Exercise, with each module unlocked as learners progress and revisitable at any time. Distinct exercises support learner progression from a simple starting point of cognitive visualization activities through visual and haptic activities involving household objects, with drawing activities included at deliberate points in the course, serving as visual representations of cognitive understanding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each webpage provides clear instructions, guidance through a sequence of tasks, gathering of appropriate materials, and setting of expectations such as time required to complete tasks
#### Constraints
- Modules are unlocked in the recommended sequence (left to right; top to bottom), though they can be revisited at any time

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate medical students
- Novice anatomy learners

### Learning Goals
- Progressive development of cognitive and physical spatial awareness
- Visualization of two-dimensional cross-sections of anatomical planes
