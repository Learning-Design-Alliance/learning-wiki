# Three-level creative tasks with assistance cards for mini investigations in physics laboratory work

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Within each laboratory work, creative tasks for mini investigations were developed at three levels of difficulty and presented in methodological guidelines for students' independent work under control. First-level tasks are most difficult and only specify the problem; second-level tasks keep the problem but add clues such as a limited range of devices and materials and outlined ways of performance; the third level is maximally simplified with more tips while retaining creativity elements. Individual tasks are provided with assistance cards containing a pre-arranged question, scheme, pattern or formula. The stated main purpose is development of experimental research competencies of all undergraduate students regardless of their abilities.
