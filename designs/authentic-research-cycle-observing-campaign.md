# Authentic research-cycle structure: target selection, on-site observing, and student-led data reduction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course is organised as a full observational research cycle mirroring professional practice. Students first select galaxies from online archives under visibility constraints, divided into non-overlapping coordinate intervals; then they carry out the observations at the telescope under expert guidance; finally they reduce and analyse the data in small groups, each group analysing one galaxy. The authors state "All the steps done during the observations are, by all means, what is customarily done by professional astronomers for their science research", making authenticity of the workflow the organising feature of the design.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Small groups (maximum 3 people) with non-overlapping target assignments
- Teacher verification of preliminary target lists before the run
- A data processing manual guiding the reduction steps
#### Constraints
- Roughly three-hour observing window requiring students to learn and act fast, since mistakes cost observing time
- Discussion of the practical part of the course is mostly qualitative, per the authors

### Target Learners
- Bachelor and master undergraduate physics and astronomy students

### Learning Goals
- Hands-on mastery of observing techniques, astronomical data handling, regression analysis and data modelling, teamwork and collaboration
