## Description
The article structures the virtual simulation scenario on two reference frameworks: the National League for Nursing/Jeffries Simulation Theory (NLN/JST) and the Healthcare Simulation Standards of Best Practice™ of the International Nursing Association for Clinical Simulation and Learning (INACSL). The authors state that "The scenario structure was based on the National League for Nursing/Jeffries Simulation Theory (NLN/JST) and the Healthcare Simulation Standards of Best Practice™", with technical content grounded in current evidence and guidelines on Standard Precautions and PPE use.
