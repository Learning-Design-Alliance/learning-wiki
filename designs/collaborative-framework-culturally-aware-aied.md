# Collaborative framework for culturally aware AIED across education, technology, and culture dimensions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article contributes a "collaborative framework for culturally aware AIED" organizing the field around three dimensions—education (students become co-producers), technology (AI augments while "people validate"), and culture (community insight guides meaning)—supported by participatory elicitation of cultural knowledge, bilingual representation, and stakeholder validation. It bridges social work's Community-Based Learning methodologies with computational science, treating students as knowledge producers whose final projects are released as open-source software to widen participation in underrepresented Asia-Pacific contexts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multi-stakeholder collaboration spanning education, technology, and culture sectors

#### Constraints
- As a single-site pilot, the authors state generalizability requires multi-site validation

### Target Learners

### Learning Goals

- Community-engaged design for cultural heritage preservation and sustainable development
