# Polya Problem-Solving Model (four-stage heuristic)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The Polya Problem-Solving Model (1957) is described as "one of the earliest problem-solving models" comprising four stages: "i. Understanding the Problem ii. Devising a plan that will lead to the solution iii. Carry out the plan iv. Looking back". In this study the model was operationalized as an instructional treatment: the experimental school's physics teacher was trained in the Polya Instructional Model and taught selected current electricity concepts with it for four weeks, and its effect was compared against lecture method on the PTCE.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must be trained in the model before use; in the study the regular physics teacher was trained by the researcher during the first week
#### Constraints
- The study applied the model to current electricity concepts in senior secondary school physics over six weeks in two purposively selected Nigerian schools

### Target Learners
- Senior Secondary School Two physics students

### Learning Goals
- Problem solving in physics, specifically current electricity concepts
