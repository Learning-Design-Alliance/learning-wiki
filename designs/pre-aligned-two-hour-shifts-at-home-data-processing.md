# Two-hour pre-aligned laboratory shifts with at-home data processing for quantum optics tomography

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A reusable lesson-level design for advanced physics labs: the optical setup is pre-aligned and maintained, students attend in small groups during pre-booked two-hour shifts to control the apparatus and take quadrature data, then process the data at home and submit a report. The article states "The laboratory activities consisted in attending the laboratory in groups of maximum three students, who visited the lab in different pre-booked two-hour shifts." This structure supported both in-person and remote participation during the pandemic.
