# Eleven common TA set-aside activities for charter school applicants, led by pre-award workshops and webinars

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The report identifies 11 common activities grantees funded to support subgrant applicants, spanning pre-award workshops and webinars, application-process TA, grants-management resources and training, school-opening resources, governing board training, and vendor contracts. It reports "Seventeen grantees reported holding pre-award workshops and webinars for potential applicants to support them through the application process." Fifteen grantees each shared grants-management resources, shared applicant resources, and provided post-award grants management training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Grantees must allocate TA set-aside funds between support for quality authorizing and TA to applicants
#### Constraints
- The two grantees not using TA set-aside funds for pre-award workshops used 3% administrative funds or state funds instead

### Target Learners
- Charter school developers and applicants for CSP subgrants

### Learning Goals
- Successfully applying for and managing CSP subgrants and opening and operating charter schools
