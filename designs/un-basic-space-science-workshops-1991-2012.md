# Series of twenty UN basic space science workshops (1991-2012) with donated astronomical and space weather instruments

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A series of twenty annual workshops organized by the UN Programme on Space Applications with ESA, NASA, JAXA, IAU, and COSPAR, spanning basic space science (1991-2004), the International Heliophysical Year 2007 (2005-2009), and the International Space Weather Initiative (2010-2012). The article reports that "These instruments have been made available to respective host countries, particularly developing nations, through the series of twenty basic space science workshops", including planetariums, astronomical instruments, and space weather instruments. Reports on each workshop are open access in the six official UN languages.
