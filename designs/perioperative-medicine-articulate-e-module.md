# Interactive 1-hour perioperative medicine e-learning module built on Articulate Storyline 360

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An interactive, approximately 1-hour e-module created on the Articulate Storyline 360 platform and hosted on Canvas, mapped to six educational objectives. Content design utilized "brief audiovisual didactics with a variety of embedded interactive elements such as drag-and-drop surgical risk categorization and case-based clinical decision prompts", frequent self-assessment quizzes with immediate feedback, and a single case vignette revisited at multiple points to place objectives in clinical context. It was offered as an elective self-directed learning experience during rotations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content based on published evidence and, where available, consensus guidelines
- Mapped to six educational objectives
- Designed to require approximately 1 hour to complete
#### Constraints
- Content was not presented in any other formal learning experience during the perioperative medicine rotation

### Target Learners
- third- and fourth-year medical students
- interns (PGY 1)
- residents (PGY ≥ 2) in internal medicine, anesthesiology, neurology, and physical medicine and rehabilitation

### Learning Goals
- preoperative risk assessment
- selection of lab and ancillary testing
- cardiac stress testing modalities
- management of high-risk perioperative medications
- common postoperative complications
