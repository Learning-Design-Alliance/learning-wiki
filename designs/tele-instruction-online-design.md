# Tele-instruction: a customized online-learning design combining appointments, videoconferencing, meeting-content recording, learning management, progress tracking, and Trusted Technical Agents

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Tele-instruction is a proposed instructional design for online courses whose "main idea of design is based on appointments, videoconferencing, and learning management." It supplements traditional online learning systems with on-demand, real-time face-to-face virtual interaction among the learning community while preserving the pace and flexibility of online education. The system aims to let peers and the instructor interact at their convenience once needed, and to help students get answers from previously recorded responses or two-way communication with the instructor.
