# Enriched time-concept activity program: one hour a week for 10 weeks with researcher-prepared concrete materials

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A recurring activity program in which preschool sessions are enhanced with time-concept content and reinforced with concrete materials such as illustrated cards. The article reports that "activities that were enhanced with time concept and reinforced with materials prepared by the researchers were organized to be applied one hour a week for 10 weeks". Activities were planned from the Ministry of National Education Preschool Education Curriculum so children could express themselves easily, work in groups and individually, and perceive the time concept effortlessly.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Activities built on the Ministry of National Education Preschool Education Curriculum acquisitions and indicators appropriate to children's development
- Concrete materials prepared in advance
- Children's interests and needs considered in planning
#### Constraints
- Applied to children with normal development attending preschool institutions

### Target Learners
- preschool children aged 60 months and older

### Learning Goals
- acquisition of the time concept and its subconcepts
