# Inclusive learning activities combining the Montessori method with open-source educational robots

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article addresses the challenges of few experts and limited resources in a Mexican town "with the creation of inclusive learning activities with Montessori method and open-source educational robots". The Montessori method is described as focused on child-centred development where teachers help children individually or in small groups engaging in self-directed activities; the authors adapt it to develop physical and social requirements plus collaborative and literacy skills through hands-on activities such as group work, games, and a final showcase where groups of four children presented their projects.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors who can help children individually or in small groups
- Low-cost open-source educational robots and everyday-context activities
#### Constraints
- Designed for settings with little to no experts and limited educational material in low- and middle-income countries

### Target Learners
- Children aged 6 to 9 from low- to mid-income families

### Learning Goals
- Inclusive learning of AI and Robotics fundamentals
- Collaborative skills and understanding of concepts
