# Three-part course architecture pairing content sequences with evolving epistemological justification prompts

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The course is built in three parts: "Der Kurs besteht aus drei Teilen" — wave physics, a toolbox of concepts (energy, probability, curvature) for interpreting the Schrödinger equation, and application to simple models of an atom, a molecule and radioactive radiation. Tabelle 1 pairs each section's content with the prompt "Woher wissen Sie das?", whose expected justification evolves from having seen it, to having been told, to having worked it out — an explicit epistemological scaffold across the course.
