# Detector-as-common-thread course design: students progress through all stages of a real particle physics experiment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This pattern organizes a multi-day laboratory course around one physical device that students carry through every stage of a real experiment. The article states "the idea that governed the courses was that the students could plow through all the stages of a real Particle Physics experiment", from understanding the phenomenon and conceiving the setup, through design comprehension, fabrication, characterization and operating-parameter determination, to taking and analyzing data. The shared detector gives coherence to lectures, laboratory sessions and computer analysis classes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A functional, cost-effective detector that student groups can assemble and operate within the course duration
- Computer laboratory access for the data-analysis sessions
#### Constraints
- Depth of the analysis component "strongly depended on participants background in programming"
- Course length of 4 or 5 days, accommodating the host institution's academic schedule

### Target Learners
- advanced undergraduate and graduate STEM students

### Learning Goals
- understanding particle and cosmic ray physics
- radiation detection and instrumentation skills
- data acquisition and object-oriented data analysis (C++ and ROOT)
