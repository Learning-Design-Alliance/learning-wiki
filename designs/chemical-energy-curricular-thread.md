# Chemical energy as a curricular thread

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The article conceptualizes chemical energy not as an isolated unit but as an instructional thread that recurs across a course, linking lecture, homework, recitation, labs, and assessment. Threads build expertise over time by giving students repeated encounters with ideas in different contexts and explicit opportunities to examine links between concepts. Multiple interacting threads (conceptual, epistemological, representational) simultaneously develop different dimensions of scientific expertise. The authors state that "Chemical energy is included in the course as an instructional “thread” that runs through and links many aspects of the course and is not merely an independent unit."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

- Tasks must include opportunities for students to examine the links between concepts, not just a conceptual sequence
