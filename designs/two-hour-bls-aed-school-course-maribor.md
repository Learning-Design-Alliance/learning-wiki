# Two-hour school BLS and AED course: interactive lecture plus hands-on workshop with individual training manikins

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A course for classes of up to 30 seventh- and ninth-grade students, identical throughout the study and divided into two parts of one academic hour (45 min) each. In the first part an emergency physician gave "an interactive lecture on principles of BLS and AED" supported by an overhead presentation; the practical workshop followed in which "each student practised on their own training torso manikin" with an AED prop (a cardboard sample with adhesive paper electrodes) and a single real training AED used for guidance and cardiac arrest simulation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- One training torso manikin per student for the practical workshop
- Instructors familiar with the latest resuscitation guidelines
#### Constraints
- Courses were led by 3 different instructors, and uncontrollable factors such as instructor communication skills and student attentiveness remain, which the authors note reflects real-life situations

### Target Learners
- Seventh- and ninth-grade elementary school students

### Learning Goals
- Basic life support knowledge and skills, and use of an automated external defibrillator
