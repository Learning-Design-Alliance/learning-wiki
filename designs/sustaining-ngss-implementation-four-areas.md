# Sustaining implementation through people, policies, processes, and practices

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Domain 4 represents the overall goal of leaders' work: sustainable implementation of the NGSS. Leaders continuously assess whether effective implementation has been achieved and what is needed next, examining their impact on four key areas: People, Policies, Processes, and Practices. The article states "leaders examine their impact on four key areas that influence sustaining the use of the NGSS." Based on their continuous improvement cycle, leaders gauge movement toward sustainable implementation and may enhance their own knowledge and refine their critical actions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Leaders must engage in continuous improvement cycles and use data to inform improvements to ensure sustainability.
#### Constraints
- Sustainability is assessed within the framework's overall continuous improvement cycle rather than as a one-time determination.

### Target Learners
- State, district, and school leaders responsible for long-term standards implementation

### Learning Goals
- Sustaining NGSS implementation over time
