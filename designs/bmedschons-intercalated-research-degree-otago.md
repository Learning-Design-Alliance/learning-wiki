# BMedSc(Hons) intercalated research degree at the University of Otago

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The BMedSc(Hons) is a year-long intercalated honours research degree embedded in the University of Otago's six-year MBChB program, in which students "briefly suspend their normal training to pursue a focused research topic" and submit a thesis. It is offered only to students who have completed three or more years of the MBChB, taken after the third year (more common) or fifth year. The study evaluated its long-term academic outcomes against matched controls.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must have completed three or more years of the MBChB degree to enrol
- Award requires submission of a thesis on the research topic
#### Constraints
- The study's findings are based on this single program in New Zealand and may not generalize to other countries with differing degree structures

### Target Learners
- Medical students who have completed at least three years of the MBChB program

### Learning Goals
- Research experience and training in a field of medical science
