# Group simulation projects split into overlapping subprojects with varying difficulty

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A course-level design in which five distinct micromagnetic simulation projects are completed by "groups of two to four" students, each project divided into several mostly independent but overlapping subprojects so that interacting with peers and discussing solutions benefits students. Tasks are offered "with varying degrees of difficulty" to cater to a broad spectrum of students with different backgrounds. Projects run alongside lectures, with reports due around two months after assignment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Projects divided into several mostly independent subprojects with a certain overlap so that peer interaction is beneficial
- Tasks with varying degrees of difficulty to cater to students with different backgrounds and prior programming exposure
#### Constraints
- Each calculation is designed to run for a reasonably short period, seconds to minutes
- Project reports are due around two months after the projects have been assigned

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and graduate students with vastly different prior programming experience

### Learning Goals
- in-depth exploration of course contents through problem solving
- peer discussion of solutions to overlapping subprojects
