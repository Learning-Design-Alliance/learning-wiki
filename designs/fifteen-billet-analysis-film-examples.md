# Fifteen produced film examples varying script, sound recording, lighting, camera speed, and photographer experience

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
As part of the billet analysis feasibility research, fifteen film examples were planned and produced to test recording approaches. They included "products of no-script and minimum script procedures", magnetic and single-system sound recording, lighting from simple to complex, five camera speeds, and pictures shot by individuals with differing photographic experience; film analysts reviewed the films to indicate their usefulness.
