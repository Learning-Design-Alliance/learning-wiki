# WhatsApp-delivered REE stress management program: 10 weekly 75-minute virtual sessions with homework and the ABCDE model

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The treatment condition was a weekly REE series of "duration of 10 weeks which lasted 75 minutes per session," delivered through WhatsApp. It adapted therapeutic techniques from a previous rational emotive stress management program and aimed to aid students "through a sequence of REE virtual exercises geared towards decreasing their stress level." Participants were exposed to therapeutic techniques for managing stress, homework assignments were given at the end of every session, and the ABCDE model of the rational emotive approach was applied to illustrate how students can handle stress.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each participant provided a cell phone number linked to WhatsApp and a functional email address; sessions ran weekly for 10 weeks at 75 minutes per session with homework assignments after every therapeutic session
#### Constraints
- Evaluated among religious education undergraduates in public universities in Southwest Nigeria who had high stress scores at baseline on the PSS-10

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students of religious education programs with high perceived stress

### Learning Goals
- recognizing and managing stress through behavioral, cognitive, and emotive coping skills
