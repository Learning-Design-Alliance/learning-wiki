# Mandatory whole-class participation in partnered inquiry programming to reach reluctant high-ability students

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Discovery's design pattern is whole-class, mandatory participation in a term-long partnered inquiry project, chosen deliberately for inclusivity: "The mandatory nature of program participation was established to reach highly capable students who may be reluctant to engage on their own initiative, as a means of enhancing the inclusivity and impact of the program." Students work as full class cohorts with their teacher and graduate mentors rather than as self-selected volunteers, which the article links to building a relevant learning community and to capturing under-engaged students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A post-secondary partner able to provide facilities, graduate student mentors, and co-developed curriculum with teachers 1–2 months before each term
#### Constraints
- Requires institutional investment and accessible post-secondary collaboration; the second-school replication had only two terms, and the authors call for larger collections to separate SES and cohort effects

### Target Learners
- All students in a senior science class cohort, including capable students reluctant to opt in voluntarily

### Learning Goals
- STEM persistence and interest
- Critical thinking and problem-solving practice within curriculum subjects
