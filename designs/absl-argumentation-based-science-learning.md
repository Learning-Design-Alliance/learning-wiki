## Description
The ABSL approach, originally named the Science Writing Heuristic, is a constructivist-based instructional method in which students determine research questions, design activities, develop claims, support them with evidence, and defend results in small and large group discussions. The article describes it as built on constructivist learning theory and requiring guided inquiry activities, interactive group work, meaning via collective negotiated exchange of ideas and argumentation, and reflective writing. Its argumentation structure follows Toulmin's model, in which data, claims, and warrants are mandatory and backing, rebuttals, and qualifier increase the validity of the argument.
