# 3P Framework: full integration of Pedagogy, Physics, and Professional Development for GTA preparation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The 3P Framework is the organizing model the article develops for comprehensive GTA preparation. It posits that a program must fully integrate pedagogical knowledge, physics content, and professional development, visualized as a Venn diagram whose intersections matter as much as the circles. The article states that there must be full integration between pedagogical knowledge, physics content, and professional development strategies, because pedagogy alone, physics alone, or professional development alone is insufficient for novice physics instructors. The Pedagogy-Physics intersection is grounded in the Pedagogical Content Knowledge framework.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Full integration of all three themes; each P alone is stated to be insufficient for a useful and valuable program
- Instructor capacity to map course topics onto the three themes and act on the gaps revealed
#### Constraints
- Developed for first-time GTAs teaching introductory physics labs and recitations at an R1 institution; the authors propose it can be applied to other institutional or disciplinary contexts

### Target Learners
- first-year Ph.D. students serving as first-time graduate teaching assistants in physics

### Learning Goals
- learner-centered teaching practices
- explaining physics concepts and addressing preconceptions
- giving and receiving feedback on teaching
- classroom management and grading
- professional identity and transferable skills
