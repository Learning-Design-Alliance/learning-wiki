# REPeaT trial design: double-blind, active-controlled RCT with four assessment time points and 74 participants

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
REPeaT is a double-blinded, active-controlled randomized trial conducted and reported according to CONSORT guidelines at the Murdoch Children's Research Institute and the Royal Children's Hospital. It will recruit 74 eligible children (37 per arm), with data collected at four time points: T0 screening, T1 pre-intervention baseline, T2 immediately post-intervention, and T3 6-month follow-up. The sample size was powered "to detect a statistically significant difference of 0.8 SD between the treatment group and an active control group based on Automated Working Memory Assessment, with a significance level of 0.05 and power of 0.80".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prospective registration (ACTRN12617000085370), ethics approval (HREC 35181), 1:1 block randomization generated by an independent statistician, intention-to-treat analysis
#### Constraints
- Predicted challenges include recruiting the large screened sample, ensuring adherence regardless of randomisation, participant follow-up, and incomplete parental blinding

### Target Learners
- Children aged 7–15 at least 6 months post-TBI with reduced working memory or executive dysfunction at screening

### Learning Goals
- Working memory, decision-making, and functional outcomes (academic, social, behavioural, quality of life)
