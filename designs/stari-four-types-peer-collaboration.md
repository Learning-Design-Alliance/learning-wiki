# Four types of peer collaboration embedded in a reading intervention

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
STARI was designed to promote social interactions that foster student engagement, structuring peer collaboration in four recurring formats across its units. The program "uses four types of peer collaboration: partner-assisted fluency practice, reciprocal teaching of comprehension strategies, partner reading and responding to novels and nonfiction texts, and peer debate, in which teams gather text evidence and build arguments." Peer contexts also push students to articulate personal stances on texts and compare them with partners or classmates.
