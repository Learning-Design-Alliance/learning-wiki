# Mixed-methods design combining 19 speech measures, listener ratings, and teacher introspective reports

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The study's methodology combined quantitative and qualitative strands: "a mixed-methods approach was used" in which "speech samples of 40 native French learners of English were analyzed using 19 quantitative speech measures, including segmental, suprasegmental, fluency, lexical, grammatical, and discourse-level variables." These measures were correlated with 60 native English listeners' scalar judgments, and three ESL teachers provided introspective reports on the linguistic aspects they attended to, with findings integrated through data triangulation.
