# Five-step prompting framework (Clarify, Organize, Integrate, Differentiate, Correct) operationalizing Generative Learning Theory in AI-supported review

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article introduces a five-step interaction framework that translates Generative Learning Theory's selecting, organizing, and integrating processes into observable student–AI interaction behaviors during review. "The framework guides students through a structured sequence consisting of Clarify, Organize, Integrate, Differentiate, and Correct." Each step has an instructional purpose and example prompt (e.g., asking for the 2–3 most important key points, a real-world example, a commonly confused concept, and the most important misconception). The sequence structures AI use as guided cognitive activity rather than open-ended querying.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A course-aligned generative AI platform with which learners can pose the structured prompts for each topic before proceeding to another
#### Constraints
- The structured sequence may not accommodate all individual learning preferences, and the study tested only this single prompting strategy without comparison to alternative designs

### Target Learners
- undergraduate construction engineering students without prior construction management coursework

### Learning Goals
- conceptual understanding, organization, and integration of course content during self-directed review
