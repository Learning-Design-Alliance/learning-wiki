# Learning Together session structure: a timed circle of Welcome & Context, a single prompt, turn-based reflection, and closing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The recurring session-level structure runs four phases: "Welcome & Context (10 min)", "The Prompt (5 min)", "Reflection (40 min)", and "Closing (5 min)". A land acknowledgement and summary of pre-session materials open the session; a single prompt anchors reflection, framed as not a debate nor even a conversation; participants take turns around a circle, completing it once or twice depending on attendance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- a room with a circular seating arrangement
- a small number of overview and prompt slides, including a Land Acknowledgement slide
- one prompt to anchor the session
#### Constraints
- The structure was run in a research institute; the authors say the underlying structure has been applied across a range of educational and community contexts and may be adaptable to other STEM settings, with attention to power dynamics

### Target Learners
- STEM institute and department members: faculty, postdocs, graduate students, and staff

### Learning Goals
- structured speaking and listening on historical, cultural, and equity-related topics; relational listening and co-creation of understanding
