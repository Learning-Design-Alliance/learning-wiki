# A four-phase, one-semester course structure that simulates the startup journey from kick-off through delivery

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The QEL is a one-semester, project-based course whose structure "follows a structured, multi-phase approach designed to simulate the startup journey". It runs roughly six months with four months of lectures and two months for deliverables, moving through a two-week kick-off, a two-month ideation phase with progress reports, a one-month development phase where combined technical-business teams explore commercialization, and a two-month delivery phase. The pattern culminates in three deliverables: a publication-style technical report, a business white paper, and an 8-10 minute startup pitch at a Demo Day.
