# GAI role framework: tutor, teammate, and tool mapped onto teaching, social, and cognitive presence

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article integrates Role Theory with the Community of Inquiry (CoI) model to propose that GAI can assume three roles in marketing education. As a tutor, GAI provides personalized instruction and helps with learning specific concepts and skills; as a teammate, it collaborates by sharing ideas and offering different perspectives; as a tool, it completes assigned tasks efficiently. Each role is posited to influence teaching, social, and cognitive presence differently, shaping learning experience and workplace readiness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learning objectives of the marketing course determine which role GAI should assume
- Instructors provide instructions (system prompts) to the GAI chatbot for each role
#### Constraints
- The roles often overlap and interact dynamically in practice, particularly in online and hybrid formats
- Framework validated only on a convenience sample and should be expanded in future research

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and graduate marketing students

### Learning Goals
- marketing concepts and skills
- critical thinking
- collaboration
- workplace readiness
