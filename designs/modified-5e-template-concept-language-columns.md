# Modified 5E lesson design planning template with concept, teacher, and student columns and Evaluate embedded in every phase

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The Montebello project built on the 5E Instructional Model (Engage, Explore, Explain, Elaborate, Evaluate) and modified its lesson design planning template. WestEd's K–12 Alliance added a "concept" column so teachers kept the lessons' conceptual flow in mind, and teachers included the Evaluate phase in every stage to monitor and assess student understanding. The template organizes planning around "Concept Teacher Student" rows for each E stage, with the Engage row targeting the prior-knowledge concept and the Explore row connecting it to the lesson concept.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers complete the template stage by stage, keeping conceptual flow in mind while planning activities and discussion prompts
#### Constraints
- The template is adapted from the 5E lesson design planning template (Bybee, 1997) as used in elementary science lessons in the Montebello project

### Target Learners
- elementary students, including high populations of English learners and low-income students

### Learning Goals
- science conceptual understanding
- academic language development
