# Nurse-led complex package of care for gout combining holistic assessment, individualised patient education, shared decision-making and treat-to-target ULT

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An intervention in which nurses provide holistic assessment, individualised patient education and engagement, lifestyle advice, and urate-lowering therapy managed with a treat-to-target approach. The article describes it as care that "combining individualised patient education and engagement, lifestyle advice, and ULT using a treat-to-target approach improves drug persistence, reduces flare frequency and tophi, and improves quality of life". This qualitative study explored how its educational and engagement components facilitated ULT uptake.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Nurses with condition-specific expertise able to up-titrate ULT against a target serum urate level and provide timely access
#### Constraints
- Evaluated in patients with at least one flare in the twelve months before the RCT; generalisability to people with less frequent flares is unclear

### Target Learners
- Adults with gout, predominantly older and white, in UK primary care

### Learning Goals
- Understanding of gout causes, consequences and treatment; confidence in ULT; long-term adherence
