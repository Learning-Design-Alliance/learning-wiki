# Postgraduate guideline-based ultrasound courses can be effectively adapted for undergraduate clinical curricula

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article demonstrates that a certified postgraduate course concept can be transferred to undergraduate teaching: "an MSUS course designed for postgraduates can be effectively adapted for undergraduate medical students", with both subjective and objective measures supporting the adaptation. Incorporating MSUS training into the clinical curriculum is recommended to enhance future medical professionals' educational experience and practical skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Transferred concepts tend to include undergraduates who have completed at least the preclinical phase, ensuring the necessary anatomical background to facilitate learning ultrasound
#### Constraints
- Allocation to groups was not randomized and no pretesting of objective skills was conducted
- Lack of follow-up testing precludes statements about long-term learning success

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate medical students in the clinical phase of their degree

### Learning Goals
- Musculoskeletal ultrasound theoretical knowledge and practical examination skills
