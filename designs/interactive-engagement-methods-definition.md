# Interactive Engagement (IE): pedagogy based on heads-on and hands-on activities with immediate feedback through discussion

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size

## Description
Interactive Engagement (IE) is the article's organizing category for reform pedagogies in introductory physics. IE methods are "designed at least in part to promote conceptual understanding through interactive engagement of students in heads-on (always) and hands-on (usually) activities which yield immediate feedback through discussion with peers and/or instructors". IE courses are those whose instructors report substantial use of IE methods, contrasted with Traditional courses relying on passive-student lectures, recipe labs, and algorithmic-problem exams. The survey used this operational definition to classify its 62 courses.
