# Drop-in game lab structure preserving flexible play sessions for ecological validity

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The study's delivery pattern was a supervised lab open on a drop-in basis (9am-5pm Tuesdays, Wednesdays and Fridays) where students managed their own play time within a prescribed per-game log target. The author argues this "partially addresses a common criticism of laboratory-based video game studies", because an arbitrarily defined time limit on play does not mirror normal play circumstances, and that "the lab was arranged to provide better ecological validity" than a rigid temporal structure.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants take responsibility for their own time management within prescribed play targets
#### Constraints
- Even this relaxed drop-in structure is not an accurate reflection of how games are played at home
