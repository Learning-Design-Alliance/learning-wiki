# Needs analysis triangle framing with three stakeholder groups plus clinical significance as the organising framework for simulation curriculum prioritisation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study structured its needs analysis around Hoadley-Maidment's needs analysis triangle, which distinguishes student-perceived, teacher-perceived and company-perceived needs, alongside Golden and McGaghie's argument that healthcare education design must link to healthcare needs. The researchers "structured the analysis around three stakeholder groups, that is, FY doctors, clinical educators and foundation programme directors", and added clinical significance as a key indicator. This framework organised the six-step prioritisation process across the whole cycle.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multiple stakeholder groups must participate across the process steps to represent diverse perspectives
#### Constraints
- The balance of rigour and feasibility meant only two clinical specialties were represented in the expert clinician group

### Target Learners
- junior doctors in the UK Foundation Programme

### Learning Goals
- identifying and prioritising learning needs for simulation-based education
