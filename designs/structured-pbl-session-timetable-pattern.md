# Minute-by-minute structured PBL session format (recap, presentation, cross-group challenge, synthesis, reflection)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each WeChat-based PBL session followed a fixed internal structure intended to ensure balanced pacing and consistent coverage across groups. "The guide provided a minute-by-minute timetable covering the entire 2-hour process (10 min recap, 45 min group presentation, 30 min cross-groups challenge, 25 min instructor synthesis, and 10 min reflection)." The guide also specified 5 must-cover learning objectives, prompt questions addressing common misconceptions identified during the pilot phase, and a required 1-slide take-home summary displayed at the end of each session.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All instructors follow the same structured facilitator's guide distributed 1 week before the session
- Instructors intervene only when discussion drifts more than 2 minutes off task or critical misconceptions arise, otherwise guiding by inquiry-based questioning
- Instructors complete a structured checklist after the session documenting which objectives were achieved
#### Constraints
- The pattern was applied to a single ophthalmology topic (PACG) in one institution

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students working in small groups

### Learning Goals
- covering mandated syllabus objectives
- correcting misconceptions
- peer presentation and cross-group critique
