# Hybrid model structure: synchronous online didactic teaching with twice-monthly on-campus anatomy, clinical skills, and integration days

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The programme's hybrid model combines online synchronous instruction with periodic in-person immersion: students "take all their lectures and small group teaching online, coming to campus twice a month for anatomy and clinical teaching". The design aimed to widen access to learners outside Dublin, support mature learners and those from geographically disparate regions, and address workforce shortages in remote areas, with peer-to-peer meetings and faculty advisors layered on to support integration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Additional classroom cameras and microphones, timetabling adjusted for twice-monthly campus days, and reliable broadband for students, which is uneven in parts of Ireland
#### Constraints
- Internet challenges (adequate broadband coverage) in some parts of Ireland were acknowledged as a barrier to effective remote educational experiences

### Target Learners
- graduate-entry health professions students, including mature learners and those living outside the programme's home city

### Learning Goals
- physician associate didactic and clinical competencies delivered across online and in-person modes
