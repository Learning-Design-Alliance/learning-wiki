# Modeling Based Science Education Program (MBSEP), a 16-hour electricity unit for 7th grade

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The MBSEP is an instructional program the authors prepared to explore modeling based science education's influence on critical thinking. It was "designed in the way to include the objectives and the modeling process steps" of the Physical Phenomena Learning Field unit Electricity in Our Lives from the 2005 Turkish 7th grade Science and Technology program, which comprises 32 objectives and 16 class hours. Daily plans and activities covered 16 hours, with special teacher and student forms per activity, and student activity flyers were collected as a qualitative data source.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers received a 4-hour training on scientific models' roles in science education, the modeling process and the program
- Delivery by the regular science and technology teachers within normal class meetings
#### Constraints
- The unit was limited to the 7th grade Electricity in Our Lives unit and its 16 class hours

### Target Learners
- 7th grade primary school students

### Learning Goals
- Critical thinking skills
- Science objectives on electrification and electrical current
