# Combining a whole-course clinical trajectory with case-based learning in a specialty clerkship

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's combined teaching model builds on CBL by adding IWCL, "emphasizing case analysis, interactive participation, and continuous clinical engagement." The clerkship curriculum integrates theoretical review, specialty physical examinations, outpatient shadowing, anatomical study, and admission note writing, with different teaching methods matched to each subject's characteristics. The design aims to maximize students' subjective initiative and connect theoretical knowledge to clinical practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Different teaching methods must be integrated across curriculum subjects; supervising surgeons provide explicit guidance and discussion on imaging, surgical indications, and surgical plans
#### Constraints
- Evaluated in a single institutional otolaryngology clerkship setting; group-level allocation with preexisting clerkship groups limited concealment and blinding

### Target Learners
- Fifth-year undergraduate medical students on clinical clerkships

### Learning Goals
- Integration of theoretical knowledge and practical clinical skills
- Clinical thinking and specialty competence in otolaryngology
