# Five-day summer-school structure: two skill-building school days, a monument field day, a university analysis day, and a student-run presentation day

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The summer school followed a five-day arc: two days at Meden School introducing topics and developing skills through practical experiments and competitions, day three visiting two ancient monuments, day four analysing collected data in a workshop at CELS, Nottingham Trent University including an observatory and planetarium visit, and day five presenting to parents. The authors state the first two days "were designed to introduce the topics covered in the summer school and develop the required skills". The final day put students "in total control of the creative process".
