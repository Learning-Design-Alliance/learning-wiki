# LTG theoretical framework: professional learning as interactions among teacher educator, content, and teachers, adapted from Cohen, Raudenbush and Ball

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The project's frame adapts Cohen, Raudenbush and Ball's framework in which "the content of the video case materials focus on the interactions between the teacher, the content (in this case, similarity tasks), and the students, within the context of an authentic classroom environment." The materials mirror this for professional learning: the teacher educator, the content of teaching and learning geometric similarity, and the participating teachers. "The LTG Study is based on the premise that both classroom learning and professional learning opportunities are a function of these reciprocal relationships."
