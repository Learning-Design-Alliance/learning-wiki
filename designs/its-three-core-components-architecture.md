# Intelligent tutoring systems as three-model architectures operationalizing formative assessment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes ITS as autonomous technologies that continuously assess learners' progress through formative assessment and deliver tailored instructional support. It states that "ITS typically consist of three core components: a domain model (representing the knowledge to be learned), a student model (tracking individual learning progress and misconceptions), and a tutoring model (or pedagogical module) that determines what instructional support to provide". Based on these models, ITS provide individualized support such as hints and error-specific feedback and adapt task sequencing to ensure mastery before progression. In this study the architecture was implemented in a CTAT+TutorShop tutor with Bayesian Knowledge Tracing driving task selection.
