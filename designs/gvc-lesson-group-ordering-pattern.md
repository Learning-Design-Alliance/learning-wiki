# Grammar-Vocabulary-Conversation lesson groups with fixed instructional ordering within Concept Blocks

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Each Concept Block is organized as a sequence of GVC groups, each pairing a grammar lesson, a vocabulary lesson, and a conversation lesson. The curriculum enforces a fixed progression within each group: "The ordering constraint, Gt,i → V t,i → Ct,i, guarantees that structural and lexical knowledge precede use." A coverage condition requires that each conversation task's minimal lexical requirements draw only on vocabulary introduced in the current or prior groups, so task completion depends only on previously taught content. Conversation lessons operationalize grammatical rules and lexical sets in contextualized communicative use, with objectives derived from the CEFR Companion Volume.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Conversation tasks must be specified with minimal lexical requirements covered by current or prior vocabulary lessons
- Learning objectives derived from CEFR descriptors
#### Constraints
- Instantiated and evaluated at CEFR A2 (Spoken Interaction) level

### Target Learners
- English learners working toward CEFR A2 proficiency

### Learning Goals
- Applying grammar rules and vocabulary in contextualized conversation
