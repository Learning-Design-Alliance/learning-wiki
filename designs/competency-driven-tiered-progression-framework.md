# Competency-driven tiered progression framework for gynecological laparoscopic training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An explanatory framework in which laparoscopic training is organized around a tiered progression from residents through attending physicians to senior consultants, tailored to stage-specific competency development. The curriculum design followed a systematic five-phase development cycle: "frontline needs assessment, competency-based framework construction, iterative hands-on training module refinement, high-fidelity simulation integration, and continuous performance evaluation with feedback-driven optimization." The framework posits that educational strategies must dynamically adapt to trainees' experiential levels.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multidisciplinary expert collaboration for curriculum development
- Continuous performance evaluation with feedback-driven optimization
#### Constraints
- Preliminary evidence from a single-center, single-arm feasibility study; further studies with control groups and long-term clinical outcomes are needed

### Target Learners
- Gynecologists from residents to senior consultants

### Learning Goals
- Standardized, scalable laparoscopic surgical skill development
