# Microintervention scaffolds delivered at word, phrase, and story levels during oral reading practice

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Coach delivers scaffolded support as responses to errors detected during oral reading, at three moments within a reading session: word level when a student is stuck on a word, phrase level when a student misreads words within a sentence, and story level as a wrap-up building comprehension at the end of the story. Each microintervention is described as a "scaffold" aligned to the Reading Rope, with dozens employed to build foundational reading skills in English and Spanish.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Automated speech recognition to detect oral reading errors in real time; assessment data identifying skills needing work toward mastery
#### Constraints
- Microinterventions respond to errors in oral reading of connected text; they are delivered within the tutoring session rather than as separate lessons

### Target Learners
- K-5 emerging readers using the Coach tutoring pathway

### Learning Goals
- foundational reading skills
- decoding
- fluency
- comprehension
