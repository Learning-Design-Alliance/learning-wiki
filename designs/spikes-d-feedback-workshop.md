# Three-part workshop with didactic SPIKES-D content and videos of poor, intermediate and good practice diagnostic feedback sessions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A two-and-a-half-hour, three-part face-to-face workshop delivered to memory-clinic clinicians, supplemented by an online session for clinicians unable to attend. Part one covers workshop goals and discussion of challenges; part two presents didactic content on "best practice guidelines (predominantly the SPIKES-D protocol"; part three shows three videos of mock diagnostic feedback meetings. Participants rated and discussed the scenarios, which "included examples of poor practice and good practice, based on the SPIKES-D guidelines".
