# Cross-disciplinary community of practice spanning multiple training levels as the staffing model for modular data science curriculum

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
EDUCE is delivered through a community of practice in which TAs, postdocs, instructors, and research faculty cocreate learning materials and work together across courses, which the article describes as "a zone of proximal development for curriculum development". Although coordinated by MICB, "the teaching team spans 10 departments across 3 faculties", with statistics faculty consulting on modules and mathematics providing resource support. EDUCE TAs are recruited across campus rather than assigned to a single course, giving students diverse resources, viewpoints, and role models while TAs gain teaching experience and portfolio material.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A dedicated lead postdoc (or equivalent faculty or education-focused graduate student) responsible for overall curriculum flow, coordination, teaching, and communication among community members
#### Constraints
- The article notes this team-teaching model reflects changes in MICB where a growing number of courses (+24% from 2018 to 2019) are taught by faculty teams, so adoption depends on institutional teaching culture

### Target Learners
- undergraduate life sciences students
- teaching assistants and postdoctoral fellows as developing instructors

### Learning Goals

- hands-on teaching experience for trainees
