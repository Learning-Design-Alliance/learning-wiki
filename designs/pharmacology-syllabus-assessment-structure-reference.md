# Pharmacology syllabus and assessment structure as a curricular alignment reference

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article publishes the pharmacology syllabus delivered during the study (Table 1), covering basic and systemic pharmacology topics across terms 3 and 4 with quizzes, mid-term, and final exams of single-response multiple-choice items. The authors state the syllabus "may serve as a useful reference for others interested in comparing or aligning their curricula". Assessment reliability (KR-20) ranged from 0.6–0.89.
