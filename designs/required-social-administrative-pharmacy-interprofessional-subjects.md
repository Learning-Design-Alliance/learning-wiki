# Include social and administrative pharmacy and interprofessional practice as required subjects in postgraduate pharmacy training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because senior trainees significantly outperformed first-year trainees specifically in organization and efficiency and communication skills, the authors suggest these abilities "could be improved with training in multidisciplinary teamwork, as well as social and administrative pharmacy". They recommend that "social and administrative pharmacy, as well as interprofessional practice be listed as required subjects in the Taiwanese PGY training program", noting trainees currently could take such electives only if already performing well after basic training. They further urge policy encouraging teaching hospitals to improve instruction quality in required courses.
