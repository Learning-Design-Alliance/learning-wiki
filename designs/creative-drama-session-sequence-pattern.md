# Creative drama unit plan structured as sequenced sessions with warm-up, improvisation, interim assessment and closing activities

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's drama treatment was organised as a sequence of sessions aligned to unit learning goals: "Creative drama plans consist of 9 sessions in line with the learning goals" of the 4th-grade unit, totalling 15 class hours at 3 hours per week. The appendix exemplifies one 40-minute session with a warm-up game, a 30-minute improvisation with role play and a leader in role, an interim assessment, and a closing group activity. Techniques named include improvisation, role playing and Leader in Role.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A leader/facilitator who enters and exits role and structures warm-up, improvisation and assessment phases
- Materials such as clock drawings, cardboard, calendar information sheets and clock models for the exemplar session
#### Constraints
- Sessions were designed for 9-10 year old groups of 20-25 participants in the exemplar plan
- The treatment lasted 15 class hours within one social studies unit

### Target Learners
- 4th-grade primary school students (9-10 years)

### Learning Goals
- Social studies unit content on time measurement tools and calendars
- Communication, group work and problem-solving skills
