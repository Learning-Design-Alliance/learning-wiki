# Board Examination Simulation Exercise (BESE): biweekly online formative quizzes for pediatric residents

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
BESE is a series of intermittent online quizzes implemented at three pediatric residency programs from 2011 (NCH, UTH) and 2013 (UL). Each year offered "Twenty-three online quizzes (22 in Year 1)" at approximately 2-week intervals, each of 20 multiple-choice questions drawn randomly from a 40–50-item bank per topic, completed in 25 minutes. Immediate feedback revealed correct answers with 2–5 paragraphs of teaching points; review access was limited to 1 hour to minimize recall.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Questions written and peer reviewed by the study authors, modelled after accepted question-writing guidelines, and content derived from ABP content specifications
- Delivery via each institution's learning management system with immediate scoring and explanatory feedback
#### Constraints
- Quizzes sample knowledge in topic areas rather than covering all ABP content
- Questions and answers are viewable for only 1 hour after the quiz and cannot be copied

### Target Learners
- Pediatrics and Internal Medicine-Pediatrics residents

### Learning Goals
- Medical knowledge sampled across ABP content specification areas
- Preparation for the ABP Certifying Examination format and timing
