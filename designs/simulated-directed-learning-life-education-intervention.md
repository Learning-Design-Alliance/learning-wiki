# Simulated directed-learning life-education intervention: three 100-minute weekly lectures with e-learning materials

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A life-education intervention delivered within a health education course for third-year nursing students. A nursing educator designed three weekly lectures of 100 minutes each, totaling 300 minutes, on three topics: the meaning of life, positive beliefs, and well-being. Teaching and learning contents were "linked to YouTube videos, e-books, and Internet movies materials", and students could also download the topic contents from an e-learning platform at a future time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered by a nursing educator within a health education course, with contents linked to YouTube videos, e-books, and Internet movies and downloadable from an e-learning platform
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only with third-year nursing students in a 5-year program at a university in Kaohsiung City, Taiwan, with no appropriate placebo control for the control group

### Target Learners
- third-year nursing students in a 5-year program

### Learning Goals
- meaning of life
- positive beliefs
- well-being
- physical, psychological, spiritual, and social health education and promotion
