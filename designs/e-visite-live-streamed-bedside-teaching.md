# Electronic ward round (E-Visite): live-streamed bedside teaching via tablet and Zoom

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The electronic ward round replaced the suspended in-person ENT clerkship once weekly: a mentor streamed real-time bedside teaching via the Zoom app on a 9th-generation iPad, with patient presentations, demonstrations of examination techniques, and microscope/endoscope camera findings shown on screen. Groups of 7–10 students joined with a weekly changing password, and the roughly 60-minute session allowed questions and chat interaction. The article states "Der digitale Unterricht am Krankenbett erfolgte einmal wöchentlich für jeweils eine Gruppe zu einem vorher festgelegten Termin."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Patients gave written consent for anonymized transmission of history and disease presentations for training purposes
- The platform must use secure encryption technology to ensure confidentiality of patient data, per the discussion
#### Constraints
- Practical skills such as examination techniques and patient contact could only be conveyed at a theoretical level

### Target Learners
- Seventh-semester medical students in the ENT block clerkship

### Learning Goals
- Learning history-taking and fundamentals of clinical findings
- Becoming familiar with ENT disease presentations
