# ERWC instructional focus on discussion, critical thinking, oral language, genre writing, and rhetorical analysis

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The ERWC's design centers English instruction on a set of interrelated practices: "discussions of text meaning, developing critical thinking skills, encouraging group discussions, developing oral language skills, developing writing skills in multiple genres, and analyzing the rhetorical features of texts and applying them in student writing." These foci operate across the whole twelfth-grade English course.
