# AI-integrated take-home assessment framework built on Bloom's taxonomy with mandatory ChatGPT use

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes an assessment framework in which conventional higher-order questions are retained, but "the learners are instructed to use ChatGPT as a mandatory, yet not exclusive, source". Learners must document all prompts and responses in raw form, comment on the AI's responses, and produce a concluding statement. The author argues the prompts reflect learner understanding, the comments necessitate in-depth evaluation, and the concluding statement applies the responses, so the scheme "encompassing all the levels of Bloom's taxonomy" assesses understanding, application, analysis, evaluation and creation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learners must have access to ChatGPT or a similar superchatbot and submit their prompts and the AI's responses in raw form as screenshots or equivalent
#### Constraints
- The author notes a related ChatGPT-devised evaluation approach would require the instructor to possess some level of fluency in computer languages, whereas the proposed technique is founded on the traditional take-home exam scheme

### Target Learners
- undergraduate engineering students in a four-year technical education program

### Learning Goals
- critical thinking and problem solving at the higher levels of Bloom's taxonomy: understanding, application, analysis, evaluation and creation
