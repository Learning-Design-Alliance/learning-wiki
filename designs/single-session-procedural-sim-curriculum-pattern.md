# Single-session procedural simulation curriculum pattern: prework, peer debate, team-based steps competition, deliberate-practice stations, then integrated task-trainer performance

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This curriculum pattern organizes a single 2- to 3-hour session into a sequence: prework assignments, a learner-led site-selection debate, a team-based competition to list the steps of the procedure, rotation through four hands-on deliberate-practice stations covering separate components, and finally full performance of the procedure on a task trainer. The skill was broken down so learners had "time for deliberate practice in each of the steps." Station 4 focused practice on "dynamic needle tracking in the out-of-plane technique."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- One facilitator per station with a facilitator-to-learner ratio of 1:2; a shared study guide reviewed by facilitators to standardize instruction; task trainers and US machines
#### Constraints
- Activities were removed from the schedule for learners with prior CVC placement experience; the pattern was designed for a complex procedural skill, not reported for other domains

### Target Learners
- novice to experienced pediatric procedural learners (PCCM and PEM fellows, APPs)

### Learning Goals
- complex procedural skill acquisition (US-guided CVC placement) and its component steps
