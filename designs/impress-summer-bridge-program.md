## Description
The IMPRESS (Integrating Metacognitive Practices and Research to Ensure Student Success) program is a two-week pre-matriculation summer program at RIT for incoming first-generation and/or Deaf/hard-of-hearing (DHH) undergraduate students. It uses intensive laboratory experiments, reflective practices, and discussion in small groups (3-4 students) and with the whole class (19-20 students) to teach students to "reﬂect on, evaluate, and change their own thinking". Its objectives are to engage students in authentic science practice, facilitate a supportive community, and strengthen learning habits for STEM futures.
