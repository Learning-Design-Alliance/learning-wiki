# Interactive case-based neuroscience eModule built in Storyline 360

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An eModule teaching stroke recognition, cerebral arterial anatomy, and the relationship between arterial system and clinical presentation, developed by medical students over six months with multidisciplinary input and an estimated 60 h of development. It used "a case-based approach with clinically relevant neuroimaging" and Storyline 360 software enabled "drag-and-drop, multiple-choice and click-and-point questions". It cost $649 per academic user per annum via an Articulate 360 subscription.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Development required multidisciplinary input including a senior neurology professor, an educational specialist and supervision by an academic neurosurgical fellow
#### Constraints
- Development, delivery and maintenance carry opportunity costs; the module was designed to take approximately 20 to 30 min to complete

### Target Learners
- UK medical students on clinical placements

### Learning Goals
- Recognise common presentations of stroke
- Know the anatomy of the cerebral arterial system
- Understand the relationship between the arterial system and clinical presentation
- Understand core clinical management for stroke
