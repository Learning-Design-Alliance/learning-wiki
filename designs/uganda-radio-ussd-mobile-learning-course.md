# Radio and USSD phone-based mobile learning course on simple machines for out-of-school Ugandan youth

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A nonprofit-facilitated mobile learning course (August 11 to December 28, 2022, Northern Uganda) teaching out-of-school youth the science of simple machines and engineering mindsets. As the article describes it, "Passive learning involves listening to live radio lectures, while active learning involves phone-based multiple-choice quizzes that provide immediate feedback for until learners select the correct answer." Quiz interactions were logged via a USSD text message protocol; each week featured three radio broadcasts and corresponding assessments with 2-4 questions, and learners chose when to engage. The dataset identified 4,135 user profiles, 2,407 enrolled learners, and 332 final exam takers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learners need access to a basic mobile phone with USSD service and reception of community radio broadcasts; the course relies on a database logging USSD interactions.
#### Constraints
- The authors note design and evaluation limitations of basic phones such as screen size, keypad interactions, and cellular service fees, and that multiple broadcasts made it challenging to directly observe when, whether, and which broadcast learners listened to.

### Target Learners
- Out-of-school youth in Northern Uganda who may not have completed secondary education

### Learning Goals
- Science of simple machines
- Engineering mindsets
