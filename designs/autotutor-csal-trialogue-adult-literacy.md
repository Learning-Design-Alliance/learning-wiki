# AutoTutor for CSAL: trialogue-based intelligent tutoring system for adult reading comprehension

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
AutoTutor for CSAL is a web-based conversation-based ITS in which two computer agents (a teacher agent and a peer agent) hold trialogue conversations with adult learners. The three-way conversations are designed to provide instruction on reading comprehension strategies, help adults apply strategies to texts, assess performance, and guide computer use. Lessons begin with a 2–3 min video reviewing a comprehension strategy, then agents scaffold learning by asking questions, providing short feedback, explaining answers, and giving correct responses. Game-like competitions between the student and peer agent are programmed so the human student never loses.
