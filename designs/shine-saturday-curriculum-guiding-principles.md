# SHINE guiding principles for a creative Saturday-school curriculum

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
SHINE specifies guiding principles that host schools use to design their own Saturday curriculum: a core focus on literacy and numeracy around a termly theme; opportunities to revisit areas of struggle and extend beyond weekday subjects; hands-on project work and enrichment applying learning to real-world contexts; curricular flexibility for trips and visits to re-engage demotivated pupils; and "an emphasis on investigative and collaborative learning and research projects which encourage problem solving and independent learning". The pattern structures a 25-week enrichment-oriented catch-up programme.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A termly theme chosen by the school linking literacy and numeracy work
- Educational visits connected to the theme and a subject area, funded from a dedicated budget
- Medium-term planning demonstrating how learning links between Saturdays
#### Constraints
- Schools, not SHINE, design the specific curriculum, so implementation varies by host school

### Target Learners
- underachieving Year 7 pupils in the transition year of secondary school

### Learning Goals
- literacy and numeracy consolidation
- problem solving and independent learning
- engagement and motivation
