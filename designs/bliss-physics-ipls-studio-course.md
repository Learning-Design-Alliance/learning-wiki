# BLiSS Physics: a reformed introductory physics for the life sciences studio course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
BLiSS Physics is an introductory physics for the life sciences course at Michigan State University reformed to leverage connections to biology. "BLiSS Physics adapts curriculum, discursive structures, and participation frameworks from Modeling Instruction for introductory university physics," uses student-led investigations, whiteboard meetings and argumentation, and vPython computational activities where students correct minimally working code. The course "intentionally positions students as being expert in their home disciplines and has space designed to allow students to bring in their expertise."
