# Quantum Teaching content sequence: enroll, experience, demonstrate, and celebrate

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The content element of Quantum Teaching follows a five-step lesson sequence proposed by DePorter (1992). The teacher first "enroll"s students to capture interest, curiosity, and attention; then has them "experience" content through exploration, which the article says better maintains long-term memory than reading or listening, followed by discussion, verification, and labeling of concepts; then students "demonstrate" their capability, followed by reflection and repetition to strengthen nerve connections; and finally the class "celebrate"s to appreciate those who finished tasks successfully, providing new energy for upcoming learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Exploration activities followed by discussion, verification, and labeling of discovered concepts
- Reflection and repetition after demonstration to strengthen nerve connections
#### Constraints
- The article presents this sequence as part of the Quantum Teaching framework rather than as an independently tested component

### Target Learners
- sixth-grade students in inclusive elementary schools

### Learning Goals
- content mastery in Bahasa Indonesia and Science
- long-term memory retention
- student confidence through demonstration and celebration
