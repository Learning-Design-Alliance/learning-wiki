# Critical frameworks for destabilizing hegemonic logics in STEM

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The seminar series organizes its critique of STEM knowledge production around a set of named critical frameworks. The article states the series "will examine how to destabilize hegemonic logics and hierarchies in STEM under some of the following frameworks" — queer and black geographies, gender essentialism, colonial sexuality, queer ecology and eco-feminism, non-essentiality, (Latinx, black) futurity, indigenous temporalities, and queer time. These frameworks, drawn from queer theory, radical feminism, and black analyses, serve as the organizing taxonomy for the course's panel discussions.
