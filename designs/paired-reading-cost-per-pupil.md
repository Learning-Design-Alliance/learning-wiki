# Paired Reading delivery cost estimate of £10.50 per pupil

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The evaluation reports the cost of delivering the Paired Reading programme as an estimated £10.50 per pupil. This estimate "includes training and support costs and materials required for schools to be able to deliver the programme". The costing is based on a class of 25 pupils with five classes per school, covering the teacher training, manual and digital resources needed for delivery.
