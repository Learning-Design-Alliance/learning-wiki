# Full-immersion residential inquiry school pattern: lessons, laboratories, peer sharing, seminars

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A reusable lesson/unit-level design: a residential multi-day program organized around one non-trivial theme, with morning participatory lessons giving background, afternoon small-group inquiry-based laboratories in which students investigate experimental situations without an authority's solution, evening astronomical observation or problem solving, peer presentations for sharing knowledge, and closing 'Insight labs' seminars by young physicists oriented to science degrees. The authors note 'a puzzle or a paradox involving the concept at issue can be presented in order to struggle towards a solution'.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- At least 8 laboratory spaces; groups formed inhomogeneously by teacher suggestions for best collaboration; almost all laboratories lead to at least one measure with error valuation; at least one How-it-works activity per lab
#### Constraints
- Designed for selected, highly motivated students; total commitment of about 10 hours per day from all participants

### Target Learners
- selected high-school students aged 16-17

### Learning Goals
- inquiry and measurement skills
- understanding a meaningful physics topic
- orientation toward science degrees
