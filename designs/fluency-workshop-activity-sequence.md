# Fluency workshop: a 10-activity sequence building familiarity with target multiword expressions from input through practice to free production

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (1 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A reusable lesson-level sequence in which learners repeatedly read, hear, write, and speak target multiword expressions in meaning-focused, time-pressured activities. The article's sequence "flowed from input through to practice and free production": listening with gist questions, marking pauses, phrase instruction, shadowing, dictogloss, mingle jigsaw, role-play train, decreasing-time role-play, record role-play, and free related-situation role-play. It was implemented once weekly over 6 weeks with three travel-related dialogue topics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Activities should be meaning-focused, use familiar language, and be performed under time pressure (as the article states fluency activities require)
#### Constraints
- The article states the workshop's fluency and in-speech use gains were not statistically greater than a general English class, so implementation should be weighed against language learning goals

### Target Learners
- low-proficiency EFL university students in Japan

### Learning Goals
- speaking fluency
- productive knowledge and use of multiword expressions in travel-related conversational situations
