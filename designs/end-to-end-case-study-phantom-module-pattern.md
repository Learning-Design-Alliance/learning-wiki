# End-to-end case study simulation pattern spanning the radiotherapy patient pathway within a single module

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A module-level instructional pattern in which a single IMRT-related treatment site (head) is carried through the full radiotherapy pathway — pre-treatment CT imaging and immobilization, data transfer, treatment planning in the TPS, and delivery on a linear accelerator — replacing a prior procedural workbook method that used multiple body-site cases. The single-scenario design was chosen "to maintain simplicity and consistency" and to avoid the additional costs of custom-designed phantoms for different body sites.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Equipment at key stages: CT scanner/simulator, Eclipse TPS, and VERT platform, with the phantom integrated at each stage
#### Constraints
- Less favoured alternative of multiple phantoms for different body sites due to additional costs and a preference for a single scenario

### Target Learners
- Year 2 undergraduate radiotherapy students

### Learning Goals
- Practical skills and theoretical understanding across the full radiotherapy planning pathway
