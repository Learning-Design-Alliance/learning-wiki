# Four-phase blended FC/TPS design with authentic prescriptions and gamification

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a blended learning intervention "structured into four sequential phases": pre-class self-study with digital materials and real de-identified prescriptions, a 30-minute interactive lecture, a 60-minute structured Think-Pair-Share session analyzing novel prescriptions, and a gamified motivation phase. Prescriptions were tiered into Basic, Intermediate, and Advanced difficulty by two clinical pharmacy faculty reviewers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a university LMS (Moodle) for pre-class materials
- A database of real, de-identified prescriptions reviewed and tiered by clinical faculty
- Instructor time for interactive lecture, TPS facilitation, and public point tracking
#### Constraints
- Implemented within a compulsory cardiovascular pharmacology module at one Iranian pharmacy school
- Evaluated only at Kirkpatrick Level 1 in this pilot

### Target Learners
- undergraduate pharmacy students in their fourth year

### Learning Goals
- rational prescribing
- clinical reasoning in cardiovascular pharmacotherapy
- active engagement with authentic prescriptions
