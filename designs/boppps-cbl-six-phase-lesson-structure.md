# Six-phase BOPPPS-CBL lesson structure for clinical case teaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article operationalizes BOPPPS-CBL as a lesson-level sequence: bridge-in where residents present chest-pain content and the teacher connects it to pathophysiology and diagnosis; explicit objectives; a 30-question pre-assessment; one hour of group-based participatory learning with simulated consultations on classic cases; a 30-question post-assessment; and a summary where residents summarize and the teacher provides "a learning outline and a flowchart for diagnosing and treating chest pain."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cases and teaching outlines sent to residents one day in advance for independent preparation
- Instructors board-certified in cardiology or emergency medicine with chest pain management experience
#### Constraints
- Class length matched to LBL at one hour per session in this implementation

### Target Learners
- Resident physicians in standardized training

### Learning Goals
- Chest pain diagnosis, differential diagnosis and treatment in emergency settings
