# Active learning methods for the formation of learners' interest in physics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article selects six active methods for teaching physics according to their influence on the development of learners' thinking: heuristic conversation, creation of problematic situations, home experiments, excursions, solving crosswords, and conducting physics dictations. For example, creation of problematic situations introduces learners to activity during which they are faced with facts that contradict their experience, and home experiments let learners reproduce fundamental physics experiments so that they will feel themselves "discoverers". These methods form the second component of the developed technique.
