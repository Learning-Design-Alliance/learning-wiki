# Comic-book character diagnostic evaluation assignment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An instructional element in which students select an instructor-approved graphic novel, choose a character, "perform a m ock clinical evalu ation, and provide a written psychological report". The report must include client demographics, referral reason, psychosocial history, assessment procedures, differential diagnosis with rule-out rationale, DSM-IV-TR multiaxial diagnoses, case formulation, prevalence and incidence, treatments, treatment recommendations, and prognosis. Comic books were chosen because symptomatology is not presented as straightforwardly as in film, challenging students to develop clinical acumen.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students submit their chosen graphic novel for instructor approval; the instructor provided a list of potential comic books in the syllabus and arranged for a local comic book store's employees to offer selection assistance
#### Constraints
- The version used was possible because of the authors' familiarity with the medium; none of the experimental students selected a graphic novel the authors had not read, which the authors note will not hold for all instructors

### Target Learners
- undergraduate psychology majors in an abnormal psychology course

### Learning Goals
- diagnostic evaluation skills
- DSM-IV-TR diagnosis
- clinical report writing
