# UNITE five-component pedagogical framework for e- and m-Learning in secondary schools

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article presents a pedagogical framework (PF) developed within the Europe-wide UNITE project for secondary schools. It "consists of five components": pedagogical framework context, pedagogical approaches, assessment techniques, teacher education, and current pedagogical practices implemented in national curricula and national specifics. These components "produce the set of parameters which drive and guide the production of learning scenarios", making the learning scenario the mechanism that holds together the pedagogical and technical sides of development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Integration of the five PF components enables generation of parameters that drive and guide the production of learning scenarios
#### Constraints
- Designed for secondary schools in a Europe-wide network of 14 schools

### Learning Goals
- learning enhanced through e- and m-Learning scenarios across curricula
