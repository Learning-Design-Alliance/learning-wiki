# Epistemological Principle: a clearly specified standard interpretation with explicit correspondence rules to structure discussion of the foundational debate

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course is designed around a clearly specified form of standard interpretation, set apart from other schools by rules of correspondence between theory structure and physical referents, including eigenstate-eigenvalue link and collapse. This serves "to build a coherent educational proposal" and to identify which facets of the foundational debate each interpretive choice triggers, discussing them at the students' educational level. A field ontology is adopted for wave-particle duality.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires explicit rules of correspondence between the structure of the theory and its physical referents
#### Constraints
- Each interpretive choice has been questioned by part of the scientific community, with the projection postulate the most unsatisfactory

### Target Learners
- secondary school students learning quantum mechanics

### Learning Goals
- develop awareness of the cultural significance of the foundational debate and its open issues
