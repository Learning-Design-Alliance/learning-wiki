# SOM-PBL intervention package: mind-map templates, PBL sessions, scaffolded cases and formative feedback

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The element is the concrete instructional package used in the trial's intervention arm. It included "pre-designed structured templates with central symptoms (e.g., dyspnea) provided through MindManager software", twice-weekly 90-minute PBL sessions with collaborative map expansion, authentic critical care cases in scaffolded problem sequences, and post-session submission of finalized mind maps for formative assessment with written feedback within 72 hours. The control arm received traditional lecture-based teaching covering identical core topics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- MindManager software with structured symptom-centered templates
- Trained facilitators for twice-weekly PBL sessions
- Authentic critical care cases sequenced from symptom recognition to evidence-based decision-making
#### Constraints
- Both groups completed a four-week supervised ICU clerkship; efficacy was evaluated only over that brief period
- Participants and teaching facilitators were not blinded to group assignment

### Target Learners
- Fifth-year medical students on mandatory ICU clerkships

### Learning Goals
- Integration of multidisciplinary theoretical knowledge
- Clinical reasoning in complex critical illness
- Procedural skills in critical care
