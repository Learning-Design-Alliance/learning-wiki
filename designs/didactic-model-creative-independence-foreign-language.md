# Didactic model for forming students' creative independence in foreign-language teaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors developed a didactic model whose target is to form students' creative independence, with tasks of "formation of students' creative thinking", transferring scientific and informative knowledge, and mastering experience of creative activity. It specifies teacher didactic activity stages, pedagogical conditions, and forms and methods of student work (frontal, collective, group and individual creative tasks, creation of creative language situations). The model was realized during the formative stage of the experiment and, per the authors, "helped to increase the effectiveness of the development of students' creative independence in linguistic higher educational institutions".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires diagnosing students' level of creative potential, initial opportunities, cognitive activity and motivation before instruction
- Requires the teacher to plan lessons in compliance with problems of formation of students' creative independence and correct the level of formation
#### Constraints
- Tested with first- and second-year university students in English language classes at one institution

### Target Learners
