# Student-run free clinics should rigorously assess the quality of care they provide

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The article argues that SRFCs have a two-fold obligation to assess care quality: the ethics of parity, under which "both patients and students benefit from service-learning experiences, rather than one benefiting at the expense of the other," and the modern-medicine tenet that best practices rest on objective evidence. Assessment should compare process and outcome measures against best-practice benchmarks and track longitudinal clinical outcomes, as this study did against ADA benchmarks.
