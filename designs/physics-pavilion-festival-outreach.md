# Physics Pavilion: a multi-day festival-based science outreach installation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Physics Pavilion is a dedicated science installation embedded in a music or culture festival for its full duration, offering talks, workshops, virtual link-ups and continuous hands-on activities. The article states the format 'includes a curated programme of talks, dedicated workshops such as "Build Your Own Cloud Chamber"', a live virtual link-up, and outdoor hands-on activities available throughout the day. It has run at WOMAD, Colours of Ostrava, Pohoda and Roskilde since 2016.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- a collaboration between CERN, the festival itself and local physics departments, with shared resources and finance managed by a core team
- a good team of volunteer physicists of all ages on hand
#### Constraints
- requires a multi-day festival venue (3 or more days); live events were suspended by Covid-19 in 2020, with only a virtual talk series run

### Target Learners
- general festival-going public of all ages, including people who would not normally seek out science

### Learning Goals
- general science literacy and particle physics awareness
- breaking down the misconception that science is 'not for me'
