# "World + You - World": a five-level side-scrolling serious game prototype mapping cognitive distortions to game mechanics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
"World + You - World" is a Unity-built side-scrolling platformer serious game prototype developed with TPR-MMF. "This study selected five common types of cognitive distortions and completed the deconstruction and game mechanism transformation of the five core cognitive distortions through TPR-MMF (Table 2)." Each level embodies one distortion: e.g., the Overgeneralization level's escaping platform retreats less with each attempt, and the Magnification level's giant spike is revealed to be fake when players jump decisively.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Players familiar with basic video game operation; built in Unity (2022.3.52f1c1)
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only with stressed but non-clinical university students (PSS-10 ≥ 14, no major depressive disorder diagnosis) in 20-minute sessions

### Target Learners
- first-year university students aged 18-19 with general stress

### Learning Goals
- recognizing and restructuring five common cognitive distortions
- mental health awareness and real-life transfer
