# Situational training game design pattern: authentic scenarios plus progressive challenge and real-time feedback

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A reusable lesson/game-level design pattern for cognitive training games: embed classic cognitive tasks in authentic real-life situations and layer motivational game elements. The article states that "the game integrated key design elements, including authentic scenarios, sound effects, animations, progressive difficulty stages, time limits, and scoring mechanisms". Progressive stages, timers and scoring serve achievement-oriented motives, while authentic contexts such as restaurants and apartments with animated transitions and sound effects foster presence, relatedness and autonomy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tasks must target a specific EF sub-skill based on classic cognitive assessments
- Staged progression with selectable difficulty levels
- Immediate feedback via sound effects and animations
#### Constraints
- Evaluated in 15-minute single-session training; long-term effects unknown

### Target Learners
- university students

### Learning Goals
