# Liquid crystals teaching module: lecture, chemistry lab and physics lab

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A teaching module about liquid crystals for undergraduate non-physics students, in which "The teaching module has three parts: lecture (1st week), lab work at c hemistry (2nd week) and lab work at physics (3rd week)", each estimated at 90 minutes. Students synthesize the liquid crystal MBBA and use their own product in four physics experiments covering phase transitions, polarization, double refraction and colours. In the pilot, "40 syntheses out of 40 were successfully carr ied out".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The MBBA synthesis has to be carried out in a fume hood for safety reasons.
#### Constraints
- The chemistry lab part can be left out if the laboratory is not available, with the physics lab extended to two 90-minute meetings.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate non-physics students
- pre-service primary school teachers

### Learning Goals
- understanding how liquid crystal displays work
- recognizing the liquid crystalline phase and phase transitions
- understanding polarization, double refraction and colours of anisotropic materials
