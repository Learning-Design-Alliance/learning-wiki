# Three types of online interaction: content, instructor, and other learners

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The article organizes online course design around three interaction types, following the interaction framework it attributes to Moore (1989) and Kuo and Belland (2016): learners' interaction "with the content, with the instructor, and with other learners." Instructors are advised to determine, for each type, how students will interact when designing online coursework. The framework serves as the organizing structure for the article's equity recommendations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must plan for each of the three interaction types when designing online courses
#### Constraints
- Applied here to undergraduate online courses in the context of the COVID-19 shift to online delivery
