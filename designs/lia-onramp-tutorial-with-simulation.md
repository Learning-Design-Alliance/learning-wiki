# Research-based lock-in amplifier tutorial with an integrated LIA simulation, iteratively refined 18 times

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A research-based OnRamp tutorial for the lock-in amplifier, built from professor interviews, a cognitive task analysis, and a LIA simulation that forms an integral part of the tutorial. Most questions ask students to predict outputs from given input parameter sets, with hints, feedback, and both mathematical and conceptual explanations. The tutorial "was iteratively refined 18 times, based upon feedback from graduate students and professors".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Development relied on interviews with physics faculty who use LIAs and a cognitive task analysis of the knowledge involved in operating a LIA.
#### Constraints
- Assessment reported in the article involved six physics graduate students; the pretest and posttest questions were matched for content but not identical.

### Target Learners
- physics graduate students beginning research using lock-in amplifiers

### Learning Goals
- purpose and function of the mixer and low-pass filter
- connecting conceptual and mathematical aspects of LIA operation
