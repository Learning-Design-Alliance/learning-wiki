# 24-week clinical clerkship covering 60 clinical skill modules in biomedicine and traditional medicine

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The clinical curriculum allocates "a total of 24 (20 + 2 + 2) weeks of clinical education in affiliated hospitals". Twenty weeks cover 56 clinical modules in eight departments of an affiliated traditional Korean medicine hospital; two weeks cover four medical-herb processing modules; and two weeks cover research ethics and clinical research design. The 60 skill modules divide into 39 biomedicine and 21 traditional medicine modules described in the Guidebook for Clinical Skills.
