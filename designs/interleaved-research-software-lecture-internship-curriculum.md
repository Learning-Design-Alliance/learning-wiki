# Internship curriculum interleaving hands-on research, software training and lectures

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The program's curriculum interleaves three components across ten weeks: an initial software training phase, ongoing physics project work, and a weekly lecture series. "Hands-on experience was interleaved with 3 lectures per week on topics ranging from basics of particle physics, to Discovery Physics, handling Big Computing/Software platforms, and introduction to the basics of a particle physics detectors." The first three weeks were devoted to software skills needed for the projects, while lectures and networking sessions ran throughout.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Software training must precede or accompany project work, since interns' prior software exposure was none to negligible
- Weekly lectures and networking opportunities with scientists at multiple career stages
