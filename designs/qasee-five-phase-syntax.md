# QASEE five-phase lesson syntax combining individual preparation, group sharing, transfer, and reflection

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The QASEE syntax structures a lesson cycle in five phases: questioning, answering, sharing, extending, and evaluating. Individual questioning and answering before class build initial knowledge; sharing uses group discussion in which higher-ability students act as tutors; extending applies understanding to new real-world contexts; evaluating is written self-reflection. The article reports this combination gave space for thinking through questions, cooperative groups, oral presentations, authentic activities, and written reflections.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each phase must be implemented in sequence, with questioning and answering completed before class begins
#### Constraints
- The article notes the model requires students to become accustomed to its various activities over repeated implementation
