# Four-day instructional cycle moving from explicit instruction to collaborative application

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The intervention organized instruction into repeating 4-day cycles: "The intervention followed a specific sequence of instruction in 4-day instructional cycles." Day 1 provided explicit instruction and tutor modeling; Day 2 added supported and independent practice; Day 3 gave independent practice with trade books; Day 4 used a collaborative reading approach with student coaches applying the strategy to a biographical text. This sequence scaffolds learners from modeling toward peer-mediated application.
