# A spiral school astronomy curriculum that revisits the Sun across grade levels and culminates in student research

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
At Gimnasio Campestre in Bogotá, astronomy teaching is organized as a progression in which younger children learn basics of orientation and observation, and older grades take subject blocks such as "a.) origin of matter and the universe, b.) the light and the Sun and c.) gravity and asteroid impacts". From seventh to eleventh grade students may deepen into a curricular unit called "Jóvenes Investigadores" (young researchers). The Sun anchors activities that recur every year, building toward independent student research and astrophotography.
