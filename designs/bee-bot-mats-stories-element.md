# Bee-Bot floor robot with research-designed mats and ludic stories for early-childhood programming activities

## Description
The Bee-Bot is a bee-shaped floor robot programmed with buttons for advance, reverse, turning, start, pause and deleting commands; the article notes "The robot displaces in 15 cm movements, makes 90º turns and stores up to 40 instructions in its memory". Researchers designed mats explicitly for the training objectives and elaborated a story for each rug, presented before each challenge to situate it in a playful, motivating context suited to young children.
