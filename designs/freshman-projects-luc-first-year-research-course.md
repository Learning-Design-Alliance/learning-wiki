# Freshman Projects: a required first-year research course with mentor-led small groups

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Freshman Projects program engages all physics majors in research starting in their first year, running continuously since the 1995-1996 academic year. Students take it in the Spring of their first year in sections with a maximum enrollment of 3-5 students, agree on a project with a faculty mentor, write a proposal, and meet weekly for two hours. The course structure is designed to model several aspects of scientific research, from proposal to final presentation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each group must choose a topic of research in consultation with their mentor and write a proposal including a statement of the problem, background literature review, materials list, budget, and timeline
#### Constraints
- Projects are appropriate for first-year physics students and do not always involve cutting edge research

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate physics majors

### Learning Goals
- deepening understanding of introductory physics concepts
- familiarizing students with research methods employed in the field
