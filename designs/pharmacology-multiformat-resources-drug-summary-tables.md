# Multi-format pharmacology curricular resources with editable drug summary tables

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The curriculum supplies pharmacology content in multiple formats so students can use their preferred mode: voice-over PowerPoint videos, slides with html links, concise written notes with mechanism-of-action diagrams, self-study questions ranging from simple recall to USMLE-style vignettes, and drug summary tables listing class, mechanism, side effects, and toxicities. "These summary tables are supplied in a Word document that can be downloaded and edited by the student with additional notes from the case presentation." The authors report the summary tables were well received as study aids.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Recorded videos released to students at least 2 weeks before the case presentation
- Multiple short videos of 10–15 minutes rather than one long video, to help students organize and digest information
