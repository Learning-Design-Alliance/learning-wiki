# Summer bridge programs combine orientation, advising, college-success skills training, and accelerated coursework in the 2–4 week bridge period

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Summer bridge programs occur in the summer period between high school and college and, although content varies across institutions and populations, share a characteristic multi-component design. The report states they "typically last 2–4 weeks and involve (a) an in-depth orientation to college life and resources, (b) academic advising, (c) training in skills necessary for college success (e.g., time management and study skills), and/or (d) accelerated academic coursework." Nonacademic components are designed to provide cultural and social capital and promote adjustment to college culture.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programs must be delivered in the summer bridge period between high school and college
#### Constraints
- Content varies across institutions and by the population served; components are combined with 'and/or', so not all programs include all four

### Target Learners
- Incoming college freshmen, historically targeting ethnic/racial minority, low-income, first-generation, or other at-risk populations

### Learning Goals
- College readiness, adjustment to college culture, time management and study skills, academic preparation for college-level work
