# Inclusive meeting and facility design: accessibility registration questions, lactation rooms, gender-neutral bathrooms, and audio/visual access technology

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A bundle of facility and technology practices the section layered onto its meetings: an ADA comment box in online registration asking about accessibility needs, a lactation room added to facility requirements and advertised on postcards, gender-neutral bathroom availability, and equitable access to presentation technology. The lactation room minimum is "a place to sit, an electric outlet and a lockable door", close to a bathroom if it lacks a sink. Technology practices include required microphone use, auto captioning in Microsoft PowerPoint365, QR codes or short links to online versions of slides, and supplying common laptop-to-projector connectors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Online registration system for the accessibility question; host-site agreements that specify facility requirements; a functional audio system or a portable one for inadequate venues
#### Constraints
- Gender-neutral bathroom accessibility is site specific, especially in older buildings; the microphone requirement was adopted only recently after a long-time member with hearing trouble struggled; recorded and closed-captioned meeting videos remain a future hope

### Target Learners
- Physics teachers attending section meetings, including people with disabilities, nursing parents, and attendees with hearing or viewing difficulties

### Learning Goals
- Full participation in professional meetings regardless of disability, caregiving status, or gender identity
