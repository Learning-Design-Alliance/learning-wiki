# Four-day pain-medicine course combining simulated-patient training and small-group bedside teaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An elective course, "Competence and Safety in Pain Medicine", combining training with simulated patients (two short lectures and three scenarios with feedback) and clinical teaching on the ward in acute and chronic pain medicine. "The training took place in small groups with three students trained with simulated patients, as well as learning in clinical situations with patients on a ward." The course ran 4 days from 8:30 am to 03:00 pm, with days one and three as training days and days two and four as exam days, and included interprofessional instruction by physicians and a pain nurse.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Four instructors for teaching 12 students per week on training days and two instructors on assessment days
- Simulated patients drawn from an experienced amateur-actor SP team who completed scenario- and feedback-training
- Checklist-based practical examinations scoring communication and therapy skills
#### Constraints
- Capacity limited to a maximum of 12 students per week
- Personnel expenses were already very high for the instructor ratio used

### Target Learners
- medical students in the 8th semester with interest in pain medicine

### Learning Goals
- patient-oriented conversation criteria
- physical examination of a pain patient
- post-operative pain therapy according to the WHO level scheme
- recognizing epidural haematoma symptoms
