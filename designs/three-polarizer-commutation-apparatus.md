# Low-cost three-polarizer commutation-relation apparatus (laser diode, three adjustable polarizers, photometer)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A benchtop optical apparatus in which a 630–680 nm laser diode shines through three polarizers with adjustable polarizing angles (0°, 45°, 90°) onto a TT Technic VC1010 photometer measuring 0.1 to 50,000 lux with 0.1 lux resolution. Each polarizer represents a quantum operator, and measured luminosities encode whether operators commute. The authors stress that "The experimental tools are chosen to be easily accessible and economic", unlike earlier costly demonstrations.
