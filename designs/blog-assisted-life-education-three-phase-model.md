# Three-phase ideal model for blog-assisted life education instruction (preparation, implementation, finish)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article proposes an ideal instructional model, shown in Figure 5, organized into three phases. In preparation, teachers design topics, become familiar with blog functions and arrange heterogeneous grouping; in implementation, film watching and experiential activities come first, followed by blog publication, cross-group reading, comments and revision; in the finish phase, "Instructional films, learning sheets, instructional data, activity photos, and portfolios are placed on the blogs for students to browse at any time."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must be familiar with blog use and functions so students can successfully share learning on the blogs
- Heterogeneous grouping arranged with attention to special students' physical and mental development
#### Constraints
- The authors note grouping can never be perfect and some groups continued to have disputes
- Blog instruction was slow and confusing at the beginning of the experiment

### Target Learners
- fifth-grade elementary school students

### Learning Goals
- life education values
- collaborative learning
- information literacy
