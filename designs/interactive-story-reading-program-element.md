# Interactive Story Reading Program: nine researcher-authored stories on scientific concepts with pre- and post-story activities

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An eight-week classroom program in which experiment group teachers, after three days of training, read nine stories on scientific concepts authored by the researchers using the interactive story reading method, with preparatory and evaluative activities. The article states teachers "had read the nine stories on scientific concepts, authored by the researchers, using the interactive story reading method, and implemented activities to prepare for and to evaluate the stories." Activities ran two days a week for 45-50 minutes a day, with the researcher providing ongoing guidance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers received a three-days training on the method's features, communication with children, physical environment, stimulants, and methods and techniques before application
#### Constraints
- The program was implemented with 48-72 month old children at nurseries of primary schools in Ankara; the article does not report effects outside this setting

### Target Learners
- preschool children aged 48-72 months

### Learning Goals
- receptive language vocabulary
- expressive language vocabulary
