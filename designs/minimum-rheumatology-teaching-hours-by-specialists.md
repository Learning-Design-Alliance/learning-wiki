# Mandate a minimum required number of dedicated rheumatology teaching hours delivered by specialists

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The review recommends that undergraduate programmes introduce a minimum dedicated number of rheumatology teaching hours delivered by specialists, because limited exposure has decreased student awareness of the specialty and is associated with poor career uptake. The authors state the need "to not only introduce a minimum hour of rheumatology-specific teaching delivered by the those in the specialty". This addresses the wide variation in hours (4–120 h) found across institutions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dedicated curriculum time allocated specifically to rheumatology
- Delivery by rheumatology specialists rather than only adjacent specialties
#### Constraints
- Cross-sectional evidence base; the review notes results may not represent current teaching levels

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Learning Goals
- rheumatology and musculoskeletal clinical knowledge
- career awareness of rheumatology
