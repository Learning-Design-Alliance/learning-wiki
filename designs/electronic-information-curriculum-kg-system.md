# Automated multi-source curriculum knowledge graph system for Electronic Information with over 60k entities and 80k triples

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article constructs multi-source, large-scale knowledge graphs for Electronic Information courses from textbooks, slides and syllabi, imported into the Neo4j graph database for visualization. Students can search knowledge concepts and view node attributes; the graphs cover "more than 60k entities and 80k triples" and are built automatically rather than manually.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Textbooks and course slides must be converted from original format to editable format before automated extraction
#### Constraints
- Tailored for the major of Electronic Information, though the authors state the framework is applicable to other disciplines

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in Electronic Information

### Learning Goals
- understanding relationships between concepts and courses across the curriculum
