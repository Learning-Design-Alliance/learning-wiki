# University of Oslo Computing in Science Education project and its synchronized physics bachelor program

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The Computing in Science Education project at the University of Oslo, later the Center for Computing in Science Education (a Center of Excellence in Education), has since 2003 introduced computing across disciplines in a synchronized way. Its elements include "a compulsory programming course with a strong mathematical flavour", coordination of computational exercises in most undergraduate courses, staff development support, and new textbooks.
