# Simulation-based lab manuals with step-by-step lesson videos for introductory physics I laboratories

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A series of simulation-based lab manuals developed for the laboratory component of introductory physics I, paired with lesson videos that provide "a step-by-step process of simulation, data collection, and data analysis with Microsoft Excel." The manuals support the set of aligned experiments accompanying the course, and computer simulations are also used during short lectures to visualize concepts, which the authors report as the most effective way to initialize a new topic.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Aligned laboratory experiments for the course
- Microsoft Excel for data analysis
- Lesson videos demonstrating the simulation, data collection, and analysis workflow
#### Constraints
- Developed for the introductory calculus-based physics I laboratory at a community college

### Target Learners
- community college students in introductory calculus-based physics I

### Learning Goals
- experimental simulation, data collection, and data analysis
- conceptual understanding of mechanics
