# Tracker-based video analysis of the historical Huaso jump recording as a kinematics data source

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The element is the kinematic analysis of the 1949 Huaso–Larraguibel jump audiovisual record using the free Tracker software, which "makes it possible to sequence videos and extract motion data frame by frame". Frames 63 to 131 were selected, covering 68 frames at a 0.02 s interval (1.36 s total), tracking points including the horse muzzle, forelimb, hind limb, and the horse and rider centers of mass. The known 2.47 m obstacle height served as the spatial scale standard; horizontal displacement was discarded due to camera motion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A known reference length in the recording (the 2.47 m obstacle height) to establish the spatial scale
#### Constraints
- The recording presents a slight angular sweep and variations in camera height, so horizontal displacement was discarded and the study focused on vertical motion
- All available audiovisual records originate from the same historical video

### Target Learners
- physics students analyzing real video data

### Learning Goals
- uniformly accelerated motion
- kinematics and dynamics of vertical motion
- energy and momentum concepts
