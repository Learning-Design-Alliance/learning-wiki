# Learners' Integrative Collaboration Model

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A model developed by the author in which learners act as a united unit in a team context, constructively reconciling their actions while each participant is responsible for his/her own part of the work. It defines seven functions of integrative collaboration (learning, socio-cultural, communicative, self-realisation, diagnostic, correction, motivating) and didactic principles including learners' cognitive activation, learning process intensification, learning material flexibility and the reflection principle. The article developed and experimentally verified the model for teaching Building Constructions at Riga Construction College.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A common objective and fulfilling tasks assigned by the teacher in which each individual expresses creativity and individuality as part of a fully integrated team
- Pedagogical conditions including content enrichment, compromise and cooperation as conflict-resolution strategies, non-verbal communication tools and a changed approach to evaluation
#### Constraints
- Tested and can be used for teaching the study subject Building Constructions at Riga Construction College

### Target Learners
- vocational secondary school learners studying basic level building management

### Learning Goals
- working in a team
- mutual learning
- business communication
- creative action
- professional self-education and creative potential
