# E-learning course on physician-patient communication with embedded reflection prompts

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A three-module, self-study e-learning course (45 minutes per module) preparing first-year clinical students for simulated patient interviews. “Each module combined theoretical content with quizzes and videos of simulated physician-patient interactions”, and each module ended with three guided reflection prompts adapted from Koole et al., covering awareness, understanding, and transfer. A minimum of 500 characters per prompt was required, reflections were ungraded, and students had to answer to proceed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must answer the reflection prompts to proceed in the course
- Minimum of 500 characters excluding spaces per prompt
#### Constraints
- Designed to prepare students for a subsequent in-person teaching session; no prior training in reflective thinking

### Target Learners
- first-year clinical medical students

### Learning Goals
- physician-patient communication skills
- consultation structuring
- empathy and emotions
