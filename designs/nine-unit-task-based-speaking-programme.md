# Nine-unit, eleven-lesson task-based speaking programme organised by function and genre

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The programme taught speaking skills through "nine units comprising eleven lessons", each unit focusing on "a particular main function or a certain spoken genre". Across the lessons students performed tasks such as describing and finding differences, discussing ideas and opinions, solving problems, conducting interviews, exchanging information in information-gap tasks, narrating stories to peers, and role-playing social and interactional functions. Sample lessons were reviewed by four ESL specialists, who approved the programme and suggested giving students more practice and allocating more teaching time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each unit focuses on a particular main function or spoken genre
- Tasks spanning description, discussion, problem solving, interviewing, information gap, narration and role play
#### Constraints
- Sample lessons were reviewed by four ESL specialists, who suggested more practice and more teaching time would be appropriate

### Target Learners
- first-year university ESL students

### Learning Goals
- overall speaking proficiency
- social and interactional spoken functions
