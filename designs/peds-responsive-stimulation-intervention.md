# PEDS responsive stimulation intervention delivered by Lady Health Workers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The PEDS trial was a 2x2 factorial, community-based cluster-randomized control trial with a responsive stimulation arm and an enhanced nutrition arm. Lady Health Workers delivered an adaptation of the UNICEF and WHO Care for the Child Development package, which "promotes caregivers' sensitivity and responsiveness through developmentally appropriate play activities", via home visits and monthly group community meetings until children reached age two. Caregivers tried age-appropriate activities while workers coached them and gave feedback on interaction quality.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivery by trained local community women (Lady Health Workers) through home visits and group meetings integrated within existing health services
#### Constraints
- The intervention was delivered only to a birth cohort until age two; the study focuses on the responsive stimulation arm with the enhanced nutrition intervention controlled for in all models

### Target Learners
- children from birth to age two in rural, disadvantaged communities in Sindh, Pakistan, and their primary caregivers

### Learning Goals
- caregivers' sensitivity and responsiveness
- children's cognitive development and school readiness
