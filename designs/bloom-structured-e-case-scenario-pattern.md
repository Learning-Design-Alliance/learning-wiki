# Bloom's-taxonomy-structured e-case scenario from knowledge to evaluation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each e-case is constructed along Bloom's taxonomy criteria, moving through six levels. At the knowledge level students identify differential diagnoses from a presenting history; at comprehension they learn pathomechanisms through visual graphs and a question-based feedback system; application requires classifying trauma type and clinical decision-making; synthesis trains cross-linking thinking on comorbidity and research gaps; analysis and evaluation involve portfolio work, feedback reflection, and OSCE performance. This gives the article's case collection a reusable lesson-level design structure.
