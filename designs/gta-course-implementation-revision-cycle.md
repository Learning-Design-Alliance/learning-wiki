# Yearly cycle of implementation and revision guided by instructional design, Design Research, and Action Research

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course evolves through a yearly cycle: fall delivery with ongoing assessments and instructor observations, spring compilation of assessments and planning of revisions using GTA feedback and supervisor input, and summer crafting of the revised curriculum. The article grounds changes to the curriculum in the principles of instructional design and situates the process within Design Research and Action Research, in which feedback from the GTAs is crucial for the revision part of the process.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor observations and assessments collected during the fall semester
- GTA feedback on what was useful and what needs improvement
- Input from GTA supervisors about changes to teaching assignments
#### Constraints
- Cycle is annual (fall implementation, spring planning, summer revision) and situated in a department with CTL partnership

### Target Learners
- first-time physics GTAs (first-year Ph.D. students)

### Learning Goals
- effective teaching of labs and recitations
- professional development
