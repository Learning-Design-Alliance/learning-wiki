# Four-week situated vocational English curriculum pairing in-class authentic tasks with parallel out-class video and e-mail activities

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A unit-level instructional pattern in which each week anchors target vocabulary in a realistic accounting situation: a balance sheet video with group problem-solving, analyzing a cash flow statement and writing a response e-mail as an accountant, discussing a real local shop's weekly sales report, and computing financial ratios from self-found statements. Students prepared videos outside class, shared them in a class whatsapp group, and gave peer feedback while the instructor provided instructional support. The article states this design "included out-class authentic activities which were parallel to in-class activities", which the authors say minimized misunderstandings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- access to video-recording tools and a class communication group for sharing and peer feedback
- instructor availability to model vocabulary and provide feedback
#### Constraints
- students perceived weekly assignments as time consuming and overloaded

### Target Learners
- undergraduate Accounting Information Systems students in vocational English courses

### Learning Goals
- vocational English vocabulary for accounting
- communicating about financial statements in English
