# Bottom-up lecture sequence from atoms to materials to devices to systems as the organizing structure of the course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course content is organized as a bottom-up progression: lectures begin "developing an atomistic approach towards the materials and structures , then understanding various devices based on the material characteristics and finally more complex systems comprising of various devices". This contrasts with "the top-down approach of the conventional engineering design". Supporting topics include quantum transport, nanoscale devices and systems, nanofabrication aided by Silicon Run documentaries and lab tours, and nanoscale characterization.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Topic list spanning atomic structure, electronic structure, quantum transport, nanoscale devices and systems, nanofabrication, and characterization
- Laboratory tours and documentaries to support the fabrication and characterization units
#### Constraints
- Designed for a first-year seminar audience, treating each topic "in an introductory manner in a fairly informal setting"

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate engineering students

### Learning Goals
- understand the bottom-up approach to nanoscale engineering
- understand how atoms form materials, devices, and systems
