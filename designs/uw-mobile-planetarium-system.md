# UW Mobile Planetarium: inflatable dome, laptop, HD projector and first-surface mirrors projecting WorldWide Telescope

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A transportable inflatable dome system that projects WorldWide Telescope from a laptop through an off-the-shelf HD projector and first-surface mirrors onto the dome interior, sized to fit a classroom and a class of about 30 high school students. It cost "$14,000 in parts" including a $1,500 laptop, funded within an HST/EPO grant, and is run by approximately ten undergraduate volunteers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- First-surface mirrors, since coated mirrors produce blurry images as light reflects from the coating's interior surface at a new angle
- A laptop with a video card capable of extending an HD display and dedicated hard drive space for WWT to cache imagery
#### Constraints
- A fish-eye lens solution was found to be prohibitively expensive as a single purchase
- Dome height limited to no more than 10 feet so two to three undergraduates can transport it by car and it fits a classroom

### Target Learners
- high school science students
- Seattle community audiences

### Learning Goals
- astronomy education
- visualizing astronomical systems from moons to galaxies
