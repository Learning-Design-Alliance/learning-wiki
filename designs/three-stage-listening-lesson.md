# Three-stage listening lesson: pre-listening, while-listening, post-listening

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a lesson-level structure for listening instruction in which "A good listening activity consists of three stages: pre-listening, while-listening and post-listening". Pre-listening prepares students for what they will hear (about 10 to 15 minutes at most, per Houston); while-listening has students listen and complete tasks, with the recording played a few times with a different task each time; post-listening gives feedback and lets the teacher analyse difficulties. Table 1 contrasts early practices (pre-teaching vocabulary for maximum understanding) with current practices (establishing context, creating motivation, pre-teaching only critical vocabulary, and final play with transcripts).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each stage needs its own activity: preparation before listening, a task during listening, and feedback after listening
#### Constraints
- The pre-listening stage should not be too long, about 10 to 15 minutes at the most, per the article citing Houston (2016)
