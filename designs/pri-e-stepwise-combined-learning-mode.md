# PRI-E: a step-by-step combined learning mode sequencing PBL, CBL and EBL under a Passion-Research-Innovation philosophy

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
PRI-E (Passion, Research, Innovation, Education) is a combined learning mode that sequences problem-based, case-based and evidence-based learning in three ordered steps: students first give a faculty-assigned PBL presentation, then a CBL presentation once the PBL content is delivered well, then receive an EBL task and join a higher-level contest. The article states that "PRI-E adopts a step-by-step approach to guide students through orderly progress" and that this individualized mode lets students grasp the essence of PBL, CBL and EBL. Sessions run 80 minutes weekly over a six-month rotation with identical content across arms.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- PBL first requires little clinical experience and builds confidence before the heavier CBL and EBL presentations
- CBL and EBL steps require more student experience and workload, so they are assigned only after the PBL content is delivered well
- Staff must receive pre-job training and control the presentation delivery and clinical practice details
#### Constraints
- Weaknesses reported: lack of systematic teaching content leads to low efficiency in a single class and staff inexperience lowers initial efficiency
- Extra time demands may cause student dissatisfaction and anxiety under exam-oriented education
- Short internship time and busy clinical work can adversely affect learning outcomes

### Target Learners
- postgraduate stomatology students rotating in oral and maxillofacial head and neck oncology

### Learning Goals
- clinical presentation skills
- critical thinking and problem solving
- evidence-based clinical decision making
- communication with patients
