# Blended learning design combining H5P self-directed modules, flipped-classroom face-to-face sessions, standardized patient practice, and AI tool exploration

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The course design sequences digital self-directed learning before in-person application: three H5P online modules delivered via ILIAS cover narrative communication, structured information gathering (WWSZ technique, four-sides model), and technological advancements including genAI tools. Four face-to-face sessions follow a flipped-classroom model, "focused on discussion and consolidation of previously covered content, as well as its application in practice-oriented scenarios", including standardized patient consultations with structured feedback and exploration of Ada Health, arriba, and a Character.AI patient chatbot.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- H5P modules delivered via a learning management system with sequentially released content
- Access to a standardized patient program for face-to-face communication training
#### Constraints
- Local data protection regulations prevented collection of objective learning management system analytics

### Target Learners
- Sixth- and eighth-semester medical students

### Learning Goals
- Patient-centered physician–patient communication
- Communication with digitally informed patients and use of AI-based tools
