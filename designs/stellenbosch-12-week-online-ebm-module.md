# Stellenbosch 12-week fully online EBM module for Family Medicine registrars

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 12-week, completely online EBM module within the Family Medicine division at Stellenbosch University, offered to medical specialists in their first year of training, averaging 50 students annually, mostly based outside Cape Town and across Africa. Structured into themes mirroring the steps of EBM — from phrasing questions and searching the literature, through critical appraisal using the GATE framework, to applying evidence and a final assignment based on a knowledge gap from the student's own practice. Since 2008 it includes formative individual assessments and group work, with a discussion forum replacing earlier chat room sessions.
