# Integrated flipped classroom design framework combining FL cycles, SRL cycles, and metacognitive FC orientation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes a conceptual framework of FC design with three main aspects: Flipped Learning (FL) Cycle, Self-Regulated Learning (SRL) Cycle, and Metacognitive FC Orientation. It is founded on interconnected theories — First Principles of Instruction, Self-Regulated Learning, the MUSIC Model of Academic Motivation, and Metacognition — because "a successful flipped classroom requires not only effective and engaging instructions and activities, but also self-regulated and motivated learners with metacognitive awareness." The FL cycle comprises online, in-class, and after-class stages; SRL cycles run in parallel with Proactive Planning, Performance Monitoring, and Learning Reflection stages; and orientation develops students' metacognitive knowledge about FC tasks, strategies, and themselves as learners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires integration of the three SRL phases with recurring pre-class, in-class, and after-class learning activities to develop students' self-regulation skills required in FC
- Requires effective FC design guided by a robust instructional design framework grounded in learning theories
#### Constraints
- The authors state there are no existing FC studies that have fully implemented the entire framework; supporting evidence comes from three studies that incorporated one or multiple design aspects

### Learning Goals
