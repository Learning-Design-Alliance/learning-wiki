# IVR mLearning system combining interactive voice response, SMS prompts, and spaced education on simple mobile phones

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A prototype mLearning system built on open source technologies (FreeSWITCH for voice, Gammu for SMS, Moodle for quiz management) that delivered audio-recorded multiple choice questions and explanations to any type of phone. Participants received a daily SMS prompt, texted back when ready, and answered questions via telephone keypad, hearing detailed explanations after each answer. The course used "a spaced-education approach in which questions and detailed explanations are spaced and repeated over time", retiring questions after two correct answers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants need a simple mobile phone with network reception; the system requires a telephony platform, SMS modem, and quiz management software
- A 3-hour orientation prepared participants to use the system
#### Constraints
- The medium does not permit clinical practice, demonstrations, instructor interaction, or display of images such as diagrams and graphs
- Reliability of cellular networks in remote areas remains a challenge

### Target Learners
- public-sector nurses and midwives in Senegal

### Learning Goals
- management of contraceptive side effects and misconceptions
