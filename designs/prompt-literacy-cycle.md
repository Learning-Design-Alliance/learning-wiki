# Prompt Literacy Cycle: a five-stage recursive structure for daily prompting practice

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Prompt Literacy Cycle is the instructional tool nested within Phase Two of the framework, giving students a concrete, repeatable structure for developing prompt literacy at the level of daily practice. It guides students through five recursive stages: clarify purpose, craft the prompt, engage with output, refine the prompt, and reflect. The authors state it encourages experimentation, reflection, and interactive improvement in students' interactions with LLMs, and can be introduced through demonstrations, scaffolded assignments, or workshops.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors introduce it through demonstrations, scaffolded assignments, or workshops so students internalize both the mechanics of prompting and the ethical and epistemological dimensions of LLM engagement
#### Constraints
- It operates at the level of daily practice, while the five-phase model guides the overall arc of LLM-integrated learning

### Target Learners
- higher education students

### Learning Goals
- prompt literacy
- critical interpretation of LLM outputs
- reflective revision
