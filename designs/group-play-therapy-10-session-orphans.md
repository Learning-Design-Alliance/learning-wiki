# Group play therapy programme (10 sessions) for orphans

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A group play therapy intervention delivered to orphaned children aged 6–10 in an orphanage setting, carried out "for the treatment group during 10 sessions". The article describes play as "a structured and treatment theory-based approach, which lay the foundations for learning processes and natural and normal children's relationships". Outcomes were measured with the SSRS teacher form and RADQ before, immediately after, and two months after the programme.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered in group format to children selected for elevated insecure attachment and low social skills
- Measured with teacher-rated SSRS and caretaker-rated RADQ
#### Constraints
- Studied only with orphans aged 6–10 in one orphanage in Ahvaz City

### Target Learners
- Orphaned children aged 6–10

### Learning Goals
- Reduce insecure attachment
- Increase collaboration, assertiveness and self-control social skills
