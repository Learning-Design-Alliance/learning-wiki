# Twelve-approach evaluation matrix for a RaT programme (Table 4)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes, in Table 4, twelve approaches for evaluating a RaT programme at the Faculty of Medicine of Sousse, inspired by the Kirkpatrick model. Each approach pairs an outcome with measures: self-assessment (self-reflection journals, checklists, rubrics), peer evaluation, student feedback, direct observation with standardised tools, Objective Structured Teaching Exercises (OSTEs) assessing "Teaching skills in simulated scenarios", teaching portfolios, assessment of student learning outcomes via pre- and post-tests, longitudinal tracking, faculty mentor feedback, programme evaluation surveys, comparative analysis of participants versus non-participants, and qualitative interviews or focus groups among the faculty scientific council.
