# Learning-context personalization engine: mastery, spacing, and help-seeking signals guide when and how AI assistance is delivered

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's learning-context modeling approach maintains student-level learning state to tailor support and sequencing. The engine 'aggregates signals such as mastery by concept, attempt history, time-on-task, spacing intervals, and help-seeking behavior', and on that basis determines when to recommend review, when to escalate to teacher attention, and what scaffolding type fits the student's current context. This operationalizes feedback, self-regulated help-seeking, and spaced-practice mechanism families from the learning sciences inside the LMS.
