# Competency-Driven Hemostasis Training (CDHT): a three-component procedural skills training program

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
CDHT is a training program the authors developed for radial artery compression hemostasis, comprising "three progressive components": baseline assessment of residents' current techniques and outcomes, didactic instruction using a three-dimensional vascular reconstruction model plus evidence-based compression parameters, and supervised hands-on training with competency verification. In the study, 10 first-year anesthesiology residents completed it, and their patient outcomes before and after training were compared.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Baseline assessment of trainees' current techniques, didactic instruction, and supervised hands-on practice with competency verification, as the article structures the program
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only with anesthesiology residents at a single center; the authors state this may limit applicability to other specialties or settings

### Target Learners
- early-career anesthesiology residents

### Learning Goals
- safe and effective radial artery compression hemostasis after catheter removal
