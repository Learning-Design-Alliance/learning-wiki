# MORAD ESCAPE: a five-station research-based escape room for assessing research competencies

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
MORAD ESCAPE is a research-based escape room at Tehran University of Medical Sciences designed to assess research competencies of health professions students. The game "was structured across five stations, following a linear plot" so teams could not skip ahead, with puzzles unlocking clues rather than directly teaching content. Sessions lasted 90 minutes; 60 students in 12 groups of five participated voluntarily, and game masters monitored via cameras with two-way radios for hints.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A research competency checklist and game evaluation checklist; game masters; a scenario-aligned physical location; orientation meeting before play
#### Constraints
- Puzzles were not intended to serve direct educational purposes but to facilitate access to research-related data; participation was voluntary, which the authors note may have introduced selection bias

### Target Learners
- health professions students at Tehran University of Medical Sciences

### Learning Goals
- research competencies including literature searching, data extraction, methodology, and statistics interpretation
