# Constructive alignment as the design rationale linking learning objectives, case-based teaching, and collaborative assessment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article applies the principle of constructive alignment to justify the whisper exam design: the curricular learning objectives, teaching methods and assessment format are aligned so that “assessments validly reflect students’ achievement of these objectives”. The assessed learning objectives covered clinical topics in medical microbiology, virology, and infection control, “all of which had been covered in the preceding laboratory course mostly in a case-based format”. The authors state instructors are expected to clearly communicate learning objectives and align teaching methods accordingly.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clearly communicated learning objectives
- Teaching methods aligned to those objectives
- Assessment format that validly reflects achievement of the objectives
#### Constraints
- The alignment depends on the preceding laboratory course having covered the assessed objectives in a case-based format

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate medical students

### Learning Goals
- Clinical reasoning and diagnostic decision-making in infectious disease topics
