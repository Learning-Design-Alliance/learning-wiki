# Align field-trip activities with state curriculum objectives via a correlation chart

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The packet aligns every activity with state standards so teachers can justify the trip instructionally. A correlation chart "shows how each activity in this Environmental Education Learning Experience (EELE) correlates with the North Carolina Department of Public Instruction (DPI) objectives in science, mathematics, social studies and English language arts," listing objective numbers by grade with a pointer to the DPI Teacher Handbook for full descriptions. Each activity page also prints its own curriculum objectives by grade.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers need the DPI Teacher Handbook for each subject area to interpret the listed objective numbers
#### Constraints
- Alignment is specific to the North Carolina standard course of study

### Target Learners
- Grades 5-8 students

### Target Learning Objectives
- Science, mathematics, social studies, and English language arts objectives addressed through environmental education
