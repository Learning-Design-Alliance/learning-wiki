# Eight-module radiological science e-learning course with weekly e-mail reminders, self-assessment questions and staffed online Q&A

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The TEL condition was an online course on the Virtual University of Bavaria platform comprising "eight online modules" containing texts, images, graphs, videos, hyperlinks, reference lists and tests. Each module was planned for one week, opened with learning targets and initial multiple-choice self-assessment questions, ended with further questions with automated feedback, and was prompted by an invitation e-mail and a reminder after three days. Students could post questions on the Medlearn platform, answered by academic staff within 24 hours.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students had to sign in to the e-learning platform to access the online content
- An optional introductory session familiarized students with the platform to avoid non-users
#### Constraints
- During the course no questions were posted on the online platform, despite the possibility of contacting staff online at all times

### Target Learners
- third-year dental students

### Learning Goals
- theoretical radiological science: radiation physics, X-ray production and interactions, radiation dose, imaging equipment, radiation protection, image creation, normal radiological anatomy of teeth and jaws
