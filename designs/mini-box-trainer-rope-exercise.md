# Take-home mini box trainer with marked and unmarked rope exercises for laparoscopic bowel length estimation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A low-cost take-home training apparatus built from a plexiglass Alphatron Surgical box trainer without a camera, fitted with two laparoscopic graspers, holding a 3-meter rope fixed at its starting point to simulate Treitz's ligament. The article states it "makes it possible to be used multiple times, at low cost, and can be used to practice at home next to a laparoscopic box with a camera system." Exercise one uses a rope marked every 5 cm, then 10 cm, then 50 cm; exercise two uses an unmarked rope with tape-measure validation feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A plexiglass box trainer base with two laparoscopic graspers and a 3-meter rope fixed at its starting point
#### Constraints
- The box trainer does not use a laparoscopic camera; graspers and cord are manipulated under direct sight, excluding camera magnification and giving 3D instead of 2D sight
