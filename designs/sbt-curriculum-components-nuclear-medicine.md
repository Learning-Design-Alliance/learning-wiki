# SBT curriculum components: virtual simulations, mannequin-based practice, radiation-free exercises, emergency scenario training, and AI-driven performance tracking

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The SBT curriculum at University A comprised five components: virtual simulations replicating PET/CT and SPECT imaging procedures and radiation safety protocols; mannequin-based training for patient care, injection techniques, and positioning; radiation-free practice for dose calculation, camera operation, and image processing; emergency scenario training for radiopharmaceutical spills, adverse patient reactions, and equipment malfunctions; and "real-time feedback and artificial-intelligence-driven performance tracking provided individualized assessment." These components aimed to develop technical competencies prior to clinical exposure.
