# Embed climate resilience education in climatology or hydrometeorology courses based on experiential learning models

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that climate resilience education be included in climatology or hydrometeorology courses in Indonesian universities so students can transform educational outcomes in society. The climate resilience education model can apply experiential learning models such as problem-based learning, project-based learning, and discovery-inquiry learning, within a life-based curriculum that provides space for implementation in the context of life in society.
