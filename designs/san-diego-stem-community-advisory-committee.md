## Description
The CAC is a cross-sector coalition formed in 2024 to lay the groundwork for regional STEM pathway initiatives. The report describes "a robust coalition of local and regional partners working to ensure alignment with the local economy, community context, and values". This 14-member committee spans early childhood education, K-12, postsecondary, workforce development, community-based organizations, and philanthropy. Members meet monthly, connect asynchronously between meetings, and have established collaboration principles, engaged learners and industry leaders in co-design, and developed four solution concepts for STEM pathways.
