# Quantum Interactive Learning Tutorials (QuILTs)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
QuILTs are interactive learning tutorials being developed and evaluated by the author to reduce documented student difficulties with quantum mechanics formalism relevant to quantum computing. The article states the project is "developing and evaluating quantum interactive learning tutorials (QuILTs) to reduce the difﬁculties," targeting state preparation, time development, measurement, and two level systems and product space. The tutorials "can provide scaffolding support to students in science and engineering pursuing quantum computing research."
