# Design learning technology for and by students: let a student who took the course lead its tool development

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article frames WinterLab as "a case of design both for and by students, with the lead designer undertaking the project after attending the course in question." The lead designer, then an undergraduate who had taken the course the previous year, developed the platform as a foundational step in her graduate career, gaining skills and confidence through the design process itself. The authors argue this rich individual learning opportunity, broadened by interactions with course students, produced a versatile and accessible learning platform.
