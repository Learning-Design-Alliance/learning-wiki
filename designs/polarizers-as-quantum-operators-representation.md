# Polarizers-as-operators representation: light polarization states model quantum operators, commutation relations and qubit systems

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The article maps geometric-optics polarization onto quantum formalism: each polarizer is an operator (Â1 horizontal 0°, Â2 diagonal 45°, Â3 vertical 90°) acting on light states written in Dirac notation, where acting on non-polarized state the horizontal polarization operator results in |H>, the vertical in |V>, and the diagonal in |H>+|V>. Order-dependent light transmission then physically embodies commuting versus non-commuting operators, connecting to the Heisenberg uncertainty principle. The authors extend the mapping: polarization states can be identified with spin-1/2 states, turning the polarizers into spin-orientation mechanisms in "qubit (quantum bit) systems".
