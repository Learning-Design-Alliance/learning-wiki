# Implement SSCS+MS learning in classes with low academic ability students to develop creative thinking

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "teachers implement SSCS + MS learning model in schools or classes that have low academic ability students," because the model improved low academic students' creative thinking and fostered "awareness of learning and awareness of thinking." It argues the model's collaborative problem solving plus underlining and self-assessing makes low academic students self-conscious, independent learners.
