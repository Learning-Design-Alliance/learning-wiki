# Kern's six-step framework combined with a shared governance model guides nursing AMS curriculum development

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article uses Kern's six-step framework for healthcare curriculum design—problem identification, targeted needs assessment, goals and objectives, educational strategies, implementation, and evaluation and feedback—integrated with a shared governance model to guide the development of a nursing AMS curriculum. The authors state that "Combining the framework and model was necessary for shared decision-making and fostering responsibility and ownership among the departments involved in this initiative." Kern's framework guides implementation by considering factors influencing curriculum outcomes, while the shared governance model guides step-by-step planning by identifying key stakeholders, facilitators, and barriers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Collaboration between the AMS team and nursing department leadership at multiple levels to enable shared decision-making
#### Constraints
- The steps are listed sequentially but were implemented non-sequentially using dynamic and interactive processes

### Target Learners
- Registered nurses at a tertiary healthcare institution

### Learning Goals
- Antimicrobial stewardship knowledge and clinical practice
