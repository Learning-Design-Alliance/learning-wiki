# Middle school science teachers should implement a variety of ESL strategies frequently, for at least 60% of instructional minutes

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Based on their observation findings, the authors advocate focused instructional time engaging ESL strategies that promote academic language in science. They recommend that middle school science teachers "implement a variety of ESL instructional strategies frequently and for periods of at least 60% of their instructional minutes" to make content comprehensible and engaging for ELL students and economically-challenged English-speaking students placed in the same classrooms.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Professional learning that supports teachers in becoming comfortable using ESL strategies in content-area instruction
#### Constraints
- The recommendation is grounded in observed teacher implementation differences, not in student outcome data analyzed in this paper

### Target Learners
- Middle grades English language learners and economically-challenged English-speaking students

### Learning Goals
- Comprehensible, engaging science content and academic English language development
