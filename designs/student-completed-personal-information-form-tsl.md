# Student-completed personal information form for generating personalized Turkish texts

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A 19-item form collecting personal information used to build personalized grammar examples and test questions for foreign learners of Turkish. It was developed after a literature review, consultation with specialists, and asking 45 foreign students which three pieces of personal information they would like to see in a text. Items covered "name and surname, country, date and place of birth, names of parents" and other details such as profession, height, and place of residence.
