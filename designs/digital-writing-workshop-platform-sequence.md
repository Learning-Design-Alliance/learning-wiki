# Digital writing workshop implementation pairing each writing-process stage with a different digital platform

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The experimental implementation carried out "each stage of the writing process (preparation, planning, creating a draft, review, redaction and publication)" using a digital platform chosen for that stage: Spider Scribe for mind maps at preparation, Edmodo for sharing drafts, Story Jumper for revision after feedback, Wikispaces for final revisions, and Emaze for publishing presentations. The programme ran 4 lesson hours per week over 14 weeks, with one hour of story-writing instruction, two hours of platform practice, and one group hour for exchanging ideas and feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- a continually available internet connection and a laboratory or classroom having an adequate number of computers
- students possessing technological tools for use outside school
#### Constraints
- the digital platforms used during the research process were operated on one single type of text (the story)

### Target Learners
- fourth-grade primary school students

### Learning Goals
- story writing skills
- writing motivation
- new literacy skills
