# Four-week asynchronous web-based EBP training program (evidence-gate.com)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A standardized web-based EBP training program delivered asynchronously via a website (https://evidence-gate.com/) with an orientation session on the Blackboard platform. Sessions lasted "45–60 minutes" and nurses were advised to complete "at least two sessions per week", so the program was "intended to be completed in approximately four weeks". Content was validated by five external reviewers and face-validated by seven nurses without EBP experience, and sessions were accessible on laptops, computers, and mobile devices.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Nurses need access to laptops, computers, or mobile devices and an orientation to the learning management system
#### Constraints
- The article notes web-based health education requires instructor time and effort to build, teach, maintain, upgrade the platform, and address technical issues

### Target Learners
- professional nurses with at least one year of employment and a bachelor's degree in nursing or higher

### Learning Goals
- evidence-based practice knowledge, skills, and attitudes including asking answerable questions, acquiring research, critical appraisal, and applying, evaluating, and disseminating evidence
