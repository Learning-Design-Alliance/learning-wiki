# Four business ethics board games varying in ethical sense-making, social interaction modes, and themes

## Description
The study employed four business ethics board games adopted from prior research: LMX: The Game!, the Ethics Argumentation Game, Privacy by Design, and the Trading Trainers Game. They were "adopted from prior research and designed to vary in ethical sense-making, modes of social interaction, and themes", implemented as independent pedagogical tools without teacher facilitation, with printed materials and clear rules. Table 1 reports participants' evaluations of each game on a 1 (Very Poor) to 7 (Excellent) scale.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Games were new to participants, used printed materials, and were accompanied by clear rules
#### Constraints
- The games were implemented as independent pedagogical tools without any intended facilitation from teachers or instructors

### Target Learners
- adults of diverse cultural, professional, and age backgrounds, aged 18-59

### Learning Goals
- ethical sensitivity, ethical reasoning, ethical motivation, and ethical action in business contexts
