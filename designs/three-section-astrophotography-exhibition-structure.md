# Three-section astrophotography exhibition structure: night landscape, deep sky, instrumentation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The 54 selected photographs were arranged on the exhibition walls divided into three sections: night landscape (wide-field photographs of comets, meteors, zodiacal light, and atmospheric phenomena), deep sky (telescopic images of nebulae, galaxies, clusters, planets and the Moon, including photographic technique difficulties), and instrumentation (pictures of telescopes, detectors, mirrors and researchers at work). Each picture carried an A6 plate with title, author, shot details, position, and a brief scientific description, plus a QR-code to the audio-guide.
