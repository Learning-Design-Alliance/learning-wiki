# Adult narrative comprehension gesture observation-and-production experiment

## Description
An experimental study with adult learners that manipulated gesture observation (typical versus atypical gestures) and gesture production to test their roles in narrative comprehension. The article describes it as a study that "examined the role of gesture observation and production in adult narrative comprehension, through investigating whether gesture production moderates the effect of gesture observation." It provides evidence that iconic gestures can benefit adult narrative comprehension and highlights conditions of benefit.
