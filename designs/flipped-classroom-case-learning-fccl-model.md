# Flipped Classroom Case Learning (FCCL) as an integrated pedagogical model

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
FCCL integrates the flipped classroom (FC) model — flexible, self-managed pre-class videos and in-class group activities — with case-based learning (CBL), a guided inquiry method linking theoretical knowledge to real-world clinical scenarios. The article describes this integration as a combined pedagogical model likely to yield synergistic benefits, producing a teaching effect where "1 + 1 > 2." Students engage in self-directed theoretical learning before class and case discussion and analysis during class, fostering long-term retention of knowledge and clinical analysis abilities, plus communication and collaboration skills.
