# Open Talks about Artificial Intelligence: annual public lecture series

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Open Talks are an annual cycle of lectures open to students, researchers, industry professionals, educators, and AI enthusiasts, with all talks publicly available through LIA's YouTube channel. Lectures aligned with course topics are incorporated into undergraduate and graduate courses as complementary learning resources, while students present their own research outcomes, gaining scientific communication experience. Students organize the entire event, developing leadership, communication, and project management skills.
