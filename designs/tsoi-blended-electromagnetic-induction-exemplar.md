# Four-phase blended learning exemplar for electromagnetic induction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A lesson-level pattern in which each TSOI phase is realized by a different modality: Phase 1 a face-to-face demonstration of a hand-held AC generator for concept exposure; Phase 2 a face-to-face data-logger investigation for concept construction; Phase 3 online simulation learning for concept internalization; and Phase 4 a data-logger project where learners "devise a method to deduce the value of free fall acceleration based on the information that they figure out" from a falling-magnet induction graph.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sequencing of real demonstration, data-logger investigation, online simulation, and transfer project
#### Constraints
- The exemplar addresses the abstract topic of electromagnetic induction in physics; it was designed by a Physics Task Force of a master teacher, an educational technology officer, an NIE professor and a scientist

### Target Learners
- physics students

### Learning Goals
- concept exposure, construction, internalization and application of Faraday and Lenz's Laws
