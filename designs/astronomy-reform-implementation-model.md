# A three-part reform-implementation model: workshops, peer-instructor teams, and researcher collaboration

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article presents a model of reform implementation for introductory astronomy built on three key features. The authors state: "First, our astronomy instructors learned to use research-based curricular materials by attending workshops before and after first implementation." Second, instructors collaborated with student Peer Instructors to document and reflect on implementation; third, the team worked with science education researchers to collect information on common student conceptual difficulties and develop strategies from the research literature. The model targets novice university instructors adopting research-based instructional strategies.
