# Video Instructional Package (VIP) on geometry with four strategy variants

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The VIP is a self-instructional, user-friendly, interactive video package developed by the researchers with video production experts using the ADDIE instructional design model. It "lasted for 6 hours for an average student for six weeks" and covered nine lessons from the Nigerian senior secondary geometry curriculum (angle at a point, angles and parallel lines, angle properties of a triangle, congruence and similarity, angles of a polygon, parallelograms, circles, loci, and construction). Four variants were produced: COOVIP, COMVIP, IVIP, and CVIP, deployed on desktop computers or projected via LCD. The test instrument was the 50-item multiple-choice Geometry Achievement Test drawn from past WAEC questions, with reliability 0.87 (KR-20).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schools must have computers available for students' use and computer-literate students, which is why schools were purposively sampled
#### Constraints
- The package was validated by three mathematics lecturers and educational technology experts, and usability-tested with twenty SSSII students from Bosso Secondary School, Minna

### Target Learners
- second year senior secondary mathematics students in Nigeria

### Learning Goals
- geometry concepts in the Nigerian senior secondary mathematics curriculum
