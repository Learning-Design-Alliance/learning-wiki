# Mechanism account: PBL fosters deep learning through active and self-directed learning, possibly mediated by intrinsic motivation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review proposes that "The mechanisms through which PBL is assumed to enhance deep learning are active and self-directed learning." Students actively analyze, compare, contrast, and explain information and develop and explain hypotheses, while self-directed learning gives them ownership through freedom to select their own resources. The review suggests this effect is possibly mediated through intrinsic motivation, citing a PBL study in which students in a self-directed condition scored higher on autonomous motivation.
