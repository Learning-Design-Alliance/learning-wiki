# The Merrimack College Mobile Lightboard: a shared mobile office-based lightboard with a 3.5-foot by 5-foot low-iron glass pane

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A mobile lightboard built in 2017 and still in use in 2025, closely based on the 2014 Duke University Lightboard but scaled down. It features "a T-slotted aluminum frame and a 3.5-foot by 5-foot pane of low-iron glass" and is "small enough to roll into offices and stockrooms, but large enough to make videos that present reasonably-detailed introductory physics examples." The article gives build details, a frame parts list (Appendix F), and cost estimates.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Glass mounting holes must be drilled by the supplier prior to tempering, since glass cannot be drilled afterwards
- Very careful alignment and spacing of the support slots and near dead-on accuracy of hole centers are essential for glass installation
#### Constraints
- The board's overall height was adjusted to pass through doorways, the building exit, and a small elevator
- The authors present the build as an example that worked, not something that must be carefully copied

### Target Learners
- higher education students in introductory physics courses

### Learning Goals
- instructional video production for physics examples and explanations
