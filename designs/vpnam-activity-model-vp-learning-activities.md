# Virtual patient Nursing Activity Model (vpNAM): translating the design model into a VP-centric learning activity

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The vpNAM is an activity model that combines the vpNDM with a specific educational context and intended learning outcomes, reinforcing that "VPs are not merely an object/tool but rather a learning activity." As the article states, "A virtual patient Nursing Activity Model (vpNAM) was also developed as a guide when creating VP-centric learning activities." When applied, the student is introduced to the patient and activity aims, collects relevant data to frame the patient's story and needs, and afterwards receives formative feedback formulated in advance by the case author, which the student uses to reflect and identify further learning needs. It was illustrated in the third semester of the undergraduate nursing program at Karolinska Institutet.
