# Course framework built on problem-based learning, diversified learning opportunities, and interest-based specializations

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes the pedagogical framework of the proposed program as resting on three fundamental ideas: diversified learning opportunities supporting deep conceptual understanding, teaching most modules using problem-based learning, and offering specializations aligned with student interests because quantum computing is a diverse field with a wide range of applications. The framework positions the curriculum at the cutting edge of current quantum technology research trends.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Modules incorporating modern research issues such as quantum error correction and fault tolerance
- Practical laboratory sessions and a sizable second-year research project
#### Constraints
- Designed for the rapidly dynamic nature of the quantum field, requiring continual updating of module content

### Target Learners
- Master's students in quantum science and technology

### Learning Goals
- Capacity for successful application of technical and professional skills
- Conversance with the most recent advances in quantum science and technology
