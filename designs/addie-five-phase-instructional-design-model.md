# ADDIE model as a five-phase instructional design framework

## Description
The article uses the ADDIE model as the organizing framework for designing its computer program, describing it as "one of the most common models used in the instructional design field a guide to producing an effective design". Its five phases are Analysis (learners, instructional analysis, goals, objectives), Design (assessment, course form, instructional strategy), Development (integrating technology and producing materials), Implementation (training instructors, preparing learners, organizing the environment), and Evaluation (formative during the study, summative at the end). The article applies each phase concretely to build the listening and reading program.
