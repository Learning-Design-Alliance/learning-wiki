# NIHSS certification quality improvement intervention with dual blinded EM–neurology scoring

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A six-month quality improvement intervention implemented April 2022 at Zuckerberg San Francisco General Hospital in which NIHSS certification was required for all EM residents (PGY 2–4) and attending physicians via a "6-part 8 hour online module through the American Heart Association". During the pilot, EM and neurology residents separately recorded NIHSS scores for each patient using a REDCap survey form, with results blinded until both scores were submitted, then displayed side by side for discussion. The NIHSS served as the main clinical decision-making tool for stroke management.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires NIHSS certification of EM residents and attendings through the American Heart Association online module
- Requires paired EM and neurology resident scoring via the REDCap survey form with blinding until both scores are submitted
#### Constraints
- The examination was performed in tandem and was not completely blinded
- Surveys were voluntarily submitted and anonymized; resident level of training was not recorded

### Target Learners
- emergency medicine residents (PGY 2–4) and attending physicians

### Learning Goals
- competent NIHSS-based assessment and management of acute ischemic stroke
