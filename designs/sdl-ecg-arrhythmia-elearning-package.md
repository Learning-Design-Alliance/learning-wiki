# Self-directed e-learning (SDL) package for ECG arrhythmia interpretation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An online self-directed learning package of "six lessons covering foundational ECG interpretation knowledge," covering cardiac electrical activity, normal rhythms, waveform components, lead placement, common rhythm abnormalities, and their mechanisms and management, each lesson ending with a brief exercise. Expert panel review confirmed construct validity of the package and tests. Post-test used 25 image-based ECG strip questions at basic to advanced difficulty, completed within 45 minutes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Online platform access; completion of the six lessons with their concluding exercises before the post-test
#### Constraints
- Evaluated in one cardiology department with 50 nurses; short-term knowledge only, with long-term retention not assessed

### Target Learners
- staff nurses in coronary care, high dependency, and cardiac interventional units with at least one year of cardiac nursing experience

### Learning Goals
- accurate identification and interpretation of cardiac arrhythmias from ECG strips
