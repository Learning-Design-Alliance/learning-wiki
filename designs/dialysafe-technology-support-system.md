# Dialysafe technology support system: preloaded tablets, training calls, and a toll-free helpline with engagement support

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The intervention's technology support comprised "shipping preloaded tablets to mentees, technology training calls, and on-demand support via a toll-free helpline", plus proactive engagement support calls and login monitoring. A full-time technology support specialist provided most support; the helpline was available Monday to Friday, 9-5 PM, or by appointment, from October 2022 to November 2024. Training had a set curriculum with individualized supplemental assistance, and tablets were returned via prepaid packaging for reuse.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A dedicated full-time technology support specialist
- IRB-approved scripts and interpersonal-skills training for staff
#### Constraints
- Helpline staffed weekdays 9-5 PM; rare unresolvable issues required family or facility staff assistance

### Target Learners
- hemodialysis patients (mentees)
- peer mentors

### Learning Goals
- completing technology training
- connecting to and completing video-based peer mentoring sessions
