# Five-stage spoken learning loop with supplementary visual affordances

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Kutti AI's interaction design is "a spoken conversation with five stages": welcome (a single large haptic-enabled control), spoken lesson delivery paced with natural pauses, voice response captured by a large microphone control or hands-free mode, adaptive response (encouragement, hint, or simplified question), and a spoken summary with an engagement score (0–100). Visual affordances—screen-reader compatibility, large touch targets, haptic confirmation, high-contrast scheme—are strictly supplementary for low-vision users and sighted caregivers.
