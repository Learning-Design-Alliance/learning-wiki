# Structured group guide arranged in gradual levels of complexity following the Fourier-transform sequence

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course uses a structured group guide, implemented as a follow-up to the Fourier-transform didactic sequence, that breaks down applying the Fourier transform across four contexts: discrete or continuous signals, with periodic or aperiodic properties. The guide is arranged in 'gradual levels of complexity', allowing students to progressively advance and helping them build a simulation environment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The guide is solidly based on the subject's central reference book, 'Applied Digital Signal Processing'.
#### Constraints
- It is positioned as a follow-up within a specific signal-processing course sequence, after the Fourier-transform activity.

### Target Learners
- university signal-processing students

### Learning Goals
- progressive mastery of the Fourier transform across signal types
