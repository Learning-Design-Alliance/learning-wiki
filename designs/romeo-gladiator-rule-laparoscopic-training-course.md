# Romeo Gladiator Rule two-day laparoscopic suturing training course with pelvic trainer

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A two-day laparoscopic suture training course built around the Romeo Gladiator Rule knot-tying technique, in which "the needle holder performs a clockwise turn (from 6 to 12 o'clock) or a counterclockwise turn (from 12 to 6 o'clock) with the jaws of the tip open" to keep the thread in place. The course combines 5 hours 30 minutes of theory (ergonomics, energy, suture) with 8 hours 30 minutes of hands-on practice in a pelvic trainer simulator, three residents per supervised workstation, using landmarks called the home base and the thread horizon. The study used it to compare monomanual versus bimanual knot-tying training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Resident participants without prior laparoscopic suture training, working in groups of 3 per supervised workstation with a tutor
- A pelvic trainer with all-in-one laparoscopic video system, 10-mm 0-degree optics, and two 5-mm lateral trocars with needle holders
#### Constraints
- The authors state the data reflect specifically the studied population in a specific condition of knowledge and training, and that clinical practice with living tissue was not tested

### Target Learners
- second- and third-year gynecology/obstetrics and general surgery residents without prior laparoscopic suture training

### Learning Goals
- intracorporeal laparoscopic knot tying
- laparoscopic suturing skill and knot quality
