## Description
The Toulmin Model of Argumentation (Toulmin, 1958) explains the basic constituents of argumentation and their functional relations. Three major constituents are grounds, warrant and claim, supported by three additional elements: backing, rebuttal and qualifiers. The model is used in many fields of study, including science courses for the analysis of discussions, and in this study all argumentation activities were based on the Toulmin model.
