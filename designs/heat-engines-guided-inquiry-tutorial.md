# Heat Engines guided-inquiry tutorial for upper-division thermal physics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
A guided-inquiry tutorial used in upper-division thermal physics courses at School 1, in which small groups (2-5 students) work through activities about heat engines. The article describes that "The tutorial presents students with several extreme cases (one in which QL = 0, and one in which W = 0) before having them consider the limit on thermodynamic eﬃciency imposed by the ﬁrst and second laws of thermodynamics." Its primary goals include having students show explicitly that a QL = 0 engine would violate the 2nd Law, and encouraging the expert-like practice of reasoning about imagined impossible situations. Video data from tutorial sessions supplied much of the observed difficulty evidence.
