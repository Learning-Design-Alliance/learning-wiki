# Technology-enhanced digital workbook (Moodle-based) for inquiry-based physics instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A Moodle course implementing a technology-enhanced workbook that "replaced paper-based worksheets and notebooks" in an inquiry-based energy unit. It featured task formats from multiple-choice to open-ended answers, recorded student artifacts across 36 tasks, and gave each student a personal laptop or tablet. The recorded artifacts supplied the cognitive data stream (107 scores per student via evidence-centered design scoring), while self-report instruments after each of five unit parts supplied affective and metacognitive data.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each student needs access to a laptop or tablet; teachers participate in professional learning on project-based pedagogy and the workbook
#### Constraints
- Data collection is limited by classroom affordances such as missed lessons and teacher instructions to skip tasks, which produced missing task responses

### Target Learners
- middle school students in grades 7 and 8 in Gymnasium and Gemeinschaftsschule school types

### Learning Goals
- energy concept understanding
- scientific practices such as planning investigations and constructing explanations
