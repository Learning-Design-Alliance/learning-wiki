# Guided simulation of clinical cases as the CAI's core learning design

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The CAI's instructional pattern is a step-by-step guided simulation: the user registers a patient, then moves through hierarchical menu levels in the Medical History, Diagnoses and Quality of Life modules, filling in screen formats while algorithms display learning material based on validated clinical tools. The authors state the CAI 'guides the user in performing simulations of clinical cases', helping learners integrate nutritional data, patient information and professional knowledge to support clinical reasoning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Algorithms that display learning material as the user fills in screen fields, drawing on a database of validated questionnaires, clinical guidelines and disease risk calculation tools
#### Constraints
- Designed for nutritional assessment simulation rather than real patient care; participants noted time cost of filling all areas without a search engine

### Target Learners
- nutrition students
- health professionals
- clinical specialists

### Learning Goals
- nutritional status assessment
- clinical reasoning
- diagnosis of nutrition-related diseases
