# Contextual learning model grounded in Piaget, constructivism, and Bruner

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article presents contextual learning as an approach that "recognizes and shows the natural conditions of knowledge", linking classroom material to real contexts so experience becomes relevant and meaningful. It rests on three learning theories the article names: Piaget's assimilation and accommodation processes, constructivism (students actively construct their own knowledge rather than receiving transferred ideas), and Bruner's theory of reorganizing structures of ideas. In the study it is implemented as the experimental teaching model contrasted with expository instruction.
