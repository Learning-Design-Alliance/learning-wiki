# Guided computational lessons pairing visualization of abstract quantum concepts with model-chemistry exploration

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The QCP lessons follow a reusable instructional pattern: students first visualize an abstract concept (e.g., molecular orbitals or particle-in-a-box wavefunctions) with simple commands, then explore 'more complex connections between a model chemistry (electronic structure method and underlying atomic orbital basis set) and the resulting energies and molecular properties.' Each lesson provides learning objectives, pre-written code, and a narrative that guides the student, and worksheets can be modified by students or instructors to fit class needs.
