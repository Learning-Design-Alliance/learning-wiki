# Observer training should be completed before using OPTIS to achieve good to very good reliability

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends that a three-step, eight-hour observer training programme be realised before using OPTIS. The training comprises individual study of the protocol and user manual, observation of at least one 45-minute video-recorded lesson, and comparison and discussion of ratings with other observers. The article reports that by applying this training, good to very good reliability values could be achieved.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- At least one 45-minute video-recorded lesson must be observed during training, with ratings compared and discussed with other observers
#### Constraints
- The article notes that training length itself does not affect rating quality, positioning eight hours midway between common established trainings of 5–24 hours

### Target Learners
- Observer trainees (university students, researchers)

### Learning Goals
- Developing consistent understanding of observation categories for reliable classroom observation
