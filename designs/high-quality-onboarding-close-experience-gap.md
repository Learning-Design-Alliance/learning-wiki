# Focus on high-quality onboarding programmes to close the experience-related quality gap in stroke thrombolysis

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because less experienced registrars had longer door-to-needle times, the article recommends investing in onboarding to compensate for experience gained only through treating real patients. It states: "We suggest a focus on high-quality onboarding programmes to close the experience-related quality gap." The authors note that with high physician turnover, a larger proportion of patients are treated by less experienced registrars, negatively affecting DNT, and that simulation plus systemic changes produced larger gains than experience alone.
