# doktormitSDM training module: manual, video tutorial, and 15-minute measurement-based face-to-face feedback

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
doktormitSDM is a short in-situ training module for health professionals to involve patients in medical decision making, built on the MAPPIN'SDM measurement inventory. "It has three parts:" a 40-page manual illustrating 15 SDM skills, a 20-minute video tutorial of real decision consultations edited according to the MAPPIN'SDM taxonomy, and a 15-minute individual feedback session on the trainee's own recorded consultation. At its core it is "a practitioner‐trainer discourse on patient involvement, using an actual consultation recorded by the practitioner and its MAPPIN'SDM analysis by the trainer."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires the trainee to provide a recorded own consultation for the MAPPIN'SDM analysis and about 2 hours of study time plus 15 minutes for the feedback session
#### Constraints
- Feedback comments never refer to general communication performance or to work samples other than the one given in that session

### Target Learners
- specialist doctors in outpatient clinics and medical practices
- other health professionals such as nurses or physical therapists

### Learning Goals
- involving patients in medical decision making
- evidence-based patient information and risk communication
