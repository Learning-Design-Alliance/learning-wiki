# Ten-hour metalinguistic coursework module for spelling instruction embedded in a literacy education course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 10-hour instructional module (5 x 2-hour lectures) delivered within a 40-hour literacy education course for first-year preservice teachers, covering phonological awareness (~4 hours), orthographic awareness (~2 hours), morphological awareness (~2 hours), and spelling analysis and selection of appropriate teaching focus (~2 hours). The course "utilised a blended model to support students' learning", with readings, video clips and interactive activities online, and lecture recordings accessible multiple times. The final component had students work in small groups to score and analyse 17 spelling samples from children aged 6-7 years, group children by instructional focus, and report findings with lecturer feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Lecturer facilitation during group analysis activities, including whole-cohort feedback sessions and circulation among groups
- Online course website hosting recordings and spelling samples for review
#### Constraints
- The spelling instruction selection measure is not a direct measure of teacher competency in spelling assessment or instruction

### Target Learners
- first-year preservice teachers in a three-year undergraduate teaching degree

### Learning Goals
- metalinguistic knowledge for spelling instruction
- application of phonemic, morphological and orthographic knowledge to spelling error analysis
