# 360-degree video VR simulation program for schizophrenia symptoms (five scenarios, ten clips, Oculus Go HMDs)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A mental health nursing VR simulation delivering "360-degree videos of five different scenarios reflecting clinical symptoms of schizophrenia patients and related treatment tasks" via Oculus Go head-mounted displays, filmed with Gear 360 cameras and developed in Unity3D. "Five different scenarios were developed that incorporated five different symptoms of schizophrenia—risk of violation, auditory hallucination, visual hallucination, delusion, and risk of suicide," enacted by professional actors in two constructed rooms (a patient room and a day room). Ten clips of 60-90 seconds each are followed by on-screen decision-making questions and scenario-based quizzes with therapeutic-communication tasks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Scenario scripts written by a researcher and reviewed by two professors and one mental health nursing professional, with videos validated for realism by psychiatric professionals and a nurse practitioner.
- Participants receive phased instructions on HMD and controller use (about 15-20 minutes) before the simulation.
#### Constraints
- Participants were instructed not to use the device for more than 20 minutes at a time.
- Rooms were open two to three hours per day under research-assistant supervision.

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate nursing students in their last semester who have completed most clinical placements

### Learning Goals
- Identify clinical symptoms of schizophrenia
- Therapeutic communication with psychiatric patients
- Clinical decision-making in acute psychiatric settings
