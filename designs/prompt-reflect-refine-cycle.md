# Prompt–Reflect–Refine cycle as a model for AI-mediated dialogic learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Prompt–Reflect–Refine cycle is the study's structured pedagogical routine for AI-mediated learning: students formulate a prompt, evaluate the AI response against pedagogical frameworks (TPACK and AITSL Standards), then modify their artefact or prompt and document reasoning. The article states "the prompt initiates cognitive activation, reflection triggers evaluation, and refinement consolidates learning". It operationalises dialogic metacognition, constrains interaction away from one-shot querying, and creates analysable traces of metacognitive regulation across successive prompts and artefacts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must explicitly evaluate AI outputs against nominated pedagogical frameworks rather than accept them
- Weekly tasks structured around the three-cycle steps with documented reasoning
#### Constraints
- Replication should focus on reproducing the pedagogical workflow rather than an immutable underlying language model, since OpenAI periodically updates foundation models

### Target Learners
- pre-service secondary teachers in Initial Teacher Education

### Learning Goals
- conceptual understanding of Digital Technologies curriculum
- pedagogical reasoning and lesson design
- metacognitive regulation
- critical AI literacy
