# Sequenced weekly assessment moving from kinematics and curvilinear coordinates toward relativistic dynamics and particle decays

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Across the fifteen control works the topic ordering progresses with the semester: the earliest works pose pursuit-trajectory, coordinate-system and curvature problems; middle works introduce Lorentz transformations, rapidity, and relativistic addition of velocities; later works (11 through 15) add small-oscillation and anharmonic-perturbation problems alongside particle-decay kinematics such as K0 → π+π− and τ lepton radiative decay. This sequencing lets weekly checkpoints track a course that builds mechanics technique before relativistic dynamics.
