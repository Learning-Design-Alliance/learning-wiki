# Three-test retention design for vocabulary teaching (pre-test, immediate post-test, two-week delayed post-test)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The study's measurement instrument is a translation test of ten sentences, each containing one target equivocal word, administered three times. Test I before instruction showed participants did not know the words; Test II immediately after instruction checked understanding; Test III two weeks later measured retention and correct use. German learners took an inverse translation from German into Spanish.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The same ten-sentence translation test administered at all three time points to both control and experimental groups
#### Constraints
- Retention was assessed only at a two-week delay in this pilot study

### Target Learners
- Adult Spanish foreign-language learners of English and German (B1)

### Learning Goals
- Retention and correct use of equivocal vocabulary
