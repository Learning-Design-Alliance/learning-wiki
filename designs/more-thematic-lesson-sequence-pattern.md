# MORE thematic lesson sequence interleaving vocabulary, read-alouds, wide reading, collaborative research, and structured word inquiry

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The MORE intervention organizes content literacy instruction as recurring thematic lesson sequences built on a pyramid structure in which a general schema unifies topics in a domain. Teachers "interleaved practices involving direct teaching of domain-specific academic vocabulary networks, interactive read-alouds to provide recurrent exposures to schema-related vocabulary, and wide reading of thematically related informational texts with autonomy and competence supports." The Grade 2 science unit contained 45 lessons with nine lesson activity types repeated five times across a 10-week unit, ending each sequence with a reflection activity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Thematic units sequenced across grades around a shared general schema
- Teacher professional development and ongoing lesson-level support
- Thematically related informational text sets for wide reading
#### Constraints
- The Grade 2 social studies unit could not be implemented after COVID-19 school closures in spring 2020

### Target Learners
- First- and second-grade students in Tier I literacy instruction

### Learning Goals
- Building domain and topic knowledge schemas
- Academic vocabulary development
- Reading comprehension of informational texts
