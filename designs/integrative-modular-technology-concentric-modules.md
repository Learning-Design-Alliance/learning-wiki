# Integrative-modular technology with concentric modules

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The model reflects the usage of integrative-modular technology, which structures the educational material according to the principle of concentric modules. The least module fulfils the function of a fractal: a chain of actions entering the structure of subject pedagogical activity — analysis, purpose, planning, realization, control, estimation — treated as a unit of the educational process. The technology provides "concentrated organization of the educational material" and study of pedagogical knowledge via theoretical schemes of systematic analysis.

## Design Implications
