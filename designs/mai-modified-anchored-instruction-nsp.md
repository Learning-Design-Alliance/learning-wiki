# Modified Anchored Instruction (MAI): transferring AI design principles from videodiscs to newspaper story problems

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
MAI is the article's framework combining the authenticity and story character of the original Anchored Instruction approach with the practicability of newspapers as anchor media. It retains AI design principles (affective authentic anchor media, narrative format, embedded data, curriculum links, related problems, complex problems, active and autonomous problem solving) while modifying two features: anchor media are not limited to video, and problem complexity is adjustable. Tab. 1 contrasts AI and MAI/NSP features, e.g. adjustability is "Yes, cf. above and text" for MAI.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Anchor media must be authentic, affectively and cognitively engaging stories with curriculum-related content, tested with pupils for perceived authenticity
#### Constraints
- NSP differs from original AI in scale: it is an intermediate, not macro, approach; video-based AI anchors are hard to adjust in content, complexity and extent due to practical and technical difficulties

### Target Learners
- Secondary level I physics pupils (7th/8th and 9th/10th grade) of diverse academic backgrounds

### Learning Goals
- Physics content learning (kinematics, electrical energy) with transfer to real-life contexts, and motivation/interest in physics
