# Einstein-First relativity learning progression: eight concepts taught step by step on the space-time simulator

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article organises general-relativity teaching as a step-by-step learning progression built on the lycra-membrane space-time simulator, moving from matter curving space through photon trajectories, lensing, Newton's law, Kepler's law, geodetic precession and gravitational waves. The authors state: "We introduce concepts step by step using qualitative and quantitative experiments, depending on the age group." The progression is the backbone of the Einstein-First curriculum for relativity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A space-time simulator (lycra membrane on an elevated frame) plus balls, toy cars and video-recording tools
#### Constraints
- Content depth is adjusted to age group; qualitative or quantitative treatment is chosen accordingly

### Target Learners
- primary and high school students (trials began with Year 6)

### Learning Goals
- conceptual understanding of general relativity: curved space-time, motion in curved space, lensing, Kepler's laws, precession, gravitational waves
