# VirtuIng course: multiplayer online game environment for meta-competence training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
VirtuIng was an elective online course for bachelor Civil Engineering students (n=5) that promoted meta competences using a multiplayer online game. Students first learned "how to operate the multiplayer online game (CCP 2012)" and then worked in groups to find strategic game objectives such as specialization within the game. Along the way, collaborative challenges demanded that students exercise their meta competences, making the game the course's central learning environment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must learn to operate the game before pursuing objectives
- Group-based play with strategic objectives to force collaboration
#### Constraints
- Implemented with only five participants in a pilot study

### Target Learners
- bachelor students in Civil Engineering

### Learning Goals
