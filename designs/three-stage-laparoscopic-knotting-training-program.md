# Three-stage laparoscopic knotting training program: introduction with video demonstration, guided basic skill training, then independent consolidation practice

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The teaching plan "consisted of three stages, with the first stage lasting one week, and the rest two stages lasting two weeks", with two 40-minute classes per week. Stage one introduces the surgery, suturing technology and the assigned knotting method with video demonstrations and ends with a pre-training assessment and NASA-TLX; stage two is instructor-guided basic skill training; stage three is "consolidation skill training mainly focusing on independent practice" ending in post-training assessment. Both groups shared one instructor and room at different times.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An experienced instructor, identical practice time across participants, and a simulator with a standardized suturing module
#### Constraints
- Studied with novice residents without prior laparoscopic surgery experience, to eliminate differences in past training volumes

### Target Learners
- junior residents learning basic laparoscopic skills

### Learning Goals
- laparoscopic suturing and knotting proficiency
