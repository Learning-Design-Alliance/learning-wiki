# Six-component student outcome assessment battery (three SATs, science test, reasoning test, PASS)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study assessed six outcomes: Mathematics, SPAG and Reading SATs supplied by teachers, a specially prepared Year 6 science test on inheritance and evolution and fair testing, a specially designed general reasoning test, and the PASS attitude survey. For the science test, "Pilot data indicated that the items had excellent scale properties (Cronbach's alpha across all items=.95; mean square out-fit scores all below 2.00 on Rasch analysis)"; the reasoning test alpha was .98 and PASS alpha .93.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher-supplied anonymized SAT scores
- Trained markers with a scoring manual for science short-text items
- Start-of-year NFER baselines matched to each outcome
#### Constraints
- Mathematics SAT analyses were based on 61 classes because classes where a colleague taught mathematics were discounted
- The science test was omitted in classrooms that had not covered inheritance and evolution

### Target Learners
- Year 6 students in English primary schools (N up to 1784)

### Learning Goals
- Curriculum mastery in mathematics, literacy and science
- General reasoning
- Attitudes to school, self as learner and relationship with teacher
