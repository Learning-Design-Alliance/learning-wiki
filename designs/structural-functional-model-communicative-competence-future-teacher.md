# Structural-functional model of forming communicative competence of the future teacher

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article works out a functional-structural model of forming communicative competence of a future teacher under transforming pedagogical education. It includes purposeful, methodological, content, organizational-processual and regulative components, built on competent, businesslike and module approaches. The model reflects "four levels of structural contents of educating the future teacher, possessing communicative competence: informational, methodological, methodical and practical". Its basic condition is development of the student's subjective position.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

#### Constraints
