# Design characteristics of the effective eCME multimedia program

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article enumerates the prominent aspects participants agreed influenced their learning and motivation: interactivity allowing learners to "manipulate the representation, so that learners were in complete control of the speed, orientation, and time"; examples, non-examples, frequently asked questions, practice and feedback, and end-of-course quizzes; multimedia presentations (animation, graphics, photographs, narrative, videos); content relevant to learners' lives and work; simulated real-world activities; appropriately difficult content; hands-on activities; performance feedback; pages easy to navigate with linear and nonlinear layouts; and overall learning-experience satisfaction.

## Design Implications

#### Requirements

- The pattern was evaluated only in a single CME topic (Acute Respiratory Failure) at one Iranian university
