# HeiTiMed second-year peer-guided examination preparation course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A voluntary, peer-guided examination preparation course for second-year (third and fourth semester) international medical students at Heidelberg, running 14 weekly Tuesday-afternoon sessions parallel to the third-semester lectures in physiology, biochemistry and histology. Each session opened with a five-question multiple-choice pre-test; the semester ended with a post-test re-using all 60 questions. Sessions combined student presentations, tutor explanations, interactive exercises, and discussion of uncertainties, aiming to deliver essential examination knowledge and presentation skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Scheduling in faculty-allocated self-organised learning time so voluntary attendance is not blocked by compulsory sessions.
#### Constraints
- Participation was voluntary; only twelve of twenty students took the post-test, which the authors attribute to students focusing on final-examination preparation.

### Target Learners
- international full-time medical students in their second year

### Learning Goals
- knowledge for third-semester examinations
- presentation skills in German
- confidence in examination-related abilities
