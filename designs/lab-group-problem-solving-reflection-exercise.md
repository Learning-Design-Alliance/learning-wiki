# Weekly lab group context-rich problem solving exercise with metacognitive reflection rubric

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A weekly first-hour laboratory exercise adapted from a metacognition study, in which lab groups of two or three students work collaboratively on a context-rich problem tailored to the week's lab experiment, with instructor and Learning Assistant support. Afterwards the instructor sketches the solution and students write reflections in a rubric, focusing on aspects of the solution upon which they struggled. The exercise was conducted in two semesters of an introductory algebra-based physics course.
