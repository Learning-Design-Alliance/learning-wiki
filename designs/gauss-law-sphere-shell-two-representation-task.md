# Gauss’s law sphere-and-shell two-representation task

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A two-representation Gauss’s law task: students write an expression for the electric field in four regions and plot E(r) for a configuration where ‘the net charge on the solid conductor is +Q and the net charge on the concentric spherical shell is –Q’. The article used it in think-aloud interviews to probe why students’ mathematical and graphical representations lack consistency.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students had completed the study of electrostatics and been tested on it via a midterm exam before using the task in interviews.
#### Constraints
- Prior research suggests this type of problem results in many students constructing representations that are not consistent with one another.

### Target Learners
- introductory calculus-based physics students

### Learning Goals
- constructing consistent mathematical and graphical representations of the electric field
- applying Gauss’s law with spherical symmetry

- [Plots Inconsistent With Mathematical Expressions](../claims/plots-inconsistent-with-mathematical-expressions.md) [+M]
