# Partially blind participants to the intervention and randomised design to minimise control-arm attrition in online trials

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The protocol's blinding procedure recommends that online trial participants be partially blinded to both the intervention and the existence of a randomised controlled design. Rather than telling participants that half will receive the intervention and half will not, students are informed they will receive an online training resource in one of two different formats, a wording the authors state is chosen to minimise attrition in the control group. This applies to a fully online, geographically dispersed trial.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Wording of participant-facing materials must present both arms as receiving a training resource in different formats
#### Constraints
- Participants remain only partially blinded; researchers can see arm counts for data monitoring, with allocation concealed from monitoring researchers and the statistician until analysis is complete

### Target Learners
- UK medical students recruited across up to 33 UK medical schools

### Learning Goals
- Participation and retention in online educational research on prognostic skill training
