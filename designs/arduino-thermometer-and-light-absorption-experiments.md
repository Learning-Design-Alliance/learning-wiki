# Two Arduino-built classroom experiments: the termômetro (thermology) and absorção luminosa (optics)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The research produced two classroom experiments. The termômetro was built "com o objetivo de captar a temperatura, pressão atmosférica e altitude", supporting initial concepts of "temperatura, energia térmica, calor e as transformações gasosas". The absorção luminosa experiment aims to "discutir os conceitos sobre absorção da luz em diferentes cores", using two BPM 180 sensors attached to the outside of two metal hemispheres with different internal colors under an incandescent lamp. Both use the Arduino UNO Rev. 3 with LCD, potenciômetros, jumpers and a protoboard, and were applied with written activity scripts (Apêndices A and B).
