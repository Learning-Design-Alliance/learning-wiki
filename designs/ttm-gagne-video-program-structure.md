# Program structure combining Transtheoretical Model stages with Gagné's instructional events for a 30-minute video

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The program's content structure combined the Transtheoretical Model of health behavior change with Gagné's instruction. Because awareness of these children is low in Japan, the design focused on pre-contemplation and contemplation stages, using "consciousness-raising, emotional arousal, and self-re-evaluation". Gagné's sequence ran from gaining attention, to stating goals, to new knowledge, to a narrative feedback story, to asking participants what they learned and how they would apply it.
