# Daily 30-minute moderate-intensity running practice intervention

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The core instructional element evaluated in the study: students participated in "running practice on the university's playground for thirty minutes each day over a period of four months" on a standard 400-meter track, at a moderate intensity aligned with WHO recommendations. Attendance was verified through "a digital form with an embedded timestamp on WeChat, accessible via a QR code placed at the designated track," with rotating research assistants supervising and recording attendance. The element was assessed at three time points using the adapted AMS, MWBS and PAQ-A scales.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A safe, adequately sized running track; a structured daily timeframe; attendance tracking via digital check-in with supervisory verification
#### Constraints
- The article states the study "lacked a control group, which limits interpretation and does not account for the effects of other physical activities"; sampling was convenient and restricted to one university, limiting generalizability

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in higher education without medical problems hindering physical activity

### Learning Goals
- mental health and wellbeing
- academic motivation (intrinsic and extrinsic)
- physical activity engagement
