# Science literacy and technology course (MKU 6217): compulsory science-literacy course for non-science undergraduates at UNY

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
MKU 6217 is a compulsory first-year course at Universitas Negeri Yogyakarta, offered since 2020 and "predominantly offered for non-science students to furnish their competencies in being the future Indonesian educators". Topics span nature of science, scientific method, civilization development, universe as a system, science-technology implementation, and big ideas in science. It was delivered via the Moodle-based BESMART LMS with distance learning during the pandemic transition, and evaluated here with the SLA-D, a midterm exam, and SLA-MB.
