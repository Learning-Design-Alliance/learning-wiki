# Carpentry-style mapping of class design principles and design patterns to collaborative exercises

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The APC distills software design teaching into "teaching recipes to solve re-occurring problems in OOP which go by the names of class design principles and design patterns", practiced "within well deﬁned exercises together with peers". Rather than teaching academic depths of requirement analysis and software modeling, the school extracts carpentry-style aspects of design and maps them to exercises and examples participants can relate to, so they take concrete code snippets back to their home institute.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Exercises and examples that participants can relate to from their own HEP work
#### Constraints
- The core motivation was not to teach the academic depths of multi-facetted requirement analysis, user story identiﬁcation and software modeling

### Target Learners
- beginners in object-oriented programming among young physicists

### Learning Goals
- applying proven OO design solutions collaboratively
