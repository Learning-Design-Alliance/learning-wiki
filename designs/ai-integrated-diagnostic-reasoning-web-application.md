# Custom-built web application integrating an AI chatbot into a three-part diagnostic reasoning case

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A custom-built web application integrated a GPT-4o-powered AI chatbot into a three-part acute kidney injury case requiring iterative Bayesian reasoning. Within each part of the case, students first ranked their differential diagnoses without AI access, and the integrated AI chatbot then became available for students to refine their reasoning and adjust their rankings on a voluntary basis. Students could submit up to 10 prompts across the whole case.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must select and include relevant clinical information in their prompts, as the chatbot did not have automatic access to the clinical case data
#### Constraints
- The chatbot was powered by GPT-4o, the most capable model available at implementation in August 2025

### Target Learners
- Second-year pre-clerkship medical students

### Learning Goals
- Diagnostic reasoning
- Differential diagnosis ranking
