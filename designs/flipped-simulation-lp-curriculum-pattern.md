# Flipped-classroom simulation curriculum combining online pre-learning modules with hands-on deliberate practice

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A six-month curriculum in which "trainees completed each self-paced online pre-learning module before attending the corresponding in-person workshop", with a short pre-test confirming engagement and instructor-led time reserved for hands-on deliberate practice. Landmark training used a 15-minute module plus a 2-hour workshop; ultrasound training used a 20-minute module plus a 2.5-hour workshop with three stations, both at a 1:6 instructor-to-trainee ratio with immediate feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Self-paced online pre-learning modules before each in-person workshop
- Instructor-to-trainee ratio of 1:6 with immediate feedback during hands-on practice
#### Constraints
- The study did not compare flipped classroom versus traditional didactic approaches, so relative effectiveness of delivery methods cannot be concluded

### Target Learners
- Emergency medicine residents
- PGY trainees

### Learning Goals
- Landmark-guided and ultrasound-guided lumbar puncture procedural skills
