# Metaphorical thinking in mathematics learning via conceptual metaphors

## Description
The article presents metaphorical thinking as a way of organising abstract mathematical concepts through concrete structures: "Abstract concepts that are organized through metaphorical thinking are expressed in concrete things based on structures and ways of reasoning based sensory -motor system called conceptual metaphor." It names three metaphorical conceptual forms — grounding, linking, and re-definitional metaphors — and links the approach to questioning by having students connect statements to questions, find conjectures, create ideas, and apply thinking to posed questions.
