# School in Motion (ScIM) physically active learning and Don't worry – Be Happy interventions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
ScIM comprised two multicomponent school-based PA interventions for ninth-grade adolescents, each targeting approximately 120 min/week of additional PA on top of mandatory PE. The PAL intervention "consisted of three components" — 30 min of PA in academic subjects, 60 min of PE, and 30 min of student-chosen activity — grounded in a socio-ecological framework, with an online toolkit of activities. DWBH consisted of two components: a self-organized 'Be happy' activity class chosen by interest-based groups across classes, and a student-led 'Don't worry' PE lesson, anchored in an integrative relational developmental systems approach promoting friendship through PA.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- At least one teacher per intervention school attended a one-day delivery course; all intervention components were mandatory and adherence self-reported weekly on an online platform
#### Constraints
- Delivery averaged 78–83% of the 120 min/week target, ranging 67–95% across schools, mainly due to exams, holidays and school trips

### Target Learners
- 14-year-old lower secondary school students (ninth grade) in Norway

### Learning Goals
- Increased physical activity during school hours and improved academic performance in numeracy and reading
