# Two-phase mixed-methods study of board exam prep resources across 42 accredited COM programs

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A study combining a 10-item Qualtrics survey (phase one) and semi-structured Zoom interviews (phase two) with COM library directors. Thirty-five of 42 invited directors completed the survey ("an 83% response rate"), and 13 participated in interviews. The instrument underwent "expert review by eight osteopathic health sciences librarians" for face and content validity. The IRB classified the study as exempt.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional access to accredited COM library directors for recruitment
#### Constraints
- Self-reported director data; findings limited to osteopathic institutions and institutionally licensed resources, excluding student-driven informal tools

### Target Learners
- library directors of accredited colleges of osteopathic medicine

### Learning Goals
- characterizing resource availability, funding, governance, and evaluation practices for board exam preparation
