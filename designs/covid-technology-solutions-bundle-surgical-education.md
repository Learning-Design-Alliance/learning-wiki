# A bundled set of five technology-based solutions for maintaining surgical resident education during restrictions on in-person activities

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article organizes its response to pandemic restrictions on gatherings, elective operations, and rotations into a bundle of five solutions, listed in Table 2: flipped virtual classroom model, online practice questions, academic conferences via teleconference, telehealth clinics with resident involvement, and facilitated use of surgical videos. The authors state that "although there is no substitute for hands-on learning through operative experience and direct patient care, these may be ways to mitigate the loss of learning exposure during this time."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional commitment to adapting existing technology-based education strategies
- Cooperation between institutions, particularly regionalization of educational teleconferences and shared access to nearby institutions
#### Constraints
- These solutions mitigate but do not replace hands-on operative experience and direct patient care
- There were not yet nationwide or multi-institutional recommendations at the time of writing

### Target Learners
- surgical residents
- surgical educators and program leadership

### Learning Goals
- maintaining rigorous surgical education during restrictions on in-person gatherings and elective operations
