# Joint statistical learning complementary SRTT hyperscanning paradigm with OSF data release

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A modified serial reaction time task in which a fixed 12-location stimulus sequence is randomly divided and assigned to each co-actor for response. The experiment comprised eight blocks: Blocks 1 to 6 and 8 were "constructed from a ﬁxed sequence of locations, while the seventh block was constructed from an interference sequence". Dyads were assigned to a joint context (both respond to pre-assigned colours) or a baseline context (actor plus passive observer), with concurrent dual-EEG recording at 512 Hz. All behaviour and EEG data, and in-house scripts, are deposited on the Open Science Framework.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dual-EEG recording system with synchronized amplifiers
- Paired participants randomly assigned to joint or baseline context
#### Constraints
- Interference and main sequence assignment counterbalanced across dyads; only female participants recruited

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in dyads

### Learning Goals
- implicit statistical learning of fixed stimulus sequences in joint and individual contexts

### Claims

- [A joint context with an active co-actor produces a steeper reaction-time learning slope than a baseline context with a passive observer in a complementary statistical learning task](../claims/joint-context-steeper-rt-learning-slope-statistical-learning.md) [+M]
- [Theta-band brain-to-brain coupling increased more steeply during learning in the joint context and was modulated by the presence or absence of statistical regularity](../claims/theta-btbc-increases-joint-context-regularity-modulated.md) [+M]
- [Inserting an interference sequence disrupted and then recovered joint-context performance: RT rose from Block 6 to 7 and fell from Block 7 to 8 only in the joint context](../claims/joint-context-interference-recovery-rt.md) [+M]
