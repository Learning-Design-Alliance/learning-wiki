# Second-screen app structure with episode-synced conversation starters, hands-on activities, and a topical guide

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A reusable design pattern for family joint media engagement apps: a second-screen resource that syncs to episode content and organizes support into a Dive In section with per-episode conversation starters, science themes, characters, and clips; hands-on Activities connecting science ideas to everyday experiences; and an Ocean Guide with environments, topics, and clips. The app "includes detailed information about the science concepts in the show, allows parents to easily access ideas for conversation during or after they watch the show with their children, and provides hands-on activities."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content tied to a media program with identifiable science themes
- Conversation starters accessible via sync or episode search
#### Constraints
- Component usefulness varies with the child's interest or age

### Target Learners
- Families with young children watching educational media together

### Learning Goals
- Science talk and connecting show science ideas to everyday life
