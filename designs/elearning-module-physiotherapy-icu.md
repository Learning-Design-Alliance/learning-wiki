# E-learning module 'Physiotherapy in the ICU': a seven-chapter evidence-based online course with videos, interactive assignments and quizzes

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A compulsory e-learning module developed with clinical, didactic and research experts to prepare final-year physiotherapy students for ICU rotations. The article states "The final module contained 7 chapters" covering ICU introduction, assessment, interventions and collaboration, built on "interactive assignments, background literature and short quizzes, self-developed and online videos" and narrated slide-format text. Each module ended with a quiz providing direct feedback, and an online community forum supported lecturer-student exchange.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students complete the module, including a passing mark on a 25-question online exam, before starting their clinical ICU rotation
#### Constraints
- Content decisions aimed to provide a general introduction into the topic rather than site-specific training

### Target Learners
- Final-year undergraduate physiotherapy students in an international curriculum

### Learning Goals
- ICU safety criteria and GO/NO GO judgement
- physiotherapeutic screening and assessment in the ICU
- evidence-based ICU physiotherapy interventions
- multidisciplinary collaboration
