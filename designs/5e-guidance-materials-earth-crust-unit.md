# 5E-based guidance materials for "What is the Earth's Crust Composed of?" combining case studies, conceptual change texts and worksheets

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A set of guidance materials developed on the 5E model (Engage, Explore, Explain, Elaborate, Evaluate) for the topic "What is the Earth's Crust Composed of?" in the Turkish 5th-grade science curriculum. The Engage step used a case study entitled "Mines"; the Explore step used a worksheet with a make-a-fossil activity; the Explain step used a conceptual change text entitled "Fossils"; the Elaborate step used a case study entitled "Travertines of Pamukkale are in Danger"; and the Evaluate step used a concept map. The application process was completed within 8 hours of classroom activity, matching the control group's 8 hours with the existing textbook.

- Developed for the 5th-grade "Mystery of Earth's Crust" unit

### Target Learners

### Learning Goals
- Conceptual understanding of rocks, minerals, fossils and natural monuments
