# Two-semester Quantum Mechanics course sequence at SPT

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The core quantum course at SPT begins in the second (spring) semester of the third year and spans two semesters of lectures plus weekly practical problem sessions, with students presenting solutions to the professor via an online whiteboard. The first semester covers the physical and mathematical foundations (quantization, superposition, wave function, operators, angular momentum, Schrödinger equation, identical particles, approximation methods); the second covers atomic and molecular structure and quantum scattering. The authors note that "the theoretical knowledge gained in lectures is reinforced through practical sessions, where students solve problems independently or with the help of the professor."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Weekly practical sessions and self-preparation problem solving presented to the professor; the course is taught by former SPT graduates with active research backgrounds
#### Constraints
- During the war the course is delivered online via Zoom and online boards rather than in lecture halls

### Target Learners
- third-year undergraduate physics students at SPT

### Learning Goals
- foundations of quantum mechanics, operator formalism, scattering theory, atomic and molecular structure
