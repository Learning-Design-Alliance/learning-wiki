# Four-lesson AI and Robotics curriculum for young children (L01-L04)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A curriculum of four lessons of one and a half hours (L01-L04) reduced from an original ten-lesson design due to limited human and material resources. Lesson 1 introduces human senses and how AI relates to identifying cats and dogs using protoboards and simple electric circuits; lesson 2 uses a tangram activity on how AI recognizes animals or humans plus a mini code introduction; lesson 3 uses a bingo game with key terms, a quesadilla-cooking algorithm activity, and sensor action/reaction activities; lesson 4 closes with a garden activity programming teammates, real-life AI applications, and team robot programming with logo design. Arrows in the curriculum figure connect the end and start of each lesson to summarise progress and connect the full curriculum.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Four instructors of varying experience levels plus a logistics lead to run the workshop
- Open-source educational robots and materials such as protoboards and simple electric circuits
#### Constraints
- Delivered as four lessons instead of the original ten because of limited material and human resources and a self-funded budget

### Target Learners
- Children aged 6 to 9 from low- to mid-income families in a Mexican town

### Learning Goals
- Fundamental concepts of AI and Robotics
- Awareness of the potential of AI and Robotics applications
- Collaborative and literacy skills through hands-on activities
