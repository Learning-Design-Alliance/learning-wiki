# Six-member ED-based trauma team with fixed bedside positions and assigned roles

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes a novel teamwork pattern in which "the team was composed of six ED nurses and doctors": a team leader coordinating the team, an airway physician (J1) at the head of the patient, a primary assessment physician (J2) performing the ABCDE approach and hemostasis by compression, and three nurses handling oxygen and venous lines, instrument preparation and blood sampling, and recording and order entry. Each member is informed of specific responsibilities so the resuscitation proceeds in parallel rather than waiting for consulted specialists.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Every team member must know his or her assigned responsibilities; the article states the key to a team's success is to inform every member of his or her responsibilities
#### Constraints
- The team was composed of medical staff from the emergency department and was designed for the initial phase of care, a task the authors describe as different from that of a full multidisciplinary trauma team

### Target Learners
- Emergency department nurses and physicians in resource-limited hospitals

### Learning Goals
- Coordinated initial management of severely injured trauma patients, including airway management, assessment, hemostasis and test ordering
