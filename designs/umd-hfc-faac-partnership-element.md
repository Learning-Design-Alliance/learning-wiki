# UM-D, HFC and FAAC partnership facilities and programs

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The whitepaper describes a formalized partnership between a 4-year PUI (UM-D), a 2-year college (HFC), and an amateur astronomy club (FAAC), including "theusebystudentsofeachothers'Astronomicalfacilities,andthedevelopmentof educational materialsusingthem." UM-D operates a campus observatory with an 0.4m telescope and four smaller telescopes and offers an Astronomy minor; HFC operates a 38-seat planetarium used in undergraduate education, K-12 outreach, and free public programs; FAAC has approximately 120 members and runs monthly lecture and public outreach observing events.
