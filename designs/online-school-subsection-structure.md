# Online School section structured into six course-aligned subsections

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The resource's largest section, Online School, packages an astrophysics course into six subsections — Data Analysis, Lections, Seminars, Labs, Popular Science and Tasks. It is "mostly original, partly based on the new course in astrophysics launched at the ISU in 2019", so the online structure mirrors a university course while remaining open to external students, and the Data Analysis subsection hosts the interactive CNN client for gamma/hadron separation.
