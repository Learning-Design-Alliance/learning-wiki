# Tablet-based single-word dictation task of 40 words varying orthographic and graphic complexity

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A digital-tablet single-word dictation task used to dissociate spelling from graphomotor performance. Children wrote 40 words whose "target words varied in orthographic complexity and in graphic complexity (simple vs. difficult)", and the task "was scored on spelling accuracy, handwriting quality and handwriting speed", allowing group comparisons across both complexity dimensions simultaneously.
