# Four-phase SNAPPS implementation: preceptor sensitisation, traditional baseline, resident sensitisation, SNAPPS-guided presentation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article structures its intervention as "four structured phases": sensitising preceptors with calibration exercises, baseline case writing by the traditional method with an MCQ pretest, sensitising residents to the six SNAPPS components, and SNAPPS-guided case presentation with checklist scoring, a post-test MCQ and a feedback survey. This phased design embeds the model in existing departmental activities rather than separate sessions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Two trained preceptors supervising sessions
- Standardized SNAPPS checklist with 5-point scoring per component
#### Constraints
- Single-centre, convenience-sampled cohort of 35 residents; same cases used in both phases, which the authors note as a performance-bias risk

### Target Learners
- postgraduate pathology residents at three training levels

### Learning Goals
- structured case presentation
- diagnostic reasoning
- clinicopathological correlation
