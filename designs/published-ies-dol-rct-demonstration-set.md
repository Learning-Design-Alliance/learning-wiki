# Demonstration set of published education RCTs funded by IES, the Department of Labor, and foundations

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article plans to demonstrate the proposed estimation methods using several published RCTs. As printed: "These RCTs tested the effects of a wide range of education interventions, including early elementary school math curricula, , selected reading comprehension interventions, Teach for America, and Job Corps." Random assignment was at the student, school, or teacher (classroom) level in low-performing school districts, with math or reading test scores of elementary students as key outcomes for most studies.
