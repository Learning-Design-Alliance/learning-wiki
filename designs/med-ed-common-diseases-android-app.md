# Android mobile application for teaching common diseases to medical interns (education, examinations and reporting)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes an Android application developed in Java within Android Studio, with content served from an ASP.NET MVC server using C#, HTML and an MS SQL database, connected "through two-way encryption". Its education section was "organized into eight general chapters, encompassing topics like COVID, Cardiology, Pediatrics, Pulmonology, Psychiatry, Orthopedics, Obstetrics & Gynecology, and Diabetes" with text and video content, multiple-choice exams scored immediately, a Report section for historical results, and a web-based management panel for uploading or modifying content.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Android platform and a server delivering content through two-way encryption; instructor-validated educational videos
#### Constraints
- Content production was limited to 15 common diseases due to financial and time constraints

### Target Learners
- medical interns and trainees at a medical university

### Learning Goals
- diagnosis and management of common diseases across eight medical topic areas
