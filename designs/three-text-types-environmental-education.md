# Three text types for environmental education, with problem-definition-without-solution texts most valued

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
For didactically correct selection of foreign-language material, the article distinguishes three types of texts: those containing the description of a problem's definition and its decision, those with only the solution, and those with only the problem definition. The authors write that "The last type of texts refers to the number of problematic and has the particular value". Text work was supported by tasks such as paragraphing, formulating the main idea, and annotating articles around environmental problems.
