# Six-week emotional intelligence skills training programme for secondary students

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A training programme delivering instruction in emotional intelligence skills — self-awareness, self-motivation, self-regulation, empathy, and adeptness in relationships — to an experimental group, while the control group received only conventional classroom teaching. Sessions took place during normal school hours and 'lasted for the period of six weeks where each session lasted 30 minutes twice a week'. Outcomes were measured with the LOCS and ASES before and after treatment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered during normal school hours in scheduled sessions of 30 minutes twice weekly over six weeks
- Measured with the LOCS and ASES administered before and after treatment
