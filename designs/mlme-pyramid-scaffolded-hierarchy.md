# Five-level pyramid hierarchy for AI-integrated engineering curricula based on scaffolded instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The MLME course is organized as a pyramid representing scaffolded instruction, in which guidance is gradually reduced as students gain mastery. The article states the course "is a project -based course designed according to the principles of scaffolded instruction [44], i.e., gradually reducing guidance as students gain mastery, which is represented as a pyramid in Fig. 2." The levels are, from the base: curiosity and self-motivation as the foundational attitude; foundational STEM knowledge provided just-in-time within each project; an introduction level teaching core ML theories and techniques; an application level practicing algorithms on publicly available datasets; and an advanced level covering thermal theory topics and the four integrative projects.
