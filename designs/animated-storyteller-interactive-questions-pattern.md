# Narrative-driven e-learning with interspersed videos and interactive decision-making questions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The web course design combines an animated storyteller narrative with multimedia segments and interactive questions. The article states the narrative "is interspersed with videos, animations and ten interactive questions" and that "the purpose of the interactive questions is to engage the students and force them to use decision-making skills". This pattern was evaluated as a preparatory activity before hands-on CPR training in school children.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a computer for individual completion, or a classroom setting for whole-class delivery
#### Constraints
- Duration limited to 20–30 minutes; delivered within 7 days before practical training in this study

### Target Learners
- School students around age 13

### Learning Goals
- Symptom recognition and health knowledge engagement before practical skills training
