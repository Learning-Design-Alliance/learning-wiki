# Scenario-based simulation day integrating crew resource management skills with increasing difficulty and video-supported three-phase debriefing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The main training day's design alternates scenario performance with structured debriefing. "Crew resource management skills such as speak up, leadership, situational awareness, and decision-making will be integrated in every scenario with increasing difficulty levels." Debriefing uses synchronized video recordings and proceeds through "reaction, analysis and take home" phases, with instructor feedback on both medical technical and crew resource management skills, and learners set individual learning goals that are evaluated at the end.
