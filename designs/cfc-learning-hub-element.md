# Community Fracture Capture (CFC) Learning Hub

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The CFC Learning Hub is a secure, adaptable web-based platform for case-based, interactive small-group learning on osteoporosis management, delivered through the Praxhub platform to primary care physicians. Central to its design is "an interactive Discussion Forum that encourages participant engagement and peer-to-peer exchange on course material," guided by experienced PCP moderators supported by specialist moderators. Between May 2022 and October 2023 it ran four 6-week cycles involving 55 PCPs in groups of 12 to 16, with 33 (60%) actively commenting or asking questions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- PCP and specialist moderators active simultaneously to guide discussions and select case content
#### Constraints
- Designed to address the osteoporosis treatment gap in community settings; participation was voluntary and uncompensated

### Target Learners
- Primary care physicians across a range of professional backgrounds and experience levels

### Learning Goals
- Evidence-based bone health and osteoporosis management in primary care
