# Blended composition course pattern: online lectures, readings, and writing exercises paired with weekly F2F feedback sessions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
This course-level pattern splits a genre-based first-year composition course so that core content delivery and writing practice occur online while class time concentrates on discussion, workshop, and formative feedback. The blended version used "short recorded lectures, electronic readings, and frequent opportunities for online writing and feedback alongside weekly student-instructor interaction time," with recorded discussions of discipline-specific writing practices. Students absent from a session were required to view material, relate it to the textbook, and complete an online writing exercise for a nominal 2% mark.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A learning management system hosting video lectures, electronic readings, editing exercises, and an online dropbox; coordinated content, assignments, and rubrics when multiple instructors teach the format
#### Constraints
- Students were required to attend two thirds of the course F2F; the online discussion group provided was not used by students; two instructors introduced variations in timing and delivery styles

### Target Learners
- first-year university composition students

### Learning Goals
- research writing, summary, analysis, persuasion, revision, and scholarly writing style
