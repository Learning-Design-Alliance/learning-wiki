# Faculty development curriculum combining lectures, group discussions, role-model observation, and scenario-based practice with videotaped feedback

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a faculty development curriculum structure combining multiple instructional modes: a 40-hour Faculty Development Program in General Medicine including a 4-hour feedback course, delivered through lectures and both large- and small-group discussions, followed by an OSTE design for summative evaluation. The conclusion recommends teachers complete "the teaching principle curriculum, which involves learning about theories and general rules in lectures, participating in small group discussions, observing the actions of role models in outstanding teaching cases, and scenario writing and amendment for actual teaching units", with videotaping to provide feedback. This pattern pairs structured instruction with simulated practice and objective observation-based evaluation.
