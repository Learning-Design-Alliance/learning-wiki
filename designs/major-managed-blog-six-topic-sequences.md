# A major-managed course blog structured into six topic sequences split between relevance-targeting and confidence-targeting content

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's blog design organizes weekly articles by upper-level majors (and the instructor) into six topic sequences: Physics in the News, Physics in Aviation, Physics in Entertainment, Physics Humor, Sample Problems, and Graphing Project Head Start. In the authors' motivational framework, "topic sequences 1 through 4 were designed to enhance the introductory students’ sense of relevance, while topic sequences 5 and 6 were designed to enhance the introductory students’ sense of conﬁdence." Each major posted one article weekly under posting guidelines for professionalism and engaging the introductory students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Upper-level majors each required to post an article weekly following posting guidelines designed to ensure professionalism, consistency, effective writing and multimedia use, and engaging introductory students
#### Constraints
- The sequence-to-motivation mapping relies on the Keller/ARCS-based motivational framework the authors adopt, and the design was piloted with a single small Aviation-major cohort

### Target Learners
- introductory physics students (Aviation majors)

### Learning Goals
- sense of relevance of physics concepts
- problem-solving confidence
