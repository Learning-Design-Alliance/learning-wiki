# Combined virtual-jaw simulation training program for preclinical oral implant education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A preclinical training program in which students first receive two-hour theory lectures, then complete an eight-hour operation training session on a virtual simulation system (Zhonghui Technology Institute, UniDental-MS01, China) and a jaw simulation model, following the Dentium implant surgical manual. Assessment consists of a subjective operation score by a senior clinician, CBCT-based implant accuracy analysis (angular, apical, and entrance deviation), and comparison of two theoretical examinations, plus a Likert satisfaction questionnaire.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Virtual simulation platform and jaw simulation model with dental implant toolbox
- CBCT scanning and GALILEOS Viewer software for accuracy assessment
- Instructors with more than 10 years of clinical experience for lectures
#### Constraints
- Training time was too short to fully predict the long-term effect of the teaching methods
- The pig mandible model is not completely equivalent to real clinical cases

### Target Learners
- Second- and third-year undergraduate stomatology students

### Learning Goals
- Implant apparatus and operative procedure knowledge
- Standardized, professional implant surgical technique
