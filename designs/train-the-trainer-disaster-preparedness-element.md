# Train-the-trainer strategy for rapid scale-up of disaster preparedness training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A train-the-trainer team strategy prepares large numbers of health professionals rapidly by training cohorts who can then cascade the training. In the reviewed study, a 3-day workshop in the Philippines used "a train‐the‐trainer team strategy" to rapidly prepare 364 hospital-based health professionals to detect, isolate, and safely manage Ebola virus disease, with the workshop consisting of 18 lectures and 10 practical/small-group sessions including repeated PPE donning/doffing practice with contamination simulation using red paint.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Collaborative development with health authorities (DOH and WHO in the reviewed study)
- Practical small-group sessions including PPE donning/doffing practice
#### Constraints
- Knowledge regarding transmission improved but remained suboptimal (post 59.1% correct for the transmission routes item)

### Target Learners
- hospital-based health professionals
- nurses

### Learning Goals
- Ebola preparedness
- screening and triage
- PPE use
- infection prevention and control
