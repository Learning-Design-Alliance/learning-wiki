# Research feedback pharmacology experimental teaching model (RFPETM)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
RFPETM is a student-centered pharmacology experimental teaching model that feeds the college's scientific research achievements into undergraduate teaching. It "included 10 experimental teaching projects, all of which came from the research achievements of our college," each lasting 4 teaching hours. Students work in self-selected groups of four, learn from self-built online resources and virtual simulation projects, independently search literature and design experimental routes, obtain teacher review of plans, then collect data, analyze statistics, and write reports. The model aims to exercise scientific research thinking and cultivate experimental operation, research thinking, and comprehensive practical abilities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teaching project library based on the pharmacology experimental teaching syllabus and institutional research achievements
- Supervising teachers to review experimental plans and score classroom performance
- Online and virtual simulation resources for pre-class learning
#### Constraints
- Studied at a single Chinese medical university with integrated traditional Chinese and Western medicine majors; retains a proportion of confirmatory and comprehensive experiments

### Target Learners
- medical undergraduate students

### Learning Goals
- scientific research thinking
- experimental operation ability
- innovation ability
