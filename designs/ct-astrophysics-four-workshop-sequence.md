# Four-workshop didactic sequence integrating computational thinking skills into stellar astrophysics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The intervention was a sequence of four workshops on stellar astrophysics — nocturnal observation, the visible spectrum, the non-visible spectrum and radioastronomy — each pairing physics concepts with CT skills such as decomposition, algorithmic thinking, abstraction, debugging, automation and generalization. The article states that "La secuencia didáctica tuvo como objetivo fortalecer y facilitar la apropiación de conceptos físicos, mediante la astrofísica y las habilidades del PC." Supporting tools included diagnostic questions, Export, Caja de Herramientas and ¡Hola Mundo!.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each workshop averaged two class sessions of two hours, as the article prints: "cada taller tuvo una duración promedio de dos sesiones cada una de dos horas de clase."
#### Constraints
- Designed and evaluated with a single Year-11 group at one Colombian school, selected by non-probabilistic convenience sampling.

### Target Learners
- Year-11 secondary students aged 16-18

### Learning Goals
- Stellar astrophysics concepts (stellar observation, electromagnetic spectrum, circuits, radioastronomy)
- Computational thinking skills (decomposition, algorithmic thinking, abstraction, debugging, automation, generalization)
