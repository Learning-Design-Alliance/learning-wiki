# Standing desk with balance pad classroom intervention setup (Hüba-Ergofit desk plus unstable floor mat)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The intervention equipment consisted of height-adjustable standing desks (Hüba-Ergofit 03.AF.OK.FMH.R, 650 × 650 mm top, fixable between 750 and 1200 mm) each fitted with a foam floor mat (450 × 450 × 60 mm) on which pupils stood, creating an unstable surface. A non-slip layer underneath the mat was applied "To avoid accidents". Children had to maintain standing for at least 10 min once standing, and self-recorded transitions with arrows on a 10-min-unit report sheet covering 775 available minutes per school week.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Height-adjustable desks and floor mats fitted between the desk legs
- A self-report sheet with 10-min units for real-time recording
- Teacher reminders to reach the daily standing target
#### Constraints
- Standing had to be maintained for at least 10 min per episode
- Self-report sheets require discipline and are probably not appropriate for longer periods

### Target Learners
- primary school children aged 10–12 years

### Learning Goals
- reduce sedentary classroom time and increase standing during lessons
