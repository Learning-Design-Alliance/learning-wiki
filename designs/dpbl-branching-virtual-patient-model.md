# Decision-PBL (D-PBL): branching virtual patient cases with structured debate at management decision points

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
D-PBL is a variant of problem-based learning that replaces linear paper cases with Web-based branching virtual patients. As the article describes it, "A D-PBL virtual patient case is designed as a series of interconnected nodes, each of which is presented as a Web page", with some nodes offering multiple paths so learners "choose which path they will follow" and experience the consequences of their decisions. The facilitator encourages learners to consider and debate alternative courses of action at each decision point before the group takes a majority-vote path. The model keeps the traditional small-group PBL structure (6-8 learners, weekly face-to-face sessions, independent research) and differs only in the dynamic case medium and the decision debates.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Web-based virtual patient delivery so scenarios respond dynamically and independently to different learner groups and paths are logged centrally
- Cases written and tested under formal scenario-construction guidelines so all paths engender equivalent learning opportunities
- Facilitators who stimulate debate around predefined choices at decision points
#### Constraints
- Suited to learners who already have some PBL experience and sufficient clinical knowledge to tackle patient management problems
- Options appear only after preceding case steps have been fully discussed

### Target Learners
- midstage undergraduate medical students (graduate entry)

### Learning Goals
- clinical decision making and patient management
- integration of biomedical knowledge through PBL
