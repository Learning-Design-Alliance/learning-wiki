# CSCL-based framework combining tool-supported collaboration with structured evaluation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's pedagogical framework integrates computer-supported collaborative learning principles with digital toolchains and multi-source assessment. It combines real-time cloud coding (Google Colab), experiment tracking and reporting (W&B), and rubric-guided peer and instructor evaluation to foster engagement, transparency, and fair evaluation in project-based STEM courses. The authors ground it in social constructivist theory, noting that "learning is inherently social and shaped by cultural context".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured rubrics shared in advance
- Digital platforms supporting shared workspaces and contribution visibility
#### Constraints
- Single-institution, single-discipline implementation may limit generalizability
- Cohort-specific variables such as group dynamics and instructor style could influence outcomes

### Target Learners
- Master's-level STEM students

### Learning Goals
- Collaborative problem-solving
- Technical proficiency
- Evaluative judgment
