# QLoRA-based SFT pipeline for phoneme-controlled children's story generation with three 8B LLMs and four experimental designs

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article builds a pipeline that fine-tunes Llama 3 8B, Apertus 8B-instruct-2509, and Granite 3.3 8b-instruct using QLoRA (4-bit NF4 quantization with LoRA adapters, rank 32, alpha 64) on input-target pairs pairing curriculum lesson phonemes with 2,580 stories generated zero-shot by GPT-4o and Llama 3.3 70B from the 129-lesson UFLI K-2 curriculum. Four designs are compared: baseline SFT, "Good Stories" quality filtering (996 stories selected), Rewarded SFT with reward-weighted loss, and SFT with simulated children's reading errors generated by GPT-OSS-120B. Evaluation uses Spache readability, LM-PPL, coherence, syntactic complexity, toxicity, and Self-BLEU.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training data derived from an expert-designed K-2 phonics curriculum specifying target phonemes per lesson
- Sufficient stories per lesson as SFT targets (2,580 stories, 50-350 words each)
#### Constraints
- Computational resources limited to three L4 GPUs, preventing some SFT strategies and constraining hyperparameters such as training epochs
- Sample size was insufficient for a full RLHF pipeline
- Only a single expert-designed curriculum and one language were used

### Target Learners
- Children in kindergarten through 2nd grade learning English reading

### Learning Goals
- Phoneme-targeted reading practice
- Oral reading fluency through engaging level-appropriate stories
