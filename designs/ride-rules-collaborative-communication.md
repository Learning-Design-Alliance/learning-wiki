# RIDE rules: four principles for effective collaborative communication

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The RIDE framework groups literature-derived characteristics of effective collaboration under four principles: Respect, Intelligent collaboration, Deciding together, and Encouraging. The article states that "The rules we extracted were grouped under four principles" and each rule carries sub-rules, such as 'everyone will have a chance to talk' for Respect and 'ask until you understand' for Encouraging. Each rule is mapped onto expected communicative activities, e.g. Intelligent collaboration onto informative responses, asking for understanding, and argumentative activities.

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instruction must be given before interaction and reminded during it, e.g. via prompts when assignments are opened
#### Constraints
- The Respect rule is represented by components not expressed in frequency of activities, so it was not analyzed via activity frequencies
