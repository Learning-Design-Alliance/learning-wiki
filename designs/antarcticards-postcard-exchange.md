# Antarcticards — a trans-generational postcard exchange with South Pole scientists

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A trans-generational activity for schools and families in which Belgian citizens sent greetings to scientists spending December at the South Pole, designing their own postcard or using an existing one. Participants received a postcard sent from the South Pole carrying a unique post stamp. The article describes it as "a framework for passive science communication" focused on scientists' life, inviting participants to question why they could send a postcard there and what the people at the South Pole were doing and under which conditions.
