# Simultaneous multi-institution Zoom-based laparoscopic training curriculum with training boxes and real-time remote coaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A three-session curriculum of lectures, practical training, and assessments delivered 1 hour per week, connecting a university hospital and three rural hospitals via Zoom. The article states "all sites were connected via Zoom … for real‐time interactive sessions", with a supervising surgeon giving 15-min lectures and monitoring 30-min practice via an iPad and training box at each facility. A maximum of six trainees trained simultaneously, with no more than two per facility.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Standard Wi-Fi internet connection at each facility
- A training box, an iPad, and laparoscopic instruments and consumable supplies at each facility
- At least one senior surgeon present at each facility in case of unexpected situations
#### Constraints
- A maximum of six trainees were trained simultaneously, with no more than two participants from each facility
- Time available to supervise each participant was limited because multiple facilities trained simultaneously

### Target Learners
- Postgraduate year 1–5 surgical trainees at university and rural hospitals

### Learning Goals
- Laparoscopic suture ligation skills, including knot tying and advanced techniques such as overwrapping and running sutures
