# CBE-MDT rotation model combining weekly multidisciplinary sessions, simulation modules, and continuous formative assessment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 12-week rotation design in which weekly multidisciplinary teaching sessions are delivered by radiation oncologists, medical physicists, radiobiologists, and oncology nurses, using "clinical case presentations, treatment planning exercises, and collaborative decision-making simulations". Biweekly simulation-based training modules are paired with structured formative assessments and individualized faculty feedback, and small-group discussions and peer-to-peer evaluations are integrated. Competency achievement is tracked continuously against internationally recognized learning outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An interdisciplinary teaching team spanning multiple specialties
- Structured formative assessments with individualized faculty feedback
#### Constraints
- Evaluated in a single-institution cohort comparison without long-term retention follow-up

### Target Learners
- hospital standardized training trainees in radiation oncology

### Learning Goals
- clinical competencies in radiation oncology
- interdisciplinary collaboration
- clinical reasoning
