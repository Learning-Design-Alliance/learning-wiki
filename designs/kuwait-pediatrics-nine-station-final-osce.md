# Nine-station pediatrics final OSCE with external examiners, real patients and standardized marking

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The final pediatrics OSCE at Kuwait University consists of "9 clinical stations: neurology, pulmonology, gastroenterology, cardiology, communication skills, history taking, clinical procedure demonstration, normal development assessment and one other station that could be genetics or dermatology", run on real pediatric patients, healthy children and simulated parents over two days in four hospitals. Six external examiners from Europe and North America assess students each year. Examiners get protected time before the exam to agree on physical signs and standardize marking, and a global mark is awarded based on fluency and mastery alongside checklists.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- External examiner orientation sessions, pre-exam examiner standardization time, and a pass mark set before the exam
#### Constraints
- The in-training OSCE is graded with feedback only to failing students; exam contents differ across years to keep cases unknown to students

### Target Learners
- 6th-year medical students at Kuwait University

### Learning Goals
- Assessment of clinical competence in history taking, physical examination, communication, procedures and development assessment
