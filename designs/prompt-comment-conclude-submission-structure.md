# Three-part submission structure: prompt record, commentary on AI responses, and concluding statement

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The AI-integrated take-home exam requires each submission to contain three characteristic components: a raw record of the handling of ChatGPT through prompts and responses, comments on the AI-generated answers, and the learners' own concluding thoughts. The author states these components "are evaluated to gauge the learning", with the prompts reflecting understanding and analysis, the comments necessitating in-depth evaluation of the response content, and the concluding statement applying ChatGPT's response alongside other sources.
