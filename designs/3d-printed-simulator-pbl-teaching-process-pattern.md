# 3D-printed simulator integrated PBL teaching process: case exploration, presentation and discussion, instructor demonstration, then hands-on simulator practice

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A lesson-level teaching sequence for surgical education combining PBL with a high-fidelity simulator. Students first receive an authentic clinical case and the simulator for "collaborative exploration encompassing identification of anatomical landmarks within nasolabial subunits, quantitative morphometric analysis of deformity characteristics, and surgical approach formulation," then present a PowerPoint and discuss, after which the instructor demonstrates the surgical process on the simulator and students practice the procedure, with the tutor summarizing remaining questions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Experienced educators assigned as tutors
- Comparable teaching environment conditions including instructors, textbooks and equal learning hours
- Faculty members require specialized training in both technical operations and PBL pedagogy
#### Constraints
- Phased training is recommended to ease student adaptation from low-fidelity models such as sponge-based analogs

### Target Learners
- senior stomatology undergraduates

### Learning Goals
- cleft lip repair theory
- operative skill acquisition
- clinical problem-solving
