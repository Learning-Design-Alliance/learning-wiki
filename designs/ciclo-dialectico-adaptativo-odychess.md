# Ciclo Dial´ectico-Adaptativo: a five-phase iterative teaching-learning cycle (thesis, antithesis, synthesis, reflection, adaptation)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's core process model: learning proceeds through an iterative "Ciclo Dial´ectico-Adaptativo" in which a problem scenario is posed (thesis), the facilitator confronts the learner's ideas with questions and counterexamples generating cognitive conflict (antithesis), the learner builds a solution integrating what was learned (synthesis), reflects and abstracts a transferable principle, and instruction is adapted before the next challenge. The article states it "asegura tanto la construcci´on dial´ogica del conocimiento como la adaptaci´on continua al nivel del aprendiz" and that it operates "desde la micro-interacci´on en un solo problema hasta la planificaci´on a lo largo de m´ultiples sesiones". It combines dialectics, constructivism, scaffolding, problem-based learning and adaptability, and can be orchestrated by a teacher with a group or by a student one-to-one with the IA tutor.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A facilitator (human or IA) able to pose problem scenarios, confront learner ideas with questions and counterexamples, and adjust the difficulty of the next challenge according to observed performance
#### Constraints
- The article presents the cycle as the method's design backbone; its effectiveness is evidenced by the quasi-experimental study rather than by component-level tests of the cycle itself

### Target Learners
- secondary students aged 13-15, beginner-intermediate chess level (ELO estimado < 1200 FIDE)

### Learning Goals
- chess knowledge and strategic understanding
- dialectical and metacognitive reasoning
- problem-solving in novel chess contexts
