# Four-week antenatal orientation interviews training delivered through home visits to prospective parent couples

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (1 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
An intervention in which couples in the experimental group received orientation interviews through home visits over one month, with presentations used so the content was more understandable and information leaflets given after each session. The article states the couples in the experimental group "received orientation interviews through home visits" and that "All interviews were completed in one month." Four sessions covered pregnancy psychology, health and lifestyle during pregnancy, birth preparation and breastfeeding, and baby care, postnatal maternal and paternal psychology, and parent-baby communication.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants must be literate enough to respond to the questionnaire and free of serious prior mental illness history, per the study's inclusion criteria
#### Constraints
- Delivered as informative orientation interviews rather than skills-based counseling, which the authors suggest may have limited its effectiveness

### Target Learners
- prospective mothers and fathers in the antenatal period

### Learning Goals
- adapting to the new life with a baby
- preventing postnatal depression
- informing families about antenatal risks and baby care
