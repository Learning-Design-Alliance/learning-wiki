# Multi-station surgical skills course combining cadaveric, animal tissue, simulator, and dry lab practice with pre-course online lectures and live demonstrations.

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The ANZMSC combines eleven practical stations using varied materials (skills boards, pig eyes, model eyes, cadavers, EyeSi simulators, dry lab sets) with pre-course online lectures, live didactic lectures, and skills demonstrations before practical sessions. The article describes this as "a unique combination of learning stations, each with their own advantage" and notes it is "not assessing only a single learning station, but a range of teaching methods delivered in a package over three days".

## Design Implications

#### Requirements
