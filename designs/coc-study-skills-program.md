# 8-week Co-Curricular (CoC) study-skills program for first-year introductory biology

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
An 8-week voluntary program meeting once weekly for 80 minutes on Fridays, led by a biology faculty member assisted by undergraduate peer leaders, focused on time management and active-learning strategies without content review. The article states it "met once a week on Fridays, for 80 minutes each session, for eight consecutive weeks." Students applied to join, received extra credit for attending seven or eight sessions, and could attend drop-in sessions staffed by peer leaders as makeup or study-group time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must complete an application and attend all eight sessions (or make up via drop-in) to count as full participants and receive extra credit
- Sessions led by a faculty member not teaching the course, assisted by peer leaders who earned at least a B in Bio101
#### Constraints
- Participation was voluntary and self-selected, so student motivation may be a factor in findings
- Only 17% of enrolled students participated; attendance was inversely related to preparedness

### Target Learners
- First-year undergraduates in introductory biology, particularly those with low and medium predicted preparedness

### Learning Goals
- Time management
- Active-learning study strategies
- Metacognition and course retention in a STEM gateway course
