# Five-module one-day didactic training for skills lab student tutors

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A one-day didactic training conducted in parallel groups of no more than 8 participants with two experienced instructors, comprising five modules: presentation with 1:1 video analysis and structured peer feedback, precise explanation via geometric-figure description, spontaneous 'explain on impulse' tasks, Peyton's 4-Step Approach for practical skills, and handling critical incidents via group-dynamics analysis. Tutors worked "learning by doing" and transferred new information to their own tutorial setting.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Student tutors receive the training at the beginning of their teaching activities, before their skills lab tutorial duties
#### Constraints
- Module 3 (explain on impulse) was provided only for tutor cohort 3, so not all modules were taught to every surveyed tutor

### Target Learners
- experienced medical student tutors in an undergraduate skills lab for Internal Medicine

### Learning Goals
- didactic and presentation techniques
- explaining practical skills
- giving and receiving feedback
- managing difficult group situations
