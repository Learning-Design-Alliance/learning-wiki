# Model of Educational Reconstruction as the framework linking teacher PD to curriculum development

## Description
The Model of Educational Reconstruction (MER), attributed to Duit et al. (2012), is the study's organizing framework for improving instructional practices and teacher professional development in science education. The article states that "The MER integrates research and development perspectives in three interconnected strands: 1) research on teaching and learning, 2) clarification of content, and 3) evaluation of learning resources." In the Einstein-First project, teacher perspectives played an integral role in each strand: informal training sessions let teachers select the most effective activities, lessons were refined from teacher feedback and school trials, and collaboration clarified teacher misconceptions and refined lesson plans and videos.
