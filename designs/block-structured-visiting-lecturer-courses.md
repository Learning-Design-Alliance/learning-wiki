# Block-based intensive course structure with lectures and exam inside each block

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article organises the whole educational program into intensive blocks because a large section of courses is given by visiting lecturers. Each block is 'lasting between 2 to 4 weeks, contains the lectures as well as the exam', so instruction and assessment are completed within one short unit. This pattern allows an international faculty to deliver a full master curriculum without long residencies, while students progress through required and elective courses semester by semester.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A large section of courses must be given by visiting lecturers, which the article gives as the reason the whole educational program is organised in blocks
#### Constraints
- Each block lasts between 2 and 4 weeks and contains both the lectures and the exam, so pacing is fixed by the block length

### Target Learners
- master students in astrophysics

### Learning Goals
- thorough astrophysical background
- completion of required and elective master courses
