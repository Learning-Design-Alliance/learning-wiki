# Model-lead-test lesson format with progress monitoring and re-teaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each SSL lesson sequences four to six activities and follows a model-lead-test format: "The teacher first models the target skill. Students replicate the example and practice the skill with assistance from the teacher." Teachers then monitor student progress and re-teach skills to students who do not fully master them, and lessons can be repeated to support mastery. Each lesson begins with a set of nursery rhymes before the sequenced instructional activities.
