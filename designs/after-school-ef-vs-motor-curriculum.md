# After-school EF/VS curriculum of constructional, paper-and-pencil, and gross motor activities

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A non-academic after-school curriculum for K–1 children built from increasingly complex constructional tasks, tracing and motor precision "Paper and Pencil Activities", and gross motor games such as Red Light Green Light and Simon Says selected to exercise impulse control, sustained attention, and working memory. Activities were screened with four criteria including adaptability, complexity, trainability, and variety of materials, and were delivered by trained paraprofessionals to groups of four to seven children.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors require training; implementers received 11 hours of additional curriculum training beyond a week-long behavior-management workshop
#### Constraints
- Delivered in a 45-minute block, 4 days a week, over 24 weeks of after-school programming

### Target Learners
- kindergarten and first-grade children from low-income communities

### Learning Goals
- executive function skills
- visuospatial skills
- classroom behavioral adjustment
