# The SIAPeC Quiz: a gamified, case-based pathology quiz session for junior pathologists

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The SIAPeC Quiz was an interactive session at the 2024 SIAPeC annual congress designed to "evaluate the utility of gamification in pathology education" for junior pathologists. It used case-based challenges simulating real-world diagnostic scenarios, immediate feedback, and digital tools including whole-slide images and multimedia platforms. Questions spanned histology, general pathology, subspecialties, and information-constrained formats, allowing trainees to "safely encounter and learn from errors in a simulated environment".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A web-based quiz platform compatible with text, static images, links to whole-slide images, multiple-choice and open-text answers, and real-time pacing
- Participants need personal smartphones or similar devices to access the quiz
#### Constraints
- Open-text answers caused spelling-variation problems in answer recognition (e.g., "spiroadenoma" vs. "spiradenoma")
- A minority of participants experienced initial connection difficulties via smartphones

### Target Learners
- Junior pathologists (residents and fellows under 35 years)

### Learning Goals
- Diagnostic skills in pathology
- Active learning with immediate feedback
- Engagement with digital tools including whole-slide images
