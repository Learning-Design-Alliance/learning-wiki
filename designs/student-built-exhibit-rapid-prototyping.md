# Student-built public exhibit with rapid prototyping as the culminating format

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
On day two, students collectively design and build a coherent public exhibit of all project results: “The goal of the second day is to present all the project results in a shared original exhibit, for which the students are responsible.” They are strongly encouraged to go beyond posters toward mock-ups, short activities, discussions, or quizzes, and use fast prototyping — thirty minutes building rough mock-ups from frugal materials — before three hours of design and construction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A strong public deadline: the opening of the exhibit, midafternoon, fixes the deadline.
- An inspiration room with references on science, design, art, and do-it-yourself projects, plus an introduction to fast prototyping and thirty minutes of mock-up building with frugal materials.
#### Constraints
- The exhibit should be coherent and not simply a juxtaposition of different stands.
- The exhibit is short-lived.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics students

### Learning Goals
- communication and transmission of results to a public
- creativity and soft skills
- thinking outside the usual lab reports
