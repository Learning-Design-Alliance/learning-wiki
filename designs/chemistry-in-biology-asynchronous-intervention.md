# Chemistry-in-Biology asynchronous intervention: prequiz, postquiz, and six optional modules with student-narrated videos and practice problems

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention is an asynchronous, mastery-based package consisting of a graded prequiz, a graded postquiz with an isomorphic question bank, and "optional asynchronous instructional 5–20 min videos narrated by former students and practice problems targeting six chemistry-in-biology concepts: size and scale, chemical structures, chemical bonds, electronegativity, polarity, and hydrophobic and hydrophilic interactions". Collectively it comprised about 1.5 hours of short videos and 22 practice problems designed modularly so students could choose which modules to engage. Videos followed recommendations including segmenting, conversational dual narrators, learning-glass drawing, and active-learning prompts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students complete the prequiz in week 1 and receive feedback with hyperlinks to relevant modules; the postquiz is taken in week 2 before midterm 1, with mastery defined as 90% correctness (83% for one instructor).
#### Constraints
- The design required students to complete optional assignments outside of class, which the authors acknowledge could further disadvantage students with time constraints.

### Target Learners
- introductory biology undergraduates, including majors and nonmajors, with no chemistry prerequisite

### Learning Goals
- chemistry-in-biology concepts: size and scale, chemical structures, chemical bonds, electronegativity, polarity, and hydrophobic and hydrophilic interactions
