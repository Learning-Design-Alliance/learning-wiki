# Nurse case management program with monthly follow-up and quarterly group sessions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Case management in this article follows a model including "basic assessment; improvement planning; management of implementation; coordination between patient and nurse care; and supervision by the medical team". An experienced nurse case manager followed up participants at least once a month in 30-minute sessions (office appointments and telephone calls) and ran group sessions once every 3 months focused on "realistic aspects of physical activity and nutrition", goal setting, strategies to overcome barriers, and food preparation demonstrations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An experienced nurse case manager able to sustain at least monthly follow-up over the program period
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only as an addition to standard DSME in combination with simulation education, in a single-center 6-month trial

### Target Learners
- Adults with type 2 diabetes receiving hospital-based diabetes education

### Learning Goals
- Sustained self-management behavior change: physical activity, nutrition, goal setting and barrier management
