# Life course module contrasting the virtual patient's SDH trajectory with a privileged companion

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An interactive module within the app that "delves into the patient's prior life experiences, especially those related to SDH" across different ages of the main virtual character. It presents "a series of illustrations that represent significant life events of the main virtual characters", contrasting them with "a privileged companion character" to underscore how social determinants shape health trajectories. The article states this module fosters empathy, compassion, and understanding of enduring SDH effects on patient well-being and clinical encounters.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Adapted content originally developed by CityMatCH and later adapted by Wright State University; tailored illustrations for each virtual case scenario
#### Constraints
- Content is specific to each virtual patient's case scenario

### Target Learners
- health care professionals and other learners using the app

### Learning Goals
- recognition of how SDH intensifies patient challenges
- deepened understanding of patients' lived experiences
- reduced bias toward marginalized patients
