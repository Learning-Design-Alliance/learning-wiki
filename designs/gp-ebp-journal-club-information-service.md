# Evidence-based information service embedded in an EBP journal club in general practice

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes an information service piloted in 2017, embedded within an EBP journal club in general practices in New South Wales and Queensland, in which practices "formulate and submit clinical questions arising from daily practice that they felt warranted answering and further discussion/critical appraisal in a subsequent journal club". Questions were submitted via an assigned GP liaison, stored in an internal database, and retrieved for coding. Over 1.5 years, "A total of 126 questions from the five participating practice groups were entered into the database".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An assigned GP liaison to submit questions
- an internal database for storing and retrieving questions
- a journal club for subsequent discussion and critical appraisal
#### Constraints
- Practices were purposively selected as early adopters and opinion leaders; submission volumes varied from 0–10 questions per practice per month.

### Target Learners
- general practitioners in participating practices

### Learning Goals
- answering prioritised clinical questions
- supporting evidence-based practice
