# Four-week "Learning, Thinking & Conveying" training program with experiential learning for emergency department rotations

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A 4-week structured training program combining "Learning, Thinking & Conveying" steps with experiential learning, developed against hospital teaching requirements and emergency department clinical practice needs. "The program included 10 thematic medical records, covering emergency triage, sudden death, acute myocardial infarction, acute cerebral infarction, trauma, poisoning, and shock, with 3 randomly selected for each internship cycle." Each week follows the five steps, ending with teacher-guided bedside practice and a written reflection journal.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teaching teacher guidance for group PPT production, nursing rounds and one-on-one bedside supervision
- Thematic medical records aligned with emergency department clinical practice needs
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only over 4-week internship cycles in one tertiary care emergency department

### Target Learners
- full-time undergraduate nursing students beginning clinical practice

### Learning Goals
- theoretical knowledge
- self-directed learning
- critical thinking disposition
