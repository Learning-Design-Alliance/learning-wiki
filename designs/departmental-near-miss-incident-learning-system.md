# Departmental high-volume near-miss incident learning system as a learning environment for residents

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The department operates a high-volume near-miss incident learning system that anchors the program's continuous learning component: "The department operates a high‐volume near‐miss incident learning system where approximately 25 quality reports are analyzed every week." Residents attend weekly safety committee meetings from the start of residency, first observing and later contributing more actively — a change made at residents' own request after the program was "developed in an iterative manner, with feedback from the residents." The authors argue such systems engage learners because incidents are highly relevant to radiotherapy practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A functioning departmental incident learning system and a strong culture of safety are needed; the article links safety culture to patient outcomes.
#### Constraints
- Programs lacking such a system may substitute literature case studies or publicly available RO-ILS cases, which the article treats as a partial substitute.

### Target Learners
- medical physics residents

- learning from near-miss events
