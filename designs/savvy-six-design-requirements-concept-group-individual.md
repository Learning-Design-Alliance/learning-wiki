# Six design requirements organized by concept, group, and individual analysis objects for attention visualization

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
From a preliminary study with four education experts, the authors derived six design requirements (R1-R6) categorized by three analysis objects: the course concept, the student group, and the individual student. The Concept category analyzes attention from the course structure for rapid screening and preliminary attribution; the Group category focuses on temporal attention trends and audiovisual instructional design; and the Individual category centers on "each student's detailed attention trajectory and audiovisual attention allocation." The decomposition matches the experts' agreed top-down workflow from overall course structure to specific causes of attention fluctuations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Accurate extraction of concepts and their relationships as a prerequisite for helping teachers review the overall course structure
#### Constraints
- Group aggregated results may mask individual differences, as attention patterns observed at the group level may not hold consistently across individual students

### Target Learners
- teachers and course instructors analyzing pilot-cohort attention data

### Learning Goals
- locating low-engagement segments and diagnosing audiovisual instructional design deficiencies in instructional videos
