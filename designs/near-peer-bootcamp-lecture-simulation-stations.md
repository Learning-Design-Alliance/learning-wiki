# Near-peer otolaryngology bootcamp pattern: lectures followed by rotating hands-on simulation stations

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The workshop pattern combines anatomy and procedural-overview lectures with small-group rotation through three hands-on simulation stations, each guided by near-peer teachers. The article states "Hands-on training was preceded by lectures encapsulating relevant anatomy, indications, complications, and step-by-step instructions," with participants divided into small groups rotating through the stations. Educational material was preapproved by otolaryngology faculty before delivery.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educational and teaching material preapproved by otolaryngology faculty before delivery
#### Constraints
- Topics selected based on consultation with residents about challenging areas requiring hands-on training

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students

### Learning Goals
- management of otolaryngology emergencies including epistaxis, cricothyrotomy, and nasolaryngoscopy
