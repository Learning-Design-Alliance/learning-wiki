# INSIGHT: a three-hour teacher professional development training session on AI-based technologies for individualised instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
INSIGHT (Information on Schooling with Generative AI and Helpful AI Tools) is a three-hour in-person TPD session for in-service teachers, designed as a pilot intervention to be iteratively refined before larger-scale transfer. It "combines theoretical input, hands-on experiences, and guided reflection" and consists of four parts: an introduction to AI, a hands-on session training an ML system with Google’s Teachable Machine, a session on LLM-supported individualised instruction, and a session on teacher-guided support of students’ AI competencies. It was evaluated in a pilot pre-post study across five schools in one German federal state.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered in person by the same two trainers following the same training concept, materials, and activities; group sizes ranged from 11 to 29 participants
#### Constraints
- Designed as a pilot to refine content and didactical design rather than as definitive evidence of effectiveness

### Target Learners
- in-service primary and secondary teachers in Germany

### Learning Goals
- basic understanding of AI-based technologies and their pedagogically meaningful use for supporting individualised instruction, including critical reflection on opportunities and limitations
