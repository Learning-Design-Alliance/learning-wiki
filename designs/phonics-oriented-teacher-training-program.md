# Phonics-oriented teacher training program with five content sections

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The four-month training-of-teachers program was organized so that "five sections were covered: phonics as the core, Oral English skills, English teaching skills, English teaching principles, and English textbook teaching." The phonics core taught 26 single phonograms, eight types of multiple phonograms and phonics spelling rules, while other sections built oral English, interactive teaching skills (games, songs, stick figures), teaching theory, and textbook analysis. Spelling rules were learned through games, nursery, story and body language rather than directly.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trainees need prior English foundation; core phonics content includes letter-sound knowledge, phonograms and spelling rules
#### Constraints
- Only about 50% of vocabulary has completely transparent sound-form relationship, so rules of sound change and syllabification are needed

### Target Learners
- In-service rural primary EFL teachers without an English language teaching diploma

### Learning Goals
- Standard English pronunciation
- Phonics-based word decoding and spelling instruction
- Interactive English teaching skills
