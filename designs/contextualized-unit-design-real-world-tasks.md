# Contextualized unit design: same fundamental concepts taught through real-world application, video, and authentic tasks

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In the experimental treatment, slideshow content presented the same fundamental concepts as the comparison treatment but applied them to real-world situations highlighting how technologies operate in industry. Lessons used "extensive use of video presentations such as virtual field trips and how–to type documentaries", and activities included case studies, problem-solving scenarios, and team-based exercises, several requiring students to produce artifacts a teacher would create in a job-related scenario. Assessment likewise employed authentic tasks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All teaching and assessment materials must be designed with the components of contextual teaching and learning in mind
#### Constraints
- Delivered via distance education through Moodle; results limited to one course at one university with small class sizes

### Target Learners
- postsecondary students, primarily agricultural education majors

### Learning Goals
- scientific concepts of environmental biotechnology
- application of content to real-world agricultural contexts
