# Swiss QT literary intervention package: nine mini-lessons, four literary-fiction books, literacy journals, and small-group discussions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The adapted intervention is a one-year language-arts program in which students were "instructed on how to identify various authentic questions, justify their claims, and collaboratively construct knowledge in discussions through nine mini-lessons". Students read and discussed four award-winning children's books in small groups across 11 literary discussions, kept literacy journals to prepare authentic questions and responses, and followed discussion rules establishing normative expectations for a dialogic culture. Teachers implemented the program with high fidelity, spending 34.07 hours versus a prescribed 32.92 hours.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher professional development: two workshops plus three to four video-based coaching sessions
- Heterogeneous small groups of three to seven students formed at the study's outset
#### Constraints
- The adaptation exclusively focuses on literary fiction with a moral-dilemma theme matching the assessment story

### Target Learners
- Fourth- and fifth-grade students in German-speaking Swiss primary schools

### Learning Goals
- Asking authentic questions
- Justifying claims
- Collaboratively constructing knowledge in discussion
