## Description
The model rests on design thinking principles and constructivist learning theory, emphasizing imagination, collaborative learning, and scaffolding. The article states the design thinking approach "provides a structured process of five stages comprising empathy, definition, ideation, prototyping, and testing, leading students in building innovation competencies." It also grounds the methodology in disruptive innovation theory, Kieran Egan's imaginative education, and the Kolb learning model with its four learning modes.
