# Intensified Tier II design: narrowed content targets, smaller groups, separated strands, and progress-monitoring-based pacing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
This design pattern describes how the authors revised their Tier II preschool intervention between studies. They narrowed content within each strand "to allow more time on topic across fewer distinct content targets", reduced the maximum small-group size from 6 to 4 children, separated phonological awareness and print knowledge into distinct strands, and added regular progress-monitoring assessments that determined whether groups advanced or repeated lessons. Lesson plans also explicitly included upward and downward scaffolding instructions so within-group instruction could be differentiated.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Detailed written lesson plans describing daily activities, duration, and scripts; interventionists trained (approximately 20 hours in Study 2) with audio-recorded sessions reviewed for fidelity and weekly feedback
#### Constraints
- Evaluated with 11-week small-group supplemental instruction for preschoolers already failing to progress under an evidence-based Tier I curriculum in Title I preschools

### Target Learners
- preschool children at significant risk of reading difficulties identified via RTI screening

### Learning Goals
- phonological awareness, print knowledge (letter names and sounds), and oral language/vocabulary
