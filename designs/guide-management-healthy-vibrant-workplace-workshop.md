# A guide to management for healthy vibrant workplace: a 150-minute manager training workshop with five sections

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An original training program developed by a clinical psychologist and a professor of occupational mental health based on the HSE management competencies framework. It was "offered in the form of a 150‐min workshop comprised lectures on various topics, group works, and homework", delivered to 20-30 participants per session across four sessions by the same instructor. Its five sections are self-check with the SMCIT, announcing strengths, group discussion, presentation, and setting personal goals with action plans.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants are managers who supervise at least one subordinate worker; managers with management experience of <3 months were excluded
#### Constraints
- Delivered in a single finance company in Japan without a control group; the authors note it may not have been intense enough for effects lasting to 1-year follow-up

### Target Learners
- Workplace managers in a finance company

### Learning Goals
- Assessing own management competencies, gaining ideas about management behaviors through group discussion, and improving everyday management behaviors via personal goals and action plans
