# Six-week multi-component social studies vocabulary intervention package for fourth grade

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A researcher-designed six-week curriculum for fourth-grade social studies in which treatment teachers spent at least 90 minutes per week of language arts time on vocabulary instruction, preferably in 30-minute segments three times per week. "The training  included the use of each of the following: explicit instruction; student study teams; active engagement in learning tasks; vocabulary maps; connections webs; semantic feature analysis." Teachers received curriculum notebooks, supplemental materials, and games such as Ready, Set, Go, Vocabulary Memory, and Jeopardy, providing repetition and use of words in multiple contexts. Implementation was monitored weekly by trained observers using a checklist with quality rated on a 0-2 scale.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher training in the vocabulary instructional methods prior to implementation
- A minimum of 90 minutes per week of instructional time, preferably 30 minutes three times per week
#### Constraints
- Teachers reported lack of time to complete the instructional strategies and concern that the vocabulary focus limited coverage of textbook content

### Target Learners
- fourth-grade social studies students

### Learning Goals
- retention of social studies word meanings in isolation and in context
- comprehension of expository content-area text
