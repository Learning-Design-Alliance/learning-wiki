# SDH-embedded CBL as a generalizable teaching paradigm for the biopsychosocial model in clinical education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes an integrated teaching model combining SDH content with active learning strategies within the CBL framework. It states: "The key to its success lies in embedding SDH into core teaching components and leveraging standardized patients and reflective writing to strengthen contextual immersion and metacognition. This model provides a generalizable teaching paradigm for implementing the biopsychosocial model of medicine." The framework organizes SDH integration around three core teaching components: social history-taking, social factor analysis, and individualized support plan development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Embedding SDH into core teaching components rather than appending it, with standardized patients, reflective writing, and structured feedback
#### Constraints
- The single-center, single-case design limits direct generalizability; the pedagogical framework is adaptable to other disciplines but the specific SDH content would need to be tailored to each specialty's relevant social determinants

### Target Learners
- fifth-year undergraduate medical students on surgical internship

### Learning Goals
- comprehensive clinical competence to identify, analyze, and address social health issues
