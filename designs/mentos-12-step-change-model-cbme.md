# Mento's 12-step change-management model as a framework for pedagogical change in CBME

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Mento's model is a change-management framework blending Kotter's, Jick's and GE's change models, organized into 12 steps from defining the idea through integrating lessons learned. The article uses it to blueprint the introduction of flipped teaching in phase-1 structure-function courses, stating it "draws from the precepts of Kotter's model, but also incorporates axioms from two other model of change". Steps include preparing recipients, creating a Leader Team, creating small wins, measuring progress, and integrating lessons learned.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A Leader Team assembled to maximize appropriate skill-sets
- A sponsor among academic leadership to legitimize the change effort
- A faculty development plan to prepare recipients of change
#### Constraints
- The article presents it as a proof-of-concept framework; outcomes of the change process are not yet measured

### Target Learners
