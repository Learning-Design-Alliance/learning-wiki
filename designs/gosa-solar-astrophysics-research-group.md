# Group of Solar Astrophysics (GoSA) at the National Astronomical Observatory of Colombia

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
GoSA, begun in 2011 at Universidad Nacional de Colombia's National Astronomical Observatory, convenes undergraduate and graduate students in solar data analysis. After a 2012 International Summer School, many physics undergraduates chose solar physics research projects, trained in IDL and later Python as part of the Sunpy collaboration. The project "Magnetic field in the solar atmosphere" comprises "A number of 7 master students, and 12 undergraduate students", and the group developed three solar radio interferometers, including the First Colombian Radio-interferometer (FiCoRi).
