# RFETM research project cycle: small groups design, get teacher review, run, and report on exploratory experiments

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The RFETM implementation cycle organizes each project so that 'The students were divided into groups, each group consisted of 4 students'; students consult literature on the research background, then 'Under the guidance of the experimental guidance teachers, students designed technical routes and implementation plans, completed feasibility assessments, and developed specific experimental steps.' Teachers review the design's rationality and safety before students conduct the experiments, analyze results and problems, and write experimental reports with teacher inspiration and guidance.
