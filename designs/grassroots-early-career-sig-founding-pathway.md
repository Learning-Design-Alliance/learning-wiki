# Grassroots pathway for founding an early-career special interest group within a professional association

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A repeatable sequence for establishing an early-career group inside an existing professional body: establish need via informal peer communication and surveying, draft a collectively signed proposal to the association's CEO, hold management meetings, draft formal Terms of Reference covering "group definition, mission, accountability , roles and responsibilities, and eligibility", obtain formal approval, present at a conference, and run an Expression of Interest process for a leadership committee. NESIG followed this pathway in 2021.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Adequate demonstrated interest amongst early-career peers
- Support and guidance of senior members
- Approval by the association's management and board structures
#### Constraints
- The group was started in an informal manner and only later developed into a formal special interest group

### Target Learners
- Newly-joined and early career members of a professional association

### Learning Goals
- Professional development, networking, and succession planning
