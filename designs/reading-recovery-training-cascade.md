# Reading Recovery training cascade: university-affiliated teacher training sites, teacher leaders, and certified teachers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Reading Recovery® sustains implementation through a three-level training infrastructure. Teacher training sites in public schools, affiliated with partnering universities, house teacher leaders who train teachers. Teacher leaders complete a yearlong training of graduate coursework and supervised practicums, and are expected to "provide initial training for up to 15 teacher candidates and provide ongoing support for 40 to 60 Reading Recovery® teachers or teachers in training annually." Teachers complete a yearlong graduate-credit training and must maintain registration by teaching at least eight students per year and participating in ongoing professional development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher leaders hold a master's degree and teaching credentials and complete a yearlong certification training
- Teachers must be certified, experienced early elementary teachers and complete a year of training with graduate credit
- Teachers must maintain registration by teaching at least eight students per year and attending ongoing professional development, typically no less than six sessions per school year
#### Constraints
- Neither contributing study provided details about the Reading Recovery® teacher training sites or the training of teacher leaders
- Registration lapses after 1 year if teachers do not maintain the requirements

### Target Learners
- Grade 1 students with low literacy achievement in districts implementing Reading Recovery®

### Learning Goals
- Early literacy intervention delivered with training fidelity
