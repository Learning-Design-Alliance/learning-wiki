# Online study group (OSG) for geriatric psychiatry CPD

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
An interactive, expert-facilitated, asynchronous online study group for psychiatrists and residents preparing for the RCPSC geriatric psychiatry certification exam. It comprised 22 fourteen-day modules; each facilitator recommended readings, and participants used four discussion rooms: short-answer questions, reflective discussion, a journal club, and a "Clinical Corner" for cases and questions. The first cohort ran October 2012 to August 2013 with 45 practicing psychiatrists and 5 trainees.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A facilitator (geriatric psychiatrist) per module who recommends readings and poses questions
- A web portal with group discussion rooms and access to parallel didactic lecture recordings
#### Constraints
- Participation was not mandatory and the course was self-accredited, which the authors identify as possible barriers to active participation

### Target Learners
- Practicing psychiatrists caring for the elderly
- Geriatric psychiatry residents

### Learning Goals
- Preparation for the RCPSC geriatric psychiatry certification examination
- Knowledge of assessment and treatment of geriatric psychiatric disorders
