# What/Why/When/Where/How framework for useful narrative evaluations

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A framework guiding faculty supervising residents to write narrative evaluations useful for clinical competency committees. As printed, "The what/why/when/where/how framework highlighted key features of useful narrative evaluations: behaviors of strength and growth, contextualized observations, improvement over time, and actionable next steps." The what/why components elicit specific behavioral examples and their salience; when/where situate observations in training stage and setting; the how component prompts next steps for improvement. It was informed by a local qualitative needs assessment of the CCC and literature, and was designed to decrease observing faculty's cognitive load.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty apply it during or after direct observation of residents in the clinical setting
- Faculty must know the trainee's training stage and the observation context
#### Constraints
- The article notes writing evaluations with the framework "may take more time and effort than traditional narrative evaluations"
- Developed and assessed in one internal medicine residency at a single institution

### Target Learners
- Faculty supervising internal medicine residents

### Learning Goals
- Writing narrative evaluations that are useful for clinical competency committee assessment
