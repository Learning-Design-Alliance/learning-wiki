# Weekly vocabulary cycle: native-language preview, English introduction, small-group word tasks, and word-analysis Friday

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
VIP organizes instruction as a repeating weekly cycle around a weekly reading assignment. "On Mondays English language learner students are given the weekly reading assignment in their native language to preview before it is introduced in English on the following day." Tuesdays bring whole-group lessons defining target vocabulary; Wednesdays use heterogeneous language groups for two cloze activities; Thursdays use small groups for word association, synonym/antonym, and semantic feature analysis; Fridays cover root-word analysis and multiple meanings of words.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Bilingual capacity or materials to provide the native-language (Spanish) preview on Monday
- Heterogeneous grouping based on English language proficiency for mid-week activities
#### Constraints
- Evaluated in grades 4-6 with Spanish-speaking English language learners in bilingual and mainstream classrooms

### Target Learners
- fifth-grade English language learners
- fluent English speaking classmates

### Learning Goals
- depth of vocabulary knowledge
- word meaning, morphology, and multiple meanings
