# Activity-based resiliency module combining self-directed reflection with group sharing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The researchers designed an instructional module of classroom activities targeting selected protective factors: creativity, self-esteem, self-efficacy, internal locus of control, autonomy, problem-solving skill, optimism and hope, sense of humor, stress coping skills and teacher-student relationship. "The modules is an amalgamation of self-directed reflection activities with group sharing in order to hear, more in- depth, other peoples’ stories and to identify which skills they feel need additional practice." It was delivered in one-hour daily sessions over three months by a researcher acting as resilience teacher, and was validated through expert opinion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A resilience teacher spending one hour daily with a separate section of non-resilient at-risk students
- Validation through expert opinion before use
#### Constraints
- Offered by a researcher who was not the students' regular teacher; limited to the selected protective factors due to a tight time schedule

### Target Learners
- Non-resilient at-risk of failure students at secondary school level

### Learning Goals
- Protective factors contributing to academic resilience
