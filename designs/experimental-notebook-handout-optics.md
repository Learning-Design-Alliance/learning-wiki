# Course handout designed as an experimental notebook with summary boxes and 23 further-reading inserts

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The handout guides students through the sessions in the spirit of training manuals and is conceived as an experimental notebook in which students record observations, schematics and measurement tables. "Measurements of a given quantity with different methods are synthesized in special summary boxes" that give a feeling of which method is best suited to the goal pursued. Elements of the course are interleaved so the handout can be read linearly, and 23 further-reading boxes cover topics such as optical fibers, the Hubble Space Telescope, and lasers.
