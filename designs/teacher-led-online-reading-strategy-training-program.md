# Teacher-led online reading strategy training program embedded in a digital literacy course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A training program implemented and tested as part of a digital literacy course, in which instructors delivered strategy training after "instructors received background information and instructional materials prior to the beginning of the term." One hundred sixty-seven university students were assigned to either a treatment or a control condition, and outcomes were measured with search and evaluation tasks representative of the target skills.
