## Description
The article describes Model Driven Design (MDD), attributed to Stephen (2012), as "the concept of developing complex learning experiences through the use of a team model (how the team is staffed and empowered), a process model (how the experience is developed) and a perceptual model (how the experience is discussed and visualized)". It provides the ability to share models and learning units beyond the borders of the institution. The article presents blended learning as a process-model instructional design committing to steps from developing a curriculum concept and high-level design through detailed unit design, development of supporting digital materials, review and refinement, implementation, and ongoing assessment of program effectiveness.
