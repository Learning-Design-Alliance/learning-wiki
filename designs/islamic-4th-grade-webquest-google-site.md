# Islamic 4th-grade WebQuest site built on Google Sites

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A WebQuest site designed and developed by the researchers using Google Sites at https://sites.google.com/site/islamic4thgrade/, presenting the six WebQuest components (Introduction, Task, Process, Resources, Evaluation, Conclusion) as pages. The site content "relies on students cooperative work groups within classes using work sheet for each of the 6 session", combining face-to-face teaching in computer labs with asynchronous navigation and communication with teachers. It was validated by two PhD referees for accessibility and piloted with six students for ease of use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Validated by two referees holding PhDs in Instructional Technology to assure accessibility
- Three teachers were trained to use the web site and its instructional design for the experimental groups
#### Constraints
- Content is specific to spiritual intelligence topics for 4th graders in the Jordanian context

### Target Learners
- 4th grade students in Jordanian public and private schools
