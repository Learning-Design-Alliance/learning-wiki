# Scale SKIT through scenario standardization, instructor certification, and VR/AI integration

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors' forward-looking rollout recommendations for adopting SKIT beyond the pilot: build a standardized scenario library, certify instructors, and integrate technology. They state "the need for scenario standardization requires the establishment of a scenario library covering typical emergency scenarios such as poisoning, trauma, and cardiopulmonary resuscitation, and incorporation into the mandatory residency training curriculum", alongside special certification training for instructors in script design, dynamic intervention, and debriefing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must master script design, dynamic intervention, and debriefing guidance skills
- A scenario library covering typical emergency scenarios
#### Constraints
- Generalizability limited by single-center pilot with 18 participants per group

### Target Learners
- Resident physicians in emergency and high-collaboration specialties

### Learning Goals
- Standardized emergency competency training at scale
