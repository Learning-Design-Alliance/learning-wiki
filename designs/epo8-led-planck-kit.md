# Low-cost portable LED Planck-constant kit distributed to every EPO8 participant

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
EPO8 supplied each participant with a small kit for measuring the Planck constant by LED. Figure 1 lists its contents: "1 small plastic bag containing 8 light emitting diodes (LED), 1 paper clip and 2 small rubber rings, 1 potentiometer, 1 piece of black plastic straw, 3 metal binder clips, 1 battery holder for 3 AA-type batteries, 1 double sided adhesive tape, 1 compact disk (CD), 7 connecting crocodile cables, 1 torch with battery holder taken out, 5 bands of 6 differently colored sticky labels." Participants additionally supply multimeters, batteries, paper and a darkened room.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants must provide two multimeters, 3 AA and 3 AAA batteries, millimeter paper, paper cubes, scissors and a darkenable room.
#### Constraints
- The room must be darkened with dense curtains for the visual threshold measurement, and the human eye as sensor makes the U* results subjective, as the paper notes.

### Target Learners
- high school students
- university students

### Learning Goals
- measuring the Planck constant
- wavelength measurement by diffraction
- current-voltage characterization of diodes
