# Goggleminds VR sepsis simulation delivered on Meta Quest 2 head-mounted displays

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The VR intervention was a sepsis management simulation developed by Goggleminds, codeveloped with NHS training providers and endorsed by the Sepsis Trust, accessed on Meta Quest 2 headsets. Two-hour sessions included prebrief, orientation, 2-3 individually completed scenarios watched by peers via screencast, and whole group debrief following each scenario. Earlier study of the scenario found it could elicit high intrinsic motivation, perceived learning, and immersion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Meta Quest 2 head-mounted displays running the Goggleminds scenario software
- session prebrief and orientation to the virtual environment, with whole group debrief following each scenario
#### Constraints
- Some participants noted motion or cybersickness, a known occurrence in VR settings, managed by completing the simulation in a seated rather than ambulatory position

### Target Learners
- final-year medical students

### Learning Goals
- sepsis recognition and management
- recognition of septic shock and need for critical care
