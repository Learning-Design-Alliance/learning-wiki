# Grain size: lesson — inquiry activity (API) series mapped one-to-one onto content chapters

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The book's structural design is a two-part pairing: Part I presents conceptual content chapters, and Part II presents "Aprendizaje por Indagación (API)" — inquiry-based learning activities that mirror the chapters. Each API takes a chapter topic as its object of student investigation: "API 1: La Tierra y el Sol", "API 2: Escalas en el sistema solar", "API 3: Explorando el diagrama HR", "API 4: Exoplanetas", "API 5: La Vía Láctea", "API 6: Las galaxias más lejanas". This gives teachers ready-made inquiry lessons anchored to the reading.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each inquiry activity requires the corresponding Part I content chapter as its conceptual background
#### Constraints
- The activities are written for the astronomy topics of this book; teachers must adapt them to their own curriculum ordering

### Target Learners
- school students in teacher-led classrooms

### Learning Goals
- scientific inquiry skills in astronomy
- understanding Earth-Sun-Moon dynamics
- solar-system scales
- the HR diagram
- exoplanets
