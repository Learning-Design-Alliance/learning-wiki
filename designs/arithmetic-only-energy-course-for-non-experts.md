# Design energy-literacy courses for non-experts requiring no mathematics beyond simple arithmetic and no prior physics or chemistry knowledge

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article frames an entire course around preparing non-expert citizens to take part in technological and energy-resource decisions. It states that "This course is oriented toward providing an educational base for participating in these decisions" and deliberately sets the entry bar low: "there is no need for any mathematics beyond simple arithmetic, nor for any previous knowledge of physics or chemistry." The principle is that conceptual energy literacy for civic participation can be built without mathematical prerequisites.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learners need no prior knowledge of physics or chemistry; the course content must be expressible with simple arithmetic only
#### Constraints
- The stated scope is preparing non-experts for participation in decisions about energy-resource development, not professional physics training

### Target Learners
- non-expert adult members of the general public

### Learning Goals
- energy literacy sufficient to participate in societal decisions about energy-resource development
