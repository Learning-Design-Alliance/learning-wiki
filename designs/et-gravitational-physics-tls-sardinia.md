# Teaching-learning sequence on gravitational physics and the Einstein Telescope for upper secondary students

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A TLS of four three-hour meetings (January–February 2024) for 119 students (68 male, 51 female) in 3rd–5th year classes across four Sardinian schools, part of the Italian PNRR initiative 'Le frontiere della fisica'. Sessions covered motion of bodies (Aristotelic, Newtonian, Einstein's gravity), the universe (geocentrism to relativistic astrophysics and the Einstein Telescope), and formative evaluation, using experiments, videos, embodied activities, debates, and Q&A. The authors state it is "aimed to contextualize gravitational physics along the lines of the Einstein Telescope educational program in Sardinia".
