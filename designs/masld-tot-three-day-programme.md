# Three-day MASLD Training-of-Trainers programme under the Healthy Liver Healthy India initiative

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A three-day Training of Trainers session consisting of six modules, with approximately 5–6 hours of structured instruction per day, delivered to medical officers from all 33 districts of Chhattisgarh. The article states each day incorporated "lectures, interactive case discussions, and practical demonstrations of MASLD diagnostic tools (e.g., FIB-4, waist circumference, and CAP values)". Faculty were hepatologists, public health specialists, and state NCD program managers with over a decade of experience. Pre-assessments occurred immediately before Day 1 and post-assessments at the end of Day 3.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered by experienced faculty (hepatologists, public health specialists, state NCD program managers with over a decade of experience)
#### Constraints
- Knowledge assessed only immediately pre- and post-training, without longitudinal follow-up

### Target Learners
- Medical officers posted at primary, secondary and tertiary health centres in Chhattisgarh, India

### Learning Goals
- Early identification and referral of MASLD in accordance with operational guidelines under India's NP-NCD programme, including use of FIB-4, waist circumference, LSM and CAP tools
