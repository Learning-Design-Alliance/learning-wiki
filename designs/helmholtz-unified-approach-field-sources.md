# Unified Helmholtz-theorem approach to teaching the sources of static electromagnetic fields

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends organizing instruction about the sources of E, D, B and H around the Helmholtz theorem, which reconstructs any sufficiently regular vector field from integrals over all space of its divergence and curl. Applying Maxwell's static equations as the theorem's inputs yields exact general integral formulas (15), (20), (25), (26) that make each field's sources explicit. As the abstract states, a "Unified approach to the study of electromagnetic static field sources based on the Helmholtz theorem is recommended", replacing the traditional inconsistent treatment of the auxiliary vectors D and H.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- charges and currents located in a bounded region of space
- the vector field must be regular at infinity, decaying faster than ~1/r, and differentiable
#### Constraints
- the article treats only the static case, with zero time derivatives of B and E

### Target Learners
- university physics students in the general physics electromagnetism course

### Learning Goals
- correctly identify the sources of electrostatic and magnetostatic fields
- state the applicability limits of textbook formulas involving D and H

### Claims

- H Field Sourced By Conduction Currents And Fictitious Magnetic Charges [+M]
