# Five-component blended teaching-practicing module for SRP instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article structures its blended sim-lab module into five sequential parts: pre-class preparation and quiz, theoretical lecture, teacher's demonstration, guided students' operation, and post-class support. Demonstrations use dual-view filming (long-range and close-range), and an endoscopic demonstration shows "the placement of the fulcrum during operation, the way the instrument entered, and the movement form of the instrument under the gingiva." Guided practice occurs on standardized typodonts with instructor supervision, followed by post-class on-site preceptorship and synchronous tele-mentoring.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Identical syllabus and standardized curriculum across sites
- Instructors with pedagogical training, collaborative lesson planning and procedural calibration
- An instructor-to-student ratio of 1:7.5 (2:15 per group) during practical instruction
#### Constraints
- Group allocation was by institutional affiliation since random allocation of individual students was not feasible

### Target Learners
- fourth-year undergraduate dental students

### Learning Goals
- subgingival scaling and root planing knowledge and operative skill
