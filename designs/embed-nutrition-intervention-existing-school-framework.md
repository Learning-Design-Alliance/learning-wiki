# Embed experiential nutrition interventions within schools' existing health education framework to ensure feasibility without additional teacher burden

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The protocol deliberately situates the experiential intervention inside the school's existing health education structures rather than adding a parallel program. The authors state: "Importantly, the intervention is embedded within schools' existing health education framework, ensuring feasibility without imposing additional burdens on teachers." This supports sustainability and scalability of school-based experiential nutrition education, and the three-month period was chosen to fit academic semester schedules.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Standardized materials (slides, lesson plans, videos, activity tools, specified time frames) and teacher training are provided so regular teachers can deliver the activities.
#### Constraints
- The article notes that longer interventions of six months or more are more effective for sustained dietary behaviors, and future programs should be extended over a longer period such as one academic year.

### Target Learners
- Primary school students in nutrition and health education programs

### Learning Goals
- Nutrition literacy and healthy eating habits integrated with regular schooling
