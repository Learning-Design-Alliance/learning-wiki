# Low-cost magnet-spring nonlinear oscillator apparatus with Tracker video analysis

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A simple experimental device: a cylindrical weight (mass 213.7 g) hanging from a spring, carrying five small neodymium cylinder magnets on a ferromagnetic disk, above a second disk holding two bar magnets. Motion is recorded with a tripod-mounted video camera and analyzed with the Tracker software using its autotracker function on a three-circle marker, with a ruler as length reference and precautions against parallax, lens distortion and motion blur. The article presents it as an oscillator system "de fácil construcción" for basic physics courses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A video camera on a tripod and the Tracker software with the autotracker function
- A ruler in the same plane as the weight's trajectory, parallel to the camera objective, with adequate lighting and minimal exposure time
#### Constraints
- Acceleration determined by video analysis carries large uncertainty, preventing close comparison with simulated values

### Target Learners
- first-year university experimental physics students

### Learning Goals
- characterizing nonlinear oscillatory motion
- relating force models to measured motion
