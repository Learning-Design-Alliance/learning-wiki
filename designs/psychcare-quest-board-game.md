# PsychCare Quest: a purpose-designed 72-square spiral board game for psychiatric nursing internships

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
PsychCare Quest is a newly developed, non-commercial board game created for this study with three psychiatric nursing education experts. It uses "a 72-square spiral game board with four critical obstacles positioned at squares 18, 36, 54, and 72", a die labeled Easy/Medium/Hard, 150 multiple-choice questions across three difficulty sets, and 20 scenario cards with 80 open-ended questions. Students play in groups of four, advancing six spaces for correct answers and facing scenario questions at obstacles, with up to three guided hints permitted per session.
