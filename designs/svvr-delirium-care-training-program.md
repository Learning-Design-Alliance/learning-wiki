# SVVR delirium care training program: four interactive 360° modules grounded in Bandura's self-efficacy theory

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The program comprises four modules covering delirium assessment, safety measure analysis, risk factor identification and management, and interprofessional holistic care, delivered as a single 15-minute session via a head-mounted display with 10 embedded interactive questions answered by spoken response. Each module "included components of Bandura's self-efficacy theory, including vicarious learning, simulated mastery experiences, and verbal encouragement". Content validity from a five-member expert panel yielded a scale-level content validity index of 0.92, and the program was pilot-tested with 10 nurses before the trial.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered individually in a private simulation room following a standardized protocol, with fast-forwarding and skipping disabled
- Mandarin Chinese speech recognition technology to capture spoken responses to embedded questions
#### Constraints
- Image resolution, speech recognition capture, and equipment adjustments (near-sightedness, astigmatism, presbyopia) were reported by participants as areas needing improvement

### Target Learners
- Registered nurses caring for acutely ill patients at risk of delirium
- Newly graduated nurses

### Learning Goals
- Delirium assessment
- Safety measures and risk factor management
- Interprofessional collaboration in delirium care
