# Four-year longitudinal capstone structure with progressive deliverables

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The capstone is structured across four years of medical school with defined deliverables each year. During the first year, each student identifies a particular global health issue, performs a literature review, writes a brief paper, and delivers a short oral presentation. In the second year, each student identifies a specific project, defines his/her role, establishes methods and a timeline, and prepares and presents a scientific poster. In the third and fourth years, students focus on capstone project implementation and evaluation, culminating in oral presentations summarizing their work.
