# Fully online virtual bedside teaching course for pharmacy students (Universität München)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Developed during the SARS-CoV-2 pandemic for the clinical pharmacy seminar, this exclusively online course on a learning platform comprises three phases: repetition of communication, drug information and pharmacology content using an interactive web-based case collection; small-group medication analyses for two fictional patients; and access to a virtual ward built from screenshots of fictional patient records, where students develop a pharmaceutical care plan and answer a physician's medication query. "Die Bewertung dieser Arzneimittelinformation erfolgte anhand eines kollaborativen Peer-Assessments." Badges with varying difficulty served as motivational incentives.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A learning platform hosting the course, an interactive web-based case collection, and fictional patient documents (excerpts from patient files, physician letters, laboratory values) realized via screenshots of clinical documentation software
#### Constraints
- The article notes the course structure could be a good suggestion especially for sites that have no possibility of direct patient contact

### Target Learners
- pharmacy students in the clinical pharmacy seminar (final term)

### Learning Goals
- medication history taking, medication analysis, pharmaceutical care planning, drug information provision
