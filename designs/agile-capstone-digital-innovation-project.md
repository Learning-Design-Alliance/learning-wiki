# Capstone project with agile methodology funnelling all course tasks into one digital innovation artefact

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An instructional element added after iteration 1 in which "a new principle was added to allow all tasks to be funnelled into a comprehensive capstone project that applies agile methods". Students work in teams with industry partners to design and implement a working digital innovation such as a mobile application or website, using open-source software. "The application of Agile allows changes to group formation, regular feedback and the assessment of the overall process and not merely the end solution."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clear team roles and regular feedback opportunities
- Assessments measuring outcomes per week so teams can make improvements
#### Constraints
- Interpersonal issues in teams and requirement limitations with industry partners could not be pre-empted

### Target Learners
- Final-year and postgraduate Information Systems students

### Learning Goals
- Implementing a working digital innovation in a business
- Collaboration and project management

### Claims
- [In iteration 3, when student teams partnered with start-up entrepreneurs, industry partners rated student conduct highest of the three iterations on 7 of 10 items, but innovation usefulness highest on only 3 of 10](../claims/industry-feedback-improved-iteration3.md) [+M]
