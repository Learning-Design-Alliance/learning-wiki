# Composite daily math lesson structure with six engagement-oriented components

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The observed middle school math lessons shared a common structure of six components: a warm-up, direct instruction, group activity, share-out, note-taking, and demonstration of understanding. Each teacher integrated at least five of these six components, whether the class period was 45 or 90 minutes. The structure deliberately builds in social interaction, with "Random assignment to groups; not ability-based grouping" at standing whiteboard stations, and low-stakes warm-ups and checks "with no grade."
