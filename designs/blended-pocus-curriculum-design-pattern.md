# Blended learning with case-based scenarios and a structured mnemonic to compensate for lower prior knowledge in time-constrained professional training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The curriculum's design pattern combines a digital preparation phase (live webinar plus e-learning with short videos and quizzes), an on-site hands-on phase focused on practical training, and a digital follow-up phase. To "compensate for paramedics' lower level of prior knowledge and ultrasound experience, the duration of the curriculum in our study was extended in comparison with existing curricula", while blended learning kept on-site sessions "to an acceptable minimum", permitting greater focus on practical training. Case-based learning and the iPOCUS mnemonic provided what participants evaluated as a familiar learning atmosphere.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sufficient e-learning infrastructure (specially designed program with videos and quizzes)
- Extended overall duration relative to physician-oriented curricula to offset lower prior knowledge
#### Constraints
- The article notes extremely brief curricula likely contributed to failure to achieve primary endpoints in some prior studies, so the pattern depends on adequate duration

### Target Learners
- paramedics and other professional learners with limited prior ultrasound experience

### Learning Goals
- point-of-care ultrasound knowledge and practical examination skills
