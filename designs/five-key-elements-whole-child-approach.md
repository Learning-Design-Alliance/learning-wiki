# Five key elements of a whole child approach

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide enumerates five key elements that constitute a whole child approach: "positive developmental relationships; environments filled with safety and belonging; rich learning experiences and knowledge development; development of skills, habits, and mindsets; and integrated support systems." The fifth element, integrated support systems, is the guide's central focus, operationalized as integrated, school-based systems of care built through cross-sector collaboration. The list is grounded in the science of learning and development literature the guide cites.
