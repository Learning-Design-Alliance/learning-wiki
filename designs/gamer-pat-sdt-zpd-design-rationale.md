# Design rationale mapping serious-game mechanics to Self-Determination Theory, ZPD scaffolding, and cognitive apprenticeship

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
GAMER PAT's mechanics are mapped (Table 2) onto three pedagogical foundations: SDT's needs for "autonomy, competence, and relatedness"; Vygotsky's ZPD with scaffolding that enables learners to progress until they can act autonomously; and cognitive apprenticeship's making of tacit expert reasoning visible. Reviewer missions serve competence and guiding progression, co-author dialogue serves relatedness and elaborating/rephrasing, the ten questions serve competence and structuring, player-led goal setting serves autonomy with fading control, and narrative framing supports meaning-making and persistence.
