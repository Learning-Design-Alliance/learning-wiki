# Intensive program design combining concentrated instruction with co-curricular and engagement activities

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The program paired high-intensity language instruction (120 hours of study per month) with co-curricular activities including visits, competitions, sports, motivational talks at universities, weekend workshops by post-graduate students, and a Readerthon combining extensive and intensive reading. The authors state that visits to interesting sites "would provide an escape from the intensive exposure of the students to the materials and help them avoid monotony and boredom" and that workshops and the Readerthon "would add variety and foster learner engagement".
