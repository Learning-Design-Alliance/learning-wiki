# Ten-session NLP treatment program for ESP reading

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The treatment consisted of ten sessions, each presenting one NLP topic: introduction and presuppositions, goal setting with guided fantasy, sensory language across two sessions, metaphor, emotion, belief (flexibility), "There is no Failure; only Feed back", finding the best strategy, and review. Each technique presentation was followed by reading a medical text and answering related questions, with learners applying their diagnosed sensory style. The control group instead received traditional skimming and scanning instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Ten sessions with one NLP topic each
- Medical texts from 'Reading Science and Medicine in English' (Kamyab et al., 2016) followed by comprehension questions
#### Constraints
- Implemented with two intact classes of 60 Iranian undergraduate medical-science students

### Target Learners
- Iranian undergraduate EFL learners in medical sciences

### Learning Goals
- ESP reading comprehension
