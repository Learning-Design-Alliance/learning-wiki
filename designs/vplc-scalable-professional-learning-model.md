# Virtual professional learning community (vPLC): eight 75-minute synchronous sessions with asynchronous Slack activities, facilitated by former teachers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The vPLC is a scalable professional learning model designed to support effective ASSISTments implementation, particularly in rural and underresourced settings. "Teachers participated in eight 75-minute virtual sessions, each preceded by thought-provoking asynchronous activities delivered via Slack", facilitated by two former middle school teachers using a facilitation guide. Sessions follow a consistent structure including icebreakers, a Story From the Field, breakout discussions in small groups, a Teachers' Lounge segment, and digital sticky-note reflections.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Treatment teachers were expected to attend at least seven of eight synchronous vPLC sessions held between September and February and to complete at least four of eight asynchronous activities on Slack.
#### Constraints
- The vPLC was designed for rural educators who often lack access to local PLCs or peer networks; an optional ninth and tenth session were provided.

### Target Learners
- Rural middle school math teachers in grades 6-8

### Learning Goals
- Effective use of ASSISTments, formative assessment practice, and data-driven instructional decision-making
