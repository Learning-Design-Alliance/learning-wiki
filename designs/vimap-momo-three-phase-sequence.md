# ViMAP-MoMo three-phase learning activity sequence: aesthetics, real-world modeling, graphing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
ViMAP-MoMo organizes kinematics learning into a sequence in which "The sequence of activities consists of three phases and each phase consists of multiple activities." Phase 1 (Physics of Aesthetics) builds programming fluency through drawing constant-speed and constant-acceleration shapes; Phase 2 has students model real-world motion of balls on ramps with iterative validation; Phase 3 introduces graphing of motion and reverse-engineering of speed-time graphs. Each phase's rationale ties aesthetic shape generation to domain-specific kinematics goals.

#### Requirements

### Target Learners

### Learning Goals
- modeling motion as continuous change
- scientific modeling practices of refinement and validation
- graphing speed and distance vs. time
