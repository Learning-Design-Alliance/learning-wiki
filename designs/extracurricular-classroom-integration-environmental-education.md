# Integrate extracurricular activities with classroom foreign-language study to strengthen environmental education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The sixth pedagogical condition holds that integrating appointed and extracurricular activities creates the possibility of effective environmental education through the foreign language. The article reasons that "Extracurricular work is the continuation of academic studies and allows improving the knowledge and skills in a foreign language that pupils acquire in the classroom". In the experiment this was realized in a fan club of English and ecology, debates, and participation in regional environment-oriented school activities.
