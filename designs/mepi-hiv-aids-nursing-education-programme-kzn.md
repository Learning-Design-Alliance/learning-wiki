# HIV and AIDS education intervention programme (Medical Education Partnership Initiative) for undergraduate nursing students at a selected KwaZulu-Natal university

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size

## Description
An innovative HIV and AIDS clinical education programme embedded in the undergraduate Bachelor of Nursing curriculum at a selected KwaZulu-Natal university, whose broader objective was to 'improve and strengthen the HIV and AIDS content in the existing curriculum'. Sessions lasted on average 45–60 minutes and the programme was first introduced amongst second-year students within the community nursing science course. It was delivered by a professional nurse with a postgraduate diploma in clinical management of HIV and AIDS, and was evaluated 3 years after implementation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Inputs the article lists as needed before implementation: existing curriculum content, infrastructure, stakeholders, project goals, a budget managed by the principal investigators, and tutor training guidelines.
#### Constraints
- Only students in their second to fourth years of study were allocated to the programme; first-year students were excluded because of high attrition rates and insufficient exposure.

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate Bachelor of Nursing students (second to fourth year) at a South African university

### Learning Goals
- HIV and AIDS knowledge and clinical management competencies, including prevention, transmission, diagnosis, staging, counselling and antiretroviral treatment
