# PROBE (Practical Obstetric team-training): a mandatory, recurring simulation-based shoulder dystocia team-training program

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
PROBE is the simulation-based team-training program introduced in 2008 at the Linköping delivery ward, with objectives "to improve obstetric emergency skills and develop interprofessional teamwork, thus promoting better patient outcome". Participation is mandatory, sessions run six times yearly at an interval of 1.5 years during working hours, each 3-hour session includes two scenarios plus a skills station, and a 40-minute shoulder dystocia scenario has been part of every event. Teams of one or two midwives, a doctor and a nurse assistant work scenarios led by ALSO-trained certified instructors, preceded by individual theoretical study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mandatory, scheduled participation during working hours for all delivery-ward obstetricians, midwives and nurse assistants
- A simulation center with an obstetric skills laboratory and certified experienced instructors
#### Constraints
- The HELPERR management mnemonic and clinical guidelines remained unchanged after PROBE introduction, so outcome changes are attributed to training rather than protocol change

### Target Learners
- Delivery-unit obstetricians, midwives and nurse assistants

### Learning Goals
- Recognition and standardized management of shoulder dystocia and obstetric emergencies
- Interprofessional teamwork and communication
