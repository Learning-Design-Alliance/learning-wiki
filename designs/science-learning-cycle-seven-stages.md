# Science Learning Cycle (SLC) model with seven stages of investigation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
SLC is a learning approach that "use science literature including reflection journals, class notes, the inquiry data, and other artifacts of learning that reflect the stages of a scientific investigation", represented through discussion between group roles. It consists of three interconnected components — organizing the science record, role on literacy cycles, and student-made artifacts — and proceeds through seven stages: questions, prediction, planning, observation, conclusions, new questions or ideas, and conclusion journal. The article applies it in grade-11 physics on Direct Current Electricity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Group roles (such as great idea developer and discussion leader) must be assigned so students document predictions and lead discussion
- Learning instruments including syllabus, lesson plan, and student worksheet aligned to national competency standards
#### Constraints
- The article notes responsibilities of each role should be adjusted to the ability development of each student, since not all students have the same ability

### Target Learners
- secondary vocational school students, grade 11

### Learning Goals
- conceptual understanding of physics (Direct Current Electricity)
- scientific literacy
- independent learning skills
