# Methodical system of teaching physics as a five-component system (objectives, content, methods, tools, organizational forms)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article defines the methodical system of teaching physics at the university as an integrated set of components. It states that "The components of the methodical system of teaching physics at the university are learning objectives, content of physical education, methods, tools and organizational forms of education." The system prepares future physics teachers on a scientific-theoretical basis, including training material, exercises, laboratory work and demonstrations, and is meant to create motivation for independent knowledge acquisition. In the article it functions as the organizing framework for teacher preparation under informatization.
