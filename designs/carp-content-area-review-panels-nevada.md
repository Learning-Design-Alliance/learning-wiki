# Content Area Review Panels (CARPs)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
CARPs are the Nevada panels that "Evaluate and rate instructional materials submitted by publishers." Per Table 1, they include subject-matter experts, content-area and special education teachers, curriculum directors, technology specialists, and English language development experts. Panelists correlate content standards to materials, participate in training and rubric calibration, conduct independent reviews, and take part in full-group deliberations to reach a unified recommendation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Panelists must know and understand the NVACS, participate in training and rubric calibration, and submit ratings and summary reports on time to the panel facilitator
#### Constraints
- Panels are recruited at 5–7 panelists per content area, with RPDP representation secured for English language arts, mathematics, and science

### Target Learners
- K-12 students who will use the adopted instructional materials

### Learning Goals
- Reliable, calibrated evaluation of instructional materials for statewide adoption
