## Description
The review describes the program of psychological examining in the United States Army during the recent war, conducted under the Surgeon General's Office. As printed, "Hundreds of psychologists were engaged in the service and nearly two million men were examined." The reviewed volume, edited by R. M. Yerkes in the Memoirs of the National Academy of Sciences (Vol. XV, 1921, 890 pp.), is divided into three parts under Yerkes, Terman and Boring, with Part I covering the history and organization of the service.
