# Three-module progressive course structure from foundational theory to frontier engagement to practical integration

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The reformed course was organized as a progressive sequence of three interconnected modules that build from knowledge acquisition to higher-order skills. As the article states, "The course was structured in three interconnected modules: (1) foundational theory aligned with students' chosen research questions, (2) engagement with frontier developments in immunology, and (3) practical integration of knowledge through presentations and written assignments." Each module was intentionally sequenced to move students from basic knowledge toward critical analysis, problem-solving and innovative thinking, with content flexibly adapted to students' individual research topics rather than confined to textbook content.
