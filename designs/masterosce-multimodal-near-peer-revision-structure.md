# MasterOSCE program structure: multi-modal near-peer revision combining lectures, small-group seminars, online notes and a course booklet

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The MasterOSCE program was a two-day revision course for final-year medical students, delivered exclusively by recent medical graduates six weeks before the final OSCE. "Several modalities of teaching were incorporated in the program including lectures, small group seminars, videos, interactive modules and revision notes (made available online and in a course booklet)." Broad core topics were taught in lectures while concern areas such as ethics and dermatology were reserved for small-group seminars, plus an 'Ask the panel' session on OSCE technique.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Near-peer tutors recruited with an expressed passion to teach and motivation to develop teaching skills
- Content developed by each delivering tutor and peer reviewed
#### Constraints
- Enrolment limited to 125 students in five seminars of 25, on a first-come-first-served basis

### Target Learners
- final-year medical students

### Learning Goals
- preparation for the final objective structured clinical examination (OSCE)
