# Tohoku University compulsory introductory science experiment course for freshmen with 12 themes including science-and-culture

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In 2004 Tohoku University created a new introductory science experimental course for freshmen that is a compulsory subject for students in all natural science fields. The course is explicitly liberal rather than professional education, in which students are trained to become familiar with nature and to discover natural laws for themselves. It comprises 12 themes, one of which is the science-and-culture string-instrument theme described in this article.
