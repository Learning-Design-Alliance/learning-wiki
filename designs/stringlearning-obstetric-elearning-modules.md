# Two e-learning modules on postpartum haemorrhage and preeclampsia built on the StringLearning platform

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The authors created two e-learning modules covering all learning objectives of the obstetric anaesthesia course curriculum in PPH and PE, using the platform StringLearning ApS. The modules "consisted of obstetric cases, segments of conference videos and online lectures, pictures and summary tables with voice‐over explanation", with MCQs presented during learning to stimulate reflective thinking. Expert panels approved academic content and usability was tested on obstetric anaesthetists and residents before the trial.
