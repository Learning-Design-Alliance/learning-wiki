# Embed science-of-learning principles (meaningful contexts, gestures, concreteness fading, integrated visual-verbal models, interleaved retrieval practice, process-oriented feedback) in fraction intervention design

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The FSI explicitly employs principles grounded in research in the science of learning to maximize effectiveness and encourage retention of fraction concepts. Lessons anchor instruction in a meaningful color-run race narrative, use animated gestures with an arrow sweeping from 0 to the fraction, apply concreteness fading from physical fraction bars to drawings to mental models, place text next to visualizations to decrease memory load, use side-by-side comparisons of worked examples, spiral concepts with increasingly larger denominators for distributed and interleaved practice, and provide corrective and process-oriented feedback.

### Target Learners
- sixth-grade students with mathematics difficulties

### Learning Goals
- fraction sense-making rather than procedure memorization
- retention and application of fraction concepts
