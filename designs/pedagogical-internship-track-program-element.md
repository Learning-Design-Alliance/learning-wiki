# The pedagogical internship track: a two-month near-peer teaching curriculum within Swedish medical internship.

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
A voluntary faculty development program launched in 2018 at a regional non-university hospital in Western Sweden, designed as a near-peer teaching program for junior doctors. It includes formal coursework in pedagogical skills and feedback, clinical teacher training at the University of Gothenburg, and simulation training at the hospital's simulation center. The article states: "In total the curriculum is two months, spread over the 1.5-year-long internship." Participants supervise peers and medical students in clinical settings and simulations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Managed and financed by head program directors for the hospital group
- Candidates with documented interest in and merit for teaching
#### Constraints
- Originally four candidates per year (2018–2022), expanded to eight per year from 2022 to meet demand for supervisors and simulation facilitators

### Target Learners
- Junior doctors during the mandatory Swedish internship (AT)

### Learning Goals
- Clinical supervision of medical students and peers
- Simulation design, facilitation, and debriefing
- Feedback competencies
