# Community-based participatory research framework for community college DBER collaborations

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors structure their community college/4-year institution collaboration on the community-based participatory research framework, which "aspires to collaborations that build on the strengths and resources of each partner; facilitates equity in expectations, roles, and benefits; and involves all partners in dissemination of results." In practice, the community college partner defined the question and intervention, designed assessments, taught all terms, and handled IRB and data organization, while university partners advised on design, obtained funding, and performed analysis; all three contributed to the manuscript.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Complementary strengths on both sides
- Summer meeting time and modest salary support for the community college partner
- University partners to supply grant funds, statistical expertise, and journal access
#### Constraints
- Community college research faces time constraints, limited infrastructure, and lack of grant and statistical support; multi-year data collection is needed given small class sizes

### Target Learners
- Community college students in introductory STEM courses

### Learning Goals
- Generating discipline-based education research evidence from community college settings
