# Five key computational thinking competencies organizing K-12 content

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The pathway conceptualizes content at every grade level as relating to one of five competencies: "Abstraction, Data Collection and Analysis, Algorithmic Thinking, Building Models & Simulations, and Decomposition." This gives the district a common competency spine for vertically and horizontally aligning CT across grades, from Pre-K through high school, and a frame students can use to articulate how CT integrates into classroom practice.
