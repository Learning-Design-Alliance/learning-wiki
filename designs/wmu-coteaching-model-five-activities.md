# WMU co-teaching model: five activities mapped onto cognitive apprenticeship

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A professional development model in which an experienced research-based instructor and a new faculty member share one course: they alternate being in charge weekly, the experienced instructor sets up the course structure in advance, the new instructor reuses prior materials, then teaches alone the next semester. The article reports 'There were five basic co-teaching activities,' mapped onto the cognitive apprenticeship framework.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An instructor experienced in teaching the target course in a research-consistent manner must be available to set up and model the course structure.
#### Constraints
- The article reports a single case and notes the personalities of co-teaching participants likely must be somewhat compatible.

### Target Learners
- new tenure-track science faculty

### Learning Goals
- implementing research-based instructional practices
