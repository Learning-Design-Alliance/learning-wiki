# Hybrid small-subgroup simulation format adapted to pandemic restrictions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
During COVID-19 restrictions the course ran in a hybrid online-lecture plus stationary model, dividing participants into smaller teams: "there were maximum 4 people per room and 1 instructor in theoretical part and debriefing and 2 instructors for every 2 participants in ECMO technical skills and 1 for 4 during simulations", with simultaneous simulations and debriefings in three 4-person subgroups supported by audio-visual solutions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Innovative audio-visual solutions enabling simultaneous simulations and debriefings in three 4-person subgroups
- Instructor staffing of 16 staff people for a 12-participant course
#### Constraints
- Applied specifically to adapt to COVID-19 pandemic restrictions and epidemiological limits on contact training

### Target Learners
- postgraduate physicians training in extracorporeal life support

### Learning Goals
- ECMO deployment and management skills
- team leadership in ECMO emergencies

### Claims

- [Free comments from participants indicated the 1-hour practice session was perceived as too short for hands-on practice and that face-to-face training was valued during the pandemic](../claims/postcourse-comments-course-too-short-face-to-face-valued.md) [~W]
