# 7-month modified physical education exercise intervention programme

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A structured physical education intervention for 14–15-year-old adolescents in which the experimental group "participated in modified physical education lessons two times a week" and "Once a month they received a theory class where they were taught about communication disorders of adolescents and ways of preventing them by means of physical activities." Practical classes included sports and games (basketball, volleyball and football) as well as Pilates. The programme aimed to strengthen psychosocial adaptation-related behaviours during physical education lessons, and was evaluated against unmodified PE lessons with the Rogers and Dymond questionnaire and the RCMAS.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered within twice-weekly physical education lessons by the same teacher for both groups
- Includes a monthly theory class on adolescent communication disorders and their prevention through physical activities
- Combines team sports and games with Pilates to enhance physical abilities
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only in 14–15-year-old non-physically active adolescents in one randomly selected Lithuanian secondary school
- The control group received no treatment beyond unmodified physical education lessons

### Target Learners
- adolescents aged 14–15 in secondary school physical education

### Learning Goals
- psychosocial adjustment (self-esteem, evaluation by others, emotional comfort, internality, dominance)
- reduction of somatic, personality and social anxiety
