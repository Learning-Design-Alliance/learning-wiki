# CALI unit structure: Connect, Clarify, Research, and Apply lessons cycling through disciplinary-specific practices over 3-week units.

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each CALI unit follows a four-phase lesson framework delivered over three weeks, four days per week. Connect lessons link content to students' lives; Clarify lessons focus on reading secondary sources; Research lessons teach primary sources (social studies) or experiments (science); Apply lessons focus on making connections through projects and writing. An initial fifth Appraise phase was dropped and incorporated into Apply. The framework builds on a prior 5-E learning cycle unit and was adapted across grades and disciplines.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Semi-scripted lesson plans
- Leveled readers, graphic organizers, and scientist notebooks
- Flexible learning groups of no more than 5 students
#### Constraints
- Unit length settled at 3 weeks after finding 2 weeks too short and 4 weeks interrupted by school calendars

### Target Learners
- Kindergarten through fourth grade students

### Learning Goals
- Content knowledge in science and social studies
- Reading and learning from expository text
- Disciplinary practices (primary sources, experiments)
