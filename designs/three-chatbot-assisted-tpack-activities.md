# Three structured chatbot-assisted activities in the capstone course Innovative Tools in Physics Education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study designed "Three structured educational activities" embedded in an upper-division capstone course titled "Innovative Tools in Physics Education" for pre-service physics teachers. Activity 1 has students deconstruct a complex physics concept into a high-school-level explanation using a chatbot, evaluate and revise the output, and reflect in 250-300 words. Activity 2 requires a Novak-style concept map with 15-20 key concepts integrating vector diagrams, equations, and LaTeX notation with documented AI contributions. Activity 3 asks students to design a 2-session lesson plan incorporating student-facing chatbot use and a preliminary SWOT analysis.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each activity requires critical evaluation and documentation of AI contributions rather than uncritical adoption
#### Constraints
- Activities were situated in one upper-division elective capstone course at a single university

### Target Learners
- pre-service physics teachers

### Learning Goals
- pedagogical translation of complex concepts
- symbolic accuracy in physics representation
- AI-supported instructional design
