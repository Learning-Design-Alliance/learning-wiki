# ChatGPT-assisted PBL implementation pattern: AI-generated cases and questions before class, classroom discussion, bedside skills practice, and instructor review of AI content

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
This pattern structures a PBL unit around ChatGPT 4.0 as a learning aid. Students are first trained on and tested in ChatGPT use, then use it pre-class to explore AI-generated renal tumor cases and open-ended questions and to interview simulated patients; in class groups report on the AI-generated problems and practice history taking and physical examination at the bedside. Throughout, the "instructor critically reviewed the content provided by ChatGPT and provided instant, personalized feedback to ensure its accuracy."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must first be familiarized with ChatGPT and assessed on their mastery of it before class, and teaching objectives must be sent to students beforehand.
- Teachers must review the accuracy of information provided by ChatGPT during instruction.
#### Constraints
- The authors observed that the probability of errors occurring in AI was approximately 15%, some resolvable by adding qualifying words and others requiring teacher review.
- Students often fail to create effective prompts, which can lead to answers with biases.
- ChatGPT should be viewed as a supplement to teaching methods, rather than a substitute.

### Target Learners
- fifth-year medical interns in a urology rotation

### Learning Goals
- theoretical knowledge of renal tumor diagnosis and treatment
- history taking and physical examination skills
- clinical judgment and overall clinical competence
