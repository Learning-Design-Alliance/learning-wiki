# Weeklong intensive integrating didactic instruction, simulation, interprofessional collaboration, industry engagement, and an innovative capstone across six curricular themes

## Description
The UAB/SNMMI Nuclear Medicine Therapy Intensive is a weeklong, practice-focused advanced education program for board-certified NMTs and NMAAs that integrates "didactic instruction, simulation-based learning, interprofessional collaboration, and innovative capstone experiences". Its curriculum is organized across "6 major themes": foundations in RPT, radiation safety and compliance, patient-centered communication and eligibility, simulation and applied skills, clinical trials and research literacy, and an escape-room capstone. Program integration and assessment occur through pretests, posttests, team projects, and a challenge activity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Qualified, board-certified nuclear medicine professionals already in active practice
- Enrollment capped to preserve small-group interaction and personalized mentorship
- Access to therapy suites, dosimetry labs, and expert faculty for simulations and tours
#### Constraints
- Currently offered only annually in the spring
- Applications require a letter of recommendation, statement of purpose, video submission, and documented therapy experience

### Target Learners
- Practicing board-certified nuclear medicine technologists
- Nuclear medicine advanced associates

### Learning Goals
- Radiation safety and regulatory compliance in radiopharmaceutical therapy
- Patient eligibility assessment and therapeutic communication
- Radiopharmaceutical preparation, administration, dosimetry, and follow-up care
- Interprofessional collaboration and clinical trial literacy
