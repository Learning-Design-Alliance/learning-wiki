# Simultaneous multi-facility training with peer observation fosters a collaborative learning environment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Training multiple facilities simultaneously over videoconferencing lets trainees watch peers' procedures in real time. The article states "Peer observation fostered a collaborative learning environment and increased overall involvement in the training process, which likely enhanced the value of the remote laparoscopic surgery training", and suggests conducting training simultaneously across multiple facilities has benefits. Trainees listed observing other participants' procedures among the positive aspects.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An online videoconferencing system connecting all participating sites simultaneously
#### Constraints
- The time available to supervise each participant was limited when multiple facilities trained simultaneously

### Target Learners
- Surgical trainees in postgraduate years 1–5

### Learning Goals
- Laparoscopic psychomotor skills and engagement/motivation in skills training
