# 15-week MIT additive manufacturing course combining lectures, labs, and hands-on projects

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A semester-long course taught at MIT to graduate students and advanced undergraduates. The article states that "The course includes lectures, laboratory sessions, and hands-on projects." Lectures begin with in-depth technical analysis of major AM processes and later cover special topics, while labs use desktop AM machines. Learning objectives include learning fundamentals of AM of polymers, metals, ceramics and emerging materials, hands-on machine experience, design for AM, comparison with conventional manufacturing, emerging applications, and integration in a cumulative project proposing, prototyping, and evaluating a novel AM capability.
