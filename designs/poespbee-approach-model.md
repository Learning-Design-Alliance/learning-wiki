# POESPBEE: POE technique integrated with project-based learning at the beginning, during and after the PBL process

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
POESPBEE is a learning approach in which students develop a project to solve an environmental problem using the scientific research method, reaching generalizations through predictions, observations and explanations while sharing and discussing the project. The article states that "PBL method is supported by POE technique at the beginning, during and after the PBL process," distinguishing it from prior work that used POE only at the start. Students are liable for their own learning-comprehension in a doing-living model.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Environmental topics must be observable events capable of causing contradiction in the learner's mind during the observation stage
- Students must have preliminary knowledge about environmental issues, which the POE stage provides before project work begins
#### Constraints
- The article notes no prior study on POE-supported PBL except Güven (2011), whose procedure was not explained in detail

### Target Learners
- eighth-grade secondary school students

### Learning Goals
- positive attitudes toward the environment
- environmentally friendly behaviors
- environmental awareness and literacy
