# 4-hour interactive case-based EBM workshop covering question formulation, searching, appraisal, and application

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention was an interactive 4-hour EBM workshop co-conducted by a faculty member and a librarian in a computer lab. It emphasized four EBM steps and used case-based teaching with individual and group exercises covering PICO question formatting, database searching, validity appraisal, calculation of ARR, NNT, RRR, and likelihood ratios, and application of evidence to patient care decisions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A computer lab and co-facilitation by a faculty member and a librarian
- Access to multiple evidence databases for individual searching exercises
#### Constraints
- Delivered as a single 4-hour session in the PGY-2 year; the trial found no measurable effect on EBM test scores

### Target Learners
- PGY-2 internal medicine residents

### Learning Goals
- Formulating answerable clinical questions
- Searching and appraising evidence
- Applying evidence to patient care decisions
