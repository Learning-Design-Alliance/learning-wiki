# r-Dynamic coloring of graphs as a technique for tessellation decoration

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article defines r-dynamic coloring: "By an r-dynamic coloring of a graph G, we mean a proper k-coloring of graph G such that the neighbors of any vertex v receive at least min{r, d(v)} different colors." The r-dynamic chromatic number is the minimum k for which such a coloring exists. The technique is applied to block paving tessellation decoration via a five-step procedure including drawing a dual graph, and the article instructs: "Please make sure that there does not exist two adjacent block paving sharing the same colors."
