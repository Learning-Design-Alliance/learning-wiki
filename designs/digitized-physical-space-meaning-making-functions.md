# Three functions of (inter)actions between digitized and physical space in classroom meaning-making

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article identifies "three functions of (inter)actions between digitized and physical space" in the classroom meaning-making process: incoming information, in which the classroom receives the digitized performance; transferability, in which bodily interactions apply elements from the video in the physical space; and blended integration, in which meaning is constructed at the intersection of the two narrative spaces.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- a digitized performance video and a changeable physical classroom space between which (inter)actions can occur
#### Constraints
- the digitized video is inert: neither the teacher nor the students can directly interact with it, change, or alter its course

### Target Learners
- pre-service teachers in science education
- small seminar groups

### Learning Goals
- constructing scientific concepts through bodily meaning-making
- interpreting embodied physics explanations
