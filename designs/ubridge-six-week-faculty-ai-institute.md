# UBRIDGE Institute: a six-week synchronous interdisciplinary faculty development program for ethical AI integration

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The UBRIDGE (Universal design, Bloom's taxonomy, Reflection, Integration, Design thinking, Growth mindset, Ethics) Institute was a six-session synchronous Zoom program, two hours weekly, for interdisciplinary faculty across arts, sciences, education and health professions. It aimed to scaffold AI integration through "inclusive pedagogy, ethical reasoning, and course redesign principles". Sessions combined hands-on AI experimentation, design-thinking worksheets, discussion boards, and capstone AI-integration plans.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Voluntary participation with sustained six-session duration, active learning, coherence with participant needs, and collective participation, following Darling-Hammond et al. as the article cites
#### Constraints
- Self-selection bias: participants volunteered and received modest stipends, limiting generalizability to faculty who might resist such opportunities

### Target Learners
- Higher education faculty and staff across disciplines, from novice to experienced AI users

### Learning Goals
- Ethical AI integration in teaching
- Inclusive course design
- AI-informed pedagogical content knowledge
