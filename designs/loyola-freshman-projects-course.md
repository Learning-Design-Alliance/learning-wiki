# Loyola Freshman Projects course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Freshman Projects is a required one-credit semester course at Loyola University Chicago in which first-year physics majors design a project with a faculty member, carry out an experimental and/or theoretical analysis across the semester, and present their findings at the end of the term. Started in Spring 1996, it is described as a "project-based learning experience" that gives early research experience, scientific writing and communication practice, and broadened problem-solving skills; some projects are incorporated into larger research work culminating in publications.
