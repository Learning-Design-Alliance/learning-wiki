# Day and Bamford's ten principles defining a successful extensive reading program

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article presents Day and Bamford's (2002) ten principles that "define a successful ER program for L2 learners", covering easy and varied materials, learner choice, reading quantity, pleasure and general understanding as purposes, reading as its own reward, faster reading speed, individual silent reading, teacher orientation, and the teacher as a role model of a reader. Teachers are encouraged to use the principles "as a tool to examine their beliefs about reading". The study's treatment operationalised several of these principles.
