# Endodontic multimedia e-learning application with instructional videos on tooth models and patient cases

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A multimedia learning application produced by the Audiovisual Media Centre of RWTH Aachen University, available via password-protected internet access to test group students. It contained "6 educational videos" demonstrating root canal treatments on DRSK RCT tooth models plus three patient-case videos, shortened to a maximum of 10 minutes with jump tags for section selection and tutor-recorded commentary. Students rated it 4.3 ± 0.6 on a 5-point Likert scale and 81.4 on the SUS.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Internet access and password authorization; videos produced according to departmental clinical standards and American Association of Endodontists guidelines
#### Constraints
- Available only to participating students of the test groups; study design did not distinguish the application's effect from the effect of the tooth models

### Target Learners
- dental students in the initial clinical endodontic course

### Learning Goals
- root canal treatment procedure knowledge and practical skills
