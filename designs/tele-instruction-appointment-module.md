# Appointment module: instructor-defined calendar slots (as short as five minutes) that students book, with merging of students who have similar issues

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Appointment module lets the instructor specify available time slots, "as short as a five-minute slot," in a shared calendar; students book meetings and receive automatic invitations and reminders. Appointments can be merged when students have similar issues or concerns, enabling peer interaction and future peer discussions. The module is intended to provide learner-instructor and effective learner-learner interaction, and to give students feedback, the feeling of inclusion, and satisfaction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires the instructor to maintain a designed calendar of available slots for the upcoming week; merging can be offered by the system or decided by the instructor.
#### Constraints
- The article notes the feature must not ask a lot from the instructor; recorded contents and instructor responses are expected to reduce appointments over time.

### Target Learners
- online learners
- online course instructors

### Learning Goals
- learner-instructor interaction
- learner-learner interaction
- prompt resolution of misunderstandings
