## Description
The article presents LXD as a design framework extending beyond traditional instructional design, defined as "creating learning situations that extend beyond the formal classroom learning environment and often integrate online and virtual technological formats". It adopts Floor's (2018) five fundamentals: human-centered, goal-oriented, grounded in theories of learning, learning through practice, and heavily interdisciplinary. In this study the authors implemented the LXD framework to build a blended learning pedagogical approach integrating interactive video-based mathematics for elementary students in the classroom.
