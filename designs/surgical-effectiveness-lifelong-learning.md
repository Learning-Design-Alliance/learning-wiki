# Surgical effectiveness as a dynamic, lifelong learning process

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The article proposes that "surgical effectiveness is a dynamic process", in which continuous exposure to complex laparoscopic tasks in high-fidelity environments facilitates skill refinement and consolidation even after formal training ends. It challenges "the traditional assumption that once competence is achieved, additional technical training yields limited benefits", attributing observed gains to enhanced dexterity, economy of motion, decision-making speed, and increased readiness and confidence rather than mere procedural familiarity.
