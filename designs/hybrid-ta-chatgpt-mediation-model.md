# Hybrid TA-plus-ChatGPT mediation model for AI-supported instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 3 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative, 1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes a balanced model in which the TA remains an active, pedagogically informed mediator rather than being replaced by AI. As the authors put it, "the TA not only curates and validates AI-generated content but also integrates it into a structured learning process that actively supports and guides students." ChatGPT supports routine tasks while the TA handles critical thinking, individual needs, and complex instruction, reducing TA workload and offering a scalable solution for larger classes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured prompts guiding ChatGPT use
- TA verification of all AI-generated answers and exercises before students receive them
#### Constraints
- Demonstrated in one DSA course at a single university; the authors note the findings are relevant to other computer science disciplines but additional variables such as TA teaching style and student motivation may influence outcomes

### Target Learners
- undergraduate computer science students

### Learning Goals
- learning of data structures and algorithms
- exam readiness in complex CS courses
