# In-service professional support system for rural EFL teachers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
After training, a professional support system was constructed involving an expert group, English teaching specialists, education bureau directors, headmasters, core EFL teachers and ordinary EFL teachers, with core teachers connecting all actors. "A learning community of 5 to10 people was built up in central school districts" carrying out weekly face-to-face teaching-research activities, plus a virtual platform (http://www.mtevideo.cn) with regularly-updated resources and a forum. The system was intended to reduce frustration when teachers met difficulties in teaching practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Core EFL teachers to convene weekly learning-community activities
- Access to teaching resources such as workbooks, cards, CDs and course-wares, and an online platform for virtual communication
#### Constraints
- The website was closed four years later after fulfilling its responsibilities, and most rural schools have only one English teacher, limiting local oral-English practice opportunities

### Target Learners
- In-service rural primary EFL teachers

### Learning Goals
- Sustained professional development in English teaching
- Applying phonics in classroom practice
