# Traditional lecture and practice (LP) condition

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The lecture and practice condition is the traditional baseline against which productive failure and facilitated problem solving were compared in this three-condition study. Students taught by the same teacher experienced traditional lecture and practice instruction, and their post-test performance on well-structured and higher-order application problems, plus representation flexibility, was compared with the other two designs.
