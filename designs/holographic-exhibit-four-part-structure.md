# Four-part extension-activity structure: initial lecture, demonstration experiments, hologram exhibition with holoprojector, and closing lecture

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The Holographic Exhibit is organized as "4 partes esenciales": a 60-minute initial lecture with demonstrations of image formation, reflection, refraction and diffraction; 30 minutes of demonstration experiments with simple materials in three teams; a 20-minute demonstration of holoimages with a holoprojector and holographic screen plus an exhibition of white-light-reconstructible holograms; and a 20-minute closing lecture integrating the topics and distributing a DVD of physics experiments. The sequence moves from motivating presentation to hands-on participation to integration.
