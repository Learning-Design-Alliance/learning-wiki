# Model of communicatively developing technology of teachers' speech activation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article presents a model of communicatively developing technology of learning in which "the technology of communicatively developing type is assigned as the algorithm of reflexive activity of two subjects in educational process (trainer and trainee)". It rests on psychological and methodological principles such as successive formation of the communicative core, maintaining high-level difficulty, and pedagogic communication equality moving trainees from object to active subject of self-education. The model is built on activity, system-related, structural and person-centered approaches, with a controlling and a controlled cluster.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trainer acts as facilitator of interpersonal communication, role model, group leader and partner
- Positional places determining position and relation to the subject of activity must be distributed between trainees
#### Constraints
- Assigned mainly to teachers' personal self-realization and formation of individual verbal behavior

### Target Learners
- Teachers of Tatar schools attending advanced training (IAT) courses

### Learning Goals
- Professional literate Russian speech
- Communicative competence
- Oral activity activation
