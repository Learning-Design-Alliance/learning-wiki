# Tutorial step cycle of pre-text, interactive action, dynamically updated visualization, and post-text

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each of the tutorials is divided into steps connected by a series of mathematical equations deriving a concept, and each step follows a recurring cycle: "Each step starts with a pre-text that explains what they can expect to get out of the step." Students then perform an action, such as interacting with a visual plot or pressing a button, after which new data are interactively visualized so students see the impact of their actions, and a post-text usually explains the results. While a main narrative drives each tutorial, students are given freedom to explore uncommon cases on their own.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interactive plots that both visualize the concept and allow students to explore different aspects of it; each step is connected by a series of mathematical equations deriving a concept.
#### Constraints
- The pattern is described for web tutorials embedded in a course module on color science, built with modern visualization packages.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and graduate EECS students

### Learning Goals
- deriving color science concepts from first principles
- building intuitive geometric understanding of 3D color concepts
