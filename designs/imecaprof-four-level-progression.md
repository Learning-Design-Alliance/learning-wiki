# Four-level pedagogical progression from orientation visualization to dynamics in iMecaProf

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article defines levels in iMecaProf that "define a pedagogical progression": Level 0 shows a virtual smartphone mirroring the real one; Level 1 adds lab-frame and smartphone-frame unit vectors; Level 2 splits the screen in four parts showing projections of unit vectors; Level 3 enters dynamics with acceleration and velocity vectors in both frames plus real-time energy plots. Each level builds on the previous one.
