# Residential immersion with deliberately restricted internet and phone access to maximize peer interaction and learning pace

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
AstroCamp's design pattern is full residential immersion: students, teachers and camp monitors (former AstroCamp students now doing university degrees) live and work together for 14 days. "By choice the students have no internet access (and only have their mobile phones for very limited periods) to maximize their interactions with their peers and to enable them to focus on learning at a much faster pace than in their normal school classes."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- a secluded residential setting where students, teachers and monitors live together for the camp duration
#### Constraints
- the no-internet rule was adopted by the organizers' choice for this camp; the 2020 hybrid edition required online collaboration tools for remote students

### Target Learners
- pre-university students in the last 3 years of secondary education

### Learning Goals
- peer interaction across backgrounds
- focused learning at an accelerated pace
