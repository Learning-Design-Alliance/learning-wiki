# Personalised feedback as individualised, performance-specific information across cognitive, psychomotor and affective domains

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article defines personalised feedback in simulation as "individualised, performance‐specific information directed at a learner's cognitive, psychomotor and affective domains, with the aim of improving understanding and guiding targeted skill development". Unlike standardised group debriefing, it addresses each learner's unique errors, gaps and strengths, enabling more precise remediation and deeper clinical learning. The article uses this construct as its independent variable, operationalised through video-assisted debriefing tailored to the individual student's recorded performance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Knowledge of each individual learner's specific performance, errors and strengths, obtained here through video recording and instructor observation
#### Constraints
- The article notes a lack of specific evidence on how personalised feedback influences long-term skill acquisition in high-risk obstetric emergencies
