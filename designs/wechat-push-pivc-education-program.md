# WeChat push-based PIVC education program (twice-daily micro-messages over 10 days)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A mobile continuing-education intervention in which nurses received "two to three concise educational messages regarding PIVC placement techniques were delivered through the WeChat platform twice daily (10:00 Am and 6:00 Pm) over a 10-day period". Content followed a 10-day framework from PIVC basics to complication prevention, and message design drew on cognitive load theory principles for clarity. Reading feedback was collected via WeChat to check timely reading. In the trial it outperformed a no-intervention control on knowledge and observed practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants need WeChat access and schedules permitting message reading at set times; the study obtained nurses' work schedules and read-receipt feedback
#### Constraints
- Delivered to nurses at a single tertiary hospital; control nurses received no intervention during the study

### Target Learners
- Licensed clinical nurses with PIVC placement qualifications scoring below 75% on baseline knowledge assessment

### Learning Goals
- PIVC placement knowledge
- PIVC insertion and maintenance clinical practice skills
