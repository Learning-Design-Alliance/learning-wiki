# Near-, mid-, and far-transfer passage framework for measuring transfer in reading comprehension

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors developed a sensitivity framework for assessing transfer on an end-of-Grade-2 science reading comprehension measure, building on the content and context dimensions of transfer. Passages differed in exposure to taught vocabulary: "the content of the near-, mid-, and far-transfer passages provided extensive, limited, and no exposure, respectively, to the domain-specific vocabulary networks that children learned in the lessons." The context dimension captured similarity to the taught thematic lessons, with near-transfer contexts similar to instruction and mid and far contexts less similar.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Passages must vary systematically in exposure to directly taught vocabulary networks
- Comprehension items must be aligned to the content and context dimensions of transfer
#### Constraints
- The framework was developed for a Grade 2 science content reading comprehension outcome in this study

### Target Learners
- Second-grade students reading science informational texts

### Learning Goals
- Transfer of domain and topic knowledge to novel reading comprehension tasks
