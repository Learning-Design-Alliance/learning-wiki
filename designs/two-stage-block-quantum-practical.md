# Two-stage intensive block course: worksheet phase followed by a group simulator-building project phase

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The practical is delivered as an intensive seven-day block in two stages. In the worksheet phase, students work through six worksheets (one recap plus one per simulation method, roughly half a day each, three days total); in the project phase, groups of two or three choose one simulation method and implement and optimize their own simulator in a language of their choice. The block format "encourages a deeper immersion into the quantum circuit simulation topic and allows students to complete more ambitious projects within a condensed time frame".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students need prior completion of an introductory quantum computing lecture, since the practical builds on the lecture rather than replacing it.
#### Constraints
- The course targets graduate students with prior knowledge of quantum computing basics; worksheets 4 and 5 (stabilizer formalism, MPS) are pen-and-paper only.

### Target Learners
- graduate computer science students who completed an introductory quantum computing lecture

### Learning Goals
- in-depth understanding of superposition, entanglement, and noise effects
- ability to choose an appropriate quantum circuit simulation method
