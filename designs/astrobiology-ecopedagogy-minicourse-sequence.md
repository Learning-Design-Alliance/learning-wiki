# A continuing-education minicourse sequence built on a chronological cosmic-history thread, national education parameters, and Earth Pedagogy-aligned environmental discussion

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes a six-session remote minicourse (16–23 November 2021) whose theme sequence followed three explicit criteria: "estabelecer uma sequência cronológica da história do Universo e da vida a partir do que a Ciência propõe", relevance within national education parameters, and capacity to generate environmental discussion compatible with Earth Pedagogy foundations. Sessions moved from the Big Bang and star dust, through Earth's rarity and the origin of life, to extraterrestrial life and Ecopedagogy, closing with an evaluation circle. Presenters were active researchers in diverse fields, chosen to value interdisciplinarity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Presenters with research formation and practice in the session's field, previously selected and contacted
- Sessions recorded so teachers with varied attendance could follow the full course
#### Constraints
- Attendance varied across days; teachers relied on recordings to follow the course in full

### Target Learners
- practising basic-education science teachers
- teacher-education undergraduates (licenciandos)

### Learning Goals
- interdisciplinary science content on cosmic and life history
- environmental perception and planetary citizenship
- critical reflection on teaching practice
