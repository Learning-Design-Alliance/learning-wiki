# University-led teacher professional development combining student-tested activities with misconception training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The professional development pattern proceeds in stages: test the interactive activity with varied audiences and students, use questionnaire results to select the topics that could be effectively treated with teachers, dedicate a relevant part of the work to the definition of the model, its simplifications and guiding discussion, then test and finalize the methodology with another teacher training course. The explicit aim is making teachers "confident and autonomous in treating GR in their classrooms". The pattern ends by collecting all material in an open access manual with guidelines and tricks for autonomous classroom use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a space-time simulator and the manual's guidelines and tricks
- A university department able to run outreach events and teacher training courses
#### Constraints
- The Covid-19 emergency prevented the authors from following teachers in their experimentation with their students

### Target Learners
- high school teachers

### Learning Goals
- autonomous treatment of gravity and General Relativity in the classroom
