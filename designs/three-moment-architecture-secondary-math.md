# Three Moment Architecture for secondary mathematics lessons with English Learners

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Three Moment Architecture is a bounded instructional sequence in which a lesson, possibly spanning several class periods, moves through three distinct moments with different pedagogical purposes: Preparing the Learners, Interacting With the Concept, and Extending Understanding. The brief uses it to structure how defining evolves: working definitions generated early enable exploration, and more formal definitions emerge later. The authors present it as "a bounded instructional sequence" that may help rethink how definitions are handled in secondary mathematics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Lessons must connect students' prior knowledge with the main ideas during Preparing the Learners, then provide exploration across multiple representations, procedures, and contexts before formalizing definitions in Extending Understanding.
