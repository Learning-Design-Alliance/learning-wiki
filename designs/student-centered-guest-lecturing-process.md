# Student-centered guest lecturing process: team question-generation, question-and-answer interview, recorded video, reflection, and term paper

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article defines a repeatable lesson-level sequence for guest lecturing in online courses. Students study course content and work in groups to determine questions for the guest speaker; the speaker is interviewed in a pure question-and-answer format by an on-site representative or instructor, remotely if needed; the interview is recorded and shared; students revisit the video and reflect in discussion forums; the cycle may repeat with more speakers; finally students review the video series and write a term paper summarizing lessons learned. Teams are formed and coordinated through discussion forums in the course management tool.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Guest speakers carefully aligned with course content
- Discussion forums in the course management tool for team formation and group activities
- Recorded interviews made available to all students
- Required discussions and a term paper to encourage participation
#### Constraints
- Ideally multiple speakers repeat the process, which the article treats as an ideal rather than a guaranteed condition

### Target Learners
- online graduate students

### Learning Goals
- student engagement
- active construction of course knowledge
- reflection on guest-expert content
