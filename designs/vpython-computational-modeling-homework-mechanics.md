# VPython computational modeling homework in introductory mechanics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 2 against) · 4 studies (2 design, 1 causal, 1 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A suite of computational homework activities in which students in the Matter & Interactions mechanics course at Georgia Tech write VPython programs to model physical systems. "Over the course of the semester, students write several VPython programs, but the activities focus on translating the physical model to VPython code using the appropriate syntax and on updating the object's momentum and position." Students first build a TA-guided program modeling a craft orbiting the Earth over one virtual year, then complete a proctored central-force modeling assignment (Table 6).

### Learning Goals
- Computational modeling of physical systems: translating physical models into code, iterative prediction of motion
