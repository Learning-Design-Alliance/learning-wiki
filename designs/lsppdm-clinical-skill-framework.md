# LSPPDM (Learn, See, Practice, Prove, Do, Maintain) framework for clinical skill training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The LSPPDM model is "a framework for teaching clinical skills" that sequences six phases: Learn (online courses, videos and rating tables studied in advance), See (teacher demonstration and observation), Practice (small-group role-rotated practice with peer checklist feedback), Prove (video assessment scored by teachers and peers), Do (operating on real patients during clinical rotation), and Maintain (maintaining skill level through constant practice and staged examinations). The article integrates peer mutual learning into each phase.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires an SRT information management platform for sharing videos and rating tables, a skills center with reservable training rooms, and at least two people training together to strengthen peer learning
#### Constraints
- In this study the mode was limited to basic clinical skills course training and has not been applied to more teaching scenarios

### Target Learners
- medical residents in standardized residency training

### Learning Goals
- basic clinical skills including internal medicine, surgery and first-aid procedures
