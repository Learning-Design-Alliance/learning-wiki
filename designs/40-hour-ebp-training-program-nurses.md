# 40-hour EBP training program combining lectures, hands-on practice, and mentored consultation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A 40-hour evidence-based practice training program for clinical nurses delivered over 2 weeks, built around the five EBP steps and the PICO format. It comprised "20 hours of face-to-face lectures, 10 hours of group and individual consulting, and 10 hours of hands-on training and participation in evidence-based campaigns and nursing conferences." Participants worked in four teams, "each team mentored by a well-trained, evidence-based nursing facilitator," with debriefing meetings after each class and consultation by appointment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors with nursing, medical, and statistics background as well as expertise in empirical research; facilitators able to guide article searches, interpretation, and solving technical difficulties in actual practice
#### Constraints
- Delivered and evaluated in a single Taiwanese hospital setting with a convenience sample; group allocation was not randomized but based on participant intentions

### Target Learners
- currently practicing clinical nurses in hospitals

### Learning Goals
- evidence-based practice competencies: asking clinical questions, acquiring and appraising research evidence, applying and assessing results, and addressing barriers and facilitators
