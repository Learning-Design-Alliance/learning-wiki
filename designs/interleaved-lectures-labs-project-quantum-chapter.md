# Interleaving lectures with lab classes and a practical project in a compact course-chapter

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The course-chapter organizes two weeks of study so that lectures, interactive problem-solving, lab work and a practical project are tightly coupled: each week includes 4 hours of lectures and 3 hours of lab classes plus autonomous work, and the last two weeks are devoted to the quantum chapter with a guided first lab and autonomous project work afterwards. The author reports that "interleaving lectures with lab classes and practical projects, in a very synchronous way, have motivated students for studying and learning the main topics". The pattern pairs guided instruction with hands-on simulator work and independent study within a 56-hour workload.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- 14 hours of contact with teachers and 42 hours of autonomous work within the two-week chapter
- a first guided lab class introducing projects, materials and simulators before autonomous project work
#### Constraints
- designed for a two-week chapter inside a 7-week, 6-ECTS elective course in an ECE MSc program

### Target Learners
- Engineering graduate students (MSc in Electrical and Computer Engineering)

### Learning Goals
- identify quantum gates and analyze quantum circuits
- design quantum algorithms
- design and simulate circuits for quantum computation
- understand the limits of quantum technology and quantum systems
