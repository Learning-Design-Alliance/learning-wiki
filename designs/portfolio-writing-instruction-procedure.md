# Portfolio-based writing instruction procedure with deferred grading, revision cycles, and reflective cover letter

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes a 16-week portfolio procedure in which experimental-group students submitted drafts each session, received emailed feedback within two days, and were credited for revisions. At term's end each student presented "a portfolio of work to the instructor for evaluation and the term grade", containing two polished papers with all drafts and "a meta-analytical cover letter" justifying the selection. An independent scorer evaluated final portfolios to reduce instructor bias.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students submit drafts each session and receive feedback by the next class meeting
- A well defined scoring guide or rubric developed by both teacher and learner
#### Constraints
- Implemented in a second-semester freshman English composition course at one college with a single instructor-researcher

### Target Learners
- EFL university freshman composition students

### Learning Goals
- L2 writing skill development and preparation for timed writing examinations
