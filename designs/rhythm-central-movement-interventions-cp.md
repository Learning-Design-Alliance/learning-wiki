# Make rhythm the center of movement interventions for people with cerebral palsy rather than background music

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "clinicians should actively incorporate rhythmic music into movement intervention programs for people with CP because rhythmic music can be a powerful enhancer of the program", and that they should put "more emphasis into making rhythm as the center of the intervention rather than the background". Unlike preceding studies that used music only as background, this study made music the major intervening variable and reported significant improvements.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A structured program with music tracks of controlled meter and tempo (4/4, 70-90 bpm) synchronized to movement tasks
#### Constraints
- The study could not separate the differential effects of movement, psychological support and stimulating music

### Target Learners
- adults with cerebral palsy
- athletes in throwing events with CP

### Learning Goals
- improved gait, balance and psychological parameters
