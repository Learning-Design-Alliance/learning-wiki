# PEG+CAT curated home transmedia intervention package with parent supports

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A 12-week series of 12 approximately 30-minute opportunities for children and families to engage with PEG+CAT episodes, video clips, online games, a tablet app, and print activities across multiple devices. Materials focused on patterns, geometry, measurable attributes, ordinal numbers, and counting, plus ATL skills, and "The intervention included a series of supports for parents" covering co-viewing, math talk, and problem solving and persistence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Families needed provided technology resources (an Android tablet and a Chromebook laptop) and supports for joint engagement with the curated content
#### Constraints
- Time and intensity of each interaction depended on the child's interest and motivation and the availability and motivation of parents

### Target Learners
- 4- and 5-year-old children from low-income families attending preschool

### Learning Goals
- early mathematics skills (patterns, geometry, measurable attributes, ordinal numbers, counting)
- approaches to learning such as flexible problem solving, persistence, and productive collaboration
