# Interdisciplinary activity design guided by five characteristics of typical interdisciplinary problems, including tasks not easily solved by ChatGPT

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The lesson-level design pattern the authors used for the AI and Blockchain activities: authentic real-world scenarios, explicit expectations of multidisciplinary contributions, authentic cognitive advancements such as explaining or designing, tasks not easily solved by AI tools, and scaffolding that breaks tasks into smaller steps. They state they "were guided by the characteristics of typical interdisciplinary problems synthesized from relevant literature," and that activities "should not be easily solved by ChatGPT or other AI tools to ensure students' efforts in and control of their learning."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Group tasks structured in steps with scaffolding questions
- Mixed-discipline groups of five to six members
#### Constraints
- The authors note there might still be nuanced differences between the two tasks despite following the five characteristics

### Target Learners
- first- or second-year undergraduate students in mixed STEM and non-STEM groups

### Learning Goals
- collaborative interdisciplinary problem-solving
- argument construction and case analysis
