# Lock-in amplifier tutorial with integrated computer simulation (released on ComPADRE)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A research-based tutorial for developing a functional understanding of lock-in amplifiers, paired with a computer simulation in which students manipulate the reference frequency, time constant, filter rolloff, and input-signal characteristics including amplitude modulation, a secondary coherent frequency, and white noise. The article states that 'The simulation is integral to the tutorial and was built into the structure of the tutorial,' and the tutorial, simulation, pretest and posttest are available for download on ComPADRE.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Developed through faculty interviews, cognitive task analysis, and iterative refinement based on feedback from graduate students and professors
#### Constraints
- The tutorial and paper treat the lock-in as an idealized version of the instrument

### Target Learners
- physics graduate students beginning to use lock-in amplifiers in lab research
- graduate students already using lock-ins in their research

### Learning Goals
- predict lock-in output from input parameters and settings
- integrate conceptual and quantitative understanding of the mixer and low-pass filter
- troubleshoot unexpected outputs in lab work
