# Conjecture map linking planning-tool design to self-regulation outcomes in CSCL

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The article uses a conjecture map, described as "A representation of the design and theoretical conjectures concerning the planning phase of self-regulation." Its design conjectures state that if students engage in a team activity with goal-setting and planning tools, a time of debate and organization of the collective activity will be observed and students will create intermediate milestones and task lists. Its theoretical conjecture holds that clarifying task representations and adapting work plans leads students to share task conditions and use them in execution and adaptation. The map connects the tool design to the research questions.
