# RESET Cycle Model for teaching emotional intelligence online

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The RESET Cycle Model (Recognizing or regulating Emotions through Social and Emotional Team-based learning supported with mindfulness) is the article's proposed framework for teaching EI online. It begins with face-to-face introduction to team-based learning, mindfulness, and a Myers-Briggs workshop to build a foundation of knowledge. The cycle then alternates between online collaborative content workshops and online collaborative EI workshops, with new mindfulness instruction introduced face-to-face between each workshop. The article offers it as a potential foundation for a program to teach EI skills in higher education and organizational settings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Face-to-face introduction to team-based learning and mindfulness at the start
- A Myers-Briggs workshop presented face-to-face to set the stage for awareness of self and others
- Strong and active instructor presence to create community, collaboration and trust
#### Constraints
- Findings may not be generalizable to non-team-based learning settings
- Mindfulness practices were only taught by one instructor, so this factor cannot be separated from the instructor

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in information systems and business programs

### Learning Goals
- emotional intelligence skills including self-awareness, empathy, and emotional regulation
