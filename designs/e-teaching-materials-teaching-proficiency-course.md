# E-teaching materials for the 'Teaching proficiency' course with subject-oriented and methodological modules

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The developed artifact is a set of e-teaching materials for the 'Teaching proficiency' course, used in training future History and English language teachers. It "include[s] the following modules:1) subject ori ented (containing an e -textbook, consisting of theoretical, practical and control and diagnostics modules); 2) methodological (includes course steering document, methodological recommendations on e-teaching materials use, interactive assignments and manuals on e -teaching materials use)." Content is supported by text, audio, video, graphics and animation. In the experiment, tests for experimental groups were offered "in electronic form as one of the modules of e -teaching materials."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content must be supported by text, audio, video, graphics, animation, determined according to didactics.
#### Constraints
- Designed for humanities disciplines in future teachers' training at Kazan (Volga region) Federal University.

### Target Learners
- full-time and part-time university students of History and the English language faculty

### Learning Goals
- subject knowledge and skills in the 'Teaching proficiency' course
- methodological skills of using e-teaching materials in the learning process
