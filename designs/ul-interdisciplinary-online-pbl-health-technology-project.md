# Interdisciplinary online PBL 'Health Technology' project pairing dietetic and engineering students at University of Limerick

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A four-stage online PBL task conducted during COVID-19 in which "students to work in small interdisciplinary teams to design a solution to the authentic problem of conducting height assessment remotely using the sensors and instrumentation available on their smartphones". Teams of one dietetic student with one to two engineering students used MS Teams channels, recorded 1-min video proposals, used Miro collaboration boards, undertook self-directed learning (LabVIEW development for engineers, nutrition assessment understanding for dietitians), and submitted technical reports and reflections with 3-min prerecorded presentations marked jointly by both module leads. Modules were CT6061 (15 first-year graduate-entry dietetic students) and ET4305 (23 third-year engineering students), each 12 weeks, 6 ECTS.
