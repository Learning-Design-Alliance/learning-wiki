# Problem-based learning as a seven-step, student-centered, problem-driven instructional process

## Description
The article describes PBL as "a student-centered learning strategy that uses a problem as a starting point to facilitate learning", in which pharmacy students collaborate in small groups to improve cognitive capacities and clinical and critical thinking skills. It enumerates seven steps: identifying the problem, exploring prior knowledge, generating potential theories, identifying learning needs, individually gathering research, re-evaluating and applying new knowledge, and reflecting on the learning process. In this study PBL was implemented in the Authentication of Chinese Medicines course, an applied discipline on identifying and studying varieties and quality of Chinese medicines.
