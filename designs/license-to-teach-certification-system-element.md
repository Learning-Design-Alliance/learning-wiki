# “License to Teach” certification system for lead clinical nursing instructors

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An institutional certification system for lead clinical nursing preceptors developed by a Chinese tertiary hospital based on its Clinical Nursing Teaching Management Standards (2021). It combines eligibility criteria (degree, experience, professional title) with a "multi-dimensional evaluation model comprising three modules: classroom teaching, skill training, and teaching ward rounds", each assessed by expert panels using structured scoring sheets, with certification requiring at least a “good” rating in all three modules. It functions as a faculty development intervention intended to standardize preceptor qualifications.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Candidates must meet eligibility criteria including a full-time bachelor's degree or higher, over 5 years of specialty experience (≥3 for postgraduates), an intermediate professional title or higher, and some internship teaching experience.
#### Constraints
- The article states the system was developed and implemented within a single tertiary hospital under a college–hospital collaborative model and "may limit cross-institutional standardization".

### Target Learners
- Clinical nursing chief teachers (lead clinical instructors) at teaching hospitals

### Learning Goals
- Standardized clinical teaching competency, including theory teaching, skill demonstration, and interactive teaching ward rounds
