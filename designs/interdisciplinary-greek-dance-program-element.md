# Interdisciplinary Greek traditional dance intervention program (music and sociology topics)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
An eight-lesson, four-week interdisciplinary program teaching Greek traditional dance with "thematic choices from music and sociology", delivered twice weekly in 45-minute physical education classes. Four lessons drew on music concepts (rhythm, tempo, meters) and four on sociology concepts (coexistence, cooperation, bullying), using aids such as metronome marks and collaborative teaching, and was compared against eight typical curriculum folk dance lessons.
