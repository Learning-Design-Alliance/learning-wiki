# Nitobe School trans-graduate-school PBL program with team-based learning across 18 graduate schools

## Description
The Nitobe School is a trans-graduate-school program at Hokkaido University launched in 2015 under the MEXT Top Global University Project, with a one-year basic program and a one-year advanced program. Its teaching style is "primarily PBL and Team-Based Learning (TBL) system where students from diﬀerent graduate schools are mixed to form a team of 5-7 students with multiple tutors in classroom". The basic program runs four sequential courses (Basics of Team-Based Learning, Practice of Team-based Learning, Problem Solving, Problem Finding), awards 2 credits per course with 8 credits required, is taught in English, and is supported by e-portfolios to monitor progress.
