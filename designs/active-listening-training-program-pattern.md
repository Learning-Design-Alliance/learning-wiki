# Eight-week active listening training program organized around listening types

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The experimental intervention was a weekly two-hour course for eight weeks (16 hours total) in which students studied informative texts, narrative texts, a poem and a video, each tied to a listening type (discriminant, listening for information, empathetic, critical, aesthetic) and "performing activities in accordance with the process steps of active listening method". Sessions progressed from defining active listening and listening types to applying each type to a curriculum-appropriate text.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Texts appropriate to students' levels, selected from books suitable for fourth and fifth grade levels and approved materials; audiovisual tools including a TRT School short film
#### Constraints
- Delivered within Turkish lesson hours (40-minute lessons, two per week); control group studied the same materials via traditional listening exercises such as note taking and teacher questions

### Target Learners
- fourth-grade primary school students

### Learning Goals
- vocabulary development and listening skill improvement
