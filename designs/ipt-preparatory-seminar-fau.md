# Problems of the International Physicists' Tournament seminar at FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A seminar at FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg, called 'Problems of the International Physicists' Tournament', at two credit hours and 5 ECTS points (about 140 hours workload), first held winter term 2018/2019. Students work on IPT problems, give 45-minute talks on approaches of their choice, and take part in training fights under IPT rules. Each student is assigned a supervisor who helps with theoretical background, literature, modelling approaches, and code development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Supervisors assigned to each student to help with theoretical background and contacts
- Talks and training fights scheduled between semester start and the GPT in December
#### Constraints
- For the IPT, students had to work on additional problems beyond the seminar, which turned out to be difficult without full seminar support

### Target Learners
- university physics students preparing for the IPT

### Learning Goals
- solving open physics problems
- presenting and defending solutions
- scientific communication
