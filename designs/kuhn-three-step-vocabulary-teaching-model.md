# Kühn's three-step model of vocabulary teaching (receptive, reflective, productive)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Kühn's (2000) three-step model holds that vocabulary exercises should proceed in three steps: "The first step is receptive vocabulary practice, which involves understanding and explaining words with different semantization techniques", followed by reflective practice (notation, gathering, linking, ordering of words) and productive practice, "which involves the use of the vocabulary in appropriate texts and situations". The model follows three principles: success measured by learner output, functional orientation toward writing and/or speaking competence, and textual orientation. The present study operationalizes its third step as text production and tests its retention effects.
