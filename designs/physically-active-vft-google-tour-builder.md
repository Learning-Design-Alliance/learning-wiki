# Physically active Virtual Field Trip sessions using interactive whiteboards and Google Tour Builder

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 unmarked) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Virtual Field Trips are physically active lessons combining curriculum content with globe-based movement on interactive whiteboards, technology found in over 70% of UK classrooms. The demonstrated VFT used Google Tour Builder, a browser-based modification of Google Earth allowing users to plot journeys via locations worldwide. In the sample session, pupils stood and completed on-the-spot movements simulating travel and actions at London 2012 Olympic locations before visiting Rio; a prior pilot study showed significantly increased light, moderate and vigorous activity in an active VFT session compared to a sedentary version.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interactive whiteboard or display, Google Earth-based software, and pre-built journey content with embedded questions
#### Constraints
- Novelty of maps and media may fade with repeated lessons; teachers with low ICT confidence may resist use

### Target Learners
- primary school pupils

### Learning Goals
- curriculum learning combined with increased physical activity during lessons
