# Video-based tutorials on propagating waves

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Based on preliminary research into student difficulties with wave propagation, the authors designed a set of video-based tutorials using pre-recorded videos of propagating waves (available on the VideoPoint CD-ROM). These tutorials "are designed to elicit student difficulties, confront students with incorrect predictions, and help students resolve any difficulties they have." They were delivered as part of the modified instruction whose post-instruction results are compared with traditional instruction.
