# Key-concepts interdisciplinary curriculum framework: energy and matter as transversal organizing concepts in a CTS-structured science-teacher education program

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article proposes organizing the entire science-teacher curriculum around the key concepts of energy and matter rather than around disciplines: "A proposta que apresentaremos a seguir é predominantemente construída a partir dos conceitos de energia e matéria." As the conceptual structure evolves, technological products resulting from manipulation of the key concepts are addressed, so that "A estrutura curricular atende, portanto, aos princípios CTS (Ciência, Tecnologia e Sociedade)" — for example, thermodynamics is treated together with the products of the first industrial revolution and discussions of its political, social and economic aspects.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Integration is achieved by rearranging the conventional curricular structure: "a perspectiva interdisciplinar é constituída simplesmente com o rearranjo da estrutura curricular convencional, sem qualquer perda de conceitos básicos essenciais"
#### Constraints
- The article does not discuss the humanities, mathematics or final-year specific disciplines, stating they "podem ser tratadas do mesmo modo como são nos atuais cursos de licenciaturas em biologia, física e química"

### Target Learners
- undergraduate science-teacher education (licenciatura) students in Brazil and Latin America

### Learning Goals
- interdisciplinary conceptual understanding of biology, physics and chemistry grounded in energy and matter
