# Dialogue-based values and policy curriculum module for graduate environmental science courses

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A customizable curriculum module for graduate environmental science courses built around locally relevant case studies, student-developed discussion prompts, and a facilitated dialogue following the Toolbox dialogue protocol. Materials include instructor guidance, a customizable lecture presentation, readings, FAQs, learning objectives, and "two lesson plans–one for a 6-hour (2-week) implementation and one for a 3-hour implementation–which makes the curriculum adaptable for different situations." "The centerpiece of the curriculum is a group discussion structured around the prompts."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Documents were "sufficiently structured to alleviate the need for faculty to have deep expertise in these domains", so instructors from any discipline can use them without formal training in values and policy.
#### Constraints
- The six supplied case studies "would likely not fit perfectly for many situations"; a guidance document helps instructors select locally relevant cases.
- All materials are available from the authors upon request.

### Target Learners
- Graduate students in environmental science and related programs
- Practicing K-12 science teachers in a graduate environmental education course

### Learning Goals
- Awareness of and reasoning about values and policy in environmental research
- Reflexivity and perspective-taking through structured dialogue
