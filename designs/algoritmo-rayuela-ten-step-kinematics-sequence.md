# The ten-step Algoritmo-Rayuela for kinematics: an explicit, re-readable, step-listed exercise-resolution sequence used throughout a first-year course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Algoritmo-Rayuela is a ten-step algorithm presented as one of many possible aids for tackling physics exercises: it starts with step 1) "Leer detenidamente todo el enunciado del problema" and runs through drawing the initial schema, defining the reference system, writing general and then particular horarias laws, symbolic development, tandem graphs, checking, exhausting the problem, to step 10) ¡Disfrutar!. The first algorithm was presented in the first class and every board-solved exercise referenced its steps. A schematic representation of the kinematics steps appears in Figure 1.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Steps are presented, explained and discussed in class, with the intention and characteristics of each step commented on and doubts answered
- Instructors reference the algorithm's steps in every worked exercise to build the habit
#### Constraints
- The algorithm is topic-specific: for each topic it was reworked, enriched and modified to fit the exercise type of each field of vector classical mechanics — Cinemática, Estática y Dinámica

### Target Learners
- first-year students of the Profesorado en Física

### Learning Goals
- interpreting and analyzing problem statements
- developing symbolic algebraic resolutions
- connecting reference systems, motion functions and graphs
- ordered and clear written communication of solutions
