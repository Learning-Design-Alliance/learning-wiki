# QuTE4E pilot project: Physics Outreach Research and culturo-scientific storytelling for quantum technology outreach

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Quantum Technologies Education for Everyone (QuTE4E) project developed a research-based approach called Physics Outreach Research (POR) to address the what, why, and how of quantum technology outreach. Its theoretical framework is culturo-scientific storytelling (CSS), which "combines the theoretical framework of the discipline-culture with the inquiry cycle to address Gardner's minds". The project produced a community toolbox of resources for creativity, experimentation, and formalisation, used in events such as Europe Quantum Day 2022.
