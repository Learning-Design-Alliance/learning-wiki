# Adapted Engineering LA Program pedagogy workshop with weekly online reflections

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The Engineering LA Program replaced the College of Science's 2-credit pedagogy course with a non-credit workshop plus online reflections. "The workshop contained 8-hours of contact time, including a 4-hour pre-term meeting, and two 2-hour meetings during the term," and LAs "completed 10 weekly online reflections" hosted in the AIChE Concept Warehouse, covering learning theories and pedagogical methods situated in the context where LAs would work.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- LAs receive a stipend for the workshop and online reflections in addition to hourly payment for their role in course instruction
- Weekly reflection prompts ask LAs to read a short article about learning and pedagogy and relate it to their teaching experiences through a 250-word written reflection
#### Constraints
- Designed for engineering curricula where students did not have room in their plans to add the 2-credit LA pedagogy course of the College of Science LA Model

### Target Learners
- undergraduate engineering learning assistants at a large public university

### Learning Goals
- pedagogical knowledge and methods for facilitating active learning with near peers
