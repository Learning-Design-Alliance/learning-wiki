# Five-session story drama training program for preschool instructional staff

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The intervention began with five 2-hour story drama training sessions delivered over five weeks to the entire instructional staff, using resources derived from Carleton's story drama lesson plans for special needs classrooms. Sessions covered introduction to story dramas, modeled lesson plans with staff as students, and theater techniques with a professional actor. The article states "Five 2-hour story drama lesson training sessions were developed and delivered over a 5-week period to the entire instructional staff".
