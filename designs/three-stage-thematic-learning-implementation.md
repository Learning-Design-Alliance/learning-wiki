# Three-stage implementation of thematic and inquiry-based integrated thematic learning: preparation, implementation, evaluation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Both the thematic instruction model and the inquiry-based integrated thematic instructional model are implemented through three linked stages. The article states "three stages are required in the implementation of thematic learning, namely the preparation, implementation, and evaluation", and repeats that the inquiry-based integrated model "also consists of three stages, namely preparation, implementation and evaluation". Figures 1 and 2 illustrate the correlation of components in these stages in relation to character education in primary school.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Preparation includes lesson plan (RPP) development; implementation and evaluation stages are linked to character education outcomes as illustrated in the article's Figures 1 and 2
#### Constraints
- The article reports teacher obstacles at each stage: difficulty elaborating competencies into indicators and developing themes at preparation, limited knowledge, materials and facilities at implementation, and difficulty assessing grade-1 students and documenting results at assessment

- Primary school students
