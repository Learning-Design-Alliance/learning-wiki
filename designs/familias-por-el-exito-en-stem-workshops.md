# Familias por el Éxito en STEM: Spanish-language virtual parent workshops delivered with PIQE

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A culturally responsive parent intervention developed with the Parent Institute for Quality Education (PIQE), a community-based organization with over 30 years of experience with the local Spanish-speaking community. "The parent intervention workshops consisted of five virtual (Zoom) seminars, each meeting for 90 minutes once a week over five consecutive weeks", held after work hours (6:00-7:30 pm). Sessions covered the value of STEM education, STEM and Latinx identity, university resources, and STEM career pathways, and included Latinx STEM alumni role models and a graduation ceremony. Grounded in Yosso's Community Cultural Wealth, the design emphasized familial, aspirational, linguistic, and social capital.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training and materials conducted in Spanish to value parents' linguistic capital
- Delivery via community partner organization staff bilingual in Spanish and English
- Sessions scheduled after typical work hours to accommodate working parents
#### Constraints
- Originally planned in person but moved online due to COVID-19
- Parents who partially participated were included in the treatment group

### Target Learners
- Spanish-speaking parents of first-year Latinx or Hispanic-identifying STEM undergraduates, predominantly of Mexican descent

### Learning Goals
- Parents' utility value of STEM careers
- Supportive parent behaviors including STEM conversations and balancing family and school obligations
