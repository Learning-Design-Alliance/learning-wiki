# Patient-specific interactive 3D anatomical models reconstructed from CT scans for three anatomical variants

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Three anatomical variations were modelled: "normal anatomy" (NA), "short cystic duct" (SCD) and "double cystic artery" (DA). For each, a 3D model was reconstructed manually from an anonymised CT scan using in-house software (Volume Viewer, University of Leeds) and exported to open-source visualisation software (MeshLab). Group B reviewed the model matching each case's anatomy at most checklist steps before each simulated procedure, while Group A was informed of the variation but had no model access.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to anonymised CT scans, 3D reconstruction software, and 3D model viewing software with training in its use
#### Constraints
- The fusion of mental rehearsal and anatomical models does not require a simulator and can be practiced repeatedly on a personal computer, but the study was conducted in a simulation suite rather than a clinical environment

### Target Learners
- Medical students and surgical trainees preparing for patient-specific procedures

### Learning Goals
- Pre-operative preparation incorporating patient-specific anatomical variation
