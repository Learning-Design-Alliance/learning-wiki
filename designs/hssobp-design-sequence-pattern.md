# Standards-based simulation design sequence: needs assessment, SMART objectives, authentic scenarios, and standardized pre-briefing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
This is the design sequence the article's intervention followed under the HSSOBP: a needs assessment aligned to training goals and clinical requirements, construction of objectives integrating needs assessment with learners' competence levels, scenario development from real clinical cases, and standardized pre-briefing. Objectives were designed to be "Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound (SMART)", ensuring they guided the simulation activities and aligned with the program's broader educational goals. Scenarios replicated authentic clinical workflows while targeting essential professional knowledge and clinical skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A multidisciplinary teaching team (the article used one obstetrician, one midwifery expert, and two simulation instructors with at least ten years of experience and a master's degree or higher), preliminary trial runs to ascertain task feasibility, and a high-fidelity environment replicating an authentic delivery room setting
#### Constraints
- The article links the intervention's failure to improve self-confidence to this brief 12-class-hour duration and to the unchanged Support dimension of the design

### Target Learners
- third-year undergraduate midwifery students

### Learning Goals
- obstetric emergency management skills
- integration of theory and clinical practice
