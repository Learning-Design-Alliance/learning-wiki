# Rotating HPC curriculum set: MPI, Big Data/Machine Learning, OpenMP, and OpenACC agendas

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Table I presents the four agendas in the ACCESS monthly rotation: a two-day MPI course, a two-day Big Data and Machine Learning course, a one-day OpenMP course, and a one-day OpenACC course. Agendas are tightly integrated with exercises and deliberately short, typically "six hours, including breaks, per day", to accommodate multiple time zones. The Big Data and Machine Learning event "has quickly become the most popular" and is the most rapidly evolving.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Short days to accommodate multiple time zones humanely, and tightly integrated exercises with close attention to evaluations for pacing
#### Constraints
- Programs remain oversubscribed by community demand; a third Big Data day would be logistically difficult for students who struggle to clear two days

### Target Learners
- Scientists and faculty lacking access to HPC content hidden in specialized computer science curriculums

### Learning Goals
- Applying MPI, OpenMP, OpenACC, Spark, and deep learning techniques directly to learners' own applications
