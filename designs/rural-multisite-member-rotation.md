# Rural multisite rotation: each member works across a rotation of sites to maximize coverage where resources are scarce

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In rural settings with severe workforce shortages, YMHC members are assigned across a rotation of sites (school, high school, alternative school, public library, restorative justice program) rather than a single placement. The report states this "multisite model, in which each member works across a rotation of sites, addresses the scarcity of resources in rural settings by maximizing one member's impact across diverse settings," keeping members present where students naturally congregate.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Flexible scheduling based on student needs and site demand
- Evening and weekend programming to accommodate rural transportation barriers
#### Constraints
- Designed for rural contexts with limited access to mental health services; the report describes it as addressing resource scarcity in such settings

### Target Learners
- rural middle and high school students

### Learning Goals
- access to mental health and academic support
- connection to school and community resources
