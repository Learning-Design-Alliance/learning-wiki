# The Macher–Schaffner-Bielich compact-star undergraduate research project

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The element is the research project itself: "a three–month research project for undergraduate students about the mass-radius relation of compact stars", in which the student derived mass-radius relations by numerically solving the Tolman-Oppenheimer-Volkoﬀ equation for a parametric equation of state constrained by established crust EOSs. A mentor proposed the topic and guided the student through it, and the work became a publishable report with figures, tables and parameter-study results.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mentor guidance from proposal through completion
- Access to compiled crust equation-of-state tables (BPS and NV, compiled at the institute by Stefan Rüster)
- Numerical TOV solver with adaptive stepsize
#### Constraints
- The authors call the work unfinished and note parameter correlations make results tricky to sort out

### Target Learners
- An undergraduate physics student in his second year of the Magistère des sciences de la matière

### Learning Goals
- Numerical stellar-structure computation, equation-of-state modelling, and scientific report writing
