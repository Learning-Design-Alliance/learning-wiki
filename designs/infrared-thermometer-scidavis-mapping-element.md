# Infrared thermometer and SciDAVis-based thermal mapping apparatus for student field investigation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The activity's core instruments: a non-contact infrared thermometer (Celsius scale, accuracy of ±0.5°C) operating on thermal radiation as described by the Stefan-Boltzmann law, printed spreadsheets for recording, and the open-source software SciDAVis (version 2.8) used to organize measurements into a matrix and generate two-dimensional color-scale temperature maps. The article notes measurement accuracy "can be influenced by several factors, such as the heat -emission capability of the object, the distance between the device and the target, and the presence of obstructions along the radiation path."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Standard measurement protocol to avoid shade cast by the measurer and non-standard surfaces
- Monitor training on proper use of the measuring equipment and the software
#### Constraints
- Emissivity of the surface affects readings, varying with material, surface finish, temperature, observation angle and wavelength

### Target Learners
- public-school students in investigative physics workshops

### Learning Goals
- surface temperature measurement
- thermal radiation concepts
- data organization and visualization
