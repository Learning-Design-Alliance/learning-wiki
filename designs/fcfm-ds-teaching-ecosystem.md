# A campus-wide data science teaching ecosystem distributed across three departments

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A collection of teaching initiatives at the Faculty of Physical and Mathematical Sciences, Universidad de Chile: "The ecosystem is distributed in a collaborative fashion across three departments in the above Faculty and includes postgraduate programmes, courses, professional diplomas, data repositories, laboratories, trainee programmes, and internships." It serves engineering undergraduates, MSc students, research interns, project engineers and practising professionals, and extends to public outreach.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The curriculum builds on three units — the Center for Mathematical Modelling, the Department of Computer Science and the Department of Electrical Engineering — whose areas jointly cover the DS skill base.
#### Constraints
- The ecosystem "is not exclusive to any programme or department in our engineering school, but rather exists as a collection of campus-wide resources, which can be extended as demand requires".

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and graduate engineering students
- research interns and project engineers
- practising professionals
- the general public

### Learning Goals
- data science qualifications blending theory, methods and applications
