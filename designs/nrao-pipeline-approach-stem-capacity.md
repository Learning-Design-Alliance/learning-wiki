# NRAO's observatory-wide pipeline approach to STEM capacity development

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a comprehensive, observatory-wide pipeline model in which learners move through a seamless flow of STEM activities from K-12 engagement through undergraduate, graduate, and post-doctoral research, training, education, and mentoring programs. The approach has "particular emphasis on broadening participation of underrepresented groups and diverse institutions" and aligns with NSF priorities such as preparing a diverse STEM workforce and integrating research with education. The ngVLA is positioned as vastly increasing the opportunities the pipeline can offer.
