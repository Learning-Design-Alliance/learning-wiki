# Four standardized pregnancy sexual counseling scenarios spanning 16th- and 32nd-week concerns

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Four practice scenarios developed by the researchers, covering a 16th-week pregnant woman avoiding intimacy over harm fears, a 16th-week woman whose partner avoids sexuality fearing harm, a 32nd-week woman with sexual reluctance from breast tenderness, pelvic congestion, and positional difficulties, and a 32nd-week woman avoiding sexuality because she "does not find herself attractive". Scenarios were shared across the virtual patient and both peer simulation conditions, with peer roles portrayed by the same trained student.
