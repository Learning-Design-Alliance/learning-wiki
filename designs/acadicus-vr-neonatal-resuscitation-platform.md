# Acadicus-based VR neonatal resuscitation training platform simulating a 30-week neonate aligned to NRP 8th edition guidelines

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes a VR training platform built in Acadicus (Arch Virtual) with a multidisciplinary team, simulating "a 30-week neonate experiencing respiratory distress, with scenarios aligned to the Neonatal Resuscitation Program® (NRP) 8th edition guidelines". The virtual neonate exhibits chest rise, retractions, and vital-sign changes providing real-time dynamic feedback, and participants use Meta Quest 2 headsets to practice mask ventilation, MR. SOPA corrective steps, and post-resuscitation care in a fully equipped virtual resuscitation room.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Meta Quest 2 head-mounted displays and handheld controllers; stable internet connectivity for real-time remote instructor participation; pre-recorded English-language instructions with human translation
#### Constraints
- The VR system interface and pre-recorded instructional content were English-only, lacking built-in multilingual capabilities; procedural elements such as endotracheal intubation and chest compressions may lack tactile realism because haptic feedback systems remain in early stages of development

### Target Learners
- healthcare providers involved in neonatal care (neonatologists, pediatric residents, NICU hospitalists, nurses, allied health professionals)

### Learning Goals
- neonatal resuscitation cognitive decision-making, NRP algorithm adherence, and team-based communication skills
