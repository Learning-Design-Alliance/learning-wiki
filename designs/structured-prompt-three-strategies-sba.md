# Structured prompts combining structural formatting, few-shot prompting, and chain-of-thought reasoning shape AI-generated MCQ quality

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's institutional workshop prompt was designed around three key strategies: "structural formatting, few-shot prompting, and chain-of-thought reasoning." As illustrated in supplementary figure 2, the degree of contextual information provided to the LLM shapes the alignment of output with assessment intent and the overall quality of AI-generated MCQs — their validity, reliability, and educational impact. Well-structured prompts also reduce post-generation editing, improving cost-efficiency.
