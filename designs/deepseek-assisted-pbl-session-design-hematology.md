# DeepSeek-assisted PBL session design for hematology residency training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
An augmented PBL session structure integrating DeepSeek V3, its R1 version and a Web Searching module into case-based residency sessions, accessed via the DeepSeek online platform. Each session ran: DeepSeek-generated case introduction with interactive prompts, real-time feedback on diagnostic propositions, personalized guidance and simulations of rare conditions, group integration of AI and web-derived insights, and a collaborative wrap-up. The core case content was identical to the traditional PBL group's, and residents received training on querying DeepSeek and on "critically appraising DeepSeek-generated information". Facilitators followed standardized protocols to validate and correct AI outputs in real time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Facilitators trained in a standardization workshop who actively validate clinical accuracy of DeepSeek outputs and correct misleading suggestions in real time
- Residents receive explicit instruction in critically appraising DeepSeek-generated information and cross-referencing it with established knowledge
#### Constraints
- The study evaluated this design only in second-year hematology residency training at a single institution over two courses
- Residents were not provided with pre-structured or curated prompts for direct DeepSeek input; prompts were generated by DeepSeek within the case

### Target Learners
- Second-year hematology residents

### Learning Goals
- Clinical reasoning and diagnostic decision-making
- Interdisciplinary integration and research literacy
- Emergency management and ethical reasoning in hematology
