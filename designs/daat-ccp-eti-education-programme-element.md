# DAAT structured ETI education programme: supervised-intubation competency threshold, theatre placements, video laryngoscopy case-review debriefs, and cadaveric training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The Devon Air Ambulance Trust programme is a bundle of educational and organisational interventions listed in Table 1 across 2018–2024. CCPs "require 60 supervised intubations before being deemed competent, of which at least 25 must be undertaken in a controlled setting", with an ongoing airway management logbook. Theatre placements expanded to a designated three-week placement, video laryngoscope attempts were recorded in a database and reviewed in formal team sessions, and cadaveric surgical skills training was delivered twice. CCPs are also trained to use '30-second drills' to optimise laryngoscopy views after a failed attempt.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- CCPs must have completed additional formal postgraduate study in pre-hospital critical care, retrieval and transfer prior to independent practice
- Ongoing and regularly-reviewed airway management logbook recording all intubation attempts including training, simulation and clinical practice
