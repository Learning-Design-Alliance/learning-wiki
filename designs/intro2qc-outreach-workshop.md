# Intro2QC single-session outreach workshop for high school STEM students

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Intro2QC is a single-session outreach workshop for high school STEM students in Grades 9-12, comprising "three (3) parts with a total time of 80 to 90 minutes": an analogy-based introduction to computational hardness, quantum concepts via demos and discussion, and an online Qiskit programming tutorial. It was "delivered to approximately 160 students in multiple school groups from Grades 9-12 in British Columbia Canada" during the International Year of Quantum Science and Technology.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a central projector, screen or another central display for the first two segments
- Students need access to contemporary computing devices and the internet for the programming portion
#### Constraints
- No hard pre-requisites for students, though familiarity with terms like bits and binary code is assumed for grades 9-12; basic programming skills beneficial but not asked for
- The workshop may be also be shortened to a 50-minute session, condensing the first two segments into a 20-minute introduction

### Target Learners
- high school STEM students in Grades 9-12

### Learning Goals
- rouse curiosity in quantum science and quantum computing
- introduce a conceptual basis for quantum physics including superposition, interference and entanglement
- apply quantum ideas in a practical programming tutorial
