# Systematic teaching: student discovery of knowledge with the teacher as guide

## Description
The article describes systematic teaching as based on "discovering, making sense of, using, and reproducing knowledge by students," in which "A teacher can only be a guide." Teachers arrange settings and resources so students discover principles themselves, providing "cues, feedback, and correction." Major components include educational games, contests, scientific research, a democratic environment, and multidimensional evaluations.
