# Weblog-and-email blended delivery for teaching resumptive pronouns in L2 writing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
This element is the blended learning condition's delivery system: lesson materials were split between a class teacher and a public Weblog, with writing assignments submitted by email under fixed deadlines. The article states the material "was partially given through a Weblog (http://learningenglishonline.mihanblog.com) and partially in the class" and that "The participants were to hand in their writings by e-mail." Lessons alternated between Weblog and class presentation across the term, each with a two-day submission deadline.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants need access to the Weblog and functioning email; the article reports delivery failures requiring individual re-sending of materials by email.
#### Constraints
- The article reports that motivating some students to participate in the virtual class was very difficult, that some participants delayed emailing homework, and that data collection took longer than expected.

### Target Learners
- Intermediate EFL learners aged 15-19 at English institutes

### Learning Goals
- Correct use of resumptive pronouns in L2 writing
- Paragraph organization including topic, supporting, and concluding sentences
