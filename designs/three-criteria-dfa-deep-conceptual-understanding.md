# Three quality criteria for digital formative assessment of deep conceptual understanding: relevance, validity, and in-depth informativity

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes that a DFA for deep conceptual understanding must fulfill three criteria: relevance (focusing on the most relevant components of a topic), validity (appropriateness of inferences, including actionable diagnostic information for teachers), and in-depth informativity (combining in-depth insights into student thinking with automatable analysis). These criteria structure the four-step design process and are summarized in Fig. 5.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prior to item construction, epistemological and empirical research in the subject-matter area is required to unpack topics into the most relevant concept elements
#### Constraints
- The digital assessment of deep conceptual understanding is more challenging to realize than the digital assessment of procedural skills

### Target Learners
- Grade 5–7 students who struggle in mathematics

### Learning Goals
- Deep conceptual understanding of basic arithmetic concepts
