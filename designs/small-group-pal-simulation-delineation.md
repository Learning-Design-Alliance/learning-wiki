# Small-group simulation with peer-assisted learning for delineation training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The design phase limits attendees to "a group of 10 junior radiation oncology residents, to allow personalized interaction with the facilitators." The article states this group setting enhances peer-assisted learning, enabling trainees to benefit from diverse experiences shared among peers during collaborative contouring exercises.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Group size limited to about 10 residents to allow personalized facilitator interaction
#### Constraints
- Described for junior radiation oncology residents in a department designated as a center of excellence

### Target Learners
- junior radiation oncology residents

### Learning Goals
- tumor and OAR delineation skills
- collaborative learning
