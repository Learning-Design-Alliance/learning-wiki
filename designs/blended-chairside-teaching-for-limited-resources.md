# Adopt blended PBL-CBL-seminar chairside teaching to make efficient use of limited clinical teaching resources

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "medical educators may consider adopting this blended pedagogical approach" because it "efficiently leverages limited teaching resources, facilitating students' systematic acquisition of clinical technical competencies." The principle rests on the trial's findings of higher Mini-CEX, DOPS, and questionnaire scores versus lecture-based learning, while acknowledging the model demands extra educator time for case curation and seminar facilitation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators able to dedicate extra time to curating representative PBL and CBL cases, motivating engagement, and managing seminar interactions
#### Constraints
- Did not yield significant gains in clinical judgment or precise, proficient technique
- Caution generalizing beyond single-institution, homogeneous undergraduate samples

### Target Learners
- undergraduate dental students in chairside clinical teaching

### Learning Goals
- standardized clinical operations
- communication and humanistic care
- overall clinical competence
