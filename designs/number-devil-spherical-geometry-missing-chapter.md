# A missing-chapter dialogic narrative for The Number Devil that teaches spherical geometry through a dream adventure

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This element is a self-contained supplementary chapter written in the style of Enzensberger's The Number Devil, in which the boy Robert is visited in dreams by the Number Devil. The author supplies a chapter Enzensberger "overlooked," where the Number Devil introduces Robert to "geometry not-of-Euclid, great circles, parallel transport, the pendulum of Foucault, and the genius of Euler." Learning happens through dialogue between a skeptical learner and an enthusiastic expert figure, with worked calculations embedded in the story.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Must be read by an audience familiar with, or receptive to, the framing of The Number Devil adventure series
#### Constraints
- Fictional narrative form rather than a textbook exposition

### Target Learners
- school students learning geometry, in the role of the boy Robert

### Learning Goals
- distinguish spherical from Euclidean geometry
- compute distances and areas on a sphere
- understand Euler's formula
