# Three-phase model of the breaking bad news process (pre-delivery, delivery, post-delivery)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article organises breaking bad news as a three-phase process adapted to triadic consultations. The "‘pre-delivery phase'" is "devoted to preparing the patient and his/her relative for the delivery of bad news by assessing what they know, understand, and feel about the current situation"; the delivery phase delivers the news precisely and concisely; the post-delivery phase provides emotional support and additional information to both patient and relative. The model structured both the training programme and the outcome analysis, with phase boundaries defined by the utterance delivering the cancer diagnosis.
