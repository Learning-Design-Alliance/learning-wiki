# SAAFE application examples in SCORES, ATLAS and HIIT for Teens school-based interventions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Table 2 of the article documents how each SAAFE principle was operationalized in three school-based physical activity interventions: SCORES for primary school children, ATLAS for low-active adolescent boys, and HIIT for Teens integrating vigorous activity into PE. Examples include small-sided modified games, circuit cards with body weight and Gymstick exercises, student-selected HIRT difficulty levels, heart rate monitors with effort-based success criteria, and high-tempo music during HIIT sessions. The article states the SAAFE principles "have also since evolved to support the delivery of school-based physical activity interventions targeting adolescents".
