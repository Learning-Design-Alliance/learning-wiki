# Ten-week folk games teaching activity schedule for fine motor development in kindergarten A

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The article's intervention artifact is a schedule of traditional folk game activities (Table 1) run three times weekly from April to June 2016, with topics such as Paper Folding Change, Cut Window, Cat's Cradle, Embroidery: Fan's Making and Making Zong Zi. The article states "There were three times of teaching intervention weekly and each intervention took 40 to 50 minutes", with actual interventions per child ranging from 26 to 30 due to attendance. Activities were revised by the kindergarten principal, teachers and university teachers, and practiced in two micro-teachings before classroom use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Activities were applied only after revision by the kindergarten principal, kindergarten teachers and professional university teachers, and practiced by the team after two micro-teachings
#### Constraints
- Due to varying attendance, children received different actual interventions ranging from 26 to 30 times

### Target Learners
- children aged 4 to 5 in middle classes of a public urban kindergarten

### Learning Goals
- fine motor skills of pinching, touching, drawing, cutting, folding and stringing
