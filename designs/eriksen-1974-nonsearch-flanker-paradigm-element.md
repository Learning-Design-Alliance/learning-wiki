# Eriksen and Eriksen nonsearch flanker paradigm (six-condition tachistoscopic letter task)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A tachistoscopic task in which the subject responds with a right or left leverpress to a single target letter (H or K vs S or C) that always appears at a fixed location above fixation during a 1-sec exposure. The target is flanked by three noise letters on each side under five noise conditions (identical, response-compatible, response-incompatible, heterogeneous-similar, heterogeneous-dissimilar) at three between-letter spacings (.06, Yz, and 1 deg of visual angle), plus mixed and blocked target-alone controls. It was designed to provide "an anchor or baseline condition" for noise effects when no visual search is required.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Subjects thoroughly instructed to respond only to the letter in the target location and ignore all other letters
#### Constraints
- Eliminating search removes the confounding of noise effects with assumptions about how display search is carried out, so findings are specific to the nonsearch case

### Target Learners
- adult volunteers with normal or corrected-to-normal vision

### Learning Goals
- studying the effects of noise stimuli on target identification independent of visual search

### Claims

- [Response-incompatible flanking noise letters impair target reaction time significantly more than response-compatible noise in a nonsearch task](../claims/eriksen-1974-incompatible-noise-slows-target-rt.md) [+W]
- [Target RT decreases as between-letter spacing increases, and the noise-condition effect is greatest at the closest spacing (Noise Condition by Spacing interaction)](../claims/eriksen-1974-spacing-decreases-rt-interaction.md) [+W]

- [Heterogeneous noise letters with set-related features produce intermediate RT impairment, consistent with stimulus generalization at wider spacings](../claims/eriksen-1974-similar-dissimilar-noise-intermediate.md) [+W]
