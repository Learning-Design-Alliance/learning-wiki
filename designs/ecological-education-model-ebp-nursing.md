# Ecological education model for evidence-based practice competency integrates educational ecology theory with the IEO framework

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article constructs a competency-oriented ecological education model grounded in educational ecology theory and Astin's input-environment-output (IEO) framework. Educational ecology theory, rooted in Bronfenbrenner's ecological systems perspective, guided the design of a multilayered educational ecosystem comprising physical, classroom, and practice environments. The IEO model operationalized students' prior competencies as input variables, the three-tier ecological environment as environmental variables, and evidence-based practice competency outcomes as output variables, informing a four-year progressive pathway from evidence-based awareness in Year 1 to comprehensive application in Year 4.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires institutional designation as a Best Practice Spotlight Organization (BPSO) to access evidence-based practice guidelines and resources
#### Constraints
- Developed and evaluated at a single institution (a double first-class university in Beijing, China) with resources that may exceed those of regional institutions

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate nursing students in a four-year Bachelor of Science in Nursing program

### Learning Goals
- Evidence-based practice competency including evidence seeking, appraisal, synthesis, and application
