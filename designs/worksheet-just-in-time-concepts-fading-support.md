# Worksheet-driven problem sequence with just-in-time concepts and fading support

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
This instructional pattern sequences learning as worksheets, each describing a simulated problem to solve in Alice, with concepts introduced only when needed and teacher support progressively withdrawn. "Each worksheet had a few concepts which would only be approached in the moment in which they would be necessary to the resolution of the problem." After the first problems, "the students solved new problems in which they would have to use the previous concepts but relying on less support by the teacher", culminating in a challenge solved without any help. Students demonstrating autonomy were invited to explore other approaches, while others continued with heavy teacher support until prepared for the next problems.
