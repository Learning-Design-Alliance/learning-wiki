# Adopt Connect Science without additional staffing, using existing classroom teachers trained through its professional development

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The program is designed to be delivered by regular classroom teachers rather than added personnel: the article states "No additional staffing requirements." Implementation instead depends on teacher preparation through the staged professional development sequence and provision of a manual, science materials kit, and trade books, making adoption a matter of teacher training and materials rather than new hires.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers complete the professional development sequence and receive the lesson manual and materials kit
#### Constraints
- Teachers will need computers to access the web for videos, resources etc.
