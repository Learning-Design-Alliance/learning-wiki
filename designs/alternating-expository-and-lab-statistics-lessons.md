# Alternating traditional expository lessons with computer-laboratory practical lessons in basic statistics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The study's didactic activities for basic Statistics were organized so that their application occurred in alternating lessons, combining traditional expository teaching with practical lessons in a computer laboratory, where students worked with software, model creation and simulation on everyday-focused content. The article describes "aulas expositivas tradicionais e aulas práticas em um Laboratório de Informática" as the main focus of characterization and reflection during the statistics teaching-learning process.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A computer laboratory with statistical software available for the practical lessons.
#### Constraints
- Described for basic statistics teaching in undergraduate courses at Campus Universitário de Rondonópolis/UFMT.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in courses offering Statistics (Informática, Matemática, Ciências Contábeis, Enfermagem)

### Learning Goals
- construction of statistical knowledge
- understanding statistics through everyday problems
