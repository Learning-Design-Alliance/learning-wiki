# Preregistered 10-week online QP pilot intervention trial with 75 UK high-school students and post-test evaluation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article details a preregistered intervention trial delivered online through weekly one-hour sessions with half an hour of self-study, spanning 10 weeks, evaluated with a post-test design. "A purposive sampling approach was employed to recruit 75 volunteer high-school students aged 16 to 19", selected from 734 applicants via random subsampling balanced on diversity criteria, with a goal of around 50 completers. Outcome variables focus on the number of problems students solve using QP, marked against a rubric covering methodology, correctness, conciseness, originality/creativity, evidence of understanding, and structuring/communication; analyses planned include ANOVA, hierarchical regressions, path analyses, multilevel modelling and exploratory factor analysis.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tutors with DBS checks and a one-hour training session; weekly tutor feedback forms; attendance at tutorials and completion of post-testing required for data inclusion.
#### Constraints
- Sample size was determined by the number of available tutors; the article notes more evidence will be needed with diverse control groups and larger samples.

### Target Learners
- volunteer UK high-school students aged 16 to 19

### Learning Goals
- solving quantum information problems using diagrammatic methods
- assessing changes in attitudes toward STEM careers
