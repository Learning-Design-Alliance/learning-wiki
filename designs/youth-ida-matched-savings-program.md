# Youth IDA program with matched savings for college tuition paired with a 1-week financial fitness camp

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A 5-year educational individual development account program run with U.S. Bank for 8th graders in three California Title I middle schools. Eligible students "save $10 a month that is matched by our program with $20 a month for 5 years," with accumulated savings transferred directly toward college tuition at an accredited college. An integral component was a 1-week financial fitness camp teaching the five Financial Fitness for Life themes plus a stock market simulation game, attended by 83 students, 59 of whom had IDAs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Income eligibility per U.S. Department of Health and Human Services criteria; students must attend at least one financial literacy camp over the 5-year period
#### Constraints
- No withdrawals are permitted from the account; non-college-enrolling participants may withdraw only their own savings plus interest, not the match

### Target Learners
- low-income 8th-grade students in Title I middle schools

### Learning Goals
- financial knowledge, saving for postsecondary education
