# Five-activity predict–observe–explain sequence moving from aligned-basis measurement to finite-sample statistics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The module's five activities follow a repeated structure: "Each activity uses a predict–observe–explain structure. Students first record a written prediction, then compare it with the simulator output, and finally write a brief explanation reconciling the two." Activities move from aligned-basis measurement (Activity 1), through global-phase labeling (2), incompatible-basis coherence versus mixture (3, the conceptual core), general basis exploration (4), to finite sampling and uncertainty (5). Written explanations are collected as the main assessment artifacts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Predictions are completed individually before simulator interaction
#### Constraints
- The ordered-operations extension is assigned only as a supplementary challenge so the main sequence remains focused on basis and probability

### Target Learners
- introductory undergraduate quantum mechanics students

### Learning Goals
- basis-aware measurement prediction
- separating sampling variability from state change
