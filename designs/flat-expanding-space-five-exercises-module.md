# Five-exercise instructional module on flat uniformly expanding space with complete solutions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A self-contained set of five exercises with solutions that lets advanced undergraduates explore a flat, uniformly expanding space: the model and photon trajectory solution, numerical trajectories, the Hubble horizon, a small-t expansion, and clock synchronization. The author states the "Exercises with solutions are presented which should allow advanced undergraduate students to understand properties of a flat, uniformly expanding space". The material "could be used stand alone or would fit well at the end of a treatment of special relativity".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Only prerequisite knowledge stated is that the speed of light c is a constant; no knowledge of general or special relativity beyond that is needed
#### Constraints
- The author states the exercises aim at qualitative understanding and cannot yield quantitatively relevant estimates of physical observables
- Transformations to other coordinate systems are not considered; the model is not invariant under general coordinate transformations

### Target Learners
- advanced undergraduate physics students

### Learning Goals
- understand properties of a flat, uniformly expanding space
- resolve the apparent conflict between superluminal Hubble separation and special relativity
- understand clock synchronization in an expanding space
