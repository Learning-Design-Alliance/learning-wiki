# Miller and Moyers' eight stages of MI training as the organising framework for the online course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article adopts Miller and Moyers' staged account of developing MI competence, in which training "should ideally incorporate eight stages across two phases (phase 1: enhancing clients' motivation for change; phase 2: strengthening and consolidating commitment to change)". The online MI course is structured directly on these "eight stages of MI training": the Spirit of MI, OARS client-centred skills, recognising and reinforcing change talk, eliciting and strengthening change talk, rolling with resistance, developing a change plan, consolidating client commitment, and switching between MI and other approaches. The staged approach aims to embed MI skills and support their maintenance in future practice.
