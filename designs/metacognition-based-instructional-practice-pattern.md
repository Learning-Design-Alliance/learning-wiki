# Metacognition-based instructional practice combining reflective tasks, self-reflection and concept mapping

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A ten-week instructional pattern implemented in a Testing and Evaluation in Education course, built on the notion of cognition embedded with metacognition, with in-class and out-of-class activities and tasks. It includes "tasks towards learning the learning and thinking about the thinking", reflective questions about prospective teachers' own performances, and "a conceptual mapping , which is suggested as a metacognitive skill by Daley [33] has also been included in the process". Planning, observing and evaluating stages of metacognitive regulation were observed during the activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants share experiences prior to, during, and following the tasks; activities target defining, explaining, giving examples, interpreting, guessing, applying, summarizing, arguing, contacting and inquiring
#### Constraints
- Was implemented over ten weeks in a single course; the authors note improving adults' metacognitive skills takes time and metacognition should be planned together with the teaching program and extended over a long period

### Target Learners
- 3rd-year prospective teachers at a department of Turkish language education

### Learning Goals
- metacognitive awareness, planning, questioning, awareness of learning strategies
