# Modular ultrasound curriculum: introductory module plus application-specific modules with practical examinations

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's proposed curriculum pattern sequences ultrasound training as an 'introductory' module covering fundamental knowledge and proficiency, followed by one or several application-specific modules (RUSI, diagnostic or interventional). Each module combines didactic and/or practical instruction and 'each culminating in a practical examination of safety, technical aspects, and image generation and interpretation competence'. The modular structure allows flexibility for adding future US applications and can be supplemented with mentorship, supervision and case-based examination.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Modules must culminate in practical examinations of safety, technical aspects, and image generation and interpretation competence
- Training should, where possible, involve other imaging disciplines and focus on pathologies physical therapists treat
#### Constraints
- Learning outcomes vary by operator skill level (introductory vs advanced) and application

### Target Learners
- physical therapists in postgraduate continuing education

### Learning Goals
- image generation and optimisation
- image interpretation
- safe US practice
