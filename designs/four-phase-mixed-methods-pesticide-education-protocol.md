# A four-phase mixed-methods trial protocol combining cross-sectional KAP assessment, qualitative interviews, module development, and a two-arm cluster-randomized controlled trial

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This protocol structures evaluation of a need-based educational intervention in four phases over three years: an analytical cross-sectional study of 175 farmers, qualitative interviews, development and piloting of the educational video, and a two-arm, single-blind, parallel-group RCT with villages randomized 1:1. Outcomes are KAP scores and SF-36 quality-of-life scores, correlated with laboratory parameters, analysed with paired t-tests, Wilcoxon Signed Ranks Tests, and intention-to-treat analysis.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Ethics approval (IEC1: 25/2023, Kasturba Medical College, Manipal), CTRI registration (CTRI/2024/06/069678), SPIRIT 2025 and CONSORT 2025 compliance, stratification by age and education level
#### Constraints
- Single-blinded only (participants blinded); outcome assessors and analysts unblinded because it is an educational interventional study; conducted in three Karnataka districts

### Target Learners
- Farmers actively engaged in regular pesticide spraying in Udupi, Mysuru, and Shivamogga districts

### Learning Goals
- Improved knowledge, attitudes, and practices regarding pesticide safety, with quality-of-life correlates
