# Numbers in Nappies programme

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size

## Description
A parent-delivered early childhood development programme developed for this study, using flashcards of numbers, shapes and colours shown to infants for approximately 2–3 seconds each. The article states the programme "is applied on a daily basis, two to three times a day, for approximately 1 min per session, over an average of 60 days", with stimuli presented visually via flashcards and auditorily through the parent's voice. It avoids technology and promotes bonding in a positive learning environment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Parent or primary caregiver delivery through direct daily communication, with training provided to parents before implementation
#### Constraints
- Developed as part of the present study and therefore not previously evaluated; evaluated only in a middle-income-sector sample of infants without developmental problems

### Target Learners
- infants aged 3–12 months

### Learning Goals
- number concepts including addition, subtraction, multiplication, greater than and less than
- shape and colour recognition
- memory, problem solving, classification and attention maintenance
