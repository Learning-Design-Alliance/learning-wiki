# Two types of biographic material study: detailed biographies and fragmentary biographical information

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes that study of biographic materials can take two forms: "first, the detailed biographies of individual scientists and, secondly, the fragmentary biographical information". Detailed biographies suit extracurricular activities such as learners' conferences and seminars, while fragmentary information fits limited lesson time. A biography is treated not as a chronological set of dates but as a "biography" of thoughts, words and actions against the scientist's socio-political context.
