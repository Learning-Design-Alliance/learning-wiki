# Lettuce footprint spreadsheet computation released as supplementary material

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article releases a spreadsheet with the lettuce carbon footprint computation as supplementary material. It records the breakdown of individual processes for a conventionally grown head of lettuce as it leaves the farm and as it arrives prepared on the dinner table, with comments and citations of sources included so the class could check its work and reuse the spreadsheet as a reference for future footprints.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Used alongside class discussion defining the footprint scope and identifying needed data
#### Constraints
- The computation was customized to lettuce grown conventionally, since 80% of lettuce consumed is conventionally grown

### Target Learners
- college students learning process-based carbon footprinting

### Learning Goals
- building a multi-process life cycle carbon footprint in a spreadsheet
