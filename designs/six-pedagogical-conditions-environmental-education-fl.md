# Six pedagogical conditions for environmental education of senior pupils through a foreign language

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article organizes its forming experiment around six conditions: accounting for age peculiarities, updating the axiological core content of the foreign-language course, didactically justified selection of material, creative use of foreign teaching materials, use of technologies forming intellectual communicative activity, and integrating curricular and extracurricular work. The first condition holds that effectiveness requires "to take into account the age peculiarities of the students of school age". The conditions were implemented together in experimental classes.
