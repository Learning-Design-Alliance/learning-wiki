# Learning path sequencing measurement and observable before state and superposition to reduce cognitive load

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course sequences linear polarization, measurement, system quantity, state, vector, superposition, interference, and entanglement, deliberately postponing the quantum state. This "allows us to focus on the revision of a notion at a time" and reduces cognitive load in math modelling activities, since the state of polarization is presented as a change of perspective on already-discussed phenomena. The path is organized into four units.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a framework for discussing quantum measurement and observables without resorting to the concept of state
#### Constraints
- Restricted to superposition states with real coefficients; no bra vectors or complex numbers introduced in the polarization context

### Target Learners
- secondary school students learning quantum mechanics

### Learning Goals
- revise one notion at a time
- manage cognitive load during mathematical modelling
