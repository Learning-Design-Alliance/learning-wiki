# Mini-games as brief, scope-constrained, feedback-rich activities for focused L2 practice

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article relies on the notion of mini-games, defined as gameful activities that can be completed in brief sessions, are constrained in scope, provide consistent feedback, and thus lend themselves well to focused practice. In the study, mini-games were embedded in a mystery story read in class, first in a tutorial version without time pressure or rewards and then in a full version with time pressure, rewarding, between-learner competition, and vivid CF adapted to the game fantasy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Consistent feedback provision within the game activity
#### Constraints
- Observation of learners in practice suggests that they were treating the practice tasks rather mechanically

### Target Learners
- Dutch-speaking secondary education learners of English

### Learning Goals
- English quantifiers and the double object construction
