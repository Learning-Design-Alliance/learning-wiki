# UNSW outcomes-based integrated medical program structured on eight educational outcomes

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In 2004 UNSW Faculty of Medicine replaced its content-based curriculum with a 6-year undergraduate entry outcomes-based integrated program, "structured around the explicit development in students of eight educational outcomes": five generic capabilities (effective communication, teamwork, self direction, ethical practice, reflective practice) and three discipline-specific capabilities (scientific basis of medicine, social aspects of health, patient assessment and management). Its novel assessment system "drives learning in, and measures achievement of all eight educational outcomes".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The program's design must start from a wider scope of desired graduate capabilities, and its assessment system must drive learning in and measure achievement of all eight educational outcomes.
#### Constraints
- Outcomes-based programs are fundamentally different from other integrated programs in their primary emphasis on achievement of defined learning outcomes, rather than adherence to a particular learning process.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate-entry medical students in a 6-year medical program
