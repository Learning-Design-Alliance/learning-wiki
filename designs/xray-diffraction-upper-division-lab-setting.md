# Upper-division X-ray diffraction laboratory experiment as the setting for studying group dynamics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The setting is an upper division laboratory course at a US university where students "usually spend three, three-hour lab meetings on a single experiment" and must produce individual lab reports. The studied group of three second-year physics students worked on the X-ray diffraction experiment, figuring out how to operate the X-ray apparatus with different crystals and collecting data to ascertain crystals' structures. This setting provided the video data for the inchargeness analysis.
