# Boundary conditions instead of initial conditions to reinforce the PLA framework

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
All three simulations share a common characteristic: they ask the user for boundary conditions instead of initial conditions, thus reinforcing the difference between the PLA and Classical Newtonian Mechanics. In Classical Newtonian Mechanics, initial position and velocity are given. In the Action Principle, boundary conditions — the endpoints of the trajectory — are given instead. This design choice is a deliberate pedagogical pattern across all three simulations.
