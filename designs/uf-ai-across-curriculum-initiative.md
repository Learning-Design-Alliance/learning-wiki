# University of Florida AI Across the Curriculum initiative and HiPerGator infrastructure

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The paper describes the University of Florida's systemic embedding of AI literacy across academic programmes, supported by the HiPerGator supercomputer. Its pillars include AI Medallion Programs, external stakeholder partnerships, curriculum development through a dedicated AI academic initiative centre (AI2), the five-category UF AI Literacy Model (Enabling AI, Know & Understand AI, Use & Apply AI, Evaluate and Create AI, AI Ethics), and infrastructure investment totalling $120.4 million through the planned 2025 upgrade (Table 4).
