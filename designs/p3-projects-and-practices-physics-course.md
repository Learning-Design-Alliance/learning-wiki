# P3 (Projects and Practices in Physics) introductory mechanics course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
P3 is a transformed, introductory, calculus-based mechanics course for science and engineering majors grounded in evidence-based pedagogy and built on Matter & Interactions. The article states "students will solve one of these complex, real-world problems in each two-hour class meeting (twice per week)", with roughly 30 such problems across the semester, 7 of them computational modeling problems. Students negotiate approaches in groups of 4 with minimally facilitated discussion, present solutions to tutors, and the course is organized around conservation of momentum, energy, and angular momentum.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructional staff positioned as facilitators; groups of 4 with minimally facilitated discussion; tutors probe solutions with tutoring questions.
#### Constraints
- The article states full adoption "might be challenging in diﬀerent contexts" and that the separation of lecture and lab at MSU meant students could not work with laboratory equipment in lecture.

### Target Learners
- science and engineering majors in introductory calculus-based mechanics

### Learning Goals
- developing and using models
- using mathematics and computational thinking
- group problem solving and communication
