# Embedded Tier 2 intervention: HMH Journeys Strategic Intervention plus Curious about Words

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A Tier 2 intervention drawn from the core reading program HMH Journeys: Strategic Intervention, which "is aligned with Tier 1 (i.e., classroom) content and provides students with explicit and systematic instruction in phonemic awareness, phonics, ﬂuency, comprehension, and vocabulary through scripted lessons", plus Curious about Words, a vocabulary supplement introducing eight to 12 new words weekly through read-alouds, discussion, graphic organizers, and partner activities. Unlike the stand-alone materials, it had no implementation manual, so REL Southeast staff developed training materials covering scope and sequence and instructional procedures.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- REL Southeast-developed training materials on scope and sequence and instructional procedures, since the embedded materials had no implementation manual
- Two-day interventionist training plus ongoing support and monthly audio-recorded lesson review
#### Constraints
- Strategic Intervention does not include specific provisions for remediation despite including a skills assessment every 10 lessons

### Target Learners
- K–2 students below the 30th percentile in vocabulary and reading-related skills in schools using the HMH Journeys core program

### Learning Goals
- Phonemic awareness, phonics, fluency, comprehension, vocabulary, and listening and speaking vocabulary
