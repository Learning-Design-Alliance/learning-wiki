# Introduce QIST early through an interactive, low-prerequisite introductory course open to students from multiple majors

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends introducing QIST as early as possible because early introduction promotes interest and lets students adjust their academic paths. Participants "felt that the most eﬀ ective introduction to QIST at the lower-division level would be achieved through a new course oﬀ ering" with only college algebra as prerequisite, additional math taught in-course. The article states "interactive engagement is critical and highly appropriate for this type of course," with teamwork and project-based elements building collaboration soft skills valued by industry.

- Prerequisites must be minimal so the course is open to multiple majors and backgrounds
