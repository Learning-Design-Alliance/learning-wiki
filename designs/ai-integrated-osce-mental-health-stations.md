# AI-integrated objective structured clinical examination with three simulation-based stations plus care-plan writing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An OSCE structure embedding the AI-simulated patient across stations: a live voice interaction assessing therapeutic communication with a structured rubric, a viva voce on clinical presentation and management, and a voice-only guided full mental status examination using behavioral cues such as "fidgets with hands" and "avoids eye contact". Students received 5 minutes of preparation per randomly assigned case and 30 minutes afterward to record findings and formulate a nursing care plan. The authors present this as ensuring assessment consistency.

### Context
#### Requirements
- A pool of prepared case scenarios containing presenting problem, history, diagnosis, medications, and risk factors

- The current implementation included no formal validation, reliability testing, or outcome comparisons
