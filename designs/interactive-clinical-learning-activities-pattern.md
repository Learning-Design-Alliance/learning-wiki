# Interactive clinical learning pattern combining e-learning, flipped classroom, group activities, simulation and preceptorship

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
This pattern bundles the interactive learning activities the article implemented for intern nurses during a 12-month internship: "e-learning, flipped classroom, group learning activities, simulation and preceptorship were included as interactive learning activities". It replaces classroom-based lecturing alone and is designed to shift students "from passive to active participants" during clinical training. The article applies it as a clinical learning approach for Saudi nursing interns.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires learning environments that encourage critical thinking and educator support
- Access to electronic resources for the e-learning components
#### Constraints
- Successful e-learning implementation can be obstructed by restricted access to computing facilities, per the discussion citing Barteit et al.

### Target Learners
- Saudi intern nurses in their fifth year / 12-month National Internship Program

### Learning Goals
- Personal development factors (caring, enthusiasm, initiative, confidence, cooperation)
- Professional development factors (theoretical knowledge, critical thinking, clinical competence, documentation)
