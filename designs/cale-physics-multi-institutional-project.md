# Applied example: the Characterizing Active Learning Environments in Physics multi-institutional data collection project

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's applied example is a national project comparing the implementations and impacts of four distinct active learning methods for introductory physics and astronomy. "Data were collected from 31 total instructors at 28 institutions over the course of three semesters: fall 2023, spring 2024, and fall 2024," using pre/post concept inventories, social network surveys, and classroom video observations, all coordinated via email without site visits, under project-wide IRB approval with a $1,000 instructor incentive.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Recruitment began shortly after IRB approval in spring 2022, about 1.5 years before data collection started
#### Constraints
- Only one instructor requested a separate IRB protocol, and there was not enough time to prepare it, so that instructor did not participate

### Target Learners
- Introductory physics and astronomy instructors and their students at U.S. colleges and universities

### Learning Goals
- Characterizing implementations and learning impacts of four active learning methods
