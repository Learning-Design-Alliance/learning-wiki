# Electronic textbook and model curriculum for optimizing swimming instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
An instructional element the authors developed alongside their exercise sets: an electronic textbook plus a model curriculum and practical training programs, intended "to optimize the process of teaching and sports training" in swimming. It was used in the pedagogical experiment as part of the experimental program, and the study led the authors to amend the typical program for the discipline "Swimming with the methodology of teaching."
