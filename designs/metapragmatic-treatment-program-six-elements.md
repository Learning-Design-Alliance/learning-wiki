# Ten-session explicit metapragmatic treatment program with six instructional elements

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The treatment consisted of ten classroom sessions of 30 minutes each, developed from deviations found in learners' questionnaire answers. The article states "These materials consist of six different elements": explicit explanation of request behaviors, information sheets, role-play activities, pair-work discussions of appropriateness, listening to native-speaker dialogues, and classroom discussions of how requests are realized in English. Sessions began with teacher-fronted discussion of how one utterance conveys different meanings in context.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learners work with researcher-prepared informative papers and materials based on available literature on request speech acts
- Sessions include role plays, pair work, listening to native-speaker dialogues, and classroom discussion
#### Constraints
- Studied only with intermediate-level, above-18, Persian-speaking learners at institute level over ten 30-minute sessions

### Target Learners
- intermediate EFL learners at institute level

### Learning Goals
- awareness and appropriate use of the request speech act in English
