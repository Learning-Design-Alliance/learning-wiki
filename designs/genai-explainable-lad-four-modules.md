# GenAI-supported explainable learning analytics dashboard with four SRL-grounded modules

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A learning analytics dashboard for human-AI collaborative academic abstract writing with four modules: preparation (radar-chart target setting), writing progress monitoring (bar charts scored by a backend DeepSeek-R1 model), reflection (scores overlaid with initial targets), and AI dialogue quality monitoring. The dashboard is "specifically designed to support students in human -AI collaborative academic writing tasks" and was evaluated with thirty-nine randomly assigned university students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a backend large language model (DeepSeek-R1) with task-specific prompts to automate progress evaluation and scoring
- Requires a source paper with its abstract removed so students co-write the abstract with a GenAI
#### Constraints
- Evaluated with non-native English-speaking university students on a single academic abstract writing task

### Target Learners
- non-native English-speaking university students

### Learning Goals
- academic abstract writing
- self-regulated learning in writing
