# Multi-phase professional learning structure: summer institute, school-year follow-ups, annual reunions, and pre-program needs assessment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Academy's design runs "for three and a half days in August, followed by three additional full-day follow-up sessions later in the school year, plus annual two-day follow-up seminars ("reunions") for prior Academy participants." Sessions are highly interactive and customized: prior to the Academy, participants are asked what their biggest issues are and what they want addressed, informing coverage of current topics such as transition planning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pre-program collection of participant issues to customize content
- Ongoing sponsorship to fund follow-up sessions and reunions across years
#### Constraints
- Designed for a small cohort; annual attendance averages between 15 and 20 participants

### Target Learners
- Experienced special education directors

### Learning Goals
- Updating legal and leadership knowledge
- Sustaining peer support networks over time

### Claims
- [In a recent survey, 100 percent of polled Academy graduates said they would recommend participation to a colleague](../claims/academy-graduates-recommend-survey.md) [+W] — satisfaction, not a test of the structure
- [Academy graduates report feeling more empowered and staying on the job longer](../claims/academy-graduates-empowered-retention.md) [+W]
