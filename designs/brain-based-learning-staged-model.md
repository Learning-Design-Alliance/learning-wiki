# Brain Based Learning as a staged learning model aligned with how the brain naturally learns

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The article defines brain based learning as "a learning process that is aligned with a brain designed naturally to learn" (Jensen, 2008). In this study it was implemented as a sequence of stages — pre-explanation, preparation, initiation and acquisition, elaboration, incubation, verification, and integration — applied to polyhedron instruction in the experimental class. Each stage pairs a brain-oriented activity (mind mapping, group discussion, music and brain games, celebration) with conceptual-understanding goals.
