# Reciprocal teaching implementation sequence with prompt cards and rotating leader roles

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article operationalises reciprocal teaching as a nine-step weekly cycle over 8 expository texts: explicit teaching of the four stages with a written prompt worksheet, teacher modelling by reading aloud and thinking aloud at the start of each week, gradual transfer of responsibility, small groups of 4-5 members, and rotating leader and participant roles. The leader manages discussion using prompt cards asking questions such as "What is the main idea of the text?" while participants fill in their own prompt cards, and the teacher circulates, gives feedback and takes the participant role in each group.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher modelling repeated at the beginning of every week; written prompt worksheets and prompt cards; small groups of 4-5 members
#### Constraints
- The teacher reported initial student difficulty understanding the strategy, which repeated modelling reduced

### Target Learners
- fourth-grade primary school students

### Learning Goals
- reading comprehension of expository texts through predicting, clarifying, questioning and summarizing
