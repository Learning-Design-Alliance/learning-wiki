# Conversation partner activity: an out-of-class naturalistic small-group activity with an advanced ESL conversation leader

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The conversation partner activity is an out-of-classroom Program in Intensive English activity in which learners meet without a teacher but are "accompanied by a proficient speaker of English as a second language even if a formal t eacher is not present in the group". In this study one MA TESOL student with native-like proficiency, assigned by the PIE director, led a group of three learners of mixed L1 background. Conversations were recorded over three weeks and followed by tailor-made review tests of 3-5 questions, so the activity doubles as a natural setting for observing interactional feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A conversation leader who is an advanced or near-native proficient English speaker, in this study an MA TESOL student with a high TOEFL score
#### Constraints
- As an outdoor activity, recordings were made on the move or in public places and were at times unclear or filled with background noise; visiting members dominated conversations and limited interaction time for the observed partners

### Target Learners
- international intensive English program students of mixed L1 background

### Learning Goals
- second language interaction and uptake of feedback on grammar, vocabulary and pronunciation
