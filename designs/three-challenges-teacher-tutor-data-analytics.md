# Three teacher-derived design challenges for intelligent tutor analytics: summarizing engagement, identifying step-level issues, and comparing performance over time

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
From a formative focus group with five teachers, affinity diagramming plus prior work yielded three design challenges shaping the analytics system: C1 summarizing student engagement in terms of problems attempted and aggregate accuracy; C2 identifying specific issues at each problem-solving step or knowledge component; and C3 comparing students' performance over time, since teachers observed “a correlation between how much time [students] are spending on practicing the homework and their grade”. Each VisTA view maps explicitly to one or more of these challenges.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Analytics must let teachers quickly identify which students engage with the tutor and evaluate accuracy, see step-wise provenance across all students, and visualize total time spent plus temporal breakdowns of each step
#### Constraints
- Derived from five teachers at one state university who had deployed the tutors for at least a semester

### Target Learners
- Teachers integrating intelligent tutors into classroom instruction

### Learning Goals
- Responsive pedagogy: adjusting classroom instruction to individual student needs
