# Three role-integration design principles: integrated data governance, context-driven role switching, and synergistic human-AI collaboration

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article specifies three paramount design principles for orchestrating multiple AI roles into a cohesive whole. "Permissions and Data Sharing form the ethical backbone of the framework," implemented through Role-Based Access Control so the Teacher Assistant role provides detailed analytics while the Parent Advisor role generates aggregated, non-identifiable summaries. Context-driven adaptability operates on two levels: explicit role switching via pre-defined triggers and implicit stylistic adaptation that recalibrates scaffolding in real time. Synergistic human-AI collaboration requires communication protocols so insights from one role, such as the Student Peer mode, inform another, such as the Teacher Assistant interface.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Robust Role-Based Access Control and privacy-preserving data protocols compliant with regulations such as GDPR and FERPA
- Clear, pre-defined triggers for explicit role switching
- Interface loops designed to manage the interplay of roles, users, activities, and data flows
#### Constraints
- Stated for the proposed framework's orchestration of roles, not empirically tested in the study

### Target Learners
- Elementary school students
- Teachers
- Parents

### Learning Goals
