# Decreasing-support grouping sequence across a task week

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study's tasks were administered with the level of student support decreasing each day, moving through group configurations of total class participation (TL), small group (SG), partners (P), and individual (I) work. This graduated release of support pairs a graphic organizer with progressively more independent grouping formats across a week of non-routine tasks. Groups of four were composed of one high, two average, and one low student based on prior-year state assessment scores.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Groups formed with heterogeneous ability composition based on prior assessment scores
#### Constraints
- Studied in sixth-grade geometry over nine weeks with the same teacher for both classes

### Target Learners
- sixth-grade middle school mathematics students

### Learning Goals
- initiating and completing non-routine problem solutions
- geometry concepts
