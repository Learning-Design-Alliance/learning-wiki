# A seven-week, 21-hour personalized Evidence-Based Practice course for healthcare professionals

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
An interactive EBP course delivered weekly over seven weeks in three-hour sessions, totaling 21 hours, guided by a meta-analysis indicating that "programs with a total duration of 20–24 h, delivered over four to seven weeks, yielded the most significant improvements in EBP skills". The syllabus covers EBP definition, search strategies (PICO, Boolean operators), scientific databases, review and epidemiological study types, quality of evidence, and the PET process. Participants scoring at least 70% on assessments and attending at least 70% of sessions received a certificate.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants hold higher education degrees, have worked at the hospital at least six months, and have never previously participated in an EBP course; a minimum 70% attendance is required.
#### Constraints
- The intervention was conducted in a single private hospital, and the authors caution about generalizing to public or academic settings.

### Target Learners
- healthcare professionals (nurses, physicians, pharmacists) in a hospital setting

### Learning Goals
- evidence-based practice competencies: formulating clinical questions, searching literature, appraising evidence, and applying evidence to clinical practice
