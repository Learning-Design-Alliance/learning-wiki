# Cognitive Conflict-Based Generative Learning Model (GLBCC)

## Description
The GLBCC is a constructivist instructional model that integrates cognitive conflict strategies with generative learning principles. It rests on the premise that "when students encounter information contradicting their existing physics concepts, the resulting psychological disequilibrium motivates them to restructure their understanding, leading to conceptual change and deeper learning". The model uses authentic case studies from natural phenomena and consists of six syntaxes: orientation, cognitive conflict, disclosure, construct, application, and reflective evaluation. In this study it was implemented as the experimental treatment in high school physics.
