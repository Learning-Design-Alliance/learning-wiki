# Combined case-based and problem-based learning delivery for a neurosurgery clinical course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The neurosurgery course in this study was delivered to both groups using a combination of case-based learning (CBL) and problem-based learning (PBL). Before class the teacher selected a representative case, compiled questions on key concepts, and uploaded a PPT; students were divided into five groups and consulted textbooks, websites, and literature to answer the questions. During class the teacher introduced the content, presented the case, guided group discussions, summarized results, expanded on the knowledge, and assigned critical thinking questions, with post-class answers and explanations posted online. Both groups used the same cases and PowerPoint to standardize the comparison.
