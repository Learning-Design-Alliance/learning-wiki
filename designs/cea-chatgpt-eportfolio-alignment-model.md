# The CEA model: ChatGPT aligned with e-portfolio assessment as an integrated EFL learning framework

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The CEA model integrates ChatGPT with e-portfolio assessment so that AI-generated individualized feedback and portfolio-based documentation, peer feedback, and reflection operate as one learning ecosystem. In the study, "Padlet as the primary e-portfolio environment and ChatGPT as the individualized feedback system represented the entire practical framework of the CEA model." Students practiced speaking with ChatGPT voice-mode interaction, recorded video performances, uploaded them to Padlet, exchanged peer comments, and wrote self-reflections via Google Form. The model is grounded in social cognitive, constructivist, and connectivist theories and was designed to cultivate critical assessment of AI feedback, since AI feedback cannot be assumed universally reliable.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Structured pedagogical integration: students must be taught how to interact with ChatGPT (e.g., voice-mode interaction) and to use the e-portfolio for feedback, reflection, and archiving, rather than having unrestricted access
#### Constraints
- The control condition in the study was traditional e-portfolio practice without structured AI integration, not a completely AI-free group; the researchers could not rule out independent out-of-class AI access
- The authors identify the differing affordances of Padlet versus Google Drive as a possible confounding variable

### Target Learners
- first-semester vocational college EFL students (ages 18-20)

### Learning Goals
- EFL speaking performance (fluency, accuracy, complexity)
- feedback literacy
