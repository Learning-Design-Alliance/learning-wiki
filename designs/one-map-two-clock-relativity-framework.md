# One-map two-clock relativity: a teaching framework using coordinate and proper time/velocity pairs defined on a single map frame

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The framework defines two time variables, map-time t and traveler proper-time τ, for a single traveler described with respect to one inertial map frame, giving two velocities: coordinate-velocity and proper-velocity. The paper states proper-velocity measures "map-distance traveled per unit traveler time" and that w = γv. This lets relativistic momentum, energy, and time dilation be taught without Lorentz transforms or multiple inertial frames.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All distances must be measured with respect to a single inertial map frame, per the paper's cardinal rule
#### Constraints
- The paper notes the full (3+1)D acceleration development is too complicated for an introductory class, so simplified unidirectional cases are used

### Target Learners
- high school physics students
- college introductory physics students

### Learning Goals
- understanding special relativity concepts such as time dilation, momentum, and energy before mastering Lorentz transforms
