# E-learning course on the use of EORTC PRO measures in oncological practice (eight modular topics with mixed presentation formats)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A web-based e-learning course under development to support health care professionals in the routine use of EORTC PRO assessments in oncology, designed via a user-centred, participatory approach following the CeHRes Roadmap. Its final planned structure (Table 3) comprises eight modules — from "Basic information on PROs in clinical routine" to "Self-management recommendations for patients based on PROs" — each combining written information and graphics, with topic-specific practical examples, interactive case vignettes for communication, and "Interactive components to enhance knowledge-practice transfer"; blended learning, certification and tests of learning progress are to be considered.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Digital training tools must take "the clinical realities of limited human resources, time and funding into account"; a participatory approach during each stage of development is recommended to maximise acceptance.
#### Constraints
- The article notes awareness of the limitations of e-learning, including individual or structural barriers for its use.

### Target Learners
- Oncology health care professionals (physicians, nurses, psycho-oncologists, allied health professionals, IT specialists)

### Learning Goals
- Competences in using EORTC PRO measures in clinical routine: interpreting PRO data, integrating PROs into patient communication, PRO-based clinical actions, and implementing routine PRO assessment
