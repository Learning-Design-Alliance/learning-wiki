# Model of using the CoCalc cloud service as a tool for forming mathematics teachers' professional competencies

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A model for using the CoCalc cloud service as a tool for forming mathematics teachers' professional competencies. The model "covers three stages of the formation of p rofessional competencies: propaedeutic, forming and developmental" and comprises target, stimulating-motivational, content, operational-activity and valuation-regulatory components, each assessed at high, sufficient, average and low levels.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Built on the designed system of professional competencies of mathematics teachers, with each implementation stage closely linked to the basic mathematical disciplines studied by undergraduate students
#### Constraints
- Developed for pre-service mathematics teachers in Ukrainian pedagogical universities, in a context where foreign cloud-based mathematical services were found non-adaptable to the requirements of national educational standards

### Target Learners
- pre-service mathematics teachers at pedagogical universities

### Learning Goals
- formation of general and special professional competencies for teaching mathematics
