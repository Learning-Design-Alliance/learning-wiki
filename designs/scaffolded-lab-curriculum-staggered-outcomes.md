# Scaffolded lab curriculum that staggers learning outcomes and gradually reduces instructional guidance

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The curriculum staggers the introduction of new learning outcomes in each lab activity (up to 11), so students encounter a manageable number per session, and sequences more complex cognitive tasks after foundational skills are gained. Guidance is reduced over time: "While students receive substantial guidance through the use of probing questions at the onset of the course, this guidance gradually diminishes as students become proficient in several scientific skills and progress through the courses." The pattern culminates in student projects fostering independence and self-directed learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Specific, measurable, fine-grained learning outcomes (about 30-40 skills per experimental activity) defined in advance so practice activities can be scaffolded
#### Constraints
- The scaffolding strategy decreased the variety of experiments and physics topics covered compared with the rotating pre-transformation structure

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics major students in their first four semesters

### Learning Goals
- acquisition of scientific experimental skills
- progression toward independent experimental design
