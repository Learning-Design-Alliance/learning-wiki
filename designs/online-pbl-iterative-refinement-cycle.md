# Iterative action-research refinement of fully online PBL from static triggers to a collaborative virtual learning environment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
This pattern describes the article's own five-version evolution of fully online PBL between 2014 and 2020. Version 1 delivered static "text only" triggers with no peer-to-peer interaction, and by version 5 the design used "a fully integrated virtual learning environment using the LMS and collaborative peer‐tutor option", with clear online discussion protocols and a shift to group assessment. The teaching team used reflection and observation within an action research process, driven by student feedback, to refine delivery each year.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a learning management system with synchronous/collaborative tools (Blackboard Collaborate) for online peer and tutor interaction
#### Constraints
- Fully online students still reported difficulties with time zones, small or uncommunicative groups, technology issues and feeling disconnected from the campus despite the refinements

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate health science students studying fully online

### Learning Goals
- Critical thinking, self-directed research, academic writing, communication and collaboration skills
