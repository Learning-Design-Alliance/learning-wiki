# Sequence a mass-spring experiment immediately after the pendulum to revise techniques in a new context within a coherent theme

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course is organized as a two-experiment path within one physical theme. The article states: "Setting the mass-spring experiment immediately after the pendulum highlights the question of resonance, revises the gap between theory and practice in another context, and provides another occasion to practice further techniques in data analysis." The mass-spring sessions revisit idealization (zero-mass spring), straight-line fitting, damping, and add the resonance curve and its FWHM.
