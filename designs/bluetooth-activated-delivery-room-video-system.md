# Bluetooth-activated ceiling camera system for recording real-life obstetric emergencies

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A video-capture setup installed in delivery rooms at two Danish hospitals, using two to three mini dome surveillance cameras and a ceiling microphone, recording in continuous 5-minute loops. The system was "activated using Bluetooth on the on‐call obstetrician's phone to minimize the recordings of normal deliveries", saving the preceding 5 minutes and all subsequent time. Videos were deleted unless all participants consented within 48 hours.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Consent from women, partners, and all healthcare providers within 48 h, otherwise videos are automatically deleted from the server
#### Constraints
- In some instances the view was obscured by working personnel, limiting assessment of some checklist items

### Target Learners
- obstetric teams and researchers analyzing real-life emergency team performance

### Learning Goals
- identifying technical and non-technical skill challenges in real-life emergency management for training and debriefing
