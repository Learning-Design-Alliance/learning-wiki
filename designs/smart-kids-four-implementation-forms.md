# Four forms of implementation of Smart Kids technology scaled to school facilities and teacher ICT qualification

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes four forms of implementing Smart Kids technology: "Smart Case, Smart Teacher, Smart Class, and Smart Kids". Each form pairs an aim (stimulating learning activity, ubiquitous access, or developing an individual pupil's trajectory) with a work format (teamwork, frontal, individual, or group) and a required equipment set, from a teacher's briefcase with E-textbooks/EEGR, projector and interactive whiteboard up to tablets for each pupil, a teacher's virtual office and pupils' home computers. The forms were designed considering the facilities of every school and the ICT qualification of the teacher.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Necessary equipment per form, e.g. teacher's briefcase with E-textbooks/EEGR, projector, interactive whiteboard, teacher's computer, and for fuller forms tablets for each pupil and a teacher's virtual office
#### Constraints
- The form chosen depends on the facilities of the school and the level of ICT qualification of the elementary school teacher
