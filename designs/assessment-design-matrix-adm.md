# Assessment Design Matrix (ADM) linking graduate competencies, assessment strategies, and assessment methods

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The ADM is a tripartite structure that integrates graduate competencies, assessment strategies, and assessment methods into a practical guide for assessment designers. The article states it "provides a practical, hands-on guide for assessment designers to craft integrity-focused assessments that assess ICT graduate competencies", with competencies informing strategy selection, strategies guiding method choice, and methods generating evidence of competency attainment.
