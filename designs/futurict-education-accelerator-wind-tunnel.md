# FuturICT Education Accelerator: a 'wind tunnel' for testing educational theory with an international virtual campus

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Education Accelerator is the paper's proposed instrument for translating FuturICT's complexity, social and computing sciences into educational practice. It is described as a testing environment — "a 'wind tunnel' for testing these ideas in the context of real educational programmes" — with an international virtual campus delivering complex systems education. It is intended to produce practical analytical and computational tools for researchers, practitioners and leaders, generative principles for resilient educational ecosystems, and innovation in scalable yet personalised learner engagement and assessment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Close dialogue with educational theory and practice, and grounding in the emerging data science and learning analytics paradigms
#### Constraints
- Short-term focus is on tertiary and postgraduate interdisciplinary complex systems science; the paper argues that if the challenges cannot be met in this benign context they are unlikely to be met in primary and secondary education

### Target Learners
- tertiary and postgraduate complex systems students in the short term; all levels in the longer term

### Learning Goals
- interdisciplinary complex systems science competence; more effective and lower-cost learning generally
