# Compound Line Graph lesson (Jiangsu Education Edition, Grade 5) integrating AI prediction comparison into mathematics instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A Stage 2 lesson built on the textbook's vacuum-flask temperature dataset, in which "The lesson was organized into three instructional segments: Mathematical Inquiry (15 minutes), AI Prediction Comparison (8 minutes), and Reflection on Prediction Failures (5 minutes)." Students construct compound line graphs, estimate when the stainless steel flask reaches 70°C (around the 130-minute point, dropping about 6°C per 30 minutes), compare their estimates with an AI prediction, and then reflect on a prediction failure caused by an opened lid. Mathematical literacy objectives are core; AI cognitive objectives are extended.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multimedia display capability, with the predictive demonstration software executed and debugged prior to the session
- Introduction after students have mastered data extraction and trend analysis using single-line graphs
#### Constraints
- The case validates only Stage 2 of the three-stage pathway

### Target Learners
- Grade 5 elementary mathematics students

### Learning Goals
- trend analysis and estimation from compound line graphs
- explaining the basic logic of AI trend prediction and its limitations
