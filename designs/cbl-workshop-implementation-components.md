# Key components for implementing a student-led CBL workshop (Table 1)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article enumerates, in Table 1, the components it deems key to implementing a CBL workshop: participant grouping (groups of 8), volunteer facilitators (1-2 per group, current medical students, faculty optional), a curriculum outline with objectives and case components plus a facilitator guide, facilitator training before and feedback after the workshop, facilitator roles (engage all students, redirect focus, pose questions, clarify concepts), resources, assessment methods, and stakeholder engagement. The authors "encourage similar programs to adapt CBL components aligning with their medical school."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Facilitator guide and designated case distributed one week in advance; pre-workshop meeting 1-2 days before; feedback collected one day post-workshop
#### Constraints
- Medical school faculty members are listed as not required; objective assessments (MCQs, short answer tests) are optional

### Target Learners
- undergraduate pre-medical students in integrated BS/MD pathways

### Learning Goals
- orientation to CBL process and clinical case reasoning
