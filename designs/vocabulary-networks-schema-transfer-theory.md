# Theory of change: networks of domain-specific vocabulary as the visible components of schemas mediate comprehension transfer

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's theoretical framework holds that schemas are "an abstract knowledge structure" whose visible components are semantically related networks of vocabulary words. Teaching domain-specific words in conceptually related networks helps students build topic and domain schemas, instantiate them during reading via construction-integration processes, and transfer them to related topics. The intervention theory of change links thematic lessons, concept mapping, interactive read alouds, argumentative writing, collaborative research, and word inquiry to vocabulary growth, which in turn supports word-to-text integration on transfer passages.
