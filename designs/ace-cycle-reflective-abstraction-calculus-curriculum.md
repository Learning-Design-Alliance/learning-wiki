# ACE teaching cycle-based experimental calculus curriculum on limits

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An experimental curriculum modeled on the ACE (Activity, Classroom Discussion, Exercise) teaching cycle, designed to initiate reflective abstraction on the concept of limit. Each day's lesson included reflective activities done in cooperative groups, an instructor debriefing discussion, and individual exercise sets. It covered the same topics, time, and textbook sections as the traditional curriculum, with identical limit examples and the same homework assignments; the stated differences were the collaborative activities and the teacher scripted interactions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Scripted lesson plans, class activities, class work sets, and homework sets validated by an expert panel via a Delphi method of iterations
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only against a matched traditional section at one midwestern community college

### Target Learners
- first-semester community college calculus students

### Learning Goals
- understanding of the concept of limit
