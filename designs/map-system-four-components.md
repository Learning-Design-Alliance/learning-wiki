# My Academic Plan (MAP) system: nudging, guaranteed counseling, online planning tool, and integrated data system

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The MAP system is a technology-based intervention the South Orange County Community College District developed to scale counselor-approved academic planning. It has four components: "Targeted nudging" delivered through Sherpa, a recommendation and personalization engine, via emails, texts, MySite portal notices, and robocalls; guaranteed counseling appointments in two-hour group workshops or one-on-one sessions; access for all students to the online MAP planning tool; and an integrated data system coordinating MAP, Sherpa, and the SARS scheduling software to deliver nudges, track responses, schedule sessions, and track attendance and plan completion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Every student's academic plan must be reviewed and approved by a counselor, a requirement the counseling department added beyond the Student Success Act mandate
- Counseling capacity must be organized so guaranteed appointments do not lengthen wait times for non-study students, using strategies such as greater use of adjunct counseling faculty
#### Constraints
- The study was not designed to assess which type of nudge worked best
- In fall 2012, before the intervention, less than 10 percent of students opened the district's MAP tool

### Target Learners
- First-time community college students

### Learning Goals
- Semester-by-semester academic planning aligned to education goals, majors, and transfer or credential requirements
