# Doctor-patient communication learning software with random, scenario, and patient-satisfaction modules

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A web-based learning software built in PHP with a MySQL backend and HTML5 front end, hosted on Sina cloud, designed to "transform the traditional model of communication skills training for Chinese medical students". The "random module teaches students theoretical knowledge of doctor-patient communication skills", drawing 25 of 100 questions per session with standard answers and repeatable practice. The scenario module presents 4 sets of clinical-case questions scored without standard answers, and questionnaires generated from answers are emailed to friends or family "who could play the role of the patient". A patient satisfaction questionnaire provides interns with feedback from the patient's point of view.
