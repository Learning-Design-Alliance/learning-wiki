# Two reusable learning objects on meta-analysis (Introduction to Meta-analysis; Presenting and interpreting Meta-analysis)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Two interactive, multimedia web-based RLOs were developed on meta-analysis for healthcare students. The first "provided an introduction to meta-analysis, sections covered pooling results, improving precision and improving power"; the second "was more detailed covering effect measures, forest plots, heterogeneity and effect methods". Both incorporated activities and a self assessment activity to provide formative feedback, take about 15 minutes to complete, and are openly accessible under a Creative Commons licence.
