# Multidisciplinary teaching approach adapting quantum communication to the background of technical profiles

## Description
The article's organizing framework is a multidisciplinary teaching approach in which content and methods are adapted to the target audience's background, where "the goals are making quantum communication accessible to technical proﬁles while connecting with their own background." Research instrumentation technicians are described as "a multidisciplinary community" sharing domain-specific fields such as signal processing, so both lecture-based and experiential-based teaching materials can be adapted to them.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teaching material should be accessible and connected with the background of the students, so that it can be approached in a natural and progressive way
#### Constraints
- Developed for research instrumentation technicians, a multidisciplinary community with engineering, computer science and physics backgrounds

### Target Learners
- research instrumentation technicians
- young engineers and physicists working with sophisticated instrumentation
- PhD students

### Learning Goals
- making quantum communication accessible to technical profiles
- building on students' domain-specific background knowledge
