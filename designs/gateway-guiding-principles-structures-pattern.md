# Gateway's three guiding principles instantiated through a coherent set of schoolwide structures and practices

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The case study documents how Gateway's founding ideals became three guiding principles—inclusivity and equity, relationships, and continuous improvement—that inform a set of structures and practices. Table 2 lists small schools and class sizes, advisories, an inclusion model, authentic project- and inquiry-based instruction and assessments, scaffolding and differentiation, attention to social-emotional and cognitive development, continuous improvement around an equity agenda, and educator collaboration. The report stresses that "each of the structures and practices works in tandem with the others, so there is a mission-driven coherence schoolwide."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schoolwide mission-driven coherence: structures must operate in tandem rather than as isolated programs
#### Constraints
- Documented at one charter organization serving about 800 students with small schools and small class sizes

### Target Learners
- middle and high school students
- students with learning disabilities
- students from low-income families

### Learning Goals
- self-advocacy for learning needs
- collaboration and community contribution
- critical and creative thinking
