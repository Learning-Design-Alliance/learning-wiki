# A nine-step technical roadmap for co-designing, launching, and acting on a first alumni survey

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide structures the whole survey effort as a nine-step technical roadmap moving from pre-design through action. Steps 1 to 3 cover assessing benefits, assessing readiness, and assembling a diverse design team; Steps 4 to 6 cover aligning on rationale and vision, selecting the audience and designing the instrument, and launching; Steps 7 to 9 cover interpreting results, forming actionable steps, and implementing and adjusting actions with feedback loops. The roadmap is based on the authors' multi-year pilots and expert interviews.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sufficient leadership and staff buy-in, since designing, launching, interpreting, and acting on a survey takes significant time and attention
- Resources including staff time, a secure survey platform, and staff for data cleaning, visualization, and analysis
#### Constraints
- The roadmap targets teams designing and launching their first alumni survey

### Target Learners
- District and school teams in K-12 education

### Learning Goals
- Transforming alumni feedback into a driver for institutional change
