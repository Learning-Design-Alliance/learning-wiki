# The Hannover Medical School (MHH) OSCE in the module 'Diagnostic Methods'

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The MHH OSCE is the final examination of the second-year module 'Diagnostic Methods', taken at the end of the second academic year en route to M1. It is "a course of nine stations" covering internal medicine and neurological examination (125 points), structured X-ray diagnosis (25 points), two practical skills stations such as blood collection and intramuscular injection (50 points), and a medical consultation with anamnesis and diagnosis delivered to a standardised patient (100 points). Sixty percent of points are needed to pass; underperformance in one station can be compensated by good results elsewhere.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Stations worth 25 points are completed in seven minutes and 50-point stations in 15 minutes, within a 95-minute course including changeover time.
#### Constraints
- Contents and timing of the OSCE are specific to the MHH and its curriculum; the authors state results cannot be generalized to other sites.

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate medical students in their second year

### Learning Goals
- Practical clinical skills, diagnostic competence, and doctor-patient communication
