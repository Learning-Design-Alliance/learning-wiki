# Two-phase hands-on laboratory activity: theoretical explanation followed by practical realization

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A lesson-level design for laboratory experiences with everyday materials, in which staff first explain and then learners perform. The article states the activities "prevedono due fasi: la prima in cui il personale spiegherà sia i fondamenti teorici alla base del fenomeno osservato, sia la realizzazione dell'esperimento; e la seconda che consiste nella realizzazione pratica di ciò che è stato appena appreso". It is proposed as particularly suited to primary schools, where the second phase can be a playful learning moment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Experiments executable by users themselves, made with easily available materials of everyday use, under the supervision of laboratory personnel
#### Constraints
- Demonstrations with complex apparatus or materials unsuitable for younger users are reserved to qualified staff rather than performed by learners

### Target Learners
- primary school pupils
- individual citizens interested in science

### Learning Goals
- connecting everyday observable phenomena to the laws of physics, chemistry and mathematics
