# Powerful learning principles: personal and accessible, authentic and challenging, collaborative and connected, inquisitive and reflective

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 2 studies (2 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size

## Description
The paper defines powerful learning as "a set of principles that guide educators to design learning experiences that meaningfully engage students": "personal and accessible, authentic and challenging, collaborative and connected, and inquisitive and reflective." The HP Learning Studio is presented as exemplifying these: it is centrally located and not tied to one class, supports authentic community-serving projects, enables collaborative creation of shareable learning artifacts, and fosters a safe space to fail and iterate. The authors cite research that authentic, project-based STEM environments feel more motivating and psychologically safe.
