# Developmental sequence of physical exam teaching: head-to-toe, then core + clusters, then hypothesis-driven PE

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The article organizes physical exam pedagogy into three approaches that "can be implemented in a developmental sequence." The head-to-toe (HTT) approach focuses on technique and a comprehensive exam but "does not integrate the PE maneuvers with the clinical picture at hand." The core + clusters approach adds limited clinical reasoning through predetermined maneuver clusters per condition. The hypothesis-driven PE (HDPE) requires the most advanced reasoning: learners formulate maneuvers from a differential diagnosis determined by the presenting history, identifying maneuvers that support or refute diagnoses. The authors propose combining all three into a longitudinal curriculum that progresses as learners develop knowledge and clinical reasoning.
