# Multisemester capstone writing series with peer-review senior seminar (University of La Verne biology)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A curricular intervention for all biology majors: a junior-year two-course series in research methods and biostatistics plus a redesigned senior seminar, culminating in a 25- to 30-page thesis and oral presentation. The article reports that "The writing assignments included the following: a five-page literature review (Fall semester of junior year); a five-page grant proposal, including a summary of methods and budget (Spring semester of junior year)" and a thesis-introduction draft. Senior seminar added peer-review training with four peer-review sessions per semester on five-page thesis sections, plus writing goals and a work plan.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mandated for all biology majors in the junior and senior curriculum; assignments dovetail with students' senior thesis topics selected with faculty research mentors
#### Constraints
- Requires faculty teaching-load accommodation, shared course responsibilities, and teaching-assistant support, per the article's discussion of workload

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate biology majors, including first-generation and Hispanic students at a Hispanic-serving institution

### Learning Goals
- Science inquiry and analysis skills
- Scientific writing and communication
- On-time completion of a senior research thesis
