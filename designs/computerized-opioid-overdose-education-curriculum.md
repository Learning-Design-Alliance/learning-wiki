# Standardized computerized opioid overdose education curriculum covering opioid effects, overdose signs and risks, and naloxone-free overdose response

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A standardized educational intervention developed from prior overdose education work, designed to be "direct, simple, and precise" and emphasizing three knowledge domains: opioid effects, opioid OD risks and symptoms, and effective opioid OD response. The computer version contained 3 introductory slides and 25 educational slides combining text, pictures, and/or videos, hosted in Qualtrics. Naloxone administration was deliberately excluded to increase generalizability and complementarity with naloxone training programs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content applicable to diverse patient populations rather than drug-user-specific concepts such as injection risk behaviors
- Deliverable as a stand-alone intervention or as a complement to naloxone-training sessions
#### Constraints
- Naloxone administration was not discussed in the curriculum
- The Computer + Mastery variant could not provide corrective feedback for incorrect responses due to Qualtrics limitations

### Target Learners
- adults being treated for opioid use disorder, such as patients in opioid detoxification

### Learning Goals
- knowledge of opioid effects
- recognition of opioid overdose symptoms and risk factors
- recommended overdose response behaviors such as sternal rubs, rescue breathing, and the recovery position
