# Information mastery workshop with self-learning slide-shows and software-recorded clinical scenarios

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A one-day information mastery workshop at Tehran University of Medical Sciences in which 44 residents received a one-hour lecture on information mastery principles including the 5S approach, then used self-learning PowerPoint slide-shows covering PICO question formulation, keyword choice, and how to search UpToDate and PubMed Clinical Queries effectively. Purpose-built Microsoft Excel Visual Basic software presented 16 PICO-formatted clinical scenarios from the University of Toronto Centre for Evidence Based Medicine, randomly assigned, recorded answers, and timed retrieval with a 20-minute cap per scenario.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Self-learning slide-shows before using each resource
- Ten minutes of familiarization with each resource
- Clinical scenarios with definite answers in PICO format
#### Constraints
- Blinding was not applicable to users and outcome assessors because they could recognize the layout of the resources; a technical problem with internet speed occurred in the second workshop

### Target Learners
- postgraduate year-one or two residents across ten residency programs

### Learning Goals
- information retrieval and management for evidence-based practice
