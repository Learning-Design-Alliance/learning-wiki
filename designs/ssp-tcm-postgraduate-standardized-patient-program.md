# SSP-TCM program: trained TCM postgraduate student standardized patients as a lower-cost simulation alternative

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The SSP-TCM program recruits voluntary first-year TCM postgraduate students and trains them as standardized patients of TCM through "a systematic training course consisting of didactic sessions and skill practice, and self-study sessions" delivered by senior SP trainers. Of 18 recruits, 15 passed a qualification test assessed by blinded SP instructors and internal medicine specialists. The program ran alongside an existing OSP-TCM program at Chengdu University of TCM, which the authors describe as efficient but costly and time-intensive.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Voluntary postgraduates with physical and mental fitness based on psychological test files and health examination records; those with acting talent and strong sense of responsibility prioritized
- Four months of systematic training and passage of a blinded qualification test before participation
#### Constraints
- Graduate student mobility requires retraining new batches as previous SSPs graduate
- Age limitations of graduate students make portraying elderly, infant, or pregnant patients difficult

### Target Learners
- Fourth-year TCM undergraduates in clinical competency training

### Learning Goals
- TCM clinical competency including history-taking, syndrome differentiation, and doctor-patient communication
