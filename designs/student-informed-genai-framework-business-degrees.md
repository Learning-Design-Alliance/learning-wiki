# A student-informed conceptual framework for integrating GenAI education throughout business degree programmes

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article presents a new conceptual framework for integrating GenAI education throughout business degree programmes. It is described as "based on student feedback and existing research", so it combines the survey evidence from 149 first-year undergraduates with the literature on student AI literacy. The framework offers "practical guidance for educators" and positions GenAI learning as spanning the whole degree rather than a single module.
