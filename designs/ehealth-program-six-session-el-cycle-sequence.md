# Six-session curriculum sequencing eHealth skills across the experiential learning cycle

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The program's six sessions form a reusable lesson-level sequence: session 1 ("A new realm of health technology care") introduces smart health care functions and motivates engagement; session 2 ("Fighting against sugar") focuses on regular health monitoring and self-management with practice tools; session 3 addresses complications, particularly chronic kidney disease; session 4 ("eHealth care by your side") uses competitions and hands-on activities to foster independent operation; session 5 ("My smart eHealth in daily care") integrates smart health care into daily self-management; and session 6 ("My eHealth care practice journey") synthesizes and reviews all prior content with comprehensive hands-on practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sessions need hands-on practice tools, wearable devices, and research-assistant support for practical operation
#### Constraints
- Content is diabetes-specific (blood sugar, blood pressure, diet monitoring; chronic kidney disease prevention)

### Target Learners
- Adult patients with type 2 diabetes

### Learning Goals
- operating eHealth applications accurately
- integrating health technology into daily self-management
