# Conceptual physics-and-astronomy service course built on three aspects: methodology, knowledge body, and everyday-life relevance

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A conceptual introduction to physics and astronomy 'intended for general liberal arts students' that 'engages students from the broadest spectrum' through three aspects: the methodology of physics illustrating the scientific approach; the knowledge body covering major developments of mechanics and modern astronomy with emphasis on basic ideas rather than problem-solving techniques; and evaluating arguments in current scientific controversies such as environment, energy, and evolution debates.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The course is offered each year and taught by a part-time adjunct faculty member.
#### Constraints
- Emphasis is on basic ideas and concepts rather than techniques of problem-solving.

### Target Learners
- General liberal arts students across the broadest spectrum of backgrounds

### Learning Goals
- Understanding the methodology of physics
- Appreciation of major developments in mechanics and modern astronomy
- Ability to evaluate merit in arguments in current scientific controversies
