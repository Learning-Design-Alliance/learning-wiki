# Grow-Your-Own credential pathway for classified staff

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
One LEA implemented a Grow-Your-Own program "structured to set up pathways for classified staff to earn credentials—in part because classified staff in the LEA are, on the whole, more diverse than classroom teachers." It focuses on teacher assistants who already hold college degrees, partnering with four higher education institutions and the state teacher's union to offer varied transition-to-teaching licensure programs. The administrator estimated the LEA currently has 15 to 20 classified staff members working toward earning their teaching license, who will "serve as a pool of individuals to feed into our teacher pipeline down the road."
