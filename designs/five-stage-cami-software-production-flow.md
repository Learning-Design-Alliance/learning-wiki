> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The article describes a five-stage development process for the CAMI software: "preparation stage, software design and development stage, materials integration stage, software test stage, and modification stage." Preparation includes understanding teachers' teaching demands and students' learning situations; design includes choosing teaching materials, determining teaching design and completing script design; the final stages combine components with the script, test the software and modify it into complete teaching software.
