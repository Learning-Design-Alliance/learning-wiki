# Project-based CLIL model integrating language form practice with subject-matter projects

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article applies content and language integrated learning combined with project-based learning, in which students "explore their field of study using English as a medium of communication" and "Language form practice was done integratedly within the subject study." Groups of 4-5 students complete mathematics, physics, biology, or chemistry projects culminating in English oral presentations. The model draws on immersion and ESP arguments that combining language with other subjects benefits learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Groups of 4-5 students assigned projects in their field of science
- Presentations conducted entirely in English
- One week of group preparation time
#### Constraints
- Tested only in a mathematics study program at one Indonesian university over a single semester

### Target Learners
- undergraduate mathematics students in Indonesia

### Learning Goals
- English oral proficiency
- academic communication in science fields
