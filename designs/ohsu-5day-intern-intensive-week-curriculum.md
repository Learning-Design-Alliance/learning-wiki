# 5-day OHSU internal medicine intern 'Intensive Week' simulation curriculum

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A sequential 5-day curriculum held at the OHSU Simulation Center, with 2 days as one cohort and 3 days rotating through six half-day stations in groups of 5–6. Content targets five needs: managing urgent calls, difficult conversations, EHR proficiency, standardized procedural training, and point-of-care ultrasound. It uses "high-fidelity case-based simulations, procedural simulation with task trainers, communication simulations with standardized patients, and electronic health record (EHR) simulated exercises".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All 'Intensive Week' clinical obligations covered by upper level residents so interns participate for 5 consecutive days
- A minimum of six faculty members over 5 days, approximately 14 faculty per year for a total of 312 hours
#### Constraints
- Requires substantial departmental commitment for faculty time and protected time for interns
- Significant time commitment in training faculty to participate, which decreases with return faculty

### Target Learners
- Internal medicine interns (PGY-1 residents)

### Learning Goals
- Procedural skills (ABG, PIV, CVC, lumbar puncture, paracentesis, thoracentesis)
- Management of acutely ill patients
- Communication during difficult conversations
- EHR navigation and manipulation
- Point-of-care ultrasound
