# Five-step instructional sequence: motivation, comprehension, hypothesis, pondering, evaluation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The developed model's implementation component has five aspects: "motivation, creation of understanding in various incidents or situations, associating the basic experience with previous understanding, considering and accepting the consensus through reference and negotiation, and the evaluation and assessment of performance practice". In Phase 2 the researcher elaborated these as five learning-management steps: motivation, comprehension, hypothesis, pondering, and evaluation, each with described activities such as stimulating curiosity through problem situations and making decisions to accept or reject a hypothesis.
