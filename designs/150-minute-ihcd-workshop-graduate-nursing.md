# 150-minute in-person Innovation and Human-Centered Design (I&HCD) workshop

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A 150-minute in-person experiential workshop developed and refined during a pilot phase and facilitated by the author team following "a detailed agenda to ensure intervention fidelity." The team has degrees in "nursing, sociology, peace studies, and engineering" and completed formal and informal training in design thinking and HCD methodologies. Workshop components were developed by applying the 5E Instructional Model (engagement, exploration, explanation, elaboration, evaluation), with experiential exploration before concepts are labeled and defined, including brainstorming, preliminary design solutions, and reflection. Online pre- and posttest surveys and a 4-question reflection survey accompanied the workshop.
