## Description
The article describes an outcomes-based approach in which program goals, course objectives, and assessment are explicitly aligned. It states that "An outcomes-based approach to education clearly specifies what students are expected to learn and arranges the curriculum such that these intended outcomes are achieved", with mapping tied to institutional and state degree-level expectations. The process flows from course to college to state/province, and was adopted department-wide for accreditation.
