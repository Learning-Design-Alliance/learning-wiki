# Robot Study Companion (RSC) v4.1 open-source social robot chassis

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The RSC is an affordable, compact, 3D-printed desktop social robot built around a Raspberry Pi with peripherals enabling multimodal interaction, including a touchscreen, servo-driven flippers, an LED ring, microphones, and speakers. The v4.1 chassis redesign applies DfA and DfD interventions: a twist-lock chassis replacing internal screws, snap-fit speaker base and front panel, one-way component fits with fiducial markers, print-in-place latches giving tool-free SD/USB access, a bespoke expansion PCB consolidating wiring breakouts, and a rewritten step-by-step guide with synchronised video and text. All open-source artefacts are released at the project site for community contribution.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assembly relies on 3D-printed reinforced parts and off-the-shelf electronics; the rewritten documentation guide at the project site supports the build.
#### Constraints
- The v4.1 documentation was deliberately kept in an alpha state during evaluation, and the designations v4 and v4.1 refer specifically to the chassis mechanical design, not electronics or software tracks.

### Target Learners
- university students facing isolation and motivational challenges
- first-time robot builders in Guyana and Estonia

### Learning Goals
- constructionist hands-on learning through building a social robot
- technical agency and emotional support via a physical companion
