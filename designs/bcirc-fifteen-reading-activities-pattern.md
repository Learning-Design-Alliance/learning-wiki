# BCIRC before-, during-, and after-reading activity structure

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
BCIRC organizes instruction as a recurring cycle of fifteen activities occurring before, during, and after reading. As the report describes, "Fifteen activities occur before, during, and after reading, including using BCIRC materials to develop vocabulary, making predictions of a story's content based on its title, and reading with a partner followed by silent reading". Teachers model reading strategies such as making and confirming a prediction before, during, and after reading, and cooperative groups then apply the demonstrated strategy while attempting to comprehend stories from their classroom text. The pattern embeds vocabulary, prediction, partner reading, and writing within cooperative teams.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers combine CIRC strategies with transitional and English as a Second Language strategies
- Extensive staff development on facilitating cooperative learning discussions
#### Constraints
- Designed for students transitioning from Spanish primary-language reading instruction in grades 2-5

### Target Learners
- Elementary English language learners whose primary home language is Spanish

### Learning Goals
- Reading comprehension
- Vocabulary development
- Cooperative discussion and academic English use
