## Description
The researchers built a model from the common points of existing instructional design models (including Dick, Carey, and Carey; ASSURE; and ADDIE) to guide development of the design. It comprises three main steps: need assessment, development of the instructional design, and assessment, with sub-steps for each. The article states that "The instructional design was prepared in three main steps: need assessment, development of the instructional design, and assessment. Sub-steps were then set for each main step."
