# Ten-stage diagnostic-to-seminar activity sequence combining questionnaire, videos, didactic text and student seminars on ethnic-racial themes

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article structures its formative actions as a ten-stage sequence (Quadro 3): an initial written questionnaire serving as pre-test, video-supported lesson openings, discussion and systematization of the questionnaire, a didactic support text with an accompanying questionnaire, teacher and student seminars whose themes must relate to ethnic-racial questions, and a final questionnaire equal to the initial one as post-test. The sequence moves from diagnosing prior conceptions to collective discussion and student-led seminars, letting the teacher "avaliar se houve avanço na compreensão dos estudantes".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A written initial questionnaire relating the theme to students' personal experience
- Instructional videos and a didactic support text
- Student seminars with themes distributed in advance
#### Constraints
- Designed and applied in one high school physics class in a Rio de Janeiro state school

### Target Learners
- high school students in physics classes

### Learning Goals
- radiation concepts and applications
- African and Afro-Brazilian history and science
- critical reflection on ethnic-racial relations
