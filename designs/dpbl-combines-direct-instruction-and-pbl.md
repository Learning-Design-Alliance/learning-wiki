# Direct Problem-Based Learning (DPBL) as a combination of direct instruction and problem-based learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article defines DPBL as a hybrid teaching approach: "The DPBL approach is a combination of DIA and PBL approach". It draws on direct instruction characteristics (face-to-face delivery, deliberate sequencing, small groups) and problem-based learning characteristics (student-centred, real-world ill-structured problems, teacher as facilitator, groups no larger than nine). In this study DPBL was paired with multimedia learning and implemented with groups of four to six members presenting work reports.
