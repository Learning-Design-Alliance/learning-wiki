# Teach introductory electricity and cell physiology in parallel using simplified equivalent circuits

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article argues that introductory-level electricity courses can be taught in parallel with basic cell physiology, showing that simple equivalent electrical circuits and basic knowledge of their functioning provide a foundation for modeling resting and excitable cell membrane behavior. The authors state this is an original approach in life-sciences curricula as introductory physics and physiology are rarely taught together.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A specific textbook covering all course topics may need to be written or sourced, as no particular textbook covers exactly all of the topics.
#### Constraints
- The problems are not suitable for a pure electrophysiology class because they omit real experimental artefacts.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate premedical and biomedical sciences students

### Learning Goals
