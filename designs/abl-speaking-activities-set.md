# Five warm-up and game-based speaking activities used in the ABL treatment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes five speaking activities drawn from Holmes (2003) and the British Council's E.T.T.E project: a pair interview ("Getting to Know You Interview"), the command game "Simon Says", a tactile guessing game ("It's in the Bag"), an impromptu one-minute team speaking game, and a lie-detecting question game ("Detective"). Warm-up activities "help the students get to know each other in a comfortable atmosphere" and come in two types: pair interviews with little teacher supervision, and fun games.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Activities require pairs or teams of students speaking English with limited teacher supervision
- A guideline sheet, a bag of about twenty everyday items, or impromptu topics as materials
#### Constraints
- Context-appropriate topics were suggested for the Pakistani classroom, such as the electric power shortage crisis

### Target Learners
- 6th-grade elementary students learning English as a second/foreign language

### Learning Goals
- English speaking skill development, including fluency, accuracy, vocabulary and description
