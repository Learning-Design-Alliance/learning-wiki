# Bilingual Community Outreach Assistant (BCOA) position

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The BCOA was a new staff position created to promote engagement of Spanish-language-dominant parents of English learners. The article states the BCOAs "answered questions for parents; reminded them of upcoming events, activities, and deadlines; forwarded helpful resources to parents; assisted parents in the enrollment process; and provided language interpreting for parents." BCOAs also hosted outreach events, supported EL parent leadership, coordinated recruitment, and, during COVID-19 closure, made weekly check-in calls, ran a daily tech hotline, and delivered distance-learning packets, Chromebooks, and hotspots to homes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Bilingual staff able to communicate with Spanish-language-dominant families in person and virtually
#### Constraints
- During school closure the role shifted to remote supports such as phone calls and home delivery of resources

### Target Learners
- Pre-K English learner and migrant children and their Spanish-language-dominant families

### Learning Goals
- Family engagement, kindergarten transition preparation, and parent leadership development
