# Course features implementing the CREDE Five Standards for Effective Pedagogy for students challenged by language or cultural barriers

## Description
Table 1.1 maps Making Sense of SCIENCE course features onto the CREDE Five Standards for Effective Pedagogy. Features include "Collaborative group science investigations and sense-making discussions," reading, writing, and speaking activities to develop academic language proficiency, hands-on activities based on natural phenomena, instructional tasks focused on making meaning of complex science ideas, and small-group opportunities for developing English proficiency through authentic communication about science ideas. A full quarter of the program focuses on identifying and evaluating literacy supports.
