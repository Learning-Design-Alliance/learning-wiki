# Self-contained self-study module series for marble game courses

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The teaching materials are "a self-contained series of self-study guides (modules) that can be used for both courses", twelve modules spanning the marble game, algorithms and pain relief, finite difference methods, model validation, diffusion, least-squares fits, Newtonian dynamics, equilibrium distributions, Poisson processes, molecular dynamics, membrane voltage and ion channels. Students write and implement their own algorithms from a blank Excel spreadsheet, and the pedagogical method uses no computational black boxes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Specific questions contained in the modules are assigned in advance for each class for a small amount of credit, and students must make an initial attempt before class
#### Constraints
- The modules include optional calculus sections that are not required; all of the materials are accessible to students without calculus

### Target Learners
- upper-division Biophysics and Physiological Modeling students
- argued by the author to be accessible at the introductory level

### Learning Goals
- model development and validation
- data analysis and graphing in Excel
- introduction to programming and computer science
