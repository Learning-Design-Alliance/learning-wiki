# Alluvial and bubble diagram representations of student pathways through a physics major

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article develops two visual representations of student pathways from registrar data. An alluvial diagram (FIG. 1) shows movement from first declared major to graduating major, helping "visualize how student initial conditions affect graduation outcomes." A bubble diagram (FIG. 2) shows the order and timing of required course completion relative to the recommended schedule, with bubble size indicating the relative proportion of students taking each course. Together they describe pathways into and out of the major at a single institution.
