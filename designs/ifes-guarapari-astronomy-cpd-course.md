# Ensino de Astronomia para Alunos do Ensino Fundamental (Ifes-Guarapari continuing-education course)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A 180-hour continuing-education course offered in 2018 by Ifes Campus Guarapari with the municipal networks of Laranja da Terra and Guarapari, which enrolled '105 (cento e cinco) servidores municipais matriculados' (66% PML, 33% PMG). Half the load was in person, with fortnightly meetings combining theoretical exposition, telescope-based night-sky observation, participant presentations and guest seminars, and it concluded with 17 groups delivering an experience report and an interdisciplinary teaching project to an examining board.
