## Description
Science Gateway is CERN's flagship education and outreach centre, opened in October 2023, housing three permanent exhibitions — Discover CERN, Our Universe, and Quantum World — that "stand out through their combination of immersive scenography with interactive exhibits (physical hands-on and multimedia type) as well as real scientific objects". Visitors explore the spaces themselves while CERN volunteers provide guidance on demand. The exhibitions address particle physics, its role in the Universe and everyday life, and open questions in modern physics, complemented by lab workshops, science shows and guided visits.
