# OCW-integrated flipped classroom: pre-class self-study of open course materials followed by in-class collaborative group learning with TA support

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A two-stage instructional pattern in which learners use OCW materials (instructional videos of about 30 minutes, course materials, syllabus, calendar) for self-directed pre-class study, then attend weekly 90-minute flipped sessions with small-group collaborative learning. Groups of five were mixed by prior physics knowledge, with one TA assigned to every three groups. The article states "the classroom activity chosen for this flipped classroom model was group collaborative activities, focusing on teacher-student interactions."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learners watch the pre-class OCW videos independently before class
- Sufficient TAs to provide guidance, with one TA per three groups
- Heterogeneous grouping based on prior knowledge scores
#### Constraints
- The scope of the study is limited to asynchronous distance learning for the pre-class materials

### Target Learners
- freshman university students in a compulsory physics course

### Learning Goals
- physics conceptual understanding and problem solving
- higher-order thinking through collaborative application of knowledge
