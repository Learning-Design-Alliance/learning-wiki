# Eight critical appraisal worksheets tailored to specific study types, with faculty answer keys

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes "These eight worksheets" adapted from Center for Evidence-Based Medicine templates, tailored to specific preselected studies and to study types CEBM does not cover, such as case reports. Each worksheet contains six to eight questions assessing purpose, validity, importance, and applicability, with guidance passages under each question. Faculty facilitators receive answer keys (Appendices I-P) so they can support group discussion, and students gain access to the keys after the session.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Worksheets are paired with a preselected health science research article relating to the weekly PBL learning objectives, and faculty facilitators are assigned to read the study and the answer key.
#### Constraints
- An effort was made to select studies with manuscripts under five pages in length because of constraints on the students' existing workload.

### Target Learners
- first-year medical students

### Learning Goals
- critical appraisal of health science literature
- evidence-based medicine skills
