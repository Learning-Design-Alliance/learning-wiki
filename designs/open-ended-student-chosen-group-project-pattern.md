# Extended open-ended group final project with student project choice fostering ownership

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In the second half of the course, students worked in groups of 2-4 on final projects of their choosing for seven weeks, ranked from a portfolio of six instructor-research-informed projects. There were "no lab guides that students could reference," students coordinated their own time management and division of labor, and projects culminated in oral presentations and a journal-style report.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Groups formed based on student project preferences, each student assigned to first or second choice
- Access to relevant scientific journal articles and frequent instructor access as resources
#### Constraints
- Students spent roughly 10-15 hours per week, so the pattern carries a substantial time commitment

### Target Learners
- upper-division undergraduate physics students

### Learning Goals
- technical skills
- conceptual understanding
- confidence in experimental physics abilities
- ownership over projects
