# Two complementary E-learning platforms for TB health workers: a synchronous telemedicine training platform and an asynchronous online training and qualification website

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The China-Gates TB project's E-learning subproject (May 2017–June 2019, three provinces) delivered training through two platforms described by the article: the "National TB Telemedicine Consultation and Training Platform", a live synchronous platform offering "lectures, case studies, and online meetings" for county-level-and-above clinical staff, and the "China TB prevention Online Training Website", an asynchronous system of recorded video modules with certification, aimed at all TB health workers. Both were delivered by national-level TB experts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Both synchronous and asynchronous training sessions were delivered by national level TB experts
#### Constraints
- The asynchronous website sessions focus on basic theory and routine treatment according to the latest national TB treatment and control guideline; the synchronous platform focused on complex clinical conditions for county-level and above clinical staff

### Target Learners
- TB clinical physicians
- public health physicians
- primary care medical staff in China

### Learning Goals
- TB diagnosis and treatment knowledge
- basic theory and routine treatment per the national guideline
- handling complex clinical conditions
