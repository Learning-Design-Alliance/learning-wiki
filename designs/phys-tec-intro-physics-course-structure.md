# WMU PhysTEC introductory physics course structure (Physics 2050)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The reformed introductory calculus-based physics course structure embodying the WMU PhysTEC design principles: pre-class reading assignments, 'most of the class time is spent with students working in assigned groups (and, usually white boards) on instructor-assigned activities,' online exercises and homework, group written homework using a General Approach–Procedure–Implementation problem-solving framework, 21 main ideas, weekly quizzes with conceptual questions, and quiz corrections.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The structure requires sustained instructor time for grading, quiz corrections, and group written homework.
#### Constraints
- When teaching alone in Spring 2006, MF dropped quiz corrections and made written homework individual to reduce time and perceived student dissatisfaction.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate engineering majors in introductory calculus-based physics

### Learning Goals
- conceptual understanding and multi-step problem solving in mechanics and heat
