# 1-day simulation-based training curriculum for mobile pre-hospital SARS-CoV-2 diagnostic teams

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A 375-minute curriculum combining "90 minutes of theoretical instruction (COVID-19 and hygiene)", 135 minutes of technical skills training, 90 minutes of simulator-based algorithm training emulating the whole prehospital process, and 60 minutes of OSCE assessment. It was delivered at the SIM CAMPUS simulation hospital in Eisenerz using high-fidelity patient simulation in rooms resembling a private home, for Austrian Red Cross mobile diagnostic teams.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants are emergency medical personnel preparing for prehospital SARS-CoV-2 testing duties; a simulation hospital setting with high-fidelity simulators is used
#### Constraints
- The schedule did not allow for a pretraining evaluation of skills, per the article's limitations section

### Target Learners
- emergency medical assistants
- practicing physicians engaged with the Austrian Red Cross

### Learning Goals
- correct use of personal protective equipment
- collection of naso-/oropharyngeal swabs for RT-PCR testing
- patient identification and communication
- sample logistics
