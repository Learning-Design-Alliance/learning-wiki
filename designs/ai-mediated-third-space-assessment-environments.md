# AI-mediated third space assessment environments connecting formal curriculum with students' interests, experiences, and learning needs

## Description
Drawing on third space theory, the paper argues that AI agents implemented "within relational and ethically governed environments" can help mediate assessment settings that "connect formal curriculum with students' interests, experiences, and learning needs". Third space here names a hybrid assessment environment shaped jointly by formal curriculum and learner experience.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- AI agents must be implemented within relational and ethically governed environments for third space assessment mediation.
#### Constraints
- The paper presents this mediation as an argument to be examined further, listing relational assessment practice and equity among its future research directions.
