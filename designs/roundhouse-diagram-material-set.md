# Roundhouse Diagram implementation material set: empty sheet, worksheet, checklist, and animation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Students "were provided with an empty sheet of Roundhouse Diagram, the Roundhouse Diagram Worksheet and the Roundhouse Diagram Checklist". The empty sheet has 7 sections (adjustable to 5–9); the 6-item worksheet guides planning from stating the main idea through drawing symbolic figures; the 10-item Likert-type checklist supports self-evaluation of each step; and a researcher-prepared computer animation demonstrated how to form diagrams.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The worksheet's 6 items are completed before forming the diagram, and the checklist's 10 items are filled in for self-evaluation
- An animation prepared by the researchers is used to show students how to form Roundhouse Diagrams
#### Constraints
- The article notes that students who fail to form the Roundhouse Diagram are likely to have negative attitudes towards the course, so preliminary work on simple subjects is needed

### Target Learners
- high school 9th grade physics students

### Learning Goals
- planning and organizing knowledge about Force and Motion concepts
- self-evaluation of diagram quality
