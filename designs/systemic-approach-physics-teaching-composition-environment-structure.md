# Systemic approach to teaching and learning physics via the composition-environment-structure framework

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes a systemic approach, following Bunge, in which physics teaching and learning is represented as "a framework involving the ordered triple compositio n-environment-structure" together with a mechanism integrating teaching and learning. It is motivated by three problems: instructors' need to balance conceptual and quantitative reasoning, students' need for materials developing mathematical reasoning, and the absence of a coherent problem-solving strategy. The approach holds that physics, mathematics and reasoning are interrelated, illustrated by the Millennium Bridge crowd-synchronization analysis.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must incorporate both conceptual and mathematical reasoning systematically in their teaching

- The article presents this as a plausible framework, not a tested intervention
