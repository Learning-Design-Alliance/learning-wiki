# Three-question mastery structure for oral exam question categories

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each oral exam question category is built from three question types: "an 'explanation question,' an 'application question,' and an 'extension question.'" The structure is chosen to assess mastery in the sense of Ambrose et al., in which students "first acquire component skills" (explanation), "then they integrate those skills into a broader problem" (application), "before finally transferring them to a new context" (extension). The article states that later questions reach higher taxonomic levels, mirroring Bloom's taxonomy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor prepares a set of question categories spanning the material covered, with all three question types written for each category
#### Constraints
- Question categories must be prepared in advance; example categories were designed for upper-level physics and astronomy courses

### Target Learners
- upper-level undergraduate physics and astronomy majors

### Learning Goals
- conceptual mastery of course material
- transfer of knowledge to new contexts
- verbal explanation of physics concepts
