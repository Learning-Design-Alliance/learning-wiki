# Matched pre/post low-fidelity simulation scenario set with scenario-specific scoring sheets for studying time constraints

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The study used fourteen low-fidelity simulated clinical scenarios of acutely decompensating patients, targeted at junior (PGY-1 to early PGY-2) or senior (late PGY-2 to PGY-3) levels, each performed first without and then with a disclosed time constraint ranging "from seven to 14 min". Scenario-specific scoring sheets awarded two points for leader-completed items and one for assistant-completed items, covering evaluation, diagnostics, interventions, appropriate activation, and time to activation. Videos were recorded via Zoom and scored independently by three investigators.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Scenarios proctored by a single pediatric critical care attending physician to reduce execution variability, with videos re-reviewed before matched post sessions to ensure uniformity
#### Constraints
- Low-fidelity mannequin models may affect scenario realism, and results from simulated scenarios may be difficult to extrapolate to real-life patient care

### Target Learners
- Pediatric residents (PGY-1 to PGY-3)

### Learning Goals
- Recognition of acutely decompensating patients, activation of the pediatric RRT or code team, and patient management until the responding team arrives
