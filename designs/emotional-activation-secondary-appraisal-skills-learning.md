# Emotional activation and secondary appraisal as a mechanism for enhancing practical skills learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The article draws on neuroscientific and psychological research holding that emotions modulate attention and memory, especially when a stimulus connects to existing neuronal networks and implies social importance. Confronting learners with a threatening scenario triggers "secondary appraisal", the person's estimate of their ability to cope with a threat, which may heighten emotional activation and focus attention on learnable features, prompting rehearsal in working memory and motivation to acquire coping strategies such as BLS skills.
