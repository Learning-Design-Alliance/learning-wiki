# A cyclical integrated reading/writing learning cycle using discussion boards, blogs and wikis

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes a recurring instructional cycle in which the tutor introduces reading/writing topics, assigns cooperative online work teams, and guides students through engagement in reading, discussion-board responses, personal blogging, and collaborative wiki construction. Each course unit takes three classes plus homework per week, with teams' wikis made public for inter-team reading, feedback and editing. The cycle is cyclical and continues with each course unit until the end of the semester.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A tutor-monitored process with wiki evaluation rubrics to help inter-team members provide and assess feedback on students' writings
#### Constraints
- Inter-team work is permitted only on wiki proofreading, editing and revising

### Target Learners
- EFL college students

### Learning Goals
- integrated reading and writing skills
