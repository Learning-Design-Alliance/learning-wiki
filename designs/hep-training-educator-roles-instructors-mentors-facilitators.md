# Three educator roles: instructors, mentors and facilitators

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The HSF training program divides the work of a training event among three named roles, collectively called educators. Instructors are subject-matter experts who develop training material and teach it, and are the primary academic drivers of the program. Mentors work closely with participants, for example by conducting small group mentoring sessions with ideally only five students per mentor, optimizing the learning environment and helping participants persevere. Facilitators take care of organizational aspects and serve as the primary point of reference for participants. The article notes that mentors and facilitators also develop transferable pedagogical and leadership skills through these roles.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Creating training material and teaching requires a lot of commitment and time, so the efforts of everyone involved need acknowledgement.
#### Constraints
- In-person events have been managed by about five educators, which the article says is necessary for the hands-on aspect to be successful.

### Target Learners
- HEP scientists participating in training events

### Learning Goals
- software skills acquisition supported by mentoring and event organization
