# Prognosis curriculum pattern: small-group sessions plus guided experiential exercise with real patients, built on Kolb's experiential learning model

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A curriculum pattern combining three small-group sessions with a clinical exercise: case-based teaching of prognostic tools, role play with standardized patients for prognosis communication, then a real-patient exercise in which residents assess a patient's prognosis, use it to frame a clinical decision, and discuss it, followed by reflection and group feedback. The authors state it is "innovative in combining role play with subsequent guided experiential learning with actual patients in clinical practice" and that development "drew from Kolb's model of experiential learning".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An existing required rotation in which the clinical exercise can be embedded
- A summary of prognostic tools and resources provided to residents to facilitate application
#### Constraints
- Implemented at one institution; results may not reflect residents or patients elsewhere
- Evaluation focused on short-term outcomes; long-term impact not assessed

### Target Learners
- First-year internal medicine residents

### Learning Goals
- Assess prognosis using evidence-based tools
- Communicate prognosis explicitly and within benefits/harms discussions
- Apply prognosis to inform clinical decisions in older adults with multimorbidity
