# Native Skywatchers planetarium programming at the SCSU Planetarium integrated into regular shows

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Native Skywatchers is integrated into the regular programming of the SCSU Planetarium, which has a state-of-the-art fiber optic Chronos star projector bringing more than 8,500 stars and 24 constellations and galaxies to life under a 30-foot dome. The planetarium is the only one in the region to offer shows at no cost, and over 7,000 people visit each year. Educators, scout leaders, and educational organizations can request a planetarium show about Ojibwe and/or D(L)akota star knowledge, and in May 2015 a collaboration with the St. Cloud School District brought hundreds of middle school students to Native Skywatchers shows.
