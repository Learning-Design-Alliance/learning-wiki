# Five organizational capacity levers leaders used to support Skyline ELA implementation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The infographic identifies five domains of strategic investment through which school leaders strengthened organizational capacity for curriculum implementation: "Championed professional learning", "Protected teacher time", "Expanded staff support", "Engaged external support", and "Strengthened collaboration structures". Leaders who targeted these key organizational levers supported teachers' engagement with the Skyline ELA curriculum. The pattern organizes implementation support as leadership investment across these five domains rather than as a single intervention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Leaders must have discretion to allocate resources across professional learning, teacher time, staffing, external support, and collaboration structures.
#### Constraints
- Documented in the context of the K-2 Skyline ELA curriculum launch in Chicago Public Schools.

### Target Learners
- Elementary school teachers in grades K-2

### Learning Goals
- Implementation of a new literacy (ELA) curriculum with instructional change
