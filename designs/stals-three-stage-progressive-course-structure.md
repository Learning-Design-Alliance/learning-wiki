# STALS three-stage progressive course structure integrating theory, practice, assessment, and feedback

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The STALS course organizes laparoscopic training into three stages of progressively increasing difficulty, each combining online and offline components. The article states "Each stage incorporates both online and offline components, consisting of theory, practice, assessment, and feedback (Table 1)". Trainees must pass each stage's assessment before progressing, retaking a stage on failure, and complete a feedback questionnaire at each stage's end. Stage one uses box trainers, stage two the LapSim VR simulator, and stage three animal organ perfusion simulation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assessment thresholds must be met before progression to the next stage; failed trainees retake the current stage
#### Constraints
- The extended learning cycle across all three stages poses a challenge due to the time-consuming nature of full implementation

### Target Learners
- surgical residents and fellows at varying experience levels

### Learning Goals
- laparoscopic skills progression from basic tasks toward operating-room practice
