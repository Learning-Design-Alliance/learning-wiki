# Needs-sensing before design: surface state-specific structures, communication gaps, and coordination complexity to shape a collaborative

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
R15CC conducted needs-sensing during the Collaborative's development phase, and this work "critically impacted the design of this FACE Collaborative." It surfaced diverse leadership structures, communication challenges across SEAs, and coordination complexity within states, allowing design choices to respond to the region's actual conditions rather than assumed ones.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A needs-sensing process conducted during the development phase
- Attention to how regional characteristics affect the consistency and distribution of programs and services
#### Constraints
- Insights were specific to the four-state region's family and community engagement landscape

### Target Learners
- State education agency leaders
- Technical assistance providers

- Designing responsive capacity-building collaboratives
