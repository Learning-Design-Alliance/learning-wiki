# IWA waiting-list nationality algorithm inspired by Entrofy

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Inspired by the Entrofy algorithm for candidate selection (Huppenkothen et al. 2019), IWA developed an algorithm that assessed the waiting list for an underrepresented nationality each time a spot opened up if the next few candidates were nearly equally ranked. In the article's example, with zero confirmed participants from country B and many from country A, "The algorithm recommends ﬁlling the open spot with the perso n from country B." It was not used on the first round of acceptances and was not a replacement for meritocratic evaluation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Used only after a first round of acceptances, when a spot opens and the next few waiting-list candidates are nearly equally ranked
- Applied alongside, not as a replacement for, meritocratic evaluation of applications
#### Constraints
- The algorithm was used so rarely and conservatively that no participant who attended the camp was affected by it, and the authors are unlikely to employ it in future rounds, though developing it sparked important discussion about the application process

### Target Learners
- IAYC waiting-list applicants aged 16-24

### Learning Goals
- increasing nationality diversity among camp participants
