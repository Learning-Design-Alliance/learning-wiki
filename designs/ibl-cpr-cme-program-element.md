# Internet-based CPR CME program with scenario-based simulation, multimedia, and self-assessment delivered via LMS and CD-ROM

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An interactive Internet-based CME tutorial built on the newest American Heart Association CPR curriculum guidelines and principles of self-directed learning, burned onto CD-ROM and deployed through a university Learning Management System. It incorporated "photography, video, text, narrative and animation" and was organized into three sections: objectives, the CPR algorithm, and "a simple simulation which included examples and Scenario-based with feedback". Pre- and post-tests were automatically uploaded to the LMS, and access was authenticated against an HR database.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants were authenticated through their number and secure password against the HR database, and were required to complete post-assessments to receive CME credit.
#### Constraints
- The study examined this particular program in a very specific CME clinical area, which the authors name as a generalization limitation.

### Target Learners
- postgraduate general physician trainees in continuing medical education

### Learning Goals
- knowledge of the newest CPR curriculum guidelines and basic life support
