# Four short-term WebQuest activities for ninth-grade data analysis and probability

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The researchers developed four short-term WebQuests — two for data analysis (DAW1 pickle-making, DAW2 used-car selection) and two for probability (PW1 Turkish lottery, PW2 traffic accidents) — each designed to take about 3 class hours (135 minutes) and containing "introduction, task, process, resources, evaluation, and conclusion". Tasks were tied to daily-life situations, included open-ended questions requiring higher-order thinking, and specified curated internet resources so students would not waste time searching. Students accessed them at http://www.MathWebQuests.com and solved them in groups in a computer laboratory.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Schools need computer laboratories, internet networks and computers with necessary hardware
- Teachers and students need sufficient computer-use skills
#### Constraints
- Teachers reported WebQuests on the internet are limited and inadequate and that preparing activities is difficult and time-consuming

### Target Learners
- ninth-grade students aged 15-16

### Learning Goals
- data analysis and probability learning outcomes of the ninth-grade mathematics curriculum
