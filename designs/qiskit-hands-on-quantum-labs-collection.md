# Seven Qiskit-based hands-on quantum programming labs with handouts and templates

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A collection of seven dedicated lab sessions (Python/Jupyter warm-up, entanglement, quantum gates and circuits, QKD, Deutsch and Deutsch-Jozsa, Simon's algorithm, Grover's algorithm with 3-SAT) built on IBM's Qiskit. Students follow handout procedures step by step, modify program templates, and verify results against textbook examples. The authors state "The full lab handouts and program templates are provided for interested instructors" and that the report can serve as an instructor's manual for quantum programming education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A single quantum programming environment (Qiskit) taught throughout the course
- Access to simulators or actual IBM quantum computers via free accounts
#### Constraints
- Many experiments are tied to the Bernhardt textbook Quantum Computing for Everyone and verify results against its examples

### Target Learners
