# Blended online practice with speech-recognition feedback plus teacher-led small-group oral language instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The program's instructional design combines interactive, student-directed online lessons, in which "speech recognition to provide immediate feedback" supports listening and speaking practice in academic contexts, with teacher-led instruction on oral language, grammar, and vocabulary. Content is organized across 19 content-based levels spanning subjects such as science, math, and social studies, and teachers receive real-time progress data with aligned small-group lesson plans.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Technology supporting speech recognition and real-time progress reporting
- Teachers review performance data and deliver small-group lessons
#### Constraints
- Designed as a supplement to core instruction, not a replacement

### Target Learners
- English Learners in grades K–6

### Learning Goals
- Academic English listening and speaking
- Grammar and vocabulary development
