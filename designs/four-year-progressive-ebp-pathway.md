# Four-year progressive competency development pathway structures EBP learning from awareness to application

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The model organizes evidence-based practice competency development across the full undergraduate program as a four-year progressive pathway. Year 1 establishes evidence-based awareness through a mandatory course introducing evidence-based practice concepts, literature retrieval, and critical thinking. Years 2-3 emphasize competency building through guideline-integrated courses and practice platforms, advancing to systematic review methods and critical appraisal. Year 4 concentrates on comprehensive application and outcome demonstration through an evidence-based nursing round practicum during clinical internships and evidence-based graduation thesis options.
