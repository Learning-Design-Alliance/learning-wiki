# Mobile-based learning application with procedure videos, images, and electronic notes for operating theatre techniques

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A smartphone application developed as the study's intervention, containing "videos, images, and electronic notes" for four operating theatre techniques: scrubbing, gowning, gloving, and assisting ET intubation. The researcher performed and recorded all procedure videos (durations from 2 minutes 52 seconds to 6 minutes 19 seconds) and uploaded them to the application, whose URL was shared via a WhatsApp group; experimental students had access for two weeks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must own a compatible smartphone; the study required participants to have their own Smartphone and included only students with android phones.
#### Constraints
- The application is exclusively compatible with android phones, which the authors state limited the sample.

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate and post-basic B.Sc. nursing students learning operating theatre procedures

### Learning Goals
- Knowledge of operating theatre procedures
- Clinical skills in scrubbing, gowning, gloving, and assisting endotracheal intubation
