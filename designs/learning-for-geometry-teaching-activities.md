# Learning for geometry teaching activities: geometry activities plus student work analysis

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The intervention consisted of two activity types: geometry activities (sorting shapes, attributes of shapes, classification of polygons) and a pedagogical activity analyzing elementary students' geometry work. Participants completed a worksheet of open-ended definition questions and 10 quadrilateral figures, then analyzed six selected student worksheets in pairs and small groups using a protocol, discussing what the student knew, misconceptions, and how to teach the concepts. The combination of activities "provided coherence" across individual, pair, and whole-class formats.
