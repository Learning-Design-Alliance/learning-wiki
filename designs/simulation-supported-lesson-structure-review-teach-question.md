# Simulation-supported lesson structure: review questions, presentation with simulations, closing question round

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
In the experimental group each lesson opened with about five minutes of questions reviewing prior content and checking readiness, then the subject "was taught with the support of relevant presentations and simulations", and the final five minutes were devoted to questions and answers so unclear points could be identified and re-explained. Simulations were drawn from mebvitamin.com and youtube.com and projected with computer-aided projection in audio-visual quality.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to simulation resources and a computer-aided projector providing audio-visual quality
- Four hours per week of course time across five weeks
#### Constraints
- The pattern was applied to one seventh-grade science-and-technology unit in a single school

### Target Learners
- Seventh-grade primary education students

### Learning Goals
- Understanding of human body systems (digestive, urinary, nervous systems and sense organs)
