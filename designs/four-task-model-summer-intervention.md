# Four-task model of summer intervention: planning, financing, logistics/information, and anticipatory socialization

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Figure 2 presents a model specifying the content of summer melt interventions, showing four major kinds of tasks required of college-intending graduates: postsecondary planning, financing, logistics/information, and anticipatory socialization. The model indicates interrelations among requirements: financing obstacles force students back into planning decisions, and informational barriers within logistics can likewise send students back to financing tasks. Very few low-income participants reached the anticipatory socialization level that higher-income students typically occupy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Intervention content must address interrelated clusters of financial, logistical, and socialization tasks rather than any single task type
#### Constraints
- Supports that help students actualize admission might have limited relevance to increasing their capacity to succeed academically once enrolled

### Target Learners
- college-intending low-income high school graduates

### Learning Goals
- completing pre-enrollment tasks and making an optimal transition into college
