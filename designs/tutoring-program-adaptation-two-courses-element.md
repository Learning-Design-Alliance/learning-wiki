# Tutoring program for psychological and pedagogical support with two thematic courses

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A tutoring program comprising a plan of tutor work and two thematic courses: "Training activities technology", aimed at learning planning, self-organization and understanding of psychological and pedagogical patterns of high-school learning activity, and "Life science", targeting responsibility, awareness, orientation and comprehension of purposes and meaning of the new period of student life. Tutors worked in five areas: diagnostics, preventive maintenance, development, compensation, and counseling, in cooperation with teachers, the student union, deans, future employers and successful graduates.
