# Sequencing Virtual Patients and other educational activities according to complexity, aligned with instruction and assessment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A curriculum-level pattern for embedding VPs in a clerkship: VPs and other activities, including bedside teaching with real patients, are sequenced according to complexity, and instruction and assessment are aligned with VP use. The article reports that studies 4–5 suggest "sequencing VPs and other educational activities, including bedside teaching with real patients, according to complexity" and "aligning instruction and assessment with the use of VPs". Students proposed a concrete sequence of lecture, one to two VPs, tutor-led small group discussion, then a real patient.

#### Requirements

- Findings derive from student perceptions in one paediatric clerkship setting; no longitudinal implementation study was included
