# Georgia Department of Education LEAD staged certification and recognition pilot for whole child education model schools

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
From 2021 to 2023, the Georgia Department of Education piloted a certification and recognition process for community schools implementing a whole child education model. The process recognized schools as they progressed through stages characterized by the acronym LEAD (Learning, Emerging, Achieving, Distinguished), each with benchmarks for structures and processes; later stages expected evidence of integration with the schoolwide improvement plan, impact, and outcomes. The finalized standards and coaching were praised by educators, but the pilot faced capacity challenges and was shut down for consolidation and reorganization.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sufficient capacity within the certifying agency and completed certification materials, including consensus-defined standards and a rubric, before launch.
#### Constraints
- The pilot faced challenges throughout due to capacity issues within the Department of Education and a rush to begin before certification materials were completed.
