# 45-minute immersive VR training based on TeamSTEPPS principles with pre- and postdebriefing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention is a 45-minute immersive VR-based training delivered via a head-mounted display (Pico Neo 3 Pro Eye with 6DoF), built from 360° filmed scenes of a surgical error whose contributing factors are explained by the Swiss Cheese Model and HFACS. As the article states, "Participants were exposed to a 45-minute immersive VR-based training based on TeamSTEPPS principles to improve safety behaviors." Training included a standardized pre- and postbriefing grounded in TeamSTEPPS principles, in which participants interpreted the TENTS- and HFACS-related factors behind the depicted patient safety incident.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A VR head-mounted display and 360° filmed scenarios of a realistic surgical error with actors who have lived health care experience
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only as a pilot in a single academic medical center with 10 intervention surgeons

### Target Learners
- surgeons (attendings and residents) in operating room settings

### Learning Goals
- TeamSTEPPS-related safety behaviors including communication, leadership, situation monitoring, and mutual support
