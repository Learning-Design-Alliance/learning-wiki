# Twenty-session six-hats training program for pivotal thinking

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The study's experimental intervention is a training program defined as "a set of a ctivities and strategies of training consisting of (20) formed a lecture", each lecture a 50-minute training session. It was validated by 10 arbitrators in educational and psychological sciences, with adjustments made from their recommendations. Training covered each hat in individual sessions, merged the six hats in comprehensive application sessions, and ended with a discussion session and post-test administration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Validation by arbitrators before application; sessions sequenced from individual hats to merged application
#### Constraints
- Implemented during the first semester of the academic year 2014/2015 at Al-Hussein Bin Talal University

### Learning Goals
