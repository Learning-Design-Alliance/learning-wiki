# Curriculum tapestry of warp and weft threads integrating theory, laboratory, mathematics, computing and professional skills

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The programme's architecture weaves five along-programme warp threads — "theory, laboratory, mathematics, computing, and professional skills" — with across-programme weft themes or topics connecting learning across the degree. For example, first-year waves content appears simultaneously in core physics, mathematics, computational physics (finite-difference time-domain waveguide design) and laboratory work, so students encounter the same topic in different contexts and gain fresh insight. This necessitated strict top-down assignment of module ILOs and content-delivery timetables.
