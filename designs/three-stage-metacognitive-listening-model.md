# Three-stage metacognitive listening instruction model (planning, listening, post-listening)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article operationalizes metacognitive strategy instruction in listening comprehension as three stages. The planning stage uses "planning and directed attention" with pre-questioning advance organizers; the listening stage has students hear the task three times in pairs, applying selective attention, monitoring, problem solving and self-management; the post-listening stage is a self-assessment stage using class discussion, a check back system, learning logs and reflection and evaluation strategies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instruction delivered across a semester within the regular listening comprehension course, with the teacher demonstrating and assisting the strategies
#### Constraints
- Stated limitation: the experiment ran for only one term with a very small group in a single class of students

### Target Learners
- Level III EFL student-teachers of average to below average listening proficiency

### Learning Goals
- EFL listening comprehension
- metacognitive awareness of listening strategies
