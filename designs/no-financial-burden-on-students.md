# Remove financial barriers so all competition activities are free to students

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
CzAO's inclusion goal is underwritten by dedicated public funding. The article states: "we regularly submit proposals for dedicated funding from the Ministry of Education, Youth, and Sports, which allows us to organise all activities without imposing any financial burden on our students." This funding also enables hosting the IWAA and other annual workshops, attending international competitions, and providing further mentorship to top students to prepare them for future academic endeavours. The principle is that accessibility requires actively securing funding rather than charging participants.
