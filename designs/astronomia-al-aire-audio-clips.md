# Astronomía al Aire audio clips: short scientist-narrated weekly podcasts

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Audio clips of about four to five minutes "are the central part of Astronom´ ıa al Aire project and currently 35 of them have been produced and disseminated weekly" through the project website. Topics include scientists who built theories, supernovae, stars, black holes, exoplanets, neutrinos, antimatter and the large structure of the universe, emphasising the relation between theory and observations. Background music deliberately avoids science clichés, using tango, salsa, Beatles, Stravinsky or Xenakis themes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The clip author is the storyteller narrator: "The voice of a scientist that knows and understands the emphasis on content, is preferable and more credible than the neutral, trained voice of a professional broadcaster"
#### Constraints
- "There are necessary overlapping of themes and subjects": clips on the evolution of stars share concepts with those on neutron stars, pulsars and black holes

### Target Learners
- general radio audiences in Spanish-speaking Latin America

### Learning Goals
- interest in scientific inquiry about astronomical objects and events
- appreciation of the intimate relation between theory and observations
