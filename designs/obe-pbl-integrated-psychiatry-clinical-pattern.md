# OBE-integrated PBL instructional pattern for psychiatric clinical practice teaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A four-component teaching pattern combining OBE goal orientation with PBL small-group case work: teacher-student training, analysis of learning situation with customized goals, group teaching with dynamic adjustment, and a "process-formative-summative" three-dimensional evaluation system. Groups of "4–5 students with similar self-development plans" work with a responsible teacher, and teaching is "optimized... in real time according to students' feedback" via the Rain Classroom platform.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors and interns receive systematic training in the core connotations of OBE and PBL before implementation
- Student career development plans are collected to set customized training objectives
- A responsible teacher is assigned to each small group
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only in psychiatric clinical practice teaching with 480 credit hours of internship

### Target Learners
- fifth-year undergraduate psychiatry majors in clinical internship

### Learning Goals
- theoretical knowledge of psychiatry
- clinical skills and clinical reasoning
- communication skills
- career preparation for postgraduate entrance exams or tertiary-hospital employment
