# Climate resilience education as a framework for preparing, anticipating and strengthening student responses to climate change

## Description
The article defines climate resilience education as an effort to prepare, anticipate and strengthen student responses to climate change and its impacts, directed at increasing adaptation capacity through understanding, changing habits, and developing practical skills. It incorporates social science and behavioral principles into climate science and offers students the opportunity to identify possible interventions with direct and long-lasting impacts. In higher education it is positioned as a transformer of society, building disaster response attitudes and adaptability that students transfer to communities.
