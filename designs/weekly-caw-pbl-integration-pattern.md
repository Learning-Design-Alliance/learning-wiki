# Weekly pre-read worksheet plus brief small-group discussion integrated into an existing PBL curriculum

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each week for 8 weeks, one worksheet was shared in advance of a PBL session; students read the preselected article and completed the worksheet beforehand. A brief portion (5-7 minutes) of group time was reserved to discuss the study and resolve worksheet answers, and the answer key was released after the session. The article states the CAWs "have been designed to give students a structured activity with definitive examples of accurate critical appraisal for a variety of study designs," replacing a loosely structured student-led journal presentation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty facilitators confirm each student completed the worksheet prior to attending the PBL session, and a brief portion of group time is reserved for worksheet discussion.
#### Constraints
- Students noted that 5-7 minutes was not always sufficient to discuss the worksheet and article, and 40% of surveyed students agreed the exercise was burdensome on the PBL small group.

### Target Learners
- first-year medical students in PBL small groups of eight to 10 students

### Learning Goals
- structured critical appraisal of research studies
- applying literature findings to patient cases
