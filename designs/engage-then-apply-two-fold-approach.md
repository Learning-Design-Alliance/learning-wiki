# Two-fold approach: engage with an unconventional context, then learn through application and discussion

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A lesson-level design pattern combining an engaging, unconventional narrative context with a follow-up application worksheet. The article states the combination is "intended to provide a two-fold approach towards learning about quantum teleportation where the students first engage with the content, and then they learn through application and discussion". The narrative supplies application and context that teachers in prior studies found lacking, while the worksheet pushes students to apply and critically discuss the concept immediately.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A recognisable popular-culture character as the narrative anchor
- Support materials (worksheet) so students practise rather than only hear the story
#### Constraints
- Designed for quantum teleportation; adaptation to other quantum concepts is stated as outside the current work's scope

### Target Learners
- high school students
- early university students

### Learning Goals
- engagement with quantum physics content
- application and critical discussion of quantum teleportation
