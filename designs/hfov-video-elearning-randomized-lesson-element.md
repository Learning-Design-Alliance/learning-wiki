# Randomized e-learning lesson on HFOV with 10-question free-text pre-, post-, and retention tests

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A single computer-based lesson plan, built "on the commercial e-learning platform Softchalk," delivered a peer-reviewed 23-minute video on high frequency oscillatory ventilation from the OPENPediatrics platform. Three content experts developed free-text test questions scored for content validity; residents completed a 10-question pretest, posttest, and 6-month retention test with different questions testing similar concepts, scored binary (0 or 1 point) by three independent graders and reported as percent correct.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content-expert-validated free-text questions; monitored initial testing sessions; graders scoring binary responses
#### Constraints
- No immediate feedback was given after test questions, to avoid confounding retention results by restudying

### Target Learners
- first- and second-year pediatric residents

### Learning Goals
- basic conceptual understanding of high frequency oscillatory ventilation
