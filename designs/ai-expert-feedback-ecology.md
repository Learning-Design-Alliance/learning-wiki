# AI Expert Feedback Ecology: a closed-loop cognition framework linking AI Twins, AI Tutors, dashboards, and learners

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article defines the AI Expert Feedback Ecology as "a closed-loop educational ecosystem in which AI Twins, AI tutors, dashboards, and learners continuously interact through cognition-aware feedback processes." AI Twins generate adaptive tasks; AI Tutors interpret behaviours into cognition reports; the ECD aggregates them; AI Twins reason through aggregated structures to generate interventions that start a new cycle. Cognition is treated as "an evolving interpretive structure continuously shaped through interaction, reflection, and intervention."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Continuous AI Tutor interpretation and dashboard aggregation to sustain the feedback loop
#### Constraints
- AI Twins perceive learners indirectly through cognition reports rather than raw behavioural logs

### Learning Goals
