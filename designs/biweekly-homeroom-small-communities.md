# Biweekly homerooms as relationship-centered small communities

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
McLane High School transformed its homeroom structure as a core RCS effort to build consistent relationships in a large school. The report explains that "Biweekly homeroom periods aimed to form small communities for McLane's large student population and enable consistent, multiyear connections." Through its RCS partnership, McLane refined this pattern by training select students to lead homeroom community-building efforts and providing related professional development to educators, embedding relationship-building into the regular schedule rather than treating it as an add-on.
