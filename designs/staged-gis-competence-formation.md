# Staged development of GIS competence across secondary grades

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes a two-stage model for forming GIS competence. The first stage, in grades 6-7, covers initial acquaintance with the atlas and basic techniques of working with electronic information. The second stage, in grades 8-9 studying 'Geography of Kazakhstan', adds digital overlay of map layers, preparation of territory descriptions, statistical data analysis, and construction of students' own thematic maps.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Progressive availability of atlas-based tasks aligned with the geography curriculum across grade levels
#### Constraints
- Stages are tied to the Kazakhstan geography course structure (initial course grades 6-7; 'Geography of Kazakhstan' grades 8-9)

### Target Learners
- Students in grades 6-9

### Learning Goals
- Progressive GIS competence
- Thematic map construction
- Spatial data analysis
