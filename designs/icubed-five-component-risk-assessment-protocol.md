# Five-component ongoing risk assessment protocol feeding mentor outreach to at-risk STEM students

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The mentoring interventions use an ongoing risk assessment with five components: scores from a first-day Quiz Zero in key math classes, responses to an Early Warning Survey sent to faculty teaching STEM courses, responses to a campus-wide First Year Initiative survey, student self-identification, and fall semester grades. Students identified as at risk are invited to meet their mentors, who may use an established protocol to help students assess and address risk factors. The Program Coordinator assists mentors with meetings, communication, and conducting the assessment.
