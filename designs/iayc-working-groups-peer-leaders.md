# IAYC working groups with peer leaders

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Working groups are the central organisational unit of the IAYC. Each group of around eight people is led by a former participant, often an undergraduate or postgraduate student with experience in astronomy or a relevant subject. The article states that "Working groups are central to the IAYC" and that this structure reflects the idea of affinity spaces, with participants coming together through shared interest in astronomy and advancing knowledge by working with others from different backgrounds.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A leader who is a former participant with astronomy or relevant subject experience
- A shared interest in astronomy bringing participants together
#### Constraints
- For many participants the camp is their first experience working independently or away from home

### Target Learners
- Young people aged 16-24 from 81 nationalities, mixed backgrounds and experience levels

### Learning Goals
- Collaboration across cultural backgrounds
- Independent project work
- International networking
