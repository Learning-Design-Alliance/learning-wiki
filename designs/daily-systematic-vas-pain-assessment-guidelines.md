# Implementation of guidelines for daily systematic VAS pain assessment integrated with routine vital parameters

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The second intervention component implements guidelines for daily, systematic pain assessment on the intervention ward immediately after the education. Daily pain assessment is "integrated as a required standard parameter together with the ordinary routine vital parameters such as temperature, pulse and blood pressure", assessed by VAS at three time points across three work shifts, with pain intensity, nursing action and follow-up documented in a bedside patient-specific study protocol.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assessment performed by RNs across three work shifts with documentation in the patient-specific study protocol at the bedside
#### Constraints
- At the time of writing it was not standard practice on general hospital wards in Sweden to screen for or systematically assess pain daily

### Target Learners
- registered nurses on surgical cancer wards

### Learning Goals
- systematic routine assessment of cancer-related pain intensity and follow-up
