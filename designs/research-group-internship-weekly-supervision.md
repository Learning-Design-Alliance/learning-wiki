# Research-internship pattern: full integration into a research group with at least weekly individual supervisor meetings and professional presentations

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The training embeds students in active research groups across a wide range of science and technology, from data analysis to detector and accelerator design. Supervisors "meet with their students on an individual basis, with meetings held at least once per week", and students participate in collaboration meetings where they present results in a wide professional environment. Students are encouraged to interact with as many colleagues as possible so the group profits from their productivity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Groups must be interested in offering a training program and assign adequate office space and computing power
#### Constraints
- Training spans only two months (August-September at Fermilab; end of July to end of September for ASI-sponsored space science placements)

### Target Learners
- Graduate-level physics and engineering students

### Learning Goals
- Experimental data analysis, detector and accelerator design, superconducting materials, advanced computing and technical tools
