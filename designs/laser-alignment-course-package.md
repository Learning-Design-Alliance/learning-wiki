# Complete laser alignment course package of eight tutorials, assessments, and instructor guides

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article presents an integrated instructional package for teaching experimental optical laboratory skills, consisting of eight tutorials (from aligning a laser along a bolt-hole line to aligning a lens to a beam path), a matching proficiency test for each tutorial, and an instructor guide for each tutorial. The author states that "A series of tutorials, assessments, and instructor guides are presented as a complete package for an upper-level undergraduate, or lower-level graduate, laboratory-based course, or extended new-student seminar." The package assumes no prior optical laboratory training and is designed so students follow the tutorials independently.
