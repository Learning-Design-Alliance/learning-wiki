# OpenHPI quantum channel with a nine-course MOOC curriculum on quantum computing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A dedicated channel on the OpenHPI platform, established in 2022, hosting free MOOCs on quantum computing. The kernel is an introductory curriculum of nine interconnected MOOCs developed from June 2022 to July 2023, surrounded by six additional specialized independent courses. The article states: "In 2022, a dedicated channel for quantum computing was established on OpenHPI (https://open.hpi.de/channels/quantum). The channel features 15 MOOCs addressing various aspects of quantum computing." Each MOOC comprises about 20 concise videos, 10 self-tests, and one final examination test within a two-week timeframe.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A MOOC platform supporting videos, self-tests, a moderated forum, and a one-attempt final exam within a defined timeframe.
#### Constraints
- After the timeframe ends, forums are closed and the final exam can no longer be taken; self-learners can only attend non-interactively.

### Target Learners
- Adult learners with little prior knowledge, including IT professionals, educators, and retirees

### Learning Goals
- Introductory quantum computing competences, mostly at Awareness (A1) and sometimes Literacy (A2) level of the European Competence Framework for Quantum Technologies
