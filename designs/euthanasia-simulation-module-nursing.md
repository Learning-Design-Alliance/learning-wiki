# Euthanasia simulation module for nursing students: online learning module, good-practice video, role-play scenarios, and DML debriefing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A simulation module developed to prepare nursing students for dealing with a euthanasia request. In the authors' words, "three key components were incorporated: a preparatory phase consisting of an online PowerPoint, a good-practice video, and a simulation scenario; a simulation role-play; and a debriefing session." The module's content was validated by four Belgian end-of-life care experts, and it was completed by 17 final-year nursing students from two University Colleges in Flanders, Belgium.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students complete a preparatory online learning module covering conversation techniques and the legal framework before the simulation session
- Content validated by domain experts in end-of-life care
#### Constraints
- Focused on the first phase of the euthanasia process, initial care and accompaniment, not the later phases
- Conclusions apply only to nursing students, not other healthcare students

### Target Learners
- final-year undergraduate nursing students

### Learning Goals
- handling euthanasia requests
- end-of-life communication
- knowledge of euthanasia legislation
