# Systems-based integrated preclinical curriculum with a structured humanities and community-based axis

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The new curriculum implemented in 2019 at BMS-UNESP replaced the traditional three-cycle, subject-based design with two cycles: a pre-clinical cycle (years 1–3) and a clerkship (years 4–6). Basic sciences were taught using a systems-based approach, integrating traditional subjects into courses such as one on the "cell" and one on the "nervous system". Social sciences disciplines previously taught independently were organized along a structured axis for the humanities, including interdisciplinary and community-based approaches.

### Learning Goals

- early contact with community and humanities perspectives
