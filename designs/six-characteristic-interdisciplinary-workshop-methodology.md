# A six-characteristic methodology for high-quality open interdisciplinary engagement between professionals and philosophers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article reports a workshop methodology developed over a five-workshop series to enable open interdisciplinary engagement between medical professionals and philosophers. It comprises six characteristics, including that "more time devoted to plenary discussion than to introductory speakers" and "matched multidisciplinary introductions providing two points of view on each topic". The authors consider the methodology crucial to the workshops' success and believe it could be usefully employed in other settings.
