# Tri-sectional modular surgical training pattern: didactic and virtual training, intensive centralized dry- and wet-lab training, then supervised real-life procedural training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The SERGS pilot curriculum organizes training into three consecutive modules: a roughly 1-month in-house didactic and virtual training part, a 1-week intensive hands-on course at a dedicated training center, and about 6 months of mentored in-house procedural training, followed by logbook and video-case assessment for certification. The article concludes that this "tri-sectional modular structure with a didactic part and a simulator- and expert-mentored stepwise procedural training in the dry- and wet-lab setting, followed by supervised performance under real-life-conditions in the operating room, proves feasible, acceptable and with good educational impact." It was modeled on the ERUS urology curriculum.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dedicated supervisors or proctors at the home center and access to a fully equipped training center for the intensive week
#### Constraints
- Virtual simulator access was limited at some home institutions, and structured assessment proved difficult to implement in practice

### Target Learners
- Certified gynecologists who are novices in robotic surgery

### Learning Goals
- Robot-assisted laparoscopic hysterectomy and pelvic lymphadenectomy skills
