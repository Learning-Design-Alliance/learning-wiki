# Conceptual change instructional approach for countering naïve science conceptions

## Description
The conceptual change method is a science-instruction framework the article adopts as the organizing approach for its electricity curriculum. It has two key processes: students are "presented with unavoidable and incontrovertible evidence that explicitly contradicts their existing na¨ıve conception(s)" and are then "guided just enough to allow them to generate an accurate conceptualization" of the domain. BEETLE II implements it with simulation exercises that contradict misconceptions followed by reflective dialogue, leveraging Socratic tutoring and self-explanation research.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instruction must present evidence that directly contradicts students' existing naïve conceptions
- A guided reflection stage must help students generate accurate new conceptualizations
#### Constraints
- The article applies it to the sub-topic of electricity and electric circuits

### Target Learners
- students with no prior knowledge of electronics and electricity

### Learning Goals
- conceptual understanding of basic electricity and electric circuits
- overcoming common physics misconceptions
