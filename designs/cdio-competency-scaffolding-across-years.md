# Scaffold CDIO competencies across program years (I-O, then D-I-O, then C-D-I-O) with ILB project complexity increasing and gap years between projects

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes acquiring CDIO competencies through instructional scaffolding across the four-year program: first-year courses focus on I-O, second and third year on D-I-O, and fourth year on C-D-I-O guidelines as a whole. At least one ILB of 16 credits is formed in each of the first three years, with design complexity evolving from one CDIO-ILB project to another. To avoid overwhelming students with major hands-on group work, a gap year is deliberately placed between each CDIO-ILB project.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Program map must schedule ILB projects in terms 2, 4, and 6 with gap years between them
- Course sequencing must align CDIO competency focus to year of study
#### Constraints
- The authors ask whether three ILBs in an eight-semester program is sufficient or excessive, leaving frequency an open question

### Target Learners
- undergraduate Bachelor of Engineering students across all four years

### Learning Goals
- progressive mastery of conceive-design-implement-operate competencies
- managing increasingly complex engineering design projects
