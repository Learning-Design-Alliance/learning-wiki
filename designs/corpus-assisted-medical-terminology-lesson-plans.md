# Corpus-assisted lesson plans for medical terminology using COCA Medical subcorpus data (concordancing, collocation, frequency)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Three 70-minute corpus-assisted lesson plans, each targeting the same eight cardiovascular terms from Chapter 10 of Medical Terminology: An Illustrated Guide, were compared against a lecture-based plan. The concordancing plan used "Handouts with 10–15 concordance lines per term from COCA Medical subcorpus" with pairs highlighting syntactic, collocational, and discourse-level patterns; the collocation plan used top-5 collocations per term (e.g., treat embolism) with sentence-writing; and the frequency plan used handouts with normalized frequencies and morphological breakdowns (e.g., fibrill- = 42.1/million). All groups shared identical learning goals, differing only in teaching method and post-teaching activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the COCA Medical subcorpus and corpus tools such as Sketch Engine or AntConc, which the authors note are open-access
#### Constraints
- The study used only eight cardiovascular terms of 2–3 syllables, selected to control frequency, semantic, and morphological complexity

### Target Learners
- EFL medical students at intermediate proficiency (CEFR B1–B2) in a medical terminology course

### Learning Goals
- Retention and contextual understanding of specialized medical terminology in English
