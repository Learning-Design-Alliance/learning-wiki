# A six-principle workflow framework guides the design of experimental tasks in physics lab courses

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article introduces a framework providing "a list of six design principles" arranged "in the pattern of a workflow guiding lab instructors in their development of experimental tasks for physics lab courses." The principles are target group, learning objectives, task conception, design of materials, implementation, and circumstances, each with research-informed categories. Principles 1–3 link to the Model of Educational Reconstruction, principles 3–5 to Action Research, with principle 3 bridging both and principle 6 linked to neither.
