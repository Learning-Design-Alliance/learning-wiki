# Four-pillar sustainability model for online study programs

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article applies a four-pillar sustainability model in which "The model consists of four sustainability pillars: economic, social, environmental, and ethical." The economic pillar covers resources and funding, the social pillar internal dynamics and community, the environmental pillar the physical, technical, cultural and psychological contexts, and the ethical pillar the values behind goals and working principles. The pillars are mutually interrelated and can be traded off; the authors use the model to analyze critical events and pivotal factors of an online doctoral program.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Analysis requires identifying pivotal factors connected to critical events from the perspective of each pillar
#### Constraints
- The authors note relying too heavily on a single pillar or even two could be potentially problematic for the sustainability of the program

### Target Learners
- doctoral students in online study programs

### Learning Goals
