# Tailored three-component FLUS training program for general practitioners (theoretical self-study with validated MCQ test, one-day hands-on course with LUS-OSAUS assessment, and supervised real-life scans)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The training program prepared GPs to learn the indications for FLUS, execute the validated 14-zone FLUS protocol, and interpret findings. It "consisted of" theoretical self-study concluding with a validated multiple-choice test (pass cut-off score 20 of 0-30), a one-day hands-on course completing with a practical LUS-OSAUS assessment under standardised conditions, and five to ten FLUS scans in the GP's clinic supervised remotely by respiratory-medicine FLUS specialists at EFSUMB level III competence. Its content aligned with Danish and European Respiratory Society FLUS training courses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants must pass the theoretical MCQ test at a cut-off score of 20 (range 0-30) and complete a practical LUS-OSAUS assessment, plus five to ten remotely supervised FLUS scans within one month of the hands-on course.
#### Constraints
- The study evaluated the program only in nine GPs who were already POCUS users, so generalizability to a broader GP population is uncertain.

### Target Learners
- general practitioners using point-of-care ultrasound

### Learning Goals
- learn indications for performing FLUS
- execute the 14-zone FLUS protocol
- interpret FLUS findings
