# Two-stage reading strategy training: awareness raising followed by application practice

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's training design unfolded in two stages. Stage one raised awareness through discussion of the questionnaire results and explicit explanation and demonstration of the selected strategies, using multimedia-computer aids. Stage two integrated the strategies into regular classroom activities, with teacher demonstration, group and pair work grounded in Vygotsky's view that "cognitive development occurs wh en concepts first learned through social interaction become internalized", and summary tasks linking reading to writing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Strategies selected for training should be those showing significant differences between efficient and non-efficient readers
- The same amount of reading text should be provided across conditions
#### Constraints
- The training was carried out in a natural teaching environment with second-year non-English majors over five weeks

### Target Learners
- non-English-major college students in an extensive reading course

### Learning Goals
- reading comprehension
- strategic reading
- summarizing
