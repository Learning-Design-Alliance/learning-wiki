# Developmental-Critical Model for AI-Mediated SSH Pedagogy with Six Checkpoints

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A three-stage pedagogical framework guiding learners from AI-dependent toward AI-critical interpretation, grounded in cognitive-developmental theory, hermeneutics, and critical pedagogy. It is "operationalized through six pedagogical checkpoints that scaffold interpretive autonomy": interpretive grounding prior to AI use, contextual anchoring of AI outputs, critical examination of algorithmic structures, hybrid authorship transparency, reflexive epistemic positioning, and dialogic engagement under human control. Each checkpoint addresses an observed risk including interpretive shortcutting, epistemic outsourcing, and loss of authorial voice, and is derived from the observed interactional patterns.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learners engage directly with primary materials before using AI tools (interpretive grounding)
- Pedagogical mediation that keeps AI functioning as scaffold rather than substitute for reasoning
#### Constraints
- The article states the model is "a flexible framework whose application may vary across disciplines, learner profiles, and levels of AI integration"
- Based on qualitative, context-specific observations from a single classroom; does not aim for statistical generalization

### Target Learners
- Lower secondary students (aged 13-15) in SSH education

### Learning Goals
- Critical AI literacy
- Epistemic responsibility
- Interpretive autonomy
