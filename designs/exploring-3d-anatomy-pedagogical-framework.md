# Pedagogical framework of Exploring 3D Anatomy: multimodal, experiential, and social learning with art-based cycles

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The course is underpinned by pedagogical principles including multimodal, experiential, and social learning; critical observation and visualization; and evidence-informed art-based learning approaches including Haptico-visual observation and drawing and Observe-reflect-draw-edit-repeat. The modular course structure is deliberately underpinned by Observe-reflect-draw-edit-repeat experiential learning cycles, with elements of building upon prior knowledge, experiential cycles, and social learning underpinned by theories of cognitive constructivism, experiential learning, and social learning respectively.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each exercise requires learners to engage in haptic and visual observation during practical art-based activities using easily available and accessible household objects
#### Constraints
- Anatomical resources/models are not required nor recommended; exercises use household objects such as forks, jars, citrus fruit, and boxes

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate medical students
- Novice anatomy learners

### Learning Goals
- Spatial awareness development
- Visualization of anatomical planes
- Critical observation skills

### Claims
