# Interactive educational video on oral medication administration hosted on Moodle

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A faculty-produced interactive educational video demonstrating oral medication administration on a trained standardised patient, uploaded to Moodle version 3.11.2 two days before the laboratory session. The video was recorded in one two-hour session, edited with Adobe Premiere Pro, synced with voice narration, and divided into three six-minute parts. It was verified for accuracy and quality by two professors and two clinical instructors before release.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to a learning management system (Moodle) for distribution
- Technical support team for video recording and editing
- Faculty verification of content accuracy before release
#### Constraints
- Developed and evaluated for a single nursing skill at one institution

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate second-year nursing students

### Learning Goals
- Oral medication administration knowledge and skill competency
