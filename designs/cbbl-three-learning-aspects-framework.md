# Three learning aspects framework: declarative knowledge, associative learning, and polythematic-crosslinking thinking

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article organizes what its case presentations teach around three learning aspects. First, "declarative knowledge has to be acquired" in basic medical sciences and stored accessibly in long-term memory. Second, "associative learning leads to the formation of neuronal connections" and underlies learning and discovering. Third, polythematic-crosslinking thinking links information meaningfully across domains. The authors present these steps as characteristic of gifted learners and researchers who connect seemingly unrelated areas and drive innovation, and they use the framework to justify the interdisciplinary, translational design of their e-learning cases.
