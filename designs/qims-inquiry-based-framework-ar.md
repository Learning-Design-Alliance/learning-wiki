# QIMS: an inquiry-based learning framework for leveraging the affordances of AR

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
QIMS is an inquiry-based learning framework proposed by the authors to leverage the affordances of augmented reality in teaching and learning. The article states that "In leveraging the affordances of AR, an inquiry-based learning framework, referred to as QIMS, was proposed in this study." It was operationalised into a learning package on plant reproduction for primary 5 students and evaluated against non-QIMS conditions.
