# Faculty development program using nano-lectures and Padlet to prepare stakeholders for flipped teaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A multi-stage faculty development program prepares stakeholders to implement flipped teaching. Pre-recorded 2–5 minute nano-lectures are circulated via WhatsApp; stakeholders record and submit their own videos, which are posted on Padlet, an online bulletin board; and a 60-minute workshop includes video evaluation, case discussion, reflection, and group discussion. The article reports that "majority of the stakeholders agree to implement flipped-teaching in their respective courses" following the program.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A WhatsApp group already in place for dissemination
- Access to the Padlet application
- A 60-minute faculty development session
#### Constraints
- Stakeholder support was assessed through informal feedback, not a validated instrument

### Target Learners
- medical school faculty stakeholders

### Learning Goals
- understanding and implementing flipped teaching
- active learning pedagogy
