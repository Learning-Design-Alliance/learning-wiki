# The reciprocal lattice as an intuitive tool for locating diffraction maxima, from tilted gratings toward X-ray diffraction on 3D crystals

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article introduces the reciprocal lattice as "the most useful tool for understanding the peak positions" of diffraction gratings. Using a vector derivation of the phase difference k∆l = −q·δR valid in general 3D configurations, the peak condition becomes kout,parallel = kin,parallel + 2πm/d, visualized by reciprocal-lattice points G0m = 2πm/d on the grating line and outgoing wave vectors ending where perpendiculars cross a circle of radius k (Fig. 11). The 1D construction is presented as a stepping stone to 2D and 3D reciprocal lattices and the Ewald sphere construction for X-ray diffraction on crystals.
