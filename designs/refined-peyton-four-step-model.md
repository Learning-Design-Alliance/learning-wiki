# Refined Peyton Four-Step Teaching Model combining video demonstration, interactive deconstruction, peer group practice, and PEARLS-based assessment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A refinement of the Peyton four-step method (demonstration, deconstruction, comprehension, performance) for OB-GYN basic skill training. The article states its adaptation "incorporates video instruction, peer learning, and Promoting Excellence and Reflective Learning in Simulation (PEARLS) feedback, creating a synergistic and complementary approach". Step one adds pre-course videos for self-paced study, step two adds interactive questioning, step three uses teams of 3–4 residents rotating recitation, demonstration, and scoring roles, and step four adds independent performance with PEARLS feedback and a mastery standard.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor training conducted prior to implementation, standardizing each skill's specific operation steps and integrating patient communication skills and medical humanities
- A standardized teaching process and the Clinical Basic Skill Operation Scoring Form established before delivery
- Learning resources such as instructional videos, lecture slides, and clear assessment criteria uploaded to a teaching management platform
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only in first-year OB-GYN residents at a single institution; the authors call for multi-center studies across specialties and contexts

### Target Learners
- first-year obstetrics and gynecology residents

### Learning Goals
- acquisition and retention of clinical basic procedural skills
- patient communication and professional behavior
- self-directed learning
