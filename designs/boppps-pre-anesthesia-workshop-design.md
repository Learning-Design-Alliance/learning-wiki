# BOPPPS-based pre-anesthesia care workshop with pre-class preparation, scenario-based participatory learning, and real-time feedback

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A three-session workshop implementing all six BOPPPS stages for pre-anesthesia care; the model "was purposefully adapted to pre-anesthesia care, incorporating pre-class preparation, scenario-based participatory learning, and real-time formative feedback." Pre-class, a Telegram group received a bridge-in video, guiding questions, objectives, and a diagnostic quiz; in class, "a combination of simulation, role-play, group discussion, and problem-solving methods was employed", followed by group case-scenario post-assessment and an instructor summary.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivery as a workshop of "three sessions, each lasting two hours" with identical educational content across comparison groups.
- A facilitation platform such as a Telegram group for pre-class bridge-in, objectives, and pre-assessment, plus simulation resources including mannequins and a clinical skills facility.
#### Constraints
- The article states "The single-center design and relatively short duration of the intervention limit the generalizability of our conclusions"; findings are preliminary evidence from one institution.

### Target Learners
- second- and third-year nurse anesthesia students

### Learning Goals
- patient assessment
- anesthetic equipment and drug preparation
- teamwork and readiness for safe pre-anesthesia practice
