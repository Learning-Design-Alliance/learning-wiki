# Near-peer workshop session structure: simplified concept explanations, then cadaveric prosection images, then clinical cases and interactive quizzes

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Each workshop follows a recurring session architecture: review sessions "start with simplified explanations of neuro-musculoskeletal or visceral concepts", are "followed by explanations of cadaveric prosection images", and are consolidated through "medical clinical cases and interactive quizzes". Workshops are collaboratively designed by three to four tutors, "requiring on average 6-10 hours to develop, and typically last one hour".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires cadaveric prosection images and medical clinical cases for each session
- Workshops require on average 6-10 hours of collaborative tutor design time
#### Constraints
- Sessions typically last one hour

### Target Learners
- medical students in a problem-based learning undergraduate medical curriculum

### Learning Goals
- consolidating anatomical concepts through clinical application
- connecting anatomy to medical pathologies
