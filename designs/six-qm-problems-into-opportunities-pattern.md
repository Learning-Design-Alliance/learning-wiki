# Reframing six teaching problems as six corresponding opportunities in quantum mechanics instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size

## Description
The article organizes its whole approach around a one-to-one mapping between six macro critical aspects of teaching QM and six opportunities, printed in Table 1. For example, "Discouraging conceptual intricacy" maps to "Appealing epistemological richness" and "Copenhageners' low popularity" to "Captivating narration of QM story". The pattern treats each obstacle as a motivational entry point rather than a barrier.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The teacher identifies which of the six critical aspects the class faces and selects the corresponding opportunity strategy
#### Constraints
- The author found addressing at most three critical aspects per year workable, and later combinations gave overall lower results

### Target Learners
- secondary students ages 14-19 in the final pre-university year of scientific high school

### Learning Goals
- foundational understanding of quantum mechanics
- learning motivation towards modern physics
