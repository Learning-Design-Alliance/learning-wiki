# Four-category differential diagnosis of learner needs: knowledge gaps, inadequate skills, attitude barriers, and adverse circumstances

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Before teaching about disparities, the teacher is directed to diagnose the learner's need using a four-way taxonomy applied to each case (Table 1): knowledge gaps, inadequate skills, attitude barriers, and adverse circumstances. The paper states "Learners may possess gaps in knowledge, attitudes and/or skills," and that "adverse circumstances — the context of clinical care — may challenge learners and contribute to provider-driven health care disparities," so the context of care itself becomes a teaching opportunity. Each category is matched to diagnostic questions (Table 2) and to a teaching strategy (Table 3).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A diagnostic step (probes or brief observation) to identify at least one need area before selecting a teaching strategy
#### Constraints
- Framed for teaching disparities at the bedside or in clinic; the taxonomy is illustrative, derived from the authors' four cases rather than validated

### Target Learners
- Medical students
- Residents

### Learning Goals
- Identifying whether a disparity-related performance problem stems from knowledge, skill, attitude, or care-context factors
