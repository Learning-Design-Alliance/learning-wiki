## Description
The article proposes human-centered design as an approach through which university teachers can adapt ERT designs to student needs. It fosters "a problem-solving approach through empathy building" (Baran and AlZoubi 2020), helping teachers build empathy with students and tailor teaching designs that reflect students' needs and supportive online learning environments. It operationalizes humanizing pedagogy and pedagogy of care in ERT contexts.
