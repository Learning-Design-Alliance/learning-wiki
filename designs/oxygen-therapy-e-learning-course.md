# Interactive e-learning oxygen therapy course for ED clinicians

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An interactive, fully online e-learning course covering oxygen therapy concepts and methods, developed from reference textbooks and national/international guidelines using Camtasia, Flip PDF and Storyline. It had three main sections including "definitions", "low pressure oxygen therapy methods", and "high pressure oxygen therapy methods", with voiced contents, pre- and post-test questions, a SCORM file for multi-platform use, and was hosted at www.oxygentherapy.ir for about a 20-hour course.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the website via individual usernames and passwords defined for the ED clinicians
#### Constraints
- Invasive oxygen therapy methods were not included, as they needed surgical skills

### Target Learners
- emergency department nurses and physicians

### Learning Goals
- knowledge of oxygen therapy concepts, methods, risks and complications
