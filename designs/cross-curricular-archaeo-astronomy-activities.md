# Cross-curricular outdoor activities in which one activity targets multiple subjects simultaneously

## Description
The project's summer school and master classes use a cross curriculum strategy in which multiple subjects are targeted during the same activity, for example an archaeological excavation activity using both history and geography to explain relative dating as stratigraphy. The authors report that the team were then able to reflect upon their experience and include improvements, and that feedback from teachers and students was positive. Ancient monuments such as megalithic stone circles are presented as offering an ideal learning and teaching environment where students work on project based learning activities.
