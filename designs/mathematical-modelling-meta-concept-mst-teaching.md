# Mathematical modelling as meta-concept for teaching measurement science and technology

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes that mathematical modelling of material entities should serve as the organising meta-concept for MS&T educational frameworks, because it is indispensable for defining measurands, designing measuring systems and evaluating measurement uncertainty. Semantic modelling of an entity precedes its mathematical modelling, crossing the boundary between material entities and their abstract images. In the AI era, this emphasis matters because AI tools are themselves semantic or mathematical models, such as large language models or universal approximators, so students equipped with modelling patterns can autonomously characterise any sensor type instead of memorising catalogues.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Detailed descriptions of instances of mathematical modelling in MS&T belong in undergraduate courses, and general methodology of mathematical modelling in graduate courses
- Students need patterns of sensor characterisation based on mathematical models to autonomously acquire exhaustive specifications
#### Constraints
- Some weakly defined measurements cannot be properly understood under the article's working definition of understanding as recognition of causal relationships

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and graduate students of measurement science and technology

### Learning Goals
- mathematical modelling of material entities
- defining measurands
- designing measuring systems
- evaluating measurement uncertainty
