# Aytürk Technique

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Aytürk Technique, developed by Ertürkler, is an original classroom technique for music and other disciplines built on "a rhythmic table pattern" and drawing "upon brainstorming, mind mapping, educational games, and gallery walk (board) techniques." Students arrange concepts or rhymes in a 16-cell Rhythmic Table and turn it into rhythmic or melodic structures, producing a musical product at the end of every activity. It aims at a participatory, creative and interactive environment in which students imagine, create and express themselves.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- It requires group work and some creativity from each participating student
- It requires the teacher to have class management skills
#### Constraints
- It may be useless when not associated with the course
- It is difficult to use in crowded classes
- It is not effective in solving difficult and complicated subjects
- Talented students may monopolize; shy students or those with speech disorders may struggle

### Target Learners
- preschool students
- elementary school students

### Learning Goals
- creativity
- rhythm and music education
- consolidation of course content
