# Real-world middle school family engagement strategies with accessibility, resources, and outcome monitoring

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The playbook catalogs recurring strategy structures used in middle schools: orientation sessions (How to Junior High 101), porch visits and community center meetups, positive postcards, High-Five Fridays phone campaigns, Family Learning Workshops (Parent University), Weekly Learning Connection updates, FACE committees, community partnerships with organizations like United Way, district strategic-plan integration, and leader book studies. Each is described through strategy, implementation, accessibility, resources, and outcomes components.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Accessibility provisions such as translation, interpretation, and virtual alternatives; allocated time and materials; tracking systems for equitable recognition
#### Constraints
- Strategies are drawn from middle school contexts and are meant to be tailored to each school's needs and cultures

### Target Learners
- middle school students and their families, including multilingual families

### Learning Goals
- family connection, communication, trust, attendance, and support of student learning at home
