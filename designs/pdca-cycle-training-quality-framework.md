# PDCA cycle management as a four-phase continuous quality improvement framework for procedural training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The PDCA (Plan-Do-Check-Act) cycle, "also known as the 'quality loop', is a management approach for continuous improvement". In this article it organizes ETI training for standardised training residents: Plan sets goals and key indicators, Do implements tiered training, SOPs and quality control, Check monitors real-time procedural compliance and feeds back data weekly, and Act implements targeted optimisations such as mandatory airway assessment. Root-cause analysis via a fishbone diagram identified factors in four aspects: "man, material, method and environment".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A PDCA team conducting root cause analysis and demonstration of possible influencing factors
- Real-time monitoring documentation and weekly data feedback meetings
#### Constraints
- Applied in a single emergency department with standardised training residents; the authors note it is a single-center retrospective study subject to temporal confounding

### Target Learners
- standardised training residents rotating through the emergency department

### Learning Goals
- endotracheal intubation technique
- difficult airway identification and management
- complication prevention
