# Compact 3.5-hour midwife training package on pregnancy nutrition, physical activity and weight management embedding motivational interviewing principles

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A half-day (3.5 hours) training package developed from an integrative-review scoping study, evidence-based guidelines, consultation with an MI trainer and an exercise physiologist, and observation of community midwives. It used group discussions, short lectures, food models and worksheets, plus filmed conversational scripts "utilising key principles of motivational interviewing" to support raising the sensitive issue of weight. Delivery used cost-neutral NHS venues with a registered dietitian as facilitator, limited to 10 participants per session to maintain interaction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered by a registered dietitian working closely with midwifery services
- Pre-course reading list emailed to participants
- Small session size (10 participants) to maintain interaction
#### Constraints
- Half-day format regarded as limited given breadth of content and complexity of some material
- UK has no guidelines for gestational weight gain, so US IOM guidelines were used as reference

### Target Learners
- practising community and integrated midwives (NHS pay bands 6 and 7)

### Learning Goals
- understanding risks of obesity in pregnancy
- maternal nutritional requirements
- physical activity recommendations in pregnancy
- supporting women to maintain a healthy safe weight
