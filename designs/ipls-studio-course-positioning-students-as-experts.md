# Studio-format IPLS course adapting Modeling Instruction discursive structures and positioning life-science students as experts

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The course is a first-semester introductory physics for life sciences course "taught in the studio format" that adapts available IPLS curricula and designs new materials aiming to make physics relevant to life science students. It adapts "discursive structures and participation frameworks from Modeling Instruction" and was designed so that "classroom structures, norms, and participatory roles were designed to position students as experts", leveraging students' biology and chemistry experience. Students engage in physics through argumentation and whiteboard meetings to reach classroom consensus.
