# One-day one-problem PBL cycle with five distinct learning phases

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
At the studied polytechnic, students work on a single problem for a whole day in teams of five under a tutor. "At the polytechnic the PBL day is divided into five distinct learning phases": problem definition, initial self-study, initial findings sharing, self-study, and presentation and elaboration. The pattern alternates collaborative theory construction and goal setting with individual and collaborative information search, ending in synthesis and evaluation of learning goals.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Small teams of about five students with a tutor, and learning resources available for self-study
#### Constraints
- Described for a polytechnic in which PBL is the instructional method for all modules and programs

### Target Learners
- polytechnic students in science-related modules

### Learning Goals
- self-directed learning
- theory construction
- collaborative problem solving
