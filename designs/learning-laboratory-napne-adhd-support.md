# Learning Laboratory (LL): weekly after-hours resource classes for students with learning disorders and ADHD

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Learning Laboratory is a Colégio Pedro II support setting, part of its Special Education Service (NAPNE), where students with learning disorders, ADHD and other underachievement-related issues (such as anxiety) receive extra classes once a week. A therapist and a teacher offer "computer and board games, plays, painting" among a collection of activities rich in learning potential. The LL aims to help students compensate for executive function difficulty by teaching management of externalized information and time control.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A therapist and a teacher, Resource Room-style after-hours scheduling, and a repertoire of activities rich in learning potential
#### Constraints
- The article states it is not possible to correct directly an executive function deficit; the LL instead teaches compensatory strategies

### Target Learners
- Elementary school children with learning disorders, ADHD, anxiety or other underachievement-related issues

### Learning Goals
- Compensating for executive function deficits; achieving academic success
