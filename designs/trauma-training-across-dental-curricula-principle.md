# Embed dental trauma management training and knowledge evaluation throughout undergraduate and postgraduate dental curricula

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Because surveyed dentists showed incomplete knowledge of avulsion management, the article recommends that theoretical courses, specific training and continuing education programs be built into both undergraduate and postgraduate dental curricula, paired with evaluation of acquired knowledge. It states these are "necessary to guarantee a high standard of care". It also points practitioners to the Italian national guidelines for prevention and management of dental trauma in children.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Curriculum space in dental schools for theoretical courses and specific training
- Continuing education programs for practising dentists
- Evaluation of the acquired knowledge
#### Constraints
- Grounded in a single-country survey of Italian dentists during the COVID-19 lockdown

### Target Learners
- dental students
- practising dentists

### Learning Goals
- emergency management of avulsed teeth
- adherence to Italian and international guidelines
