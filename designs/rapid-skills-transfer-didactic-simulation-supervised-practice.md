# Rapid procedural skills transfer pattern: short didactic and simulation sessions followed by supervised clinical practice with real-time checklist-based formative assessment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
This two-part training model combines Part I didactic and simulation sessions (lectures, demonstrations, 'dry' laboratory practice on mannequin stations, an MCQ examination with feedback) with Part II supervised clinical practice over two days. Competence assessment "was based on directly observing the individual steps performed correctly", using a global rating and instant formative feedback with repeats required for incorrectly performed steps. The authors used this model to train ten health workers in PrePex placement and removal in three days.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trainees must be SMC-certified health workers with prior surgical male circumcision experience of at least 50 SMC cases, per the article's eligibility criterion
#### Constraints
- The study only included operators with significant prior surgical SMC experience; extrapolation to those without such experience may be done with caution

### Target Learners
- SMC-certified health workers (nurses, clinical officers, physicians) in resource-limited settings

### Learning Goals
- Safe PrePex device placement and removal, screening, and adverse event management
