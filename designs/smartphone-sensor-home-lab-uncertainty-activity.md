# Home-lab activity using smartphone sensors and Phyphox to teach error analysis and uncertainties

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A home-lab experimental activity in which students use the built-in sensors of their own smartphones, accessed through the Phyphox app, to record hundreds or thousands of repeated measurements in seconds and analyse fluctuations, histograms and Gaussian fits. The article proposes these experiments, performable at home, "to teach error and uncertainties", and reports the activity can be "carried out in an agile way, without waiting times", leaving more time for discussion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students need access to a smartphone with built-in sensors and an app (the article uses Phyphox) to record and analyse the repeated measurements.
#### Constraints
- The optimal-number estimate "is optimistic and must be examined in case of having other sources of systematic errors".

### Target Learners
- first-year science and engineering students in introductory laboratory courses

### Learning Goals
- statistical treatment of errors and uncertainties
- interpreting histograms, mean values and standard deviations
