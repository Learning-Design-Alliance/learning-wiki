# Spiraling professional learning design for abstract science content with elementary generalist teachers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The professional learning program applies prior K-12 quantum education recommendations—using visuals and hands-on approaches, analogies to make abstract concepts accessible, focusing on concepts over mathematics, and aligning with existing standards—organized through "a spiraling method to return to concepts over subsequent professional development sessions." Interactive Google Slides and small group discussions support and assess teacher learning, with deliberate surfacing of misconceptions about atomic structures and quantum concepts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multiple sessions across which concepts can be revisited
- Interactive materials and small group discussion structures
- Books and compiled online resources available at key points
#### Constraints
- Designed for subject-matter generalist elementary teachers who may lack confidence teaching STEM

### Target Learners
- elementary school teachers

### Learning Goals
- quantum conceptual understanding
- age-appropriate pedagogical strategies for quantum
