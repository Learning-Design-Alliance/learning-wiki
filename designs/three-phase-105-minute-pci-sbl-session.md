# Three-phase 105-minute SBL session for percutaneous coronary intervention CPD

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The SBL session studied: a 105-minute format with introduction, simulation, and debriefing phases, in which "Four trainees formed a team and stayed at the same station to complete the hands-on simulation procedure, where each trainee had 15-20 minutes to be the first operator in pre-assigned scenarios". Sessions included 5 minutes of introduction, 15 minutes of interactive case discussion, 70 minutes of hands-on simulation, and 15 minutes of debriefing, delivered by senior interventional cardiologists to junior trainees at an international conference.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trainers prepared with clear goals and methods; trainees with basic knowledge, skills, and a positive attitude to teamwork
#### Constraints
- Limited to CPD training organized by a specific academia-industry collaboration for cardiovascular training

### Target Learners
- Junior interventional cardiologists in CPD

### Learning Goals
- Management of complications during percutaneous coronary intervention
