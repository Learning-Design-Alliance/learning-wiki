# Two-year Diploma in Optometric Technician program at Asmara College of Health Sciences

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A two-year diploma program started in 2009 at Asmara College of Health Sciences with support from Brien Holden Vision Institute and the Ministry of Health, training mid-level optometry technicians to provide category II (Visual Function) services. The curriculum comprises 74 credit hours, with "33 credit hours (44.6% of total 74 credit hours)" of basic, biomedical and social science courses in year one and professional courses in year two, including ophthalmic optics, dispensing optics, ocular disease, clinical optometry procedure and clinical hospital practice. Students are exposed to didactic, practical and clinical activities under faculty supervision in affiliated eye care centers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Entrants must have completed high school (grade 12) with physics, biology and mathematics as core subjects, and graduation requires a cumulative grade point average of > 2.0.
#### Constraints
- The article states training on ocular diseases is very basic, training time and scope in pediatric refraction are limited, and low vision training is basic.

### Target Learners
- high school graduates in Eritrea selected from a national pool of applicants

### Learning Goals
- refractive care, dispensing services, primary eye care including identification and referral, primary low vision rehabilitation and community eye care
