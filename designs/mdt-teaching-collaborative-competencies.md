# Multidisciplinary team teaching as a mechanism for communication, clinical reasoning, and collaborative decision-making

## Description
The article describes the multidisciplinary team approach as one that "integrates professionals from various specialties, enhancing students' communication, clinical reasoning, and collaborative decision-making through real-time, simulated team interactions". In the intervention, weekly sessions by an interdisciplinary team used case presentations, treatment planning exercises, and decision-making simulations to situate learning in authentic multidisciplinary contexts.
