# Standardized operation pathway structure for skills training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A reusable lesson-level design in which each clinical operation is converted into a procedural pathway with fixed components. The article states that "Every operation procedure formed different but regular operation pathways, including quality requirements, pre-operation preparation, operation process, post-operation treatment, and effect evaluation." Scores are detailed for the operation assessment standards, reducing invigilator subjectivity, and scientific predictions of reasonable mistakes during procedures are emphasized and analyzed during lesson preparation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Collective and individual lesson preparation by a trained skills operation group
- Reference to textbooks, guidelines, consensus, and literature when condensing teaching content
#### Constraints
- Developed and evaluated for hospital nursing skills training; the article does not claim generalization beyond that setting

### Target Learners
- Newly admitted nurses in hospital in-service training

### Learning Goals
- Standardized, safe execution of clinical nursing operations
- Error prevention in nursing procedures
