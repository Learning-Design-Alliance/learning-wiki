# 'Communication between healthcare professionals in the clinical field' training session

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 3-hour interprofessional communication training for third-year medical students consisting of "small group role-play, small group discussions and presentations, and an overall presentation and discussion for the whole class." 149 students were randomly assigned to 12 small groups of 12–13; each group role-played and video-recorded either a good or a bad example of conflict resolution for one of two conflict cases, evaluated performers with a worksheet, and discussed improvements with a facilitator. The authors position it as the earliest ('isolation') stage of IPE on Harden's spectrum because no other professions participated.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A facilitator per small group discussion room; cases developed by faculty; video-recording equipment and group discussion rooms
#### Constraints
- Not complete IPE because students majoring in other professions did not participate together; groups of 12–13 meant some students only watched role-play; each group addressed only one case and one good-or-bad example

### Target Learners
- Third-year medical students early in clinical rotation

### Learning Goals
- Interprofessional communication and conflict resolution skills with other healthcare providers
