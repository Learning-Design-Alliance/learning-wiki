# Four-workshop virtual faculty development curriculum on acknowledging and addressing microaggressions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A longitudinal, voluntary, virtual faculty development curriculum built with Kern's six-step approach, comprising four workshops on "recognizing and naming microaggressions," apologizing when harm has been experienced, setting expectations surrounding microaggressions, and debriefing microaggressions. Each workshop ran 2-3 hours on Zoom and combined community agreements, ice breakers, didactic content, small-group experiential exercises, and large-group debriefing, with facilitator guides, handouts, and pre/postsurveys released as appendices.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- At least one facilitator per workshop with DEI knowledge, small-group facilitation, formative feedback, and debriefing skills
#### Constraints
- Developed and piloted at one institution with GME program directors and associate program directors; funding and facilitator time may not transfer to other institutions

### Target Learners
- Faculty, initially residency and fellowship program directors and associate program directors

### Learning Goals
- Acknowledge microaggressions
- Structure apologies as the source of a microaggression
- Compose expectations surrounding microaggressions
- Facilitate debriefs after witnessing or experiencing a microaggression
