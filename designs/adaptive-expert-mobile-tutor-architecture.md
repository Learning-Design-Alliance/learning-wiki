# Adaptive mobile expert-system tutor architecture with four functional levels and server-side expert knowledge base

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes an "adaptive and intelligent tutor by expert system for mobile devices" that estimates each learner's reading ability and "recommends appropriate content to individual learners according to learner's simple feedback responses and difficulty parameters of the contents". Its expert knowledge splits into "course knowledge" and "learning technique"; the system is "based on SMS, MMS and so on" with the expert simulator and knowledge base at server side, aiming at every-time, everywhere low-cost learning. The system is described at four functional levels so different parties can address individual levels.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mobile devices and high-bandwidth wireless networks with connectivity, since "disconnection caused by handovers seriously affect the quality of information services"
#### Constraints
- The article states M-Learning implementation "is more complex than computer-based learning" and that wireless environments differ dramatically from wireline ones as learners move

### Target Learners
- mobile learners, with examples drawn from 5th and 6th grade students

### Learning Goals
- adaptive delivery of course content matched to learner level and learning style
