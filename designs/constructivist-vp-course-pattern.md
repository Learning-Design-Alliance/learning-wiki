# Constructivist course design pattern: repeated varied cases, learning by doing, safe error, and permanent feedback around a VP

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The course was structured around five stated constructivist principles: "Learning is a process of individual construction" through activity and interaction with others; meaningful learning anchored on prior experiences; "To confront repeatedly the same problem with different variations"; learning by doing in situations similar to the real professional environment; and the chance of making mistakes and getting proper permanent feedback. Each weekly two-hour session centered on a VP abdominal-pain case targeting a specific aspect of history taking and clinical reasoning, followed by small group question-guided discussions.
