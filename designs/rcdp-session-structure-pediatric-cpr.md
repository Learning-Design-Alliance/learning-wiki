# One-hour RCDP session structure: objectives review, uninterrupted baseline scenario, 30 minutes of cycled RCDP practice, and closing feedback with performance graphs

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's session design divides 1 hour into 50 minutes of instructional time and 10 minutes of course feedback and evaluation. Instruction begins with "the first 10 min for presenting the objectives, program strategy, and a punctual review of the CRA care algorithm", then 10 minutes of uninterrupted team performance for baseline data, then 30 minutes of RCDP in which incorrect actions pause the scenario for directed feedback and restart. Closing feedback shows each group a graph of compression quality before and after training.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Two instructors per round: one operating the scenario, one recording and extracting performance data
- A high-fidelity manikin and compression-recording software to capture pre- and post-intervention data
#### Constraints
- Session size of six participants was determined by the institution's CRA care protocol

### Target Learners
- Multidisciplinary pediatric hospital care teams

### Learning Goals
- Mastery of the pediatric resuscitation algorithm and high-quality chest compressions
