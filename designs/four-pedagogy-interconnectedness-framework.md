# Interconnectedness of experiential learning, active learning, flipped classroom and collaborative learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article argues (Figure 1) that "the four theories of learning are interconnected to form both theoretical and practical perspectives". It traces collaborative learning's antecedents to Kolb's experiential learning and Dewey, defines active learning through student activity and engagement, and defines collaborative learning as "an instruction method in which students work in groups toward a common academic goal". The flipped classroom supplies the technology tools through which students learn concepts before applying them collaboratively in real-world projects.
