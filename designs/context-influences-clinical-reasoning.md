# Context influences the clinical reasoning process, and changed contexts require adaptation of the learning environment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Drawing on the perspective that context shapes clinical reasoning, the authors argue that the pandemic's rapid global change of the teaching context "requires manifold and quick adaptations of the teaching environment", while offering "a great opportunity for students to experience how context influences the clinical reasoning process". The VP adaptations were designed to demonstrate this: pre-assessing possible COVID-19 infection, considering COVID-19 as a new differential diagnosis, and weighing regional probability of diagnosis.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teaching cases must reflect the contextual factors (e.g. pandemic status, regional disease probability) under which learners will reason
#### Constraints
- VPs were originally designed for a non-pandemic environment and do not automatically reflect new contextual factors

### Target Learners
- Medical students
- Health profession students

### Learning Goals
- Understanding how context influences the clinical reasoning process
