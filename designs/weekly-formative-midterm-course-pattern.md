# Weekly formative midterm course pattern with creative grading, feedback, and metacognitive pep talks

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
This lesson-level course pattern converts two traditional midterms into weekly comprehensive formative exams with immediate in-class feedback, metacognitive pep talks, and five grading schemes from which the highest result is automatically awarded. The article distinguishes it from weekly quizzes: "there was a grading scheme that elevated the importance of the exams while allowing for success despite early failures", explicit regular metacognition emphasis, immediate feedback and class discussion, comprehensive midterms, and close alignment with the final exam. It is designed to provide multiple opportunities to attempt demanding problems and incentives to improve.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Exams must feel as important as graded exams rather than practice quizzes, with immediate detailed feedback after each exam and a grading scheme that drops early low scores without penalty.
#### Constraints
- The article notes several components (tests, grading scheme, feedback, pep talks) could individually be responsible for the gains and had not been disaggregated.

### Target Learners
- first-year introductory biology students, including majors and premedical/predental nonmajors

### Learning Goals
- scientific reasoning skills such as evaluating experimental data
- higher-order cognitive skills at the apply/evaluate levels of Bloom's taxonomy
