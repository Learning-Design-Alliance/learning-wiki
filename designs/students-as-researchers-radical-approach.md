# Students as Researchers: a radical approach positioning students as agents of change rather than data sources

## Description
Students as Researchers is presented as a radical alternative to conventional student consultation, in which students undertake research on their own schooling and act as agents of change. The abstract contrasts it with consultation that embeds "unwitting manipulation" and reaffirms "the compelling nature of the ``Students as Researchers'' approach". The approach is grounded in the author's joint work with a UK high school running since 1996.
