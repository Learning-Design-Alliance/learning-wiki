# Eight-stage guided inquiry laboratory activity cycle structured into the 5E lesson format

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The GIBLEI approach organizes each laboratory lesson as an eight-stage cycle: receiving semi-structured problems a week ahead, researching and planning experimental procedures, outlining experiments with hypotheses and variables, sharing designs with peers, conducting experiments and recording data, presenting conclusions linked to theory, answering follow-up questions, and assessment. Daily lesson plans were structured into the 5E format (engage, explore, explain, elaborate, evaluate), with most guided inquiry activities placed in the exploration phase.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher and laboratory technician support at each stage, including provision of materials and facilitation of peer design discussions
#### Constraints
- Designed for Grade 10 biology unit topics; implemented over a complete unit spanning eight weeks

### Target Learners
- Grade 10 secondary school biology students

### Learning Goals
- Hypothesis formulation, experimental design, data interpretation, and drawing conclusions
