# Transformed PHYS-1140 introductory physics lab course with electronic lab notebooks

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The transformed course is a one-credit stand-alone first-year lab serving over 700 students each semester, structured around "six 50-minute lectures and 12 two-hour lab activities" grouped into four modules: skill building, mechanics, electronics, and optics. A new component is electronic lab notebooks kept in OneNote on tablets and graded online with detailed rubrics by 22 TAs; "Lab notebooks constitute 72% of the ﬁnal grade for the course." Grades also come from clicker questions, online prelab activities, lab participation, and online assessments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A tablet-based OneNote workflow, a learning management system (Canvas) for notebook submission, detailed rubrics, and graduate TAs with the skills to grade notebooks online
#### Constraints
- The structure and content are constrained by institutional space, funding, and the skills of the graduate teaching assistants

### Target Learners
- first-year engineering and physical science majors

### Learning Goals
- development of experimental skills and experimental physics epistemology, including scientific communication via lab notebooks
