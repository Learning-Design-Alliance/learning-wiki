# KOMPIDEM inter-professional dementia care course (lectures, PbL tutorials, care-facility visitations, final colloquium)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A roughly 30-contact-hour (one credit point) optional undergraduate course developed by the KOMPIDEM project at the University of Lübeck to improve medical and nursing students' competencies in evidence-based care for people with dementia. It comprises four components: "Introductory lectures to ensure a common basic knowledge level among all participants", "Problem-based learning (PbL) in small groups to promote a more detailed and activity-oriented engagement of the students with certain aspects of dementia care as well as inter-professional communication", visitations to care facilities, and a final colloquium with PbL-group presentations. Three major topics structure the content: causes of cognitive and non-cognitive symptoms of dementia, communication strategies, and treatment strategies for cognitive and non-cognitive symptoms.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Integration into both medical and nursing degree programs with attention to differing education levels (academic versus non-academic)
#### Constraints
- Course schedule compatibility with compulsory classes in both programs is limited, as stated by the authors

### Target Learners
- Medical students in the clinical training phase (semesters 5-7)
- Third-year general nursing students

### Learning Goals
- Evidence-based care for people with dementia
- Inter-professional communication and cooperation
- Communication with people affected by dementia
