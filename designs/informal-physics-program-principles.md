# Shared program principles across informal physics programs: design and build, teach and serve the public, communicate, lead, work in teams, and build connections across academic levels

## Description
The five Texas A&M informal physics programs share a common set of design principles. As the article states, "All programs have similar principles: through participation in these programs the students design and build, teach/serve the public by applying their physics knowledge, communicate scientiﬁc principles to non-scientists in an exciting way, lead, work in teams, and last but not least, have a chance to build connections across academic levels." These principles are schematized in Figure 1 and provide opportunities for leadership, experiential learning, peer-mentoring, and networking within an academic department.
