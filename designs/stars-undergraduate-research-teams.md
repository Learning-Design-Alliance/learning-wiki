# STARS program: undergraduate research teams conducting critical NANOGrav science through weekly telecons and hands-on observing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The STARS program engages undergraduate students at NANOGrav institutions as a point of entry into pulsar and gravitational-wave research, with two concurrent goals: educating students in GW science and developing "teamwork, leadership, and speaking/presenting skills", and providing meaningful research experiences with tangible contributions to core NANOGrav science. Students present short talks in weekly videoconferences, and undergraduate observers use Arecibo and the GBT remotely or on site to conduct a sizable fraction of routine timing observations — in one recent semester, 40% of all Arecibo timing observations. In 2019, 90 undergraduates were involved in NANOGrav research, including 60 regularly attending STARS telecons across eight institutions, and 11 NANOGrav publications in the past two years included undergraduate co-authors.
