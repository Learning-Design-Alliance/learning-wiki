# AI-assisted multi-advisor BOPPPS teaching model for clinical pharmacy education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A hybrid teaching model for clinical pharmacy training at Xiangya Hospital combining a multi-advisor system, staged rotations, BOPPPS sessions, and supervised ChatGPT use. The article reports that "a teaching model was developed that consists of five components: advisor system, teaching strategy, teaching method, teaching mode, and assessment methods," implemented across a staged learning process from theoretical course through TDM and genetic testing to clinical practice, with periodic assessment plus OSCE replacing a traditional final examination.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A multi-advisor team recruited from the hospital with clearly defined teaching responsibilities (TDM advisors, genetic testing advisors, clinical pharmacists, physicians), holding mid-semester and end-of-semester review meetings
- A staged curriculum bridging the theoretical course and clinical practice, with all groups belonging to the pharmacy department
#### Constraints
- The article states the proposed teaching approach "may not work out as well in other professional training contexts" beyond organ-transplant clinical pharmacy

### Target Learners
- clinical pharmacy interns
- postgraduate pharmacy students
- advanced students with work experience seeking additional professional knowledge

### Learning Goals
- clinical practice abilities
- critical thinking
- accuracy in medication decision-making
- interdisciplinary communication
