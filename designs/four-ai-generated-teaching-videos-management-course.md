# Four AI-generated short teaching videos for a management course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 against) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study's materials are "four short teaching videos for a management course" developed with generative AI tools, compared against human-generated videos on the same subjects. Each video served as a treatment unit in the online experiment, with participants rating learning experiences after each video and completing a multiple-choice exam at the end.
