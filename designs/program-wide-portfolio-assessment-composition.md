# Program-wide portfolio assessment in composition as a professional-development and consistency practice

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An assessment practice in which writing-program faculty review sampled student portfolios across sections to gauge consistency and improve instruction. Interviewees "identified program‑wide portfolio assessment as a best practice, but the practice has not yet been widely implemented." Faculty engaging in it report value both for gauging cross-section consistency and for improving their own classroom activities through shared discussion of assignments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Resources and capacity to support faculty time for cross-section norming and review
#### Constraints
- One campus dean noted the campus no longer has resources for paid lecturer time to read assignments across sections for norming purposes

### Target Learners
- First-year writing students across course sections

### Learning Goals
- Consistent instructional quality and assignment expectations across writing course sections
