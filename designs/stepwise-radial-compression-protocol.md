# Stepwise compression protocol: rolled gauze placement, ≥ 5 min full pressure, distal pulse monitoring, and 6-h in-situ fixation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The CDHT protocol teaches a fixed sequence for radial artery decannulation hemostasis: rolled gauze placed 1–2 cm proximal to the entry point with its long axis parallel to the artery, "Consistent pressure was maintained for at least 5 min to achieve primary hemostasis", pressure then reduced to assess oozing, finger pulse oxygen saturation monitored to prevent over-compression, and gauze/tape left in situ for 6 h with a removal-time tag. Gauze coverage is kept within 15–20% of wrist perimeter.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sterile rolled gauze and adhesive tape, a tag recording catheter removal and gauze-removal times, and pulse oximetry on the ipsilateral index finger during compression
#### Constraints
- Manual compression force remains variable and operator-dependent; the authors note it is "very variable and not reproducible owing to subjective fatigue"

### Target Learners
- early-career residents learning arterial catheter management

### Learning Goals
- achieving hemostasis within 5 min while preventing delayed hematoma and over-compression
