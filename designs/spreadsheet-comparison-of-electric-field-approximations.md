# Spreadsheet-based comparison of electric field approximations

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A spreadsheet-based tool and activity in which students enter a charged object's parameters and a distance step, then compute columns for the field of a charged particle and the quotient of the two fields. The article presents spreadsheets as tools that let students "compare the validity of diﬀerent approximations and quickly analyze the dependence with diﬀerent parameters", and notes the analysis extends to problems without analytical solutions.
