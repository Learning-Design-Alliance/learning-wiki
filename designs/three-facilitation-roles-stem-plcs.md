# Three facilitation roles for STEM learning teams

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Drawing on research the synthesis cites, facilitators of STEM learning teams fulfill three distinct roles: "facilitating knowledge, including helping to find relevant STEM or STEM education expertise if/as needed; processing facilitation to attend to the structure and interaction of the groups; and focusing facilitation to keep the group on target." Facilitation, whether face-to-face, online, or combined, requires engagement, tact, careful listening, and content and pedagogical knowledge. The framework treats helping adults learn as a distinct expertise from teaching children.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Facilitators need content and pedagogical knowledge plus engagement and tact
#### Constraints
- An expert teacher does not necessarily make an expert facilitator of a learning team

### Target Learners
- Facilitators of K-12 STEM teacher learning teams

### Learning Goals
- Supporting productive collaborative teacher inquiry in STEM PLCs
