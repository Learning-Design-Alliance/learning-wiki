# TFA summer training institute combining supervised practice teaching, coaching, and coursework

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The TFA training institute is a 5- to 7-week residential program in which corps members practice teaching in a real classroom under supervision while receiving observation, feedback, and coursework. The report describes how the institute "includes teaching summer school in a real classroom under the supervision of a regular classroom teacher", observation and feedback from TFA instructional coaches, small-group practice and reflection sessions, lesson planning clinics, and coursework in instructional planning, classroom management, diversity, and literacy development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A residential training institute with summer school teaching placements, plus follow-up regional orientation of 1-2 weeks covering student achievement goals, lesson planning, data use, and relationship building
#### Constraints
- Training varies by region, though the report says it usually includes an induction, the institute, and a regional orientation

### Target Learners
- novice teachers entering high-need public schools

### Learning Goals
- instructional planning and delivery
- classroom management
- literacy development
