# Course infrastructure: IBM Qiskit framework with RasQberry simulator and cloud access to real quantum machines

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course gives students access to the university's HPC infrastructure and selects IBM Qiskit as the quantum programming framework because it "provides sufficient didactic resources, including an online textbook and a series of tutorials to support the students in their learning". Quantum hardware access includes a laboratory simulator, a RasQberry device offered by IBM that augments students' personal simulation capabilities, and execution on real quantum machines through the open plans of providers such as IBM Quantum and Amazon Braket.
