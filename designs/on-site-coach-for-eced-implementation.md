# Designate an on-site instructional coach to monitor and support ECED implementation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
ECED's staffing requirements state that implementation "Requires a designated on-site instructional coach or leader to monitor and support implementation", noting "This position may look different depending on size of school". Both curricula also "require a reallocation of existing school staff to teach each of the courses". The coach role is part of the program's implementation model rather than an evaluated manipulation in the outcome study.
