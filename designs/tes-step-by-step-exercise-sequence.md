# Step-by-step simulation training sequence from basic synthetic exercises to biological-material exercises

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A structured progression of seven timed exercises moving from basic synthetic-material tasks (Teddy bears, Matches, Beans for tissue manipulation; Circles for cutting and dissection; Glove finger for suturing) to biological-material tasks on swine stomach (dissection and excision; suturing) that more accurately replicate intraoperative conditions. Each exercise has defined completion requirements assuring quality of execution, and the program "consists of standardized exercises with targeted training of essential skills". Supervision with feedback during and after each round is built into the delivery.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each exercise explained and demonstrated by a trainer before the start; supervised hands-on activity with cues, practical tips and feedback during and after each round
#### Constraints
- The program trains basic skills only; it does not go as far as teaching TaTME, serving as a base before more advanced techniques

### Target Learners
- general surgery residents
- surgeons in training focused on colorectal surgery
- specialist surgeons new to transanal endoscopic surgery

### Learning Goals
- basic skills for transanal endoscopic surgery: tissue manipulation, cutting, dissection and suturing
