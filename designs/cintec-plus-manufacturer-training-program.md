# Manufacturer-provided two-course dual stain training program with examined teaching slides and expert-led review of difficult cases

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The training program consisted of two 1-day courses provided by the manufacturer and led by an expert in dual stain interpretation. The first course "included lectures of the interpretation guide" and "ended with an exam of 40 teaching slides provided by the manufacturer, with a passing grade of 90%"; both cytotechnicians passed. A second, identical course in December 2019 reviewed difficult patient slides and consensus slides with the expert using a multiheaded microscope. The program preceded slide evaluation beginning in August 2019.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Training delivered by an expert in dual stain interpretation
- An examined set of teaching slides with a defined passing grade
- Access to difficult and consensus-based cases for joint review with an expert
#### Constraints
- Both trained cytotechnicians had no previous experience with preparation and interpretation of dual stained slides
- The study used SurePath liquid-based cytology samples; results might not be replicated with other cytology fixatives

### Target Learners
- Novice cytotechnicians in routine screening laboratories

### Learning Goals
- Accurate interpretation of p16/Ki67 dual stain cytology slides

- Dual Stain Concordance Rises With Slides Reviewed [+M]
