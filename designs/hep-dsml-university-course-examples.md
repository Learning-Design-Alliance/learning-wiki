# University courses at the physics/DS/ML intersection developed by HEP researchers (UCSD, MIT, Princeton, Illinois)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A set of whole courses and teaching modules developed by particle physicists for undergraduate and graduate instruction at the intersection of physics, data science and machine learning, offered as reference points. The authors note that "Particle physicists have been active in generating course curriculum and in many cases entire courses around the intersection of physics, data science and machine learning", describing the UCSD capstone, MIT, Princeton and Illinois courses with their topics, formats and projects.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Curriculum may take the form of whole courses or specific teaching modules usable in multiple courses or other forms of educational delivery.
#### Constraints
- The authors present only "a small sampling of courses and development eﬀorts in data science and machine learning for education", included as a point of reference.

### Target Learners
- fourth-year data science majors
- undergraduate and graduate physics students
- non-computer-science students wanting ML foundations

### Learning Goals
- applying modern machine learning techniques to particle physics data
- building and testing an ML pipeline
- analyzing and interpreting scientific data
