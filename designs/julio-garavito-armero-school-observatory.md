# Julio Garavito Armero school observatory and its solar observation program

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Julio Garavito Armero Observatory at Gimnasio Campestre in Bogotá, built in 2000, hosts classes and activities that involve children of different ages in science. It keeps a record of solar observations since 2001, and the acquisition of a H-alpha solar telescope "allowed to acquire images of prominences, filaments and solar flares". Teachers and students publish solar-cycle articles in the school journal El Astrolabio and share images with the Spaceweather website, and the observatory now mentors two public schools in Bogotá.
