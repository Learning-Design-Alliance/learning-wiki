# Edtech Cybersecurity and Privacy AAA online professional development course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An online PD developed in Articulate Rise for K-12 administrators, technology facilitators, and teachers, comprising a getting-started module, pre-test and self-assessment, and four content modules mapped to the AAA framework and aligned with ISTE standard 1d. Each module "included an overview, learning objectives, informative videos, interactive practices, and wrap-ups", with wrap-ups containing real-life scenarios. Participants completed ongoing knowledge checks with feedback through professional characters and scenario-based assessments applying content to real-world school cases. Fifty K-12 personnel from nineteen public school systems in a southeastern US state completed it.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivery via a web-based e-learning authoring tool supporting multimedia integration
- topics identified by cybersecurity experts and tailored via a needs assessment
#### Constraints
- Evaluated with a single-group pre-post design without a comparison group; all participants were from one southeastern state, which the authors note may limit generalizability

### Target Learners
- K-12 school administrators
- technology facilitators
- teachers

### Learning Goals
- cybersecurity awareness, vendor questioning skills, and response actions for educational technology
