# 3D printing-assisted family-school-hospital collaborative education model for urinary system tumor early screening

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
An educational model combining 3D printing-assisted visual models of urinary system anatomy and pathology with a family-school-hospital collaborative program delivered via family WeChat groups over 6 months. Weekly online group activities included health education, case discussion and remote tutor guidance. The model integrates "3D visual training, multi-stakeholder collaboration, and family-level health decision-making" to cultivate medical interns' core competencies in tumor early detection.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Family WeChat groups with universal coverage among extended families in China
- 3D printing technology and professional medical digital software for model reconstruction
- Clinical tutors, technical staff and family participants for multi-stakeholder collaboration
#### Constraints
- Validated only for urinary system tumors
- Non-randomized convenience grouping by dormitory allocation
- 6-month intervention reflects only short-term effects

### Target Learners
- Clinical medical interns

### Learning Goals
- Urinary system tumor early screening knowledge
- Core competencies including social medical curiosity, self-directed learning ability, doctor-patient communication, inter-professional teamwork, and transformative learning readiness
