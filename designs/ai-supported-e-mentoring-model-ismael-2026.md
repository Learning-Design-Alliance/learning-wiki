# AI-supported e-mentoring model integrating human mentoring with AI-driven support across engagement, analysis, feedback, and reflection cycles

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes a dynamic, iterative e-mentoring system in which pre-service teachers generate inputs through learning activities, teaching practice, and reflective interaction; an AI system analyzes these inputs to provide personalized feedback, while human mentors offer contextual guidance and socio-emotional support. The authors state it is 'a dynamic, iterative system integrating human mentoring with AI-driven support to enhance both pedagogical and emotional development'. AI is positioned as augmenting, not replacing, the human mentor.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Human mentors must provide contextual guidance, professional judgment, and socio-emotional support alongside AI-driven feedback
#### Constraints
- AI does not replace the human mentor but augments the mentoring process, per the article's own framing

### Target Learners
- EFL pre-service teachers in practicum

### Learning Goals
- teacher self-efficacy
- emotional intelligence
- pedagogical and emotional development
