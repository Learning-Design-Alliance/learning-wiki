# cellVIEW VR immersive learning unit on HIV

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An immersive VR learning environment built on the tool cellVIEW for this study, presenting a guided tour through an HIV cell in 3D. Macromolecular structures "only became visible when the learner moved towards or into a component of the virus", and learners could move freely and observe structures from all sides. Verbal explanatory texts were presented either as integrated visual text whose rotation dynamically adapted to the learner's position, or auditorily via headset, with identical wording across conditions. The learning phase lasted on average 11 minutes and was self-paced, with no replay possible.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Head-mounted display with roomscale tracking and controllers
- Text integrated into the VRLE and dynamically rotated for readability
#### Constraints
- Replaying content or returning to previous content was not possible

### Target Learners
- adult students (aged 19-37, mostly psychology/education participants)

### Learning Goals
- macromolecular biology: HIV structure and processes within the virus
