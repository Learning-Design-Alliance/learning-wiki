# Visioning and alignment process adapted from the cycle of value approach

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A department-level change process adapting the cycle of value approach, consisting of three iterated phases: align, act, and adjust. The alignment phase elicits faculty mental maps via surveys and interviews; the action phase enacts 30-, 90-, and 180-day action plans and builds capacity; the adjustment phase assesses success and generates new plans. In the Charms Department, 26 of 36 faculty (72%) responded to the survey and interviews, and the department voted unanimously to engage.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A departmental champion to encourage productive engagement
- Departmental support secured through existing governance processes
- Redistributed or additional resources to overcome time constraints and bolster communication and collaboration
#### Constraints
- The process is anticipated to last between one and two years; the first adjustment phase had not yet occurred at writing

### Target Learners
- an entire academic department's faculty

### Learning Goals
- developing a shared vision for undergraduate education and mechanisms for achieving it
