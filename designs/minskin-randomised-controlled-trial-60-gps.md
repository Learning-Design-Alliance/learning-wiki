# minSKIN randomised controlled trial of 60 GPs comparing multifaceted teledermatology feedback plus equipment against training-course-only control

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a prospective, two-armed randomised controlled trial "randomised and controlled at the GP level" involving 60 general practitioners in the Canton of Zurich. Competence in skin cancer diagnosis is the primary outcome, "measured as the percentage of correctly classified pictures of skin lesions", scored on a visual analogue scale at three time points (T0, T1, T2) with new pictures each time. Randomisation is carried out centrally by the study centre using Stata software, and blinding of GPs is stated to be impossible.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- 60 GPs providing basic medical care with a workload of at least 20 hours per week who will not retire or move away during the study period
- Registration at a trial register (ISRCTN29854485) and ethics approval from the Zurich ethics committee
#### Constraints
- Blinding of the GPs is not possible

### Target Learners
- general practitioners

### Learning Goals
- competence in the diagnosis of skin cancer
