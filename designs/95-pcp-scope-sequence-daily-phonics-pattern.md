# Structured scope-and-sequence lesson pattern with daily 20-30 minute explicit phonics instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The program organizes instruction around a scope and sequence of lessons per grade — 25 lessons for Kindergarten and 30 lessons for each of Grades 1-5. Each lesson "focuses on specific phonics skills, provides examples of high-frequency words, and contains information about other skills addressed within the topic," delivered in short daily sessions of 20-30 minutes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher's edition, student workbooks, manipulatives, and digital presentation files per grade kit
#### Constraints
- Instruction is designed to fit a 20-30 minute daily period, which teachers in one study reported was sometimes challenging to fit without minor adaptations

### Target Learners
- Kindergarten through fifth grade students in whole-class instruction

### Learning Goals
- Sequential phonics skills such as short vowel CVC, consonant blends, consonant digraphs, long vowel silent-e, and phonograms
