# The Responsible & Ethical AI (R&E AI) course: a bichronous, college credit-bearing course with near-peer mentorship for Title I high schools

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The R&E AI course is a college credit-bearing course on responsible and ethical AI for students in Title I and Title I-eligible high schools, offered free of charge in partnership with the National Education Opportunity Network. It "integrates foundational AI concepts with ethical and socio-technical reasoningthroughasynchronousinstruction,near-peermentorship,andsynchronous, discussion-based instruction", delivered via Canvas with a secure AI sandbox. In its inaugural year nearly 180 students from 12 schools completed it with a 97.8% completion rate.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Partnership infrastructure for recruitment, coordination with school-based educators, and alignment with college access initiatives
- Near-peer teaching fellows and school-based co-teachers, with standardized rubrics supervised by the course professor
#### Constraints
- Offered at no cost to students, supported through donations or school- and district-level subsidies

### Target Learners
- High school students in Title I and Title I-eligible under-resourced schools

### Learning Goals
- Foundational AI concepts
- Ethical and socio-technical reasoning about AI tradeoffs
- Academic agency and college-level expectations
