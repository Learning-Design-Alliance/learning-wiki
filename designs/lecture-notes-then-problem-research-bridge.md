# Lecture-notes-then-problem structure bridging undergraduate teaching to research

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The package pairs each computational assignment with handbook theory chapters, mimicking the familiar undergraduate rhythm of reading lecture notes followed by attempting a problem. The article says the handbook is written to "promote the development of intuition early in the study of the relevant physics", especially intuition that is difficult to promote in ordinary research papers, and that it is "styled to mimic typical undergraduate texts, while still being oriented towards research students".
