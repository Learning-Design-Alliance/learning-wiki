# Show format elements: short home-made movies introducing topics and upbeat music between experiments

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Two ingredients introduced by the students in the show's first year structure its presentation style. First, "Short ( < 90 sec) home-made entertaining movies" introduce the physics topics into which the show is divided, such as mechanics, magnetism, and low temperatures. Second, experiments are accompanied by up-beat music between explanations, which the article says creates "a unique energetic atmosphere" appreciated by the young audience and a local radio station that twice broadcast live from the show.
