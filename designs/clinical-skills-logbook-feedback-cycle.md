# Clinical skills laboratory logbook feedback cycle with global ratings and debriefing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The clinical skills laboratory operates an experiential learning cycle in which "each learning cycle begins with students engaging in concrete experience by performing clinical examination skills", followed by structured tutor and peer feedback recorded in a logbook. Performances are evaluated "using global ratings rather than numerical marks, aiming to foster mastery orientation rather than performance orientation", with tutor debriefing after each logbook assessment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must demonstrate competence in clinical examination skills on standardized patients at the end of each six-week hybrid PBL body system-based theme
#### Constraints
- There is no standardized progression in feedback expectations from years 1 through 3, which may impact the development of feedback literacy over time

### Target Learners
- first- to third-year undergraduate medical students

### Learning Goals
- clinical examination skills competence
- self-regulated learning
- feedback engagement
