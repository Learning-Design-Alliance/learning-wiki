# Connectivist teaching sequence for Contemporary Physics in initial physics teacher education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A structured teaching experience in which 21 prospective physics teachers in four working groups identified digital materials on physical phenomena, exchanged interpretations in WhatsApp discussions, related course concepts to classmates' contributions and external resources, and produced group reports. The sequence "brought together the selection of resources, discussion of their practical applications, interaction through digital spaces, the preparation of reports and an assessment of the work produced". Groups produced eleven reports each on topics such as quantum mechanics, optics and radiation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students organised into working groups with weekly resource selection
- A digital discussion space (WhatsApp) for exchanging interpretations
- Group reports recording topics, resources selected and conclusions
#### Constraints
- Assessed within one particular sequence; the article says the assessments "should be understood in that context"

### Target Learners
- prospective physics teachers in initial teacher education

### Learning Goals
- understanding contemporary physics concepts
- argumentation and critical questioning
- interdisciplinary connections
- use of digital resources for learning
