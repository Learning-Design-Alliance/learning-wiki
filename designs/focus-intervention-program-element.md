# FOCUS Intervention co-requisite program with wrap-around academic support services

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The FOCUS Intervention is a comprehensive co-requisite program in which students placed into developmental mathematics bypass the traditional developmental sequence and enroll concurrently in a developmental mathematics course and the credit-bearing College Algebra course. The article states "Students enrolled in the FOCUS Intervention program were registered in a developmental mathematics course and credit-bearing mathematics course concurrently". Wrap-around services included weekly/monthly seminars, mentoring, and required tutoring; a faculty panel selected applicants based on criteria such as repeated developmental failure and first-generation status.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty selection of candidates from the applicant pool
- Mandatory pretest and posttest THEA assessment at the university testing center
- Required participation in academic support services
#### Constraints
- Admission was selective rather than open; placement was based on university-defined cut-scores often higher than state standards

### Target Learners
- Underprepared college students placed into developmental mathematics, including first-generation students and veterans

### Learning Goals
- Completion of credit-bearing College Algebra
- Reduced time to degree
