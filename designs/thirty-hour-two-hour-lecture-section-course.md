# Thirty-hour lecture course with each section corresponding to a two-hour lecture

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The course was sized and segmented as follows: "The course was initially designed to fit in thirty hours, each section corresponding to a two-hour lecture." The lecture notes are organised into major sections (Newton's physics, Einstein's theory of relativity, the general-relativistic world) that each map onto a two-hour lecture block, giving a modular structure for an intensive master-level programme.
