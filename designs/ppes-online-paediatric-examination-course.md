# PPES online multimedia paediatric physical examination skills course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Paediatric Physical Examination Skills (PPES) course is an online multimedia course on the University's Canvas instance (Ulwazi), consisting of five modules: General Examination, Vital Signs Measurement, Nutritional Assessment, Respiratory Distress and Noisy Breathing Assessment, and Assessment of Hydration and Perfusion. It includes narrated and unnarrated skills demonstration videos, audio recordings, clinical photographs, and infographics, with "the use of local South African patients and instructors in clinical settings in the videos". The recording sequence "approximates the first two steps of Peyton’s four-step teaching approach", with testing videos followed by feedback videos.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Hosted on the university learning management system; developed by paediatricians, a health professions educationalist, curriculum designers and instructional designers.
#### Constraints
- Videos range from 30 s to 5 min; the course was designed to introduce the basic paediatric examination and augment lecture-based and bedside teaching at the pre-clinical GEMP II level.

### Target Learners
- Fourth-year (GEMP II) undergraduate medical students

### Learning Goals
- Core paediatric physical examination concepts and skills
