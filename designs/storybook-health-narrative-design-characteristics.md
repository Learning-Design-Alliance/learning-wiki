# Storybook design characteristics for sensitive health content with young children: rhyming narrative, neutral characters, and no tobacco-product display

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The CHANT storybook embodies a set of design characteristics the authors enumerate for teaching young children about tobacco smoke without provoking curiosity. The narrative "uses rhyming words, with the text having a sing-song quality" and is read aloud; characters are "depicted as essentially neutral for race and ethnicity"; and "There will be no display of tobacco products so as not to provoke curiosity." The exposure scene shows a smoke cloud rather than tobacco products, and the story models a protective response (reacting with concern, then bathing at home).
