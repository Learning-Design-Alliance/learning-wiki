# Disciplinary knots in wave physics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Drawing on the Model of Educational Reconstruction and previous empirical investigation, the authors identify five conceptual knots where learning difficulties usually appear: "Waves as function of several variables", described as a hidden trouble even for brilliant students; energy transport, essential to distinguish waves from other periodic phenomena; the superposition principle, which clarifies many phenomena; analogy in wave phenomena, where difficulties in recognizing the same behaviour across contexts such as total reflection, diffraction, beats and interference are common; and resonance, which is easy to observe but difficult to understand and which many students have never studied. The learning path was designed as a pilot study to address these knots in the best conditions for teaching and deepening physics topics.

### Learning Goals
