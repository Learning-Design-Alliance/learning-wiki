> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
Wellness spaces are dedicated school environments established to foster community and well-being. Project Cal-Well supported "six wellness spaces in the three participating districts," including the Bear Cave at Ross Middle School. The brief emphasizes that the spaces "were developed with direct input from students," aligning the physical environment with student preferences so the spaces act as inviting, supportive environments within the Tier 1 universal supports component.
