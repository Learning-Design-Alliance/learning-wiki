# Puzzle booklet of ~20 puzzles on excretory activities used as post-lecture reinforcement

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The researchers created 'A total of 200 questions on excretory activities' using wordmint.com, organized into 'Approximately 20 puzzles … each containing approximately 10 questions', printed as a booklet. It was distributed at the end of each lesson hour, collected, checked for mistakes, discussed individually with students, and returned for one week of repetition. Five faculty members evaluated its content and quality before use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students solve the puzzles after theoretical and laboratory instruction on the same topic
- Faculty expert review of puzzle content and quality
- Prevention of sharing between intervention and control groups
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only for the topic of excretory activities in a first-year fundamentals of nursing course

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate nursing students

### Learning Goals
- theoretical knowledge of excretory activities (urinary and intestinal elimination)
