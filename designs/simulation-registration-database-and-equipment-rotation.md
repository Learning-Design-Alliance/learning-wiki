# Individual-level simulation registration database with local ambassadors, plus rotating pediatric simulation equipment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The intervention's data infrastructure is an online database in which "local simulation ambassadors register all simulation training sessions", capturing individual unique identification, profession, department, date and time, and scenario learning objectives, running from January 2023 to April 2024 in both regions. Supporting the training itself, pediatric mannequins and hardware were acquired and "Equipment will be placed on a rotation schedule, with each department having access to it for three weeks", with maintenance and servicing between rotation cycles. Equipment includes two each of Premature Anne, Sim NewB Light, Sim baby Light, and SimJunior Kid mannequins, plus SimPads and monitors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Local ambassadors responsible for registration completeness and post-registrations; three-weekly updates to ambassadors; budget for equipment upkeep and potential replacements
#### Constraints
- The protocol states it cannot be entirely confident that the registration of conducted simulations is fully comprehensive, and missing data consistency across groups must be considered

### Target Learners
- Healthcare professionals in pediatric departments in both intervention and control regions

### Learning Goals
- Enabling dose-response analysis of outcomes against actual extent of simulation-based team training
