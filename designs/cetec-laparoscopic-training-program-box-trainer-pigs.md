# Standardized 32-hour laparoscopic training program combining box trainer exercises and pig surgery

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A structured laparoscopic skills program at the Centro de Experimentação e Treinamento em Cirurgia of Hospital Israelita Albert Einstein for third-year Gynecology and Obstetrics residents. Training occurred "four hours weekly, for eight consecutive weeks", with three weeks of box trainer and bench exercises (eye-hand coordination, dissection, suturing) and five weeks of surgeries in pigs, totaling 32 hours assisted by the same preceptors with advanced laparoscopic experience.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Attendance requirement: absence for more than 15% of proposed periods excluded the resident from the study
- Same experienced preceptors assisted throughout the 32 hours
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only in third-year Gynecology and Obstetrics residents at a single Brazilian center
- No validated evaluation tools for laparoscopic skills existed in Brazil per the authors

### Target Learners
- third-year Gynecology and Obstetrics residents

### Learning Goals
- basic laparoscopic surgical skills including eye-hand coordination, dissection, and suturing
