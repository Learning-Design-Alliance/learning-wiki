# Boston Public Schools public prekindergarten program with curricula and coaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A district-run, non-means-tested prekindergarten program that in 2008–2009 "served approximately 2,045 children in 69 elementary schools" and provided "a year of free full-day prekindergarten in an urban public school setting". It paired the OWL language and literacy curriculum and the Building Blocks mathematics curriculum with a district-developed coaching system, and was evaluated with a regression-discontinuity design on 2,018 children.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All prekindergarten teachers must have at least a BA and obtain a masters degree within 5 years; early childhood coaches held masters degrees and had prior early childhood teaching experience.
#### Constraints
- The study could not identify causally which of the inputs, curricula, coaching, or simply attending prekindergarten, constituted the most active ingredients.

### Target Learners
- four- and five-year-old children in an urban public school district

### Learning Goals
- early language and literacy
- early mathematics and numeracy
- executive function
- emotion recognition
