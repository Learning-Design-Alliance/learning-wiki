# Plotting LHC Discovery activity

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Plotting LHC Discovery is a Data Portfolio activity directed at preparing students to read discovery plots. Students first make a simple mass histogram of the J/psi meson with background events, then distinguish the background model from the bump at the meson's mass. The paper states students learn "to identify signal and background, to find the mass of the particle from the peak of the signal, and to qualitatively evaluate the significance of the peak from its width and its height relative to the background."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students work from data tables assembled into histograms, then apply the same feature-identification steps to recent discovery plots.
#### Constraints
- Actual discovery plots, such as the 2015 CMS and LHCb plots, are substantially more complicated than the J/psi signals used in the activity.

### Target Learners
- pre-university students

### Learning Goals
- identifying signal and background in mass histograms
- finding particle mass from a peak
- qualitatively evaluating peak significance
