# Level-graded physics problem sets (PPM levels 1–3) distinguished by the number of linked cases and equations

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article uses a three-level problem scheme introduced by Abdullah (2014) that distinguishes test difficulty "based on the number of equations needed to solve the problem". Level-1 items need a single embedded formulation (S = vt); level-2 items connect two cases with different conditions; level-3 items link three cases and require deep thought across multiple concept schemes. The pattern was applied to linear motion at constant speed with escalator problems at each level.
