# Four-component design of eMINTS: sequenced professional development, coaching, technology integration, and online resources

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report enumerates four key components of eMINTS. Sequenced professional development delivers "approximately 140 hours of professional development focused on integrating technology into the classroom and developing lessons that use inquiry-based learning" over 2 or more years. Coaching provides one-on-one classroom sessions by instructional specialists plus group coaching to build school learning communities. Technology integration supports inquiry-based instruction, differentiated learning, and assessment. Online resources supply an eMINTS portal with assessments, lesson plans, example classroom websites, and online courses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained instructional specialists to deliver professional development and coaching
- Individual student access to Internet-connected computers or tablets for technology integration
#### Constraints
- A shorter version, eMINTS4All, includes fewer professional development sessions and coaching visits

### Target Learners
- K–12 classroom teachers

### Learning Goals
- Inquiry-based, technology-supported instruction
- Classroom assessment and classroom management
- Building a community of learning in schools
