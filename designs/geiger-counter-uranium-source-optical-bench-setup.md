# Geiger-counter experimental setup with aligned uranium sources on an optical bench

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The path uses a commercial Geiger counter usually used for dosimetry that can detect alpha, beta and gamma rays, collecting data as counts without unit conversion and downloading them into a computer. The detector and sources are aligned by means of supports on an optical bench. Sources include small Uranium sheets in a plastic holder (m1 = 0.152 g), other Uranium sheets (m2 = 0.888 g), and fluorescent marble, as shown in Fig. 1.
