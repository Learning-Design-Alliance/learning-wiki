# ADDIE-based computer program for ninth-grade English listening and reading comprehension

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A researcher-designed computer-based program whose content was taken from the listening materials and reading comprehension texts in Student's Book (Action Pack 9), built with the help of a computer engineer. Its design "depended on mastery learning", in which students who do not achieve mastery on a task "are given additional support in learning and reviewing the information" before moving on. In listening exercises a student can repeat the audio scripts depending on his ability, and reading exercises included pictures to aid explanation. Questions and answers were accessed through the program to facilitate comprehension.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A computer laboratory equipped with not less than twenty computer sets
- A trained teacher for the experimental group
- Piloting of the program before classroom implementation
#### Constraints
- Validated for ninth-grade modules 5 and 6 of the Jordanian curriculum only; judged by a jury of two university professors and two Ministry of Education computer teachers

### Target Learners
- ninth-grade male EFL students aged 14-15 in Jordan

### Learning Goals
- listening comprehension
- reading comprehension
