# Three-level developmental progression of word-recognition instruction with an older-student variant

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The curriculum organizes word-recognition instruction into "three developmental levels of progression in word recognition: simple alphabetic (SIPPS Beginning Level), spelling-pattern (SIPPS Extension Level), and polysyllabic/morphemic (SIPPS Challenge Level)." A separate variant, SIPPS Plus, covers the simple alphabetic and spelling-pattern phases but is designed specifically for older students in grades 4–12, allowing the same progression to serve adolescent struggling readers.
