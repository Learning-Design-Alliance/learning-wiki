# Creative Computational Problem Solving (CCPS) model: five interactive phases structuring computational thinking development through creative problem solving with programming and robotics

> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The CCPS model (Chevalier et al., 2020, 2021) is a framework for structuring the development of computational thinking in educational settings, described by the article as "one of the most comprehensive and validated frameworks" in this area. It organizes learning through creative problem solving and integrates programming and educational robotics as central tools. It is structured around five interactive phases: Understanding, Idea Generation, Formalization, Implementation/Programming, and Evaluation, in which outcomes are analyzed, errors debugged, and processes improved iteratively. This study operationalized the five phases through a two-hour workshop with rotating stations and guided creative challenges.
