# Didactic template structuring each e-learning lesson with objectives, explanation, practice, self-assessment and tests

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The curriculum follows a repeating didactic template: introduction; defining goals and requirements; lessons containing "Objectives, Explanation of the topic , Practice tasks , Summary, Self-assessment questions , Test questions"; supplements; bibliography; glossary and key notions; trial tests A-C; and exam tests A-B. The material was also designed to fulfil educational requirements attributed to Josef Kraus based on modern brain research, including multi-channel teaching, activation of students, practice, restful parts, provoking attention and curiosity.
