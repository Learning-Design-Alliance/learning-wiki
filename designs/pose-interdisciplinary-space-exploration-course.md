# PoSE: a team-taught interdisciplinary course on philosophy and science of space exploration at UTSA

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
PoSE is a new team-taught interdisciplinary course at the University of Texas at San Antonio in which philosophers, astrophysicists, and STEM educators cross-fertilize their disciplines. The authors state their goal is to design and teach a course that will "help overcome disciplinary silos to advance our understanding of space and critically examine its ethical ramifications" and "better educate the public on how science works." Content spans history and philosophy of astronomy, demarcation criteria, and ethical questions about space exploration.
