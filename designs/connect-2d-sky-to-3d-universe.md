# Connect the two-dimensional projected night sky to the three-dimensional structure of the universe through a simulated journey

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Voyage module applies the principle that learners should ”conne ct the two-dimensional, projected view of the night sky to the actual three-dimensional struct ure of the universe”. By travelling in an animated starship through the Solar System and the Milky Way, users learn the distance scale and the layout of the local universe while re-encountering objects met in the first module.
