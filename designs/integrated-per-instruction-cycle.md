# A cycle of integrated instruction combining physics pretests, research-based curricula, PER literature discussion, homework, and a tri-area post-test

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's recurring lesson-level cycle for integrating content, pedagogy, and education research: pretests on the physics and on what introductory students might believe, instruction using published research-based curricula, discussion of the related research literature, physics-focused homework, and a post-test covering physics, pedagogy, and research. The article states the cycle includes "pretests on the physics which will be studied" and "a post-test on all three areas of physics, pedagogy, and research and how they intersect." Class time is split approximately equally among the three elements.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to published research-based curricula and the associated PER literature for each content unit
#### Constraints
- Courses were altered from a two-semester sequence to two stand-alone courses due to the many interdisciplinary courses taken by MST students

### Target Learners
- practicing and future high school and university physics teachers
- physics graduate students doing PER

### Learning Goals
- deep physics content knowledge
- familiarity with research-based instructional materials
- insight into student thinking through education research
