# Three-condition within-subjects teacher study of AI-assisted learning design

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A within-subjects study in which thirteen higher education teachers designed learning activities under three conditions: without AI, with AI access, and with AI after training on effective AI interaction strategies. Design artifacts were evaluated for attainment of predefined pedagogical goals and complemented with measures of cognitive effort, training evaluation, and adoption intentions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Design artifacts evaluated against predefined pedagogical goals
- Cognitive effort, training evaluation, and adoption-intention measures
#### Constraints
- Small sample of thirteen higher education teachers
- Findings reported at study level for this population and task

### Target Learners
- higher education teachers

### Learning Goals
- learning design quality
- teacher cognitive experience with AI tools
