# Weyl area rule and periodic orbit theory activities comparing theoretical spectral staircases with numerically generated eigenvalue data for 2D wells

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In L13 students compared the Weyl area rule N(k) = (A/4π)k² − (P/4π)k against a numerically generated 'staircase' function of eigenvalues for 2D infinite-well geometries such as the isosceles right triangle (A = L²/2, P = (2 + √2)L); Fig. 3 shows this comparison including the perimeter-correction term. The activity extends to modern periodic orbit theory, where 'it is easy to generate a minimal Mathematica©R program to evaluate the necessary Fourier transforms to visualize the contributions of the familiar (and some n ot so familiar) orbits in such geometries,' linking coursework spectra to research methods and experiments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A long list of k (or E) values for a given geometry, available in closed form for several shapes
#### Constraints
- The Weyl result 'will be an approximation to a smo othed out version' of the staircase data

### Target Learners
- junior-senior undergraduate physics majors

### Learning Goals
- spectral statistics
- connections between classical orbits and quantum spectra
