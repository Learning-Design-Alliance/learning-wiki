# MBCL platform: iPad, Apple Pencil, LiveBoard, Google Forms, Google Drive, and Blackboard for in-class cooperative learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The MBCL platform is a mobile technology-based cooperative learning setup whose core components are the 2018 iPad, Apple Pencil, the free LiveBoard app, and the classroom projection screen, which together "provide a real‐time information exchange of students' group study progress between the students and instructor." Blackboard manages student groups, Google Forms runs peer evaluation with results saved automatically to the instructor's Google Drive, and Google Drive stores whiteboard results. The platform is portable across classrooms and shareable among instructors, and Android or Windows tablets and alternative whiteboard apps can substitute for the Apple hardware and LiveBoard.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A podium computer, a projection screen, and a wireless Internet connection in the classroom
- Instructor familiarization with hardware and software, estimated at 1–2 hours of facilitated workshops plus 1–2 hours of self-study
#### Constraints
- Requires purchasing a limited number of iPads and Apple Pencils; students need time in the first MBCL class to learn and practice the platform

### Target Learners
- undergraduate biology students

### Learning Goals
- cooperative learning
- knowledge integration
- knowledge synthesis
