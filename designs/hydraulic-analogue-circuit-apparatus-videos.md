# Hydraulic analogue apparatus and freely available YouTube experiment videos for online first-year EM labs

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A physical apparatus set comprising a large glass container battery (~35 cm tall, ~10 L), soap-treated colored water, polyethylene and Teflon capillary resistors of 94.5 cm length, and a glass-tube capacitor with a ruler for 0.5 mm resolution height reading. The author states "The videos of the experiments are freely available for anyone to use," and the recorded videos "allow each student to collect unique datasets that they then analyze."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A digital scale to measure mass flowrate as an ammeter, calibrated microscope imaging with ImageJ to measure tube radii, and video recording of the fluid heights
#### Constraints
- Soap is added to suppress surface tension effects so the water penetrates the narrow capillaries; a few psi of air pressure is used to discharge the capacitor instead of a valve

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate physics students in fully online electromagnetism courses

### Learning Goals
- collecting one's own unique experimental dataset
- understanding Ohm's law and RC circuit charging
