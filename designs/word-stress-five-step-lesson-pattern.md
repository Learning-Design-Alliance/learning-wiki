# Word-stress lesson pattern: model correct and incorrect stress, discriminate, clap stress patterns, peer-mark dialogues, present orally with self-monitoring criteria

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A five-step lesson pattern the digest offers as an instantiation of the communicative-cognitive framework. The teacher presents vocabulary with "both correct and incorrect word stress" and introduces the concept; learners identify stressed syllables in nonsense syllable sequences; they clap stress patterns of vocabulary words in unison; pairs read scripted dialogues while one marks stress patterns and they give one another feedback; finally learners give oral presentations assessed for correct production and evidence of self-monitoring of word stress.
