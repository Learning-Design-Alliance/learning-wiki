# Four interconnected conversation flows (MI-consistent, MI-inconsistent and two neutral) as the V-CCT's core interaction design

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The V-CCT's dialogue is built from "four conversation flows; an MI-consistent flow, an MI-inconsistent flow and two neutral conversation flows", where the neutral flows are somewhat MI consistent or somewhat MI inconsistent. The flows are interconnected so the user can move between MI-consistent, MI-inconsistent and neutral conversations and get back on track when diverging from the MI-consistent interaction. The authors present this interconnection as an instructional process supporting conscious effort and reinforcing MI knowledge through activities enhancing higher order thinking rather than replication of content.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The MI-consistent flow is developed first, following the four MI processes of engaging, focusing, evoking and planning, and serves as the basis for the other three flows
- Response options are presented in random order to mitigate against the user guessing which option to select
#### Constraints
- The neutral flows allow the designers to limit the number of VC responses while still maintaining a logical flow in the conversation

### Target Learners
- Postgraduate health professional students practising MI conversations

### Learning Goals
- Recognise and consciously choose MI-consistent versus MI-inconsistent dialogue moves during client conversations
