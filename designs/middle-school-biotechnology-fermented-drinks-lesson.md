# Middle school biotechnology fermented-drinks lesson demonstrating asset-based instruction and divergent formative assessment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A snapshot vignette of a middle school science lesson in which English learners at various proficiency levels work with non-English learner peers to apply biotechnology principles: researching fermented drinks made by family members and neighbors, creating one from an existing family recipe, and developing an original drink for a local target audience. A student's aunt shares her fermentation expertise, and the teacher checks in with groups, asking open-ended questions and providing feedback.
