# Implement mandatory ward-based clinical courses in the national pharmaceutical curriculum to teach clinical skills

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article concludes that because pharmacists must be taught clinical skills such as application of clinical knowledge and communication skills, and because the study demonstrates improvements in students' clinical skills achieved with a ward-based course, the implementation of mandatory clinical courses in the national pharmaceutical curriculum is advisable. The authors further state that the teaching module should become a fully integrated standard of teaching and learning in future clinical pharmaceutical education in Germany, supported by the experience of ward-based courses led by a teacher practitioner in other countries and other disciplines.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Ward-based teaching performed in small groups with close supervision
- Pharmacists who work regularly on wards and are competent in teaching as tutors
- Teacher practitioner positions established on a regular basis
#### Constraints
- In Germany, implementation of ward-based clinical education is hampered due to financial restrictions
- Currently, no German pharmaceutical educational curriculum systematically teaches and learns in a ward-based clinical setting
- Generalization to German pharmacy education requires further studies involving multiple study sites in Germany

### Target Learners
- pharmacy students in Germany

### Learning Goals
- application of clinical knowledge
- communication skills
- patient-centred care competencies
