# Cumulative assessment program of the University of Groningen bachelor's medical curriculum

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An assessment program applied to ten-week integrated modules in the preclinical bachelor's program, in which "the test is divided into three separate mandatory subtests scheduled at the end of weeks four, eight and ten of the module (frequent testing)". Each subtest covers all preceding weeks' content, the final grade is based on the total number of questions across the three subtests (compensation), and performance information is published shortly after each subtest through the digital learning environment. The study evaluated this program in four modules.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students receive information about their performance between the tests, provided when students are in a position to actively process it
#### Constraints
- The study could not attribute effects to any separate aspect of the program

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students in a preclinical bachelor's program

### Learning Goals
- continuous study effort throughout a course
- knowledge retention through repeated testing
