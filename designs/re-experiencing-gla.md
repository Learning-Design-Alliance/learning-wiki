# Re-experiencing: a generative learning activity in which learners watch a 3D VR replay of their own past performance to engage visualizing and enacting modes

## Description
Re-experiencing is a newly conceptualized generative learning activity in which a learner is recorded performing a task and then rewatchs the recording in immersive VR, shifting "from being the one carrying out a task to being a reflective observer". Building on the generative sense-making framework, it is designed to engage the visualizing mode (the replay provides visual material as an instructional scaffold) and the enacting mode (learners can move freely, watch from any angle, and mimic the replay), while reducing the extraneous load of recalling past actions. The replay pauses at mistakes and displays temporally aligned feedback on a wrist info screen.
