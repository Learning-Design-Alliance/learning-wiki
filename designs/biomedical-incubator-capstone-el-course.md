# Biomedical Incubator Capstone (CAP): a simulated biotechnology startup experiential learning course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
CAP is a simulated biomedical incubator in which students work in interdisciplinary teams of 3–5 from across life science Departments "to design and develop student‐initiated research ideas with a focus on translation and commercialization." Students spend at least 8 hours per week for 8 months (approximately 250 hours total) designing experiments, developing standard operating procedures, managing timelines and budgets, completing wet-lab experiments, performing data analyses, and conducting market research, culminating in pitches to industry representatives and a written research and development report.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interdisciplinary teams of 3–5 students from across a variety of life science Departments including Pharmacology
- At least 8 h per week of course activity over 8 months
#### Constraints
- It is a university-based EL occurring on campus, simulating a start-up in partnership with the campus-linked accelerator network rather than operating at external sites

### Target Learners
- senior undergraduate life science students, predominantly in their final year

### Learning Goals
- entrepreneurial and commercialization skills
- research design and execution
- teamwork and communication skills
