# Five-cycle WebQuest intervention pattern: surf, teamwork, information exchange, teaching and feedback

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The intervention pattern organized instruction into five cycles of four 120-minute sessions each, preceded by an orientation session on the WebQuest. In each cycle "participants were required to choose one lesson from the WebQuest and prepare a lesson plan to peer-teach at the end of the cycle." Session 1 was surfing the WebQuest in the university lab, Session 2 teamwork within sub-groups, Session 3 exchanging of information in one big circle, and Session 4 teaching and feedback with a post conference assessing performance against the WebQuest's evaluation rubric. Target teaching skills were rotated among sub-groups across cycles.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A university Internet lab, a designed WebQuest with lessons and an evaluation rubric, sub-groups of candidates, and an instructor monitoring and offering help when needed
#### Constraints
- The pattern was studied only with third-year male PE teacher candidates aged 19 to 20 at one university over three months

### Target Learners
- preservice physical education teacher candidates

### Learning Goals
- planning, implementation and evaluation of PE lessons; information gathering and synthesis from Internet resources
