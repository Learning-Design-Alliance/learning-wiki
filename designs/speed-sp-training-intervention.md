# SPEED standardized-patient training intervention: 2-day program of didactics, seven SP encounters, SP feedback, and group debriefing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The SPEED intervention is a 2-day in-service training for healthcare workers caring for HIV-positive adolescents and young adults in Kenya. "The 2-day training intervention focuses on skill-building through practice, with a series of SP encounters, individual feedback by SPs, and group debriefings." Participants receive short didactics on adolescent-friendly services and motivational interviewing, then rotate through seven video-recorded SP encounters at stations resembling counseling rooms; SPs give non-technical, emotional feedback after each encounter, and facilitated debriefings review videos to identify strengths and areas for improvement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Up to 10 HCWs per clinic who provide clinical and/or counseling services to adolescents; trained SP actors; a training venue with private stations; video-recording equipment
#### Constraints
- Training occurs at the clinic level and is delivered in waves to minimize disruption to clinic operations, reflecting a real-world in-service training

### Target Learners
- Healthcare workers providing clinical and counseling services to adolescents in Kenyan HIV care clinics

### Learning Goals
- Clinical assessment, communication, and empathy skills for adolescent-friendly HIV care; adherence to clinical guidelines; motivational interviewing techniques
