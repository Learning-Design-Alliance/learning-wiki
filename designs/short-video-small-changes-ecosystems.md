# Short video on the effect of small changes on complex ecosystems

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
A short video (http://tinyurl.com/onoktvl) on the effect of small changes on complex ecosystems, which the authors tried to finish the lecture at the High School and report it "was an instant hit". They say it makes the need for understanding ecological networks extremely clear and connects complex networks to complex systems more broadly.
