# Metronome-guided 2-min chest compression cycles on a Resusci Anne Simulator as a protocol for assessing compression-rate skill

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This simulation protocol has participants perform 2-min cycles of chest compressions paced by a metronome set to different frequencies, with parameters recorded by the manikin's SimPad PLUS software. As printed, "The lifeguards then participated in the targeted study in which they were asked to perform 2-min cycles of chest compressions at different rates: 80, 90, 100, 110, 120, 130, 140, and 150 CPM." Order of participants and frequencies was randomized with Research Randomizer software, with 20-min breaks between cycles.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An adult manikin simulator with recording software (Resusci Anne with SimPad PLUS), a metronome, and prior BLS training conducted by AHA instructors based on the 2015 guidelines
#### Constraints
- The authors note the study used manikin models with all the limitations of that research type, and that a 2-min cycle may yield different values than resuscitations longer than 10 min

### Target Learners
- lifeguards
- professional rescuers in basic life support training

### Learning Goals
- calibrating chest compression rate and depth to resuscitation guideline targets
