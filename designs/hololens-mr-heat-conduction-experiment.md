# HoloLens MR heat conduction experiment with three switchable data representations

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A laboratory apparatus for the paradigm heat conduction experiment in which cylindrical Aluminum and Copper rods (L = 26 cm, d = 5 cm) are heated at one end and cooled at the other, with temperature data extracted from infrared thermal images and passed via WiFi to the Microsoft HoloLens. The App "provides three diﬀerent representations of the data: false-color image of the temperature values projected as “hologram” directly onto the sample cylinder, numerical values at three pre-deﬁned points and a temperature graph as a function of the position along the rod". Users toggle representations with virtual buttons and can export data as a csv file.
