# Structured 14-week group-based CBT-MBSR intervention program

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention is a "structured 14-week guided self-help program" delivered in weekly 120-minute group sessions led by four CBT experts and supervised by two MBSR therapists. It combines conventional CBT methods (behavioral experiments, thought records, thematic discussions) with core MBSR practices: mindfulness meditation, body scan, and mindful breathing. Sessions progress through three phases: initial rapport and anxiety psychoeducation (sessions 1–3), mid-phase cognitive restructuring of anxiety-provoking events (4–7), and later deep application of techniques (8–11), with homework assigned to reinforce skills.

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants must meet inclusion criteria: enrollment in measurement and evaluation courses, documented low academic achievement, mild-to-severe test anxiety on the SGAATS, and voluntary consent; exclusion of prior similar therapy or current psychiatric treatment
#### Constraints
- Evaluated among TVET students in measurement and evaluation courses at two Southeast Nigerian universities; effects at two-month follow-up only

### Target Learners
- TVET/business education undergraduate students in Nigerian universities

### Learning Goals
- Reducing test and examination anxiety
- Improving academic achievement in measurement and evaluation
- Enhancing mindfulness and subjective well-being
