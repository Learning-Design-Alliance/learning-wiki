# Nine-step question-based procedure for safe emotions' teaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The curriculum's structured sequence for teaching even difficult emotions: (1) naming the emotion, (2) external recognition, (3) internal recognition, (4) stages of emotion, (5) causes of emotion, (6) causes of emotion in other people, (7) usual ways of reacting, (8) estimation of consequences of reported behaviors, and (9) choosing the best ideas. The article states this "nine step question-based procedure ... ensures even difficult and complicated emotions' teaching in a safe way" and characterizes it as "mainly cognitive, since it is question-based, with a psychoanalytic perspective".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher writes down all given answers without criticism
- Teacher guides students to estimate consequences of each reported behavior
#### Constraints
- Designed for elementary students aged 6–9 in a circle class arrangement

### Target Learners
- first and second grade elementary students

### Learning Goals
- emotions' identification and expression
- emotions' management
