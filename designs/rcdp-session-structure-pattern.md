# RCDP session structure: prebrief, progressive mastery of microskills with pause-rewind cycles, and closing debrief

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The curriculum's session structure moves learners through scenarios of increasing difficulty: a 15-minute prebrief and orientation, a case stem, then facilitator pauses for feedback on microskills starting with initial assessment skills. "Learners were required to perfect each skill and repeatedly perform it perfectly throughout the simulation before moving on to the next," with cases rewound to the beginning until mastery of initial skills was achieved before advancing to skills such as defibrillator operation. A short group debriefing followed each case, with one hour dedicated to each case and debriefing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Stopping points determined by learner actions rather than predetermined
- Facilitators with RCDP-specific training
- Checklists of critical actions for each case
#### Constraints
- One hour of time was dedicated to each case and debriefing

### Target Learners
- junior anesthesiology residents

### Learning Goals
- mastery of ACLS microskills
- teamwork and communication
