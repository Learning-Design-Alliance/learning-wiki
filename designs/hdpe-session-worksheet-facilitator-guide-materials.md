# HDPE session instructional materials: student worksheet, facilitator guide, and survey appendices

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The session's released materials comprise a student worksheet, a facilitator guide, and survey instruments, all peer reviewed as integral parts of the publication. The worksheet "included a prompt for generating differential diagnoses and a table into which students entered their brainstormed HDPE maneuvers and justified the choice of each maneuver," followed by text and visual demonstrations of normal exam technique and findings. The facilitator guide adds "a suggested time line for the session, suggested differential diagnoses and potential approaches to each chief complaint, suggested PE maneuvers with rationale, and a proposed wrap-up section."

## Design Implications

- The intended electronic real-time worksheet data collection failed due to internet connectivity problems, so worksheet data were excluded from analyses

### Target Learners
- Rising third-year medical students and the physician facilitators who lead their small groups

### Learning Goals
- Generate and justify differential-diagnosis-driven physical exam maneuver selection
