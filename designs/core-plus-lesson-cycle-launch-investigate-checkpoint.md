# Core-Plus multi-day lesson cycle: launch, small-group investigation, Checkpoint discussion, and independent task

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Core-Plus units are organized around multi-day lessons cycling through instructional activities: a full-class launch of a problem situation with the teacher as "director and moderator"; small-group investigation in which students gather data, look for patterns, construct models, and make and verify conjectures with the teacher as facilitator; a whole-class Checkpoint discussion with the teacher as moderator; and an individual task with the teacher as "an intellectual coach". Materials promote collaborative small-group investigations followed by teacher-led whole-class summarizing that leads to analysis, abstraction, and further application.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Classroom configurations supporting small-group work in class and individual work outside class, with teacher roles shifting across director, facilitator, moderator, and coach
#### Constraints
- The cycle is described for the first edition of the curriculum reviewed by the WWC

### Target Learners
- High school mathematics students

### Learning Goals
- Mathematical thinking, problem solving, and conceptual understanding through investigation of problem situations
