# South Fayette SD's CS/STEAM pathway organized around four throughlines

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The South Fayette School District pathway, which the Kentucky project adapts, is organized around four throughlines found in its CS/STEAM pathway: "Maker Education (artistic expression and creativity)", "Computational Thinking (computer science including coding in the virtual and physical environment)", "Spatial Reasoning & Discrete Math (the ability to imagine things in three-dimensions & understanding mathematical modeling)", and "Manufacturing and Design (engineering, human centered design thinking)". Lesson plans in the pathway include "student agency, computational thinking competencies and practices, habits of mind and examples of student artifacts."
