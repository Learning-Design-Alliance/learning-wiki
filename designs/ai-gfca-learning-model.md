# The AI-GFCA learning model: four scaffolding stages (generalization, formulation, correction, appreciation) supported by an AI speaking app

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The AI-GFCA learning model is a four-stage scaffolding framework for technology-supported role-play in L2 learning. As the article states, "the study employed generalization, formulation, correction, and appreciation strategies by integrating AI speaking learning by proposing the AI-GFCA learning model to scaffold students' performance in role-play tasks, English speaking skills, L2 errors, and interaction mindset." Generalization clarifies task goals and success criteria from O'Donnell and Topping's objectives-and-criteria approach; formulation builds collaborative readiness; correction conducts intergroup corrective feedback with internal and external monitoring; appreciation fosters positive appraisal to reduce tension. The model was tested against AI-supported conventional learning lacking the pedagogical theory.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An AI-based speaking learning app providing real-time speaking feedback
- A platform for sharing role-play recordings and conducting intergroup corrective feedback
- Scaffolding stages delivered in sequence across role-play tasks
#### Constraints
- The study used semi-scripted role-plays combining closed role-play practice with open role-play flexibility
- Tested with first-year university EFL students at A1-B1 CEFR levels in Taiwan

### Target Learners
- First-year university EFL students with A1-B1 CEFR English proficiency

### Learning Goals
- L2 role-play performance
- English speaking skills
- Noticing and correcting L2 errors
- Positive interaction mindset
