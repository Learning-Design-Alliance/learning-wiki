# Changing the leading type of activity within one lesson actualizes historical material from new perspectives

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes lesson practice in which the form of the leading type of activity changes in the course of one lesson: training starts in the form of a game, moves to an organized discussion and group interactive work, and is completed with creative individual homework. Such a change of activities, the authors report, actualizes the theoretical material every time from a new perspective and contributes to a more objective attitude towards the historical process.
