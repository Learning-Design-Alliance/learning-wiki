# Short, mobile-delivered case-based format suited to busy clinicians: small doses on the phone with emailed prompts

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Participants valued the program's brevity and phone delivery: 'It was short and to the point, and it didn't require me to do five thousand screen changes and bits and pieces to physically do it.' They could 'do five or ten minutes of, you know, after a twelve hour day', and the emailed questions provided 'prompt reminders and easy access'. The format suited clinicians who lacked time for long screen-clicking modules.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Questions emailed to participants, providing prompt reminders and easy access, with access on their phones
#### Constraints
- Several participants stated they would have liked more information and more of an educational component in future programs

### Target Learners
- busy senior clinicians in oncology

### Learning Goals
- efficient updating of clinical guideline knowledge in small time increments

### Claims
- Qstream Acceptable For Guideline Dissemination [+M]
