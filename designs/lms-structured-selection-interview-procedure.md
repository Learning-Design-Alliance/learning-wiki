# Lübeck medical school structured selection interview procedure

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A university-specific admission procedure at Lübeck medical school combining a bonus-improved pu-GPA preselection with semi-structured selection interviews assessed via a fully structured rating scheme. "Five primary dimensions that have to be rated individually by panel members address motivation, knowledge about the course of study, social engagement, (self-)reflection and communication." Twelve three-member panels use a standardised interview guide with situational and behavioural questions; interview scores are z-standardised for panel strictness, transformed to a 30-point scale, and summed with a 31-point pu-GPA score.
