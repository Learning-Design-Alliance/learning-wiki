# Cultural Astronomy as a disciplinary resource for rethinking didactic strategies in astronomy teaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Cultural Astronomy (CA) is presented as a discipline that studies "las distintas formas en las que los objetos y fenómenos del cielo se registran, influyen, impactan y guían las creencias, los sistemas de conocimiento y las tradiciones culturales". The article uses CA as the organizing framework for two contextualized teaching units, arguing it shows that sciences are conditioned by the society in which they develop and helps value astronomy's relations with society and technology. The authors note the historical-epistemological approach "aún resta profundizar".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students require prior knowledge of cardinal points, the daily movement of the Sun as seen by a local observer, the horizontal coordinate system, common constellations, and characteristic moments of the solar cycle such as equinoxes.
#### Constraints
- The proposals use CA elements as entry points "sin profundizar explícitamente en aspectos culturales e históricos como sería deseable".

### Target Learners
- secondary school natural sciences students
- teacher-training institute students

### Learning Goals
- space and time concepts in astronomy
- establishing correspondences between constructions from micro to mega-space
- critical thinking about science as situated knowledge
