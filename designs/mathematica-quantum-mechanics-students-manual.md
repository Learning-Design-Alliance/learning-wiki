# A student's manual with embedded Mathematica source code notebooks for direct reader evaluation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A book accompanying a yearly one-semester lecture at the Department of Physics, University of Basel, aimed at "Master's and PhD students in physics and related ﬁelds". It teaches quantum mechanics through Mathematica implementations, with embedded source code files that the reader can directly evaluate: "Some sections of this book contain embedded Mathematica source code ﬁles, for direct evaluation by the reader". A list of attached notebooks is provided, and exercises follow most sections.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A PDF reader supporting embedded files is needed to open the double-clickable source code links; the article recommends Adobe Acrobat Reader otherwise
#### Constraints
- Written in the Wolfram language of Mathematica version 11, though the author notes other languages such as Matlab or Python may be used with suitable translation

### Target Learners
- Master's and PhD students in physics and related fields

### Learning Goals
- quantum mechanics concepts including states, operators, spin systems, real-space quantum motion, and quantum circuits
