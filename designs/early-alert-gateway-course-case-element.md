# Illustrative early alert system for first-year gateway courses used to demonstrate LEAGUE

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article demonstrates LEAGUE's operational value on a scenario in which "Students receive a risk score from 0 to 100 based on four indicators: no LMS login for seven days, two missing assignments, attendance below 75 percent, and a current course average below 70 percent." Students scoring 60 or higher appear on an advisor dashboard, with outreach expected within 48 hours. The case is illustrative, not an empirical evaluation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Weekly combination of LMS, attendance, and gradebook data; advisor outreach within 48 hours for flagged students
#### Constraints
- The case is a hypothetical university scenario used for demonstration, not a studied deployment

### Target Learners
- first-year students in gateway courses

### Learning Goals
- early identification of academic difficulty and targeted advising support
