# Three-stage scheme of reality cognition through aerospace images in geography training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes that students' cognition of reality through aerospace images proceeds in three stages, illustrated in Fig. 1. The first stage, "the understanding of aerospace images", builds knowledge about image characteristics and how objects appear on them; the second, "images decoding", is recognizing geographic objects and identifying qualitative and quantitative indicators, called the main focus; the third, "the reading of aerospace images", involves compiling descriptions of objects and phenomena based on decoding results, using inductive and deductive generalizations to expand knowledge.
