# Three-step design model for GAI-powered characters in IVR

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's Pedagogical Design answer to RQ2 is a three-step design model integrating pedagogy with GAI and VR: "selecting the applicable reference framework, identifying the learning objectives and the instructional methods that could be feasible in IVR, and designing the GAI characters." It was applied in a sustainability education context, grounding character design in the refined sustainability competence framework and producing the Tero and Madida use cases. The authors present it as how AI technologies, learning content, and pedagogical approaches "can be integrated having the form of a pedagogical GAI character in IVR."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A reference framework covering the needed competencies with learning objectives and instructional methods

#### Constraints
- The model covers only the Needs Analysis and Pedagogical Design phases; Development, Evaluation, and Reflection phases were beyond the report's scope
