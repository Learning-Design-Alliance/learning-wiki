# An inquiry-based curriculum developed specifically for the introductory algebra-based physics course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a curriculum, not a text, built by modifying and adapting existing Workshop Physics materials and integrating them with new units and a new format. It is "designed to be taught in a laboratory-based environment – no lecture" and is inquiry-based in the manner of Physics by Inquiry, including Socratic dialog and the results of physics education research. All TTU course materials are posted on the web.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Laboratory-based environment with computers and experimental equipment; students work through units in class in groups
#### Constraints
- Designed for the algebra-based physics course population; parts of the materials can be used in other settings

### Target Learners
- introductory algebra-based physics students, mostly juniors and seniors intending health-science careers

### Learning Goals
- physics concepts useful in future careers
- scientific skills such as developing models from experimental results
- an epistemology closer to a scientist's epistemology
