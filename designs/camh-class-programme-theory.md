# Preliminary programme theory: teacher-embedded ER activities activate emotion regulation as a transdiagnostic mechanism

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article presents a preliminary programme theory mapping how teacher behaviour change within daily classroom contexts activates ER as a transdiagnostic mechanism for change. As printed in Table 1, if teachers "embed emotion regulation activities into everyday routine classroom lessons", then mechanisms activate ER strategies and capabilities, leading to proximal outcomes of improved pupil ER repertoire and decreased emotion dysregulation, and distal outcomes of increased wellbeing and reduced anxiety and depression symptoms.
