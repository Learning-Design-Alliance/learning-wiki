# A 24-session combined protocol of five activity types: three executive-function areas and two sensory-integration areas

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's intervention pattern combines two treatment strands delivered in groups of five children. As the article states, "The independent variable in this study includes the hybrid therapy based on executive functions and sensory integration, including 24 sessions of one hour and a half, three times a week for 2 months in form of four groups of five people." Activities span five types: three executive-function areas (concentration, working memory, behavior inhibition) and two sensory-integration areas (tactile-system stimulation, deep-system stimulation).
