# Columba-Hypatia: Astronomy for Peace outreach project on Cyprus

## Description
A joint astronomy outreach project by GalileoMobile and the Association for Historical Dialogue and Research (AHDR) taking place on the island of Cyprus. It aims to "inspire young people, through astronomy, to be curious about science and the cosmos" while using astronomy "as a tool for promoting meaningful communication and a culture of peace and non-violence". After a 2016 pilot it ran throughout 2017, funded by the IAU's Office of Astronomy for Development, combining mono-communal school visits, bi-communal activity days in the buffer zone, and public events.

### Context
#### Requirements

#### Constraints
- Designed for the post-conflict Cypriot context of two communities separated for over 50 years

### Target Learners
- Greek-Cypriot and Turkish-Cypriot school children
- youth
- teachers
- general public

### Learning Goals
- curiosity about science and the cosmos
- intercommunal communication
- empathy and global citizenship
