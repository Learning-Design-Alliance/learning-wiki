# BOPPPS-Rain Classroom blended lesson structure for clinical rotations

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 4 studies (3 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
This pattern operationalizes the six BOPPPS stages on the Rain Classroom platform within clinical clerkship teaching. Each session follows a fixed sequence: a clinically relevant vignette to open, clear measurable objectives, a short pre-assessment quiz, a participatory learning phase using real-time quizzes, bullet-screen comments, and small-group case discussions, a post-assessment on newly taught content, and a summary addressing misconceptions revealed in the post-assessment. The article reports this structure was delivered by the same instructional team using the same textbooks and curriculum content as the lecture comparison group.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Same instructional team, core textbooks, and curriculum content across comparison groups; standardized examination developed by a faculty panel against the shared syllabus
#### Constraints
- Tested in a single-center, one-week nephrology rotation with fixed weekly (non-random) allocation and no blinding

### Target Learners
- fifth-year undergraduate medical students on clinical rotations

### Learning Goals
- nephrology knowledge
- problem-solving and communication skills
- engagement and satisfaction
