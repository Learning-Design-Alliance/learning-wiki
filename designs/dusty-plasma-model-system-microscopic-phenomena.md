# Dusty plasma as a model system for understanding complex phenomena at a microscopic level

## Description
The article presents dusty plasma — an ionized gas into which micron-sized solid grains are introduced, which "acquire large negative charges on their surface and exhibits collective behavior similar to the ambient plasma medium" — as a model system for understanding complex physical phenomena. Because the grains' extremely small charge-to-mass ratio leads to new plasma eigenmodes at very low frequency (1-100 Hz), dust dynamics can be visualized even with naked eyes, allowing study of the medium at the microscopic level. The article uses this account to justify each proposed laboratory experiment for physics students.
