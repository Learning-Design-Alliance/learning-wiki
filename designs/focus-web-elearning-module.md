# Web-based FOCUS e-learning module structured into basics and cardio-specific learning with interactive revision cards

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A German-language, browser-based e-learning module designed by ultrasound experts, didactics, and IT specialists, developed following the waterfall model. It was "structured into two parts: 'basics', covering ultrasound physics, image production, and topographic anatomy; and 'cardio-specific learning'" with chapters on section planes, probe handling, sonoanatomy, and examination protocols. Individual revision cards formed its foundation, with interactive features such as fillable tables and labellable graphics; pathologies were not taught.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access via internet browser; completion expected before the in-person FOCUS workshop
#### Constraints
- Pathologies were not taught in the module

### Target Learners
- medical students in their clinical years
- physicians preparing for certified FOCUS workshops

### Learning Goals
- theoretical FOCUS competencies including ultrasound physics, anatomy, section planes, and examination protocols
