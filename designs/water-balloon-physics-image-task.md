# Water Balloon Physics Image Require-GenAI task

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A second Require-GenAI task in which GenAI has a dual role: "it is simultaneously the source of the artifact being analyzed and the tool students use to correct it." Students receive a GenAI-generated image of a child swinging a water balloon in horizontal circles, identify physically impossible elements, use GenAI to generate a more accurate image, and ask GenAI to construct a correct free body diagram. A three-way error classification (what GenAI identified, what the student identified independently, what the student believes GenAI misidentified) makes independent judgment directly observable and separable from AI contribution.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The accuracy-ceiling criterion: GenAI consistently missed at least one expert-anchored error in the image and FBD, as confirmed by the expert answer key
#### Constraints
- A student who cannot independently identify errors beyond what GenAI flags produces an artifact that fails the distinguishability condition

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate introductory physics students

### Learning Goals
- Independent identification of Newtonian mechanics violations and physics-grounded critique of AI-generated diagrams
