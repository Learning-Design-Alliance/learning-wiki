# Gravitron problem in open-ended and scaffolded formats

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Gravitron problem asks whether riders on "a rotating cylindrical amusement ride in which riders lean against the wall" would slide off under specified parameters (radius 4.6 m, static friction coefficient 0.2, mass range 25-160 kg, angular velocity 3 rad/s). It was posed in open-ended and scaffolded formats, the latter explicitly requesting assumptions and a free body diagram, and was solved by sixteen introductory students in think-aloud interviews and prompted sixteen times to ChatGPT-3.5.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Solvers must explain their reasoning using diagrams, equations and words in the open-ended version
#### Constraints
- The scaffolded version explicitly asked for the underlying assumptions and the free body diagram required to solve the problem

### Target Learners
- introductory physics students

### Learning Goals
- determining whether riders would fall off the Gravitron's walls under the specified parameters
- unpacking the underlying mechanism of a real-world context using physics and mathematical principles
