# Laboratory tours of microfabrication, microscopy, and nanoscale facilities as a core component of the seminar

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course includes guided visits to three University of Iowa facilities — the Micro Fabrication Facility, the Microscopy Facility, and the Nanoscale Devices and Systems Laboratory — so students see the equipment they study in class. Tours expose students to "Various lithography equipment including mask aligner, photolithography , electron-beam/thermal evaporators, reactive ion etchers, atomic layer deposition system", microscopy tools such as SEM, AFM and TEM, and CVD furnaces for graphene and CNT sample preparation. The authors state the tours were "planned to give students a practical feel of how devices are fabricated, characterized and integrated".
