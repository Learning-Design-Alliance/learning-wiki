# ParentCorps three-component model: staff professional development, a 14-week parenting program, and a classroom SEL program

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
ParentCorps operates through three coordinated components. First, professional development helps staff "build strong, culturally responsive relationships with families" and reflect on their beliefs about families while learning the science of early childhood development. Second, a group-based 14-week parenting program affirms families' experiences, builds family-to-family connections, and shares evidence-based parenting practices. Third, Friends School is a classroom program developing children's social-emotional skills.
