# Co-designed AI-integrated literacy lesson plans with three AI tool categories

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Three co-created lesson plans for grades 1–2, 3–4, and 5–6 on the curriculum theme "The Summer", each integrating at least three AI tools drawn from three categories: text tools (ChatGPT, Gemini), image tools (Copilot, Ideogram, Muse.ai), and video tools (VideoGen, Fliki, Invideo AI). The article states that "Tools like ChatGPT are used to create, edit, and summarise text so that students can compare it with their "humanised" texts and identify language errors", image tools let students "create images based on their texts", and video tools support collaborative video creation and discussion. Each plan follows a standardized, adaptable structure with learning objectives, brainstorming, hands-on AI tasks, differentiation options, and assessment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers with basic knowledge of AI tools such as Gemini AI and ChatGPT
- At least three different AI tools integrated per lesson plan
- Alignment with the national elementary curriculum
#### Constraints
- Piloted only with 62 students in four classrooms (grades 1, 2, 4, and 5) in Cypriot schools
- Designed for Greek language literacy in elementary grades 1–6

### Target Learners
- Elementary students in grades 1–6

### Learning Goals
- Text comprehension
- Writing, reading, and speaking skills
- Familiarity with AI concepts

### Claims

- [Students most enjoyed creating images with AI, followed by generating text, with video creation least favoured](../claims/students-enjoyed-image-creation-most.md) [+W]
