# Mono-communal preparation visits followed by bi-communal joint activity days

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The project's core enactment pattern separates the design into two phases: mono-communal school visits, where trainers visited Greek-Cypriot and Turkish-Cypriot schools separately to introduce basic astronomical concepts, and bi-communal activity days in the UN-controlled buffer zone where children from both communities met and did astronomy activities together. The authors focused on these "since these could reach the most diverse audience and have the largest impact". The pattern prepares participants separately before bringing them into contact.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A neutral meeting venue accessible to both communities (the Home for Cooperation in the buffer zone)
- bilingual activity materials
#### Constraints
- Contact phase requires a politically accessible meeting point between the separated communities

### Target Learners
- school children from separated communities

### Learning Goals
- intergroup contact and communication
- astronomical concepts
