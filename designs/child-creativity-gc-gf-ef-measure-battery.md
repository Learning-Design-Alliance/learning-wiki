# Battery of creativity, intelligence and executive-function measures for 8- to 13-year-olds

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study administered a battery measuring creativity, crystallized (Gc) and fluid intelligence (Gf), and executive functions, which the article specifies as "working memory, inhibition and shifting", to 209 children of both sexes aged 8 to 13 (boys n = 86, girls n = 123). The measures were analyzed with correlational analyses and a Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) approach to examine relations among the three constructs.
