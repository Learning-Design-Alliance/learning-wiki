# What Is a Place? introductory module teaching the urban-developer perspective on city spaces

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The What Is a Place? module was an introductory module developed by an urban development researcher at Aalborg University and a Danish learning platform developer to bridge skills between students and the professional urban development community. "The module comprised an introduction by the teacher or urban planning researcher and a five-minute video of Tivoli," a familiar Copenhagen amusement park. Students analyzed urban spaces using three questions about what can be measured and sensed, felt and valued, and explained, with an urban development researcher participating as a co-teacher leading the discussion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An urban development researcher participating as a co-teacher throughout the module and leading the discussion
- A familiar local place, such as the Tivoli video, that students can analyze with the three professional perspectives
#### Constraints
- Designed to introduce the skilled perspective of urban developers on city space, not to substitute for the full design-thinking process

### Target Learners
- Students aged 11–15 in grades 5–9 in Copenhagen vulnerable-area schools

### Learning Goals
- Applying the perspectives of urban developers in analyzing urban spaces, including senses, feelings, and the purpose or identity of a place
