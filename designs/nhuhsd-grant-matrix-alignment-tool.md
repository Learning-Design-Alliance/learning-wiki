# Grant matrix aligning district school-based mental health grants and initiatives

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
NHUHSD created a grant matrix to align its many school-based mental health grants, projects, and initiatives. "By tracking all of this information in one physical document, NHUHSD leadership can better understand its assets and how various activities align with each other and be intentional when making decisions about adopting new practices." The tool supports coordination and sustainability planning across the 5-year grant period.
