# AI-augmented practical instruction versus conventional human-led instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The study's instructional arrangement contrasts two lesson-level designs for practical STEM teaching: an AI-augmented format in which students work with a real-time AI tutor providing synchronous correction, and a conventional format of human-led instruction. The article reports that "The experimental group utilized the Kwara-STEM AI Tutor, while the control group received conventional human-led instruction" in a pre-test/post-test control group design across three institutions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires an AI feedback tool capable of real-time diagnosis and correction of practical work
#### Constraints
- Evaluated in Kwara State colleges of education with 240 students; the article does not claim generalization beyond this setting
