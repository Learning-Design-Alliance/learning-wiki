# AI Family Medicine Exam Expert: a customized ChatGPT-4 configured to take the ABFM Certification Examination

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The AI Family Medicine Exam Expert is a specialized version of ChatGPT-4 programmed to take the ABFM Certification Examination. It was instructed to adopt the persona of a final-year family medicine resident, prioritize three family medicine textbooks loaded into its configuration, fall back on web browsing of scholarly sources such as UpToDate, and finally use its own medical knowledge for educated guesses, explaining its reasoning and resources for each answer. Questions came from the AAFP Family Medicine Board Review Questions, presented one at a time as stand-alone tasks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the three configured textbooks (Rakel, Baldor, Al-Gelban et al.)
- Prompt instructions specifying persona, resource hierarchy, and output format
#### Constraints
- The textbook knowledge base is static and may not capture the evolving nature of medical knowledge, per the authors' limitations section

### Target Learners
- medical students preparing for board examinations
- family medicine residents

### Learning Goals
- family medicine board examination preparation
- clinical knowledge assessment
