# The Mechanics-Dynamics-Aesthetics (MDA) gamification architecture adapted to education for inclusive gamified atmospheres

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study used the Mechanics-Dynamics-Aesthetics (MDA) architecture, adapted to the education field, to achieve an inclusive gamified atmosphere for all student-player types, instead of the Points-Badges-Leaderboards architecture which fits only the "achiever" profile. Mechanics refers to the system's set of constituent elements and their relations; Dynamics refers to how mechanics effectively work adding the player factor; Aesthetics refers to perceptions and sensations-emotions experienced by students while playing. Design elements included positive points, rewards, classifications, levels of difficulty, challenges, achievements, badges, cooperative and competitive teams, and customizable virtual avatars, with no negative points of any kind.
