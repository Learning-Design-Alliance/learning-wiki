# PBHNOS training (Project-Based History and Nature of Science training)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
PBHNOS training taught the History and Nature of Science course syllabus through project activities: the article states it "was carried out by teaching the subjects in the course syllabus through project activities including the practice steps of PBL" together with techniques such as discussion, question-answer and brainstorming. It ran for 14 weeks in the experimental group, with a detailed project-practice syllabus in Appendix 1, and was compared against conventional lecture-based instruction in the control group.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A course syllabus whose subjects can be organized into project activities, and the practice steps of PBL plus supporting techniques such as discussion and brainstorming
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only with 3rd-grade pre-service science teachers in one Turkish education faculty, in a non-equivalent control group design over one semester

### Target Learners
- 3rd-grade undergraduate pre-service science teachers

### Learning Goals
- views about the nature of scientific knowledge
