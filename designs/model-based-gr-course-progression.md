# Model-based general relativity course structure: step-by-step introduction from sphere to spacetime sector models

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The introductory general relativity course for pre-service physics teachers uses the model-based approach including calculation of sector models from their metrics, introduced step by step starting with the sphere via the equatorial plane of a black hole to the spacetime of a radial ray. Students are taught skills for studying physical phenomena associated with a given metric, obtaining answers graphically that in a standard course would be found by calculations.
