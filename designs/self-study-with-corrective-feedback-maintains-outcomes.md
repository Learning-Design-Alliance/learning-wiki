# Structured self-study time combined with continuous corrective feedback can maintain objective learning outcomes while reducing contact hours

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The ZPDT course reduced on-site contact hours from 280 to 70 semester hours but introduced defined self-study time (127 semester hours) during which students completed theoretical and practical tasks supported by continuous instructor feedback. The authors conclude this combination maintained objectively assessed learning outcomes while students subjectively rated knowledge transfer differently. They recommend continuing this format: "Diese sollten aus Sicht der Autorenschaft weiterhin angeboten werden."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Defined self-study hours must be curricularly allocated and supported by continuous instructor feedback
- Practical tasks must be verifiable through testate documentation
#### Constraints
- Evaluated in a single-institution, non-randomized cohort comparison at one German dental school; future multicentre studies are recommended by the authors

### Target Learners
- first-semester dental students in preclinical propaedeutic courses

### Learning Goals
- theoretical dental propaedeutic knowledge
- practical preclinical skills including tooth preparation and dental technology fundamentals
