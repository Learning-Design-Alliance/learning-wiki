# The learning persona: a three-assumption analytical framework tracing student movement from a traditional single-ground-truth mindset to a subjectivity-aware mindset

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The learning persona is a theoretical construct derived from user-centred design principles representing what students should ideally understand after the annotation task. It rests on three assumptions: students start with a traditional mindset treating disagreement as error, disagreement reflects domain complexity, and students must recognise their own role in constructing biased datasets. The trajectory operationalises five concepts — annotation ambiguity, data quality, bias and fairness, implementation barriers, and pedagogical effectiveness — mapping students from "Single ground truth" to "Multiple valid labels". The authors use it as an analytical guide, not a grading rubric.
