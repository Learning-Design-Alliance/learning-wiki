# Limb type, then three consecutive rehabilitation phases, then nine focus topics: the navigation structure of RehAmpTT

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The module uses a hierarchical navigation pattern: the user first chooses upper or lower extremity, then selects one of three consecutive, colour-coded treatment phases (postoperative, interim, maintenance), and then reaches topic-level content. The article states "It distinguishes between rehabilitation after arm or leg amputation, after which three consecutive rehabilitation phases (postacute, intermediate and maintenance phases) can be selected." Nine focus topics, marked by intuitively understandable pictograms, follow phase selection.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Colour coding and intuitively understandable icons to distinguish phases and topics
#### Constraints
- Some content is identical across two or all three phases (e.g. pain and aids topics), so the total scope exceeds the printed page count

### Target Learners
- medical professionals
- amputees
- relatives

### Learning Goals
- locating phase-appropriate rehabilitation information
- understanding the temporal course of rehabilitation care
