# Four-phase instructor-cascade pattern for establishing local resuscitation training capacity

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A reusable unit-level design in which an expert coordinating group trains local professionals, who then progressively take over teaching. The project in each country comprised four phases: "CPR training and preparation of instructors; training for instructors; supervised teaching; and independent teaching." Instructors trained in each country joined the educational team for the next country, reducing dependence on the coordinating group until local groups delivered and certified courses independently.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participation of a local institution that supports the project and undertakes its maintenance
- A sufficient pool of trained local instructors in each country
#### Constraints
- The Spanish group provided scientific support but no specific accreditation in the final phase

### Target Learners
- Health professionals in low- and middle-income countries (physicians, nurses, residents, students)

### Learning Goals
- Paediatric and neonatal cardiopulmonary resuscitation skills
- Instructor-level teaching capability in resuscitation
