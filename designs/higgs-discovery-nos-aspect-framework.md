# Framework linking the Higgs boson discovery to five aspects of Nature of Science

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article organizes the Higgs boson discovery story around five NoS aspects drawn from Abd-El-Khalick: theory-laden, empirical and inferential, creative, tentative, and social dimensions of science. For each aspect it states the general NoS principle and connects it to specific milestones, e.g. "The theory describing the BEH field predicted the existence of the Higgs boson." This framework structures both the narrative text and the classroom reflection prompts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students read and reflect on the discovery story so the NoS aspects can be connected to concrete milestones
#### Constraints
- The BEH theory itself is only accessible using graduate level maths, so the framework works through the story rather than the formal theory

### Target Learners
- secondary school physics students

### Learning Goals
- understanding aspects of Nature of Science
