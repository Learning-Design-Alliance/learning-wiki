# WhatsApp-based tele-education program for hospital hyperglycemia (online lecture plus one month of microlearning)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A structured tele-education intervention consisting of a live online theoretical lecture followed by a one-month program of short videos and supporting materials prepared by three endocrinologists, delivered via WhatsApp. Videos were shared multiple times, residents confirmed exposure at the end, and questions could be submitted during the period. The article describes it as illustrating "the feasibility of implementing scalable, low-cost educational programs across diverse training contexts."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Residents must confirm they watched the shared video content to ensure full exposure; delivery requires a widely available messaging platform
#### Constraints
- Exposure verification relied on self-report; the program was strictly educational and noninterventional in patient care

### Target Learners
- internal medicine residents at teaching hospitals

### Learning Goals
- knowledge of inpatient hyperglycemia management
- appropriate insulin prescribing and glucose monitoring
