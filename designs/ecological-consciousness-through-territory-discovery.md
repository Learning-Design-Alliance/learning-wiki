# Ecological consciousness formation through environmental education and discovery of the local territory

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The project's organizing educational vision is that environmental education and education in discovering the territory are the means to form citizens' ecological knowledge and consciousness, to be translated into a spontaneous eco-compatible lifestyle for the whole community: "guardare all'educazione ambientale e all'educazione alla scoperta del territorio, come al mezzo per formare una conoscenza ed una coscienza ecologica nei cittadini". The naturalistic laboratory operationalizes this through direct user-topic contact and differentiated deepening per audience category, with every recreational activity keeping the educational aim of understanding human actions' effects on ecosystems.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interdisciplinary conduct by experts of each naturalistic sector (biologists, naturalists, foresters, agronomists) so that multiple scientific sectors communicate during both research and divulgation
#### Constraints
- Grounded in the specific local territory (the Piana, Monte Morello, wetlands and canals) and its archaeological and natural features

### Target Learners
- citizens of all ages
- school groups from kindergarten to upper secondary
- at-risk categories and disabled people

### Learning Goals
- ecological consciousness and eco-compatible lifestyle
- knowledge of the local natural and historical territory
