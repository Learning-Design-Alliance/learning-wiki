# Padova resident CVC training pathway (didactic lessons, simulation center practice, and supervised procedural experience)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An institutional training pathway described in the article's Methods: from the first year of residency, residents follow frontal lessons (anatomy, CVC usage and complications), attend operating rooms to become familiar with surgical patients and devices, and participate in simulation courses at an active simulation center (running since 2007) using a variety of skill trainers to develop ultrasound and eye-hand coordination. "From the first year of residency residents follow frontal lessons (e.g. anatomy, CVC usage and related complications) and attend operating rooms in order to be familiar with surgical patients." All in-room procedures are then performed under direct consultant supervision.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Availability of a simulation center with skill trainers and simulation courses
- Consultant supervision available during all in-room procedures
#### Constraints
- Proficiency status is defined case by case by local institutional protocol, usually when last-year residents have performed at least 50 catheterizations, an arbitrary cut-off the authors acknowledge

### Target Learners
- anesthesia residents from first year onward

### Learning Goals
- ultrasound-guided CVC placement skill and eye-hand coordination
