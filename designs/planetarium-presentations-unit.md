# Planetarium presentations unit: students author and present their own WWT tours in a mobile dome

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The project's model supports teachers during a "planetarium presentations unit" lasting one or two weeks: small groups of high school students choose an astronomy topic, storyboard a short (3-5 minute) presentation using WWT imagery, build the tour together, and present it to peers inside the dome. The team demonstrates WWT tour creation when the teacher is not trained, then returns with the mobile planetarium for presentation day.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A one-or-two-week window in the teacher's schedule for the unit
- An initial classroom visit to demonstrate WWT tour creation and check in with each student group when the teacher is not trained in WWT
#### Constraints
- Tours show well only if students avoid projecting text
- No reading from scripts inside the dome; students record a voiceover or memorize their narration

### Target Learners
- high school science students

### Learning Goals
- astronomy research and storytelling
- communication and presentation skills
