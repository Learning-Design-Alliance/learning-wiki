# CoCalc collaboration model for pre-service mathematics teacher training with organizational, content and technological components

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a model of CoCalc use to support various forms of collaboration in training pre-service mathematics teachers, consisting of organizational, content and technological components. Built on the general adaptive cloud-based system structure, it includes teachers' and students' individual or collective spaces whose structural elements combine to provide different types of interaction organization, such as "individual and group work of students, students and teachers cooperation and active communication". Differentiating tasks helps include more cloud tools: Chatroom, LaTeXDocument, ManageaCourse, TaskList, beyond the worksheet.
