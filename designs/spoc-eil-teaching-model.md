# The SPOC + evaluative interactive learning (EIL) teaching model

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes a blended teaching model combining a small private online course with evaluative interactive learning, defined as "a learning model that integrates evaluation-based discussions and interactive competitions to increase student engagement and active learning". It follows "a gradually progressive third-order learning process": preclass SPOC self-study with a preclass test, in-class group discussion of evaluative materials (videos and cases mixing correct and incorrect procedures) scored by number of speeches, and postclass online interaction. The model targets comprehension, application, and analysis levels of Bloom's taxonomy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A SPOC platform (XuetangX) for distributing videos, preclass testing, and monitoring learning processes
- Evaluative learning materials embedding errors, and faculty trained to lead discussions following the 3 'cans' and 3 'avoids' rules
#### Constraints
- EIL places tremendous demands on teachers, requiring deep consideration of teaching logic and reasonable arrangement of activities
- Studied only in a dental adhesion course; applicability to other disciplines not yet validated

### Target Learners
- fourth-year undergraduate dental students

### Learning Goals
- understanding and applying dental adhesion knowledge
- clinical thinking and operational skills
- active learning and engagement
