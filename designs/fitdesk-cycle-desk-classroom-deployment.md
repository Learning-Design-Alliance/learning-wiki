# FitDesk stationary cycle desks deployed in a college lecture classroom

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Stationary cycle desks (FitDesks®, FD Products, Kernersville, NC, USA) placed randomly throughout a lecture classroom so students could pedal during class, contrasted with "standard tables and chairs". Each desk carried an attached odometer and notebook for recording pedal time, mph and RPE each class period; two weeks of familiarization preceded the intervention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Two weeks of familiarization with the cycle desks before the intervention begins
- Attached odometer and per-class log for pedal time, mph and RPE
#### Constraints
- Physical confines of an academic classroom limit feasibility, per the article's limitations

### Target Learners
- undergraduate college students

### Learning Goals
- enabling physical activity during lectures without disrupting course learning
