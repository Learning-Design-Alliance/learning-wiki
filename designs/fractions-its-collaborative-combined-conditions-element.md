# Intelligent tutoring system for fractions used in individual, collaborative, and combined learning conditions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An intelligent tutoring system for fractions served as the core learning environment in this study, deployed with "382 4th and 5th grade students" across three conditions: a combined condition and "individual or collaborative-only learning conditions." The ITS logged process data on students' hints and errors over time, which the authors analyzed to compare conditions, and the activity supported reported situational interest measures.
