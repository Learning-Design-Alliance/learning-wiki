# Quantum Mechanics for Everyone: a four-module MOOC teaching quantum mechanics to nonscientists with algebra only

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Quantum Mechanics for Everyone is a MOOC running on EdX that delivers "a self-contained treatment of quantum theory that is accessible to all with a modest knowledge of high-school-level algebra." It is organized into four one-week modules: introduction to quantum mechanics via Stern-Gerlach experiments and probability; advanced quantum mechanics with spin (analyzer loops, two-slit analog, Wheeler's delayed choice, EPR, Bell experiments, MRI); quantum mechanics of light via Feynman's path-integral model; and quantum seeing in the dark with the Mach-Zehnder interferometer, quantum-Zeno effect, and Hong-Ou-Mandel photon bunching. The course combines text, images, video lectures, simulations, animations, and tutorials.
