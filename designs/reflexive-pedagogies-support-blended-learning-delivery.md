## Description
Getsmart's learning activities were "driven by reflexive pedagogies" comprising four design characteristics: "a) activities which encouraged dialogue and group collaboration; b) a range of task options which catered for learner diversity; c) a feedback loop to facilitate learning, and d) activities which represented a mix of knowledge acquisition processes." The discussion maps these onto cognitive apprenticeship methods such as coaching, scaffolding, and articulation through online chat.
