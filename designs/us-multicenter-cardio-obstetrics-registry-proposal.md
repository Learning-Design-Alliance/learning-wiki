# Proposed large US-based multicenter registry of cardiovascular disease management across pregnancy

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article calls for "a large US‐based multicenter registry collecting CVD management data before, during, and after pregnancy," modeled on the California Maternal Quality Care Collaborative registry, which tracks data for all births in that state. Such a registry would guide pregnancy heart team members on current practices and outcomes, shape management protocols, record outcomes of future pregnancies and neonatal health into early childhood, and support quality-improvement initiatives identifying disparities in maternal and postpartum morbidity and mortality.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional and national grants (eg, NIH, AHA, Preeclampsia Foundation); collaborations with tertiary care centers with dedicated ACHD, high-risk obstetrics, and inherited heart disease programs
#### Constraints
- Existing guideline data derive largely from Canadian and European registries, limiting generalizability to a US population with higher rates of obesity and cardiovascular risk factors

### Target Learners
- Cardio-obstetrics researchers
- Pregnancy heart team clinicians

### Learning Goals
- Determining current practices and outcomes
- Developing management protocols to preserve cardiovascular health
