# Five-step community needs assessment process with engagement and interagency coordination throughout

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Roadmap Needs Assessment Five-Step Process structures the assessment as a pyramid of five sequential steps, with community engagement and interagency coordination as essential components throughout. The report states the team "used the Roadmap Needs Assessment Five-Step Process (see Figure 2) to conduct the needs assessment" and that the process "encompasses all aspects of the methodology." The steps move from identifying existing assets by the Ten Aspects of Community and district, to identifying needs, interviewing key informants, developing recommendations filtered through guiding principles, and aligning systems to achieve desired outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Community engagement and interagency coordination throughout the process
- Data from multiple sources including asset databases, engagement sessions, interviews, and public quantitative data
#### Constraints
- Developed and applied for the Yolo County Roadmap to the Future initiative; recommendations are filtered through the initiative's three guiding principles

### Target Learners
- Children, youth, and families in Yolo County

### Learning Goals
- Coordinating and improving community assets and services that support children's and youth's healthy development
