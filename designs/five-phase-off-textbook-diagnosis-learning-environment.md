# Five-phase online learning environment for diagnosing off-textbook students' conceptions using video vignettes

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 2.5-hour online unit extending an existing intervention on textbook conceptions, structured into five phases: autonomous homework comparing one's own explanation of a spoon sinking in honey with scientific literature; small-group discussion and plenary clarification; a multiple-choice activation task plus impulse talk on diagnosis strategies; a 45-minute group exercise diagnosing conceptions from "video vignettes of authentic students' statements" of two students (Jonas and Tim); and a plenary collaborative reflection scripted in a shared document. It was piloted twice in 2021 with pre-service physics teachers who had completed the original intervention.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants should have already completed the original intervention on textbook students' conceptions
#### Constraints
- Originally planned as face-to-face, it was rearranged as an online session due to the COVID-19 pandemic

### Target Learners
- pre-service physics teachers (5th-6th semester of bachelor studies)

### Learning Goals
- diagnosing off-textbook students' conceptions
- interconnecting content knowledge with pedagogical content knowledge
