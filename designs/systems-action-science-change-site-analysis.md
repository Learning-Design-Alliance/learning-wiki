# Three-perspective organizational site analysis: systems theory, action science, and change theory

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The monograph analyzes the research site organization from three theoretical perspectives to understand its behavioral relationships, structure, and technology before implementing the language intervention. Systems theory describes the organization as interacting components with a goal-seeking component; action science (Argyris and Schon) examines alignment between espoused theories and theories-in-use; change theory (Eoyang's complex adaptive systems and self-organization quadrants) predicts how organizational agents will react to the intervention. This analysis informed scheduling, ethics review, and staff coordination.
