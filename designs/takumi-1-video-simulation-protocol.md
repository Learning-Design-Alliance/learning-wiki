# TAKUMI-1 video-plus-simulation training protocol on the da Vinci Skills Simulator

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
TAKUMI-1 is the Training program in Okayama University for minimally invasive surgery evaluated in this trial. It uses the da Vinci Skills Simulator basic course with nine drills (e.g., sea spikes, camera targeting, suture sponge, thread the ring, energy switching, ring and rail, needle targeting, 30° scope swap), performed consecutively as one cycle for ten cycles, with the intervention arm adding drill-specific educational videos beforehand.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All participants perform the nine drills consecutively as one cycle and complete ten cycles in total
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only in trainees with no prior robotic simulation experience

### Target Learners
- surgical trainees and experienced surgeons in gastrointestinal and hepatopancreatobiliary surgery

### Learning Goals
- foundational robotic surgery manipulation skills measured by overall, efficiency, and penalty scores
