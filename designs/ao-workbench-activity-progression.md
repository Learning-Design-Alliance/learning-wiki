# AO workbench activity progression from systems outlining to optimization

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article organizes workbench teaching as a progression of activity types of increasing depth: a short systems outline/block-diagramming warm-up, multi-station investigations of sensing and correction, a re-alignment design challenge, and finally systems optimization with quantitative performance measurement. Content spans what the authors list as "optical alignment, collimation, beam expansion and compression, re-imaging, optical relays, phase conjugation, aberration, wavefronts, wavefront sensing and beam correction" alongside systems engineering topics such as feedback loops and characterization. The same hardware supports activities from short undergraduate investigations to thorough professional-level engineering challenges.
