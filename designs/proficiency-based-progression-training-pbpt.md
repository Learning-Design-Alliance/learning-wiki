# Proficiency-based progression training (PBPT): trainees practice until an unambiguously demonstrated quantitative performance benchmark is reached, with metric-based feedback

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
PBPT is a training approach for high-volume, high-risk technical and non-technical skills in which trainees receive precise feedback proximate to performance and "a quantitative performance benchmark to work towards" that "should be a valid representation of the procedure". It requires learners to practice until the benchmark is unambiguously demonstrated, relying on a comprehensive quantitative characterisation of the skill. In this study PBPT principles underpinned the six-stage development of the ANTT wound-dressing observational metric, whose metrics and operational definitions offer specific goals for a training curriculum.
