# Embedding equity-conflict scenarios into pediatric pneumonia case-based learning materials

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article embeds social-contextual variables with equity-conflict attributes directly into CBL materials for pediatric pneumonia. Teaching cases situate typical pneumonia presentations within composite backgrounds including "a primary caregiver who is a mother with limited health literacy, a dialect-based communication barrier, and a damp, crowded home environment lacking access to nebulization therapy", requiring residents to integrate biomedical and social-structural reasoning simultaneously.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assessment anchors aligned with the conflict scenarios
- Evaluators observe whether residents identify social obstacles and adjust management plans
#### Constraints
- Scenario backgrounds are composite rather than real patient cases

### Target Learners
- pediatric residents

### Learning Goals
- integrating biomedical and social-structural reasoning
- designing context-sensitive care plans
