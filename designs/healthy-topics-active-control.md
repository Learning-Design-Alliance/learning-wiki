# Healthy Topics: a 12-session health education active control condition controlling for non-specific factors

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Healthy Topics is a 12-session health education program adapted from the Glencoe Health Curriculum (McGraw Hill), covering "physical activity, nutrition, skin care, substance use, and health literacy." It is "consistent with RAP Club in session frequency and length, group size, and time offered during the school day" and controls for meeting with a positive adult instructor, positive peer group experience, learning new material, attention, and time. The authors state prior RCTs showed it is an effective active control condition.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A 1-day group training delivered by the study PI and a co-investigator, plus weekly supervision calls and community co-facilitation matching the intervention arm
#### Constraints
- Serves as an active control rather than a no-treatment condition; it is anticipated to benefit students by teaching health information

### Target Learners
- Eighth graders in urban public schools

### Learning Goals
- Health and wellness knowledge
