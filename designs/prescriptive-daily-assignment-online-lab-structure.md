# Highly prescriptive daily-assignment structure with 24-hour grading, electronic lab notebook, and separated analysis and reporting

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The online course is administered asynchronously via Canvas with "daily assignments that link content both forward and backward" and grading "generally within 24 hours", providing feedback before the formal report. Background reading, data collection and analysis are graded and returned so students can amend analysis before the report, allowing detailed feedback on report format items. Students keep an Electronic Lab Notebook with an indexed Table of Contents and an Activity Log of hours spent on tasks, which feeds back into balancing assignment length and difficulty.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- timely grading within about 24 hours to provide critical feedback for future assignments
- separation of analysis from reporting so students can amend analysis before the formal report
#### Constraints
- assignments are due essentially every class day and range from 30 minutes to several hours

### Target Learners
- online adult learners across multiple time zones

### Learning Goals
- research notebook maintenance
- formal report preparation
- data analysis with uncertainties
