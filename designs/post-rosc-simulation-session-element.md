# A 90-minute post-ROSC simulation session with two cases, debriefs, and an intervening chalk talk

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 90-minute simulation session for internal medicine interns on postarrest care, including 2 simulation cases (ventricular tachycardia arrest in the ICU and pulseless electrical activity arrest in an acute care setting), each followed by a structured debrief based on PEARLS, with a teaching chalk talk between cases reviewing a post-ROSC framework. Interns alternated between participant and observer roles. The session was implemented at 3 hospitals in the University of Washington internal medicine residency program.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- High-fidelity simulation lab with human patient simulator, defibrillator, monitoring equipment, and standardized nurse and consultant facilitators
- Two faculty facilitators per session and simulation technicians
#### Constraints
- Planning, organizing, and implementing high-fidelity simulation sessions is resource-intensive and requires facilitator expertise, which may not be available at all programs

### Target Learners
- Internal medicine interns (PGY-1)

### Learning Goals
- Managing patients in the post-ROSC period
- Communication and handoff skills
- Leading code response teams
