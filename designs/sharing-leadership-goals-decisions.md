# Sharing-based cooperation structure: shared leadership, shared goal-setting and shared decision-making in case conferences

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Drawing on Packard et al. and the sharing concept, the article structures the extra-linguistic tasks of case conferences around shared practice team leadership, shared goal-setting and shared decision-making. Leadership is assigned to the profession with the greatest expertise in the patient's health issues and "must be renegotiated at each case conference". Information exchange makes the professions' approaches apparent and helps prioritise objectives and decisions, on which basis an interprofessional care plan aligned with current evidence is developed by all participants.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Information exchange across professions to make approaches apparent and prioritise options
#### Constraints
- Leadership role is situational and renegotiated per conference rather than fixed

### Target Learners
- interprofessional student teams in health professions education

### Learning Goals
- negotiating leadership, goals and decisions
- developing interprofessional care plans
