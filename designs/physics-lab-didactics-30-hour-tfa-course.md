# Physics Lab Didactics: a 30-hour active learning course with report-and-proposal assessment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 30-hour course within TFA entitled "Physics Lab Didactics", split for many activities into two groups (Math & Science and Math & Phys) covering lessons with discussion (8 h) and laboratory (22 h) on measurement, uncertainties and basic physical quantities. Assessment required two lab reports with didactic analysis, or one report plus a proposal for an active learning lab activity for a well-defined class, written for peer readers and discussed informally before the exam.
