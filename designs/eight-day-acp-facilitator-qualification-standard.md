# The eight-day ACP facilitator qualification standard of ACP Germany and ACP Swiss with SP-supported training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article is based on the joint standard of the professional societies ACP Germany and ACP Swiss for an eight-day ACP facilitator qualification, developed in 2017/18. Figure 1 shows that with the introduction of SP-supported training the course 'was divided into three modules in order to intensify the facilitation between the modules in practical application'.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants should only pass the qualification and be certified if the required skills have been confirmed by a reliable skill and performance assessment
#### Constraints
- Internationally there is considerable variability in ACP facilitator qualifications; only a few programmes are implemented with SPs and the authors note there is no systematic survey on the subject

### Target Learners
- Healthcare professionals qualifying as ACP facilitators under ACP Germany or ACP Swiss standards

### Learning Goals
- The four sections of the standardised ACP conversation, introduced theoretically and practised with SPs and in real life
