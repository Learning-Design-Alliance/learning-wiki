# Curriculum development framework: five requirements for designing activities that build discipline fluency

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A framework guiding creation of instructional strategies that deliberately develop learners' discipline fluency, defined as the ability to unpack and transition through multiple modes of representation, discern their affordances, and engage in cognitive exercises to articulate meaning. The framework "requires developers to" (1) unpack a topic in terms of learning outcomes for the intended audience, (2) examine canonical representations for appropriate affordances, (3) determine whether new Pedagogical Discipline Representations are needed, (4) design complex scenarios combining essential features of various modes with intellectual tasks, and (5) structure the activity at a high enough cognitive level, i.e. QCR = 4.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All five requirements must be addressed, culminating in activities at QCR = 4
- Acknowledging disciplinary affordances of multiple complementary representations and integrating them with multiple intellectual tasks
#### Constraints
- Fluency-oriented activities are designed for post-instruction contexts, after lecture and other collaborative active learning strategies

### Target Learners
- Novice learners in introductory astronomy and physics courses

### Learning Goals
- Developing discipline fluency: unpacking, discerning, making meaning, and articulating reasoning across representations
