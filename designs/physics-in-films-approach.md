# Physics in Films: using popular movie scenes analyzed against physical laws as the teaching vehicle for general-education physical science

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The approach uses popular movies to illustrate the principles of physical science, "analyzin g individual scenes against the background of the fundamental physical laws." Students watch films at home and submit written analyses of physics principles in scenes of their choosing, then some present oral analyses in class; in class, film clips are integrated with lecture materials, quizzes, demonstrations, and slides. The targeted audience is science and non-science majors alike, and the course is positioned as a substitute for traditional Physical Science courses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students watch the films at home and turn in written analyses of scenes; written and oral analyses count toward the course grade
- In-class film clips must be integrated with lecture materials, quizzes, demonstrations, and slides
#### Constraints
- Developed for a general education Physical Science course; versions for majors in other science disciplines are described only as a future option

### Target Learners
- college and university general-education students, science and non-science majors alike

### Learning Goals
- basic physical science principles and frontier discoveries of science
- science literacy
