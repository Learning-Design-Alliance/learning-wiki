# Geoinformation (GIS) competence as a learnable learner characteristic

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article adopts Orekhova's definition of geoinformation competence as "the characteristic of the individual learner, willing and able to solve practical problems and cognitive geographical focus" requiring "effective decision-making and their execution with taking into account the existing experience with GIS technologies means". The article treats this competence as a formable outcome of school geography education, developed through the staged use of the electronic atlas, and links it to career advantages since GIS expertise is highly valued in many professions.
