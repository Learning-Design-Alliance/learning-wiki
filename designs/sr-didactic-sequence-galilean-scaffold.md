# Three-step didactic sequence for Special Relativity with representations at each step

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Following Arlego and Otero, the article lays out a sample didactic sequence: first analyzing Galilean reference frames and relative velocity without a preferential frame, then introducing the light postulate with Galilean situations as a scaffold and transitioning to SR by replacing moving objects with light beams, and finally presenting relativistic kinematic results such as length contraction, time dilation, and loss of simultaneity. The article states that "must be present at each of these steps, and more importantly pedagogically linked to situations and schemes as they are presented in the didactic sequence." Steps 1 and 2 rely on everyday experience and physical demonstrations; computer-generated animation is reserved for step 3, where conceptual breakpoints concentrate.
