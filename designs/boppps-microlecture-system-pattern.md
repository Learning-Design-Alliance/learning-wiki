# BOPPPS-based micro-lecture system: pre-rotation online micro-lectures with a pre-test gate, in-rotation participatory teaching, and feedback-driven summary

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
This instructional pattern sequences a rotation around the six BOPPPS stages: students complete micro-lectures on basic orthopaedic knowledge through an online chatting group at least two weeks in advance, may begin the rotation only after passing a pre-clinical learning test, then receive a new set of micro-lectures delivered four times (eight hours total) alongside Mini-CEX, DOPS, CBL, PBL, and TBL, followed by individualised feedback after assessments. The article states this lets micro-lectures "function as key components rather than supplements".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires continuous investment and well-established protocols for producing serial micro-lectures, and preceptors must complete a BOPPPS teaching design form.
#### Constraints
- The article states standalone micro-lectures are primarily suitable for self-contained, fundamental knowledge points and less effective for macroscopic topics with complex logical structures; high production cost limits micro-lectures to a supplementary role.

### Target Learners
- Orthopaedic postgraduate residents

### Learning Goals
- Systematic mastery of orthopaedic subject knowledge and clinical competency
