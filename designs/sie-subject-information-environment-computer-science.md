# Subject information environment for learning computer science

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The subject information environment (SIE) is the digital infrastructure the authors built to bring the methodological system to life. Its main element is the Interactive digital course, which includes "the methodological support of academic classes, the communication component, and the interactive check and measurement complex". Structural elements cover requirements for discipline acquisition results, an initial test, types of academic classes, modular and competency-based learning technology, and the schedule of the academic process. For independent work, reproductive, productive and creative assignments are offered at various levels, and MOOCs serve as interactive study guides on individual sections without compulsory result checking.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The SIE must provide a mechanism for seamless inclusion of MOOC elements into the students' independent work
#### Constraints
- MOOCs are used without any compulsory requirements for checking the results of mastering the courses

### Target Learners
- university students studying computer science at Russian and Kazakh institutions

### Learning Goals
- mastering computer science content in a blended format
- self-regulated independent work
- development of information and communication competency
