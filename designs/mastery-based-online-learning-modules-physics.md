# Mastery-based online learning modules for introductory physics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Each module pairs an assessment component of 'just one or two questions' with an instruction-and-practice component; students get five attempts and must attempt the assessment before accessing learning resources, a design inspired by preparation-for-future-learning and productive struggle. A sequence of 7–12 modules covers one to two weeks of course content. The assessments serve as formative self-assessment, letting students with higher incoming knowledge pass quickly while less-prepared students get practice on basic modules.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must make a first assessment attempt before studying the learning resources
#### Constraints
- Modules focus on one topic each, blending textbook content with end-of-chapter problems by sub-topic

### Target Learners
- University students in introductory physics with diverse incoming knowledge

### Learning Goals
- Introductory physics content mastery and self-assessment
