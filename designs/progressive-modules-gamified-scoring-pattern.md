# Progressive training modules with gamified scoring from basic target practice to full procedure simulation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The simulator structures practice as progressive difficulty: training begins with basic target practice and advances to complete procedure simulation with placental models, with five target boards that increase in difficulty. Each level measures accuracy and time to produce an overall score and grade, and a persistent leaderboard "records and displays top scores across users, introducing an element of competitive motivation within the educational framework". Hidden assessment points enable objective evaluation of procedural competency.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- procedure time and accuracy metrics feeding a weighted scoring algorithm
- an educator to interpret performance data for competency determination
#### Constraints
- the automated assessment of definitive procedural competency is not yet fully integrated

### Target Learners
- fetal therapy fellows
- maternal-fetal medicine specialists

### Learning Goals
- laser targeting accuracy
- procedural timing
- technical skill in fetoscopic laser surgery
