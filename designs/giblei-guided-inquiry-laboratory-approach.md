# GIBLEI: Guided Inquiry-Based Laboratory Experiments enriched Instructional approach

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
GIBLEI is an instructional approach that integrates hands-on laboratory experimentation with guided inquiry, in which students "investigate, hypothesize, experiment, and draw conclusions" through an eight-stage cycle from semi-structured problems to assessment. It is grounded in constructivism, cognitive apprenticeship, and self-determination theory, using scaffolded teacher facilitation, collaborative design discussions, and reflective conclusions. The article positions it as a response to resource constraints in Ethiopian secondary schools, incorporating locally available materials and structured teacher support.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher training in inquiry-based pedagogy and the 5E lesson plan format, as provided in the study's three-day training program
- Scaffolded teacher and lab-technician facilitation across the eight-stage activity cycle
#### Constraints
- Developed and tested in resource-limited Ethiopian secondary schools; the open-ended nature of inquiry can cause cognitive overload without sufficient foundational knowledge

### Target Learners
- Grade 10 secondary school biology students

### Learning Goals
- Science process skills: formulating hypotheses, designing experiments, interpreting data, and drawing conclusions
