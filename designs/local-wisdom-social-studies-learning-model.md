# Social studies learning model based on local wisdom (teaching materials, lesson plans, and knowledge and social-attitude tests)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A social studies learning model for grade IV elementary students that integrates local wisdom values — material culture such as traditional houses, clothing and crafts, and non-material philosophical values — into teaching materials, syllabus, lesson plans and assessment instruments. The article states the model's content preparation "combined with local wisdom values", so that "These values are used as a guide and direction for students in carrying out life", while education and real life are synergized to create knowledge and character. It was developed through a five-stage design process and validated by experts and users before classroom implementation.

### Learning Goals
- social studies knowledge
