# Seven-week comics-as-literary-didactic-method intervention programme with teacher training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A structured programme embedding comics in Slovene language and art lessons for primary pupils, delivered in "eight units, at 19 Slovene and art lessons" over approximately seven weeks. It progresses from learning comics' basic characteristics and speech bubbles, through creating comics and detecting text incoherence, to active work with literary texts. Teachers received a six-hour training in three parts and a graded lesson-plan manual (one version for grades 2–3, another for grades 4–5, differing mainly in text difficulty).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers completed a six-hour training in comics as a literary-didactic method and a detailed lesson-plan manual before implementation
#### Constraints
- The programme was designed for grades 2–5 in Slovene language and art instruction, with texts graded by class level rather than individually per class

### Target Learners
- primary pupils in grades 2 to 5

### Learning Goals
- reading comprehension
- ability to perceive text incoherence
- interest in reading literary texts
- attitude towards reading
