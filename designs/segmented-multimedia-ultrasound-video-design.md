# Ultrasound teaching videos segmented step by step and designed around Mayer's multimedia learning principles

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's video design pattern segments each tutorial into learning objectives, basic skills (physics, image interpretation, probe handling), advanced standard-view examination, and a final summary, following the segmenting principle. Signaling colors, synchronous text/spoken words/visuals, dual coding, spatial and temporal contiguity, and modality, personalization and voice principles were applied; raw footage was repeatedly student-tested and externally reviewed before use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cooperation with multimedia production expertise for graphical visualization and video-technical implementation
- Student feedback iterations and external a-priori product-level evaluation before first use
#### Constraints
- The pre-training principle was excluded from implementation

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students inexperienced in ultrasound

### Learning Goals
- ultrasound probe handling, standard view acquisition, anatomical identification and physiological measurement
