# riAniMO pedagogical framework mapping learning theories to training phases

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article explicitly grounds the riAniMO program in a set of pedagogical theories assigned to its two phases: individual constructivism and experiential learning for the e-learning preparatory phase, and social constructivism, experiential learning, and situated learning for the in-situ simulation phase. As printed, "The RiAniMO project is based on various pedagogical theories as shown in Table 1." The e-learning phase lets individuals experiment in an online environment with gamified reviews and on-demand paths, while the simulation phase uses horizontal peer and facilitator feedback, progressive scenarios, and reflection on experience in the real workplace.
