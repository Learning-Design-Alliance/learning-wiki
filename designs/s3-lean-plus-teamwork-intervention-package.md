# S3 combined intervention package: lean process engineering plus aviation-style CRM teamwork training with in-theatre coaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention combined two components delivered by an experienced specialist team over three months. Lean process engineering comprised a half-day training session for core frontline staff (11 attended) covering concepts including Muda, Poka-Yoke, Kaizen, flow, Just in time, process mapping and PDCA cycles, followed by frequent on-site coaching of staff project groups. Teamwork training comprised educational content on the psychology of human error and aviation CRM-based teamwork content (situational awareness sharing, flat hierarchy, communication protocols, checklists), delivered in three sessions (20 staff attended one each), followed by "5 days in-theatre coaching spread over 6 weeks" supporting WHO checklist completion and other non-technical skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivery by experienced specialist trainers and consultants
- On-site coaching presence over the intervention period
- Frontline staff participation in training sessions and project work
#### Constraints
- Observers and staff could not be blinded to study arm; the amount of external support used would be infeasible at whole-hospital scale

### Target Learners
- operating theatre team members (nursing, surgical and anaesthetic staff)

### Learning Goals
- non-technical teamwork and communication skills
- reliable completion of the WHO surgical safety checklist
- lean process improvement methods
