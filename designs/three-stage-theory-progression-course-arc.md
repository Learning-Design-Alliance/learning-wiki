# Three-stage course arc: consolidate known theory, introduce the new theory, then explore its concrete manifestations

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course follows a three-part structure described by the author: "The second chapter then introduces special and general relativity at the same time, while the third chapter explores concrete manifestations of relativistic gravitation, notably gravitational waves and black holes." This follows an opening chapter that reformulates known Newtonian concepts. The pattern moves from consolidation, to new formalism, to applications, giving students a full arc from familiar ground to frontier phenomena.
