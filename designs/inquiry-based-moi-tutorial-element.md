# Inquiry-based tutorial on the method of images with scaffolded tests A and B

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A research-based guided inquiry tutorial for upper-level E&M on the method of images, developed iteratively via expert feedback, think-aloud interviews, and four years of in-class use. It scaffolds recognizing when the method applies, checking boundary conditions, and computing potential via superposition. It was paired with scaffolded tests: "The scaffolded pre-/posttests each consist of six questions based on a problem with a charge and grounded conductor(s)." Posttest reliability was moderate: "The Cronbach's alpha for posttest ranged between 0. 6 and 0.7 for three instructors, indicating moderate internal consistency".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administered after traditional lecture-based instruction on the method of images; instructors assigned it as homework rather than in-class group work
#### Constraints
- Cronbach's alpha of 0.6-0.7 is moderate, which the authors attribute to the test measuring multiple, partly independent aspects of understanding

### Target Learners
- upper-level undergraduate E&M students
- graduate physics students

### Learning Goals
- identify when the method of images applies
- determine correct image charge configurations
- apply the uniqueness theorem and boundary conditions
- compute electrostatic potential via superposition
