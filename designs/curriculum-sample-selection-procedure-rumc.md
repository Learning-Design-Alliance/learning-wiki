# Curriculum sample selection procedure: online course plus on-site exam mimicking early medical school

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A medical school admission procedure in which applicants complete a 4-week online course (estimated load 80 h) followed by an on-site multiple choice test (70% weight) and an essay (30%). The article states the course and exam "were designed to mimic the courses and examinations in our programme as closely as possible", integrating biomedical, clinical, sociological, ethical and psychological perspectives. It was open to all applicants, tested 392–441 applicants per year, and ranked final scores to admit 106, 104 and 164 applicants in 2010–2012.
