# Formative Intervention Module (MIF) on sound and music integrating disciplinary, learning-problem and transversal elements

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The MIF is a 10-lecture modular teacher-education course structured in six steps: disciplinary elements analysis ("wave production, propagation, transmission and reception processes" through consonance and rhythm), curricular aspects analysis, learning analysis by single elements, analysis by mental models or learning swindles, environmental learning analysis, and transversal elements analysis. It ends with participants drafting original curricular proposals for secondary school. It operationalizes the PCK paradigm by transforming disciplinary content into accessible forms for students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prospective teachers with science degrees who can engage in disciplinary analysis, discussion groups and project production
#### Constraints
- Designed and evaluated in the context of the biennial SSIS specialization school at Udine University, Italy

### Target Learners
- prospective secondary-school physics teachers

### Learning Goals
- understanding of waves and sound
- pedagogical content knowledge for teaching sound and music
