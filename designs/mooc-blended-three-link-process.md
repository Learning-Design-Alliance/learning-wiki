# Three-link MOOC-based blended teaching process: pre-class video self-study, in-class flipped application, post-class group homework

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article structures its blended teaching as a before/in/after-class sequence on the university MOOC platform. Before class, the platform issues notices and videos and students "complete the preliminary study independently" and fill in a study survey form. In class, basic knowledge is not repeated; flipped forms such as case application, scenario demonstration, and student demonstration with group reports and challenge sessions address key and difficult points. After class, students finish homework in groups, consult literature, and complete a book report while teachers interact via the platform's bulletin board system.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Recorded lectures made available on the MOOC platform before class
- A study survey form collected before class so the teacher can design classroom content
- Group sessions in class for discussion, guidance, and reflection
- Same learning objectives, materials, homework, and assessment items across formats
#### Constraints
- Implemented in a single school's Fundamental Nursing II course (22 theoretical hours, 26 practical hours)
- Course content limited to injection methods, disease observations, pain care, and hospice care

### Target Learners
- Third-year undergraduate nursing students

### Learning Goals
- Fundamental nursing knowledge and clinical operations
- Critical thinking dispositions
