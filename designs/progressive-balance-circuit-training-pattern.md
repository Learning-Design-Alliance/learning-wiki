# Progressive, varied balance circuit training with reactive postural challenges

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The intervention was a balance circuit training on varying surfaces with eight stations per session of 5 minutes each, designed to "induce reactive postural adjustments, forcing participants to permanently re-stabilize". Exercises were progressively adjustable by increasing strap tension, partner distance or removing vision, and were replaced with a new set after six weeks to keep training challenging. Sessions ran twice weekly for 12 weeks in groups of 10–12.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Progressive difficulty adjustment to participant skill level; new exercise sets after six weeks to maintain challenge
#### Constraints
- No explicit strategies were taught; effects were demonstrated against an active relaxation control, not a passive control

### Target Learners
- healthy adults without extensive balance-training experience
