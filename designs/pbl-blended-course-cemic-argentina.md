# A blended-learning PBL course combining fortnightly face-to-face vignette meetings with two weeks of online forum work

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This element is the four-week, non-mandatory PBL course reported by the article, run for 68 senior medical students (36 in 4th year, 32 in 5th year) at a private Buenos Aires health sciences center. Students met tutors "twice a week for face-to-face lessons" complemented by online tasks; tutors prepared contextualized health vignettes, and "the activities on the learning platform were mainly based on discussion forums for the students to intervene in groups." The course aimed to improve students' performance using the learning platform through PBL strategies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tutors who are specialists in medicine and experienced PBL teachers, highly committed and available for face-to-face interactions
- Senior students with at least three years of prior face-to-face PBL experience
#### Constraints
- Non-mandatory but a programme requirement; only 4th- and 5th-year students participated
- Only two tutors oversaw the forums, which the authors describe as overburdening

### Target Learners
- 4th- and 5th-year medical students

### Learning Goals
- Technological skills for using the online learning platform
- PBL strategies such as group work, information searching, hypothesis generation and critical thinking
