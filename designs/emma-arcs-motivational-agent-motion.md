# Emma: an ARCS-based female animated pedagogical agent embedded in the SimQuest motion inquiry environment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Emma is a female animated pedagogical agent created by linking SimQuest with Elckerlyc and Loquendo, delivering audible motivational messages 2 s after an assignment opened and 1 s after it was answered. Her messages were based on Keller's ARCS model, addressing task relevance through goal orientation, motive matching and familiarity, and self-efficacy through learning requirements, opportunities for success and personal control. She had the role of a fellow student, with facial expressions and head movements attuned to her messages, and her behavior was hard-coded so reactions were the same for all participants.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Messages delivered via headphones with 5 extra minutes of training time to compensate for listening
#### Constraints
- Only Emma's face and a small part of her upper body were visible
- Newly-constructed sentences sounded more monotonous than pre-programmed ones, a technical limitation the authors say may have diminished her impact

### Target Learners
- third-year secondary school students in the Netherlands

### Learning Goals
- motivation (task relevance and self-efficacy) during kinematics inquiry learning
