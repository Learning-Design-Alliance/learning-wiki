# fMRI diagnostic-reasoning task with FFA-localizer runs for radiological expertise

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A task and scanning protocol built for the scanner: "66 radiographs were extracted from an existing teaching file" for the diagnostic-reasoning runs, each trial presenting the radiograph for 2s or 10s, followed by a 1s scrambled mask and a yes/no diagnostic question. Two FFA-localization runs (45 face and 45 object images in alternating 30s blocks) identified individual right-FFA ROIs before the diagnostic task. The yes/no button-box format eliminated speech-related motion artifacts while capturing diagnostic reasoning during image presentation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Individual right FFA ROIs must be defined with an established functional localizer; ROIs of at least 20 voxels were required
#### Constraints
- Radiographs were all abnormal and were presented twice (short runs first), so the order of short- and long-presentation runs could not be counterbalanced and order or novelty effects were possible

### Target Learners
- laypeople without radiology experience
- radiologists in training (residents and fellows)

### Learning Goals
- visual diagnostic expertise in radiograph interpretation
