# Deutsche SchülerAkademie course 'Paul Klee and vector fields' (summer 2019)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A thirteen-day summer school course within the Deutsche SchülerAkademie, the main German institution for supporting gifted high-school students since 1988. The course had 16 participants (10 female, 6 male) aged 15 to 18, required basic knowledge of vector and differential calculus, and had students independently prepare talks on first-year university vector calculus and linear algebra before creating their own artistic projects related to Klee and vector fields.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants need basic knowledge of vector and differential calculus and prepare talks from a reading list before the course.
#### Constraints
- Participants must attend a German school, be in grades 10 to 12, be no older than 20, and have outstanding intellectual abilities; each can attend only one DSA in their lifetime.

### Target Learners
- gifted high-school students in grades 10-12

### Learning Goals
- introducing higher-level mathematics (vector fields, gradient, curl, divergence)
- communicating mathematics to lay audiences
- creating math-related artwork
