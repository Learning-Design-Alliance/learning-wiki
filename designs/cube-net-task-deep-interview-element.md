# Cube-object geometry exploration task with unstructured deep interview

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study's core instrument is a geometry test built on the cube object: "There were two instruments used in this studyto explore the student's internal process in geometry concept with using cube object." The first contains statements letting students explore cube nets from a given cube object with justifications; the second is a 36-item learning-style test (12 items per style, per Gunawan) and a 10-question mathematics ability test adopted from the national examination. Students work the test while an unstructured deep interview follows their narration, recorded in audio and video.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires an unstructured interview in which the interviewer follows the interviewee's narration and generates questions spontaneously
- Requires audio and video recording of subjects from test solving through the interview section
- Requires prior classification of students by learning style test and mathematics ability level
#### Constraints
- Explored with visual-learning-style 5th-grade students of middle mathematical ability in the cube/cube-net context

### Target Learners
- elementary school students (5th grade)

### Learning Goals
- geometry: internal representation processes in cube-to-cube-net transformation
