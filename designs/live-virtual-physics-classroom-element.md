# Live Virtual Physics Classroom (LVPC)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A live virtual classroom implementation in which an instructor presented weekly physics lessons online to distance-education students. The article states that "the instructor presented one-dimensional motion, Newton mechanic, force concept, two-dimensional motion, energy conservation and momentum subjects in each week". Students could ask questions at misunderstanding points, watch the instructor solve problems in detail online, and follow recorded lessons whenever they wanted. Achievement effects were examined via midterm, final and make-up scores, and student opinions via interviews.
