# The seminar-case learning (SCL) model as a combination of seminar learning and case-based learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The SCL model is a teaching approach that "is a combination of seminar and case-based learning." It integrates "the efficient communication of seminar learning and the clinical thinking of case-based learning." In practice, a teacher selects a typical authentic case and distributes anonymized patient information to students before class. Students independently prepare responses, then during class the teacher gives a brief lecture, introduces the case, and facilitates group analysis and open seminar discussion.
