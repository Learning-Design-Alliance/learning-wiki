# CREATE: a six-strategy meta-creative pedagogy for fostering creative thinking

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
CREATE is an explicit, theory-grounded meta-creative pedagogy whose acronym names six strategies: comprehending the core problem and subproblems, reconstructing connections, exploring and experimenting, adding more ideas, thinking over constraints and reservations ("True but…"), and evaluating. It combines regulation of cognition, creative self-regulation, and metacognitive pedagogy, training students "to be aware of their creative thinking process, while also fostering fluency, flexibility, and the generation of original ideas." The article presents it as suitable for natural classroom settings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Explicit instruction with clear, specific guidance on approaching tasks creatively
- Tasks that are complex, unfamiliar, and non-routine (CUN tasks) so meta-creative strategies are needed
#### Constraints
- The article reports the flexibility component responded less to the pedagogy on the immediate assessment

### Target Learners
- elementary school students (sixth grade, aged 11–12)

### Learning Goals
- creative thinking: fluency, flexibility, and originality of ideas
- meta-creative self-regulation during creative problem-solving
