# Immunology Concept Mapping Chart linking core concepts, learning objectives, and case questions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Table 1 of the article is an Immunology Concept Mapping Chart with three columns: core immunology concept, learning objective, and question on the case. Rows run from "PAMP and DAMP activation of innate immunity" through TLR activation and signaling, actions of mediators of acute inflammation, and physiological and pathological effects of acute inflammation, each mapped to a specific case question about the septic-shock patient. It operationalizes the backwards-design principle by showing how each concept is interrogated in the case.
