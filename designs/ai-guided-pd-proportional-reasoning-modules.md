# Ten-module AI-guided asynchronous PD program on proportional reasoning with eight designed AI-mediated mechanisms

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The AI-guided PD comprised ten asynchronous modules completed across one academic semester, typically requiring 30-45 hours, each centered on rate and proportional reasoning. Eight intentionally designed AI-mediated mechanisms structured engagement: adaptive prompting, real-time feedback, progressive scaffold fading, simulated student reasoning, supportive nonjudgmental tone, flexible pacing, representational nudges, and cycles of creative challenge and reflection. Teachers solved tasks, generated multiple strategies, posed problems, interpreted simulated student thinking, and reflected on instruction.

## Design Implications

#### Requirements

### Target Learners
- in-service Grade 5-6 mathematics teachers
