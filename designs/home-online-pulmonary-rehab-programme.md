# Nine-week home-based online comprehensive pulmonary rehabilitation programme for COPD (education, group exercise, consultations, digital health diary)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A multidisciplinary home programme adapted from a hospital outpatient programme and the authors' previous 6-week home study. It combined “weekly group-based education and exercising, as well as individual consultations, for 9 weeks at home,” delivered by a specialist, nurse, physiotherapist, nutritionist and social worker via TV-based videoconferencing. Patients answered daily health-diary questions, entered SpO2 and step counts shown with a traffic-light colour code, watched preparatory videos, and used a follow-along exercise video.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Broadband connection at home
- Technical training session and user manual at deployment
- A physiotherapist-led weekly group exercise schedule and weekly 60-minute education sessions
#### Constraints
- The multi-point control unit supported a maximum of six simultaneous participant sites, i.e. five home patients and one healthcare site
- Privacy required agreements about others present in the living room during sessions

### Target Learners
- Adult COPD patients across GOLD grades I–IV, including homebound patients

### Learning Goals
- Self-management of COPD (medication use, breathing strategies, energy conservation, exacerbation prevention)
- Maintenance of physical activity and exercise habits
