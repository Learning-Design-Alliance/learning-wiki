# Dialogue-based virtual exhibition guide in the form of a curator

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A virtual exhibition guide taking the form of the exhibition's curator is present in the space to add interaction richness and address loneliness. Interaction is dialogue-based with selectable dialogue options; the guide's main functions are "to provide the background story of this exhibition, give tips on the highlights of this exhibition, and help the visitor navigate the space." The dialogue system is implemented with the ink narrative scripting language, and a map of the exhibition can be requested.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Dialogue-based interaction implemented using the ink narrative scripting language developed by Inkle Studio
#### Constraints
- The primary avatar takes the form of the curator; the authors note future developments can explore an avatar in the form of a fellow visitor

### Target Learners
- art and archaeology students using VR headsets

### Learning Goals
- orientation and navigation in the virtual exhibition
- engagement with exhibition background story
