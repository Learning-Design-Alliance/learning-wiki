# Conceptual model of cultural and contextual factors shaping students' discussion behaviors in student-centered education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study built a conceptual model (Figure 2) linking cultural and contextual factors to students' discussion behaviors and skills in PBL. It holds that students externalize cultural backgrounds and internalize the discussion aspect of PBL, so that 'students' discussion behaviors and skills and their development from the first to the third year were shaped by complex interactions between contextual factors, cultural factors, and the PBL discussion sessions.' The model organizes four cultural factors and six mediating contextual factors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Data from interviews, observations, documents and contextual information across multiple institutions to populate the model
#### Constraints
- Derived from a qualitative comparative case study in three medical schools; findings do not lend themselves to generalization
