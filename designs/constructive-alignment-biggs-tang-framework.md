# Constructive alignment (Biggs & Tang) as a student-centered framework for aligning learning outcomes, teaching-and-learning activities, and assessment tasks

## Description
The study is underpinned by the theory of constructive alignment coined by Biggs and Tang, derived from constructivism and advocating active involvement in creating understanding. As the article states, "CA is a student-centered model focusing on what MPTs undertake and value about learning rather than what teacher educators accomplish", with teacher educators facilitating while pre-service teachers construct knowledge through classroom activities. Aligning a module involves establishing the outcomes to achieve, the activities to engage in, and the tasks measuring achievement, so that assessment tasks mirror learning outcomes and activities and tasks inform each other.
