# Dynamic web technology-supported problem-based collaborative learning environment built on Google tools

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An 8-week online collaborative environment for problem-based learning, developed with Nelson's (2009) collaborative problem solving method and expert input. It used "Google+ Circle, Google Chat, Google+ Documents, Mind 42 (for creating online concept maps), Google+, Blogger, Google Hangouts (for verbal, written and visual calls) Google+ Homepage (sharing the video records), Google Calendar, and Google+, Drive (Survey)". Experimental-group students saved weekly work in blogs and Google Drive while solving real designed problems.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students need adequate technological knowledge and experience with the environment and the dynamic web technologies available, as the research group had received prior IT coursework
#### Constraints
- The environment was designed and evaluated with pre-service ICT teachers in a department with substantial computer laboratories and Internet infrastructure

### Target Learners
- pre-service ICT teachers in teacher education

### Learning Goals
- collaborative problem solving
- 21st century proficiencies such as communication and cooperation
- course content achievement
