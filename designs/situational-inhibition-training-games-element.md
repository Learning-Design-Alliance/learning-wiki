# Four situational inhibition training games (button-selection and voice-recognition)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A series of four digital inhibition training games developed with Construct 3 per the situational cognitive training game framework. As printed, "the game system comprises two types of games: button-selection and voice-recognition games". Button-selection games are based on the Simon task and require suppressing automatic responses across three difficulty stages; voice-recognition games are based on the Stroop task and require verbally responding while inhibiting conflicting visual and textual information. Situations include soccer, baseball, sushi-eating, and pet training, and the system provides immediate feedback and accurate response-time recording.
