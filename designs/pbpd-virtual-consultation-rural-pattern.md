# Workshop plus virtual consultation practice-based professional development for rural teachers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
This professional development pattern compresses in-person practice-based PD into a 1-day summer workshop delivered to school teams at a central location, then extends support through virtual consultation. The article states that "Virtual consultation was considered a critical element in reinforcing and extending what was learned during in-person training," with one-on-one on-demand video consultation plus two mandatory 90-min video-conferencing sessions reviewing completed lesson plans. The in-person session was subdivided into four learning phases covering learning theory, strategy instruction, SRSD for POW + TREE, and the writing manual.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- School teams including teachers, specialists, and administrators participate together in a team-based approach
- Ongoing follow-up support is provided, since teachers with no follow-up support are unlikely to implement a new practice successfully
#### Constraints
- Treatment fidelity verification was limited by the large geographic area and remote location of the rural schools
- Access to broadband Internet is limited in many rural settings

### Target Learners
- General education teachers, classroom support specialists, and administrators in low-wealth rural schools

### Learning Goals
- Teachers' knowledge and delivery of SRSD persuasive writing instruction
