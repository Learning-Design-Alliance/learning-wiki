# Five-component didactic curriculum draft for AI training of radiation oncology and medical physics trainees

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The Working Group proposes a high-level curriculum draft so that trainees in medical physics and radiation oncology can "adequately grasp the basic principles of AI". It comprises five components: responsible conduct of AI, bias and disparities; methodology and data science basics; interpreting data and models; practical experience and applications; and data sharing, logistics and culture. The authors state these principles are generalizable to medicine as a whole and expect the curriculum to build a trainee core versed in data science fundamentals, ethics and data sharing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Curriculum should address the needs of medical physicists, radiation oncologists, and data scientists and their respective roles in the process.
#### Constraints
- There are currently no educational guidelines for AI training in radiation oncology or medical physics residents.

### Target Learners
- radiation oncology residents and fellows
- medical physics residents

### Learning Goals
- basic principles of AI and data science
- responsible conduct and bias awareness
- interpreting data and models
- data sharing
