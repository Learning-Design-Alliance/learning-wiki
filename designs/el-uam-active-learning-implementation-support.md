# Active-learning implementation support package: workshops, learning-goals documents, shared materials, and added TA staffing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The institutional change initiative at Universidad Autónoma Metropolitana-Iztapalapa equipped two volunteer instructors and four TAs with a preparation sequence: a five-day June 2025 workshop, July meetings articulating learning objectives with Bloom's Taxonomy placement, a two-day consolidation workshop, a WhatsApp coordination group, and a third November workshop with personalized feedback using the Reformed Teaching Observation Protocol. The article stresses the workshop "was not a targeted training in a specific active-learning technique, but rather a hands-on application of canonical theories of learning rooted in decades of empirical findings". Question banks, clicker questions, and worksheets were developed and refined during the summer and term.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sustained instructor and TA preparation time, including a steep learning curve and substantial preparation of instructional materials
- Additional TA staffing: each AL section was assigned two TAs reflecting the logistical demands of first-time implementation
#### Constraints
- AL and TL are operational section-level labels, not perfectly standardized protocols; some TL instructors occasionally used active-learning-like activities without the structured preparation

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in introductory mechanics at a large public STEM university in Mexico

### Learning Goals
- improving passing outcomes in the gateway course Elementary Mechanics I under shared learning goals
