# 19-week learning-outcome-oriented Cybersecurity project semester with portfolio-based final assessment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The project is a 19-week, 24-ECTS second-semester BSc Cybersecurity unit. Week one covers team forming, self-chosen subjects and a study manual with LOs, deadlines and rules. Students produce nine formal products (five essays, two solution-oriented and two customer-oriented team products, a reflection) plus informal products for formative feedback, and finish with a portfolio-based assessment determining the final grade.
