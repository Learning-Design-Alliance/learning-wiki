# CCD modeling activity sequence: airplane-model starter, blueprinting, model building, presentations, synthesis

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A two-session, five-hour inquiry activity in which students first explore trade-offs among airplane models, then blueprint, build, and present a physical model of the CCD readout mechanism, closing with a synthesis talk. The schedule allots 60 minutes to blueprinting and 75 minutes to model building. The activity was "designed around this principle" of inquiry-based learning mirroring research practices of professional scientists and engineers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teams of two to three students with one or two facilitators assigned to monitor and support each team's progress
- Inexpensive materials such as beads, push-pins, ice trays, and plastic screens for model construction
#### Constraints
- It is impossible to model all aspects of a CCD using only household materials in a few hours time, so the activity targets the shift-register readout mechanism

### Target Learners
- first and second year community college engineering technology students

### Learning Goals
- understanding how a CCD readout process works
- understanding modeling in the engineering design process
