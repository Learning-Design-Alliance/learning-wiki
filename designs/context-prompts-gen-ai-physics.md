# Context prompts: instructor-designed prompts defining role, task, and boundaries of a Gen AI interaction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's central framework is the context prompt: instructor-designed prompts that establish guidelines for generative AI to follow during student interactions. The authors write that through a context prompt, instructors establish guidelines for Gen AI to follow that can guide students in exploration and reflection activities, and that it is important to provide Gen AI specific guidelines in order to scaffold it to stay on target. The prompt is prioritized at the start of the chat and maintained across the conversation.
