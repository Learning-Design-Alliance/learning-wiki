# Three-level sophistication ranking of data-based physics activities

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Data Portfolio ranks its activities into three levels of sophistication, providing a progression from small datasets to extended inquiry. The paper states the activities are "ranked into three levels of sophistication." Level 1 is introductory and deals with relatively few data points; level 2 has substantially large sets of data and requires more analysis, with masterclasses at this level; level 3 uses very large datasets, allows longer-term study, and gives a wider range of inquiry.
