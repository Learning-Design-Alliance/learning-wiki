# Seven-component scheme for forming social tolerance within the holistic university educational process

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's organizing framework is a seven-part scheme spanning the definition of social tolerance as "benevolent respect for differences", the formation goal (understanding of social differences with active negative attitude toward asocial behavior), content (familiarity with history, culture and social interaction of subcultures), interactive methods, a substitution mechanism redirecting group energy into socially significant creative activity, influence on the causes of asocial behavior, and criteria (moral values, tolerance index, conflict-related competence). It was embedded fragment-wise in courses, electives and teaching practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Implementation within the integral educational process at the university, including disciplines of choice, practical classes and teaching practice
#### Constraints
- The scheme was tested only with future teachers in pedagogical university profiles over one semester

### Target Learners
- university students training to be teachers

### Learning Goals
- social tolerance for differences within legal norms
- intolerance toward violations of moral norms
- competent behavior in conflicts
