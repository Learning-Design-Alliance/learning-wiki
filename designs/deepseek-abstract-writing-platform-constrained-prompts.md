# Custom GenAI writing platform with 30-word prompt limit and copy-paste disabled

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The learning task used a custom-built platform integrated with the DeepSeek Large Language Model in which students wrote an academic abstract for a research paper. To encourage meaningful interaction, "the platform imposed a 30-word limit per prompt and disabled copy-pasting, forcing students to synthesize AI outputs rather than passively adopting them." The full interaction log of timestamped prompts and AI responses served as the data source.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires an integrated LLM writing environment that logs timestamped prompts and responses.
#### Constraints
- The 30-word limit and copy-paste ban constrain how findings generalize to unconstrained GenAI use.

### Target Learners
- university students (M age = 20.1)
