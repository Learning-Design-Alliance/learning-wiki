# Cyclical sequence of five metaconceptual activities externalising, monitoring, and evaluating civic concepts

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The intervention organized instruction as a non-linear, cyclical sequence of five activities: analyzing concepts and their features, extracting hidden concepts from Atatürk's sayings via group posters, concept mapping with a law-making simulation, concept cartoons with concept cards, and interviews with family members. The article states: "Metaconceptual teaching activities such as poster preparation, journaling, concept maps, concept cartoons, and cards were used." Each activity was paired with group and class discussions and journal writing, and the tools were used "sequentially and cyclically to ensure continuous metacognitive engagement." Activities were mapped onto the three metaconceptual processes, e.g., cartoons maximising evaluation through cognitive conflict and role-based discussion.
