# Mapping each OD3R stage to learning domains: orientation builds affective motivation, decision trains design, do trains psychomotor skills, discuss develops communication, reflect assesses attitudes

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article assigns each OD3R stage a domain profile: orientation generates motivation (affective); decision trains designing group investigations (cognitive and affective); do requires observation, manipulative, drawing, data interpretation, and teaming skills spanning all three domains; discuss develops written reports and presentations across all domains; and review relates to scientific attitudes (affective). Figure 5 presents this mapping as the route to "Meaningful learning in Biochemistry Course".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each stage must include its paired activities, e.g. lecturer explanation and group discussion in orientation, individual report writing and lecturer feedback in discuss
#### Constraints
- The mapping is the authors' interpretive account of how the stages relate to domains, grounded in Bretz et al.'s criterion that meaningful learning integrates cognitive, affective, and psychomotor aspects
