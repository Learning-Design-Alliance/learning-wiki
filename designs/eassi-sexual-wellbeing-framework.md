# EASSi (Engagement, Assessment, Support and Signposting) framework for structuring sexual wellbeing discussions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The EASSi framework is introduced in section two of the e-learning resource "as a means of addressing key barriers to routine engagement around sexual wellbeing with patients in the clinical setting". It has four components: Engagement ensures routine discussions take place with all men; Assessment includes questions on treatment type and relationship status for tailored support; Support provides information on common sexual challenges and coping strategies; Signposting provides other support options including online self-management. The authors state it draws on models such as PLISSIT but is theoretically closer to brief behaviour change models like the 5 A's model.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered as part of the e-learning resource, with a simplified A4 poster version provided to remind healthcare professionals to use the framework in routine practice
#### Constraints

### Target Learners
- healthcare professionals working in prostate cancer care (urology, oncology, primary care)

### Learning Goals
- structuring and initiating sexual wellbeing conversations with prostate cancer patients and partners
