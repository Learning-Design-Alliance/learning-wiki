# Standardised small-group simulator session using the modified Peyton four-step process with a mastery-learning passing standard

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A reusable session design for introducing a procedural skill: each session involves a maximum of four residents, one teacher and one simulator, and teaches a normal neonatal TTE "through the modified Peyton four‐step process: demonstration, deconstruction, comprehension and performance", followed by pathology cases and an evaluated performance. Mastery learning is enforced because "The minimum passing score for validation of the training session was 12 out of 14", with additional sessions until the requirement is met. The whole session typically lasted 2.5–3.0 h.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- One EchoCom Neo simulator and one teacher per session of up to four residents
- Pre-recorded videos covering the session presentation, the simulator and Peyton's first and second steps
- A minimum passing score of 12 out of 14 with additional training sessions until met
#### Constraints
- The session served as an introduction to neonatal TTE, developing fundamental skills before bedside practice
- The simulator was set to a normal heart scenario, followed by persistent ductus arteriosus and pericardial effusion cases

### Target Learners
- Neonatology residents with all levels of training who had completed a 6-month residency or at least night shifts in neonatology

### Learning Goals
- Performing the seven standard sections of a normal neonatal transthoracic echocardiography
- Recognising visible anatomical structures in each section
