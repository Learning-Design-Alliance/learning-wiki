# Tennessee EPP report card combining public accountability with secure individual data access

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Legislation passed in 2007 requires Tennessee's State Board of Education, Department of Education, and Higher Education Commission to produce a report card for all EPPs in the state. The report card includes quantitative data required by law (licensure pass rates, placement, retention, and teacher growth data) plus qualitative data such as principal observations, delivered through an online interactive tool and downloadable PDFs. The State Board also gives EPPs secure, non-public access to their graduates' individual teacher data, including value-add scores broken out by subject, for targeted program improvements.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Ongoing collaboration with EPPs so reports include data programs want and need
- Investment in standardizing data collection and data cleaning to ensure reliable, correct information
- Varying levels of access and specificity to balance stakeholder needs while protecting individual teacher data privacy
#### Constraints
- Graduates' individual teacher data, including value-add scores, is not available to the public

### Target Learners
- EPP program leaders
- prospective teacher candidates
- K–12 principals
- policymakers

### Learning Goals
- understanding graduates' performance to make targeted program improvements and informed enrollment and hiring decisions
