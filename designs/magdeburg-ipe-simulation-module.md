# Interprofessional learning module combining lectures, simulation scenarios, role-playing and structured debriefing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A newly developed IPE module at the University Medical Center Magdeburg spanning an academic year, consisting of two lectures (135 min total) and a 135-minute small-group seminar. "The seminar involves simulation scenarios, role-playing and structured debriefing." The seminar included Belbin team-role reflection, feedback rules, an SBAR emergency handover scenario, and an interprofessional ward-round scenario with roles of physician, medical student, nurse, social worker and patient, debriefed by experienced instructors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A clearly defined curricular placement within the medical program
- Experienced instructors with expertise in medical communication to conduct debriefing and moderate the seminar
#### Constraints
- The module included only medical students, which the authors state may limit generalizability to an interprofessional education context

### Target Learners
- Third-year medical students

### Learning Goals
- Team competencies and interprofessional collaboration
- Constructive feedback skills
- Understanding professional roles in clinical decision-making
