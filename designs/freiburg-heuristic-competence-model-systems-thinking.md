# Freiburg heuristic competence model of systems thinking (four dimensions, each with four sub-capabilities)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's framework for systems thinking builds on Riess and Mischo's definition of systems thinking as "the ability to recognize, describe and model (e.g., to structure, to organize) complex aspects of reality as systems". After revising an earlier three-dimensional model used by Bräutigam (2014), the authors developed a heuristic competence model with "four dimensions, each divided into four sub-capabilities", covering declarative/conceptual systems knowledge, modeling systems, procedural problem solving with system models, and evaluation of models. It structures both the CK conceptualisation and the intervention content.
