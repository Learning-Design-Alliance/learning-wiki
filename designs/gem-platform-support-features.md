# Learning platform support features: customizable experience, multi-modal collaboration, enhanced assessment, note-taking, task scheduling, AI-assisted co-regulation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article enumerates technology supports initially identified through examination of the personas: customizable digital experience (background and font color, font size and type, haptic settings, read-aloud functions), multi-modal collaboration (text, email, voice, or video with instructors and peers via a staff-moderated virtual community space), enhanced assessment (rejection-reactivity-aware feedback and trauma-informed proctoring), note-taking with searchable, exportable annotations, task scheduling with checklist and calendar views and reminders, and AI-assisted co-regulation modeling "emotion identification and productive coping to guide learners in developing self-regulation skills."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Integration into the learning platform with learner opt-in
- Generative AI capability to sustain scalability of recommendations
#### Constraints
- These are exploratory possibilities initially identified through the personas, not yet empirically evaluated

### Target Learners
- neurodivergent and situationally limited online higher education learners

### Learning Goals
- removing learning barriers to activate pursuit of academic goals
