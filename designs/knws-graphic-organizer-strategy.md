# K-N-W-S graphic organizer strategy for mathematical word problems

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The K-N-W-S strategy is a mathematics graphic organizer comprised of four steps: identifying what students "KNOW (K) from the information stated in the problem", what information is "NOT (N) need[ed]", "WHAT (W) exactly does this problem ask me to find", and what "STRATEGY (S) or operation will I use to solve this problem". It is intended to support students in planning, organizing, and analyzing word problems in a structured, visual, and concrete way.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The organizer should be paired with strong, direct instruction of mathematical concepts
#### Constraints
- The K-N-W-S graphic organizer had not yet been explored with students with ASD prior to this study

### Target Learners
- students with disabilities solving mathematical word problems

### Learning Goals
- planning, organizing, and analyzing mathematical word problems
