# Iterative PSA refinement pattern: more questions, layered scenarios, strict multi-answer marking, and Cloze scaffolding

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Across successive offerings the teaching team applied a recurring set of refinements: increasing the number of questions, expanding scenario complexity, greater use of parameterised questions, multi-answer MCQs with strict marking, widespread Cloze formats, and structural consistency with workshop content. The article reports that multi-answer MCQs with strict marking "Required confident selection of all correct answers only; reduced guessing", and that Cloze formats integrated "multi-step reasoning within a single task to assess problem-solving processes". These changes were applied across all units using the PSA model, not tied to a specific subject or level.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Internal teaching team reviews and performance outcome data to guide each refinement cycle
- Balance between increased question numbers and expected completion times
#### Constraints
- Changes were designed to maintain rigour while promoting fairness, authenticity, and alignment with learning goals; the article reports no controlled evaluation of individual refinements

### Target Learners
- Tertiary computing students in large-scale core units

### Learning Goals
- Critical thinking, multi-step problem-solving processes, and applied reasoning aligned with unit learning outcomes
