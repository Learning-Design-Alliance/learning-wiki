# Trial design: two consecutive RCT experiments with 68 students each, videotaped role-plays, and blinded independent competence ratings

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The protocol specifies a single-center RCT in which "In each experiment, 68 bachelor's and master's students of psychology will be randomly assigned to the experimental and control groups." Each student completes three 20-minute role-plays (baseline, post, 3-month follow-up), all videotaped. Two independent raters, blinded to conditions and trained for 10 hours, assess competencies via the German adaptation of the Cognitive Therapy Scale and a self-developed therapeutic skills checklist. Analyses use intention-to-treat ANOVAs/ANCOVAs with Bonferroni correction; power analysis assumes d = 0.60, giving an overall sample of 136.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Random 1:1 allocation by computer-generated numbers
- Raters blinded to experimental conditions and independent of the study team
- Videotaped role-plays rated on a standardized competence scale
#### Constraints
- Educational low-risk trial with no formal trial steering or data monitoring committee
- External validity lower than in patient studies because SPs approximate but do not replace clinical encounters

### Target Learners
- bachelor's and master's psychology students

### Learning Goals
- cognitive behavioral therapy competencies for depression treatment (behavioral activation and cognitive interventions)
