# Circular multi-directional stitching training module with a GESEA-based scoring rubric and NASA-TLX workload measure

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study used a specially designed circular suturing training module with "four incisions in different directions on a slightly harder sponge material" and marked needle entry/exit points 0.5 cm in diameter, 0.75 cm from the incision. Performance was scored with a rubric slightly modified from the GESEA system covering needle insertion accuracy, knotting stability, tissue integrity and tissue tightness (0–2 points each), plus suture time and thread length; perceived workload was measured with the six-domain NASA-TLX on a 20-point scale.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Two experienced laparoscopic surgeons scoring blinded, muted, de-identified operation videos to reduce bias
#### Constraints
- Scoring criteria derive from the authors' own multi-directional stitching study, slightly modified from GESEA

### Target Learners
- junior surgical residents in skills training

### Learning Goals
- objective assessment of laparoscopic suturing quality and cognitive workload
