## Description
The article recommends that public-engagement installations be built for mobility and scientific authenticity. The escape room "must be portable, easy to assemble, and manageable by only one or two operators," with commercially available materials and content "closely tied to real experiments to ensure scientific relevance and authenticity." This follows the precedent of HEPscape!, whose portability, low-cost replicable materials, and multi-language adaptability made it well-suited for science fairs and outreach events.
