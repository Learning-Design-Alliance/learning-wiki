# Noun Phrase Accessibility Hierarchy (NPAH) as predictor of relative clause acquisition difficulty

## Description
The NPAH is a typological markedness hierarchy ordering relative clause types by accessibility of the relative pronoun's function. The article reports that "the implicational accessibility ordering continues as follows: DIRECT OBJECT, then INDIRECT OBJECT, then OBJECT OF A PREPOSITION, then POSSESSIVE, and finally OBJECT OF A COMPARISON", with SUBJECT relativization universally easiest. It was used to select the instructional sequence and to interpret acquisitional findings from FLA and SLA studies.
