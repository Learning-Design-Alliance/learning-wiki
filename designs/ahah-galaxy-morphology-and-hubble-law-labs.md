# Two AHaH lab designs: galaxy morphology inspection and Hubble's Law data plotting

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's educational materials comprise two labs. In the galaxy morphology lab, "Students use the AHaH tool to 'fly' through the universe and inspect the morphology of galaxies", classify them by the Hubble Classification Scheme, and connect features to stellar populations. In the Hubble's Law lab, students use the built-in pop-up to learn each galaxy's redshift and comoving radial distance DR, plot their data points, and calculate H0 from the slope of their graphs. Stated learning outcomes include identifying galaxies by appearance, understanding redshift, and gaining familiarity with the expansion of the universe.
