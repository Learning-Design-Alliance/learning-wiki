# Project-based learning as a constructivist, inquiry-driven approach with three organizing principles

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article frames project-based learning as "an inquiry driven, student-centred approach that challenges learners to tackle authentic problems and produce concrete outcomes", resting on constructivist principles it enumerates as context-specific learning, active involvement, and social construction of understanding. Students move cyclically through problem identification, research, data collection, analysis, strategy development, and product creation. The article applies this framework to bridge abstract quantum computing concepts and a real-world interdisciplinary task.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each phase must be tightly integrated with coursework and include coordinated individual, group, and classroom activities
#### Constraints
- The article notes many learners still cling to faulty reasoning primitives after traditional lecture-based instruction, motivating the approach for quantum topics

### Target Learners
- beginners without a background in quantum mechanics in introductory quantum computing courses

### Learning Goals
- grasping complex abstract concepts
- cultivating positive attitudes toward the subject matter
- sharpening reasoning and critical-thinking skills
