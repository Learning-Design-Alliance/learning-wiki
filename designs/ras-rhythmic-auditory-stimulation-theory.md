# Rhythmic Auditory Stimulation (RAS) as the theoretical basis for music and movement interventions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article defines RAS as "a neurological technique using the physiological effects of auditory rhythm on the motor system to improve the control of movement in rehabilitation and therapy". It grounds the intervention: rhythmic auditory cuing provides "a mechanism for planning movements", and the authors state auditory rhythm "increases the excitability of motor neurons in the spinal cord" and activates motor areas including the SMA, premotor cortex, basal ganglia and cerebellum. The 8-week program was designed on this theory.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
