# AstroDance multimedia dance-theater performance on gravitational-wave astrophysics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
AstroDance is an original dance and theatrical multimedia performance communicating gravitational-wave astrophysics, created by a collaboration of astrophysicists, theater artists, educators, and scientists at RIT for "diverse audiences such as deaf and hard-of-hearing students". It paired scientific visualizations drawn from the RIT group's numerical modeling of gravitational-wave sources with dance, music, and narration in spoken English and American Sign Language, and was performed primarily for secondary and post-secondary audiences.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Collaboration among researchers, scientists, artists, and educators; visual imagery obtained from the research group's modeling and simulation of gravitational-wave sources was a cornerstone of the project
#### Constraints
- The project website evaluation was planned but not completed due to unforeseen delays in the website's development
- Because the performance was consistent across venues, the individual effects of dance, signed narration, or scientific visualizations cannot be isolated

### Target Learners
- secondary and post-secondary students
- deaf and hard-of-hearing audiences
- members of the general public

### Learning Goals
- gravitational-wave astronomy and gravitational physics concepts, including black holes, neutron stars, and supernovae
