# Concept Focused Discussion Environment (CFDE)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
CFDE is an LMS-integrated discussion environment inspired by the wiki connection metaphor: significant concepts in content texts appear as dark, underlined links, and clicking a concept opens a discussion frame that slides from the right side of the same screen rather than moving students to a separate module. The article built the LMS with Adobe Flash and the discussion infrastructure with PHP-based Vbulletin on a Linux host. It was compared against a traditional threaded discussion environment over six weeks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content texts must mark concepts directly related to the subject and significant to learning the content as links, and significant concepts are also listed under the content text
#### Constraints
- The study reports the environment as an independent module alternative to traditional hierarchical threaded discussions within LMSs; effectiveness evidence comes from one six-week university course

### Target Learners
- undergraduate education faculty students

### Learning Goals
- concept-focused discussion of course content in an online blended learning environment
