# MICA: the first professional scientific organization based exclusively in virtual worlds

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Meta-Institute for Computational Astrophysics (MICA) was, to the authors' knowledge, "the first professional scientific organization based exclusively in virtual worlds (VWs)", running from early 2008 to June 2012 mainly in Second Life and OpenSimulator. Its charter goals included exploring VW/VR technologies for professional astrophysics research, providing social networking venues for scientific collaboration, and using VWs for education and public outreach. It served as a testbed and gateway for scholars new to VWs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A persistent virtual-world platform with ready audiences; MICA used Second Life and later OpenSimulator as its platforms
#### Constraints
- The institute lasted from early 2008 to June 2012 and did not succeed in engaging a broader segment of the astrophysics community in adoption

### Target Learners
- professional astrophysicists
- amateur astronomers
- university students
- general public science enthusiasts
