# Our Place in the Universe: an interdisciplinary common core course using astronomy history to teach rational thinking

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A non-discipline-based common core course developed by the author in 2010 and taught eight times to roughly 100 students per year from all HKU faculties. It uses "the historical development of astronomy to illustrate the process of rational reasoning" and its effects on philosophy, religion and society. Students evaluate competing theories' pros and cons rather than receiving an axiomatic presentation, and computer labs and a mobile app support simulated observations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students from any faculty, no previous physics or astronomy training required
- Suitable teachers, as not all teachers are equipped to teach interdisciplinary courses
#### Constraints
- More expensive and time-consuming to develop and run than a typical introductory course

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students from all faculties, non-science and science majors

### Learning Goals
- understanding the scientific process
- rational reasoning
- independent, logical and analytical thinking
