## Description
The article adopts the inquiry-based learning strategy (Aprendizaje Basado en Indagación, ABI) as its organizing framework, defined as an educational strategy based on scientific methods in which students formulate a hypothesis and explore their answer through experiments, with permanent student participation emphasized. The article states the classroom proposal "debe cumplir cinco fases para evaluar, de forma adecuada, el proceso de enseñanza -aprendizaje, las cuales son: orientación, conceptualización, investigación, conclusión y discusión", following Pedaste et al.
