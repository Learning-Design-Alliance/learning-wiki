# Modeling Instruction studio classroom: small classes, groups of three, and whiteboard board meetings

## Description
The Modeling Instruction learning environment at FIU is a studio-format class of about 30 students with integrated lab and lecture time and minimal lecturing. "In a Modeling Instruction class, the students typically work on an activity in groups of three students and then present their results on a small portable whiteboard to the whole class." In these board meetings students discuss their whiteboards while the instructor facilitates discussion aimed at class consensus, building a view of physics as a coherent system of knowledge where models are subject to change as new data become available.
