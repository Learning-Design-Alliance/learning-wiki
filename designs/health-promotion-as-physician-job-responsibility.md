# Make patient counseling and preventive information dissemination explicit job responsibilities of primary care physicians

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article observes that health promotion "has never been incorporated in the duties or job responsibility of physician during primary care delivery services in India", leading to its lay-away beside curative practice. It recommends "introducing patient counseling or information dissemination on preventive aspects of prevalent diseases, as job responsibilities of primary care physicians", since primary care providers are the first point of contact with the health system.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Formal assignment of preventive counseling and information dissemination duties within primary care physician job descriptions
#### Constraints
- The article notes physicians may perceive their role as sole point of curative care with immediate outcomes, and preventive service outcomes are difficult to measure, with no official, patient, peer or community recognition or financial incentive attached

### Target Learners
- primary care physicians in government health systems

### Learning Goals
- preventive counseling on prevalent diseases
- health information dissemination
