# A 29-hour inter-professional PBL course pattern for short in-service training, delivered over four consecutive weeks

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The curriculum is a 29-hour course delivered in four days over four consecutive weeks, giving priority to tutorial groups based on real problems and practical activities including simulations and role plays (36% and 35% of time), with supervised clinical practice (16%), three introductory lectures (8%) and miscellaneous activities (6%). It is "vertically and horizontally integrated, i.e., across time and across disciplines, with a progression from simple to complex", with ethics, communication and woman-centred clinical management running throughout as pillars. Groups are capped at 24 participants per course, 8 per tutorial group and 4 per practical session to stimulate interaction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained PBL tutors facilitating parallel tutorial groups
- Manuals for tutors and participants provided at the beginning of the course
- Maximum 24 participants per course with 8 per tutorial group and 4 per practical session
#### Constraints
- It was difficult to schedule courses keeping professionals away from clinical duties for four days and to make locations with enough rooms available

### Target Learners
- maternal and child health professionals: pediatricians, gynecologists, midwives, nurses and other professionals in hospitals and community services

### Learning Goals
- protection, promotion and support of breastfeeding, including physiology, difficulties and support strategies, communication, ethics and continuity of care
