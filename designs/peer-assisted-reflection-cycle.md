# Peer-Assisted Reflection (PAR): a four-step weekly cycle of independent work, self-reflection, peer feedback exchange, and revision

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
PAR is a formative-assessment-based approach in which students use peer feedback to improve work on open-ended homework problems. Students are required to: (1) complete a homework problem outside of class, (2) reflect on their solution attempt by indicating confidence, (3) trade their initial attempt with a peer and exchange feedback during class, and (4) revise their work outside of class. At the end of each week-long cycle students submit a packet containing their initial attempt, reflection, peer feedback, and revised work.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students submit a packet containing their initial attempt, reflection, peer feedback, and revised work at the end of each week-long cycle
#### Constraints
- The peer feedback portion of the cycle typically takes around 10 minutes of class time; all other activities are completed outside of class

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics students
- pre-service physics teachers

### Learning Goals
- improving solutions to open-ended problems
- communication, collaboration, and persistence
