# Recruit para-teachers from the local community with at least 12th-grade education and a qualification exam

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The protocol specifies hiring para-teachers drawn from the community: "We will aim to hire only para-teachers who have completed the 12th grade, pass an Effective Intervention qualification exam, and reside or choose to reside in that particular village." Where no candidate has at least 12th-grade education, the team selects someone who passes the exam with the most suitable combination of proximity and schooling level, such as a 9th-grade-educated resident of a neighboring village. This keeps instructors local while setting a minimum education and tested qualification bar.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Para-teachers must pass an Effective Intervention qualification exam and be selected in consultation with community members
#### Constraints
- In the absence of a 12th-grade-educated local candidate, a lower-schooled but exam-passing candidate from a neighboring village may be chosen

### Target Learners
- Children in the target village communities

### Learning Goals
- Delivery of remedial literacy and numeracy lessons by trusted, qualified local instructors
