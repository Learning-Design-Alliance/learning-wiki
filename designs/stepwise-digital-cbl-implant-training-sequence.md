# Stepwise digital-CBL training sequence: theory, case analysis, digital planning, then model-based operation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's instructional pattern arranges implant training as a graduated sequence in which digital tools are introduced early and reused throughout. As printed, "The training process was arranged step by step, starting with digitally assisted theoretical learning, followed by real case analysis, digital planning on the navigation platform, and finally model-based operation." The first two hours of the course were taught beside the chair using a typical case with the navigation system's digital design module, showing three-dimensional reconstructed anatomy and implant plan design, after which trainees could independently practice on the digital platform and complete navigation-assisted and free-hand model procedures.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to an implant navigation platform with digital planning and simulation modules, a case database with imaging and surgical data, and models for scanning and free-hand practice
#### Constraints
- The article states digital equipment is relatively expensive and limited in number, so when student numbers are large, trainees need to make appointments for practice time

### Target Learners
- oral and maxillofacial surgery specialists without prior implantology training

### Learning Goals
- theoretical implantology knowledge
- procedural skills in implant placement
- clinical case analysis and treatment planning
