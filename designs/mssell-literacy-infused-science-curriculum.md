# MSSELL literacy-infused science curriculum and bi-weekly professional learning for sixth-grade treatment teachers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Project MSSELL (DRL-0822343) provided treatment teachers ongoing bi-weekly, 90-minute, face-to-face professional learning sessions for one year, covering upcoming lessons and ESL strategies in researcher-developed, scripted lessons. Students received "85 minutes of daily literacy-infused science instruction" following a highly-structured curriculum aligned to state and national science and reading standards, embedding expository text features, pre-taught academic vocabulary, pronunciation and decoding, hands-on 5E activities, questioning, scaffolding, cooperative grouping, and technology integration.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Ongoing bi-weekly professional learning sessions taught by research coordinators specializing in science and second language development
#### Constraints
- The treatment curriculum replaced the district curriculum; randomization was at the school level and quasi-experimental at the student level in accordance with Texas law

### Target Learners
- Sixth-grade English language learners and economically-challenged students in a large urban district in southeast Texas

### Learning Goals
- Science content knowledge, academic English language, and literacy development
