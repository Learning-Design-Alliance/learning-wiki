# Finnish HEMS new-physician onboarding programme of skill stations, full-scale simulations and senior on-scene support

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
New Finnish HEMS physicians complete a structured onboarding period before independent practice. The article states: "Before independent operative practice, new physicians undergo one to two months of training, including skill stations, full-scale simulations, and on-scene senior physician support." Selection also includes a suitability assessment testing psychological capacity and interpersonal skills plus interviews, and recruits are consultants or final-year anaesthesiology residents.
