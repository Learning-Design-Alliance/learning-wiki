# Interprofessional student-faculty refugee clinic as an academic-community partnership

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A student-faculty collaborative clinic in a large US city whose mission is to "provide free, culturally appropriate medical and dental care to refugees in a welcoming environment." Established in 2011 by the Schools of Nursing, Dentistry and Medicine, it has served as a healthcare safety net for more than ten years, "evaluating and treating around 500 medical and 400 dental patients each year." Two community partners support it: a non-profit refugee social services organization supplying interpreters and navigation help, and a church hosting weekly Wednesday evening sessions within walking distance of refugee housing.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Community partners providing space, social services and interpreters
- Faculty from all three schools providing patient care supervision, research mentorship and community collaboration
#### Constraints
- Refugees lose Medicaid eligibility after an eight-month grace period, which the clinic addresses as a safety net

### Target Learners
- student volunteers from nursing, medicine and dentistry
- undergraduate nursing students on population health rotations

### Learning Goals
- interprofessional teamwork
- cultural humility
- patient advocacy
- compassionate care
