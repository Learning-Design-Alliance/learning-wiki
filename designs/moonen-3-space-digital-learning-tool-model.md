# Moonen's 3-Space digital learning tool model as the design framework for Medifeeding

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article adopted "Moonen’s 3-Space digital learning tool model" as the guiding design framework for Medifeeding, structuring development into three areas. The pedagogical content area took Falchikov's peer feedback marking (PFM) model as its pedagogical element and structured the faculty's learning guides accordingly; the interaction area combined classical features (emojis, likes, text and voice comments) with the innovative feature of video comments; and the technological infrastructure area used "a rapid-prototyping, iterative software-development approach" culminating in release on both app stores.
