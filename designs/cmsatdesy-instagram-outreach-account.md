# CMSatDESY Instagram account for crowd-sourced particle physics outreach

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The @CMSatDESY Instagram account is an outreach platform for the DESY-based CMS group, launched in November 2023. The article states it "highlightstheworkofyoungscientists" and disseminates particle physics content, with short videos explaining basic concepts such as the LHC, cosmic rays and the photoelectric effect, plus "life at the lab" videos and social aspects of lab life. Multiple contributors crowd-source content while a three-person team organizes, produces, schedules and manages the account.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A three-member team (one staff member, one postdoc, one PhD student) that searches for volunteers, collaborates on content, maintains a posting schedule, and manages the account, alongside crowd-sourced contributions from the group
#### Constraints
- The project depends on volunteers from within the group and on team members doing outreach work while also working full time in scientific activities

### Target Learners
- science-interested general public aged 18-34
- final-year bachelor's and master's students, recent graduates, and early career researchers

### Learning Goals
- public understanding of particle physics and day-to-day research
- career-relevant science communication skills for early career researchers

### Claims

- [More than 70% of CMSatDESY followers fall within the 18-34 age range, indicating the target audience is reached](../claims/cmsatdesy-followers-18-34-majority.md) [+W]
- [Typical institute outreach activities (bars, schools, TV, newspapers) do not reach the young adult to mid-30s age group](../claims/typical-outreach-misses-young-adults.md) [+W]
