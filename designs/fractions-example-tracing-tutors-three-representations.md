# Fractions example-tracing tutors with three graphical representations

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A set of example-tracing tutors for fractions learning created with the Cognitive Tutor Authoring Tools (CTAT), covering fraction identification, fractions as division, equivalent fractions, ordering fractions, and fraction addition. The tutors used three graphical representations — pie charts (part-whole), numberlines (measurement), and sets (ratio) — presented one at a time. Students manipulated graphical representations before solving symbolically, received error feedback and three-level hints, and were prompted to self-explain by selecting answers from a drop-down menu.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students never worked with the set representation first, as it is the representation students are least familiar with
#### Constraints
- One graphical representation was presented at a time; problems involving multiple graphical representations simultaneously were not included in this study

### Target Learners
- 5th- and 6th-grade students

### Learning Goals
- Fraction identification, fractions as division, equivalent fractions, ordering fractions, fraction addition
