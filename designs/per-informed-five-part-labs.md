# Locally written PER-informed laboratories with a five-part structure

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The locally written PER-informed labs used in the reformed ABII and CBII courses "consisted of five parts: Objectives, Overview, Explorations, Investigations and Summary". Explorations were qualitative measurements or thought experiments targeting persistent conceptual difficulties; Investigations involved quantitative measurement, data-taking, graphing and interpretation; the Summary asked students to focus on and summarize a particular part of the lab. Labs required no formal write-up but included laboratory homework, and were designed for interactive engagement during the lab.
