# Progressive blended case-based teaching pattern: pre-class preparation, stepwise in-class case discussions, and post-class consolidation via a digital platform

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A three-tier instructional pattern combining online pre-class materials, in-class interactive case discussion, and post-class personalized review, delivered through the Rain Classroom platform. The in-class discussions progress through "Keyword extraction", "Standardized cases", and "Inquiry-based thinking", moving students from first exposure to real clinical scenarios toward student-to-student and teacher-to-student debate. The article implements it in an integrated urology module for fifth-year medical students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors prepare pre-class lecture videos, reference papers, and guidelines distributed via the platform
- A digital platform that supports in-class questioning, answer aggregation, and automated post-class review推送 such as Rain Classroom
- Instructors of similar experience across comparison groups and equivalent instructional time
#### Constraints
- Evaluated over a 4-week single-institution module; the authors note instructor styles, classroom dynamics, and peer interactions were not fully standardized

### Target Learners
- fifth-year clinical medical students

### Learning Goals
- clinical knowledge application and diagnostic reasoning
- communication, teamwork, and other CanMEDS-based clinical competencies
