# Physics in the Public Square: a station-based outreach event of 12 low-cost experiments designed and presented by pre-service physics teachers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An outreach activity held at Praça da Cultura in Imperatriz, Maranhão on November 22, 2025, in which undergraduate students from Physics I, III and IV designed and exhibited didactic experiments at stations with tables and tents, allowing free visitor circulation. As the article states, "In total, 12 experiments were developed and exhibited, covering topics in classical mechanics (Phy sics I), electromagnetism (Physics III), and optics and electromagnetic waves (Physics IV)." Students built the artifacts from low-cost materials and rehearsed accessible explanations for a lay audience.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pre-service teachers organized into groups with assigned responsibilities, periodic planning meetings, construction of artifacts, and peer rehearsal sessions to refine accessible explanations
#### Constraints
- The event ran in a non-formal public space with voluntary participation and a heterogeneous audience with no prior connection to the content

### Target Learners
- pre-service physics teachers in the Physics Teacher Education Program at UEMASUL
- heterogeneous community visitors including children, adolescents, and adults

### Learning Goals
- communicating fundamental physics concepts to lay audiences
- integrating theory, practice, and social responsibility in teacher education
