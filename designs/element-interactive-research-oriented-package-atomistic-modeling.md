# Interactive research-oriented teaching package for "Atomistic Materials Modeling": group worksheets, computer exercises and a voluntary research-proposal sketch with bonus points

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A package of three interactive 90-minute lecture units plus about 10% of self-study time replaces traditional lecturing: students work through worksheets in groups of four and apply a computer-based method in pairs in tutorial-style computer exercises, with lecturers acting as learning companions rather than transmitting knowledge. In self-study, small groups of up to four may voluntarily sketch a research proposal on a self-chosen topic and earn bonus points by presenting it, worth "die maximal 10 % der bei der Klausur erreichbaren Punkte ausmachen". The authors state the elements are broadly usable and see "keine Einschr¨ankung auf bestimmte Vorlesungen oder Fachrichtungen".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Preparation of didactically valuable worksheet and computer-exercise tasks is time-consuming; ideally one instructor per one to two small groups should be present during the interactive units.
- Existing tutorials (from the SFB 986 graduate programme) can keep preparation effort bounded; the original materials took several weeks to produce.
#### Constraints
- Due to time constraints the units treat only one of the lecture-relevant methods, so effects are expected mainly for that specific method.
- The proposal sketch is deliberately not a fully worked-out proposal, and the allotted time is about one work day (roughly 10% of self-study time) per student.

### Target Learners
- first-semester master's students in materials science

### Learning Goals
- understanding concepts and limitations of atomistic materials modeling methods
- applying modeling methods to concrete problems
- gaining insight into research processes and proposal writing
