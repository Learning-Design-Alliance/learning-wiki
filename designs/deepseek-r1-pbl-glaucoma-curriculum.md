# DeepSeek-R1-assisted PBL curriculum for glaucoma clerkship

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A two-week glaucoma teaching intervention in which fourth-year students used the DeepSeek-R1 model within PBL: preclass AI-supported case preparation, simulated patient consultations, in-class group discussion of AI-generated diagnostic results, and collaborative summary writing. The same instructor and textbook were used for both groups, and "The two groups received the same amount of instruction time." Faculty reviewed AI content before class.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- On-premises or institutional access to the DeepSeek model
- Faculty review of AI-generated content before classroom use
#### Constraints
- Results should not be generalized to other ophthalmic subspecialties without further validation, per the article

### Target Learners
- fourth-year undergraduate medical students in ophthalmology clerkship

### Learning Goals
- glaucoma clinical presentation, diagnosis and treatment
- history-taking and physical examination skills
