# Moodle-based Problem-Solving Assessment (PSA) model

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The PSA model is an automated, Moodle-delivered assessment design built from "parameterised, scenario-based tasks" scheduled at Weeks 5, 9, and 12 and embedded in weekly workshops. Students receive "equivalent but unique versions of each task with varied inputs, values, or sequences", delivered under supervision with full-screen sharing and camera use. Question types include numerical calculated, single- and multi-answer MCQs, Cloze embedded answers, and match/select formats, with automated marking using partial scoring rules. The model aims to support authenticity, integrity, and cognitive depth while minimising marking overhead.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Moodle question banks with parameterised variables, timed delivery, and randomisation
- Weekly workshop sessions that provide practice problems similar to PSA tasks
- Institutional support for large-scale Zoom sessions and stable Moodle/Zoom performance
#### Constraints
- Some platform limitations, e.g., lack of native SEB integration with Zoom
- Time needed to develop high-quality question banks
- Generalisability is context-aware; cultural attitudes, prior active-learning exposure, and language proficiency may influence engagement

### Target Learners
- Large-scale tertiary computing unit cohorts (systems and networks units)

### Learning Goals
- Applied problem-solving in areas such as operating systems, computer architecture, and networks, including threshold concepts and higher-order reasoning
