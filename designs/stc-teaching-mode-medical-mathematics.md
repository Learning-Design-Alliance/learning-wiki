# STC teaching mode: self-directed learning, traditional teaching, and cross-collaborative learning for medical advanced mathematics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The STC teaching mode, formally proposed by the authors' group in 2016, combines three learning components: “S refers to the self-directed learning”, “T refers to the traditional teaching”, and “C refers to cross-collaborative learning”. It is delivered through a smart classroom integrating “student side, teacher side and interconnected cloud side”, intended to free teaching from fixed class schedules and align content with professional needs. The article positions it as the proposed solution to the surveyed problems of content-profession disconnect and unrealized educational-model innovation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Internet-based quality learning resources for the self-directed learning component
- a smart classroom infrastructure integrating student, teacher and cloud sides
- coordination across multiple university departments for implementation
#### Constraints
- The article reports no controlled evaluation of the mode's learning effects; support consists of the authors' own teaching awards and student outcomes
- data come from China only, which the authors state may lead to biased results

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical university students in majors offering advanced mathematics

### Learning Goals
- mathematical operation and data analysis ability
- applying mathematical methods and tools to solve practical problems in medicine
