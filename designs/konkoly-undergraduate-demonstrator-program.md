# Konkoly undergraduate demonstrator program: paid, supervised research and telescope training for university students

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Konkoly Observatory demonstrator program, launched in 2017, recruits university students for financially supported supervised research work and observing duties at the Piszkéstető Mountain Station. The first call included "the learning of telescope operation through regular trips to the M´atra mountains as a compulsory element, in addition to participating in research work." Between 2017 and 2022 it supported 37 students across three tracks: observational astronomy (29), numerical astrophysics (four), and engineering and instrument development (four), with calls synchronized to the academic year for 5 or 11 months.
