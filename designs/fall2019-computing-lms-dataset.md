# Fall 2019 observational dataset of 1651 computing-major students with Blackboard LMS logs and SIS records

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An observational dataset combining Blackboard LMS interaction logs with Student Information System records for undergraduate computing majors, in which "A total of 1651 students were enrolled in these two departments in the Fall 2019 semester." The Blackboard system delivered course material, assessment, and grading; the study was IRB-approved and student demographic and personal information were anonymized following GDPR standards.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Blackboard LMS logs plus a standalone Student Information System capturing demographics and cumulative GPA
#### Constraints
- Restricted to Information Systems and Computer Science undergraduates at one large public US university during Fall 2019.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate Information Systems and Computer Science students

### Learning Goals
- modeling student LMS behavior to understand and support academic performance
