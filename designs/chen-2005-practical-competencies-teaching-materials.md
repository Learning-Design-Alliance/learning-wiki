# Chen (2005) practical competencies-oriented teaching materials and local cases for business programs

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A set of business-program teaching materials developed by Chen (2005) from practical competencies identified for technical-university graduates. The former study "invited 30 experienced supervisors and personnel from the business circle to participate in three Delphi questionnaire surveys", yielding "the 70 professional and practical competencies needed by graduates of the business program", which "had transformed the competencies into 15 courses". In this study the human resource management unit's materials and local cases were taught to the experimental group for three weeks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delphi input from experienced business supervisors and personnel to identify the competencies the materials target
#### Constraints
- In this study the materials were tested on only one subject (Human Resource Management), which the author says limits inference

### Target Learners
- freshmen in four-year technical universities in Taiwan

### Learning Goals
- professional and practical competencies needed by business-program graduates in the workplace
