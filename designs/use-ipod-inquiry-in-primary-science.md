# Use iPod inquiry teaching for primary school science units

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
On the basis of the study's results, the article concludes that the iPod inquiry teaching method is suitable for primary school science teaching: it enhances learning motives, strengthens thinking skills, deepens peer discussions, and promotes construction of scientific concepts, while its diversified activities stimulate enthusiasm for exploration and offer instant communication and mutual feedback. The recommendation rests on the two ANCOVA findings in this single quasi-experiment.
