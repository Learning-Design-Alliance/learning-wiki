# AI-focused learning activities in the first-year undergraduate business curriculum

## Description
The instructional element evaluated in this case study is a set of newly implemented AI-focused learning activities introduced into the first-year undergraduate curriculum of a UK business school. The article states the school "introduced GenAI into its curriculum for first-year undergraduates" and that a mixed-methods survey of 149 students "gathered feedback on newly implemented AI-focused learning activities". Student feedback on these activities informed the resulting framework.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Activities should address the areas students flagged: business applications of AI, ethical considerations, future advancements, and clarity on avoiding misconduct
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only with first-year undergraduates at one UK business school

- first-year undergraduate business students
