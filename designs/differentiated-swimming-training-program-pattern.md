# Differentiated swimming-training program pattern: specialization-specific exercise sets with assistive equipment, per-group plans and varied exercise dosage

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A lesson-level instructional pattern in which swimming instruction is differentiated by students' long-term sport specialization and initial ability. Distinctive features include "The use of fins, 'a net', auxiliary belts, partner employment assistance" for developing swimming equipment skills, increased practice volume through fins, specialization-specific foot and hand exercises, high-density lessons with optimal rest pauses, and a separate plan of educational material for each group with differing exercise volume, intensity, rest intervals and repetitions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Determination and systematization of basic common errors committed by athletes of different sports
- Preparation of sets of exercises tailored to individual and group characteristics caused by long-term specialization
#### Constraints
- Student training is limited to the number of allocated hours for swimming training

### Target Learners
- bachelor students of physical culture and sport of various specializations

### Learning Goals
- mastering swimming techniques (front crawl, back crawl, breaststroke) and navigation methods
