# Compendium of qualitative quantum-mechanics teaching examples

## Description
A compendium of "tricks of the trade" for teaching qualitative methods in quantum mechanics, a traditionally non-intuitive subject. It covers dimensional estimates (electron velocity, Bohr radius, Landau levels), dimensional reduction of differential equations, De Broglie quantization (rigid rotator, hydrogen atom, potential box, harmonic oscillator, triangular well), De Broglie tunneling, the uncertainty principle (zero-point energy, diffraction, quantum conductance), spin-orbit interaction, transition rates, and QED estimates. The material is "accessible to a variety of college and grad students in science and engineering, as guided by qualified instructors."
