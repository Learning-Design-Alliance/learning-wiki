# Kreyol-medium math and science intervention with nonequivalent control group design and switch replication

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The study's intervention taught the same math and science concepts to two student groups, one in Kreyol and one in French, at an all-female private school in Haiti with 246 girls in the target population. It used a quasi-experimental nonequivalent control group design with pre-test and post-test, consecutive non-random sampling, and a switching replication method in which French-condition participants also experienced the Kreyol condition in consecutive interventions. Data included achievement tests, a student perception questionnaire, and group interviews.
