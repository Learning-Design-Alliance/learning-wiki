# Interactive individualized lesson structure combining reading, letter/word work, and story writing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Each Reading Recovery lesson follows a recurring structure: "reading familiar or novel stories, manipulating letters and words, and writing and assembling stories." Lessons are "interactive between the teacher and student, with the teacher carefully monitoring each student's reading behavior," and teachers tailor instruction to accommodate each student's needs. This pattern combines text reading, orthographic work, and composing within a single one-on-one session, with continuous teacher observation guiding what each lesson contains.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A trained teacher who carefully monitors each student's reading behavior and tailors lessons to individual needs
#### Constraints
- Implemented as one-on-one pull-out tutoring for first-grade struggling readers, 30 minutes daily over 12–20 weeks

### Target Learners
- First-grade students struggling in reading and writing

### Learning Goals
- Reading and writing strategies
- Literacy skills
