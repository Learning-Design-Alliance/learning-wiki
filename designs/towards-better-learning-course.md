# The 'Towards better learning' course: supervised group meetings with reflective assignments empowering students to analyse their study situation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A course at Chalmers University of Technology, developed from the study's results, built on the conjecture that students "through articulation and reflection around their learning can become more aware about their own learning". It ran as meetings of groups of 6 to 8 students with a supervisor during the first semester, revolving around assignments such as the study diary and an interview with a teacher, with supervisor feedback on reflective essays similar to peer review. Its essence is empowerment of students to analyse and be creative in being students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assignments take their starting point in observations of students' own study situations, and essays are expected to use academic argumentation from observation through reasoning to conclusion.
#### Constraints
- The article reports the course's design and rationale, not an evaluation of its effects.

### Target Learners
- first-semester physics engineering students

### Learning Goals
- awareness of one's own learning
- taking control of one's study situation
