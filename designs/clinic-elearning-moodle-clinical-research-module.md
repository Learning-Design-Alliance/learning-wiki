# CLINIC E-LEARNING: a Moodle-based learning management system for a 9-hour clinical research module

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
CLINIC E-LEARNING is a learning management system developed on "Moodle platform Version 3.2" to deliver a 9-hour module on the fundamentals of clinical research to final-year Pharm D students. "CLINIC E-LEARNING contained five chapters as described above, and each chapter had pre-and post-test, case study questions, case study for references, important links and files, simulated forms, assignments and feedback forms." Access was restricted to eligible participants via login id and password, and the same audiovisual slides served the DT, WEL and BL arms.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students sign up and log in to the web page to access the learning materials
- Contents in each chapter verified by experts from relevant areas
#### Constraints
- Additional case studies and simulated forms were available only to WEL students; BL students could not access them on the website
- The study did not assess how many times students logged in or how long they spent viewing materials

### Target Learners
- final year Pharm D students

### Learning Goals
- fundamentals of clinical research, including starting a clinical trial, randomization, informed consent, case report forms and adverse drug reaction reporting
