# Inquiry-based integrated thematic instructional model

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article defines this model, per Regulation of Ministry of Education and Culture No. 57 of 2014, as "a learning model that combines various competencies of various subjects in a variety of themes to provide a meaningful experience to the students", while highlighting students' critical and analytical thinking to seek answers or solve problems. It merges inquiry strategy with thematic learning, with the teacher acting as facilitator and motivator. The article lists eleven characteristics including child-centeredness, direct experiences, flexibility, fun learning while playing, and self-study skill improvement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires teachers to act as facilitators and motivators with available or enriched facilities, infrastructure, tools/media and resources
#### Constraints
- The article states the inquiry-based model complicates activity control and student performance, and lesson-plan preparation is more difficult since it does not match students' learning habits and must fit available time

### Target Learners
- Primary school students (grades 1-3) in Indonesia

### Learning Goals
- Character values (spiritual and social attitudes)
- Integrated knowledge across subject areas
- Critical and analytical thinking
