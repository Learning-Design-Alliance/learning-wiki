# Algoritmos-Rayuela: the flexible problem-solving algorithm device as a compensatory, prior-knowledge-rebuilding didactic tool

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Algoritmos-Rayuela is the article's own didactic device: problem-solving algorithms taken in the broad sense of "un conjunto ordenado y finito de operaciones que permite hallar la solución de un ejercicio o problema", presented with deliberately flexible step order. They were incorporated into Introducción a la Física, first year of the Profesorado en Física at UNR, alongside Nature of Science content and an introduction to vector classical mechanics, and the authors describe them as "dispositivos que promuevan procesos de carácter compensatorio y de reconstrucción de saberes previos." The device aims to strengthen self-confidence and autonomous regulation of learning amid heterogeneous secondary-school trajectories.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Presentation together with the rest of the curricular content, with sustained use across topics
- Per-topic adaptation of the algorithm to each field of vector classical mechanics
#### Constraints
- The strategy targets first-year university students whose prior knowledge is described as insufficient and whose educational trajectories are heterogeneous; the article offers no evidence about other populations

### Target Learners
- first-year university students in initial teacher training
- students transitioning from secondary school with heterogeneous prior preparation

### Learning Goals
- comprehending problem statements
- incorporating habits that favor resolution and communication of solutions
- strengthening self-confidence and autonomous learning regulation
