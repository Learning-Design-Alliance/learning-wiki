# Blended learning metric of key indicators comparing UG, KNUST and UPSA

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Table 2 of the review compares the three universities on key indicators used in assessing blended learning implementation, showing "whether or not the different activities are conducted, and in some instances the extend to which the activity is implemented". Indicators include learning management system, Moodle teaching, instructor personal webpages, video recording, smart/virtual classrooms, e-learning tools, online assignments, live chats, online learning resources, level of online distance learning (80, 90, 70 percent), virtual labs, video-conferencing rooms and podcasts, alongside location, student population and public/private status.
