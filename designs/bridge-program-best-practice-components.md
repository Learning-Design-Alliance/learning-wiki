# Seven best-practice components attributed to successful bridge and partnership institutions increasing URM participation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article reports that Bridge and Partnership Institutions achieving increased URM participation attribute success to a list of best practices, including "senior faculty involvement and support", "utilization of a more holistic approach to graduate admissions", and "securing ﬁnancial support for at least one-year of bridging experience that allows students to resolve areas of under preparation". The list further includes providing multiple mentoring resources, being flexible in coursework, sustained progress monitoring and induction into the graduate culture, and a strong research match.
