# ABSL lesson pattern of group claim generation, public defence, and cross-group negotiation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A recurring lesson-level design in which students first form self-selected groups of 4-6 people, propose their own claims for a sample question on the blackboard, and solve questions by agreeing and discussing within the group. A group spokesman writes the solution on the blackboard while supporting claims with applicable information, and must convince the other groups, who support or invalidate the claim with solid evidence. The researcher fosters negotiation with questions such as "Do you agree with your friend's solution? Why?" and closes each example by summarizing the agreed solution.
