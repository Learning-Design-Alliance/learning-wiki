# Genre-flavored course packages: building parallel course versions around well-defined film themes

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
After the pilot, the authors created versions nicknamed flavors of the course, "whereby each ﬂavor used a particular genre or theme of movies." They list seven planned flavors: Action/Adventure, Sci-Fi, Superhero, Modern Physics, Astronomy, Pseudoscience, and Metaphysics, each using films with a well defined theme. The Superhero and Pseudoscience flavors were being built starting Summer 2003.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each flavor uses films with a well defined theme or genre
#### Constraints
- Superhero and Pseudoscience flavors were still under development; the Astronomy flavor for the general education course was only being discussed

### Target Learners
- college and university general-education students

### Learning Goals
- basic principles of physical science
- topics from modern physics and astronomy
- recognizing pseudoscience
