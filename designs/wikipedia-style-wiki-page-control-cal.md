# Wikipedia-style webpage control activity with identical content but no interactivity

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A Wikipedia-style page created on Wikidot.com as the control computer-assisted learning activity. Its content was "identical to the eModule: including use of the same images, but without interactivity or clinical cases", and its design followed the structure of medical articles on Wikipedia. Creation took an estimated 10 h, compared with roughly 60 h for the eModule, illustrating the sharply different cost of the two delivery formats.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content must match the interactive alternative to isolate the effect of interactivity
#### Constraints
- Lacks interactivity, complex 3D graphics, virtual cases and reliable peer review relative to eModules

### Target Learners
- UK medical students on clinical placements

### Learning Goals
- Learning stroke and cerebrovascular anatomy content equivalent to the eModule
