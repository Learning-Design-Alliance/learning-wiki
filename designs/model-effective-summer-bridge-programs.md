# Model for Effective Summer Bridge Programs

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A model developed by the authors in 2013 and enhanced each year from program findings, specifying four main components for summer bridge programs: academic preparation, emotional quotient, college experience, and leadership. The model holds that "learnable content must be engaging and culturally relevant to today's college student," and that each component's content must include connection to pop culture, physical and virtual interaction, consistent use of media, and gamification. It rests on a foundation of student-relevant content, early expectations for success, and cultural relevance to the population served.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content must be engaging and culturally relevant to today's college student
- Foundation of student-relevant content, early expectation for success, and cultural relevance to the population served
#### Constraints
- Developed and refined at a single predominately Hispanic (96%) 2-year community college

### Target Learners
- First-time, full-time incoming community college students, predominantly Hispanic

### Learning Goals
- College readiness in gatekeeper courses
- Retention and transfer to a 4-year institution
