# Directed Reading-Thinking Activity (DR-TA): a student-centered reading method built on a predicting-reading-proving cycle

## Description
DR-TA, created by Stauffer (1969) and adapted by Tierney et al. (1995), is a student-centered reading method in which "readers through guessing overall reading content before starting to read, are able to set their reading objectives" and then read and prove predictions in a repeating cycle. The teacher acts as a facilitator, "assisting students in connecting background knowledge and new information". The article applies it with tales of virtue texts to teach English reading comprehension while raising ethical awareness.
