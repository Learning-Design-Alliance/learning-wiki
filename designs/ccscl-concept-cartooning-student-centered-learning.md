# Concept Cartooning Student-Centered Learning (CCSCL): a five-aspect framework for student-centered cartooning seminars

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes CCSCL, applying the five aspects of student-centered learning in higher education to cartooning seminars. The teacher's role is a "Cartoon and content guide, act as the facilitator of learning"; content serves skill acquisition rather than memorizing biological cascades as lists; responsibility for learning is shared; evaluation is a means to learn, practice skills and give and receive feedback; and the balance of power is dynamic and changeable based on the facilitator's pedagogical leanings. The framework is grounded in the study's seminar design and survey evidence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students and teachers must work in partnership, with students putting forth effort and institutions providing an appropriate environment to facilitate learning
#### Constraints
- The balance of power was not fully evaluated within this study and requires further consideration; if not managed carefully it may create confusion, uneven participation, or gaps in guidance

### Target Learners
- undergraduate bioscience students (UK levels 4–6)

### Learning Goals
- understanding complex multi-step biological processes
- higher-order thinking beyond remembering
- collaboration and communication skills
