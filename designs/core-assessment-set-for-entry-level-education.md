# Define a core set of standardised assessments for entry-level education to overcome barriers to routine use and promote evidence-based practice from the onset of professional development

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article argues that because standardised assessments are not yet used regularly in physiotherapy practice, defining a core set for entry-level education addresses barriers such as lack of time, inadequate knowledge and attitudes, and limited availability of manuals in the appropriate language. "Their inclusion in physiotherapy education therefore remains crucial to promote future uptake and inclusion from the onset in the professional development of evidence-based practice." Familiarity with assessments is named as a key facilitator of daily-practice use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educational programmes designed specifically to address competencies for selecting and using standardised assessments
#### Constraints
- The recommended assessments are intended to support, not replace, clinical reasoning; overreliance without understanding limitations can lead to misapplication, particularly among less experienced clinicians

### Target Learners
- entry-level physiotherapy students
- practicing physiotherapists seeking to update skills

### Learning Goals
- evidence-based practice competencies including selection and use of standardised assessments
