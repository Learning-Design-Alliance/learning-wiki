# Conceptual framework of VGC outcomes: three mediating dimensions (collaborative experience, communication and cultural dynamics, logistical strategies) influence three outcome dimensions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes a conceptual framework in which VGC participation influences student outcomes through "three interconnected mediating dimensions: collaborative experience, communication and cultural dynamics, and logistical strategies." These mediators are expected to influence three primary outcome dimensions: learning and skill development, perceived value, and global citizenship and readiness, plus challenges and opportunities as a reflective outcome. The framework is informed by research on collaborative learning, computer-supported collaborative learning, and virtual exchange, and generated hypotheses H1-H7 tested in the study.
