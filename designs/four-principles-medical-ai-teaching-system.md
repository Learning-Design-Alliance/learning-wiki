# Four design principles — constructive alignment, experiential progression, research–teaching nexus, and CDIO — govern the curriculum architecture

## Description
The paper draws four principles from the education literature and translates each "into a testable constraint on the artefact". Constructive alignment supplies criterion-referenced thresholds: "Biggs argues that intended learning outcomes, learning activities and assessment tasks must be mutually consistent". Kolb's experiential cycle motivates cumulative tiers, the research–teaching nexus transfers open problems rather than only results, and CDIO motivates the engineering layer. Each principle is operationalised as a concrete feature of the curriculum.
