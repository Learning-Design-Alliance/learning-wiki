# Five measurable physics-related learning outcomes for pharmacy curricula spanning fluid dynamics, thermodynamics, spectroscopy, imaging, and quantum modeling

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review states that "A well-structured curriculum should also focus on measurable learning outcomes" and enumerates physics-related outcomes for pharmacy students: understanding fluid dynamics applied to blood flow and intravenous drug delivery; applying thermodynamic principles to evaluate drug solubility, stability, and shelf life; utilizing UV-Vis and IR spectroscopy to analyze formulations; explaining nuclear physics in MRI and PET; and modeling molecular interactions using quantum mechanics to predict drug efficacy and receptor binding. This outcome set functions as a reusable design pattern for curriculum planning.
