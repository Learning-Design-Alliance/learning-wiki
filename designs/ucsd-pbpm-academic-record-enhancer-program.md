# UCSD PBPM program: a yearlong, 48-unit academic record enhancer postbaccalaureate program

## Description
The UCSD Extension PBPM program, launched in 2013 in partnership with the School of Medicine and Division of Biological Sciences, is a "yearlong, 48-unit program" for applicants who have already completed prerequisite coursework. Students take upper-division biomedical science courses plus cohort-only advanced courses with frequent testing and feedback, MCAT preparation support, faculty advising, and a mock multiple mini interview. The article evaluates its alumni's medical school outcomes.
