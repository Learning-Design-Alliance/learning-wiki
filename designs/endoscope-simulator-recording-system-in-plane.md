# Endoscope-equipped simulator with simultaneous endoscopic and ultrasound recording

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The teaching system is a simulator device consisting of an ultrasound probe, a simulated vessel, a needle, and an endoscope connected to a computer to visualize the image inside the simulated vessel. An endoscope is inserted into the simulated vessel, and "Endoscopic and ultrasound views are recorded and saved in the computer simultaneously", enabling blinded expert evaluation of success, puncture accuracy, and needle tip visualization. Recordings were numbered and randomized by computer to conceal participant identity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Recorded images must be measurable at different magnifications, with actual distances calculated using a magnification ratio derived from measured versus actual needle diameter
#### Constraints
- Images from 5 of 47 participants could not be used for accuracy evaluation (1 unclear, 4 computer error)

### Target Learners
- health care providers learning ultrasound-guided vascular access

### Learning Goals
- visualizing and evaluating needle position inside the vessel during in-plane puncture
