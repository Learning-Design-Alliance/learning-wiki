# Three-stage model of formation of creative civil activity of student youth

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article presents formation of civil activity as a model realized during three interconnected stages: a theoretical-procedural (search) stage, an interiorization (transformative-reflexive) stage, and an eksteriorization (practice-oriented) stage. Each stage includes purposes, psychology and pedagogical conditions, contents and a system of pedagogical means, and the stages are "directed on formation of theoretical, practical and personal readiness of students". The model structures civil activity as a three-part unity of informative, motivationally-demanding and personal-reflexive components, grounded in personally focused, activity and axiological approaches.
