# Pregnancy heart team (PHT): multidisciplinary team-based care model for cardio-obstetric patients

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A pregnancy heart team is a formal multidisciplinary care structure the article recommends establishing, including "cardiologists with expertise in cardio‐obstetrics, obstetricians, maternal‐fetal medicine specialists, primary healthcare providers, obstetric anesthesiologists, neonatologists, geneticists, pharmacists, social workers, nurses, and other care team members." Team-member involvement and prenatal visit frequency vary with CVD complexity (Tables 2 and 3). PHTs also run quality-improvement initiatives at monthly multidisciplinary meetings and provide trainees immersive outpatient clinic and inpatient labor-and-delivery educational experiences.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Strong partnerships and exposure to multidisciplinary team-based care; institutional collaboration between MFM/obstetrics, medicine, and anesthesia
#### Constraints
- For patients living far from a specialized high-risk delivery hospital, the PHT needs additional involvement before the third trimester with detailed antepartum and delivery plans

### Target Learners
- General CVD fellows
- Advanced trainees on labor and delivery rotations

### Learning Goals
- Managing pregnant patients with heart disease
- Recognizing effects of adverse pregnancy outcomes on long-term cardiovascular outcomes
