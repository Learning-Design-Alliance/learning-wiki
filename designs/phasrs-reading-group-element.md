# PhASRS: the SJSU Physics and Astronomy Student Reading Society online reading group

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
PhASRS was an online reading group at San José State University running from summer 2020 through fall 2020, founded by students and faculty in response to canceled summer internships. In the fall it was "codified into a one-unit class employing a credit/no credit grading scheme" with exposure-to-research as an underlying value and learning outcomes including the ability to identify and reflect on the salient points of scientific articles. Nine faculty members and 12 students participated in total; sessions drew 3–5 faculty and 6–10 students. Support structures included Zoom meetings, a shared Google Document schedule, a Discord channel for asynchronous reactions, shared article-notes documents, and a Canvas course page with discussion assignments for the credit-bearing version.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty participation (3–5 per session)
- Technological support: Zoom, shared Google Documents, a Discord channel, and Canvas for the credit-bearing version
#### Constraints
- Ran online during the COVID-19 pandemic at a single institution; attendance and discussion participation defined student grades in the fall version

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics majors
- master's students
- recently graduated students entering physics graduate programs

### Learning Goals
- understanding and processing scientific content
- exposure to modern physics research topics
- community-building and career preparation
