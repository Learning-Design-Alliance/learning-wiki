# Multiple complementary representations of the same information enabled by simultaneous recording and processing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article argues that smartphones and tablets can "simultaneously record and process data", which "enables multiple, complementary representations of the same information" (citing Treagust et al., 2017). For projectile motion, representations include the video capture, the position graph and the data table; for musical sounds, the waveform, frequency spectrum, spectrum map and exported numerical data. This representational richness gives students more time to process information and discuss the experiment.
