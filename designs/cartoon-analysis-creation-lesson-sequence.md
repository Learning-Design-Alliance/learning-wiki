# Cartoon analysis-and-creation lesson sequence for teaching affective concepts

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A reusable lesson pattern in which each session opens with discussion of printed cartoons using prompts such as 'What is happening?' and 'How does the character feel?', then progresses across lessons to completing partly-finished cartoons, small-group skits applying a cartoon's concept to a new situation, and collaborative completion of a multi-panel strip. The pattern applies academic or affective concepts to new cartoon situations, using humor, wordplay, and creative drawing to motivate students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A set of numbered cartoons focused on the target concepts so the teacher can refer to them during discussion
#### Constraints
- The article reports the approach tested in one suburban school with primary-grade students; generalizability to other populations is untested

### Target Learners
- elementary students of mixed abilities

### Learning Goals
- acquiring concepts of an affective or academic topic
- applying concepts to new situations
