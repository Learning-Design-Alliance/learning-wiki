# PBL tutorial structure: small groups with Maastricht seven-step sessions and facilitator role

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes PBL sessions as "small groups with about eight to ten students and one facilitating professor". Through two sessions, students discuss scenarios ruled by the Maastricht seven steps: a first session where a secretary and coordinator are chosen, individual self-study, then a second session where participants discuss the results of each student's individual studies. The facilitating professor oversees the process, ensuring students stay on topic and providing guidance when needed.
