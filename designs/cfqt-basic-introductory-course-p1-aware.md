# Basic summer/winter introductory quantum course targeting the P1 'QT Aware Person' profile

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A four-week introductory course (two hours per week) with no prerequisites, mapped in the CFQT to the P1 'QT Aware Person' profile and the Quantum Background domain. It 'aims to familiarize students with tangible experiences through the use of games, visual activities, or simulations', promoting conceptualization and intuitive understanding rather than extensive mathematical competence, and is suitable for non-STEM degrees and professionals from diverse fields.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- No prerequisites; designed to provide basic knowledge to those without a technical background
#### Constraints
- Emphasizes conceptualization and intuitive understanding rather than extensive mathematical competence

### Target Learners
- Non-STEM students and professionals from diverse fields working in quantum-related projects

### Learning Goals
- General awareness of the quantum technology landscape, its interconnections, applications, and basic quantum concepts
