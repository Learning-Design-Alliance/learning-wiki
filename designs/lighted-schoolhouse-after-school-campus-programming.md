# Lighted schoolhouse: after-school programming on the school campus

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The lighted schoolhouse model hosts events on the school campus during after-school hours to "provide a healthy, positive environment for youth." In One Life, each district assigned a staff member and events occurred biweekly, alternating between districts, including recreation, leisure, and physical activity. The authors link it to improved school bonding and home-school relations through school-community partnership and facility use beyond normal hours.
