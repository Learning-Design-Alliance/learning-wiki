# SOONER high-fidelity opioid overdose simulation protocol

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A three-component simulation protocol — standardized video briefing, a staged near-fatal overdose scenario using a Laerdal SimMan® ALS manikin in a staged bedroom, and a PEARLS-adapted debriefing — based on 2015 American Heart Association bystander resuscitation recommendations. Scenario design and testing was "done with community members who had lived experience with opioid overdose", and performance was video-recorded and scored by two clinicians blinded to allocation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Successful resuscitation required participants to administer intranasal naloxone and perform chest compressions, with recognition, positioning, EMS activation and organization also evaluated
#### Constraints
- Designed to mimic a very near-fatal overdose; without prompt intervention the simulated patient would proceed to cardiac arrest and death

### Target Learners
- Non-clinicians at high risk of experiencing or witnessing opioid overdose, aged over 16

### Learning Goals
- Navigating the bystander resuscitation algorithm for opioid overdose
