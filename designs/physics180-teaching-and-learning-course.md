# Physics 180: a ten-week course blending physics content, theories of teaching and learning, and practical teaching experience

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Physics 180 is an upper-division elective for junior/senior physics majors and early graduate students, limited to roughly 10 students (13 participated in this study), with introductory physics as the only prerequisite. Its three curricular components — physics content reviewing about two-thirds of an introductory E&M course, readings on learning theory and research, and weekly pre-college teaching at a local Boys and Girls Club — are temporally aligned so students study, read about difficulties with, and teach the same concept in the same week. The course was created as a form of design experiment, simultaneously used to study student learning in context and effect change.
