# Borup, West, and Thomas (2015) study on text versus video communication for instructor feedback

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
A mixed-method research study comparing video feedback and text-based feedback in a blended learning course through the perceptions of student teachers and teacher educators. The study critically analyzed feedback "from the perspective of media richness theory" and identified "three elements: content and utility, timing and efficiency, and delivery and affective support". It examined how asynchronous text feedback capitalizes on asynchronous text communication and adds characteristics of verbal and non-verbal cues.
