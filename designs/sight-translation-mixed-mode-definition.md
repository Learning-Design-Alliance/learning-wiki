## Description
The article defines sight translation, citing Gonzalez, Vasquez and Mikkelson (2012), as "the act of the oral translation of a written text and is a combination of translation and interp reting" — the oral reproduction in the target language of a source-language document for listeners or readers. It is widely applied in translation pedagogy, and the article treats it as a language-teaching tool as well as an interpreter-training activity. Related accounts describe it as requiring quick analysis and creative, exact recreation of a text, and as cognitively demanding.
