# STEM magnet school design combining themed pathways, project-based learning, blended learning with 1:1 devices, and STEM electives and extracurriculars

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The three MSAP-funded magnet schools share a design built around a STEM theme with dedicated magnet coursework, project-based learning, and blended learning with 1:1 Chromebooks. Gehring used "Magnet coursework via STEM labs in computer science, engineering, and biomedical science" with PLTW Launch; Lied offered magnet pathways in architecture/construction, robotics/manufacturing, and computer science with inquiry-based learning; O'Callaghan offered tracks in video game design, video production, web design, and robotics/engineering. All schools added STEM electives taught by grant-funded teachers and STEM-related extracurricular activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- MSAP grant funds to hire licensed teachers for STEM electives and extend instructional time
- 1:1 Chromebook access for blended learning
#### Constraints
- Design evaluated only during the first two years of magnet implementation and disrupted by COVID-19 school closures in 2019-20

### Target Learners
- Elementary students in grades 1-5
- Middle school students in grades 6-8

### Learning Goals
- STEM content knowledge in mathematics, science, and technology
- Enrollment and completion of STEM courses
