# Three-dimensional didactic structure for developing clinical competence: curricular, processual, and performative dimensions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The CCASP organizes its teaching around a didactic structure with three dimensions, detailed in the article's Table 1. The "Curricular dimension" covers knowledge-based content, evidence-based medicine, and state-of-the-art therapy via lectures, reading, and virtual classroom teaching. The "Processual dimension" addresses analysis and reflecting clinical treatment, case-based learning, and procedural knowledge through clinical cases, virtual clinical cases, and e-grand rounds. The "Performative dimension" targets "Presenting and performing clinical treatment implementing good clinical practice" via participant clinical case presentations and e-grand rounds.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- four to five e-learning modules of pre-recorded video lectures with a chat function for lecturer questions
- three face-to-face seminars enabling case presentations and e-grand rounds
#### Constraints
- each module unit equates to 3 h of workload consisting of a 30-min lecture and 2.5 h of additional reading

### Target Learners
- postgraduate oncologists and scientists in the breast cancer, lymphoma, and lung cancer programs

### Learning Goals
- clinical competence in cancer management, including knowledge, procedural knowledge, and clinical performance
