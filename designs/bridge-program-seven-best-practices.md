# Seven best practices shared by successful physics bridge programs

## Description
Successful bridge programs share key components the article enumerates: substantial tenured faculty involvement, holistic graduate admissions, funded bridging of at least one year to resolve gaps in undergraduate education, multiple mentoring resources, flexible coursework, sustained progress monitoring, and a great research match. The Outcomes section ties the programs' high retention to these practices, noting 'other programs have similar results.'
