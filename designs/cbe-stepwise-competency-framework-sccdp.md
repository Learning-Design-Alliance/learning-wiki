# Competency-based education framework for stepwise clinical competency development

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The SCCDP's conceptual framework is grounded in competency-based education (CBE), "an educational approach focused on ensuring that learners acquire specific competencies and effectively apply them to real-life situations". The framework organizes a step-by-step progression from a basic stage to an advanced stage around clearly defined competency goals, and incorporates competency-based assessment at four time points, continuous feedback to enable goal-oriented learning, and flexibility to adapt to changing healthcare requirements. It is used in this article to structure the new-nurse program and frame its longitudinal outcome evaluation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clearly defined competency goals at each stage
- Competency-based assessment conducted longitudinally
- Continuous feedback mechanisms
#### Constraints
- The framework was applied to new nurses transitioning from beginner to practitioner levels in a hospital setting

### Target Learners
- New nurses in clinical practice

### Learning Goals
- Clinical competency development and application to real clinical situations
