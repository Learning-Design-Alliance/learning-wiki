# Instructional package of five prewriting strategies for EFL writing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A compiled instructional package drawn from three writing textbooks (Zemach and Islam 2011; White 2006; Smally, Ruetten, and Kozyrev 2011) covering "freewriting, brainstorming, listing, clustering, and asking wh-questions during the planning stage of writing". The same materials were used for the conventional, web-based, and hybrid groups, with only the mode of input delivery differing. The package was reviewed by the researchers and two colleagues before use.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Review and revision of the materials by the researchers and two colleagues, with feedback from academic writing instructors
#### Constraints
- The control group's textbook (Paragraph Development) deliberately contained no prewriting strategies, so the package applies only to the experimental conditions

### Target Learners
- Iranian EFL university sophomores

### Learning Goals
- Idea generation and planning before writing
- Increased writing quantity (idea units)
