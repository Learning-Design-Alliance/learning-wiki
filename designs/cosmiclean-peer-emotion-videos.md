# CosmiClean game and peer-model emotion videos

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
CosmiClean is a game "designed ... to teach secondary school and university students the principles of separation processes for recycling materials", in which players build separation chains of conveyors, separators, and receptors across cargo levels with corrective feedback. For the intervention, three recorded videos (about 23 minutes each, 13 game levels) showed a male peer model playing the same script with enjoyment, frustration, or a neutral expression, filmed from a first-person perspective and hosted on Edpuzzle.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants watch the video carefully and cannot skip it; a manipulation check after the posttest verifies perceived emotion
#### Constraints
- The study used a virtual peer participants did not know, and an online rather than classroom format, to keep exposure to a single emotion clearly controlled

### Target Learners
- Secondary school and university students

### Learning Goals
- Chemistry separation processes: using nine separators, twelve materials, and eight properties
