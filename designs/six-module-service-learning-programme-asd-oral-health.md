# Six-module service learning programme integrating theory, field service, and reflective evaluation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The programme was "structured into six sequential modules that integrated theoretical knowledge with community service focused specifically on the ASD population": theoretical teaching, field investigation, data analysis and reporting, educational material development, health education implementation, and programme evaluation and reflection. Each module pairs an academic activity with a service or reflective component, culminating in pre-post assessment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A partner institution specializing in education and rehabilitation for children with ASD, supervising lecturers, and faculty formative feedback on student-produced materials.
#### Constraints
- Evaluated at one dental school in China with a single-group pretest-posttest design, which the authors state may limit generalizability.

### Target Learners
- Fourth-year undergraduate dental students

### Learning Goals
- Oral epidemiology and examination skills, ASD-specific communication, and health education delivery for children with ASD
