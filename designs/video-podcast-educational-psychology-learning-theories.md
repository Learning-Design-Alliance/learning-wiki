# Video Podcast on Educational Psychology learning theories for B.Ed trainees

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A video podcast instructional resource covering four learning theories topics from the B.Ed Educational Psychology syllabus: Trial and Error, Classical Conditioning, Operant Conditioning, and Insight Learning. Development followed five steps from script preparation through uploading to a website, with "each video has a separate Uniform Resource Locator (URL) address." Content validity was established through expert panel review.
