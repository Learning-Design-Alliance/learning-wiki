# Object-oriented collaborative research project pattern with phased knowledge-object development

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The redesigned Bachelor Thesis course organized small groups to set up, conduct, and report an empirical research study for external clients across four phases — project initiation, research preparation, construction, and delivery — each producing intermediate objects (project plan, research plan, instruments, drafts) culminating in a final research article and presentation slides. Shared knowledge objects were requested as part of the envisioned problem solutions, positioning students as producers of knowledge rather than mere course-takers. Groups were formed around client-proposed topics and scaffolded to organize and manage their own projects.

### Learning Goals
