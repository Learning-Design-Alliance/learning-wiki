# Four-domain AI literacy curriculum framework for dental education: fundamental knowledge, use cases, evaluation, and governance

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The review proposes a curriculum development framework for AI literacy in dental education, drawing on the FDI World Dental Federation Delphi consensus process that identified "four key domains: fundamental knowledge, use cases, evaluation of medical and dental AI, and additional miscellaneous aspects." The first domain covers definitions, machine learning, training/validation/testing, and explainability; the second addresses AI use cases in dentistry; the third covers evaluation matrices and impact on dental health outcomes; the fourth covers generalisability, autonomy and accountability, and governance. The authors note these outcomes "primarily emphasise knowledge acquisition" with lesser focus on skills and critical analytical thinking.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Curriculum revision must aim to equip students with skills necessary to understand emerging innovations, requiring faculty training and dynamic curriculum responsiveness
#### Constraints
- The proposed learning outcomes primarily emphasise knowledge acquisition with lesser focus on competencies related to skill development and critical analytical thinking
- Data privacy and security are notably absent in the proposed competency domains in dental education

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate and postgraduate dental students

### Learning Goals
- AI literacy including fundamental knowledge, use cases, evaluation of AI, and ethics and governance
