# Dual language (Spanish/English) narrative intervention with embedded vocabulary instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A dual language version of the Story Champs narrative intervention in which 12 Spanish lessons with 12 comparable English lessons targeted storytelling and vocabulary in 20-minute small groups four days a week. The Spanish stories "were not translations of the English stories" but included equivalent linguistic features and vocabulary targets (e.g., brincar-leap) to maximize cross-linguistic transfer. Each of the 12 words was embedded in four lessons, two per language.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Stories carefully constructed with equivalent linguistic features and vocabulary targets across languages
- Interventionists trained via a 1-hr training plus demonstration and coaching in the classroom
#### Constraints
- Vocabulary targets were adjectives and verbs that are more abstract and challenging to picture, which may have limited the receptive assessment's sensitivity

### Target Learners
- Spanish-speaking preschool dual language learners in Head Start

### Learning Goals
- English narrative retelling
- English and Spanish receptive vocabulary
- story grammar and complex language use
