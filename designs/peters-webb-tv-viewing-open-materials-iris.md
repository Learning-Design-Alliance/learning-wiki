# Open materials for the viewing experiments hosted in the IRIS database

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The experiment's materials were shared for transparent practices and are available in the IRIS database. As printed: "The experiment in this article earned an Open Materials badge for transparent practices. The materials are available at https://www.iris-database.org/iris/app/home/detail?id=york:933919." This makes the viewing and testing materials from the two experiments reusable by other researchers studying incidental vocabulary learning through audio-visual input.
