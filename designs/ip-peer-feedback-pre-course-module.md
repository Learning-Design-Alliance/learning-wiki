# Pre-course interprofessional peer feedback module (videos, readings, TTCA-24 guidance) for trauma simulation events

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A flipped-classroom pre-course module created by an educational subject matter expert, containing recorded Zoom videos and PDF links to recommended readings based on a librarian-aided literature review. It covers the theory and efficacy of peer feedback, interprofessional learning principles, best-practice strategies, use of the TTCA-24 to structure feedback, reflection on and in action, psychological safety, and word choice for honest, sincere, relevant, actionable feedback. Learners received it three weeks before the event with reminder emails, and compliance was checked on arrival.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learners review the materials before the event; compliance is checked on arrival with space provided to review if incomplete
#### Constraints
- Piloted at one institution with 25 learners during pandemic social distancing, with simulation and debrief groups connected via Zoom

### Target Learners
- general surgery residents
- emergency medicine residents
- nurses
- medical students

### Learning Goals
- delivering effective interprofessional peer feedback
- structuring feedback to specific actionable items
