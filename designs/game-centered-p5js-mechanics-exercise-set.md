# A set of seven game-like p5.js programming activities for introductory mechanics, released on the PICUP site, with a p5.js-based learning management system

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article presents six required hour-long activities plus one optional extra-credit activity at OSU Marion — Planetoids, Lunar descent, Bellicose birds, Planetoids with momentum, with torque, with a spring, and Bellicose birds with energy — which build on each other, share a vector color scheme matching the textbook, and contain optional challenges. The full set and code are available at http://compadre.org/PICUP. A companion website, "which can be thought of as a p5.js-based learning management system", lets teachers quickly view submissions and give feedback, and was used for the first time in spring 2017 at OSU Marion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A browser and the p5.js library; no installations or plugins, so no IT involvement is needed
- Exercises must be completed in sequence, since they build off each other in a way that makes starting in the middle hard
#### Constraints
- About 125 students from OSU's regional campus in Marion have completed the exercises; the energy exercise is more difficult than the others and very few students have completed it so far

### Target Learners
- absolute beginner programmers in algebra-based or calculus-based introductory physics at high school or college level

### Learning Goals
- iterative application of forces, momentum conservation, torque, harmonic motion, projectile trajectories, and energy conservation
