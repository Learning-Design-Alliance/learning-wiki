# Electrosurgery-compatible plant-derived (VTT) oral cancer resection simulation task with a 15 mm phantom tumor and 10 mm safety margin design

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A soft-tissue simulation task built by modifying a commercially available Versatile Training Tissue (VTT) model made of konjac potato flour, whose glucomannan content turns black with carbonization above 200 °C, enabling replication of electrosurgery. The task uses a 15 mm hemispherical phantom tumor buried in tissue and requires a 10 mm safety margin in all directions. Surgeons work alone with a monopolar handpiece in the dominant hand and DeBakey forceps in the non-dominant hand.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An electrosurgical unit (VIO50C) with a coagulation-mode monopolar handpiece and DeBakey forceps, plus a spectral colorimeter and ruler for assessment
#### Constraints
- The phantom tumor is hemispherical with a distinct color, whereas actual oral tumors are often irregular, not clearly visible, and extend deeper than they appear, requiring palpation

### Target Learners
- Final-year medical students
- Head and neck surgery trainees

### Learning Goals
- Ensuring appropriate resection margins
- Minimizing thermal tissue damage during electrosurgery

### Claims

- [Expert surgeons outperformed novices on all four quantitative measures of the resection task: smaller margin errors, lower carbonization, faster resection, fewer grasps](../claims/experts-outperform-novices-four-measures.md) [+W]
- [Experts rated the electrosurgical oral cancer resection task highly for realism and quantitative assessment, with all 10 rating its training usefulness 5 of 5](../claims/expert-questionnaire-ratings-resection-task.md) [+W]
- [Novices achieved adequate mean margins mainly by over-resecting deep margins, while experts secured more uniform 10 mm margins across directions](../claims/deep-margin-difficulty-novice-over-resection.md) [+W]
- [The four quantitative measures of the resection task showed good internal consistency (Cronbach's alpha 0.803)](../claims/cronbach-alpha-0803-four-measures.md) [+W]
