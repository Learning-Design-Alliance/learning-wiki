# Place-based teaching activity sequence: outdoor investigation, stakeholder consultation, solution proposals, and public awareness products

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The experimental activities formed a multi-week sequence in which students investigated a real local site, consulted stakeholders, and produced solutions and awareness products. Activities included a trip to a public picnic area where students noted positive and negative aspects, interviewing visitors, creating "solution proposals in groups", negotiating with officials, receiving feedback, presenting oral and written reports, and producing brochures, posters, clean-up activities and tree protection on campus.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Extracurricular activities (sightseeing, observation, group work) carried out over 4 weeks to support the place based teaching activities
- Access to a local site and to the people responsible for it
#### Constraints
- Students later reported problems of time, cost, weather, security, transport and supervising students as drawbacks of out-of-class implementation

### Target Learners
- first-year classroom teacher candidates (university)

### Learning Goals
- science process skills
- understanding of the local environment
