# E-content package on nomenclature of organic compounds

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A self-instructional e-content package developed by the investigator to teach IUPAC nomenclature of organic compounds to undergraduate chemistry majors. It was "based upon the chapter 'Nomenclature of organic compounds'" from standard organic chemistry textbooks, and "The total duration of the e-content package was thirty seven minutes." It served as the treatment for the experimental group in the quasi-experiment and incorporated technological features such as images, audio, and video.
