# Cohorts-in-large-course design: large lecture plus ~20-student discussion sections builds disciplinary community while serving large numbers

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Exploring Biology serves ~200 students per semester using a cohorts-in-large-course design: all students meet in large lecture for the first hour, then split into small discussion sections of ~20 students. According to the article, this "creates communities of students with common interests, which enhances opportunities for networking among peers and informal interactions with faculty, and promotes retention in the discipline through an increased sense of belonging." The design combines small-group learning benefits with large-course capacity.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A faculty-led instructional team spanning career stages and campus professionals to populate the network of support
#### Constraints
- Availability of a dedicated course director and teaching fellow instructors may be limiting at other institutions

### Target Learners
- first-year undergraduate students intending to major in biology

### Learning Goals
- ease the transition to college
- build disciplinary community and sense of belonging
- develop networking and academic navigation skills
