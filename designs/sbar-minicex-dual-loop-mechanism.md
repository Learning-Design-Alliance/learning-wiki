# Structured communication–dynamic feedback dual-loop mechanism combining SBAR and mini-CEX

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes that SBAR and mini-CEX work synergistically as a "'structured communication-dynamic feedback' dual-loop mechanism": SBAR's S–B–A–R framework standardises the expression logic of intern communication, while mini-CEX provides phased (week 1/week 3) real-time evaluation that exposes communication vulnerabilities and drives targeted improvement of SBAR's weak links. The authors present this closed-loop design as the key mechanism behind the observed gains in communication, thinking and comprehensive ability.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A trained teaching team that can deliver SBAR theoretical teaching, case teaching and mini-CEX scoring with feedback
- A formative assessment cycle with repeated mini-CEX evaluation points so feedback can correct deviations during the rotation
#### Constraints
- The mechanism account is the authors' interpretation of a single-centre 3-week trial; long-term effects are untested

### Target Learners
- nursing interns (junior college and undergraduate) in clinical rotations

### Learning Goals
- clinical communication ability
- clinical thinking ability
- comprehensive clinical ability
