# Huygens pendulum laboratory device with GeoGebra-printed cycloid cheeks optimised for Tracker video analysis

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An interdisciplinary device comparing an ordinary pendulum with a Huygens pendulum, built by printing a cycloid generated in GeoGebra to shape the cheeks in wood. The final device "was optimized for video analysis with Tracker choosing an effective shape and colour for the pendent mass". It allowed simultaneous movement of both pendula and measurement of isochronism, and connects mathematics, physics and technical progress through the cycloid's properties demonstrated dynamically in GeoGebra.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- GeoGebra to generate and print the cycloid shape
- Tracker for video analysis of the pendulum motion
#### Constraints
- Isochronism was tested only for initial angles in the range 15°–50°

### Target Learners
- secondary-school students studying cycloid properties and pendulum isochronism

### Learning Goals
- verifying Huygens pendulum isochronism and demonstrating cycloid properties experimentally
