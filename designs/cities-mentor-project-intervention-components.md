# Cities Mentor Project intervention components and timing.

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Cities Mentor Project pairs 6th-grade students attending schools in low-income urban neighborhoods with university mentor-advocates who receive course credit and supervision through a service-learning course. Mentors and mentees attend coping training sessions after-school at partner schools administered by school-affiliated staff from a university-based community mental health center. Mentors connect with teachers and parents for academic advocacy, connect mentees to protective settings at partner community organizations, and practice coping strategies with mentees in real-life situations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mentors are required to make at least a two-year commitment to youth
- The program makes an eight-year commitment to youth with the plan to replace mentors as needed
#### Constraints
- The intervention was developed for low-income urban communities and its transferability had not yet been established

### Target Learners
- low-income urban middle school youth

### Learning Goals
- coping skills
- academic engagement
- academic achievement
- school completion
