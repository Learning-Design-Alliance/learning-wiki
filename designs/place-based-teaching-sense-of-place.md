# Place-based teaching grounded in 'sense of place' as a framework for archaeo-astronomy in science education

## Description
The article situates archaeo-astronomy activities within place-based teaching, an approach the authors say has only been integrated into science in the past decade. It rests on the concept of 'sense of place', defined via Tuan as the idea that "What begins as undifferentiated space becomes place when we endow it with value". The approach includes emotional bonds, values, place meanings and awareness of cultural heritage; after archaeo-astronomy activities students become grounded within the region they call home.
