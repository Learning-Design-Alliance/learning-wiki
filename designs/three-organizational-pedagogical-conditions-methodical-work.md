# Manage teacher scientific-methodical work through three organizational-pedagogical conditions: a management system, purposeful motivation, and a training system

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article proposes that efficient teacher scientific-methodical work on a project-target basis requires a complex of organizational-pedagogical conditions. As printed, "The complex of organizational -pedagogical conditions includes the management system of teacher scientific -methodical work in vocational educational institutions; purposeful motivation of teachers for scientific -methodical work" plus stimulating self-development and a complex of events raising teachers' qualification. The conditions were defined taking into account each organization's facilities and federal state educational standard requirements.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A management system of teacher scientific-methodical work built on the structural-functional model
- Building profound teacher motivation, including discovering and eliminating factors of teachers' dissatisfaction
- A system of teacher training in scientific-methodical work on the basis of project-target approach
#### Constraints
- Conditions were defined for specific secondary vocational educational institutions and the requirements of the federal state educational standard for secondary vocational education

### Target Learners
- teachers in secondary vocational educational institutions
- heads and deputy heads of vocational educational organizations

### Learning Goals
- scientific-methodical competence
- professional reflexion and educational mobility of teachers
