# Fifteen-session topic-based metacognitive awareness listening and speaking course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The treatment was a fifteen-week topic-based listening and speaking course "based on Cotterall's (2000) principles of autonomous learning". Six metacognitive strategies drawn from Oxford (1990) were taught at three levels: centering learning (overviewing, focusing attention), arranging and planning learning (identifying task purpose and planning), and evaluating learning (self-editing, self-evaluation). Tasks were linked to the recognition-to-production (PPP) model from Communicative Language Teaching and practiced under the study's three planning conditions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tasks and strategies congruent with course objectives and linked to a model of language teaching
- Opportunities to discuss and practice strategies known to facilitate task performance
#### Constraints
- The tasks were not authentic and focused on a limited set of grammatical structures (simple past and past continuous)

### Target Learners
- Iranian TEFL university sophomores

### Learning Goals
- metacognitive awareness
- oral fluency
- strategic self-planning of speech
