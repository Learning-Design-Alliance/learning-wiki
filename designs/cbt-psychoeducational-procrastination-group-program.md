# Eight-session CBT-based psycho-educational group program for academic procrastination

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A researcher-developed group program of eight 90-minute sessions guided by Beck's ten principles of cognitive therapy, targeting automatic thoughts, intermediate beliefs, and core beliefs about academic tasks. Sessions combined "problem-solving, cognitive, and behavioral techniques" and used activities, visual materials, metaphors, homework, and Socratic dialogue. Each session opened with a summary of the previous session and homework discussion and closed with summarizing, feedback, and new homework.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sessions require a leader and structured per-session plans; the article reports a pilot study with a leader and an expert deputy leader was used to refine activities before the experiment
#### Constraints
- Tested with university students without psychiatric diagnoses or treatment; the article notes effectiveness in students with mild or moderate depression remains untested

### Target Learners
- university students with academic procrastination behaviors

### Learning Goals
- managing and reducing academic procrastination behaviors
- cognitive, affective, and behavioral self-regulation skills for academic tasks
