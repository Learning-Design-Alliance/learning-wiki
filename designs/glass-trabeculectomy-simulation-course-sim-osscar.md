# GLASS one-week trabeculectomy simulation course with Sim-OSSCAR competency assessment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The GLASS intervention is a one-week intensive trabeculectomy simulation-based surgical education course combining theoretical and practical teaching. "The surgical procedure was deconstructed and instruction in surgical steps was provided using a modified Peyton's four-stage approach", with low-cost moderate-fidelity materials (foam for suturing, apple peels for scleral flaps) and full procedures on high-fidelity synthetic Advanced TrabEye eyes under Zeiss microscopes. Competency was assessed with the validated Sim-OSSCAR rubric by two independent, masked expert graders scoring eight core skills to a maximum of 16 per video.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires high-fidelity synthetic simulation eyes, operating microscopes, video recording equipment, and graders trained in the Sim-OSSCAR rubric
#### Constraints
- The article states results only apply to simulated surgical skill competency using high-fidelity artificial eyes, and live surgical skills could not be evaluated due to low trabeculectomy case numbers

### Target Learners
- resident ophthalmologists in postgraduate training years two to four

### Learning Goals
- competency in the eight core trabeculectomy surgical skills including scleral incision, scleral flap, releasable suturing, conjunctival suturing and sclerostomy
