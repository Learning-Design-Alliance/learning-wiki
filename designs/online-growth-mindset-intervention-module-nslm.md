# Short online growth mindset intervention module (two self-administered sessions)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q4` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A scalable, low-cost online module of two self-administered sessions of roughly 25 minutes each, delivered about 20 days apart during school hours, that teaches that intellectual abilities can be developed. It uses the metaphor that the brain is like a muscle, stories from older students and admired adults, and a self-persuasion exercise teaching the idea to a struggling future ninth-grader. The article states "The intervention consisted of two self-administered online sessions that lasted approximately 25 min each and occurred roughly 20 days apart during regular school hours". The module will be available at no cost to secondary schools in the United States or Canada via PERTS.
