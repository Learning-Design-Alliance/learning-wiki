# 18-activity plasticized-clay art program covering six basic mathematics skills

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A structured art-activity program in which preschool children work with plasticized clay to practice six basic mathematics skills: observation, categorization, comparison, classification, order arrangement and measurement. The study covered "all the 6 basic skills in mathematics ... which consists of 3 activities per skill, totaled to 18 activities". Sessions ran 6 weeks, 3 days per week, 30 minutes per day, delivered Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday mornings.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A researcher-made 30-point basic mathematics assessment (reliability 0.86) to identify children with low basic mathematical skill before participation
#### Constraints
- Teachers must ensure children do not put the plasticized clay in their mouth, which the authors note could be dangerous

### Target Learners
- preschool children aged 3-4 years attending kindergarten 1

### Learning Goals
- six basic mathematics skills: observation, categorization, comparison, classification, order arrangement and measurement
