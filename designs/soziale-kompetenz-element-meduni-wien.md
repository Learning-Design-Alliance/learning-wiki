# Mandatory social competence element (soziale Kompetenz), Medical University of Vienna

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A mandatory curriculum element at the Medical University of Vienna in which students learn social competences in contact with people in need of care in a nursing home. The review states the "Intended learning outcomes of this training are adequate communication, empathy, appreciation and professional behaviour in an interdisciplinary team." It is offered as an example of including general interpersonal qualities in a curriculum.
