# Research feedback experimental teaching model (RFETM): a five-component reform of basic medical experimental teaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The research feedback experimental teaching model (RFETM) is a reform of basic medical experimental teaching built 'from five aspects: RFETM teaching syllabus, teaching projects, teaching assessment, teaching conditions, and textbooks'. Its project library 'included 10 teaching projects and a total of 32 class hours', the first eight mandatory and the last two optional, supported by a virtual simulation platform for pre-class self-study and a newly compiled innovation experimental textbook.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Experimental teaching projects derived from the institution's scientific research achievements, with corresponding experimental conditions to ensure implementation of the teaching projects
#### Constraints
- Aimed at medical undergraduate students with a certain theoretical and experimental skills foundation

### Target Learners
- medical undergraduate students

### Learning Goals
- basic medical experimental skills
- innovation ability
- scientific research ideas
