# Cardio-obstetrics curriculum design pattern: five content areas for fellowship-level training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article enumerates five content areas that cardio-obstetrics curricula should cover: "cardiovascular pharmacotherapies in pregnancy", physiologic and pathological cardiovascular changes of pregnancy, basic obstetrical modes of delivery, echocardiographic changes and pregnancy-specific normal values, and case-based reviews of pregnant and postpartum patients with simple or complex CVD. It functions as a lesson/unit-level curriculum blueprint for cardiology fellowship education, to be incentivized through ACGME standards, ABIM blueprints, and Core Cardiology Training Statement requirements.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Joint multisocietal guidelines and incorporation into ACGME standards, ABIM blueprints, and COCATS requirements to incentivize training programs
#### Constraints
- Limited time in training to gain competencies; no formalized training requirements currently exist in COCATS, ACGME, ACC, or AHA educational statements

### Target Learners
- General cardiovascular disease fellows
- Advanced cardio-obstetrics trainees

### Learning Goals
- Preconception risk stratification and counseling
- Cardiovascular pharmacotherapy in pregnancy
- Management of pregnancy complications with CVD
