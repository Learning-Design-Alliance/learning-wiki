# CA Mapping Model linking course learning outcomes, assessment tasks and teaching-learning activities

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The CA Mapping Model operationalises Biggs' Constructive Alignment inside SPPA: instructors map Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs) to Assessment Tasks (ATs) and Teaching and Learning Activities (TLAs) at coarse or fine granularity. The article notes that "Instructors specify the course's CLOs and map them to ATs and TLAs in the CA Mapping Model." This mapping is then used to identify students' knowledge gaps and to auto-generate personalised study/revision plans for interventions, and to locate course areas for improvement during course evaluation. Finer-grained mappings yield more detailed feedback and evaluation information.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors create and maintain the mapping; finer granularity is expected to be developed over multiple course iterations
#### Constraints
- Feedback detail depends on mapping granularity; coarse mappings provide less specific revision guidance

### Target Learners
- tertiary course students

### Learning Goals
- identifying knowledge gaps
- personalised revision planning
- aligned course design
