# Four-team role structure with a doubled eavesdropper team and seven sequenced tasks

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The workshop organizes students into role-based teams that jointly operate and attack one shared QKD system. "The students are divided into four teams corresponding to Alice, Bob and Eve (two teams)." Eve is split into Eve-classical and Eve-quantum because she "had to acquire the same knowledge as Alice and Bob, in addition to hacking the protocol." "Each team consists of 3-5 members" with a facilitator assigned to each group, and seven tasks spanning channel building, key generation, encryption, eavesdropping, and message cracking are completed within three hours.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A facilitator per group; pre-assembled and pre-aligned optical components; pre-written programs for the classical channel
#### Constraints
- More students were assigned to Eve because the workshop duration limited what each team could accomplish; teams only experience their own tasks unless time is allocated for exchange at the end

### Target Learners
- pre-university students aged 15 to 19

### Learning Goals
- assembling and operating a QKD system
- eavesdropping on quantum and classical channels
- cracking an encrypted secret message collaboratively
