# Virtual professional development book club pattern: learner-selected books, small-group breakouts, and large-group report-out over two hours

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A two-hour virtual session in which learners each read one of five books selected by group consensus from a suggested list, then discuss in small-group breakouts and report out to a large group, ending with summary and reflection. Learner book choice was used "to augment learner agency and engagement," and multiple books let "the group to learn from each other as each book was discussed." Open-ended discussion questions written to the session goals were used to maximize depth of response. Faculty facilitators read the books and moderated discussions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty time and a virtual platform, which the article names as the major resource requirements
- Faculty facilitators who read the books and are oriented to the session goals
- A book list addressing professional development topics, with participants assigned books according to their ranked preferences
#### Constraints
- The article reports participants recommended an in-person setting for improvement, reflecting the less personal nature of the virtual environment

### Target Learners
- incoming emergency medicine interns
- graduate medical education trainees

### Learning Goals
- professional development
- personal development
- self-reflection
- communication skills
