# Standardized clinical case with SP script for end-of-course oral Spanish assessment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The authors developed a standardized clinical case "outlined by a lead instructor and then circulated it amongst the rest of the Medical Spanish course instructors," with each instructor reviewing content and wording and a final version agreed by consensus. The case includes chief concern; medical and surgical history; medications; allergies; family and social history; and health beliefs. An SP script was created in Spanish and English, and volunteer native-level Spanish-speaking SPs practiced mock interviews with instructors before the assessment. The case and script are released as openly accessible appendices.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Native-level Spanish-speaking standardized patients who rehearse the script with instructors
- Facilitators who are Spanish-speaking physicians with native-level proficiency
#### Constraints
- SPs were unpaid volunteers, mostly physicians or medical students already serving as teaching assistants, which the authors note is not representative of the broader patient demographic students will encounter

### Target Learners
- medical and dental students in Medical Spanish courses

### Learning Goals
- oral Spanish communication with patients
- medical history taking in Spanish
