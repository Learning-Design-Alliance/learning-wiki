# SURGE Symbolic: a disciplinarily integrated game organized around navigating and coordinating formal representations

## Description
SURGE Symbolic is an example of a disciplinarily integrated game that inverts earlier SURGE designs by "layering informal representations over formal representations while organizing gameplay explicitly around navigating and coordinating across representations". The position graph presents information about regions affected by electrical storms and locations of rewards or allies, so "the Cartesian space emerges as a set of scientific instruments for the player", providing access to data unavailable through other means. The Cartesian graphs also serve as an instrument panel or mission planner offering fine-grained control over the Surge spacecraft's movement.
