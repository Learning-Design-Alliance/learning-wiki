# Cyber-Cosmos citizen science summer camp

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A two-week citizen science summer camp held in Pampilhosa da Serra, Portugal, in Summer 2020, sponsored by the Ciência Viva program. Three young students engaged in a project-based learning activity with about 2-3h observing sessions every night, using an Unistellar eVscope digital telescope. The camp combined night-sky observation with afternoon preparation sessions in which students planned observations and collected information about celestial objects.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to an Unistellar eVscope digital telescope
- A dark-sky location with low light pollution
- Sponsorship or institutional support for the program
#### Constraints
- Conducted during the COVID-19 pandemic with public sessions limited by restrictions
- Small number of participants (three students)

### Target Learners
- young students in a summer camp

### Learning Goals
- astronomy knowledge
- citizen science observation skills
- STEAM engagement
