# The Pigelleto Summer School of Physics: a full-immersion residential orientation program

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A four-day (about 70 hours) full-immersion summer school in the Pigelleto Natural Reserve in which forty students aged 16-17, selected by teachers in the National Plan for Science Degree network, study a single physics theme. Mornings give background through participate lessons; afternoons run inquiry-based laboratories in small mixed-school groups; evenings include astronomical observation or problem solving. The authors state the purpose is 'offering to really motivated students an opportunity of testing the scientific method, the laboratory experience in a stimulating context' to orient them toward physics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students are selected by their teachers from a network of schools within the National Plan for Science Degree
- Laboratories use poor materials or educational devices provided by schools, and each group prepares a presentation for sharing results
- Selection of students by teachers in the network of schools involved in the National Plan
- Financial support from provincial administration and foundations
- Inhomogeneous student groups formed following teachers' suggestions
- Involvement of an expert and a young teacher in the laboratory to improve teacher practice
#### Constraints
- Residential setting in a natural reserve is described as positively predisposing concentration; in 2012 no suitable structure was available and the school could not run
- Places limited to a selected group of forty students from third and fourth classes

### Target Learners
- motivated high-school students aged 16-17 from southern Tuscany
- Selected motivated high-school students in third and fourth classes

### Learning Goals
- testing the scientific method through laboratory experience
- orientation towards physics degrees
- Orientation to physics
- Experimental and measurement skills
- Collaboration and communication skills across schools

# Pigelleto's Summer School of Physics: a four-day full-immersion residential orientation school for selected students

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
Pigelleto's Summer School of Physics hosts forty selected high-school students for a four-day full-immersion school in the Pigelleto Natural Reserve on Mount Amiata, combining morning background lessons, afternoon small-group laboratories in which students "are forced to take an active role", evening astronomical observation, and student presentations to share what they have learned. The 2011 edition was titled Thousand and one energy: from sun to Fukushima. In 2011 the Ministry asked regional offices to select best practices, and "In Tuscany, the best practice for Physics is Pigelleto's summer school." The article calls it the most successful laboratory and "the activity more interesting and enjoyable for students".
