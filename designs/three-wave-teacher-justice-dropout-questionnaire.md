# Three-wave teacher justice and dropout-intention questionnaire

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A repeated questionnaire measuring students' perceptions of teacher justice and their intentions to drop out, administered at the beginning (T1), middle (T2) and end (T3) of the school year. Participants "answered a questionnaire on teacher justice and intentions to drop out" at each wave. The three-wave design enabled latent growth modeling of change in intentions and of the justice association over time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administration at three time points across the school year to capture growth
#### Constraints
- Self-report measure with 9th-grade students; perception-based rather than observational

### Target Learners
- 9th-grade students

### Learning Goals
- Measuring perceived teacher justice and dropout intentions over time
