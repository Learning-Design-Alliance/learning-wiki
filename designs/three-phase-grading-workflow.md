# Three-phase grading workflow: preparation, grading and feedback, finalization

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article breaks a grading process into three phases - preparation, grading and providing feedback, and finalization - each with its own tasks, and groups tasks into pedagogical and administrative types. The tasks in the grading phase "need to be repeated for each submission to grade", which is where most grading time goes for open-ended assignments. The pattern guides which steps an automated grading workflow should target.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A rubric, grade sheet, and organized submission storage set up in the preparation phase
#### Constraints
- Most time for grading open-ended data science assignments is typically spent in Phase 2, evaluating submissions and assigning grades and feedback

### Target Learners
- data science and statistics students in moderately sized classes

### Learning Goals
- timely, consistent grading and individualized feedback
