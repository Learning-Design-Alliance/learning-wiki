# Integrate structured, guideline-based dental trauma education into medical curricula

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends integrating structured, guideline-based dental trauma education into medical curricula to improve preparedness for managing traumatic dental injuries. The authors state that "integrating structured, guideline-based dental trauma education into medical curricula may help improve preparedness," noting that formal training was the only experiential factor linked to improved decision-making and that passive exposure did not correlate with accuracy.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Education should be structured and based on current guidelines (e.g., IADT 2020)
#### Constraints
- Findings from a single-institution cross-sectional study; further multicenter research needed to confirm

### Target Learners
- Medical students (fourth, fifth, and sixth year)

### Learning Goals
- Emergency management of traumatic dental injuries, particularly tooth avulsion and crown fractures
