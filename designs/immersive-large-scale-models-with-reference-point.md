# Prefer large, immersive, collaborative models that include the solar system's location over compact table-top models when teaching stellar distances

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q1`–`q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article argues that existing table-top and small classroom models of Orion are 'so compact that a critical engagement with scale becomes very abstract reducing the impact of the model,' and that non-virtual models neglect the location of our solar system, leaving them abstract and hard to engage with. A large walk-through installation with a solar-system reference pole keeps scale concrete and allows discussion and activities inside the model itself.
