# Marketing incubator experiential component

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The marketing incubator is an optional final-exam replacement in a Consumer Behavior course in which "the class is given an opportunity to interact with a “real world” company and aid them in some portion of their marketing function." Student teams negotiate a scope of work with a client, act as a mock advertising agency with the professor as account executive, and are graded on deliverables against the agreed scope, with an informal end-of-semester presentation. It was worth 20% of the final grade, with extra credit available for exceeding scope. Fall 2011 deliverables included event planning, research, a social media plan, and creative planning for print advertising.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A real-world client company willing to identify marketing challenges and act as client; identical class time, syllabus, and assignments for participants and non-participants except the final
#### Constraints
- Participation is elective, so participants self-select rather than being randomly assigned

### Target Learners
- undergraduate business students, primarily marketing majors and minors and arts administration students

### Learning Goals
- critical thinking
- applying marketing knowledge to real-world client work
