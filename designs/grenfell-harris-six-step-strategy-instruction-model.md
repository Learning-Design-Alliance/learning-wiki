# Grenfell and Harris's six-step model of strategy instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article implements Grenfell and Harris's (1999) model of strategy instruction as the pedagogical framework for teaching prewriting strategies in all three experimental conditions. The model proceeds through an ordered sequence of steps: "awareness raising, modeling, general practice, action planning, focused practice, and evaluation". It was delivered in class for the conventional group, online for the web-based group, and both in class and online for the hybrid group, over five sessions with one strategy per session.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivery across five sessions, with each prewriting strategy taught and practiced in one session
#### Constraints
- In this study the model was applied only to the planning phase of writing, teaching freewriting, brainstorming, listing, clustering, and wh-questions

### Target Learners
- Iranian EFL university sophomores in an Advanced Writing Course

### Learning Goals
- Generating ideas during the planning stage of writing
- Increasing writing quantity
