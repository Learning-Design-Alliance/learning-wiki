# Pseudoconstructivist bottom-up approach to game-based learning with student-generated questions

## Description
The article frames its method as a pseudoconstructivist bottom-up approach grounded in Huizinga's play theory, which holds that play is a necessary condition for culture generation and that epistemology displays features of play. "it is from this perspective that we devised the pseudoconstructivist bottom-up approach underlying our method." Students take an active role in authoring and revising knowledge (question posing), while the instructor retains a comprehensive reviewing and amending role, and the exam serves as the prize for good preparation. The authors connect the higher-order skills involved to key notions from Vygotsky's theory of education.
