# Collaboration between multiple organizations is important for the success of intensive school-adoption programs

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article concludes that "Collaboration between organizations is important for the success of such programs", citing the contributions of MARA (coordination), the school principal and staff, UPM (residential college, TESL student helpers), ELS Language Centers (tuition scholarships amounting to RM54000, about $18000), UNITEN (motivational talks), and JUST ENGLISH Sdn. Bhd. (300 magazines donated). This multi-party support enabled the residential intensive program to run.
