# Three-stage blended teaching workflow: online independent learning plus offline flipped classroom

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The RG-UClass model organizes teaching into three stages: pre-class online preview and testing, in-class combination of online lecturing and offline interactive discussion, and post-class online assignments, feedback, and discussion. The article describes this as an "online independent learning" plus "offline flipped classroom" model that eliminates time and space restrictions of traditional education to achieve complementary advantages, shifting students from passive to active learning and from shallow to deep knowledge acquisition.

## Design Implications
