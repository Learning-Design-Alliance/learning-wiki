# Simulated open suturing setup with SurgTrac finger tracking and a time-and-distance composite score

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A previously validated simulation setup for basic open suturing consisting of a wooden casing (PediatrickBoxx) holding an EduStitch suturing pad at a 45-degree angle, recorded by a tablet camera running SurgTrac tracking software that tracked red and blue finger markings. The article states the setup "created a 45-degree angle between the pad and the table, resulting in the possibility to record the tasks with the camera of a tablet". Tracked parameters include time, distance traveled, distance between hands, hands off-screen, speed, acceleration, smoothness and handedness; time and distance combine into a 0–100 composite score with construct and concurrent validity from prior research.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A tablet camera positioned 60 cm from the casing over the participant's right shoulder, red and blue finger markings on gloves, and min/max time and distance ranges from prior expert-inclusive research for the composite score formula.
#### Constraints
- Other measured parameters did not show construct and concurrent validity in previous studies and are therefore not used as outcome parameters.

### Target Learners
- Medical students training basic open suturing skills

### Learning Goals
- Objective assessment and training of basic open suturing performance (time and movement efficiency)
