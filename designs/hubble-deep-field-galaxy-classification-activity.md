# Hubble Deep Field galaxy classification activity

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An activity aiming to have children realize the diversity and quantity of galaxies. Using images of the Hubble Deep Field, students sort bright sources by shape and color, separate foreground stars from far-away galaxies, and count galaxies in a small image cell to estimate a lower limit of the number of visible galaxies in the universe. The article reports that classifying the galaxies was a success, but the counting part proved too complicated for this level.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Hubble Deep Field images and a provided multiplication number for the counting extension.
#### Constraints
- The galaxy-counting extension proved too complicated for 10 yr-old kids, since orders of magnitude and powers of ten are not well understood at this level.

### Target Learners
- 4th-grade students (10 yr-old kids)

### Learning Goals
- realize the diversity of galaxies and their incredible quantity
- practice classification like a scientist
