# Three-phase flipped learning unit: pre-class online tasks, in-class problem-solving, post-class self-assessment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Each learning unit of the two-semester EFL flipped course had three phases: "1) pre-class online completion of TOEIC course materials; 2) individualized problem-solving instruction in class; and 3) post-class online completion of self-assessment and reflection learning tasks". This structure integrates online and in-class tasks in a single learning environment, reserving classroom time for teacher-student analysis of performance on the pre-class learning tasks.
