# Use astronomy teaching as a vehicle for scientific literacy and interdisciplinary work in elementary teachers' continuing education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article positions astronomy teaching as a resource for basic formation, valuing 'seu potencial de letramento científico e seu caráter multidisciplinar', and argues that continuing education lets municipal-network teachers contact specialists and researchers in astronomy. It follows Candau's orientation of valuing teachers' professional knowledge and Harres's call for gradual change in professional knowledge, so the course targets critical reflection on pedagogical practice and the insertion of new practices and knowledge in the classroom.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Contact with specialists and researchers in astronomy, since textbook-only practice keeps professionals 'alheios ao pensar científico' and distant from the current state of the art.
#### Constraints
- A large share of professionals use only the textbook as source of knowledge and teaching instrument, which limits the investigative goals of the national curriculum's scientific-literacy proposal.

### Target Learners
- Elementary-school teachers in municipal education networks

### Learning Goals
- Scientific literacy instruction
- Interdisciplinary connections with mathematics, history, literature and the arts
- Change in teaching and learning conceptions through participatory dialogue
