# Peer-tutored 15-hour undergraduate ultrasound course based on DEGUM curricula with hands-on practice in groups of five

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A voluntary extracurricular ultrasound course for third-year medical students, developed "based on the current national resident course curricula of the German Society for Ultrasound in Medicine (DEGUM)". It comprises 20 teaching units of 45 min each (15 h) over 10 weeks, emphasizing abdominal sonography with some head and neck content. Students practised examinations on each other in groups of 5 taught by 1 peer tutor, with weekly review of theoretical principles and a final practical ultrasound exam.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Groups of 5 participants taught by 1 peer tutor with access to ultrasound devices for hands-on practice
#### Constraints
- The course was voluntary and extracurricular; emphasis was on abdominal sonography with only some head and neck sonography

### Target Learners
- Third-year medical students

### Learning Goals
- Practical and theoretical ultrasound competencies
- Understanding of anatomical spatial relationships
- Radiological cross-sectional image understanding
