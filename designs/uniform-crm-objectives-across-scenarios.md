# Uniform CRM objectives embedded in every simulation scenario across five skill categories

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
While each simulation case has specific medical-management objectives, all cases incorporate uniform CRM objectives listed in Table 1, spanning five categories: leadership (e.g., team members "verbally identify the leader within the first minute"), problem solving, situational awareness, resource utilization, and communication (e.g., consistent closed loop communication). These objectives structure both the scenarios and the debriefs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Scenarios designed to mimic the intense time pressures, rapidly evolving situations and high-acuity illnesses routinely experienced in the ED
- A facilitator who addresses both medical management and uniform CRM learning objectives in each debrief
#### Constraints
- The objectives cover only a subset of CRM skills needed for EM, per the article's limitations

### Target Learners
- Novice emergency medicine residents

### Learning Goals
- Leadership, problem solving, situational awareness, resource utilization, and communication in crisis teams
