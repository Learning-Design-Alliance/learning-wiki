# Alternating live-scenario and video-assisted reflective debriefing cycle for emergency-care simulation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Each training unit pairs a short immersive simulation with a longer instructor-facilitated debriefing: "20 min of live simulation followed by 25 min of video-assisted debriefing". The debriefing "served as a guided reflection on the experiential learning cycle, utilizing video aids to address gaps in knowledge and skills" and followed the PEARLS guidelines. Instructors adjusted the simulated patient's condition during the scenario based on participant performance, then consolidated knowledge in the debrief.
