# Writing and Research course covering 14 skills through five broad aspects of learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The writing and research (WR) course is a required first-year course in the Saudi preparatory program culminating in a 3,500-5,000-word research paper. The instructor emphasized five broad aspects of learning, including "the requirement for cooperation, mutual support, and teamwork" and "the appropriate utilization of technology in order to find an answer." Through these five areas, 14 writing and research skills (Table 1) are covered, ranging from differentiating qualitative and quantitative research types to designing appropriate research tools. Student mastery was measured by a 20-question multiple-choice final exam checked for content validity by professionals in ESL and education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must complete the foundational preparatory year English language program or IELTS level 5.0 in writing before enrollment
#### Constraints
- The study was conducted on the women's campus only because education is gender segregated in Saudi Arabia

### Target Learners
- female undergraduates ages 18-21 with Arabic as a first language in a Saudi preparatory year

### Learning Goals
- academic research skills such as writing proposals, literature reviews, hypotheses, and APA-formatted papers

### Claims

- [Active learning students outperformed traditional-instruction students on each of the 14 individual writing and research skills, with an average 10.5-point higher score](../claims/active-learning-gains-fourteen-individual-skills.md) [+W]
