# The Quantum Curriculum Transformation Framework (QCTF): a four-step decision-tree for QIST curriculum design

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The QCTF is a decision-tree based methodology for designing and transforming QIST educational material, structured as a what-why-how sequence akin to backwards design. It moves from content selection via the European Competence Framework, through targeted skills and Bloom-based learning goals, to teaching approaches grounded in didactical research. As the authors state, "The Quantum Curriculum Transformation Framework (QCTF) consists of four steps: 1. choose a topic, 2. choose one or more targeted skills, 3. choose a learning goal and 4. choose a teaching approach that achieves this goal." Subsections may be used independently, and the framework is intended to be extended as new research results emerge.
