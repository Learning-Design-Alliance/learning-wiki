# Lecture-Tutorial pattern: sequenced Socratic questions with multiple representations completed collaboratively after lecture

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 unmarked) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A Lecture-Tutorial is a two- to six-page worksheet of "carefully sequenced and conceptually-scaffolded Socratic-style questions, utilizing multiple representations of scientific information" completed by collaborative student groups after a lecture on the same content. This new tutorial extends the format to molecular rotations, vibrations, and synchrotron radiation in three sections that build from basic energy-state reasoning to application with real telescopes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students receive a carefully crafted lecture on the relevant astrophysics before working through the activity
#### Constraints
- Takes approximately fifteen minutes of class time

### Target Learners
- Astro 101 introductory astronomy students for non-science majors

### Learning Goals
- emission and absorption of light by molecular rotations and vibrations
- synchrotron radiation and thermal spectra
- matching telescopes to wavelength regimes
