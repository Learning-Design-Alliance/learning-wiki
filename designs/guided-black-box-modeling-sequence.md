# Guided black-box modeling sequence: data organization, representation construction, breakdown, and reconstruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A laboratory activity pattern in which a familiar physical system is treated as a black box defined by observable input–output relations, and students proceed through a modeling sequence in which "preliminary observation and variable selection are followed by data collection, scaling and data collapse, graphical linearization through variable transformations, and construction of an empirical representation". The framing is "guided rather than discovery-based": experimental conditions, measurement procedures, and graphical tasks are provided while the functional form and circuit-theoretical interpretation are withheld. A deliberately engineered breakdown (testing the discharging representation with charging data) makes representation diagnosis and revision experimentally explicit.
