# Graduated scaffolding of strategy instruction through I do it, We do it, You do it phases

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The intervention scaffolded strategy learning in three stages: "I do it, in which the teacher models the strategy by thinking aloud for students", a We do it stage of cooperative group practice with teacher support, and a You do it stage in which students apply strategies more independently with supportive scaffolding. This operationalised the principle that the teacher gradually removes supports so students can practice independently, within the Scaffolding Reading Experience planning and implementation phases.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher modeling via think-aloud in the first phase
- Cooperative groups with defined roles in the second phase
- Independent reading tasks with supportive scaffolding in the third phase
#### Constraints
- The course consisted of sixteen sessions of 70 minutes delivered by the researcher within normal class hours

### Target Learners
- Intermediate and advanced secondary EFL readers

### Learning Goals
- Independent strategic reading
- Transfer of strategies across texts and tasks
