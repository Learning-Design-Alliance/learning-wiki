# Structured 120-minute game-based lesson flow with alternating group, individual, and plenary phases

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article presents a 16-step flow chart (Table 2) for a 120-minute socio-pedagogical activity built around the marble game, alternating game play, referee scoring, flash-card work, clothesline reading, puzzle solving, and plenary recitation. The article states that "the teaching and learning processes for the lesson in which the educational games are used should be planned in detail". The flow allocates 30 minutes to the marble game and 90 minutes to additional games and teaching materials.
