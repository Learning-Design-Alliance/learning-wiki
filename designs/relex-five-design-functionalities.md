# Five-function personalized example feedback pattern: on-demand analysis, similar better example, colored highlighting, dismissible suggestions, reflection space

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
RELEX operationalizes personalized example-based feedback through five interface functionalities: F1 an Analyze button requesting feedback and new examples on demand; F2 a better, similar recipe retrieved per learner; F3 colored highlighting of missing structure, clarity, and specificity aspects; F4 short, deletable explanation tips on highlighted elements; and F5 a Reflection Space prompting self-explanation and comparison. "The five design functionalities of RELEX (see Table 1) are based on design requirements derived from user interviews as well as from literature."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each functionality must trace to at least one user requirement (U1-U5 from ten user interviews) or literature requirement (L1-L5 from example-based learning design principles)
#### Constraints
- Explanations should be short and minimalist and should not tell learners things they already know, per the cited design principles

### Target Learners
- Learners writing procedural texts, including culinary students and study participants aged 18-30

### Learning Goals
- Improving structure, clarity, and specificity of procedural texts through active example processing
