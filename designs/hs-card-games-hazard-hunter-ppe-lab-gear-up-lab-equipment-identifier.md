# Three health and safety card games: Hazard Hunter, PPE Lab Gear Up and Lab Equipment Identifier

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes three card games designed for laboratory health and safety inductions: "three card games were designed with the rationale of supporting student understanding in the field of laboratory health and safety". Health and Safety Hazard Hunter is a matching game for GHS hazard symbols; PPE Lab Gear Up is a scenario-based game dressing a character in appropriate PPE; Lab Equipment Identifier is a matching pairs game for liquid-measurement equipment and its accuracy category. Students played each game for 15 minutes in groups of approximately 3–5.
