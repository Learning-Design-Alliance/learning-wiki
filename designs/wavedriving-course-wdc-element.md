# WaveDriving Course (WDC): a six-module simulator-based DI course with video tutorials, practice, and Electro-Car-Diagram feedback

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The WDC is a 50–60 minute instructional course teaching the Driving to Keep Inertia strategy through a cloud-based driving simulator. As the article states, "The WDC consists of 6 modules, each of which begins with a video tutorial (explaining the specific objectives of the module) followed by a practice period in the simulator." Modules cover controls, speed variations, traffic lights, and springs, with pre/post evaluations; Teaching DI modules introduce an anti-jam distance concept. Participants also receive personalized Electro-Car-Diagrams (ECDs) summarizing speed, fuel, distance, and safety performance across six graphs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A laptop or tablet/smartphone with access to the WDC cloud software and simulator
- A session facilitator delivering video tutorials, as in the classroom administration described
#### Constraints
- Sessions in the course are time-limited (between 2.5 and 5 min)
- The current version uses mouse-position-based pedal handling, which the authors link to unexpected simulator distance results

### Target Learners
- Licensed adult drivers without prior knowledge of the DI strategy

### Learning Goals
- Adopt an inertia-oriented car-following strategy
- Understand the connection between following strategy and congestion formation
- Drive more smoothly and with lower fuel consumption
