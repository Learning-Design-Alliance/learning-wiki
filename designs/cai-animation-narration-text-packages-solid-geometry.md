# Researcher-developed CAI packages: Animation with Narration (AN) and Animation with On-Screen Text (AT) for solid geometry

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Two multimedia treatment packages were "developed by the researchers and programmers" using software including flash 8 and dream-weaver. Both presented "animation of the major concepts in solid geometry and on-screen texts with lesson objectives, examples, illustrations in animation format, quizzes"; the only difference was narration in AN versus on-screen text in AT. The AT package presented words and graphics near each other to reduce cognitive load.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Computers displaying the animation packages for experimental group use over the six-week treatment
#### Constraints
- The packages covered specific solid geometry topics: surface area and volume of cubes, cuboids, cones, cylinders, spheres, hemispheres and pyramids

### Target Learners
- Senior Secondary class Two (SSII) students in Nigeria

### Learning Goals
- Solid geometry: surface area and volume of cubes, cuboids, cones, cylinders, spheres, hemispheres and pyramids
