# Next Level Teaching Blueprint (NLTB): three-stage constraint-first design for Strategic Dialogue

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A replicable instructional design system with three sequenced stages and explicit interaction protocols, described by the article as having "a theoretical logic linking each element to Path 3 engagement." Stage 1 has students engage the problem fully without AI and document reasoning, securing epistemic agency and structurally preventing Paths 1 and 2. Stage 2 operationalizes Path 3 under explicit constraints: students share their Stage 1 reasoning, ask AI to challenge it, and probe responses; answers, summaries, and drafts are not permitted. Stage 3 requires metacognitive comparison of original versus AI-augmented thinking and construction of an integrated, evaluated position.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Assignments must be structured so that Path 1 and Path 2 responses are structurally non-compliant, and students must have their own documented position before AI contact
#### Constraints
