# Three-phase inquiry activity model: Starters, Investigations, Meaning making

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The AMSC instructors, trained through the PDP and using the Institute for Inquiry model, structure each inquiry activity in three phases. In the Starters phase, "Learners notice puzzling phenomena, which stimulate question raising" at three or four stations; in Investigations, small teams of 2-3 learners design their own way to understand the phenomena; in Meaning making, learners explain findings to the whole group and the instructor synthesizes, covers gaps, and summarizes. All four AMSC inquiry activities follow this model.
