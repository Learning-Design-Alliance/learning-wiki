# A-BASE-DE-PROS service-learning project on SDG 12 at the Universidad Politécnica de Madrid

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A-BASE-DE-PROS (grant APS22.2003) is a service-learning (ApS) project run at the UPM during 2022, using "el ODS 12 de producción y consumos responsables" as its central axis to raise awareness of the 2030 Agenda among university and pre-university students. Fourteen professors from three centres and 50 university students carried out the service with more than 600 secondary students from six centres in the Comunidad de Madrid, both public and private/concerted. Activities also implicitly addressed SDG 6, 2, 7, 8, 13 and 14.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- University students carry out real service activities with secondary-school students as final recipients of the service
#### Constraints
- Of more than 700 enrolled students, only 50 carried out all assigned activities, though many others participated in some activities

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and postgraduate university students
- secondary education students (ESO and Bachillerato)

### Learning Goals
- awareness of the 2030 Agenda and the SDGs
- responsible production and consumption
- environmental conservation
