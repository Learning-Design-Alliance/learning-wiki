# A 12-week scenario-based simulation course pattern: three themed units, each following a four-step weekly sequence from introduction to role-play with simulated patients to tested counseling with debriefing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The course pattern organizes clinical communication training into three four-week units — nutrition assessment of diabetes, diabetes education, and nutrition education for hyperlipidemia — each with a two-hour weekly session. Weeks progress from introduction via narration and Q&A, through video simulation observation with small-group discussion, to hands-on role-play with trained simulated patients who give immediate feedback, ending with a tested counseling encounter and a debriefing led by a senior dietitian. The design used Jeffries et al.'s simulation framework with "a gradual increase in complexity to facilitate deep learning".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained simulated patients (senior students or certified community members) performing structured scripts and providing immediate feedback
- Instructional materials developed with senior dietitians and reviewed by experts for content validity
- Examiners with OSCE accreditation and consensus/scoring training for SPs and examiners
#### Constraints
- The intervention was conducted at a single institution, which the authors state limits generalizability
- The 12-week duration aligned with academic schedules but may have constrained long-term skill development

### Target Learners
- third-year undergraduate dietetic students

### Learning Goals
- clinical communication confidence
- communication self-efficacy
- clinical communication competence in nutrition assessment and education
