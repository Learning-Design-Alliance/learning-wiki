# Use project-based learning with immersive night-sky observation to build student autonomy in astronomy

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A student-centred project-based learning approach in which students prepare nightly observation lists, plan observational strategies, and progressively take over telescope operation. The article states this approach "focuses on teaching students through immersive and engaging projects that emphasizes autonomy rather than the more standard teacher-student relation of listen and take notes approach." It was applied in the Cyber-Cosmos camp to build independent operation and public outreach skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Nightly observing sessions over an extended period (about two weeks)
- Afternoon preparation time for planning and background research
- Progressive reduction of supervisor intervention
#### Constraints
- Applied with a small group of three students in a summer camp setting

### Target Learners
- young students
- secondary school students

### Learning Goals
- astronomy knowledge
- observational planning
- autonomy
- STEAM skills
