# Scenario-based simulation session structure: briefing, short simulation, and extended video-based debriefing using the GAS technique

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's simulation sessions followed a fixed structure: "The simulation sessions included briefing (5 min), simulation (10 min) and debriefing (30 min)". The debriefing was held immediately after the simulation, during which students viewed performance videos and analysed them with the GAS (Gather/Analyse/Summarise) technique. Pretest and posttests were themselves conducted as scenario-based simulations scored by two independent observers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Video recordings of performances for review in debriefing; trained observers; scenario materials piloted before use
#### Constraints
- Video recordings were deleted after the same-day debriefing session, per the study's ethics procedure

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate nursing students in intensive care courses

### Learning Goals
- Clinical skill performance for VAP prevention and safe care of mechanically ventilated patients
