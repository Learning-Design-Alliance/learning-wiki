# MUMS multi-tool, outcome-based medical school selection procedure (V-SJT and WAT)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Maastricht University selection procedure is a two-round, three-tool procedure based on a blueprint of derived competencies from the CanMEDS framework. The article states "The selection procedure consists of two rounds containing three tools (hence, multi-tool)": an online portfolio pre-screening, then a selection day with a video-based situational judgment test (90 min, 11 items) and a written aptitude test (75 min, 8 items). Scores are z-transformed, averaged per tool, and ranked for admission decisions. The V-SJT focuses on interpersonal competencies; the WAT covers a broader array.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A selection committee of subject matter experts who translate CanMEDS competencies into measurable entry-level derived competencies and create items with answer keys
#### Constraints
- Validity evidence in this study covers only the second round; evidence should be gathered for the procedure as a whole
- The issue of how constructs and tools should be weighted remains an open question in the general selection literature

### Target Learners
- Applicants to the Bachelor of Medicine at Maastricht University

### Learning Goals
- Selecting candidates on predetermined (inter)personal and cognitive competencies
