# Congenital cytomegalovirus: prevention is in your hands — a free 2-hour five-module eLearning course for midwives built on adult learning principles

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A free 2-hour eLearning course with five modules delivered via the Australian College of Midwives Learning Management System, covering what CMV is and how it spreads, effects on babies, serological testing recommendations, prevention strategies, and the midwife's counselling role. The article states "Adult learning principles were applied to the development and provision of the course", including voluntary self-initiated participation, self-paced learning, case examples prompting reflection, and alignment with clinical practice guidelines. Completion entitled participants to claim two CPD hours.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access via the Australian College of Midwives Learning Management System
- Content aligned with current clinical practice guidelines and developed with an expert reference group including midwives, specialists, a virologist, epidemiologists and affected families
#### Constraints
- Developed for maternity health professionals in the Australian context; the article notes programs targeting other maternity health professional groups should be developed separately

### Target Learners
- Midwives
- Midwifery students
- Nurses

### Learning Goals
- Knowledge of CMV transmission, congenital CMV outcomes, serological testing and prevention strategies
- Confidence in providing CMV prevention advice to pregnant women
