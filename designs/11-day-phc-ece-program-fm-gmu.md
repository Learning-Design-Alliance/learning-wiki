# Eleven-day early clinical experience program in Indonesian primary health care centers (Puskesmas)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An 11-day early clinical experience program, dispersed over a five-week period, in which preclinical students in duos or trios worked in primary health care centers (Puskesmas) around Yogyakarta. Each of eleven centers "hosted groups of five or six students for 11 days", and "PT students were assigned to provide health services, patient management, and health promotion in communities" under GP supervision. It was compared against an equal-time additional skills laboratory program for a matched peer group.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- GP supervisors trained in the school's skills standards; PHC centers with adequate supervising staff near the medical school.
#### Constraints
- Advanced equipment and procedures may not be available in PHC centers compared with teaching hospitals.

### Target Learners
- fourth-year preclinical medical students

### Learning Goals
- clinical skills application with real patients
- community health promotion
- interprofessional collaboration
