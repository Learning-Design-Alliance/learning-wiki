# Split-ballot survey intervention with mirrored 5-item support scales

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 5–10 minute online Qualtrics survey, open two weeks in June 2015, that randomly assigned K-12 teachers after consent to treatment or control. All participants answered a 5-item support for student perception surveys scale (𝛼 = .86); treatment participants additionally answered a mirrored 5-item support for teacher perception surveys scale (𝛼 = .75) about evaluating administrators. Both measures were developed for this study following survey design best practices and are printed in the appendix.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Random assignment via the Qualtrics platform after consent; the two scales mirror one another so the teacher-survey items parallel the student-survey items.
#### Constraints
- Recruited through snowball sampling via NNSTOY; 309 of 407 participants completed the intervention and 279 completed the entire survey.

### Target Learners
- K-12 teachers

### Learning Goals
- Measuring and shifting support for student perception surveys as an evaluation source
