# Journey maps structured as entry, action, and goal for learners and school systems

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The blueprint uses journey maps as a reusable design structure for codifying success criteria. It states: "The purpose of journey maps are to understand and codify the criteria for success by mapping the steps in the pathways journey," with each map structured as entry, action, and goal. Two scenarios are mapped: a learner journey for a high school student completing college and career readiness graduation requirements, and a school system journey for a system creating the conditions enabling that completion.
