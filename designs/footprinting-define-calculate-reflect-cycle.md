# Footprinting as a cyclical define-calculate-measure-reflect process aimed at consciousness of scope decisions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's footprinting pedagogy is "a cyclical process of defining, calculating and measuring, reflecting on the results", in which the goal is not only the correct answer but "the consciousness of the decisions that define our results". When an initial footprint is unrealistic, the instructor surfaces unstated assumptions, returning students to quantitative discussion, more careful problem definition, and refined computation. This pattern highlights the choices and limits involved in process-based carbon footprints and gives students full access to what is done in the computations, unlike online calculators.

### Learning Goals

- practicing transparent quantitative modeling
