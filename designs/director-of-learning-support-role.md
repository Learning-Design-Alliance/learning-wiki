# Director of Learning Support for First Year post

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A departmental post whose "primary purpose of the role is to enhance the department's effective learning environments by identifying early-on “at-risk” students as well as providing extra targeted academic support". It was created after cohort analysis showed many level 1 students did not progress to level 2 physics, and targets primarily students who profess a desire to continue beyond level 1, especially marginal-pass students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The remit of the role ought to be well-defined; if not, there was the possibility of being swamped by requests for help.
#### Constraints
- Support is targeted primarily at students who profess a desire to continue beyond level 1, particularly the ‘marginal-pass’ students identified from previous years' assessment data.

### Target Learners
- first-year physics and astronomy undergraduates at the University of Glasgow

### Learning Goals
- progression from level 1 to level 2 physics and astronomy
- successful completion of level 1 classes
