# Content-intensive 93-hour math PD program combining Intel Math, Mathematics Learning Community, and Video Feedback Cycles

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 against) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q4` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 93-hour professional development program focused on deepening grade 4 teachers' general math knowledge and some math knowledge for teaching. Its core was "Intel Math , an intensive 80 -hour workshop delivered in summer 2013 that focused on deepening teachers' knowledge of grades K–8 math". Two school-year components totaling 13 hours reinforced the content: five structured 2-hour collaborative meetings reviewing math content and analyzing student work, and three rounds of individualized video-based coaching with feedback on mathematical explanations using the MQI rubric.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Summer workshop plus school-year reinforcement components delivered during the 2013–14 school year
- Coaching feedback based on video analysis of lessons using the MQI rubric
- Delivered by experienced instructors (one mathematician and one math educator per district) for Intel Math and trained district-based facilitators for the school-year components
- Video feedback based on lessons on topics covered in Intel Math and the Mathematics Learning Community, coded with the MQI instrument
#### Constraints
- The program targeted elementary teachers' K–8 math content knowledge; it did not improve student achievement in this study
- Evaluated with grade 4 teachers in self-contained classes across six districts and five states
- The report states the PD did not improve student achievement despite improving teacher knowledge and one practice dimension

### Target Learners
- fourth-grade teachers
- elementary school teachers
- Grade 4 elementary teachers of mathematics

### Learning Goals
- deepening teachers' general math content knowledge
- improving quality and clarity of teachers' mathematical explanations
- Deepen teachers' conceptual understanding of grades K–8 mathematics
- Improve mathematical quality of instruction

# Content-Intensive Math PD Program: Intel Math plus Mathematics Learning Community plus Video Feedback Cycles
