# Feedback-based prostate mpMRI training platform

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A custom platform used in the study that enabled standardized entry of PI-RADSv2.1 scores, PI-QUALv2 quality scores, EPE grades, and lesion locations marked as a single T2 slice, with automatic recording of readout time. Cases were accessed via the institutional PACS using an anonymized list and standardized hanging protocol, and "Immediate feedback on answer correctness, cumulative performance, and graphical progress summaries was provided after each submission."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructional videos on platform use and expert readouts of training cases provided before study start
- Embedded references (PI-QUALv2 publication, EPE grading graphic, PI-RADSv2.1 educational resources) accessible throughout interpretation
#### Constraints
- The article states the learning platform may not fully replicate real-world clinical complexity

### Learning Goals
