# Strategy of organizing rhythmic structures through synthesis

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A cognitive strategy the authors name and define for performing difficult polyrhythms that "cannot be divided into equal durations" in standard notation. It has eight operational phases: calculating the least common denominator, determining the units, matching units with suitable notations, classifying note forms, arranging grouped units according to the meter's main beat, identifying the relationship between the original and new structure, doing mental pre-rhythmic synthesizing, and applying the rhythm using the integrated hierarchical approach. Mental pre-rhythmic synthesizing provides preliminary mental preparation, with the rhythm counted in the mind during performance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements

### Target Learners
- undergraduate music education students

### Learning Goals
- performing 3:2, 4:3, 8:3, 5:4 and 3:5 polyrhythmic structures
