# Tales of virtue based on His Majesty the King's teaching concepts as reading material for instilling moral values

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article uses nine tales of virtue, fictional stories based on King Bhumibol Adulyadej's teachings, as reading material alongside DR-TA. The tales "deal mainly with moral lessons, which learners can apply in their daily life" and are intended to teach sufficiency, honesty, cooperation, conserving resources, responsibility, self-evaluation, perseverance, speaking and acting truthfully, and giving. The article reports they make learners feel proud of their Thainess and become patriotic.
