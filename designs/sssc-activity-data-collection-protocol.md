# Two-phase SSSC activity data collection protocol (Qualtrics survey plus Smartsheet workspaces)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The evaluation's activity data collection is a two-phase instrument for retrospectively documenting SSSC services over calendar year 2021. Phase 1 is a Qualtrics survey with 23 questions across five sections covering training events, technical assistance, resources, compliance activities, and grant administration; Phase 2 provides each center a customized Smartsheet workspace for service-level detail. The report states that "Seventeen SSSCs from 16 states completed the Qualtrics survey portion of the activity data collection, and 9 SSSCs from 9 states submitted at least one type of Smartsheet." The interview analysis used a codebook with 33 code families and 208 subcodes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Centers must complete the Qualtrics survey before receiving Smartsheets, which are provided based on survey responses about which services the center provides
#### Constraints
- Participation in the Smartsheet phase was partial: 9 SSSCs submitted training data, 4 technical assistance data, 9 resources data, 3 compliance data, and 3 grant administration data

### Target Learners
- State school safety center staff
- State educational agency staff

### Learning Goals
- Documenting and characterizing the services, activities, and outputs of state school safety centers
