# Techniques of Theoretical Mechanics sophomore course with sense-making on equal footing with physics goals

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The second new sophomore-level course, Techniques of Theoretical Mechanics, ramps up mathematical ability before the junior year through advanced Newtonian mechanics, Lagrangian and Hamiltonian techniques, and special relativity. Its theme is strategies for making sense of physics problems and symbolic problem solving, including coordinating symbolic expressions with conceptual understanding, geometric relationships, and physical intuitions. Sense-making goals are on an equal footing with physics goals and are integrated explicitly in exams, homework, and in-class activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Aimed at students taking or having completed the last course in the introductory sequence
- Taught in winter and spring quarters so transfer students can postpone it
#### Constraints
- Constructed to not teach content required for Fall or Winter junior-year courses, so it can be taken concurrently with them

### Target Learners
- sophomore physics majors transitioning from numeric to symbolic problem solving

### Learning Goals
- sense-making strategies
- symbolic problem solving
- special relativity reasoning with spacetime diagrams
