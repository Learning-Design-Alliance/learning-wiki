# Deliberate practice structure for surgical simulation: acquisition session followed by improvement-and-maintenance session

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article frames the IAD_PLSC's spaced two-session design as an instantiation of the medical education concept of deliberate practice: "the first session is dedicated to skills acquisition, and the second to skills improvement and maintenance". The two sessions are separated by five months, during which trainees have no simulator access and variable access to laparoscopy in their departments. The authors argue the observed 3-mm improvement across this interval "reinforces the validity of the IAD_PLSC training model in two separated sessions".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Two separated practical sessions with an interval without simulator access
- Expert supervision and repeated practice of the target task with objective assessment
#### Constraints
- The interval length and trainees' interim laparoscopic exposure were variable and, in the authors' words, unknown in this study

### Target Learners
- residents in paediatric surgery and urology

### Learning Goals
- acquisition, improvement and maintenance of laparoscopic suturing skills
