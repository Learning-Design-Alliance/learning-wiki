# The Process Approach model of writing as a non-linear, recursive, three-stage process

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article presents writing as "a non-linear, recursive and generative process" comprising pre-writing, composing/writing and re-writing stages, with editing occurring at the end of each basic stage. Its Figure 1 model organizes pre-writing activities (reminiscing, brainstorming, researching, observing, imagining), composing around audience, intention and time-and-tense relations, and final writing with proofreading, polishing and peer-editing. The author uses this model as the treatment delivered to the Experimental group. Errors are expected and are terminated through recursive editing rather than prevented in advance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students receive procedural information from the teacher before the final writing is done
- Editing is systematically carried out at every stage of the work
#### Constraints
- The article reports the approach tested only with senior secondary ESL students in two Nigerian state-capital schools

### Target Learners
- senior secondary school ESL students

- essay writing: content, organization, expression, mechanical accuracy
