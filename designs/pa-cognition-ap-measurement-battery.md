# Multi-instrument baseline assessment battery: sealed pedometers, CNS Vital Signs, and e-asTTle reading and maths tests

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study's measurement battery combines three instruments to operationalise its model variables for primary school children. Physical activity was measured with "sealed NL-1000 pedometers" over five consecutive days, split into weekday home, weekday school and weekend step counts. Cognition was measured with CNS Vital Signs, a web-based battery from which four domains were used: Composite Memory, Executive Function, Psychomotor Speed and Reaction Time. Academic performance was measured with e-asTTle reading (10 questions) and maths (12 questions) tests under a 10-minute limit.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pedometers require children to switch between labelled School and Home devices at the start and end of the school day
- CNSVS assessment requires school computer facilities or libraries and researcher assistance
#### Constraints
- Pedometers do not consider the intensity of the steps or time of day
- The CNSVS measures used do not consider all areas of cognition, and students were not familiar with the assessment

### Target Learners
- primary school children in school years 3–5

### Learning Goals
- measuring physical activity volume, cognitive function, and reading and maths achievement
