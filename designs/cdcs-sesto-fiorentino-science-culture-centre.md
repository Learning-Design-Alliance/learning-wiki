# Centro per la Divulgazione della Cultura Scientifica (Cdcs) di Sesto Fiorentino

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Cdcs is a proposed autonomous, self-managed, non-profit local institution for science communication, described as "ente autonomo ed autogestito, senza finalità di lucro". It is planned as a multifunctional structure combining a scientific-naturalistic laboratory, a multimedia production and diffusion station, a seminar and audiovisual room, a base for didactic excursions, and a permanent mediateca of consultable scientific material open to the population.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A single dedicated structure assigned by the municipal administration, plus collaboration with existing local structures such as the Biblioteca Comunale as attractors and meeting points
#### Constraints
- Planned for the specific territory and institutional context of the Comune di Sesto Fiorentino, in synergy with its administration and public schools

### Target Learners
- citizens of Sesto Fiorentino of all ages
- students of local schools of every order and grade

### Learning Goals
- informal scientific literacy in physics, chemistry, mathematics and natural sciences
- ecological awareness and knowledge of the local territory
