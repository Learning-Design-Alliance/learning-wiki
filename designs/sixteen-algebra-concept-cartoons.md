# Sixteen researcher-developed concept cartoons for algebraic expressions and equations

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The study's instructional artifact is a set of 16 concept cartoons on "Algebraic Expressions and Equations", created by the researcher and three mathematics education specialists and reviewed for content and suitability to students' level. Inter-evaluator agreement was computed with the Miles and Huberman formula, yielding "Concordance percentage was calculated 91". The cartoons were used as worksheets, projected on screen, and handed out as colour printouts during the four-week unit.
