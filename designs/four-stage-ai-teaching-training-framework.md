# Four-stage AI-supported training framework for teaching competence development

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article proposes a structured framework comprising baseline assessment, AI platform training, AI-assisted cyclical teaching practice, and summative evaluation. DeepSeek served as the core AI tool for lesson preparation, lecture script drafting, concept map generation, and interactive teaching design. The framework was implemented over two semesters with five rounds of teaching practice, each lasting two weeks and organized around a unified teaching theme.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty experienced in AI-assisted education to conduct training
- A standardized scoring rubric with calibrated faculty evaluators
- Different teaching topics assigned in each round to minimize practice effects
#### Constraints
- The study was designed to evaluate a pedagogical training framework centered on AI tools rather than to isolate the independent effect of a single AI system

### Target Learners
- First-year prosthodontics graduate students

### Learning Goals
- Teaching competence development including course design, classroom organization, and reflective teaching practices
