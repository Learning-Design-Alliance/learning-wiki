# Use the six BOPPPS stages to scaffold innovation content for novices, moving from authentic problems to evidence-informed solutions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article recommends structuring innovation content within orientation, nurse-residency and continuing-education programmes using the six BOPPPS stages: "begin with an authentic practice problem, state assessable objectives, identify learners' baseline needs, embed small‐group PBL in participatory learning, assess both learning and project quality, and close with guided reflection." This sequence offers novice learners a scaffolded transition from problem identification to solution design, combining the structure of BOPPPS with the exploration of PBL.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Explicit objectives and assessment checkpoints so novice learners know expectations, addressing PBL's demand for scaffolding
#### Constraints
- Transferability depends on local cultural and organisational conditions; in settings with limited institutional innovation resources the model may need adaptation in workload, project scope or follow-up duration

### Target Learners
- Newly recruited nurses
- Novice learners accustomed to teacher-centred education

### Learning Goals
- Creativity
- Problem identification and collaborative solution design
- Self-efficacy
