# Four-Level Instructional Design Framework for e-AV Biology

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The authors adapted Armani's Integrated Framework (2004), which originally proposed three levels of design consideration, into a four-level framework for guiding the design and development of the e-AV Biology website. The four levels are the Educational Context Level, the Requirements Level, the Design Level, and the Learning Outcomes Level. The Learning Outcomes Level emphasizes knowledge, attitude change, and interest improvement in the subject matter, in this case biology. This framework structures how multimedia technology is integrated with instructional design principles.
