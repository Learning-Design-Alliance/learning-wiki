# Whole school oracy culture built through four initiatives: assemblies, coaching groups, e-portfolios, and story of learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A key feature of the School 21 approach is developing a whole school oracy culture. "The Oracy Curriculum, Culture and Assessment Toolkit includes four initiatives to support a whole school oracy culture": assemblies where students sit in the round and engage in interactive activities; daily coaching groups of twelve students and one teacher using talking points and open questions; e-portfolios where students present their best work to a panel of parents, coach and governor; and a 'story of learning' in which students recount their successes to visitors. The evaluation found this multi-stranded approach effective in generating commitment from students and staff.
