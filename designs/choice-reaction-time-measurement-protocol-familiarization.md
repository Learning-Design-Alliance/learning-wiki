# Choice reaction-time measurement protocol using 20-25 minutes of machine familiarization

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An instructional-lab measurement element: students perform choice reaction-time tasks, responding to a stimulus corresponding to a displayed character among random displays, after 20-25 minutes of familiarization with the machine. The article contrasts this with two published approaches: sampling 20 or more people who repeat the experiment over a long time, or taking a single reading after a practice period. Fitted Gaussians estimate each class's mean reaction time (b) and deviation from the mean (c).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Allowing a subject to familiarize with the machine for 20-25 minutes before taking their reaction time
#### Constraints
- Applied to students of physics/electronics in the age group 18-21, where studies report reaction time to be at its minimum in a human life span

### Target Learners
- undergraduate physics/electronics students aged 18-21

### Learning Goals
- measuring and understanding human reaction time in a physics laboratory context
