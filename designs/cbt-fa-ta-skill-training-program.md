# Eight-session CBT-based Overcoming Future Anxiety and Trait Anxiety Skill-Training Program

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A psycho-educational group program of eight 90-minute sessions designed by the researchers, "inspired by Robichaud and Dugas's (2006) CBT program protocol used f or generalized anxiety disorder," and built on a structure of applications, objectives, and practices. Sessions covered the ABC model, an FA-specific anxiety cycle, functional versus dysfunctional anxiety, acceptance of uncertainty, a relaxation experiment, and personal goal review, using techniques such as "cognitive restructuring, goal setting, role -playing, relaxation, and homewo rk." Four counseling psychologists and one curriculum specialist reviewed the program.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sessions were recorded and supervised by three Counseling Psychology experts to check consistency with the program; applicants with high BDI and BSI scores were excluded from group participation
#### Constraints
- The program was developed for a non-clinical community sample of university students; the article notes no instrument directly measures future anxiety for the Turkish population

### Target Learners
- university students aged 21-24 at a Turkish public university

### Learning Goals
- coping with future anxiety and trait anxiety
- restructuring negative beliefs about the future
- tolerance of uncertainty and relaxation skills
