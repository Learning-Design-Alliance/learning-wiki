# Blended flipped-classroom course structure: pre-class podcasts, mandatory consolidative tutorials, then hands-on practicals

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The course was storyboarded by theme and restructured so online pre-class material preceded timetabled face-to-face events: "Mandatory consolidative tutorials and hands-on practical sessions were timetabled after the lecture slides and podcasts had been made available." Tutorials, held just before practicals, allowed the lecturer to adapt teaching to learning demands, emphasise key points and enable student-led discussion, with sporadic OMBEA quizzes. The design was mapped to Honey and Mumford's four learning styles and to Bloom's taxonomy, with online lectures imparting knowledge and practicals progressing to application and analysis.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Expectations that lectures be viewed before tutorials must be communicated verbally and in the course handbook
- Interactive, engaging face-to-face sessions with expected student participation
#### Constraints
- The facilitator is key to the success of the FC approach and must have knowledge sufficient to take discussion in any direction and in sufficient depth

### Target Learners
- undergraduate dental students

### Learning Goals
- applied dental materials and biomaterials understanding and practical handling skills
- higher-order thinking
