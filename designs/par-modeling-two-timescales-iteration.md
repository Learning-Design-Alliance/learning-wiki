# Pairing PAR with Modeling Instruction to iterate on the same scenario at two timescales

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
This pattern combines PAR's small-scale revision cycles with Modeling Instruction's longer-term model refinement. Students revisited the same 100 m dash scenario under constant velocity and then uniform acceleration models, so "PAR provided opportunities for revision on small timescales while Modeling allowed for a larger arc of iteration and revision in the course." Modeling units initiated with open-ended, phenomenological inquiry labs support the out-of-class PAR homework. The approaches are synergistic but can also be used effectively in isolation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Modeling units initiated with inquiry-based lab activities that are both open-ended and phenomenological in nature support the out-of-class PAR homework assignments
#### Constraints
- Both PAR and Modeling can also be used effectively in isolation

### Target Learners
- upper-division undergraduate physics students
- pre-service physics teachers

### Learning Goals
- iteratively improving solutions to open-ended physics problems
- relating model assumptions to ambiguous concepts such as fairness
