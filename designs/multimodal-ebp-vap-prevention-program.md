# Multimodal EBP education program for VAP prevention (lectures, VR, simulation, skill training)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A four-session, one-month weekly education program combining a 3-hour lecture and 2 hours of simulation-based training per session, built around the CDC-based VAP prevention bundle. The article states: "We utilized multi-model training sessions including lectures, virtual reality (VR) and simulation-based active learning." Sessions paired lectures on bundle components (hand hygiene, oral care, patient position, suction, weaning) with VR training and simulator skill practice, followed by case studies and bedside implementation with mentor monitoring.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained mentors recruited with at least 2 years of clinical experience and English fluency to deliver education and monitor nurses
- A skill-based instructional video produced by the research team to standardize training and reinforce learning
#### Constraints
- Delivered in a single tertiary ICU in Bangladesh; rotational nurse assignment precluded a control group

### Target Learners
- Registered ICU nurses in a tertiary hospital in Bangladesh

### Learning Goals
- Evidence-based VAP prevention competencies: skills, knowledge, clinical practice, motivation and work engagement
