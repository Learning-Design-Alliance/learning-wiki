# Scaffolded progressive notebook sequence moving from foundational physics to authentic data-analysis workflows

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The curriculum is "built as a progressive sequence of computational Jupyter notebooks that guide students from foundational GW physics to simplified yet authentic data analysis workflows". Each notebook combines narrative, executable code, guided exercises, and interactive plotting. "The scaffolded structure is intentional as students build enough conceptual and computational footing in early notebooks to engage meaningfully with GW data by the end of the sequence."
