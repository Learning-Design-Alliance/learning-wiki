# Universal mental health screening toolkit: SAEBRS, SDQ, ProQOL, and monthly Kelvin real-time pulses

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
PUSD implemented universal mental health screeners starting in 2019/20, including the Social, Academic, & Emotional Behavior Risk Screener (SAEBRS), the Strengths and Difficulties Questionnaire (SDQ), and the Professional Quality of Life (ProQOL) measure. In 2023/24 the district advanced this approach "by integrating data from monthly Kelvin real-time pulses," which allow "site administrators to customize monthly questions based on their specific goals." Screening and data use is one of the district's Tier 1 priorities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- administration beginning in 2019/20 and data integration processes for site administrators
#### Constraints
- ProQOL is a Professional Quality of Life measure, i.e., aimed at staff quality of life rather than students, as cited by the brief

### Target Learners
- PUSD students in grades served by the screeners, plus staff via ProQOL

### Learning Goals
- identifying social, academic, and emotional behavior risk; monitoring student and staff well-being for data-informed support
