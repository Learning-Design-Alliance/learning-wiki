# Three-phase self-regulation model (pre-action, action, evaluation) organizing intervention sessions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The intervention uses self-regulation theory, citing Maes and Karoly, as one of its central models. Self-regulation occurs in three phases: "a) a pre-action phase, in which the existence of a problem, such as low fruit consumption, is recognized, an intention to change is formed and a goal and action plan are set; b) an action phase, in which people execute their plans ... and; c) an evaluation phase, in which people evaluate their behavior change". Each dietary module has three sessions mapped onto these phases, and performance objectives were formulated per phase.
