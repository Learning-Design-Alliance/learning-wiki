# Three-term junior-level computational physics lab course sequence with Jupyter notebook assignments and computational essays

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
At Oregon State University the authors teach "a sequence of three 10 week single credit lab courses at the junior level", taken alongside other upper-division physics courses in the paradigms program, with two 80-minute sessions per week and most coursework done in class. The sequence teaches programming basics and their application to physics through "a series of Jupyter notebook assignments", with final group projects and individual midterm mini-projects based on the structure of the computational essay.
