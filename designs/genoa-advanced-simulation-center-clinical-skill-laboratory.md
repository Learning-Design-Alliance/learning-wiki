# Clinical skill laboratory programme at the Advanced Simulation Center of the University of Genoa

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A week of laboratory sessions at the University of Genoa’s Advanced Simulation Center whose seven topics were chosen to “contribute to the development of communication and gestural skills”, from venipuncture and bladder catheterization to physical examination and history taking. Doctor and nurse trainers, simulated patients, interactive multimedia simulators, student-built checklists and a video-recorded role-play session with observation grid supported learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Facilities that allow several laboratories to run at the same time in adjacent rooms with a life-like, realistic quality
- An interprofessional team of medical doctor and nurse trainers plus simulated patients
#### Constraints
- In this implementation only the teaching staff was interprofessional, and the students were only 3rd Year students of medicine

### Target Learners
- third-year medical students

### Learning Goals
- communication and gestural skills
- clinical procedural skills such as venipuncture and bladder catheterization
