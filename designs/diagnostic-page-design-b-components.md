# Diagnostic page design B: seven AI-powered components giving layered analytics and explainability

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
Page design B is the article's more informative and explainable diagnostic interface, created by changing design A "in the direction of making better use of AI features". The article enumerates that "The page design B consists of the following seven components: Estimated Score, Analysis of Problem-Solving Process, Skill Proficiency, Recommended Lectures, Analysis of Users Similar to You , Adaptively Offered Curriculum and Santa Labs." Components include a five-skill radar chart on a 1-to-10 scale, peer comparison curves for users at a similar level, a personalized study plan computed from similar users' progress, and pop-up explanations of the underlying AI models.
