# Blended-learning undergraduate MSUS course adapted from the DEGUM postgraduate basic course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A blended-learning musculoskeletal ultrasound curriculum for undergraduates, adapted from the DEGUM postgraduate basics course and aligned with NKLM learning objectives. The training was "segmented into a pre-course period, a face-to-face course, and a post-course phase, comprising a total of 24 sessions of 45 min each", combining Moodle e-learning videos, lecture notes, impulse lectures with live demonstrations, small-group practical training, and assessment via written test and DOPS.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Speakers and tutors had already carried out more than 200 independent sonographic examinations of the musculoskeletal system and received additional training and a briefing in advance
#### Constraints
- The hand was intentionally excluded from the curriculum to keep the course within its intended scope and maintain a balanced, manageable workload

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate medical students from the fifth semester onwards (clinical phase)

### Learning Goals
- Theoretical MSUS knowledge: equipment basics, anatomy, sonomorphology of normal findings
- Practical skills: transducer handling, orientation views, functional joint examinations, documentation
