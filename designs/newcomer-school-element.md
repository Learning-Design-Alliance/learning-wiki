# Newcomer schools as self-contained learning environments for recently-arrived immigrant English Learners

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Newcomer schools are "an alternative, relatively self-contained learning environment specifically for newcomer students," created because "traditional schools are not organized to meet the needs and challenges that newcomer students face." Per the report, "All of these schools serve newly-arrived immigrant English Learners, have voluntary enrollment," and share goals: acculturation to American schools, foundational academic English, remediation or grade-level core content, native language literacy development, and wide-ranging culturally-appropriate academic, health, social, emotional, and family supports. Most are transitional, with clear entry and exit points.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Clear entry and exit points for transitional designs
- specialized supports addressing academic, social, and emotional needs
#### Constraints
- Enrollment is voluntary; most students attend 1-2 years before transitioning to traditional schools that continue English language development supports

### Target Learners
- Newcomer students in grades K-12, including SLIFE and unaccompanied minors

### Learning Goals
- Foundational academic English language skills
- learning-to-learn skills
- grade-level content mastery
