# Level-by-level TPSR lesson strategies mapped to syllabus social outcomes

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Table 1 of the protocol enumerates concrete PE strategies for each TPSR level: a social theme of each lesson, eye contact, and not raising one's voice for respect; team huddles, strategy time, and giving everyone a turn for effort and teamwork; discussing strategy, problem-solving, and students proposing game modifications for self-direction; equipment rosters, coaching and refereeing responsibilities, and a new captain each lesson for helping and leadership; and reflective class discussion plus paired student discussion for transfer.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Strategies are shared with teachers during the co-design process and embedded within existing PE units
#### Constraints
- Examples are framed for the NSW PDHPE K-10 syllabus context and PE settings specifically

### Target Learners
- Students in stages 3 and 4 of the NSW PDHPE syllabus (years 5, 6, and 8)

### Learning Goals
- Respectful interaction, inclusion, teamwork, self-management, interpersonal skill development, and transfer of learning beyond school
