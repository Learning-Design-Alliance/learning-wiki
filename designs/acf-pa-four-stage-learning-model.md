# The ACF-PA learning model: a four-stage foreign language speech learning cycle combining AI technology with corrective feedback

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes a four-stage foreign language speech learning model based on the corrective feedback learning loop (Hattie & Timperley, 2007), comprising "acquisition, correction, reflection, and application." In the acquisition stage, learners receive lectures on pronunciation rules; in the correction stage, the AI tool "French Assistant" provides immediate automated corrective feedback using automatic speech recognition; in the reflection stage, peer assessment is adopted after courses using rubrics covering vowel, consonant, stress, intonation, and fluency; in the output stage, learners complete pronunciation exercises and peer assessment across five learning tasks. The model integrates AI technology with corrective feedback strategies to provide timely, personalized guidance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An AI learning app with automatic speech recognition capability (French Assistant) that provides immediate automated corrective feedback on pronunciation
#### Constraints
- The study was conducted over only 5 weeks, which the authors note was not enough to form long-term learning habits

### Target Learners
- College students learning foreign language pronunciation

### Learning Goals
- French pronunciation accuracy
- Self-regulated learning
- Intrinsic motivation
