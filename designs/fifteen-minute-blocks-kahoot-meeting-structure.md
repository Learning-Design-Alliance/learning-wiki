# Online meeting structure alternating 15-minute activity blocks with discussions and a Kahoot! quiz

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Each two-hour online meeting followed a fixed structure: greetings, questions and curiosities, introduction of the day's topic, then theoretical and practical activities through web platforms or recorded videos. "All the activities were designed to be divided into blocks of about 15 minutes", interspersed with short discussions starting from students' questions, and "At the end of each block, w e used Kahoot!".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Activities must be designed as blocks of about 15 minutes, with a short discussion and a Kahoot! quiz closing each block
#### Constraints
- Described for two-hour afternoon online meetings within courses lasting from 20 to 30 hours

### Target Learners
- high school students

### Learning Goals
- sustained attention across a two-hour online meeting
- reinforcement of the main concepts covered in each block
