## Description
The Teacher Immersion Course is Chicago State University's adaptation of the Colorado LA Model's pedagogy component, created because of cultural, population, and resource differences from CU-Boulder. The article states: "At CSU the pedagogy course, or the Teacher Immersion Course, was created through the collaborative efforts of CSU faculty and local high school in-service teachers." It has three objectives covering student learning processes, creating research-based instructional materials, and creating an active-engagement classroom activity, each with a corresponding assessment.
