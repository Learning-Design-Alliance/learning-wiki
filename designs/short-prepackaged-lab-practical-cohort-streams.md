# Short (under 3 hours) pre-packaged wet-lab practical enabling division of crowded classes into small sequential cohorts

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A lesson-level design in which all time-consuming preparative steps (plasmid preparation, cell transfection, RNA extraction) are completed by staff in advance, so each student group completes only gel preparation, sample analysis, RT-PCR and electrophoresis. The article states the design "needed no more than 3 hours to be completed, allowing classes to be divided into multiple sequential streams", which facilitated cleaning and social distancing during COVID-19. Students were fully prepared before arrival using pre-lab workflow plans and Supporting Information materials.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Preparative biological materials (plasmid, RNAs) must be produced and characterized by staff before the session
- Students must be fully prepared before coming to the laboratory class, using pre-lab workflow plans and presentation of the scientific materials
#### Constraints
- Requires availability of instruments for PCR, described as the most critical facility
- Under COVID-19 restrictions the preparative activities were presented by streaming rather than performed by students

### Target Learners
- university students in Biotechnology, Applied Biology, Pharmaceutic and Technology Chemistry, Translational Oncology

### Learning Goals
- molecular biology laboratory techniques (PCR, RT-PCR, gel electrophoresis)
- SARS-CoV-2 diagnostics concepts
