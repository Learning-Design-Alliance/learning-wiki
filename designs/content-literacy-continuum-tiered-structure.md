# Content Literacy Continuum: tiered schoolwide literacy structure combining whole-class routines with targeted intensive instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The Content Literacy Continuum (CLC) organizes schoolwide literacy efforts into tiers: whole class/whole school instruction through CLC routines applied across classes by content and support teachers, and a targeted tier (Xtreme Reading, Learning Strategies Curriculum, Fusion) providing further support and intensive instruction to struggling readers. The review's structure diagram lists "Whole Class/Whole School", "Content Literacy Continuum (CLC)", "Targeted", "Xtreme Reading", "Learning Strategies Curriculum", and "Fusion". Instructional routines and learning strategies are applied across classes, while targeted SIM adds a daily reading period.
