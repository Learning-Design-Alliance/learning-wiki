# HI-AI collaborative framework for the AI-enhanced flipped classroom

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes the AI-enhanced flipped classroom as building on the flipped model by integrating a human intelligence (HI)-AI collaborative framework across pre-class, in-class, and post-class phases. Students execute AI-assisted tasks including information gathering, content comprehension, and presentation preparation, while teachers design higher-order tasks and provide in-depth feedback, and AI provides "real-time information resources and support for instructional process analysis". The framework represents a progressive evolution from knowledge transmission to student-centred knowledge construction to HI-AI collaborative interaction and higher-order thinking guidance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to free or low-cost generative AI tools and basic digital infrastructure
- Instructor guidance on AI use rules and verification of AI outputs against authoritative sources
#### Constraints
- Tested with international students from South and Southeast Asia comfortable with English-medium instruction; educational systems that emphasise teacher-centred learning or discourage reliance on AI would require tailored adaptation and faculty development

### Target Learners
- International undergraduate medical students in pharmacology

### Learning Goals
- Pharmacology mechanism understanding
- Presentation and knowledge-organisation skills
- Higher-order clinical thinking
