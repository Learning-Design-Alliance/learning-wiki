# Multicomponent intensive reading intervention pattern: phonemic decoding, comprehension strategies and fluency training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention pattern combines three daily-trained components delivered one-to-one by trained special needs teachers over 12 weeks: "The current programme was based on three main elements: (1) phonemic decoding/awareness; (2) reading comprehension strategies; and (3) ﬂuency training." Around 60% of time went to phonemic decoding and awareness, with articulation support for phoneme/grapheme mapping; comprehension was trained by teacher-read texts and discussion; fluency by repeated reading with progress graphs. The pattern targets both decoding and comprehension, following the Simple View of Reading.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered by special needs education teachers trained by the research leader, following a very structured and detailed plan
- 40 minutes of one-to-one instruction every school day for 12 weeks
#### Constraints
- Designed for students with poor word decoding but not poor listening comprehension
- Reading speed is noted as hard to improve

### Target Learners
- 9-year-old students with poor word decoding skills

### Learning Goals
- word decoding
- phoneme/grapheme mapping
- reading comprehension strategies
- reading fluency
- spelling
