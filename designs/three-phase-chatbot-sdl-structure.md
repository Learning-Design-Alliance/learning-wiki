# Three-phase chatbot self-directed learning structure ending in case-based alarm scenarios

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The chatbot program is organized into three phases: the first two deliver SDL content for knowledge acquisition through natural conversation, greetings, emoticons, support messages, photos, and videos, while "the third stage, structured as case-based learning, students can explore and learn about nursing care for situations in which alarms occur during mechanical ventilation." Three scenarios cover high-pressure, low-pressure, and high respiration alarm cases, with feedback regardless of response correctness.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content structured from a prior literature analysis identifying mechanical ventilation learning content; preliminary survey with an ICU nurse and a nursing student informed interface adjustments
#### Constraints
- Scenarios are limited to the three alarm case types the authors built (high-pressure, low-pressure, high respiration)

### Target Learners
- fourth-year undergraduate nursing students

### Learning Goals
- mechanical ventilation nursing knowledge and alarm response decision-making
- self-directed learning
