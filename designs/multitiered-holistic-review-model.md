# Multitiered holistic applicant review model with hidden tier status and an internal review committee

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a two-tier review model in which all applicants are reviewed without overloading the committee. Tier I applicants (GPA ≥ 3.0 and GRE above the 50th percentile) go directly to formal committee review, but their "tier I statuses are hidden." Tier II applicants are reviewed by "an internal admissions committee of three assistant/associate deans who have doctoral degrees in the biomedical sciences," and advance to full committee discussion when at least two of three members consider their applications potentially acceptable. Committee scores are simple averages of member scores and inform deans' admission decisions.
