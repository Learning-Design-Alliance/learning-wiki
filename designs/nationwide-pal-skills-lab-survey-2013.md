# Nation-wide survey of PAL in German medical Skills-Labs (16-question questionnaire, all 36 faculties)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A survey instrument and data collection assessing the distribution, extent, structure and content of PAL programs in German medical faculties' Skills-Labs. Between June and July 2013, "All 36 medical faculties in Germany were contacted via telephone and asked for their participation in the survey." The final questionnaire comprised 16 central questions with open and closed items and pre-assigned checklists, developed by a four-member focus group and screened with three cognitive interviews; data were analyzed quantitatively and by Mayring qualitative frequency analysis.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Contact persons with the greatest expertise and deepest insight into each faculty's PAL program organization and content
#### Constraints
- Claims were obtained only from individual representatives per faculty, a heterogeneous group in occupation and qualification; no objective findings on tutor training could be reported and financial resources were only indirectly inferred via personnel costs

### Target Learners
- Medical students as tutees and tutors in Skills-Labs

### Learning Goals
- Practical clinical and procedural skills acquisition
