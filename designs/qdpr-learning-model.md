# Quiz-Demonstration-Practice-Revision (QDPR) learning model for pronunciation teaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
QDPR is a four-step pronunciation instruction model: Quiz (diagnosing difficulties via flashcards), Demonstration (explaining production in students' L1 while demonstrating), Practice (sound-spelling analysis in small groups plus oral practice), and Revision (remediation individually, in group, or classically). The article states "The medium of teachers' explanation in Bahasa Indonesia was the most prominent characteristic of QDPR learning model." It was designed to pursue global intelligibility for Indonesian EFL learners.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers must be able to explain phoneme production in the students' L1 or a familiar language
- Flashcards of the target phonemes, student worksheets, laptop and LCD projector are the recommended media
- Student teachers attended an upgrading class on pronunciation instruction through QDPR before implementation
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only with learners sharing one linguistic background (Bahasa Sasak speakers) and only on phonemes identified as common and serious mispronunciations

### Target Learners
- Indonesian university EFL students at second-semester level with diagnosed pronunciation difficulties

### Learning Goals
- Knowledge of target English phonemes
- Ability to pronounce target English phonemes
- Global intelligibility
