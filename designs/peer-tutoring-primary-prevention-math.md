# Peer tutoring as a primary-prevention evidence-based practice in first-grade math

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
At primary prevention, the classroom teacher supplements the district curriculum with a peer-tutoring program run "3 times per week for 30 minutes each session." Each session opens with teacher-led introduction or review, then higher-performing students are paired with lower-performing students to work on the lesson. Topics include number concepts, comparing numbers, addition and subtraction concepts, place value, double-digit addition and subtraction, and missing addends.
