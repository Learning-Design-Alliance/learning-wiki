# Three-chapter asynchronous digital educational training on red flag screening delivered on Talent-LMS

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A digital educational training built on the Talent-LMS platform to improve physiotherapists' ability to detect serious pathologies. Content was "separated into three chapters, all asynchronously available": an introduction with five 5-minute videos, three clinical vignette modules (internal medicine, oncology, orthopaedics) each with six parts (background document, vignette, quiz, digital background material, solution, forum), and a feedback chapter. Vignette content came from the authors' previous validated vignette work, and module orders were randomised across six combinations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants must register, sign a confidentiality agreement, and access the online learning platform via an automated link
#### Constraints
- The prefabricated platform fixed the module order once set by researchers, so participants could not change it
- Only three clinical vignette modules were prepared, and participants requested more vignettes of varying complexity

### Target Learners
- qualified physiotherapists registered in Austria

### Learning Goals
- recognising signs and symptoms of serious pathologies (red flags) in musculoskeletal patients
