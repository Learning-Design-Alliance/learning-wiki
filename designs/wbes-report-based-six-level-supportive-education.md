# Six-level supportive education routed by WBES diagnostic reports

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
In this pattern, students first take a diagnostic test through the expert system, which reports mastery per RBT cognitive process level; the teacher then runs supportive education organized into six levels matching the RBT processes. "Each student was taken to the one that he /she was lacking, which was determined in accordance with the reports provided by the WBES system." The sequence took two weeks and was delivered by the same teacher to both groups in the study.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A test instrument aligned to the RBT cognitive process dimension
- A teacher informed about RBT to interpret the reports
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only in eighth-grade mathematics supportive-education courses over two weeks

### Target Learners
- eighth-grade middle school students in weekend supportive education

### Learning Goals
- closing diagnosed gaps in RBT cognitive process levels for algebraic expressions
