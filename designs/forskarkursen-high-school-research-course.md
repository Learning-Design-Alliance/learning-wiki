# Forskarkursen: a high school research course partnering with university research groups

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Forskarkursen ("The science course") is a special course at Hulebäcksgymnasiet offered as an alternative way of carrying out the high school diploma project, in collaboration with Göteborg University and Chalmers University of Technology. Yearly "16–24 students are given the opportunity to apply to the course", selected for motivation rather than grades. It starts months before the university project week with one lesson hour per week, in which students are "trained to read scientific papers and discuss ethic scientific dilemmas", and culminates in a report and poster presentation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must be highly motivated and have the ability to grasp the complexness of science projects; the course requires a university research group willing to host a one-week student visit
#### Constraints
- Students are not selected based on their grades, per the article

### Target Learners
- senior-year high school students

### Learning Goals
- understanding of academic research practice
- reading scientific papers
- applied quantum physics and computational methods
