# WFUMB practical training components for undergraduate ultrasound education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The review enumerates the practical training components outlined in the WFUMB Position Paper Part II: "early exposure to hands-on sessions, exposure to various equipment and patient positions, training in diverse student groups, continuous assessment opportunities, structured scanning as per guidelines, and access to common pathologies relevant to the students' skill levels". It further lists mastering documentation techniques, understanding regular anatomy, and performing both fast and slow examinations as essential, with progression to supervised patient scanning and post-basic access to non-supervised skills labs. The review reports these key practical training elements substantially improved learning outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A student/teacher ratio the WFUMB recommends at 1:4, with one machine for every 4–5 students
#### Constraints
- Staff shortages and limited equipment availability constrain implementation in many clinics

### Target Learners
- medical students in undergraduate ultrasound education

### Learning Goals
- image acquisition, interpretation, documentation, and anatomy knowledge
