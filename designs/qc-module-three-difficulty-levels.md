# Three labeled difficulty levels with skippable sections let one module serve diverse student backgrounds

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course was "designed with three diﬀerent levels of complexity depending on the students’ experience with mathematics and abstract reasoning". All sections and problems are labeled by difficulty level, and intermediate and advanced sections within each chapter are labeled so students and teachers can skip them. Chapters can be skipped while leaving the core content fully understandable, and Fig. 1 outlines minimum recommended prerequisites for each unit for those with limited time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers should use knowledge of students' strengths to guide their choice of topics, as outlined in Fig. 1
#### Constraints
- Given usual constraints on teaching time, the materials are suggested for use after AP examinations, in an extracurricular club, or as an independent project resource

### Target Learners
- high school students with diverse mathematics and physics backgrounds

### Learning Goals
- quantum computing concepts at levels matched to students' mathematical and abstract-reasoning experience
