# Low-cost cerebral aneurysm simulator with hollow elastic vascular model, skull and brain models fabricated by desktop 3D printing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A low-cost surgical training simulator integrating a 3D-printed skull model, a silicone brain model and a hollow elastic vascular model with blister-like dilation bulges, driven by a cam-mechanism blood flow driver. The article states 'A hollow elastic vascular silicone model as constituent part is a feature of the proposed simulator, allowing for blood flow', and reports a first-time fabrication cost of $22.88.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A desktop FDM 3D printer, patient DICOM datasets (CT, MRI, CT angiography) reconstructed in Mimics, and silicone casting and coating over a water-soluble PVA sacrificial model
#### Constraints
- The authors state more effort is needed to 'improve the simulator to cover more of the cerebral vasculature and validate the overall simulator'

### Target Learners
- neurosurgical residents
- medical students

### Learning Goals
- surgical clipping of cerebral aneurysms
- three-dimensional understanding of aneurysm anatomy and its relationship to neighboring arteries, brain and skull
