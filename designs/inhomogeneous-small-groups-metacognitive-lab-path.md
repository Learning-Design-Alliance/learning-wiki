# Inquiry-based lab path with small inhomogeneous groups, minimal initial instruction and metacognitive reflection

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course organised laboratory work in small groups of four with "minimal initial instruction", using inquiry-based activities in conditions similar to schools with few and poor materials. "Groups were formed in an inhomogeneous way in order to stimulate cooperative learning", and each activity closed with metacognitive reflection on which activities were effective and on the teacher's role in favouring active learning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Small inhomogeneous groups to stimulate cooperative learning
- Metacognitive reflection on the activity and the teacher's role after laboratory work
#### Constraints
- Activities were sometimes performed in conditions very similar to those usually found in schools, with few and poor materials and missing or ill-equipped laboratories

### Target Learners
- Pre-service secondary science and mathematics-physics teachers

### Learning Goals
- Active learning in the physics laboratory
- Measurement and evaluation of uncertainties
- Cooperative learning
