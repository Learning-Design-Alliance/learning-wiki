# Molstad's chronological study of Robert Flaherty's films and their criticism

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A study summarized in the same review issue that examined Flaherty's films in chronological order, drawing on "Primary sources contained in the Flaherty Collection and in the Museum of Modern Art Film Library" plus newspapers and periodicals from 1922 to 1951. For each film it covered production circumstances, a synopsis, critical reactions, and the author's comments, with special attention to the four full-length features Nanook of the North, Moana, Louisiana Story, and Man of Aran.
