# Virtual professional learning community (vPLC) for ASSISTments teacher training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The vPLC is a virtual professional learning model designed to connect rural and geographically isolated teachers with peers and learning opportunities. It consisted of eight 75-minute virtual facilitated sessions during the school year, supplemented by asynchronous activities, led with three core goals: supporting effective ASSISTments use following the four steps, engaging teachers in analysis-of-practice, and modeling NCTM norms of excellent math teaching. Teachers responded positively: most (87%) participated fully, and about half rated the vPLC as comparable to or better than other professional development they had experienced.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained facilitators, a facilitation guide, and session structures such as breakout-room discussions with peer teachers
#### Constraints
- Designed for teachers with limited access to in-person PLCs; rural vPLCs are more effective when flexible, interactive, relevant, and ongoing, per the article's cited research

### Target Learners
- rural middle school math teachers and teachers lacking peer networks or in-person professional learning access

### Learning Goals
- effective use of ASSISTments and the four steps of formative assessment
- data-informed math instruction
- professional community building
