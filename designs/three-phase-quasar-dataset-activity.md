# Three-phase astronomy dataset activity using a 200-quasar SDSS/FIRST spreadsheet subset

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A three-phase, 1.5-hour classroom activity in which participants work with a 200-entry Google Sheets dataset of quasar records drawn from SDSS Release 5 and the FIRST radio survey. The activity "required students to perform tasks of classification, identifying outliers, and data visualization, within the context of understanding quasars", alongside data manipulation, analysis, and visualization tasks. The subset "contained 200 quasars (cases) in rows, and five descriptors in columns: quasar name, two angular coordinates, distance (given as redshift or z), and FIRST radio magnitude." Learning was measured with an eight-question multiple-choice pre/post-test.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants work through the three phases in groups using the 200-entry spreadsheet, with learning measured by an eight-question multiple-choice pre/post-test covering dataset skills and astronomy content
#### Constraints
- "The study highlighted only short-term learning and retention in content and skills acquisition of participants."

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students in introductory astronomy courses
- science educators including in-service and pre-service teachers

### Learning Goals
- dataset skills of classification, outlier identification, and data visualization
- astronomy content knowledge of quasars
