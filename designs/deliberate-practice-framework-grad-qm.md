# Deliberate practice framework as applied to graduate problem-solving instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article adopts deliberate practice, defined as "a structured, intensive activity with an explicit goal of improving performance" characterized by clearly defining the skills to be practiced, providing timely feedback, allowing students to incorporate that feedback, and rewarding effort and progress rather than summative performance. The authors operationalized it as supplemental assignments targeting a subset of problem-solving decisions with feedback and bonus incentives.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Skills practiced must be appropriately tailored to students' prior knowledge and specific difficulties
- Sufficient time for feedback and additional practice
#### Constraints
- In this study the approach was supplemental rather than integrated throughout the course, and total deliberate-practice time was low relative to the overall course

### Target Learners
- graduate physics students

### Learning Goals
- expert-like decision-making and problem-solving skills

### Claims
- Deliberate Practice Supplement No Significant Qm Decision Gain [~M]
