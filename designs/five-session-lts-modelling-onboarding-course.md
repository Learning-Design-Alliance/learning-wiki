# Five-session LTS modelling course with structured articulation template embedded in SaaS onboarding

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A short, practice-situated course delivered over five sessions within one month, designed to help learners "view software as a system model" and "apply LTS modelling to real-world software". Sessions combined conceptual input with hands-on activities using product modules at Company Z, and learners articulated understanding via a structured template scored on a four-dimension, 0-5-per-dimension rubric (20 points maximum). Content was adapted from a semester-long university course and streamlined for onboarding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A structured articulation template and complexity-matched pre/post assessments with a scoring rubric
- Examples and exercises drawn from the host company's real product modules
#### Constraints
- Delivered without a control group because it was part of the onboarding program
- Content streamlined from a semester-long course, described as a thin slice of a broader design

### Target Learners
- new campus hires (interns) in a SaaS product company's engineering organisation

### Learning Goals
- software comprehension of large existing systems
- systems thinking and LTS modelling
