# Two-level assessment design: concept checks and matched pre/post prompts with a 10-point rubric

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Assessment is built at two levels: brief in-class concept checks (CC1-CC5) and matched pre/post prompts (PP1-PP5), all designed for paper collection and rubric-based grading without a learning management system or automated scoring. The 10-point rubric allocates 2 points per learning goal, with performance levels defined by the presence or absence of specific identifiable elements rather than holistic impression. Barrier labels on the rubric provide the reverse trace to the documented difficulties.
