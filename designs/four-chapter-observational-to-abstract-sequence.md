# Four-chapter course sequence ordered from observationally grounded topics toward more abstract, mathematically rigorous ones

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The proposed course distributes its minimum content across four chapters (introduction to the terrestrial atmosphere; solar energy; dispersion and transport in the atmosphere; climate) ordered so that conceptualization of increasingly abstract or mathematically rigorous topics becomes gradual. The authors state the distribution fulfils "el objetivo de introducir gradualmente l os temas partiendo de aquellos con mayor base observacional y práctica", which is why solar energy is treated before atmospheric dispersion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Solar energy must be developed before dispersion in the atmosphere so the sequence remains gradual.
#### Constraints
- The distribution is designed for a one-semester (cuatrimestre) elective.

### Target Learners
- undergraduate engineering students of all specialties

### Learning Goals
- progressive conceptualization of atmospheric processes from observational to abstract topics
