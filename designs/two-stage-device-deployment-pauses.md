# Two-stage mechanical device deployment pattern with separate pauses for back plate and upper part

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Both training approaches in this trial taught a two-stage deployment of the mechanical chest compression device: teams "were trained to deploy the device in two stages, such that there was a pause for the deployment of the device back plate followed by the resumption of CPR and then a subsequent pause to enable deployment of the upper part of the mechanical device". This staged structure was common to both arms, so it does not explain between-group differences.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teams practise a coordinated sequence in which CPR resumes between the two deployment pauses
#### Constraints
- Described for the LUCAS-2 mechanical chest compression device in simulated in-hospital cardiac arrest

### Target Learners
- Hospital cardiac arrest team clinicians with intermediate or advanced resuscitation training

### Learning Goals
- Minimise pauses in chest compressions while deploying a mechanical compression device
