# Combine patient-specific anatomical models with mental rehearsal for pre-operative preparation of complex procedures

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article proposes that pre-operative preparation combining mental rehearsal checklists with patient-specific interactive 3D anatomical models may increase operative quality of complex procedures. The authors state these "results suggest that patient-specific preparation with the combination of anatomical models and mental rehearsal may increase operative quality of complex procedures", and that this fusion "does not require the use of a simulator and can be practiced repeatedly in the surgeon's own time using a personal computer."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A mental rehearsal checklist derived from expert practice and patient-specific anatomical models matching the case's variation
#### Constraints
- Benefit demonstrated only for challenging anatomies in a simulated environment with medical students; the authors state the next step is testing within a clinical randomised controlled trial with surgical trainees

### Target Learners
- Surgical trainees and medical students

### Learning Goals
- Improved operative quality and safety in procedures with challenging anatomical variation
