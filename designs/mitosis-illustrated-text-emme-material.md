# Illustrated mitosis learning material (11 pages, 1113 words) with EMME videos demonstrating multimedia processing strategies

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 3 studies (3 causal), `q3` · 2 of 3 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The study's learning material is an expository illustrated text on mitosis distributed across 11 pages with an overall length of 1113 words, text on the left and a complementary picture on the right of each page. Part 1 (four pages) covered cell structures, chromosomes and DNA and carried the EMME guidance; Part 2 (seven pages) covered the phases of mitosis and was identical for all conditions, with all posttest items referring only to Part 2. The EMME videos, generated from an instructed model's recorded gaze and visualized with focus maps, had "an overall duration of 382 s and varied between 52 and 115 s per page (M = 95.5 s)" with no verbal instruction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- To build a comprehensive mental model, learners must process both the text information and the picture information, since text and picture were complementary.
#### Constraints
- Each page was displayed for a minimum of 50 s before learners could continue, and learners could not go back to previous pages during the learning phase.

### Target Learners
- University students without biology, medicine or related fields of study

### Learning Goals
- Understanding cell structures, chromosomes, DNA and the phases of mitosis from complementary text and pictures
