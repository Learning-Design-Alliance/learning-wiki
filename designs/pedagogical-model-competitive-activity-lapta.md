# A pedagogical model of competitive activity in Russian lapta structured into seven interrelated blocks

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article develops a pedagogical model of competitive activity in Russian lapta as a targeted, organized process. Its structure comprises "the following interrelated components: 1) the work -out and study session; 2) play activity; 3) internal factors that determine the functioning of the modeled object; 4) control; 5) a set of criteria for evaluating the effectiveness of training of lapta players; 6) external factors; 7) the conditions for model implementation." Quality of the model is expressed in logical interdependence of its structural elements, correspondence to implementation conditions, and flexibility for adjustments.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Implementation by coaches of a specially organized activity forming an athlete developing environment connected to the national culture of the native land
#### Constraints
- The model was developed for the specific conditions of competitive activity in Russian lapta

### Target Learners
- lapta players of various skill levels and gender, ages 14 to 40

### Learning Goals
- improvement of technical, tactical, physical and competitive performance in Russian lapta
