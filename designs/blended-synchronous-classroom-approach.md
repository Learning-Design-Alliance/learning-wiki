# Blended Synchronous Classroom (BSC) approach

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
BSC is an approach in which "two or more physical classrooms" are "fully integrated together to support online real-time interaction among teachers and students in different locations". It blends multiple physical classrooms with synchronous cyber classrooms and asynchronous cyber classrooms, using tools such as video conferencing systems, electronic whiteboards and interactive response systems. The article applies BSC to bring urban teachers to rural schools and evaluates its effectiveness over two years.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Synchronous tools enabling online real-time interaction among teachers and students in different locations, such as video conferencing systems, electronic whiteboards and interactive response systems
- Teachers who pay equal attention to students in both local and remote classrooms
#### Constraints
- Motivated by the problem that rural schools cannot attract enough good teachers, creating education inequality

### Target Learners
- Primary school students in rural and urban schools

### Learning Goals
- Improving rural students' learning performance through access to urban teachers
