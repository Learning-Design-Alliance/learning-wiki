# Blended-structure communication training unit: asynchronous video preparation plus synchronous simulated-patient consultations in videoconference break-out rooms with layered feedback

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention consists of an asynchronous preparation video on communicative competences and audiology (causes of hearing disorders, cochlear implant indications, speech audiogram interpretation), followed by synchronous small-group work in videoconference break-out rooms where pairs of students conduct consultations with a simulated patient portraying a hearing-impaired person. Observer tasks based on the COM-ON-Check and NURSE models structure peer feedback, followed by self-reflection and feedback from the simulated patient and tutor. The article describes it as "in Aufbau und Dauer vergleichbar" to the prior face-to-face event, with a conversation of about 15 minutes and a feedback round of about 15 minutes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A platform for videoconferences with break-out rooms
- A simulated patient and a tutor assigned to each small group
- A central learning platform hosting the preparation videos
#### Constraints
- The online version was voluntary attendance whereas the face-to-face version was mandatory, a difference the article names
- Technical disruptions were reported by students
- Nonverbal aspects were harder to evaluate online

### Target Learners
- Medical students in the otolaryngology block practicum, third and fourth clinical semester

### Learning Goals
- Communication skills for consultations with hearing-impaired patients, including history taking, explaining findings, and counselling about diagnosis and treatment
