# ADDIE instructional design model guiding the phased development of an AI-based learning tool

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The ADDIE model "consists of the phases of analysis, design, development, implementation, and evaluation" and guided each phase of VEGA's development. The analysis phase informed selection of body systems and disease conditions; design determined vignette structure and feedback format; development covered algorithm construction and content validation; implementation involved student use; and evaluation incorporated quantitative usability data and qualitative interviews.
