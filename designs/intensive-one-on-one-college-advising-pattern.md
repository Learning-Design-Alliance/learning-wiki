# Intensive one-on-one advising across the college application, transition, and first years of college

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q4` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The Bottom Line model pairs each student with an individualized advisor across sequential phases: application advising, financial aid support, college selection guidance, summer transition assistance, and continued campus support. Advisors interacted with students during high school, on average, 13 times during a 15-month period, entailing 10-15 hours of contact time, with support continuing at target colleges for up to six years.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Full-time advisors with college degrees who complete a first year learning the organization's curriculum and mastering advisor competencies; offices in served communities and at each target college.
#### Constraints
- Costs are nearly fully funded by private and corporate donors and foundations; students and families incur no costs.

### Target Learners
- High school juniors and seniors from low-income backgrounds transitioning to college

### Learning Goals
- Applying to and selecting high-quality, affordable colleges; persisting in college
