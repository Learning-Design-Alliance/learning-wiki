# Four-part structure for hands-on AI project modules integrated into core subjects

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's modules for high schools are split into four parts: Part 0 reviews course background material in the context of the module's problem; Part 1 covers collection and exploration of relevant datasets through data visualization and feature engineering; Part 2 covers design and training of an AI model or experimenting with an existing model; Part 3 applies the AI model to real-world situations to obtain tangible results. The teacher controls time allocated to each part, parts can be completed independently, and the module can be divided between different classes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teacher control over time allocation per part; optional pre-written code can condense Part 2
#### Constraints
- Designed for high school modules integrated into core subjects with school-university-nonprofit-corporation alliances for support

### Target Learners
- high school students

### Learning Goals
- applying AI to real-world problems
- core-subject content (math, science, English, history)
