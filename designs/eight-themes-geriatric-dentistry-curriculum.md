# Eight thematic categories organize geriatric dentistry curriculum content: public health, aging changes, geriatrics/gerodontology, oral and maxillofacial alterations, pharmacy, dental practice, communication, and ethical/legal issues

## Description
The review's thematic synthesis produced eight major thematic categories spanning the knowledge, clinical skills, and professional attitudes required for care of older adults. The article states: "A comprehensive review identified eight major thematic categories relevant to geriatric dentistry curricula: (1) public health issues; (2) aging and age-related changes; (3) geriatrics and geriatric dentistry; (4) aging-related oral and maxillofacial alterations; (5) pharmacy; (6) dental practice; (7) communication; and (8) ethical and legal issues." These were later consolidated into six competency domains.
