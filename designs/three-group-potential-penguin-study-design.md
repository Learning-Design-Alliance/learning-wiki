# Three-group controlled study design with concurrent gameplay data streams and eye tracking

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes a controlled experiment with three groups: two playing Potential Penguin (one with changing font sizes, one with fixed font sizes) and an active control group playing unrelated casual games requiring puzzle solving and fine motor skill but no physics knowledge. Measures include pre-post problem solving tests, image labelling tests, gameplay logs (time to solve levels, mouse movements, attempts), PENS competence and control scales, and optional eye tracking of fixation duration on the mathematical expressions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The control group must be equally challenged on similar parameters as Potential Penguin
#### Constraints
- Eye tracking is proposed only for a subset of participants in each Potential Penguin playing group

### Target Learners
- high school students learning classical physics

### Learning Goals
- explicit understanding of kinetic and potential energy and transfer to Quantum Moves
