# Interactive online e-Learning histology module built in the SmartSparrow Adaptive e-Learning Platform

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An online module developed in the SmartSparrow Adaptive e-Learning Platform consisting of interactive activities to learn histology techniques completable in 20–30 min. It incorporated embedded expert videos recorded on GoPro or iPhone 6s, adaptive feedback, drop-and-drag morphology activities, multiple choice questions, roll-over annotated answers, extended feedback from a virtual Biomedical Scientist, and student-led choice of activities. Analytics were available to the academic to monitor each question and adapt activities to suit the learner.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The SmartSparrow Adaptive e-Learning Platform or similar interactive e-Learning software
- Embedded videos of expert technique demonstrations
- Learning analytics for monitoring student responses
#### Constraints
- The module was voluntary; only half the 2017 cohort completed it
- Completion was concentrated in the video group (77%, N = 10 of 13 completers)

### Target Learners
- First-year undergraduate medical laboratory science students

### Learning Goals
- Learning histology techniques such as microtomy outside the laboratory
- Formative self-assessment through interactive activities with instant feedback
