# Blended course pattern integrating a virtual learning space for theory delivery with an intelligent classroom space for hands-on practice

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The blended course design integrated two kinds of learning spaces: a virtual learning space for online digital resource autonomous learning of theoretical knowledge, and an intelligent classroom space for offline entity teaching of practical operations such as video shooting and software use. The design followed the national standard of "Two attributes One degree" and the principle of "student-centred learning", aiming at an efficacious integration of online self-learning resources and offline collaborative learning resources, with homework tasks designed under Outcomes-based Education and mutual peer evaluation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Online resources for theory and micro-class videos; offline smart classroom with intelligent interactive tools (e.g., Rain-Classroom, Xuexitong) for real-time interaction
#### Constraints
- Offline group-learning was implemented in seminar groups of 5–6 students in a smart classroom integrating information technology and equipment

### Target Learners
- Chinese university PE students

### Learning Goals
- multimedia courseware design
- aesthetic and innovative abilities
- information literacy
