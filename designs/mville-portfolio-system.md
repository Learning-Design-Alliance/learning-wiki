# Portfolio System: college-wide advisor-partnered education planning with continuous checks from freshman Preceptorial to graduation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Portfolio System is Manhattanville's college-wide advising framework in which 'the students set out to plan his/her college education together with their advisors from the beginning' and receive 'constant checks and ad justments on their plans through out the course of study'. It starts with the freshman Preceptorial, a one-year general liberal arts course introducing college learning and life, and continues until graduation. The physics major's flexible structural options are designed to fit within this framework.
