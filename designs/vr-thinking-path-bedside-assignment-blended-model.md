# Blended training model combining VR simulation, thinking-path tracking map method, and bedside assignments

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article's blended training model combines three components: VR simulations of oral cancer treatments, a clinical staging-specific thinking-path tracking map method in which nurses draw decision paths on printed treatment algorithms, and bedside assignments providing pre-treatment education to patients. The article states the model combined "VR simulations combined with bedside assignments" and the "thinking-path tracking map method," and reports it significantly increased nurses' familiarity, confidence, and skills.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All-in-one VR educational materials
- Preceptor evaluation of bedside assignments
- Printed treatment algorithm for the thinking-path map test
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only in a small pilot study in a single oral cancer ward

### Target Learners
- Primary nurses in an oral cancer ward

### Learning Goals
- Treatment decision-related knowledge
- Confidence and skills in pre-treatment patient education
