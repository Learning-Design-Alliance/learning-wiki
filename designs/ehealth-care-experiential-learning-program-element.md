# Six-session eHealth care experiential learning program for patients with type 2 diabetes

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 3-month program of "6 biweekly sessions over 3 months, each lasting around 90 minutes," developed over 3 years from a qualitative needs study and refined from 8 to 6 sessions after a Delphi study and pilot. Sessions combined theoretical knowledge with hands-on training and at-home practice using eHealth tools common in Taiwanese clinical settings, including wearable devices (Xiaomi), apps such as Health2Sync, LINE, and My Health Bank, and blood pressure and glucose monitors. Development cost was approximately US $66 including wearable devices and Bluetooth-enabled health monitors.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants need a mobile phone or tablet with an internet connection and the ability to communicate in Mandarin or Taiwanese
#### Constraints
- Developed and evaluated specifically for patients with type 2 diabetes receiving case management services in Taiwan

### Target Learners
- Adults with type 2 diabetes receiving case management services

### Learning Goals
- eHealth literacy
- use of eHealth care technology for daily self-management
- patient health engagement
