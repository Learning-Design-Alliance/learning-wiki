# E-learning system implementing the cognitive representation approach with simulated cases, dual-mapping tool, and scaffolding

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An e-learning system with three main functions: "a simulated exploratory problem context for learners to interact with the problem and obtain relevant information, a cognitive representation tool that helps learners to articulate and represent their problem-solving and knowledge-construction processes, and scaffolding and coaching support". The simulated context works as an interactive virtual patient with case information categorized into patient history, physical examinations, lab tests, imaging records, patient state, and prescription history. A learning flowchart decomposes the process into iterative tasks including performing clinical actions, identifying critical information, recalling and updating knowledge, generating and justifying hypotheses, and making a diagnostic conclusion.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Face-to-face introduction and demonstration videos before independent use
- A sample case for demonstration and pre-study practice
- Domain experts and an instructor to select and adapt cases
#### Constraints
- The instructor was available to provide only limited support during the 4-week self-study period

### Target Learners
- Senior medical students (Year 3 to Year 5) with little practice with clinical problems

### Learning Goals
- Clinical reasoning in nephrology (kidney disease cases)
- Hypothesis generation, justification, and diagnosis
- Conceptual knowledge construction
