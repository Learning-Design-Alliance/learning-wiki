# Fifteen-week digital game-based language learning program at a Thai university

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The studied intervention was a fifteen-week digital game-based learning program at a university in Thailand, in which five students participated. The program served as the setting for investigating how learners "actually experience the use of games" and how gameplay affected their affect and willingness to communicate in English. Its effects were traced through six interviews per participant, for a total of 30 interviews.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sustained gameplay over a fifteen-week period with student participation sufficient to support repeated interviews.
#### Constraints
- Studied with only five participants at a single Thai university.

### Target Learners
- University students learning English as a foreign language

### Learning Goals
- L2 interaction and willingness to communicate in English
