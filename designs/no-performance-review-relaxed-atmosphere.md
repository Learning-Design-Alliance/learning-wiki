# Omit performance review in enrichment courses with hard content to keep a relaxed atmosphere

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Courses at the Deutsche SchülerAkademie purposely include no performance review of participants. The authors state that "the absence of any review contributed tremendously to a relaxed course atmosphere" and that when confronting students with comparably hard contents it is important to relieve them of any additional pressure. They take a neutral stand on whether a final exam should be included, leaving the decision to the intended course outcome.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Course goals must not require certified measurement of learning gains.
#### Constraints
- Without tests, no statements can be made on the gain of knowledge induced by the course.

### Target Learners
- gifted high-school students

### Learning Goals
- engaging with demanding university-level mathematics without test pressure
