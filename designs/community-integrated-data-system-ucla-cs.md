# Community Integrated Data System for coordinating student supports

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
During the COVID-19 pandemic, UCLA-CS partnered with UCLA's Life Course Intervention Research Network to create a Community Integrated Data System (CIDS) that helps three support teams, faculty, and staff communicate about each student's needs and strengths. A high school math teacher reported that access to CIDS shed light on a student's prior challenges and helped her communicate that she needed "additional assistance that was above and beyond my scope in the classroom".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coordination among the School Support, Community Resource, and UCLA Partnership teams working with more than 20 partner organizations
#### Constraints
- Created specifically to facilitate resource coordination during the COVID-19 pandemic

### Target Learners
- TK-12 students receiving school- and community-based Tier 2 and Tier 3 supports

### Learning Goals
- Integrated whole-child support
- timely identification of student needs
