# Peer Education Framework with empowerment, engagement and sustainability dimensions guiding Phase 2

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The study adapts a Peer Education Framework as the implementation guide for Phase 2, in which trained student ambassadors transition from learners to peer educators. The framework "highlights three dimensions of peer-led learning: Empowerment (building advocacy confidence), Engagement (interactive workshops fostering collaboration), and Sustainability (encouraging continuous advocacy in the pharmacy community)". It informed workshop activity design (student-led discussions, scenario-based teaching, collaborative knowledge sharing) and the interpretation of knowledge and advocacy-motivation shifts, and is credited with creating a cascading effect that broadens the reach of disposal education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained student ambassadors who have completed experiential engagement before facilitating peer workshops
#### Constraints
- The article notes student ambassadors reported varied confidence levels when facilitating discussions, suggesting additional training or rehearsal time may strengthen delivery consistency

### Target Learners
- undergraduate pharmacy students (Year 1 to Year 3) attending peer-led workshops

### Learning Goals
- knowledge of safe medication disposal
- advocacy motivation
- peer education and leadership skills
