# MGH faculty mentorship survey with a 10-area mentorship quality score

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 12-page, ~15-minute electronic survey administered via REDCap to 988 full-time Department of Medicine faculty, yielding 553 responses (56.0%). Mentorship was defined as "someone who serves as a career role model and who advises, guides and promotes his or her mentee's career or training." Mentorship quality was rated on a 5-point Likert scale across ten areas, averaged and dichotomized as low (< 3) or high (≥ 3) for respondents answering at least 5 of 10 questions. Outcomes included having a mentor, mentoring role, stalled in rank, and job satisfaction.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Respondents must answer at least 5 of the 10 mentorship quality questions to receive a score
#### Constraints
- Mentorship quality was assessed only among faculty who currently had a mentor
- The stalled-in-rank variable excludes faculty with the rank of Professor

### Target Learners
