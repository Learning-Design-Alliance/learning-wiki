# Just-in-time teaching as a web-mediated feedback loop between out-of-class preparation and classroom instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article presents JiTT as a teaching and learning strategy combining classroom activities that promote active learning with web resources. Its "essential element is feedback between the web-based and classroom activities": students complete web-based preparatory assignments due a few hours before class, submit them electronically, and the teacher adjusts lessons in response to these just-in-time submissions. The cycle recurs several times weekly in short sessions to avoid fatigue.
