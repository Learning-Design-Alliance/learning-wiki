# Sustained, coordinated multi-element program improvement rather than any single quick fix

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The revitalization combined fifteen interlocking elements — academic programs, emphases, honors, dual degree, laboratories, instruction quality, research, presentations, funding, scholarships, seminars, advising, advertising, administrative cooperation, and teamwork — so that each effort reinforced the others. The authors stress that 'No one idea is a quick ﬁx that will work but a s us- tained concerted eﬀort is needed over several years.' Faculty worked as a team, moving to maintenance only after success.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Multiple faculty committed to working as a team over several years
- Attention to every element, since no single element suffices
#### Constraints
- Cannot be done by one person, the article states; financial commitments from administration must be obtained and followed through

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate physics students

### Learning Goals
- Program growth, retention and quality of undergraduate physics education
