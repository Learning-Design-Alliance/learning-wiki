# ML for Physics and Astronomy course materials: a multi-year course, a textbook draft, and a photometric-redshift ensemble-methods teaching unit with published-paper target performance.

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article's named artifacts are the author's five-year "ML for Physics and Astronomy" course, taught at both undergraduate and graduate levels; the first draft of a textbook on the same subject; and an exemplar teaching unit that "discuss and use ensemble methods (in particular, bagging and boosting algorithms) to solve the problem of determining distance (parameterized by redshift, z) to faraway galaxies", benchmarked against the published performance of Zhou et al. (2019). Materials from the most recent iteration are shared on request.
