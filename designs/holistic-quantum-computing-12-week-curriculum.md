# A 12-week holistic quantum computing curriculum spanning hardware, three algorithm families, applications, and introductions to communication and metrology

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course runs 12 weeks with "teaching time lasting 4×45 minutes" each week, following a Gantt-chart schedule (Fig. 1). It moves from computing history and basic quantum mechanics, through hardware for universal quantum computers using "superconducting qubits, cold atom or ion trap, linear photonics and different spin systems", to universal, analog and variational algorithms, application sessions, and a basic introduction to quantum communication and metrology. The authors frame this as building "a holistic view for quantum computing" so students are not biased toward any single approach.
