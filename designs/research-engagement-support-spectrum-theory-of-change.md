# Theory of Change spectrum from passive dissemination to active supported research engagement

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q4` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Appendix D sets out a Theory of Change positioning dissemination approaches on a spectrum from limited support (passive dissemination to a large number of schools, cheapest, least effect) through light support with an individual/classroom focus, to more active school-wide support with a smaller number of schools (more expensive, greatest effect). The trial tested the passive end: "The Theory of Change for the Literacy Octopus Dissemination Trial posits the question whether inexpensive ‘push’ dissemination approaches will have an effect." The null result answers that this end of the spectrum alone does not improve attainment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Testing the spectrum's active end requires the companion Evidence-based Literacy Support trial, which added proactive provider support
#### Constraints
- The spectrum's ordering of effect by support level is a hypothesis; this trial only tested the passive end and found no effect

### Target Learners
- Primary schools and their headteachers in England

### Learning Goals
- School-wide use of research evidence to improve literacy outcomes
