# Remote physics laboratory package: experiment and theory videos, PhET-based virtual experiments, and optional Kahoot! quizzes

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The pandemic-modified laboratory course delivered weekly materials online: TAs recorded narrated "experiment videos" (four to eight minutes) and "theory videos" (six to ten minutes), web-based virtual laboratories were built mostly on the PhET platform with GeoGebra, NTNU Java Virtual Physics Laboratory, and MIT Mathlets also used, and ten optional nine-question Kahoot! quizzes aligned with Weeks 3 through 12. Students followed instructions for simulated experiments, collected data, performed error analysis, and drew conclusions. Quiz answers could always be found in at least two of the laboratory manual, theory video, or experiment video.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Weekly online lectures by TAs and a class website distributing the laboratory manual, worksheets, videos, virtual experiments, and Kahoot! access codes prior to meetings
#### Constraints
- Participation in Kahoot! quizzes was optional; virtual experiments simulate rather than replace in-person equipment use

### Target Learners
- undergraduate introductory physics laboratory students (calculus-based and algebra-based sections)

### Learning Goals
- experimental physics self-efficacy
- understanding of physics concepts underlying each laboratory experiment
- experience with laboratory equipment and procedures
