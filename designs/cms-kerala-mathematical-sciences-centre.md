# A.M. Mathai Centre for Mathematical and Statistical Sciences, Kerala, India (1982-2011)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A centre in Kerala, India that, in cooperation between the two authors starting in 1982, built infrastructure for education, teaching, and research in mathematics, statistics, and related disciplines. The article states that "Until 2011 the centre established an infrastructure for fully operating education, teaching, and research in mathematics, statistics, and related scientific disciplines with continuing international cooperation". Its research and education programs covered fractional calculus, special functions (G and H-functions), multivariate analysis, astrophysics reaction-rate theory, and other topics, and its published teaching material supplied graduate books for the UN regional centres.
