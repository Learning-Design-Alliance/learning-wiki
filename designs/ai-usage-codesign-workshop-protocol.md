# Three-activity co-design workshop protocol for exploring student AI usage reporting in PBL

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A co-design workshop protocol run in seven three-hour virtual iterations with 18 college students, using video conferencing and the Miro whiteboard. It comprises "AI-involved PBL journey speculation, Imagine the ideal student , and AI usage report design", adapting design fiction's alternative-present approach so participants re-imagine past projects with AI ten years more advanced.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants need prior PBL experience and experience using AI tools; sessions require pre-workshop learner-journey mapping and a collaborative whiteboard
#### Constraints
- The authors state that due to the study's qualitative nature, statistical generalizability to other scenarios or populations is limited

### Target Learners
- college students with project-based learning experience
- higher education students who use AI tools

### Learning Goals
- envisioning and reporting AI usage to support assessment of traits such as creative thinking, critical thinking, and efficacy in using AI
