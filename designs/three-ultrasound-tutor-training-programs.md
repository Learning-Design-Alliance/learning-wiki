# Three student-tutor training programs for basic ultrasound teaching: structured course, internship, or course plus internship

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes three ways to prepare student-tutors: a five-day structured faculty-led course (7 hours lecture, 21 hours hands-on training, 2.5 hours job shadowing); a laissez-faire internship of 21-35 days roaming six ultrasound laboratories with a two-hour faculty meeting; and the course followed by a structured 21-day rotation through seven laboratories. All tutors also sat a standardized 1.5-day didactical training. The course gives "full control over the content" and scales to many tutors but limits scanning practice; the internship offers ample practice with low organizational effort but a "factor of chance" in topic coverage.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A standardized 1.5-day didactical training for all tutors regardless of route
- The same script covering all learning goals handed to tutors in every route
#### Constraints
- Internship capacity is limited by ultrasound laboratory capacity
- A combined training carries both the high organizational effort and the laboratory-capacity limit

### Target Learners
- 3rd-5th year medical students recruited as tutors in order of application

### Learning Goals
- Ultrasound scanning technique and curriculum content needed to teach tutees
