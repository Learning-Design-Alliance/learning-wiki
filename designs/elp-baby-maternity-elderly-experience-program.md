# Experiential Learning Program (ELP) with intelligent baby care, maternity experience, and elderly experience

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A semester-long experiential learning program for freshmen nursing students comprising three embodied activities: two days of home care of a real intelligent baby with chip-sensor feeding and diapering cues, 4 h of maternity experience wearing a pregnancy dress simulating fetal limb pressure and 25–30 pounds of weight gain, and 4 h of elderly experience wearing assistive appliances simulating visual impairment, restricted movement, and joint stiffness. After the ELP, students wrote journals and participated in group discussion sharing their experiences.
