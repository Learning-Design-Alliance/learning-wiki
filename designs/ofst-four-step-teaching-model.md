# Online Four-Step Teaching (OFST) model grounded in Bloom's Taxonomy

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
OFST is a structured, team-based, task-driven online teaching model adapted from the traditional Four-Step Teaching of encouraging and sharing. It sequences four steps — teacher guidance, self-regulated learning, team learning and practice, and experience sharing — mapped onto Bloom's six hierarchical cognitive levels so that "The six levels of the taxonomy are integrated within the four steps of OFST, ensuring a scaffolded learning approach". Steps 1–2 build lower-order skills and autonomy; Steps 3–4 integrate peer-assisted learning, interprofessional collaboration, case-based problem-solving, peer feedback, and instructor moderation aligned with evaluate and create.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Online platform supporting synchronous teaching (the study used Ding Talk Online Classroom)
- Trained teachers familiar with the OFST model and its implementation
- Interprofessional group composition with at least two different disciplines per group
#### Constraints
- In the reported implementation the model was applied to a single complex physiology topic (coronary circulation) with limited classroom time

### Target Learners
- undergraduate health-professions students across medicine, nursing, pharmacy, medical imaging and related majors

### Learning Goals
- foundational physiology knowledge
- clinical application and problem-solving
- self-regulated learning
- teamwork and interprofessional collaboration
