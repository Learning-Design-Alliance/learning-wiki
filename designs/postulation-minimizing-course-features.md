# Seven essential features of a postulation-minimizing electromagnetism course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article enumerates the essential features of its approach: field equations are proposed from the beginning in Maxwell's format; Helmholtz's theorem clarifies concepts such as field source and vector magnetic potential; 'The whole theory follows from trivial principles and from elementar y experimental observations'; electric and magnetic phenomenology are always presented side by side, which 'requir es less expenditure of time in class'; Maxwell's equations are unambiguously derived; the formalism extends to other vector fields; and every system of units fits it.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A mathematical review of vector algebra and calculus, particularly Helmholtz theorem, precedes the theory
- Elementary experimental observations on field sources anchor the phenomenological realm
#### Constraints
- The presentation here covers only fields in a vacuum; macroscopic theory follows later

### Target Learners
- senior undergraduate physics students

### Learning Goals
- overcoming fragmentation of electromagnetic theory
- axiomatic economy with conceptual understanding
