# Eureka Institute week-long translational medicine course and community

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Eureka Institute, founded in 2007, is a worldwide community of over 200 fellows trained in interdisciplinary teamwork, open-minded thinking and principles of translational medicine during a week-long course held in Siracuse, Italy. Teaching comprises interactive sessions led by experts including scientific directors, journal editors, industry, and patient organization representatives. "To maximize the learning experience, the training technique requires students to work together to design strategies and solutions to real-life issues previously encountered by experienced TMPs." The network maintains meetings, seminars, social-network sharing, and a database under development.
