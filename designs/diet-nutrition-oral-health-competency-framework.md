# A three-question framework establishes diet and nutrition competencies for oral health care education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The framework defines diet and nutrition competencies for the graduating oral health care student by answering three foundational questions: identifying diet- and nutrient-related oral health domains, identifying diet and nutrition principles supporting those domains and systemic health, and identifying behavioral concepts necessary to support dietary counseling for oral health. This sequential approach ensured that competencies were grounded in disease relationships, supported by scientific principles, and operationalized through behavioral skills. Educational objectives were then organized according to Bloom's knowledge and cognitive process dimensions, followed by behavioral expectations and evaluation criteria.
