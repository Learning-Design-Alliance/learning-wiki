# Seven-phase peer coaching facilitator process for online peer-facilitated learning

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a structured seven-phase peer coaching facilitator process adapted from Schaefer, Fabian and Kopp (2020) and Wolf et al. (2018) for implementing peer-facilitated learning online. The phases are: start and problem selection, problem and situation, vision and resource, collecting further resources, setting goals, solution and next step, and feedback. Each phase defines specific roles for team members and the peer facilitator in guiding collaborative system development.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A facilitator must be selected for each team based on observed programming performance and interest
#### Constraints
- Designed for first-year undergraduates without prior programming experience in teams of six
