# The diphoton framing: entangled photon pairs behave as one single quantum object with one common state

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
This is the article's conceptual framing device for entanglement: two photons produced in one process share a common "history" and are described by one common state ψ which is not just the addition of the states of the single photons. The author proposes to call the pair a "diphoton" to emphasize "that they build one whole", supporting the interpretation that the photons behave in spite of their spatial separation as one single quantum object. In instruction it prepares the conclusion that photon spin directions are not determined before measurement and only show up after a measurement.
