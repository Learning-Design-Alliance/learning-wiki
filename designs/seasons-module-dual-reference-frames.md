# Seasons module alternating Earth-based and space-based reference frames with discrepant evidence

## Description
The ALIVE seasons instructional module follows the Willard & Roseman learning progression and AAAS benchmarks, beginning with diurnal solar motion from different latitudes and times of year before introducing space-based perspectives. "The lecture on seasons intersperses Earth-bound views of the sky from different latitudes with orbital views looking back at Earth, while the instructor connects the two different sets of perspectives in the oral presentation." Discrepant information counters the distance misconception, and graphical markers (cardinal directions, meridian, altitude-azimuth grid, ecliptic line) orient the audience.
