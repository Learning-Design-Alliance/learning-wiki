# Six-part case study documentation format with stakeholder matrices for RAI teaching materials

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A documentation template the authors used to comprehensively document each case study: Overview, Background and context of use, Technical details, Legal and ethical considerations, Stakeholder analysis, and Transparency and explainability. On top of the documentation they built "comprehensive matrices that systematically outlined the benefits, harms (and their possible origins), tensions, and strategies for tension mitigation or resolution between stakeholders," used to design interactive activities. Matrices were left unfilled so learners co-created content, and formal definitions of RAI concepts were deliberately avoided to shift focus from terminology to interpretation within cases.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Comprehensive documentation of each case before crafting handouts and matrices
#### Constraints
- Training materials intentionally avoided formal definitions of RAI concepts like fairness, agency, and safety

### Target Learners
- industry practitioners in RAI workshops

### Learning Goals
- stakeholder analysis
- mapping RAI concepts to benefits and harms
- identifying mitigation strategies
