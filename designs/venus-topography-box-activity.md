# Venus topography box activity

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An activity from the Project ASTRO book showing how scientists get information about a planet's surface hidden by a thick atmosphere, also applicable to Titan. A surface model is hidden inside a covered shoe box; students probe the holes with a color-encoded stick and draw a topographic map of the modeled Venus surface, then present their best landing site in front of the class. The article reports that during this activity the students really act like scientists by getting the data, and then analyzing, interpreting and presenting them.
