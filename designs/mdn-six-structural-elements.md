# Six structural elements underpinning an EDI-focused community of practice in scientific collaborations

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The MDN relies on and promotes six structural elements to advance equity, diversity, and inclusion in multimessenger collaborations: (1) opportunity to go beyond individual accomplishments, (2) structure through organizational principles and tools, (3) training for members, (4) support from each other and for current and future STEM professionals, (5) presence at conferences, on websites, and on media outlets, and (6) legitimacy in broadening participation efforts. These elements underpin monthly meetings where members share experiences and knowledge, learn from others, and seek and provide support. A website, conference presentations, and a submitted Astro2020 State of the Profession white paper lend legitimacy to the often volunteer-based EDI efforts of collaborations.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Monthly meetings of the network that provide opportunities for members to share experiences and knowledge, learn from others, and seek and provide support
- A website and conference presence to lend legitimacy to volunteer-based EDI efforts
#### Constraints
- Some planned activities were paused due to the COVID-19 pandemic, and planned activities have been adapted or abandoned based on member interest and availability

### Target Learners
- Representatives (engagement leads) from multimessenger astronomy and astrophysics collaborations

### Learning Goals
- Advancing equity, diversity, and inclusion in large scientific research collaborations
