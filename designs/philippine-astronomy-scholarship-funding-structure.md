# Philippine scholarship and free-tuition funding structure supporting astronomy students

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
BS Astronomy students at RTU are supported by the Republic Act 10931 (UAQTEA), which "mandates the free tuition and miscellaneous fees in state universities and colleges", alongside DOST-SEI recognition of the program as a priority course under undergraduate and junior-level science scholarships. Scholarship acts include RA 7687 for talented students whose families' socio-economic status does not exceed set cut-off values, the DOST-SEI Merit Scholarship for students with high aptitude in science and mathematics, and RA 10612 fast-tracking science graduates to teach in secondary schools. Table 2 itemizes privileges including P 40,000/year tuition, P 7,000/month living allowance, and book and transportation allowances.

### Target Learners
