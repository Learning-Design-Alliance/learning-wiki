# Four half-day interactive EBP course structured around the six steps of evidence-based practice

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A compulsory, intensive course for family medicine residents comprising "a four half-day training course designed to develop the knowledge and skills required to practice evidence-based care". Sessions mix interactive lectures, workshops and case-based studies around six topics: writing a clinical question, searching the literature, selecting evidence, critical appraisal, interpreting results, and applying evidence to care. Time is set aside after each session for reflection on applying new skills in daily practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Lecturers experienced in EBP teaching, with a health librarian assisting the searching session
- An end-of-course evaluation is mandatory
#### Constraints
- Many topics must be explained in a short period, limiting practice opportunities, especially for question formulation

### Target Learners
- second-year family medicine residents

### Learning Goals
- formulating focused clinical questions
- searching the medical literature
- critical appraisal of evidence
- applying evidence to clinical care
