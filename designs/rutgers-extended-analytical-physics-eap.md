# Rutgers Extended Analytical Physics (EAP) extended course pathway

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The Extended Analytical Physics (EAP) program at Rutgers, running since 1986, is a parallel introductory physics sequence for engineering majors who place below calculus. It works "by removing the calculus prerequisite and adding credit hours to develop PQL in real time, alongside core physics content," and enrollment has grown from 90 to approximately 300 students annually.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The pathway spans three or four semesters totaling 9 to 12 credit hours, granting an extra credit hour each semester for increased contact time and deeper engagement with physics concepts and PQL.
#### Constraints
- The course "does not teach remedial math"; it helps students apply algebra, precalculus, and introductory calculus concepts within physics contexts, introducing topics as needed.

### Target Learners
- engineering majors who place below calculus
- mathematically underprepared students at a diverse urban research institution

### Learning Goals
- stay on track for engineering degrees without a calculus prerequisite
- develop physics quantitative literacy alongside core physics content
