# Four-step lesson algorithm: motivation, lecture, game training, reflection

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Classes held by tutors within the two courses followed a repeated four-step algorithm. As the article prints it: "motivation (goal - to generate interest in the topic under discussion); Lecture (objective - to inform the student); game training (goal - practical development of the material); Reflection (goal - fixing, the analysis of information received)". Each lesson in both courses lists talk, lecture/workshop, training workshop and reflection components with a stated proposed outcome.
