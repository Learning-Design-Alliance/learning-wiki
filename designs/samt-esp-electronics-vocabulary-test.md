# ESP textbook-based vocabulary pre/post-test instrument from SAMT electronics textbook

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The study's vocabulary tests were extracted from an ESP textbook of "twenty short passages on different issues in electronics" written by Manoochehr Haghani in 2013 and published by SAMT. Two passages with the same readability and vocabulary profile were chosen for the DA sessions. The pre/post-tests of incidental vocabularies were drawn from this textbook to measure learning gains.
