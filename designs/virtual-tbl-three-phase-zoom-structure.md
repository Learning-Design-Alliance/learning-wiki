# Virtual TBL delivery structure: three phases using Zoom breakout rooms with remotely administered readiness assurance and proctored summative assessments

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article describes a virtual TBL implementation designed to mirror face-to-face TBL in structure, content, and assessments, delivered via Zoom. It is "divided into three phases": pre-class preparation materials (Phase I), remotely administered iRATs and tRATs followed by breakout-room team discussion (Phase II), and team application exercises in breakout rooms with instructor facilitation (Phase III). Summative assessments used remote proctoring platforms. This structure was applied to all three study courses.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors with TBL experience (the article's instructors had at least four years of TBL experience)
- Teams of 4–6 students kept constant across the semester
- Technology resources: Zoom teleconferencing, ExamSoft or Canvas for testing, and remote proctoring platforms
#### Constraints
- The study's virtual implementation occurred under emergency pandemic circumstances, so generalization to intentionally designed virtual TBL warrants further examination per the authors
- The authors acknowledge possible compromised integrity of iRATs, tRATs, and tAPPs due to student access to prior-year assessments

### Target Learners
- Professional (P1–P3) pharmacy students

### Learning Goals
- Course-content mastery assessed via individual and team readiness assurance tests, team application exercises, and proctored summative exams
