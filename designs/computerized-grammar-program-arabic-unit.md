# Computerized Grammar Program for seventh-grade Arabic grammar unit

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A researcher-designed computerized instructional program covering one unit from the seventh-grade Arabic grammar textbook, built with computer applications. It "includes a set of general and specific unit objectives, new concepts,  activities, educational games, and evaluation", with "sequential steps from easy to difficult and immediate feedback". Validity was checked by university professors and supervisors from Jordan and Iraq, whose comments led to modifications of objectives and activities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Content analyzed into measurable behavioral goals according to the first three levels of Bloom's Taxonomy
- Expert validation by professors, supervisors and teachers, with revisions made in response to their comments
#### Constraints
- Designed for one unit from the seventh-grade Arabic grammar textbook, not the whole curriculum

### Target Learners
- intermediate first-grade students in Iraq

### Learning Goals
- Arabic language grammar achievement
