# Blend e-learning modules with face-to-face seminars and staged assessments for postgraduate oncology education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article presents blended e-learning training for postgraduate health professionals as an approach that integrates online and face-to-face learning experiences and "is getting more popular and improves the participants' clinical competencies through shifting the education orientation from teacher-centered to student-centered." The CCASP implements this through pre-recorded video lectures attendees can use at their convenience, seminars, module tests after each module, and a certification exam only during the third seminar. The authors conclude the program "seems to successfully develop the knowledge of the participants."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- staged assessment: passing all online module tests is required before eligibility for the certification exam
- pre-recorded lectures plus a chat function so participants can ask lecturers questions
#### Constraints
- the article's conclusion is a descriptive appraisal of one program's eight-year experience, not a controlled comparison of blended versus other formats

### Target Learners
- postgraduate physicians and scientists in oncology

### Learning Goals
- updated comprehensive management of breast cancer, lymphoma, and lung cancer
