# Online diabetes CME intervention package: Active Learning Module, specialist advice access, and town-based and comparative performance feedback

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention combined an online Active Learning Module of approximately 6 h of learning activity carrying continuing professional development points, an online request form for diabetes specialist advice, and town-based and comparative performance feedback derived from de-identified pathology data. The ALM included "evidence-based Australian clinical guidelines, video demonstrations, case studies, knowledge-based quizzes, clinical audit, self-reflection activities and a moderated peer discussion forum" and was accessible on any Internet-enabled device at any time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- GPs needed to complete the whole ALM to receive professional development points
- Feedback depended on de-identified data provided by local pathology laboratories
#### Constraints
- The specialist advice mechanism was never used in this trial
- A major limitation was a lack of data regarding the availability and uptake of other online or face-to-face learning opportunities during the study period

### Target Learners
- Rural and remote general practitioners in Australia

### Learning Goals
- Prerequisite knowledge for optimal primary care management of type 2 diabetes and skills practice in the practice setting
