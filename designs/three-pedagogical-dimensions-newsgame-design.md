# Three pedagogical dimensions of Newsgame design activities

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
From their observations the authors identified three dimensions that seem interesting from a pedagogical point of view: development of an approach for addressing a current event supported by journalistic information sources, exchange of points of view using video games as a medium for discussion, and organization of student debates on the validity of video games as media for discussing current events. They define reasoning skills as documenting a subject to develop a point of view, then comparing, contrasting, and defending it against other people's views.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learners must document their subject using journalistic resources available on the web so the information can be used in the game and in debate.
#### Constraints
- The authors observed that different learners do not subsume these three academic dimensions in the same way.

### Target Learners
- engineering students in higher education

### Learning Goals
- reasoning skills
- media education
- knowledge of current events
