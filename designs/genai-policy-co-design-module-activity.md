# GenAI policy co-design module: guided inquiry activity with curated resources, individual policy design, and group discussion

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A 3-4-week course module on GenAI in education within an undergraduate course, Technology and the Global Society. Students received curated resources (readings, videos, reports, policies), used GenAI to brainstorm policy ideas, wrote an individual policy detailing at least five justified elements, then joined a group discussion of 4-6 peers lasting approximately 45 minutes to develop a collective policy, followed by written reflection. "The module culminated in a group discussion, with prior activities designed to prepare students for it." Each policy element needed to be justified with evidence.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Curated starter resources provided in the first week
- Individual writing assignment with feedback before group discussion
- Reflection debrief and written assignment after discussion
#### Constraints
- The module assumed students would have access to GenAI tools; the activity was both a graded assignment and a source of research data, a dual role the authors note may have influenced student responses

### Target Learners
- junior and senior undergraduate information technology and cybersecurity students

### Learning Goals
- understanding how GenAI functions
- ethical and policy reasoning about AI use
- multi-stakeholder policy design
