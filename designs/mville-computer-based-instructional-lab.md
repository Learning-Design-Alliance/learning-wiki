# Computer-based instructional laboratory with interface-bundle stations for data acquisition and simulated advanced experiments

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A computer-based instructional laboratory component in which students engage in 'computer-based data acquisition and analysis' and perform 'computer simulated intermediate and advanced experiments otherwise not accessible to them'. The setup comprises three laboratory stations, each with a PASCO physics (USB) 750 interface bundle and a Dell small-form desktop computer, to be fully integrated into the curriculum starting fall 2004 with students performing experiments in rotation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Laboratory stations equipped with computer interface bundles and desktop computers, scheduled so students perform experiments using them in rotation.
#### Constraints
- Computer-simulated experiments stand in for intermediate and advanced experiments otherwise not accessible to students at the institution.

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate physics students in introductory through advanced laboratory instruction

### Learning Goals
- Experimental training in data acquisition and analysis
- Access to intermediate and advanced experiments beyond the institution's conventional laboratory capacity
