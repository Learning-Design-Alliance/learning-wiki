# Recruit mostly female volunteers and cap at 2-3 per room so girls find role models without classes becoming less interactive

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article recommends recruiting volunteers early from mathematics and physics departments, with volunteers usually female students; male volunteers are assigned non-teaching tasks, "trying to maximise the opportunities for the young girls to find role models amongst the volunteers". It also warns that "too many volunteers can make the participants shy and the classes less interactive", and that "2 to 3 volunteers per room is ideal".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Volunteers recruited from graduate and undergraduate communities in mathematics and physics departments
- Universities typically require background checks for anyone working with minors
#### Constraints
- Too many volunteers can make participants shy and classes less interactive

### Target Learners
- female students aged 7-17

### Learning Goals
