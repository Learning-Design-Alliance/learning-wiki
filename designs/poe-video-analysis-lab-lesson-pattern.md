# Predict-observe-explain worksheet-guided video analysis lesson

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The lesson pattern uses two 75-minute computer laboratory lessons in which a worksheet guides students through the Tracker software and prompts them to "predict, observe explain" the toss-up motion, letting students gain personal experience of the physics. A teacher demonstrates the tool with a projector while another teacher circulates to support hands-on computer activity, connecting real-life video to scientific graphs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A customized worksheet with steps to open Tracker and load the video
- Teacher's computer and laboratory projector for demonstration
- A second teacher available to support issues arising during hands-on computer activities
#### Constraints
- Students new to the tool spent considerable time setting up the video, which one student noted could reduce effective learning time

### Target Learners
- secondary school physics students (age 15, secondary three)

### Learning Goals
- relating abstract physics concepts to real life
- understanding free fall and toss-up kinematics graphs
