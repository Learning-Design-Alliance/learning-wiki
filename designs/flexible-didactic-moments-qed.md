# Flexible didactic moments: a sequence divided into adaptable units the teacher can rearrange

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The teaching sequence is structured as a set of malleable didactic moments rather than a fixed course. The article states that "o presente trabalho já está estruturado de maneira propícia a essa abordagem, por estar dividido em propostas de momentos didáticos maleáveis, adaptáveis aos propósitos do(a) docente". This answers the predictable criticism that limited physics course load prevents adding topics beyond qualifying-exam preparation, since moments can be woven into lessons on electromagnetism, photons or the photoelectric effect.
