# Four-activity VR training arc: onboarding, avatar history-taking, algorithm tutorial, then a stressor-laden timed assessment with detailed feedback and unlimited repetition

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The VR package is structured as four distinct activities within the virtual environment. Onboarding teaches controller interactions and movement; history taking involves a verbal conversation with an AI avatar of the patient's mother to gather information such as tracheostomy insertion date, last suction, allergies, and fasting status; the algorithm tutorial is a stress-free guided, step-by-step walkthrough aligned with the national algorithm card; and the algorithm simulation assessment exposes the user to an emergency with added triggers to simulate high pressure conditions. After assessment, "detailed feedback is provided including the number and sequence of steps performed along with the time taken", and users can repeat the tutorial and assessment until they feel competent.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An AI avatar capable of verbal question-and-response interaction for the history-taking activity
- Assistive prompts in the tutorial and their removal in the assessment, with a timeframe for the assessment
#### Constraints
- The tutorial scene contains no elements of stress; stressors are introduced only in the assessment section

### Target Learners
- Healthcare staff
- Healthcare students

### Learning Goals
- Following the NTSP blocked tracheostomy emergency algorithm under time pressure
- Taking a relevant clinical history to determine whether the procedure can proceed
