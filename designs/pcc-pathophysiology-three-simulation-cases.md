# Three representative fellow-developed simulation cases: hepatic encephalopathy, sepsis with coagulopathy and AKI, and status epilepticus

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
This element is the set of three illustrative simulation cases in the curriculum. Each "covered a specific pathophysiologic process" and required participants to acutely manage an 18-year-old with hepatic encephalopathy, an 8-year-old with sepsis, coagulopathy, and acute kidney injury, or a 12-year-old with status epilepticus. Cases were developed by fellows of different years, run with a low- or high-fidelity mannequin, and each could be conducted in a simulation suite or an acute care unit bed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A low- or high-fidelity mannequin, monitor, code cart with resuscitation equipment, a simulation technician, and at least one faculty member, as listed under Equipment/Environment and Personnel
#### Constraints
- Each case was used only once within the 2018–2019 academic year, and fellow participants varied depending on clinical responsibilities

### Target Learners
- pediatric critical care fellows

### Learning Goals
- identify and treat cerebral edema in hepatic encephalopathy
- develop a differential diagnosis and treatment plan for coagulopathy in sepsis
- manage airway, breathing, and circulation in status epilepticus
