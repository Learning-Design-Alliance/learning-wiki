# Blended-learning diabetic foot course with online platform and hands-on clinical training for Primary Care nurses

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A blended-learning training course promoted by the SMS Professional Development Unit and UDICA, open to nursing professionals and family and community nursing residents across all SMS health areas. The online component on the IDEA platform included "theoretical content, assessment activities, and educational dynamics such as an interactive 'The Alphabet Game' – style game". The in-person component provided hands-on training in vascular assessment, neurological assessment, visual foot inspection, referral criteria, and clinical case resolution involving active ulcers, allowing practical evaluation of acquired competencies.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Voluntary enrolment via institutional e-mail and intranet; the questionnaire was administered as a diagnostic activity prior to the training phase
#### Constraints
- Participation was linked to a voluntary training course, which the authors state could imply a sample already motivated by or interested in the topic

### Target Learners
- Primary Care nurses
- family and community nursing residents

### Learning Goals
- diabetic foot examination and assessment skills
- dressing selection and wound care
- referral criteria
