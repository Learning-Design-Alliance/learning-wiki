# Revised heavy ion facility emergency response protocol with emergency buttons and monitoring systems

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The project produced a revised emergency response protocol for the heavy ion facility, illustrated in figure 2, in which "the emergency button is used to gather the first responders, ensure rapid activation of the MET call from the facility's reception desk and coordinate the MET and patient transport." Supporting equipment included emergency buttons throughout the building sounding alarms at the reception staff station, antennas for monitoring devices, a central monitor in the operator's room, and a monitoring information-sharing system using tablet terminals. The protocol and equipment were confirmed to work in both simulation sessions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Emergency buttons placed throughout the building
- cooperation between the ICU and the emergency department
- identification of the shortest ICU-to-facility route
#### Constraints
- Electronic equipment use in the treatment room is limited because of neutron emissions from the treatment beam

### Target Learners
- GHMC staff including receptionists, physicists, technicians, nurses and physicians

### Learning Goals
- rapid emergency detection and MET activation
- coordinated patient transport
