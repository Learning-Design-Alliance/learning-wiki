# New Zealand Medical Student Journal (NZMSJ) as a venue for student-authored research and early peer-review experience

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The NZMSJ is a student-run medical journal publishing original contributions (research papers, review articles, case reports) and non-original contributions (feature/perspective articles, book/media reviews, conference reports) written by New Zealand medical students. It "employs a double-blind peer-review process undertaken by a combination of student and expert reviewers" and is indexed in Google Scholar, having published over 300 articles since its 2003 launch. This study evaluated its association with graduates' later academic outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Student authors identified via author biographies; double-blind peer review by student and expert reviewers
#### Constraints
- Predominantly caters for medical students from New Zealand, which the authors state limits generalizability of findings to other cohorts and MSJs

### Target Learners
- medical students in New Zealand

### Learning Goals
- academic publishing experience
- peer-review familiarisation
- development of research-related skills
