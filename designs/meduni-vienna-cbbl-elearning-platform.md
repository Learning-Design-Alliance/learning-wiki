# Case-based blended eLearning (CBBL) platform with over 100 anonymized clinical cases

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An interactive, interdisciplinary e-learning platform at the Medical University of Vienna into which anonymized real-life patient cases are transferred from hospital information management into a Moodle database. The framework consists of "A) case based textbook material, B) online e‑CBL with question driven learning scenarios and C) simulated patient (SP) contact seminars". "Over 100 different cases" across specialties from internal medicine to psychiatry are offered, with varying complexity and learning objectives, aligned with Dublin descriptors and the CanMED framework.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Anonymized real-life patient cases supplied by teachers and students
- an e-learning platform database
- alignment with competence frameworks such as Dublin descriptors and CanMED
#### Constraints
- Implemented in the Vienna medical curriculum; the article notes the advantage of case-based learning is underrated and research on student attitudes, knowledge and competencies is missing

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students
- postgraduates

### Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning
- differential diagnosis
- interdisciplinary competence development
