# Random Problem-Based Learning: random teams solving randomly selected problems within a blended lecture-plus-PBL course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
RPBL is a course-level learning management system for Vector Dynamics that blends traditional lecturing with PBL. Its defining feature is that "a random selection of 3-4 students to form teams to solve a randomly selected problem during examinations" occurs on exam day, with desks rearranged as learning centers. Student polls rate problem difficulty, and teams present problems orally at semester's end.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A problem set with exercises rated by difficulty (easy, intermediate, hard) and enough exercises per set to attain perceived fairness across students
#### Constraints
- Students expressed discomfort with random group selection and prefer choosing their own teammates

### Target Learners
- sophomore non-mechanical engineering majors in a Vector Dynamics course

### Learning Goals
- rigid-body dynamics problem-solving skills
- peer cooperative learning
