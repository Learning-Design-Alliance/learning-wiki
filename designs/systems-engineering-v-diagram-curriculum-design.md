## Description
The article adapts the classic systems-engineering V-diagram to curriculum development, treating the degree as an integrated system with verifiable performance. It is a requirements-driven methodology: "you must understand what you want to achieve, first and foremost, before considering how you might achieve it". Design proceeds from concept of operations and system-level requirements through high-level and component-level design, then verification and validation at increasing levels of complexity, with iteration and continuous improvement rather than a strictly ordered process.
