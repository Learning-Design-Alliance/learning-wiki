# Instructional Mode for teaching tense forms with the Comprehensive Charts

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A circling classroom sequence appended to the article (Appendix 4, Figure 1) consisting of "four classroom teaching activities with three teaching approaches which are the grammar-translation, the cooperative learning (the Tic Tae Toe game), and the communicative approaches, as well as four practices/tests". Teaching of active and passive tenses is divided into two sections to avoid confusing learners, and the mode interchanges teaching with practice/test so users may take a second round. It integrates reading, listening, speaking, and writing practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sufficient practice opportunities such as form recognition, gap filling, sentence building, and essay writing
- A communicative teaching approach added to the presentation process if teachers value communicative grammar learning
#### Constraints
- Active and passive tenses are taught in separate sessions due to the complexity and variety of the tenses

### Target Learners
- EFL college freshmen

### Learning Goals
- mastering English tense-aspect forms across the four language skills
