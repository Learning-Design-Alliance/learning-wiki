# Your experiment at the South Pole? — an experiment-design contest for school students

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A contest for primary and high school students (aged 10-14), open to science and non-science oriented students, challenging them to design an experiment answering "Belgium-South Pole: what is the difference?". An international jury selected three experiments carried out at the South Pole, with results presented at a science fair. Goals included introducing Antarctica, letting students design their own experiment, "teaching how to write a scientific report, using a template based on actual reports written by researchers", introducing experimental bias and reproducibility, and engaging students in presenting their work.
