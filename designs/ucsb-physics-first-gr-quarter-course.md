# UCSB one-quarter 'physics first' general relativity elective

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A junior/senior elective at the University of California, Santa Barbara: "A one quarter (∼ 28 lectures) 'physics first' course in general relativity has been a standard junior/senior elective for physics majors ... for approximately thirty years." In a quarter the instructor reviews special relativity, motivates gravity as geometry, derives Schwarzschild orbits, describes experimental tests, and introduces black holes and Friedman-Robertson-Walker cosmological models. The course is routinely taught by faculty from many areas of physics, including particle physics and astrophysics, both theorists and experimentalists, some for whom it was their first serious experience with general relativity.
