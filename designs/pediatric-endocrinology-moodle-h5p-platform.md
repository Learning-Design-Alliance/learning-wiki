# Moodle-based pediatric endocrinology e-learning platform with 27 H5P interactive clinical cases

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A freely accessible e-learning platform built on Moodle with the H5P module, transforming real patient cases into structured interactive content including "comprehensive learning objectives, reactivation of prior knowledge, current history, prior history, familial history, physical examination, additional examinations, therapeutic interventions, and key messages". Chapter structure and objectives derive from the 2021 ESPE Pediatric Endocrinology syllabus. A total of 27 clinical cases based on real patients were developed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Log-in and personal registration
- Content written and validated by the unit's medical staff
- A user guide to help learners navigate the platform
#### Constraints
- Designed for computers, tablets, and smartphones, but computers and tablets seemed the most suitable devices

### Target Learners
- pediatric endocrinology residents
- fourth-year medical students

### Learning Goals
- applied clinical reasoning in pediatric endocrinology
- general knowledge, red flags, and patient management
