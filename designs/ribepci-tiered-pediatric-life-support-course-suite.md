# Tiered paediatric life support course suite (BLS, ILS, ALS, and instructor course) using interactive simulation with mannequins

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The program designed four course types: a 20–24 h on-site ALS course "combining theory and practice and using a teaching approach based on advanced interactive simulation with mannequins"; a 4–6 h BLS course; a 10–14 h ILS course adapted to local health-professional needs; and an ALS instructor course in which instructor students designed scenarios and ran simulation cases with self-evaluation, instructor evaluation and feedback. Practical groups were capped at six students with individual supervised practice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mannequins and complementary teaching material (approximately 10,000 euros per country)
- A maximum of six students per practical group for individual supervised practice
#### Constraints
- AED content was excluded from BLS because none of the countries had public access to AEDs at that moment

### Target Learners
- General population, schoolchildren and teachers, firemen and policemen (BLS); nurses, physicians and students (ILS); paediatrics residents and emergency and critical care staff (ALS)

### Learning Goals
- Paediatric basic, intermediate and advanced life support skills, including airway management, vascular access, arrhythmia management, neonatal resuscitation, trauma care and team leadership
