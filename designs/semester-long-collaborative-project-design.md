# Spread a collaborative group project across the semester with clearly delineated session tasks rather than a final-weeks report

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends that "a collaborative comprehensive group project consist of many interrelated components and be spread across the semester, as opposed to requiring students to prepare the final report, in its entirety, during the last few weeks of the semester". In the study, students completed seven collaborative sessions with predetermined objectives, performing 19 detailed tasks guided by a seven-page blueprint integrated with the textbook. Project information, reading assignments and tutorials should be carefully prepared and communicated in advance.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All relevant information, reading assignments, tutorials and online assignments should be carefully prepared in advance and communicated effectively
- Project objectives need to be unambiguous and tasks in each session delineated clearly
#### Constraints
- Scheduling meetings among students was the most important impediment, given course loads and work and family demands

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students working in project teams

### Learning Goals
- developing a marketing plan
- leadership and communication skills
- critical thinking through considering others' ideas
