# Technology-supported course plans and activities for the 'Our Country and the World' unit

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A set of 11 lesson plans with five activities, one per unit subject, prepared by the researcher to teach global-education attainments in the 6th-grade 'Our country and the world' unit using communication technologies. The article states "11 lesson plans with five activities were prepared for teaching the attainments related to global education by using communication technologies", evaluated by two field experts and two social studies teachers and pre-applied for four weeks with 21 students. Technologies included newspaper clippings, television, video and news blogs across activities such as Invitation to City, Product Marketing, Newspaper Page Designing, Help Mobilization and Addressing the World.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Evaluated by field experts and social studies teachers before application
- Delivered across 11 sessions of 40 minutes over four weeks
#### Constraints
- Applied only to the 'Our country and the world' unit in 6th-grade social studies

### Target Learners
- 6th-grade secondary school students

### Learning Goals
- Global-education attainments: population and economic activity distribution, economic relations, cultural and political relations with other countries, solidarity in disasters, inter-communal interaction
