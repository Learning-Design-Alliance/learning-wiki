# BioApp biomedical design module with structured creativity tool TRIZ

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An interdisciplinary senior-undergraduate module, established within the EU-funded BioApp project, in which "Student teams, each with members of both disciplines, select one real-world clinical problem from a menu of options, and are paired with a senior clinician who acts as clinical mentor over a 12 week semester." Teams are taught the structured creativity tool TRIZ, a multi-stage ideation process for developing novel design solutions. Learning objectives cover multidisciplinary team co-operation, conceptual design participation, multidisciplinary analysis of real-world clinical problems, and presenting to stakeholders. Unlike many modules, its focus is on the pedagogical process rather than only the project output.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teams must include members of both medical and engineering disciplines
- A senior clinician must act as clinical mentor over the 12-week semester

- The survey found medical students may need preparatory work before participating in such modules
