# Picture-based speaking lesson sequence with pre-activity, main activity, and post-activity phases

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article describes a recurring lesson structure in which each session had "three main parts: pre-activity, main activity, and post-activity". Warm-up questions activated prior knowledge, the main activity used picture sequences with comprehension checks, brainstorming and prediction, and the post-activity moved into pair and group speaking tasks such as dialogues, discussions and role plays drawn from Top Notch 2.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sessions require sets of sequencing or contrasted pictures selected to match the lesson topic and learners' proficiency level
#### Constraints
- Developed and tested with female intermediate learners at a private institute; the article warns the result may not be replicable to other Iranian educational curricula

### Target Learners
- female intermediate EFL learners aged 16-20

### Learning Goals
- L2 speaking proficiency
- fluency and participation in oral communication tasks
