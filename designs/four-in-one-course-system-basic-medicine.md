# Four-in-one course system for top-notch basic medicine curricula

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The model's operational layer organizes the curriculum into four coordinated course categories. The article states: "At the operational layer, the focus is on implementing a four-in-one course system covering general education, professional integration, interdisciplinary research, and internationalized online learning." General education courses nurture analytical thinking and cultural awareness; professional integrated courses combine core biomedical knowledge with organ-system modules; interdisciplinary and research-oriented courses converge medicine, engineering and informatics; and international modules, MOOCs and top-university practice courses foster global outlook.
