# Three-type taxonomy of newcomer schools: programs-within-a-school, transitional, and comprehensive

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The report organizes newcomer schools into three structural types. "These newcomer schools use a standalone facility" distinguishes transitional and comprehensive schools, while programs-within-a-school are "embedded programs co-located at a traditional school" where newcomers join recess, meals, electives, and sports with host-school students. The types differ in enrollment duration and exit pathways: transitional schools are designed for 1-2 years, while comprehensive schools let students remain through graduation.
