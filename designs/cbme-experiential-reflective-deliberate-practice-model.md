# Triadic teaching model integrating experiential learning, reflective practice, and deliberate practice within competency-based medical education

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 review), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article frames its intervention as a theory-based instructional model aligning scenario-based simulation ward rounds with video feedback under CBME. It draws on experiential learning ("actively engaging in the experience"), reflective practice ("critically examining clinical performance"), and deliberate practice ("the importance of feedback and repetition in the development of clinical skills"), forming "a continuous cycle of experience, reflection and refinement of performance". The intervention operationalizes this through role-play immersion, video-based reflection-on-action, and repeated targeted feedback.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- High-fidelity clinical scenarios with role playing
- Video recording and structured debriefing
- Repeated cycles of simulation, feedback, and refinement
#### Constraints
- The model was developed and tested in a single gastroenterology department; the authors note generalizability may be limited by the single-center design and specialty-specific cases

### Target Learners
- residents in standardized residency training

### Learning Goals
- holistic clinical competencies
- critical thinking
- clinical readiness
