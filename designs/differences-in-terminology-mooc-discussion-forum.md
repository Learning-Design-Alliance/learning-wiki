# 'Differences in terminology' discussion forum embedded in two autism-education MOOCs

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
A Week 1 discussion forum in two FutureLearn MOOCs (SMART-ASD and Good Practice in Autism Education) developed and run by the host university. Learners were first given background information and findings from Kenny et al. (2016) to establish common knowledge, then asked two questions: what names and terms they use when talking about autism, and how important the issue of different terms is. Across six runs between 2017 and 2019, "803 learners participated in this discussion forum across the MOOCs", producing 1203 comments monitored by a faculty member and a doctoral student who fostered an interactive online learning environment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learners need background information on terminology research to establish a common level of knowledge before discussing, since no prior knowledge could be assumed
#### Constraints
- The authors could not gather detailed demographics of forum participants because user profiles could not be traced via FutureLearn

### Target Learners
- self-advocates
- family and friends of autistic individuals
- education and healthcare professionals

### Learning Goals
- awareness of stakeholder differences in autism terminology preferences
- reflection on how language choices convey attitudes and judgement
