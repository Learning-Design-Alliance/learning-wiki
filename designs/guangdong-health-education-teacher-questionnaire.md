# Self-made three-section questionnaire surveying health education teachers' basic information, teaching situation, and school-level conditions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A self-made questionnaire (Supplementary File 1) administered anonymously online to teachers in 232 primary and secondary schools in Guangdong. It comprised three sections: "basic information of teachers", "teaching situation, such as teaching grade and the degree of teaching difficulty", and "school level investigation" covering uniform teaching materials, teaching standards, evaluation criteria, and school value for health education. The teaching difficulty outcome was recorded on five levels and merged into easy versus difficult categories for analysis.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants currently engaged in frontline teaching work who volunteered; health education teachers identified by reporting undertaking or participating in health education teaching in the last academic year
#### Constraints
- The assessment of teaching difficulty remained inherently subjective, relying on self-reported evaluations without objective validation metrics; survey schools were selected by the local Education Bureau, which may have introduced selection bias

### Target Learners
- Frontline primary and secondary school teachers in Guangdong Province

### Learning Goals
- Measuring teaching difficulty and its influencing factors in school-based health education
