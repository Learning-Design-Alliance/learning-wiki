# VCL lesson pattern pairing student-centered experiment design with process evaluation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Table 2 enumerates the design characteristics the authors built into the VCL: configuration information that inquires into prior knowledge and adapts lab experiences to daily life; student-centered learning through individual or group decision making, open-ended questions during experiments, and interactive experimentation; a teacher role of starting with an interesting question; planning through multimedia with students selecting inventory and equipment; and evaluation by comparing students' prior knowledge with the experiment result at the end. Free science experiments can be done in class or at home using VCL.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students select the inventory and all equipment before beginning an experiment
- Predictions and data are recorded in the VCL at the predict and observation steps
#### Constraints
- The pattern was implemented and observed only for the chemical changes unit in 9th-grade chemistry

### Target Learners
- 9th-grade secondary school chemistry students

### Learning Goals
- Constructing knowledge of chemical changes actively
- Relating experiments to daily life
