# Aptitude Treatment Interaction model procedure as implemented in this study

## Description
The ATI model in this study proceeds in stages: "the initial treatment stage given by the teacher to stimulate students to think creatively," after which "the teacher divides students into groups, which then the teacher will give intensive treatment to each group," followed by an achievement test as evaluation. The article attributes the model's advantage to the grouping stage and intensive, aptitude-matched treatment, which it says lets students implement new ideas and find various solutions.
