# Seven-stage ChatGPT-mediated inquiry cycle: obtain simulated data, analyze, solve problems, confirm with the chatbot, plenary discussion

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The NOVA activity is structured as a seven-stage cycle: teacher introduction, virtual data collection from the chatbot, three problem-solving stages (verifying Kepler's third law, graphical and algebraic determination of stellar mass), a confirmation stage returning to NOVA, and a closing plenary. The article writes that "in Stage 6, they must return to NOV A to compare their own results with the solutions to the posed problems; and in Stage 7, the activity concludes with a plenary discussion." The activity is written in Student Worksheet format with group discussion questions at each stage.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- ChatGPT account access (single projected account or one computer per student/group)
- students familiar with spreadsheets
#### Constraints
- The author reports the second option, each student or group using their own computer, works best because it provides greater richness and diversity of results

### Target Learners
- high school students working in small groups

### Learning Goals
- hypothesis formulation and testing
- data analysis and graphing
- verifiable conclusion drawing
