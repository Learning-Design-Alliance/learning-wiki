# Pharmacology and Toxicology in Society (PT SOC): a community-engaged experiential learning elective with harm reduction partners

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
PT SOC is an elective enrolling approximately 35 third- and fourth-year students per year in which students "collaborate with harm‐reduction and mental health organizations over one semester." Students work mainly in teams on partner organization-led activities requiring a minimum of 20 hours of work placement outside class time, including community outreach, knowledge translation resources, and community-led research, complemented by course content on drug policy and debates and a community partner panel.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students complete a minimum of 20 h of work placement with a community partner organization outside of class time
#### Constraints
- It is a partnership-based form of EL whose components occur directly with community partners at community sites, and enrollment is limited to approximately 35 students per year

### Target Learners
- third- and fourth-year undergraduate pharmacology and toxicology students

### Learning Goals
- pharmacological and toxicological understanding of substance use disorders
- professional and interpersonal skills
- communication skills
