# CIS 102Q Problem Solving Using LEGO Robotics service-learning course with four cooperative labs

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A service-learning course at Pace University, offered to undergraduates with no prerequisites, combining a one-hour weekly lecture (robotic principles, creative thinking, problem solving, elementary programming) with a cooperative robotic laboratory. Students work in teams of three or four on weekly robotic challenges, then spend the last four to five sessions mentoring middle-school teams. Table 1 lists four labs: Introduction (gears and gear ratios), Car Racing (chassis, motors, basic programming), Sensors (light and touch sensors), and The Big Race, each designed to be completed within 20 minutes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teams of three or four members who divide builder, parts specialist, programmer, and tester roles
- Labs designed to fit roughly 45-minute school visits, completable within 20 minutes
#### Constraints
- Enrollment data showed only 23 women enrolled versus 63 men across the course sections studied

### Target Learners
- undergraduate students
- sixth-grade middle school students
- girls in partner elementary and middle schools

### Learning Goals
- problem solving with robotic technology
- programming concepts
- cooperative teamwork
- mentoring younger students
