# IAD_PLSC practical simulation training program

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The inter-academic paediatric laparoscopic surgery degree (IAD_PLSC) is a French nation-wide training program combining theoretical training by experts with mentored practical training on pelvitrainers. Its "structure of practical simulation training over two one-week sessions of 12 h each, five months apart" is described as corresponding to deliberate practice. Trainees work in pairs alternating training and observation every 15 minutes, practice intracorporeal laparoscopic knots with 5-mm and 3-mm instruments, and are assessed with an adapted OSATS score out of 40 plus knot completion time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Supervision by consultants with a minimum of four-year experience in laparoscopic surgery and in simulation
- Trainees attend the practical afternoons and the final practical evaluation (absence from more than two afternoons was an exclusion criterion)
#### Constraints
- The study evaluated it retrospectively, without a control group of trainees with only theoretical and video learning
- Only one adult-size pelvi-trainer box and one length of 3-mm instruments were offered

### Target Learners
- residents in paediatric surgery and paediatric urology

### Learning Goals
- intracorporeal laparoscopic knot tying and suturing skills
- basic laparoscopic skills (peg transfer, cord manipulation, pattern cutting)
