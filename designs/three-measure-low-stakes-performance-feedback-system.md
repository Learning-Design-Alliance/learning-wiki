# Three-measure low-stakes educator performance feedback system (CLASS/FFT observations, value-added growth reports, VAL-ED leadership feedback)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The study provided eight districts resources and support to implement three performance measures: a classroom practice measure "with subsequent feedback sessions conducted four times per year based on a classroom observation rubric" (CLASS or FFT), a student growth measure (value-added scores provided once per year), and a principal leadership measure (VAL-ED) with feedback sessions twice per year. No formal stakes were attached; measures were used to give educators and supervisors information about performance. Schools were randomly assigned to implement the measures or not.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Districts needed data systems sufficient to support value-added analysis
- Feedback sessions with trained observers and printed or online report dissemination
#### Constraints
- The measures were not used for staffing decisions such as tenure or continued employment, with exceptions in three districts where principal observations counted in official ratings
- Rubric assignment was by district preference, not random, so CLASS/FFT differences cannot be attributed to the observation systems

### Target Learners
- Grades 4–8 teachers of reading/ELA and mathematics
- Elementary and middle school principals

### Learning Goals
- Improving classroom instructional practice
- Improving principal instructional leadership
- Raising student mathematics and reading achievement
