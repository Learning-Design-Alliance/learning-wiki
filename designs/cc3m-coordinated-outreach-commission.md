# Coordinate outreach institution-wide through a national commission with an independent budget, guidelines requiring visibility, national impact, and built-in ex-post evaluation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
CC3M (the National Commission for Third Stream Activities, born in late 2016) is INFN's standing body to "coordinate, organize, supervise, and record" third-mission activities. It combines management representation and Communication Office membership with its own budget, maintains one local contact per structure for bottom-up input, and referees proposals in yearly meetings of the whole community, a "transparent approach that comparatively allocates existing resources". Proposals must state aims, expected impact, resources, milestones, and evaluation instruments, and are judged against strategic goals such as broadening publics, tackling the gender gap in STEM, and strengthening lifelong learning.
