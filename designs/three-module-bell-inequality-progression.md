# Three-module progression of increasing difficulty from Bell-Wigner to original Bell to CHSH inequalities

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The material is organized in three modules of increasing difficulty: the Bell-Wigner inequality first, chosen because "the required mathematics is trivial and the basic assumption can be visualized in a simple way", then the original Bell inequality based on correlations rather than probabilities, and finally the CHSH-type inequalities. Thanks to the unified notation and the increasing level of difficulty, the authors intend the contribution as a useful educational tool for quantum computing courses and outreach events. Each module pairs a short theoretical presentation with the quantum circuit and associated Qibo code.
