# Interpolation versus extrapolation as a distinction between model domains

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article frames its second set of activities around the distinction between using a model within and beyond its original domain. It defines: "When the model is used to predict data within its initial domain, it is often said to interpolate. When, in contrast, the model is exploited beyond its initial remit, one is generally extrapolating." The neutron-star activities instantiate extreme extrapolation of the liquid-drop model, and the authors argue extrapolation based on models has led to discoveries from DNA structure to exoplanet research, making it a productive topic for classroom discussion of model limits.
