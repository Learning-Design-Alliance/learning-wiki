# Paired quiz-based graph-structure serious game and equivalent instructional video for basic graph terminology

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article introduces two graph structure education modules teaching the same content: a Unity-built quiz-based serious game and a 10.5-minute PowerPoint video with animation and audio. Both present "the same figures and options" across seven trials covering graph definition, complete graphs, edges, loops, directed versus undirected graphs, degree, and in-/out-degree. The game adds a timer, sound effects, confetti, clicking, and drag-and-drop, with immediate scored feedback; the video group verbally responded without on-screen interaction or numerical scores.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants with limited or no familiarity with fundamental graph terminologies
- Equivalent content, figures, and session lengths across game and video conditions for comparability
#### Constraints
- The video condition lacked a timer, numerical score, and evaluative feedback, differing from the game condition on these game elements

### Target Learners
- adult graduate students over 18 proficient in English

### Learning Goals
- basic graph structure terminology in STEM+C / computer science education
