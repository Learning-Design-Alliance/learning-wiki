# Build FSP-TCM case scripts primarily around different TCM syndromes rather than different diseases

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Based on teacher feedback from the trial, the article reports that teachers preferred constructing FSP-TCM case scripts around TCM syndromes rather than diseases: "teachers preferred to various TCM syndromes (strongly agree: 4, 33.33%; agree: 6, 50.0%) rather than different diseases (strongly agree: 1, 8.33%; agree: 2, 16.67%)". This reflects TCM's disciplinary emphasis on syndrome differentiation as a core clinical skill.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- case scripts should be developed for the FSP-TCM simulation course and aligned with the TCM syndromes being taught
#### Constraints
- this preference reflects the opinion of the 12 surveyed teachers in this trial, not a tested outcome

### Target Learners
- undergraduate TCM students in simulation-based clinical skills courses

### Learning Goals
- TCM syndrome differentiation and treatment ability
