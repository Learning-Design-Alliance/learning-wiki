# Five-activity lesson structure with short, predictable activities for intervention lessons

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each FSI lesson follows five predictable activities: Sprint (~3 min) multiplication fluency practice, Warm Up (~1-3 min) review of prior content, Huddle (~18 min) main instruction with slides and manipulatives, Game-based Practice (~5 min) retrieval-based games with whiteboards, and Cool Down (~3-5 min) interleaved independent problems serving as formative assessment. The article states "Short activities hold students' attention by providing a variety of contexts for learning targeted concepts." The Cool Down structure "allowed for distributed and interleaved practice at the conclusion of each lesson."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A workbook with color-coded pages for each activity and animated PowerPoint slides presented by the teacher
#### Constraints
- The intervention was designed for a 30-minute class, which can be difficult for teachers preparing 50-minute sessions

### Target Learners
- sixth-grade students with mathematics difficulties in intervention classes

### Learning Goals
- fraction magnitude concepts
- fraction arithmetic fluency
