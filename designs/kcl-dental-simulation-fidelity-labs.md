# Paired dental simulation fidelity labs: haptics (SIMtoCARE) and phantom-head (A-dec) environments

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The faculty operates two dental simulation fidelity labs used in the undergraduate curriculum. FL18-A houses 18 SIMtoCARE haptic task-trainer simulators with digital audio-visual technologies, integrating a phantom-head interface for a blended augmented reality learning experience, where teachers can demonstrate and observe students. FL20-B is fully integrated with 90 A-dec phantom heads, AV facilities and intra-oral scanners, offering a digitally augmented workflow combining lecture and laboratory as a complementary environment.
