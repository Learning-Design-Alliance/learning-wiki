# Ten-phase JiTT lesson sequence for English reading and writing with vocational students

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article operationalizes JiTT through web technologies as a ten-phase lesson sequence: warm-up exercise/motivation, classroom setting with real-world connection, traditional assignments for critical thinking, estimation, defining problems, teamwork, communication, time management, recitation dealing with new vocabularies, and interactive lecture. Phases alternate online work (discussion boards, web searches, Gmail submissions) with teacher-led group discussion, feedback, and paragraph writing on food and eating-habits topics.
