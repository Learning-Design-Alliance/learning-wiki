# TPACK-grounded, sociocultural constructivist design for STEM teacher professional development

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
SATTAA's design rests on sociocultural constructivism, in which learning takes place "by building on prior knowledge, is extended through action and interactions with others", and on the TPACK framework, which posits that teachers' competencies must include content, pedagogical and technological knowledge in their interactions with each other. The program intertwines astronomy content sessions designed by research astronomers with pedagogical and technological expertise contributed by teacher-preparation specialists.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Intertwining content with productive pedagogies and technologies in secondary classrooms requires team members with expertise in teacher preparation and STEM education.
- Prolonged interventions and combinations of tools for learning with reflective experiences are more effective, per the TPD literature the article cites (Avalos, 2011).
#### Constraints
- The article applies this design specifically to an astronomy-focused TPD for pre- and in-service teachers in San Antonio, where out-of-field teaching of astronomy is the rule rather than the exception.

### Target Learners
- pre- and in-service STEM teachers
- out-of-field teachers of astronomy

### Learning Goals
- astronomy content expertise
- confident astronomy teaching
- pedagogical and technological tool integration
