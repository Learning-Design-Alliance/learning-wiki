# Model of using computer modeling systems (CMODS) to form students' natural-mathematical competences

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article develops a theoretical model for using computer modeling systems in general secondary education to form students' competences in natural and mathematical subjects. As the abstract states, "The model of use of the computer modeling system for developing students' competences in natural and mathematical subjects has been developed." The model rests on four approaches: "child -centric, systemic, activity, differentiated approaches". It integrates selection criteria for CMODS, organizational arrangements, cognitive tasks, and evaluation forms into one design framework.
