# Maker Lab course: an open-inquiry, Arduino-based, flipped physical science lab course

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A four-month, six-ECTS lab course cross-listed between physics and information science tracks at Amsterdam University College, open to natural science students who completed first-year mathematics. Students "conceive and set up their own experiments using affordable microcontrollers", following the full empirical research cycle twice in pairs. Initial materials cost less than €100 per student, with much lower annual costs thereafter.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Initial tool delivery or campus collection must be arranged for fully online instruction from day one
- Instructor contact time devoted largely to individual team meetings
#### Constraints
- One student's project scope was adapted due to severe lockdown restrictions in South Africa, using smartphone sensors and working individually

### Target Learners
- interdisciplinary undergraduate natural science majors

### Learning Goals
- defining a feasible research question
- designing measurement and data-analysis procedures
- evaluating reliability of experimental work
