# Mini-group research-and-development projects in which graduate students design interviews, surveys, and draft learning materials

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A course component in which graduate students work in small groups of 2-4 to carry out one cycle of a research and development process. Building on a literature review, they design interview protocols and conduct individual interviews, develop free-response and multiple-choice surveys from the results, analyze data from a relevant population, and design a draft set of narrowly focused learning materials matched to their data and the literature. The article states students "design interview protocols and conduct individual interviews on a topic, use results to develop free-response and multiple-choice surveys to get written data."
