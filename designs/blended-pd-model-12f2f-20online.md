# Blended learning professional development model for teacher ICT training (12 face-to-face + 20 online sessions)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A flexible blended learning model in which an online platform delivers most of the curricula: face-to-face instruction comprised 12 sessions in a computer lab (project discussion, feedback, workshop, computer-assisted instruction), and online learning comprised 20 asynchronous (e-mail, discussion forum, Facebook) and synchronous (chat, video, audio conference) sessions. The program ran approximately 8 weeks, 4 days per week, totalling 32 two-hour periods, and was delivered via a web-based learning platform (https://sites.google.com/site/alearningy/). The control group received 32 classroom and computer-laboratory sessions with trainer-discussed assignments instead.

### Learning Goals
