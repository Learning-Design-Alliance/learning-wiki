# LSVC hybrid professional development model

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
LSVC is a year-long hybrid teacher PD model that integrates Lesson Study and Video Club principles, using technology (live videoconferencing, teacher-recorded videos, facilitator-selected video content, online asynchronous collaboration tools) to connect teachers across geographies and experience levels. Activities include "whole group workshops, individual self-study, semi-structured conversations with partners, and small group reflective seminars." It was implemented with 71 dual language elementary teachers over three cohorts, and the fully remote format was retained after its benefits became evident.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sustained, year-long delivery with cycles of collaborative reflection, full-group discussion, and instructional coaching
- Widely available technology for synchronous and asynchronous collaboration
#### Constraints
- Delivery varied across the studied years to accommodate COVID-19 pandemic requirements
- The model was mediated by shifting district distance-learning requirements during the study period

### Target Learners
- in-service elementary dual language teachers across the career span, from first-year to 27 years of experience

### Learning Goals
- integrating language, literacy, and mathematics instruction in dual language settings
- bilingual instructional practices and mentoring capability
