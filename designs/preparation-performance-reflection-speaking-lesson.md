# Three-stage lesson structure of preparation, performance, and reflection for self-regulated speaking practice

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Each intervention lesson was divided into three main stages — "Preparation, Performance, reflection" — each with an explanation sub-stage where the instructor explicitly teaches and models strategies, and an application sub-stage where students practice them. In preparation, students set goals, generate and organize ideas, and seek help; in performance, small groups of five discuss the topic while the instructor circulates; in reflection, students use reflective journals and evaluation checklists. The pattern embeds self-regulated strategy training directly into content-based speaking lessons.
