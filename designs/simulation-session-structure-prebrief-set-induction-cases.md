# Multi-modality simulation session pattern: didactic lecture, pre-briefing, set induction, model demonstration, and case-based discussion

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The teaching–learning intervention layered multiple modalities around a 2-hour didactic lecture: a 10-minute pre-briefing outlining learning outcomes with a video recap, a set-induction demonstration of surfactant using a bowl of water and objects of varied density, hands-on lung-model demonstrations of altered pathology, and discussion of "5 case histories along with pulmonary function tests on obstructive and restrictive disorders." The authors state the strategy "combined multiple learning modalities—reading, visual, auditory, and kinesthetic."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A pre-intervention needs analysis (faculty and student topic ratings) and a pilot study with 30 students to refine the questionnaire and session duration
#### Constraints
- Students were divided into groups of 20 with a facilitator; the article notes multiple faculty members with distinct teaching styles may have introduced variability

### Target Learners
- first-year medical undergraduate students in large-cohort programs

### Learning Goals
- deep comprehension of respiratory physiology concepts and their clinical applications
