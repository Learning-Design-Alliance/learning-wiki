# Imaginative Activity-Based Model for enhancing innovation in Chinese medical students

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A structured learning model developed through a three-phase Design and Development Research process (need analysis, design and development, evaluation) to cultivate innovation competencies among Chinese medical students. Its content was built from expert-ranked activities organized via Interpretive Structural Modeling, producing a prototype the article describes as having "potential for developing innovation in Chinese medical students." The model integrates imaginative activities with design thinking processes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Expert consensus on activity elements obtained through NGT and ISM processes
- Validation through Fuzzy Delphi evaluation with expert agreement thresholds
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only for usability by expert ratings, not yet tested for effects on student innovation outcomes
- Developed specifically for the context of Chinese medical education

### Target Learners
- Chinese medical students

### Learning Goals
- Innovation competencies
- Creative problem-solving
- Critical thinking
