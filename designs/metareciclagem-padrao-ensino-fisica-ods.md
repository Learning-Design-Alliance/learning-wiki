# Metarecycling of e-waste as an interdisciplinary physics-learning pattern in Basic Education aligned with the UN SDGs

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The pattern consists of reusing discarded electronic components so that students build working devices while applying physics knowledge. The article defines metarecycling as "uma prática que consiste no reaproveitamento de componentes eletrônicos descartados para a criação de novos dispositivos" and states the practice "foi alinhada com os ODS 3 (vida saudável), ODS 4 (educação de qualidade) e ODS 7 (energia acessível e limpa)". It mobilizes maker-learning characteristics: interaction, dialogue, investigation, interdisciplinarity, hypothesis formulation, data collection and analysis, and scientific communication.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to discarded electronic devices obtained as donations, basic electronics tools (soldering irons, multimeters, wires, connectors), and a maker space in the school
#### Constraints
- The article frames the approach for Educação Básica physics teaching and notes the school curriculum context of the Novo Ensino Médio itineraries

### Target Learners
- first-year Ensino Médio students, ages 14–15, in public schools

### Learning Goals
- physics concepts of electricity and energy conversion
- scientific investigation and communication skills
- environmental awareness and sustainability competencies
