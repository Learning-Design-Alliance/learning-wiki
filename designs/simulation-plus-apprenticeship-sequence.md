# Classroom scenario simulation plus clinical apprenticeship teaching sequence

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The intervention's teaching process "included classroom scenario simulation and clinical apprenticeship." In classroom sessions, teachers demonstrate the modified Mini-CEX and SBAR, then students in six-member groups complete case studies with role-playing and SBAR-based reporting, followed by faculty assessment and feedback. In clinical probation, students "were required to proactively apply the SBAR model in practice to communicate effectively with mentors," with instructors providing timely feedback and students sharing experiences afterward.
