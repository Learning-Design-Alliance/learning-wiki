# Intervention for Health and Sustainable Development module (40-credit environmental health module)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 40-credit final-year module in the Environmental Health BSc at Cardiff Metropolitan University, equating to "400 notional learning hours of study," shared with Ulster University (20 credits) and Flinders University (20 credits, master's level). Students assess community health needs and assets using live public health and census data, then design and evaluate community-level interventions through problem-based learning in groups of four to six. Locations included Maerdy and Ferndale (Wales), Great Palm Island (Australia), and Ardoyne (Belfast).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to good internet and adequate IT equipment is described as an essential element of the module
- Scenario locations require sufficient public health observatory data and online resources for thorough needs and assets analysis
#### Constraints
- The subject area requires strong public health observatory data which cannot always be replicated in other nations, and this prevented extension to a Ugandan provider

### Target Learners
- Final-year BSc environmental health students
- Master's/Graduate Diploma students

### Learning Goals
- Assessing and prioritizing community health needs
- Designing and evaluating community-level interventions
- Health impact assessment
