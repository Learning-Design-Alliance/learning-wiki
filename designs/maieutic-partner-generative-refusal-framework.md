# Maieutic Partner / Generative Refusal framework for Tools for Thought

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The paper proposes shifting GenAI from a "Co-author" role to a "Maieutic Partner" that facilitates inquiry by strategically withholding text generation. Generative Refusal treats refusal "not as a lack of functionality, but as a core mechanic for Tools for Thought": the AI creates "a structured gap that demands user input, rather than filling that gap with synthetic text", shifting interaction from delegation to activation. The framework draws on desirable difficulties and scaffolding within the Zone of Proximal Development, and is operationalized in Actor's Note, which refuses to generate text and instead asks context-aware questions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The domain must be one where the mental effort of articulation and interpretation is central to learning, such as artistic training
#### Constraints
- The paper argues the efficiency-first paradigm is detrimental specifically in educational and training contexts, not universally

### Target Learners
- professional and student actors in artistic training

### Learning Goals
- internalizing character roles through first-person reflective journaling
- critical articulation rather than cognitive offloading
