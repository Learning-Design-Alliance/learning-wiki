# 12-scenario physiotherapy OSCE with HRV monitoring protocol

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An OSCE composed of 12 scenarios assessing physiotherapy competences (musculoskeletal, respiratory, neuromuscular and obstetrics), delivered in a simulated hospital. Each student had 10 minutes to solve an unknown situation, with scenarios separated by 2 minutes and the same written information given for 2 minutes before each scenario. Students wore a "validated [27] Polar V800 heart rate monitor" and R-R intervals were analysed with Kubios HRV software across time-domain, frequency-domain and nonlinear variables. Scenario order was randomly assigned by alphabetical order of last names.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A heart rate monitor validated for R-R interval measurement and HRV analysis software
- Simulated hospital setting with standardised written information before each scenario
#### Constraints
- The datasets are not publicly available due to confidentiality agreements approved by the Universidad Europea Ethical Committee

### Target Learners
- final-year physiotherapy bachelor's degree students

### Learning Goals
- clinical reasoning
- physical examination
- patient communication
