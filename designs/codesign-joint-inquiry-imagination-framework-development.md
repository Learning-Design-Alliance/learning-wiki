# Co-design as a collaborative process of joint inquiry and imagination for developing competency frameworks

## Description
The article applies co-design, defined as "a collaborative process of joint inquiry and imagination, where diverse stakeholders collectively explore problems and develop solutions". Moving beyond traditional top-down policymaking, it incorporates public perspectives, enabling shared decision-making and flattening hierarchies. In this project, patients, healthcare staff and the public were engaged throughout all phases, with iterative co-design meetings generating shared values that informed context-specific QPS domains and competencies. The article presents this as an adaptive approach tailored to panel members' needs, combining a Delphi survey, scoping review evidence, and iterative workshops.
