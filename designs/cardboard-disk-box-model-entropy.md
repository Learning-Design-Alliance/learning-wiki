# Cardboard-disk box model representing molecules and glass halves to teach entropy probabilistically

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The model represents a sample of 40 molecules (20 of liquid A, 20 of liquid B) with cardboard disks whose two coloured sides encode which half of the glass the molecule occupies. Shuffling the box simulates thermal motion after a separating film is removed, and students count disks to see the mixture. The author states the model 'aims to reproduce the probabilistic aspects of the mental experiment of the glass' and stresses it is 'not a realistic description of the real glass' but deals with the same probabilistic problems.
