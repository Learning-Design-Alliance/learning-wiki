# Seven-chapter e-learning programme on local anaesthesia with formative quiz

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A self-contained e-learning programme covering local anaesthesia in seven chapters: jaw anatomy, pharmacokinetics, materials (with a handling video), patient information and documentation obligations, seven videos of injections at different intraoral sites, complications including "intoxication, nerve injury, allergic reactions and vasovagal syncope", and the German accounting system. It also contains "a formative (practice) quiz with 8 questions". It served as the digital component of the e-learning and blended conditions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a computer room or individual login access; blended-group students received login details 5 days before the lecture
#### Constraints
- In this study the e-learning group had to work through the programme at the same time the other groups attended the lecture, so it was not time- and place-independent as used

### Target Learners
- 3rd-year dental students preparing for supervised patient treatment

### Learning Goals
- Theoretical knowledge of local anaesthesia anatomy, pharmacokinetics, materials, complications and practical injection technique
