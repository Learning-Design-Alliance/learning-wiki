# Scaffolded GenAI-collaboration assignment sequence progressing from introductory to advanced co-creation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
In the music example, an introductory composition course is mostly taught with GenAI prohibited to build unaided proficiency, then includes one required GenAI-collaboration assignment that "might scaffold different steps in the collaboration process, from brainstorming the theme, feel, and instrumentation of the music to the actual writing of melody and harmony." Later courses, such as an advanced composition course, revisit the outcome so "students may have a deeper collaboration with GenAI to continue developing PLAI-LO four."
