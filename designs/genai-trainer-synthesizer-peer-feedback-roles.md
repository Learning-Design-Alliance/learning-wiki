# GenAI as Trainer and Synthesizer: two complementary roles in EFL speaking peer feedback

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes a theory-informed design framework positioning generative AI in two constrained roles within L2 speaking peer feedback. The Trainer supports feedback givers through "calibration with exemplars, rubric-guided practice, and feedback-on-feedback", while the Synthesizer supports recipients by aggregating peer input into concise, actionable guidance linked to criteria and learning goals. The roles are design patterns, not validated systems, targeting the twin bottlenecks of feedback production and uptake.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers remain in the loop to approve prompts, audit outputs, and integrate GenAI-generated, teacher-curated artifacts into instruction
#### Constraints
- The design patterns' scope "excludes automated grading, unsupervised private tutoring, and high-stakes decisions"
- The account is conceptual and "does not present new empirical data"; the expectations "remain provisional and require empirical testing"

### Target Learners
- EFL/ESL higher education students in performative speaking tasks

### Learning Goals
- Peer feedback quality and uptake in L2 speaking
- Feedback literacy development
