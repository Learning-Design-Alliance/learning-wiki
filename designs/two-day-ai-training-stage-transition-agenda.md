# Two-day intensive training agenda sequenced to move learners from Stage 0 through Stage 4 transitions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Table 5 maps each activity of the 2-day hands-on AI/LLM training to the stage transition it facilitates: demystification of platforms moves Stage 0 to 1; hands-on API use, local model deployment, and RAG-based verification move Stage 1 to 2; tool evaluation, fine-tuning concepts, and bias detection move Stage 2 to 3; advanced fine-tuning, auto-coding agents, and building a small language model from scratch move Stage 3 to 4. The article reports this as "a practical pathway from Stage 0 (Not Yet Engaged) through Stage 4 (Improvement) within an intensive 2-day format."
