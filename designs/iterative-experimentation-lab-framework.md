# Introductory lab framework of meaningful reflection and iterative experimentation to refine models

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The redesign was inspired by an introductory lab framework in which students engage in meaningful reflection on their data and results and use that reflection to iterate and improve their results or knowledge of a system. Through this iterative experimentation process, students were seen to engage more deeply with physical and measurement models, even leading them to reexamine assumptions about the system and refine physical models seen in class based on their measurements. The framework structures the lab goals around analyzing data, connecting data with models, and evaluating and refining models.
