# WhatsApp-based mobile learning intervention with interactive multimedia and videocast

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The m-learning intervention delivered a 30-minute interactive multimedia learning resource (IMLR) built in Storyline software with text, images, and audio, followed by a 10-item knowledge check quiz requiring 100% to pass, then a 15-minute videocast demonstrating practical techniques on premature infants. Content was shared via a URL link on WhatsApp and remained accessible after the intervention period. Engagement strategies included reminder texts, follow-up questions every 3 days, phone calls, and WhatsApp Q&A support.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants must own a smartphone capable of running WhatsApp and be proficient in its use
#### Constraints
- Active researcher support concluded after 2 weeks, restricting opportunities for continuous feedback

### Target Learners
- NICU nurses

### Learning Goals
- Developmental positioning of preterm infants
