# iPod inquiry teaching method

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The iPod inquiry teaching method is a constructivist science inquiry sequence integrating the POE strategy with the STS strategy. As the article states, Lai (2009) proposed "a format for scientific inquiry teaching with the features of 'invitation- Prediction- operation- discussion (referred to as iPod inquiry teaching)'". It retains POE's prediction step, converts observation into hands-on operation, and converts explanation into peer discussion, aiming to give students space to construct their own ideas and understand a concept framework. In this study it structured the experimental group's teaching of the air concept unit.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers retain the prediction step to expose students' prior misconceptions and avoid over-guiding during invitation
- Students record findings during hands-on operations and every student shares ideas in the discussion step
#### Constraints
- Evaluated here only for a third-grade air concept unit in Taiwanese primary schools with equal teaching hours across groups

### Target Learners
- third-grade primary school students

### Learning Goals
- scientific concepts about air
- scientific attitudes
- inquiry and experimental skills
