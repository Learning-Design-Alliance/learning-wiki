# Three-phase renal biopsy simulation workshop: lecture, peer ultrasound practice, hands-on simulated biopsy

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The workshop comprised "three sequential phases: a brief introductory lecture, ultrasound practice, and hands-on biopsy simulation", delivered in small groups of four to five students over approximately 50 minutes per group. A ~5-minute lecture covered indications, complications, ultrasound principles, sterile technique, and needle handling; students then scanned each other (~5 minutes per student) and practiced simulated biopsy (~5 minutes each), supervised by a single board-certified nephrologist.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Small groups of four to five students to allow close supervision and active participation
- Supervision by an experienced nephrologist instructor
#### Constraints
- Total training time was limited; the authors state it did not provide sufficient repetition or mastery-based progression

### Target Learners
- fifth-year medical students on nephrology clerkship

### Learning Goals
- renal biopsy procedure knowledge
- ultrasound probe handling
- biopsy needle handling confidence
