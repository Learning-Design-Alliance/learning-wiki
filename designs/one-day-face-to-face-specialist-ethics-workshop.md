# One-day face-to-face specialist ethics workshop combining didactic presentations, group work, case studies and problem-solving activities

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A reusable workshop design for specialist ethics training: a one-day, face-to-face format with participant numbers capped at 24, combining "didactic presentations, group work, case-studies and problem solving activities". Materials were developed in line with Adult Learning Theory and the Skills Approach, tailored to each state's legislation, and supplemented by a handout booklet that 90% of deferred-survey respondents used in later reviews. Local data linkage unit staff attended for the full day to answer technical questions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Small group sizes (capped at 24) to preserve feedback, guidance and reinforcement
- Multiple facilitators with complementary expertise in ethics and law
- Training materials developed in consultation with domain experts and tailored to local legislation
#### Constraints
- Sustainability and geographical availability depend on appropriate funding; fees were set only to recover logistical costs

### Target Learners
- Human Research Ethics Committee members
- Ethics office staff

### Learning Goals
- Understanding data linkage methodology
- Assessing privacy risks and benefits of linked data
- Evaluating waivers of participant consent
