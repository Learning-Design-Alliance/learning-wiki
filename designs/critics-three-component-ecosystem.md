# CRITICS three-component ecosystem for cross-lingual scientific knowledge transfer

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The CRITICS approach integrates machine translation and educational technology as a three-component system: an LLM-based scientific document translation system, a Teaching and Learning Sequence (TLS) generator, and an LLM-based automatic assessor. The components are placed in "a continuous improvement loop where assessments feed back into translation quality, teaching resource development, and assessment criteria refinement". The system aims to translate scientific content and transform it into educationally meaningful resources with built-in assessment capabilities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- LLMs adapted to the translation, argumentation and assessment tasks
- machine-translated scientific knowledge as input for generating education materials in students' native languages
#### Constraints
- The project will comply with TRL 5, technology validated in relevant environments, with qualitative evaluation by domain experts
- focuses on natural sciences (biology, chemistry, physics) and Artificial Intelligence

### Target Learners
- science students in higher education reading in Basque, Lithuanian, French or German

### Learning Goals
- scientific literacy
- evidence-based argumentation
- critical thinking in science
