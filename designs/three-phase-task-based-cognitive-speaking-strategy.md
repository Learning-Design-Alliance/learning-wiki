# Three-phase task-based teaching strategy integrating communicative tasks with the cognitive approach

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The article proposes a teaching strategy in which "each speaking lesson was divided into three main phases: pre-task phase, during-task phase and post- task phase". The pre-task phase uses planning, consciousness raising and teaching to prepare learners and draw attention to form; the during-task phase focuses on communicating meaning while the teacher facilitates, records performance and assigns peer observers; the post-task phase uses self/peer evaluation of recordings and supportive feedback to remedy errors and raise awareness of accuracy and restructuring. The strategy operationalises the cognitive-approach goal of managing learners' focus of attention across fluency, accuracy and complexity.
