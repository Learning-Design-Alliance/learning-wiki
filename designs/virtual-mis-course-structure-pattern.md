# Virtual MIS skills course structure: video demonstration, mentored practice, then blinded formal assessment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Each course session followed a fixed sequence: participants watched a 5-minute video demonstration, completed a 30-minute practice session per participant with either F2F or virtual trainers, then undertook a 30-minute formal assessment recorded on video. The MISTELS peg transfer and SUTT suturing tasks were performed on laparoscopic box trainers, and videos were scored by Consultant Surgeons blinded to the method of teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Two expert trainers (attending surgeons) per room, fully briefed on the tasks
- Two facilitators per room for task setup and strict timekeeping
- Video recordings of final assessments for blinded scoring
#### Constraints
- Sessions were 1-day courses in a clinical skills unit; facilitators addressed technical problems such as low laptop battery and wireless drops using power chargers and wired ethernet cables

### Target Learners
- Novice learners in minimally invasive surgery

### Learning Goals
- Laparoscopic peg transfer and intracorporeal suturing proficiency
