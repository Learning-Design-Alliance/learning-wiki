# Offline course 'Introduction to experimental astroparticle physics' with lectures, seminars and three laboratory setups

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
ISU developed a regular course 'Introduction to experimental astroparticle physics' for bachelor and master students, including lectures, practical and laboratory works. The theoretical part consists of about ten lectures on ultra-high-energy cosmic rays, detection methods, and gamma- and neutrino astronomy; seminars introduce C/C++ and Python and the analysis frameworks ROOT, numpy, scipy and matplotlib. Three laboratory setups were developed to familiarize students with astrophysics detectors, on which students perform measurements and analyze and interpret data.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to the three custom laboratory setups and to the analysis frameworks taught in seminars (ROOT, numpy, scipy, matplotlib)
#### Constraints
- Course materials and interactive parts are planned to be incorporated into astroparticle.online

### Target Learners
- bachelor and master students at Irkutsk State University

### Learning Goals
- understanding cosmic-ray and gamma/neutrino detection methods
- modern data analysis skills in C/C++ and Python
