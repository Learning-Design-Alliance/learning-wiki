# CoSIL with appropriate guidance as an approach for shifting science education to digital when in-person schooling is not viable

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Computer-supported inquiry learning (CoSIL) with appropriate guidance is presented as an approach for moving science education online when in-person schooling is not viable, as highlighted by COVID-19. The author writes that "one approach to facilitate this shift, as reviewed by Zacharia et al. (2015), is to involve students in computer supported inquiry learning (CoSIL) with appropriate guidance." With knowledge of guidance forms and efficacy across inquiry phases, educators may "develop decent guidance so that students can experience scientific inquiry virtually as they used to do in-person."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Appropriate guidance tools matched to each phase of scientific inquiry
#### Constraints
- The approach is framed for situations where in-person schooling is not viable

### Target Learners
- Science students in virtual or remote lab settings during school disruptions

### Learning Goals
- Experiencing scientific inquiry virtually, comparable to in-person inquiry
