# Small-sided games session design built on representation, tactical complexity and exaggeration

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The article's training sessions follow a recurring design pattern: "All training sessions were based mainly on the NLP pedagogical principles of representation, tactical complexity and exaggeration." Representation used four modified games of 15 minutes per session with reduced player numbers and pitch size; tactical complexity set the opposition level (numerical superiority first, equality second) to give players more or less decision time; exaggeration manipulated rules and reward systems to emphasise each session's tactical objective, such as extra points for passes made while running.
