# Combine video modeling with video reflection in e-learning for communicative competence, following 4C/ID

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The voLeA learning environment pairs video modeling (presenting very good and very detrimental communicative behaviour patterns, linked with targeted explanations to promote action schemata) with video reflection (work assignments such as identifying key points, justifying selections, or analyzing scenes against criteria). The article grounds this combination in the 4C/ID model: "The authors recommend, among other things, the combination of deductive instructional procedures (as they are to be carried out within the framework of the theory-based VM modules) with inductive elements (such as reflection via video examples in the VR modules)". Fictional video cases are used because they allow realistic presentation and narrative extension beyond what is shown.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Video examples combined with reflective instructional items and guiding questions; text-based instructional stages continuously linked with video sequences
#### Constraints
- The planned effectiveness research will examine which design principle is most effective: video reflection, video modeling, or a combination of the two

### Target Learners
- medical students

### Learning Goals
