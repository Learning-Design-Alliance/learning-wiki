# Researcher-developed differentiated fifth-grade fractions program built on the Grid Curriculum Model, the Parallel Curriculum Model, and Project M3 materials

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A differentiated mathematics program for the fifth-grade 'Fractions' unit, developed by the researcher using Kaplan's Grid Curriculum Model, Tomlinson et al.'s Parallel Curriculum Model, and the M3 (Mentoring Mathematical Minds) teaching materials. The article states the Grid model is "a model developed to construct the necessary elements and format for the differentiated curriculum". Content, process and product were differentiated around a 'Balance' theme, with fractions taught across Social Sciences, Music, Science and Turkish, projects, discussions and games, guest academicians working in a master-apprentice fashion, and M3 'Deep Thinking' worksheets, activity cards and activity tools. The researcher added subheadings on multiplication and division, fraction problems, ratio and percentage.
