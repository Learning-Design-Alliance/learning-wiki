# A two-year universal quantum technology master's curriculum balancing hardware and software skills (UQTEP)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes a novel two-year master's curriculum in quantum science and technology that aims at a balance between quantum hardware and software skills. It is structured into four semesters moving from core quantum foundations to specialization tracks and a final research project, thesis, or internship. The authors state the program is meant to "enhance the employability of professionals thereby reducing the skill shortage" faced by academic institutions and organizations.
