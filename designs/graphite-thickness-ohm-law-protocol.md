# Five-stage low-cost experimental protocol for measuring nanometer-scale graphite layer thickness

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article describes a five-stage experimental activity: "determinação da resistividade do grafite 6B; deposição de camada de grafite sobre papel vegetal; medição da largura média da camada de grafite; determinação da espessura da camada de grafite depositada; e medição da espessura média da camada de grafite usando o SPM". It uses a 6B pencil, tracing paper, bench multimeters, a power supply, a digital caliper, ImageJ for width measurement from cellphone photos, least-squares linear fits, and optionally an SPM for validation. Fifty deposition strokes and 50 width measurements per 2-cm segment support the statistical treatment.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A 6B graphite pencil, tracing paper, multimeters, a power supply and basic measurement tooling; 50 deposition strokes in a single direction; width measurements referenced to a caliper scale in ImageJ
#### Constraints
- The thickness value is an approximation, since "a camada de grafite não é perfeitamente lisa nem possui seção transversal retangular"; SPM validation requires access to a partner institution's microscope

### Target Learners
- Higher Education students in Electricity courses
- High School students (with adaptations)

### Learning Goals
- Understanding Ohm's Law
- Applying resistivity and geometric relations to a real measurement
- Experimental data analysis and uncertainty propagation

- 6B Pencil Graphite Resistivity Higher Than Pure [+S]
