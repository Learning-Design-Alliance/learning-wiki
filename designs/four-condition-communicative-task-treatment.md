# Four-condition communicative task treatment contrasting negative feedback and modified output opportunities

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The treatment consisted of a series of communicative tasks carried out with native English speakers under four conditions that differed in the negative feedback and modified output opportunities they provided. Sixty Thai EFL learners completed these tasks and four oral production tests over an 8-week period, allowing the study to separate the contribution of feedback provision from learners' production of modified output.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Native-speaker interlocutors during communicative tasks and repeated oral production tests to measure change.
#### Constraints
- Findings pertain to Thai EFL learners interacting with native English speakers on these tasks over 8 weeks.

### Target Learners
- Thai EFL learners (n = 60)

### Learning Goals
- ESL question development
