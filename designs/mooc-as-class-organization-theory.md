# MOOCs are organized as classes: registered groups with fixed dates, weekly regularity, forums and mutual homework correction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes that the essence of present-day MOOCs is the class, abstractly defined as a group of students meeting regularly for instruction on a subject. Registration defines the group, there are starting and end dates, regularity is provided through weekly organization, students meet on the forum and during mutual correction of homework. The author calls this "the prime originality of MOOCs, compared to other forms of distance learning", and reports that students embraced the idea of the MOOC being a class rather than an illustrated and animated text book.
