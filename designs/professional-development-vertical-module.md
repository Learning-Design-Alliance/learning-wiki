# Professional development vertical module integrating communication skills teaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A vertical module in the first two years of a UK medical curriculum that "integrated communication skills, practical clinical skills, ethics and law, health promotion, community-oriented medicine and evaluation of evidence". Students attended one morning a week each year, taught via lectures, small-group seminars, meeting patients, one simulated patient and community visits, in groups of approximately 15 students facilitated by trained tutors using centrally prepared lesson plans, with case scenarios linking to basic medical sciences teaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained tutors facilitating groups of approximately 15 students using centrally prepared lesson plans, tutor guides and student course books
- Case scenarios linking the module to basic medical sciences teaching
#### Constraints
- The study compared non-randomised consecutive cohorts admitted via normal admissions procedures

### Target Learners
- undergraduate medical students in years 1 and 2

### Learning Goals
- communication skills with patients
- professional attitudes and clinical skills
