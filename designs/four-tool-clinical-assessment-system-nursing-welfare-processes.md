# Four-tool assessment system for clinical competence in "Nursing Care in Welfare Processes"

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The course evaluates second-year Nursing students through four instruments: the Guide of Evaluation of Clinical Practices (GEPC) with 30 sequenced learning activities verified by Reference Nurses at weeks 3, 5, 8, 11 and 12; a public defense of a clinical case; a portfolio containing the registered case, presentation and reflective diary; and an electronic record system filled in on tablets. Together these carry the 50% clinical-practice share of each discipline grade. The article states the tools "are arranged in sequence, increasing their complexity during the entire period of the clinical practice".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty consensus is required so assessment results can be used for improvement
- Anonymity procedures for students and Reference Nurses in evaluation records
#### Constraints
- The assessment tools were developed by professor consensus but still need a validity and reliability study

### Target Learners
- Second-year undergraduate Nursing students

### Learning Goals
- Acquisition of clinical competencies and nursing methodology skills
