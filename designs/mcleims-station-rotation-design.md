# mCLEIMS station design: six teams rotating through six 25-minute sequential simulation stations

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The 2-day mCLEIMS course organized 108 students into six teams of six per session, rotating through six specialty stations (pediatrics, traumatology, internal medicine, geriatrics, obstetrics/gynecology, emergency medicine). "Each station lasted 25 min: 2 min of facilitator briefing, 18 min of team task performance, 1 min of team-leader summary, and 4 min of reflection and feedback." Stations were linked as a patient journey rather than isolated exercises.
