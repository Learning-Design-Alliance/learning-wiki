# Community of practice as a mechanism for supporting educators integrating data science into physics teaching

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
DSECOP is organized as a community of practice whose mission is "to support physics educators who wish to integrate data science into their existing courses by providing resources as well as a community of like-minded educators for support." It brings together graduate students and physics educators from different institutions to share best practices and lessons learned. Fellows propose module ideas, refine them through discussion and outside faculty feedback, and units are tested by other faculty; the community also runs an annual workshop, a webinar series on topics from ethics to industry careers, and conference workshops where instructors interact directly with fellows.

#### Requirements
- Sustained funding (the project began with a 2021 American Physical Society Innovation Fund award) and fellow contributors to develop and test modules.
#### Constraints
- The model addresses educator training gaps and resource constraints; the authors state its flexibility and accessibility are crucial for overcoming the survey-identified challenges, making data science education feasible even in resource-constrained environments.
