# Six-module hybrid POCUS program structure progressing from online theory to supervised clinical practice to summative assessment

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The Fundamentals of POCUS Certification Program is a one-year, part-time hybrid program organized into six sequential modules: theoretical knowledge acquisition, skills development, clinical workplace practice, skills refreshment, e-portfolio completion, and competency assessment. The article states the design "integrates theoretical knowledge, hands-on practice, and ongoing longitudinal feedback," combining asynchronous online units, two-day hands-on workshops, supervised scans with faculty feedback via an LMS, and a final written exam and OSCE. Grading weights the e-portfolio at 40% and quiz/exam/OSCE at 10/25/25%.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants must be licensed physicians with access to an ultrasound machine with image storage capabilities in their healthcare setting
- Faculty with advanced POCUS training to supervise and provide formative feedback
#### Constraints
- Assessed at Kirkpatrick Level 2 only; Level 4 patient outcomes not yet evaluated
- Program studied at a single UAE institution

### Target Learners
- Practicing physicians with varied baseline POCUS experience

### Learning Goals
- Point-of-care ultrasound technical skill and image acquisition competence
- Confidence and clinical integration of POCUS
