# Three-component theory-based session sequence: awareness session, motivation session, then two practical skills sessions

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The El-Poems program organizes four online sessions into three sequenced components aligned with the I-Change model. Raising awareness is "comprised of a theoretical session to raise awareness and promote knowledge about the importance of proper posture"; building motivation involves one session addressing attitudes, social influence, and self-efficacy; developing skills "consists of two practical sessions" on posture during playing, lifting and carrying, stretching, and warming up. This grain size (a multi-session unit-level sequence) is reusable for theory-based health education programs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A behavior change model specifying phase content; methods matched to each phase (lecture for awareness, group discussion and modeling for motivation, direct and vicarious experience for skills)
#### Constraints
- Designed for online delivery with 90-minute weekly sessions; the article notes face-to-face delivery is also possible

### Target Learners
- Adolescent music students

### Learning Goals
- Posture behavior change and musculoskeletal pain prevention
