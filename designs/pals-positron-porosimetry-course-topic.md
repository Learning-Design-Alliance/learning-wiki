# Positron annihilation lifetime spectroscopy (PALS) as a nanosecond-range course topic

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A course example connecting nanosecond timescales to a real technology: positron annihilation lifetime spectroscopy. Orthopositronium lives 142 ns in vacuum, but inside matter the lifetime is reduced; in porous media the reduction is less severe because o-Ps can exit into pores. The o-Ps lifetime thus serves as "a convenient method to measure porosity and the typical pore size of nanoporous materials", departing from its vacuum value for pores smaller than 100 nm and dropping below 10 ns for pores around 1 nm.
