# Three-hour shock resuscitation simulation session with pre-test, four shock-type scenarios, and post-scenario debriefing

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A reusable lesson-level design for simulation-based resuscitation training: a 3-hour session beginning with goals and objectives and a 10-minute pre-test, followed by orientation to the manikins and assignment to four groups corresponding to four types of shock resuscitation scenarios (septic, cardiogenic, obstructive and anaphylactic). Each group ran a 10-minute resuscitation with roles of leader, nurses, and proceduralist(s), then a 20-minute debriefing, and finished with a post-test and confidence survey.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- High-fidelity patient simulator with haemodynamic and respiratory monitoring
- Case scenarios validated by expert review before student participation
- Instructors to play supporting roles and evaluate team performance
#### Constraints
- Only one student per group can take the leader role due to time limitations in group assignments

### Target Learners
- fifth-year medical students during internal medicine rotation

### Learning Goals
- diagnosis of shock types, identification of causes, and initial management of circulatory shock
