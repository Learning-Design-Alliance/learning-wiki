# Integrated clinical skills program alternating lectures, simulator training, bedside practice and case-based integration

## Description
The article's program arranges each teaching section across four components: classroom lectures, repeated simulator training before patient contact, practice on real patients to strengthen and verify clinical ideas, and "Multidisciplinary integration of clinical information and basic medical science, and clinical skills based on the cases in the ward or the scenarios". Table 1 allocates hours to these components for PE, four internal punctures, diagnostics, internal medicine and internship.
