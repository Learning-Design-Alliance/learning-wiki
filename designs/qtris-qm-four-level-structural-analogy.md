# Four-level structural analogy between QTris and qubit Quantum Mechanics: observables, states, unitary operations, measurement

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article defines a structural analogy between QTris (basic version) and QM for qubit systems, summarized in Table 1 at four levels: observables (Color and Orientation analogous to maximally incompatible observables Z and X), states (color-tiles and orientation-tiles as eigenstates {|0>,|1>} and {|+>,|->}), unitary operations (move cards I, X, Y, Z, H analogous to Pauli operators plus Hadamard), and measurement (die-roll probabilities analogous to the Born Rule for a Z measurement). By this analogy, game problems can be framed as typical QM problems in disguise, translatable into Dirac notation.
