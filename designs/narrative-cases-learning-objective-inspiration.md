# Narrative cases associated to learning objectives for narrative inspiration

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A Case "represents part of a fabula that implicitly reflects an authorial vision of an interesting sequence of events" and serves narrative inspiration. A case demonstrates a narrative concept, such as "seeding of a conflict", and must be context complete regarding it. Each learning objective has one or more associated cases, and case selection generalises the current fabula to retrieve applicable cases.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each case must be context complete with regard to its narrative concept
- Knowledge representation of the world domain to perform generalization transformations of the current fabula
#### Constraints
- Elements within a case may remain uninstantiated if they do not contribute to expressing the narrative concept

### Target Learners
- learners in TARGET serious games

### Learning Goals
- learning objectives such as the conflict management competence
