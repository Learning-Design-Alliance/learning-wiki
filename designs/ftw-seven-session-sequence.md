# Seven-session FTW sequence circling from far future through present to near future

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The FTW is delivered as a series of seven sessions — Imagineering, Modelling, Role-play, Retrofit, Everyday, Futurefit and Requirements — each with a defined outcome recorded by facilitators. The sequence "starts in Box 4 in Table #2, examining new activity and technology in the far future", then circles through Boxes 3, 1 and 2 and back to 4, travelling from the far future to the present and near future. It is typically run as a half-day event with six to twenty participants plus two or three facilitators.
