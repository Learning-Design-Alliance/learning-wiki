# Extra-credit Art-and-Physics story project: a hoop and a solid cylinder friendship narrative grounded in rolling motion

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An example student submission from an algebra-based introductory physics course: a story about empathy and friendship between two friends shaped like a hoop and a solid cylinder, in which the hoop friend uses the physics of rolling motion to cheer up his cylinder friend. The article states "The story accurately depicts physics, but the focus is on emotions, empathy and friendship". The submission built on an in-class demonstration and problem solving students had already completed, and is depicted in Figures 1 and 2.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students had already seen the in-class demonstration that a solid cylinder outpaces a hollow one and had solved problems using the physics equations proving this fact.
#### Constraints
- The showcased example is from a class taught before generative AI such as ChatGPT was introduced.

### Target Learners
- algebra-based introductory physics students, including bioscience, health-related majors and non-science majors

### Learning Goals
- connecting physics concepts to everyday life and the humanities
- deepening physics knowledge of rolling motion through creative contemplation
