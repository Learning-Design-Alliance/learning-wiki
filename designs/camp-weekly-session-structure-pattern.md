# Weekly structured cross-age mentoring session format: icebreaker, connectedness lesson, snack, and group activity over nine weeks

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
The modified CAMP intervention ran once weekly for nine weeks during the middle school exploratory period, facilitated by school counselors. "Weekly classroom activities included an icebreaker, a school connectedness lesson, snack, and a group activity," with mentors and mentees working in pairs and then joining a larger group. Mentors received training and followed a structured list of mentoring activities at each meeting, with activities addressing connectedness to school, teachers, peers, culturally different peers, reading, self in the future, attendance, behavior, and grades.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Trained high-school peer mentors following a structured activity list
- A scheduled recurring period (here the exploratory period) and counselor facilitation
#### Constraints
- Nine-week duration is shorter than the 18-week to full-academic-year interventions the authors report in the literature; logistical disruptions (math remediation, athletics) reduced participation

### Target Learners
- seventh-grade mentees
- high school peer mentors

### Learning Goals
- school connectedness
- student-school belonging
