# Multidirectional stitching technology and circular suture training module

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A training module in which learners practice laparoscopic needle delivery using varied needle-holding directions — positive, reverse, oblique (left, right), and swivel ("loosely clipping") — on a circular sponge pad with four incisions in different directions. Each incision is marked with "a small dot with a diameter of 0.5 cm on both sides of the incision" placed 0.75 cm from the incision centre, guiding needle entry and exit points. The module contrasts with traditional instruction on a 3D silicone model pad where the suture angle is fixed at 90 degrees to the needle.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A laparoscopic trainer box with camera and video display, two lockable needle holders, and a No. 1, 26 mm, 1/2 c round-bodied needle with 30 cm thread
- An experienced instructor guiding four hands-on courses
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only against traditional teaching, in a small single-centre sample of junior residents

### Target Learners
- junior residents novice to laparoscopy

### Learning Goals
- manual laparoscopic suturing and knot-tying proficiency
