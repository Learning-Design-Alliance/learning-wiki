# AI-integrated LMS for middle school with bounded, policy-gated AI assistance embedded in everyday coursework

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article proposes a middle-school LMS that couples standard capabilities (course organization, assignments, assessments, communication) with bounded AI features. It states the aim to 'provide structured, safe, and scalable learning support that mirrors and enhances day-to-day classroom practice', adding formative feedback, adaptive practice sequencing, study planning, and teacher-facing analytics. The platform is designed as a complement to classroom instruction, deployable in authentic school settings as routine coursework.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Operates as a complement to classroom instruction rather than a replacement
- Requires workflows that fit classroom constraints and support variable connectivity and device access
#### Constraints
- The platform is proposed rather than deployed at scale; no empirical outcomes are reported

### Target Learners
- Middle school students in grades 6-8

### Learning Goals
- Foundational academic skills in reading, writing, and mathematics
- Learning habits such as persistence, planning, and effective help-seeking
