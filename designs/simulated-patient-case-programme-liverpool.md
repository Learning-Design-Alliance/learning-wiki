# Simulated patient case programme: e-portfolio workbooks, tutor case videos, simulated clinics and 3D-printed teeth

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A hybrid learning intervention for 4th- and 5th-year dental students consisting of five simulated patient cases over 14 weeks. Students completed online e-portfolio (PebblePad) workbooks divided into "History & Examination, Special Investigations, Risk Assessment, Diagnosis & Treatment Planning and Reflection", watched asynchronous tutor case presentation videos lasting "between 15 and 20 min", attended simulated clinic sessions on phantom head simulators with customised 3D-printed teeth, and completed post-clinical reflection using Rolfe's model and SMART goal-setting.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access to PebblePad e-portfolio software, tutor case presentation videos, clinical environments with phantom head simulators, and registered clinician oversight of treatment plans
#### Constraints
- Cases were "unable to fully recreate the complexities of true patient interaction" and students could not replicate the experience of delivering care to a live patient

### Target Learners
- 4th- and 5th-year undergraduate dental students

### Learning Goals
- Clinical skills and confidence across technical and non-technical domains
- Contextual competency and the patient journey
- Reflective practice and self-regulation
