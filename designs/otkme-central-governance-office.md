# Office of Traditional Korean Medicine Education as central governance body

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Office of Traditional Korean Medicine Education (OTKME) is "the central body of development and evaluation of the curriculum, research on teaching-learning methods, management of human resources, equipment and consumables". It publishes the guidebooks, trains standardized patients, supervises practice facilities, organizes the Clinical Performance Test, supports PBL classes, and manages the CBT web server. It is led by a chairperson and three full-time officers with two steering committees and two quality-improvement committees.

## Design Implications
