# Six-session MS self-care education program covering self-care importance, diet, pain and fatigue management, urinary disorders, stress coping, and exercise

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A structured patient education program delivered in both intervention groups of the trial: six one-hour sessions held twice weekly in the MS Society conference room. Session 1 covers familiarization and "talking about self-care and its importance in controlling disease complications"; later sessions address proper diet in MS, "pain management methods and fatigue reduction and muscle relaxation methods," improving urinary disorders, coping with stress, anxiety and depression, and exercise in MS disease. The program was followed by one month of researcher follow-up with phone and in-person question answering.
