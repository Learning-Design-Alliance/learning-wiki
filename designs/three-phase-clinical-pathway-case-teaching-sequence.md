# Three-phase integrated clinical-pathway and case-based teaching sequence for residency rotations

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A rotation-level instructional pattern that structures endocrinology residency teaching in "three sequential phases": first, supervised independent study of clinical pathway manuals for common disorders; second, supervised patient care with orders issued according to the pathways; third, twice-weekly instructor lectures and student-led discussion of representative cases of rare or ultra-rare diseases following full admission-to-orders protocols. It combines standardized disease-management procedures with case-based instruction to streamline complex concepts.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty trained in the method who are attending physicians or higher with at least five years of teaching experience
- A departmentally developed clinical pathway manual and compiled representative cases
#### Constraints
- Developed and tested in a single endocrinology department over a four-week rotation

### Target Learners
- Residents in standardized residency training

### Learning Goals
- Standardized diagnostic and therapeutic procedures for endocrine and metabolic disorders
- Comprehensive clinical patient management
- Clinical reasoning
