# Simulated desaturation intubation protocol with randomized monitor positions on a difficult airway trainer

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A standardized simulation protocol in which subjects performed direct and video-assisted laryngoscopy once each on a Deluxe Difficult Airway Trainer with randomized monitor position (head, left, right). The simulated SpO2 was "programmed to begin at 100% and decrease at a rate of 1% per second 10 seconds after the start of the subject's attempt," with the pitch tone muted. Vital signs were displayed on a 24-inch portable monitor on a rolling cart 1.5 meters above the ground, with cart wheel positions taped to standardize setup across sites.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Laerdal LLEAP simulation software and a 24-inch portable monitor
- a difficult airway trainer with fully insufflated tongue
- audiovisual capture of attempts for blinded measurement review
#### Constraints
- The maximally insufflated tongue and 1%-per-second desaturation rate may not match all clinical scenarios
- findings apply only to scenarios without auditory pulse oximetry feedback

### Target Learners
- emergency medicine residents

### Learning Goals
- recognition of desaturation during intubation
- procedural performance under standardized simulated conditions
