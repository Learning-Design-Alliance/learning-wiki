# Floreo VR and Google Arts & Culture platforms used as the VR social competence education materials

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The intervention combined two VR applications across eight sessions. Floreo VR "teaches children social, behavioral, communication, and life skills through a range of simulations concerning children's daily life scenarios", delivered via Cardboard VR with instructor guidance; the research team selected only inclusive-learning modules since some are designed for children with autism spectrum disorder. Sessions 5–8 used Google Arts & Culture with Meta Quest 2 and Tablet VR for 360-degree videos, virtual museum tours, and cooperative games.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Affordable VR gear (Cardboard VR, Meta Quest 2, Tablet VR) and an instructor who guides and interacts with the child while watching the same session on a tablet
#### Constraints
- Some Floreo VR modules are designed for children with autism spectrum disorder, so only relevant inclusive-learning modules were selected

### Target Learners
- Rural elementary and middle school children aged 9–15 in Southwest China

### Learning Goals
- Social competence: social skills, communication, and social knowledge in school and public settings
