# ChatGPT standardized-patient role-play exercise with faculty-iteratively-developed Bell palsy prompt

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A virtual standardized-patient exercise in which a medical student inputs a faculty-developed prompt directing ChatGPT 4.0 to present as a patient with a neurological condition (Bell palsy), then interviews the chatbot verbally via a voice control extension and attempts a differential diagnosis. The prompt "underwent an iterative process" with a 6-member faculty team assessing consistency, accuracy, likelihood of misleading, output quality, and adherence to instructions. The exercise was used with 9 students and evaluated with pre/post open-ended surveys.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A carefully engineered prompt, developed iteratively by faculty, directing the LLM to adhere to the patient role and illness script
- ChatGPT accounts with voice interaction capability
- Ethical orientation of students on deidentifying data, AI limitations, and bias
#### Constraints
- The study used ChatGPT version 4.0; the model provided only positive feedback until the prompt was revised
- ChatGPT declined to answer questions on sensitive topics such as sexual history
- No visual or tonal cues are available in the interaction

### Target Learners
- medical students in the basic sciences section of an MD program

### Learning Goals
- history-taking and clinical interviewing skills
- differential diagnosis reasoning
- prompt engineering competence
