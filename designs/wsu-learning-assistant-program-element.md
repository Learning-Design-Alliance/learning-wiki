# Learning Assistant program for interdisciplinary science content courses for elementary education majors

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A program in which experienced and talented undergraduates, identified by a previous instructor as having "excellent interpersonal skills, leadership skills, proficient content knowledge, and a significant potential to be a teacher", support inquiry-based science content courses (Scie Ed 201 and 203) for elementary education majors. LAs facilitate group activities rather than lecture or grade, help plan and implement lessons, attend weekly coordination meetings, participate in a weekly LA training discussion group, and keep a weekly reflective journal. Eight LAs, one per class, served classrooms of roughly 30 students in 2009-2010, supported by a small stipend.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- LAs must be facilitators of student learning during group activities, not lecturers or graders
- LAs attend the weekly coordination meeting, participate in a weekly training discussion group, and keep a weekly journal/portfolio
- LAs are selected from students identified by a previous instructor as having strong interpersonal skills, leadership, content knowledge, and teaching potential
#### Constraints
- The initial funding source (a Center for Teaching and Learning grant) was depleted, so sustainability relies on non-monetary rewards such as a 1-credit transcript notation

### Target Learners
- elementary education majors in interdisciplinary science content courses
- undergraduate Learning Assistants from introductory science or STEM teaching majors

### Learning Goals
- scientific content knowledge
- scientific thinking and reasoning skills
- positive attitudes toward science and science teaching
- reflective practice about teaching and learning
