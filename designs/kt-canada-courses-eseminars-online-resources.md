# KT Canada modular courses, e-seminar series, and online resources for KT training

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The initiative delivers curriculum through modular courses in systematic reviews, pragmatic randomized trials, end-of-grant KT, and integrated KT; a monthly e-seminar series on KT research methodology with webcasts on the KT Canada website; a quarterly 'Research Operations' e-seminar for grant writing, manuscript preparation, and project management; and online modules and expert career interviews at ktclearinghouse.ca. Courses target audiences including trainees, researchers, physicians, and knowledge users, with 2011 offerings spanning evidence-based medicine, end-of-grant KT, systematic reviews, and pragmatic KT trials.
