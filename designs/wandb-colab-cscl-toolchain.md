# Weights & Biases and Google Colab as integrated CSCL toolchain

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Google Colab served as the cloud-based environment for real-time co-development of registration pipelines, while Weights & Biases logged experiments, tracked metrics per user, supported cross-team comparison, and hosted collaborative report authoring. Table 1 maps these features to CSCL principles such as joint task engagement, mutual awareness, and shared knowledge construction. The article states the setup enabled "synchronous and asynchronous collaboration, transparent workload distribution, and structured reflection on experimental outcomes".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Onboarding and support materials for students unfamiliar with W&B
- Instructor access to live dashboards for formative feedback
#### Constraints
- Linear editing constraints of W&B create difficulties for simultaneous collaboration
- Varying familiarity with tools may have affected engagement and perceived usefulness

### Target Learners
- Master's engineering and health technology students

### Learning Goals
- Collaborative coding
- Reproducible experimentation
- Transparent contribution tracking
