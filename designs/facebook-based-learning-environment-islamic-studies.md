# Facebook-based learning environment for Islamic Studies creativity

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A Facebook-based learning environment designed using the Isman Instructional Design Model to enhance creativity among Islamic Studies students. The teaching and learning is done through social network applications provided in Facebook such as wall, private message, chat box, note as well as other applications such as drama and film editing, TV documentary, and religious discussion programs uploaded in YouTube format. Students are given the choice to follow the lesson anywhere and any time they wish.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The environment requires Facebook social network applications including wall, private message, chat box, and note, plus uploaded video content.
#### Constraints
- The study was conducted over 14 weeks with Form Four Islamic Studies students in Malaysian secondary schools.

### Target Learners
- secondary school students

### Learning Goals
- creativity in writing
- creativity in problem solving
- creativity in producing missionary motto
