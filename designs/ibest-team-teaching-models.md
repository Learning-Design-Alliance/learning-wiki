# Six team teaching models for integrating basic skills and occupational instruction

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
I-BEST's signature feature is team teaching, in which a basic skills instructor and an occupational instructor are both present for at least half of instructional time. The report enumerates six forms: "Traditional team teaching", "Collaborative teaching", "Complementary-supportive teaching", "Parallel instruction", "Differentiated split class", and "Monitoring teacher". In the Accelerating Opportunity evaluation all six types were used, with complementary-supportive most popular; in ACE the complementary-supportive model was typical; in the Washington I-BEST study collaborative teaching and two variants of traditional team teaching were implemented.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Both the basic skills instructor and the occupational training instructor are required to be present in class for at least half of the total instructional time in an I-BEST course
- Instructors collaborate to identify joint learning outcomes for students in their class
#### Constraints
- The ACE model prescribes a 50 percent minimum of team teaching while Accelerating Opportunity calls for a minimum of 25 percent

### Target Learners
- Adult basic skills students in postsecondary occupational training programs

### Learning Goals
- Basic skills in the context of a chosen occupational field
