# Divergence-groan-convergence participatory decision-making structure for group ethics discussion

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The system structures each discussion stage around a participatory decision-making model comprising "divergence (generating diverse ideas), groan (struggling with differences), and convergence (narrowing down to shared decisions)." Each stage cycles through Idea-gathering (90 seconds of free idea presentation), Deliberation and Questioning (alternating turn-based phases), and Wrap-up (key idea selection). This structure was adapted from Kaner et al. and applied to all stages except Write Reviews, where participants write individually.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A facilitator (human or LLM) to manage phase transitions, speaking turns through stacking, and time management
#### Constraints
- Some participants noted the stacking-based turn format limited opportunities for in-depth exploration of a single idea

### Target Learners
- Undergraduate STEM students in group-based ethics education

### Learning Goals
- Structured ethical deliberation covering diverse perspectives within limited time
