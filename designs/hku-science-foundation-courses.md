# HKU Science Foundation courses: two required integrated courses teaching method and concepts over computation

## Description
A sequence of two one-semester courses required of all incoming science students regardless of intended major, introduced at HKU in 2012. The first teaches the scientific method, logical and quantitative reasoning, and mathematics through "the formulation of problems but not calculations"; the second surveys physics, astronomy, earth sciences, chemistry and biology to give "an overview of natural science" with unifying concepts. The courses start with real-life problems, and exams present new situations rather than lecture tasks.
