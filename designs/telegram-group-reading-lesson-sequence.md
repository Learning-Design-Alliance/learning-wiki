# Weekly supervised Telegram-group reading lesson sequence

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A recurring lesson routine for teaching a reading passage through a messaging group: within a fixed two-and-a-half-hour online time span, the teacher first posts key vocabulary with synonyms, poses pre-reading questions for group discussion, gives time for silent reading and idea sharing, sends an audio file of the text, and closes with comprehension questions answered collaboratively under teacher supervision. The article used this sequence weekly for six passages with intermediate university EFL students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- All students must be online during the designated weekly time span
- Students share questions, ideas and problems only in the group, not privately
- A teacher supervises discussion and answers questions at the next session
#### Constraints
- Chat outside the designated time span receives no teacher feedback
- The article applied the pattern only to intermediate-level university students

### Target Learners
- intermediate EFL university students

### Learning Goals
- reading comprehension
- reading strategy use
