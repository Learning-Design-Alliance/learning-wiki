# Student participation in professional Venus observations with NASA/IRTF SpeX (guidedog imaging, bigdog spectrograph, GXUI/GDV/T3 interfaces)

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An instructional experience element in which secondary school students join scheduled professional observing programs (2022A058 and 2022A038) at NASA/IRTF. The observation techniques shared by the programs included "sets of Venus images with several ﬁlters using the guide camera (hereafter guidedog), hyperspectral data with the slit of the spectrograph (hereafter bigdog)", guiding on a star for calibration spectra, and inference of flats, darks and bias. Students controlled the GXUI, GDV and T3 remote TCS interfaces.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- access to a scheduled professional observing program at the facility
- training session (remote attendance at a run) prior to independent operation
- supervision by experienced researchers during the student run
#### Constraints
- observations were of Venus with the SpeX instrument at NASA/IRTF; the note reports one independent student run of about 2 hours

### Target Learners
- secondary school students aged 15-16

### Learning Goals
- acquiring a basic knowledge of Venus and its atmosphere
- discussion about its habitability
- knowing the methods for observing Venus
