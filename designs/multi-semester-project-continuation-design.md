# Long-term multi-semester research projects carried forward across student groups

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Many Optics/Lasers projects are long-term, spanning many semesters and carried forward by successive student groups, senior thesis students, and summer researchers. Continuing groups may rely on a previous group's final report or project notebook, discuss the project with prior students, and are sometimes challenged to make an existing apparatus more accurate, more versatile, or more portable than what a previous group accomplished. Instructors emphasize that notebooks must be detailed enough to be useful to future groups.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- notebooks sufficiently detailed and well-organized to be useful to future student groups
- instructors who collaboratively brainstorm project topics aligned with departmental equipment
#### Constraints
- project topics are informed by the instructors' research interests and available equipment in the department

### Target Learners
- upper-division undergraduate physics students working in groups of 2 to 4

### Learning Goals
- engagement with a long-term research endeavor
- documentation practices
- iterative improvement of apparatus
