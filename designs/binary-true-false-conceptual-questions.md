# Use a binary True/False format rather than four-choice multiple choice for the conceptual section of timed tests

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article recommends replacing four-choice multiple-choice items with a binary format in the conceptual section of a timed test, because "Using a binary (True or False) system instead of multiple-choice system with four choices in the conceptual section reduces the amount of time spent in this section." This time saving is what allows conceptual questions to coexist with free-response quantitative problems inside a one-hour exam.
