# Set of 415 revisions to visuals in the 7th-grade CMP2 curriculum

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
The research team revised visuals in the Connected Mathematics 2 seventh-grade curriculum as part of a randomized controlled trial. Table 2 reports "The total number of revisions was 415 across 397 instances," with 69% being whole-visual removals of decorative or representational illustrations, 3% added mathematically-relevant visuals, and the remainder text-visual integration moves and modifications within existing visuals such as labels and color coding. Table 1 shows 47% of the original 432 visuals were math-irrelevant.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Revisions were guided by the multimedia, signaling, contiguity, and coherence principles and implemented by the research team
#### Constraints
- The article notes the math-relevant visuals were already well designed, so revisions focused mostly on removing math-irrelevant visuals and improving integration

### Target Learners
- 7th-grade mathematics students

### Learning Goals
- mathematics learning from textbook visuals
