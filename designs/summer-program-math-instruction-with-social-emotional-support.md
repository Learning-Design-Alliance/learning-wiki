# Pairing intensive math instruction with emotional and social support in a summer program

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Math Corps pairs an academic component with a relational one: its founders' "primary goal for the camp was to support the emotional and social needs of the students in Detroit," and they used their math-teaching backgrounds to add "high-quality math instruction" to that support. The pattern is an intensive summer camp format in which mentoring and positive influences are delivered alongside substantive instruction in a school subject, rather than as separate programming.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Staff with subject-matter teaching backgrounds who can deliver high-quality instruction while also attending to students' emotional and social needs.
#### Constraints
- The article describes this pattern as implemented in a summer camp for middle school students in a single city; it does not claim the pattern generalizes to other settings or grade levels.

### Target Learners
- middle school students who could benefit from mentoring and positive influences

### Learning Goals
- mathematics achievement
- social and emotional development
