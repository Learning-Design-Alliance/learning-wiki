# Four-module unplugged learning path integrating AI foundations with primary mathematics

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A structured, replicable sequence of four two-hour unplugged modules: introduction to AI, classification principles, classification representations, and final assessment with reflection. It builds AI concepts (perception, representation and reasoning, learning, societal impact) on mathematical elements already in primary curricula, using hands-on activities such as the Monster family classification, mushroom and fish classification with Euler-Venn diagrams, tables, and decision trees, and a floor-based decision tree. The authors state: "We ground in constructivism and constructionism [26,25], and adopt a spiral learning approach [4], ensuring that concepts are reintroduced at increasing levels of complexity."
