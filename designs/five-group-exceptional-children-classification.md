# Five-group classification of exceptional children via teacher questionnaire census

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A preliminary census of exceptional children was taken by questionnaire to teachers, after which "the children were roughly classified into five groups": feeble-minded or insane, backward, exceptionally gifted, incorrigible, and children of good intelligence suffering physical defects. This classification organized the department's subsequent intensive case studies.
