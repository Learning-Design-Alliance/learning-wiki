# P2BPS: a scientific-approach-based learning program structured as a sequence of scientific-method stages

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
P2BPS is a learning program designed so that students "construct and find their own knowledge through a series of scientific methods". It proceeds through the stages of observing, formulating problems and hypotheses, collecting data by experiment, processing and associating data, drawing conclusions, and communicating what was found. The article applies it in general physics II for prospective physics teachers, beginning each activity with an authentic real problem encountered in everyday life and organizing group investigation, analysis and communication of results to other students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Learning activities begin with an authentic real problem found, seen and experienced by students in everyday life
- Experiments must give students the opportunity to identify problems, formulate questions and hypotheses, plan and conduct experiments, analyze results, and report and communicate
#### Constraints
- Studied in a general physics II course for prospective physics teachers at one Indonesian university; the article does not claim generalization beyond this setting

### Target Learners
- prospective physics teachers (undergraduate physics and mathematics education students)

### Learning Goals
- generic science skills such as observation, symbolic language, logical inference, and mathematical modeling
