# Audio-guided point-of-care EEG e-learning module covering normal waveforms, artifacts, and seizure patterns

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A novel e-learning module devised by a PEM expert and a neurophysiologist, using "audio-guided PowerPoint (Microsoft Corp) presentations." Its ~60-minute main segment has two objectives: "providing a systematic framework for assessing cortical activity by familiarizing participants with normal EEG waveforms," and educating on seizure patterns in pocEEGs, aligned with American Clinical Neurophysiology Society 2021 guidelines. A 20-minute technical chapter covers electrode placement, recording, and troubleshooting; content was unlocked via passcode after the pretest.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access via passcode after pretest completion; delivered through REDCap survey links at the study site
#### Constraints
- The technical chapter content is not tested; the module was tailored to the local educational context and the test instrument was not formally validated

### Target Learners
- Senior medical staff (PEM attendings and fellows)
- Junior medical staff (residents)
- Registered nurses in a pediatric emergency department

### Learning Goals
- Identify normal cortical activity, artifacts, and seizure patterns in 2-channel point-of-care EEG
- Basic EEG knowledge of montages, the 10-20 system, and signal generation
