# Age-graded German particle physics outreach programme portfolio

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The German particle physics outreach programme operates four age-graded formats: the Kinder Uni lecture for children under 11, weekly school science clubs for ages 10-13, cloud chamber workshops for ages 12-16, and full-day masterclasses for students over 16, of which "Netzwerk Teilchenwelt held over 122 masterclasses in Germany" in 2023. The formats progress from entertaining, minimally abstract lectures with audience participation to intensive engagement with authentic scientific data from particle physics experiments. The portfolio is designed to reach pupils at different stages, with the younger formats targeting students before the age-15 decision about continuing physics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Interventions must reach pupils at an early stage, before the age-15 subject choice that sets the trajectory for future STEM paths
- Researchers or undergraduate students as instructors to create a non-judgmental atmosphere of academic collaboration
#### Constraints
- Masterclasses are directed almost exclusively at students who have already chosen to keep physics as a school subject in their last years of school
- Each federal state has its own educational system, which makes generalised statements challenging

### Target Learners
- Children under 11
- Teenagers aged 10-13
- Students aged 12-16
- Students over 16 who have chosen physics

### Learning Goals
- Demystify particle physics and showcase career opportunities to increase fading interest among young students
