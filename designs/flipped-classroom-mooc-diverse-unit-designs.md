# Flipped Classroom MOOC with diverse unit-level learning designs as research context

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A seven-week MOOC developed by a Chinese university in 2014 and offered on the iCourse 163 platform, targeting in-service and pre-service teachers. Each unit followed a different design: "Unit 1 followed a traditional lecture-based design", Unit 2 case-based learning, Unit 3 project-based, Unit 4 case-based with reading activities, and Unit 5 problem-based. The article states that "it is precisely this variety and diversity of the learning design that made this MOOC unique and provided a suitable research context to address the research questions".

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each unit uses different learning resources and offers different learning pathways to cater to different pedagogical intentions
#### Constraints
- No unit quiz and peer review were offered for unit 0 and unit 6; the study used offerings from the 4th to the 16th round, after the curriculum structure stabilised

### Target Learners
- in-service and pre-service teachers in China

### Learning Goals
- understanding and applying the flipped classroom pedagogy in daily teaching practice
