# QCaMP student research projects in weeks 3–4

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
During the last two weeks, student groups completed largely student-driven projects with mentor guidance including daily check-ins, developing "skills in collaboration, experimental design, troubleshooting, and synthesizing their results to be communicated clearly through a scientific-style poster". Nine projects are listed in Table III: two QKD tabletop experiments (ThorLabs and University of Waterloo kits), double-slit interference (TeachSpin), quantum eraser, Planck's constant with LEDs, quantum sensing with a photoluminescence microscope, quantum simulation and quantum machine learning with VQE, quantum games, and quantum annealing on Star Battle puzzles.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mentor guidance including daily check-ins
- Project equipment such as commercially available kits or instructor support (e.g., QICK-DAWG for quantum sensing)
#### Constraints
- D-Wave prohibits providing accounts on their cloud platform, Leap, to minors, so the quantum annealing project ran on a local classical simulated annealing simulator
- The quantum annealing project required more Python training than the Python Primer provided

### Target Learners
- High school students

### Learning Goals
- Experimental design, troubleshooting, collaboration, and science communication through scientific posters
