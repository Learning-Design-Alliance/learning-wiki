# Stress paradigm logic model: intervention components strengthen coping and adult relationships that buffer effects of urban poverty on academic and emotional-behavioral outcomes.

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The article's logic model, grounded in the stress paradigm, holds that exposure to environmental stressors predicts negative outcomes and that protective processes, particularly coping skills and supportive adult relationships, can mitigate these effects. As the article states, "components of the developing intervention are expected to strengthen coping strategies and relationships with adults in the lives of adolescent participants in ways that buffer against the deleterious effects of stressors". Relationships with adults are expected to foster adaptive coping, which further strengthens relationships, extending beyond mentors to improve relationships with teachers and parents and thereby impact student engagement, achievement, and school completion.
