# New Education Model (NETM): structured, interactive, individualized vaccine education grounded in Social Cognitive Theory and the Information Processing Model

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
The New Education Model (NETM) is a "structured, theory-informed, and interactive educational intervention" grounded in Social Cognitive Theory and the Information Processing Model. Unlike passive information delivery, NETM "adopts an interactive, individualized, and feedback-oriented framework that actively engages parents in the learning process and targets the deconstruction of vaccine-related misinformation". Content covers scientific foundations of vaccination, the neonatal immunization schedule, and deconstruction of common misconceptions, delivered one-on-one with structured question-and-answer sessions and visual and written materials supported by peer-reviewed evidence. In this study it was validated by a five-expert panel and delivered individually by a trained researcher midwife and a specialist neonatologist.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivery by trained healthcare professionals (a trained researcher midwife and a specialist neonatologist)
- Content validated by an expert panel
- A private, distraction-free setting (a designated Parent Education Room)
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only in a NICU setting with parents self-reporting vaccine hesitancy or refusal
- Sessions lasted approximately 115 ± 11 min individually

### Target Learners
- Parents of infants hospitalized in a Neonatal Intensive Care Unit

### Learning Goals
- Reduce vaccine hesitancy and misinformation
- Improve vaccine-related attitudes and informed decision-making
