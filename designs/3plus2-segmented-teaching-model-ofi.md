## Description
The article proposes a modular lab-session structure in which students first complete guided experiments and then pursue open exploration. As printed, the "modular “3+2” teaching design (3 hours of guided experiments + 2 hours of open exploration) accommodates diverse learners", letting undergraduates focus on foundational principles while graduate students tackle advanced signal processing tasks. It is used to structure the OFI forced-vibration module and is offered as a scalable case study bridging classical theory with modern optical techniques.
