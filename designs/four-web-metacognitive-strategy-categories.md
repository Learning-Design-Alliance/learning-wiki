# Four instructional strategy categories for web-based metacognitive skills training

## Description
The article integrates prior work into "four main categories, including advance organizer, concept map, scaffolding, and problem-solving strategies" for designing web-based metacognitive training. Advance organizers, drawn from Meaningful Learning Theory, help students integrate new knowledge with existing superordinate concepts; concept maps visualize relationships among concepts; scaffolding includes prompts, checklists, and procedural questions; and problem-solving strategies such as the IMPROVE method train students to raise metacognitive questions. The authors applied these categories in designing their website's four sections.
