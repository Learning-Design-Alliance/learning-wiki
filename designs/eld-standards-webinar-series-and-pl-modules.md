# Five-part ELD standards webinar series and forthcoming professional learning modules supporting OPTEL implementation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Because OPTEL relies on the 2014 English learner development (ELD) standards, R15CC helped CDE design and deliver a five-part webinar series building the field's capacity to use that foundational document, deepening understanding of the standards and their proficiency levels, "which align with OPTEL ratings." CDE is also collaborating with county and district leaders on professional learning modules scheduled for release in fall 2024, focusing on parent perspectives and accommodating English learners with disabilities.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The rollout sparked renewed interest in the foundational ELD standards, which underpin OPTEL ratings
#### Constraints
- The professional learning modules were scheduled for release in fall 2024 and are not yet evaluated in this report

### Target Learners
- California teachers, county and district leaders, and teacher preparation programs

### Learning Goals
- Effective use of ELD standards and OPTEL elements in reclassification and formative proficiency support
