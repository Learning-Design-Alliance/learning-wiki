# STSE (CTSA) approach as the framing perspective for teaching nuclear physics for scientific literacy

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The article frames the entire teaching sequence in a Science, Technology, Society and Environment perspective (CTSA in Portuguese, STSE in English), aiming at scientific literacy. The abstract states: "The goal of the sequence is to approach the subject in a way as to stimulate scientiﬁc literacy, from a perspective involving Science, Technology, Society and Environment (STSE)". Nuclear physics is presented as intrinsically suited to this approach because it connects to contemporaneity and interdisciplinarity — fission and its military and peaceful applications, fusion as the Sun's energy source, nucleosynthesis ("somos poeira das estrelas"), the energy matrix, and radioactive decays and applications.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Subthemes with rich STSE discussion potential, such as fission applications, fusion in stars, the energy matrix and radioactive decay applications
#### Constraints
- The approach is developed for high-school (ensino médio) nuclear physics content in the Brazilian BNCC context

### Target Learners
- High school students

### Learning Goals
- Scientific literacy
- Understanding connections between nuclear physics, technology, society and environment
