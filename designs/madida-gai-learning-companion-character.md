# Madida: a fictional GAI learning companion supporting the backcasting exercise

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Madida is a fictional GAI character serving Use case 2 as a learning companion for the values-thinking backcasting exercise; "Its role includes helping the student prepare their backcasting presentation by engaging in a sparring session about their backcasting exercise." Madida's sparring approach "was prompt-engineered based on the backcasting questions formulated according to the I SEE project," and the character offers emotional support while helping students accomplish the task. Domain experts described Madida's interaction as natural and its nature as encouraging, supportive, and approachable.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Prompt engineering based on structured backcasting questions, iterated through multiple testing rounds
#### Constraints
- The associated exercise includes a limited number of structured questions, which is why testing required fewer questions than Tero
