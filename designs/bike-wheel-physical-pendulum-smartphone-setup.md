# Physical pendulum apparatus with smartphone sensors and Tracker video analysis

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The experiment uses a rigid body — a bicycle wheel with its hub fixed on a horizontal axis — rotating in a vertical plane, with a smartphone affixed to the outer edge of the tire. An Android LG G2 D805 with a 3-axis accelerometer (0.001 m/s2 precision) and 3-axis gyroscope (0.001 rad/s precision) recorded readings via the Androsensor app, and a digital camera recording was examined with the free, open Tracker video analysis software.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An appropriate app (Androsensor) to communicate with the sensors and record readings
- Setup of the experiment duration and sampling period
- Technical information about the exact location of the sensors within the smartphone
#### Constraints
- Only the radial and tangential acceleration components and the angular velocity in the perpendicular plane are relevant in this experiment

### Target Learners
- high school students
- undergraduate physics students

### Learning Goals
- analysing the dynamics of a compound pendulum
- comparing measurement techniques for angle, angular velocity and angular acceleration
