Use the WSCC model as a framework for school improvement plans, accountability measures, and cross-sector initiatives¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article's implications section recommends concrete uses of the WSCC model by multiple audiences. School districts and schools can use it "as a framework for school improvement plans and initiatives," structuring school improvement teams with representation from each component. State education and health departments can use it for accountability measures and funding announcements; higher education programs can design preservice and in-service professional development toward effective implementation; philanthropic organizations can emphasize community engagement aspects.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Collaborative development of local school policies, processes, and practices; representation from each model component on school improvement teams

Constraints¶

  • Specific interventions, curricula, classroom practices, and resources brought together to implement the framework depend on the local context

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students, school staff, administrators, families, and community partners

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Advancing educational attainment and healthy development through coordinated school improvement efforts

  • The coordinated school health approach saw scattered US adoption, hindered by pressures on administrators and lack of integration into School Improvement Plans [+W]
