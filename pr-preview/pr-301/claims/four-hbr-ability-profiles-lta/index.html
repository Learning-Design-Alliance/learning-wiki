Hanna Grimm · Peter A. Edelsbrunner · Kornelia Möller. (2023). Accommodating heterogeneity: the interaction of instructional scaffolding with student preconditions in the learning of hypothesis-based reasoning. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09601-9

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Model fitting for the latent transition analysis; the four-profile model had the lowest BIC among converging solutions, and profiles capture patterns across the affirmative, refuting, and irrelevant-evidence scores while handling ceiling effects.

"In the latent transition analysis, the best model fit (Table 3) was achieved by extracting four different ability-profiles based on the data of students in both intervention conditions."

Hanna Grimm · Peter A. Edelsbrunner · Kornelia Möller 2023 (2)¶

Hanna Grimm · Peter A. Edelsbrunner · Kornelia Möller. (2023). Accommodating heterogeneity: the interaction of instructional scaffolding with student preconditions in the learning of hypothesis-based reasoning. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09601-9

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Description of the extracted profiles: the relevant profile (38% pretest, 19% posttest) solved relevant-evidence items but not irrelevant ones; the low profile described 34% (11% posttest), the intermediate 17% (39% posttest), and the full profile 11% (39% posttest).

"The biggest profile at pretest described 38% of students (19% at posttest), who could successfully solve all items involving evidence from events relevant to the hypothesis; that is, these students had high ability levels on the affirmative and refuting items, but low ability levels on the irrelevant items."
