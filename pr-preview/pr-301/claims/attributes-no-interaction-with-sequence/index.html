Evidence¶

Eman Ganaiem 2025¶

Eman Ganaiem, Ido Roll. (2025). Investigating the order of example-problem sequences when learning experimental design and graphing competencies. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09750-7

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

In the graphing MANCOVA controlling for attributes and pre-test, interest in science significantly predicted application and transfer scores, partial η² = 0.065; no condition × attribute interaction reached significance.

"A significant positive effect was found for factor 2 (interest in science): Wilk's Λ = 0.935, F (2, 98) = 3.385, p = 0.038, partial η 2 = 0.065."

Eman Ganaiem 2025 (2)¶

Eman Ganaiem, Ido Roll. (2025). Investigating the order of example-problem sequences when learning experimental design and graphing competencies. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09750-7

q3 · i? · causal · r2

In the experimental design MANCOVA with the three survey-derived attribute factors, neither condition nor any attribute factor was significant, and no condition × factor interactions were significant.

"Results reveal that there is no significant main effect for condition (Wilk's Λ = 0.997, F (4, 184) = 0.076, p = 0.989, partial η2 = 0.002), or any of the three fac tors: inquiry experience (Wilk's Λ = 0.969, F (2, 92) = 1.894, p = 0.156, partial η 2 = 0.04)"
