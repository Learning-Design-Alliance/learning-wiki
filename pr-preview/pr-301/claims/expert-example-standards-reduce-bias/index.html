Evidence¶

Linda Froese 2022¶

Linda Froese, Julian Roelle. (2022). Expert example standards but not idea unit standards help learners accurately evaluate the quality of self-generated examples. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09293-z

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

2×2 factorial ANOVA on overall bias in the N = 131 experiment found a significant main effect of expert example standards, ηp2 = .09, with lower bias in the expert-standards groups; the interaction was not significant.

"significant main effect of expert example standards, F(1, 127) = 12.00, p = .001, ηp 2 = .09. S tudents who received expert example standards showed lower overall bias."
