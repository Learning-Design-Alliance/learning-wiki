In the editors' summary of Enyedy et al., young children (6–8 years old) in a CSCL classroom combining computer support (augmented reality) and collaborative learning (socio-dramatic play) made significant progress along the trajectory from embodied cognition to explicit discourse about forces and motion. → Stahl 2012

Editorial summary of Enyedy, Danish, Delacruz, and Kumar's qualitative analysis, extending their CSCL 2011 best-paper quantitative study of the Spases project. The cognitive-trajectory finding is attributed to that article.

"the authors show how young children (6 – 8 years old) in a CSCL classroom can already make significant progress along this cognitive trajectory"
