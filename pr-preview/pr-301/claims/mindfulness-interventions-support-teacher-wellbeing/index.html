Pauliina Avola 2025¶

Pauliina Avola, Tiina Soini-Ikonen, Anne Jyrkiäinen, Viivi Pentikäinen. (2025). Interventions to Teacher Well-Being and Burnout A Scoping Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-09986-2

q3 · i? · review · r2

Review's outcome synthesis for the mindfulness/meditation theme (12 studies). "Of the mindfulness and meditation interventions, ten (n =10) supported teacher well-being", with reported benefits including reduced burnout, improved perceived stress and depression, increased resilience and self-compassion, and maintained or enhanced gains at follow-up (Rupprecht et al., 2017).

The review reports two non-supportive mindfulness studies: "participants reported reductions in perceived stress and emotional exhaustion, but the differences were insignificant" (Ancona & Mendelson, 2014), and Schwind et al. (2022) reported improved well-being and self-compassion scores that were not statistically significant.
