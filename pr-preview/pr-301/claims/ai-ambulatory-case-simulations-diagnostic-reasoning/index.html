q2 · i? · review · r2

In the review's ambulatory-settings theme, it describes longitudinal case-based simulations with AI-generated diagnostic suggestions and concludes that "AI-driven case simulations in outpatient contexts sharpen diagnostic skills and encourage predictive, patient-centered reasoning." This is narrative synthesis; no effect sizes are printed.
