Mackiewicz 2011¶

Mackiewicz, S. M., Wood, C. L., Cooke, N. L., & Mazzotti, V. L. (2011). Effects of Peer Tutoring With Audio Prompting on Vocabulary Acquisition for Struggling Readers. Remedial and Special Education, 32(4), 345–354. https://doi.org/10.1177/0741932510362507

Social validity questionnaire with six 4-point Likert-type items read aloud plus two open-ended questions. "All of the students indicated that they agreed or strongly agreed" they learned new words using the VoicePod; six of eight reported using tutored words in writing assignments.
