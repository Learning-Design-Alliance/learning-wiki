Heather Toomey Zimmerman 2023¶

Heather Toomey Zimmerman, Susan M. Land, Lillyanna Faimon, Yu-Chen Chiu. (2023). Mobile augmented reality supporting families’ immersive collaborative learning: Learning-on-the-move for place-based geoscience sense-making. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-023-09399-9

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Video-based qualitative analysis of 17 families; the article states all families navigated between microsites with the GPS map, and a 5-year-old boy "successfully used the GPS map for wayfinding" while his mother cared for a younger sibling. Qualitative finding; no effect sizes printed.

"All the families in our dataset were able to engage in the actional immersion of the wayfinding map to navigate from one microsite to the next."
