Evidence¶

Sinha 2021¶

Sinha, T., Kapur, M., West, R., Catasta, M., Hauswirth, M., & Trninic, D. (2021). Differential Benefits of Explicit Failure-Driven and Success-Driven Scaffolding in Problem-Solving Prior to Instruction. Journal of Educational Psychology, 113(3), 530–555. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000483

q3 · i? · causal · r?

In the same classroom intervention (N = 221), the failure-driven scaffolding condition showed a differential benefit on constructive reasoning quality relative to success-driven scaffolding, as the article reports students "demonstrated higher quality of constructive reasoning."

"Students exposed to failure-driven scaffolding, however, demonstrated higher quality of constructive reasoning."
