Evidence¶

Charles Crook 2022¶

Charles Crook. (2022). CSsCL: the performance of collaborative learning. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-022-09364-y

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The commentary summarises the Vogel et al. Special Issue study: learners themselves made the script adaptive by selecting minimal or maximal support detail, and the reported moderator was prior self-regulation skill; no effect size is printed.

"In the end, their notion of learner agency is shown to be a feasible basis for designing adaptive guidance in a collaborative script. But here such guidance only impacted regulatory actions among those students who started the project with more advanced self-regulation skills."
