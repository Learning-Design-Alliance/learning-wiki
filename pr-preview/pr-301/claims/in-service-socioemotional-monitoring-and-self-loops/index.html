q2 · i? · associational · r1

Process-mining comparison of socioemotional challenge transitions in the aggregated event logs; this indicated "a greater tendency for monitoring activities to follow socioemotional challenges in the in-service groups".

"In-service teachers exhibited stronger sequential transitions between socioemotional challenges and monitoring (f = 16, 25) than pre-service teachers (f = 7, 8)."

Faisal Channa 2026 (2)¶

Faisal Channa, Piia Näykki, Päivi Häkkinen, Kristóf Fenyvesi, Takumi Yada. (2026). Sequential patterns of challenges and socially shared regulation of learning: a comparison of pre-service and in-service teachers’ collaborative learning. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09818-y

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Recurrent-pattern analysis of the process models; pre-service groups repeated strategy-oriented controlling more often, while in-service groups showed more monitoring self-loops (f = 213 vs. 156), reflecting recurring evaluative activity.

"Pre-service teachers exhibited more frequent self-loops of controlling ( f = 233) than in-service teachers ( f = 106), indicating greater repetition of strategy-oriented regulation within their interaction sequences."
