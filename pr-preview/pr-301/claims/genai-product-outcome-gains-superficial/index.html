Narrative review attribution: the article reports, citing Mariñoso et al. (2025), that "AI-assisted students can achieve higher academic scores than peers using traditional resources". It notes these studies' primary outcomes capture product quality—grades, attitudes, well-being—and do not answer whether AI helps students build durable, transferable knowledge.

"quasi-experimental evidence indicates that AI-assisted students can achieve higher academic scores than peers using traditional resources"
