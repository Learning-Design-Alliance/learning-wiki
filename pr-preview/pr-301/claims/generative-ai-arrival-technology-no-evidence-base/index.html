Perl-Nussbaum 2026¶

Perl-Nussbaum, D., & Finkelstein, N. D. (2026). A framework for institutional change in the age of AI. International Journal of STEM Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-026-00649-4

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical argument, drawing on Reich and Dukes (2025), that AI inverted the adoption-technology sequence in which research and development precede scaling. The article offers this as an argued characterization, not a tested result: "widespread and spontaneous student and teacher use" preceded institutional response.

"AI enters our classrooms not through deliberate adoption of evidence-based tools but through widespread and spontaneous student and teacher use"
