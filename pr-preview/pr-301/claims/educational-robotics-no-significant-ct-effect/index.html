Evidence¶

Fan Ouyang and Weiqi Xu 2024¶

Fan Ouyang and Weiqi Xu. (2024). The effects of educational robotics in STEM education: a multilevel meta-analysis. International Journal of STEM Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-024-00469-4

q3 · i0 · quant-synthesis · r2

Random-effects meta-analysis of the computational-thinking outcome from only 4 effect sizes in 4 studies (no hierarchical structure, so no multilevel model). The mean effect was g = 0.079, p = 0.434, CI crossing zero — "a minor and insignificant effect".

"The random effect model analysis found a mean effect size of 0.079 (SE = 0.101; p = 0.434; 95% CI was − 0.119–0.277), which indicated that educational robotics had a minor and insignificant effect on students' CT in STEM education"
