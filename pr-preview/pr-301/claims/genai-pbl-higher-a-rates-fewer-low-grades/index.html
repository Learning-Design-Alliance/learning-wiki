Micky C. Nnamdi 2026¶

Micky C. Nnamdi, J. Ben Tamo, Benoit Marteau, Wenqi Shi, May D. Wang. (2026). Advancing Problem-Based Learning in Biomedical Engineering in the Era of Generative AI. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.16558 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2503.16558

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Historical cohort comparison of grade distributions before (2016–2017, 2020) versus after (2021–2023) GenAI integration, prespecifying A-rate and low-grade-rate endpoints. The A-rate difference was "∆ = +27.3percentage points" (Welch t= 4.28, p= 0.042); low grades fell 16.3 points (p= 0.0076). No standardized effect size is printed.

"the Intervention group exhibited a clear rightward shift in the grade distribution, characterized by higher A-rates compared to the Control group (Control:39.1 % vs.Intervention:66.4 %;∆ = +27.3percentage points; Welch t= 4.28,p= 0.042"
