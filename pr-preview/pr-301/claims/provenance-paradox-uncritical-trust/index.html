Ruqi Sun 2026¶

Ruqi Sun, Jiaping Li, Wenhui Tao, Ximing Zheng, Yuefeng Tan, Jiahao Wei, and Yuxin Ma. (2026). VeriForge: Mitigating Latent Knowledge Gaps in Narrative Drafting via Mixed-Initiative Scaffolding. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09698 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.09698

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Controlled deception in the within-subjects study: the source passage on the second Knowledge Card was replaced with an unrelated passage while keeping the AI-generated description unchanged, creating a detectable mismatch. The test measured evidence inspection, not hallucination detection.

"Only 1 of 12 participants detected the source-card mismatch. The remaining 11 did not notice."
