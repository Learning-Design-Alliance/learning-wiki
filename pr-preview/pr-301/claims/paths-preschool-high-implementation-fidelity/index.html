Evidence¶

Domitrovich 2007¶

Domitrovich, C. E., Cortes, R. C., & Greenberg, M. T. (2007). Improving Young Children's Social and Emotional Competence: A Randomized Trial of the Preschool "PATHS" Curriculum. Journal of Primary Prevention, 28, 67–91. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10935-007-0081-0

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Process evaluation using monthly coordinator ratings on 13 items rated on a 4-point Likert scale; fidelity ranged from 2.79 to 3.96, generalization from 2.78 to 3.93, and openness to consultation from 2.57 to 4.0 across teachers.

"The overall means for each of these dimensions were high (3.62, 3.37, and 3.67, respectively), indicating that teachers delivered the curriculum as designed in the manual and were positively engaged with coordinators in the implementation process."
