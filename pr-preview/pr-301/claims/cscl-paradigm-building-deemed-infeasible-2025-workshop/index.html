Qualitative outcome of the on-site third workshop group and panel discussion at ISLS 2025, reconstructed from the shared slide deck and participants' memories. Consensus was that "the challenge is to maintain paradigm diversity in the field" rather than adopt one paradigm.

"The previous sections and discussions evidenced consensus across groups and the panel that, instead of aligning CSCL to a normal science with a single paradigm, the challenge is to maintain paradigm diversity in the field."
