Mendes 2017¶

Mendes, G. G., & Barrera, S. D. (2017). Phonological Processing and Reading and Writing Skills in Literacy. Paidéia (Ribeirão Preto), 27(68), 298-305. https://doi.org/10.1590/1982-43272768201707

q2 · i? · associational · r1

The study investigated the contribution of phonological awareness, phonological memory, rapid naming and visual processing to reading and writing in a sample of 50 third-grade students of an Elementary Public School, finding that "phonological awareness and phonological memory are the skills that contributed most."

"The results indicated that phonological awareness and phonological memory are the skills that contributed most to the initial performance in reading and writing."
