Conceptual synthesis in the technical foundation section of the review, arguing from the stereoscopic presentation and multimodal capabilities of XR that agent realism is higher than in non-immersive media; the review offers no new empirical test of this assertion.

"In summary, immersive media environments enable a higher degree of both visual and behavioral agent realism compared to non-immersive formats (cf. Petersen et al., 2021)."
