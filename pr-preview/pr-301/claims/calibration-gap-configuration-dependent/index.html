q2 · i? · review · r2

The review attributes this experimental finding to Steyvers et al. (2025), reporting it as a measured consequence of training objectives that reward probable continuations. The same experiments showed that adjusting explanations to reflect internal confidence narrowed both the calibration gap and the discrimination gap.

"the confidence users attributed to model answers systematically exceeded the models’ internal confidence, and longer explanations increased user confidence even where they did not improve accuracy."
