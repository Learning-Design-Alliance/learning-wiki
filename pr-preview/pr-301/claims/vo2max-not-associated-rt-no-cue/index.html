  • Higher cardiorespiratory fitness is associated with faster reaction times for stimuli following valid cues in healthy male high-school students — related
  • Cardiorespiratory fitness (VO2max) is the physical fitness measure most strongly correlated with cognitive functioning in adolescents — related
  • Neither muscle mass nor weekly training frequency is associated with reaction time across cue conditions in healthy male high-school students — related
  • Later time-point of cognitive testing is associated with faster reaction times, with significantly slower RT in the morning — related
