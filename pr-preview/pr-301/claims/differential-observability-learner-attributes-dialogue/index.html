Evidence¶

Naiming Liu 2025¶

Naiming Liu, Brittany Bradford, Johaun Hatchett, Gabriel Diaz, Lorenzo Luzi, Zichao Wang, Debshila Basu Mallick, Richard Baraniuk. (2025). Learning Context: A Unified Framework and Roadmap for Context-Aware AI in Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.24362 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2512.24362

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Pilot of 35 simulated multi-turn student–tutor interactions with the GPT-5.1 model: one LLM generated dialogues conditioned on an explicit LC, a second blinded LLM recovered misconceptions and profile attributes from dialogue alone, revealing a "signiﬁcant “observability gap”" between conceptual and behavioral data.

"While anxiety was recovered with 100% accuracy, conscientiousness (68.6%) and language proﬁciency (60%) were substantially more difficult to infer."
