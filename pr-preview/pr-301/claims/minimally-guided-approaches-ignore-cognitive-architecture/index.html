Kirschner 2006¶

Kirschner, P; Sweller, John; Clark, Robert. (2006). Why minimal guidance during instruction does not work: An analysis of the failure of constructivist, discovery, problem-based, experiential, and inquiry-based teaching. Journal Article. http://hdl.handle.net/1959.4/50516. doi:10.1207/s15326985ep4102_1

This is the article's theoretical argument, made in the abstract: minimally guided approaches are "very popular and intuitively appealing" yet, the authors argue, they "ignore both the structures that constitute human cognitive architecture." No new data are reported for this point.
