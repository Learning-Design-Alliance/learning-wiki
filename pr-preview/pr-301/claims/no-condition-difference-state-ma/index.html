The mindfulness interventions did not significantly change anticipatory or executive state-MA relative to cognitive support alone¶

Claim · All claims Evidence · 1 study (2 entries) · causal r2 · q3

In Rief et al. (2026), seventh graders practised algebra in the "Math with Matt" tutoring system. The Mindful version added an LLM-based mindful chat, in which the pedagogical agent Matt gave context-sensitive emotional support, and mindful (not only evaluative) feedback and hint messages; the Cognitive version gave cognitive support only.

Evidence¶

Rief 2026¶

Rief, V., Hladký, M., Yoo, M., Heel, S., Sato, S., Nagashima, T. (2026). Beyond Problem Solving: Large Language Models for Emotional and Reflective Support in Mathematics Learning. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02611 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.02611

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Linear mixed-effect model on anticipatory state-MA (STAI-6 administered before pre/posttest) in the 42-student classroom experiment found no significant group, time, or interaction effects; equivalence was not tested.

"A linear mixed-effect model revealed no statistically significant effects of group, time, and interaction between groups and time for anticipatory state-MA."

Rief 2026 (2)¶

Rief, V., Hladký, M., Yoo, M., Heel, S., Sato, S., Nagashima, T. (2026). Beyond Problem Solving: Large Language Models for Emotional and Reflective Support in Mathematics Learning. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02611. doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.02611

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Summary of the state-MA mixed-effect models in the classroom experiment: the Mindful condition showed no significant advantage over the Cognitive condition on either state-MA type; no effect size was printed for the between-group contrasts.

"Thus, the Mindful ITS version did not significantly change the anticipatory or executive state-MA when compared against the Cognitive ITS version without the mindfulness interventions."
