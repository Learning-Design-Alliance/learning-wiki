Evidence¶

Hester 2022¶

Hester, C., Lazarev, V., Zacamy, J., Nardi, C., & Feygin, A. (2022). Student group differences in Arkansas' indicators of postsecondary readiness and success. Regional Educational Laboratory Southwest. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/edlabs/regions/southwest/rel_2023-145.asp

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Descriptive analysis of statewide administrative data for 63,679 Arkansas grade 6 students in 2008/09 or 2009/10, using t tests for proportions with differences of 10 percentage points or more considered substantial. The authors report that "substantial differences occurred for seven of the eight indicators" for each of these three comparison groups.

"The largest number of substantial differences in attaining academic indicators of postsecondary readiness and success were between Black and White students, between students eligible for the National School Lunch Program and students not eligible, and between students who entered grade 6 after age 13 and students who did not."
