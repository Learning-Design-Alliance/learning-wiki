Dennis Condron 2021¶

The seasonal-comparison analysis of MAP Growth data reports the compensatory pattern holds for reading, language, and science "but not math skills". No effect size or test statistic is printed in the available text.

"Our results suggest that schooling has a compensatory effect on inequality in reading, language, and science skills but not math skills."
