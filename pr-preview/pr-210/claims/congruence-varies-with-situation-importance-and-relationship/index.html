Duke 1979¶

Duke, Marshall P. and Nowicki, Stephen. (1979). A Social Learning Theory Conceptualization of Interpersonal Relationships. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED183975

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

In a study (Ladd, Nowicki, & Duke, 1979), sixty female college students responded to a Triandis-type questionnaire across a 2 x 2 of situation importance and relationship level, with all subjects exposed to all combinations. The article reports "More congruent responses were associated with those instances in which the consequences and relationships were important."

"As seen in Figure 4, congruence varied with the importance of the situation and the importance of the relationship."
