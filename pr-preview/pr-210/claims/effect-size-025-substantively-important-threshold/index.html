A methodological convention stated in the guide's introduction to govern how impact magnitudes are interpreted throughout the recommendations. The panel defines the threshold and its percentile interpretation; no study data underlie this statement, it is an adopted reporting standard.

"In accordance with the What Works Clearinghouse criteria we describe an effect size of +0.25 or higher as substantively important. This is equivalent to raising performance of a group of students at least 10 percentile points on a valid test."
