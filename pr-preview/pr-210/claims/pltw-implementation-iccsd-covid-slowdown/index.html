Digital Promise 2021¶

Digital Promise. (2021). The “Science” of Computing: Inclusive Computing Pathways in Iowa City Community School District. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/133

q2 · i? · design · r1

District case report of implementation: PLTW was offered at two elementary and two middle schools in 2019-20 and expanded to five schools in 2020-21. The report notes "challenges with COVID-19 in Years 2 and 3 slowed progress" and teachers implemented individual units.

"ICCSD offered PLTW at two elementary schools and two middle schools over the 2019-20 academic year. During the 2020-21 academic year, five schools in the district were selected to implement PLTW at a variety of grade levels, and the district specifically recruited a district-wide CS Specialist to support teacher PD and integration."
