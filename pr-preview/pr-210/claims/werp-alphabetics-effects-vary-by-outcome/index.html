What Works Clearinghouse 2007¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2007). WWC Intervention Report: Waterford Early Reading Program™. July 16, 2007. http://www.pearsondigital.com/pdfs/werp/researchsummary-werp.pdf

q2 · i? · causal · r2

WWC improvement-index computation across the nine alphabetics findings in Hecht & Close (2002), reported in Appendix A3.1. The domain average improvement index was "+19 percentile points", with individual findings ranging from –26 to +37 percentile points; all were statistically non-significant.

"The average improvement index for alphabetics is +19 percentile points for the single study, with a range of –26 to +37 percentile points across findings."
