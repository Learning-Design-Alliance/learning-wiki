Fitzpatrick 1985¶

Fitzpatrick, Kathleen A. (1985). Group-Based Mastery Learning: A Robin Hood Approach to Instruction? Paper presented at the annual meeting of the American Educational Research Association, Chicago, Illinois. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED255556

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Off-task behavior was recorded at five-minute intervals in the 21 treatment and 19 control classrooms. Pre-post incidence correlated r .56 across the 40 classrooms; an analysis of covariance using pre-observation incidence as covariate found the adjusted group difference significant beyond the .001 level by the F test. Table 3 shows adjusted post means of 8.30 (treatment) versus 13.42 (control).
