Evidence¶

Neil S. Seftor 2009¶

Neil S. Seftor, Arif Mamun, Allen Schirm. (2009). The Impacts of Regular Upward Bound on Postsecondary Outcomes 7-9 Years After Scheduled High School Graduation. Princeton, NJ: Mathematica Policy Research. http://www.ed.gov/about/offices/list/opepd/ppss/reports.html#upward-2009

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Final-round impact analysis of the national Upward Bound evaluation, using survey, transcript, National Student Clearinghouse, and federal Student Financial Aid records for participants 7-9 years after scheduled high school graduation. The report reports "a statistically significant increase in the likelihood of earning a postsecondary certificate or license from a vocational school."

"Except for a statistically significant increase in the likelihood of earning a postsecondary certificate or license from a vocational school, the report finds no detectable effects on postsecondary outcomes"
