Survey of participating teachers (Figure 2 reports n=49) in the five-district Challenge Collaborative. The report states "Approximately three-quarters (73 percent)" could articulate the purpose of micro-credentials; it also reports 84 percent agreed completion was significant to their learning and 76 percent would recommend them.

"Approximately three-quarters (73 percent) of participating teachers surveyed reported that they could clearly articulate the purpose of micro-credentials to a colleague, indicating the teachers’ understanding of micro-credentials (Figure 2)."
