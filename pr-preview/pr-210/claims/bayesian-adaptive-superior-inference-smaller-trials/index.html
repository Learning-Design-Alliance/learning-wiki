q2 · i? · design · r2

Simulation study comparing the Bayesian adaptive design against a standard design, using a randomized experiment of students in an online course as the motivating example. The paper reports the probability advantage and sample-size reduction under stated conditions; no effect size is printed.

"we describe conditions under which there is more than a 90 percent chance that inference from the Bayesian adaptive design is superior to inference from a standard design, using less than one-third the sample size"
