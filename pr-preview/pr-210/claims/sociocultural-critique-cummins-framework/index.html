Evidence¶

Rivera 1984¶

Rivera, Charlene, Ed. (1984). Language Proficiency and Academic Achievement. Multilingual Matters 10. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED240882

q1 · i? · theoretical · r?

Theoretical argument from Troike's chapter (as summarized in the introduction), an interpretive critique rather than an empirical test. The article offers this as a theorized alternative account; no data are reported for it.

"cultural and social factors rather than linguistic factors may account for most of the disparities in academic achievement among minority students and that for this reason the CALP factor may be merely an indicator of a student's acculturation rather than a cognitive ability"

Rivera 1984 (2)¶

Rivera, Charlene, Ed. (1984). Language Proficiency and Academic Achievement. Multilingual Matters 10. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED240882

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Genesee's critique as summarized in the introduction: the framework is valuable but based primarily on cognitive and linguistic considerations and lacks a detailed account of the broader social context of language development and use.

"it would be premature to use the framework "to develop test programs, or eNen bilingual curricula on the basis of these putative language proficiencies without understanding their relationship with more fundamental causal factors.""
