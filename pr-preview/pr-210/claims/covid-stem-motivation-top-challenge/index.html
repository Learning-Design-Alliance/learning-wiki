Means 2020¶

Means, B., Peters, V., Neisler, J., & Griffiths, R. (2020). STEM Courses During the COVID Pandemic: Lessons from Spring 2020. Digital Promise Global. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/7900cb79-6ea5-44d0-aad6-34aa81e9bf4b

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Survey of 620 STEM students rated six non-technology challenges (Figure 1). Motivation was the most prevalent; other challenges included not knowing where to get help and family responsibilities, each cited as a major problem by almost 1 in 5 respondents.

"The proportion of students who reported maintaining motivation as a major problem (45%) on the survey was double that for the next most frequently experienced challenge (finding a quiet place to work on the course, a major problem for 21%)."
