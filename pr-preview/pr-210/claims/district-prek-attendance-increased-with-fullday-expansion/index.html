Evidence¶

Ehrlich 2021¶

Ehrlich, S.B., Allensworth, E.M., & Tansey, J. (2021). Meeting families' needs: Attendance rates in full-day vs. half-day pre-k. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/meeting-families-needs

q2 · i? · causal · r2

District-wide CPS administrative data analysis, 2012-13 to 2015-16. The article reports a "statistically significant overall increase in pre-k attendance rates (1.5 percentage points"; Black students gained 3 percentage points, more than twice other groups, and EL and lowest-income-community students gained 2 points.

"the higher attendance rates of students enrolled in full-day pre-k contributed to a modest but statistically significant overall increase in pre-k attendance rates (1.5 percentage points"
