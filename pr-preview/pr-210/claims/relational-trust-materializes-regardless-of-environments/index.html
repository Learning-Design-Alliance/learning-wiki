Key Questions: relational trust in schools in Valparaíso 2021¶

Key Questions: relational trust in schools in Valparaíso, Chile and Illinois, US. (2021). International Journal of Leadership in Education. https://www.tandfonline.com/journal/rled

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Authors' interpretive summary of the cross-national comparison between Valparaíso, Chile and Illinois, US, in which similar positive trust responses emerged despite differences in geography, language, culture, and education-system structure.

"Relational trust, which involves respecting, caring for, and believing in the honesty and competence of one’s colleagues, seems to materialize in schools, regardless of vast differences in environments."
