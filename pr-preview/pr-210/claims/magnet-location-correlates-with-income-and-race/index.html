Elaine M. Allensworth 2000¶

Elaine M. Allensworth, Todd Rosenkranz. (2000). Access to Magnet Schools in Chicago. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/access-magnet-schools-chicago

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Correlational analysis using 1990 U.S. Census STF3 tract-level data compared with magnet school neighborhood zones (Figures 5-8). The report states areas outside the zones "have an aver age median family income of $29,845," slightly lower than covered areas.

"Using census data in this analysis, we find that parts of the city that are not within the neighborhood zone of any magnet elementary school have an aver age median family income of $29,845. This is slightly lower than the $33,837 average income in the parts of the city covered by the neighborhood zone of at least one magnet elementary school."
