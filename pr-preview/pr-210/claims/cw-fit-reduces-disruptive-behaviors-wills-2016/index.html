wills-2016-disruptive¶

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2023, May). Class-Wide Function-Related Intervention Teams (CW-FIT). https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/729

q4 · i2 · causal · r2

Cluster randomized controlled trial rated Meets WWC Group Design Standards Without Reservations, with 313 students in grades K to 5 across 17 schools, outcomes observed at multiple time points spanning 6 months. Intervention classes averaged 7.01 (5.23) versus comparison 15.72 (11.93), effect size 1.00, improvement index +34, statistically significant (p<0.01).
