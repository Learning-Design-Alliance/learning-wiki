Evidence¶

Smith 1973¶

Smith, Kendon. (1973). On the Possibility of a Reinforcement Theory of Cognitive Learning. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED085078

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Conceptual argument opening the paper: cognitive events are described as functionally "embedded in the ongoing stream of behavior" (citing Skinner, Berlyne, Homme, and Smith), from which the author derives the expectation that cognitive learning follows the same rules as overt learning.

"If cognitive events are indeed a form of behavior, they should be subject to the same laws as are other forms of behavior.Specifically, they should manifest learning in accordance with the same rules."
