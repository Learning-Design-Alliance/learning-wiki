q2 · i? · associational · r2

Administrative placement data for the 2014-15 CPS cohort, mapped in Figure 1, show that schools on the North, Northwest, or Far North Sides were "three times more likely to serve as a field placement school" than South or West Side schools.

"In 2014-15, schools on the North, Northwest, or Far North Sides of Chicago were three times more likely to serve as a field placement school compared to schools on the South or West Sides of Chicago."

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of 2014-15 administrative placement data, student teacher surveys, and My Voice My School survey data found student teachers were "significantly less likely to be placed in low-SES schools" and in historically disinvested neighborhoods.

"We found that student teachers were significantly less likely to be placed in low-SES schools—schools with primarily Black students—and schools located in historically disinvested neighborhoods (see Table 2)."

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Comparing 2014-15 placement schools with non-placement schools on My Voice My School survey measures, the brief reports "no differences between the schools where student teachers trained and other district schools" on ambitious instruction and supportive environment.

"we found no differences between the schools where student teachers trained and other district schools in the level of ambitious instruction, or the degree to which the schools had supportive environments"
