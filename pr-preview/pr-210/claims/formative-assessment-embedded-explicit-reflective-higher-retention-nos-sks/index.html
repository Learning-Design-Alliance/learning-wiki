Evidence¶

Mustafa Metin 2022¶

Mustafa Metin. (2022). Comparing Effects of Two Different Explicit–Reflective Instructions on Pre-School Prospective Teachers' View about Nature of Science and Scientific Knowledge. Journal of Science Learning, 5(1), 165-175. https://doi.org/10.17509/jsl.v5i1.33190

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Independent samples t-test on retention-test NOSS scores administered after four weeks in the same quasi-experimental study; experimental mean 100.47 vs control 90.59, "a significant difference (t = 17.286; p < 0.05) in favor of the experimental group." No effect size printed.

"the means of pre-service teachers in the experimental group in NOSS as a retention test is higher than the control group's ( x̄Experimental = 100.47; x̄Retention = 90.59). The results of the t-test show that there is a significant difference (t = 17.286; p < 0.05) in favor of the experimental group."
