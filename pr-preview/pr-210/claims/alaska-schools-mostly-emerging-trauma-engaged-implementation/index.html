q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of fall 2022 Trauma-Engaged Schools Survey data from 271 Alaska schools (54 percent of the state's public schools). Implementation scores ranged 14–70 and were binned by natural breaks: "Most schools (170 schools, or 63 percent of study schools)" were emerging, with 57 limited and 44 high.

"Most schools (170 schools, or 63 percent of study schools) that participated in the survey had implementation scores at the emerging implementation level—the middle level of implementation relative to all schools in the study."
