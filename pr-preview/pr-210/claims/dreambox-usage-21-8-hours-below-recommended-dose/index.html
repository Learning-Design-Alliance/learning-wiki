Descriptive usage data from the Wang and Woodworth (2011) RCT, conducted mid-October through mid-February of the 2010–11 school year: intervention students were scheduled for 20 to 40 minutes per day but "students averaged 21.8 hours of usage over the course of the study."

"usage statistics show that students averaged 21.8 hours of usage over the course of the study, or approximately 16 minutes per day."
