The WWC's extent-of-evidence categorization (Appendix A5) lists one study, one school, and 34 students in each domain, below the threshold of at least two studies, two schools, and 350 students or 14 classrooms for a medium-to-large rating.

"The WWC considers the extent of evidence for Read Well ® t o be small for both reading achievement and English language development for English language learners."

Of five reviewed studies, only Frasco (2008) met WWC evidence standards; the other four do not meet evidence standards or eligibility screens, including one excluded because the intervention and comparison groups were not shown to be equivalent at baseline.

"Five studies reviewed by the WWC investigated the effects of Read Well ® on English language learners. One study (Frasco, 2008) is a randomized controlled trial that meets WWC evidence standards."
