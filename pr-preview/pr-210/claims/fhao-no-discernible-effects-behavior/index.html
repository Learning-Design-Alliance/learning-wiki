Evidence¶

What Works Clearinghouse 2006¶

What Works Clearinghouse. (2006). WWC Intervention Report: Facing History and Ourselves. U.S. Department of Education. www.facinghistory.org. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/WWC/InterventionReport/169

q2 · i? · causal · r2

WWC re-analysis of the Schultz, Barr, & Selman (2001) quasi-experimental study of 346 eighth-grade students in 22 classrooms. The fighting outcome showed an effect size of 0.20 (improvement index +8), not significant at α = 0.05 after clustering corrections; equivalence was not tested.

"Facing History and Ourselves students reported less fighting than students in the comparison group, but this difference was neither statistically significant (as calculated by the WWC) nor large enough to be considered substantively important using WWC criteria."
