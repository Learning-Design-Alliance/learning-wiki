Michos 2025¶

Michos, K., Schmitz, M.-L., & Petko, D. (2025). Learning Analytics in Schools: Is Digital Data Use Influenced by Teacher-Level or School-Level Factors? Journal of Learning Analytics, 12(3), 87–101. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2025.8567

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Post hoc comparisons following the multilevel regression in the same survey (N = 2,247). Descriptively, "teachers in languages, arts, humanities, and social sciences tend to report higher skill and use of digital data" than STEM teachers, while vocational and multiple-subject teachers reported the highest levels.

"Post hoc analysis with Bonferroni correction showed a significant difference in digital data use between teachers who teach languages, arts, and humanities subjects and those who teach STEM subjects (z = 4.49, p < =.001). Moreover, significant differences were found between teachers in vocational schools and teachers who teach languages, arts, and humanities (z = 2.82, p =.048) or STEM subjects (z =5.57, p < =.001)"
