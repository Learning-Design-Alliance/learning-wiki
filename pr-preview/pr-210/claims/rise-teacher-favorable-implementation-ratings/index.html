Schechter et al 2024¶

Teacher and interventionist survey reported in the Florida-based study (Schechter et al., 2024) implementation notes; respondents commended "the quality of materials and their effect on student engagement". Positive ratings were notable for books, passages, text-based questions, and program completeness.

"over 90% of the teachers (a) felt comfortable or very comfortable in implementing the program and (b) found that it supplemented the core reading program well or very well"
