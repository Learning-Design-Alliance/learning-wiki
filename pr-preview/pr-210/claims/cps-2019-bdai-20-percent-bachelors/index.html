Nagaoka 2020¶

Nagaoka, J., Mahaffie, S., Usher, A., & Seeskin, A. (2020). The Educational Attainment of Chicago Public Schools Students: 2019. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://toandthrough.uchicago.edu/tool/cps/2019/#/milestones

Figure 4 takeaway reporting the 2019 Direct Bachelor's Degree Attainment Index. The report adds that an additional 2 percent take an indirect path, "resulting in a total 2019 BDAI of 20 percent," compared to 40 percent of adults nationally aged 25–34 holding a bachelor's degree.

"The 2019 DBDAI is 18 percent. This means that if the current high school graduation, college enrollment, and bachelor's degree completion rate do not change over the next 10 years, 18 percent of current CPS ninth-graders will complete a bachelor's degree within 10 years through a direct pathway."
