Magnetic Reading (Evidence Rating: Moderate) 2023¶

Magnetic Reading (Evidence Rating: Moderate). (2023). https://www.curriculumassociates.com/professional-development

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Prospective study during the 2022-2023 school year matching 473 grade 3-5 students from four treatment schools to 2,628 students from 30 comparison schools on demographics and fall achievement; accounting for student characteristics and prior achievement, the effect size was "+0.22".
