Evidence¶

Nicole Shechtman 2019¶

Nicole Shechtman, Jeremy Roschelle, Mingyu Feng, Corinne Singleton. (2019). An Efficacy Study of a Digital Core Curriculum for Grade 5 Mathematics. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/2332858419850482

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Exploratory RQ3 descriptive analysis of end-of-year teacher survey self-reports in both experimental groups (response rates 97.4% and 84.8%). Treatment classrooms used technology markedly more; 60.7% of control teachers reported students used technology 26% to 50% of the time, so control technology use was "clearly supplemental."

"On the survey, in response to the question about how much their students used technology in a regular week of math class, 89.5% of treatment teachers and 10.7% of control teachers indicated students used technology for at least half the class time, respectively (t = 10.0, p < .0001)."
