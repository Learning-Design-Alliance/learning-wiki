In its current-state section the report states that "The lack of diversity in computing is not due to the lack of ability nor interest in computing among women and people of color," citing Google Inc. & Gallup Inc. (2016), Pinkard et al. (2017), and Ryoo (2019). It attributes the gap instead to social and structural barriers including stereotypes and inequitable tracking (Margolis et al., 2008; Ryoo, 2019; Wang & Moghadam, 2017).
