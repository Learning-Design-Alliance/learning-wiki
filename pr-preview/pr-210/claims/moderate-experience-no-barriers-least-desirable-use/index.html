Evidence¶

Lina Shanley 2017¶

Lina Shanley, Mari Strand Cary, Ben Clarke, Meg Guerreiro, Michael Thier. (2017). Instructors’ technology experience and iPad delivered intervention implementation: A mixed methods replication study. Educational Technology Research and Development 65, 815–830. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/instructors-technology-experience-and-ipad-delivered-intervention-implementation-a-mixed-methods-replication-study/

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Across the two KinderTEK development studies, the authors report that instructors with "moderate experience with iPads and/or no barriers to technology integration demonstrated the least desirable student use patterns," a counterintuitive pattern relative to the experience–use correspondence.

"Lastly, instructors who reported moderate experience with iPads and/or no barriers to technology integration demonstrated the least desirable student use patterns."
