District and CBO leaders held three interrelated goals for the WSS Framework: mitigating one-size-fits-all policymaking by centering individual school communities, facilitating power-sharing between district, schools, and community, and redefining safety beyond metal detectors, security officers, and cameras to include school climate and social-emotional wellbeing. → Johnson 2026

Findings statement from the Question 2 section of the interview-based field scan. District officials described how a safe and welcoming environment "is not always the same thing for everyone," and a CBO leader explained that true partnership "means [that] you share power." District officials sought to "build culture and climate into the definition of [school] safety."
