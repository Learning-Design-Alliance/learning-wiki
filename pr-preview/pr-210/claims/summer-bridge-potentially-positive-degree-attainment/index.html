Summer Bridge Programs 2016¶

Summer Bridge Programs. (2016). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, Supporting Postsecondary Success. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/661

q2 · i? · causal · r2

A quasi-experimental study of 2,222 undergraduates at a selective technical university in the southeastern United States, with 770 summer bridge participants and 1,452 non-participants, followed a minimum of 5 years. Graduation rates were "significantly higher for students in the intervention group" (70% vs 67%); the WWC assigned an average improvement index of +4 percentile points and characterizes the finding as "a potentially positive effect". No standardized effect size is printed, so impact is coded null.

"Murphy et al. (2010) reported that postsecondary graduation rates were significantly higher for students in the intervention group, compared to those in the comparison group (70% vs 67%). The WWC characterizes this finding as a potentially positive effect."
