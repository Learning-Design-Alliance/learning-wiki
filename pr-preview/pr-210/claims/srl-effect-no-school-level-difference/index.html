Ergen, B., & Kanadli, S. (2017). The effect of self-regulated learning strategies on academic achievement: A meta-analysis study. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 69, 55-74. https://doi.org/10.14689/ejer.2017.69.4

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Moderator analysis across primary (k=4), secondary (k=8), and undergraduate (k=8) studies under the random effects model; Qb = 1.159, p = 0.560. Descriptive subgroup effects were large for primary (1.077) and undergraduate (0.944) and moderate for secondary (0.693).
