El-Koumy 1999¶

El-Koumy, Abdel Salam A. (1999). Effects of Three Semantic Mapping Strategies on EFL Students' Reading Comprehension. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED435193

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Pretest ANOVA across all 187 completers in the randomized experiment; the author attributes baseline equivalence to all subjects having studied the same textbooks for the same amount of time and notes all subjects were poor comprehenders at pretest.

"a one-way analysis of variance comparing the mean scores on the pretest yielded no significant differences among the three groups of the study (f = 0.37; p > 0.05)"
