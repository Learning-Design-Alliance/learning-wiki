The brief's descriptive comparison reports that the enrollment pattern at HBCUs was "corresponding to the larger decline in PLUS recipients at HBCUs". This is an observed correspondence, not a tested causal estimate; no effect size is printed.

"Enrollment at HBCUs decreased more than enrollment at other institutions, corresponding to the larger decline in PLUS recipients at HBCUs."
