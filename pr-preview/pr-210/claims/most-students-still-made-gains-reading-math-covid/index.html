Descriptive finding from the fall 2020 MAP Growth analysis of nearly 4.4 million students in grades 3-8: "most students made some learning gains in both reading and math" since the pandemic started, though gains lagged typical-year patterns in math.

"The results show in almost all grades, most students made some learning gains in both reading and math since the COVID-19 pandemic started."
