What Works Clearinghouse 2020¶

q2 · i? · causal · r2

QED using propensity score matching (caliper of 0.4 standard deviations) blocked by cohort and postsecondary institution, with 1,234 intervention and 4,629 comparison students. Table 4 reports means of 82.60 versus 78.30 percent, effect size 0.17, improvement index +7, p < .01; the quoted finding sentence prints no effect size.

"Findings from Linkow et al. (2019) show evidence of a statistically significant positive effect of Success Boston Coaching on progressing in college (Table 7). This finding is based on an outcome analysis that includes 5,863 students."
