Nagaoka 2016¶

Nagaoka, J., & Healey, K. (2016). The educational attainment of Chicago Public Schools students: 2015: A focus on four-year college degrees. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. http://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/educational-attainment-chicago-public-schools-students-2015-focus-four-year-college

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Descriptive comparison of successive CPS graduating classes' ACT scores and participation. The brief reports the score increases occurred alongside expanded test-taking, but provides no statistical test, so the co-occurrence is descriptive only.

"The average ACT score increased from 17.6 for the class of 2006 to 18.5 for the class of 2014. This increase occurred while nearly 5,800 additional students were taking the ACT because more students stayed in school until the spring of their junior year."
