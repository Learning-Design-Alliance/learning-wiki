Evidence¶

Sartain 2020¶

Sartain, L., Zou, A., Gutiérrez, V., Shyjka, A., Hinton, E., Brown, E.R., & Easton, J.Q. (2020). Teacher Evaluation in CPS: Perceptions of REACH Implementation, Five Years In. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Survey of approximately 12,000 CPS teachers comparing perceived accuracy of REACH components. Teachers judged "observation feedback scores were the most accurate at representing their teaching effectiveness" and VAMs the least accurate; about 70 percent still said VAMs were mostly or highly accurate.

"According to approximately 12,000 CPS teachers surveyed, observation feedback scores were the most accurate at representing their teaching effectiveness and value-added measures (VAMs), based on student growth on standardized tests, were the least accurate."
