Iwatani 2019¶

Comparative re-analysis of two regression studies on the NELS:88 dataset, with predictive accuracy compared via confusion matrices and F-ratio across ruleset algorithms and regression, shown in Figures 2 and 3. The author found accuracies were "comparable or slightly worse predictive accuracies relative to regression."
