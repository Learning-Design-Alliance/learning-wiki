Niess 2018¶

Niess, M. L., & Roschelle, J. (2018). Transforming Teachers' Knowledge for Teaching Mathematics with Technologies through Online Knowledge-Building Communities. Proceedings of the 40th annual meeting of the North American Chapter of the International Group for the Psychology of Mathematics Education, Greenville, SC. https://www.pmena.org/proceedings/

Authors' summary of the qualitative analysis of the first TPACK component (overarching conceptions), drawing on case reflections such as Janis's revised view that virtual manipulatives "provided a larger variety of learning experiences" important for student understanding. Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • In-service mathematics teachers in the online TPACK program shifted from teacher-centered to valuing student-centered instructional strategies for teaching with technologies — possibly the same claim (merge candidate)
  • After the IDEAS academy, both studied teachers' classrooms moved toward more student-centered methods, with inquiry and collaborative learning emerging — related
  • Both case-study teachers increased web and technology use for lesson preparation, presentation, and classroom exploration over the project — related
  • Changes traced to historical and activity factors, but the authors conclude the IDEAS academy itself may not be the major factor in observed changes — related
  • Teachers reported that students needed dedicated time to learn and explore technologies before being expected to gain mathematics understanding from them — related
