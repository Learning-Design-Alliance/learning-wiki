q2 · i? · associational · r2

Cohort progression analysis (Section III). Graduation rates for 18-year-olds declined slightly with the 1996 cohort but remained above the 1991 and 1992 levels; the report notes it is too early to determine whether the trend will reverse.

"the likelihood of graduating by age 18 was slightly higher in later years, with the exception of the 1996 cohort. Thirty to 31 percent of students in the 1991 and 1992 cohorts graduated by age 18, compared to 34 percent for the 1994 and 1995 cohorts."
