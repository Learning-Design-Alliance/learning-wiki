Manjeet Singh Usma 2025¶

Manjeet Singh Usma, Shaun Bangay and Atul Sajjanhar. (2025). Augmented Reality Enhanced Analytics for Education: A Systematic Review. Journal of Learning Analytics 12(3). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2025.8447

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Chang and colleagues (2014), an ARFlora system letting students manipulate virtual objects to observe plant growth, with motivation measured by questionnaire after the activity. The review attributes the experimental-over-control motivation difference to this study; no effect size is printed.

"After the activity, students completed a learning motivation questionnaire. The experimental group, using AR technology, showed higher motivation than the control group did, indicating AR’s effectiveness in plant study engagement."
