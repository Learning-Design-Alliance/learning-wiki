Evidence¶

Read Well® 2010¶

Read Well®. (2010). WWC Intervention Report. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. http://store.cambiumlearning.com/resource.aspx?page=ProgramOverview&site=sw&parentId=019005451

q3 · i? · causal · r?

The WWC review of the Frasco (2008) randomized controlled trial reports no statistically significant differences on two reading outcomes. Appendix A3.1 prints effect sizes of –0.06 (DIBELS) and –0.02 (GORT-4), with a domain average improvement index of "–1.46 percentile points".

"Frasco (2008) reported a not statistically significant difference in reading gains, as measured by the Dynamic Indicators of Basic Literacy Skills (DIBELS) Nonsense Word Fluency subtest, and a not statistically significant difference in fluency and comprehension gains, as measured by the Gray's Oral Reading Test–Fourth Edition (GORT-4)."

q3 · i? · causal · r?

WWC-calculated improvement indices for the reading achievement domain in the Frasco (2008) study are slightly negative, "–1.46 percentile points" on average, across the DIBELS and GORT-4 findings.

"The average improvement index for reading achievement is –1.46 percentile points across the study, with a range of –2.30 to –0.61 percentile points across findings."
