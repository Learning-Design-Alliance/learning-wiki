q2 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r?

WWC synthesis of the two qualifying quasi-experimental studies (Texas effect size 0.37, Florida 0.49; domain average 0.43 per Appendix A3). The improvement index "represents the difference between the percentile rank of the average student in the intervention condition versus the percentile rank of the average student in the comparison condition."

"The average improvement index for completing school is +17 percentile points across the two studies, with a range of +14 to +19 percentile points across the findings."
