Evidence¶

Karma Wangda 2020¶

Karma Wangda, Kinzang Dorji, Kinley. (2020). Teachers and Students Perception on the Impact of Kagan Cooperative Learning Structures at Higher Secondary School. ASEAN Journal of Open and Distance Learning, 12(2). https://rcl.sce.rub.edu.bt/ajod/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Content analysis of 611 student open-ended responses found "bright teammates tend to dominate the team task" (16.5%) and that KCLS was not practised in some schools (9%). Student comments indicated low achievers were left idle during teamwork.

"16.5% (101 out of 611) of the student participants have expressed that while working in teams to complete assigned task, bright teammates tend to dominate the team task while on the other hand, 9% (55 out of 611) of the student participants have reported that KCLS is not practised in their schools"

q2 · i? · associational · r2

From the 36 teacher open-ended responses, "5.6% (2 out of 36) teacher participants have expressed that those students in higher classes were not cooperative to practice KCLS", consistent with earlier Bhutanese reports of adolescent reluctance.
