Raković 2023¶

Raković, M., Ahmad Uzir, N., Matcha, W., Eagan, B., Jovanović, J., Shaffer, D. W., Pardo, A., & Gašević, D. (2023). q2 · i3 · associational · r1

Series of two-sample t-tests in a mean-rotated ENA space (Figure 14; Table 5) comparing high performers (Nabove90th = 15) and low performers (Nbelow25th = 31) on both exams across 12 active weeks. The article also reports significant svd2 differences in Weeks 5, 6, and 10, with Cohen's d up to 1.221.

"We found a statistically significant difference between the two groups of students in Week 2 (t = 0.486, p = 0.028, Cohen's d = 0.972), Week 3 (t = 0.373, p = 0.039, Cohen's d = 0.747), Week 6 (t = 0.291, p = 0.010, Cohen's d = 0.583), and Week 7 (t = 0.468, p = 0.006, Cohen's d = 0.936)."
