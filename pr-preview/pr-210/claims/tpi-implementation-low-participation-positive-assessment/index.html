In the implementation case study with UNPA Master's Degree tutors using Mahara, out of 9 potential tutors only one tutor-trainee researcher pair could be formed, yet the two participants who put the procedure into practice assessed it highly positively. → Darder 2015 q2 i? The authors attribute participant loss to lack of motivation, skills and vision, or shortage of time and resources, noting change in the academic environment may be seen as additional and unnecessary work. → Darder 2015 (2)

Evidence¶

Darder 2015¶

Darder, A.; Pérez Garcias, A. (2015). Online tutoring procedure for research project supervision: management, organization and key elements. New Approaches in Educational Research 4(2). https://doi.org/10.7821/naer.2015.4.110

q2 · i? · design · r2

Implementation case study in which tutors and their students organized the virtual tutoring environment in Mahara; information was collected through an observation card and a questionnaire. The two implementers called it "an excellent piece of work which guides, clarifies, and specifies a suitable itinerary".

"It is worth highlighting that, out of 9 potential tutors, it was only possible to have a tutor-trainee researcher pair."
