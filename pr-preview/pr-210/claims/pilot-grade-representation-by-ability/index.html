q2 · i? · causal · r2

Table 2.1 analysis by ability decile groups showed more grades represented for the lower and upper 10% of pilot students than the middle 80%, while "the middle 80% tended to have more grades represented" in the comparison group, the opposite of expectations.

"It indicates that more grades were represented by items for in the lower and upper pilot study groups than for the middle 80%. In the comparison group, the middle 80% tended to have more grades represented than either the lower or upper 10%."
