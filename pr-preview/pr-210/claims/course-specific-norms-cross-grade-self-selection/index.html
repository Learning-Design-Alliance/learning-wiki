Portland, OR: NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/achievement-and-growth-norms-for-course-specific-map-growth-algebra-1-geometry-and-algebra-2-tests/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive summary statistics (Table 3.1) of RIT means and SDs by grade and term for Grades 6–12. The report states "average test scores decreased as grades increased" and that Grade 7 students achieved the highest average score in Algebra 1 and Geometry, Grade 8 in Algebra 2; means are descriptive with no significance tests reported.

"With few exceptions, average test scores decreased as grades increased for each course-specific test. Lower-grade students (i.e., Grades 6–8 students) tend to perform better than the students of the grade at which a course is usually targeted"
