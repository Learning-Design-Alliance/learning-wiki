Evidence¶

Megan Kuhfeld 2020¶

Megan Kuhfeld, James Soland. (2020). Avoiding bias from sum scores in growth estimates: An examination of IRT-based approaches to scoring longitudinal survey responses. Psychological Methods. https://doi.org/10.1037/met0000367

q2 · i? · causal · r?

A series of simulation and empirical studies compared growth parameter recovery across scoring approaches in a single-cohort repeated measure design. The article reports that multidimensional IRT scores "leads to better recovery of growth parameters relative to models using sum scores and other IRT approaches."

"Results indicate that using scores from multidimensional IRT approaches that account for latent variable covariances over time in growth models leads to better recovery of growth parameters relative to models using sum scores and other IRT approaches."
