Evidence¶

Koch 2025¶

Koch, M., & Peters Hinton, V. (2025). Voices of Undergraduate Students Taking Introductory Statistics Courses [Project Report]. Digital Promise.

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Across interviews about instructor practices, "Approximately half of the students mentioned that their instructors helped them bridge the gap between the courseware's guidance and their understanding." Five students reported insufficient support and ten reported only inconsistent support.

"Approximately half of the students mentioned that their instructors helped them bridge the gap between the courseware's guidance and their understanding through active outreach, high availability and responsiveness."
