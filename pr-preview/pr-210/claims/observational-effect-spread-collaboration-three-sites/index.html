q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

Interview and site-visit findings from the district-charter collaboration grant evaluation. The report describes an "observational effect of collaboration" through which collaboration spread slowly in three grantee sites, while in other sites it did not extend beyond the grant. No effect size is reported.

"Collaboration appears to have slowly spread outside the formal grant activities in three grantee sites via the “observational effect of collaboration” but has not extended beyond the grant in other sites."
