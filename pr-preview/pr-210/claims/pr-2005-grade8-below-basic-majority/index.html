Achievement-level results from the 2005 NAEP grade 8 mathematics assessment in Puerto Rico. The report prints "6 percent of eighth-graders performed at or above Basic," "94 percent" below Basic in Puerto Rico, and "32 percent of students in the nation scored below Basic."

"the results from the 2005 NAEP Puerto Rico assessment indicate that 6 percent of eighth-graders performed at or above Basic. The majority of students in Puerto Rico scored below Basic (94 percent). In comparison, 32 percent of students in the nation scored below Basic."
