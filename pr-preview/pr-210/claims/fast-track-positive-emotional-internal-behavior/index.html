Fast Track: Elementary School 2014¶

Fast Track: Elementary School. (2014). WWC Intervention Report, U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/628

q4 · i? · causal · r3

Findings from the Conduct Problems Prevention Research Group (1999a) randomized controlled trial, which met WWC standards without reservations, after one year of implementation. The domain rating is "potentially positive effects," with an average improvement index of +9 percentile points (range +7 to +11) for 855 students in 54 schools; no standardized effect size is printed.

"a positive and statistically significant difference between the intervention and comparison groups on the Emotion Recognition Question-naire and Interview of Emotional Experience"
