Descriptive comparison of charter and non-charter administrators' 2017 survey responses (Figure 3); the brief reports charter administrators rated recruiting and hiring higher (55 vs. 42 percent), turnover higher (34 vs. 14 percent), and removing poor teachers lower (18 vs. 54 percent).
