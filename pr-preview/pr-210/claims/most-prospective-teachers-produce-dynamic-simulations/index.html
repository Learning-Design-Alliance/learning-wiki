Ragonis 2026¶

Ragonis, N., & Hazzan, O. (2026). Computational Thinking Across Disciplines: A Taxonomy of Pedagogical Approaches as Reflected in Prospective Teachers’ Simulations of Computational Processes. Informatics in Education, 25(1), 173–199. https://doi.org/10.15388/infedu.2506.019

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Quantitative frequency analysis of all 375 summative-assignment submissions, categorized by the study's taxonomy. The analysis found "the prevalence of simulations for demonstration, with and without exploration (92%)", with half of those enabling exploration; only 8% reflected a tutoring model.

"The data, presented in Table 5 (rows 3 and 4), shows the prevalence of simulations for demonstration, with and without exploration (92%), with exactly half of these simulations incorporating options for exploration"
