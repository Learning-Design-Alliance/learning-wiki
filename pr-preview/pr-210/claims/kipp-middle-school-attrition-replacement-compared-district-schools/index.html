Ira Nichols-Barrer 2012¶

Ira Nichols-Barrer, Brian P. Gill, Philip Gleason, Christina Clark Tuttle. (2012). Student Selection, Attrition, and Replacement in KIPP Middle Schools. Working Paper 11. Washington, DC: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/student-selection-attrition-and-replacement-in-kipp-middle-schools

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The paper's own abstract describes an observational analysis of longitudinal, student-level data examining "the entry and exit of students in KIPP middle schools" and comparing attrition and replacement rates with nearby district-run schools. No effect sizes or statistics are printed in the available text.

"Using longitudinal, student-level data, this working paper examines the entry and exit of students in KIPP middle schools, comparing KIPP's rates of attrition and replacement with rates in nearby district-run schools."
