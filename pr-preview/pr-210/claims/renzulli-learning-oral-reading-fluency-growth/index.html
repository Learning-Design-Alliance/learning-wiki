Evidence¶

Renzulli Learning 2024¶

Renzulli Learning. (2024). Renzulli Learning program description. https://renzullilearning.com/en/Menus/21-technology

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

A cluster-randomized study of 383 elementary and middle school students in two schools (a high-poverty urban middle school in Georgia and a suburban elementary school in southern California). Treatment classrooms used the program 2-3 hours weekly for 16 weeks; the page reports "significantly higher growth in oral reading fluency (effect size = +0.10)" versus business-as-usual.

"After 16 weeks, students in classes assigned to use Renzulli Learning demonstrated significantly higher growth in oral reading fluency (effect size = +0.10) than those in classes assigned to business-as-usual instruction"
