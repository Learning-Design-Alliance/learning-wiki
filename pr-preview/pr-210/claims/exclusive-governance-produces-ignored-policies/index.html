Jacob 2026¶

Jacob, S., Miceli, H., Schneider, H., & Peters Hinton, V. (2026). Institutional conditions for integrating emerging technologies in higher education. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/294

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Focus group finding: faculty and frontline staff were often only vaguely aware of AI committees or unrepresented in them; a contrasting example was an "AI enablement team" pairing IT experts with faculty representatives, credited with better decisions and smoother rollouts.

"Governance structures that exclude the faculty and support staff responsible for implementation produce policies that don’t stick. Committees that built in genuine instructional input produced guidelines faculty trusted and used; those that didn’t produced policies faculty ignored or worked around."
