Project self-assessment of an initial needs assessment conducted by New Careers, Inc. using task analysis in two priority areas (ESL and individualization of instruction). The report states the assessment "was inadequate for essentially two reasons," the first being insufficient funds for a region-wide effort.

"concluded that the needs assessment was inadequate for essentially two reasons.One, the available funds, it was apparent, were too modest to provide for the extensive resources necessary to conduct a region-wide needs assessment."
