Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • The four Power4Kids interventions delivered about 100 hours of pull-out instruction in small groups of three students over one school year — related
  • The Power4Kids first-year report presents impacts on reading achievement at the end of the intervention year, with nearly 800 children in the study — related
  • The Power4Kids evaluation used two-level random assignment: 50 schools to four interventions and struggling readers within schools to treatment or control — related
  • Word attack gains from the Wilson Reading System were concentrated among students with high initial word attack or vocabulary scores and students not eligible for free/reduced lunch — related
  • Effects of Corrective Reading varied by student subgroup: positive for students with high pretest vocabulary scores and for students not eligible for free/reduced lunch, but not for their counterparts — related
  • Failure Free Reading, the only word-level-plus-comprehension program, showed impacts for third graders but no impacts for fifth graders — related
