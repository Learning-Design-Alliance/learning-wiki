Analyses of video recordings of teachers in student hat activities during PD workshops and teacher interviews (Lowell, 2022; Lowell & McNeill, 2020), summarized by the review; qualitative, no effect sizes.

"These analyses revealed that the student hat activities supported teachers' learning about science content ideas, their students, and the OpenSciEd instructional approach."

planning-tool-tensions¶

McElhaney, K. W., Mills, K., Kamdar, D., Baker, A., & Roschelle, J. (2023, March). A summary and synthesis of initial OpenSciEd research: Updated version [White paper]. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/171

q1 · i? · qualitative · r?

Case study of one teacher using the discussion planning tool across all three discussion types, summarized by the review; planning supported noticing tensions between equitable participation and conceptual depth, time constraints, and epistemic ownership.

"Mr. Kelly navigated "tension between the coherence that was planned for in the storyline materials and his enactment of these materials in a manner that is coherent from the student perspective" (Cherbow & McNeill, 2022, p. 20)."
