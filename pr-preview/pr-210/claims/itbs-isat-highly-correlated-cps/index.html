Evidence¶

Easton 2003¶

Easton, J. Q., Correa, M., Luppescu, S., Park, H.-S., Ponisciak, S., Rosenkranz, T., & Sporte, S. (2003). How Do They Compare? ITBS and ISAT Reading and Mathematics in the Chicago Public Schools, 1999 to 2002. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://www.consortium-chicago.org/publications/briefs/onlineresources.html

q2 · i3 · associational · r2

Analysis of the 2002 CPS spring administrations linking student scores on both tests in grades three, five, and eight, using students with complete scores on both tests regardless of public reporting status. The printed correlation coefficients include r = 0.83, 0.85, and 0.85 in reading and r = 0.86, 0.87, and 0.86 in mathematics, all large correlations; the article reports they rise to around 0.90 when corrected for unreliability.

"In reading, the correlations for grades three, five, and eight are 0.83, 0.85, and 0.85, respectively. In mathematics, the correlations are slightly higher at 0.86, 0.87, and 0.86."
