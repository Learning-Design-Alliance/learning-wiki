Imagine Language & Literacy 2023¶

Imagine Language & Literacy. (2023). Evidence rating: promising. https://www.evidenceforessa.org/programs/imagine-language-literacy

q2 · i1 · associational · r2

A 2022-2023 matched study in a large Louisiana school district compared over 1,000 students in grades 1-5 (100% English Learners, 92% Hispanic) on Louisiana's English Language Proficiency Test; users outperformed matched non-users with "effect size = +0.16".

"Overall, the performance of Imagine Language & Literacy students was significantly higher than the control group of students (effect size = +0.16)."
