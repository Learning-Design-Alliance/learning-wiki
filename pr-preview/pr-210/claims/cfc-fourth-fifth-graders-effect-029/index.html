q3 · i1 · causal · r?

A large two-year study of Content-Focused Coaching in an urban district in the Southwest compared CFC schools to control groups. The program page reports "fourth and fifth graders who received CFC scored significantly higher, with an effect size of +0.29" across 2983 students, qualifying CFC for the Strong category.
