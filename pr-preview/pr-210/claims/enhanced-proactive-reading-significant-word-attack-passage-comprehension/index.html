Enhanced Proactive Reading 2006¶

Enhanced Proactive Reading. (2006). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Randomized controlled trial with randomization problems (41 first-grade Hispanic English language learners, 14 classrooms, Texas). Appendix A3.1 reports Word Attack effect size 1.53 (improvement index +43) and Passage Comprehension effect size 1.32 (+41), both statistically significant.

"Vaughn, Mathes, et al. (2006) found statistically significant differences favoring the Enhanced Proactive Reading students in two of the four reading measures (Word Attack and Passage Comprehension)."
