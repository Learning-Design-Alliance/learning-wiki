Survey evidence from the Challenge Collaborative's cohort-model implementation. Teachers pursuing micro-credentials in peer cohorts "pointed to this collaboration as integral to their learning," and 74 percent cited collaborative planning time as the top support.

"Among participating teachers surveyed, collaborative planning time with colleagues was the top support, cited by roughly three-quarters (74 percent) of the respondents."
