WWC Intervention Report: ALAS 2006¶

WWC Intervention Report: ALAS. (2006). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://www.alasdropoutprevention.com

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Subgroup analysis (Gándara et al., 1998; 81 of 94 initially randomized students) meeting WWC standards with reservations. Appendix A4.3 reports a Cox effect size of 0.14, improvement index 6, marked ns; the 11th-grade on-track difference (effect size 0.21) was also ns.

"ALAS students had higher graduation rates at the end of 12th grade (32% compared with 27% of the control group), but the difference was not statistically significant."
