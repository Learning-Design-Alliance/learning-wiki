The report's research summary section describes its literature search reflecting documents publicly available by March 2019, reviewed under WWC Procedures and Standards Handbook version 3.0. The screening yielded "12 studies" with the stated distribution across standards categories.

"The WWC identified 12 studies that investigated the effectiveness of OLI (Figure 1): • 2 studies meet WWC group design standards without reservations • 1 study meets WWC group design standards with reservations • 3 studies do not meet WWC group design standards • 6 studies are ineligible for review"
