Early Steps 2024¶

Early Steps. (2024). Evidence for ESSA program description. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/early-steps/

q3 · i3 · causal · r1

One qualifying study of Early Steps in urban Tennessee compared tutored students with similar students in different schools who received no tutoring; the summary reports an "average effect size = +0.86" on Woodcock Johnson Word Attack and Passage Comprehension scales.

"Compared to similar students in different schools who received no tutoring, those in Early Steps scored substantially higher on Woodcock Johnson Word Attack and Passage Comprehension scales (average effect size = +0.86)."
