Case study findings from 11 SIG schools with high ELL proportions in the Study of School Turnaround: "Administrators in 3 of the 11 schools reported considering ELL expertise and experience when hiring classroom teachers," and 2 of 11 reported it factored into classroom assignment.

"Administrators in 3 of the 11 schools reported considering ELL expertise and experience when hiring classroom teachers, while respondents in 2 of the 11 schools reported that teachers’ ELL expertise and experience purposefully factored into assignment of teachers to specific classrooms."
