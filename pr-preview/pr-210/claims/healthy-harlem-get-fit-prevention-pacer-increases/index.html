Interim findings from the evaluation of the Harlem Children's Zone Healthy Harlem initiative, monitoring students during a baseline year and measuring impacts after two and three years of participation. The brief reports "large and statistically significant increases in PACER laps" for overweight or obese participants; no effect size is printed.
