Ehrlich 2021¶

Ehrlich, S.B., Allensworth, E.M., & Tansey, J. (2021). Meeting families' needs: Attendance rates in full-day vs. half-day pre-k. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/meeting-families-needs

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Year 1 of the NLCI propensity-matched comparison, when two of four schools had converted. The 3.8 percentage point advantage was not statistically significant; no effect size is printed, so the finding is reported as a non-significant difference without claiming equivalence.

"When two of the four NLCI schools offered full-day pre-k (2013-14), average pre-k attendance rates were higher in the NLCI schools than in similar schools by 3.8 percentage points, but the difference was not statistically significant"
