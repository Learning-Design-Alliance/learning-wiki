One qualifying comparison study of Pirate Math took place in Nashville and Houston, comparing low-achieving tutored students to similar controls. The page reports students "scored significantly higher than controls, with an effect size of +0.37" on Key Math.

"On Key Math, students in Pirate Math scored significantly higher than controls, with an effect size of +0.37."
