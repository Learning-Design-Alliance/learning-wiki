Evidence¶

Nicole Shechtman 2019¶

Nicole Shechtman, Jeremy Roschelle, Mingyu Feng, Corinne Singleton. (2019). An Efficacy Study of a Digital Core Curriculum for Grade 5 Mathematics. AERA Open. https://doi.org/10.1177/2332858419850482

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Exploratory two-level MLM extension with multiply imputed data, adding time logged in, objectives met, problems given, and problem accuracy to the model of Grade 5 WVGSA. Table 4 reports correlations of metrics with Grade 5 WVGSA (e.g., objectives met r = .548, accuracy r = .547, all p < .0001) and the Grade 4–Grade 5 correlation of .798. The article states "it was impossible to tease apart causality."

"Together, these added variables accounted for an additional 7.3% of the variance. Thus, after controlling for prior knowledge, these RM-CC5 usage metrics had a small positive linear relationship with Grade 5 WVGSA."

Related Claims¶

  • Assigning schools to use RM-CC5 as their core Grade 5 mathematics curriculum for a full year did not significantly change state assessment achievement compared with business-as-usual schools — related
  • Treatment students substantively engaged with RM-CC5 across the school year and all treatment schools met the program's implementation intensity benchmarks, though engagement varied widely between schools and students — related
  • No significant differential treatment effects by prior (Grade 4) achievement level or gender; the Grade 4 Level 3 subgroup difference was marginal (p = .053) — related
  • The authors interpret the null result as partly reflecting a tension between adaptive mastery learning and curricular coverage: students who master early topics slowly may not reach later topics tested by the state assessment — related
  • RM-CC5 treatment teachers shifted toward one-on-one instruction with individual students, while control teachers led more whole-group and small-group instruction — related
  • Treatment classrooms used technology far more intensively than control classrooms, whose technology use remained supplemental — related
