Value Added Modeling and Growth Modeling with Particular Application to Teacher and School Effectiveness, 237–269. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/the-effective-use-of-student-and-school-descriptive-indicators-of-learning-progress-from-the-conditional-growth-index-to-the-learning-productivity-measurement-system/

q1 · i? · theoretical · r?

The abstract states this policy context as background: NCLB required modeling student growth, and RTTP added evaluation of teacher effectiveness based on added value to student learning and growth. This is a policy statement, not an empirical finding.

"Modeling student growth has been a federal policy requirement under No Child Left Behind (NCLB). In addition to tracking student growth, the latest Race To The Top (RTTP) federal education policy stipulates the evaluation of teacher effectiveness from the perspective of added value that teachers contribute to student learning and growth."
