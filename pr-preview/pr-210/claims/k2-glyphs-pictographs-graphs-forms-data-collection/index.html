In the 'Using Glyphs for Data Collection and Analysis' segment, the webinar asserts that "Glyphs, pictographs, bar graphs and Google Forms are all ways to collect and analyze data" in K-2 classrooms. This is a presentational claim from a PD slide deck, with no empirical test reported.

"Use a culture themed children’s book to generate data. Glyphs, pictographs, bar graphs and Google Forms are all ways to collect and analyze data!"
