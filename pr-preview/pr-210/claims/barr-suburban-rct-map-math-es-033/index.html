q4 · i2 · causal · r?

A second student-level randomized study in a large suburban southern California high school found "a larger statistically significant effect on NWEA MAP math scores (effect size = +0.33)". Impacts were "somewhat stronger impacts for struggling students (+0.35)"; the sample was 52% White, 37% Hispanic, 17% English learners, 68% free or reduced-price lunch.

"A second student-level randomized study in a large suburban high school in southern California (n = 483; 52% White, 37% Hispanic, 17% English learners, 68% free or reduced-price lunch) found a larger statistically significant effect on NWEA MAP math scores (effect size = +0.33), with somewhat stronger impacts for struggling students (+0.35)."
