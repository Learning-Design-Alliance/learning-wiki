Supplemental findings table reporting students’ outcomes one year after the end of the intervention for 100 students in eight school units. The WRMT-R Passage Comprehension effect size was –0.06 with p > 0.05; the Oral Reading Fluency effect size was –0.07 and GRADE Passage Comprehension –0.07, all non-significant.
