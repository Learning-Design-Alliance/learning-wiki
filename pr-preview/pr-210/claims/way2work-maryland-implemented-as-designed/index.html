Key finding of the final implementation evaluation of the Way2Work Maryland demonstration. The report concludes that "the agency and the University of Maryland implemented the Way2Work Maryland model as designed" and that services were received as intended.

"Overall, the agency and the University of Maryland implemented the Way2Work Maryland model as designed, and participants received the model services as intended."
