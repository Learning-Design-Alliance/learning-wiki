Evidence¶

waite-total-math¶

U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, What Works Clearinghouse. (2015, November). Primary Mathematics intervention report: Everyday Mathematics®. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/166

q2 · i1 · causal · r2

WWC re-analysis of Waite (2000), a matched quasi-experimental comparison of six intervention schools against 12 matched comparison schools in a large north Texas urban district (3,436 students, grades 3–5). The TAAS Total Math effect size was 0.27 with an improvement index of +11; the "effect size was substantively important" but not statistically significant after clustering correction (WWC-computed p = .30).

"Based on WWC calculations, this effect was not statistically significant once corrections for clustering were made. However, the WWC determined that the effect size was substantively important (that is, 0.25 or greater)."
