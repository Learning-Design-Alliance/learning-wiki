q2 · i? · design · r2

Small-scale qualitative coding of 63 randomly selected video segments (21 interaction events × before, during, after) by one researcher using a codebook of gaze, overt help-seeking, and teacher presence. Gaze results included "30 of 63 segments (47.6%) were labelled “at task” gaze"; help-seeking appeared in 1 of 63 segments (1.6%) and teacher presence in 31 segments (49.2%).

"30 of 63 segments (47.6%) were labelled “at task” gaze, “at teacher” in 16 segments (25.4%), “at peer” in 10 segments (15.9%), “away - distracted” in four segments (6.3%), and ambiguous “other” gazes in three segments (4.8%)."
