Sarré 2011¶

Sarré, C. (2011). Computer-mediated negotiated interactions: how is meaning negotiated in discussion boards, text chat and videoconferencing? In S. Thouësny & L. Bradley (Eds.), Second language teaching and learning with technology: views of emergent researchers (pp. 189-210). Dublin: Research-publishing.net. https://dx.doi.org/10.14705/rpnet.2011.9781908416001

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Across all 12 groups, negative feedback totalled 119 occurrences: 112 self-corrections, 6 recasts, 1 explicit correction, and 0 questions and incorporations (Table 7). The authors interpret this via Schegloff, Jefferson and Sacks (1977): "learners thus prefer to use self-initiated, self-completed repairs because they are less face-threatening acts".

"Occurrences of negative feedback were more modest, as shown in Table 7, with the exception of unprompted self-corrections (SELCO)."
