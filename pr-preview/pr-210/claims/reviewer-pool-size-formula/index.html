A definitional formula with a worked example from the Step 3B section. The article states the pool size is determined by anticipated submissions within a scoring period, associate reviewers per submission, and submissions assigned per reviewer.

"if the issuer anticipates receiving 200 submissions per month, having three reviewers score each submission, and assigning 10 submissions per month to each reviewer, then the formula would be: (200*3)/10 = 60 reviewers."
