STEMscopes evidence rating summary 2024¶

STEMscopes evidence rating summary. (2024). Evidence rating: promising. https://www.stemscopes.com

q2 · i1 · causal · r?

A 2023-24 quasi-experimental study in an urban Texas district with a matched analytic sample of 990 students from 8 treatment and 8 comparison schools. The study reports STEMscopes students "did significantly outperform comparison students" on STAAR Science (effect size= +0.17), but the school-level result was not significant.

"While not significant at the school level, STEMscopes students did significantly outperform comparison students on the State of Texas Assessments of Academic Readiness (STAAR) Science test (effect size= +0.17)"
