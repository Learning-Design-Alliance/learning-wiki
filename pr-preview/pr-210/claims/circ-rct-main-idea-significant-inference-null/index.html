stevens-main-idea¶

U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, What Works Clearinghouse. (2012, June). Beginning Reading intervention report: Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition®. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/109

q3 · i3 · causal · r3

Cluster randomized trial of 30 classrooms (486 students) in four Harrisburg, Pennsylvania schools; 153 students in 10 CIRC classrooms versus 167 comparison students using basal readers. WWC calculations show a Main Idea Questions effect size of 0.85, improvement index +30, p < 0.01, favoring CIRC.

"Main Idea Questions Grades 3 and 4 20 classes/ 320 students 6.40 (1.83) 4.74 (2.03) 1.66 0.85 +30 < 0.01"

stevens-inference¶

U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, What Works Clearinghouse. (2012, June). Beginning Reading intervention report: Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition®. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/109

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Same cluster randomized trial (Stevens et al., 1991), WWC calculations for the author-developed 10-item Inference Questions test: effect size 0.19, improvement index +8, p > 0.05 — not statistically significant. The study reported outcomes after four weeks of implementation.

"Inference Questions Grades 3 and 4 20 classes/ 320 students 5.69 (2.14) 5.28 (2.08) 0.41 0.19 +8 > 0.05"
