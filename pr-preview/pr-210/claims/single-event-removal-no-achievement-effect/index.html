Evidence¶

Martin 2003¶

Martin, F., Klein, J., & Sullivan, H. (2003). Effects of Instructional Events in Computer-Based Instruction. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED484984

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Posttest means from the same six-version experiment: the three single-deletion treatments scored "between 17.16 and 17.36" versus 17.61 for the full program, a non-significant difference; equivalence was not formally tested.

"The absence of each of these elements individually in one of the three different treatments in the pres ent study (program without objectives, without examples or without review) consistently yielded a posttest score between 17.16 and 17.36 that varied only slightly and non -significantly from the score of 17.61 for students in the full program. Thus, there i s no evidence from this study that any of these three elements individually contributed to increased student learning."
