Bates 2013¶

Bates, S. P., Galloway, R. K., Riise, J., and Homer, D. (2013). Assessing the quality of a student-generated question repository. https://arxiv.org/abs/1308.2202

q3 · i1 · design · r2

Chi-squared tests on actual and expected cognitive-level distributions of sampled questions, comparing Physics 1B 2010 (N=179) and 2011 (N=73) repositories, shown in Figure 2. The article reports "a statistically signiﬁcant diﬀerence (p=0.022)". No effect size is printed.

"In contrast to the results for Physics 1A, Figure 2 exhibits a statistically signiﬁcant diﬀerence (p=0.022) between sampled 1B questions from subsequent years. In particular, there is a smaller fraction of questions in the lower taxonomic levels in the 2011 data."

q3 · i? · design · r2

Chi-squared test comparing Physics 1A cognitive-level distributions for 2010 (N=150) and 2011 (N=200) samples, shown in Figure 1. The article reports "no statistically signiﬁcant diﬀerence ( p=0.27)"; equivalence was not tested. No effect size is printed.

"A χ-squared test indicates that there is no statistically signiﬁcant diﬀerence ( p=0.27) between the distributions from the two years."
