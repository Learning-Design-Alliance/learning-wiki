Descriptive statement of the study's scope from the methods summary itself, not a reported result. The Mathematica team, in collaboration with Ecree, designed a study of "teachers’ and students’ use of the tool" and of impacts on student and teacher outcomes during the 2021-2022 school year. No effect sizes are printed in this summary.
