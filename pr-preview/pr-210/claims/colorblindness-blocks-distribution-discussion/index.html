Evidence¶

Payne 2005¶

Payne, Charles. (2005). Still Crazy after All These Years: Race in the Chicago School System. Consortium on Chicago School Research, University of Illinois at Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/still-crazy-after-all-these-years-race-chicago-school-system

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical/interpretive argument drawing on the author's observations of Chicago schools and on cited literature (Wellman 1977; Bonilla-Silva 2003; Lewis 2003; Pollock 2004). Payne argues color blindness functions as "the new, acceptable face of racism" and reinforces the racial status quo.

"Color blindness precludes a discussion of the distribution of the goodies, the distribution of privilege by race."
