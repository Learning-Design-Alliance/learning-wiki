q2 · i? · associational · r?

Quantitative finding from CPS postsecondary data on college application behavior of graduates of selective enrollment, IB, and AP programs. The report emphasizes that strong qualifications do not alter these students' need for concrete support in applying.

"In fact, one-fifth of students in academically advanced programs do not even apply to a four-year college."
