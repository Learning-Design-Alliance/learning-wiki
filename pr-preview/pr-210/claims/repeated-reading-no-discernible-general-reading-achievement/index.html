What Works Clearinghouse 2014¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2014). Repeated Reading: WWC Intervention Report, Students with Learning Disabilities. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/576

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

WWC review of the Wexler et al. (2010) trial using the Test of Silent Reading Efficiency with 62 students in 11 classrooms. The WWC-calculated effect size was not large enough to be substantively important; the report rates the domain no discernible effects with a small extent of evidence.

"Wexler et al. (2010) found no statistically significant effect of repeated reading on one measure of general reading achievement: the Test of Silent Reading Efficiency (TOSRE)."
