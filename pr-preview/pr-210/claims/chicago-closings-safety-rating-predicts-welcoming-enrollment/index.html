de la Torre 2015¶

de la Torre, M., Gordon, M. F., Moore, P., & Cowhy, J. (2015). School Closings in Chicago: Understanding Families' Choices and Constraints for New School Enrollment. UChicago CCSR Research Report. https://www.cps.edu/qualityschools/Pages/Parents.aspx

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Administrative-data analysis in Chapter 2 comparing enrollment at designated welcoming schools by safety levels reported on the My Voice, My School survey. The report describes this positive safety-enrollment relationship without printing an effect size.

"Families were more likely to enroll into designated schools where reported safety levels were higher than the district average, compared to designated schools with average or below-average safety levels."
