Evidence¶

Ceballos & Nutta 2022¶

Ceballos, M., & Nutta, J. W. (2022). Emergent bilinguals and multimedia instructional design: Applying the science of learning principles to dual language instruction. Education Leadership Review, 23(1). ERIC EJ1380081

q2 · i? · review · r2

A practice-to-theory article (the authors' own term), not a review of its own: it reports improved post-test outcomes for the multimedia principle (Frumuselu et al., 2015; Issa et al., 2013) and the Herrlinger et al. (2017) biology-text finding quoted here. No effect sizes printed.

"Herrlinger et al. (2017) found that learning improved when learners were presented with a biology text that used both words and pictures, particularly when words were delivered orally."
