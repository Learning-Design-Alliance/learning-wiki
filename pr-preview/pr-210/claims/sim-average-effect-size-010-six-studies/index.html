Strategic Instruction Model (SIM) 2024¶

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

Synthesis of six studies of SIM that met evidence standards, five of targeted forms and one of CLC. The review reports "four of the six effect sizes falling in the range from +0.07 to +0.15, with an average of +0.10"; no per-study effect sizes or sample-level statistics are printed.

"A total of six studies met standards. Five involved targeted forms of SIM and one involved CLC. Outcomes were remarkably consistent, with four of the six effect sizes falling in the range from +0.07 to +0.15, with an average of +0.10."
