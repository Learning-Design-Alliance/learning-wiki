q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Cluster randomized study with 41 fourth-grade classrooms in a large public school district in the South Central U.S.; treatment teachers implemented 30 CS lessons over a 14-22-week period while control teachers used local curricula. The article reports students in Connect Science classrooms "scored significantly higher on a science achievement measure" with "effect size = +0.36".

"Students in Connect Science classrooms scored significantly higher on a science achievement measure than students receiving business-as-usual science instruction (effect size = +0.36), earning Connect Science a Strong rating."
