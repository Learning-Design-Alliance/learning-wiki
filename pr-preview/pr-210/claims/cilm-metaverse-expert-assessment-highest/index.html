Sapliyan 2023¶

Sapliyan, S., Chatwattana, P., & Nilsook, P. (2023). Constructionism Imagineering Learning Model via Metaverse to Enhance Young Innovators. Journal of Education and Learning, 12(4). https://doi.org/10.5539/jel.v12n4p81

q2 · i? · design · r2

Expert assessment study: five experts in designing and developing learning models and systems from higher education institutions rated the model's compound elements on a five-level rating scale, with interpretation criteria from Kanasutra (1995) classifying 4.50-5.00 as highest. All five assessment issues were rated highest, with item means from 4.71 to 4.86. The result "are the highest appropriate (Mean = 4.74, S.D. = 0.05)".
