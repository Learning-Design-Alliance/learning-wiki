What Works Clearinghouse 2016¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2016). What Works Clearinghouse intervention report: University of Chicago School Mathematics Project (UCSMP). What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/641

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

Site-level results from the Hirschhorn (1993) longitudinal quasi-experiment diverged across the two sites; the WWC pooled them and found "no statistically significant differences" on the standardized Mathematics Level 1 Achievement Test, so equivalence was not established.

"Hirschhorn (1993) found no statistically significant effects in one site, and a positive and statistically significant effect in another, on the Mathematics Level 1 Achievement test. The WWC pooled the results from both sites and found no statistically significant differences between multiple UCSMP courses and comparison groups on the test."
