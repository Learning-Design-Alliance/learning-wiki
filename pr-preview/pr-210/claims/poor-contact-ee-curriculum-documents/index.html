Related Claims¶

  • Brazilian EE legislation shifted from Law 6938/1981's naturalistic, conservation-restricted discourse to the socio-environmental perspective of Law 9795/1999 and the 2013 DCNEB — related
  • The biology teacher-training curricular matrix devotes most environment-related hours to biological perspectives, with few courses promoting EE from other perspectives — related
  • Pre-service biology teachers' discourses about environmental education are predominantly restricted to conservation or preservation, with some confusion between EE and ecology — related
  • The dedicated EE course 'Instrumentation in Environmental Education' received no mentions among courses students cited as including EE — related
  • Student discourses showed little association between EE and socioeconomic issues, contrary to the interdisciplinary approach recommended by Brazilian legislation — related
