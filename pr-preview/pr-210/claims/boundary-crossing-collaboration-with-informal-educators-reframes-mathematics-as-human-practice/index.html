Evidence¶

Lili Zhou 2023¶

Lili Zhou. (2023). Humanizing Mathematics through Boundary Crossing Collaboration. Proceedings of the forty-fifth annual meeting of the North American Chapter of the International Group for the Psychology of Mathematics Education (Vol. 2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED658339

q1 · i? · qualitative · r2

Author's interpretation in the Discussion of a narrative inquiry with one informal educator, drawn from field texts and two interviews. She concludes the new practices "create a space that blurs the border of mathematics in different contexts". No outcome measure or effect size is reported.

"The new collaborative practices emerged as crossing boundary between mathematics education and informal education create a space that blurs the border of mathematics in different contexts which leads to a new understanding of mathematics as normal human practices rather than mandatory."

Lili Zhou 2023 (2)¶

Lili Zhou. (2023). Humanizing Mathematics through Boundary Crossing Collaboration. Proceedings of the forty-fifth annual meeting of the North American Chapter of the International Group for the Psychology of Mathematics Education (Vol. 2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED658339

q1 · i? · qualitative · r2

Narrative inquiry, author's concluding account of her collaboration with the GEMS founder. Both collaborators "have been involved in developing non-traditional mathematics curriculum" and report a changed understanding of mathematics. Self-reported, qualitative, no effect size.

"Through this boundary crossing collaboration, Laura and I both have been involved in developing non-traditional mathematics curriculum and have developed a new understanding of mathematics."
