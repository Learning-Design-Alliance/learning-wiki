Evidence¶

Deborah K. Anderson 2023¶

Deborah K. Anderson, Michelle Schoenleber, and Stuart Korshavn. (2023). Higher-Order Clicker Questions Engage Students and Prepare Them for Higher-Order Thinking Activities. Journal of Microbiology and Biology Education. https://doi.org/10.1128/jmbe.00151-22

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Paired-sample t tests following the 3 (pedagogical feature) × 2 (clicker type) × 2 (semester) mixed ANOVA on survey ratings. The article reports "students rated the textbook as less effective than both clicker questions" and lecture, with medium-to-large effect sizes.

"students rated the textbook as less effective than both clicker questions (t [48] = 4.74, P < 0.001, Cohen’s d = 0.70) and standard lecture/discussion (t [48] = 6.51, P < 0.001, Cohen’s d = 0.80)"
