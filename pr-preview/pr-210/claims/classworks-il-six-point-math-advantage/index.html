Classworks Individualized Learning 2022¶

Classworks Individualized Learning. (2022). Evidence summary. https://www.classworks.com

q2 · i1 · associational · r1

Retrospective study in the 2021-2022 school year of 1,367 second-grade students below the 25th percentile in math from 45 schools across seven districts. The summary reports "an average of six points higher on the spring math assessment than non-users" with effect size = +0.13.

"Classworks users scored an average of six points higher on the spring math assessment than non-users. This significantly positive impact (effect size = +0.13) qualifies Classworks Individualized Learning for a “Promising” evidence rating."
