q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

A 2022 research discovery drawn from teacher reflections: a teacher wrote about using "Appalachian people as examples of the scientists" to inspire students, and another described talks from Sam and Rusty as giving "goosebumps and tears" about regional possibilities.

"I want to use Appalachian people as examples of the scientists we discuss in my classroom to inspire my students that their birthplace does not hinder them and they can stay and follow their passions in Appalachia."
