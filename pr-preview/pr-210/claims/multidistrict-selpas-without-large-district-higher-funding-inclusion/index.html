Evidence¶

Doutre 2021¶

Doutre, S. M., Barrett, T., & Rice, J. (2021). California's Special Education Local Plan Areas: Funding patterns, inclusion rates, and student outcomes. Regional Educational Laboratory West at WestEd. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/edlabs

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive comparison of 24 versus 59 multidistrict SELPAs; table 3 reports per pupil apportionment means of $600 versus $522 with a printed difference statistic of 0.68, marked as meaningful. The authors attribute the gap largely to historical per pupil rates in the funding formula.

"Per pupil state and federal special education apportionment ($) 522 (506–547) 600 (494–1,230) 0.68*"

Doutre 2021 (3)¶

Doutre, S. M., Barrett, T., & Rice, J. (2021). California's Special Education Local Plan Areas: Funding patterns, inclusion rates, and student outcomes. Regional Educational Laboratory West at WestEd. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/edlabs

Same SELPA-level descriptive comparison; table 3 reports K–12 80-percent-or-more inclusion of "57 (42–78)" versus "53 (42–68)" percent with a printed difference statistic of 0.56, and lower under-40-percent inclusion in SELPAs without a large district (17 versus 22 percent, statistic 0.88).

"K–12 – percent of students who spend 80 percent or more of the school day in a general education setting 53 (42–68) 57 (42–78) 0.56*"
