Evidence¶

What Works Clearinghouse 2007¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2007). WWC Intervention Report: Peer Tutoring and Response Groups. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Randomized controlled trial of 42 Spanish-speaking migrant students in grades 3–5 in Florida, randomly assigned to bilingual tutoring, English-only tutoring, or teacher-led whole-group comparison, with daily 20-minute sessions over three months (37 sessions, 12.3 hours). The average effect size across the two versions was neither statistically significant nor substantively important. Equivalence was not tested.

"Serrano (1987) examined effects of the tutoring by a bilingual tutor and the tutoring by an English-only speaking peer on the IDEA Oral Language Proficiency Test (IPT I) and found no statistically significant effects for either strategy."
