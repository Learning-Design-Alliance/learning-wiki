Evidence¶

Imagine Math Facts 2023¶

Imagine Math Facts. (2023). Evidence rating summary, Johns Hopkins Institute for Education Policy style evidence review. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/imagine-math-facts-2/

q2 · i1 · associational · r2

Retrospective study in a southwestern school district during the 2022-2023 school year; over 2,000 users were matched with non-users on baseline demographics and math proficiency using Renaissance Star. The early elementary analytic sample (grades 1 and 2) showed "significantly more growth on the Ren Star" with effect size +0.11.

"Early elementary students using Imagine Math facts demonstrated significantly more growth on the Ren Star (effect size = + 0.11), earning a Promising rating for this grade band."
