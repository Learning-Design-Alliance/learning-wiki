What Works Clearinghouse 2007¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2007). WWC Intervention Report: Wilson Reading System®. What Works Clearinghouse, Beginning Reading. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/546

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Subgroup analyses reported in the alphabetics appendix of the Torgesen et al. (2006) randomized controlled trial, by initial skill level (WRMT-R word attack and PPVT) and socio-economic status. No subgroup differences were found for fluency or comprehension outcomes.

"The study found statistically significant positive effects on WRMT–R word attack scores at posttest only for students with initial high word attack scores and students with initial high PPVT scores. Finally, the study found statistically significant positive effects on WRMT–R word attack and TOWRE-PDE posttest scores only for students who were not eligible for free/reduced lunch program, but not for those students who were eligible for free/reduced lunch."
