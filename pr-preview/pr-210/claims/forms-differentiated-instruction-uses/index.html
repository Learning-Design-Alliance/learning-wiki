Digital Promise 2018¶

Digital Promise. (2018). Meeting Learners Where They Are: Using Microsoft Forms to Drive Improvement in Learning Outcomes. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/8a12967d-b67d-404f-875f-3dc61d0cc32e

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Interview evidence from one Scottish high-school teacher: Forms results were used to group pupils by performance level and to work directly with lower-achieving students on difficult content.

"If there was a wide spread of answers across the class he would group the top and middle-level pupils together so that the top students can provide support to the middle-level students."

Digital Promise 2018 (2)¶

Digital Promise. (2018). Meeting Learners Where They Are: Using Microsoft Forms to Drive Improvement in Learning Outcomes. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/8a12967d-b67d-404f-875f-3dc61d0cc32e

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Interview evidence from one U.S. high-school teacher who duplicates a Forms assessment and removes or modifies questions to satisfy Individualized Education Program accommodations.

"If an IEP requires students to have less answer choices or less questions,” he explains, “I remove some and send the IEP students the modified copy instead of the original one."
