Tare 2018¶

Tare, M., Nobles, S., & Xiao, W. (2018). Partnerships that work: Tapping research to address learner variability in young readers. Washington, DC: Digital Promise Global. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/e59c6e47-4209-4187-a0ef-b8da08283ec6

q1 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative interview study of three Midwestern K-3 teachers using the redesigned ReadWorks platform, interviewed in September and December 2017. The report states reactions were "unanimously positive" and audio was offered to all students.

"Teacher reactions to this feature were unanimously positive, and they often provided the audio option to all students, not just to readers who they felt needed extra supports."
