Dan Dewey 2024¶

Dan Dewey, Erin Fahle, Tom Kane, Megan Kuhfeld, Sean F. Reardon. (2024). Reconciling Recent Evidence on Academic Recovery in the US from 2022 to 2023. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/reconciling-recent-evidence-on-academic-recovery-in-the-us-from-2022-to-2023/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Same-method, different-sample comparison applying the ERS recovery methodology to approximately 2,300 NWEA districts and comparing with approximately 5,300 ERS districts. The comparison "still showed significantly different results in the two studies."

"The NWEA data show a far smaller recovery in math than the ERS data (.015 compared to .045 SDs); NWEA data show a negative recovery in ELA (-.008 SDs), while ERS data show a small positive ELA recovery trend (.019 SDs)."
