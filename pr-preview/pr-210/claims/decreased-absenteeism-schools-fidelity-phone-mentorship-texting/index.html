q2 · i? · associational · r1

Descriptive comparison of district-coded fidelity ratings for 39 PPSD schools in 2018/19, contrasting 8 schools with decreases of more than 2 percentage points against 20 with increases. The report states "all differences were greater than 5 percentage points" and prints 88 percent versus 45 percent for text messaging (figure 3).

"Schools in which chronic absenteeism decreased used phone calls, mentorship programs, and text messaging with moderate or high fidelity especially more frequently than schools in which chronic absenteeism increased; all differences were greater than 5 percentage points."
