Delgado 2023¶

Delgado, W. (2023). Is the Best Teacher the Best for Everyone? Variation of Teacher Effectiveness by Student Race. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/is-the-best-teacher-the-best-for-everyone

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of teacher characteristics linked to race-specific value-added in Chicago Public Schools data; the brief reports that experience and master's degrees raised scores for all students "but much less for Black students," while other characteristics were not strongly related to differential effectiveness.
