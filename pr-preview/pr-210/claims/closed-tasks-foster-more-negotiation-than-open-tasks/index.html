Sarré 2011¶

Sarré, C. (2011). Computer-mediated negotiated interactions: how is meaning negotiated in discussion boards, text chat and videoconferencing? In S. Thouësny & L. Bradley (Eds.), Second language teaching and learning with technology: views of emergent researchers (pp. 189-210). Dublin: Research-publishing.net. https://dx.doi.org/10.14705/rpnet.2011.9781908416001

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Distribution analysis across the six collaborative tasks (three open: CT4, CT6, CT8; three closed: CT3, CT5, CT7) in 12 groups shows negotiation routine totals of 68 in open tasks versus 102 in closed tasks, and negative feedback totals of 53 versus 66 (Table 9).

"negotiation routine phases are consistently more numerous in interactions produced during the completion of closed tasks, the total number of all four phases being 50% higher in such tasks than in open tasks."
