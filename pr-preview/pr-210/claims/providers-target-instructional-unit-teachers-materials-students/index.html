Evidence¶

Kara Finnigan and Jennifer O'Day 2003¶

Kara Finnigan and Jennifer O'Day, with the assistance of David Wakelyn. (2003). External Support to Schools on Probation: Getting a Leg Up? https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED474395

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

From the study's provider strategies section: providers "locate the problem of low performance within the schools"; external partners primarily focus on teachers when targeting individual classroom capacity, and most partners provide some type of materials, from assessments to lesson plans. Few partners emphasize content knowledge and learning.

"External partners and probation managers locate the problem of low performance within the schools — either at the classroom or organizational level."
