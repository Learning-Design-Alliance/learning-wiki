Qualitative analysis of coaches' implementation plans, self-report logs, focus groups, and co-design sessions across three districts. All districts started by building guiding coalitions, and coaches laid out plans to "create the climate for change" and "make the change happen."

"Following the session, coaches collaborated in district- specific pairs or teams, to co-construct implementation plans that represented their change leadership strategy, rooted in Kotter's framework."
