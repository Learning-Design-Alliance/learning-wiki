Chi, M. T. H., Adams, J., Bogusch, E. B., Bruchok, C., Kang, S., Lancaster, M., Levy, R., Li, N., McEldoon, K. L., Stump, G. S., Wylie, R., Xu, D., & Yaghmourian, D. L. (2018). The article's review of the K-12 engagement literature concludes that "cognitive engagement seems to be the least well-deﬁned" and is typically measured by surveys and self-reports, with deep/meaningful engagement not always linked to achievement.

"cognitive engagement seems to be the least well-deﬁned (Greene, 2015; Sinatra et al., 2015) and conﬂates with related constructs such as motivation, self-regulation, metacognition, and strategy usage"
