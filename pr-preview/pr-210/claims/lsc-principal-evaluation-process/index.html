Susan Ryan, Anthony S. Bryk, Gudelia Lopez, Kimberley P. Williams, Kathleen Hall, Stuart Luppescu. (1997). Charting Reform: LSCs - Local Leadership at Work. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/charting-reform-lscs%E2%80%94local-leadership-work

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Survey of ten questions on the principal evaluation process (Table 18): more than half report a comprehensive or very comprehensive evaluation, another third a minimal evaluation, and "Twelve percent of the members report that the LSC did no evaluation of the prin- cipal."

"Twelve percent of the members report that the LSC did no evaluation of the prin- cipal. This is a troubling sign for these councils, given the centrality of the principals' work to school improvement."

Susan Ryan 1997 (2)¶

Susan Ryan, Anthony S. Bryk, Gudelia Lopez, Kimberley P. Williams, Kathleen Hall, Stuart Luppescu. (1997). Charting Reform: LSCs - Local Leadership at Work. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/charting-reform-lscs%E2%80%94local-leadership-work

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Comparison of principal responses with other council members on evaluation-process items (Table 19); the greatest disparities concerned clarity, timing, and impact of the evaluation. The authors expect such discrepancies between those doing and receiving evaluations.

"Less than half of the principals say they were informed of the evaluation criteria at the be- ginning of the year, compared to two- thirds positive responses from other council members"
