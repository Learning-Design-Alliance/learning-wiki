Philadelphia Mayor's Commission on Literacy 1994¶

Philadelphia Mayor's Commission on Literacy. (1994). Collaborative Learning: An Alternative Approach to Adult Literacy Instruction. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED376329

q2 · i? · design · r1

The guide cites research at primary and secondary educational levels as the basis for its pragmatic rationale, stating students "learn more and learn better through non-competitive, collaborative group work." No effect size or specific study is reported, and the guide admits the adult extension is an untested assumption.

"research at the primary and secondary educational levels reveals that students learn more and learn better through non-competitive, collaborative group work than in classrooms that are highly individualized and competitive."
