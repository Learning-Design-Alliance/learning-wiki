q2 · i? · associational · r2

Trend analysis of teacher survey reports on access to computers, Internet, and email in classrooms and elsewhere in school (Figure 12). By 2003, 84 percent of elementary and 64 percent of high school teachers had classroom computers, and classroom Internet access practically doubled to about 60 percent.

"While substantially more teachers had access to computers in their classrooms, the biggest improvement in access to technology was more Internet connections."

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Trend analysis of teacher reports on assigning technology to students (Chapter 3). Despite a substantial rise in teachers' professional use of technology—including accessing model lesson plans, where never-use fell from about 40 percent to about one-quarter—assignment increases were modest.

"However, teachers did not increase their assignment of technology to students at the same rate at which they increased their own use of technology. Instead, those increases were modest."
