Districtwide descriptive estimate from CPS administrative data reported in the 2015 attainment brief. The brief states the estimate that "18 percent of CPS ninth-graders will obtain a four-year-college degree within 10 years"; no effect size is applicable.

"As of 2015, we estimate that 18 percent of CPS ninth-graders will obtain a four-year-college degree within 10 years of starting high school (the Bachelor's Degree Attainment Index or Bachelor's DAI; see Table 1 ), com- pared to an estimate of 17 percent in the 2014 brief."
