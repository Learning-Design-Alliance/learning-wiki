Rijmen 2009¶

Figure 6 plots the bifactor model's estimates of specific-dimension loadings divided by general-dimension loadings for the English assessment data. Were the testlet model to hold, these quotients should be approximately constant within each testlet, but "these quotients are far from constant within a testlet."
