Authors' interpretation (type e) in the Results and discussion section, mapping survey and interview data onto Chapelle's (2001) CALL criteria including usefulness, authenticity, focus on meaning, and learner fit.

"Participants found the technology useful (it promoted language learning) and authentic (it was used to write authentic texts) , and it fostered a focus on meaning (not form). There was learner fit, as ASR allowed participants to work at their level of English on texts for their ESL class"
