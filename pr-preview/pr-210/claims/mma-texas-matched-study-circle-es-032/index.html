My Math Academy evidence summary 2022¶

My Math Academy evidence summary. (2022). Age of Learning. https://www.ageoflearning.com/schools/systemcheck/

q2 · i1 · causal · r?

A matched study in 17 high-need Texas schools with 781 pre-kindergarten students (128 treatment, 653 control), analyzed using propensity score weighting. Treatment students "scored significantly higher on the overall math score" with effect size = +0.32.

"Students who used My Math Academy scored significantly higher on the overall math score on the CIRCLE Progress Monitoring System assessment (effect size = +0.32)."
