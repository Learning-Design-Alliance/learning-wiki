q3 · i? · review · r2

Systematic review of 156 papers applying NLP in LA, reporting frequencies of educational tasks in its results section. The review found "Collaborative learning was the most prominent task" with assessment (43) and feedback (35) next; remaining tasks included reading comprehension, outcome prediction, curricula analysis, and privacy.

"Collaborative learning was the most prominent task across the studies reviewed (N = 49; Figure 4). Next, 43 studies focused on assessment, 35 studies on feedback, seven on recommendation systems, and five on student modelling and self-regulation."
