q2 · i? · design · r2

Focus group with Student Ambassadors who led the Small Writing Groups plus other Core Team members in Pilot District 4. The mentor teacher corroborated, stating students "became regulars and would want to show their work," and described struggling writers agreeing to have their poem and story published. Qualitative perceptions only.

"Students highlighted the growing interest in the Small Writing Groups, noting an increase in attendance as more peers became eager to join."
