This is the author's own interpretive caution in the conclusion, responding to a skeptical colleague's question about students who have not made the effort to read. It is an author interpretation, not a tested result; no effect size is printed.

"Yes, motivation is key, and group activities, even those structured according to CL principles such as PIES, are not a magic wand that guarantees everything will work well. CL activities are only a potential part of the solution."
