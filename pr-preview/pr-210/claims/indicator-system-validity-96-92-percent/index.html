Bryk 1993¶

Bryk, A. S., Easton, J. Q., Kerbow, D., Rollow, S. G., & Sebring, P. A. (1993). A View from the Elementary Schools: The State of Reform in Chicago. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/view-elementary-schools-state-reform-chicago

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Validity assessment comparing indicator-based classifications against field-staff categorizations of 42 case-study synthesis schools for politics and 41 for improvement. The report states "Ninety-six percent of the sites classified as consolidated power or strong democracy by field staff were classified correctly by our analysis" and 92 percent for the restructuring approach.

"Ninety-six percent of the sites classified as consolidated power or strong democracy by field staff were classified correctly by our analysis. Similarly, 92 percent of the case-study schools were classified correctly in terms of an unfocused versus systemic restructuring approach"
