Evidence¶

Jones 2021¶

Jones, C. J., & Li, D. (2021). Testing the Impact and Scalability of the Future Forward Literacy Program. University of Wisconsin-Milwaukee. https://uwm.edu/sreed

q3 · i1 · review · r?

The report's summary of its own prior evaluations (Table 1): the 2011-13 pilot found a 0.12 SD reading impact; the 2013-15 RCT found 0.23 SD literacy gains and 4.53 fewer days absent; the 2018-19 RD study found a non-significant literacy impact (β = 0.16, p = 0.394).

"Each of these three studies meets the Institute for Education Sciences What Works Clearinghouse (WWC) evidence standards Without Reservations (WWC, 2020)."
