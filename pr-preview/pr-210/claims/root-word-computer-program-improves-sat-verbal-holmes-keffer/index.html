ACT/SAT Test Preparation and Coaching Programs 2016¶

ACT/SAT Test Preparation and Coaching Programs. (2016). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/664

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Randomized controlled trial at a rural Georgia high school in which intervention students received two 45-minute computer sessions per week for 6 weeks matching root words to definitions and derivatives. The WWC table row reports intervention mean 402.94 (SD 88.44) versus comparison 361.39 (SD 81.00), mean difference 41.55, effect size 0.49, improvement index +19, p .03 — a statistically significant positive effect.
