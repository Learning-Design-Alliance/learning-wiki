Documentary design rationale from the report's assessment-target section: the authors compared WHP course-guide skills with four U.S. frameworks and, citing "significant overlap between the categories ofhistorical thinking skills in WHP and skills described in the National Standards/Common Core, C3Framework, and AP World History Framework", selected the six dimensions listed in Table 1.

"Drawing from agreements across frameworks mentioned above, we drafted brief descriptionsfor each of the selected historical thinking practices/skills: historical argumentation, causation,comparison, contextualization, continuity and change over time, and sourcing"
