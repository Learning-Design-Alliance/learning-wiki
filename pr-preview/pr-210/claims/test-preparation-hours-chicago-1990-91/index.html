q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

Teacher survey data reported in Charting Reform: The Teachers' Turn, cited in the report's discussion of standardized testing burdens. The estimate aggregates teachers' reported test-preparation time across CPS elementary schools, described as equivalent to 720 complete school years of teacher time.

"The responses lead to a total estimate of 155,464 hours spent in test preparation in the CPS elementary schools during 1990-1991."
