Within the seven-case analysis, session-level redundancy indices on three VRM role dimensions were correlated with client and therapist WAI ratings each session; correlations were "generally non-significant, or significant in the wrong direction" (Table 5).

"Nor was relationship development (indexed in terms of response redundancy for the various role dimensions) related to the development of the working alliance (see Table 5). In this instance, the correlations between redundancy and WAI subscales were also generally non-significant, or significant in the wrong direction."
