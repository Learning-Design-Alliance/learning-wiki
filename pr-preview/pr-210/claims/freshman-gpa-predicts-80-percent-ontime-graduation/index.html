Evidence¶

Melissa Roderick 2009¶

Melissa Roderick, Jenny Nagaoka, and Vanessa Coca. (2009). College Readiness for All: The Challenge for Urban High Schools. The Future of Children. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/college-readiness-all-challenge-urban-high-schools

q2 · i? · review · r2

The article reports, citing Allensworth's analysis for the Consortium on Chicago School Research, that freshman-year GPA alone correctly predicted "80 percent of on-time high school graduates"; by contrast, eighth-grade test scores and risk measures together predicted only 65 percent. No effect size is printed.

"Using only freshman-year GPA, Elaine Allensworth from the Consortium on Chicago School Research at the University of Chicago was able to correctly predict 80 percent of on-time high school graduates."
