Xiong 2014¶

q2 · i? · causal · r2

ANOVA on total posttest scores in the three-group experiment; the pretest covariate was insignificant. With GPA in the model, the strategy effect was significant at the 5% level (F=2.96, p=0.05).

"The effect of the cognitive elaboration strategies is significant at the 10% level for the posttest (F = 2.5, P= 0.09). The pretest is an insignificant covariate for the posttest performance (F= 0.24, p=0.63)."
