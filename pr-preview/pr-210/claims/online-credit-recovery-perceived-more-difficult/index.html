Online and Face-to-Face Algebra I Credit Recovery: A Randomized Trial in Chicago Public High Schools 2017¶

Online and Face-to-Face Algebra I Credit Recovery: A Randomized Trial in Chicago Public High Schools. (2017). Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness. https://www.tandfonline.com

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Randomized trial in which 1,224 ninth-graders who failed algebra in 17 Chicago public high schools were assigned to online or face-to-face credit recovery; students in "online credit recovery reported that the course was more difficult" than face-to-face peers. No effect size is printed.

"Compared to students in face-to-face credit recovery, students in online credit recovery reported that the course was more difficult"
