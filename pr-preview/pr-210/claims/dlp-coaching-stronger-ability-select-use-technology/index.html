Bakhshaei 2019¶

Bakhshaei, M., Hardy, A., Ravitz, J., & Seylar, J. (2019). Scaling Up Classroom Coaching for Impactful Technology Use: Results from Year 2 of the Dynamic Learning Project. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/79337f05-0795-4bac-b1d7-f4758c23285c

q2 · i? · associational · r1

End-of-year teacher survey in DLP participating schools comparing coached and non-coached teachers. The report adds that "DLP teachers also more frequently reported being better able to select and use technology to teach their specific content area (74 percent versus 62 percent)."

"Compared to non-DLP teachers, by the end of the year, more DLP teachers (72 percent versus 60 percent) reported having a strong or very strong ability to select and use technology to improve their teaching approaches (pedagogy)"
