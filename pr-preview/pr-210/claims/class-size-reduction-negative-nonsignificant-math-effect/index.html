Filges 2018¶

Filges, T., Sonne‐Schmidt, C. S., Nielsen, B. C. V. (2018). Small class sizes for improving student achievement in primary and secondary schools: a systematic review. Campbell Systematic Reviews 2018:10. https://doi.org/10.4073/csr.2018.10

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-analysis of non-STAR studies of mathematics achievement using random-effects models; the pooled SMD was ‐0.03 (95% CI ‐0.22 to 0.16), with inconsistency in direction and magnitude across primary studies and no statistically significant effect.

"Primary study effect sizes for mathematics lies in the range ‐0.41 to 0.11. Two of the study‐level effects were statistically non‐significant. The weighted average was negative and statistically non‐significant."
