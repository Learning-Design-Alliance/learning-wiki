q2 · i? · associational · r2

NCES-based district enrollment analysis (Figure 3), operationalized as four-year-old preschool participation relative to first-grade enrollment, comparing AY2012-13 to AY2022-23. Growing-category districts rose "from 12.0% to 25.7%"; High and Low districts also grew more than expected (+51% and +52%).

"PreK4 participation rates among districts in the Growing category more than doubled from 12.0% to 25.7%, representing an increase of 13.7 percentage points or 114%."

Figure 4 plots district-level PreK4 enrollment by state category with axes hidden for de-identification. The report states there is "substantial variation in PreK4 enrollment trends within each state category."

"As shown in Figure 4, there is substantial variation in PreK4 enrollment trends within each state category. For example, some districts’ enrollment rates grew substantially even if they were not part of the Growing state category."
