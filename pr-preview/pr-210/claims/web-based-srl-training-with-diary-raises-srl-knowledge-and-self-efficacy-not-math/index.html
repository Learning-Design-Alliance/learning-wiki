Evidence¶

Bellhäuser 2022¶

Bellhäuser, H., Liborius, P., & Schmitz, B. (2022). Fostering Self-Regulated Learning in Online Environments: Positive Effects of a Web-Based Training With Peer Feedback on Learning Behavior. Frontiers in Psychology, 13, 813381. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.813381

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Planned contrasts testing each group's pre-to-post gain in the randomized trial. Group TD (training + diary) "showed statistically significant increases in SRL knowledge (β = 3.95; p < 0.001)", SRL overall score (β = 0.31) and self-efficacy (β = 0.20; p = 0.04) "but not in mathematics scores".

"As seen in Table 3, Group TD showed statistically significant increases in SRL knowledge (β = 3.95; p < 0.001), in the SRL overall score (β = 0.31; p < 0.001) and in self-efficacy (β = 0.20; p = 0.04) but not in mathematics scores."

Bellhäuser 2022 (2)¶

Bellhäuser, H., Liborius, P., & Schmitz, B. (2022). Fostering Self-Regulated Learning in Online Environments: Positive Effects of a Web-Based Training With Peer Feedback on Learning Behavior. Frontiers in Psychology, 13, 813381. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.813381

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Planned gain contrasts on the seven SRL questionnaire subscales. "Group TD showed statistically significant increases in planning (β = 0.55; p < 0.001) and in metacognition (β = 0.44; p < 0.01)", whereas Group C and Group D showed no significant subscale increases.

"Although Group C and Group D showed no statistically significant increases in any of the SRL subscales, Group TD showed statistically significant increases in planning (β = 0.55; p < 0.001) and in metacognition (β = 0.44; p < 0.01)."
