WWC synthesis of the evidence base: one randomized controlled trial (Sivin-Kachala & Bialo, 2005) in five schools across two districts, final analyses based on 66 intervention and 62 comparison students; extent of evidence rated small in both reviewed domains (Appendix A6).

"The WWC considers the extent of evidence for Fluency Formula™ to be small for fluency and comprehension. No studies that met WWC evidence standards with or without reservations addressed alphabetics or general reading achievement."
