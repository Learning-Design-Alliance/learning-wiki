Roderick, M., Arney, M., Axelman, M., DaCosta, K., Steiger, C., Stone, S., Villarreal-Sosa, L., & Waxman, E. (1997). Habits Hard to Break: A New Look at Truancy in Chicago's Public High Schools. q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of transcripts and attendance records for the CPS ninth grade class of 1995-96 (about 30,000 students), measuring full-day absences and absences from the student's worst major subject. The brief reports "the majority (64 per- cent)" missed more than two weeks, and 42 percent missed a month or more.

"In the second semester, the majority (64 per- cent) of CPS ninth graders missed more than two weeks of instruction in one or more major subjects— English, Math, Social Studies, or Science."
