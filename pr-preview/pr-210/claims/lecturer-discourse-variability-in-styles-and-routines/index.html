The review reports that lecturers characterized as teaching within the traditional DTP format nonetheless differed: Weber (2004) discerned logico-structural, procedural, and semantic teaching styles, Hemmi (2010) identified progressive, deductive, and classical styles, and Viirman (2014, 2015) found differences in the frequency and character of routines across lecturers. → Karavi 2022

Evidence¶

Karavi 2022¶

Karavi, Mali, & Avraamidou. (2022). Commognition as an approach to studying proof teaching in university mathematics lectures. EURASIA Journal of Mathematics, Science and Technology Education. https://doi.org/10.29333/ejmste/12173

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review synthesizes Weber's (2004) observational and interview study of one lecturer's real analysis course and Hemmi's (2010) interview study of mathematicians' pedagogical views, reporting the style typologies each produced. It also reports Viirman found lecturers used routines in "different ways and to different extent".

"the studies provided the language to characterize the teaching styles of the lecturers that took into account students' learning (i.e., progressive, deductive, and classical (Hemmi, 2010), and logico-structural, procedural, and semantic (Weber, 2004))."
