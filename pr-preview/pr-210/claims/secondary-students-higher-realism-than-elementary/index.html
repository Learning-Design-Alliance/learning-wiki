Starkey 1972¶

Starkey, John D.; Barr, Rita L. (1972). The Philosophical Nature of Teachers--Graduate and Undergraduate. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED072018

q2 · i? · the article prints no effect size for this finding · associational · r1

Survey comparison of undergraduate secondary (mean 40.035) and elementary (mean 28.500) education students using the Ames Philosophical Belief Inventory; the article reports "a significant difference (at the .001 level) in the area of Realism" with a t value of 5.02.

"students in secondary education compared with those in elementary show a significant difference (at the .001 level) in the area of Realism.Those enrolled in secondary programs indicate a belief in Realism at a highermean than those in elementary."
