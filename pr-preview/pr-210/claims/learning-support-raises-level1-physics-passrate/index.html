MM Casey and S McVitie 2009¶

MM Casey and S McVitie. (2009). Academic performance & student engagement in level 1 physics undergraduates. https://arxiv.org/abs/0911.2733

q2 · i2 · design · r2

Observational comparison of passrates across academic years 2002-03 to 2007-08 (Figure 3) after introduction of the support regime. The article reports "the P1X passrate had increased by 5.7%" to 82.9% and P1Y by 8.5% to 80.9%, both exceeding 3 historical standard deviations.

"Compared to the historical averages, the P1X passrate had increased by 5.7% (and 3 σP 1X ) to 82.9% while P1Y had increased by 8.5% (and 4 σP 1Y ) to 80.9%."
