Robust variance estimation meta-analysis of 349 student-level effect sizes from 4 reports (Table 2). The weighted mean was "Student‐level −0.09" with p = 0.657 and a wide CI [−0.86, 0.69]; no statistically significant difference, and equivalence was not tested.
