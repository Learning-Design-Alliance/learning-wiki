Haller 1963¶

Haller, Archibald O. and Miller, Irwin W. (1963). The Occupational Aspiration Scale: Theory, Structure and Correlates. Michigan State University Agricultural Experiment Station. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED016712

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Summary statement of the monograph's empirical tests of hypotheses about LOA's correlates, drawn from extensive data including previously unpublished material. The abstract reports that "PREDICTED CORRELATIONS IN THE LOA CONCEPT ARE BORNE OUT SO PERCENT OF THE TIME" (apparently a typographical rendering of 50 percent) while noncorrelates held 70 percent of the time.

"EMPIRICAL DATA PRESENTED SHOWS THAT THE. PREDICTED CORRELATIONS IN THE LOA CONCEPT ARE BORNE OUT SO PERCENT OF THE TIME WHILE PREDICTED NONCORRELATES ARE FOUND TRUE 70 PERCENT OF THE TIME."
