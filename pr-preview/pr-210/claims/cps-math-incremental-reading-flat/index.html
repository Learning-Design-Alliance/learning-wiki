q2 · i? · associational · r2

Trend analysis of rescaled CPS elementary/middle-grade test scores across the three reform eras. The report describes math gains as incremental: students now score like students "one year older in the early 1990s," yet remain at the low end of the meets-standards range. No effect size is printed.

"Math scores have risen in the elementary/middle grades; students are now scoring at a level similar to students who were one year older in the early 1990s, at least in some grade levels."

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Trend analysis of CPS reading scores by age cohort across three reform eras. The report states reading scores "have not shown much improvement," with some Era 2 lower-grade gains and modest gains among white and Asian students only. No effect size is printed.

"Reading scores in the elementary/middle grades have not shown much improvement over the three eras of school reform."
