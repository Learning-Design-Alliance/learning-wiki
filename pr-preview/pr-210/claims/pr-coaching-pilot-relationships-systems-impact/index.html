Carol Lopez and Odelia Younge 2021¶

Carol Lopez and Odelia Younge. (2021). Cultivating a Culture of Coaching to Support Powerful Use of Technology: Results from the Challenge-based Coaching Model Pilot in Puerto Rico. Digital Promise report. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/71c26d6b-e789-4ca3-b643-dcc98422a36d

q2 · i? · design · r2

Focus group and report Impact section (Relationships and Systems) from the end-of-pilot evaluation. The report prints that the pilot "was instrumental in strengthening the school leaders' relationships with the coaches" and that one school leader worked to transition a temporary MRUC (coach) to a full-time employee in following years; no effect sizes are reported.

"The pilot encouraged school leaders to advocate for full-time dedicated coaches to scale the coaching model."
