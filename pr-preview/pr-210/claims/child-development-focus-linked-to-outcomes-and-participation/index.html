A Case for Increasing Empirical Attention to Head Start's Home-Based Program: An Exploration of Routine Collaborative Goal Setting. The review attributes this to a national Early Head Start evaluation across 17 programs (Raikes et al., 2006), where about 57% of visit time concerned child development; it also cites Roggman et al. (2008) and McCurdy et al. (2006) on participation. The reviewed studies' designs are not described here in detail.

"At the conclusion of Early Head Start services, the three-year-old children whose home visiting had more attention to child development content showed the greatest developmental gains."
