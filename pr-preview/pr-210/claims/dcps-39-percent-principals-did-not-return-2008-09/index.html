Descriptive background from the brief on the scale of principal replacement under Chancellor Rhee, following the 2007 Public Education Reform Amendment Act that established mayoral control of DCPS. The printed figures are 39 percent and 51 individuals.

"For the 2008–2009 school year, 39 percent of the principals in the school district—51 individuals—did not return, and more were replaced in the following years."
