q3 · i? · causal · r?

WWC aggregation of all Bacon (2001a) outcomes in the randomized controlled trial. Appendix A3 prints the study average as "0.35 ns +14"; individual outcomes including nonviolence attitudes (0.33) and assertiveness (0.29) were marked ns.

"The average effect size across all outcomes in this study in the knowledge, attitudes, and values domain was large enough to be considered substantively important using WWC criteria, although it was not statistically significant."
