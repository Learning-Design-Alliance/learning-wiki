Evidence¶

Grădinariu 2021¶

Grădinariu, T., & Assante, G. M. (2021). An Exploratory Factor Analysis and Reliability Analysis of the Perceived Severity and Response to Bullies and Victims of Bullying Questionnaire. Journal of Educational Sciences. https://doi.org/10.35923/JES.2021.2.03

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Exploratory factor analysis of the 18-item questionnaire with 322 Romanian middle school teachers, using Principal Axis Factoring with Oblimin rotation. The article reports "KMO=0.81>0.60" and that the three-factor structure "explained 51.45% of the variance in the pattern of relationships among the items".

"Barlett's test of sphericity χ² (153) = 3762,570, p <0.001, suggests that items-correlations are sufficiently large for EFA and a three-factor solution resulted."
