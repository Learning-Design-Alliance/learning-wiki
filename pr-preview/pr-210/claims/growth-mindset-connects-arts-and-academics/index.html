The Art and Science of Learning: How New Mexico School for the Arts Uses Research. (2016). Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/4e829bdc-764f-4f2e-85de-abc138e21bdd

q1 · i? · qualitative · r1

This is a qualitative case-study account: the school president's interpretation that growth mindset, the understanding that failure is part of learning and improvement is possible with hard work, bridges arts and academics. No outcome data are reported for this assertion.

"Montoya believes growth mindset already helped connect the art and academic aims of the school: "Each thing that we do is just one step towards getting better at something. This really resonates in the arts and it crosses over to the academics.""
