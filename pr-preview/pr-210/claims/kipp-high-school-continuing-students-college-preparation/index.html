Evidence¶

Tuttle 2015¶

Tuttle, C. C., Gleason, P., Knechtel, V., Nichols-Barrer, I., Booker, K., Chojnacki, G., Coen, T., & Goble, L. (2015). Going to Scale: As KIPP Network Grows, Positive Impacts Are Sustained (In Focus Brief). Washington, DC: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/going-to-scale-as-kipp-network-grows-positive-impacts-are-sustained

q3 · i? · causal · r?

The Key Findings section reports positive impacts on college preparation activities and on the likelihood of applying to college for continuing KIPP students, the same group whose achievement impacts were not statistically significant. No effect sizes are printed in the brief.

"For this group of continuing KIPP students, KIPP high schools have positive impacts on a variety of college preparation activities and the likelihood of applying to college."
