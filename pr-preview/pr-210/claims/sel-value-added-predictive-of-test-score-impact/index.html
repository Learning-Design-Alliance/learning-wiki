Value-added analysis of annual Chicago Public Schools student survey data identified high schools' impacts on ninth-grade social-emotional development and test scores; the authors report the comparison, stating the measures are "nearly as predictive."

"Compared to test-score value-added, measures of social-emotional value-added are nearly as predictive of a high school's impact on test scores."
