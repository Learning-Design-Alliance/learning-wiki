Evidence¶

Nagaoka 2021¶

Nagaoka, J., Lee, J.S., Usher, A., & Seeskin, A. (2021). Navigating the maze: Understanding CPS graduates' paths through college. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/navigating-the-maze

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Observational analysis of transfer behavior among approximately 63,000 CPS graduates. The study reports downward transfer at "29 percent of four-year college enrollees" versus upward transfer at "22 percent of two-year college enrollees."

"It was more common for CPS graduates to enroll in a four-year college and then transfer to a two-year college (29 percent of four-year college enrollees) than to enroll in a two-year college and transfer to a four-year college (22 percent of two-year college enrollees)."
