Allensworth 2026¶

Allensworth, E.M., Antony, M., Delgado, W., & de la Torre, M. (2026). Connection, trust, and learning: Student attendance in the middle and high school grades following the COVID-19 pandemic. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/connection-trust-and-learning

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Descriptive analysis of CPS administrative attendance records for the population of students in grades 6-11 enrolled at least 20 days, comparing 2017-2019 with 2022-2024. The brief reports middle-grade chronic absenteeism "jumped to 33% in 2022" and high school rates reached 49% to 57%.

"Before the pandemic (2017-2019), chronic absenteeism rates in the middle grades were at or just over 10%, and that number jumped to 33% in 2022 after schools returned to full in-person instruction that year."
