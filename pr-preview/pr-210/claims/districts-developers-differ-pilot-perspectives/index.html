Evidence¶

Adams-Bass 2015¶

Adams-Bass, V., Atchison, D., & Moore, L. (2015). Piloting Ed-tech Products in K-12 Public Schools: Pilot-to-Purchase Project. A Report from the University of California Davis School of Education to Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/38af8409-82b3-4f0e-baff-2a093963bada

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

The report cites the Fostering Market Efficiency in K-12 Ed-tech Procurement study by Johns Hopkins University and Digital Promise as finding that "school districts and developers have different perspectives" about pilots' role and the value of pilot information.

"school districts and developers have different perspectives about the role that school-based education technology pilots play in this process and the value of information collected during pilots."
