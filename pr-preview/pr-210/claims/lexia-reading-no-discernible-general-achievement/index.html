What Works Clearinghouse 2009¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2009). WWC Intervention Report: Lexia Reading, Beginning Reading. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/274

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

WWC synthesis of general reading achievement from Macaruso, Hook, and McCabe (2006), a randomized controlled trial using the Gates-MacGintie Reading Test, Level BR: Form S. The effect size was 0.23, not statistically significant and not substantively important; the report states the index reflects "only one outcome."
