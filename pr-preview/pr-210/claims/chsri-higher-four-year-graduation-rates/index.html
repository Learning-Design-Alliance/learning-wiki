q2 · i? · causal · r2

Cohort analysis of four-year graduation rates for students entering as freshmen in 2002, 2003, and 2004. In the 2004-05 cohort, 57.2 percent of CHSRI students graduated versus 49 percent of similar students; rates remained below 60 percent for all cohorts. No effect size printed.
