The report's observed model (Figure 2), developed from analysis of over 130 hours of observation, formal interviews with 20 teens and 15 staff, and a stakeholder focus group. The model highlights the interactive, responsive, and dynamic nature of the environment and the crucial role of peer-to-peer and youth-staff relationships.
