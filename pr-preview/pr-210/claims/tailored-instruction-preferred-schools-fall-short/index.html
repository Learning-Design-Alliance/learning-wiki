Digital Promise Global 2019¶

Digital Promise Global (2019). Learning in the 21st Century: How the American Public, Parents, and Teachers View Students’ Potential and Their Learning Experience. Digital Promise Global: Washington, DC. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/32480546-27ed-4785-8703-bf66b74f6bfd

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Survey preference question contrasting whole group instruction with tailored instruction; respondents chose tailored instruction as "the “better way for students to learn.”" More than six in 10 felt that way strongly, including 67 percent of teachers.

"Vast majorities—79 percent of parents, 80 percent of the general public, and 86 percent of teachers surveyed—see tailored instruction as the “better way for students to learn.”"

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Survey ratings of local schools' support for learner variability found "comparatively few think public schools currently do an excellent or good job"; only 20 to 39 percent across groups believed tailored instruction is commonly used in schools today.

"Yet, comparatively few think public schools currently do an excellent or good job supporting learner variability—35 percent of the general public, 42 percent of parents, and 53 percent of teachers ."
