New York Charter Schools: Remote Instruction During COVID Crisis (Spring 2020) – Results for All Authorizers. (2020). Survey slide report of New York State charter schools, May 2020. https://credo.stanford.edu/reports/item/report-3/

Descriptive survey of ELL/MLL support during remote instruction: "Just under half (45.8%) of schools reported no challenges in supporting ELL/MLL students during this period." Among the rest, the most cited challenges were communicating with families (34%) and locating effective online instructional materials (20%).
