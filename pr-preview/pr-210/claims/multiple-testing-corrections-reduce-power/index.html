Peter Z. Schochet 2008¶

Peter Z. Schochet. (2008). Guidelines for Multiple Testing in Impact Evaluations of Educational Interventions. Princeton, NJ: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/guidelines-for-multiple-testing-in-impact-evaluations-of-educational-interventions

q1 · i? · theoretical · r?

The report's abstract states as a methodological premise that "Statistical procedures that correct for multiple testing typically result in hypothesis tests with reduced statistical power". No empirical study or effect size is reported on the page; this is the authors' stated methodological position.

"Statistical procedures that correct for multiple testing typically result in hypothesis tests with reduced statistical power because adjustment methods reduce the likelihood of identifying real differences between contrasted groups."
