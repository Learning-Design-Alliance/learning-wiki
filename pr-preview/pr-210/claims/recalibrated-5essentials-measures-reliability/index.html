Evidence¶

Hart 2020¶

Hart, H., Young, C., Chen, A., Zou, A., & Allensworth, E.M. (2020). Supporting school improvement: Early findings from reexamination of the 5Essentials survey. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/supporting-school-improvement

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Psychometric recalibration recreating all measures from recent data (2014–19) to check whether they maintained expected reliability and could still differentiate schools, since some original item parameters were created as long as 25 years ago.

"We found each of the measures had acceptable Rasch reliability, with the majority able to differentiate several strata of respondents (>0.80)."

Hart 2020 (2)¶

Hart, H., Young, C., Chen, A., Zou, A., & Allensworth, E.M. (2020). Supporting school improvement: Early findings from reexamination of the 5Essentials survey. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/supporting-school-improvement

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Intraclass correlation analysis of the recalibrated measures, checking school-level variance. All measures exceeded the ICC 0.05 threshold the report sets for usefulness in differentiating schools.

"We also calculated the intraclass correlation coefficient (ICC) for schools and found that all of the measures explained sufficient variance at the school level to be useful in differentiating schools (ICC >0.05)."
