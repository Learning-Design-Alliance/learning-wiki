The guide's introduction reports, as field context rather than a tested study, that district edtech tool access "grew by more than 350%" between 2019 and 2025 while most tools see little use. No data source or analysis is printed for these figures.

"Between 2019 and 2025, the average number of edtech tools accessed per school district grew by more than 350%. Yet most edtech tools are not used at all or used at low levels of intensity, with only a small share of students engaging at the dosage needed for impact."
