q2 · i? · causal · r?

Two retrospective quasi-experimental comparisons of math achievement between ST Math schools and business-as-usual schools in grades 2-5. The page reports "Results were stronger for students in the ST Math schools" but prints no effect size for these studies.

"The two retrospective studies involved students using ST Math in grades 2-5 in over 800 schools and compared their math achievement to students exposed to business-as-usual teaching in over 500 schools. Results were stronger for students in the ST Math schools."
