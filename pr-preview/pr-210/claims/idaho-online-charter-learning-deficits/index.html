CREDO 2019¶

CREDO, Center for Research on Education Outcomes, Stanford University. (2019). Charter School Performance in Idaho. https://credo.stanford.edu

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Delivery-system comparison benchmarking online and brick-and-mortar charter students against statewide average TPS VCRs. Online charter students show similar reading growth and weaker math growth, a gap of 59 fewer days of learning in math.

"students attending online charter schools have similar growth in reading and weaker growth in math compared to the average TPS VCRs . The gap translates to 59 fewer days of learning in math for online charter students ."

CREDO 2019 (2)¶

CREDO, Center for Research on Education Outcomes, Stanford University. (2019). Charter School Performance in Idaho. https://credo.stanford.edu

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Figure 8a comparison of online versus brick-and-mortar charter students. Online students gain significantly less in both subjects, behind brick-and-mortar charter students by 47 days of learning in reading, with a greater math lag of 77 days.

"Online charter school students gain significantly less in both subjects. To be specific, they are behind brick-and-mortar charter students by 47 days of learning in reading."
