Luppescu 2011¶

Luppescu, S., Allensworth, E. M., Moore, P., de la Torre, M., Murphy, J., with Jagesic, S. (2011). Trends in Chicago's Schools Across Three Eras of Reform. Consortium on Chicago School Research, University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/trends-chicagos-schools-across-three-eras-reform-full-report

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Trend analysis of CPS student-level data across three reform eras, reported in the executive summary. The report states students went from being "about as likely to drop out as to graduate" to more than twice as likely to graduate, with improvements among all racial/ethnic groups and both boys and girls. No effect size is printed.

"In the early 1990s, students who entered Chicago high schools were about as likely to drop out as to graduate. Now they are more than twice as likely to graduate as to drop out."

"high school students have improved their performance on the tests administered to all high school juniors in Illinois, with ACT scores rising by about a point over the last decade"
