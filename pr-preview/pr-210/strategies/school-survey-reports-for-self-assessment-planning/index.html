Use individualized school survey reports for self-assessment, internal program review, and improvement planning¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report is 'specially prepared to assist in self-assessment and long-term planning.' It states that information about where a school is and how it is developing can help the school assess its progress and plan for the future, and that it 'can prove invaluable in carrying out an internal program review as part of the preparations for the School Improvement Plan for Advancing Academic Achievement (SIPAAA).' Survey data also supplement analyses of test scores and performance indicators to identify effective classroom practices and school organizational characteristics.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A sufficient response rate (at least 42 percent of teachers or 50 percent of students) for a school to receive a report

Constraints¶

  • Reports are confidential and require the principal's written permission for distribution outside the school

Target Learners¶

  • Chicago Public School principals, teachers, and Local School Councils

Target Learning Goals¶

  • school improvement planning
  • identifying effective classroom practices and organizational characteristics
