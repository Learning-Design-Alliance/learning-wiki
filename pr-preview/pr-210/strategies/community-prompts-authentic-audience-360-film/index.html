Use community-focused prompts and authentic audiences to make creative projects personally meaningful¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Program observations identified the challenge prompts (share what makes your community special, inspire action for positive change, or help people learn something new) and creating with a purpose about issues students care about as valuable design features. The prompts' community focus helped teachers become more aware of their students' lives and helped students find value in their own story, while the competition element and possibility of reaching a broad audience beyond their schools motivated students to push themselves.
