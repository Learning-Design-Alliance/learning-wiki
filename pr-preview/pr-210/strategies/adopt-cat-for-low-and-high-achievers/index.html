Adopt computerized adaptive testing to serve both low- and high-achieving students¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The strategy is for schools to adopt computerized adaptive testing as their achievement assessment, replacing fixed-form testing. The article reports that this approach is "being used by increasing numbers of schools" and explains that it pictures the achievement of "both low-achieving and high-achieving students" more precisely by adjusting questions during testing, so no student is tested far above or below their level by a fixed form.
