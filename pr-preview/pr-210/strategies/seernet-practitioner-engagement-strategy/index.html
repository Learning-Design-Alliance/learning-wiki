Engaging practitioners through an advisory board, co-design office hours, and guiding-principles publications¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

After finding a common practitioner database across DLPs unlikely and footprint reports risky for customer information, SEERNet adopted a broader engagement approach that engages educators rather than merely identifying them. Digital Promise "Published Practitioners at the Center: Catalyzing Research on Problems of Practice in Realistic Settings," hosted conversational Office Hours where researchers and practitioners co-designed research questions, and launched a SEERNet practitioner advisory board to engage K-12 educators in conversations about meaningful research and dissemination. Wave 3 plans extend this to higher education.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Respect each DLP's distinct modes of engagement with schools; customer information release restrictions shaped the design

Constraints¶

  • Practitioner engagement so far has been easiest to imagine in K-12 and needs expansion to higher education over time

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 educators and leaders
  • higher education practitioners over time

Target Learning Goals¶

  • shaping research questions toward practical problems of practice
  • broadening dissemination and impact of research findings

Related Strategies¶

  • Run conversational office hours in which practitioners and researchers co-design research questions with platform representatives
