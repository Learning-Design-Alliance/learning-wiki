Conduct voluntary, pair-based teacher home visits focused on hopes and dreams across a cross-section of students¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

This strategy implements PTHV's five non-negotiables as concrete practices for schools adopting home visits. Teachers visit in pairs, arrange visits in advance, keep them voluntary, focus conversation on hopes and dreams, and visit a cross-section of students rather than targeting struggling students. The page states "Educators visit a cross-section of students (not a targeted intervention)" and "Educators go in pairs and reflect."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers are trained and compensated for visits
  • Visits are voluntary and arranged in advance

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Elementary school students and their families

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Teacher-family relationship building and improved classroom instruction

Related Strategies¶

  • Home Visits
  • Two-visit structure: relationship-building visit first, academic-focused visit later
