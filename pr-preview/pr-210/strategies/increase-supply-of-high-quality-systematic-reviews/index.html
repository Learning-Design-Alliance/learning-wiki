Increase the supply of high quality systematic reviews so decision makers have more useful evidence summaries¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article's stated aim is that its recommendations "increase the number of such reviews in order to provide decision makers with a greater number of useful evidence summaries that can inform policy and program decisions." The strategy treats the production of high quality systematic reviews as a supply problem: more well-conducted reviews means more evidence summaries available to support policy and program decisions in education and human services.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Recommendations for conducting high quality systematic reviews must be followed by review producers

Constraints¶

  • The supplied text does not print the specific recommendations, only the goal of increasing the number of reviews

Target Learners¶

  • Policy and program decision makers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Accessing useful evidence summaries to inform policy and program decisions

Related Strategies¶

  • Support effective data use by offering targeted recommendations and strategies to state officials, district-level staff, and system developers
  • Use newsflashes—succinct email blasts announcing new research findings—to inform decision makers, stakeholders, and practitioners
