Layered professional development: summer institute plus weekly web conferencing follow-up¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

TRI professional development combines an initial face-to-face summer institute with ongoing remote support: "weekly web conferencing from a university-based consultant to follow up training, discuss individual children, and resolve problems." Additional components include an end-of-year advanced training day and a website with training videos, instructions, and manuals.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • University-based consultants (literacy coaches) with a degree in education and teaching experience.

Constraints¶

  • Initial training requires a 1-2 day offsite or Chapel Hill location session.

Target Learners¶

  • classroom teachers in kindergarten and first grade

Target Learning Goals¶

  • tutoring skills
  • general reading instruction

Related Strategies¶

  • Weekly webcam coaching meetings with observation and feedback sustain teacher implementation
  • Two-tier professional development: intensive teacher training with weekly coach follow-up, plus a five-day summer institute and weekly seminars for coaches
  • Brief initial training with extensive follow-up coaching and on-site facilitation for whole-school program implementation
  • Implement Reading Edge with two days of initial teacher training plus monthly in-class coaching and peer support structures
  • Combine summer professional development with school-year on-site coaching and provision of complete instructional materials
  • Provide comprehensive professional development and ongoing coaching when implementing a structured literacy program
  • Layered implementation support for teachers adopting a new science curriculum: preservice training, follow-up training, and a science coach
  • Support curriculum implementation with initial multi-day training plus ongoing onsite consultation during the school year
