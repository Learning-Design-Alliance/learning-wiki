Provide guidance to policy makers and evaluators on accounting for disengagement in their own work¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The authors state they "provide guidance for policy makers and evaluators on how to account for disengagement in their own work". The guidance directs analysts using achievement test data to consider disengagement corrections, weighing that sample removal will likely induce bias while overall corrections had minimal impact on the examined metrics.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • The article notes the "promise and limitations of using achievement test metadata for related purposes"

Target Learning Goals¶
