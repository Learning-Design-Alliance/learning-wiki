Train teachers to implement peer-tutoring programs through an all-day modeling and role-playing workshop plus a program manual¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

PALS prepares teachers through a one-day training event followed by written support materials. The report states that "Peer-Assisted Learning Strategies offers teacher training in an all-day workshop at which teachers learn to implement the program through modeling and role playing. Teachers also are provided with a manual describing the program." In the qualifying study, project staff also attended seven 45-minute lessons to provide help as necessary, and these sessions were not counted as part of the 15-week treatment.
