Scaffold self-evaluation against rubrics, forms, and checklists¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The reflect step leans on self-evaluation protocols so students internalize feedback. The guide advises educators to "Invite students to independently evaluate their learning artifacts against rubrics, or use self-evaluation forms and checklists to track and evaluate their progress." Scaffolding helps students analyze their own learning process rather than relying solely on teacher judgment.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Rubrics, self-evaluation forms, or checklists that students can apply independently

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Self-evaluation of learning artifacts
  • Internalizing feedback

Related Strategies¶

  • Collect three kinds of artifacts: learning stories, wins, and lessons learned
  • Identify 2-3 intentional pause points for collecting and reflecting on artifacts
  • Vary reflection timing between in-the-moment and future-focused reflections
