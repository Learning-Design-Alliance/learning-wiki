Have classroom teachers develop CT-integrated example lessons and practical integration routines to operationalize a competency map¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

To operationalize the competency map for teachers, IPSD had a team of six teachers add "classroom-relevant examples of CT-integrated lessons aligned to the IPSD standards and curricula" and, with district leaders, produce a one-page summary with integration activity ideas and example routines such as mind mapping. This makes the map usable at the elementary level.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Time for a teacher team to work during the school year in collaboration with district leaders

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Elementary teachers and students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Integrating computational thinking competencies into disciplinary lessons

Related Strategies¶

  • Leverage school-based librarians to create and deliver K-6 CS/CT lessons when classroom integration slows
  • Show teachers concrete examples of computational thinking in practice before asking them to design CT lessons
