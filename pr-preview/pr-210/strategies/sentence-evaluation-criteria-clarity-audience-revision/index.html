Provide sentence evaluation criteria (clarity, intended audience) and model revision against them¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The guide recommends giving students criteria to assess sentences based on meaning, style, and grammar, with an example checklist asking "Does this make sense?", "Is it easy to read?", and "Is it appropriate for the audience?" Teachers demonstrate how to revise a sentence that fails the criteria by identifying missing parts, incorrect punctuation, wordiness, or words too simple or complex for the audience, then review student work using the criteria; older students use the criteria to review one another's work. Adapted from Neman (1995).

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A printed list of evaluation criteria
  • Teacher demonstration of revision against the criteria before student use

Constraints¶

  • Peer review with the criteria is suggested for older students

Target Learners¶

  • elementary school students
  • older elementary students for peer review

Target Learning Goals¶

  • evaluating and revising sentences for meaning, style, and grammar
