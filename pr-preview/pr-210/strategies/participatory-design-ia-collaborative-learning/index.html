Engage stakeholders in participatory design and research for AI-augmented collaborative learning¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

In closing, the presentation poses guiding questions for advancing intelligence augmentation for collaborative learning: whether it is a compelling grand challenge, what is known that can be brought to bear to accelerate advances, and "Who needs to be engaged in participatory design and research?" It treats participatory design and research engagement as a necessary part of pursuing the agenda, alongside the paramount issues of ethics and equity it flags.
