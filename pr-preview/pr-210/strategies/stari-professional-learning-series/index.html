Online asynchronous professional learning series completed in teams across the school year¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

SERP offers an online professional learning series in which teachers and coaches explore STARI practices and skills and learn to implement the program. The series is entirely online and asynchronous, but educators are encouraged to engage in collaborative teacher teams where possible. "It is estimated that the STARI Professional Learning Series will take approximately 20 hours, with about 8 hours intended for before implementation (Sessions 1-6) and the rest spread across the year (1-2 hours/month)." Sessions include videos, readings, and hands-on activities, and a certificate is provided on completion.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Approximately 20 hours total, about 8 hours before implementation and 1-2 hours/month across the year; individual teacher license starts at $375 with volume discounts

Constraints¶

  • Entirely online and asynchronous; certificate usable only in districts that provide professional development credits

Target Learners¶

  • middle school teachers implementing STARI
  • literacy coaches

Target Learning Goals¶

  • effective implementation of STARI practices and skills

Related Strategies¶

  • Layered implementation support for teachers adopting a new science curriculum: preservice training, follow-up training, and a science coach
  • Sustain tutor quality through layered professional development: live and asynchronous learning, observations, coaching, and weekly office hours
  • Provide structured multi-phase professional development with ongoing support for intervention teachers
  • One-day teacher workshop plus optional training-of-trainers for implementing CSC
  • Multi-format teacher professional development for implementing the Facing History and Ourselves curriculum
  • Provide implementation professional development and support approximately every three weeks
