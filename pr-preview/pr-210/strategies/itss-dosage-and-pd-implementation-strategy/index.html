Implement ITSS twice weekly for about 30 minutes per session over 7 months to 1 year, with teacher professional development and coaching¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report specifies an implementable dosage and support package for schools adopting ITSS: "Developers recommend students use ITSS twice a week for about 30 minutes per session for 7 months to 1 year." Implementation also requires teachers to attend a 2-day professional development session, administrators a 1-day session, plus an additional 4 to 6 days of in-school coaching; the web-based program itself is free to access, with training costs typically covered by districts.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • School staff must complete the required professional development sessions and in-school coaching; computer labs might be necessary for classrooms without personal computers.

Constraints¶

  • In both contributing studies, actual usage was 30 to 45 minutes a week over 6 to 7 months, which was lower than the developer-recommended dosage.

Target Learners¶

  • Students in grades K-8 in schools adopting ITSS as a supplemental English language arts program

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Improved comprehension of factual text

Related Strategies¶

  • Provide sustained developer support (training, coaching, progress reports, and rapid technical help) when implementing ITSS
