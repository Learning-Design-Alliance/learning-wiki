Validate micro-credentials through construct validity review plus interrater reliability checks on the first 50 submissions¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The Friday Institute validates each micro-credential in two phases: construct validity, gathering educator and expert feedback on questions and rubrics before release, then piloting the first 50 submissions, each receiving two or more independent evaluations by outside educators. A third evaluator checks interrater reliability, and "The Friday Institute team validates a rubric only if it has at least 80 percent agreement between the two reviewers"; otherwise rubrics are reviewed and clarified.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Each submission must receive two or more independent evaluations from outside educators with in-depth understanding of the content

Constraints¶

  • Validation extends only through the first 50 submissions of any new micro-credential

Target Learners¶

  • issuing organizations and rubric developers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • ensuring assessment questions and rubrics are clear, construct-aligned, and reliably scored
