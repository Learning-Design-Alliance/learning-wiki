Adapt existing footage for branching simulation practice rather than only annotating it¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends using simulation templates to adapt existing footage so students get different kinds of practice beyond annotation. In the Kontakte demonstration for the Defense Language Institute, segments were programmed to play sections of the disc, ask students questions about what they heard and understood, and, based on their response, branch back to pieces of the segment and replay them, while providing the German script, English translations, and a glossary. The authors argue this approach "has a great deal of potential and is comparatively inexpensive as no production costs are incurred" beyond the license fee to transfer and use the existing footage.
