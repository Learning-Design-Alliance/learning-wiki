Weekly teacher team meetings to review each student's strengths, progress, and obstacles¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A core BARR strategy is regular teacher team meetings: "teacher teams meeting regularly to discuss each student's strengths and progress and identify obstacles to learning." Teams of teachers "need to meet weekly using the BARR process," making student-level review of both strengths and barriers a routine, scheduled part of school practice.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A designated BARR coordinator to manage, coordinate, and integrate the model.
  • Teams of teachers meeting weekly using the BARR process.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • K–12 students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • identifying and addressing obstacles to learning
  • monitoring student progress
