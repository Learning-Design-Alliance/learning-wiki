Use external consulting and technical assistance to help colleges adapt programs to time-variant CBE models¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief describes Western Governors University as "providing consulting and technical assistance to help the consortium colleges adapt their programs to time-variant, competency-based models." This pairs an experienced CBE provider with colleges converting fixed-time programs, alongside an external evaluator of the grant.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A provider with CBE experience (WGU) available to supply consulting and technical assistance

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Community college students in information technology programs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Adapting institutional programs to competency-based delivery

Related Strategies¶

  • Competency Based Learning
  • Competency Based Progression
  • Engage industry partners to inform curriculum development, including the competencies needed, and develop new programs in response to employer input
