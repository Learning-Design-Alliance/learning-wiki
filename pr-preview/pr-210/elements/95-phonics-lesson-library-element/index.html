95 Phonics Lesson Library (PLL)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The 95 Phonics Lesson Library is a comprehensive phonics program for Tier 2, small-group instruction with ready-to-use lesson plans and all relevant teacher and student materials. It breaks phonics mastery into 75 distinct subskills separated into 3 levels: Basic, Advanced, and Multisyllabic, so educators can "target specific skill gaps with precision." Two matched quasi-experimental studies support its effectiveness on Acadience Reading.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Best used in small group settings with 1 teacher to 4-5 students
  • Complete 95 Phonics Lesson Library, virtual product training, customer success visits and coaching support

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Grade 1 students Below or Well Below Benchmark on early literacy screening
  • Grade 4-5 students receiving Tier 2 and Tier 3 intervention

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Phonics mastery across 75 subskills
  • Reading growth measured on Acadience Reading, NWEA MAP Reading, and Ohio State Test

Claims¶

  • Grade 1 students in California schools using 95 Phonics Lesson Library showed significantly higher Acadience Reading composite scores than comparison schools (ES +0.23) [+W]
  • In a full-year subset analysis, 95 PLL students showed significant reading gains relative to comparison on Acadience (+0.19), MAP (+0.30), and the Ohio State Test (+0.54, fifth grade) [+W]
  • Fourth and fifth grade students using 95 PLL as Tier 2 and Tier 3 intervention demonstrated significantly greater mid-year growth on Acadience Reading than comparison students (ES +0.18) [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • 95 Phonics Screener for Intervention (progress-monitoring screener)

Examples¶

  • Deliver PLL as small-group Tier 2 instruction with one teacher per 4-5 students
