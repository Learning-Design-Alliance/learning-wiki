A district guide for embedding RCTs into planned policies and programs¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

This guide, published by the U.S. Department of Education's Institute of Education Sciences, is written for "district and school leaders" and "shows how to recognize opportunities to embed randomized controlled trials (RCTs) into planned policies or programs." It also "outlines the key steps to conduct RCTs and responds to common questions and concerns about RCTs." It is a practical reference document rather than an empirical study.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Intended for use by district and school leaders considering whether and how to run an RCT of a planned policy or program

Target Learning Goals¶

  • recognizing opportunities to embed RCTs into planned policies or programs
  • conducting RCTs to generate evidence for education decisions

Claims¶

  • Opportunistic RCTs can generate strong evidence for education decisions with minimal added cost and disruption [+W]
