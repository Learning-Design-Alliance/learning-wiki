UChicago Consortium 2001 conference and edited volume on Chicago high school reform efforts¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

An edited volume produced from the conference "Research on High School Reform Efforts in Chicago," held at the University of Chicago's Gleacher Center in March 2001. The UChicago Consortium organized it "to establish a viable research agenda and prepare for a program of research on Chicago public high schools." It brought together scholars and educators who were, for the most part, "studying Chicago's public high schools, including the Design's efforts to improve them." Compilation was supported by The Chicago Community Trust.
