Fluency Formula™ supplemental reading fluency curriculum for grades 1–6¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (1 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Fluency Formula™ is a supplemental curriculum "designed to promote reading fluency for first- through sixth-grade students," emphasizing "automatic recognition of words, decoding accuracy, and oral expressiveness." It delivers a daily 10- to 15-minute classroom lesson using workbooks, read-aloud anthologies, library books, fluency activity cards, and audio CDs, and includes a Fluency Formula™ Assessment System with one-minute grade-level passages and a timer. Distributed by Scholastic; a grade-level kit costs $625.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Delivered as a supplement to the schools' standard reading/English language arts curriculum
  • Teacher professional development via the Professional Guide and Scholastic Red online courses

Constraints¶

  • The WWC considers the extent of evidence to be small for fluency and comprehension, based on a single study

Target Learners¶

  • first- through sixth-grade students
  • second-grade students in the evaluated study

Target Learning Goals¶

  • reading fluency
  • decoding accuracy
  • oral expressiveness
  • phonics, vocabulary, and comprehension

Claims¶

  • The evidence base for Fluency Formula™ consists of a single small randomized controlled trial, with no studies addressing alphabetics or general reading achievement [~W]
  • Fluency effects of Fluency Formula™ differ by initial ability: positive for low-ability students (effect size 0.66) and negative for high-ability students (effect size –0.21), both non-significant [~W]
  • Fluency Formula™ shows a negative but statistically non-significant effect on second-grade reading comprehension (effect size –0.28, improvement index –11) [-W]
  • Fluency Formula™ shows a positive but statistically non-significant effect on second-grade oral reading fluency (effect size 0.26, improvement index +10) [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • Read Naturally® supplemental fluency program: modeled reading, repeated timed reading, and progress monitoring

Examples¶

  • Use one-minute timed grade-level passages to monitor oral reading fluency progress
