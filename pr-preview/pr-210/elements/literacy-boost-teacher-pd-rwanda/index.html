Literacy Boost teacher professional development program (Save the Children, rural Rwanda)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Literacy Boost is Save the Children's early literacy intervention, whose teacher professional development component was evaluated in rural Rwanda as part of a randomized controlled trial. The article states the study "investigates the impact of a teacher professional development (PD) program in rural Rwanda, part of a randomized controlled trial of Save the Children's early literacy intervention, 'Literacy Boost.'" The PD targeted teachers' pedagogical content knowledge, classroom practices, and classroom print environments in early literacy. It was evaluated at the sector level, with some sectors assigned to receive PD and others serving as controls.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Delivered as part of the Literacy Boost early literacy intervention, with sector-level assignment allowing comparison of PD and control sectors

Constraints¶

  • Evaluated in rural Rwanda, in the context of early literacy instruction in a least developed country

Target Learners¶

  • Early-grade students and their teachers in rural Rwanda

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Early literacy reading skills

Claims¶

  • Classroom observations suggested increases in desired pedagogical practices after PD, but differences were not statistically significant [+W]
  • Teacher PD in rural Rwanda raised teachers' early literacy pedagogical content knowledge relative to control sectors [+W]
  • The PD program had a large and significant impact on classroom print environments in rural Rwanda [+W]
  • PD-assigned teachers reported using significantly more research-based literacy pedagogical practices than control teachers [+W]
