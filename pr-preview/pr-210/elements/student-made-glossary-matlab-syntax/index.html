Student-made glossary assignment for MATLAB syntax¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A homework intervention in which students complete a five-column glossary form: "syntax, overview of the syntax, student developed test case(s) based on instructor guidelines, hand computations to predict results of test cases, and MATLAB results". Column complexity follows a hierarchical analysis of intellectual skills (discrimination, concepts, rules), letting students build a schema and gain a sense of accomplishment on smaller parts of the learning task before solving application problems.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Students complete the glossary before working on application problems, with items tied to immediate assignments

Constraints¶

  • Implemented only in the last quarter of the semester, late relative to the pre-motivational survey

Target Learners¶

  • first-year engineering students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • learning and transferring MATLAB syntax to engineering problem-solving

Claims¶

  • Implementing a student-made glossary significantly lowered first-year engineering students' satisfaction with the M-Tutor tutorial [-W]
  • Students' reactions to the glossary were significantly and positively related to their post-satisfaction level [+W]
  • Pre-intervention, students gave the least positive response to the Satisfaction component of the ARCS model [+W]
  • The authors caution that the glossary likely was not the cause of the observed motivational loss due to confounds and a long temporal gap [~W]
