Classworks Individualized Learning: assessment-driven online intervention units¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Classworks Individualized Learning is a component of Classworks tiered intervention support composed of online units matched to student learning needs. As the summary states, "An Individualized Learning Path (ILP) is generated from student assessment data and instructional units include direct instruction, activities to apply learning, and a short formative test focused on strengthening a specific skill." Units use media-rich activities with voice, text, video, graphics, photographs, and animation, and are typically implemented by classroom teachers, interventionists, and special educators with no additional staffing.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers actively monitor student progress and reassign assignments as needed to support mastery

Constraints¶

  • Designed for students performing below the 25th percentile in math in the studied context

Target Learners¶

  • Elementary students needing tiered math intervention

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Math skill mastery through individualized practice

Claims¶

  • Classworks students used the program an average of 11 minutes per week over 30 weeks [~W]
  • Classworks Individualized Learning users scored six points higher on spring math assessment than non-users [+W]
