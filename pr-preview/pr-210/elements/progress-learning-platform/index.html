Progress Learning: K-12 standards-aligned personalized learning platform¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Progress Learning is a comprehensive, state standards-aligned instructional resource for Grades K-12 in ELA, Math, Science, and Social Studies. It gives each student a learning path with standards aligned to individual needs, is accessible on any Wi-Fi-enabled device, and lets teachers create and share assessments and assignments and track progress. Resources include videos, bellringers, and printables, with integrations with Clever, Classlink, Canvas, and Google Classroom.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Classroom teachers are the primary users; school and district administrator access is included with subscriptions
  • Supported browsers: Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, Apple Safari

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students across multiple subjects

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Standards-aligned achievement in ELA, math, science, and social studies

Claims¶

  • Progress Learning shows a statistically significant positive effect on Grade 5 NWEA MAP science scores [+W]
  • Progress Learning qualifies for a Promising evidence rating based on one quasi-experimental study [+W]
  • Progress Learning shows a directionally positive effect on STAAR science scores [+W]
  • Subgroup analyses show strongest Progress Learning effects for SPED, Hispanic, and Black students on MAP science [+W]
