Assessment education edited volume (Tarasawa, Gotwals, & Jackson, 2020)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

An edited book published by Rowman & Littlefield in September 2020, edited by Beth Tarasawa, Amelia Gotwals, and Cara Jackson. Per its description, it "focuses on practical uses of assessment in education" and aims to shift focus "away from the rhetoric of punitive testing" toward quality assessments supporting student learning. It serves as a resource collection for educators pursuing equity-oriented, data-informed instruction.
