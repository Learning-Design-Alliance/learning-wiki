KIPP (Knowledge Is Power Program) charter middle school network¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), q3 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Knowledge Is Power Program (KIPP) is a network of charter schools whose middle schools were evaluated for achievement impacts in this study. The article describes the evaluation as covering "41 Knowledge Is Power Program (KIPP) charter middle schools nationwide", making KIPP the instructional setting whose effects on reading, math, science, and social studies scores were measured. The page also lists an associated project, "KIPP: Preparing Youth for College", with a 2007-2017 time frame.
