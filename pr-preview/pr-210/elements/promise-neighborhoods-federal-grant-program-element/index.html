Promise Neighborhoods federal grant program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 3 studies (3 qualitative), q1–q2 · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Promise Neighborhoods is a U.S. Department of Education grant program funding place-based community supports for children in poverty. Since 2010, ED awarded three rounds of grants totaling nearly $100 million to nonprofit organizations, institutions of higher education, and American Indian tribes. The article reports this included "46 planning grants and 12 implementation grants to 48 lead agencies in 23 states and the District of Columbia."
