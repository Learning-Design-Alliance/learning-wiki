Phonological Awareness Lessons: explicit, systematic phonological awareness instruction for Pre-K through 1st grade¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), q2–q4 · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Phonological Awareness Lessons is the instructional program evaluated in the two studies. The article describes it as offering "explicit, systematic instruction for Pre-K - 1st grade students in a format that is accessible to children and easy to implement for their teachers." It is positioned as early intervention for struggling readers who have not mastered phonological awareness, a foundational skill for other reading skills. Implementation includes progress monitoring with the Phonological Awareness Screener for Intervention every three weeks.

Claims¶

  • Across two studies of PA Lessons, the average effect size on Acadience Reading composite scores is +0.49, with both studies rated Moderate [+M]
  • Kindergarten students in schools using PA Lessons showed significantly increased Acadience Reading composite scores compared to comparison schools (effect size +0.21) [+W]
  • A second study replicated the benefit of PA Lessons on Acadience Reading composite scores with a larger effect size (+0.74) [+W]
  • Phonemic Awareness Training Improves Reading [+M]
