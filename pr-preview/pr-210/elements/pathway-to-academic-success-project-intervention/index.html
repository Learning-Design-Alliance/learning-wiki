Pathway to Academic Success Project intervention (teacher professional development, coaching, and instructional materials)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Pathway to Academic Success Project is a professional development and coaching intervention that "trains teachers to improve the reading and writing abilities of ELs who have an intermediate level of English proficiency by incorporating cognitive strategies into reading and writing instruction." It targets grades 6-12 general education ELA teachers of English learners and has three key components: teacher professional development, coaching from experienced Pathway teachers, and instructional materials and supplies such as wall posters, class sets of readings, sentence starters, and model lessons based on culturally relevant texts.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers receive 46 hours of professional development per year over 2 years, including five or six full-day meetings and five after-school meetings each school year, plus coaching and instructional materials

Constraints¶

  • Designed for English learners with an intermediate level of English proficiency in general education classrooms; most teachers report spending at least 60 hours of class time per year on the strategies

Target Learners¶

  • English learners in grades 6-12 general education classrooms with intermediate English proficiency

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Reading and writing abilities, including analytical writing and use of cognitive strategies

Claims¶

  • Implementation differences between intervention and comparison classrooms emerged by the end of the intervention period [+W]
  • The Pathway to Academic Success Project has potentially positive effects on literacy achievement for English learners in grades 6-12 [+W]
  • The Pathway to Academic Success Project has potentially positive effects on writing conventions for English learners in grades 6-12 [+W]
  • The Pathway to Academic Success Project has potentially positive effects on writing quality for English learners in grades 7-12 [+W]

Related Elements¶

Examples¶

  • Sustained teacher professional development with coaching and professional learning communities
