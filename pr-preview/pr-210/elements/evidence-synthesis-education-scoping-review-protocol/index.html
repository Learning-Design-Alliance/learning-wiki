Registered scoping review protocol on evidence synthesis education for pharmacy students¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A protocol for a scoping review registered in the Open Science Framework (registration DOI:10.17605/OSF.IO/ZQVGY), designed per PRISMA-P and to be reported per PRISMA-ScR. It will search PubMed, Scopus, Embase, CINAHL Ultimate and Web of Science from 2010, screen in Covidence with two independent reviewers and Cohen's Kappa, extract data on training programme, delivery method, duration and outcome assessment, and compile results with descriptive statistics and narrative synthesis.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Two reviewers screen and extract independently, with a third reviewer consulted to resolve disagreements; inter-rater agreement for pilot extraction uses Cohen's Kappa with a coefficient (k) value >0.70 considered strong

Constraints¶

  • The scoping review has not yet commenced; formal screening is anticipated to begin in June 2026
  • Included studies were not subjected to critical appraisal, given that the review's purpose is to map and explore available evidence rather than assess methodological quality
