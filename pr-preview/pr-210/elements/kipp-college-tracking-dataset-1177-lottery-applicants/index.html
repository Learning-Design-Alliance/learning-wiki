KIPP middle school college-outcome tracking dataset of 1,177 lottery applicants¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A tracking dataset following 1,177 students who applied through 5th or 6th grade admissions lotteries in 2008 or 2009 to enter 1 of 13 oversubscribed KIPP middle schools. The study "uses a randomized controlled trial design" contrasting students offered admission with those not offered, enabling long-run comparison of college enrollment and early college persistence. The page describes the dataset and design but prints no outcome results.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Students must have applied to an oversubscribed KIPP middle school through a 5th or 6th grade admissions lottery in 2008 or 2009, creating randomized treatment and control groups

Constraints¶

  • Findings are limited to students old enough to have attended college for at least two years at the time of tracking

Target Learners¶

  • middle school students applying to oversubscribed KIPP charter schools in grades 5 and 6

Target Learning Goals¶

  • college enrollment
  • early college persistence
