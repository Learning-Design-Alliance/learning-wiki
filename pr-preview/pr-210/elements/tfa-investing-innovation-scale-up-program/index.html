Teach For America Investing in Innovation scale-up program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Teach For America (TFA) Investing in Innovation scale-up was a program that recruited and trained new corps members placed as teachers in high-poverty schools. The report evaluates first- and second-year corps members hired during the first two years of the scale-up period, comparing their students' reading and math achievement with that of students of other teachers "in the same high-poverty schools." The evaluation covered reading and math outcomes across elementary grade bands.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Corps members are recruited and trained through the TFA program before placement as teachers in high-poverty schools.

Constraints¶

  • Findings apply to corps members hired in the first two years of the scale-up period in high-poverty schools.

Target Learners¶

  • Students in high-poverty schools, prekindergarten through grade 5

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Reading achievement
  • Math achievement

Claims¶

  • TFA teachers in grades 1 and 2 had a positive effect on math achievement of 0.16 standard deviations that was almost statistically significant (p-value = 0.054) [+W]
  • TFA corps members hired in the first two years of the i3 scale-up were as effective as other teachers in the same high-poverty schools in teaching reading and math [+M]
  • TFA teachers in lower elementary grades (prekindergarten through grade 2) had a positive, statistically significant effect on reading achievement of 0.12 standard deviations [+W]
  • No statistically significant TFA impacts were found for upper elementary grades (3 through 5) in reading or math or for other examined subgroups [~M]
