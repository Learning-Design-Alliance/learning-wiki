Digital Promise Product Certifications Pilot and Learning Sciences Research-Based Design certification¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), q1 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Product Certifications Pilot is a third-party market signal intended to help education leaders, educators, and product investors quickly determine whether an edtech product was designed and developed with learning sciences research. The report announces that in fall 2019 Digital Promise will "pilot our first product certification, Learning Sciences Research-Based Design," aimed at disrupting how edtech is chosen by certifying products grounded in learning sciences research.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires cross-sector collaboration with education leaders, edtech product partners, and partner nonprofits to establish trusted third-party verification

Constraints¶

  • The certification is described as a pilot launching in fall 2019; the report presents no evidence of its effects

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 districts and education leaders selecting edtech
  • the full spectrum of learners served by edtech products

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Evidence-based edtech selection and procurement
  • identification of products grounded in learning sciences research

Claims¶

  • Education stakeholders report that limited, hard-to-find, and rarely trustworthy information about edtech products creates significant barriers to selection [+W]
  • Stakeholders report that education leaders, educators, and investors lack common language or indicators to assess edtech quality, hindering evidence-based decisions [+W]
  • The report calls for reframing edtech certification applications to help developers identify equity gaps in the research they use [+W]
