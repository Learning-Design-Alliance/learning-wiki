Twig Science: phenomena-based K–8 science curriculum aligned to NGSS¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Twig Science is "a comprehensive, phenomena-based science curriculum for Grades K–8" that "follows the 5E instructional model" and aligns with the Next Generation Science Standards. It emphasizes student inquiry, integrates science and engineering practices, and includes multimedia-rich investigations, collaborative tasks, and built-in literacy supports. Districts may purchase print materials instead of relying on the digital platform.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Each implementing classroom needs one instructional teacher; no additional staffing is required.

Constraints¶

  • Access to the digital platform requires networked computers with mice or tablets, though print materials can be purchased instead.

Target Learners¶

  • Students in Grades K–8

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Science proficiency aligned to the Next Generation Science Standards
  • Science and engineering practices
  • Literacy supports within science instruction

Claims¶

  • Grades 6–8 students using Twig Science showed significantly greater gains on the Imagine Galileo science benchmark than matched non-using peers (effect size +0.15) [+M]

Related Elements¶

  • STEMscopes digital science curriculum
  • STEMscopes Math comprehensive core mathematics curriculum (K–Algebra 1)
  • TEEMSS inquiry-based science curriculum with handheld computers and probeware
  • Connect Science curriculum: 31 lessons integrating NGSS practices, social and collaborative skills, and service-learning

Examples¶

  • Use NGSS-aligned K-8 science coursework as the starting point for district CT integration
