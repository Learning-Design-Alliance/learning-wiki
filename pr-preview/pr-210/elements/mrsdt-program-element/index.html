Mandatory-random student drug testing (MRSDT) program for high schools¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Mandatory-random student drug testing (MRSDT) is a school-based program in which high school students are subject to random drug testing, evaluated here for its effectiveness in reducing substance use. The article describes it as "school-based mandatory-random student drug testing (MRSDT)" tested in "the largest experimental evaluation to date". The program operates at the school level, with MRSDT schools compared against comparable schools without the program.
