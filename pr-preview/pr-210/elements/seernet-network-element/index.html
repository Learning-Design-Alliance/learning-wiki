SEERNet network connecting platforms, researchers, and educators¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

SEERNet is an IES-funded initiative in which "IES funded Digital Promise to form a network of connections among people representing platforms, researchers, and educators." It builds on five IES-funded platforms opened to researchers (ASU L@S, Kinetic/OpenStax, Terracotta/Canvas, Upgrade/Mathia, eTrials/ASSISTments) and funds improvement research including exploratory studies, design experiments, and efficacy and replication studies. Its Office Hours initiative convenes a community to co-design research questions relevant to emerging technology tools.
