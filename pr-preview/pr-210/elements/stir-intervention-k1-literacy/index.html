STIR intervention: a set of resources, tools, trainings, and coaching sessions for K-1 literacy instruction¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The STIR intervention is a co-developed approach "consisting of a set of resources, tools, trainings, and coaching sessions" supporting educators in integrating evidence-based strategies into K-1 literacy instruction with a focus on decoding unfamiliar words. It targets three educator capacities: effective phonological awareness and phonics practices, decision-driven data use, and supportive leadership practices. REL Midwest develops, tests, and refines it iteratively across cohorts.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Co-development with participating districts and schools, plus coordination of partnership activities

Constraints¶

  • Scoped to kindergarten and grade 1 reading, focused on students' ability to decode unfamiliar words

Target Learners¶

  • Kindergarten and grade 1 students in Michigan partner districts

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Decoding unfamiliar words
  • Phonological awareness and phonics knowledge

Claims¶

  • The STIR partnership anticipates that schools will report more students at or above early literacy benchmarks relative to prior years [+W]

Related Elements¶

Examples¶

  • Use a diagnostic and implementation monitoring tool to give educators formative feedback on their instructional practices
  • Disseminate partnership information through blog posts, videos, and infographics to support acceptability and feasibility
