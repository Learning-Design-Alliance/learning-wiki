National Student Clearinghouse StudentTracker for High Schools¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A service that provides states, districts, and schools with postsecondary outcomes data on former K-12 students. The brief states that services such as StudentTracker "provide states, districts, and schools with the capability to examine college enrollment, persistence, and completion data on students they previously served in K–12 settings." It is the data source the brief builds its guidance around, enabling K-12 agencies to track the college outcomes of their former students.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Access to a postsecondary outcomes service such as StudentTracker that reports college enrollment, persistence, and completion for former students

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 state, district, and school officials

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Using postsecondary outcomes data to inform K-12 policy and practice

Claims¶

  • K-12 officials increasingly attend to postsecondary outcomes but many are unsure how best to use the data [+W]
  • Raw postsecondary data are useful for descriptive purposes but may not give educators substantial new information beyond high school indicators [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • National Student Clearinghouse (NSC) data as a source for two-year college outcomes
