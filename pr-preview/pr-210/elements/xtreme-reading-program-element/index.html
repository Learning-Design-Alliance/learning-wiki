Xtreme Reading supplemental reading program for struggling adolescent readers¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (2 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Xtreme Reading is a supplemental program derived from the University of Kansas Strategic Instruction Model, designed to teach struggling adolescent readers step-by-step strategies including "word identification, vocabulary, self-questioning, visual imagery, paraphrasing, and inference". It is recommended for students at the 4th grade level or higher scoring 2 grades or more below grade level, and teachers are trained in highly structured methods with structured materials.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers willing and able to implement the instructional steps provided by the curriculum
  • A proficient professional developer to guide implementation and coach the instructor/teacher

Constraints¶

  • Recommended only for students at the 4th grade level or higher, scoring 2 grades or more below grade level

Target Learners¶

  • Struggling adolescent readers in middle and high school

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Reading strategies including word identification, vocabulary, self-questioning, visual imagery, paraphrasing, and inference

Claims¶

  • Five studies yield a modest average effect size of +0.09 for Xtreme Reading on reading measures, qualifying it for the Strong category [+W]
  • A large national study and a smaller western Massachusetts study found no differences for Xtreme Reading on grade-level and state-test outcomes [-W]
  • A Portland, OR study found positive Xtreme Reading outcomes compared to control schools in both middle and high schools (effect size = +0.15) [+W]
  • Teachers reported overall satisfaction with Xtreme Reading, but buy-in was more positive among middle school than high school teachers [~W]
  • Variations of Xtreme Reading showed mixed results: Kentucky's Learning Strategies Curriculum was significantly positive (ES = +0.10) while Michigan's Fusion Reading was non-significant (ES = +0.07) [~W]

Related Elements¶

  • Strategic Instruction Model (SIM) and its Learning Strategies Curriculum (Xtreme Reading, Fusion Reading)
  • Strategic Adolescent Reading Intervention (STARI)

Examples¶

  • Deliver Xtreme Reading professional development through a 5-day site training followed by monthly implementation coaching
