Early Science with Nico and Nor preschool science program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Early Science with Nico and Nor is a preschool science program "co-designed by teachers, families, curricula and media developers, and researchers." It "promotes early science learning through playful learning experiences inspired by children’s and families’ interests and every day experiences," links science with engineering and math, includes digital apps to support STEM learning, and includes a Family and Teacher guide that connect home and school learning. A Spanish version, Ciencia Preescolar con Nico and Nor, is also provided.

Target Learners¶

  • preschool children and their families

Target Learning Goals¶

  • early science learning
  • engineering and math learning connected to science

Claims¶

  • Children who engaged in the program at both home and school made significantly more improvement in science learning than children who implemented at school only [+M]
  • Parents used the digital Family Guide flexibly, modifying activities to fit their routines or creating new activities based on family interests [+W]
  • Parents reported that the home activities were fun, promoted science learning, and meaningfully connected to everyday experiences [+W]
  • Children in classrooms implementing the Early Science with Nico and Nor program made significantly more improvement in science learning than peers in comparison classrooms. [+M]
  • In pilot testing, teachers successfully integrated science instruction and children deepened their understanding of science core ideas while engaging with science practices and connecting to math and engineering. [+M]
