Planetary Health and Climate Resilient Health Systems course¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

An interdisciplinary and international 6-week course at the intersection of global and planetary health, climate change, and health systems transformation, built by the EWUU alliance (TU/e, WUR, UU, UMCU) with St. Luke's Medical Center in the Philippines. "The course built on the five foun - dational domains of the planetary health education framework." It integrated CBL, CEL, and COIL, and "The 6-week course took place between February and March, the first edition in 2023 and the second in 2024."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Seed grant funding from the EWUU alliance and a Virtual International Collaboration grant from the Dutch Ministry of Education, Welfare and Sports

Constraints¶

  • Course delivery reimbursement was available only for students from the hosting faculty of the UU

Target Learners¶

  • students from four Dutch alliance institutions and St. Luke's Medical Center, Philippines

Target Learning Goals¶

  • transformative competencies to tackle planetary health challenges

Claims¶

  • In the course's COIL component, the intercultural environment did not create significantly larger problems than those typically observed in standard group work [+W]
  • Developing and delivering an integrated CBL/CEL/COIL planetary health course yielded three key lessons on flexibility, structural reform, and equitable partnerships [+W]
