Cognitive Tutor® secondary mathematics curriculum (Carnegie Learning)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (1 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Cognitive Tutor® is a secondary mathematics curriculum covering Algebra I, Algebra II, Geometry, and Integrated Math I–III, developed by Carnegie Learning. It "focuses on how students think about and learn mathematics," with teachers facilitating as students acquire and apply new information and discuss their work. Problems "emphasize connections between verbal, numeric, graphic, and algebraic representations," and materials include textbooks, adaptive software, teacher implementation guides, assessments, and professional development resources.

Claims¶

  • Cognitive Tutor® Algebra I has mixed effects on algebra outcomes for secondary students, with an average improvement index of +4 percentile points across five studies [+W]
  • Cognitive Tutor® Algebra I shows no discernible effects on general mathematics achievement for secondary students [~W]
  • Cognitive Tutor® Geometry shows potentially negative effects on geometry outcomes, with an improvement index of –8 percentile points [-W]
  • No studies of Cognitive Tutor® Algebra II or Integrated Math I–III meet WWC standards, so the report makes no effectiveness findings for those courses [~W]
