MAP Growth grades 1–2 achievement dataset (spring 2019 and spring 2022 samples)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief analyzes NWEA MAP Growth test-score data comparing a pre-COVID sample (spring 2019) with a COVID sample (spring 2022) in grades 1–2. As printed: "we used test-score data from 1.6 million students in grades 1–2 who took MAP® Growth™ assessments in reading and math in approximately 11,000 public schools in 2021–22". Tables 1–3 report sample demographics, school characteristics, and RIT means, standard deviations, median percentiles, and standardized differences by grade, subject, race/ethnicity, school poverty, and urbanicity.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Schools administering MAP Growth assessments in first or second grade in the comparison terms

Constraints¶

  • Students in kindergarten in 2021–22 were excluded because the composition of tested kindergarten students differed substantially from prepandemic samples

Target Learners¶

  • First- and second-grade public school students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Reading achievement
  • Mathematics achievement
