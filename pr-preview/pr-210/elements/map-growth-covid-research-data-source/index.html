NWEA MAP Growth assessment data as the measurement basis for COVID-19 learning-loss research¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The page situates the technical appendix within NWEA's portfolio of research using MAP Growth assessment data, including the "MAP Growth National Dashboard" cited as the data source for related post-COVID trend reports. MAP Growth serves as the achievement measure underpinning the brief's comparisons of 2020-21 gains and spring 2021 achievement to pre-pandemic trends and levels.
