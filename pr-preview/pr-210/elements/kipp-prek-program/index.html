KIPP Pre-K program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

KIPP Pre-K is a pre-kindergarten program offered by the KIPP charter school network, evaluated as an offer of admission in a study conducted as part of the associated project "KIPP: Preparing Youth for College" over the time frame 2007-2017. The study examined whether "any impacts persist as students advance beyond kindergarten," comparing students who won an offer of admission to KIPP Pre-K with peers who did not. The program is studied in combination with KIPP early elementary schooling.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Evaluated in combination with KIPP early elementary school for cumulative academic impacts

Constraints¶

  • Evidence of persistence beyond kindergarten is described by the report as preliminary

Target Learners¶

  • Pre-kindergarten children entering KIPP charter schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Reading achievement
  • Math achievement
  • Executive function

Claims¶

  • KIPP Pre-K may provide an additional benefit for reading achievement above and beyond KIPP elementary school [+M]
  • KIPP Pre-K combined with KIPP early elementary school has positive, statistically significant impacts on reading and math achievement after five years [+M]
  • KIPP Pre-K combined with KIPP early elementary school may also have a positive impact on students' executive function [+M]
  • The KIPP impact on reading skills persists over time, but impacts on reading comprehension largely dissipate by grade 2 [+M]
