Description¶

The article presents new data describing children's math and reading skills as they enter kindergarten, intended to characterize academic preparation at school entry. Its title names the two domains — "children’s math and reading skills as they enter kindergarten" — and the abstract frames the data as telling "a story of both declining achievement and shrinking gaps."
