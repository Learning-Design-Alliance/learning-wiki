MATHia tutoring software¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

MATHia is "the technology component of Carnegie Learning High School Math Solution", described as "a web technology that runs on desktop/laptop computers as well as Chromebooks and iPad, Android, and Windows tablets." It delivers the self-paced individualized instruction via automated tutoring software that the blended curriculum combines with textbook and workbook materials.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Access to desktop/laptop computers, Chromebooks, or iPad, Android, and Windows tablets.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • High school students
  • Eighth-grade students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Self-paced individualized algebra instruction

Claims¶

  • In a cluster randomized study across 73 high schools, Carnegie Learning students scored higher on the Algebra Proficiency Exam, with cohort effect sizes of +0.07 and +0.21 [+W]
  • Carnegie Learning High School Math Solution Algebra I shows a weighted average effect size of +0.04 across three qualifying studies [+W]
