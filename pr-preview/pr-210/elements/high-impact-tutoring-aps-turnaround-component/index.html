High Impact Tutoring as a component of the Atlanta Public Schools Turnaround Strategy¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

High Impact Tutoring is one of the components of the Atlanta Public Schools Turnaround Strategy evaluated by Mathematica Policy Research. The report page identifies it as one of "two Strategy components" receiving in-depth analysis in the first-year (2016-17) interim report, alongside the Purpose Built Schools partnership's operation of Thomasville Heights Elementary. The evaluation project ran from 2016 to 2019 and was prepared for Atlanta Public Schools. The page does not describe the tutoring model's design, dosage, or staffing in detail.
