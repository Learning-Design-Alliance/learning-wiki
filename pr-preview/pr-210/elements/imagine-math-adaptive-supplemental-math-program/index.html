Imagine Math: adaptive digital supplemental mathematics program with on-demand live certified teacher support¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Imagine Math by Imagine Learning is "a digital supplemental mathematics learning solution designed to support Pre-Kindergarten through Geometry students of all backgrounds." It provides "adaptive and developmentally appropriate instruction that focuses on building students' conceptual understanding of mathematics," supplemented by "scaffolded, rigorous instruction by live, certified teachers ... available on-demand."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires networked computers or tablets, headsets, and access to the Imagine Math instructional content.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Pre-Kindergarten through Geometry students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Conceptual understanding of mathematics

Claims¶

  • K–8 students using Imagine Math in a charter network scored significantly higher than matched non-users on NWEA MAP Growth Math (ES +0.08) [+W]
  • Imagine Math effects were statistically significant for Indiana grades 3–5 (ES +0.13) but not for other grade bands or for Texas students [~W]
  • Across two studies, the weighted average effect size of Imagine Math was +0.06 [+W]
