Reading Plus professional development and support system¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Reading Plus offers "a comprehensive system of training and support" for educators and administrators, including customized support packages for school or district needs, a resource website with on-demand video and webinar training, written resources, and additional online and offline teaching tools. Live support is available via chat, email, and phone. In the evaluation study, supervising teachers completed two hours of instructor-led training and used these resources as needed.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Educator access to the training resources and support channels
  • Approximately two hours of instructor-led training for supervising teachers, as used in the study
