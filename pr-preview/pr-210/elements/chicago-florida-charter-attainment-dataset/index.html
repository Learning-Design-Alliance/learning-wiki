Chicago and Florida administrative data for estimating charter high school attainment effects¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The study draws on data from Chicago and Florida to estimate the effects of attending a charter high school on high school completion and college attendance. The authors state that "we use data from Chicago and Florida to estimate the effects of attending a charter high school," and the analysis controls for "demographics, prior test scores, and the prior choice to enroll in a charter middle school." The dataset supports comparing charter high school attendees with traditional public high school students on attainment outcomes.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Student characteristics including demographics, prior test scores, and prior charter middle school enrollment must be measurable to apply the study's controls

Constraints¶

  • Findings are specific to charter high schools in Chicago and Florida as studied; the article does not claim generalizability beyond these locations

Target Learners¶

  • high school students in charter and traditional public high schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • high school completion
  • college attendance

Related Elements¶
