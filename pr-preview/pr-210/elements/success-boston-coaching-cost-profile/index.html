Cost profile of Success Boston Coaching: $5,762 per student during scale-up, decreasing to $3,037¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The report documents the major resources needed to implement Success Boston Coaching, listing cost ingredients covering personnel, facilities, equipment and materials, direct assistance, and other supports. In the 2015-16 academic year, when the intervention was scaled from serving about 300 students to 1,000 students per cohort annually, "the estimated total cost was $5,762 per student". Without scale-up administrative costs, the cost per student decreased to $3,037 in 2016-17. Funding came from The Boston Foundation, a Social Innovation Fund grant, and coaching partner organizations.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires coaches from nonprofit coaching organizations, program administrators, college liaisons, and a uAspire contract for financial aid training and text messaging; standard IT equipment and partner-provided facilities.

Constraints¶

  • The report states this preliminary list of costs is not designed to be exhaustive, and costs may vary depending on the size and type of organization delivering the coaching.

Target Learners¶

  • High school seniors transitioning to college who are traditionally underrepresented in college

Target Learning Goals¶

  • College persistence and completion

Claims¶

  • In the Linkow et al. (2019) scale-up QED, coaching showed no statistically significant effect on cumulative college GPA (effect size 0.01, p = 0.82) [~W]
  • In the Linkow et al. (2019) scale-up QED, coaching produced a statistically significant positive effect on second-year college enrollment [+W]
