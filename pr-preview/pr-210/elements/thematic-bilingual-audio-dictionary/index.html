Thematic Bilingual Audio Dictionary delivered via Telegram¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A set of short audio vocabulary lessons created by the first researcher and delivered to students' mobile phones through the Telegram app. Each lesson presents a new word first in Ukrainian (L1) and, after a short pause, in English (L2), with students instructed to repeat each word and phrase aloud; difficult words are additionally presented in phrases. For the studied unit, "7 audio lessons were created" covering topics such as materials and opinions about housing arrangements, each "about 2 to 8 minutes long" so students could listen and repeat multiple times during walks or commutes.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Students need a mobile device with the Telegram app to access the audio lessons
  • Students must pronounce the words and phrases aloud, not silently or in whisper, to activate auditory, verbal-logical, articulatory and muscular memory

Constraints¶

  • The lessons were organised in the sequence that new vocabulary was covered in class and were designed for listening during routine physical activity beyond the classroom

Target Learners¶

  • first-year university ESL students (A-2 CEFR proficiency)

Target Learning Goals¶

  • receptive and reproductive skills of L2 vocabulary translation and communication

Claims¶

  • Audio lessons training vocabulary from L1 to L2 yield better L1-to-L2 translation than traditional L2-to-L1 training [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • Thematic bilingual audio dictionary lessons delivered via Telegram for incidental mobile listening

Examples¶

  • Distribute audio vocabulary listening across a spaced repetition schedule during routine physical activity
