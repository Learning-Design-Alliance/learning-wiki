Early Warning Intervention and Monitoring System (EWIMS)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q4 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

EWIMS is "a systematic approach used by dedicated teams of school staff to identify students at risk of not graduating on time, assign students to interventions, and monitor their progress." Schools use three indicator domains — engagement (attendance), behavior (suspension), and course performance (grades and credits) — to identify the at-risk population and provide targeted support, with the aim of strengthening persistence and improving on-time graduation rates. Schools establish a team to lead and carry out the process.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Schools establish a team to lead and carry out the EWIMS process.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Grade 9 and 10 high school students at risk of not graduating on time

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Improved attendance and persistence
  • On-time graduation

Claims¶

  • EWIMS shows positive effects on course failures [+W]
  • EWIMS significantly reduces chronic absenteeism among grade 9 and 10 students after one year of implementation [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • ALAS dropout prevention program
  • Check & Connect: monitored mentoring dropout-prevention program

Examples¶

  • Implement EWIMS through combined in-person and online training with ongoing technical assistance liaisons
  • Use a systematic approach to identify dropout-prevention approaches for program developers while the scientific basis for new programs is still being built
  • Use an early warning system based on linked school and human services data to target resources and lessen risks before they become more serious
  • Build early-warning indicators and leverage online-learning-system process data to support students before gaps widen in Grade 3
  • Use school-level data systems to identify which students need which types of support for dropout prevention
  • Use longitudinal student-level data systems to diagnose dropout scope and identify at-risk students before key transitions
