Collaborative Strategic Reading (CSR) program for reading comprehension¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

CSR is a reading comprehension program in which students work in small cooperative learning groups while teachers focus on metacognitive comprehension strategies. The article describes it as teaching "strategies such as activating prior knowledge, identifying breakdowns in understanding, finding the main idea, and generating questions." It runs during regular English language arts or content area instruction.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Two days of initial training for teachers, followed by school-based booster sessions and in-school coaching

Constraints¶

  • No additional school staff are required to implement CSR
  • There are no technology requirements for CSR

Target Learners¶

  • school students in English language arts and content area classes
  • secondary students across multiple teachers' classes

Target Learning Goals¶

  • reading comprehension
  • metacognitive comprehension strategies

Claims¶

  • CSR shows small positive effects on GMRT and TOSREC with an average effect size of +0.04 across three evaluations [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • Free online CSR teacher support materials
  • Reciprocal Teaching

Examples¶

  • Schedule CSR within regular ELA and content-area instruction, spreading sessions across teachers in secondary settings
  • Collaborative Strategic Reading
