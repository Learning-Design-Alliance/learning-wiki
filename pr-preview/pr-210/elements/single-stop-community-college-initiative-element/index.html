Single Stop USA's Community College Initiative¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 5 studies (5 causal), q2 · 5 of 5 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Single Stop is a national nonprofit program that helps low-income community college students address nonacademic barriers to college by connecting them to public benefits and wraparound services. It uses "a web-based benefits screening program called Single Stop Technologies 3.0" through which site coordinators conduct benefits screening, application assistance, wraparound services such as free tax preparation and legal services, and case management. Since 2009 it has expanded to 30 colleges across the United States and served over 250,000 students; supports are designed to increase college persistence and completion.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Dedicated staff and site coordinators paid for by the community college, with experience in social work, counseling, or student services; an on-campus Single Stop office; and a Single Stop Technologies 3.0 software license costing $5,000 to $45,000 annually

Constraints¶

  • The intensity of supports depends on each student's needs; the WWC's findings apply to first-time community college students in five state systems and effects are not known for outcomes such as degree attainment, employment, or earnings

Target Learners¶

  • First-time community college students, including low-income students and their families

Target Learning Goals¶

  • College persistence and completion
  • Academic achievement

Claims¶

  • One-year persistence favored Single Stop students but college-level credits earned after two semesters showed only a marginal, statistically non-significant difference [+W]
  • Single Stop has potentially positive effects on academic achievement of first-time community college students [+W]
  • Single Stop has potentially positive effects on progressing in college among first-time community college students [+W]

Related Elements¶

Examples¶

  • Provide on-campus one-stop benefits screening, application assistance, and case management to reduce nonacademic barriers to persistence
