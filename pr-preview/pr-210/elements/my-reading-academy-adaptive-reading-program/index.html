My Reading Academy: adaptive game-based foundational reading curriculum for pre-K to grade 2¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

My Reading Academy is an adaptive game-based curriculum for pre-K through 2nd grade targeting phonemic awareness, phonics, vocabulary, fluency, and comprehension. It delivers "explicit and systematic phonemic awareness and phonics instruction, paired with rich reading and language experiences" and uses initial diagnostic assessments to place each child. Games use characters, songs, rhythms, movement, repetition, adaptive challenges, and just-in-time feedback to promote engagement and perseverance.

Claims¶

  • My Reading Academy kindergarteners showed significantly greater gains on the Alphabetic Knowledge strand (ES +0.32) [+W]
  • My Reading Academy impacts were greater for students starting with lower literacy skills and for those who used the program more [+W]
  • Kindergarten students using My Reading Academy outperformed comparison students on end-of-year state literacy assessments (ES +0.17) [+W]
  • Prekindergarten results for My Reading Academy were not positive, yielding an average effect size of +0.04 across grades [-W]
  • Teachers surveyed about My Reading Academy reported highly positive responses, perceived phonological-awareness gains, and found the program easy to use [+W]
