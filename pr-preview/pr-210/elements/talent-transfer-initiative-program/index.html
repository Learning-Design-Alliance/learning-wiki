Talent Transfer Initiative (TTI) transfer-incentive program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 9 claims (9 for) · 4 studies (4 causal), q2–q3 · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 9 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Talent Transfer Initiative is a transfer-incentive strategy piloted in seven school districts from 2007 to 2014, offering $20,000 per teacher to move high-performing teachers into hard-to-staff schools. The report describes it as "a transfer-incentive strategy, known as the Talent Transfer Initiative (TTI)," which is "intended to expand disadvantaged students' access to the most effective teachers." Districts used it to fill targeted vacancies with top-performing teachers.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Districts must identify targeted vacancies in hard-to-staff schools and offer a per-teacher payment ($20,000 in this pilot) to attract high-performing transfers.

Constraints¶

  • The pilot covered seven districts; test-score impacts appeared in elementary schools but not middle schools.

Target Learners¶

  • Students in hard-to-staff, disadvantaged schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Expanding disadvantaged students' access to the most effective teachers

Claims¶

  • Offering $20,000 per teacher filled 90 percent of targeted vacancies in hard-to-staff schools with high-performing teachers across seven districts [+M]
  • TTI increased elementary school test scores but not middle school test scores [+M]
  • Most TTI teachers stayed on the job even after transfer payments ended [+M]
  • A $20,000 selective transfer incentive offers high-performing teachers in 10 districts payment to move into their district's hardest-to-staff schools [+W]
  • TTI transfer incentives increased elementary school students' math and reading test scores by an estimated 4 to 10 percentile points [+M]
  • A $20,000 selective transfer incentive prompted behavioral responses from high-performing teachers in 10 districts [+W]
  • The transfer incentive targeted high-performing teachers into the hardest-to-staff schools within their own districts [+W]
  • TTI was more cost-effective than similar teacher-transfer incentive interventions [+W]
  • Most TTI teachers stayed on the job even after incentive payments ended [+M]
