Weights simulation tool for Texas community college funding scenarios¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A simulation tool the study team developed alongside the cost function analysis. It "allows policymakers to analyze different funding scenarios based on assumptions about student need factors, institutional contexts, and desired outcomes to generate cost projections." Policymakers can adjust the outcome target, which affects projections of necessary spending, and apply the estimated weights to annually collected data to determine adequate funding levels per student. REL Southwest supported the Texas Higher Education Coordinating Board in using the tool in 2022.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires assumptions about student need factors, institutional contexts, and desired outcome targets to generate cost projections

Constraints¶

  • Available upon request from contact.IES@ed.gov

Target Learners¶

  • Community college students in Texas, including first-generation, economically disadvantaged, academically disadvantaged, older-than-24, and English learner students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Adequate and equitable institutional funding to support student success points milestones and certificate or degree attainment

Claims¶

  • Cost function analysis indicates substantially higher spending is needed for equal opportunity: weights of 2.49 for first-generation and 2.63 for students older than 24 [+W]
  • Colleges serving the highest shares of first-generation students tended to have larger adequacy gaps, spending $1,475 below projected adequate cost [+W]
  • Texas's current funding system is progressive for several need factors, but the additional spending may not be enough for equal opportunity [+W]
  • Smaller community colleges tended to have larger adequacy gaps, with colleges under 4,001 students spending $1,871 (17 percent) below projected adequate cost [+W]
