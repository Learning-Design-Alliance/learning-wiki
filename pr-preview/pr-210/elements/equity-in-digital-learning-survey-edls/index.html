Equity in Digital Learning Survey (EDLS)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 5 studies (4 design, 1 associational), q2 · 0 of 5 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The EDLS is a student survey developed by Digital Promise for the RPP, administered in spring 2021 and spring 2022 and also to a nationally representative undergraduate sample in May 2021 for comparison. Per the report, "The Equity in Digital Learning Survey (EDLS) solicited students' course experiences and perspectives related to course quality, digital learning practices and challenges, and equity and inclusion practices." It asks students to self-report age, race/ethnicity, gender, urbanicity, and family income category to support disaggregation, and results were provided to teams via interactive dashboards.

Claims¶

  • Data dives with disaggregated course success data surfaced equity gaps previously invisible to faculty [+W]
  • RPP workshops on concrete equity mechanisms influenced instructor practice within one term [+W]
  • Launching RPP work with disaggregated student data highlighted equity issues without assigning blame [+W]
  • During spring 2020 remote instruction, internet connectivity and hardware/software problems interfered with course participation for large shares of undergraduates [+W]
