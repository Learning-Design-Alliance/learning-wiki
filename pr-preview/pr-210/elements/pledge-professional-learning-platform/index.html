PLEDGE platform for professional learning participants¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The PLEDGE platform is an online professional learning platform that program participants access independently. Participants use it to "complete screeners, set goals, access additional content to personalize and deepen their knowledge, and upload artifacts of application of learning." It was added by Greenfield Learning after the study's completion, together with additional course sessions, to support customized depth and ongoing learning for all educators.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Participants independently access the platform as part of the professional learning series.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • educators participating in the professional learning series

Target Learning Goals¶

  • personalize and deepen literacy knowledge
  • document application of learning

Claims¶

  • Students eligible for free or reduced-price lunch showed larger gains (+0.39) from Greenfield-trained teachers on the CAASPP ELA assessment [+W]
  • Students receiving special education services showed larger gains (+0.33) from Greenfield-trained teachers on the CAASPP ELA assessment [+W]
  • Third graders taught by Greenfield-trained teachers scored higher on the CAASPP ELA assessment than students taught by untrained teachers (effect size +0.23) [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • Open Learning Initiative (OLI) online courseware with embedded feedback activities and instructor dashboard

Examples¶

  • Combine live cohort sessions, self-guided application, peer coaching, and virtual office hours in literacy professional learning
