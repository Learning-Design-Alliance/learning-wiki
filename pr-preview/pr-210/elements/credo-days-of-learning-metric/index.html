Days of Learning: CREDO's translation of statistical growth into days of a 180-day school year¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Days of Learning is CREDO's accessibility translation of its statistical growth estimates. The report states that while its tools "create precise and reliable answers, they are presented in technical terms that are not user -friendly to a general audience", so "CREDO developed Days of Learning". Average year-to-year learning on state tests is equated to a typical 180-day school year, and above- or below-average progress is expressed as 180 days plus or minus X days, making growth magnitudes interpretable to non-technical audiences.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • State testing data across consecutive grades from which average year-to-year learning can be computed for each grade transition

Constraints¶

  • Growth is expressed relative to the state average year of learning on a typical 180-day school year, not as an absolute measure of achievement

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students in tested grades

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Annual academic growth in reading and mathematics
