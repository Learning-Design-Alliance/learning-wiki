MAP Growth Spanish Math and Reading assessments¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Computer adaptive Spanish-language assessments parallel to English MAP Growth, administered up to four times per school year in fall, winter, spring, and an optional summer window. "While the MAP Growth Spanish Math assessments use the same scale and norms as the English Math assessments, MAP Growth Spanish Reading is on its own scale and has independent norms", reflecting construct differences between English and Spanish literacy. Spanish Math tests cover grades K–12 using shared English blueprints; Spanish Reading covers K–8 with Spanish-specific content, targeted audio support in K–2, and grade-band splits to keep texts age-appropriate.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Items aligned to mathematics or language arts standards and mapped to grade-band instructional areas
  • Blueprints with a maximum of 43 items per test event and minimum Aspects of Rigor category counts for Math

Constraints¶

  • Spanish Reading item sets with extended passages currently include only field test items until a sufficient calibrated pool exists
  • Spanish Math items are not separately field tested; they adopt the RIT values of their calibrated English counterparts

Target Learners¶

  • Students who speak Spanish as their first language or receive instruction in Spanish, grades K–12 (Math) and K–8 (Reading)

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Mathematical achievement and growth without the barrier of English-language fluency
  • Spanish reading achievement and longitudinal growth in reading

Claims¶

  • Measurement precision is strongest for students in the middle of the Spanish score distribution, with CSEM near the 3.3 RIT target [+W]
  • Most Spanish test items show negligible differential item functioning across gender and Hispanic–White comparisons [+W]
  • The vast majority of Spanish Math (91%) and Reading (98%) items show no misfit across fall, winter, and spring terms [+W]
  • Spanish Math items show well-balanced difficulty and stable strong discrimination across 2024–2025 terms [+W]
  • Spanish Reading items show gradually increasing mean p values and stable discrimination with no negatively discriminating items [+W]
  • Spanish Math and Reading scores show high marginal reliability and moderate-to-strong test-retest stability, lowest for fall–spring and kindergarten [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • MAP Growth interim computer adaptive assessment system
  • Spanish MAP Reading Fluency Foundational Skills assessment
  • NWEA MAP Growth assessment and longitudinal achievement database
  • Spanish Foundational Skills achievement and growth norms tables (grades K-3)
  • MAP Growth: computer-adaptive, vertically scaled interim assessment on the RIT scale
  • Spanish MAP Growth Reading assessment: an adaptive K–8 reading assessment on a RIT scale linked to the English version
