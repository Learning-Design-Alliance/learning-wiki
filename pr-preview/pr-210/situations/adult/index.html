Designing for adult¶

Age · 18 and over, including university students · All situations

69 rows from 63 pages say what to change when the brief's age is adult; 28 of them cite a claim. Pages whose rows cite claims come first. Read the basis before relying on a row: many carry a claim to learners or settings it did not test, and say so.

Instructor Accessibility¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-instructor-led, asynchronous, adult learners working or caring Make the forum and private message the main routes with a published reply window of one working day; put live slots outside working hours and record them; accept questions as text or a short voice note Creating community and interacting with students online required deliberate, planned effort unlike spontaneous face-to-face co-presence [+W] (interviews with web-based high-school teachers; carried to adult courses); the channel choices are untested
Adults in work settings (workplace training, clinical placement, supervision) (from the wording) Name the person to ask on each shift or project and a back-up; agree an escalation route for urgent questions and a slower one for learning questions; schedule short regular meetings rather than relying on ad hoc asking Tutors and trainee researchers most value supportive attitude, mastery of the research field, and accessibility in a research project supervisor [+W] (online doctoral supervision; what people value, not an effect); the arrangement is untested

Social Presence¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-instructor-led, asynchronous, text-only, adult learners who work or care for others Run the full default: course-tied introductions with two named replies, weekly named announcements, small stable groups, a reply frame, one optional live meeting a fortnight at a time outside working hours, recorded Online community required deliberate, planned effort [+W] (interviews with web-based high-school teachers; carried to adult courses); the steps are untested
Adults in work settings or professional programmes (an MBA, clinical or workplace training) (from the wording) Make introductions professional: role, expertise, a problem from their work they want help with; group learners so their expertise complements; let them judge peers by what they offer, and build peer review of work on that Peer monitoring among MBA learners goes beyond CoI social presence by involving professionally relevant evaluation of peers' contributions [~W] (interviews with six MBA students)

Ask Experts¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult professionals or trainees in a workplace-clinical setting Hold consultations on real cases from the learner's work; the mentor models the decision on one case, then observes the learner on the next and debriefs; plan when the mentor stops being present Mentor modelling and a school-wide behaviour program's strategies transferred to the mentee's classroom management practices [+W] (one preservice teacher, qualitative case study)

Blended Learning¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult working professionals (adult-workplace) Put content and repeated practice online where they can fit it around work; use in-person time for practice with equipment, cases or colleagues and for feedback on performance. Blended learning improves outcomes [~M], pooled mostly across higher education and professional training, with extra time and resources confounded

Cognitive Activation¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adults or professionals (adult, adult-workplace) Draw the two cases for comparison from the learners' own work, differing on the one feature that matters, and ask what they share before naming the principle. Comparing contrasting cases improves learning [+M] (the transfer experiment was with undergraduates learning negotiation; untested in workplace training)

Dual Coding¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adolescent or adult, goal is a principle in science or mathematics Add learner drawing of the process or system, with a model to compare against Drawing in STEM [+M] (STEM only, abstract read)

Engagement¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult or in their first year of college, unsure they belong Before or at the start of the course, a short exercise in which learners read and write about adversity as common and temporary; check that the setting gives them real opportunities to belong Belonging Interventions Improve Outcomes [+M] (first-year college students; worked only where the institution afforded belonging)

Epistemic Cognition¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
adult learners, university, judging research Run small-group critique and redesign of flawed studies in the discipline across the course (three tasks in a 12-week course), and teach credibility of sources separately if it is also a goal. Collaborative critique-and-redesign [+M]: first-year psychology, single group, no control

Error Analysis¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
adult or professional, intermediate Collect a confidence rating on each answer; review confident errors first and in most depth; low-confidence errors may need re-teaching, not analysis. high-confidence errors [+M]; tested on recall, untested on professional judgement

Evaluating Sources¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
adult learners at university Two or more 75-minute lessons inside a writing or critical-thinking course, with a check on unseen sites several weeks later Civic Online Reasoning Instruction Improves Evaluation [+M] (randomised pilot, four sections; five-week posttest)
adult learners re-entering study, or adult basic education Start from sources they already use (health, benefits, news, job information); teach the off-page move on those first; keep reading load of practice sites manageable and model searching step by step Untested; the claim's adults are university students. From the earlier page's target learners

Experience with Languaging Activities (ELAs) guided by a sequence of experience-based questions¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in work settings (adult-workplace, workplace-clinical) (from the wording) Use a recent work incident or case as the experience; make step 4 a statement of what they would do differently and why; make step 5 a question to take to a colleague or the next shift. Specific reflection prompts [+S] and guided reflection [+M], carried from undergraduates and graduate students to workplace learners

Fostering Communities of Learning (FCL)¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in a work setting (a professional learning cohort, workplace-clinical) (from the wording) Make the theme a recurring problem of practice; each member researches a case or source and teaches it back; the shared task is a protocol or guide the team will use; observe practice afterwards, not only self-report. Communities of practice principle, whose evidence is descriptive; mathematics PLC review [+M] (a review of descriptive studies, no comparison)

Goal Setting & Monitoring¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
adult or adult-workplace learners Elicit the learner's own goal first and tie the task goal to it; add an if-then plan for the named obstacle (work, family time) Implementation intentions [+M]; mostly university students and online-course adults

Guided Practice¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are child (primary age), or adult / professionals Child: keep the cue–hint–demonstration ladder with one-to-one or small-group checking, and short rounds. Adult or professional: ask what they already do, and move guided items onto tasks from their own work as soon as the step is stable Child: Contingent scaffolding improves learning more than fixed or absent support. [+M] (fourth and fifth graders, long division, 8 per condition). Adult: untested proposal

Journaling¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in work settings (placements, clinical rotations, apprenticeships, coaching) (from the wording) A fieldwork log: after each shift or visit, one observed event, what was done and why, what to try next; the mentor reads the "try next" line before the next shift; a monthly review entry discussed with the mentor. reflection interventions [+M] (higher education, pooled across reflective activities; not specific to logs or workplaces)

Modeling¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
adult learners in a work setting (workplace-clinical), or teachers in training Model the performance in the real setting (the mentor's own lesson, a supervised shift), then debrief the decisions it involved, then have the novice try the same move with the mentor watching Mentor modelling and a school-wide behaviour program's strategies transferred to the mentee's classroom management practices [+W] (one preservice teacher, qualitative case); Vicarious experiences are positively related to agriculture teachers' general teaching efficacy, with observing a first year teacher explaining 11% of variance [+W] (correlational, reported in a review)

Multimodal Instruction¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners work in a second language (adult L2 readers) Keep the text available alongside narration and video rather than removing it as redundant. Monitor comprehension of the words alone. Video with narration and text [+M]: one randomized study of Chinese-speaking university EFL learners, immediate test only; redundancy [~M] reports reversals for abridged text

Online Course Design (Community of Inquiry)¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in work settings, studying around jobs and family (from the wording) Publish the full term's due dates in week 1; make the if-then plan name a work or family obstacle; offer a reduced catch-up route after a missed week. Implementation intentions [+S] (MCII helped most when the obstacle was predictable, such as work or family obligations); withdrawal reasons [~M] were led by no stated reason, transfer and schedule conflict in one programme

Social Interdependence¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult / postsecondary, in a live course Use small structured groups for problem sets, labs and design tasks, with individual assessment kept separate from the group product Small group learning improves STEM achievement [+M] (39 undergraduate STEM studies, d = 0.51; compared groups with non-group instruction and does not isolate interdependence)

Strengths-based Approach¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult in work settings (trainees, career changers, professionals) Ask what they already do at work that the course builds on, and use those tasks as entry points; feedback comes from the supervisor or mentor in person, naming a specific performance, rather than through a rating form Adults: untested proposal. Feedback channel: The effect of social persuasion on student teacher efficacy depends on its form [~M] (student teachers, reported in a review: verbal feedback from the cooperating teacher related to efficacy; a structured rating tool was detrimental against a control)

Structured Peer Review¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in a work setting (design teams, clinical or teaching placements) (from the wording) Review the real work product against the workplace's own standards; the author states what decision the draft must support; a senior colleague reviews alongside peers and requires a revise-and-resubmit where evidence is missing. Probing feedback with revise-and-resubmit [+W]: a case study of teacher candidates in clinical placements, instructor feedback not peer; progress was not linear

Supporting Gifted and Talented Students¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in a work setting who are far ahead of a training course (an experienced hire in onboarding) (from the wording) Let them test out of modules by doing the module's task to its standard; give them the hardest cases or a role reviewing others' work for the freed time expertise reversal [+M] (electrical trainees, among others); untested as a test-out design

Supporting Students with Intellectual Disabilities¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The student is an adolescent or young adult near transition (work, independent living) Move practice into community and workplace tasks; teach the student to set and review their own goals weekly and to lead their own planning meeting, rehearsed beforehand Self-determination instruction [+M] and student-led IEP meetings [+M] (high-school students with intellectual or learning disabilities); post-school outcomes not measured

Transfer of Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult or professionals Take the late-stage cases from their own work; ask them to bring one; check that their workplace offers the cases and equipment to use the skill The closer training content is to authentic clinical scenarios, the smoother its translation into practice [+W] (interviews with neurosurgical nurses; perceived, not measured)

Universal Design For Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult or professionals, including older beginners or learners with disabilities Ask learners which barriers they meet; keep individual accommodations; offer formats drawn from their own work modifications may be necessary for older beginners and learners with disabilities [~W] (one practitioner's judgment, adult ESL)

Validity, Reliability, and Bias in Classroom Assessment¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in work settings, or the capability is a job task (from the wording) Assess the task as it is done at work, with an observer checklist; use two observers on a sample; check validity by comparing the assessment's ratings with independent observation of practice An interview rating of teachers' use of an innovation correlated .65 with readers' ratings of written ethnographic protocols, which its authors read as support for its validity (LoU interview validated against observation [+W]; teachers, research instrument; the way validity was checked is carried, not a result about learners)

Criterion- and Norm-Referenced Testing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in work settings: licensing, certification or job entry (from the wording) Use a criterion-referenced judgment on tasks sampled from the job, with the cut set before scoring; use a norm-referenced score only if places are limited, and then with a work sample untested proposal; the threshold model is Competency-Based Assessment's

Cultural & Life Experiences Connections¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult, in work or with family responsibilities Make cases from learners' work and lives the main material from the second session; ask each learner for one case per unit, and compare across them untested; from the earlier page's target learners

Culturally Responsive Classroom Norms¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult, in adult-workplace or professional training Ask directly about expectations of hierarchy, questioning a trainer, and group versus individual credit; never treat silence as lack of knowledge; give written or private ways to raise a question untested proposal

Debriefing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
adult learners in a work setting, after a real shift, project or incident Use an after-action format (what was planned, what happened, why the difference, what to keep and what to change); keep it separate from performance appraisal and from any incident investigation; write the changes into the team's next briefing untested proposal; from the earlier page's examples

Developing Your Cultural Awareness¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in work settings (supervisors, trainers of a diverse workforce) (from the wording) Use the workplace's own data (where staff come from, prior jobs, how names are read, first associations with nationalities and job titles), as in stereotype-awareness activities for supervisors; the decision examined is allocation of tasks, feedback and promotion, tallied over a month untested

Digital Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult or professionals studying around work Use short sets on a phone that can be done in one sitting, and tasks taken from their own work; keep the first-week contact task untested proposal; contact task from Online attrition during Orientation Week is twice that of onground classes' first week [~M] (university continuing education)

Effective Classroom Management Plan Criteria¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adults in a work setting, adult education or training Replace rules with a short working agreement drafted with the group in the first session (phones, start times, how to disagree); keep the routines for materials, breaks and getting help; handle problems in a private conversation; drop rewards and the consequence ladder untested proposal; no claim here tests adult groups

Explicit Instruction: Vocabulary¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are fluent adults or professionals entering a new field (technical or workplace terms) Take the words from their real documents and tasks; teach word parts that recur in the field; check use in the job task (a handover, a report, a conversation with a client), not in a quiz untested; carried from school-age evidence on taught words and word parts (Morphological Instruction Improves Vocabulary [~M], school-age children only)

Feedback Loops¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in work settings (adult-workplace, workplace-clinical) (from the wording) Loop on real work outputs (a case note, a call, a procedure); a supervisor or peer reads one output per cycle against a stated criterion, and the next output is the re-observation untested; in one teacher-education case, probing feedback with required resubmission helped one candidate and not another (probing feedback [+W])

Flexible Grouping¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult, including adults in a work setting or adult basic education Group by the task or job role in hand and by what learners say they need, not by test scores; let adults choose to join a support session; use mixed groups to pool workplace experience untested proposal; the earlier page names adults, including those with learning disabilities and multilingual learners

Flipped Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult in a work setting (workplace-clinical), preparing between shifts Keep pre-class work to 10 minutes or less on a phone, tied to a task they will do in the session; use the session for practice on their own work tasks; do not penalise missed preparation, run a 5-minute catch-up at the start instead untested proposal

Functional Behavior Assessment¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adults in a work setting or adult education Drop the IEP and parent steps; ask the person directly what makes the session hard (shift fatigue, a task they cannot read, being put on the spot); agree the change with them privately; document only what the organisation's policy requires untested proposal

Game-based Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult or professionals in a workplace-clinical setting Build the game around one job decision with real consequences in its rules; debrief against the real procedure; keep competition optional untested proposal; the earlier page's whole-task argument is a design argument, not a tested effect

Gamification¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
adults in a workplace-clinical setting (compliance, onboarding) Tie levels to job tasks passed at a criterion; no badges for completion or seat time; no public ranking among colleagues; keep the theme sober untested proposal; the earlier page's view that adults prefer progress views to badges has no claim behind it

Graphic Organizers¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult literacy learners, and the goal is writing Use a planning organizer matched to the genre (claim–reasons–evidence for argument, a sequence for narrative), modelled, then used before each draft, then dropped once the plan can be made in note form untested; from the earlier page and its sources on adult literacy writing, which no claim records

Include parents, teachers, or mentors in any self-regulation intervention and support their own skills and coaching capacity¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are young adult (18–25), at college or work The young person names the adult (mentor, supervisor, tutor, peer mentor); the adult's role is coaching on request and a regular short review, not monitoring; skip parents unless the learner asks untested proposal
Adults have low literacy or low confidence (from the wording) Teach by demonstration and role-play, not handouts; one move per session; let adults log by voice note or a tick; praise what they already do well untested; coached practice beat written self-directed materials for parents at post-test (parent coaching [+M], ages 0–7)
Adults in a work setting (supervisors of apprentices or trainees, workplace-clinical) (from the wording) Make the supervisor the co-regulating adult; fit coaching into existing supervision meetings (10–15 minutes); use the workplace's own incident types as role-play scenarios untested proposal

Knowledge Organization¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in a work setting (from the wording) Build the top level from the decisions or tasks of the job (for example, "what to check first, what to escalate"), using the learners' own cases as the items to sort untested; the task-match finding is carried from physics students to workplace tasks (A hierarchical organization facilitates tasks only when matched to the task domain [~M])

Learning by Producing (multimedia production as learning)¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult in a work setting (workplace-clinical, onboarding, professional development) Make the product something colleagues will use (a 2-minute procedure video, a job aid), with a subject-matter expert reviewing the blueprint for accuracy before recording; individual check on the procedure itself. Untested proposal; learning by teaching [+M] supports making each learner explain their part, not tested with adults at work

Match the form and content of staff development interventions to clients' assessed Stages of Concern¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in a non-school workplace adopting a new procedure or system (adult-workplace, e.g. clinical or office staff) (from the wording) Use the same diagnosis and stage-matched support, with the questions worded for their work; record whether the stages read the same way untested; every claim here is from teachers, carried by analogy

Memory Consolidation¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in work settings, learning for use on the job (from the wording) Make the later encounters job tasks that need the material (a case at a team meeting, a checklist step on shift), and check unaided in the form the job uses; agree the horizon with the learner untested proposal

Motivation¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in work settings, training they did not choose (compliance, onboarding) (from the wording) Ask what each learner wants from it; tie each module to a task from their own job; cut time cost (short modules in work time); give a reason for each required item untested proposal; task value [+M] carried from school and college reviews to the workplace

Multiple Methods of Assessment¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in work settings, or the goal is a job task (from the wording) Combine observation of the task at work (a checklist) with a short conversation about why each step was done, and a work product; use the workplace's own records where they show the goal untested proposal; from the earlier page (adults with applied experience)

Note-taking¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult in a work setting (training, briefings, meetings) Notes are for doing something: a one-page template with "what to do", "when", "who to ask", "one question"; review is applying the note on the next real task, followed by a check with a colleague or supervisor untested proposal
Adults whose notes become a job aid (a handover, a shift, a procedure they will use again at work) (from the wording) Replace fill-in-the-blank guided notes with a reusable domain grid the learner keeps, built from the categories the brief or the workplace already uses (if none are given, for a handover: facts, actions due, what is uncertain, what to report), with a pre-printed prompt for any item whose omission is dangerous, modelled once with a think-aloud, then used on a real or recorded case; check it by having the learner brief a partner from the grid, not by reading back. In one session of about 75 minutes: 10 minutes modelling the grid on a short clip, 20 minutes filling it on the full case, 10 minutes comparing grids in pairs, 20 minutes briefing a partner from it (with sentence frames such as "The priority is… because…" for learners working in a second language), 10 minutes fixing the grid for reuse untested; the guided-notes claim [+W] tests teacher-made gapped notes with students, carried here only as "give the structure", not the worksheet form

Observation/Shadowing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
adult learners in a work setting (workplace-clinical, adult-workplace): new staff shadowing a colleague Shadow on a real shift in blocks of 1–3 hours, with a pocket focus card of two to four items; a 5-minute check-in before and a 10–15-minute conversation after; follow within the week with the learner doing the same task under supervision untested proposal; the debrief is carried from Contingent scaffolding improves learning more than fixed or absent support. [+M] (one-to-one tutoring of children)

Phonics¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult, in a literacy programme or at work Use adult texts and words from the learner's own life or job for blending and decodable reading; say why the sound work is there; keep checks private; move to the learner's real texts as soon as a correspondence is secure untested proposal for adult literacy. An artificial-script experiment with 24 adults found print-to-sound training made new words faster to read than print-to-meaning training, not more accurate (Systematic phonics outperforms unsystematic or no-phonics instruction on children's reading overall, but its advantage over whole-word teaching on novel words is untested in children and contested [~S]). No claim page here tests phonics teaching with adults learning to read their own language

Positive Self-talk¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in a work setting (from the wording) Take cue words from the job's own procedure or checklist (the step names already used on the floor or ward); keep the cards private to the learner Untested proposal

Pre-reading Questioning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in work settings reading a procedure, policy or case (from the wording) Write questions as decisions they will face ("A patient refuses X: what does the policy let you do?"); check against the exact clause; ask what they do now that the text changes untested proposal

Procedural Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
adult learners in a work setting, using the procedure on the job Ask what they already do; use items from their own work as soon as the steps are stable; give a job aid laid out in the workplace's own categories and step names, used during practice and kept afterwards; check on a real or realistic task untested proposal

Process-based Writing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult, including in work settings (reports, case notes, proposals) Use the writer's own work documents as the texts; take the reader from the real setting (the manager, the client, the colleague); keep the strategy modelling but shorten it; respond as that reader first untested; neither meta-analysis covers adults (strategy studies stop at grade 10)

Self-monitoring¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in a work setting (from the wording) Write the checklist from the job's own standard (a procedure, a safety step, a quality criterion); keep records private to the learner and a supervisor, so the check is not read as surveillance; check against outcomes the work already produces (errors caught, rework) Untested proposal

Sequencing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in a work setting (from the wording) Order by the job's own sequence where it has one, start from a task they already do, and bring forward the steps they need soonest at work untested proposal; skill develops through engaging in the target activity [~W] (an argument from second-language acquisition, no study)

Social Emotional Learning¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
adult learners in work settings (health care, teaching, management, customer service) Present the skills as professional competence; use cases and structured reflective discussion of real encounters (Balint-style groups) instead of scripted lessons; assess on observed practice. Untested: no claim here tests SEL with adults; the examples are the earlier page's

Standardized Test Fairness and Bias¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in work settings: a licensing, promotion or job-entry test (from the wording) Check that the items sample the job's tasks, not general reading or cultural knowledge; validate the score against observed job performance by group; add a work-sample task for candidates near the cut untested proposal; the prediction check (step 3) applied to a job criterion

Structured Academic Controversy¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Age adolescent or adult in civics, ethics, history or policy Use the default sequence. For adults, ask for the learner-valued goal explicitly, since adults often arrive with a held view; collect the starting position privately. untested; from the earlier page (target learners); untested proposal for the adult change

Summative Assessment¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult professionals, or in adult-workplace training Make the end-point a task from their work, observed or produced on the job, judged against a stated competency threshold; ask what they already do and test only what is new untested proposal; threshold decisions are held by Competency-Based Assessment

Text-to-Speech¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in work settings (manuals, procedures, compliance documents, forms) (from the wording) Let workers listen to the document on their own device, in sections, and act on each section (find the step, fill the field) rather than only listen; fix the voice's pronunciation of product names and codes first untested proposal

Wise Feedback Across Racial and Ethnic Difference¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
adult learners in workplace-clinical settings (a supervisor, mentor or clinical educator across a difference) Name the job's own standard (the competency, the protocol, the client outcome) as the standard; say what in the learner's practice shows they can meet it; agree one change to try on the next shift or case untested; Feedback improves learning by a medium amount on average, but its effect varies widely with the information it carries, and more than a third of feedback interventions reduce performance [~S] covers field and work settings and warns that feedback turning attention to the self can lower performance
