Designing for workplace-clinical¶

Setting · workplace, clinical or professional training · All situations

78 rows from 64 pages say what to change when the brief's setting is workplace-clinical; 32 of them cite a claim. Pages whose rows cite claims come first. Read the basis before relying on a row: many carry a claim to learners or settings it did not test, and say so.

Structured Peer Review¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Quality turns on correctness (a calculation, code, a clinical or technical claim) (from the wording) Peers comment on clarity, structure and whether claims are supported; the teacher or an expert checks correctness; or give reviewers a check against a known answer. Peer accuracy [~M]: secondary-school writing only; the correctness boundary is the claim pages' Discussion, untested
Adults in a work setting (design teams, clinical or teaching placements) (from the wording) Review the real work product against the workplace's own standards; the author states what decision the draft must support; a senior colleague reviews alongside peers and requires a revise-and-resubmit where evidence is missing. Probing feedback with revise-and-resubmit [+W]: a case study of teacher candidates in clinical placements, instructor feedback not peer; progress was not linear

Summative Assessment¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult professionals, or in adult-workplace training (from the wording) Make the end-point a task from their work, observed or produced on the job, judged against a stated competency threshold; ask what they already do and test only what is new untested proposal; threshold decisions are held by Competency-Based Assessment
Goal is a complex-skill (a design, a case, a clinical or practical performance) (from the wording) Include a whole-task performance, scored with a rubric, plus short component items so a failure can be traced to a part A training-design argument, not tested by any study recorded here, holds that practising whole complex tasks improves transfer of complex skills [~W] (an argument about training, carried here to assessment)
The capability is one integrated performance (a clinical scenario, a full repair, a live service) and the brief asks for an authentic assessment (from the wording) Keep a single whole-task scenario as the judgment; do not split it into paper items. Put recall and recognition checks into the low-stakes practice before it; inside the scenario, prompt the learner to say decisions aloud from the start and tell them the assessor will not confirm answers, so "is that what you wanted?" gets no reply and is not marked down the whole-task claim [~W] is a design argument; the rest is untested

Validity, Reliability, and Bias in Classroom Assessment¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The goal is a complex-skill or extended performance (a lab, an essay, a presentation, a clinical task) (from the wording) Use a performance task scored by a rubric with level descriptors and anchors; calibrate markers before marking and double-mark a sample; report the score with the number of tasks it rests on Raters differed in severity [~M] (open-ended items, three raters); rubric guidance improved peer raters' placement of writing on complex criteria [+M] (MOOC and graduate peers, carried to teachers); one class's rubric scores varied by a mean of 1.2 of 9 points across six scorers (engineering rubric low scoring reliability [~W])
Adults in work settings, or the capability is a job task (from the wording) Assess the task as it is done at work, with an observer checklist; use two observers on a sample; check validity by comparing the assessment's ratings with independent observation of practice An interview rating of teachers' use of an innovation correlated .65 with readers' ratings of written ethnographic protocols, which its authors read as support for its validity (LoU interview validated against observation [+W]; teachers, research instrument; the way validity was checked is carried, not a result about learners)

Ask Experts¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult professionals or trainees in a workplace-clinical setting Hold consultations on real cases from the learner's work; the mentor models the decision on one case, then observes the learner on the next and debriefs; plan when the mentor stops being present Mentor modelling and a school-wide behaviour program's strategies transferred to the mentee's classroom management practices [+W] (one preservice teacher, qualitative case study)

Authentic Assessment¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
workplace-clinical or a placement-based course Use structured clinical or practical stations with checklists and global ratings, or a case simulation, and check with learners whether the simulated case behaves like real clients. Trainees' reports on clinical scenarios [+W] (one qualitative study of neurosurgical nurses); simulated cases judged unrealistic [~W] (10 graduate students, focus groups)

Blended Learning¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult working professionals (adult-workplace) (from the wording) Put content and repeated practice online where they can fit it around work; use in-person time for practice with equipment, cases or colleagues and for feedback on performance. Blended learning improves outcomes [~M], pooled mostly across higher education and professional training, with extra time and resources confounded

Cognitive Activation¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adults or professionals (adult, adult-workplace) (from the wording) Draw the two cases for comparison from the learners' own work, differing on the one feature that matters, and ask what they share before naming the principle. Comparing contrasting cases improves learning [+M] (the transfer experiment was with undergraduates learning negotiation; untested in workplace training)

Cognitive Flexibility¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Expertise intermediate or advanced, an ill-structured domain (clinical reasoning, law, policy) (from the wording) Use criss-crossing: revisit each concept in several short cases under different themes across sessions; drop the step-by-step part tasks. Presenting multiple cases from different perspectives supports transfer in ill-structured domains [+M]; tested with first- and second-year undergraduates, untested with professionals

Debriefing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
adult learners in a work setting, after a real shift, project or incident (from the wording) Use an after-action format (what was planned, what happened, why the difference, what to keep and what to change); keep it separate from performance appraisal and from any incident investigation; write the changes into the team's next briefing untested proposal; from the earlier page's examples
Goal is a procedure or psychomotor skill in workplace-clinical training Treat the debrief as the feedback step of a repeated attempt: debrief against a stated mastery standard, then rerun the scenario or a changed one until the standard is met Simulation Based Education With Deliberate Practice Improves Clinical Outcomes [+M] (14 studies, health professions); Simulation Based Education Improves Outcomes [~M]: could not isolate feedback as the active feature

Deliberate Practice¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is a procedure or psychomotor skill in workplace-clinical training Use a simulator or part-task trainer with a mastery standard per step, immediate expert or device feedback, repetition until the standard is met and rising difficulty; add booster sessions later Simulation-based education with deliberate practice improves clinical outcomes [+M] (14 studies, health professions, procedural skills, against traditional clinical education)

Epistemic Games¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
adult-workplace learners in workplace-clinical training Use cases drawn from the learners' own workplace, a practitioner as mentor, and an outside-game task that matches a real work task; record transfer to work separately from in-game performance. Simulation training in the health professions [+M] (against no training); practice-near content [+W] (nurses' perceptions)

Error Analysis¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
goal is a complex-skill (writing, a whole clinical or technical task) (from the wording) Ask learners to look for one or two named error types in their own draft or performance, then revise; do not mark every error at once. noticing particular errors in writing [+W] (one study, reported in a review, EFL college writing)
high stakes, public setting, or workplace-clinical where real errors are unsafe Use constructed erroneous examples or simulation rather than live errors; analyse errors privately or in writing; keep error analysis out of graded assessment. untested; from the earlier page

Evaluating Sources¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
workplace-clinical learners (e.g. nurses and health information) Practise on the health sites and summaries they will meet, including misinformation sites; add judging study methods if they read research One-session checklist (CRAAP) instruction did no better than the alternatives it was compared with [~M] (nursing students, one 110-minute session); the multi-session design is untested in this setting

Experience with Languaging Activities (ELAs) guided by a sequence of experience-based questions¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in work settings (adult-workplace, workplace-clinical) Use a recent work incident or case as the experience; make step 4 a statement of what they would do differently and why; make step 5 a question to take to a colleague or the next shift. Specific reflection prompts [+S] and guided reflection [+M], carried from undergraduates and graduate students to workplace learners

Experiential Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adult professionals, workplace-clinical, a procedure or psychomotor skill Replace one-off exposure with simulated practice repeated to a stated mastery standard, immediate feedback after each attempt, rising difficulty, and a debrief; add booster sessions if the skill is not used soon Simulation Based Education With Deliberate Practice [+M]; medical education, procedural skills only; boosters untested

Experiential Learning Cycle¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
workplace-clinical, a procedure or psychomotor skill Run the experience as simulated practice repeated to a stated mastery standard, feedback after each attempt, rising difficulty, then a debrief; move to the real setting only when the standard is met. Simulation with deliberate practice [+M]; medical education, procedural skills only

Fostering Communities of Learning (FCL)¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in a work setting (a professional learning cohort, workplace-clinical) Make the theme a recurring problem of practice; each member researches a case or source and teaches it back; the shared task is a protocol or guide the team will use; observe practice afterwards, not only self-report. Communities of practice principle, whose evidence is descriptive; mathematics PLC review [+M] (a review of descriptive studies, no comparison)

Goal Setting & Monitoring¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
adult or adult-workplace learners (from the wording) Elicit the learner's own goal first and tie the task goal to it; add an if-then plan for the named obstacle (work, family time) Implementation intentions [+M]; mostly university students and online-course adults

Instructor Accessibility¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in work settings (workplace training, clinical placement, supervision) (from the wording) Name the person to ask on each shift or project and a back-up; agree an escalation route for urgent questions and a slower one for learning questions; schedule short regular meetings rather than relying on ad hoc asking Tutors and trainee researchers most value supportive attitude, mastery of the research field, and accessibility in a research project supervisor [+W] (online doctoral supervision; what people value, not an effect); the arrangement is untested

Journaling¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in work settings (placements, clinical rotations, apprenticeships, coaching) (from the wording) A fieldwork log: after each shift or visit, one observed event, what was done and why, what to try next; the mentor reads the "try next" line before the next shift; a monthly review entry discussed with the mentor. reflection interventions [+M] (higher education, pooled across reflective activities; not specific to logs or workplaces)

Modeling¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
adult learners in a work setting (workplace-clinical), or teachers in training Model the performance in the real setting (the mentor's own lesson, a supervised shift), then debrief the decisions it involved, then have the novice try the same move with the mentor watching Mentor modelling and a school-wide behaviour program's strategies transferred to the mentee's classroom management practices [+W] (one preservice teacher, qualitative case); Vicarious experiences are positively related to agriculture teachers' general teaching efficacy, with observing a first year teacher explaining 11% of variance [+W] (correlational, reported in a review)
High stakes or safety (clinical, lab, machinery) (from the wording) Model the whole procedure once at normal speed, then step by step with what to check and why; include the commonest error and how it is caught; require an unaided demonstration under supervision before the learner works alone untested proposal; the error step is carried from Erroneous examples improve conceptual understanding by forcing comparison with correct models. [+M]

Online Course Design (Community of Inquiry)¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in work settings, studying around jobs and family (from the wording) Publish the full term's due dates in week 1; make the if-then plan name a work or family obstacle; offer a reduced catch-up route after a missed week. Implementation intentions [+S] (MCII helped most when the obstacle was predictable, such as work or family obligations); withdrawal reasons [~M] were led by no stated reason, transfer and schedule conflict in one programme

Positive Self-talk¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The goal is a motor or performance skill (sport, a lab or clinical technique, an instrument) (from the wording) Use instructional cue words for fine, precise movements and motivational cues for endurance; train them before use Self Talk Improves Learning And Performance [+S] (the setting the evidence comes from)
Adults in a work setting (from the wording) Take cue words from the job's own procedure or checklist (the step names already used on the floor or ward); keep the cards private to the learner Untested proposal

Reflection¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is a complex-skill or professional practice (teaching, clinical, design) (from the wording) Reflect on a recorded or observed performance against named criteria, then reattempt a comparable task; repeat over the course. Reflection interventions in higher education [+M]; tested with adult learners in higher education, outcomes pooled

Social Interdependence¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult-workplace teams (from the wording) Build belonging and shared goals (harmony, a sense of one team) rather than formal role dependence alone; measure what each member can do Social interdependence, not role interdependence, correlated with performance [~W] (40 brokerage offices, correlational; "social interdependence" there means team belonging, not goal structure)

Social Presence¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in work settings or professional programmes (an MBA, clinical or workplace training) (from the wording) Make introductions professional: role, expertise, a problem from their work they want help with; group learners so their expertise complements; let them judge peers by what they offer, and build peer review of work on that Peer monitoring among MBA learners goes beyond CoI social presence by involving professionally relevant evaluation of peers' contributions [~W] (interviews with six MBA students)

Strengths-based Approach¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult in work settings (trainees, career changers, professionals) (from the wording) Ask what they already do at work that the course builds on, and use those tasks as entry points; feedback comes from the supervisor or mentor in person, naming a specific performance, rather than through a rating form Adults: untested proposal. Feedback channel: The effect of social persuasion on student teacher efficacy depends on its form [~M] (student teachers, reported in a review: verbal feedback from the cooperating teacher related to efficacy; a structured rating tool was detrimental against a control)

Supporting Gifted and Talented Students¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in a work setting who are far ahead of a training course (an experienced hire in onboarding) (from the wording) Let them test out of modules by doing the module's task to its standard; give them the hardest cases or a role reviewing others' work for the freed time expertise reversal [+M] (electrical trainees, among others); untested as a test-out design

Transfer of Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
workplace-clinical, and the course teaches advanced competencies the setting rarely uses Agree with the workplace when the learner will meet the cases; plan refresher cases or simulation if they will not; report use separately from knowledge Transfer of advanced competencies is limited by institutional case exposure, technical platforms, and multidisciplinary maturity rather than training deficiencies [~W] (interviews, one programme in China)

Criterion- and Norm-Referenced Testing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in work settings: licensing, certification or job entry (from the wording) Use a criterion-referenced judgment on tasks sampled from the job, with the cut set before scoring; use a norm-referenced score only if places are limited, and then with a work sample untested proposal; the threshold model is Competency-Based Assessment's
The brief wants a whole-task performance kept whole (a recital, a clinical scenario, a product for a real audience) (from the wording) Judge the whole performance against criteria the audience would recognize; do not split it into paper items to get skill scores; record per-criterion notes from the one performance instead untested proposal

Cultural & Life Experiences Connections¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners do not want to share personal experience (refugees, people in recovery, a mixed workplace) (from the wording) Make every personal prompt optional and private first; offer composite or fictional cases as equal alternatives; never ask a learner to represent a group untested; from the earlier page's constraints

Culturally Responsive Classroom Norms¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult, in adult-workplace or professional training (from the wording) Ask directly about expectations of hierarchy, questioning a trainer, and group versus individual credit; never treat silence as lack of knowledge; give written or private ways to raise a question untested proposal

Developing Your Cultural Awareness¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in work settings (supervisors, trainers of a diverse workforce) (from the wording) Use the workplace's own data (where staff come from, prior jobs, how names are read, first associations with nationalities and job titles), as in stereotype-awareness activities for supervisors; the decision examined is allocation of tasks, feedback and promotion, tallied over a month untested

Effective Classroom Management Plan Criteria¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adults in a work setting, adult education or training (from the wording) Replace rules with a short working agreement drafted with the group in the first session (phones, start times, how to disagree); keep the routines for materials, breaks and getting help; handle problems in a private conversation; drop rewards and the consequence ladder untested proposal; no claim here tests adult groups

Epistemic Cognition¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
workplace-clinical learners (e.g. nurses judging health information) Use the claims they will meet at work (health websites, research summaries); teach both source checking and study-method judgments if both occur, and assess each. Untested proposal; one nursing study is single-session and is read in the checklist row

Explicit Instruction: Vocabulary¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are fluent adults or professionals entering a new field (technical or workplace terms) (from the wording) Take the words from their real documents and tasks; teach word parts that recur in the field; check use in the job task (a handover, a report, a conversation with a client), not in a quiz untested; carried from school-age evidence on taught words and word parts (Morphological Instruction Improves Vocabulary [~M], school-age children only)

Feedback Loops¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in work settings (adult-workplace, workplace-clinical) Loop on real work outputs (a case note, a call, a procedure); a supervisor or peer reads one output per cycle against a stated criterion, and the next output is the re-observation untested; in one teacher-education case, probing feedback with required resubmission helped one candidate and not another (probing feedback [+W])

Flexible Grouping¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult, including adults in a work setting or adult basic education (from the wording) Group by the task or job role in hand and by what learners say they need, not by test scores; let adults choose to join a support session; use mixed groups to pool workplace experience untested proposal; the earlier page names adults, including those with learning disabilities and multilingual learners

Flipped Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult in a work setting (workplace-clinical), preparing between shifts Keep pre-class work to 10 minutes or less on a phone, tied to a task they will do in the session; use the session for practice on their own work tasks; do not penalise missed preparation, run a 5-minute catch-up at the start instead untested proposal

Functional Behavior Assessment¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adults in a work setting or adult education (from the wording) Drop the IEP and parent steps; ask the person directly what makes the session hard (shift fatigue, a task they cannot read, being put on the spot); agree the change with them privately; document only what the organisation's policy requires untested proposal

Game-based Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult or professionals in a workplace-clinical setting Build the game around one job decision with real consequences in its rules; debrief against the real procedure; keep competition optional untested proposal; the earlier page's whole-task argument is a design argument, not a tested effect

Gamification¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
adults in a workplace-clinical setting (compliance, onboarding) Tie levels to job tasks passed at a criterion; no badges for completion or seat time; no public ranking among colleagues; keep the theme sober untested proposal; the earlier page's view that adults prefer progress views to badges has no claim behind it

Guided Practice¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The task has many steps or hidden decision points, or an error on the job is costly (safety, compliance, harm to a client) (from the wording) Plan more guided rounds per step; raise the release criterion (more consecutive unaided successes, on representative tasks); add a stage of unassisted work under supervision before full independence; define what "working alone" means (no supervision, or supervision with a way to escalate) untested proposal; from the earlier page ("multiple steps, hidden decision points, or a high likelihood of early error")

Holistic Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adults in a work setting (adult-workplace) (from the wording) Take the whole from their own work: the complete workflow or a real case they handle; elicit what they already do and start at the part where their account breaks down untested proposal; whole-task practice and transfer [+W] is an argument, not a study

Include parents, teachers, or mentors in any self-regulation intervention and support their own skills and coaching capacity¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in a work setting (supervisors of apprentices or trainees, workplace-clinical) Make the supervisor the co-regulating adult; fit coaching into existing supervision meetings (10–15 minutes); use the workplace's own incident types as role-play scenarios untested proposal

Knowledge Organization¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in a work setting (from the wording) Build the top level from the decisions or tasks of the job (for example, "what to check first, what to escalate"), using the learners' own cases as the items to sort untested; the task-match finding is carried from physics students to workplace tasks (A hierarchical organization facilitates tasks only when matched to the task domain [~M])

Learning by Producing (multimedia production as learning)¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult in a work setting (workplace-clinical, onboarding, professional development) Make the product something colleagues will use (a 2-minute procedure video, a job aid), with a subject-matter expert reviewing the blueprint for accuracy before recording; individual check on the procedure itself. Untested proposal; learning by teaching [+M] supports making each learner explain their part, not tested with adults at work

Match the form and content of staff development interventions to clients' assessed Stages of Concern¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in a non-school workplace adopting a new procedure or system (adult-workplace, e.g. clinical or office staff) (from the wording) Use the same diagnosis and stage-matched support, with the questions worded for their work; record whether the stages read the same way untested; every claim here is from teachers, carried by analogy

Memory Consolidation¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in work settings, learning for use on the job (from the wording) Make the later encounters job tasks that need the material (a case at a team meeting, a checklist step on shift), and check unaided in the form the job uses; agree the horizon with the learner untested proposal

Motivation¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in work settings, training they did not choose (compliance, onboarding) (from the wording) Ask what each learner wants from it; tie each module to a task from their own job; cut time cost (short modules in work time); give a reason for each required item untested proposal; task value [+M] carried from school and college reviews to the workplace

Multiple Methods of Assessment¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The goal is a complex-skill or performance (a lab, a clinical task, a design) (from the wording) Combine an observed performance or product with the learner's short written or spoken account of the decisions behind it; use one rubric for both untested; from the earlier page ("performance plus reflection"); the Authentic Assessment pattern holds the task design
Adults in work settings, or the goal is a job task (from the wording) Combine observation of the task at work (a checklist) with a short conversation about why each step was done, and a work product; use the workplace's own records where they show the goal untested proposal; from the earlier page (adults with applied experience)

Note-taking¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult in a work setting (training, briefings, meetings) (from the wording) Notes are for doing something: a one-page template with "what to do", "when", "who to ask", "one question"; review is applying the note on the next real task, followed by a check with a colleague or supervisor untested proposal

Observation/Shadowing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
adult learners in a work setting (workplace-clinical, adult-workplace): new staff shadowing a colleague Shadow on a real shift in blocks of 1–3 hours, with a pocket focus card of two to four items; a 5-minute check-in before and a 10–15-minute conversation after; follow within the week with the learner doing the same task under supervision untested proposal; the debrief is carried from Contingent scaffolding improves learning more than fixed or absent support. [+M] (one-to-one tutoring of children)
The material is private or sensitive (a colleague's own lesson video, clinical cases, a learner's reflective record) (from the wording) The observed person controls who sees the recording and the notes, and may end the arrangement; observers' notes are shared with the observed person first and with a supervisor only by agreement; keep observation for development apart from evaluation untested proposal; that clinical supervision tied to evaluation has inconclusive research results is a monograph's report (Research on clinical supervision's effectiveness is inconclusive, partly because practice is diluted and tied to evaluation [~W])

Pre-reading Questioning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in work settings reading a procedure, policy or case (from the wording) Write questions as decisions they will face ("A patient refuses X: what does the policy let you do?"); check against the exact clause; ask what they do now that the text changes untested proposal

Procedural Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The brief wants a whole-task performance kept whole (a full clinical procedure, a complete repair, a full presentation) (from the wording) Keep every practice attempt a whole task; simplify the task (fewer variables, an easier case) rather than splitting it, and put the support around the whole; isolate a step only for a few minutes when one step keeps failing untested proposal; whole-task argument [~W]
adult learners in a work setting, using the procedure on the job (from the wording) Ask what they already do; use items from their own work as soon as the steps are stable; give a job aid laid out in the workplace's own categories and step names, used during practice and kept afterwards; check on a real or realistic task untested proposal
Errors are costly on the job (safety, compliance, harm to a client) or stakes are high (from the wording) More supported rounds per step; release only after several consecutive unaided successes on representative tasks; a stage of supervised unaided work before full independence; judge readiness only on unaided performance untested proposal

Process-based Writing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are adult, including in work settings (reports, case notes, proposals) (from the wording) Use the writer's own work documents as the texts; take the reader from the real setting (the manager, the client, the colleague); keep the strategy modelling but shorten it; respond as that reader first untested; neither meta-analysis covers adults (strategy studies stop at grade 10)

Professional Development¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Higher-education faculty, teaching assistants or adult-education tutors (adults in work settings with little release time) (from the wording) Use the same cycle with a peer observer from the same department; let the observed session be a seminar, lab or online class; time the cycle to the teaching calendar (one cycle per three or four weeks of teaching). Untested; the coaching claims are from K–12 classrooms

Self-monitoring¶

Text-to-Speech¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adults in work settings (manuals, procedures, compliance documents, forms) (from the wording) Let workers listen to the document on their own device, in sections, and act on each section (find the step, fill the field) rather than only listen; fix the voice's pronunciation of product names and codes first untested proposal
Adult second-language learners in short evening classes, reading workplace instructions (from the wording) Use short texts (about 100–250 words, one procedure) read sentence by sentence; teach playback control as a skill (slow the voice, replay a sentence, replay a single word for pronunciation) and let learners practise privately with headphones; give teacher feedback in a form TTS can read aloud; each lesson end with one unaided reread of a step. If the brief names its own assessment (accuracy, control of playback, a reflection comparing listening and unaided reading), assess exactly those, and keep checks to one or two items so the class is not mostly testing untested proposal
