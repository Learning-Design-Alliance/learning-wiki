Designing for concept¶

Kind of goal · categories and their features · All situations

20 rows from 20 pages say what to change when the brief's kind of goal is concept; 18 of them cite a claim. Pages whose rows cite claims come first. Read the basis before relying on a row: many carry a claim to learners or settings it did not test, and say so.

Ask Experts¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is a concept, principle or transfer, not only a procedure Have the expert ask and prompt more and explain less; include one changed problem in each consultation for the learner to reason through aloud Contingent scaffolding improves learning more than fixed or absent support. [+M] (interactive tutoring gave better transfer with similar immediate scores) and Human tutoring has a medium effect over no tutoring, and in one small study students learned as well when tutors only prompted them as when tutors also explained and gave feedback [~M] (eighth graders, circulatory system)

Cognitive Flexibility¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal a STEM concept taught through representations Add a second representation (sketch, simulation) beside the formula, with tasks that connect them; monitor reported load and representational skill. Multiple representations improve learning [~M]; one undergraduate physics field study and a review; benefit depends on representational competence

Debriefing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is judgement, a concept or a principle (diagnostic reasoning, a negotiation, an ethical decision) Spend most of the time on the learners' reasoning at two or three decision points; end with the general rule stated in the learner's words and tested on a new case Prompting learners to self-explain… [+M] (immediate tests, study of examples and text; carried to debriefs)

Digital Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is a concept, a principle, or transfer Keep answer-checking, but add explanation prompts and changed items; hold discussion of reasons in a live or threaded session Prompting learners to self-explain improves understanding and problem solving on immediate tests by a moderate average amount, with little evidence yet on delayed or classroom gains. [+S] (not specific to digital delivery)

Dual Coding¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is a concept or hierarchy to be understood across a unit Have learners build and revise a concept map across sessions, with labelled links, starting from given nodes Concept mapping [+M] (STEM and non-STEM; constructing showed more benefit than studying, against different comparisons)

Engagement¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is a concept or principle, measured by conceptual-understanding Judge success by an explanation or prediction probe, not by on-task time; make the engaged task one of explaining or predicting Constructive learning beats active and passive learning [~M] (ordering only partly held in the field)

Error Analysis¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
goal is a concept or principle with a known misconception Build one incorrect/correct pair around the misconception, require a written "why" explanation, and test with changed problems, not only the practised procedure. erroneous examples [+M] (children, decimals, one session); productive failure [~M] (gain on conceptual, not procedural, outcomes)

Experience with Languaging Activities (ELAs) guided by a sequence of experience-based questions¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The goal is understanding of a concept or principle in a course Write a content-specific step 4 prompt for each session and score the answer against the named idea; keep the number of reflections constant rather than adding more. Specific reflection prompts [+S] (engineering undergraduates; school writing-to-learn studies)

Experiential Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The goal is a science or mathematics concept Have learners manipulate the system (physical or virtual), predict before acting, and map the result onto the formula or diagram afterwards; if a physical lab is unavailable, a virtual one is a fair substitute Physical Experience Enhances Science Learning [~M] (undergraduate physics); Hands-on learning improves achievement [+M]

Experiential Learning Cycle¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The goal is a science or mathematics concept Make the experience a predict-then-manipulate task (physical or virtual), with a stated prediction before acting, and map the result onto the formula or diagram during the principle step; use a virtual lab when a physical one is not available. Physical experience [~M] (undergraduate physics; physical and virtual manipulation equally effective); cognitive conflict [+M] (science-education conceptual-change strategies)

Guided Practice¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is a concept, principle or transfer, not only a procedure Replace some feedback-giving with prompts that ask the learner to explain or predict the next step; include one changed item in each round Human tutoring has a medium effect over no tutoring, and in one small study students learned as well when tutors only prompted them as when tutors also explained and gave feedback [~M] (eighth graders, 11 tutor–student pairs: prompting without feedback learned as well)

Knowledge Organization¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is telling similar categories or cases apart (concept) Organize as a comparison: place two or three cases side by side and have learners list similarities and differences before the principle is named Comparing cases side by side improves learning and transfer by a moderate average amount, though in one algebra study it raised procedural knowledge and flexibility but not conceptual knowledge [+S] (tests comparison, not organization as such)

Modeling¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is a concept or principle, or transfer, not only a procedure Model on two items that differ on the surface; at each pause ask why, not what; include one error and its repair, and ask learners what gave it away Prompting learners to self-explain improves understanding and problem solving on immediate tests by a moderate average amount, with little evidence yet on delayed or classroom gains. [+S]; Erroneous examples improve conceptual understanding by forcing comparison with correct models. [+M] (grades 4–5, decimals, tutoring; carried to live modelling)

Multimodal Instruction¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The goal is a principle or concept in a mathematical or physical domain (how a quantity behaves) Add a dynamic or manipulable representation (simulation) and a learner-made sketch beside the symbolic form. Budget for higher effort while learners work. Multiple representations [+M]: one undergraduate electrodynamics course; untested with children or outside physics

Sequencing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is conceptual understanding or transfer (a concept or principle), learners older than the early primary grades Put a short problem-solving attempt before instruction, then teach from learners' attempts; judge it on conceptual and transfer items, not on procedural ones Productive Failure Improves Conceptual Learning [~S] (no difference on procedural knowledge; mostly mathematics and physics)

Structured Academic Controversy¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is concept or principle knowledge of the topic, not argument Put the content check in the source pack and in step 8 (an individual item on the evidence itself). Do not assume discussion delivered the content. structured discussion methods [~M]: only a minority of discussion approaches improved comprehension or critical thinking (general discussion, not SAC)

Transfer of Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is a principle or concept to be used in new cases Pair cases and prompt comparison before stating the principle; test on new cases rather than definitions Analogical Reasoning Improves Transfer [+S] (undergraduates, negotiation; meta-analysis across domains)

Universal Design For Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is a concept or principle Pair a visual and a verbal representation of the same relation and ask learners to translate between them; feedback on the process, not only the answer Multiple representations improve learning [+M] (undergraduate physics, one field study) and Adding relevant pictures or graphics to text improves learning from it, with a small pooled effect on reading comprehension, though whether dual coding explains the benefit is disputed [+S] (comprehension from text with graphics)

Game-based Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The goal is a concept, principle or complex-skill Make the core move the reasoning itself; add a debrief and one non-game transfer task; expect slower gains than for facts untested; from the earlier page

Gamification¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is a concept, principle or complex-skill Award points for correct explanations and for solving changed problems, not for speed; avoid timed, speed-ranked quiz rounds untested; from the earlier page (timed leaderboard formats reward speed and suit fluency more than reasoning)
