Designing for novice¶

Expertise · little or no prior knowledge of the target · All situations

57 rows from 54 pages say what to change when the brief's expertise is novice; 49 of them cite a claim. Pages whose rows cite claims come first. Read the basis before relying on a row: many carry a claim to learners or settings it did not test, and say so.

Sequencing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are absolute novices and the task's parts interact heavily (several decisions at once) (from the wording) Practise the two or three hardest parts in isolation first, then join them in a whole task within the same week Part-task practice reduces cognitive load for absolute novices during initial skill acquisition. [~M] (one experiment; the joining step is untested)
Goal is a procedure, novice learners, an immediate test Show a worked example before the first problem, then alternate example and problem Example–problem sequences reduce cognitive load and improve learning outcomes [+M] (secondary-school novices, circuit troubleshooting, immediate test); the advantage was not detected at the final test with identical pairs (identical pairs [~M])

Universal Design For Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice on this content Curate options to two or three; integrate representations rather than offering many in parallel; model each choice before asking for it Instructional guidance that helps novices can become redundant or counterproductive as expertise grows. [~M] and Multiple representations improve learning [~M] (benefit depends on skill at connecting representations); bounding the number of options is untested
Learners are adult or professionals, including older beginners or learners with disabilities (from the wording) Ask learners which barriers they meet; keep individual accommodations; offer formats drawn from their own work modifications may be necessary for older beginners and learners with disabilities [~W] (one practitioner's judgment, adult ESL)

Authentic Assessment¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice in the domain (first course, first placement) Spend the first half of the unit on component practice with worked examples; use a simpler, well-structured version of the task for the first draft; plan two revision cycles; give feedback on each component before the whole task. Guided rather than unassisted discovery [~S]; its studies did not use authentic assessment tasks, and the meta-analysis does not break results down by prior knowledge

Cognitive Activation¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice for this relation Open with one worked example or short explanation; then a single, narrow "why" prompt per step; give a model answer after each attempt; no open discovery task first. Minimal guidance is less effective for novices than explicit instruction [~S]

Cognitive Flexibility¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Expertise novice in the domain, high element interactivity Start with one case and part tasks; add the second and third case only after the simple version is completed unaided; prompt the named dimension of comparison. Part-task practice reduces cognitive load for absolute novices during initial skill acquisition. [~M]; population not reported on the claim page

Collaborative Evaluation¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice in the domain (cannot yet tell strong from weak work unaided) Use anchor exemplars with known quality, three or four criteria with described levels, teacher modelling each session, and the reference comparison after every round; keep criteria to what learners can see in the object. Delay judging classmates' work until alone judgments of anchors are within a level. Prior knowledge and self-assessment [~M] and self-assessment accuracy [~M], both about judging one's own performance, carried to judging others'

Concept Attainment¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice in the domain (cannot yet name the parts of an instance) Teach what the instances are first; keep two or three candidate attributes and give the checklist; use only clear exemplars until the rule is stable; give feedback on each classification and state the rule soon after the induction rather than waiting for the class to find it. Guided against unguided discovery [~S]; the meta-analysis does not break results down by prior knowledge

Constructive Alignment¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice on the assessed tasks Add a worked or modelled example and at least one practised attempt with feedback per outcome before the graded task; show an exemplar against the criteria. Practice presence [+M] for including practice (undergraduates, one computer-based lesson); the per-outcome dose and exemplar are untested proposals

Criterion- and Norm-Referenced Testing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The decision is what to teach next for novice learners early in a domain Use short criterion-referenced checks on the first skills in the teaching order, three to five items per skill; report mastered / not yet per skill; no percentiles Items should represent essential skills in a teaching hierarchy [+W] (an argument about reading tests, carried to other domains)

Cultural & Life Experiences Connections¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice on the content and have little experience related to it Teach the concept explicitly first, with one familiar context as the example; ask for learners' own contexts only after they can explain the concept once Advance organizers aided learning in a 1980 meta-analysis, but activating prior topic knowledge did not improve primary pupils' text comprehension in one experiment [~M] (carried from topic-knowledge activation in reading to cultural connection)

Debriefing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice on the task (first simulations, first placements) Facilitator-led throughout; give the criterion or a model performance before analysis; ask about cues and steps, not outcomes; name what the expert would have noticed; one change each, stated as an action Process goals lead to better skill acquisition for novices than outcome goals. [+M] (high-school writing revision, carried to debrief focus); Self Assessment Accuracy Is Low Without Training [~M]

Deliberate Practice¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice on this task, at first exposure Do not isolate components yet: model the whole task, use guided practice with support, and set process goals; start targeted refinement only once the learner can perform unaided Instructional guidance that helps novices can become redundant or counterproductive as expertise grows. [~M] (review of cognitive-load studies, carried to skill refinement); process goals for novices [~M]

Develop Understanding¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice on this idea Keep steps 2–3 in full: explain first, two or three worked cases with explanation prompts and sentence starters before any case learners work alone; give hints in response to their explanations. Explicit instruction against unassisted discovery [+S]; the meta-analysis does not break results down by prior knowledge

Digital Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice on the task Use items close to worked examples, the full cue–hint–worked-step ladder, an explanation prompt every three to five items, and feedback on every item Contingent scaffolding improves learning more than fixed or absent support. [~M] (one-to-one tutoring, carried to software)

Dual Coding¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice on this content Supply a complete, labelled visual first, with linking prompts; give construction only as a partly completed diagram Graphic organizers for novices [~M] (lower-ability learners benefited from maps, higher-ability did not, in small, low-powered subsets)

Elaboration Theory¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice and the content is high in element interactivity Keep the epitome very short (two or three ideas); pre-teach the one or two elements it depends on in isolation, then present the epitome; make each level small and end it with a learner-built synthesis. Isolated parts first, for novices [~M] (one experiment; sample and horizon not reported on the claim page)

Engagement¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice on the task and withdraw after early failure Shorten steps, give a worked example first, set process goals; do not add hooks or rewards Process goals lead to better skill acquisition for novices than outcome goals. [~M] (high-school writing revision); the step-size move is untested

Epistemic Games¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice to the practice Open each session with a mentor modelling one decision aloud; one decision type per early session; feedback with the reason after every decision; fade prompts only when a learner justifies unprompted. Guided against unguided discovery [~S], carried from classroom and laboratory studies to games

Experience with Languaging Activities (ELAs) guided by a sequence of experience-based questions¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice in the content, or the text is hard for them Give the meaning of key terms and the gist first; make step 2 a checked account (compare with the text) rather than free recall; give a stem for every step for longer (sessions 1–5); correct each "learned" answer against the named idea. Activation [~S] and activation instructions [~M]: topic activation alone did not help (primary pupils; one other experiment), carried to this routine

Experiential Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novices on the target task Teach or demonstrate the key idea first, with a worked example; narrow the experience to one or two variables; supply the reflection prompts and the vocabulary for naming the concept; check an unaided step before removing support Unassisted discovery produces less learning than explicit instruction, while discovery enhanced with guidance outperforms other instruction [~S]; tested on discovery tasks, school and mixed domains, not on placements

Experiential Learning Cycle¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice on the target task Demonstrate the first loop's task with a worked example before the experience; let one or two variables vary; supply the debrief prompts and the vocabulary for stating the principle; check each learner's rule before the retry; keep this support for at least the first two loops. Guided against unassisted discovery [~S]; discovery tasks with mostly school learners and mixed domains, not placements or simulations; the meta-analysis does not break results down by prior knowledge

Explicit Instruction: Vocabulary¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are second-language learners, novice in the language Gloss unfamiliar words in the text (first-language or picture glosses), teach the most frequent words first, and do not rely on inference from context until most surrounding words are known Glossing yields medium-to-large positive effects on L2 vocabulary learning… [+W] (second-hand report of meta-analyses); Correct word inference in reading requires knowing roughly 95–98% of surrounding words… [~W] (second-hand); Proficiency moderates gloss effects… [~M] (one EFL study: benefits at middle proficiency levels only)

Feedback Loops¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice on the task Run the loop for them: task-level information on every attempt, a matched retry at once, the step re-modelled after two failed cycles Contingent scaffolding [+M] (children, one-to-one, long division)

Fostering Communities of Learning (FCL)¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice in the domain Teach the core ideas directly before research; give each research group a short, pre-selected source pack and a note frame; check each group's understanding before jigsaw. Guided against unguided discovery [~S]: explicit instruction beat unassisted discovery, guided discovery beat other instruction; the meta-analysis does not break results down by prior knowledge, and is not of this pattern

Game-based Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice on the target task Add one or two worked examples or a modelled round before unaided play and again between rounds; keep game controls minimal Worked examples between rounds, puzzle game [~M]; tested with university students, untested with children

Gamification¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novices, or the material is complex Strip theme, animation and decoration from the screen while items are worked; celebrate only between levels Seductive details [~M] (school and university readers, not gamified); cognitive overload [~M]
Learners are uncomfortable choosing (some high power-distance settings, anxious novices) (from the wording) Offer two options with a recommended default rather than an open menu untested; noted in the autonomy claim [~W]'s Discussion with no study recorded

Goal Setting & Monitoring¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice on the task (new procedure, first attempts); or intermediate/advanced Novices: process goals naming the strategy steps, split into proximal sub-goals, outcome goals only once the process runs unprompted. Experienced learners: outcome goals with a stated criterion, which they may set or negotiate (learner-set goals are an untested proposal) Process goals for novices [+M]; tested with high-school writers, untested in other domains

Graphic Organizers¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is conceptual-understanding or transfer from a demanding text, with novice readers (from the wording) Give a complete, author-built organizer with the text rather than asking for construction; add explanation prompts on its links Learner-constructed graphic organizers are not shown to outperform provided ones: the one direct test, with college readers, favoured provided organizers on transfer [~M] (college readers, three experiments; transfer favoured provided organizers)

Holistic Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice and the content is high in element interactivity Keep the first whole to two or three ideas; pre-teach the one or two elements it depends on in a short isolated segment, then present the whole in the same session; make each later part small Part-task practice for novices [~M] (one experiment; sample and horizon not reported); pretraining [+M] (one VR lesson, carried to other content)

Instructor Accessibility¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice on the course's tasks Schedule contact (a booked slot or a required check of the first attempt) rather than waiting to be asked, and answer with prompts before explanations Contingent scaffolding improves learning more than fixed or absent support. [+M] (one-to-one tutoring; not a test of access)

Journaling¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
novice to the content or the practice (first-year students, first placement) Two or three specific prompts per entry with sentence starters; every "I understand" line followed by one check item scored against a key; teacher reply on a rotating sample in the first weeks. Self Assessment Accuracy Is Low Without Training [~M] (secondary and university students); structured reflection [+M] (first-year engineering students, school writing-to-learn)

Knowledge Organization¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice on this topic, holding unconnected facts Give the organization complete at first, with sorting tasks; move to partial frames only after they rebuild the top level from memory A hierarchical internal knowledge organization facilitates performance on complex recall and problem-solving tasks more than a single-level organization of the same knowledge [+M] (college physics, one report)

Learning by Producing (multimedia production as learning)¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice in the content Teach the essential content explicitly first (step 2), provide the sources, use a blueprint template with one scene filled in, fix the product to 2 minutes, and check content individually before production. Minimal guidance and novices [~S]; tested on discovery and inquiry, carried to production

Match the form and content of staff development interventions to clients' assessed Stages of Concern¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Most people have not used the innovation yet (novice for this innovation) Lead with clear information, visits and examples of the innovation in use, and individual reassurance; hold back detailed logistics and student-outcome data until use begins concerns shifted from nonuser towards user profiles after a two-week workshop [+M] (one science curriculum, elementary teachers, no comparison group)

Memory Consolidation¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice on this material Keep the opening recall close to how it was taught, with answers shown at once; group the material into a few units before recall; plan returns at short gaps first Chunking reduces working memory load by grouping information into fewer, more meaningful units. [~S] (reviews and lab experiments on immediate memory; carried to recall planning, untested); gap choice in Spaced Learning

Motivation¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice on the task and withdraw after early failure Treat expectancy as the low component: an item they can do first, process goals, feedback naming the strategy that worked; hold back rewards and hooks Process goals lead to better skill acquisition for novices than outcome goals. [+M] (high-school writing revision); self-efficacy predicts persistence [+M] (predictive reviews, not a test of the move)

Multimodal Instruction¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice on this content and unfamiliar with its representations; or advanced Novice: use words plus one visual type. Teach how to read it first (what the axes, arrows or symbols mean), add one explicit translation prompt per idea, and hold back a second visual type until learners can read the first. Advanced: cut to the most efficient representation, drop duplicating modes, and use translation tasks for checking rather than teaching. Novice: multiple representations [~M] (the review ties the benefit to representational competence). Advanced: untested proposal (the claim pages discuss expertise reversal; no entry here tests it for modes)

Multiple Methods of Assessment¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice on the goal and new to judging quality Teacher-chosen methods; criteria with an example of each level; postpone learner choice of format and self-assessment that counts until learners have practised judging two or three examples Self Assessment Accuracy Is Low Without Training [~M] (secondary students, one randomised experiment; a higher-education meta-analysis read as an abstract)

Positive Self-talk¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice on a new skill with steps (a procedure, a technique) Write instructional cues that name the steps, as process phrases; model and rehearse them; prompt at the step that fails Self Talk Improves Learning And Performance [+S] (sport; larger on novel tasks); Process goals lead to better skill acquisition for novices than outcome goals. [~M] (writing revision, about goals)

Pre-reading Questioning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Readers are novice on the topic and will know almost none of the answers Keep the attempt-before-reading step; tell them most guesses will be wrong; keep to two or three questions Pretesting enhances learning [+M] (undergraduates, items answered at floor, laboratory)

Procedural Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The procedure has many interacting steps and learners are novice Teach the hardest steps in isolation for one or two short rounds, then join them on a simple whole task in the same or next session; never leave the parts unjoined Part-task practice reduces cognitive load for absolute novices during initial skill acquisition. [+M]; joining: whole-task argument [~W] (no experiment)

Process-based Writing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Peers are novices as reviewers (from the wording) Train them on example texts with the criteria first; ask them to point to the place in the text and say what a reader would experience; teacher checks a sample peer reviewer accuracy and helpfulness [~M] (secondary writers, correlational); rubrics and peer reviewers [~M] (online, two quasi-experiments; one found less reflective comments)

Reflection¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
novice to the task (first placements, first problem sets) Give a rubric row or an exemplar pair before asking for evaluation; prompts name process steps ("which step?") rather than "how did it go?"; check a sample of self-ratings against a scored result before relying on them. Self-assessment accuracy [~M]; tested with secondary and university students

Self-monitoring¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice on the task, or low achievers Use a printed process checklist, checks that are tests (answer a question, rework a step) not ratings, every record co-checked in the first two sessions, and more sessions at the shared stage before hand-over Prior Knowledge Needed For Accurate Self Assessment [~M] (synthesis of 22 meta-analyses; undergraduates' exam predictions); Process goals lead to better skill acquisition for novices than outcome goals. [~M] (writing revision, high school)

Structured Peer Review¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Reviewers are novice in the genre or domain Run collaborative evaluation on two anchors first; give three criteria with described levels and sentence frames; one draft per reviewer; the teacher reads and returns a sample of comments each cycle; keep the teacher's own comments on content accuracy. Rater training [+M] (moderator across 58 studies, roles pooled); rubric guidance [+M] (MOOC writers; graduate students)

Summative Assessment¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice on the format (first exam of this kind, first-year students, a new kind of task) Add a full practice run in the end-point's format a week before, with worked answers; keep the first summative of the course lower in weight Classroom quizzing delivered by clickers, computer software, or paper improves student performance on classroom exams in middle school and college courses [+M] for quizzing during the course; the practice run itself is untested

Transfer of Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice on this task, or the task is high in element interactivity Secure the taught case first with worked examples and guided practice; start with two cases, not four; practise parts briefly before integrating; delay mixing until each type has been introduced Part-task practice reduces cognitive load for absolute novices during initial skill acquisition. [~M] (load, not transfer); interleaving [~M] (claim page notes novel material can reverse the benefit)

Blended Learning¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice in the content Keep each online exposition segment short, with an embedded check and worked material, and spend the in-person time on guided practice with feedback before independent work. untested proposal

Error Analysis¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
novice: no correct model of this task yet Do not open with errors. Teach or show one or two correct worked examples first; add one erroneous example per misconception only once the learner can follow a correct one. untested; from the earlier page

Flipped Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice on the topic and the material has many interacting parts Keep the first explanation live, or use a worked-example video with a pause-and-try item after each step; flip only the later, more practised topics; give the opening check before any independent application untested; Active learning improves exam performance [~S] is a course-level average that does not separate novices; the guidance limit for novices is on Active Learning

Online Course Design (Community of Inquiry)¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice in the content Put more of each week into the content strand, with worked examples and self-checks, and keep discussion tasks to applying what was just shown; give feedback on every early piece. Untested here. The SRL synthesis [+M] does not break results down by prior knowledge

Social Interdependence¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice on the content, or members differ widely in what they know Teach and model the content first; use interdependence for practice and explanation, not first acquisition; set improvement-based team scores so low starters can contribute untested proposal; improvement-based scoring from the Discussion of group rewards combined with individual accountability [~M], not from its entries

Social Presence¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
novice learners on the content, afraid of being wrong in public Have them post first in their small group or as a draft that peers comment on before the whole class sees it; the instructor replies to the attempt, not only to the correct posts untested proposal

Structured Academic Controversy¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Expertise novice on the topic (cannot name evidence on either side) Lengthen preparation (a prior reading, or 20+ minutes with a structured source pack per side) and give an argument frame. Shorten open discussion. Defer SAC until a short explicit input on the evidence has been given. untested proposal; argumentation improves reasoning [~M] names prior knowledge as an open question on its claim page, not a tested moderator
