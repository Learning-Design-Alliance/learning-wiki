Designing for online-self-paced¶

Setting · digital, learner-paced, no live instructor · All situations

64 rows from 62 pages say what to change when the brief's setting is online-self-paced; 37 of them cite a claim. Pages whose rows cite claims come first. Read the basis before relying on a row: many carry a claim to learners or settings it did not test, and say so.

Blended Learning¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The online part is online-self-paced and learners have little experience of it Add planning prompts, a visible schedule with intermediate deadlines and self-monitoring checks; review them at the next in-person session. SRL interventions in online and blended environments [+M], pooled across 15 studies, populations not reported on the claim page
No teacher present between sessions, or a large group (from the wording) Monitor completion each week, contact non-starters within the first week or two, and keep a named person learners can ask. Online continuing-education persistence [~W], fully online adult continuing education, associational; untested for blends

Authentic Assessment¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher present Give the rubric and scored exemplars up front; have learners score an exemplar and see the expert's score before self-assessing; use structured peer review in a timed window; reserve teacher or practitioner judgement for the final product. Peer assessment [+M] (robust to online or offline delivery in the meta-analysis); self-assessment training [~S]; carried to authentic tasks untested

Cognitive Activation¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher present Put the prompt in the material with a required typed answer before the model explanation is revealed; the learner then compares the two. Self-explanation prompts improve learning from worked examples [+M] (prompting is tested; the reveal-after-commit step is untested)

Cognitive Flexibility¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting online-self-paced or hypertext Give a guided route through the cases (a theme per pass) rather than free browsing, and build the comparison prompts into the pages. Students in the flexible hypertext-based course design scored higher on achievement tests (76%) than students in the direct online course design (59%) [~W]: groups studied different software, so it does not isolate the design; navigation burden is untested, from the theory page

Constructive Alignment¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting is online-self-paced with no teacher present Build practice with immediate feedback into each module ahead of its assessed task; make criteria and exemplars part of the module; audit automatically scored items for recall-only questions. Practice presence [+M] (computer-based lesson, undergraduates); the audit is untested

Debriefing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no facilitator Replace the live debrief with a written self-debrief: three to five specific questions tied to the criteria, then a model performance or expert commentary to compare with, then one change for the next module, shown back at its start Reflective Practice Improves Outcomes When Structured [+M] (classroom engineering course); Metacognitive prompts improve learning [+M] (computer-based environments)

Deliberate Practice¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting is online-self-paced, no coach present Give items with automatic checking or a worked answer to compare; have the learner score against annotated exemplars and keep an error log; require one whole-task submission per session reviewed by a person or a rubric-based check Self-monitoring improves self-regulation and supports better learning decisions. [+M] (reviews; untested within deliberate practice or online)

Develop Understanding¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher Make every prompt require a typed explanation before the next screen; give worked explanations to compare against; include automatically checked prediction items with feedback; end with an unseen changed item. Practice present in a computer-based lesson [+M] (undergraduates, one lesson, horizon not reported); the explanation-comparison step is untested

Digital Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting is online-self-paced, no teacher present Add the contact task in week 1, a fixed weekly rhythm with a deadline, visible progress against a criterion, and a weekly plan prompt; check logins by day 3 Self-regulated learning interventions have a moderate effect (0.65) on learning outcomes in online and blended environments [+M] (15 studies); a diary alone did not help in one course (A daily learning diary alone (Group D) did not produce statistically significant pre-post gains on any measured outcome in an online mathematics preparation course [~M])

Dual Coding¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher present Build drawing templates, a model drawing to compare with, and a self-check list into the material Drawing in STEM [+M] (online drawing benefited more from instructor-provided scaffolds than paper-based drawing)

Elaboration Theory¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher present Keep the epitome as a persistent map on every page, with the current level highlighted; make each synthesis an activity the system checks (arrange items into the map, mark the new detail), not a passage to read. Learners explicating the organisation [+M] for the synthesis activity (same 20-student high-school experiment; untested online); the persistent map is an untested proposal

Error Analysis¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher present The system must supply the correct solution and the explanation; log error streaks and self-reported confusion, and switch to a worked example after repeated errors on one item type. confusion with prior incorrect trials [~W] (exploratory); the switch rule is an untested proposal

Experience with Languaging Activities (ELAs) guided by a sequence of experience-based questions¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher Embed the four prompts after each segment; after the learner commits a step 4 answer, show a model answer and ask them to mark what they missed; adapt the stem to the content of the segment. Metacognitive prompts [+S]: in computer-based environments prompts helped, more so with feedback, task-specific and adaptive (meta-analysis read from its abstract)

Experiential Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no facilitator present Replace the live debrief with specific written prompts after each task and a model answer to compare against; let learners retry a changed task with automatic feedback Reflective Practice Improves Outcomes When Structured [+M] for specific written prompts (engineering undergraduates, in a classroom course); untested online

Experiential Learning Cycle¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no facilitator Replace the live debrief with three written prompts after each task and a model answer to compare against; offer an automatically scored changed task as the retry. Structured reflection [+M] for written specific prompts (a classroom engineering course); untested online

Explicit Instruction: Vocabulary¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting is online-self-paced, phone-only, or no teacher present Build each word as a short card (meaning, example, picture, audio) with recall prompts scheduled by the app over two to three weeks; put the words into short readings or audio inside the course; ask for one typed or spoken use per word Supplemental computer-based spaced repetition… [+M] (EFL cadets, alongside classroom teaching, not instead of it)

Feedback Loops¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher present Build automatically checked items with a retry on a new item after each piece of information; route a learner who fails twice to a worked example; keep a delayed recheck in a later session Adaptive learning improves outcomes [+S] (intelligent tutoring beat large-group, textbook and other computer instruction, not human tutoring or small groups)

Goal Setting & Monitoring¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher present Build goal setting and an if-then plan into the first session (a short writing activity); show progress against the goal on every return; do not expect the prompt to help every learner Implementation intentions [~M] (course completion rose for learners in individualist cultures, not collectivist)

Graphic Organizers¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting is online-self-paced, no teacher present Place the organizer next to the text it summarises (not on a separate page); give partial organizers with a reference version revealed after the learner submits; add one explanation question per organizer Adding relevant pictures or graphics to text improves learning from it, with a small pooled effect on reading comprehension, though whether dual coding explains the benefit is disputed [+M] (graphics added to text helped reading comprehension, elementary to adult; carried to organizers, which it did not isolate); the reveal-and-compare step is untested

Guided Practice¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting is online-self-paced, no teacher present Build practice items with automatic checking and feedback after each item into the lesson; do not cut them to save time; add an unaided check item before the next topic In one experiment with 256 undergraduates, computer-based lesson versions that included practice produced significantly higher posttest scores than versions without practice [+M] (undergraduates, one computer-based lesson; horizon not reported)

Holistic Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher present Keep the map on every screen with today's part highlighted; make the closing placement an arrange-or-label activity the system checks; add a redraw-from-memory item from the middle of the course Learner-built organisation [+M] (classroom study, untested online); concept mapping [~S] for the redraw; the persistent map is untested

Journaling¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher present Put the prompts in the platform at the end of each module, tied to that module; require a "next step" line; show the learner their last "next step" at the start of the next module; pair the diary with short self-regulation training. training with diary [~M] and diary alone [-M] (prospective university students, online mathematics preparation; no mathematics gain either way)

Memory Consolidation¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Online, self-paced, or phone-only (from the wording) Put the returns in the app's queue or a dated reminder, with automatic checking; show a delayed check, not a streak, as the progress signal Learners Misjudge Spacing Benefits [+M] (adults, lab and flashcard software: learners prefer massing, so do not leave scheduling to them)

Motivation¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced and voluntary (an open online course) Do not open with a test on material not yet taught, or make it optional and say errors are expected; track return days and completion, not clicks; build the four questions into the first activity Pre-tests reduced adult learners' persistence in a MOOC, though they improved post-test scores among those who completed it [~M] (one randomized MOOC experiment, abstract); the rest untested

Multimodal Instruction¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, learner-paced media On-screen text beside a static visual is acceptable. Give pause, replay and a text version. Do not convert all text to audio. Modality [~M]: its advantage shrinks when learners control pacing
One session only, or no teacher present (from the wording) Use one representation pair, not a full set. Build the translation prompts and their model answers into the material, so a learner without a teacher can check a sketch or explanation. untested proposal

Note-taking¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting is online-self-paced video Put the pause points into the video (a prompt screen at each break); give a downloadable outline for the first modules; end each module with a short recall quiz learners answer before reopening their notes Pauses and outline: untested here. Quiz: Retrieval Practice Improves Long Term Retention [+S] (carried to reviewing notes)

Online Course Design (Community of Inquiry)¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced with rolling enrolment, or one teacher for hundreds of learners Replace teacher feedback on each piece with automatic feedback on self-checks, a weekly teacher summary of common errors, and peer review against a rubric; keep the week-1 if-then plan; give the teacher's time to learners flagged by a missed week. Implementation intentions [+S] (the two field experiments were in large online courses); the rest is untested

Pre-reading Questioning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced course or module, no teacher present Keep pre-questions to two, framed as a puzzle with no score; show the answer straight after the relevant section; track drop-off at the pre-question screen and remove it if completion falls Pre-tests reduced adult learners' persistence in a MOOC, though they improved post-test scores among those who completed it [-M] (399 adults in a MOOC: pre-tests reduced persistence; post-test gains only among those who finished)

Procedural Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher present, or phone-only Build the worked example and five to eight practice items with automatic checking and a worked answer after each into the lesson; one step per screen on a phone; an unaided check item before the next procedure; scheduled review prompts two days and one week later practice included in computer-based lessons [+M] (undergraduates, one lesson; horizon not reported); schedule untested

Process-based Writing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced or asynchronous course, no teacher reading every draft Build in a rubric-guided peer review with practice on rated examples, a reviewer memo with each revision, and a self-check against the criteria before submission rubrics and peer reviewers [~M] (one MOOC writing course, one online graduate course); Peer assessment improves academic performance by a small average amount [+M] (online and offline did not differ)

Reflection¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher present Use task-specific prompts tied to that week's content, require a written next step, and open the next module by showing it back; flag entries that repeat word for word. Structured reflection [+M]; tested in a classroom engineering course, untested self-paced

Self-monitoring¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting is online-self-paced, no teacher present Build the checks into the software as task-specific prompts at natural pauses, each followed by an item with automatic feedback that shows the learner whether their judgement was right; do not rely on a free-text diary Metacognitive prompts improve learning [+M] (meta-analysis in computer-based environments, g = 0.40 on learning outcomes, larger with feedback, task-specific and adaptive prompts); diary alone [-M]

Social Interdependence¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting is online-self-paced or asynchronous online groups Do not rely on assigned roles or a group reward to raise achievement; require each member to post a stated number of contributions that respond to others', and assess individually Interdependence type did not affect posttest achievement [~M] (280 undergraduate business majors, triads; non-significant, no effect size); more interactions went with higher posttest scores [+W] (same study, correlational)

Structured Peer Review¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-instructor-led or online-self-paced, asynchronous Use a tool that assigns drafts at random, hides authors' names, locks a review once submitted and shows the author all reviews together; a calibration anchor before the first review; a deadline for the revision plan. Assessor meta-analysis [+M]: computer-mediated peer assessment did better than paper; peer assessment [+M] found no online/offline difference. The tool rules are untested

Transfer of Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting is online-self-paced, no teacher present Build the comparison prompt and the principle statement into the materials; use automatically checked retrieval questions in changed formats; add an unprompted transfer item before the next unit Retrieval Practice Improves Transfer [~S] (lab and prose-passage studies; carried to self-paced courses untested)

Universal Design For Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting is online-self-paced, no teacher present Build the access layers into the media (editable text, captions and transcripts, pause and replay, glossary links); give automatic, criterion-referenced feedback on each product format Letting learners pace instruction helped on complex material in one experiment [~M] (primary-school animation and a meta-analysis of learner control; achievement gain not general); the rest untested

Ask Experts¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting is online-self-paced, no expert live Use an asynchronous question queue: each question carries the learner's attempt; the expert answers the commonest ones with a recorded think-aloud and the rest in writing within a set time (say two working days); add an adaptive tutor or worked-feedback items for routine steps untested proposal; human and intelligent tutoring had similar effects over no tutoring in one review read from its abstract (tutoring claim [~M])

Concept Attainment¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher The system gives the yes/no label after the learner commits a written hypothesis; show the hypothesis history beside the items; end with a classification set with feedback and a stated definition. Untested proposal

Criterion- and Norm-Referenced Testing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Self-paced mobile study for school-age learners with no live teacher (from the wording) Teach the difference with short worked examples and a tap-to-sort activity (statements sorted into "tells you what I can do" and "tells you how I rank"), and assess with auto-scored items against a stated criterion; do not ask learners to write test items on a phone untested proposal

Cultural & Life Experiences Connections¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting is online-self-paced, no teacher present Give prompts that ask the learner to write their own example and explain the concept in it, with a bank of worked examples from several contexts; require no public sharing untested

Culturally Responsive Classroom Norms¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher present Leave response windows open rather than timed; give discussion-board options to post after reading others or to reply privately; state the platform's conventions on help and copying in plain terms untested proposal

Engagement¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting is online-self-paced, no teacher present Replace the greeting with a named welcome and first-minute check-in; build a visible progress record and automatic learning checks; track return days, not only clicks untested; Intervention engagement (narratives consumed) showed no clear association with outcome changes [~W] warns that usage counts need not track outcomes (a mental-health app, not learning)

Epistemic Cognition¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher present Provide recorded demonstrations of the expert move, live practice items with model reasoning shown after the learner commits a judgment and a confidence rating, and a delayed check on new items. Untested proposal; the online-argumentation synthesis does not report teacher presence

Epistemic Games¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher Mentor characters must carry all guidance: a modelled decision before each new decision type, required written justification, scripted feedback with reasons, and a written debrief prompt at the end of each session. Expect weaker debriefs than with a person. Untested proposal; feedback [+S] carried to software feedback

Evaluating Sources¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher present Short recorded demonstrations of the off-page move; practice items where the learner commits a verdict, a reason and a confidence rating before a model answer and the fact-checker's search are shown; a delayed check on new sites Untested; all studies were taught in classes

Flexible Grouping¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no live teacher Replace live regrouping with branching: a short check routes each learner to a support module or an extension; asynchronous discussion groups are assigned by the course for mixing, and change each unit untested here; individualised routing is modelled in Adaptive Learning

Fostering Communities of Learning (FCL)¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced or phone-only, no live sessions Replace live jigsaw with asynchronous teaching: each expert posts a short explanation and two questions; peers answer and the expert replies; an automatic individual quiz on every part decides progress. Untested proposal; learning by teaching [~M] found interactive teaching better than non-interactive, so expect less benefit without live questions

Game-based Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher present Put the modelling, hints and debrief questions inside the game (a worked example screen between levels, a "why did that work?" prompt after each level); log attempts per level to find where learners stall untested proposal; the tested child gains had an adult present

Gamification¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher present The software must give the informational message after each item (step 3) and run the level checks (step 4); if streaks are used, allow a missed day without losing the streak, so one absence does not end practice untested proposal; the meta-analysis pooled informal and online settings with school ones

Knowledge Organization¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher present Use drag-and-drop sorting into the organization with automatic checking, then a closed-book rebuild the learner compares with a reference map; do not drop the rebuild to save time untested; the closed-book rebuild rests on The same quizzing or concept-mapping activity produces more learning when done without viewing the material, as retrieval, than while viewing it [+M] (second-hand, settings not stated)

Modeling¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting is online-self-paced, or video only Record short clips, one decision each (about 2–5 minutes), each ending in a predict-or-explain question the learner must answer before the next clip; follow with automatically checked practice items untested proposal; the explanation prompt is carried from Prompting learners to self-explain… [+S]

Multiple Methods of Assessment¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher present Pair auto-scored items with one short constructed or recorded response per goal, reviewed by a peer with the criteria or sampled by a tutor; have the learner self-assess against the criteria with an example first untested proposal; self-assessment limit from Self Assessment Accuracy Is Low Without Training [~M]

Observation/Shadowing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, recordings only, no one to debrief Short authentic clips (5–15 minutes) each with two focus questions the learner must answer before going on, then a written expert account of the clip's decisions to compare with; one replay of the clip after the comparison untested proposal; the explanation prompt is carried from Prompting learners to self-explain improves understanding and problem solving on immediate tests by a moderate average amount, with little evidence yet on delayed or classroom gains. [+S]

Phonics¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
No teacher present: an app or self-paced programme (from the wording) Choose or build software that models every sound in audio, has the learner blend before revealing the word, checks reading aloud (speech recognition or a recorded sample a person reviews), and gates new correspondences on the weekly check untested proposal; no claim here tests phonics delivered without a teacher

Positive Self-talk¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher present Show the learner's own cue at the point where errors or long pauses occur (after a wrong answer, after a set idle time), with the next action as a button; let the learner edit their cues Untested proposal

Sequencing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no teacher present Build the prerequisite check and the move-on rule into the software or the materials: each stage ends with two unaided items that unlock the next or route back untested proposal

Social Emotional Learning¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, no adult present Use short video models and private reflection prompts; ask learners to log one real use of the skill a week; add a human contact (tutor message, peer partner) where possible, since coaching and modelling in the moment cannot happen. Untested proposal

Strengths-based Approach¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting is online-self-paced, or learners work on phones only Build the strengths record into the platform: a short self-report on what has worked, and feedback that names the specific item the learner got right and the next item it unlocks; keep each entry to a few lines untested proposal

Structured Academic Controversy¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting online-self-paced, or no teacher present Run it asynchronously over days-weeks: positions posted, restatements as replies that the original author must accept, reversal posts, then a joint document. Grade the restatement and the individual position, not post counts. A shared misconception and a caricatured reversal will go unseen, so add a facilitator check before the synthesis. untested proposal

Summative Assessment¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting is online-self-paced, phone-only, or some learners remote while others sit in the room Give remote and in-room learners the same tasks, time and permitted aids; prefer open-book items that ask for application to a new case over recall that can be looked up; follow a sample of submissions with a short oral check of the learner's own work; make tasks readable on a phone screen untested proposal

Text-to-Speech¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Setting is online-self-paced, no teacher present Build the speech control into every page and question, put a question or summary prompt at each section break with automatic checking where possible, and add the periodic with/without check from the default arc untested proposal

Wise Feedback Across Racial and Ethnic Difference¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
online-self-paced, automated feedback, no instructor present A system cannot credibly state personal belief; state the standard, the specific gap and the next step, and say that learners at this point commonly reach the standard on revision (only if your data show it); route learners who stall to a person untested; carried from the belonging message's "common and passing" framing (Belonging Interventions Improve Outcomes [~M])
