Designing for procedure¶

Kind of goal · a step sequence to carry out · All situations

24 rows from 24 pages say what to change when the brief's kind of goal is procedure; 18 of them cite a claim. Pages whose rows cite claims come first. Read the basis before relying on a row: many carry a claim to learners or settings it did not test, and say so.

Ask Experts¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is a concept, principle or transfer, not only a procedure Have the expert ask and prompt more and explain less; include one changed problem in each consultation for the learner to reason through aloud Contingent scaffolding improves learning more than fixed or absent support. [+M] (interactive tutoring gave better transfer with similar immediate scores) and Human tutoring has a medium effect over no tutoring, and in one small study students learned as well when tutors only prompted them as when tutors also explained and gave feedback [~M] (eighth graders, circulatory system)

Blended Learning¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The goal is a complex-skill or procedure that needs practice with feedback Reserve most in-person time for learners carrying out the task with feedback, not for exposition; move the exposition online. Active learning improves exam performance [+M], tested against lecture in undergraduate STEM courses, not within a blend

Cognitive Activation¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The goal is a procedure Compare two solution methods side by side and ask when each is better; expect gains in using and choosing methods, and check conceptual understanding separately. Comparing contrasting cases improves learning [~M] (one algebra study: procedural and flexibility gains, no added conceptual gain)

Cognitive Flexibility¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Age adolescent, goal a mathematics procedure Have learners compare alternative solution methods side by side; expect gains in choosing methods, and add a separate item asking why each method works. Comparing cases side by side improves learning and transfer by a moderate average amount, though in one algebra study it raised procedural knowledge and flexibility but not conceptual knowledge [~M]; tested with seventh-graders, procedural flexibility rose, conceptual knowledge did not

Cultural & Life Experiences Connections¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is a procedure or verbal-association assessed on a standard test Keep connections to one opening example and a short weekly utility-value reflection; do not cut practice time for them Learners invest more effort in tasks they perceive as interesting, personally important, or useful to their future goals. [+M] (reviews of utility-value writing; the trade-off with practice is untested)

Culturally Responsive Classroom Norms¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is recall or fluency (verbal-association, procedure) rather than reasoning Choral and quick-fire response are fine for well-practised content; keep long wait time and pair talk for questions that need reasoning Questioning Strategies Improve Learning [+M] (higher-cognitive questions and wait time; not tested by culture)

Debriefing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is a procedure or psychomotor skill in workplace-clinical training Treat the debrief as the feedback step of a repeated attempt: debrief against a stated mastery standard, then rerun the scenario or a changed one until the standard is met Simulation Based Education With Deliberate Practice Improves Clinical Outcomes [+M] (14 studies, health professions); Simulation Based Education Improves Outcomes [~M]: could not isolate feedback as the active feature

Deliberate Practice¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is a procedure or psychomotor skill in workplace-clinical training Use a simulator or part-task trainer with a mastery standard per step, immediate expert or device feedback, repetition until the standard is met and rising difficulty; add booster sessions later Simulation-based education with deliberate practice improves clinical outcomes [+M] (14 studies, health professions, procedural skills, against traditional clinical education)

Digital Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is a procedure or verbal-association with checkable answers Put the bulk of practice online with automatic checking; use an intelligent tutor if one exists for the domain Adaptive learning improves outcomes [+S] (tutoring-system meta-analyses; gains smaller on standardised than on locally developed tests)

Experiential Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Adult professionals, workplace-clinical, a procedure or psychomotor skill Replace one-off exposure with simulated practice repeated to a stated mastery standard, immediate feedback after each attempt, rising difficulty, and a debrief; add booster sessions if the skill is not used soon Simulation Based Education With Deliberate Practice [+M]; medical education, procedural skills only; boosters untested

Experiential Learning Cycle¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
workplace-clinical, a procedure or psychomotor skill Run the experience as simulated practice repeated to a stated mastery standard, feedback after each attempt, rising difficulty, then a debrief; move to the real setting only when the standard is met. Simulation with deliberate practice [+M]; medical education, procedural skills only

Goal Setting & Monitoring¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is facts or a procedure with a clear score A specific numeric target with frequent progress checks is reasonable (e.g. "18 of 20 items correct on Friday's check") Specific, difficult goals [+W]; work-task evidence, untested for learning

Guided Practice¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is a concept, principle or transfer, not only a procedure Replace some feedback-giving with prompts that ask the learner to explain or predict the next step; include one changed item in each round Human tutoring has a medium effect over no tutoring, and in one small study students learned as well when tutors only prompted them as when tutors also explained and gave feedback [~M] (eighth graders, 11 tutor–student pairs: prompting without feedback learned as well)

Memory Consolidation¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is a procedure or motor skill, not facts or vocabulary Reactivate by doing the procedure, not by recalling a description of it; for motor skills use shorter gaps than for verbal material and check on the whole performance Spaced Practice Improves Retention [~S] (one meta-analysis summarised there: longer lags helped verbal tasks up to a point but reduced effects for skill tasks such as typing and music)

Modeling¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is a concept or principle, or transfer, not only a procedure Model on two items that differ on the surface; at each pause ask why, not what; include one error and its repair, and ask learners what gave it away Prompting learners to self-explain improves understanding and problem solving on immediate tests by a moderate average amount, with little evidence yet on delayed or classroom gains. [+S]; Erroneous examples improve conceptual understanding by forcing comparison with correct models. [+M] (grades 4–5, decimals, tutoring; carried to live modelling)

Sequencing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is a procedure, novice learners, an immediate test Show a worked example before the first problem, then alternate example and problem Example–problem sequences reduce cognitive load and improve learning outcomes [+M] (secondary-school novices, circuit troubleshooting, immediate test); the advantage was not detected at the final test with identical pairs (identical pairs [~M])

Supporting Gifted and Talented Students¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The target is cumulative (procedure, principle, as in mathematics or a language's grammar) Prefer acceleration: move to the next unit's content once the pre-check is passed, through adaptive or self-paced material with a teacher check each week Adaptive learning improves outcomes [+M] (general populations, not gifted students); expertise reversal [+M] carried from training studies

Transfer of Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is choosing among several procedures (which method fits which problem) Mix problem types in practice once each is introduced, so the learner must decide which applies; expect slower, error-prone practice Interleaving category examples improves inductive category learning for visual and mathematical materials, but not for expository texts or word categories [~M] (classification of new examples; smaller for mathematics, reversed for word categories)

Develop Understanding¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The goal is a procedure the learner must carry out fluently, not an idea to explain Use the Direct Instruction pattern or Guided Practice instead; keep one explanation prompt per worked case at most. Untested; from the earlier page's scope ("not sufficient for highly complex skills alone")

Multimodal Instruction¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The goal is a procedure or physical action (skill-performance) Show it (a demonstration or video) with spoken steps, and have learners perform it. Keep a short written step list to consult. untested; from the earlier page (demonstration and gesture are untested here)

Multiple Methods of Assessment¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The goal is verbal-association or a procedure One well-sampled method (many short items) is usually enough; add a second method only to check that a correct answer is not recognition alone untested proposal

Supporting Students with Intellectual Disabilities¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The target is a daily-life procedure (money, time, travel) Teach it as a task analysis of 4–8 steps in its real setting, practise until three consecutive unaided successes on two days, then fade to weekly review untested proposal; mastery pacing (Mastery learning improves outcomes [+M]) carried from general classes

Universal Design For Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is a procedure or complex-skill whose format is the goal (handwriting, oral fluency, a clinical technique) Keep the expression format fixed; vary only the routes into the content and the practice supports untested; from the earlier page (options in how learners engage and access, not in what they are expected to learn)

Validity, Reliability, and Bias in Classroom Assessment¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The goal is verbal-association or a procedure recalled or carried out quickly Use many short selected-response or short-answer items for breadth and reliability; keep the language plain; add a few items that ask for the procedure on a changed case if transfer matters untested; from the earlier page (item-count arithmetic, selected-response trade-off)
