Designing for verbal-association¶

Kind of goal · facts, vocabulary, paired associates, names · All situations

19 rows from 19 pages say what to change when the brief's kind of goal is verbal-association; 13 of them cite a claim. Pages whose rows cite claims come first. Read the basis before relying on a row: many carry a claim to learners or settings it did not test, and say so.

Cognitive Flexibility¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal verbal-association (facts, terms), or recall-weighted high-stakes assessment Do not make criss-crossing the main activity; give facts their own retrieval practice, and use varied cases only for the transfer part of the objective. Presenting multiple cases from different perspectives supports transfer in ill-structured domains [~M]: the drill group recalled more facts

Constructive Alignment¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is facts or recall (verbal-association) and the assessment is recall Do not restrict practice to recall: include some questions at a higher level (explain, apply) in class practice. HOCS clickers [~M] (one nonmajors biology course, controlled-nonrandom)

Cultural & Life Experiences Connections¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is a procedure or verbal-association assessed on a standard test Keep connections to one opening example and a short weekly utility-value reflection; do not cut practice time for them Learners invest more effort in tasks they perceive as interesting, personally important, or useful to their future goals. [+M] (reviews of utility-value writing; the trade-off with practice is untested)

Culturally Responsive Classroom Norms¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is recall or fluency (verbal-association, procedure) rather than reasoning Choral and quick-fire response are fine for well-practised content; keep long wait time and pair talk for questions that need reasoning Questioning Strategies Improve Learning [+M] (higher-cognitive questions and wait time; not tested by culture)

Deliberate Practice¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is recall of facts or vocabulary (verbal-association) Use spaced retrieval practice with corrective feedback instead of component drills Desirable difficulties enhance learning [~S] (spaced retrieval beats massed; interleaving helps some materials and not words); see Retrieval Practice

Digital Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is a procedure or verbal-association with checkable answers Put the bulk of practice online with automatic checking; use an intelligent tutor if one exists for the domain Adaptive learning improves outcomes [+S] (tutoring-system meta-analyses; gains smaller on standardised than on locally developed tests)

Dual Coding¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is verbal-association (vocabulary, names, facts), including second-language vocabulary Pair each new word with a picture and a short text gloss; for students with learning disabilities, teach a keyword-image mnemonic L2: text-plus-picture glosses [+W] (one study of 90 Iranian EFL learners, immediate recall, reported second-hand). Disabilities: mnemonic instruction [+M] (mnemonics broadly, not only pictorial ones)

Elaboration Theory¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The goal is verbal-association (facts, vocabulary, names) Do not build an elaboration sequence; use a plain grouping and retrieval practice instead. No detected effect on tasks resting on single details [~M] (a non-significant difference, not equivalence)

Error Analysis¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
goal is verbal-association (facts, vocabulary, terms) Skip erroneous-example analysis. Use retrieval attempts followed by the correct answer after every error; always give feedback on multiple-choice items. corrective feedback after failed retrieval [+M]; multiple-choice feedback [+M]

Game-based Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The goal is verbal-association (vocabulary, facts) A quiz or practice game is a reasonable vehicle; add glosses or supplementary word material inside it, and test recall a week later, not only at the end of play Kahoot! and outdoor games with migrant children [+W] (confounded); adventure game with supplementary material [+W] (second-hand)

Gamification¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is verbal-association (second-language vocabulary, terms) A quiz or points layer is a reasonable vehicle; test recall a week after the last session, not only in the app Mangomon app, 21 Thai undergraduates [+W] (pre-post, no control); Quizizz [+W] and mixed-methods study [+W] (both known only through one review)

Holistic Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The goal is verbal-association (facts, names, vocabulary) Do not build a whole-first sequence; group the items plainly and use retrieval practice No effect on local tasks [~M] (non-significant, not equivalence)

Supporting Students with Intellectual Disabilities¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The target is facts or vocabulary (verbal-association) Add a keyword or acrostic cue the student can decode, practise retrieving through it, and review it at spaced intervals Mnemonic instruction [+M] (includes mild developmental disabilities; recall outcomes, abstracts)

Blended Learning¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The goal is mostly facts or vocabulary (verbal-association) Put spaced, self-checked practice online with immediate answers; keep in-person time for using the material and for checks of what was missed. untested proposal

Cognitive Activation¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The goal is facts or vocabulary (verbal-association) Do not build explanation or comparison tasks around isolated facts; use retrieval with feedback, and keep activation for the relations among the facts. untested proposal

Journaling¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is verbal-association (vocabulary, facts) Shorten the entry to one line ("which items did I miss, and what will I do about them?") and spend the time on retrieval practice with feedback. untested proposal

Multiple Methods of Assessment¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The goal is verbal-association or a procedure One well-sampled method (many short items) is usually enough; add a second method only to check that a correct answer is not recognition alone untested proposal

Reflection¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Goal is verbal-association (facts, vocabulary) Drop or shorten reflection; spend the time on retrieval practice and feedback, with at most a one-line "which items did I miss?" untested proposal

Validity, Reliability, and Bias in Classroom Assessment¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
The goal is verbal-association or a procedure recalled or carried out quickly Use many short selected-response or short-answer items for breadth and reliability; keep the language plain; add a few items that ask for the procedure on a changed case if transfer matters untested; from the earlier page (item-count arithmetic, selected-response trade-off)
