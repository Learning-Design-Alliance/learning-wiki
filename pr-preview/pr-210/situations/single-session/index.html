Designing for single-session¶

Duration · one sitting · All situations

76 rows from 74 pages say what to change when the brief's duration is single-session; 16 of them cite a claim. Pages whose rows cite claims come first. Read the basis before relying on a row: many carry a claim to learners or settings it did not test, and say so.

Cognitive Flexibility¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Duration single-session Use two or three contrasting cases with a similarities prompt and the principle stated after; test on a new case the same day. If the course allows, revisit the cases later under a new theme. Comparing cases side by side improves learning and transfer by a moderate average amount, though in one algebra study it raised procedural knowledge and flexibility but not conceptual knowledge [+M]: comparison helped in single experiments, more at immediate tests

Constructive Alignment¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Large lecture class, one session at a time (from the wording) Replace part of lecture with questions or problems answered in class that demand the outcome; use some at a higher level than the exam. Active learning [+M] (undergraduate STEM; held across class sizes); HOCS clickers [~M]
Only one session or unit can be changed (from the wording) Audit the whole course, then align the one unit's outcome with an assessed item that exists; do not promise a course-level effect. Untested; from the earlier page ("a single aligned lesson inside a misaligned course changes little")

Culturally Responsive Classroom Norms¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
A single session only (from the wording) State the session's conventions at the start (how to answer, how to ask for help, what counts as working together); use written or paired answers before any public one; do not run a one-off identity or belonging exercise and expect a lasting change Belonging boundary: Belonging Interventions Improve Outcomes [~M] (college entry). Rest untested

Developing Your Cultural Awareness¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
A single session only (one workshop, one in-service hour) (from the wording) Run the scenario step (20–30 minutes), one group comparison of readings (15 minutes), and one written decision change with a named follow-up check (10 minutes); arrange for a peer tally or data review a few weeks later, even by email. Do not claim lasting change from the session Brief empathic-discipline exercise reduced suspensions [~S] (one targeted belief about discipline, middle-school mathematics teachers; carried to other decisions, untested); otherwise untested

Effective Classroom Management Plan Criteria¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
A single session only (cover teacher, a one-off workshop, a guest) (from the wording) Do not build a plan; state three expectations at the start, greet each learner, use the host's existing routines where you know them, redirect with the least intrusive move, and leave a short note on what happened for the regular teacher Greetings at the door [+M], carried to a single session it did not test; the rest untested

Epistemic Cognition¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Only one session (e.g. a library visit) (from the wording) Teach one move (leave the page and search what others say about the source) rather than a long checklist, and expect modest gains; schedule a follow-up task if at all possible. Checklist-based evaluation [~M]: one-session checklist and networked framework did not differ; gains modest

Error Analysis¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
single-session: one lesson or less Use one comparison set per target misconception with explanation prompts and a short immediate check; do not expect the delayed effect to be shown. erroneous examples [+M] (brief session, immediate outcome)

Evaluating Sources¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Only one session (a library visit, an orientation) (from the wording) Teach one move (leave the page and search the source's name) and one practice round rather than a long checklist; tell the course teacher what to follow up One-session checklist (CRAAP) instruction did no better than the alternatives it was compared with [~M] (first-year business and nursing students; one session of either method, modest gains, no difference)

Functional Behavior Assessment¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Only one session (a cover teacher, a one-off workshop) (from the wording) Do not attempt an assessment; use antecedent defaults: greet each learner by name at the door, give instructions one step at a time, show a way to ask for help or take a short break, and pass a note on what happened to the regular teacher Greetings at the door [+M] (middle-school classes, carried to a single session it did not test); the rest untested

Modeling¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time is one session only (from the wording) Model one item fully with narration, then a partial model the learners finish, then one unaided item; record what the unaided item shows and do not claim retention Example-based sequences outperform problem-only practice for novices, and fading support is argued to aid transfer [+M] (example-first beat problem-first in one short troubleshooting task, immediate test)

Professional Development¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Only a single session is available (an inset day, a one-off workshop) (from the wording) Use it to model and rehearse one narrow practice and to set up a follow-up the school can run without the presenter: one peer observation with a look-for list within two weeks and one team meeting to review the clips. Do not promise changed practice from the session alone. Half of inservice activities lasted four hours or less, mostly talks or brief workshops [~W] (prevalence, not effect); short, fragmented training is reported to hinder a holistic view [-W]; the follow-up is untested

Reflection¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time available: one session (single-session) or a term-plus course One session: one reflection of three prompts, about five minutes, at the end, ending in one change to try; no portfolio. A term: reflect after each comparable performance, keep the same criterion, and compare across attempts at least once. single session untested, from the earlier page; intervention duration is a moderator in reflection interventions [~M], direction not stated on the claim page

Social Emotional Learning¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
A single session, assembly or workshop only (from the wording) Do not promise durable change. Teach one skill and leave a routine (a check-in card, a breathing step) the teacher will run daily; or spend the session on the adults' discipline mindset instead. Empathy-building claim [~M]: most prejudice-reduction studies test light-touch interventions with unclear long-term effects; empathic discipline [+M]: a brief teacher exercise reduced suspensions for high-risk groups, with a marginal overall effect in the preregistered replication

Strengths-based Approach¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time is one session only (from the wording) Give one low-stakes task, then one piece of specific feedback naming what worked and a next target; do not run a mindset or encouragement talk in place of it, and claim nothing about lasting change Growth-mindset interventions have weak average effects on achievement, including a negligible GPA gain (d = 0.11) for lower-achieving US ninth-graders in one preregistered national trial [~S] (a brief online intervention moved ninth graders' GPA by a negligible amount; meta-analyses weak overall); carried from mindset messages to strengths framing

Transfer of Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time is one session only (from the wording) Teach two cases side by side, prompt the comparison, state the principle, and end with one near-transfer item; do not claim far or delayed transfer Analogical Reasoning Improves Transfer [+S] (immediate tests favoured in the meta-analysis)

Wise Feedback Across Racial and Ethnic Difference¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
A single session only (one workshop, one assessed piece, a one-off review) (from the wording) Consistency cannot build; make the assurance concrete by pointing to evidence in the work, and give one revision in the session; claim no lasting effect A brief values-affirmation writing exercise raised African American seventh-graders' grades in two small field experiments, but a large same-district replication found no effect [~M] and Growth-mindset interventions have weak average effects on achievement, including a negligible GPA gain (d = 0.11) for lower-achieving US ninth-graders in one preregistered national trial [~M]: one-off identity-targeted messages are fragile

Ask Experts¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time is one session only (a guest visit, a site visit) (from the wording) Collect learners' questions beforehand and send them to the expert; spend the session on the expert's reasoning about two or three of them; set a follow-up task within a week that applies one point, reviewed by the course teacher; do not claim lasting change untested proposal

Authentic Assessment¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Short time (one or two weeks, or a single session) (from the wording) Shrink the task to one realistic decision or product, give one feedback point before submission, and drop the public exhibition. Untested proposal

Blended Learning¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Only one session, or a few days (from the wording) Use a single online preparation task before the session and a short online follow-up; the course-level split does not apply. untested proposal

Cognitive Activation¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
One session only (single-session) One relation, one activating task, one probe at the end; leave comparison of methods and justification for later. In a unit (days-weeks), sequence example, then prompts, then comparison, then justification, with a delayed probe. untested proposal

Collaborative Evaluation¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
single-session, no follow-up One round: two anchors of known quality, alone judgment, reveal and compare, reference, then one new object judged alone. Do not attempt co-constructed criteria. Untested proposal

Concept Attainment¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
single-session and many concepts in the unit Use concept attainment for the one or two concepts learners most often confuse; teach the rest definition-first with examples and non-examples. Untested; from the earlier page

Criterion- and Norm-Referenced Testing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Only a single session is available (a one-off placement) (from the wording) Use a criterion-referenced placement check on the entry skills, 20 to 30 minutes; treat near-cut results as provisional and re-check in the first week of teaching untested proposal

Cultural & Life Experiences Connections¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time is one session only (from the wording) Use one private prompt for a learner-supplied context and one explanation prompt; record what learners offer and claim nothing about lasting change untested

Debriefing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
One session only (single-session) One short experience, a debrief of at least equal length, then a two-minute retry of the key moment with the change; do not claim retention untested proposal

Deliberate Practice¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time is one session only (from the wording) One component, three rounds of three to five attempts, then a scored whole-task attempt; report the change against the baseline as immediate only, not as retained untested proposal

Develop Understanding¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
single-session only Run the five steps once, keep step 5 to one comparison, and record that understanding at a delay was not checked. Untested proposal

Elaboration Theory¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time is one session (single-session) rather than a unit or course Present the epitome and one level of elaboration only, end with one synthesis task and one application task; leave further levels for later sessions rather than compressing them. untested proposal

Engagement¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time is one session, or a one-off workshop (from the wording) Use the reason-for-task and a constructive task; do not claim durable engagement; record one learning probe untested proposal

Epistemic Games¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
single-session only (one workshop) Drop the full game: one case, one role, a modelled decision, one learner decision with feedback, and a 15-minute debrief on the frame. Do not expect a change in identity from one session. Untested proposal; Communities of Practice notes norms take a term to form

Experience with Languaging Activities (ELAs) guided by a sequence of experience-based questions¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
single-session only Run one cycle on one experience; end with each learner's step 5 question written on an exit card and answered in a short follow-up message or handout. Untested proposal

Experiential Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
One session (single-session) Run one complete loop (brief experience, prompted debrief, named rule, one retry) rather than a long experience with no debrief; cut loops, not stages untested proposal, from the experiential-learning-cycle pattern

Experiential Learning Cycle¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
single-session One complete loop: a 15-minute experience, a 10-minute written debrief, a stated rule, one changed attempt; no second loop. Untested; from the earlier page's compressed-timeframe guidance

Explicit Instruction: Vocabulary¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
A single session only, or a fixed short time budget (for example 20 minutes) (from the wording) Teach 3 words, not more: 6–8 minutes introducing them, 8–10 minutes reading or doing a task that uses them, 3–4 minutes of use in the learner's own sentence; give a take-away card for later review; do not claim retention untested proposal

Feedback Loops¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
One session only (from the wording) Run at least two complete cycles inside the session and end with one unaided new item; report what that item shows and claim no retention untested proposal

Flexible Grouping¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
One session only (from the wording) No regrouping cycle is possible: run a two-minute opening check, one similar-need group with the teacher for 15 minutes while others work in mixed pairs, and an individual exit item; do not claim regrouping effects untested proposal

Flipped Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Only one session, with no time before it (from the wording) Do not flip; give 10 minutes of exposition in the session, then application with feedback and an exit item untested proposal
The brief fixes its own session structure (two short sessions a week, a retrieval check before class, a simulation in one session) (from the wording) Keep that structure: put the pre-class check where the brief puts it and give the application or simulation the session the brief names; change only the pre-class dose, what the opening minutes check, and how peers support each other when facilitator cover is thin untested proposal

Fostering Communities of Learning (FCL)¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
single-session only Do not attempt the full pattern. Run one jigsaw on pre-read material with a teaching protocol and an individual check on every part; leave reciprocal-teaching handover out. Untested; reciprocal teaching's claim page says the routine needs sustained practice over multiple sessions

Game-based Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time is a single-session Use one short game round, a worked example, a second round and a debrief; do not expect durable gains; if days-weeks are available, prefer several short sessions untested proposal; the board-game dose is the only tested schedule here

Gamification¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time is a single-session Do not build a points economy; at most one team quiz round with feedback after each question and a short debrief; do not claim a motivational effect untested; no claim here tests a single gamified session

Goal Setting & Monitoring¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time available is one session (from the wording) Set one proximal goal for the session and one check at its end; skip multi-week goal sheets untested proposal; proximal goals evidence is second-hand [+W]

Graphic Organizers¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time is one session only (from the wording) Provide one organizer with the material, one completion task and one explanation prompt; do not ask for construction from scratch; record what an unaided check shows and do not claim retention untested proposal; construction costs time and load (Placing heavier cognitive demands on learners can be counterproductive in mapping tasks [~W])

Guided Practice¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time is one session only (from the wording) Plan the session as model, three or four guided items, one unaided check; record what the check shows and do not claim retention untested proposal

Holistic Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time is one session only (from the wording) Give the whole and one part only; end with one placement and one integrative task; do not compress later parts into the same session or claim retention untested proposal

Include parents, teachers, or mentors in any self-regulation intervention and support their own skills and coaching capacity¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
A single session only (from the wording) Spend it on the adults, not only the youth: one own-regulation routine and one co-regulation move, rehearsed; leave a cue card both sides share; do not promise lasting change untested proposal

Instructor Accessibility¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
A single session only (a workshop, a one-off training) (from the wording) State before the end how learners can reach you afterwards, for how long and for what (a two-week reply window by email, say), and collect one question from each learner before they leave untested proposal

Journaling¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
One session only (from the wording) Do not set up a journal. Use one end-of-session reflection of three prompts (see Reflection), ending in one change to try. untested proposal; the gains above come from term-long programmes (Writing To Learn Improves Understanding [+M], larger with longer treatment)

Knowledge Organization¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time is one session only (from the wording) Show the organization, one sort task, one explained link, one closed-book rebuild at the end; record what the rebuild shows and claim nothing about retention untested proposal

Learning by Producing (multimedia production as learning)¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
single-session only Drop production of new media: teams annotate or re-sequence provided clips or slides into a 1-minute explanation, present to another team, and answer one question each. Untested proposal; the project-based meta-analysis (project-based learning [+M]) excludes short single activities

Match the form and content of staff development interventions to clients' assessed Stages of Concern¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Only a single session is possible (from the wording) Use the first 10 minutes to collect written concern statements, then spend the session on the commonest peak; give one practical next step for use, and name who will follow up and when; do not claim the session changed use untested proposal

Memory Consolidation¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Only one session, no follow-up (from the wording) Teach, then put a different 10–15 minute task in between, then an unaided recall; hand over a dated self-test sheet for days 2, 7 and 21; state that retention was not measured untested proposal

Motivation¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time is one session only (from the wording) Give the reason, one first success, and one choice; record the behaviour; do not claim lasting motivation untested proposal

Multimodal Instruction¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
One session only, or no teacher present (from the wording) Use one representation pair, not a full set. Build the translation prompts and their model answers into the material, so a learner without a teacher can check a sketch or explanation. untested proposal

Multiple Methods of Assessment¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time is one session only (from the wording) Use two methods inside the session (a written item and a 1-minute spoken or drawn one) for the one goal that matters; use the result to steer the next step, not to grade untested proposal

Note-taking¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
A single session only (from the wording) Guided-notes outline, two pauses with a restatement prompt, and a 5-minute recall at the end from covered notes; do not plan the fade; do not claim retention untested proposal

Observation/Shadowing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
One session only (from the wording) One focused observation of a whole performance and one debrief; state the outcome as what the observer can now name and explain, not as a change in their own performance untested proposal

Phonics¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
A single session only (from the wording) Screen, then teach one correspondence and blend five to ten words with it; give the learner or family the next three correspondences to practise; do not report decoding gains untested proposal

Positive Self-talk¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time is one session only (from the wording) Give 10–15 minutes: find the moment, model one cue, each learner writes one or two cues and rehearses on two items; report use, not a lasting change Untested proposal; brief one-off psychological exercises have weak average achievement effects (Growth-mindset interventions have weak average effects on achievement, including a negligible GPA gain (d = 0.11) for lower-achieving US ninth-graders in one preregistered national trial [~M], a different intervention)

Pre-reading Questioning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
One session only (from the wording) Two questions, attempt, read, check, one whole-text summary; do not claim a lasting effect untested beyond the laboratory sessions on Pretesting enhances learning [~M]

Procedural Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time is one session only (from the wording) Model, three or four supported attempts, one unaided item at the end; record what the unaided item shows and do not claim retention or fluency; give learners a job aid and items to practise later untested proposal

Process-based Writing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Only one session is available (from the wording) Plan for 10 minutes against a model text, draft, then one revision against a single criterion with a partner's response; collect the draft and the revision; do not claim a gain in writing ability untested; the strategy studies taught over at least three days (strategy instruction [+M])

Self-monitoring¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time is one session only (from the wording) Teach and run the routine once on two or three items with every record checked; report the match between judgement and score; do not claim a habit or later effect Untested proposal

Sequencing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
A single session only (from the wording) Order the session as primer or map (5 minutes), one example, guided items, one unaided check; teach only as many stages as the check can confirm, and do not claim retention untested proposal

Social Interdependence¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time is one session only (from the wording) Use pairs, one short shared task, and an individual check at the end; do not claim an achievement effect untested; the reward evidence comes from programmes of four weeks or more (free-riding without accountability [~M])

Social Presence¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
A single session only (a workshop, a one-off training) (from the wording) Replace profiles with a 3–5-minute paired introduction that asks each person what they want from the session and one related experience, then use those answers in the session's first task untested proposal; the earlier page says benefits shrink in short settings

Standardized Test Fairness and Bias¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Only a single session is available before the real test (from the wording) Use it for one timed practice in the real format with immediate feedback and a walk-through of the instructions; do not add new content untested proposal

Structured Academic Controversy¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
single-session only (under 45 minutes) Keep restate, reverse, synthesise and the individual check. Cut open discussion and do preparation from a one-page source sheet. Do not cut the synthesis or the individual check: they are what distinguish SAC from debate. untested; from the earlier page ("strongest when the synthesis stage is treated as the instructional endpoint")

Structured Peer Review¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
single-session only One short draft brought to the session; 10 minutes on one anchor; one review of one draft; 10 minutes to plan and start revising in the room; the author leaves with the plan. Untested proposal

Summative Assessment¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time is a single session only (a one-day workshop) (from the wording) Do not report a summative judgment of the capability; give an end-of-session check, report it as immediate performance, and say what it cannot show untested proposal

Supporting Gifted and Talented Students¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Only a single session is available (a one-off enrichment day, a visiting class) (from the wording) Give one hard, open task with a model of the standard, time to attempt it, and feedback naming what the student did; do not claim it as provision untested proposal

Supporting Students with Intellectual Disabilities¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Only a single session is available (a one-off workshop, a visiting class) (from the wording) Teach one step of one functional task, practise it three or four times with feedback naming what the student did, and end with one unaided attempt; record what it shows and do not claim retention untested proposal

Text-to-Speech¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
A single session only (from the wording) Ten minutes on the controls with a short text, then one section-by-section reading of the real text with supplied questions; record the answers and do not claim lasting benefit untested proposal

Universal Design For Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time is a single session (from the wording) Do only steps 1, 3 and 4 (goal, two routes into the content, segmenting and pause); skip format choice untested proposal

Validity, Reliability, and Bias in Classroom Assessment¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Time is one session only, or the assessment is a single short quiz (from the wording) Use it to steer the next lesson, not to grade an individual; aim the few items at the one decision you need; combine with other evidence before any grade untested; from the earlier page (a single item's weight in a short test)
