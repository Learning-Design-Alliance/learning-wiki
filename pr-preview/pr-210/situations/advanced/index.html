Designing for advanced¶

Expertise · substantial prior knowledge, or experts · All situations

54 rows from 54 pages say what to change when the brief's expertise is advanced; 32 of them cite a claim. Pages whose rows cite claims come first. Read the basis before relying on a row: many carry a claim to learners or settings it did not test, and say so.

Ask Experts¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced on this task, or already self-diagnose most errors Drop the explanation and modelling steps; the learner presents work and their own critique, and the expert adds only what the learner missed; use peer critique between consultations Instructional guidance that helps novices can become redundant or counterproductive as expertise grows. [~M] (review of cognitive-load studies, not consultations; gives no crossover point)

Cognitive Flexibility¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Expertise intermediate or advanced, an ill-structured domain (clinical reasoning, law, policy) Use criss-crossing: revisit each concept in several short cases under different themes across sessions; drop the step-by-step part tasks. Presenting multiple cases from different perspectives supports transfer in ill-structured domains [+M]; tested with first- and second-year undergraduates, untested with professionals

Collaborative Evaluation¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced or practising professionals Have them co-construct criteria, judge genuinely borderline cases, and record reasoned disagreement instead of forcing consensus; use a rubric for the shared vocabulary, not as a score sheet. Rubric guidance [~M]: in a graduate course a rubric changed feedback content but restrained reflective and divergent commentary

Debriefing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced, or experienced practitioners Learners choose the decision points and lead; the facilitator asks for alternatives considered and the reasoning behind them, and adds only what was missed; keep it shorter Instructional guidance that helps novices can become redundant or counterproductive as expertise grows. [~M] (cognitive-load studies; no study of debriefs)

Deliberate Practice¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced and have plateaued Let the learner pick the component from their own log, raise the standard, use outcome targets beside process goals, and give feedback mainly when the log shows a plateau Process goals lead to better skill acquisition for novices than outcome goals. [~M] (reports that a shift to outcome goals after mastery can help; high-school writing)

Develop Understanding¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced, or explain the idea correctly at step 1 Skip the worked cases; go to boundary cases, comparison with a neighbouring idea, and use in a richer task, with selective feedback. Expertise reversal [~M] (a review of cognitive-load studies; gives no crossover point)

Digital Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced on the task, or succeed unaided on the first set Remove most hints and prompts; give mixed, whole-task items and feedback only on request Instructional guidance that helps novices can become redundant or counterproductive as expertise grows. [~M] (a cognitive-load review; gives no crossover point)

Dual Coding¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced on this content Drop most supplied visuals; ask for a redraw from memory or a learner-made summary diagram, checked against a model Graphic organizers for novices [~M] (the higher-ability subset showed no benefit; the authors say this cannot show maps fail them); the redraw is untested

Feedback Loops¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced, or already accurate unaided Shorten external loops: ask for self-evaluation first, give information only on what the self-evaluation missed, and loop on harder or transfer items Expertise reversal [~M] (review of guidance studies, carried to feedback; no crossover point given)

Flexible Grouping¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced on this task, or most already pass the check Skip the support group for them; form an extension group with a harder or wider task, or place them in mixed groups as explainers with an explaining role, not answer-givers Instructional guidance that helps novices can become redundant or counterproductive as expertise grows. [~M] (cognitive-load studies, not grouping); Learning By Teaching Improves Tutor Learning [+M] (tutors' own gains)

Goal Setting & Monitoring¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice on the task (new procedure, first attempts); or intermediate/advanced Novices: process goals naming the strategy steps, split into proximal sub-goals, outcome goals only once the process runs unprompted. Experienced learners: outcome goals with a stated criterion, which they may set or negotiate (learner-set goals are an untested proposal) Process goals for novices [+M]; tested with high-school writers, untested in other domains

Graphic Organizers¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced on this material, or already explain its structure Drop the provided organizer; ask learners to sketch their own quickly, or use none; spend the time on application Graphic Organizers Support Novice Comprehension [~M] (in five studies each, low-ability learners gained from maps and high-ability learners did not; underpowered subsets); Instructional guidance that helps novices can become redundant or counterproductive as expertise grows. [~M] (a review, not tested on organizers)

Guided Practice¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced on this task, or succeed on the first item unaided Drop most guided rounds; give one check item, then independent or whole-task practice with selective feedback Instructional guidance that helps novices can become redundant or counterproductive as expertise grows. [~M] (review of cognitive-load studies; gives no crossover point)

Holistic Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are intermediate or advanced for this content Replace the overview with the opening sketch; start at the first part they cannot explain; spend the time on integrative tasks Expertise reversal [~M] (review of cognitive-load studies, carried to overviews; no crossover point)

Knowledge Organization¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced, or already state the organization unaided Skip the given organization; ask them to rebuild it from memory, then reorganize it for a new task or critique where it fails Instructional guidance that helps novices can become redundant or counterproductive as expertise grows. [~M] (review of cognitive-load studies; carried to organizations, which it did not test)

Match the form and content of staff development interventions to clients' assessed Stages of Concern¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
People are experienced users (advanced in this innovation), or already show impact concerns Drop introductory sessions; give time to look at student work and effects, to collaborate and to try modifications; keep a short check for leftover management problems concerns moved from lower to higher stages over a year of use [+M] (associational, one curriculum); the matching move is untested

Modeling¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced on this task, or succeed on the first item unaided Skip the full model: let them attempt first, then compare their work with an expert model and name the differences; model only the one new decision Instructional guidance that helps novices can become redundant or counterproductive as expertise grows. [~M] (review of cognitive-load studies; no crossover point)

Motivation¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced or already interested in the topic Do not add tangible rewards or points for taking part; offer harder goals and choices of topic or product Expected tangible rewards reduce later free-choice engagement with a rewarded task, while verbal praise does not [~S] (undermining is for tasks already found interesting); choice and challenge untested here

Multimodal Instruction¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are novice on this content and unfamiliar with its representations; or advanced Novice: use words plus one visual type. Teach how to read it first (what the axes, arrows or symbols mean), add one explicit translation prompt per idea, and hold back a second visual type until learners can read the first. Advanced: cut to the most efficient representation, drop duplicating modes, and use translation tasks for checking rather than teaching. Novice: multiple representations [~M] (the review ties the benefit to representational competence). Advanced: untested proposal (the claim pages discuss expertise reversal; no entry here tests it for modes)

Note-taking¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced on the content, or already take selective notes Skip guided notes and modelling; state the purpose, keep one recall review; let them choose the format Instructional guidance that helps novices can become redundant or counterproductive as expertise grows. [~M] (review of cognitive-load studies; not tested on note-taking aids)

Observation/Shadowing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced or experienced practitioners (in-service teachers, senior staff observing each other) Let them set their own focus, observe whole live performances, and compare directly with their own practice; replace the designer's focus sheet with the observed person's question; shorten or drop the corrective part of the debrief Instructional guidance that helps novices can become redundant or counterproductive as expertise grows. [~M] (cognitive-load studies on instructional formats; carried to observation guidance)

Online Course Design (Community of Inquiry)¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced or practitioners (an MBA, a professional course) Use their work as the discussion material (cases from their own settings), let them facilitate earlier, and keep the content strand short; keep the fixed structure. Template and organisation ratings [+W] from one accelerated online MBA, descriptive; the rest is the earlier page's guidance

Phonics¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners already decode accurately (advanced for decoding) Drop phonics lessons; give the screening only, teach the specific gaps it shows, and spend the time on fluency, vocabulary and comprehension Instructional guidance that helps novices can become redundant or counterproductive as expertise grows. [~M] (a cognitive-load review, carried to reading)

Positive Self-talk¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced, or the skill is already automatic Drop step cues; keep at most one motivational or focusing cue for pressure moments Instructional guidance that helps novices can become redundant or counterproductive as expertise grows. [~M] (cognitive-load studies of instructional formats; carried to self-talk untested)

Procedural Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced on this procedure, or complete the first item unaided Skip modelling and guided attempts; give mixed, spaced practice on full tasks and selective feedback, and refine weak components Instructional guidance that helps novices can become redundant or counterproductive as expertise grows. [~M] (cognitive-load studies; gives no crossover point)

Process-based Writing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Writers are advanced in the genre (they already plan and revise globally) Drop the scripted strategy and prompt cards; let the writer set the revision focus; keep one outside reader's response per piece Instructional guidance that helps novices can become redundant or counterproductive as expertise grows. [~M] (cognitive-load review, not writing); untested for writing

Sequencing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced on this target, or pass the prerequisite check Skip the early stages; start at the first stage they cannot do unaided; drop the primer and the part-task runs; use problem-first and mixed practice from the start Instructional guidance that helps novices can become redundant or counterproductive as expertise grows. [~M] (a review of cognitive-load studies; gives no point at which to switch)

Social Presence¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
advanced learners or experienced professionals Shorten the social opening; move quickly to peer critique of each other's work and decisions; presence comes from the value of what peers offer Peer monitoring [~W] (MBA interviews); otherwise untested

Strengths-based Approach¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced on the task, or already confident Little framing is needed: name strengths briefly and spend the time on harder, specific goals Specific, difficult goals lead to higher performance than easy or vague "do your best" goals. [~M] (one narrative review, adult task performance; carried to classrooms)

Structured Peer Review¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced or practising professionals Let the author name the criteria and the questions to answer; drop frames and anchors; ask for one substantial alternative rather than a list of fixes; use the rubric as shared vocabulary, not a score sheet. Rubric guidance [~M]: in a graduate course a rubric changed comment content and restrained reflective and divergent commentary

Transfer of Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced, or already solve the taught case unaided Skip the early stage; give unprompted varied and far cases with selective feedback, and ask learners to generate their own cases Instructional guidance that helps novices can become redundant or counterproductive as expertise grows. [~M] (review of cognitive-load studies; not about transfer designs)

Universal Design For Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced, or succeed on the first task unaided Drop the extra representations and step-by-step supports by default and leave them available on request; widen choice of task and format Instructional guidance that helps novices can become redundant or counterproductive as expertise grows. [~M] (cognitive-load studies; no crossover point given)

Authentic Assessment¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced or practitioners (final-year students, continuing professional education) Drop most component practice; give an ill-structured task in which learners define part of the problem; one draft with practitioner feedback; judge by a practitioner panel. Untested; from the earlier page

Cognitive Activation¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced for this relation Drop the opening example and step-by-step prompts; give an unfamiliar case to explain, or two solutions to compare and choose between, and ask for a justification. untested proposal

Concept Attainment¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced or already use the concept Skip the induction; give the definition and go straight to near misses and borderline cases, or ask them to generate the contrasting set. Untested; from the earlier page

Constructive Alignment¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced or professionals Fewer, more integrated outcomes; assess on a realistic task in their own setting; cut practice of components they already perform. Untested proposal

Criterion- and Norm-Referenced Testing¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced or near the top of the range Add items at the upper skills of the hierarchy, or a broader task, so the check is not at ceiling; if relative standing matters (an enrichment place), use a norm-referenced score from an above-level test and a work sample untested proposal

Elaboration Theory¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are intermediate or advanced for this content Replace the epitome with the elicitation sketch; start at the first level they cannot explain; drop syntheses they can already produce. untested proposal; advance organizers [~M] found no special benefit for low-knowledge learners, so neither direction of an expertise effect is established for overviews

Epistemic Games¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced practitioners (continuing education, in-service) Skip the early modelled sessions; build the unit around two or three atypical or breakdown cases; debrief on what their usual framing missed. Untested; from the earlier page's "transformation" games

Experience with Languaging Activities (ELAs) guided by a sequence of experience-based questions¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced in the content Make steps 2 and 3 implicit; spend the time on step 5, with learners generating questions and choosing the next source to answer them. Untested proposal

Experiential Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced or experienced practitioners Shorten the preparation, make the experience less structured, and have learners write their own debrief questions; keep the second attempt with feedback untested proposal; the guided-inquiry claim's narrative review argues that the advantage of guidance recedes with prior knowledge, which no recorded study tests here

Experiential Learning Cycle¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced or experienced practitioners Skip the demonstration; make the first experience ill-structured; have learners write their own debrief questions from the first loop; keep the changed attempt with feedback. Untested; the guided-discovery claim's narrative review argues the advantage of guidance recedes with prior knowledge, which no recorded study tests here

Flipped Learning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced on the topic, or professionals updating practice Make pre-class material a short brief or a reading, and use the session for cases, disagreement and their own examples; drop the note-taking guide and the recap untested proposal

Gamification¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced, or already accurate Let them test out of early levels on the step 4 check; give harder challenge levels rather than more points on easy items untested proposal

Instructor Accessibility¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced (experienced professionals, final-year students) Make contact on request and use it for critique of their work and for decisions they are unsure of, not explanation; keep the reply window but drop the required first contact untested proposal (the expertise reasoning is on Ask Experts)

Journaling¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
advanced learners or practitioners One open prompt about a decision or case, a fortnightly review entry, and peer reading in small groups; drop sentence starters. untested proposal; peer interaction is a reported moderator in reflection interventions [~M], direction not stated on the claim page

Learning by Producing (multimedia production as learning)¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced in the content but new to the media Skip the content investigation to a short review; spend the time on exemplars and media criteria; let learners choose the audience and write their own criteria. Untested proposal

Memory Consolidation¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced or already recall it on a delayed check Drop it from the opening rotation; keep one item in cumulative checks; spend the time on newer material untested proposal

Multiple Methods of Assessment¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced, or adults with applied experience Let them propose a method and the evidence it will produce, checked against the criteria before they start; accept work products from their practice untested; from the earlier page (adults with rich applied experience)

Pre-reading Questioning¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Readers are advanced on the topic, or answer most questions before reading Replace teacher questions with one higher-order question, or with the reader's own questions about what they still want to know untested; the earlier page's caution against over-directing applies

Reflection¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
advanced learners or practitioners Use fewer, more open prompts; add peer discussion of cases; ask for alternatives considered, not a recount. untested proposal; peer interaction is a moderator in reflection interventions [~M], direction not stated on the claim page

Self-monitoring¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are advanced, or their first checks already match an outside score Start at the low-support stage: learner-chosen checkpoints, no printed checklist, a weekly outside check Untested; the age and expertise moderators on the self-assessment claims (Self Assessment Accuracy Is Low Without Training [~M]: closer agreement in advanced courses) suggest it

Structured Academic Controversy¶

Pattern

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Expertise advanced (graduate students, professionals who know the debate) Cut preparation and the source pack. Spend the time on the reversal and synthesis. Raise the synthesis bar: name the evidence that would settle the disagreement. untested proposal

Text-to-Speech¶

Principle

If the brief says… Then change… Basis
Learners are fluent readers of this text (advanced for its print), or the goal includes reading the words unaided Do not build the lesson around speech; offer it as an optional control for fatigue or proofreading only, and keep it off during reading practice and any check of reading untested; from the earlier page ("some learners comprehend better through print")
