Micro-credential earning as professional learning aligned to Learning Forward standards and Knowles's adult learning principles¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The paper frames earning micro-credentials as a high-leverage professional learning activity aligned with Learning Forward's Standards for Professional Learning—Learning Communities, Leadership, Resources, Learning Designs, Data, Implementation, and Outcomes—and with the Core Adult Learning Principles of Knowles, Holton, and Swanson's Andragogy in Practice model, including "Self-Concept of the Learner (autonomous and self-directing)" and "Orientation to Learning (problem-centered and contextual)." Personalized choice of micro-credentials is said to fit three research-backed interest-enhancing interventions: contexts evoking prior individual interest, problem-based learning, and enhancing utility value.
