SEERNet's framework of Digital Learning Platforms as research infrastructure for education research¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The paper articulates SEERNet's core framework: positioning DLPs as research infrastructure so that researchers can ask and answer questions "grounded in realistic, widespread use of learning technologies." The network connects five DLPs (ASU Learning @ Scale, E-TRIALS, OpenStax Kinetic, Terracotta, UpGrade), a hub (Digital Promise with Empirical Education), researchers, and practitioners, aiming to lower barriers to research, increase relevance to practice, and rapidly incorporate findings back into widely used platforms. The framework draws on IES's SEER principles, including the newly added equity standard.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • DLPs must be regularly used in teaching and learning situations for research to be timely and applicable

Constraints¶

  • Complete convergence across DLPs into a single infrastructure model is not within the network's current scope or desires

Target Learners¶

  • education researchers
  • K-12 educators and school districts
  • higher education instructors

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • improving student learning through research on digital learning platforms
  • building researcher capacity to use platform infrastructure

Claims¶

  • SEERNet's five DLPs each have more than 100,000 regular users, which the network cites as grounding research in practice [+M]
  • The DLP-as-research-infrastructure community is small and fragmented: the DLP-focused cluster was one of five bibliographic-coupling communities and only 20.6% of filtered papers [~M]
