Equipercentile linking with conditional growth norms as the methodology for deriving MAP Growth cut scores¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article's methodology combines the equipercentile linking method with NWEA's conditional growth norms. The equipercentile procedure "matches scores on the two scales that have the same percentile rank" to find the RIT equivalent of each state cut score, with polynomial loglinear pre-smoothing applied. Fall and winter cuts are then projected using expected growth gains, and Grade 2 cuts are interpolated from longitudinal Grade 3 cohort data. This framework underlies all state linking studies described.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A minimum sample size of 1,000 students per grade and subject
  • Districts must provide state summative data and permission to use MAP Growth data
  • Students must take both assessments in the designated spring term

Constraints¶

  • The linking study sample is voluntary and may differ from the general student population in important characteristics, addressed via post-stratification weighting

Target Learners¶

  • Students in Grades 2–8 taking MAP Growth and state summative assessments
  • High school and science students where data are available

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • Predicting performance on state summative assessments in ELA, mathematics, and science
