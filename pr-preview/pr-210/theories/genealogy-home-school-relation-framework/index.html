Genealogical framework for analyzing the home/school relation and parent involvement¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article offers a genealogical account of the home/school relation, tracing how ideas about this relationship and parent involvement have been constructed over time. As the authors state, they "examine a century of rhetoric on the home/school relation and associated parent involvement." The framework treats parent involvement as a historically produced set of ideas rather than a neutral practice, and uses this historical lens to question current assumptions in educational research and policy.
