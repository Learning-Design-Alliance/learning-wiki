Original charter school design rationale: alternative public schools enabling experimentation and innovation within the public school system¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The publication describes the founding conception of charter schools as publicly funded but deliberately different institutions. In its words, "Charter schools were originally designed to be alternative public schools that would allow for greater experimentation and innovation within the public school system." This design rationale frames the later debate about charters' relationship to the broader public system. The page uses this origin story as context for the Point/Counterpoint exchange on charter schools and the public interest.
