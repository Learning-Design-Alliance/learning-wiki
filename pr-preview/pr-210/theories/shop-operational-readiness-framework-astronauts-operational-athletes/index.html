SHOP operational readiness framework positioning astronauts as operational athletes¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Spaceflight Human Optimization and Performance (SHOP) framework is a translational, multidisciplinary framework that positions astronauts as operational athletes and organizes countermeasures and monitoring across mission phases. The review states it proposes "an operational readiness framework that positions astronauts as operational athletes and couples NASA human-system capability needs with pragmatic monitoring strategies and multifaceted countermeasures". It integrates sports science concepts such as systematic athlete monitoring of fatigue, recovery, and chronic adaptation to optimize astronaut readiness before, during, and after long-duration spaceflight.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires collaboration across multiple disciplines including aerospace medicine, human performance, sports science, nutrition, and engineering

Constraints¶

  • Framed for long-duration spaceflight missions to low-Earth orbit, the lunar surface, and Mars

Target Learners¶

  • astronaut crews preparing for exploration-class missions

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • sustained health, fitness, and mission readiness across pre-flight, in-flight, and post-flight phases
