Multivariate true score model for deriving achievement and growth norms¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 6 claims (6 for) · 4 studies (4 associational), q2 · 1 of 4 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The norming procedure is a model-based approach from the course-specific MAP Growth Mathematics norming study (Thum & He, 2019). It uses "a multivariate true score model that accounts for the known imprecision of scores from the fall, winter, and spring terms", introducing each score's standard error of measurement into the model. True scores are assumed multivariate normal, and the estimated joint distribution of predicted fall, winter, and spring scores provides the basis for marginal achievement norms and conditional growth norms.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Standard errors of measurement for each observed score must be available to weight score imprecision in the model

Constraints¶

  • The joint normality assumption of scores underlies inferences from the model; the report checks it with Q-Q plots, CDF curves, and residuals

Target Learners¶

  • Students in Grades K-3 in the norming population

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • Normative interpretation of early reading foundational skills scores and between-term growth
