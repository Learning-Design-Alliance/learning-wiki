Four-dimension coding scheme for SRL strategies in online and blended learning (metacognitive, motivational, cognitive, mixed)¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 7 claims (6 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), q3 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article organizes SRL interventions using a coding scheme with four primary strategy dimensions applied to online learning (OL) and blended learning (BL) contexts. As the authors state, "we employed a coding scheme including four primary strategies (metacognitive, motivational, cognitive, and mixed)". The metacognitive dimension covers self-monitoring, planning, reflection, and goal setting; the motivational dimension covers intrinsic and extrinsic motivation, interests, and values; the cognitive dimension covers information processing, concept acquisition, and problem-solving; and the mixed dimension examines the interplay among the three. The scheme was used to code the 15 included studies for moderator analysis.

Claims¶

  • Srl Effect No Significant Moderator Differences [+M]
  • SRL interventions combining metacognitive, cognitive, and motivational components were most frequent and, per the authors, had the greatest impact [+M]
  • Effect sizes across SRL intervention studies are highly heterogeneous, requiring a random effects model [+M]
  • Self-regulated learning interventions have a moderate effect (0.65) on learning outcomes in online and blended environments [+M]
  • Publication bias is unlikely to explain the consistency of SRL intervention effect sizes [+W]
  • In a randomized trial of SRL interventions in an online mathematics preparation course, group and time interacted significantly on SRL knowledge, self-efficacy and the SRL overall score, with Group TDP (training + diary + peer feedback) showing the most prominent gains [+M]
  • A daily learning diary alone (Group D) did not produce statistically significant pre-post gains on any measured outcome in an online mathematics preparation course [-W]
