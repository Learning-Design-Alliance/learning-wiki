Balanced Leadership framework of 21 leadership actions and behaviors associated with improved student outcomes¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 2 claims (2 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The content of the Balanced Leadership® professional development is organized around a framework of leadership actions and behaviors derived from research on school leaders. The report states that "The professional development focuses on 21 specific actions and behaviors of school leaders that may be associated with improved student outcomes, including designing and implementing school curriculum, instruction, and assessment; fostering shared beliefs and community; and monitoring teaching and student learning." Year-one sessions cover the framework overview, initiating and leading school change, maintaining a focus on school performance, and fostering shared beliefs and a purposeful school community, taught through a case study approach.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Delivery through structured training sessions using a case study approach in which participants read about and reflect on real-life applications of the leadership framework

Constraints¶

  • The WWC did not review the underlying research (Marzano et al. 2005) used to identify the actions and behaviors, stating a review of this research is beyond the scope of the report

Target Learners¶

  • current and aspiring school leaders in K–12 schools; district-level leaders in a separate program variant

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • initiating and leading school change, maintaining a focus on school performance, and fostering shared beliefs and a purposeful school community

Claims¶

  • Comparison-group contamination occurred in the Balanced Leadership® trial [-W]
  • Effects of Balanced Leadership® on student achievement, teacher retention, and leadership practice are not established by standards-meeting evidence [-W]
