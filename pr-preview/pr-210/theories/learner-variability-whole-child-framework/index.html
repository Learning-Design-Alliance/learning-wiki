Learner variability whole child framework: strengths and challenges across content, cognition, social-emotional learning, and student background¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The guide's organizing framework treats learner variability as a design challenge rather than a student problem: "Learner variability is the recognition that every student has a unique set of strengths and challenges across a whole child framework that are interconnected and vary according to context." The framework spans four domains — content, cognition, social and emotional learning, and student background — so a student strong at building relationships but weak in decoding can be supported with recorded text and peer book clubs. It underpins the Learner Variability Navigator and the guide's reflection questions.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers consider all four domains rather than only academic factors when identifying strengths

Constraints¶

  • Factors of learning are organized for grades preK-12 in literacy and math, plus an adult learner model

  • Adult learners via the LVN adult model
