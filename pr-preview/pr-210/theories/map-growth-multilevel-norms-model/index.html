MAP Growth norms: multilevel growth model with student-level and school-level norms (CGI and CGP)¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

MAP Growth norms compare student performance to "a nationally representative sample of test takers for the fall, winter, and spring terms" using "a multilevel growth model that has individual students nested within schools." Achievement status norms provide percentiles at a point in time; growth norms describe change relative to a national population, including the Conditional Growth Index (CGI) and Conditional Growth Percentile (CGP), which compare students to similar students with similar instructional time and prior scores.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • The level of aggregation should match the level of decision-making; school percentiles should be obtained from school norms and not individual norms

Constraints¶

  • The variability of growth for a group of students (a school) is smaller than the variability in growth for individual students, so percentiles differ across the two levels of the model

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students
  • schools and districts interpreting mean scores

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • Interpreting achievement status and growth relative to national norms
