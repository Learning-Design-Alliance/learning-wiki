Deploy an educational chatbot to give students instant question-and-answer support outside class when teacher interaction time is limited¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends providing suitable learning tools for out-of-class biology study because teachers "do not have enough class time to interact with students, and students often do not have the opportunities to raise questions in class." A chatbot offers instant interactions and supports anytime and anywhere, letting students review biology knowledge on faulty foundations rather than falling behind.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A chatbot capable of instant interaction and learning support must be available to students outside class hours

Constraints¶

  • The article's evidence covers a grade 7 biology course in a junior high school; out-of-class use assumes students can access the tool independently

Target Learners¶

  • junior high school students in biology courses

Target Learning Goals¶

  • reviewing and consolidating biology knowledge out of class
  • obtaining instant answers to questions teachers cannot address in limited class time

Claims¶

  • A biology learning chatbot system had a positive impact on grade 7 students' biology learning achievement in a quasi-experiment [+W]
  • Students perceived ChatGPT as a valuable learning tool for asking questions, getting explanations, and clarifying understanding [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Use asynchronous AI tutoring for students' first engagement with challenging material, reserving class time for higher-order skills
  • Use a non-generative AI chatbot with a pre-programmed, human-curated knowledge base to answer student questions with pre-approved responses
  • Evaluate AI systems before deploying them with students
