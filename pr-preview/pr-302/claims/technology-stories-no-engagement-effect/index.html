Evidence¶

Takacs 2015¶

Takacs, Z. K., Swart, E. K., & Bus, A. G. (2015). Benefits and Pitfalls of Multimedia and Interactive Features in Technology-Enhanced Storybooks: A Meta-Analysis. Review of Educational Research, 85(4), 698-739. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654314566989

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analytic results: 5 contrasts targeted communication initiated by the child, 6 targeted engagement during reading (on-task behavior, looking at the material, or skin conductance), and 1 combined the two. Table 2 reports g+ = 0.26, 95% CI [-0.21, 0.74], p = .28.

"There was no significant effect of the technology-enhanced condition on child engagement and communication during reading."
