Evidence¶

Wiley 2019¶

Wiley, J. (2019). Picture this! Effects of photographs, diagrams, animations, and sketching on learning and beliefs about learning from a geoscience text. Applied Cognitive Psychology, 33, 9-19. https://doi.org/10.1002/acp.3495

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Same 120-participant experiment; absolute error was the absolute difference between prediction and outcome composites. The ANOVA showed "a significant effect of condition on absolute error" with η² = .17, F(5, 114) = 4.67, p < . 01.

"there was a significant effect of condition on absolute error, F(5, 114) = 4.67, p < . 01, partial eta squared = .17. As shown in Figure 1, the least absolute error was seen in the sketching condition."
