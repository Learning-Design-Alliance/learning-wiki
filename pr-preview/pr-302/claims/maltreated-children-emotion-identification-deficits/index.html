q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative review synthesis of emotion recognition studies in maltreated children. The review reports these children "show less accurate identification of facial emotions in general" and "particular difficulty identifying positive emotions," alongside abnormalities in emotion expression and regulation. No effect sizes are printed.

"Studies of maltreated children (including those who experience neglect and other forms of abuse) also show less accurate identification of facial emotions in general [41, 131] and particular difficulty identifying positive emotions [ 134]."

Narrative review synthesis reporting rumination findings. The review states "Maltreated children also show higher levels of rumination," associated with an attention bias to sad faces and potentially contributing to depressive symptomatology risk. No effect sizes are printed.
