Evidence¶

Xin Liu 2023¶

Xin Liu, Kajsa Yang Hansen, Jan De Neve, Martin Valcke. (2023). Teacher versus student perspectives on instructional quality in mathematics education across countries. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-023-09652-6

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Country-by-country CFA of TALIS 2013 teacher data in the rTPL database. When modeling countries separately, the article reports "Teacher support could not be identified in Latvia and Romania" and a three-item teacher support factor in Finland.

"Teacher support could not be identified in Latvia and Romania, and only three items helped in constituting this dimension in Finland"

Xin Liu 2023 (2)¶

Xin Liu, Kajsa Yang Hansen, Jan De Neve, Martin Valcke. (2023). Teacher versus student perspectives on instructional quality in mathematics education across countries. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-023-09652-6

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Measurement invariance testing of the teacher measurement model across the seven countries in Mplus. Because country factor structures differed, the article concludes "the configural invariance level was not achieved" for teacher-perceived data.

"Since configural invariance requires the factor structure to be equal across groups, these analysis results imply that the configural invariance level was not achieved."
