Geers Ann E. 2011¶

Geers Ann E., Sedey Allison L. (2011). Language and Verbal Reasoning Skills in Adolescents with 10 or More Years of Cochlear Implant Experience. Ear and Hearing. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3157037/

Results section reports standardized WISC-III Verbal IQ for the 112-participant CI-HS sample against the normative sample of typically developing age-mates, with "Seventy-one percent of the teenagers obtained VIQs within or above one SD". The mean VIQ of 91.3 was almost 10 points below the expected mean of 100.

"Seventy-one percent of the teenagers obtained VIQs within or above one SD of age-mates in the NS-TD sample (i.e., they obtained a VIQ ≥85)."
