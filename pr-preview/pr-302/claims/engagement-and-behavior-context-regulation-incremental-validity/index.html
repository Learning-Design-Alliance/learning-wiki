Authors' interpretation of the Step 2 regression results, reporting "unique incremental validity of academic engagement and regulation of behavior and context" over previous AP, inductive reasoning, and MSLQ Factor 1; in the printed tables these variables retained significant β values in Step 2 for years 1 and 3, and MSLQ Factor 2 for all three years.
