Kluger 1996¶

Kluger, A. N., & DeNisi, A. (1996). The effects of feedback interventions on performance: A historical review, a meta-analysis, and a preliminary feedback intervention theory. Psychological Bulletin, 119(2), 254-284. https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.119.2.254

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Quasi-d statistics were created by transforming ARIMA t values from six field studies of the behavior modification tradition with reasonable quasi-experimental designs. The printed effect size is d = 1.69, though the authors state this value "cannot be interpreted because there is no metric for converting this value into a real d," and that field studies differ systematically from most studies in the literature.

"Finally, the 17 quasi ds derived from time series design were all positive with a mean d of 1.69 and a variance of 0.45."
