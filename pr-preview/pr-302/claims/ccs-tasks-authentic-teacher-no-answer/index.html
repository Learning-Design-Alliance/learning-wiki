Evidence¶

Rikke Magnussen 2023¶

Rikke Magnussen, Yotam Hod. (2023). Bridging communities and schools in Urban development: community and citizen science. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-023-09641-9

q2 · i? · design · r3

Pre/post survey question across the CCS iterations on whether tasks came from outside professionals and whether the teacher already knew the answers. The article prints 52.2% versus 8% descriptively; no effect size is reported.

"students in the CCS project felt that professionals outside school gave them the tasks to develop and that the teachers did not already know the answer to the tasks they had to solve in the CCS project (52.2%) compared to everyday school (8%)."

Related Claims¶

  • Students' perception of possessing local knowledge that professional urban planners lack rose from 9% at baseline to 45% at endline in City at Play — related
  • Students responded most positively to projects that felt authentic and were organized in a clear sequence with manageable milestones — related
