Saab 2012¶

Saab, N., van Joolingen, W., & van Hout-Wolters, B. (2012). Support of the collaborative inquiry learning process: influence of support on task and team regulation. Metacognition and Learning, 7, 7-23. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-011-9068-6

q3 · i? · causal · r2

ANOVA on team scores (percentage correct assignments) across the three conditions; means were 55%, 58%, and 59% for Control, RIDE, and CHT. No statistically significant difference; equivalence was not tested.

"An ANOV A was performed to detect differences in scores within the learning environment (SWLEP) between conditions. No significant differences between conditions were found."
