Evidence¶

Susanne Carroll 1992¶

Susanne Carroll, Merrill Swain, and Yves Roberge. (1992). The role of feedback in adult second language acquisition: Error correction and morphological generalizations. Applied Psycholinguistics, Volume 13, Issue 2. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0142716400005555

q3 · i? · causal · r?

After the feedback session, all subjects guessed responses to novel stimuli and were retested twice on the feedback items. The abstract reports that comparisons of guessing responses between experimental and comparison groups "showed no evidence of learned generalizations"; no test statistic or effect size is printed.

"Afterward, all subjects “guessed” responses to novel stimuli and were retested (twice) on the feedback items."
