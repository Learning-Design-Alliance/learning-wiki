Steven C. Pan and Timothy C. Rickard 2018¶

Steven C. Pan and Timothy C. Rickard. (2018). Transfer of Test-Enhanced Learning: Meta-Analytic Review and Synthesis. Psychological Bulletin. http://dx.doi.org/10.1037/bul0000151

q4 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Synthesis across the review's six major transfer categories, comparing category-level transfer effect sizes. The review reports transfer "is greatest across test formats, to application and inference questions, to problems involving medical diagnoses, and to mediator and related word cues."

"That transfer of learning is greatest across test formats, to application and inference questions, to problems involving medical diagnoses, and to mediator and related word cues; it is weakest to rearranged stimulus-response items, to untested materials seen during initial study, and to problems involving worked examples."
