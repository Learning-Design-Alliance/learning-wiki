Voight 2013¶

Voight, A., Austin, G., and Hanson, T. (2013). A climate for academic success: How school climate distinguishes schools that are beating the achievement odds (Full Report). San Francisco: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED559741

Comparative climate analysis contrasting 40 BTO schools with 20 chronically underperforming schools identified by the same residual-based criteria; the report states the BTO-CU climate differences were "twice as large" as BTO-other differences.
