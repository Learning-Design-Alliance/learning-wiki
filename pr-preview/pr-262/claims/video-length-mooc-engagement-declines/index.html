Evidence¶

Brame Cynthia J 2016¶

Brame Cynthia J. (2016). Effective Educational Videos: Principles and Guidelines for Maximizing Student Learning from Video Content. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-03-0125

q2 · i? · review · r2

The essay reports, citing Guo et al. (2014), an observational analysis of video-watching sessions in four edX MOOCs. "the median engagement time for videos less than 6 minutes long was close to 100%". The essay adds that median engagement was ~50% for 9- to 12-minute videos and ~20% for 12- to 40-minute videos.

"They observed that the median engagement time for videos less than 6 minutes long was close to 100%–that is, students tended to watch the whole video (although there are significant outliers; see the paper for more complete information)."
