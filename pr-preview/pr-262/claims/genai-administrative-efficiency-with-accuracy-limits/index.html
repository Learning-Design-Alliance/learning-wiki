The review reports Danahy et al.'s (2024) mixed-methods study of AI-supported versus self-selected group formation, in which students judged AI-analysed strengths and weaknesses "not entirely accurate"; the authors recommend human safeguarding after initial AI administrative work.

"Students reported that these strengths and weaknesses were not entirely accurate, with a tendency toward exaggeration and hallucination by GenAI."
