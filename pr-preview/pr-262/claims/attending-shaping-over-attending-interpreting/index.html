Selen Galiç, Mathias Tejera, Sebastián Parodi, Markus Hohenwarter, Zsolt Lavicza. (2026). Preservice mathematics teachers' noticing in AI-based simulations: transitions among attending, interpreting, and shaping. ZDM – Mathematics Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11858-026-01805-4

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Group-level transition-frequency analysis across all coded noticing instances from the 17 preservice teachers over the three-week intervention. The article states the A→S transition "occurred more frequently than transitions from Attending to Inter- preting (A→I)".

"Transitions from Attending to Shaping (A→S) occurred more frequently than transitions from Attending to Inter- preting (A→I) suggesting that PTs more often moved di- rectly from attending to shaping rather than through inter- pretation."

Selen Galiç 2026 (2)¶

Selen Galiç, Mathias Tejera, Sebastián Parodi, Markus Hohenwarter, Zsolt Lavicza. (2026). Preservice mathematics teachers' noticing in AI-based simulations: transitions among attending, interpreting, and shaping. ZDM – Mathematics Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11858-026-01805-4

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

ENA co-occurrence analysis at group level across the three weeks. The article reports attending–shaping edges were stable (0.47, 0.46, 0.46) while "Attending–Interpreting links remained comparatively weaker" with the printed weekly values, and concludes interpreting was more strongly coordinated with shaping than with attending.

"Connections between Attending and Shaping were also present across all three weeks (Week 1: 0.47; Week 2: 0.46; Week 3: 0.46), while Attending–Interpreting links remained comparatively weaker (Week 1: 0.30; Week 2: 0.54; Week 3: 0.23)."
