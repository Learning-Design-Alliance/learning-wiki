Evidence¶

Zara Contractor and Germán Reyes 2026¶

Zara Contractor and Germán Reyes. (2026). Experimental Evidence on the Learning Impact of Generative AI. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08849 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.08849

q4 · i? · causal · r3

Intent-to-treat estimate from a proctored, in-person randomized experiment (211 undergraduates, Session One) in which the AI-allowed group could use any generative AI during a 35-minute learning phase and all students took the test without resources. The article reports "a 13 percent improvement over the control mean" and a standardized 0.28 SD effect (p= 0.021); the corresponding TOT estimate is 10.4 pp, or 0.41 SD.

"Control students’ fraction of questions answered correctly rises from 30.6 percent to 56.3 percent (p<0.001); AI access adds a further ˆβ= 7.2pp, a 13 percent improvement over the control mean."
