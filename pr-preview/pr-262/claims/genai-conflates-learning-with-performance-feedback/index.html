Peter Wulff and Marcus Kubsch 2025¶

Peter Wulff and Marcus Kubsch. (2025). Learning against the machine: the double edged sword of (Gen)AI in STEM education. International Journal of STEM Education 12:66. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-025-00588-6

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical argument in this commentary by Wulff and Kubsch, offered without new data. The authors state that "many current GenAI applications conflate learning with performance and feedback" and that commercial products "may instead promote superficial understanding."

"we argue that many current GenAI applications conflate learning with performance and feedback, neglecting the active, reflective, and embodied processes that underpin meaningful learning in STEM disciplines"
