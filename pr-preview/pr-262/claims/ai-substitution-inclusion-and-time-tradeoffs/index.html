Rummel 2026¶

Rummel, N., Nachtigall, V., & Panadero, E. (2026). “With a Thermomix You Lose the Ability to Cook”: A Kitchen Machine Analogy for Applications of Generative AI in Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09856 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.09856

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Authors' reasoned conjecture in the substitution section: while unlikely to foster high cognitive engagement, AI substitution of assignments "might nonetheless promote inclusion and participation" for struggling students. Presented as a hypothetical opportunity, not a tested result.

"might nonetheless promote inclusion and participation among students who would otherwise struggle to complete their work due to special needs or language barriers."

Rummel 2026 (2)¶

Rummel, N., Nachtigall, V., & Panadero, E. (2026). “With a Thermomix You Lose the Ability to Cook”: A Kitchen Machine Analogy for Applications of Generative AI in Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09856. doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.09856

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Authors' stated trade-off on the substitution opportunity: freeing time for searching, reading, synthesizing, and revising comes with the risk that "Reliance on AI for basic functions risks diminishing the acquisition of foundational skills". A theoretical caveat, not an empirical finding.

"Reliance on AI for basic functions risks diminishing the acquisition of foundational skills - such as composing grammatically correct texts from scratch - and presupposes a certain level of prior knowledge."
