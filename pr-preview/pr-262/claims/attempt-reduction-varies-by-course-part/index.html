q2 · i? · causal · r2

NB-GLMM on attempts with hint type × course part interaction (Table 3). Single-Issue × Old Content β=−0.131 (HDI [−0.248, −0.018]); General × Old Content β=−0.107 (HDI including zero). Unlike time to success, no significant between-part difference appeared without feedback.

"For the New Content , again the Diagnostic Feedback is associated with fewer attempts, but interestingly, the General Feedback , which also hints to solution, shows a reduced number of attempts. For the Old Content , the Single-Issue Feedback is associated with fewer Attempts to Success."

Related Claims¶

  • Advanced students showed reduced attempts with all hint types, while novices did not clearly benefit and made slightly more submissions with Single-Issue Feedback — related
  • All three LLM feedback types credibly reduced attempts to success relative to the baseline, with the largest reduction for Diagnostic Feedback — a broader claim this one bears on
  • Feedback effectiveness varied by content recency: guidance-heavy feedback performed less consistently on newer material released after the model's knowledge cutoff — related
  • All three LLM-generated feedback types are associated with faster time to solution than a compiler-errors-only baseline, with Single-Issue Feedback showing the largest reduction — related
  • Student-voted Good feedback was associated with far fewer attempts and shorter solution times than Poor feedback, and thematic analysis identified outdated references and false positives as common failure modes — related
