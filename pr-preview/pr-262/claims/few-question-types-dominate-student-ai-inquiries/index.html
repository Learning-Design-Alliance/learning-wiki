Matin Amoozadeh and Amin Alipour 2026¶

Matin Amoozadeh and Amin Alipour. (2026). Analysis of Types of Inquiries in Student-AI Interaction: A case study of two CS2 tasks. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17919 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.17919

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Descriptive frequency analysis of 830 logged prompts (432 in Session 1, 398 in Session 2) classified into Graesser categories. The article reports Assertion was "the most frequent category in both sessions (S1 = 86, S2 = 82)". Higher-order categories such as Comparison and Enablement remained minimal.
