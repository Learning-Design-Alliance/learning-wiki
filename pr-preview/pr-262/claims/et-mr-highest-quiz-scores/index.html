This is the article's own statement in its Implications section comparing the three conditions' quiz outcomes; it reports no test statistic or effect size, only that "ET and MR conditions produced the highest quiz scores."

"Although ET and MR conditions produced the highest quiz scores, feedback indicated that relying on a single modality could reduce engagement over time due to diminished novelty, underscoring the need for integrated multimodal designs."
