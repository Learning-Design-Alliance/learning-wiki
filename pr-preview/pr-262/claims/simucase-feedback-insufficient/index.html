Evidence¶

Elliott 2021¶

Elliott, Haley and Brumbaugh, Klaire (2021). Student Perceptions of a Simulated Clinical Experience: A Pilot Study. Teaching and Learning in Communication Sciences & Disorders, 5(1), Article 7. https://doi.org/10.30707/TLCSD5.1.1624982519.538001

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Focus-group theme from the 10-participant qualitative pilot. Typed-response subtests provided no feedback, leading some students to type anything for credit; participants suggested the platform supply acceptable answers for comparison. This replicates limited-feedback findings in Clinard and Dudding (2019) and Ellis (2017).

"All participants indicated the feedback provided was not beneficial. It was stated by numerous participants that Simucase (2019) would mark something as incorrect but would not give reasoning."
