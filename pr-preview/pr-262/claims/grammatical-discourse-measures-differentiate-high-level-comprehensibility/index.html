Talia Isaacs and Pavel Trofimovich 2012¶

Talia Isaacs and Pavel Trofimovich. (2012). DECONSTRUCTING COMPREHENSIBILITY: Identifying the Linguistic Influences on Listeners' L2 Comprehensibility Ratings. Studies in Second Language Acquisition, Volume 34, Issue 3. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0272263112000150

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Same correlational analysis of 40 French L2 speakers' speech with 60 listeners' ratings. The article reports that "grammatical and discourse-level measures differentiated between high-level learners."

"Lexical richness and fluency measures differentiated between low-level learners; grammatical and discourse-level measures differentiated between high-level learners; and word stress errors discriminated between learners of all levels."
