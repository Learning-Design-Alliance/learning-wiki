Tertiary review of reviews synthesising secondary research on AI in higher education indexed across seven databases plus snowballing. The corpus characteristics are reported as printed: "predominantly systematic reviews (66.7%)", team authorship 89.4%, domestic-only collaboration 71.2%. No effect sizes are printed.

"66 publications were included for data extraction and synthesis in EPPI Reviewer, which were predominantly systematic reviews (66.7%), published by authors from North America (27.3%), conducted in teams (89.4%) in mostly domestic-only collaborations (71.2%)."
