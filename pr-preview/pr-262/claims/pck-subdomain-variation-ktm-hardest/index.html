Evidence¶

Copur-Gencturk 2026¶

Copur-Gencturk, Y., Moreno, K., Chu, Y., Li, H., & Tang, J. (2026). Automated coding of content and pedagogical content knowledge of mathematics using a multi-agent large language model. ZDM – Mathematics Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11858-026-01796-2

q2 · i? · design · r3

Subdomain-level results from the same test-set evaluation of nine PCK items. The article attributes the difficulty to complex teaching scenarios and cognitive demands such as applying a teaching strategy and explaining the rationale, noting these items were also challenging for human coders.

"GradeOpt's performance was acceptable for overall PCK (Cohen's κ = .68) but varied across PCK subdomains, with Cohen's κ values of .66 for knowledge of mathematical tasks, .79 for knowledge of students' mathematical thinking, and .59 for knowledge of teaching mathematics. These results indicate that all models, including GradeOpt, were particularly challenged by coding the knowledge of teaching mathematics items."
