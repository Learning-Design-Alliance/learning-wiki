The review's synthesis of the 26 included VET studies reports that "Intelligent Extended Reality shows consistent positive effects on procedural competence, practical skills, and learner motivation". No pooled effect sizes were computed; the review states a quantitative meta-analysis was not feasible given intervention heterogeneity.

"Intelligent Extended Reality shows consistent positive effects on procedural competence, practical skills, and learner motivation; Intelligent Tutoring Systems foster declarative and procedural knowledge;"
