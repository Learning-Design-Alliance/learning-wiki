Tomlinson 2026¶

Tomlinson, B., Black, R.W., Patterson, D.J., van der Hoek, A., Ferguson, J. & Bietz, M.J. (2026). Students prefer personalized, AI-generated educational videos over non-personalized, human-recorded videos. Scientific Reports 16:21804. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-52798-9

q1 · i? · associational · r1

Illustrative analysis of two open-ended survey questions about benefits and drawbacks of the personalized AI videos; the authors state no formal qualitative coding was conducted. Students also "described the videos as monotone, robotic, or unsettling, with several explicitly invoking uncanny valley effects" and some questioned whether content was human-verified.

"Students also repeatedly highlighted clarity, consistency, and conciseness as strengths of the AI-generated videos."
