Farnaz Baksh, Matevž B. Zorec, Feiazie Baksh, and Karl Kruusamäe. (2026). Co-Creating Buildable and Open Social Robot Study Companions with University Students. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15239 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.15239

q2 · i? · design · r2

Study 2 NASA-TLX subscale comparison against Study 1 developers, reported via Fig. 4. The article attributes the difference to part identification effort per participants; no statistic is printed.

"However, user Mental Demand during assembly remained higher than that of developers (Fig. 4), which participants attributed to the effort of identifying parts and reinforcing wiring identification."

Farnaz Baksh 2026 (2)¶

Farnaz Baksh, Matevž B. Zorec, Feiazie Baksh, and Karl Kruusamäe. (2026). Co-Creating Buildable and Open Social Robot Study Companions with University Students. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15239

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Study 2 qualitative debriefs with the seven first-time builders. The article notes this tactile fragility was absent among developers in Study 1 and represents a feedback issue distinct from documentation clarity.

"Several participants reported concerns around snap-fit components, un-certain whether resistance signalled an error or correct insertion: “Scared I would break things with the components that are meant to snap in” (P3-GY)."
