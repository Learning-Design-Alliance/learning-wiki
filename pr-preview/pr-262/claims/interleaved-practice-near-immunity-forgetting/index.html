Evidence¶

Rohrer 2015¶

Rohrer, D., Dedrick, R. F., & Stershic, S. (2015). Interleaved practice improves mathematics learning. Journal of Educational Psychology, 107(3), 900-908. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000001

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Authors' descriptive comparison of interleaved-condition test means across the two delays in the classroom experiment. Only the printed means (80% vs. 74%) support this; no test of the within-condition delay effect is quoted.

"apart from its superiority to blocked practice, interleaved practice provided near immunity against forgetting, as the 30-fold increase in test delay reduced test scores by less than a tenth (from 80% to 74%)."
