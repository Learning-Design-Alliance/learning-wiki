Fan Ouyang and Weiqi Xu 2024¶

Fan Ouyang and Weiqi Xu. (2024). The effects of educational robotics in STEM education: a multilevel meta-analysis. International Journal of STEM Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-024-00469-4

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Omnibus moderator tests in the multilevel model for the seven remaining categorical moderators. None reached significance; e.g., educational level F(3, 25) = 2.5690 and instructor support F(1, 28) = 0.1951, both p > 0.05. Kindergarten and engineering categories were excluded for too few effect sizes.

"However, educational level, instructor support, instructional strategy, interactive type, intervention duration, robotic type and control group condition were insignificantly associated to the students' learning effects (p > 0.05)."
