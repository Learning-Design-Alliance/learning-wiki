Evidence¶

Fan Ouyang and Weiqi Xu 2024¶

Fan Ouyang and Weiqi Xu. (2024). The effects of educational robotics in STEM education: a multilevel meta-analysis. International Journal of STEM Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-024-00469-4

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

Multilevel meta-analysis of the learning-performance outcome (academic achievement, task performance, conceptual knowledge gains) from 14 effect sizes in 12 studies. The mean effect was g = 0.665, p < 0.01, described as "a moderate-to-large effect".

"The multilevel modeling analysis found a mean effect size of 0.665 (SE = 0.18; p < 0.01; 95% CI was 0.275–1.054), which indicated that educational robotics had a moderate-to-large effect on improving students' learning performances in STEM education"
