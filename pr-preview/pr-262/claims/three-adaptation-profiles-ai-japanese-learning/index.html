Evidence¶

Wu Y 2026¶

Wu Y (2026) Proﬁles and transitions of psychological adaptation in AI-assisted Japanese language learning. Front. Psychol. 17:1837484. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1837484

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Latent profile analyses at each of three waves using five technostress dimensions and resilience as indicators; the three-profile solution had the lowest information criteria, entropy of 0.906 (T1), 0.879 (T2), and 0.929 (T3), and significant LMR and BLRT results.

"At Time 1, the three-proﬁle model yielded lower AIC, BIC, and adjusted BIC values than the one- and two-proﬁle models, together with high classiﬁcation accuracy (entropy= 0.906) and signiﬁcant LMR and BLRT results."

Related Claims¶

  • Higher self-rated Japanese proficiency, more frequent AI use, and prior AI-related training predicted membership in the positive versus maladaptive adaptation profile at Time 1 — related
  • Positive adaptation showed the highest temporal stability while maladaptive adaptation was the least stable profile across the semester — related
  • At Time 3, profile membership was significantly associated with Japanese learning self-efficacy and learning burnout, with all three profiles differing pairwise — related
  • Trust and intrinsic motivation means rose across the semester while extrinsic motivation stayed relatively stable, and longitudinal measurement invariance held across waves — related
  • Latent learner profiles did not moderate the effectiveness of the adaptive ITS — related
