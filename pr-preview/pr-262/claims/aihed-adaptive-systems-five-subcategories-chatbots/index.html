q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Synthesis of the 36 AIHEd reviews addressing adaptive systems and personalisation, all situated at the teaching and learning level. The five subcategories and their counts are as printed in the review's synthesis. No effect sizes are printed.

"Five subcategories were found: chatbots/virtual assistants (n = 20), providing personalised content (n = 14), facial recognition/mood detection (n = 9), recommender systems/course scheduling (n = 5), and robots (n = 3)."

q2 · i? · review · r3

The tertiary review reports, citing Klímová & Ibna Seraj (2023) and Zhai & Wibowo (2023), that chatbots positively influenced EFL speaking skills, possibly partly due to reduced anxiety. The reviewed studies' designs were not read directly; attributed as the source attributes it.

"a review of English as a foreign language literature (Klímová & Ibna Seraj, 2023) finding chatbots having a particularly positive influence on developing speaking skills (intonation, stress, and fluency), possibly in part due to feelings of reduced anxiety (Zhai & Wibowo, 2023)"
