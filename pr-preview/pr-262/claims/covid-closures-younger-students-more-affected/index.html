Evidence¶

Hammerstein Svenja 2021¶

Hammerstein Svenja, König Christoph, Dreisörner Thomas, Frey Andreas. (2021). Effects of COVID-19-Related School Closures on Student Achievement-A Systematic Review. Frontiers in Psychology. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.746289

q3 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r2

Review synthesis of differential effects across the eleven included studies, reporting the age contrast with printed effect sizes d = −0.37 SD for elementary versus d = −0.10 SD for secondary students (Tomasik et al., 2020). The review attributes this to younger children's reliance on cognitive scaffolding and less developed self-regulated learning.

"First, the main finding was that younger children were more negatively affected in their learning than older children were (-0.37 SD vs.−0.10 SD; Tomasik et al., 2020)."
