Step 3 of the hierarchical regression on the same 303-student survey added digital competence (SDiCoS scale). The article reports "The R-square value of model 3 is 0.32 in contrast with 0.29 in model 2", and notes the perceived-resources coefficient fell from 0.39 to 0.24 while remaining significant.
