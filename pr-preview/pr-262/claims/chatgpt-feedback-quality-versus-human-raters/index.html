Steiss 2023¶

Steiss, Jacob; Tate, Tamara; Graham, Steve; Cruz, Jazmin; Hebert, Michael; Wang, Jiali; Moon, Youngsun; Tseng, Waverly; Warschauer, Mark. (2023). Comparing the Quality of Human and ChatGPT Feedback on Students' Writing. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Comparing+the+Quality+of+Human+and+ChatGPT+Feedback+on+Students+Writing

q3 · i? · causal · r2

One-way ANOVA comparing blinded coded ratings of human and ChatGPT feedback on the same 198 secondary history essays. Human raters won four components; "AI outperformed human evaluators by .24 points on average (p = .03)" on criteria-based.

"We find that human feedback is better than AI feedback in every category except criteria-based, where AI outperformed human evaluators by .24 points on average (p = .03)."
