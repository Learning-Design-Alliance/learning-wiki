Use real-world problems, open-ended class discussions, and inquiry-oriented experiments as higher order thinking strategies¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article names three concrete classroom strategies for promoting higher order thinking: "dealing in class with real-world problems, encouraging open-ended class discussions, and fostering inquiry-oriented experiments". These are offered as examples of the persistent practice the study links to critical thinking development, and are implementable in science lessons.
