Cultivate AI literacy as relational rather than transactional work through reflective engagement with human-AI entanglement¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that educators cultivate AI literacy through a posthumanist prism as relational work: encouraging reflective, critical conversations about participation in an AI-mediated world and a critical sensibility to human-AI entanglement, rather than framing AI as a mere tool or over-anthropomorphizing it. The authors state that "cultivating AI literacy through a posthumanist prism is, essentially, relational work rather than transactional work." This avoids both punitive responses to student AI use and uncritical reliance on AI affordances.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Educators must move beyond the human/tool binary and the modernist anthropocentric paradigm that attributes agency solely to humans

Constraints¶

  • The authors warn that corporate anthropomorphic discourse, e.g. claims that AI boosts productivity, shapes a transactional perspective that this strategy must question

Target Learners¶

  • language and literacy educators
  • multilingual undergraduate writers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • critical AI literacies
  • meta-awareness of AI-mediated meaning-making
  • ethical engagement with AI

Affordances¶

  • Posthumanist Ai Literacy Intra Action Framework
