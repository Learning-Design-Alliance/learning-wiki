Implement an AI-Peer-Teacher hybrid integrated feedback model staged across drafting, revision, and polishing phases¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article proposes a staged hybrid feedback workflow for L2 writing instruction: "during the initial drafting phase, AI provides real-time standardized content logic guidance and basic error marking; in the in-depth revision phase, peer collaboration focuses on deep mutual evaluation and negotiation; and in the final polishing phase, teachers offer 'expert' high-level feedback". The model aims to integrate the breadth of tool-mediated support, the depth of expert guidance, and the extent of social interaction, supporting learners across different levels of ZPDs. It is grounded in the study's finding that the two feedback modes are heterogeneous scaffolds with complementary strengths.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers assume supervisor and supporter roles, including sampling reviews of feedback quality and timely personalized interventions for struggling groups
  • Students receive training to improve their intermediary capabilities for effective interaction with AI

Constraints¶

  • The article states a single mode cannot comprehensively address the multidimensional challenges of L2 writing ability development, motivating the hybrid design

Target Learners¶

  • Second-year English majors in application-oriented universities

Target Learning Goals¶

  • L2 argumentative writing quality
  • content and organizational development
  • syntactic and lexical development
