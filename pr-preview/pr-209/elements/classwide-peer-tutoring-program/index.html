ClassWide Peer Tutoring (CWPT) program for beginning reading¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

CWPT is a peer-assisted instructional strategy designed to be integrated with most existing reading curricula, in which pairs of students take turns tutoring each other with immediate feedback, reinforcing skills initially taught by the teacher. It is "practiced 30 minutes a day throughout the week, including 20 minutes for tutoring and 10 minutes for material preparation," with weekly individual testing and pretesting. Students are paired into two teams, award points for correct answers, and the team with the most points is announced daily and recognized each Friday.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • The teacher creates age-appropriate peer teaching materials that take into account tutees' language skills and disabilities

Constraints¶

  • The report focuses on CWPT for beginning reading at elementary grade levels emphasizing reading fluency and comprehension skills

Target Learners¶

  • elementary school students in beginning reading, including regular and special education students and English language learners

Target Learning Goals¶

  • reading fluency and comprehension skills
  • drill and memorization content such as spelling words, reading workbooks, and vocabulary words

Claims¶

  • Greenwood et al. (1993) reported a statistically significant CWPT effect on CTBS-Reading, but the WWC-corrected analysis was not statistically significant [~W]
  • The evidence base for CWPT in beginning reading is small, with no standards-meeting studies of alphabetics, fluency, or comprehension [~W]
  • Severe and differential attrition at grade 4 (68.2% intervention vs. 44.2% comparison) led the WWC to exclude grade-4 outcomes from review [~W]

Related Elements¶

  • CWPT Learning Management System (CWPT–LMS) software support

Examples¶

  • Implement CWPT through daily peer-tutoring sessions with points, teams, and weekly testing
