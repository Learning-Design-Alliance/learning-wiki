Teacher Potential Project (TPP): CCSS-aligned EL Education Language Arts Curriculum combined with intensive professional development¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Teacher Potential Project is a professional learning program developed by EL Education that pairs "a Common Core State Standards (CCSS)-aligned EL Education Language Arts Curriculum" with "intensive PD". It was designed "to address the need for high quality and lasting professional learning for teachers" and was evaluated by Mathematica Policy Research in an independent study funded by a U.S. Department of Education Investing in Innovation (i3) grant, examining teachers' practices after one and two years of engagement.

Claims¶

  • Students taught by TPP teachers engaged significantly more often in reading, writing, and speaking about texts in the second year of TPP [+W]
  • Significantly more TPP teachers asked students to cite evidence from texts in discussions and writing [+W]
  • Significantly more TPP teachers encouraged students' higher-order thinking skills than district-supported comparison teachers [+W]
  • A significantly greater proportion of TPP teachers demonstrated and sustained CCSS-aligned instructional practices compared with district-supported teachers [+W]
