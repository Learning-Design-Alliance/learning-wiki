American Board for Certification of Teacher Excellence (ABCTE) alternative teacher certification¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 3 studies (1 causal, 1 associational, 1 design), q1–q2 · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

ABCTE is an alternative route to teacher certification whose effectiveness the study evaluates by comparing the classroom achievement outcomes of its certificated teachers with those of other Florida teachers. The article describes it as the subject of the question of whether "alternatively certified teachers produce student achievement gains on par with other teachers." The evaluation covered 30 ABCTE and matched non-ABCTE teachers over two years of student reading and math performance in Florida.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Evaluation of the certification route requires a matched comparison group of non-ABCTE teachers and student achievement data, as the study obtained through propensity score matching.

Constraints¶

  • The evidence base described here is limited to 30 teachers over two years in Florida.

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students in Florida public schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Student achievement in reading and mathematics

Claims¶

  • Students of ABCTE teachers scored lower than students of non-ABCTE teachers on the Florida state math test [-W]
  • Students of ABCTE-certified teachers and non-ABCTE teachers show no difference in reading achievement gains in Florida [+W]
  • Propensity score matching was used to build a comparison group of Florida teachers for estimating ABCTE certification effects [+W]
  • A five-year evaluation is assessing the impact of the Passport to Teaching credential on teacher performance measured by student achievement [+W]
  • School administrators gave a generally positive assessment of American Board-certified teachers based on supervisor surveys [+W]
