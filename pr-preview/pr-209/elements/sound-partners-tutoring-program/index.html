Sound Partners one-to-one paraprofessional tutoring program in early reading¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Sound Partners is a 1-1 tutoring program that uses paraprofessionals as tutors. Students receive "tutoring 30 minutes a day, 4 days a week, for 18-20 weeks." The tutoring "focuses on phonics, phonemic awareness, sight words, and oral reading practice using decodable texts," with somewhat different content in kindergarten and first grade.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A local trainer (reading specialist, teacher, or special education teacher) trains the local tutors (educational assistants and paraeducators).

Target Learning Goals¶

  • phonics

Claims¶

  • Sound Partners outcomes for English learners persist two years later on Word Reading and Comprehension [+W]
  • Sound Partners produces significantly positive but smaller reading outcomes for English learner first graders (effect size +0.15) [+W]
  • Sound Partners produces significantly positive reading outcomes for English learner kindergartners (effect size +0.60) [+W]
