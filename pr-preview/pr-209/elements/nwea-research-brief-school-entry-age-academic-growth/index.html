NWEA research brief on school entry age and academic growth through second grade¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A June 2020 NWEA research brief by Angela Johnson and Megan Kuhfeld addressing the question "Does entering school older give students an edge?" It summarizes a multi-state regression discontinuity analysis of academic growth through second grade, and is listed under the topics "Student growth & accountability policies" and "Early learning". It is associated with a working paper of the same study.

Target Learning Goals¶

  • The school-entry-age findings were produced with a multi-state regression discontinuity analysis covering academic growth through second grade [+W]
