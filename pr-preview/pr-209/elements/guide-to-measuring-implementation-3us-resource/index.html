Guide to Measuring Implementation: The 3Us — a practical measurement guidance resource¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A published guidance resource from Mathematica (February 2023) that, in its own words, "provides guidance on when and how to measure these indicators, and what implementing organizations can do with the results." It addresses both the mechanics of measurement (when and how) and the use of findings, making it a practical tool rather than a research report.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Users should be implementing organizations or funders of educational solutions seeking to measure usability, usefulness, and utilization

Constraints¶

  • The resource is guidance rather than an empirical evaluation; it reports no measured outcomes itself

Target Learners¶

  • implementing organizations
  • education funders

Target Learning Goals¶

  • measuring implementation quality of educational solutions

Affordances¶

  • 3Us Framework Usability Usefulness Utilization

Related Elements¶

  • Suite of Mathematica tools supporting grantees to develop, refine, and scale evidence-based solutions
  • Companion Mathematica publications supporting M&E planning: Prior Research Table, Targets Guide, Sample Size Guide, and Reporting Template

Examples¶

  • Measure usability, usefulness, and utilization during development and early implementation
  • Use a suite of measurement and evaluation tools to develop, refine, and scale evidence-based educational solutions
