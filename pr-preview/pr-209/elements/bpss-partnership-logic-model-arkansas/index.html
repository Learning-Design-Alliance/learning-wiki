BPSS partnership logic model for supporting ADE college and career readiness programming¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The BPSS partnership is organized around a stated goal: "To support the Arkansas Department of Education (ADE) in both examining and improving programming to increase college and career readiness for Arkansas students." The logic model arranges activities and outcomes in short-term, medium-term, and long-term columns spanning ADE staff capacity, district participation, and student outcomes. It anchors two studies (AIIM and THRIVE) and a planned 2024–2026 impact-assessment phase.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Key assumptions must hold: "(1) THRIVE and AIIM will exist long enough to realize potential benefits from improvements. (2) Short- and medium-term outcomes can be realized during the REL Southwest contract period."

Constraints¶

  • Outcomes are conditioned on the REL Southwest contract period, per the partnership's stated key assumptions.

Target Learners¶

  • Arkansas K-12 students in districts participating in THRIVE and/or AIIM

Target Learning Goals¶

  • College and career readiness
  • reduction of disparities in academic and behavioral outcomes

Claims¶

  • The BPSS logic model targets increased college readiness among Arkansas student populations with persistent outcome disparities [+W]
  • The BPSS logic model sets reduced discipline disparities as a long-term student outcome for THRIVE and AIIM districts [+W]
  • The logic model's medium-term outcomes center on ADE staff data-use and formative-evaluation capacity [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • Studies of Arkansas AIIM and THRIVE with findings presentations and infographic products

Examples¶

  • Provide formative evaluation and implementation coaching to strengthen ADE initiative design
