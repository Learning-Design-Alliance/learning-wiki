Principal time-use log dataset from approximately 100 elementary schools in eight districts¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The snapshot is based on detailed principal time-use logs collected to measure how elementary principals spend their workweek. The article states it "analyzes detailed principal time-use log data from approximately 100 elementary schools in eight school districts," providing descriptive evidence on the share of time devoted to instructional support. It serves as the data source for all findings in the snapshot.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Detailed time-use logs completed by elementary school principals across participating districts

Constraints¶

  • Descriptive snapshot covering elementary schools in eight districts; no causal effects are estimated

Target Learners¶

  • Elementary school principals

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Understanding how principals allocate workweek time to instructional support for teachers

Claims¶

  • Elementary school principals spend more than one-third of their workweek providing instructional support to teachers, split fairly evenly between direct and indirect support [+W]
  • Principals with elementary school teaching experience reported providing more extensive direct instructional support to teachers [+W]
  • According to teachers, principals with elementary teaching experience provided more helpful and actionable direct instructional support in some cases [+W]
