AMSTI: professional development strategy with summer training, on-site coaching, and rotating materials kits¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

AMSTI is a professional development strategy for elementary and middle school teachers and principals. Participants attend a 2-week summer training session followed by on-site coaching during the school year, with a focus on hands-on, inquiry-based instruction in math and science. Teachers are also provided with all necessary materials organized in "kits" that are rotated among schools, including teacher and student guides, student assessments, software, manipulatives, and hands-on activities.

Claims¶

  • AMSTI schools scored significantly higher than control schools on math problem solving at the student level, with an average effect size of +0.05 [+M]
  • AMSTI qualifies for the Promising evidence rating based on one study of 9343 students [+M]
  • Teachers participating in AMSTI reported more engaged students, greater content knowledge, and increased use of active learning strategies [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • Rotating materials kits supplying all hands-on math and science instructional materials

Examples¶
