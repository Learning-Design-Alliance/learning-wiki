Content-Focused Coaching (CFC) professional development program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Content-Focused Coaching is a professional development approach in which district coaches work with all teachers through in-class demonstrations, weekly grade-level meetings, and individual monthly visits for co-teaching, observation, and feedback. Coaches receive extensive training in the literacy strategies and in coaching methods, with schools receiving "about 15 days per year for 2 years" of professional development and coaching. No additional staff or technology are needed.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Coaches receive extensive training in the literacy strategies and in coaching methods
  • Schools receive professional development and coaching from trained coaches, about 15 days per year for 2 years

Constraints¶

  • No additional staff needed
  • None needed in the way of technology

Target Learners¶

  • Fourth and fifth grade students
  • English learners

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Reading comprehension through questioning, interpretation, and active response to texts

Claims¶

  • Content-Focused Coaching shows a larger effect for English learners (+0.46) than for non-English learners (+0.18) [+M]
  • Content-Focused Coaching raises fourth and fifth graders' achievement by +0.29 compared to control groups [+W]

Related Elements¶

Examples¶

  • Combine in-class demonstrations, weekly grade-level meetings, and monthly individual coaching visits for all teachers
