EarthTime data visualizations provide interactive, time-lapse representations of large data sets for classroom discovery¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), q3 · 2 of 2 report an effect size

Description¶

EarthTime, from the CREATE Lab, is a data-visualization technology used in the session to let students watch satellite time-lapse imagery of places such as Las Vegas, Lake Mead, and Dubai. The deck states the lab "provides interactive and visual representations of large data sets which reveal patterns that encourage discovery and facilitate communication." Teachers slow the playback, hone in on small areas, and capture gifs of change over time.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Access to the EarthTime interactive visualization technology and internet-connected devices for viewing time-lapse imagery

Constraints¶

  • The deck frames the tool for guided observation of geographic change over time, with research questions about causes handled through outside research such as the National Park Service

Target Learners¶

  • Students in grades 3-8

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Observing and describing change over time in data visualizations
  • Generating questions from data patterns
