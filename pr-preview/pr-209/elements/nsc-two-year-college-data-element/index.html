National Student Clearinghouse (NSC) data as a source for two-year college outcomes¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (2 for, 2 against) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

NSC is a nonprofit central repository for college enrollment and completion data, with institutions serving 97 percent of college students voluntarily sharing enrollment data. The article describes its strengths as consistent, comprehensive, and national in coverage, including out-of-state and private institutions. Its limitations include limited program information, incomplete certificate and degree information, missing colleges and students, and possible matching errors, since "Colleges that provide records to NSC may opt to not share records for non-degree seeking students."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Institutional participation in voluntary NSC reporting and a data services agreement (CPS obtains NSC data for its graduates)

Constraints¶

  • Credential name field often blank for two-year completers
  • Some colleges, particularly for-profit two-year colleges, do not provide data
  • Students may opt out under FERPA, and some colleges omit records for students without SSNs
  • Matching is based on name and birthdate and can introduce a small amount of error

Target Learners¶

  • High school graduates whose postsecondary enrollment is tracked by districts and states

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Reporting college enrollment and credential completion rates for research, policy, and accountability

Claims¶

  • NSC data cannot be used alone to calculate two-year credential-type completion rates because 57.5 percent of completion records have a blank credential name field [-W]
  • Full-time immediate two-year enrollment rates differ substantially between ICCB and NSC data, suggesting limited reliability of the NSC full-time status field [-W]
  • NSC and ICCB data agree on any-credential completion within four years for 96 percent of immediate two-year enrollees [+W]
  • NSC and ICCB data agree on immediate two-year college enrollment for about 96 percent of students, with ICCB showing a slightly higher enrollment rate [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • National Student Clearinghouse StudentTracker for High Schools
  • Illinois Community College Board (ICCB) data via the Illinois Longitudinal Data System

Examples¶

  • Use state longitudinal data systems to supplement NSC data when reporting detailed two-year college outcomes
