SAILS: Tennessee's computer-based senior-year remedial math course waiving college math remediation¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

SAILS is a computer-based remedial math course that Tennessee high school students could complete during their senior year beginning in 2012; completion waives college math remediation. The article describes it as a way students could "avoid the cost and delay of remedial math in college." It was evaluated with regression discontinuity and difference-in-difference designs and was later juxtaposed with co-requisite remediation adopted in 2015.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Students must complete the computer-based SAILS course during their senior year of high school to receive the college remediation waiver

Constraints¶

  • The credit boost associated with SAILS disappeared once co-requisite remediation replaced pre-requisite remediation in 2015

Target Learners¶

  • High school seniors in Tennessee who would otherwise require college math remediation

Target Learning Goals¶

  • College-level math readiness and avoidance of developmental math coursework in college

Claims¶

  • Completing SAILS boosted first-year community college students' enrollment in college-level math by nearly 30 percentage points [+W]
  • Under Tennessee's 2015 co-requisite remediation policy, completing SAILS no longer produced any boost in college credits [~W]
  • The high school SAILS remedial math course did not improve math achievement more than a typical senior year math course [-W]
  • SAILS completers had completed only 1.5 additional college courses after two years [+W]
