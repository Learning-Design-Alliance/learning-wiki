Classroom Organization and Management Program (COMP)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The Classroom Organization and Management Program (COMP) is a manualized classroom management program that appeared in 7 of the 12 included studies (58%), making it the most frequently used intervention in the review. It involves teacher training in classroom management, with the teacher as implementer of the strategies targeting student problem behavior as the outcome. COMP studies produced a statistically significant effect size ES=.75 (p =.00).

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Delivered as teacher training with the classroom teacher as implementer of the strategies

Constraints¶

  • Its effects did not differ significantly from other classroom management approaches in the posthoc comparison

Target Learners¶

  • Students in K-12 classrooms of teachers trained in COMP

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Reducing problem classroom behavior

Claims¶

  • Manualized COMP programs and other classroom management approaches do not differ significantly in effectiveness [~M]
