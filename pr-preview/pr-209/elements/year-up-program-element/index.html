Year Up occupational training and internship program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (1 for, 4 mixed) · 6 studies (5 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), q3 · 2 of 6 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Year Up is an occupational and technical education intervention for low-income young adults ages 18 to 24 with a high school diploma or equivalent. Students enroll in learning communities of about 40 students and complete "six months of full-time occupational and technical training followed by a six-month internship in the information technology or financial services sectors." Supports include advising, mentoring, instructional support, social services, weekly stipends of around $150 during training and $200 during internship, and post-program job search and placement support for up to four months. Training covers technical skills, business communications, and workplace functioning skills, structured so students can earn college credit.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires local employers in target occupational fields to host six-month internships
  • Requires staff advisors (each assigned four to eight students), social workers, and external mentors from the local business community
  • Operating costs are primarily financed by employer payments for interns, foundation grants, and charitable donations; average cost was $28,290 per student

Constraints¶

  • The training is delivered face-to-face from a single central location in each city, so implementation depends on local office infrastructure
  • Stipend amounts are contingent on compliance with and progress in the program

Target Learners¶

  • Low-income young adults ages 18 to 24 with a high school diploma or equivalent

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Technical skills in occupational tracks such as information technology, finance, quality assurance, project management, and customer service
  • Business communications including writing and public speaking
  • Workplace environment skills such as business etiquette and workplace relationship skills

Claims¶

  • Year Up may result in little to no change in industry-recognized credential, certificate, or license completion [~M]
  • Year Up may result in little to no change in medium-term earnings [~M]
  • Year Up may result in little to no change in medium-term employment [~M]
  • Year Up may result in little to no change in short-term employment [~M]
  • Year Up is likely to increase short-term earnings for low-income young adults [+M]
