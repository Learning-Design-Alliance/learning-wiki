Teacher Incentive Fund (TIF) performance pay evaluation study design report¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 8 claims (8 for) · 4 studies (3 causal, 1 design), q1–q3 · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A study design report prepared by Mathematica Policy Research for the U.S. Department of Education, National Center for Education Evaluation, as part of the Pay-for-Performance: Evaluating the Teacher Incentive Fund project (2009-2017). The article states it "provides contextual background on performance pay programs, characteristics of participating TIF grantee schools, and details on the TIF evaluation study design." It is a planning artifact for a rigorous evaluation rather than a report of results.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • The evaluation design is situated within the Teacher Incentive Fund grantee schools whose characteristics the report documents

Constraints¶

  • The supplied text reports design and context only; no impact estimates are printed

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students in TIF grantee schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Student achievement under teacher performance pay; educator attitudes, awareness, mobility, and recruitment

Claims¶

  • The TIF evaluation is designed to estimate impacts of performance pay on student achievement and on educators' attitudes, awareness, mobility, and recruitment [+W]
  • After three years of Teacher Incentive Fund implementation, pay-for-performance bonuses had small positive impacts on student math and reading achievement in ten evaluation districts [+W]
  • Few districts structured pay-for-performance bonuses to align well with TIF grant guidance [+W]
  • Offering pay-for-performance bonuses in 10 TIF districts increased student reading achievement by 1 percentile point after two years, with a similar-magnitude but statistically non-significant math effect [+W]
  • Many teachers misunderstood whether they were eligible for performance bonuses or the amount they could earn [+W]
  • A $20,000 selective transfer incentive prompted behavioral responses from high-performing teachers in 10 districts [+W]
  • In the 2010 TIF pay-for-performance programs, over 60% of teachers received a bonus, with average bonuses of about $1,800 and top performers earning more than 3 times the average [+W]
  • The average TIF bonus was about 4% of average teacher salary, below the 5% recommended by TIF grant guidance for substantial bonuses [+W]
