Chicago Public Schools elementary teacher stability dataset, 2003-2006¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

This element is a published data table of one-year teacher stability rates for Chicago Public Schools unit elementary schools across four fall snapshots (2003-2006). The document states it shows the "Percent of teachers who were still in their CPS schools in the fall of the year listed, compared to the previous fall." Each row lists a school with its four yearly stability percentages, covering hundreds of schools identified by CPS unit number and name.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Only schools with enrollment data in 2006-2007 are included, per the document's own scope statement.

Constraints¶

  • The document states: "These numbers do not reflect any adjustment for staff losses due to decreasing or increasing student enrollment."

Target Learners¶

  • Elementary school students in Chicago Public Schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Understanding staffing continuity in schools as a condition for instructional quality

Claims¶

  • Some Chicago elementary schools sustained very high teacher stability, at or near 100%, across the 2003-2006 period [+W]
  • Some Chicago elementary schools experienced sharp single-year drops in teacher stability, such as Ashburn falling from 86.7% to 20.0% [+W]
  • One-year teacher stability varies widely across Chicago elementary schools and years, with some schools below 50% and others at or near 100% [+W]
