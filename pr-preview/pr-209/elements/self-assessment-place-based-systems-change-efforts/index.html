Self-Assessment of Place-Based Systems Change Efforts instrument¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A self-assessment tool developed by Mathematica and Equal Measure that helps community partnerships 'take a closer look at their areas of strength and areas where they can go deeper to effect place-based systems change.' It draws on learning from cradle-to-career education partnerships and structures reflection on how well a partnership is functioning as a system-change effort.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A community partnership or collective effort willing to self-assess its strengths and gaps

Constraints¶

  • Draws from learning in cradle-to-career education, which the authors note when positioning it for broader application

Target Learners¶

  • community partnership members and collective-impact stakeholders in education and other sectors

Target Learning Goals¶

  • reflective self-assessment of partnership strength and depth for place-based systems change

Related Elements¶

  • Measurement and Evaluation Needs Inventory: a self-assessment tool for organizational M&E capacity
  • The Community Ecocycle tool for funder reflection and action in place-based systems change

Examples¶

  • Use a place-based systems-change self-assessment across sectors
  • Conduct an organizational M&E self-assessment early in the research cycle to target external support
