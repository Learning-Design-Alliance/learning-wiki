Longitudinal MAP reading dataset for 224 students with visual impairments¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A longitudinal dataset of reading achievement drawn from "Northwest Evaluation Association’s Measures of Academic Progress assessment" covering "224 students with visual impairments in grades 3–10, in four states over an eight-year time period." The article uses it to describe reading growth for this population and to benchmark it against a nationally normed general-population group. It is the evidentiary basis of the study's analysis.
