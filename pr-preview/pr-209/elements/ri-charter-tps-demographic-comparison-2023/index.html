Rhode Island charter, feeder TPS, and statewide TPS demographic comparison dataset¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The report tabulates 286 statewide TPS, 86 feeder TPS, and 31 charter schools, with total enrollments of 132,589, 42,204, and 8,859 students respectively. Charters serve higher-poverty populations (71% vs 67% in feeders), more Hispanic students (55% vs 38%), and fewer special education students (14% vs 20%). This demography table establishes the comparison context for the impact analysis.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • State enrollment and demographic records for all three school sectors

Constraints¶

  • Describes Rhode Island only; charter students are compared to feeder TPS populations rather than the statewide average

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 public school students in Rhode Island

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Equitable comparison of academic growth across school sectors

Claims¶

  • Charter attendance in Rhode Island is associated with positive, significant one-year growth gains in reading (0.16 SD) and math (0.15 SD) relative to matched TPS controls [+W]
  • A larger share of Rhode Island charter schools outperform local options (43% reading, 57% math) than the national rates found in the 2013 CREDO Study (25% reading, 29% math) [+W]
  • Substantial learning inequalities persist: TPS students in poverty, TPS English learners, and special education students in either setting show significant learning gaps [+W]
