Alumni Survey Design Toolkit for sustainability and scalability¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The Alumni Survey Design Toolkit is a planned artifact for sustainability and scalability, comprising four components: "1. Simplified survey for graduating seniors", "2. Guidance for LinkedIn usage and profile creation", "3. Instructions on how to repeat the design process independently", and "4. Sample survey questions". It is intended to let school communities repeat the co-design process on their own after the research partnership ends.
