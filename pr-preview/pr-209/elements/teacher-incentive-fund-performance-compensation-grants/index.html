Teacher Incentive Fund (TIF) performance-based compensation grants for high-need schools¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 7 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 4 against) · 3 studies (3 causal), q2–q3 · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Teacher Incentive Fund is a federal grant program established by Congress in 2006 that "provides grants to support performance-based compensation systems for teachers and principals in high-need schools." The evaluated intervention centers on performance bonuses tied to educator effectiveness and student achievement, implemented within districts' performance-based compensation systems over four grant years.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A performance-based compensation system for teachers and principals, supported by TIF grant funding

Constraints¶

  • Implemented in high-need schools; fewer than half the districts planned to keep offering performance bonuses once the grant was over

Target Learners¶

  • Students in high-need schools
  • Teachers and principals in high-need schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Improving student reading and math achievement
  • Improving educator effectiveness through performance-based compensation

Claims¶

  • Many teachers had only a limited understanding of the TIF bonus program: about 40 percent of eligible teachers knew they could receive a bonus, and teachers typically estimated the maximum bonus at about 40 percent of the actual maximum [-W]
  • By the second year of implementation, TIF performance bonuses improved reading and math achievement by 1 to 2 percentile points, equivalent to about four weeks of additional learning [+W]
  • Districts implemented most components of the performance-based compensation system throughout all four years, but fewer than half planned to keep offering performance bonuses once the grant ended [~W]
  • The average TIF bonus was about 4% of average teacher salary, below the 5% recommended by TIF grant guidance for substantial bonuses [-W]
  • Few districts structured pay-for-performance bonuses to align well with TIF grant guidance [-W]
  • After three years of Teacher Incentive Fund implementation, pay-for-performance bonuses had small positive impacts on student math and reading achievement in ten evaluation districts [+W]
  • Many teachers misunderstood whether they were eligible for performance bonuses or the amount they could earn [-W]
