Micro-credentialing (digital badges) for recognizing teacher competencies¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Micro-credentialing, or badging, is described as "a competency recognition system aligned with a series of gates or milestones recognized by a community." Issuers create credentials that can reward participation, completion, or—most valuably—demonstration of specific skills. The paper positions micro-credentials as marking initial preparation and recognizing ongoing development and leadership, noting their unique value is that they are specific, personal, efficient, flexible, transparent and portable.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Credentials awarded based on performance, which the paper says will be of greater value than those awarded strictly on participation
  • A community that recognizes the gates or milestones the badges mark

Constraints¶

  • The paper focuses only on credentials awarded for demonstrations of competence rather than participation or content consumption

Target Learners¶

  • Pre-service teachers
  • Practicing teachers seeking ongoing development and leadership recognition

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Recognition of demonstrated teaching skills and competencies
  • Ongoing professional growth across aspects of the education profession

Claims¶

  • Districts and universities increasingly attach concrete incentives to earning Digital Promise micro-credentials [+W]
