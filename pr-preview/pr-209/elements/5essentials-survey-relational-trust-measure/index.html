5Essentials school survey as a measure of school relational trust and climate¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article tags its work with "5Essentials school survey" and "school climate," indicating the comparative Valparaíso–Illinois relational trust study is associated with the 5Essentials survey measurement approach. The survey supplies the empirical data on trust among teachers, between teachers and principals, and between students and teachers collected from teachers and students in both regions.

Claims¶

  • Relational trust correlates with school leadership and with collaboration among teachers in both contexts [+W]
  • Relational trust appears to materialize in schools regardless of vast differences in environments [+W]
  • Teachers and students in Valparaíso and Illinois responded similarly and positively about trust among teachers, between teachers and principals, and between students and teachers [+W]
  • Teacher-parent trust was much lower in Valparaíso than in Illinois [+W]
  • School size and student socioeconomic status conditioned trust levels differently in the two contexts [~W]
