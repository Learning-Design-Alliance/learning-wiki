Early Science with Nico & Nor preschool science program for home and school¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 9 studies (5 causal, 4 design), q2 · 0 of 9 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Early Science with Nico & Nor is a preschool science program spanning three curricular units—Plants, Ramps, and Shadows—designed to promote science, math, and engineering across home and school. The report states "The program includes a Teacher's Guide, a Family Science Fun Guide, three digital science journals, and eight digital games." The Teacher's Guide organizes activities into four weeks per unit matching preschool formats, and the Family Science Fun Guide offers brief adaptable activities in English and Spanish. Guides are freely available via WGBH's First 8 Studios website and iPad apps via the Apple store.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Digital tools require iPad tablets; the program was implemented with iPads delivered to classrooms and orientation sessions for teachers and parents.

Constraints¶

  • Implementation required adjustments for COVID-19 restrictions, including a staggered start and remote administration options for assessments and family debrief sessions.

Claims¶

  • Observed classroom implementation promoted science practices and core ideas, with Observing and Describing most frequent and coverage varying by unit [+M]
  • Children who received both the classroom and home components made significantly more improvement in science learning than children who received the classroom component only [+W]
  • The program showed no significant overall math difference, though items on visual-spatial vocabulary and nonstandard measurement improved for the treatment condition [~M]
  • Children in intervention classrooms made significantly more improvement in science learning than children in comparison (business as usual) classrooms [+W]
  • Teachers reported the program was easy to implement and children enjoyed the activities, with modifications made for time, space, darkness, and plant-growth constraints [+M]
  • Children in classrooms implementing the Early Science with Nico and Nor program made significantly more improvement in science learning than peers in comparison classrooms. [+W]
