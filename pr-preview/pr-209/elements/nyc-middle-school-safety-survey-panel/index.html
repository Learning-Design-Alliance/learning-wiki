New York City middle school student safety survey panel dataset (2007-2010)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A unique panel data set of survey responses from New York City middle school students, including reported feelings of safety for more than 340,000 students annually from 2007 to 2010 in more than 700 middle schools. The article uses this dataset to examine the relationship between "feelings of safety in the classroom and academic achievement." It serves as the evidence base for the study's central finding.
