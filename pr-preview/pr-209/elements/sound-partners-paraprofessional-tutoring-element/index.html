Sound Partners one-to-one tutoring program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Sound Partners is "a 1-1 tutoring program that uses paraprofessionals as tutors." Students receive "tutoring 30 minutes a day, 4 days a week, for 18-20 weeks." Instruction differs somewhat between kindergarten and first grade but at both levels "focuses on phonics, phonemic awareness, sight words, and oral reading practice using decodable texts." The program was evaluated in kindergarten and first-grade studies meeting ESSA Strong criteria.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A local trainer (reading specialist, teacher, or special education teacher) to train local tutors
  • Internet access for training materials

Constraints¶

  • Tutoring content is somewhat different in kindergarten and first grade, so implementation varies by grade level

Target Learners¶

  • kindergarten students
  • first-grade students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • phonics
  • phonemic awareness
  • sight words
  • oral reading with decodable texts

Claims¶

  • Sound Partners tutoring produces an average effect size of +0.58 on reading measures for kindergarten and first-grade students, meeting ESSA Strong criteria [+W]
  • Sound Partners effects on word reading and comprehension are maintained two years after tutoring for kindergartners and first graders [+W]

Related Elements¶

Examples¶

  • Train paraprofessionals to deliver Sound Partners lessons using a local trainer plus manual and video-based professional development
