Curriculum Associates professional development for iRCL: on-site development days and the Online Educator Learning platform¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Curriculum Associates offers "research-driven professional development for a variety of different user types," with training for new, practicing, and advanced users. On-site development days are up to six hours, with an annual number of sessions varying by implementation length, and an Online Educator Learning platform provides digital courses available at any time.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Training is tailored to user type and implementation length; annual session recommendations vary depending on implementation length

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Teachers implementing iRCL
  • School and district staff

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Effective implementation of the iRCL curriculum

Claims¶

  • iRCL effects were consistent across grades, especially pronounced in Grades 4 and 5, with significant benefits for Hispanic, Black, economically disadvantaged, English learner, and disability subgroups [+W]
  • iRCL students were significantly more likely to meet Typical Growth (+0.17) and Stretch Growth (+0.16) targets [+W]
  • Students in schools implementing i-Ready Classroom Mathematics outperformed matched comparison peers on the spring i-Ready Diagnostic for Mathematics (average ES +0.10) [+W]
