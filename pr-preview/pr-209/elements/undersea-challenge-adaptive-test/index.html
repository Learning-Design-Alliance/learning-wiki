Undersea Challenge adaptive test bundled with DaisyQuest¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 8 claims (8 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), q3 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Undersea Challenge is an adaptive assessment included with the DaisyQuest bundle that measures children's phonological knowledge and generates reports for adults. The report states: "Included with the program is an adaptive test called Undersea Challenge. This test measures children's knowledge of rhyming; beginning, middle, and ending sounds; and phoneme blending and segmenting." The software also generates statistical reports enabling parents and teachers to view children's performance.

  • Assessment of rhyming, beginning/middle/ending sounds, and phoneme blending and segmenting

Claims¶

  • DaisyQuest has positive effects on phonological processing in preschool children, with a domain-average effect size of 0.68 across two randomized controlled trials [+M]
  • In Foster et al. (1994), DaisyQuest produced statistically significant positive effects on both phonological processing measures in 4- to 6-year-olds [+M]
  • In Lonigan et al. (2003), DaisyQuest effects were statistically significant on four of eight phonological processing outcomes and not significant on the others [+M]
  • DaisyQuest improved phonological awareness over regular kindergarten curriculum on three of four measures [+W]
  • DaisyQuest outperformed math-oriented control software on phonological awareness and phonics measures [+W]
  • DaisyQuest outperformed alternative reading software on phonological awareness in at-risk first graders [+W]
  • DaisyQuest improved phonological awareness over regular schooling in a child-care facility randomized trial [+W]
  • DaisyQuest matched teacher-delivered phonological awareness instruction but beat other instructional software in low-progress readers [+W]
