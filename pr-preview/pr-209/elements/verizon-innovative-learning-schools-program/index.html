Verizon Innovative Learning Schools program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 5 studies (4 associational, 1 qualitative), q1–q2 · 0 of 5 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Verizon Innovative Learning Schools is a partnership between Verizon and Digital Promise, running since 2014, that now reaches over 560 Title I middle and high schools nationwide. Students and educators receive free devices and up to four years of data plans for off-campus access. The program focuses on developing educators' digital skills through extensive training and technology-driven STEM curriculum, and "each school is required to deploy a full-time learning coach who receives further training from Digital Promise and is dedicated to supporting educators in successful technology usage." Digital Promise supports schools in creating sustainability plans and has published a toolkit for all districts creating technology plans.

Claims¶

  • A majority of surveyed teachers (61%) reported that participating in the Verizon Innovative Learning Schools program improved their job satisfaction [+M]
  • In Irving ISD, 56% of educators who served as program coaches since 2017 were promoted to new positions [+W]
  • Majorities of surveyed teachers and coaches reported the program enhanced career advancement and leadership opportunities [+M]
  • 71% of surveyed Miami-Dade coaches reported the program improved their job satisfaction, up from a pre-program baseline where none reported being completely satisfied [+M]
