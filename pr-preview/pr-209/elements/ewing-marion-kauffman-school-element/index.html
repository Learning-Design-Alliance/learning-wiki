Ewing Marion Kauffman School¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Ewing Marion Kauffman School is a public, tuition-free charter school serving students in Kansas City, Missouri. In the 2017-18 school year it enrolled 994 students in grades 5 through 11, of whom the report notes "Most (87 percent) of the students were low-income, and 89 percent were black or Hispanic." The school is the subject of a Mathematica evaluation running from 2011 to 2024, prepared for the Ewing Marion Kauffman Foundation.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Serves students in Kansas City, Missouri as a public, tuition-free charter school

Constraints¶

  • Impact estimates rely on a matched comparison group of similar students in other Kansas City district and charter schools

Target Learners¶

  • Students in grades 5 through 11, predominantly low-income and black or Hispanic

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Achievement growth in mathematics, English language arts, and science; college and career readiness as measured by ACT scores

Claims¶

  • The Kauffman School has higher ACT scores than the Kansas City high school average, but its impact on ACT scores could not be calculated [~W]
  • The Kauffman School's achievement impacts are higher than the average impacts found in studies of other charter schools [+M]
  • During its first seven years, the Kauffman School had substantial positive impacts on student achievement growth in mathematics, English language arts, and science beyond growth in other Kansas City public schools [+M]
