Ohio AI Toolkit: web-based guidance with step-by-step policy development for policymakers, teachers, and parents¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Ohio AI Toolkit is one of the seven reviewed state guidance documents, at 18,000 words. It guides policymakers, teachers, and parents in understanding and implementing AI in education, providing a step-by-step approach for policy development, an inventory of resources for each step, and a review of key AI governance guidelines. The report notes its "web-based design of the toolkit is particularly effective in curating external documents" through well-organized, expandable descriptions that let a reader build a reading list.

Claims¶

  • Seven state AI guidance documents share seven major themes, including human-centered use, AI literacy, equity, and data privacy, though not all themes appear in all documents [+W]
  • State guidance documents describe opportunities for AI in education with encouraging tones that recommend safe exploration [+W]
  • State AI guidance describes risks but risk-management work is at an early stage, with some documents possibly too upbeat given school readiness [~W]
