MARSI-based questionnaire adapted to Vietnamese for measuring metacognitive reading strategy use¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A 30-item, 5-point Likert questionnaire adopted from the Metacognitive Awareness of Reading Strategies Inventory (MARSI) by Mokhtari and Richard (2002), administered in Vietnamese with items clustered into three categories: Global Reading Strategies (items 1–13), Problem-Solving Strategies (items 14–22), and Support Reading Strategies (items 23–30). In this study it showed high internal reliability (α = .87) and was scored using high (≥3.5), moderate (2.5–3.4), and low (≤2.4) usage levels.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Administered in the learners' first language and with items clustered by category to support understanding

Constraints¶

  • Self-report instrument; the authors note some learners may report awareness of strategies without actually using them when reading

Target Learners¶

  • adolescent and adult EFL learners

Target Learning Goals¶

  • awareness and self-reported use of metacognitive reading strategies

Claims¶

  • Vietnamese EFL grade-11 learners report above-average overall metacognitive strategy use in reading [+W]
  • Metacognitive strategy use correlates positively with EFL reading comprehension achievement (r = .54) [+W]
  • Successful readers out-use less successful readers on global and support strategies but not problem-solving strategies [+W]
