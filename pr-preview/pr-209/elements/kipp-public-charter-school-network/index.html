KIPP public charter school network¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 8 claims (8 for) · 4 studies (4 causal), q2–q3 · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

Description¶

KIPP is a network of public charter schools serving students from elementary through high school grades, evaluated here by Mathematica Policy Research over a 10-year period as part of the project "KIPP: Preparing Youth for College" (time frame 2007-2017, prepared for Arnold Ventures). The evaluation examined impacts on achievement in math, reading, science, and social studies, and on college preparation activities. The study found "positive impacts on student achievement, particularly at the elementary and middle school levels."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • The network operates public charter schools spanning elementary and secondary grade levels, as examined in the evaluation.

Constraints¶

  • High school achievement impacts were not statistically significant on average for students continuing from KIPP middle schools.

Target Learners¶

  • elementary school students
  • middle school students
  • high school students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • reading and mathematics skills
  • science and social studies achievement
  • college preparation and application

Claims¶

  • This is the first high-quality study to rigorously examine the impacts of the KIPP charter school network at all elementary and secondary grade levels [+W]
  • For students continuing from KIPP middle schools, KIPP high schools have positive impacts on a variety of college preparation activities and the likelihood of applying to college [+W]
  • KIPP high schools have positive, statistically significant achievement impacts for students new to the KIPP network, but not for students continuing from KIPP middle schools [+W]
  • Average impacts of KIPP middle schools were positive and statistically significant throughout the 10-year study period, though higher in earlier years than recent years [+W]
  • In the vast majority of 22 KIPP middle schools, impacts on students' state assessment scores in math and reading are positive, statistically significant, and educationally substantial [+W]
  • KIPP Pre-K combined with KIPP early elementary school has positive, statistically significant impacts on reading and math achievement after five years [+W]
  • KIPP elementary schools have positive, statistically significant, and educationally meaningful impacts on three of four measures of reading and mathematics skills [+W]
  • KIPP Pre-K may provide an additional benefit for reading achievement above and beyond KIPP elementary school [+W]
