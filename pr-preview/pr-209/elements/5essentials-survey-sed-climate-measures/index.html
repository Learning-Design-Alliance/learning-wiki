5Essentials Survey measures of socioemotional development and school climate¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The 5Essentials Survey provided the study's measures of student socioemotional development (SED) and school climate. The study used "5Essentials Survey measures of socioemotional development (SED) from 8th and 9th grade students who attended a CPS high school between 2011 and 2017 (160,148 students)" and 5Essentials Survey measures of school climate completed by high school students and teachers, alongside administrative records of demographics, attendance, and discipline.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Administered to 8th and 9th grade students and to high school students and teachers.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • 8th and 9th grade students in Chicago Public Schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • measuring socioemotional development
  • measuring school climate

Claims¶

  • High schools that foster 8th-to-9th-grade student growth across multiple dimensions positively influence students' social and academic trajectories [+W]
  • School quality measures that exclude schools' impacts on socioemotional development underestimate or misidentify many impactful schools [+W]
  • Schools' impacts on socioemotional development and behaviors in 9th grade matter substantially more for long-run trajectories than test score impacts [+W]
  • Fostering socioemotional development and fostering test score growth had nearly identical impacts on 9th grade test scores [+W]
