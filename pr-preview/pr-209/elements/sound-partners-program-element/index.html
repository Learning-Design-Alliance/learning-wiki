Sound Partners phonics-based tutoring program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Sound Partners is "a phonics-based tutoring program that provides supplemental reading instruction to elementary school students grades K–3 with below average reading skills." It consists of scripted lessons in alphabetic and phonics skills emphasizing letter-sound correspondences, phoneme blending, decoding and encoding phonetically regular words, and reading irregular high-frequency words, with oral reading practice in Bob Books® decodable texts. It is designed for tutors with minimal training and can be delivered as pull-out, after-school, or homeschool tutoring.

Context¶

Constraints¶

  • Designed specifically for use by tutors with minimal training and experience

Target Learners¶

  • Elementary school students in grades K–3 with below average reading skills

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Letter-sound correspondences
  • Phoneme blending
  • Decoding and encoding phonetically regular words
  • Reading irregular high-frequency words

Claims¶

  • The evidence base for Sound Partners is medium to large for alphabetics, fluency, and comprehension and small for general reading achievement [+W]
  • Sound Partners shows no discernible effects on general reading achievement [-W]
  • Sound Partners tutoring has positive effects on the alphabetics of beginning readers in grades K–3 [+W]
  • Sound Partners tutoring has positive effects on reading comprehension of beginning readers [+W]
  • Sound Partners tutoring has positive effects on reading fluency of beginning readers [+W]
