Get Fit and Prevention components of Healthy Harlem¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 7 claims (6 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Get Fit and Prevention are the two Healthy Harlem components to which the brief attributes fitness gains. The key findings state that "students who were overweight or obese and participated in Get Fit and Prevention had large and statistically significant increases in PACER laps two and three years later," and the brief concludes that "participation in Healthy Harlem's Get Fit and Prevention components increased levels of physical fitness."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Participation by students identified as overweight or obese within the Healthy Harlem initiative.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Overweight and obese middle and high school students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Increased physical fitness as measured by PACER laps and Harlem Fitness Zone status

Claims¶

  • Overweight or obese students who participated in Get Fit and Prevention had large and statistically significant increases in PACER laps two and three years later [+W]
  • Increases in the percentage of students in the Harlem Fitness Zone were generally larger for boys than for girls [~W]
  • The percentage of students in the targeted Harlem Fitness Zone was significantly greater after two and three years of participation than at baseline [+W]
  • Among students overweight or obese at baseline who received both Get Fit and Prevention, BMI z-scores and overweight/obesity prevalence were reduced [+W]
  • Healthy Harlem generally increased students' fitness levels, with variation by gender [+W]
  • Healthy Harlem had some positive impacts on other measures of student self-efficacy and social support [+W]
  • Healthy Harlem increased students' nutrition knowledge and physical-activity self-efficacy, with gains sustained over three years [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • Healthy Harlem initiative (Harlem Children's Zone after-school program)
  • Healthy Harlem two-tier program: universal Prevention component plus targeted Get Fit program
