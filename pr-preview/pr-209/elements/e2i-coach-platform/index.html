Evidence to Insights (e2i) Coach: a free online platform for testing what works in remote learning¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The Evidence to Insights (e2i) Coach is described as "a free, online platform" offered by the Regional Educational Laboratory Mid-Atlantic. Its stated purpose is to help schools and teachers "find out what works best in your schools and for your students" in remote learning, using their own data to refine strategies.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Schools and teachers supply their own data on remote learning approaches so the platform can indicate what works best for their students

Constraints¶

  • The publication notes the evidence base on what works in remote learning is scant, so the platform is positioned for local evidence generation rather than applying established findings

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students in schools using remote learning

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Improving the effectiveness of remote learning strategies through local data use

Related Elements¶

  • Exploratory research review of promising practices and approaches to support remote learning (REL Mid-Atlantic, 2020)
  • ASSISTments/E-TRIALS: free A/B testing platform integrated with a K-12 math practice platform

Examples¶

  • Use local data to identify which remote learning strategies work best for your own students
