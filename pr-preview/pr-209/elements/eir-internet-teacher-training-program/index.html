EIR® nine-month Internet-based teacher professional development program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

EIR® teacher training lasts nine months and combines monthly two-hour Internet sessions with telephone support from an EIR® trainer. The report states "The training program lasts nine months and consists of two-hour Internet sessions conducted once a month, along with telephone support from an EIR® trainer." Each session typically includes 45 minutes in a large group led by an onsite facilitator, 45 minutes in small groups where teachers share videos of their own practices, and a half-hour conference call with an EIR® trainer. Training modules cover program use, research background, grade-level routines, assessments, involving parents, training coaches, running records, and implementation evaluation.
