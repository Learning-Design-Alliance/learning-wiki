A2i Professional Support System¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The A2i Professional Support System is a systems-level approach intended to improve reading success in Pre-K through Grade 3 by individualizing instruction so that "learning experiences are tailored to account for each students' unique needs". It prepares teachers to vary the content and form of instruction according to students' prior performance, using quick online assessments, instructional recommendations, lesson planning support, and customized professional development, with Learning Ovations as the partner organization.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A main district-contact is critical for ideal adoption across an entire district
  • Identification of a Literacy Champion as the main school-level contact and resource

Constraints¶

  • The online assessments require a device with a screen wider than 768 pixels

Target Learners¶

  • Pre-K through Grade 3 students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Reading at or above grade level by the end of 3rd grade

Claims¶

  • A2i-informed differentiated literacy instruction improves reading outcomes with an average effect size of +0.23 across three randomized-controlled studies [+W]
  • Benefits of A2i-informed literacy instruction accumulate over multiple years, with students receiving it in grades 1-3 showing the highest growth (cumulative effect size 0.60) [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • A2i Software Platform

Examples¶

  • Deliver A2i professional development through a 90-minute launch session, grade-team Literacy Huddles, and individualized coaching sessions over a 3-year rollout
  • Appoint a Literacy Champion as school-level contact to support A2i rollout
  • Use ongoing assessments to tailor early-childhood learning experiences
  • Layered professional learning and support for teachers implementing individualized software
