Math Corps tutoring program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Math Corps is a grade 4-8 tutoring program in which full-time AmeriCorps tutors serve approximately 25 students and part-time tutors 12-15 students, each receiving "90 minutes of intervention support per week" in 30- or 45-minute sessions two or three times weekly. Tutoring is curriculum neutral, aligned to grade-level state and national standards, and targets foundational skills in whole and rational numbers, operations, and algebraic reasoning. Students exit when assessment data indicate they meet exit eligibility and are then monitored until year end.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • School partners must identify an Internal Coach who provides coaching support to tutors (6-9 hours per month per tutor)
  • Access to an updated computer or similar electronic device

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Students in grades 4-8 needing math intervention support

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Foundational skills with whole and rational numbers
  • Operations and algebraic reasoning
  • Conceptual understanding, procedural proficiency, and word problem solving

Claims¶

  • Math Corps students significantly outperform controls on STAR Math and score higher on the state assessment after one year [+W]
  • Math Corps effects are larger for students receiving the optimal dosage [+W]
  • Across two randomized studies, Math Corps has a sample-size-weighted mean effect size of +0.16 [+W]
  • Math Corps tutoring significantly improves STAR Math scores after one semester relative to control [+W]
