Ecree automated writing feedback tool¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 8 claims (4 for, 4 against) · 3 studies (1 causal, 1 review, 1 associational), q2 · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Ecree is "an automated feedback tool that supports student writing in the classroom." The brief describes it as a classroom writing-support technology studied in grade 8-11 English language arts classrooms during the 2021-2022 school year, where it provided feedback on students' writing that teachers and students found easy to use and helpful, though some students may be overwhelmed by it.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Used in English language arts classrooms to support student writing

Constraints¶

  • Some students may be overwhelmed by the tool's feedback about their writing

Target Learners¶

  • Middle and high school students in grades 8-11

  • Improving the quality of students' writing

  • Teachers and students found Ecree easy to use and helpful for improving writing [+W]

  • Some students may be overwhelmed by Ecree's feedback about their writing [-W]
  • Teachers found Ecree and MI Write useful for their writing instruction and for helping students improve their writing [+W]
  • Teachers used Ecree less than intended, and some reported they did not have enough time to learn it and integrate it with instruction [-W]
