Imagine Language & Literacy adaptive supplemental literacy program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Imagine Language & Literacy is an adaptive supplemental program for students in pre-kindergarten through sixth grade that "provides individualized instruction with exercises tailored to each user's needs, accelerating instruction levels based on the user's rate of progress". It targets vocabulary, comprehension, fluency, phonics and phonemic awareness, and incorporates art, music, sports and multi-disciplinary subject matter. It is web-based and compatible with PC, MAC, iPads, Kindle, and Nook.

Claims¶

  • ELL users of Imagine Language & Literacy in grades 4-5 showed significantly greater growth than similar non-users on the FAST assessment (ES +0.12) [+W]
  • Louisiana English Learners in grades 1-5 who used Imagine Language & Literacy scored significantly higher on the ELPT than matched non-users (ES +0.16) [+W]
  • Students using Imagine Language & Literacy in Texas grades 4-5 showed significantly greater improvement in reading skills than non-using students (ES +0.18) [+W]
  • ELL users of Imagine Language & Literacy showed significantly greater growth than similar non-users on WIDA ACCESS (ES +0.14) [+W]
