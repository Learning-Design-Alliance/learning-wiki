Roster confirmation process for verifying teacher/student links¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 3 studies (3 associational), q1–q2 · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A roster confirmation process is a data-quality procedure in which "teachers verify the subjects and students they taught," producing confirmed teacher/student links. The working paper uses these confirmed links as the benchmark against which links from unconfirmed administrative data are compared, in the context of the Value-Added Assessment System for DC Schools and Teachers project (2009-2015) prepared for District of Columbia Public Schools.

Target Learners¶

  • elementary school students in grades 4 and 5

Target Learning Goals¶

  • accurate teacher effectiveness measurement in value-added models

Claims¶

  • About one in six grade 4 and 5 math and reading/ELA teachers is linked in unconfirmed administrative data to a subject they do not teach [+W]
  • Using unconfirmed teacher/student links in value-added models affects research differently depending on the circumstances of the data [+W]
  • Teacher-confirmed roster data show levels of co-teaching far beyond what appears in administrative data [+W]
  • Instruction in upper elementary grades is often departmentalized: about one in six elementary school teachers in the original administrative data was linked to a subject he or she did not teach [+W]
