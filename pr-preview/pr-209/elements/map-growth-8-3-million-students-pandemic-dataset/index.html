MAP Growth assessment dataset used to measure pandemic achievement effects (8.3 million students, grades 3-8)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief's evidence base is a large-scale observational dataset of MAP Growth assessment records in reading and math. It covers "8.3 million students in grades 3-8 who took MAP Growth assessments in reading and math between 2018–19 and 2021–22" alongside "a similarly sized sample of students who tested between 2015–16 and 2018–19" as a pre-pandemic comparison. The dataset supports grade-, subgroup-, and school-poverty-level analyses of pandemic impacts and recovery.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires MAP Growth assessments administered in reading and math across the 2018-19 to 2021-22 period and a comparable pre-pandemic testing sample

Constraints¶

  • Limited to students in grades 3-8 who took MAP Growth assessments; findings may not generalize to other grades or assessments

Target Learners¶

  • Students in grades 3-8 in US schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Measuring reading and math achievement and growth relative to pre-pandemic norms

Related Elements¶

  • MAP Growth test-score dataset covering 4.9 million U.S. students in Grades 3-8
  • MAP Growth assessment dataset covering 5.5 million students in grades 3-8 in reading and math
  • MAP Growth reading and mathematics assessment dataset (7 million students, grades 3-8, 25,000 schools, 2020-21 to fall 2022)
  • MAP Growth assessments as the data source for pandemic learning-loss analysis
