Reading Plus® web-based individualized scaffolded silent reading intervention¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Reading Plus® is a web-based reading intervention providing "individualized scaffolded silent reading" practice for students in grade 3 and higher, aiming to develop silent reading fluency, comprehension, and vocabulary. The program adjusts content difficulty and reading duration so students proceed at a pace matching their skill level, and includes differentiated reading activities, computer-based assessments, progress-monitoring tools, implementation support, and supplemental offline activities.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Students complete initial assessments that determine the individual independent silent reading rate and level before being assigned to an intervention path

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Students in grade 3 and higher, including adolescent learners in grades 5–9

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Silent reading fluency
  • Reading comprehension
  • Vocabulary

Claims¶

  • Baseline equivalence was established only for low-achieving students, so analyses for other grades and subpopulations do not meet WWC evidence standards [~W]
  • The extent of evidence for Reading Plus® on adolescent learners is small, resting on a single study that met WWC standards with reservations [~W]
  • Reading Plus® produced a statistically significant positive effect on FCAT reading comprehension for low-achieving adolescent learners, with a small WWC-calculated effect of 0.06 [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • Reading Plus web-based literacy program for grades 3-12+

Examples¶

  • Pair offline skills lessons targeting identified comprehension deficiencies with small-group and whole-group teacher-led instruction
