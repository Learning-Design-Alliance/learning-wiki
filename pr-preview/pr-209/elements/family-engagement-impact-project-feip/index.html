Family Engagement Impact Project (FEIP)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The Family Engagement Impact Project (FEIP) is a public-private partnership initiative funded by the Heising-Simons Foundation in 2013. Its purpose is "to offer new ways to build the capacity of parents and professionals for family engagement and to promote positive educational outcomes for low-income immigrant children from birth through age 8 in California's San Mateo and Santa Clara counties." The Phase II report documents and evaluates grantees' experiences and short-term outcomes during the implementation phase.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Leveraging public-private partnerships in support of family engagement, as the report describes the initiative's strategy.

Constraints¶

  • Scope is limited to low-income immigrant children from birth through age 8 in California's San Mateo and Santa Clara counties, as stated in the report.

Target Learners¶

  • Low-income immigrant children from birth through age 8

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Family engagement capacity of parents and professionals; positive educational outcomes for children
