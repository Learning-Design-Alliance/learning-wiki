Framework for Teaching (FFT) observation instrument with 22 components scored into four domains and an overall Professional Practice Rating¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), q2 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Framework for Teaching of Charlotte Danielson is an observation-based teacher performance measure whose "22 components" each "is designed to capture a separate teaching practice." The pilot used observation data to estimate four domain scores and one overall Professional Practice Rating (PPR) score, which were merged with value-added measures based on student test scores for analysis.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Classroom observation data describing teacher performance on the 22 FFT components, plus student test-score data to compute value-added measures for validity analysis

Constraints¶

  • As piloted in Pennsylvania districts including Pittsburgh public schools during the 2010-2015 evaluation pilot

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 teachers in Pennsylvania public school districts

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Measuring teaching practice across planning, instruction, and classroom domains for educator evaluation

Claims¶

  • Rating distributions and FFT-VAM relationships were broadly similar across the two pilot phases, though top-two-category ratings decreased somewhat in Pittsburgh [+M]
  • FFT scores showed acceptable-to-good internal consistency, with the overall PPR score more internally consistent than any single domain score [+M]
  • FFT-based teacher ratings in Pennsylvania's pilot were overwhelmingly concentrated in the top two performance categories, with less than 0.1 percent rated failing [+M]
  • FFT scores correlated positively with value-added measures, with domain correlations ranging from 0.19 to 0.22 and generally statistically significant [+W]
  • Professional practice ratings, student surveys, and value-added measures each have the potential to differentiate teacher performance in Pittsburgh Public Schools [+M]

Related Elements¶

  • Pennsylvania teacher evaluation pilot dataset covering 6,676 teachers in 269 districts across two school years

  • Charlotte Danielson Framework for Teaching as a classroom-observation evaluation tool
