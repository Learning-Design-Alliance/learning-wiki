Hoot Reading online one-to-one literacy tutoring program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q4 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Hoot Reading provides online, one-to-one literacy instruction delivered by trained teachers, with sessions taking place over video call through the company's proprietary platform. The platform was developed through an earlier research collaboration involving the Nokia Research Lab, Sesame Workshop, and the Joan Ganz Cooney Center. The program works with school districts across North America to advance student reading achievement, and tutors are provided by Hoot Reading while school staff supervise at each location.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Computers or tablets with reliable internet access and headphones are required for students to participate in virtual tutoring
  • School staff are required to supervise the program at each location

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Students in grades 1–4, primarily MTSS Tier III struggling readers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Reading achievement / early literacy instruction

Claims¶

  • Grade 3–4 impacts of Hoot Reading on the Missouri Assessment Program were positive but not statistically significant [+W]
  • No positive effects of Hoot Reading were found for MTSS Tier II students [-W]
  • MTSS Tier III students assigned to Hoot Reading significantly outperformed control peers on the i-Ready assessment (effect size = +0.08) [+W]
