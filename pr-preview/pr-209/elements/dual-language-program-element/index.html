Dual language programs: multi-year instruction in English and a partner language¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (1 for, 4 mixed) · 5 studies (5 quant-synthesis), q2–q3 · 1 of 5 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Dual language programs are school programs in which "classroom teachers instruct students from an early age and over multiple years in both English and a second language called the partner language." They can be one-way (one language group) or two-way (two language groups), implemented schoolwide or as a strand within a school, and the report examines programs spanning at least the elementary grades with at least 50 percent of instruction in the partner language. Programs starting with more partner-language time reduce it toward 50% by about grade 4.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Programs must span at least the elementary grades and provide at least 50 percent of instruction in the partner language to fall within the reviewed model.

Constraints¶

  • Because reaching proficiency in a second language may require substantial exposure over an extended period of time, the report excludes stand-alone preschool programs and interventions below 50% partner-language instruction.

Target Learners¶

  • English learners
  • native English speakers in grades PK-12, most commonly K-5 or K-8

Target Learning Goals¶

  • academic achievement
  • bilingualism and biliteracy
  • awareness and appreciation of diverse cultures

Claims¶

  • The evidence base on dual language programs is thin: only two of 122 identified studies met WWC standards [~W]
  • Literacy benefits of dual language programs did not extend to the English learner subsample in the strongest study [~W]
  • Dual language programs improved English literacy achievement, with a potentially positive effects rating based on one study [+W]
  • Dual language programs showed uncertain effects on mathematics achievement across two studies [~W]
  • Dual language programs showed uncertain effects on science achievement [~W]
