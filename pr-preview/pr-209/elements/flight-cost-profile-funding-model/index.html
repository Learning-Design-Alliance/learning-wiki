FLIGHT cost profile and funding model¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The WWC assembled a preliminary list of FLIGHT's major cost components because the cost of FLIGHT was not reported in the reviewed study or on the TSIC® website. Schools that implemented FLIGHT "conducted one 2-hour mentor training, one 1-hour staff training, and nine hour-long workshops for staff each year." Facilities costs included dedicated space for mentor and advocate meetings during the school day and for workshops; students and parents bore transportation costs to workshops and college costs not covered by the scholarship. Funding came from a public-private model of corporate, foundation, and private donations matched by the Florida Prepaid College Foundation funded by the Florida Legislature, with in-kind services from volunteers and community agencies.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Personnel for mentor and staff training, dedicated school facilities for meetings and workshops, and a public-private funding model covering scholarships and student services.

Constraints¶

  • The preliminary cost list is not designed to be exhaustive; equipment and materials cost information is not reported.

Target Learners¶

  • low-income middle and high school students in public schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • college access and enrollment support
