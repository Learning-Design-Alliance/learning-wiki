Family Science Fun Guide¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Family Science Fun Guide is a digital guide that "includes indoor and outdoor activity ideas spanning three topics: Plants, Ramps and Shadows." It "includes brief activities and fun projects to inspire families to engage in science, math & engineering in playful ways and material to help parents and caregivers feel prepared to engage in rich conversations with their children." A Spanish-language version, the Guía para Familias, covers the same three topics and notes some activities can be done at home and others outside during common outings.

Claims¶

  • Children who engaged in the program at both home and school made significantly more improvement in science learning than children who implemented at school only [+M]
  • Parents used the digital Family Guide flexibly, modifying activities to fit their routines or creating new activities based on family interests [+W]
  • Parents reported that the home activities were fun, promoted science learning, and meaningfully connected to everyday experiences [+W]
  • Children in classrooms implementing the Early Science with Nico and Nor program made significantly more improvement in science learning than peers in comparison classrooms. [+M]
