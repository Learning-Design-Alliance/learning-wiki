BookNook virtual tutoring platform and science-of-reading curriculum for K-8 reading intervention¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

BookNook is a virtual tutoring provider for reading intervention that "uses a synchronous teaching platform and curriculum grounded in the science of reading to deliver high-dosage tutoring services to K-8 students." Lessons center on a text and build "phonological awareness, phonics, fluency, vocabulary, and comprehension skills." Tutoring is delivered through BookNook's online platform, with tutors provided by BookNook and on-site school staff supervising sessions.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Tutors provided by BookNook plus on-site school staff to supervise tutoring sessions
  • Tutors complete a certification prior to the tutoring start date
  • Technology: tutoring is delivered through BookNook's online platform

Constraints¶

  • Evaluated with first through fourth grade students in charter schools; the page's evidence covers K-8 programming generally but the study sample is grades 1-4

Target Learners¶

  • K-8 students needing reading intervention

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Phonological awareness
  • Phonics
  • Fluency
  • Vocabulary
  • Comprehension

Claims¶

  • Students receiving the recommended BookNook dosage of 20 or more sessions show markedly stronger reading gains (ES = +0.26) than the overall assigned sample [+W]
  • Assignment to BookNook tutoring produces a small but statistically significant increase in reading growth on the NWEA MAP for grade 1-4 students [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • OnYourMark virtual early literacy tutoring program
  • Education Corps in-person high-dosage literacy tutoring program model
  • Hoot Reading online one-to-one literacy tutoring program
  • Chapter One one-on-one in-class tutoring program for early literacy
  • Project On-Track high-dosage small-group literacy tutoring program with Amplify Reading mCLASS intervention

Examples¶

  • Deliver high-dosage tutoring as recurring 30-minute sessions two to three times per week over 12 weeks
  • Certify tutors before tutoring begins and provide professional development to supervising school staff
