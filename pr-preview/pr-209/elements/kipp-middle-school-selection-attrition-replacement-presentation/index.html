Mathematica presentation on student selection, attrition, and replacement at KIPP middle schools¶

Description¶

A presentation by Mathematica researchers, published April 9, 2011, addressing "Student Selection, Attrition, and Replacement at KIPP Middle Schools." It was "Prepared for: Arnold Ventures" under the associated project "KIPP: Preparing Youth for College" with a time frame of "2007-2017." The page records the author team: Ira Nichols-Barrer, Christina Clark Tuttle, Brian Gill, and Philip Gleason. It sits within Mathematica's Education and School Choice and Charters work areas.
