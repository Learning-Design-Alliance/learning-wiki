Thermos learning analytics dashboard¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (2 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Thermos dashboard is a student-facing, enhanced visualization type LAD designed to support the appraisal and preparatory phases of self-regulation of study behaviour. It presents the Motivation and Engagement Wheel, the Group Work skill graph, and a Study Progress widget, combined with a feedback box that "provides actionable feedback, self-improvement exercises" under Reflect, Explore, and Get Skilled buttons and support suggestions under a Connect button. Students use it mostly of their own volition; no teacher or tutor can monitor student results.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • MEW and GSQ data are gathered via a validated self-assessment questionnaire; study progress data comes from the university's data management system

Constraints¶

  • As an enhanced visualization type it does not actively recommend activities; uptake of actionable feedback is largely the student's responsibility

Target Learners¶

  • Bachelor and master students in higher education

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Appraisal of study behaviour; awareness and insight into motivation, engagement, group work skills, and study progress

Claims¶

  • Perceived impact of LAD use on study behaviour varied, with most participants reporting no impact [~W]
  • Students and tutors rated predetermined LAD constructs similarly in importance but differed in suggesting and ranking new constructs [~W]
  • The LAD did not support the preparatory phase because most participants did not use the actionable feedback despite perceiving it as potentially useful [-W]
  • The LAD was mostly perceived to support student appraisal of study behaviour by providing new insights or awareness [+W]
  • After design adjustments, the LAD achieved good–excellent usability (SUS M = 4.13, equal to 78.25%) [+W]
