CPS Elementary Teacher Survey and General Reform Index (1991)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

An 8-page survey of Chicago Public Schools elementary teachers administered in late May and June of 1991, completed by 12,708 teachers with a 70 percent overall response rate, covering governance, work conditions, school quality, and instruction. The report combines reform attitude and change items into a "General Reform Index that combines the teachers' responses to the questions on attitudes towards school reform" and ten school-quality clusters with acceptable Cronbach alpha internal consistency. School profiles were returned to 401 schools with response rates of 50 percent or greater.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A response rate sufficient for representative school-level profiles; the survey relied on voluntary teacher participation with no tangible incentives

Constraints¶

  • The report discusses only the first five pages of the survey; reading and math instructional practice results were deferred to later reports
  • Self-reports were not independently verified by observation

Target Learners¶

  • Chicago Public Schools elementary teachers and school communities

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Understanding teacher perceptions of school reform, school quality, and instructional change to support school improvement planning
