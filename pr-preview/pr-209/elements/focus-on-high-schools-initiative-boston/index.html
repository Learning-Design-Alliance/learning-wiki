Focus on High Schools initiative (Boston Public Schools)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Focus on High Schools initiative was a Boston Public Schools reform "intended to transform Boston's 12 comprehensive high schools into more effective institutions by breaking them down into smaller, more intimate learning communities and reforming their English and language arts programs." The Mathematica report evaluated its effects on student academic outcomes, finding score reductions alongside improved attendance and promotion outcomes.
