SEERNet RFA feasibility-letter process with post-award 6-month planning¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

To bring third-party researchers onto the DLPs, IES, DLPs, and SEERNet designed a Request for Applications in which researchers "submit their broad research concept and a request for a letter of feasibility to the DLP," because completely determining feasibility at proposal time would be impractical. After award, the process builds in a 6-month planning period to resolve implementation challenges and to surface the limits and boundaries of what each DLP infrastructure can support.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Proposers must obtain a feasibility letter from their target DLP before award
  • A 6-month post-award planning process to resolve implementation challenges

Constraints¶

  • IES has sole responsibility for defining research solicitations
  • fully determining feasibility at proposal time is impractical

Target Learners¶

  • external education researchers applying to conduct studies on DLPs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • conducting feasible, high-quality education research on digital learning platforms

Related Elements¶

  • SEERNet researcher support resources: platform research guide and proposal-writing resource
