Illinois Community College Board (ICCB) data via the Illinois Longitudinal Data System¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

ICCB data, accessed through the ILDS, is a state administrative dataset covering all public two-year colleges in Illinois. The article states that "Students cannot opt out of the data, making it a more comprehensive source of information on CPS graduates who enroll in two-year colleges than NSC data." It classifies credentials as associate degrees, short-term certificates, or long-term certificates, and includes program types, credits attempted and earned, and early college enrollment. Records link across state agencies via the CDDA ID without transferring personal identifying information.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A data sharing agreement through the ILDS and CDDA IDs linking ISBE K-12 records to ICCB records

Constraints¶

  • Covers only two-year colleges in Illinois
  • Less research and guidance exist for working with and interpreting ICCB data in external research
  • No consistent data system and decision rules between IBHE and ICCB for overall postsecondary outcomes

Target Learners¶

  • CPS high school graduates enrolling in Illinois two-year colleges

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Accurate reporting and research on two-year college enrollment and credential completion

Claims¶

  • Linking CPS, ISBE, and ICCB records through the CDDA ID matched 97 percent of the 2012 CPS graduating class to ICCB data [+W]
  • NSC data cannot be used alone to calculate two-year credential-type completion rates because 57.5 percent of completion records have a blank credential name field [+W]
  • Full-time immediate two-year enrollment rates differ substantially between ICCB and NSC data, suggesting limited reliability of the NSC full-time status field [+W]
  • NSC and ICCB data agree on any-credential completion within four years for 96 percent of immediate two-year enrollees [+W]
  • NSC and ICCB data agree on immediate two-year college enrollment for about 96 percent of students, with ICCB showing a slightly higher enrollment rate [+W]

Examples¶

  • Use state longitudinal data systems to supplement NSC data when reporting detailed two-year college outcomes
