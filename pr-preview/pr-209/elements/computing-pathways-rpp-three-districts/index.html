Computing Pathways Research Practice Partnership with three districts¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A research-practice partnership, supported by NSF Grant No. 1837386, in which Digital Promise works with three districts to develop inclusive K-12 computing pathways. The paper prints a table of the districts: one of 14,000 students described as becoming urban (37% low income, 12% Latinx, 19% Black) focused on greater inclusion of growing numbers of Black, Latinx and ELL students; one suburban district of 28,000 (17% low income) focused on Title I schools with large achievement gaps; and one rural district of 7,500 (71% low income, 33% Black) focused on engaging students from low socio-economic households and female students. Pathway documents focus on algorithms, data, and systems and computational models.

  • Inclusive participation in K-12 computing pathways covering algorithms, data, and systems and computational models

Claims¶

  • A research-practice partnership is creating a K-8 computational thinking pathway in two rural Kentucky districts by applying South Fayette School District's model and training 75+ teachers over three years [+W]
  • The partnership's research plan studies pathway creation, grade-band competencies, and whether a competency-based PD framework assists instructors in demonstrating teaching proficiency [+W]
