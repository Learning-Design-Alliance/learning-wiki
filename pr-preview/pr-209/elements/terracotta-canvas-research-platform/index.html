Terracotta: a SEERNet platform enabling randomized research within the Canvas LMS¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), q1–q3 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Terracotta is described as "a platform that enables research within a popular LMS". It "works with the common Learning Management System Canvas": an instructor can create multiple forms of the same assignment (e.g., retrieval practice vs. restudy), and "Terracotta handles the logistics of randomizing the assignment of these two forms to the population of students" along with consent, de-identification, and exporting results. The paper uses Terracotta as the vehicle for both a published study and the hypothetical district improvement scenario.
