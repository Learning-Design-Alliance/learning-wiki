Flashlight360 speaking and writing progress-monitoring and assessment tool for multilingual students¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Flashlight360 is a tool "designed to monitor and assess progress in speaking and writing for multilingual students." It "provides scoring and feedback across five areas of speaking and three areas of writing, helping students demonstrate their language skills." It runs in major browsers on PCs, Macs, Chromebooks, and iPads, and can be managed by paraprofessionals, teachers, EL coaches, and administrators.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Runs on major browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) and devices including PCs, Macs, Chromebooks, and iPads

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Grade 1-12 multilingual/ELL students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • English speaking proficiency
  • English writing proficiency

Claims¶

  • One-year Flashlight360 use outgains matched comparison on WIDA ACCESS composite scores (effect size +0.10) [+W]
  • Two-year Flashlight360 use outgains matched comparison on WIDA ACCESS composite scores (effect size +0.13) [+W]
  • One-year Flashlight360 students significantly outgain matched comparisons on WIDA ACCESS Writing and Speaking subscales by more than 10 points [+W]
