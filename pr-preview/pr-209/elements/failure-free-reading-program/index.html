Failure Free Reading language development program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (1 for, 2 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Failure Free Reading is a language development program designed to improve vocabulary, fluency, word recognition, and reading comprehension for students in Kindergarten through grade 12 who score in the bottom 15% on standardized tests and have not responded to conventional beginning reading instruction. Its three key dimensions are "repeated exposure to text, predictable sentence structures, and story concepts that require minimal prior knowledge." It combines scripted teacher instruction, talking software, workbook exercises, and independent reading activities, delivered through small group or individual instruction, and includes the reading-neutral Joseph Readers' Talking Software in which every item on the screen can be read aloud.
