Phase-based behavioral readiness training spanning pre-flight, in-flight, and post-flight¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The review organizes behavioral readiness training by mission phase, with emphases shifting across phases. It states that "Pre-flight processes such as astronaut selection, team formation, stress resilience training, and development of inter- and intra-personal communication skills underpin the completion of the subsequent mission in LDS", shifting in-flight to maintenance of sleep management, workload distribution, and recovery, and post-flight to behavioral reconditioning supporting reintegration into terrestrial life.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Baseline assessments of fatigue and workload awareness and stress management before flight, plus self-reflection entrainment so crew members can identify their own tendencies

Constraints¶

  • Post-flight, astronauts may struggle with circadian entrainment, social role reinstatement, identity transitions, and recovery of cognitive function

Target Learners¶

  • astronaut crews across the full mission arc

Target Learning Goals¶

  • stress management, communication skills, operational autonomy, post-mission reintegration

Related Strategies¶

  • Stress inoculation training for crews operating without external support
  • Team training with variable leadership roles and simulated communication delays
