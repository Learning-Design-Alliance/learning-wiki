Multi-format professional learning for curriculum implementation (on-site, virtual, blended, coaching)¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The program offers professional development in multiple formats: "webinars, in-person workshops, and blended formats," ranging "from foundational training on curriculum content and platform use to ongoing support through coaching and advanced learning modules." Districts can request customized plans aligned to local goals and educator needs, so implementation support is differentiated by district context rather than one-size-fits-all.
