Build certification-track career pathway programs through consortium colleges partnered with industry associations and universities¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article describes a workforce training strategy in which a nine-college consortium led by Broward College, funded by a $24.5 million TAACCCT grant, developed certification track content and exams in eight supply chain management areas. The colleges "partnered with the Council of Supply Chain Management Professionals, a global association for SCM, which served as subject matter experts and developed certification exams," and with three universities that developed exam-preparation materials.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A funded consortium of colleges integrating content into courses or short-term workshops
  • Industry association subject matter experts to develop certification exams
  • University partners to develop exam-preparation materials

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • early-career professionals in supply chain management
  • dislocated and low-skilled community college students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • industry certification preparation
  • employment in supply chain management positions

Related Strategies¶

  • Use multi-college consortia funded through TAACCCT to build career pathways for dislocated and low-skilled workers
  • Engage industry partners to inform curriculum development, including the competencies needed, and develop new programs in response to employer input
