Secure enclaves for privacy-protecting researcher access to student-level data¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends engineering better data access through secure enclaves: the Kinetic team "is developing a secure enclave, which would allow researchers privacy-protecting access to student-level information to run their analyses virtually without exposing the underlying data." This approach is described as of great interest to those with equity-relevant research questions, addressing the tension between equity-relevant research and open science.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Analyses must run virtually inside the enclave without exposing underlying data

Constraints¶

  • Navigating FERPA, GDPR, and department-specific review processes raises administrative and technical challenges

Target Learners¶

  • Higher education students whose institutional data is analyzed

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Enabling equity-relevant research while protecting student privacy
