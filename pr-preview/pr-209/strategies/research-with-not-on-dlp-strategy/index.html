Move from extractive research toward research-practice partnerships, practitioner-facing outputs, and transparent privacy documentation¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report proposes a path forward toward a "research with, not research on" model for DLP research. It recommends engaging practitioners in early study design through Research-Practice Partnerships, translating findings into "actionable pedagogical supports" via practitioner-facing summaries, and having platforms provide "transparent, plain-language documentation" explaining de-identification processes to build educator trust.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Clearer and more frequent communication with educators about the process and value of DLP research, including how their data is secured

Constraints¶

  • The report notes K-12 educators' lack of time and accountability pressures can make external research feel extractive, and that compliance training is often passively consumed

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 educators
  • higher education educators

Target Learning Goals¶

  • improving student outcomes through research-informed practice
  • ethical and trusted use of student data in DLP research

Related Strategies¶

  • Five recommendations for integrating Improvement Science with SEERNet
  • Future RPP work should investigate which problems of practice suit the RPP framework, gather honest practitioner input, and consider the role of the whole RPP team
  • Integrate PETs at every stage of the research lifecycle
