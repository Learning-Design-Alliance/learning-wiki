Foster teacher agency through low-risk, high-reward opportunities with escalating responsibility¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The project reports fostering teacher agency by "repeatedly creating multiple, low-risk high-reward opportunities where educators' perspectives matter", with messaging that participants are working toward a shared future together and failure carries no penalty. Implementation escalated from micro-lesson implementations, to teachers presenting their work, to about twenty teachers designing much more complex project-based lessons incorporating computational thinking, heritage, and storytelling.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Repeated opportunities where educators' perspectives matter
  • Messaging that emphasizes shared ownership of the future vision

Constraints¶

  • The article describes this strategy in one rural K-8 partnership context and reports no controlled evaluation of it

Target Learners¶

  • K-8 teachers in Eastern Kentucky

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Teacher agency and willingness to implement computational thinking lessons

Affordances¶
