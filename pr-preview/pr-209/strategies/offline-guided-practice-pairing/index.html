Pair online practice with offline Guided Practice materials for small group or 1:1 sessions¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

For every skill covered in the program, offline Guided Practice materials are available. Educators use these materials to structure small group or 1:1 work sessions with students, extending the digital intervention with teacher-led practice.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Offline Guided Practice materials matched to every skill covered in the program

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • K-8 students using the math intervention

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Consolidating mathematical skills through teacher-led small group or individual practice

Related Strategies¶

  • Use data dashboards and alerts so teachers monitor progress and intervene when students struggle
  • Pair offline skills lessons targeting identified comprehension deficiencies with small-group and whole-group teacher-led instruction
  • Offline Learning Packets
