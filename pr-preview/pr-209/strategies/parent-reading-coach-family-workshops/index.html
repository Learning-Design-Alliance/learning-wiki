Train parents as one-on-one reading coaches through weekly family workshops¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A core Springboard Summer strategy is developing parents as teachers: the program's stated goal is "Developing parents as teachers by training them to be effective one-on-one reading coaches at home." Each site offers four to five family workshops (four main workshops plus a potential make-up session), and Springboard sets a participation target of attending 80 percent or more of the scheduled workshops as sufficient for engagement and program success.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Weekly family workshops offered at each site, with home visits and make-up sessions to accommodate parents

Constraints¶

  • In the evaluation, family workshop attendance showed no significant association with reading growth, which the report attributes to minimal variation because 88 percent of families met the target

Target Learners¶

  • Parents and family members of rising kindergarten through fourth grade scholars

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Effective one-on-one reading coaching at home
  • Cultivating children's reading habits
