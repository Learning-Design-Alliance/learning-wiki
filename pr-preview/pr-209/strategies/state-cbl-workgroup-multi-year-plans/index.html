Every state should convene a CBL workgroup to develop multi-year plans for scaling competency-based learning¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The publication recommends that every state create a competency-based learning workgroup, like Washington State's Mastery-based Learning Work Group, that develops recommendations and multi-year plans addressing barriers such as grade-level accountability systems, inadequate teacher preparation, and postsecondary reliance on standardized tests and grades. State education agencies would lead implementation and work with legislatures to revise policies on time-based requirements, accountability, and college admissions. The strategy responds to evidence that districts often stall at pilots, with Getting Smart identifying nearly 20 obstacles.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A state planning apparatus and policies that acknowledge the complexity of transforming districts, schools, and classrooms at scale; engagement strategies for families and the education community

Constraints¶

  • The publication warns, via Maine's repealed mastery requirement, that poor planning, communication, and absent clear definitions of competencies can crush such efforts; most state efforts remain limited to pilots

Target Learners¶

  • All K-12 learners statewide, including historically and systematically excluded populations

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Scaling learner-centered, personalized, competency-based education

Related Strategies¶

  • Policy strategies to personalize learning: competency-based progressions, phased implementation, networks, and interoperability
