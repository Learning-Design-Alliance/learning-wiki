DCPS recommends regular AP teacher participation in the Advanced Placement Summer Institute to promote quality AP instruction¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

To promote quality instruction in AP courses, DCPS recommends that AP teachers participate regularly in the Advanced Placement Summer Institute (APSI), a staff-training program for AP teachers. The report examined this recommendation as part of its review of AP staffing and training in DCPS high schools, finding that "Fewer than one-fifth of AP teachers participated in the APSI at least once every three years."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • AP teachers must have access to APSI training opportunities for the recommendation to be implementable.

Constraints¶

  • Actual participation falls short of the recommendation: fewer than one-fifth of AP teachers participated at least once every three years.

Target Learners¶

  • AP teachers in DCPS high schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • quality instruction in Advanced Placement courses

Related Strategies¶

  • Brief initial training with extensive follow-up coaching and on-site facilitation for whole-school program implementation
  • Multi-format teacher professional development for implementing the Facing History and Ourselves curriculum
