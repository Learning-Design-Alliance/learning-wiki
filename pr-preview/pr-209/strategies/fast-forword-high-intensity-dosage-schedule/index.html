Implement Fast ForWord Language at high intensity over a compressed period (100 minutes a day for four to six weeks, or 50 minutes a day for 8 to 12 weeks)¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The program prescribes intensive daily use: "Intensity of the program ranges from 100 minutes a day for four to six weeks to 50 minutes a day for 8 to 12 weeks." In the reviewed studies, treatment groups used the program about 100 minutes a day, five days a week for five to eight weeks, with each participant working on multiple 20-minute training exercises every day. Implementation models include after-school, classroom, or computer lab settings.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Workstation or site licenses and teacher training of one to three days; automatic monthly progress reports allow teachers to examine student and class progress

Constraints¶

  • The dosage range is the developer's stated intensity range for this program, not a generally tested dosage comparison
