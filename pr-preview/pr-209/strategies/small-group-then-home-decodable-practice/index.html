Introduce take-home decodable packs through teacher-led small-group instruction before sending them home¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The program sequences practice so students first encounter the decodable texts in small-group instruction, where teachers model decoding strategies and guide application, and only then take the packs home for continued practice with family support. The article states that "students first engage with the materials in small-group instruction, where teachers model decoding strategies and guide application" before home practice begins.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers use the packs and lesson plans weekly during small-group instruction before sending them home.
