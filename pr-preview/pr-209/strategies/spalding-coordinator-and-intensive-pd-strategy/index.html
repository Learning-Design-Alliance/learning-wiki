Designate a Spalding coordinator and provide 45-70 hours of formal teacher training with ongoing mentoring to support implementation fidelity¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The program's implementation model pairs classroom teachers with a designated Spalding coordinator who "oversees program fidelity, manages assessment data, and provides coaching for teachers as they master the multi-sensory instructional methods." Professional development occurs "through comprehensive in-person workshops, ongoing mentoring, reference materials, and classroom observations," requiring approximately 45-70 hours of formal training with certification before proper implementation.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A reading specialist, curriculum coordinator, or experienced teacher to assume the coordinator role
  • Trained classroom assistants may support small-group instruction during practice sessions

Constraints¶

  • Teachers require approximately 45-70 hours of formal training with certifications to begin implementation properly

Target Learners¶

  • K-6 classroom teachers
  • reading specialists
  • classroom assistants

Target Learning Goals¶

  • faithful implementation of multi-sensory literacy instruction
  • teacher mastery of Spalding Method strategies

Related Strategies¶

  • Provide structured multi-phase professional development with ongoing support for intervention teachers
  • Sustained teacher professional development with coaching and professional learning communities
  • Provide 70 hours of professional development in three phases: intensive training, practice, and implementation
  • Layered implementation support for teachers adopting a new science curriculum: preservice training, follow-up training, and a science coach
  • Implement BARR with a dedicated coordinator, weekly teacher-team meetings, multi-year professional development, and intensive coaching
  • Provide formal training, ongoing supervisory support, and colleague collaboration when introducing new child assessments
  • Coach-the-coaches model for sustaining teacher professional learning implementation
  • Use a full-time program facilitator plus multi-mode professional development to sustain comprehensive reform implementation
