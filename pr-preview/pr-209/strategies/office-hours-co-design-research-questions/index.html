Run conversational office hours in which practitioners and researchers co-design research questions with platform representatives¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Office Hours: A Conversational Series was SEERNet's first application of its guiding principles, grounded in participatory design. "Through a series of meetings, we convened a community of practitioners and researchers to work with representatives from the five DLPs to co-design research questions based on problems of practice." Registration included an interest form capturing expertise; conversations were synthesized by a researcher into draft research questions; and a feedback loop with participants refined them into viable questions. The resulting resources are available at www.seernet.org.

Related Strategies¶

  • Engaging practitioners through an advisory board, co-design office hours, and guiding-principles publications
