Preteach core classroom content in small Tier 2 groups of 3-5 students¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

In Enhanced Core Reading Instruction, Tier 2 small groups of 3-5 students are pretaught content soon to be taught in the core classroom, so supplemental instruction is directly aligned with Tier 1. Teaching focuses on "phonemic awareness, sound blending, word reading, fluency, reading decodable text, and irregular words."
