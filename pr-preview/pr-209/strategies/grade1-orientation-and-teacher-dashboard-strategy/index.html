Provide an orientation period for Grade 1 students and teacher-facing monitoring dashboards¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

For first-time young users, the authors recommend a distinct orientation period: "An orientation period with proper training could help to familiarize these very young learners with using comprehensive learning systems effectively and correctly." They further recommend translating profile findings into pedagogical solutions, suggesting a teacher-facing dashboard could allow teachers to monitor students' emerging profiles and offer class-wide interventions guiding transitions toward more effective profiles.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Extra support for students using the platform for the first time while adjusting to a new school environment

Constraints¶

  • Evidence for dashboard effectiveness is drawn from higher education contexts, and the authors note these tools could be impactful in primary and middle school settings

Target Learners¶

  • Grade 1 students new to e-learning platforms

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Effective and comprehensive use of online learning systems

Related Strategies¶

  • Adapt e-learning platforms to distinct student engagement profiles with adaptive recommendations and guidance
