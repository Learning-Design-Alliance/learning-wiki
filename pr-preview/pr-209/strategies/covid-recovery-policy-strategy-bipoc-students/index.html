Multi-pronged policy strategy for COVID-19 learning recovery for BIPOC students¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report offers a set of policy recommendations meant to encompass a wide range of supports: improving remote learning (broadband, devices, technical support, family communication, teacher professional development), extending learning time (summer enrichment, early school start, extended days, high-quality tutoring tied to school curriculum), targeting federal resources equitably to districts and schools with higher concentrations of BIPOC and low-income students, and addressing academic, social, and emotional needs through regular assessment, mental health screening and counseling, and expanded school meal programs.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Recommendations assume policymakers can direct federal resources and that districts can coordinate with community-based organizations with a proven track record of serving BIPOC communities

Constraints¶

  • The report states data should not be used to underinvest: in some places data has been used to underinvest when it should be used to target more resources

Target Learners¶

  • BIPOC students in grades 3-8, particularly Black, Latinx, AIAN, and Native Hawaiian students in high-poverty schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Recovery of math and reading achievement and growth toward and beyond pre-pandemic rates of learning

Related Strategies¶

  • Use summer learning loss research to help educators, policy makers, and families plan for and address impacts of extended school closures
  • When planning for the school year during COVID-19, schools and districts should weigh public health factors alongside educational factors such as potential learning loss
  • Use federal ESSER and IDEA funding to provide early intervention, extended school year support in the summer, and other evidence-based supports for students with disabilities
  • Deploy federal recovery funding to support recovery, accelerate learning, and transform schools
