Select survey audiences around three postsecondary milestones: recent graduates, the 2-3 year sweet-spot cohort, and long-term alumni¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The guide recommends organizing the audience strategy around three distinct postsecondary milestones. Recent graduates one year out are ideal for immediate transition experiences; the sweet spot cohort two to three years out has enough distance to speak to the utility of their preparation while memories remain sharp; long-term alumni five-plus years out are best for career mobility, degree completion, and building partnership opportunities. Decisions about instrumentation may shift earlier audience decisions.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Access to a solid set of alumni email addresses, which the guide calls often the largest obstacle to a successful alumni survey

Constraints¶

  • Districts cannot claim direct causal credit for long-term outcomes of the 5+ year cohort

Target Learners¶

  • High school alumni at 1, 2-3, and 5+ years post-graduation

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Matching alumni cohorts to the feedback questions a school needs answered

Related Strategies¶

  • Sustain alumni feedback loops through strategic planning integration, cross-functional interpretation teams, transparent value-exchanges, and phased data collection
