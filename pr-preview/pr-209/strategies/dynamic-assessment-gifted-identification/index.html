Use dynamic assessment to identify gifted learners' potential¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The essay recommends the field "turn to experimenting with methods of dynamic assessment" for identifying learning potential. Such assessment "works to isolate the interaction between the learner and a given task," and, in a classroom-like setting, can reveal what a student knows and can learn while incorporating interaction with more capable others, new strategies, and activation of prior knowledge.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Administration in a setting resembling the traditional classroom so results shed light on what the student knows and can learn

Constraints¶

  • The essay frames this as experimentation with methods rather than an established practice
