Train paraprofessional tutors with one initial day of training plus two two-hour booster sessions¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Number Rockets prepares non-specialist tutors — retired teachers and substitute teachers — with a light-touch training model: "Tutors receive one day of training before beginning, and two two-hour booster sessions afterwards." The scripted curriculum makes this brief training sufficient, and booster sessions reinforce practices once tutoring is underway.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A scripted curriculum so tutors do not need to design instruction
  • Scheduled booster sessions after tutoring begins

Constraints¶

  • Training is provided through AIR and must be requested by type, grade level, number of trainees, and location

Target Learners¶

  • first-grade students struggling in math

Target Learning Goals¶

  • faithful delivery of the scripted math tutoring curriculum

Related Strategies¶

  • Train paraprofessionals to deliver Sound Partners lessons using a local trainer plus manual and video-based professional development
  • Support Math 180 teachers with initial start-up training plus 2-3 coaching visits during the year
  • Prepare teachers with one full training day plus a mid-year follow-up day
  • Staff Passport to Literacy with trained paraprofessional interventionists receiving 8 hours of training over 2 days
  • Intensive trainer-led observation and feedback model for tutoring professional development
  • Train teachers to implement peer-tutoring programs through an all-day modeling and role-playing workshop plus a program manual
  • Train tutors with an initial week plus twice-weekly hour-long follow-ups supported by structured manuals
