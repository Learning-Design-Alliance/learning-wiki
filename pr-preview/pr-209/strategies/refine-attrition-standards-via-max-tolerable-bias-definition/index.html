Refine attrition standards by revisiting the definition of maximum tolerable bias¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The authors recommend that evidence reviews seeking to improve attrition standards direct their efforts at the standard's bias-tolerance parameter rather than at other model inputs. Because their sensitivity analysis shows the attrition boundaries respond most to "the maximum level of bias that an evidence review is willing to tolerate," they conclude that "the most productive refinements to existing attrition standards may be with respect to the definition of 'maximum tolerable bias.'" This makes the tolerance definition the highest-leverage point for adapting standards to new contexts.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • An operational definition of maximum tolerable bias must be chosen by the evidence review, since attrition bounds depend on it most.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Researchers conducting studies reviewed by federal evidence reviews such as the WWC

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Designing and interpreting randomized controlled trials of educational interventions with acceptable attrition bias
