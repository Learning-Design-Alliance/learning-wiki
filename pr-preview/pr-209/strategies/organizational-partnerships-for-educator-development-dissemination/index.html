Build organizational partnerships to disseminate educator development strategies¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The blueprint presents an approach to building organizational partnerships whose purpose is to share and strengthen educator development strategies. It states that it "describes an approach to building organizational partnerships that support and strengthen educator development," and offers guidance and tools intended to help organizations form such partnerships for disseminating educator development innovations.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Organizations willing to enter partnerships to share educator development strategies

Constraints¶

  • The blueprint focuses specifically on developing and supporting SEED partnerships, though it claims broader relevance

Target Learners¶

  • educators participating in professional development through partner organizations

Target Learning Goals¶

  • improved educator development and dissemination of effective practices

Related Strategies¶

  • Facilitate knowledge sharing between organizations experienced with evidence-based strategies and organizations seeking to use them
  • Disseminate partnership information through blog posts, videos, and infographics
