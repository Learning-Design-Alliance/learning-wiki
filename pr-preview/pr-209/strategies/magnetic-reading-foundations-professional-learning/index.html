Tiered professional learning for Magnetic Reading Foundations implementation¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Curriculum Associates offers research-driven professional learning for new, practicing, and advanced users, focused on preparing teachers to use strategic routines, content-rich decodable texts, and targeted assessments to teach foundational skills efficiently. Delivery combines six-hour on-site development days with free digital courses on the OEL platform covering product features and implementation best practices.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • On-site development days are six hours, with the recommended number of sessions varying depending on implementation length

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Teachers of grades K-2 implementing Magnetic Reading Foundations

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Teaching foundational reading skills efficiently and effectively using program resources

Related Strategies¶

  • Use Curriculum Associates professional learning: introductory course, on-site development days, and free OEL digital courses
  • Implement i-Ready with designated points of contact and Curriculum Associates professional development support
