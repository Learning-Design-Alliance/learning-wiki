Adapt ERWC pacing by combining activities and supplementing with teachers' own materials¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Teachers who found the course pacing challenging made modifications: they were "able to make some modifications to the curriculum by combining activities," and other teachers reported "supplementing ERWC with their own instructional materials." This adaptation strategy kept the course deliverable within real classroom schedules.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers need discretion to combine activities and add their own materials when course pacing is challenging.

Constraints¶

  • Some teachers reported challenges related to the pacing of the course.

Target Learners¶

  • 12th grade English teachers implementing ERWC

Target Learning Goals¶

  • sustained implementation of the ERWC curriculum
