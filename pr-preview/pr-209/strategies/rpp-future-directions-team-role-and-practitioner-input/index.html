Future RPP work should investigate which problems of practice suit the RPP framework, gather honest practitioner input, and consider the role of the whole RPP team¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

In its concluding remarks the presentation sets forward-looking directions for RPP methodology: investigating which problems of practice are more suitable for the RPP framework, the "Need for honest input from practitioners", relevance to Dimension 5 (building capacity of both sides to do partnership work), and considering the "role of RPP team" rather than only the role of the researcher or the practitioner. These are recommendations for how the field should develop the beyond-research roles question.
