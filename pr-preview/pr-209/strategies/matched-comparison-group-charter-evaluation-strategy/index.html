Evaluate charter school impacts by matching students to demographically and academically similar comparison students¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report measures a charter school's impact by identifying a comparison group of students in other district and charter schools who were similar on observed characteristics, then comparing outcomes. As the report states, "we identified a group of students in other Kansas City district and charter schools who had similar demographic characteristics and achievement at the end of 4th grade." This matching strategy grounds the year-7 impact estimates.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Baseline data on student demographics and prior achievement (end of 4th grade) for both groups
  • Outcome data for students in grades 5 through 11 in both the treated and comparison groups

Constraints¶

  • Causal estimates depend on the quality of the match; ACT impact could not be calculated because necessary data were not available

Target Learners¶

  • Students in grades 5 through 11 in urban charter and district schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Estimating school-level impacts on achievement growth and college-readiness outcomes

Related Strategies¶

  • Use a matched comparison group design with qualitative mechanism exploration to evaluate charter school impacts
  • Estimate cumulative impacts of district-wide reforms by comparing achievement changes to similar students in similar geographic areas without the reforms
