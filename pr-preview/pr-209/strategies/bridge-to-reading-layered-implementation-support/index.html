Layered implementation support: initial on-site training plus follow-up modeling, observations, coaching, and monthly virtual sessions¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

In the evaluated studies, implementation was supported through a sequence of professional learning activities: "a Heggerty literacy specialist provided on-site training before the start of the school year," followed by classroom modeling 12 weeks later, January lesson observations with coaching, and monthly virtual coaching sessions during the school year. On-demand PD and demonstration videos are also available via myHeggerty.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Access to a Heggerty literacy specialist to deliver training, modeling, and coaching

Constraints¶

  • Professional development - virtual or in-person - for teachers implementing Bridge to Reading is recommended, but it is optional

Target Learners¶

  • First-grade students
  • Kindergarten students
  • Second-grade students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Foundational reading skills including phonics and phonemic awareness
