Implement programs to improve students' classroom behavior and social skills¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The guide's fourth recommendation has students 'establish attainable academic and behavioral goals and be recognized when they accomplish them,' teaches strategies to strengthen problem-solving and decision-making skills, and partners with community-based agencies to address external factors affecting social and behavioral interactions. Adult advocates or other engaged adults help students set goals with specific benchmarks.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Adult advocates or engaged adults to help students establish goals with specific benchmarks
  • Partnerships with community-based program providers and agencies such as social services, welfare, mental health, and law enforcement

Constraints¶

  • The panel rated the evidence for this recommendation as low

Target Learners¶

  • Middle and high school students at risk of dropping out with behavioral or social-skill challenges

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Improved classroom behavior, problem-solving and decision-making skills, and social competence

Related Strategies¶

  • Provide academic support and enrichment to improve academic performance of at-risk students
  • Positive Behavior Support
  • Explicit Interpersonal Skills Instruction
  • Create a Classroom Code of Conduct
  • Establishing Classroom Norms
  • Take A Break Station
  • Model Empathy and Explain
  • Personalize the learning environment and instructional process to foster belonging
  • Provide rigorous and relevant instruction connecting academics to postsecondary options
