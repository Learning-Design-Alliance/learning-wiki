Use a monthly school attendance team to review data and problem-solve¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

ATI-UP requires each school to "designate a team of at least three members (administrator, interventionist, and teacher) who meet monthly to review attendance data and problem-solving." This regular data-review cycle is the operational core of the school-wide framework, connecting monitored attendance data to coordinated school action.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • At least three team members: an administrator, an interventionist, and a teacher
  • Access to attendance data

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • all students in participating schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • improved school attendance

Related Strategies¶

  • Provide two days of pre-implementation training plus ongoing technical support for school teams
  • Establish a monthly school implementation team with two days of pre-launch training and ongoing technical support
  • Monitor tutoring implementation and attendance regularly during program rollout
  • Monthly Action Team meetings with shared leadership and activity-quality evaluation
