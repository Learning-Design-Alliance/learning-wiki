Attach micro-credentials to district initiatives, incentives, and job-embedded structures to strengthen professional learning systems¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Across the four cases, organizations gained traction by aligning micro-credentials to existing initiatives and giving them real value: BCPS tied them to S.T.A.T. and CPD credits, KMSD to salary, MTLC to PowerUp, and the Friday Institute to MOOC-Ed units. The report concludes that 'educator micro-credentials can create a stronger professional learning system that supports continuous educator growth, and in turn, the students they serve.'

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Alignment between micro-credential competencies and the district's instructional priorities so teachers see them as an on-ramp to district goals

Constraints¶

  • Value depends on recognizing entities defining how micro-credentials count toward advancement or credit

Target Learners¶

  • classroom teachers and school and district leaders

Target Learning Goals¶

  • continuous, competency-based educator professional growth applied in classrooms
