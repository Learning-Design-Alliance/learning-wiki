Thomas 1975¶

Thomas, Mollie B. (1975). Underlying Constructs of Locus of Control of Reinforcement. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED108039

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Author's conclusion from comparing the component structures of the career-development-domain CDR scale and the academic-domain IAR questionnaire in the 188-student sample, both of which produced eleven components under principal components analysis.

"The present study provides evidence that instruments purporting to measure locus of control in domains other than those proposed by Rotter (e.g., academic achievement) serve to increase the number of dimensions."
