District supports for teachers' standards-aligned instructional practices in the Chicago Public Schools 2015¶

District supports for teachers' standards-aligned instructional practices in the Chicago Public Schools. (2015). AERA Open. https://journals.sagepub.com

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Mixed-methods exploratory study of Chicago Public Schools teachers comparing implementation supports; the article reports that "professional learning opportunities were strongly related to instructional practices" while resources showed weaker, mixed relationships. No effect sizes are printed in the supplied text.

"It provides comparative evidence that professional learning opportunities were strongly related to instructional practices, whereas curriculum and instructional resources had more modest and mixed relationships."
