Charter School Performance in New Mexico 2019¶

Charter School Performance in New Mexico. (2019). Center for Research on Education Outcomes, Stanford University. https://credo.stanford.edu/reports/item/charter-school-performance-in-new-mexico-2/

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Matched VCR analysis by school grade configuration (Figure 9), using NCES elementary/middle/high/multi-level designations; reading surpluses reported in days of learning, math null.

"charter elementary school students register learning surpluses in reading equivalent to 47 additional days of learning; charter middle school students outperform their TPS VCRs in reading by 53 extra days of learning. The math growth of students attending charter elementary and middle schools is not statistically different from that of their peers in TPS."
