What Works Clearinghouse 2010¶

What Works Clearinghouse. (2010). WWC Intervention Report: Reading Plus®. U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/419

q2 · i? · review · r3

The report's review of 18 studies found that one quasi-experimental study met standards with reservations while the remaining 17 did not meet standards or eligibility screens. The report states "the extent of evi- dence for Reading Plus ® on adolescent learners to be small for the comprehension domain."

"Based on one stu dy, the WWC considers the extent of evi- dence for Reading Plus ® on adolescent learners to be small for the comprehension domain."
