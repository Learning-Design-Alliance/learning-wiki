Evidence¶

Son 2022¶

Son, E. H., & Penry, T. (2022). Variations in Project-Based Course Design. Journal of Problem Based Learning in Higher Education, Vol. 10, No. 1.

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Qualitative finding from Course C's syllabus and student reflections; one student reported that when plans changed, "having a team with similar goals made finding the next step way easier." The authors frame this as a possible relationship, not a tested one.

"In this course, even though faculty had a clear plan for outcomes, technologies, and methods, the Flexible approach to problem-solving suggested a possible relationship between the two spectrums, with a Flexible course strategy supporting student learning at the Cooperative end of the work-style spectrum."
