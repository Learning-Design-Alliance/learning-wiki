Challenges of Pronunciation Practices in the ESL Curriculum within the CLT Framework in Bangladesh: A Systematic Review. World Journal of Education, 14(3). https://doi.org/10.5430/wje.v14n3p137

q2 · i? · review · r1

Narrative synthesis in the review's literature section. The review reports that because CLT prioritizes fluency and meaning over accuracy, pronunciation lacks systematic evaluation, and "Without dedicated pronunciation assessment tools and criteria, teachers must struggle to accurately examine students' progress in pronunciation."

"the absence of focused pronunciation assessment within the CLT framework significantly contributes to the challenges faced in teaching and learning ESL (English as a Second Language) pronunciation"
