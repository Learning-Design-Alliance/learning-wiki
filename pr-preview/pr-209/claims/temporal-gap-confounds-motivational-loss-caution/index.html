Evidence¶

David Wenhao Huang 2004¶

David Wenhao Huang, Hans Aagard, Heidi Diefes-Dux. (2004). Impact of Cognitive-based Instructional Intervention on Learning Motivation: The Implementation of Student-made Glossary in a Programming-oriented Engineering Problem-solving Course and Its Impact on Learning Motivation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED485015

q1 · i? · design · r2

Authors' interpretation in the Discussion: the pre-motivational survey was administered in the first quarter of the semester while the glossary was not in place until the last quarter, and the study ran simultaneously with course progression, confounding the one-group pre/post result.

"Therefore it is very likely that the glossary is not the cause of the motivational loss. Various factors such as course structure, complexity of learning tasks, interaction between teaching staff"
