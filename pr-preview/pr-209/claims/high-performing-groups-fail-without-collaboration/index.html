Evidence¶

Fusco 2025¶

Fusco, J., DiMaria, S., Yeadon, B., Kolodner, J., & Shell, A. (2025). Assessment During Collaborative Learning. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/252

q2 · i? · review · r1

The review attributes this finding to Barron (2003) as the rationale for assessing collaborative skills: understanding how students collaborate, assessing the quality, and giving feedback to help improve it. No effect size is reported.

"Barron’s work showed that even when groups were made of high performing students, they would often fail at a problem-solving task if they didn’t collaborate. Groups that were successful on a task generally took time to discuss each group member’s ideas."
