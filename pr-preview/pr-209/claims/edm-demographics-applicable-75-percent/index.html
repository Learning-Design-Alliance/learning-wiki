Evidence¶

Paquette 2020¶

Paquette, Li, Baker, Ocumpaugh, & Andres. (2020). Who's learning? Using demographics in EDM research. Journal of Educational Data Mining, Volume 12, No 3. https://jedm.educationaldatamining.org/index.php/JEDM/article/view/404

q2 · i? · review · r2

Applicability coding of all 385 surveyed papers (JEDM and EDM conference, 2015–2019) found 288 papers (74.81%) where demographic data was potentially relevant, stable across publication types (73–75%).

"nearly 75% of EDM publications in this study contained analyses where demographic information was applicable. This finding holds across all five years of publications that we coded."
