access-stats¶

Mills, K., Coenraad, M., Ruiz, P., Burke, Q., & Weisgrau J. (2021, December). Computational thinking for an inclusive world: A resource for educators to learn and lead. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/138

q2 · i? · review · r1

The report's review of the current state of computing education, citing Banilower et al. (2018) and Code.org et al. (2020), states that "Less than half (47%) of high schools in the United States offer computer science." It further reports high-poverty schools (26%) and smaller high schools (23%) were less likely to offer CS than low-poverty (46%) and larger high schools (43%).

"Less than half (47%) of high schools in the United States offer computer science, 38% of middle schools, and 26% of elementary schools offer instruction in computer programming"
