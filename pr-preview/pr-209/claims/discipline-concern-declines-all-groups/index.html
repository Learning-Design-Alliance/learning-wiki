Evidence¶

Olsen 1990¶

Olsen, Dwayne G.; Heyse, Kathy L. (1990). Development and Concerns of First-year and Reentry Teachers with and without Mentors. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED323188

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Analysis of discipline as a concern examined separately in Table 8 across the three interviews. First-interview discipline percentages were 43% (first-year with mentors), 38% (first-year without), 60% (reentry with), and 18% (reentry without); the authors note this is consistent with Odell's (1986) findings.

"The data of Table 8 indicate that all four groups of teachers--first-year with mentors, first-year without mentors, reentry with mentors, and reentry without mentors, declined in concern for discipline/classroom management"
