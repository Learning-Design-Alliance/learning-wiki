A Focused Look at Rural Schools Receiving School Improvement Grants 2014¶

Descriptive comparison of SIG model adoption reported in the brief's introduction, drawing on state-reported data for Tier 1 and 2 schools funded in 2010-11. Rural schools adopted the transformation model at 95 percent versus 74 percent for nonrural schools.

"Rural SIG schools were more likely than nonrural schools (95 versus 74 percent) to adopt the transformation model, one of the four allowable SIG models."
