The report's framing statement, offered without cited evidence, asserts that alternative teacher preparation programs expanded rapidly while research on the effectiveness of different teacher training strategies remained scarce.

"In recent years, alternative programs have expanded rapidly. Despite the expansion of these new routes into teaching, little research exists to provide guidance on the effectiveness of different teacher training strategies."
