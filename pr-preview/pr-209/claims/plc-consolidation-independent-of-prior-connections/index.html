Mann-Whitney U tests and Cohen's d compared PLC dimension ratings across connection types (Table 8). "There are no significant differences between the three types of PLCs according to the connections"; effect sizes null in some cases, low in most.

Layton-Jaramillo 2025 (2)¶

Layton-Jaramillo, S.E., Duarte-Velasco, O.G., Aguaded-Ramírez, E.M., & Carrillo-Rosúa, F.J. (2025). Innovation in university science teaching through professional learning communities. Journal of Technology and Science Education, 15(1), 142-161. https://doi.org/10.3926/jotse.3115

q2 · i? · design · r2

The authors interpret the null connection-type differences as an effect of I3P team monitoring, stating "Type 1 PLCs required greater support from I3P professionals throughout the project than Type 2 and Type 3 PLCs".

"This result is directly related to the monitoring carried out for the teams since Type 1 PLCs required greater support from I3P professionals throughout the project than Type 2 and Type 3 PLCs."
