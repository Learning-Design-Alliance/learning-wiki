q2 · i? · causal · r2

Subgroup analyses by initial skill level and socioeconomic status reported in the footnotes to the WWC findings tables for the Torgesen et al. (2006) trial; no effect sizes are printed for the subgroup contrasts.

"The study reported that Corrective Reading had a statistically significant positive effect on alphabetics for students with high pretest PPVT scores and no effects on students with low pretest PPVT scores."

Corrective Reading—What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report 2007 (2)¶

Corrective Reading—What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report. (2007). U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://www.ed.gov/rschstat/eval/disadv/title1interimreport/index.html

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Socioeconomic-status subgroup analysis from the same trial, reported in the alphabetics findings footnote; the fluency footnote reports the same pattern for the Oral Fluency Test. No subgroup effect sizes are printed.

"An additional subgroup analysis found statistically significant positive effects on the TOWRE sight word efficiency subtest for students not eligible for the free/reduced lunch program, but not for students eligible for the free/reduced lunch program."
