REDI (Research Based 2024¶

REDI (Research Based, Developmentally Informed). (2024). Program description page, Evidence Rating: promising. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/redi-research-based-developmentally-informed/

q3 · i1 · causal · r?

A randomized study in which Head Start centers in rural and urban Central and Southwestern Pennsylvania were assigned to REDI or control conditions, with 356 students. The mean effect of "+0.23" qualified REDI for the “Strong” category.

"At the end of the preschool year, effect sizes were +0.16 for TOPEL Print Awareness, +0.39 for TOPEL Blending, and +0.15 for EOWPVT Vocabulary, for a mean of +0.23, qualifying REDI for the “Strong” category."
