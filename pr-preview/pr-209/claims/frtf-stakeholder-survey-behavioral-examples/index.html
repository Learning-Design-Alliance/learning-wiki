q2 · i? · design · r2

Stakeholder consultation survey in the extension-and-validation study. Participants were given FRTF talent definitions and "asked to share examples of such talents in the workplace." Employers were a convenience sample who had hired a WIL student in the previous year, across varied industries and positions.

"A total of 1,806 responses were provided. The average number of examples for each talent was 41.1 (SD = 3.22)."

q2 · i? · design · r2

The selected steering committee, comprising experts in WIL and talent development in higher education, reviewed, deduplicated, and synthesized the survey responses; the resulting 59 behavioral examples appear in Table 4 and were carried into the sorting validation.

"After removing duplicates and much discussion, 59 behavioral examples were identified (Table 4)."
