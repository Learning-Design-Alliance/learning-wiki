Evidence¶

Guntur 2024¶

Guntur, M., & Purnomo, Y. W. (2024). A Meta-Analysis of Self-Regulated Learning Interventions Studies on Learning Outcomes in Online and Blended Environments. Online Learning Journal, 28(3). https://doi.org/10.24059/olj.v28i3.4025

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analysis of 15 studies (5,519 participants) from Scopus, IEEE Explore, and ERIC, using Cohen's d under a random effects model. The forest plot yielded a pooled effect size of 0.65, which the authors classify as "moderate" per Cohen et al.'s thresholds (moderate = 0.50–0.80).

"Figure 3 shows that a random effects model (RE Model) with a value of 0.65 was found. This means that self-regulated learning intervention studies affect learning results in online and blended environments by 65%"
