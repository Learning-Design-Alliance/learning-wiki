Evidence¶

Shavelson 1986¶

Shavelson, Richard J.; Webb, Noreen M.; Lehman, Penny. (1986). The Role of Symbol Systems in Problem Solving: A Literature Review. CSE Report No. 269. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED338680

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing DiVesta and Peverly (1984), an experiment manipulating the context in which novel information was learned (e.g., antique dealers) and measuring far transfer as recognizing concept instances in a new context (e.g., magic shows). The review attributes the hypothesis-and-finding to that study.

"[Practice] on examples from a variety ofcontexts (encoding variability) will result in decontextualizationdue to the availability of multiple retrieval paths"
