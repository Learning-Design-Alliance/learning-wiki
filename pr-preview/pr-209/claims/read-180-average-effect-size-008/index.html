Synthesis of five studies meeting inclusion standards (3,992 students). The summary reports "The average effect size across all studies was a modest +0.08" and that two studies with significant positive effects qualified the program for the Strong category.

"Five studies of READ 180 met inclusion standards. Two of these had statistically significant positive effects in comparison to control groups, qualifying READ 180 for the “Strong” category."
