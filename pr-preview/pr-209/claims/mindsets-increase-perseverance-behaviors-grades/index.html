Farrington 2012¶

Farrington, C.A., Roderick, M., Allensworth, E., Nagaoka, J., Keyes, T.S., Johnson, D.W., & Beechum, N.O. (2012). Teaching adolescents to become learners. The role of noncognitive factors in shaping school performance: A critical literature review. Chicago: University of Chicago Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/teaching-adolescents-become-learners-role-noncognitive-factors-shaping-school

q2 · i? · review · r2

Review synthesis in Chapter 2 of psychological evidence on mindsets. The review also describes a reciprocal negative loop: negative mindsets stifle perseverance and undermine behaviors, resulting in poor performance that reinforces the negative mindsets.

"Overall, the evidence clearly demonstrates that the four academic mindsets outlined above each increase students' academic perseverance and improve academic behaviors, leading to better performance as measured by higher grades."
