National Academies of Sciences 2018¶

q1 · i? · review · r?

The statement of task names integrative methodological approaches — "network modeling, multi-level modeling, simulation modeling" — as deserving attention for studying learning complexities across contexts, including micro–macro interplay and teacher–learner interactions within curricula over time.

"Attention will be given to ethological advances and designs that permit the integration of knowledge from multiple fields (e.g., network modeling, multi-level modeling, simulation modeling) and that enable study of the complexities of learning across various contexts."
