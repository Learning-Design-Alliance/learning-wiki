Reading Plus — Evidence Rating Summary 2024¶

Reading Plus — Evidence Rating Summary. (2024). https://evidenceforessa.org/program/reading-plus/

q3 · i? · causal · r?

A randomized study in six elementary schools in an urban district in the northeastern United States, involving 426 students, compared Reading Plus to other types of targeted reading instruction. Reading Plus students made "significantly larger improvements in reading achievement (effect size = +0.11)" on the GRADE.

"The study found that students who engaged in Reading Plus showed significantly greater improvements in reading proficiency than did control students who received other types of targeted reading instruction. Specifically, Reading Plus students made significantly larger improvements in reading achievement (effect size = +0.11), as measured by the Group Reading Assessment Diagnostic Evaluation (GRADE)."
