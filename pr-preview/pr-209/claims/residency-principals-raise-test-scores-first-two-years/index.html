Rivkin 2023¶

Rivkin, S.G., Sartain, L., & Schiman, J.C. (2023). Training and retaining effective school leaders: Results from efforts to improve principal effectiveness in Chicago Public Schools. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/training-retaining-effective-school-leaders

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental event-study analysis of CPS principals and residency participants entering the principalship 2013–2019, addressing staggered treatment timing. The printed effect sizes are 0.07 standard deviations per year in math and roughly 0.05 standard deviations in reading, favoring residency completers' schools over non-residency principals' schools.

"In math, their students scored 0.07 standard deviations more per year than at schools with non-residency principals. In reading, their students scored roughly 0.05 standard deviations more compared to schools with non-residency principals."
