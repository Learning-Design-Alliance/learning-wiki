Related Claims¶

  • In simulations, Bayesian GLASSO estimation outperforms frequentist GLASSO on bias in edge weights, centrality measures, correlation between estimated and true partial correlations, and specificity — related
  • Sensitivity is better for the frequentist GLASSO than for the Bayesian GLASSO, though Bayesian GLASSO performance is usually close — related
  • The frequentist graphical LASSO has difficulty estimating centrality indices and uncertainty in these measures in symptom networks — related
  • Bayesian GLASSO shows good coverage of uncertainty for strength and closeness centrality, but uncertainty in betweenness centrality is estimated less well — related
