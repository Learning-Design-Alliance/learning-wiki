q2 · i? · design · r1

The article reports, citing Kim (2006), that Fluspeak was tested with 36 university students in a hybrid teaching approach with a comparison between human and automatic scoring; "it gave poor results overall for advanced learners trying to gain fluency." No effect size is printed.

"It was tested with 36 university students through a hybrid teaching approach mixing Face to face teaching with individual work with the software. The study included a comparison between human scoring and automatic scoring with Fluspeak. Although Fluspeak gave good results with beginners focussing on phoneme production, it gave poor results overall for advanced learners trying to gain fluency."
