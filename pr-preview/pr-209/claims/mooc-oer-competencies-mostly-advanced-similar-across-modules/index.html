Evidence¶

García-López 2017¶

García-López, R.-I., Cuevas Salazar, O., Ramírez-Montoya, M.-S., & Tenorio-Sepúlveda, G.-C. (2017). Competencies for Production, Search, Diffusion and Mobilization of Open Educational Resources. International Education Studies, 10(4). https://doi.org/10.5539/ies.v10n4p78

q2 · i? · design · r2

Descriptive analysis of rubric self-assessment survey (37 questions, four modules) applied to 134 teachers after the four-week MOOC, analyzed with frequency distributions and contingency charts. The authors conclude "the level of competence of teachers is very similar in the four modules".

"It is remarkable that in the three levels of competence the total percentage is very similar in all four modules; that is, the percentages at the basic level are between 0.5% and 3.9%, in the intermediate range between 30.0 % and 31.5%, and in the advanced are between 65.4% and 69.5%."
