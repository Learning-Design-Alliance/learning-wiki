What Works Clearinghouse 2007¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2007). WWC Intervention Report: Peer Tutoring and Response Groups. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

The WWC's effectiveness rating for the English language development domain, based on three studies meeting WWC evidence standards. The rating scheme requires statistically significant positive effects with no overriding contrary evidence; the report states the intervention received "the highest applicable rating."

"The WWC found Peer Tutoring and Response Groups to have positive effects on English language development"

What Works Clearinghouse 2007 (2)¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2007). WWC Intervention Report: Peer Tutoring and Response Groups. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r1

WWC-computed improvement indices across the three studies' findings in the English language development domain. Appendix A3 reports a domain average effect size of 0.56 across all studies. The improvement index reflects percentile-rank differences favoring the intervention group.

"The average improvement index for the English language development domain is +17 percentile points across the three studies, with a range of +1 to +48 percentile points across findings."
