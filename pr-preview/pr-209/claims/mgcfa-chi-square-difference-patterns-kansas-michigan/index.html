Construct Validity and Measurement Invariance of Computerized Adaptive Testing: Application to Measures of Academic Progress (MAP) Using Confirmatory Factor Analysis. Nested-model chi-square difference tests in the MGCFA analyses of Kansas MAP Reading and Michigan MAP Mathematics tests across grades. The article notes the chi-square test may be misleading with large samples and recommends other GOF differences as adjuncts.

"For the Kansas MAP Reading Tests (see Table 8), χ2 increases ( Δχ2) are significant for testing L invariance at different terms, but not significant for testing LV invariance. The rest of the states’ results show a similar pattern. For Michigan Mathematics Tests, all χ2 increases are significant for both L and LV invariance."
