Comparison study in the McKay Institute projects section: an intermediate Spanish class using an annotated videodisc of the film Marario (no lecture, lab interaction) versus a video-tape group that received a lecture and viewed the film at the LRC. The article reports the video-disc group "almost two full standard deviations above the video-tapegroup (S.D. difference .192)" and significance exceeding the 0.01 level of confidence; no standardized effect size is printed.
