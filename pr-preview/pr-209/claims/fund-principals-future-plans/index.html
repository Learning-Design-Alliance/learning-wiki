Survey analysis of CCSR 2007 principal survey future-plans items, collapsing elementary and high school principals together (65 Fund-supported, 159 other principals answering all three questions). Fund-supported principals also plan two additional years as school leaders and four additional years in education.

"However, when we add time already spent as a principal to respondents' expectations for the future, Fund-supported principals plan to spend three fewer years in the principalship than do other leaders."
