GLM models predicting i-Ready growth (Tables 1 and 2). Referral coefficients were significant for math in grade 2 (B = -0.39*) and grade 4 (B = -0.36); reading coefficients for suspensions and referrals were mostly non-significant.

"Similarly, referrals negatively affected growth in grades 2 and 4. In reading, suspensions and referrals did not show as strong or consistent negative effects as they do in math, though some negative trends are observed."
