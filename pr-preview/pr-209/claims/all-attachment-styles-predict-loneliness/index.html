Akbağ 2010¶

Akbağ, M. & Erden İmamoğlu, S. (2010). The Prediction of Gender and Attachment Styles on Shame, Guilt, and Loneliness. Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 10(2), 669-682. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ889191

In the loneliness regression with the 360-student sample, "Attachment styles and gender together accounted for 23% of variance (F=21.29, p<.001)", with secure style negative (t secure=-2.96, p<.01) and insecure styles positive (tfearful=3.49, tpreoccupied=4.62, tdismissing=4.98, all p<.001).
