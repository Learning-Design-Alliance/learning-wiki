Coombs 1987¶

Coombs, Lucinda H. (1987). The GED Writing Skills Test Essay Teacher Training Manual. Maine Dept. of Educational and Cultural Services / University of Maine. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED324536

The manual cites New York state's annual writing competency testing program as evidence that premature writing without task analysis and organization causes the greatest difficulty. The review attributes this finding without reporting study details or effect sizes.
