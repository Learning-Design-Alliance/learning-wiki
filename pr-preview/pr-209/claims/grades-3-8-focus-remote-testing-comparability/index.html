q2 · i? · design · r1

The guide's data-notes section states the visualizations include tests administered both remotely and in person, and cites NWEA's comparability analysis of remote and in-person fall 2020 MAP Growth testing as the basis for the grade focus.

"NWEA research vi suggests that the validity and reliability of remote test results are largely comparable with results from in-person testing for students in grades 3 and up; thus, the dashboard primarily focuses on grades 3 through 8."
