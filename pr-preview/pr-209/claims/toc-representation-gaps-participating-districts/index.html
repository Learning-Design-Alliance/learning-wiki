Observational descriptive analysis of district-provided demographic data for nine participating districts (Table I). The table reports non-white student and teacher percentages; the text notes 'the districts with the lowest gaps were also those with the lowest level of diversity.'

"The gap between the proportion of Black and Latino students and teachers ranged from 0 to nearly 60 percentage points. The gaps were even greater when we include all non-white students and teachers. Here the gap ranged from 4 to 65 percentage points."
