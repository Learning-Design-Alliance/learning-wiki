Evidence¶

DaisyQuest 2006¶

DaisyQuest. (2006). WWC Intervention Report: DaisyQuest. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Randomized trial of 27 students in a child-care facility assigned to DaisyQuest or regular program, using two phonological awareness tests. The report states effects were found "in favor of the DaisyQuest group" on both measures, confirmed by WWC calculations.

"For both measures, the authors found, and the WWC conﬁrmed, positive, statistically signiﬁcant effects in favor of the DaisyQuest group."
