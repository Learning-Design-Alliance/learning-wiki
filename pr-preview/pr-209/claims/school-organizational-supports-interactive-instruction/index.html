Julia B. Smith 2001¶

Julia B. Smith, Valerie E. Lee, and Fred M. Newmann. (2001). Instruction and Achievement in Chicago Elementary Schools. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/instruction-and-achievement-chicago-elementary-schools

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Survey-based analysis comparing schools high versus low on three organizational supports measured in the 1997 Consortium survey. Results show "More effective instruction occurs in schools where teachers are oriented toward innovation, where teachers engage in reflective discussions about their practice, and where principals demonstrate leadership in instruction."
