Stuart Luppescu 1995¶

Stuart Luppescu, Anthony S. Bryk, Paul Deabster, John Q. Easton, and Yeow Meng Thum. (1995). School Reform, Retention Policy, and Student Achievement Gains. University of Chicago draft report. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/school-reform-retention-policy-and-student-achievement-gains

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Validity check comparing mean propensity for retention of retained versus not-retained students, shown for 1988 in Figure 3 with very similar patterns in later years. The article also reports seventh-grade log-odds about 50% greater for retained students (5% vs 1%).

"In first grade, the log-odds of being retained on the propensity measure are more than three times greater for the students who were actually retained. This corresponds to a 23% probability of being retained for the students who were actually retained, compared to a probability of 2% for the students who were not retained."
