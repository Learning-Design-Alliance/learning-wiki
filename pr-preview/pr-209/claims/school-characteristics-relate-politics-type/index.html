Descriptive analysis of school size and politics type. The report states "adversarial politics is considerably less likely in schools with fewer than 350 students" and that mixed politics and strong democracy occur slightly more often in small schools, implying small size minimizes sustained conflict.

"Not surprisingly, adversarial politics is considerably less likely in schools with fewer than 350 students. Mixed politics and strong democracy also occur slightly more often in small schools."
