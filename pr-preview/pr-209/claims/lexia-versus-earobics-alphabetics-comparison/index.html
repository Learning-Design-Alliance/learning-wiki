q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

Randomized comparison in Gale (2006) of Lexia Early Reading versus Earobics Step 1 among at-risk kindergarten and first-grade students (analysis sample of 39 kindergarten and 37 first-grade students). The WWC found the quoted negative effects not statistically significant but substantively important; the study average effect size for this comparison was –0.39.

"The WWC found that this effect was not statistically significant, however, the WWC determined that three of the negative effects were large enough to be considered substantively important: Initial Sounds Fluency (kindergarten), Phoneme Segmentation Fluency (grade 1), and Nonsense Words Fluency (grade 1)."
