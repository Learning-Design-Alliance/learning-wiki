Slepkov 2013¶

Slepkov, A. D. (2013). Integrated Testlets and the Immediate Feedback Assessment Technique. https://arxiv.org/abs/1308.4365

q2 · i? · the article prints no effect size for this finding · design · r2

Reliability analysis of the two case-study examinations, whose unadjusted Cronbach's alpha values were 0.71 (midterm) and 0.82 (final). The author reports "very reliable for classroom exams" after length adjustment to 50 items.

"When our exam reliabilities are adjusted to correspond to a test length of 50 items (see Reference 24 for formulas), our obtained values of α 50 = 0. 86 and 0.90 allow for comparisons with a wide group of other MC tests,20 and are found to be very reliable for classroom exams."
