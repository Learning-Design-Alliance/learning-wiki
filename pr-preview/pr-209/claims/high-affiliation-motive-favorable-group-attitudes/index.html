Attitude survey analysis by affiliation motive (measured with the Work Motivation Inventory affiliation scale) in the four-condition experiment; no effect size printed. Achievement did not differ by affiliation motive.

"When differences in attitude are examined by affiliation motive, participants with high affiliation motive had significantly higher agreement with six of the twelve attitude statements."

Discussion¶

One study is recorded (Brewer & Klein 2003). Affiliation motive was measured (Work Motivation Inventory) and used to block participants, not assigned, so this comparison is associational even though the interdependence conditions in the same experiment were randomised; it is coded accordingly. The outcome is agreement with attitude statements (six of twelve differed), not achievement, which did not differ by affiliation motive. This study's entry on this page does not describe the participants. The same study's entry on the achievement claim describes them as 280 undergraduate business majors, blocked by affiliation motive and randomly assigned to triads; an earlier title called them adult online learners, which no entry for this study says, so the scope now follows that entry.
