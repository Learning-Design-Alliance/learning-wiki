What Works Clearinghouse 2009¶

What Works Clearinghouse. (2009). WWC Intervention Report: Earobics®. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://www.earobics.com

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

WWC synthesis of four studies (two RCTs, two meeting standards with reservations) totaling 246 K–3 students. Appendix A3.1 prints the domain average effect size for alphabetics as 0.49 with an improvement index of +25, which the report summarizes as "The average improvement index for alphabetics is +25 percentile points across the four studies."
