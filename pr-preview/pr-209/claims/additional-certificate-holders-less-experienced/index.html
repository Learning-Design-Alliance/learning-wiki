Descriptive comparison of mean years of experience between the 9,865 additional certificate holders and non-holders in the 2015/16 experienced-teacher sample; the report flags differences of more than 5 years as substantive.

"Additional certificate holders had an average of 7 years of teaching experience in New York State compared with an average of 15 years for teachers who did not earn additional certificates, a substantive difference."
