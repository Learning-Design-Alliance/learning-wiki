Kate Place 2019¶

Kate Place, Philip Gleason. (2019). Do Charter Middle Schools Improve Students' College Outcomes? (Study Highlights). Washington, DC: U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/do-charter-middle-schools-improve-students-college-outcomes-study-highlights

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Lottery-based study of students who entered admission lotteries at 31 charter middle schools across the United States; the study reports that "Being admitted to a charter middle school in the study did not affect college enrollment." No effect size is printed in the highlights.
