Roschelle 2020¶

Roschelle, J., Cheng, B. H., Hodkowski, N., Haldar, L., & Neisler, J. (2020). Transfer for future learning of fractions within Cignition's microtutoring approach [Project Report]. Digital Promise. http://hdl.handle.net/20.500.12265/95

q1 · i? · design · r3

Qualitative analysis of a subset of five recordings focusing on students with higher pretests but different transfer results. Observations revealed transfer difficulty related to "multiplicative/additive strategies" connections.

"For "Fraction Division," initial observations revealed transfer was hard for students mostly due to the extent to which students learned to connect multiplicative/additive strategies to that of division. Students often used multiplication or repeated addition to solve problems."

Roschelle 2020 (2)¶

Roschelle, J., Cheng, B. H., Hodkowski, N., Haldar, L., & Neisler, J. (2020). Transfer for future learning of fractions within Cignition's microtutoring approach [Project Report]. Digital Promise. http://hdl.handle.net/20.500.12265/95

q1 · i? · design · r3

Qualitative analysis of the same five recordings. Transfer difficulty for Generalization was attributed to "the varied extent to which students learned and used variable notation."

"For "Fraction Generalization," transfer was hard given the varied extent to which students learned and used variable notation."
