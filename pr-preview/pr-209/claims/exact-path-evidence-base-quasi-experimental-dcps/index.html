Exact Path Growth: Evidence Rating 2024¶

Exact Path Growth: Evidence Rating. (2024). https://www.edmentum.com

q2 · i? · causal · r?

The evidence summary reports a single quasi-experimental study conducted during the 2023-24 academic year in Duval County Public Schools, Florida, evaluating grade 6 ELA and Mathematics achievement on the Florida Assessment of Student Thinking (FAST); no randomized assignment is described.

"The study included nearly 10,000 students (4,597 in the math analysis, 4,367 in the reading analysis), with a diverse demographic composition: 42% African American, 15% Hispanic, 33% White, 47% economically disadvantaged, 22% Exceptional Student Education (ESE), and 5% English Language Learners (ELL)."
