Wei He 2021¶

Wei He, NWEA Psychometric Solutions. (2021). An Investigation of Item Parameter Invariance Using Focused Calibration Samples for MAP Growth. NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/an-investigation-of-item-parameter-invariance-using-focused-calibration-samples-for-map-growth/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Study 1 recalibrated 2,716 MAP Growth items with on-grade samples using the standard MAP Growth Rasch calibration procedure and compared the estimates to the original all-grade estimates. The report states the average differences "are less than 0.1 logit (i.e., 1 RIT)" across subjects and grades.

"are less than 0.1 logit (i.e., 1 RIT). The average on-grade item response counts ranged between 393 for reading and 643 for math, which accounted for the 19%–27% of the responses used to derive the original item difficulties."

Related Claims¶

  • Average drift in MAP Growth item difficulty estimates is negligible across subjects, with original-new correlations above 0.99 — related
  • At least 90% of reading and science items and about 80% of math items show difficulty differences within 0.3 logits between on-grade and all-grade calibration — related
  • For items aligned to multiple target grades, difficulty estimates from different on-grade calibration samples differ negligibly, at most 0.1 logit on average — related
  • Item difficulty estimates from target-plus-adjacent-grade samples differ no more from original estimates than on-grade estimates do, with a largest average difference of 0.05 logit — related
  • Calibration sample size differences did not affect the size of on-grade versus all-grade item difficulty estimate differences — related
  • Item calibration results from Pychometrik and the existing tool are mostly comparable across 1,180 AGC-calibrated MAP Growth items, with average RIT differences ranging from 0.33 (mathematics) to 0.91 (science) — related
