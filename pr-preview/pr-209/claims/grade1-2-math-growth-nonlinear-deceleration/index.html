E4 Center, Northwestern University. https://e4.northwestern.edu/2024/06/25/new-normal-in-early-elementary-mathematics-learning-part-i-initial-findings-on-growth-and-domains-in-grade-1-through-grade-2/

Three-level hierarchical linear growth model with a quadratic term, fit by grade level to Diagnostic scores from 309,230 students in 6,348–6,350 schools; Table 1 prints a Grade 1 linear estimate of 1.27 and quadratic of -0.11, versus 1.02 and -0.06 in Grade 2, showing "student growth was nonlinear with slight deceleration".

"The results presented in Table 1 show that for both grade levels, student growth was nonlinear with slight deceleration, as found in previous research"
