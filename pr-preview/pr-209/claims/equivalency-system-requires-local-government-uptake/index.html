The Congressional Commission on Education's proposed equivalency system enabling learners to transfer from the non-formal to the formal education stream will not function unless local government units make a serious effort to take up the non-formal adult education program, because the new educational structure provides virtually no government provision for non-formal, non-vocational/technical adult education. → Doronila 1997

Doronila 1997¶

Doronila, Maria Luisa C. (1997). A Research and Development Approach to the Delivery of Comprehensive Functional Education and Literacy in the Philippines. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED418217

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

Analytical conclusion in the Analysis of Trends subsection of Part II. The author reasons from the structural observation that with the creation of TESDA and CHED, and the eventual abolition of DECS-BNFE, there will be virtually no government provision for non-formal, non-voc-tech adult education, making the equivalency pathway dependent on local government units.

"Therefore the proposed equivalency system will not work unless local government units make a serious effort to take up the non-formal adult education program."
