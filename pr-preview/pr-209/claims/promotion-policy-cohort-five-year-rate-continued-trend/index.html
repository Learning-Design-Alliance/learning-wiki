Cohort comparison across the 1991–1995 cohorts using the age-13-defined cohort method. The report prints the five-year rates for each cohort; no significance test or effect size is reported.

"The five-year dropout rate for the 1995 cohort is 37.5 percent, compared to 37.9 percent for the 1994 cohort, 38.6 percent for 1993, 38.9 percent for 1992, and 39.4 percent for 1991."
