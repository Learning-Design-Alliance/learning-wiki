Hossein Hashem Neghad 2014¶

Chi-square analysis of the frequency distribution of the three learning theories across the textbooks. Table 4 prints a Chi-square of 77.2 against a critical value of 9.48, so "significant differences exist" and the reflection of theories is not by chance.

"Table 4 indicates that Chi-Square is far larger than critical value. That is significant differences exist in Iranian ELT textbooks with regard to the implications of learning theories"
