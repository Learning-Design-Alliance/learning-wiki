The brief's introductory framing defines undermatch as a descriptive concept: students attend "a college that is less selective than what their academic achievement in high school would allow." No data or effect sizes are printed in the available text.

"Sometimes students attend a college that is less selective than what their academic achievement in high school would allow, a phenomenon known as undermatch."
