Brian Gill 2021¶

Brian Gill, Danielle DeLisle. (2021). Hybrid Instructional Approaches and COVID-19 Infection Trends in a Sample of Pennsylvania School Districts, Fall 2020. Mathematica. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/hybrid-instructional-approaches-and-covid-19-infection-trends-in-a-sample-of-pennsylvania-school

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Observational comparison of COVID-19 incidence trends for students and staff in seven Pennsylvania hybrid-instruction districts against trends in their local communities during fall 2020. The report states increases were "no greater than increases experienced by others in their communities outside of school."

"For the examined districts implementing hybrid instruction with precautions, increases in infections among students and staff were no greater than increases experienced by others in their communities outside of school, suggesting that the districts successfully mitigated in-school transmission risks."
