Eric Isenberg 2015¶

Eric Isenberg, Elias Walsh. (2015). Accounting for Co-Teaching: A Guide for Policymakers and Developers of Value-Added Models. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness, vol. 8, issue 1. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/journal-article-accounting-for-coteachng-a-guide-for-policymakers-and-developers-of-valueadded

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The article's description of the Full Roster Method (FRM), attributed to Hock and Isenberg (2012), states that the method is "feasible and practical" but that co-taught students "receive extra weight" because they get full weight with each of their teachers.

"it effectively counts co-taught students more than once—these students receive a full weight with each of their teachers, so such students receive extra weight when calculating the relationship between student characteristics and achievement"
