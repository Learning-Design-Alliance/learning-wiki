Uptake statistics reported from the Mama Aweza trial intervention arm: "94% responding to one text message or more" and a median of 20 messages (IQR 15–24) sent over the six-month enrollment period.

"Families in the intervention arm had high uptake of the intervention with 94% responding to one text message or more over the period of enrollment [8]. Caregivers in the intervention sent a median of 20 messages (interquartile range (IQR): 15–24) over the six-month enrollment period."
