ParentPowered READY4K! Evidence Summary 2016¶

ParentPowered READY4K! Evidence Summary. (2016). https://evidenceforessa.org/program/parentpowered-ready4k/

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Randomized controlled trial with the San Francisco Unified School District during the 2013-14 and 2015-16 school years; over 1,000 parents of preschool children, half randomly assigned to receive weekly texts. The treatment group "performed significantly better than the control group" on PALS with effect size = +0.11.

"Results from the RCT showed that children whose parents received READY4K! texts performed significantly better than the control group on the Phonological Awareness Literacy Screening (PALS) assessment (effect size = +0.11)"
