q2 · i? · associational · r1

Survey of education leaders and technology executives (over 300 respondents, 59 providers) conducted with the Center for Research and Reform in Education at Johns Hopkins University. The provider results show "only 4 percent of companies agree" that procurement meets contemporary needs.

"only 4 percent of companies agree that today's ed-tech procurement processes meet contemporary needs for product acquisitions, with nearly two-thirds of providers reporting that the processes directly influence their own product development."
