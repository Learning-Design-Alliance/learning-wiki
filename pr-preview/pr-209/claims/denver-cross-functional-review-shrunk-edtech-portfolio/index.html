Illustrative district case reported in the brief: Denver Public Schools created a rubric and cross-functional team reviewing AI-enabled edtech products for usage, compliance, technical compatibility, privacy, and evidence of learning impact. The brief reports the portfolio "reduced the district's edtech portfolio from over 1,000 tools to fewer than 350" with millions saved.

"This process reduced the district's edtech portfolio from over 1,000 tools to fewer than 350, saving millions of dollars while ensuring the remaining products had clear instructional purposes"
