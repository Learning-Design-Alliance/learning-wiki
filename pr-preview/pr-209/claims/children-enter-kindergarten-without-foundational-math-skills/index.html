The article states, citing Garcia & Weiss (2015), that many children enter kindergarten without the foundational mathematics skills needed to succeed; this is background motivation reported as cited literature, not a finding tested in this study.

"Despite research demonstrating the importance of mathematics achievement to children’s educational success and trajectories, many children enter kindergarten without the foundational mathematics skills needed to succeed (Garcia & Weiss, 2015)."
