q1 · i? · qualitative · r?

Qualitative finding from student listening sessions conducted by district staff with Regional Educational Laboratory support to explore barriers to flex-time use. Students reported they "avoided learning centers due to the perception that they were only for students who were struggling"; no quantitative effect size is reported.

"Many students, including those who were struggling, said they avoided learning centers due to the perception that they were only for students who were struggling. This stigma discouraged usage, even when support was needed."
