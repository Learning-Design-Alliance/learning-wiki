Read Well® 2010¶

Read Well®. (2010). WWC Intervention Report. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. http://store.cambiumlearning.com/resource.aspx?page=ProgramOverview&site=sw&parentId=019005451

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Study characteristics of the Frasco (2008) trial: 36 first-grade ELL students were randomly assigned (17 Read Well®, 19 comparison; two control attrition left 34 analyzed). The comparison group used Macmillan/McGraw-Hill 2003 with the same 90 minutes of daily instruction.

"For approximately three months, students received a minimum of 90 minutes of daily instruction in Read Well ® 1, which was utilized as their core reading program. The pacing depended on the level of mastery for each individual participant, allowing students to accelerate or slow down according to their grasp of the material."

Read Well® 2010 (2)¶

Read Well®. (2010). WWC Intervention Report. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. http://store.cambiumlearning.com/resource.aspx?page=ProgramOverview&site=sw&parentId=019005451

q3 · i? · causal · r2

The trial was conducted in a rural eastern Colorado elementary school where 61% of students were English language learners and 82% qualified for free and reduced-price lunch; three teachers and four instructional aides received professional development to implement the program.

"The study took place in a rural elementary school in eastern Colorado. English language learners constituted 61% of the school population. Eighty-two percent of the study body qualified for free and reduced-price lunch during the 2007–08 academic school year."
