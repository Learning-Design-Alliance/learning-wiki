Tims 1983¶

Tims, Albert R., Jr. (1983). Development of Public Affairs Media Use. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED238044

q2 · i? · no effect size could be confirmed in the full text · associational · r2

Conclusions from the two-wave survey's analyses of news specials viewing (e.g., 60 Minutes): consistency rose from .34 (ages 10-12) to .62 (ages 16-17), overall .48, and parent-child similarity was .21-.22 overall, substantial after age 12.

"While the frequency of exposure totelevision news specials was notfound to increase in a consistent orsubstantial rate, evidence ofsystematic increases in over-time stability andintergenerational similarity were evident."
