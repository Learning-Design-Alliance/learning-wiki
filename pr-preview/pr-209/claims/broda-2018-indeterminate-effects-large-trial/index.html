Growth Mindset Interventions 2022¶

Growth Mindset Interventions. (2022). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, NCEE 2022-006. https://whatworks.ed.gov

q4 · i? · causal · r?

RCT at a public university in Michigan with incoming first-year students blocked on race and ethnicity; the WWC calculated effect sizes and p-values, characterizing the study as having indeterminate effects "because the mean effects are not statistically significant" across academic achievement, college enrollment, and progressing in college.
