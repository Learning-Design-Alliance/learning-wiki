wj-null¶

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2023, August). World of Words. https://whatworks.ed.gov

q3 · i0 · causal · r?

Cluster randomized trial of 331 children across 12 Head Start preschool sites, assessed at the end of 24 weeks on the Woodcock-Johnson Picture Vocabulary Subtest (Forms A and B); the WWC determined the finding not statistically significant, with printed effect size 0.05 (below d = 0.2) and means "99.10 (13.81)" versus "98.40 (12.46)".

ppvt-null¶

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2023, August). World of Words. https://whatworks.ed.gov

q3 · i0 · causal · r?

Cluster randomized trial of 143 children across 5 state-funded preschool sites in an urban fringe area, assessed at the end of 12 weeks on the PPVT-IV; the finding was not statistically significant, with printed effect size 0.06 (below d = 0.2) and means "101.47 (14.18)" versus "99.96 (13.33)".
