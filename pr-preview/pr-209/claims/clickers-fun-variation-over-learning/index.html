Miller 2013¶

Miller, T. K. (2013). Pre-service Elementary Teachers' Perceptions of a Modified Peer Instruction Implementation of Clickers in their Mathematics Content Course. IUMPST: The Journal, Vol 3 (Technology). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1043050

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Table 4 reports Instructor A's students' perceived changes: Variation in lessons/fun 34 (54.0%), More thinking/better understanding 16 (25.4%), More notes/reinforcement/review 8 (12.7%), No change 4 (6.3%).

"The influence of clicker use upon learning was perceived as a distant second behind providing a fun variation to lessons."
