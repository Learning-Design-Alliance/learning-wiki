White 2026¶

White, L. P., Gaviria, G. A., & Frausto, A. (2026). Participatory evaluation: The capacities that shape engagement. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/314

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative analysis of structured reflections gathered from 10 researchers and 11 program designers within the Center for Learner Pathway Innovations, coded within the three dimensions of skills, knowledge, and dispositions. The report states these capacities "most often included skills related to data engagement" and related knowledge and dispositions.

"These capacities most often included skills related to data engagement, knowledge grounded in teaching, coaching, and community contexts, and dispositions such as openness to learning and an orientation to improvement."
