Jonah Deutsch 2020¶

Key finding from the Mathematica study of Louisiana public high schools, which used statistical modeling of statewide administrative data. The report states promotion power measures "substantially reduce or even eliminate the relationship between students’ poverty" and performance ratings. No effect size is printed.
