Weighted descriptive analysis of the mentoring survey reported in figure 1 and table C4 (n = 255–256 depending on item). The report adds that "44 percent of first-year teachers spending an hour or more a month in one-on-one meetings with their mentor, and 53 percent spending less than an hour."

"For example, 21 percent of teachers reported never having been observed by their mentors, and 25 percent reported having been observed for an hour or more a month."
