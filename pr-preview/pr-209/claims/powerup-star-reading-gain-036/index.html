Lexia Learning 2023¶

Lexia Learning. (2023). Lexia PowerUp Literacy — Evidence Rating: Promising. Program evidence summary page. https://www.lexialearning.com/powerup-literacy

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

A cluster-randomized study with 155 students in grades 6-8 across two Title 1 middle schools; ten classes reading below the 35th percentile were randomly assigned to PowerUp or business-as-usual. The page reports "PowerUp students performed significantly higher on the STAR Reading Assessment (effect size = +0.36)".
