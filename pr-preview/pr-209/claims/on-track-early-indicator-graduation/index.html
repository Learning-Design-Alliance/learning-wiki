Evidence¶

Shazia Rafiullah Miller 1999¶

Shazia Rafiullah Miller, Stuart Luppescu, Robert M. Gladden, John Q. Easton. (1999). How Do Barton Graduates Perform in CPS High Schools? Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/how-do-barton-graduates-perform-cps-high-schools

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Definitional statement in the report's Definitions section about the on-track measure used throughout the report: students with no more than one F in a core course and enough credits to advance on time. The correlation claim is the report's own, unaccompanied by a printed statistic.

"Whether or not students are on track is correlated with whether they will graduate, so it is an early indicator of students' academic success."

Shazia Rafiullah Miller 1999 (2)¶

Shazia Rafiullah Miller, Stuart Luppescu, Robert M. Gladden, John Q. Easton. (1999). How Do Barton Graduates Perform in CPS High Schools? Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/how-do-barton-graduates-perform-cps-high-schools

q2 · i? · associational · r1

A second definitional statement from the same section, defining off-track students as those with more than one core-course F or insufficient credits, and stating that being off track correlates with dropping out. No effect size is printed.

"Being off track correlates with dropping out."
