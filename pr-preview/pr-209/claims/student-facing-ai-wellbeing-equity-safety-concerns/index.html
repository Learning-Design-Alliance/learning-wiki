anchor-student-facing¶

Griffiths, R., Peters Hinton, V., Daal, S., Pattenhouse, M. (2026, July). K‑12 AI Infrastructure: Findings from educator and developer outreach. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/309

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Findings from educator interviews on student-facing AI concerns. A superintendent noted more affluent parents push back most on AI use while less privileged parents may be less aware of risks.

"Not surprisingly, educators raised wide ranging concerns about cognitive offloading, screen time, chatbot companionship, and social-emotional harm. A core worry is that AI could erode interpersonal relationships and students' cognitive effort and engagement."

anchor-teacher-facing¶

Griffiths, R., Peters Hinton, V., Daal, S., Pattenhouse, M. (2026, July). K‑12 AI Infrastructure: Findings from educator and developer outreach. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/309

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Interview finding in the student-facing AI concerns section. The executive director of a state CTO council said in lower grades the model should be highly teacher-centric rather than having young students interface directly with an open chatbot.

"A couple of educators expressed greater confidence in teacher-facing tools than in student-facing ones, favoring a strategy of limiting student-facing tools to high-leverage, clear value-add opportunities and focusing first on supporting teachers' use of AI."
