Angela Johnson 2020¶

Angela Johnson, Megan Kuhfeld, and Greg King. (2020). Catching up or Falling Behind: Measuring Middle School Achievement Trajectories for College Readiness. EdWorkingPaper No. 20-233, Annenberg Institute at Brown University. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/catching-up-or-falling-behind-measuring-middle-school-achievement-trajectories-for-college-readiness/

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Descriptive analysis of trajectory groups in a longitudinal sample of 363,685 middle school students assessed up to six times in math and reading. The quote reports that "the majority (73%) of students did not change status between 6th and 8th grade" in math.

"In math, the majority (73%) of students did not change status between 6th and 8th grade. Specifically, 54% of students were always off-track to be college ready in math throughout middle school while 19% of students were always on-track."
