q2 · i? · associational · r1

Survey analysis of CCSR 2005 principal survey data comparing LAUNCH, NLNS, comparable-experience, and veteran principals on a combined scale of data-driven decision making. Elementary LAUNCH mean was 2.05 versus 1.23 for comparable principals, significant at p <.05; the article notes self-reports of this socially desirable behavior should be scrutinized.

"Among elementary principals, LAUNCH principals reported significantly more data-driven decision making than comparable or veteran principals.8 No differences between groups were found at the high school level."
