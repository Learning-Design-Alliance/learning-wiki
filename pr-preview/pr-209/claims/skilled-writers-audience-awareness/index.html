Yu-Ling You; Shih-Guey Joe 2001¶

Yu-Ling You; Shih-Guey Joe. (2001). Investigating the Metacognitive Awareness and Strategies of English-Majored University Student Writers. ERIC Document ED465281. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED465281

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Interview analysis of the nine skilled EFL writers on audience awareness. Subjects asked "Can the readers understand me?" and noted a writer understanding the text does not mean readers can; the article states this knowledge helps writers determine whether their writing is coherent.

"All of the nine subjects in this study are found to possess this type of declarative knowledge."
