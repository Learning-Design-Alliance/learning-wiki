Newmann 2001¶

Newmann, F. M., Smith, B., Allensworth, E., & Bryk, A. S. (2001). School Instructional Program Coherence: Benefits and Challenges. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED

q1 · i? · associational · r2

Theoretical argument drawing on research on learning, cognition, and motivation to explain why instructional program coherence should promote achievement. The report argues that "students at all ages are more likely to learn when their ex- periences connect with and build upon one another." No empirical test of this mechanism is presented in the report.

"Research on learning and cognition indicates that stu- dents at all ages are more likely to learn when their ex- periences connect with and build upon one another."
