Evidence¶

Chapter One 2024¶

Chapter One. (2024). Chapter One — Evidence Rating: Strong. https://www.evidenceforlearning.org/chapter-one

q3 · i1 · causal · r?

Randomized study in a large southeastern school district during 2021-22, with kindergarten students stratified by classroom and randomly assigned to Chapter One tutoring or business-as-usual instruction. The sample was 72% Black, 21% Hispanic, 28% English-Language Learner, 11% Special Education, and 56% FRPL. Tutoring "significantly outperforming control students on the ORF (effect size = +0.23)".

"Results from year one of the study, assessing the intermediate impact of Chapter One tutoring on kindergarten students’ reading development, show Chapter One students significantly outperforming control students on the ORF (effect size = +0.23)"
