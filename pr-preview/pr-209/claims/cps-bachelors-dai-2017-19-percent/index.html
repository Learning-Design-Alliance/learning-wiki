District-level descriptive analysis using CPS administrative records and National Student Clearinghouse data, multiplying the most recent milestone rates. The brief states the "2017 Bachelor’s DAI is 19 percent, up from 11 percent in 2006."

"Thus, the 2017 Bachelor’s DAI is 19 percent, up from 11 percent in 2006 and from 18 percent in 2016. The increase over the past year was primarily due to the 3-percentage-point improvement in the four-year college enrollment rate"
