Duke 1979¶

Duke, Marshall P. and Nowicki, Stephen. (1979). A Social Learning Theory Conceptualization of Interpersonal Relationships. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED183975

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

This is a theoretical prediction derived from the article's social learning theory reinterpretation, resting on Rotter's assumption that in novel situations people base behavior on generalized expectancies, which give way to specific expectancies as situation-specific information is obtained.

"The equation would lead to the prediction that the use of a "favorite' opening and general interpersonal style like a generalized expectancy would be apparent very early in rela-tionship stages but as specific experiences in interacting and com-municating are amassed, individuals should show the potential, lit least, to vary their interpersonal styles of behavior."
