Allensworth 2026¶

Allensworth, E., Gordon, A. & Young, C. (2026). Combining early grade assessments to study literacy skills: Addressing the variability in tests taken across schools and students. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness, 19(1), 1-26. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/combining-early-grade-assessments-to-study-literacy-skills

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Post-pandemic cohort analysis in Chicago data. All individual assessments and latent scores showed correlations of 0.30 to 0.64 with third grade literacy grades and 0.47 to 0.81 with third grade IAR scores, except kindergarten TRC-Spanish.

"Post-pandemic, the correlations between K-2 literacy scores and third grade ELA outcomes are smaller than observed in cohorts of students that reached third grade before the pandemic, but still moderate- to large- in size."
