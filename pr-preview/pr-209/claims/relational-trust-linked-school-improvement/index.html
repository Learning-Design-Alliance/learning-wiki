Penny Bender Sebring and Anthony S. Bryk 2000¶

Penny Bender Sebring and Anthony S. Bryk. (2000). School Leadership and the Bottom Line in Chicago. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/school-leadership-and-bottom-line-chicago

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Case studies and survey analyses from the Consortium studies compare improving schools with flat or declining ones on trust outcomes; the improving schools' students "experience greater academic challenge" and feel safe and cared for.

"In improving schools, where trust and cooperative adult efforts are strong, students also report that they feel safe, sense that teachers care about them, and experience greater academic challenge."

Penny Bender Sebring and Anthony S. Bryk. (2000). School Leadership and the Bottom Line in Chicago. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/school-leadership-and-bottom-line-chicago

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Case-study evidence on principals' role in enabling LSCs reports that "they make sure their councils are well informed and prepared for the decisions they need to make," and that principals also seek council ideas and input for the School Improvement Plan.

"Like good chief executives, they make sure their councils are well informed and prepared for the decisions they need to make-for example, understanding the budget."
