q2 · i? · review · r?

WWC intervention rating for the completing school domain (Appendix A4). Criterion 1 for potentially positive effects was met by "two studies of Talent Search that demonstrated statistically significant positive effects"; the stronger positive-effects rating failed because no study met evidence standards for a strong design.

"It did not meet the criteria for positive effects because both Talent Search studies involved quasi-experimental designs."
