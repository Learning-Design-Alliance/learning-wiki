WWC extent-of-evidence categorization based on one study (Phillips et al., 1990) with 6 schools and 314 students in the general reading achievement domain. Appendix A5 lists 0 studies, 0 schools, and 0 students for alphabetics, fluency, and comprehension.

"The WWC considers the extent of evidence for Little Books to be small for general reading achievement. No studies that met WWC standards with or without reservations addressed alphabetics, fluency, or comprehension."
