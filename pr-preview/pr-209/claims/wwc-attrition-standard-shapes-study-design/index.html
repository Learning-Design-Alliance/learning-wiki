Authors' interpretive statement (no data reported): the WWC, Home Visiting Evidence of Effectiveness Review, and Teen Pregnancy Prevention Evidence Review have established attrition standards, making the standard "an important consideration for researchers conducting studies that could potentially be reviewed."

"Meeting the WWC attrition standard (or one of the attrition standards based on the WWC standard) is now an important consideration for researchers conducting studies that could potentially be reviewed by the WWC (or other evidence reviews)."
