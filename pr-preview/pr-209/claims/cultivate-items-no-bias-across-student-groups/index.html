Porter 2025¶

Porter, S., Shyjka, A., & Kling, K. (2025). Early insights from the revalidation of the Cultivate Survey: Learning conditions shape students' beliefs about being valued. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/early-insights-from-the-revalidation-of-the-cultivate-survey

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Revalidation item-functioning analyses of Cultivate responses in CPS. The article reports "no systematic bias in how the survey items functioned across student groups," meaning questions were not easier or harder to endorse for one group than another.

"analyses revealed no systematic bias in how the survey items functioned across student groups based on race/ethnicity, gender, economic disadvantage, learning differences, or English Learner status."
