NWEA Psychometrics and Analytics 2024¶

NWEA Psychometrics and Analytics. (2024). Predicting Amira Reading Mastery Based on NWEA MAP Reading Fluency Benchmark Assessment Scores. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/predicting-amira-reading-mastery-based-on-nwea-map-reading-fluency-benchmark-assessment-scores/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Authors' interpretation of the concordance results in Table 3.8, which lists ARM performance-level ranges and corresponding SWCPM ranges for each grade and term. The report attributes the grade 1 anomaly to the transitional nature of first-grade reading and differing fall/winter samples.

"Overall, the score ranges generally show an expected pattern of monotonic progression across the terms within grades, except for the first grade."
