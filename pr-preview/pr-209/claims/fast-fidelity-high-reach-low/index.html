Evidence¶

Bos 2018¶

Bos, J., Spier, E., Bandeira de Mello, V., González, R., & Huang, F. (2018). Investing in Innovation (i3) Validation Study of Families and Schools Together (FAST) Final Report. American Institutes for Research. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/families-and-schools-together-fast/

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Fidelity was measured with the FAST Program Integrity Checklist across 30 treatment schools, 12 components each measured twice per year. Overall fidelity was "97 percent" in Year 1 and "98 percent" in Year 2, with minimal difference between years.

"In Year 1, overall fidelity of the offered program services to the model was 97 percent. In Year 2, this rate was 98 percent."

Bos 2018 (2)¶

Bos, J., Spier, E., Bandeira de Mello, V., González, R., & Huang, F. (2018). Investing in Innovation (i3) Validation Study of Families and Schools Together (FAST) Final Report. American Institutes for Research. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/families-and-schools-together-fast/

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Program-reach data for Cohort 1 treatment schools showed 37 percent attended one or more sessions and 25 percent graduated; Cohort 2 figures were lower (31 percent, 24 percent, 16 percent). These were "far below the 60 percent participation rate and the 80 percent FAST completion rate anticipated."

"In the treatment group, for Cohort 1, 37 percent (n = 118) attended one or more FAST sessions, 30 percent (n = 91) attended three or more sessions, and 25 percent (n = 78) attended six or more sessions (graduated)."
