Evidence¶

Roschelle 2020¶

Roschelle, J., Cheng, B. H., Hodkowski, N., Neisler, J. & Haldar, L. (2020). Evaluation of an online tutoring program in elementary mathematics [Project Report]. San Mateo, CA: Digital Promise. http://hdl.handle.net/20.500.12265/94

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Randomized controlled trial with 144 fifth graders matched on pretest and assigned within pairs to tutoring-plus-game or game-only conditions; the t-test on aggregate gain scores found results that were "statistically significant (t = 4.0545, df = 132.66, p-value < .001)". Analysis was by an independent external team.

"We conducted a t-test on the aggregate gain scores, comparing conditions; the results were statistically significant (t = 4.0545, df = 132.66, p-value < .001)."
