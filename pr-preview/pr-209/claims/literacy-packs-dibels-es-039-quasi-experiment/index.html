Just Right Reader Take-Everywhere Literacy Packs™: Evidence Rating 2025¶

Just Right Reader Take-Everywhere Literacy Packs™: Evidence Rating. (2025). Program description with quasi-experimental evaluation. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/just-right-reader-take-everywhere-literacy-packs/

q2 · i1 · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental study in the 2024–2025 school year with 399 kindergarten students across six schools in a diverse Texas district; three schools implemented the Literacy Packs and three served as matched comparisons using other decodable texts. The article reports students who used the packs "scored significantly higher than comparison students on end-of-year DIBELS composite scores (effect size = +0.39)".
