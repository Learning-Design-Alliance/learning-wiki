Growth model run by Grade 1 fall placement level (on/above, one below, two below); Table 2 prints a Grade 3 quadratic estimate of 0.04 for the on-or-above group versus negative quadratic terms for the two below-grade groups.

"in Grade 3, the linear growth rate declined in all three placement groups, whereas the quadratic term of those who had placed on or above grade level in first grade became positive, meaning that their growth was accelerated in the second half of the year."
