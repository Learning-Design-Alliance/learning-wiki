Random effects from the three-level growth model (Table 1) show school-level linear-growth variance proportions of 29% (Grade 1) and 27% (Grade 2), described as substantial variation in both grades.

"There were substantial variations in the linear growth rate at the school level in both grade levels (29% and 27% in Grades 1 and 2, respectively)."
