Evidence¶

Claire Durwood 2010¶

Claire Durwood, Emily Krone, and Christopher Mazzeo. (2010). Are Two Algebra Classes Better Than One? The Effects of Double-Dose Instruction in Chicago. Consortium on Chicago School Research, University of Chicago. http://ccsr.uchicago.edu/content/page.php?cat=3&content_id=34

q3 · i? · causal · r2

The CCSR study found the policy's weakest outcomes among very low-skill students, many with identified learning disabilities, who moved from small homogeneous classes into larger double-period algebra courses without specific accommodations.

"Very low-skill students were more likely to fail algebra after the implementation of the double-dose algebra policy, and test score gains were lower for these students than for students closer to the policy cutoff."
