Steven Wise 2022¶

Steven Wise, Megan Kuhfeld, John Cronin. (2022). Assessment in the time of COVID-19: Understanding patterns of student disengagement during remote low-stakes testing. Educational Assessment, 27(2), 136-151. https://doi.org/10.1080/10627197.2022.2087621

q3 · i? · associational · r?

The study compared disengagement of students remotely administered an adaptive interim assessment in spring 2020 with in-school administration in fall 2019; the abstract reports that "disengagement gradually increased across grade level." No effect size is printed in the available text.

"Results showed that disengagement gradually increased across grade level."
