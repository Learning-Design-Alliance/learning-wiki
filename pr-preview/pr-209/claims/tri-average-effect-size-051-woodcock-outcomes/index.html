q3 · i2 · causal · r?

Program outcomes summary reporting two comparison studies with a total of 471 students. On the Woodcock measures, "the average effect size was +0.51," which the source states qualifies TRI for the Strong evidence category.

"Two studies compared students in TRI to those in control schools. On Woodcock Letter-Word Identification, Word Attack, and Passage Comprehension, the average effect size was +0.51."
