Saga Math (Evidence Summary) 2020¶

Saga Math (Evidence Summary). (2020). Evidence for ESSA program evidence summary. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/saga-math/

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Second RCT in 15 CPS high schools randomized 2,710 9th and 10th graders to Saga tutoring or routine services. The page reports the ITT effect "was significantly positive (effect size = +0.14)" and the TOT effect "was even greater (effect size = +.40)".

"The estimated ITT effect of being assigned to treatment on end of year math achievement tests was significantly positive (effect size = +0.14) and the estimated TOT effect (impact for those actually receiving tutoring) was even greater (effect size = +.40)"
