Evidence¶

Nagaoka 2004¶

Nagaoka, J., & Roderick, M. (2004). Ending Social Promotion: The Effects of Retention. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/ending-social-promotion-effects-retention

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental comparisons of third graders just below versus just above the promotional cutoff in 1998 and 1999, plus a selection-model estimate, converge: a small one-year boost with no substantial effects two years after the retention decision.

"In all three cases, we reach similar conclusions, that retention did not proffer any sustained academic benefits to third graders who were retained nor did it have any substantial negative effects."
