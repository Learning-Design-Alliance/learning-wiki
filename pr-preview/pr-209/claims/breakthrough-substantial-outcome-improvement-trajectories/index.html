Conceptual definition of the substantial-outcome-improvement factor in the field note. The article notes that "high-dosage tutoring has demonstrated large, consistent gains in student achievement, particularly for students who have previously struggled, shifting academic trajectories rather than producing incremental change," citing Nickow, Oreopoulos, and Quan's 2020 meta-analysis.

"Produces improvements that can change learning or life trajectories rather than incrementally improving them. These advances often generate progress on problems that have historically resisted improvement."
