Evidence¶

ParentPowered READY4K! Evidence Summary 2016¶

ParentPowered READY4K! Evidence Summary. (2016). https://evidenceforessa.org/program/parentpowered-ready4k/

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Follow-up extension of the study with kindergarten students comparing personalized and differentiated messaging to general literacy messages. The READY4K! group "performed higher on the Fountas and Pinnell Benchmark Assessment System (BAS)" with effect size = +0.09.

"Findings indicated that children in the READY4K! group performed higher on the Fountas and Pinnell Benchmark Assessment System (BAS) than those in the control group (effect size = +0.09)"

ParentPowered READY4K! Evidence Summary 2016 (2)¶

ParentPowered READY4K! Evidence Summary. (2016). https://evidenceforessa.org/program/parentpowered-ready4k/

q2 · i1 · causal · r2

Within the same kindergarten follow-up, "the largest literacy gains observed among students in the personalized text arm of the treatment" with effect size = +0.18, exceeding the overall +0.09.

"with the largest literacy gains observed among students in the personalized text arm of the treatment (effect size = +0.18)"
