External validation correlating pre-test scores with first-half CCM skill estimates and post-test scores with second-half estimates, across attributes A1–A4. "The correlations ranged from .018 to .495" — A4 pre-test correlation was nonsignificant (r = 0.018).

"The correlations ranged from .018 to .495, with most being significantly different from zero, but a few nonsignificantly different from zero, indicating minor positive correlations between the model-predicted skill mastery and the pre- and post-test results."
