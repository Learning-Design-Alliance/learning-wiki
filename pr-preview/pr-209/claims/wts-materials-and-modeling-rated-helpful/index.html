Teacher survey (N = 18) shows 79% rated the binder practice descriptions, 79% the templates, and 74% the curriculum-integration guidance as helpful or very helpful.

"At least three quarters of teachers found the professional learning materials helpful or very helpful."

Winter and spring interviews with purposively selected teachers and coaches produced coded themes that modeling of practices during coach institutes and group professional learning was very helpful; quotes are illustrative, not quantified.

"Teachers and coaches benefited from seeing the practices modeled."
