What Works Clearinghouse 2018¶

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2018). Adolescent Literacy intervention report: Achieve3000®. https://whatworks.ed.gov

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Two-year cluster RCT in Wake County, NC (22,583 students in the general literacy analysis); spring 2014 effect size was −0.04 (p < .05) and spring 2015 was 0.21 (p < .01), with the direction reversing between years.

"The authors also reported, and the WWC confirmed, a statistically significant negative effect of Achieve3000® for students in grades 2–5 on the LevelSet Lexile score in spring 2014."
