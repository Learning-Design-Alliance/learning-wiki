WWC Intervention Report: Positive Action 2007¶

WWC Intervention Report: Positive Action. (2007). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://www.positiveaction.net

q2 · i? · review · r?

The WWC's extent-of-evidence categorization (Appendix A6) lists 2 studies, 56 schools, and over 2,660 students for behavior and academic achievement, rated "Moderate to large," and 0 studies for knowledge, attitudes, and values. Of 12 studies reviewed, only two met standards (one with reservations); the other 10 did not meet evidence screens.

"No studies that met WWC evidence standards with or without reservations addressed knowledge, attitudes, and values."
