Evidence¶

Education Corps 2025¶

Education Corps. (2025). Education Corps program evaluation summary. https://www.evidenceforesight.org

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Subgroup analysis within the same randomized study of 867 K-3 students found boys' gains of +0.33 while "effects for girls were near zero". The authors attribute this pattern to girls benefiting more from standard Tier 1 classroom instruction, not to differences in tutoring experience.

"Boys showed particularly robust gains (effect size = +0.33), while effects for girls were near zero, a pattern the authors attribute to girls benefiting more from standard Tier 1 classroom instruction during the same period."
