Chen 2016¶

Chen, J. (2016). Demonized Learners in Sociocultural Theory. Advances in Language and Literary Studies, 7(3), 168-177. https://doi.org/10.7575/aiac.alls.v.7n.3p.168

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Analytical critique of Lave and Wenger's (1991) Community of Practice model as applied in SLA: the author argues legitimacy is extended rather than co-constructed by oldtimers, and that nonparticipation relegates learners to the fringes. Theoretical argument, no data.

"learners who may not, cannot, or chose not to progress towards the center of a CoP through nonparticipation are relegated to the fringes where their learner identities are invalidated and subject to negative interpretations."
