Seeskin 2018¶

Seeskin, A., Nagaoka, J., & Mahaffie, S. (2018). Hidden risk: Changes in GPA across the transition to high school. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/hidden-risk-changes-gpa-across-transition-high-school

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of the 2016-17 CPS freshman cohort by eighth-grade core GPA category (Figure 3). Students above 3.5 declined 0.53 points (3.83 to 3.30); students below 2.0 "saw their core grades fall about one-half a point" contrasted with a 0.12-point increase for the lowest group, who continued to earn mostly Ds and Fs.

"the students who earned eighth-grade core GPAs above 3.5 saw their core grades fall about one-half a point, from an A/A- to a B+"

Seeskin 2018 (2)¶

Seeskin, A., Nagaoka, J., & Mahaffie, S. (2018). Hidden risk: Changes in GPA across the transition to high school. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/hidden-risk-changes-gpa-across-transition-high-school

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of 2016-17 cohort members with both NWEA scores and grades (Figure 4). Students exceeding standards (N=3,796) declined 0.37 points; students meeting standards (N=6,380) declined 0.36; academic-warning students (N=663) declined 0.08. The quote states "the core GPAs of students exceeding NWEA eighth-grade reading proficiency standards fell from a A-/B+ in eighth grade to a B in ninth grade."
