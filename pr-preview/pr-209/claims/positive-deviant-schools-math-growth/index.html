School-level growth estimates (Figures 4 and 5, ranging .76–2.27 in Grade 1 and .56–1.74 in Grade 2) were compared against average-growth confidence bounds using two-SE intervals to identify positive deviants.

"The approach from prior research (Yamada et al., 2018) revealed 14% and 13 % of schools whose lower bound of the growth estimates were higher than the upper bound of the average growth in Grades 1 and 2, respectively."
