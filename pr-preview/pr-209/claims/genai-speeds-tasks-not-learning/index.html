Evidence¶

Judi Fusco 2023¶

Judi Fusco, Pati Ruiz, Jeremy Roschelle. (2023). Artificial Intelligence and the Future of Teaching and Learning. Digital Promise presentation. https://tech.ed.gov/ai/

q1 · i? · theoretical · r1

The presenters assert this principle in their 'Questions about Learning with GenAI' section, offering no empirical study; they ground it in the argument that learning requires processing, illustrated by a student's account of instant AI-generated thesis statements.

"GenAI can help people finish tasks more quickly, NOT learn faster. No AI can speed up what happens in the head."
