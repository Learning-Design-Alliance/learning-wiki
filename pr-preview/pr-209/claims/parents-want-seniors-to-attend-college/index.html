Senior survey item asking what seniors thought their parents wanted them to do after graduation; the report states "nearly 90 percent of seniors reported that their parents want them to go to college after they graduate from high school."

"Across all Chicago public high schools, nearly 90 percent of seniors reported that their parents want them to go to college after they graduate from high school. In fact, there are more seniors whose parents want them to continue with their education than there are seniors who are planning to continue their education in the fall."
