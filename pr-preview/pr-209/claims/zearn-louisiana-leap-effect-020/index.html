Evidence¶

Zearn Math evidence review 2024¶

Zearn Math evidence review. (2024). https://zearn.org

q2 · i2 · causal · r?

A matched comparison group study during the 2023-24 school year in three Louisiana school districts, Grades 4-8, comparing Zearn users with similar non-users on LEAP. The review reports Zearn users "scored significantly higher than demographically and academically similar non-Zearn users".

"Students using Zearn as a supplemental program with dedicated implementation support scored significantly higher than demographically and academically similar non-Zearn users on the state assessment, LEAP (effect size = +0.20)."
