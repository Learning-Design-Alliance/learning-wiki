Stoo Sepp 2025¶

Stoo Sepp. (2025). Towards More Transparency in Learning Analytics: Sharing Information with University Students Increases their Awareness of Data Collection Practices. Journal of Learning Analytics, 12(3), 34–46. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2025.8713

In the same experiment, aggregated acceptance means were 4.12 (control), 4.27 (text), and 4.27 (label) on a 1–5 scale; because ANOVA assumptions failed, the Kruskal-Wallis test was used and "revealed no significant differences between groups". H3 was not supported; equivalence was not tested.

"Given that the assumptions for an ANOVA were not satisfied, a non-parametric Kruskal-Wallis H test revealed no significant differences between groups (χ 2(2) = 2.28, p =.321)."
