q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Thematic analysis of focus group Activity 2 discussions (20 students) produced themes on predictive data, including contextual information limits and suggestions that forecasts cover the whole module. One student noted being in hospital yet performing well. The article reports "predictions not being able to capture the context of studying."

"students raised some concerns regarding predictions not being able to capture the context of studying and the potential impact of personal conditions on performance."
