q2 · i? · associational · r2

Equity analysis of survey and administrative data across 434 schools finds home-use digital divide disparities but small school-level disparities; the second stated exception is that "elementary schools with predominantly African-American enrollments show slightly lower levels of access to computers than other types of schools."

"However, differences in a school's availability and use are not strongly related to the racial composition or family income levels of students in the school . There are only two significant exceptions: First, teachers at selective admissions high schools assign more tasks that require their students use technology"
