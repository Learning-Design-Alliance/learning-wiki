Descriptive analysis of CPS administrative testing records for 2004 reported in the accountability chapter. The report states "About 85 percent of the CPS students enrolled in eleventh grade in 2004 took at least part of the PSAE," and 91 percent including alternative assessments, below the NCLB requirement.

"About 85 percent of the CPS students enrolled in eleventh grade in 2004 took at least part of the PSAE. Students taking alternative assessments are added to those taking the PSAE to determine compliance with NCLB. Adding the students who took the IMAGE or IAA would raise the percentage tested in 2004 to 91 percent. However, this is still below the 95 percent participation requirement for NCLB."
