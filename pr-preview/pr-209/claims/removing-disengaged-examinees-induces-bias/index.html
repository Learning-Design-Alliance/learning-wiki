Evidence¶

Megan Kuhfeld 2019¶

Megan Kuhfeld, James Soland. (2019). Using assessment metadata to quantify the impact of test disengagement on estimates of educational effectiveness. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness 13(1), 147-175. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/using-assessment-metadata-to-quantify-the-impact-of-test-disengagement-on-estimates-of-educational-effectiveness/

q3 · i? · associational · r?

The study's reported result on the sample-removal technique: the authors state that "removing disengaged examinees from the sample will likely induce bias in the estimates". No effect size is printed in the available text.

"Our results indicate that removing disengaged examinees from the sample will likely induce bias in the estimates, although as a whole accounting for disengagement had minimal impact on the metrics we examined."
