Quantitative analysis of student scores on the EXPLORE to PLAN tests using a 'meets expectations' metric, reported in the Early Student Outcomes section of the evaluation overview. The analysis found "no differences in test score growth" between IDS schools and the rest of the system; no effect size was printed.

"Exhibit 1 shows that, in general, no differences in test score growth were found between schools implementing IDS and the rest of the system; neither group met EXPLORE to PLAN expectations."
