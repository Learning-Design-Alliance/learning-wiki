Null structural path in the main WLSMV model (β=-0.055, n.s. per the hypothesis table). The article offers a "ceiling effect" account, noting self-efficacy responses "cluster at the high end of the 0–6 scale (M=4.81)"; equivalence was not tested.

"First, sustained exposure to workplace harassment did not significantly degrade teacher self-efficacy (H3). This attenuation may stem from a ceiling effect: self-efficacy responses cluster at the high end of the 0–6 scale (M=4.81), restricting the observable variance"
