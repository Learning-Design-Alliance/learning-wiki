Program review reports two qualifying studies of Experience Corps in New York City, Boston, and Port Arthur, Texas, finding "The average effect size was +0.13" and qualifying the program for the Strong category.

"Two qualifying studies, in New York City, Boston, and Port Arthur, Texas, evaluated Experience Corps. The average effect size was +0.13, with significant differences on ECLAS in one study and Woodcock Johnson Passage Comprehension in the other, qualifying Experience Corps for the “Strong” category."
