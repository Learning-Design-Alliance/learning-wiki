Catherine Corbin 2020¶

Catherine Corbin, Erik Ruzek, Jason Downer, Amy Lowenstein, Joshua Brown. (2020). Correlates of change in elementary students' perceptions of interactions with their teacher. Journal of Applied Developmental Psychology 69, 101144. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.appdev.2020.101144

In the study of a racially/ethnically diverse sample of third and fourth grade students, students "rated their teacher more positively from Time 1 to Time 2 when female". No effect size is printed in the available text.

"Results indicated that students rated their teacher more positively from Time 1 to Time 2 when female and in classrooms characterized by positive teacher-student interactions."
