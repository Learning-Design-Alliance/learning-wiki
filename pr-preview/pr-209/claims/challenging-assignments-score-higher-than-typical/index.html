Observational comparison within the three-year assignment collection, in which teachers submitted both typical daily assignments and assignments they believed best indicated high-level understanding. The report found challenging assignments "tended to score higher than typi- cal assignments."

"As expected, the assignments designated by teachers as challenging tended to score higher than typi- cal assignments."
