Medha Tare 2022¶

Medha Tare, Alison R. Shell, Jessica Jackson. (2022). Shifting Mindsets: Designing Lessons for Learner Variability. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/c1921acf-1964-4f4d-8232-1e8af6ad7367

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Task 2 survey of 163 K-12 educators who led peer professional development workshops with at least three colleagues. The article reports "explaining the idea of learner variability helped them to understand it better themselves"; no effect size printed.

"100 percent of participants reported that explaining the idea of learner variability helped them to understand it better themselves, supporting the idea that explaining ideas to others is a valuable learning approach."

Medha Tare 2022 (2)¶

Medha Tare, Alison R. Shell, Jessica Jackson. (2022). Shifting Mindsets: Designing Lessons for Learner Variability. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/c1921acf-1964-4f4d-8232-1e8af6ad7367

q2 · i? · design · r2

Task 2 post-workshop survey. The article reports "98 percent of educators said they could" make connections to existing practices, and separately that 100 percent would use the LVN again; no effect size printed.

"When asked if the educator and their colleagues were able to make connections between the LVN and existing practices at their school, 98 percent of educators said they could."
