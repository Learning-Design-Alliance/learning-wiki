Spending projections reported in the New Urgency section of the report, contrasted with the Department of Education's recommendation that schools allocate at least 30 percent of technology budgets to professional development. The figures are descriptive projections, not tested effects.

"In 1998-1999, schools project they will spend $5.65 per student on computer training of teachers. In comparison, schools expect to spend $88.19 on instructional hardware, software, and con- nectivity in 1998-1999."
