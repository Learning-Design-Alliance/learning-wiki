BookNook: Evidence Rating Strong 2023¶

BookNook: Evidence Rating Strong. (2023). https://www.booknook.org

q3 · i2 · causal · r1

Subgroup analysis within the same cluster-randomized Rocketship study: students who received the recommended dosage of 20 or more sessions showed a printed effect size of +0.26, markedly stronger than the overall assigned-sample estimate of ES = +0.05.

"For students receiving the recommended dosage (20 or more sessions), results were markedly stronger (effect size = +0.26)."
