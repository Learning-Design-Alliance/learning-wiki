de la Torre 2021¶

Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/English-learners-in-Chicago-Public-Schools-an-exploration-of-the-influence-of-pre-K

The report states ELs with identified disabilities "made progress, but at a slower pace, toward acquiring English skills" and most had lower attendance. The release adds they were more likely to miss school and to opt out of bilingual services, limiting access to services and academic progress.
