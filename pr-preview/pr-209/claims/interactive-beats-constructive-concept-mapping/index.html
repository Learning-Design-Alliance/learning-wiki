Chi 2018¶

Chi, M. T. H., Adams, J., Bogusch, E. B., Bruchok, C., Kang, S., Lancaster, M., Levy, R., Li, N., McEldoon, K. L., Stump, G. S., Wylie, R., Xu, D., & Yaghmourian, D. L. (2018). q2 · i? · design · r2

The article reports, citing prior ICAP papers, a study comparing concept-mapping alone versus concept-mapping with a peer, showing that "doing so collaboratively enhances learning more than doing it individually", supporting the I > C pairwise comparison. The study's design is not described beyond this summary.

"using the same task of concept mapping, a study that compared concept-mapping alone (that can be interpreted as typically a generatively/ Constructively engaging activity) and concept-mapping with a peer (a collaboratively/ Interactively engaging activity), shows that doing so collaboratively enhances learning more than doing it individually"

Discussion¶

The only entry is Chi et al.'s (2018) one-sentence summary of a prior study they cite; its design, sample, outcome and effect size are not given, so the Interactive-over-Constructive comparison rests here on a second-hand report. The cited primary study has not been identified or read for this page.
