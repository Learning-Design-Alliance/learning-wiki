Project CRISS® (CReating Independence through Student-owned Strategies) 2010¶

Project CRISS® (CReating Independence through Student-owned Strategies). (2010). WWC Intervention Report: Project CRISS®, Adolescent Literacy. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/388

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Randomized controlled trial assigning 89 schools in ten geographically diverse low-income districts to four curricula or control; the CRISS portion covered 1,155 CRISS students and 1,183 comparison students after nine months. Appendix A3 prints small negative-to-null effect sizes (GRADE –0.04, ETS science 0.02, ETS social studies –0.03, all ns). The report states the trial "did not find any statistically significant effects of Project CRISS®."

"James-Burdumy et al. (2009) did not find any statistically significant effects of Project CRISS® on the passage comprehension subtest of the Group Reading Assessment and Diagnostic Evaluation or either the science or social studies reading comprehension assessments."
