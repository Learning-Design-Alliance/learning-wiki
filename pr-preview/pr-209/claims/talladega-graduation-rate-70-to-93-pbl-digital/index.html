Case-study profile of Talladega County Schools (about 7,000 learners, 71 percent receiving free and reduced-priced meals) reported in the publication's opening section. The article reports descriptively that "a shade above 70 percent in 2008 now stands at 93 percent"; no test or effect size is printed.

"Achievement is up since the district transitioned to PBL . The graduation rate that was a shade above 70 percent in 2008 now stands at 93 percent . At Winterboro, that rate was 100 percent by the end of the 2018–19 school year ."
