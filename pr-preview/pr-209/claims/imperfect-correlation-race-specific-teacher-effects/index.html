Evidence¶

Delgado 2023¶

Delgado, W. (2023). Is the Best Teacher the Best for Everyone? Variation of Teacher Effectiveness by Student Race. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/is-the-best-teacher-the-best-for-everyone

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Value-added analysis of Chicago Public Schools data estimating race-specific teacher effects; the brief reports correlations "between 0.28 and 0.67" across subject and grade level, meaning teachers more effective for Black students were often, but not always, more effective for non-Black students.

"The correlation between teacher effectiveness for Black students and effectiveness for non-Black students was between 0.28 and 0.67 (on a scale where zero means no relationship and 1.0 means a perfect relationship), depending on the subject and grade level."
