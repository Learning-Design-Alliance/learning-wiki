Eric Isenberg 2009¶

Impacts of Comprehensive Teacher Induction: Results from the Second Year of a Randomized Controlled Study. Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/impacts-of-comprehensive-teacher-induction-results-from-the-second-year-of-a-randomized-controlled-study

q3 · i? · causal · r?

The report states the null second-year findings "was true whether induction programs were delivered as one-year or two-year programs", indicating program duration did not change the absence of measurable impacts. No effect size is printed on this page.
