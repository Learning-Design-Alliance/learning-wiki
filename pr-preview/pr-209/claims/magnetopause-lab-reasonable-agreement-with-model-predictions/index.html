Related Claims¶

  • Students find magnetopause crossings well in magnetic field data but have more difficulty with particle data, and some data sets contain no crossings at all — related
  • Students' empirical fits of simulated magnetopause locations show good internal agreement, though student estimates and simulation-calculated locations often disagree beyond uncertainties — related
  • Using a stair-step pattern of solar wind input conditions in the simulation portion of the lab led to much better student results than linearly varying conditions — related
