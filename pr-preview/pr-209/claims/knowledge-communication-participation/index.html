q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative finding from staff reflections across Collaborative Innovation projects. The report states "Knowledge alone, however, did not ensure meaningful participation"; partners could understand constituent engagement without moving from gathering input to shared decision-making.

"Knowledge became more inﬂuential when it was clearly communicated, built into programming, and applied through problem deﬁnition, interpretation, planning, design, and decision-making."
