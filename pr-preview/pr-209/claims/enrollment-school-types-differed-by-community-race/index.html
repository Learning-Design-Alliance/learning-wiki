Descriptive analysis in Chapter 1 (Table 4) of 2018–19 ninth-grade enrollment across five school types by majority race/ethnicity of community areas. The report also notes only two majority-Black and no majority-Latinx areas had at least a quarter of ninth-graders in selective enrollment schools.

"When we examine enrollment patterns by school type, we see that 22 out of 30 majority-Black community areas had at least 25 percent of ninth-graders enrolled into charter schools, as did 11 of 26 majority-Latinx community areas."
