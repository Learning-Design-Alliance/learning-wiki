Evidence¶

Barrow 2020¶

Barrow, L., Freire, S., & de la Torre, M. (2020). Trends in computer science education: Access, enrollment, and performance in CPS high schools. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED606832

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Analysis of student grades in CS courses compared to core courses across CPS high school cohorts. The report states that "in 2018, almost 70 percent of the grades earned in CS classes were As or Bs, and only 4 percent were failing grades."

"Across cohorts, average CS grades exceeded students' grade averages in core courses by about 0.2 grade points. These differences in average grades were similar across gender, race/ethnicity, and neighborhood SES groups."
