Evidence¶

Greene Nolan 2023¶

Greene Nolan, H. L., & Vang, M. C. (2023). Automated essay scoring in middle school writing: Understanding key predictors of students' growth and comparing artificial intelligence- and teacher-generated scores and feedback. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/187

q2 · i? · associational · r2

HLM models regressed the four student growth outcomes on 35 teachers' pre-implementation survey items (2,308 students); no significant coefficients emerged for experience, practices, preparedness, or enjoyment beliefs.

"Students' growth in writing was unrelated in our analyses to teachers' years of experience, to their reported instructional practices, to how prepared they felt for teaching writing, and to how much they thought their students enjoyed writing."

Greene Nolan 2023 (2)¶

Greene Nolan, H. L., & Vang, M. C. (2023). Automated essay scoring in middle school writing: Understanding key predictors of students' growth and comparing artificial intelligence- and teacher-generated scores and feedback. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/187

q2 · i? · associational · r2

The same survey-predictor models showed non-significant negative trends for frequency-based practice items; the article reports these as "not significant" with no effect sizes printed.

"Although not significant, our results showed growth trending negative when teachers reported engaging in more frequent writing opportunities, more frequent teaching of the writing process, more frequent feedback, and more frequent revisions."
