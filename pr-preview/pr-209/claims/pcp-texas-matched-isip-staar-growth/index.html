95 Phonics Core Program — Evidence Rating 2024¶

q2 · i1 · causal · r?

A matched study in Texas during 2023-2024 extended the evidence to upper elementary grades, including nearly 800 fourth and fifth grade students. "Students using 95 PCP showed significantly greater growth than their peers on both the ISIP and STAAR assessments (effect sizes = +0.15 and +0.10 respectively)", with the strongest gains in comprehension and fluency.
