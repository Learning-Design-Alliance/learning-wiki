Designing and Implementing the Next Generation of Teacher Evaluation Systems 2017¶

Designing and Implementing the Next Generation of Teacher Evaluation Systems. (2017). https://consortium.uchicago.edu

q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

Qualitative interview study of members of evaluation committees in five Illinois case study districts at different stages of planning and implementation. The brief reports that districts "dealt with challenges around cultivating buy-in and understanding" of the new systems. No effect sizes are reported.

"Based on qualitative interviews with members of each district’s evaluation committee, the brief provides a snapshot of how districts assessed and dealt with challenges around cultivating buy-in and understanding"
