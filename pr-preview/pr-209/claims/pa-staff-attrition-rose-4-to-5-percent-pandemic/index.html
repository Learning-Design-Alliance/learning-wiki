Stephen Lipscomb 2023¶

Stephen Lipscomb, Ijun Lai, Duncan Chaplin, Alma Vigil, Hena Matthias. (2023). Staff Attrition from Pennsylvania Public Schools During the COVID-19 Pandemic. Mathematica. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Statewide observational analysis of Pennsylvania public school staff attrition comparing the fall 2020 to fall 2021 pandemic year with preceding years. The brief reports "an increase from 4 percent attrition in preceding years" to five percent; no effect size or significance test is printed.

"Five percent of staff working in Pennsylvania local education agencies (LEAs) in fall 2020 left the statewide public school sector by fall 2021, an increase from 4 percent attrition in preceding years."
