Roschelle 2020¶

Roschelle, J., Cheng, B. H., Hodkowski, N., Neisler, J. & Haldar, L. (2020). Evaluation of an online tutoring program in elementary mathematics [Project Report]. San Mateo, CA: Digital Promise. http://hdl.handle.net/20.500.12265/94

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Design verification in the randomized controlled trial: after a matched-pair randomization on diagnostic pretest scores, "Pretest scores in the Treatment and Control Group were equivalent in all sites". Attrition was limited to 4 students (2 pairs), total 3%, with zero differential attrition. Scorers were blind to student identity, location, and condition.

"Pretest scores in the Treatment and Control Group were equivalent in all sites (Figure 1)."
