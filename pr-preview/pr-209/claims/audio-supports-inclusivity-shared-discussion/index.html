From the same three-teacher interview study of the redesigned ReadWorks platform; teachers described a student on the Autism Spectrum who could read about the same topics as classmates via audio.

"Ultimately, the teachers noted how the audio supports led to greater inclusivity because all of their students could come together to discuss the content that they may have accessed in different ways."
