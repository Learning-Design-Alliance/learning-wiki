Analysis of institutional research office data for the 41 fall 2019 pilot courses. For students of color, "we found an average course success rate of 61 percent for these 41 courses," and the average difference versus white and Asian students was 14 percent (Figure 1).

"Prior course success rates varied considerably across the courses identified for redesign, ranging from 39 percent to 90 percent with a weighted average of 66 percent."
