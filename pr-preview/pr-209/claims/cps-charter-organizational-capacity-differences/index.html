Evidence¶

Gwynne 2017¶

Gwynne, J.A., & Moore, P.T. (2017). Chicago's charter high schools: Organizational features, enrollment, school transfers, and student performance. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. http://cps.edu/NewSchools/Pages/RFP2017.aspx

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of charter annual-report policy data and teacher/student surveys in the Executive Summary, finding charter schools had "more requirements for grade-level promotion" with comparable instructional days, plus survey-reported innovative strategies and college-readiness emphasis.

"Typically, charter schools had more requirements for grade-level promotion and high school graduation, although most had a comparable number of instructional days as non-charter schools."
