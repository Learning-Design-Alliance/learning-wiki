What Works Clearinghouse 2023¶

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2023). Good Behavior Game. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/728

q4 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r2

WWC weighted-average synthesis of 10 studies of Good Behavior Game, based on 3,901 students in grades K-11; the summary row reports a statistically significant pooled effect size of 0.91 (improvement index +32, p<0.01), described as "positive effects 0.91 +32 Yes (p<0.01)". The report states this constitutes strong evidence (Tier 1).
