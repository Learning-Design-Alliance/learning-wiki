Brandhorst 1976¶

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical argument in the individualized-learning section, applying Harvey's developmental stages: stage one individuals may need social- and interdependent-orientation training instead; stage three individuals would need conceptualization of leadership under the interdependent orientation and evaluation of self and group situations. No empirical data are reported.

"Thus stage one individuals (most concrete) probably have no need for training in leadership or followership under the power orientation."
