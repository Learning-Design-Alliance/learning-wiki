"The average number of patients assessed in detail in Period I was 228.8 per year (SD= 26.04), in Period II was 246.0 per year (SD= 23.78) and in Period III was 227.17 per year (SD=27.04). The difference in the number of patients seen in these three time periods was not statistically significanty."
