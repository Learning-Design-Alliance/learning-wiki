Bridge to Reading evidence summary 2025¶

Bridge to Reading evidence summary. (2025). https://www.heggerty.com

q3 · i1 · causal · r?

Separate fall-to-spring analysis of 520 kindergarten students on the MAP Reading Fluency assessment. Effect sizes of +0.22 in both Phonological Awareness and Phonics and Word Recognition; the article reports this as greater growth for program students.

"In a separate analysis of 520 kindergarten students assessed from fall to spring, students using Bridge to Reading demonstrated greater growth on the MAP Reading Fluency assessment in Phonological Awareness and Phonics and Word Recognition (effect size = +0.22 for both)."
