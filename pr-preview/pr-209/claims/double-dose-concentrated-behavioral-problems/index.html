Takako Nomi 2010¶

Takako Nomi, Elaine M. Allensworth. (2010). The Effects of Tracking with Supports on Instructional Climate and Student Outcomes in High School Algebra. Consortium on Chicago School Research, University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/effects-tracking-supports-instructional-climate-and-student-outcomes-high-school

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Cross-cohort discontinuity analysis of classroom disciplinary and absentee measures built from student surveys corroborated with CPS discipline records. Declines in peer ability levels explained these post-policy increases, consistent with prior tracking studies.

"Peers in double-dose algebra were more likely to be absent from school by one day (-.38+1.37) and have disciplinary problem by 0.07 SD (-0.05+0.12) than classmates in pre-policy algebra classes."
