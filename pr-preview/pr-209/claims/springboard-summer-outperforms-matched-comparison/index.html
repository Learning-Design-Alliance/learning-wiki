Evidence¶

Piccinino 2020¶

Piccinino, K., Pepper, S. K., Salomon, H., Greenfield, S., McClanahan, W., & Robertson-Kraft, C. (2020). Springboard Summer Reading Program Evaluation Report. https://www.cde.state.co.us/cdelib/summerslide

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental propensity score matching with replacement on seven variables (with exact matches on testing in Spanish and district), followed by weighted t-tests. The report states "The study demonstrates a statistically significant positive effect of Springboard Summer on reading levels."

"Springboard Summer scholars show larger improvements in reading scores, on average, when compared to similar students who did not participate. The quality of evidence from this study satisfies the Every Student Succeeds Act (ESSA) Tier 2 standards."
