John Duplice. (2025). Generation and L2 Vocabulary Learning: A Classroom Action Study on the Efficacy of the Generation Desirable Difficulty in Learning L2 Vocabulary. Vocabulary Learning and Instruction, 14(1). https://doi.org/10.29140/vli.v14n1.102482

Post hoc GAMLj pairwise analyses from the same 23-participant study, reported alongside Figure 3. No test point showed a significant generation-versus-baseline difference; equivalence was not tested.

"Post Hoc pairwise analyses with Bonferroni correction for multiple comparisons showed there were no significant effects of treatment at each test point: pre-test (z = 0.44, p = 1.00), post-test 1 (z = –1.85, p = .972), or post-test 2 (z = –1.03, p = 1.00)"
