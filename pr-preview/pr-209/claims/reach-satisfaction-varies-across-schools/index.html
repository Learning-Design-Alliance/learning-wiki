Evidence¶

Sartain 2020¶

Sartain, L., Zou, A., Gutiérrez, V., Shyjka, A., Hinton, E., Brown, E.R., & Easton, J.Q. (2020). Teacher Evaluation in CPS: Perceptions of REACH Implementation, Five Years In. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Districtwide survey findings on overall satisfaction with REACH after five years. The release reports "wide variation in teachers’ satisfaction with REACH across schools," which the researchers link to school culture and leadership support for feedback and coaching.

"About 60 percent of teachers and 70 percent of administrators were satisfied with the REACH evaluation process as a whole. However, there is wide variation in teachers’ satisfaction with REACH across schools."
