Olga Viberg 2022¶

Olga Viberg, Chantal Mutimukwe, Åke Grönlund. (2022). Privacy in LA Research: Understanding the Field to Improve the Practice. Journal of Learning Analytics, 9(3), 169–182. https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2022.7751

q2 · i? · review · r2

Categorization of the 59-article scoping review sample against the Smith et al. (2011) framework found "the definition of privacy as control was present in 15% of the reviewed articles," with the review also reporting 6% right, 11% state, 16% other, and no commodity definitions. Percentages exceed 100% because some articles used more than one definition.
