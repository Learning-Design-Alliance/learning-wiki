Null school-level finding from the Augustine et al. (2018) Pittsburgh RCT of 44 schools randomized to program or control over 2 years. The article reports "Results were not significant at the school level"; equivalence was not tested.

"Results were not significant at the school level but were significant for both average daily attendance (effect size = +0.07) and chronic absence (effect size = +0.05) at the student level"
