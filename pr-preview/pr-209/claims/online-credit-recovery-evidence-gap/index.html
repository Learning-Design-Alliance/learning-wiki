Online and Face-to-Face Algebra I Credit Recovery: A Randomized Trial in Chicago Public High Schools 2017¶

Online and Face-to-Face Algebra I Credit Recovery: A Randomized Trial in Chicago Public High Schools. (2017). Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness. https://www.tandfonline.com

q1 · i? · causal · r?

The article's stated rationale for the randomized trial: online credit recovery was widespread but "no rigorous evidence" existed on relative efficacy. This is the authors' characterization of the evidence base, not a tested finding.

"Online courses are now widely used for credit recovery, yet there is no rigorous evidence about the relative efficacy of online versus face-to-face credit recovery courses."
