Evidence¶

Rachelle Esterhazy 2025¶

Rachelle Esterhazy, Rogers Kaliisa, Daniel Sanchez, Malcolm Langford and Crina Damsa. (2025). Advancing Multimodal Collaboration Analytics: A Scoping Review. Journal of Learning Analytics 12(2). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2025.8625

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Schneider and Pea (2015), a methodological study combining NLP with joint visual gaze analysis to predict productive collaborative learning markers during problem-solving. "linguistic coordination and coherence by which students build on each other's ideas is indicative of productive collaborative learning". No effect sizes are printed.

"The authors find that the linguistic coordination and coherence by which students build on each other's ideas is indicative of productive collaborative learning, as rated by an external observer. The predictive power of linguistic coherence is further strengthened by including data on joint visual attention."
