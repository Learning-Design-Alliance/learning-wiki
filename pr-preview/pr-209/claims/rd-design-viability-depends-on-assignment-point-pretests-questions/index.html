q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

The report's concluding assessment, offered as an authors' interpretation rather than a tested result: RD design viability "may be limited" and hinges on assignment point, pretest availability, and research questions. No empirical test of these conditions is reported in the available text.

"The viability of using RD designs for new impact evaluations of educational interventions may be limited and will depend on the point of treatment assignment, the availability of pretests, and key research questions."
