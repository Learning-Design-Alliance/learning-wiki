Alice Xu 2026¶

Alice Xu, Icy (Yunyi) Zhang, Adam B. Blake and James W. Stigler. (2026). Modelability as a Strategy for Improving the Generalizability and Scalability of Predictive Models. Journal of Learning Analytics, 13(1). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2026.9099

q2 · i? · design · r2

The article's reported result for its cross-institutional case study: models developed on CourseKata data from one institution were tested on another, and the article reports they "generalize effectively across diverse educational contexts". No effect size or performance statistic is printed in the available text.

"Results show that a system designed with modelability in mind can produce predictive models that generalize effectively across diverse educational contexts."

Alice Xu 2026 (2)¶

Alice Xu, Icy (Yunyi) Zhang, Adam B. Blake and James W. Stigler. (2026). Modelability as a Strategy for Improving the Generalizability and Scalability of Predictive Models. Journal of Learning Analytics, 13(1). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2026.9099

q2 · i? · design · r2

Case study design: models were developed on one introductory statistics course and evaluated on a second course using the same CourseKata textbook, differing in instructor, institution, and course length (ten-week vs. accelerated six-week session).

"We then evaluated the model's generalizability in a different course using the same textbook taught by another instructor, at a different institution, and over a different course length."
