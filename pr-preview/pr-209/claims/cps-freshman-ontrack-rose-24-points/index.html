Nagaoka 2017¶

Nagaoka, J., Seeskin, A., & Coca, V.M. (2017). The educational attainment of Chicago Public Schools students: 2016. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://toandthrough.uchicago.edu/data

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive trend analysis of CPS Freshman OnTrack rates (Figure 6; Class of 2019 N=18,878). The report states “of the Class of 2019, 88 percent were on-track,” attributing the rise to CPS paying more attention to freshman year course passing.

"Of the Class of 2006, only 64 percent of students were on-track at the end of their freshman year; of the Class of 2019, 88 percent were on-track, an increase of 24 percentage points."
