Shira Haderlein 2021¶

Shira Haderlein. (2021). How Do Parents Evaluate and Select Schools? Evidence From a Survey Experiment. American Educational Research Journal (online ahead of print). https://www.mathematica.org/publications/how-do-parents-evaluate-and-select-schools-evidence-from-a-survey-experiment

q2 · i? · causal · r2

A randomized survey experiment assessed parents' preferences for school qualities in a controlled environment; the reported result is that "achievement matters to parents but status matters more when evaluating quality." No effect size is printed in the available text.

"Results suggest that achievement matters to parents but status matters more when evaluating quality and growth matters more when choosing between schools."
