Evidence¶

Amira 2024¶

Amira. (2024). Amira — Evidence Rating. https://www.amiralearning.com

q2 · i1 · causal · r2

Matched comparison study in a large urban Texas district during the 2024–25 school year; 15,424 kindergarten and first grade students were "matched on baseline DIBELS scores and demographics". Both grade-level effects were statistically significant, with printed effect sizes of +0.26 and +0.06.

"Results showed statistically significant positive effects, with Amira students outperforming matched controls by an effect size of +0.26 in kindergarten and +0.06 in first grade on end-of-year DIBELS."
