Greenfield Learning's Comprehensive Literacy: Making the Connections 2025¶

Greenfield Learning's Comprehensive Literacy: Making the Connections. (2025). Evidence summary, Promising evidence rating. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/comprehensive-literacy-making-the-connections/

q2 · i1 · causal · r1

A quasi-experimental study in a suburban California district serving a high proportion of students from low-income backgrounds compared third graders taught by Greenfield-trained versus untrained teachers on the Spring 2025 CAASPP ELA assessment. The final analytic sample included 482 third graders across 15 elementary schools, and "effect size = +0.23" favoured trained teachers.

"Students taught by Greenfield-trained teachers scored higher on the state assessment than students taught by teachers not trained by Greenfield (effect size = +0.23)."
