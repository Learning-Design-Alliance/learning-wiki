The white paper reports, citing Reeder et al. (2015), a six-week study comparing an intelligent reading tutor used 20 minutes a day with human tutoring of "30 minutes or more a day," finding "time efficiencies over conventional human tutoring." No effect size is printed.

"results demonstrated that the group with the intelligent reading tutor offered time efficiencies over conventional human tutoring (Reeder et al., 2015)."
