Evidence¶

Anthony S. Bryk 1998¶

Anthony S. Bryk, Yeow Meng Thum, John Q. Easton, Stuart Luppescu. (1998). Mining Productivity: How to Read Your Productivity Profile. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Statistical analysis of Chicago elementary school productivity profiles reported in the School Improvement Summary section. The guide states that "averaging across at least a couple of adjacent grades provides a more stable estimate of school productivity."

"Our statistical analyses have identified negative relationships between profiles in adjacent grades. That is, improving productivity at one grade tends to be followed by some declines at the next, and the reverse is also true."
