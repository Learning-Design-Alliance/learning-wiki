WWC calculations from the Bettinger & Baker (2014) RCT, seven effectively randomized 50/50 lotteries with a 12-month follow-up. The intervention group was "66.4" percent retained versus 61.4% comparison, mean difference 5.0, effect size 0.13, improvement index +5, p < .01, based on 3,527 students.
