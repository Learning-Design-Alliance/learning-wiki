Evidence¶

Jennie Y. Jiang 2015¶

Jennie Y. Jiang, Susan E. Sporte, Stuart Luppescu. (2015). Analytic Memo: Evaluation Data from the First Year of REACH. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://www.cps.edu/Pages/reachstudents.aspx

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Correlational analysis of 2012-13 REACH data (Table 1), disattenuated for measurement error, printing an "adjusted correlation of 0.349" between observations and combined individual value-added, with math and reading value-added correlated at 0.28 and 0.29, similar to prior teacher-evaluation research. No standardized effect size beyond these correlations is printed.

"the association between observations and the combined individual value-added displays the strongest association, with an adjusted correlation of 0.349. Individual math and reading value-added are correlated at 0.28 and 0.29."

Jennie Y. Jiang 2015 (2)¶

Jennie Y. Jiang, Susan E. Sporte, Stuart Luppescu. (2015). Analytic Memo: Evaluation Data from the First Year of REACH. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://www.cps.edu/Pages/reachstudents.aspx

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Same correlational analysis (Table 1) printing weak correlations of 0.108 and 0.119: "Performance tasks and school-wide value-added, however, are only weakly associated with observation scores, with correlations of 0.108 and 0.119, respectively." Only unadjusted correlations were computed for performance tasks because reliability could not be calculated.

"Performance tasks and school-wide value-added, however, are only weakly associated with observation scores, with correlations of 0.108 and 0.119, respectively."
