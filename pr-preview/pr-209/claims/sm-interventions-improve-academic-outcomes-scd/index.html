Evidence¶

Smith 2022¶

Smith, T. E., Thompson, A. M., & Maynard, B. R. (2022). Self‐management interventions for reducing challenging behaviors among school‐age students: A systematic review. Campbell Systematic Reviews, 18, e1223. https://doi.org/10.1002/cl2.1223

q4 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Same single-case meta-analysis (105 academic effects among 456 total effects). The pooled log response ratio effect size for academic outcomes, including achievement and work completion, was "LRRi = 0.58, 95% CI [0.41, 0.76]"; no standardized effect size (d, g, r) is printed for this finding.

"Single‐case design results indicated that self‐management interventions significantly and positively impacted both student classroom behaviors (LRRi = 0.69, 95% confidence interval [CI] [0.59, 0.78]) and academic outcomes (LRRi = 0.58, 95% CI [0.41, 0.76])."
