WWC review combining the Elliott & Roder (2017) RCT and the Juniper et al. (2020) propensity-score-matched QED of Capital IDEA. Table 4 reports employment four quarters after completion (effect size 0.14, p = 0.07) and year-round employment year 3 (effect size -0.17, p = 0.21); outcome average -0.01.

"The WWC determined that two studies that meet WWC group design standards without reservations (Elliott & Roder, 2017; Juniper et al., 2020) showed evidence of an indeterminate effect of Project QUEST on short-term employment."
