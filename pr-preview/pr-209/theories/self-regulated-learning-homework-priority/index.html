Self-regulated learning as the priority outcome of remote independent work¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The paper defines self-regulated learning as "the ability for a student to plan, monitor, reflect on, and adjust their own learning" and argues it should be the priority during pandemic schooling. Because schools cannot cover the same content, teachers should focus on helping students become better independent learners rather than cramming material, using homework as practice in time management, help-seeking, and learning what works in their own lives.
