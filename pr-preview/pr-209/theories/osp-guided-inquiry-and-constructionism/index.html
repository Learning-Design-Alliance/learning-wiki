OSP's two pedagogical approaches: guided inquiry and constructionism¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article states that the two main pedagogical approaches used in OSP are guided inquiry and constructionism. Guided inquiry covers investigating modeled phenomena (EJS) and video analysis (Tracker); constructionism covers learning by making new models (EJS) and video modeling (Tracker). This framework, presented in Figure 1 from the author's 2012 AAPT conference presentation, organises how the tools are used for learning.

  • scientific inquiry skills and learning physics by building and analyzing models

  • After a guided-inquiry lesson with the collision-carts simulation, students' self-reported knowledge of the physics increased and most rated the lesson enjoyable and valuable [+M]
  • Some students found the guided-inquiry worksheet activities insufficiently thought-provoking and the data exchange troublesome [-W]
