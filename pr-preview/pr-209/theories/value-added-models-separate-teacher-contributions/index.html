Value-added models as a method for separating teachers' contributions to student achievement from other factors¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), q1–q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Value-added models are statistical models used in Oklahoma's evaluation system to compute educator results. As the brief explains, "The results are calculated using value-added models, which separate teachers' contributions to student achievement from other factors." The approach underlies the results distributed to teachers and administrators in spring 2016, and the brief describes updates to these models for that release.
