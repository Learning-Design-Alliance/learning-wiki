Contextualised SWOT Model for School Units (CSM-SU)¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The CSM-SU is a theoretically informed framework that repositions SWOT analysis as a four-phase strategic management cycle for school units: Engage and Diagnose, Interpret and Prioritise, Develop and Implement, and Review, Learn, and Re-Cycle. Developed from an integrative review of 82 studies and refined through a two-round Delphi study, it treats SWOT as "a cyclical, evidence-informed, and participatory process" rather than a static planning matrix. The model embeds collaborative sense-making, stakeholder involvement, and trust-building as essential conditions, and is grounded in open-systems theory and distributed leadership scholarship.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Broad stakeholder participation including teachers, support staff, parents, and students in diagnosis and interpretation

Constraints¶

  • Validated only through Delphi consensus with a Cyprus-based expert panel; empirical testing in school settings remains advisable

Target Learners¶

  • School leaders and principals
  • Teachers and school staff

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • Strategic decision-making
  • Organisational learning and school improvement
