Balanced assessment system encompassing a wide range of methods beyond tests¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article defines a balanced assessment system as an organizing framework for school assessment practice. It notes that while tests are often the first thing that comes to mind when you hear the word assessment, a balanced assessment system encompasses a wide range of methods for evaluating a child's academic, social, and emotional abilities. The framework is used to situate the seven assessment practices the article recommends for supporting classroom learning.
