Digital Equity Framework: three divides of access, use, and design¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The Digital Equity Framework is described as "a set of principles and guidelines designed to ensure equitable access, opportunities, and outcomes in the realm of digital technology and education." It organizes digital inequity into three divides: the Digital Access Divide (connectivity, devices, digital content), the Digital Use Divide (creation, exploration, critical analysis versus passive use), and the Digital Design Divide (educator time and support to design learning experiences with digital tools). Competencies support the framework by equipping students, educators, coaches, IT professionals, and system leaders to implement equitable practices. The presentation applies each divide to generative AI adoption questions.
