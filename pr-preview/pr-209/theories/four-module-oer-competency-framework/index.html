Four-module competency framework for production, search, diffusion and mobilization of OER¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article organizes the competencies at stake into four modules: production of OER (creative thinking, digital literacy, multimedia management literacy), search/selection (information management, English and other languages, self-directed learning), diffusion/research (critical thinking, scientific thinking, teamwork, research-based learning, online social behavior literacy), and mobilization/appropriation (educational practice innovation, problem posing and solving, communication). The framework was "defined in function of the contents that were implemented in the MOOC that was designed as a tool to develop the abilities of production, search and selection, diffusion and appropriation of the open educational resources", following UNESCO (2011) guidelines.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Competencies broken down into main aspects to allow measurement via rubric indicators

Constraints¶

  • Defined for the specific MOOC seminar context and its four content modules

Target Learners¶

  • teachers in Mexico and other Latin American countries participating in open distance education

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • OER production, search and selection, diffusion, and mobilization/appropriation competencies

Claims¶

  • Mooc Oer Competencies Mostly Advanced Similar Across Modules [+M]
  • Doctoral-level teachers show the highest advanced-level percentage in each of the four OER competency modules [+W]
  • Three OER competencies depend on educational level, though the definition-of-elements association is weak and non-linear [~W]
  • Most teachers report precise identification of OER sources plus at least two diffusion improvements when implementing a diffusion strategy [+W]
