Mathematical learning progressions as a lens for evaluating teachers' curriculum adaptations¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The study uses mathematical learning progressions as the standard against which teachers' content adaptations to middle school math curricula are judged. Adaptations are classified as productive when they align with learning progressions, such as adapting "lessons to below-grade-level content," and nonproductive when they risk moving "students away from the intended learning goal." The framework organizes both the classification of adaptations and the motivations teachers report for adapting.
