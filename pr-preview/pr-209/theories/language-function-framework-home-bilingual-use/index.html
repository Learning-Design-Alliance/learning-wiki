Language function framework for describing bilingual children's communicative functions in home settings¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The study applies "a language function framework" to provide "a descriptive analysis of the communicative functions performed by these children." It organizes analysis of home language use around the functions children perform and how their proficiency level, the interlocutors, and their home settings may influence their language use. Data sources include English and heritage language proficiency assessments, interviews with children and their mothers, and multiple video recordings of home interactions.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Multiple data sources: proficiency assessments, child and mother interviews, and video recordings of home interactions

Constraints¶

  • Descriptive analysis of a small sample of 20 second-generation children ages 6-8 from Mexican and Korean immigrant families in the United States

Target Learners¶

  • second-generation immigrant children ages 6-8

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • heritage language maintenance and bilingual communicative development
