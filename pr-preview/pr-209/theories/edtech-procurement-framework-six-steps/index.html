Edtech Procurement Framework: a six-step process for education leaders to select, implement, evaluate, and sustain edtech¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 3 studies (3 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 3 report an effect size

Description¶

The framework is "A step-by-step process to help education leaders develop and improve infrastructure around edtech selection, implementation, evaluation, and procurement to drive student learning." It organizes procurement into six numbered steps: Analyze & Inventory, Discover & Decide, Design & Plan, Mobilize & Implement, Evaluate & Reflect, and Sustain & Scale. Each step specifies concrete leader actions, from cross-departmental data analysis through sharing pilot results with critical partners.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Leaders collaborate with educators and school leaders at the planning step and share results with critical partners at the sustainability step

Constraints¶

  • Framed for education leaders procuring edtech for schools; not a classroom-level instructional method

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 school and district leaders procuring edtech

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • Aligning edtech purchases with targeted teaching and learning goals

Claims¶

  • Developing outcomes-based contracts enabled districts to engage in collaborative, data-driven decision-making processes to select populations, outcomes, and pricing. [+W]

Related Theories¶

  • Five EdTech Quality Indicators framework for evaluating edtech products

Examples¶

  • Analyze & Inventory: use cross-departmental data analysis and an inventory of existing interventions before buying new edtech
  • Evaluate & Reflect then Sustain & Scale: analyze pilot data against targeted goals and share results with critical partners to plan expanded use
  • Discover & Decide: establish evidence-based selection criteria and instructional alignment in an RFP process to select edtech for priority learner populations
  • Mobilize & Implement: provide professional learning throughout the pilot and use real-time data dashboards to keep usage aligned with dosage requirements
  • Design & Plan: build a professional learning and measurement plan to monitor implementation fidelity and evaluate impact
