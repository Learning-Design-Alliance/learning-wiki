Jagged profiles concept of learner variability¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article adopts Todd Rose's concept of "jagged profiles," which the paper describes as recognizing "that each learner has distinctly different strengths and areas of growth or variability." This concept underpins the Learner Models in the LPS initiative: rather than treating a learner as uniformly high, low, or average, the profile approach expects an uneven pattern of factors across learners. The Models combine these profiles with content area factors to create a rich understanding of each learner for personalizing teaching and product design.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires identifying which factors research shows affect learning and vary among learners

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • Accurate positioning of each learner's strengths and growth areas to guide personalization
