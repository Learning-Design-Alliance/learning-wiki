Model-comparison approach for linear vs non-linear mental-effort relations (Models 0–4)

A reusable analytic procedure the article introduces: five competing two-level mixed-effects models (random participant intercepts) ordered by complexity — no relation, linear, quadratic, cubic, and more complex than cubic (mental effort as factor) — compared on marginal R2, AIC and BIC.
