Scanpath theory of gaze as read-out of a cognitive model

Scanpath theory holds that a scanpath is "a 'repetitive sequence of saccades and ﬁxations, idiosyncratic to a particular subject [person] and to a particular target pattern'" and that scanpaths reflect "the read-out of the internal representation of pictures, the so-called 'cognitive model'".
