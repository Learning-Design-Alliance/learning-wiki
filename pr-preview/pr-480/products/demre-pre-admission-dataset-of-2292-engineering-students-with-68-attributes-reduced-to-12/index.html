DEMRE pre-admission dataset of 2,292 engineering students with 68 attributes reduced to 12 features

A de-identified dataset of 2,292 engineering students admitted to the University of Tarapacá from 2018 to 2022, compiled from DEMRE-linked personal, academic, socioeconomic, geographic, grant, and entrance-exam attributes for studying dropout, failure, and passing outcomes.
