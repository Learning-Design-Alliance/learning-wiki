Claims

English recall posttest scores differed significantly across three groups (video games with English language concepts, games without them, and no games) after controlling for pretest scores; the English-concept games group scored highest, but no pairwise comparisons were reported

In a quasi-experiment with 90 male 2nd-grade high school students, an ANCOVA found a significant omnibus difference among the three groups in English recall posttest scores after controlling for pretest; the English-concept games group had the highest raw posttest mean, but no pairwise tests or adjusted means were reported.
