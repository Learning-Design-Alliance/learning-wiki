Guo et al. found engagement depends on video production style: short videos, talking heads on slides, Khan-style drawing more engaging than slides or coding, classroom recording weak online

A 2018 review reports, citing Guo, Kim and Rubin (2014), that engagement with instructional video depends on production style: short videos, talking heads on slides and Khan-style drawing engage more than slides, coding sessions or classroom recordings
