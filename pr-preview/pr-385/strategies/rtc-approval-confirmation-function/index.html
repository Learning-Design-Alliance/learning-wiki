Add an approval/confirmation function to RTC so learners choose whether to accept a collaborator's change¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends that RTC platforms add a mechanism governing shared edits: "it is suggested that RTC should provide one more confirmation button, which will allow the users to choose whether to accept their collaborator's change in the co-editing program." This addresses interruptions observed during multi-user programming, since users were forced to accept another collaborator's revision by default.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • The RTC platform currently applies collaborators' revisions to blocks or components by default, which the study inferred may interrupt work

Constraints¶

  • The recommendation is based on interviews and the study's observed interruption of the CS-major member of the heterogeneous group, not on a tested comparison

Target Learners¶

  • adult novices and CS majors co-editing block-based programs in RTC

Target Learning Goals¶

  • maintaining team function and reducing interruptions during collaborative programming

Related Strategies¶

  • Make partners' AI use visible through transparency mechanisms in early collaborative programming phases
