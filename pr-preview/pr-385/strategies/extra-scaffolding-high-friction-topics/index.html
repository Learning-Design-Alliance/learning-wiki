Detect high-friction topics and add extra scaffolding there rather than weakening the weekly gate¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A forward-looking design response from the run's reflection: keep the strict weekly gate but make the production harness identify topics where students struggle and require additional scaffolding, such as more figures, worked examples, and clearer proof steps, before those weeks reach students. The article states the design response is "not to abandon the gate but to make the harness detect high-friction topics and require extra scaffolding there."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A harness that can flag high-friction topics from student performance and feedback

Constraints¶

  • In this run the scaffolding was insufficient for later proof-heavy topics; the recommendation is for the next iteration, not a tested manipulation

Target Learners¶

  • upper-level undergraduates in proof-heavy courses

Target Learning Goals¶

  • understanding difficult formal proofs and reductions
