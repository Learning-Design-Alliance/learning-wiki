Correlate mathematical and scientific studies through manual activities as a plan for lesson concentration¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Mr. Arnold Schrag's congress report embodied a concentration plan in which, as the article prints it, "mathematical and scientific studies are correlated by means of manual activities." This is an implementable time-tabling approach for higher classes where concentration of lessons is desired, linking otherwise separate subjects through practical work. The article reports it as a plan, not as an evaluated intervention.
