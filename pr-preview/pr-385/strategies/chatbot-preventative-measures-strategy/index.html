Implement preventative measures: student awareness programs and regular professional development training for educators¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The review recommends that educational institutions implement preventative measures when integrating AI chatbots. These include creating awareness among students about digital inequality, reliability and accuracy of AI chatbots, and ethical considerations, plus regular professional development training for educators covering integration of diverse in-class activities, nurturing critical thinking, and best practices for integrating chatbots into teaching methods.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Institutional commitment to ongoing educator professional development training

Constraints¶

  • The review states AI cannot completely replicate human instructors' emotional support and mentorship

Target Learners¶

  • higher education students
  • educators

Target Learning Goals¶

  • responsible and effective use of AI chatbots in learning
  • critical thinking and fair performance evaluation

Related Strategies¶

  • Implement sustained educator development programs including needs assessment, coaching, communities of practice, and experiential learning
  • Institutions should respond to GenAI with clear governance frameworks and structured professional training programs for staff.
  • Train teachers in prompt engineering, evaluating AI-generated responses, ethical and privacy considerations, and integrating AI outputs into pedagogical decision-making
  • Develop clear institutional GenAI policies, communicate them, raise awareness, and promote digital literacy
  • Institutional integrity programs combining ethical AI-use guidelines, clear definitions of dishonesty, and risk-awareness education
  • Allocate ongoing professional development so teachers learn to evaluate and integrate AI systems appropriately
  • Prevent AI-assisted plagiarism through student education, signed declarations, draft submissions, clear guidelines, and close monitoring
