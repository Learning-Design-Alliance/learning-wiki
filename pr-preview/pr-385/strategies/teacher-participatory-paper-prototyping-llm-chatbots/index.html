Engage teachers in participatory design with low-fidelity paper prototypes to surface LLM chatbot design considerations¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The study's method is an implementable strategy: engage teachers as domain experts in participatory design of LLM-powered tools. The authors "engaged six secondary teachers in creating low-fidelity paper prototypes and discussing design considerations for LLM-powered chatbots". Low-fidelity prototyping keeps the focus on authority distribution, safety, and supervision rather than technical implementation, and the article states participatory design methodologies can surface practical solutions to abstract AI deployment challenges.
