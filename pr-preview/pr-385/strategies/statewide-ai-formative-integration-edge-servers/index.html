Integrate AI-formative tools state-wide and deploy offline-capable Edge AI servers¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article's forward-looking recommendation is directed at state education authorities: "The study recommends state-wide curriculum integration of AI-formative tools and the deployment of offline-capable Edge AI servers to ensure instructional continuity in resource-constrained environments." It pairs curriculum-level adoption of real-time AI formative assessment with infrastructure provisioning so that feedback tools keep working where connectivity is unreliable.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires curriculum-level adoption decisions and deployment of offline-capable Edge AI server infrastructure

Constraints¶

  • Motivated by resource-constrained environments where instructional continuity is at risk

Target Learners¶

  • STEM students in Kwara State tertiary institutions

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Technical skill acquisition supported by continuous real-time feedback

Related Strategies¶

  • Deploy quantized adapted small models offline with a teacher in the loop rather than as autonomous graders
