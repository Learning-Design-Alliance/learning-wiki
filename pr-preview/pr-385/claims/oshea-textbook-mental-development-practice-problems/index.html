New Publications 1921¶

New Publications. (1921). New Publications. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.jstor.org/journal/jeducpsych

q1 · i? · review · r?

A review of O'Shea's Mental Development and Education (1921), a textbook designed for teachers in service and those preparing to teach. The reviewer describes its two organizing questions, the first of which is quoted here, and its three-part structure of dynamic aspects, educational interpretations, and exercises.

"The book is written around two questions: 1. How does the individual normally respond at different periods in his development to the typical situations—physical, intellectual, esthetic and social— in which he is placed?"
