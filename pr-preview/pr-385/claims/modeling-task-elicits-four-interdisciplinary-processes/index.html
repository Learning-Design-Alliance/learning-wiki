Idit Adler · Saja Rabie 2026¶

Idit Adler · Saja Rabie. (2026). Towards interdisciplinarity: emerging patterns of discourses during collaborative multidisciplinary modeling. Instructional Science (2026) 54:36. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09785-4

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Multiple case study of three multidisciplinary group discussions (audio-recorded, 1h11min to 1h22min) analyzed with Gee's discourse analysis; the authors report all four Boix Mansilla processes were "revealed student engagement in the four cognitive processes of interdisciplinary learning" in every group.

"In all three groups, the analysis of students' discourse as they attempted to engage in collaborative sense-making and create a visual representation of the phenomenon (Renger et al., 2008) revealed student engagement in the four cognitive processes of interdisciplinary learning"
