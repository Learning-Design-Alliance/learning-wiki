Evidence¶

Rebecca Covarrubias 2024¶

Rebecca Covarrubias. (2024). On Being Accepted: Interrogating How University Cultural Scripts Shape Personal and Political Facets of Belonging. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09970-2

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Laiduc et al. (2021), an experiment in which first-generation students read about faculty who either shared or did not share their background; belonging to the university was higher in the shared-identity condition. No effect size is printed in the review.

"exposing first-generation students to successful faculty role models who share their first-generation identity increased their belonging to the university compared to those who read about faculty who did not share this background (Laiduc et al., 2021)."
