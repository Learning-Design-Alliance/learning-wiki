Evidence¶

Sapey-Triomphe 2023¶

Sapey-Triomphe, L.-A., Sanchez, G., Hénaff, M.-A., Sonié, S., Schmitz, C., & Mattout, J. (2023). Disentangling sensory precision and prior expectation of change in autism during tactile discrimination. npj Science of Learning, 8:54. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00207-5

q3 · i3 · causal · r1

In Exp. II's TOE blocks, "Both groups exhibited a TOE" with ITOE strongly above zero in NT (30 ± 15) and ASD (47 ± 16), both p < 0.0001; a parallel result held in Exp. III, indicating both groups learned and used the stimulus-range prior.

"Both groups exhibited a TOE (Fig. 4a, b), with an ITOE signiﬁcantly different from zero in NT (ITOE = 30 ± 15, t(16) = 8.5, p < 0.0001, d = 2.1, BF = 5.98 × 104) and ASD ( ITOE = 47 ± 16, t(15) = 12.1, p < 0.0001, d = 3.0"
