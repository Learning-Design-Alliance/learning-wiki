M. Danish Shakeel · Paul E. Peterson 2022¶

M. Danish Shakeel · Paul E. Peterson. (2022). A Half Century of Progress in US Student Achievement: Agency and Flynn Effects, Ethnic and SES Differences. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09657-y

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Agency Effects results compare the four survey agents. PISA estimates a median trend of –0.10sd in math at age 15 while NAEP estimates 0.27sd for 8th grade, "a difference of 0.37sd in m and 0.14sd in rd" despite similar student ages.

"The range of estimates is 0.37sd per decade in m and 0.14sd in rd (m: –0.10sd to 0.27sd, rd: –0.02sd to 0.12sd). (See Table 1) NAEP and TIMSS-PIRLS estimate the steepest upward trends."
