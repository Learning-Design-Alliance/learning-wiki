Irving King and Hugo Gold 1911¶

Irving King and Hugo Gold. (1911). A Tentative Standardization of Certain "Opposites Tests". University of Iowa. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=A+Tentative+Standardization+of+Certain+Opposites+Tests

Normative results from the same 100 subjects show the hardest words with printed accuracy percentages: deceitful 58, ignorant 58, exciting 56, down to unless 22, pride 21, and suave 20. The words are given in their order of difficulty.

"The following list of twenty-five words was selected from the four lists of hard opposites as being the most difficult. In each case the accuracy of response was less than 69%."
