Evidence¶

Nathan McJames 2023¶

Nathan McJames, Andrew Parnell, and Ann O'Shea. (2023). Little and Often: Causal Inference Machine Learning Demonstrates the Benefits of Homework for Improving Achievement in Mathematics and Science. https://timssandpirls.bc.edu/timss2019/international-results

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Secondary moderation analysis of the TIMSS 2019 Irish eighth-grade sample, plotted in Figure 4, examining parental education and home educational resources as moderators of homework treatment effects in mathematics and science. The article states this "lack of a clear trend here is evident in both subjects and at both frequencies".

"The results show no clear trend, indicating that regardless of the number of books a student has at home, or the level of education of their parents, all students are predicted to benefit by approximately the same amount from the homework they are assigned."
