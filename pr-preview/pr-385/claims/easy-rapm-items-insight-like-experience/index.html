Evidence¶

Adam Chuderski 2021¶

Adam Chuderski, Jan Jastrzębski, Bartłomiej Kroczek, Hanna Kucwaj, Michał Ociepka. (2021). Metacognitive experience on Raven’s matrices versus insight problems. Metacognition and Learning 16:15–35. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09236-6

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Comparison of correct-trial ratings across the 20 RAPM items against the four interleaved computerized insight problems (BF10 > 3.0 criterion), shown in Fig. 3c. Easy items matched or exceeded insight-level Certainty; difficult items fell below it.

"For Certainty, 8 difficult RAPM item ratings were reliably lower than for the rating for insight items, 8 primarily easy items surpassed or equated it, and 4 moderate items yielded an inconclusive difference."

Adam Chuderski 2021 (2)¶

Adam Chuderski, Jan Jastrzębski, Bartłomiej Kroczek, Hanna Kucwaj, Michał Ociepka. (2021). Metacognitive experience on Raven’s matrices versus insight problems. Metacognition and Learning 16:15–35. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09236-6

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Item-by-item comparisons of Suddenness ratings against the computerized insight problems (Fig. 3b). Moderate and difficult items were reliably less sudden than insight items; the easiest item exceeded the insight value.

"For Suddenness, as many as 11 moderate and difficult RAPM items yielded reliably lower ratings than did insight items, the 8 easy items yielded inconclusive comparisons, and the easiest item even surpassed the insight items value."

Related Claims¶

  • Mean solution time almost perfectly predicts item-level Suddenness and Certainty, and fast performance signals easy items while deliberation signals hard ones — related
  • Suddenness and Certainty ratings decrease as RAPM item difficulty increases, with Certainty showing a strong effect — a broader claim this one bears on
  • The four-dimensional subjective-experience ratings are validated against classic insight and analytic problems, with Intuition and Suddenness differentiating the problem categories — related
  • Across participants, RAPM scores correlate positively with Certainty but not with Intuition or Suddenness — related
  • Intuition ratings are unaffected by RAPM item difficulty, indicating stable strategy across items — related
