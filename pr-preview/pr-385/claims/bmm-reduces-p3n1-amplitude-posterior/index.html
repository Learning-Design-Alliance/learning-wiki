Huang 2026¶

Huang, Y., Guo, J.-H., Zheng, S., Yin, R., Wang, J. & Jiang, C.-L. (2026). Enhancing attention network and neural efficiency through brief mindfulness meditation. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00455-1

q3 · i? · causal · r1

ERP amplitude analysis of grand-average waveforms in the BMM group; N1 magnitude also decreased and "the composite peak-to-peak P3N1 amplitude (PP) decreased by ~1 μV in BMM sessions". Control participants showed a contrasting pattern with little net change.

"At the grand-average level, P3 peak amplitude decreased from 2.68 μV at pre-test to 2.19 μV at post-test (a ~ 0.48 μV reduction), as shown in Fig. 4D–F."
