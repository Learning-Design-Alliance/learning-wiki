Evidence¶

Nadja M. Gentner 2024¶

Nadja M. Gentner, Lisa Respondek, Tina Seufert. (2024). Effects of short- and long-term prompting in learning journals on strategy use, self-efficacy, and learning outcomes. Instructional Science (2024) 52:919–950. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09671-x

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Mixed ANOVA (condition short-term vs. long-term as between factor, t5–t8 as within factor) during unit 2, when only the long-term group still received prompts. "Learners’ self-reported cognitive strategy use decreased during unit two irrespective of the condition".

"No interaction of time × condition was found, F(3, 216) = 0.23, p = .87. Learners’ self-reported cognitive strategy use decreased during unit two irrespective of the condition"

Nadja M. Gentner 2024 (2)¶

Nadja M. Gentner, Lisa Respondek, Tina Seufert. (2024). Effects of short- and long-term prompting in learning journals on strategy use, self-efficacy, and learning outcomes. Instructional Science (2024) 52:919–950. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09671-x

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Mixed ANOVA on self-reported metacognitive strategy use across t5–t8 comparing the short-term and long-term prompting groups. "No time × condition interaction, F < 1" with a main effect of time, ηp² = 0.10, indicating decrease in both groups.

"There was a main effect of time, F(2.65,190.69) = 7.82, p < .001, ηp² = 0.10, but no main effect of condition, F < 1, and no time × condition interaction, F < 1."
