Evidence¶

Ansari 2018¶

Ansari, A. (2018). The persistence of preschool effects from early childhood through adolescence. Journal of Educational Psychology, 110(7). https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000255

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Difference-score analyses of matched ECLS-K samples compared regression slopes between waves to locate when convergence occurred. The article reports approximately 20% attenuation from fall to spring of kindergarten (p < .05) and roughly 20% more to spring of first grade (p < .10, marginal); no standardized effect sizes were printed.

"roughly 70% of the convergence that was documented by the end of eighth grade had already manifested during the two years after preschool."

Ansari 2018 (2)¶

Ansari, A. (2018). The persistence of preschool effects from early childhood through adolescence. Journal of Educational Psychology, 110(7). https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000255

q2 · i? · associational · r3

The same convergence analyses showed the preschool-related externalizing-behavior disadvantage shrinking by a little over 70% through fifth grade, while for social skills convergence largely occurred between first and third grade; no effect sizes were printed for these convergence estimates.

"the negative associations between preschool enrollment and children’s externalizing behavior shrunk by a little over 70% through the end of fifth grade"
