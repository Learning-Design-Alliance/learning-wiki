Evidence¶

Dilnot 2017¶

Dilnot, J., Hamilton, L., Maughan, B. and Snowling, M. (2017). Child and Environmental Risk Factors Predicting Readiness for Learning in Children at High-Risk of Dyslexia. Development & Psychopathology, 29(1). http://dx.doi.org/10.1017/S0954579416000134

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Secondary regression analysis replacing the composite child health index with its eight individual indicators. The article reports "hearing problems at T1 (5.5% of variance)" as the only unique predictor, and notes reports of hearing concerns were more frequent in all high-risk groups.

"When each of the child health risks is entered into the model in the place of the overall index, only one indicator accounts for unique variance in reading readiness: hearing problems at T1 (5.5% of variance)."
