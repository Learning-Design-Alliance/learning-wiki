Evidence¶

Walkington 2025¶

Walkington, C., Beauchamp, T., Ikram, F., Koçyiğit Gürbüz, M., Xia, F., Lee, M., Lan, A. (2025). Mathematics Teachers’ Interactions with a Multi-Agent System for Personalized Problem Generation. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12066. doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29770-9_5

q2 · i? · design · r2

Thematic analysis of teacher prompting moves in the PPB logs found that topic adjustment was the most common code under the authenticity theme. Teachers also made 16 prompts asking the LLM to adjust to fit their local geographical context and 17 moves asking to change names of people, often to the names of students in their class.

"In 48 of the 179 moves, the code indicated that the teacher adjusted the problem’s topic; e.g., “Add that the student loves Dr. Pepper but his parents won't buy for him.”"
