Poorthuis 2015¶

Poorthuis, A. M. G., Juvonen, J., Thomaes, S., Denissen, J. J. A., de Castro, B. O., & van Aken, M. A. G. (2015). Do grades shape students' school engagement? The psychological consequences of report card grades at the beginning of secondary school. Journal of Educational Psychology, 107(3), 842-854. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000002

q3 · i1 · associational · r2

Preliminary comparative analyses of report card records and PANAS ratings in the longitudinal sample; printed effects d = -0.34 for grades and d = 0.27 for negative affect, with no positive-affect difference (p = .30). "girls experienced less negative affect in response to their grades than boys."
