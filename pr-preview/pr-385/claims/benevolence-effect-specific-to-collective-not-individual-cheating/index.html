Evidence¶

Caroline Pulfrey 2018¶

Caroline Pulfrey, Kevin Durussel, and Fabrizio Butera. (2018). The Good Cheat: Benevolence and the Justification of Collective Cheating. Journal of Educational Psychology, Vol. 110, No. 6. http://dx.doi.org/10.1037/edu0000247

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Reanalysis regression models on the same Study 1 sample tested whether the benevolence effect was unique. The model with individual cheating as outcome "revealed no significant effects", and a model with universalism values showed no significant interaction with the competitive contrast either, supporting the specificity of the benevolence-collective cheating link.

"We also ran a third regression model with benevolence value adherence as independent variable and individual cheating as dependent variable. This revealed no significant effects"
