Hollingworth 1917¶

Hollingworth, H. L. (1917). Mentality Tests: A Symposium. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22Mentality+Tests%3A+A+Symposium%22+Hollingworth

q1 · i? · theoretical · r1

This is the opening assertion of Hollingworth's symposium commentary, an expert opinion offered without data. He states his belief that "it is still premature to attempt to settle upon final scales for age ranges" or universal scales, while encouraging continued work of this character.

"it is still premature to attempt to settle upon final scales for age ranges or upon "universal" scales"

Hollingworth 1917 (2)¶

Hollingworth, H. L. (1917). Mentality Tests: A Symposium. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22Mentality+Tests%3A+A+Symposium%22+Hollingworth

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

In the same commentary, Hollingworth gives his stated reason for the prematurity judgment: forcing a decision on scoring methods or test systems is miscalculated "particularly when the systems involved continue to show correlations of over 90 per cent." No correlation table or study is reported.

"as to test systems is miscalculated,, particularly when the systems involved continue to show correlations of over 90 per cent."
