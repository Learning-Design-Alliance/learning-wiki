Evidence¶

Peter A. Edelsbrunner 2025¶

Peter A. Edelsbrunner, Bianca A. Simonsmeier, Michael Schneider. (2025). The Reliability, But Not the Cronbach's Alpha, of Knowledge Tests Matters: Response to Zitzmann and Orona (2025). Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10023-5

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical argument responding to ZaO's recommendation of cutoff values for the confidence interval around alpha: the authors agree confidence intervals are generally useful and should be reported, but argue they do not help evaluate knowledge-test reliability.

"However, if alpha does not indicate test reliability for general conceptual reasons, then its confidence interval also does not indicate the reliability."
