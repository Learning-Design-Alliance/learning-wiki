q2 · i? · review · r3

Review of Goddard's examination of 1547 pupils in the Vineland, N. J., schools, in which pupils showed an almost normal curve of distribution about the at-age group, which Goddard took as evidence the tests are carefully graded age for age.

"He finds that only about half of the mentally "over age" and "under age" pupils show a corresponding advancement or retardation in their grading. Half of them are therefore said to be improperly graded."
