In Croft et al.'s institutionalization study as reviewed, children with two and three periods of institutionalization "did not differ in their non-verbal abilities," suggesting a threshold effect at two periods of exposure.

"However, children with two and three periods of institutionalization did not differ in their non-verbal abilities, indicating that a threshold effect may have been reached at two periods of exposure."
