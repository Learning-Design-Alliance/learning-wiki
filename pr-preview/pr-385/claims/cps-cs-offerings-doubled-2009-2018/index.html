Evidence¶

Barrow 2020¶

Barrow, L., Freire, S., & de la Torre, M. (2020). Trends in computer science education: Access, enrollment, and performance in CPS high schools. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED606832

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Longitudinal analysis of CPS high school course schedules from 2008-09 through 2017-18, counting schools with at least five students enrolled in a CS course. "Most of this increase occurred following the launch of the CS4All initiative in December 2013."

"In 2009, only 32 high schools (34 percent) offered at least one CS course, while by 2018, 72 schools (80 percent) offered at least one CS course. Most of this increase occurred following the launch of the CS4All initiative in December 2013."
