Evidence¶

Grace E. Bird 1917¶

Grace E. Bird. (1917). Pupils' Estimates of Teachers. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0072302

q2 · i3 · associational · r1

Correlational analysis of frequency percentages of teacher-quality votes across the three pupil groups, computed "By the Pearson product-moments formula". The article reports r = .80 (P.E. .05) for high school girls versus boys and r = .88 (P.E. .07) for normal school girls versus high school girls.

"By the Pearson product-moments formula, the correlation between the opinions of high school girls and high school boys is .80. Between high school girls and normal school girls is .88. The probable error for the first correlation is about .05, and for the second .07."
