Evidence¶

Janet Mannheimer Zydney 2025¶

Janet Mannheimer Zydney, Erin Rumpke, Gi Woong Choi, Lauren Angelone. (2025). Understanding interactions between scientists and elementary school students in a citizen science project. Instructional Science (2025) 53:949–971. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09710-1

q2 · i? · associational · r1

A homophily analysis categorized participants by post totals into high, medium, and low participation and computed an E-I index; the non-significant result (E-I = 0.25, p < 0.52) indicates more active students were not more likely to engage in scientific discussion. The E-I index is not a standardized effect size.

"The homophily analysis on the level of participation was not significant (E-I index = 0.25, p < 0.52)."
