Luis Marquez-Carpintero 2025¶

Luis Marquez-Carpintero, Alberto Lopez-Sellers, Miguel Cazorla. (2025). Simulating Students with Large Language Models: A Review of Architecture, Mechanisms, and Role Modelling in Education with Generative AI. https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.06078 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2511.06078

q1 · i? · review · r2

The review's own thematic synthesis comparing LLM-based simulated students with earlier rule-based and cognitive-model approaches. It reports the LLM advantage in "situational flexibility" while flagging persistent concerns; no effect sizes are printed.

"While LLMs surpass rule-based systems in nat- ural language generation and situational flexibility, ongoing concerns persist regarding algorithmic bias, evaluation reliability, and align- ment with educational objectives."
