Narrative review of international measurement approaches: the OECD notes ECEC has greater variation in quality measurement approaches than other educational contexts; the review observes that "national quality ratings align with child development is often shielded from public scrutiny", e.g. Australian NQS ratings are public but outcome-linked data are not.

"Also problematic, the extent to which these national quality ratings align with child development is often shielded from public scrutiny."

Related Claims¶

  • Prior meta-analyses report few or weak associations between ECEC quality indices and child progress, with process quality more predictive than structural quality — related
