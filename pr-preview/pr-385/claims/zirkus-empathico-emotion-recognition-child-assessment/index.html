Naumann 2023¶

Naumann, S., Bayer, M., Kirst, S., van der Meer, E. & Dziobek, I. (2023). A randomized controlled trial on the digital socio-emotional competence training Zirkus Empathico for preschoolers. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00169-8

ITT ANCOVA on child-assessed emotion recognition change scores from the pre-registered RCT (N = 74) covarying baseline scores. Child assessment "yielded signi ﬁcantly larger increases" (d = 0.57, p = 0.006), whereas parent ratings were not significant (d = 0.04, p = 0.15).
