RQ3 content analysis of the 66 interventions against Karlen et al.'s (2020) SRL professional competences framework (Table 4). The review concludes interventions "hardly addressed content related to SRL content knowledge and SRL assessment".

"self-reflection of professional practice was promoted in many interventions (42%), as was the use of technology (38%), in order to train SRL through online tools and environments"

Related Claims¶

  • Only 13 of 66 interventions promoted pre-service teachers' dual role in SRL, and only three covered all four subcategories of the dual role — related
  • SRL interventions for pre-service teachers unevenly cover SRL areas: cognition (n = 62) and metacognition (n = 55) dominate, while emotion (8 interventions) and context regulation (n = 6) are rare — related
