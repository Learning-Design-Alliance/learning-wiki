Susan E. Sporte and Joseph E. Kahne 2007¶

Susan E. Sporte and Joseph E. Kahne. (2007). Educating for Democracy: Lessons from Chicago. Consortium on Chicago School Research Research Brief. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/educating-democracy-lessons-chicago

q2 · i? · associational · r2

The longitudinal survey analysis examined neighborhood social capital, school sense of belonging, and parent discussion of current events. It found "students who witnessed concern for the community and current events in their home, school, or neighborhood were more likely to be committed to civic participation," including feeling people at school cared about them.
