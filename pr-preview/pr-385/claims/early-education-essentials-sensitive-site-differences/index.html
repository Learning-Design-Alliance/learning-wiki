Early Education Essentials: Testing Measurement Validity of an Organizational Conditions and Practices Tool for Early Childhood Education Settings 2018¶

Early Education Essentials: Testing Measurement Validity of an Organizational Conditions and Practices Tool for Early Childhood Education Settings. (2018). https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/early-education-essentials-validation-surveys-measuring-early-education-organizational

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Multilevel modeling of survey data from 81 ECE sites produced site-level essential scores; the overview reports the instrument "is sensitive to site-level differences." No effect sizes or test statistics are printed in the available text.

"Multilevel models took into account individual measurement error to create site-level essential scores and assessed relationships between programs’ essential scores and site-level teacher–child interactions scores and student attendance."
