Evidence¶

Markus H. Hefter 2026¶

Markus H. Hefter, Benjamin Paaßen, Kirsten Berthold. (2026). GenAI Knowledge, Epistemic Orientation, and Intellectual Values Predict Undergraduate Students’ Critical GenAI Use. AI Educ. 2026, 2, 27. https://doi.org/10.3390/aieduc2030027

q2 · i2 · associational · r2

Exploratory correlation analyses in the same correlational study (N = 67) using the 4-item NFC short scale (α = 0.54). The study found NFC “correlated positively with their critical use of genAI, r = 0.25” and negatively with perceived usefulness, r = −0.27.

"students’ perceived usefulness correlated negatively with their need for cognition, r = −0.27, p = 0.027, suggesting that individuals with a higher need for cognition perceive the genAI tools’ utility more skeptically. Finally, the students’ need for cognition correlated positively with their critical use of genAI, r = 0.25, p = 0.044."
