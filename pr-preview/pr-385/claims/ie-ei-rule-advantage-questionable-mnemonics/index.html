University freshman test results for the ie-ei word group; the author interprets the small advantage of rule-citers as partly due to mnemonic reliance rather than the rule, noting that "most of the collegians who cited a version of the ie-ei rule as consciously used relied upon the word "Alice"". No significance test is printed.
