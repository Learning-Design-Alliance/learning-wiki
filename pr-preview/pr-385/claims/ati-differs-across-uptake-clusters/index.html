q3 · i2 · associational · r2

Comparison of Affinity for Technology Interaction scores (German ATI scale) across clusters in the n=131 study; "significantly higher for text integrators" with d = 0.59. Text reproducers had the highest ATI score, measured descriptively.

"An independent samples t-test showed that ATI scores were significantly higher for text integrators (M = 3.88, SD 0.96) than for text reconstructors (M = 3.36, SD = 0.80) (t(119) = − 3.21, p = 0.002, 95% CI [− 0.83, − 0.20], d = 0.59)."
