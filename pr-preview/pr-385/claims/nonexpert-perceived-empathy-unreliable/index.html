Evidence¶

Li Siyan 2026¶

Li Siyan, Kai-Hui Liang, Shopnil Shahriar, Yilin Ye, Shiyoh Goetsu, Wei-Wei Du, Masahiro Yoshida, Tsunayuki Ohwa, Xuhai Xu, and Zhou Yu. (2026). Invisible Impact of Empathy on Behavioral Change: Isolating the Effect of Empathy in Long-term Physical Activity Coaching Chatbot Interactions. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26641 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.26641

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Exit interviews with nine participants in the longitudinal user study, focusing on overall experience and whether users could detect tone shifts between weekly chatbot versions. The article reports that "many participants did not notice the empathy differences between chatbot versions"; no effect size is printed.
