Evidence¶

Laninga-Wijnen 2018¶

Laninga-Wijnen, L., Ryan, A., Harakeh, Z., Shin, H., & Vollebergh, W. A. M. (2018). The Moderating Role of Popular Peers' Achievement Goals in 5th- and 6th-Graders' Achievement-Related Friendships: A Social Network Analysis. Journal of Educational Psychology, vol. 110, no. 2, pp. 289-307. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000210

q3 · i? · associational · r2

RSiena influence (average similarity) parameters estimated in multigroup analyses across classrooms grouped by mastery goal popularity norms, in the longitudinal study of 46 Grade 5 and 6 classrooms. The Educational Impact statement adds that in mastery-norm classrooms "achievement increases when adolescents have high-achieving friends but decreases when they have low-achieving friends."

"Furthermore, friendship influence on achievement took place in classrooms with high mastery goal popularity norms, but not in classrooms with low mastery goal popularity norms."
