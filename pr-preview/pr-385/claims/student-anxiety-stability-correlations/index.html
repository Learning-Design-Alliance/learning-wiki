McLean 2023¶

McLean, L., Janssen, J., Espinoza, P., Lindstrom Johnson, S., & Jimenez, M. (2023). Associations Between Teacher and Student Mathematics, Science, and Literacy Anxiety in Fourth Grade. Journal of Educational Psychology. Advance online publication. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000790

Preliminary bivariate correlation analysis (Table 2) of student anxiety measures. The strongest printed stability correlation is T1–T2 mathematics anxiety r = .64 (p < .01), which exceeds the |r| ≥ .37 threshold for a large correlation; ELA stability r = .41 and science stability r = .41.

Related Claims¶

  • Teachers' beginning-of-year anxiety for teaching mathematics, science, or ELA shows no direct significant association with students' end-of-year anxiety in that content area — related
  • Math achievement and math anxiety show reciprocal longitudinal relationships in primary school children: achievement at T1 predicts anxiety at T2 and anxiety at T1 predicts achievement at T2 — related
  • Teachers' years of experience correlate negatively with their mathematics and ELA anxiety but show no association with science anxiety — related
