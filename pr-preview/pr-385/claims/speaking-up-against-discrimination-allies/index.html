Theme 2 focus group subthemes on avoiding assumptions and providing support. Parents recounted staff who did “not do anything” about bullying; a parent perceived differential discipline of her son over cellphone use and requested clear school-wide rules.

"Participants also wished for individuals in more privileged positions to speak up against discrimination and to support them when dealing with cultural stressors."
