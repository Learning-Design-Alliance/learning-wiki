Harris-Thomas 2026¶

Harris-Thomas, B., Nicolai, K. D., Chen, X.-Y., & Areizaga Barbieri, C. (2026). Examining Black and Latine Students' Sense of Belonging within the Mathematics Classroom: A Narrative Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10181-0

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative review assertion citing prior reviews (Allen et al., 2021; Korpershoek et al., 2020). The review states "sense of belonging is robustly associated with psychological well-being" (Pittman & Richmond, 2007), academic motivation, and academic performance in several contexts. No effect sizes are printed.

"Numerous empirical studies have demonstrated the benefits of students feeling a sense of belonging in learning contexts (see Allen et al., 2021; Korpershoek et al., 2020, for reviews). Namely, sense of belonging is robustly associated with psychological well-being"
