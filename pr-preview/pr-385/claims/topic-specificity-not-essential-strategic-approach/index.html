Evidence¶

Sijmkens 2024¶

Sijmkens, E., De Cock, M., & De Laet, T. (2023). Scaffolding Students' Use of Metacognitive Activities Using Discipline- and Topic-Specific Reflective Prompts. Metacognition and Learning, 18(3), 811–843. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09363-w

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Hierarchical regression with strategy score as outcome (N=272). Modules 3 and 4 emerged as significant predictors; module 8 (angular momentum) did not. The 'time and study environment and effort regulation' prescore was also a significant predictor.

"In this case, completion of module 3 and 4 emerged as significant predictors for students' strategic approach (see Table 3 and Table 6 in Appendix C). Completion of the module on angular momentum (module 8), however, did not appear as a significant predictor in the models, confirming that the topic-specificity of the reflection modules is not very important for the development of students' strategic approach."
