Mutlu Cukurova 2025¶

Mutlu Cukurova, Wannapon Suraworachet, Qi Zhou, and Sahan Bulathwela. (2025). Towards Synergistic Teacher-AI Interactions with Generative Artificial Intelligence. https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.19580 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2511.19580

q2 · i? · review · r2

The chapter attributes this contextual factor to Vaccaro et al.'s meta-analysis, adding that the same study shows synergy is more likely accomplished if and only if humans exceed AI performance, not vice versa. No effect size is printed.

"synergy is more anticipated in simpler content generation tasks, whereas in complex and abstract decision-making tasks, synergy is hardly achieved."
