Fyfe Emily R. 2023¶

Fyfe Emily R., Borriello Giulia, Merrick Megan. (2023). A developmental perspective on feedback: How corrective feedback influences children's literacy, mathematics, and problem solving. Educational Psychologist. https://doi.org/10.1080/00461520.2022.2108426

q3 · i? · review · r3

Feature coding of the 60 unique feedback conditions identified feedback alerts provided before the child responded; they were "present in approximately half of all feedback conditions" and least frequent for ages 10 to 11.

"Feedback alerts were present in approximately half of all feedback conditions, and they were included in papers that spanned the full age range, but least frequently with the oldest group (ages 10 to 11)."
