Benard 1991¶

Benard, B. (1991). Fostering Resiliency in Kids: Protective Factors in the Family, School, and Community. Western Regional Center for Drug-Free Schools and Communities, Northwest Regional Educational Laboratory. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED335781

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing two meta-analyses (Tobler 1986; Bangert-Drowns 1988) comparing the effects of more than 200 studies, that "peer programs (in-cluding cooperative learning strategies) are the single most effective school-based approach for reducing 'alcohol and drug use in youth." No effect size is printed.
