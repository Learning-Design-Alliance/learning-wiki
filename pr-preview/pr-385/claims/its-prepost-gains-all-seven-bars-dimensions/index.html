Evidence¶

Hung-Yue Suen and Kuo-En Hung 2026¶

Hung-Yue Suen and Kuo-En Hung. (2026). An Interpretable Closed-Loop Intelligent Tutoring System for Multimodal Affective Feedback in Asynchronous Presentation Training. IEEE Transactions on Learning Technologies. https://doi.org/10.1109/TLT.2026.3693864

q3 · i2 · design · r2

Pre–post field study of 204 adult learners recruited from partner MOOC platforms who practiced with the system for 30 days; paired-samples t-tests (Table IV) show Cohen's d from 0.39 (Content) to 0.90 (Overall Rating), with "the latter showing more noticeable shifts" for delivery-related elements.

"As shown in Table IV, oral presentation performance was higher at posttest than at pretest across all assessed dimensions. Changes were observed in both structure-related aspects of presentation and delivery-related elements, with the latter showing more noticeable shifts."
