Evidence¶

Mattingly Jameson K MD 2018¶

Mattingly Jameson K MD, Castellanos Irina PhD, Moberly Aaron C MD. (2018). Nonverbal Reasoning as a Contributor to Sentence Recognition Outcomes in Adults with Cochlear Implants. Otology & Neurotology. https://doi.org/10.1097/MAO.0000000000001998

q3 · i2 · associational · r2

Correlational analysis in 43 age-matched normal-hearing controls who listened to 8-channel noise-vocoded sentences simulating the degraded CI signal (Table 3). Correlations ranged from r = .36 (PRESTO % words) to r = .57 (Harvard % words), described as "significantly correlated with sentence recognition."

"The overall number of items answered correctly on the 48-item RPM significantly correlated with sentence recognition in CI users (r = 0.35 – 0.47) and NH controls (r = 0.36 – 0.57)."
