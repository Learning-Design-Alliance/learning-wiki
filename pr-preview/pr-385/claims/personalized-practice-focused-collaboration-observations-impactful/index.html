Evidence¶

Shyjka 2022¶

Shyjka, A. (2022). Reaching for improvement: Teacher evaluation and its role in instructional improvement. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://www.cps.edu/about/departments/talent/

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Across 7 CPS schools, teachers reported that "Structures that facilitated personalized, practice-focused opportunities for teacher collaboration around classroom observations were particularly impactful to teachers' practice improvement efforts." Observations paired with debriefing increased perceived benefit.
