Lessons in Character 2006¶

Lessons in Character. (2006). WWC Intervention Report, What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/WWC/InterventionReport/270

q3 · i? · causal · r2

WWC-computed improvement indices across the two reviewed studies, representing the percentile-rank difference between the average intervention and comparison student. Indices favor the program in all three domains regardless of statistical significance.

"The average improvement index for academic achievement is +16 percentile points, with a range of +12 to +18 percentile points (Dietsch, Bayha, & Zheng, 2005)."
