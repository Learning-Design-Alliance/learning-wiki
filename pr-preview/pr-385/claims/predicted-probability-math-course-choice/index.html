Evidence¶

Jansen 2020¶

Jansen, M., Becker, M., & Neumann, M. (2020). Dimensional Comparison Effects on (Gendered) Educational Choices. The Journal of Educational Psychology, 113(2), 330-350. https://nbn-resolving.org/urn:nbn:de:0111-pedocs-220459

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Illustrative analysis of model-predicted probabilities over predictor values (Figure 2), with other predictors set to zero (means or reference groups). The article reports the 16% increase for mathematics self-concept and, contrarily, a 6% decrease in probability of selecting advanced mathematics for a 1-SD increase in German self-concept.

"for an increase in mathematics self-concept from the mean to one standard deviation above the mean (+1 SD), the predicted probability of selecting an advanced mathematics course increased by 16%"
