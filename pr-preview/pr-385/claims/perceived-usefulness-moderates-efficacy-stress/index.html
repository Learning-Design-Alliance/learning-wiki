Evidence¶

Liu L 2026¶

Liu L, Zhuang M and Wang J (2026). Are students dependent on AI in writing courses? Analyzing factors influencing dependence on generative AI through the I-PACE model. Front. Psychol. 17:1905037. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1905037

q2 · i? · associational · r?

SPSS moderation regression with standardized ASE, AS, and PU from the survey sample tested the ASE×PU interaction; the article reports the interaction coefficient as −0.1732 (p = 0.0317) and PU's main effect as 0.2335 (p = 0.0184), a "signiﬁcant moderating eﬀect". No standardized effect size is printed for this finding.

"the regression coeﬃcient of the interaction term ASE∗PU on AS is−0.1732, with ap-value of 0.0317. This result indicates a signiﬁcant moderating eﬀect, suggesting that the relationship between ASE and AS is negatively moderatedbyPU."
