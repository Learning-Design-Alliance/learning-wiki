q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical argument in the essay's introduction, contrasting conceptual replications, which retain theoretical reasoning but vary procedures, with direct replications. The author offers this as a reasoned position, not a tested result; no empirical data are reported for it in this section.

"In this paper, I argue that not only can inadequate theory development lead to conceptual replication failure, but conceptual replication failure can also be an important component of subsequent theory development."
