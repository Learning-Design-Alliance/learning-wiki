Evidence¶

Kim Young-Suk Grace 2022¶

Kim Young-Suk Grace, Wolters Alissa, Mercado Janet, & Quinn Jamie. (2022). Crosslinguistic Transfer of Higher Order Cognitive Skills and Their Roles in Writing for English-Spanish Dual Language Learners. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000516

q3 · i3 · associational · r2

In the best-fitting CFA model for the 317-child sample, the article prints the correlation (effect size r = .59) between the cognitive general factor and the writing factor: "The bivariate correlation between the higher order cognitive general factor and writing factor was .59 (p < .001)".

"The bivariate correlation between the higher order cognitive general factor and writing factor was .59 (p < .001)."

Kim Young-Suk Grace 2022 (2)¶

Kim Young-Suk Grace, Wolters Alissa, Mercado Janet, & Quinn Jamie. (2022). Crosslinguistic Transfer of Higher Order Cognitive Skills and Their Roles in Writing for English-Spanish Dual Language Learners. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000516

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Multiple group structural regression on the same sample with covariates (sex, poverty, grade, EL status, school, biliterate status). Dual immersion coefficient was .41, p < .001, "not statistically significantly different" from the English immersion estimate of .43, p < .001; combined predictors accounted for 39–40% of writing variance.

"For the students in the English immersion program, the higher order cognitive general factor significantly predicted writing quality, .43, p < .001."
