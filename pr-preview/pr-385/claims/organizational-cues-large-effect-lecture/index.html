Evidence¶

Jansen 2017¶

Jansen, R. S., Lakens, D., & IJsselsteijn, W. (2017). An integrative review of the cognitive costs and benefits of note-taking. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2017.10.001

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative review of Titsworth and Kiewra (2004), in which half of 60 participants heard a structured audio lecture with organizational keywords and half heard the same lecture without them. The review reports the cueing benefit but prints no effect size.

"Cueing was found to have a large effect both on students’ understanding of the lecture structure, and on their memory for the details presented in the lecture."
