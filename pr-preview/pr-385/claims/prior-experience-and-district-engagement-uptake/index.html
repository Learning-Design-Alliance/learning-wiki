Wojcikiewicz 2025¶

Wojcikiewicz, S. K., Ralph, K., Snyder, J., Bland, J. A., & Wechsler, M. E. (2025). Teacher residencies in Texas: Supporting successful implementation. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/630.675

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Qualitative interview and document-analysis study of TCLAS implementation at three EPP–district sites and with state and technical assistance leaders. The report identifies prior EPP residency experience and district engagement as the two common enabling conditions; no effect size is printed.

"the EPPs in the initial group of Vetted Teacher Residency programs, designated by the state as eligible to partner with TCLAS districts, were already running residency programs when the grant was launched."
