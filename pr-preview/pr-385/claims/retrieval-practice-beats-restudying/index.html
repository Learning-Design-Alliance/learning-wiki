Anne M. Cleary 2026¶

Anne M. Cleary, Daniel H. Robinson. (2026). Why Growth Mindset Training Alone may not Improve Academic Outcomes: A Call for Research on Bundling Growth Mindset and Retrieval Practice Interventions. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10188-7

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative review attributing this to Rowland (2014): a meta-analytic review of 159 studies found people "2.5 times as likely to remember information following testing than restudying", with the benefit rising to 4.35 times when testing involved free recall rather than recognition; no standardized effect size is printed.

"In his review, Rowland examined 159 studies and found that people were 2.5 times as likely to remember information following testing than restudying. This benefit jumps to 4.35 times as likely to remember information if the type of testing involves free recall"

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative review attributing this to Agarwal et al. (2021): a meta-analytic review focused on testing versus restudying in actual classroom contexts found "a medium to large effect over restudying" in most studies; no numeric effect size is printed for this finding.

"Agarwal et al. ( 2021) conducted a meta-analytic review that was specifically focused on examining testing compared to restudying in actual classroom contexts and found most studies confirmed that testing enjoyed a medium to large effect over restudying."
