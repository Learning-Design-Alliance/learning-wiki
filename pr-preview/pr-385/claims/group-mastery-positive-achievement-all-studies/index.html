Guskey 1985¶

Guskey, Thomas R.; Gates, Sally L. (1985). A Synthesis of Research on Group-Based Mastery Learning Programs. Paper presented at the Annual Meeting of the American Educational Research Association, Chicago, IL. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED262088

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analytic synthesis of 38 studies of group-based, teacher-paced mastery learning; the achievement studies all "showel positive effects as a result of the application of group-based mastery learning strategies", with no study where control students outperformed mastery students overall.
