Evidence¶

Soemer 2015¶

Soemer, A. (2015). The Multicomponent Working Memory Model, Attention, and Long-term Memory in Multimedia Learning: A Comment on Schweppe and Rummer (2014). Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-015-9303-9

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical argument: both CLT and CTML assume the visual and auditory modalities have their own capacity limitations, so the single-versus-multiple-limit question bears directly on multimedia design. The commentary reports no experiment testing this.

"It is not difficult to see that the question of whether there is a single capacity limit (as assumed in the embedded-processes model), or multiple capacity limits originating from functional specialization (as assumed in the multicomponent model), is highly relevant for the design of multimedia materials."
