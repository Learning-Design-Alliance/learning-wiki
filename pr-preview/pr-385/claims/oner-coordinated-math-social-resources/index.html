Gerry Stahl 2013¶

Gerry Stahl. (2013). Learning across levels. Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning, 8, 1–12. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-013-9169-0

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

Öner's case study of two students working face-to-face on a shared dynamic-geometry problem, analyzed via Laborde's spatio-graphical versus technical reflection distinction. The article reports she 'shows how interrelated these can be,' e.g. a self-repair utterance naming equilateral triangles spanning conversational, linguistic and mathematical levels.

"Participants rely on two major categories of resources when working on a geometry problem within a computer-based dynamic-geometry environment: (1) mathematical and tool-enabled resources (math-content-related) and (2) collaboration resources (relational or social)."
