Null findings from the meta-analysis of K = 69 studies: the review reports no support for "a general association between a familiar situational narrative or the required operation" and performance. No effect size or test statistic for these null results is printed in the supplied text.

"However, the findings did not provide support for a general association between a familiar situational narrative or the required operation with mathematical word problem-solving."
