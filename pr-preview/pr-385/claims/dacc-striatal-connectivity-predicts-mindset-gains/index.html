Evidence¶

Lang Chen 2022¶

Lang Chen, Hyesang Chang, Jeremy Rudoler, Eydis Arnardottir, Yuan Zhang, Carlo de los Angeles and Vinod Menon. (2022). Cognitive training enhances growth mindset in children through plasticity of cortico-striatal circuits. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00146-7

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

gPPI multivariate network regression in the training group (38 children with high-quality fMRI). The strongest individual link, right dACC–right dorsal striatum, had r = 0.449 (FDR-adjusted p = 0.028); joint model adj. R² = 0.208, F(6,31) = 2.615, p = 0.036; not significant in controls (adj. R² = 0.047, p = 0.411). Connectivity did not predict math-skill changes (p = 0.148).

"increased connectivity of two individual links of the right dorsal ACC were positively correlated with changes in growth mindset with training: right dorsal ACC - left dorsal ACC, r(36) = 0.361, p = 0.026 (0.078, FDR-adjusted), and right dorsal ACC - right dorsal striatum, r(36) = 0.449, p = 0.005 (0.028, FDR-adjusted)"
