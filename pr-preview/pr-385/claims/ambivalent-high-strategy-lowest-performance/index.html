Evidence¶

Zhu 2026¶

Zhu, S., Yao, Y., Cheong, C. M., & Zhu, X. (2026). Latent motivational-emotional profiles and their effects on strategy use and performance in online multimodal reading tasks: A mixed-methods investigation. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2026.102386

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Wald chi-square tests (BCH approach) comparing the three latent profiles on OMR strategies and performance in the sample of 280 students; mean scores were G1 = 20.138, G2 = 15.337, G3 = 13.967, with the "Ambivalent Group (G3)" lowest.

"despite the Positive Group's less proactive strategy usage as reported, their online reading scores were significantly higher than those of the Ambivalent Group (G1-G3 = 4.801, p < .01), and also significantly surpassed those of the Moderate Group (G1-G2 = 6.171, p < .01)."
