Evidence¶

Sten Ludvigsen 2016¶

Sten Ludvigsen, Ulrike Cress, Nancy Law, Carolyn P. Rosé, & Gerry Stahl. (2016). Collaboration scripts and scaffolding. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning, 11, 381–385. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-016-9247-1

q2 · i? · causal · r?

The editors report on the experimental study by Erkens, Bodemer, and Hoppe, in which a text-mining tool transforms students' written text into variables used for group formation and cognitive visualization. They state the GRT "lead to improved outcomes under specific conditions"; no effect sizes are printed.

"The results from this study are positive for both students and teachers. The GRT is able to scaffold the students’ learning processes and lead to improved outcomes under specific conditions."
