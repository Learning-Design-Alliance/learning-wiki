Evidence¶

Lukas Wesenberg 2026¶

Lukas Wesenberg, Sebastian Jansen, Felix Krieglstein, Felicia Meusel, Sascha Schneider, Georg Valtin, Günter Daniel Rey. (2026). The more, the worse? The influence of the seductive detail amount on learning. Instructional Science (2026) 54:29. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09781-8

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Replication regression with 101 students assigned to five conditions (0%, 12.5%, 25%, 37.5%, 50% SD amount) using a 960-word text; the recall model was significant, F(5, 95) = 5.93, p <.001, R² = 0.24, and "With 1% more SDs, the recall score worsens by 0.33%".

"the SD amount significantly affected learning performance as measured by recall, b = −0.33, p =.005. The greater the SD amount, the worse the recall of the learning material (see Fig. 5 for a line chart). With 1% more SDs, the recall score worsens by 0.33% of the maximum achievable score."
