Chan 2023¶

Chan, M.-k., & Benner, A. D. (2023). A Consideration of Racial/Ethnic Diversity Conceptualization and Measurement: Clarifying Ambiguities and Advancing Scholarship. Educational Psychologist (accepted manuscript). https://orcid.org/0000-0001-6451-2417

q3 · i? · review · r3

Systematic review (PRISMA, 1970–2023; 114 included studies) coding diversity measures. The review reports that "Mathematical indices of diversity are a prevalent measure in the existing school racial/ethnic diversity literature (n = 85)", with Simpson's D the most widely applied (n = 75).

Related Claims¶

  • The range and value of diversity indices are constrained by the number of racial/ethnic groups included, complicating comparisons across studies — related
  • Simpson's D index is insensitive to small groups, so contexts with vastly different racial/ethnic compositions can receive nearly equivalent index values — related
  • A substantial minority of school diversity studies operationalize normic diversity via minority representation, while representative diversity is rarely measured — related
  • Ingroup representation and racial/ethnic diversity are distinct constructs that are often conflated in school diversity research — related
