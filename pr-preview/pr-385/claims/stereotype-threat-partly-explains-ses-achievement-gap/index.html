Evidence¶

Spencer 2007¶

Spencer, B; Castano, E (2007). Social class is dead, long live social class! Stereotype threat among low-socioeconomic individuals. Social Justice Research. https://dx.doi.org/10.1007/s11211-007-0047-7

q1 · i? · causal · r?

Authors' interpretation offered in the abstract: the experimental threat effects "may partly explain the disparity on standardized test scores between low- and high-SES students." This is an interpretive claim about the gap, not a direct measurement of it; the article offers no effect size for the gap explanation.

"These results help us better understand the role stereotyping plays in the academic performance of low-SES students, and may partly explain the disparity on standardized test scores between low- and high-SES students."
