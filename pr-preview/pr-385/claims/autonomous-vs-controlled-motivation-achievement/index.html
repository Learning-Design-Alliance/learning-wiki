A second meta-analysis reported in the same section found introjected and external regulation "not significantly related" to self-reported or objective school performance; equivalence was not tested.

"Howard et al. (2021) found that introjected and external regulation were not significantly related to self-reported (introjected ρ = .07, external ρ = − .02) or objective school performance (introjected ρ = − .01, external ρ = − .03)."

Related Claims¶

  • Promoting autonomous motivation was linked to higher motivation, lower experienced cognitive load, and higher achievement, but causality cannot be interpreted — related
  • Autonomous regulation correlates positively with self-efficacy, learning strategies, and behavioral engagement, while controlled regulation shows no significant linear relations with these outcomes — related
  • Adult learners' motivational profiles differ significantly in self-efficacy, learning strategies and behavioral engagement: the good-quality motivation group scores higher on all three, the high-quantity group on strategies and engagement only — related
  • Adult learners' motivation clusters into four profiles combining autonomous and controlled regulation, replicating the structure expected from SDT research with younger students — related
  • Dependent versus autonomous cognitive offloading to GenAI shows opposite motivational and cognitive outcome patterns — related
  • Children with ADHD show higher external regulation, introjected regulation and amotivation, and lower intrinsic motivation and identified regulation, toward learning than TD children — related
  • Perceived Competence, Intrinsic Motivation, and Psychological Safety each significantly positively predict Deep Revision Engagement — related
  • Satisfaction of basic psychological needs is positively associated with autonomous motivation, with competence the strongest predictor — related
  • Autonomous and intrinsic forms of motivation are positively associated with time management, while controlled motivation and amotivation show negative or null associations — related
  • Receiving grades is positively associated with achievement and external motivation compared with receiving no performance feedback (review-reported meta-analysis) — related
  • Introjected regulation is positively related to Korean students' achievement and self-regulated learning despite its association with academic stress — reports the opposite
  • SDT-based predictions on motivation and achievement hold for about 93% of students but may not generalize to extreme states of autonomous or controlled motivation — related
  • Teachers' load-reducing strategies correlate with lower cognitive load and higher motivation, engagement, and achievement, and their motivating styles with lower load and higher motivation and engagement (Evans et al., 2024) — related
  • Effort's relation to achievement differs by operationalization: quality-of-learning effort shows a positive link, quantity-of-learning effort is weak, conditional, or negative — related
