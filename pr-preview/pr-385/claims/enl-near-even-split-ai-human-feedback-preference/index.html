Escalante 2023¶

Escalante, J., Pack, A., & Barrett, A. (2023). AI-generated feedback on writing: insights into efficacy and ENL student preference. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education, 20:57. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-023-00425-2

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Study 2 weekly Qualtrics survey with 43 ENL students receiving both feedback types; forced-choice item 9 averaged near the 1.5 even-split point (grand mean 1.53). "The six-week average of the number of students that preferred feedback from human tutors was 18" vs 19.667 for AI.

"The six-week average of the number of students that preferred feedback from human tutors was 18, which was slightly lower than the average of those that preferred AI-generated feedback (19.667)"
