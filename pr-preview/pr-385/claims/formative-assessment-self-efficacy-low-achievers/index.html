Shute 2013¶

Shute, V. J., & Kim, Y. J. (2013). Formative and stealth assessment. In J.M. Spector et al. (eds.), Handbook of Research on Educational Communications and Technology. Springer. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4614-3185-5_25

q2 · i? · review · r2

The chapter reports, citing Kim (2007) and Black et al. (2003), that "formative assessment can promote signiﬁcant gains in student self-efﬁcacy and motivation," and separately cites Fuchs et al. (1997) for "beneﬁts for disadvantaged and low-achieving students." No effect sizes are printed for either cited finding.

"there is evidence that formative assessment can promote signiﬁcant gains in student self-efﬁcacy and motivation (Kim, 2007), which are predictors of high school graduation"
