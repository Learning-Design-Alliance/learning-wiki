Evidence¶

Ceri Ngai and Sam J. Gilbert 2026¶

Ceri Ngai and Sam J. Gilbert (2026). Metacognitive training facilitates optimal cognitive offloading. Cognitive Research: Principles and Implications. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41235-026-00714-0

q3 · i1 · causal · r3

Pre-registered four-group Experiment 2 (N = 416) with additive intervention components. The ANOVA on absolute metacognitive bias showed a significant group effect and significant linear contrast, and "the planned comparison between Group 1 and Group 4 was also significant" (d = 0.37), replicating the unsigned-bias finding from Experiment 1.

"the planned comparison between Group 1 and Group 4 was also significant (t(194.49) = 2.66, p = .004, d = 0.37)."
