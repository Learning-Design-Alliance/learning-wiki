Evidence¶

Lang Chen 2022¶

Lang Chen, Hyesang Chang, Jeremy Rudoler, Eydis Arnardottir, Yuan Zhang, Carlo de los Angeles and Vinod Menon. (2022). Cognitive training enhances growth mindset in children through plasticity of cortico-striatal circuits. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00146-7

q3 · i2 · causal · r1

Mixed ANCOVA with group and time factors and baseline growth mindset as covariate, in a longitudinal training study of 79 children (52 training, 27 control). Between-group post-training difference of Cohen's d = 0.508; the article also reports a "signiﬁcant group by time interaction, F(1,76) = 4.975, p = 0.029, η2 = 0.032".

"this group difference was signi ﬁcant after 4 weeks, t(77) = 2.142, p = 0.035, Cohen ’s d = 0.508, 95% CI [0.029, 0.987], with higher levels of growth mindset in the training group ( M = 4.52, SD = 0.49), compared to the controls ( M = 4.21, SD = 0.79)."
