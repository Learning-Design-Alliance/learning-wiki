Kenzhebayeva Z, Matayev B, Sarsembayeva E, Kolyukh O, Assenova N and Ospanova A (2026) Designing an AI-integrated role-rotation pedagogical model to support competence-related learning in pre-service educational psychologists. Front. Educ. 11:1905781. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1905781

q2 · i? · design · r2

Observational field note (OBS-G6) from the eight-week intervention's later cycles, documenting more frequent critical and collaborative examination of AI-generated information during group discussions, including questioning inconsistencies and revising proposed interventions.

"During the later learning cycles, students increasingly challenged AI-generated explanations by asking for theoretical justification, proposing alternative interpretations, and debating the contextual appropriateness of suggested interventions rather than accepting AI responses without discussion"

Kenzhebayeva Z, Matayev B, Sarsembayeva E, Kolyukh O, Assenova N and Ospanova A (2026) Designing an AI-integrated role-rotation pedagogical model to support competence-related learning in pre-service educational psychologists. Front. Educ. 11:1905781. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1905781

q2 · i? · design · r2

Results-section observation on the unevenness of critical engagement across participants in the same intervention. The article reports this as suggesting "differences in confidence and willingness to challenge apparently authoritative responses" rather than uniform development.

"Some participants continued to seek confirmation from AI before contributing their own interpretations, including during later learning cycles, suggesting differences in confidence and willingness to challenge apparently authoritative responses."
