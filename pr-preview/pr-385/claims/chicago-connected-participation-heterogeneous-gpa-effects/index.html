Evidence¶

Schachner 2025¶

Schachner, J.N., Marwell, N.P., de la Torre, M., Gwynne, J.A., & Allensworth, E.M. (2025). How did Chicago Connected broadband access eligibility and participation affect remote learning engagement and achievement during the COVID-19 pandemic? Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://edworkingpapers.com/ai25-1153

q2 · i? · associational · r2

OLS models predicting fall 2020 and spring 2021 GPAs as a function of participation among eligible students with controls. The summary reports "a 0.06 point higher fall 2020 GPA for students with high pre-pandemic GPAs" and a 0.05 point lower GPA for low-GPA students, with a similar spring 2021 pattern; these unstandardized GPA-point coefficients are the only magnitudes printed.

"Among eligible students, participation was associated with a 0.06 point higher fall 2020 GPA for students with high pre-pandemic GPAs. For students with low pre-pandemic GPAs, participation was associated with a fall 2020 GPA that was 0.05 points lower."
