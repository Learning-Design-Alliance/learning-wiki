The report's introductory rationale asserts, without presenting data on this page, that "Faculty who serve as mentors and advisors are in a pivotal position" for developing responsible researchers. This is the study's motivating premise, not a tested result.

"Faculty who serve as mentors and advisors are in a pivotal position to promote the development of young scientists’ research skills in a responsible and ethical manner."
