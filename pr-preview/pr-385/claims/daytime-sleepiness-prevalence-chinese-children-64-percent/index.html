Evidence¶

Li Shenghui 2013¶

Li Shenghui, Arguelles Lester, Jiang Fan, Chen Wenjuan, Jin Xingming, Yan Chonghuai, Tian Ying, Hong Xiumei, Qian Ceng, Zhang Jun, Wang Xiaobin, Shen Xiaoming. (2013). Sleep, School Performance, and a School-Based Intervention among School-Aged Children: A Sleep Series Study in China. PLoS ONE. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0067928

q3 · i? · associational · r2

National cross-sectional survey of 20,778 children aged 9.00 years (SD = 1.61) in eight Chinese cities, using the parent-administered CSHQ. The survey found "daytime sleepiness was shown to be 37.96% and 64.44%" for short sleep and sleepiness respectively, with sleepiness rated on the CSHQ 3-point scale.

"The prevalence of short sleep duration (<9 hours per day) and daytime sleepiness was shown to be 37.96% and 64.44% (sometimes 37.50% vs. frequently 26.94%), respectively."
