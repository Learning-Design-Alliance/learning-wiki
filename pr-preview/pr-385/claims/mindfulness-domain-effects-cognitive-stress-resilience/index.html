Evidence¶

Charlotte Zenner 2014¶

Charlotte Zenner, Solveig Herrnleben-Kurz and Harald Walach. (2014). Mindfulness-based interventions in schools—a systematic review and meta-analysis. Frontiers in Psychology. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2014.00603

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r3

Domain-specific synthesis in the review's quantitative analysis across five outcome domains (cognitive performance, stress, resilience, emotional problems, third person ratings). The review reports "cognitive performance g = 0.80, stress g = 0.39, resilience g = 0.36, (all p < 0.05)" with emotional problems and third person ratings not significant.

"Between group effect sizes for domains were: cognitive performance g = 0.80, stress g = 0.39, resilience g = 0.36, (all p < 0.05), emotional problems g = 0.19 third person ratings g = 0.25 (both n.s.)."
