Evidence¶

Margaret E. Raymond 2022¶

Margaret E. Raymond, Won Fy Lee. (2022). City Studies Project Meta-Analysis by CREDO of Stanford University: Cohort 1 Cities. Center for Research on Education Outcomes, Stanford University. https://credo.stanford.edu/city-studies/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Section 7 compares achievement levels of first-time charter entrants versus students enrolled for more than one year (incumbents) across the 10 cities, testing the cream-skimming hypothesis. Entrants show "similar or lower overall academic achievement than charter school incumbents" in all cities.

"In all cities, charter school entrants have similar or lower overall academic achievement than charter school incumbents, which suggests that charter schools are not cream skimming, ie., enrolling students who are academically well prepared."
