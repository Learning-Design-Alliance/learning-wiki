q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

Critique of the SIM-NEW data-generating model: it collapses ASC and ACH into one latent factor, whereas meta-analyses report a moderate correlation "around r =.30"; the data are entirely simulated and analysed with a non-REM time-reversed model.

"the simulation model assumes that both ASC and ACH are fully determined by a single latent “ability” factor, thereby collapsing two distinct psychological constructs into one, despite meta-analytic evidence showing that they are only moderately correlated (around r =.30)"
