q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's synthesis of cross-phase advantages, mentioned in 104 instances across the 24 reviewed studies. The review reports these as themes attributed to the underlying primary studies, which were not independently re-analyzed here.

"The most frequent learner-side themes included increased learning motivation (e.g. Li et al., 2025a, 2025b), critical thinking (e.g. Cheng, 2025; Ruff et al., 2024), and information and procedural accessibility (e.g. Min et al., 2025; Seebut et al., 2024)."
