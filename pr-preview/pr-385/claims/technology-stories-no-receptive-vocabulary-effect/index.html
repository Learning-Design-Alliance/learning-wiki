Evidence¶

Takacs 2015¶

Takacs, Z. K., Swart, E. K., & Bus, A. G. (2015). Benefits and Pitfalls of Multimedia and Interactive Features in Technology-Enhanced Storybooks: A Meta-Analysis. Review of Educational Research, 85(4), 698-739. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654314566989

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Meta-analytic results section: seven contrasts used book-based receptive vocabulary and two targeted general receptive vocabulary. Table 2 reports an overall receptive vocabulary effect of g+ = -0.08, 95% CI [-0.31, 0.15], p = .51, so no significant effect was detected; equivalence was not tested.

"Technology did not have a significant additional effect on receptive vocabulary as compared to more traditional storybook reading conditions."
