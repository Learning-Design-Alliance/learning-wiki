q2 · i? · design · r2

The author's stated reliability judgment in the RELIABILITY section: class graphs sufficient, individual graphs usefully suggestive, with only four scores possible per ability (none right to three right). No effect size is printed.

"The reliability of conclusions expressed, upon the basis of the present test, by graphs for classes of pupils, would be sufficient; for individuals, usefully suggestive."
