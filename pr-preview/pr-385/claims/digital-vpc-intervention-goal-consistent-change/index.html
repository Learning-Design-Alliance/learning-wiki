Evidence¶

Mathias Allemand 2026¶

Mathias Allemand, Brent W. Roberts, Ulrich Trautwein. (2026). Integrating Insights from Volitional Personality Change Interventions to Improve Self-Regulated Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10127-6

q3 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Stieger et al. (2021), a three-month RCT (N = 1,523; M age = 25) of twice-daily chatbot-based microinterventions. "Effect sizes for self-reported changes were in the large range according to educational intervention standards, whereas observer-reported effects were small to medium in magnitude."

"Compared to a waitlist control group, participants exhibited greater, goal-consistent personality changes, supported by self-reports and, to a lesser extent, observer-reports (the latter detecting increases but not decreases)."
