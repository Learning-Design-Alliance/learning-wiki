Evidence¶

Lortie-Forgues 2017¶

Lortie-Forgues, Hugues and Siegler, Robert S (2017) Conceptual knowledge of decimal arithmetic. Journal of Educational Psychology. pp. 374-391. ISSN: 0022-0620. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000148

q3 · i3 · associational · r1

Study 1, a repeated-measures ANOVA of direction of effect judgments by 55 middle school students on 16 inequalities, showed the decimal size X operation interaction (F(3, 159) = 38.24, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.42); accuracy was "below the chance level of 50% with decimals below one" for multiplication and division.

"Accuracy was below the chance level of 50% with decimals below one for both multiplication (t(54) = 6.05, p < 0.001) and division (t(54) = 6.49, p < 0.001)."
