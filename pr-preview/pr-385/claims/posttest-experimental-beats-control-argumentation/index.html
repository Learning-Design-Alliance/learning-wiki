Related Claims¶

  • Before the training, experimental and control PESTs were largely equivalent in argumentative competence, except justifications in the tree growth activity where the control group scored lower — related
  • Preservice elementary science teachers who completed the argumentation training programme made significant pre-to-post gains across the three argument elements and overall argumentative performance — related
  • Six months after training, the experimental group showed a slight improvement over the control group in transferring argumentation to teaching practice, especially in mentioning argumentation in practice portfolios — related
