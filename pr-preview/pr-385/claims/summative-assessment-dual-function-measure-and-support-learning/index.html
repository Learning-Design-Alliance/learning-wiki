Hartwig Grabowski 2026¶

q1 · i? · design · r2

Theoretical argument in the article's didactic-requirements section, citing Crooks, Biggs, Black & Wiliam, Entwistle & Entwistle, and Roediger & Karpicke: students use past and expected examinations as central learning material, so "examinations should support conceptual consolidation". The article offers this as an argument, not a tested result.

"This gives summative assessment a dual function. In addition to measuring the achieved level of learning, examinations should support conceptual consolidation."
