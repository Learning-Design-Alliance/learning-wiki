Evidence¶

Arnold B. Bakker 2024¶

Arnold B. Bakker, Karina Mostert. (2024). Study Demands–Resources Theory: Understanding Student Well-Being in Higher Education. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09940-8

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Robayo-Tamayo et al. (2020), a 5-day quantitative diary study in which daily self-efficacy and curiosity predicted mobilized social support and higher engagement.

"Robayo-Tamayo et al. (2020) investigated the influence of early-day personal resources on end-of-day student engagement through study resources. They used a 5-day quantitative diary study and showed that on the days students felt more self-efficacious and curious, they mobilized more social support from their professors and peers, increasing their engagement."
