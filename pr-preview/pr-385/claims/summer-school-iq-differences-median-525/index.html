Lincoln 1922¶

Lincoln, Edward A. (1922). The Constancy of Intelligence Quotients (A Case Study). The Journal of Educational Psychology, 14, 484-495. https://archive.org/details/journalofeducat14

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Analysis of repeated Stanford-Binet examinations of summer-school pupils from 1918 to 1921, comparing each IQ with all others for the same child. The printed distribution shows "The median of these differences is 5.25 points and 11.5 is the upper quartile," with three-quarters of differences described as not significantly large.

"The median of these differences is 5.25 points and 11.5 is the upper quartile. Thus it seems that three-quarters of the differences are not significantly large"
