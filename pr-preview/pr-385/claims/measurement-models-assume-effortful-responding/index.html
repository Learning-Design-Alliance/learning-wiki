Steven Wise 2015¶

Steven Wise. (2015). Effort analysis: Individual score validation of achievement test data. Applied Measurement in Education, 28(3), 237–252. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/effort-analysis-individual-score-validation-of-achievement-test-data/ doi:10.1080/08957347.2015.1042155

q2 · i? · theoretical · r?

The abstract states the assumption directly: "current measurement models underlying achievement testing assume students respond effortfully to test items," which motivates identifying materially distorted scores. This is a conceptual argument, not a tested result.

"Because current measurement models underlying achievement testing assume students respond effortfully to test items, it is important to identify test scores that have been materially distorted by non-effortful test taking."
