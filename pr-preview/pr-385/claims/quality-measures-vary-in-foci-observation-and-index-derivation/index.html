q2 · i? · review · r3

Descriptive review statement about the most widely used scales — CLASS, ECERS-3 (and predecessors), ITERS and SSTEW — which "share the aim of identifying high-quality provision" yet differ in foci, observation approach and index derivation.

"yet vary widely in foci (e.g. the balance of structural and process quality dimen- sions they consider; curricular or interactional aspects of quality included), approach to observing quality (e.g. sustained observation over half a day or more; frequent but brief observation intervals; self-reported recollections) and their approaches for deriving quality indices therefrom"

Related Claims¶

  • Global ECEC quality indices show little evidence of relating to child outcomes — related
  • Prior meta-analyses report few or weak associations between ECEC quality indices and child progress, with process quality more predictive than structural quality — related
  • National quality rating systems show divergent operationalisations across countries, and links between ratings and child development are often shielded from scrutiny — related
  • Consistency of association with child outcomes varies across interaction quality dimensions, improving for supporting play but remaining low for instructional support even against aligned outcomes — related
