Evidence¶

Lemke 2023¶

Lemke, M., Murphy, D., Soo Ping Chow, A., Spencer, H., & Zhang, A. (2023). Opportunity Gaps: Findings From Initial Analysis of Student Performance in Early Literacy in Massachusetts. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED638519

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Observational analysis of 2020/21 and 2021/22 screening data across all grantee schools, reported in Table 2. The brief prints a relative risk of 1.4 for economically disadvantaged students and notes English learner (1.4), Hispanic (1.4), special education (1.3), and Black (1.2) students' elevated risks.

"76 percent of economically disadvantaged students were ever below benchmark compared to 53 percent of non–economically disadvantaged students. The relative risk of economically disadvantaged students ever being classified as below benchmark was 1.4 times that of non–economically disadvantaged students."
