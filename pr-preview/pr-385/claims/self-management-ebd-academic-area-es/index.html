Evidence¶

Mooney 2005¶

Mooney, P., Ryan, J. B., Uhing, B. M., Reid, R., & Epstein, M. H. (2005). A Review of Self-Management Interventions Targeting Academic Outcomes for Students with Emotional and Behavioral Disorders. Journal of Behavioral Education, 14, 203–221. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10864-005-6298-1

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r1

The review reports the distribution of targeted academic areas and printed effect sizes by area across the included studies: math ES = 1.97, writing ES = 1.13, reading ES = 2.28, social studies ES = 2.66. Math calculation was the most frequently targeted area, while writing showed the lowest mean ES and social studies the highest. No between-area significance tests are reported in the available text.

"Interventions targeted improvement in math calculation skills more than any other area. The mean ES by academic area were math interventions (1.97), writing (1.13), reading (2.28), and social studies (2.66)."
