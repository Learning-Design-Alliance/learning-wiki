Evidence¶

Jesse A. Rodríguez 2026¶

Jesse A. Rodríguez. (2026). LaTA: A Drop-In, FERPA-Compliant Local-LLM Autograder for Upper-Division STEM Coursework. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05410 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.05410

q2 · i3 · associational · r1

Anonymous post-term Canvas survey of N=159 students (~80% response rate) using a retrospective pre/then-post design; pre/post distributions treated as independent for Mann–Whitney U testing. Printed deltas were +1.57 to +1.88 Likert points.

"All four comparisons achieved Mann–Whitney U p <10 −27 uncorrected (well below the threshold any standard multiple-comparisons correction would impose at q= 0.05 across only four tests) with rank-biserial effect sizes rrb ranging from 0.688 (PDEs) to 0.825 (BVPs), conventionally "large" effects."
