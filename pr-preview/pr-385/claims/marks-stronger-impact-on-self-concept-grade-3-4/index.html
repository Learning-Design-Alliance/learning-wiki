Evidence¶

Helmke 1995¶

Helmke, A., & van Aken, M. A. G. (1995). The causal ordering of academic achievement and self-concept of ability during elementary school: A longitudinal study. Journal of Educational Psychology, 87(4), 624-637. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.87.4.624

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Author-reported path coefficients from the two-indicator LISREL models comparing marks-to-self-concept and tests-to-self-concept across waves; the article hedges this as "a tendency toward a stronger impact of marks." These coefficients are reported as descriptive paths without test statistics.

"Whereas from second to third grade both influences are equally strong (.16 and .17), there is a tendency toward a stronger impact of marks on self-concept (.28) from third to fourth grade, as compared with the path from tests to self-concept (.12)."
