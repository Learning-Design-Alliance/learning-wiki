Evidence¶

Amanda Vite 2024¶

Amanda Vite, Erika A. Patall, Man Chen. (2024). Relationships Between Experiences of Autonomy and Well(Ill)-Being for K-12 Youth: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review (2024) 36:127. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09967-x

q4 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Meta-analytic comparison of pooled correlations within the same synthesis. The ANF-well-being correlation (r = − 0.17) differed significantly from the ANS-well-being correlation (0.35), p = 0.002; the article also reports a significant difference (p < 0.001) between ANS-ill-being (r = − 0.23) and ANF-ill-being (r = 0.30).

"Results showed that the average correlation between ANF and well-being was r = − 0.17 compared to 0.35 for the relationship between ANS and well-being when controlling for publication status and measurement reliability. This difference was statistically significant (p = 0.002)."
