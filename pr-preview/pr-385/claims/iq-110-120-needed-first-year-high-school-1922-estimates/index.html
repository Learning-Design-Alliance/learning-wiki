Section I compares the Providence school report's estimated limit of school progress by IQ with Terman's estimates, noting the estimates are identical at some points and never more than a year apart. The text states the Providence table "would seem to indicate" IQ 120 is necessary for first-year high school work.

"The Providence table would seem to indicate that in order to do successful work in the first year of high school, an intelligence quotient of 120 is necessary. Terman would predict average high school success where the intelligence quotient is 110."
