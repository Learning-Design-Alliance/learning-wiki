Hennessy E. A. 2018¶

Hennessy E. A., Tanner‐Smith E. E., Finch A. J., Sathe N. A., Kugley S. A. (2018). Recovery schools for improving behavioral and academic outcomes among students in recovery from substance use disorders: a systematic review. Campbell Systematic Reviews. https://doi.org/10.4073/csr.2018.9

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review's Results section reports the outcome of its systematic search across electronic databases, research registers, grey literature, reference lists, and expert contacts: only one RHS quasi‐experimental study qualified, and no CRC studies met the eligibility criteria for the review's primary analysis.

"Only one study met criteria for inclusion in the review. This study used a QED to examine the effects of RHSs on high school students' academic and substance use outcomes. No eligible studies examining CRCs were identified in the search."
