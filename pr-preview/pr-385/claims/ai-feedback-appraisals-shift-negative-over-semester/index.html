Andres Karjus 2026¶

Andres Karjus, Janika Leoste, Tiia Õun. (2026). Student Evaluation of Repeated AI Feedback Across a Semester of Writing. arXiv:2607.16115 [cs.CY]. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16115 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.16115

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Over-time analysis of annotated appraisal themes across homework 1–13 in the same observational corpus. The most common theme was actionable support (53% overall), but positive stances fell over the semester while misreading complaints rose.

"Both positive stances remained prevalent but fell over the semester, actionable-support from 67% in HW1 to 43% in HW13 (Fig. 3). Responses noting that the AI misread the essay or misunderstood a task detail peaked at 19% in HW12."
