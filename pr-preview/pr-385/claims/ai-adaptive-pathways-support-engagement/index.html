Evidence¶

Ana Katalinic 2026¶

Ana Katalinic, Vanja Slavuj, Danijela Jaksic. (2026). Artificial Intelligence in Online Education: A Systematic Review of Its Impact on Learner Engagement and Satisfaction. Educ. Sci. 2026, 16, 389. https://doi.org/10.3390/educsci16030389

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Fisher & Baird (2020), that adaptive platforms personalize instruction via granular content units and dynamic pathway adjustment. The review attributes this to the included study; its design is not described here in detail.

"AI-driven adaptive learning platforms, such as ALEKS and Squirrel AI, personalize instruction by breaking content into granular learning units and dynamically adjusting learning pathways, thereby supporting learner engagement and satisfaction through tailored learning experiences"
