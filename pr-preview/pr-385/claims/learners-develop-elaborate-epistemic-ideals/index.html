Evidence¶

Sarit Barzilai & Clark A. Chinn 2024¶

Sarit Barzilai & Clark A. Chinn. (2024). The AIR and Apt-AIR Frameworks of Epistemic Performance and Growth: Reflections on Educational Theory Development. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09927-5

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The review reports findings from the authors' design studies with middle school students developing criteria for evaluating models, arguments, or sources. It further reports that "students as young as fifth graders can not only develop and use productive ideals for good models" but also "metacognitively justify why these criteria are valuable."

"we observed that, given appropriate materials and prompts, learners can successfully develop, articulate, and elaborate rich epistemic ideals for good models (e.g., answers the question, provides visual aids, supported by evidence) or good sources (e.g., expert authors, no vested interests)"
