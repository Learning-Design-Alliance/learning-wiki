Ben Swift 2026¶

Ben Swift. (2026). LLMs Unplugged: Teaching Resources for a ChatGPT World. 28th Australasian Computing Education Conference (ACE 2026). https://doi.org/10.1145/3786228.3786237

q2 · i? · design · r2

Practitioner observation across repeated deliveries of the Fundamentals lessons at the Australian National University. The author reports “there is an inflection point after the first “shareback” of the newly-generated text” and that questions become more incisive from that point.

"there is an inflection point after the first “shareback” of the newly-generated text. TheTraininglesson is necessary set-up, but the room really starts to buzz duringGenerationwhen we go around the room and people get to share what came out of their new model"
