Correlational analysis in School O.K. for the practised half of the class only, based on four reasoning tests and ten numerical-accuracy exercises; the printed effect size is r = +.85, as the Pearson formula "gives +.85".

"The correlation is obviously a high one, and its exact de- termination from the individual cases by means of the Pear- son formula of correlation gives +.85."

Winch 1910 (3)¶

Winch, W. H. (1910). Accuracy in school children. Does improvement in numerical accuracy "transfer"? The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0072557

q1 · i? · causal · r2

Author's general conclusion from the four series: a theoretical argument that high positive correlation between two functions need not mean causal dependency, a trainable common factor, or transferability; the author warns educationists against drawing inferences "which do not properly flow from it".

"We cannot conclude without further inquiry on other lines, that two highly correlated m&ital powers are causally related."
