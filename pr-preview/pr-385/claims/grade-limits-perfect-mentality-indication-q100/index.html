Evidence¶

Toops 1918¶

Toops, H. A., and Pintker, R. (1918). Mentality and School Progress. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Mentality+and+School+Progress+Toops+Pintner

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Distributional analysis of 1218 classified cases by years of retardation/advancement and I.Q. diagnosis (Table IV). The article adds that the chances greatly favor a backward diagnosis for four-grade retardation and a bright diagnosis for two-grade advancement; no standardized effect size is printed.

"By reference to Table IV, we see that no individual who is four or more grades retarded in school is above normal in mentality; also, that no individual who is more than one grade advanced in school is below normal in mentality. With these, limits, than, for our "normal" mentality group, we secure a perfect positive association, QVI = +1.00."
