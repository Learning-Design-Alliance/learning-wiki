Overview of the evidence base in the guide's introduction: 21 studies met WWC standards, and "The number of student participants in the studies ranged from 124 to 42,984." Studies used comparison group designs published 1999 or later at U.S. community colleges.

"Recommendation 1 is supported by 12 studies, while Recommendation 2 is supported by nine studies. Six studies support Recommendation 3, while 15 studies support Recommendation 4 and 11 studies support Recommendation 5."
