The article reports Goddard's Binet-scale testing of New Jersey public-school children, citing his published study; 1347 children were tested, with 554 passing age-matched tests, 599 who "passed tests lower than their age", and 394 passing higher tests; 1195 passed within one year of their age.

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

The author's evaluative judgment about the scale's standing, that "the Binet-Simon tests are the best systematic experimental de- vice yet evolved for measuring intellectual development", arguing imperfection should not hinder school use, by analogy with the automobile. This is expert opinion, not a comparative trial.

"While there thus remains much work still to be done with tin's measuring scale of intelligence, the fact remains that the Binet-Simon tests are the best systematic experimental de- vice yet evolved for measuring intellectual development"
