Evidence¶

Charleen Brand 2023¶

Charleen Brand, Christian Hartmann, Katharina Loibl, Nikol Rummel. (2023). Do students learn more from failing alone or in groups? Insights into the effects of collaborative versus individual problem solving in productive failure. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-023-09619-7

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Mediation analysis (N = 135, PROCESS model 82, 5000 bootstrap samples, cluster-corrected standard errors) in the randomized PF experiment. The indirect effect through solution quantity was a1b1 = 0.01 with a confidence interval including zero.

"The bootstrap confidence interval showed no evidence for an indirect effect of the condition on conceptual knowledge that is mediated through quantity of solutions, a1b1 = 0.01, SE = 0.05, 95% CI [− 0.06, 0.14]."
