Lydia Manikonda and Dominique Outlaw 2026¶

Lydia Manikonda and Dominique Outlaw. (2026). Policy Fragmentation or Institutional Alignment? Institutional Governance of AI in Universities and Business Schools. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03584 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.03584

q2 · i? · associational · r2

NLP sentiment analysis of a corpus of 130 university AI policies crawled from R1/R2 institutions across 34 states. The figure caption reports polarity "mean=0.11, min=-0.08, and max=0.25" on a -1 to 1 scale; only two institutions show a negative tone, both near neutral.

"Polarity values in Figure 1(a) highlight that most universities are positive in sentiment; however, they are closer to 0 than 1, suggesting that the majority are moderately positive."
