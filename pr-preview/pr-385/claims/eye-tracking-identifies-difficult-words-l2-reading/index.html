Evidence¶

Kai Kunze 2013¶

Kai Kunze, Masakazu Iwamura, Koichi Kise, Seiichi Uchida, and Shinichiro Omachi. (2013). Activity Recognition for the Mind: Toward a Cognitive “Quantified Self.” Computer, IEEE Computer Society, October 2013. http://kaikunze.de/papers/2013Kunze-1.pdf

q2 · i? · design · r2

Study of 10 native Japanese speakers learning English who read 10 TOEIC passages while wearing the iView X eye tracker, then marked words they did not understand. Eye gaze was mapped to digitized passages using locally likely arrangement hashing (LLAH), and fixation histograms revealed trouble words; the method "worked for all words marked as difficult". No effect size is printed.

"The method worked for all words marked as difficult by the partici- pants."
