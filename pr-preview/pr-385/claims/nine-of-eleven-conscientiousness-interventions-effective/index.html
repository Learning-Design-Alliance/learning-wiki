Evidence¶

Melanie Turner 2025¶

Melanie Turner, Flaviu A. Hodis. (2025). Strengthening Conscientiousness by Means of Interventions: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10016-4

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

Systematic review synthesis (RQ3) of 11 studies published 2017–2024. The review reports "Nine out of the 11 interventions (82%) significantly increased participants’ conscientiousness"; one behavior activation intervention and one coaching intervention were not effective.

"Nine out of the 11 interventions (82%) significantly increased participants’ conscientiousness at either the trait, facet, or state level."

Related Claims¶

  • Digital coaching increased the organization and productiveness facets of conscientiousness but not responsibility, with no observer-reported facet changes — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Digital coaching significantly increased trait conscientiousness, with effects stable one year after the intervention ended — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Intervention gains in conscientiousness were generally maintained at follow-up, but mediation was never tested and only one intervention examined moderation — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Behavior activation interventions show a dose-response pattern — completing more conscientiousness challenges produced larger increases — but one behavior activation trial found no significant change — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • One in-person coaching intervention produced a small significant reduction in conscientiousness while the control group showed no change — reports the opposite
