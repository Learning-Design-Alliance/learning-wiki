Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • A significant three-way interaction of group, prime type and arithmetic operation governs arithmetic-affective priming, with DD children slower overall than controls
  • Children with developmental dyscalculia respond faster to simple arithmetic targets after negative than positive affective primes, across all four arithmetic operations
  • Math anxiety in DD is specific to the math context and occurs in the absence of generalized anxiety — related
  • Typically-developing children show the standard affective priming pattern, responding faster to arithmetic targets after positive than negative primes — related
