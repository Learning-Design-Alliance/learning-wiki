Cross-specification comparison of teacher ratings for a district with many disadvantaged students. The article reports such teachers "receive lower ratings when background characteristics are omitted." No effect size or test statistic is printed in the available text.

"In particular, teachers in a district with a large fraction of disadvantaged students receive lower ratings when background characteristics are omitted."
