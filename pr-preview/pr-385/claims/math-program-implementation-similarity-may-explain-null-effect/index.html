Cambridge, MA: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/elementarygrade-math-programs-in-the-pittsburgh-public-schools-a-comparison-of-everyday-mathematics-and-harcourt-math

q1 · i? · causal · r?

Authors' interpretation offered without supporting statistics on the page: the null result "may be related to" the two programs being "more similar in practice than they are in theory" in their Pittsburgh implementation.

"The absence of a difference in achievement effects may be related to the fact that, in their implementation in Pittsburgh, the two programs were more similar in practice than they are in theory."
