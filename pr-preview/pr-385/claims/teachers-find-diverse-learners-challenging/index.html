Rowan, L., Bourke, T., L'Estrange, L., Lunn, J., Ryan, M., Walker, S., & Churchward, P. (2021). How does Initial Teacher Education Research frame the challenge of preparing future teachers for student diversity in schools?: A systematic review of literature. Review of Educational Research, 91(1), 112-158. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654320979171

The article's framing section reports, citing prior studies from the United States, Canada, Korea, Sweden, The Netherlands, Australia, and France, that working with diverse learners "is still experienced as challenging and complex" by many teachers including new graduates.

"research across many countries (e.g. the United States, Canada, Korea, Sweden, The Netherlands, Australia, and France) has persistently shown that for many teachers, including new graduates, working with the groups referred to either specifically or generically as diverse learners is still experienced as challenging and complex"
