Lowell 1917¶

Lowell, Frances. (1917). A Preliminary Report of Some Group Tests of General Intelligence. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/edu

q2 · i? · design · r2

Observational analysis of Table IV data from the survey's grades one to three children (I.Q.s ranging .40-1.30). The author reports the Grade I figure and parallel Grade II (10 tests) and Grade III (12 tests) figures, attributing the increase to added school experience.

"For example, Table IV shows that in Grade I children who are chronologically 7 years and whose average mental age is 7.1 — 7.5 pass 9.8 tests out of Groups V, VI, and VII"
