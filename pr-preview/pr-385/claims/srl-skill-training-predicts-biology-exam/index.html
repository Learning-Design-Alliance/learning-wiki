Evidence¶

Joep van der Graaf 2023¶

Joep van der Graaf, Michelle Taub, Yizhou Fan. (2023). Introduction to special issue on facilitating self-regulated learning with scaffolds: Recent advances and future directions. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09364-9

q2 · i? · review · r2

The introduction reports, citing Bernacki et al. (this issue), a study with university biology students of a redesigned video-based multimedia digital skill training addressing SRL, which showed beneficial effects on learning like the original design, with moderating effects studied to explain why. No effect sizes are printed.

"Specifically, learners’ knowledge about behavioral and environmental regulation demonstrated during the training predicted biology exam performance. Cognitive and metacognitive knowledge showed nonsignificant, positive relations with exam performance."

Matthew L. Bernacki 2023¶

Matthew L. Bernacki, Megan Claire Cogliano, Shelbi L. Kuhlmann, Jenifer Utz, Christy Strong, Jonathan C. Hilpert, Jeffrey A. Greene. (2023). Relations between undergraduates’ self-regulated learning skill mastery during digital training and biology performance. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09356-9

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Study 2 replicated Study 1 analyses with students who completed a multimedia redesign in which digital videos replaced textual content to save time. The article reports that "knowledge demonstrated during a redesigned video-based multimedia version of behavioral and environmental regulation again predicted biology exam performance."

"In Study 2 (n = 62), knowledge demonstrated during a redesigned video-based multimedia version of behavioral and environmental regulation again predicted biology exam performance."
