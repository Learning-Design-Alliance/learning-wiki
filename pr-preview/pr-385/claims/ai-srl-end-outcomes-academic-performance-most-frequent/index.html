q3 · i? · review · r2

The review's end-outcome categorization found "The most frequently reported outcome was an improvement in academic performance, cited in 40 of the reviewed studies (48%)". Smaller outcome categories included reasoning skills (6%), learning behavior profiles (5%), satisfaction (5%), anxiety reduction (4%), social interaction (4%), and single-study outcomes such as student agency and burnout reduction.
