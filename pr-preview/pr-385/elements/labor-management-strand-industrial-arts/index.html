Labor-management relations strand repeated across all four communications units¶

Element · All elements
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Each of the four units includes a common labor-management content strand. Students learn to "distinguish between Labor and Management personnel," list four common causes of disagreement (rate of wages, working conditions, hours of work, benefits), list three methods of settling arguments (negotiation, arbitration, strike), and list three cooperation mechanisms (training programs, safety programs, work promotions). Role-playing a labor-management dispute settlement is the recurring suggested activity.

Context¶
