q2 · i? · associational · r2

SEM hypothesis testing on the pooled 588-respondent dataset; hypotheses H7–H9 were rejected as quoted, and the UA→BI path (coefficient .016, p = .645) was likewise non-significant.

"The cultural dimensions of IC, MF, and PD did not influence BI. Their path coefficients of −0.012, −0.008, and 0.007, respectively, were weak."
