q2 · i? · associational · r2

Regression analysis of dialogue act counts against normalized assessment scores in both semesters. Significant negative coefficients for Contextual Questions include Assignment Clarification (a3: −0.0034, p=0.021; a5: −0.0072, p=0.039) and Other (a5: −0.0075, p=0.017). The authors suggest this negative correlation "is perhaps an indicator of student confusion."
