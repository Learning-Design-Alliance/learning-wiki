Evidence¶

Yu Fanyi 2025¶

Yu Fanyi, Nyanamba Juliet M., Anderson Brittany, DeerInWater Kathy, Estrada Mica, Hosoda K. Kanoho, Maldonado Natalia, Smith Tiffany, Flores Lilibeth, & Hernandez Paul R. (2025). Navigating Science Pathways: Belonging Mediates the Impact of Goal Congruity on Persistence (but Not Life Satisfaction) Among Indigenous Students. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000967

q2 · i2 · associational · r2

Preliminary paired-sample t-tests in the longitudinal study comparing communal and agentic goal endorsement and affordance perceptions. The article prints "d = 0.50" for the endorsement difference and "d = 0.15" for the non-significant affordance difference.

"Results showed that Indigenous students endorsed communal goals more strongly than agentic goals (t[141] = 5.93, p < .001, d = 0.50). However, no significant difference was found between perceptions of communal and agentic affordances in STEM fields (t[141] = 1.80, p = .07, d = 0.15)."
