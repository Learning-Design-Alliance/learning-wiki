Evidence¶

Olivo 2024¶

Olivo, G., Persson, J. & Hedenius, M. (2024). Exploring brain plasticity in developmental dyslexia through implicit sequence learning. npj Science of Learning, 9:37. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00250-w

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Behavioral results reported in the Results section, described in detail in the authors' prior report; Table 1 prints ISL day 1 of 0.06 (DD) versus 0.10 (TD) and day 2 of 0.08 versus 0.13, with the group change comparison reported as p = 0.390.

"Overall, both groups improved on their ISL score with the same pattern; however, TD children consistently performed better than DD at both time-points."
