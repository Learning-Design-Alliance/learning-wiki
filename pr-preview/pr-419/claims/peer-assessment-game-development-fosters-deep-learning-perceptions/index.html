Hwang 2014¶

Hwang, GJ., Hung, CM. & Chen, NS. (2014). Improving learning achievements, motivations and problem-solving skills through a peer assessment-based game development approach. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-013-9320-7

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Analysis of open-ended question responses from the experiment found that "most of the students perceived peer assessment-based game development as an effective learning strategy" supporting deep learning. This is self-reported perception data, not a tested effect size.

"it was found from the open-ended questions that most of the students perceived peer assessment-based game development as an effective learning strategy that helped them improve their deep learning status in terms of "in-depth thinking," "creativity," and "motivation.""
