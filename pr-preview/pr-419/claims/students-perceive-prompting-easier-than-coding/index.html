q2 · i? · associational · r1

Likert-item survey (Ref3) administered to 916 students in an introductory C programming course who attempted at least one Prompt Problem. The mean response of "3.80 (SD= 0.93)" exceeded the neutral point, t(915) = 29.91, p < .001; no standardized effect size is printed.

"Students tended towards agreeing that it was easier to solve problems by writing natural language than by writing code, with the mean response of 3.80 (SD= 0.93). This was significantly greater than if students perceived both activities equally (t(915) = 29.91,p< .001, 95% CI = 0.74, 0.86)."
