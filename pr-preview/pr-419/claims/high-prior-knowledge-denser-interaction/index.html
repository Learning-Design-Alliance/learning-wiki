Group comparison of dialogue volume in the MAIC course. The article also reports more AI-peer responses in the high group (M = 80.06 vs. 30, z = -3.033, p = .002) and more AI-teacher responses (M = 54.82 vs. 27.32, z = -2.866, p = .004).
