Evidence¶

Erica L. Snow 2016¶

Erica L. Snow, Aaron D. Likens, Laura K. Allen, & Danielle S. McNamara. (2016). Taking Control: Stealth Assessment of Deterministic Behaviors Within a Game-Based System. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0085-5

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Linear regression with 40 students: model one showed pretest self-explanation predicted daily training scores (R² = .26, F(1,38) = 13.60, p < .01); model two added Hurst exponents, which the article reports "accounted for 18 % of the additional variance" (R² = .44, F(1,37) = 11.93, p < .01).
