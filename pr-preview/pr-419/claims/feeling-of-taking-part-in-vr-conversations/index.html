Evidence¶

Kenneth Silseth 2024¶

Kenneth Silseth, Rolf Steier, Hans Christian Arnseth. (2024). Exploring students’ immersive VR experiences as resources for collaborative meaning making and learning. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-023-09413-0

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Moment-by-moment turn-by-turn analysis of one group's dialogue (three students and their teacher) after viewing TWB. The analysis "shows how students, together with their teacher, talk about their experiences in TWB through the lens of co-presence and dialogue with the people in the film," with students building on each other's contributions.
