Abramson SL 2026¶

Abramson SL, Sora-Cardenas J, Nandakumar P, Bruce JE, Gopinath N, Zangari LG and Feijóo-García PG (2026). Trust and interaction design in AI-enabled systems: a systematic literature review. Front. Psychol. 17:1932655. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1932655

q3 · i? · review · r2

Across the 33-study corpus, population sizes ranged from n = 10 to n = 1,511 (mean n = 245.73); 33.33% of studies reported Western-country participants, 84.84% reported participants aged 18 or older, and 27.27% involved university-degree holders or pursuers.

"These demographic patterns point toward a systematic bias in participant recruitment, consistent with the WEIRD framework (i.e., Western, Educated, Industrialized, Rich, and Democratic) (Henrichetal.,2010 )."
