Li 2025¶

Li, H., Li, J., Hao, X., & Liu, W. (2025). Behavioral and eye-tracking investigation of event segmentation following short video watching. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00378-3

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Study 2, an independent sample of 60 young adults, used a picture-based encoding-retrieval paradigm (faces and houses) with Remember/Know recognition after short- or long-video manipulation. Neither group comparisons nor TikTok-score correlations reached significance on recognition, familiarity, or recollection measures.

"No signiﬁcant differences were found in any of the memory metrics investigated (allp > 0.10; Fig. 6, left panels). Furthermore, across all participants, no signi ﬁcant correlations were observed between TikTok scores and memory metrics (all p > 0.10; Fig. 6,r i g h t panels)."
