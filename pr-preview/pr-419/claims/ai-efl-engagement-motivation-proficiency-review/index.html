Evidence¶

Arnab Kundu 2024¶

Arnab Kundu, Tripti Bej. (2024). Transforming school EFL teaching with AI: A Systematic Review of Empirical Studies. Research Square preprint. https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-4805086/v1

q2 · i? · review · r2

A systematic review using PRISMA screening identified 22 relevant studies reporting hands-on experiential learning outcomes of EFL students on AI use, from 189 eligible studies. The review's synthesis reports "signi cant improvements in student engagement, motivation and language pro ciency attributed to AI interventions."

"The review highlights signi cant improvements in student engagement, motivation and language pro ciency attributed to AI interventions while also addressing concerns regarding accessibility, teacher training, and ethical considerations."
