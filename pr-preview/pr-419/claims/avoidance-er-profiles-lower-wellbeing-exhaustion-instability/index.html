Evidence¶

Hilger 2025¶

Hilger, K. J. E., Scheibe, S., Frenzel, A. C., Grund, S., & Keller, M. M. (2025). Exploring paths of change: Analyzing teachers’ emotion regulation repertoire over time using multi-level latent profile analysis. Learning and Instruction, 101, Article 102234. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102234

q3 · i? · associational · r?

The study explored profiles' associations with occupational well-being across the two bursts spaced six months apart. It reports the avoidance-characterized profiles "were also linked to lower affective well-being and more emotional exhaustion, and displayed lower stability over time". No effect sizes are printed for these associations.

"Those profiles were also linked to lower affective well-being and more emotional exhaustion, and displayed lower stability over time."
