Katia Tödt 2026¶

Katia Tödt, Corinne Büching, and Bastian Granas. (2026). Digital Education and Innovation: Objectives and Conceptual Classification of the Volume. In Digital Education and Innovation. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-26816-7_1

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical argument in the chapter's framing section. The authors distinguish micro-level didactic interaction, meso-level organisational development and institutional culture, and macro-level political and legal negotiation, arguing these levels are interdependent rather than isolated.

"digital transformation cannot be vie wed in isolation, but must be understood as an interdependent structure which permeates all levels of the education system"

Discussion¶

The chapter's response is to design adaptive, reflexive structures at macro, meso and micro levels.
