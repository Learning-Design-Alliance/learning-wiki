Evidence¶

Regina Kaplan-Rakowski 2021¶

Regina Kaplan-Rakowski. (2021). Addressing students' emotional needs during the COVID-19 pandemic: a perspective on text versus video feedback in online environments. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09897-9

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The article reports, citing Borup et al. (2015), survey evidence from instructors and students in blended courses: "both students and instructors perceived text and video feedback as equally valuable with regard to quality and delivery". Qualitative analyses and end-of-term interviews showed text was more efficient and organized; video was more conversational and supportive.

"As evidenced by instructors' and students' surveys, Borup et al. (2015) found that both students and instructors perceived text and video feedback as equally valuable with regard to quality and delivery."
