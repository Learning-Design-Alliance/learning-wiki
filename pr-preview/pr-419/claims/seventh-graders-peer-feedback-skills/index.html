The article reports a study of how students assessed each other's ELOs, citing Tsivitanidou et al. (2011), in which seventh graders gave peer feedback on ELOs. The article concludes their skills "still needed further development"; no effect sizes are printed.

"students (seventh graders, in this case) could in principle give relevant peer feedback on ELOs, but that their skills still needed further development"
