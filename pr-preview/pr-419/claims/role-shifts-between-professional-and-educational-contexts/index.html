Evidence¶

Charlott Sellberg · Amit Sharma 2025¶

Charlott Sellberg · Amit Sharma. (2025). Toward multimodal learning analytics in simulation-based collaborative learning: A design ethnography of maritime training. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-024-09435-2

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Authors' analytical conclusion (type e) drawn from the episode analyses, noting that in-scenario corrections are delivered as roleplay first and then clarified through explicit instructor role shifts when students show no displays of understanding the correction.

"A conclusion from the analysis is that the recurring movements between these activity contexts—the professional (in-role) and the educational (out-of-role)—is important for simulation training to fulfill its pedagogical potential of training students how to think, perform, and act as professionals."
