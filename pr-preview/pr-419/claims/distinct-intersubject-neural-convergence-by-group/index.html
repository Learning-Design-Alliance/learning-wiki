Evidence¶

Joshua S. Cetron 2020¶

Joshua S. Cetron, Andrew C. Connolly, Solomon G. Diamond, Vicki V. May, James V. Haxby and David J. M. Kraemer. (2020). Using the force: STEM knowledge and experience construct shared neural representations of engineering concepts. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0065-x

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Multivariate intersubject DM correlation analysis across 20,484 surface nodes at fMRI run 1 in N = 31 participants found engineering-student-only convergence (1,863 nodes), novice-only convergence (842 nodes), and overlapping convergence (2,426 nodes). Engineering convergence localized to "motor regions including primary motor cortex (M1)" and ventral PFC; novices converged in more anterior frontal and superior parietal regions.

"Representa- tional convergence among engineering students was notably localized to motor regions including primary motor cortex (M1), portions of ventral prefrontal cortex (PFC), and inferior parietal regions. Related Claims¶

  • By the final fMRI run, group differences in convergent neural representations shrink and are no longer strongly reflective of mechanical category information, consistent with task-specific rather than conceptual learning — related
  • Mechanical category knowledge learned in school is implicitly represented in engineering students' bilateral anterior ventral occipitotemporal cortex, but not significantly in novices — related
  • Visual similarity information peaks in primary visual cortex for both groups with a gradient opposite to mechanical category information, showing mechanical categories are not explained by visual features — related
  • Engineering students significantly outperform novices on a naturalistic free body diagram task measuring Newtonian force knowledge — related
  • Novices improve more than engineering students across FBD task runs, eliminating the group difference by the final run — related
  • Focal node analysis showed advanced learners' central concepts converged with experts' after the game, while novices' remained divergent — related
