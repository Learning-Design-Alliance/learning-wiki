Evidence¶

Colin Lescarret 2022¶

Colin Lescarret, Julien Magnier, Valérie Le Floch, Jean-Christophe Sakdavong, and 2 more. (2022). Do you trust this speaker? The impact of prompting on middle-school students’ consideration of source when watching conflicting videos. Preprint, Research Square. https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-1998147/v1

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Mixed ANOVA on recognition scores in the randomized experiment: no main effect of the instructional video factor, "F(1,253)=0.499, p=.542, η²p=.001". A separate chi-square on convincingness choice also showed no association (χ²(1)=0.228, p=.633, Cramer's V=.029). No effect size beyond these statistics is printed for the null.

"There was no effect of the factor Instructional Video, F(1,253)=0.499, p=.542, η²p=.001"
