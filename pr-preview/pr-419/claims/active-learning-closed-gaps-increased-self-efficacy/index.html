Renninger 2026¶

Renninger, K. A., Barton, L. K., Bautista, G., Garcia-Barrios, M., Ogunyinka, I., & Riley, K. R. (2026). Interest, Self-efficacy, and Belonging in Classroom Learning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10102-7

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Ballen et al. (2017), a study of underrepresented and non-underrepresented minority undergraduates in an entry-level biology course in which "the use of active learning closed gaps in academic performance between the two groups." The review narrates the study without printing its statistics.

"The researchers reported that the use of active learning closed gaps in academic performance between the two groups and that all students reported increases in self-efficacy."
