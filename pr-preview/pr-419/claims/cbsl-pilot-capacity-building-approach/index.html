Descriptive account of the CBSL pilot initiative run by Mathematica and Catalyst:Ed with the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation and nine K-12 education grantees from May 2020 to September 2021. The article states the initiative's goal was to strengthen grantee capacity through four mechanisms; no outcome evaluation is reported.
