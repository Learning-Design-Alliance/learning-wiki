Romain Puech 2026¶

Romain Puech, Dewang Kumar Agarwal, Antonio Santamaría Escobar, Dimitris Bertsimas. (2026). Bespoke: Generating MOOC-Quality Industry-Personalized Lecture Videos at Scale. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26540 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.26540

q2 · i? · design · r2

Qualitative grouping of reviewer free-text comments from the 92-video expert evaluation. The article reports 235 comments on 73 videos, with slide visual quality, layout, and voice the most remarked categories, and quotes complaints about pronunciation, reveal lag, and unused space.

"Reviewers left 235 free-text comments on 73 of 92 videos. Slide visual quality, slide layout, and voice drew the most remarks (31, 30, and 27 comments)."

Voice-related reviewer comments from the same qualitative analysis: reviewers flagged digit-by-digit reading of two-digit numbers, missing pauses, and a playback speed that sounded too fast, though several still judged the voice usable.

"Several videos read two-digit numbers digit by digit (“30” as “three zero, ” “60” as “six zero”), in at least six cases “repeated across the entire video” and “very distracting. ”"
