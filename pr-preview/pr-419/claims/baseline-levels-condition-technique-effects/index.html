Eitel 2026¶

Eitel, A., Kienitz, A., Krebs, M.-C., & Schöne, C. (2026). Advocating the FoRe-Squares Model of Technology-Augmented Instruction: a Reply to Mayer (2025). Educational Psychology Review, 38, 65. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10163-2

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical rationale in the reply, applied to reinterpret prior findings: signaling was not impactful when the coherence technique was already applied, because coherence had already provided enough focus support. The authors cite Kienitz et al. (2023) and Schneider et al. (2021) for such interaction findings.

"the FoRe-Squares model predicts that any instructional technique is likely to produce significant learning gains when it successfully compensates for an otherwise too low level of focus support and/or reward in the instruction."
