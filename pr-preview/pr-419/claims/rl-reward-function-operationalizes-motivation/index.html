q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative review assertion about reinforcement learning agents in educational settings. The review notes that shaping reward schedules can simulate motivational profiles such as valuing immediate progress versus delayed mastery, and that educational RL can treat reward as a composite of performance and affective or motivational reinforcement.

"RL is also one of the most direct ways to operationalize motivational constructs in learning contexts because motivation can be represented in the reward function"
