The review reports, citing Fong Lam et al. (2015), that "emotions, specifically academic emotions, moderate the relationship between belonging and achievement." The reviewed studies' designs are not described in this article.

"Other studies have shown that emotions, specifically academic emotions, moderate the relationship between belonging and achievement (Fong Lam et al., 2015)."
