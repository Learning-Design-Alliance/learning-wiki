Evidence¶

Rhoda Lai 2026¶

Rhoda Lai, Jennifer Southam, Luella Mageean, Sue Roffey, Kelly-Ann Allen. (2026). Associations Between Teacher-Student Relationship Quality and Middle and Secondary School Teachers' Wellbeing: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10107-2

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports, citing Field (2019), that in that earlier review of 21 studies "conflictual relationships were more highly linked to stress and burnout". Field's review defined wellbeing largely as absence of stress, burnout, and poor mental health, which may explain the stronger conflict findings.

"conflictual relationships were more highly linked to stress and burnout, while less evidence was found for the association between relationship closeness and teacher wellbeing"
