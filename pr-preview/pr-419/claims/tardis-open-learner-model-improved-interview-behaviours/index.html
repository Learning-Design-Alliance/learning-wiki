Webb 2021¶

Webb, M. E., Fluck, A., Magenheim, J., Malyn-Smith, J., Waters, J., Deschênes, M., & Zagami, J. (2021). Machine learning for human learners: opportunities, issues, tensions and threats. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09858-2

q2 · i? · review · r2

The article reports a comparison study of the TARDIS project, in which young people at risk of exclusion practised job interviews with machine learning based agent recruiters while an Open Learner Model gave them interactive timelines of their own actions. The study "showed improvement in the quality of participant interview answers". No effect size or sample size is printed.
