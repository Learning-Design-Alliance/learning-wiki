Evidence¶

Damacharla 2018¶

Damacharla, P., Dhakal, P., Stumbo, S., Javaid, A. Y., Ganapathy, S., Malek, D. A., Hodge, D. C., & Devabhaktuni, V. (2018). Effects of Voice-Based Synthetic Assistant on Performance of Emergency Care Provider in Training. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-018-0166-3

q2 · i? · design · r2

Descriptive analysis of VBSA interaction metrics (TP, TN, FP, FN) from 19 experiment-set participants each executing 13 steps in three training sessions, plotted in Fig. 9 (e.g., FP of 2, 3, 2 across Train 1–3). The authors observe "the value of FP is very low"; no statistical test is reported.

"As we can observe from the graph above, the value of FP is very low and suggests that it is highly unlikely that the VBSA will accept any unwanted command, making the developed system reliable."
