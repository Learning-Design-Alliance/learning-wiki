School-level factor synthesis across seven articles coding time on task: longer duration linked to greater gains, particularly for low achievers; Aunio & Mononen (2018), Cary et al. (2020), and Parks & Tortorelli (2021) cited insufficient time for null or weaker results.

"Although the total duration varied across the reviewed studies, findings generally indicated that a longer duration was associated with greater learning gains, especially for low-achieving students"
