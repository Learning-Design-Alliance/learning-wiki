Evidence¶

Zamecnik 2024¶

Zamecnik, A., Kovanović, V., Joksimović, S., Grossmann, G., Ladjal, D., Pardo, A. (2024). The perceptions of task cohesion in collaborative learning teams. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-024-09424-5

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Survey analysis of 194 WIL teams at Weeks 1 and 3 using four Carless and De Paola adapted items, weighted Kappa grouping at 0.33, and Kruskal–Wallis testing: significant differences (p < 0.0001) for each of the four items. Team teams with similar perceptions reported, on average, higher task cohesion scores. Survey reliability was "alpha 0.93 (Week 1) and 0.94 (Week 3)".

"Looking at the average cohe- sion scores of the two groups, we see that teams with similar perceptions reported, on aver- age, higher task cohesion scores than teams with different team cohesion perceptions."
