Evidence¶

Iman YeckehZaare & Paul Resnick 2025¶

Iman YeckehZaare & Paul Resnick. (2025). Counting days is a spacing incentive that unlocks the potential of low GPA students. npj Science of Learning 10:35. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00322-5

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Within-class randomized experiment with 143 analyzed students in an introductory programming course; counting days group mean final exam score was "85.3% vs. 81.7% in the counting questions condition". Table 2 reports a significant two-sample t-test for Final Exam Score (1.988*, p < 0.05).

"The mean final exam score in the counting days treatment was 85.3% vs. 81.7% in the counting questions condition."

Iman YeckehZaare & Paul Resnick 2025 (2)¶

Iman YeckehZaare & Paul Resnick. (2025). Counting days is a spacing incentive that unlocks the potential of low GPA students. npj Science of Learning 10:35. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00322-5

q3 · i? · causal · r2

The same within-class experiment reports that "these differences are statistically signiﬁcant" under both parametric and non-parametric tests; the printed t statistic for Final Exam Score is 1.988 (p < 0.05). No standardized effect size is printed.

"Table 2 conﬁrms that these differences are statistically signiﬁcant, according to both parametric t-tests and non-parametric Wilcoxon rank- sum (Mann-Whitney) tests."

  • The counting days treatment's benefit for exam scores is mediated almost entirely by the number of days practiced, not the number of questions — related
