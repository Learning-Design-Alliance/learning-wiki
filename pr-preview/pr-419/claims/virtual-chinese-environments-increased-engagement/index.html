Evidence¶

Mei Si 2015¶

Mei Si. (2015). A Virtual Space for Children to Meet and Practice Chinese. Int J Artif Intell Educ 25:271–290. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-014-0035-7

q2 · i? · design · r2

Month-long deployment at Bethel Tutoring Center with twenty children aged 6–8; evaluation data were obtained through the teachers' subjective reports. Table 1 estimates engagement rose from "60–70 % of the class" to "80 % of the class". No effect size or test is reported.

"In addition, more students were engaged in the practices— they actively participated in the practice."
