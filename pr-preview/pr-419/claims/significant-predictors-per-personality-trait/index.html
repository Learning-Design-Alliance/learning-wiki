Evidence¶

Wen Wu 2019¶

Wen Wu, Li Chen, Qingchang Yang, You Li. (2019). Inferring Students' Personality from Their Communication Behavior in Web-based Learning Systems. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-018-00173-9

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Significance of predictors entering the LASSO model was tested via the covariance test at p<0.05. For Openness to Experience, predictors included number of question marks in chat messages (p=0.0002), total keyboard behavior, and tentative words in forum messages.

"Table 6 lists the significant predictors of Big-Five personality traits, where the significance (p-value) is determined via the covariance test for LASSO (Lockhart et al. 2014)."
