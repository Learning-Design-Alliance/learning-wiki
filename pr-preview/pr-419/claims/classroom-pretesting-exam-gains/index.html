q3 · i? · review · r3

Narrative review of Soderstrom and Bjork (2023), a 10-week undergraduate research methods course with pen-and-paper pretests and no feedback; the review reports students "performed 8–9% better on questions addressing content that had been previously pretested".

"On a high-stakes final exam administered at the end of the course, students performed 8–9% better on questions addressing content that had been previously pretested"
