Digital Promise and the Institute for Applied Neuroscience 2019¶

Digital Promise and the Institute for Applied Neuroscience. (2019). Introduction to the Learning Sciences: 10 Key Insights & Practices for Educators. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/d5550311-8f60-4d0d-9d4d-14821054e0e8

q2 · i? · review · r1

The synthesis reports neuroscience findings that "aerobic exercise can transiently improve the brain’s plasticity" and increase hippocampal volume, that sleep solidifies learning, and that basic nutrition matters for brain health.

"Aerobic exercise can transiently improve the brain’s plasticity and can increase hippocampal volume (a key part of the brain involved in learning new information). Sleep is critical for solidifying learning from the day"
