Evidence¶

Research Abstracts 1954¶

Research Abstracts. (1954). https://eric.ed.gov/?q=instructional+film+research+program

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Two conditions of light were compared: daylight viewing and darkened room, with 1100 trainees tested on the assembly task. The authors themselves qualify the finding's scope to extreme seating angles and distances.

"Although the experiment indicated that a high level of room illumination is detrimental to efficient learning, this was thought to be true only if a large number of learners are seated at extreme angles to, or distance from, the screen."
