Huang-Yao Hong 2025¶

Huang-Yao Hong, Mei-Ju Chen, Bodong Chen, Chao-Yu Guo, Li-Ting Tseng (2025). Sense of promisingness: towards creative expertise through knowledge-building discourse in online graduate education. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10483-0

Stepwise regression analysis (Table 8) relating discourse-move frequencies to knowing-why for the 32 participants; Model 1 reports β = 0.707, R² = 0.483, and Model 2 R² = 0.604.

"integration-oriented discourse alone can explain 48.3% of the variance in the development of knowing-why as a more essential type of knowing (see Model 1), but with sharing-oriented discourse being further added, the new model can help explain additional 12.1%"
