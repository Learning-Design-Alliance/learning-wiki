Evidence¶

Kailea Saplan · Sam Abramovich · Peter Wardrip 2024¶

Kailea Saplan · Sam Abramovich · Peter Wardrip. (2024). Analyzing properties of success for assessment development in maker-based learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10286-1

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Authors' interpretation of interview examples: example A described skills and mindset changes, while examples B and C described in-the-moment aha moments and pride in made work. The alignment with Sfard's metaphors is the authors' scholarly reading, not a tested result.

"Drawing from our expertise as scholars, we noticed that these two ways of demonstrating success aligned Sfard's two metaphors for learning, the acquisition metaphor and the participation metaphor (Sfard, 1998)."
