Winslow Burleson & Armanda Lewis 2016¶

Winslow Burleson & Armanda Lewis. (2016). Optimists' Creed: Brave New Cyberlearning, Evolving Utopias (Circa 2041). Int J Artif Intell Educ 26:796–808. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-016-0096-x

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

The article's historical/interpretive account of the field's trajectory, merging two CRA Grand Challenges ('Providing a Teacher for Every Learner' and 'A Team of One's Own') into a multifaceted initiative. This is the authors' characterization of field-level change, not a measured comparison.

"Today, a new gold standard has emerged. It is based on the broad appreciation of the transformative potential and transdisciplinary depth and breadth of both individuals and teams, working in dynamic, co-located, and distributed configurations"
