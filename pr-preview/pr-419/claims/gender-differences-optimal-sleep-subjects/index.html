Wang 2025¶

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Gender-specific quadratic OLS models (Table 5) with individual-level covariates, run separately for male and female samples across all five subjects, showing significant inverted U-shapes for both groups with differing vertex values.

"For most subjects, the estimated optimal sleep duration is slightly longer among girls than boys. In contrast, for mathematics, boys show a higher estimated optimal sleep duration than girls"

Related Claims¶

  • Sleep duration shows an inverted U-shaped association with academic performance in all five subjects, with a peak around 8 hours of nightly sleep — a broader claim this one bears on
  • Optimal sleep duration varies by subject: about 8–9 hours for mathematics and 7–8 hours for Chinese, English, science, and arts — related
  • Sex-specific subgroup models show the same functional forms for boys and girls, though cubic coefficients for boys were not significant after correction — related
  • Sleep duration contributes more to variance in Chinese, mathematics, and science performance than to English and arts, but less than socioeconomic status in every subject — related
