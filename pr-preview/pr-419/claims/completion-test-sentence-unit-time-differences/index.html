Evidence¶

Walter R. Miles and Julian E. Butterworth 1912¶

Walter R. Miles and Julian E. Butterworth. (1912). A Tentative Standardization of a Completion Test. The Journal of Educational Psychology, pp. 329-336. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22A+Tentative+Standardization+of+a+Completion+Test%22

q2 · i? · design · r2

Timed laboratory administration of the completion test to 25 students or faculty at the State University of Iowa yielded per-unit time percentages (unit 1: 12.8%; unit 11: 3.4%), showing "the average of the times of the twenty-five subjects" differed markedly across units. Mean variations were large, the authors noting the need for more observers.

"There are eleven of these sentence units in this test, and the average of the times of the twenty-five subjects necessary to complete each unit are as follows, in percentages of the time required for the whole test"
