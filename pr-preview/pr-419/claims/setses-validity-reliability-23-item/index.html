Evidence¶

Serkan Perkmen 2026¶

Serkan Perkmen, Sara Dexter. (2026). From sources of self-efficacy to technology integration intentions: a new social cognitive model. Educational technology research and development. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10623-0

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Psychometric analysis of the new SETSES with 144 Turkish preservice teachers; after confirmatory factor analysis removed six items, the 23-item scale showed sub-scale alphas of 0.74–0.84 and "The Cronbach's alpha of the overall scale was found to be 0.90". AVE and CR values supported convergent validity.

"The Cronbach's alpha values for the 23-item scale were found to be 0.80 for mastery experiences, 0.74 for vicarious information, 0.76 for verbal persuasion and 0.84 for the emotional arousal. These values showed that the sub-scales had acceptable levels of reliability. The Cronbach's alpha of the overall scale was found to be 0.90, indicating a high reliability."

Serkan Perkmen 2026 (2)¶

Serkan Perkmen, Sara Dexter. (2026). From sources of self-efficacy to technology integration intentions: a new social cognitive model. Educational technology research and development. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10623-0

q2 · i? · associational · r2

HTMT analysis of SETSES sub-scales in the same 144-participant sample showed mastery experiences were "a distinct construct from verbal persuasion but not from vicarious information", so discriminant validity was only partially established.

"The HTMT value between mastery experiences and vicarious experience was found to be 0.91, which was slightly higher than the threshold value of 0.90. However, the HTMT between mastery experiences and verbal persuasion was found to 0.86, which was less than the threshold value of 0.90."
