Evidence¶

Fei Gao 2023¶

Fei Gao, Lin Hua, Paulo Armada-da-Silva, Juan Zhang, Defeng Li, Zhiyi Chen, Chengwen Wang, Meng Du, Zhen Yuan. (2023). Shared and distinct neural correlates of first and second language morphological processing in bilingual brain. npj Science of Learning 8:33. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00184-9

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Channel-wise two-way repeated-measures ANOVA on fNIRS beta values from 30 bilinguals found three channels with a significant language main effect, including Channel #1 (partial η2 = 0.1922) and Channel #3 (partial η2 = 0.224244) overlapping the frontopolar area, with activation increasing from English to Chinese tasks.

"Channel #19 (47.83% over- lapping with Pre-Motor and Supplementary Motor Cortex): F(1,29) = 8.982393, p = 0.00554, partial η2 = 0.236488] showed signiﬁcant main effect of language ( ps < 0.05), such that hemodynamic responses signi ﬁcantly increased from English tasks to Chinese ones."

Fei Gao 2023 (2)¶

Fei Gao, Lin Hua, Paulo Armada-da-Silva, Juan Zhang, Defeng Li, Zhiyi Chen, Chengwen Wang, Meng Du, Zhen Yuan. (2023). Shared and distinct neural correlates of first and second language morphological processing in bilingual brain. npj Science of Learning 8:33. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00184-9

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Paired comparison of the morphological-minus-semantic activation difference between languages found only a marginal difference in Channel #10 (t(29) = 1.88, p = 0.07), with the Chinese morphological effect showing enhanced activation relative to English.

"Only a marginal signi ﬁcant difference was identiﬁed from Channel #10 (80% overlapping with DLPFC), t(29) = 1.88, p = 0.07, where Chinese morphological effect showed enhanced activation than English."

  • fNIRS reveals a neural dissociation between morphological and semantic priming in the left fronto-temporal network of bilinguals — related
  • In adult Chinese-English bilinguals, Chinese items are recognized faster and more accurately than English items, and morphologically primed items faster than semantically primed items — related
