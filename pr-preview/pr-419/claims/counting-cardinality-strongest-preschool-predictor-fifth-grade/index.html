Evidence¶

Nguyen 2016¶

Nguyen, T., Watts, T. W., Duncan, G. J., Clements, D. H., Sarama, J. S., Wolfe, C., & Spitler, M. E. (2016). Which Preschool Mathematics Competencies Are Most Predictive of Fifth Grade Achievement? Early Childhood Research Quarterly. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecresq.2016.02.003

q2 · i3 · associational · r3

Longitudinal OLS regression analysis of 781 children from the TRIAD evaluation, controlling for child and family characteristics, treatment status, site, and preschool teacher fixed effects. Standardized coefficients show counting and cardinality (β = 0.42) ahead of geometry (β = 0.13) and patterning (β = 0.10).

"Counting and cardinality competencies are the strongest predictors of later math achievement (β = 0.42, SE = 0.04, p < 0.001), followed by geometry (β = 0.13, SE = 0.04, p < 0.001), and patterning (β = 0.10, SE = 0.03, p < 0.01)."
