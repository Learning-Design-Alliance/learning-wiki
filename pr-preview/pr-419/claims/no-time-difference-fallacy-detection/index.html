Felix Sachs 1913¶

Felix Sachs. (1913). Preliminary Study of the Relative Efficiency of Inductive and Deductive Teaching of Logical Reasoning. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.jstor.org/journal/jeducpsyc

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Timing was recorded from reading each test example to the end of the observer's report, though observers were told time was incidental. The class group's frequent failure even to attempt naming would account for any real difference in times. No statistical test is reported.

"From the record of time taken in solving the examples no conclusions can be drawn; nor, indeed, was it contemplated as a matter of particular import. The average time per example was 2.21 min. for the tutored and 2.05 min. for the class group."
