Cohort-control regression analysis of N = 2740 first graders (Cohort 1 pre-pandemic, Cohort 2 during pandemic) with all pretest measures as covariates, cluster-robust SEs, and FIML for missing data. The article reports "no signiﬁcant negative effects" on reading or math achievement; 95% CIs implied small negative to small positive effects were compatible with the data.
