Evidence¶

Patrick C. Kyllonen & Susanne P. Lajoie 2003¶

Patrick C. Kyllonen & Susanne P. Lajoie (2003). Reassessing Aptitude: Introduction to a Special Issue in Honor of Richard E. Snow. Educational Psychologist, 38(2), 79–83. https://doi.org/10.1207/S15326985EP3802_2

q2 · i? · design · r2

The article summarizes Lajoie's (2003) study, based on her doctoral dissertation under Snow, in which verbal protocols from experts and novices constructing a missing side view informed a model and a computer-based learning environment. The summary reports a significant pre–post gain; no effect size is printed.

"students receiving the approximately hour-long interactive computer tutorial showed a significant pre–post performance gain, suggesting the importance of the spatial process instruction on the orthographic projection task"
