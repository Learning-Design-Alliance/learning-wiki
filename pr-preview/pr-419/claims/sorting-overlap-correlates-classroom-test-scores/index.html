Evidence¶

Chen 2025¶

Chen, X., Wang, W., Peng, C., Guo, W., & Clariana, R. B. (2025). An exploratory study of a new concept macrostructure measure when learning history in middle school. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10536-4

q3 · i3 · associational · r1

Study 1 correlational analysis of 255 grade 7 students who completed a computer sorting task of 43 Qin Dynasty topic terms one month after the Unit 3 exam. Pearson correlations showed "correlation coefficient between overlaps and score on Unit 3 was r = 0.37, p <.001" and r = 0.25 with the Qin test.

"The correlation coefficient between overlaps and score on Qin was r = 0.25, p <.001, between scores on Qin and Unit 3 was r = 0.58, p <.001, and most interestingly, correlation coefficient between overlaps and score on Unit 3 was r = 0.37, p <.001."
