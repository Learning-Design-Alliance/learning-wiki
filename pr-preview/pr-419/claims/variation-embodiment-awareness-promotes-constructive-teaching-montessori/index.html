Evidence¶

Ahlquist 2020¶

Ahlquist, E.-M. T., & Gynther, P. (2020). Teaching in the Montessori Classroom: Investigating Variation Theory and Embodiment as a Foundation of Teachers' Development. Journal of Montessori Research, 6(1). https://journals.ku.dk/jmr doi:10.17161/jomr.v6i1.12051

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Qualitative single-case study with a formative intervention in a Swedish Montessori class with 7- and 8-year-olds, analyzed with Epistemological Move Analysis across five observed sessions. The authors report the teacher's theoretical awareness "seemed to help her successfully use epistemological moves in a more encouraging and constructive way."

"this teacher's awareness of why a specific object of learning was to be treated in accordance with variation theory and embodiment seemed to help her successfully use epistemological moves in a more encouraging and constructive way."
