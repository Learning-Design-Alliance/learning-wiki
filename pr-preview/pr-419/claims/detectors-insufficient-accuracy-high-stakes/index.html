Evidence¶

Victor Angelier 2026¶

Victor Angelier. (2026). The pitfalls of AI detection in academic writing: bias, false positives, and the need for inclusive assessment. AI and Ethics. https://doi.org/10.1007/s43681-026-01376-w

q3 · i? · review · r3

The review reports the Weber-Wulff et al. independent evaluation across "multiple human-, machine-, and manipulated-text conditions"; errors occurred in both directions and the overall error pattern was biased towards classifying output as human-written. No effect sizes are printed.

"Testing fourteen detection tools across multiple human-, machine-, and manipulated-text conditions, the authors concluded that the tools were neither sufficiently accurate nor sufficiently reliable for high-stakes use."
