Evidence¶

Anouk ten Peze 2024¶

Anouk ten Peze, Tanja Janssen, Gert Rijlaarsdam, Daphne van Weijen. (2024). Instruction in creative and argumentative writing: transfer and crossover effects on writing process and text quality. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-023-09647-3

q2 · i? · causal · r2

The appreciation analysis used a seven-part student questionnaire (five-point scale) analyzed with repeated measures, group as between-subjects factor. It found participants "generally positive about the planned elements in the instructional design" with only two below-neutral items.

"Participants in both panels were generally positive about the planned elements in the instructional design, as only two items scored below neutral (see Online Resource 1)."
