Evidence¶

K. Paige Harden 2020¶

K. Paige Harden, Benjamin W. Domingue, Daniel W. Belsky, Jason D. Boardman, Robert Crosnoe, Margherita Malanchini, Michel Nivard, Elliot M. Tucker-Drob and Kathleen Mullan Harris. (2020). Genetic associations with mathematics tracking and persistence in secondary school. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0060-2

q3 · i1 · associational · r2

Year-by-year logistic analyses (Supplementary Table 5) of students enrolled in math in grades 9-11 predicting next-year enrollment, reported in Results. Odds ratios OR = 1.26 (grade 10), 1.15 (grade 11), and 1.13 (grade 12) show the PGS added "novel information above and beyond what could be observed from students'performance in math class."

"After controlling for these covariates, a 1-SD increase in the education-PGS was still associated with 1.26 times greater odds of taking a math class in 10th-grade (95% CI = [1.05–1.56]), 1.15 times greater odds in 11th-grade"
