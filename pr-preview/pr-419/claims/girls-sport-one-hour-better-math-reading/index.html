Evidence¶

Bidzan-Bluma Ilona 2018¶

Bidzan-Bluma Ilona, Lipowska Małgorzata. (2018). Physical Activity and Cognitive Functioning of Children: A Systematic Review. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph15040800

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The review attributes this finding to Carlson et al.: sport-participating girls had "significantly better results in math and reading" than non-participating girls, with no such relationship for boys. No effect size is printed.

"Research by Carlson et al. [49] showed that girls who engage in sport for at least one hour per week had significantly better results in math and reading than girls who did not do at least one hour of sport. This relationship was not found to hold for boys in this study."
