q2 i? For imperfectly correlated measures of the same trait, the mean of every array of y values associated with a single value of x is nearer to the mean of all y values than is the value of y that truly corresponds to that x. → Arthur S. Otis 1922

q2 i? The regression line indicates the theoretical mean of associated second-form scores for a first-form score, including the error in that first score, so it does not show the correspondence between true scores. → Arthur S. Otis 1922

Evidence¶

Arthur S. Otis 1922¶

Arthur S. Otis. (1922). The Method for Finding the Correspondence Between Scores in Two Tests. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0075112

q2 · i?

A hypothetical thermometry demonstration with deliberate reading errors: two readers misread one scale half the time, one-fourth too high and one-fourth too low, and the plotted array means, in the article's words, "tend to be nearer to the mean" than the true corresponding values. No empirical data are involved.

"The mean value of Centigrade readings found associated with each of the Fahrenheit readings tend to be nearer to the mean (10°) of all the Centigrade readings than are the Centigrade values to which these Fahrenheit readings correspond."
