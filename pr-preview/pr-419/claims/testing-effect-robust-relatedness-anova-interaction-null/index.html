Evidence¶

Gupta 2026¶

Gupta, M.W., Pan, S.C., & Rickard, T.C. (2026). Semantic relatedness and the efficacy of retrieval practice. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00416-8

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Mixed-factors ANOVA on final test proportion correct from the laboratory cued-recall experiment (231 UCSD participants analyzed; training task within, relatedness between). The article reports "a significant main effect of Relatedness" with F(1, 229) = 126.6 and a non-significant interaction with p = 0.74.

"a significant main effect of Relatedness, F(1, 229) = 126.6, p < 0.0001, 𝜂p2 = 0.36, BF10 = 3.01e20, and a non-significant interaction, F(1, 229) = 0.108, p = 0.74, 𝜂p2 = 0.0005, BF01 = 6.86"

Gupta 2026 (2)¶

Gupta, M.W., Pan, S.C., & Rickard, T.C. (2026). Semantic relatedness and the efficacy of retrieval practice. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00416-8

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Same mixed-factors ANOVA on final test proportion correct. The article reports "a significant (at α = .05) main effect of Training Task" with F(1, 229) = 514.1 and BF10 = 5.91e56, confirming the testing effect in both relatedness groups.
