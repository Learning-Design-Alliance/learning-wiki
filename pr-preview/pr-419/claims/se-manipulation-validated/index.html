Evidence¶

Betty Tärning 2019¶

Betty Tärning, Annika Silvervarg, Agneta Gulz, Magnus Haake. (2019). Instructing a Teachable Agent with Low or High Self-Efficacy – Does Similarity Attract? International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-018-0167-2

q3 · i3 · causal · r?

Manipulation check on the full dataset of 142 students using the questionnaire item on Lo's confidence in math (1–5 scale). Median rating was 2 for the low-SE tutee vs. 4 for the high-SE tutee, "r = .38", supporting the intended manipulation.
