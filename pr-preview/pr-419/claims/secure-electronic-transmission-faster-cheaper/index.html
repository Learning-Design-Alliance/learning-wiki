Evidence¶

David W Chadwick 1999¶

David W Chadwick, Rana Tassabehji and Andrew Young. (1999). Experiences of Using a Public Key Infrastructure for the Preparation of Examination Papers. IT Institute, University of Salford. http://www.salford.ac.uk/its024/X500.htm

q1 · i? · design · r2

Time-and-cost comparison of the traditional versus secure electronic processes for ITI examination papers; no comparison was possible for CMS. The article reports the 4-hour, zero-cost result "with only one examination paper out of the 12 selected", with originator and external examiner both outside the UK at the time.

"In the secure electronic case, the whole transition time took approximately 4 hours with no additional costs. This occurred with only one examination paper out of the 12 selected."
