Evidence¶

Park 2025¶

Park, A.T., Colantonio, J., Delgado Reyes, L., Sharp, S.D.S., Koepp, A.E., Bonawitz, E., & Mackey, A.P. (2025). Question asking practice fosters aspects of curiosity in science content in young children. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00384-5

q2 · i? · causal · r3

Within-condition analysis of transcribed lessons: a 1-tailed paired t-test on first vs last lesson relevant questions (t(40) = 2.36, p = .001); a Poisson mixed-model slope analysis found no significant linear trend (b = 0.01, p = .395), suggesting non-linear improvement.

"Children in the question-asking condition asked an average of 5.51 relevant questions (SD = 3.68) during their first lesson and 7.43 questions during their last lesson (SD = 5.30), showing a significant increase"
