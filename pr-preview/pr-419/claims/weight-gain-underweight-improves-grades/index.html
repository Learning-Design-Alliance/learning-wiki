Ishihara 2020¶

Ishihara, T., Nakajima, T., Yamatsu, K., Okita, K., Sagawa, M. & Morita, N. (2020). Longitudinal relationship of favorable weight change to academic performance in children. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0063-z

q3 · i2 · associational · r2

Simple slope analysis from the main hierarchical regression in the 2-year longitudinal study of 197 children; the quote shows the slope at baseline BMI = mean − 1 SD was " B = 0.40" with β = 0.31, p = 0.03, a positive relationship to grade points.
