Anatolia Batruch 2026¶

Anatolia Batruch, Nicolas Sommet, Eva G.T. Green. (2026). Teachers' immigration attitudes and students' performance. Learning and Instruction 102 (2026) 102303. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102303

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Robustness analyses re-estimated the fixed-effects interaction using multilevel models, double-demeaned models, stratified analyses, and sampling weights (Fay's method, senate weights). One weighted mathematics model with controls was marginal (p < .10); MICE models confirmed the first-generation interaction in all three domains.
