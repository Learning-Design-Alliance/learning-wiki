q2 · i? · design · r3

Follow-up survey distributed 2 months after sessions; 563 participants contacted, 21% responded (N = 124; 57 males, 67 females), seven items on a 3-point Likert scale analyzed with descriptive statistics (Fig. 8). The article reports "The highest score (1.21)" for general awareness; only descriptive statistics are printed.

"The highest score (1.21) is for the general item “I am more aware about personal data”; a slightly lower impact can be measured for the more specific items, which in any case received a positive level of agreement."
