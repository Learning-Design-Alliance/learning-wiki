Evidence¶

Maass 2016¶

Maass, J.K. and Pavlik Jr., P.I. (2016). Modeling the Influence of Format and Depth during Effortful Retrieval Practice. Proceedings of the 9th International Conference on Educational Data Mining. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED592715

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Exploratory affect modeling in the PFA-based regression: six affects were rated on 5-point Likert items before and after practice; only confusion improved model fit. The authors state the affect work "was meant to serve only as an exploration of affect during retrieval practice".

"Unlike previous work by Baker, et. al., [2] we did not find any significant impact of frustration or boredom"
