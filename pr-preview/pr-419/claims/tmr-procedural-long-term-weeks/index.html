Evidence¶

Julia Carbone & Susanne Diekelmann 2024¶

Julia Carbone & Susanne Diekelmann. (2024). An update on recent advances in targeted memory reactivation during sleep. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00244-8

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review attributes to Rakowska and colleagues a bimanual serial reaction time task with sequences cued during NREM sleep. The review reports "better performance for the cued compared to the uncued sequence after 10 days", absent at 24 h and gone by 6-8 weeks; no effect size printed.

"Interestingly, while no effect of TMR was observed after 24 h, subjects showed better performance for the cued compared to the uncued sequence after 10 days, with this effect vanishing after another 6 –8 weeks 12."
