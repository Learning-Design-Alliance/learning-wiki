Guido Makransky 2025¶

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

In the pre-registered Study 1 between-subjects experiment (175 university students), the immediate post-test ANOVA "revealed no significant differences between the three conditions, F(2, 172) = 1.886, p = 0.155, η2 = 0.021"; equivalence was not tested.
