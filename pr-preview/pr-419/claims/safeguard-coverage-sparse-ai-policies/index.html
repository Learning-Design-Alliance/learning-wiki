Safeguard-coverage coding across the 30-institution corpus, counting eight safeguard indicators. The audit found packages contained "a mean of 2.75 of 8 possible safeguards," with privacy at 67% and, per the text, workload/proportionality at 4%.

"Packages contained a mean of 2.75 of 8 possible safeguards. The most visible categories were privacy at 67%; detection caution at 47%; accessibility at 42%; appeal or due process at 33%; equity of tool access at 32%"
