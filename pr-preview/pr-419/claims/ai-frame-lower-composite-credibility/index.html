Evidence¶

Mutlu Cukurova & Rosemary Luckin & Carmel Kent 2020¶

Mutlu Cukurova & Rosemary Luckin & Carmel Kent. (2020). Impact of an Artificial Intelligence Research Frame on the Perceived Credibility of Educational Research Evidence. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-019-00188-w

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Kruskal-Wallis test on the composite credibility variable in the online framing experiment; post hoc comparisons found significant differences between AI and neuroscience (p = .001) and between AI and educational psychology (p = .001).

"Median credibility scores were statistically significantly different between groups,H(2) = 18.463,p =0 . 0 0 0 . Pairwise comparisons were performed using Dunn ’s( 1964) procedure with a Bonferroni correction for multiple comparisons."
