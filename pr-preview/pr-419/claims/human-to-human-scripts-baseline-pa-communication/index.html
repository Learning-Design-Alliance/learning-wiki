Evidence¶

Sikström 2024¶

Sikström, P., Valentini, C., Sivunen, A., & Kärkkäinen, T. (2024). Pedagogical Agents Communicating and Scaffolding Students' Learning : High School Teachers' and Students' Perspectives. Computers and Education, 222, Article 105140. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2024.105140

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews with eleven teachers and five student focus groups (sixteen students) in Finland, autumn 2022–winter 2023. The analysis found that "teacher's communication is the baseline teachers and students use" for judging PA communication.

"This study's results show that teachers and students apply teachers' instructional communication behavior to PA communication, and teacher's communication is the baseline teachers and students use to assess what communicative behaviors PAs should perform."
