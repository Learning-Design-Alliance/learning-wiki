Education Tech Research Dev, 70, 205–230. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10072-x

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Exploratory case study using formative research method with eight first-grade students, collecting multimodal audio and video data along with observational, interview, and questionnaire data. Results showed "high levels of engagement, less attention focused on the screen (projected wall), and more physical activity."
