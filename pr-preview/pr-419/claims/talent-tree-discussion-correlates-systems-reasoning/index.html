Constance Steinkuehler 2008¶

Constance Steinkuehler, Sean Duncan. (2008). Scientific Habits of Mind in Virtual Worlds. J Sci Educ Technol. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10956-008-9120-8

q2 · i3 · associational · r2

Correlational analysis relating scientific habits of mind codes to WoW content codes found the relationship named in the quote: "systems-based reasoning (and its concomitant ''understanding feedback'') and discussion of the priest ''talent tree''" at r = 0.48 and 0.42. Talent-tree allocation poses a limited-resource optimization problem across variables with mathematical relationships to avatar characteristics.
