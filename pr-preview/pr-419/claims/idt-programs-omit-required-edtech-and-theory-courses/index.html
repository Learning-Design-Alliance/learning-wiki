Evidence¶

West 2017¶

West, R. E., Thomas, R. A., Bodily, R., & Wright, C. (2017). An analysis of instructional design and technology departments. Education Tech Research Dev, 65, 869-888. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-016-9490-1

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Curriculum analysis of required courses across the five departments at master's and PhD levels. The authors report that "no programs were observed to require any courses in educational technologies" at either level, though many were offered as electives.

"Perhaps surprisingly, no programs were observed to require any courses in educational technologies at either the M.S or PhD level, even though many such courses were offered as electives."
