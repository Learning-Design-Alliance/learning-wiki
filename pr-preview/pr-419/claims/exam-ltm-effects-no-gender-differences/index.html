Evidence¶

P. Lopes da Cunha 2018¶

P. Lopes da Cunha, D. Ramirez Butavand, L. B. Chisari, F. Ballarini and H. Viola (2018). Exams at classroom have bidirectional effects on the long-term memory of an unrelated graphical task. npj Science of Learning 3:19. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0036-7

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Two-way ANOVA (control type CTRw/CTRs × gender M/F) on Memory Index showed only a main effect of control type; gender and interaction effects were non-significant. Exam-induced increments and decrements were also similar for M and F students (e.g., +1 h weak: exam F(1,56) = 11.89, p < 0.01; gender p = 0.84).

"We found a signi ﬁcant main effect of control type, F(1,258) = 57.13, p < 0.0001, but no main effect of gender, F(1, 258) = 0.02, p = 0.88, neither signi ﬁcant interaction, F(1, 258) = 1.97, p = 0.16."
