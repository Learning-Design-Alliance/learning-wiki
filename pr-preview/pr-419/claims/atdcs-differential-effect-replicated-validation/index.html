Maceira-Elvira 2024¶

Maceira-Elvira, P., Popa, T., Schmid, A.-C., Cadic-Melchior, A., Müller, H., & Schaer, R. (2024). Native learning ability and not age determines the effect of brain stimulation on motor skill acquisition. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00278-y

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Validation dataset (randomized, placebo-controlled, double-blinded study of 20 middle-aged and 20 older adults trained for ten days): "The results con ﬁrm the differential effect of stimulation observed in the Modeling dataset, in which atDCS beneﬁts suboptimal learners only". The detriment in optimal learners was less apparent.
