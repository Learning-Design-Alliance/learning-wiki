Related Claims¶

  • Reflexivity, perspective taking, analogical reasoning, and tolerance of ambiguity and uncertainty each positively relate to knowledge integration in inter- and transdisciplinary work — a broader claim this one bears on
  • The competency–knowledge integration relationships differ between scientific and economic samples: reflexivity is significant only in the scientific sample, while tolerance of ambiguity and uncertainty and perspective taking are significant only in the economic sample — related
  • Expertise positively predicts knowledge integration, and the four competencies remain positively related to knowledge integration at different levels of expertise — related
  • Experts do not exhibit higher levels of reflexivity than novices, consistent with reflexivity as a meta-cognitive competence — related
  • Uncertainty in challenging tasks was linked to meta-reflexive deliberation about improving one's own learning — related
  • Epistemological beliefs relate to tolerance of uncertainty: evaluativist approaches pair better with uncertainty in problem-solving than absolutist ones — related
