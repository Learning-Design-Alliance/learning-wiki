Newmann 1998¶

Newmann, F. M., Lopez, G., & Bryk, A. S. (1998). The Quality of Intellectual Work in Chicago Schools: A Baseline Report. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/quality-intellectual-work-chicago-schools-baseline-report

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Analysis of scored assignments shown in Figure 11 comparing subjects and grade levels. The report also notes that within mathematics, grade six had a higher percentage in the top two categories than grades three and eight, while in writing grade eight led, followed by grade six, with grade three lowest.

"Compared with third grade, sixth and eighth grade tended to show a higher proportion of both writing and mathematics assignments in the top two categories of moderate and extensive challenge; compared to mathematics, writing assignments made more challenging demands in all three grades."
