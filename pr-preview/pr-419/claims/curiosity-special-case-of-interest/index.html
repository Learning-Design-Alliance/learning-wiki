Evidence¶

Pekrun 2019¶

Pekrun, R. (2019). The Murky Distinction Between Curiosity and Interest: State of the Art and Future Prospects. Educational Psychology Review, 31, 905–914. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-019-09512-1

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical argument in the commentary, derived from the author's common-core definitions rather than from data. The article states that curiosity is a state of engaging with a specific object, namely an information gap, so the relation is a part-whole relationship.

"From common core definitions, it follows that curiosity and interest are neither the same nor completely disjunct. Rather, it follows that they are overlapping."
