Evidence¶

Marcus 1998¶

Marcus, N. (1998). Understanding Instructions. PhD thesis, University of New South Wales. https://hdl.handle.net/1959.4/70247

q2 · i? · associational · r1

The thesis reports, citing Chi, Feltovich, and Glaser (1981), a study in which eight expert (PhD) and eight novice (undergraduate) physicists sorted 24 physics problems by similarity of solutions. Experts categorised by principles such as conservation of energy or Newton's Second Law.

"Novices tended to group problems together according to surface features or common objects included in the problem description."
