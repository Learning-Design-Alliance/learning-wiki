Evidence¶

Andri Ioannou 2021¶

Andri Ioannou. (2021). Mobile game-based learning in the era of “shifting to digital”. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-09969-4

q1 · i? · theoretical · r1

The editorial's own assessment of the field's evidence base, stating mobile technologies "allow for just-in-time and just-in-place learning" while effects on engagement and achievement await documentation. The article offers no data for this assertion.

"the use of smartphones, tablets, sensors, games (e.g., location-based games), allow for just-in-time and just-in-place learning however, the important effect of mobile game-based learning on learners’ engagement and achievement is still to be documented in research."
