Descriptive policy analysis in the introduction: some states "have passed laws requiring that African American history be taught in public schools," others require Indigenous community histories, and some include LGBTQ experiences. Terminology varies (ethnic studies, multicultural education, diversity studies).

"Over the years, as states have sought to incorporate more inclusive and relevant academic standards and curriculum for K–12 students, they have interpreted ethnic studies in both broad and narrow terms."
