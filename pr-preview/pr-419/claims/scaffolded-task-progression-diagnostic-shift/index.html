q2 · i? · design · r2

Thematic analysis of interviews and video recordings across the three task types: behavior "changed from "completing" to "diagnosing"", with students pointing to suspicious segments and making small verifiable edits; the stance emerged only after earlier tasks built block literacy.

"As students moved to fixing incorrect blocks (intermediate), their behavior changed from "completing" to "diagnosing. " Students began to inspect programs as artifacts that could contain specific faults and to articulate what might be wrong before editing."
