Evidence¶

Yang Fu 2024¶

Yang Fu, Xiaohui Yan, Jiaqi Mao, Haibin Su & Fan Cao. (2024). Abnormal brain activation during speech perception and production in children and adults with reading difficulty. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00266-2

q3 · i3 · associational · r2

Pearson correlations between channel activation and phonological awareness within RD groups. The quote reports the printed correlation "r = −0.629, pFDR = 0.084" for children with RD, marginal after FDR correction; Steiger's Z test showed the child and adult correlations differed (z = 2.90, p = 0.004).

"A negative correlation was found between the activation of CH37 during perception of Spanish syllables with high similarity to Chinese and pseudoword rhyming judgment (r = −0.629, pFDR = 0.084) for children with RD"
