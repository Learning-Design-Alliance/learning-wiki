Evidence¶

Stephen B. Gilbert 2015¶

Stephen B. Gilbert, Stephen B. Blessing, Enruo Guo. (2015). Authoring Effective Embedded Tutors: An Overview of the Extensible Problem Specific Tutor (xPST) System. Int J Artif Intell Educ 25:428–454. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0045-0

q2 · non-randomised group comparison · i2 · causal · r1

Comparison of three groups (assignment to groups not reported as random) of 75 students learning image editing via book excerpt, expert screen-capture movie, or the embedded Paint.NET ITS (groups of roughly 24/24/26 by measure). Only the book–movie difference was significant; the ITS group did not differ significantly from either. The quote reports the omnibus task-performance result with ω2=0.08, "a medium effect size".

"Task performance was significantly different among the groups, with a medium effect size (Book M=25.0, SD =8.8; Movie M=17.7, SD=9.4; ITS M=22.3, SD =8.1; F(2,64)=3.859, p=.0 26, ω2=0.08)."
