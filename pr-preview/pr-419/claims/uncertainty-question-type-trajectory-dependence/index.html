Ali, F., Yang, Y., & Poh, J.-H. (2026). Curious behaviors in times of uncertainty: dynamics of student question-posing in online educational discussion. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00440-8

Chi-square tests of parent-child question-type dependence against a null model of no dependence (Table 5), with residual examination in Table 6. The authors call the possibility-thinking transmission finding "preliminary evidence" and note lexical mimicry cannot be ruled out.

"during the uncertainty period, dependence emerged more strongly with statistically significant Chi -square values for exam preparation and university discussion topics specifically."
