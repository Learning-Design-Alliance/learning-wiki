Dang 2026¶

Dang, C. T., & Nguyen, A. (2026). Verified, not generated: expert-verified AI study materials and the distribution of learning gains in a university course. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07097 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2610.07097

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Brown–Forsythe variance comparison in the distributional analysis of the same examination marks. The article adds that the treated half's interdecile range narrowed from 19.6 to 11.6 marks while the untreated half's widened from 10.0 to 11.6.

"The Part 2 standard deviation fell from 7.09 to 5.03 (Brown–Forsythe p = 0.004), while Part 1’s moved from 4.32 to 4.67 (p = 0.873)."
