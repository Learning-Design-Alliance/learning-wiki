Istenič 2021¶

Istenič, A. (2021). Shifting to digital during COVID-19: are teachers empowered to give voice to students? Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-09956-9

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

The commentary reports this as a cited finding attributed to Van Dijk (2017); no new data are analyzed here. It states the usage gap "has profound impact on social inequality".

"The so called “usage gap” in online resources marked by gender, educational level and age has profound impact on social inequality indicating lower educated individuals not utilizing advanced applications in all spheres of life and especially in education and career (Van Dijk 2017)"
