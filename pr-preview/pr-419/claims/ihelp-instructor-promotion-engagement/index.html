Evidence¶

Vassileva 2015¶

Vassileva, J., McCalla, G. I., & Greer, J. E. (2015). From Small Seeds Grow Fruitful Trees: How the PHelpS Peer Help System Stimulated a Diverse and Innovative Research Agenda over 15 Years. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0073-9

q2 · i? · review · r2

Observational finding mined from data generated by experiments run over iHelp's 10-year deployment with thousands of students in large computer science courses. The article reports the instructor effect hedged ("seemed to engender"), attributing part to increased motivation and part to student confidence in iHelp as legitimate academic support; no effect size is printed.

"Instructors who strongly promoted the use of the tool, who were visibly present in the online discussions, and who encouraged the peer help model, seemed to engender greater engagement by their students."
