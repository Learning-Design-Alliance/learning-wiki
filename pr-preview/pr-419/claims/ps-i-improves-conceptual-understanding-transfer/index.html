Evidence¶

Katharina Loibl 2025¶

Katharina Loibl, Valentina Nachtigall, Timo Leuders. (2025). Advances in research on composite instructional designs – investigating intermediate knowledge and preparation effects. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09751-6

q2 · i? · review · r2

The editorial's narrative synthesis of prior PS-I studies reports that the design "can improve conceptual understanding" and transfer, with effectiveness especially demonstrated for domain-specific learning in mathematics and science. No effect sizes are printed.

"Overall, the results from PS-I studies suggest that this instructional design can improve conceptual understanding (DeCaro & Rittle-Johnson, 2012; Kapur, 2012, 2014; Kapur & Bielaczyc, 2012; Loibl & Rummel, 2014a, b) and transfer"
