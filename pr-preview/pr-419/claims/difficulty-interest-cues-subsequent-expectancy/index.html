Evidence¶

Kamila Urban 2025¶

Kamila Urban, Marek Urban. (2025). Metacognition and motivation in creativity: examining the roles of self-efficacy and values as cues for metacognitive judgments. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-025-09421-5

q2 · i? · associational · r2

The two-wave SEM of 360 university students supported the hypothesis that "H3c was supported for both perceived difficulty" and interest, meaning these metacognitive experiences informed not only judgments but also subsequent expectancy beliefs.

"The results suggested that H3c was supported for both perceived difficulty (β = − 0.06, p =.005) and interest (β = 0.09, p <.001)."
