Danxuan Liang 2026¶

Danxuan Liang, Chun Yin Li, Zheng Wei, Xian Xu, Meng Xia, Huamin Qu, Wai Tong. (2026). LumiNote: LLM-Assisted Multimodal Instruction for VR Stage Lighting Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17335 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.17335

q2 · i? · design · r2

Observational data from three instructors teaching in VR with LumiNote, drawing on session recordings, interaction logs, and post-session interviews. Instructors retained "authority over how generated suggestions were accepted, rejected, or iteratively refined within their teaching flow."

"Second, LLM assistance functioned as a controllable refinement process, with instructors retaining authority over how generated suggestions were accepted, rejected, or iteratively refined within their teaching flow."
