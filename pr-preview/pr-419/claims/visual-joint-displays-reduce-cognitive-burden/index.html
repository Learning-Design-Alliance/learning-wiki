Evidence¶

Peters 2024¶

Peters, M., & Fàbregues, S. (2024). Missed opportunities in mixed methods EdTech research? Visual joint display development as an analytical strategy for achieving integration in mixed methods studies. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10234-z

q1 · i? · design · r2

The article reports this as a claim from prior methodological literature (Guetterman et al., 2021), not as a finding tested in the present study. At the reporting level, visual displays can make integration more explicit and facilitate interpretation of complex findings.

"According to Guetterman et al. (2021), visual joint displays can reduce the “cognitive burden” (p. 7) of the integration analysis and reporting processes compared to tabular joint displays."
