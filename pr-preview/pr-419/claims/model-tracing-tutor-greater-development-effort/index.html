Evidence¶

Viswanathan Kodaganallur 2005¶

Viswanathan Kodaganallur, Rob R. Weitz, David Rosenthal. (2005). A Comparison of Model-Tracing and Constraint-Based Intelligent Tutoring Paradigms. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://telearn.hal.science/hal-00197305

q2 · i? · design · r?

The authors' development-experience comparison of the two tutors built for statistical hypothesis testing found that "the development effort required to build a model-tracing tutor is greater than that for building a constraint-based tutor".
