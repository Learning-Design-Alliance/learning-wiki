q2 · i? · associational · r2

In the SEM of 758 Chinese university EFL students, the direct path from teacher support to engagement was supported (β = 0.372, C.R. = 6.357, p < 0.001), i.e., "Teacher support →Engagement" was among the significant paths in the hypothesized model.
