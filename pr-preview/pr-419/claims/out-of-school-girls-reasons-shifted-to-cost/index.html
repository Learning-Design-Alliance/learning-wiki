Evidence¶

Nandi 2023¶

Nandi, A., Haberland, N., Kozak, M., & Ngô, T. D. (2023). The gendered effects of the COVID-19 pandemic on adolescent literacy and schooling outcomes in India. npj Science of Learning, 8, 42. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00193-8

q3 · i? · causal · r2

PSM and IPW analysis of reasons for non-enrollment among out-of-school girls (n=25,794) from NFHS-5. "3.5% –3.9% and 3.2%–3.6% fewer girls reported marriage and employment" while cost of schooling was cited "at 6% higher rates" post-COVID (all p<0.01).

"Among out-of-school post-COVID girls, 3.5% –3.9% and 3.2%–3.6% fewer girls reported marriage and employment as the reasons for being out of school respectively, as compared with the matched pre-COVID group. Post-COVID girls also reported the cost of schooling as the reason for being out of school at 6% higher rates"
