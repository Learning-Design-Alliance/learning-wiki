Evidence¶

Darling-Hammond 2023¶

Darling-Hammond, S. (2023). Fostering belonging, transforming schools: The impact of restorative practices. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/169.703

q2 · i? · associational · r2

School-level analyses of changes in restorative practice utilization (measured via California Healthy Kids Survey items) linked to school-level changes in student outcomes across the 485 middle schools, as summarized in the report's findings section.

"School-level use of restorative practices, caused declines in schoolwide student misbehavior, gang membership, victimization, depressive symptoms, and substance abuse."

Darling-Hammond 2023 (2)¶

Darling-Hammond, S. (2023). Fostering belonging, transforming schools: The impact of restorative practices. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/169.703

q2 · i? · associational · r3

School-level change analyses reported in the findings section: increases in utilization tracked with GPA and climate gains, and reductions tracked with declines, in the report's own printed summary of its school-level results.

"Schools that increased utilization of restorative practices also saw improvements in average school GPA and school climate. Schools that reduced their utilization of restorative practices saw declines in these outcomes."
