Riichiro Mizoguchi 2026¶

Riichiro Mizoguchi, Tomoki Aburatani, Kento Koike, Machi Shimmei. (2026). Vibe Compiler: A Research-Logic Synthesis Tool That Runs without Prompt Engineering —Toward Enhancing Metacognition for Sustaining Agency in the Age of Generative AI—. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05545 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.05545

q2 · i? · design · r2

The paper reports, citing Gerlich (2025), Lee et al. (2025), and Kosmyna et al. (2025), that greater trust in GenAI accompanies less critical-thinking effort and that delegating writing accompanies declined neurophysiological engagement. These are second-hand citations narrated in the introduction; the cited studies' designs were not read here.

"Users who place greater trust in GenAI report expending less cognitive effort on critical thinking ( Gerlich, 2025 ; Lee et al., 2025 ). Delegating the act of writing is likewise accompanied by a decline in neurophysiological engagement ( Kosmyna et al., 2025 )."
