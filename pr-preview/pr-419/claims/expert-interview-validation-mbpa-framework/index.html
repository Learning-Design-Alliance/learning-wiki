Evidence¶

Sebastiaan de Klerk 2018¶

Sebastiaan de Klerk, Bernard P. Veldkamp, Theo J. H. M. Eggen. (2018). A framework for designing and developing multimedia-based performance assessment in vocational education. Education Tech Research Dev (2018) 66:147–171. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-017-9559-5

q2 · i? · design · r2

Qualitative validation study using five semi-structured expert interviews with Dutch assessment and multimedia experts, each with more than 10 years of experience. The authors report that "28 text fragments were extracted from the interviews" and used to adjust the prototype; fragments were selected independently by each author with 77% selected by all.

"In total, 28 text fragments were extracted from the interviews that relate directly to the framework."
