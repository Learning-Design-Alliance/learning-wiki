Evidence¶

Samuel Falcon 2023¶

Samuel Falcon, Jaime Leon. (2023). How do teachers engaging messages affect students? A sentiment analysis. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10230-3

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Mediation analysis at the student level within the partial mediation ML-SEM, with the indirect effect computed via the delta method (Sobel, 1982). The article reports the indirect effect as significant and the direct TEM→MTL path as strong (β = 0.28).

"The standardised indirect effect between TEM and students’ MTL was significantly different from 0 (β = 0.012; S.E. = 0.006; p = .041)."
