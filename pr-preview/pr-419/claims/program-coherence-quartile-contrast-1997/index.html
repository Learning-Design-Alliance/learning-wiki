Descriptive quartile comparison from the Program Coherence scale summary, which assesses whether teachers believe school programs are coordinated with one another and consistent within and across grade levels. The report also notes bottom-quartile schools show 44 percent strong or very strong coherence. No significance test is reported.

"In the top quartile schools more than half of the teachers describe moderate program coherence, with another 15 percent noting strong program coherence in their school. Relatively few teachers (32 percent) consider these schools to have little or no coherence."
