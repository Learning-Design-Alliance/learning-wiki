Evidence¶

Carmen Álvarez-Álvarez & Samuel Falcon 2023¶

Carmen Álvarez-Álvarez & Samuel Falcon. (2023). Students' preferences with university teaching practices: analysis of testimonials with artificial intelligence. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10239-8

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Reliability analysis in which two researchers independently coded a random 10% of the 1081 responses and agreement with GPT-3 was computed as percentage of agreement using ReCal3. The article reports "89.07%" overall agreement, considered satisfactory.

"The overall percentage of agreement between the two researchers and GPT-3 was 89.07%, which is considered satisfactory (O'Connor & Joffe, 2020)."
