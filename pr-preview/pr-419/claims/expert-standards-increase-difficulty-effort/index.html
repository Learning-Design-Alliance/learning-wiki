Evidence¶

Linda Froese 2022¶

Linda Froese, Julian Roelle. (2022). Expert example standards but not idea unit standards help learners accurately evaluate the quality of self-generated examples. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09293-z

q3 · i1 · causal · r3

In the N = 131 experiment, ANOVA on 7-point subjective task-difficulty ratings during evaluation showed a significant main effect of expert example standards, ηp2 = .03; mental effort showed a parallel significant effect, ηp2 = .03.

"However, there was a statistically significant main effect of expert example standards, F(1, 127) = 4.16, p = .043, ηp2 = .03. The learners who received expert example standards experienced evaluating their examples as more difficult than their counterparts."
