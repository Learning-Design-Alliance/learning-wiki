Evidence¶

Jarkko Suhonen · Erkki Sutinen 2022¶

Jarkko Suhonen · Erkki Sutinen. (2022). Critical events and pivotal factors as tools for analyzing the sustainability of online study programs. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10133-9

q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

Authors' guideline derived from the sustainability analysis of the IMPDET program. They report two incentives for external supervisors: ethical motivation to offer fee-free flexible doctoral training, and co-authored papers and supervised dissertations supporting the supervisors' careers. No effect sizes are reported.

"If a study program needs to find a voluntary work force, such as when economic resources are scarce, it is crucial to consider incentives that can motivate those volunteers."
