Evidence¶

Sydney Enock Msonde 2017¶

Sydney Enock Msonde, Jan Van Aalst. (2017). Designing for interaction, thinking and academic achievement in a Tanzanian undergraduate chemistry course. Education Tech Research Dev (2017) 65:1389–1413. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-017-9531-4

q3 · i3 · design · r3

Repeated-measures ANOVA of forum post-writing effort for 70 students in the MIL and HIL conditions across five forums. The article reports a significant main effect for time, F (4, 272) = 27.646, p \ 0.0005, partial η² = 0.289 (large effect), and a significant between-groups effect, F (1, 68) = 25.136, partial η² = 0.270 (large).

"Results of repeated-measures ANOVA, given in Table 1, show that the main effect for time for the students' writing efforts was statistically significant, F (4, 272) = 27.646, p \ 0.0005, partial g2 = 0.289 (large effect)."

Sydney Enock Msonde 2017 (2)¶

Sydney Enock Msonde, Jan Van Aalst. (2017). Designing for interaction, thinking and academic achievement in a Tanzanian undergraduate chemistry course. Education Tech Research Dev (2017) 65:1389–1413. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-017-9531-4

q3 · i2 · design · r3

Follow-up one-way ANOVAs on forums 3-5 found significant between-group differences in post-writing effort, with partial η² = 0.102, η² = 0.104 and η² = 0.184; the article reports forums 1 and 2 were not statistically different (p = 0.075 and p = 0.116).

"The mean post-writing effort per student in forums 3, 4 and 5 were significantly different between the groups, F (1,69) = 7.773, p = 0.007, partial g2 = 0.102 (medium); F (1,69) = 7.901, p = 0.006, partial g2 = 0.104 (medium); and F (1,69) = 15.316, p \ 0.0005, partial g2 = 0.184 (large)."
