q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical argument in the review's introduction defining the jingle fallacy and its role in fragmenting SR research. The article offers this as conceptual analysis, citing Wulff & Mata, and reports that the SR/SRL label confusion was a central motivation for the paper.

"a jingle fallacy occurs when the same label (the same "jingle") is used to refer to distinct constructs (measured by different indicators), so that the same label represents separate phenomena."
