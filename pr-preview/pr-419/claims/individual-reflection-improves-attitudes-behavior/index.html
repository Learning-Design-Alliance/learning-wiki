Evidence¶

Vanderhoven 2015¶

Vanderhoven, E., Schellens, T., Vanderlinde, R., & Valcke, M. (2015). Developing educational materials about risks on social network sites: A design-based research approach. Ghent University. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-015-9415-4

q3 · i? · design · r3

Multilevel analysis of 1487 pupils (average age 14.90) across control, collaborative-learning, and individual-reflection conditions; per Table 4, EXP2-control differences on attitudes and behavior were significant (p < .02) while EXP1-control were not.

"However, with regard to teenagers’ attitudes and behavior, a change could only be observed when the course gave the opportunity of individual reflection, compared to the control group"
