Evidence¶

Morrison 2014¶

Morrison, J. R., Ross, S. M., Corcoran, R. P., & Reid, A. J. (2014). Fostering Market Efficiency in K-12 Ed-tech Procurement. Center for Research and Reform in Education, Johns Hopkins University. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED563768

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Survey and interview findings from district participants. The report states there are "no readily accessible sources of 'rigorous' evidence" and that districts "largely depend on recommendations from peers and from their own teachers and principals."

"There are no readily accessible sources of "rigorous" evidence on the effectiveness of the vast majority of ed-tech products. As a result, school districts largely depend on recommendations from peers and from their own teachers and principals who have familiarity with the products."
