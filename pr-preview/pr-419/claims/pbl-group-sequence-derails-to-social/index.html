Evidence¶

Mohammed Saqr 2023¶

Mohammed Saqr, Sonsoles López-Pernas. (2023). The temporal dynamics of online problem-based learning: Why and when sequence matters. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-023-09385-1

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Sequence mining and distribution plots of group-level interactions (RQ1) showed Organize taking "most of the first two time points (76.5% for t = 1, and 75.3% f or t = 2)"; beyond roughly the 120th time point the pattern is Socialize, Agree, and Discuss with decreasing Argue.

"As such, co-regulation derails, and the groups are less into higher cognitive interactions, but more about social bonding and sharing."
