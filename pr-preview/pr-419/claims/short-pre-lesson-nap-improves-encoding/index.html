Evidence¶

Vidal 2025¶

Vidal, V., Pretel, M. R., Capurro, L., Tassone, L. M., Moyano, M. D., Malacari, R. G., Brusco, L. I., Ballarini, F. M. & Forcato, C. (2025). Short naps improve subsequent learning in a high school setting. npj Science of Learning 10:15. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00307-4

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Randomized one-day classroom experiment: Nap group (n = 26) slept up to 35 min in the school library with EEG; Control group (n = 50) conversed calmly; both then received a 25-min biology lesson and an immediate 20-question multiple-choice test. The Nap group scored higher, "Cohen’s d = 0.76".

"Nap group: 53.46 ± 2.58, Control group: 43.00 ± 2.01; Two-sample t-test t(74) =−3.114, p = 0.003, Cohen’s d = 0.76"
