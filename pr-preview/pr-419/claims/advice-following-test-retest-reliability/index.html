Evidence¶

Maayan Pereg 2024¶

Maayan Pereg, Uri Hertz, Ido Ben-Artzi and Nitzan Shahar. (2024). Disentangling the contribution of individual and social learning processes in human advice-taking behavior. npj Science of Learning 9:4. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00214-0

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Participants completed the same bandit task in two sessions on adjacent days with changed visual stimuli; hierarchical Bayesian regression of the reveal effect across sessions yielded a test-retest Pearson correlation coefficient (Fig. 2d) of r = 0.73.

"We performed a hierarchical Bayesian regression analysis and found good test-retest reliability (posterior median = 0.73, HDI 89% = 0.64 to 0.81"
