Evidence¶

Alicja Piotrkowicz 2021¶

Alicja Piotrkowicz, Kaiwen Wang, Jennifer Hallam, Vania Dimitrova. (2021). Data-driven Exploration of Engagement with Workplace-based Assessment in the Clinical Skills Domain. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 31:1022–1052. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-021-00264-0

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Observational analysis of weekly aggregated WBA counts from the Clinical Skill Passport app for three Year 1 cohorts (2016/17–2018/19), summarised as boxplots in Fig. 1 by cohort (P1, P2, S1, S2). The analysis found "more assessments completed in secondary care" in nearly all blocks. No effect size is printed.

"In nearly all blocks across all years there are more assessments completed in secondary care, especially when secondary care is the first placement block, and early in the placement more generally."
