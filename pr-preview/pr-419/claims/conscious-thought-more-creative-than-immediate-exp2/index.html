Evidence¶

Ritter 2012¶

Ritter, S.M.; van Baaren, R.B.; Dijksterhuis, A.J. (2012). Creativity: The role of unconscious processes in idea generation and idea selection. Thinking Skills and Creativity, 7, 21-27. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tsc.2011.12.002

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Experiment 2 ANOVA on average creativity across the three conditions yielded a significant main effect with the printed effect size /DC12 = .11 (the article's notation for eta-squared, a medium effect); "compared to participants who immediately listed their ideas, conscious thinkers were more creative, t(43) = 2.75, p < .05", while unconscious and conscious thinkers did not differ, t(44) = 1.64, p ns.

"An ANOVA on the average creativity revealed a signiﬁcant main effect of condition, F(2,65) = 4.10 p < .05, /DC12 = .11."
