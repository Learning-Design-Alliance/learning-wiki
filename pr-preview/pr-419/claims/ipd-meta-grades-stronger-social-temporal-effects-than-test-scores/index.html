Moderator analysis in the IPD meta-analysis, coding achievement operationalization as a dummy variable (grades vs. test scores) and testing product terms with the achievement variables. The printed result is that "the social and temporal comparison effects were stronger in studies using grades"; no coefficient is quoted in the available text.

"In particular, the social and temporal comparison effects were stronger in studies using grades instead of test scores as achievement indicators."
