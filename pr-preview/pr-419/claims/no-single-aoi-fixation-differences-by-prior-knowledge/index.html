Evidence¶

Taub 2019¶

Taub, M., & Azevedo, R. (2019). How Does Prior Knowledge Influence Eye Fixations and Sequences of Cognitive and Metacognitive SRL Processes during Learning with an Intelligent Tutoring System? Int J Artif Intell Educ (2019) 29:1-28. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-018-0165-4

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Eye-tracking subset (n = 30): two MANCOVAs and one ANCOVA with prior knowledge group as independent variable, proportions of fixations on nine AOIs as dependent variables, condition as covariate. None significant (e.g., MANCOVA 1 Wilks' λ = .82, p = .29; agent ANCOVA p = .64); equivalence was not tested.

"there were no significant differences in the proportion of time spent fixating on the text, diagrams, notes, or table of contents between prior knowledge groups."

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • High prior knowledge students show higher frequencies of transitions from text content to diagram, notes, table of contents, SRL palette, sub-goals, and learning goal AOIs — related
  • Students with low prior knowledge show significantly higher proportional learning gains than students with high prior knowledge when learning with MetaTutor — related
  • Metacognition and critical-thinking tutors show mixed results: Operation ARA gained 1.4 σ, SEEK showed no significant improvement, and MetaTutor's learning-efficiency advantage disappeared when agent time was counted — related
