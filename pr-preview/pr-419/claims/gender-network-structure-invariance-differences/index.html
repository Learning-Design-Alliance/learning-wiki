Evidence¶

Ma 2023¶

Ma, C., Song, M., & Zhao, X. (2023). The impact of executive functions on English academic performance among Chinese primary school students: A network analysis. https://www.nwnu.edu.cn

q2 · i? · associational · r2

NetworkComparisonTest permutation analyses compared male (n=436) and female (n=427) networks. The structure test showed "tighterinternalconnectionsinthemalesample"; the expected influence test (S = 0.77, P<0.05) indicated stronger executive function–English connections in males.

"The network structure invariance test revealed significant differences (M = 0.34, P<0.001),indicatingtighterinternalconnectionsinthemalesample."

Ma 2023 (2)¶

Ma, C., Song, M., & Zhao, X. (2023). The impact of executive functions on English academic performance among Chinese primary school students: A network analysis. https://www.nwnu.edu.cn

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Local invariance tests with Holm-Bonferroni correction found three differing edges, all tighter in males; centrality invariance differed for A1 and D2 nodes, but "bridge centrality invariance test showed no significant differences (Ps > 0.05)".

"The edge weight invariance test identified significant differences in three edges:A4–D1, D1–D2, and D2–D3 (Ps < 0.05), indicating tighter connections in the male sample between working memory span and listening, listening and reading, and readingandwriting."
