Content analysis of coded responses to a survey question about barriers to using technology in classrooms. The article reports "lack of access to technology" cited 17 times pre and 22 times post, linked to cost and to teachers' and students' readiness.

"The most frequently cited barrier on both the pre-and post-surveys was "lack of access to technology" (17 on the pre-survey and 22 on the post-survey)."
