Evidence¶

Kim Young-Suk Grace 2024¶

Kim Young-Suk Grace. (2024). Writing Fluency: Its Relations With Language, Cognitive, and Transcription Skills, and Writing Quality Using Longitudinal Data From Kindergarten to Grade 2. Journal of Educational Psychology, 116(4). https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000841

q3 · i? · associational · r2

In the longitudinal SEM, neither executive function measure was directly related to Grade 2 outcomes: working memory and attentional control were "not directly related to Grade 2 writing fluency or writing quality". The article reports working memory was related to Grade 1 spelling (.15, p = .008).

"Kindergarten working memory (ps ≥ .32) and attentional control (ps ≥ .08) were not directly related to Grade 2 writing fluency or writing quality after accounting for Grade 1 language, cognitive, and transcription skills and other control variables."
