Evidence¶

Vuorre 2021¶

Vuorre, M., & Metcalfe, J. (2021). Measures of relative metacognitive accuracy are confounded with task performance in tasks that permit guessing. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09257-1

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Comparison of each experiment's observed performance-resolution regression slope against the simulation's slope at the experiment's mean da and guessing = 0.25, using one-sided one-sample t-tests. The authors state this analysis "while far from conclusive, indicated that the data slopes...were equal to or smaller than a relationship caused by correct guessing".

"This analysis showed that only one experiment ’s da-performance correlation was marginally greater than the simulated relation, all others were not significant (Exp 1A: p = 0.218, Exp 1B: p = 0.327, Exp 1C: p = 0.224, Exp 1D: p = 0.055, Exp 1E: p = 0.473, Exp 1F: p = 0.285)."
