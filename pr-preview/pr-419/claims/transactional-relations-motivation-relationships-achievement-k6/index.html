Evidence¶

M. Zee 2021¶

M. Zee, K. Moritz Rudasill, R.J. Bosman. (2021). A Cross-Lagged Study of Students' Motivation, Academic Achievement, and Relationships With Teachers From Kindergarten to 6th Grade. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000574

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Cross-lagged panel models fitted to three waves of the Dutch COOL cohort study (N = 1,267 students, 207 teachers) showed that "student–teacher closeness, conflict and dependency, students' motivation, and academic achievement informed each other" across grades K-6.

"These models generally indicated that student–teacher closeness, conflict and dependency, students' motivation, and academic achievement informed each other across the elementary grades."

Related Claims¶

  • Teacher-child closeness shows concurrent benefits but no prospective spillover to other domains once conflict covariation is controlled — related
  • Across basic and extended RI-CLPMs and CLPMs of five-wave secondary-school data, math self-concept and achievement show consistent reciprocal (bidirectional) directional ordering — related
  • Low self-regulation (inattention and impulsivity) at kindergarten entry predicts greater teacher-child conflict by kindergarten spring, and this effect persists into first grade — related
