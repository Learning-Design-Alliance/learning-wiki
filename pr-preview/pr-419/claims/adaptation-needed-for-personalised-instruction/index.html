Vania Dimitrova 2003¶

Vania Dimitrova. (2003). STyLE-OLM: Interactive Open Learner Modelling. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://telearn.hal.science/hal-00197312

q1 · i? · design · r?

The article reports, citing Self (1999a), the field-level argument that systems "need to adapt to the needs of learners if they are to provide for effective personalised instruction". This is a reported position, not a new empirical result of this article.

"here is a strong argument in Artificial Intelligence in Education which advocates that computer-based learning systems need to adapt to the needs of learners if they are to provide for effective personalised instruction"
