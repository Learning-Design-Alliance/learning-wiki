Evidence¶

Tuononen 2023¶

Tuononen, T., Hyytinen, H., Räisänen, M., Hailikari, T., & Parpala, A. (2023). Metacognitive awareness in relation to university students' learning profiles. Metacognition and Learning, 18, 37–54. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09314-x

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Bonferroni post hoc comparison following the one-way ANOVA on 462 students' profiles. A second Results sentence reports "There was no statistically significant difference in the regulation of cognition" between these two profiles; equivalence was not tested.

"In addition, students belonging to Dissonant profile had statistically significantly lower scores on knowledge about cognition than those representing Unorganized students applying a deep approach."
