Evidence¶

Hertzog C 2011¶

Hertzog C, Dunlosky J. (2011). Metacognition in Later Adulthood: Spared Monitoring Can Benefit Older Adults' Self-regulation. Current Directions in Psychological Science. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3903298/

q2 · i? · review · r2

Experimental study presenting paired associates once, twice, or four times with delays used to equate recall and recognition performance across age groups; the article reports higher FOK resolution with repetitions and "a significant Age X Repetition interaction indicating that this benefit was larger for older adults."

"It also resulted in higher FOK resolution for both age groups (Figure 2), with a significant Age X Repetition interaction indicating that this benefit was larger for older adults."

Hertzog C 2011 (2)¶

Hertzog C, Dunlosky J. (2011). Metacognition in Later Adulthood: Spared Monitoring Can Benefit Older Adults' Self-regulation. Current Directions in Psychological Science. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3903298/

q2 · i? · review · r2

In the same episodic FOK experiment, older adults tested with a 48-hour delay and younger adults (7-day delay) showed comparable FOK resolution, supporting the memory-quality account rather than a monitoring deficit.

"Most important, FOK resolution on average was at least as good for older adults tested with a 48-hour delay and younger adults."

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • FOK resolution for general-knowledge information is similar for younger and older adults, whereas the literature argues for a deficit in FOK resolution for episodic memory tasks in older adults — related
  • The literature is mixed on age differences in the accuracy of retrospective confidence judgments during recognition memory tests, with older adults sometimes producing more high-confidence false alarms and poorer resolution with deceptive lures — related
  • Knowledge of individual differences in children's memories should shape recitation questioning, and psychology's analysis of school subjects underlies teaching methods — related
