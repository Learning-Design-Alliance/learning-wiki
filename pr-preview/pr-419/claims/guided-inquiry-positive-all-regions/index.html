q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports Aditomo and Klieme (2020) analyzed PISA 2015 data from the 10 highest and 10 lowest performing regions (over 150,000 students from over 5000 schools), contrasting independent inquiry with guided inquiry; "guided inquiry was positively associated with science achievement in all 16 regions."

"The results showed that guided inquiry was positively associated with science achievement in all 16 regions where this form of instruction was applied."
