Evidence¶

Wei 2026¶

Wei, M., Liu, J., Wang, H., Li, Q. & Dong, G.-H. (2026). Fragmented learning from short videos modulates neural activity and connectivity during memory retrieval. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00399-y

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Pearson correlation between ROI beta values and behavioral measures within the short video group (n=28). "the beta value in the claustrum was significantly negatively correlated with memory accuracy (r = -0.442)"; the authors interpret this as an inefficient compensatory integration effort.

"In the short video group, the beta value in the claustrum was significantly negatively correlated with memory accuracy (r = -0.442, p = .018, Fig. 3a)."

Wei 2026 (2)¶

Wei, M., Liu, J., Wang, H., Li, Q. & Dong, G.-H. (2026). Fragmented learning from short videos modulates neural activity and connectivity during memory retrieval. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00399-y

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Within-group Pearson correlations between neural measures and short-video use scales: caudate beta with SVAS (r = 0.411) and caudate–claustrum FC with SVUSCF (r = 0.379) in the short video group; caudate beta with SVDS (r = -0.377) in the long video group.

"Additionally, beta values in the caudate showed a significant positive correlation with SV AS scores (r = 0.411, p = .030, Fig. 3b). Furthermore, FC between the caudate and claustrum was positively correlated with SVUSCF scores (r = 0.379, p = .047, Fig. 3c). In the long video group, beta values in the caudate showed a significant negative correlation with SVDS scores (r = -0.377, p = .044, Fig. 3d)."
