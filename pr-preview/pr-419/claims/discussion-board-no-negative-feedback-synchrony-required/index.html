Sarré 2011¶

Sarré, C. (2011). Computer-mediated negotiated interactions: how is meaning negotiated in discussion boards, text chat and videoconferencing? In S. Thouësny & L. Bradley (Eds.), Second language teaching and learning with technology: views of emergent researchers (pp. 189-210). Dublin: Research-publishing.net. https://dx.doi.org/10.14705/rpnet.2011.9781908416001

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Negative feedback counts per mode (Table 11) show 61 occurrences in text chat (2.7% of turns), 58 in videoconferencing (6.4%), and none in discussion board interactions; asynchronous contributions can be carefully checked for language before posting.

"It is thus suggested that the nature itself of discussion board interaction is accountable for the total absence of negative feedback, as synchrony seems to be required to foster all types of negative feedback, namely explicit correction, recasts and self-correction."
