Evidence¶

Preya Shabrina 2024¶

Preya Shabrina, Behrooz Mostafavi, Mark Abdelshiheed, Min Chi, Tiffany Barnes. (2024). Investigating the Impact of Backward Strategy Learning in a Logic Tutor: Aiding Subgoal Learning Towards Improved Problem Solving. Int J Artif Intell Educ 34:825–861. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-023-00338-1

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Performance and BW-action analysis of the 168 completers showed the BWE-only group “behaved and performed mostly like the control group C”; T1 received insignificant higher average scores than C in problems 3.4–5.4 and showed no consistent signs of improvement.

"On the other hand, T1 students who only received BWE, behaved and performed mostly like the control group C, who were not introduced to the backward strategy at all."
