Analysis of the combination of definitional, theoretical and measurement information across the 88 included studies in the scoping review. The review reports "There were 49 (55.7 %) studies that made no explicit mention of a theory, definition, or measure for engagement", and that only Georgiou et al. (2019) provided all three.
