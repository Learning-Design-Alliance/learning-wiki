Evidence¶

Adurangba Victor Oje 2026¶

Adurangba Victor Oje, Nathaniel J. Hunsu. (2026). Desktop virtual reality with scaffolded interaction and reflective explanation: effects on cognitive, motivational, and learning outcomes in engineering education. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10722-y

q3 · i3 · causal · r1

A one-way between-subjects ANOVA compared knowledge application scores on a novel engineering scenario. The VR + self-explanation group scored higher ("M = 8.47, SD = 2.59") than controls (M = 6.26, SD = 1.91), with a moderate-to-large effect.

"Results revealed a statistically significant group difference, F(1, 37) = 8.97, p <.05, partial η2 =.20, indicating a moderate-to-large effect. Students in the VR + self-explanation condition achieved a higher mean score (M = 8.47, SD = 2.59) than those in the control group (M = 6.26, SD = 1.91), with a Cohen's d of 0.97 (95% CI [0.29, 1.64])"
