H. Ulrich Hoppe 2021¶

H. Ulrich Hoppe, et al. (2021). Using Sequence Analysis to Determine the Well-Functioning of Small Groups in Large Online Courses. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-020-00229-9

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Prior observational analysis of group formation strategies in the CmC course (cited as Wichmann et al. 2016), where productivity combined delivered text volume, domain concepts and forum contributions, and formation levels were based on prior forum activity, not pre-knowledge. No effect sizes are printed.

"It was also interesting to see that high-level individual students were more productive in heterogeneous groups, whereas low-level students were more productive in homogeneous groups."
