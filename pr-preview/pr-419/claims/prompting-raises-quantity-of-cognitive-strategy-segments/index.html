Evidence¶

Nadja M. Gentner 2024¶

Nadja M. Gentner, Lisa Respondek, Tina Seufert. (2024). Effects of short- and long-term prompting in learning journals on strategy use, self-efficacy, and learning outcomes. Instructional Science (2024) 52:919–950. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09671-x

q3 · i3 · causal · r?

Qualitative trace data from the open-ended part of the journals: segments coded by two raters (κ = 0.85) and counted per category. "The results indicate a significant increase in the number of segments coded as cognitive strategy use over time."

"Analysis revealed a main effect of time, F(2.35,133.97) = 14.78, p < .001, ηp² = 0.21. The results indicate a significant increase in the number of segments coded as cognitive strategy use over time"

Nadja M. Gentner 2024 (2)¶

Nadja M. Gentner, Lisa Respondek, Tina Seufert. (2024). Effects of short- and long-term prompting in learning journals on strategy use, self-efficacy, and learning outcomes. Instructional Science (2024) 52:919–950. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09671-x

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Same qualitative trace-data analysis for metacognitive segments in the open-ended journals during unit 1. "The results suggest that the quantity of metacognitive strategy use did not change over time", ηp² = 0.01.

"The analysis indicated no main effect of time, F(3,171) = 0.75, p = .52, ηp² = 0.01. The results suggest that the quantity of metacognitive strategy use did not change over time"
