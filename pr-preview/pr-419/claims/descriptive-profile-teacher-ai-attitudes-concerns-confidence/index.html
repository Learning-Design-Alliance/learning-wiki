Descriptive statistics from the 35-item, five-point Likert survey of 260 teachers: attitudes mean = 4.92 (SD = 0.86), concerns mean = 2.14 (SD = 0.89), confidence in using AI tools mean = 4.72 (SD = 0.89), institutional support mean = 4.32 (SD = 1.08).
