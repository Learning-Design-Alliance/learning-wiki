Evidence¶

Baten 2020¶

Baten, E., Vansteenkiste, M., De Muynck, G., De Poortere, E., Desoete, A. (2020). How Can the Blow of Math Difficulty on Elementary School Children's Motivational, Cognitive and Affective Experiences be Dampened? The Critical Role of Autonomy-Supportive Instructions. Journal of Educational Psychology. Advance online publication. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000444

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Moderation follow-up in the randomized experiment: autonomous motivation moderated the difficulty-disengagement relation (β = -.09, p = .037), with more disengagement under difficulty among children low (≤ M - 1SD) but not high (≥ M + 1SD) in autonomous motivation.

"First, autonomous motivation moderated the relation between difficulty level and cognitive 22 disengagement, β = -.09, p = .037 . More difficult exercises predicted more cognitive 23 disengagement among individuals with lower (≤ M - 1SD; p = .099; ηp ² = .03) but not among 24 participants with higher levels of autonomous motivation (≥ M + 1SD; p = .511; ηp ² = .01)."
