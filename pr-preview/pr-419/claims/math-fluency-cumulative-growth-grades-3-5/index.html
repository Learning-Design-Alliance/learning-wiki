Evidence¶

Little 2021¶

Little, C. W., Lonigan, C. J., & Phillips, B. M. (2021). Differential Patterns of Growth in Reading and Math Skills during Elementary School. Journal of Educational Psychology, 113(3), 462-476. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000635

q3 · i1 · associational · r2

Univariate latent-growth-curve model of WJ-III Math Fluency across grades 3-5 in the same sample. The study reports r = .16, p < .03, a small positive intercept-slope correlation indicating cumulative growth; correlations for early math, applied problems, and calculation were non-significant.

"For math fluency, the correlation between intercept and slope was small, positive, and significant (r = .16, p < .03), indicating a cumulative growth pattern in which higher math fluency in third grade was positively associated with development of math fluency through fifth grade"
