Yu-Ju Lan 2018¶

Yu-Ju Lan. (2018). Technology enhanced learner ownership and learner autonomy through creation. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-018-9608-8

q2 · i? · causal · r?

The editorial reports, citing Song, a study adopting productive failure (PF) instructional design in a seamless learning environment with 53 sixth graders, collecting both quantitative and qualitative data. Benefits for "collaborative problem-solving competency" were reported; no effect size is printed.

"The results included the beneﬁts of implementing the PF instructional design to increase elementary school students’ collaborative problem-solving competency in science learning in a seamless learning environment"
