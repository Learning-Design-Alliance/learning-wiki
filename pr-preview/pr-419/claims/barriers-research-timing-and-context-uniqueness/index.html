Evidence¶

Hollands 2020¶

Hollands, F., & Escueta, M. (2020). How research informs educational technology decision-making in higher education: the role of external research versus internal research. Education Tech Research Dev, 68, 163–180. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09678-z

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Barrier theme from the qualitative interview study: by the time research is completed the technology may have been upgraded or changed multiple times, and during a study the technology might change, making it hard to pinpoint which iteration works. The article reports "the length of time it takes to complete research is incompatible with the fast pace of technology change."

"Many interviewees noted that the length of time it takes to complete research is incompatible with the fast pace of technology change."
