Mehedi Hasan Anik 2023¶

Mehedi Hasan Anik, Shahriar Nafees Chowdhury Raaz, Nushat Khan. (2023). Embracing AI Assistants: Unraveling Young Researchers' Journey with ChatGPT in Science Education Thesis Writing. https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-3481002/v1

q1 · i? · qualitative · r2

Expert-interview finding from a professor with over two decades of research experience and nearly ten years supervising Master's theses. The expert voiced concern about copy-pasting despite appreciating AI's advantages, and the article notes "the inability of existing plagiarism detection technologies to distinguish AI-generated content." Expert opinion; no effect size.
