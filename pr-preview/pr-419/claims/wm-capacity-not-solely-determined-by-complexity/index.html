q2 · i? · causal · r2

Experiment 6 regression modeling on averaged data from 30 participants, with visual search rate as the complexity index; the final binary polynomial model was verified on 30 further participants, with mean difference between actual and predicted values of 0.081.

"The RMSE of the model was 0.112, indicating a good ﬁt. The coef ﬁcient of determination was r2 = 0.399, F = 58.928, p < 0.001, indicating that the regression model was valid."
