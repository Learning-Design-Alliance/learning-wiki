Evidence¶

Käser 2013¶

Käser, T., Busetto, A.G., Solenthaler, B., Baschera, G.-M., Kohn, J., Kucian, K., von Aster, M., Gross, M. (2013). Modelling and Optimizing Mathematics Learning in Children. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-013-0003-7

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Repeated measures GLM with group (training/waiting) as between-subject factor on 63 children with mathematics difficulties; training group trained 6 weeks. The article reports "a significant improvement in subtraction" with HRT and AC interactions both p<.001; no effect size printed.

"The training induced a significant improvement in subtraction, while no improvement was found after the waiting period (HRT interaction:p<. 001, AC interaction:p<. 001)."
