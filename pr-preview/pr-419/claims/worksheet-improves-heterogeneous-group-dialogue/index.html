Evidence¶

Tessa H. S. Eysink 2020¶

Tessa H. S. Eysink, Alieke M. van Dijk, Ton de Jong. (2020). BE COOL! a digital learning environment to challenge and socially include gifted learners. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09754-9

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Experimental study in which 136 fourth to sixth graders worked cooperatively in heterogeneous groups of four while being videotaped, comparing groups with and without the worksheet; cooperative processes were coded on participation and content. The article reports that "the group dialogues benefited from working with the worksheet."

"Results showed that the group dialogues benefited from working with the worksheet: Children of all ability levels who worked with the worksheet participated more equally in domain-related dialogue, groups who received the worksheet spent more of their dialogue on discussing content regarding the four topics that were central to the assignment, and children who used the worksheet also spent a larger proportion of their dialogue on providing theoretical explanations about the domain than the unsupported groups."
