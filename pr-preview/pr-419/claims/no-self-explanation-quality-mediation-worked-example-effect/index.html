Evidence¶

Bichler 2022¶

Bichler, S., Stadler, M., Bühner, M., Greiff, S., & Fischer, F. (2022). Learning to solve ill-defined statistics problems: does self-explanation quality mediate the worked example effect? Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09579-4

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Conditional process analysis (PROCESS model 59) of a randomized two-condition experiment (worked examples n1 = 36 vs problem-solving n2 = 38) with N = 74 university students. The indirect effect was non-significant at mean −1 SD, mean, and mean + 1 SD of the moderator; the article reports "no evidence for an indirect effect of worked examples through self-explanation quality".

"In sum, we found no evidence for an indirect effect of worked examples through self-explanation quality or its dependence on the moderator prior knowledge."
