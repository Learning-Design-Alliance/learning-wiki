Evidence¶

Szczygieł M.; Szűcs D.; Toffalini E 2024¶

Szczygieł M.; Szűcs D.; Toffalini E. (2024). Math anxiety and math achievement in primary school children: Longitudinal relationship and predictors. LEARNING AND INSTRUCTION. https://dx.doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2024.101906

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Longitudinal analysis of 337 Polish primary school children assessed at three time points (T0 beginning of first grade, T1 end of first grade, T2 end of second grade) found bidirectional cross-lag prediction: "MATH at T1 predicted MA at T2 and MA at T1 predicted MATH at T2".

"MATH at T1 predicted MA at T2 and MA at T1 predicted MATH at T2 (supporting the Reciprocal Theory)."
