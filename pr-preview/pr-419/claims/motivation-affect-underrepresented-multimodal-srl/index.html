The discussion paper attributes this finding to the de Mooij et al. (2025) systematic review, reporting that "motivational and affective dimensions remain underrepresented." The Bühler et al. review similarly found motivational and affective dimensions largely absent. No effect sizes are printed.

"Yet, motivational and affective dimensions remain underrepresented — a structural gap that persists despite theoretical consensus on their importance (de Mooij et al., 2025)."
