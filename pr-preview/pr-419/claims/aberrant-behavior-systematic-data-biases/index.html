Evidence¶

Pelánek 2025¶

Pelánek, R. (2025). Adaptive Learning is Hard: Challenges, Nuances, and Trade-offs in Modeling. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-024-00400-6

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

Log analysis in the author's own learning environment, reported in the Aberrant Student Behavior section, found "signiﬁcant cheating in reading comprehension exercises" concentrated in harder exercises, distorting analytics.

"For example, we have detected signiﬁcant cheating in reading comprehension exercises; cheating occurs only in the more difﬁcult exercises, which leads to misleading analytics results – objectively complex exercises seem to be easier than simple ones"

Pelánek 2025 (2)¶

Pelánek, R. (2025). Adaptive Learning is Hard: Challenges, Nuances, and Trade-offs in Modeling. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-024-00400-6

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

Log analysis across the author's domains: a "significant portion of rapid guessing" in computational thinking multiple-choice questions, much lower in English grammar. Fitting models to such biased data non-trivially affects parameter values.

"For rapid guessing, we detected a significant portion of rapid guessing in multiple-choice questions on computational thinking (which were a bit laborious), whereas in many other domains (like English grammar), the presence of rapid guessing was much lower."
