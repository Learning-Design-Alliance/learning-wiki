Evidence¶

Sanna Järvelä 2023¶

Sanna Järvelä, Carolyn P. Rosé. (2023). Reflecting on what counts as collaboration: Reaching forward without losing what is behind. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning, 18, 457–464. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-023-09415-y

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The editorial reports, citing Kasepalu, Chejara, Prieto and Ley, a quasi-experiment in authentic classrooms where students used a collaborative writing tool face-to-face; teachers (N = 24) were observed, interviewed, and surveyed across three dashboard conditions.

"The results show that a mirroring dashboard helped teachers the situational awareness and alerting dashboard dropped their workload and decreased frustration."

Related Claims¶

  • The alerting & guiding dashboard reduced teachers' overall workload more than the mirroring dashboard in authentic settings (H2 supported) — related
  • With the alerting & guiding dashboard, teachers' workload dropped most on the performance and frustration subscales, while cognitive load, temporal demand and effort differences were non-significant — related
  • Teachers reported the highest confidence in their situational awareness in the alerting & guiding dashboard condition — related
  • The alerting & guiding dashboard did not increase teacher coregulation more than the mirroring dashboard (H3 not supported); mirroring increased intervention rates (non-significant) while guiding-condition interventions were rare and mostly coregulation strategies — related
  • Response time declined across dashboard trials in all conditions, with no statistically significant difference between mirroring, alerting, and advising conditions — related
  • Teacher-facing dashboards that mirror or guide increase teacher awareness and targeted interventions during classroom orchestration — a broader claim this one bears on
  • Mirroring-condition teachers relied more on indicator views while alerting and advising teachers opened more group overviews — related
  • Guiding support stimulated targeted interactions: teachers posted more messages to groups (14 vs 4) and performed more dashboard interventions than under mirroring — related
  • Without dashboard support, teachers could not make announcements about time, phase transitions, or participation and relied on perception and surveying to diagnose collaboration — related
  • Real-time AI alerts can expand teachers' situational awareness but in some cases overload teachers' attention or draw focus away from their own observation flow — related
  • SAGLET's real-time learning analytics provided alerts that enabled the teacher to monitor groups and give appropriate feedback — related
  • Threshold changes in early alert systems can drastically reshape flagged-student volume and advisor workload and should not occur informally — related
  • Dashboard support type did not influence detection of CSCL events but did influence teachers' interpretation of the situation — related
  • Reported cognitive load was lowest in the mirroring condition (5.4), compared with no dashboard (5.6) and guiding (6.2) — reports the opposite
  • Guiding dashboard support enabled teachers to perform more orchestration actions, more targeted interactions, and more class announcements than mirroring support — related
  • Mirroring systems display basic actions to collaborators — related
  • Correctness overviews alone seldom lead to real insights into how to improve learning; dashboards must present teacher-actionable information supporting goal-setting, monitoring, and planning — related
  • Learning analytics interventions for study success include alerts enabling teachers to give more individual attention, peer-to-peer communication facilitation, and recommendations for adaptive learning materials — related
  • Teachers reported the tool shifted their practice toward goal-oriented teaching and dashboard-informed adjustment, but increased planning workload and instructional time — related
  • Teacher feedback during co-design directly produced four dashboard design changes: predictive alerts, navigation redesign, integrated attendance-performance view, and a Help tab — related
