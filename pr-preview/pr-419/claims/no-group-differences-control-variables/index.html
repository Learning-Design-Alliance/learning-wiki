Evidence¶

Inka Sara Hähnlein 2024¶

Inka Sara Hähnlein, Pablo Pirnay-Dummer. (2024). Promoting pre-service teachers' knowledge integration from multiple text sources across domains with instructional prompts. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10363-z

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Baseline equivalence checks before the intervention in the three-group experiment. The article also reports no significant differences in metacognitive abilities (Wilks' λ = 0.88, p = .24), epistemological beliefs (Wilks' λ = 0.95, p = .60), self-efficacy (p = .11), text-material perception (p = .43), and post-extrinsic intrinsic motivation (p = .053).

"Results from MANOVA showed no significant differences in students’ separative and integrative learning in teacher education between the groups (Wilks’ λ = 0.97, F[4,220] = 0.93, p = .45)."
