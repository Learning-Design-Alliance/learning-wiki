Evidence¶

Allensworth 2018¶

Allensworth, E.M., Farrington, C.A., Gordon, M.F., Johnson, D.W., Klein, K., McDaniel, B., & Nagaoka, J. (2018). Supporting social, emotional, & academic development: Research implications for educators. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/page/presentations

q2 · i? · review · r?

The review reports, citing Sporte, Hart, & Wechsler (2009) and Allensworth et al. (2009), that unsupported increases in challenge "can lead to student frustration, withdrawal, and disruption," and that students can end up learning less than under less challenging work. No effect size is printed.

"Introducing more challenging work without sufficient supports or attention to students' emotions and learning strategies to handle the more difficult work can lead to student frustration, withdrawal, and disruption."
