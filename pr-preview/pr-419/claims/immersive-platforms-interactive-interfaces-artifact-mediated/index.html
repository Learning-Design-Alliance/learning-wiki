Jinhee Kim 2025¶

Jinhee Kim, Seongryeong Yu, Rita Detrick, Xi Lin, Na Li. (2025). Designing AI-powered learning: adult learners' expectations for curriculum and human-AI interaction. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10549-z

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Findings on artifact-mediated interaction (theme in 65% of e-portfolios per Table 1); learners named augmented reality, virtual reality, mixed reality, extended reality, haptic technologies, and digital identity.

"the embodied affordances of immersive game-based learning platforms (e.g., gesture, voice, hand controls) allow learners to acquire knowledge intuitively and engage with a heightened sense of presence, enhancing motivation, interest, and knowledge creation"

Jinhee Kim 2025 (2)¶

Jinhee Kim, Seongryeong Yu, Rita Detrick, Xi Lin, Na Li. (2025). Designing AI-powered learning: adult learners' expectations for curriculum and human-AI interaction. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10549-z

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Findings on artifact-mediated interaction; learners expected interfaces encompassing visual, textual, and auditory modalities to convey information, suggestions, and feedback.

"learners suggested that the interface should evolve from static, model-centric explanations to dynamic, interactive dialogues that grow with the learner's understanding and needs"
