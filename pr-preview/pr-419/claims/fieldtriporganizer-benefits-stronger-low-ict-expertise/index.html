Evidence¶

Noemi Mauro 2024¶

Noemi Mauro, Liliana Ardissono, Federica Cena, Livio Scarpinati, Gianluca Torta. (2024). An Intelligent Support System to Help Teachers Plan Field Trips. Int J Artif Intell Educ 34, 793–824. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-023-00366-x

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Secondary analysis splitting the 18 participants into advanced ICT users (7) and intermediate/low users (11); the article reports "M=4.27 for medium-low expertise teachers and M=3.71 for the expert ones" on the motivation item.

"The motivation is the dimension where the difference between intermediate/low and advanced users seems more evident: M=4.27 for medium-low expertise teachers and M=3.71 for the expert ones."

Noemi Mauro 2024 (2)¶

Noemi Mauro, Liliana Ardissono, Federica Cena, Livio Scarpinati, Gianluca Torta. (2024). An Intelligent Support System to Help Teachers Plan Field Trips. Int J Artif Intell Educ 34, 793–824. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-023-00366-x

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Same split analysis on the anxiety item; the article states "The reduction of anxiety is stronger in intermediate/low users than in advanced ones", attributing it to the application's information finding and partial automation support.

"The reduction of anxiety is stronger in intermediate/low users than in advanced ones (M=3.45 vs. M=3.29)."
