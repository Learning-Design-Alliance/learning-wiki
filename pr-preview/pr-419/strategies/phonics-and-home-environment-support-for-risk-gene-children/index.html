Continue phonics training and strengthen educational and home environments, especially for children who carry reading-risk genes¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article's educational recommendations follow directly from its moderated mediation findings: "In the classroom, teachers should continue to target phonics training to enhance reading performance." Because risk genes tended to affect reading among children with low parental education and in low SES families, the authors recommend that "strategies to improve educational and home environments could be especially fruitful for children who carry risk genes for reading." They also state that children's phonological awareness should be supported alongside positive home environment.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Awareness of which children may carry genetic risk, or universal application of phonics and enriched environments
  • Instruction targeting phonological awareness and phonics

Constraints¶

  • The mediation of genetic risk was observed only under low and medium parental education and low SES, so the strongest expected benefit of environmental improvement is for children in disadvantaged home environments

Target Learners¶

  • Children at genetic risk for reading disability, particularly those in low-SES or low-parental-education homes

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Phonological awareness and reading achievement

Claims¶

  • The observed gene-by-environment pattern supports the diathesis-stress model over the bioecological model of G × E interaction on reading, in this sample of unrelated children [+W]
  • Parental education and SES moderate the relationship between RU2-Short and reading outcomes [+W]
  • The indirect effect of RU2-Short on reading outcomes through phonological awareness is conditional on home environment: significant at low and medium parental education and low SES, but not at high parental education or high SES [+W]
  • Phonological awareness mediates the effect of the DCDC2-READ1 RU2-Short allele on word reading accuracy, word reading fluency, and reading comprehension [+W]
  • RU2-Short correlates negatively with phonological awareness and reading comprehension, and positively with SES, in the overall sample [+W]
