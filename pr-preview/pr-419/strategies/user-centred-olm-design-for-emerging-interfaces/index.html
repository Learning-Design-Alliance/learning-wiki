Design OLMs for emerging interface technologies using user-centred design with the teacher¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends that OLMs for emerging interface technologies such as interactive tabletops and robotic tutors should be designed in collaboration with the teacher, based on user-centred design approaches, so that the OLM serves the purposes the teacher actually needs. In the interactive tabletop example, the teacher wanted to maintain awareness of each group's progress to decide which group most required attention, and the OLM was designed accordingly.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Designers must work with the teacher to identify the purposes the OLM needs to serve in the authentic classroom setting.

Constraints¶

  • When moving from lab to authentic settings, practical constraints such as a 60-min scheduled tutorial class and curriculum requirements may mean the learner model becomes a simpler representation than the research vision anticipated.

Target Learners¶

  • teachers
  • learners in small-group classroom activities

Target Learning Goals¶

  • classroom orchestration
  • collaborative learning progress monitoring

Related Strategies¶

  • Enrich story co-creation by adding visual stimuli and interaction turns, and prioritise interaction fluency
  • Prioritise analytics for learning over accountability, testing, organisational change or financial efficiency, with user-centred tools for stakeholders
