Use the framework and SAICS scale for intervention evaluation, learning-outcome design, teacher education, and national AI standards¶

Description¶

The article proposes four application areas for the framework and SAICS scale. "Researchers can use SAICS to reproduce and update the instruments used to evaluate their intervention and learning design for AI instruction in schools." Teacher educators can introduce the framework and scale in teacher education programs; teachers can use them "as learning objectives/outcomes to design AI learning activities," because assessment can influence learning design; and policymakers can leverage them "to create national AI competency standards, inform school and public education programs, and design assessment and policy parameters."

Claims¶

  • A three-round Delphi study with 29 K-12 AI teachers produced an eight-dimension, 32-item student AI competency framework at a 75% agreement level [+M]
  • Confirmatory factor analysis with 448 secondary students showed the 32-item SAICS measurement model has good fit and high validity and reliability [+M]
  • The SAICS measurement model is consistent across male and female students, with changes in CFI below 0.01 in multiple-group invariance tests [+M]
  • All eight SAICS dimensions showed high internal consistency (Cronbach's alpha ≥ 0.89), high factor loadings (0.76–0.96), and convergent validity (inter-dimension correlations r > 0.34) [+M]
  • Self-reported instruments measure AI self-efficacy (perceptions) rather than AI literacy abilities, so both should be included in comprehensive assessment [+W]
