Comprehensive integration approach: experiential teacher learning, iterative design, context-specific tool adaptation, and anxiety mitigation¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The review recommends a multi-part strategy for integrating AI into school EFL education, drawn from challenges across the included studies. It states the challenges "underscore the necessity for a comprehensive approach that includes experiential learning for teachers, iterative design processes, context-speci c tool adaptation, anxiety mitigation, and consideration of multimodal learning styles." Specific supports include professional development programs that increased teachers' self-efficacy, and iterative design processes for ChatGPT use in EFL writing where students experienced high cognitive load during prompt engineering.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers require experiential learning opportunities to shift from conventional methods to AI-incorporating ones

Constraints¶

  • Students' limited familiarity and literacy in AI; anxiety about misrecognition by chatbots; potential for easy distraction with some tools

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 EFL students and their teachers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Effective integration of AI tools into EFL teaching practice

Related Strategies¶

  • Teachers cultivate approach-oriented goals and help students manage AI-feedback anxiety in AI-assisted language learning
  • Integrate LLM-based CRP support tools within professional development to encourage teacher reflection beyond template-based outputs
