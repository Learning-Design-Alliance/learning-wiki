Consolidate key actions into heuristics or cognitive strategies as general exploration methods¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The third strategy is "Consolidating sets of key actions into heuristics or cognitive strategies as general methods for exploring real-world problems." Research has focused on "structuring or decomposing an open-ended, complex exploration task into a set of main actions or key questions based on heuristics or disciplinary strategies."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Heuristics should be seen being modified in certain contexts and repurposed for new contexts to prevent ritual application

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • secondary school students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • open-ended exploration
  • inquiry skills

Related Strategies¶

  • Expose learners to real-world problems embedded in complex systems with multiple interactive components
  • Make disciplinary norms of thinking explicit for effective reflection
  • Make the implicit aspects of the exploration process visible to learners
  • Provide necessary learning resources and simplify non-critical aspects of complex practice
  • Use visual forms to communicate complex ideas about exploring real-world problems
