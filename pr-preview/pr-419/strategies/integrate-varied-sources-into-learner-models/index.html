Integrate increasing and varied information sources, including wearables and ambient intelligence devices, into learner models¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that future work manage and combine new information sources for personalization: "future research should enhance their management and integration, so that this wide range of situations and features involved can reinforce the model of the learner representing them and their impact". This includes wearables with physiological and context sensors, big data settings, and ambient intelligence devices that gather information on the whole context where learning takes place, over short-, medium- and long-term periods. The goal is personal assistance that accounts for learners' affective state and supports open learner models built on interoperable standards.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Standards supporting interoperability so open learner models can combine different external learning services

Constraints¶

  • It is currently not clear how the new information sources are to be managed and combined in order to positively impact the essentially adaptive learning process

Target Learners¶

  • learners across contexts ranging from daily life activities to MOOCs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • personalized support sensitive to learners and their context

Claims¶

  • The Lantern ambient awareness device significantly increases the efficiency of supervised collaborative problem-solving sections [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Use multimodal data and time-sensitive analyses to capture situational motivational processes
  • Diversify AIED: use diverse technologies, address big problems, and reuse existing resources rather than reinventing content
