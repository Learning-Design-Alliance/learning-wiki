Embed personal-ethical and personal-professional competencies across subjects, planned experiences and incidental teachable moments¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends teacher education students be encouraged to identify where, across all subjects and the full array of school activity, opportunities exist to build learners' knowledge, skills and dispositions aligned with the integrated pillars. Integration could occur at planning, pedagogical and practice levels: in planned experiences using digital resources for subject outcomes, in experiences focused on other learning (e.g., lessons investigating game addiction in health curriculum), and incidentally as 'teachable moments' within normal classroom programmes. The article states students 'should be encouraged to identify and capitalise upon them when they arise' during school placements and practicums.
