Design implications for web-enhanced case-based activity environments¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends five sets of design activities: designing and indexing experts' case libraries (including experts' stories, artifacts, and situational vignettes, indexed by domain knowledge structure); analyzing and using experts' case libraries (case discussion and written analysis between scenario work and planning); designing CBA structures (sequencing authentic tasks from simple to complex); supporting participation in social practices (peer feedback, group discussion, instructor feedback); and developing learners' case libraries (reflection stories with contextual information, lessons, and keywords for indexes).

  • Experts' cases must be selected and indexed to represent the specific practices emphasized

  • The article notes the effectiveness of CBA learning environments needs to be verified through iterative implementation

Claims¶

  • Preservice teachers reported that case analysis expanded and deepened their understanding of teaching with technology [+W]
  • Preservice teachers in web-enhanced CBA showed improved understanding of technology integration through sequenced authentic tasks [+W]
  • More than 40% of surveyed faculty reported improved preservice teacher technology knowledge after three semesters of InTime video case use [+W]
