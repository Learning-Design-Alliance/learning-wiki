Instructors on television should speak at a deliberate rate and use deliberate movements¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The abstract's conclusions on instructor presentation specify two on-camera behaviors: "A deliberate rate of speaking is desirable" and "Deliberate movements by the instructor are mandatory". These are implementable presenter techniques for anyone teaching over television, derived from the army training research summarized on the page.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • An instructor presenting on camera who can control speaking pace and gesture deliberately.

Constraints¶

  • Stated as conclusions of army training TV research; no supporting statistics are printed.

Target Learners¶

  • Army trainees viewing instructional television

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Clear, followable instructor presentation on television

Related Strategies¶

  • Use direct narration by an instructor talking directly to trainees when producing television instruction.
  • Use Body Language and Demonstrations
  • Increase Synchronous Teacher Presence
