Address the usage gap through learning design that crosses boundaries between academic, personal lifestyle and professional spheres¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that learning design address the usage gap by "crossing boundaries between academic, personal lifestyle and professional sphere eliciting for authentic learning", citing Istenič Starčič et al. (2018). It further urges that in the rapid shift to online, learning design could capitalize on the increased availability of learning resources for interactive authentic learning when designing inclusive learning environments for emergent requirements. Digital practices saturating students' lifeworlds are to be scrutinized when designing students' learning experience in search of authentic social practices.
