When pivoting quickly to digital, use systematic models and tools and deliberate reflection in the design process¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Among the responses on the practice of designing for learning, the editorial reports that when pivoting quickly to the digital space, "design practices should include the use of systematic models and tools to support teachers in their development processes" (Connolly and Hall 2020) and that teachers should "deliberately reflect as part of the design process" (Hrastinski 2020). This strategy pairs structured design support with intentional reflection during emergency transitions to digital delivery.

Target Learning Goals¶

  • effective instructional design for online and blended learning

Claims¶

  • Rapid shifts from face-to-face to digital delivery during the pandemic forced teachers to sacrifice appropriate designs to meet deadlines [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Support rapid digital pivots with non-systematic design cycles, humanizing pedagogy, culturally responsive designs and flexible pathways
