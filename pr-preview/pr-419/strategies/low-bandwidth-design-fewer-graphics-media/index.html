Design systems to accommodate low bandwidth limitations by using fewer graphics and media¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

For contexts where users may only have internet access through low bandwidth connections, the article recommends that designers "aim to design systems that accommodate low bandwidth limitations by using fewer graphics and media." This is an implementable design recipe drawn directly from the infrastructure challenges section.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Knowledge that target users face low bandwidth connectivity

Constraints¶

  • Stated for developing-country contexts with unreliable telecommunications grids

Target Learners¶

  • internet-connected learners with low bandwidth access

Target Learning Goals¶

  • reliable access to web-delivered instruction

Related Strategies¶

  • Design OEP for accessibility and marginalized participation rather than open for all
  • Consider Socio-Cultural Factors
