Five design guidelines for educational chatbots: comprehensive CCQs, better NLU, error detection, easy access, progressive refinement¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Drawing on their interaction-pathway findings, the authors recommend that future chatbot designers: redesign CCQs to probe knowledge beyond true/false judgements; improve natural language understanding to manage a wider range of conversational forms; enhance the chatbot's ability to detect and help correct student errors; ensure easy access, e.g. embedding the chatbot window or sidebar in a webpage; and progressively refine the chatbot from user input data. They state "Progressive refinement from user input can successively improve chatbot performance and enhance student learning and interaction".

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Iterative analysis of user interaction logs to drive successive redesign of chatbot scripts

Constraints¶

  • The guidelines arise from a pilot with 23 participants and one feedback task; the authors recommend generalizing to other genres such as lab reports or expository essays

Target Learners¶

  • post-secondary students using instructional chatbots

Target Learning Goals¶

  • effective peer feedback and writing skills

Related Strategies¶

  • Design hybrid instructional chatbot systems that balance breadth and depth via multi-agent architectures or adaptive interaction modes
  • Design chatbots for differentiated engagement across proficiency levels, beyond simple error correction
  • Chatbot support should reinforce that learning ability develops through effort, strategy use, feedback, and reflection
