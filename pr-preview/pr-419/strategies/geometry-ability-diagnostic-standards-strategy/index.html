Use diagnostic test standards to decide when a class needs special work on a fundamental ability¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends administering tests like A-D early in the geometry course and comparing a class's positive and negative scores against the published medians, 25th and 75th percentiles. A class "above the median scores" and especially near or above the 75th percentile signals the abilities are sufficiently developed; a class below the median, especially near or below the 25th percentile, calls for special effort to develop the ability in question.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • The tests must be applied fairly early in the study of the subject, while there is yet time to remedy difficulties; positive and negative scores are kept separately

Constraints¶

  • The standards in Tables V and VI come from classes that had just completed the first two books of geometry and may not generalize beyond that point

Target Learners¶

  • High school geometry classes early in the course

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Diagnosing and remediating weaknesses in the abilities fundamental to geometric proof
