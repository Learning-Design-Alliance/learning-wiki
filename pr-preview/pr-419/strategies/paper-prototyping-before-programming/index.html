Use paper prototyping before programming to help students formulate app design ideas¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article's lessons used a paper prototyping activity in which students brainstormed how to implement area calculation of different shapes and drew app interfaces with visible components on paper before programming in App Inventor. This let students 'formulate their working flow by listing possible inputs, outcomes, and cases' and decompose problems before coding. Teachers can adopt this sequence to scaffold the transition from mathematical thinking to computational design.

Claims¶

  • Students transferred abstract area concepts to concrete app examples, cultivating mathematical thinking [+W]
  • Students articulated problem solutions in well-defined rules and steps, proposing gamified area-calculation games [+W]
  • Students developed communication and collaboration skills through group-based app design [+W]
  • Students developed mobile CT concepts, practices, and perspectives through app development activities [+W]
  • Students successfully transformed mathematical area formulas into computational representations using blockly components [+W]
