Invest in retention efforts for teachers of color that improve working conditions and provide personal and professional growth opportunities¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The third policy lever is to invest in retaining teachers of color through improved working conditions and opportunities for personal and professional growth. Proposed solutions include creating a reimbursement program aiding pre-service and in-service educators, pushing state leaders to allocate funding to specific retention efforts, and creating a task force. Minnesota's $6 million investment in 2022 and 2023 for affinity groups and other retention strategies prioritizing teachers of color is cited as a promising practice.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Dedicated state funding for retention efforts such as reimbursement programs, affinity groups, and task forces

Target Learners¶

  • pre-service and in-service educators of color
  • state leaders

Target Learning Goals¶

  • retaining teachers of color in the profession

Related Strategies¶

  • Retain teachers of color through leadership pipelines, multi-year induction and mentoring, and culturally responsive school climates
  • Underwrite comprehensive preparation, improve teaching conditions, raise compensation, and expand induction to retain teachers of color
  • State and federal policy recommendations: recruit and prepare more candidates, retain new teachers through early-career mentoring, and improve working conditions
  • A federal Marshall Plan for Teaching focused on seven areas: compensation, debt-free preparation, high-retention pathways, mentoring, expertise-sharing, school redesign, and accountability
  • State policy actions across the career continuum: attract, prepare, and develop/support/retain educators
  • Build high-retention, supportive pathways into teaching for candidates of color
  • Six state-level strategies for increasing the racial diversity of the educator workforce
  • Set clear numeric educator-diversity goals at the state, district, and teacher preparation levels with public progress reporting
  • Invest in high-quality induction and mentoring programs, which are associated with higher retention particularly for teachers of color
  • Invest in retention of teachers of color through residencies, affinity groups, and induction/mentoring
  • Institutional, state, and federal actions to increase faculty diversity
