Apply DCT to educational research and teacher education in the science and practice of educational psychology¶

Description¶

The article identifies specific application areas for dual coding theory within educational psychology: educational research and teacher education. It states that "DCT also has important implications for the science and practice of educational psychology — specifically, for educational research and teacher education." The strategy is to bring DCT's mechanistic, imagery-and-verbal account to bear on how educational research is conducted and how teachers are prepared.
