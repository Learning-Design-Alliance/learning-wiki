Align AI guidelines with broader institutional policies while addressing discipline-specific learning objectives and workforce demands¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that AI guidelines in higher education be aligned with broader institutional policies while also addressing discipline-specific learning objectives and evolving workforce demands. It argues policies supporting student competency development in emerging technologies must move beyond safety and adoption questions to address how AI is integrated into teaching and learning, and that faculty and students should be brought into policy design with continuous iteration.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Regular policy review as technological capabilities and workforce demands evolve
  • A continuous feedback loop where teaching and pedagogy informs the policy and vice versa

Constraints¶

  • Most institutions are not yet meeting accreditation expectations of unified integration of curriculum, policy, assessment, and infrastructure

Target Learners¶

  • university and business school students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • AI literacy as a competency for entry-level professionals
  • integration of AI into discipline-specific teaching and assessment

Claims¶

  • Only eight of 130 sampled universities had a business-school-specific AI policy, limiting discipline-specific alignment [+W]
  • AI adoption levels and policy implementation vary unevenly across academic units, shaped by discipline and external industry alignment [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Ground AI integration in higher education on core principles of human agency, academic freedom, transparency, ethics, inclusivity, and critical thinking
  • Structure GenAI use guidelines as a scaffold progressing from what GenAI is, through affordances, to consequences and collective responsibilities
  • Four strategic recommendations for supporting AI literacy across settings
