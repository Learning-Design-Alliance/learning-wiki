K-12 institutions can use the Willis et al. typology as a starting place for reviewing their ethical oversight of student data analysis¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends a concrete first step for K-12 schools shifting to digital: use the existing typology to begin reviewing ethical oversight when analyzing student data. As printed, "K-12 educational institutions can utilize the manuscript as a starting place for review of their ethical oversights when analyzing student data as more schools are shifting to digital."
