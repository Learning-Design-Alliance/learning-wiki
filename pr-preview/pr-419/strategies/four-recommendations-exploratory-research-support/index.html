Four field-level recommendations for supporting exploratory, interdisciplinary research on emerging learning technologies¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Based on the thematic analysis of PI interviews, the report recommends four actions for funders and the field. The first is to "Create consistent opportunities for the field to engage in future-oriented, exploratory research across directorates and programs in directorates." The remaining recommendations are to intentionally nurture interdisciplinary research identities and researcher-practitioner partnerships; to continue uncovering processes, methods, and flows uniquely important in exploratory, interdisciplinary research; and to support coordination networks composed of researchers and practitioners to grow the field, synthesize outcomes, and amplify broader impacts.

Requirements¶

  • Recommendations derive from a thematic analysis of interviews with PIs in one NSF portfolio, not from an experimental test

  • Research funders and program officers
  • learning sciences and computer science researchers
  • practitioners partnering in research

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Guiding effective use of emerging technology to increase learning and broaden participation in STEM
