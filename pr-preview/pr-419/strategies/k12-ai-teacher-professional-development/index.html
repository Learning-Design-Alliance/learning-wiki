Create AI professional development for K-12 teachers addressing both AI content knowledge and AI pedagogical knowledge, for in-service and pre-service teachers¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (1 causal, 1 review, 1 design), q2 · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article argues for developing "the most effective AI professional development (PD) for K-12 teachers," entailing "a research program in design principles for creating professional development around both AI content knowledge and AI pedagogical knowledge." PD research should take into account the broad range of competencies in AI that teachers and administrators begin with, and should address pre-service as well as in-service teachers.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Research program in design principles for professional development around AI content knowledge and AI pedagogical knowledge

Constraints¶

  • Teachers and administrators begin with a broad range of competencies in AI that PD approaches must take into account

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 teachers
  • K-12 administrators
  • pre-service teachers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • AI content knowledge
  • AI pedagogical knowledge

Claims¶

  • Short-duration TPACK-based professional development improved teachers' ML knowledge and interest (ML4STEM) [+W]
  • An online PD program using unplugged explainable machine-learning activities increased K–12 educators' AI self-efficacy from below-moderate to above-moderate levels [+W]
  • After an SDT-driven redesign of a two-day teacher workshop (one cohort of 26, no control group), self-reported AI-TPACK (d = 0.70), IPACK (d = 0.86) and AI attitude (d = 0.58) rose significantly from pre to post [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Develop research programs across four areas of K-12 AI education: Learning & Teaching, Integration into STEM/Language Arts/Social Science, Professional Development, and Assessment
  • Integrate AI education throughout the K-12 curriculum via innovative approaches to integrative AI-science, AI-math, AI-language arts, and AI-social science education
