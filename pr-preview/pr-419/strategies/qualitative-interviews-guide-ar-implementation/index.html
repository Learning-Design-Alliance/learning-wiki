Use qualitative interview data from teachers and students to account for quantitative results and derive productive AR implementation strategies¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The study complemented its quasi-experiment with qualitative analysis of interview data from teachers and students. The article states this analysis "aids in accounting for the quantitative results and indicate productive implementation strategies", and that findings will guide the design of future AR interventions for researchers and practitioners.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Interviews with both teachers and students to interpret quantitative outcomes

Constraints¶

  • Findings are framed as insights for designing future AR interventions, not as validated prescriptions

Target Learners¶

  • Primary school students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Effective integration of AR with pedagogical approaches in science lessons
