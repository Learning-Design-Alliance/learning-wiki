Engage pre-service instructional designers early in identity work through autobiographical writing and deconstructed situated experiences¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

In its final thoughts the article asks how graduate programs in instructional design might be redefined within the framework of agentic practice, recommending "engaging pre-service designers early in identity work through approaches such as autobiographical writing, providing more situated experiences that are then deconstructed in group conversations." The aim is to develop the moral dimensions of practice through reflexive dialogue rather than treating designers as technicians.
