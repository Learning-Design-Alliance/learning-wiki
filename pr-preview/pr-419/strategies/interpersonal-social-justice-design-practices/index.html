Interpersonal design practices for centering social justice: co-design, cultural competence, building understanding, and listening¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article documents specific implementable practices participants used to address justice-based tensions. These include treating all team members as co-designers from the project's start, developing cultural competence and accountability practices, making knowledge systems visible, relying on a cultural insider, listening to partners "in their own voices," front-loading DEI issues with faculty, using DEI frames to analyze courses, developing team norms, and using multiple modes of communication and reflection.
