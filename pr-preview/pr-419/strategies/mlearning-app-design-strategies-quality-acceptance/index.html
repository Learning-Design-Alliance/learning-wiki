Design m-learning apps for offline access, cross-device synchronization, context-awareness, personalized paths, and responsive support services¶

Strategy · All strategies

Description¶

Based on the finding that mobility, compatibility, and service quality drive acceptance, the authors recommend concrete app design practices. For mobility: "these apps should allow learners to download content for offline use" with optimal caching and cloud-based cross-platform synchronization; they also recommend collaborating with app developers on GPS-based context-aware learning experiences. For compatibility, they recommend leveraging AI and machine learning to provide personalized learning paths. For service quality, "m-learning developers must ensure their apps are accessible, personalized, responsive, and trusted by learners," including 24/7 support through multiple channels, self-help resources, and knowledgeable support teams.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • App quality factors must be addressed for the app to achieve adoption; the article states that failing to address quality, an m-learning app may fail to achieve widespread adoption or long-term engagement

Constraints¶

  • Recommendations are grounded in the out-of-classroom adult-learner context studied; the authors note the role of the content quality dimension outside the classroom should be further studied

Target Learners¶

  • Adult m-learning app users learning outside the classroom, both students and non-students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Sustained acceptance and use of m-learning apps for voluntary learning beyond the classroom

Claims¶

  • Perceived compatibility of an m-learning app positively influences both perceived usefulness and perceived ease of use in out-of-classroom settings [+W]
  • Perceived mobility of an m-learning app positively influences both perceived usefulness and perceived ease of use in out-of-classroom settings [+W]
  • Service quality positively influences perceived usefulness but not perceived ease of use of m-learning apps outside the classroom [+W]
  • Content design quality and interactivity show no significant influence on perceived usefulness or perceived ease of use of out-of-classroom m-learning apps [-W]
