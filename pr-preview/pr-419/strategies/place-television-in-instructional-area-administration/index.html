Place television within the administrative organization of the instructional area of the college¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Based on the college television instruction experience, the abstract recommends that television should be placed within the administrative organization of the instructional area of the college. It further reports that under the existing conditions it was feasible to offer a lecture-discussion course for 950 students and a more expensive course with a minimum of 1440 students, tying administrative placement to scale of operation.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Institutional television instruction should be administered within the instructional area of the college rather than outside it.

Constraints¶

  • The feasibility figures apply under the existing conditions of that college, as stated in the abstract.

Target Learners¶

  • college students in large lecture-discussion courses

Target Learning Goals¶

  • delivering instruction at scale via television

Claims¶

  • College students taught by television compared favorably with those receiving regular instruction, with attitudes differing only for the on-campus television group [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Substitute sound filmstrips for more expensive films or television, and use reading assignments in place of classroom lectures
