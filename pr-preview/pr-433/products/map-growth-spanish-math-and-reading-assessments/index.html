MAP Growth Spanish Math and Reading assessments¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

NWEA's computer-adaptive Spanish-language Math and Reading assessments measure student achievement and growth across specified grades and testing windows, using MAP Growth scales, norms, and blueprints where applicable.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Measurement precision is strongest for students in the middle of the Spanish score distribution, with CSEM near the 3.3 RIT target [+W]
  • Most Spanish test items show negligible differential item functioning across gender and Hispanic–White comparisons [+W]
  • The vast majority of Spanish Math (91%) and Reading (98%) items show no misfit across fall, winter, and spring terms [+W]
  • Spanish Math items show well-balanced difficulty and stable strong discrimination across 2024–2025 terms [+W]
  • Spanish Reading items show gradually increasing mean p values and stable discrimination with no negatively discriminating items [+W]
  • Spanish Math and Reading scores show high marginal reliability and moderate-to-strong test-retest stability, lowest for fall–spring and kindergarten [+W]
