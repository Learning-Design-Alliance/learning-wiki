ELM-ART adaptive and intelligent Web-based LISP textbook¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

ELM-ART is a Web-based interactive and intelligent textbook developed by Gerhard Weber and colleagues for teaching introductory LISP programming through adaptive navigation, assessment, diagnosis, feedback, and learner modeling.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Students performed better in final programming tasks after learning with ELM-ART than with ELM-PE, especially students with lower levels of previous programming knowledge [+M]
  • Monitoring drop-out rates at specific pages identified difficult text, and revising those pages lowered drop-out rates back to normal [+M]
