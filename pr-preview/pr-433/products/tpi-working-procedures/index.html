TPI working procedures¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A structured framework developed for the TPI programme to guide online trainee researchers, tutors, and students through progressive research-project stages and related learning activities.

Components¶

  • Highly structured, sequenced progression of research stages guiding online trainee researchers: The TPI working procedures evolved from an initial version covering only research project stages to a final version adding work patterns for tutor and student, action and implementation, and relation to virtual tutoring environments. The stages "can be understood as gradual phases that will help to build the research project", following the Ewing model of "a highly structured and sequenced curriculum" that guides students through "a series of progressive research stages", facilitating dialogue, collaborative learning, and assessment based on research skills performance. (Darder et al. (2015))

Claims¶

  • Implementation of the TPI procedure reached only one tutor-trainee pair out of nine potential tutors, though participants assessed it positively [~W]
