bettermarks¶

Description¶

bettermarks is a mathematics intelligent tutoring system that provides adaptive practice, detects learner errors and knowledge gaps, and assigns targeted learning materials for school students.

Components¶

  • Adaptive knowledge gap mechanism: repeated-error detection triggers tailored additional learning materials closed at 60% accuracy: The bettermarks ITS automatically detects knowledge gaps when students commit the same error repeatedly, reflecting specific misconceptions such as adding fractions with uncommon denominators. It then adaptively assigns tailored additional learning materials in a separate Knowledge gaps tab, which students work through on their own schedule. A gap counts as closed when students "achieve a minimum accuracy of 60 %" on the additional materials, at which point the red badge is removed or its count reduced. The mechanism operates alongside teacher-assigned work, so the materials are system- rather than teacher-assigned. (Spitzer et al. (2025))

Claims¶

  • Students closed a higher relative number of knowledge gaps when a red notification badge nudged them than when it was turned off [+M]
  • Closing knowledge gaps showed the highest association with performance among all considered variables (partial r = 0.17) [+M]
