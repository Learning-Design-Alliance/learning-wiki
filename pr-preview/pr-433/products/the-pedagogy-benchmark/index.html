The Pedagogy Benchmark¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A publicly released dataset developed by Lelièvre et al. from curated Chilean teacher-exam multiple-choice questions for evaluating large language models' general and special-education pedagogical knowledge.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Some LLMs exhibit a selection bias against selecting option D on multiple-choice pedagogy questions [~W]

  • PersonaPath benchmark for Knowledge-Centric learning path planning

  • EEG familiarity benchmark dataset and evaluation framework (3960 samples, 23 participants, two validation protocols)

  • PhysicsMate benchmark of 1,834 curriculum-grounded Bengali physics QA pairs
