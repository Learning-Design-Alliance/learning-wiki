Chapter One¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Chapter One is a push-in, one-on-one early-literacy tutoring programme that embeds interventionists in K–1 classrooms and provides aligned digital practice, operated by Chapter One.

Components¶

  • Chapter One one-on-one in-class tutoring program for early literacy: Chapter One is a "one-on-one personalized tutoring program that embeds part-time tutors into the classroom to provide short bursts of instruction to individual students each week over the course of the school year." It uses a push-in model with Early Literacy Interventionists serving multiple classrooms, a digital curriculum for sessions and tracking, and daily 15-minute independent tablet practice aligned to recent tutored instruction. The program targets phonics development and reading fluency in kindergarten and first grade. (Chapter One (2024))
