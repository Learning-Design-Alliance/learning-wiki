Building Pathways for Student Success (BPSS)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A partnership-led logic model supporting the Arkansas Department of Education in examining and improving college- and career-readiness programming for Arkansas students.

Components¶

  • BPSS partnership logic model for supporting ADE college and career readiness programming: The BPSS partnership is organized around a stated goal: "To support the Arkansas Department of Education (ADE) in both examining and improving programming to increase college and career readiness for Arkansas students." The logic model arranges activities and outcomes in short-term, medium-term, and long-term columns spanning ADE staff capacity, district participation, and student outcomes. It anchors two studies (AIIM and THRIVE) and a planned 2024–2026 impact-assessment phase. (Partnership Activities — Building Pathways for Student Success (BPSS) (2026))

Claims¶

  • The BPSS logic model targets increased college readiness among Arkansas student populations with persistent outcome disparities [+W]
  • The BPSS logic model sets reduced discipline disparities as a long-term student outcome for THRIVE and AIIM districts [+W]
  • The logic model's medium-term outcomes center on ADE staff data-use and formative-evaluation capacity [+W]
