Cortex¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), q2 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Cortex is a proprietary platform developed by Cortex Global that uses handwritten-response recognition and mark-scheme-aligned automated grading to assess complex upper-secondary STEM answers and provide annotated feedback.

Components¶

  • Cortex automated handwritten STEM assessment marking platform: Cortex is a proprietary automated grading platform for complex handwritten A-Level STEM responses, combining Handwritten Text Recognition fine-tuned on "complex mathematical notation, chemical structures, and physical diagrams" with deterministic mark-scheme-aligned evaluation. It operates through educator, student, and administrator portals; students handwrite solutions on paper, photograph and upload them, and receive grades with granular marginalia within 2 hours. Its defining feature is deterministic Error Carried Forward logic that docks method marks for early errors while awarding ECF marks for valid subsequent logic. (Matey Yordanov et al. (2026))

Claims¶

  • Automated marking reduced assessment turnaround from 11.2 days to 0.1 days and enabled intervention students to complete 4.1 times the practice volume of controls [+M]
  • Step-by-step automated annotations accelerated correction of procedural misconceptions, with intervention students rectifying fundamental errors weeks before control peers [+M]
  • AI total scores correlate strongly with official human scores on handwritten physics assessments (r = 0.91–0.97 in Round I; 0.93–0.96 in Round II) [+W]
