Ladders to Literacy¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A supplemental kindergarten literacy curriculum developed by Notari-Syverson, O’Connor, and Vadasy and published by Sopris West, with activities targeting print awareness, phonological awareness, and oral language.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Ladders to Literacy shows mixed effects on comprehension, with no statistically significant vocabulary effects in any study despite an average improvement index of +9 [~W]
  • No studies meeting WWC evidence standards addressed general reading achievement for Ladders to Literacy [~W]
  • Ladders to Literacy shows potentially positive effects on alphabetics, with an average improvement index of +25 percentile points and a domain average effect size of 0.69 [+W]
  • Ladders to Literacy shows potentially positive effects on fluency based on a single non-significant finding with an effect size of 0.72 and an improvement index of +26 [+W]
  • Segmentation is the only alphabetics outcome the WWC confirmed statistically significant across multiple studies [+W]
