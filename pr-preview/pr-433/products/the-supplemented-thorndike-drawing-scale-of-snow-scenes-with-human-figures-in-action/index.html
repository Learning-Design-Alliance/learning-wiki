The supplemented Thorndike drawing scale of snow scenes with human figures in action¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A norm-referenced rating scale developed by Childs for judging elementary and secondary pupils' snow-scene drawings by comparison with anchored sample drawings and averaged ratings from two judges.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Gaps in the scale's sample range produce distribution anomalies, with fewer ratings in the 4 to 4.99 band than in the 3 to 3.99 band [~W]
  • Drawing ability scores rise from grade IB to grade 8A, but the data support group norms rather than a distinctive norm for each grade [+W]
