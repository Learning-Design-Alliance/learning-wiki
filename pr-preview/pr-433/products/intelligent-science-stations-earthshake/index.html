Intelligent Science Stations (EarthShake)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A mixed-reality science-learning system developed by Yannier et al. that uses depth sensing, computer vision, and an animated guide to support elementary learners' inquiry into balance and stability.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Adaptive condition shows only a non-significant trend for transfer to engineering outcomes (tower building) [~W]
  • Adaptivity does not sacrifice enjoyment [+W]
  • Log analysis shows adaptive condition concentrates practice on unmastered concepts while random condition over-practices easy ones [+W]
  • BKT adaptivity in a mixed-reality Intelligent Science Station improves children's post-test learning of scientific principles compared to random task selection [+W]
