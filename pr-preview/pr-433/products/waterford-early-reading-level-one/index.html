Waterford Early Reading Level One™¶

Description¶

Waterford Early Reading Level One™ is a Waterford computer-based emergent-literacy curriculum that provides individualized early reading instruction and related print, phonological-awareness, and teacher-monitoring materials for preschool learners.

Components¶

  • Waterford Early Reading Level One™ computer-based emergent literacy curriculum: Waterford Early Reading Level One™ is "an emergent literacy curriculum that uses computer-based technology to prepare children for reading." It begins with a computer/mouse tutorial and a reading placement evaluation assigning children to Level One objectives (capital letters, lowercase letters, or beginning decoding skills). It provides individualized, year-long instruction in daily 15-minute sessions, supplemented by phonological and phonemic awareness activities, letter recognition, story and print concepts, books, videos, and take-home materials, with teacher reports for monitoring progress. (What Works Clearinghouse (2007))
