Description¶

myHeggerty is Heggerty's digital platform providing optional resources for teachers' planning, professional learning, and classroom instruction.

Components¶

  • myHeggerty optional digital resources for planning, professional learning, and classroom instruction: myHeggerty is the program's optional digital platform. The curriculum does not require classroom technology, but "the program does include digital resources via myHeggerty that teachers can use to support their planning and professional learning and enhance classroom instruction." Use of these materials may involve laptops, iPads, or interactive whiteboards or projectors. (Bridge to Reading evidence summary (2025))
