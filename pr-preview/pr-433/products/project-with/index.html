Project With¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Project With is a 12-lesson literary-based relationship-skills and self-management curriculum adapted from The Art of Loving Well for justice-involved youth.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Youth reported a mixed pattern of impact on the success sequence: 60–61 percent were more likely to plan a steady full-time job before having a child or marrying, but only 28–35 percent were more likely to plan to delay sexual intercourse until milestones [~W]
  • Youth most positively reported Project With's impact on goal setting, with 81 percent saying it made them more likely to make plans to reach their goals [+W]
  • Youth reported positive perceived impacts on healthy relationships (77 percent) and on self-regulation and decision-making (62–66 percent) [+W]
  • Justice-involved youth who remained at their sites completed an average of 11.0 of 12 Project With lessons, while those who left early completed 5.4 [+W]
  • Project With was implemented with high fidelity: 89 percent of activities were completed and youth listened during 83 percent and interacted during 76 percent of activities [+W]
