Eight-dimension AI competency framework for K-12 students¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A competency framework developed by Thomas K. F. Chiu and colleagues to organize and assess how K–12 students can use AI ethically, safely, healthily, and productively across eight learning-related dimensions.

Components¶

  • Eight-dimension AI competency framework for K-12 students grounded in teacher perspectives: The framework defines student AI competency as the capacity "to use AI to support their learning ethically, healthily, and productively" across eight dimensions: Interdisciplinary Learning with AI (ILAI), Assessment with AI (ASAI), Decision Making with AI (DMAI), Data, Ethics, and AI (DEAI), Designing AI (DGAI), Multimedia Creation with AI (MCAI), Human-Centric Learning (HCLG), and Confidence with AI (CFAI). It was derived from teacher panelists via Delphi consensus and extends UNESCO's four-component framework by adding learning-activity dimensions such as interdisciplinary learning, assessment, and multimedia creation. Each dimension is elaborated with example competencies, e.g., judging AI-generated essays (DMAI) and protecting personal data (DEAI). (Thomas K. F. Chiu et al. (2025))

Claims¶

  • A three-round Delphi study with 29 K-12 AI teachers produced an eight-dimension, 32-item student AI competency framework at a 75% agreement level [+M]
