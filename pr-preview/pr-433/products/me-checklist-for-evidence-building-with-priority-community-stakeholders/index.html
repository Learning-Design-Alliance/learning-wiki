M&E Checklist for evidence-building with priority-community stakeholders¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes

Description¶

A project-developed checklist from the Evaluating the Development of Middle Years Math Teaching and Learning Solutions project, prepared for the Gates Foundation, that guides solution teams through stakeholder-collaborative evidence building and testing.

Components¶

  • Measurement and Evaluation (M&E) Checklist for evidence-building with priority-community stakeholders: The M&E Checklist is a resource that guides users through an evidence-building process to design, refine, and test a solution in collaboration with stakeholders from a priority community. It was developed under the project Evaluating the Development of Middle Years Math Teaching and Learning Solutions (2018-2023), prepared for the Gates Foundation. The checklist structures evidence building so that solution teams work directly with community stakeholders rather than testing in isolation. (Ryan Ruggiero et al. (2023))
