East Alabama Regional Cybersecurity Alliance (EARCA)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A regional alliance run by partner school districts, industry, and postsecondary organizations in Alabama Workforce Region 2 to expand rural cybersecurity education, workforce preparation, and career opportunities.

Components¶

  • Inclusive Cybersecurity Pathways with Workforce Credentials: An inclusive cybersecurity pathways initiative offering workforce credentials, enrolling "Over 800 students... across 10 school districts reflecting a uniquely diverse student population — 25 percent female, 12 percent with learning differences, 43 percent free and reduced lunch, 54 percent students of color." The pathway is one of the differentiated solutions produced through Inclusive Innovation that reflects centering the leadership, voice and needs of educators, students and communities. (Smith et al. (2024))

Claims¶

  • Talladega County's cybersecurity program grew from 81 participating students in 2023-24 to 208 in 2024-25 [+M]
