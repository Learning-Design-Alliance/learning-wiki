Description¶

CIRCLS.org is a Digital Promise–maintained online community hub connecting and supporting NSF-funded research on emerging technologies for teaching and learning.

Components¶

  • CIRCLS community hub for NSF emerging-technology teaching and learning research: The presentation introduces CIRCLS.org as the "Community Hub for NSF's 'Research on Emerging Technology for Teaching and Learning'", maintained in the author's context at Digital Promise, which works with large national networks of innovative schools such as the League of Innovative Schools and Verizon Innovative Learning Schools. CIRCLS is cited as a resource accompanying the CSCL scenarios the field is exploring for intelligence augmentation. (Roschelle J (2021))
