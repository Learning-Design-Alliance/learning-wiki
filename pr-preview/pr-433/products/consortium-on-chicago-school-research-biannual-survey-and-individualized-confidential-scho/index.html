Description¶

A biannual school-conditions survey and confidential school reporting system operated by the Consortium on Chicago School Research to support school self-assessment and long-term planning.

Components¶

  • Improving Chicago's Schools biannual survey and individualized confidential school reports: The Consortium on Chicago School Research's biannual survey collected responses from 'more than 132,000 students, teachers, and principals across the Chicago public school system' in spring 2005. The Consortium prepares individualized, confidential reports for every school meeting response-rate criteria; in 2005, 479 elementary and high schools qualified, and reports show how measures have changed over the ten years schools have participated. Reports are provided at the request of the principal to assist self-assessment and long-term planning. (Luppescu et al. (2005))
