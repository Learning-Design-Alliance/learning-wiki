Teaching is My Favorite Color¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative, 1 design), q2 · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A teacher-designed and teacher-led support and professional-development community for teachers of color across three southwestern Pennsylvania school districts.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Over 90 percent of Bristol teachers who participated in student-led mental health PD reported being more conscious of the experiences of students of color in school [+W]
  • Network learning spread beyond the network through a website, conference presentations, school and district sharing, and AIR-led professional learning communities [+W]
  • A regional collective initiative (Teaching is My Favorite Color) provided teachers of color a safe space, belonging, and authentic identity expression [+M]
