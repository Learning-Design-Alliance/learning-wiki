Insta-Reviewer¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 against) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Insta-Reviewer is a research-developed software system that extracts salient sentences from student project reports and uses a fine-tuned BART model to generate textual feedback.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • In one essay-feedback system, fine-tuned models produced no usable deep-level feedback output, so only directly prompted LLMs were evaluated; the authors suggest prompting may be more effective than supervised fine-tuning for this task [-W]

Related Products and Programmes¶

  • Dataset of 484 de-identified student project reports with instructor reviews collected over twelve semesters
