Roadmap Needs Assessment Five-Step Process¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The Roadmap Needs Assessment Five-Step Process is a named five-step community-needs-assessment framework developed and used by the Roadmap team, incorporating engagement and interagency coordination throughout.

Components¶

  • Five-step community needs assessment process with engagement and interagency coordination throughout: The Roadmap Needs Assessment Five-Step Process structures the assessment as a pyramid of five sequential steps, with community engagement and interagency coordination as essential components throughout. The report states the team "used the Roadmap Needs Assessment Five-Step Process (see Figure 2) to conduct the needs assessment" and that the process "encompasses all aspects of the methodology." The steps move from identifying existing assets by the Ten Aspects of Community and district, to identifying needs, interviewing key informants, developing recommendations filtered through guiding principles, and aligning systems to achieve desired outcomes. (Roadmap to the Future for Yolo County Children et al. (2024))
