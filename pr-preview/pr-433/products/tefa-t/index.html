TEFA-T¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A teaching-factory instructional framework developed for automotive vocational education that integrates vehicle troubleshooting with a nine-step work-process syntax and supporting instructional materials.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Students taught with the TEFA-T model rated their own metacognitive skills higher than a conventionally taught class [+M]
  • The TEFA-T model improves students' critical thinking skills on all measured indicators compared with conventional instruction [+M]
  • The TEFA-T model produces significantly higher learning achievement than teacher-based instruction in automotive vocational courses [+M]
  • The TEFA-T model syntax meets goodness-of-fit criteria in confirmatory factor analysis, supporting its construct validity [+M]
  • The TEFA-T model and its supporting products are rated Very Practical by instructors and students [+M]
