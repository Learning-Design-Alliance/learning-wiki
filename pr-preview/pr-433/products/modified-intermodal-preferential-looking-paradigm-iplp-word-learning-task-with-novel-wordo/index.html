Modified Intermodal Preferential Looking Paradigm (IPLP) word-learning task with novel word/object pairings¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A researcher-modified preferential-looking assessment developed and used by Houston et al. to measure infants' and young children's ability to associate spoken novel words with novel object referents.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Normal-hearing children demonstrate novel word learning on the IPLP task at approximately 18 months of age and older [+M]
  • Early implanted children performed significantly better on the word-learning task than late implanted children [+M]
  • Early implanted children and their NH age-matched peers demonstrated word learning, while late implanted children did not unlike their peers [+M]
  • Word-learning performance correlates with later vocabulary size in children with cochlear implants [+M]
