Description¶

iTalk2Learn is an EU-funded adaptive digital learning platform that combines an exploratory learning environment with an intelligent tutoring system and sequences activities using student interaction data.

Components¶

  • iTalk2Learn adaptive digital learning platform combining an ELE and an ITS: The iTalk2Learn platform is an adaptive digital learning platform developed in the EU-funded iTalk2Learn project that sequences content from two types of systems, an ELE and an ITS. It "was configured in two parallel versions, English and German, both of which combined an ELE delivering exploratory tasks and an ITS delivering structured tasks." A Student Needs Assessment component uses inputs such as screen/mouse actions, feedback-message counts, and speech to decide whether to keep a student in the ELE or switch to structured ITS practice. (Mavrikis et al. (2022))
