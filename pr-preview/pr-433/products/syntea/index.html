Description¶

Syntea is IU International University of Applied Sciences' AI-based learning-assistant software for question answering, interactive tutoring, personalized feedback, progress tracking, and exam preparation.

Components¶

  • Syntea AI-based learning assistant at IU International University of Applied Sciences: Syntea is an AI-based learning assistant developed by IU and integrated into its learning platform, also accessible via web and mobile applications. It supports "factual question answering, open-ended chat, interactive tutoring, and personalized exam training" and employs deep-dialog learning methods inspired by the Socratic approach. It can provide personalized feedback, track learning progress, and personalize content to career goals. During the study period it was built on GPT-4, GPT-4-Turbo, and GPT-3.5-Turbo, and was offered only in distance learning. (Kristina Schaaff et al. (2026))
