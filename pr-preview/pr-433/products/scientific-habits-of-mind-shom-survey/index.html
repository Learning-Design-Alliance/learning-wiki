Scientific Habits of Mind (SHOM) Survey¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A 32-item, seven-subscale self-report instrument developed by Çalık and Coll for measuring scientific habits of mind such as skepticism, objectivity, rationality, open-mindedness, curiosity, and resistance to authority.

Components¶

  • Scientific Habits of Mind framework (Çalık & Coll): SHOM characterizes how scientists think through habits including "skepticism, objectivity, rationality, suspension of belief, open-mindedness, mistrust of judgment from authority, and curiosity" (Gauld, 2005), elaborated with key features by Çalık and Coll (2012). The study uses this framework and its 32-item, seven-subscale survey to measure whether online argumentation fosters teacher candidates' SHOM. (Çakiroğlu et al. (2025))

Claims¶

  • Medium positive correlation between SHOM and argumentation skill levels [+M]
