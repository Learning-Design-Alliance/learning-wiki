Learning Management System Acceptance Scale¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The LMSAS is a self-report scale developed by Sezer and Yilmaz to measure learning management system acceptance across performance expectancy, effort expectancy, facilitating conditions, and social influence.

Components¶

  • LMSAS: Learning Management System Acceptance Scale with four UTAUT dimensions: The Learning Management System Acceptance Scale (LMSAS) by Sezer and Yilmaz (2019) was the data collection instrument. It "has four dimensions of performance expectancy, effort expectancy, facilitating conditions and social influence". Reliability was high: "The α coefficient for the whole scale is .926", with subscale coefficients of .934 (PE, 8 items), .785 (EE, 5 items), .785 (FC, 5 items) and .838 (SI, 3 items), all above the 0.70 threshold the authors cite for reliability. (Ozkan et al. (2020))
