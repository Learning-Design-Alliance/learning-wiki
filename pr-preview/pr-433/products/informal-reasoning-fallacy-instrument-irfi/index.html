Informal Reasoning Fallacy Instrument (IRFI)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A 50-item multiple-choice assessment developed by Ramasamy to measure undergraduate reasoning quality across five informal fallacies using a 0–2 scoring rubric.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Malaysian undergraduates scored a mean of 71.20 (SD 10.88) on the overall IRFI, with 183 of 189 scoring above the midpoint of 50 [+M]
  • Undergraduates' fallacy sub-scale means ranged from 13.45 to 15.31, with slippery slope highest and false analogy lowest [+W]
  • Among undergraduates who fell into a fallacy trap, false analogy was the most common and slippery slope the least common [+W]
