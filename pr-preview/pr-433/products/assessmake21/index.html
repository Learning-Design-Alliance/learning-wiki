Assessmake21¶

Description¶

Assessmake21 is a web-based digital self-assessment tool developed by Ourania Miliou and colleagues to help K–12 learners identify, reflect on, and document 21st-century skills during makerspace activities.

Components¶

  • Assessmake21 digital self-assessment tool for 21st-century skills in makerspaces: Assessmake21 is a web-based digital self-assessment tool, developed as an expansion of SkillsTrack, that supports self-assessment of 21st-century skills in makerspace contexts. Students tag a skill they are using during making, which triggers reflection challenges such as building a visual portfolio with uploaded photos; completing challenges progresses them through levels, and educators award badges. It offers a Definitions feature with conceptual and operational definitions drawn from frameworks such as P21, and an educator's dashboard showing individual and classroom progress. The article reports it offers tagging of five skills: Collaboration, Creativity, Problem-solving, Life/Social Skills, and Communication. (Ourania Miliou et al. (2024))

Claims¶

  • Teachers and students reported that the digital self-assessment tool raised students' awareness of 21st-century skills and helped them identify the skills during making [+M]
  • Tagging skills and completing challenges engaged students in self-assessment and reflection on their skill development [+M]
  • Making activities overshadowed self-assessment, which students sometimes perceived as an unnecessary interruption [-M]
