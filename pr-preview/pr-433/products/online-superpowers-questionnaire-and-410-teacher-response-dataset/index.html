Online superpowers questionnaire and 410-teacher response dataset¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A Ferreira et al. dataset of 410 Brazilian teachers' demographic, open-ended superpower, classroom-application, and smart-classroom perception responses collected through an online Google Forms questionnaire.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Teachers report a need for faster lesson planning and material preparation to gain time for higher-value activities [+W]
  • Teachers aspire to enhanced cognitive abilities such as superintelligence and super memory to support teaching [+W]
  • Teachers report a burden of daily bureaucratic tasks and a desire to automate them [+W]
  • Teachers report a need for automated grading and assessment to free time and prevent exhaustion [+W]
