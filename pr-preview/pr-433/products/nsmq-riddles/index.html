NSMQ Riddles¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A benchmark dataset created by George Boateng and colleagues containing 1,840 scientific and mathematical riddles from Ghana's National Science and Maths Quiz for evaluating automated question-answering systems.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • The best LLMs perform below the best student contestants in the real-time proxy riddle round [+W]
  • LLMs struggle most with Chemistry riddles, with best EM accuracy at or below 75% versus above 90% in other subjects [+W]
  • LLM performance drops from offline to real-time proxy evaluation when clues arrive incrementally [+W]
  • In offline evaluation on NSMQ Riddles, closed LLMs generally outperform open models and higher reasoning settings improve performance [+W]
  • Answering early on few clues correlates with worse real-time proxy performance [+W]
