Multimodal OBE examination-answer dataset of 485 scanned submissions with criterion-level rubric marks¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes

Description¶

A restricted research dataset created by Jahangir Alam SM et al. pairing 485 scanned higher-education examination submissions with criterion-level rubric marks and answer metadata for outcome-based education assessment research.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • A full answer-level audit confirmed 485 unique identifiers and validated that every stored total equals the sum of its criterion marks [+W]
  • The dataset's 12 question templates contain 47 criteria, with question maxima ranging from 5 to 16 marks [+W]
  • Subject distribution is uneven, with E-commerce contributing five answers and Machine Learning 88 of 485 [+W]
