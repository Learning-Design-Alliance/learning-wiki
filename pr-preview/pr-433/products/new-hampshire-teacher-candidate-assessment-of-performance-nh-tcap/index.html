New Hampshire Teacher Candidate Assessment of Performance (NH TCAP)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A teacher-candidate performance assessment adapted from the Performance Assessment for California Teachers and implemented by a consortium of New Hampshire higher-education institutions to support candidate learning and assess readiness to teach.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Preservice candidates pass teaching performance assessments at higher rates than intern candidates [+W]
  • Residents pass TPAs on their first attempt at higher rates than student teachers or interns in the linked subsample [+W]
  • Sufficient clinical feedback predicts TPA passing for preservice candidates but not for internship candidates [+W]
