Structured Word Inquiry (SWI)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A morphology-centered framework for teaching English reading and spelling by integrating grapheme–phoneme correspondences with morphology, etymology, and vocabulary through tools such as word sums and morphological matrices.
