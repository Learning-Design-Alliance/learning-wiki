Tool #2: Auditing Your Data Collection for Indicators of Distress¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A structured worksheet for school authorizers to audit how they collect data on indicators of school distress and identify gaps in those processes.

Components¶

  • Tool #2: Auditing Your Data Collection for Indicators of Distress: Tool #2 supports authorizers in "reviewing their data collection methods to determine where they are already collecting data on indicators and to close gaps to improve the identification of schools in distress." It maps each distress indicator to example artifacts and sources—board minutes, check-ins, audits, enrollment data, staff retention data—and asks authorizers to document informal versus formal collection mechanisms and identify gaps. Estimated time is 30 minutes. (Aimee Evan et al. (2021))
