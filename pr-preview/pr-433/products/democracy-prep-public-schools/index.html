Democracy Prep Public Schools¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 3 studies (3 causal), q2–q4 · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A charter-school network operated by Democracy Prep that serves middle- and high-school students through a civic-education-focused school model intended to prepare them for effective citizenship.

Components¶

  • Democracy Prep Public Schools charter network: Democracy Prep is a charter school network that, in the report's words, "seeks to prepare students for effective citizenship." The network operates middle and high-school grades, admitted early cohorts through admissions lotteries, and its civic outcomes were evaluated by matching lottery records to a national registration and voting database. (Brian Gill et al. (2018))

Claims¶

  • There is a 98 percent probability that enrolling in Democracy Prep produced positive impacts on both voter registration and voting [+M]
  • Enrolling in Democracy Prep increases student voter registration rates by about 16 percentage points [+M]
  • Enrolling in Democracy Prep increases student voting rates in the 2016 election by about 12 percentage points [+M]
  • Civic-education-focused schooling can boost civic participation in adulthood [+M]
  • Democracy Prep charter schools boost student voting, according to the article's headline finding [+M]
