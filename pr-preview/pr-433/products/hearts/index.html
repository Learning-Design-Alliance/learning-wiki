HEARTS¶

Description¶

A whole-school, trauma-informed programme developed by the University of California, San Francisco and implemented in San Francisco Unified School District schools through tiered supports, staff training, consultation, and student interventions.

Components¶

  • HEARTS whole-school trauma-informed program in San Francisco elementary schools: The Healthy Environments and Response to Trauma in Schools (HEARTS) program is "a whole‐school program developed by the University of California, San Francisco to promote school success for students who have been impacted by trauma," implemented in K-8th grade schools in the San Francisco Unified School District. It used the response-to-intervention three-tier framework of universal, selected, and targeted interventions with supports at system, adult (teacher/staff), and student levels, including policy changes, staff training and consultation on trauma-sensitive practices, and evidence-informed trauma-informed interventions. (Maynard BR et al. (2019))
