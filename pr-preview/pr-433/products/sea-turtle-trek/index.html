Sea Turtle Trek¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A 124-page environmental-education curriculum packet for grades 6–8, developed for Hammocks Beach State Park and providing hands-on sea-turtle and conservation activities aligned with North Carolina standards.

Components¶

  • Sea Turtle Trek Environmental Education Learning Experience packet: A 124-page educator's activity packet developed by a park superintendent for Hammocks Beach State Park, providing "environmental education through a series of hands-on activities geared to Hammocks Beach State Park." It targets grades 6-8, meets North Carolina standard course of study objectives, and includes an introduction to reptiles, a vocabulary list, scheduling worksheet, parental permission form, evaluation forms, and appendices with sea turtle fact sheets and conservation information. (Bland (1995))
