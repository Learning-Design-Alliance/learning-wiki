National Guard Youth ChalleNGe Program¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A National Guard-run residential programme for youth ages 16–18 who have left or been expelled from high school, combining GED preparation with quasi-military training and leadership, citizenship, service, life-skills, fitness, health, job-skills, and academic components.

Components¶

  • Three-phase residential quasi-military program with post-residential structured mentoring: The program organizes intervention delivery into three phases: a two-week residential pre-ChalleNGe introduction to rules and expectations, a 20-week highly structured quasi-military residential phase on a National Guard base or school campus, and a one-year post-residential phase. The article states "This phase involves structured mentoring with a mentor of the trainee's choosing," with staff maintaining at least monthly contact. The pattern pairs intensive residential structure with sustained community-based support during transition. (National Guard Youth ChalleNGe Program (2010))

Claims¶

  • The National Guard Youth ChalleNGe Program has potentially positive effects on completing school for at-risk youth, with 61% of the program group reporting a diploma or GED versus 36% of controls 21 months after random assignment [+W]
  • The evidence base for the program is a single RCT of 3,074 randomly assigned youth, with 1,196 in the follow-up analysis sample, and the WWC rates the extent of evidence as small [+W]
