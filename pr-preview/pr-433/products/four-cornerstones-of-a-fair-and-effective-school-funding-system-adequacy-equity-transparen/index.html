Description¶

An EdBuild-developed framework, summarized by The Education Trust–Midwest, for evaluating and reforming state school-funding systems through adequacy, equity, transparency, and responsibility.

Components¶

  • Four cornerstones of a fair and effective school funding system: adequacy, equity, transparency, and responsibility: Developed by EdBuild and summarized in this report, the framework holds that a fair school funding system rests on four cornerstones. "ADEQUACY: The school funding allocated through the state formula should be sufficient to support a rigorous, high-quality education program for all students." EQUITY requires targeting funds to districts according to students' greater needs; TRANSPARENCY requires spending reporting that creates a feedback loop between state decisions and district needs; RESPONSIBILITY treats district and school leaders as accountable, flexible decision-makers. The report uses the framework to evaluate Michigan's system and propose reforms. (The Education Trust–Midwest (2021))

Claims¶

  • Increases in school funding can improve student outcomes when funds support the specific needs of students [+M] (the premise of the adequacy and equity cornerstones)
  • Research on school finance reforms finds increased spending improves educational and life outcomes, with larger effects for students from low-income backgrounds, including causal evidence from California's Local Control Funding Formula [+M]

Related Products and Programmes¶

  • Weighted student-based funding formulas that allocate resources according to student needs: the funding mechanism the equity cornerstone calls for
  • Local Control Funding Formula (LCFF): a state formula built on need-based weights
