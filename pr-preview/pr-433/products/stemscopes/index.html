Description¶

STEMscopes is a comprehensive digital and print K–12 science curriculum with hands-on materials, interactive investigations, and embedded assessments, developed and provided by Accelerate Learning, Inc.

Components¶

  • STEMscopes digital science curriculum: STEMscopes is a comprehensive digital science curriculum for elementary science instruction that "uses a hands-on, inquiry-based approach aligned to state standards." It combines "digital and print resources as well as hands-on materials" and embeds "interactive investigations, real-world problem solving, and embedded formative assessments" to promote student engagement across diverse classrooms. (STEMscopes evidence rating summary (2024))
  • STEMscopes Math comprehensive core mathematics curriculum (K–Algebra 1): STEMscopes Math is "a comprehensive, standards-aligned core mathematics curriculum for grades K through Algebra 1, developed by Accelerate Learning." It is built around the 5E+IA instructional model, supports whole-group instruction, small-group differentiation, and blended learning, follows a Concrete-Representational-Abstract progression, and integrates digital tools including virtual manipulatives and PhET simulations alongside print materials. All student resources are available in English and Spanish. (STEMscopes Math Evidence Summary (2024))
