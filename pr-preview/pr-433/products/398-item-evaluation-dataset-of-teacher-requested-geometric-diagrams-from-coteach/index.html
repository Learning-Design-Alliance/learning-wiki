398-item evaluation dataset of teacher-requested geometric diagrams from Coteach¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes

Description¶

A dataset released by Vishal Kumar et al. containing 398 real teacher requests and generated geometric-diagram code instances from Coteach for evaluating the mathematical and spatial soundness of AI-generated educational diagrams.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Back-translation evaluation outperforms LLM-as-a-Judge (code+image) in agreement with human raters across four models [+M]
  • Back-translation yields higher human agreement on spatial checks, while LLM-as-a-Judge considerably outperforms it on the angle-label check [~W]
