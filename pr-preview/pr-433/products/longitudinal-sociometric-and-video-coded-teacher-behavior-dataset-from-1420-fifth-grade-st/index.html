Description¶

A research dataset assembled by Hendrickx et al. containing three-wave sociometric measures and first-time-point video-coded teacher behavior for 1,420 fifth-grade students across 56 classes.

Claims¶

  • Negative teacher behavior toward a student predicts peers' disliking of that student via peers' perceptions of the teacher's disliking, in a longitudinal mediation model [+M]
