Betty's Brain¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 review), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Betty's Brain is a teachable-agent learning environment in which students construct and teach causal models to a virtual agent while developing domain knowledge and self-regulated learning skills.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Students' learning strategies can be detected and interpreted from activity sequences in the Betty's Brain open-ended learning environment [+W]
  • In one video study of Betty's Brain, 77% of the feedback delivered by the agents was ignored by students. [-W]
  • More successful Betty's Brain students showed significantly higher science learning gains, more accurate causal map edits, and more contextually focused, less random activities. [+W]
