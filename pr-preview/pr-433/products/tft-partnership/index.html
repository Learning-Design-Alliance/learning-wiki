TFT partnership¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A REL Midwest partnership framework that organizes professional-learning activities and expected administrator, teacher, and student outcomes across short-, medium-, and long-term horizons.

Components¶

  • TFT partnership logic model linking administrator, teacher, and student outcomes across three time horizons: The partnership's theory of change organizes activities and outcomes into short-term, medium-term, and long-term columns for three actor groups. Short-term outcomes include teachers gaining "pedagogical knowledge and strategies related to fraction computation and rate and ratio"; medium-term outcomes include administrators aligning "district or school practices and PD offerings with recommendations"; the long-term student outcome is solving fraction computation and ratio and rate problems. (REL Midwest (2023))

Claims¶

  • The partnership's long-term goal is that students can solve fraction computation and ratio and rate problems [+W]
  • The partnership targets improved teacher instruction in eliciting student thinking, using representations, and focusing on math reasoning [+W]
