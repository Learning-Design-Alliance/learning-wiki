ECED¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Every Classroom, Every Day (ECED) is a two-year stand-alone high-school literacy and mathematics curriculum programme using Reading Matters, Writing Matters, and Power 10 strategies.

Components¶

  • Every Classroom, Every Day (ECED) two-year stand-alone literacy and math program for high schools: ECED is "a program for literacy and math in high schools provided over two years as a stand-alone class". It supplements regular English with "a structured literacy curriculum based on authentic expository texts", doubling students' exposure to English language arts. Year one (Reading Matters) builds comprehension and information-gathering; year two (Writing Matters) builds sharing, communicating, and personalizing knowledge; both years use the Power 10 strategies. The program reached 8,250 students in the evaluating study. (Every Classroom (2019))
  • Writing Matters second-year ECED curriculum: Writing Matters is "The second year curriculum" of ECED. It "strengthens students' abilities to share and communicate information with other, and identify ways to express and personalize their knowledge". Like Reading Matters, it is taught as a stand-alone class by reallocated school staff and is supported by the same professional development structure, though second-year professional development is optional but recommended. (Every Classroom (2019))
  • Designate an on-site instructional coach to monitor and support ECED implementation: ECED's staffing requirements state that implementation "Requires a designated on-site instructional coach or leader to monitor and support implementation", noting "This position may look different depending on size of school". Both curricula also "require a reallocation of existing school staff to teach each of the courses". The coach role is part of the program's implementation model rather than an evaluated manipulation in the outcome study. (Every Classroom (2019))
