Acciona Program (AP)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A Chilean National Council for Culture and the Arts, Ministry of Education, and Balmaceda Youth Art Foundation programme in which professional artists lead semester-long weekly arts workshops in public high schools, alongside schoolteachers.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Participation in at least two AP workshops produced significant GPA improvements of 0.55 sd overall, 0.61 sd language, 0.36 sd math, and 0.33 sd art [+M]
  • Two or more semesters of AP art workshops significantly raised the graphical TTCT 13-forces creativity score in a doubly robust regression, but not total TTCT scores [+M]
