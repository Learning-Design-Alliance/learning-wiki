EduGage dataset¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (2 design), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A multimodal learning-engagement dataset released by Zikang Leng and colleagues, containing synchronized sensor streams, engagement-probe ratings, video metadata, and quiz responses from self-guided video-learning sessions for participant-independent engagement-modeling research.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Videos experienced as higher attention difficulty were associated with lower normalized quiz learning gains, providing convergent evidence that the probe captures an educationally meaningful aspect of momentary engagement [+W]
  • A modality-aware reference model outperforms sensor-free, statistical, deep temporal, foundation-model, and LLM-based baselines for estimating minute-level attention difficulty from wearable sensing [+M]
  • Chest-worn ECG provides the strongest cardiac-sensing performance for attention-difficulty prediction, and fusing all cardiac streams does not improve upon chest ECG alone [+M]
  • Predictive performance of sensor configurations does not change monotonically with the number of sensing streams; preferred configuration depends on target metric and sensing cost [+M]
  • A head-confined multimodal configuration (EEG, eye tracking, forehead EDA) predicts held-out learners' five-level engagement ratings at 75.0% balanced 1-off accuracy, 12.0 points above the mode baseline [+M]
  • One-minute-ahead direct forecasting performs similarly to contemporaneous estimation, but performance degrades at longer horizons and a carry-forward baseline outperforms the sensor model at every future horizon [+W]
