North Carolina Generative AI Implementation Recommendations¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A framework issued by North Carolina to guide K–12 generative-AI implementation, including shared levels and disclosure recommendations for AI-assisted assessment work.

Components¶

  • North Carolina's five levels of AI Assessment with disclosure and citation recommendations: A feature of the North Carolina Generative AI Implementation Recommendations document is a five-level AI Assessment scale. Each level has a description, along with disclosure or citation recommendations. The report states that "A scale such as this can help build the common understanding and language to ensure fair and equitable treatment of issues of suspected plagiarism or cheating with AI in the K-12 setting." It is intended to support consistent handling of suspected AI-assisted academic integrity issues. (Roschelle et al. (2024))
