WWC seven-recommendation framework for preparing young children for school¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 4 studies (2 quant-synthesis, 2 review), q2–q4 · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A What Works Clearinghouse practice-guide framework that organizes seven evidence-informed recommendations for preschool educators across social-emotional, executive-function, mathematics, and literacy instruction.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • The recommendation to provide intentional, engaging social-emotional instruction carries strong evidence from five studies meeting WWC standards without reservations [+M]
  • A WWC expert panel distilled recent research into seven recommendations for preschool educators on preparing young children for school [+W]
  • No reviewed study directly compared developmental-progression-guided math instruction with instruction not guided by a progression [~W]
  • A WWC practice-guide panel rates developing and using clear behavior expectations as supported by strong evidence from 14 studies [+M]
  • Targeted instruction in number and operations improves young children's early math achievement, with a moderate level of supporting evidence [+M]
