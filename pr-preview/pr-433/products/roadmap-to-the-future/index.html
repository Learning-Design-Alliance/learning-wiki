Roadmap to the Future¶

Description¶

Roadmap to the Future is a community planning initiative whose team develops shared principles, priorities, needs assessments, and strategic actions for youth, families, and communities.

Components¶

  • Ground community needs assessment and planning in guiding principles of youth and family voice, access and equity, and alignment and coherence: The Roadmap to the Future initiative developed three guiding principles—Youth and Family Voice, Access and Equity, and Alignment and Coherence—alongside four shared priorities, during Phase 0 convenings. The report states these "informed the Roadmap to the Future team's work to develop the needs assessment" and will continue to guide the long-term strategic plan. Recommendations and strategic actions were filtered through these principles, and the shared priorities span quality childcare and TK–12, mental health and wellness, welcoming beacons of community life, and thriving in young adulthood. (Roadmap to the Future for Yolo County Children et al. (2024))

Claims¶

  • The Yolo County needs assessment identifies eight countywide needs for children, youth, and families, led by childcare, mental health access, and transportation [+W]
  • Yolo County children and youth need expanded learning, engagement, and leadership development opportunities, including integration of a youth development framework into all youth-serving organizations [+W]
  • Community engagement for the needs assessment reached nearly 630 community members who provided over 1,400 points of collected data [+W]
