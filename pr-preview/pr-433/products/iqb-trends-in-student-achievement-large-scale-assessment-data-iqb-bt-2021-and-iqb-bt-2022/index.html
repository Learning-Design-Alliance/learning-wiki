IQB Trends in Student Achievement large-scale assessment data (IQB-BT 2021 and IQB-BT 2022)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

German large-scale educational assessment data produced by the Institute for Educational Quality Improvement (IQB), containing achievement scores and contextual questionnaires for representative student cohorts before and during the COVID-19 pandemic.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • In Grade 9, negative achievement trends emerged for non-SEN and SEN-L-R students in all domains, and SEN-L-R trends were always significantly more pronounced than for non-SEN peers [+W]
  • Grade 4 cohorts affected by COVID-19 restrictions showed significant negative trends in verbal achievement for non-SEN and SEN-L-R students in all three domains, and for SEN-L-S students only in orthography [+W]
  • During the pandemic, Grade 4 students with SEN-L experienced less favorable home learning environments and lower parental support than non-SEN students, while Grade 9 group differences were largely non-significant [+W]
  • In Grade 9, fewer problems during distance learning were associated with better verbal achievement, summer school attendance was associated with lower reading and listening achievement, and learning-condition relations did not differ between student groups [+W]
