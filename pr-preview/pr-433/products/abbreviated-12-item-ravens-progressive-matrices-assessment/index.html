Abbreviated 12-item Raven's Progressive Matrices assessment¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A shortened computerized Raven's Progressive Matrices assessment developed by Mattingly Jameson K MD et al. to estimate nonverbal reasoning and explore prediction of degraded speech-recognition outcomes in adults who use or are considering cochlear implants.

Components¶

  • Abbreviated 12-item Raven's Progressive Matrices assessment for clinical prediction of sentence recognition: A shortened computerized version of the Raven's Progressive Matrices consisting of the 12 test items that significantly differentiated low- versus high-performing CI users on PRESTO sentence recognition. The article states this "abridged version of the RPM, consisting of only twelve test items instead of the standard 48 items, should be able to be completed in only three to four minutes and may have clinical utility in predicting post-operative performance." It was validated by correlations with sentence recognition in both CI users and normal-hearing controls listening to noise-vocoded speech. (Mattingly Jameson K MD et al. (2018))

Claims¶

  • An abbreviated 12-item RPM subset predicts sentence recognition in CI users with comparable validity to the full 48-item test [+M]
  • The 12-item RPM subset similarly predicts degraded sentence recognition in normal-hearing controls, replicating the CI finding [+M]
