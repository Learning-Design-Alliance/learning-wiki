Bright Beginnings¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 6 claims (1 for, 1 mixed, 4 against) · 8 studies (6 causal, 2 quant-synthesis), q3–q4 · 6 of 8 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Bright Beginnings is a child-centered, literacy-focused preschool curriculum developed for early childhood education, comprising nine thematic units with classroom and home activities and parent involvement.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • The Bright Beginnings preschool curriculum shows no discernible effects on preschool children's school readiness across oral language, print knowledge, phonological processing, and math domains [-W]
  • Bright Beginnings shows no discernible effects on preschool math (domain average effect size 0.07, not statistically significant) [-W]
  • Bright Beginnings shows no discernible effects on preschool oral language (domain average effect size 0.15, not statistically significant) [-W]
  • Bright Beginnings shows no discernible effects on preschool phonological processing (effect size -0.08, not statistically significant) [-W]
  • Bright Beginnings shows a substantively important positive effect on the TERA-3 print knowledge measure (effect size 0.32) but no discernible effect on the print knowledge domain overall [~W]
  • Parent Involvement Improves Early Reading [+M]
