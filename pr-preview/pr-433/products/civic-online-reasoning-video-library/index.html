Civic Online Reasoning Video Library¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A library of videos developed by the Civic Online Reasoning project to teach students and educators how to evaluate online information and data sources.

Components¶

  • Civic Online Reasoning video library supports teaching accurate online data evaluation: The resource list includes the Civic Online Reasoning (COR) Video Library, described as "Library of videos that explain the importance of finding accurate data and strategies for analyzing online data sources." The deck marks it as appropriate for students in grades 7-12 or teachers of any grade, supporting the data-evaluation side of data analysis instruction. (Computational Thinking Boosters: Data et al. (2020))
