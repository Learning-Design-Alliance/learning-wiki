The Pressey 'Cross-out' brief group intelligence scale for grades 4-8¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A four-test group intelligence assessment developed by Pressey for measuring general ability in grades 4–8 and preliminarily identifying pupils for further individual study.

Components¶

  • Design brief group tests for convenience, brevity, and teacher-interpretable scores: The article argues that group intelligence tests for schools should be built around "convenience, brevity, cost and ready interpretation of results in terms of school practice". The Cross-out scale realizes this: one 9" by 12" sheet, twenty-five minutes of testing, scoring at "90 an hour" by student help or one minute per blank by a teacher, and cheap blanks, so a survey is possible "at a minimum of expense and labor". (Pressey (1920))

Claims¶

  • School surveys with the scale found clear-cut group differences between sections, schools, and institutional children [+W]
  • The scale differentiates pupil material between schools in neighborhoods of different character [+W]
  • Partial and multiple correlation show the last two tests contribute most and the tests are fairly well selected [+W]
