Teaching Monster Challenge benchmark¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A benchmark released by Lin et al. containing persona-conditioned instructional-video generation items, evaluation rubrics, scoring traces, and human judgments for assessing end-to-end systems.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Systems adapt to the specified learner persona in ways human raters can perceive, scoring well above chance in persona identification [+M]
  • The LLM-judge separates weak submissions but its ranking of the strongest systems has almost no agreement with crowd ranking (Spearman ρ = −0.17) [+M]
