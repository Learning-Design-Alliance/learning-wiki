Description¶

I3Learn is a WordPress-based web learning platform developed for independent, inclusive ion-bonding chemistry learning by 9th- and 10th-grade students.

Components¶

  • I3Learn web-based learning platform for inclusive ion-bonding chemistry: I3Learn is a WordPress-based web learning platform on ion bonding for 9th and 10th-grade inclusive chemistry teaching in Lower Saxony, Germany, designed for 180 minutes of remote, independent learning. Its development was guided by the UDL framework: learners can choose between texts and videos, work on tasks with or without help, and use self-assessments (star ratings), plus a customizable interface, read-aloud function, glossary, simple language, and device-independent access. (Marvin Roski et al. (2024))
