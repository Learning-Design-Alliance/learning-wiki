Extended labelled dataset of 345 OER sentences with ethnic-bias annotations from 193 students¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A dataset released by Josmario Albuquerque and colleagues containing 345 contextualized OER sentences annotated by 193 higher-education students for perceived ethnic bias, with contextual metadata, aggregate labels, bias scores, and reproducibility code.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Linguistic and psycholinguistic features such as verb ratio and aggressiveness are positively correlated with perceived ethnic bias [+W]
  • The naive Bayes model achieved the highest precision (0.75) for ethnic bias detection on the test set [+W]
  • Social sciences content is positively correlated with perceived ethnic bias in text-based learning materials [+W]
  • A stacking ensemble of LG, SVM, NB, KNN and XGB achieved the best fine-tuning F1-score (0.88), outperforming individual models such as SVM (0.86) [+W]
  • SVM and random forest classifiers showed consistent performance in classifying ethnic bias, with F1-scores of 0.71 and 0.70 on the test set [+W]
