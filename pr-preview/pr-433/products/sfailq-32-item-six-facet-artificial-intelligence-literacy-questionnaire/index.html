SFAILQ: 32-item Six-Facet Artificial Intelligence Literacy Questionnaire¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A 32-item self-report assessment developed by Liu Qingqi et al. to measure adolescents' and adults' AI literacy across affective experiences, usage skills, cognitive evaluation, ethical norms, responsible use, and self-development.

Components¶

  • SFAILQ: 32-item Six-Facet Artificial Intelligence Literacy Questionnaire: The SFAILQ is a 32-item self-report questionnaire assessing six AI literacy dimensions: affective experiences (5 items), usage skills (5 items), cognitive evaluation (6 items), ethical norms (6 items), responsible use (4 items), and self-development (6 items). It was developed with 2443 Chinese participants aged 12 to 60 using a random split-half design: item reduction on Sample 1 (N = 1217) and reliability/validity testing on Sample 2 (N = 1226). The article reports it "demonstrated high internal consistency, construct validity, convergent validity, discriminant validity, and criterion-related validity." (Liu Qingqi et al. (2026))

Claims¶

  • SFAILQ shows high reliability, convergent validity with digital literacy (r = 0.49–0.68), and discriminant validity (HTMT below 0.80) [+W]
  • All SFAILQ dimensions correlate positively with academic self-efficacy (usage skills strongest) and academic engagement (responsible use strongest) [+W]
  • SFAILQ shows scalar measurement invariance across adolescents, young adults, and middle-aged adults [+W]
  • Negative affective experience items were dropped due to floor effects and poor discrimination [+W]
