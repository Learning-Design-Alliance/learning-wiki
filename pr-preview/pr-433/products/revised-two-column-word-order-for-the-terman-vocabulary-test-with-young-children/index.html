Revised two-column word order for the Terman vocabulary test with young children¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A revised, mimeograph-ready ordering of Terman vocabulary-test words intended to improve difficulty balance and accuracy when assessing the vocabulary of kindergarten and Grade I children.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • The Terman (Stanford Revision) vocabulary test words show an order of difficulty for kindergarten and Grade I children that is noticeably different from the order indicated on the test blank. [+M]
