TARGET-tool¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The TARGET-tool is an online teacher dashboard and mobile student-scan tool developed by Weeldenburg et al. to support secondary-school PE teachers in assessing and improving the motivational learning climate.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Perceived usability of the TARGET-tool improved from mid-term (SUS 57.3) to the end of the experimental phase (SUS 67.0), a medium effect that was not statistically significant [+M]
  • Teachers rated the TARGET-tool's core features as easy to learn and efficient, but found scheduling student scans complex and the abundance of motivating strategies overwhelming [+M]
