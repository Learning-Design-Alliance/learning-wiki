Go-Lab ecosystem¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Go-Lab ecosystem, including the Graasp authoring and implementation platform and Golabz repository, is a technology environment developed and maintained to help teachers create and use inquiry-based STEM learning spaces.

Components¶

  • Graasp ILS authoring platform and the Go-Lab ecosystem for inquiry-based learning: Go-Lab is a technological ecosystem supporting inquiry-based learning in STEM built around Inquiry Learning Spaces (ILSs): "pedagogically structured learning environments that can contain online labs, apps, and resources." It comprises Golabz, a repository of apps, labs and ready-to-use ILSs, and Graasp, "a platform for ILS authoring and implementation, as well as for community gathering," plus a help desk and face-to-face training events. From 2009 through December 2019 Graasp reached 37,380 registered teachers and over 100,000 students from 152 countries, with an overall ILS adoption rate of 5.76%. (María Jesús Rodríguez-Triana et al. (2020))
  • Go-Lab ecosystem for teacher-designed inquiry learning spaces: The Go-Lab ecosystem is a two-platform architecture supporting teacher design of inquiry-based science learning. The sharing and support platform (Golabz) presents digital resources including over 600 online laboratories, while the authoring and learning platform (Graasp) enables co-creation and implementation of Inquiry Learning Spaces. The ecosystem was developed following "participatory design, respon-sive design, and privacy–by–design methodologies" and is fully GDPR compliant. It aggregates labs from multiple third-party repositories under one umbrella, offering a one-stop portal for STEM inquiry learning resources. (de Jong et al. (2021))

Claims¶

  • Re-used Inquiry Learning Spaces were about four times more likely to be implemented than those created from scratch, and scaffolding increased the implementation rate [+W]
  • The higher the maturation level of an Inquiry Learning Space (create, share, co-create, publish), the higher its probability of being implemented in a classroom [+W]
