i-Read¶

Description¶

i-Read is an online reading environment developed by Omheni et al. in which learners upload, annotate, archive, and share texts while the system analyzes annotation traces for reader profiling.

Components¶

  • i-Read online reading environment with personality profiling from annotations: i-Read is an online reading environment where learners upload reading materials, annotate them, and share annotated documents. Its architecture includes a user annotation interface, an annotation analyzer module, a profile constructor module, and three databases with two servers. The analyzer computes six annotation features (total, average, graphical, textual, reference, and compounding annotation counts), and the profile constructor applies multivariate linear regression to estimate Big Five trait scores. The system "offers, besides the traditional annotation functionalities of creation, archiving and sharing, the functionality of readers' personality modeling based on their digital annotation traces." (Omheni et al. (2017))
  • IRead interactive textbook reading tool: IRead is an interactive tool for textbook reading that "uses LLMs with visualization and interaction techniques, to enhance students' reading and learning experiences." It comprises an enhanced textbook reader with highlighting, note-taking, minimap, and search; a hierarchical concept tree visualization with information cards generated by an LLM and refined through human oversight; and Question Wiz, an AI-driven question bot presenting interaction-specific questions. A fuzzy concept mapping technique using sentence-transformer embeddings and cosine similarity links concepts to text terms exceeding a predefined similarity threshold. Its content is produced by a one-time textbook data processing workflow per imported textbook. (Xiaoyu Zhang et al. (2025))

Claims¶

  • Digital annotation traces can predict conscientiousness and neuroticism scores with reasonable accuracy [+W]
  • In a between-subject user study, IRead outperformed the baseline PDF reader system in 8 out of 14 measured dimensions, with 5 reaching statistical significance [+M]
  • The IRead question generation model, fine-tuned on in-text OpenStax questions, matched reference questions in human ratings and outperformed zero-shot GPT-4 on automatic metrics [+M]
