Description¶

A process developed and applied by the Corporation for National and Community Service to help funders and grantees evaluate, select, and scale evidence-based interventions.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Foundations can serve more people by identifying and supporting effective interventions that are ready to be scaled [+W]
  • The SPREE process can help build or strengthen a culture of measurement, learning and evaluation even where it is initially absent [+W]
