Louisiana middle-school communications industrial arts curriculum guide (draft)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A Louisiana state curriculum guide for grades 6–9 that organizes communications-focused industrial arts instruction in drafting, electricity-electronics, graphic arts, and photography around performance objectives, activities, and resources.

Components¶

  • Louisiana middle-school communications industrial arts curriculum guide (drafting, electricity-electronics, graphic arts, photography): A tentative state curriculum guide for grades 6-9 implementing career education through industrial arts. Per the abstract, "the course is designed to help youth understand the basic concepts of the communications career cluster; including, drafting, electricity, graphic arts, and photography." It is organized as topic outlines pairing performance objectives with suggested activities and resources, each resource coded to a bibliography following each major section. (Communications: Industrial Arts Curriculum Guide (1974))
