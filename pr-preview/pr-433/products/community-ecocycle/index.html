Community Ecocycle¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A reflection and action framework published by Equal Measure and Mathematica to help philanthropic funders consider developmental phases and co-design investment approaches with communities pursuing place-based systems change.

Components¶

  • The Community Ecocycle tool for funder reflection and action in place-based systems change: The Community Ecocycle is a tool published by Equal Measure and Mathematica intended to help funders who invest in place-based systems change efforts. The article states it is "intended to help funders who have elected to invest in place-based systems change efforts in two ways": considering developmental phases and co-designing investment approaches with communities. It serves as a reflection and action aid for philanthropic funders working with communities. (Meg Long et al. (2021))
