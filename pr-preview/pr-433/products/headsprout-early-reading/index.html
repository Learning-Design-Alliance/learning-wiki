Headsprout Early Reading¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Headsprout Early Reading is an Internet-based supplemental early-literacy curriculum developed by Headsprout, using adaptive animated lessons, stories, flashcards, and readers to teach foundational reading skills.

Claims¶

  • The evidence base for Headsprout Early Reading is limited to one small RCT, supporting only a potentially positive rating [-W]
  • Headsprout Early Reading has a statistically significant positive effect on the oral language of at-risk preschool children [+W]
  • Headsprout Early Reading has a statistically significant positive effect on the print knowledge of at-risk preschool children [+W]
