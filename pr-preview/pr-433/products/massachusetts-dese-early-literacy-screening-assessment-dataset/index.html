Massachusetts DESE early literacy screening assessment dataset¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A multi-year dataset of K–3 early literacy screener results collected and maintained by the Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education from participating grantee schools and districts.

Components¶

  • Massachusetts DESE literacy screening assessment data collection from grantee schools and districts: Beginning in the 2020/21 school year, the Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education began collecting literacy screening assessment data from schools and districts participating in certain state grants, drawing on a list of state-approved commercial screener products. The analysis in this brief uses 'More than 4,000 students from the 2021/22 school year, including 28 districts and 63 schools,' with scores from DIBELS 8th Edition, FastBridge aReading, i-Ready Diagnostic, Lexia Rapid, MAP Growth, mClass, and Star Reading. The stated goal is analyzing performance patterns to inform improvement efforts and target resources. (Lemke et al. (2023))

Claims¶

  • Early screening benchmark status predicts later performance: most beginning-of-year at-risk students stay at risk, and meeting benchmarks by end of grade 1 is associated with much higher grade 3 MCAS odds [+W]
  • Early literacy screening assessments provide multiple benchmarks or performance levels defined in different ways, with no common definition of 'at risk' [+M]
