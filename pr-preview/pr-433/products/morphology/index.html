Description¶

IMSE's Morphology+ is a structured literacy curriculum and educator-training program developed by the Institute for Multisensory Education for grades 3–5 morphology, vocabulary, and comprehension instruction.

Components¶

  • IMSE's Morphology+ structured literacy professional development and curriculum program for grades 3-5: IMSE's Morphology+ combines 30 hours of educator training with 148 sequenced lessons providing "explicit instruction in prefixes, suffixes, Greek and Latin roots, vocabulary development, and comprehension." It is designed for grades 3-5, builds on foundational phonics instruction, and supports students in analyzing complex academic vocabulary through systematic word study and cumulative review. It can be implemented by general education, special education, and intervention teachers. (IMSE's Morphology+ (2025))

Claims¶

  • Third-grade students whose teachers implemented IMSE's Morphology+ scored significantly higher on NWEA MAP Growth Reading than comparison students (effect size +0.05) [+W]
