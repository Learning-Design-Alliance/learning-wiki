PISA 2015 German reading constructed-response dataset for fairness analysis¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A research dataset assembled by Nico Andersen et al. from 2015 German PISA reading-assessment responses for studying demographic disparities in automated versus human scoring of constructed responses.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Systematic linguistic differences exist between demographic groups in PISA text responses, including response length, lexical diversity, and out-of-vocabulary tokens [+W]
  • The most accurate scorer showed no gender disparity but a small significant language-background disparity favoring foreign-language speakers [+W]
  • Lower-performing groups gain relative advantage because the scorer detects incorrect responses better for them, producing more false positives [+W]
  • SVM with RoBERTa sentence embeddings was the most accurate automatic scoring method (κ = .82) among the four tested combinations [+W]
  • Methods with higher scoring accuracy showed lower demographic disparity across the tested model combinations [+W]
  • Demographic disparities in automatic scoring are concentrated at the item level, tied to class distributions and item difficulty [+W]
