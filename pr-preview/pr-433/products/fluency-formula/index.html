Fluency Formula™¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (1 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A Scholastic supplemental curriculum for grades 1–6 that provides brief daily lessons and materials designed to develop word-recognition automaticity, decoding accuracy, and oral reading expressiveness.

Components¶

  • Fluency Formula™ supplemental reading fluency curriculum for grades 1–6: Fluency Formula™ is a supplemental curriculum "designed to promote reading fluency for first- through sixth-grade students," emphasizing "automatic recognition of words, decoding accuracy, and oral expressiveness." It delivers a daily 10- to 15-minute classroom lesson using workbooks, read-aloud anthologies, library books, fluency activity cards, and audio CDs, and includes a Fluency Formula™ Assessment System with one-minute grade-level passages and a timer. Distributed by Scholastic; a grade-level kit costs $625. (Fluency Formula™ WWC Intervention Report (2007))

Claims¶

  • The evidence base for Fluency Formula™ consists of a single small randomized controlled trial, with no studies addressing alphabetics or general reading achievement [~W]
  • Fluency effects of Fluency Formula™ differ by initial ability: positive for low-ability students (effect size 0.66) and negative for high-ability students (effect size –0.21), both non-significant [~W]
  • Fluency Formula™ shows a negative but statistically non-significant effect on second-grade reading comprehension (effect size –0.28, improvement index –11) [-W]
  • Fluency Formula™ shows a positive but statistically non-significant effect on second-grade oral reading fluency (effect size 0.26, improvement index +10) [+W]
