Open audit dataset of 1,800 LLM history-tutor responses across five personas¶

Description¶

Alexis Popovici et al.'s open corpus of 1,800 API-generated history-tutor responses across four LLMs, five student personas, and three prompt types for researchers and auditors evaluating educational AI bias.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Safety-aligned LLM tutors refuse educational requests asymmetrically, with low-resource students facing the highest refusal rates (76.7% vs 62.2%) [+W]
  • LLaMA produces a fivefold higher victimization-to-politics vocabulary ratio for the Roma persona (≈0.15) than for elite peers (≈0.03) [+W]
  • Deepseek's justification scores vary by student socio-economic profile, with Top Tier students averaging 9.60 versus 6.90 for Low Tier students [+W]
  • LLaMA shows a persona-conditioned vocabulary collapse for the Roma minority persona, with TTR falling 0.018 points below its own cross-persona mean [+W]
  • LLM history tutors reduce marginalized students' access to contested geopolitical framing, with the coup theory appearing in 2.6% of Low Tier responses versus 7.9% for the baseline [+W]
  • The two personas produced qualitatively different questions on the same essay, indicating the persona constraint mechanism alters the model's analytical focus [+W]
