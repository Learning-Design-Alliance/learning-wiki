Kutti AI¶

Description¶

Kutti AI is a React Native/Expo learning-companion application developed by Kadharmoideen Fadurudeen that delivers curriculum instruction, spoken questions, speech-based responses, difficulty detection, and feedback through audio, with offline-capable and multilingual support.

Claims¶

  • Speech detection showed preliminary success but remained challenging in noisy settings, particularly for speaker differentiation, children's voices, and diverse dialects [~W]
  • Pre-deep-learning speech recognition was markedly less accurate on non-native speech, undermining automated oral feedback for learners, as the article reports citing prior studies [~W]
