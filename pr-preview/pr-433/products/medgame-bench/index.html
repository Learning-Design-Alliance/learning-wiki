MedGame Bench¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A 5,000-case medical narrative-generation and story-direction benchmark created by Qian Wu et al. from the PMC-Patients Dataset for evaluating medical AI systems.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Task-specific fine-tuning substantially improves open-source LLMs on MedGame Bench and narrows the gap with commercial models [+M]
  • Without fine-tuning, commercial frontier models outperform open-source LLMs on MedGame Bench structural validity and content metrics [+M]
  • Related-case overlap between MedGame Bench train and test splits shows no significant performance difference [~M]
