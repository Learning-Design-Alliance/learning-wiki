Description¶

The EDIT is a measurement tool developed for the Office of Planning, Research, and Evaluation by Mathematica Policy Research and partners to examine early childhood teachers' ongoing assessment, individualization, and instructional decision-making practices.

Components¶

  • EDIT measure for examining teachers' use of ongoing child assessment to individualize instruction: The EDIT is a measurement tool developed for OPRE by Mathematica Policy Research and its partners. It is "designed to help researchers understand how teachers conduct ongoing assessments for individualization and use those assessments to guide instruction." It targets early childhood teachers' assessment and instructional decision-making practices. (Monahan et al. (2015))
