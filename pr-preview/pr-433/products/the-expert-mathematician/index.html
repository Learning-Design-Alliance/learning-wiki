The Expert Mathematician¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A three-year middle-school mathematics curriculum developed by the program's creators that combines Logo programming software with consumable print materials across general mathematics, pre-algebra, and algebra I.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • The Expert Mathematician shows a positive but not statistically significant effect on middle school math achievement (effect size 0.35, improvement index +14 percentile points) [+W]
  • The WWC rates The Expert Mathematician as having potentially positive effects on middle school mathematics achievement [+W]
