MetaTutor¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 7 claims (4 for, 1 mixed, 2 against) · 2 studies (2 associational), q2–q3 · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

Description¶

MetaTutor is a hypermedia-based intelligent tutoring system developed by educational technology researchers that uses pedagogical agents and self-regulated-learning tools to support learners studying scientific content.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • More fixations on the learning content after an add-subgoal prompt are associated with higher learning gains, suggesting complying with that prompt may not be effective [~W]
  • Compliance with MetaTutor's summarize, stay-on-subgoal, move-to-next-subgoal, and open-diagram prompts shows no significant effect on learning gains [-M]
  • No behavioral measure of compliance with MetaTutor's stay-on-page and move-to-next-page prompts relates to learning gains [-W]
  • Higher compliance with MetaTutor's review-notes prompt is associated with larger proportional learning gains [+M]
  • Higher compliance with MetaTutor's revise-summary prompt is associated with larger proportional learning gains [+M]
  • Higher compliance with MetaTutor's suggest-subgoal prompt is associated with larger proportional learning gains [+M]
  • Differential sequence mining shows significant differences in frequencies of SRL process sequences by prior knowledge group while controlling for condition [+M]
