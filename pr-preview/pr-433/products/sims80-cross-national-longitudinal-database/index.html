SIMS80 cross-national longitudinal database¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A cross-national longitudinal mathematics achievement and self-concept database from the IEA's Second International Mathematics Study, covering 13-year-old students, schools, and classroom and student background variables in the USA, Ontario, and New Zealand.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Positive class-level peer spillover effects on mathematics achievement fail to replicate once measurement error is controlled with doubly latent models [+M]
  • The Big-Fish-Little-Pond effect on mathematics self-concept is negative and statistically significant in all three countries under a fully specified doubly latent model [+M]
