Description¶

DLM Early Childhood Express Math software, developed by Douglas H. Clements and Julie Sarama and distributed by McGraw-Hill School Education as part of the DLM Express Math Resource Package, provides individualized early-childhood activities in numerical, geometric, and spatial mathematics.
