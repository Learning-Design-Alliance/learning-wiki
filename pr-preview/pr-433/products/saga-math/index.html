Saga Math¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 5 claims (2 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Saga Math is an in-school, high-dosage mathematics tutoring programme for secondary students, offered in small-group traditional and technology-enhanced models and implemented by Saga Education.

Components¶

  • Saga Math high-dosage tutoring program (traditional and technology-enhanced models): Saga Math is a supplemental in-school math tutoring program for 9th and 10th graders offered in a traditional model at 1:2 ("research-proven"), 1:3, or 1:4 tutor-to-student ratios, and a technology-enhanced model at up to 1:8 in which half the students work with the tutor while the other half use "a computer-assisted learning platform called ALEKS", switching every other session. Tutoring is embedded in the school schedule at least three times weekly for 90 minutes total, aligned to Common Core standards, with tutorial instruction informed by assessments, observations, and progress-monitoring data. (Saga Math (Evidence Summary) (2020))

Claims¶

  • Saga blended tutoring improved student math grades across districts and tutoring models [+W]
  • Saga blended tutoring produced large positive impacts on algebra scores in one district and geometry scores in another, but no effects on two other standardized tests [~W]
  • Implementation analysis identified student engagement as a challenge for technology-driven tutoring programs [~W]
  • Saga tutoring impacts were larger in schools with fewer staffing challenges and in tutoring groups of two students or fewer [~W]
  • Saga tutoring impacts were larger for students with lower prior math achievement and for Black students [+W]
