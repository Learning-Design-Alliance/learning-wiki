Genetics Cognitive Tutor three-factor cross dataset with transfer, PFL and problem-solving post-tests¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A dataset of Carnegie Mellon undergraduates’ interactions with the Genetics Cognitive Tutor three-factor cross module and subsequent problem-solving, transfer, and preparation-for-future-learning assessments, released for training and validating shallowness detectors.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • K* detectors identify no-PFL-shallow learners with cross-validated A′ of 0.790 and kappa 0.55 [+W]
  • K* detectors identify no-transfer-shallow learners at student-level cross-validated A′ of 0.766–0.791 [+W]
