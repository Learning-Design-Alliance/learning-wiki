Milpitas Early Learning Transitions Model¶

Description¶

A Milpitas Unified School District programme funded by the Heising-Simons Foundation to strengthen supports for preschool children and families preparing for elementary school.

Components¶

  • Milpitas Early Learning Transitions Model (ELTM): The ELTM is a district program funded by the Heising-Simons Foundation in 2012–2013 to "enhance supports for children and families as they prepare for elementary school." It has many components, one key feature being integration of a computerized adaptive assessment into preschool teachers' practice. Mathematica Policy Research conducted a formative evaluation of it in the district's two preschool child development centers. (Emily Moiduddin et al. (2014))

Claims¶
