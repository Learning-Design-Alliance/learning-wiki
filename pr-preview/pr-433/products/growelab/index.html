GroweLab¶

Description¶

GroweLab is engage2learn's all-in-one coaching and talent-development platform for facilitating and documenting educator coaching and tracking professional-growth data and outcomes.

Components¶

  • GroweLab all-in-one coaching and talent development platform: GroweLab is engage2learn's technology platform for delivering and documenting educator coaching. The summary describes it as "an all-in-one coaching and talent development platform" that gives leaders "actionable insights into effective initiatives in driving long-term change and improvements". Coaching was facilitated through this platform, which documented all interactions and tracked attendance, evidence of learning, and adult learning/growth data, integrating educator growth data with student and staff outcomes. (engage2learn (e2L): Evidence Rating (2022))

Claims¶

  • Students of e2L-coached teachers outperformed students in comparison schools on end-of-year STAAR math in a propensity-score-matched study [+W]
  • Students of engage2learn teachers scored significantly higher on NWEA MAP than students of non-e2L teachers in the same schools and in comparison schools [+W]
