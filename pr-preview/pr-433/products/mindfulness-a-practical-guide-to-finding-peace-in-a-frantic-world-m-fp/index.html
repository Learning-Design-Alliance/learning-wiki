Mindfulness: A Practical Guide to Finding Peace in a Frantic World (M-FP)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

An introductory, skills-based mindfulness curriculum developed from mindfulness-based cognitive therapy that sequences four themes across eight sessions and includes daily home practice for general adult learners.

Components¶

  • M-FP mindfulness curriculum sequencing four understandings-and-skills themes across eight sessions with daily home practice: The Mindfulness: A Practical Guide to Finding Peace in a Frantic World (M-FP) curriculum is an introductory, skills-based training built on mindfulness-based cognitive therapy core elements and designed for improving well-being in the general population. It develops four sequenced themes: recognizing autopilot and bringing mindfulness to everyday present-moment experience; recognizing thoughts, emotions, sensations, and impulses while stabilizing attention; recognizing unhelpful patterns of thinking, feeling, and acting; and learning skills for keeping balanced and responding skillfully when difficulties arise. "The curriculum has eight sessions that work sequentially through these four main themes," with home practices including a daily 20-minute mindfulness practice. (Montero-Marin et al (2021))

Claims¶

  • Self-taught mindfulness training may be a useful option for teachers who cannot access or commit to an instructor-led program, provided they engage with mindfulness practice [+W]
  • Mindfulness practice frequency had indirect effects on teacher outcomes through mindfulness gains in both the self-taught and instructor-led arms [+W]
  • The instructor-led mindfulness arm, compared with the self-taught arm, indirectly improved teacher outcomes by enhancing mindfulness and self-compassion as mediating factors [+W]
