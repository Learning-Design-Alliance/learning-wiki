ELEVATE (Efficient LLM Education with Virtual Avatar Teaching Engine)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A three-stratum framework developed by Lorenzo Stacchio et al. for building locally deployed GenAI avatar-tutor systems with student interaction, local GenAI execution, and teacher governance layers.

Components¶

  • ELEVATE: a three-stratum framework for locally deployed GenAI avatar tutors: ELEVATE (Efficient LLM Education with Virtual Avatar Teaching Engine) is a framework for developing GenAI-driven avatar tutors governed by institutional epistemic infrastructures. It integrates "LLM-driven dialogue with embodied 3D avatars for multimodal interaction" and adopts a local-first execution model. The framework "formalizes a three-stratum design that separates (i) a student-facing virtual avatar interaction layer, (ii) a local GenAI execution and multimodal synthesis core, and (iii) a teacher-facing governance layer", forming a closed loop in which teacher-defined constraints govern engine behavior and the avatar embodies responses. (Lorenzo Stacchio et al. (2026))
