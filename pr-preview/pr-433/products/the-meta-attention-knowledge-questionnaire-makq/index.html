The Meta-Attention Knowledge Questionnaire (MAKQ)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A self-report and open-ended questionnaire developed by Mulvihill et al. to measure early adolescents' self-perceived attentional control and declarative attention-regulation strategy knowledge.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • The MAKQ dimensions show acceptable internal consistency across both studies (alphas 0.77-0.93) [+W]
  • MAKQ dimensions correlate moderately with each other, with strategy knowledge only weakly related to the self-perception scales [+W]
  • The MAKQ self-knowledge items show a stable two-factor structure: meta-attention control (15 items) and meta-attention monitoring (5 items) [+W]
  • MAKQ strategy knowledge, but not the self-perception scales, converges with behavioural inhibitory control on the Go-No-Go task [+W]
  • MAKQ dimensions show convergent validity with parent-rated attention and executive functioning (moderate-to-strong negative correlations) [+W]
  • Cluster analysis across both studies identifies three meta-attention knowledge profiles, including an overconfident group with a self-knowledge/strategy mismatch [+W]
