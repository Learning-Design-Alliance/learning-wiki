Virtual professional learning community (vPLC)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The vPLC is a scalable professional-learning programme run by the ASSISTments initiative to help teachers, especially in rural and underresourced settings, implement ASSISTments effectively.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • The vPLC-augmented ASSISTments treatment sample comprised 59 teachers and 2,855 students in grades 6-8 from 36 public schools across 24 U.S. states, half in rural areas [+W]
  • vPLC-augmented ASSISTments did not significantly improve overall middle school math achievement relative to a virtual comparison group [-W]
  • Low-performing students (below the 50th pretest percentile) showed significant improvement with vPLC-augmented ASSISTments [+W]
