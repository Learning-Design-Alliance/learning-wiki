Components¶

  • Real-world middle school family engagement strategies with accessibility, resources, and outcome monitoring: The playbook catalogs recurring strategy structures used in middle schools: orientation sessions (How to Junior High 101), porch visits and community center meetups, positive postcards, High-Five Fridays phone campaigns, Family Learning Workshops (Parent University), Weekly Learning Connection updates, FACE committees, community partnerships with organizations like United Way, district strategic-plan integration, and leader book studies. Each is described through strategy, implementation, accessibility, resources, and outcomes components. (Wallace et al. (2024))
