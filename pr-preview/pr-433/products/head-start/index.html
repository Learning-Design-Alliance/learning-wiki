Head Start¶

Description¶

Head Start is a federally funded U.S. programme providing education, health, nutrition, family-engagement, and related services to promote school readiness for children from birth to age five, administered through local grantees.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • The evidence base for Head Start's school-readiness effects is small: only one of 40 eligible studies met WWC group design standards [~W]
  • Head Start shows no discernible effects on mathematics achievement for 4-year-old children [-M]
  • Head Start shows no discernible effects on social-emotional development for 3- and 4-year-old children [-M]
  • Head Start has potentially positive effects on general reading achievement for 3- and 4-year-old children, based on parent-rated emergent literacy [+M]
  • Supplemental analyses show statistically significant positive Head Start effects on one social-emotional subscale and two mothers' race/ethnicity subgroup comparisons for the 3-year-old cohort [+M]
