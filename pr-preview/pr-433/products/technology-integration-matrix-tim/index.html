Technology Integration Matrix (TIM)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The Technology Integration Matrix is a framework developed by the Florida Center for Instructional Technology at the University of South Florida for describing learning-environment characteristics and levels of classroom technology integration.

Components¶

  • The Technology Integration Matrix (TIM) and the Rubric for Effective Teacher Technology Use as evaluation tools for technology coaching: The Technology Integration Matrix, from the Florida Center for Instructional Technology at the University of South Florida, "describes five characteristics of learning environments (active, collaborative, constructive, authentic, and goal-directed) that are associated with five levels of technology integration (entry, adoption, adaptation, infusion, and transformation)." The paper proposes combining the matrix with coach roles and goals to evaluate technology coaching, alongside the Rubric for Effective Teacher Technology Use, which describes three proficiency levels across four Danielson-framework domains. (Tina Ehsanipour et al. (2017))
