Level of Adoption (LoA) survey¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 design), q1–q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

An online collaborative self-report survey developed by Orr et al. to assess individuals' adoption of technologies across the eight Concerns-Based Adoption Model levels of use.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Self-reported average level of technology use rose across three administrations of the LoA survey, though not for every technology [+M]
  • Loa Revised Ten Level Scale From Respondent Feedback [+W]
  • The authors declined requests to name specific technologies or focus the standards on AI, citing rapid technological change [+W]
