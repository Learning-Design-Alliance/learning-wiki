Guide to Measuring Implementation: The 3Us¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A Mathematica practical guidance resource that helps implementing organizations decide when and how to measure implementation indicators and use the findings.

Components¶

  • Guide to Measuring Implementation: The 3Us — a practical measurement guidance resource: A published guidance resource from Mathematica (February 2023) that, in its own words, "provides guidance on when and how to measure these indicators, and what implementing organizations can do with the results." It addresses both the mechanics of measurement (when and how) and the use of findings, making it a practical tool rather than a research report. (Mikia Manley et al. (2023))
