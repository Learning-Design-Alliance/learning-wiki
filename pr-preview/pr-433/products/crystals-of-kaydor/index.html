Crystals of Kaydor¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Crystals of Kaydor is a story-based iPad game developed to help adolescents practise emotion recognition, emotion-intensity gauging, empathic responding, perspective taking, and helping behaviours.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Adolescents who engaged more with Crystals' empathy training mechanics showed greater increases in empathic accuracy-related RTPJ activation, controlling for total playtime [+M]
  • Crystals gameplay increased DMPFC–posterior cingulate resting-state connectivity relative to Bastion, specific to the empathic accuracy-related network [+M]
  • Improvements in empathic accuracy after Crystals gameplay were associated with strengthened amygdala–MPFC resting-state connectivity relative to Bastion [+M]
  • Playing the empathy training game Crystals of Kaydor produced no greater improvement in adolescents' empathic accuracy than playing the commercial control game Bastion [~M]
