My School, My Voice survey¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

CCSR's My School, My Voice product combines teacher and student school-climate survey responses into 31 school-level measures with comparative trend reports and item-level details.

Components¶

  • My School, My Voice surveys and the 31-measure individual school survey report: In spring 2007 CCSR surveyed all Chicago Public School teachers, principals, and students in grades six through twelve, "receiving almost 158,000 responses". The individualized report combines a 312-item teacher survey and a 172-item student survey into thirty-one measures (e.g., Program Coherence, Academic Engagement, Teacher-Principal Trust), each displayed with trend lines from 1999-2007 for the school, demographically similar schools, and the system. A companion Details report breaks measures down to individual survey items. (Luppescu et al. (2007))
