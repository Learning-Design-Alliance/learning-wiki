Adult Promise initiative¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A Lumina Foundation multiyear state-grant program launched in 2017 to engage and support adults ages 25 to 64 in postsecondary education, with Mathematica serving as its learning and evaluation partner from 2017 to 2021.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • The brief reports that adult learners' priorities differ from traditional-age students', making appropriate messages critical to engaging them [+W]
  • The brief reports that adult learners often need high-touch support such as navigators through enrollment and financial aid [+W]
  • The brief reports that online tools can engage prospective adult learners and make enrollment convenient given work and family demands [+W]
