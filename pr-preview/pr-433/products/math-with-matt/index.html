Math with Matt¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Math with Matt is a CTAT-based intelligent tutoring system developed by Rief et al. that teaches linear-systems algebra through adaptive hints and feedback alongside an LLM-supported mindful pedagogical agent.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Using the ITS reduced executive state-math anxiety and improved algebra learning across conditions [+M]
  • Mindful-condition students completed fewer problem-solving steps and requested fewer hints per step than Cognitive-condition students [+M]
  • Mindful-condition students rated the pedagogical agent as more supportive and more concerned about their well-being on single items [+M]
