Calvani et al.'s three-dimensional digital competence framework¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A multidimensional framework for defining and assessing digital competence across technical, cognitive, and ethical dimensions, developed by Calvani and colleagues.

Components¶

  • Digital competence frameworks: DigComp 2.1's five dimensions and Calvani et al.'s three-dimensional framework: The article reviews two theoretical frameworks for digital competence. DigComp 2.1, as described via Carretero et al. (2017), "includes 21 sub-competencies structured to improve citizens' digital competence, consisting of five dimensions: information and data literacy, communication and collaboration, digital content creation, digital safety, and problem-solving", with eight proficiency levels. Calvani et al.'s (2012) framework "includes three technical, cognitive, and ethical dimensions". The study adopted Calvani's framework because it is multi-dimensional and interconnected with other basic competencies such as reading, problem-solving, and metacognitive skills. (Mehrvarz et al. (2021))
