Pathway to Academic Success Project¶

Description¶

The Pathway to Academic Success Project is a professional-development and coaching programme for grades 6–12 ELA teachers serving English learners, combining teacher training, coaching, and instructional materials.

Components¶

  • Pathway to Academic Success Project intervention (teacher professional development, coaching, and instructional materials): The Pathway to Academic Success Project is a professional development and coaching intervention that "trains teachers to improve the reading and writing abilities of ELs who have an intermediate level of English proficiency by incorporating cognitive strategies into reading and writing instruction." It targets grades 6-12 general education ELA teachers of English learners and has three key components: teacher professional development, coaching from experienced Pathway teachers, and instructional materials and supplies such as wall posters, class sets of readings, sentence starters, and model lessons based on culturally relevant texts. (What Works Clearinghouse et al. (2021))
