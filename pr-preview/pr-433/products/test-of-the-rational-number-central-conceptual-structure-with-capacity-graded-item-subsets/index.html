Test of the rational-number central conceptual structure with capacity-graded item subsets¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A study-developed assessment by Morra et al. that diagnoses sixth- and seventh-graders’ rational-number understanding using capacity-graded item subsets.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Rational-number CCS test performance is predicted by working memory capacity and, to a lesser extent, by intrusion errors in the listening span [+M]
  • Two subsets of rational-number CCS items demand working memory capacities of 4 or 5 units respectively [+M]
