I CAN Learn¶

Description¶

I CAN Learn® is a self-paced, mastery-based computer mathematics curriculum developed by JRL Enterprises, Inc., with interactive lessons, placement-based learning paths, practice, quizzes, and classroom tracking tools.

Components¶

  • I CAN Learn® self-paced, mastery-based computer math curriculum: I CAN Learn® is a full-curriculum mathematics software program developed by JRL Enterprises, Inc., delivered through "a series of computer-based interactive lessons that students work on individually at their own computers". Students typically begin with a placement test that customizes their lesson plan, and each lesson includes a warm-up, lesson presentation, guided practice, and a quiz to ensure mastery before progressing. Courses include Fundamentals of Math, Pre-Algebra, Algebra, and Geometry, containing 20 to 180 self-paced, mastery-based lessons with multimedia content, plus a Classroom Explorer system for tracking attendance, homework, grades, and individualized learning paths. (I CAN Learn® Primary Mathematics)

Claims¶

  • I CAN Learn® has no discernible effects on the mathematics test scores of eighth-grade students in primary mathematics courses [-S]
  • Supplemental analyses of I CAN Learn® in alternate second-year samples showed no effects that survived multiple-comparison adjustment [-M]
  • Only one of seven eligible studies of I CAN Learn® met WWC group design standards, and it met them only with reservations [~W]
