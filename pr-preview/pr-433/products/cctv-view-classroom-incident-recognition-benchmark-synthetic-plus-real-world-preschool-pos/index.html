CCTV-view classroom incident recognition benchmark (synthetic plus real-world preschool pose data)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A privacy-aware benchmark dataset from Paritosh Parmar et al. combining synthetic CCTV-style videos with anonymized real-world preschool pose data annotated for classroom incident-recognition research.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • A distilled multi-order kinematic model outperforms pose-based baselines on a synthetic classroom incident dataset while using one-tenth as many parameters as PoseC3D [+M]
  • Models trained on synthetic classroom incident data transfer zero-shot to real-world footage, with the proposed method best at 63.41% though a synthetic-to-real domain gap remains [+M]
  • Classroom incident recognition is substantially harder than established general action-recognition benchmarks, with PoseC3D dropping from over 94% accuracy to 70.54% [+W]
