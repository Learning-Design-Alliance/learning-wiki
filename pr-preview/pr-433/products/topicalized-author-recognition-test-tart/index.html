Topicalized Author Recognition Test (tART)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A reading-experience assessment developed by Song et al. that measures print exposure and topic- or genre-specific reading profiles through author recognition and book-reading responses.
