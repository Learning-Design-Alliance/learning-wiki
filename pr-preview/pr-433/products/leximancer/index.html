Leximancer¶

Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Leximancer is a text-analysis and concept-mapping software product developed by Leximancer Pty Ltd for extracting and visualizing concepts and relationships in document collections.

Components¶

  • Leximancer text-analysis software for systematic review coding: Leximancer is the qualitative data-analysis software the review used to extract meaning from the 99 retrieved articles. The article describes it as "a tool that adopts a quantitative approach, to conduct a qualitative analysis", using "Bayesian measurements that record the event of a word and associates it to the event of a progression of different words", coding fragments of content from one sentence to groups of sentences. It produced the concept map underlying the review's four-dimension framework. (Rodrigues et al. (2019))
