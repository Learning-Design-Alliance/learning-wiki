Modern Classroom Project¶

Description¶

The Modern Classroom Project is a teacher-created instructional model run by the Modern Classroom Project organization for self-paced, competency-based classroom instruction.

Components¶

  • The Modern Classroom Project's instructional-video model for self-paced, competency-based classrooms: The Modern Classroom Project uses simple video technology to transform teaching and learning. Teachers create short videos averaging six minutes in length that replace in-person lectures and direct instruction, freeing teachers to work one-on-one or with groups, and enabling students who missed instruction due to chronic absenteeism to catch up online. Students progress to advanced material only when ready, assessed on competency rather than seat time, and modules include must-do, should-do, and aspire-to activities to account for learner variability. Teachers house videos and materials in district-provided LMSs. (Brizard (2023))
