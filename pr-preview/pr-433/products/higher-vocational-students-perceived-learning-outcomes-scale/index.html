Higher Vocational Students' Perceived Learning Outcomes Scale¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A 5-point Likert self-report scale developed by Song Yafeng et al. to measure Chinese higher vocational students' perceived learning outcomes in knowledge mastery, skill application, and competency development.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Perceived learning outcomes differ across grades only in the skill application dimension, with first-year students scoring significantly higher than second-year students [+W]
  • Chinese higher vocational students report medium-high GenAI usage (M = 3.75) with usage habits highest, and above-average perceived learning outcomes (M = 4.01) with competency development ranked highest [+W]
