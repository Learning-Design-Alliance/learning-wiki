UCSMP secondary mathematics curriculum¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 6 claims (4 for, 2 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2 · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A University of Chicago School Mathematics Project curriculum package for secondary mathematics courses, including student textbooks, teacher editions and resources, assessment materials, and technology resources.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Four years of multiple UCSMP courses have potentially positive effects on general mathematics achievement, with site-level and measure-level inconsistency [+W]
  • No studies meeting WWC group design standards exist for UCSMP Geometry, Advanced Algebra, Functions Statistics and Trigonometry, or Precalculus and Discrete Mathematics [~W]
  • UCSMP Algebra shows mixed effects on the algebra domain: one study finds a statistically significant positive effect and one finds an indeterminate effect [~W]
  • UCSMP Algebra has potentially positive effects on general mathematics achievement for secondary students, with a small extent of evidence [+W]
  • The WWC rates multiple UCSMP courses as having potentially positive effects on mathematics achievement for secondary students [+W]
  • The WWC rates UCSMP Algebra as having potentially positive effects on mathematics achievement for secondary students [+W]
