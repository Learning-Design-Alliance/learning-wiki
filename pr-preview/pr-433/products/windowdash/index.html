Description¶

WindowDash is a synthetic food-delivery teaching-case dataset and accompanying instructional materials developed by Bang An et al. for undergraduate business analytics and visualization instruction.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • The Olist and WindowDash simulation inputs are matched in scale and analytical scope, with overlapping distributions but differing duration-rating associations [+W]
  • Designed patterns can rest on very small supporting subgroups: only three orders meet the Price-Rating Sensitivity conditions, with a mean rating of 2.0 [+W]
  • AI agents recover a smaller share of reference patterns from the synthetic WindowDash dataset than from the real-world Olist dataset [+W]
