Phonak Roger Focus II remote microphone system¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Phonak Roger Focus II is a Sonova/Phonak wireless classroom remote-microphone and receiver system that transmits a teacher's speech directly to a student's ear-level receivers to improve listening access in noise.

Components¶

  • Phonak Roger Focus II remote microphone system for classroom use: The trialed intervention is a teacher-worn Phonak Roger Touchscreen Microphone transmitter wirelessly connected to ear-level Roger Focus II-312 receivers worn bilaterally by the student. Unlike hearing aids, "these receivers exclusively obtain wireless signals from the Roger Touchscreen Transmitter at safe, comfortable and adjustable (-8 to +8 dB) volumes", fitted with open domes to minimize occlusion and adjusted in 2 dB increments to individual comfort. (Shiels et al. (2026))

Claims¶

  • RMT use improves speech recognition in noise for inattentive children with listening difficulties during a 4-week crossover RCT [+M]
  • Students and teachers report improved classroom listening during RMT use, with reduced teacher-rated inattention and hyperactivity [+M]
