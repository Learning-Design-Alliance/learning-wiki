FTW toolkit for running Future Technology Workshops¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 design), q1–q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A workshop implementation package that provides the structure, materials, and session instructions for running Future Technology Workshops, enabling facilitators to conduct participatory design with children or adults without specialist software-design training.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Starting the FTW process in the far future (Box 4) frees participants from present technological limitations [+W]
  • Early FTW pilots produced only general non-functional requirements, prompting method modifications [+W]
  • Toolkit implementation requires modest physical and technological infrastructure: one meeting space, one computer per participant, and 12 to 18 hours of internet connection [+W]
