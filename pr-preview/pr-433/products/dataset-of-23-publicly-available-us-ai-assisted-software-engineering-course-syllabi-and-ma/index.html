Dataset of 23 publicly available U.S. AI-assisted software engineering course syllabi and materials¶

Description¶

A dataset compiled by Francis Geng et al. containing publicly available syllabi and course materials from 23 U.S.-based upper-division software engineering courses with explicit, required generative-AI use in graded tasks.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Among AI-assisted software engineering courses with public objectives, human-AI collaboration, tool development, and evaluation are the most common learning-objective themes [+W]
  • Only 9 of 23 courses publicly named AI tools, with Claude Code, Cursor, and GitHub Copilot most frequent and a fragmented long tail [+W]
  • AI/ML fundamentals, agentic development, prompting, and testing are the most commonly documented topics in AI-assisted software engineering courses [+W]
