Red Light, Purple Light¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A preschool music-and-movement programme that uses progressively complex cue-and-action games to develop children's self-regulation, including attention switching, instruction memory, and impulse inhibition.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Red Light, Purple Light has potentially positive effects on preschool children's self-regulation, with moderate evidence [+M]
  • Red Light, Purple Light shows uncertain effects on preschool language outcomes [~W]
  • Red Light, Purple Light shows uncertain effects on reading and literacy related achievement [~W]
