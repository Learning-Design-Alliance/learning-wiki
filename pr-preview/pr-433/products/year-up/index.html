Description¶

Year Up is a workforce-development programme that combines occupational and technical training, advising and support, paid internships, and post-program job-placement assistance for low-income young adults, operated by Year Up.

Components¶

  • Year Up occupational training and internship program: Year Up is an occupational and technical education intervention for low-income young adults ages 18 to 24 with a high school diploma or equivalent. Students enroll in learning communities of about 40 students and complete "six months of full-time occupational and technical training followed by a six-month internship in the information technology or financial services sectors." Supports include advising, mentoring, instructional support, social services, weekly stipends of around $150 during training and $200 during internship, and post-program job search and placement support for up to four months. Training covers technical skills, business communications, and workplace functioning skills, s (Year Up)
