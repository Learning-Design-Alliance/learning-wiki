PRISM (Program for Research Into Self-Modifying Systems)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A production-system interpreter developed by Pat Langley and Neches that augments LISP and lets users select among alternative architectural mechanisms and control options.

Components¶

  • PRISM: a production-system interpreter offering user-selectable options at key architecture choice points: PRISM (Program for Research Into Self-Modifying Systems), developed by Pat Langley and the author, is a production-system interpreter implemented by augmenting LISP, owing a major debt to Forgy's OPS4. Its design philosophy is that "thereare too many. Unresolvedquestionsabout the detailsofhow a production system. should work," so instead of fixing one architecture it identifies key choice points, offers plausible options, and lets sophisticated users implement alternatives. It expands the traditional data-memory/production-memory recognize-act cycle with user-controlled options such as three spreading-activation schemes (spread-to-depth, spread-to-limit, and directed activation) and split me (Neches (1982))
