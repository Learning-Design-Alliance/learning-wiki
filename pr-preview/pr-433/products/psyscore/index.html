PsyScore¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A psychometrically aware automated essay-scoring and formative-feedback framework developed by Wei Xia et al. that combines latent-ability scoring with ZPD-conditioned instructional feedback.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • PsyScore-AES achieves a state-of-the-art average QWK of 0.747 on ASAP++, surpassing the strongest baseline SaMRL-large (0.722) and ranking first on 6 of 8 prompts [+M]
  • In pairwise LLM-judge comparisons PsyScore-AEF wins over 90% in Actionability against GPT-4o and Llama, and removing the psychometric module reduces Adaptivity and Tone wins with PsyScore retaining above 75% [+M]
  • Removing grid-search initialization of IRT discrimination and difficulty parameters drops scoring performance from QWK 0.747 to 0.705 [~W]
  • At trait level PsyScore-AES ranks first in 10 of 11 dimensions (mean QWK 0.735), improving Voice scoring from 0.619 to 0.740 while trailing T-MES on Style [+W]
  • Three education experts rate PsyScore feedback significantly higher than all LLM baselines on every dimension (p < .001), with the No-IRT ablation declining in Adaptivity and Accuracy (p < .05) [+W]
