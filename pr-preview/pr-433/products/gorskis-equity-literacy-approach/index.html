Gorski's equity literacy approach¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A framework developed by Paul Gorski for helping educators recognize, analyze, and respond to inequity through equity-oriented knowledge, skills, dispositions, and action.

Components¶

  • Gorski's equity literacy approach as an organising framework for analysing teacher learning about diversity and inclusion: The review adopts Gorski's equity literacy approach (2016) as its analytical framework for characterising what drama and theatre-based work forms achieve in teacher education. As printed, the 26 eligible articles "were analysed using Gorski's equity literacy approach (2016) as a framework." Within the review, equity literacy encompasses skills and competences such as empathy, perspective-taking and collaboration, attitude shifts regarding diversity and inclusion, and advocacy and willingness to teach for social justice. (Meedendorp et al. (2025))
