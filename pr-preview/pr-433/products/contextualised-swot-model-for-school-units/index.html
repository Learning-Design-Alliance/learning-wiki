Contextualised SWOT Model for School Units¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A theoretically informed, participatory four-phase SWOT-based strategic-management framework for diagnosing, prioritising, implementing, and reviewing school-unit improvement, developed by Keravnos et al.

Components¶

  • Contextualised SWOT Model for School Units (CSM-SU): The CSM-SU is a theoretically informed framework that repositions SWOT analysis as a four-phase strategic management cycle for school units: Engage and Diagnose, Interpret and Prioritise, Develop and Implement, and Review, Learn, and Re-Cycle. Developed from an integrative review of 82 studies and refined through a two-round Delphi study, it treats SWOT as "a cyclical, evidence-informed, and participatory process" rather than a static planning matrix. The model embeds collaborative sense-making, stakeholder involvement, and trust-building as essential conditions, and is grounded in open-systems theory and distributed leadership scholarship. (Keravnos et al. (2025))
