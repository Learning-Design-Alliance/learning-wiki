Emerging Technology Adoption Framework¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A living edtech evaluation and procurement framework developed by Digital Promise and Educator CIRCLS, organizing stakeholder questions and criteria across initial evaluation, adoption, and post-adoption stages.

Components¶

  • Emerging Technology Adoption Framework question-and-criteria instrument: A living-document instrument from Digital Promise and Educator CIRCLS that structures edtech evaluation as objectives, criteria, and stakeholder-specific questions across initial evaluation, adoption, and post-adoption stages, plus a guidelines and curated resources section (equity, research-based, privacy, and teaching-with-emerging-technologies resources). It is "a living document to help guide evaluation and procurement processes — it is intended to evolve and inspire discussions." (Ruiz et al. (2022))
