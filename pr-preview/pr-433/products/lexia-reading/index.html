Lexia Reading¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 6 claims (4 for, 2 against) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Lexia Reading is a computerized supplemental phonics and reading-skills practice program with adaptive independent practice and web-based progress reporting, developed and provided by Lexia Learning.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Lexia Reading shows no discernible effects on reading fluency [-W]
  • Lexia Reading shows no discernible effects on general reading achievement [-W]
  • Lexia Reading has potentially positive effects on alphabetics for beginning readers [+W]
  • Lexia Reading has potentially positive effects on comprehension [+W]
  • Subgroup advantages for low performers and Title I students exceed full-sample effects in two qualifying studies [+W]
  • Lexia Early Reading outperforms no supplemental instruction on kindergarten alphabetics outcomes in Gale (2006), significant by author but not WWC calculations [+W]
