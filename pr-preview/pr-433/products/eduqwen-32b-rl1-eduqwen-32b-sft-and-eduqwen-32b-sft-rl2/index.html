Description¶

Singh et al.'s EduQwen family comprises open-source 32-billion-parameter pedagogical LLMs designed for customizable, auditable tutoring and educational-platform integration.

Claims¶

  • The EduQwen 32B models achieve up to 96.52% accuracy on the CDPK Benchmark, surpassing the previous leaderboard leader Gemini-3 Pro at 90.55% [+M]
  • The domain-specialized 32-billion-parameter model surpasses significantly larger general-purpose models such as Qwen3-235B-A22B-Thinking (86.63%) and Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct (78.60%) on the CDPK Benchmark [+W]
