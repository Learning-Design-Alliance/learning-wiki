Guidance-based ChatGPT-assisted Learning Aid (GCLA)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

GCLA is a ChatGPT-assisted learning aid developed by Lee et al. that uses Apple Shortcuts to require students' proposed answers before providing hint-based guidance and logging interactions for reflection.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • GCLA use improves cognitive and behavioral engagement and self-efficacy compared with traditional ChatGPT use in blended learning [+M]
  • GCLA use enhances higher-order thinking skills—critical thinking, problem-solving, and creativity—compared with traditional ChatGPT use [+M]
  • GCLA use improves chemistry knowledge construction, including two-week retention, compared with traditional ChatGPT use [+M]
