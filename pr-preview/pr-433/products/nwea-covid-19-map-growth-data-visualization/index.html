NWEA COVID-19 MAP Growth data visualization¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), q1–q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

An interactive NWEA Research dashboard that visualizes state-level MAP Growth achievement and projected proficiency data for the 2020–2021 school year to inform understanding of pandemic-related learning impacts and recovery.

Components¶

  • NWEA state-level data visualization of MAP Growth performance during the 2020–2021 school year: An interactive data visualization developed by NWEA Research that, in the article's words, was "developed to provide state-level insights into how students performed on MAP Growth in the 2020–2021 school year." It presents assessment data as one lens on pandemic-related student impact and is paired with a user guide for interpreting the associated NWEA state dashboard. (Greg King (2023))

Claims¶

  • The NWEA COVID-19 visualization provides state-level insights into 2020–2021 MAP Growth performance to inform academic recovery [+W]
  • The dashboard reports median achievement percentiles normed to a nationally representative pre-COVID sample and projected proficiency against state cut scores [+W]
