Transcend's Designing for Learning framework¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A Transcend-developed framework and accompanying primer and card deck that organize professional learning around individual variability, identity, motivation, and cognition.

Components¶

  • Transcend's Designing for Learning framework as the organizing structure for the series: The Designing for Learning framework is Transcend's learning sciences framework used to structure the four Edcamp sessions around the principles of individual variability, identity, motivation, and cognition. The report describes distributing "the Designing for Learning Primer and accompanying card deck" through the Edcamp Community, and quotes Transcend's principle explanations for each session (e.g., for motivation: "one of our central goals as designers should be to support motivation with each learner"). Each principle anchors one event's panel discussion and breakout topics. (Edcamp Design for Learning Series: A New Bridge Between Research and Professional Learning)

Claims¶

  • Participants reported increased familiarity with each Designing for Learning topic after attending the Edcamp sessions [+M]
