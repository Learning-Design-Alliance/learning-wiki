Critical Maker-Entrepreneurialism Program¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A four-phase, multi-month youth program in which participants identify community-rooted problems, develop solutions with iterative community input, and reframe entrepreneurial practices for community benefit.
