Stages of Concern Questionnaire (SoCQ)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A 35-item Likert self-report instrument developed by Loucks and colleagues within the Concerns-Based Adoption Model to measure educators' intensity across seven stages of concern about an educational innovation.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Individual teachers' concern profiles vary widely and can diverge from group trends during implementation [+W]
  • Anticipating a workshop versus having prior workshop experience differentiates teachers' concern profiles [+W]
  • The two-week SCIS workshop shifted participants' concerns from lower-stage nonuser patterns toward higher-stage user patterns [+W]
  • Teachers' concerns follow a developmental trend from lower to higher stages during curriculum implementation [+W]
