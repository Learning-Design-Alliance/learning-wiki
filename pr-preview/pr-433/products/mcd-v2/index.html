MCD-v2¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

MCD-v2 is a computerized adaptive cognitive diagnostic test developed by Vy Le et al. to report college introductory-mechanics students' mastery of 14 fine-grained learning objectives during instruction.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • The combined FCI, FMCE, and EMCS item bank covers all 14 learning objectives with at least six items each, mostly single- or two-LO items [+M]
  • DINA models fit FCI and EMCS posttest data well but fit FMCE data only marginally [+M]
  • The 14-LO attribute structure improved DINA model fit over MCD-v1's four-skill structure for all three RBAs [+W]
