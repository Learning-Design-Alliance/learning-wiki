Description¶

TEEMSS is a Concord Consortium physical-science curriculum for grades 3–8 that combines inquiry-based investigations with handheld computers, sensors, interactive models, and teacher reporting tools.

Components¶

  • TEEMSS inquiry-based science curriculum with handheld computers and probeware: TEEMSS is a physical science curriculum for grades 3–8 that "utilizes computers, sensors, and interactive models to support investigations of real-world phenomena." It comprises 15 inquiry-based instructional units, five per grade band (3–4, 5–6, 7–8), each targeting the five National Science Education Standards. Every unit contains two one-week investigations with a discovery question, several trials, analysis, and ideas for further investigations. Student data are managed by software on handheld computers and can be transmitted to other students and the teacher; a web-based teacher-reporting tool supports assessment and class discussion. Developed by the Concord Consortium with National Sc (Technology Enhanced Elementary and Middle School Science (TEEMSS))

Claims¶

  • The supporting evidence for TEEMSS comes from a quasi-experimental study meeting WWC standards only with reservations [+M]
  • The WWC rated TEEMSS as having potentially positive effects on general science achievement with a small extent of evidence [+M]
  • TEEMSS produced a statistically significant positive effect on the grade 3–4 sound unit test in a quasi-experimental study [+M]
