Hong Kong Knowledge-Building Teachers Network (KBTN)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A Hong Kong Ministry of Education-supported teacher network that connects policy, peer support, CSCL research, knowledge-building theory, and classroom technology to support teachers adopting knowledge-building pedagogy.

Components¶

  • Knowledge-Building Teacher Network (KBTN) in Hong Kong: A teacher network funded by the Hong Kong Education Bureau since 2006, consisting of more than 50 teachers across primary and secondary schools. It uses a secondment scheme (50% release time) and later a university-school partnership scheme with teacher associates. The network connects researchers and teachers to implement and scale up knowledge building across 18-29 schools per year, spanning curriculum areas from science to liberal studies. (Chan (2011))

Claims¶

  • Teachers who focused on knowledge-building principles showed deeper understanding than those focused on surface procedures or technical aspects [+W]
