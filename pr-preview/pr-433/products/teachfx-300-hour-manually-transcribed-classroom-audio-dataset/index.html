TeachFX 300-hour manually transcribed classroom audio dataset¶

Description¶

A 300-hour manually transcribed classroom-audio dataset created by TeachFX to improve transcription models and support research on equitable automated speech recognition across diverse English dialects.

Components¶

  • TeachFX 300-hour manually transcribed classroom audio dataset for equitable ASR research: TeachFX manually transcribed 300 hours of classroom audio recordings to create a dataset with dual purposes: refining transcription models within TeachFX to improve product accuracy, and providing external researchers with resources for more equitable AI and education research. The project aimed to enhance transcription for teachers and students who speak diverse cultural and regional English dialects, addressing known biases in automated speech recognition. (An Ethical and Equitable Vision of AI in Education: Learning Across 28 Exploratory Project)

Claims¶
