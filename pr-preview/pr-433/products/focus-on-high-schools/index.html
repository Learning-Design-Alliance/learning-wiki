Focus on High Schools¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes

Description¶

A Boston Public Schools reform programme that reorganized comprehensive high schools into smaller learning communities and reformed English and language arts programmes.

Components¶

  • Focus on High Schools initiative (Boston Public Schools): The Focus on High Schools initiative was a Boston Public Schools reform "intended to transform Boston's 12 comprehensive high schools into more effective institutions by breaking them down into smaller, more intimate learning communities and reforming their English and language arts programs." The Mathematica report evaluated its effects on student academic outcomes, finding score reductions alongside improved attendance and promotion outcomes. (Susanne James-Burdumy et al. (2008))

Claims¶

  • After the Focus on High Schools initiative, days absent, days tardy, and student suspensions were lower [+W]
  • Boston's Focus on High Schools initiative was associated with reductions in student math and English scores after implementation [-W]
  • The initiative was associated with an overall increase in promotions to the next grade, concentrated among special education students [+W]
