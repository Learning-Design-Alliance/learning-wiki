Career Academies¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Career Academies is a school-within-a-school programme model that combines small learning communities, career-themed academic and career courses, work experience, and partnerships with employers, higher education, and the community.

Components¶

  • Career Academies program element: Career Academies are a dropout prevention program for high school youth organized as a school-within-a-school with a career theme such as health care, finance, technology, communications, or public service. Students "take both career-related and academic courses and acquire work experience through partnerships with local employers." The National Career Academy Coalition's standards of practice identify "three basic features" as key elements: small learning communities, a focused career-themed curriculum, and partnerships with employers, higher education institutions, and the community.
