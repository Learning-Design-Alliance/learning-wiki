TEAM Student¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A branded whole-school framework that centers students and families in school decisions through individualized academic, social, and emotional support and family partnership.

Components¶

  • Celebratory traditions: quarterly awards, bridging ceremonies and MVP Button recognition: the community-building events the article describes

Claims¶

  • Parent involvement increased for three consecutive years under family-centered engagement approaches [+W]
  • Out-of-school suspensions decreased 41% at a middle school implementing PBIS under the TEAM Student framework [+W]
  • Student mastery increased modestly from 17.5% to 19.2% during TEAM Student implementation [+W]
