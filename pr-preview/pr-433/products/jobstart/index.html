JOBSTART¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

JOBSTART is a nonresidential alternative education and occupational-training programme for disadvantaged high school dropouts, combining GED preparation, occupational skills training, support services, and job placement assistance.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • JOBSTART's positive completion effect held across gender and parenthood subgroups, with the largest effect for women living with their own children [+M]
  • JOBSTART's completion gain came entirely from GED receipt, with a small significant negative effect on earning a high school diploma [+M]
  • JOBSTART youth participated in substantially more education and training during follow-up than control youth [+M]
  • JOBSTART increased the likelihood that disadvantaged high school dropouts earned a high school diploma or GED certificate within 48 months of random assignment [+M]
  • The JOBSTART and control groups showed no statistically significant baseline differences on compared characteristics [+M]
  • The WWC rated JOBSTART as having potentially positive effects on completing school, based on a small extent of evidence from a single study [+M]
