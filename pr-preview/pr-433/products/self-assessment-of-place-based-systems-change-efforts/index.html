Self-Assessment of Place-Based Systems Change Efforts¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A self-assessment instrument developed by Mathematica and Equal Measure to help community partnerships examine their strengths and identify areas for deeper place-based systems-change work.

Components¶

  • Self-Assessment of Place-Based Systems Change Efforts instrument: A self-assessment tool developed by Mathematica and Equal Measure that helps community partnerships 'take a closer look at their areas of strength and areas where they can go deeper to effect place-based systems change.' It draws on learning from cradle-to-career education partnerships and structures reflection on how well a partnership is functioning as a system-change effort. (Kristin Hallgren et al. (2021))
