California's job-embedded administrator induction model¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

California's two-year induction programme for Administrative Services Credential holders, administered through Commission-approved providers and combining individualized coaching, professional development, and standards-aligned assessments.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Frequency of coaching field support varies across California induction programs and is positively related to administrators' ratings of program effectiveness [+M]
  • California administrator induction is rated effective by over 90% of completers, with at least 85% reporting strong preparation on every CPSEL leadership skill [+M]
