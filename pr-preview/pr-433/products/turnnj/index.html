Description¶

TurnNJ is Uncommon Schools' whole-school turnaround programme, implemented in schools in Camden and Newark to extend its school-improvement model to existing schools.

Components¶

  • TurnNJ whole-school turnaround project: TurnNJ is a project of Uncommon Schools, "a nonprofit charter management organization that starts and manages charter schools," intended "to continue and expand Uncommon's whole-school turnaround efforts in six schools in Camden and Newark." It was supported by a 2016 i3 grant from the U.S. Department of Education and evaluated with propensity score matching. (Alyson Burnett et al. (2021))

Claims¶

  • Enrollment in a TurnNJ school produced positive, statistically significant ELA achievement gains persisting up to four years, equal to an additional 1.9 years of learning after one year [+M]
  • Enrollment in a TurnNJ school produced positive, statistically significant math achievement gains persisting up to four years, equal to an additional 1.8 years of learning after one year [+M]
  • The Uncommon Schools whole-school model, typically built from the earliest grades in new schools, produced substantial positive achievement effects when applied in a turnaround setting [+M]
