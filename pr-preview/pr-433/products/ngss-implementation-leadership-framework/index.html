NGSS Implementation Leadership Framework¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A framework for guiding leaders and leadership development in implementing the Next Generation Science Standards, developed for the NGSS education community.

Components¶

  • Six foundational leadership knowledge areas for NGSS implementation: Domain 1 of the framework identifies six distinct but related knowledge bases leaders need: NGSS and the Innovations in NGSS; Equity and Access for All Learners; Instructional Materials, Curriculum, and Assessment; Professional Learning; Systems Change; and Continuous Improvement. The article states "leaders need in-depth knowledge within six distinct, but related knowledge bases (Figure 1.1)." These areas were identified through a summit with national experts and interviews with leaders in two states, and they inform all critical actions in Domain 2. (Stiles et al. (2017))
