Paired self-report and objective-based AI literacy assessment instruments for K–12 teachers¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 4 studies (3 associational, 1 quant-synthesis), q2 · 1 of 4 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A psychometrically validated scenario-based multiple-choice test and Likert self-report scale developed to measure K–12 teachers' AI literacy across knowledge, application, evaluation and creation, and ethics.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • The objective-based AI literacy assessment demonstrates psychometric stability with high reliability (KR-20 = 0.862) and approximate unidimensionality [+M]
  • The self-report measure supports a refined 13-item, four-factor model with good fit [+M]
  • Teachers' self-reported AI literacy correlates only weakly with their objectively measured performance (r = 0.07–0.24) [+M]
  • Perceived and objectively demonstrated AI literacy show only limited alignment in the same samples (pooled r = .055, interval including zero) [~W]
