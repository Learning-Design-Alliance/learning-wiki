Expeditionary Learning¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size

Description¶

A whole-school educational model developed and supported by EL Education that organizes schools around experiential, project-based learning and related schoolwide practices.

Components¶

  • Expeditionary Learning (EL) model schools: Expeditionary Learning (EL) model schools are whole-school designs evaluated in this report. The page describes "the first rigorous study of the impacts of Expeditionary Learning (EL) model schools," commissioned by EL Education and prepared by Mathematica Policy Research, focusing on five EL middle schools and their academic achievement impacts relative to other public schools. (Ira Nichols-Barrer (2013))
