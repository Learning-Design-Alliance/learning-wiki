New Chance¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

New Chance is a two-phase education-and-employment programme developed and implemented by MDRC and partner sites for young welfare mothers who had dropped out of school.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • The evidence base for New Chance is a single randomized controlled trial of 2,322 women across 16 sites, rated small in extent [+W]
  • New Chance's completion effect came entirely from GED receipt, with a small negative effect on earning a high school diploma [~W]
  • The average improvement index for New Chance on completing school is +8 percentile points [+W]
  • New Chance produced a statistically significant increase in the likelihood of earning a high school diploma or GED certificate [+W]
