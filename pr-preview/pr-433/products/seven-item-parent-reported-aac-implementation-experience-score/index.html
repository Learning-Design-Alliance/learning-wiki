Seven-item parent-reported AAC implementation experience score¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A seven-item parent-reported questionnaire developed by Jarrar Hala et al. to measure experiences of implementing augmentative and alternative communication, including support, confidence, fit, teamwork, follow-up, and emotional needs.
