Description¶

REL Midwest's Teaching Fractions Toolkit is a grade 6 mathematics professional-development and support package featuring teacher learning modules, reflection tools, formative-assessment probes, and resources for educational leaders.

Components¶

  • Teaching Fractions Toolkit (TFT) providing grade 6 teacher PD modules, reflection tool, and formative assessment probes: The TFT is a professional development and support package for grade 6 math teachers on evidence-based fraction instruction. It includes "six PD modules, a teacher reflection tool, and formative assessment probes", plus resources for administrators and math leaders such as "videos, infographics, checklists, and teacher PD facilitator guides". (REL Midwest (2023))

Claims¶

  • The partnership's long-term goal is that students can solve fraction computation and ratio and rate problems [+W]
  • The partnership targets improved teacher instruction in eliciting student thinking, using representations, and focusing on math reasoning [+W]
