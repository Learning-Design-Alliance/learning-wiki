Skusie¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Skusie is a bilingual humanoid social-robot system developed by Yanghee Kim and colleagues to mediate collaborative interaction and peer play among culturally and linguistically diverse kindergarten children.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Added cooperation prompts helped disagreeing pairs of children choose together [+W]
  • Personalized, friend-like communication calling children by name invited even shy children to participate [+W]
  • Flexible design with a human wizard anticipated kindergarteners' developing communication styles in robot-mediated interaction [+W]
  • Anchoring activities in familiar contexts, such as photos of the children's own school, immediately engaged every child [+W]
  • A bilingual robot speaking Spanish and English equally was essential for integrating Spanish and English speakers [+W]
  • Wizard prompts that waited for delivery and a growing lexicon improved the robot's responsiveness over iterations [+W]
