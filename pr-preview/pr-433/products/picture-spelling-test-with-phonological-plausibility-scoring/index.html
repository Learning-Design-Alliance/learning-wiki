Picture Spelling Test with phonological plausibility scoring¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A researcher-designed picture-based spelling assessment with phonological-plausibility scoring, developed by Geers and Hayes for adolescents with prelingual deafness and cochlear implants.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Adolescents with cochlear implants spell significantly less accurately than hearing peers, though 55% spell within one standard deviation of them and 45% two or more below [+M]
  • Adolescents with cochlear implants perform below hearing peers on phonological processing measures including word attack, spelling plausibility, elision, and nonword repetition [+M]
