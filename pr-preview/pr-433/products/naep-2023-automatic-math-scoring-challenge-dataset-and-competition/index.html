NAEP 2023 Automatic Math Scoring Challenge dataset and competition¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A National Center for Education Statistics challenge dataset and benchmark comprising scored and withheld-score grade 4 and grade 8 NAEP mathematics constructed responses for evaluating automated scoring accuracy and demographic fairness.

Components¶

  • NAEP 2023 Automated Math Scoring Challenge dataset and competition: The NAEP 2023 Automatic Math Scoring Challenge provided a training dataset of 251,370 responses from 10 items (five grade 4, five grade 8) and a test dataset of 27,930 responses with withheld scores. Items required short or extended constructed responses with maximum scores between 2 and 5. The competition goal was to predict scores within QWK 0.05 of human inter-rater reliability without demographic bias (pairwise SMD > 0.10). (Morris et al. (2025))

Claims¶

  • Finetuned DeBERTa-v3-large models achieved human-like agreement on nine of ten NAEP math constructed response items in the held-out test set [+M]
