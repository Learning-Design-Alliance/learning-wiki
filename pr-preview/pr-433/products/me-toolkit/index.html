M&E toolkit¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A monitoring and evaluation toolkit that helps practitioners generate and tailor evidence for solutions at different stages of development.

Components¶

  • M&E toolkit accompanying the four-phase approach: The M&E approach ships with an associated toolkit that supports evidence building across the four phases. The publication states the "approach and associated toolkit provide a road map for generating evidence about new or evolving solutions." The toolkit operationalizes the phased approach so practitioners can tailor evidence generation to a solution's current stage of development. (Kate Place et al. (2023))
