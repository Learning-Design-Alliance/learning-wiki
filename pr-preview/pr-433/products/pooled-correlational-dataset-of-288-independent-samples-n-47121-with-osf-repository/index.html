Description¶

A pooled correlation-matrix dataset of Chinese-speaking children's cognitive-linguistic skills and word-reading outcomes, released by the study authors through an OSF repository.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Across grade levels, children shift from a phonology-based strategy (PA) to a meaning-based strategy (MA and vocabulary) in Chinese word reading, while RAN and OA effects remain stable [+W]
  • Children reading traditional Chinese script rely more heavily on morphological awareness for word reading than children reading simplified script [+W]
  • All five cognitive-linguistic skills (RAN, OA, PA, MA, vocabulary) significantly and independently predict Chinese word reading, with MA and vocabulary showing the largest effects [+W]
