GPT Tutor¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), q2–q3 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

GPT Tutor is a GPT-4 chat-based mathematics tutoring interface developed and studied by Bastani Hamsa et al. that uses teacher-provided solutions, misconceptions, and hints to support high-school students' practice without directly giving answers.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • A guardrailed GPT-4 tutor with teacher-designed hints improves assisted practice performance more than a standard GPT-4 interface [+M]
  • Guardrails in the GPT Tutor largely eliminate the negative exam effect, though no positive exam effect is observed [+M]
  • GPT-generated tutor feedback is predominantly positive or neutral in sentiment [~W]
