DynamicEmo¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

DynamicEmo is a computer-based online measure developed by Jheng et al. that uses sentence-by-sentence emoji selections to assess learners' momentary epistemic emotions during reading.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Paragraphs presenting inconsistent information in refutation texts elicit activating epistemic emotions and suppress deactivating emotions, with text condition × paragraph interactions [+M]
  • Negative in-the-moment emotions toward correct-outcome sentences predict significantly lower open-ended knowledge revision scores [+M]
