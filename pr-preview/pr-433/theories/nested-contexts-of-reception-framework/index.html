Nested contexts of reception (NCOR) framework¶

Theory · All theories
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The NCOR framework, from Golash-Boza and Valdez (2018) building on Portes and Rumbaut, "conceptualizes educational institutions as one of several interrelated contexts which shape educational outcomes for immigrant youth." Contexts are not monolithic: an undocumented student may face exclusion at the federal level yet attend a welcoming campus in a state allowing scholarships. The article applies NCOR to school social workers' perceptions and argues local contexts like schools can attenuate hostile state or federal reception.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Analysis must treat contexts as interrelated fields rather than isolated federal, state, or local levels

Constraints¶

  • The framework, as the article notes, does not explicitly address the role of race, racial attitudes, and racial discrimination in institutions such as schools

Target Learners¶

  • Immigrant students in K-12 schools, including undocumented students

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • Educational access, belonging, and equity for immigrant students
