Research-practice-technology partnerships (RPTPs) as the ecosystem sustaining modelable systems¶

Theory · All theories
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article extends research-practice partnerships into research-practice-technology partnerships, arguing that "Creating a modelable ecosystem requires coordinated effort across the research-practice-technology partnerships (RPTPs)". Institutions support implementation at scale and long-term data collection; instructors provide contextual insight for interpreting measures; researchers bring theoretical and methodological tools; developers supply the technical infrastructure to translate insights into usable tools.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Sustained collaboration among institutions, instructors, researchers, and developers; a platform alone handed to instructors does not guarantee adoption or interpretable data.

Constraints¶

  • Measurement and scalability alone are stated to be necessary but not sufficient without the collaborative ecosystem.

Target Learners¶

  • students in institutions participating in multi-stakeholder learning analytics initiatives

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • sustainable, large-scale educational improvement through learning analytics

Claims¶

  • Predictive models built in a modelable ecosystem generalize across institutions [+W]

Examples¶

  • Use the EPIS model's four stages (Exploration, Preparation, Implementation, Sustainment) to bring an educational technology intervention to scale in elementary schools
