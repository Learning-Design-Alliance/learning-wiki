Theory of change: blended learning combining technology, pedagogy, and content knowledge to support student self-direction and improved outcomes¶

Theory · All theories
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The project's theory of change, illustrated with a TPACK image ("image ©2012 by tpack.org"), rests on three objectives: build teacher capabilities to create blended learning cultures, increase culturally relevant standards-aligned digital content in Kreyol, and improve broadband and electricity access. It proposes that "Blended learning will combine technology, pedagogy, and content knowledge to support students to self-direct their learning, thereby changing classroom culture and habits, eventually leading to improved student outcomes." The pilot model operationalizes this through infrastructure and technology, digital content, and professional development components.
