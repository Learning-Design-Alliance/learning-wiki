Soft skills as a distinct set of capabilities employers seek in new hires¶

Theory · All theories
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The publication treats soft skills as a recognizable category of capabilities that employers evaluate when hiring, distinct from technical credentials. It frames these skills as identifiable and describable through multiple evidence sources: "peer-reviewed research, research conducted by regional and national industry representatives, and a convening of regional employers." This framing positions soft skills as a legitimate target for educational and workforce development efforts.
