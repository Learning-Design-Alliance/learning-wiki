Multi-grade charter high school model as a promising configuration for long-term attainment¶

Theory · All theories
Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2–q3 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The report highlights multi-grade charter high schools — those that "include grades 6-12, 7-12, or K-12" — as a school configuration associated in Chicago with substantial positive effects on ACT scores, graduation, and college entry. The report treats this configuration as the basis of its headline findings and suggests "promise for multi-grade charter high schools (and perhaps charter high schools more generally)". The supplied text describes the configuration and its reported outcomes but not its internal instructional mechanisms.
