Homeschooling research compared with research on charter schools, voucher programs, and private schools¶

Theory · All theories
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article organizes homeschooling research by placing it alongside the research literatures on other school-choice sectors. Its abstract states: "The author compares research on homeschooling to research on charter schools, voucher programs, and private schools." This comparative framing treats homeschooling as one member of the broader school-choice family and uses the methods and findings of those adjacent literatures as a reference point for what quantitative homeschooling research has and has not established.
