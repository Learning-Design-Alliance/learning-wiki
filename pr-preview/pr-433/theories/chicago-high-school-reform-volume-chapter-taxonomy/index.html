Description¶

The volume's table of contents organizes research on Chicago high school reform into strands: whole-school redesign effects on schools and achievement (Hess and Cyntrynbaum), student performance trends from 1993 to 2000 (Miller and Allensworth), small schools transforming teacher and student experiences (Holland), whole school change through collaborative learning (Tozer, Yowell, and Gelb), comprehensive professional development (Kahne, Bridge, and O'Brien), cultural modeling (Lee), math teacher community and biography (Gutierrez and Morales), and implementation strength of research-based reforms (McPartland). The overview states the papers "document the results of reforms during the late 1990s at the district, school, and departmental levels."
