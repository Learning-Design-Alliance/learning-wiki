Joint latent curve modeling of achievement and self-efficacy as parallel developmental processes¶

Theory · All theories
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article proposes fitting latent curve models that jointly estimate growth in mathematics achievement and academic self-efficacy from repeated measures, allowing the two constructs to have unique developmental trajectories while estimating how their growth relates. It also examines "how the constructs relate within persons" by analyzing deviations from the trajectories at given time points. The approach is presented as addressing limitations of prior longitudinal models and yielding implications for education, psychology, and policy.
