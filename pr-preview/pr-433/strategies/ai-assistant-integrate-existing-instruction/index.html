Integrate AI assistants with existing curricula and teaching practices rather than replacing instruction¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Coursemojo's design approach positions the AI assistant as a complement to the teacher: it is "designed to 'supercharge the work that is already happening in classrooms' through seamless integration with existing curricula and teaching practices." The platform maintains "the collaborative, social nature of classroom learning" while providing individualized support. This integration-first stance is the implementation model the evaluated studies tested.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Seamless integration with existing curricula and teaching practices
  • The AI works alongside, not in place of, the classroom teacher

Constraints¶

  • Evidence base is limited to middle school English Language Arts in two districts

Target Learners¶

  • middle school students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • English language arts achievement

Related Strategies¶

  • Integrate AI literacy across grades and subject-area learning rather than confining it to electives
  • Integrate computational thinking into existing disciplinary teaching across grade bands rather than adding it on
