Educators share experiences, strategies, and insights about generative AI tools to keep the education community informed¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Given the dynamic landscape of AI technologies, the article recommends collaboration among educators: sharing experiences, strategies, and insights about generative AI tools can help the education community stay informed and make better decisions about tool selection and integration. The authors welcome feedback on the typology and encourage educators to share their experiences with these tools to build collective understanding of effective generative AI use in education.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Mechanisms or networks through which educators can share tool experiences

Constraints¶

  • The article notes the rapid pace of tool development makes it complicated to keep on top of the latest tools and recommendations

Target Learners¶

  • educators using or considering generative AI tools in their practice

Target Learning Goals¶

  • staying informed about generative AI tools and improving tool selection and integration decisions

Related Strategies¶

  • Foster communities of practice for AI governance instead of rigid top-down policies
  • Concentrate AI-edtech adoption priorities through collaborative networks that share tools, policies, and evaluation practices
  • Reorganize workshop modules in response to students' dynamic requirements as the semester unfolds
