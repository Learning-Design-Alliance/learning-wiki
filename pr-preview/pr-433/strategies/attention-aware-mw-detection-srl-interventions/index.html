Use automated mind-wandering detection in attention-aware learning technologies to trigger real-time interventions that promote SRL strategies¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 3 studies (2 review, 1 quant-synthesis), q2 · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review recommends that automated, unobtrusive mind-wandering detection via multimodal data streams and machine learning be used in attention-aware, adaptive learning technologies to deliver targeted interventions. It states that integrating mind-wandering research into SRL frameworks "can improve the effectiveness of real-time interventions based on automated mind-wandering detection in attention-aware learning technologies, thereby supporting SRL," for instance by promoting SRL strategies when detection signals disengagement.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Reliable automated detection of mind wandering from multimodal data streams (e.g., eye tracking, physiological sensors)

Constraints¶

  • Most current unobtrusive detection studies did not include SRL procedures, so integrated designs remain rare

Target Learners¶

  • university students
  • school students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • sustaining attention
  • self-regulated learning strategy use

Affordances¶

  • Mind Wandering Srl Conceptual Integration

Claims¶

  • Learners engage in mind wandering approximately 30% of the time spent in educational activities, and this is associated with deteriorated learning outcomes [+W]
  • Task-unrelated thoughts are consistently associated with lower test performance, accounting for approximately 7% of the variation in learning outcomes [+W]
  • Mind wandering can both prompt SRL and function as a regulatory strategy, and multimodal machine-learning sensing offers scalable assessment of attentive states [+W]
  • Providing elaborate explanations and summary interactives instead of just scores increases students' perceptions of engagement and understanding and reduces mind wandering [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Use multimodal data and time-sensitive analyses to capture situational motivational processes
