Draw on diagnosed errors and misconceptions to inform the teaching of integration and limits¶

Strategy · All strategies

Description¶

The article's teaching recommendation is that instruction in integration and limits should be informed by what clinical analysis reveals about students' understanding. It reports that "Some conclusions were drawn concerning the teaching of integration and limits", based on data about "the common errors and misconceptions" students displayed. Teachers can use such diagnosed misconceptions to target instruction on the points where understanding breaks down.
