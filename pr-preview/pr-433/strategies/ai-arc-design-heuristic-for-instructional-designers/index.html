Instructional designers use AI-ARC across analysis, design, development, and evaluation to align outcomes with responsible AI integration¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

As a design heuristic, AI-ARC helps instructional designers structure activities and assessments that foster increasingly complex cognitive and ethical engagement with AI. During analysis and design, designers can "map course objectives to each ARC stage to determine where AI should support inquiry, where human judgment must remain central." During development it guides prompts, reflection checkpoints, and assignment scaffolds; during evaluation the ARC stages serve as criteria for reviewing whether a course supports ethical AI use and preserves student authorship.
