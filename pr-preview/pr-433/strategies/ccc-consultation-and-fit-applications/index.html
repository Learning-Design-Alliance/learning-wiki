Use Classroom Carrying Capacity to guide class-wide consultation, intervention targeting, and person-environment fit research¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The essay proposes applying CCC in class-wide consultation models such as The Classroom Check-up, stating that CCC "compliments class-wide consultation frameworks and calls to assess and intervene in systems rather than at the individual student level." Administrators could administer CCC measures to identify assets and growth areas, visualize factors via heatmaps or radar charts to guide resource allocation, and use the framework to study how resource-rich classrooms buffer student-environment mismatches in person-environment fit research.
