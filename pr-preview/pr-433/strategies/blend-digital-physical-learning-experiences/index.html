Blend digital and physical learning experiences to address screen-time concerns in early childhood technology¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends technology usage that blends digital and physical learning experiences for young children, so that engagement with technology does not come at the cost of physical activity and social engagement. Kid Space embodies this by engaging children "more actively in physical movement and social collaboration during play-based learning."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Immersive computing experiences with multimodal sensing to support physical and collaborative interaction

Constraints¶

  • Studied only with first-grade students in a preliminary deployment

Claims¶

  • Kid Space use shifted children's attention away from the screen toward the physical environment [+W]
  • First-graders using Kid Space showed physical activity during play-based learning, in an exploratory case study without a comparison [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Blend virtual ed-tech practice with regular in-person meetings and check-ins in summer learning programs
  • Implement home-based digital SEC trainings with screen-time limits and family-participation options
  • Incorporate real-time personalization in immersive learning environments for future deployments
