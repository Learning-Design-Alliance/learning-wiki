Support virtual lab-based learning by mapping faculty guidance strategies onto the five inquiry phases¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends supporting virtual student lab-based learning by gathering and applying guidance strategies phase by phase. The authors "report strategies that we learned from STEM faculty about how they supported and guided virtual student lab-based learning in these five phases during the recent COVID-19 shutdown." The strategy treats each inquiry phase as a locus for distinct faculty guidance practices in emergency remote teaching.

Requirements¶

  • The strategies were collected in the context of COVID-19 emergency remote teaching with STEM faculty
