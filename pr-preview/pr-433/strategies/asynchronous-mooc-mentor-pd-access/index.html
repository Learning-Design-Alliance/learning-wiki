Offer mentor PD as an online asynchronous MOOC to fit teachers' working conditions¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 theoretical), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The authors recommend delivering subject-specific mentor PD as a short online asynchronous course on a MOOC platform, so teachers can participate at a time and pace suiting their contexts. They argue that the asynchronous mode "successfully facilitates access to practicing mathematics teachers on a full-time teaching timetable, as the work can be carried out from any geographical location and at times convenient to each participant". Six optional live sessions per year supplement the asynchronous design.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • a platform providing online spaces where participants interact as prompted by activities

Constraints¶

  • the authors note, following Laurillard (2014), that engaging participants fully in online collaborative learning activities aimed at developing research-informed practice is a challenge

Target Learners¶

  • full-time practicing mathematics teachers and mentors in the UK and abroad

Target Learning Goals¶

  • access to subject-specific PD despite time, workload and cost constraints

Claims¶

  • The VAS steps encouraged explicit engagement with mathematics education research, reflection on teaching practices, and application of research-informed strategies among mentor course participants [+W]
