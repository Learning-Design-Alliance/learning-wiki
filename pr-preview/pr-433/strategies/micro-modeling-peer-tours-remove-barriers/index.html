Strategies

Use micro-modeling, demonstrations, and peer-to-peer classroom tours to remove technical and pedagogical barriers during AI tool adoption¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Coaches supported teachers through mini demonstrations and modeling of Coursemojo, including in-the-moment "micro-modeling or thinking aloud with teachers in the moment," scheduled launch activities, and peer-to-peer modeling in which seventh- and eighth-grade teachers toured sixth-grade trailblazer classrooms. These demonstrations modeled concrete instructional practices, built teacher confidence and excitement, and helped teachers envision a shift from monitoring students to more interactive teaching.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Opportunities for teachers to see the tool used with their own curriculum
  • just-in-time guided support matched to coaches' and teachers' immediate level of need

Constraints¶

  • Coaches wanted even more on-demand support, such as joining a video conference call upon request to troubleshoot or learn

Target Learners¶

  • classroom teachers
  • instructional coaches

Target Learning Goals¶

  • reducing technological anxiety
  • building pedagogical fluency with AI tools
