Build students' mathematics ability, alongside STEM exposure, to foster STEM career interest during middle school.¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on the finding that STEM students show higher mathematics mastery and learning gain, the conclusion recommends that "if a teacher would like to build up students' interest in STEM during the middle school years, not only can she increase opportunities for students' exposure to STEM activities, but also build up their ability in mathematics."

Claims¶

  • Girls show less confidence and interest in math and science from early adolescence, and a strong math/science self-concept predicts course choice and performance for both genders [+M]
  • Math circle participants reported positive mathematics attitudes, with most respondents indicating mathematics was important, interesting, liked, and that they felt confident solving problems [+W]
