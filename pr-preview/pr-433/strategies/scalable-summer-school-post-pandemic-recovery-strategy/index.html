Offer scalable summer school as a post-pandemic academic recovery intervention for districts¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief positions summer school as a scalable academic intervention districts can use for post-pandemic recovery, addressing "Seasonal learning patterns & summer loss". Its description frames the report around "the effects of post-pandemic summer school on student achievement and district recovery", making summer programming a district-level recovery lever to evaluate against best practices and alternatives like tutoring.
