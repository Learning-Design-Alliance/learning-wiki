Professional development and schoolwide seminars supporting the ethics curriculum¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The Institute for Global Ethics offers "one-day or two-day on-site professional development workshops conducted by a Global Ethics staff trainer," plus on-site seminars and assemblies for students, using "lectures, group discussions, and small-group activities" with topical sessions on moral awareness, values definition, ethical analysis, and dilemma resolution. A one-day Ethical Fitness® seminar for school faculties costs $2,500, and three-day train-the-trainer seminars ($12,600) certify trainers to teach it. These supports build teacher capacity to deliver the curriculum's key concepts.
