Refine: use inherent LLM limitations as organic adversarial examples for critique exercises¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The fourth framework component leverages the inherent limitations of current LLMs — cultural biases, hallucinations in complex medical cases, and confident but incorrect citations — as organic adversarial examples for students to critique, rather than artificially engineering misinformation. The authors argue that 'finding and refuting AI hallucinations turns the tool’s weakness into a critical thinking exercise, transforming the interaction from passive consumption to active evaluation'.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Access to AI-generated explanations containing authentic limitations such as hallucinations or confident but incorrect citations that students can be asked to identify and refute.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • medical students
  • students evaluating AI-generated explanations

Target Learning Goals¶

  • critical thinking
  • active evaluation of AI output
  • verification against sources
