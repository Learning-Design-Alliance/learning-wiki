Scenario-targeted professional development interventions matched to learners' current metacognitive configuration¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends replacing generic metacognitive training with interventions designed for a learner's diagnosed scenario. For novices, "Interventions should provide rich, structured feedback (strengthen F→I) and scaffold self-monitoring development (introduce O→I) before attempting to develop bidirectional integration." For developing professionals the recommendation is diversifying entry patterns and enhancing external connectivity; for specialized developing professionals, adding bottom-up monitoring to prevent expertise rigidity.
