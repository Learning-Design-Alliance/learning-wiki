Derive requirements for personalised nudges from empirical studies of learner engagement¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The authors distilled four requirements from Studies 1 and 2 to guide the extension of AVW-Space with nudges: add intelligent support encouraging students to write comments while watching; make specifying aspects mandatory and encourage a diverse range of reflective aspects; include comment frequency and micro-scaffold use in the learner profile for personalisation; and add interaction means such as indicating areas of videos with interesting points and showing example comments. As printed: "Requirement 1: Further enhance AVW-Space with intelligent support to promote Active/Constructive engagement by encouraging students to write comments while watching videos." This empirically-driven strategy links observed engagement deficits directly to nudge design targets.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • User studies with interaction logs, comments and questionnaires from which engagement deficits and requirements can be distilled

Constraints¶

  • The requirements address the Personal Space of AVW-Space and were formulated from the two initial studies with postgraduate and undergraduate students

Target Learners¶

  • university students, particularly those less experienced in self-regulated learning

Target Learning Goals¶

  • active/constructive engagement with video content for transferable skills learning

Affordances¶

  • Choice Architecture Personalised Video Nudges

Claims¶

  • Students who chose to comment on videos and rate comments (active/constructive) were reported to gain conceptual understanding of presentation skills while passive watchers did not, in self-selected groups [+W]
  • Specified aspects acted as micro-scaffolds: comments on example videos were almost equally distributed over the aspects, indicating participants watched with those aspects in mind [+W]
