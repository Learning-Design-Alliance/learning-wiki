Integrate universal design into teacher technology training and promote teacher AT training plans to support inclusion¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Based on the finding that teacher training is the most prevalent barrier, the review recommends that teachers acquire the necessary skills and competences to promote AT use, and that training plans be promoted so teachers can implement these tools properly in the classroom. It states it is "a need to integrate universal design into teacher technology training to maximise the benefits for all learners". Researchers are also advised to explore AT use in relation to the type and degree of disability of learners and to investigate effective teaching and learning strategies for these learners.
