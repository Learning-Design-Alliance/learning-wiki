Cluster-specific engagement and personalization strategies for the six learner profiles¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article proposes a rule-based decision mechanism that, once a learner is assigned to a behavioral cluster, tailors engagement strategy and personalization style to that cluster's characteristics. Table 10 pairs each cluster with a strategy, for example C1 (At-Risk Learners) receives "Increase scaffolding and reduce cognitive load" with simplified structured content and step-by-step support, while C0 receives challenging problem-solving tasks to sustain self-regulation. Learning objects are classified by content nature, cognitive demand, interactivity, and instructional objective to match content to the learner's performance and cognitive-behavioral state.
