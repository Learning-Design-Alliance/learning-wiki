Supplement TV demonstrations with a printed bulletin or similar printed matter¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The USDA sewing study concluded that television demonstrations of practices should be paired with printed support material. The digest states: "The data point emphatically to the importance of a bulletin or similar printed matter as a supplement to TV demonstrations in teaching sewing practices." The bulletin gave viewers a lasting reference for the demonstrated practices.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Viewers must be able to obtain the printed bulletin, as the sampled women had requested a Department of Agriculture sewing bulletin

Constraints¶

  • Evidence comes from a selected audience of homemakers who owned TV sets and requested the bulletin

Target Learners¶

  • adult homemakers learning sewing practices

Target Learning Goals¶

  • acquisition and adoption of demonstrated home sewing practices

Claims¶

  • In the sewing telecast series, most homemaker viewers learned new things and practice use increased with the number of programs viewed [+W]
  • The sewing telecast audience favored longer programs, the twice-weekly schedule, and more sewing TV, suggesting TV's potential for adult education of homemakers [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Use direct narration by an instructor talking directly to trainees when producing television instruction.
