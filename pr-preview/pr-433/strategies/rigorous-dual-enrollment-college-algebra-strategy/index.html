Enroll college-ready high school students in rigorous dual enrollment college algebra classes to support college entry and completion¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article presents dual enrollment in a rigorous college algebra class as a route from high school into postsecondary success. It reports that students who passed the placement test "and enrolled in a rigorous dual enrollment college algebra class" went to college and earned degrees at higher rates than similar non-participants. The strategy is implementable where a college algebra placement test defines eligibility.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Students must pass a college algebra placement test with a score near the cut-off for eligibility to participate as studied

Constraints¶

  • The supporting evidence applies to students with placement scores very near the eligibility cut-off, not to all dual enrollment participants

Target Learners¶

  • high school seniors

Target Learning Goals¶

  • college enrollment
  • college degree attainment

Related Strategies¶

  • Use dual enrollment and a shared pathway template so students complete prerequisites while still in high school
  • Implement or expand IB Diploma Programs in urban neighborhood high schools to promote college readiness
