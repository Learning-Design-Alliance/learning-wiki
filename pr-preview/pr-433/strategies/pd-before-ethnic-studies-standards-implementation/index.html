Provide professional development on the pedagogy and content of ethnic studies before implementing new ethnic studies standards¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Oregon's legislation pairs new ethnic studies standards with a recommendation for teacher preparation: the report states the Oregon SBE will adopt new standards "with the recommendation that schools provide professional development on the pedagogy and content of ethnic studies before utilizing the new standards." This treats educator capacity-building as a precondition for standards implementation. A parallel Kansas effort offers a four-week summer intensive for public school teachers.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Professional development must precede schools' use of the new standards

Constraints¶

  • The report presents this as a recommendation accompanying Oregon's standards adoption, not an evaluated intervention
