Team teaching: teachers form local and remote alliances, distributing teaching activities according to individual strengths¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The paper proposes that teachers form alliances, locally and remotely, to conduct team teaching. It states that "smart learning technologies can enable teachers to consider forming alliances with other teachers, locally as well as remotely to conduct team teaching in order to make plans, organize teaching materials, and categorize various teaching activities based on individual teacher ’s strengths and expertise." Each learning activity is supported by the best-suited teacher, learners see topics from multiple perspectives, and technology-supported team teaching circumvents classroom-size resource constraints, enabling thousands of learners to enroll.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Smart learning technologies supporting remote collaboration
  • Matching activities to individual teachers' strengths and expertise

Constraints¶

  • Team teaching may cause problems when teachers debate an issue or propose different solutions; teaching is a highly professional job requiring many skills and much effort

Target Learners¶

  • school and university learners, including thousands of remote enrollees and differentiated learners

Target Learning Goals¶

  • enhanced teaching quality, multiple perspectives on concepts, and access to courses of choice

Affordances¶

  • Smart Learning Environments Framework

Related Strategies¶

  • Staff online tutoring with experienced, highly qualified classroom teachers and provide training in literacy, trauma-informed teaching, and remote instruction
