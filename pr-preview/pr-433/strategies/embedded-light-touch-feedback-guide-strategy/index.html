Embed a light-touch guide with tips and worked examples in the peer feedback interface¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The intervention presented four desirable feedback traits (rubric alignment, detail and specificity, suggestions for improvement, constructive language) as concise tips with sample comments, popping up when students first began reviewing and remaining accessible any time. The authors report "The training was provided to 95 students with no demands on instructors or class time, and students were in fact free to ignore it," making the approach scalable to large cohorts with no additional outlay of time or resources.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Guide housed in the platform so it appears at the point of feedback writing and can be re-consulted
  • Tips accompanied by sample comments demonstrating how feedback should be constructed

Constraints¶

  • The study examined only a short-term, one-semester intervention; the authors suggest greater impact could potentially be achieved with in-class discussion of the tips or required reflection

Target Learners¶

  • postgraduate students
  • large-cohort higher education students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • writing constructive peer feedback
  • developing evaluative judgement

Affordances¶

  • Spark Peer Feedback Quality Model

Claims¶

  • The guide increased the percentage of feedback comments displaying at least one S.P.A.R.K. quality trait [+W]
  • The guide increased co-occurrence of multiple S.P.A.R.K. traits within a single feedback comment [+W]
  • An instructional guide and examples led peer reviewers to write longer feedback comments, with a small effect size [+W]
  • Even with the guide, few comments displayed all five S.P.A.R.K. traits, showing limited comprehensive feedback ability [-W]
