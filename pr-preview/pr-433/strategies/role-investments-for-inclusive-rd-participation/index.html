Strategies

Change stakeholder roles and investments so marginalized participants can engage as co-experts in R&D¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report lists concrete changes to traditional roles and investments that enable partners to engage in Inclusive Innovation: pay for participants' time, buy out practitioner time, provide transportation and/or child care, build skills as needed to engage in R&D, provide recognition of contributions through micro-credentials, and build protocols and facilitation processes that ensure equal voice and participation of marginalized populations. These address typical challenges such as contextual knowledge not being perceived as expertise and lack of experience with R&D processes.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Differences in incentive structures, decision-making power, and definition of expertise must be addressed
  • Funders adjust processes to fund inclusive innovation programs without requiring a stated deliverable in advance of funding
