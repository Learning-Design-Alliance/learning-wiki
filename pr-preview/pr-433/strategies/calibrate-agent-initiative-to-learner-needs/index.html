Strategies

Calibrate AI writing support by providing contextual feedback, dialogic scaffolding, and initiative matched to learner needs and task complexity¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Derived from learners' reflective accounts in the study's thematic analysis (RQ4), this strategy holds that effective AI writing support should offer feedback tied to the learner's actual visuals and text, scaffold reasoning through dialogue rather than one-way output, and adjust how much initiative the agent takes to the learner's needs and the task's complexity. The article synthesises its qualitative themes into three interaction design heuristics for equitable, autonomy-supportive multimodal writing systems.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires the ability to vary agent initiative (reactive vs proactive triggering) and to generate feedback grounded in the current task context

Constraints¶

  • The heuristics derive from post-task reflections of 79 medical and nursing students in one writing task context; the article treats them as design heuristics, not tested causal recommendations

Target Learners¶

  • higher education students using AI agents for analytical writing

Target Learning Goals¶

  • multimodal data interpretation
  • independent writing performance
  • learner autonomy with AI support
