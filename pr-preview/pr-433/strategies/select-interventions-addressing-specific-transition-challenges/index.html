Policymakers select transition interventions that directly address particular transition challenges¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report recommends that policymakers use the variety of proposed interventions to match solutions to specific problems: "The wide variety of interventions address a range of transition challenges, giving policymakers the latitude to select ideas that directly address particular challenges." Stakeholders can fund programs and policies aligned with the challenges they prioritize and the resources they have.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A range of candidate interventions addressing different transition challenges, so policymakers have options to choose among

Constraints¶

  • Selection guidance rests on the proposal papers' characteristics rather than demonstrated employment effects, which the report identifies as a common gap

Target Learners¶

  • youth receiving Supplemental Security Income (SSI) with disabilities

Target Learning Goals¶

  • employment outcomes and transition to adulthood

Related Strategies¶

  • Align selected data directly to the purpose or problem the data use is meant to serve
