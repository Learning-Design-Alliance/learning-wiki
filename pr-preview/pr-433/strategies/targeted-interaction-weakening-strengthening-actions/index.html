Targeted actions that weaken interaction blocks and strengthen inhibition-promoting interactions¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Once strong interactions are identified, teachers design targeted scenarios per profile: "action strategies may be carried out to weaken specifically interactions that are considered to be blocking the inhibition procedure, and, to strengthen the ones that are promoting it". Example actions include more fieldwork, observation and drawing, textbook critical analysis, promoting peer-to-peer interactions with achievable challenge, better media habits, and increased hands-on and theoretical scientific training.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Prior profiling of each student's strongest interactions
  • teacher formation and connection to research noted as supporting actions

Constraints¶

  • Actions differ by profile because human variance means not everyone has the same kind of interactions

  • primary and secondary school students
