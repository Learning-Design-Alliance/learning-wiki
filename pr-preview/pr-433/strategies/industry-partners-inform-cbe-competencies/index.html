Engage industry partners to inform curriculum development, including the competencies needed, and develop new programs in response to employer input¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief reports that "Industry partners informed curriculum development, including the competencies needed, and new programs were developed in response to employer input." This strategy makes employer input a direct input into which competencies programs teach and whether new programs are launched, aligning CBE offerings with workforce skills gaps. It is presented as a feature of how the consortium's CBE programs were built.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Industry partners willing to advise on needed competencies
  • A mechanism for translating employer input into new or revised programs

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • college students, including dislocated and low-skilled workers preparing for specific jobs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • workforce-relevant competencies aligned with employer needs

Related Strategies¶

  • Use external consulting and technical assistance to help colleges adapt programs to time-variant CBE models
  • Build certification-track career pathway programs through consortium colleges partnered with industry associations and universities
  • Build direct employer-listening channels and AI-specific advisory structures for small and mid-sized firms
  • Engage industry and train postdoctoral researchers as part of a learning-technology research center
