Mobilize community colleges to address low-income students' basic needs and financial stability to support completion¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report concludes that "community colleges can mobilize to address the needs of low-income students and students of color" by integrating services that help students "improve their financial knowledge, budgeting skills and choice of financial products, and develop and implement achievable career plans." This strategy pairs intensive one-on-one services, customized to individual needs and goals, with less intensive group-delivered services to expand reach. Implementation and outcomes studies of the WSSN together ground this recommendation.
