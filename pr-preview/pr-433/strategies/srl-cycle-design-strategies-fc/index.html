SRL cycle design strategies: goal setting, strategic planning across four strategy types, accessible self-control prompts, and self-monitoring records¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 6 studies (2 causal, 2 quant-synthesis, 1 review, 1 associational), q1–q4 · 2 of 6 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A set of implementable strategies for the SRL cycle aspect of the framework. Each SRL cycle should start with goal setting, where students first set learning goals to increase competence and may optionally set performance goals; strategic planning should cover four types of strategies — task, time, environment, and help-seeking; self-control is supported by making planned strategies easily accessible through embedded prompts and tools; and self-monitoring is facilitated by having students record strategy use and interim outcomes, for example in a spreadsheet template. Students should be given "the choice to select which strategies to adopt or adapt based on their learning needs and preferences".

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • SRL cycles should iterate multiple times throughout FC learning to allow students to utilize self-regulatory experience from previous cycles
  • The length of an SRL cycle is best defined by the learning milestones that provide feedback on learning outcomes (e.g., exams, projects)

Constraints¶

  • FC studies using matched SRL and FL cycle rhythms were often conducted in an experimental setting of several weeks, which may not be feasible for longer periods of learning due to the cumulative amount of SRL activities; in semester-long learning, one SRL cycle can be aligned with multiple FL cycles to avoid SRL fatigue

Claims¶

  • SRL support modules (progress dashboard and reflection prompts) in a flipped unit produced higher SRL skills, engagement, and learning performance than control [+W]
  • Interviewed students reported that the SRL intervention elicited self-regulated learning behaviors across goal setting, strategic planning, self-observation, and self-reflection [+W]
  • Goal setting and strategic planning positively predict attainment of personal course goals in MOOCs [+W]
  • Self-regulated learning improves achievement [+S]
