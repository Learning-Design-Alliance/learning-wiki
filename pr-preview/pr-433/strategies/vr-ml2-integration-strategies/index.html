Participant-envisioned strategies for integrating VR into Maltese language teaching¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article reports strategies teachers envisioned for integrating VR into ML2 teaching: adapting existing VR applications designed for other languages and incorporating Maltese language content, using VR to virtually explore Maltese culture and history, applying gamification elements from VR games, and facilitating collaborative learning in virtual environments. Participants 3 and 17 agreed that formal training and resources would also be necessary to fully realize VR's potential in Maltese teaching.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Professional development programs that equip teachers with the skills to effectively use VR in teaching, as Participant 3 demanded

Constraints¶

  • No Maltese VR apps currently exist, so strategies depend on adapting applications designed for other languages

Target Learners¶

  • international adult ML2 learners
  • primary, middle, and secondary school students learning Maltese

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Maltese listening and speaking skills
  • vocabulary acquisition
  • cultural and historical understanding in Maltese

Claims¶

  • Teachers reported VR could enable Maltese speaking practice at a level unachievable in classrooms or online, especially for students abroad [+W]
  • Thematic analysis identified immersion as the dominant theme, with teachers reporting VR could remove physical barriers and provide authentic practice settings for ML2 [+W]
