Use a place-based systems-change self-assessment across sectors¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The authors recommend that partnerships use this self-assessment to examine multi-sector collective efforts. Although it 'draws from learning in cradle-to-career education,' they state it 'can be used to examine multi-sector partnerships and collective efforts in areas such as health, transportation, and economic development,' making structured self-assessment a transferable improvement activity.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A multi-sector partnership or collective effort seeking to identify strengths and areas to deepen

Constraints¶

  • Originates in cradle-to-career education contexts; broader-sector use is the authors' stated extension

Target Learners¶

  • cross-sector partnership teams in health, transportation, economic development, and education

Target Learning Goals¶

  • identifying partnership strengths and gaps to guide systems-change improvement
