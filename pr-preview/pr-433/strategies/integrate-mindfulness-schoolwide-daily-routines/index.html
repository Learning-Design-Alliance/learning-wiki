Integrate mindfulness throughout the school day via mindful moments, pausing before reacting, and reflective norms¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief recommends schoolwide integration of mindfulness into existing routines: starting class with a "mindful moment" — a deliberate pause in stillness or slow deliberate movement — teaching students to pause before reacting to something provocative (e.g., teachers modeling three deep breaths before responding), helping students understand that a calm mind and body increase awareness of thoughts and feelings, ensuring norms encourage self-reflection, and providing space and permission to pause and reflect.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Norms and expectations of classrooms and the school reflect a culture that encourages self-reflection and development of self-awareness

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • all K-12 students in schoolwide implementations

Target Learning Goals¶

  • self-regulation
  • self-awareness
  • empathy and compassion

Affordances¶

  • Mindfulness Attention Self Regulation Framework

Related Strategies¶

  • Train and support educators to implement mindfulness through programs, coaching, and shared leadership
  • Embed brief daily wellbeing check-ins across academic courses to build student-teacher connection
  • Self-Regulation - Mindfulness Techniques
  • Begin class with a mindful moment to settle students’ minds and bodies in preparation for learning
  • Select evidence-based mindfulness programs matched to school needs assessment data
  • Embed practical stress-management and self-care strategies into existing daily routines
  • Use targeted mindfulness supports for at-risk students with trauma-informed adaptation and participation choice
  • Measure and respond to student emotions continuously in digital platforms and promote mindfulness and emotional awareness
  • Include emotional assessments into new digital learning platforms to address emotional stress and teacher burnout before attrition
