Conduct one-on-one empathy interviews with open-ended questions to uncover root causes of system challenges¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Empathy interviews are "one-on-one conversations that use open-ended questions tailored to elicit input and personal stories about a topic." They offer deeper questioning than typical engagement activities and allow leaders to "more deeply understand root causes of system challenges," which can result in refocusing priorities and designing equitable solutions.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Questions must be open-ended and tailored to elicit personal stories rather than surface-level responses

Constraints¶

  • Positioned as going beyond the surface where many data collection and engagement activities end

Target Learners¶

  • Students, caregivers, staff, and community partners in K-12 systems

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Understanding partner experiences deeply
  • identifying root causes of inequities and system challenges

Affordances¶

  • Five Considerations Engaging Educational Partners
