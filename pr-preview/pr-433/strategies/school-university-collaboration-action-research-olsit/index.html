Support OLSit implementation through school-university action research collaboration¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article proposes that the OLSit model is very applicable for action research by teachers, student teachers, and teacher educators, generating local knowledge about what works, for whom, and under what conditions. Because of the premium on every professional's time, "action research may be best supported through school-university collaboration to share the workload and triangulate insights about what is being observed". Artifacts generated through the model contribute to evidence collection without significant additional effort.
