Leverage cognitive strengths of low-SES children via group work, real-world application, and oral/narrative learning and assessment¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 against) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 associational), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Drawing on the "hidden talents" literature, the article proposes that classroom practices could build on cognitive advantages low-SES children may possess, including "social perception, attention shifting, and creativity". Suggested forms include increased team or group work, emphasis on applying learned material to real-world problems, and incorporating oral or narrative learning and assessment strategies. The authors state these suggested strategies need to be tested through empirical research.
