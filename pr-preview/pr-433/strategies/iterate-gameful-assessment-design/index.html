Iterate and innovate on gameful assessment design in response to mixed engagement evidence¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article concludes that gameful assessment design should continue to be iterated and improved. Given higher student satisfaction but "conflicting attitudes from teachers", the authors "discuss[] opportunities for continued iteration and innovation in gameful assessment design". This positions design refinement, rather than wholesale adoption, as the practical next step for assessment developers.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Design teams must attend to both student engagement evidence and teacher perceptions

Constraints¶

  • Teacher concerns about intrinsic motivation and desire to learn remain unresolved in the current prototypes

Target Learners¶

  • Grades 3–7 students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Student engagement and motivation during assessment
