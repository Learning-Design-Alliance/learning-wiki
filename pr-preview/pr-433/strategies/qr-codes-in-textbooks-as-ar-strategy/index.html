Embed QR codes in student textbooks as an augmented reality entry point to enhance performance¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), q1–q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article's strategy is to use QR codes printed in student textbooks as a form of augmented reality that links physical pages to digital learning content. The study "examined the effects of using QR codes as an AR to enhance student performance in Saudi education", and its findings indicate students using QR codes performed at higher levels and faced no technical issues integrating the technology into their learning.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Students need smart devices capable of scanning QR codes and displaying the linked AR content

Constraints¶

  • The author notes the absence of technical issues could be specific to the alpha generation, for whom technology became part of their lives

Target Learners¶

  • Saudi K-12 students, second intermediate grade in this study

Target Learning Goals¶

  • improving student performance in textbook-based subject learning

Claims¶

  • Students who used QR codes as augmented reality in their education performed at higher levels than students who did not [+W]
  • Students integrating QR-code AR into their learning reported no technical issues, attributed to alpha-generation technology familiarity [+W]
  • Augmented reality and QR-code mobile systems enable context-aware language learning in designated physical zones [+W]
