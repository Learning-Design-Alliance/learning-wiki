Guide adult students through self-regulated learning skills within the course of study using educational technology¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article's practitioner notes recommend that educators "should not assume that all adult students are self-regulated learners with effective study skills" and that guiding adult students through learning and applying self-regulated learning skills within the course of study can help their learning, especially online. Educational technology can enable this learning and application, and the practice of self-regulated learning skills within the technological system should be personalised and adaptive. The strategy was implemented through the ePSRL Management System embedded as a mandatory course requirement.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • The self-regulated learning practice must be embedded within the course of study rather than delivered only before it begins
  • The technological system used should be personalised and adaptive

Constraints¶

  • The study's evidence comes from healthcare-domain learners in Singapore, and the two student groups were not matched for age, gender and ability

Target Learners¶

  • Adult postgraduate students in online continuing professional education

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Self-regulated learning skills including goal setting, time management, reflection and learning strategies

Related Strategies¶

  • Provide explicit teaching of SRL methods as structured guidance for regulating learning in online and blended settings
  • Accommodate Varying Technology Experience
  • Regulate regulative tool use: guide students to the supports that match their task activity
