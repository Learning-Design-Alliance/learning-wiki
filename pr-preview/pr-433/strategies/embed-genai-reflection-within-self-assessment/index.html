Embed GenAI-focused reflection within self-assessment as a pedagogical strategy for ethical inquiry¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends embedding GenAI-focused reflection within self-assessment, stating this "reframes ethical inquiry as a pedagogical strategy". Rather than treating academic integrity as rule enforcement, educators position students as reflective practitioners who evaluate their own GenAI use for critical thinking, writing, and conceptual understanding. The approach is described as scalable and adaptable across disciplines and year levels.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Self-assessment tasks that include open-text prompts on the ethical implications of GenAI use

Target Learners¶

  • commencing university students
  • students across disciplines and year levels

Target Learning Goals¶

  • ethical use of GenAI
  • academic integrity
  • critical evaluation of emerging technologies

Related Strategies¶
