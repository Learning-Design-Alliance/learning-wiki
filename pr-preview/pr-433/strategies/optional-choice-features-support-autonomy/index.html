Incorporate optional choice features (remediation buttons, enrichment questions, self-check options) to support autonomy¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article describes incorporating choice through optional check buttons, optional remediation buttons, and optional guided enrichment questions that do not penalize skipping. These features were designed to reduce pressure and increase self-regulation through authentic student choice, supporting autonomy alongside competence.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Optional features must not negatively affect students' overall scores if skipped

Constraints¶

  • The effect of choice on autonomy in online lesson design warrants further research per the authors

Target Learners¶

  • Online secondary mathematics students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Supporting learner autonomy and self-regulation

Claims¶

  • All-in-one lesson structure with prior knowledge, optional help and challenge, and chunking supported autonomy and competence [+W]

Related Strategies¶
