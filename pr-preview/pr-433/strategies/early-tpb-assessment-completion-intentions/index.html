Assess TPB constructs early in high school to anticipate completion intentions¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

This strategy implements the article's approach: administer a questionnaire measuring attitudes, subjective norms, and perceived behavioral control toward completing school early in students' second year, then use these measures to anticipate intentions to complete the year. The article's support is that intentions were "accurately predicted from attitudes, subjective norms, and perceived behavioral control" (R=0.71) in its longitudinal sample.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A TPB-based questionnaire for the target behavior and an administration point early in the school year

Constraints¶

  • Evidence base is a single longitudinal study of 166 African American students aged 14 to 17

Target Learners¶

  • High-school students in their second year

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Early identification of students' intentions regarding high-school completion

Affordances¶

  • Theory Of Planned Behavior Completion Decisions

Claims¶

  • Among African American high-school students, intentions to complete the school year are accurately predicted from attitudes, subjective norms, and perceived behavioral control (R=0.71) [+W]
