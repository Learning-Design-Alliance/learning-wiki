Consider alternatives to self-explanation — instructional explanations, sequenced problem solving, retrieval practice — given its time demands¶

Strategy · All strategies

Description¶

Because generating self-explanations takes considerable time and is not a widely reported study strategy, the article recommends considering alternative activities that achieve the same outcomes more efficiently. It notes that "practice testing can promote better comprehension over a long delay than self-explanation" and that providing instructional explanations "can sometimes be more efficient and as effective as prompted self-explanation."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Learner-generated explanations should be of reasonable quality; training or structuring responses can improve quality where needed

Constraints¶

  • Whether self-explanation is more consistently beneficial than instructional explanations when responses are structured and time is comparable remains an open question the article flags
