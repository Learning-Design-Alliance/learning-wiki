Conduct a large pilot study to validate effectiveness with high confidence¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Phase 4 of the checklist prescribes a specific implementable activity: conduct a large pilot study and demonstrate with a high degree of confidence that the solution leads to improved outcomes for students in a priority community. This is the final validation step after earlier phases of design, refinement, and early-evidence assessment. The strategy scales up testing to build confidence before broader adoption.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Completion of earlier evidence-building phases before attempting validation
  • A solution already designed and refined through Phases 1-3

Constraints¶

  • Validation targets outcomes for students in a priority community, not general populations

Target Learners¶

  • students in a priority community

Target Learning Goals¶

  • demonstrating improved student outcomes

Related Strategies¶

  • Conduct a small pilot study to assess early evidence of success before full validation
  • Refine a solution based on lessons learned during implementation
  • Pretest the assessment and evaluate fitness for purpose before live administration
  • Phase-specific research-project activities: selecting situations and simulators, iterative design with expert workshops and pilot studies, and standardized study conduct
