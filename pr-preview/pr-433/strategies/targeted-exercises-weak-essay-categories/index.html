Strategies

Suggest targeted exercises from weak essay categories based on relative student performance¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (1 review, 1 associational, 1 design), q2 · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The system identifies a student's potential weakness in summary writing from relative performance across essay categories (narrative, descriptive, analytical, situational, general). When a student's performance measure in a category falls below a pre-defined threshold, "the system will then suggest more essays from that category to the student in upcoming exercises". This implements adaptive sequencing within a scaffolding model.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A student model recording per-category and overall performance measures and the number of exercises taken

Constraints¶

  • Rests on the stated assumption that summary writing performance can be measured by assessment results

Target Learners¶

  • secondary school students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • summary writing skills across essay categories

Claims¶

  • In a self-referential framework, LLMs, particularly GPT-4.1, tend to outperform a single operational human rater at identifying relative weaknesses (negative feedback) across several Language and Content aspects [+W]
  • Learning analytics tools can provide personalized feedback on students' academic writing by identifying mistakes and offering constructive suggestions (e.g., AcaWriter) [+W]
  • ChatGPT-3.5 feedback on EAP argumentative essays aligns closely with human instructor scoring and identifies surface-level issues, but is less effective on rhetorical structure and contextual nuance [~W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Adaptive scenario generation: introduce training scenarios targeting weaknesses identified from real-time performance metrics
  • Combine inter-learner rankings with intra-learner diagnostics in assessment and feedback
  • Use automatic reflection classification for preliminary feedback and workload support, with final assessment retained by educators
