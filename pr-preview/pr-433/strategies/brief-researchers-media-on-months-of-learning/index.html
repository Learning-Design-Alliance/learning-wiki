Brief researchers and media on how to calculate and interpret months-of-learning figures before using them¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief explicitly targets future users of the months-of-learning metric: "To inform future uses by researchers and media, we discuss in this brief how to calculate this metric as well as its trade-offs." The recommended strategy is to equip researchers and media with calculation guidance and an understanding of the metric's trade-offs before they translate test scores into months or years of learning in public communication.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Access to guidance on calculating the months-of-learning metric and its trade-offs

Constraints¶

  • The metric's perceived accessibility can mask major downsides, so communication guidance is needed

Target Learners¶

  • Researchers and media who communicate about student achievement

  • Responsible interpretation and communication of months-of-learning figures
