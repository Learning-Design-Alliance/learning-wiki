Create and foster diverse, effective magnet schools through coordinated federal, state, district, and school policies¶

Strategy · All strategies

Description¶

The report recommends a multilevel policy approach so that diverse magnet schools incorporating evidence-based components can be created and sustained. Federal recommendations include reinstating and updating federal guidance on evidence-based school diversity strategies and expanding investment in the Magnet Schools Assistance Program, which it notes was "funded at just $107 million in 2020, compared to $440 million provided to charter schools." State recommendations include targeted grant funding and permitting interdistrict transfers, as Connecticut has done. District recommendations support first door practices such as outreach to diverse families, inclusive enrollment, and strategic school siting; school-level recommendations support second door efforts including whole school programs, culturally responsive curriculum, and restorative discipline.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Districts must support ongoing outreach to diverse families through multiple platforms and open, inclusive enrollment practices such as lotteries, interviews, and essays.

Constraints¶

  • The report notes congressional prohibitions on federal funds for transportation slowed desegregation, and that MSAP funding has declined in real dollar terms and has not kept pace with demand.
