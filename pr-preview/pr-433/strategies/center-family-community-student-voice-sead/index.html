Engage students, families, and communities as full partners, centering marginalized voices in policy and practice decisions¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief holds that strong partnerships between educators, students, families, and communities are essential for SEAD: engaging communities gives educators deeper understanding of students' strengths, identities, and needs, fortifying relationships and increasing academic success. It recommends centering the voices of students of color and their families and those from low-income and marginalized backgrounds in decisions.
