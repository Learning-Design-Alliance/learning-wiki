Chatbot support should reinforce that learning ability develops through effort, strategy use, feedback, and reflection¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Drawing on the Axis 5 (source of ability to learn) findings, the authors recommend that when students seek support, "custom-built chatbots can be designed to recommend a different representation, prompt students to revisit relevant concepts, break a problem into smaller steps, or encourage them to reflect on which strategy was unsuccessful." Instructors can reinforce this by treating chatbot assistance as support for developing competence rather than as a substitute for thinking.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Chatbots able to offer strategy-level scaffolds (alternative representations, concept revisits, problem decomposition, reflection prompts) rather than only final answers

Constraints¶

  • The Axis 5 association was nominally significant only and did not survive Bonferroni adjustment

Target Learners¶

  • introductory physics students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • growth-oriented beliefs about the source of ability to learn science

Related Strategies¶

  • Design chatbots that begin with guided inquiry and progressively provide explicit guidance when students remain stuck
