Call for research clarifying the importance of specific choice-policy elements and assessing a wider range of student outcomes¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The authors propose a forward-looking research agenda for school choice policy: studies should "help clarify the importance of specific policy elements" and "assess a deeper and wider range of student outcomes" beyond achievement. This recommendation follows from their conclusion that program design details drive effects and that current evidence covers a limited set of outcomes and locations.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Research must examine specific policy elements of voucher and charter programs rather than treating choice programs as uniform

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students in voucher and charter school programs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Student achievement and broader student outcomes under school choice policies

Related Strategies¶
