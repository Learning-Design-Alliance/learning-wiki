Apply the review's findings to prepare K-12 teachers for online and blended learning¶

Description¶

The response article's stated application focus is translating the findings of the Philipsen et al. (2019) meta-aggregative review into practice for K-12 teacher preparation. The abstract says it "discusses the value of the findings and how they could be applied to prepare K-12 teachers for online and blended learning." This extends a review originally framed for teacher professional development generally toward the K-12 setting.
