Use co-design approaches to develop grade-appropriate AI curricula for elementary students¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The preface reports that elementary AI education work emphasizes grade-appropriate content developed through co-design. It states that Ottenbreit-Leftwich et al., "Emphasizing grade-appropriate content for elementary school students, they present a co-design approach for AI curricula." Co-design is thus presented as a method for creating AI curricula suited to young learners.
