Use implicit annotation-based personality measurement alongside questionnaires for online learning personalization¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 7 claims (7 for) · 3 studies (1 review, 1 associational, 1 design), q2 · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends computing personality traits implicitly from learners' annotation traces rather than relying solely on psychometric questionnaires, which learners may refuse or falsify. "Personality recognition based on learners' annotations is not presented here as a replacement for psychometric tests, but rather as additional information that may help combat some of the difficulties encountered with questionnaires." Measurement can be taken in parallel with interaction without burdening learners with long forms.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • An online reading environment that captures digital annotation traces is needed.

Constraints¶

  • The approach was validated only for conscientiousness and neuroticism, and only with graduate students who annotate frequently.

Target Learners¶

  • online learners
  • graduate students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • learner profiling
  • personalized reading support

Claims¶

  • Digital annotation traces can predict conscientiousness and neuroticism scores with reasonable accuracy [+W]
  • Bland-Altman analysis shows small bias between i-Read and NEO-IPIP scores for conscientiousness (−0.72) and neuroticism (1.29) [+W]
  • Conscientiousness is positively related to the number of textual annotation acts [+W]
  • Extraversion is negatively correlated with the average number of annotation acts [+W]
  • Neuroticism is negatively correlated with all annotation activity features [+W]
  • Self-report measures of SRL capture perceptions of strategy use rather than implementation quality, and a combined trace-data approach may be best [+W]
  • When SRL is measured at a fine grain size (specific strategies), self-reports show low calibration with behavioral measures [+W]
