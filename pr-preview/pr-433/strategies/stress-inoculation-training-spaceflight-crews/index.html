Stress inoculation training for crews operating without external support¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The review recommends stress inoculation training, drawn from high-tempo operational domains, as a behavioral readiness strategy for crews who must operate without mission-control resolution. It describes a model "which teaches individuals to anticipate stress responses, practice coping mechanisms, and develop skills under controlled conditions". It pairs this with emotion-regulation strategies such as reappraisal, attentional control, and response modulation, and recommends resilience training combining individual mental skills with team reflection and exposure to realistic mission stressors.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Training must be transferable and adaptive, conducted in settings that include communication delay, uncertainty, and incomplete information while under fatigue

Constraints¶

  • Recommended for exploration-class or long-duration spaceflight crews; the review presents it as a translational model rather than a spaceflight-tested intervention

Target Learners¶

  • astronaut crews on lunar and Mars missions

Target Learning Goals¶

  • anticipating stress responses, coping under uncertainty, emotional regulation, resilience under fatigue

Related Strategies¶

  • Phase-based behavioral readiness training spanning pre-flight, in-flight, and post-flight
  • Team training with variable leadership roles and simulated communication delays
  • Cognitive performance training embedded in operational scenarios under fatigue
