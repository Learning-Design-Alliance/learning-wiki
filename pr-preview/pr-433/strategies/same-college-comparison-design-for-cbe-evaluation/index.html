Compare CBE program participants with students in traditional online and brick-and-mortar programs at the same colleges¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The evaluation's design compares CBE participants' education and employment outcomes "to those of students enrolled in traditional online and brick-and-mortar information technology programs at the same colleges." This same-institution comparison strategy grounds summative findings about CBE in a relevant counterfactual population, while the authors note that differences "may reflect unobservable differences between the groups."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Access to outcome data for both CBE participants and traditional-program students within the same colleges.

Constraints¶

  • Because participants and comparison students were not randomly assigned, outcome differences may reflect unobservable differences between the groups, as the report itself states.

Target Learners¶

  • Community college students in online and traditional IT programs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Gatekeeper course completion
  • Credential completion
  • Employment and wage outcomes

Related Strategies¶

  • Evaluate CBE programs against both implementation milestones and participant outcome targets, including comparison benchmarks
