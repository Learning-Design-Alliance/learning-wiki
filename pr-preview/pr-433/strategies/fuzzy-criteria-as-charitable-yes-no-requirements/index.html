Handle fuzzy effort criteria as charitable yes/no binary requirements with periodic check-ins¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

When a contract names indefinable but central qualities such as "consistent effort" or "thoughtful feedback", the authors avoid arguments by never accusing a student of failing a fuzzy criterion unless the violation is grossly flagrant, and by taking the student's word in any dispute. They settle for "charitable, crude yes/no decisions in a realm where people usually emphasize subtle differences of degree", sometimes building in formal check points twice a semester, and they ask students to self-assess the criteria in process notes or cover letters.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Violations must be grossly flagrant before being called
  • Students' own word is accepted when they argue against a violation call

Constraints¶

  • The criteria focus on experiences inside the student rather than observable features in a text, so they remain arguable in principle

Target Learners¶

  • first-year college writing students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • effort, perplexity, and progression of thinking in writing
