Use the paper as a blueprint for eliciting learner fairness concepts and monitoring equality principles on platforms¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The authors position the work as a theoretical foundation for future studies: the paper "can be used to create examples of what questions to ask as part of interviews with learners, what scenarios to explore to elicit their concepts of fairness, and how to process data in the platform to monitor and ensure the equality principles". This makes it a concrete recipe for platform teams and researchers planning fairness audits of educational recommenders.

  • A post-processing re-ranking approach balancing personalization and equality of recommended opportunities leads to higher equality with negligible loss in personalization, as reported by the authors [+W]
