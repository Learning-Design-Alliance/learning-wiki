Description¶

The partnership plans to "Develop dissemination resources (blog posts, videos, infographics) to share information about the partnership, intervention, and relevant research." A stated note explains the design intent: "Dissemination materials are designed to support acceptability and feasibility of the approach," making communication artifacts an explicit part of implementation rather than an afterthought.
