Deliver TPD for OBL as a guided do-it-yourself course development service with training, templates, and milestone reviews¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article describes a TPD design in which faculty "enroll in an online asynchronous training course" covering "backwards design, accessibility and copyright, and best practices in assessment and student interactions," then use "a course template" to develop their online course. During development, faculty receive "the contact of eLearning support professionals who review their course at key development milestones." This implements the framework's components of support, clear goals, and templates.
