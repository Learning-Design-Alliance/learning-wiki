Provide on-site first-year training of 4-5 hours plus building-level ongoing support each year of ST Math implementation¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

MIND Research Institute provides initial professional development: self-guided online training on "startup, teacher and student navigation, and reporting", followed by courses on "the research basis and pedagogical aspects of the ST Math visual/conceptual approach". To match the support in the successful research, schools should provide on-site first-year training of 4-5 hours and building-level ongoing support each year.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • On-site first-year training of 4-5 hours and building-level ongoing support for each year of implementation, included in student and building pricing from MIND.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Elementary and middle school teachers implementing ST Math

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Effective implementation of visual/conceptual math instruction

Related Strategies¶

  • Implement Classtime with a kick-off training day plus ongoing grade-specific follow-up sessions
  • Brief initial training with extensive follow-up coaching and on-site facilitation for whole-school program implementation
  • Combine summer professional development with school-year on-site coaching and provision of complete instructional materials
  • Implement World of Words with developer training, ongoing coaching, and structured text-set materials
  • Layered professional development for curriculum implementation (webinars, workshops, coaching)
  • Layered implementation support for teachers adopting a new science curriculum: preservice training, follow-up training, and a science coach
  • Multi-format teacher professional development for implementing the Facing History and Ourselves curriculum
  • Ongoing math-specialist coaching and curriculum-based professional development for implementing teachers
