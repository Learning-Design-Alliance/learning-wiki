Register analysis plans through a publicly accessible registry system before analysis¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 theoretical), q2–q3 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

For the Preregistered badge, the disclosure form operationalizes preregistration as depositing a time-stamped, immutable registration and answering whether the plan was registered before data examination and whether all registered analyses are reported. It specifies that "Preregistration of an analysis plan must take place via a publicly accessible registry system", giving OSF, ClinicalTrials.gov, WHO Registry Network registries, and institutional systems as examples.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • The registration must have a permanent public path, and additional registrations or changes to the confirmatory analysis plan must be linked and explained.

Constraints¶

  • No badge will be awarded if the registration path is not provided, or if additional registrations or unreported registered analyses lack strong justification.

Target Learners¶

  • Researchers conducting experimental learning research

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Confirmatory analysis transparency

Claims¶

  • Offering Open Science Badges is associated with increased uptake of data and materials sharing and preregistration [+W]
  • In a four-wave MOOC intervention study, statistically significant first-wave findings failed to replicate in subsequent preregistered waves [~W]
