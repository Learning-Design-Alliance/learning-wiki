Strategies

Integrate educators and developers through regular cross-functional meetings so pedagogical intent survives technical implementation¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report's ReadWorks case study recommends that product managers, engineers, and educators meet regularly to transfer knowledge and surface changes immediately, since a research-based plan can end up "unrecognizable by the time it comes out the other end" without communication. Regular check-ins, even when nothing specific is pending, build trust and mutual understanding between pedagogical and technical staff.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Structured recurring meetings across pedagogy and engineering roles
  • Willingness to balance pedagogical fidelity with technical feasibility

Constraints¶

  • Teams where either side enters with a mindset of being the expert in the room can seed tension; the practice addresses an edtech-specific division of expertise

Target Learners¶

  • learners served by products developed by mixed pedagogical and technical teams

Target Learning Goals¶

  • literacy and other learning goals the product's research basis is meant to serve

Related Strategies¶

  • Plan and design backwards from a research-based learning goal to features, metrics, and measurement tools
  • Build cross-disciplinary collaboration between AIED, robotics and electrical engineering to develop educational cobots
