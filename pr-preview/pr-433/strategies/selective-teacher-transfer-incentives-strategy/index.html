Offer selective financial transfer incentives to staff hard-to-staff schools with high-performing teachers¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A district staffing strategy in which high-performing teachers are offered a large one-time payment — here $20,000 — to transfer into the district's hardest-to-staff schools. The article examines teachers' behavioral responses to such an offer across 10 school districts, framing the incentive as a tool for staffing low-performing schools.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A mechanism for identifying high-performing teachers eligible for the offer
  • Identification of the hardest-to-staff schools within the district
  • Sufficient funding for the $20,000 transfer payment

Constraints¶

  • Evidence in the article covers 10 school districts; behavioral responses were the studied outcome rather than guaranteed staffing gains

Target Learners¶

  • Students in hard-to-staff, low-performing schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Equitable access to high-performing teachers

Claims¶

  • The paper examines high-performing teachers' willingness to transfer and the transfer offer's effect on receiving schools' internal dynamics [~W]
