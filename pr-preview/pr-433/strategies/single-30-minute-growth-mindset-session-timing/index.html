Strategies

Deliver growth mindset messages once, near the start of college or around first exams, in roughly 30-minute sessions¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

An implementable delivery recipe drawn from the six studies meeting WWC standards: "Growth Mindset interventions in postsecondary settings typically occur once, either at the start of a student's first year of college enrollment or around the time that students complete their first or midterm course exams, and are administered in one or more sessions, each lasting about 30 minutes." Delivery is individual or in groups, via online modules or classroom settings. Comparison groups received alternative non-growth-mindset information with a parallel application prompt.
