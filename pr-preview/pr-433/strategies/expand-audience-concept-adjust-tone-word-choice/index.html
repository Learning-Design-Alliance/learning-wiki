Expand students' concept of audience by writing the same topic for different audiences¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The guide recommends designing writing activities that naturally lend themselves to different audiences, helping students generate a list of potential audiences for an assignment, and having students "practice writing about the same topic for different audiences, to develop the skill of adjusting tone and word choice to suit the audience." Examples include writing persuasive letters to parents, friends, or teachers, and writing a short story for a magazine or a fable for a preschool class. An example lists students, teachers, administrators, and parents as audiences for a persuasive request.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Activities that naturally invite multiple audiences
  • Audience lists generated with students, who choose the best-fitting audience
