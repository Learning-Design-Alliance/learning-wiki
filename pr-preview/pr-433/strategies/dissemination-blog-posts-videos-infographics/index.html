Disseminate partnership information through blog posts, videos, and infographics¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

As one of its outputs, the LEAD Partnership plans to "Develop dissemination resources (blog posts, videos, infographics) to share information about the partnership, intervention, and relevant research". This makes multi-format public communication an explicit component of the partnership's work rather than an afterthought.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Resources must be developed that share information about the partnership, the intervention, and relevant research

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • school leaders
  • preK and kindergarten teachers
  • education stakeholders

Target Learning Goals¶

  • awareness of research-based early learning practices and the LEAD intervention

Related Strategies¶

  • Disseminate partnership information through blog posts, videos, and infographics to support acceptability and feasibility
  • Develop dissemination resources to support acceptability and feasibility of the toolkit approach
  • Build organizational partnerships to disseminate educator development strategies
