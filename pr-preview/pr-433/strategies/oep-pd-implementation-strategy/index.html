Implement OEP in teacher education and professional development with formative milestones and adequate time¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that teacher educators implement OEP in teacher education or professional development programs to foster preliminary readiness and intention of using OER, since OEP as worked examples improved teachers' self-efficacy and perceived ease of use. It specifies that "including formative assessment as a milestone for each phase of the OEP experience may scaffold teachers to reflect on worked examples and receive formative feedback." It also recommends school leaders allocate sufficient time and that school districts participate in open education initiatives such as #GoOpen.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • An elaborated design assuring effective OEP experiences, with formative assessment milestones per phase
  • Sufficient allocated time for teachers to work on OEP activities

Constraints¶

  • Time constraints and teachers' lack of prior experience with OER hampered teachers' experience with OEP in this study

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 in-service teachers in professional development
  • Teacher educators and professional development specialists

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Readiness, self-efficacy, and intention to adopt and implement OER in K-12 teaching

Claims¶

  • Engaging K-12 teachers in open educational practices significantly improved their perceived ease of use and self-efficacy toward OER, with small-to-medium effect sizes [+W]
  • Qualitative findings indicate OEP cultivated authentic OER awareness, increased self-efficacy, and fostered positive attitudes among K-12 teachers [+W]
