Strategies

Include facilitated social learning opportunities (introduction and debriefing) around individual IVR safety scenarios¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A concrete design recipe drawn from the simulation training framework and the trainer findings: wrap the individually performed IVR scenario in trainer-facilitated group phases. In the introduction, the trainer presents key concepts, adjusts the training to the group, and prompts reflection on relevance to learners' experiences; in the debriefing, learners discuss and analyze the scenario together and share personal experiences of work hazards. As the authors conclude, "rather than relying solely on IVR's engaging features, it would be beneficial to consider including social learning opportunities in the IVR safety training design."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A trainer who facilitates the discussions, maintains a positive atmosphere, and guides reflection on the training experience in relation to the organization's safety practices

Constraints¶

  • Evaluating training viability or impact was not a prominent theme among the interviewed trainers, so outcome-evaluation support may need to be added

Target Learners¶

  • adult employees in work organizations
  • small learning groups in safety training

Target Learning Goals¶

  • factual and conceptual safety knowledge
  • safety motivation and self-efficacy
  • transfer of learning to work practice
