Consult online students directly during the needs analysis phase of orientation resource design¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article recommends that the student voice be heard during the needs analysis phase of orientation site design, rather than relying solely on staff expertise and literature reviews. The authors state: "we would recommend that the student voice be heard during a needs analysis of future orientation site design phases", noting that student needs may differ from those assumed by staff or reported in the literature.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Time and resources to gather student input early in the design process.

Constraints¶

  • In this project, student input was deferred to the final stage due to time constraints, which the authors identify as a limitation.

Target Learners¶

  • Online university students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Accurate identification of student support needs

Claims¶

  • Students are dissatisfied with college websites and want interactive, dynamic, accurate, and timely online information [+W]
