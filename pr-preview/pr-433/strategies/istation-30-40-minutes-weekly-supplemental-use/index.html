Implement Istation as a supplemental curriculum at the recommended 30–40 minutes of weekly use¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article specifies a dosage recommendation for the program: "Istation recommends 30–40 minutes of supplemental curriculum use per week." In the evaluated studies, students generally averaged about 15–16 hours of total Istation use per year, which is consistent with this supplemental dosage. The strategy positions Istation alongside, not in place of, core reading instruction.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Classroom access to desktops or tablets, since students can use Istation on desktops or tablets.

Constraints¶

  • The recommendation is for supplemental use; the article does not evaluate Istation as a core program.

Target Learners¶

  • Students from prekindergarten through Grade 8

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Supplemental reading skill development across phonemic awareness, comprehension, decoding, fluency, spelling, and vocabulary

Related Strategies¶

  • Use Istation's teacher reports to target small-group instruction and lessons for struggling readers
