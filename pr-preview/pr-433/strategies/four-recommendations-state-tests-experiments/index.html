Four recommendations for using state tests in education experiments: gauge alignment, ensure reliability, collect baseline data, and consider combining results carefully¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The paper's concluding chapter distills its guidance into four numbered recommendations for researchers considering state test data in RCTs. First, gauge the alignment of specific assessments with the outcome objectives of, and research questions about, the intervention of interest. Second, ensure the assessment is reliable and appropriate for the study target population. Third, whenever possible, collect and use baseline measures. Fourth, carefully consider whether and how to combine results based on distinct assessments, given variation in content and proficiency standards across grades and states.

Requirements¶

Constraints¶

  • Combining results across distinct assessments requires assumptions about rescaling that study reports typically do not discuss or verify
