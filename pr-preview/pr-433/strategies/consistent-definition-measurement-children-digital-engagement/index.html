Adopt more consistent approaches to defining and measuring children's digital engagement in educational research¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The review's central recommendation is that researchers studying children's engagement with digital technology in educational spaces should define and measure engagement more consistently and explicitly. The review states: "This review highlights the need for more consistent approaches in defining and measuring children's digital engagement." Given that most included studies lacked explicit definitions, theories or measures, researchers should state their engagement conceptualisation and select measures aligned to it.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Researchers should explicitly define engagement and link it to a theory or framework when studying children's digital engagement

Target Learners¶

  • children aged 5-8 in primary school
