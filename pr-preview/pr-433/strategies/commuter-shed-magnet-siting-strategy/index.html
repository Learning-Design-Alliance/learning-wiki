Use job center and commuter shed analysis to site and design magnet schools¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report recommends siting magnets near job centers so parents can transport children during commutes, increasing demand for open-enrollment seats. It states: "Job centers and commuter sheds are important concepts in the design and creation of magnet schools because they show the geographic areas in which centrally located schools are most likely to be accessible to the largest number of students." Commuter shed analysis also supports modeling expected school demographics, informing themes and curricula, and coordinating school, housing, and transit planning.
