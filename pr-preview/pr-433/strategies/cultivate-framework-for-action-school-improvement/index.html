Use Cultivate data as a framework for action to identify strengths and improvement areas in learning environments¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article positions Cultivate as "more than just a measurement tool; it is a framework for action." Schools use the valid, reliable student-experience data to "identify areas of strength and areas for continuous improvement in the learning environment," amplifying existing work and providing "a structured, evidence-based resource for educators and students to realize their aspirations for rigorous, relationship-centered, and supportive classroom and school environments."

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Valid and reliable survey information on student experiences, as established by the revalidation analyses

Target Learners¶

  • Students in grades 5-12 in Chicago Public Schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Improving classroom learning conditions
  • Fostering relationship-centered, supportive, and engaging learning environments

Related Strategies¶

  • Use diagnostic survey results to craft an evidence-based school narrative and action plan
  • Use annual 5Essentials Survey data to identify school-environment strengths and areas to improve for bullying prevention
  • Use 5Essentials survey data to identify where to improve students' school experiences and teachers' relationships with parents to support attendance
