Direct future pedagogical agent research toward assessment, reflective practice, administration, personalisation and media richness¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Based on the survey's identified gaps, the article recommends future research explore "the use of conversational agents for enhancing assessment, reflective practice and to support more effective administration and management practice". On the technological side, it proposes that "future research would benefit from focusing on enhancing the level of personalisation and media richness of interaction" achievable by AI conversational agents. These directions follow directly from the underexplored categories and technological limitations identified in the 92-study survey.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Future work should target the pedagogical gaps (assessment, reflective practice, administration) and technological gaps (personalisation, media richness) the survey identified

Constraints¶

  • Recommendations are grounded in a literature sample limited to 2014–2022 higher education studies within the review's predefined search criteria

  • higher education students

  • assessment feedback, reflective practice, administrative efficiency, personalised interaction

Claims¶

  • Reviewed literature identifies personalisation, adaptive support, rapid feedback, and teacher-facing automation as recurring pedagogical opportunities of AI in mathematics education [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Explore hybrid assessment models combining AI conversational agent-based and face-to-face formats, with long-term study of effects on performance and well-being
  • Prioritise longitudinal validation, pedagogical grounding, governed adoption of agent infrastructures, and ethics with human oversight in educational agentic AI research
