Strategies

Prioritise longitudinal validation, pedagogical grounding, governed adoption of agent infrastructures, and ethics with human oversight in educational agentic AI research¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 theoretical), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Drawing on the converging gaps across the reviewed literature, the review recommends that future work pursue "longitudinal and real-world validation, stronger pedagogical grounding, more governed adoption of emerging agent infrastructures, and more systematic integration of ethics and human oversight". This agenda is technically informed but educationally appropriate, addressing the mismatch between rapid frontier agent development and limited educational evidence.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Research designs must move beyond small-scale, short-term empirical evaluations toward real-world and longitudinal studies.

Constraints¶

  • The review notes educational adoption is mediated by pedagogy, learner development, assessment, institutional infrastructure, ethics, and classroom ecology.

Target Learners¶

  • Researchers, developers, and educators working with educational agentic AI

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Responsible, educationally meaningful development and deployment of agentic AI systems

Claims¶

  • Many agentic multi-agent implementations remain at prototype or conceptual stage with limited large-scale deployment and empirical validation [+W]
  • CAI-in-education research shows a framework gap: no end-to-end design guidance, weak CAI-specific usability methods, unclear pedagogical guidance, and limited AI literacy support [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Deploy agentic AI in education with transparency about agentic decisions, human oversight and institutional responsibility
  • Adopt a roadmap for ethical CAI implementation in education
  • Prioritize inclusive-by-design architectures, interoperable agent frameworks, human-AI collaboration models, and real-world implementation studies
  • Establish comprehensive institutional AI governance frameworks including task forces, roadmaps, and transparent policies
  • Collaborative cross-cultural research agenda for validating AI-enabled pedagogical accompaniment
  • Pursue future research on ChatGPT in higher education to fill the gaps the scoping review identifies
  • Integrate autonomy and agency paradigms, standardise instruments, probe non-demographic moderators, and extend evaluations to longitudinal real-world settings
  • Prioritize construct-to-data mappings, multimodal validation, ethical implementation, and cross-disciplinary collaboration for domain-specific adaptive systems
  • Prioritize longer-term longitudinal studies and analysis of learners' virtual-world behaviors for SLA research
  • Direct future pedagogical agent research toward assessment, reflective practice, administration, personalisation and media richness
