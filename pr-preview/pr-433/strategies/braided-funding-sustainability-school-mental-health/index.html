Sustain district mental health initiatives by braiding grant funding into annual plans, ongoing surveys, and reimbursement streams¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief's sustainability approach embeds mental health in existing district structures so work continues after the grant ends: schools included mental health in their annual School Plans for Student Achievement, a federal Mental Health and Training Grant will continue the annual student wellness surveys initiated by the evaluation, and the district "plans to expand Medi-Cal reimbursement for behavioral health services" through a statewide school-linked fee schedule and provider network.
