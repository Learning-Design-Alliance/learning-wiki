Foster a zest for learning by combining and developing pupils' drives¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

This strategy translates the mechanism-drive analysis into a forward-looking educational aim: rather than relying on native endowment, schools should actively develop and combine pupils' motivational drives. The article writes that "the possibility of combining and developing 'drives' in pupils suggests the results that might be attained by fostering a zest for learning." It is presented as a possibility suggested by the framework, not a tested procedure.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Knowledge of individual pupils' drives, gained through study of their likes, dislikes, desires, and aspirations

Constraints¶

  • Part of pupil attitude depends on native endowment and is little subject to modification, per the article

Target Learners¶

  • school pupils

Target Learning Goals¶

  • positive attitude toward studies and effortful school learning

Affordances¶

  • Mechanism Drive Dynamics Of Motivation

Claims¶

  • Baerwald's study of motor imagery finds no relation between development of musical performance skill and degree of native motor endowment [+W]
  • Musical comprehension that improves on second hearing was attributed to developing motor attitudes, with negative findings on native motor endowment [+W]
