Apply evidence-based approaches from self-regulation, multimedia learning, motivation, and collaborative learning theories in distance and online course design¶

Description¶

The chapter recommends that designers of the next generation of distance and online learning draw on "principles and applications stemming from the theories of self-regulation and multimedia learning, research-based motivational principles, and collaborative learning principles". These evidence-based approaches are offered as ways to foster instructional improvement grounded in the interaction findings.

Claims¶

  • A meta-analysis quantitatively verified the importance of three types of interaction in distance and online learning: among students, between instructor and students, and between students and content [+W]
  • Staying motivated was the most pervasive challenge of learning remotely, and students missed instructor feedback, peer collaboration, and hands-on experiences [+W]
