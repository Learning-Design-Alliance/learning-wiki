Strategies

Deploy automated reflection analytics for immediate, scalable feedback on reflective writing¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article recommends machine-learning-based analysis of reflective writing as a way to remove the constraints of manual coding: "These findings enable reflection analytics that is immediate and scalable." Beyond saving time, the article argues automated aids may suit reflective writing's sensitive nature, since "Students may also feel more comfortable with an automated reflective writing aid to self-disclose those private thoughts relevant for their reflection", citing research that self-disclosure is easier by computer.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Machine learning models trained on reliably annotated sentences, with reliability benchmarked against the intended stakes of the context (stricter for high-stakes use)

Constraints¶

  • Machine accuracy averaged 10% below manual accuracy, and low-stakes rather than high-stakes contexts are indicated by the reliability thresholds discussed

Target Learners¶

  • higher education students writing reflective journals or essays

Target Learning Goals¶

  • reflective thinking
  • improving reflective writing through feedback

Affordances¶

  • Model For Reflection Detection Eight Categories

Claims¶

  • Manual content analysis of reflective writing is time-consuming, constraining pedagogical use and large-scale research [+W]
  • Automated analysis accuracies were on average 10% lower than the accuracies of manual analysis [~W]
  • Machine learning detects five of eight reflective-writing categories with substantial or almost perfect reliability and three with moderate reliability (Cohen's κ .53–.85) [+W]
  • The eight-category model of reflective writing can be reliably annotated and showed empirical validity in this context [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Use automatic reflection classification for preliminary feedback and workload support, with final assessment retained by educators
  • Automate self-affirmation essay coding with a fine-tuned model while double-coding a random sample for reliability
