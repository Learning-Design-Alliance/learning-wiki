Convert earned micro-credentials into continuing education units for re-licensure and master's equivalency¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report's first incentive strategy is implementing systems that allow earned micro-credentials to convert into a specific number of CEUs usable for re-licensure or master's equivalency petitions. Baltimore County Public Schools converts every two approved micro-credentials into one Maryland CPD credit, and North Carolina DPI lets teachers include micro-credentials earned through MOOC-Eds in license renewal. This connects micro-credentials to high-stakes professional standing such as salary advancement and licensure renewal.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • District or state systems must define a conversion rate between micro-credentials and CEUs/credits and accept them for re-licensure or master's equivalency

Constraints¶

  • Nomenclature and unit systems vary by district and state (continuing education units, credits, contact hours, points)

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 educators seeking re-licensure, salary advancement, or leadership opportunities

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Formal recognition of competency-based professional learning toward licensure and advancement

Related Strategies¶

  • Link earned micro-credentials to permanent base salary increases
  • Count approved micro-credentials toward professional growth plan contact units for salary advancement
