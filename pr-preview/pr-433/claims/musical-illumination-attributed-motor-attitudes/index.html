The editorial recounts Baerwald's finding that music clearing up on second hearing occurred "in conjunction with a well-defined motor disposition", an attributional interpretation rather than a tested comparison; no effect size is printed.

"In all cases studied, this musical illumination was found in conjunction with a well-defined motor disposition, and the phenomenon was attributed to the development of motor attitudes which reinforce the satisfaction produced by the musical ideas."
