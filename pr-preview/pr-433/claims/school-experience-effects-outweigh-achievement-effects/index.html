The agenda reports, citing its own prior Consortium research (endnote 3, Jackson et al. 2024), that "school and teacher effects on students' school experiences" influence trajectories "even more so than school and teacher impacts on measures of academic achievement." No effect size is printed in this document.

"Consortium research has shown that school and teacher effects on students' school experiences, as measured on surveys, influence students' social and educational trajectories from early childhood through adulthood even more so than school and teacher impacts on measures of academic achievement."
