What Works Clearinghouse 2021¶

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2021). Bottom Line. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/714

q4 · i1 · causal · r3

Findings from the one study meeting WWC standards, a student-level RCT with low attrition. The intervention group enrolled at 80.60 percent versus 70.30 percent for the comparison group, a mean difference of 10.30, with a printed effect size of 0.34, improvement index +13, and p < .01.
