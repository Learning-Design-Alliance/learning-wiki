Qian 2026¶

Qian, Y. (2026). Governing generative AI in higher education: emerging policy approaches and support ecosystems at innovative U.S. Universities. International Journal for Educational Integrity. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40979-026-00233-x

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Document analysis mapping institutional support resources across the 50-institution corpus (RQ2 findings, Fig. 3). The article reports that "support resources are clustered into four interlocking units," with teaching centers as the translation layer, libraries as infrastructure for scholarly provenance, IT/security as the risk layer, and integrity offices as the due-process layer.
