Marrone 2024¶

Marrone, Lam, Barthakur, Brinkman, Dawson, & Gabriel. (2024). The Relationship between Well-being and Academic Achievement: A Comprehensive Cross-Sectional Analysis of System Wide Data from 2016–2019. Journal of Learning Analytics, 11(3). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2024.8357

The hierarchical mixed-model analysis of the linked WEC–academic dataset found the emotional well-being index (life satisfaction, optimism, sadness, worries, happiness, emotion regulation) had the weakest coefficients of the three indexes, "in the hundredth of a standard deviation range" (e.g., adjusted NAPLAN numeracy 0.042).
