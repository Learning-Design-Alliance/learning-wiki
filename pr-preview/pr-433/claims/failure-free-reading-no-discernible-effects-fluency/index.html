The WWC summary of the Torgesen et al. (2006) randomized controlled trial reports the single fluency outcome (Oral Reading Fluency, words correct per minute) with an effect size of 0.05, not statistically significant, and an improvement index of +2 percentile points for the 93 third-grade students.

"Torgesen et al. (2006) examined one outcome in this domain (the Oral Reading Fluency test) and reported no statistically significant effect for this outcome. The effect size not large enough to be considered substantively important."
