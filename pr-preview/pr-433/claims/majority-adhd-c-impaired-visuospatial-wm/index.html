Evidence¶

S. Dovis 2015¶

S. Dovis, S. van der Oord, H.M. Huizenga, R.W. Wiers, P.J.M. Prins. (2015). Prevalence and diagnostic validity of motivational impairments and deficits in visuospatial short-term memory and working memory in ADHD subtypes. European Child & Adolescent Psychiatry, 24(5), 575-590. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00787-014-0612-1

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Prevalence analysis across 86 children with ADHD-C assessed on visuospatial WM tasks under standard and high-reinforcement conditions. The abstract concludes "The majority of children with ADHD-C is impaired on visuospatial WM."

Related Claims¶

  • Visuospatial working memory and short-term memory impairments are most prevalent in children with ADHD-C relative to ADHD-I and typically developing controls — related

  • Memory and motivation represent independent neuropsychological domains in children with ADHD — related
  • Children with ADHD-C show impaired visuospatial STM and CE relative to typically developing controls, whereas children with ADHD-I show impaired CE but intact STM — related
