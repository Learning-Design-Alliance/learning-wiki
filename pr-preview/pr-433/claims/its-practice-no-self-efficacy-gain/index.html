Xinfei Cen, Vincent Aleven, Kenneth R. Koedinger, Conrad Borchers, and Paulo F. Carvalho. (2026). Self-Efficacy and Favorability Shape Learning from Tutoring Systems and Paper Practice. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17470 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.17470

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Linear mixed-effects model with post-training self-efficacy as dependent variable (Table 2), same 54-student within-subject dataset. Neither condition nor favorability was significant; no interaction reached significance. No effect size printed.

"In contrast, training conditions (tutor vs. paper),t(57.96) = 0.49,p=.629, and favorability,t(90.00) = 0.96,p=.341, did not independently predict changes in self-efficacy."
