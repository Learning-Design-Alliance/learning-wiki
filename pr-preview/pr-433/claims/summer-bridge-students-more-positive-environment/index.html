Based on student surveys collected during both the school year and summer 1999 plus qualitative interviews, students portrayed summer classrooms as places where they were expected to work hard and where teachers were supportive.

"students who attended Summer Bridge were significantly more positive in the summer than in the school year about the academic environments of their classrooms and the attention they received from teachers"
