Observational analysis of adjacent-year school averages on Insight survey domains compared leader-stable years with leader-change years; averages were "more similar for adjacent years in which a school had the same leader." No effect size is printed.

"School averages of (Insight) survey domains are more similar for adjacent years in which a school had the same leader than when the school leader changed, suggesting principals have effects on these measures."
