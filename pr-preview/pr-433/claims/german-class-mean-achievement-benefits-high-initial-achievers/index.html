q3 · i? · associational · r2

The abstract reports this cross-level interaction from multilevel models on the German sample: "students with higher initial achievement in German benefited more from higher class mean levels." The Educational Impact statement adds that being in a class with higher achieving classmates benefits higher achieving students in German but not in mathematics. No effect size is printed in the available text.
