Cosette Crisan 2025¶

Cosette Crisan, Eirini Geraniou. (2025). Designing for actively engaging with mathematics education research in mentoring practice: a Vignette Activity Sequence approach. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10538-2

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Qualitative analysis of anonymised asynchronous forum posts from the September 2022 course run, organised by participant and discussion thread. The authors report that "the VAS steps encouraged explicit engagement with research". No effect sizes are reported.

"Data analysis revealed that the VAS steps encouraged explicit engagement with research, reflection on teaching practices, and the application of research-informed strategies."
