Chait 2022¶

Chait, R. (2022). Balancing district impacts and community interests: Considerations for district charter authorizers. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED620722

q2 · i? · review · r2

The authors' own review of all state charter statutes and relevant regulations identified the district impact assessments states require. The review found "16 states with district impact provisions in statute or regulation" organized into four primary categories of impact.

"For context, we reviewed all state charter statutes and relevant regulations to better understand the types of district impact assessments that states require. Of 16 states with district impact provisions in statute or regulation, we found that states had four primary categories of impact: fiscal, demographic, programmatic, and general."
