Frequency analysis of feedback components across the 39 papers, reported in Results Tables 8-11. The quote gives real-time feedback at 84.62%, post-session at 28.21%, and immediate at 10.26%; Tables 9 and 10 report dynamic feedback at 69.23% and discrete feedback at 35.9% versus continuous at 33.33%.

"Table 11 shows that 84.62% of papers provided real-time (online) feedback, while post-session (delayed offline) and immediate (offline) feedback occurred in 28.21% and 10.26% of papers, respectively."
