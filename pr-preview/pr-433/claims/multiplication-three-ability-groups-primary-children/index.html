S.H.G. van der Ven 2015¶

S.H.G. van der Ven, M. Straatemeier, B.R.J. Jansen, S. Klinkenberg, H.L.J. van der Maas. (2015). Learning multiplication: An integrated analysis of the multiplication ability of primary school children and the difficulty of single digit and multidigit multiplication problems. Learning and Individual Differences. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lindif.2015.08.013

Analysis of primary school children's mental multiplication ability in the study's child analyses identified three ability groups, described as "single digit problem solvers, multidigit problem solvers, and high performers." No effect size is printed for this grouping.
