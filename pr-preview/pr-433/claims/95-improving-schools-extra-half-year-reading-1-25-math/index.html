Evidence¶

Sebring 2006¶

Sebring, P. B., Allensworth, E., Bryk, A. S., Easton, J. Q., & Luppescu, S. (2006). The Essential Supports for School Improvement. Research Report, Consortium on Chicago School Research at the University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/essential-supports-school-improvement

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Citywide analysis of Chicago elementary schools' ITBS-based academic productivity trends, 1990-1996. The report identified 95 substantially improving schools and estimated accumulated effects over eight grades from base learning gains of 0.87 (reading) and 0.82 (math) grade equivalents.

"We identiﬁed 95 schools that showed substantial improvement in academic productivity in reading and mathematics."
