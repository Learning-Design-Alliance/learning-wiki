(2025). “So no one feels left out:” Latinx youths’ and parents’ re-imagined school realities in the context of cultural stressors. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000956

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Theme 2 subtheme on resources and supportive infrastructure for Latinx youth and parents. Students raised these needs when reflecting on cultural stressors related to family financial circumstances and long commute distances to school.

"Participants also expressed a desire to have programs at school that would teach them about "everyday life" such as how to do their taxes, build up credit, and get a title loan."
