Evidence¶

Silvia Bonacina 2021¶

Silvia Bonacina, Stephanie Huang, Travis White-Schwoch, Jennifer Krizman, Trent Nicol and Nina Kraus. (2021). Rhythm, reading, and sound processing in the brain in preschool children. npj Science of Learning 6:20. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00097-5

q3 · i3 · associational · r2

One-way ANOVAs comparing synchronization groups on preliteracy measures, with sex as covariate; phonological awareness administered to a subset of 108 of 156 participants. The article reports "phonological awareness ( F(1,55) = 11.680, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.175)" and auditory short-term memory F(1,91) = 4.773, p = 0.031.

"“Synchronizers” scored higher than “Non-synchronizers” on all of the preliteracy skills that were evaluated. The preliteracy skills included phonological awareness ( F(1,55) = 11.680, p < 0.001, ηp2 = 0.175), auditory short-term memory ( F(1,91) = 4.773, p = 0.031, ηp2 = 0.050), and rapid automatized naming"
