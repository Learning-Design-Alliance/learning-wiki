Evidence¶

Kochmar 2022¶

Kochmar, E., Vu, D. D., Belfer, R., Gupta, V., Serban, I. V., & Pineau, J. (2022). Automated Data-Driven Generation of Personalized Pedagogical Interventions in Intelligent Tutoring Systems. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 32, 323–349. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-021-00267-x

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Remote pilot study on the Korbit platform (January–February 2020) with 796 annotated interactions from 183 students. Expert annotation agreed with the automated solution verification module in 80.53% of cases and served as gold standard. The article reports statistical significance with only a z-test p-value and no effect size: "statistically significant at 95% confidence level based on a z-test (p = 0.03)".

"the difference between the success rate for the DISCOURSE PERSONALIZATION MODEL and BASELINE MODEL for the students before their second attempt is statistically significant at 95% confidence level based on a z-test (p = 0.03)."
