Benedict du Boulay & Rosemary Luckin 2015¶

Benedict du Boulay & Rosemary Luckin. (2015). Modelling Human Teaching Tactics and Strategies for Tutoring Systems: 14 Years On. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0053-0

q3 · i? · review · r2

The review reports the Olney et al. (2012) evaluation of GURU, a conversational Biology tutor built on the expert-teacher coding plus the INSPIRE framework, comparing three teaching conditions; the two augmented conditions produced similar gains, both better than classroom teaching alone.

"An evaluation of GURU compared standard classroom teaching vs classroom teaching augmented by small group sessions with a human (non-expert) teacher vs classroom teaching augmented by sessions with GURU. They researchers found that the two augmented modes of teaching produced similar learning gains, with both being better than classroom teaching alone (Olney et al. 2012)."
