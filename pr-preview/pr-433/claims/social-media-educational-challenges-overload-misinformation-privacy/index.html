Evidence¶

Adrie A. Koehler 2023¶

Adrie A. Koehler, Daniela Rezende Vilarinho-Pereira. (2023). Using social media affordances to support Ill-structured problem-solving skills: considering possibilities and challenges. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10060-1

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative synthesis section on challenges with integrating social media for educational purposes. The review reports that the intense flow of updates "can be overwhelming for learners, leading to cognitive overload," which is especially an issue when using social media to solve complex problems; it also cites misinformation circulation and credibility difficulties from middle school to college.

"This intense flow of messages, photos, videos, and links can be overwhelming for learners, leading to cognitive overload"

Adrie A. Koehler 2023 (2)¶

Adrie A. Koehler, Daniela Rezende Vilarinho-Pereira. (2023). Using social media affordances to support Ill-structured problem-solving skills: considering possibilities and challenges. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10060-1

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review attributes this finding to prior studies it cites (Demirbilek & Talan, 2018; Karpinski et al., 2013; van der Schuur et al., 2015), reporting that multitasking with social media increases cognitive load and harms learning processes.

"Multitasking behavior is detrimental to students' learning processes (Demirbilek & Talan, 2018; Karpinski et al., 2013; van der Schuur et al., 2015), as they attempt to engage with course and non-course-related activities simultaneously and increase their cognitive load"
