Evidence¶

Knellesen 2024¶

Knellesen, J., Händel, M., & Golke, S. (2024). Effects of reading instructions on pre-service teachers' judgment bias when learning from texts. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09371-w

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Testing phase of Experiment 1: univariate ANOVA on reading time (seconds per word) showed a significant effect of instruction, F(2, 127) = 13.63, p < .001, η² = 0.18; the planned contrast showed "students in the application instruction group had significantly longer reading times".

"Planned contrast showed that students in the application instruction group had significantly longer reading times than students in the other two groups, t(127) = 4.99, p < .001, d = 0.93."

Knellesen 2024 (2)¶

Knellesen, J., Händel, M., & Golke, S. (2024). Effects of reading instructions on pre-service teachers' judgment bias when learning from texts. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09371-w

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Experiment 2: ANOVA on reading time showed a significant effect of instruction, F(2, 161) = 9.59, p < .001, η² = 0.11; the planned contrast again showed "students in the application instruction group had significantly longer reading times" than the other two groups.

"Planned contrast showed that students in the application instruction group had significantly longer reading times than students in the other two groups, t(161) = 4.36, p < .001, d = 0.75."
