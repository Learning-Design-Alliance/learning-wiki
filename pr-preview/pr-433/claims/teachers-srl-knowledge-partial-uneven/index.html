q2 · i? · review · r?

The review's overall synthesis across 33 heterogeneous studies, many using non-standardized assessment approaches, which the authors state constrained the synthesis. This is a narrative synthesis judgment, not a pooled estimate; no effect sizes are printed.

"Overall, the evidence suggests that teachers have partial, unevenly developed knowledge across all three dimensions, with limited and inconsistent associations between knowledge and other aspects of professional competence or instructional practice."
