Per-class results from the confusion-matrix analysis of the 192-text evaluation (Table 1) show Turnitin Hybrid sensitivity 0.31 and Originality Hybrid sensitivity 0.02; the article states "Performance on Hybrid texts was weak across all metrics". Hybrid texts were constructed with approximately 50% human and 50% AI content.
