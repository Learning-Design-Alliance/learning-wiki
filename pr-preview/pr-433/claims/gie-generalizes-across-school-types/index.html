Evidence¶

Arens 2016¶

Arens, A.K., & Möller, J. (2016). Dimensional comparisons in students' perceptions of the learning environment. Learning and Instruction, 42, 22–30. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2015.11.001

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Multi-group invariance testing (Models 5–8) compared sixth-graders in elementary school (N = 3169) with same-aged students in high-ability secondary schools (N = 1757). The article reports that constraining paths "did not lead to a change in the CFI value," supporting generalizability of the GI/E pattern across learning environments.

"Constraining the paths to equal size in both groups (Model 8) did not lead to a change in the CFI value indicating that the path model is generalizable across the two groups of students."
