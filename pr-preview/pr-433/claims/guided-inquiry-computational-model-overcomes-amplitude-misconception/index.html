Evidence¶

Farr 2012¶

Farr, Schelbert, & Trouille. (2012). Gravitational-wave science in the high school classroom. https://arxiv.org/abs/1109.3720 doi:10.48550/arxiv.1109.3720

q2 · i? · design · r2

Classroom implementation across 8 mixed junior/senior, honors and non-honors classes of about 25 students. The authors report that "over 80% of students were able to predict and explain" the increasing frequency after the demonstration and guided inquiry worksheet; no statistical test is reported.

"We found that through the demonstration and the guided inquiry worksheet, over 80% of students were able to predict and explain why the frequency of the gravitational wave will increase with time as the binary system inspira ls."
