Evidence¶

Alina Deriyeva 2025¶

Alina Deriyeva, Jesper Dannath, and Benjamin Paaßen. (2025). SCRIPT: Implementing an Intelligent Tutoring System for Programming in a German University Context. Artificial Intelligence in Education (Springer). https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-99261-2_16

q1 · i? · design · r?

Descriptive statement (type d) about the system's research function: it enables investigating which knowledge tracing models best represent user performance, which task/intervention suggestion models support skill acquisition, and what hints to give in which situations. The article notes it has already supported data collection for learner model and feedback research (Dannath et al. 2024, 2025; Deriyeva et al. 2024).
