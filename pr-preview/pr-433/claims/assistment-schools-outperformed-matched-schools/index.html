Shute 2013¶

Shute, V. J., & Kim, Y. J. (2013). Formative and stealth assessment. In J.M. Spector et al. (eds.), Handbook of Research on Educational Communications and Technology. Springer. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4614-3185-5_25

q3 · i? · review · r2

The chapter describes ASSISTment as "a Web-based platform that allows teachers to develop formative assessments for fourth- to tenth-grade mathematics classes" and reports the cited quasi-experimental evaluation: "schools using ASSISTment signiﬁcantly outperformed matched schools on the state mathematics test." No effect size is printed.
