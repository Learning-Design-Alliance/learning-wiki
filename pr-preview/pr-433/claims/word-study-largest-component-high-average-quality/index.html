Evidence¶

Swanson 2010¶

Swanson, E. A., & Vaughn, S. (2010). An Observation Study of Reading Instruction Provided to Elementary Students With Learning Disabilities in the Resource Room. Psychology in the Schools, 47(5). https://doi.org/10.1002/pits.20484

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Observational coding of 2,178 minutes across 10 classrooms using the ICE-R. The results report "Phonics/word study instruction encompassed 696 minutes, or 31.96% of the total observed reading instruction", average quality 3.05, 25.2% rated weak, and 66% of word-study time with high student engagement.
