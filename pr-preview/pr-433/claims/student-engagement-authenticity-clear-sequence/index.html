Suleiman 2026¶

Synthesis of post-project student focus group feedback in the Student Feedback section of the deployment evaluation. The section also reports students valued projects that "felt real, practical, and connected to authentic cybersecurity or software tasks". Qualitative synthesis; no effect sizes reported.

"Students responded more positively when projects were organized in a clear sequence, with manageable milestones and tasks that clearly built toward the final project."
