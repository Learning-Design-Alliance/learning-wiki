Gerry Stahl 2011¶

Gerry Stahl. (2011). Collaborating around the tabletop. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning, 6:485–490. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-011-9135-7

q2 · i? · causal · r?

The editorial reports on the Szewkis, Nussbaum and colleagues study in Chile, where a whole classroom of students shared a projected computer display using multiple mice and instituted 'silent collaboration' exchanges of computer icons between dyads. No effect size is printed.

"The experimental results indicate significant collaborative-learning gains with this approach."
