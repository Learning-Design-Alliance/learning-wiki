Evidence¶

Markus Huff 2025¶

Markus Huff, Natalia Gagarina, Ekaterina Varkentin, Irina R. Brich. (2025). Education, Not Age, Linked to Narrative Comprehension. https://aspredicted.org/WT9_TK6

q3 · i1 · causal · r3

In the preregistered representative-sample experiment (N = 1487), the codality fixed effect favored pictures over text; the Bayesian model printed an odds ratio of 0.57 (95% CI 0.37–0.88) for text versus picture. Quote: "narrative comprehension was higher in the picture than in the text codality, χ2(1) = 5.97, p = .015."

"Contrary to H3, narrative comprehension was higher in the picture than in the text codality, χ2(1) = 5.97, p = .015."

Markus Huff 2025 (2)¶

Markus Huff, Natalia Gagarina, Ekaterina Varkentin, Irina R. Brich. (2025). Education, Not Age, Linked to Narrative Comprehension. https://aspredicted.org/WT9_TK6

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Convenience-sample material test (final N = 239, ages 18–69) validating the bridging inference paradigm; mean comprehension was M = .88 (SD = 0.33) and "lower comprehension in the text than in the picture condition, t(238) = -4.57, p < .001." No effect size was printed; the authors note a possible ceiling effect in the picture condition.

"The mean narrative comprehension score was high (M = .88, SD = 0.33; Figure 2), with lower comprehension in the text than in the picture condition, t(238) = -4.57, p < .001."
