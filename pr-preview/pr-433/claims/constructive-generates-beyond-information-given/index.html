Evidence¶

Michelene T. H. Chi & Ruth Wylie 2014¶

Michelene T. H. Chi & Ruth Wylie. (2014). The ICAP Framework: Linking Cognitive Engagement to Active Learning Outcomes. Educational Psychologist, 49(4), 219-243. https://doi.org/10.1080/00461520.2014.965823

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The article's operational definition of the constructive mode. The authors illustrate that if a student's self-explanation is verbatim to what was read, the student is only self-explaining actively rather than constructively because no new information is provided.

"To meet the criteria for constructive, the outputs of generative behaviors should contain new ideas that go beyond the information given; otherwise such behaviors are merely active/manipulative."
