A design-based research case study of the COMP272 course revision, in its completed first analysis & exploration stage. The authors report the barriers were confirmed and the strategies adopted; effectiveness evaluation is planned but not yet conducted.

"So far, the learning barriers identified in SPOL are confirme d by this course case, the course design strategies have been adopted for revision, and the LA applications proposed by this study have been put on the implementation list."
