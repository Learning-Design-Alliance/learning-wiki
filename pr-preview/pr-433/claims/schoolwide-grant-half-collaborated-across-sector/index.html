Scott Richman 2016¶

Scott Richman, Alyson Burnett, Erin Dillon, Lisbeth Goble, Moira McCullough, Christina Clark Tuttle. (2016). District-Charter Collaboration Grant Implementation: Findings for Teacher and Principal Surveys. Washington, DC: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/districtcharter-collaboration-grant-implementation-findings-for-teacher-and-principal-surveys

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Survey of district and charter educators sampled from 21 cross-sector collaboration activities across seven grantee cities in 2014-2015. The brief reports that "about half of respondents in the schools collaborated with educators from the opposite sector" in schoolwide-activity cities. No effect size or test statistic is printed.

"Across the cities with schoolwide grant activities, about half of respondents in the schools collaborated with educators from the opposite sector."
