Evidence¶

Nilsen 2016¶

Nilsen, A. P. (2016). Navigating windows into past human minds: A framework of shifting selves in historical perspective taking. Journal of the Learning Sciences, 25, 372-410. https://doi.org/10.1080/10508406.2016.1160830

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Authors' interpretation (type e) drawn from the think-aloud corpus, applying Csikszentmihalyi and Hermanson's view of learning as self-expansion. The article states "It is in these shifts that we can locate moments of learning," describing self-development through perspectives that are "abstract and fleeting."

"It is in these shifts that we can locate moments of learning in Csikszentmihalyi and Hermanson’s (1995) terms: developing and expanding the self, exploring uncertainties and new possibilities through selves that may be abstract and fleeting."
