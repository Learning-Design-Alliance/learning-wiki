Evidence¶

Eichmann 2019¶

Eichmann, B., Goldhammer, F., Greiff, S., Pucite, L., & Naumann, J. (2019). The role of planning in complex problem solving. Computers & Education, 128, 1-12. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.08.004

q3 · i? · associational · r2

GLMM analysis of log data from N = 1346 German students on 21 interactive PISA 2012 CPS tasks found a significant negative fixed effect for the delay indicator (estimate = -0.13, p = 0.005), "indicating that in general it is bene ﬁcial to plan early during the process."

"Research question 2: For the delay indicator, a signi ﬁcant ﬁxed eﬀect and a signi ﬁcant random e ﬀect was found. The ﬁxed eﬀect of −0.13 indicates that in general it is bene ﬁcial to plan early during the process."

Task-dependent random effects in the same GLMM analysis showed the delay effect varied across tasks, with a correlation of r = -0.51 between task easiness and the random effect, "near zero for diﬃcult tasks and negative for easy tasks."

"The correlation between task easiness and the random e ﬀect of the indicator was r = −0.51. The random e ﬀect shows that the e ﬀect of the delay indicator was near zero for diﬃcult tasks and negative for easy tasks."
