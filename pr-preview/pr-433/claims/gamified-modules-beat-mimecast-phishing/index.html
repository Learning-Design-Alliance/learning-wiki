Evidence¶

Bingjun Li 2025¶

Bingjun Li, Christopher Buzaid, Weihao Qu. (2025). Security Education in Higher Education through AI-Powered Gamification. Journal of Cybersecurity, Digital Forensics and Jurisprudence. https://doi.org/10.65879/3070-5789.2025.01.07

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Comparative evaluation from the authors' preliminary work with 51 college participants randomly divided into gamified (18), Mimecast (18), and no-training (15) groups, followed by a 25 multiple-choice phishing quiz. The article reports the gamified group's advantage over Mimecast; the printed means appear in Table I and no effect size or significance test is reported.

"The result in Table I show that interactive (gamified) learning modules, even though not mobile friendly, has better learning effect on phishing detection topic over traditional platform such as Mimecast to some degree."
