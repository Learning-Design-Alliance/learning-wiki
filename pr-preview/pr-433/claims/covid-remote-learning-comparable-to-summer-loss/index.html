Evidence¶

Hammerstein Svenja 2021¶

Hammerstein Svenja, König Christoph, Dreisörner Thomas, Frey Andreas. (2021). Effects of COVID-19-Related School Closures on Student Achievement-A Systematic Review. Frontiers in Psychology. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.746289

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

The review's discussion-level comparison of its synthesized closure effects against summer-loss research (d = −0.005 to −0.05 SD per week, citing Kuhfeld et al., 2020a). This is the authors' interpretive conclusion, not a new statistical test; no effect size is printed for the comparison itself.

"Hence, even though remote learning was implemented during COVID-19-related school closures, the effects achieved by remote learning were similar to those achieved when no teaching was implemented at all during summer vacation."
