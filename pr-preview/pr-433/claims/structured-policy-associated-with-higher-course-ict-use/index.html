Czerniewicz 2009¶

Czerniewicz, L. & Brown, C. (2009). A study of the relationship between institutional policy, organisational culture and elearning use in four South African universities. Computers & Education, 53, 121-131. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2009.01.006

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Survey findings section (4.3) of a 2007 questionnaire study of four South African universities; percentages are descriptive, no inferential test printed. "highest in B-S Corp (87% said over half their courses used ICTs)".

"More students from Structured Corporate institutions report that their courses used ICTs compared to the other two institution types. This was highest in B-S Corp (87% said over half their courses used ICTs) and lowest in the Unstructured Bureaucratic institution (33%)."
