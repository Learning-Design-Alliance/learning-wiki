q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

In the school C case study, sustained collective improvement activities, such as aligning curriculum to standards, happened through professional development organized by the principal; without deliberate coordination, teachers did not engage in regular systematic improvement activities in group meetings.

"Well-defined instructional priorities alone, however, will not lead to developmental practices. We found that developmental practices also require intentional efforts to initiate and organize them."
