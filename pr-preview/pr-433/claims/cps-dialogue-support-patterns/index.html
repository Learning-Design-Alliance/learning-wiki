Jiun-Wei Guo · Hsiao-Ching She · Meng-Jun Chen · Pei-Yi Tsai 2023¶

Jiun-Wei Guo · Hsiao-Ching She · Meng-Jun Chen · Pei-Yi Tsai. (2023). Can CPS better prepare 8th graders for problem-solving in electromagnetism and bridging the gap between high- and low-achievers than IPS? Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-023-09407-y

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Analysis of CPS group online discussion turns, coded into providing support, requesting support, and reminding, found "no significant difference was found" for providing support or reminding; low-achievers requested more support (0.84 vs. 0.58, F = 1.96, p = 0.17). The authors interpret these patterns as showing low-achievers requested and received support.

"high achievers provide more turns labeled providing support (1.72 vs 0.96, F = 2.66, p = 0.11) and reminding (1.44 vs 1.09, F = 1.22, p = 0.27) to their partners than low-achievers, however, no significant difference was found"
