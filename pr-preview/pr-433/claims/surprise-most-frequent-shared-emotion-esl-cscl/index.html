Duong Ngo 2024¶

Duong Ngo, Andy Nguyen, Belle Dang, Ha Ngo. (2024). Facial Expression Recognition for Examining Emotional Regulation in Synchronous Online Collaborative Learning. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 34:650–669. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-023-00378-7

FER output aligned across group members showed surprise was the most shared emotion; the authors reviewed shared-emotion video segments and suggest learners "often expressed surprised emotions while listening to their group members' sharing information and thoughts" in the ESL context.
