Evidence¶

Chen C and Liu T 2026¶

Chen C and Liu T (2026) Impact of automated scoring on interpreting performance and self-regulated learning: evidence from a pedagogical experiment. Front. Psychol. 17:1867463. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1867463

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

The 2 (Group) × 2 (Time) mixed-design ANOVA on total scores found a main effect of time and an interaction with a large printed effect size, "η2ₚ = 0.29" for the interaction and η2ₚ = 0.52 for time; the article states both exceed conventional thresholds for a large effect.

"There was a significant main effect of time [ F(1, 44) = 46.83, p < 0.001, η2ₚ = 0.52], showing improvement in both groups, and a significant time × group interaction [ F(1, 44) = 18.00, p < 0.001, η2ₚ = 0.29]"
