Evidence¶

Carolina Cuesta-Hincapie 2025¶

Carolina Cuesta-Hincapie. (2025). Exploring expert perceptions of creativity in instructional design: a phenomenographic study. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10560-4

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Phenomenographic analysis of 2019 unstructured Zoom interviews and 2023 open-ended questionnaire responses from six academic experts, iterated through Marton's analysis process, produced the categories. The article reports "four distinct ways of experiencing creativity and creativity in instructional design".

"Data revealed four distinct ways of experiencing creativity and creativity in instructional design (Table 2). These categories of description show participants' conceptualizations and ways they experience creativity in their designs and practices."
