Evidence¶

Lucia Mason 2022¶

Lucia Mason, Christian Tarchi, Angelica Ronconi, Lucia Manzione, Natalia Latini, Ivar Bråten. (2022). Do medium and Context Matter when learning from multiple complementary Digital texts and videos? Instructional Science (2022) 50:653–679. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-022-09591-8

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Self-report preference comparison among the 255 participants reported in the Participants section. Despite this stated preference, the experiment found no comprehension or integration advantage for videos over texts.

"Participants reportedly preferred learning from videos (M = 6.79, SD = 2.16) more than learning from digital texts ( M = 4.31, SD = 2.08), t(154) = 12.01, p < .001, Cohen’s d = 0.75)."
