Conway 2025¶

Conway, C. M., Jenkins, H. E., Milne, A. E., Singh, S. & Wilson, B. (2025). Addressing the theory crisis in statistical learning research. npj Science of Learning 10, 68. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00359-6

q2 · i? · review · r2

This Perspective article, a narrative synthesis of the statistical learning literature, evaluates robustness using the criteria that phenomena be consistent, replicable, and "verifiable and detectable in several independent ways". The authors judge the general phenomenon of incidental pattern learning robust across many tasks and stimuli.

"the overall phenomenon of statistical learning itself, that is, the general finding that people are sensitive to patterns in the environment under incidental conditions, is robust, demonstrated across many studies in a multitude of situations using various laboratory tasks and stimuli"
