Evidence¶

Bracco 2020¶

Bracco, K., Barrat, V., & Finkelstein, N. (2020). Student progress under California State University's Executive Order 1110: A systemwide analysis of outcomes in 2018/19. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED613408

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Observational analysis of full-year credit accumulation for all 59,790 students, with pairwise Least Squares Means tests adjusted with a Bonferroni correction. Category I students earned "significantly more than the average earned by students in other placement categories"; Category IV averaged about 20 units, a 7-unit gap.

"those placed in Category I earned an average of about 27 baccalaureate units per student during their first year of college, significantly more than the average earned by students in other placement categories"
