Evidence¶

Liu W 2026¶

Liu W (2026) AI-peer integrated feedback in second language writing classes: exploring students' engagement and writing performance. Front. Psychol. 17:1871951. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1871951

q3 · i3 · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental pretest-posttest study with 122 Chinese EFL students; three one-way ANCOVAs on engagement posttest scores with pretest covariates and Bonferroni-corrected alpha. The experimental group's adjusted mean (M = 4.03) exceeded the control group's (M = 3.05), "mean difference = 0.98, 95% CI [0.70, 1.26]"; partial η2 = 0.29.

"ANCOV A revealed a significant main effect of group on posttest affective engagement after controlling for pretest scores ( F(1, 119) = 49.32, p < 0.001, partial η2 = 0.29)."
