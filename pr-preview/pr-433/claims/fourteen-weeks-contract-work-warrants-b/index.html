Danielewicz 2009¶

Danielewicz, Jane and Peter Elbow. (2009). A Unilateral Grading Contract to Improve Learning and Teaching. College Composition and Communication 61(2). https://hdl.handle.net/20.500.14394/23309

q1 · i? · design · r1

The authors' experiential report from their own courses, not a controlled study: they say the faith was "borne out" and that portfolios, repeated revision, and reflection help weaker writers improve enough to warrant a B. No measures or data are printed.

"when students spend fourteen weeks doing everything the contract requires (which is a great deal), the quality of their writing improves enough to warrant a B by the end of the semester."
