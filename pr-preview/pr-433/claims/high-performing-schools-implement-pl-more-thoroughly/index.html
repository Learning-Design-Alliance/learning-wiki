Evidence¶

Dabae Lee 2021¶

Dabae Lee, Yeol Huh, Chun-Yi Lin, Charles M. Reigeluth, Eunbae Lee. (2021). Differences in personalized learning practice and technology use in high- and low-performing learner-centered schools in the United States. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-09937-y

q2 · i? · associational · r?

National survey comparison of ELA teachers in identified learner-centered schools, with schools classified as high- or low-performing by standardized test results. The survey found teachers in high-performing schools "implemented PL more thoroughly and utilized technology for more functions". No effect sizes are printed.

"Generally, teachers in high-performing schools implemented PL more thoroughly and utilized technology for more functions than those in low-performing schools."

Related Claims¶

  • Teachers in high-performing learner-centered schools more frequently used technology for sharing resources and reported having a more powerful technology system — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
