Evidence¶

Ioana Alexandra Iuga · Oana Alexandra David 2024¶

Ioana Alexandra Iuga · Oana Alexandra David. (2024). Emotion Regulation and Academic Burnout Among Youth: a Quantitative Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09930-w

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r3

Pooled random-effects correlations between ER difficulties and the three burnout dimensions, as printed: cynicism r = 0.28, lack of efficacy r = 0.17, emotional exhaustion r = 0.27. Post hoc power exceeded 0.80 for cynicism (0.82) and emotional exhaustion (0.92) but was 0.06 for lack of efficacy.

"cynicism (k = 9), r = 0.28 (95% CI = 0.195; 0.353) p < 0.001, lack of efficacy (k = 8), r = 0.17 (95% CI = 0.023; 0.303), p < 0.05 and emotional exhaustion (k = 11), r = 0.27 (95% CI = 0.207; 0.335) p < 0.001"
