Evidence¶

Nuhu 2026¶

Nuhu, K. M., Ahmed, H. S., & Olumorin, C. O. (2026). Impact of real-time AI feedback on the technical skill acquisition of STEM students in Colleges of Education in Kwara State. Aminu Kano Academic Scholars Association Multidisciplinary Journal, 5(2), 65-76. https://doi.org/10.64726/ve5g5r44

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Quasi-experimental pre-test/post-test control group study of 240 students at three Kwara State colleges. ANCOVA analysis showed the AI-augmented group "achieved a mean gain score of 38.05, nearly double that of the control group's 18.33". No effect size is printed, so impact is unquantified.

"Data analysis using Analysis of Covariance (ANCOVA) revealed that the AI-augmented group achieved a mean gain score of 38.05, nearly double that of the control group's 18.33."
