Washington, DC: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/kipp-middle-schools-impacts-on-achievement-and-other-outcomes

The executive summary describes the evaluation's first report (2010), which covered "the 22 KIPP middle schools for which data were available at the time" and reported strong positive impacts in math and reading. No effect sizes are printed in the summary.
