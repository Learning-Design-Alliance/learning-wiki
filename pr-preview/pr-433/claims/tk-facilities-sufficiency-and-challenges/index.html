Statewide LEA survey reported in the TK Facilities section. The report states LEAs without sufficient space "projected needing a total of 488 more classrooms by 2025–26, about half as many as the 946 classrooms projected" the prior year.

"most LEAs (88%) projected having enough classroom space, an increase from 82% of LEAs in 2022–23. Differences across district size were not statistically significant; 89% of small LEAs, 85% of medium-sized LEAs, and 88% of large LEAs projected having enough classroom space by 2025–26."
