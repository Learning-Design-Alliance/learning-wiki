Evidence¶

Yu S 2026¶

Yu S, Lin Y and Chen C (2026) Designing for AI literacy: psychological mechanisms and self-determination. Front. Psychol. 17:1937845. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1937845

q2 · i? · causal · r2

fsQCA of the same 320 cases identified three sufficient configurations: TBI+STT+high competence, SBI+OET+high autonomy, and TBI+OET+high competence. Overall solution coverage 0.588, consistency 0.913, demonstrating equifinality.

"The three suﬃcient conﬁgurations are presented in Table 8. Their consistencies were 0.923, 0.896, and 0.941; raw coverages were 0.386, 0.357, and 0.458; and unique coverages were 0.089, 0.072, and 0.156. Overall solution coverage was 0.588 and overall solution consistency was 0.913."
