q2 · i? · design · r2

Block 2 of the anonymous post-term survey asked students to count grading errors observed over the term; counts were capped and verified against free-text items. The quote reports the printed first-pass false-positive statistics.

"Across the entire term, students reported a mean of 2.94 (SD = 2.99, median = 2) first-pass false-positive deductions (instances where LaTA took points off incorrectly) with a range of[0,20] ."
