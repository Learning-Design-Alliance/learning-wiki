Evidence¶

Gutiérrez-Palma 2026¶

Gutiérrez-Palma, N., Calet, N., Robles-Bello, M.A., & Valencia-Naranjo, N. (2026). The role of rhythm in learning to read in children with and without reading difficulties: an intervention study. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102273

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Bootstrapped mediation analyses (Process, 10000 samples, HC3 standard errors) on the whole sample with intelligence and vocabulary as covariates. Table 3 shows a significant post-test indirect effect of stress awareness through PA1 (Effect = .51, LLCI = .07, ULCI = 1.04).

"Accordingly, indirect effects (post-test) were found for stress awareness and word reading through phonological awareness (oddity or blending), and for non-linguistic rhythm and word reading through one of the phonological awareness measures (oddity)."
