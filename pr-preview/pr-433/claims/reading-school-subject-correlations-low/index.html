Arthur I. Gates 1921¶

Arthur I. Gates. (1921). An Experimental and Statistical Study of Reading and Reading tests (Concluded). The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0064253

From Tables XVIII and XIX correlations across grades III-VIII, the article reports reading's correlations with single subject composites are low, while the composite of school achievement correlates about 0.70, largely because reading is included in it.

"Correlations of comprehension with spelling average 0.40, with arithmetic 0.24, with writing 0.10. The correlations of these subjects with rate are about the same."
