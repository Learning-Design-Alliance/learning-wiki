Evidence¶

Andrea Horbach 2024¶

Andrea Horbach, Joey Pehlke, Ronja Laarmann-Quante, Yuning Ding. (2024). Crosslingual Content Scoring in Five Languages Using Machine-Translation and Multilingual Transformer Models. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-023-00370-1

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Dataset analysis of answer length in characters across the six datasets, plotted per score and per prompt (Fig. 3), including a DeepL-translated comparison to factor out language. The article reports the length-score tendency and that English crowd-worker answers are shorter than original ASAP answers.

"We observe that across all datasets, better answers, i.e. answers with a higher score, tend to be longer than answers with a lower score. We also see differences between prompts, with prompt 2 eliciting the longest answers."
