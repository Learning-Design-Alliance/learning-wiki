Andrea Roxana Bellot 2025¶

Andrea Roxana Bellot, Mar Gutiérrez-Colón Plana, Katarzyna Agnieszka Baran. (2025). Redefining Literature Education: The Role of ChatGPT in Undergraduate Courses. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00497-3

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Open-ended survey item Q18, reported as qualitative themes in the Results for Q18 section. The experimental group "revealed a generally positive reception towards the inclusion of ChatGPT in their literature class," highlighting novelty, efficiency, and educational value; some worried AI could detract from personal creativity.

"The majority of students in the control group found the activity of writing alternative endings motivating, citing reasons such as the enjoyment of creative freedom, the fun and interactive nature of the task, and the opportunity to explore different perspectives on a classic work like "Animal Farm.""
