Evidence¶

wills-2018-praise¶

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2023, May). Class-Wide Function-Related Intervention Teams (CW-FIT). https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/729

q4 · i3 · causal · r2

From the multisite randomized replication trial (Wills et al., 2018), researcher observation of 157 teachers in K to 6 across 21 schools found praise statements averaged 15.20 in intervention versus 3.10 in comparison classes, effect size 2.36, improvement index +49, statistically significant (p<0.01).

wills-2018-reprimands¶

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2023, May). Class-Wide Function-Related Intervention Teams (CW-FIT). https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/729

q4 · i? · causal · r3

Same trial, same 157 teachers: reprimand statements averaged 3.60 in intervention versus 4.60 in comparison classes, effect size 0.11, improvement index +4, not statistically significant (p=0.49). Equivalence was not tested.
