Evidence¶

Chenglu Li & Wanli Xing 2021¶

Chenglu Li & Wanli Xing. (2021). Natural Language Generation Using Deep Learning to Support MOOC Learners. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-020-00235-x

q1 · i? · design · r2

Validation experiment with four upper-level graduate students rating 10 post-reply pairs each on origin, support type, and 10-point quality; descriptive statistics only, no inferential test reported.

"Regarding the content quality, the average value rated by P1 was 7 for the machine-generated replies ( n = 5) and 6 for the human- generated replies (n =5 ) ."

Chenglu Li & Wanli Xing 2021 (2)¶

Chenglu Li & Wanli Xing. (2021). Natural Language Generation Using Deep Learning to Support MOOC Learners. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-020-00235-x

q1 · i? · design · r2

Descriptive comparison between Group A (MOOC A, trained context) and Group B (MOOC B, new context): similar correct-label ratios (M = .4523 vs M = .4857) and similar content-quality ratings (M = 4.90 vs M = 5.10).

"The findings suggest that the trained GPT-2 model's performance can be generalizable in a similar while new context."
