The editorial reports that Holtz et al. describe three prototypical big data methods using Wikipedia as their empirical example: automatic text classification, cluster analysis of knowledge dynamics in Wikipedia articles, and social network analysis.

"Their analysis shows that all three approaches can provide insights into the ways that learning and knowledge development take place."
