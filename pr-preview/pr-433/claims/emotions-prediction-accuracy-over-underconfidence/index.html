Evidence¶

Prinz-Weiß 2023¶

Prinz-Weiß, A., Lukosiute, L., Meyer, M., & Riedel, J. (2023). The role of achievement emotions for text comprehension and metacomprehension. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09331-w

q3 · i1 · associational · r2

Correlational analyses (N = 358) of prediction accuracy as bias: positive emotions (enjoyment, hope, pride, positive overall) correlated positively and negative emotions (anxiety, shame, hopelessness) negatively with prediction accuracy (e.g., shame r = -.18); anger, boredom, and negative emotions overall were exceptions (the latter marginally significant).

"Although the correlations were of rather small size, this means that in line with the prediction-accuracy hypothesis, with increasing positive emotions, participants' predictions became more overconfident (trajectory from underconfidence to overconfidence)."
