Structural equation modeling of the direct and mediated model of discourse-level language (Figure 1) fit the data very well (CFI = 1.00, RMSEA = .048) for 193 Grade 1 children; approximately 61% of total variance in discourse-level oral language was explained. Inference 'did not quite reach the conventional statistical significance (p = .058).'

"theory of mind (β = .27, p = .003), vocabulary (γ = .26, p = .007), and working memory (γ = .18, p = .02) were directly related to discourse-level oral language, whereas inference (β = .19, p = .058) and grammatical knowledge (γ = .08, p = .39) were not."
