Digital Promise Global 2019¶

Digital Promise Global (2019). Learning in the 21st Century: How the American Public, Parents, and Teachers View Students’ Potential and Their Learning Experience. Digital Promise Global: Washington, DC. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/32480546-27ed-4785-8703-bf66b74f6bfd doi:10.51388/20.500.12265/66

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Teacher survey item on sources of influence found teachers "rely more on experience (96 percent) and instinct (90 percent)" than on education and professional development or academic research; administrators ranked lowest at 32 percent.

"When asked how much they rely on each of a series of supports to inform their work, teachers say they rely more on experience (96 percent) and instinct (90 percent) than their education and professional development (66 percent) or academic research (53 percent)."

Digital Promise Global 2019 (2)¶

Digital Promise Global (2019). Learning in the 21st Century: How the American Public, Parents, and Teachers View Students’ Potential and Their Learning Experience. Digital Promise Global: Washington, DC. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/32480546-27ed-4785-8703-bf66b74f6bfd

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Teacher survey cross-analysis found those relying on academic research "more likely than others to say they are “very comfortable” with edtech, 64 percent versus 46 percent"; they were also more apt to say edtech greatly improves learning, 21 versus 6 percent.

"Notably, teachers who rely on academic research are more likely than others to say they are “very comfortable” with edtech, 64 percent versus 46 percent."
