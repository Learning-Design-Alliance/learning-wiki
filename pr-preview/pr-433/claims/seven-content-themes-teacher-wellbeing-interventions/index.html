Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Most reviewed teacher well-being interventions (37 of 46) were categorised supportive, while 9 showed no significant impacts
  • Intervention contents align with PERMA-H mainly through engagement (25), relationships (17), positive emotions (14) and health (8), with no content matching meaning or accomplishment
  • Gratitude, therapy-based, and the single physical activity intervention positively contributed to teacher well-being, though the physical activity intervention did not mitigate burnout — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Mindfulness and meditation interventions supported teacher well-being in 10 of 12 studies, with two studies showing non-significant changes — related
  • Professional development interventions showed mixed outcomes, with 14 of 20 studies supportive and 6 non-supportive — related
