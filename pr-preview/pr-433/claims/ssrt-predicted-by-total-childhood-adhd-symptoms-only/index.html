Evidence¶

Einziger Tzlil 2021¶

Einziger Tzlil, Ben-Shachar Mattan S., Devor Tali, Shmueli Michael, Auerbach Judith G., Berger Andrea. (2021). "My Brain Can Stop": An ERP Study of Longitudinal Prediction of Inhibitory Control in Adolescence. Brain Sciences, 11(1), 100. https://doi.org/10.3390/brainsci11010100

q3 · i2 · associational · r2

Hierarchical regression predicting SSRT at 17 years in the longitudinal sample; the model was significant with adjusted R2 of 10%. The article reports total symptoms "significantly predicted the SSRT at 17 years of age, β = 0.28, p < 0.05"; SSRT could not be estimated for 14 participants.

"Inattention and hyperactivity-impulsivity symptoms throughout childhood did not uniquely predict the SSRT. In other words, neither of these symptom domains showed specificity for the prediction of the SSRT (when testing the same model with the total ADHD symptoms scale, total ADHD symptoms throughout childhood significantly predicted the SSRT at 17 years of age, β = 0.28, p < 0.05)."

Einziger Tzlil, Ben-Shachar Mattan S., Devor Tali, Shmueli Michael, Auerbach Judith G., Berger Andrea. (2021). "My Brain Can Stop": An ERP Study of Longitudinal Prediction of Inhibitory Control in Adolescence. Brain Sciences, 11(1), 100. https://doi.org/10.3390/brainsci11010100

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Null finding from the path analyses in the same longitudinal sample. The article reports "The early precursors did not predict the SSRT in adolescence"; no effect size is printed, so this is a non-detected effect, not a measured small one.
