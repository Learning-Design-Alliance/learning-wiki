Evidence¶

Skar 2023¶

Skar, G. B., Graham, S., & Huebener, A. (2023). The Long-Term Effects of the COVID-19 Pandemic on Children's Writing: a Follow-up Replication Study. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09729-1

q3 · i0 · associational · r3

Three-level random-intercept regression models (lme4 in R) compared R2 with and without the cohort covariate. Table 2 prints R2 differences of 0.003 (text quality), 0.006 (handwriting fluency), and 0.001 (attitude), with f2 "well below the small effect threshold".

"The effect size f2 = R21−R2 was given in parentheses; an intuitive interpretation for f2 is that values of 0.02, 0.15, and 0.35 represent small, medium, and large effects (Lorah, 2018). Thus, the cohort covariate is well below the small effect threshold for all three outcome measures."
