Evidence¶

Liu 2011¶

Liu, T.-C., Lin, Y.-C., Tsai, M.-J., & Paas, F. (2011). Split-attention and redundancy effects on mobile learning in physical environments. Computers & Education. https://ro.uow.edu.au/edupapers/784

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Randomized between-subjects experiment with 81 fifth-graders (27 per condition) learning leaf morphology; planned contrasts compared TP and TO. The study found "no significant difference for comprehension test performance" (t(78)=0.14, p>.05) or efficiency (t(78)=0.23, p>.05); equivalence was not tested.

"The results of planned contrasts between the TP condition and the TO condition showed no significant difference for comprehension test performance (t (78) =0.14, p > 0.05) and learning efficiency (t (78) =0.23, p > 0.05)."
