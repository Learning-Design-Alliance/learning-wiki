Evidence¶

Leonardo Bevilacqua 2021¶

Leonardo Bevilacqua, Yvonne Kelly, Anja Heilmann, Naomi Priest, Rebecca E. Lacey. (2021). Adverse childhood experiences and trajectories of internalizing, externalizing, and prosocial behaviors from childhood to adolescence. Child Abuse & Neglect 112 (2021) 104890. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chiabu.2020.104890

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Linear mixed effects models on 8,823 MCS children (N= 8,823 in Table 2) tested ACE scores against log-transformed SDQ externalizing trajectories across ages 3-14, adjusted for gender, ethnicity, and poverty. The article reports "a graded association between the number of ACEs reported and externalizing symptoms at age 3" that "remained through to age 14".

"externalizing (ACE score of 1: β= 0.142; ACE score of 2: β= 0.299; ACE score of 3: β= 0.415)"
