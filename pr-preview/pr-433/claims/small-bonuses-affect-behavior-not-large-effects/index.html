Evidence¶

Kevin Booker 2009¶

Kevin Booker, Steven Glazerman. (2009). Effects of the Missouri Career Ladder Program on Teacher Mobility. Washington, DC: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/effects-of-the-missouri-career-ladder-program-on-teacher-mobility

q2 · i? · causal · r?

The report's own summary states that "small bonuses can affect behavior but not necessarily lead to large effects," indicating that modest financial incentives in the Career Ladder Program influenced teacher decisions despite their size. No effect size is printed.
