Bick 2016¶

The review's framing statement asserts, based on the body of evidence it reviews, that children exposed to early adversity are "at increased risk for a broad range of developmental difficulties" spanning cognitive and emotional adjustment. No effect size is printed in the supplied text.

"Children exposed to various forms of adversity early in life are at increased risk for a broad range of developmental difficulties, affecting both cognitive and emotional adjustment."
