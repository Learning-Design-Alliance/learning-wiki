Evidence¶

Duong Ngo 2024¶

Duong Ngo, Andy Nguyen, Belle Dang, Ha Ngo. (2024). Facial Expression Recognition for Examining Emotional Regulation in Synchronous Online Collaborative Learning. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 34:650–669. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-023-00378-7

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative coding of 379 segments from 14 groups in the synchronous online English task showed CoRL at f = 5.3% and SSRL at f = 0.8%; the authors note these results "reflect those of Malmberg et al. (2017)" for face-to-face settings.

"Furthermore, our findings reported rare occurrences of CoRL and SSRL in synchronous online collaborative learning (f = 5.3% and f = 0.8% respectively)."
