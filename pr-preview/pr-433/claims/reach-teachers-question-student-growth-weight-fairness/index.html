Evidence¶

Jiang 2014¶

Jiang, J. (2014). Teacher Evaluation in Practice: Year Two Teacher and Administrator Perceptions of REACH. University of Chicago Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/teacher-evaluation-practice-year-2-teacher-and-administrator-perceptions-reach

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Teacher survey in the year-two REACH brief; student growth accounted for up to 25 percent of a teacher's REACH rating in 2012-13 and 2013-14. "65 percent of teachers" saw too much weight on growth and only half judged the growth assessments fair.

"When asked if their evaluation relies too heavily on student growth, 65 percent of teachers agreed or strongly agreed. Moreover, only half of teachers said the assessments used to measure student growth are fair assessments of their students' learning"
