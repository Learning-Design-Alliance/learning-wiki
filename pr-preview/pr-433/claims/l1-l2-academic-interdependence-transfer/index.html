Rivera 1984¶

Rivera, Charlene, Ed. (1984). Language Proficiency and Academic Achievement. Multilingual Matters 10. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED240882

q2 · i? · review · r2

Review of findings reported initially by Skutnabb-Kangas & Toukomaa (1976) and replicated in subsequent studies, as cited in Cummins' framework chapter. The pattern shows "developed L2 academic proficiency more rapidly than younger immigrant students". No effect size is printed.

"older immigrant students (10-12 years old), whose academic proficiency (e.g. literacy skills) in LI was well-established, developed L2 academic proficiency more rapidly than younger immigrant students"
