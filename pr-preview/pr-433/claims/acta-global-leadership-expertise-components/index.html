Olivia Brown 2024¶

Olivia Brown, Nicola Power, and Julie Gore. (2024). Cognitive Task Analysis: Eliciting Expert Cognition in Context. Organizational Research Methods. https://doi.org/10.1177/10944281241271216

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The article reports this as the outcome of Osland et al.'s (2013) ACTA study: two coders independently conducted inductive content analysis of Knowledge Audit transcripts from over 30 hours of data with 20 interviews. Findings are qualitative and descriptive.

"The ﬁndings indicated that the expertise of global leaders included reading people closely, bridging cross-cultural communication, active listening, perspective taking, engaging in conscious questioning of parochial organizational views, and coaching others to develop a global mindset"
