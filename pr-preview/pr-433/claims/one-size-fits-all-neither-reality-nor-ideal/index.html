Theoretical argument from this commentary: the 'one size fits all' model rests on two analogies (factories, State monopolies) which the author rejects, arguing comprehensive schooling expressed 'a commitment to democracy' and that no working teacher practises one-size-fits-all.

"faith in the virtues of the comprehensive school, in the UK at least, was not the result of industrial era values, but rather a commitment to democracy"
