School Belonging Predicts whether an Emerging Adult will be Not in Education, Employment, or Training (NEET) after School. https://www.pwc.com.au/government/government-matters/youth-unemployment.html

Final multilevel logistic model adding high-school graduation as a covariate (Table 4): belonging OR = 0.82 [0.79, 0.85]; the coefficient "barely changed", so indirect effects via graduation were not estimated.

"While high-school graduation was indeed a strong predictor of NEET status, school belonging was still a significant predictor. Indeed, it was not even a notably weaker predictor in this model."
