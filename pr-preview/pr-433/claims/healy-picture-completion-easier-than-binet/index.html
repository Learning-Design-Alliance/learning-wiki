Evidence¶

E. B. Skaggs 1920¶

E. B. Skaggs. (1920). A Comparison of Results Obtained by the Terman Binet Tests and the Healy Picture Completion Test. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0076088

q2 · i? · associational · r1

A descriptive comparison from the same 65-case dataset: children classified by age level on the Healy and by mental age on the Binet. The article states a child at age level on the Healy "will test below age by the Binet"; no statistical test of the difference is reported.

"If a child ranks at a given age by the Healy Picture Completion test, then he, in all likelihood, will test below age by the Binet (especially for children of lower mentality)."
