Evidence¶

Araiza-Alba 2020¶

Araiza-Alba, P., Keane, T., Matthews, B., Simpson, K., Strugnell, G., Chen, W. S., & Kaufman, J. (2020). The potential of 360-degree Virtual Reality videos to teach water-safety skills to children. https://www.lsv.com.au/vr

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

One-way ANOVA on the IMI interest/enjoyment subscale across the three randomly assigned conditions found a significant difference ("Partial η2=.23"), with post hoc tests showing the 360° VR group exceeded both TV (p <.001) and poster (p <.001) groups.

"a one-way analysis of variance test indicated that there was a significant difference in the average level of enjoyment across different conditions (F(2, 167) = 18.602, p <.001, Partial η2=.23)."
