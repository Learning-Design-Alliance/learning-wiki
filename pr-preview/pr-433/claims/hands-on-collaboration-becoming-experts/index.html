Winslow Burleson & Armanda Lewis 2016¶

Winslow Burleson & Armanda Lewis. (2016). Optimists' Creed: Brave New Cyberlearning, Evolving Utopias (Circa 2041). Int J Artif Intell Educ 26:796–808. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-016-0096-x

q1 · i? · design · r1

The authors' interpretive report from their cyberlearning design experience, expanding on Schank's observation that those with the best series of questions learn the most when walls can answer questions. The article offers this as design experience, not a controlled study: learners 'begin to understand things from multiple perspectives'.

"we have found that when individuals and teams of learners actively engage in hands-on collaborative activities, they begin to understand things from multiple perspectives–they begin to become experts"
