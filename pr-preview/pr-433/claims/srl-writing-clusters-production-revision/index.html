Joep van der Graaf, Michelle Taub, Yizhou Fan. (2023). Introduction to special issue on facilitating self-regulated learning with scaffolds: Recent advances and future directions. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09364-9

The introduction reports, citing Taub et al. (this issue), a self-reflective writing task with university writing-class students in which four clusters were identified by SRL stage content, informing a discussion of advantages and disadvantages of event-based versus time-based scaffolds. "No differences were found in pausing." No effect sizes are printed.

"The anti-planner cluster produced more than the performer cluster, and the planner cluster revised more than the anti-planner cluster. No differences were found in pausing."
