The article reports "the β1 (b1) coefficient was equal to .03(p<.001), the β2 (b2) coefficient equalled .23 (p<.001) and the β3 (c´) coefficient was equal to .44 (p<.001)"; the model R-sq was .3465. Related Claims¶

  • Greater teamwork is positively and significantly correlated with critical thinking in secondary school students (r=.532, p<.001) — related
  • Teamwork predicts critical thinking directly and via work skills (Ind1: Twork→WSk→CThink, β=.053, 95% CI .0312-.0717) — related
  • Teamwork predicts critical thinking via educational motivation (Ind2: Twork→EdMot→CThink, β=.052, 95% CI .0318-.0747) — related
  • Serial mediation through work skills then educational motivation is significant but smaller (Ind3: Twork→WSk→EdMot→CThink, β=.004, 95% CI .0010-.0079) — related
