Evidence¶

John Chick 2026¶

John Chick, Laura Morello and Stephanie Staffey. (2026). From fear to innovation: a case study of transformative faculty development for ethical AI integration in higher education. International Journal for Educational Integrity. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40979-026-00225-x

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Pre/post survey of the ten-participant cohort (Table 12). The synthesis section reports these percentage shifts descriptively; no statistical test or effect size is printed for the perception change.

"Post-institute, there were no participants that responded with negative perceptions of AI pushed down to 0% from 20%. Participants with positive perceptions increased from 40% to 90%. Participants who reported being neutral about AI usage in their teaching decreased from 40% to 10%."
