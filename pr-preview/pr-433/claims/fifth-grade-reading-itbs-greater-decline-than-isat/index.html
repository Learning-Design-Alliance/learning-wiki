Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • In fifth-grade mathematics, ITBS scores declined while ISAT scores rose, a statistically significant difference in opposing trends — related
  • ITBS and ISAT inclusion rules differ: ISAT reports scores for all test-takers, while about 20 percent of ITBS test-takers are excluded from public reporting — related
  • ITBS and ISAT scores are highly correlated among CPS students in reading (r = 0.83 to 0.85) and mathematics (r = 0.86 to 0.87) — related
  • ISAT scores are not comparable over time: students with identical earlier and later test scores earned different seventh-grade ISAT scores depending on the year tested — related
  • ITBS mathematics is heavily weighted toward computation while ISAT contains up to three times as many items on algebra, geometry, measurement, and probability and statistics — related
  • CPS elementary ITBS scores improved across the 1990s in both reading and math for all age groups, but trend gains flattened after 1998 — related
