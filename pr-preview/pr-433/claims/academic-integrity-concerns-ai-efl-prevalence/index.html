Cross-study frequency analysis of the 54 included empirical studies; the review reports "Academic integrity concerns (P3) were reported in 20 studies (37%)" as the third most prevalent pedagogical limitation. Descriptive prevalence only; no effect size printed.

"Academic integrity concerns (P3) were reported in 20 studies (37%), making this the third most prevalent pedagogical limitation overall. The reviewed studies highlighted concerns related to plagiarism, cheating, ghostwriting, unclear authorship, and the inappropriate use of AI-generated content in coursework and assessment activities."
