Related Claims¶

  • Cheat sheet type was not significantly associated with exam performance on either exam, though a moderate non-significant trend favoured self-created sheets on the final — the general claim this finding bears on, with the studies behind it
  • Most students used cheat sheets as an occasional backup or continuous aid rather than a one-time reference — related
  • Students using self-created cheat sheets reported greater perceived coverage of needed final exam content than instructor-provided users — related
  • Self-created cheat sheet users reported significantly longer midterm preparation time than instructor-provided users, but the difference was not significant for the final exam — related
  • Preferences for instructor-provided sheets were grounded in trust, alignment and efficiency, while self-created preferences reflected personalization, control and studying through creation — related
