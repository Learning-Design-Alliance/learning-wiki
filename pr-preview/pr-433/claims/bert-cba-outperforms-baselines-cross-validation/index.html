Olatinwo 2025¶

Olatinwo, S. O., & Demmans Epp, C. (2025). Predicting Tags for Learner Questions on Stack Overflow. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-024-00441-x

q2 · i? · design · r2

Cross-validation comparison of six tag recommendation models on the Stack Overflow dataset. BERT-CBA "achieved higher Precision@K (Table 8), Recall@K (Table 9), and F1@K (Table 10) scores compared to the existing, baseline methods." Differences were tested with the 5×2 CV test using precision.

Related Claims¶

  • BERT-CBA outperformed BERT-FFNN by 6–15% on Precision@K, with t(5) = 6.56, p = .001, d = 4.64 — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • BERT-CBA outperformed BERT-LSTM by 4–9% and BERT-GRU by 2–10% on F1@K — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • BERT-CBA outperformed MNB by 40–73% across metrics, with t(5) = 5.55, p = .002, d = 10.20 for precision — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • BERT-CBA outperformed SVM by 44–73% on Precision@K, with t(5) = 5.62, p = .002, d = 7.13 — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
