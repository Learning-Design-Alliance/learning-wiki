Evidence¶

Jill M. Constantine 2006¶

Jill M. Constantine, Neil S. Seftor, Emily Sama Martin, Tim Silva, David Myers. (2006). A Study of the Effect of Talent Search on Secondary and Postsecondary Outcomes in Florida, Indiana, and Texas. Washington, DC: U.S. Department of Education. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/a-study-of-the-effect-of-talent-search-on-secondary-and-postsecondary-outcomes-in-florida-indiana-and-texas

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Quasi-experimental study using administrative data with matched comparison groups in Florida, Indiana, and Texas. The report states Talent Search participants were "more likely than comparison students to apply for federal financial aid"; no effect size is printed.

"Based on administrative data and a quasi-experimental design to create matched comparison groups, researchers found that Talent Search participants were more likely than comparison students to apply for federal financial aid and enroll in public secondary institutions."
