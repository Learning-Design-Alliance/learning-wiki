Design inspection of the Sorjonen et al. (2023) simulation: X1 and X2 were independently generated from the same latent variable, so the data "lacked a temporal structure" and did not model the change processes at stake in LCSM methodology.

"inspection of their simulation design reveals that their simulated data did not reflect change scores (nor an LCSM), because it lacked a temporal structure"
