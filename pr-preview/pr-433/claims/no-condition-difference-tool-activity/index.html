Evidence¶

Jeroen Janssen 2008¶

Jeroen Janssen, Gijsbert Erkens, Paul A. Kirschner, Gellof Kanselaar. (2008). Effects of representational guidance during computer-supported collaborative learning. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-008-9078-1

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Multilevel analyses of students' logged online activity in the 124-student study; the article reports no significant condition effects on any of the four activity measures, so outcome differences cannot be attributed to activity volume.

"No signiﬁcant effects of condition on time online ( b =- 1.46, p = .16), adding ( b = 0.00, p = .99), editing (b = 0.17, p = .80), and deleting elements ( b =- 0.10, p = .72) in the Debate-tool were found using multilevel analyses."

Related Claims¶

  • GD groups wrote essays significantly better in total grounds quality, total conceptual quality, and average conceptual quality, but did not cover more topics and were not better in average grounds quality. — related
  • Groups using the Graphical Debate-tool constructed representations of significantly higher quality than groups using the Textual Debate-tool, for both debate positions. — related
  • The GD advantage in total grounds and total conceptual essay quality depended on topic length: it held for short to average topics and disappeared for longer topics. — related
  • Interaction volume during AI-supported practice sessions did not differ significantly across conditions, supporting comparability of practice exposure — related
