Manjeet Singh Usma 2025¶

Manjeet Singh Usma, Shaun Bangay and Atul Sajjanhar. (2025). Augmented Reality Enhanced Analytics for Education: A Systematic Review. Journal of Learning Analytics 12(3). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2025.8447

q3 · i? · review · r2

Systematic review synthesis (NVivo content analysis of 59 shortlisted studies) reporting frequency counts of effectiveness factors for AR enhanced analytics. The review states tracking interaction was coded in 38 studies, context awareness 19, behaviour monitor 15, multimodal interactions 14, and better learning outcome measures 13.

"The factors affecting effectiveness are “tracking interaction” (38), with “context awareness” (19) and “behaviour monitor” (15), capturing “multimodal interactions” (14) and ensuring “better learning outcome measures” (13)."
