Evidence¶

Fred M. Newmann 2000¶

Fred M. Newmann, Karin Sconzert. (2000). School Improvement with External Partners. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://ccsr.uchicago.edu/sites/default/files/publications/ExternalPartners.pdf

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Authors' interpretive conclusion drawn from the qualitative case study across nine partner networks and 18 schools, based on three years of interviews, observations, and document analysis. The report presents this as a structural constraint inherent to the external-partner model rather than a tested hypothesis.

"Because partners’ involvement in schools usually occupies only a minor part of schools’ attention and activ- ity, their capacity to influence the work of schools is neces- sarily limited."
