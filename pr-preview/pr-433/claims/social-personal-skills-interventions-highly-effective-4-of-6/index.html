Pandey A 2018¶

Pandey A, Hale D, Das S, Goddings A-L, Blakemore S-J, Viner RM. (2018). Effectiveness of Universal Self-regulation–Based Interventions in Children and Adolescents: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis. JAMA Pediatrics. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6059379/

Narrative review of 6 studies evaluating social and personal skills training in small groups using frameworks of personal responsibility, model behavior, conflict resolution. The review reports "These interventions were highly effective, with only 265,71 of the 6 studies (33%) reporting a small and statistically insignificant change, while others reported17,64,69,75 significant improvements in SR measures (Figure 3)."
