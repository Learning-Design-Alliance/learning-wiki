Rachel Lambert 2026¶

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative design-research teaching experiment: a 6-week online course for 45 California educators, analyzed via pre/post Qualtrics surveys (44 pre, 33 post) and two Zoom focus groups about nine months later. The article reports that educators "attributed shifts in their beliefs and practices to empathy."
