Related Claims¶

  • Turnover rates are about 50% higher in Title I schools than in non-Title I schools — related
  • Teachers of color disproportionately enter through alternative certification pathways, which predict higher turnover — related
  • Teachers who are not fully certified have higher turnover than fully certified teachers (20.1% vs. 14.7%), with a larger gap among early-career teachers — related
  • Mathematics/science, special education, and foreign language teachers have predicted turnover rates 37%, 46%, and 87% greater than elementary teachers — related
  • Alternatively certified teachers are 25% more likely to leave their schools and the profession, controlling for other factors — related
  • Minority teacher turnover rates have been significantly higher than nonminority turnover, and the gap widened after 2004 — related
  • Teacher shortages fall disproportionately on high-poverty and high-minority schools, which had four times as many uncertified teachers in 2013–14 — related
