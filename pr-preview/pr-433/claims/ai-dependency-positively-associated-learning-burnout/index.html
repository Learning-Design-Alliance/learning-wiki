Huang Q 2026¶

Huang Q, Tu S, Lin J, Lu L and Lv C (2026). The dark side of AI in education: AI dependency as a mediator linking academic self-efficacy and teacher support to learning burnout among university students. Front. Psychol. 17:1889053. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1889053

q2 · i3 · associational · r2

Regression Model 3 in the cross-sectional survey, controlling for academic self-efficacy and teacher support. AI dependency was "signiﬁcantly and positively associated with learning burnout" with β = 0.451, R2 = 0.203, f 2 = 0.255.

"AI dependency was signiﬁcantly and positively associated with learning burnout ( β = 0.451, p < 0.001, 95% CI [0.392, 0.634]), explaining 20.3% of the variance in learning burnout ( R2 = 0.203, f 2 = 0.255)."
