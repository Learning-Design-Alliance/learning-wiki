Hartshorne Joshua K. 2018¶

Hartshorne Joshua K., Tenenbaum Joshua B., Pinker Steven. (2018). A critical period for second language acquisition: Evidence from 2/3 million English speakers. Cognition. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6559801/

Descriptive reading of performance-versus-age curves for native speakers in the quiz dataset. The authors state native speakers "did not reach asymptote until around 30 years old", with most learning in the first 10-20 years.

"Inspection of Fig. 5B suggests that native speakers did not reach asymptote until around 30 years old, though most of the learning takes place in the first 10–20 years."
