Chiara Marcoccia 2026¶

Chiara Marcoccia, Walter Quattrociocchi, Valerio Capraro. (2026). AI advice suppresses people's willingness to say "I don't know", even when the advice is wrong and accuracy is incentivized. https://osf.io/nwx6r/overview?view_only=a03ae0ca587c4c84a442c44d55ab97b6 doi:10.31234/osf.io/5y6m4_v1
