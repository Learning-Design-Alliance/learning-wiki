Evidence¶

Philip A. Higham 2020¶

Philip A. Higham, Bettina Zengel, Laura Bartlett, and Julie A. Hadwin (2020). The Benefits of Successive Relearning on Multiple Learning Outcomes. https://osf.io/nx4va/?view_only=049ee04aab4f420e9fa0091b3b69b9d0

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

A 3 (session) × 2 (practice task) repeated-measures ANOVA on VAS anxious affect ratings taken after each practice session. Main effects of session and practice task were "qualified by an interaction between session and practice task, F(2, 410) = 119.51, p < .001, ηp2 = .368".

"both of which were qualified by an interaction between session and practice task, F(2, 410) = 119.51, p < .001, ηp2 = .368"

Philip A. Higham 2020 (2)¶

Philip A. Higham, Bettina Zengel, Laura Bartlett, and Julie A. Hadwin (2020). The Benefits of Successive Relearning on Multiple Learning Outcomes. https://osf.io/nx4va/?view_only=049ee04aab4f420e9fa0091b3b69b9d0

q2 · i1 · causal · r2

Follow-up t-tests on the anxious-affect interaction. Anxious affect was higher for relearn in Session 1, and "the difference reversed in Session 3" (relearn M = 34.18 vs restudy M = 35.89), d = -0.230.

"the difference reversed in Session 3 (relearn: M = 34.18, SD = 16.42; restudy: M = 35.89, SD = 17.10), t(205) = -3.30, p = .001, d = -0.230."

Philip A. Higham 2020 (3)¶

Philip A. Higham, Bettina Zengel, Laura Bartlett, and Julie A. Hadwin (2020). The Benefits of Successive Relearning on Multiple Learning Outcomes. https://osf.io/nx4va/?view_only=049ee04aab4f420e9fa0091b3b69b9d0

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

Follow-up t-tests on the mastery interaction (higher VAS values indicate less mastery) showed "students’ self-rated mastery was less for relearn" than restudy in Session 1, d = 0.700.

"students’ self-rated mastery was less for relearn (M = 55.06, SD = 12.28) than restudy (M = 46.68, SD = 12.97) in Session 1, t(205) = 10.05, p < .001, d = 0.700"

Philip A. Higham 2020 (4)¶

Philip A. Higham, Bettina Zengel, Laura Bartlett, and Julie A. Hadwin (2020). The Benefits of Successive Relearning on Multiple Learning Outcomes. https://osf.io/nx4va/?view_only=049ee04aab4f420e9fa0091b3b69b9d0

q2 · i? · causal · r2

The mastery interaction follow-ups showed the Session-1 disadvantage for relearning "was eliminated by the second session", and "By the third session, relearning students rated having more mastery than the restudying students".

"By the third session, relearning students rated having more mastery than the restudying students, and this difference was present for every week of the semester."
