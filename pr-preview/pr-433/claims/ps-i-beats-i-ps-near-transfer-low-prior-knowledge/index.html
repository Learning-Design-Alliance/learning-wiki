Evidence¶

Cheng-Wen He 2025¶

Cheng-Wen He, Logan Fiorella, Paula P. Lemons. (2025). Does Instruction-First or Problem-Solving-First Depend on Learners' Prior Knowledge? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-09993-3

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Randomized classroom experiment with 367 introductory biology students assigned to I-PS (n=183) or PS-I (n=184), analyzed with non-parametric Quade ANCOVA controlling for prior knowledge test score. The results indicated that "significantly outperformed the I-PS students" on near transfer, supporting the productive failure hypothesis.

"the PS-I students ( M=6 .34,SD = 4.21 ) significantly outperformed the I-PS students ( M=5 .07,SD = 3.76 ) on the near-transfer test, F(1,365) = 7.18 , p = .008"
