Evidence¶

Carmen Álvarez-Álvarez & Samuel Falcon 2023¶

Carmen Álvarez-Álvarez & Samuel Falcon. (2023). Students' preferences with university teaching practices: analysis of testimonials with artificial intelligence. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10239-8

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Descriptive analysis (JASP 0.16.2) of GPT-3 classifications of 1081 student responses to an open-ended question about the best practice seen in class. The two leading categories were "Interaction and relationships" (30.53%) and "Clarity" (28.86%).

"We can see that there are two categories of teaching practices that stand out, "Interaction and relationships" and "Clarity", in which 30.53% and 28.86% of the responses were classified, respectively."
