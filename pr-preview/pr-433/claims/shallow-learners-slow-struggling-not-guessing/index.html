Gowda 2013¶

Gowda, S. M., Baker, R. S., Corbett, A. T., & Rossi, L. M. (2013). Towards Automatically Detecting Whether Student Learning is Shallow. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-013-0006-4

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive comparison of the average certainty of slip feature, the first feature in both step regression models. The authors offer the alternative interpretation that a slip indicates imperfect acquisition of skill, consistent with shallow learning.

"Shallow students have an average certainty of slip of 29.53 %, compared to non-shallow students, who have an average certainty of slip of 25.3 %."
