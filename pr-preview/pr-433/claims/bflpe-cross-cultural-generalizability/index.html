Related Claims¶

  • Equally able students have lower academic self-concepts in high-average-ability schools (the BFLPE), with a cross-national effect size of 0.49 — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • The BFLPE is domain-specific: school-average achievement affects academic self-concept but not global self-esteem or non-academic self-concept — related
  • Reanalyses of SCT choice-paradigm studies support the BFLPE, which was not moderated by students' chosen comparison targets — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Virtual patient effectiveness evidence comes from both high-income and low- and middle-income countries — related
  • Class-average achievement and chosen upward comparisons each make substantial, unique negative contributions to math self-beliefs when modeled simultaneously — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • The BFLPE is one of the most robust findings in educational psychology, with negative school-average achievement effects on ASC in 181 of 191 PISA samples — possibly the same claim (merge candidate)
