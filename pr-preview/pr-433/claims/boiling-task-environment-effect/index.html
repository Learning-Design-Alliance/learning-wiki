Scharlott 2024¶

Scharlott, L. J.; Rippey, D. W.; Rosa, V.; Becker, N. M. (2024). Progression toward Causal Mechanistic Reasoning through Phenomenon-Based Learning in Introductory Chemistry. Journal of Chemical Education. https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jchemed.3c00517

q3 · i0 · associational · r1

Chi-square test of independence on boiling point task responses across the three environments, reported in Results. The article reports "χ2 = 14.571, p < 0.005, effect size = 0.168 {small}" and states the small effect size suggests other factors could contribute to differences.

"a chi-square test of independence indicated that for the boiling point task, the learning environment had a less significant impact on student responses (χ2 = 14.571, p < 0.005, effect size = 0.168 {small})."
