Related Claims¶

  • Constructive engagement is ecologically organized: amplified by explicit learning-oriented framing, higher in coding than writing, and more visible in sustained exchanges — possibly the same claim (merge candidate)
  • Learning-oriented engagement is recurrent but selective in everyday human–LLM conversations, with cognitive engagement in 31.9% and constructive engagement in 4.9% of user turns — related
  • Scaffolded assistant support marks richer constructive participation at the conversation level across all six task settings — related
  • Support form differentiates the scaffolding–engagement association: feedback and explaining align most with constructive engagement, with a task-ecology crossover between instructing and modelling — related
