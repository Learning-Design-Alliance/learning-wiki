Jonathan Firth 2021¶

Jonathan Firth, Ian Rivers and James Boyle. (2021). A systematic review of interleaving as a concept learning strategy. Review of Education. https://doi.org/10.1002/rev3.3266

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Narrative synthesis of working memory span studies. Sana et al. (2017) found the benefit disappeared for highest-WM learners; Guzman-Munoz (2017) found the opposite marginal correlation when pooling three experiments. Birnbaum (2013, experiment 3) found the advantage persists under incidental learning.

"In their test of statistical problem solving, Sana et al., (2017) found an interaction such that the benefit of interleaving disappeared for learners (undergraduates) with the highest WM scores."
