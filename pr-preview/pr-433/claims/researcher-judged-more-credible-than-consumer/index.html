Evidence¶

Colin Lescarret 2022¶

Colin Lescarret, Julien Magnier, Valérie Le Floch, Jean-Christophe Sakdavong, and 2 more. (2022). Do you trust this speaker? The impact of prompting on middle-school students’ consideration of source when watching conflicting videos. Preprint, Research Square. https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-1998147/v1

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Mixed ANOVA on post-viewing perceived expertise ratings (McCroskey items, α=0.88) with effect size "η²p=.417" (F(1,254)=182.049, p<.001). Students judged the researcher more expert than the consumer, 95% CI [4.99:5.31] vs [3.14:3.47], M difference=1.85, t(254)=13.5, pbonferroni<.001, supporting Hypothesis 2a.

"Within-subjects effects revealed a strong simple effect of the factor Source, F(1,254)=182.049, p<.001, η²p=.417"

Colin Lescarret 2022 (2)¶

Colin Lescarret, Julien Magnier, Valérie Le Floch, Jean-Christophe Sakdavong, and 2 more. (2022). Do you trust this speaker? The impact of prompting on middle-school students’ consideration of source when watching conflicting videos. Preprint, Research Square. https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-1998147/v1

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Mixed ANOVA on perceived trustworthiness ratings (α=0.85) with effect size "η²p=.185" (F(1,125)=57.595, p<.001). The researcher was judged more trustworthy than the consumer, 95% CI [4.89:5.20] vs [4.02:4.31], M difference=0.886, t(245)=7.59, pbonferroni<.001.

"A main effect of the factor Source was noted, F(1,125) = 57.595, p<.001, η²p=.185"
