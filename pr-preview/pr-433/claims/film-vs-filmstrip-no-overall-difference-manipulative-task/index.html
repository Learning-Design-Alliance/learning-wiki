Lasser 1954¶

Laner, S. (1954). The Impact of Visual Aid Displays Showing a Manipulative Task. Quarterly Journal of Experimental Psychology, 6(3), 95-106. doi:10.1080/17470215408416655

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Controlled experiment with 12 university students and 63 RAF members comparing a commercially produced film with an especially produced filmstrip; identical commentary and no other instruction. The article reports "No significant difference was found" between groups' average performance; no effect size is printed.

"Results: No significant difference was found between the average perform- ance of the film and of the filmstrip groups."
