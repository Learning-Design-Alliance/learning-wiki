Evidence¶

Fyfe Emily R. 2023¶

Fyfe Emily R., Borriello Giulia, Merrick Megan. (2023). A developmental perspective on feedback: How corrective feedback influences children's literacy, mathematics, and problem solving. Educational Psychologist. https://doi.org/10.1080/00461520.2022.2108426

q3 · i? · review · r3

Systematic review synthesis of 44 experimental papers comparing feedback to no-feedback conditions in children ages 3-11; the review reports that "41 (93%) reported at least one positive effect of feedback" across literacy, mathematics, and problem solving.

"Across all 44 papers, 41 (93%) reported at least one positive effect of feedback on measures of learning and performance."

Related Claims¶

  • Feedback effects on children's learning are variable: 57% of papers reported at least one neutral or negative effect, especially on maintenance and transfer — related
  • Feedback alerts orienting children to upcoming feedback appear in about half of feedback conditions and are least frequent for ages 10-11 — related
  • Immediate trial-by-trial feedback was more helpful than delayed feedback for children ages 8-11, but timing conclusions remain weak overall — related
  • Positive feedback effects occur across the full age range 3-11, with no pattern of stronger effects in older children — related
  • Feedback benefits are stronger for children with low prior knowledge, resembling an expertise-reversal effect — related
