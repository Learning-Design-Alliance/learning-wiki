Burin 2018¶

Burin, D. I., Irrazabal, N., Injoque Ricle, I., Saux, G., & Barreyro, J. P. (2018). Self-reported internet skills, previous knowledge and working memory in text comprehension in E-learning. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education, 15:18. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-018-0099-9

Reliability analysis within the Study 1 factor analytic validation (n = 254). The article reports ω values for the three subscales and suggests the lower Operational reliability may reflect items rendered outdated by technological change, such as "watching videos".

"Internal consistency was, for Factor 1 (Formal/Navigation), ω = .72; for Factor 2 (In-formational/Evaluation), ω = .70; for Factor 3 (Operational), ω = .63."
