Table S7 (Study 2) reports the distribution of overchallenge and underchallenge responses across the two experimental conditions and counterbalancing groups. In the difficult condition 65 participants (49.24%) selected the high overchallenge categories; in the easy condition 74 participants (56.06%) selected the high underchallenge categories.

"in the difficult condition, 65 participants chose one of the two high categories, 4 or 5, when asked whether they were overchallenged during the task"
