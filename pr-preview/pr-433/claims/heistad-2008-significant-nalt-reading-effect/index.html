Quasi-experimental study meeting WWC standards with reservations (Heistad, 2008): students in three Minneapolis Read Naturally® schools were matched with comparison students from other district schools. The report states the author reported, and the WWC confirmed, a statistically significant positive effect on the NALT Reading portion; no effect size is printed.
