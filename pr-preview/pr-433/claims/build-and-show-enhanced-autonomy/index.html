Evidence¶

Yu-Ju Lan 2018¶

Yu-Ju Lan. (2018). Technology enhanced learner ownership and learner autonomy through creation. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-018-9608-8

q2 · i? · design · r2

The editorial reports, citing Yeh and Lan, a 4-month study in which 29 fifth graders used Build & Show to create 3D virtual worlds, with autonomy questionnaires, non-participant classroom observation, student and teacher feedback, and analysis of student-created videos. Results "suggested" the platform enhanced autonomy; no effect size is printed.

"The results suggested that the proposed Build & Show platform was a medium for enhancing students’ autonomy in learning"
