Evidence¶

Wei 2026¶

Wei, M., Liu, J., Wang, H., Li, Q. & Dong, G.-H. (2026). Fragmented learning from short videos modulates neural activity and connectivity during memory retrieval. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00399-y

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Between-subjects fMRI experiment: 57 university students randomly assigned to short video (n=28) or long video (n=29) watched 10 minutes of matched tourist-attraction content, then completed a four-alternative forced-choice memory test. The short video group's mean accuracy of 0.433 was significantly worse than the long video group's 0.664, "Cohen’s d = -1.645".

"An independent-samples t-test revealed a significant difference in memory accuracy between the two groups (t = -6.209, p < .001, Cohen’s d = -1.645), with the short video group (0.433 ± 0.163) performing significantly worse than the long video group (0.664 ± 0.113)"
