Andreas B. Eder & Vanessa Mitschke 2025¶

Andreas B. Eder & Vanessa Mitschke. (2025). Pavlovian to instrumental transfer of control over fight or flight decisions. npj Science of Learning 10:34. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00331-4

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Experiment 1's outcome-general test compared defensive response counts during novel-monster CSs (CS+CD, M=288, SE=7.89) against neutral CS- (M=270, SE=7.80). The paired-samples t-test printed an effect size dz = 0.398 (also written dz=0.398, 95% CI [0.178, 0.617]).
