Related Claims¶

  • Essay quality was highest for AI-generated drafts and lowest with no AI, negatively correlated with ownership, suggesting an ownership-quality tradeoff. — related
  • AI support shifts perceived attribution of ideas and text toward the AI, most strongly for AI-generated drafts, and an AI draft based on the writer's own outline contributed more ideas than expected (27%). — related
  • AI support during drafting lowers ownership substantially more than support during planning or revision, and planning support preserves more ownership than revision support. — related
  • Engage-to-Unlock participants incorporated less verbatim AI-response text into final essays than Standard Chatbot participants — related
  • Essays within each tool group showed consistent homogeneity across named entities, n-grams, and topic ontology — related
  • Essays written under Standard Chatbot sat closest to AI-only essays in semantic embedding space, and Engage-to-Unlock differed significantly from Standard Chatbot along PC1 — related
