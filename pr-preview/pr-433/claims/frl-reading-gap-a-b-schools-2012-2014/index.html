Analysis of achievement gaps in A and B schools (Figure 3) from the Oklahoma A–F evaluation shows FRL students scored "approximately .47 standard deviation units lower than Non-FRL students"; the printed effect size is a gap of .47 standard deviation units in 2012–2013, increasing to .54 standard deviation units in 2014, which the authors call a sizable difference of about 45 scale score units.

"For 2012 and 2013, FRL students scored approximately .47 standard deviation units lower than Non-FRL students. In 2014, this gap increased to .54 standard deviation units."
