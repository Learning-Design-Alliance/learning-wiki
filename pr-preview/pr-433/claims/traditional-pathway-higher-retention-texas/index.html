Wojcikiewicz 2025¶

Wojcikiewicz, S. K., Ralph, K., Snyder, J., Bland, J. A., & Wechsler, M. E. (2025). Teacher residencies in Texas: Supporting successful implementation. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/630.675

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of Texas Education Agency retention data by preparation pathway (Figure 1), tracking new-to-profession teachers from 2018–19 hires through 2023–24. The report states traditional-pathway educators were "retained at higher rates, across a 5-year period" than alternative or noncertified entrants; no effect size is printed.

"research has shown that educators coming through traditional certification pathways have been retained at higher rates, across a 5-year period, than those coming through alternative certification programs or without any certification"

Related Claims¶

  • Teachers who enter without completed preparation are typically less effective and have significantly higher turnover than prepared teachers — a broader claim this one bears on
  • Alternatively certified teachers are 25% more likely to leave their schools and the profession, controlling for other factors — related
  • An evaluation study compares teachers trained through different routes to certification — related
  • For-profit alternative certification pathways account for over 70% of Texas educator preparation enrollment while overall completion fell 14% over a decade — related

  • Students of university-prepared Texas teachers gained 2.2 additional months of 9th-grade ELA learning and 1.8 additional months of 9th-grade math learning versus students of alternatively prepared teachers — related
  • Alternative certification enrollment growth in Texas did not increase teacher supply because completion rates were low — related
  • Little is known about teachers certified through alternative certification programs and their experiences after certification — related
  • Teacher residency completers are more effective and more likely to stay in teaching than teachers entering through other pathways or without preparation — related
