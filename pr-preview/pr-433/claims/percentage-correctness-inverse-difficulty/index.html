q2 · i? · design · r2

Methodological statement from the article's calculation section, describing the assumption underlying the scaling rather than a tested result. The author concedes the assumption "may not really be true in either case, but practically it is true" for present purposes.

"The percentage of correctness in answering a question is assumed to be in inverse ratio to its difficulty, just as the percentage of those who spell a word is an inverse measure of its difficulty. This may not really be true in either case, but practically it is true"
