Evidence¶

Borchers 2025¶

Borchers, C., Fleischer, H., Schanze, S., Scheiter, K., & Aleven, V. (2025). High scaffolding of an unfamiliar strategy improves conceptual learning but reduces enjoyment compared to low scaffolding and strategy freedom. Computers & Education, 236, 105364. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105364

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Survey data from the crossover study showed students rated ORCCA more usable (M = 3.12) than StoichTutor (M = 2.40), and "students reported liking working with ORCCA more (M = 3.31, SD = 0.99) than StoichTutor (M = 2.17, SD = 1.29)".

"A one-way mixed-effects ANOVA confirmed that this difference was statistically significant, F(1, 58) = 51.51, p < .001. Accordingly, students reported liking working with ORCCA more (M = 3.31, SD = 0.99) than StoichTutor (M = 2.17, SD = 1.29)."
