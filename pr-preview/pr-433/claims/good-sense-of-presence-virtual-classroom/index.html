Evidence¶

Angélique Remacle 2023¶

Angélique Remacle, Stéphane Bouchard, Dominique Morsomme. (2023). Can teaching simulations in a virtual classroom help trainee teachers to develop oral communication skills and self-efficacy? A randomized controlled trial. Computers and Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104808

q3 · i? · causal · r2

The Gatineau Presence Questionnaire was administered after the VR immersion at each of the three direct sessions. Descriptive results showed a good sense of being there and the experience appraised as being real. The repeated measures ANOVA showed no significant difference for the total GPQ scores between the three direct sessions (F(2, 40) = .58, p = .566, η² = .006).
