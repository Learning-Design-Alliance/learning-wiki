Evidence¶

Denervaud 2021¶

Denervaud, S., Christensen, A. P., Kenett, Y. N. & Beaty, R. E. (2021). Education shapes the structure of semantic memory and impacts creative thinking. npj Science of Learning 6:35. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00113-8

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Comparison of empirical group networks against 1000 simulated Erdös–Rényi random networks with the same nodes and edges. The empirical measures were "statistically different from random networks"; no effect size was printed.

"the simulated random network analysis revealed that the empirical network measures (ASPL, CC, and Q) for both groups were statistically different from random networks (all p's < 0.001)."

Denervaud 2021 (2)¶

Denervaud, S., Christensen, A. P., Kenett, Y. N. & Beaty, R. E. (2021). Education shapes the structure of semantic memory and impacts creative thinking. npj Science of Learning 6:35. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00113-8

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Chi-square and t tests on demographic variables and nonverbal intelligence for the 67 children (36 Montessori, 31 traditional). No significant differences were found; no effect size was printed for these null tests.

"Across gender, age, nonverbal intelligence, and parental SES, no signi ﬁcant differences were found, revealing comparable groups (Table 1)."
