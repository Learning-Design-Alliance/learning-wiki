Evidence¶

Jeroen Janssen 2020¶

Jeroen Janssen, Paul A. Kirschner. (2020). Applying collaborative cognitive load theory to computer-supported collaborative learning: towards a research agenda. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09729-5

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The article reports an experiment by Bause et al. (2018) in which 219 students in triads solved a hidden profile task on a multi-touch table, comparing a joint-workspace experimental condition with a private-workspace control condition; no effect size is printed.

"Results showed that groups in the experimental condition showed greater discussion intensity, more indicators of mutual understanding, and better decision performance than groups in the control condition."
