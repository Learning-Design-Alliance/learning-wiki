Evidence¶

Slezak 2011¶

Slezak, C., Koenig, K. M., Endorf, R. J., & Braun, G. A. (2011). Investigating the Effectiveness of the Tutorials in Introductory Physics in Multiple Instructional Settings. https://arxiv.org/abs/1110.0050 doi:10.48550/arxiv.1110.0050

q2 · i1 · causal · r2

Post-hoc analysis of audio recordings of Style 5 sessions (N=47; post-test 31% kinetic energy, 31% momentum). The teaching assistant was not efficient in determining if student reasoning was correct and struggled asking appropriate questions.

"Although it appears most student diﬃculties were eventually addressed, some checkpoints were so lengthy and lacked succinct summaries, that it is quite possible students exited the checkpoint without clear understand- ing."
