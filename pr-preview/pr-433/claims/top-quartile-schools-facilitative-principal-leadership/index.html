Sebring 1995¶

Sebring, P. A., Bryk, A. S., Luppescu, S., & Thum, Y. M. (1995). Charting Reform in Asbury High School: Results of Student and Teacher Surveys. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/charting-reform-asbury-school-results-student-and-teacher-surveys

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Survey report summary of Principal Leadership scale results for teachers in top-quartile schools, based on ten questionnaire items about shared decision making, vision, and community. The report states "Almost 90 percent agree or strongly agree with all the statements" and notes 15 percent at scores of 9-10.

"Teachers in these schools were very positive about their principals being facilitative, inclusive, committed leaders. Almost 90 percent agree or strongly agree with all the statements (scores of 3-5 and higher), with 15 percent registering very high regard (scores of 9-10)."
