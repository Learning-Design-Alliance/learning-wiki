q2 · i? · design · r2

Main results table comparing fine-tuned models (trained on TURTLEAI-Train, 738k samples) against base models across DSReal, DSCraft, DSSyn, and the full dataset. For example, Qwen2-VL-72B-TURTLE reached 35.29% symbolic success on DSReal versus 11.76% for the base model; the article reports "increasing symbolic success rates by over 10%". No effect size is printed.

"Fine-tuning models (e.g., Pixtral-12B-TURTLE, Qwen2-VL-7B-TURTLE, and Qwen2-VL-72B-TURTLE) consistently improves performance on TURTLEAI-DS, increasing symbolic success rates by over 10% over their base counterparts."

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Fine-tuning is associated with more compact generated code, with the fine-tuned Qwen2-VL-72B maintaining a length ratio near 1.0 while the base model's failed outputs become substantially longer — related
  • Fine-tuning primarily improves alignment between code generation and visual reasoning rather than visual understanding or spatial reasoning — related
  • Fine-tuning on clean synthetic data yields limited gains on hand-drawn tasks, indicating limited robustness to out-of-distribution hand-drawn inputs — related
  • Current vision-language models struggle on education-oriented Turtle Graphics tasks, with even top models far below reliable performance on real-world tasks for grades 3-6 — related
  • All evaluated models struggle with spatial reasoning and precise visual replication on Turtle Graphics tasks — related
