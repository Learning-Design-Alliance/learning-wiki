Kaliisa 2025¶

Kaliisa, R., Baker, R. S., Wasson, B., & Prinsloo, P. (2025). The Coming but Uneven Storm: How AI Regulation Will Impact AI and Learning Analytics Research in Different Countries. Journal of Learning Analytics, 12(2). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2025.8495

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Comparative analysis across 11 regulatory documents found a discernible tension: the EU, China, and USA focus on safety and algorithmic discrimination, while countries such as Rwanda, Egypt, UAE, and Japan emphasize AI's economic and societal potential. The article reports this as a pattern from its OECD-framework coding.
