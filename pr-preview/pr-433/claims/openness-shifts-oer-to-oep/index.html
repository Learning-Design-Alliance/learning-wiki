Markus Deimann and Robert Farrow 2013¶

Markus Deimann and Robert Farrow. (2013). Rethinking OER and their Use: Open Education as Bildung. International Review of Research in Open and Distance Learning, Vol 14 No 3. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1017477 doi:10.19173/irrodl.v14i3.1370

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Position statement in the abstract, attributed to Mackey & Jacobson (2011) and Weller (2011): "'openness' in education necessarily shifts the focus from content (OER) to practices (OEP)." The article presents this as an adopted argument rather than a tested finding.

"We follow other commentators (Mackey & Jacobson, 2011, p. 62; cf. Weller, 2011) who argue that 'openness' in education necessarily shifts the focus from content (OER) to practices (OEP) that are necessary for the use of that content."
