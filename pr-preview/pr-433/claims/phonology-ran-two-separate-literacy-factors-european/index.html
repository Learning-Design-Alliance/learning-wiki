q2 · i? · associational · r2

Principal component analysis (Varimax rotation) of the four predictor measures in the pooled European sample yielded two components: "phoneme deletion and digit span loading on the first component" (phonology factor) and "the two RAN measures loading on the second component" (RAN factor).

"The results showed again a two component solution with phoneme deletion and digit span loading on the first component (phonology factor: eigenvalue = 1.1 3 ; factor loadings = .8 1 and .77, respectively), and the two RAN measures loading on the second component (RAN factor: eigenvalue = 1.6 4 ; factor loadings =.8 7 and .83, respectively)."

Related Claims¶

  • The component analysis of the five literacy measures yielded a speed factor (word and nonword reading speed) and an accuracy factor (word and nonword reading accuracy), with spelling analysed separately due to less clear-cut loadings — related
  • RAN explained more unique variance in reading speed than phonological measures in English, French, German and Hungarian (16.7 to 22.8%), with Finnish the exception where all proximal factors contributed low, roughly equal amounts — related
  • Phonological awareness accounted for more unique variance in reading accuracy than RAN in the orthographies studied, while English was the only orthography where RAN added substantial unique variance (14.2%) and total explained variance was highest (39%) — related
  • Cognitive predictors explained more spelling variance in English (34.7%) than in the other orthographies (8.9 to 16.2%), and English was the only orthography where RAN made a significant and highest contribution to spelling — related
  • Wagner and Torgesen's (1987) tripartite phonological processing model transfers to German and its transparent orthography — related
  • Factor analysis identifies three components of ICT consideration: help/importance, tools for an end, and negative valuation — related
