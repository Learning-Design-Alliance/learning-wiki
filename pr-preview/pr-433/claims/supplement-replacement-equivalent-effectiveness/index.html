School-level factor synthesis: the review reports that only two of the included articles directly compared supplement versus replacement integration, and both suggested "equivalent effectiveness"; most other studies adopted one mode without comparison.

"Notably, two articles directly compared the two ways of integration, suggesting that using tablet-based games as either a supplement or a replacement may have equivalent effectiveness (Outhwaite et al., 2017, 2019)."
