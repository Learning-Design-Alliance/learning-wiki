q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Qualitative theme from interview accounts: multiple participants named ego and childishness when rejecting metaphors; P18A, a student without prior experience who could benefit, avoided Metaphor (10.4% dwell time) to signal seriousness. Interpretive finding; no effect size.

"Representations that felt "too easy" or "too playful" were avoided even when they might have helped, because the social cost of appearing unserious outweighed the potential learning benefit."
