q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Conceptual analysis in Phase 2 (instrumentation and measurement), applying Chang's problem of nomic measurement to supervised and unsupervised learning, where the measurement function "emerges from the training data and the optimization procedures of the chosen algorithm." This is an analytical argument, not an empirical test.

"AI methodologies may intensify the problem of nomic measurement rather than resolve it, because the validity of the inferred measurement functions is often obscured by a black‑box algorithm that offers no epistemic justification for why student data are mapped onto specific cognitive attributes"
