Steve Woollaston 2026¶

Steve Woollaston, Brendan Flanagan, Isanka Wijerathne, and Hiroaki Ogata. (2026). Agentic AI and Pedagogical Best Practice: The Tension Between Automation and Learning. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04543 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.04543

q1 · i? · review · r1

Theoretical argument in the article's introduction, citing prior research on AI and human reasoning. The article offers this as a conceptual warning rather than a tested result: learners in cognitive surrender "effectively abdicates intellectual agency, allowing the AI to perform the critical synthesis and analysis".

"While productive cognitive offloading (delegation of routine mental tasks to external tools) can potentially free up mental bandwidth for higher-order reasoning, it frequently shifts into cognitive surrender."
