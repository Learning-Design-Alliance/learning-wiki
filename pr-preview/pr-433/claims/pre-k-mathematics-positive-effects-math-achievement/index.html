math-summary-1¶

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

WWC synthesis of five cluster randomized controlled trials in Head Start and state-funded preschool sites, totaling 2,913 children. The summary effect size is 0.38 with improvement index +15 (p < 0.01). The report states "The research provides strong evidence that Pre-K Mathematics improved student mathematics achievement."
