Evidence¶

Freeman Scott 2011¶

Freeman Scott, Haak David, Wenderoth Mary Pat. (2011). Increased Course Structure Improves Performance in Introductory Biology. CBE—Life Sciences Education. https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.10-08-0105

q3 · i? · causal · r2

ANOVA comparing average student Predicted Grade (from a regression on UW GPA and SAT-verbal scores) across the six quarters. Mean predicted grades ranged from 2.46 to 2.85; the pairwise differences crossed structure levels, so ability did not vary systematically with structure.

"An ANOVA indicates significant heterogeneity in Predicted Grades across quarters (Table 6; F = 13.2, df = 5, 2351, p ≪ 0.001). Post-hoc Tukey's Honestly Significant Difference tests demonstrate that this heterogeneity was distributed across quarters in the study: Of the 15 pairwise tests, only six were not significantly different, and these crossed levels of course structure."
