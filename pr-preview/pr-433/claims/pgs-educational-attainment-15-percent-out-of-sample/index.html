Evidence¶

Peter M. Visscher 2022¶

Peter M. Visscher. (2022). Genetics of cognitive performance, education and learning: from research to policy? npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00124-z

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The commentary reports the latest GWAS of educational attainment, from a sample of more than 3 million individuals, reporting about 3000 DNA variants reaching stringent statistical significance. It states the PGS "explains about 15% of individual differences in out-of-sample prediction"; it separately states the correlation between outcome and the PGS is approximately 0.4, among people of European descent only. No standardised effect size for this finding is printed in the quoted passage.

"a “polygenic score” (PGS) derived from the estimated effects of the DNA variants in the study explains about 15% of individual differences in out-of-sample prediction, among people who are genetically of European descent"
