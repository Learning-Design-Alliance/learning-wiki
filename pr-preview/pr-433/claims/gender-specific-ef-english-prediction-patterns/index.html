Ma 2023¶

Ma, C., Song, M., & Zhao, X. (2023). The impact of executive functions on English academic performance among Chinese primary school students: A network analysis. https://www.nwnu.edu.cn

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Gender-stratified network estimation: the male network had 16 non-zero edges (avg. 0.14) with 8 cross-community edges; the female network had 7 non-zero edges (avg. 0.16) with 4 positive cross-community edges, where "A4 and A3 both strongly predict D3(weights=0.21)".

"Figure 3 shows thatA4 has the strongest positive connection to D3 (weight = 0.20), followed by A3 and A5 to D3 (weights = 0.17, 0.15), and A1 to D2 (weight = 0.06). Conversely, A4 negatively predictsD1(weight=−0.10)."

Ma 2023 (2)¶

Ma, C., Song, M., & Zhao, X. (2023). The impact of executive functions on English academic performance among Chinese primary school students: A network analysis. https://www.nwnu.edu.cn

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Authors' summary of the gender comparison results in the Discussion, highlighting "distinct roles of executive function subcomponents in predicting English academic performance across genders".

"working memory span had a stronger negative bridging connection with listening in males, while cognitive flexibility had a greater bridging influence on overall performance in males. Conversely, working memory span's bridging influence was strongerinfemales."
