Theoretical claim about the child's tendency to treat 'the tones of the human voice' as play material long before proper speech; the article reports no systematic observation or data for this.

"The practice which the voice receives in this way is incessant, and it serves largely in developing the full range of articulate sounds and facilitates their production."
