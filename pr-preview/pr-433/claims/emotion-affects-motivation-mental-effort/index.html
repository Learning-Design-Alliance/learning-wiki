Evidence¶

Plass 2019¶

Plass, J. L., & Kalyuga, S. (2019). Four Ways of Considering Emotion in Cognitive Load Theory. Educational Psychology Review, 31, 339–359. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-019-09473-5

q1 · i? · theoretical · r?

Theoretical argument from the article's abstract, the fourth perspective. It proposes an indirect pathway in which "emotion affects motivation, and, in turn, mental effort investment," linking emotion to the effort learners invest.
