q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Qualitative classroom observation study of 23 English/language arts classes across three CHSRI schools, spring 2008. Researchers categorized activities by skill type and found "Students spent at least part of the period in activities aimed at building high-level skills in 21 of the 23 observed classes."
