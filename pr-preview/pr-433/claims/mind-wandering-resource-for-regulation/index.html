The discussion paper reports, attributing to Bühler et al. (2025), a systematic review classifying 134 empirical studies on mind wandering with the SMA Grid, finding "a strong reliance on self-reports" and limited vertical or integrated designs. The review argues mind wandering can act as both symptom and trigger of regulatory processes.
