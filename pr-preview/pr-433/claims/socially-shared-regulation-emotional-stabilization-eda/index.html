The discussion paper describes the Moreno et al. (2025) study drawing on data from high-fidelity medical simulation settings, combining behavioral coding of regulatory strategies with EDA and using supervised machine learning to link regulatory behaviors to physiological arousal. EDA is positioned as a dependent variable, not a predictor.
