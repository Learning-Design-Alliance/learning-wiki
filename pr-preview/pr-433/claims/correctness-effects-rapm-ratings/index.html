Evidence¶

Adam Chuderski 2021¶

Adam Chuderski, Jan Jastrzębski, Bartłomiej Kroczek, Hanna Kucwaj, Michał Ociepka. (2021). Metacognitive experience on Raven’s matrices versus insight problems. Metacognition and Learning 16:15–35. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09236-6

q3 · i1 · associational · r2

BANOVA on all 1855 rating values (1017 correct, 838 incorrect solutions) with item and correctness as fixed factors. Each correctness effect was reliable, each BF10 > 999, with ηp2 = .02 for Intuition, .05 for Suddenness, and .21 for Certainty.

"The correct trials yielded larger values of Suddenness (ΔM = 2.52) and Certainty (ΔM = 6.36), but were accompanied by lower values of Intuition ( ΔM = −1.32), as compared to the incorrect trials."

Adam Chuderski 2021 (2)¶

Adam Chuderski, Jan Jastrzębski, Bartłomiej Kroczek, Hanna Kucwaj, Michał Ociepka. (2021). Metacognitive experience on Raven’s matrices versus insight problems. Metacognition and Learning 16:15–35. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09236-6

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Same BANOVA: the Certainty item × correctness interaction was reliable (BF10 = 230.7), with the correct-incorrect difference dropping from ΔM = 5.94 on the five easiest items to ΔM = 3.88 on the five hardest, the latter still highly reliable (BF10 > 999).

"In contrast, for Certainty there was a reliable interaction, BF10 = 230.7. It indicated a gradual decrease of the difference in rating between the correct and incorrect trials."
