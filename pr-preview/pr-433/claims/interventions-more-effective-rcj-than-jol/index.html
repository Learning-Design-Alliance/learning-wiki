Evidence¶

Noortje Janssen · Ard W. Lazonder 2024¶

Noortje Janssen · Ard W. Lazonder. (2024). Meta-analysis of Interventions for Monitoring Accuracy in Problem Solving. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09936-4

q4 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Exploratory moderator analysis of judgment type in the 35-study meta-analysis; Table 3 reports RCJ k = 25, g = 0.30 and JOL k = 10, g = 0.15. The judgment-type Q-test was nonsignificant (p = 0.245).

"Interventions were more effective for retrospective confidence judgments than judgments of learning, z = 2.03, p = 0.042, and studies conducted in laboratory settings had significantly higher effect sizes than classroom studies, z = 4.98, p < 0.001."
