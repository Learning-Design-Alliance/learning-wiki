Evidence¶

Anna Filighera 2024¶

Anna Filighera, Sebastian Ochs, Tim Steuer, Thomas Tregel. (2024). Cheating Automatic Short Answer Grading with the Adversarial Usage of Adjectives and Adverbs. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 34, 616–646. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-023-00361-2

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

Spearman rank correlation analysis of grader ratings from the 7-grader survey, reported in Results. Control-group mistrust correlates with naturalness (ρ = -0.41) and correctness (ρ = -0.51); experimental-group correlations drop to ρ = 0.20 and ρ = 0.07, indicating "graders suspect poorly written and wrong answers in the absence of other clues".

"Additionally, there was a moderate negative Spearman’s rank correlation ( /u1D70C ) between mistrust and naturalness ( /u1D70C= -0.41) as well as mistrust and correctness ( /u1D70C= -0.51) in the control group. In contrast, the correlations in the experimental group were much weaker with /u1D70C= 0.20 and /u1D70C= 0.07, respectively."
