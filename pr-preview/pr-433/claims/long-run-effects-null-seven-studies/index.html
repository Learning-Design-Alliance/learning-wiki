Evidence¶

Dietrichson J 2020¶

Dietrichson J, Filges T, Klokker RH, Viinholt BCA, Bøg M, Jensen UH. (2020). Targeted school‐based interventions for improving reading and mathematics for students with, or at risk of, academic difficulties in Grades 7–12: A systematic review. Campbell Systematic Reviews. https://doi.org/10.1002/cl2.1081

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Separate meta-analysis of follow-up outcomes from seven studies with longer follow-up periods. The review reports "ES = 0.05" with a confidence interval crossing zero; equivalence was not tested, and variation was substantial.

"Seven studies included follow‐up outcomes. The average effect size was small and not statistically significant (ES = 0.05, 95% CI = [−0.096, 0.192]), but there was substantial variation."
