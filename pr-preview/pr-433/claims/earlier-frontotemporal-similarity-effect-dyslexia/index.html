Cao 2026¶

Cao, Y., Zhang, M., Gentile, F. & Bonte, M. (2026). Dynamic behavioral and neural correlates of letter-speech sound learning in typical and dyslexic readers. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00410-0

Second ANOVA with group factor in the EEG training data: "a significant Block × Condition × Group three-way interaction in frontotemporal clusters" (η²P = .05, a medium effect by the η² code table); pairwise comparisons located the SM–DM reduction at block 2 in DD and blocks 3–4 in TR.

"revealed a significant Block × Condition × Group three-way interaction in frontotemporal clusters (F(3,180) = 1.89, p = .021, η²P = .05), with no significant group main effect or two-way interaction effects"
