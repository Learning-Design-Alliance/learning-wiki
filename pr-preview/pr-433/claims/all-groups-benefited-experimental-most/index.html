Ahlem Ammar and Nina Spada 2006¶

Ahlem Ammar and Nina Spada. (2006). ONE SIZE FITS ALL?: Recasts, Prompts, and L2 Learning. Studies in Second Language Acquisition, Volume 28, Issue 4. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0272263106060268

Quasi-experimental study with recast, prompt, and control groups tested before, immediately after, and 4 weeks after a 4-week intervention. The article reports "All three groups benefited from the instructional intervention," with the experimental groups benefiting most. No effect size is printed in the available text.
