Authors' interpretation in the Discussion, offered as one of two alternative accounts of the pattern of shorter number strings with intact object-counting accuracy; it is an interpretation, not a separately tested result.

Related Claims¶

  • Cross-cultural evidence: adults without a counting system fail to develop exact representations of numbers beyond 3 or 4 despite a normal ANS — related
  • Deaf children produce shorter conventional number strings in abstract counting than hearing children of the same school level, with a roughly 2-year delay — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • In object counting, deaf and hearing children make similar attention and principle errors, but deaf children make more number-string errors — related
  • Deaf children's abstract-counting errors reflect the rule-bound base-5 structure of Belgian French Sign Language: they stop at production-rule boundaries and make fewer omission errors than hearing children — related
