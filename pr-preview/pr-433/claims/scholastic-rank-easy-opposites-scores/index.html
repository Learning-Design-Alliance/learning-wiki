Table III reports median scores and ranges for the 100 subjects by group on easy lists (faculty 9 cases, median 139; graduates 23, 158; seniors 47, 166; juniors 21, 179) and hard lists (faculty 679; graduates 668; seniors 786; juniors 843).

"In the case of the easy lists it will be noted that there is a definite correlation between scholastic ranks and ability in these tests. In these lists correlation is not so marked for the median standing of the faculty is slightly inferior to that of the graduate group."
