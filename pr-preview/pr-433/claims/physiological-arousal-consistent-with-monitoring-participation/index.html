Shihui Feng 2025¶

Shihui Feng, Ziyi Wei. (2025). The CoMPAS Framework for Modeling Individual Participation in Collaborative Learning Processes: a Systematic Review. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-025-09450-x

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Malmberg et al. (2019), a study aligning individuals' contributions to metacognitive monitoring with physiological responses. The review's account is descriptive; no effect size is printed in the text reviewed.

"They found that individuals' participation in monitoring processes was consistent with their physiological arousal. This study suggested an observable indicator for assessing individual participation in group regulation in collaborative learning"
