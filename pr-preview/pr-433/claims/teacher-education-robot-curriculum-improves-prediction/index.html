Evidence¶

Hughes 2026¶

Hughes, A., Gerson, S. A., & van Schaik, J. E. (2026). Teacher-led robot intervention in early primary school classrooms improves pupil and teacher outcomes. Computers & Education, 242: 105510. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105510

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Multilevel analysis of prediction task data from 409 pupils in a pre-test post-test design with three conditions; the printed interaction for Intervention+ vs Control was β = 0.33, p = .02. The article reports "a significant interaction effect of condition and time on children’s prediction performance".

"The analysis revealed a significant interaction effect of condition and time on children’s prediction performance (see Table 2). Children in Intervention+ showed significantly larger improvements in scores compared to those in Control. The differences between Intervention and Control were not significant."
