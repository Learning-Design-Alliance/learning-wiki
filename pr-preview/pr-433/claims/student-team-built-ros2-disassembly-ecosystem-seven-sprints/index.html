This is the article's demonstration project in the winter semester 2025/2026 class, described as an application scenario rather than an evaluated study. The team worked on "the automated disassembly of products in the context of the Circular Economy", transferred to plastic building bricks, with core contributions in computer vision, disassembly-sequence planning and the controller ecosystem. No outcome measures are reported.

"The project managed by the authors as POs and conducted by the student team focuses on the automated disassembly of products in the context of the Circular Economy to salvage valuable parts, which can be reused for repair and remanufacture."
