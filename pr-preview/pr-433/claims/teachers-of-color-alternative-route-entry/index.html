Descriptive analysis of 2020–21 National Teacher and Principal Survey data on certification route by race/ethnicity. The report finds teachers of color "nearly twice as likely as White teachers to have entered teaching through an alternative route (31% vs. 17%)".

"Teachers of color were nearly twice as likely as White teachers to have entered teaching through an alternative route (31% vs. 17%) in 2020–21, with Black teachers being the most likely of all groups to enter through an alternative route."
