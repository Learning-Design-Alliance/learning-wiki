A non-social control version of the student-teacher task replaced the virtual teacher with a lottery wheel (reported in Supplementary Information); the reveal effect was smaller than in the social framing condition.

"The results indicated a smaller reveal effect in the non-social experiment (i.e., increased tendency to choose the advised card in trials where the advice was revealed vs. concealed)"
