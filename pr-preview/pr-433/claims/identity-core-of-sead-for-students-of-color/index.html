Evidence¶

Duchesneau 2020¶

Duchesneau, N. (2020). Social, Emotional, and Academic Development Through an Equity Lens (#ReEnvisionSEL). The Education Trust. https://edtrust.org/resource/social-emotional-and-academic-development-through-an-equity-lens/

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Focus group participants responded with factors related to racial, ethnic, and cultural identity alongside common social-emotional skills. One Black mother said "Knowledge of self — who they are, their culture," and participants discussed needing to "code switch" to survive in a White society.

"The key theme emerging from this conversation was the need for an explicit focus on racial identity development and how it relates to all of the social, emotional, and even academic needs of students."
