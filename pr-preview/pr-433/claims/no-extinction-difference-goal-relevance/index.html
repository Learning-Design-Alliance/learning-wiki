Evidence¶

Yoann Stussi 2019¶

Yoann Stussi, Aude Ferrero, Gilles Pourtois and David Sander. (2019). Achievement motivation modulates Pavlovian aversive conditioning to goal-relevant stimuli. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0043-3

q3 · i? · causal · r2

GLM analysis of the extinction phase (US no longer delivered) in 72 participants found no category difference (η2p = 0.008, negligible); the moderation by achievement motivation was also non-significant, F(1, 70) = 0.15, p = 0.696. Equivalence was not tested.

"Analysis of the extinction phase showed that the CR did not statistically differ across the three stimulus categories, F(2, 140) = 0.54, p = 0.586, η2p = 0.008"
