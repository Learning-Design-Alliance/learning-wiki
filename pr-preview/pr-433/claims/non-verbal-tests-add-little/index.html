Evidence¶

Arthur I. Gates 1923¶

Arthur I. Gates. (1923). The Correlations of Achievement in School Subjects with Intelligence Tests and Other Variables (Concluded). The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0066477

q3 · i? · associational · r?

Regression-weight and multiple correlation analyses comparing verbal and non-verbal test materials as predictors of achievement. The article reports "the non-verbal materials added but little" beyond verbal tests and Stanford MA; no effect size is printed for this contribution.

"it was consistently found that the non-verbal materials added but little when the independent weights were found by the regression coeffiecient and by multiple correlation."
