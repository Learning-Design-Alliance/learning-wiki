Analysis of open-response answers from 163 students in the experimentation unit. The article reports "83% (N=163) of students reported Euglena responded to light" with 62% identifying negative phototaxis as the dominant response.

"Prompted by the open-ended question: “What do you see?”, 83% (N=163) of students reported Euglena responded to light, among which 62% recognized negative phototaxis, 10% positive phototaxis, 7% both types of phototaxis and 4% “spinning” without linear motion."
