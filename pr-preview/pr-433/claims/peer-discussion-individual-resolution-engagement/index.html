Lehesvuori 2026¶

Lehesvuori, S., Järvinen, M., Lämsä, J., Heilala, V., Hämäläinen, R. (2026). Exploring interconnections between communicative activities, situational stress reactions and cognitive engagement in undergraduate mathematics lesson. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09786-3

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Examples 2–3, combining whole-class video, eye-tracking and cardiac data: peer comparison of solutions caused Student C to notice an error ("Oh, that one is incorrect for me"), after which he resolved it individually with ASV peaks, later decreasing as he confirmed the new solution.

"the peaking ASV values during individual problem solving set up the interpretations for co-occurrence of situational stress reactions and cognitive engagement favouring learning"
