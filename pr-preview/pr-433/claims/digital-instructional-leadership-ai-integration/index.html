Evidence¶

Marcus Pietsch · Dana-Kristin Mah 2025¶

Marcus Pietsch · Dana-Kristin Mah. (2025). Leading the AI transformation in schools: it starts with a digital mindset. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10439-w

q2 · i2 · associational · r?

Structural equation model of 179 German school leaders' survey responses; the digital instructional leadership path to AI integration was β = 0.336, SE = 0.094, p < 0.001, supporting H2. The authors conclude both leadership behaviors matter.

"Transformational and digital instructional leadership have a positive effect on comprehensive AI integration in schools, while the digital mindset of school leaders exerts a positive influence on both leadership behaviors."
