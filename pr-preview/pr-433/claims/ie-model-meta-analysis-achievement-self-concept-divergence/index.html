Evidence¶

Jens Möller 2024¶

Jens Möller. (2024). Ten Years of Dimensional Comparison Theory: On the Development of a Theory from Educational Psychology. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09918-6

q3 · i0 · review · r2

Renewed meta-analysis by Möller et al. (2020) of k = 118 independent datasets with N = 213,121 participants, updating the 2009 meta-analysis. The review reports "The average self-concept correlation was remarkably lower (r = 0.07, 2009: r = 0.10)" than the achievement correlation of r = 0.56.

"the average correlation between achievements in math and L1 was highly positive (r = 0.56, 2009: r = 0.67). The average self-concept correlation was remarkably lower (r = 0.07, 2009: r = 0.10)"

Jens Möller 2024 (2)¶

Jens Möller. (2024). Ten Years of Dimensional Comparison Theory: On the Development of a Theory from Educational Psychology. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09918-6

q3 · i3 · review · r2

Same renewed meta-analysis (k = 118 datasets, N = 213,121). Within-domain social comparison paths were substantial and positive (β = 0.57 math, β = 0.46 verbal), while "the dimensional comparison effects were significantly negative" across domains, replicating the 2009 meta-analysis of 69 studies.

"the dimensional comparison effects were significantly negative from math achievement to verbal self-concept (β = − 0.17; 2009: β = − 0.21), and β = − 0.20 from verbal achievement to math self-concept (2009: β = − 0.27)"
