Exploratory moderator analysis coded at the article level (primary, secondary, or mixed school level). The review reports "stronger associations in secondary school settings"; no effect size or test statistic for this moderator is printed in the available text, so the magnitude is unreported.

"Moderator analyses indicated stronger associations in secondary school settings. Associations were also larger when teacher and student variables were reported by the same informant, although this difference reached conventional significance only for teacher ill-being and approached significance for teacher well-being."
