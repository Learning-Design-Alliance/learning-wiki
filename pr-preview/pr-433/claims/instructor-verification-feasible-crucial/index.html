The paper's related-work synthesis attributes this conclusion to instructor-facing systems such as SPHERE's structured instructor review of LLM feedback outputs, VizGroup, and ClassAid; the review narrates these systems without re-analyzing their data.

"Together, prior works show that A) instructor verification and oversight of AI-generated content before delivery is both feasible and crucial for maintaining quality, and B) the interface for that oversight must be designed carefully to be usable by the instructor at scale."
