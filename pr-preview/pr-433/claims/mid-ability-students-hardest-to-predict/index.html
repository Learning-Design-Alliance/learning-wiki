Benjamin Mawdsley 2026¶

Benjamin Mawdsley, Tom Quilter, Richard Turner, Sarah Jackson, and Paul Edwards. (2026). Archetypes or ability? Clustering for modelling student mathematical competence. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26063 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.26063

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Ability-cohort analysis (Figure 9) binning test students by mean exam score excluding the test question and computing log loss per model; the authors find "students of intermediate ability are much harder to predict". No demographic data was provided, so ability level was the only subgrouping available.

"All models perform best for high or low performing students, re-emphasising how overall ability is the primary determinant of model predictions. Students of intermediate ability are much harder to predict, and have a higher log loss."
