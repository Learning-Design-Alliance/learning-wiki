What Works Clearinghouse 2016¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2016). What Works Clearinghouse intervention report: University of Chicago School Mathematics Project (UCSMP). What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/641

q2 · i? · causal · r1

WWC review of Peters (1992), an RCT in one Nebraska junior high school in which 17 UCSMP Algebra students were compared with 19 Saxon Algebra I students; the study finding table prints an improvement index of "–6" and "No" statistical significance, characterized as an indeterminate effect.

"Peters (1992) found, and the WWC confirmed (using a difference-in-differences approach), no statistically significant differences between the UCSMP Algebra and comparison groups in the algebra domain."
