Narrative review of pharmaco-epidemiological analyses of large-scale register databases, reporting a Danish record-linkage study attributed to Dalsgaard et al. (2015a). The review states medication "reduced the risk for injuries by up to 43% and emergency ward visits by up to 45%" in children. Percentages only; no effect size printed.

"A Danish register-based study showed that treatment with ADHD medication reduced the risk for injuries by up to 43% and emergency ward visits by up to 45% in children with ADHD ( Dalsgaard et al., 2015a )."
