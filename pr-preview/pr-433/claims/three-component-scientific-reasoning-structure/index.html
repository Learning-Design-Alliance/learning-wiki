Van de Sande 2019¶

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Exploratory factor analysis (Oblimin rotation) of the SRI administered to 269 sixth graders. The three factors explained "45.69% of the variance", with loadings of 0.41-0.87 across factors; factor scores were used in analyses.

"The results from the EFA indicated that three factors could be ex- tracted from the data. These three factors covered a ﬁnal set of 23 items in total, together explaining 45.69% of the variance."
