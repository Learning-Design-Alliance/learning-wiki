Evidence¶

Nick Naujoks 2022¶

Nick Naujoks, Bettina Harder, Marion Händel. (2022). Testing pays off twice: Potentials of practice tests and feedback regarding exam performance and judgment accuracy. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09295-x

q2 · i2 · associational · r2

Independent-samples t-test merging the Study 1 and Study 2 samples comparing self-reported post-processing of practice tests; the feedback group scored higher with a medium effect, d = 0.57.

"in Study 2, where students received individual corrective feedback, they post-processed the practice tests more intensely, which can be considered a medium effect, t(175) = 3.66, p < 0.001, d = 0.57."

Nick Naujoks 2022 (2)¶

Nick Naujoks, Bettina Harder, Marion Händel. (2022). Testing pays off twice: Potentials of practice tests and feedback regarding exam performance and judgment accuracy. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09295-x

q2 · i2 · associational · r2

Independent-samples t-test on the five-item perceived-usefulness-for-monitoring scale between the no-feedback and feedback samples; the difference was a medium effect, d = 0.55.

"students who received individual corrective feedback (Study 2) rated the usefulness of participating in the practice tests as higher for monitoring their performance than students without individual corrective feedback in Study 1, t(175) = –3.47, p < 0.001, d = 0.55."
