Evidence¶

Du P 2026¶

Du P, Zhou Y, Zhou Z, Wang H and Cao Q (2026). The synthetic position: generative artificial intelligence and the suspension of self-authorship in emerging adulthood. Front. Psychol. 17:1922087. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1922087

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Proposition 5, framed as the account's sharpest, most readily testable prediction. The article specifies an operationalization: coherence scored by external raters using established autobiographical-narrative coding; authorship registered concurrently through rated ownership and behavioral markers of endorsement under mild challenge.

"installing the synthetic position as a promoter can raise the coherence of a young person’s self-narrative while lowering their authorship of it, so that an instrument sensitive only to coherence records improvement where the model predicts impoverishment."
