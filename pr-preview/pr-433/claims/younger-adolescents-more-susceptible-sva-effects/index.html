Evidence¶

Ziyan Xu 2022¶

Ziyan Xu, Xinqiao Gao, Jun Wei, Huiqin Liu, & Yu Zhang. (2022). Adolescent user behaviors on short video application, cognitive functioning and academic performance. https://www.statista.com/statistics/1095186/tiktok-us-users-age/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Two-group nested model comparison in the SEM, testing whether each path coefficient differs between the 12-13 and 15-16 cohorts. The article reports the younger cohort's stronger negative effects and that the model accounted for 45.6% versus 4.6% of academic-performance variance.

"Generally, the younger cohort experience stronger negative influence of SVA on their cognitive functioning and academic performance than their older counterpart, as is testified by the two-group nested model comparison."
