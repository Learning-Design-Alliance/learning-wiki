Decision analysis reported from Rietveld et al. (SOM Table S27), comparing RCT designs with baseline covariates explaining 10% of outcome variance: "the investigator should be indifferent between the two options at q = 0.87", a "cost-saving equivalent to reducing the sample by 13%".
