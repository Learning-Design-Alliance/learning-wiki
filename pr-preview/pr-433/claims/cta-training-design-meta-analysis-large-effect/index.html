The article reports, citing Tofel-Grehl and Feldon (2013), a meta-analysis of 20 studies on CTA in training design with "Hedges ’ g = 0.871". The article presents this as grounds to expect similar benefits in a management context; the meta-analysis itself is second-hand.

"The large effect size (Hedges ’ g = 0.871) reported in a meta-analysis of 20 studies reviewing the use of CTA in training design indicates that we can expect to see similar bene ﬁts in a management context"
