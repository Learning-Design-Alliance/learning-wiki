The review reports, citing Waugh & Brownell (2017), a laboratory finding on toddlers' helping reliability: "18-month-olds help on about half of laboratory trials." By comparison, the review states 30-month-olds are less likely to engage in unhelpful behaviors.

"For example, 18-month-olds help on about half of laboratory trials, often opting to play or observe instead of helping an adult (Waugh & Brownell, 2017)."
