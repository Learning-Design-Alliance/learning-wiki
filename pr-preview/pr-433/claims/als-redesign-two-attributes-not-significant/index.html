María-Jesús Martínez-Argüelles 2023¶

María-Jesús Martínez-Argüelles, Dolors Plana-Erta, Àngels Fitó-Bertran. (2023). Impact of using authentic online learning environments on students' perceived employability. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10171-3

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Mann–Whitney U comparison in the same quasi-experiment (Table 6): teacher support p = .417 and performance polished p = .365, both non-significant. The article reports "The mean for teaching support rose from M = 4.10 to M = 4.27" without significance; no effect sizes are printed.

"The mean for teaching support rose from M = 4.10 to M = 4.27, although it did not increase at a significant level. While the redesign did not aim to increase the already elevated level of teaching support, students did perceive an improvement in said level."
