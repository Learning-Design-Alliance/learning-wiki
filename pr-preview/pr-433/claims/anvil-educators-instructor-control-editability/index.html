q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative focus-group study with nine educators from the earlier expert evaluation, analysed by inductive thematic analysis of verbatim transcripts; Theme 2 captured control and low-effort customization, with multiple participants suggesting interactive refinement via natural language instructions.

"Educators consistently emphasized that adoption depends on editability and instructor control. A black-box generator that outputs only a finished video was seen as impractical: participants wanted to adjust objects, text, pacing, and terminology without needing to learnmanim."
