Oliver 2011¶

Oliver, R. M., Wehby, J. H., & Reschly, D. J. (2011). Teacher classroom management practices: Effects on disruptive or aggressive student behavior. https://www.sree.org. doi:10.4073/csr.2011.4

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Posthoc moderator analysis comparing the 7 studies using the Classroom Organization and Management Program (COMP) with other studies. COMP studies produced a statistically significant ES=.75 (p =.00), but the between-group comparison was non-significant; equivalence was not tested.

"Based on the random effects analysis, differences between mean effect sizes were not statistically significant for either ICC=.05 (Qbetween = .38, df = 1, p = .54) or ICC=.10 (Qbetween = .07, df = 1, p = .54)."
