Related Claims¶

  • Achievement emotion regulation strategies are related to, but distinct from, general emotion regulation strategies — related
  • Strategy interrelations: expression and suppression are negatively related, while social support seeking and expression are positively related across emotions — related
  • Exploratory structural equation modeling (ESEM) differentiates victimization components better than traditional CFA, which positively biases factor correlations — a broader claim this one bears on
  • Academic emotions show weak between-domain relations: the same emotion in different school subjects is only weakly correlated, even for similar domains — related
  • Bifactor-ESEM shows the highest fit among CFA, ESEM, bifactor-CFA and bifactor-ESEM models, supporting AITAQS as one general factor plus four specific dimensions. — related
