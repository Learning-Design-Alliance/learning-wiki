Evidence¶

Denton Carolyn A 2012¶

Denton Carolyn A. (2012). Response to Intervention for Reading Difficulties in the Primary Grades: Some Answers and Lingering Questions. Journal of Learning Disabilities. https://doi.org/10.1177/0022219412442155

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review states the four dimensions on which responsiveness methods differ (status vs. slope, direct criteria vs. discrepancy formulas, reading domains evaluated, and specific cut points) and concludes "there may be little or no overlap in the identified groups." The field has not agreed on a gold-standard performance level for adequate response.

"Approaches that differ on these dimensions are likely to identify different students as adequate and inadequate responders, and there may be little or no overlap in the identified groups (Barth et al., 2008; D. Fuchs et al., 2008)."
