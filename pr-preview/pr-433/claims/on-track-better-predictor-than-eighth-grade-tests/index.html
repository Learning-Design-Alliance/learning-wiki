Evidence¶

Allensworth 2005¶

Allensworth, E. M., & Easton, J. Q. (2005). The On-Track Indicator as a Predictor of High School Graduation. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/track-indicator-predictor-high-school-graduation

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Observational analysis of the fall 2000 CPS freshman cohort by eighth-grade achievement quartile, reported with Figure 3. On-track students in the bottom quartile graduated at 68 percent, while off-track students in the top quartile graduated at 37 percent; the report concludes the indicator "is a much better predictor of graduation than eighth-grade test scores."

"More than 40 percent of the freshmen entering ninth grade in fall 2000 with low eighth-grade test scores (those in the bottom quarter of their class) were on-track at the end of their freshman year and 68 percent of these on-track students graduated four years later"
