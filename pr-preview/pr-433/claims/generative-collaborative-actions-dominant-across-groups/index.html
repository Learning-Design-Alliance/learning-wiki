Related Claims¶

  • Individual synthesis artifacts facilitated collaborative discourse by guiding the flow of group work and by supporting knowledge sharing, building shared understanding, and generating and advancing ideas — related
  • Groups performing generative epistemic actions more frequently produced higher-quality knowledge objects than a division-of-labor group — related
  • The three groups exhibited three distinct collaboration forms—a centralized model, loosely coordinated collaboration, and balanced and regulated collaboration—with group 3 showing the highest regulative-action percentage (40% during synthesis) — related
  • Engineering team focused on generative collaborative actions, product design team on creating shared understanding, and service design team on alleviating lack of knowledge — related
