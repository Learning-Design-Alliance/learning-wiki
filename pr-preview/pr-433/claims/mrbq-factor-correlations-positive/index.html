q2 · i? · associational · r1

Correlational analysis of the four PCA factors from the survey of 365 Flemish junior high students. The printed intercorrelations are r = .48 (Factors 3 and 2), r = .41 (Factors 3 and 1), and r = .38 (Factors 1 and 2), indicating "students holding a more social, dynamic view of mathematics" also value mathematics and feel confident about it.

"The correlations between the different factors indicate that students holding a more social, dynamic view of mathematics (Factor 3) attach more value to mathematics and have more confidence in their mathematical capacities (Factor 2) (r = .48)."
