Related Claims¶

  • Not passing a dual enrollment course is associated with an 11-12 percentage point reduction in college enrollment probability compared to passing, with a similar penalty across student groups — related
  • In OUSD dual enrollment courses, students most often identify the point person as their main source of support, at higher rates than the instructor, peers, or family — related
  • Students who enrolled in dual enrollment and did not pass are about 6 percentage points more likely to enroll in college than comparable students who never took advanced coursework — related
  • Students from socioeconomically disadvantaged backgrounds fail dual enrollment at higher rates than equally achieving peers, with the gap widening as achievement rises (the readiness illusion) — related
  • Automatic (opt-out) enrollment policies increase enrollment and success of students of color and low-income students in advanced courses, per research the brief cites — related
