Evidence¶

Arthur I. Gates 1921¶

Arthur I. Gates. (1921). An Experimental and Statistical Study of Reading and Reading Tests (Continued). The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0068367

q3 · i3 · associational · r2

Correlational survey of the Thorndike-McCall against other reading tests and intelligence composites (Table VIII). Printed correlations include r = 0.50 with single comprehension tests, r = 0.50 with vocabulary tests, and r = 0.57 with an oral reading test; the mean correlation with group intelligence composites is 0.69.

"It yields a correlation of 0.50 with almost any other single test of comprehension except the Brown, and a somewhat smaller coefficient with single measures of rate. It yields correlations of 0.50 with vocabulary tests and 0.57 with an oral reading test."
