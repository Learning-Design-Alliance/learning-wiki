Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Percentage of conditioned eyelid responses increases across conditioning sessions, from 7% (initial) to 25% (halfway) to 81% (final) — related
  • Conditioning brings eyelid EMG onset forward by roughly 150 ms, and IPn firing precedes the detected EMG change — related
  • IPn neurons show long-lasting error responses early in learning that are significantly reduced by the halfway session, with predictive US responses emerging in the final session — related
  • IPn firing decreases in spontaneous and late time windows and increases during CS and CS-US windows as conditioning progresses — related
  • Classical conditioning shifts the strongest IPn-EMG cross-correlations from negative to positive lags, improving IPn-eyelid synchrony
