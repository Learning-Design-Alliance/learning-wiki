Evidence¶

Paré 2017¶

Paré, D. & Quirk, G. J. (2017). When scientific paradigms lead to tunnel vision: lessons from the study of fear. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-017-0007-4

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The article reports Choi and Kim's foraging-task finding: rats await before foraging and show apprehension under control conditions, but "following amygdala inactivation, rats ignore the Robogator". The article attributes the drastic increase in risk taking to reproducing the Type-1 firing suppression needed to initiate foraging, not to reduced threat signaling.

"Choi and Kim observed that following amygdala inactivation, rats ignore the Robogator, as if they have become fearless"
