Evidence¶

Tina Seufert 2020¶

Tina Seufert. (2020). Building Bridges Between Self-Regulation and Cognitive Load—an Invitation for a Broad and Differentiated Attempt. Educational Psychology Review, 32, 1151–1162. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09574-6

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Baars, Wijnia, de Bruin, and Paas, a meta-analysis finding "an overall negative correlation between learner 's effort appraisals and their monitoring judgments." No correlation coefficient is printed in the review.

"In their meta-analysis, Baars et al. found an overall negative correlation between learner 's effort appraisals and their monitoring judgments, i.e., if a task needed high mental effort, it was more likely associated with poorer judgments of one 's performance."

Related Claims¶

  • Monitoring judgment timing moderates the correlation: concurrent judgments show a stronger negative association than prospective judgments — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Effort measurement type moderates the correlation: self-agency ratings show a positive association and subjective ratings a stronger negative one than objective measures — related
  • Effort and monitoring judgments show a medium negative correlation (r = −.355) during learning — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Goal-driven manipulations weaken the negative effort–monitoring correlation, which is nonsignificant under incentives, time pressure or self-agency ratings taken singly — related
  • In the multivariate meta-regression, goal-driven manipulation, judgment timing, and task type remain significant moderators — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Effort-rating item wording changes what learners reflect on: requirement wording correlates negatively and engagement wording positively with judgments of learning — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Training learners with an effort-and-performance algorithm via video modeling improved monitoring accuracy, task selection accuracy, and learning outcomes — related
  • Task type moderates the correlation: problem-solving tasks show a stronger negative association than word/paired-associate tasks — related
  • Effort ratings correlate negatively with judgments of learning (r = −0.355), but the correlation disappears under a goal-driven interpretation of effort — related
  • Perceived mental effort serves as a cue for monitoring judgments, which mediate the effort–outcome relation (David et al., 2024 meta-analysis) — possibly the same claim (merge candidate)
  • Mental effort and judgments of learning show a medium-sized negative correlation, moderated by regulation mode and task type — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Perceived mental effort has a negative indirect association with learning outcomes, mediated by monitoring judgments (β = -0.05) — related
  • Perceived mental effort is negatively associated with monitoring judgments (β = -0.19) in a meta-analytic structural equation model — related
  • Students' subjective appraisals of the remote inquiry lesson are comparable across conditions, and perceived cognitive demands correlate negatively with test scores — related
