Evidence¶

Atkinson 2000¶

Atkinson, R. K., Derry, S. J., Renkl, A., & Wortham, D. (2000). Learning from Examples: Instructional Principles from the Worked Examples Research. Review of Educational Research, 70(2), 181-214. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543070002181

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports, citing Reed and Bolstad, an experiment in which college students learned work-rate word problems across six conditions varying example format; 'students provided with both simple and complex examples outperformed all others on the entire posttest'.

"those students provided with both simple and complex examples outperformed all others on the entire posttest, including those with a single example, as well as those provided with an example plus procedures."
