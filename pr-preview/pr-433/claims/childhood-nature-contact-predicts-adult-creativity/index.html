The review reports this large cross-country correlational study (N = 783, 42 countries) in which "contact with nature in childhood predicted" adult creative imagination; the relationship was particularly pronounced for frequent childhood forest contact. No effect size is printed.

"Snell et al.'s (2020) study on 783 participants from 42 countries found contact with nature in childhood predicted higher levels of openness to experience, characterized by creative imagination and intellectual curiosity, in adulthood."
