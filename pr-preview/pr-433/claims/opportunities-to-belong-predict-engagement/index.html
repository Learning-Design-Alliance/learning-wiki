Harris-Thomas 2026¶

Harris-Thomas, B., Nicolai, K. D., Chen, X.-Y., & Areizaga Barbieri, C. (2026). Examining Black and Latine Students' Sense of Belonging within the Mathematics Classroom: A Narrative Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10181-0

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative review reporting Gray et al. (2024), a survey study of 255 sixth-grade students in a STEM-focused magnet middle school (44% Latine, 40% Black). The review reports the dose-like relation between perceived "opportunities to belong" and engagement. No effect sizes printed.

"The more students perceived their teacher to offer opportunities to belong, the more students reported engaging in class. In addition, students' overall ratings of opportunities to belong (across all teachers) predicted their overall engagement in school"
