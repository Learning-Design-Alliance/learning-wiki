Evidence¶

Girard 2021¶

Girard, C., Bastelica, T., Léone, J., Epinat-Duclos, J., Longo, L. & Prado, J. (2021). Nurturing the reading brain: home literacy practices are associated with children's neural response to printed words through vocabulary skills. npj Science of Learning 6:34. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00112-9

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Control analysis in the fMRI sample using a digit adaptation task (Arabic digits 1–8) replacing words; null results in all ROIs including the pIFs. The authors conclude effects "cannot be easily explained by low-level perceptual adaptation effects".

"There was no positive relation between digit adaptation and either frequency of home literacy practices or vocabulary in any of the seven ROIs (home literacy practices: all r's < 0.004, all p's > 0.493; vocabulary: all r's < 0.188, all p's > 0.117)"
