Evidence¶

Dupéré 2021¶

Dupéré, V., Dion, E., Cantin, S., Archambault, I., & Lacourse, E. (2021). Social contagion and high school dropout: The role of friends, romantic partners, and siblings. Journal of Educational Psychology, 113, 572-584. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000484

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Model 4 of the logistic regressions on cases and matched controls (n = 366) used a five-level recency variable. The article states "only recent (within the past year) occurrences of dropout in one's network predicted dropout among participants (OR = 3.11; 95% CI 1.78-6.27)"; no standardized effect size was printed.

"in Model 4, only recent (within the past year) occurrences of dropout in one's network predicted dropout among participants (OR = 3.11; 95% CI 1.78-6.27)"

Dupéré 2021 (2)¶

Dupéré, V., Dion, E., Cantin, S., Archambault, I., & Lacourse, E. (2021). Social contagion and high school dropout: The role of friends, romantic partners, and siblings. Journal of Educational Psychology, 113, 572-584. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000484

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Bivariate comparisons (Table 2) showed cases reported more recent network dropout, while "the proportions were not significantly different for occurrences of dropout dating back more than a year"; equivalence was not tested.

"dropout cases reported more occurrences of recent (within the past year) dropout in their network than matched or contrasted controls. In contrast, the proportions were not significantly different for occurrences of dropout dating back more than a year"
