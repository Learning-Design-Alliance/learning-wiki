Hernández 2026¶

Hernández, L., & Germain, E. (2026). Community schools in rural California: Leveraging shared resources in West Kern County. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/643.620

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Aggregated Ed-Data chronic absence figures (Figure 3) reported in the Chronic Absence section. The report describes the trend descriptively; no inferential test or effect size is printed.

"since peaking at 29% during the 2021–22 school year, chronic absences dropped 9 percentage points in 2 years across all schools in the consortium"

Hernández 2026 (2)¶

Hernández, L., & Germain, E. (2026). Community schools in rural California: Leveraging shared resources in West Kern County. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/643.620

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Disaggregated district data from the same Chronic Absence section. The report also notes Taft Union High went from 44.5% in 2021–22 to 23.4% two years later, and Maple had the lowest rate among all Kern County schools at 7.4% in 2022–23.

"Elk Hills, which saw a significant uptick in its chronic absence rate in 2022–23, when it exceeded the county average, has reduced its rate to 9.7%—a 68% reduction from the previous year and lower than its 2018–19 prepandemic rate"
