Evidence¶

Arthur S. Otis 1917¶

Arthur S. Otis. (1917). An Absolute Point Scale for the Group Measurement of Intelligence.—Part II. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0074753

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Graphical analysis (Fig. 5) of the 16 problems of Arithmetic Test I for 121 individuals ranked by total point score, with passes counted in overlapping groups of 30. The author reports "the diagnostic value of Problem 8 appears to be the best" and that, excepting Problems 6 and 7, diagnostic values seem satisfactory.

"The problems may now be ranked in diagnostic value according to the slope of the curve at the 50% point. Thus it will be seen that the diagnostic value of Problem 8 appears to be the best."
