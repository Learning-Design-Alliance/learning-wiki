Hingle, A., & Johri, A. (2025). A Guided Inquiry Approach to Students Co-Designing Generative AI Course Policies. https://www.google.com/search?q=A+Guided+Inquiry+Approach+to+Students+Co-Designing+Generative+AI+Course+Policies doi:10.1108/aiie-05-2025-0123

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Hybrid inductive-deductive thematic analysis (Cohen's Kappa .84) of individual policy assignments and group discussions from 55 consenting undergraduates in a technology-ethics course, organized around the ED-AI Lit Framework. The study "showed that participants prioritized training for students and instructors, standardized procedures for disclosing AI use, and stronger institutional support."
