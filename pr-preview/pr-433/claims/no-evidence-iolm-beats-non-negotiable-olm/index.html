Vania Dimitrova & Paul Brna 2016¶

Vania Dimitrova & Paul Brna. (2016). From Interactive Open Learner Modelling to Intelligent Mentoring: STyLE-OLM and Beyond. Int J Artif Intell Educ 26:332–349. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0087-3

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Authors' own statement of a gap in the evidence base, made after reporting the SAIC controlled study. The article offers no comparative data on this question; it is an acknowledged open question rather than a tested finding.

"Despite this controlled study, we have not examined empirical evidence showing that an interactive open learner model leads to better learning, compared say to an open learner model that is not negotiable, or to not having an open learner model at all."
