Evidence¶

Olga C. Santos 2016¶

Olga C. Santos. (2016). Training the Body: The Potential of AIED to Support Personalized Motor Skills Learning. Int J Artif Intell Educ 26:730–755. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-016-0103-2

q2 · i? · review · r2

The author's narrative review of IJAIED papers on procedural learning found that "no specific IJAIED papers addressing motor skills personalized learning were found in the review", attributing the gap to past limitations in low-cost, low-intrusion movement detection.

"Limited attention to physical and motor aspects of learning has been carried out in the AIED community so far. This is likely due to the lack of appropriate technological support available in the past for movement detection at low cost and with low intrusion."
