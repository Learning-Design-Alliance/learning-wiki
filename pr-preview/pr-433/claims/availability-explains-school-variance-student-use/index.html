Holly M. Hart 2002¶

Holly M. Hart, Elaine Allensworth, Douglas L. Lauen, Robert M. Gladden. (2002). Educational Technology: Availability and Use in Chicago's Public Schools. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/educational-technology-its-availability-and-use-chicagos-public-schools

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Quantitative analysis of district survey and administrative data reports that availability accounts for "about 45 percent of the school variance" in student use; the brief also states "Small improvements in computer availability are associated with substantial increases in student use, especially in schools with limited access." No standardized effect size is printed.

"Computer availability explains about 45 percent of the school variance in student computer use beyond schools' demographic characteristics and student-level characteristics alone."
