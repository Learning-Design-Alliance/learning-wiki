Evidence¶

Christa Cody 2021¶

Christa Cody, Mehak Maniktala, Nicholas Lytle, Min Chi, Tiffany Barnes. (2021). The Impact of Looking Further Ahead: A Comparison of Two Data-driven Unsolicited Hint Types on Performance in an Intelligent Data-driven Logic Tutor. https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.05741

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Hint metrics during training for 114 students showed Next-Step hints justified at 89%(7) versus Waypoints at 84%(12), and adopted at 83%(10) versus 74%(17). This provides evidence supporting H3 that Waypoint hints are harder to derive.

"The Next-Step group justiﬁed a signiﬁcantly ( F (2, 111) = 12 .96, p < 0.01) higher percentage of hints, as shown by the Justiﬁcation Rate. Additionally, of the justiﬁed hints, we also saw a signiﬁcantly ( F (2, 111) = 5 .49, p = 0 .01) lower Adoption Rate of the WP hints in students’ ﬁnal proofs"
