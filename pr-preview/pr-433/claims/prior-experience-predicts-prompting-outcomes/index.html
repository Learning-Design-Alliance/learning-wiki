Keith Tran 2026¶

Keith Tran, Samiha Marwan, and Thomas Price. (2026). Teaching Prompt-Based Programming with LLMs: A 45-Minute Lesson with Guided Practice for End-User Programmers. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30547 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.30547

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Covariate analysis within the same randomized controlled study's linear mixed-effects models; experience was a composite of prior courses, programming frequency, and self-assessed comfort, and was also a significant self-efficacy predictor consistent with prior work.

"Prior Programming Experience was a significant predictor of performance (𝐵 = 0.563, 95% CI[0.085, 1.042],𝑝 = 0.021), estimating that each standard deviation of additional experience corresponded to 0.56 more problems solved."
