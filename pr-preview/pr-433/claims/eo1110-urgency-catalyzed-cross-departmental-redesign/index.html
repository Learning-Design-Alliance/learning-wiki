Bracco 2019¶

Bracco, K. R., Schrager, C., Calisi, G., Gutierrez, P., Salciccioli, M., & Finkelstein, N. (2019). College-ready in the California State University system: Campus experiences implementing EO 1110. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://www.calstate.edu/eo/EO-1110.html

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Interviewee-perspective finding from the implementation-process section of the nine-campus qualitative study. Some campuses reported EO 1110 catalyzed new committee structures for cross-department curricular alignment.

"Executive Order 1110 helped create a sense of urgency that catalyzed important cross‑departmental curriculum redesign efforts."

Bracco 2019 (2)¶

Bracco, K. R., Schrager, C., Calisi, G., Gutierrez, P., Salciccioli, M., & Finkelstein, N. (2019). College-ready in the California State University system: Campus experiences implementing EO 1110. San Francisco, CA: WestEd. https://www.calstate.edu/eo/EO-1110.html

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Campus interviewee feedback on the implementation process. The executive summary reports stakeholders "said they want to be engaged early in the development of initiatives to build on local expertise and context."

"The Chancellor’s Office could have done more to acknowledge local expertise and build on successes already underway on the campuses."
