q1 · i? · qualitative · r1

Practitioner report of an open-ended final project in a 700-student systems programming course, graded on a written document explaining how specifications were met, with a build script and demo video. The instructor attributed rising in-person test failure to self-deception about learning, not cheating.

"Failure rates rose on the course's in-person tests, and the instructor's reading was not that students were cheating but that they “deluded themselves into thinking they were learning when they weren’t.”"
