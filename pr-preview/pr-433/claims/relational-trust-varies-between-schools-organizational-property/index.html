Bryk 1996¶

Bryk, A. S., & Schneider, B. (1996). Social Trust: A Moral Resource for School Improvement. University of Chicago, Center for School Improvement (draft). https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/social-trust-moral-resource-school-improvement

q3 · i? · associational · r?

Three-level hierarchical linear model analysis of 1994 Chicago elementary teacher survey responses decomposing variance into measurement error, within-school, and between-school components. The article reports "the proportion of variance between schools is about 3094" (printed as such, evidently 30%), with teacher-parent trust highest at 34%.

"In general, the proportion of variance between schools is about 3094, with the greatest between-school variance, 34%, occurring for teacher-parent trust."
