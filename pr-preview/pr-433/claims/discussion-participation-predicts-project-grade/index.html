McManus 2015¶

McManus, M. M., & Aiken, R. M. (2015). Supporting Effective Collaboration: Using a Rearview Mirror to Look Forward. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 26, 365–377. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0068-6

Narrative review of Yoo and Kim's predictive analysis of an online asynchronous discussion board in a computer operating systems course, combining quantitative entry counts and qualitative emotion coding correlated with domain understanding. The review reports that 'students who answered other students' questions more frequently received a better grade for the team project.'
