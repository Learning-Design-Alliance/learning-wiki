Evidence¶

Kestin 2025¶

Kestin, G., Miller, K., Klales, A., Milbourne, T. & Ponti, G. (2025). AI tutoring outperforms in-class active learning: an RCT introducing a novel research-based design in an authentic educational setting. Scientific Reports 15:17458. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-97652-6

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Crossover RCT in an undergraduate physics course: students took a pre-test, experienced one lesson via AI tutor and one via in-class active learning, then post-tests. The AI group's "higher median (M) post-score" was 4.5 versus 3.5 in-class.

"Students in the AI group exhibited a higher median (M) post-score (M = 4.5, N = 142) compared to those in the in-class active learning group (M = 3.5, N = 174)."
