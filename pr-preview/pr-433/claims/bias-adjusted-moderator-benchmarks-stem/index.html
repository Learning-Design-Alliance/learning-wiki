q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Benchmarking synthesis of 1,786 absolute g values from 89 meta-analyses (distribution right-skewed, mean 0.59, median 0.53) contrasted with 10,000 posterior-predictive draws from RoBMA-PSMA (Monte Carlo SE = 0.003).

"The observed data yielded quartiles of 0.29, 0.53, and 0.77, whereas the fully adjusted posterior- predictive distribution shifted downward to 0.18, 0.38, and 0.64."
