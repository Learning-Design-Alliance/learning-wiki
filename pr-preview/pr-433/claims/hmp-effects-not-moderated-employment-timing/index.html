Hirshberg Matthew J. 2022¶

Hirshberg Matthew J., Frye Corrina, Dahl Cortland J., Riordan Kevin M., Vack Nathan J., Sachs Jane, Goldman Robin, Davidson Richard J., Goldberg Simon B. (2022). A Randomized Controlled Trial of a Smartphone-Based Well-Being Training in Public School System Employees During the COVID-19 Pandemic. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000739

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Exploratory moderation analyses (not preregistered) on the N=662 intention-to-treat sample. Both moderator tests were null with "all ps>.05 uncorrected"; no effect sizes were printed.

"Employment (teacher/other) category did not moderate treatment effect on the primary or any secondary outcome (all ps>.05 uncorrected). Similarly, outcomes did not differ between participants who completed the entire intervention over the summer or finished the intervention during the new school year (ps>.05 uncorrected)"
