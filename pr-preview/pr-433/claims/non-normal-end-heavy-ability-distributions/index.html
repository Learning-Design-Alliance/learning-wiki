Evidence¶

Chambers 1909¶

Chambers, W. G. (1909). Individual Differences in Grammar Grade Children: A Comparative Study of Forty-Four Seventh and Eight Grade Pupils. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0070330

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Distribution analysis dividing each test's range into twelve ranks and plotting combined frequency surfaces (Fig. I) against a small normal curve. The article reports the "end-heaviness" and attributes it to a selective agency eliminating part of the group.

"This "end-heaviness" indicates that some selective agency has been at work and has eliminated the portion of the group which would have made the surface symmetrical"
