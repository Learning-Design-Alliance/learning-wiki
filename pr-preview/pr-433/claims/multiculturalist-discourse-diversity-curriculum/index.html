The review's analysis of the multiculturalist version, spanning difference to transfigurative multiculturalism, reports that "Multiculturalists argue that, in a democratic context, diversity and plurality—even if they undermine freedom—must be protected (Kessel, 2015)," and recommends curricula reflecting different cultures' histories and aspirations, learning in native languages, and developing intercultural, translation, or dialogue competency.
