Wilcoxon signed-rank subgroup analyses by district type and school level in the 50-participant PD evaluation. The quote reports the significant suburban result and non-significant urban and rural results; the article separately reports no significant increase for elementary, middle, or high school levels.

"participants from suburban areas increased their knowledge significantly (V = 25.50, P = 0.005 < 0.05), while those from urban (V = 30.50, P = 0.309 > 0.05) and rural areas (V = 34.00, P = 0.964 > 0.05) did not."
