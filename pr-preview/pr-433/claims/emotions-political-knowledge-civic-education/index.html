Evidence¶

Graf 2023¶

Graf, E., Stempfer, L., Muis, K. R., & Goetz, T. (2023). Classroom emotions in civic education: A multilevel approach to antecedents and effects. Draft version 1.0, University of Vienna / McGill University (not peer-reviewed preprint). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2023.101869

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Multilevel mediation models on knowledge (N = 1,117, 90 classes) with random slopes. The article reports the within-level paths above and states that "the effect of classroom climate on political knowledge was not significantly mediated via emotions" (Table 6 indirect effects with confidence intervals including zero).

"enjoyment related positively to political knowledge (R2w = .147), and all negative emotions (shame: R2w = .197, anxiety: R2w = .211, and boredom: R2w = .159) revealed a negative path to political knowledge at the within-level"
