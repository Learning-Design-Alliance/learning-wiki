Evidence¶

Magdalena Novak & Stephan Schwan 2021¶

Magdalena Novak & Stephan Schwan. (2021). Does Touching Real Objects Affect Learning? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09551-z

q3 · i3 · causal · r?

Three-way mixed ANOVA (vision × haptics × showroom) on PANAS negative affect showed "a significant main effect for factor showroom" with ηp² = 0.48; negative affect rose from before the exhibition (e.g., M = 1.21–1.43) to after showroom 1 (M = 1.81–2.23). No significant main effects of vision or haptics were found.
