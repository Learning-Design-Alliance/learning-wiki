Pain-point identification during the July 2025 in-person co-design meeting at CICS, reported by the case study authors from participant discussion. Participants noted "the core pain points were time and complexity," including platform overwhelm, data silos, and SEL integration gaps.

"Educators spent 2-3 hours building a single student intervention plan because data and tools were fragmented, which made intervention planning slow, inconsistent, and hard to act on."
