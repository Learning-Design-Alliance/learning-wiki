Evidence¶

Thomas 2026¶

Thomas, D.R., Borchers, C., Vanacore, K.P., Koedinger, K.R., & Kizilcec, R.F. (2026). Modernizing Ground Truth: Four Shifts Toward Improving Reliability and Validity in AI in Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29141 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2603.29141

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The article reports, citing prior work, that multimodal math models "excel at rote arithmetic (κ= 0.90)" but "fail on conceptual illustrations (κ≈0.47)". Table 1 similarly lists κ = 0.90 (Arithmetic) and κ ≈ 0.47 (Illustrations) for multimodal LLMs grading handwritten work.

"in multimodal math, models excel at rote arithmetic (κ= 0.90) but fail on conceptual illustrations (κ≈0.47), showing technical skill does not equal pedagogical "tacit knowledge" [17]"
