Pecuchova 2025¶

Pecuchova, J., Benko, Ľ., & Drlik, M. (2025). Automated Grading of Open-Ended Questions in Higher Education Using GenAI Models. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00517-2

q1 · i? · design · r2

Modeling phase design iteration: after reference-based prompts underperformed, a contextual prompt evaluating answers "without relying on external references" was developed and improved assessment.

"The introduction of the contextual evaluation prompt marked a significant improvement in the model’s ability to accurately assess open-ended student responses."
