Discussion-section recommendation, offered as a possibility ("may help") rather than a tested intervention: engaging students with scholarship on active learning's challenges may support critical reflection on discomfort. No effect size is printed.

"This understanding can help us to anticipate and be proactive about student resistance to AL. For example, incorporating and explicitly engaging students with scholarship around the challenges of active learning into courses like ours—as opposed to treating AL unproblematically—may help students critically reflect on their own feelings of discomfort and resistance."
