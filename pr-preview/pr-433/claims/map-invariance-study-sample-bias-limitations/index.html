q1 · i? · associational · r2

The authors' own stated limitation in the discussion: complete five-wave participation was required, so "students who missed one test were excluded from the sample". No effect size is printed; this is an author interpretation, not a tested result.

"However, since the study was based on longitudinal data, students who missed one test were excluded from the sample, which might introduce sample bias."

Wang 2013 (2)¶

Wang, S., Jiao, H. and Zhang, L. (2013) 'Validation of longitudinal achievement constructs of vertically scaled computerised adaptive tests: a multiple-indicator, latent-growth modelling approach', Int. J. Quantitative Research in Education, Vol. 1, No. 4, pp.383–407. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/validation-of-longitudinal-achievement-constructs-of-vertically-scaled-computerised-adaptive-tests-a-multiple-indicator-latent-growth-modelling-approach/. doi:10.1504/ijqre.2013.058307

q1 · i? · associational · r2

Stated limitation: the pooled state samples were heterogeneous by gender and ethnicity, and the article states the impact on model parameter estimates is unknown. A cited prior study found statistically significant different rates of change by gender. No effect size is printed.

"All samples used in this study are not homogeneous with respect to at least gender and ethnicity, and the impact of heterogeneity on model parameter estimates is unknown."
