q2 · i? · associational · r2

Step 5 of the LTA regressed post-test AI literacy on transition classes controlling for pre-test AI literacy (Table 9). The quote reports the overall pattern; Table 9 shows significant changes for Developing no-transition (d = 0.23), Developing-to-Self-Determined (d = 0.27), and Self-Determined no-transition (d = 0.30) paths.

"students who remained in or transitioned to the Self-Determined profile demonstrated greater improvements in AI literacy compared with students who remained in lower motivational profiles."
