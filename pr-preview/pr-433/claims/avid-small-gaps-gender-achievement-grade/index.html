q2 · i? · associational · r?

Summary of measures by student type from the 2007 CPS AVID survey, comparing standardized measure scores across ITBS achievement categories, gender, and grade. The report states "Girls are generally more positive about AVID than boys, particularly about teacher support and the skills they have learned through AVID", that lower-achieving students are generally more positive, particularly about skills, and that tenth graders are more positive than ninth graders, particularly on the postsecondary measure.
