Evidence¶

Freshman Year: The Make-it or Break-it Year 2007¶

Freshman Year: The Make-it or Break-it Year. (2007). Consortium on Chicago School Research at the University of Chicago. http://ccsr.uchicago.edu

q2 · i? · associational · r?

An observational analysis of Chicago freshman attendance and course-failure records reports that "Freshmen who miss more than two weeks of school flunk, on average, at least two classes" regardless of incoming test scores. The brief adds that high-scoring freshmen missing two weeks per semester are more likely to fail a course than low-scoring freshmen missing just one week.

"Freshmen who miss more than two weeks of school flunk, on average, at least two classes—no matter whether they arrive at high school with top test scores or below-average scores."
