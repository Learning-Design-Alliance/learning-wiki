Evidence¶

Sora Chi-Fang Huang 2026¶

Sora Chi-Fang Huang, Zhi-Hong Chen, Yu-Tzu Lin. (2026). Enhancing inhibition ability through situational training games: effectiveness, motivation and experience. Education Tech Research Dev 74. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10593-3

q3 · i2 · causal · r1

ANCOVA on Trail Making Test post-test scores with 38 college students, game type as between-group variable and pre-test as covariate. The between-group comparison showed a medium effect size, η2ₚ = .068 (F (1, 37) = 5.35, p = .024).

"After using the situational training games, participants’ inhibition ability was significantly higher than after using casual games, with an average improvement ranging from 0.39 to 0.51, F (1, 37) = 5.35, p = .024, η2ₚ = .068, observed power = .64, indicating a medium effect size."
