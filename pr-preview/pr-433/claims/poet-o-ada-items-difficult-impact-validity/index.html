Jia 2025¶

Jia, Y., Spagnolo, A. B., Barrett, N., Murphy, A. A., Basto, P. M., Rothpletz-Puglia, P., & Luther, S. (2025). Validation of a peer observation and evaluation tool for online teaching in the U.S. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10428-z

q2 · i? · design · r2

In the MFRM analysis, items 14, 15, and 16 (ADA Compliance/Accessibility) grouped as a separate construct outside the ±0.4 loading range. The authors report these "were the most difficult questions and impacted the construct validity."

"The ADA Accessibility items were the most difficult questions and impacted the construct validity (e.g., unidimensionality) of the instrument. Also, faculty in this pilot study reported lack of expertise in rating the ADA Accessibility items."
