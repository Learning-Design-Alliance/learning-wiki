Evidence¶

Lujain Ibrahim 2026¶

Lujain Ibrahim, Franziska Sofia Hafner, Myra Cheng, Cinoo Lee, Rebecca Anselmetti, Robb Willer, Luc Rocher, Diyi Yang. (2026). Sycophantic AI makes human interaction feel more effortful and less satisfying over time. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07912 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.07912

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Pre-post outcomes in the longitudinal study (N=1364) analyzed by regressing post-treatment scores on condition and baseline. Feeling understood by AI was "the only measure that increased over time"; affect and certainty remained flat or slightly declined. Self-enhancement showed only a marginal tendency versus specific people (d=0.11, p=0.080).

"Compared to the neutral condition, the sycophantic condition showed no statistically significant effect on feeling understood by other humans (d=−0.08, p= 0.295) or intellectual humility (d= 0.01,p= 0.861)."
