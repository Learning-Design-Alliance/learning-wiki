Li 2025¶

Li, H., Li, J., Hao, X., & Liu, W. (2025). Behavioral and eye-tracking investigation of event segmentation following short video watching. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00378-3

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Robustness re-analysis of Study 1 eye-tracking data after correcting for low-level features. The Group effect on boundary ISC remained significant (F = 5.04, p = 0.003, ηp² = 0.131), and the HMM K-value group effect also held (F = 3.49, p = 0.018, ηp² = 0.095), with Short-Random showing more fragmented segmentation.

"the Short-R group exhibited signiﬁcantly lower boundary ISC compared to the Short-P group (mean difference =−0.025, 95% CI [ −0.045, −0.006], p = 0.006, Cohen’sd = −0.93, 95% CI [ −1.69, −0.16])."
