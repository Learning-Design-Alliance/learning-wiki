Evidence¶

Girard 2021¶

Girard, C., Bastelica, T., Léone, J., Epinat-Duclos, J., Longo, L. & Prado, J. (2021). Nurturing the reading brain: home literacy practices are associated with children's neural response to printed words through vocabulary skills. npj Science of Learning 6:34. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00112-9

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Whole-brain and ROI analyses in the fMRI sample of 44 children replicated the left-lateralized word adaptation network (inferior frontal, temporal, occipital cortices) found in English-speaking adults, though with less extensive activation in left precentral gyrus and left parietal cortex.

"Averaging across all children from the fMRI sample ( n = 44), word adaptation effects were signiﬁcant after Bonferroni correction for multiple comparisons in three of these ROIs: the pIFs ( t(43) = 2.698, pcorr = 0.035, d = 0.407), pdSTS ( t(43) = 3.794, pcorr = 0.002, d = 0.572)"
