Margaret Ellis 2026¶

Margaret Ellis, Nikitha Donekal Chandrashekar, Sehrish Basir Nizamani, Mohammed Farghally, Jake O'Brien, and Naren Ramakrishnan. (2026). Demystify, Use, Reflect, Assess (DURA): An Experience Report on LLM Integration in CS2. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30908 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.30908

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative coding of Week 13 advice responses using 35 codes for recommendations for future help-seeking. Students also cautioned LLM use, e.g., to avoid AI doing large parts of the work. No effect size printed.

"Similar to Penney's findings [27], students predominantly recommended that future students use office hours and they commented on how helpful the TAs were."

Margaret Ellis 2026 (2)¶

Margaret Ellis, Nikitha Donekal Chandrashekar, Sehrish Basir Nizamani, Mohammed Farghally, Jake O'Brien, and Naren Ramakrishnan. (2026). Demystify, Use, Reflect, Assess (DURA): An Experience Report on LLM Integration in CS2. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30908

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Same Week 13 advice analysis: students also explicitly recommended trying problems on their own first or reviewing course materials, and said the main reason to seek assistance is to understand the material. Descriptive; no effect size.

"There was a noticeable trend to recommend starting assignments early in order to be able to get help and not be overwhelmed."
