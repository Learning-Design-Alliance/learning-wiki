Evidence¶

Kale 1955¶

Kale, S. V., and Grosslight, J. H. (1955). Exploratory Studies in the Use of Pictures and Sound for Teaching Foreign Language Vocabulary. Technical Report SDC 269-7-53, Instructional Film Research Program, Pennsylvania State University. Abstracted by L. Twyford (1956), AV Communication Review. doi:10.1007/bf02718464

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Conclusion of two experimental studies in which Russian words were taught to college students by five film-based methods, with performance measured by ability to write the words; the report concludes "pictures of an object or act are an aid to learning".

"It was concluded that for teaching a foreign vocabulary in which the criterion of learning is the ability to write the foreign words thar (a) pictures of an object or act are an aid to learning"
