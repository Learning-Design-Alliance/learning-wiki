Wise 2019¶

Wise, S. (2019). The Relationship Between Test-Taking Disengagement and Performance on MAP Growth Retests. (The Collaborative for Student Growth at NWEA Working Paper). https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/the-relationship-between-test-taking-disengagement-and-performance-on-map-growth-retests/

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive finding from the same-day retest analysis: engagement levels were cross-tabulated into 16 initial-test-by-retest combinations, and mean RIT differences were computed for each and plotted in Figures 1-4. Each of the four tests showed a highly similar pattern, though the magnitude of differences varied across test types.

"Test-taking disengagement was found to frequently differ between the initial test and retest. For each of the 16 disengagement combinations, mean differences between the two sets of RIT scores were calculated. Figures 1-4 show the mean RIT differences for the different combinations."
