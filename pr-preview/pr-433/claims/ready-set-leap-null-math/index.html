What Works Clearinghouse 2008¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2008). WWC Intervention Report: Ready, Set, Leap!®. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/430

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Math findings from one RCT (21 schools). Appendix A3.5 prints effect sizes of 0.04 (WJ III Applied Problems), –0.24 (CMA-A Composite), and 0.08 (Shape Composition), all ns after WWC corrections; domain average –0.04, improvement index –2.

"PCER Consortium (2008) showed that Ready, Set, Leap!® had a negative and statistically significant effect on math when measured using Building Blocks, Shape Composition task."
