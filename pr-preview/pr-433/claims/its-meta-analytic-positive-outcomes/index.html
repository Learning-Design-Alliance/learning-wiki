Evidence¶

Dahal N 2026¶

Dahal N, Hasan MK and Islam MT (2026). GenAI and AI tools for STEAM educators: evidence-informed strategies and resources for the future of teaching. Front. Educ. 11:1948064. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1948064

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review attributes this secondhand to Ma et al. (2014); the underlying meta-analysis was not charted and no effect size is printed in the review. VanLehn (2011) is cited for the claim that well-designed systems approach some benefits of human tutoring in structured domains.

"Meta-analytic evidence indicates that intelligent tutoring systems can yield positive learning outcomes compared with non-ITS conditions (Ma et al., 2014), while VanLehn (2011) argues that well-designed tutoring systems can approach some of the benefits of human tutoring in structured domains."

Kenneth R. Koedinger & Vincent Aleven 2016¶

Kenneth R. Koedinger & Vincent Aleven. (2016). An Interview Reflection on “Intelligent Tutoring Goes to School in the Big City”. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education 26, 13–24. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0082-8

q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

This is the interviewer's summary statement in the interview, attributing the confirmation to named meta-reviews; the article reports no pooled effect size itself, so impact is null and the claim is attributed exactly as the source attributes it.

"by now, many high-quality studies and meta-reviews (Steenbergen-Hu and Cooper 2013, 2014; Ma et al. 2014; VanLehn 2011; Pane et al. 2014; Kulik and Fletcher 2015) confirm the value of intelligent tutoring systems, perhaps with smaller effects than we might hope to see or that we have seen in some earlier studies."

  • Affective support in AutoTutor benefits low-knowledge learners but not high-knowledge learners, and gaze-based disengagement reactivity yields significant gains only on deep learning — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Apprentice learner models are domain-general: they learn expert models across eight tutor domains without specialized domain knowledge — related

  • AI-supported adaptive systems are reported to enhance adult learner engagement, motivation, and outcomes when aligned with learner goals and prior knowledge — related
