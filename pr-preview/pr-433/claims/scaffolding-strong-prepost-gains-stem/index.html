Evidence¶

Belland Brian R. 2017¶

Belland Brian R., Walker Andrew E., Kim Nam Ju. (2017). A Bayesian Network Meta-Analysis to Synthesize the Influence of Contexts of Scaffolding Use on Cognitive Outcomes in STEM Education. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654317723009

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Bayesian network meta-analysis of within-subject (pre–post) differences from scaffolding in 56 studies, with posterior distributions generated from 20,000 MCMC samples. The article reports that "Scaffolding has a consistently strong effect across student populations, STEM (science, technology, engineering, and mathematics) disciplines, and assessment levels."

"Scaffolding has a consistently strong effect across student populations, STEM (science, technology, engineering, and mathematics) disciplines, and assessment levels, and a strong effect when used with most problem-centered instructional models"
