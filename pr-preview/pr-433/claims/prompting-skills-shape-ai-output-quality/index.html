Arzu Akbaba 2026¶

Arzu Akbaba, Afra Calik Kus. (2026). Nursing students’ and faculty experiences with artificial intelligence in education: a qualitative study using the technology acceptance model. BMC Nursing. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12912-026-04986-1

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Qualitative interview finding; a faculty participant stated "having the skill to write the correct prompts" is the most important factor for effective results, and a student reported learning to ask the right questions over time yields more accurate answers.

"Participants stressed that to achieve meaningful, accurate outputs from AI, users must write effective prompts."
