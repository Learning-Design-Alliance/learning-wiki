Audio-Visual Communication Review 1953¶

Audio-Visual Communication Review. (1953). Audio-Visual Communication Review, 158. https://eric.ed.gov/

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Reported in the digest as finding (c), Medium Preference, from the interview study of the same boys. The digest attributes comics' decline to "the relative lack of appeal" of comics compared with movies and TV, not to situation of contact.

"Kindergarten, second and fourth grade children tend to prefer television, and sixth grade children tend to prefer movies. Comics drop out, apparently they lack the appeal of movies and TV, and the fact that they might be contacted in a preferred situation (alone) cannot overweigh the relative lack of appeal."
