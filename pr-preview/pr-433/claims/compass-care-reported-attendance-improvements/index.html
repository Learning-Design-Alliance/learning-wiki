Qualitative site-visit evidence from spring 2025; one school staff member stated kids are coming to school and our absentee rate has gone down, crediting trust built with an adult. These are reported perceptions, not school-record attendance data.

"Participation in Compass Care helped students improve their attendance and reduce tardiness. Throughout the program, Compass Care staff emphasized the importance and impact of attendance."
