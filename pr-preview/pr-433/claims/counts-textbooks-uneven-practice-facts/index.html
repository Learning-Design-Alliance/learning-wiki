Thorndike 1921¶

Thorndike, E. L. (1921). The psychology of drill in arithmetic: The amount of practice. The Journal of Educational Psychology, 12(4). https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/edu/12/4

q2 · i? · design · r2

Frequency counts of arithmetical bonds in recent textbooks A and B, Books I and II (Tables II–VII), covering addition, subtraction, multiplication and division bonds through grade VI. The article gives 2 + 2 used 226 times, 9 X 9 totaling 110, and subtraction bonds like 16 — 7 occurring only about 20–30 times.

"Tables II to VII show that even gifted authors make instruments for instruction in arithmetic which contain much less practice on certain elementary facts than teachers suppose; and which contain relatively much more practice on the more easily learned facts than on those which are harder to learn."
