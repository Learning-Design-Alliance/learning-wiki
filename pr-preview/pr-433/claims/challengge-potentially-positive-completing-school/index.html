Evidence¶

National Guard Youth ChalleNGe Program 2010¶

National Guard Youth ChalleNGe Program. (2010). WWC Intervention Report: National Guard Youth ChalleNGe Program. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/329

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Randomized controlled trial by Millenky, Bloom, and Dillon (2010) in 10 sites across 10 states; the analysis sample included 1,196 youths who completed the 21-month follow-up survey. The WWC confirmed "a statistically significant positive difference between the National Guard Youth ChalleNGe Program group and the control group."

"At follow-up, 61% of program group members reported having a diploma or GED, compared with 36% of control group members."

National Guard Youth ChalleNGe Program 2010 (2)¶

National Guard Youth ChalleNGe Program. (2010). WWC Intervention Report: National Guard Youth ChalleNGe Program. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/329

q3 · i? · causal · r2

WWC-calculated improvement index for the completing school domain, representing the percentile-rank difference between the average intervention and comparison student. The article reports the effect size was "large enough to be considered substantively important according to WWC criteria (at least 0.25)" but prints no exact effect size.

"The improvement index for completing school is +22 percen- tile points based on one finding in one study."
