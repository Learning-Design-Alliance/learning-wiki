H. Chad Lane 2026¶

H. Chad Lane. (2026). AIED's Unfinished Mission: Centering Agency and Motivation in the Age of Effortless Bypass. AIED 2026, CCIS 3032, pp. 502–516. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29791-4_38

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The paper reports, citing Taub et al.'s game-based learning study, that moderate agency beat both no agency and high agency on learning outcomes while maintaining "inter-est and engagement". No effect size is printed in this account.

"students given a moderate degree of agency in a game-based learning environment achieved better learning outcomes than students with either no agency or high agency, while still maintaining inter-est and engagement."
