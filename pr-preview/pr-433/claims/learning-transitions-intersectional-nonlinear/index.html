intersectionality-1¶

Page, K., Merritt Johnson, A., Franklin, K., Carter, B., Galindo, M., Solorzano, T., Lee, S., and Shah, Z., (2023, July). Learning Transition Design Principles for Learning and Employment Records: Co-designing for Equity. Digital Promise https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/185

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Inductive thematic analysis of 31 one-on-one interviews with HSE learners and workers, each sharing a significant transition story; dominant framing contexts included family, health or job change, financial circumstance, military retirement, cultural and race-related transitions, and geographic movement (Figure 2).

"One key learning from the 31 stories of transition was that all learning transitions are intersectional, dynamic, and nonlinear, with people often experiencing multiple changes at the same time."
