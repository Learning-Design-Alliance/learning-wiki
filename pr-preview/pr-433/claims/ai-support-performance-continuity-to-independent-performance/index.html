Evidence¶

Yueqiao Jin 2025¶

Yueqiao Jin, Kaixun Yang, Roberto Martinez-Maldonado, Dragan Gašević and Lixiang Yan. (2025). The Agency Gap in AI-Supported Writing: How Reactive and Proactive Agent Designs Shape Multimodal Reasoning. https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.04398 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2507.04398

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Ordinal regression results section reports AI-support score as a significant predictor for all six outcomes with printed coefficients (e.g., Critical Thinking b=3.57, z=4.67, p<.001; Composite b=0.61, z=5.03, p<.001); the quoted sentence gives the authors' interpretation.

"This continuity indicates that students who performed well with support also tended to perform well immediately afterwards, although it cannot distinguish learning from stable differences in writing competence."
