Evidence¶

Geneviève Allaire-Duquette 2021¶

Geneviève Allaire-Duquette, Lorie-Marlène Brault Foisy, Patrice Potvin, Martin Riopel, Marilyne Larose, Steve Masson. (2021). An fMRI study of scientists with a Ph.D. in physics confronted with naive ideas in science. npj Science of Learning 6:11. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00091-x

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Behavioral accuracy comparison between expertise domains in the statement-verification task of 25 Ph.D. physicists; the printed means were 88.4% (physics) and 83.0% (biology), and the authors note "the difference is small (5.4%)".

"physicists are more accurate in assessing physics statements than biology statements ( t(24) = 4.360, p < 0.001, d = 0.5"
