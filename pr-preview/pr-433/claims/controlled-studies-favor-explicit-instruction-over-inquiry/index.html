The commentary's synthesis of controlled studies (Hsu et al., Klahr & Nigam, Matlen & Klahr, Zhang, among others) comparing investigation-based teaching with explicit instruction, reporting that "learners receiving these forms of direct, explicit instruction demonstrated greater learning". No effect sizes are printed.
