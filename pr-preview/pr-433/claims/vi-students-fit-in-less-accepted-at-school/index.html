Evidence¶

Jessup 2018¶

Jessup, G; Bundy, AC; Broom, A; Hancock, N. (2018). Fitting in or feeling excluded: The experiences of high school students with visual impairments. https://doi.org/10.1177/0145482X1811200305

q3 · i? · associational · r1

In a seven-day experience-sampling study, twelve visually impaired high school students rated internal variables seven times daily across home, school, and other contexts; the study reports they "fitted in significantly less and felt significantly less accepted at school than elsewhere."
