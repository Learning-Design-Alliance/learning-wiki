q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Result of two-coder thematic analysis of focus group and interview transcripts from the four participating institutions, using Maxwell's qualitative methods including researcher memos and categorizing strategies. A qualitative taxonomy of themes; no effect sizes apply.

"we identified five key themes across all the rural micro-credentialing initiatives, including: 1) partnerships, 2) employer recognition, 3) program sustainability, 4) program appeal and 5) potential for learning/higher education attainment."

partnerships¶

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative finding from the Key Findings section: partnerships with colleges, community colleges, adult education, skills training, prison education programs, nonprofits, and government were seen as central to developing, enrolling, and sustaining the initiatives.

"The most prevalent theme that emerged was the importance of fostering and maintaining key partnerships in order to develop and sustain programming."
