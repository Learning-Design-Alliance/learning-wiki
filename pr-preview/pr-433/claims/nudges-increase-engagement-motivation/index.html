Lichand 2024¶

Lichand, G., Christen, J., & Van Egeraat, E. (2024). Neglecting students’ socio-emotional skills magniﬁed learning losses during the pandemic. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00235-9

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Treatment effects on student participation in school activities (administrative data) and motivation to return to school, measured via SMS surveys over the first 5 weeks after the intervention onset, reported in the Supplementary Materials and described in the Mechanisms section.

"While 7.21% of students in the control group had not followed remote learning activities right before the 2020 winter break, that ﬁgure was only 0.33% in the treatment group - an over 95% reduction"
