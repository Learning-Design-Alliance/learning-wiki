What Works Clearinghouse 2008¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2008). WWC Intervention Report: Ready, Set, Leap!®. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/430

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Phonological processing findings from two RCTs. Appendix A3.3 prints RMC effect sizes of 0.28, 0.20, and 0.15 (all ns) and PCER –0.09 (ns); the cross-study domain average is 0.06 with improvement index +2 percentile points.

"PCER Consortium (2008) reported no significant effect of Ready, Set, Leap!® as measured by the Preschool Comprehensive Test of Phonological and Print Processing, (Pre-CTOPPP) Elision subtest."
