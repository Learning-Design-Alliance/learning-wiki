Evidence¶

Northcutt 2015¶

Northcutt, C. G., Ho, A. D., & Chuang, I. L. (2015). Detecting and Preventing “Multiple-Account” Cheating in Massive Open Online Courses. arXiv preprint arXiv:1508.05699. doi:10.48550/arxiv.1508.05699

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Comparison across 37 STEM courses, 18 of which employed prevention mechanisms such as embargoed solutions or algorithmically generated items. The observed CAMEO rate was 0.1% in prevention courses versus 1.2% in courses with no or limited prevention.

"Table 2C shows that the CAMEO rate in courses that employed these preventive strategies in half or more of the assessment items was substantially lower (0.1%) than the rate in courses that did not employ preventive strategies (1.2%)."
