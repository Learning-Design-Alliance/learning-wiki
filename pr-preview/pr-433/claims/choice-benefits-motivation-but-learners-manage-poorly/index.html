Evidence¶

Garvin Brod 2023¶

Garvin Brod, Natalia Kucirkova, Joshua Shepherd, Dietsje Jolles, Inge Molenaar. (2023). Agency in Educational Technology: Interdisciplinary Perspectives and Implications for Learning Design. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09749-x

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports studies on declarative learning showing perceived control benefits engagement and test scores, citing Cordova and Lepper (1996), and notes that tricking people with perceived but not actual control likewise improved memory, citing Murty et al. (2015) and Schneider et al. (2018).

"Even if students can only control incidental aspects of the learning context, this benefits their engagement in learning and learning test scores (Cordova & Lepper, 1996)."

Garvin Brod 2023 (2)¶

Garvin Brod, Natalia Kucirkova, Joshua Shepherd, Dietsje Jolles, Inge Molenaar. (2023). Agency in Educational Technology: Interdisciplinary Perspectives and Implications for Learning Design. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09749-x

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review states there is little evidence for agency benefits in complex learning scenarios such as choosing which tasks to work on, and that students make ineffective choices such as selecting too easy tasks or ineffective strategies like rereading and underlining.

"a wealth of evidence suggests that most students do not know how to manage their learning effectively (e.g., Bjork, Dunlosky, & Kornell, 2013)."
