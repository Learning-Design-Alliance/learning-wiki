q2 · i? · design · r2

Instructor and staff notes plus survey themes from the KT1 course: pre-disclosed tutorial problems were copied "onto the board during the tutorials without engaging in the intended reasoning process," and many students found blackboard turns intimidating. HW1 revealed programming as an undeclared prerequisite.

"Disclosing the problems in advance gave students time to prepare AI- generated solutions, which some then copied onto the board during the tutorials without engaging in the intended reasoning process. In addition, staff notes and survey themes indicate that many students found taking their turn at the blackboard intimidating."
