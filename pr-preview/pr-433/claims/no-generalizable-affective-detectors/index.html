affective-detectors¶

Schellinger, A., & Roschelle, J. (2024, January). Looking Back to Move Forward. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/203

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

This is the article's report of Baker's (2019) LAK keynote challenge to the learning analytics community — an authorially reported claim, not a new study; no effect size or data are printed.

"One unsolved problem is creating generalizable "detectors" of students' affective states, such as boredom, while they use a DLP. Knowing when a student is bored with educational content in a DLP could allow for targeted interventions to improve their engagement and effort, and although affective detectors have been built for specific platforms, no generalizable approach exists to date."

Related Claims¶

  • No significant impact of frustration or boredom (or anxiousness, discouragement, distractedness) on practice performance was found — related
  • Enriched DLP data enable two types of theory testing: sustained observation of individuals in a single setting and modeling the same type of learner across contexts — related
  • EDM methods have enabled real-time inference of a broader range of student attributes, including gaming the system, self-efficacy, off-task behavior, boredom, and frustration — related
  • Student disengagement in adaptive learning platforms is linked to technical problems, boredom with long or irrelevant content, insufficient challenge and feedback, and declining engagement over time — related
  • Automated gaming-the-system detectors built with XGBoost reliably recognize gaming in both problem-solving and self-explanation steps — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Frustration at not understanding a concept is perceived to motivate exploration and persistence, while frustration attributed to teachers or curriculum impairs motivation — related
  • Self-efficacy interventions triggered by interaction-based detectors enhanced outcomes in one of two intervention experiments, with mixed overall results — related
