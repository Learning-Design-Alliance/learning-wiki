Evidence¶

Walkington 2025¶

Walkington, C., Beauchamp, T., Ikram, F., Koçyiğit Gürbüz, M., Xia, F., Lee, M., Lan, A. (2025). Mathematics Teachers’ Interactions with a Multi-Agent System for Personalized Problem Generation. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12066. doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29770-9_5

q2 · i? · design · r2

Open-ended feedback from 237 students on 47 personalized problems showed that almost twice as many responses described the topic as something the student did not like or did not engage with in their lives, compared to those who liked it. The authors note that what is authentic to one student may not be authentic to another.

"A total of 235 student responses (out of 422) were coded as commenting on the problem’s topic, and 83 of those responses were coded as the student saying the topic of the problem was something that they liked."
