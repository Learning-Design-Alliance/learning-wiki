Evidence¶

Donnelly 2011¶

Donnelly, D., McGarr, O. and O'Reilly, J. (2011). A Framework for Teachers' Integration of ICT Into Their Classroom Practice. Computers and Education;57(2), 1469-1483. https://hdl.handle.net/10344/2340

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Initial interview findings from seven science teachers and six educational stakeholders. Responses "centred on first-order barriers in terms of resources and students", covering sharing of resources, better organisation, time saving, alternative transmission, better explanations, modernity, critical thought and ICT overload.

"When asked what they viewed as ICT's role in meeting the challenges of teaching science, teachers' and stakeholders' responses to this question centred on first-order barriers in terms of resources and students."
