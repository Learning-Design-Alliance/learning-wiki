q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Finding from the barriers-and-supports analysis (Table 9): supports "were most eﬀective when they were deliberately matched to the constraints participants were experiencing." Supports were not interchangeable, and their presence did not guarantee full participation.

"Protected time addressed inconsistent participation, clear communication reduced uncertainty about purpose and roles, trusted relationships made constituent engagement more feasible, and ﬂexible structures helped teams navigate organizational constraints."
