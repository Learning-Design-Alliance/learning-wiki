Related Claims¶

  • Phenotypic and genetic associations with musicality are robust to genetic confounding via educational attainment — related
  • Polygenic scores for rhythmicity associate with school-age speech-related communication and verbal-cognition outcomes, with two associations passing multiple-testing correction and four at the nominal level — related
  • The association between preschool musicality and school-age speech-related communication is partially accounted for by polygenic scores for rhythmicity — related
  • Genetic variation shared with rhythmicity does not account for the association between preschool musicality and school-age verbal cognition — related
  • Preschool and school-age musicality measures predict school-age communication and social communication, explaining up to 6% of phenotypic variance, while infant nursery-rhyme measures explain less than 1% — related
  • Latent musicality correlates moderately with speech-related communication (r = 0.50) and modestly with cognition (r = 0.33), but not with pragmatic or social-communication factors
