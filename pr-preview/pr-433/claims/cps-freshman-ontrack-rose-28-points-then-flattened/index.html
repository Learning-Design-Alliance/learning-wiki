Nagaoka 2020¶

Nagaoka, J., Mahaffie, S., Usher, A., & Seeskin, A. (2020). The Educational Attainment of Chicago Public Schools Students: 2019. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://toandthrough.uchicago.edu/tool/cps/2019/#/milestones

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Figure 6 takeaway from this descriptive report tracking Freshman OnTrack rates for first-time ninth-graders from 2007 to 2019. The report notes an increase of 28 percentage points compared to the 2007 cohort's 61 percent rate.

"Of the 2019 first-time CPS ninth-graders, 89 percent were on-track to graduate, a rate that has held steady since 2015. The Freshman OnTrack rate for CPS is higher than the rate for the state of Illinois, which is 87 percent."

Nagaoka 2020 (2)¶

Nagaoka, J., Mahaffie, S., Usher, A., & Seeskin, A. (2020). The Educational Attainment of Chicago Public Schools Students: 2019. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://toandthrough.uchicago.edu/tool/cps/2019/#/milestones

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Table 2 takeaway disaggregating 2007 and 2019 Freshman OnTrack rates by race/ethnicity and gender. Latino young men and Black young women each increased 27 percentage points between 2007 and 2019.

"In 2007, fewer than half of Black young men were on-track; they had the largest increase in their Freshman OnTrack rate, 36 percentage points, going from 48 percent to 84 percent."
