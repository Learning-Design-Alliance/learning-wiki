Evidence¶

Putwain 2021¶

Putwain, D. W., Nicholson, L. J., & Kutuk, G. (2021). Warning Students of the Consequences of Examination Failure: An Effective Strategy for Promoting Student Engagement? https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1329876

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Between-level ML-SEM paths using class-aggregated fear appeal frequency (ICC2 = .80 and .86). The article states this finding "runs contrary to the theoretical proposition that fear appeals prompt evaluations" and previous studies showing positive concurrent relations.

"T1 fear appeal frequency did not predict T2 challenge (β = .13, p = .45) or T2 threat (β = .05, p = .63) evaluation, over and above the variance accounted for by autoregressive relations"
