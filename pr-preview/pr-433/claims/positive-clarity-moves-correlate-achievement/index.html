Related Claims¶

  • A clear variable coordinate lesson produced higher achievement than an unclear lesson with the same concept structure on all five measures — the general claim this finding bears on, with the studies behind it
  • Clarity and concept structure interact for defining and applying concepts, each accounting for about 40-44% of variance — related
  • Instructional clarity is a significant main effect on concept achievement, accounting for roughly half of score variance across measures — related
  • Students accurately perceive differences between clear and unclear lessons regardless of concept structure — related
  • Clarity, task orientation, time on task, criterion-material learning, and structuring correlate positively with learning — a broader claim this one bears on
  • University students most often named teaching practices focused on interaction and relationships and on clarity as the best they had experienced — related
