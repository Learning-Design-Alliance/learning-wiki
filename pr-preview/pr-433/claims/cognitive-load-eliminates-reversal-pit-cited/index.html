Andreas B. Eder & Vanessa Mitschke. (2025). Pavlovian to instrumental transfer of control over fight or flight decisions. npj Science of Learning 10:34. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00331-4

Narrative review section of this article attributing the finding to Seabrooke and colleagues: specific PIT appeared under standard instructions even with high cognitive load, whereas the reversal instruction reversed PIT only without load. No effect size is printed for this cited finding, so no magnitude is reported.

"high cognitive load eliminated the instruction-induced reversal of the PIT effect, suggesting that the use of this instruction was presumably more complex and therefore less efﬁcient."
