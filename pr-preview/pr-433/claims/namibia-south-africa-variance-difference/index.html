Evidence¶

le Roux 2021¶

le Roux, D.B., Parry, D.A., Totolo, A., Iyawa, G., Holloway, J., Prenter, A., Botha, L. (2021). Media Multitasking, Online Vigilance and Academic Performance among Students in three Southern African Countries. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2020.104056

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Country-specific regressions of media use and online vigilance on academic performance; the Namibian model (n = 402) explained R² = .27 versus .04 in South Africa (n = 904), and the article reports "No overlap in 95% CIs for R2" between the two samples. Botswana was excluded due to small sample size.

"The variance explained by the model in the Namibia sample is substantially higher ( R2 = .27;F (6; 395) = 24.52,p < .001)."

  • Adding online vigilance to the media use model increases explained variance in academic performance only from 8% to 9% for the full sample — related
