Sakaki 2025¶

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Lagged dynamic panel models with over-aspiration and the expectation–aspiration mean as predictors of next-year emotions; the mean predicted higher anxiety, whereas 'the predictive effects of over-aspiration were not significant for any of the achievement emotions'.

"we found that higher means of aspiration and expectations were predictive of higher levels of anxiety. However, the predictive effects of over-aspiration were not significant for any of the achievement emotions in this and the additional analyses (Table S17)."
