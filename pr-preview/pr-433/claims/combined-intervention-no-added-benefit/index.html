Tsai Chia-Wen 2023¶

Tsai Chia-Wen, Lin Michael Yu-Ching, Cheng Yih-Ping, Lee Lan-Yu, Chyr Wen-Li, Lin Chih-Hsien, Lin Jian-Wei, Tsai Meng-Chuan. (2023). The effects of online peer-facilitated learning and distributed pair programming on students’ learning. Computers & Education, 203, 104849. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104849

One-way ANOVA across four groups on three dependent variables. G3 scored significantly lower on programming skills than G2 (mean difference -11.264, p<.001) and G4 (mean difference -7.846, p=.020).

"learners in G1, who simultaneously received peer-facilitated learning and distributed pair programming, did not have a significantly better performance than other groups"
