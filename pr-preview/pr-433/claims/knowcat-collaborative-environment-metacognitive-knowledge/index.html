Related Claims¶

  • Students' use of metacognitive actions to direct group work increased over time when using the Knowledge Catalyser tools — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Primary students' wiki negotiation contains collaboration and reasoning keywords distributed throughout the interaction — related
  • A wiki with a thinking-together approach can provide a dialogic space in which students co-construct shared understanding — related
  • Specific pedagogic design characteristics supported students' joint interaction in the wiki: initial text propositions, Thinking Together preparation, a collectively created artefact, and asynchrony — related
  • Collaborative wiki texts are longer, contain more t-units and more reasoning connectors than the initial pair-written texts — related
  • Wiki negotiation unfolds in four episodes with distinct intersubjective orientations, beginning with an exploratory orientation during content generation — related
  • The final episode of wiki collaborative writing is characterised by a disputational orientation, possibly driven by time pressure to finish the text — related
  • During collaborative learning, low and high cognition have remarkably long lasting (6 lags) positive effects on themselves, planning induces low cognition which sparks high cognition, and scaffolding does not alter these sequences — related
