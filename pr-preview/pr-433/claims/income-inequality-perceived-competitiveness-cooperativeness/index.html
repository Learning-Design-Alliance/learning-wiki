Evidence¶

Nicolas Sommet 2022¶

Nicolas Sommet, David L. Weissman, and Andrew J. Elliot. (2022). Income Inequality Predicts Competitiveness and Cooperativeness at School. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000731

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Three-level multilevel analysis of the OECD PISA 2018 data set (496,455 students, 20,643 schools, 75 countries), regressing perceived competitiveness on the Gini coefficient with preregistered student- and country-level controls; "the higher the income inequality, the higher the perceived competitiveness" with b = .07 (p = .008).

"the higher the income inequality, the higher the perceived competitiveness, b = .07 [.02, .13], p = .008, and bc = .08 [.01, .15], pc = .019"

Nicolas Sommet 2022 (2)¶

Nicolas Sommet, David L. Weissman, and Andrew J. Elliot. (2022). Income Inequality Predicts Competitiveness and Cooperativeness at School. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000731

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Same PISA 2018 multilevel analysis for perceived cooperativeness; without controls the estimate was not significant (p = .082), but with controls "the higher the income inequality, the lower the perceived cooperativeness" (bc = −.07, pc = .009).

"the higher the income inequality, the lower the perceived cooperativeness, b = /C0 .04 [ /C0 .09, .01], p = .082, and bc = /C0 .07 [ /C0 .13, /C0 .02], pc = .009"
