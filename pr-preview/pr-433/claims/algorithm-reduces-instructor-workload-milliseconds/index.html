Authors' report in the Results and Discussion section on the practical cost of manual assignment in their capstone courses, contrasting the "over 20 hours of instructor effort" with the algorithm's millisecond-scale runtime.

"Finally, it is worth noting the practical cost of manual assignment. Creating teams manually required over 20 hours of instructor effort to carefully review data and assign students. In contrast, the algorithm produces assignments of higher quality in a matter of milliseconds, demonstrating not only better balance but also high efficiency."
