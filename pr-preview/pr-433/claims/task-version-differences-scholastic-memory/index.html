Keller 2024¶

Keller, N. E., Salvi, C., Leiker, E. K., Gruber, M. J. & Dunsmoor, J. E. (2024). States of epistemic curiosity interfere with memory for incidental scholastic facts. npj Science of Learning 9:22. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00234-w

Generalized mixed model across the three task versions of the online experiment. A significant contrast "between Early Anticipation vs. Late Anticipation" (OR = 0.68, p = 0.034), with no Early vs. Post Satisfaction effect. Only odds ratios and p-values are printed; no standardized effect size.
