q3 · i? · associational · r2

Predictor finding from the main survey study (N=382) at a minority-serving institution where approximately one-third of undergraduates are first-generation. The article reports significance without a printed effect size, so no magnitude is stated.

"First-generation status emerged as a significant predictor of reliance intensity, indicating a compensatory dynamic in which AI supplements academic resources that are otherwise more accessible to advantaged peers."
