Emily Brooks 2024¶

Emily Brooks, Sarah Wallis, Joshua Hendrikse & James Coxon. (2024). Micro-consolidation occurs when learning an implicit motor sequence, but is not influenced by HIIT exercise. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00238-6

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Figure-reported correlational result (Fig. 1g,h) described in the figure caption for the SRTT experiment. The article reports the between-subjects relationship r = -0.96 and the within-subjects group mean correlation r = -0.22, SD .37. The authors state the reasons for this relationship are unclear and require further investigation.

Related Claims¶

  • Early learning of an implicit motor sequence is accounted for by micro-offline gains during brief rest periods rather than by gains during active practice — related
  • Performance improvements during early motor skill learning occur predominantly during short rest periods (micro-offline gains), replicated in a large crowdsourced sample and in a laboratory study — related
  • Lower resting GABAB-mediated inhibition in M1 (measured as LICI) is strongly negatively associated with micro-offline motor consolidation gains during early learning — related
  • No other neurophysiological measure (MEP, SICI, ICF, sICF) showed a significant correlation with micro-online or micro-offline learning — related
  • The GABAB–micro-offline correlation is significantly stronger than the corresponding GABAA (SICI) correlation — related
