Evidence¶

Toops 1918¶

Toops, H. A., and Pintker, R. (1918). Mentality and School Progress. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Mentality+and+School+Progress+Toops+Pintner

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Numerical reanalysis of the same three-school mental-survey data, with middle I.Q. diagnostic limits (e.g., I.Q. of .89 or below; I.Q. of 1.10 or above at age 7). The authors state this coefficient is not such "as to warrant our using school grade as a prediction of mentality" until middle classes on both sides are eliminated; no standardized effect size is printed.

"This procedure results in a QV--value, when "normal" school and "normal" mentality groups are eliminated, of +.97."
