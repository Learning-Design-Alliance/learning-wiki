Evidence¶

Nagaoka 2021¶

Nagaoka, J., Mahaffie, S., Usher, A., & Seeskin, A. (2021). College during the pandemic: Immediate enrollment and retention of CPS Graduates in Fall 2020. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://toandthrough.uchicago.edu/nsc-iccb-technical-report

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Spring-to-fall continuation analysis of first-year CPS graduates enrolled in two-year colleges (Figure 4 takeaways), cohorts 2016–2020. The executive summary reports 66.4 percent in 2019 vs. 61.9 percent in 2020; no statistical tests are reported.

"The percentage of first-year students enrolled in the spring at a two-year college who continued at the same college in the fall has been decreasing since 2017, a trend that accelerated slightly in 2020."
