Evidence¶

Xiaoqi Wang 2026¶

Xiaoqi Wang, Lawrence Jun Zhang, Yanan Zhang. (2026). Generative artificial intelligence-supported self-regulated learning (GenAI-SRL) in L2 writing: scale development, validation, and short-form construction. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-026-09481-1

q2 · i? · design · r2

Reliability and validity analysis of the 34-item scale on Sample two (N = 342). The article reports α from 0.761 to 0.943, CR 0.759–0.943, AVE 0.513–0.675 supporting convergent validity, and "all HTMT ratios fell below 0.85" supporting discriminant validity.

"Cronbach’s α values ranged from 0.761 (social-behavioral) to 0.943 (metacognitive). Composite reliability (CR) values ranged from 0.759 to 0.943, all exceeding the recommended threshold of 0.70."
