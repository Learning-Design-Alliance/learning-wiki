Shin 2019¶

Shin, D. D., & Kim, S. (2019). Homo Curious: Curious or Interested? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-019-09497-x

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative review synthesis. Benefits include personal relevance, usefulness for superordinate goals, and expected availability; costs include effort cost and emotional cost, including the ostrich effect of avoiding information that could validate negative predictions.

"Individuals are likely to engage in information-seeking behavior when the benefit of resolving curiosity is perceived to be greater than the cost."
