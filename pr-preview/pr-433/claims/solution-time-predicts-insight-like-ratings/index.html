Evidence¶

Adam Chuderski 2021¶

Adam Chuderski, Jan Jastrzębski, Bartłomiej Kroczek, Hanna Kucwaj, Michał Ociepka. (2021). Metacognitive experience on Raven’s matrices versus insight problems. Metacognition and Learning 16:15–35. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09236-6

q2 · i1 · associational · r2

Exploratory correlational analysis across the 100 participants: solution time correlated negatively with RAPM score on the five easiest items (r = −.126) but positively on the five hardest items (r = .377, BF10 > 999), meaning effective performance indicated rapidness on easy problems but deliberation on difficult ones.

"a reliable difference was found between a negative correlation of ST with the total RAPM score on the easiest five items, r = −.126, and its positive correlation on the hardest five items, r = .377, BF 10 > 999"
