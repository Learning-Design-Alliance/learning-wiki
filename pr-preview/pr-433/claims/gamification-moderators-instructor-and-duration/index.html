Evidence¶

Liuyufeng Li 2024¶

Liuyufeng Li, Khe Foon Hew, Jiahui Du. (2024). Gamification enhances student intrinsic motivation, perceptions of autonomy and relatedness, but minimal impact on competency: a meta-analysis and systematic review. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10337-7

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Moderator analysis (Q-test) across the 35 interventions comparing instructor-equivalence categories. Identical-instructor studies (n = 9) showed g = 0.613; different-instructor studies (n = 8) showed g = −0.143; no-data studies g = 0.264.

"Effect sizes were larger in studies in which both the control and experimental groups were taught by the same instructor (Hedges' g = 0.613) than in those in which the groups were taught by different instructors (Hedges' g = − 0.143)"

Liuyufeng Li 2024 (2)¶

Liuyufeng Li, Khe Foon Hew, Jiahui Du. (2024). Gamification enhances student intrinsic motivation, perceptions of autonomy and relatedness, but minimal impact on competency: a meta-analysis and systematic review. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10337-7

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Moderator analysis of intervention duration across the interventions. The 1-month-to-3-months subgroup showed the highest effect size (g = 0.610); interventions of a semester or longer showed g = −0.068.

"significant differences were found across various intervention durations (Q = 9.509, df = 4, p = 0.050), and interventions conducted over a period of 1 to 3 months had the highest effect sizes"
