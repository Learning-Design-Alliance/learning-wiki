Evidence¶

Ludo W. van Meeuwen 2018¶

Ludo W. van Meeuwen, Saskia Brand-Gruwel, Paul A. Kirschner, Jeano J. P. R. de Bock, Jeroen J. G. van Merriënboer. (2018). Fostering self-regulation in training complex cognitive tasks. Education Tech Research Dev 66, 53–73. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-017-9539-9

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

Non-parametric comparison of pretest-to-posttest increases between the original condition (n = 12) and integrated condition (n = 17) using the Dutch STPQ. The integrated condition's increase was significantly greater, with "r =- 0.37" reported as a medium effect.

"The increase in self-efﬁcacy for learning in the integrated condition ( Mdn = 20, range 1–29) is signiﬁcantly greater than in the original condition ( Mdn = 10, range 4–23, U = 57.50, z =- 2.00, p = 0.023, r =- 0.37)."

Ludo W. van Meeuwen 2018 (2)¶

Ludo W. van Meeuwen, Saskia Brand-Gruwel, Paul A. Kirschner, Jeano J. P. R. de Bock, Jeroen J. G. van Merriënboer. (2018). Fostering self-regulation in training complex cognitive tasks. Education Tech Research Dev 66, 53–73. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-017-9539-9

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

Mann–Whitney comparison of task-value increases between the two conditions in the same quasi-experiment; the integrated condition's increase was significantly greater, with "r =- 0.54" printed for this effect.

"The increase in the integrated condition ( Mdn = 20, range 3–29) is signiﬁcantly greater than in the original condition ( Mdn = 8.5, range 1–28, U = 37.50, z =- 2.91, p = 0.001, r =- 0.54), indicating that the intervention positively affected the increase of self-efﬁcacy for learning and an increased interest in the task."
