Evidence¶

Blachman 1994¶

Blachman, B.A., Ball, E.W., Black, R.S. & Tangel, D.M. (1994). Kindergarten teachers develop phoneme awareness in low-income, inner-city classrooms. Read Writ 6, 1–18. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01027275

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Controlled intervention study in inner-city kindergarten classrooms with 84 treatment and 75 control children. The article reports that "the treatment children significantly outperformed the control children" across phoneme segmentation, letter knowledge, reading, and invented spelling measures; no effect sizes are printed in the available text.

"After the 11 week intervention, the treatment children significantly outperformed the control children on measures of phoneme segmentation, letter name and letter sound knowledge, two of three reading measures, and a measure of invented spelling."
