Evidence¶

Early Risers 2012¶

Early Risers. (2012). WWC Intervention Report: Children Classified as Having an Emotional Disturbance. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://www.med.umn.edu/psychiatry/research/earlyrisers/home.html

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Randomized controlled trial in which 20 Minnesota schools were randomly assigned to Early Risers or comparison; analysis sample after three years was 199 students (n = 100 intervention, n = 99 comparison). The WWC confirmed "no statistically significant differences between the intervention and comparison groups on aggression, hyperactivity, or impulsivity composite measures," with a domain average effect size of 0.11.

"August et al. (2002) found, and the WWC confirmed, no statistically significant differences between the intervention and comparison groups on aggression, hyperactivity, or impulsivity composite measures."
