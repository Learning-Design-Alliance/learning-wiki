Evidence¶

Ethan Dickey 2026¶

Ethan Dickey, Marios Mertzanidis, Alexandros Psomas. (2026). Is Solving Better Than Evaluating GenAI Solutions? https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27586 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.27586

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Pre-midterm Qualtrics survey (N=208, 95% response rate) with five-point Likert items; Comfort (alpha=0.79) and Confidence (alpha=0.84) composites compared via Mann-Whitney U tests between crossover conditions.

"No statistically significant differences were observed between groups on either the Comfort composite (𝑈= 5,788,𝑝= .374) or the Confidence composite (𝑈= 5,152,𝑝=. 561) after three assignments under their respective conditions."

Ethan Dickey 2026 (2)¶

Ethan Dickey, Marios Mertzanidis, Alexandros Psomas. (2026). Is Solving Better Than Evaluating GenAI Solutions? https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27586

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Topic-level analysis of the same survey found Asymptotic Analysis (Comfort median=4) and Divide & Conquer rated highest; Comfort and Confidence composites were strongly associated (Spearman's rho=0.714, p<.001).

"Friedman tests indicated significant within-subject variation across topics for both Comfort ( 𝜒 2(4)= 156.78,𝑝<. 001) and Confidence (𝜒 2(4)= 166.50,𝑝<. 001)."
