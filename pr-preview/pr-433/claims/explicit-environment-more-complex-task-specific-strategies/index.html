Evidence¶

Aiso Heinze 2018¶

Aiso Heinze, Julia Arend, Meike Gruessing, Frank Lipowsky. (2018). Instructional approaches to foster third graders' adaptive use of strategies: an experimental study on the effects of two learning environments on multi-digit addition and subtraction. Instr Sci 46:869–891. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-018-9457-1

q3 · i2 · causal · r?

χ2-homogeneity tests on the frequencies of 10 merged strategy categories at each measurement point. At post-test the group distributions differed significantly with a medium effect size [χ2(9, N = 552) = 94.82, p < .001, V = .41].

"children from the explicit learning environment used these strategies more than twice as often as children from the implicit learning environment (indirect subtraction type: 61 vs. 26; simplifying type: 45 vs. 18)."
