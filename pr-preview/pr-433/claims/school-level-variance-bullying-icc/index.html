Evidence¶

Fink 2017¶

Fink, E., Patalay, P., Sharpe, H., & Wolpert, M. (2017). Child- and School-Level Predictors of Children's Bullying Behavior: A Multilevel Analysis in 648 Primary Schools. Journal of Educational Psychology. http://dx.doi.org/10.1037/edu0000204

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Baseline empty multilevel model (model 1) with children nested within 648 schools, ML estimation; Table 3 prints ICC .091 and random effects .57 (0.03). The ICC declines across successive models to .044 in model 4.

"The baseline model (model 1; Table 3) indicates that 9.1% of the variance in bullying is accounted for by schools before any other child- and school-level variables were included."
