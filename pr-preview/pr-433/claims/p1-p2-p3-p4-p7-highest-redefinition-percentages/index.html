Rute Nogueira de Morais Bicalho, César Coll, Anna Engel, Maria Cláudia Santos Lopes de Oliveira. (2023). Integration of ICTs in teaching practices: propositions to the SAMR model. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10169-x

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Percentage analysis of the same questionnaire; the article reports that "practices P1 (21.6%), P2 (20.7%), P3 (18.1%), P4 (15.5%) and P7 (17.2%) were those with the highest percentages at the Redefinition level".

"In turn, practices P1 (21.6%), P2 (20.7%), P3 (18.1%), P4 (15.5%) and P7 (17.2%) were those with the highest percentages at the Redefinition level, that is, they indicate that ICTs made it possible to redefine them significantly."
