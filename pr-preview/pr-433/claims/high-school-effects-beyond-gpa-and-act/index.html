Allensworth 2019¶

Allensworth, Elaine M., & Clark, Kallie. (2019). Are GPAs an Inconsistent Measure of College Readiness across High Schools? Examining Assumptions about Grades versus Standardized Test Scores. University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/are-gpas-inconsistent-measure-achievement-across-high-schools-examining-assumptions

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Authors' synthesis of the variance-component results across models (n=17,753): school effects remain after controlling for either measure, and are larger among students with the same HSGPA.

"There are large high school effects on college graduation, such that students with either the same HSGPA or the same ACT score graduate from college at different rates, based on which high school they attended. Neither capture all of the ways in which high schools influence college graduation."
