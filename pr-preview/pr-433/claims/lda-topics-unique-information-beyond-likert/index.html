Evidence¶

Hujala 2020¶

Hujala, M., Knutas, A., Hynninen, T., & Arminen, H. (2020). Improving the quality of teaching by utilising written student feedback: A streamlined process. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2020.103965

q2 · i? · design · r2

Statistical evaluation of the 2017-18 sample using multilevel regression, reporting Level 1 (student level) explained variance for each topic model. The authors conclude that "it seems that the LDA topics also can provide unique information not covered by the Likert-scale questions".

"However, the R2 values of the models predicting topic probabilities are relatively low (1.0% - 14.6%), indicating that the Likert scales can explain, at best, only 14.6% (see Topic 6 in Table 5) of the variances in topic probabilities."
