Overarching-trends analysis across both coded themes for all 49 participants: "a vast majority of students (27 of 37) also had a positive view of the problem-posing technique with Gen AI", with 5 of 37 mixed and 2 of 49 negative on the technique; only 1 of 49 was negative on both.
