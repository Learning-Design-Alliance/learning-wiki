Arnold 2025¶

The brief reports national NAEP statistics for 2022, stating that fourth graders with disabilities scored "40 points lower in reading and 28 points lower in mathematics than their peers without disabilities." Table 1 shows grade 4 reading scores from 1998 to 2022, with the 2022 gap (183 vs. 223) comparable to 1998 (176 vs. 217).
