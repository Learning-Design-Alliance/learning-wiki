Evidence¶

Paneth 2024¶

Paneth, L., Jeitziner, L. T., Rack, O., Opwis, K., & Zahn, C. (2024). Zooming in: The role of nonverbal behavior in sensing the quality of collaborative group engagement. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-024-09422-7

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive results from Figure 4 aggregating mean nonverbal frequencies per rating level across time and groups; also reported: "the mean frequency of head nodding increases with higher ratings of CC: it is 1.42 times when rated low and 3.16 times when rated high". These are descriptive means only, no inferential test.

"frequencies of nonverbal behaviors increase with higher ratings. For example, the frequency of laughing increases strongly with higher ratings of SE: on average, laughing occurs 1.91 times when rated low and 5.06 times when rated high."
