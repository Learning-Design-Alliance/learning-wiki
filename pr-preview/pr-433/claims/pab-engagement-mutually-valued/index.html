pab-survey-1¶

Pautz Stephenson, S., Banks, R., & Coenraad, M. (2022, June). Outcomes of increased practitioner engagement in edtech development: How strong, sustainable research-practice-industry partnerships will build a better edtech future. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/158

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Post-participation survey of the six-member Merlyn Mind Practitioner Advisory Board, part of the white paper's combination of surveys and interviews. All six members replied "either agree or strongly agree" on value, relevance, and whether feedback was taken seriously. No effect sizes are printed.

"When asked if engaging in the Practitioner Advisory Board was a valuable use of their time, all six Board members replied either agree or strongly agree. All agreed or strongly agreed that the topics they were asked for input on were relevant to them and that their feedback was taken seriously."
