Evidence¶

Hughes 2024¶

Hughes, A. M., Torvik, F. A., van Bergen, E., Hannigan, L. J., Corfield, E. C., Andreassen, O. A., Ystrom, E., Ask, H., Davey Smith, G., Davies, N. M. & Havdahl, A. (2024). Parental education and children’s depression, anxiety, and ADHD traits, a within-family study in MoBa. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00260-8

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Complete-case (unimputed) sensitivity analysis on the much smaller subsets with full data (around 15% of the analytic sample). The child's education PGI was clearly associated with ADHD traits (−0.06, p = 0.001) but not anxiety or depressive traits in this analysis. The authors flag the positive maternal estimates as unexpected and selection-prone.

"Unexpectedly, in Mendelian randomization models adjusting for the child’s genotype, mother’s educational attainment was positively related to the child’s depressive (0.16, CI: 0.02, 0.30,p = 0.03) and ADHD-inattention traits (0.21, CI: 0.06, 0.35,p = 0.006)."
