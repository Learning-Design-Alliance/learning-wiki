Evidence¶

Dave Neale & David Whitebread 2019¶

Dave Neale & David Whitebread. (2019). Maternal scaffolding during play with 12- to 24-month-old infants: stability over time and relations with emerging effortful control. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-019-09196-6

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Bivariate correlational tests of consistency in scaffolding variables in the longitudinal dyadic-play sample. The article reports contingency "consistent across toys at 12-months" but with no over-time consistency, in agreement with Conner and Cross (2003).
