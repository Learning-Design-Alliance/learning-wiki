q2 · i? · associational · r1

A 2024 Digital Promise survey sample of teachers in Jefferson County, Kentucky, and Miami-Dade County, described by the report as showing "a positive correlation" between the program and improved job satisfaction, with 61% indicating participation improved it. No effect size is printed.

"Our 2024 survey sample of Jefferson County, Kentucky, and Miami-Dade County teachers provides evidence that there is a positive correlation between the Verizon Innovative Learning Schools program and improved job satisfaction. Sixty-one percent of teachers surveyed indicated that participating in Verizon Innovative Learning Schools improved it."
