Wouters 2011¶

Wouters, P., van der Spek, E. D., & van Oostendorp, H. (2011). Measuring learning in serious games: A case study with structural assessment. Educational Technology Research and Development, 59(6), 741–763. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-010-9183-0

q2 · i? · design · r2

Case study with 19 participants (9 novices, 10 advanced learners) learning triage with the serious game Code Red: Triage. The article reports that "structural assessment measures an individual's understanding of a domain at least differently from verbal assessment."

"Altogether the results suggest that structural assessment measures an individual's understanding of a domain at least differently from verbal assessment."
