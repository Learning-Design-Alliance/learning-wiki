Evidence¶

Gherghel 2023¶

Gherghel, C., Yasuda, S., & Kita, Y. (2023). Interaction during online classes fosters engagement with learning and self-directed study both in the first and second years of the COVID-19 pandemic. Computers & Education, 200, 104795. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104795

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Bootstrap path analysis of the 2020 and 2021 questionnaire data. The article reports this indirect effect as "positive in 2020 and 2021" with the printed estimates; the year difference was not significant (b = 0.187, p = .332). The authors interpret emotional engagement as a more distal predictor working through behavioral engagement.

"the indirect effect of emotional engagement on self-directed study through behavioral engagement was positive in 2020 and 2021 (2020 estimate: b = 0.648, SE = 0.14, p < .001, 95% CI [0.375, 0.944]; 2021 estimate: b = 0.461, SE = 0.12, p < .001, 95% CI [0.238, 0.737])"
