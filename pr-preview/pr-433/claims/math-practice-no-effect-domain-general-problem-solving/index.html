Evidence¶

Avvisati 2020¶

Avvisati, F., & Borgonovi, F. (2020). Learning Mathematics Problem Solving through Test Practice: a Randomized Field Experiment on a Global Scale. Educational Psychology Review, 32, 791–814. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09520-6

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Study 2 (N = 19,481), a separate random assignment within PISA 2012 to forms with varying mathematics content, with a criterial test of non-curricular problem-solving tasks. The coefficient for math practice on the problem-solving score was 0.36 (SE = 1.81), not statistically significant (p = 0.844 from Table 3).
