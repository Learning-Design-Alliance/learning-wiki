Evidence¶

Hagger 2016¶

Hagger, M. S., & Chatzisarantis, N. L. D. (2016). The Trans-Contextual Model of Autonomous Motivation in Education: Conceptual and Empirical Issues and Meta-Analysis. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654315585005

q1 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Theoretical argument in the review's discussion of generalizability: variations from extraneous moderators are expected in "the relative size or magnitude of the effect sizes across moderator groups rather than a change in the overall pattern of effects." The article offers this as a proposal, not a tested result.

"Candidate moderators that should be tested in the model include educational domain, cultural orientation, age, gender, and socioeconomic status."
