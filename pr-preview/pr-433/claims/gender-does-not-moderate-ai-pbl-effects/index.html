Ayanwale 2026¶

Ayanwale, M. A., & Omeh, C. B. (2026). AI-supported problem-based learning for enhancing computational thinking skills in STEM education. Computers in Human Behavior: Artificial Humans 7 (2026) 100263. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chbah.2026.100263

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Two-way ANCOVA interaction term (Group × Gender) predicting Post_CT_Total in the 103-student quasi-experiment, reported in the Results section; the article states Hypothesis 3 is not supported.

"The interaction effect was not statistically significant, F (1, 70) = 0.41, p = .527, partial η2 = .006 (see Table 2a), suggesting that the influence of instructional group on computational thinking outcomes did not differ by gender"
