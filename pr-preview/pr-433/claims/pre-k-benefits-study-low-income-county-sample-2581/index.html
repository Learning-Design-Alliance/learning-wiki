Erik Ruzek 2020¶

Erik Ruzek, Arya Ansari, Robert Pianta, Jessica Whittaker, Virginia Vitiello. (2020). Persistence and convergence: The end of kindergarten outcomes of pre-K graduates and their nonattending peers. Developmental Psychology, 56(11), 2027–2039. https://doi.org/10.1037/dev0001115
