Evidence¶

Gielen 2011¶

Gielen, S., Dochy, F., & Onghena, P. (2011). An inventory of peer assessment diversity. Assessment & Evaluation in Higher Education, 36(2), 137–155. https://doi.org/10.1080/02602930903221444

q2 · i? · review · r2

A narrative-review description introducing the 'Quality control' variable: reactive examples include teachers re-checking student-awarded scores or automated systems detecting outlying scores, while proactive examples tie rewards or sanctions to feedback quality, as in Bloxham and West (2004) where a quarter of the assessor's mark depended on peer assessment quality.

"Quality control may act proactively or reactively. Reactive examples are teachers who run quality checks of scores awarded by students by assessing some of the work themselves"
