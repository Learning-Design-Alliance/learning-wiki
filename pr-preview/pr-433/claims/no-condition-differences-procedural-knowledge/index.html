Christian Hartmann 2021¶

Christian Hartmann, Tamara van Gog, Nikol Rummel. (2021). Preparatory effects of problem solving versus studying examples prior to instruction. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-020-09528-z

ANCOVA on the procedural knowledge subscale (applying the mean absolute deviation procedure), controlling for mathematical ability and prior knowledge, found "no significant differences among conditions", F(2, 168) = 0.15, p = 0.863, consistent with prior PF research.

"an ANCOVA on procedural knowledge showed that there were no significant differences among conditions with respect to procedural knowledge acquisition, F(2, 168) = 0.15, p = 0.863, ηp 2 = 0.002"
