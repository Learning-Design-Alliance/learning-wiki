Haozhe Jiang 2025¶

Learning-strategy coding of the 117 reviewed articles (Fig. 9). The review reports situated, task-based and game-based learning as the top strategies, illustrated by examples such as an immersive underwater world simulating ocean acidification, and notes "The least used learning strategy is storytelling (used in only six studies)".

"the top three applied learning strategies are situated learning (51 articles), task-based learning (37 articles), and game-based learning (35 articles)."

Related Claims¶

  • Most VR/AR-supported K-12 STEM studies targeted elementary students and used quantitative methods, with most samples under 100 participants — related
  • Publications on VR/AR-supported K-12 STEM learning grew rapidly after 2017, with 2022 output four times the 2010 level — related
  • VR/AR-supported K-12 STEM research is unevenly distributed across subjects: science dominates, engineering has no studies — related
  • Non-immersive devices, especially smartphone/tablet-based AR, dominated VR/AR-supported K-12 STEM studies; immersive AR was rarely used
  • Review syntheses: VR/AR most often improved academic achievement and motivation, but sometimes performed no better than conventional technologies
  • VR/AR implementation challenges fall on three fronts: learner distraction and discomfort, teacher classroom-management and design burdens, and school cost and infrastructure limits
