Betty Tärning 2019¶

Betty Tärning, Annika Silvervarg, Agneta Gulz, Magnus Haake. (2019). Instructing a Teachable Agent with Low or High Self-Efficacy – Does Similarity Attract? International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-018-0167-2

The article's own summary statement of the performance pattern across the agent x student SE groups; the abstract describes this as especially apparent for low-SE students in the matched condition. No separate test statistic is printed for this subgroup contrast in the text provided.

"This was especially apparent for students who had reported low self-efficacy themselves, who performed on par with students with high self-efficacy when interacting with a digital tutee with low self-efficacy."
