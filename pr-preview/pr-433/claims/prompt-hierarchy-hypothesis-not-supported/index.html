Evidence¶

Vasileios Lampos 2021¶

Vasileios Lampos, Joseph Mintz and Xiao Qu (2021). An artificial intelligence approach for selecting effective teacher communication strategies in autism education. npj Science of Learning 6:25. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00102-x

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Sequential-pattern analysis of the collected observations testing whether teachers escalate to more intrusive prompts after failure. The hypothesis that hierarchies explain the patterns "could justify why in the second time step (t + 1) we observe a steep increase in full student response for non-intrusive communications", but the authors state the data do not fully support it. Printed probabilities only; no effect size.

"when a teacher communication, other than a physical prompt, does not result in a full student response, then the subsequent communication contains a physical prompt only with a probability of 0.310 and when that happens the probability of a full student response is 0.549 (success rate)."
