Xi Xiang & Michael E. Meadows. (2025). Being proactive about anthropogenic environmental changes: augmenting students' decision making with artificial intelligence (AI) technology. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10523-9

Comparative case study design analysis of two learning activities. The authors report that the modes vary in "AI prediction, student engagement and teacher scaffolding"; no empirical learning outcome data were collected, so this is a design-derived comparison.

"It is apparent the two modes, viz. i) identify-model-specify-decide (IMSD) and ii) frame-model-program-decide (FMPD), vary in AI prediction, student engagement and teacher scaffolding"
