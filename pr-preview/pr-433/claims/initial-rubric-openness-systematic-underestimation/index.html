Evidence¶

Pei-Yu Tseng 2026¶

Pei-Yu Tseng, Mahir Akgun, and Peng Liu. (2026). Why Machines Misread Pedagogical Quality: Human–Machine Alignment in LLM-Based Pretest Question Evaluation. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23629 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.23629

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Experiment 1 (initial rubric, direct evaluation) compared human instructor ratings against GPT-5.1 machine ratings on 60 sampled questions per rubric version. Openness bias was positive with agreement of "55.7%", the lowest of the four dimensions; no effect size is printed.

"openness showed the largest discrepancy: its positive bias indicates systematic underestimation by the machine, and its low agreement (55.7%) suggests it was the most difficult dimension for machine rating."
