Hanna Dumont and Douglas D. Ready 2023¶

q3 · i? · review · r3

The review cites established research on the expertise-reversal effect: "students with lower cognitive abilities and lower levels of prior knowledge are less capable of self-regulating their own learning process" and need more instructional support and guidance.

"There is robust empirical evidence showing that students with lower cognitive abilities and lower levels of prior knowledge are less capable of self-regulating their own learning process and require increased instructional support and guidance, a phenomenon known as the “expertise-reversal effect”"
