Pizzie 2025¶

Pizzie, R. G., Sortino, R. M., Kim, C. E.-Y., & Inghram, R. (2025). Math anxiety and science anxiety are associated with spatial cognition and STEM interest in deaf, hard of hearing, and hearing people. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00336-z

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Linear regression (N = 115) with MR commission error rate as outcome; math anxiety b = 4.31, p < 0.01 and science anxiety b = 3.31, p < 0.05 were significant; overall fit R2 = 0.221.
