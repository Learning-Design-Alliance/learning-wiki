The report's framing section states, as background motivation for the evaluation, that "students from poor families lagging behind those from more affluent families" in math performance. No effect size or statistic is printed for this assertion.

"Students from different socioeconomic backgrounds also differ in math performance, with students from poor families lagging behind those from more affluent families."
