q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive comparison of 2023/24 SBAC performance levels by At-risk status, with outcomes observed roughly 7 months after October 1 identification. "60 percent of students identified as At-risk scored at a Level 1 on the ELA SBAC" vs. 26 percent of Not At-risk; math 70 percent vs. 32 percent at Level 1; science 73 percent vs. 38 percent. Only descriptive percentages are printed; no statistical test or effect size.

"60 percent of students identified as At-risk scored at a Level 1 on the ELA SBAC, while only 26 percent of students who were not identified as At-risk scored at Level 1."
