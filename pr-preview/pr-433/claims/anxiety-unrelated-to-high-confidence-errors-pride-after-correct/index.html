Evidence¶

Berweger 2023¶

Berweger, B., Kracke, B., & Dietrich, J. (2023). Preservice Teachers' Epistemic and Achievement Emotions When Confronted With Common Misconceptions About Education. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000792

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Same Bayesian Model 1 (accuracy x confidence interaction) in the 275-participant sample. The interaction for anxiety had a credibility interval comprising zero; separately, "wrong answers as such (regardless of participants’ confidence in them) resulted in higher anxiety (-.238 [-.297, -.179])" and pride was higher after correct answers (1.166 [1.114, 1.218]).

"Contrary to H1a, making high-confidence errors was unrelated to the experience of epistemic anxiety (-.039 [-.080, .000])."
