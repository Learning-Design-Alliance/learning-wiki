Evidence¶

Julia Schindler · Tobias Richter 2023¶

Julia Schindler · Tobias Richter. (2023). Text Generation Benefits Learning: a Meta-Analytic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09758-w

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

Subset moderator analyses within the three-level meta-analysis, examining generation task separately for expository and narrative texts. The article reports the expository-text contrast as significant and the narrative-text contrast (letter completion g = 0.57 vs. sentence unscrambling g = 0.36) as descriptively stronger but not significant.

"In studies based on expository texts, sentence unscrambling tasks yielded stronger generation effects (g = 0.77, p < 0.001) than letter completion tasks (g = 0.23, p = 0.006), and this difference was significant, Q M (df = 1) = 6.44, p = 0.011, k = 57."
