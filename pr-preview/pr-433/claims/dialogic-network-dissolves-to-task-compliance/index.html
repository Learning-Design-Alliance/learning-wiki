Evidence¶

Baruch B. Schwarz 2025¶

Baruch B. Schwarz, Udi Tsemach, Mirit Israeli, Erez Nir. (2025). Actor-network theory as a new direction in research on educational dialogues. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09669-5

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

ANT analysis of turns 147–210 of the same small-group discussion: at turn 157 a student called peers to start working, and by turn 184 the argumentative dialogue ended as students reorganized to read and classify text cards on the board.

"There is a shift of networks as the dialogic network is replaced by a less dialogic one. In this shift, students' agency abruptly changes from strong in collaborative argumentation as students elaborate arguments and challenge each other, to weak agency when the making of an artifact dictated by non-human actors makes students' opinions and judgments irrelevant."
