Baldwin 1913¶

Baldwin, B. T. (1913). The learning of delinquent adolescent girls as shown by a substitution test. The Journal of Educational Psychology, IV(6), 317-332. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0069968

q1 · i? · associational · r1

The author's interpretive conclusion drawn from the substitution-test performance of the institutional sample; it is an argument rather than a tested result, offered alongside the claim that inferior measured mentality helps explain some commitments.

"But since a large number of girls of both races have the ability to learn quickly, accurately and extensively, as shown by repeated trials for a given interim, it follows that the moral defects of delinquents must be partially explained on other grounds than inferior mentality."
