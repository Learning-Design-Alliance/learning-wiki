Robert DeKeyser 2010¶

Robert DeKeyser. (2010). Practice for Second Language Learning: Don't Throw out the Baby with the Bathwater. International Journal of English Studies, 10(1). https://www.um.es/ijes doi:10.6018/ijes/2010/1/114021

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical argument reviewing prior critiques (e.g. DeKeyser 1998; Krashen). The article argues repetitive drills fail because they do not teach "the target behavior of conceptualizing a meaning and expressing it through linguistic means".

"It has been argued many times that such drills are of limited use because they do not teach the student s to engage in the target behavior of conceptualizing a meaning and expressing it through linguistic means, let alone doing so in creative ways"
