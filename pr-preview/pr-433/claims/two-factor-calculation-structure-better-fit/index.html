Evidence¶

Namkung 2016¶

Namkung, J. M., & Fuchs, L. S. (2016). Cognitive Predictors of Calculations and Number Line Estimation with Whole Numbers and Fractions among At-Risk Students. Journal of Educational Psychology, 108(2), 214-228. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000055

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Confirmatory factor analysis of the calculation outcome measures in 139 at-risk fourth graders: the two-factor model (whole-number and fraction calculation latent variables) fit excellently (χ2(4) = 3.23, p = .519; RMSEA = 0.000) and the quoted Satorra-Bentler adjusted difference test confirmed both dimensions were necessary.

"An adjusted chi-square difference tests (i.e., Δχ2) using the Satorra-Bentler scaling correction yielded a significantly worse fit of the one-factor measurement model, Δχ2(1, N = 139) = 23.11, p = .000."
