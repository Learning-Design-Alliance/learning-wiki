Evidence¶

Leung-Gagné 2025¶

Leung-Gagné, M., Maria Castillo, M., Patrick, S. K., Carver-Thomas, D., Kini, T., Bingener, C., & Giani, M. V. (2025). Strengthening the educator pipeline through service scholarships: California's Golden State Teacher Grant Program. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/518.478

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Administrative analysis of CSAC GSTG records reports that the program "has supported over 28,600 aspiring educators", comprising 22,851 teacher candidates and 5,812 PPS candidates across the first 5 years.

"Over its first 5 years of implementation—from 2020–21 to 2024–25—the Golden State Teacher Grant (GSTG) Program has supported over 28,600 aspiring educators pursuing a teaching or Pupil Personnel Services (PPS) credential."

Leung-Gagné 2025 (2)¶

Leung-Gagné, M., Maria Castillo, M., Patrick, S. K., Carver-Thomas, D., Kini, T., Bingener, C., & Giani, M. V. (2025). Strengthening the educator pipeline through service scholarships: California's Golden State Teacher Grant Program. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/518.478

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of administrative data and CTC program-reported data (Table 2) shows uptake rising from 2% of new teacher candidates in 2020–21 to 45% in 2023–24, when eligibility covered all teacher candidates.

"In 2023–24, when GSTG was available to all teacher candidates, nearly half of California's new teacher candidates received a grant (Table 2)."
