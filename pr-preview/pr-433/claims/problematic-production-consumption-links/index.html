Huatong Sun 2006¶

Huatong Sun. (2006). The Triumph of Users: Achieving Cultural Usability Goals With User Localization. Technical Communication Quarterly, 15(4), 457-481. https://doi.org/10.1207/s15427625tcq1504_3

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Analysis of interview transcripts and observational notes through the circuit-of-culture lens. The author reports rarely finding production responding to use patterns, with Amoi's female-oriented phone modifications (e.g., the Dancing Queen model) as the sole exception found.

"I rarely found production processes responding to the use patterns emerging from the processes of consumption, regulation, representation, and identity except that one phone manufacturer, Amoi, responded to the enthusiasm of female users on wireless phones and modified the product for this group of users by adding female-oriented applications"
