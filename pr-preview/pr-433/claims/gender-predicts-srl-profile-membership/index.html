Evidence¶

Chungsoo Na 2024¶

Chungsoo Na, Soojeong Jeong, Jody Clarke-Midura, Youngin Shin. (2024). Linking self-regulated learning to community of inquiry in online undergraduate courses: A person-centered approach. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10380-y

q3 · i2 · associational · r2

Multinomial logistic regression with competent regulators as reference group, predicting profile membership from gender, major, prior experience, and time commitment. Male gender significantly predicted membership in three lower profiles, e.g. "OR = 2.36, 95% CI [1.14, 4.86]" for minimal vs. competent regulators.

"male students were more likely to be in the minimal regulators profile (OR = 2.36, 95% CI [1.14, 4.86], p = 0.020 in Profile 1 vs. Profile 5)"

Chungsoo Na 2024 (2)¶

Chungsoo Na, Soojeong Jeong, Jody Clarke-Midura, Youngin Shin. (2024). Linking self-regulated learning to community of inquiry in online undergraduate courses: A person-centered approach. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10380-y

q3 · i0 · associational · r2

Same multinomial logistic regression analysis. The three non-gender predictors were non-significant; the authors attribute this partly to a sample homogeneous in major and prior experience, and to reliance on self-report rather than LMS activity logs.

"We did not find any significant effects of the other three individual difference variables (i.e., major, prior experience, and time commitment) on determining SRL profile membership."
