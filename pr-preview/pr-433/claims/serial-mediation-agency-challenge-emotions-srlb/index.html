Evidence¶

Yang J 2026¶

Yang J, Liu X, Zhao W and Wang T (2026). GenAI literacy and GenAI-assisted self-regulated learning behaviors: the roles of learning agency and challenge emotions. Front. Psychol. 17:1902668. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1902668

q2 · i0 · associational · r2

Bootstrap serial mediation analysis (5,000 resamples); the serial indirect pathway through learning agency then challenge emotions had β = 0.050 with a 95% bootstrap CI of [0.030, 0.122], reported as "statistically significant".

"In addition, the serial indirect association through learning agency and challenge emotions were statistically significant(B=0.075,SE =0.023,95%bootstrapCI[0.030,0.122], β = 0.050)."
