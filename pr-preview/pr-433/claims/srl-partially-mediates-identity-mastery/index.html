Evidence¶

Jamaal S. Matthews 2014¶

Jamaal S. Matthews, Meeta Banerjee, Fani Lauermann. (2014). Academic Identity Formation and Motivation Among Ethnic Minority Adolescents: The Role of the “Self” Between Internal and External Perceptions of Identity. Child Development. https://doi.org/10.1111/cdev.12318

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Path analysis of cross-sectional survey data from 600 adolescents in seven Harlem and South Bronx schools, with bias-corrected bootstrap CIs (5,000 iterations). Direct effects were value c1 = .40 and belonging c2 = .25, and "significant indirect effects conﬁrmed that SRL functions as a mediator" with h1 = h2 = .14, p < .001; predictors explained 42% of mastery variance.

"SRL partially mediated the relation between the two identity dimensions and mastery orientation. Speciﬁcally, value ( c1 = .40, p<. 001) and belonging (c2 = .25, p<. 001) had a direct positive relation to mastery"
