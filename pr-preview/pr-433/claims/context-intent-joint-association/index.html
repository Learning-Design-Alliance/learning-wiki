Joint-distribution analysis of context by cognitive intent across the full corpus (Figure 1). The article reports a "structured association between context and cognitive intent", with AI-output interactions spanning multiple levels but less common for apply and analyze.

"Examining the joint distribution reveals a structured association between context and cognitive intent: task-directed interactions are concentrated at lower to mid Bloom levels, while references to students’ own work are more often associated with higher-level requests."
