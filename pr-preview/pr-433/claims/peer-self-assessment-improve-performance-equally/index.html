q3 · i? · review · r1

The primer reports, citing Double et al. (2020), a meta-analysis of control group studies on peer and self-assessment effects. The article states significance without printing an effect size, so no magnitude is asserted here.

"Both peer and self-assessment show significant effects on improving academic performance and no significant differences between their impacts (Double et al., 2020)."
