New Publications in Educational Psychology and Related Fields of Education 1922¶

New Publications in Educational Psychology and Related Fields of Education. (1922). https://eric.ed.gov/

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Conclusion drawn in the review of Terry's University of Chicago reading monograph, based on photographic recording of eye movements as six adult subjects read problems, numerals, and prose. The review states that "Ordinary prose is read much faster than either the problem material or isolated numerals."
