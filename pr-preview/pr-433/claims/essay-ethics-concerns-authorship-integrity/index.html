Descriptive coding of the 49-essay corpus: "When students raised ethics, it concerned authorship and integrity rather than privacy or data". One student asked how the school can know AI-assisted work is original rather than AI generated. This contrasts with the paper's broader ethics dimension, which emphasizes surveillance and data exploitation from cited literature.
