Pavlik Jr 2015¶

Pavlik Jr, P. I., Yudelson, M., & Koedinger, K. R. (2015). A Measurement Model of Microgenetic Transfer for Improving Instructional Outcomes. International Artificial Intelligence in Education Society. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0039-y

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Analysis of randomized warm-up 1 data from 247 students (after filtering) in the Bridge to Algebra tutor. A four-parameter aggregate CPFA model gave LCM→LCM=.237, Product→Product=.196, LCM→Product=.099**, Product→LCM=.021, and the pairwise contrast was tested: 'overall transfer from LCM to Product is significantly stronger than transfer from product to LCM'.

"we find that overall transfer from LCM to Product is significantly stronger than transfer from product to LCM ( t=2.3, df=247, p<.05)"
