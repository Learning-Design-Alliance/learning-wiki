q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Conceptual argument in the synergetic reframing section, where science is characterized as an open system in continuous evidential exchange with material reality and the generative model as operationally closed to new evidence at inference. The claim is offered as a family-level tendency, not a necessary and sufficient criterion of membership in the family of sciences.

"All five categories, namely aims, methods, practices, knowledge, and institutions, point in the same direction, since each is organized in part by the same feature: openness to material feedback, which science possesses and the conversational model lacks."
