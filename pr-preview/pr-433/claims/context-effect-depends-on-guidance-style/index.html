Evidence¶

Madeleine Eastwood 2026¶

Madeleine Eastwood, Harshith Narne, Joseph Hilby, Paul Denny, Ashish Aggarwal, and Amanpreet Kapoor. (2026). Guardrails or Roadblocks? Effects of Pedagogical Style and Context Awareness in AI Teaching Assistants for Programming. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29995 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.29995

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Authors' interpretation of the interaction plots in Figure 2 (mean ratings by context awareness and guidance style, N=132) from the randomized experiment, indicating "context awareness was not uniformly beneficial" and its effect depended on the paired pedagogical style.

"When Direct guidance was available, students rated the chatbot similarly for supporting task completion regardless of how much context was provided. In contrast, when the chatbot was Socratic, providing full context reduced students' perceived support for task completion."
