Evidence¶

Rotem Israel-Fishelson 2026¶

Rotem Israel-Fishelson, David Weintrop. (2026). Authenticity in a high school data science curriculum. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10622-1

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Qualitative interview analysis within a mixed-methods study of 25 12th-grade students in two computer science classes; interviews were coded for authenticity with Cohen's Kappa of 0.79 in relevancy coding. Students cited entertainment datasets like Billboard Hot 100 as personally authentic, while others "wouldn't say it is my passion or anything."

"The analysis of the interviews revealed that students found the use of interest-driven data exploration activities engaging and aligned with their interests, and thus, personally authentic."
