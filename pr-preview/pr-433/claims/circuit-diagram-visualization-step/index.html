Evidence¶

Ioannou 2025¶

Ioannou, A., Miliou, O., Georgiou, Y., Timotheou, S., Barkhuus, L., & Rode, J. (2025). Learning design for short-duration e-textile workshops: outcomes on knowledge and skills. Education Tech Research Dev, 73, 443–463. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10411-8

q2 · i? · design · r2

Qualitative finding from facilitator fieldnotes during the project development phase: children drew pre-test diagrams on A4 paper, labeled polarity, and marked current flow; facilitators scaffolded rather than corrected, and the authors later called this direct facilitation example instrumental in creating functioning circuits.

"these visualizations provided a window into the children's ideas and misconceptions, which the facilitators used as an opportunity to address. They initiated discussions with the groups around these visualizations—so-called 'teachable moments'—asking them to elaborate further on their ideas, questions, and concerns around their circuitries."
