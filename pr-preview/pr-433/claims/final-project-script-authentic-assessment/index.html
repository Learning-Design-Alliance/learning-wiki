q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Analysis of final project materials coded under the authentic assessment dimension. Student reflections showed data-type distinction, systematic inquiry with NFL statistics, and visualization-based conclusions; teacher rubric evaluation data were not analyzed in this study.

"As part of the final project submission, students had to complete a script. This script guided students in articulating the rationale and evaluation criteria for selecting their dataset, evaluating characteristics of the retrieved data such as its accuracy, reliability, completeness, and value"
