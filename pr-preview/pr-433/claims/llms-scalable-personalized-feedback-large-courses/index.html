Martínez-Peláez 2025¶

Martínez-Peláez, R.; Mena, L.J.; Toral-Cruz, H.; Ochoa-Brust, A.; Potes, A.G.; Flores, V.; Ostos, R.; Ramírez Pacheco, J.C.; Félix, R.A.; Félix, V.G. (2025). Can Large Language Models Foster Critical Thinking, Teamwork, and Problem-Solving Skills in Higher Education?: A Literature Review. Systems 2025, 13, 1013. https://doi.org/10.3390/systems13111013

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's RQ3 synthesis reports students can access LLMs from anywhere at any time, and that LLMs enhance self-regulated learning by enabling students to generate practice tests or self-assessment exercises to monitor progress. No effect sizes are printed.

"Evidence shows that LLMs can respond to student questions almost instantly, thus shortening the delay and reducing dependency on teachers' availability"

Martínez-Peláez 2025 (2)¶

Martínez-Peláez, R.; Mena, L.J.; Toral-Cruz, H.; Ochoa-Brust, A.; Potes, A.G.; Flores, V.; Ostos, R.; Ramírez Pacheco, J.C.; Félix, R.A.; Félix, V.G. (2025). Can Large Language Models Foster Critical Thinking, Teamwork, and Problem-Solving Skills in Higher Education?: A Literature Review. Systems 2025, 13, 1013. https://doi.org/10.3390/systems13111013

q2 · i? · review · r2

Faculty-perspective synthesis in the RQ3 section: automation of routine feedback enables teachers to focus on "complex, high-level tasks that need human expertise and disciplinary judgment", and LLMs can support course design by reviewing assessment items. No effect sizes are printed.

"the reviewed studies emphasize that LLMs can significantly reduce teaching workload by automating feedback on routine tasks such as drafts, quizzes, or structured assignments."
