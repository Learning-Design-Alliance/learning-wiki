Evidence¶

Schmidt 1989¶

Schmidt, H. G., De Volder, M. L., De Grave, W. S., Moust, J. H. C., & Patel, V. L. (1989). Explanatory Models in the Processing of Science Text: The Role of Prior Knowledge Activation Through Small-Group Discussion. Journal of Educational Psychology, 81(4), 610-619. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.81.4.610

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Experiment 1, a randomized two-condition experiment with 39 college students (20 problem-analysis, 19 control), measured free recall of osmosis knowledge with no text studied. The problem-analysis group produced more than twice as many propositions (M = 27.2 vs. 11.8).

"Subjects who analyzed the blood cell problem produced significantly more propositions related to osmosis than did subjects in the control condition, F(1, 37) = 31.06, MSe = 36.49, p < .00 I."
