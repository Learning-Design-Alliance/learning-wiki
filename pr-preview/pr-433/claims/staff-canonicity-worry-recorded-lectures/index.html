Evidence¶

MacKay 2018¶

MacKay, J. R. D. (2018). Show and ‘Tool’: How lecture recording transforms staff and student perspectives on lectures in higher education. University of Edinburgh. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2019.05.019

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Qualitative interview finding from staff participants in the grounded theory study. One staff participant stated 'as soon as you have a recorded lecture, that then becomes canonical'; another reported students regurgitating identical phrases from slides or spoken words in answers.

"I think, as soon as you have a recorded lecture, that then becomes canonical and it’s seen as something people can, sort of, reference and quote verbatim, and I’m not keen on that."
