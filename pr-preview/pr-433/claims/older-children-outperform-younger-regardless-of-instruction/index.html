Smit 2025¶

Smit, N., de Kleijn, R., Wicherts, J. M., & van de Pol, J. (2025). What it takes to tutor—A preregistered direct replication of the scaffolding experimental study by D. Wood et al. (1978). Journal of Educational Psychology. Advance online publication. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000965

q2 · i? · causal · r3

Exploratory analysis of posttest performance by age across all participants, displayed as a bimodal distribution in Figure 6; the article reports the age advantage without a test statistic or effect size. The authors note the finding is consistent with the 1976 Wood, Bruner, and Ross study.

"We found that overall, older children ( .3.5 years old) outperformed younger children, regardless of the type of instruction they had received prior to the posttest"
