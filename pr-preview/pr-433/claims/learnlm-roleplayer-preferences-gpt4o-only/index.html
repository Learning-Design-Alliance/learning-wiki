LearnLM Team 2024¶

LearnLM Team, Google. (2024). LearnLM: Improving Gemini for Learning. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.16429 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2412.16429

q3 · i? · design · r2

Appendix A.1 reports preference ratings from the Stage 2 participants who role-played learners, on four comparative categories (Figure 7). The text states these participants "revealed a preference toward LearnLM over GPT-4o for allfour comparative assessment categories", strongest for overall pedagogy and similarity to a quality human tutor.

"The participants role-playing as learners revealed a preference toward LearnLM over GPT-4o for allfour comparative assessment categories (Figure 7)."

LearnLM Team 2024 (2)¶

LearnLM Team, Google. (2024). LearnLM: Improving Gemini for Learning. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.16429
