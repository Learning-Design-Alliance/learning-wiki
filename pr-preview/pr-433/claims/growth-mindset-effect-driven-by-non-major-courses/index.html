Melody M. Chao 2025¶

Melody M. Chao, Allen H. Huang, Anirban Mukhopadhyay & Janghoon Shon. (2025). Divergent effects of mindsets on performance trajectories. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00355-w

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Course-level regressions of 33,607 student-course observations on mindset with intake-year and course fixed effects split grades by major vs non-major status. The positive average association (B = 0.022) was "driven by courses outside of a student's major specialization"; the major-course coefficient was non-significant.
