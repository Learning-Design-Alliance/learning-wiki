Epistemic network analysis compared structural characteristics and variability of feedback quality between the three conditions, using the coded feedback-quality units. The detailed ENA results section was beyond the provided text; this finding is stated in the abstract.

"The epistemic network analysis revealed that the PS-GenAI PF group had strong associations with negative emotions and higher-order feedback content, such as identification-suggestion and explanation-suggestion."
