Evidence¶

Sartain 2020¶

Sartain, L., Zou, A., Gutiérrez, V., Shyjka, A., Hinton, E., Brown, E.R., & Easton, J.Q. (2020). Teacher Evaluation in CPS: Perceptions of REACH Implementation, Five Years In. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Districtwide survey of approximately 12,000 CPS teachers plus administrators after five years of REACH implementation. Respondents reported perceived improvements: "About 70 percent of CPS teachers and administrators agreed that the teacher evaluation system improved student learning." Perceptions are self-reported, not measured outcomes.

"83 percent of teachers and 93 percent of administrators in Chicago Public Schools (CPS) said the teacher evaluation process has helped them improve classroom instruction. About 70 percent of CPS teachers and administrators agreed that the teacher evaluation system improved student learning."
