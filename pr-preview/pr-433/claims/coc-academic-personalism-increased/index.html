Evidence¶

Rachel Levenstein 2011¶

Rachel Levenstein, Sue Sporte, Elaine Allensworth. (2011). Findings from an Investigation into the Culture of Calm Initiative. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/findings-investigation-culture-calm-initiative

q2 · i3 · associational · r3

HLM analysis of student survey trends found Academic Personalism "improved for CoC schools between 2009 and 2011 compared to non-CoC schools"; the printed effect size is d=1.1. The authors note schools are quite similar on this measure, so small changes seem statistically substantial.

"This measure improved for CoC schools between 2009 and 2011 compared to non-CoC schools. We expected this measure to improve because perceived support from teachers might increase as a result of the attention paid to student safety."
