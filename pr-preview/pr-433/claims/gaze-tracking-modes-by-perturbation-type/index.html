Evidence¶

Inmaculada Márquez & Mario Treviño 2025¶

Inmaculada Márquez & Mario Treviño. (2025). Dissociable sensory, motor, and visuomotor predictive functions. npj Science of Learning 10:85. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00377-4

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Eye-tracking analysis (60 Hz binocular gaze) during the interception task with angular discrepancies: GTD and GUD amplitudes and AUCs both scaled significantly with discrepancy magnitude, described as "a coordinated use of visual prediction and motor monitoring".

"These results indicate that both externally and internally oriented gaze behavior con- tributed to compensatory responses during angular perturbations, reﬂecting a coordinated use of visual prediction and motor monitoring (Fig.2a)."

Inmaculada Márquez & Mario Treviño 2025 (2)¶

Inmaculada Márquez & Mario Treviño. (2025). Dissociable sensory, motor, and visuomotor predictive functions. npj Science of Learning 10:85. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00377-4

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Eye-tracking analysis under speed discrepancies: GUD increased strongly and significantly with magnitude while GTD slopes were non-significant, suggesting engagement of "self-referential monitor- ing". Gaze velocity and pupil diameter showed no reliable changes.

"These ﬁndings indicate that speed perturbations engage self-referential monitor- ing, possibly re ﬂecting increased reliance on proprioceptive feedback or internal velocity estimation (Fig.2b)."
