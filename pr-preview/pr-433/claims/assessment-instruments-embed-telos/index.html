Peter Smagorinsky 1995¶

Peter Smagorinsky. (1995). The Social Construction of Data: Methodological Problems of Investigating Learning in the Zone of Proximal Development. Review of Educational Research, 65(3), 191-212. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543065003191

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical argument in the Telos and Evidence section: the author argues that "any assessment procedure or instrument carries with it a sense of telos," illustrated by McCarthy and Gerring's account of the DSM revision's biomedical model. No data are reported; the support is the argument.

"Any assessment procedure or instrument carries with it a sense of telos; that is, it embodies the researcher's sense of an appropriate developmental path for people to follow, and produces data that identify people's progress or achievement according to the direction of that path."
