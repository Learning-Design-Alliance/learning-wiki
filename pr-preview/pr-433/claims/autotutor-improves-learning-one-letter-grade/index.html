Nicole C. Krämer & Gary Bente 2010¶

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Graesser et al. (2007), that "AutoTutor improves learning by nearly one letter grade compared with reading a textbook for an equivalent amount of time or in comparison with a pretest." No effect size is printed.

"Based on more systematic research in at least a dozen experiments, Graesser et al. (2007) concluded that AutoTutor improves learning by nearly one letter grade compared with reading a textbook for an equivalent amount of time or in comparison with a pretest."

Related Claims¶

  • AutoTutor's learning gains are virtually equivalent to those of 1-to-1 human tutoring in direct comparisons — related
