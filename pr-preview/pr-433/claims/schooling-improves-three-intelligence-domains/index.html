Evidence¶

Nicholas Judd 2022¶

Nicholas Judd, Bruno Sauce and Torkel Klingberg. (2022). Schooling substantially improves intelligence, but neither lessens nor widens the impacts of socioeconomics and genetics. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00148-5

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Regression discontinuity analysis over grades 3 to 5 in 6567 ABCD children, controlling for age, sex, cogPGS, SES, and 20 ancestry PCs. One year of schooling contributed "0.22 SD, 0.14 SD, and 0.14 SD" to cIQ, fIQ, and WM respectively.

"We found that schooling had a signiﬁcant effect (all p's < 0.001) for each cognitive domain we examined ( cIQ: β = 0.13, fIQ: β = 0.10, WM: β = 0.09)."
