Evidence¶

Manu Kapur 2012¶

Manu Kapur, Nikol Rummel. (2012). Productive failure in learning from generation and invention activities. Instr Sci 40:645-650. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-012-9235-4

q3 · i? · review · r2

Four-week, in vivo experiment with 76 university students relearning four topics of mathematical analysis for upcoming examinations, comparing TAU role-script support to direct instruction. No effect size is printed.

"Westermann and Rummel found that students in the TAU condition outperformed students in the DI condition in all weeks but the ﬁrst."
