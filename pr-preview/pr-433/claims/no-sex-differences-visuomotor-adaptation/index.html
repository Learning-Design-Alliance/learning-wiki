Malone 2025¶

Malone, L. A., Hill, N. M., Tripp, H., Zipunnikov, V., Wolpert, D. M. & Bastian, A. J. (2025). The control of movement gradually transitions from feedback control to feedforward adaptation throughout childhood. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00304-7

Null analysis in the Push ball sample testing sex effects on initial and final angles. The discussion attributes the contrast with Tsay et al. 2024 to the gradual perturbation, which likely minimized explicit strategy use, and notes video game usage was not assessed.
