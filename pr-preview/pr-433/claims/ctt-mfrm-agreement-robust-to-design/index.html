Evidence¶

Ilhan 2018¶

Ilhan, M., & Guler, N. (2018). A Comparison of Difficulty Indices Calculated for Open-Ended Items According to Classical Test Theory and Many Facet Rasch Model. Eurasian Journal of Educational Research, 75. doi:10.14689/ejer.2018.75.6

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Authors' discussion-level inference combining this crossed-design study with the nested-design study of Huang et al. (2014), which the review-style discussion describes as rating 124 grant applications with 64 experts. This is the authors' interpretation, not a single tested contrast.

"Therefore, upon considering the results obtained in Huang et al. (2014) and th ose obtained in this study, the CTT and the MFRM yielded similar results in terms of item difficulty indices for open -ended questions, no matter which design – crossed or nested – was used."
