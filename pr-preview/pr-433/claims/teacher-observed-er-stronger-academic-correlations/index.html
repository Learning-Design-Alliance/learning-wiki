Ivcevic Zorana; Eggers Catherine 2021¶

Ivcevic Zorana; Eggers Catherine. (2021). Emotion Regulation Ability: Test Performance and Observer Reports in Predicting Relationship, Achievement and Well-Being Outcomes in Adolescents. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8003666/

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Zero-order correlations in the same sample showed "teacher-observed emotion regulation was correlated with both academic outcomes (GPA: r = 0.33, p < 0.001 and academic outcomes: r = 0.38, p < 0.001)" compared with r = 0.28 for ERA on both measures.

"Similar to performance-measured ERA, teacher-observed emotion regulation was correlated with both academic outcomes (GPA: r = 0.33, p < 0.001 and academic outcomes: r = 0.38, p < 0.001) and both interpersonal outcomes (understanding others: r = 0.19, p = 0.005 and leadership potential: r = 0.20, p = 0.003)."

  • Emotion regulation variables predict interpersonal outcomes (peer nominations of understanding others and leadership potential) beyond Big Five traits and gender — related

  • Secondary academic performance is predicted by cognitive ability more strongly than by curiosity — related
  • Self-regulation and metacognition correlate strongly with mathematics achievement (r = .42 and .37) — related
