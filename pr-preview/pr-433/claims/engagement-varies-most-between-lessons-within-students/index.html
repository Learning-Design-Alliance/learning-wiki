Evidence¶

Pöysä 2018¶

Pöysä, S., Vasalampi, K., Muotka, J., Lerkkanen, M.-K., Poikkeus, A.-M., & Nurmi, J.-E. (2018). Variation in situation-specific engagement among lower secondary school students. Learning and Instruction, 53, 64-73. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2017.07.007

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Preliminary intra-class correlation analysis of the one-week lesson-to-lesson data (57 students, 1,328 ratings) in a two-level hierarchical structure, showing "varied most strongly between lessons and somewhat between students." Days within week showed only small ICCs.

"Thus, these results showed that students' behavioral/cognitive engagement, emotional engagement, and disaffection varied most strongly between lessons and somewhat between students."
