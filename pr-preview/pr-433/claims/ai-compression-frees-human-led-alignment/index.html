Authors' practitioner claim (core insight) about AI-enabled workflows, presented without data. Worked examples illustrate it: speculative UI generation and code-first design in V0 and Cursor moved quickly from concept to functional prototypes.

"By compressing the time to go from idea to prototype, UX teams can spend more time on the human-led work of understanding the problem and aligning your team around how to solve it."
