Evidence¶

Carrier 1992¶

Carrier, M., & Pashler, H. (1992). The influence of retrieval on retention. Memory & Cognition, 20(6), 633-642. https://doi.org/10.3758/bf03202713

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Laboratory experiment with 60 undergraduates learning 30 Eskimo/English pairs whose first two presentations were pure ST for all items; the third presentation was TTST or pure ST. Means were 9.0 (pure ST) and 10.1 (TTST); a sign test was reliable (z = 1.83, p < .05).

"More pairs from the TTST condition than the pure ST condition were correctly completed [F(1,59) = 11.52, MSe = 2.96,p < .005]."
