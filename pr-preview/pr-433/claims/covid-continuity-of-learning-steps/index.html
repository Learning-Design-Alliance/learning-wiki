Regional Educational Laboratory Mid-Atlantic 2020¶

Regional Educational Laboratory Mid-Atlantic. (2020). Keeping Pace: Strategies for Ensuring Equitable Continuity of Learning During the COVID-19 Pandemic, Fact Sheet. Regional Educational Laboratory Mid-Atlantic. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/edlabs/regions/mid-atlantic

q1 · i? · review · r?

A fact sheet published by Regional Educational Laboratory Mid-Atlantic in July 2020 frames its purpose as highlighting "evidence-based and promising practices" for local education agencies developing or updating continuity of learning plans; it reports no data or study results.

"What steps can schools take to ensure that students continue to progress academically whether at home or at school? This fact sheet highlights evidence-based and promising practices that local education agencies can consider when developing or updating their continuity of learning plans."
