Dupéré 2021¶

Dupéré, V., Dion, E., Cantin, S., Archambault, I., & Lacourse, E. (2021). Social contagion and high school dropout: The role of friends, romantic partners, and siblings. Journal of Educational Psychology, 113, 572-584. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000484

q3 · i3 · associational · r2

Model 3 of the logistic regressions on cases and matched controls (n = 366) tested cumulative exposure. The OR for two or more intimate types "was particularly large — almost twice the OR estimated for the occurrence of dropout in only one type of intimate (4.88/2.53 = 1.93)"; the abstract reports OR = 4.88, 95% CI 2.54-12.5.

"the OR for the occurrences of dropout among two types of intimates or more was particularly large — almost twice the OR estimated for the occurrence of dropout in only one type of intimate (4.88/2.53 = 1.93) — although both were significantly associated with dropout"
