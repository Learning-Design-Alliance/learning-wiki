Evidence¶

Pulfrey 2011¶

Pulfrey, C., Buchs, C., & Butera, F. (2011). Why grades engender performance-avoidance goals: The mediating role of autonomous motivation. Journal of Educational Psychology, 103(3), 683–700. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0023911

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Longitudinal field experiment with 97 students in six classes: a graded versus nongraded (or comment-only) exercise was manipulated, autonomous motivation was measured afterward with the Reported Autonomy Index, and pretask performance-avoidance goals were measured before a second similar exercise. The article reports that reduced autonomous motivation "mediated the relationship between grading and adoption of performance-avoidance goals".

"Experiment 3 showed that reduced autonomous motivation measured after having completed a task for a grade versus no grade mediated the relationship between grading and adoption of performance-avoidance goals in a subsequent task."
