Steinert 2024¶

Steinert, S., Avila, K. E., Ruzika, S., Kuhn, J., & Küchemann, S. (2024). Harnessing large language models to develop research-based learning assistants for formative feedback. Smart Learning Environments. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40561-024-00354-1

The authors' own observation while customizing and testing prompts within the LEAP interface for the Newton's cradle example. No systematic evaluation, criteria, or statistics are reported for this result.

"As expected, we found that more complex prompts tend to elicit superior results with advanced versions of LLMs."
