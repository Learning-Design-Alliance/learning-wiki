Evidence¶

Banihashem 2024¶

Banihashem, S. K., Taghizadeh Kerman, N., Noroozi, O., Moon, J., & Drachsler, H. (2024). Feedback sources in essay writing: peer-generated or AI-generated feedback? International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-024-00455-4

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Spearman's correlation analysis relating essay quality (coded on eight argumentative elements) to overall feedback quality from peers and ChatGPT. No significant overall relationship was found; equivalence was not tested and no overall effect size is printed.

"Overall, the results indicated that there was no significant relationship between the quality of essay writing and the feedback generated by peers and ChatGPT."
