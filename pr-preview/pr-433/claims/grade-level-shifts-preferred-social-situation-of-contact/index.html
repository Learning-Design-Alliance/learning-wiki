Interview study of boys in kindergarten, second, fourth, and sixth grades, reported in the digest as finding (b), Situation Appeal. The digest reports grade-linked preferences with the hedge "tend to prefer" for each grade level.

"Kindergarten and second grade children tend to prefer family situations. Fourth grade children tend to prefer being alone. Sixth grade children tend to prefer being with peers."
