Evidence¶

Chang 2025¶

Chang, A., Mauer, E., Wanzek, J., Kim, S., Scammacca, N., & Swanson, E. (2025). Examining the academic effects of cross-age tutoring: A meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review, 37, 19. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-09997-z

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Sensitivity analysis identified outliers by confidence-interval overlap criteria and re-ran meta-regression models. For tutees, 13 effect sizes from 5 studies were extremely small and 8 from 6 studies extremely large; one tutor study had an extremely large effect size.

"These results show no meaningful differences in the mean effect sizes from the tutor and tutee analyses before and after removing outliers."
