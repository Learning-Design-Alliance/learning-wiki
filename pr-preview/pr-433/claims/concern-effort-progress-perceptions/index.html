Evidence¶

Daniusevičiūtė-Brazaitė L 2026¶

Daniusevičiūtė-Brazaitė L, Gaižiūnienė L and Pučėtaitė R (2026) AI in inclusive education: teachers' visions of future-oriented practices. Front. Educ. 11:1890925. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1890925

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Descriptive statistics on 7-point Likert items from the questionnaire. The article also reports high concern (M = 6.00, SD = 1.15) and that participants "tended to associate AI with human progress rather than regression (M = 5.41, SD = 1.97)".

"Participants generally regarded their envisioned educational futures as plausible (M = 5.21, SD = 1.32) and perceived themselves as having some capacity to contribute to their realization (M = 4.90, SD = 1.39). At the same time, there was a particularly strong perception that the envisioned changes would require active effort from participants in the educational process rather than occurring spontaneously (M = 5.88, SD = 1.58)."
