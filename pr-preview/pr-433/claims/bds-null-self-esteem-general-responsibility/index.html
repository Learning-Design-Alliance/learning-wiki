q2 · i? · causal · r?

In the Leming (2001) quasi-experiment, Appendix A3 prints small WWC-computed effect sizes for these outcomes: self-esteem 0.07 (improvement index +3), social responsibility (general) 0.09 (+4), and anticipated community participation 0.22 (+9), all marked ns. Equivalence was not tested.
