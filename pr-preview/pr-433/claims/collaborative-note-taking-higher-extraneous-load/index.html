Jamie Costley 2021¶

Jamie Costley, Mik Fanguy. (2021). Collaborative note-taking affects cognitive load: the interplay of completeness and interaction. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-09979-2

Same two-sample t-test comparison of treatment (n = 74) and control (n = 97) groups. The extraneous load difference was "statistically sig-nificant (t = − 4.307, p < .000)"; the treatment mean was higher. No effect size was printed.
