Evidence¶

David W Chadwick 1999¶

David W Chadwick, Rana Tassabehji and Andrew Young. (1999). Experiences of Using a Public Key Infrastructure for the Preparation of Examination Papers. IT Institute, University of Salford. http://www.salford.ac.uk/its024/X500.htm

q1 · i? · design · r2

Process analysis of the pilot's electronic workflow: the administrator had to decrypt scripts from lecturers and re-encrypt to external examiners and back, and became "a source for the introduction of errors" by forwarding encrypted mail that recipients could not decrypt. Most lecturers questioned felt the administrator should oversee but not actively participate in the flow.

"Our findings reveal that the administrator now becomes a bottleneck in the new system, rather than providing a value-added function."
