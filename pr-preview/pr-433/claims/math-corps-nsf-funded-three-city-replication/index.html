Descriptive background from the brief: an NSF Advancing Informal STEM Learning grant awarded in 2015 enabled replication of the camp in Cleveland, Philadelphia, and Utica, and WSU contracted Mathematica to conduct an independent study of Math Corps.

"In 2015, the National Science Foundation awarded WSU an Advancing Informal Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics Learning grant, enabling the Math Corps summer camp to be replicated in three cities (Cleveland, Ohio; Philadelphia, Pennsylvania; and Utica, New York) in summers 2017, 2018, and 2019."
