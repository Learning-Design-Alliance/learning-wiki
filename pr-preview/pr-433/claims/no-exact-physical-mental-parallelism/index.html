De Sanctis 1911¶

De Sanctis, S. (1911). Mental development and the measurement of the level of intelligence. The Journal of Educational Psychology, 2, 498-507. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0069835

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical position on the body-mind parallelism question. The author believes the parallelism exists but concedes it is not evident in particular functions such as memory or suggestibility, and that correspondence is not always apparent.

"the correlations vary enormously from individual to individual, and the laws of correlation are probably far from general."
