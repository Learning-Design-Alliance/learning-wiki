WWC Intervention Report: Positive Action 2007¶

WWC Intervention Report: Positive Action. (2007). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://www.positiveaction.net

q3 · i? · causal · r?

In the Hawaii randomized controlled trial (2,660 grade 5 students in 20 schools), the WWC-reviewed findings show HCPS math proficiency effect size 0.17 and reading 0.19, both marked "ns" (not statistically significant). No statistically significant difference was detected; equivalence was not tested.

"The study also examined the impact on state standardized test scores in reading and math but found no statistically significant differences."
