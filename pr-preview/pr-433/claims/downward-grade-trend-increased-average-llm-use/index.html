Jošt 2024¶

Jošt, G.; Taneski, V.; Karakatič, S. (2024). The Impact of Large Language Models on Programming Education and Student Learning Outcomes. Appl. Sci. 14, 4115. https://doi.org/10.3390/app14104115

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Visual inspection of jittered scatterplots (Figure 3) relating reported LLM use to final grades in the thirty-two-participant experiment; the authors describe this as a visible trend, with formal significance tested separately via Spearman correlation.

"Looking at the overall use of LLMs, there is a visible trend of decreasing final grades with increased average LLM use."
