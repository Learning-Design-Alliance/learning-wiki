Evidence¶

Zanchi 2024¶

Zanchi, P., Mullier, E., Fornari, E., Guerrier de Dumast, P., Alemán-Gómez, Y., Ledoux, J.-B., Beaty, R., Hagmann, P., & Denervaud, S. (2024). Differences in spatiotemporal brain network dynamics of Montessori and traditionally schooled students. npj Science of Learning, 9, 45. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00254-6

q3 · i? · associational · r2

ANCOVA on connected-component characteristics with pedagogy as fixed factor and age as covariate (F statistic as printed); the article reports a significant age effect on CC height with no effect size given.

"However, the analysis revealed a signiﬁcant effect of age on the height of the CCs (F(1, 83) = 5.99, p < 0.016)."

Zanchi 2024 (2)¶

Zanchi, P., Mullier, E., Fornari, E., Guerrier de Dumast, P., Alemán-Gómez, Y., Ledoux, J.-B., Beaty, R., Hagmann, P., & Denervaud, S. (2024). Differences in spatiotemporal brain network dynamics of Montessori and traditionally schooled students. npj Science of Learning, 9, 45. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00254-6

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Null ANCOVA results for CC number and length against age, pedagogy and the age × pedagogy interaction, reported with p-values only and no effect sizes; equivalence was not tested.

"Neither the number of CCs, nor their length was related to participants’age, schooling experience (i.e., pedagogy), nor their interaction term (allps > 0.211)."
