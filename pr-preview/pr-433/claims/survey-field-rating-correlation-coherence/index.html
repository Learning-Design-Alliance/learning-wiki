Newmann 2001¶

Newmann, F. M., Smith, B., Allensworth, E., & Bryk, A. S. (2001). School Instructional Program Coherence: Benefits and Challenges. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Comparison of teacher survey coherence measures with field-study rubric ratings across 11 Chicago Annenberg Challenge elementary schools. The study found "a strong correlation of 0.70" between the two measures, supporting the validity of the survey-based measure used in achievement analyses.

"We found in the field sample of 11 schools, a strong correlation of 0.70 between teacher survey reports in 1997 about instructional coherence, and the researchers' ratings of each site, based on the 13-point rubric."
