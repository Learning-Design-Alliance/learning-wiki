Evidence¶

Thao M. T. Nguyen and Lap Q. Trinh 2011¶

Thao M. T. Nguyen and Lap Q. Trinh. (2011). Learners' Metacognitive Strategy Use and Reading Comprehension: Insights from a Vietnamese Context. i-manager's Journal of English Language Teaching, Vol. 1 No. 1, January - March 2011. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1071083 doi:10.26634/jelt.1.1.1348

q2 · i? · associational · r1

One-sample t-test on questionnaire data from 76 valid MARSI-based responses (α = .87) in a case study of grade-11 EFL learners; the mean "M= 3.29, SD= 0.55" exceeded the accepted average of 3.0, indicating above-average reported strategy use.

"The sample mean (M= 3.29, SD= 0.55) was significantly different from 3.0- the average point (t= 4.53, df= 75, p= .00). This means that participants' use of metacognitive strategies in reading is above average."
