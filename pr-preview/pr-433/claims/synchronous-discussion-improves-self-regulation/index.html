Evidence¶

Yorganci 2025¶

Yorganci, S. (2025). The impact of synchronous online discussions and online flipped learning on student engagement and self-regulation among preliminary undergraduates in a basic math course. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10459-0

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

The same two-way ANCOVA found a significant main effect of discussion type on self-regulation, F(1, 116) = 6.565, "η2 = .054". The synchronous group (M = 3.47) outperformed the asynchronous group (M = 3.31).

"the main effect of online discussion also showed a significant effect on self-regulation (F (1, 116) = 6.565, p > .05, η2 = .054)). According to the group means, the synchronous online discussion group (M = 3.47, SD = .35) outperformed the asynchronous online discussion group (M = 3.31, SD = .32) in self-regulation."
