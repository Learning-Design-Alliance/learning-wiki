WWC synthesis of two randomized controlled trials meeting evidence standards without reservations, covering 137 students in grades 5 and 6. The summary table prints an average improvement index of "+23" with range "–9 to +49" for alphabetics, based on 2 studies and 137 students, with extent of evidence rated "Small".
