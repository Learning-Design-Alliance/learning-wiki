Evidence¶

Tommi Kokkonen & Lennart Schalk 2021¶

Tommi Kokkonen & Lennart Schalk. (2021). One Instructional Sequence Fits all? A Conceptual Analysis of the Applicability of Concreteness Fading in Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and Biology Education. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09581-7

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

Narrative review of Johnson et al.'s study of students' procedural learning of electrical circuit problems. The review reports the concreteness introduction advantage in near transfer, better far transfer than concrete-only, and no difference relative to the idealized-only condition.

"Johnson and colleagues report that presenting conventional circuit diagrams before contextualized ones (i.e., concreteness introduction) produces better near transfer than does the reverse order (i.e., concreteness fading) or when only the concrete representations are presented."
