Mayer 2018¶

Mayer, I. (2018). Assessment of Teams in a Digital Game Environment. Simulation & Gaming, 49(6), 602-619. https://doi.org/10.1177/1046878118770831

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive assessment of the logged dataset of 106 teams. The study reports "The best performing team made only two errors, while the worst performing team made 37", with an average of 12 errors; time ranged from 15 to 68 minutes. These are descriptive statistics with no test reported.

"The best performing team made only two errors, while the worst performing team made 37. On average, teams made 12 errors, with most teams making around 10."
