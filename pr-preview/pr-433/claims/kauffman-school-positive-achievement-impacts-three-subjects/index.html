Quasi-experimental matched comparison group evaluation of the Kauffman School's first three school years (2011-2012 through 2013-2014). The report states the school had "positive, statistically significant, and educationally meaningful impacts" on achievement growth in all three subjects; no effect sizes are printed in the available text.
