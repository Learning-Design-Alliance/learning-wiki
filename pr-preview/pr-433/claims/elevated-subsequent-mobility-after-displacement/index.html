Evidence¶

Marisa de la Torre 2009¶

Marisa de la Torre, Julia Gwynne. (2009). When Schools Close: Effects on Displaced Students in Chicago Public Schools. Consortium on Chicago School Research, University of Chicago Urban Education Institute. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/when-schools-close-effects-displaced-students-chicago-public-schools

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Mobility analysis comparing displaced and matched comparison students (Figures 4 and 5): school-year moves 10.8%** vs 4.9% (p < 0.01) and summer moves 30.4%** vs 13.6% (p < 0.01); prior to closing, displaced students had lower school-year mobility (6.8% vs 8.1%).

"during their first year in a new school, they were twice as likely to change schools as the comparison group: nearly 11 percent of displaced students changed schools, while only 5 percent of students in the comparison group with similar characteristics did so"
