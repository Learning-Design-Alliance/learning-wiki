Evidence¶

Watts 1991¶

Watts, Mike; Bentley, Di. (1991). An Instructional 'Mali-Trap': Anthropomorphic and Animistic Thought in Constructivist Science Education. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED336269

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

In the authors' classroom data, 14-year-olds learning about fuses gave mostly mechanistic explanations (hot wires melting, electricity scorching), with only Kim's 'excited' electricity anthropomorphic. The authors state anthropomorphic explanations were "present in older children, butless frequently".
