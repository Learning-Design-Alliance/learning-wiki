The review's narrative synthesis of prior AI-motivation findings in its introduction. It attributes positive effects to some studies, nonsignificant effects to others, and negative-consequence concerns to further work, citing each by name.

"While some evidence points to AI’s capacity to enhance motivation (Wu & Yu, 2024), other studies report nonsignificant effects (e.g., Hou et al., 2022). Still other studies raise concerns about its potential negative consequences (e.g., Abbas et al., 2024; Bastani et al., 2025)."
