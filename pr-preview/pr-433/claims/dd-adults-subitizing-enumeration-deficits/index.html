Liane Kaufmann 2020¶

Liane Kaufmann, Michael von Aster, Silke M. Göbel, Josef Marksteiner, and Elise Klein. (2020). Developmental Dyscalculia in Adults: Current Issues and Open Questions for Future Research. Lernen und Lernstörungen, 9(2), 126–137. https://doi.org/10.1024/2235-0977/a000294

q2 · i? · review · r2

Review reports Gliksman and Henik (2019) enumeration study: adults with DD showed a "smaller subitizing range (i. e., 3 instead of 4 elements)" versus controls plus a larger alerting effect; alerting did not facilitate subitizing. Cohen et al. (2019) extended this, finding DD adults less accurate on random visual and tactile sets.

"According to Gliksman and Henik (2019), adults with DD have a smaller subitizing range (i. e., 3 instead of 4 elements) compared with controls and moreover, present a larger alerting effect"
