Herbert W. Marsh 2023¶

Herbert W. Marsh, Jiesi Guo, Philip D. Parker, Reinhard Pekrun, Geetanjali Basarkod, Theresa Dicke, et al. (2023). Peer Victimization: an Integrative Review and Cross-National Test of a Tripartite Model. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09765-x

q2 · i1 · review · r3

The review reports that Marsh et al. (2011) found "boys experienced much more physical victimization (ES = − .30) and slightly more verbal victimization (− .11)" across adolescence, while boys did not differ significantly from girls in relational victimization.

"Marsh et al. (2011) found that across adolescence, boys experienced much more physical victimization (ES = − .30) and slightly more verbal victimization (− .11)."

Herbert W. Marsh 2023 (2)¶

Herbert W. Marsh, Jiesi Guo, Philip D. Parker, Reinhard Pekrun, Geetanjali Basarkod, Theresa Dicke, et al. (2023). Peer Victimization: an Integrative Review and Cross-National Test of a Tripartite Model. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09765-x

q3 · i? · associational · r2

The article's own PISA2018 cross-national results showed that "gender differences varied significantly across the three components", supporting the need to treat the components separately; no component-specific effect sizes are printed in the supplied text.

"For example, gender differences varied significantly across the three components, and all 13 well-being indicators were more strongly related to verbal and particularly relational victimization than physical victimization."
