Author position statement in the eBook's AI chapter, citing Bubeck et al. (2023). The article offers no empirical test of its own; it argues AI "needs more specific contextual understanding and subject matter expertise for accurate evaluation" while remaining optimistic about future applications that enhance equitable access without compromising quality and trust.

"Assessing skills and competencies is crucial in micro-credentialing, but using AI for assessments, especially competency-based work, is currently not the best practice (Bubeck et al., 2023)."
