Elaine Allensworth 2019¶

Elaine Allensworth. (2019). Elementary School Case Studies. University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/how-do-principals-influence-student-achievement

q1 · i? · qualitative · r1

Interview evidence from Ivy Elementary's case study. Teams set targets for NWEA reading, math growth, and attendance; Principal Walker stated that on school improvement and materials decisions, "teachers are involved and included".

"Thus, the teachers take the lead in creating their specific grade-level goals, and they also provide feedback on the goal-setting of other teams of teachers."
