Achraf Ammar 2024¶

Achraf Ammar, Khaled Trabelsi, Mohamed Ali Boujelbane, Atef Salem, Omar Boukhris, Jordan M. Glenn, Piotr Zmijewski, Haitham A. Jahrami, Hamdi Chtourou, Wolfgang I. Schöllhorn. (2024). The Effects of Contextual Interference Learning on the Acquisition and Relatively Permanent Gains in Skilled Performance: A Critical Systematic Review with Multilevel Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09892-z

q4 · i0 · quant-synthesis · r3

Multilevel (three-level) meta-analysis of 36 included studies pooling 183 performance outcomes from healthy participants (total 1135; low CI n = 572, high CI n = 563). The pooled acute gain comparison showed "No statistically significant overall difference" with ES = 0.1, p = 0.35; mean gains were 28.9 ± 59.5% (low) vs 27.9 ± 52.8% (high).

"No statistically significant overall difference was detected for “Δ pre-post” between low (blocked) (28.9 ± 59.5%) and high (random/serial) (27.9 ± 52.8%) CI (effect size (ES) = 0.1, p = 0.35)."
