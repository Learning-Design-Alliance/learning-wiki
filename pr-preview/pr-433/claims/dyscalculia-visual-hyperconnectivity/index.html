Evidence¶

Bulthé Jessica 2019¶

Bulthé Jessica, Prinsen Jellina, Vanderauwera Jolijn, Duyck Stefanie, Daniels Nicky, Gillebert Céline R., Mantini Dante, Op de Beeck Hans P., De Smedt Bert. (2019). Multi-method brain imaging reveals impaired representations of number as well as altered connectivity in adults with dyscalculia. Neuroimage, 190, 289-302. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2018.06.012

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Resting-state functional connectivity analysis between the eight level-III seed ROIs in 24 dyscalculia and 24 control participants; Pearson correlations were Fisher r-to-Z transformed and group differences tested with t-tests at pFDR < 0.05.

"there were significant group differences for the connectivity between PVC (primary visual cortex) and IOC (t46 = −3.17, pFDR = 0.04) and between PVC and FG (t46 = −3.47, pFDR = 0.03) with higher connectivity in individuals with dyscalculia than in controls"
