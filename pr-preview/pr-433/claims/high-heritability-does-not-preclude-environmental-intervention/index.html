Evidence¶

David Cesarini and Peter M. Visscher 2017¶

David Cesarini and Peter M. Visscher. (2017). Genetics and educational attainment. npj Science of Learning 2:4. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-017-0005-6

q1 · i? · review · r2

Theoretical argument in the review's interpretation section: the authors label the inference from substantial heritabilities to doomed interventions "a common albeit mistaken inference", supporting it with examples rather than new data.

"One common albeit mistaken inference is that since all the four traits studied appear to have substantial heritabilities, efforts to modify them through environmental interventions are doomed to failure."

David Cesarini and Peter M. Visscher 2017 (2)¶

David Cesarini and Peter M. Visscher. (2017). Genetics and educational attainment. npj Science of Learning 2:4. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-017-0005-6

q1 · i? · review · r3

Illustrative counterexample in the same argument: "the provision of eye-glasses could yield large returns" even where "the heritability of eye sight is near 100%".

"For example, the provision of eye-glasses could yield large returns in a population where very few children have eyeglasses and the heritability of eye sight is near 100%."
