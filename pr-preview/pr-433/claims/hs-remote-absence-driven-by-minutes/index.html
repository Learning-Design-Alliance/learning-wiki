Evidence¶

Gwynne 2024¶

Gwynne, J.A., Allensworth, E.M., Young, C., & Hart, H. (2024). Student experiences through the COVID-19 pandemic: Attendance, online learning platform use, and annual survey reports. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://www.cps.edu/about/district-data/metrics/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Comparison of 2020-21 official absence rates against fall 2020 Google Meets non-login days for 270,981 students in district-run schools. The report found non-login days were "about one-half the absence rate for high school students," suggesting partial rather than full-day non-participation.

"Official year-long absence rates for high school students in the 2020–21 school year ranged from 15% to 19%. The proportion of days in fall 2020 without a Google Meets login was about one-half the absence rate for high school students, ranging from 7-10%."
