Evidence¶

Kollar 2006¶

Kollar, I., Fischer, F. & Hesse, F.W. (2006). Collaboration Scripts – A Conceptual Analysis. Educational Psychology Review, 18, 159–185. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-006-9007-2

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

The article's own conceptual analysis of prototypical script approaches, comparing face-to-face and computer-mediated lines of research. The analysis finds face-to-face scripts emphasize "individual knowledge acquisition" while computer-mediated scripts emphasize coordination among group members.

"As our analysis reveals, collaboration scripts for face-to-face learning often focus on supporting collaborators in engaging in activities that are specifically related to individual knowledge acquisition."
