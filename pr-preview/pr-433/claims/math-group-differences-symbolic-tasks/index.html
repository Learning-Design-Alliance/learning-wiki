Nurit Viesel-Nordmeyer 2023¶

Nurit Viesel-Nordmeyer, Julia Reuber, Jörg-Tobias Kuhn, Kristina Moll, Heinz Holling, Christian Dobel. (2023). Cognitive Profiles of Children with Isolated and Comorbid Learning Difficulties in Reading and Math: a Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09735-3

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Post hoc moderator testing (Tukey's HSD, Holm-corrected) on math outcome categories coded via the Triple-Code Model in the meta-analysis. Differences were "higher differences between groups in math tasks addressing symbolic verbal-auditory and symbolic visual processing" than non-symbolic tasks.

"Results for "type of math outcomes" in C-RDMD as well as MD-RDMD indicated higher differences between groups in math tasks addressing symbolic verbal-auditory and symbolic visual processing compared to math tasks addressing non-symbolic approximate processing."
