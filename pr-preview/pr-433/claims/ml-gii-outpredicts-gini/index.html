Evidence¶

Li Zhang 2024¶

Li Zhang, Baolige Chao, Yan Gao, Wenjing Wang, Yingzi Yuan, Chuangsheng Chen & Ziqiang Xin. (2024). Gender Inequality is negatively associated with academic achievement for both boys and girls. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00261-7

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Student-level "horse race" using LASSO, random forest, and XGBoost for the 2018 PISA shows GII "exhibits near-topranking explanatory power" and "consistently surpassing the predictive strength of the Gini coefficient"; results hold when boys' and girls' data are analysed separately. No effect size is printed.

"All three machine learning results (see Table9) show that GII exhibits near-topranking explanatory power for academic performance among the three country-level variables, consistently surpassing the predictive strength of the Gini coefficient, which is consistent with the results from the multilevel mixed-effects model."
