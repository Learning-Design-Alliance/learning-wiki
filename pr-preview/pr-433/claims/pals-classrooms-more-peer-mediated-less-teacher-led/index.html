Implementation-fidelity check within the Saenz, Fuchs, and Fuchs (2005) randomized controlled trial: lesson plans of PALS and business-as-usual comparison teachers were compared, showing PALS teachers "more likely than comparison teachers to use one-on-one activities and peer-mediated instruction".
