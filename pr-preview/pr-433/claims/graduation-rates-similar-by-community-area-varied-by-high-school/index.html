Descriptive analysis of 2019 CPS graduation rates organized by the 77 community areas where students live versus by high school, from the report's key findings. The report states rates were "similar across community areas" while the by-high-school range was 49 to 99 percent; 29 of 127 schools exceeded 90 percent and 19 fell below 70 percent.

"In 2019, high school graduation rates were similar across community areas; in most community areas the rates were between 70 and 90 percent. However, the rates for the same students by high school ranged from 49 percent to 99 percent."
