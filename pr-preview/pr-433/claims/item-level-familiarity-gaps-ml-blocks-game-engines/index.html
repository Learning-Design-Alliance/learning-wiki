Developing computational thinking in teacher education: a brief experience with coding, robotics, and machine learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10744-6

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Item-level descriptive analysis of all 20 questionnaire items within the experimental group (n = 105). The article also reports machine-learning knowledge (A2; M = 2.80, SD = 0.87; 38.1% lower-category) and game-engine programming (B5; M = 2.88, SD = 0.80; 33.3% lower-category) as less favorable items; these are descriptive statistics with no inferential test.

"The clearest area of lower self-reported familiarity concerned prior experience with visual block-based programming at university (A5; M = 2.50, SD = 0.95), for which 53.3% of participants selected one of the two lower response categories."
