What Works Clearinghouse 2006¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2006). WWC Intervention Report: Too Good for Drugs. https://www.mendezfoundation.org

q3 · i2 · causal · r?

End-of-program findings from the Bacon (2003) randomized controlled trial reported in Appendix A4.1: intervention mean 3.79 (0.87) versus comparison 3.37 (0.72), mean difference 0.42, effect size 0.52, improvement index +20, marked ns after WWC clustering correction. Personal and social skills (0.29) and inappropriate behaviors (0.16) showed smaller effects.
