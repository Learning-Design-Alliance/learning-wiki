Evidence¶

Arsalidou 2016¶

Arsalidou, M. & Pascual-Leone, J. (2016). Constructivist developmental theory is needed in developmental neuroscience. npj Science of Learning 1, 16016. https://doi.org/10.1038/npjscilearn.2016.16

q2 · i? · review · r1

The review reports, citing prior work, this mathematical method for recognising invariant stages. It notes whether such mathematical methods could detect developmental stages in the brain remains unknown, while task-analytical constructivist methods appear to detect them.

"researchers have used hidden semi-Markov models for a multi-voxel pattern analysis of mathematical problem-solving tasks, which yields a sequence of invariants indexing stages of problem solving; these mathematically obtained brain processing stages conﬁrm the rationally expected steps or stages of problem solving"
