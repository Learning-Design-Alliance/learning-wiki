Evidence¶

Yanjun Pan 2024¶

Yanjun Pan, Elizabeth L. Adams, Leanne R. Ketterlin-Geller, Eric C. Larson, Corey Clark. (2024). Enhancing middle school students' computational thinking competency through game-based learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10400-x

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

Mixed factorial repeated measures ANOVA (n = 19) testing group-variable by time interactions on engagement subscales. The lessons-by-gender interaction on Perceived Usability was significant, F(2.127, 36.158) = 5.47, p = .007, η2 = .24; female means decreased from Tutorial to Lesson 2 then rose, while male means decreased from Lesson 1 to Lesson 3.

"There was a significant interaction between different lessons (i.e., time points) and gender, F(2.127, 36.158) = 5.47, p = .007, η2 = .24."
