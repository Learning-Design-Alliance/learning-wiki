Alexandra Patzak 2025¶

Alexandra Patzak, Xiaorong Zhang. (2025). Blending Teacher Autonomy Support and Provision of Structure in the Classroom for Optimal Motivation: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-09994-2

q3 · i3 · quant-synthesis · r3

Random-effects meta-analysis of 110 correlations between autonomy support and structure drawn from 94 studies (85 journal articles, 9 dissertations). The pooled correlation was "r = 0.53, SE = 0.03", a large effect by the Funder–Ozer/Gignac–Szodorai benchmarks the article adopts.
