Evidence¶

Hilger 2025¶

Hilger, K. J. E., Scheibe, S., Frenzel, A. C., Grund, S., & Keller, M. M. (2025). Exploring paths of change: Analyzing teachers’ emotion regulation repertoire over time using multi-level latent profile analysis. Learning and Instruction, 101, Article 102234. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102234

q3 · i? · associational · r?

The study applied multilevel latent profile analyses to daily-diary assessments of 12 ER strategies at two time points, identifying profiles "at the person level". The analysis yielded "Five ER profiles emerged, of which three were common and two unique to the two bursts". No effect sizes are printed for this finding.

"Five ER profiles emerged, of which three were common and two unique to the two bursts, differing in the specific combination of strategies."
