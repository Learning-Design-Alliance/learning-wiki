Sartain 2014¶

Sartain, L., Allensworth, E., & Porter, S. (2014). Suspending Chicago's Students: Differences in Discipline across Schools. UChicago Consortium. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/suspending-chicagos-students-differences-discipline-practices-across-schools

q2 · i? · associational · r?

District-wide observational analysis of Chicago discipline data reported in the Consortium report, comparing the contribution of school attended versus student background characteristics to suspension risk. The report states students' risk of suspension is "more strongly determined by which school they attend than by their backgrounds".

"Students’ risk of suspension is more strongly determined by which school they attend than by their backgrounds—including their race, gender or income."
