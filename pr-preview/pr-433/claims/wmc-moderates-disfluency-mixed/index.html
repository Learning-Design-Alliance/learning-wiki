Evidence¶

Tim Kühl & Alexander Eitel 2016¶

Tim Kühl & Alexander Eitel. (2016). Effects of disfluency on cognitive and metacognitive processes and outcomes. Metacognition and Learning, 11, 1–13. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-016-9154-x

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative review of Lehmann et al. (2015), an aptitude-treatment-interaction study with 65 university students learning a 1070-word expository text. The review reports that "working memory capac ity was related to retention and compre- hension outcomes in the disfluent but not in the fluent font conditions"; no effect size is printed.
