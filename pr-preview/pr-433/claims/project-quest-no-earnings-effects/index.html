q4 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

WWC review of the Elliott & Roder (2017) RCT (n = 410 for earnings outcomes). Table 4 reports year-5 earnings of $24,713 versus $20,792 (effect size 0.18, p = 0.10), year-7 earnings effect size 0.11 (p = 0.28), and year-3 earnings effect size 0.11 (p = 0.26), all not statistically significant.

"The WWC determined that one study that meets WWC group design standards without reservations (Elliott & Roder, 2017) showed evidence of an indeterminate effect of Project QUEST on medium-term earnings."
