Henk G. Schmidt & Jerome I. Rotgans 2021¶

Henk G. Schmidt & Jerome I. Rotgans. (2021). Epistemic Curiosity and Situational Interest: Distant Cousins or Identical Twins?. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09539-9

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Manipulation check counting evaporation-explanation mentions in students' written responses. Control students mentioned it more ("Mcontrol =. 3 4" vs "Mtreatment =. 1 5"), F(1,146) = 5.70, p = .018, confirming differential prior knowledge.
