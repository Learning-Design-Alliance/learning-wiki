Chen-Chen Liu, Gwo-Jen Hwang, Peng Yu, Yun-Fang Tu, Youmei Wang. (2025). Effects of an automated corrective feedback-based peer assessment approach on students' learning achievement, motivation, and self-regulated learning conceptions in foreign language pronunciation. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10484-z

Semi-structured interviews with 10 students per group (162 coded responses); thematic analysis identified four categories of perceived advantages, with students describing the AI tool as "convenient" and praising its accuracy and personalized guidance.

"the researcher adopted thematic analysis to classify the students' responses about the advantages into four main categories, namely "technical benefits," "enriched learning content," "optimized learning process," and "motivated to think deeply""
