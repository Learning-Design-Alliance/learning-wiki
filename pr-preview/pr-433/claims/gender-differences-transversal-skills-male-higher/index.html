Okada 2024¶

Survey analysis by gender; the article reports 80% of male students scored above 3, compared with 77% of female students (the printed sentence contains a typo repeating "male"), and 67% among students of other genders.

"Gender differences showed that 80% of the male students had slightly greater perceptions of transversal skills than 77% of the male students. This rate dropped to 67% among students who identied with a sex other than male or female."
