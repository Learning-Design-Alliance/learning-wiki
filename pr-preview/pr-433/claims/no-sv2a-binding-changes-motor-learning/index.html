Evidence¶

Hehl 2026¶

Hehl, M., Toyonaga, T., Carson, R. E., Dupont, P., Van Laere, K., Swinnen, S. P. & Cuypers, K. (2026). No significant changes in synaptic density and gray matter volume following motor learning—a pilot study. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00451-5

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Linear mixed models per VOI on [18F]SynVesT-1 SUVR (PRE vs POST) in the 22-participant pilot, with SUBJECT as random effect and GROUP and TIMING as fixed effects. No main effects or interaction reached significance in any of the six visuomotor VOIs.

"Neither GROUP, nor TIMING, nor their interaction signiﬁcantly inﬂuenced SUVR in any of the VOIs (main effect TIMING: all,p > 0.23)"

Hehl 2026 (2)¶

Hehl, M., Toyonaga, T., Carson, R. E., Dupont, P., Van Laere, K., Swinnen, S. P. & Cuypers, K. (2026). No significant changes in synaptic density and gray matter volume following motor learning—a pilot study. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00451-5

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Correlation analyses between baseline SUVR or SUVR change and BTT training progress in the same pilot. For both groups pooled, all p > 0.24 and |r| < 0.26; per group, all p > 0.17 and |r| < 0.45. No effect sizes were reported as significant.

"There were no correlations between SUVRPRE and BTT training progress"
