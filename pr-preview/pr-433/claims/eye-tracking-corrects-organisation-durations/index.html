Evidence¶

Yizhou Fan 2022¶

Yizhou Fan, Lyn Lim, Joep van der Graaf, Jonathan Kilgour, Mladen Raković, Johanna Moore, Inge Molenaar, Maria Bannert, Maria Bannert, Dragan Gašević. (2022). Improving the measurement of self-regulated learning using multi-channel data. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09304-z

q2 · i? · design · r2

Descriptive duration analysis across the three channels for 25 participants. The article reports the Organisation median dropped to 4.58% with eye-tracking because Enhanced_log NOTE_EDITING actions were inaccurately measured without gaze data showing when learners returned to reading.

"the First-reading processes detected from the Log+eye-tracking data channel accounted for 49.86% of the whole learning sessions, which was shorter duration than that of the First-reading detected from the Nav_only data (65.09%)"

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Adding peripheral data to navigational logs sharply changes the detected distribution of SRL processes, revealing many more Organisation and far fewer First-reading processes
  • Adding data channels refines roughly 29–52% of detected SRL process time, measured by a new percent-of-processes-refined metric
  • Only with eye-tracking data does Monitoring appear as an intermediate process linking most other SRL processes in the process map — related
  • Most refinements from adding peripheral data convert First-reading or Re-reading into Organisation, and eye-tracking then corrects many back to First-reading — related
