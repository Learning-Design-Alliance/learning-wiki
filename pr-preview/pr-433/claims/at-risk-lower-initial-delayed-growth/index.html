Verkerk 2026¶

Verkerk, J. C., van de Rijt, B., & van Viersen, S. (2026). Development of numerical relational and counting skills as components of early numeracy. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102313

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Multigroup LGCM comparison of at-risk (n = 120) and typically developing (n = 294) children in Cohort 1, defined by a z-score cut-off below -.67 at T1; the reported summary states at-risk learners "displayed lower initial levels and delayed growth, especially in counting skills".
