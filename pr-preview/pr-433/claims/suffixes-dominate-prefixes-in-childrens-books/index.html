Evidence¶

Korochkina 2025¶

Korochkina, M. & Rastle, K. (2025). Morphology in children's books, and what it means for learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00313-6

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Structural analysis of multimorphemic words in CYP-LEX (Fig. 1). Suffixes occur in 66%, 66%, and 67% of complex words across the three age bands versus prefixes in 27%, 28%, and 29%; most suffixed words contain no prefixes (83%, 82%, 80%).

"in each age band, more than two thirds of complex words include at least one suffix (66%, 66%, and 67% in the 7–9, 10–12, and 13+ age bands, respectively). In contrast, prefixes are encountered in less than one third of multimorphemic words (27%, 28%, and 29% in the 7–9, 10–12, and 13+ age bands, respectively)."
