E. B. Skaggs 1918¶

E. B. Skaggs. (1918). The Correlation of General Intelligence Tests and School Standing. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org/journal/edu

q2 · i? · associational · r1

An observational correlational study of 153 normal-school students (mostly women): Army Alpha scores were correlated with school standing computed as the average of final marks for one term. The article reports "r=+.31—.039 (Pearson's Product-Moment Formula)" with mean G. I. score of 117.1 points and mean S. S. grade of 81.00.

"The writer gave the Alpha test to juniors and seniors in the Millersville (Pa.) State Normal School and, after dropping those cases having no previous Normal School record, had left 153 individuals (mostly women)."
