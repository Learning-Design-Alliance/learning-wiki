A tribute commentary (Mandell) reports, citing Kitsantas and Zimmerman (2006), a dart-throwing study in which "process and transitional goals could outperform outcome goals." The tribute offers no effect sizes or sample details; the finding is second-hand.

"Using dart-throwing as the learning task, the study elegantly demonstrated how process and transitional goals could outperform outcome goals."
