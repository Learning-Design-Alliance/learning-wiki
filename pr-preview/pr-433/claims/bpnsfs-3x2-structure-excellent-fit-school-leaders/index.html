Marsh 2025¶

Marsh, H. W., Ryan, R. M., Dicke, T., Pekrun, R., Guo, J., Bradshaw, E. L., Reeve, J., Lüdtke, O., Clarke, T., & Waterschoot, J. (2025). Basic Psychological Needs Under Constrained Autonomy: A Substantive–Methodological Reflection and Analysis of School Leaders' Needs from a Self-Determination Theory Perspective. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10079-3

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Measurement-model analysis of two-wave survey data from 1,950 Australian school leaders. The 12-factor ESEM with target rotation showed excellent fit, with the article reporting "RMSEA = .012; CFI = .991; TLI = .989", target loadings M = .68, and small cross-loadings (M = .02).

"The best-fitting 3×2 model with loading invariance and correlated uniquenesses showed excellent fit (RMSEA = .012; CFI = .991; TLI = .989; Table 1). Target loadings were moderate–strong (M = .68; range = .41–.90) and cross-loadings were small (M = .02)."
