Analytical estimation using formulas based on ordinary least squares and empirical Bayes estimators applied to student test score gain data for upper elementary grades. The article reports that "error rates for comparing a teacher’s performance to the average are likely to be about 25 percent with three years of data and 35 percent with one year of data."
