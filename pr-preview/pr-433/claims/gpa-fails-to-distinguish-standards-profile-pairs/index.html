Post hoc Tukey pairwise comparisons of mean GPAs among the five clusters; the article reports the three named pairs "do not differ in GPA". The authors state the latent groups captured attainment differences the GPAs did not. No effect size is printed.

"Specifically, the following three group pairs do not differ in GPA: a) the Engaging and effective group and the Knowledgeable and strategic group, b) the Novice group and the Student-centred group, c) the Engaging and effective group and the Student-centred group."
