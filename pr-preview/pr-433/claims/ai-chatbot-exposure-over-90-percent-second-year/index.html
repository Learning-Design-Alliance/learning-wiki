Evidence¶

Ruishi Chen 2026¶

Ruishi Chen, Victor R. Lee, Annie Camey Kuo, Denise Clark Pope, Sarah Miles. (2026). Cheating in the second year of generative AI chatbots: a follow-up study on high school student cheating behaviors. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10587-1

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Descriptive survey analysis of the AI chatbot exposure item in the 2023-24 six-school survey, asked of all respondents. The article reports the "more than 90%" figure and contrasts it with prior-year private and public school use rates of 35.6% and 54.0%. No inferential test of change was applied.

"Overall, at the time of the survey for the 2023–2024 academic year, more than 90% of respondents reported either knowing about or having used an AI chatbot."
