Evidence¶

Jake O'Brien 2026¶

Jake O'Brien, Margaret Ellis, Alma Robinson, John Simonetti, and Naren Ramakrishnan. (2026). Skepticism vs. Convenience: Physics Students' Perceptions and Use of Large Language Models Before and After Instruction. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21037 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.21037

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pre-lesson Likert survey of the FYE physics cohort on usage reasons and comfort. The article reports "strong agreement on motivation-convenience (71% agreement, 15% disagreement)" and deadline pressure, alongside self-reported weekly usage of 60% for guidance/hints; descriptive percentages only.

"Regarding the reasons for using LLMs, we see strong agreement on motivation-convenience (71% agreement, 15% disagreement) and motivation-deadlines (65%, 19%)."
