Evidence¶

Meier 2024¶

Meier, J., Hesse, P., Abele, S., Renkl, A., & Glogger-Frey, I. (2024). Better self-explaining backwards or forwards? Prompting self-explanation in video-based modelling examples for learning a diagnostic strategy. Instructional Science, 52, 613–638. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-023-09651-7

q3 · i? · causal · r2

ANOVA on posttest-only load measures in the prompt-condition comparison; extraneous load F(1, 40) = 5.394, p = .025, η2partial = 0.119, with descriptive means of 3.89 (retrospective) versus 3.01 (anticipatory). The authors write that "ECL was lower in the anticipatory prompt group".
