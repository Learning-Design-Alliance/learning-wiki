q1 · i? · design · r2

Theoretical argument in the framework-rationale section. The authors further state that Marzano's framework situates metacognitive reflection and ethics within its metacognitive system and self-system, offering clear theoretical positioning that the revised Bloom taxonomy lacked.

"Bloom's unidimensional framework could not accommodate these three types of knowledge simultaneously, whereas Marzano's knowledge domain dimension provided clear theoretical classification criteria."
