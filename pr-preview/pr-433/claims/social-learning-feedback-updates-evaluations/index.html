Evidence¶

Danni Chen 2024¶

Danni Chen, Tao Xia, Ziqing Yao, Lingqi Zhang & Xiaoqing Hu. (2024). Modulating social learning-induced evaluation updating during human sleep. npj Science of Learning 9:43. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00255-5

q3 · i0 · causal · r2

Repeated-measures ANOVA in the overnight sleep lab experiment with 34 analyzed participants: when peers' evaluations were higher than participants' initial evaluations, "participants'evaluations increased accordingly." The feedback main effect on immediate evaluation change was significant, F(1, 33) = 23.42, p < 0.001, η²G = 0.18.

"feedback signiﬁcantly modulated immediate ΔEvaluation (i.e., changes of evaluation from pre- to post-learning;F (1, 33) = 23.42,p < 0.001,η2 G = 0.18"
