Evidence¶

Huang 2026¶

Huang, S. C.-F., Chen, Z.-H., Lin, Y.-T., & Yeh, M. K. (2026). Bridging strategy gaps in programming: the role of visual scaffolding for learners with low cognitive ability. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10684-1

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Between-group comparison of 17 high-EF learners (9 EG, 9 CG per the article's assignment, analyzed with t (16)) on eye-movement measures. All comparisons showed "no significant differences between EG and CG among high-EF learners"; equivalence was not tested.

"independent samples t-tests revealed no significant differences between EG and CG among high-EF learners across all three measures: fixation duration (top-down tracking window: t (16) = −.541, p >.05, Hedges' g = −.121"
