Multivariate time series regression (dynamic structural equation modeling) of rubric-rated engagement across video intervals (Research Question 2). Table 4 prints DE(t-1)→BEt = 0.25 and DE(t-1)→CEt = 0.30, described as "a small-to-moderate effect of 0.30 also measured on a correlation metric."

"The results suggest that DE at time t – 1 is associated with increases in both BE and CE at the subsequent rating occasion, time t (see Table 4 for numeric results and Fig. 4 for a visualization)."
