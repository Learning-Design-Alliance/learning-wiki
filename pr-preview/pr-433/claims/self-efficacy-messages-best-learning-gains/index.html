DeFalco 2018¶

Experiment 2 (Fall 2015, 124 cadets) compared control-value, social identity, self-efficacy, non-motivational, and no-message conditions in TC3Sim via GIFT. The article identifies "the best condition (self-efficacy messages)" as advancing to Experiment 3; detailed statistics appear in the truncated portion.
