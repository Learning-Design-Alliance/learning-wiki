Martin 2026¶

Martin, A. J., Malmberg, L.-E., Holliman, A. J., Teig, N., Putwain, D. W., Collie, R. J., Burns, E. C., Bostwick, K. C. P., Kennett, R., & Pearson, J. (2026). Academic buoyancy and adaptability: A multi-sample study of reciprocal effects—Supplementary materials. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/edu/

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Measurement invariance testing preceding the latent cross-lagged modeling: invariance criteria "was first met" with ∆CFI = .004 and ∆RMSEA = .008 from configural to scalar and metric models, per criteria attributed to Chen (2007) and Cheung & Rensvold (2002).

"Criteria for fundamental measurement invariance between the studies was first met (∆CFI = .004 and ∆RMSEA = .008 from configural to scalar and metric models; Chen, 2007; Cheung & Rensvold, 2002)."

Martin 2026 (2)¶

Martin, A. J., Malmberg, L.-E., Holliman, A. J., Teig, N., Putwain, D. W., Collie, R. J., Burns, E. C., Bostwick, K. C. P., Kennett, R., & Pearson, J. (2026). Academic buoyancy and adaptability: A multi-sample study of reciprocal effects—Supplementary materials. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/edu/

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Follow-up multi-group comparison of the latent cross-lagged model: "invariance was demonstrated" between the unconstrained-betas and equal-betas-across-studies models (∆CFI < .001, ∆RMSEA = .001), supporting pooling of cross-lagged paths across the parallel-data studies.

"In follow-up multi-group modeling, invariance was demonstrated between a model where betas were unconstrained and a model where betas were constrained to be equal across studies (∆CFI < .001 and ∆RMSEA = .001)."
