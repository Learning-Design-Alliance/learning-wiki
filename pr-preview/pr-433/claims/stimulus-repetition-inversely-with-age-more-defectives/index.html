Evidence¶

Otis 1915¶

Otis, M. (1915). A Study of Association in Defectives. The Journal of Educational Psychology, Vol. VI, No. 5. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22A+Study+of+Association+in+Defectives%22+Otis

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Graded analysis of repetition tendency in defectives and normals using a four-grade percentage scale (Tables III and IV). The author reports excessive repetition "varies inversely with age, both with normals and with defectives"; no effect size is printed.

"The second and third degrees (31-60 per cent, and 61-100 per cent.) or an excessive amount of this repetition varies inversely with age, both with normals and with defectives. The third degree disappears with normals at 8, with defectives at 10."
