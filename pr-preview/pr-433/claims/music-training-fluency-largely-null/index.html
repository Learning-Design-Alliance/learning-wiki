The review's fluency domain covered seven articles with 572 children (n = 261 in music education), mean age 110.70 months. Only Zuk et al. (2014) showed "signiﬁcantly improved scores in the "Verbal Fluency" subtest of the DKEFS battery ( p = 0.016)"; the rest were null.

"This study found signiﬁcantly improved scores in the "Verbal Fluency" subtest of the DKEFS battery ( p = 0.016). However, the remaining studies in this domain did not identify a signiﬁcant association between music education and ﬂuency test results."
