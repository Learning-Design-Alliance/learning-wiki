Evidence¶

Maikki Pouta 2020¶

Maikki Pouta, Erno Lehtinen & Tuire Palonen. (2020). Student Teachers' and Experienced Teachers' Professional Vision of Students' Understanding of the Rational Number Concept. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09536-y

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Cued retrospective reporting analysis of episodes from four student teachers and four experienced teachers teaching fractions. The article reports no significant difference in selection of "mathematical and non-mathematical episodes"; no effect size is printed.

"There was no significant difference in student teachers'and experienced teachers'selection of mathematical and non-mathematical episodes, χ2 (1, N = 152) = 1476."
