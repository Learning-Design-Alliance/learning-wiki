Evidence¶

Tapio Rasa 2022¶

Tapio Rasa, Elina Palmgren, Antti Laherto. (2022). Futurising science education: students' experiences from a course on futures thinking and quantum computing. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09572-3

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Interview excerpt (Henry) coded under the theme of generally broadening or pluralising perception of the future, identified in 76% of the 21 interviews; many students recalled the course's Futures Cone visualisation.

"Well, I can remember really well that image with one present and many futures and then there's the plausible and preferable. And especially the whole idea of there actually being an infinite number of futures."

Tapio Rasa 2022 (2)¶

Tapio Rasa, Elina Palmgren, Antti Laherto. (2022). Futurising science education: students' experiences from a course on futures thinking and quantum computing. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09572-3

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Interview excerpt (Aaron) illustrating the theme 'Deterministic thought patterns questioned', identified in 29% of interviews; students also noted that even the most probable future is not bound to happen.

"maybe previously I thought that there's no use thinking about the future because it will come eventually and there's nothing you can do about it. But maybe I now think like it might be useful [and] interesting to think about different scenarios."
