What Works Clearinghouse 2023¶

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2023). Good Behavior Game. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/728

WWC synthesis of one single-case design study (Fallon et al., 2020) with 6 students in grades 1 and 2 in one northeastern U.S. school; the writing conventions summary reports a statistically significant effect size of 0.31 (improvement index +12, p=0.05), described as "potentially positive effects 0.31 +12 Yes (p=0.05)".

What Works Clearinghouse 2023 (2)¶

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2023). Good Behavior Game. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/728

The same single-case study (Fallon et al., 2020) measured total words written; the productivity summary reports a statistically significant effect size of 0.75 (improvement index +27, p<0.01), described as "potentially positive effects 0.75 +27 Yes (p<0.01)". Both domains are rated promising evidence (Tier 3) based on this single study.
