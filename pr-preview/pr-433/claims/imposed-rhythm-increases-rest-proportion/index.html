Evidence¶

West 1918¶

West, P. V. (1918). The Relation of Rhythm to the Handwriting Movement. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=%22The+Relation+of+Rhythm+to+the+Handwriting+Movement%22

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Finding from the first kymographic experiment, reported in the section analyzing stroke and rest periods under spontaneous versus imposed rhythm across adult and child groups using finger and arm movement.

"The imposed rhythm is found to increase the proportionate amount of time spent at rest for all groups, but this is especially notable in the case of the younger children, and when finger movement is used. The total duration of the stroke is increased, but the time spent on the stroke proper tends to remain constant."
