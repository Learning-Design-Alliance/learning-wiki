Evidence¶

Alyson Burnett 2021¶

Alyson Burnett, Moira McCullough, Breyon Williams. (2021). Impacts of Uncommon Schools in a Turnaround Setting. Mathematica. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/impacts-of-uncommon-schools-in-a-turnaround-setting

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Evaluation of the TurnNJ project, funded by a 2016 U.S. Department of Education Investing in Innovation Fund (i3) grant, assessing whole-school turnaround in six schools in Camden and Newark; outcomes were examinable for three schools using propensity score matching.

"In this study, we use a rigorous propensity score matching approach to assess the effectiveness of the TurnNJ project, as required by the i3 grant. We find that the three TurnNJ schools for which we could examine outcomes produced substantial positive effects on student achievement in math and English language arts."
