Evidence¶

Nicola Brocca 2026¶

Nicola Brocca, Davide Garassino. (2026). LLMs in text linguistics teaching: an exploratory study with genAI novices in higher education. Computers and Education Open 11 (2026) 100414. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeo.2026.100414

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Deductive coding of Dataset 1, five pair-produced reports from a home assignment using GPT-3.5, coded against categories derived from prompting-strategy literature and the HITL workflow. The analysis found "a trial-and-error approach" with initial single zero-shot prompts followed by iterative refinement.

"The prompting strategies employed by the five groups were developed through a trial-and-error approach. Initially, students formulated a single prompt closely aligned with the wording of the assignment"
