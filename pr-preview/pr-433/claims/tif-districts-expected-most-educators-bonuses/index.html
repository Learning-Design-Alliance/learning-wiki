q2 · i? · associational · r?

Implementation analysis of all TIF districts' bonus design expectations, reported in the key findings. Grantees expected bonuses to be "somewhat substantial and differentiated," yet most educators were expected to earn one.

"districts expected most educators would receive a bonus, suggesting that the award criteria were not consistent with TIF guidance for challenging pay-for-performance bonuses."
