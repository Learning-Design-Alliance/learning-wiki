Evidence¶

Dicke 2026¶

Dicke, T., Ferber, K., Devine, E., Collie, R., Granziera, H., Parker, P., Guo, J., Marsh, H., et al. (2026). Association of Teacher Well-Being With Student Motivation and Achievement: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10216-6

q3 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r2

Three-level meta-analytic synthesis of teacher ill-being indicators (burnout, stress, adverse emotional experiences, mental health difficulties) against student outcomes, reporting a pooled association of "ill-being (r = –.13)". Effect sizes were sign-reversed so negative values indicate associations in the expected direction.

"Using three-level meta-analytic models, we found small to moderate associations between student outcomes and teacher well-being (r = .18) and ill-being (r = –.13). These associations were similarly patterned across student motivation and student achievement, with no significant differences by outcome type."
