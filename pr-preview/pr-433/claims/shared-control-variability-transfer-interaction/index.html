Slava Kalyuga 2010¶

q2 · i? · review · r2

Cited experiment by Corbalan, Kester and van Merriënboer on shared versus system control of task selection, reported by this review. The interaction shows "shared control yielded higher transfer performance in the condition of high variability over surface features" while system control won under low variability.

"Corbalan et al.( 2009) demonstrated that shared control yielded higher transfer performance in the condition of high variability over surface features. In contrast, system control resulted in higher transfer performance in the condition of low variability."
