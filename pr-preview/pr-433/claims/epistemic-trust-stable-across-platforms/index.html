George Efthimiou 2026¶

George Efthimiou, Katerina Plakitsi. (2026). Preservice Early Childhood Educators' Engagement with Generative AI in Science Activity Design: The Case of Magnetism. https://www.mdpi.com/journal/educationsciences doi:10.35542/osf.io/m5erf_v1

q2 · i? · associational · r1

One-way ANOVA (N = 131) on epistemic trust scores across the five platforms, reported in the findings section. The authors state the result "suggest[s] that participants' pedagogical skepticism was relatively stable across the different LLM models used"; no effect size beyond η² = .05 is interpreted.

"However, the one-way ANOV A for epistemic trust found no statistically significant differences among the five AI platforms, F (4, 126) = 1.65, p = .166, 𝜂2 = .05 (Table 4)."
