In the expectancy findings of this interview study, five students tied confidence to AI availability. P10 said "if I was to not use AI at all, I would probably fail the class," and P8 estimated skills would drop from an eight to a six.

"Five students (P6, P8, P10, P11, P13) described confidence that appeared to depend on AI being available."
