q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of CDE CALPADS enrollment data, 2014–15 through 2022–23, using both Census Day and cumulative counts. In 2022–23, Census Day counted 31,722 students in foster care; cumulative enrollment counted more than 42,000 unique students.

"From 2014–15 through 2022–23, the number of students in foster care enrolled on Census Day dropped by over 13,000 students, with a similar pattern observed for cumulative enrollment"

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of district-level Census Day enrollment distribution, 2016–17 through 2022–23. The percentage of districts serving 100 or more students in foster care decreased from 18 percent to 13 percent, corresponding to 132 districts in 2022–23.

"these districts enrolled nearly 60 percent of students in foster care in the state, with the 10 districts with the most students in foster care serving one quarter of this particular student population"
