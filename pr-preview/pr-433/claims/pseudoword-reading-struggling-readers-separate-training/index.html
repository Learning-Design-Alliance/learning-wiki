Evidence¶

Gutiérrez-Palma 2026¶

Gutiérrez-Palma, N., Calet, N., Robles-Bello, M.A., & Valencia-Naranjo, N. (2026). The role of rhythm in learning to read in children with and without reading difficulties: an intervention study. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102273

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Non-parametric longitudinal analysis of the 29 children with reading difficulties; Table 2 prints a G x Time effect size of Cliff's delta = .47 for pseudoword reading (ATS = 10.29, p < .01), with a simple effect for the rhythm-and-mathematics group of Cliff's delta = .45.

"In the case of the sample with reading difficulties (Table 2), there was also an improvement in word reading in all three groups, but in pseudoword reading, there was an interaction, and the combined group did not improve."
