Evidence¶

Lortie-Forgues 2017¶

Lortie-Forgues, Hugues and Siegler, Robert S (2017) Conceptual knowledge of decimal arithmetic. Journal of Educational Psychology. pp. 374-391. ISSN: 0022-0620. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000148

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Analysis of Study 2 explanations found "none of the students’ explanations referred to solving WD multiplication judgment problems" via repeated addition; instead many invoked part-whole logic, e.g., "You are multiplying five by a number less than one so the solution is going to be less than one whole five".

"Contrary to our expectation, none of the students’ explanations referred to solving WD multiplication judgment problems with a decimal below one by using repeated addition -- estimating the result of adding the decimal the number of times indicated by the whole number"
