Evidence¶

Buket Bilgiç and Demet Sever 2026¶

Buket Bilgiç and Demet Sever (2026). Understanding ethical dimensions of AI in higher education: insights from faculty members and students. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00601-0

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Authors' integration of the risk-management theme. Faculty reported guidance practices (usage reporting criteria, awareness raising, scenario-based content, changed tasks, detection tools, human control) and students reported verifying AI outputs via multiple sources and original sources; the article notes these efforts fall short.

"The ethical risk management process regarding the use of AI in education has been shaped by a bottom-up approach due to the lack of an institutional roadmap."

Buket Bilgiç and Demet Sever 2026 (2)¶

Buket Bilgiç and Demet Sever (2026). Understanding ethical dimensions of AI in higher education: insights from faculty members and students. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00601-0

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Concluding sentence of the Resources and support theme from the interviews. Both groups accessed information via social media, digital resources, peers, and informal learning, while both reported a lack of professional support from institutions.

"Overall, the findings show that both faculty members and students rely on personal efforts to access information, and institutional structures are not yet sufficiently effective in supporting this process."
