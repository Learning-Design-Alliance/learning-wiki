Daria Katharina Benden and Fani Lauermann 2021¶

Daria Katharina Benden and Fani Lauermann. (2021). Students' Motivational Trajectories and Academic Success in Math-Intensive Study Programs: Why Short-Term Motivational Assessments Matter. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000708

q3 · i? · associational · r?

Latent change score models in Study 1a (N = 1,004) tested predictive effects of initial motivations and latent change scores on three end-of-term outcomes. The article reports that "maladaptive motivational changes predicted students' end-of-term study program satisfaction, exam performance, and course dropout."
