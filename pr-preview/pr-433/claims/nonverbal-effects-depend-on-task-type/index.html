Evidence¶

Nicole C. Krämer & Gary Bente 2010¶

Nicole C. Krämer & Gary Bente. (2010). Personalizing e-Learning. The Social Effects of Pedagogical Agents. Educ Psychol Rev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-010-9123-x

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Baylor and Kim (2008), that "facial expressions were particularly valuable for attitudinal learning, but detrimental for procedural learning" — a task-moderated effect.

"Baylor and Kim (2008) showed that the effects of nonverbal behavior are dependent on the task: facial expressions were particularly valuable for attitudinal learning, but detrimental for procedural learning."
