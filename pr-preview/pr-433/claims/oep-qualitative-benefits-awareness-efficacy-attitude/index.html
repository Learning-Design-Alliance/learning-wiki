Evidence¶

Hengtao Tang 2021¶

Hengtao Tang, Yu-Ju Lin, & Yingxiao Qian. (2021). Improving K-12 Teachers' Acceptance of Open Educational Resources by Open Educational Practices: A Mixed Methods Inquiry. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-10046-z

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Deductive thematic analysis of reflection notes from 24 participants with extreme intention change (14 above, 10 below one SD), with inter-coder reliability Cohen's Kappa k = 0.878; counts reported include n = 15 for awareness, n = 14 for self-efficacy, n = 16 for positive attitude.

"Three themes emerged in describing how teachers' OEP experience altered their perception of implementing OER: (1) developed an authentic awareness of OER; (2) increased self-efficacy about using OER; (3) cultivated a positive attitude towards OER."

Related Claims¶

  • Engaging K-12 teachers in open educational practices significantly improved their perceived ease of use and self-efficacy toward OER, with small-to-medium effect sizes — related
  • OEP experience did not significantly change teachers' perceived usefulness, attitudes, subjective norm, job-fit, or intention to use OER — reports the opposite
  • Teachers' challenges in accepting OER included time to sift resources, inconsistent quality, district discouragement, and negative attitudes — related
