anchor-context¶

Griffiths, R., Peters Hinton, V., Daal, S., Pattenhouse, M. (2026, July). K‑12 AI Infrastructure: Findings from educator and developer outreach. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/309

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Findings from educator interviews conducted winter and spring 2026 as part of Digital Promise's outreach. Educators across multiple interviews described wanting tools aligned with district instructional materials and local context, with one superintendent stating "context engineering" is more important than prompt engineering.

"Educators expressed a desire for AI-based systems to reflect their district's specific values, pedagogical frameworks, and student populations. One superintendent articulated this desire as a shift from "prompt engineering to context engineering""
