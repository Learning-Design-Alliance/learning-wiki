Evidence¶

Rebecca Krebs 2022¶

Rebecca Krebs, Björn Rothstein, Julian Roelle. (2022). Rubrics enhance accuracy and reduce cognitive load in self-assessment. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09302-1

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Controlled between-subjects experiment with N = 93 eleventh- and twelfth-grade students randomly assigned to rubric (n = 46) or control (n = 47) after writing a scientific abstract. The rubric group "showed better absolute accuracy than the control group", d = 0.39.

"In terms of absolute accuracy, a t test indicated a statistically significant difference, t(90) = -1.85, p = 0.034 (one-sided), d = 0.39. The rubric group showed better absolute accuracy than the control group"
