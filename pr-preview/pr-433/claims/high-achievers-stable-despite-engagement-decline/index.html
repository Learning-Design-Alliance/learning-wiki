Evidence¶

Mohammed Saqr 2023¶

Mohammed Saqr, Sonsoles López-Pernas, Satu Helske, Stefan Hrastinski. (2023). The longitudinal association between engagement and achievement varies by time, students' profiles, and achievement state: A full program study. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2023.104787

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Mixture hidden Markov model clustering of combined engagement-achievement trajectories identified an Engaged high-achieving starters cluster (n = 28, 26.4%) whose students mostly remained high achievers while lowering activity; State 1 emitted 87.4% Active engagement and 82.6% Achiever, and students were more likely to shift to the lower State 2 (transition probability 11%) than back (2.1%).

"Thus, in engaged high-achieving starters, decreasing engagement of students in State 2 was not associated with a notable decrease in their achievement level."
