Evidence¶

James Soland 2021¶

James Soland, Megan Kuhfeld. (2021). Identifying students who are off-track academically in 9th grade: The role of social-emotional learning trajectories. British Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjep.12463

q2 · i? · associational · r2

The article's statement of the research gap motivating the study: it reports that little is known about SEL stability and the relative predictive value of status versus change. No effect size is printed; this is the authors' framing of prior knowledge.

"Yet, little is known about whether students’ SEL skills are stable over time, and if they are not, whether a student’s initial level of SEL skills at the start of intermediate school or change in SEL skills over time is a better indicator of whether the student will be off track academically in 9th grade."

  • The stability of social-emotional learning competencies over time is an open empirical question with implications for teachers and schools — possibly the same claim (merge candidate)

  • In a large urban district, 6th-grade SEL status is more frequently a significant predictor of being off track academically in 9th grade than SEL growth between 6th and 8th grade — related
  • Understanding how a student develops social-emotionally over intermediate school can improve identification of students not on track to succeed in high school — related
