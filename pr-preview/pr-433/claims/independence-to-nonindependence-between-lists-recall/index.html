Evidence¶

Glenberg 1980¶

Glenberg, A. M., & Lehmann, T. S. (1980). Spacing repetitions over 1 week. Memory & Cognition, 8(6), 528-538. https://doi.org/10.3758/bf03213772

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Secondary analysis in Experiment 2 testing the independence equation P1 + P2 - P1P2 against observed recall, using once-presented words from each list as estimates of P1 and P2.

"Usingthese proportions, the predicted recalls were .70, .56, and .57 for the 0-0, l-O, and 7-0 conditions, respectively, almost exactly the same as the observed recall. Based on these data, it appearsthat the items repeated between the lists generate two inde­pendent representations."

Glenberg 1980 (2)¶

Glenberg, A. M., & Lehmann, T. S. (1980). Spacing repetitions over 1 week. Memory & Cognition, 8(6), 528-538. https://doi.org/10.3758/bf03213772

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Statistical test of observed minus predicted proportions in Experiment 2's delayed conditions. The authors offer retrieval-based explanations, including a shift from two effective retrieval cues at short retention to one at long retention.

"The differences between observed and predicted proportions were then used to test the null hypothesis of no dif­ference. The hypothesis could be rejected for the 0-7 and 1-7 conditions [ts(15)=2.10 and 3.01, respec­tively]. For the 7-7 condition, the null hypothesis could not be rejected [t(15) =1.22]."
