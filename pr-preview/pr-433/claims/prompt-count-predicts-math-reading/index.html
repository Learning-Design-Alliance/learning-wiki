Evidence¶

Kirsten W. J. Touw 2019¶

Kirsten W. J. Touw, Bart Vogelaar, Merel Bakker, Wilma C. M. Resing. (2019). Using electronic technology in the dynamic testing of young primary school children: predicting school achievement. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09655-6

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Ordinal regression analyses for the training condition only (mathematics N = 79, technical reading N = 77), with pre-test, post-test and prompts as predictors. Fewer prompts needed related to higher odds of a higher mathematics score; for technical reading the prompts coefficient was −0.077 (p = 0.048).
