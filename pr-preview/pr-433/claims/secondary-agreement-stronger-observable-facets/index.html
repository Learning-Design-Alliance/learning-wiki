The review section of this article reports, citing Clausen (2002) and Kunter and Baumert (2007), that secondary-level results "indicate stronger alignment on observable facets (e.g., classroom management; r /C25 .64)". The printed effect size is r = .64 for classroom management; the cited studies' designs were not described here.

"At the secondary level, results similarly indicate stronger alignment on observable facets (e.g., classroom management; r /C25 .64) and weaker or less consistent alignment for more internal or interpretive aspects (Clausen, 2002 ; Kunter & Baumert, 2007 )."
