Helmke 1995¶

Helmke, A., & van Aken, M. A. G. (1995). The causal ordering of academic achievement and self-concept of ability during elementary school: A longitudinal study. Journal of Educational Psychology, 87(4), 624-637. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.87.4.624

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Narrative review (Table 1) of prior longitudinal SEM studies (e.g., Byrne 1986; Newman 1984; Shavelson & Bolus 1982). The review reports some supporting each model "and even one with no cross-lagged effects at all," attributed as printed to the individual studies.

"Obviously, the pattern of results with regard to the causal predominance of self-concept or achievement is quite heterogeneous, as an inspection of the last column in Table 1 reveals."
