Focus group interview data. Participants reported that "the agents' questions prompted deeper ethical reflection." One participant noted agents asked questions people "might want to clarify but would feel uncomfortable asking themselves because the questions seem too obvious or general."

"participants reported that the agents' questions prompted deeper ethical reflection, encouraging them not only to clarify their perspectives but also to reflect more critically on their own views"
