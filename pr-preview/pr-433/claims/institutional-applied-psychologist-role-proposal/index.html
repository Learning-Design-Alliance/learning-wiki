An editorial proposal, not a study: the author specifies the conditions of a new professional position in institutional applied psychology, saying the rank and salary should be "about that of a physician" for stability, that laboratory training should be thorough without a full medical course, and that women might well qualify for the position.

"Women might well qualify for the position and find it an attractive field. The rank and salary should be about that of a physician"
