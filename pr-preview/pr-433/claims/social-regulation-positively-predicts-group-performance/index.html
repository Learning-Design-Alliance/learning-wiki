Evidence¶

Janssen 2010¶

Janssen, J., Erkens, G., Kirschner, P. A., & Kanselaar, G. (2010). Task-related and social regulation during online collaborative learning. Metacognition and Learning. doi:10.1007/s11409-010-9061-5

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Multiple regression analysis using the four interaction factor scores to predict group performance for 101 groups; regulation of social activities (summed percentages of MSociPlan, MSociMoni, MSociEvl-) was a significant positive predictor, β=.358, p=.03. No effect size beyond β is printed.

"In contrast, regulation of social activities was found to significantly predict group performance ( β=.358, p=.03). This indicates that the more effort group members invest into regulating the process of collaboration, the better they perform as a group."
