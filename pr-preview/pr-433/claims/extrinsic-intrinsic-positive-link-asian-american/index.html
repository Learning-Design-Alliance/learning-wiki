The review reports, citing Lepper et al. (2005), a study in which extrinsic motivation to please teachers showed opposite correlational directions across ethnic groups: negative among Caucasian students, positive among Asian American students. No correlation coefficients are printed in the review.

"In one study, extrinsic motivation to please teachers was negatively related to intrinsic motivation among Caucasian students but positively related among Asian American students (Lepper et al., 2005)."
