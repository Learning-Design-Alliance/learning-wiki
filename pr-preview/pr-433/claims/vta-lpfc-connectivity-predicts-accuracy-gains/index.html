Boroshok 2022¶

Boroshok, A. L., Park, A. T., Fotiadis, P., Velasquez, G. H., Tooley, U. A., Simon, K. R., Forde, J. C. P., Delgado Reyes, L. M., Tisdall, M. D., Bassett, D. S., Cooper, E. A. & Mackey, A. P. (2022). Individual differences in frontoparietal plasticity in humans. npj Science of Learning 7:14. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00130-1

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Linear models in 46 adults predicting accuracy gains from baseline VTA rsFC with five task-based ROIs, controlling for baseline accuracy, age, sex, motion, and number of volumes. Right LPFC: β = 0.086, pFDR = 0.029; left LPFC: β = 0.067, pFDR = 0.068. VTA connectivity with mPFC, parietal cortex, and striatum was not significantly associated with accuracy gains.

"stronger resting-state functional connectivity between the VTA and the bilateral LPFC at baseline predicted greater improvements in accuracy (Fig. 3b, c: left LPFC: β = 0.067, 95% CI [0.011, 0.123], p = 0.020, pFDR = 0.068; right LPFC: β = 0.086, 95% CI [0.027, 0.146], p = 0.006"
