Evidence¶

Yu-Ju Lan 2018¶

Yu-Ju Lan. (2018). Technology enhanced learner ownership and learner autonomy through creation. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-018-9608-8

q2 · i? · causal · r?

The editorial reports, citing Shadiev, Hwang, and Liu, a study of a mobile multimedia learning system with 53 junior high students, 26 in the experimental MMLS group and 27 in the conventional control group. The MMLS group "outperformed their counterparts" at posttest; no effect size is printed.

"It was found in Study 1 that the students learning with MMLS outperformed their counterparts learning with the conventional approach at the posttest"

Yu-Ju Lan 2018 (2)¶

Yu-Ju Lan. (2018). Technology enhanced learner ownership and learner autonomy through creation. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-018-9608-8

q2 · i? · causal · r?

In Study 2 of the same MMLS research, students carried out both individual and cooperative tasks; the editorial reports students "performed better when they completed cooperative tasks." No effect size is printed.

"The results indicated that the students performed better when they completed cooperative tasks than when they did individual ones"
