Daria Katharina Benden and Fani Lauermann 2021¶

Daria Katharina Benden and Fani Lauermann. (2021). Students' Motivational Trajectories and Academic Success in Math-Intensive Study Programs: Why Short-Term Motivational Assessments Matter. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000708

Predictor analyses in both Study 1a and Study 1b included gender, high school GPA, and SES as predictors of latent change scores. The article reports that "female students and students with comparatively lower prior achievement experienced more negative motivational changes."

"Across both studies, female students and students with comparatively lower prior achievement experienced more negative motivational changes."
