Evidence¶

Janneke M. Frambach 2014¶

Janneke M. Frambach, Erik W. Driessen, Philip Beh and Cees P.M. van der Vleuten. (2014). Quiet or questioning? Students' discussion behaviors in student-centered education across cultures. Studies in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1080/03075079.2012.754865

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Discussion section conclusion of the qualitative comparative case study: difficulties can coexist with cross-culturally different, context-specific yet solid discussion processes in student-centered higher education.

"this study appears to demonstrate that it is quite feasible to use a student-centered approach in higher education in different cultures – a claim that is supported by the fact that the three institutions had each been applying the model for over a decade."

Janneke M. Frambach 2014 (2)¶

Janneke M. Frambach, Erik W. Driessen, Philip Beh and Cees P.M. van der Vleuten. (2014). Quiet or questioning? Students' discussion behaviors in student-centered education across cultures. Studies in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1080/03075079.2012.754865

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Discussion section: the comparison of one Western and two non-Western cases yielded the conceptual model, and the substantial involvement of contextual factors implies such methods pose challenges in any culture, including Western ones.

"a student-centered, discussion-based educational model poses more challenges in non-Western cultures than in a Western culture, leading to different processes and outcomes in terms of students' discussion behaviors and skills."

Related Claims¶

  • Four cultural factors inhibit students from speaking up, asking questions and challenging others in PBL discussions, with stronger impact in non-Western schools
  • Six contextual factors mediate the influence of cultural factors on discussion behaviors, either inhibiting or enhancing discussion
  • Students' discussion skills increased from first to third year in all three institutions, but at different rates across cultures — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • The scope of PBL implementation shaped discussion quality: hybrid lecture-based implementation reduced uncertainty but yielded less critical discussion than full PBL — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • The assessment system determined whether an exam-directed focus inhibited or enhanced discussion behaviors — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
