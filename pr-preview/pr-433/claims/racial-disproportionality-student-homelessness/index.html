Levin 2022¶

Levin, S., Espinoza, D., & Griffith, M. (2022). Supporting students experiencing homelessness: District approaches to supports and funding. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/557.894

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

The report's background synthesis of research on disproportionality, attributing the patterns to systemic factors including discriminatory housing policy, higher child poverty, and lower wealth. English learner and special education overrepresentation figures appear in the adjacent paragraph.

"Black students are 80% more likely to be experiencing homelessness than would be expected based on their numbers among all students. Similarly, Latino/a students are 36% overrepresented and Native American students are 60% overrepresented among students experiencing homelessness."
