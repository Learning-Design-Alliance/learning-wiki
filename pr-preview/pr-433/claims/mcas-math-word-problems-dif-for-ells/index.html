Maria Martiniello 2008¶

Maria Martiniello. (2008). Language and the Performance of English-Language Learners in Math Word Problems. Harvard Educational Review. https://doi.org/10.17763/haer.78.2.70783570r1111t32

The abstract reports a study of MCAS fourth-grade math word problems that "were found to exhibit differential item functioning for English-language learners (ELLs) and non-ELLs." DIF statistics identified items posing disproportionate difficulty for ELLs; no effect size is printed in the available text.
