Tierney 2009¶

q2 · i? · review · r2

The panel's review of course-taking studies for Recommendation 1. Six studies potentially met standards; two provided mixed evidence on a rigorous high school curriculum, and four studies showed positive effects of AP courses on high school completion, college entry, and degree completion.

"None of the studies examining the impact of offering a college-ready curriculum met WWC standards. The lack of evidence partly reflects the challenge of rigorously evaluating the impact of high school course taking—students who choose to enroll in rigorous courses can differ in important ways"
