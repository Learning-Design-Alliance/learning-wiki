Evidence¶

familiarity-1¶

Noakes, S., Modica, A., & Palazzolo, K. (2022, February). Edcamp design for learning series: A new bridge between research and professional learning. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/149

q2 · i? · design · r2

Pre/post survey analysis across the four Edcamp events, with more than 500 responses across eight surveys coded by the Digital Promise team. The report states "individual variability saw a 17 percent increase, identity saw a 29 percent increase, motivation saw the highest increase of 32 percent, and cognition saw a 21 percent increase". No effect sizes or significance tests are printed.

"In each post-survey, respondents reported an increase in being very familiar with the topic compared to responses in the pre-surveys; individual variability saw a 17 percent increase, identity saw a 29 percent increase, motivation saw the highest increase of 32 percent, and cognition saw a 21 percent increase"
