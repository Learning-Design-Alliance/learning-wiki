Evidence¶

Arthur (WWC Intervention Report) 2006¶

Arthur (WWC Intervention Report). (2006). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://pbskids.org/arthur/index.html

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Randomized controlled trial conducted in six schools in a large urban district on the East Coast, in Spanish-English classrooms with bilingual teachers. The intervention group "watched a 30-minute episode of Arthur at school, three times a week between October and May of one school year, for a total of 54 episodes."

"The study involved 108 kindergarten students (47 girls and 61 boys). Fifty-one children were assigned to watch Arthur; 57 were assigned to watch Between the Lions."
