Evidence¶

Maria Ofelia Z. San Pedro 2014¶

Maria Ofelia Z. San Pedro, Ryan S. J. d. Baker, & Ma. Mercedes T. Rodrigo. (2014). Carelessness and Affect in an Intelligent Tutoring System for Mathematics. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-014-0015-y

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Repeated analysis of BROMP field observations across the two halves of tutor usage in both conditions. Changes in affect proportions were not significantly different between the NoScooter and Scooter conditions.

"In both conditions, students exhibited less confusion in the second half of their usage than the first half, F(1,124)=61.76, p<0.001. They also exhibited more engaged concentration over time, F(1,124)=40.26, p<0.001, and exhibited more boredom over time, F(1,124)=11.89, p=0.001."
