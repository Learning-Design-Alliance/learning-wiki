Statewide test analysis of the linked dataset compared foster care students against low-SES, ELL, and disability subgroups across all tests. In English language arts and mathematics, foster care students "scored at the two lowest performance levels at twice the rate of all students statewide."

"results from all the statewide tests showed that students in foster care were consistently outperformed by low-SES students, and they had an achievement gap similar to that of ELL students and students with disabilities."
