Evidence¶

Koskinen 2023¶

Koskinen, A., McMullen, J., Hannula-Sormunen, M., Ninaus, M., & Kiili, K. (2023). The strength and direction of the difficulty adaptation affect situational interest in game-based learning. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2022.104694

q3 · i? · causal · r2

F-tests of equality of variances across the nine level pairs showed "significantly smaller" variance in adapted than basic levels for task correctness; a parallel test found adapted-level perceived difficulty variance (var = 4.56) significantly smaller than basic-level variance (var = 5.46).

"The mean variance of adapted-game level task correctness (var = 0.05) across all levels of adaptation was significantly smaller than the mean variance of basic-game level task correctness (var = 0.7), confirming H1.3."
