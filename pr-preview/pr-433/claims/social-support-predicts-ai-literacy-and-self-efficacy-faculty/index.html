Evidence¶

Liu 2026¶

Liu, W.; Li, Y.; Yan, Y.; Wang, J.; Wang, J.; Zhang, H. (2026). Exploring University Faculty’s AI Well-Being: A Structural Equation Model of Social Supports, AI Literacy, and Technological Self-Efficacy. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16071168

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Structural equation model of cross-sectional survey data from 502 faculty at one Chinese research university, reported in Results §4.2. The authors report that "social support exerted a significant positive influence" on both mediators, with the standardized coefficients and significance levels as printed.

"Social support exerted a significant positive influence on both AI literacy (β = 0.435, p < 0.001) and technological self-efficacy (β = 0.272, p < 0.001)"
