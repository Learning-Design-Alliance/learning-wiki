Audio-Visual Communication Review 1953¶

Audio-Visual Communication Review. (1953). Audio-Visual Communication Review, 158. https://eric.ed.gov/

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Interview study of 79 third-generation Polish-Catholic upper-lower class public-school boys from kindergarten through sixth grade, reported in the digest's Results section as finding (a) on social situations of contact. The digest reports "a strong tendency for the particular situations to be characteristic of particular media".
