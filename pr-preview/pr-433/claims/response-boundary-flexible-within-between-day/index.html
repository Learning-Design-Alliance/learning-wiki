Evidence¶

Cochrane 2023¶

Cochrane, A., Sims, C. R., Bejjanki, V. R., Green, C. S., & Bavelier, D. (2023). Multiple timescales of learning indicated by changes in evidence-accumulation processes during perceptual decision-making. npj Science of Learning 8:19. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00168-9

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Point estimates from the best-fitting mixed-effects DDM (21 participants, 4 days) show heterogeneous response boundary dynamics: within-day decreases in 80.9%, 61.9%, 76.2%, and 55.1% of participants across the four days, and between-day decreases from day 1 to day 2 in 95.2% of participants. No effect size is printed.

"Within-day decreases in RB were present in most participants (80.9%, 61.9%, 76.2%, and 55.1% of participants, on each of the four days respectively). Between-day changes in RB were primarily decreasing from day 1 to day 2 (95.2% of participants)"
