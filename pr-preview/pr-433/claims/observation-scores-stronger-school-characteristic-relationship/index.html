Evidence¶

Jennie Y. Jiang 2016¶

Jennie Y. Jiang, Susan E. Sporte. (2016). Teachers with lowest evaluation scores are overrepresented in high-poverty schools. University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. http://consortium.uchicago.edu/page/teacher-evaluation

q2 · i? · associational · r2

The release summarizes the comparative pattern from the Consortium's analysis of value-added and observation measures in the 2013-14 REACH data: "observation scores have a stronger relationship with school characteristics, such as poverty, than value-added scores."

"In other words, observation scores have a stronger relationship with school characteristics, such as poverty, than value-added scores."
