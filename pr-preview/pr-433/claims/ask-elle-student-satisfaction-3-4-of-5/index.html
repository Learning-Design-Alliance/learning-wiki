Gerdes 2016¶

Gerdes, A., Heeren, B., Jeuring, J., & van Binsbergen, L. T. (2016). Ask-Elle: an Adaptable Programming Tutor for Haskell Giving Automated Feedback. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0080-x

q2 · i? · design · r2

Questionnaire administered to 40 students after two tutoring sessions in a functional programming course with over 200 students at Utrecht University (September 2011). "The average of the first seven question gives an overall score of the tutor of 3.4 out of 5." Table 1 shows the complete-solution item scored 4.25 and step-size correspondence scored 2.85.

"The average of the first seven question gives an overall score of the tutor of 3.4 out of 5. This is sufficient, but there is clearly room for improvement."
