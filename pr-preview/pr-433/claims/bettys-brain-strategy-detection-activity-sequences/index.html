Evidence¶

Azevedo 2014¶

Azevedo, R. (2014). Issues in dealing with sequential and temporal characteristics of self- and socially-regulated learning. Metacognition and Learning, 9, 217–228. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-014-9123-1

q2 · i? · review · r2

The commentary reports, citing Kinnebrew et al., that 73 seventh-grade students used Betty's Brain while the authors analyzed activity sequences comparing categories of adaptive scaffolding, showing "it is possible to detect and interpret students ' learning strategies."

"Their results showed that it is possible to detect and interpret students ’ learning strategies as they worked in the Betty ’s Brain environment."
