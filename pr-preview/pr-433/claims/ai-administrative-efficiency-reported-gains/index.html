Martin Sposato 2025¶

Martin Sposato. (2025). Artificial intelligence in educational leadership: a comprehensive taxonomy and future directions. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-025-00517-1

q1 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Kuleto et al. (2021), that advanced scheduling systems using machine learning "reduce administrative workload by up to 40% while improving resource utilization". The review does not describe the underlying study design.

"Advanced scheduling systems utilizing machine learning algorithms have been shown to reduce administrative workload by up to 40% while improving resource utilization (Kuleto et al., 2021)."

Martin Sposato 2025 (2)¶

Martin Sposato. (2025). Artificial intelligence in educational leadership: a comprehensive taxonomy and future directions. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-025-00517-1

q1 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Jiao et al. (2022), that AI-powered enrollment management systems predict enrollment patterns with "accuracy rates exceeding 85%", integrating demographic trends, historical enrollment data, and economic indicators. The underlying study design is not described in the review.

"AI-powered enrollment management systems have demonstrated the ability to predict student enrollment patterns with accuracy rates exceeding 85% (Jiao et al., 2022)."
