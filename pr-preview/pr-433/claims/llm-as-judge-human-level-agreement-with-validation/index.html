Li 2026¶

Li, W., Kurimchak, M., & Whitmer, J. (2026). ATS Field Note: AI Model Evaluation. ATS Hub Field Note Series. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/300

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The field note's synthesis of evaluation methods describes the custom automated evaluation approach, stating it "can achieve human-level agreement" when well designed. No empirical study or agreement statistic is reported in this article itself.

"When well designed, this "LLM as judge" approach can achieve human-level agreement, but requires careful validation (e.g., assessing inter-rater reliability between humans and LLMs) before deployment."
