Lauren Margulieux 2025¶

q2 · i? · review · r2

Review narrative citing Bechara (2000) and Bechara and Damasio (2005); the association is reported without an effect size. The review adds that serotonin acts on the prefrontal cortex, making people less likely to frame experiences as threatening, and inhibits amygdala activity.

"Related to tolerance for failure, higher levels of serotonin are associated with per- sisting in challenging situations or leaning into novel experiences (Bechara, 2000; Bechara & Damasio, 2005)."

q2 · i? · review · r2

Review narrative citing Esteban et al. (2004) on timing of tryptophan intake and serotonin synthesis; no effect size printed. The review adds that with sufficient tryptophan, exercise helps convert it into serotonin by increasing the amount reaching the brain.

"Esteban et al. (2004) found that consuming tryp- tophan in the morning or early afternoon resulted in more serotonin synthesis com- pared to in the evening."
