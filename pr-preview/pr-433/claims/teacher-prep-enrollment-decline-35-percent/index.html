Sutcher 2016¶

Sutcher, L., Darling-Hammond, L., & Carver-Thomas, D. (2016). A coming crisis in teaching? Teacher supply, demand, and shortages in the U.S. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/coming-crisis-teaching doi:10.54300/247.242

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of Higher Education Act Title II data (2005–2014) reported that "teacher education enrollments dropped from 691,000 to 451,000, a 35% reduction," amounting to almost 240,000 fewer professionals on the way to the classroom in 2014 compared with 2009.

"Between 2009 and 2014, the most recent years of data available, teacher education enrollments dropped from 691,000 to 451,000, a 35% reduction."
