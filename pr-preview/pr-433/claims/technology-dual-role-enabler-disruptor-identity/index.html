Folabit 2026¶

Folabit, N. L., & Jita, L. C. (2026). Academics’ (Re) construction of teacher identities in the context of technology integration at a South African university. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10708-w

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Authors' synthesis of the interview findings across both research questions. Participants reported renewed engagement and enhanced pedagogical effectiveness through digital methods alongside workload, disengagement, and technical challenges.

"Therefore, technology emerged as both an enabler and a disruptor: it enhances pedagogical possibilities while simultaneously intensifying professional pressures and identity negotiation."
