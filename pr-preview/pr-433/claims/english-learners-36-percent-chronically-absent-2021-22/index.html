The brief reports national demographic data for the 2021–22 school year, citing Attendance Works, showing "36 percent of English learners were chronically absent versus 30 percent of students overall." It contrasts this with prepandemic data in which English learners were 1.2 times less likely to be chronically absent. No effect size is printed.
