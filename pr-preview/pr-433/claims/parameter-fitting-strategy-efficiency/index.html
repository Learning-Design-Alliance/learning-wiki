Cluster analysis of Markov transition matrices from N=1531 modeling experiments by students, using hierarchical clustering with silhouette score 0.58. The most efficient clusters "ran only 14 and 14.5 (median) experiments" while the least efficient ran 25.

"Cluster 1 and 2 (Fig. 7) reveals the most efficient strategy as students in these clusters ran only 14 and 14.5 (median) experiments respectively before posting a near optimal solution. Students in these clusters predominantly switched to changing 1 parameter only followed by multiple parameters at a time."
