Tom Murray 2015¶

Tom Murray. (2015). Coordinating the Complexity of Tools, Tasks, and Users: On Theory-based Approaches to Authoring Tool Usability. Int J Artif Intell Educ 26:37–71. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0076-6

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical argument in the article's review of ITS authoring tool design tradeoffs, framing a design space among usability, depth, and flexibility (Murray 2004). The article states that "the easier or more efficient a tool is to use, the more simplistic or constrained are the ITSs". No empirical test is reported; the article notes complex systems can sometimes be built with simple tools when representational formalisms match the domain.

"the primary one being that in general the easier or more efficient a tool is to use, the more simplistic or constrained are the ITSs that can be built from it"
