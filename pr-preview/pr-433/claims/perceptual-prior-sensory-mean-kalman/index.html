Evidence¶

Kenny Yu 2024¶

Kenny Yu, Wolf Vanpaemel, Francis Tuerlinckx & Jonas Zaman. (2024). The representational instability in the generalization of fear learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00287-x

q3 · i? · causal · r2

One-dimensional state-space (Kalman) model of perceptual dynamics fitted to size-estimation data, contrasting a most-recent-perception prior against a regression-to-the-mean prior across all stimuli. The interval strongly favors the sensory-mean prior.

"The 95% credible interval for the probability parameter πψ of the prior source is estimated to be [0.95, 1], with a median value of 0.99."

Kenny Yu 2024 (2)¶

Kenny Yu, Wolf Vanpaemel, Francis Tuerlinckx & Jonas Zaman. (2024). The representational instability in the generalization of fear learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00287-x

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Trial-level Kalman gain estimates showed perceptual likelihood dominating the prior for most participants throughout the experiment, with a discernible decrease indicating growing prior influence for a subset.

"Nevertheless, a discernible decrease in this trend indicates a slight increment in the influence of the perceptual prior on shaping perception for a subset of participants."
