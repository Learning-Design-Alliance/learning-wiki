Evidence¶

John Q. Easton 1998¶

John Q. Easton, Brian Jacob, Stuart Luppescu, Melissa Roderick. (1998). Adjusting Citywide ITBS Scores for Student Retention in Grades Three, Six, and Eight. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/adjusting-citywide-itbs-scores-student-retention-grades-three-six-and-eight

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Re-analysis of 1998 CPS citywide ITBS percent-at-or-above-norms data (Figure 15), comparing unadjusted, adjusted, and 1997 figures. The brief reports reading at 34.3 percent before adjustment and 34.4 percent after, versus 30.3 percent in 1997.

"Before the adjustment, 34.3 percent of students scored at or above national norms in reading; after the adjustment, the number is 34.4 percent. Both of these numbers are higher than 1997, when 30.3 percent of students were at or above national norms."

The same citywide re-analysis for mathematics, using the percent at or above national norms. The article states the math score falls from 39.3 to 38.7 percent after adjustment, "an increase from the 1997 score of 35.9 percent."

"In math, the 1998 unadjusted score of 39.3 percent falls slightly to 38.7 percent after the adjustment. But the adjusted score still represents an increase from the 1997 score of 35.9 percent."
