Evidence¶

Research Abstracts 1953¶

Research Abstracts. (1953). https://eric.ed.gov/

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Same four-group laboratory experiment: Groups I, II and III received 16, 32, and 64 pretraining trials respectively with letter stimuli, and Group IV received none. The abstract states "The more response pretraining given, the more superior was the performance"; no effect size or test statistic is printed.

"The more response pretraining given, the more superior was the performance in the task situation."

Peterson 1953¶

Peterson, Lewis V. (1953). Use of Graphs in Air Force Teaching Materials. Division of Communications, University of Illinois, Human Resources Research Institute Research Memo #14, Maxwell AFB, Alabama, August 1953; with Eckstrand, Gordon A., Response Practice as a Factor in Transfer of Training, WADC Tech. Rpt. #6017. Research Abstracts, p. 157.

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Same display-panel experiment: Groups I, II and III received 16, 32, and 64 pretraining trials respectively on letter stimuli; Group IV received none. The article states a dose-response relation in words only; no statistics or effect sizes are printed.

"The more response pretraining given, the more superior was the performance in the task situation."
