Evidence¶

King 1912¶

King, I., & Johnson, H. (1912). The writing abilities of the elementary and grammar school pupils of a city school system measured by the Ayres scale. Journal of Educational Psychology, 3(9), 514–520. doi:10.1037/h0071006

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Reliability substudy: 34 specimens taken at random from the collection were graded independently by eight competent judges. Because scale specimens are placed 10 points apart, the authors take the 6.1 average deviation to mean subjectivity "may be reduced to a small quantity."

"in every case but one the average of the deviations of the eight judges is below 10 and the average, of these average deviations is only 6.1."
