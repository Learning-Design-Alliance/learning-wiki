Thang Doan Viet 2026¶

Thang Doan Viet, Anh Nguyen Hoang, Tinh Luong Son, Anh Hoang Thi Ngoc, Huyen Giang Thi Thu, Tai Le Quy. (2026). ConnectED: A Curriculum-Aligned AI System for Vietnamese Instructional Lesson Planning and Student Learning. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28647 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.28647

q2 · i? · design · r2

Structured post-task surveys with 18 teachers and 214 students in grades 6–12 using five-point Likert scales; Fig. 8 reports teacher and student means around 4.2–4.8. "94% indicating their willingness to reuse ConnectED" in future lesson preparation.

"Participants rated their experience across five dimensions, with the highest scores observed for curriculum alignment and clarity of instructional materials (Fig. 8). Notably, teachers reported a high level of trust in AI-generated outputs, with 94% indicating their willingness to reuse ConnectED in future lesson preparation."
