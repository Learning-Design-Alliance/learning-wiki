Evidence¶

Amzalag M 2026¶

Amzalag M, Zviel-Girshin R and Beimel D (2026) The illusion of cognitive independence in the age of generative AI: unpacking GenAI literacy and learner profiles. Front. Educ. 11:1861548. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1861548

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Hierarchical regression predicting general GenAI reliance (N = 817; R2 = .411). Use frequency added ΔR2 = .217 and perceived usefulness was the strongest positive predictor (β = .47). The article reports critical-ethical awareness "negatively predicted reliance (β = -.11, p < .001)".

"GenAI critical-ethical awareness negatively predicted reliance (β = -.11, p < .001), indicating that learners with higher critical awareness tend to rely less heavily on GenAI tools."

Amzalag M 2026 (2)¶

Amzalag M, Zviel-Girshin R and Beimel D (2026) The illusion of cognitive independence in the age of generative AI: unpacking GenAI literacy and learner profiles. Front. Educ. 11:1861548. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1861548

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Hierarchical regression predicting GenAI shortcut reliance (N = 819; R2 = .078), with literacy dimensions adding ΔR2 = .031. The article notes this positive coefficient stands "in contrast to GenAI reliance".

"in contrast to GenAI reliance, GenAI critical-ethical awareness positively predicted the use of GenAI as a cognitive shortcut in the final model (β = .15, p < .001)."

Related Claims¶

  • Profiles differ in immediate usage behaviors: Engaged and Literate users verify most, while Skeptical Minimal users report the highest shortcut reliance — related
  • GenAI literacy dimensions predict critical monitoring beyond traits, use frequency, and perceptions, with critical-ethical awareness the strongest predictor — related
  • Gender is associated with small differences in GenAI use, literacy, and monitoring, while educational level shows no significant differences — related
  • AI literacy is the strongest predictor of reliance type: each unit increase in AI literacy is associated with roughly a tenfold reduction in the odds of Dependent versus Strategic classification — related
  • Academic integrity and transparency is the strongest predictor of ethical GenAI use, followed by ethical and technical AI literacy and critical thinking — related
  • The association between early reliance and negative impact strengthens as evaluation literacy increases — related
  • Need for cognition correlates positively with critical genAI use and negatively with perceived usefulness of genAI — related
  • Skill-based AI literacy (using/understanding and detecting AI) is positively associated with reported AI dependency among higher education students — related
  • Students recognised both the benefits and limitations of GenAI, demonstrating ethical awareness and critical evaluation of its role in academic work — related
