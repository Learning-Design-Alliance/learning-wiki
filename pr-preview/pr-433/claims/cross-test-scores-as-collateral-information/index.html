Wang 2011¶

Wang, S., & Jiao, H. (2011). Incorporating Person Covariates and Response Times as Collateral Information to Improve Person and Item Parameter Estimations. Paper presented at the annual meeting of the National Council on Measurement in Education, New Orleans, LA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/incorporating-person-covariates-and-response-times-as-collateral-information-to-improve-person-and-item-parameter-estimations/

Authors' interpretive conclusion (type e) from the overall conclusions section, based on the joint modeling with cross-test raw scores as covariates in equations (6)-(9); no separate effect size is printed for this assertion.

"construct measured by a given test can be improved by the construct measured by different test. As matter of fact, student’s mathematics score (NS34) can improve item parameter estimation for students using their reading scores (LI52)."
