Evidence¶

Julian Rudisch 2024¶

Julian Rudisch, Luis K. H. Holzhauer, Karmen Kravanja, Fred H. Hamker & Claudia Voelcker-Rehage. (2024). A systematic review of observational practice for adaptation of reaching movements. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00271-5

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

Review synthesis contrasting congruent versus incongruent observational practice against control groups across the included studies, with forest plots in Fig. 2. Nine studies showed reduced error after congruent observation; eight showed increased error after incongruent observation.

"The effect sizes for direct learning effects from observational practice from congruent stimuli ranged from d = −0.5 to d = −5.07, with an overall effect size of d= −1.71, indicating an error reductionas compared to a control group. As opposed to that, effect sizes for incongruent stimuli ranged from d = 0.55 to d = 3.36 with an overall effect size of 1.19, showing an error increase."
