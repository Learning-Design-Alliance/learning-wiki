What Works Clearinghouse 2007¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2007). WWC Intervention Report: Read, Write & Type!™. http://www.fcrr.org/TechnicalReports/RWTfullrept.pdf

q3 · i? · causal · r?

In the Torgesen et al. (2003) randomized controlled trial, passage comprehension differences favored RWT modestly versus regular instruction (effect size 0.38, ns, improvement index +15) but were near zero versus ADD (–0.05, ns); estimated verbal IQ differences were 0.00 in both comparisons. The domain average effect size was 0.08, not statistically significant.

"In the comprehension domain, one study with a strong design met WWC evidence standards. Neither of the two comparisons showed statistically significant effects."
