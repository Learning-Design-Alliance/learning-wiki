Wang 2024¶

Wang, V., Leung-Gagné, M., Melnick, H., & Wechsler, M. E. (2024). Universal prekindergarten expansion in California: Progress and opportunities. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/597.103

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive survey results on TK curricula (Table 2). California does not mandate TK curriculum, though legislation requires alignment with the California Preschool Learning Foundations, which were being updated at survey time.

"The most commonly used were a social-emotional curriculum (56%), a Prek literacy-specific curriculum (52%), district- or teacher-developed math units for Tk (45%), and district- or teacher-developed literacy units for Tk (42%)."

Wang 2024 (2)¶

Wang, V., Leung-Gagné, M., Melnick, H., & Wechsler, M. E. (2024). Universal prekindergarten expansion in California: Progress and opportunities. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/597.103

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Survey finding the report flags as a possible concern if kindergarten curricula do not support multiage, multistage teaching or if curriculum repeats across grades; large LEAs used kindergarten curriculum less (12%) than medium (22%) and small (28%) LEAs.

"While LEAs are predominately using Prek-specific curricula, about one quarter (26%) of LEAs are using kindergarten curriculum in their Tk classrooms."
