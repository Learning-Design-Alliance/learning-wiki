Evidence¶

Sammy F. Ahmed 2022¶

Sammy F. Ahmed, Janelle J. Montroy, Lori E. Skibbe, Ryan P. Bowles, & Frederick J. Morrison. (2022). The Timing of Executive Function Development is Associated with Growth in Math Achievement from Preschool through Second Grade. Manuscript in-press at Learning and Instruction. https://orcid.org/0000-0003-3814-2955

q3 · i?

Covariate multigroup model in Results with sex, maternal education, and pre-k picture vocabulary as covariates of level and slope. Constraining early/intermediate intercepts and slopes produced Δχ2 (1) = 0.335, p = .563 and Δχ2 (1) = 0.104, p = .747; no effect size is printed.

"we observed no significant misfit when constraining the intercepts (χ2 (151) = 195.95, p = .008; AIC = 11932.37; aBIC = 11957.33; CFI = .94; TLI = .94, ∆χ2 (1) = 0.335, p = .563) and slopes (χ2 (151) = 195.72, p = .008; AIC = 11932.14; aBIC = 11957.10; CFI = .94; TLI = .94, ∆χ2 (1) = 0.104, p = .747) of the early and intermediate EF groups to equality"

Covariate multigroup omnibus comparisons in Results across all three EF groups: significant misfit for basis coefficients (Δχ2 (12) = 26.23, p = .010), slopes (Δχ2 (14) = 26.36, p = .023), and intercepts (Δχ2 (16) = 30.10, p = .017); no effect size printed.
