Roessingh 2006¶

The author reports from her program of work with ESL learners that "750 contact hours can have a tangible effect" in reaching the threshold for postsecondary study. No study design, sample, or effect size is printed for this figure.

"Nevertheless, most ESL learners can benefit from ESL support in high school (750 contact hours can have a tangible effect) to reach the estimated threshold for postsecondary school studies, whether university or technical school."
