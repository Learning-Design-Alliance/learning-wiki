Evidence¶

Fitz 2025¶

Fitz, J. (with Price, H.). (2025). Positive conditions for mathematics learning: An overview of the research. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/967.472

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Large-scale nationally representative correlational study of 10th-graders; students with more positive relationship perceptions reported "significantly higher levels of self-efficacy and intrinsic motivation in math" than peers with less positive perceptions; no effect size printed.

"In a large-scale nationally representative study of 14,639 10th-graders, students with more positive perceptions of their teacher–student relationships reported significantly higher levels of self-efficacy and intrinsic motivation in math than their peers who reported less positive teacher–student relationships."

Fitz 2025 (2)¶

Fitz, J. (with Price, H.). (2025). Positive conditions for mathematics learning: An overview of the research. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/967.472

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Nationally representative study of nearly 20,000 9th-graders by Luis Miguel Fernández and colleagues; mathematics identity, shaped by teacher practices such as communicating that "mistakes are OK so long as all students learn," mediated the association between teacher relationships and math achievement.

"In this study, students’ mathematics identity mediated the association between students’ positive teacher relationships and their math achievement."
