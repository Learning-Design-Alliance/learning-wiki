Evidence¶

Nicole C. Krämer & Gary Bente 2010¶

Nicole C. Krämer & Gary Bente. (2010). Personalizing e-Learning. The Social Effects of Pedagogical Agents. Educ Psychol Rev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-010-9123-x

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Rajan et al. (2001), that "it is first and foremost the voice that is responsible for these effects" regarding AutoTutor's learning gains.

"However, Rajan et al. (2001) demonstrated that it is first and foremost the voice that is responsible for these effects (see also Bente et al. 2008b)."

Nicole C. Krämer & Gary Bente 2010 (2)¶

Nicole C. Krämer & Gary Bente. (2010). Personalizing e-Learning. The Social Effects of Pedagogical Agents. Educ Psychol Rev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-010-9123-x

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports that Moreno and colleagues found transfer, not recall, enhanced by agents, and that "the agent ’s voice is responsible" for these effects.

"In a series of studies, Roxana Moreno and her colleagues showed that it is not recall that is enhanced by pedagogical agents, but rather transfer performance. In line with the studies cited above, they demonstrated that the agent ’s voice is responsible"
