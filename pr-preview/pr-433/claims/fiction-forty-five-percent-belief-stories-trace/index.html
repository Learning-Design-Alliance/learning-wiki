Content analysis of twenty-nine school readers reported in the Dunn review. The review reports fictitious stories "make up forty-five per cent of the total number of selections" and that stories intended for belief were found only as "a trace."

"The same method would perhaps have the opposite effect when applied to fictitious stories, which, with their repetitions, are reported to make up forty-five per cent of the total number of selections."
