q3 · i1 · associational · r1

Mann–Whitney comparisons of incoming GPA and final physics course grade between female and male students in the 101-student cohort; first-year students with zero GPA (2F, 6M) were excluded from the GPA analysis. Both comparisons were non-significant with printed effect sizes d = 0.293 and d = 0.286; equivalence was not tested.

"No signi ﬁcant sex differences were observed for incoming GPA (UGPA = 1051.5, P = 0.838, d = 0.293) or physics course grade ( Ugrade = 1056.5, P = 0.148, d = 0.286)."
