Evidence¶

Tacoma 2020¶

Tacoma, S., Heeren, B., Jeuring, J., & Drijvers, P. (2020). Intelligent Feedback on Hypothesis Testing. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-020-00218-y

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Logistic multilevel regression on task-level solving data (up to six observations per student) in the randomized experiment. The interaction term significantly improved model fit; no standardized effect size was printed, so impact is null.

"The addition of an interaction effect between task number and ITS feedback availability (M3) improved the model further: the deviance change was 13.92, which, with one degree of freedom for one extra estimated parameter, results in p < .001, hence a significant improvement to the model"

Tacoma 2020 (2)¶

Tacoma, S., Heeren, B., Jeuring, J., & Drijvers, P. (2020). Intelligent Feedback on Hypothesis Testing. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-020-00218-y

q3 · i? · causal · r2

From the final regression equation of the multilevel model: the positive interaction coefficient (0.32) means the experimental group's probability of solving declined much more slowly across tasks. The article reports this as descriptive model output with no standardized effect size.

"while for students in the control group the logit decreased by 0.41 per task, for students in the experimental group it only decreased by 0.41–0.32 = 0.09 per task"
