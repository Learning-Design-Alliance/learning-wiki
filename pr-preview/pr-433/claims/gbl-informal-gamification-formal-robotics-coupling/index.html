q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Cross-tabulation of approach × context across the 95 included studies in this systematic review, analyzed with Pearson's χ² tests. The review reports gamification at "85.29%" in formal contexts versus GBL at 45.90% formal and 54.10% informal.

"Gamification was overwhelmingly tied to formal contexts (85.29%; 95% CI [69.87%, 93.55%]; see Figure 4) and only rarely in informal environments (14.71%; 95% CI [6.45%, 30.12%]"
