Zhang Hui, Qu Lihui, Zheng Jianmin, Xiong Yi, Bai Hongbo, Ji Ruiying, Liu Guohui, Chen Wanling, Cheng Zirui, Chen Youbang, Yang Chun-tao. (2026). What Platform Scores Miss: Multidimensional Evaluation of AI Teaching Agents in Medical Education. JMIR Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.2196/96819

Validation rescoring of a stratified subset of 40 dialogues spanning all 8 agents by a blinded medical-education expert using the identical rubric; the article reports "r=0.61; ICC=0.51" at the total-score level.

"AI and expert ratings were closely aligned in magnitude (mean totals 79.1 vs 78.5) and were positively correlated at the total-score level (r=0.61; ICC=0.51)."
