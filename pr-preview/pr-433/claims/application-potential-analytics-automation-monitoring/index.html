Descriptive analysis of eight binary application-potential items in the survey of 395 education managers; respondents could select multiple applications. The discussion links these priorities to data-driven decision-making and learning analytics.

"Managers perceived the greatest AI potential for data analytics (61.0%), document automation (58.0%), and quality monitoring (53.4%). Lower perceived potential was reported for personalization (29.4%) and risk detection (34.9%)."
