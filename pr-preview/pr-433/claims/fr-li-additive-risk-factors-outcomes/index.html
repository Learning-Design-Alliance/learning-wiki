Evidence¶

Dilnot 2017¶

Dilnot, J., Hamilton, L., Maughan, B. and Snowling, M. (2017). Child and Environmental Risk Factors Predicting Readiness for Learning in Children at High-Risk of Dyslexia. Development & Psychopathology, 29(1). http://dx.doi.org/10.1017/S0954579416000134

q3 · i? · associational · r2

2×2 between-groups ANOVA on reading readiness and attention/behaviour outcomes at school entry in the 251-child sample. The article reports the FR × LI "interaction between FR and LI was not significant for either outcome measure (ps>.24)".

"The interaction between FR and LI was not significant for either outcome measure (ps>.24) indicating that these risks were additive."
