Fred Paas & John Sweller 2011¶

Fred Paas & John Sweller. (2011). An Evolutionary Upgrade of Cognitive Load Theory: Using the Human Motor System and Collaboration to Support the Learning of Complex Cognitive Tasks. Educ Psychol Rev (2012) 24:27–45. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-011-9179-2

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical argument, not a tested result: the authors reason that if general cognitive and metacognitive strategies are biologically primary and acquired automatically, attempting to teach them is likely to be ineffective, and documented improvements may reflect unspecified domain-specific knowledge gains.

"As far as we are aware, there are no data demonstrating that a general problem solving strategy such as means-ends analysis (Newell and Simon 1972) is teachable."
