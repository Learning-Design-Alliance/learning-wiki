Rook 2026¶

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Qualitative thematic analysis of open-ended responses from 166 undergraduate business students enrolled in a capstone unit, examining perceptions of GenAI in academic learning and career readiness. The analysis found "strong support for integrating GenAI knowledge and application into higher education curricula". No effect size is reported for this qualitative finding.
