Descriptive subgroup analysis of treatment-only outcomes (Table 4) by mistake presence, question type, and TA role. GTAs used drafts more (82.00%) but rated them lower (2.79) than UTAs (40.58%, 3.25), suggesting perceptions vary by grader experience and context.

"the drafts receive higher usefulness ratings (3.51 vs. 2.85) on submissions with mistakes, suggesting that TAs find them more useful when feedback is clearly warranted. Similarly, the drafts are used more frequently (64.46% vs. 45.34%)"
