Case analysis of one grade VII boy's Cleveland Survey Arithmetic Tests performance (Table X). The article reports "In multiplication this boy never attained a high score, While in subtraction he is consistently capable" and calls the pattern a problem for study.

"In multiplication this boy never attained a high score, While in subtraction he is consistently capable. Failure in division in no sense describes his status so far as that process is considered. Why he should have failed in the simpler forms, but succeeded in the more complex forms is a problem for study."
