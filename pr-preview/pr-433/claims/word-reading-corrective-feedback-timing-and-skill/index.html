Evidence¶

Grønli 2024¶

Grønli, K. M., Walgermo, B. R., McTigue, E. M., & Uppstad, P. H. (2024). Teachers' Feedback on Oral Reading: A Critical Review of its Effects and the use of Theory in Research. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09957-z

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review's synthesis of ten studies of elaborative corrective feedback informing learners about mistakes and how to improve. It reports "Delayed feedback enhanced word-reading accuracy" in two studies, while immediate feedback showed benefits with mixed results in two others.

"Delayed feedback enhanced word-reading accuracy (Daly et al., 2016; Schoen & Ogden, 1995), while immediate feedback also showed benefits, though with mixed results (Cottingham, 1997; Martin-Chang et al., 2017)."

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review's synthesis of three studies of word-reading feedback methods, reporting that "phonics feedback helpful for poor readers, while better readers performed best with no feedback" — a reader-skill moderator on the effectiveness of corrective word-reading feedback.

"Three studies that examined methods for word-reading feedback found phonics feedback helpful for poor readers, while better readers performed best with no feedback (Van Gorp et al., 2017; Watson & Boon, 2009; Yang, 2011)."
