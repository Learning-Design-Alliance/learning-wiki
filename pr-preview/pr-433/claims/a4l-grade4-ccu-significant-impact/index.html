Evidence¶

Jonathan Nakamoto 2015¶

Jonathan Nakamoto, Sandy Sobolew-Shubin, Martin Orland. (2015). The Beaverton School District Arts for Learning (A4L) Lessons Project, An Investing in Innovation (i3) Development Grant: Student Impact Findings from Years 1, 2, and 3. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED560945

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Exploratory CCU analyses administered in only 12 schools (six treatment, six control), using HLM with fall CCU pre-test as covariate; Exhibit E-2 shows grade 4 effect sizes of 0.30, 0.36, and 0.31 with *p < .01. The report calls these "small positive program effects."

"In each study year, treatment group students in grade 4 had significantly higher scores than control group students in grade 4, indicating a positive impact of the A4L Lessons Project on students' literacy and life skills as assessed by the CCUs. The effect sizes indexing the differences ranged from 0.30 to 0.36"
