Evidence¶

Tara Hofkens 2022¶

Tara Hofkens, Jessica Whittaker, Robert Pianta, Virginia Vitiello, Erik Ruzek, Arya Ansari. (2022). Pathways of mathematics achievement in preschool: Examining executive function and task orientation. Journal of Applied Developmental Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.appdev.2022.101432

q3 · i? · associational · r?

In the longitudinal sample of 467 preschoolers, girls had significantly higher task orientation; the same sentence reports no statistically significant sex difference in the direct or mediated pathways.

"Although girls had significantly higher task orientation, we did not find a statistically significant difference in the direct associations between EF, task orientation, and mathematics or in the mediating effect of task orientation for boys and girls."
