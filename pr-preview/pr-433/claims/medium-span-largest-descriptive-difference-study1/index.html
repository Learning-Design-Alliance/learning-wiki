Evidence¶

Tim Kühl 2022¶

Tim Kühl, Benedict C. O. F. Fehringer, Stefan Münzer. (2022). Unifying the Ability-as-Compensator and Ability-as-Enhancer Hypotheses. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09650-5

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Illustrative subgroup analysis of the N = 77 experiment comparing static and dynamic accuracy for the medium-span group (span 8–9, n = 22). The article reports 'a descriptively higher difference between visual design conditions in terms of effect size' for this group than for the low- or high-span groups.

"The difference between static and dynamic visual design just failed to reach significance for the medium-span group, t(21) = 1.60, p = .062, Cohen's d = 0.20, [−0.41, 0.81]."
