q4 · i2 · causal · r3

Pre-registered moderation test in the national experiment, standardized β = −0.25 (a medium standardized coefficient). Medium-achieving schools showed larger effects than higher-achieving schools; the article cautions the non-significant low-versus-medium contrast "should be interpreted cautiously, owing to wide confidence intervals".

"Treatment effects on ninth-grade GPAs among lower-achieving students were smaller in schools with higher achievement levels, intervention × school achievement level (continuous) interaction, unstandardized B = −0.07 (95% confidence interval = 0.02, 0.13), s.e. = 0.03, z = −2.76, n = 6,320, k = 65, P = 0.006, standardized β = −0.25"
