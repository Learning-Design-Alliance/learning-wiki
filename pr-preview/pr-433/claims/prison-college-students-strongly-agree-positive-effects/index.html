Evidence¶

Jillian Baranger 2018¶

Jillian Baranger, Danielle Rousseau, Mary Ellen Mastrorilli, James Matesanz. (2018). Doing Time Wisely: The Social and Personal Benefits of Higher Education in Prison. The Prison Journal. https://doi.org/10.1177/0032885518776380

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Likert-scale questionnaire (1–5) administered to all four former students at the start of the pilot qualitative study. Unanimous strong agreement on overall, community, in-prison behavior, and post-release items; no effect size printed.

"All students strongly agreed that participating in college courses had a positive effect on their life overall and benefited the community of the prison as a whole."
