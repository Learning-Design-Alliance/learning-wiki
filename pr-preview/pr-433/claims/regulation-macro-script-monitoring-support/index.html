Carolyn P. Rosé 2017¶

Carolyn P. Rosé, Sten Ludvigsen, Nancy Law, Ulrike Cress & Gerry Stahl. (2017). Divisive or facilitative: The two faces of technology in CSCL. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning, 12:215–220. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-017-9265-7

The editorial describes Näykki et al.'s exploratory study: video segmented and coded in 30-s segments with a reliable scheme, producing visual time-series maps of monitoring behaviors analyzed alongside qualitative observation. The intervention aimed to increase awareness of the learning processes of self and other.

"presents an exploratory study of regulation in groups of teacher-education students whose collaborative learning was supported by a script designed to support regulation during a six week environmental-science course."
