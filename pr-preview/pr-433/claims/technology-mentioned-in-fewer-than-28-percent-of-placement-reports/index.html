McGarr 2020¶

McGarr, O., & Gallchóir, C. Ó. (2020). Examining supervising field instructors' reporting and assessment of technology use by pre-service teachers on school placement. Computers & Education, 146, 103753. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2019.103753

Document analysis of a random sample of 429 lesson appraisal guides (229 from 2011, 200 from 2016) drawn from a cohort of 207 supervising field instructors. The article reports that "Technology was mentioned in less than 28% of the total sample of 429 lesson reports."
