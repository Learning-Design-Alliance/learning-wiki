Latimier 2021¶

Latimier, A., Peyre, H., & Ramus, F. (2021). A Meta-Analytic Review of the Benefit of Spacing out Retrieval Practice Episodes on Retention. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09572-8

q4 · i0 · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analysis (subset 2) aggregating 54 effect sizes from studies comparing expanding with uniform retrieval-practice schedules via meta-regression with robust variance estimation. The review reports "no significant difference between expanding and uniform spacing schedules of retrieval practice ( g = 0.034)".
