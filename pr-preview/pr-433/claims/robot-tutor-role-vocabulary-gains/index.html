Evidence¶

Chen 2020¶

Chen, Huili; Park, Hae Won; Breazeal, Cynthia. (2020). Teaching and learning with children: Impact of reciprocal peer learning with a social robot on children's learning and emotive engagement. Computers and Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/J.COMPEDU.2020.103836

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Randomized controlled between-subject experiment in which fifty-nine children aged 5–7 years old collaborated on a word-learning activity with one of three agent-role paradigms; the analysis "revealed that children's vocabulary acquisition benefited from the robot tutor's instruction and knowledge demonstration."
