Digital Promise and the Institute for Applied Neuroscience 2019¶

Digital Promise and the Institute for Applied Neuroscience. (2019). Introduction to the Learning Sciences: 10 Key Insights & Practices for Educators. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/d5550311-8f60-4d0d-9d4d-14821054e0e8

q2 · i? · review · r?

A narrative synthesis of learning sciences research states that spaced content improves retention and that interleaving different content, such as "diﬀerent types of math problems", acts as a desirable difficulty producing more durable learning.

"The same amount of content taught or studied but spaced over time can dramatically improve learning and retention. Additionally, switching between diﬀerent content (e.g. diﬀerent types of math problems) requires increased eﬀort for students (a desirable diﬃculty)"
