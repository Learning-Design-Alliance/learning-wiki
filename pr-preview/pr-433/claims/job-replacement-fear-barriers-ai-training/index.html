The brief asserts, as an observational gap statement without printed data, that lacking professional development about AI tools fosters "widespread fear that AI will replace human jobs," which it identifies as a significant barrier to training engagement.

"In the absence of that professional development, there is widespread fear that AI will replace human jobs, which creates significant barriers to workforce engagement in AI literacy training"
