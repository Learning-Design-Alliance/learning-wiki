Orland 2010¶

Orland, M., Hoffman, A., & Vaughn, III, E. S. (2010). Evaluation of the Comprehensive School Reform Program Implementation and Outcomes: Fifth-Year Report. U.S. Department of Education. https://www.ed.gov/about/offices/list/opepd/ppss/reports.html#title

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Analysis of achievement gains from 2002 to 2005 using CSRQ Center ratings of model research bases. The report finds "Low-performing CSR elementary schools gained about 2 percentage points" in mathematics with a limited research base model and 4 percentage points with a moderate or moderately strong base.

"Low-performing CSR elementary schools gained about 2 percentage points in the percentage of students scoring at the proficient level or higher in mathematics if they adopted a model with a limited scientific research base"
