Evidence¶

Samuel P. León 2021¶

Samuel P. León, Inmaculada García-Martínez. (2021). Impact of the provision of PowerPoint slides on learning. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2021.104283

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Linear regression of engagement and study-strategy factors on exam scores by condition, using CFA-standardised factor scores. Organisation predicted Y-ppt scores (β = 0.179, p = .040); the article reports "no differential effects between the Y-ppt and N-ppt conditions (β = 0.153, p = .051)".

"As shown in Table 2, organisation was the only factor able to predict Y-ppt scores (β = 0.179)."
