Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Adding eye-tracking data corrects inflated Organisation durations and re-distributes First-reading time relative to both other channels
  • Adding peripheral data to navigational logs sharply changes the detected distribution of SRL processes, revealing many more Organisation and far fewer First-reading processes
  • Eye-tracking data enables detection of more frequent Monitoring and Evaluation processes that navigational and peripheral channels miss — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Only with eye-tracking data does Monitoring appear as an intermediate process linking most other SRL processes in the process map — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Adding peripheral data raises specific transition probabilities in the SRL process map, e.g., Elaboration-to-Monitoring from 24% to 44% — related
  • Most refinements from adding peripheral data convert First-reading or Re-reading into Organisation, and eye-tracking then corrects many back to First-reading — related
