Evidence¶

Jemstedt 2017¶

Jemstedt, A., Kubik, V., & Jönsson, F. U. (2017). What moderates the accuracy of ease of learning judgments? Metacognition and Learning, 12, 337–355. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-017-9172-3

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Secondary correlational analysis across the three experiments' high-variability conditions computed per-participant correlations of EOL judgments with pre-standardized semantic relatedness and of the cue with learnability. The authors state the cue's predictive validity "seems to limit how high the EOL accuracy can go".

"Interestingly, across all experiments and conditions the EOL judgments were almost perfectly correlated with the pre-standardized semantic relatedness. The mean correlations varied between .87 and .91."
