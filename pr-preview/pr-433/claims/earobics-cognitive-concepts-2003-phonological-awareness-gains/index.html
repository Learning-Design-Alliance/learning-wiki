Evidence¶

What Works Clearinghouse 2009¶

What Works Clearinghouse. (2009). WWC Intervention Report: Earobics®. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://www.earobics.com

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Randomized controlled trial in one Los Angeles elementary school: 39 intervention students using Earobics® 30 minutes daily plus Open Court versus 35 comparison students using Open Court only, October to December. Appendix A3.1 reports effect sizes of 0.64, 0.63, and 0.59, all statistically significant.

"Cognitive Concepts (2003) found statistically significant positive effects on three phonological awareness measures (ORAL-J: Blending into Words, Segmenting into Sounds, and Rhyming Words subtests)."

What Works Clearinghouse 2009 (2)¶

What Works Clearinghouse. (2009). WWC Intervention Report: Earobics®. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://www.earobics.com

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Same RCT analysis sample of 74 students. Appendix A3.1 shows effect sizes of 0.01 for Letter Naming and 0.22 for Sound of Letters, both marked ns; equivalence was not the focus of testing.

"The study authors did not find statistically significant effects of Earobics® on the letter knowledge measure (ORAL-J: Letter Naming subtest) or the phonics measure (ORAL-J: Sound of Letters subtest)."
