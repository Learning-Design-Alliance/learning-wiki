curiosity-1¶

Anton, G., Mills, K., Jacob, S., Chou, N., McNeil, K., & Wegemer, C. (2026, July). Powerful learning: Fostering agency, purpose, and curiosity through connection: Cultivating future-ready knowledge, skills, and dispositions. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/307

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

The report's narrative synthesis, citing Goupil & Proust and Csikszentmihalyi, states that when the knowledge-gap balance is achieved, "curiosity leads to engagement in complex learning experiences for prolonged periods of time." No effect sizes are printed.

"In education, learners must navigate a balance where they are aware of their own knowledge gaps without becoming overwhelmed by excessive gaps, which can lead to anxiety or frustration (Goupil & Proust, 2023). When this balance is achieved, curiosity leads to engagement in complex learning experiences for prolonged periods of time (Csikszentmihalyi, 1990)."
