Evidence¶

Rijmen 2009¶

Rijmen, F. (2009). Three Multidimensional Models for Testlet-Based Tests: Formal Relations and an Empirical Comparison. ETS Research Report RR-09-37. http://www.ets.org/research/contact.html doi:10.1002/j.2333-8504.2009.tb02194.x

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

Figures 4 and 5 display item intercept and loading estimates with 95% confidence intervals for the 2PL and bifactor models on the English assessment data. The article reports "somewhat shrunk towards zero" for the 2PL estimates, and that "The discrepancies in estimated values is larger than was observed for the intercept parameters."

"The estimates of the item intercepts are quite close together, with the estimates for the two-parameter logistic model somewhat shrunk towards zero, compared to the estimates for the bifactor model for the most part."
