Tristan B. Taylor & Travis LaCroix 2026¶

Tristan B. Taylor & Travis LaCroix. (2026). Purpose before policy: academic integrity, generative AI, and rhetorical stance. Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10734-026-01706-1

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

Qualitative analysis of publicly available mission statements from the Canadian U15, UK Russell Group, and US Ivy League, attending to recurring themes, lexical patterns, and appeals to ethos, pathos, and logos. The authors state the goal is not comprehensive content analysis; statements are treated as illustrative examples. Common terms included "research", "students", "teaching", and "community".

"When describing their missions, academic institutions are particularly susceptible to the Entertainer's and Advertiser's stances. Consequently, their authenticity warrants scrutiny."
