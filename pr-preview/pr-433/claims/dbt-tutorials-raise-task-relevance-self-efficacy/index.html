Evidence¶

Hans van der Meij 2016¶

Hans van der Meij, Jan van der Meij. (2016). Demonstration-based training (DBT) in the design of a video tutorial for software training. Instr Sci 44:527–542. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-016-9394-9

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Repeated measures ANOVA on task relevance in the quasi-experiment; motivation was measured before training (IEMQ) and after training (FMQ) on 7-point scales. "the tutorials yielded higher appraisals for task relevance after training than before."

"There was a signiﬁcant main effect of time, F (1, 47) = 106.40, p \ 0.001, d = 1.74. Table 2 shows that the tutorials yielded higher appraisals for task relevance after training than before."

Hans van der Meij 2016 (2)¶

Hans van der Meij, Jan van der Meij. (2016). Demonstration-based training (DBT) in the design of a video tutorial for software training. Instr Sci 44:527–542. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-016-9394-9

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Repeated measures ANOVA on self-efficacy in the same quasi-experiment. "the tutorials yielded higher appraisals for self-efﬁcacy after training than before," consistent with earlier DBT tutorial studies.

"There was a signiﬁcant main effect of time, F(1, 47) = 49.61, p \ 0.001, d = 1.25. Table 2 shows that the tutorials yielded higher appraisals for self-efﬁcacy after training than before."
