Gehlbach 2010¶

Gehlbach, Hunter. (2010). The social side of school: Why teachers need social psychology. Educational Psychology Review 22, no. 3: 349-362. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-010-9138-3

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative review of the minimal group paradigm, attributing findings to Devine (1995) and studies such as Sherif et al. (1961) and Galinsky and Moskowitz (2000), where even trivial labels like overestimators versus underestimators produced in-group favoritism across ten traits.

"In-group members usually allocate more resources to, are more cooperative with, and behave in more prosocial ways towards members of their own group."
