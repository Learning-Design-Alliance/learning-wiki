q2 · i? · design · r2

Sanity-check analysis of induced failure subtypes against the intended taxonomy across the test set. The article reports templates are "largely diagnostic" with limited but non-zero cross-mode leakage, which the authors read as evidence of a realistic grey zone in borderline tutoring behavior.

"The subtype breakdown indicates that templates are largely diagnostic: authority-mode failures are predominantly AUTH-SYC, social-mode failures are predominantly FACE-SYC, and context-switch failures are predominantly CS-SYC."
