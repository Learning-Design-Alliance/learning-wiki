Ferreira 2024¶

Ferreira, A., Lima, D. A., Oliveira, W., Bittencourt, I. I. B., Dermeval, D., Reimers, F., Isotani, S. (2024). Exploring Brazilian Teachers’ Perceptions and a priori Needs to Design Smart Classrooms. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-024-00410-4

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative thematic analysis of responses from 410 Brazilian teachers; Cognitive Abilities code within the Personal and Professional Growth theme (91 responses, 14.49%). Example: "Super memory to remember their names and each of their activity answers". No effect size reported.

"the ﬁndings suggest that participants are motivated by an aspiration for knowledge, seeking to expand their intellectual capacities without limitations. Some participants posit that enhanced cognitive abilities could result in more productive communication and stimulating learning experiences."
