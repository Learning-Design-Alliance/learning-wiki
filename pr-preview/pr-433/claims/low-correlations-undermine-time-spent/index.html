Minnick 1925¶

Minnick, J. H. (1925). Certain abilities fundamental to the study of geometry. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Certain+Abilities+Fundamental+to+the+Study+of+Geometry

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Authors' interpretation of the per-school correlation tables (Tables I-IV) for Tests A-D, where coefficients such as 0.111, 0.042 and 0.126 fell below three probable errors in several schools. This is an interpretive statement, not a tested result.

"However they are frequently low and in many cases they are so low that, if they can be taken as indices of the value of abilities, then the schools are scarcely justified in giving as much time to this phase of geometry as they now do."
