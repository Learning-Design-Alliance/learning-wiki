Evidence¶

T. Sima Paribakht and Marjorie Wesche 1996¶

T. Sima Paribakht and Marjorie Wesche. (1996). Vocabulary enhancement activities and reading for meaning in second language vocabulary acquisition. Second Language Vocabulary Acquisition: A Rationale for Pedagogy, Cambridge University Press. https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9781139524643.013

q2 · i? · causal · r?

The chapter reports classroom experiments in which instructional procedures were "designed to increase the salience and cognitive processing of targeted words encountered by L2 students in reading texts." The stated aim is to test whether intervention can make incidental acquisition more directed and efficient; no outcome statistics appear in the available text.

"The classroom experiments reported here attempt to do this, using instructional procedures designed to increase the salience and cognitive processing of targeted words encountered by L2 students in reading texts."

Discussion¶

Word knowledge in these experiments was tracked with the Vocabulary Knowledge Scale (VKS), which Paribakht and Wesche built to register partial and growing knowledge of a word rather than a known/unknown split.
