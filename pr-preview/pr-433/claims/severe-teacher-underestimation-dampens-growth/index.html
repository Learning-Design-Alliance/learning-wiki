Evidence¶

Baker Courtney N. 2015¶

Baker Courtney N., Tichovolsky Marianne H., Kupersmidt Janis B., Voegler-Lee Mary Ellen, Arnold David H. (2015). Teacher (Mis)Perceptions of Preschoolers' Academic Skills: Predictors and Associations With Longitudinal Outcomes. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000008

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Exploratory subgroup slope comparison within the longitudinal HLM. "The relationship was similar for math outcomes, but the difference in slopes was not significant, zmath = −1.8, p = .07." Overestimation showed no significant patterns.

"the group of preschoolers who were underestimated by at least two standard deviations by their teachers had considerably weaker relationships between their fall and spring academic skills than peers whose skills were neither over- nor underestimated, suggesting perhaps that their growth was dampened by these considerable teacher underestimations, zlang and pre-lit= −2, p = .045"
