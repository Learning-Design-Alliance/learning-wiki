Evidence¶

Tuval Raz & Yoed N. Kenett 2026¶

Tuval Raz & Yoed N. Kenett. (2026). Knowledge Reshapes Inquiry: Changes in Question Asking Ability and its Impact on Academic Assessment. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00402-0

q3 · i3 · associational · r1

Pearson correlation analysis at both time points in the longitudinal course study shows exam scores correlated negatively with four question-asking measures, strongest being AQT originality at Time 2, r = -.42, p < .001.

"closed-ended assessment scores correlated negatively with DS-AQT originality at Time 1, r = -.26, p = .038; DS-AQT complexity at Time 1, r = -.3, p = .02; AQT originality at Time 2, r = -.42, p < .001; and AQT complexity at Time 2, r = -.40, p < .001"

Tuval Raz & Yoed N. Kenett 2026 (2)¶

Tuval Raz & Yoed N. Kenett. (2026). Knowledge Reshapes Inquiry: Changes in Question Asking Ability and its Impact on Academic Assessment. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00402-0

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Hierarchical multiple regression on exam scores: Time 2 predictors raised total explained variance to 52.5% (ΔR² = .30). Negative unstandardized B coefficients are printed; no standardized effect size reported.

"In the final model, Time 1 AQT complexity (B = -14.13, p = .038), Time 2 AQT originality (B = -11.07, p = .006) and Time 2 AQT fluency (B = -1.56, p < .001) were significant negative predictors of exam performance."
