Medeiros W 2016¶

Medeiros W, Torro-Alves N, Malloy-Diniz LF and Minervino CM (2016) Executive Functions in Children Who Experience Bullying Situations. Front. Psychol. 7:1197. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2016.01197

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Kruskal–Wallis comparison of Digit Span Backward weighted scores across bullies, victims, bully-victims, and controls (n = 15 per group) found "no signiﬁcant diﬀerence between the groups with regard to DSB" (χ2 = 2.587, p = 0.46); equivalence was not tested.

"There was no signiﬁcant diﬀerence between the groups with regard to DSB ( χ2= 2.587, p= 0.46), indicating that they had similar patterns of performance in working memory."

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Music training RCTs show improved verbal working memory (backward digit span), but not visuospatial or forward-span tasks
  • Congenitally blind adults show higher verbal backwards digit span than sighted controls
  • Backward digit span performance does not emerge until about age 5, whereas forward span is measurable from age 3
  • When PSID-CDS data are restricted to ages 6–11, backward digit span growth is best characterized by a quadratic (not cubic) function
  • No significant group differences in Stroop color and word card times or in error rates on any Stroop card
