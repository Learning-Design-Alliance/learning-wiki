Stefano Bannò 2026¶

Stefano Bannò, Kate Knill, Mark Gales. (2026). Towards Self-Referential Analytic Assessment: A Profile-Based Approach to L2 Writing Evaluation with LLMs. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04298 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.04298

q2 · i? · design · r2

Zero-shot LLM scoring experiment on ICNALE GRA essays (N=140) with Rasch-calibrated fair average reference scores; negative feedback F0.5 results in Table 4 show GPT-4.1 "notably outperforms both the other models and OR" on the named aspects, with the highest average F0.5 (33.71).

"GPT-4.1 notably outperforms both the other models and OR onIntelligibility,Complexity,Fluency,Comprehensibility, andLogicality, indicating a stronger alignment with the reference signal in identifying aspects that deviate negatively compared to the overall average score."
