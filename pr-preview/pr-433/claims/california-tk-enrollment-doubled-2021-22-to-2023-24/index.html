Melnick 2024¶

Melnick, H., & García, E. (2024). Progressing toward universal prekindergarten in California [Brief]. Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/progressing-toward-universal-prekindergarten-california

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of California Department of Education TK enrollment data and Census population estimates for 2019–20 to 2023–24. The brief reports enrollment "rose to 151,491 (59% of the eligible population)" in 2023–24, up from 75,465 in 2021–22, with eligibility expansion as a stated influence.

"Enrollment in TK has doubled in the 2 years since 2021–22, influenced by the fact that more children are becoming eligible. In 2022–23, 116,898 children enrolled in TK (62% of the eligible population). By 2023–24, enrollment rose to 151,491 (59% of the eligible population)."
