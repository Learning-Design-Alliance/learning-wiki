Evidence¶

Gehlbach 2004¶

Gehlbach, Hunter. (2004). A New Perspective on Perspective Taking: A Multidimensional Approach to Conceptualizing an Aptitude. Educational Psychology Review 16 (3). https://doi.org/10.1023/B:EDPR.0000034021.12899.11

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports a laboratory study using the videotape procedure in which male participants were more motivated to figure out the thoughts and feelings of attractive female targets, and this interest "positively influenced SPT accuracy". No effect size is printed.

"Ickes et al. (1990) found that the perceiver's interest in the target person generally (and specifically in the target's attractiveness) positively influenced SPT accuracy."
