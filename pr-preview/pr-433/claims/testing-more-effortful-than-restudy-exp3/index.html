Tamara van Gog & Liesbeth Kester & Kim Dirkx & Vincent Hoogerheide & Joris Boerboom & Peter P. J. L. Verkoeijen. (2015). Testing After Worked Example Study Does Not Enhance Delayed Problem-Solving Performance Compared to Restudy. Educ Psychol Rev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-015-9297-3

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Experiment 3 mental effort ratings (Paas 9-point scale) during the testing/restudy phase: "the testing conditions investing more mental effort ( M=5.34, SD=1.97) than the restudy conditions ( M=2.94, SD=1.59)", F(1,111)=56.50, p<0.001, ηp2=0.337. An interaction showed isomorphic testing was less effortful than identical testing (d=0.813).

"There was a significant main effect of testing/restudy, with the testing conditions investing more mental effort ( M=5.34, SD=1.97) than the restudy conditions ( M=2.94, SD=1.59) on the two tasks, F(1,111)=56.50, p<0.001, ηp2=0.337"
