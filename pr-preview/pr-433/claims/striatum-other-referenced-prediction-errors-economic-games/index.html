Evidence¶

Jessica Joiner 2017¶

Jessica Joiner, Matthew Piva, Courtney Turrin and Steve W. C. Chang. (2017). Social learning through prediction error in the brain. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-017-0009-2

q2 · i? · review · r3

Review of human fMRI studies of observational and economic-game learning. The striatum correlates with prediction errors about partner offers in an ultimatum game, trust learning in the caudate nucleus, and interpersonal prediction errors under pure observation.

"if added payoff for social learning is removed, so that only pure observation of others is necessary for the task, an interpersonal prediction error still occurs in the striatum."
