Thematic analysis of the narrative data; the article reports the four agency dimensions "undulated and interacted" such that combined they expressed agency in micro, meso and macro ways, with interactions based on coherent, incoherent or conflicting expressions of agency.

"As areas of agency interact, we use three levels to describe the types of interactions that take place: micro-level, meso-level, and macro-level interactions."
