Uncovering Blind Spots in Education Ethics: Insights from a Systematic Literature Review on Artificial Intelligence in Education. q3 · i? · review · r2

RQ1 synthesis of Table 4, summarising 36% of the review's studies, maps technologies (ambient classroom tools, ITS, robots, adaptive assessment) to targets and ethical challenges. The review reports AI criticised as "dehumanising" for promoting "prescriptive teaching with minimal interaction". It also notes activity logs can reveal political beliefs or sexual orientation.

"AI has faced criticism for being "dehumanising" in this context, as it often promotes prescriptive teaching with minimal interaction and automated pathways, ultimately diminishing students’ agency (Miao et al., 2021)."
