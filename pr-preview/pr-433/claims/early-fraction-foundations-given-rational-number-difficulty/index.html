Authors' closing recommendation slide (type e, interpretation). The presentation offers this as a rationale without reporting data: "it is especially important to start early to build the mathematical thinking associated with fractions." No evidence or study is presented for the assertion in this document.

"Learning is effective when it builds on children's existing knowledge, making connections to what they already understand in real-world contexts. Given the difficulty that many older children experience with rational numbers, it is especially important to start early to build the mathematical thinking associated with fractions."
