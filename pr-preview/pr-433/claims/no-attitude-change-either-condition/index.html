Evidence¶

Mulders 2025¶

Mulders, M., Träg, K. H., & Kirner, L. (2025). Go green: evaluating an XR application on biodiversity in German secondary school classrooms. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09697-1

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Dependent-samples t-tests (Holm-Bonferroni corrected) on three attitude scales in both conditions; Table 7 reports only small, non-significant effects (e.g., VR attitude d = 0.09, p = 0.880). Hypothesis 3a was rejected.

"When observing the test conditions alone, neither the VR application nor the traditional lesson showed a positive effect on any of the attitude measures we employed"
