Luo, T., Muljana, P. S., Ren, X., & Young, D. (2025). Exploring instructional designers' utilization and perspectives on generative AI tools: A mixed methods study. Education Tech Research Dev, 73, 741–766. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10437-y

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Thematic analysis of interviews (RQ4). Six participants, especially adjunct instructors in academia, "voiced concerns around the issue of plagiarism related to academic integrity and copyright," and one wished universities would address SME use of GenAI in policy.

"Many participants (N = 6), especially those involved in academia who were oftentimes adjunct instructors, voiced concerns around the issue of plagiarism related to academic integrity and copyright."
