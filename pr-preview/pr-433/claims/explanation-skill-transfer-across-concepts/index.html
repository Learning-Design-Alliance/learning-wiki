Evidence¶

Xiaojuan Ke · Kristie J. Newton 2024¶

Xiaojuan Ke · Kristie J. Newton. (2024). English learners learn from worked example comparison in algebra. Instructional Science (2024) 52:831–858. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09668-6

q2 · i2 · causal · r1

Wait-list crossover comparison at Unit 2 pretest: the group that had received the Unit 1 MLS-WEPs materials (now control) significantly outscored the incoming treatment group on explanations, t(75.7) = 2.01, p = 0.048, with "Cohen’s d = 0.424", despite Units 1 and 2 covering different concepts.

"compared to students in the treatment group (M = 0.58, SD = 0.88), the students in the control group scored significantly higher in the explanation part (M = 1.11, SD = 1.43) of the Unit 2 pretest, t (75.7) = 2.01, p = 0.048, Cohen’s d = 0.424"
