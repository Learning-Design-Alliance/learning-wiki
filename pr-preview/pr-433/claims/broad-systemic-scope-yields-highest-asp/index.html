Evidence¶

Sara Berti 2023¶

Sara Berti, Valentina Grazia, Luisa Molinari. (2023). Active Student Participation in Whole-School Interventions in Secondary School. A Systematic Literature Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09773-x

q2 · i? · review · r2

Qualitative cross-program comparison from the review's analysis of coded activities (Table 5). The two climate-focused programs concentrated the highest-ASP activities; the authors describe the inference as tentative, not a tested effect.

"the interventions based on the highest levels of ASP are those aimed at generally improving the school environment, i.e., the Restorative Justice and SEHER programs. These programs included all but one of the activities defined as Very High ASP or High ASP, while all other programs usually provided only one or two of them."

Sara Berti 2023 (2)¶

Sara Berti, Valentina Grazia, Luisa Molinari. (2023). Active Student Participation in Whole-School Interventions in Secondary School. A Systematic Literature Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09773-x

q1 · i? · review · r2

Authors' interpretive conclusion (type e) drawn from the activity coding, explicitly hedged as tentative. It supports the review's argument for whole-school interventions targeted at the general learning environment rather than specific problems.

"On the basis of this result, we tentatively advance that when the study scope is broad and the theoretical approach is systemic, interventions are more directly centered on lending a voice to students and assigning to them a decision-making role."
