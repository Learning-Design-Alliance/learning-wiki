Evidence¶

Ishihara 2020¶

Ishihara, T., Nakajima, T., Yamatsu, K., Okita, K., Sagawa, M. & Morita, N. (2020). Longitudinal relationship of favorable weight change to academic performance in children. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0063-z

q3 · i2 · associational · r2

Step 3 of the hierarchical regression in 197 children predicted change in total grade points; both screen-time and learning-duration changes were significant, with printed β = 0.22 and β = 0.34. The discussion notes "changes in BMI had no main effect" on grade-point change.
