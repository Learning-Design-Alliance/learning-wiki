Evidence¶

Niels J. de Ruig 2023¶

Niels J. de Ruig, Peter F. de Jong, Marjolein Zee. (2023). Stimulating Elementary School Students' Self-Regulated Learning Through High-Quality Interactions and Relationships: A Narrative Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09795-5

q2 · i? · review · r2

Two longitudinal studies following students across elementary school in the US and Netherlands. Table 4 reports closeness-motivation β range 0.06-0.38 (M = 0.14) across four studies, but no effect size is printed in the quoted finding itself. The review characterizes dyad-level motivation findings as consistent, unlike classroom-level findings.

"Findings from two longitudinal studies that were conducted throughout elementary school in the United States and the Netherlands, revealed that students who experienced close teacher–student relationships in the early grades also had more positive attitudes about school (Heatly & V otruba-Drzal, 2019) and a higher sense of self-efficacy, task motivation, and motivational attitudes in fifth or sixth grade compared to their peers (Zee et al., 2020)."
