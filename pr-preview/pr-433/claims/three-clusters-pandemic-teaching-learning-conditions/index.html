K-means cluster analysis of country-level PISA 2022 Global Crises Module data (N = 47 countries), validated with the average silhouette approach; "a three-cluster solution was selected, and each country was assigned to one cluster". Clusters were labelled from their largest item differences.

"Based on the average silhouette analysis and visual inspection of the clustering results, a three-cluster solution was selected, and each country was assigned to one cluster"

Related Claims¶

  • Cluster 2 countries outperformed Cluster 1 countries in PISA 2022 only in science (about 20 PISA points, p = .002), with no significant differences in mathematics or reading — related
  • Countries with Accentuated Teacher-Led Synchronous Instruction showed better PISA 2022 performance than countries with Accentuated Instructional Disruption across all domains, with modest effects — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Pandemic-related performance change was more positive in Cluster 2 than Cluster 3 countries across all domains, and more positive than Cluster 1 in reading and science — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
