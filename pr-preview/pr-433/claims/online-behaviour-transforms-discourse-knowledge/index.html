Authors' interpretation of the pattern-type results (KS, KM, KD means reflected via concept mapping behaviours; Type 3's behaviour-implied cognitive means were KS = 4.00, KM = 9.60, KD = 17.20). "Type 3 had the lowest level of knowledge construction through students' online behaviours".

"our results showed that Type 3 had the lowest level of knowledge construction through students' online behaviours, which also resulted in the lowest collaborative performance among the three types. Related Claims¶

  • The three pattern types differed significantly on behavioural codes (Type 1 > Type 2 > Type 3 for concept mapping) and on several interactive, cognitive and regulative codes, but not on socio-emotional codes — related
  • The authors argue formative feedback is critical for making student behaviour 'seen' in asynchronous online learning where the teacher is not physically present — related
  • Mentored inquiry with staged mechanistic sensemaking supports the transition from intuitive to formal equation reasoning — related
  • Self-constructed collaborative concept maps trigger more deep exploratory talk and less non-dialogic talk than completing or sorting maps, which yield mostly superficial exploratory talk — related
  • Tacit knowledge in CSCL is co-constructed through socialization, externalization, combination, and internalization processes — related
  • High cognitive activities were positively associated with essay quality but not with concept maps — related
  • Computer-supported collaborative learning with simulation programs fostered conceptual change for students who were cognitively engaged and prepared to reflect on and reconstruct their conceptions — related
  • Process-tracing measures should accompany outcome measures because process changes may not be immediately reflected in outcomes — related
