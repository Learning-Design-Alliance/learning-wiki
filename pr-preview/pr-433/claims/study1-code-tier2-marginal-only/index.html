Evidence¶

Lonigan 2016¶

Lonigan, C. J., & Phillips, B. M. (2016). Response to Instruction in Preschool: Results of Two Randomized Studies with Children At Significant Risk of Reading Difficulties. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000054

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Study 1 randomized comparison of code-focused Tier II instruction versus business-as-usual Tier I for children qualified on code-related skills, analyzed with multi-level models. The only posttest difference was marginal on CLIP Letter Names, F(1, 77.00) = 3.52, p < .07; other outcomes showed "no significant differences between groups".

"At posttest, children who received the code-focused instruction scored marginally higher than did children in the BAU control group on the CLIP Letter Names test, F(1, 77.00) = 3.52, p < .07, but there were no significant differences between groups on any of the other code-related measures (ps > .18)."
