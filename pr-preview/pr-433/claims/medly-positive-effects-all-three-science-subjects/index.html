Wayne Harrison 2026¶

Wayne Harrison, Rahil Khowaja, Emma Dobson, Germaine Uwimpuhwe, and Steve Higgins. (2026). Evaluating AI Tutoring at the Speed of Innovation: Practitioner-Led Micro-Randomised Trials of an AI Tutoring Platform in GCSE Science. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14789 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.14789

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Subject-specific intention-to-treat mixed-effects models from the same trial (post-test n: Physics 312, Chemistry 217, Biology 115). All three estimates were positive, e.g. "Biology g = 0.52 (95% CI 0.19 to 0.84)".

"Positive effects were observed in all three subject-specific analyses. Physics produced g = 0.31 (95% CI 0.11 to 0.51), Chemistry g = 0.32 (95% CI 0.06 to 0.58), and Biology g = 0.52 (95% CI 0.19 to 0.84)."
