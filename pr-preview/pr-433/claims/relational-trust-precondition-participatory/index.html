q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Researcher reflection quoted in the Conditions Shaping Engagement section. The report states that in two projects funders were open to and enthusiastic about adding participatory elements, citing trust built through prior work as the foundation for their support.

"The trust we built over 2.5 years of rigorous, conventional evaluation before we floated a more experimental idea of adding participatory elements. That trust earned us this chance."
