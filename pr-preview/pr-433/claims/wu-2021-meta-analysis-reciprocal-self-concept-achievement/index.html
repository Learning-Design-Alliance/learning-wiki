The review reports a meta-analysis by Wu et al. covering "results from 68 longitudinal studies", in which "prior achievement significantly predicted subsequent self-concept (β = .16, p < .01)" after accounting for prior self-concept.

"Wu et al. (2021) conducted another more recent meta-analysis in which they considered results from 68 longitudinal studies and found that prior achievement significantly predicted subsequent self-concept (β = .16, p < .01)"
