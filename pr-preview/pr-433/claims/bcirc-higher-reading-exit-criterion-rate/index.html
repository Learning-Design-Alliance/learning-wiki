Evidence¶

Bilingual Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition 2007¶

Bilingual Cooperative Integrated Reading and Composition. (2007). WWC Intervention Report. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED405428

q2 · i3 · causal · r?

Exit-criterion finding from the Calderón, Hertz-Lazarowitz, and Slavin (1998) quasi-experiment, reported in Appendix A4.1 for 118 third graders with a printed effect size of 0.87, statistically significant, improvement index +31. The WWC summary calls this "an important and promising finding".

"At the end of third grade, 32% of students in the BCIRC group scored above the 40th percentile on the Norm-Referenced Assessment Program for Texas (NAPT) reading test, qualifying them to exit their bilingual education program. By contrast, only 10% of students in the comparison group met this criterion."
