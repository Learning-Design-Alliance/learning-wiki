Evidence¶

Sascha Schneider 2021¶

Sascha Schneider, Maik Beege, Steve Nebel, Lenka Schnaubert, Günter Daniel Rey. (2021). The Cognitive-Affective-Social Theory of Learning in digital Environments (CASTLE). Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09626-5

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review attributes this finding to two studies by Schneider and colleagues (2016; 2018a), describing emotional design elements as evoking social information because emotions communicate situation and author intention. Second-hand; no effect sizes printed.

"In a study by Schneider et al. ( 2016), positively emotional images have a learning effect compared to negative images and increase the satisfaction of the learners. This effect was replicated in another study by Schneider et al. (2018a)."
