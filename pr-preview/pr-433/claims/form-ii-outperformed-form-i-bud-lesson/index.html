Dell 1911¶

Dell, John A. (1911). Some Observations on the Learning of Sensible Material. Journal of Educational Psychology, 2, 401-406. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0074801

Secondary comparison within the bud-lesson experiment (Forms of 16 and 21 boys): Form II's percentage of correct answers exceeded Form I's in every section (Table III). The author attributes this to the pupils' "greater age and by their previous training", not to lesson order.

"The fact that pupils of Form II are more likely to get a correct answer in any section than those of Form I is ex2>lained by their greater age and by their previous training, since a number of them had already passed through Form I."
