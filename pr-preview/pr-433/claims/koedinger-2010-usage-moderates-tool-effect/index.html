Evidence¶

Frank Reinhold 2024¶

Frank Reinhold, Timo Leuders, Katharina Loibl, Matthias Nückles, Maik Beege, Jan M. Boelmann (2024). Learning Mechanisms Explaining Learning With Digital Tools in Educational Settings: a Cognitive Process Framework. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09845-6

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The review reports, citing Koedinger et al. (2010), a quasi-experimental comparison of students using vs. not using a formative assessment mathematics tool, with outcomes on a standardized mathematics test. Engagement was assessed via behavioral measures of student-tool interaction; adaptive immediate feedback and individualized tools were regarded as the cause of the effect.

"students who did not complete a certain amount of items in the tool were labeled “low usage” and students who did were labeled “high usage”—shedding light on an interaction effect in line with their hypotheses"
