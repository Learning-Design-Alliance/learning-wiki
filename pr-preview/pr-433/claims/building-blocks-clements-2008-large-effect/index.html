Cluster randomized controlled trial rated Meets WWC Group Design Standards Without Reservations; classrooms randomly assigned to Building Blocks or business as usual in Head Start and state-funded preschool sites in New York. Effect size 1.07 (improvement index +36); intervention mean 61.78 (7.46) versus comparison 53.22 (8.38) on the REMA.
