Neuschafer 2022¶

Neuschafer, T. (2022). Understanding Duolingo discussion boards as social-emotional support during the COVID-19 pandemic. Journal of Educators Online. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1393729 doi:10.9743/jeo.2023.20.2.8

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

This interpretation comes from the netnographic analysis reported in the discussion section, which attributes instrumental and informational support findings to the author's earlier dissertation (Neuschafer, 2021) and positions emotional support as the new finding of the current study.

"However, the current study has shown that Duolingo discussion boards can also provide emotional support, wherein learners obtain social encouragement and a sense of community that may give them a new perspective on learning foreign languages during the COVID-19 pandemic."
