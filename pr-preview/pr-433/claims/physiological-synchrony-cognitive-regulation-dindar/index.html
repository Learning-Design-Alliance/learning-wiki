The review attributes that "physiological synchrony among the collaborating students was related only to cognitive regulation" and motivational regulation changes related to achievement; no effect size is printed, so impact is null.

"Dindar et al. (2019), for example, studied high school students' (N = 31) advanced physics lessons where students worked on collaborative learning tasks in 15 sessions, and the researchers measured overall session-based physiological synchrony among the group members."
