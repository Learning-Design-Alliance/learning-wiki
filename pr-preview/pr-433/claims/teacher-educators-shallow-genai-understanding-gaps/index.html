Narrative synthesis finding from the review's professional knowledge gaps theme, aggregating qualitative and mixed-methods studies of teacher educators and faculty. The review reports educators' "shallow or superficial understanding" of GenAI operation and pedagogical integration; no effect sizes are printed.
