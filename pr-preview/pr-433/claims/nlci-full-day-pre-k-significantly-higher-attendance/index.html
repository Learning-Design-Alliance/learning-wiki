Evidence¶

Elaine M. Allensworth and Andrew Zou 2020¶

Elaine M. Allensworth and Andrew Zou. (2020). Supporting Neighborhood Schools from Pre-K through High School: Successes and Challenges of the North Lawndale Cluster Initiative. UChicago Consortium Research Report. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/supporting-neighborhood-schools-from-pre-k-through-high-school

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental evaluation comparing NLCI elementary schools to propensity-weighted matched comparison schools, using population data rather than samples. The report finds pre-k attendance "was significantly higher" after full-day pre-k funding, versus no significant difference in the prior year; no effect size is printed.

"In the year prior to the initiative, pre-k attendance was not significantly different from attendance at matched comparison schools; after the initiative, pre-k attendance was significantly higher."
