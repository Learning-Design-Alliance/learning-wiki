Evidence¶

Noroozi 2012¶

Noroozi, O., Busstra, M. C., Mulder, M., Biemans, H. J. A., Tobi, H., Geelen, A., van't Veer, P., Chizari, M. (2012). Online discussion compensates for suboptimal timing of supportive information presentation in a digitally supported learning environment. Education Tech Research Dev (2012) 60:193-221. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-011-9217-2

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Omnibus ANCOVA over the four timing-by-collaboration conditions in the sequential experiment. The article reports a significant overall difference between the four conditions, F (3, 79) = 5.20; p < 0.01, allowing a possible carry-over effect of timing on collaboration.

"There was a signiﬁcant effect of type of collaboration (PD and OD) on the quality of students' knowledge construction after controlling for the effect of timing of supportive information presentation (IB and ID): F (3, 79) = 5.20; p\ 0.01."
