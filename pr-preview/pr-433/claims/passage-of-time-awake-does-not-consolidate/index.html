Evidence¶

Charlène Truong 2023¶

Charlène Truong, Célia Ruffino, Jérémie Gaveau, Olivier White, Pauline M. Hilt and Charalambos Papaxanthis. (2023). Time of day and sleep effects on motor acquisition and consolidation. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00176-9

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Control experiment 2: G8awake trained at 8 p.m. and was retested 2 h later at 10 p.m., before sleep; its consolidation gain was significantly inferior to G8pm's (d = 1.25), supporting sleep as the contributing factor.

"the consolidation gain (T2_T3) of G8 awake was signiﬁcantly inferior to the ofﬂine gain of the G8 pm (t = −2.99, p = 0.007, d = 1.25; see Fig. 4c)."

Charlène Truong 2023 (2)¶

Charlène Truong, Célia Ruffino, Jérémie Gaveau, Olivier White, Pauline M. Hilt and Charalambos Papaxanthis. (2023). Time of day and sleep effects on motor acquisition and consolidation. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00176-9

q2 · i? · causal · r2

In the same control experiment, G8awake's 2 h gain against zero was not significant (p = 0.31), i.e., no offline improvement occurred before sleep; equivalence was not the primary test here.

"The comparison of T2_T3 gain to reference value zero (0) did not show improvement for G8 awake (t = −1.07, p = 0.31, d = 0.32)."
