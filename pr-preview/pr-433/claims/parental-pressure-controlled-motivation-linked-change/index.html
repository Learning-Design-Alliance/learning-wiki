Evidence¶

Wang 2025¶

Wang, J., Kaufman, T., Mastrotheodoros, S., & Branje, S. (2025). Longitudinal associations between academic motivation and school-related stressors in adolescents transitioning to secondary school. Learning and Instruction, 98, 102144. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102144

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Bivariate latent growth curve models in this preregistered four-wave study of 290 Dutch adolescents (Mage = 11.58 at T1) correlated growth factors of stressors and motivation. Table 7 reports intercept-intercept β = .39 (p < .001) and slope-slope β = .42 (p = .021), showing "significantly positively correlated" growth factors.

"the intercepts and slopes of parental academic pressure and controlled academic motivation were significantly positively correlated, indicating that a higher level of parental academic pressure was related to a higher level of controlled academic motivation"
