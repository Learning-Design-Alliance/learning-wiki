q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r?

Two-stage MASEM (18 studies with academic–intelligence correlations, 27 with executive-function–academic correlations; academic skills collapsed into one score). The article reports the FMS–academic path "dropped from r = .334 to .200, gives an indication of the extent of the mediation".

"Comparing the relationship between FMS and academic skills in the fixed-effects correlations in Table 4 with the MASEM, which dropped from r = .334 to .200, gives an indication of the extent of the mediation."
