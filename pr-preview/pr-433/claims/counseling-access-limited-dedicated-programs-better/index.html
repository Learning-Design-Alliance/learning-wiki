Evidence¶

Venezia 2010¶

Venezia, A., Bracco, K. R., & Nodine, T. (2010). One-shot deal? Students' perceptions of assessment and course placement in California's community colleges. San Francisco: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED566386

q2 · i? · associational · r2

The survey of matriculation officers (73 of 110 colleges responding, 2009) found wide variation in individualized counseling access; "about 41 percent of those surveyed said that less than half of students at their college gained individual access to an advisor or counselor to discuss placement results." These are officer estimates, not counseling data.

Venezia 2010 (2)¶

Venezia, A., Bracco, K. R., & Nodine, T. (2010). One-shot deal? Students' perceptions of assessment and course placement in California's community colleges. San Francisco: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED566386

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

In the student focus groups, "Students were most satisfied with counseling experiences focused on particular communities," where counselors better understood individual situations and goals and were more available. The report notes these programs' lower student/counselor ratios make their costs difficult to scale.

"Students were most satisfied with counseling experiences focused on particular communities, such as international programs, athletic programs, Puente, Umoja, and career programs."
