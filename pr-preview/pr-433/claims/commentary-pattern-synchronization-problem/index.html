Evidence¶

Ritter 2016¶

Ritter, S. (2016). Plug-in Tutor Agents: Still Pluggin'. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 26, 405–415. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0062-z

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The author identifies a synchronization problem inherent in the commentary pattern, where the tutor may comment on outdated student actions.

"if the user is able to act quickly (relative to the tutor's ability to react), the tool and tutor can get out of sync. It can be problematic if the tutor is commenting on an action that the student took three steps ago."
