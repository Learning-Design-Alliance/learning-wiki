q3 · i? · causal · r?

Finding from the national PROMISE evaluation, which relied on a randomized controlled trial design and information from staff interviews, surveys of parents and youth, and program administrative data. The authors report that "many SSI youth received transition services without PROMISE" while service use could still be improved.

"many SSI youth received transition services without PROMISE, but that there was substantial room for PROMISE to improve service use"
