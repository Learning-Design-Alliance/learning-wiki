Evidence¶

Cao 2026¶

Cao, Y., Zhang, M., Gentile, F. & Bonte, M. (2026). Dynamic behavioral and neural correlates of letter-speech sound learning in typical and dyslexic readers. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00410-0

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Mixed repeated-measures ANOVA (Block, Condition, Group) on 31 typical and 31 dyslexic adult readers across four 8-minute training blocks. "Accuracy significantly improved over successive blocks" with η²P = .723; by Block 4 both groups exceeded 90% accuracy on matching pairs.

"Accuracy significantly improved over successive blocks (main effect of Block, F(2.034, 180) = 156.622, p < .001, η²P = .723)."
