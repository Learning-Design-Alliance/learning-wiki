Evidence¶

Hou 2025¶

Hou, C., Kuhlmann, S. L., Greene, J. A., Bernacki, M. L., & Hogan, K. A. (2025). Process mining measures students' help-seeking transitions when completing assignments in an online learning and assessment platform. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-025-09442-0

q3 · i3 · associational · r2

Kruskal-Wallis tests of digital event frequencies across four exam-performance quartiles (n = 482 undergraduates in an introductory biology course) showed incorrect submissions differed across groups, η² = 0.15, a "large effect size (Richardson, 2011)". Dunn's post-hoc tests showed the low group exceeded all others.

"a Kruskal-Wallis test between the groups indicated a statistically significant difference in the frequency of incorrect submissions, c 2(3) = 74.64, p <.001, h2 = 0.15, with a large effect size (Richardson, 2011)"
