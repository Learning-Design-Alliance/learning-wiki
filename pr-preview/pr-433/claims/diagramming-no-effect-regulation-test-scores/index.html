Evidence¶

Pijeira-Díaz 2023¶

Pijeira-Díaz, H. J., van de Pol, J., Channa, F., & de Bruin, A. (2023). Scaffolding self-regulated learning from causal-relations texts: Diagramming and self-assessment to improve metacomprehension accuracy? Metacognition and Learning, 18, 631–658. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09343-0

Supplementary analyses in the randomized experiment found "no significant differences among conditions in terms of test scores for relations"; regulation measures for relations and facts likewise showed no significant differences (e.g., p = .067 and p = .086).

"There were no significant differences among conditions in terms of test scores for relations, F(3, 2538) = 1.189, p = .130, η 2 p =0 .002."
