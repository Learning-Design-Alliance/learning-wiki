Evidence¶

Sunny Yu 2026¶

Sunny Yu, Myra Cheng, Ahmad Jabbar, Ilia Sucholutsky, Katherine M. Collins, Dan Jurafsky, Robert D. Hawkins. (2026). Cognitive offloading and the speedup illusion in human-AI interaction. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23177 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.23177

q3 · i? · causal · r3

In the prediction sample, Need for Cognition items 3 and 4 showed negative coefficients (β=−6.55 and β=−6.66, both p<0.05), meaning "the more participants dislike having to think, the more susceptible they are to the speedup illusion". AI-use frequency and the AI Assessment scale did not predict calibration error.

"we focus on the prediction sample and identify negative coefficients for the difference between the predicted independent versus assisted completion times for item 3 ("thinking is not my idea of fun";β=−6.55,SE=3.14,t=−2.11,p<0.05)"
