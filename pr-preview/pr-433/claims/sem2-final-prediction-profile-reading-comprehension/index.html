Evidence¶

Jessica Massonnié 2018¶

Jessica Massonnié, Maryse Bianco, Laurent Lima, Pascal Bressoux. (2018). Longitudinal predictors of reading comprehension in French at first grade: Unpacking the oral comprehension component of the simple view. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2018.01.005

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Final longitudinal structural equation model (SEM 2) with 556 French first-graders, controlling for father's SES and home language, accounted for 82% of reading comprehension variance; LLL skills carried a coefficient similar in magnitude to decoding skills, while discourse skills were only "marginally" predictive.

"This new model (SEM 2, see Fig. 4) accounted for 82% of the variance in reading comprehension skills, which were predicted by T2 decoding skills (β = .33), T1 decoding precursors (β = .31), T2 LLL skills (β = .39), and marginally by T2 discourse skills (β = .11)."
