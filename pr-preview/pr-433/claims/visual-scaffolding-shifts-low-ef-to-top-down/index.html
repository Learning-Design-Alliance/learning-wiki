Evidence¶

Huang 2026¶

Huang, S. C.-F., Chen, Z.-H., Lin, Y.-T., & Yeh, M. K. (2026). Bridging strategy gaps in programming: the role of visual scaffolding for learners with low cognitive ability. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10684-1

q3 · i3 · causal · r1

Independent-sample t-tests comparing 8 low-EF learners in the scaffolding group with 6 in the control group. The article reports "fixation duration of the EG are significantly lower than the CG" with g = 1.491, and visit count g = 1.777 (t (5.64) = − 2.88, p <.05).

"The fixation duration of the EG are significantly lower than the CG (t (5.69) = − 2.55, p <.05, Hedges' g = 1.491, 95% CI [−0.18, 0]), fixation count (t (5.76) = − 2.53, p <.05, Hedges' g = 1.499"
