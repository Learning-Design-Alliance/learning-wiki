The review reports meta-analytic evidence for formative assessment (learning progress assessment / curriculum-based measurement), quoting 'several meta-analyses reporting effect sizes' with the printed value d = 0.32 in regular classrooms and higher effects for special educational needs. All procedures share dynamic, individual assessment of learning progress.
