Theoretical argument in the article's background and motivation section, reviewing the state of ontology adoption in practical e-learning. The article asserts that "Most of these problems can be partially overcome by ontology reuse." No empirical data are reported for this claim.

"Ontologies have been promoted for two decades in e-learning research (especially within Semantic Web and intelligent tutoring systems), but they are rarely used in everyday, practical e-learning platforms. The reasons are related to high cost and development complexity, poor integration with practical educational systems, performance, and scalability issues."
