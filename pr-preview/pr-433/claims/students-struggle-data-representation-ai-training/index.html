Response-pattern analysis of Item 3 (Data-FKSA3) in the main study, assessing identification of appropriate data representations. The correct answer was grayscale (0-255), but 46.9% of students selected the binary option, revealing misconceptions about data representation.

"Despite this, 46.9% of students selected Option A (binary, 0/1). This indicates that many students assumed handwriting images could be represented as binary outputs"
