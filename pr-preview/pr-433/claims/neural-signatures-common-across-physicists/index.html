Evidence¶

Robert A. Mason 2021¶

Robert A. Mason, Reinhard A. Schumacher and Marcel Adam Just. (2021). The neuroscience of advanced scientific concepts. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00107-6

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Cross-participant classification using activation levels at 120 voxel locations with consistent profiles across the nine training participants. Nine participants had accuracies greater than chance at p < 0.01 and one at p < 0.05, which the article takes to indicate commonality across participants of the neural representations of these physics concepts.

"The mean cross-participant classification rank accuracy to identify the 45 concepts for the 10 participants was 0.70 (range = 0.53–0.81)."
