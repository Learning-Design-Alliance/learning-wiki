Related Claims¶

  • In the CRT experiment, confidence appraisals predicted solving accuracy more strongly than goal-driven effort appraisals — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • In the CRT experiment, response time served as a significantly stronger cue for goal-driven effort, data-driven effort, and difficulty appraisals than for confidence appraisals — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • In the non-verbal MTT experiment, response time served as a cue for goal-driven effort appraisals more than for confidence appraisals, partly replicating experiment 1 — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • In the mental rotation experiment, item complexity (rotation angle) served as a significant cue for all four appraisal types, with difficulty appraisals most sensitive — related
