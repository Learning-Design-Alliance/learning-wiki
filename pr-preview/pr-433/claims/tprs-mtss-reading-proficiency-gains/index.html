Reading evaluations using the Oxford Reading Criterion Scale across the three cycles in the single EFL class. The article reports grade-level ranges per group; no effect size or statistical test is printed.

"Low-performing students, who began with basic app-based reading in Cycle 1, progressed to Grade 3b reading standards by Cycle 3. The majority student group steadily advanced, with their reading proficiency ranging from Grade 3a–4b in Cycle 2 to Grade 4a–5b in Cycle 3, while high-performing students reached Grade 6a–7a levels."
