Evidence¶

Dillon H. Murphy 2023¶

Dillon H. Murphy, Jeri L. Little, Elizabeth L. Bjork. (2023). The Value of Using Tests in Education as Tools for Learning—Not Just for Assessment. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09808-3

q3 · i? · theoretical · r2

The commentary reviews pretesting studies (e.g., Arnold & McDermott, 2013; Richland et al., 2009), reporting that when students answer questions about a passage or lesson they are about to receive, "their learning of the then-presented material is enhanced even if they are not able to answer any of those questions correctly." No effect size is printed.

Dillon H. Murphy 2023 (2)¶

Dillon H. Murphy, Jeri L. Little, Elizabeth L. Bjork. (2023). The Value of Using Tests in Education as Tools for Learning—Not Just for Assessment. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09808-3

q3 · i? · theoretical · r3

The commentary describes Little and Bjork (2016), who used multiple-choice and cued-recall pretests before studying and examined later learning of related information; "multiple-choice pretesting was more effective than cued-recall pretesting, even after a delay." No effect size is printed.

"Overall, results revealed that multiple-choice pretesting was more effective than cued-recall pretesting, even after a delay"
