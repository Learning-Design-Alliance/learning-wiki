Coauthorship network analysis in the R package Bibliometrix over publications of 22 workshop organizers retrieved from OpenAlex; only three of six workshops were indexed. The analysis "resulted in six total communities, with the majority (80%) of nodes falling into the largest three."

"The network had a total size of 2,197 authors and over 4,000 publications. We used Louvain community detection technique (Blondel et al., 2008) to create clusters of researchers based on their shared publications."
