Evidence¶

Ghader Kurdi 2020¶

Ghader Kurdi, Jared Leo, Bijan Parsia, Uli Sattler, Salam Al-Emari. (2020). A Systematic Review of Automatic Question Generation for Educational Purposes. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-019-00186-y

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review's OBJ4 analytical questions tracked evolution since Alsubait's review on feedback, difficulty control, and verbalisation; the abstract-level conclusion is that these areas remain under-investigated, echoing pre-2014 limitations.

"Similar to what was found previously, there is little focus in the current literature on generating questions of controlled difficulty, enriching question forms and structures, automating template construction, improving presentation, and generating feedback."
