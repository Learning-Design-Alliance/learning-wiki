q3 · i? · causal · r2

The Quint, Bos, and Polit (1997) study examined New Chance in 16 sites in 10 states; 2,322 eligible volunteer women were randomly assigned, with results based on those completing the 42-month follow-up survey.

"Between 1989 and 1991, 2,322 women who were eligible and volunteered for the program were randomly assigned: 1,553 to the New Chance group and 769 to the control group."

What Works Clearinghouse 2008 (2)¶

What Works Clearinghouse. (2008). New Chance: WWC Intervention Report, Dropout Prevention. U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/336

q2 · i? · review · r2

The WWC's extent-of-evidence categorization, which takes into account the number of studies and total sample size across studies meeting WWC evidence standards, rates the evidence base as small for the completing school domain.

"The WWC considers the extent of evidence for New Chance to be small for completing school."
