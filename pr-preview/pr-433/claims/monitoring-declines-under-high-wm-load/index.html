The review reports, citing Bryce et al. 2023, that a study which "experimentally manipulated WM load found that metacognitive monitoring declined under high WM demands". The review attributes the finding; the original study's design details are not described here.

"Notably, one recent study that experimentally manipulated WM load found that metacognitive monitoring declined under high WM demands, suggesting a functional dependence of MC on WM (Bryce et al. 2023)."
