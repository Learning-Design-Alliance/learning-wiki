Jamie Costley 2021¶

Jamie Costley, Mik Fanguy. (2021). Collaborative note-taking affects cognitive load: the interplay of completeness and interaction. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-021-09979-2

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Two-sample t-test on intrinsic load in the same flipped-course comparison; the printed difference was .40 and "not statistically significant (t = − .417, p = .677)". Equivalence was not tested; no effect size was printed.

"The difference between the treatment and control group’s level of intrinsic cognitive load was .40, and this difference was not statistically significant (t = − .417, p = .677)."
