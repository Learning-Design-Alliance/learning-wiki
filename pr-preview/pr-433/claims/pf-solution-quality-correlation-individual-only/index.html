Evidence¶

Charleen Brand 2023¶

Charleen Brand, Christian Hartmann, Katharina Loibl, Nikol Rummel. (2023). Do students learn more from failing alone or in groups? Insights into the effects of collaborative versus individual problem solving in productive failure. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-023-09619-7

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Exploratory Pearson correlations computed separately per condition (Table 4): for PF IND, quality correlated with gap awareness (r = −0.48, p < 0.001) and conceptual knowledge (r = 0.46, p < 0.001); for PF COLL neither correlation was significant.

"The exploratory correlational analyses revealed that quality of solutions was significantly associated with awareness of knowledge gaps and conceptual knowledge after instruction for the individual condition, but not for the collaborative one"
