Evidence¶

Moeller 2018¶

Moeller, E., Seeskin, A., & Nagaoka, J. (2018). Practice-Driven Data: Lessons from Chicago's Approach to Research, Data, and Practice in Education. Chicago, IL: UChicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/practice-driven-data-lessons-chicagos-approach-research-data-and-practice-education

q2 · i? · design · r1

The paper reports, citing Consortium research, that the Freshman OnTrack indicator outpredicts a combined set of background measures — eighth-grade test scores, SES, race/ethnicity, gender, and school mobility. The paper stresses its power also comes from using readily available, understandable data to focus problem-solving discussions. No effect size is printed.

"The power of the Freshman OnT rack indicator comes from much more than it being more predictive of high school graduation than eighth-grade test scores, SES, race/ethnicity, gender, and school mobility combined."

Moeller 2018 (2)¶

Moeller, E., Seeskin, A., & Nagaoka, J. (2018). Practice-Driven Data: Lessons from Chicago's Approach to Research, Data, and Practice in Education. Chicago, IL: UChicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/practice-driven-data-lessons-chicagos-approach-research-data-and-practice-education

q2 · i? · design · r1

In the characteristics-of-effective-indicators discussion, the paper reports that Freshman OnTrack serves not only as an early warning indicator but as a focus for school strategies, because practice changes that improve OnTrack rates also lead to graduation increases. The linkage is asserted from Consortium research; no statistic is printed in this passage.

"However, it is even more useful as a focus for school strategies because changes in school practice that improve Freshman OnT rack rates also lead to increases in high school graduation rates."
