Evidence¶

Center for Research on Education Outcomes 2019¶

Center for Research on Education Outcomes. (2019). Charter School Performance in Pennsylvania. Stanford University. https://credo.stanford.edu/reports/item/charter-school-performance-in-pennsylvania-2/

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental matched comparison using Virtual Control Records built from feeder-school TPS students; growth analyzed in standard deviation units across three growth periods, 2013-14 to 2016-17. The report states charter students experience "30 fewer days of learning in math" while reading differences were not statistically significant.

"in a typical school year, charter students in Pennsylvania experience less progress equivalent to 30 fewer days of learning in math in a 180-day school year. Because the difference in growth in reading is not statistically significant, Pennsylvania charter school students experience similar growth"
