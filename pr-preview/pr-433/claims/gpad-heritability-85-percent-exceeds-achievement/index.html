Evidence¶

Kaito Kawakami 2024¶

Kaito Kawakami, Francesca Procopio, Kaili Rimfeld, Margherita Malanchini, Sophie von Stumm, Kathryn Asbury, Robert Plomin. (2024). Exploring the genetic prediction of academic underachievement and overachievement. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00251-9

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Univariate twin model-fitting (OpenMx) in the TEDS twin sample estimated ACE variance components for GPAΔ and related traits (Fig. 2). The article reports heritability is "strikingly high for GPA Δ (85%)," above achievement's 67%; mathematics and English GPAΔ were 87% and 85%.

"Heritability, the proportion of phenotypic variance explained by genetic variance, is strikingly high for GPA Δ (85%), higher than for general achievement itself at age 7 (67%)."
