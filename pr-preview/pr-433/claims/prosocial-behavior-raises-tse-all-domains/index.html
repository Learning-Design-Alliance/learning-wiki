Evidence¶

M. Zee 2016¶

M. Zee, P.F. de Jong, H.M.Y. Koomen. (2016). Teachers' self-efficacy in relation to individual students with a variety of social-emotional behaviors: A multilevel investigation. Journal of Educational Psychology, 108(7), 1013-1027. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000106

q3 · i? · associational · r2

In the same multilevel analysis of 526 students and 69 teachers, prosocial behavior was a positive predictor of teacher self-efficacy in every domain examined. The article states the association held "irrespective of teaching domain." No effect sizes are printed.

"In contrast, teachers reported higher levels of self-efficacy toward students with high levels of prosocial behavior, irrespective of teaching domain."
