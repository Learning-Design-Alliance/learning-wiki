Jiang 2016¶

Jiang, J. Y., & Sporte, S. E. (2016). Teacher Evaluation in Chicago: Differences in Observation and Value-Added Scores by Teacher, Student, and School Characteristics. UChicago Consortium on School Research. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED562786

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Descriptive analysis of value-added score distributions within higher- and lower-poverty schools (Figure 2). The gap between schools narrows, closes, and reverses moving up the value-added distribution; average differences are driven by lower scores of teachers at the bottom of the distribution.
