Evidence¶

Leila Etemadi 2023¶

Leila Etemadi, Dan-Anders Jirenhed and Anders Rasmussen. (2023). Effects of working memory load and CS-US intervals on delay eyeblink conditioning. npj Science of Learning 8:16. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00167-w

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Human experiment in which groups trained with 150 ms (n = 4) and 250 ms (n = 18) CS-US intervals showed very few conditioned responses over 100 trials; the article reports "an interstimulus interval <250 ms produces very few conditioned responses".

"The two main ﬁndings of this study are that (1) an interstimulus interval <250 ms produces very few conditioned responses"
