Evidence¶

Hoyniak 2015¶

Hoyniak, C. P., Petersen, I. T., McQuillan, M. E., Staples, A. D., & Bates, J. E. (2015). Less Efficient Neural Processing Related to Irregular Sleep and Less Sustained Attention in Toddlers. Developmental Neuropsychology. https://doi.org/10.1080/87565641.2015.1016162

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Correlational result from the toddler EEG sample; behavioral accuracy on target trials averaged 40.21% (SD=22.39%) and mean response time was 1657.22 ms (SD=235.99 ms). The article reports behavioral measures were "uncorrelated with target P3 amplitudes and latencies."

"Behavioral accuracy on the task and mean response time to the target trials was uncorrelated with target P3 amplitudes and latencies, suggesting that behavioral performance on the Oddball task may capture different types of individual differences in attentional processing than ERPs."
