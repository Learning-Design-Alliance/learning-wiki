The author's first-hand laboratory observation, offered without data or test, motivating the apparatus. The article states it "takes really more time for students" to compute errors than to make the drawings.

"I have found that it takes really more time for students who are working upon mirror-draw- ing problems to compute their errors than it does to make the mirror-draw- ings in the first place."
