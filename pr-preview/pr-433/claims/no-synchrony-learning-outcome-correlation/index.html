q3 · i? · causal · r2

Correlational analysis within the hyperscanning dataset found no synchrony–outcome correlation in any significantly coupled ROI, "for both strategies"; adjusting synchrony for each ROI's optimal lag also did not yield a correlation.

"we did not find a direct correlation between child-instructor neural synchrony and learning outcomes in any of the coupled ROIs that showed significant correlations (Figure S3). This finding held true for both strategies and remained consistent when focusing specifically on word learning scores."
