Evidence¶

Lortie-Forgues 2017¶

Lortie-Forgues, Hugues and Siegler, Robert S (2017) Conceptual knowledge of decimal arithmetic. Journal of Educational Psychology. pp. 374-391. ISSN: 0022-0620. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000148

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Study 1 correlational analysis: children answered 83% of decimal magnitude comparisons correctly, yet "numbers of correct magnitude comparison and direction of effect judgments were unrelated" in both grades, and also on the below-one multiplication and division subsets (all r's n.s.).

"For both 6th and 8th graders, numbers of correct magnitude comparison and direction of effect judgments were unrelated (6th grade, r = 0.01, n.s.; 8th grade, r = 0.10, n.s.)."
