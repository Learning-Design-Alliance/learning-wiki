Carolyn Rosé 2021¶

Carolyn Rosé, Sanna Järvelä. (2021). A theory-driven reflection on context-aware support for collaborative discussions in light of analytics, affordances, and platforms. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-021-09360-8

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

Editorial account (the editors report, describing Saqr & López-Pernas's network analytic study) of an analytic construct applied at large scale: "diffusion-based centralities" demonstrated to elucidate information exchange and uptake, with validation via achievement measures and qualitative analysis.

"The analysis employs a particular analytic construct, namely diffusion-based centralities, which were demonstrated to elucidate processes of information exchange and uptake within a broadscale collaboration."
