Evidence¶

Nadja Gentner & Tina Seufert 2020¶

Nadja Gentner & Tina Seufert. (2020). The Double-Edged Interactions of Prompts and Self-efficacy. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09227-7

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Mixed ANOVA (condition between-subjects, time before/during/after learning within-subjects) on self-efficacy ratings. The time × condition interaction was significant, ηp2 = .07; within the prompted group self-efficacy rose significantly (F(2,66) = 11.91, p < .001, ηp2 = .26) but not in the control group (F(2,66) = 0.39, ns).

"the analysis indicated an interaction effect between time and condition F(2,134) = 4.70, p =. 0 1 ,ηp2 = .07 (medium effect), ε = .886."

Nadja Gentner & Tina Seufert 2020 (2)¶

Nadja Gentner & Tina Seufert. (2020). The Double-Edged Interactions of Prompts and Self-efficacy. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09227-7

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Follow-up t-tests comparing the prompt and no-prompt groups at the during-learning and after-learning measurement points found "no differences regarding learners ’ self-efficacy" at either point; the increase did not yield between-group differences.

"Analyses indicated no differences regarding learners ’ self-efficacy 20 minutes into learning, t(68) = -1.21, p = .23, and immediately after learning, t(68) = -1.26, p =. 2 1 ."
