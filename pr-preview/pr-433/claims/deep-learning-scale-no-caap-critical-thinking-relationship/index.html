Reason 2010¶

Reason, R. D., Cox, B. E., McIntosh, K., & Terenzini, P. T. (2010). Deep learning as an individual, conditional, and contextual influence on first-year student outcomes. Paper presented at the Annual Forum of the Association for Institutional Research, Chicago, IL. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED520486

HLM models predicting the CAAP Critical Thinking module (national mean 61.65, SD 5.39) for 5,905 first-year students; the individual DEEP coefficient was 0.002, non-significant, while ACT Composite was strongly correlated with CAAP-CT (r = .817; p < .01).
