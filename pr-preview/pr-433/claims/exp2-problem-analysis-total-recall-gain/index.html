Explanatory Models in the Processing of Science Text: The Role of Prior Knowledge Activation Through Small-Group Discussion. Journal of Educational Psychology, 81(4), 610-619. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.81.4.610

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Experiment 2: 88 Dutch secondary students (46 ninth-grade novices, 42 tenth-grade experts) randomly assigned to blood cell or airplane problem conditions, then read a 2,200-word text on osmosis and diffusion and completed free recall. Experts recalled more than novices overall, F(1, 82) = 82.47, p < .001.
