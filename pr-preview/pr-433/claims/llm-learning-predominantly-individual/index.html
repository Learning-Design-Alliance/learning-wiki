q2 · i? · associational · r2

Survey item on social setting of LLM-based learning among 678 LLM learners. "An overwhelming 89% ( 𝑛 = 606) of respondents said they primarily use LLMs for learning alone (individual learning)." Another 9% reported both individual and group use in equal measure.

"An overwhelming 89% ( 𝑛 = 606) of respondents said they primarily use LLMs for learning alone (individual learning). Only 1% (𝑛 = 8) primarily use them in a group or collaborative setting."
