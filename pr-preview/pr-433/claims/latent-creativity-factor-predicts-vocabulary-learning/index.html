Evidence¶

Luchini 2025¶

Luchini, S. A., Kaufman, J. C., Goecke, B., Wilhelm, O., Kenett, Y. N., Lei, D., Benedek, M., van Hell, J. G., & Beaty, R. E. (2025). Creativity supports learning through associative thinking. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00334-1

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Structural equation model in Study 2 specified a latent creativity factor indicated by drawing, writing, and openness predicting LET retention (χ² (4) = 0.85, p = 0.931; CFI = 1.00); it "yielded a medium effect of creativity on learning (β = 0.45, p < 0.001)".

"As expected, this model yielded a medium effect of creativity on learning (β = 0.45, p < 0.001)"
