Evidence¶

Negen 2011¶

Negen, J., Sarnecka, B. W., & Lee, M. D. (2011). An Excel sheet for inferring children's number-knower levels from give-N data. Behavior Research Methods. https://doi.org/10.3758/s13428-011-0134-4

q2 · i? · design · r2

Fully Bayesian inference on the calibration data set from Negen and Sarnecka (2010), using two chains with 2,000 burn-in and 25,000 collection samples each. The article also reports "an evidence value v of 16.94 ( SD=0.69)", slightly lower than values from other data sets (about 29 and 23).

"The model inferred that among the children tested, 9 were 0-knowers, 48 were 1-knowers, 50 were 2-knowers, 53 were 3-knowers, 67 were 4-knowers, and 196 were CP-knowers."
