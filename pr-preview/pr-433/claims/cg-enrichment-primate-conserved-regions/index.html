Evidence¶

Shiying Wang 2026¶

Shiying Wang, Joan Bosson-Heenan, Andrew Grotzinger, Jan C. Frijters & Jeffrey R. Gruen. (2026). Shared genetic architecture of reading and attention disorders using genomic structural equation modeling. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00433-7

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Stratified Genomic SEM using 50 binary annotations from the BaselineLD Version 2.2 model. The article reports an enrichment ratio of 14.62 (SE = 3.92, p = 2.5 × 10⁻⁴) for primate-conserved regions; no standardized effect size is printed.

"Only primate-conserved regions (conserved_primate_phastcons46way) reached Bonferroni-corrected significance (enrichment ratio = 14.62, SE = 3.92, p = 2.5 × 10⁻⁴), indicating that SNPs in genomic regions conserved across primate species contribute disproportionately to the shared genetic architecture captured by Cg."
