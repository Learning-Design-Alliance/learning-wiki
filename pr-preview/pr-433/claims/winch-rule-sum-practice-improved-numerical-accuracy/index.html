Winch, W. H. (1910). Accuracy in school children. Does improvement in numerical accuracy "transfer"? The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0072557

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Within-group practice-record analysis in School I: marks for the 1st and 2nd practice exercises were compared with the 5th and 6th for each ability section. The author reports "a very consid- erable improvement (approximately 21 '/<.) in numerical ac- curacy".

"There can be no doubt that, on the whole, a very consid- erable improvement (approximately 21 '/<.) in numerical ac- curacy has taken place."

Winch 1910 (2)¶

Winch, W. H. (1910). Accuracy in school children. Does improvement in numerical accuracy "transfer"? The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0072557

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Within-group practice-record analysis in School O.K.: marks for the 1st and 2nd practice exercises were compared with the 9th and 10th for each section, with printed percentage improvements of 27%, 26%, 18%, 16%, 24%, 17% and 19%. The author states "the improvement is, in all cases, very considerable".

"It will be seen that the improvement is, in all cases, very considerable; it now remains to be seen how far> this im- provement in accuracy of numerical computation is trans- ferred, if at all, to accuracy in arithmetical reasoning."
