Baoyi W and Khan FU 2026¶

Baoyi W and Khan FU (2026). AI and higher education: teachers' perceptions of students' ethical tensions in online pedagogical methods. Front. Psychol. 17:1881032. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1881032

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Findings from the CLT section of the thematic analysis. Teachers reported the authenticity crisis in judging communicative competence, risk-free fluency removing error-driven learning, the confidence mirage of dependence on AI availability, and pragmatic blindness from blindly repeated AI responses.

"Teachers complain that they are increasingly unable to tell whether a student can actually communicate or is just performing responses recommended by AI."
