Evidence¶

Wakefield 2018¶

Wakefield, J., Frawley, J. K., Tyler, J., & Dyson, L. E. (2018). The impact of an iPad-supported annotation and sharing technology on university students’ learning. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.03.013

q3 · i? · associational · r2

OLS regression on exam and student record data from 741 first-year accounting students, of whom 197 used the tablet. The tablet dummy was positive and significant for the financial statement analysis question, with "coefficient 0.187; p-value 0.000".

"They indicate the tablet has a positive and significant effect (coefficient 0.187; p-value 0.000) on performance in procedural equation-based questions."
