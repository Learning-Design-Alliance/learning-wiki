q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Qualitative interview finding from the scenario interviews. Teachers named the design itself, e.g. "The bot isn't sentient, and it's going to tell you the things that you want to hear, but that's not real life" (P29).

"What made agreeableness a developmental rather than merely a design problem was what it removes: the disagreement, judgment, and disappointment through which adolescents learn to sustain human relationships."
