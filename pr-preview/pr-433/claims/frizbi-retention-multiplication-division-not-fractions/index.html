Pilli 2013¶

Pilli, Olga; Aksu, Meral. (2013). The effects of computer-assisted instruction on the achievement, attitudes and retention of fourth grade mathematics students in North Cyprus. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2012.10.010

Retention was measured four months after the intervention in the same quasi-experimental comparison of 29 experimental and 26 control students.
