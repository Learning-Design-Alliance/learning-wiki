Evidence¶

Geary 2012¶

Geary, D. C., Hoard, M. K., & Bailey, D. H. (2012). Fact Retrieval Deficits in Low Achieving Children and Children with Mathematical Learning Disability. Journal of Learning Disabilities, 45(4). https://doi.org/10.1177/0022219410392046

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Multilevel analyses of a procedural competence variable, d-prime from the Number Sets Test, and number line absolute error across grades 2-4. The study's summary states retrieval deficits occur "with intact addition procedural competencies" and that unlike retrieval errors, number competencies improve across grades.

"First, the addition fact retrieval deficits of the children in the LA-Severe Fact Retrieval group are found with intact addition procedural competencies."
