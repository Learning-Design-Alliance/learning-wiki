Evidence¶

Jingjing Chen 2022¶

Jingjing Chen, Bing Xu & Dan Zhang. (2022). Inter-brain coupling analysis reveals learning-related attention of primary school students. bioRxiv. https://doi.org/10.1101/2022.06.08.495411

q2 · i3 · associational · r1

Observational correlation in one grade-3 class of 28 students wearing single-channel EEG headbands during five classroom sessions; coupling computed per 30-s epoch against class-average attention dynamics. The article reports "r = 0.446, p = .017, n = 28".

"Here, individual’s inter-brain attention coupling during lectures was found to be positively correlated with their academic performance (Fig.4a, r = 0.446, p = .017, n = 28): more similar a student's attention dynamic is to the class -average, better their academic performances are."
