Evidence¶

Yao X-C and Fan L 2026¶

Yao X-C and Fan L (2026) From automated scoring to learning diagnosis: a mechanism study of AI-supported formative assessment in English writing. Front. Psychol. 17:1905455. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1905455

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Bivariate correlations among process variables in the cycle-level dataset. "comprehension showed the strongest correlation (r = 0.501, p < 0.001)" with revision quality, exceeding actionability (r = 0.480) and diagnostic accuracy (r = 0.445).

"Diagnostic accuracycorrelatedwithrevisionquality( r =0.445, p < 0.001); actionability correlated with revision quality (r = 0.480, p < 0.001); and comprehension showed the strongest correlation (r = 0.501, p < 0.001)."

Yao X-C and Fan L 2026 (2)¶

Yao X-C and Fan L (2026) From automated scoring to learning diagnosis: a mechanism study of AI-supported formative assessment in English writing. Front. Psychol. 17:1905455. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1905455

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Exploratory indirect-effect analysis with diagnostic accuracy as predictor, actionability and comprehension as intermediates, and revision quality as outcome, using 5,000 student-cluster bootstrap samples. All adjusted indirect effects remained positive.

"After adjustment for assessment condition with 5,000 student-cluster bootstrap samples, the indirect eﬀects were 0.043 through actionability (95% CI [0.012, 0.078]), 0.024 through comprehension(95%CI[0.005,0.050]),and0.067intotal(95%CI [0.024,0.113])."
