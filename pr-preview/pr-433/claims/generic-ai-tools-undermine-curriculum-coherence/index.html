Interview finding from a chief information officer in Washington, reported in the section on where current AI tools fall short. Another educator noted using ChatGPT for 3rd grade social studies lessons was a time saver but "in danger of supplanting the curriculum."

"We are finding that many big, generic AI tools produce the same low-quality artifacts you might find on 'Teachers Pay Teachers,' which do not always match our district's pedagogical beliefs."
