Evidence¶

Zhenan Feng 2018¶

Zhenan Feng, Vicente A. González, Robert Amor, Ruggiero Lovreglio, Guillermo Cabrera-Guerrero. (2018). Immersive Virtual Reality Serious Games for Evacuation Training and Research: A Systematic Literature Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.09.002

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports Burigat and Chittaro (2016), whose learning goal was spatial knowledge for effective airplane evacuation, assessed with paper-based map marking of exits and seats plus recorded evacuation time. No effect size is printed.

"Results showed that participants trained by IVR SG obtained better spatial knowledge than those trained by safety cards."

Zhenan Feng 2018 (2)¶

Zhenan Feng, Vicente A. González, Robert Amor, Ruggiero Lovreglio, Guillermo Cabrera-Guerrero. (2018). Immersive Virtual Reality Serious Games for Evacuation Training and Research: A Systematic Literature Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.09.002

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports Aoki et al. (2007), who investigated spatial skills influenced by relative body orientation during IVR training in a spacecraft evacuation, recording evacuation time, numbers of turns and errors. No effect size is printed.

"Results showed that local training (visually upright relative to the “ local” module) enabled landmark and route learning, while station training (constant orientation irrespective of local visual vertical) improved sense of direction and performance in low visibility."
