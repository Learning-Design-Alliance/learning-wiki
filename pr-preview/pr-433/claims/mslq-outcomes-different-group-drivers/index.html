Evidence¶

Carla Haelermans 2022¶

Carla Haelermans. (2022). The Effects of Group differentiation by students' learning strategies. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09575-0

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Subgroup analysis of the MSLQ regression results by differentiation group. The article reports that "the effect on elaboration is driven by the instruction-dependent group" while other strategy outcomes are driven by different groups.

"The effect on elaboration is driven by the instruction-dependent group, the effects on critical thinking are driven by the instruction-independent group, and the effect on effort regulation is driven by the average group."
