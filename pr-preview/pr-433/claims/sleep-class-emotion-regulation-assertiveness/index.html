q2 · i? · associational · r2

Linear regression with covariates (Table 3, column 2) on teacher-rated social-emotional-behavioral competencies in the secondary analysis of 351 pre-kindergarteners. The article reports "higher ERC: emotion regulation scores, indicating greater emotion regulation (β=3.23, p<0.1), and higher T-CRS: assertiveness scores (β=1.97, p<0.1)" for the earlier, longer, consistent class.

"children classified in the earlier, longer, consistent sleep health class had higher ERC: emotion regulation scores, indicating greater emotion regulation (β=3.23, p<0.1), and higher T-CRS: assertiveness scores (β=1.97, p<0.1) than their peers classified in the later, shorter, inconsistent sleep health class."
