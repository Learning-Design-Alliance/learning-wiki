Reinhard Pekrun 2024¶

Reinhard Pekrun. (2024). Overcoming Fragmentation in Motivation Science: Why, When, and How Should We Integrate Theories? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09846-5

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

The commentary cites prior syntheses (Hattie et al., 2020; Noetel et al., 2023; Richardson et al., 2012) to support the claim that motivational variables rarely explain more than 10% of variance, using this as evidence that fundamental change in motivation theory may be overdue.

"in contrast to cognitive variables like intelligence or prior knowledge, motivational variables rarely explain more than 10% of the variance in behavior and outcomes (see, e.g., Hattie et al., 2020; Noetel et al., 2023; Richardson et al., 2012;"
