Liu YJ, Ma HZ, Luo HY, Xiong YX, Qu FW, Xie JP, Guo Y. (2026). From traditional classroom to AI-enhanced flipped classroom: a three-year pedagogical evolution for international students in pharmacology. BMC Medical Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s12909-026-09608-7

Mann-Whitney U comparisons (Fig. 2B-E) of the 100-point comprehensive assessment (pre-class/PPT 30, presentation 40, knowledge 30 points) between the 2024 and 2025 cohorts. Gains concentrated in process quality; deep mastery measures showed no significant difference.

"the score for the AI- enhanced flipped classroom group was also significantly higher than that for the flipped classroom group (p < 0.05) (Fig. 2C).
