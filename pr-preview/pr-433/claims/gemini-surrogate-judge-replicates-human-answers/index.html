q3 · i? · associational · r?

In the automatic evaluation study, Gemini-2.5-Pro evaluated the same 64 MCQs under the same within-subject design as the human study, via two-turn conversations with probability-extracting scaffolds. The authors conclude Gemini "can effectively replicate human judgments".

"The high agreement between human judges and Gemini also indicated that Gemini can effectively replicate human judgments in answering MCQs generated from our pipeline, highlighting its potential as a reliable surrogate judge for the expansive generation stage of our pipeline."
