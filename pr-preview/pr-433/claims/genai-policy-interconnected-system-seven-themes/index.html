Thematic analysis of the same cohort's policy artifacts and discussions, reported in the Findings section. The analysis found participants "framed effective policy as an interconnected system" rather than isolated rules, with themes acting as prerequisites for one another.

"Across the seven themes, participants did not treat the GenAI course policy as a set of isolated rules. Instead, they framed effective policy as an interconnected system in which knowledge, evaluation, collaboration, contextualization, autonomy, ethics, an d institutional responsibility mutually shaped one another."
