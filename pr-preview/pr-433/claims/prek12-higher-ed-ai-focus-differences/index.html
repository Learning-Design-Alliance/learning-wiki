Evidence¶

Yao Fu 2024¶

Yao Fu, Zhenjie Weng, Jiaxi Wang. (2024). Examining AI Use in Educational Contexts: A Scoping Meta-Review and Bibliometric Analysis. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-024-00442-w

q2 · i? · review · r2

Comparative synthesis in the Discussion section contrasting PreK-12 and higher education curriculum design across the included reviews. The article distinguishes AI for education from education for AI and notes many AI literacy curricula were short termed, lacked an educational theoretical framework, and were implemented in informal settings.

"we identified a nuanced difference in curriculum design between PreK-12 education and higher education: The former is focused on AI literacy courses (e.g., Casal-Otero et al., 2023; Sanusi et al., 2023; Yue et al., 2022) and the latter on incorporation of AI in teaching methodologies (e.g., Blumenstein, 2020)"

Yao Fu 2024 (2)¶

Yao Fu, Zhenjie Weng, Jiaxi Wang. (2024). Examining AI Use in Educational Contexts: A Scoping Meta-Review and Bibliometric Analysis. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-024-00442-w

q2 · i? · review · r2

Comparative synthesis of AI's administrative impact across educational settings. Both settings used AI to predict student performance and provide support systems, but dropout anticipation reviews (e.g., Ifenthaler & Yau, 2020; Sghir et al., 2023) were concentrated in higher education.

"only higher education has a heavy focus on applying AI to anticipate student dropout rates given that universities cope with the challenge of high student dropout rates"
