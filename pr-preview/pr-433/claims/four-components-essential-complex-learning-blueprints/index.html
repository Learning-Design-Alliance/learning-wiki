van Merriënboer 2002¶

van Merriënboer, J.J.G., Clark, R.E. & de Croock, M.B.M. (2002). Blueprints for complex learning: The 4C/ID-model. ETR&D. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02504993

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical argument stated in the article's abstract: the model's basic claim enumerates the four components — "(a) learning tasks, (b) supportive information, (c) just-in-time (JIT) information, and (d) part-task practice" — as essential and interrelated. No empirical test is reported in the available text.

"The basic claim is that four interrelated components are essential in blueprints for complex learning: (a) learning tasks, (b) supportive information, (c) just-in-time (JIT) information, and (d) part-task practice."
