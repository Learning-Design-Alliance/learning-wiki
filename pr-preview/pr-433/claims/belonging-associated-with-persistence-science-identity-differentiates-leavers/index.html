Katherine Rainey 2018¶

Katherine Rainey, Melissa Dancy, Roslyn Mickelson, Elizabeth Stearns and Stephanie Moller. (2018). Race and gender differences in how sense of belonging influences decisions to major in STEM. International Journal of STEM Education 5:10. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-018-0115-6

Comparison of coded belonging reasons between majors and leavers in the interview dataset. The article notes its data "is correlational, so we cannot determine" whether lack of science identity motivates dropping out or results from it; no white male major or leaver reported lacking science identity.

"No major indicated lack of science identity as a reason for not belonging. In contrast, approximately one quarter of leavers did so."
