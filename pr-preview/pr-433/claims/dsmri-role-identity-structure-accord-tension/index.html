Conceptual analysis in the article's section on role identity structure. The article describes the structural facet as a nested, fractal-like hierarchy extending from momentary role identities to the person's broadest role identity, with element relations of accord or tension. Accord is described as "one of coherence, commonly, albeit not definitively, accompanied by posi - tive emotion."

"When elements are in accord, the agent’s experi - ence is one of coherence, commonly, albeit not definitively, accompanied by posi - tive emotion and by action that corroborates other actions"
