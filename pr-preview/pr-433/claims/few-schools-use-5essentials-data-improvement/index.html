Qualitative study based on interviews conducted throughout 2019 with district officials and school staff found "few schools use the 5Essentials Survey data in their improvement efforts," attributing this to absent district-wide strategy and supports. No effect size is printed.

"interviews with district officials and school staff showed that few schools use the 5Essentials Survey data in their improvement efforts, due to the absence of a coherent district-wide strategy and supports needed to use the data effectively."
