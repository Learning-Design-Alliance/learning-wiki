Bailey 2016¶

Bailey, T., Bashford, J., Boatman, A., Squires, J., Weiss, M., Doyle, W., Valentine, J. C., LaSota, R., Polanin, J. R., Spinney, E., Wilson, W., Yeide, M., & Young, S. H. (2016). Washington, DC: Institute of Education Sciences, What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/23

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Observational comparison of 1992 high school graduates' degree attainment eight years after high school, cited in the guide's introduction as background. The guide reports community college entrants' rates as 28% versus 36%. This is a descriptive correlational pattern, not an experimental finding.

"students enrolling in at least one developmental course earning a degree at a much lower rate (52%) than first-time entrants to 4-year institutions not enrolling in developmental courses (78%)"
