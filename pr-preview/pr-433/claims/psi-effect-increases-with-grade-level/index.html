Subgroup analysis of the meta-analysis by learner grade level (2nd–5th graders, 6th–10th graders, undergraduates, others). The article reports that "The pooled effect size estimate for younger students (2nd to 5th graders) was negative", with estimates increasing across age ranges; Table 4 prints g = −0.09 for 2nd–5th graders and g = 1.03 for postgraduates/professionals.
