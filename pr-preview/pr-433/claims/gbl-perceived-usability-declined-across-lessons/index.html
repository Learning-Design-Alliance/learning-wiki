Evidence¶

Yanjun Pan 2024¶

Yanjun Pan, Elizabeth L. Adams, Leanne R. Ketterlin-Geller, Eric C. Larson, Corey Clark. (2024). Enhancing middle school students' computational thinking competency through game-based learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10400-x

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

One-way repeated measures ANOVA (Greenhouse–Geisser corrected) on User Engagement Scale subscales across four time points (n = 19). Perceived Usability differed significantly across lessons, F(2.034, 36.621) = 3.34, p = .046, η2 = .16, with a post hoc change between Tutorial and Lesson 2 (p = .025); means fell from 3.8 to 2.9.

"but not Perceived Usability, F(2.034, 36.621) = 3.34, p = .046, η2 = .16. The follow-up Bonferroni-adjusted post hoc tests revealed that there was a significant change in Perceived Usability at Tutorial and Lesson 2 (p = .025)."

Yanjun Pan 2024 (2)¶

Yanjun Pan, Elizabeth L. Adams, Leanne R. Ketterlin-Geller, Eric C. Larson, Corey Clark. (2024). Enhancing middle school students' computational thinking competency through game-based learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-024-10400-x

q2 · i? · design · r2

Repeated measures ANOVAs on the other User Engagement Scale subscales found no significant differences across the four time points for Focused Attention (F(3, 54) = 0.48, p = .697), Aesthetics (p = .222), or Satisfaction (p = .216).

"mean Focused Attention did not differ statistically significantly across the four lessons (i.e., time points), F(3, 54) = 0.48, p = .697, η2 = .03."
