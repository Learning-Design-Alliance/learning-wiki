Wilcoxon rank-sum comparison of 26 adults with DD and 26 controls on the NADL Formal Test, reported in Table 1. The DD group mean was 67.21 (SD 4.11) versus 73.07 (SD 2.71) for controls, with "−0.665" effect size and p < 0.001 after Holm–Bonferroni correction.
