Evidence¶

Zijun Yin 2025¶

Zijun Yin, Bin Xuan, Chengchi Liu, Jingchao Yi, Xiaoyan Zheng & Mingming Zhang. (2025). The influence of task and interpersonal interdependence on cooperative behavior and its neural mechanisms. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00303-8

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Repeated measures 2 (task interdependence) × 2 (friend vs stranger dyads) ANOVA on key-pressing scores of 34 same-gender dyads (68 participants). The main effect of task interdependence was significant, "F (1, 32) = 4.864*, p = 0.035, ηp2 = 0.132"; the interpersonal effect and interaction were not significant (p = 0.519 and p = 0.842).

"The behavioral results showed a signiﬁcant main effect of task interdependences,F (1, 32) = 4.864*, p = 0.035, ηp2 = 0.132."
