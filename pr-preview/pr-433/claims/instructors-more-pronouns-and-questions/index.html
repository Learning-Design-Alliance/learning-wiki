Pronoun-density analysis of FOXGLOVE feedback comments (§4.2.2), counting first- and second-person pronouns per 100 words. The article reports "Humans average 1.26 first-person pro- nouns per 100 words, compared with only 0.03 for LLMs", suggesting instructor feedback is more conversational.
