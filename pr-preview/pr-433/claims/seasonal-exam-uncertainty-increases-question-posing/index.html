Evidence¶

Ali 2026¶

Ali, F., Yang, Y., & Poh, J.-H. (2026). Curious behaviors in times of uncertainty: dynamics of student question-posing in online educational discussion. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00440-8

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Negative binomial GLM on monthly question comment counts from the SGExams corpus (2019-2023), with R2 ranging from 0.35 to 0.79. The authors report "widespread statistically significant effects of either cosine or sine terms or both, across types of questions and topics" for seasonal phases.

"the cosine term increases open, non -possibility question type count for the Exam Preparation topic by 115%, suggesting that students tended to pose more open, non -possibility questions coinciding with the Oct - Nov period of exam-related uncertainty (p < 0.001)."
