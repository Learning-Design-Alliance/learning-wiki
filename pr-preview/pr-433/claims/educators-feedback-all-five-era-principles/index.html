Qualitative analysis of audio recordings and researcher observations from two educator sessions (three educators each, two districts), deductively coded with the ERA Framework. The article reports educators "engaged positively with Roversa" and offered technical suggestions; the abstract adds feedback covered all five ERA aspects.
