Evidence¶

Peter Kahn 2017¶

Peter Kahn, Lucy Everington, Kathleen Kelm, Iain Reid, Francine Watkins. (2017). Understanding student engagement in online learning environments: the role of reflexivity. Education Tech Research Dev, 65, 203-218. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-016-9484-z

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Qualitative multiple case study; findings based on in-depth Skype interviews with eight online Masters students selected from 22 participants across Public Health, Management and Computer Science. The study found students "exercised several characteristic modes of reﬂexivity concurrently."

"Students did not manifest dominant modes of reﬂexivity, but exercised several characteristic modes of reﬂexivity concurrently."
