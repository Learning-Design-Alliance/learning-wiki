Jiang 2014¶

Jiang, J. (2014). Teacher Evaluation in Practice: Year Two Teacher and Administrator Perceptions of REACH. University of Chicago Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/teacher-evaluation-practice-year-2-teacher-and-administrator-perceptions-reach

Teacher and administrator survey data from the year-two REACH brief. "Eighty-six percent of teachers" reported the observation process changed their teaching; almost all principals reported at least half their teachers made noticeable classroom improvements.

"Eighty-six percent of teachers agreed or strongly agreed that the observation process has changed their teaching. Principals also noted changes in teachers' practice. Over 80 percent said their teachers had changed their instruction to do better on REACH"
