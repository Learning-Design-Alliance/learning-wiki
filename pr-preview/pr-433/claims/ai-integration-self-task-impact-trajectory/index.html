Gretchen Geng · Junyu Chen 2026¶

Gretchen Geng · Junyu Chen. (2026). An empirical longitudinal study of AI integration in transforming teachers' pedagogical content knowledge: insights from language educators in rural China. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10602-5

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Thematic analysis of 18 weeks of recorded weekly meetings with eight rural language teachers, coded with a hybrid deductive approach using the Concern-Based Adoption Model. The analysis found a "developmental trajectory from self to task and impact concerns".

"Moreover, across the 18-week period, teachers' experiences with AI integration followed a developmental trajectory from self to task and impact concerns, while drawing on their PCK."
