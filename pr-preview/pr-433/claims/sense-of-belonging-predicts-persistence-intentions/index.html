Evidence¶

Hausmann 2007¶

Hausmann, L.R.M., Schofield, J.W. & Woods, R.L. (2007). Sense of Belonging as a Predictor of Intentions to Persist Among African American and White First-Year College Students. Research in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11162-007-9052-9

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Individual growth curve modeling of three survey waves across the first academic year found that "sense of belonging was found to predict intentions to persist" even with background variables and other persistence predictors controlled. No effect size is printed in the available text.
