Evidence¶

Wammes J.D. 2019¶

Wammes J.D., Ralph B.C.W., Mills C., Bosch N., Duncan T.L. & Smilek D. (2019). Disengagement during lectures: Media multitasking and mind wandering in university classrooms. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.12.007

q3 · i1 · associational · r1

Repeated-measures ANOVA on probe responses from 163 participants in a live Physiological Psychology course (Study 1) showed a significant increase in media multitasking across lecture quarters, with a significant linear trend ("ηp2 =.03").

"A repeated-measures ANOVA revealed a significant main effect of Lecture Quarter on the proportion of media multitasking responses, F(2.69, 432.52) = 3.10, MSE = .05, p = .031, ηp2 =.02, with a significant linear trend, F(1, 161) = 4.46, MSE = .08, p = .036, ηp2 =.03."

Wammes J.D. 2019 (2)¶

Wammes J.D., Ralph B.C.W., Mills C., Bosch N., Duncan T.L. & Smilek D. (2019). Disengagement during lectures: Media multitasking and mind wandering in university classrooms. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.12.007

q3 · i3 · associational · r1

In Study 2 (70 participants, laptop-app probes), a repeated-measures ANOVA showed media multitasking reports rose significantly across lecture quarters, with a large linear trend ("ηp2 =.29").

"there was a significant main effect of Lecture Quarter on the proportion of media multitasking reports, F(2.568, 177.205) = 10.57, MSE = .01, p < .001, ηp2 =.13, with a significant linear trend, F(1, 69) = 28.52, MSE = .01, p < .001, ηp2 =.29."
