Evidence¶

Cancela J 2019¶

Cancela J, Burgo H, Sande E. (2019). Physical fitness and executive functions in adolescents: cross-sectional associations with academic achievement. J. Phys. Ther. Sci. 31: 556–562. https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jpts/31/7/31_17-478/_article

q3 · i3 · associational · r2

Cross-sectional partial correlation analysis of 713 adolescents, adjusted for age, gender and school level, relating nine physical fitness tests to school-record academic achievement. The study reports "aerobic endurance (r=0.49, p<0.001)" as the strongest correlate and balance as non-significant.

"Resistance strength (r=0.41, p<0.001), explosive strength (r=0.32, p<0.05), agility and speed (r= −0.30, p<0.05, r=0.33, p<0.001), aerobic endurance (r=0.49, p<0.001, r=0.49, p<0.001) and flexibility (r=0.37, p<0.001, r=0.35, p<0.001) have a positive influence on students’ AA, while balance (r=0.21; p>0.05) does not."
