Pablo Flores Romero 2025¶

Pablo Flores Romero, Kin Nok Nicholas Fung, Guang Rong & Benjamin Ultan Cowley. (2025). Structured human-LLM interaction design reveals exploration and exploitation dynamics in higher education content generation. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00332-3

q2 · i2 · associational · r1

Topic modeling (BERTopic) of 22 essays from the structured GPT interaction study. Kendall's tau showed a significant negative correlation with printed effect size τ = −0.47 (p = 0.01): "a significant negative correlation between the frequency of GPT keywords and the topic diversity in the essays of the participants".
