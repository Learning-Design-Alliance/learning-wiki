q1 · i? · review · r2

Narrative review reporting Trulson's (1986) study of adolescent juvenile delinquents assigned to traditional tae kwon do (emphasizing character development and self-control) versus modern martial arts (competitive sport, physical aspect only); no effect sizes are printed.

"Compared with the adolescents who were trained in modern martial arts, those who were trained in traditional tae kwon do showed less aggression and anxiety and improved social ability and self-esteem."
