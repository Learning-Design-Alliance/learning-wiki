Lotan 2019¶

Lotan, R. A., & Burns, D. (2019). The Instructional Leadership Corps: Teachers leading sustainable professional learning in their communities. Palo Alto, CA: Learning Policy Institute. https://learningpolicyinstitute.org/product/instructional-leadership-corps-teachers-leading

q2 · i? · design · r2

Post-workshop survey evidence from thousands of participants across the ILC project; ratings were "well above 4 on a 5-point scale" and participants also called sessions very or extremely helpful. Self-report data, not independent observation of practice.

"Post-workshop surveys of thousands of participants show that large majorities of educators consistently report that their ILC experiences have influenced their curriculum, instructional strategies, assessments, student engagement, and student learning to a “great extent”"
