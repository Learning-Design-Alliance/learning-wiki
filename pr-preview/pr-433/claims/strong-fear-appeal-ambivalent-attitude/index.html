Janis 1953¶

Janis, Irving L., and Feshbach, Seymour. (1953). Effects of Fear-Arousing Communications. Journal of Abnormal and Social Psychology 48: 78-92. doi:10.1037/h0060732

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Experimental comparison among the three appeal-intensity groups in the dental hygiene study; the strong-appeal group's attitude is reported as "more mixed or ambivalent" than the milder groups, combining positive appraisal with dislike and complaints.

"The Strong Appeal resulted in a more mixed or ambivalent attitude toward the communication, this group appraising it as more interesting and of high quality, yet showing the greatest amount of subjective dislike and making more complaints about the content."
