Backfisch 2021¶

Backfisch, I., Lachner, A., Stürmer, K., & Scheiter, K. (2021). Variability of teachers’ technology integration in the classroom: A matter of utility!. Computers & Education, 166, 104159. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2021.104159

q1 · i? · associational · r1

Qualitative content analysis of three prototypical teachers' lesson documentations found reciprocal patterns. The article reports that "teachers' motivation and technology-enhanced teaching quality may be reciprocally dependent on each other".

"our qualitative analysis suggested that teachers' motivation and technology-enhanced teaching quality may be reciprocally dependent on each other. Therefore, it can be concluded that teacher motivation should not only be regarded as a source but also as the result of teachers' actions in the classroom."
