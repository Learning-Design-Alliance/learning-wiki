Evidence¶

Melody Wilson & Jamaal Sharif Matthews 2024¶

Melody Wilson & Jamaal Sharif Matthews. (2024). Black adolescents' motivation to resist the false dichotomy between mathematics achievement and racial identity. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00219-9

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Longitudinal latent profile analysis of survey data from Black adolescents in five secondary schools, measured at two time points one year apart, in which "a three- proﬁle solution had optimal ﬁt across T1 and T2". Profiles were labeled Mathematics Devalued/Ambivalent, Moderately Math Attained, and Resistors.
