Vermunt et al. (2014) meta-analysis compared factor analyses of ILS scores across eight samples from Hong Kong, Indonesia, Sri Lanka, Spain, Venezuela, Columbia, Mexico and the Netherlands; three context-specific patterns (application-directed, passive-idealistic, passive-motivational) also appeared.

"In most studies, a meaning-directed, a reproduction-directed, and an undirected pattern emerged, although these looked somewhat differently in different countries"
