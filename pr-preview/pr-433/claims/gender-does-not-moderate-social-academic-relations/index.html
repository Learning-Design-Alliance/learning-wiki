Evidence¶

Arnold 2012¶

Arnold, D. H., Kupersmidt, J. B., Voegler-Lee, M. E., & Marshall, N. (2012). The Association between Preschool Children's Social Functioning and Their Emergent Academic Skills. Early Childhood Research Quarterly. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecresq.2011.12.009

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Nine HLM interaction analyses regressing each academic outcome onto one social predictor, gender, and their interaction in 467 preschoolers. The article reports "contrary to predictions, gender did not significantly moderate any of these relationships (all interaction ps > .05)."
