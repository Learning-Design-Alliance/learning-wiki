Evidence¶

Enrico Gandolfi · Richard E. Ferdig 2025¶

Enrico Gandolfi · Richard E. Ferdig. (2025). Exploring the relationship between motivation and augmented reality presence using the augmented reality presence scale (ARPS). Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10446-5

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Rasch Rating Scale Model analysis (WINSTEPS) of ARPS responses from 90 undergraduates after an AR tour. The article reports "sufficient item reliability (0.96)" and person reliability 0.94 with separation 3.96, supporting initial validity evidence.

"The initial modeling of the ARPS found sufficient item reliability (0.96) and an item separation of index of 5.20, suggesting that the instrument was able to distinguish between items with lower and higher ratings"
