Evidence¶

Tiruneh 2016¶

Tiruneh, D. T., Weldeslassie, A. G., Kassa, A., Tefera, Z., De Cock, M., & Elen, J. (2016). Systematic design of a learning environment for domain-specific and domain-general critical thinking skills. Education Tech Research Dev, 64, 481–505. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-015-9417-2

q3 · i3 · causal · r1

Pearson correlation across both groups pooled showed "the CTEM scores signiﬁcantly correlated with the posttest HCTA scores", r = .38. This is a correlational finding, not causal.

"Moreover, the CTEM scores signiﬁcantly correlated with the posttest HCTA scores ( r = .38, p = .01)."

Tiruneh 2016 (2)¶

Tiruneh, D. T., Weldeslassie, A. G., Kassa, A., Tefera, Z., De Cock, M., & Elen, J. (2016). Systematic design of a learning environment for domain-specific and domain-general critical thinking skills. Education Tech Research Dev, 64, 481–505. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-015-9417-2

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Linear regression showed "CTEM performance was a significant predictor, accounting for 14.5% of the variance in posttest HCTA scores"; the article prints R² = .145 but no standardized effect size for this finding.

"A linear regression analysis also revealed that the CTEM test explained a significant proportion of the variance on posttest HCTA performance, F(1, 87) = 14.7, p = .05, R2 = .145."
