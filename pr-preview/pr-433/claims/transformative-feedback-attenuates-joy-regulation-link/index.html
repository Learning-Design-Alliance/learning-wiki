q3 · i? · associational · r2

Interaction analyses in the linear mixed-effects models (H2) on eight weeks of data from 194 students, with feedback dummies coded against no feedback. Only the joy × transformative feedback interaction reached significance (p = .029 in Table 4); H2 was not supported.

"the interaction between joy and transformative feedback was significant, albeit unexpectedly indicating that the positive association between joy and regulation was attenuated when students interacted with transformative feedback."
