Re-analysis of a published coefficient from Kornhauser's study of parental economic standing (telephone possession) and children's intelligence, cited as background. The article states "The size of the coefficient is materially affected by leaving out the normal group".

"Had he included all normal cases with the advanced cases, he would haye secured a much lower coefficient, Q = + .30 for the four (now different) classes"
