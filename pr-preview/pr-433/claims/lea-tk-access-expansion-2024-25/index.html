Wang 2026¶

Wang, V. (2026). California's universal prekindergarten implementation, 2024–25. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/540.887

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive results from the 2024–25 UPK grant survey (Table 2), reporting site-level TK availability among multi-site LEAs. The report notes "70% of which offered TK across all elementary sites—an increase from 62% in 2023–24."

"Among all 1,414 LEAs offering TK in 2024–25, 578 operated more than one elementary site, 70% of which offered TK across all elementary sites—an increase from 62% in 2023–24"

Wang 2026 (2)¶

Wang, V. (2026). California's universal prekindergarten implementation, 2024–25. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/540.887

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Survey results on TK day length (Figure 3, N = 1,414 LEAs). The report also states that "88% of LEAs offered at least one program to extend the TK instructional day," primarily expanded learning programs on LEA sites (82%).

"Eighty-five percent of LEAs offered a full-day TK (more than 4 hours) option in 2024–25, similar to the 84% who did so in 2023–24."
