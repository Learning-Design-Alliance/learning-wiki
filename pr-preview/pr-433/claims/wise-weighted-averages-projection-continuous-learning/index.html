Wise "recommended weighted averages, where the weights were based on projection models that predicted summative scores"; per Dadey et al. "Based on the results of a simulation study under the Continuous Learning Model, Wise (2011) recommended weighted averages, where the weights were based on projection models that predicted summative scores. He reported that the weights were proportional to instructional time."
