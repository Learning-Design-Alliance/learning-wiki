Evidence¶

Vermunt 2004¶

Vermunt, J. D. & Vermetten, Y. J. (2004). Patterns in Student Learning: Relationships Between Learning Strategies, Conceptions of Learning, and Learning Orientations. Educational Psychology Review. https://dspacelibrary.uu.nl/handle/1874/11958

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative review of factor-analytic studies: the review reports that studies with first-year students, including Busato et al. (1998) and Schouwenburg (1996), "repeatedly conﬁrmed" the four-pattern structure found in open university (N = 654) and regular university (N = 795) samples. No effect sizes are printed for this structural finding.

"Recent research with ﬁrst-year students in higher education has repeatedly conﬁrmed the internal structure of learning styles as shown in the patterns of factor loadings of the ILS scales."
