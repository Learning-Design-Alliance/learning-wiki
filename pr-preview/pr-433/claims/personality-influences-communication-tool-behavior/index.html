Evidence¶

Wen Wu 2019¶

Wen Wu, Li Chen, Qingchang Yang, You Li. (2019). Inferring Students' Personality from Their Communication Behavior in Web-based Learning Systems. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-018-00173-9

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Multiple linear regression with 164 students' TIPI personality scores as predictors and logged communication behaviors as dependent variables, using Bonferroni-type adjustment (p<0.01). The article reports "all of the five personality traits significantly affect students' activities".

"Looking at activity features, the results show that all of the five personality traits significantly affect students' activities carried out both in and after class."

Wen Wu 2019 (2)¶

Wen Wu, Li Chen, Qingchang Yang, You Li. (2019). Inferring Students' Personality from Their Communication Behavior in Web-based Learning Systems. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-018-00173-9

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Summary of the regression analysis over 120 features from 164 students: 32 significant features, comprising 22 content features, 9 activity features, and gender. No effect sizes printed, so impact is null.

"In summary, 32 (out of 120) features are empirically found to be signifi- cantly influenced by students’ personality."
