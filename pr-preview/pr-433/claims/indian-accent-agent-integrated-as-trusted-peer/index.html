q2 · i? · causal · r1

Interaction Analysis case study of Group 6 in the Indian-accent condition. After Phoenix explained its fishing-kit ranking, participants began direct solicitation ("Phoenix, what do you think of the mosquito netting"), and the article reports this "enabled Phoenix to establish a baseline level of trust".

"During the icebreaker, Phoenix was naturally in- tegrated into sustained, multi-party discussion alongside humans, using the ac- tivity as intended rather than simply testing the agent."
