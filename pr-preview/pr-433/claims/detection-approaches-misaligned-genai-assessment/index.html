Theoretical argument in the section on why detection-oriented approaches are misaligned. The paper offers no empirical data for this claim; it reasons from the nature of generative text and cites integrity scholarship. It also notes detection tools may serve as one supplementary layer within multi-layer governance.

"Generative text is probabilistic, non-deterministic, and shaped through iterative human prompting and revision, rendering definitive attribution to either human or machine sources unreliable"
