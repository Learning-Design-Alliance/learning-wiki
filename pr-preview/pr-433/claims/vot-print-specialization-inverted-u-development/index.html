Evidence¶

Chyl 2021¶

Chyl, K., Fraga-González, G., Brem, S. & Jednoróg, K. (2021). Brain dynamics of (a)typical reading development—a review of longitudinal studies. npj Science of Learning 6:4. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00081-5

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing a four-time-point fMRI study of elementary school children, that "cortical sensitivity to print in the left vOT (but not in V1 or the right vOT) was correlated with age and, more importantly, with the rate of improvement in reading".

Chyl 2021 (2)¶

Chyl, K., Fraga-González, G., Brem, S. & Jednoróg, K. (2021). Brain dynamics of (a)typical reading development—a review of longitudinal studies. npj Science of Learning 6:4. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-00081-5

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review synthesizes longitudinal ERP findings of N1 print sensitivity emerging between pre-reading and reading stages and leveling off with practice, concluding "the evidence from visual electrophysiological responses suggest an inverted U-shape development of visual specialization to print with reading expertise".
