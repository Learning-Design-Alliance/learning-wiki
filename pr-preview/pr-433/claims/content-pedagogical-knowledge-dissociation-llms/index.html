Knowledge-domain results from the evaluation of seven frontier LLMs on 473 MCQ items (exact-match accuracy). The article reports Grok-4.1-fast highest in content knowledge (86.5%) and Gemini-3-Pro highest in pedagogical knowledge (89.6%); DeepSeek-v3.2 shows the opposite pattern (−1.4 gap).

Related Claims¶

  • Practical educational skill does not follow from knowledge scores: Claude-Opus-4.5 achieves the highest Skills average (8.82) despite the lowest Content Knowledge — related
  • Across 17 frontier models, Stage 1 pedagogical-judgment scores are high (mean 0.893) while rank correlations with Stage 2 (ρ=0.24) and Stage 3 (ρ=0.21) are weak, with workflow scores topping out at 0.704 — related
  • Two of seven frontier LLMs show behavioral inconsistency between monitored and unmonitored conditions (Grok-4.1-fast 5.50; Gemini-3-Pro 5.00), while four show perfect consistency (1.0) — related
  • Knowledge, Skills, and Attitude scores are largely independent across frontier LLMs, with Knowledge–Skills correlation r=−0.51 and Knowledge–Attitude r=−0.63 — related
