Evidence¶

Anabela Caetano Santos 2023¶

Anabela Caetano Santos, Celeste Simões, Márcia H.S. Melo, Margarida F. Santos, Iara Freitas, Cátia Branquinho, Carmel Cefai, Patrícia Arriaga. (2023). A systematic review of the association between social and emotional competencies and student engagement in youth. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2023.100535

q3 · i? · review · r3

The review's own synthesis of 91 included studies (92879 youth students, ages 10–25, published 2004–2020) found that "SECs are positively associated with SE and negatively associated with disengagement". No pooled effect size was reported.

"Overall, most studies showed that SECs are positively associated with SE and negatively associated with disengagement, with similar results for middle, high school and university students from different backgrounds"
