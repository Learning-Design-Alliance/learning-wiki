Evidence¶

McKenzie 2025¶

McKenzie, R. & Allensworth, E. (2025). Variability in the Grades Teachers Give: Teacher Grade Value-Add, Mismatch, and Long-Term Effects. American Educational Research Journal. https://doi.org/10.3102/00028312251345094

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Regressions of residualized long-term outcomes on jackknife teacher persistent effects, pooling four subjects, in the CPS long-term outcome sub-sample. All outcomes show "positive and significant coefficients associated with teachers' persistent effects."

"Having one ninth-grade teacher one standard deviation above the mean on persistent effects is associated with taking a slightly higher number of AP courses in eleventh and twelfth grade (an additional 0.129 courses), eleventh grade ACT scores that are 0.172 points higher, a graduating core GPA that is 0.140 points higher, and a 1.4 percentage point increase in the probability of graduating."
