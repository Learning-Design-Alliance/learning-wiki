Vella-Brodrick 2024¶

q2 · i? · associational · r2

The review reports this longitudinal correlational study of 841 Finnish employees: nature-based physical activity predicted vitality but "no significant effects were observed for creativity at work". No effect size is printed.

"Korpela et al.'s (2017) longitudinal study on 841 Finnish employees found that frequent physical activity in natural environments predicted significantly greater vitality; however, no significant effects were observed for creativity at work."
