Evidence¶

Paul R. Pintrich 2000¶

Paul R. Pintrich. (2000). The Role of Goal Orientation in Self-Regulated Learning. Handbook of Self-Regulation, Academic Press. https://doi.org/10.1016/b978-012109890-2/50043-3

q2 · i? · review · r2

Definitional and synthetic review statement contrasting two motivational regulation strategies. Defensive pessimism is described as harnessing anxiety about doing poorly to increase effort and perform better; self-handicapping works in the opposite direction through reduced effort.

"Self-handicapping, in contrast to defensive pessimism, involves the decrease of effort (little or no studying) or procrastination (only cramming for an exam, writing a paper at the very end of the deadline) to protect self-worth by attributing the likely poor outcome to low effort, not low ability"
