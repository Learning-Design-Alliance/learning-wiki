Subject-specific re-estimation of Model 3a on the same 23,471-student sample showed the between-school slope SD was "0.035 for maths, 0.027 for reading, and 0.004 for English", indicating the interaction is subject-specific.

"Results indicate that the interaction involves maths and reading more than English. The standard deviation of slopes for the EA-PGI effect between schools was 0.035 for maths, 0.027 for reading, and 0.004 for English."

Related Claims¶

  • Students' EA-PGI interacts with schools such that school differences in achievement are wider among students with lower EA-PGI (compensation, not Scarr-Rowe suppression) — related
  • Between-school variation in EA-PGI effects is substantial: the average EA-PGI effect is 0.22 with slope SD 0.034, and variance explained by EA-PGI ranges from ~2% in the weakest-effect schools to ~8% in the strongest — related
  • Neighbourhood, district, and municipality variation contributes little to achievement (under 2% of variance collectively) and does not interact with student EA-PGI — related
  • Five measured school sociodemographic characteristics do not explain the EA-PGI-by-school interaction — related
