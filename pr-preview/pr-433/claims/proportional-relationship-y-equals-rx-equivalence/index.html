q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

An analytical derivation in the paper's 'Tables and equations' section scales a generic table row (a, b) to obtain y = (b/a)x, concluding 'the variables satisfy the equation' y = rx and stating the two-way equivalence between scaling in tandem and the equation form.

"The data arising from any proportional relationship satisfies an equation of the form y = rx for some fixed constant r. And, conversely, the data that arises from any given equation of the form y = rx is in a proportional relationship."
