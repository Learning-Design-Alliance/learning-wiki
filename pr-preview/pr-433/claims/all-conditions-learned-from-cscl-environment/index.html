Evidence¶

Elise H. Eshuis 2019¶

Elise H. Eshuis, Judith ter Vrugte, Anjo Anjewierden, Lars Bollen, Jakob Sikken, Ton de Jong. (2019). Improving the quality of vocational students' collaboration and knowledge acquisition through instruction and joint reflection. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning (2019) 14:53–76. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-019-09296-0

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Paired-samples t-tests comparing pre- and posttest domain knowledge (12 open-ended questions, max 25 points, Cronbach's alpha .87/.85) per condition in the within-subject pretest-intervention-posttest design. All three conditions showed "significantly higher" posttest scores.

"posttest scores were significantly higher than pretest scores in all three conditions (instruction with tool condition: t(24) = 5.93, p <. 0 0 1 ,d = 1.19; instruction only condition: t(26) = 5.74, p < .001, d = 1.10; control condition: t(34) = 3.38, p =. 0 0 2 ,d = 0.57)"
