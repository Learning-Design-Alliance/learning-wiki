Evidence¶

Hanley Chiang 2016¶

Hanley Chiang, Stephen Lipscomb, Brian Gill. (2016). Is School Value Added Indicative of Principal Quality? Education Finance and Policy, vol. 11, no. 3. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/is-school-value-added-indicative-of-principal-quality doi:10.1162/edfp_a_00184

q1 · i? · associational · r?

Theoretical argument motivating the study: because school value added aggregates all school-specific influences on achievement growth, it may capture factors outside principals' control rather than principal effectiveness alone. The article offers this as the conceptual rationale for isolating principals' effects empirically.

"In theory, school value added can reflect not only principals' effectiveness but also other school-specific influences on student achievement growth that are outside of principals' control."
