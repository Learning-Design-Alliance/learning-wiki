Evidence¶

Li Shijin & Liu Junjian 2026¶

Li Shijin & Liu Junjian. (2026). Mapping the Distribution and Depth of Metacognitive Processes in Generative AI-Assisted Learning: Evidence from Interaction Logs and Concurrent Think-Aloud Protocols. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16081365

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Descriptive contrast across 166 retained planning instances (97 interaction-log, 69 think-aloud). The article suggests this may reflect verbalized intentions that were not enacted, but states the available data do not identify the mechanism.

"Higher-depth evidence (strategic plus reflective) represented 49.5% of interaction-log instances and 59.4% of think-aloud instances, or 9.9 percentage points higher in the think-aloud record."
