q3 · i? · associational · r3

Descriptive analysis of CPS administrative attendance records for roughly 26,000 preschool students per year, 2008-09 through 2011-12. The report states "45 percent of three-year-olds and 36 percent of four-year-olds in CPS were chronically absent," with chronic absence defined as missing 10 percent or more of enrolled days.

"In 2011-12, 45 percent of three-year-olds and 36 percent of four-year-olds in CPS were chronically absent (see Figure 3 ). Of these students, at least one-third missed 20 percent or more of school."
