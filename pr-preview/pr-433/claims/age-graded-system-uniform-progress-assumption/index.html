David Dockterman 2018¶

David Dockterman. (2018). Insights from 200 + years of personalized learning. npj Science of Learning 3:15. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0033-x

q2 · i? · review · r2

Historical analysis citing Cubberley's 1916 figures: the system targeted 70.4% progressing at graded level, with the rest split between lagging and accelerating, "a goal that often was not met." Tyack and Tobin's "batch processing" critique and Shearer's 1898 reforms (ability tracking, flexible reclassification) are cited as responses.

"Cubberley wrote in 1916 that 70.4% of students should be progressing according to graded level expectations. About half the remaining students would be lagging (called "retardation" in those days) and half would be accelerating."
