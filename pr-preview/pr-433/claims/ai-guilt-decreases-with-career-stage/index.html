Career-stage ANOVA in the 109-respondent survey revealed "significant differences in guilt levels across career stages" with a small-to-medium effect (η² = 0.078). Post-hoc Tukey HSD showed the primary difference between early-career and senior academics (mean difference = 0.68, p = .037).
