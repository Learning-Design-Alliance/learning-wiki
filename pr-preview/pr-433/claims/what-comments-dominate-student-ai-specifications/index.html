Aggregate analysis of LLM-classified comments from a four-year Copilot activity (1,161 students, 52,172 analyzed comment instances). The article reports "comments were dominated byWhatcomments (77.0%)" with How at 21.0% and Why at 0.4%; no effect size is printed.

"Overall, comments were dominated byWhatcomments (77.0%), followed byHowcomments (21.0%), whileWhycomments were rare (0.4%)."
