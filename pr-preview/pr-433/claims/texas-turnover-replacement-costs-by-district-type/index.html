Bland 2023¶

Cost estimates cited in the Teacher Turnover section of the report. The report states turnover is as costly and disruptive to districts as attrition, even when teachers remain in the profession, and that statewide turnover on standard certificates was 17% in 2021–22.

"Estimates exceed $10,800 in a rural district, $13,200 in a suburban district, and $25,200 in an urban district to replace each teacher who departs."
