Evidence¶

Daniel M. Oppenheimer 2025¶

Daniel M. Oppenheimer, Trent N. Cash, Allison E. Connell Pensky. (2025). You've Got AI Friend in Me: LLMs as Collaborative Learning Partners. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00521-6

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Self-reported ratings on −5 to 5 bi-valenced Likert scales after the first assignment: useful (x̄ = 2.32), challenging (x̄ = 1.42), engaging (x̄ = 2.51), enjoyable (x̄ = 2.03). All significantly above the midpoint of 0; no effect size printed.

"one-sample t-tests comparing these numbers to the neutral midpoint of 0 were run and revealed that they were all highly significant (ts(79) > 7.84, ps < 0.001)."

Daniel M. Oppenheimer 2025 (2)¶

Daniel M. Oppenheimer, Trent N. Cash, Allison E. Connell Pensky. (2025). You've Got AI Friend in Me: LLMs as Collaborative Learning Partners. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00521-6

q2 · i? · causal · r1

After the fifth assignment, ratings remained positive: useful (x̄ = 2.48), challenging (x̄ = 0.63), engaging (x̄ = 2.22), enjoyable (x̄ = 1.81). The article reports "None of these metrics significantly changed from the first to fifth iteration of the assignment."

"The one-sample t-test comparing these numbers to the neutral midpoint of 0 revealed that these were also highly significant (ts(96) > 3.06, ps < 0.002)."
