q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of English learners taking Star screening assessments in both English and Spanish, comparing through-year patterns by language. The report prints that in Spanish "fewer students (11%) improved beyond the significant risk level"; no effect size is printed.

"In English, about 21 percent of students who performed above the significant risk threshold at the beginning of the year were still there by the end of the year, and another 19 percent improved to that level. In Spanish, fewer students (11%) improved beyond the significant risk level."
