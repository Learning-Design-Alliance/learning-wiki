Evidence¶

David Mann 2021¶

David Mann, Kathleen Feeney, Todd Honeycutt, Marlena Luhr. (2021). Way2Work Maryland Demonstration: Impacts 24 Months After Enrollment. Mathematica. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/way2work-maryland-demonstration-impacts-24-months-after-enrollment

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Authors' overall interpretation of the 24-month RCT findings. Implementation appeared strong, but intended effects were absent; the report lists possible reasons including "low statistical power for the evaluation" and "delayed positive effects."

"Although the vocational rehabilitation agency and the University of Maryland appear to have implemented the program well, Way2Work Maryland did not generate the intended effects. Possible reasons include the model not leading to intended outcomes, strong existing services for youth transition, low statistical power for the evaluation, delayed positive effects, and a lack of focus on the population of youth who could most benefit from the program."
