Evidence¶

Wickliffe C. Abraham 2019¶

Wickliffe C. Abraham, Owen D. Jones and David L. Glanzman. (2019). Is plasticity of synapses the mechanism of long-term memory storage? npj Science of Learning 4:9. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0048-y

q2 · i? · review · r3

Narrative review summarizing cited mammalian and invertebrate studies. The review reports extensive DNA methylation changes for hippocampal-dependent fear conditioning, with some methylation patterns occurring weeks after training and apparently associated with long-term memory maintenance.

"These studies have shown that inhibitors of DNA methyltransferase (DNMT) block the formation and/or consolidation of memory"
