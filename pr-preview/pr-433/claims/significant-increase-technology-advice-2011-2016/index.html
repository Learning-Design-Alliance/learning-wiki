Evidence¶

McGarr 2020¶

McGarr, O., & Gallchóir, C. Ó. (2020). Examining supervising field instructors' reporting and assessment of technology use by pre-service teachers on school placement. Computers & Education, 146, 103753. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2019.103753

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Chi-square test for independence comparing the proportion of reports offering technology-related advice in the final section, across the 2011 (7%) and 2016 (14%) cohorts. The article reports "chi-square = 4.204, p < .040328" as statistically significant.

"a statistically significant difference in the two cohorts was identified suggesting that advice in relation to using technology had increased in the 5 years albeit from a low initial base (chi-square = 4.204, p < .040328)"
