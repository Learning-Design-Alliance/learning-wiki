Evidence¶

Long 2024¶

Long, Y., Luo, H. & Zhang, Y. (2024). Evaluating large language models in analysing classroom dialogue. npj Science of Learning 9:60. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00273-3

q2 · i? · design · r2

Timed comparison of a single 41 min 29 s math lesson with 82 dialogue turns: an experienced researcher coded manually in ~2.5 h, while ChatGPT processed ten-turn increments in 5 min, a "time-saving factor of ~30 times". The article notes the comparison used an experienced researcher.

"Therefore, compared to manual coding, ChatGPT afforded a time-saving factor of ~30 times (150 min for manual coding versus 5 min for ChatGPT, see Fig. 1)."
