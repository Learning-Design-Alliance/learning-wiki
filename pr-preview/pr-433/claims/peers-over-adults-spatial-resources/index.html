Evidence¶

Ramey 2019¶

Ramey, K. E., Stevens, R., & Uttal, D. H. (2019). In-FUSE-ing STEAM Learning with Spatial Reasoning: Distributed Spatial Sensemaking in School-based Making Activities. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000422

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Categorical coding of resource use during FUSE activities found "other students (44 percent of total instances of resource use) and adults (9 percent)". Percentages are descriptive; no test is reported.

"Social resources included other students (44 percent of total instances of resource use) and adults (9 percent). Material resources included help videos (10 percent), written instructions (7 percent), and diagrams from the FUSE website (3 percent), tinkering with physical or digital materials (28 percent), and sketching or working from sketches (1 percent)."
