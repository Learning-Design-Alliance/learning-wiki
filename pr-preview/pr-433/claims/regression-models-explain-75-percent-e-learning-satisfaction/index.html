Evidence¶

Francisco González-Gómez 2011¶

Francisco González-Gómez, Jorge Guardiola, Óscar Martín Rodríguez, Miguel Ángel Montero Alonso (University of Granada, Spain). (2011). Gender differences in e-learning satisfaction. FEG Working Paper Series. http://www.ugr.es/~biblio/biblioteca_ugr/bibliotecas_centros/economicas/index.html

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Ordinary Least Squares regressions on responses from 409 male and 776 female students (Table 3, R squared 0.73 and 0.76) show that "the variables introduced explain approximately 75 percent of the variability in students’ global valuation". The remaining unexplained variability is attributed by the authors to omitted variables.

"The variables introduced explain approximately 75 percent of the variability in students’ global valuation, thereby implying that the models are a good fit."
