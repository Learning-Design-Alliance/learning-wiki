Evidence¶

Benjamin Lira 2025¶

Benjamin Lira, Todd Rogers, Daniel G. Goldstein, Lyle Ungar, and Angela L. Duckworth. (2025). Coach not crutch: Evidence that AI can improve writing skill despite reducing effort. https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02880 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2502.02880

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Pre-registered forecasting study (N=150) in which participants saw screenshots of the Study 2 design and rank-ordered conditions by expected learning. "nearly twice as many forecasters (64.7%) ranked practicing alone above practicing writing with access to an AI tool"; this prediction contradicted the actual results.

"nearly twice as many forecasters (64.7%) ranked practicing alone above practicing writing with access to an AI tool as the converse (35.3%,χ2 (1) = 12.9,p< .001)"
