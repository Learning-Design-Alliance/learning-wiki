Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Humans can readily abandon operant win-stay/lose-shift outcome-action associations when a loss-maximization goal requires the reversed strategies — related
  • Behavioural consistency shows a double dissociation: actions following wins are more consistent during win maximization, and actions following losses are more consistent during loss maximization — related
  • In loss maximization, the pattern reverses: performance is more consistent following losses than wins — related
  • Win-stay/lose-shift associations are not privileged: consistency sometimes favours win-shift, and reaction times show no outcome-by-action interaction — related
  • Win-calmness appears only against exploitable opponents; exploiting opponents instead reproduce the reversed pattern — related
  • Repetitive opponents are exploited more successfully than alternating opponents — related
