Ruiz 2025¶

Ruiz, P., Liberman, B., Karim, S., Dallas, D., Zachariah, I., Peters Hinton, V., Chillmon, C., & Gonzales, L. (2025). Implementing AI literacy across learning environments: A series of briefs. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/268

q2 · i? · review · r?

The briefs synthesize survey and policy data to characterize institutional readiness. They report that 43% of U.S. districts and over 30% of postsecondary institutions still lack generative AI guidelines, "creating a critical gap between learner behavior and educational policy." No effect sizes are printed.

"In contrast, institutional readiness is slower, with 43% of U.S. districts, over 30% of postsecondary institutions, and many workplaces still lacking guidelines for generative AI use, creating a critical gap between learner behavior and educational policy."
