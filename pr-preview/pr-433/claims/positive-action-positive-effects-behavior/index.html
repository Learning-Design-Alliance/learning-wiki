WWC Intervention Report: Positive Action 2007¶

WWC Intervention Report: Positive Action. (2007). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://www.positiveaction.net

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r?

WWC aggregation of behavior-domain findings from one randomized controlled trial (Flay et al., 2006) and one quasi-experimental study (Flay & Allred, 2003), covering 56 schools and over 2,660 students. The printed "Domain average8 across studies 0.51" is an average effect size of 0.51 (a medium effect), yielding an improvement index of +19 percentile points, and the WWC rated the domain as having positive effects.
