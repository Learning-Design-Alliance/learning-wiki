Naihobe Gonzalez 2017¶

Working Paper 52, Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/how-learning-about-ones-ability-affects-educational-investments-evidence-from-the-advanced-placement

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Null result from the regression discontinuity analysis for students not surveyed in the study: they "did not respond to the signal." The article reports no effect size or test statistic for this group; equivalence was not tested.

"However, students who were not surveyed for the study did not respond to the signal. The results suggest that ability signals can be a cost-effective way to increase educational investments, but may only work when they are made salient."
