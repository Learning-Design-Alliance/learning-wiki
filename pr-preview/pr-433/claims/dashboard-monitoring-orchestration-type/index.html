Yun Hwa Noh & Hee-jeong Kim. (2026). Exploring teachers' instrumental orchestration and roles in high school mathematics classes using AI-based digital tools. The Mathematical Education 65(2), 309-335. https://doi.org/10.63311/mathedu.26.6525

q1 · i? · qualitative · r3

Qualitative case analysis of lesson recordings and teacher tablet screen captures. The dashboard-monitoring type was defined from observed episodes in which the teacher used AI digital educational materials and Snorkl dashboards; the article reports it was observed 14 times among 46 episodes, second most frequent after explain-the-screen.
