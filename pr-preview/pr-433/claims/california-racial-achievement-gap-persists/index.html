Evidence¶

Voight 2013¶

Voight, A. (2013). The Racial School-Climate Gap. A report from the Region IX Equity Assistance Center at WestEd. San Francisco: WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED580366

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of student self-reported grades from the California Healthy Kids Survey, 2006–08, across grades 7, 9, and 11 in approximately 840 districts. The studies confirmed "a higher proportion of Asian and White middle and high school students reported good academic achievement." Similar disparities appeared in 2009 California Standards Test results.

"Based on Cal-SCHLS data from 2006–08, a higher proportion of Asian and White middle and high school students reported good academic achievement compared with reports from African American, American Indian, and Hispanic students."
