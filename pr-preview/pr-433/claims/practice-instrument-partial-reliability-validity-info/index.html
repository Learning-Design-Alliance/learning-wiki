Christine Ross 2015¶

Christine Ross, Mariesa Herrmann, Megan Hague Angus. (2015). Measuring Principals' Effectiveness: Results from New Jersey's Principal Evaluation Pilot. Washington, DC: U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Regional Educational Laboratory Mid-Atlantic. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/edlabs/regions/midatlantic

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Documentary review of instrument developers' evidence for the practice instruments used in New Jersey's pilot. The report finds only "partial information" about reliability, defined as consistency across raters and observations, and validity, defined as accurate measurement of true performance.

"The developers of the principal practice instruments used by pilot districts provided partial information about the instruments’ reliability (consistency across raters and observations) and validity (accurate measurement of true principal performance)."
