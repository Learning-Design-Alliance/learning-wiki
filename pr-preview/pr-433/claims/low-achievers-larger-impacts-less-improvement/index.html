Karyn Lewis 2022¶

Karyn Lewis, Megan Kuhfeld, Meredith Langi, Scott J. Peters, Erin Fahle. (2022). The widening achievement divide during COVID-19. NWEA Research brief. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/the-widening-achievement-divide-during-covid-19/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Longitudinal follow-up of a subset of 1 million students whose pre-pandemic achievement placed them in the top or bottom decile relative to national pre-pandemic norms. The brief reports "Low achievers experienced larger initial impacts at the onset of the pandemic and less improvement during 2021-22."
