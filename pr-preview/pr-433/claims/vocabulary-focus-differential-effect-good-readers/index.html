Evidence¶

Tetzlaff 2023¶

Tetzlaff, L., Edelsbrunner, P., Schmitterer, A., Hartmann, U., & Brod, G. (2023). Modeling Interactions Between Multivariate Learner Characteristics and Interventions: a Person-Centered Approach. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09830-5

q3 · i? · associational · r?

BCH analysis of the subsample (n = 217 students of 52 teachers) regressing posttest reading comprehension on teachers' instructional foci by profile. Table 2 prints vocabulary-focus contrasts GR vs. PC Beta = 0.54 and GR vs. PD Beta = 0.552 with 90% CIs excluding 0; the article reports "Good readers benefit from a focus on vocabulary".

"While none of the instructional foci showed any main effect for all participants, the specific contrasts for the different instructional foci (see Table 2) show strong differential effectiveness of vocabulary training between good readers and poor decoders, as well as between good readers and poor comprehenders."
