Evidence¶

Matsuda 2013¶

Matsuda, N., Yarzebinski, E., Keiser, V., Raizada, R., Stylianides, G. J., & Koedinger, K. R. (2013). Studying the Effect of a Competitive Game Show in a Learning by Teaching Environment. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-013-0009-1

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Analysis of Game Show participation process data (44 students): TAC after win (M=.12) vs. loss (M=.21) did not differ, t(27)=−1.283, p=0.21, and TAC did not differ across gain quartiles, t(19)=−.590, p=0.56.

"students’ tendencies in tutoring after a competition was not predictive of their gain on the procedural skill test regardless of the results of the competition, though this is arguably due to the low TAC ratio"
