Faulkner-Bond 2026¶

Faulkner-Bond, M. (2026). Finding the sweet spot for English Learner reclassification: A study of California's criteria. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED681359

q1 · i? · associational · r3

Two virtual listening sessions in winter 2023-24 convened by the CDE for county, LEA, and site leaders and educators, facilitated by CDE and WestEd. The report counts "over 75 references to Criterion 4" among responses to a question about barriers to reclassification.

"In the responses, there were over 75 references to Criterion 4. Many educators shared that they see Criterion 4 as a barrier to exit and expressed skepticism about English-only (EO) students’ ability to meet the LEA’s local standard for EL students to exit EL status."
