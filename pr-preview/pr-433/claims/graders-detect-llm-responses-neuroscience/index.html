Evidence¶

Wanli Xing 2025¶

Wanli Xing, Nia Nixon, Scott Crossley, Paul Denny, Andrew Lan, John Stamper, Zhou Yu. (2025). The Use of Large Language Models in Education. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00457-x

q2 · i? · review · r1

The editorial reports, citing Covington and Vruwink (2024), an evaluation of GPT-3.5-generated assessment responses in an undergraduate neuroscience course where "graders can distinguish between responses" from LLMs and students better than chance. Only relative-to-chance accuracy is printed; no effect size.

"While LLMs achieve performance comparable to human students based on evaluations by graduate graders, these graders can distinguish between responses from LLMs and those from students with an accuracy better than random guessing"
