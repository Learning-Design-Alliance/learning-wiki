Related Claims¶

  • Rapid guesses on computer-based tests reflect a construct-irrelevant response process rather than the tested knowledge, skills, and abilities — related
  • Very short response times (rapid guessing) indicate disengaged test taking in both high-stakes and low-stakes testing contexts — related
  • Computer-based testing's routine capture of item-level response times has driven increased interest in applications of response time to measuring ability and achievement — related
