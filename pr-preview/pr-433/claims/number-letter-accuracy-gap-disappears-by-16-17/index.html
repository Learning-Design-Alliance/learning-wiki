Evidence¶

Susac A 2014¶

Susac A, Bubic A, Vrbanc A and Planinic M (2014) Development of abstract mathematical reasoning: the case of algebra. Front. Hum. Neurosci. 8:679. https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2014.00679

q3 · i2 · associational · r2

Two-way mixed-design ANOVA with between-subjects factor Age and within-subjects factor Abstraction level (numbers vs. letters) on accuracy; printed effect sizes η2 p = 0.126 (Abstraction level) and η2 p = 0.043 (interaction). "Participants were more accurate on equations with numbers, but only in primary school and in the 1st grade of secondary school."

"the statistically signiﬁcant main effects of Age [F(3,327) = 8.37, p < 0.001, η2 p = 0.071] and Abstraction level [F(1,327) = 47.17, p < 0.001, η2 p = 0.126], as well as the interac­tion effect [ F(3,327) = 4.89, p < 0.01, η2 p = 0.043], were found"
