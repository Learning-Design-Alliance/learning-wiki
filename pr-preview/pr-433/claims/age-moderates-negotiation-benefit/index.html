Baoshu Yi & Zhinong Sun 2013¶

Baoshu Yi & Zhinong Sun. (2013). An Empirical Study of the Effectiveness of Negotiation of Meaning in L2 Vocabulary Acquisition of Chinese Learners of English. English Language Teaching, 6(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n10p120

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Cross-group comparison across the two samples in the Discussion: high school participants showed a significant comprehensible-input advantage from negotiation, while college groups showed none, though college experimental learners still outperformed controls on vocabulary.

"the participants from the high school seem to rely more on negotiation of mean ing than the college counterparts do with a view to attaining comprehensible input since no difference is fo und in comprehensible input between the experimental group and the control group from the college"
