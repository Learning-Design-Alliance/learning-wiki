Evidence¶

Molinillo 2018¶

Molinillo, S., Aguilar-Illescas, R., Anaya-Sánchez, R., & Vallespín-Arán, M. (2018). Exploring the impacts of interactions, social presence and emotional engagement on active collaborative learning in a social web-based environment. Computers & Education, 123, 41-52. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.04.012

q2 · i? · associational · r2

PLS-SEM test in the same 416-student survey; the direct path from teacher-student interaction to active learning was supported ("Teac. - stud. interaction -> active learning 0.2687", t = 5.9873, p < 0.001).

"H4. Teac. - stud. interaction -> active learning 0.2687 5.9873 *** Yes"

Molinillo 2018 (2)¶

Molinillo, S., Aguilar-Illescas, R., Anaya-Sánchez, R., & Vallespín-Arán, M. (2018). Exploring the impacts of interactions, social presence and emotional engagement on active collaborative learning in a social web-based environment. Computers & Education, 123, 41-52. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2018.04.012

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Same PLS-SEM analysis; teacher-student interaction significantly predicted emotional engagement ("Teac. - stud. interaction -> e. engagement 0.2919", t = 6.8545, p < 0.001), supporting the indirect route.

"H3. Teac. - stud. interaction -> e. engagement 0.2919 6.8545 *** Yes"
