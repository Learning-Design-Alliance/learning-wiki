Evidence¶

Pifarré 2011¶

Pifarré, M. & Kleine Staarman, J. (2011). Wiki-supported collaborative learning in primary education: How a dialogic space is created for thinking together. International Journal of Computer Supported Collaborative Learning, 6(2), 187-205. https://www.springer.com/journal/11412

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative episode analysis of negotiation-space contributions of two groups, using Mercer's talk typology as a heuristic frame. The authors argue students "used language to open up a space for thinking together", finding communality while allowing reasoned disagreement.

"To conclude, it seems that, in this first episode, the students showed many features of Exploratory Talk, in the same way as Mercer and colleagues described."
