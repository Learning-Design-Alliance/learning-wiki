Evidence¶

Gweon 2013¶

Gweon, G., Jain, M., McDonough, J., Raj, B., & Rosé, C. P. (2013). Measuring prevalence of other-oriented transactive contributions using an automated measure of speech style accommodation. Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning (2013). https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-013-9172-5

q2 · i2 · associational · r1

Correlational hypothesis test on the freeform Ottoman Empire debate corpus: DBN accommodation scores, assigned via three-fold cross-validation, were regressed on hand-coded other-oriented transact prevalence. The article reports "a significant positive correlation" with "R = .36, p< .05".

"The evaluation demonstrates a significant positive correlation between an automatic measure of speech style accommodation and prevalence of other-oriented transacts (R = .36, p< .05)."
