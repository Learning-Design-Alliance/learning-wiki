Evidence¶

Means 2009¶

Means, Toyama, Murphy, Bakia, & Jones. (2009). Evaluation of Evidence-Based Practices in Online Learning: A Meta-Analysis and Review of Online Learning Studies. U.S. Department of Education. https://www.ed.gov/about/offices/list/opepd/ppss/reports.html

q3 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r2

Moderator analysis within the 51-contrast meta-analysis comparing studies by relative time on task: mean effect size +0.46 for studies with more online time on task versus mean effect size +0.19 for studies where face-to-face learners spent as much time or more; QBetween = 3.88, p < .05. The contrast fell just short of significance (p < .06) when the five K-12 contrasts were removed.

"The mean effect size for studies with more time spent by online learners was +0.46 compared with +0.19 for studies in which the learners in the face-to-face condition spent as much time or more on task (Q = 3.88, p < .05)."
