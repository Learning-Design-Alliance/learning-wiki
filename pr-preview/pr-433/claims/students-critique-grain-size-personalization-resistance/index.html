Evidence¶

Walkington 2025¶

Walkington, C., Beauchamp, T., Ikram, F., Koçyiğit Gürbüz, M., Xia, F., Lee, M., Lan, A. (2025). Mathematics Teachers’ Interactions with a Multi-Agent System for Personalized Problem Generation. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12066. doi:10.1007/978-3-032-29770-9_5

q2 · i? · design · r2

Coding of students' open-ended feedback found 9 meta comments critiquing the personalization approach. One student suggested students should input their own interests; another objected to personalization itself, stating they did not want to like math. The authors note personalization is not universally desirable.

"An additional 9 student responses (out of the 422 response sets) were coded as “meta” comments critiquing how the personalization was being implemented."
