Howe 2017¶

Howe, Christine; Zachariou, Antonia. (2017). Small-group collaboration and individual knowledge acquisition: The processes of growth during adolescence and early adulthood. Learning and Instruction. doi:10.1016/j.learninstruc.2017.10.007

q1 · i? · associational · r?

The article states as its framing account that "Individuals may or may not accept these constructions depending upon processes of reflection and reconciliation" triggered by difference, sometimes occurring after the group session. This is the authors' interpretive account, not a separately tested result in the available text.
