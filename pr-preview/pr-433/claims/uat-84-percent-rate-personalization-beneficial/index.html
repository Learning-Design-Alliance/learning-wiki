Zaki Kurdya 2026¶

Zaki Kurdya, Mohammed Zuqlam, Salem Amassi, Shady Telbany, Motaz Saad. (2026). Taklif.AI: LLM-Powered Platform for Interest-Based Personalized College Assignments. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05842 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.05842

q2 · i? · design · r2

Preliminary user acceptance testing at the Islamic University of Gaza with 68 participants (65 undergraduate students, 3 educators), each personalizing at least one assignment after a 2-minute introduction and completing a structured questionnaire. The reported result is that "84% of participants (57/68) rated the personalization feature as beneficial". No effect size is printed.

"84% of participants (57/68) rated the personalization feature as beneficial (items 1 and 4 scored≥4)."
