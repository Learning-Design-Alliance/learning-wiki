Hayley K. Jach 2023¶

Hayley K. Jach, Lisa Bardach, Kou Murayama. (2023). How Personality Matters for Education Research. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09807-4

Narrative review reporting, citing Spielmann et al. (2022), that "conscientiousness is robustly associated with academic performance" across education levels; the review attributes this to prior meta-analytic literature it did not re-synthesise.
