Evidence¶

Sanna Järvelä · Carolyn Rosé 2021¶

Sanna Järvelä · Carolyn Rosé. (2021). Forms of collaboration matters: CSCL across the contexts. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn (2021) 16:145–149. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-021-09348-4

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The editorial reports the Pietarinen et al. observational study of "Four high school science teachers" using the ViBSE environment, with lessons videotaped and self-report instruments on teacher and student affect. No effect size is printed.

"This analysis revealed differences between teacher guidance and support depending on teacher perceptions and group outcomes."

Sanna Järvelä · Carolyn Rosé 2021 (2)¶

Sanna Järvelä · Carolyn Rosé. (2021). Forms of collaboration matters: CSCL across the contexts. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn (2021) 16:145–149. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-021-09348-4

q2 · i? · associational · r1

The editorial reports this null finding from the same Pietarinen et al. observational analysis of four teachers' classes; no effect size is printed and equivalence was not tested.

"Groups’ prior science competence was not found to have an effect on teacher guidance and support."

Sanna Järvelä · Carolyn Rosé 2021 (3)¶

Sanna Järvelä · Carolyn Rosé. (2021). Forms of collaboration matters: CSCL across the contexts. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn (2021) 16:145–149. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-021-09348-4

q2 · i? · review · r1

The editorial reports this affect-related result from Pietarinen et al.'s videotaped lessons and self-report instruments on teacher and student affect; it is presented as the study's interpretive suggestion with no effect size.

"Teacher affect was compounded by student affect, suggesting that reciprocal perceptions are necessary for understanding the teachers’ role in collaborative learning."
