Evidence¶

Noguchi 2024¶

Noguchi, Y. (2024). Harmonic memory signals in the human cerebral cortex induced by semantic relatedness of words. npj Science of Learning, 9:6. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00221-1

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Behavioral analysis of Experiment 1's old/new judgment (signal detection theory) in 34 native Japanese speakers. Semantic relatedness among memory words facilitated retention in the left visual field: "significantly higher in SemL (3.48 ± 0.08" vs NonSemL (3.03 ± 0.15), t(33) = 3.34, p = 0.002, Cohen's d = 0.63.

"I observed thed’ signiﬁcantly higher in SemL (3.48 ± 0.08, mean ± SE across participants) than NonSemL (3.03 ± 0.15) trials ( t(33) = 3.34, p = 0.002, Cohen ’s d = 0.63)"

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Same Experiment 1 behavioral analysis, right-visual-field conditions: "No signiﬁcant difference" between SemR (d' = 3.56 ± 0.07) and NonSemR (d' = 3.45 ± 0.11), t(33) = 1.05, p = 0.30. Equivalence was not tested.

"No signiﬁcant difference, in contrast, was seen (t(33) = 1.05, p = 0.30, d = 0.20) when participants memorized words in the right visual ﬁeld (SemR: d’ = 3.56 ± 0.07, NonSemR: d’ = 3.45 ± 0.11)."
