Jennie K. Grammer 2021¶

Jennie K. Grammer, Keye Xu and Agatha Lenartowicz. (2021). Effects of context on the neural correlates of attention in a college classroom. npj Science of Learning 6:15. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00094-8

EEG multilevel regression planned contrast within the classroom study. Students were "signiﬁcantly more disengaged while watching the video" than during lecture, r = 0.52; alpha power was greatest during video watching of the four activities.

"Students were also found to be signiﬁcantly more disengaged while watching the video ( MVideo = 2.96) than when listening to the lecture (MLecture = 2.17, b = −0.41, t(55) = −4.47, p < 0.001, r = 0.52)"
