Theoretical argument in the 'The Scientific Attitude Toward Sex' section. The author illustrates with physicians who become dope fiends despite scientific knowledge of narcotics, invokes McDougall on the inseparable emotional correlate of sex feelings, and concludes sterilization by education is impossible.

"emotion is more basic than mere information in determining ethical living. The' latter leads to judgment, but the former impels to action"
