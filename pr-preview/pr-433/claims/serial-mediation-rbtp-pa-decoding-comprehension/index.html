Evidence¶

Remon 2025¶

Remon, A., Mascheretti, S., Voronin, I., Feng, B., Ouellet-Morin, I., Brendgen, M., Vitaro, F., Robaey, P., Andlauer, T. F. M., Boivin, M. & Dionne, G. (2025). Mediational effects of reading-related intermediate phenotypes from polygenic scores to reading skills. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00346-x

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Sequential multiple-mediation SEM in the twin sample (n = 328); the over-identified model fit well (χ2(2) = 2.538, p = 0.281) and accounted for 33.9% of Comprehension variance. Two simpler sequential models (PA→Decoding→Comprehension; RBTP→PA→Decoding) also showed significant total indirect effects. Flipped-order alternative models were tested.

"We found a signiﬁcant total indirect effect from Reading-PGS to Comprehension via RBTP, PA and Decoding (β = 0.071, SE = 0.022; 95% CI = 0.028/0.115), suggesting that children with a higher Reading-PGS are more efﬁcient in rapid temporal processing, which is linked to better skills in PA, which lead to higher scores in reading decoding and comprehension"
