Rickles 2016¶

Rickles, J., Heppen, J., Taylor, S., Allensworth, E., Michelman, V., Sorensen, N., Walters, K., & Clements, P. (2016). Getting Back on Track: Who Needs to Recover Algebra Credit After Ninth Grade? q2 · i? · associational · r1

District-level descriptive statistic from CPS administrative records for the year before the Back on Track Study started, cited to motivate whether online credit recovery could promote recovery more than traditional face-to-face summer classes.

"However, the rate of recovery the summer after failing the course is typically low. For example, during the year before the study started (2009–10), only 13% of CPS freshmen who failed their spring semester of Algebra I recovered the course credit over the summer."
