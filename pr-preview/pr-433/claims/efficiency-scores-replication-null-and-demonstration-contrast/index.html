Evidence¶

Smit 2025¶

Smit, N., de Kleijn, R., Wicherts, J. M., & van de Pol, J. (2025). What it takes to tutor—A preregistered direct replication of the scaffolding experimental study by D. Wood et al. (1978). Journal of Educational Psychology. Advance online publication. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000965

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Preregistered ANOVA and planned contrasts on efficiency (outcome divided by activity) in the randomized replication; the omnibus test was nonsignificant (p = .162, BF = 6.77), but the article reports the demonstration versus swing-and-verbal contrast as significant, t(281) = 2.22, p = .027.

"Efﬁciency scores showed no overall mean difference between conditions, F(3, 281) = 1.73, p = .162. The Bayes factor (BF= 6.77) highlighted substantial evidence in favor of the null hypothesis. The planned contrast of contingent versus other conditions was nonsigniﬁcant, t(281)=− 0.45, p = .656, while the contrast comparing demonstration versus the swing and verbal conditions was significant, t(281)= 2.22, p = .027"
