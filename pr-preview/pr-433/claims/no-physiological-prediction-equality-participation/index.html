Haataja 2021¶

Haataja, E., Malmberg, J., Dindar, M., & Järvelä, S. (2021). The pivotal role of monitoring for collaborative problem solving seen in interaction, performance, and interpersonal physiology. Metacognition and Learning, 17, 241–268. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09279-3

q3 · i? · associational · r2

GEE models testing tonic EDA slope, %REC, %DET, and ADL as predictors of equality of participation in monitoring across 19 groups. "None of the independent physiological data variables improved the fit of the GEE model for predicting equality of participation in monitoring interactions." The best model included only the intercept.
