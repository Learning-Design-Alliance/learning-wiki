Results from the 2019 Mathematics and Reading Assessments 2020¶

Results from the 2019 Mathematics and Reading Assessments. (2020). U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Statistics. https://www.nationsreportcard.gov

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The NAEP 2019 state results report state-level score changes since 2017, stating reading scores "decreased at grade 4 for approximately one-third of the states" and were lower in more than half of states at grade 8. The maps print decreases in 17 states (grade 4 reading) and 31 states (grade 8 reading). No effect sizes are reported.

"Average mathematics scores were mainly steady across states/jurisdictions at both grades since 2017. Average reading scores decreased at grade 4 for approximately one-third of the states, while the 2019 reading scores were lower in more than half of the states at grade 8 since 2017."
