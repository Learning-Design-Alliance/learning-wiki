Evidence¶

Ventura 2022¶

Ventura, Ana Clara. (2022). Metacognition and self-regulation in young children: does it matter if metacognitive experiences are communicated?. Early Years. https://doi.org/10.1080/09575146.2022.2065666

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Individual interview study of sixty first graders (57% boys; M age: 6.2 yr., SD = 3 months) engaged in an ecological and challenging graphic task; the study reports that "the communication of the FOD were significantly associated with the deployment of metacognition and self-regulation, with the exception of planning." No effect size is printed.

"Results showed that the communication of the FOD were significantly associated with the deployment of metacognition and self-regulation, with the exception of planning."
