Chen-Chen Liu 2025¶

Chen-Chen Liu, Gwo-Jen Hwang, Peng Yu, Yun-Fang Tu, Youmei Wang. (2025). Effects of an automated corrective feedback-based peer assessment approach on students' learning achievement, motivation, and self-regulated learning conceptions in foreign language pronunciation. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10484-z

q2 · i2 · causal · r1

Quasi-experiment measuring motivation with the Wang and Chen (2010) questionnaire; the article reports "a statistically significant difference" in intrinsic motivation favoring ACF-PA (η2 = .113), while extrinsic motivation did not differ (η2 = .001).

"there was no significant difference between the two groups for extrinsic motivation (F = .061, p > .05, η2 = .001), while intrinsic motivation showed a statistically significant difference (F = 8.051, p < .01, η2 = .113)"
