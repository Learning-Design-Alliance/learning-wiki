Evidence¶

What Works Clearinghouse 2008¶

What Works Clearinghouse. (2008). WWC Intervention Report: Accelerated Middle Schools. U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/11

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

WWC synthesis across the three RCTs. The report states the "average improvement index for progressing in school is +35 percentile points" with a range of +15 to +44, and rates the domain positive. No standardized effect size is printed for the domain average.

"Based on these three studies, the average improvement index for progressing in school is +35 percentile points, with a range of +15 to +44 percentile points across the studies."

What Works Clearinghouse 2008 (2)¶

What Works Clearinghouse. (2008). WWC Intervention Report: Accelerated Middle Schools. U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/11

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Georgia RCT (140 applicants for the 1993/94 school year) outcome on years of school completed at two-year follow-up: 8.6 for the intervention versus 7.9 for controls, reported as statistically significant and substantively important. Means are descriptive; no effect size statistic is printed for this measure.

"In the Georgia study the average number of school years completed at the two-year follow-up was 8.6 for accelerated middle school students and 7.9 for control-group students."

What Works Clearinghouse 2008 (3)¶

What Works Clearinghouse. (2008). WWC Intervention Report: Accelerated Middle Schools. U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/11

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

New Jersey RCT (535 applicants over two school years), the one study meeting WWC evidence standards without reservations, found higher average years of school completed (7.8 vs 7.5); the Michigan study similarly reported 7.3 vs 6.8. Only means are printed for these comparisons.

"The New Jersey study also found higher average years of school completed for accelerated middle school students—7.8 compared with 7.5 for the control group."
