Patrick 2026¶

Patrick, S. K., Higginbotham, C., Saunders, R., Tan, T. S., & Wojcikiewicz, S. (2026). Teacher certification in Texas: District strategies to recruit and retain a credentialed workforce. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/301.563

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative interview study of district leaders in six purposefully sampled Texas districts (Channelview, Cypress-Fairbanks, Dallas, Denton, Hutto, and Mesquite ISD) with at least 5,000 students in suburban or urban areas. Leaders emphasized "the value of having multiple partnership programs and preparation options."

"All six participating districts have a partnership with at least one teacher residency program, and all have at least one Grow Your Own program targeting high school students and/or paraprofessionals."
