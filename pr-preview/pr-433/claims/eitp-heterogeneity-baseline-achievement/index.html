Matthew P. Steinberg and Lauren Sartain 2015¶

Matthew P. Steinberg and Lauren Sartain. (2015). Does Teacher Evaluation Improve School Performance? Experimental Evidence from Chicago's Excellence in Teaching Project. Association for Education Finance and Policy. https://doi.org/10.1162/EDFP_a_00173

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Heterogeneity analysis (table 9) interacting pilot status with baseline achievement in the randomized comparison: the printed year 1 reading interaction effect size is 0.087 standard deviations, significant at the 10 percent level. The article states it found "no additional beneﬁt to high-achieving pilot schools in the second and third post-policy years."

"After the ﬁrst year of the pilot, higher-achieving schools (those that are one standard deviation above the mean in prior reading achievement) improved by 0.09 standard deviations over lower-performing pilot schools."
