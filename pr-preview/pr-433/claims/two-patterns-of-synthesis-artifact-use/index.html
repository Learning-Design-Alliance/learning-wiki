Evidence¶

Xinran Zhu · Bodong Chen · David DeLiema 2026¶

Xinran Zhu · Bodong Chen · David DeLiema. (2026). Advancing collaborative discourse through knowledge synthesis. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-025-09463-6

q2 · i? · design · r2

Interaction analysis of video and screen recordings of three groups' roughly 60-minute synthesis-week discussions. Analysis of review frequency and duration (Table 3; two students excluded due to lost screen-recording data) identified the two patterns of "relying primarily on one's own synthesis" and drawing on peers'.

"Interaction analysis of group activity videos revealed two key patterns: (1) relying primarily on one's own synthesis and (2) frequently drawing on peers' syntheses."

Xinran Zhu · Bodong Chen · David DeLiema 2026 (2)¶

Xinran Zhu · Bodong Chen · David DeLiema. (2026). Advancing collaborative discourse through knowledge synthesis. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-025-09463-6

q2 · i? · design · r2

Case analysis within the same video data: Ava's matrix synthesis was checked "30 times" while she led the discussion, whereas Emma's brief summaries offered little to build on, so she reviewed peers' syntheses and became more vocal and participatory.

"She checked it 30 times during the activity, using it as a quick and efficient reference."

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • The three groups exhibited three distinct collaboration forms—a centralized model, loosely coordinated collaboration, and balanced and regulated collaboration—with group 3 showing the highest regulative-action percentage (40% during synthesis) — related
  • Individual synthesis artifacts facilitated collaborative discourse by guiding the flow of group work and by supporting knowledge sharing, building shared understanding, and generating and advancing ideas — related
  • In the graduate course, most individual synthesis artifacts remained at surface level, with format and depth varying by group and reflecting differing conceptions of synthesis — related
  • Material arrangement of artifacts (strips centered and ordered by length, worksheets held close) affords and stabilizes the initial pattern of activity; later decentralization enables new patterns — related
