q3 · i2 · causal · r?

Appendix A3 of the WWC report prints the domain average row for reading achievement as an effect size of 0.76 and improvement index +28, computed as a simple average rounded to two decimal places across Oral Reading Fluency (+35), Letter-Word Identification (+21), and Word Attack (+26). The effectiveness summary page states an average of +28 with range +21 to +35; the improvement index section states +27.

"The average improvement index for reading achievement is +27 percentile points, with a range of +21 to +35 percentile points across findings."
