Evidence¶

Vorderobermeier 2024¶

Vorderobermeier, A., Abel, J., & Sailer, M. (2024). Theoretical foundations and approaches in research on educational escape rooms: A systematic review. Educational Research Review, 44, 100625. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2024.100625

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Hacke (2019), an empirical study of an EER in programming education classified in the pattern "theoretical grounding with empirical validation". The observation links collaborative learning to problem-based learning. No effect sizes are printed.

"It was observed that teams that coordinate well, communicate with each other, and document their intermediate results are more successful in solving the problems in an educational ER."
