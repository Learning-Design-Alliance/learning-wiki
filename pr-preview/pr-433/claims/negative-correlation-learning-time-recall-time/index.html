Individual testing of 50 normal school girls who studied words and figures for exact ordered reproduction, with stop-watch timing of learning and recall. The study found "The correlation between the learning time and recall time was- —.380" with P.E. .0816; r = -.38 exceeds the |r| = .37 threshold for a large effect.

"The correlation between the learning time and recall time was- —.380 (P. E. .0816). This negative correlation indicates that when the learning time was high the recall time was low and vice versa."
