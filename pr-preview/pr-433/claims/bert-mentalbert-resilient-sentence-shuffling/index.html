q2 · i? · causal · r?

Sentence-shuffling manipulation experiments on Expressive Narrative Stories tested order sensitivity of transformer models. The abstract reports both BERT and MentalBERT "remain resilient under sentence shuffling (P-value < 0.05)"; no effect size is printed.

"Both BERT and MentalBERT remain resilient under sentence shuffling (P-value < 0.05), a critical capability for analyzing extended narratives, particularly in ENS-based comparisons of individuals with and without self-declared mental health issues."
