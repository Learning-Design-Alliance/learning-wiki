The summary characterizes the evidence for these three practices as "moderately strong," reporting a positive association with charter schools' achievement impacts. No effect sizes are printed.

"In addition, moderately strong evidence suggests that high-dosage tutoring, frequent feedback and coaching for teachers, and policies promoting the use of data to guide teachers' instructional practices are positively associated with charter schools' achievement impacts."
