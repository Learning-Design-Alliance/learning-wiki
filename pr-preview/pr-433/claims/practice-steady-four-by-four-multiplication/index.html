Evidence¶

W. S. Painter 1914¶

W. S. Painter. (1914). Efficiency in Mental Multiplication Under Extreme Fatigue. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/edu/

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Practice-period record (Table I) covering stages from two-place by two-place up to four-place by four-place, about three days per stage. The text reports that "at the 15th day the experimenter was doing fairly steady work with four-place by four-place numbers."

"the experimenter worked at the task for one hour a day for fifteen days, almost invariably in the evening between nine and twelve o'clock."

Related Claims¶
