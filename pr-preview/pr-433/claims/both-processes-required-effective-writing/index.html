Galbraith 2018¶

Galbraith, D., & Baaijen, V. M. (2018). The Work of Writing: Raiding the Inarticulate. Educational psychologist, 53(4), 238-257. https://doi.org/10.1080/00461520.2018.1505515

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical claim stated in the article's abstract: the two proposed processes are jointly necessary, "although both processes are required for effective writing". The article offers this as a model-based argument rather than a tested empirical result.

"It suggests that, although both processes are required for effective writing, the contrasting nature of the implicit organization guiding the constitution of the writer's understanding and the explicit organization required to satisfy rhetorical goals is the source of a fundamental conflict in writing"
