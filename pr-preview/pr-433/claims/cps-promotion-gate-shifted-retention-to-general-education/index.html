q2 · i? · associational · r2

Retention trend analysis of eighth graders with and without disabilities, 1993–94 to 1999–00 (Figure 6). Because students with disabilities promote via IEP requirements, more passed into high school while general education retention rose, reducing the number of general education ninth graders and raising the disabled proportion.

"the rate of retention for students with disabilities rose from 7.3 to 9.2 percent, but then it fell to 4.7 percent by 1999–00. At the same time, general education students were substantially more likely to be retained; their rate of retention rose from 2.1 percent to a high of 14.4 percent"
