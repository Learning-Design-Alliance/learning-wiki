Evidence¶

de Jonge 2026¶

de Jonge, T. H. S., de Koning, B. B., & van Wermeskerken, M. (2026). It's all a matter of expectancy: How expectancies about task execution perspective influence learning through video modeling examples. Learning and Instruction, 105, 102422. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2026.102422

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Mixed-design ANOVA in the main analysis of the experiment with 128 undergraduates recreating circuit configurations. The main effect of recording angle was significant with "η2 = 0.02", a small effect; means were 6.83 (first-person) versus 6.08 (third-person).

"the results of the mixed-design ANOVA revealed a significant main effect of recording angle, F(1, 124) = 5.24, p = .024, η2 = 0.02, with higher performance when having watched first-person recordings (M = 6.83, SD = 2.82) compared to third-person recordings (M = 6.08, SD = 3.01)"
