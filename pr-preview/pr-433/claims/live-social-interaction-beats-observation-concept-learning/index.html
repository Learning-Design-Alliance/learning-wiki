Evidence¶

Ana Zappa 2025¶

Ana Zappa, Mel Slater & Antoni Rodriguez-Fornells. (2025). Social interaction shapes and boosts second language learning: virtual reality can show us how. npj Science of Learning 10:90. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00381-8

q2 · i? · causal · r?

A behavioral study reviewed by the article (De Felice et al., 2021, described in Fig. 1B) compared learning conceptual information about novel lexical items by observing recorded social interaction versus engaging in live social interaction; the "Live condition resulted in greater behavioral accuracy post-training overall".

"Behavioral study comparing learning conceptual information about new words by observing (Recorded) vs. engaging in (Live) social interaction. The Live condition resulted in greater behavioral accuracy post-training overall."

Ana Zappa 2025 (2)¶

Ana Zappa, Mel Slater & Antoni Rodriguez-Fornells. (2025). Social interaction shapes and boosts second language learning: virtual reality can show us how. npj Science of Learning 10:90. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00381-8

q2 · i? · review · r2

In the same reviewed study, as reported in the article's running text about Fig. 1B, seeing the teacher's face improved learning, "but only during interactive learning", suggesting distinct mechanisms for interactional versus observational learning. The article reports no effect size for this moderation.

"Seeing the teacher's face resulted in improved learning, but only during interactive learning, suggesting that different mechanisms subserve interactional vs. observational learning processes (Fig. 1B)."
