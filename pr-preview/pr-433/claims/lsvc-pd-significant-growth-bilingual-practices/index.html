Evidence¶

Yuzhu Xia 2025¶

Yuzhu Xia, Adria Patthoff, Liana Balloffet, Marco A. Bravo, Kip Téllez. (2025). Beyond boundaries: leveraging technology for differentiated professional development with lesson study video club. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-025-10524-8

q2 · i? · design · r2

Paired-sample t-tests on baseline and follow-up surveys from 57 of 71 teacher participants in three cohorts (2018–2021), with Bonferroni correction for multiple comparisons. Descriptive gains included "explicitly taught mathematics vocabulary (Δ = 0.56)"; no standardized effect sizes were printed.

"On a 1–5 scale, participants reported increased implementation of bilingual literacy strategies to strengthen students’ comprehension of mathematics (Δ = 0.70), provided more opportunities for students to write about mathematics (Δ = 0.56), explicitly taught mathematics vocabulary (Δ = 0.56), and fostered bilingual students’ communication of mathematical ideas (Δ = 0.40)."
