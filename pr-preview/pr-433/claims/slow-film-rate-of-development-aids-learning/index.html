q2 · i? · causal · r?

Experimental study in which 1100 trainees saw a film on assembly and disassembly of a breech block and were tested; two film versions "varied in the speed with which they presented the material". The conclusion is stated without printed effect sizes.

"It was concluded that a slow rate of development is more helpful to learning than a fast rate."
