Evidence¶

Ginns 2005¶

Ginns, P. (2005). Meta-analysis of the modality effect. Learning and Instruction, 15(4), 313-331. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2005.07.001

q2 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

Meta-analysis of four within-subjects design effects from two published journal articles (Brünken et al., 2002, 2004), representing 40 students, using secondary-task and comprehension-test results under system-paced conditions.

"The overall weighted mean standardised mean gain of audio-visual over visual-only presentation was moderate to large, d = 0.73 (95% confidence interval 0.50 - 0.97), and the homogeneity statistic was not significant, Q = 0.93, df = 3, p = 0.817"
