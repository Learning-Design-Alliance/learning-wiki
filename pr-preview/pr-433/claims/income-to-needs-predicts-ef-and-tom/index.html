Evidence¶

Marcovitch S. 2014¶

Marcovitch S., O'Brien M., Calkins S. D., Leerkes E. M., Weaver J. M., Levine D. W. (2014). A Longitudinal Assessment of the Relation between Executive Function and Theory of Mind at 3, 4, and 5 Years. Cognitive Development. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cogdev.2014.07.001

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Preliminary analyses of the latent variable model in the 226-child sample; tests of indirect effects showed income-to-needs continued to predict EF at 4 (B = .29, p = .01) and 5 (B = .17, p = .01) and ToM at 4 (B = .17, p = .01) and 5 (B = .15, p = .01).

"Income-to-needs measured at age 3 was significantly related to both EF (β = .31, p < .001) and ToM (β = .23, p = .02), partially consistent with Hughes and Ensor (2007)'s findings that social disadvantage related to EF but not ToM"
