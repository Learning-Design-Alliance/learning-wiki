Maike Trautner 2025¶

Maike Trautner, Carola Grunschel, Malte Schwinger. (2025). Motivating Motivation Regulation Research—An Evidence and Gap Map Approach. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10019-1

q3 · i? · review · r3

Gap analysis of the evidence and gap map built from the 289 included studies, identifying cells with absent or sparse evidence. The review reports that "we still know little about motivation regulation in pre- and primary school children and its development across school years" and that contextual characteristics have received little attention despite self-regulation being conceptualized as context dependent.

"The maps' gaps reveal that we still know little about motivation regulation in pre- and primary school children and its development across school years. Furthermore, despite the conceptualization of self-regulation as a context dependent construct, contextual characteristics have received little attention."
