Damacharla 2018¶

Damacharla, P., Dhakal, P., Stumbo, S., Javaid, A. Y., Ganapathy, S., Malek, D. A., Hodge, D. C., & Devabhaktuni, V. (2018). Effects of Voice-Based Synthetic Assistant on Performance of Emergency Care Provider in Training. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-018-0166-3

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Controlled user study with 36 novice participants (19 experiment, 17 control) performing a massive hemorrhage tourniquet task over three training-testing iterations. The experiment group's "Pscore of the experiment group in test 3" was significantly higher than control's (z = −2.1538, p = 0.01974); no effect size is printed.
