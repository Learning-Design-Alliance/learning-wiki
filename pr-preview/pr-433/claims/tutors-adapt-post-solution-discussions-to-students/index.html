Sandra Katz 2003¶

Sandra Katz, David Allbritton, John Connelly. (2003). Going Beyond the Problem Given: How Human Tutors Use Post-Solution Discussions to Support Transfer. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 13, pp.79-116. https://telearn.hal.science/hal-00197313

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Qualitative analysis of Study 1 tutoring transcripts, in which tutors individually guided 15 students in Andes, showed that "Tutors adapted the post-solution discussions" to student ability and current-problem performance. This is a descriptive finding about tutoring behaviour.
