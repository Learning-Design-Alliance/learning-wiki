roles-2¶

Ching-Jung Cheng, Yu-Chan Chung, Bing-Chen Chiu, Yu-Hsuan Lin, and Jia-Wei Liao. 2026. Exploring AI-Supported Disciplinary Mediation in Student Project Teams' Text-Based Communication: A Probe-Grounded Co-Design Study. OzCHI '26. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07503

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Thematic-analysis finding on role expansion: participants questioned the legitimacy of AI advice because, as one stated, AI "does not have the ability to take responsibility for what it says"; during deadlock members could dismiss an AI judgment. Descriptive; no effect size printed.

"As AI moved from organizing perspectives toward influencing collective judgment, the detachment that supported its perceived neutrality also weakened the authority needed to shape the team’s decision."
