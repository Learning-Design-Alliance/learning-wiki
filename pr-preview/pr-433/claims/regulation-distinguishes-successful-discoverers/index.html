Evidence¶

Ton de Jong 1998¶

Ton de Jong, Wouter R. van Joolingen. (1998). Scientific Discovery Learning with Computer Simulations of Conceptual Domains. Review of Educational Research. https://telearn.hal.science/hal-00190680v1

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review's narrative synthesis of process studies attributing to Glaser et al. the observation that "successful discoverers followed a plan over experiments" while unsuccessful learners concentrated on local decisions.

"Glaser et al. (1992) report that successful discoverers followed a plan over experiments, whereas unsuccessful ones used a more random strategy, concentrating at local decisions, which also gave them problems to monitor what they had been doing"

Ton de Jong 1998 (2)¶

Ton de Jong, Wouter R. van Joolingen. (1998). Scientific Discovery Learning with Computer Simulations of Conceptual Domains. Review of Educational Research. https://telearn.hal.science/hal-00190680v1

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports Veenman and Elshout's cross-study comparison, finding working method made "its own contribution to learning outcome on top of intellectual ability"; no effect size is printed.

"Veenman and Elshout (1995) found that, over a number of studies, individuals with a high intellectual ability showed a better working method than individuals with a low intellectual ability, but also that working method had its own contribution to learning outcome on top of intellectual ability."
