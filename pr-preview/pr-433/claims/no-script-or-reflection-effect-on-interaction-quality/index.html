Comparing the effects of a collaboration script and collaborative reflection on promoting knowledge about good collaboration and effective interaction. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. q3 · i0 · causal · r?

In the laboratory experiment's testing phase, groups collaborated without support after receiving script, reflection, or control treatment; the study reports "no effect of the script or the reflection scaffold on the quality of interaction" relative to control. Equivalence was not established; no effect size printed.

"However, we found no effect of the script or the reflection scaffold on the quality of interaction in the subsequent collaboration."
