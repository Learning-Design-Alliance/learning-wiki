Evidence¶

Marion Décaillet 2024¶

Marion Décaillet, Solange Denervaud, Cléo Huguenin-Virchaux, Laureline Besuchet, Céline J. Fischer Fumeaux, Micah M. Murray & Juliane Schneider. (2024). The impact of premature birth on auditory-visual processes in very preterm schoolchildren. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00257-3

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Partial Spearman correlations (controlling for SES and gestational age) between multisensory gain and SPM, WISC, and Digit Span scores in 28 VPT children. The gain "was not signiﬁcantly correlated with any of the clinical or cognitive measures"; e.g., WISC IQ Total r = −0.15, p = 0.51 (Table 6).

"By controlling for gestational age and SES, partial Spearman correlations were carried out. The multisensory gain was not signiﬁcantly correlated with any of the clinical or cognitive measures"
