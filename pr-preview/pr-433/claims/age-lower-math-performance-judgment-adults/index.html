Evidence¶

Maximilian Seitz 2025¶

Maximilian Seitz, Anna Hawrot, Kathrin Lockl. (2025). Performance judgment in mathematical and reading competence in adults. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-025-09416-2

q3 · i1 · associational · r3

Weighted linear regression on the NEPS SC6 adult sample (n = 5015 with the mathematics test), controlling for education level, showed "a small negative effect of age" on global postdictions (β = −0.10, p < .01); the model explained R² = 0.15.

"In the linear model predicting performance judgment in mathematical competence while controlling for education level (Fig. 1), there was a small negative effect of age, suggesting that older age groups had lower performance judgment ratings (β = −0.10, p < . 01)."
