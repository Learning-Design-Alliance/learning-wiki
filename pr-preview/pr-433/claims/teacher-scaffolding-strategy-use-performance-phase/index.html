Evidence¶

Niels J. de Ruig 2023¶

Niels J. de Ruig, Peter F. de Jong, Marjolein Zee. (2023). Stimulating Elementary School Students' Self-Regulated Learning Through High-Quality Interactions and Relationships: A Narrative Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09795-5

q2 · i? · review · r2

One of only two cross-sectional studies on classroom-level interactions and regulation of cognition, conducted in upper elementary schools in the Netherlands, using the CP-SRLI, the only included instrument distinguishing SRL components and phases. No effect size is printed for this finding.

"Results of this large-scale study showed that students who perceived high levels of scaffolding from their teachers used more surface- and deep-level strategies during the performance phase of SRL (Baas et al., 2015)."
