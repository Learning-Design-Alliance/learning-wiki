Evidence¶

Marc Deosdad-Díez & Josep Marco-Pallarés 2025¶

Marc Deosdad-Díez & Josep Marco-Pallarés. (2025). Note-by-note predictability modulates rhythm learning and its neural components. npj Science of Learning 10:59. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00353-y

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Linear mixed-effect model on note-by-note asynchrony from 70 non-musicians synchronizing with rhythms, with information content, repetition and visual-cue group as factors. The model showed "Information content was associated with higher asynchrony overall".

"Information content was associated with higher asynchrony overall (F(1,56570) = 4482.4,p < 0.001)."

Marc Deosdad-Díez & Josep Marco-Pallarés 2025 (2)¶

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Mixed-effect model of asynchrony in reproduction sequences produced without auditory feedback. Asynchrony "increased with information content (F(1,67) = 587.5" in the same model that showed a repetition effect.
