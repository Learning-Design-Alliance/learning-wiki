Evidence¶

Jinxi Wen 2025¶

Jinxi Wen, Wenjing Lyu, Jin Liu & Li Yang. (2025). Commensality and academic performance: measuring peer acceptance among college students. npj Science of Learning 10:69. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00357-8

q2 · i0 · associational · r2

Robustness check replacing average score with failed subject ratio (Table 3, OLS, N = 2485 first semester). Roommate commensality β = −0.061 (p < 0.05); third-category β = −0.060 (p < 0.05); diversity index β = −0.054 (p < 0.01). Supplementary analyses reportedly show negative correlation with number of failed subjects and positive with major ranking.
