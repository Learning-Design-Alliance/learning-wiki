Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • True-score-model correlations among fall, winter, and spring scores exceed 0.90 for almost all course-specific tests, indicating strongly correlated administrations across terms. — related
  • The published norms allow normative interpretation of both status and fall-to-spring growth, e.g., an Algebra 1 student at the 55th fall percentile has an expected gain of 8 RIT with growth SD of 9, and an 11-RIT gain places the student at the 62nd growth percentile. — related
  • Updated norms are heavily weighted toward 2021/22 pandemic-era performance, with lower norm-group performance than the prior norms — related
  • The 2025 MAP Growth norms were derived from one of the largest norming samples ever created, spanning 116,225,831 test events from 13,804,182 students in 29,720 schools across 7,000 districts over six testing terms — related
