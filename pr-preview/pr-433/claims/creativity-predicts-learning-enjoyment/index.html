Shute 2020¶

Shute, V. J., & Rahimi, S. (2020). Stealth Assessment of Creativity in a Physics Video Game. Computers in Human Behavior. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0747563220303940

q3 · i? · associational · r2

The abstract summarizes results from the 167-student pretest-posttest study: the stealth creativity estimate "significantly predicts in-game performance", game enjoyment, and physics learning. The truncated text reports the abstract-level summary of these regression findings.

"creativity (i.e., estimated using our stealth assessment of creativity) significantly predicts in-game performance (e.g., number of levels solved), game enjoyment, and learning of physics content"
