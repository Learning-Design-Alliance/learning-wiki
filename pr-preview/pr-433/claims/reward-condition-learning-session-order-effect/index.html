Evidence¶

Federico Nardi 2025¶

Federico Nardi, A. Aldo Faisal & Shlomi Haar. (2025). Motor learning mechanisms are not modified by feedback manipulations in a real-world task. npj Science of Learning 10:73. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00373-8

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Session-split comparison of average cue-ball angle in the reward condition (Fig. 3b), 32 participants across two sessions. Reward Round 1 showed no correction; Round 2 corrected "most of the perturbation (2.82 ± 0.29 degrees)", statistically indistinguishable from the error sessions by Mann–Whitney tests.

"The participants in Round 1 - who were naive to the task and the EVR - did not learn how to correct for the perturbation, whereas participants in Round 2 - who were less naive to the task and the EVR as they already had a 1st session where they aimed at the other pocket with error-feedback - corrected for most of the perturbation (2.82 ± 0.29 degrees), having results not significantly different from both sessions of the error condition (WErrR1 = 34, p = 0.083; WErrR2 = 33, p = 0.074)."
