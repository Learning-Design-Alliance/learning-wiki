Qualitative synthesis: only one study (Bock et al., 2018) examined response inhibition and reading fluency, reporting r = −.15, described as "a weak inverse relationship"; the single data point makes the finding tentative.

"The effect size was small and negative (r = − .15), suggesting a weak inverse relationship and given the single data point, findings should be considered tentative."
