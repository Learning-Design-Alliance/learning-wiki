Evidence¶

Lisi Wang 2023¶

Lisi Wang, Katherine Muenks, Veronica X. Yan. (2023). Interventions to Promote Retrieval Practice: Strategy Knowledge Predicts Intent, but Perceived Cost Predicts Usage. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000813

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Between-subjects t-test at T1 on intention to use general (non-flashcard) retrieval methods. The overcoming barriers condition was higher, 'Cohen's d = 0.18 (a small effect)'; no standardized effect size beyond d is printed.

"Participants in the overcoming barriers condition ( M = 3.60, SD = 0.81) self-reported higher intention to implement general methods of retrieval practice than those in the traditional condition ( M = 3.45, SD = 0.80), t (384) = 1.78, p = .04 (one-sided), Cohen's d = 0.18 (a small effect)"

Lisi Wang 2023 (2)¶

Lisi Wang, Katherine Muenks, Veronica X. Yan. (2023). Interventions to Promote Retrieval Practice: Strategy Knowledge Predicts Intent, but Perceived Cost Predicts Usage. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000813

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Mixed ANOVA on general retrieval practice use across both sessions: reported use at T2 was lower than intended use at T1, with 'no main effect of intervention version'. The intervention did not change actual reported use.

"The mixed ANOVA revealed only a statistically detectable main effect of time point, F (1, 278) = 19.93, p < .001, η 2 p = .067. Participants' reported use at T2 was lower than their intended use at T1. There was no main effect of intervention version, F (1, 278) = 2.37, p = .13, η 2 p = .008"
