Evidence¶

Smit 2025¶

Smit, N., de Kleijn, R., Wicherts, J. M., & van de Pol, J. (2025). What it takes to tutor—A preregistered direct replication of the scaffolding experimental study by D. Wood et al. (1978). Journal of Educational Psychology. Advance online publication. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000965

q2 · i0 · causal · r3

Exploratory correlational analysis (N = 63) of contingent-condition sessions relating tutor correctness to child posttest performance; all three correlations were nonsignificant and negligible (outcome r = .05, efficiency r = −.01, autonomy r = .04), with BF01 values of 6.3, 6.4, and 6.0 showing substantial evidence for zero correlations.

"we also correlated the percentage of correct tutor opera-tions in the instruction phase ( N = 63) with performance in the posttest phase but found no signi ﬁcant associations for outcome scores ( r = .05, p = .72), ef ﬁciency ( r =− .01, p = .94), or autonomy ( r = .04, p = .75)"

Related Claims¶
