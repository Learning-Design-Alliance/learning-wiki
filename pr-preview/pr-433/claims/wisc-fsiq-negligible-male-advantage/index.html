Evidence¶

D. Giofrè · K. Allen · E. Toffalini · S. Caviola 2022¶

D. Giofrè · K. Allen · E. Toffalini · S. Caviola. (2022). The Impasse on Gender Differences in Intelligence: a Meta-Analysis on WISC Batteries. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09705-1

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Multilevel random-effects meta-analysis of 48 studies (78 samples) reporting WISC FSIQ by gender. The pooled effect was "significant but practically negligible, with slightly higher scores in males than in females"; standardization-sample-only estimates were virtually the same (d = 0.10).

"The estimated mean effect size was significant but practically negligible, with slightly higher scores in males than in females, d = 0.09, 95%CI [0.04, 0.14], p < 0.001."
