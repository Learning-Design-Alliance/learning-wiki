A Meta-Analysis of Single Case Research Studies on Aided Augmentative and Alternative Communication Systems with Individuals with Autism Spectrum Disorders. J Autism Dev Disord 42, 60–74. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10803-011-1212-2

Meta-analysis of single-case research on aided AAC with individuals with ASD, synthesizing studies via the Improvement Rate Difference effect size measure. The article reports that "aided AAC interventions had large effects on targeted behavioral outcomes in individuals with ASD." No numeric IRD value is printed in the available text.
