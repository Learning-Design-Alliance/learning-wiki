Alexandre Clin Deffarges 2026¶

Alexandre Clin Deffarges, Nataliya Kosmyna, and Pattie Maes. (2026). Socrates went Nuclear: Comparing Interaction Strategies for AI systems in a Learning Context using Brain Sensing. arXiv preprint arXiv:2609.00584. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00584 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.00584

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Per-question analysis of learning delta by mode across the ten training questions. The Full condition achieved the highest delta on most questions (Q1 to Q7), with strong gains on Q4 (Δ= 31.53) and Q7 (Δ= 27.76), while the Socratic mode exhibited more variable performance.

"The Adaptive mode showed a progressive increase in delta across the session (from Δ= 3.16 on Q1 to Δ= 19.97on Q9), suggesting that participants benefited increasingly from the adaptive mode as they got to understand the system."
