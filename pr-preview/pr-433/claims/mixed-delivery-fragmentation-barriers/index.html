Evidence¶

Walrond 2025¶

Walrond, N., Serwanga, R., & Farrell, G. (2025). Systems alignment and coherence: Opportunities for Alameda County's early care and education system. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED673491

q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

Descriptive analysis in the report's background section enumerates challenges of mixed delivery systems, stating that "the lack of adequate funding and the variety of service modality results in fragmentation, creating barriers" such as siloed or opaque funding streams, long subsidy waiting lists, fragmented data systems, and poverty-level wages driving workforce turnover. No effect sizes are reported; this is a qualitative system characterization.
