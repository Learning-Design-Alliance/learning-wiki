Evidence¶

Reynolds 2010¶

Reynolds, R., Walker, K., & Speight, C. (2010). Web-based museum trails on PDAs for university-level design students: Design and evaluation. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2010.04.010

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Summative qualitative evaluation of the iGuides trails with HE design students at the V&A, drawing on interviews, observations and uploaded data; the review found "the trails enhanced students'knowledge of, interest in, and closeness to the objects" and that students "spent much longer looking at objects when following trails".
