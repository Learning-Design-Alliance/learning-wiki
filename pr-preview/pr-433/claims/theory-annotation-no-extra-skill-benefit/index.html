Evidence¶

Sam Sims 2026¶

Sam Sims, Harry Fletcher-Wood, Thomas Godfrey-Faussett, Peps Mccrea, Stefanie Meliss. (2026). Modelling evidence-based practice in initial teacher training: effects on teachers’ skills, knowledge and self-efficacy. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09779-2

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Pre-registered regression (Model 2) comparing Arm 3 (model with theory) against Arm 2 (model) among 58 participants, on the standardised skill outcome; the article reports “the absence of any statistically significant difference in improvement (p = 0.477)”.

"Participants allocated to the model condition and the model with theory condition show very similar improvement between their first and second simulator attempts. Indeed, there is no measurable difference between the two. Column 3 of Table 2 reports formal regression results, which confirm the absence of any statistically significant difference in improvement (p = 0.477)."
