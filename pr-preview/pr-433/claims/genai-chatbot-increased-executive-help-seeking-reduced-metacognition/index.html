Evidence¶

Li 2025¶

Li, X., Li, T., Yan, L., Li, Y., Zhao, L., Raković, M., Molenaar, I., Gašević, D., & Fan, Y. (2025). FLoRA: An Advanced AI-Powered Engine to Facilitate Hybrid Human-AI Regulated Learning. https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.07362

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Controlled laboratory study with 117 ESL university students randomly assigned to four groups. Process mining via first-order Markov models showed learners using GenAI chatbots bypassed metacognitive stages, and the AI Group showed a significantly higher Asking Help ratio than the HE Group.

"The Mann-Whitney U test results indicated a significant difference between the two groups in theAsking Helpstage ratio (𝑍= −3.334,𝐸𝑆= 1.288,𝑝 < 0.001), with the AI Group showing a significantly higher ratio than that of HE Group."
