q3 · i? · causal · r2

Experiment 1 transfer analysis in the weather-phenomena video study (N = 108). The results section reports "No significant effect was found for transfer; F(2, 105) = 2.06, p = .13"; equivalence was not tested.

"No significant effect was found for transfer; F(2, 105) = 2.06, p = .13. Descriptively, students in the deictic condition achieved the highest transfer score."

Maik Beege 2020 (2)¶

Maik Beege, Manuel Ninaus, Sascha Schneider, Steve Nebel, Julia Schlemmel, Jasmin Weidenmüller, Korbinian Moeller, Günter Daniel Rey. (2020). Investigating the effects of beat gestures and deictic gestures of a lecturer in educational videos. Psychology of learning with digital media, Chemnitz University of Technology. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2020.103955

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Experiment 2 transfer analysis (N = 121, industrial-revolution video). A medium effect was observed (ηp2 = .08) and the deictic condition exceeded the control (Mdiff = 1.53; p = .01) "but not the beat condition (Mdiff = 1.11; p = .09)".

"A significant effect of gesture instruction with a medium effect size was also observed for transfer; F(2, 118) = 4.79, p = .01, ηp2 = .08. Sidak-corrected post-hoc analyses indicated that students in the deictic condition reported higher retention scores than students in the control condition (Mdiff = 1.53; p = .01) but not the beat condition (Mdiff = 1.11; p = .09)."
