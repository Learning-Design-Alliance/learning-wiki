Evidence¶

Sabine Zorn 2022¶

Sabine Zorn, Minna Puustinen. (2022). Seeking academic help: The case of lower secondary students with autism spectrum disorder and their teachers. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2022.101624

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Video-based analysis of help-seeking episodes in general and resource class. The study reports that "Students always used verbal means to solicit help" and identified additional nonverbal and verbal attention-drawing means.

"Students always used verbal means to solicit help; in addition, both nonverbal and verbal means to draw teachers’ attention were identified."
