Evidence¶

Krebs 2021¶

Krebs, M.-C., Schüler, A., & Scheiter, K. (2021). Do prior knowledge, model-observer similarity and social comparison influence the effectiveness of eye movement modeling examples for supporting multimedia learning? Instructional Science, 49, 607–635. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09552-7

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Experiment with 174 analyzed university students in a 2×3 between-subjects design; PROCESS Model 3 regression with modeling condition, PK activation and z-standardized domain knowledge predicting recall. The article reports "no significant three-way interaction ... F < 1", so equivalence was not tested and no effect size is printed.

"Results for the three-way interaction model for recall performance as dependent variable indicated against our hypotheses no significant three-way interaction between modeling condition, PK activation and domain knowledge score, F < 1."
