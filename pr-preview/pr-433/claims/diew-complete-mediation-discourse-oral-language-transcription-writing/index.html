Evidence¶

Kim Young-Suk Grace 2017¶

Kim Young-Suk Grace, Schatschneider Christopher. (2017). Expanding the developmental models of writing: A direct and indirect effects model of developmental writing (DIEW). Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000129

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Structural equation modeling with 193 Grade 1 children compared four DIEW variations; chi-square difference tests showed no differences between models (0.12 ≤ Δχ2 ≤ 1.96), and direct paths in partial mediation models were nonsignificant (ps ≥ .19), so 'the complete mediation model (Figure 3a) was chosen as the final model.'

"Therefore, based on parsimony and the chi-square test results, the complete mediation model (Figure 3a) was chosen as the final model."

Kim Young-Suk Grace 2017 (2)¶

Kim Young-Suk Grace, Schatschneider Christopher. (2017). Expanding the developmental models of writing: A direct and indirect effects model of developmental writing (DIEW). Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000129

q2 · i? · associational · r2

In the final complete mediation SEM model (Figure 6), the included language and cognitive skills explained '67% of variance in writing' and 62% of variance in discourse-level oral language for the 193 Grade 1 children.

"A total of 67% of variance in writing and 62% of variance in discourse-level oral language were explained."

Related Claims¶

  • Total effects on writing quality are largest for discourse-level oral language (.46), working memory (.43), and spelling (.37) — related
  • Working memory is a foundational cognitive skill for language and transcription skills, but its relation to writing is primarily mediated by other component skills — related
  • Total effects of oral language, cognition, transcription, and reading comprehension vary largely across the three dimensions of written composition — related
  • Discourse oral language is strongly related to writing quality and reading comprehension but unrelated to writing productivity in Grade 2 — related
  • Discourse-level oral language is directly predicted by theory of mind, vocabulary, and working memory, but not inference or grammatical knowledge, in Grade 1 — related
  • Inference, but not theory of mind, is independently related to writing quality after accounting for transcription skills and working memory — related
  • Writing fluency completely mediates the relations of Grade 1 spelling and handwriting fluency to Grade 2 writing quality — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Kindergarten working memory is indirectly related to Grade 2 writing fluency and quality via Grade 1 spelling, while attentional control shows no direct or indirect relation — related
