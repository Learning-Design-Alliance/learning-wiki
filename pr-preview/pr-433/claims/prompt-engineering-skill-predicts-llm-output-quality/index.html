Evidence¶

Knoth 2024¶

Knoth, N., Tolzin, A., Janson, A., & Leimeister, J. M. (2024). AI literacy and its implications for prompt engineering strategies. Computers and Education: Artificial Intelligence. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2024.100225

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Study of non-expert students interacting with LLM-based AI systems through prompt engineering; the article reports that "higher-quality prompt engineering skills predict the quality of LLM output". No effect size or test statistic is printed in the available text.

"The results show that higher-quality prompt engineering skills predict the quality of LLM output, suggesting that prompt engineering is indeed a required skill for the goal-directed use of generative AI tools."
