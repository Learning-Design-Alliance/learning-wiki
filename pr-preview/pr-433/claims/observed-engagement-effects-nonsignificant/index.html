Latent growth curve analysis of CLASS-S-scored classroom observations (550 recorded lessons coded in 1,508 segments by blinded certified observers) explored whether an external data source supported self-reported engagement findings. Neither the small ITT nor the medium CACE effect reached significance.

"For observed student engagement, the ITT analysis indicated a small intervention effect, and the CACE analysis indicated a medium effect; however, none of these effects were statistically signiﬁcant."
