Margaret E. Raymond 2025¶

Margaret E. Raymond, PhD; Marzena Sasnal, PhD. (2025). The Impact of Rhode Island's Charter School Performance Review System on the Education Landscape. Center for Research on Education Outcomes, Stanford University. https://credo.stanford.edu/reports/item/rhode-island-2025/

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative interview study: CREDO conducted 12 in-depth interviews with 14 key officials (RIDE staff, Council/Governor's Office personnel, charter school leaders) between September and November 2024, analyzing transcripts for themes. Officials reported the framework "reduced political interference by utilizing standardized, transparent data and consistent processes." No effect sizes are reported.

"The new performance framework has reduced political interference by utilizing standardized, transparent data and consistent processes. Clear metrics have alleviated tensions and increased the credibility of the authorization system."
