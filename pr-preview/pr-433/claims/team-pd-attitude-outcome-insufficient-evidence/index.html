Evidence¶

Gast 2017¶

Gast, Inken; Schildkamp, Kim; van der Veen, Jan T. (2017). Team-Based Professional Development Interventions in Higher Education: A Systematic Review. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654317704306

q2 · i? · review · r3

Across the 18 included studies, only one article specifically mentioned the term attitudes as a result of a team-based intervention; studies instead focused on teacher satisfaction or enhanced teacher confidence.

"Therefore, there was not enough evidence to draw conclusions about teacher attitude changes as an outcome variable of team-based professional development interventions."
