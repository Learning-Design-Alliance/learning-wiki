Chin Yang Shapland 2021¶

Chin Yang Shapland, Ellen Verhoef, George Davey Smith, Simon E. Fisher, Brad Verhulst, Philip S. Dale and Beate St Pourcain. (2021). Multivariate genome-wide covariance analyses of literacy, language and working memory skills reveal distinct etiologies. npj Science of Learning 6:23. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00101-y

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Multi-domain GSEM (passage reading subset, N = 6453): measurement-specific genetic factors for listening comprehension and non-word repetition. The trait-specific factor for listening comprehension explained "17% (SE = 0.06)" of phenotypic variance, i.e. 56% (SE = 0.14) of SNP-h2.

"These factors explained 17% (SE = 0.06) and 12% (SE = 0.05) of the phenotypic variance respectively (Supplementary Table 13 and Fig. 2b), corresponding to 56% (SE = 0.14) and 47% (SE = 0.14) of the SNP-h2"
