Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Guidance tools supporting collaboration are relatively uncommon in educational VRLs, integrated in only 13 of 62 reviewed systems
  • Regulation tools for collaboration are very rare in educational VRLs, identified in only 8 laboratories, mostly supporting regulation by teachers
  • Synchronous communication is the most common collaboration support integrated in educational VRLs, and few labs offer none
  • Only a minority of VRL studies assess collaboration quality or learning, but most studies that measure learning report positive impact
  • A systematic review found literature reviews on collaborative learning in VR use very broad VR definitions, with few included papers using HMDs — related
  • Collaborative VRLs are predominantly STEM-focused, virtual, non co-located, synchronous, and 2D — related
