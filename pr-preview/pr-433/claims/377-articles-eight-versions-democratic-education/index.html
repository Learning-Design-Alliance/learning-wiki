q2 · i? · review · r3

Theoretical review using Laclau and Mouffe's discourse theory. Sources were found via Web of Science, ERIC, Google Scholar, and Scopus, filtered through quality, language, focus, and accessibility criteria; "This process resulted in a total of 377 articles being included in this review." Coding in NVivo 11 across four categories yielded the eight discursive patterns.
