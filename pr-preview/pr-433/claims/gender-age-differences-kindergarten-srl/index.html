Evidence¶

Heyi Zhang 2014¶

Heyi Zhang, David Whitebread. (2014). Linking parental scaffolding with self-regulated learning in Chinese kindergarten children. Faculty of Education, University of Cambridge. https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Mann-Whitney U tests in preliminary analyses of the 130-child sample (59 girls, 71 boys). Girls scored higher on all three strategic behaviour measures but "did not outperform boys in terms of task performance (U=1710.50, p>.05)".

"girls showed more 20 cognitive strategic behaviours ( U=1540.50, p<.01), metacognitive strategic 21 behaviours ( U=1637.50, p<.05), and motivational strategic behaviours ( U=1501.50, 22 p<.01), but did not outperform boys in terms of task performance (U=1710.50, p>.05)."

Heyi Zhang 2014 (2)¶

Heyi Zhang, David Whitebread. (2014). Linking parental scaffolding with self-regulated learning in Chinese kindergarten children. Faculty of Education, University of Cambridge. https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk

q3 · i2 · associational · r2

Spearman's correlations in preliminary analyses. The printed correlations were ρ=.19 (metacognitive), ρ=.22 (motivational), and "task performance (ρ=.24, p < .01)"; significant effects were controlled in later analyses.

"The results of Spearman ’s correlations showed that c hildren’s age was significantly 24 related to metacognitive strategic behaviours ( ρ=.19, p < .05 ), motivational strategic 25 behaviours (ρ=.22, p < .05), and task performance (ρ=.24, p < .01)."
