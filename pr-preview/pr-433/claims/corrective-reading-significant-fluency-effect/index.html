Corrective Reading—What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report 2007¶

Corrective Reading—What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report. (2007). U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://www.ed.gov/rschstat/eval/disadv/title1interimreport/index.html

Randomized controlled trial of 79 third-grade students in eight Pennsylvania school units; the WWC findings table for the fluency domain shows an effect size of 0.27, statistically significant at α = 0.05, and an improvement index of +11 percentile points favoring Corrective Reading.
