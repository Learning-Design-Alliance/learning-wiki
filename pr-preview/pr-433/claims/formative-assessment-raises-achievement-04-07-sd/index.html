Shute 2013¶

The chapter reports, citing Black and Wiliam (1998), that "regular use of classroom formative assessment could raise student achievement by 0.4–0.7 of a standard deviation"—enough, the authors add citing Wiliam and Thompson, to place the US in the top five in international math rankings. This is a second-hand review claim, not a study conducted in the chapter.

"Studies indicate that the regular use of classroom formative assessment could raise student achievement by 0.4–0.7 of a standard deviation (Black & Wiliam, 1998)"
