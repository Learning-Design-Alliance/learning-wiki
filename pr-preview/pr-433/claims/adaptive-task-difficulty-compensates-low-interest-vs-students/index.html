Evidence¶

Sarah I. Hofer 2025¶

Sarah I. Hofer, Frank Reinhold. (2025). Scaffolding of learning activities: Aptitude-treatment-interaction effects in math?. Learning and Instruction 99 (2025) 102177. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102177

q3 · i? · causal · r2

GLMM analysis of the randomized experiment (N = 332) showed the complex interaction term for interest was significant, X2(8,68) = 28.51, p < .001, with adaptive task difficulty implemented by adapting task sets to students' previous performance.

"For interest, the hypothesized interaction could be found for VS students with a decline of the positive effect of adaptive task difficulty for highly-interested VS students (Fig. 7)."

Sarah I. Hofer 2025 (2)¶

Sarah I. Hofer, Frank Reinhold. (2025). Scaffolding of learning activities: Aptitude-treatment-interaction effects in math?. Learning and Instruction 99 (2025) 102177. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102177

q3 · i? · causal · r2

In the same GLMM analysis, the complex interaction term for self-concept was significant, X2(8,68) = 18.03, p < .05, but the direction ran opposite to the hypothesized compensatory pattern; in APS students the hypothesis held only in tendency.

"Contrary to our hypothesis, a positive effect of adaptive task difficulty was present for VS students with high self-concept (Fig. 7)."
