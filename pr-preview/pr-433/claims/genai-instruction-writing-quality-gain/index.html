Evidence¶

Allison E. Connell Pensky 2025¶

Allison E. Connell Pensky, Jordan H. Usdan, Harley Chang. (2025). Generative AI's Impact on Graduate Student Professional Writing Productivity and Quality. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00528-z

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Same within-subjects classroom study; grades on a standardized rubric (maximum 24 points) rose from a median of 91.67% to 95.83% (IQR = 8.33%). The article reports "a statistically significant impact on writing quality" with Z = −2.41, p =.02, rrb = − 0.33.

"A Wilcoxon Signed Ranks Test revealed that the within-subjects variable of writing condition (no genAI, genAI-assisted) had a statistically significant impact on writing quality, Z = −2.41, p =.02, rrb = − 0.33."
