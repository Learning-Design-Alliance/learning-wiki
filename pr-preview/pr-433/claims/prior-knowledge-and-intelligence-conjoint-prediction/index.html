Elsbeth Stern 2017¶

Elsbeth Stern (2017). Individual differences in the learning potential of human beings. npj Science of Learning 2:2. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-016-0003-0

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports longitudinal regression findings that the confounded variance of prior knowledge and intelligence outpredicts each variable alone for "learning outcome and expertise". No effect size or coefficient is printed.

"Regression analyses based on longitudinal studies have revealed that the confounded variance of prior knowledge and intelligence predicts learning outcome and expertise better than each single variable."
