q2 · i? · review · r?

The review reports, citing Jones and Byrnes (2006), that an O-P-model-based analysis including mathematical and motivational/socioemotional propensities plus classroom factors significantly explained Algebra II test performance; no effect size is printed.

"This overall model significantly explained students' performance on Algebra II tests, with propensity factors explaining a significant amount of variance in the model."
