Evidence¶

Felix Krieglstein 2022¶

Felix Krieglstein, Sascha Schneider, Maik Beege, Günter Daniel Rey. (2022). How the design and complexity of concept maps influence cognitive learning processes. Education Tech Research Dev 70, 99–118. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-022-10083-2

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

An ANOVA on instructional efficiency (van Gog & Paas formula) in the N = 122 experiment found a significant salience effect with printed effect size ηp2 = .03 ("F (1, 118) = 4.208, p = .042"), with the low-salience group more efficient; nodes (p = .956) and the interaction (p = .100) were not significant.

"Hereby, a significant main effect was found for the salience of the map structure; F (1, 118) = 4.208, p = .042, ηp 2 = .03. It indicates that students confronted with a low salience of structure were more efficient in terms of learning time."
