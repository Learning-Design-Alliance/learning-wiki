Intention-to-treat analysis of baseline-to-postintervention change in the pilot RCT (n=14 per group). Table 5 reports Cohen d=−0.32 (95% CI −1.07 to 0.42) for MHCS, −0.45 for HHI, −0.33 for ISMI-10, and −0.53 (95% CI −1.28 to 0.22) for MHRM. No formal hypothesis testing was conducted.
