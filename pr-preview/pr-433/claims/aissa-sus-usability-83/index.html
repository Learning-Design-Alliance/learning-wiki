Alvaro Becerra 2026¶

Alvaro Becerra, Diego Gomez, Ruth Cobos. (2026). AISSA: Implementation and Deployment of an AI-based Student Slides Analysis tool for Academic Presentations. LASI Spain 26: Learning Analytics Summer Institute Spain 2026. https://orcid.org/0009-0003-7793-2682

q2 · i? · design · r2

Quantitative usability assessment in the UAM pilot: the SUS was administered to all 46 students, of whom 30 submitted responses. The article reports the average SUS score of 83.38 and characterizes it as "indicating excellent perceived usability"; no effect size or inferential test is printed.
