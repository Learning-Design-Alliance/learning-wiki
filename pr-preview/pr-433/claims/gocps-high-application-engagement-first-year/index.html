Barrow 2019¶

Barrow, L., & Sartain, L. (2019). GoCPS: A first look at ninth-grade applications, offers, and enrollment. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of CPS administrative and GoCPS application data for students entering ninth grade in fall 2018. The report states "almost all CPS eighth-graders (92 percent) used GoCPS to apply to high school," with about 24,000 CPS eighth-graders plus over 2,000 out-of-district students submitting applications.

"Engagement with GoCPS was high. In fall 2017, almost all CPS eighth-graders (92 percent) used GoCPS to apply to high school."
