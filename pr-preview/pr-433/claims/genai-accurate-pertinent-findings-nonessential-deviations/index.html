q2 · i? · causal · r1

Descriptive observation comparing the AI's answers to the ePBLM's scripted content across both genAI groups. Pertinent positive findings matched; deviations appeared on headaches and drug/alcohol queries, and the AI sometimes volunteered incidental pertinent findings or embellished with rapport-building language.

"The genAI-enabled patient presented pertinent positive findings consistently with the ePBLM. Deviations occurred when the AI responded to questions for which the ePBLM content was minimal or provided a non-pertinent negative finding."
