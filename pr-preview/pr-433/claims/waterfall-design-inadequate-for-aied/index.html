Evidence¶

Bhimdiwala 2022¶

Bhimdiwala, A., Neri, R. C., & Gomez, L. M. (2022). Advancing the Design and Implementation of Artificial Intelligence in Education through Continuous Improvement. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-021-00278-8

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

A theoretical argument in the Pursuing FATE through Improvement Science section. The authors draw three implications: designers cannot discern all user needs early; design tasks become longer-term and shared; and each school shapes use and utility of an AIEd application.

"Adaptable systems like AIEd systems have numerous states that may not be, in principle, testable and study-able in constrained design periods."
