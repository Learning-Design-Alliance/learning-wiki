Related Claims¶

  • A linear mixed-effects model of detection error showed significant paper type, tool, and interaction effects, with underestimation largest for fully AI-generated papers and Pangram consistently producing the smallest errors — related
  • Applied to 1,163 real master's theses, Pangram flagged 529 (45.5%) as containing AI-generated content, mostly at low-to-moderate levels — related
  • On hybrid and humanised AI papers, Pangram correctly identified AI content in 37 of 40 cases while GPTZero and Copyleaks performed poorly and Turnitin declined on humanised texts — related
  • After humanization with Undetectable AI, fewer than 4% of AI-labeled rewrites remain detectable (FNR > 96%) for Pangram and GPTZero — reports the opposite
  • No detection threshold simultaneously keeps original flag rates, light-edit capture, and AI-pool false negatives low — related
  • In a single-institution study, the joint system of Turnitin detection plus faculty judgement formally reported only 54.5 per cent of AI-generated submissions — related
  • Blinded review identified 68% of ChatGPT-generated abstracts as chatbot-produced while 14% of authentic abstracts were misclassified as chatbot-generated — related
  • Popular plagiarism detection tools detect plagiarism in AI-assisted essays in less than 15% of cases — related
  • The system's error detection recall rate for complex rhythm errors was 91.2% ± 1.8% — related
  • Light AI-assisted edits to human-written abstracts are flagged by commercial detectors at 64–80% (Pangram) and 38–49% (GPTZero) rates — related
  • MOSS detected far fewer suspicious cases in Spring 2026 than in prior offerings, as LLM-assisted submissions lack similarity signals — related
