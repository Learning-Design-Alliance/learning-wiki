Evidence¶

Jo Tondeur 2025¶

Jo Tondeur, Ottavia Trevisan, Sarah K. Howard, Johan van Braak. (2025). Preparing preservice teachers to teach with digital technologies: An update of effective SQD-strategies. Computers & Education 232. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105262

q3 · i? · review · r2

Meta-aggregative synthesis for the Affective Dimension statement, aggregating attitudes and beliefs (n=9), emotional sphere (n=17) and sensitivity to tech gaps (n=9) sub-categories. The review reports positive beliefs are a key factor in adoption and sustained use.

"These findings suggest that affective characteristics should be taken into account when preparing preservice teachers for digital technology use in education."

Jo Tondeur 2025 (2)¶

Jo Tondeur, Ottavia Trevisan, Sarah K. Howard, Johan van Braak. (2025). Preparing preservice teachers to teach with digital technologies: An update of effective SQD-strategies. Computers & Education 232. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105262

q3 · i? · review · r2

The review reports the sensitivity-to-tech-gaps sub-category was often framed in terms of equity issues, citing qualitative evidence from Galway et al. (2020) that some participants felt app use could marginalise some students.

"Notably, a sensitivity to tech gaps extends beyond issues of device access."
