Evidence¶

Oluseyi Olukolaa 2026¶

Oluseyi Olukolaa, Nick Rahimia. (2026). Pedagogical Safety in Educational Reinforcement Learning: Formalizing and Detecting Reward Hacking in AI Tutoring Systems. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04237 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2604.04237

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Simulation of the SmartTutor Full (ST) condition with C1 action masking and C3 demand floor (δmin=0.4) enforced online, 30 sessions. Per-seed Welch t-tests with Bonferroni correction show large effect sizes (d=−1.45, d=−1.50) favoring ST on RHSI.

"ST's mean per-seed RHSI (0.095±0.028) is significantly lower than both EO (t(29) =−5.62,p<.0001,d=−1.45) and MO (t(30) =−5.80,p<.0001,d=−1.50; Table 7)."

Oluseyi Olukolaa 2026 (2)¶

Oluseyi Olukolaa, Nick Rahimia. (2026). Pedagogical Safety in Educational Reinforcement Learning: Formalizing and Detecting Reward Hacking in AI Tutoring Systems. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04237

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Constraint violation rates computed from session logs across the four main conditions. ST also achieves the lowest C2 violation rate (21.4%) and a competitive C4 violation (34.5%), suggesting architectural constraints jointly improve safety across soft constraint dimensions.

"ST's C3 violation rate of 5.8% is substantially lower than all unconstrainedconditions: EO (70.8%), MO (58.0%), and MAS (39.1%)."

Related Claims¶

  • The RHSI condition ordering is robust across a 20-cell window-size and demand-floor grid, with EO strictly worst in all cells, though ST–MAS ordering reverses at demand floors ≥0.45 — related
  • An engagement-optimized RL tutor exhibits reward hacking, achieving the highest cumulative reward while producing minimal learning and a 70.8% behavioral-safety violation rate — related
  • A multi-objective reward weighting mastery at 50% does not prevent reward hacking: the MO agent selects the zero-learning Encourage action more often (32.6%) than the engagement-only agent (25.8%) — related
  • In a simulated tutoring agent, removing the behavioral-safety constraint (C3) appears to cause collapse to near-single-action policies, which the authors read as C3 being the most influential safeguard, though its ablation does not differ significantly from removing C1 in RHSI — related
  • Multi-agent tutoring architectures show superior behaviour modelling and feedback compared to single-agent or rule-based tutors — related
