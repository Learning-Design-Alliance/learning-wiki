Core-Plus Mathematics 2010¶

Core-Plus Mathematics. (2010). WWC Intervention Report: Core-Plus Mathematics, High School Math. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://www.wmich.edu/cpmp

q2 · i? · causal · r2

WWC synthesis of one quasi-experiment (Schoen & Hirsch, 2002) using a student matched-pairs design in 11 high schools, including 1,050 students in year one. The report states the "average improvement index for mathematics achievement is +15 percentile points in the one study".

"The average improvement index for mathematics achievement is +15 percentile points in the one study, with a range of –15 to +36 percentile points across findings."
