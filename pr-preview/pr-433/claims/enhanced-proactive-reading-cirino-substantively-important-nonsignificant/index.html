Evidence¶

Enhanced Proactive Reading 2006¶

Enhanced Proactive Reading. (2006). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Randomized controlled trial (91 Hispanic first-grade English language learners, 20 classrooms, four Texas schools) of the program as a supplement to regular reading instruction. Individual effect sizes ranged from 0.06 to 0.42, all reported as ns; the study average effect size was 0.27 with improvement index +10.

"Vaughn, Cirino, et al. (2006) found no statistically significant effects for any reading achievement measures. However, five of the seven effect sizes, as well as the average effect size, were large enough to be considered substantively important using WWC criteria."
