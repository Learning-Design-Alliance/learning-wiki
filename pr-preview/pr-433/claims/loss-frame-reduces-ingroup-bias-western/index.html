Evidence¶

Yuqing Zhou 2025¶

Yuqing Zhou, Björn Lindström, Alexander Soutschek, Pyungwon Kang, Shihui Han, Philippe N. Tobler & Grit Hein. (2025). Learning reduces ingroup bias more with perceived losses than gains across cultures. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00362-x

q3 · i? · causal · r2

LMM on impression ratings of 112 Western male participants (58 Gain, 54 Loss per Fig. 3 caption). The article reports the significant "frame × group × time interaction" with χ2 = 4.78, p = 0.029; in the Gain frame the group × time interaction was not significant (χ2 = 0.42, p = 0.52).

"An LMM with frame (Ga in/Loss), group (ingroup/out- group), time (before/after learning), and their interaction terms as pre dictors, and the impression rating scores as the dependent variable revealed as i gniﬁcant frame × group × time interaction ( χ2 = 4.78, p = 0.029, β = −0.024, SE = 0.011, 95% CI = [-0.05 -0.002], Fig. 2)"

Yuqing Zhou 2025 (2)¶

Yuqing Zhou, Björn Lindström, Alexander Soutschek, Pyungwon Kang, Shihui Han, Philippe N. Tobler & Grit Hein. (2025). Learning reduces ingroup bias more with perceived losses than gains across cultures. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00362-x

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Follow-up LMMs in the Loss frame (Study 1). Ingroup impressions significantly decreased; outgroup impressions did not change significantly. Before learning ingroup exceeded outgroup (p = 0.046) but "no signiﬁcant difference between groups after learning ( χ2 = 0.01, p = 0.93)".

"Analyzing the impression changes separately for the ingroup and the outgroup revealed a signiﬁcant reduction of ingroup impressions (χ2 = 13.1, p < 0.001, β = −0.56, SE = 0.15, 95% CI = [-0.87 0.25]) while outgroup impressions did not change signiﬁcantly (χ2 = 3.65, p = 0.056, β = −0.11, SE = 0.056, 95% CI = [-0.22 0.01])"

Related Claims¶

  • Both cultures show initial ingroup bias: more positive impressions, higher expectancy and closeness for ingroup than outgroup before interaction — related
  • In East Asian participants, the Loss-frame bias reduction occurs only for participants with low ingroup identification; on average outgroup impressions improved more than ingroup impressions changed — related
  • In Western participants, stronger weight on negative ingroup prediction errors (Wneg_in) in the Loss frame predicts greater reduction of ingroup bias, driven by ingroup impression change — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • In East Asian participants, the Wneg_in parameter relates to bias reduction only when moderated by low ingroup identification
