Evidence¶

Megan Kuhfeld 2022¶

Megan Kuhfeld, James Soland, Karyn Lewis, Erik Ruzek, Angela Johnson. (2022). The COVID-19 school year: Learning and recovery across 2020-2021. AERA Open, 8. https://doi.org/10.1177/23328584221099306

q1 · i? · associational · r2

This is the authors' interpretive conclusion drawn from their trend modeling of 4.9 million students' test scores; the abstract states the findings "support the theory that the pandemic left students behind academically across the board while also worsening existing educational inequities." It is an author interpretation rather than a separately tested result.
