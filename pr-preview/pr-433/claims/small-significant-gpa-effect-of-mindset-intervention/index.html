Related Claims¶

  • Growth-mindset interventions have weak average effects on achievement, including a negligible GPA gain (d = 0.11) for lower-achieving US ninth-graders in one preregistered national trial — a broader claim this one bears on

  • Broda et al. (2018) found indeterminate effects on GPA, full-time enrollment, and credits completed in a large randomized trial — related
