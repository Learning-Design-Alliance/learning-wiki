Evidence¶

Dai 2023¶

Dai, K., Garcia, J., & Olave-Encina, K. (2023). In-between worlds: Chilean university lecturers' experiences of teaching transition between face-to-face and virtual reality contexts during the COVID-19 pandemic. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10217-0

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Discussion of the qualitative findings, describing a constant bidirectional transfer of practices. The authors state there was "a constant 'coming and going' between the two settings".

"There was a constant 'coming and going' between the two settings: they adapted pre-existing teaching practices for the SL environment, but they would also use those refined skills in a traditional classroom setting, as commented."
