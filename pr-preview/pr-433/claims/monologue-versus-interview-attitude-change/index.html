Jarrett 1953¶

Jarrett, R. F., & Sherriffs, A. C. (1953). Propaganda, Debate, and Impartial Presentation as Determiners of Attitude Change. Journal of Abnormal and Social Psychology, 48, 33–41. doi:10.1037/h0062882 (read through Allen's 1953 research abstract, doi:10.1007/bf02713265)

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Four versions of a dummy radio program were recorded and presented to 8 classes of elementary psychology students with 47 students in each group; the article's abstract states the purpose was "To compare the relative effectiveness" of the two styles.

"To compare the relative effectiveness in changing attitudes toward the Kuomintang Government of China by two typical radio styles used in informational programs: the monologue speech vs. the interview-type presenta- tion."
