q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Semi-structured interviews with four senior educators (T1-T4), each with over five years of experience teaching the simulation unit, analysed via primarily inductive qualitative content analysis with independent second coding and high interrater reliability.

"The qualitative content analysis revealed two main themes: Scenario Discrepancies (Cohen's Kappa = 0.83) and Practical Utilities (Cohen's Kappa = 0.97)."

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Educators' interpretation of the Scenario A nulls: complexity, unfamiliarity, and chaos in Scenario A versus familiarity, structure, and debriefing advantages in Scenario B, which they suggested could explain the variance in results.

"T2 pointed out that the complexity and unfamiliarity of Scenario A might contribute to the lack of associations, highlighting that students often feel lost in this scenario"
