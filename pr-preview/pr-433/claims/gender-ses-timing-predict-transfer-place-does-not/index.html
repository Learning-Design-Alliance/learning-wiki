Ana Saavedra · Daniel L. Schwartz 2026¶

Ana Saavedra · Daniel L. Schwartz. (2026). Improving the transfer of inquiry strategies by using short instructional videos. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10648-5

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Exploratory regression predicting Tuglet transfer from gender, SES, school, place, and timing without treatment; a second model adding treatments found the treatment effect significant with similar gender and SES effects (t = −3.56 and t = 3.57, both p = .000).

"We found significant effects: Females did worse (t = − 3.51, p = .000), high-SES students did better (t = 3.94, p = .000), and the latter experiments performed more poorly (t = − 3.45, p = .001). The place of administration (at home or school; t = 0.95, p = .344) did not significantly account for differences in transfer performance."
