q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis adding California Department of Social Services subsidized child care enrollment data (Table 3) to the TK/CSPP/Head Start totals. The brief reports the state "enrolled about 37% of all 4-year-olds in 2019–20 and 55% in 2023–24."

"However, subsidized child care serves a growing number and proportion of children in California. When the number of children enrolled in subsidized child care is added to the number of children enrolled in TK, CSPP, and Head Start, the state enrolled about 37% of all 4-year-olds in 2019–20 and 55% in 2023–24."
