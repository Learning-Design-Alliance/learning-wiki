Pozdniakov 2026¶

Pozdniakov, S., Brazil, J., Banihashem, S. K., Noroozi, O., Gašević, D., Sadiq, S., & Khosravi, H. (2026). AI assistance in peer feedback provision: Pedagogically sound, but minimally adopted. Computers & Education. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2026.105591

q2 · i? · associational · r2

The review reports, citing Banihashem et al. (2024), that "AI-led assistance tended to be more descriptive and lacked specific, actionable suggestions". Peer feedback more often included targeted advice important for uptake.

"They also found that AI-led assistance tended to be more descriptive and lacked specific, actionable suggestions (Banihashem et al., 2024). In contrast, peer feedback more often included targeted advice, which is important for effective feedback uptake (Lim et al., 2020; Van Der Pol et al., 2008)."
