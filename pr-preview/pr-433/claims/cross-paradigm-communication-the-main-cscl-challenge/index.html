Participants' experiential report in the third workshop group discussion: attendees found it difficult to resonate with talks using "unfamiliar theoretical frameworks and methodologies", which they argued favors siloed research and slows scientific progress and transfer into practice.

"If the strength of the field lies in the diversity of its paradigms, then the main challenge stems from communication across paradigms, not from the lack of a single paradigm."
