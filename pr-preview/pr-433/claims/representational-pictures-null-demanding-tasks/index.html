Evidence¶

Terhi Vessonen 2026¶

Terhi Vessonen, Heidi Hellstrand, Pirjo Aunio, Anu Laine. (2026). Pictures in Mathematical Word Problems: A Systematic Review on the Role of Task and Student Characteristics. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10112-5

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Systematic review synthesis of studies of representational pictures with demanding task characteristics. The review reports these studies "showed consistently null findings for representational pictures", and that these task characteristics were absent from studies showing positive effects.

"studies that included demanding types of numbers such as rational numbers, demanding operations such as probabilistic reasoning, or tasks that contained realistic considerations, showed consistently null findings for representational pictures"
