Evidence¶

Eddy Y.C. Lee 2006¶

Eddy Y.C. Lee, Carol K.K. Chan, & Jan van Aalst. (2006). Students assessing their own collaborative knowledge building. Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-006-8997-6

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

An overall weighted depth-of-inquiry score combining question quality and frequency was compared across the three Knowledge Forum classes with univariate analyses controlling for academic achievement; η² = .14, reaching the large threshold, with paired comparisons showing the portfolio-principles class "had significantly higher mean scores than both" other classes.

"univariate analyses also showed differences on the weighted scores of depth of inquiry, F (2, 107) = 8.61, p < .001,)2 = .14."
