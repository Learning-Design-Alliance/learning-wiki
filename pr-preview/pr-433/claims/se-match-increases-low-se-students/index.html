Evidence¶

Betty Tärning 2019¶

Betty Tärning, Annika Silvervarg, Agneta Gulz, Magnus Haake. (2019). Instructing a Teachable Agent with Low or High Self-Efficacy – Does Similarity Attract? International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-018-0167-2

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Analysis on the reduced dataset of 113 students (middle-SE fifth excluded), comparing matched (n = 55, Median = 1) vs. mismatched (n = 58, Median = 0) SE-change. The matched group's increase was significant after Holm correction; the mismatched group's was not.

"two one sample Mann-Whitney ’s U tests (Holm corrected for multiple measurements) revealed a significant positive effect of SE increase for the matched agent-student SE group ( V =8 0 6 ,p = .020), but not so for the mismatched group (V =7 8 1 ,p =. 2 7 ) ."
