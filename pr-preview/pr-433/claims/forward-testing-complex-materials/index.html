q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports Zhou et al.'s text comprehension study in which participants combined several pieces of information to answer comprehension questions; the quote reports the Interim Test group's advantage over restudy, supporting that interim testing boosts integration and comprehension, not just memorization.

"Zhou et al. observed that the Interim Test group substantially outperformed the Interim Restudy group, indicating that interim testing enhances text comprehension"
