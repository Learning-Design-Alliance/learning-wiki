Narrative review attributing this finding to Canning et al. (2019), whose sample the review notes "primarily consisted of White students." The review uses it to illustrate the harm of competitive spaces. No effect sizes printed.

"Canning et al. ( 2019) found that when students viewed STEM courses as competitive, it was associated with feelings of impostorship, which in turn was associated with lower engagement and grades. This association was particu-larly strong for first-generation students."
