Lauren E. Margulieux · Brendan D. Calandra · Bryan Cox 2026¶

Lauren E. Margulieux · Brendan D. Calandra · Bryan Cox. (2026). Online professional development to support K‑12 teachers' computer science integration. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10640-z

q2 · i? · design · r2

Bottom-up content analysis of 50 randomly selected responses per question from pre- and post-surveys, coded by two authors. Problem-solving was the most common description on both surveys (20 of 50 pre; 19 of 50 post), and "relating CT to teaching (6 of 50 responses)" emerged only post.

"A new type of response on the post-survey that did not exist in the pre-survey responses was relating CT to teaching (6 of 50 responses). These responses categorized CT as a strategy for using technology in instructional practice"
