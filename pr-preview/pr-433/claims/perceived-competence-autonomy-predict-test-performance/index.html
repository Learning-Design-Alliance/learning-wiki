Evidence¶

Coppens 2019¶

Coppens, L. C., Hoogerheide, V., Snippe, E. M., Flunger, B., & van Gog, T. (2019). Effects of problem–example and example–problem pairs on gifted and nongifted primary school students' learning. Instructional Science, 47, 279–297. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-019-09484-3

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

Exploratory ANCOVAs with task sequence as independent variable and perceived autonomy and competence as covariates on posttest performance; "participants who rated their competence higher performed better on the isomorphic posttest problems", η²p = .05; autonomy predicted transfer performance, F(1, 120) = 5.58, p = .020, η²p = .04.

"there was a main effect of perceived competence [F (1, 120) = 5.70, p = .018, /u1D7022 p = .05 ], indicating that participants who rated their competence higher performed better on the isomorphic posttest problems"
