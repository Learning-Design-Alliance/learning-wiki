Evidence¶

Ioanna Taouki 2022¶

Ioanna Taouki, Marie Lallier, David Soto. (2022). The role of metacognition in monitoring performance and regulating learning in early readers. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09292-0

q3 · i2 · associational · r2

Single-subject Spearman correlations among M-ratio estimates (N=49, FDR-corrected, age and IQ covariates) showed a strong lexical decision–emotion recognition correlation; the Bayesian hierarchical model gave group-level covariance ρ=0.842, 95% HDI=[0.57, 0.99]. A weaker VA span–lexical decision correlation (r=0.355, p=0.022) had only anecdotal Bayes support.

"A strong positive correlation was observed between metacognitive efficiency on the orthographic lexical decision task and the emotion recognition task (r=0.577, p<0.001)."

Ioanna Taouki 2022 (2)¶

Ioanna Taouki, Marie Lallier, David Soto. (2022). The role of metacognition in monitoring performance and regulating learning in early readers. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09292-0

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Task-level comparison of metacognitive efficiency across the three analysed tasks (VA span, lexical decision, emotion recognition) found no significant differences; posterior-difference HDIs for all task pairs overlapped zero (e.g., lexical decision–emotion recognition 95% HDI=[-0.42, 0.17]).

"A non -parametric Friedman test wi th task as a within -subjects factor was conducted and rendered a Chi-square value of 2.00, which showed no main effect of task on the metacognitive efficiency of the participants (p=0.368)."
