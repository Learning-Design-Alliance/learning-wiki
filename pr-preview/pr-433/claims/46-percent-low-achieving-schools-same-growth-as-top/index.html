Andy Hegedus 2018¶

Observational comparison of the top 5% versus bottom 5% achieving schools (selected on Spring 2016 MAP Growth achievement) and their growth distributions (Figures 2.5–2.6). The article reports "about 46% of schools at the lowest end of the spring achievement distribution have the same growth as schools at the highest end," while noting the top 5% achieving schools tend to grow students more than the bottom 5%.

"The growth distributions reveal that about 46% of schools at the lowest end of the spring achievement distribution have the same growth as schools at the highest end."
