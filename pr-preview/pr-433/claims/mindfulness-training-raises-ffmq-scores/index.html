Kou 2025¶

Kou, H., Luo, W., Li, X. et al. (2025). Mindfulness training enhances face working memory: evidence from the drift-diffusion model. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00389-0

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Five Facet Mindfulness Questionnaire (39 items, five subscales) administered at baseline, post-training, and follow-up in the randomized experiment. The training group showed "a significantly larger improvement in dispositional mindfulness than the control group" at T2 (Cohen's d=.41) and the effect persisted at T3 (t(118)=2.39, p=.018, d=.44).
