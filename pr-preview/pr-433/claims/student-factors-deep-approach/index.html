Evidence¶

Baeten 2010¶

Baeten, M., Kyndt, E., Struyven, K., & Dochy, F. (2010). Using student-centred learning environments to stimulate deep approaches to learning: Factors encouraging or discouraging their effectiveness. Educational Research Review, 5, 243–260. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2010.06.001

q2 · i? · review · r3

Narrative review synthesis of demographic correlates of approaches to learning. The review notes a minority of cited studies "did not ﬁnd a signiﬁcant relationship between age and learning approach", calling them rather exceptions.

"Studies that have taken the variable age into account mainly showed that age was positively related to a deep approach and negatively to a surface approach"

q2 · i? · review · r2

Review synthesis of nine correlational studies on personality and approaches to learning. Similar patterns were reported for extraversion, conscientiousness and agreeableness, with reverse relationships for neuroticism.

"Main ﬁndings of the correlational analyses in these studies showed that openness to experience (e.g. being imaginative, artistically sensitive, and intellectually curious) was signiﬁcantly positively related to a deep approach and signiﬁcantly negatively to a surface approach."

q2 · i? · review · r2

Review synthesis of studies interrelating motivation and approaches to learning, citing autonomous motivation, motive for success and mastery-approach goals alongside intrinsic orientation; surface approaches were associated with extrinsic orientation, controlled motivation and fear of failure.

"a deep approach was mainly associated with intrinsic (goal) orientation"

Baeten 2010 (4)¶

Baeten, M., Kyndt, E., Struyven, K., & Dochy, F. (2010). Using student-centred learning environments to stimulate deep approaches to learning: Factors encouraging or discouraging their effectiveness. Educational Research Review, 5, 243–260. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2010.06.001

q2 · i? · review · r2

Review synthesis of correlational studies on self-confidence, self-efficacy and approaches to learning, contrasted with findings that fear of failure and motivation to avoid failure relate to surface approaches.

"By contrast, if students are more self-conﬁdent and have higher self-efﬁcacy, they are usually inclined towards the use of a deep approach and less towards the use of a surface approach"

Related Claims¶

  • Personal resources have a reciprocal relationship with study resources and relate to engagement and achievement — related
  • Student teachers' beliefs about sources of knowledge predict their learning approaches and motivation in teacher education — related
