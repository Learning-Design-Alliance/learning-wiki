Subgroup analysis in Table 1 of 2005 versus 2013 on-track rates by EXPLORE category and race/ethnicity-gender. The bottom EXPLORE quartile gained "a 24.5 percentage point increase," and African American males rose from 43 to 71 percent.

"Students with incoming EXPLORE scores less than 12—the bottom quartile of CPS students—had a 24.5 percentage point increase in their on-track rates. On-track rates improved more among African American males than among any other racial/ethnic gender subgroup, rising from 43 percent in 2005 to 71 percent in 2013."
