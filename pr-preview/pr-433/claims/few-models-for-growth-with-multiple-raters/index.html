James Soland 2021¶

James Soland, Megan Kuhfeld. (2021). A multi-rater latent growth curve model. Multivariate Behavioral Research. https://doi.org/10.1080/00273171.2021.1925076

q1 · i? · design · r2

The abstract asserts, as motivation, that "few if any models are available to estimate growth in the presence of multiple raters," despite existing multi-rater measurement models including a small number for longitudinal data. This is the authors' literature assessment without cited evidence in the available text.

"While a range of models to address measurement issues that arise when using multiple raters have been presented, including a small number for longitudinal data, few if any models are available to estimate growth in the presence of multiple raters."
