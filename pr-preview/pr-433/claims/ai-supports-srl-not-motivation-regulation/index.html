Jennifer Lock 2025¶

Jennifer Lock, Soraya Arteaga, Carol Johnson. (2025). Integrating AI in Online Learning in Higher Education: A Literature Review. International Journal on Innovations in Online Education, 9(1). https://doi.org/10.1615/IntJInnovOnlineEdu.2026060433

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Jin et al. (2023), that "learners perceived AI applications as useful for supporting metacognitive, cognitive, and behavioral regulation" across SRL areas but not motivation. The review also reports Jin et al. found learner identity, activeness, and position necessary for AI-supported SRL. No effect size is printed.
