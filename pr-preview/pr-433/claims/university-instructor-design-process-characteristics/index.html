Karakaya 2021¶

Karakaya, K. (2021). Design considerations in emergency remote teaching during the COVID-19 pandemic: a human-centered approach. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09884-0

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The commentary reports Bennett et al.'s qualitative study of 30 instructors at 16 Australian universities using semi-structured interviews, which "pinpoints key design characteristics as top-down, breadth-first, iterative, responsive, and reflective."

"This exploratory study provides rich, contextualized descriptions about university teachers' design process and pinpoints key design characteristics as top-down, breadth-first, iterative, responsive, and reflective."
