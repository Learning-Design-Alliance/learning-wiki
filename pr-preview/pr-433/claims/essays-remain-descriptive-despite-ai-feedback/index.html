Andres Karjus 2026¶

Andres Karjus, Janika Leoste, Tiia Õun. (2026). Student Evaluation of Repeated AI Feedback Across a Semester of Writing. arXiv:2607.16115 [cs.CY]. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16115 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.16115

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Coding of essay reflection depth with a Hatton–Smith-derived four-category scheme in the semester corpus. Most coded essays were descriptive reflection, connecting course content to personal experience but rarely moving into sustained alternative perspectives or critique.

"Dialogic reﬂection occurred in 5% of essays, and the deepest category, critical reﬂection, in less than 1%."
