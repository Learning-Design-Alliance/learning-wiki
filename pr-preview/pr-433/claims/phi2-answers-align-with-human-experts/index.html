q3 · i? · design · r2

In the human evaluation study of 64 generated MCQs, average pairwise Cohen's kappa between Phi-2 and the seven human judges was 0.76 (SE = 0.03) versus a human-human baseline of 0.70. Phi-2's answers were "even more aligned with human experts than the experts were with each other".

"The pairwise agreement between Phi-2 and human judges was higher than that between human judges (mean Cohen's kappa = 0.76, SE = 0.03), suggesting that Phi-2's answers were even more aligned with human experts than the experts were with each other."

phi2-answer-agreement-2¶

Yumou Wei, John Stamper, and Paulo F. Carvalho. 2026. Generate-Then-Validate: A Novel Question Generation Approach Using Small Language Models. In LAK26: 16th International Learning Analytics and Knowledge Conference (LAK 2026), Bergen, Norway. ACM, New York, NY, USA. https://doi.org/10.1145/3785022.3785100

q3 · i? · design · r2

Against the human majority vote treated as ground truth, Phi-2 answered 58 of 64 generated MCQs correctly (90.6% accuracy); both models erred on the same four MCQs. The article reports raw accuracy, not an effect size.

"Phi-2 had an accuracy of 90.6% (58 out of 64), while Gemini had an accuracy of 89.1% (57 out of 64). Both models made the same incorrect predictions on the same four MCQs."
