Tim Coughlan 2020 (2)¶

Tim Coughlan. (2020). The use of open data as a material for learning. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-019-09706-y

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Interview finding on authentic experiences: educators such as G described unpredictability from a real plant genetics database, in contrast to contrived textbook examples, supporting the view that provenance "offers authenticity for the exploration of rich and complex issues".

"the real-world provenance of the data, and the processes through which it is collected, offers authenticity for the exploration of rich and complex issues"
