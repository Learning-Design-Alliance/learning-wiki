Mahar 2006¶

Mahar, M. T., Murphy, S. K., Rowe, D. A., Golden, J., Shields, A. T., & Raedeke, T. D. (2006). Effects of a Classroom-Based Program on Physical Activity and On-Task Behavior. Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise, 38(12), 2086-2094. https://doi.org/10.1249/01.mss.0000235359.16685.a3

Within the same multiple-baseline observation study of 62 students, the subgroup of least on-task students improved "on-task behavior by 20% after Energizers activities", significant at P < 0.001 with ES = 2.20.

"Likewise, the least on-task students improved on-task behavior by 20% after Energizers activities. This improvement was statistically significant (P < 0.001) and meaningful (ES = 2.20)."
