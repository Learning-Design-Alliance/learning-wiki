Evidence¶

Dell 1911¶

Dell, John A. (1911). Some Observations on the Learning of Sensible Material. Journal of Educational Psychology, 2, 401-406. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0074801

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experiment: a lesson on buds was given independently to two forms (16 and 21 boys) with the three groups of facts arranged in different orders, and each class was tested by ten oral questions at its next meeting. Percentages of correct answers per section were computed (Table III), showing position "occupied different positions in the lessons given to the two forms" yet did not drive recall.

"shows that the probability of obtaining a correct answer is greatest for a question in Section II, next greatest for Section I and least for Section III, although the subject-matter of these sections occupied different positions in the lessons given to the two forms."
