q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative case description from teacher partner Ms. F, a fourth-grade teacher, who used a Mathspace smart shape fraction diagram that updated immediately with different denominator inputs; Figure 6 shows a static example of the fraction circles. Finding is based on teacher report and artifact, not measured student outcomes.

"Students could see in this dynamic representation that the number of equal parts and the size of those parts was changing, while the size of the whole remained the same."
