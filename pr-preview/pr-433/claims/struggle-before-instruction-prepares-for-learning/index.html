Fries 2020¶

Fries, L., Son, J. Y., Givvin, K. B., & Stigler, J. W. (2020). Practicing Connections: A Framework to Guide Instructional Design for Developing Understanding in Complex Domains. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09561-x

Narrative review claim across multiple research paradigms (citing Hiebert et al., Schwartz and Bransford, Capon and Kuhn, Lawson et al.): grappling with a task helps learners identify gaps and prepares them for subsequent explicit instruction. No effect sizes are printed.
