International comparison results track TIMSS-PIRLS and PISA trends in other countries and regions. Country examples include the UK (m 0.36sd vs rd 0.06sd) and New Zealand (0.17sd vs –0.04sd), with progress "with one exception always greater in m than in rd".

"The 4th-grade TIMSS-PIRLS surveys show larger cohort advances in m (0.24sd) than in rd (0.05sd). When TIMSS-PIRLS samples are restricted to industrialized nations (OECD members), larger differences between m (0.21sd) and rd (0.02sd) trends are observed."
