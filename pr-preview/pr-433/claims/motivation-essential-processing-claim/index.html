Eitel 2026¶

Eitel, A., Kienitz, A., Krebs, M.-C., & Schöne, C. (2026). Advocating the FoRe-Squares Model of Technology-Augmented Instruction: a Reply to Mayer (2025). Educational Psychology Review, 38, 65. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10163-2

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical argument in the reply, departing from Mayer's position that motivational processes are mainly critical for generative processing. The authors argue essential processing "can be understood as involving a motivated component—a decision to invest mental effort."

"Without some minimal degree of subjective value (e.g., curiosity, relevance for one's goals, avoidance of negative outcomes), we propose that the cognitive system is unlikely to reliably initiate or sustain the conscious selection and organization of incoming information, even at the level of essential processing."
