Oladayo 2026¶

Assessing the Influence of AI-Generated Responses on Academic Achievement: An Ethical Perspective and Self-Efficacy of Delta State Secondary School Students, Nigeria. Journal of Research in Mathematics, Science, and Technology Education, 3(4), 276–288. https://doi.org/10.70232/jrmste.v3i4.72

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental study with intact classes: School A (62 students) exposed to AI-generated responses, School B (53 students) taught conventionally, assessed with the Quadratic Equation Achievement Test. The study found "students exposed to AI-generated responses achieved higher performance"; no effect size is printed.

"The findings revealed that students exposed to AI-generated responses achieved higher performance than those taught through conventional teaching methods."

Discussion¶

Achievement was measured with the authors' own Quadratic Equation Achievement Test (QEAT), so the result rests on an instrument built for this study.
