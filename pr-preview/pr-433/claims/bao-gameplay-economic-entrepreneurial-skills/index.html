Rebecca Y. Bayeck 2026¶

Rebecca Y. Bayeck. (2026). Exploring learning in classic African board games: a review of recent literature. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-026-10733-9

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Mkondiwa (2020a), a close connection between Bao gameplay in Malawi, Tanzania, Kenya and East Africa and the economic decision-making skills of rural players, with the game principle kutakata teaching planning for future capture: "invest- ing for the long term".

"Bao facilitates the learning of basic economics, that is, invest- ing for the long term (Mkondiwa, 2020a)."

Rebecca Y. Bayeck 2026 (2)¶

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Mkondiwa (2020b), an empirical study that investigated the association of Bao gameplay with entrepreneurial skills, supposing Bao players developed entrepreneurial skills used in everyday life.

"Mkondiwa (2020b) points to Bao potential for entrepreneurship training."
