Silvia Robles 2021¶

Silvia Robles, Max Gross, Robert W. Fairlie. (2021). The Effect of Course Shutouts on Community College Students: Evidence from Waitlist Cutoffs. Journal of Public Economics, vol. 199. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/the-effect-of-course-shutouts-on-community-college-students-evidence-from-waitlist-cutoffs doi:10.1016/j.jpubeco.2021.104409

q3 · i? · causal · r2

The authors' overall interpretation of the waitlist-cutoff findings: reduced course availability (shutouts) both increased the likelihood of taking zero courses and increased transfers to nearby two-year colleges, offering "some evidence" of trajectory disruption.

"These results offer some evidence that course availability can disrupt community college students’ educational trajectories."
