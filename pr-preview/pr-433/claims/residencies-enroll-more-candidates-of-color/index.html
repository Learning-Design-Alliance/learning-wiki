The report cites a national study comparing residency and traditional pathway diversity in 2016–17: "41% of teacher residents identified as people of color" versus 28% in traditional programs. The report narrates this secondhand; the underlying study was not examined directly.

"One national study found that in 2016–17, 41% of teacher residents identified as people of color,31 compared to 28% candidates of color in traditional programs nationwide in the same year."
