Evidence¶

Darnon 2006¶

Darnon, C., Muller, D., Schrager, S. M., Pannuzzo, N., & Butera, F. (2006). Mastery and Performance Goals Predict Epistemic and Relational Conflict Regulation. Journal of Educational Psychology, 98(4), 766–776. https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.98.4.766

q3 · i3 · associational · r1

Regression analysis in Study 1 (51 participants recalling Milgram's experiment, imagining a disagreeing partner) showed "mastery goals significantly and positively predicted the reported amount of epistemic conflict regulation" (B = 0.41, η² = .27), with performance goals non-significant (B = −0.15, p = .30).
