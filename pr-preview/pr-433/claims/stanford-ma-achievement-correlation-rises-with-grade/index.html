Arthur I. Gates 1923¶

Arthur I. Gates. (1923). The Correlations of Achievement in School Subjects with Intelligence Tests and Other Variables (Concluded). The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0066477

Grade-by-grade comparison of achievement correlations with Stanford MA and verbal group tests (Tables X-XII). The article reports "the highest grades show the highest coefficients" for MA, while group test correlations are similar across grades; no effect size is printed for the rise itself.
