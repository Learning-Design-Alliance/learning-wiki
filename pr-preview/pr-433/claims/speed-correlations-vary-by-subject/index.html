Evidence¶

Bird 1921¶

Bird, Grace E. (1921). The Devious Path of Slow Work. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ123456

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Bird reports a previous experiment of hers giving standard tests to a whole school, finding positive speed correlations in reading and arithmetic, but a minus correlation between speed and legibility in handwriting, "probably because the children had been trained to write slowly."

"the highest correlations between tests occurred between comprehension and speed in Kansas Silent Reading and between speed and accuracy in Courtis arithmetic. In handwriting, however, a minus correlation was found between speed and legibility"
