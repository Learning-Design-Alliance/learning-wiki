Evidence¶

Dirk Ifenthaler 2020¶

Dirk Ifenthaler, Jane Yin-Kim Yau. (2020). Utilising learning analytics to support study success in higher education: a systematic review. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09788-z

q2 · i? · review · r2

Synthesis of the predictors reported in the 46 key publications (Table 1). The review reports that "One set of predictors is based on data collected through online behaviour, mainly logfiles and trace-data", including forum interactions and engagement with learning artefacts.

"One set of predictors is based on data collected through online behaviour, mainly logfiles and trace-data. This includes forum interactions (e.g., posts, replies, length of posts)"

Dirk Ifenthaler 2020 (2)¶

Dirk Ifenthaler, Jane Yin-Kim Yau. (2020). Utilising learning analytics to support study success in higher education: a systematic review. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-020-09788-z

q2 · i? · review · r3

Synthesis of background-variable predictors across the key publications, citing studies using enrolment age, scholarships, GPA, financial background and academic history. The review also notes survey-based predictors such as self-reported expected grades and motivation.

"another set of data used for predicting study success is based on students' background information, such as demographics (e.g., age, gender), socio-economic status (e.g., family income, background, expenditure), prior academic experience and performance"
