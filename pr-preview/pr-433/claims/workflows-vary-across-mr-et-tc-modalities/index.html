Rakib 2025¶

Rakib, M. A. N., Akter, S., Sridhar, E. P., Biswas, S., Raihan, M. R., & Rahman, M. (2025). Visualizing Engineering Fundamentals: Design of Mixed Reality and Physical Toolkits for Effective Learning. q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

In a 90-minute user study, 24 UTA students who had completed Engineering Statics were assigned to TC, ET, or MR conditions after a recorded lecture; open-ended responses were thematically coded by three researchers. Participants reported that "learners’ workflows and engagement with fundamental mechanics problems varied across instructional modalities."

"Thematic analysis of participant feedback revealed that learners’ workflows and engagement with fundamental mechanics problems varied across instructional modalities."
