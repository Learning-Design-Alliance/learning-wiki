Tsvetomila Mihaylova 2026¶

Tsvetomila Mihaylova, Evanfiya Logacheva, Arto Hellas, Jing Fan, Francisco Castro, Bita Akram, Narges Norouzi, Peter Brusilovsky, Juho Leinonen. (2026). The Effects of Structured LLM-Generated Feedback on Programming Assignment Performance. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16933 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.16933

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Mixed-effects model with hint type × student level interaction on log time to success (Figure 2, Table 10). Students were split as Novice (below 3 years experience; 111 students) or Advanced (over three years; 38 students) from self-reported experience. Interaction confidence intervals included zero.

"Single-Issue Feedback and Diagnostic Feedback are associated with improvement in the time for completion. In contrast, General Feedback does not show a clear effect. The main effect of student level is not statistically significant once hint type is accounted for."

Related Claims¶

  • Advanced students showed reduced attempts with all hint types, while novices did not clearly benefit and made slightly more submissions with Single-Issue Feedback — related
  • All three LLM-generated feedback types are associated with faster time to solution than a compiler-errors-only baseline, with Single-Issue Feedback showing the largest reduction — related
  • All three LLM feedback types credibly reduced attempts to success relative to the baseline, with the largest reduction for Diagnostic Feedback — related
  • Feedback type did not affect whether students solved assignments; solution rates were very similar across all conditions — related
