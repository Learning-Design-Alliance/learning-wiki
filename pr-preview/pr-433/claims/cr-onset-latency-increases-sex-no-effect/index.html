Evidence¶

Robert Winton 2025¶

Robert Winton, Meenam Pious & Anders Rasmussen. (2025). Training on multiple days results in better learning in embedded eyeblink conditioning in young human adults. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00347-w

q3 · i? · causal · r1

A separate linear mixed effects model with the formula 'CR onset~ Day + Sex + (1|Subject ID)' tested onset latency across the three days in the 45-participant study. Day significantly affected onset variation; sex showed no significant effect on CR onset latency (p = 0.98942) or CR percentage (p = 0.96203).

"The results showed that the day had a signiﬁcant effect, and the CR onset variation tended to increase over 3 days ( t =3 . 0 4 ,p = 0.002, CI = 2.554, 11.886). Sex did not have signiﬁcant effect on CR onset latency (p = 0.98942) or CR percentage (p = 0.96203)."
