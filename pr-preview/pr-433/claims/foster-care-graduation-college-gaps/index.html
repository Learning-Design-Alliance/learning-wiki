Burns 2022¶

Burns, D., Espinoza, D., Adams, J., & Ondrasek, N. (2022). California students in foster care: Challenges and promising practices. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/118.471

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of 2018–19 California education data reported in the Executive Summary. The report states "Students in foster care graduated at lower rates (56%) than youth not in foster care (85%)" and reports college attendance of 48% versus 64% among completers.

"Students in foster care graduated at lower rates (56%) than youth not in foster care (85%). Among graduates and other high school completers, students in foster care were less likely than their peers to attend college (48% vs. 64%)."
