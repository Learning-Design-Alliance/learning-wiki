Daniusevičiūtė-Brazaitė L 2026¶

Daniusevičiūtė-Brazaitė L, Gaižiūnienė L and Pučėtaitė R (2026) AI in inclusive education: teachers' visions of future-oriented practices. Front. Educ. 11:1890925. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1890925

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Deductive qualitative and quantitative content analysis of 50 teacher essays coded with the Future Scaffolding Skills Framework. The article reports "Broad5: 24%, Broad1: 20%, Broad2: 18%" as the most frequent broadening markers.

"The findings under the broadening markers showed an increased variety of strategies for addressing FoS, enhanced knowledge of futures thinking, and broader perspectives on conceptualizing FoS (Broad5: 24%, Broad1: 20%, Broad2: 18%; see Table 2)."
