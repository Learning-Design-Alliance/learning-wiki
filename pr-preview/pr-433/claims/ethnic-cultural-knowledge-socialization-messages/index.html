Evidence¶

Kim Yeonwoo 2018¶

Kim Yeonwoo, Calzada Esther J., Barajas-Gonzalez R. Gabriela, Huang Keng-Yen, Brotman Laurie M., Castro Ashley, Pichardo Catherine. (2018). The Role of Authoritative and Authoritarian Parenting in the Early Academic Achievement of Latino Students. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000192

q3 · i? · associational · r2

SEM results (MA: independence .22, respeto .16; DA: independence .29, respeto .42). The article notes higher acculturation and enculturation appear associated indiscriminately with higher socialization to both U.S. and Latino values, and speculates both may capture a mother's underlying approach to social interactions.

"For both MA and DA mothers, ethnic cultural knowledge was associated with more socialization to the U.S. American value of independence and to the cultural value of respeto, and socialization messages of independence, in turn, was associated with more authoritative parenting."
