Twyford 1959¶

Results of the National Program's nation-wide experiment involving 40,000 students in more than 200 elementary and secondary schools, with thirteen experiments undertaken and achievement measured by standardized tests. The printed counts favor television classes over control classes.

"Of the comparisons between con- trol and experimental groups, 68 fav- ored television and 42 favored control classes. Where the differences were sig- nificant, 29 were in favor of television classes and nine in favor of the control groups."
