Report narrative on the summer 2021 data dive sessions in which Digital Promise researchers shared disaggregated grade data with course teams. The report states "students of color and Pell-eligible students were passing at lower average rates than their peers" in some courses, prompting discussions of targeted supports.

q2 · i? · design · r2

Report's equity-practice discussion of data access barriers: faculty lacked access to disaggregated student data until RPP researchers obtained it from institutional research offices, and the "data dives were actionable in new and meaningful ways" as a result.

"Many of the faculty had never before seen their own course success data disaggregated by student population, so insights from the data dives were actionable in new and meaningful ways."
