Evidence¶

Jade Mai Cock · Ido Roll · Käser Tanja 2025¶

Jade Mai Cock · Ido Roll · Käser Tanja. (2025). Different Topics, Similar Patterns: The Consistency of Learners and Learning Across Interactive STEM Simulations. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00512-7

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Mann–Whitney–Wilcoxon comparison of prior-knowledge distributions between clusters (RQ2 analysis) found the free Beer's Law cluster had significantly more learners with no prior knowledge (p=0.0155), while for Capacitor Lab the reverse pattern was not significant (p=0.11).

"the free cluster had a consequently larger number of learners coming in with no prior knowledge across variables (low: 0.99, medium: 0.01, high: 0.0) than the systematic cluster (low: 0.88, medium: 0.12, high: 0.0, p=0.0155)"
