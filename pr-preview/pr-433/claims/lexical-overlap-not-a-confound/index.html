Follow-up analysis addressing the potential lexical overlap between training material and test sentences. The correlation between sentence-training time and audiovisual learning effects "showed no signi ﬁcant effect ( r = 0.04, p = 0.837)", which the authors interpret as support that lexical overlap was not a relevant confound.
