Evidence¶

Ard W. Lazonder 2009¶

Ard W. Lazonder, Pascal Wilhelm, Emiel van Lieburg. (2009). Unraveling the influence of domain knowledge during simulation-based inquiry learning. Instr Sci, 37, 437-451. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-008-9055-8

q3 · i? · causal · r2

In the randomized experiment, time on task (means 28.76, 25.50, and 32.56 minutes across concrete, intermediate, and abstract conditions) showed no effect of task type; equivalence was not tested.

"Univariate analysis of variance (ANOVA) showed that task type had no effect on this measure ( F(2,53) = 1.93, MSE = 123.19, p = .16), indicating that participants in the three conditions needed as much time to complete their version of the task."

Ard W. Lazonder 2009 (2)¶

Ard W. Lazonder, Pascal Wilhelm, Emiel van Lieburg. (2009). Unraveling the influence of domain knowledge during simulation-based inquiry learning. Instr Sci, 37, 437-451. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-008-9055-8

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Same experiment: a MANOVA on unique and duplicated experiment counts showed no significant task-type effect. A separate univariate ANOVA on unspecified hypotheses was also non-significant (F(2,52) = 1.49, p = .24).

"Multivariate analysis of variance (MANOVA) yielded no signiﬁcant effect of task type on the number of unique and duplicated experiments ( F(4,106) = 1.25, p = .29)."
