Box-plot analysis of the score distribution shows the narrowing is strongest for relatively low-performing students: at the 25th percentile the gap falls from 23 points to "seven points (229 vs. 236)" between third and eighth grade. No standardized effect size is printed for this percentile contrast.

"In third-grade reading, for example, CPS students lag behind the rest of the state by 23 points (168 vs. 191). By eighth grade, that difference is down to seven points (229 vs. 236)."
