Evidence¶

Ahlem Ammar and Nina Spada 2006¶

Ahlem Ammar and Nina Spada. (2006). ONE SIZE FITS ALL?: Recasts, Prompts, and L2 Learning. Studies in Second Language Acquisition, Volume 28, Issue 4. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0272263106060268

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Quasi-experimental study of 64 grade 6 intensive ESL learners in three intact classes assigned to recast, prompt, or control conditions. The article reports that "prompts were more effective than recasts" overall for learning his and her. No effect size is printed in the available text.

"Results also indicated that, overall, prompts were more effective than recasts and that the effectiveness of recasts depended on the learners' proficiency."
