Bol L and Hacker DJ 2012¶

Bol L and Hacker DJ (2012) Calibration research: where do we go from here? Front. Psychology 3:229. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2012.00229

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

Narrative review attributing to Scheck and Nelson (2005) the hypothesis that "people form a psychological anchor for their JOLs somewhere between 30 and 50% of correct recall," used to explain the underconfidence-with-practice effect. No effect sizes are printed.

"Scheck and Nelson (2005) hypothesized that people form a psychological anchor for their JOLs somewhere between 30 and 50% of correct recall and adjust their JOLs either upwards or downwards based on whether performance is above or below this band."
