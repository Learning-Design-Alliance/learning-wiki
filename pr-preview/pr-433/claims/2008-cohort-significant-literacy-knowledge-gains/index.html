Pufpaff 2010¶

Pufpaff, L. A., & Yssel, N. (2010). Effects of a 6-Week, Co-Taught Literacy Unit on Preservice Special Educators' Literacy-Education Knowledge. Psychology in the Schools, 47(5). https://doi.org/10.1002/pits.20485

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Pretest-posttest study of the 2008 cohort (n = 10) in the co-taught literacy unit. The mean pretest was 12.70 (SD = 3.16) and the mean posttest was 18.10 (SD = 3.11) of a maximum possible score of 26, with "positive gain scores ranged from 1 to 11 with a mean of 5.4 points."

"In 2008 ( n= 10), the mean score for the pretest was 12.70 (standard deviation [SD] = 3.16; range = 9 - 17) whereas the mean score for the posttest was 18.10 ( SD = 3.11; range = 15 - 23) of a maximum possible score of 26."
