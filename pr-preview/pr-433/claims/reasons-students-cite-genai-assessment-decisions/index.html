Across the survey, interviews, and assignment declarations in three teacher education courses, the study reports that "the assessment design, the perceived value of the assessment, students’ self-confidence and concerns about being wrongly accused of plagiarism were the most frequently cited reasons" for GenAI adoption decisions.
