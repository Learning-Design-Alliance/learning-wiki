q2 · i? · design · r3

Summative usability study (Phase 3) with seven UAE-based special-education practitioners using think-aloud tasks and the SUS. The article reports that "The mean SUS score was 86.8" and that overall ease of use received a mean rating of 5.00. No effect size is printed.

"The mean SUS score was 86.8, which corresponds to an "A" grade on established benchmarks, suggesting strong perceived usability among this group of practitioners."
