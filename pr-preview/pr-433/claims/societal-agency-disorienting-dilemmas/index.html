The Critical 2007¶

The Critical, Relational Practice of Instructional Design in Higher Education: An Emerging Model of Change Agency. (2007). https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-007-9061-6

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Narrative inquiry findings; designers located core values in early socialization through parents, teachers and colleagues, and several traced design decisions to "disorienting dilemmas" that reframed core beliefs. Examples include designing cases giving marginalized populations authoritative roles and work with First Nations communities.

"Societal agency is characterized by a need to see beyond the confines of immediate work to know that design is contributing to a larger, more significant societal influence."
