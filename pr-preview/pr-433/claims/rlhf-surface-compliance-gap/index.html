Peng Wang 2026¶

Peng Wang, Fred Paas. (2026). Five Educational-Psychology Lenses for Training and Evaluating AI Models. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10195-8

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The article's analysis of RLHF as feedback-based socialization reports that human evaluators' judgments create a reward signal shaping model outputs. It likens the result to superficial socialization, citing critics who call RLHF a behavioral patch achieving behavior modification without character development.

"In effect, it rapidly shapes model outputs toward rater-endorsed norms, which can improve surface-level compliance while leaving open questions about principle-level generalization and robustness across contexts."
