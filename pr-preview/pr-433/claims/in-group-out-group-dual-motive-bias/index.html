Gehlbach 2018¶

Gehlbach, H., & Vriesema, C. C. (2018). Meta‐bias: A practical theory of motivated thinking. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-018-9454-6

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Theoretical argument in the article's Efficient Self-Enhancement section: building on stereotyping, perceivers categorize people as in-group or out-group and tend to favor the in-group, often to the point of discrimination or derogation of the out-group, serving both meta-biases at once. No new empirical test is reported.

"Perhaps the best example of a bias that serves both motives simultaneously is in-group/out-group bias."
