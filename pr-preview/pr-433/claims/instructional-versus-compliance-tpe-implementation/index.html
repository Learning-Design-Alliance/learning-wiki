Evidence¶

Slotnik 2015¶

Slotnik, W. J., Bugler, D., & Liang, G. (2015). Change in Practice in Maryland: Student Learning Objectives and Teacher and Principal Evaluation. Mid-Atlantic Comprehensive Center (WestEd and Community Training and Assistance Center). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED568386

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Report finding drawing on key leader interviews, focus groups, and survey data across districts. In instructional-focus districts, “educators see more value of the evaluation results in promoting instructional conversations and measuring teacher performance.”

"When the implementation of TPE is driven to a greater extent by complying with mandates, there is generally more stress for teachers related to achieving the “highly effective” rating and a greater sense of loss of the reflection dimension of evaluation."
