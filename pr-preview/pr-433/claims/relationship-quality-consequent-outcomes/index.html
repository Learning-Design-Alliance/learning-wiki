Evidence¶

Jowett 2017¶

Jowett, S. (2017). Coaching Effectiveness: The Coach-Athlete Relationship at its Heart. Current Opinion in Psychology, 16, 154-158. http://dx.doi.org/10.1016/j.copsyc.2017.05.006

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review summarises quantitative research on consequent variables of relationship quality, reporting that athletes with good quality relationships receive better coaching from their coaches. Separate cited studies link quality to satisfaction, motivation, physical self-concept, team cohesion and collective efficacy; no effect sizes are printed in the review text.
