Ground-truth construction (Sect. 2.4): all solutions were graded by teaching assistants under instructor supervision in a "one-rater-per-item" design; an exceptions log documented adjudicated alternative solution paths, and the instructor conducted a brief audit of all graded exams.

"each problem or problem part was graded by a single responsible TA"

Jan Cvengros and Gerd Kortemeyer 2026 (2)¶

Jan Cvengros and Gerd Kortemeyer. (2026). Assisting the grading of a handwritten general chemistry exam with artificial intelligence. Discover Artificial Intelligence. https://doi.org/10.1007/s44163-026-01606-4

q2 · i? · design · r2

During the official post-exam review period only about one-third of students inspected their grading, and less than a handful of incorrect grading decisions were discovered. The article acknowledges the ground truth is best called an operational reference standard, since mistakes may still have slipped through.

"This would correspond to approximately 150 · 46 problem parts being inspected by students, which puts the grading-error rate from this sub-sample at 5/(150 · 46) ≈ 0.1%"
