Lo S, Lea T, Mavromoustakos A, Joseph B, Yii M, Exintaris B, Karunaratne N, Pearson DL, Ritchie T, Yuriev E and Pyun J (2026) Co-creating custom GPTs: an autoethnographic study of undergraduate students as partners in generative AI innovation. q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Inductive thematic analysis of summer-scholarship students' SPAR autoethnographic logs written during iterative cGPT construction. Students reported that "Edits in wording, uploaded documents, addition and deletion of sentences and paragraphs" produced inconsistent effects on model responses, requiring sustained critical evaluation of outputs.

"Edits in wording, uploaded documents, addition and deletion of sentences and paragraphs could either clearly affect how the GPT responded, or have no apparent effect"
