Kudina 2025¶

Kudina, O. (2025). Using AI in engineering education: a balancing act, driven by clear purpose. Draft version, to appear in The Routledge Handbook of the Philosophy of Engineering, 2nd ed. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16626 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.16626

q1 · i? · associational · r1

Theoretical argument drawing on Berlant (2020), grounded in the questionnaire findings on verification burden and conditional positive evaluation; this is the author's interpretation, not a separately tested result.

"the perceived or desired efficiency collides with the actual required vigilance and output evaluation. Here, the promises of ease of use and time-saving do not match the additional, and at times significant, cognitive, emotional, and moral labor"
