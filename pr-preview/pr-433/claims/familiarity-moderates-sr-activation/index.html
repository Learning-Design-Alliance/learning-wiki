Evidence¶

Xiaodong Xu 2025¶

Xiaodong Xu, Cheng Jia, Kang Chen & Lijuan Chen. (2025). Unveiling the neural mechanisms of supernatural fiction comprehension using fNIRS. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00327-0

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Follow-up emmeans analysis of the significant Scenario Type × HP familiarity interaction at left MFG [CH1: χ2 = 15.2, df = 2, pFDR < 0.001] in the 61-participant fNIRS experiment, comparing the high- (n = 30) and low-familiarity (n = 31) groups.

"the high-familiarity group showed signiﬁcantly higher activation during the reading of SR compared to RR [ t(118) = 3.99, β = 0.884, pFDR < 0.001] and higher activation during the reading of SR compared to SF [t(118) = 3.85, β = 0.852, pFDR < 0.001]. In contrast, the low-familiarity group did not exhibit signiﬁcant activation differences in any comparisons."
