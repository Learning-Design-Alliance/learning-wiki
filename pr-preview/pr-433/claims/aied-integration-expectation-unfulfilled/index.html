Evidence¶

H. Ulrich Hoppe 2015¶

H. Ulrich Hoppe. (2015). A Framework System for Intelligent Support in Open Distributed Learning Environments—a Look Back from 16 Years Later. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education, 26, 504–511. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0058-8

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

This is the author's retrospective assessment in the Problems and Unfulfilled Expectations section. He notes that interactive learning environments and, more recently, web-based learning environments were the most prominent approaches adopted in practical educational settings, with AI components hooking onto architectures not originally conceived as AI systems.

"In 1998, there was hope that AIED techniques could be extrapolated in such a way as to encompass fully integrated practical learning environments beyond systems of the ITS type - including “computer-integrated” face-to-face classrooms as well as virtual environments. This has not happened."
