Research Abstracts: Films 1953¶

Research Abstracts: Films. (1953). https://eric.ed.gov

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Controlled experiment by McTavish with four groups of college freshmen in a science survey class, each group viewing one of four general science films a different number of times. The abstract reports the pre-test to post-test difference "was highly significant, indicating that every group learned from each film." No effect size is printed.

"In every case the difference between the pre-test and post-test scores was highly significant, indicating that every group learned from each film."
