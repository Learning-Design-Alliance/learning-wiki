Evidence¶

Joshua Samani and Steven C. Pan 2021¶

Joshua Samani and Steven C. Pan. (2021). Interleaved practice enhances memory and problem-solving ability in undergraduate physics. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00110-x

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Exit-survey study-behavior analysis from the same experiment. The article concludes the interleaving benefit reflects qualitative changes in learning during homework completion rather than changes in the volume of studying.

"there were no signiﬁcant self-reported study time differences between the two conditions (Table 3). Rather, the most common pattern across both conditions involved minimal studying prior to the criterial test (≤3 h over 4 weeks) and intense studying between the criterial tests and midterms ( ≥10 h over 3 days)."
