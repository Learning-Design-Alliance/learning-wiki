How Pictures and Graphs Aid Learning From Print 1953¶

How Pictures and Graphs Aid Learning From Print. (1953). Audio-Visual Communication Review. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Audio-Visual+Communication+Review

A narrative review abstracting over 50 studies on pictures and graphs as aids to learning from print, stating "Practically all research seeks to answer which graph or which picture was chosen, or liked." No effect size is printed.

"The main issues (except attention and motivation) in the use of pictures and graphs have not been faced."
