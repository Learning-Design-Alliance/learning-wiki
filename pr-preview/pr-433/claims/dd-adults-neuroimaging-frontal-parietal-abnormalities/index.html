Evidence¶

Liane Kaufmann 2020¶

Liane Kaufmann, Michael von Aster, Silke M. Göbel, Josef Marksteiner, and Elise Klein. (2020). Developmental Dyscalculia in Adults: Current Issues and Open Questions for Future Research. Lernen und Lernstörungen, 9(2), 126–137. https://doi.org/10.1024/2235-0977/a000294

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Review reports Cappelletti and Price (2014) fMRI study: DD adults showed "stronger activations in the right superior and left inferior frontal gyrus" for number semantics, interpreted as compensatory. Voxel-based morphometry revealed reduced grey matter in a right parietal region of interest despite comparable whole-brain volumes.

"adults with DD exhibited stronger activations in the right superior and left inferior frontal gyrus for solving number (but not word) semantic tasks."
