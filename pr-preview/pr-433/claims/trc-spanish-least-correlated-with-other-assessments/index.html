Correlational analysis of Chicago K-2 assessment records. TRC-Spanish showed the lowest overall correlations, at about 0.65 with other assessments and 0.77 with TRC-English.

"The assessment that is least correlated with the others overall is TRC-Spanish, which is correlated at about 0.65 with each of the other assessments except TRC-English, with which it is strongly correlated at 0.77."
