Magdalena Novak & Stephan Schwan 2021¶

Magdalena Novak & Stephan Schwan. (2021). Does Touching Real Objects Affect Learning? Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09551-z

q3 · i2 · causal · r?

ANCOVA on the 12-item knowledge test about showroom 1 (M = 7.36, SD = 1.70 of 12), controlling for prior knowledge, interest, and objects inspected, revealed a "significant main effect"; post hoc tests showed better retention in vision-and-haptics (M = 7.82) and only-vision (M = 7.70) than only-haptics (M = 6.62).
