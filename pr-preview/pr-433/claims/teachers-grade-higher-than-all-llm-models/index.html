Evidence¶

Marcin Jukiewicz 2025¶

Marcin Jukiewicz. (2025). A systematic comparison of Large Language Models for automated assignment assessment in programming education: Exploring the importance of architecture and vendor. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.26483 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2509.26483

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive comparison of human teachers' grades with all 18 models' grades on the same submissions. The study reports "Teachers obtained the highest mean score (0.726, SD = 0.391)", indicating models tended to be stricter, assigning lower scores more frequently while teachers rewarded higher grades more often.

"Teachers obtained the highest mean score (0.726, SD = 0.391) and the highest median score (1, occurring 3 854 times, 63.38% of all cases) among all grading sources."
