Evidence¶

Mengyuan Chen 2023¶

Mengyuan Chen, Ching-sing Chai, Morris Siu-Yung Jong. (2023). Actualization of teaching conceptions in lesson design: how teaching conceptions shape TPACK regarding spherical video-based virtual reality-supported writing instruction. Education Tech Research Dev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11423-023-10295-0

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Chi-square post hoc analysis (adjusted standardized residuals bounded by ±3) of coded segments from 21 teachers' interviews; skill-oriented segments clustered at Replacement and Amplification, with a negative residual at Transformation (ASR = −8.1) reported in Table 3.

"The results showed that the "level of TPACK" of the teachers with skill-oriented conceptions was likely to be at the Replacement (ASR = 7.4) and Amplification levels (ASR = 3.2)."
