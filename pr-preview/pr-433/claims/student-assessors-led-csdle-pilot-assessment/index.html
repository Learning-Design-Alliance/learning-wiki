Evidence¶

Van Nostrand 2025¶

Van Nostrand, P., Noakes, S., Foster, A. J., & Burstein, R. (2025). Putting learners first: Broadening access through technology evaluation in California Community Colleges. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/279

q2 · i? · design · r2

Pilot assessment stage of the CSDLE badge, run with the CCC Student Centered Design Lab, which received over 500 student applications for the assessor role. After evaluating submissions, "student assessors convened with Digital Promise and the CO team to discuss discrepancies in evaluation, come to consensus, and reflect those decisions in the finalized badge application language".

"The SCDL selected five students who spanned a range of geographies, campuses, ages, and backgrounds. These student assessors were then trained on the pilot application for the CSDLE badge. At least two students were assigned to review each application."
