Evidence¶

Park 2025¶

Park, A.T., Colantonio, J., Delgado Reyes, L., Sharp, S.D.S., Koepp, A.E., Bonawitz, E., & Mackey, A.P. (2025). Question asking practice fosters aspects of curiosity in science content in young children. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00384-5

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Preregistered randomized experiment with 103 children (52 question-asking, 51 listening) in eight one-on-one virtual science lessons. Quantile regression controlling for age and race showed the question-asking group valued science knowledge more (QA Mdn = 7 vs L Mdn = 5, t = 3.19, p = .002, pFDR = .010). The article reports "Wilcoxon r = .23" in the abstract only, so no effect size is carried here from the Results section.

"Children in the question-asking condition had a significantly higher willingness-to-pay score than children in the listening condition, indicating that they valued science knowledge more"
