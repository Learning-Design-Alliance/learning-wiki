Evidence¶

Glenberg 1979¶

Glenberg, A. M. (1979). Component-levels theory of the effects of spacing of repetitions on recall and recognition. Memory & Cognition, 7(2), 95-112. https://doi.org/10.3758/bf03197590

q2 · i? · causal · r?

The review reports that Maskarinec and Thompson (1976, Experiment 2) and Shaughnessy (1976) used incidental orienting tasks that forced processing of individual words without incentives to form associations; free recall after distributed presentations beat massed presentations. No effect sizes are printed.

"As predicted, the recall after DP was superior to the recall after MP in both the Maskarinec and Thompson (1976) and the Shaughnessy (1976) experiments."
