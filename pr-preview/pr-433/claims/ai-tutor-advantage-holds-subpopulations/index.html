Kestin 2025¶

Kestin, G., Miller, K., Klales, A., Milbourne, T. & Ponti, G. (2025). AI tutoring outperforms in-class active learning: an RCT introducing a novel research-based design in an authentic educational setting. Scientific Reports 15:17458. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-97652-6

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Subgroup analysis in the crossover RCT: significant subpopulations spanned the typical 30%-50% FCI range, and the improvement was similarly observed across the 65% CLASS scientific-attitude benchmark.

"The subpopulations of students with pre-instruction FCI scores below 40% and those above 40% both showed significantly better post-test performance with AI tutoring compared to in-class active learning (p < 0.001)."
