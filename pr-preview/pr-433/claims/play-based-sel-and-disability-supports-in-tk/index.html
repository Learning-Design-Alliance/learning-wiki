Descriptive survey results on social-emotional learning strategies (Table 4). Other strategies included developmental observations (33%), staff development on SEL (22%), open-ended self-directed learning opportunities (19%), and training on (14%) or implementation of (5%) the CSEFEL Pyramid Model.

Descriptive survey results on instructional practices for children with disabilities (Table 5): specialized services in the classroom with peer models (42%), Universal Design for Learning (42%), and social-emotional strategies (40%) were also reported.

"In 2022–23, the majority of LEAs (64%) provided adaptations to instructional materials to support students with special needs, and about half (47%) provided additional staff to support participation in instruction (see Table 5)."
