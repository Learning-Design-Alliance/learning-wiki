Narrative review attribution: the article reports a word-pair memory task study by Sun et al. (2025) showing that "primary school children (approximately ages 7–11) tended to under-use external cognitive aids" while secondary students over-relied on them. No effect size is printed in the quoted passage.

"primary school children (approximately ages 7–11) tended to under-use external cognitive aids, relying on internal memory even when offloading would have improved performance, whereas secondary school students (approximately ages 12–15) tended to over-rely on external aids at the expense of internal effort"
