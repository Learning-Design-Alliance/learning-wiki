Clark 2017¶

Clark, Melissa A.; Isenberg, Eric; Liu, Albert Y.; Makowsky, Libby; Zukiewicz, Marykate. (2017). Impacts of the Teach For America Investing in Innovation Scale-Up. Princeton, NJ: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/impacts-of-the-teach-for-america-investing-in-innovation-scaleup

q3 · i1 · causal · r?

The report states TFA teachers in grades 1 and 2 "had a positive effect on student math achievement." The printed effect size is 0.16 standard deviations, about 1.5 additional months of learning, with p-value = 0.054, just missing conventional significance thresholds.

"Similarly, TFA teachers in grades 1 and 2 had a positive effect on student math achievement of 0.16 standard deviations, or about 1.5 additional months of learning. This difference was almost statistically significant at conventional levels (p-value = 0.054)."
