Sidharth Anupkrishnan 2026¶

Sidharth Anupkrishnan, Itir Sayar, Jeongah Lee, Sri Harsha Musunuri, Guan-Ming Su, Madeline Endres, Ravi Karkar, Phuc Nguyen. (2026). E3Sense: Head-Confined Multimodal Sensing of Learner Engagement. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26569 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.26569

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Reflexive thematic analysis of semi-structured exit interviews conducted after video watching. Eighteen of 24 interviews contained an explicit engagement definition coded into three overlapping indicators; the analysis is qualitative, so no effect size applies.

"We represented these definitions with three overlapping participant-level indicators: Cognitive (seven participants), Behavioral (five), and Emotional (eight). The six interviews without an explicit definition were excluded from the prediction analysis."
