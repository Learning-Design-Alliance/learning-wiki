Evidence¶

Mehmet Bicakci 2026¶

Mehmet Bicakci, Heidrun Stoeger, Diana Wengler, Albert Ziegler. (2026). Recalibrating impact in STEM education: Empirical benchmarks for interpreting intervention effects. Educational Research Review 52. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2026.100803

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Limit meta-analysis re-estimated effects at the limit of infinite precision under a random-effects model; adjusted quartiles were recalculated and are reported as "the corresponding publication-bias-adjusted quartiles were 0.14, 0.32, and 0.59".

"Raw quartiles for the overall effect size distribution were 0.21, 0.48, and 0.86 at the 25th, 50th, and 75th percentiles, respectively, whereas the corresponding publication-bias-adjusted quartiles were 0.14, 0.32, and 0.59"
