Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Post-training improves TACTutor's auxiliary guardrail flags on TACTBench: accept rate rises from 0.603 to 0.872 and leak-or-overhelp falls from 0.346 to 0.026 — related
  • In a blinded study with 50 learners, TACTutor receives the highest overall mean rating (5.54/7) and is rated above its backbone across all four teaching-behavior dimensions (+0.39 overall) — related
  • TACTutor shows no degradation relative to its Qwen backbone on external educational benchmarks, with scores rising on MRBench, TutorBench, LongTutor, and DeepTutor — related
  • GRPO with an SFS-aligned reward yields more consistent improvements than DPO, achieving the highest SFS overall — a broader claim this one bears on
  • Supervised fine-tuning on misconception-faithful demonstrations sharply improves SFS (up to +0.555 on Malrule for Qwen3-4B) — a broader claim this one bears on
  • SFS-aligned post-training generalizes out-of-domain: models trained on one dataset retain substantial SFS gains on the other — a broader claim this one bears on
