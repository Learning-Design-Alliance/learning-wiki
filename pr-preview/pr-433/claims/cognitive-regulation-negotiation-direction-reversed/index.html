How does collaborative task design shape collaborative knowledge construction and group-level regulation of learning? A study of secondary school students' interactions in two varied tasks. Intern. q2 · i? · associational · r1

T/ONA directionality analysis of networks for both tasks and the subtraction model. The article reports SimSketch cognitive regulation "led to 'negotiate,' supporting CKC" and the reverse order in Simua; edge weights were not significantly different between tasks.

"cognitive regulation was less dominant, but indicated a clearer pattern in SimSketch, where it led to "negotiate," supporting CKC. This connection was also highlighted in the subtraction model in Fig. 3. In contrast, in Simua, negotiation more often preceded cognitive regulation, indicating challenges in joint knowledge construction and a need for cognitive regulation to steer progress."
