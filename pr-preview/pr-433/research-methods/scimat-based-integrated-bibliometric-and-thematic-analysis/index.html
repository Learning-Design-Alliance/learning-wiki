SciMAT-based integrated bibliometric and thematic analysis¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A research artifact and method: the study "combines bibliometric performance and thematic analysis of the principal instructional leadership (PIL) research field using SciMAT." The analysis "included 695 articles indexed on two reputable databases, Scopus and Web of Science (WoSCC)" and covered "three periods of analysis between 1941 and 2024," offering a comparative understanding of the thematic evolution of the field.
