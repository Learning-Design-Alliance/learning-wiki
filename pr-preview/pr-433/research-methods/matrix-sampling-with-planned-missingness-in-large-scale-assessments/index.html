Matrix sampling with planned missingness in large-scale assessments¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Large-scale assessments organize items into testlets counterbalanced across students within a sampling frame, so that 'no student ever takes the entire battery of assessments, or even all the items on any one test'. In PISA, 'each student participates for 2 h, seeing only a fraction of the total set of test items', and for optional domains many students see no items yet still receive PVs. This design makes individual-level scoring from item responses impossible and necessitates the PV methodology.
