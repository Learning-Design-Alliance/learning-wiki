Working classification of chatbot and language-model technologies for AI coaching¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The review proposes a working taxonomy of coaching technologies, from rule-based programming (fixed rules and scripted responses) through task-oriented and ML-enhanced NLP/NLU chatbots to early LLMs and mass-market general-purpose LLMs. It distinguishes how systems are programmed (purpose-built versus general-purpose) and used (alone versus with human coaches), noting an AI system performs a coaching function when interaction supports goal clarification, self-reflection, feedback, action planning, motivation, or behavior change. This classification organizes the evidence review and risk analysis.
