Gap-analysis method for identifying gateway courses via predicted versus observed DFW rates and persistence gaps¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A learning-analytics method using institutional student records. A binary logistic regression predicts the probability a student posts a D, F, or W grade using Student Type, life-cycle stage, prior cumulative GPA, and prior DFW fraction; predicted and observed rates are recorded per course and "the difference (Gap) between them calculated." A second gap compares persistence of students in a course to students in all courses of the same type at the same degree-path point, by student type and stage.

Accounts¶

  • Gap-analysis method for identifying gateway courses via predicted versus observed DFW rates and persistence gaps: A learning-analytics method using institutional student records. A binary logistic regression predicts the probability a student posts a D, F, or W grade using Student Type, life-cycle stage, prior cumulative GPA, and prior DFW fraction; predicted and observed rates are recorded per course and "the difference (Gap) between them calculated." A second gap compares persistence of students in a course to students in all courses of the same type at the same degree-path point, by student type and stage. (Bloemer et al. (2017))
  • Judge course effectiveness relative to predicted DFW rates for the enrolled population rather than by raw DFW rankings alone: The article argues institutional attention should go to courses whose observed outcomes deviate from expectations. It predicts DFW probability per student using student type, life-cycle stage, prior GPA and prior DFW fraction, then computes the "difference (Gap) between them" to rank courses. This avoids misplaced or harmful fixes on courses simply serving a difficult population. (Bloemer et al. (2017))

Claims¶

  • Gap analysis comparing observed to predicted DFW rates recasts course rankings, revealing courses that exceed or fall below expectations in a way raw DFW ranking does not [+M]
