Description¶

The article acknowledges the criticism that cutoffs for classifying students' percentage change into categories may seem arbitrary, and answers it: "the cutoffs may come from theory; for example, from expert considerations of what should at least be learned during a course/period of time. Or, they may come from empirical research." When theory is vague and findings rare, illustrative cutoffs may provide a starting point but not a definite definition.
