Micro-ecological approach to cross-level CSCL analysis¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Borge and Mercier propose this approach as an alternative to the four overarching CSCL learning theories (information-processing, constructivism, socio-constructivist/socio-cultural, group cognition), arguing these lenses are "too narrow to capture the true contextual richness of CSCL in action." It cuts across multiple levels of analysis (individual, group, community) and analyzes how each level influences and is influenced by the others. Events such as different facilitator orchestration moves or a student destroying a Minecraft world would remain unexplained from single-level episode analysis.
