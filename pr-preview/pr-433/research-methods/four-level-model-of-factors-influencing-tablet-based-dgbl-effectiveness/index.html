Four-level model of factors influencing tablet-based DGBL effectiveness¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The review organizes influencing factors, adapted from Outhwaite et al. (2020b), into four levels: "app-level factors, individual-level factors, school-level factors, and macro-level factors". App-level factors include self-adaptation, multi-level content, and a parental involvement module; individual-level factors include demographic, developmental, environmental, and experience-related variables; school-level factors cover time on task and integration mode; macro-level factors cover device accessibility and educational philosophy. The review concludes effectiveness "is not determined by a single factor but by the interplay among four-level factors", shifting focus from whether DGBL works to under what conditions it works.
