Equal-difficulty word-list methodology for classroom spelling experiments¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article describes a methodology for constructing and validating two lists of spelling words of equal difficulty for a given class. Words are selected from reading material slightly above the class level, tested by preliminary dictation, and confirmed as matched when averages are practically identical and the correlation between individual performance on the two lists is very high. This enables controlled comparison of teaching methods with actual school words rather than nonsense syllables.
