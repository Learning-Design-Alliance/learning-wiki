PISA collaborative problem-solving framework¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The editorial describes the PISA framework (OECD 2017) as emphasizing "the relevance of establishing and maintaining shared understanding for collaborative problem solving" and being used as the analytic stance in the Bause et al. multi-touch study. The editors argue it "may be used to scaffold CSCL research, as it provides a clear conceptual framework for the processes that have to be considered.", helping explain under what conditions computational support may be beneficial.
