Instructional Quality and Implementation Coding system combining Wood's contingent teaching with Meichenbaum and Biemiller's self-direction dimension¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A coding system the authors developed to measure explicit instruction and scaffolding of purpose setting at fine grain. It draws on Vygotsky's ZPD and the GRR model "while also incorporating research on scaffolding", operationalizing the GRR through Wood's (2003) contingent teaching (domain and instructional contingencies) and Meichenbaum and Biemiller's (1998) self-direction dimension moving students from other-directed to self-directed. Coding covers actor, content, quality of scaffold, and communication mode within instructional cycles, and uniquely includes student application, which Wood's theory does not address.
