Three routes to theoretical integration: competition among theories, unified frameworks, and bridge-building integration of existing theories¶

Description¶

Drawing on Gigerenzer (2010), the essay presents three routes researchers can take toward integration. Route 1 is competition among theories in the Popperian tradition, using Kuhn's criteria of accuracy, consistency, scope, simplicity, and fruitfulness. Route 2 is the formation of unified frameworks, exemplified by Dweck's program and SDT's five mini-theories. Route 3, which the authors advocate and call hybridization, is 'growth through bridge-building' — building networks between existing theories and detecting coherence among them.
