Eye-tracking paradigm for studying peer feedback processing and use¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A laboratory paradigm in which 59 higher education students read the feedback of two peers on a fictional essay and revise it while their gaze behaviour is recorded. It separates a PF processing subphase (transitions, first-pass reading times) from a PF use subphase (dwell time on essay, revision quality assessed by professors), enabling process-level measurement of feedback uptake.
