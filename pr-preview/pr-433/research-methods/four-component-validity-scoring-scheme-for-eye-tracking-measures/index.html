Four-component validity scoring scheme for eye-tracking measures¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A structured scoring system adapted from Cook et al. and Naismith and Cavalcanti for evaluating validity evidence of eye-tracking measures, comprising four components: content, relations with other variables, data quality, and consequences. "The total validity score was calculated by summing the scores across all four components, yielding possible scores ranging from 0 to 4." Internal structure and response process were reinterpreted as data quality for eye tracking. The scheme enabled comparison of validity strength across learning constructs in the review.
