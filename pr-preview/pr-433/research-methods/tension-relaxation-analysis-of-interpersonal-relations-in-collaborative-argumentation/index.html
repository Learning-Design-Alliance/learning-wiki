Tension-relaxation analysis of interpersonal relations in collaborative argumentation¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Extending Bales' division of social interaction into a task zone and a socio-emotional zone, the author and Andriessen developed a formal analysis method aimed at studying "the 'interaction' between deepening of a conflict (extending down into an argument chain) and fluctuations in tension-relaxation". The expectation was that deepening conflicts raise tension up to a threshold supportable by the interpersonal relation. They also elaborated the notion of a collaborative working relation, which develops over time and acts as a buffer mediating the mutual regulation of tensions.
