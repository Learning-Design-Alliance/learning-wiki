Discrimination-index Q-matrix validation for cognitive diagnostic models¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article supplies the theoretical basis for using its discrimination index as the foundation of a Q-matrix validation method. The abstract states: "The rationale for using the index as the basis for a proposed validation method is provided in the form of mathematical proofs to several relevant lemmas and a theorem." The index is defined for CDMs subsumed by the generalized DINA model, so the framework is general rather than tied to one specific CDM.
