Sentence-level social network analysis pipeline for eye-tracking reading data¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A reusable analytic pipeline in which fixations are filtered (Tobii I-VT), sentence AOIs are classified as topic-introducing, topic-medial or topic-ending, AOI strings are converted to transition matrices, and weighted networks are built in R with igraph to compute strength, betweenness, harmonic centrality and local clustering coefficient. The authors state "The dataset and scripts are available at osf.io (https:// osf. io/ qbctd/)", supporting reuse and replication of the approach.
