Design-Based Implementation Research (DBIR)¶

Research Method · All research methods

Description¶

DBIR is an emerging research-and-development approach the article positions as the process for co-developing its differentiated instruction practice framework. It retains iterative analysis supporting incremental changes in teaching practice while adding collaborative, pragmatic problem-solving. The article states that "the approach engages four fundamental principles that define its benefits in educational settings": focus on persistent problems of practice from multiple stakeholders' perspectives; improvement through teams committed to iterative collaborative design; systematic inquiry developing both theory and knowledge about classroom learning and implementation; and developing capacity
