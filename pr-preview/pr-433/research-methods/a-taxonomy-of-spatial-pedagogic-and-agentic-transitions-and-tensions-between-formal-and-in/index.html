A taxonomy of spatial, pedagogic and agentic transitions and tensions between formal and informal learning spaces¶

Research Method · All research methods

Description¶

The article defines three categories of transitions and tensions between timetabled and non-timetabled learning spaces. 'Spatial' ones "exist as a function of timetables and architectural divides between formal and informal space"; 'pedagogic' ones exist between teacher-centred transmission intent and student-centred intent, producing 'failed', hidden and postponed interactions; and 'agentic' ones occur between the fixed power dynamic of the lecture theatre and the more democratic adjacent informal space. The taxonomy emerged from mixed-methods analysis of occupancy data, ethnographic observation and field interviews, and conceptualises the spaces as interconnected ecological zones.
