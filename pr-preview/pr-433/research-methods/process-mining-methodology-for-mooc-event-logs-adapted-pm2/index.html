Process mining methodology for MOOC event logs (adapted PM2)¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The study employs an adapted version of the Process Mining Methodology (PM2) with seven steps, from definition of objectives to evaluation of process models, using the Disco Fluxicon software tool with an adapted Fuzzy Minner algorithm. Process mining is described as "a family of techniques based on the areas of data science and process management that provide tools to support the analysis of operational processes based on event logs." The authors note it has advantages including real-time modelling of events, modelling without a base model, easy comparison of process models, and clear visualization of behaviour.
