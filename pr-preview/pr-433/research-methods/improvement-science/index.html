Improvement Science¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article presents Improvement Science (Bryk et al., 2015) as a continuous-improvement framework for AIEd, stating that "Improvement Science is guided by the following six principles": making work problem-specific and user-centered, treating variation in performance as the core problem, seeing the system that produces current outcomes, embedding measures of key outcomes, anchoring improvement in disciplined inquiry through PDSA cycles, and accelerating improvement through networked communities. It argues these principles develop user ownership needed to adapt AIEd to local contexts and address FATE issues that arise in practice.
