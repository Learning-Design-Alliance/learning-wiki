Coding scheme of young learners’ metacognitive SRL activities with domain-general and task-specific sublevel categories¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A think-aloud coding scheme for metacognitive SRL activities in lower secondary students, developed from theory and data using Bannert’s (2007) and van der Stel and Veenman’s (2014) schemes as a basis. It distinguishes four main categories — orientation, planning, evaluation, and reflection — with subcategories, and inductively derived sublevel categories separating "domain-general" from "task-specific" activities. Two trained coders independently coded 12% of participants, achieving a substantial interrater reliability (Cohen’s κ = 0.79).
