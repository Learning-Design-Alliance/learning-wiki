Classification framework distinguishing global quality, global process quality, quality pedagogical interactions, and quality interactions related to curriculum content¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The review's organising taxonomy for grouping interaction quality characteristics. Beyond the consensus distinction of structural and process quality, it delineates process quality into ECEC global quality, ECEC process quality, quality pedagogical interactions, and quality interactions related to curriculum content. Quality pedagogical interactions are "further subdivided into four categories: (i) building positive relationships; (ii) routines and structure; (iii) educator–child conversations and questioning; (iv) supporting play". Curriculum-content interactions are subdivided into seven categories including global instructional support, literacy, language, mathematics, science and environment learning, diversity, and educator use of materials/resources. The frame permitted evaluating differential associations of global versus finer-grained indices with child outcomes.
