Five-type taxonomy of research-integrity learning outcomes¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article organizes RCR training effects into five distinct learning outcome types: knowledge, attitude, sensitivity, judgment, and behavior. Its core methodological claim is that "approaches that support the achievement of one type of learning outcome of RCR may not be as helpful in achieving other learning outcomes", so effect sizes must be analyzed separately per outcome rather than pooled. The taxonomy draws on prior taxonomies of moral-domain learning outcomes and RCR learning objectives, and structures the entire moderator and meta-regression analysis.
