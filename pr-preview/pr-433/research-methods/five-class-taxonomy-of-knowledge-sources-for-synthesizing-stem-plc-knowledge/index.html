Five-class taxonomy of knowledge sources for synthesizing STEM PLC knowledge¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The synthesis organizes its evidence into five named classes of knowledge: Type 1, elaborated empirical studies since 1995 mostly in peer-reviewed journals; Type 2, empirical studies reported without method detail and non-empirical work such as theory building; Type 3, published expert knowledge and advice; Type 4, practice-based knowledge from an expert practitioner online panel; and Type 5, published model descriptions often with lessons learned. The authors explain that they "determined it would be useful to add an additional category that would explicitly attend to knowledge about models of conducting PLCs", finding "a considerable body of research in this category, some with “lessons learned” but without formal research". The framework lets the synthesis combine gold-standard and working knowledge while flagging where evidence is thin.
