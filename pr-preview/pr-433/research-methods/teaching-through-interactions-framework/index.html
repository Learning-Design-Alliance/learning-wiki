Teaching Through Interactions framework¶

Description¶

The review adopts the Teaching Through Interactions (TTI) framework as its organizing model for classroom-level interaction quality, integrating attachment theory and self-determination theory. As the review states, "The TTI-framework includes three broad domains; instructional support, classroom organization, and emotional support (Hamre et al., 2013)." Instructional support covers promoting inquiry, learning formats, quality feedback, and instructional dialogue; classroom organization covers behavior management, routines, and maximizing learning time; emotional support covers positive climate, sensitivity, and regard for student perspectives. The review uses these domains to structure its synthesis of interaction-SRL associations.

  • Teaching Through Interactions (TTI) framework: three domains of teacher–student interaction: The TTI framework conceptualizes teacher–student interaction as comprising three domains of teacher support: emotional support, classroom organization, and instructional support. Emotional support promotes motivation and security, classroom organization organizes students' behavior, time, and attention, and instructional support comprises teachers' facilitation of learning opportunities. The article states that "TSI can be conceptualized as comprising three domains of teacher support for student development and learning: emotional support, classroom organization, and instructional support," and uses this framework to structure both the INTERACT intervention topics and the outcome measures.
