Pentad-based coding scheme (P, S, T, CS, CL) for classifying authentic CSCL designs¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article operationalizes sociocultural design variation with a coding scheme crossing the pentad facets: Participants (P0 teacher, P1 non-authentic practitioners, P2 authentic practitioners), Setting (S0 classroom, S1 non-authentic settings, S2 authentic settings), Time (T0 educational timeframe, T1 authentic timeframe limited by educational timeframe), Computer support (CS0/CS1), and Collaborative learning (CL0-CL2). The scheme "help differentiate between the facets of the pentad in traditional schooling (non-authentic designs), designs for authenticity, and participation in current cultures," giving researchers one shared language across design types.
