Three swim-lane taxonomy situating short answer questions among gradable question types¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The review defines short answer questions within a hierarchical view of question types organized under three swim lanes: depth of learning, question category, and question type. Depth of learning separates recognition from recall questions; question category separates natural language from structured text and speech; question type distinguishes short answers from fill-the-gap and essay questions using the dimensions of length, focus, and openness. The figure is explicitly not exhaustive but shows common examples to differentiate ASAG questions from others.
