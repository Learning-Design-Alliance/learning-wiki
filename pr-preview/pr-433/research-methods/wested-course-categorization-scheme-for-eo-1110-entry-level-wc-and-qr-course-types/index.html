WestEd course categorization scheme for EO 1110 entry-level WC and QR course types¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A four-category scheme developed by WestEd researchers to classify how CSU campuses provide additional supports to students in entry-level Written Communication and QR courses under EO 1110. The report states that "the research team found four broad categories of courses": traditional single-semester courses; single-semester courses with additional supports (separate one-unit support modules, embedded support with additional hours, or supplemental instruction); multiterm sequences using two-semester stretch or prerequisite models; and small-unit support courses. Researchers used the scheme to examine student paths toward A2 and B4 requirements and completion rates by placement category.
