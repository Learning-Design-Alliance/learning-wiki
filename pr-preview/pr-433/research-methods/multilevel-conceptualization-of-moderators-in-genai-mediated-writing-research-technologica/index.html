Multilevel conceptualization of moderators in GenAI-mediated writing research (technological, learner, and contextual factors)¶

Research Method · All research methods

Description¶

The article proposes a multilevel framework for understanding variability in GenAI-mediated writing outcomes. It states that "Technological factors (e.g., tool type) determine the nature and depth of available feedback; learner factors (e.g., proficiency, education level) shape the capacity to interpret and internalize that feedback; and contextual factors (e.g., instructional model, sociocultural environment) influence how GenAI is integrated into learning practices." The framework holds that these moderators interact dynamically rather than operating independently, providing a principled basis for synthesizing cross-contextual studies and interpreting empirical variability.
