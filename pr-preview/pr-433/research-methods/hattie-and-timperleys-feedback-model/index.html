Hattie and Timperley's feedback model¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The article adopts this theoretical framework to analyse the effectiveness of textual feedback. It "categorized effective feedback into three dimensions (i.e., feeding up, feeding back and feeding forward) and four-level focuses (i.e., task, process, self-regulatory, and self )". Feeding up tells students where they are going; feeding back answers how they are going; feeding forward addresses where to go next. The authors chose it because it "has been adopted widely in previous research to analyse textual feedback", unlike Nicol and Macfarlane-Dick's principles judged too general.
