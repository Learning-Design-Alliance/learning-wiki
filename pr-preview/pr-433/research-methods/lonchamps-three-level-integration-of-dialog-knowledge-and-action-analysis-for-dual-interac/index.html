Lonchamp's three-level integration of dialog, knowledge, and action analysis for dual-interaction CSCL environments¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Jacques Lonchamp proposes a systematic integration of three analytic approaches, describing "three levels of analysis — dialog, knowledge, and action — which he claims fit together like semantics, syntax, and pragmatics to provide an integrated view of communication." He applies this within dual-interaction environments built using his generic and customizable Omega+ model.
