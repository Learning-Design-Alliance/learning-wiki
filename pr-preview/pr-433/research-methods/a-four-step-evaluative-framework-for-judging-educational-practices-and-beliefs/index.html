A four-step evaluative framework for judging educational practices and beliefs¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The editorial describes a shared evaluation process modeled by every article in the special issue. Each article first documents that a practice or belief exists, then sets objective criteria for what would count as good experimental evidence, then weighs the evidence for and against, and finally draws conclusions and recommendations. The editors argue this process "provides a roadmap for evaluating other practices and beliefs in education as well as other fields."
