KSA three-axis evaluation framework with a four-level Skills hierarchy (Center→Role→Scenario→Sub-scenario)¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The framework operationalizes educational LLM capability using the KSA (Knowledge-Skills-Attitude) model from educational assessment. The article states: "The Skills domain comprises 122,425 items across 6 Centers, 11 Roles, 46 Scenarios, and 81 Sub-scenarios." Knowledge covers curriculum-based content and pedagogical knowledge; Skills measures situated capability through tasks emulating real educational decisions, with difficulty tiers from Bloom's and Krathwohl's taxonomies; Attitude examines epistemic, instructional, and normative-ethical stances plus deception resistance via Alignment Faking. The hierarchy contextualizes tasks by institutional function (Center), professional responsibility (Role), task type (Scenario), and task variant (Sub-scenario).

Claims¶

  • Content knowledge and pedagogical knowledge dissociate in frontier LLMs; Claude-Opus-4.5 shows a 19.8 percentage point gap (66.3% content vs. 86.1% pedagogical) [+M]
  • Across frontier LLMs, Counseling is the easiest educational activity center (average 8.78) while Assessment and Research are hardest (8.42), and performance degrades from Easy to Hard difficulty [+M]
