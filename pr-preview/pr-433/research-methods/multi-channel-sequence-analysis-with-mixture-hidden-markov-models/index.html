Multi-channel sequence analysis with mixture hidden Markov models¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article offers a detailed methodology for studying how engagement and achievement evolve together across a full program: two chronologically ordered state sequences (engagement and achievement channels) are aligned per course, and a Mixture Hidden Markov Model — "a combination of LCA and HMM" — clusters multichannel trajectories jointly while estimating transition probabilities between hidden states. The method models "several variables evolving in parallel" and reveals for whom and when engagement-achievement relations change across a program.
