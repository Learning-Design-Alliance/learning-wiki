Agnostic multilevel gene-environment interaction framework for testing latent environmental moderation¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article advances a social-genetic research strategy that estimates total interactions between polygenic indices and environmental levels (schools, neighbourhoods, districts, municipalities) using multilevel random-slope models, "thus remaining agnostic to specific features of environments". Controlling parental EA-PGI exploits random genetic segregation to disentangle within-family genetic effects from environments selected by parents. The authors present it as a framework within which to test the interactive roles of specific school characteristics.
