Four-type taxonomy of behavioural change in team learning behaviours: dynamics, pattern, development, and minor change¶

Research Method · All research methods
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review defines four types of change in team learning behaviours, distinguished by the shape of the sequence of increases and decreases. Dynamics is "a sequence of increases and/or decreases in frequency, duration, intensity, or quality of team learning behaviours in a chaotic way without any regularity". Patterns are repetitive sequences, development is a sequence of major increases possibly with minor decreases, and minor change is a sequence of minor increases and/or decreases. The taxonomy was built from the theoretical framework and used to categorise the findings of the 15 included studies.
