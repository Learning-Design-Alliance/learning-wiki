Description¶

A working paper (Working Paper 3), published December 30, 2011 by Mathematica Policy Research in Princeton, NJ, and prepared for the U.S. Department of Education under the associated project "Charter Schools: Are They Effective?" with a stated time frame of 2003-2010. The paper "looks at findings from the first national randomized study of the impacts of charter schools on student achievement." The landing page provides only bibliographic and project context, not the study's results.
