Description¶

Table 1 of the article compiles VR websites and apps with addresses and descriptions, including SamRohn 360 VR Panoramic Photography and Virtual Tours, Blakeway Gigapixel, AirPano, World Tour 360, World in 360, YouTube, Sites in 3D Virtual Tours, You Visit 360, the Vatican tour, Kid World Citizen, Google Expeditions, Google Streets, and the InCell VR app, which takes students on a tutorial tour of a cell inside the body. The article notes "Google is a widely used medium for VR tools."

Requirements¶

  • Some resources have device limits the article states: AirPano and World Tour 360 currently do not support VR headsets, and the Vatican site is only available for 360 views, not Google Cardboard; YouTube requires a fast processor.

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students using teacher-selected VR content

  • Exploring locations, cultures, and science content (e.g., a virtual tour inside a cell during a viral attack) to support comprehension
