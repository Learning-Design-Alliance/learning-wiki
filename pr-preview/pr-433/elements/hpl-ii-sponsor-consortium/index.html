Description¶

The page lists the study's sponsors as the Alfred P. Sloan Foundation, American Educational Research Association, Department of Education, Gates Foundation, National Science Foundation, The Teagle Foundation, and William and Flora Hewlett Foundation. The activity is housed in the Division of Behavioral and Social Sciences and Education, led by the Board on Science Education and the Board on Behavioral, Cognitive, and Sensory Sciences.
