SACD multiprogram evaluation of seven schoolwide programs¶

Element · All elements
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The multiprogram evaluation is the report's central artifact: a coordinated experimental evaluation of seven named SACD programs (4Rs, ABC, CSP, Love In a Big World, Positive Action, PATHS, and Second Step). The glossary defines it as "the evaluation of the impact of the seven SACD programs on students' social and character development," examining "the average impact of all seven SACD programs combined and the average impact of each SACD program individually."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Random assignment of schools within each program's site; each research team implements one program under an experimental design

Constraints¶

  • Three research teams were led by and/or included personnel who had developed the intervention programs they evaluated, as disclosed in the report's conflicts-of-interest section

Target Learners¶

  • elementary school children in participating schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • social and character development
  • reduction of problem behavior
