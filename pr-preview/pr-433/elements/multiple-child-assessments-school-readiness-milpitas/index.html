Multiple child assessments used together to measure and evaluate preschool students' school readiness¶

Element · All elements
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

In the studied program, teachers used multiple child assessments, including a newly added assessment alongside existing tools, to "measure and evaluate preschool students' school readiness." The brief examines how teachers in this state-funded preschool integrated the new tool into practice and what supports helped them use the resulting information.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers need supports such as formal training, supervisory support, and colleague collaboration to act effectively on the information the assessments provide.
