Description¶

A technical appendix published by NWEA Research to accompany the brief "Behind at the Starting Line," which the page describes as examining Kindergarten test scores from more than 400,000 U.S. students. The appendix is positioned as supporting educators' early identification of students at risk, "enabling them to intervene and influence learning trajectories before the next critical milestone: third grade." The page provides only this description, not the appendix's contents.
