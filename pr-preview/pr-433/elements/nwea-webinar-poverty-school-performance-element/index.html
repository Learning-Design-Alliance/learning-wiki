Description¶

A webinar presentation released by NWEA Research in July 2019, authored by Andrew Hegedus. The page describes it as "An NWEA webinar by Dr. Andy Hegedus on the relationships between poverty and school performance" and tags it under the topics Equity and High-growth schools & practices. It is presented as a viewable online presentation rather than a peer-reviewed publication, and the page links it to an associated white paper on the same relationships.

Claims¶

  • School poverty is only weakly associated with median student growth in a school [+M]
