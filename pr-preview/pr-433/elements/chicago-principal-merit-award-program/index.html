Chicago principal merit award program¶

Element · All elements
Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2–q3 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A merit award program in which employers (the district) provide awards to school principals to induce greater effort. The program used an award threshold, allowing regression discontinuity comparison of principals who just exceeded versus fell just short of it. The article reports that "the award program incentives increased achievement," while award winners exited their schools at elevated rates the following year.
