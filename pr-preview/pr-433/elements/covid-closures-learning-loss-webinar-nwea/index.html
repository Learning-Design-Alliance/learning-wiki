Description¶

A NWEA Research presentation and webinar, dated April 2020 and authored by Beth Tarasawa and Megan Kuhfeld, addressing the implications of COVID-19 school closures for student achievement. It draws on "research on seasonal learning and summer learning loss" and convenes a panel with Dr. Beth Tarasawa, Sal Khan, Dr. Jesus Jara, and Kimberly Cockrell to discuss "insights for action to help educators, policymakers, and families address and plan for the impacts" of the extended pause in classroom instruction.
