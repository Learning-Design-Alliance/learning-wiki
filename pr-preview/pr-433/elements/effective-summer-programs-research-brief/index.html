Effective summer programs research brief for district leaders¶

Element · All elements
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 review), q1–q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A research brief from NWEA Research, dated June 2024, that reviews research on summer programs and distills practical guidance. Per its description, the report "dives into the research on summer programs, their implementation and design, as well as the efficacy of those programs" for literacy, math, and SEL outcomes, and provides recommendations framed for district leaders planning summer programming.
