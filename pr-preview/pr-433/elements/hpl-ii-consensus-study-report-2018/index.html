Description¶

The featured publication of this activity is "How People Learn II: Learners, Contexts, and Cultures", a Consensus Study Report completed in 2018. The page states that "the past several decades have seen an explosion of research that has important implications for individual learning, schooling, workforce training, and policy", which the report synthesizes. It is the product of the ad hoc committee described in the statement of task.
