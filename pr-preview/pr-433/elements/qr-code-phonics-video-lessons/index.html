QR-code phonics video lessons embedded in decodable books, offered in 14 languages¶

Element · All elements
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Each decodable book in the Literacy Packs includes a QR code linking to a 2-minute phonics video lesson. The article states these videos "reinforce phonics instruction in both English and Spanish, as well as 12 other languages, supporting classroom learning, small-group instruction, and extending practice at home." The multilingual videos serve teachers, students, and families across learning environments.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • QR codes printed in each book must link to the video lessons; families and teachers need devices to view them.
