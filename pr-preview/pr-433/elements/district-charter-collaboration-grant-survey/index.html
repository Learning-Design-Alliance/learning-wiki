District-Charter Collaboration Grant program evaluation survey¶

Element · All elements
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A survey of district and charter school educators (teachers and principals) sampled from 21 cross-sector collaboration activities across the seven grantee cities in 2014-2015, conducted by Mathematica for the Gates Foundation. It examined "the extent to which cross-sector collaboration and the transfer of practices occurred and the contextual factors that helped facilitate or impede cross-sector collaboration."
