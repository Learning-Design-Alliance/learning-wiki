KIPP leadership development investment for network scale-up¶

Element · All elements
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

As part of its scale-up effort, the KIPP charter network invested heavily in developing new and existing leaders; the article states that "To expand the network of KIPP charter schools, KIPP invested heavily in the development of new and existing leaders." This volume examines the leadership practices at KIPP schools and regions that accompanied that investment.
