Evidence¶

Koriat 2006¶

Koriat, A., & Bjork, R. A. (2006). Illusions of competence during study can be remedied by manipulations that enhance learners' sensitivity to retrieval conditions at test. Memory & Cognition, 34(5), 959-972. https://doi.org/10.3758/bf03193244

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Experiment 4, 40 Hebrew-speaking undergraduates, immediate versus delayed JOLs on forward, backward, and unrelated pairs; the three-way interaction was significant [F(1,19) 9.92, p .01], with overconfidence for backward pairs dropping from 32.3% to 12.0%.

"Whereas immediate JOLs yielded an overconfidence bias amounting to 6.4% and 32.3% for the forward and backward pairs, respectively, the respective values for delayed JOLs were 4.3% and 12.0%."

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Experiment 4 resolution analysis for the 20 participants with recall data: JOL–recall gamma averaged .64 for immediate-JOL items and .86 for delayed-JOL items, "averaged .64 and .86, respectively, across partici- pants".

"These correla- tions averaged .64 and .86, respectively, across partici- pants [t(19) 4.23, p .001]."
