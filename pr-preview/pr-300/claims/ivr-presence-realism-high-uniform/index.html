Evidence¶

Lisa Hasenbein 2024¶

Lisa Hasenbein, Ulrich Trautwein, Jens-Uwe Hahn, Stephan Soller, Richard Göllner. (2024). An experimental test of the Big-Fish-Little-Pond Effect using an immersive virtual reality classroom. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-023-09646-4

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Self-report check in the IVR experiment using nine presence items and six realism items on 4-point scales: means across conditions "ranged from 2.82 to 2.97" with no significant condition effects (all ps > 0.05). Only means, SDs and ps are printed; no effect size.

"The reported mean levels of experienced presence and perceived realism ranged from 2.82 to 2.97 (0.52 ≤ SDs ≤ 0.62) in all experimental conditions. The experimental conditions had no statistically significant effects on participants' experienced level of presence and their perceived realism of the IVR classroom (all ps > 0.05)"
