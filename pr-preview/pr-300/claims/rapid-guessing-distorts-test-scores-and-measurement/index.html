Steven Wise 2020¶

Steven Wise. (2020). The impact of test-taking disengagement on item content representation. NWEA Research brief. https://www.nwea.org/resources/the-impact-of-test-taking-disengagement-on-item-content-representation/

q2 · i? · theoretical · r?

The brief states as background that "Rapid-guessing can distort test scores and adversely affect measurement." The brief text prints no statistics, samples, or methods supporting this statement.

"Rapid-guessing can distort test scores and adversely affect measurement. New research shows how disengaged responses can also distort content representation."

Wise & Kuhfeld 2020¶

Steven Wise, Megan Kuhfeld. (2020). A cessation of measurement: Identifying test taker disengagement using response time. NWEA Research presentation, National Council on Measurement in Education 2020 virtual conference. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/a-cessation-of-measurement-identifying-test-taker-disengagement-using-response-time/

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The presentation's description argues that rapid guesses reflect a construct-irrelevant response process and should be excluded from scoring, citing "both data analyses and previous research findings", and states that scoring rapid guesses produces "systematic measurement errors that can seriously distort scores". The description prints no statistics.

"Moreover, if rapid guesses are included in scoring, they introduce systematic measurement errors that can seriously distort scores."

Steven Wise 2017¶

Steven Wise. (2017). Rapid‐guessing behavior: Its identification, interpretation, and implications. Educational Measurement Issues and Practice 36 (4) 52-61. https://doi.org/10.1111/emip.12189

q2 · i? · theoretical · r?

The article's abstract states the consequence of the opt-out conceptualization: "rapid guessing tends to negatively distort scores and thereby diminish validity." The supplied text prints no effect size or supporting dataset.

"As a result, rapid guessing tends to negatively distort scores and thereby diminish validity."
