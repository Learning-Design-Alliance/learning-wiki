Evidence¶

Reet Kasepalu 2023¶

Reet Kasepalu, Pankaj Chejara, Luis P. Prieto, Tobias Ley. (2023). Studying teacher withitness in the wild: comparing a mirroring and an alerting & guiding dashboard for collaborative learning. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn (2023) 18:575–606. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-023-09414-z

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

In the same quasi-experiment, RTLX subscale analyses showed "performance (how accomplished and successful the teacher was feeling during the CL activity, Cohen's d = 1.2, p = 0.003)" and frustration (Cohen's d = 1, p = 0.005) had the sharpest drops; mirroring-vs-guiding performance contrast showed a medium effect 0.8, p = 0.04; other subscales were non-significant.

"These two subscales were performance (how accomplished and successful the teacher was feeling during the CL activity, Cohen's d = 1.2, p = 0.003) and level of frustration (how discouraged, stressed and annoyed the teacher was feeling during the CL activity, Cohen's d = 1, p = 0.005)."
