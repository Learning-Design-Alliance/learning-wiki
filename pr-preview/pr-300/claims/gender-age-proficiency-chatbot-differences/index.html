Evidence¶

Stephen Colbran 2026¶

Stephen Colbran, Meena Jha and Caitlin Schiavone. (2026). Understanding student perspectives on generative AI chatbots: a human-centred mixed-methods study in higher education. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00605-w

q3 · i? · associational · r1

ANOVA results in the RQ1 section: attitude difference by gender (F = 3.44, Sig = .035, df = 2) with "males were more likely to agree than females"; grades item F = 4.31, Sig = .016; English-proficiency ANOVA on peer usage F = 3.88, Sig = .011, df = 3, with intermediate and advanced respondents more likely to agree.

"ANOVA revealed significant differences existed between group means on the issue of having a positive attitude towards the use of chatbots in education (F = 3.44, Sig = .035, df = 2). Tukey's HSD (Honestly Significant Difference) pairwise comparisons revealed that males were more likely to agree than females."

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Age ANOVA on concern about being accused of breaching academic integrity (F = 2.59, Sig = .026, df = 6): "the age groups 26–30, 31–35 and over 45 were more concerned about, this issue than the groups 18–20, 21–25 and 41–45".

"Tukey's HSD pairwise comparisons revealed that the age groups 26–30, 31–35 and over 45 were more concerned about, this issue than the groups 18–20, 21–25 and 41–45."
