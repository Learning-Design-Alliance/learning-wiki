Evidence¶

Roy Lyster 2013¶

Roy Lyster, Kazuya Saito, and Masatoshi Sato. (2013). Oral corrective feedback in second language classrooms. Language Teaching, Volume 46, Issue 1. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0261444812000365

q2 · i? · review · r?

The review presents results of research on "CF frequency across instructional contexts", synthesizing observational classroom studies of how often teachers provide corrective feedback. No specific frequency figures are given in the reviewed abstract-level statement.

"Various types of oral CF are first identified, and the results of research revealing CF frequency across instructional contexts are presented."
