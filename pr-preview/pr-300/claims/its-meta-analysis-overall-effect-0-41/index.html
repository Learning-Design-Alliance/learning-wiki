Wenting Ma 2014¶

Wenting Ma, Olusola O. Adesope, John C. Nesbit, and Qing Liu. (2014). Intelligent Tutoring Systems and Learning Outcomes: A Meta-Analysis. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0037123

Meta-analysis of 107 independent effect sizes comparing ITS to non-ITS instruction; effect sizes weighted by inverse variances and computed with Hedges's correction for small-sample bias. Under a random -effects model, the overall weighted mean effect size was also statistically significant and m oderate (g = .41, p < .001); the fixed-effect result was g = .36.
