Evidence¶

Howard Wainer 1992¶

Howard Wainer, Linda Steinberg. (1992). Sex Differences in Performance on the Mathematics Section of the Scholastic Aptitude Test: A Bidirectional Validity Study. Harvard Educational Review. https://doi.org/10.17763/haer.62.3.1p1555011301r133

q3 · i? · associational · r2

The authors' interpretive conclusion from the bidirectional validity analysis: matching on college math performance reveals that equal performance corresponds to unequal SAT-M scores by sex, i.e. "sex differences in the validity" of the test as a predictor.

"Though the data do not allow any conclusions about the cause of these differences in SAT-M scores,they do provide evidence of sex differences in the validity of the SAT-M as a predictor of college math performance."
