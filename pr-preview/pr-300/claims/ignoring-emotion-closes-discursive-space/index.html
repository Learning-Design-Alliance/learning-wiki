Dana Vedder-Weiss, Rotem Tractenberg-Maslaton, Karin Sarfati-Shaulov. (2025). Facilitation strategies responding to emotional displays in PD discourse: navigating relational and learning goals. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09737-4

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Linguistic ethnographic microanalysis of a video-recorded PD episode where a teacher expressed weariness about monotonous teaching; the facilitator continued writing and redirected with "Okay, what else?" The article concludes "Ignoring the emotion, the facilitator did not recognize nor explore it."
