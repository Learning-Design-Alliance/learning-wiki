Evidence¶

Lisa E. Kim 2019¶

Lisa E. Kim, Verena Jörg & Robert M. Klassen. (2019). A Meta-Analysis of the Effects of Teacher Personality on Teacher Effectiveness and Burnout. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-018-9458-2

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Heterogeneity assessment within the random-effects meta-analysis of teacher effectiveness correlations. Q statistics ranged from 22.76 to 159.16 (all p < .001) and I² from 42.87% to 89.95%, which the authors state indicated the suitability of moderator analyses.

"the Q-statistic (or Cochrane 's Q) was significant for each of the Big Five domains, Q(14 to 17) = 22.76 to 159.16, p < .001, indicating that there were significantly different effect sizes across studies. Furthermore, I2s ranged from 42.87 to 89.95%, indicating that there were substantial proportions of variability across the studies."
