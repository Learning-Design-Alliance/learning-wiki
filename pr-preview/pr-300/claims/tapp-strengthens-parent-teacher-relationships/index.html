Evidence¶

Teachers and Parents as Partners (TAPP) Family Engagement evidence summary 2012¶

Teachers and Parents as Partners (TAPP) Family Engagement evidence summary. (2012). https://evidenceforessa.org/program/teachers-and-parents-as-partners-tapp/

q3 · i2 · causal · r?

Second randomized controlled trial in 45 rural schools (267 K–3 students, 152 teachers). Both teacher and parent PTRS-II ratings favored TAPP, with the page reporting "significantly stronger relationships" on both sides (ES +0.46 and +0.51).

"On the PTRS-II, teachers again reported significantly stronger relationships with parents (effect size = +0.46), and parents in the TAPP group also reported significantly stronger relationships with teachers (effect size = +0.51)."
