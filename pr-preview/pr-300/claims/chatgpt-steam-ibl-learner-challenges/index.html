q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

The review's orientation-phase challenge findings, with students relying on generic responses rather than pursuing original lines of inquiry. Conceptual confusion in conceptualization was attributed to inaccurate or generic ChatGPT-generated explanations.

"studies reported that learners experienced diminished creativity when ChatGPT’s outputs lacked personalization or failed to stimulate divergent thinking"
