Sanna Järvelä 2022¶

Sanna Järvelä, Carolyn P. Rosé. (2022). Relations Matter – CSCL Research Informing and Developing CL Competencies. Intern. J. Comput.-Support. Collab. Learn (2022) 17:185–189. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-022-09373-x

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Qualitative content analysis of classroom observations and videos, online discourse, super notes, Super Talk records and student interviews in a study of four Grade 5 classroom communities (N = 76) studying human body systems with the Idea Thread Mapper.

"The results showed that the fifth-graders were able to generate high quality super notes to reflect on their inquiry progress for cross-classroom sharing."
