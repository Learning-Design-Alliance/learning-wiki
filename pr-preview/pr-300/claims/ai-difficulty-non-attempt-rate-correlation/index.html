Hongfei Yan 2026¶

Hongfei Yan, Jiangkai Xiong, Yiqing Li, and Chong Chen. (2026). From Evaluated Models to Evaluation Aids: A Multi-Evidence Study of LLM-Based Difficulty Calibration for Programming Examinations. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07523 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.07523

q2 · i3 · associational · r2

Problem-level analysis of the 79-problem cross-sectional sample with a single auditable API reviewer. Printed effect sizes: Spearman rho = -0.871 (pass rate) and Spearman rho = 0.800 (non-attempt rate). The article frames the non-attempt rate as reflecting "students' give-up behavior under time pressure and diﬃculty judgment."

"AI overall diﬃculty correlated with student problem-level pass rate at Spearman rho = -0.871 and with non-attempt rate at rho = 0.800;"
