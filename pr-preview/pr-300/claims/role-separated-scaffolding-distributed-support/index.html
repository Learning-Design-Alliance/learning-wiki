Evidence¶

LUO Lan 2024¶

LUO Lan, WANG Anqi, ZHOU Muzhi, ZHU Junhua, CAI Jie, YU Ao, and PAN Hui. (2024). ParaTutor: Coordinating Parent-Child Math Tutoring through Role-Separated LLM Scaffolding. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18030 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.18030

q2 · i? · causal · r1

User study finding from 23 parent-child dyads. The role separation allowed children to remain actively involved in reasoning while preserving the parent as the primary tutor. Support was distributed in complementary forms across the two user roles.

"ParaTutor's role-separated scaffolding distributed support across parents and children in complementary forms. Children received visual grounding that made the problem structure more accessible, inspectable, and discussable, while parents received text-based guidance"
