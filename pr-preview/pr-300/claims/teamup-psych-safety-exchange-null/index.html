Mayer 2018¶

Mayer, I. (2018). Assessment of Teams in a Digital Game Environment. Simulation & Gaming, 49(6), 602-619. https://doi.org/10.1177/1046878118770831

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Correlation analysis at team level (N=106) relating post-game questionnaire constructs to logged in-game indicators. The study reports "Team psychological safety and team member exchange, however, are not significantly correlated with these performance indicators"; hypotheses 5, 7 and 8 were not accepted. Equivalence was not tested.
