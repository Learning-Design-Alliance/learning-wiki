The COVID-19 slide research brief and technical appendix on summer learning loss and school closures¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A NWEA research brief with accompanying technical appendix and working paper, listed as associated research for the webinar. The brief is titled "The COVID-19 slide: What summer learning loss can tell us about the potential impact of school closures on student academic achievement," and a companion working paper is titled "Projecting the potential impacts of COVID-19 school closures on academic achievement." Together these products translate seasonal-learning research into projections of closure impacts.
