  • QR-code phonics video lessons embedded in decodable books - Each decodable book in the packs includes a QR code linking to a 2-minute phonics video lesson.
  • QR-code phonics video lessons embedded in decodable books, offered in 14 languages - Each decodable book in the Literacy Packs includes a QR code linking to a 2-minute phonics video lesson.
  • Quantized Learning Gain (QLG): a binary High/Low measure of whether a student benefited from a learning environment - Quantized Learning Gain is a binary qualitative measurement introduced to avoid the asymmetry problems of Normalized Learning Gain for students with high pretest scores.
  • Quantum Randi Challenge: a modifiable computer game teaching quantum mechanics - The QRC is a computer game that anybody can modify, designed to reject hidden variable models by simply teaching quantum mechanics.
  • Question Formulation - Learners generate their own questions to guide inquiry.
  • QUICK-Comments teacher-augmentation tool in ASSISTments - QUICK-Comments is a teacher-augmentation tool within the ASSISTments web-based K-12 mathematics platform that supports teachers in assessing open-ended student responses.
  • Quinmester humanities course 'Ideas of Western Man: Expressionism and Realism' integrating literature, philosophy, art, and music - This is an authorized course of instruction for the Dade County quinmester program in English and humanities.
  • Quizzes - Short, low-stakes assessments that reinforce learning and provide feedback.
