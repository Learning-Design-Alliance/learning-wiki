IXL Math personalized learning program with Real-Time Diagnostic and IXL Analytics¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

IXL Math is "a comprehensive personalized teaching and learning program supporting math instruction from PreK through Calculus." Its curriculum includes "thousands of adaptive skills aligned to state standards, popular textbooks, and assessments, as well as video tutorials, classroom engagement and motivation tools, games, and more." The Real-Time Diagnostic identifies each student's grade-level proficiency in key strands and builds an action plan with next steps, while IXL Analytics provides reports on student learning and growth at district, school, classroom, and individual student levels. It runs on Windows, Mac OS, Chrome OS, iPad and iPhone, Android devices, and Fire tablets.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • IXL requires no installation, and schools receive support from an implementation team and a dedicated IXL Account Manager.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • students in Grades 3-5 in the evaluated trial; program spans PreK through Calculus

Target Learning Goals¶

  • mathematics achievement aligned to state standards

Claims¶

  • IXL Math's impact on the M-STEP state assessment was positive but not statistically significant (effect size +0.03) [+W]
  • IXL Math shows significant positive impacts for Grade 3, Hispanic, special education, ELL, and low-income subgroups [+W]
  • IXL Math raises Grade 3-5 mathematics achievement on the Renaissance Star Math after one semester (effect size +0.13) [+W]
  • Participating teachers reported largely positive perceptions of IXL Math and its professional development [+W]
