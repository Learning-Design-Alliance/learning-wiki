UChicago Consortium biennial school survey of teachers and grade 6-10 students¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Every two years, the UChicago Consortium surveys all Chicago public school teachers and sixth- through tenth-grade students. Teachers answer questions on classroom instruction, their professional development experiences, and the conditions under which they work, while students report on their school experiences, attitudes, and activities. Responses feed about 25 composite measures of school development grouped under the five essential supports, and the report categorizes endorsement of each measure as weak to strong.
