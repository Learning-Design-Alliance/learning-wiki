Digital Promise Product Certifications ecosystem¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), q1 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Product Certifications are "competency- and evidence-based signals" giving districts an independent overview of edtech quality. Product teams apply by submitting evidence; expert assessors at nonprofit organizations review submissions, and successful products earn an Open Badge. As of May 31, 2023, 162 certifications had been awarded across Research-Based Design (77), Learner Variability (34), Project Unicorn Interoperability (45), and others. Certifications expire every two years to confirm products still meet requirements as tools update.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Product teams must apply by submitting evidence from the product to demonstrate the tool meets criteria co-designed with learning communities and content experts
  • Certifications must be renewed every two years as the tool undergoes updates

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 district and school edtech decision makers
  • educators and learners whose needs certifications amplify

Target Learning Goals¶

  • informed edtech selection and procurement decisions based on product quality evidence

Claims¶

  • Districts report recurring pain points in edtech evaluation, including no formal process, curriculum–technology team disconnects, uneven evaluation depth, and outdated tool inventories [+W]
  • Without clear quality signals, district boards default to the lowest bid when comparing edtech products [+W]
