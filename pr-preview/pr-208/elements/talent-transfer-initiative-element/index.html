Talent Transfer Initiative¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Talent Transfer Initiative was a teacher transfer incentive program evaluated between 2007 and 2014 for the U.S. Department of Education's Institute of Education Sciences and National Center for Education Evaluation. It offered high-performing teachers $20,000 to transfer into the hardest-to-staff schools in 10 school districts, and the evaluated article examines teachers' "behavioral responses" to this offer.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Offers a financial transfer incentive ($20,000) to teachers identified as high-performing
  • Targets the hardest-to-staff schools within participating districts

Constraints¶

  • Evaluated in 10 school districts across the country; scope limited to those settings

Target Learners¶

  • Students in the hardest-to-staff schools in the 10 participating districts

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Staffing low-performing schools with high-performing teachers

Claims¶

  • The transfer incentive targeted high-performing teachers into the hardest-to-staff schools within their own districts [+W]
  • A $20,000 selective transfer incentive prompted behavioral responses from high-performing teachers in 10 districts [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • Talent Transfer Initiative (TTI) transfer-incentive program

Examples¶

  • Offer selective financial transfer incentives to staff hard-to-staff schools with high-performing teachers
  • Use transfer incentives to place high-performing teachers in hard-to-staff schools serving disadvantaged students
