MAP Growth reading and mathematics assessment dataset (7 million students, grades 3-8, 25,000 schools, 2020-21 to fall 2022)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief draws on MAP Growth reading and mathematics assessments administered between 2020-21 and fall 2022 to 7 million students in grades 3-8 in 25,000 schools. The text states the data come from "7 million students in grades 3-8 in 25,000 schools who took MAP Growth reading and mathematics assessments between 2020–21 and fall 2022." It supports NWEA's ongoing research agenda on pandemic effects on achievement.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Students must have taken MAP Growth reading and mathematics assessments in the covered window

Constraints¶

  • Covers grades 3-8 only, as stated in the brief

Target Learners¶

  • Students in grades 3-8 in US schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Measuring reading and mathematics achievement and recovery from pandemic disruption
