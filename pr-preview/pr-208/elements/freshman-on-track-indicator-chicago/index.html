Freshman on-track indicator¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The freshman on-track indicator is a ninth-grade early warning indicator of dropout developed in Chicago. The article states that Chicago administrators and partners were "Catalyzed by the development of the freshman on-track indicator and research around it" to build school-improvement mechanisms, and that data tools make on-track rate information actionable for practitioners.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Data tools that make it easy to act on information about on-track rates.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • Ninth-grade high school students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Identifying students at risk of dropping out for intervention

Claims¶

  • Ninth-grade on-track status predicts graduation more strongly than prior achievement, with a 55 percentage point gap after controls [+W]
  • Three freshman warning indicators—off-track status, any course failure, and attendance below 85 percent—identify 70 percent of eventual non-graduates [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • Freshman OnTrack indicator

Examples¶

  • Monitor freshman-year grades and intervene early, treating D students as needing attention comparable to F students
  • Focus school improvement efforts on the ninth-grade transition using real-time data to monitor and support at-risk freshmen
