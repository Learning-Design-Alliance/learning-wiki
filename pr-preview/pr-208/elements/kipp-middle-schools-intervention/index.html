KIPP middle schools as an evaluated intervention across the country¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 4 studies (4 causal), q2–q3 · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

KIPP middle schools are a charter school intervention whose nationwide effects were assessed by Mathematica Policy Research. The page states the brief "Provides comprehensive evidence on the effects of KIPP middle schools across the country," positioning the schools as the unit of evaluation for achievement outcomes in multiple subjects.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Evaluation requires comparison of KIPP middle school students' achievement outcomes against those of comparable students, as implied by an effects study.

Constraints¶

  • The evidence summarized concerns middle schools specifically; the page does not state effects for elementary or high school grades.

Target Learners¶

  • middle school students attending KIPP charter schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • academic achievement in reading, math, science, and social studies

Claims¶

  • KIPP middle schools produce significant achievement gains in reading, math, science, and social studies [+W]
  • KIPP charter middle schools produce positive, statistically significant test-score effects in reading, math, science, and social studies [+W]
  • The evaluation covers 43 KIPP middle schools across 13 states and the District of Columbia and is the most rigorous large-scale evaluation of KIPP charters to date [+W]
  • In the vast majority of 22 KIPP middle schools, impacts on students' state assessment scores in math and reading are positive, statistically significant, and educationally substantial [+W]
