KIPP: a network of charter middle schools for disadvantaged students evaluated over multiple years¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 7 claims (6 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Knowledge Is Power Program (KIPP) is described as "a network of charter schools for disadvantaged students." The evaluation covered 22 KIPP middle schools as the first findings of a multi-year study (associated project time frame 2007-2017), prepared for Arnold Ventures by Mathematica Policy Research using a matched comparison group design to estimate achievement impacts.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A matched comparison group design to estimate impacts on state assessment scores

Constraints¶

  • Findings reported here cover middle schools only; the evaluation is multi-year and these are its first findings

Target Learners¶

  • disadvantaged middle school students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • mathematics achievement
  • reading achievement

Claims¶

  • In the vast majority of 22 KIPP middle schools, impacts on students' state assessment scores in math and reading are positive, statistically significant, and educationally substantial [+W]
  • KIPP charter schools have positive effects on English language arts achievement for middle and high school students [+M]
  • A lottery-based randomized controlled trial of KIPP middle schools found statistically significant positive effects on state math and reading tests [+M]
  • KIPP shows no discernible effects on high school student progression (graduation within 4 years) [~W]
  • KIPP charter schools have potentially positive effects on social studies achievement, with mixed study findings [+W]
  • KIPP charter schools have positive effects on mathematics achievement for middle and high school students [+M]
  • KIPP charter schools have potentially positive effects on science achievement for middle and high school students [+W]
