Specialty Schools National Dashboard: interactive tool for achievement and growth patterns in US private and charter schools¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Specialty Schools National Dashboard is an interactive data visualization tool from NWEA. It provides information about academic achievement and growth for categories of US schools that used MAP Growth assessments, including secular and religious private schools and public charter schools. Users can examine patterns across seasons, subjects, school types, and demographics.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Schools must have used MAP Growth assessments for their data to appear in the dashboard.

Constraints¶

  • Covers several categories of US schools: secular and religious private schools (including Roman Catholic, Jewish, Lutheran, Islamic, Seventh-Day Adventist, and other religiously affiliated institutions) and public charter schools.

Target Learners¶

  • Students in US private, religiously affiliated, and public charter schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Academic achievement and growth in reading and mathematics

Claims¶

  • In spring 2025, math recovery continued while reading remained stalled [+W]
  • Middle school reading achievement remained stagnant post-COVID, with the lowest-performing students falling further behind [+W]
  • US reading achievement showed a positive turn after years of post-COVID stagnation, with historically underserved groups showing the largest reading gains from 2025 to 2026 [+W]
  • NWEA reports positive recovery trends in K-2 academic recovery following the COVID pandemic [+W]
