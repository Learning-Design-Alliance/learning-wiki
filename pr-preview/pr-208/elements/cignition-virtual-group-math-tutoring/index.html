Cignition virtual group math tutoring program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), q2–q3 · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Cignition is a virtual math tutoring program delivering "live virtual one-on-one and group math tutoring using video conferencing and interactive tools." It uses "experienced educators as tutors" and provides "formative data for teachers and parents via an online platform." The evaluated configuration served groups of up to four 5th-grade students and was tested against a control condition in a randomized controlled trial measuring fractions knowledge, confidence, enjoyment, and student–tutor relationships.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Experienced educators serving as tutors
  • Video conferencing and interactive tools for live virtual sessions
  • An online platform providing formative data for teachers and parents

Constraints¶

  • The evaluated evidence covers 5th-grade students in group sessions of up to four students

Target Learners¶

  • 5th-grade students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • fractions knowledge
  • math confidence
  • math enjoyment

Claims¶

  • Virtual group tutoring with Cignition improved 5th-grade students' fractions knowledge by 9 percentage points (ES = 0.44 SD) in an RCT [+M]
  • Tutored students reported positive relationships with Cignition tutors, with little variation in relationship quality across tutors [+W]
  • Cignition group tutoring produced slightly higher math confidence and enjoyment than control in an RCT [+W]
  • A portfolio of eight math tutoring programs showed positive and meaningful average effects on student math knowledge, perceptions, and beliefs [+W]
  • Math tutoring programs may improve students' math confidence and sense of belonging [+W]
