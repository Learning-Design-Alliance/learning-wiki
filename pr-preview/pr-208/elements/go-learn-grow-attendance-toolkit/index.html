Go-Learn-Grow toolkit for improving pre-kindergarten and kindergarten attendance¶

Description¶

The Go-Learn-Grow materials are a toolkit produced for Regional Educational Laboratory Mid-Atlantic, aimed at early-education attendance. The page states that "The Go-Learn-Grow materials can help support districts, schools, and early childhood providers in improving school attendance in pre-kindergarten and kindergarten." The toolkit is positioned as practical support material for education agencies and early childhood providers rather than as a research study.
