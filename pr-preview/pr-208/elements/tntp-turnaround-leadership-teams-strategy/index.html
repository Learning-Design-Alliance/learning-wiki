TNTP Turnaround Leadership Teams Strategy (TLTS)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 6 claims (4 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q4 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

TLTS is a school-improvement program implemented by TNTP in two school districts in the southeastern United States under an $8 million EIR mid-phase grant. It "aimed to support professional growth for school leaders and teacher leaders; improve instructional practices, school culture, and family engagement in school activities; and, ultimately, increase student achievement." Its two key program components are diagnosing school needs and developing and supporting school leadership teams (SLTs). Per-pupil costs averaged $242 per year.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires TNTP supports organized around the two key program components: diagnosing school needs and developing and supporting SLTs

Constraints¶

  • Evaluated in two school districts in the southeastern United States; effects were positive only in the two subject areas of focus for TLTS program supports

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students in TLTS schools
  • school leaders and teacher leaders

Target Learning Goals¶

  • professional growth for school leaders and teacher leaders
  • improved instructional practices, school culture, and family engagement
  • increased student achievement in ELA and math

Claims¶

  • TNTP achieved fidelity for TLTS's two key program components across Cohorts 2 and 3 [+M]
  • Grade 8 students in TLTS schools scored 0.12 standard deviations lower in social studies than comparison students [-M]
  • TLTS showed no detectable effect on science achievement after two years [~M]
  • Per-pupil costs for TLTS averaged $242 per year [+M]
  • After two years, TLTS raised student ELA achievement by 0.05 standard deviations relative to comparison schools [+M]
  • After two years, TLTS raised student math achievement by 0.09 standard deviations relative to comparison schools [+M]
