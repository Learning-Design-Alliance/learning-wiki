i-Ready Personalized Instruction (online individualized reading lessons for grades K-8)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

i-Ready Personalized Instruction uses information from the i-Ready Diagnostic to generate an individualized program of online lessons in reading for grades K-8, serving students whether their performance is below-grade, on-grade, or above-grade. The program monitors how students are progressing in reading over time, and decision-making at student, group, class, school, and district levels is driven by comprehensive, actionable reports. It is accessed through the i-Ready Connect digital platform.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires the i-Ready Diagnostic to generate the individualized program
  • Requires points of contact to assist with initial set-up and disseminate communications to other staff members

Constraints¶

  • Accessed through i-Ready Connect on Windows, MacOS, and Chromebook machines with browsers enabling JavaScript, cookies, and pop-ups

Target Learners¶

  • Students in grades K-8

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Reading achievement

Claims¶

  • i-Ready Personalized Instruction users in grade 5 scored significantly higher on ELA MCAS than matched comparison students (ES = +0.12) [+W]
  • i-Ready Personalized Instruction use was also associated with significantly higher Mathematics MCAS scores [+W]
  • In a 2024-2025 matched evaluation, fidelity users of i-Ready Personalized Instruction consistently outperformed non-users across grades 4-8 on STAAR, but this is excluded from the rating [+W]
  • Results were more robust for students meeting the usage recommendation of 30 minutes per week over a minimum of 18 weeks [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • i-Ready Connect digital platform for teacher-facing content, with browser, SSO, and third-party integration requirements
  • Pathblazer: supplemental online individualized intervention for struggling K–8 learners in mathematics and reading
  • Edmentum Exact Path: computer-adaptive supplemental program supporting instruction and assignment
  • i-Ready digital learning platform with adaptive diagnostic and personalized instruction

Examples¶

  • Implement i-Ready with designated points of contact and Curriculum Associates professional development support
