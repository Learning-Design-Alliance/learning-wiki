Tough As Nails, Nimble Fingers (TAN) virtual computing PD project¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 design), q1 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Tough As Nails, Nimble Fingers (TAN) is an NSF-funded collaboration between Digital Promise and Bit Source delivering virtual K-8 computing professional development in rural Kentucky. The deck identifies the work as "based upon work supported by the National Science Foundation under Grant No. 1923314" and names the partner team from Digital Promise and Bit Source. Its offerings include CT Booster sessions, micro-credentials, and openly licensed slides.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Delivery depends on a cross-organization team combining education research and digital literacy expertise

Constraints¶

  • Funded for a specific rural Kentucky context under NSF Grant No. 1923314

Target Learners¶

  • K-8 teachers in rural Kentucky

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Integrating computer science and computational thinking into K-8 teaching

Claims¶

  • The TAN project delivered four virtual CT Booster sessions for K-2 and 3-8 teachers on Data & Analysis and Algorithms between November 2020 and January 2021 [+W]
  • The partnership's research plan studies pathway creation, grade-band competencies, and whether a competency-based PD framework assists instructors in demonstrating teaching proficiency [+W]
  • A research-practice partnership is creating a K-8 computational thinking pathway in two rural Kentucky districts by applying South Fayette School District's model and training 75+ teachers over three years [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • Computing Pathways Research Practice Partnership with three districts
  • Expanding rural computing education partnership across Eastern KY districts, industry, and national organizations
  • TAN micro-credentials for Data and Analysis and Algorithms
  • Two NSF CSforAll research-practice partnerships designing a K-8 Computational Thinking pathway in Kentucky Appalachia
  • NSF Tough As Nails K-8 coding pathway for Kentucky Appalachia
