Toolkit supporting evidence-based writing instruction in grades 2 through 4¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A set of free professional development materials described as designed with busy educators in mind, offering guided activities and practices that teachers can implement throughout the school year. The toolkit is described as a one-stop shop for those interested in improving students' writing quality, and it builds on the What Works Clearinghouse practice guide Teaching Elementary School Students to Be Effective Writers. It was informed by teachers, school leaders, and professional development coaches from four charter schools in the District of Columbia.
