CW-FIT: a class-wide group-contingency classroom management program with team points, skill lessons, praise, and rewards¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 7 studies (5 causal, 2 quant-synthesis), q4 · 5 of 7 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

CW-FIT is a classroom management strategy in which teachers establish classroom rules, provide instruction on target skills, place students into teams, and reward teams for demonstrating target skills. The program "seeks to reinforce appropriate behaviors, minimize social attention to inappropriate behaviors, and provide self-management tools to individual students who need extra support." Core components are teaching behavioral skills via short scripted lessons, CW-FIT game sessions with team points and rewards, teacher praise, optional targeted supports (self-monitoring charts and help cards), and teacher training with coaching. It can be used in prekindergarten through grade 12.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers receive training covering how to teach behavioral skills using lesson scripts, monitor student behavior and award points, provide skill-specific praise, and provide rewards

Constraints¶

  • The developer recommends 30- to 60-minute sessions three to four times per week during periods when students typically demonstrate disruptive behaviors

Target Learners¶

  • students in prekindergarten through grade 12
  • classrooms demonstrating high levels of disruptive behaviors

Target Learning Goals¶

  • decreasing disruptive behavior
  • improving social skills and prosocial behaviors
  • creating a positive learning environment

Claims¶

  • In a multisite randomized replication trial, CW-FIT increased the percentage of time on-task in classes in kindergarten through grade 6 [+M]
  • CW-FIT substantially increases teacher praise statements but shows no statistically significant effect on some reprimand measures [+M]
  • CW-FIT has positive effects on student behavior, with strong WWC evidence across six studies in kindergarten through grade 10 [+M]
  • CW-FIT has positive effects on teacher practice, with promising WWC evidence across five studies in prekindergarten through grade 7 [+M]
  • In a cluster randomized trial, CW-FIT classes showed a lower percentage of time displaying disruptive behaviors than comparison classes over six months [+M]

Related Elements¶

Examples¶

  • Implement CW-FIT as 30- to 60-minute sessions three to four times per week, fading monitoring and rewards as behavior improves
  • Provide additional targeted supports — self-monitoring charts or help cards — for students with significant skill challenges
