Number Rockets scripted small-group first-grade math tutoring program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Number Rockets is "a scripted tutoring approach used in first grades as a Tier 2 intervention with students struggling in math." Tutors are retired teachers and substitute teachers working with 2-3 children at a time. Sessions run 40 minutes a day over 17 weeks, for 63 days total, supplementing students' ordinary math instruction.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Tutors receive one day of training before beginning, plus two two-hour booster sessions afterwards

Constraints¶

  • Designed as a Tier 2 supplement for first-grade students struggling in math, not a replacement for ordinary math instruction

Target Learners¶

  • first-grade students below the 38th percentile in math

Target Learning Goals¶

  • early mathematics concepts and procedures, including math facts

Claims¶

  • An earlier Number Rockets study showed positive effects but was excluded from evidence ratings because its tutors were the authors' graduate students [+W]
  • Number Rockets tutoring significantly improves first graders' math performance on TEMA-3 with an effect size of +0.34 versus untutored controls [+W]
