uCoach® Technology Platform¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (1 for, 3 mixed, 1 against) · 5 studies (3 causal, 2 review), q2–q3 · 1 of 5 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The uCoach® Technology Platform is one of the two primary components of InsideTrack© Coaching. It "enables InsideTrack© Coaching to deliver proactive, one-on-one coaching through multiple channels; send automated messages at predefined intervals; track student engagement and coach observations; and predict when to reach out to students on particular issues." Automated messages remind students about deadlines, opportunities, and resources, and the platform includes self-directed resources and can be integrated with institutional systems such as learning management systems.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Students need access to personal technology (e.g., mobile phone, computer) to communicate with their coach
  • Integration with institutional systems (e.g., student information systems and learning management systems) supports monitoring and tailored delivery

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • college students receiving InsideTrack© Coaching

Target Learning Goals¶

  • persistence support
  • timely awareness of deadlines and resources

Claims¶

  • The qualifying InsideTrack© study was an RCT across 17 lotteries in eight universities, with only seven 50/50 lotteries meeting WWC standards [~W]
  • InsideTrack© Coaching costs roughly $390–$500 per student per semester depending on program scale and intensity [~W]
  • InsideTrack© Coaching shows no discernible effects on college degree completion (attainment) [-W]
  • InsideTrack© Coaching has potentially positive effects on college credit accumulation and persistence, based on one RCT meeting WWC standards [+W]
  • Only one of 10 identified studies of InsideTrack© Coaching met WWC group design standards without reservations [~W]
