Multi-district teacher effectiveness equity study of grades 4-8, 2008-09 to 2012-13¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The study underlying the snapshot covers "fourth- to eighth-grade teachers over five school years (2008-2009 to 2012-2013) in 26 school districts across the country." It examines whether low-income students are taught by less effective teachers and whether reducing inequity would close the achievement gap, and analyzes how teacher hiring and movement into and out of schools affect low-income students' access to effective teachers.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teacher effectiveness measures for teachers in the 26 study districts across the five school years

Constraints¶

  • Findings describe the 26 study districts, not all districts nationally

Target Learners¶

  • Fourth- to eighth-grade students in 26 U.S. school districts

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Equitable access to effective teachers and its relation to the student achievement gap
