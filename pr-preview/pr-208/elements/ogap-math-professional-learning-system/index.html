OGAP Math professional learning system¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

OGAP Math is a professional learning system that "develops teachers’ knowledge of mathematics and pedagogy through research-based learning progressions." It trains teachers "to analyze student work as an ongoing assessment, helping them recognize strategies and misconceptions to guide instruction in real-time and over time." The program includes research-based item banks and frameworks across core K–8 mathematics domains and aims to strengthen teacher expertise in both content and pedagogy to improve student mathematics achievement.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Districts typically provide release time or professional development days for participating teachers
  • Cohorts are formed with about 24 educators per OGAP Strand
  • Follow-up coaching is scheduled within existing PLC or professional learning structures

Constraints¶

  • Not a digital curriculum or platform; does not require devices or software

Target Learners¶

  • K–8 teachers of mathematics

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Teachers' knowledge of mathematics content and pedagogy
  • Ongoing assessment of student work to recognize strategies and misconceptions

Claims¶

  • OGAP Math improves upper-elementary math achievement on the Pennsylvania state math test with an effect size of +0.20 over two years [+W]
  • The OGAP trial population was racially diverse and predominantly low-income, supporting scope to high-poverty urban districts [+W]

Related Elements¶

Examples¶

  • Train teachers to analyze student work as ongoing assessment using research-based learning progressions
