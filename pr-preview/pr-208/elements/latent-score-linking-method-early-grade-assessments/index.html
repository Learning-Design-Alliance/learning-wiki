Latent-score linking method for combining multiple K-2 literacy assessments from administrative data¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A prediction-model approach that links scores across different K-2 literacy assessments using test records already available in administrative data. As the authors describe it, "Our method of linking scores between different assessments is best described as a prediction model where the same student was administered multiple distinct tests at the same time, and across years." Unlike traditional equating, it uses pre-existing test records rather than researcher-designed administrations, leverages within-student growth across assessments, and produces latent scores from multiple sources rather than symmetrical pair conversions.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A sufficiently large and diverse base of students who took multiple tests, so that overlap exists across assessments

Constraints¶

  • Does not produce the specific skill-area information available in individual assessment reports; it measures general progression toward third grade literacy goals
  • Districts lacking overlap in tests taken at the same time, or among students with both high and low skills, might lack common support to develop a valid combined score

Target Learners¶

  • K-2 students, including multilingual learners and non-MLLs

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Early-grade literacy achievement measurement and monitoring of literacy growth

Claims¶

  • K-2 literacy assessments are positively correlated, with MAP showing the strongest correlations with the other assessments [+W]
  • K-2 assessment and latent scores predict third grade ELA grades with correlations from 0.52 to 0.63 in pre-pandemic cohorts [+W]
  • Combined latent scores are highly predictive of third grade assessment scores in pre-pandemic years, with correlations from 0.67 to 0.82 [+W]
  • Post-pandemic, K-2 scores predict third grade ELA outcomes with smaller but still moderate-to-large correlations (0.30-0.64 for grades; 0.47-0.81 for IAR) [+W]
  • TRC-Spanish is the assessment least correlated with the others, though strongly correlated with TRC-English [+W]

Related Elements¶

Examples¶

  • Combine MLL and non-MLL early-grade test scores into a common latent scale to obtain an unbiased view of literacy growth
