Really Great Reading foundational literacy programs (Countdown, Blast, HD Word)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Really Great Reading offers structured foundational literacy curricula grounded in the science of reading, providing "explicit instruction in phonological awareness, phonics, word recognition, decoding, encoding, and fluency." The programs span Countdown (Kindergarten), Blast (Grade 1), and HD Word (Grades 2–3), with print and online teacher tools. Instruction is "systematic and cumulative" and serves learners across Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 support.

Claims¶

  • Grade 1 students receiving Really Great Reading instruction scored higher on the NWEA MAP Reading Growth assessment in winter 2022 [+W]
  • Grade 2 students receiving Really Great Reading instruction scored higher on the NWEA MAP Reading Growth assessment in winter 2022 [+W]
  • Grade 3 Really Great Reading students did not outperform non-users on the winter NWEA MAP administration [-W]
