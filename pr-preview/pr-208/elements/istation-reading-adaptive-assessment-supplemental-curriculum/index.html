Istation Reading: computer-adaptive assessment and supplemental reading curriculum for prekindergarten through Grade 8¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Istation Reading is, in the article's words, "a computer-adaptive testing and supplemental reading program for students from prekindergarten through Grade 8." Based on the science of reading, it assesses phonemic awareness, reading comprehension, listening comprehension, letter knowledge, alphabetic decoding, fluency, spelling, and vocabulary, adapting question difficulty to student responses. After assessment, students are routed into an online curriculum matched to their performance, and teachers receive reports identifying strengths and areas for improvement.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires students to complete the adaptive assessment, after which they are routed into the online curriculum based on performance.

Constraints¶

  • The article describes it as supplemental: Istation recommends 30–40 minutes of supplemental curriculum use per week.

Target Learners¶

  • Students from prekindergarten through Grade 8, including struggling readers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Phonemic awareness
  • reading comprehension
  • listening comprehension
  • letter knowledge
  • alphabetic decoding
  • fluency
  • spelling
  • vocabulary

Claims¶

  • Istation use is associated with significantly greater NWEA MAP Reading growth than comparison students in a South Carolina district (ES +0.06) [+W]
  • Istation users scored significantly higher on STAAR Reading than non-using schoolmates in a large urban Texas district (ES +0.10) [+W]
  • Across two quasi-experimental studies, Istation Reading shows a weighted mean effect size of +0.08, qualifying it for a Promising evidence rating [+W]

Related Elements¶

  • Istation professional learning for teachers, instructional coaches, and principals

Examples¶

  • Implement Istation as a supplemental curriculum at the recommended 30–40 minutes of weekly use
  • Use Istation's teacher reports to target small-group instruction and lessons for struggling readers
