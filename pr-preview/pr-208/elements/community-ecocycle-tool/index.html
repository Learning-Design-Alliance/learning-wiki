The Community Ecocycle tool for funder reflection and action in place-based systems change¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The Community Ecocycle is a tool published by Equal Measure and Mathematica intended to help funders who invest in place-based systems change efforts. The article states it is "intended to help funders who have elected to invest in place-based systems change efforts in two ways": considering developmental phases and co-designing investment approaches with communities. It serves as a reflection and action aid for philanthropic funders working with communities.
