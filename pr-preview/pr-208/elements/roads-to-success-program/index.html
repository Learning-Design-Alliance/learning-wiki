Roads to Success: a middle and high school program connecting school experiences to aspirations for adulthood¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Roads to Success is a school-based program launched in early 2005 that, per the report, was designed "to forge connections between students' school experiences and their aspirations for adulthood, as an ongoing part of middle and high school." The report describes the intervention, its evaluation design, data, and analysis methods.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Delivered as an ongoing part of middle and high school programming

Constraints¶

  • Funding for the program and study was cut in 2008, so both were scheduled to conclude in 2009

Target Learners¶

  • Middle and high school students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Connecting school experiences to aspirations for adulthood

Claims¶

  • Roads to Success was evaluated as part of an associated project running 2007-2009 [+W]

  • The Roads to Success evaluation used a treatment-control design with baseline comparison of groups [+W]
