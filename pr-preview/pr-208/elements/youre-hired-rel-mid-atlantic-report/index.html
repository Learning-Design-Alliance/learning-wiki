You're Hired! The Skills Employers Seek in New Hires (REL Mid-Atlantic report)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A publication by the Mid-Atlantic Regional Educational Laboratory, published February 2, 2018 and prepared for the U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences. It "Summarizes peer-reviewed research, research conducted by regional and national industry representatives, and a convening of regional employers in western Pennsylvania to describe soft skills that employers look for in new hires." It serves as an evidence resource connecting education to workforce skill demands.
