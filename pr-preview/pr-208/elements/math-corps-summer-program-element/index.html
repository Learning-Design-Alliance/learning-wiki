Math Corps summer program for Detroit middle school students¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Math Corps is an intensive summer program for middle school students in Detroit, started approximately 30 years ago by Steven Kahn and Leonard Boehm at Wayne State University. The educators "leveraged their backgrounds as math teachers to create an intensive summer program" that combines high-quality math instruction with mentoring and positive influences. The program was studied under a 2015 National Science Foundation Advancing Informal STEM Learning grant, with Mathematica conducting an independent evaluation of students who attended from 2004 to 2009.
