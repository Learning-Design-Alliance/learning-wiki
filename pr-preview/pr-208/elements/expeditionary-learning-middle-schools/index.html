Expeditionary Learning (EL Education) middle schools¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Expeditionary Learning middle schools are the school model evaluated in this Mathematica brief, prepared for EL Education under the associated project 'EL Education Schools and Teacher Professional Development' with a 2013-2019 time frame. The page describes the model only through its evaluated outcome: 'EL middle school students perform better in reading and math than their public school counterparts.' It is a whole-school model serving middle school students in reading and math.
