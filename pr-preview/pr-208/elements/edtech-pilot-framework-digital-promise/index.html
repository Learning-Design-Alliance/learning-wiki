Digital Promise Edtech Pilot Framework¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The Edtech Pilot Framework is a free, open-source Digital Promise resource built from a 2015 study with the University of California at Davis to help education leaders and educators build skills in conducting, interpreting, and using research for edtech decisions. It "includes nearly 150 open-source tools and resources to support educators throughout the process of conducting pilot studies." The 2018 survey examined who accesses the Framework, their motivations, and its usefulness, and the report suggests it has supported League members in more regularly accessing and using evidence.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Intended for education leaders and educators conducting edtech pilot studies to inform procurement and adoption decisions

Constraints¶

  • The survey could only uncover potential correlations between self-reported evidence use and the Framework

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 education leaders
  • educators
  • coaches and instructional specialists

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Building capacity to conduct, interpret, and use research and evidence in edtech pilot studies and procurement decisions

Related Elements¶

  • EdTech Pilot Navigator with four pilot models (Resource E)

Examples¶

  • Provide professional learning opportunities and free tools to support educators in conducting edtech pilot studies
