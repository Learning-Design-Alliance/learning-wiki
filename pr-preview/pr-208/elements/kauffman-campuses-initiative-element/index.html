Kauffman Campuses Initiative: foundation grants to eight universities for campuswide entrepreneurship education¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The Kauffman Campuses Initiative is a grant-funded program of the Ewing Marion Kauffman Foundation. The article states that the foundation "launched the Kauffman Campuses Initiative in eight U.S. universities to encourage campuswide expansion of entrepreneurship programs and activities." It is studied here as a higher-education entrepreneurship-education expansion effort whose early implementation the report analyzes across sites.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Foundation grants to participating universities to support campuswide expansion of entrepreneurship programs and activities

Constraints¶

  • The report covers only the beginning of the grants, based on the first round of site visits and an early faculty survey

Target Learners¶

  • Undergraduate and graduate students at the eight participating U.S. universities

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Entrepreneurship knowledge and skills developed through campuswide entrepreneurship programs and activities

Claims¶

  • Early implementation of the Kauffman Campuses grants was documented through a cross-site analysis of 2005 site visits and a spring 2006 faculty survey [+W]
