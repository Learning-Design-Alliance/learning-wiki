i-Ready digital learning platform with adaptive diagnostic and personalized instruction¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

i-Ready is a suite of instructional and assessment products by Curriculum Associates covering Reading and Mathematics. The i-Ready Diagnostic uses "computer-based adaptative testing" to assess students on four math domains and place them at grade levels in each. i-Ready Personalized Instruction uses diagnostic data to assign each student a personalized lesson sequence, "starting from the domain where the student placed the lowest," intended to supplement classroom teachers' instruction.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires diagnostic assessment data across the four math domains to generate the personalized lesson sequence

Constraints¶

  • Lessons are intended to supplement and work with classroom teachers' instruction, not replace it

Target Learners¶

  • Students in grades 1 through 5 in United States schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Mathematics skills across Number and Operations, Algebra and Algebraic Thinking, Measurement and Data, and Geometry

Claims¶

  • Students initially placed below grade level complete an increasing share of on-or-above-grade-level lessons as they approach the winter diagnostic [+W]
  • The i-Ready platform allocates more lessons toward the domain where a student is placed below grade level, with the strongest differentiation in Algebra and Algebraic Thinking [+W]
  • The share of on-or-above-grade-level lessons a student completes rises with the number of domains in which the student is placed on or above grade level [+W]
  • Students on the platform tend to improve over the year, with 57 percent placed on or above grade level by spring versus 20 percent in fall [+W]
  • Students complete a similar number of lessons and total time on the platform regardless of their fall placement level [+W]
