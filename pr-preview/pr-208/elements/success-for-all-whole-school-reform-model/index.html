Success for All® (SFA®) whole-school reform model¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Success for All is "a whole-school reform model (that is, a model that integrates curriculum, school culture, family, and community supports) for students in prekindergarten through grade 8." It combines a literacy program with quarterly assessments of student learning, a social-emotional development program, computer-assisted tutoring tools, family support teams for parents, a facilitator who works with school personnel, and extensive training for intervention teachers. Developed by Robert Slavin and Nancy Madden with Johns Hopkins University, it is distributed by the Success for All Foundation; as of October 2016 the average cost was $104 per child per year.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Implementation requires a school facilitator, family support teams, tutoring staff, and extensive training for all intervention teachers as part of the whole-school model

Constraints¶

  • The WWC review notes that ratings do not take into account variations in implementation of the SFA® whole-school reform model

Target Learners¶

  • students in prekindergarten through grade 8, with the reviewed evidence focused on grades K–4

Target Learning Goals¶

  • beginning reading skills, including phonics, fluency, and comprehension

Claims¶

  • SFA® shows mixed effects on reading comprehension for beginning readers [~W]
  • SFA® shows mixed effects on general reading achievement for beginning readers [~W]
  • SFA® shows positive effects on alphabetics for beginning readers in grades K–4 [+W]
  • SFA® shows potentially positive effects on reading fluency for beginning readers [+W]
