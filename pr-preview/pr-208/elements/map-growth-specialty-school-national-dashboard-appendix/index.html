MAP Growth Specialty School National Dashboard technical appendix¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A technical appendix documenting the sample and methods used to build the MAP Growth Specialty School National Dashboard, a data tool reporting growth results for U.S. specialty school types. The appendix states its purpose is to "describe the sample and methods used to create the MAP Growth Specialty School National Dashboard." It serves as methodological documentation accompanying the dashboard asset.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires MAP Growth assessment data from the specialty school types covered by the dashboard

Constraints¶

  • Coverage is limited to U.S. public charter schools and U.S. private schools as the dashboard's focus

Target Learners¶

  • Students enrolled in U.S. public charter schools and private schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Academic growth measurement in specialty school settings
