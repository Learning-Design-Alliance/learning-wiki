Content Proximity MAP Growth tests¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (2 design, 1 causal), q2 · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Content Proximity tests are an evolution of MAP Growth assessments designed by NWEA to improve content validity while retaining off-grade adaptivity. "Content Proximity Tests were designed to give greater preference to on-grade items and have different numbers of items per instructional area when necessary." Math tests emphasize mathematics Aspects of Rigor and reading tests include more reading passages at upper grade levels. The tests continue to adapt off grade to deliver suitable difficulty, but align test events more closely with grade-level content for students performing typically for their grade, intended to improve connections to curriculum materials and correlations with end-of-year summative tests.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • An enhanced item selection algorithm (ARLIS) and updated test blueprints specifying items per instructional area and reading passage counts

Constraints¶

  • The authors state the changes are a refinement, not drastic enough to represent new products; math scores run about 3 RIT points higher than traditional MAP Growth, affecting norms predictions

Target Learners¶

  • K-8 students in participating school districts

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Measuring student achievement with stronger alignment to grade-level curriculum and standards

Affordances¶

  • Arlis Reward Listed Item Selection

Claims¶

  • The Content Proximity spring 2022 pilot study examined validity, reliability, and test score comparability of MAP Growth assessments using the new item-selection algorithm [+W]
  • Content Proximity math scores were consistently higher than traditional MAP Growth math scores, with small effects varying by grade [+W]
  • Content Proximity pilot tests contained significantly more on-grade items and made better use of adjacent-grade items than traditional MAP Growth tests [+W]
  • Growth-norms predictions showed larger RMSDs for pilot math scores but similar predictions for reading [~W]
  • The adaptive algorithm's item selection produces a significantly lower SEM than fixed-form tests [+W]
