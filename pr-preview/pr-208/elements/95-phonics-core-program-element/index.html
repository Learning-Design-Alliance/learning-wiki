95 Phonics Core Program: whole-class Tier I supplemental phonics curriculum for grades K-5¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The 95 Phonics Core Program (PCP) is "a whole-class, supplemental phonics curriculum" designed as a Tier I program for students in grades K-5 to address and prevent reading gaps. It provides "explicit, structured phonics instruction for 20-30 minutes per day" based on a scope and sequence, with grade-level kits including a teacher's edition, student workbooks, manipulatives, and digital presentation files.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Initial 3-hour training, with follow-up one-hour question and answer sessions each semester and onsite coaching recommended; minimum is 3-hour initial training

Constraints¶

  • Digital presentation files are complimentary only for the initial year of implementation

Target Learners¶

  • Students in grades K-5 in whole-class (Tier I) settings

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Phonemic awareness, decoding, phonics skills, oral reading fluency, and reading comprehension

Claims¶

  • PCP use in an Arizona matched study produced significantly more aimswebPlus growth for K-1 students (ES +0.29) [+W]
  • PCP use in a Missouri randomized trial produced significantly greater gains on Acadience Reading for K-2 students (ES +0.09) [+W]
  • Teachers reported PCP easy to use and beneficial for phonics instruction, but sometimes challenging to fit into 30 minutes [+W]
  • PCP use in a Texas matched study produced significantly greater growth on ISIP and STAAR for grades 4-5 (ES +0.15 and +0.10) [+W]

Related Elements¶

Examples¶

  • Implement PCP with initial 3-hour training plus semester follow-up sessions and onsite coaching
