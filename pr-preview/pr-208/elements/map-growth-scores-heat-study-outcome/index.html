MAP Growth assessment scores as the achievement outcome data source¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

MAP Growth is NWEA's assessment product whose scores serve as the achievement outcome in this heat-achievement analysis. The brief states the study "combines daily temperature data from various sources with MAP Growth scores for grades 3–8 across six states", drawing on roughly three million test events from fall 2022, 2023 and 2024. It functions here as the student achievement measure linked to daily temperature exposure.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • MAP Growth test event records for the analyzed grades and states

  • Analysis limited to grades 3–8 in six states and fall test events from 2022, 2023 and 2024

Target Learners¶

  • Students in grades 3–8 in six U.S. states

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Math and reading achievement as measured by MAP Growth

  • MAP Growth assessment data as the evidence base for district-level absenteeism and recovery analysis
