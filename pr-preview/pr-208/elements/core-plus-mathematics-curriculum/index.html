Core-Plus Mathematics curriculum (first edition, Contemporary Mathematics in Context)¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Core-Plus Mathematics is a four-year high school curriculum replacing the traditional sequence with courses featuring "interwoven strands of algebra and functions, statistics and probability, geometry and trigonometry, and discrete mathematics". The first three courses provide a common core, while the fourth prepares students for college mathematics and statistics. It emphasizes mathematical modeling and reasoning with multiple representations (verbal, numerical, graphical, and symbolic) using technology, and was developed by the Core-Plus Mathematics Project at Western Michigan University, distributed by Glencoe/McGraw-Hill.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Designed for a four-year high school mathematics sequence, with the first three courses providing a common core and the fourth continuing preparation for college mathematics and statistics courses

Constraints¶

  • This review covers only the first edition, published as Contemporary Mathematics in Context with copyright dates of 2003 and earlier; the second edition has copyright dates of 2008 and later

Target Learners¶

  • High school students in grades 9–12

Target Learning Goals¶

  • High school mathematics achievement across algebra and functions, statistics and probability, geometry and trigonometry, and discrete mathematics

Claims¶

  • The evidence base for Core-Plus Mathematics is small: of 17 reviewed studies, only one met WWC standards (with reservations) and 16 did not meet standards or screens [~W]
  • Core-Plus Mathematics has potentially positive effects on mathematics achievement for high school students, with an average improvement index of +15 percentile points [+W]
  • The effect on SAT math scores was positive but not statistically significant, though large enough to be substantively important (effect size at least 0.25) [+W]
  • Core-Plus Mathematics showed positive, statistically significant effects on ninth-grade ITED math and CPMP Course 1 and 2 posttest subtests after WWC adjustments [+W]
