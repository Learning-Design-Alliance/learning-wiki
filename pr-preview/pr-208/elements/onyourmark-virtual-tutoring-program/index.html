OnYourMark virtual early literacy tutoring program¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

OnYourMark is an early literacy tutoring service providing "virtual, individualized, high dosage instruction focused on foundational early literacy skills". It is grounded in the science of reading, partners with schools as a complement to existing literacy instruction, and delivers a phonics, phonological awareness and fluency curriculum in 1:1 or 2:1 sessions embedded into the school day, four 20-minute sessions per week from September to May.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Tutors provided by OnYourMark plus on-site school staff to supervise tutoring sessions
  • Access to a computer, headphones (preferably noise-cancelling and with a microphone) and reliable internet

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • K-2 students, including economically disadvantaged, multilingual learners, and students with disabilities

Target Learning Goals¶

  • foundational early literacy skills: phonics, phonological awareness, and fluency

Claims¶

  • Lower-performing students and first graders in 1:1 sessions benefited most from OnYourMark tutoring [+M]
  • OnYourMark tutoring did not produce significantly better performance on the NWEA MAP [-M]
  • Virtual 1:1 and 2:1 high-dosage tutoring improved K-2 students' early literacy performance on DIBELS relative to business-as-usual control [+M]
