Socratic Circles OER suite: professional development modules, classroom guidebook, and Community Socratic Circle materials¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The RCS Core Team developed Open Educational Resources centering a Socratic Circles model: "learner-centered group discussions in which participants learn from one another with discussion centered around a text or essential questions," which teachers and students can lead or co-lead. The suite comprises Professional Development Modules on racial equity and social justice, a Socratic Circle Classroom Guidebook serving as a playbook with student-facing scaffolding materials, and Community Socratic Circle materials engaging community members, students, and school staff. The structure was designed to let students participate at their individual comfort levels, maintain civility, and safeguard against the potential harms of open dialogue on culturally sensitive topics.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers complete the professional development modules and receive support from administrators and the community before facilitating racial discourse

Constraints¶

  • The report notes the structure is intended as a safeguard for the potential harms of open dialogue about culturally sensitive topics if discussions are miscarried without training

Target Learners¶

  • Middle school students
  • Teachers in diverse districts
  • Parents and community members

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Healthy racial equity discourse
  • Understanding students' perspectives
  • Classroom cultures supporting maturity and civility in conversations about equity
