Charting Reform student and teacher surveys¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The Consortium on Chicago School Research administered the surveys Charting Reform: The Students Speak and Charting Reform: The Teachers' Turn to sixth, eighth and tenth grade students and to elementary and high school teachers during Spring 1994. In all, 266 elementary schools and 46 high schools participated with a response rate high enough to receive a school report. The purpose was to collect reliable information on students' and teachers' views of the school environment, classroom learning, parent involvement, governance, and the professional work life of teachers.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Schools needed a minimum of 50 percent of teachers and/or students completing questionnaires to receive a report; the teacher criterion was reduced to 42 percent due to inconsistencies in base numbers.

Constraints¶

  • The Consortium promised students and teachers complete confidentiality, and reports are the property of the school.

Target Learners¶

  • sixth, eighth and tenth grade students
  • elementary and high school teachers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • measuring perceptions of school environment and classroom learning
