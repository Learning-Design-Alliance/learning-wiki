TurnNJ whole-school turnaround project¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

TurnNJ is a project of Uncommon Schools, "a nonprofit charter management organization that starts and manages charter schools," intended "to continue and expand Uncommon's whole-school turnaround efforts in six schools in Camden and Newark." It was supported by a 2016 i3 grant from the U.S. Department of Education and evaluated with propensity score matching.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Operates as part of broader urban renewal initiatives and school turnaround efforts in Camden and Newark, New Jersey

Constraints¶

  • Outcomes could be examined for only three of the six TurnNJ schools

Target Learners¶

  • Students in turnaround charter schools in Camden and Newark, New Jersey

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Math achievement
  • English language arts achievement

Claims¶

  • Enrollment in a TurnNJ school produced positive, statistically significant ELA achievement gains persisting up to four years, equal to an additional 1.9 years of learning after one year [+M]
  • Enrollment in a TurnNJ school produced positive, statistically significant math achievement gains persisting up to four years, equal to an additional 1.8 years of learning after one year [+M]
  • The Uncommon Schools whole-school model, typically built from the earliest grades in new schools, produced substantial positive achievement effects when applied in a turnaround setting [+M]

Related Elements¶

  • Knowledge Is Power Program (KIPP): a national network of public charter schools with extended day/year and a Commitment to Excellence
  • Evaluating the Effectiveness of Charter Management Organizations (CMOs) project, 2008-2012
