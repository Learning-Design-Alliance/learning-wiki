NWEA professional learning modules supporting MAP Growth implementation and formative practice¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Table 1 of the article lists professional learning modules that support instructional uses of MAP Growth: Responsive Teaching and Learning (assessment-empowered classrooms, not MAP Growth specific), MAP Growth: Basics, Applying Reports, Informing Instruction, and Focusing on Growth. Modules cover "formative assessment practices that are independent of MAP Growth and are based on principles of assessment literacy" as well as MAP Growth-specific training on interpreting score reports and setting growth goals.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers learn to create and use high-quality formative assessments to support day-to-day instruction
  • Training in interpretation of score reports and setting growth goals

Constraints¶

  • The Responsive Teaching and Learning modules are not specific to MAP Growth or any other NWEA assessment

Target Learners¶

  • Teachers
  • school and district staff implementing MAP Growth

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Formative assessment practice
  • interpreting score reports
  • flexible instructional grouping
  • goal setting and data conversations

Related Elements¶

  • Istation professional learning for teachers, instructional coaches, and principals
