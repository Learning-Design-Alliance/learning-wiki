Same-day MAP Growth retest dataset spanning four test types and four testing terms¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The study extracted MAP Growth Common Core test events in Reading 2-5, Reading 6+, Math 2-5, and Math 6+ from four testing terms: Fall 2017, Winter 2018, Spring 2018, and Fall 2018. Table 1 prints total test events ranging from 4,324,638 (Reading 6+) to 4,864,398 (Math 2-5), with retests within one day of 36,971 (Reading 2-5), 31,694 (Reading 6+), 16,106 (Math 2-5), and 16,938 (Math 6+), over 100,000 retests in total.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • RIT score with standard error and percent rapid guesses recorded for each test event

Constraints¶

  • Analyses focused on retests within one day of the initial test event, which effectively ruled out changes in true achievement between the two test events

Target Learners¶

  • Students in grades 2-5 and 6+ taking MAP Growth Reading and Math tests

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Understanding the relationship between test-taking engagement and measured achievement

Claims¶

  • Test-taking disengagement frequently differs between a MAP Growth initial test and a same-day retest [+M]
  • Rapid guesses on MAP Growth reflect a lack of engagement rather than a lack of knowledge [+M]
  • Students who rapid-guess less on a MAP Growth retest tend to see score gains, while those who rapid-guess more tend to see declines [+M]
  • Seriously disengaged initial testers who stopped rapid-guessing on retest showed large RIT gains, averaging about 6 points in Reading and 12 in Math [+M]

Related Elements¶

  • MAP Growth rapid-guessing invalidation criterion and four-level disengagement classification
  • MAP Growth retest dataset for evaluating E-M scoring accuracy
