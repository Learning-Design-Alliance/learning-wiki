Group tree seasonal observation activity¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The group tree is a K-2 activity in which students draw a tree at various times during the year and observe how it changes across seasons, generating comparative data. The webinar instructs: "Draw a tree at various times during the year. Students will observe the changes throughout the seasons." It functions as a longitudinal observation and comparison task for young learners.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A tree observable across seasons, or recurring drawing opportunities across the school year

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • K-2 primary grade students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • observing seasonal change
  • comparing and contrasting observations over time

Related Elements¶

  • Seed germination observation activity for K-2 data collection

Examples¶

  • Integrate data and analysis into existing K-2 teaching practices through classroom graphs such as birthdays and word walls.
