Reading Apprenticeship Academic Literacy (RAAL) course for struggling readers¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

RAAL is "the only RA program targeted to struggling readers." Students reading 2 to 5 years below grade level receive RA instruction during a daily class period, replacing non-reading electives such as art or music. It requires at least one teacher assigned to a year-long dedicated class, with a 5-day pre-course institute, a 2-day fall institute, and on-site and online coaching.
