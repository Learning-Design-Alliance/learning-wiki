Free online CSR teacher support materials¶

Element · All elements Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article states that "Online teacher support materials are available for free" and enumerates their contents: study modules for each CSR component, a video library featuring teachers using CSR in their classrooms, introductory mini lessons to introduce the CSR strategies to students, and resources to help teachers scaffold instruction for learners from diverse learning and language backgrounds.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers have access to the free online materials to support implementation

Constraints¶

  • Introductory and tune up lessons are available in PowerPoint and interactive whiteboard formats, though there are no technology requirements for CSR

Target Learners¶

  • teachers implementing CSR

Target Learning Goals¶

  • implementing CSR strategies and scaffolding instruction for diverse learners

Related Elements¶

  • Collaborative Strategic Reading (CSR) program for reading comprehension
