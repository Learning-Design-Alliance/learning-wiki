Lexia Core5 Reading: blended, personalized fundamental literacy instruction for grades pre-K-5¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Lexia Core5 Reading is a blended learning program that "accelerates the development of fundamental literacy skills for students of all abilities in grades pre-K-5." It provides "explicit, systematic instruction through personalized learning paths," adapts to target skill gaps as they emerge, and supplies teachers with data and instructional resources for offline instruction. Embedded assessment gives teachers ongoing actionable data, and an online student dashboard encourages ownership of learning.

Claims¶

  • Across four studies of Lexia Core5, the average effect size on reading outcomes is +0.16 [+W]
  • Core5 students in grades 3-5 showed significantly larger reading achievement gains than a matched comparison group (ES = +0.11) [+W]
  • Teachers in a 2006 study reported high satisfaction with Core5, averaging 8.5 of 10 points on motivation items [+W]
