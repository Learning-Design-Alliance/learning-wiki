The Superkids Reading Program: a beginning reading program for grades K-2¶

Element · All elements Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Superkids Reading Program is a beginning reading program for grades K-2, although qualifying studies have only involved kindergarten. It "focuses on phonemic awareness, phonics, vocabulary, comprehension, writing, and spelling." Two large matched studies in kindergarten evaluated the program and found positive outcomes on sound and word reading.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers receive initial training plus a complimentary coach visit, and administrators receive an overview session, all provided at no charge.

Constraints¶

  • Qualifying studies have only involved kindergarten, so evidence does not cover grades 1-2.

Target Learners¶

  • Kindergarten students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Beginning reading: phonemic awareness, phonics, vocabulary, comprehension, writing, and spelling

Claims¶

  • Superkids Reading Program improves kindergarten sound and word reading with an average effect size of +0.23 versus controls [+W]
  • Superkids teachers report satisfaction, perceived effectiveness, and ease of implementation significantly above control teachers (Borman & Dowling, 2009) [+W]
  • Superkids teachers report higher satisfaction, ease of use, and more core-program time than comparison teachers (D'Agostino, 2009) [+W]

Related Elements¶

Examples¶

  • Provide no-cost initial training, coaching visits, administrator overviews, and year-round support when implementing Superkids
