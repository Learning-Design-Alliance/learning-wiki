Experiential learning: students develop opinions of a concept through interaction with information¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article frames VR-based instruction as an instance of Dewey's experiential learning, quoting Dewey's claim that "there is an intimate and necessary relation between the processes of actual experience and education." Experiential learning enables students to develop their own opinions of a concept based on interaction with the information, and each experience is individualized based on past experiences, so varying viewpoints are supported when classrooms provide opportunities for experience and reflection.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Classroom learning must provide individual opportunities for experiences and reflection.
