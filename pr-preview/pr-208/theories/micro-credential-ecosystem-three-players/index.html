Three-player micro-credential ecosystem¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report models a micro-credential ecosystem, 'a network of school districts, organizations, and educators across the country,' comprising three players: Issuing Organizations that develop micro-credential content in collaboration with Digital Promise, Earners who engage with and seek to earn micro-credentials, and Recognizing Entities — 'The school, district, and state agencies that define the various ways micro-credentials provide value in an educator's professional career.' The report presents two issuers (Friday Institute, MTLC) and two recognizers (BCPS, KMSD) as case studies of this ecosystem.
