LEAD logic model chain from leader and teacher knowledge to student literacy and math outcomes¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The LEAD logic model organizes the intervention as a chain of outcomes across three time horizons. Short-term outcomes are "Increased knowledge of research-based early learning practices, including play-based learning and high-quality teacher-student interactions" for school leaders and preK and kindergarten teachers. Medium-term outcomes are that school leaders "Adapt and implement school policies and practices to support use of research-based early learning practices", and teachers increase their use of these practices. The long-term outcome is "Improved academic outcomes in literacy and math" for preK and kindergarten students.
