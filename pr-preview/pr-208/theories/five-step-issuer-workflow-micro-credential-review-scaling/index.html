Five-step issuer workflow for scaling micro-credential review from creation through long-term maintenance¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article lays out a workflow covering the micro-credential trajectory from creation through long-term maintenance at scale. Step 1 creates and validates the micro-credential; Steps 2-3 build a Benchmarks Database and certify reviewers; Step 4 issues at scale; Step 5 maintains the reviewer pool. The stated aim is a review process 'capable of handling large numbers of submissions in a timely fashion, while maintaining a high level of reliability and validity of the evaluation process.'

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A review process able to handle submission volume exceeding issuer staff bandwidth
  • Transparency into scoring and reviewer certification processes to gain stakeholder trust

Constraints¶

  • The workflows are described as a sampling of many possible strategies, with several topics remaining to be explored for various use cases

Target Learners¶

  • educators earning micro-credentials for professional development

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • certification of educator competencies through micro-credentials

Claims¶

  • Reviewer certification requires scoring benchmark samples on par with issuer-determined scores, with guessing odds made quantifiable [+W]
  • For a pass/not-yet micro-credential, the chance of guessing five benchmark scores with 100% accuracy is approximately 0.001 [+W]
  • Minimum reviewer pool size is computed from anticipated submissions, reviews per submission, and assignments per reviewer [+W]
