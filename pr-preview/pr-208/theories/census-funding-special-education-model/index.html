Census funding: a special education finance model based on total district enrollment¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Census funding is a special education finance model used by several states and the federal government. Under this model, "aid levels are based primarily on total district enrollment and a fixed amount per student" rather than on counts of students with disabilities. The brief introduces the model to help policymakers, educators, and researchers understand how it operates and what trade-offs it creates.
