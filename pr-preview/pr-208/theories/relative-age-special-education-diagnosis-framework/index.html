Relative age as a factor in special education diagnosis¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article treats a student's relative age among classroom peers as a factor influencing whether the student is diagnosed as disabled and receives special education services. It operationalizes relative age as months of age difference among peers and estimates its effect on the "likelihood of receiving special education services," finding that "an additional month of relative age decreases the likelihood of receiving special education services by 2-5 percent." The framework implies that youngest students in a grade face elevated classification risk.
