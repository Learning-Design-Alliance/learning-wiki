UTAUT framework for technology acceptance¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 associational), q2 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Unified Theory of Acceptance and Use of Technology (UTAUT) explains individuals' acceptance of a technology through four determinants of an individual's aim of use. The article states that "UTAUT includes four basic elements that determine an individual's aim of use: performance expectancy, effort expectancy, facilitating conditions and social influence". It was developed by Venkatesh et al. (2003) by incorporating components from eight different models, and the present study applies it, via the Sezer and Yilmaz (2019) framework, to predict students' LMS acceptance with an ANN model.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Measurement of the four constructs, here via the four LMSAS dimensions, plus the outcome variable of behavioral intention/acceptance

Constraints¶

  • The article notes its results are not in concordance with the original UTAUT model, owing majorly to differences of context such as developing-country settings

Target Learners¶

  • postsecondary vocational school students

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • understanding and predicting students' acceptance and behavioral intention to use learning management systems
