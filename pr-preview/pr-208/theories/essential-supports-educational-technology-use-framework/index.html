Essential supports framework for educational technology use in schools¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A theory developed and tested in a previous Consortium report on educational technology availability and use in Chicago's public schools, holding that teacher assignment and student use of technology depend on factors beyond hardware and software availability. The brief states, "Chief among these were the professional development opportunities in technology for teachers and the professional community within the school supporting technology use," plus technology support staff and, most important, principal leadership for technology use. The Grow analysis applies this framework and finds its Grow findings "very similar" to the earlier technology findings.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Professional development opportunities in technology for teachers
  • Professional community within the school supporting technology use
  • Staff or consultants to support the technology infrastructure
  • Principal leadership that makes technology a priority

Constraints¶

  • Developed from data on Chicago Public Schools technology availability and use, and applied here to a single reporting technology in one district
