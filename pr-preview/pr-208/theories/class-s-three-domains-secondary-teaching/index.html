CLASS-S organizes effective secondary teaching into three domains of teacher-student interaction¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The Classroom Assessment Scoring System–Secondary (CLASS-S) defines and observes effective teaching practices in MTP-S. Its dimensions are organized into three domains: "(1) Emotional Support", "(2) Classroom Organization", and "(3) Instructional Support", each with named dimensions such as positive climate, behavior management, and quality of feedback. The video library is organized by these CLASS-S dimensions.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Consultants and teachers need training in the CLASS-S dimensions to use the system as intended

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • middle and high school teachers

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • high-quality teacher-student interactions across emotional, organizational, and instructional support
