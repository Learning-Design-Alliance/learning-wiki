Modernist responses to the postmodern challenge form three broad areas: orthodox, critical pedagogy, and performativity texts¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

On the right side of the debate field, the paper patterns modernist responses into three genres. The author writes that he patterns texts "that respond in one way or another to the postmodern challenge into three broad areas": orthodox texts defending a core modernist metanarrative such as Reason or Progress; critical pedagogy texts preserving emancipation while appropriating postmodernist or feminist ideas; and performativity texts elaborating reflexive systems narratives of late modernity. Illustrative advocates include Epstein, Watson, McLaren, Torres, and Cowen.
