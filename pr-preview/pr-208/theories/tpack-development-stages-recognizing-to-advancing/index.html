Five-stage TPACK development trajectory from recognizing to advancing¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Niess, Sadri and Lee (2007), building on Rogers' innovation-decision process, describe TPACK development as five stages: recognizing (knowledge), accepting (persuasion), adapting (decision), exploring (implementation), and advancing (confirmation). The article uses these stages to characterize where teachers are in transforming their TPACK, illustrated with contrasting spreadsheet cases (Mr. D at recognizing, Mrs. A at exploring). The stages were "proposed as an iterative process in the development of TPACK rather than a strictly linear process."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers need to explore different topics with each new technology, considering its applicability for supporting learning mathematics with that technology

Constraints¶

  • The stages are iterative, not strictly linear; learning about one technology may provide disposition toward accepting another

Target Learners¶

  • in-service mathematics teachers

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • transforming TPACK for teaching mathematics with technologies

Claims¶

  • In-service mathematics teachers in the online TPACK program shifted from teacher-centered to valuing student-centered instructional strategies for teaching with technologies [+W]
  • Teachers' overarching conceptions expanded to value multiple technologies, including technologies for communication, collaboration, and inquiry, in more student-centered ways [+W]
