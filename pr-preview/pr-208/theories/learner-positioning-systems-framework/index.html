Learner Positioning Systems and Learner Models framework¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Digital Promise Global's Learner Positioning Systems (LPS) initiative aims to build frameworks and products that provide "a fuller understanding of learners and how to support them no matter how they learn best." At its heart are Learner Models that identify factors research shows affect learning and vary among learners, combining content area factors with each learner's unique characteristics. The Models build on Todd Rose's concept of "jagged profiles," which recognizes that each learner has distinctly different strengths and areas of growth. LPS is also envisioned as a tool for evaluating and certifying personalized learning products.
