Two-category taxonomy of requirements for DLPs as research infrastructure: expected technical work versus emerging organizational and community work¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The paper organizes infrastructure-building work into two kinds: (1) expected technical platform modifications (data enhancement and documentation, content authoring, privacy, experimental apparatus, student user experience) and (2) emergent organizational and community work that was less salient. The authors state, "Specifically, this paper articulates two kinds of infrastructure-building work: (1) the work SEERNet members expected and (2) the emergent work that was either less salient or not as obvious." Table 1 lists the eleven requirements across the two categories.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A multi-platform initiative with platform teams, research teams, and a coordinating hub to identify and share both categories of requirements

Constraints¶

  • Derived from three years of SEERNet experience with twelve separately funded teams; the authors frame it as reflections on lessons learned rather than tested findings

Target Learners¶

  • educational researchers using digital learning platforms

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • conducting rigorous experimental research on digital learning platforms

Examples¶

  • Use a two-phase feasibility process — a pre-submission platform feasibility letter and a post-award six-month feasibility phase — before research funds are committed
