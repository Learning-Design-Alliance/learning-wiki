The Early Education Essentials framework: six organizational essential supports for ECE programs¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 7 claims (7 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 qualitative), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The Early Education Essentials is a measurement and organizing framework adapting the UChicago Consortium's 5Essentials for early childhood settings, comprising teacher/staff and parent surveys of organizational conditions. The article names the essentials: "Effective Instructional Leaders, Collaborative Teachers, Involved Families, Supportive Environment, and Ambitious Instruction," plus a distinct sixth essential, Parent Voice, added because parents offered a unique perspective. The framework underpins the validation study and the qualitative contrasts reported here.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Surveys measuring the essentials must be administered to teachers/staff and parents in school-based and community-based ECE settings

Constraints¶

  • The article states most ECE schools/centers fall somewhere in the middle between the very strong and very weak extreme cases studied

Target Learners¶

  • preschool children in school-based and community-based early education programs

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • children's social-emotional development and foundational skills in thinking, literacy, math, and science

Claims¶

  • Family voices contributed to differentiating strong and weak organizational conditions across all essentials, not only Involved Families [+W]
  • Parents offer a unique perspective best captured by a distinct sixth essential, Parent Voice [+W]
  • Programs with strong essential supports are more likely to have higher teacher-child interactions and better child attendance than programs with very weak supports [+W]
  • Strongly organized ECE programs create contexts more supportive of teaching, learning, and family engagement than weakly organized programs [+W]
  • The Early Education Essentials is reliable across multiple ECE settings [+W]
  • The Early Education Essentials is sensitive to site-level differences [+W]
  • Some, but not all, measured organizational conditions are associated in expected directions with site-level ECE quality metrics [+W]
