Conceptual framework for data-driven decision making (DDDM) in education¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 design, 1 theoretical), q1 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The article presents a conceptual framework for data-driven decision making (DDDM) in education, developed by Mathematica at the request of the Gates Foundation. It states the framework "aims to provide a comprehensive picture of the DDDM process in education." The framework draws on Mathematica's own evaluations of strategic data use initiatives and on existing education data-use literature.
