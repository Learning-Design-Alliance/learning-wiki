Framework for Powerful Learning with Emerging Technology organized around three mutually reinforcing principles¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The framework positions emerging technologies as a resource that educators, caregivers, and learners can leverage, grounded in the understanding that emerging technologies "complement human intelligence rather than replace it." It organizes design guidance into three principles — Learner-Centered, Evidence-Based, and Skill-Building — each with practices and strategies, and recommends considering "the practices and strategies holistically across all three principles, rather than focusing on one principle, as the concepts are mutually reinforcing."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Developers should consider how edtech tools are implemented within systems of educators and learners, ensuring the technology does not interfere with human relationships that promote learning

Constraints¶

  • The framework is guidance for product design, selection, and implementation rather than a tested intervention

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 and lifelong learners using emerging-technology-enabled edtech tools

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • Powerful learning including agency, metacognition, critical thinking, creativity, and collaboration

Claims¶

  • The framework's recommendations were derived from a research and practices review plus 59 expert interviews [+W]

Related Theories¶

  • Powerful Learning with Emerging Technologies framework: three design principles
  • Powerful learning with technology is the product of meaningful use, inclusive access, and school leadership

Examples¶

  • Discourage mental offloading to AI by balancing guidance depth and frequency with application opportunities
  • Support learners to evaluate AI outputs by verifying sources, reporting errors, and overriding recommendations
  • Have learners bring their own thinking before using generative AI to enable productive struggle
  • Set clear AI guardrails positioning AI as a complement to human instruction with age-appropriate progressions
