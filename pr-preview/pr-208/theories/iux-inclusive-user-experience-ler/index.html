Inclusive user experience (IUX) as a learner-centered design approach for LER technologies¶

Theory · All theories Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The toolkit defines IUX as "a user-centered design approach that emphasizes inclusivity through the intentional process of creating tools that everyone can use," involving diverse communities in user research across demographics like race, gender, age, ability, and socioeconomic status. Within IUX, "accessibility and inclusivity are branches of usability" (Kohler, 2023). The framework contrasts IUX with typical user-centered design, which "prioritize[s] the needs of the average user" and "often excludes users who fall outside of the characteristics of the typical target user base."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Research must include diverse communities to capture varied perspectives, needs, and challenges

Constraints¶

  • The toolkit does not cover governance issues, technology systems changes, technical specifications, or developer guidelines

Target Learners¶

  • learners and workers of any age engaging with LER technologies, including historically and systematically excluded groups

Target Learning Objectives¶

  • equitable access to lifelong learning, skill recognition, and economic mobility through usable credential technologies
