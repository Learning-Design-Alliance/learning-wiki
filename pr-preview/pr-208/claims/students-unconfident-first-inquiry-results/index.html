Peterson, E.K. and Carrico, P. (2015). Laboratory Exercise in Behavioral Genetics Using Team-based Learning Strategies. Bioscene 41(2). https://visionandchange.org/files/2011/03/Revised-Vision-and-Change-Final-Report.pdf

Authors' report on the Week 2 independent experiments, the first inquiry-based laboratory in which students designed and implemented their own experiments after prior cookbook-format laboratories. The article reports "the majority of students were not confident with their results" for the two stated reasons.

"We found that, although they gathered data for their experiments, the majority of students were not confident with their results because they either were unable to solicit the behaviors or their results were unexpected."
