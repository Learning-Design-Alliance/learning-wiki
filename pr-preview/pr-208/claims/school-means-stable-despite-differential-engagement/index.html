Steven Wise 2019¶

Steven Wise, James Soland, Yuanchao Bo. (2019). The (non)impact of differential test taker engagement on aggregated scores. International Journal of Testing 20 (1), 55-77. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/the-nonimpact-of-differential-test-taker-engagement-on-aggregated-scores/

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The study analyzed data from the computer-based version of the PISA-Based Test for Schools to examine distortive effects of differential engagement on aggregated school-level scores. The abstract reports that "the school means were highly stable due to two factors," despite "considerable differential engagement among schools."

"The results showed that, although there was considerable differential engagement among schools, the school means were highly stable due to two factors."
