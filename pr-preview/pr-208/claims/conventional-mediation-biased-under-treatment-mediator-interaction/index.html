Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • RMPW decomposes total effects into natural direct and indirect effects, with the indirect effect further split into a pure indirect effect and a natural treatment-by-mediator interaction effect — related
  • RMPW requires relatively few assumptions about outcome and mediator distributions and outcome-model functional form compared with model-based alternatives — related
  • Correct specification of the mediator propensity score models is crucial when parametric RMPW is applied — related
  • RMPW was applied to test whether employment mediated the effect of an experimental welfare-to-work program on maternal depression — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
