Double-dose algebra policy evaluation in the Chicago Public Schools 2003¶

Double-dose algebra policy evaluation in the Chicago Public Schools. (2003). Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness. https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/ujree20

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Instrumental variable and regression discontinuity analysis of outcomes for students entering high school with math scores above the national median, who were not targeted by the policy. The article reports the divergent spillover: test scores among high-ability students improved whereas their grades declined.
