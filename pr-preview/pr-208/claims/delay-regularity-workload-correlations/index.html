Borchers 2025¶

Borchers, C., Xu, Y., & Pardos, Z. A. (2025). Workload Overload? Late Enrollment Leads to Course Dropout. Journal of Educational Data Mining, Volume 17, No 1. https://github.com/CAHLR/enrollment-procrastination-edm

q2 · i3 · associational · r2

Correlation matrix over student semester-level variables in the UC Berkeley transaction dataset, with all correlations reported as statistically significant at the p < .001 level. The article prints r = 0.52 for drop delay with enrollment delay, r = -0.07 and r = -0.09 for semester workload, and notes all variance inflation factors were below 2.

"The matrix shows mostly weak relationships between variables, with the highest correlation observed between drop delay and enrollment delay (r = 0.52). Semester Workload exhibited weak negative correlations with enrollment delay (r = -0.07) and activity regularity (r = -0.09)."
