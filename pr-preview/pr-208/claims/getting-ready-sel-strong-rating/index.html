Getting Ready 2025¶

Getting Ready. (2025). Program description, University of Nebraska—Lincoln. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/getting-ready/

q3 · i1 · causal · r?

Summary of two cluster randomized controlled trials in Nebraska with a combined 487 educationally at-risk preschool children; the page reports an Average Effect Size of ES = +0.22 for the SEL domain and notes results are consistently positive across both studies.

"Across both studies, results are consistently positive, qualifying Getting Ready for a Strong rating in the SEL domain and Promising (due to a sample of <350) in Family Engagement and Academic Performance."
