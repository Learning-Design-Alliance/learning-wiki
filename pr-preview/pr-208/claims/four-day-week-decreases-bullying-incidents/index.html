Emily Morton 2022¶

Emily Morton. (2022). Effects of 4-day school weeks on older adolescents: Examining impacts of the schedule on academic achievement, attendance, and behavior in high school. Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis. https://doi.org/10.3102/01623737221097420

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Difference-in-differences analysis of district-level panel data from Oklahoma estimating causal effects of the 4-day school week on high school outcomes. The study reports that 4-day school weeks "decrease per-pupil bullying incidents by approximately 39%". No standardized effect size is printed.

"Results indicate that 4-day school weeks decrease per-pupil bullying incidents by approximately 39% and per-pupil fighting incidents by approximately 31%, but have no detectable effect on other incident types, ACT scores, or attendance."
