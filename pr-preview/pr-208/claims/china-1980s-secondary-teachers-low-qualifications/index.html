Yang Xu 2010¶

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The article cites an SEDC investigation reported in Zuo (1990) finding that "the percentage of secondary school teachers with BA degrees was only 28%" in the 1980s, with associate degrees at 4% and secondary diplomas at 29%. The article reports these figures without describing the investigation's design.

"According to the SEDC investigation, in the 1980s, the percentage of secondary school teachers with BA degrees was only 28%, 12% of whom were Russian majors and 8% of whom were graduated with a 3-year BA program during the Cultural Revolution. Teachers with associate degrees and with secondary diplomas were 4% and 29% respectively (Zuo, 1990)."
