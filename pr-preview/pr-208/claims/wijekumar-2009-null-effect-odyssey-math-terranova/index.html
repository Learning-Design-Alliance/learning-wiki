Evidence¶

wijekumar-1¶

U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, What Works Clearinghouse. (2017, January). Primary Mathematics intervention report: Odyssey® Math. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/669

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Cluster randomized controlled trial across 32 schools in Delaware, New Jersey, and Pennsylvania; 122 teachers teaching 2,456 students analyzed as teacher-level means on the TerraNova Basic Battery. The review reports "no statistically significant difference" and characterizes the effect as indeterminate.

"Wijekumar et al. (2009) reported, and the WWC confirmed, no statistically significant difference between the Odyssey® Math and comparison groups on the mathematics achievement domain."
