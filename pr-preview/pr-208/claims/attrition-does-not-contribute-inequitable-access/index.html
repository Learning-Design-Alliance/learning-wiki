Eric Isenberg 2016¶

Eric Isenberg, Jeffrey Max, Philip Gleason, Matthew Johnson, Jonah Deutsch, Michael Hansen. (2016). Are High- and Low-Income Students Taught by Equally Effective Teachers? (Study Snapshot). Washington, DC: U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/

q3 · i? · associational · r?

As a key finding, the snapshot reports that teacher attrition patterns over the five study years do not contribute to inequitable access to effective teachers, distinguishing attrition from hiring and transfer as mechanisms.
