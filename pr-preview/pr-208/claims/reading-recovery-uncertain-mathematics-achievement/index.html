WWC synthesis of National Curriculum Assessment mathematics results three years after implementation in Burroughs-Lange & Douëtil (2007), UK Year 4 students in two comparison samples of 121 and 193 students. Neither comparison was statistically significant (p=0.19 and p=0.65).

"The research does not support claims that Reading Recovery® improved student mathematics achievement. This assessment is based on one study that meets WWC standards."
