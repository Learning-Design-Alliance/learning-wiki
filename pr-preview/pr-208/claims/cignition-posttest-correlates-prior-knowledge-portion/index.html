Evidence¶

Roschelle 2020¶

Roschelle, J., Cheng, B. H., Hodkowski, N., Haldar, L., & Neisler, J. (2020). Transfer for future learning of fractions within Cignition's microtutoring approach [Project Report]. Digital Promise. http://hdl.handle.net/20.500.12265/95

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Exploratory correlational analysis of the same 22 sessions per task, examining the relationship between the Cignition posttest and only the prior knowledge elicitation portion. Results were "statistically significant" at p < 0.01 for both tasks.

"Additionally, there were statistically significant results between the Cignition posttest and the prior knowledge (portion A) part of the task for both "Fraction Division" and "Fraction Generalization" tasks, significance level of p < 0.01 for both tasks."
