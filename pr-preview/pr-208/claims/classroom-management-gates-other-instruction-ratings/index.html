Daniel C. Humphrey 2009¶

Daniel C. Humphrey, Patrick M. Shields. (2009). High School Reform in Chicago Public Schools: An Overview. SRI International / Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=High+School+Reform+in+Chicago+Public+Schools+An+Overview

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Finding from the companion observation analysis, A Snapshot of High School Instruction in CPS, as reported in this overview's discussion of ongoing hiring and teacher quality. The report states unsatisfactory classroom management "precluded both novice and veteran teachers from reaching proficiency in other instruction areas"; no effect size was printed.

"Indeed, teachers with a “basic” or “unsatisfactory” rating for classroom management were unlikely to achieve high ratings on important instructional practices, especially instructional demand."
