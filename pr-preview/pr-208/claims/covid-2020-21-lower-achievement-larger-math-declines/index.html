Learning during COVID-19: Reading and math achievement in the 2020-2021 school year 2021¶

Learning during COVID-19: Reading and math achievement in the 2020-2021 school year. (2021). NWEA Research. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/learning-during-covid-19-reading-and-math-achievement-in-the-2020-2021-school-year/

The brief's summary of its comparison of spring 2021 MAP Growth achievement for 5.5 million students in grades 3-8 against pre-pandemic trends reports "lower achievement compared to a typical year, with larger declines relative to historical trends in math than in reading."
