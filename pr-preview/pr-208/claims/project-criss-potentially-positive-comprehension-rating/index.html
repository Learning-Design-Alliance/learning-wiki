Project CRISS® (CReating Independence through Student-owned Strategies) 2010¶

Project CRISS® (CReating Independence through Student-owned Strategies). (2010). WWC Intervention Report: Project CRISS®, Adolescent Literacy. U.S. Department of Education, What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/388

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

WWC synthesis of the two randomized studies meeting evidence standards (2,569 students in 41 schools). The potentially positive rating was assigned because one study showed statistically significant positive effects, no study showed negative effects, and one study showed indeterminate effects. The report states "The average improvement index for comprehension is +20 percentile points across the two studies."

"The average improvement index for comprehension is +20 percentile points across the two studies, with a range of –2 to +38 percentile points across findings."
