Hardy 2023¶

Hardy, A., Iwatani, E., Means, B., & Seylar, J. (2023). Rubrics for examining historical thinking skills in high school world history activities and student work. Digital Promise. (Original work published 2021). https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/111

The report describes its own design process: construct validity evidence from the literature (Hardy & Iwatani, 2021) and initial validity evidence from review by teachers and researchers (Iwatani, Means, Seylar & Hardy, 2021). The report cites these companion reports rather than presenting the validity data itself.

"The rubric went through an extensive design process, including consideration of alignment with national frameworks of historical thinking skills (described in Hardy & Iwatani, 2021) and careful review by teachers and researchers"
