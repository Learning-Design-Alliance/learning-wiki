Bridges in Mathematics 2025¶

Bridges in Mathematics. (2025). https://evidenceforessa.org/program/bridges-in-mathematics/

q2 · i1 · causal · r2

Earlier quasi-experimental study during the 2013-2014 school year covering kindergarten through second grade in four school districts across three states, with 800 students in Bridges classrooms matched to a virtual comparison group with similar demographics and prior achievement. The article reports Bridges students "scored significantly higher than matched peers (effect size = +0.06)".
