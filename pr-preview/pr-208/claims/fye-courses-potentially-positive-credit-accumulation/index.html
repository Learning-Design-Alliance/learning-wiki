Evidence¶

First Year Experience Courses 2016¶

First Year Experience Courses. (2016). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/662

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Quasi-experimental evaluation of a non-compulsory first year experience course at a large public university; the intervention group included 3,754 first-time freshmen and the comparison group 4,536. The WWC confirmed "a positive and statistically significant difference in retention" to the third semester.

"The author reported, and the WWC confirmed, that there was a positive and statistically significant difference in retention between students who participated in the first year experience course and those who did not."

First Year Experience Courses 2016 (2)¶

First Year Experience Courses. (2016). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/662

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental evaluation at a medium-sized midwestern public university with 1,456 first-time freshmen in 85 first year experience sections and 413 comparison freshmen. The review reports "no statistically significant difference" on one-year retention, an indeterminate effect.

"The author reported that there was no statistically significant difference between first year experience course participants and comparison participants on retention."

First Year Experience Courses 2016 (3)¶

First Year Experience Courses. (2016). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/662

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental study at the University of Texas at San Antonio with 133 first-time freshmen in the course and 1,163 comparison freshmen. The WWC confirmed "a positive and statistically significant difference" on retention to the second year.

"The author reported, and the WWC confirmed, that there was a positive and statistically significant difference between first year experience course participants and comparison participants on retention."
