Sadtono 1991¶

Sadtono, Eugenius, Ed. (1991). Language Acquisition and the Second/Foreign Language Classroom. Anthology Series 28. SEAMEO Regional Language Centre. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED367161

q2 · i? · review · r2

Nunan's survey of 50 empirical SLA studies reported in the literature over 25 years, classified on five analytical dimensions including data-collection environment. The survey found "only fifteen were actually carried out in language classrooms", with 28 in laboratory, simulated or naturalistic settings and 7 in mixed environments.

"The most surprising outcome of the survey was the fact that, of the fifty studies, only fifteen were actually carried out in language classrooms which were constituted for the purposes of teaching and learning rather than research."
