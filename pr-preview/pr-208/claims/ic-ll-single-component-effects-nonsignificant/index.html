q2 · i? · review · r?

Subgroup findings in Appendix A4.2 report Literature Logs only versus comparison at effect size 0.47 (factual) and 0.03 (interpretive), both ns, and Instructional Conversations only at 0.27 (factual) and 0.55 (interpretive), both ns. These subgroup results were excluded from the overall rating.

"Saunders and Goldenberg (1999) included two additional study groups (Instructional Conversations only and Literature Logs only). The results pertaining to these groups were not reviewed in this intervention report."
