Kevin Booker 2008¶

Kevin Booker, Steven Glazerman. (2008). Missouri's Teacher Career Ladder Program. Nashville, TN: National Center on Performance Incentives Conference. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/missouris-teacher-career-ladder-program

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Quasi-experimental matched comparison of 524 Missouri school districts over a nine-year period, using longitudinal data on district math and reading scores; "estimated effects range from small positive effects to no effect."

"The paper compares achievement levels in participating districts with those of a matched group of nonparticipating districts. The estimated effects range from small positive effects to no effect."
