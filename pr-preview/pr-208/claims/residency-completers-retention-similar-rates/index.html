Evidence¶

Rivkin 2023¶

Rivkin, S.G., Sartain, L., & Schiman, J.C. (2023). Training and retaining effective school leaders: Results from efforts to improve principal effectiveness in Chicago Public Schools. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/training-retaining-effective-school-leaders

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Personnel-data analysis of employment trajectories found "similar rates of transfer to other CPS schools and exit from the district" for residency completers versus other principals. The authors had worried training value might be lost to outside employment but found no supporting evidence.

"Residency-completer principals had similar rates of transfer to other CPS schools and exit from the district as other principals—which does mean, on the flip side, that they are also no more likely to stay in CPS than non-residency principals."
