Finding kinship in a far away land: The relevance of Japanese cultural perspectives to education research in Eastern Kentucky 2022¶

Finding kinship in a far away land: The relevance of Japanese cultural perspectives to education research in Eastern Kentucky. (2022). https://bit.ly/TANrefs

q2 · i? · design · r2

Teacher reflections from the 2022 data (N=23 context) show teachers framing CT as problem solving: one wrote it is "just a new name of a problem solving approach," another emphasized perseverance through exploring algorithms, and a third described analyzing, building and creating steps in a process.

"Computational thinking is not a new theory—this is just a new name of a problem solving approach that involves using innovative technology to assist in the process."
