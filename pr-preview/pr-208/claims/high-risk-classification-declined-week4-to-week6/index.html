Descriptive reporting from the participant section of the trial with 30 opted-in students: high-risk classification fell from 59% in week 4 to 39% by week 6 after dashboard access began; no statistical test is reported for this change.

"Nearly 59% of the participants were classified as "high-risk" in week 4 (i.e., before being given access to the dashboard). Meanwhile, by week 6 of the semester this fell to 39%."
