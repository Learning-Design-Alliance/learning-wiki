Gadgil 2016¶

Gadgil, S., & Louw, M. (2016). Rapid Cycle Pilots: Improving Ed-Tech Products through Feedback. Learning Media Design Center, Carnegie Mellon University. Report to Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/5364ec3a-0d6d-4252-8885-4484e657fde7

q2 · i? · design · r2

Debugging task administered during the second Puzzlets classroom observation, in which pairs rearranged incorrectly ordered tiles; all pairs solved the first task and most solved the more complex second task. The report states this "suggests that they learned problem-solving strategies within the game context."

"Students’ progress through the game levels and performance on the debugging task suggests that they learned problem-solving strategies within the game context after four in-class sessions using the game."

Gadgil 2016 (2)¶

Gadgil, S., & Louw, M. (2016). Rapid Cycle Pilots: Improving Ed-Tech Products through Feedback. Learning Media Design Center, Carnegie Mellon University. Report to Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/5364ec3a-0d6d-4252-8885-4484e657fde7

q2 · i? · design · r3

Comparison of two rounds of structured classroom observations of student pairs playing Puzzlets. Early observations showed one player deciding strategy while the other executed; after the teacher introduced driver-passenger role switching, "more evidence of turn taking and discussion about strategy was observed."

"Researchers noted more evidence of turn taking and discussion about strategy was observed in the second round of classroom observations."
