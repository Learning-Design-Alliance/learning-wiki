Guskey 1985¶

Guskey, Thomas R.; Gates, Sally L. (1985). A Synthesis of Research on Group-Based Mastery Learning Programs. Paper presented at the Annual Meeting of the American Educational Research Association, Chicago, IL. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED262088

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r2

Large-scale study by Guskey (1984) involving 117 junior and high school level teachers; the printed effect sizes of .61, 1.29, and -.59 are each a printed effect size with its value, showing mixed effects including the reduced confidence the authors call a "humbling effect."

"Guskey-(1984) found that teachers who use -mastery learning and see improvement in. 'student learning outcomes begin to feel much betterabout' teaching and their roles as teachers (effect size = .61), accept far greater personal responsibility for their students' learning successes and failures (effect size = 1,29), but express somewhat less confidence in their teaching abilities (effect size = -.59)."
