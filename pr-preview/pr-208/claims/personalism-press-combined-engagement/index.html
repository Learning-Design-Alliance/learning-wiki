q2 · i? · associational · r3

The report cites the Consortium's 1996 study based on analyses of 1994 citywide student survey data, in which personalism alone and press alone had only small influences on engagement while the combination elevated engagement substantially.

"That is, the most positive reports from students about aca- demic engagement came from schools that stu- dents rate as high on both personalism and press toward academic achievement."
