Waddington 1995¶

Waddington, Tad S. H. (1995). Why Mastery Matters. Paper presented at the Annual Meeting of the American Educational Research Association. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED394982

q2 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r1

The author meta-analyzed U.S. elementary mathematics mastery learning studies (appendix lists included studies with grades, weeks, mastery criteria, and effect sizes), obtaining a printed effect size of d = 0.78, described as "a weighted mean effect size of 0.78" with N=769, statistically identical with Stevenson and Lee's d = 0.78.
