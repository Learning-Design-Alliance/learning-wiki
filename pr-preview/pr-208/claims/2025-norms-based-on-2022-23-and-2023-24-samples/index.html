FAQ documentation of the norms' data source. The guide states the 2025 norms use the national sample of tests from 2022–23 and 2023–24, and separately notes that percentiles, CGPs, and proficiency projections are not comparable with reports reflecting the 2020 norms. No effect size is printed.
