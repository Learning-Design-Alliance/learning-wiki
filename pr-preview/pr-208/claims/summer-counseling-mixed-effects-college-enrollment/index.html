Evidence¶

Summer Counseling 2018¶

Summer Counseling. (2018). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, Transition to College. U.S. Department of Education, Institute of Education Sciences, What Works Clearinghouse. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/693

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Systematic WWC review of five randomized controlled trials meeting group design standards without reservations, covering 13,614 recent high school graduates in 10 locations. The review rated the domain as "Mixed effects"; the appendix tables print a domain-average effect size of 0.13 and improvement index of +5 across all studies.

"In the five studies that reported findings, the estimated impact of the intervention on outcomes in the college access and enrollment domain was positive and statistically significant in one study, positive and substantively important in one study, and indeterminate in three other studies."
