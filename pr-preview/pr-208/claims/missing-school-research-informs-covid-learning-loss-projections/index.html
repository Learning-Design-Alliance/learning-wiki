Megan Kuhfeld 2020¶

Megan Kuhfeld. (2020). Projecting the impact of the COVID-19 spring school closures on student learning. NWEA Research presentation. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/projecting-the-impact-of-the-covid-19-spring-school-closures-on-student-learning/

q1 · i? · theoretical · r1

The presentation's framing argument: because COVID interruptions are unprecedented, the author proposes borrowing from the existing literature on missing school — "regular summer breaks, school closures related to weather, and absenteeism" — to project pandemic learning loss. No projection results or effect sizes are printed on this page.

"While the COVID-19 learning interruptions are unprecedented in modern times, existing research on the impacts of missing school (due to regular summer breaks, school closures related to weather, and absenteeism) on learning can nonetheless inform projections of potential learning loss due to the pandemic."
