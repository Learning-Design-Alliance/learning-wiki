What Works Clearinghouse 2010¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2010). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report: Lindamood Phoneme Sequencing® (LiPS®). Students with Learning Disabilities. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/279

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Posttest math finding from the Torgesen et al. (2001) RCT (n = 50), as tabulated in the report's math appendix. The printed effect size of 0.30 on the Calculation subtest (mean difference 4.0, improvement index +12) was not statistically significant but exceeded the 0.25 substantively-important threshold; the report states "the effect on the Calculation subtest was positive and large enough to be considered substantively important".
