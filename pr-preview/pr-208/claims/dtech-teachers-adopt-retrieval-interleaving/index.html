Digital Promise 2017¶

Digital Promise. (2017). Learning by Design: How Design Tech High School Uses Research. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/93bddee8-5856-4faf-8758-71aae4ce8716

q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

Practitioner accounts in the case study describe adoption of low-stakes practice tests after professional development on effective learning strategies. Adoption is described anecdotally; no retention outcome data are printed.

"Cerra said after the training, several d.tech teachers began using regular, low-stakes practice tests to improve students’ retention of information."

Digital Promise 2017 (2)¶

Digital Promise. (2017). Learning by Design: How Design Tech High School Uses Research. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/93bddee8-5856-4faf-8758-71aae4ce8716

q2 · i? · design · r1

The report describes biology teacher Neal Addicott implementing interleaved practice via flashcard software that reintroduced material after an appropriate forgetting interval. The account is descriptive with no learning outcome measures.

"Addicott found software that would help him create flashcards, and then a sequence that would show students different material at spaced intervals of time."
