Counelis 1974¶

Counelis, James Steve. (1974). WICHE/NCHEMS Work and the Need for Black Box Research. University of San Francisco, Office of Institutional Studies. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED096925

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

The author's analytical review of the NCHEMS Data Element Dictionary (November 1973), Element No. 1107, coded with seven methods of instruction. He concludes that "there is no stated relation between instructional methods and learning outcomes," one of three deficiencies he identifies in the element.

"Thirdly, there is no stated relation between instructional methods and learning outcomes."
