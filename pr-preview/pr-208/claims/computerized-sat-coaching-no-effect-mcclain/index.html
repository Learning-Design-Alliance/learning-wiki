ACT/SAT Test Preparation and Coaching Programs 2016¶

ACT/SAT Test Preparation and Coaching Programs. (2016). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/664

q3 · i0 · quant-synthesis · r2

RCT in a suburban Maryland high school where students spent 26 hours over 9 weeks with one of two computerized coaching programs versus no test preparation. The WWC reports the domain average effect size as -0.04, improvement index -2, not statistically significant; individual contrasts were -0.08 (-3, p .79) and -0.00 (0, p .99).
