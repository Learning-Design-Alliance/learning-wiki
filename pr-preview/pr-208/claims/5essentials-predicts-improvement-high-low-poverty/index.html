Quantitative study using pre-pandemic 2011–18 CPS data examined whether the 5Essentials Survey predicts school improvement; it found the survey "predicted a school’s effectiveness and ability to improve" across poverty levels and school levels. No effect size is printed.

"The quantitative study found that 5Essentials Survey measures predicted a school’s effectiveness and ability to improve in both high- and low-poverty schools at both the elementary and high school level."
