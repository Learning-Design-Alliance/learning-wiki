Descriptive comparison of fall and spring 2023-24 i-Ready math scale scores across the three state PreK4 investment categories, shown in Figure 1. The report states the "scale score point gap between High and Growing states doubled from 3 points in Fall (342 vs. 339) to 6 points in Spring."

"in the 2023 -24 school year, the scale score point gap between High and Growing states doubled from 3 points in Fall (342 vs. 339) to 6 points in Spring."

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Figure 2 reports the proportion of kindergarten students improving or maintaining grade-level math performance by state category; the report contrasts "70% of kindergarten students in High states" with 60% in Growing states. No significance test is reported for this contrast.

"only 60% of kindergarten students in Growing states improved a grade level or maintained on-grade-level performance in math from fall to spring , versus 70% of kindergarten students in High states"
