q2 · i? · review · r3

The paper cites Darling-Hammond et al. (2009), a nationwide survey finding 59% of teachers rated content-related professional development as useful or very useful, and argues one-shot sessions are not as impactful as consistent, content-specific opportunities.

"Darling-Hammond, Wei, Andree, Richardson, & Orphanos (2009) suggest that in order for professional development to have an impact on teaching and learning, it must consist of roughly 50 hours in a specific area ."
