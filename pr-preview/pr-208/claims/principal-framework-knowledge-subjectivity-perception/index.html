q1 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative case study of Walton Elementary School across two pilot years, drawing on principal and teacher interviews. The case concludes that "the principal's lack of knowledge of the Framework and inability to rate teaching practice led teachers to believe the pilot evaluation was subjective," and many teachers disengaged as a result.
