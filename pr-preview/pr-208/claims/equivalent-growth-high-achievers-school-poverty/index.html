Michael Dahlin and Beth Tarasawa 2013¶

Michael Dahlin and Beth Tarasawa. (2013). A Level Playing Field? How College Readiness Standards Change the Accountability Game. Northwest Evaluation Association. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/a-level-playing-field-college-readiness-standards-whitepaper/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

HLM analysis of school poverty rate as a predictor of growth in three-level hierarchical models applied to the elementary and middle school cohorts in reading and mathematics. The report states "Only modest differences in growth rates were observed among all high achievers", with direction varying by subject. No effect sizes are printed for this finding.

"Only modest differences in growth rates were observed among all high achievers, with poorer schools producing trivially smaller growth in math achievement and poorer elementary schools showing slightly larger growth rates in reading achievement."
