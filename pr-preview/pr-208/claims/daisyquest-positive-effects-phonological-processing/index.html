Evidence¶

DaisyQuest for Preschool Children 2006¶

DaisyQuest for Preschool Children. (2006). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r?

WWC synthesis of two randomized controlled trials (Foster et al., 1994; Lonigan et al., 2003) totaling 68 preschool children. The domain average effect size across studies was 0.68, and the report states "The average improvement index for phonological processing is +25 percentile points across two studies."

"The average improvement index for phonological processing is +25 percentile points across two studies, with a range of –13 to +34 percentile points across findings."
