A national survey statistic reported in the in-service career development section, attributed to the 1998 NCES Fast Response Survey System teacher survey, whose chart also shows 20 percent for integrating educational technology. It is a descriptive percentage; no effect size is printed.

"Although many educa- tors and policy analysts consider educational technology a vehicle for transforming educa- tion, only 20 percent of teachers feel well pre- pared to integrate education technology into classroom instruction."
