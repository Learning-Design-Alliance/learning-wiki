Cambridge, MA: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/elementarygrade-math-programs-in-the-pittsburgh-public-schools-a-comparison-of-everyday-mathematics-and-harcourt-math

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Quasi-experimental analysis of district data comparing two elementary math programs. The report "finds no difference in the effects" of Everyday Mathematics and Harcourt Math on student achievement; no effect size or test statistic is printed on the page.

"A quasi-experimental analysis of data from the Pittsburgh Public Schools finds no difference in the effects of two elementary-level math programs—Everyday Mathematics and Harcourt Math—on student achievement."
