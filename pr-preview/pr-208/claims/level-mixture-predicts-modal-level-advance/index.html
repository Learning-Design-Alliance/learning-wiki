Evidence¶

Snyder 1975¶

Snyder, S. S. & Feldman, D. H. (1975). An Experimental Test of the Effects of Internal and External Disequilibrium on Spatial Reasoning Development. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED118211

q2 · i1 · causal · r2

Analysis of variance on posttest Modal Level revealed a significant main effect of Level Mixture (F2,54=3.09, p<.05). Internal contrasts showed "high mix subjects advancedin Modal Level significantly more often than did mid mix and low mix subjectscombined" (F1,54=5.68, p<.02). The delayed posttest replicated this.
