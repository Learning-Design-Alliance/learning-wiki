Evidence¶

Arthur 1992¶

Arthur, L., & Hurd, S. (Eds.). (1992). The Adult Language Learner: A Guide to Good Teaching Practice. Centre for Information on Language Teaching and Research. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED352829

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

The guide asserts, citing Stern, that "the affective (i.e. emotional) component contributes at least as much as and often more to language learning than the cognitive skills". The guide offers no direct evidence for this; it is an attributed expert claim used to argue that feeling psychologically comfortable is a major learning need.

"It has even been suggested that the affective (i.e. emotional) component contributes at least as much as and often more to language learning than the cognitive skills6."
