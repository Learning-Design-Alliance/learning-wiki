Sharon Greenberg & Anthony S. Bryk. (2024). Change that matters: Progress made by Chicago Public Schools shows that successful change takes time, focus, and patience. Kappan, V105 N6. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/change-that-matters

The article describes Figure 1, UChicago Consortium trend data on two indicators — program coherence and teacher-principal trust — from 1994 to 2017, reporting that "teachers report increasing coherence in their own program improvement efforts and higher levels of trust in their principals."

"Longitudinal surveys from teachers about the quality of their school’s leadership show the cumulative impact of Chicago’s investments in principal development. The consortium tracked two key indicators over more than two decades."
