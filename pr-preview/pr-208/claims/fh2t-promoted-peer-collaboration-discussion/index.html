Tarik Buli, Stefani Pautz Stephenson, Rebecca Banks, and Amanda Wortman. (2025). From Here to There (FH2T): Educator Perceptions of Game-Based Algebraic Learning. Digital Promise white paper. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/7dcf9d16-b5ad-4067-be9f-be30319b1185

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Qualitative findings from the confirmatory study with 11 educators, several of whom implemented FH2T through partner or small group work. Educator Bianca Summers reported that "students have gone from asking me questions on how to work the problems to asking each other for help."

"Bianca Summers found that in her classroom, "students have gone from asking me questions on how to work the problems to asking each other for help. Now, they come to me when they are in disagreement with each other.""
