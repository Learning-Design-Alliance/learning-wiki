My Math Academy evidence summary 2022¶

My Math Academy evidence summary. (2022). Age of Learning. https://www.ageoflearning.com/schools/systemcheck/

q3 · i1 · causal · r?

A randomized study assigned 20 kindergarten and transition kindergarten classrooms at four Title I elementary schools in urban Southern California to My Math Academy (n=233) or business-as-usual control (n=195). Treatment students "significantly outperformed the control group" on the TEMA-3.

"Students in My Math Academy classrooms significantly outperformed the control group in math knowledge and skills as measured by the TEMA-3 (effect size = +0.23, p=.03)"
