Across four years of Summer Bridge achievement analyses, the report found little evidence for the concern that programs under high-stakes testing benefit only students close to test-score cutoffs, calling uniform gains one of the most positive findings.

"Summer Bridge produced relatively uniform gains across de mographic and achievement groups . Third graders who were at the highest risk of failure benefited the most from the program."
