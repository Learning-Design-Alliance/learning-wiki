Evidence¶

John Q. Easton 2001¶

John Q. Easton, Todd Rosenkranz, Anthony S. Bryk. (2001). Annual CPS Test Trend Review, 2000: Elementary School Test Score Trends in Chicago Public Schools. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/annual-cps-trend-review-2000

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Sensitivity re-analysis of the trend data using an equated Rasch score (logit) to adjust for different test levels given to students of the same age. The brief reports results differ slightly by age, especially for nine-year-olds, whose scores do not drop off due to large numbers taking a lower test level.

"The results of the Rasch analysis suggest the same overall finding of slowing and in some cases declining test score trends."
