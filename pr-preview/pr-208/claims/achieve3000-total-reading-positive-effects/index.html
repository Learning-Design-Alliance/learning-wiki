Achieve3000 Evidence Rating Summary 2017¶

Achieve3000 Evidence Rating Summary. (2017). https://www.achieve3000.com

q2 · i1 · causal · r?

A comparison study across suburban and urban U.S. districts compared students who used Achieve3000 and those who did not, finding "significant positive effects" on Total Reading Scores with the printed effect sizes above. This qualifies Achieve3000 for the Strong category.

"The study found significant positive effects for the combined sample of sixth graders (effect size =+0.22) and ninth graders (effect size = +0.44) for a weighted average of +0.29 on Total Reading Scores."
