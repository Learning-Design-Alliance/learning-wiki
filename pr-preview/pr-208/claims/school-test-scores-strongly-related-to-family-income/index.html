q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Observational analysis of 2019 data for Illinois schools plotted in Figure 1, relating percent low income to ISAT proficiency; the figure note reports correlations of r = -.77 for ELA and r = -.79 for math. At schools serving very few low-income students, 70-80 percent of students met testing norms.

"School percent low income is correlated with ELA proficiency at -.77 and math proficiency at -.79, where zero is no relationship and -1.0 is perfectly related."
