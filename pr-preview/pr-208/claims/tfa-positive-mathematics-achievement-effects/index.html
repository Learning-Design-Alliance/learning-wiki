Teach For America 2016¶

Teach For America. (2016). WWC Intervention Report: Teach For America. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://www.teachforamerica.org

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

This is a systematic review synthesis (WWC Intervention Report, mathematics achievement domain) of six studies meeting WWC group design standards. The report states: "In four of the six studies, the estimated impact of TFA teachers on mathematics achievement was positive and statistically significant", with an average improvement index of +4 percentile points and a medium to large extent of evidence.

"In four of the six studies, the estimated impact of TFA teachers on mathematics achievement was positive and statistically significant; two of these studies meet WWC group design standards without reservations."
