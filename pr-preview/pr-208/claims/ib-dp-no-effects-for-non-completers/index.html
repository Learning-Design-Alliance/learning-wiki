The report's key findings state the completion rate and the null result for non-completers: "There are no effects of IB DP participation for the 38 percent of students who do not complete the program." No effect size or test statistic is printed for the null finding.

"Only 62 percent of students who enter the IB DP Cohort in ninth grade subsequently enroll in the program in eleventh grade. There are no effects of IB DP participation for the 38 percent of students who do not complete the program."
