q2 · i2 · causal · r2

Follow-up study tracking 445 below-grade-level students from the original sample through Spring 2025 "with continued program exposure", qualifying the program for a Promising rating under the Struggling Readers banner.

"Students in Spalding schools significantly outperformed comparison peers on the AASA (effect size = +0.33), with twice as many Spalding students scoring proficient or above compared to comparison peers (28% vs. 14%)."
