Evidence¶

Li 2019¶

Li, S., & Meyer, J. P. (2019). Simulation study for evaluating MAP® Growth™ grade-level item pools. Portland, OR: NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/simulation-study-for-evaluating-map-growth-item-pools-with-grade-level-constraints/

q2 · i? · design · r2

Mathematics simulation conditions with off-grade simulees (3,000 test events per grade and content area; 500 simulees at each of six true RIT points one grade down or up). The all-grade pool was "1.01 to 3.42 times more efficient" than the on-grade pool for off-grade simulees.

"SEMs were uniformly larger for off-grade simulees than they were for on-grade simulees, regardless of the type of item pool, but the increase was largest for the on-grade pool"
