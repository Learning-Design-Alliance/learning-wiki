Bryk 1993¶

Bryk, A. S., Easton, J. Q., Kerbow, D., Rollow, S. G., & Sebring, P. A. (1993). A View from the Elementary Schools: The State of Reform in Chicago. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/view-elementary-schools-state-reform-chicago

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Survey-indicator analysis applying the three-sites-of-power indicator sets to Chicago public elementary schools. The report states "We estimate that 39 to 46 percent of the elementary schools fall into this category" for consolidated principal power, and separately estimates strong democracy at 23 to 32 percent and adversarial politics at 4 to 9 percent.

"We estimate that 39 to 46 percent of the elementary schools fall into this category. In contrast, strong democracy schools are more likely to have a principal who engenders discussion about key educational issues"
