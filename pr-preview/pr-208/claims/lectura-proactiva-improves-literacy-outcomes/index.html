q3 · i2 · causal · r?

Two randomized studies in urban Texas schools with high populations of EL learners, totaling 140 students, randomly assigned first graders below the 25th percentile in literacy to Lectura Proactiva or business-as-usual. The summary reports an “Average Effect Size” of “+0.49” and that “Lectura Proactiva students scored significantly higher on Woodcock-Johnson measures.”
