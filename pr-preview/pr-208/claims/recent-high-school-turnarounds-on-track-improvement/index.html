de la Torre 2013¶

Descriptive analysis of the small set of high schools reformed under models other than Reconstitution. The authors state they are "hesitant to make sweeping conclusions" given the small number of schools and limited data; no effect size is printed.

"Among those schools with at least one year of data, however, six out of seven showed some improvement in on-track rates above the comparison schools."
