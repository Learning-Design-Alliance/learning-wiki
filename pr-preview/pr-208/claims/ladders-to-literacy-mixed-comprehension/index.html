What Works Clearinghouse 2007¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2007). Ladders to Literacy for Kindergarten Students. WWC Intervention Report. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/259

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

WWC synthesis of three studies reporting the vocabulary construct (PPVT). The report states the authors "did not find a statistically significant effect of Ladders to Literacy on the Peabody Picture Vocabulary Test for any study"; the domain average effect size was 0.23, all non-significant, yielding a mixed rating.
