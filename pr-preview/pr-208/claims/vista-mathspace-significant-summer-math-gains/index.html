Christina Luke 2016¶

Christina Luke, Christa M. Simone, Aubrey T. Francisco. (2016). Rapid Cycle Pilots: Summer Ed-Tech Programs. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/8017846c-4099-4af3-92e4-462cd5d6262d

q2 · i? · design · r2

Mathspace pilot study brief for Vista Unified, a pre-post benchmark analysis of 27 rising 7th graders with t-tests on pre and post scores. The brief reports "statistically significant gains" but prints no effect size, and cautions results "should be treated with caution" because participation and engagement were low.

"Despite the educator’s concerns about student engagement, students who participated in the Mathspace summer learning program showed statistically significant gains in their math scores from the beginning of the summer to the end."
