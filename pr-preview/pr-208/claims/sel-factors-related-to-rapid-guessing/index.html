Evidence¶

Emily Wolk 2018¶

Emily Wolk, Sharon Bi, Tran Keys. (2018). Are test and academic disengagement related? Implications for measurement and practice. Educational Assessment 24(2), 119-134. https://www.nwea.org/research-publications/

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The study tested whether social-emotional factors, including "low academic self-efficacy and self-management", relate to rapid guessing; the abstract reports these factors are related to rapid guessing behavior.

"We also examine whether social-emotional factors like low academic self-efficacy and self-management, which research suggests are the root causes of academic disengagement, are also related to rapid guessing behavior"
