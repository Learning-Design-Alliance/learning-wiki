New Studies Examine Diversity Among CPS Principals 2023¶

New Studies Examine Diversity Among CPS Principals, Explore Their Previous Experiences. (2023). NORC at the University of Chicago. https://www.norc.org

q2 · i? · associational · r?

An observational analysis of eight years of CPS personnel- and student-level demographic data (2012-13 to 2019-20) from district-run, non-charter schools, aggregated to the school-by-year level. The report found principals "more diverse in terms of race/ethnicity than principals in other urban areas and nationally." No effect size is printed.

"researchers examined the landscape of the CPS principalship over time and found that CPS principals are more diverse in terms of race/ethnicity than principals in other urban areas and nationally, and that newer principals are more likely to be Black than the overall CPS principal population."
