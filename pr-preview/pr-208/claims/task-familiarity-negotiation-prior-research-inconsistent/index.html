Arslanyilmaz 2010¶

Arslanyilmaz, A., & Pedersen, S. (2010). Enhancing negotiation of meaning through task familiarity using subtitled videos in an online TBLL environment. TOJET: The Turkish Online Journal of Educational Technology, 9(2). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ898004

q1 · i? · causal · r1

Narrative synthesis in the introduction: the article reports that Robinson (2001) found students given an unfamiliar map engaged in more negotiated interaction, that Hardy and Moore (2004) found students without content support generated significantly more negotiation sequences, and that Yule et al. found no significant difference. Attributed second-hand; designs described only as the article describes them.

"the research on task familiarity shows some inconsistency, with Yule, Powers and McDonald (1992) reporting no significant difference between groups in a study in which the experimental group had access to sample transcripts of similar tasks and the control group did not"
