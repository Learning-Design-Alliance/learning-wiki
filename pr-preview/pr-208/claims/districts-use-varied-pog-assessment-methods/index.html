q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

Landscape scan of interviews with 16 districts, seven of which had assessments in place. The report found "a wide variance in how districts are planning for and implementing assessment," with districts often using different assessments for yearly data collection versus ongoing monitoring, and at secondary versus elementary levels.

"Of the sixteen school districts included in this report, we found a wide variance in how districts are planning for and implementing assessment on future-ready skills named in their Portrait of a Graduate."
