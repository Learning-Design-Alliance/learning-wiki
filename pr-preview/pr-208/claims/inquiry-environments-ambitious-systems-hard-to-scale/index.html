Evidence¶

Roschelle 2021¶

Roschelle, J., Mazziotti, C. & Means, B. (2021). Scaling Up Design of Inquiry Environments. In C. Chinn & R.G. Duncan et al (Eds.), International Handbook on Learning and Inquiry. Routledge. ISBN 9781138922600. https://isbnsearch.org/isbn/9781138922600

q1 · i? · review · r3

Theoretical argument in the chapter's Section 3, offered as a conjecture building on prior work. The authors argue inquiry involves new teacher and student roles, active engagement that cannot be scaled by distributing resources, and system-level coherence needs. No empirical test is reported for this framing itself.

"We conjecture that inquiry environments are challenging to scale because they are “Ambitious Learning Activity Systems” (building on the phrase in Roschelle, Knudsen & Hegedus, 2010)."
