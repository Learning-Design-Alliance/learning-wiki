Ehrlich 2020¶

Ehrlich, S.B., Connors, M.C., Stein, A.G., Francis, J., Easton, J.Q., Kabourek, S.E., & Farrar, I.C. (2020). Closer to home: More equitable pre-k access and enrollment in Chicago. Chicago, IL: UChicago Consortium on School Research, NORC at the University of Chicago, and Start Early. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/closer-to-home

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Post-hoc comparisons of regression-predicted enrollment rates within student groups from the pre-policy multilevel logistic models. Predicted enrollment fell from about 51 percent for a Black student within 0.25 miles to 31 percent at 1.0 mile; the article notes results were similar for lowest-income students and students in neighborhoods with large concentrations of Black residents.

"predicted enrollment rates for a Black student who lived within 0. 25 miles from a school with any pre-k was around 51 percent; however, a Black student who lived 1.0 mile away from a school with any pre-k had a predicted enrollment rate 20 percentage points lower, at 31 percent."
