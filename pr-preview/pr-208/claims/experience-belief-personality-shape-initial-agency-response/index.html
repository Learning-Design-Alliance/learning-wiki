Evidence¶

Qi 2022¶

Qi, G. Y., & Wang, Y. (2022). Challenges and responses: A Complex Dynamic Systems approach to exploring language teacher agency in a blended classroom. The JALT CALL Journal, 18(1). https://doi.org/10.29140/jaltcall.v18n1.569

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Findings from Period 1 of the case study, synthesising written reflection and email interview data from one teacher two weeks before semester start. The authors identify three factors: familiarity with course content and technology, belief in flexible and equal learning, and a challenge-seeking personality.

"it appeared that the interaction between her experience, her belief in TELL and her personality played a dominant agentic role in her positive response to the possible challenges brought about by the change to blended delivery."
