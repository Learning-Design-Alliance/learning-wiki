Talent Development High School 2005¶

Talent Development High School. (2005). https://eric.ed.gov/

q2 · i0 · causal · r2

Quasi-experimental matched-comparison study in Philadelphia comparing Talent Development schools to matched control schools on district-administered attendance measures, across up to three cohorts per school over 3 years. The summary reports an "average effect size of +0.17" for attendance, significant at the student level only, earning a "Promising" evidence rating.

"Adjusting for prior attendance, the attendance outcomes had an average effect size of +0.17. This was significant at the student level, but not at the school level, and qualified Talent Development High Schools for the “Promising” level of evidence."
