SRA Real Math Building Blocks PreK 2007¶

SRA Real Math Building Blocks PreK. (2007). WWC Intervention Report. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://www.gse.buffalo.edu/org/buildingblocks/

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

WWC synthesis of two randomized controlled trials (Clements & Sarama 2006, 28 teachers/202 children; 2007, 4 teachers/53-61 children) in New York State preschools. The WWC rated the intervention as having "positive effects on math", with domain-average effect size 1.07 and improvement indices from +27 to +42 percentile points.

"The average improvement index for math is +36 percentile points across the two studies, with a range of +27 to +42 per-centile points across findings."
