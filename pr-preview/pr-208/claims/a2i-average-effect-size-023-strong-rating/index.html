A2i Professional Support System 2013¶

A2i Professional Support System. (2013). Evidence review, Learning Ovations. https://www.learningovations.com

q3 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r?

Synthesis of three randomized-controlled studies of A2i, totaling 1,420 students, found an "overall average effect size was +0.23", meeting the review's Strong category threshold for differentiated literacy instruction.

"Three studies conducted on A2i qualified for inclusion. The overall average effect size was +0.23. This qualified A2i for the “Strong” category and demonstrates that the impact of differentiated literacy instruction via the A2i Professional Support System has been examined through rigorous, randomized-controlled studies in multiple contexts."
