Evidence¶

Yu-Ling You; Shih-Guey Joe 2001¶

Yu-Ling You; Shih-Guey Joe. (2001). Investigating the Metacognitive Awareness and Strategies of English-Majored University Student Writers. ERIC Document ED465281. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED465281

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Interview findings on rhetoric conventions among the nine skilled EFL writers. One subject gave a clear mental image of a well-organized English article with introductory paragraph, thesis statement, and topic sentences, and her writing sample matched that image, per the article.

"All the subjects in this study reported that they had acquired such concepts as thesis statement and topic sentence, which are preferred in contemporary American English but new to Taiwanese EFL students, some time during the years when they were learning English."
