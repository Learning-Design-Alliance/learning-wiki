Meta-regression analyses from the review comparing instructional methods while adjusting for content domains and study characteristics; the named contrasts were reported as statistically significant, with no effect size printed for these contrasts.

"In meta‐regressions that adjusted for methods, content domains, and other study characteristics, they had significantly larger effect sizes than computer‐assisted instruction, coaching of personnel, incentives, and progress monitoring. Peer‐assisted instruction also had significantly larger effect sizes than medium‐group instruction."
