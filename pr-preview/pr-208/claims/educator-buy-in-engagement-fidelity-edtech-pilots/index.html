Process Results section, Educator Buy-in subsection, drawing on teacher interviews and surveys across the six pilots: Onslow's "lack of autonomy contributed to their low usage and negative feelings about the tool."

Discussion section example: Highline teachers who reviewed and recommended Ratio Rancher "continued to be highly engaged in the pilot, and implemented the tool with fidelity." Mineola teachers were similarly encouraged by colleague endorsement.

"a group of teachers at Highline Public Schools reviewed the capabilities of several different math tools before recommending Ratio Rancher to district administrators. These teachers continued to be highly engaged in the pilot, and implemented the tool with fidelity."
