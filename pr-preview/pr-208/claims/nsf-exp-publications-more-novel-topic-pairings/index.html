Pilot bibliometric analysis comparing novelty scores of NSF EXP papers (443 of 506 papers matched in OpenAlex from 95 of 115 awards) against a random sample of 1000 OpenAlex articles from the same primary fields and 2017-2024 period. The authors report the comparison "seemed to be the case" and note the method is "still under development and subject to error."
