Elizabeth Bozoti Pasin and Reinaldo Luiz Bozelli 2016¶

Elizabeth Bozoti Pasin and Reinaldo Luiz Bozelli. (2016). An Analysis of Curriculum and Undergraduate Students' Discourses about Environmental Education: A Debate Concerning Teacher Training. International Journal of Environmental & Science Education, 11(18), 12197-12234. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1122668

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Document analysis of the curricular matrix and pedagogical project of the biological sciences teacher-training course at UFJF. The analysis found "a predominance of hours devoted to courses that discuss the environment from the biological perspective."

"The curricular matrix of this undergraduate course showed a predominance of hours devoted to courses that discuss the environment from the biological perspective in contrast to a small number of courses that discuss and promote EE from other perspectives."
