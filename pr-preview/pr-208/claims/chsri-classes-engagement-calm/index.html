Evidence¶

David Stevens 2008¶

David Stevens, Sue Sporte, Sara Ray Stoelinga, Alissa Bolz. (2008). Lessons from High Performing Small High Schools in Chicago. Consortium on Chicago School Research, University of Chicago. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/lessons-high-performing-small-high-schools-chicago

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Ethnographic observation of 23 classes; researchers assigned each class an overall engagement level (high, medium, or low) based on its most consistent level. The sample was "characterized by high or medium engagement levels" with only one low-engagement classroom.

"Overall, the sample of classes that we observed was characterized by high or medium engagement levels. Even if students were not genuinely interested in the class activities, they were still generally on-task, paying attention and responding. Only one classroom in our sample could be described as having low student engagement."
