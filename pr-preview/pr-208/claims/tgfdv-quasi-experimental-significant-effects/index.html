What Works Clearinghouse 2006¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2006). WWC Intervention Report: Too Good for Drugs and Violence. https://www.mendezfoundation.org

q2 · i2 · causal · r?

Quasi-experimental study (Bacon, 2001b) with 394 students in 11 classrooms in one large Florida high school; the WWC analysis sample was 201 students. Appendix A3 prints an effect size of 0.56 for social and resistance skills ("0.56 Statistically significant") and 0.55 for emotional competence; the study average was 0.48, statistically significant.
