Evidence¶

Angela Johnson 2020¶

Angela Johnson, Megan Kuhfeld. (2020). Impacts of school entry age on academic growth through 2nd Grade: A multi-state regression discontinuity analysis. NWEA Research working paper. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/impacts-of-school-entry-age-on-academic-growth-through-2nd-grade-a-multi-state-regression-discontinuity-analysis/

q3 · i? · causal · r?

Multi-state regression discontinuity analysis of assessment data from three states, reported in the paper's abstract. The authors state that "Being a year older leads to higher initial achievement"; no effect size is printed in the available text.

"Being a year older leads to higher initial achievement and higher kindergarten growth rates but lower growth rates during 1st and 2nd grades."
