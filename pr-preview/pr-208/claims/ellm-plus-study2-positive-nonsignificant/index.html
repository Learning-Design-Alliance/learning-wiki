Second PCER randomized study conducted one year later in 28 classrooms, measuring TERA-3, Woodcock, PPVT and TOLD. The difference was positive but not significant, with the printed value +0.12; equivalence was not tested.

"The second study took place one year later in 28 of these classrooms, and found a positive but not significant difference of +0.12 across TERA-3, Woodcock, PPVT and TOLD tests."
