Preschool Attendance in Chicago Public Schools: Relationships with Learning Outcomes and Reasons for Absences 2014¶

Preschool Attendance in Chicago Public Schools: Relationships with Learning Outcomes and Reasons for Absences. (2014). https://www.sciencedirect.com

q2 · i? · associational · r?

District-wide observational analysis of pre-kindergarten attendance in a large urban district reporting subgroup prevalence; the study reports that "absence in pre-kindergarten was widespread, particularly among African American students and those who entered pre-kindergarten with the lowest skills." No effect size is printed.

"Results indicated that absence in pre-kindergarten was widespread, particularly among African American students and those who entered pre-kindergarten with the lowest skills."
