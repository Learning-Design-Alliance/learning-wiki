Correlations (rho) of conditional Z-scores of school-level gains, produced via contrast matrices from CP models fit to all five years of MAP Growth data, comparing spring-to-spring gains to fall-to-spring gains in grades three and four (Figure 3). The printed correlations are r = .399 and r = .705.
