Evidence¶

Charter School Performance in New Mexico 2019¶

Charter School Performance in New Mexico. (2019). Center for Research on Education Outcomes, Stanford University. https://credo.stanford.edu/reports/item/charter-school-performance-in-new-mexico-2/

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Matched VCR comparison (Figure 7a) benchmarking New Mexico online charter students against brick-and-mortar charter students, whose performance is set to zero; deficits reported in days of learning.

"To be specific, they are behind brick-and-mortar charter students by 159 days of learning in reading. In math, the academic deficit for online charter students relative to students in brick-and-mortar charters is equivalent to 130 days of learning."

Charter School Performance in New Mexico 2019 (2)¶

Charter School Performance in New Mexico. (2019). Center for Research on Education Outcomes, Stanford University. https://credo.stanford.edu/reports/item/charter-school-performance-in-new-mexico-2/

q2 · i? · causal · r2

The report's own interpretation (Figures 7 and 7a discussion) of why the aggregate reading effect in Figure 3 is null: online students' negative growth cancels brick-and-mortar students' positive growth.

"the negative relative growth of online charter students is sufficiently large as to wipe out the positive relative growth of brick-and-mortar charter students in reading, which leads to the lack of overall New Mexico charter school effects on reading growth in Figure 3."
