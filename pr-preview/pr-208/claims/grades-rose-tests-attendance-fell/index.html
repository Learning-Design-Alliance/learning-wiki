Porter 2026¶

Porter, S., Ramirez Richter, R., de la Torre, M., & Allensworth, E.M. (2026). Research and Engagement for Systems Transformation: Building Knowledge, Capacity, and Coherence for Equitable Education Systems. UChicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

In its data-use priority section, the agenda describes the post-pandemic pattern that "course grades have risen while test scores and attendance have fallen," framing it as raising urgent questions about what learning measures signal. No statistics are printed.

"Families and educators need clear, trustworthy information about students' academic progress. Yet, in the wake of the COVID-19 pandemic, there is a disconnect: course grades have risen while test scores and attendance have fallen."
