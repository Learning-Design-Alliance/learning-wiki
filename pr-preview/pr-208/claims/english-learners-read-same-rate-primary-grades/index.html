Evidence¶

Gersten 2007¶

Gersten, R., Baker, S.K., Shanahan, T., Linan-Thompson, S., Collins, P., & Scarcella, R. (2007). Effective Literacy and English Language Instruction for English Learners in the Elementary Grades: A Practice Guide (NCEE 2007-4011). Washington, DC: National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/6

q2 · i? · review · r2

The guide reports, in Recommendation 1, criterion-related validity and growth studies indicating comparable early-grade reading growth. Much of this evidence "comes from research in Canada and from schools providing intensive and systematic instruction for all children" with supplementary support for those falling behind.

"These studies have demonstrated that English learners can learn to read in English at the same rate as their peers in the primary grades (K–2)."
