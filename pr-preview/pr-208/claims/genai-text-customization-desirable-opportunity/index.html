In reactions to Scenario 1, "Participants broadly agreed that using AI to improve texts was a desirable opportunity," with several educators linking interest-aligned texts to greater engagement and comprehension.

"Participants broadly agreed that using AI to improve texts was a desirable opportunity, particularly for customization to students interests."

Stephenson 2025 (2)¶

Stephenson, S. P., Leones, T., Kamdar, D., Prado, Y., & Ddamulira, J. (2025). A National Context of Generative AI for Reading to Support English Learners. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/73c883c0-4652-499d-aeb2-74f773749c60

"Participants also generally agreed that there is a strong and desirable use case for using AI to customize texts to students' reading levels."
