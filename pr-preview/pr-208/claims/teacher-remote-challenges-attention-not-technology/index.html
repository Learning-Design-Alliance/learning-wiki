New York Charter Schools: Remote Instruction During COVID Crisis (Spring 2020) – Results for All Authorizers 2020¶

New York Charter Schools: Remote Instruction During COVID Crisis (Spring 2020) – Results for All Authorizers. (2020). Survey slide report of New York State charter schools, May 2020. https://credo.stanford.edu/reports/item/report-3/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive survey of teachers' challenges with online instruction during building closure. The report prints that "66% of schools said that less than a quarter of their teachers reported challenges with technology," and lists the three most frequently reported issues as keeping students' attention, monitoring student engagement, and managing their own home/personal lives.
