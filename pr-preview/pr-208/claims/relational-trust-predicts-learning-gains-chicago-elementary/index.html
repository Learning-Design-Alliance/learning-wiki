Bryk 1988¶

Bryk, A. S. and Schneider, B. (1988). Trust in Schools: A Core Resource for Improvement. https://www.amazon.com

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Overview of a three-year study of reform in 12 Chicago elementary schools drawing on longitudinal survey and achievement data plus interviews. The authors report schools with high relational trust "were also more likely to demonstrate marked gains in student learning," while weak-trust schools saw virtually no improvement.

"As a result, these schools were also more likely to demonstrate marked gains in student learning. In contrast, schools with weak trust relations saw virtually no improvement in their reading or mathematics scores."
