This is the review's science achievement domain synthesis, resting on a single quasi-experimental study (Xu et al., 2011) of 36,104 high school students. The report states: "In the one study, the estimated impact of TFA teachers on science achievement was positive and statistically significant", measured on end-of-course science exams.
