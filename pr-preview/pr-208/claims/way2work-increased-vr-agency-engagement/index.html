Evidence¶

David Mann 2021¶

David Mann, Kathleen Feeney, Todd Honeycutt, Marlena Luhr. (2021). Way2Work Maryland Demonstration: Impacts 24 Months After Enrollment. Mathematica. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/way2work-maryland-demonstration-impacts-24-months-after-enrollment

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Randomized controlled trial of Way2Work Maryland with 401 enrolled high school students with IEPs or Section 504 plans, assessed via follow-up surveys and administrative data 24 months after enrollment. The report states the program "succeeded in increasing vocational rehabilitation agency engagement with participants."

"As intended, the Way2Work Maryland program succeeded in increasing vocational rehabilitation agency engagement with participants, and almost all treatment group members used agency-provided work-based learning experiences."
