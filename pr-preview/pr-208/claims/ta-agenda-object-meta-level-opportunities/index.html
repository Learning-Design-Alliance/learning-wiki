Evidence¶

Karavi 2022¶

Karavi, Mali, & Avraamidou. (2022). Commognition as an approach to studying proof teaching in university mathematics lectures. EURASIA Journal of Mathematics, Science and Technology Education. https://doi.org/10.29333/ejmste/12173

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Pinto (2019), a study of two TAs' tutorial discourse on real analysis, using transcripts and end-of-semester interviews. The review states adaptations at object-level learning "appeared to provide different opportunities for students' meta-level learning".

"with regard to students' meta-level learning, one emphasized the narrative that "each and every word counts in mathematics" whereas the other highlighted "how more experienced mathematics students, such as himself, do mathematics"."
