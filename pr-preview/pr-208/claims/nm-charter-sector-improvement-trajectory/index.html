Charter School Performance in New Mexico 2019¶

Charter School Performance in New Mexico. (2019). Center for Research on Education Outcomes, Stanford University. https://credo.stanford.edu/reports/item/charter-school-performance-in-new-mexico-2/

q1 · i? · causal · r2

Cross-study benchmarking (Figure 4) of the 2009 New Mexico study, the 2013 national study, and this 2019 study; the report itself labels the comparison "only suggestive" due to a changed period-by-period VCR matching method and updated days-of-learning conversion.

"Figure 4 indicates a positive trajectory in the academic progress of New Mexico charter school students across three studies. In reading, charter students fell behind TPS peers slightly in the 2009 study and caught up in the 2014 report and this current study."
