Evidence¶

DeSchryver 2015¶

DeSchryver, David A., with Deirdre Dlugoleski. (2015). Accelerating Change: How education technology developers can jump-start a new adult education market. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/29f39d7d-87f2-4e1e-920e-0c1ce86035cc

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The report attributes this to a 2014 Department of Labor report on job training that cites "a series of other corroborating reports"; the report itself conducts no new analysis. Attributed secondhand, so evidence is weak.

"belonging to a cohort, or a tightly knit, reliable group of student peers with a common purpose, contributes to developing skills and persisting in courses and programs."

DeSchryver 2015 (2)¶

DeSchryver, David A., with Deirdre Dlugoleski. (2015). Accelerating Change: How education technology developers can jump-start a new adult education market. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/29f39d7d-87f2-4e1e-920e-0c1ce86035cc

q2 · i? · theoretical · r1

The report describes early research on the two-generation approach, quoting a 2013 National Center for Families Learning report that "two- generation learning can increase student achievement" and improve adult reading behaviors. Secondhand attribution; no effect sizes printed.

"According to a 2013 report by the National Center for Families Learning, “two- generation learning can increase student achievement, expand parent engagement, improve adult reading behaviors and prepare parents to help their children with school.”"
