David Wenhao Huang 2004¶

Impact of Cognitive-based Instructional Intervention on Learning Motivation: The Implementation of Student-made Glossary in a Programming-oriented Engineering Problem-solving Course and Its Impact on Learning Motivation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED485015

Pre-motivational survey frequency analysis (Table 2) in the one-group pre/post design; combined frequencies were Attention 2692, Relevance 3568, Confidence 2749, Satisfaction 711, identifying Satisfaction as the motivational problem to address.

"Table 2 indicates that students initially provided the least of amount of positive response towards the Satisfaction component of ARCS model based on the frequencies of " Mostly True" and " Very True" that were selected for each component."
