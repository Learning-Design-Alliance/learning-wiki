Rebecka Rundquist 2024¶

Rebecka Rundquist, Kristina Holmberg, John Rack, Zeynab Mohseni and Italo Masiello. (2024). Use of Learning Analytics in K–12 Mathematics Education: Systematic Scoping Review of the Impact on Teaching and Learning. Journal of Learning Analytics 11(3). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2024.8299

Thematic synthesis of five studies (Table 2) observing teacher actions in classroom practice, yielding two themes: Supervision (monitor, reprimand, encourage, manage groupwork) and Guidance (formative and conceptual focus).

"The frequency distribution of teachers’ pedagogical actions sug gests that extracted analytics are more commonly used for supervision than for guidance. When teachers emphasize the monitoring of students, they also use DLS to a higher extent."
