Wei He 2022¶

Wei He. (2022). Achievement and Growth Norms for Course-Specific MAP® Growth™ Tests. NWEA Research Report. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/achievement-and-growth-norms-for-course-specific-map-growth-tests/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive analysis of summary statistics (Table 3.1) for the norming sample of Grades 6–12 test events across seven subjects. The report states "average test scores decreased as grades increased for each course-specific test" and that lower-grade students tend to achieve more between-term growth, which the authors attribute to higher self-selection on ability or readiness in earlier grades.

"This table indicates that, with few exceptions, average test scores decreased as grades increased for each course-specific test. Lower-grade students (i.e., Grades 6–8 students) tend to perform better than the students of the grade at which a course is usually targeted"
