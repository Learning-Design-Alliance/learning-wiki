Jennie Y. Jiang 2015¶

Jennie Y. Jiang, Susan E. Sporte, Stuart Luppescu. (2015). Analytic Memo: Evaluation Data from the First Year of REACH. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://www.cps.edu/Pages/reachstudents.aspx

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive accounting of value-added coverage in 2012-13: "almost 10,000 teachers received the school-wide literacy value-added score, while only about 5,000 received an individual-valued-added score"; high school teachers received none. Individual scores were normally distributed around a mean of approximately zero.

"In 2012-13, almost 10,000 teachers received the school-wide literacy value-added score, while only about 5,000 received an individual-valued-added score. High school teachers did not receive a value-added score in 2012-13."
