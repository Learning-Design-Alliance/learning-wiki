q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Finding from the qualitative analysis of researcher structured reflections. Table 3 summarizes the corresponding staff capacities: relational groundwork and trust, scaffolding and facilitation, emergent and programmatic knowledge, and curiosity and patience.

"Digital Promise researcher capacities in participatory evaluation were characterized less by technical control of the process than by the ability to create conditions for participatory work."
