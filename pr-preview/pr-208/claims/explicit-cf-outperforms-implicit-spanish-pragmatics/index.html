Evidence¶

Yousefi 2021¶

Yousefi, M., & Nassaji, H. (2021). Corrective Feedback in Second Language Pragmatics: A Review of Research. Teaching English as a Second Language Electronic Journal (TESL-EJ), 25(1). https://tesl-ej.org/pdf/ej97/a6.pdf

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports Koike and Pearson's (2005) study teaching pragmatic information to English-speaking learners of third-semester Spanish, operationalizing explicit CF as a correct response with metalinguistic information and implicit CF as a clarification request; the explicit group won on all measures. No effect size is printed.

"Results showed that the explicit group performed significantly better than the other experimental group and the control group on all measures."
