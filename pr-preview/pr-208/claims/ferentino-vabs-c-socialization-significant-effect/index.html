Social Skills Training 2013¶

Social Skills Training. (2013). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report: Early Childhood Education Interventions for Children with Disabilities. U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/578

Randomized trial comparing school-based My Friends and Me training to wait-list control on teacher-rated Vineland Socialization; printed means 50.44 versus 32.58, effect size 1.53, improvement index +44, p = 0.00.

"Socialization domain of the Vineland Adaptive Behavior Scales, Classroom Edition (VABS-C) Preschoolers 8 classrooms/ 65 children 50.44 (14.49) 32.58 (7.73) 17.86 1.53 +44 0.00"

Social Skills Training 2013 (2)¶

Social Skills Training. (2013). What Works Clearinghouse Intervention Report: Early Childhood Education Interventions for Children with Disabilities. U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/578

q3 · i? · review · r3

In the same trial, five social-emotional measures were analyzed; only one was significant. Printed effect sizes for the non-significant measures were 0.13 (CBCLP), -0.19 (CBCLT), -0.48 (K-ABC Face Recognition), and 0.16 (VABS-I).

"The study reported a statistically significant positive effect for one measure, which was confirmed by the WWC. The results for the other four outcomes were not statistically significant."
