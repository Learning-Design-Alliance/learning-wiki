Roderick 1998¶

Roderick, M., Stone, S., with Arney, M., Chiong, J., DaCosta, K., & Waxman, E. (1998). Changing Standards, Changing Relationships: Building Family-School Relationships to Promote Achievement in High Schools. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://ccsr.uchicago.edu/publications/changing-standards-changing-relationships-building-family-school-relationships-promote

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Comparison of Consortium teacher survey responses in 1994 and 1997 (Figure 9). Improvements were most marked at the high school level, narrowing the gap between eighth- and ninth-grade teachers' reports.

"The percentages of ninth-grade teachers who strongly agree that teachers greet parents warmly, try to understand parents’ problems, and work to com-municate to parents the mission of the school more than doubled between 1994 and 1997"

Roderick 1998 (2)¶

Roderick, M., Stone, S., with Arney, M., Chiong, J., DaCosta, K., & Waxman, E. (1998). Changing Standards, Changing Relationships: Building Family-School Relationships to Promote Achievement in High Schools. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://ccsr.uchicago.edu/publications/changing-standards-changing-relationships-building-family-school-relationships-promote

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Longitudinal comparison of 39 high schools surveyed in both 1994 and 1997 (Figure 10). Communication scale scores rose in 24 schools, stayed flat in 3, and declined in 12; nearly half of declining schools were later reconstituted.

"the average score of teacher reports increased in 24 high schools between 1994 and 1997, with three showing no change and twelve declining"
