Washington, DC: U.S. Department of Education, National Center for Education Evaluation and Regional Assistance, Institute of Education Sciences. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/

The report's stated purpose, from its abstract: an examination of variability in pretest-posttest correlations for state assessment data across achievement-level samples, "to determine how sample characteristics (such as achievement level) affect pretest-posttest correlation coefficients." No effect sizes are printed on the available page.
