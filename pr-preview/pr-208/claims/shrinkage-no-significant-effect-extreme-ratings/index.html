Herrmann 2013¶

Herrmann, M., Walsh, E., Isenberg, E., & Resch, A. (2013). Shrinkage of Value-Added Estimates and Characteristics of Students with Hard-to-Predict Achievement Levels. Cambridge, MA: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/shrinkage-of-valueadded-estimates-and-characteristics-of-students-with-hardtopredict-achievement-levels

q2 · i? · causal · r?

The paper reports a null finding: shrinkage has "no statistically significant effect on the relative probability" that teachers of hard-to-predict students receive extreme value-added estimates. No effect size or test statistic is printed in the available text; equivalence was not tested.

"this paper found shrinkage has no statistically significant effect on the relative probability that teachers of hard-to-predict students receive value-added estimates that fall in the extremes of the value-added distribution"
