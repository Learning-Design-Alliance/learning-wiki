Project QUEST (Quality Employment through Skills Training) Intervention Report | Postsecondary Career and Technical Education Topic Area 2021¶

Project QUEST (Quality Employment through Skills Training) Intervention Report | Postsecondary Career and Technical Education Topic Area. (2021). What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/723

q4 · i2 · review · r3

WWC review of the Rolston et al. (2017) RCT of VIDA, an adaptation of the Project QUEST model, with 958 participants randomized. Table 4 reports total college credits earned within 24 months: 34.04 versus 27.49, effect size 0.35, improvement index +14, p < .01.

"The WWC determined that one study that meets WWC group design standards without reservations (Rolston et al., 2017) showed evidence of a positive and statistically significant effect of Project QUEST on credit accumulation."
