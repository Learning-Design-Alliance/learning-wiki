Evidence¶

Early Risers 2012¶

Early Risers. (2012). WWC Intervention Report: Children Classified as Having an Emotional Disturbance. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. http://www.med.umn.edu/psychiatry/research/earlyrisers/home.html

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

Supplemental second-year findings from the same 20-school randomized sample (199 students): the Academic Competence Composite showed a mean difference of 0.17, effect size 0.26, improvement index +10, and p-value 0.02, favoring the intervention group. These supplemental findings do not factor into the determination of the intervention rating.
