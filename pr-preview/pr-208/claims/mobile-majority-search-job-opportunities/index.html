Descriptive survey results from the broader learning and work ecosystem sample show mobile devices dominate opportunity search: "61% use a mobile smartphone, tablet, or notebook to search for opportunities." Percentages are descriptive; no significance test is printed. The article adds that about 9.7% use devices six to 10 years old.

"Respondents indicated that while approximately 38.4% use laptops or desktops to search for educational and job opportunities, 61% use a mobile smartphone, tablet, or notebook to search for opportunities."
