Finding kinship in a far away land: The relevance of Japanese cultural perspectives to education research in Eastern Kentucky. (2022). https://bit.ly/TANrefs

q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

Qualitative interview data from 2021 with project partners, analyzed using Yosso's (2005) community cultural wealth concepts. A participant (Neil, 5/27/21) described "a push against" stereotypes and helping neighbors; another (Payton) linked aspirational capital to Bit Source founders maintaining hopes after the coal industry's collapse.

"[Something] indicative of that Appalachian culture is that, while there may be certain stereotypes and stigmas associated with Appalachia, there's a push against that and that we're going to do whatever it takes in order to help our neighbors"
