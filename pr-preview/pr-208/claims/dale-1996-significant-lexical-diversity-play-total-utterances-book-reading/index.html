Evidence¶

Dialogic Reading 2010¶

Dialogic Reading. (2010). WWC Intervention Report, What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/InterventionReport/136

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Randomized controlled trial with 33 mother-child dyads of children ages three to six with mild to moderate language delays; dialogic reading versus Conversational Language Training Program. Appendix A3 prints effect sizes of 0.92 (lexical diversity—play) and 1.67 (total utterances—book reading), both statistically significant; the other four measures were not significant.

"According to WWC calcula - tions, there were statistically significant differences between the intervention and comparison groups on two of the six measures (lexical diversity during the play activity and total number of utterances during the book-reading activity)."
