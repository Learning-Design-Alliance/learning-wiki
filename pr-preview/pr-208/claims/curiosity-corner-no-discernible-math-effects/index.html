Curiosity Corner 2009¶

Curiosity Corner. (2009). WWC Intervention Report, What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://www.successforall.org/early/early_curiosity.htm

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Randomized controlled trial (PCER Consortium, 2008); math assessed with WJ III Applied Problems, the CMA-A Composite Score, and the Shape Composition task across 18 preschools. No difference was statistically significant or substantively important.

"The study shows, and the WWC confirms, none of the differences between children in the treatment and control groups are statistically significant or substantively important."
