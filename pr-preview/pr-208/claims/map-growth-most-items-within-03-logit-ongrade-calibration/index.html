Wei He 2021¶

Wei He, NWEA Psychometric Solutions. (2021). An Investigation of Item Parameter Invariance Using Focused Calibration Samples for MAP Growth. NWEA. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/an-investigation-of-item-parameter-invariance-using-focused-calibration-samples-for-map-growth/

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Study 1 categorized items by the size of the on-grade minus all-grade difficulty difference (Figure 3.2). The report states "at least 90% of reading and science items had differences within 0.3 logits for almost all grades" and about 80% of math items. Box plots (Figure 3.1) show a few math and reading items exceeded 1 logit.

"at least 90% of reading and science items had differences within 0.3 logits for almost all grades. Math tended to see fewer items, about 80% of items for most grades, with differences within 0.3 logits."
