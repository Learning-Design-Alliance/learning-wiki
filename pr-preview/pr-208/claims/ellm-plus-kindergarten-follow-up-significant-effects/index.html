Early Literacy and Learning Model (ELLM/Plus) 2008¶

Early Literacy and Learning Model (ELLM/Plus). (2008). Evidence rating summary, Preschool Curriculum Evaluation Research (PCER) studies. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/early-literacy-and-learning-model-plus-ellm-plus/

q2 · i2 · causal · r?

Kindergarten follow-up component of both PCER studies: significant positive TERA-3 Reading Quotient and Alphabet effects in study one, and significant PPVT and TOLD effects in study two, with printed effect sizes of +0.34 and +0.44 respectively.

"Both studies involved a kindergarten follow-up, which in the first study found significant positive effects on the TERA-3 Reading Quotient and the Alphabet scales and in the second study significant positive effects on the PPVT (effect size=+0.34) and TOLD (effect size=+0.44)."
