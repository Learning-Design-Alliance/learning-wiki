What Works Clearinghouse 2006¶

q3 · i? · causal · r?

In the same randomized controlled trial, the student protective factor survey total scores showed "no statistically signiﬁcant impacts" at 20-week follow-up, yet the effect size was large enough to be considered substantively important under WWC criteria.

"Although the study authors reported no statistically signiﬁcant impacts on elementary school students’ total scores using the student protective factor survey 20 weeks after the end of the program, the effect size was large enough to be considered substantively important using WWC criteria."

  • Too Good for Violence produces statistically significant positive effects on elementary students' behavior 20 weeks after the program ends — related
