Narrative synthesis (SWiM) of neuroimaging studies of meditation; the review reports, citing Brewer et al. 2011, "reduced activation in medial PFC and posterior cingulate cortex (PCC) nodes" with enhanced PCC–dlPFC connectivity. No effect sizes printed.

"Experienced mindfulness meditators showed reduced activation in medial PFC and posterior cingulate cortex (PCC) nodes during all meditation types, alongside enhanced functional connectivity between the PCC and dorsolateral PFC, reflecting a trained capacity to detect and disengage from self‐referential mind‐wandering"
