White 2026 (2)¶

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

From the same tenet-analysis section: equity and inclusion were present as values, but researchers' reflections rarely showed partners redefining or measuring progress in multidimensional ways. The report attributes this to outcomes often being defined before evaluation begins.

"By contrast, Reimagining Progress was the least explicitly evidenced tenet."
