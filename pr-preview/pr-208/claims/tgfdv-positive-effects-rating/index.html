Evidence¶

What Works Clearinghouse 2006¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2006). WWC Intervention Report: Too Good for Drugs and Violence. https://www.mendezfoundation.org

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r?

WWC aggregation across the two reviewed studies (one RCT, one quasi-experimental) yielded a domain-average effect size of 0.42 ("0.42 na +16"). The report states "The average improvement index for knowledge, attitudes, and values is +16 percentile points, with a range of +5 to +21 percentile points across findings."

"Domain average9 for knowledge, attitudes, and values across studies 0.42 na +16"

What Works Clearinghouse 2006 (2)¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2006). WWC Intervention Report: Too Good for Drugs and Violence. https://www.mendezfoundation.org

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

WWC rating rationale: criterion 1 required two or more studies showing statistically significant positive effects, at least one meeting standards with a strong design; criterion 2 required no statistically significant or substantively important negative effects, which the WWC analysis confirmed was met.

"Positive effects: Strong evidence of a positive effect with no overriding contrary evidence."
