Bruce Thompson 2006¶

Bruce Thompson. (2006). Evaluating Three Programs Using a School Effectiveness Model: Direct Instruction, Target Teach, and Class Size Reduction. Third Education Group Review, Vol. 2, No. 3. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ874476

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Before-after analysis of ratings for 14 Milwaukee SAGE schools (Table 4: Sage-1996 +0.03, p=.356; Sage-1998 -0.02, p=.376; All SAGE 0.00, p=.485). The author notes comparing schools is a substantially greater hurdle than comparing individual students, given only 14 schools.

"Table 4 shows the average annual change in the SAGE schools’ scores over time. The first group of schools shows a slight increase and the second a slight decrease. Neither change is statistically significant."
