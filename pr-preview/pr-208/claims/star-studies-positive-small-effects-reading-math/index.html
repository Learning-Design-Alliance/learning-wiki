Evidence¶

Filges 2018¶

Filges, T., Sonne‐Schmidt, C. S., Nielsen, B. C. V. (2018). Small class sizes for improving student achievement in primary and secondary schools: a systematic review. Campbell Systematic Reviews 2018:10. https://doi.org/10.4073/csr.2018.10

q3 · i1 · quant-synthesis · r3

Synthesis of four studies analysing the Tennessee STAR RCT data (class size reduction in grades K–3), with effect sizes and standard errors corrected for clustering using an intra-cluster correlation of ρ = 0.22; study-level effect sizes ranged 0.17–0.34 (reading) and 0.15–0.33 (mathematics), all statistically significant.

"All reported results from the four studies analysing STAR data indicated a positive effect favouring the treated; all of the study‐level effects were statistically significant. The study‐level effect sizes for reading varied between 0.17 and 0.34 and the study‐level effect sizes for mathematics varied between 0.15 and 0.33."
