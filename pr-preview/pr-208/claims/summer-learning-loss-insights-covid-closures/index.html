Beth Tarasawa 2020¶

Beth Tarasawa, Megan Kuhfeld. (2020). COVID-19 closures & learning loss. NWEA Research presentation. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/covid-19-closures-learning-loss/

q1 · i? · theoretical · r1

The April 2020 NWEA presentation description argues, with no data reported on the page itself, that "research on seasonal learning and summer learning loss can offer some insights" into what missing months of school may mean for student achievement.

"While it is difficult to speculate on what missing months of school may mean for student achievement, research on seasonal learning and summer learning loss can offer some insights."
