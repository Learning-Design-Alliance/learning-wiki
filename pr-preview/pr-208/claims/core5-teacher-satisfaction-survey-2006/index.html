A teacher survey from a 2006 study (Macaruso et al.) with four of five treatment teachers responding. Teachers "reported an average of 8.5 points related to motivation, and ranging from 8.4 to 9.0 for the other areas"; overall they reported being satisfied with the program.

"In one study from 2006 (Macaruso et al.), four of the five treatment teachers completed a survey related to program effectiveness. On a ten-point scale with items related to student motivation, content, instructional design, ease of use, and program management, teachers reported an average of 8.5 points related to motivation"
