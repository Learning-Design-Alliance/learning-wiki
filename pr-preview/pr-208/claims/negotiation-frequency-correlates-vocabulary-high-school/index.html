Evidence¶

Baoshu Yi & Zhinong Sun 2013¶

Baoshu Yi & Zhinong Sun. (2013). An Empirical Study of the Effectiveness of Negotiation of Meaning in L2 Vocabulary Acquisition of Chinese Learners of English. English Language Teaching, 6(10). https://doi.org/10.5539/elt.v6n10p120

q3 · i3 · causal · r1

Pearson correlation analysis (Table 4) of the high school experimental group (N=56): r = 0.761 between negotiation frequency and vocabulary score (p=0.000); comprehensible input correlated with vocabulary score at r = .313 (p=.019) and with negotiation frequency at r = .310 (p=.020).

"vocabulary score and frequency of negotiation of meaning have a strong correlation with each other (P=0. 000<0.05). It is worth mentioning that Pearson Correlation between vocabulary score and frequency of negotiation of meaning is much higher (0.761)"
