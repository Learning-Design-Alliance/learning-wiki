Istation Reading 2024¶

Istation Reading. (2024). Evidence summary for Istation Reading. https://www.istation.com

q2 · i1 · causal · r?

Second quasi-experimental study: 884 students in Grades 5–7 from 11 schools in a large urban Texas district (56% Hispanic, 87% eligible for free or reduced-price lunch, nearly 60% emergent bilingual). Students with any recorded Istation usage were matched with same-school students with no recorded usage; the article reports they "scored significantly higher on STAAR Reading" with "effect size = +0.10".
