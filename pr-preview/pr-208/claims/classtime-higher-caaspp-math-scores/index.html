Classtime 2024¶

Classtime. (2024). Classtime evidence summary. https://www.evidenceforessa.org

q2 · i? · causal · r2

A quasi-experimental study of Classtime's math assessment included 6,853 students across 28 schools (10 implementing, 18 comparison) in a large urban California district, with 88% economically disadvantaged students. The study found "effect size = +0.05" favoring Classtime schools on CAASPP mathematics.

"Students in Classtime schools scored significantly higher on the California Assessment of Student Performance and Progress (CAASPP) mathematics assessment than students in comparison schools after controlling for baseline math achievement and student demographics (effect size = +0.05)."
