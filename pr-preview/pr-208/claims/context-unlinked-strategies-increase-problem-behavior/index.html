The guide states this association in its evidence summary for recommendation 1, citing the same single-subject literature. The wording is associational and hedged with "perhaps", and no effect size is printed.

"In fact, strategies not linked to the specific context of a problem be-havior are associated with increases in the occurrence of the problem behavior, perhaps because such strategies can inad-vertently reinforce the misbehavior."
