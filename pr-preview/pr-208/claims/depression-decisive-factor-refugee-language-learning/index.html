q1 · i? · review · r1

The digest's factors section asserts, without cited data, that depression is common in old age and somaticized in refugees. It states "Refugees' depression does not permit them to concentrate well, thus reinforcing the cycle of not being able to speak English."

"Mental health is probably the single most decisive factor in refugeelanguage learning. Depression is very common in general in old age, and forrefugees it is often somaticized in such forms as loss of appetite, short attention span, nightmares, and inability to sleep."
