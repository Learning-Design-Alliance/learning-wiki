Operational item analysis of Spanish Math items administered during 2024–2025 (Tables 4.1–4.2). Mean p values increased over terms and "mean point-biserial correlations remaining constant at 0.32 across terms", with most items in the 0.2–0.4 discrimination range.

"Spanish Math items demonstrated a well-balanced difficulty distribution, with mean p values increasing from fall (0.48) to winter (0.51) and spring (0.52)"
