Sartain 2014¶

Sartain, L., Allensworth, E., & Porter, S. (2014). Suspending Chicago's Students: Differences in Discipline across Schools. UChicago Consortium. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/suspending-chicagos-students-differences-discipline-practices-across-schools

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Observational within-school comparison of suspension rates across student groups in Chicago. The report finds African American boys suspended at about 13 percentage points higher rates than other students at the same diverse schools, indicating disparities beyond school composition alone.

"At schools that have racial/ethnic diversity, suspension rates for African American boys are about 13 percentage points higher than the suspension rates for other students who attend the same school."
