Evidence¶

Brewer 2003¶

Brewer, S. A., & Klein, J. D. (2003). Small Group Learning in an Online Asynchronous Environment. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED484997

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Qualitative categorization of interaction behaviors from newsgroup transcripts of 40 triad learning teams (10 triads per treatment), inter-rater agreement .91; differences significant at p < .01 per Table 3. No effect size printed.

"Groups with no structured interdependence had the highest number of cognitive interactions, groups with role interdependence had the highest number of group process interactions, and groups with reward interdependence had highest number of off task interactions."

Brewer 2003 (2)¶

Brewer, S. A., & Klein, J. D. (2003). Small Group Learning in an Online Asynchronous Environment. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED484997

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Interaction quantity analysis of transcript data from the same 40 triads; combining role and reward interdependence produced more interactions than either reward alone or no structure. No effect size printed.

"individual participants in groups with role -plus -reward interdependence had the highest number of interactions, statistically significant compared to those in groups with only reward interdependence or no structured interdependence."
