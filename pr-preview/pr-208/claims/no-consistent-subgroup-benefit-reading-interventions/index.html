The report estimated impacts for subgroups including students with weak and strong initial word attack skills, low or high beginning vocabulary scores, and students who did or did not qualify for free or reduced-price lunches, finding no consistent subgroup advantage. No subgroup effect sizes are printed in the text provided.

"However, the interventions did not consistently benefit any one subgroup more than another."
