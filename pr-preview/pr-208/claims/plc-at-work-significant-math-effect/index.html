Professional Learning Communities at Work (PLC at Work) 2020¶

Professional Learning Communities at Work (PLC at Work). (2020). Program description and evidence summary. https://evidenceforessa.org/program/professional-learning-communities-at-work-plc-at-work/

q2 · i? · causal · r2

A quasi-experimental study compared over 2,500 students in 12 Arkansas schools using PLC at Work to similar students in well-matched schools not involved in the initiative. The page reports "a statistically significant positive effect on the state-administered ACT Aspire math achievement test scores (effect size = +0.08)".

"PLC at Work had a statistically significant positive effect on the state-administered ACT Aspire math achievement test scores (effect size = +0.08), though it did not significantly affect ELA scores."
