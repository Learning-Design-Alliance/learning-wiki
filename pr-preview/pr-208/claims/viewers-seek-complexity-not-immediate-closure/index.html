McWhinnie 1991¶

McWhinnie, Harold J. (1991). Chaos Theory in the Arts and Design. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED368622

q2 · i? · review · r2

The paper reports, citing Hunt (1963) and Gibson (1969), experimental psychological findings that humans and primates seek stimulation with an optimum amount of novelty, surprisingness, complexity, or change; the review narrates these second-hand and prints no effect sizes.

"In experimental psychological work, such as that done by Hunt (1963) and others, it has been shown that human beings as well as primates seek stimulation that offers an "optimum amount of novelty, surprisingness, complexity, or change"."
