Evidence¶

Rebecca Hinze-Pifer and Lauren Sartain 2015¶

Rebecca Hinze-Pifer and Lauren Sartain. (2015). Rethinking Universal Suspension for Severe Student Behavior. Stanford University and University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Survey-based analysis using perceived safety measures constructed from My Voice, My School questions on feeling safe in class, elsewhere in the building, and outside. The article reports a "small decline in perceived safety"; no effect size is printed.

"In schools serving predominantly Latino students, there was a small decline in perceived safety when suspension use for severe infractions declined."
