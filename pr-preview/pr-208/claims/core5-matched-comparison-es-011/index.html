Lexia® Core5® Reading — Evidence Rating: Moderate 2022¶

Lexia® Core5® Reading — Evidence Rating: Moderate. (2022). https://www.lexialearning.com/core5

q3 · i1 · causal · r2

A matched-comparison study of over 6,000 Core5 students in grades 3-5 during the 2021-2022 academic year compared a Core5-using district with similar non-using schools nationwide. Core5 students showed "significantly larger reading achievement gains on the NWEA MAP (effect size = +0.11)".

"The study compared students in a Core5-using school district with a matched comparison group from similar schools across the United States that did not use Core5. Results showed that Core5 students experienced significantly larger reading achievement gains on the NWEA MAP (effect size = +0.11)"
