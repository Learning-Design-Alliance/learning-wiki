Evidence¶

Hanley S. Chiang 2014¶

Hanley S. Chiang, Melissa A. Clark, Sheena McConnell. (2014). Supplying Disadvantaged Schools with Effective Teachers: Experimental Evidence on Secondary Math Teachers from Teach For America. Princeton, NJ: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/supplying-disadvantaged-schools-with-effective-teachers-experimental-evidence-on-secondary-math

q3 · i? · causal · r?

The experimental study compares early-career TFA teachers with more experienced non-TFA teachers in the same schools and finds TFA teachers in their "first two years of teaching are more effective than more experienced non-TFA teachers." No effect size for this comparison is printed on the page.

"Addressing concerns about the fact that TFA requires only a two-year commitment, we find that TFA teachers in their first two years of teaching are more effective than more experienced non-TFA teachers in the same schools."
