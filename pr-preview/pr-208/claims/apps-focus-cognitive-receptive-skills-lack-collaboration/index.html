Narrative review, section 2.1. The review attributes this finding to Kim and Kwon's (2012) review of language-learning mobile apps; the reviewed study's design is not described in this article.

"in their review of language-learning mobile apps, Kim and Kwon (2012) highlighted that most apps focus on cognitive processes (recognition, recall, and comprehension) and receptive language skills. They note the lack of sociocognitive activities or opportunities for collaborative learning"
