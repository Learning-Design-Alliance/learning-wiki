Evidence¶

Rebecka Rundquist 2024¶

Rebecka Rundquist, Kristina Holmberg, John Rack, Zeynab Mohseni and Italo Masiello. (2024). Use of Learning Analytics in K–12 Mathematics Education: Systematic Scoping Review of the Impact on Teaching and Learning. Journal of Learning Analytics 11(3). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2024.8299

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

The review reports, from Faber et al. (2017), positive effects of a DLS with a formative assessment tool on mathematics achievement and motivation, and that its feedback functions led to better learning performance, satisfaction, and confidence.

"Using a DLS with a formative assessment tool can have a positive effect on student mathematics achievement and motivation (Faber et al., 2017)."

Rebecka Rundquist 2024 (2)¶

Rebecka Rundquist, Kristina Holmberg, John Rack, Zeynab Mohseni and Italo Masiello. (2024). Use of Learning Analytics in K–12 Mathematics Education: Systematic Scoping Review of the Impact on Teaching and Learning. Journal of Learning Analytics 11(3). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2024.8299

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review reports Rodríguez-Martínez et al.'s (2023) comparison of personalized versus non-personalized homework generated from an LA-based formative assessment tool, with analytics designed according to a taxonomy of error types.

"Personalized homework from an LA -based formative assessment tool can lead to a higher level of mathematical understanding compared to non-personalized homework (Rodríguez-Martínez et al., 2023)."

Rebecka Rundquist 2024 (3)¶

Rebecka Rundquist, Kristina Holmberg, John Rack, Zeynab Mohseni and Italo Masiello. (2024). Use of Learning Analytics in K–12 Mathematics Education: Systematic Scoping Review of the Impact on Teaching and Learning. Journal of Learning Analytics 11(3). https://doi.org/10.18608/jla.2024.8299

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports Yang and Lu's (2021) finding that reading time predicted learning only for personalized feedback, with no apparent impact for general feedback.

"Longer reading time of personalized feedback can have a positive effect on learning, while time spent reading general feedback does not seem to have an impact on learning (Yang & Lu, 2021)."
