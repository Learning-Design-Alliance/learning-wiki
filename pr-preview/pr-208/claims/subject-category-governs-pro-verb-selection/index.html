Evidence¶

Babcock 1967¶

Babcock, Sandra Scharff. (1967). Syntactic Dissimilation. ERIC. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED024020

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Analytical argument from grammaticality judgments on English dissimilated sentences. 'Some thought was given to the matter' and 'The suggestion was made by John' passivize, but 'A walk was taken by John' and 'A drink of water was taken by John' do not, which the paper explains via an optional reflexive D under A.

"dissimilated sentences with Asubjects can normally be passivized, whilethe D-governed,HAVE-dissimilated sentences can never bepassivized."
