Journal of Adolescent Health, vol. 50, issue 2. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/ja-the-effectiveness-of-mandatoryrandom-student-drug-testing-a-cluster-randomized-trial

q3 · i? · causal · r?

A cluster randomized trial described as "the largest experimental evaluation to date of school-based mandatory-random student drug testing (MRSDT)" compared MRSDT schools with schools without MRSDT and found less reported past-30-day substance use among MRSDT students. No effect size is printed on this page.

"The study found that students who were subject to MRSDT reported less substance use in the past 30 days than comparable students in schools without MRSDT."
