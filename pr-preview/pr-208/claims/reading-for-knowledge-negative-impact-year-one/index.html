Impact analysis from the first year of a large-scale randomized controlled trial of four supplemental reading comprehension curricula. The article reports "a statistically significant negative impact of Reading for Knowledge on students' reading comprehension scores"; no effect size is printed.

"The impact analyses in the study's first year revealed a statistically significant negative impact of Reading for Knowledge on students' reading comprehension scores and no other significant impacts."
