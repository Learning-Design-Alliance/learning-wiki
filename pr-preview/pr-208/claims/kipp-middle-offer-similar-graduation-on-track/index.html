Alicia Demers 2023¶

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Long-term tracking study of the first two lottery cohorts, observable for five years of college persistence, found similar four-year graduation and on-track rates for offer and non-offer students. No effect size or test statistic is printed.

"students who received an admission offer to a KIPP middle school graduated from a four-year degree program, or were on-track to graduate, at rates similar to those of students not offered admission."
