Stephenson 2025¶

Stephenson, S. P., Leones, T., Kamdar, D., Prado, Y., & Ddamulira, J. (2025). A National Context of Generative AI for Reading to Support English Learners. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/73c883c0-4652-499d-aeb2-74f773749c60

q2 · i? · qualitative · r?

Qualitative thematic analysis of interviews and listening sessions with 16 educators found that "many districts prioritize the Science of Reading, though implementation varies." One participant noted only about one-half of K–8 schools opted into a Science of Reading program.

"The findings indicate that many districts prioritize the Science of Reading, though implementation varies because of competing initiatives, curriculum changes, and inconsistent support for English language learners (ELLs)."

Stephenson 2025 (2)¶

Stephenson, S. P., Leones, T., Kamdar, D., Prado, Y., & Ddamulira, J. (2025). A National Context of Generative AI for Reading to Support English Learners. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/73c883c0-4652-499d-aeb2-74f773749c60

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Interview participants reported that "districts had less to say" about how ELLs were attended to within Science of Reading priorities, quoting an educator who said students "weren't designing for them within the Science of Reading context."

"Science of Reading was named as a priority, but when asked how ELLs were attended to, districts had less to say. They knew the students existed but weren't designing for them within the Science of Reading context."
