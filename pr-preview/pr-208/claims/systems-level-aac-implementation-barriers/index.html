Narrative background synthesis in the protocol introduction, drawing on cited studies of AAC services in schools. The protocol reports that "a lack of clinical expertise on the part of speech and language therapists and teachers working with children who use AAC is reported", alongside need–system mismatch, funding difficulty and reduced social networks.
