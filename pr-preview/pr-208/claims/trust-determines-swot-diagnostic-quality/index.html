Keravnos 2025¶

Keravnos, N., & Orphanos, S. (2025). Utilisation of SWOT analysis for decision-making in educational contexts. European Journal of Educational Management, 8(4), 219-234. https://doi.org/10.12973/eujem.8.4.219

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The article's theoretical synthesis of leadership and trust research reports that high-trust environments foster transparent data engagement and collective sense-making that enhance diagnostic accuracy; no effect size is printed.

"In environments characterised by high trust, staff members engage transparently with data, challenge assumptions, and participate in collective sense-making—behaviours that enhance diagnostic accuracy and improve decision-making quality."
