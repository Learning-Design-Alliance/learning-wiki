Powell 2007¶

Powell, S. R., & Seethaler, P. M. (2007). Grade One: Math Computation (Math Case Study #1). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED502453

q1 · i? · design · r1

In the secondary prevention progress-monitoring section, the case study reports that students plot their own weekly CBM scores and that the teacher uses the graphs to discuss progress with them. The motivating effect is attributed to unspecified research and the teacher's informal observation, not tested here.

"After the assistant scores each probe, the students plot their scores on their own graphs. Research has demonstrated that self-awareness of progress may be particularly motivating."
