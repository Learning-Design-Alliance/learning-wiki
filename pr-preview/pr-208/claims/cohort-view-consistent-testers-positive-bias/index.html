The guide's cohort-view section explains that this view shows students who tested in both selected terms (Spring 2020 excluded because very few students tested at the pandemic's onset) and warns results "may be positively biased."

"When interpreting cohort results, it is important to consider that the outcomes for students who consistently tested may be notably different than outcomes for students with less consistent testing patterns (that is, results may be positively biased)."
