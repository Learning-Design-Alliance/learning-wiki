Karma Wangda 2020¶

Karma Wangda, Kinzang Dorji, Kinley. (2020). Teachers and Students Perception on the Impact of Kagan Cooperative Learning Structures at Higher Secondary School. ASEAN Journal of Open and Distance Learning, 12(2). https://rcl.sce.rub.edu.bt/ajod/

Content analysis of open-ended survey responses identified the "voluminous content laden syllabus at HSS level that makes it difficult to practice KCLS as it consumes time", raised by 41.7% of teachers and 22.6% of students; students tied their worry to examination-determined results.

"This concern was raised by 41.7% (15 out of 36) teacher participants and 22.6% (138 out of 611) student participants"
