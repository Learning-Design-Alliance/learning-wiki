wwc-summary¶

What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education. (2023, August). World of Words. https://whatworks.ed.gov

q3 · i2 · quant-synthesis · r?

WWC systematic review synthesizing four studies (two cluster RCTs without reservations, two with reservations) totaling 992 children in Head Start and state-funded preschool sites, using a weighted average across the language domain. The summary effect size was 0.43, with an improvement index of +16 and p < 0.01 per Table 4.

"World of Words had positive effects on language development because the average effect across all outcomes and studies was statistically significant and positive for measures in the language domain."
