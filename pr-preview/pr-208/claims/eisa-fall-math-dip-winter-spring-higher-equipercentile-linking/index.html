2025 MAP Growth Norms Technical Manual 2025¶

Section 2.6 reports concordance studies (attributed to Lewis and Kuhfeld 2024) comparing EISA and traditional MAP Growth; the manual states fall Mathematics scores "were slightlylowerforEISAuserscomparedtocomparisongroupsusingtraditionalMAPGrowthassessments" and that winter and spring scores in all grades were higher. No effect sizes are printed.
