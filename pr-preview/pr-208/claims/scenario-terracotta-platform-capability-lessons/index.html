scenario-lessons¶

Pakhira, D., & Fusco, J. (2022, October). Designing for the future of research: Putting equity-relevant research into practice with scenarios and personas. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/163

q1 · i? · design · r1

The authors' design exercise considering one scenario against the Terracotta DLP produced these three lessons, reported as the article's own interpretation rather than a tested result. The scenario combined platform indicators with a survey of "social belonging, academic belonging, and perceived institutional support."

"Researchers may need awareness of how they can add additional measures to the system to measure what matters to them. Direct human observation is likely to remain an important component of research, and researchers will need ways to connect their observational data to platform-collected data."
