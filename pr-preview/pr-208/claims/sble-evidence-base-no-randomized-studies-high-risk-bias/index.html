Fisher 2023¶

Fisher, B. W., Petrosino, A., Persson, H., Guckenburg, S., Fronius, T., Benitez, I., & Earl, K. (2023). School‐based law enforcement strategies to reduce crime, increase perceptions of safety, and improve learning outcomes in primary and secondary schools: A systematic review. Campbell Systematic Reviews, 19, e1360. https://doi.org/10.1002/cl2.1360

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

In the Results section on risk of bias, the review reports "The risk of bias in the included studies was high" and that no study used random assignment. Several studies used rigorous quasi-experimental designs (instrumental variables, comparative interrupted time series, difference-in-differences).

"The risk of bias in the included studies was high. There were no studies that used random assignment, so our assessment of risk of bias was based on the methodological features of the quasi‐experiments"
