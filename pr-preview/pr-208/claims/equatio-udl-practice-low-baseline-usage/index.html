McMahon 2024¶

McMahon, L. H., Pautz Stephenson, S., & Corrigan, S. (2024). The future of math inclusion: The promise of digital math tools for universally accessible mathematics instruction [Project Report]. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/211

q2 · i? · design · r2

Descriptive analysis of a survey of 332 Equatio users in the United States, analyzed with frequencies and percentages. The article reports that "on average approximately 22% of respondents reported using Equatio to support a given UDL-based practice" across the 11 UDL-affordance items; no effect size is printed.

"Across the 11 survey items about awareness of specific affordances for inclusive instruction and UDL, on average approximately 22% of respondents reported using Equatio to support a given UDL-based practice."
