James Soland 2019¶

James Soland, Megan Kuhfeld. (2019). Do students rapidly guess repeatedly over time? A longitudinal analysis of student test disengagement, background, and attitudes. Educational Assessment, 24(4), 327-342. https://doi.org/10.1080/10627197.2019.1645592

q1 · i? · associational · r?

The article's statement of the research gap: existing work addressed identifying disengaged item responses via rapid guessing measures, but "little is known about students who rapidly guess over the course of several tests." This is the authors' framing of prior literature, offered without cited evidence in the available text.

"Considerable research has examined the use of rapid guessing measures to identify disengaged item responses. However, little is known about students who rapidly guess over the course of several tests."
