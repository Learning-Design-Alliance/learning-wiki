Matthew P. Steinberg 2011¶

Consortium on Chicago School Research, University of Chicago Urban Education Institute. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/student-and-teacher-safety-chicago-public-schools-roles-community-context-and-school

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Statistical models of school safety using CPS survey data, census data, and police crime records find that "after accounting for the incoming academic achievement of the school's students," neighborhood crime and poverty no longer predict safety. Achievement composition is the stronger compositional correlate.

"After accounting for the incoming academic achievement of the school's students, there is effectively no relationship between crime and poverty and school safety. Crime and poverty are related to school safety largely because students living in high-poverty, high-crime neighborhoods are more likely than children from other areas to enter school with low academic achievement."
