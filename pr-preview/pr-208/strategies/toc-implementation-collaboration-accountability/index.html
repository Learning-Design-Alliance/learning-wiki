Next-step implementation plan: collaboration, accountability with metrics, and bold steps sourcing ideas from lived experience¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report's conclusions commit to three key steps for advancing the work: collaboration engaging multiple stakeholders from teachers unions to district leaders to community-based organizations; accountability through 'clear processes and metrics that will guide and assess the implementation of innovative solutions'; and bold steps grounded in the Inclusive Innovation premise that the most powerful solutions come from those with lived experiences.
