Continuously assess and re-evaluate feature implementation through user-centered research, including default-setting experiments¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report's third key takeaway is continuous assessment of feature implementation: examining feature use and engagement at the student level is a first step in demonstrating how teachers and students may incorporate product features to meaningfully tailor instruction through edtech. The experimental audio-default study showed that when audio was available to more students, more students used it, leading the authors to conclude that continuous re-evaluation of how features are implemented may be a valuable source of information for product developers and may suggest ways to reach more learners through user-centered research.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires partnership resources for rigorous investigation, which the report notes most edtech products lack internally.

Constraints¶

  • Further research is needed to determine how features such as these affect learning; personalized learning comprises many interrelated strategies whose combinations need sorting out.

Target Learners¶

  • Edtech product developers and researchers serving K-12 learners

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Improving edtech design and implementation for diverse learners

Related Strategies¶

  • Pursue continuous collaboration between digital learning platform providers and school districts, with teacher training, to interconnect platform use with instruction
  • Regularly evaluate parent councils through surveys and feedback, supported by a family engagement coordinator
  • Raise teacher awareness of new features through professional development and outreach
