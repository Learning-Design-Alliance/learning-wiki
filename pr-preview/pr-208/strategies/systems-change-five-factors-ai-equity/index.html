Implement a systems change approach addressing five factors simultaneously for AI and digital equity¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Digital Promise works with educational leaders to implement a systems change approach to both AI and Digital Equity. The brief states that "five factors must be simultaneously addressed: leadership; resources and policies; consistent access; AI literacy and professional development for teachers; and design of powerful learning experiences for students."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Simultaneous attention to leadership; resources and policies; consistent access; AI literacy and professional development for teachers; and design of powerful learning experiences for students

Constraints¶

  • Requires work with educational leaders as the implementing actors

Target Learners¶

  • students
  • teachers
  • educational leaders

Target Learning Goals¶

  • powerful learning experiences with AI that are safe, equitable, and meaningful

Related Strategies¶

  • Develop IDC as a leadership tool for district-level equity discourse, not only a curriculum design framework
  • Systemic, human-centred approach treating digital data use as a school-and-teacher system, with AI integrated into professional development
  • AI in Education Toolkit for Racial Equity
  • Make nine 'big plays' across three focus areas — sustaining access infrastructure, upskilling the educator workforce, and upskilling students, families, and caregivers — in state digital equity and BEAD plans
  • Focus equity-relevant research at the classroom and systems levels, treating teachers—not just technology—as drivers of change
