States and districts should treat identifying and developing effective principals as a priority given the central importance of school leadership¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The fact sheet directs state and district attention to school leadership as a lever for improvement. It argues that because of "the central importance of school leadership," it is important to ask "what makes for an effective principal," positioning leader development as a system-level strategy for raising student achievement. The fact sheet itself is framed as guidance on how states and districts can develop effective school leaders.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • District and state decision makers must treat principal effectiveness as a question worth investigating, per the fact sheet's framing.

Constraints¶

  • The page provides the rationale and framing but does not print specific development program components or evidence about particular programs.

Target Learners¶

  • School principals
  • School leaders in K-12 districts and states

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Instructional leadership
  • Creating collaborative environments for staff
  • Supporting student academic progress

Related Strategies¶
