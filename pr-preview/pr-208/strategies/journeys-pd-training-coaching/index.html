Brief initial training plus follow-up courses and coaching for program implementation¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The professional development model for Journeys begins with a "Getting Started" course focused on the vision of the program, use of the materials, and implementation of the key components. Initial training is brief — a 5-6 hour training at the beginning of the year followed by a half-day follow-up — and ongoing support with follow-up courses and coaching is available to extend professional learning and help teachers evaluate and improve their instructional practices.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers need the initial Getting Started course covering the program vision, materials, and key components

Target Learners¶

  • Elementary classroom teachers implementing Journeys

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Effective implementation of the Journeys reading and language arts program
