Plan engagement by defining objectives, aligned questions, engagement type, and piloted materials¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Phase 1 prescribes four steps: 'Define engagement objectives', develop questions 'aligned with the purpose of community engagement sessions, answerable, actionable, and specific', determine the engagement type (e.g., interviews, focus groups, surveys, town halls, or a mix), and 'Create accessible, culturally responsive materials; pilot materials with a small group; and adjust as needed.' It also advises recruiting the right people and identifying trusted individuals or organizations to help recruit.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Engagement questions must be answerable, actionable, and specific and aligned with session purpose

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • State and local education agency staff planning community engagement

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Design targeted, effective, and respectful engagement activities

Affordances¶

  • Three Phase Community Engagement Process

Related Strategies¶

  • Facilitate sessions with a welcoming environment, probing questions, and structured pauses
  • Codebook development, theme generation, and community validation of findings
  • Dissemination planning and accurate, accessible data visualization
  • Record sessions with consent, data-protection communication, disclosure norms, and detailed notetaking
  • Notetaking practices: verbatim statements, contextual notes, and 24-hour review
