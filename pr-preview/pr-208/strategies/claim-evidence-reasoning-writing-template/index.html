Scaffold scientific explanations with a claim, evidence, reasoning writing template¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The guide recommends the common science-classroom practice of writing claims using a claim, evidence, reasoning framework, initially scaffolded with a fill-in template: "My claim is __ . The evidence that supports my claim is __ . I believe my evidence supports my claim because _____ ." Because persuasive reading and writing require evaluating evidence, this practice builds a skill that transfers to reading and writing outside science. Students also apply it when proposing playground design solutions.
