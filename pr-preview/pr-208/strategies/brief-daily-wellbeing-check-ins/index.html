Embed brief daily wellbeing check-ins across academic courses to build student-teacher connection¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report describes small, embedded moments of wellbeing focus throughout the school day as a low-cost strategy with outsized impact. A CASEL panelist suggested a math teacher spend 45 seconds polling the class on how they felt about the lesson, noting that "the research shows if a teacher does that literally every day, in a month those kids are going to feel like they're connected to that teacher." Suffern is operationalizing this by setting an expectation that 90 seconds of each course include a teacher check-in, yielding nearly 10 minutes of daily wellbeing focus across five subjects.
