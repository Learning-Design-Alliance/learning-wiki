Use NGSS-aligned K-8 science coursework as the starting point for district CT integration¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

ICCSD leveraged its recently NGSS-aligned K-8 science courses, which entail computational models of natural phenomena, as the entry point for integrating CT across the district. The district's reflection states that science and NGSS represented a starting point for K-8 curricular integration, and it is now working to expand to math, English language arts, and social studies.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Science courses already aligned to NGSS with computational modeling of natural phenomena
  • Substantial effort for developing modeling activities and assessing them via rubrics and checklists

Constraints¶

  • Developing modeling activities was a substantial lift and assessing them took time; PLTW coursework prioritizes coding more than integrative CT

Target Learners¶

  • K-8 students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Computational thinking through computational models of natural phenomena in science

Related Strategies¶

  • Appoint a district-level CS/CT leadership position to center and prioritize computing as a curricular offering
  • Leverage school-based librarians to create and deliver K-6 CS/CT lessons when classroom integration slows
