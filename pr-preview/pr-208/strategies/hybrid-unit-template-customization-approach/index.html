Provide teachers with exemplar NGSS-aligned CBL units and templates to customize rather than requiring design from scratch¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report recommends a hybrid approach in which teachers have examples of well-designed units and unit templates but engage in customizing the templates for their own students and curriculum. The authors state this "could reduce time requirements and teacher effort while still engendering a sense of ownership", addressing how difficult teachers found designing lessons from scratch. Published exemplar units and refined deeper learning rubrics from this project are offered as starting content for such efforts.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers engage in customizing the unit templates for their own students and curriculum

Constraints¶

  • Prior experience suggests giving students encouragement and access to existing units alone would be insufficient to support high-quality implementation by most teachers
