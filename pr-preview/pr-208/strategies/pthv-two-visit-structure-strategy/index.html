Two-visit structure: relationship-building visit first, academic-focused visit later¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

PTHV prescribes a two-visit sequence: the first visit builds the relationship and occurs in the summer or early fall, followed by ongoing communication throughout the year, and a second visit focused on academics or another issue relevant to the student or family. The article states PTHV "encourages the first relationship-building visit to occur in the summer or early fall, followed by ongoing communication throughout the year and a second visit that focuses on academics or any other relevant issue to the student or family."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Visits must be voluntary and arranged in advance; teachers trained and compensated

Constraints¶

  • The first visit is relationship-building, not academic; academic content is reserved for the second visit

Target Learners¶

  • Elementary students and their families

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Building family-teacher relationships and supporting academic follow-up

Related Strategies¶

  • Pthv Program Element
  • Home Visits
  • Connect with Parents Early in the School Year
  • Conduct voluntary, pair-based teacher home visits focused on hopes and dreams across a cross-section of students
  • Schedule teacher home visits before the start of the school year to build family relationships
