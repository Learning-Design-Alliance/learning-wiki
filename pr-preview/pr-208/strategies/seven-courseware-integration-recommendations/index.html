Seven recommendations for effective courseware integration by instructors¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Based on findings from high-performing classes, the report offers seven recommendations: explore the courseware's instructional tools, redesign activities to leverage courseware data, interweave peer learning, make connections to previous learning, address anxiety through empathy and confidence-building, focus on deliberate practice with formative assessments, and leverage courseware data and communication tools. These derive from spotlight analysis of five classes with both above-average affective engagement and higher-than-expected achievement.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Instructors must have access to the courseware's instructor-facing features and data dashboards

Constraints¶

  • Recommendations are drawn from observational analysis of high-performing classes, not from experimental manipulation

Target Learners¶

  • College instructors teaching gateway statistics with digital courseware

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Effective integration of courseware with evidence-based teaching to improve engagement and achievement
