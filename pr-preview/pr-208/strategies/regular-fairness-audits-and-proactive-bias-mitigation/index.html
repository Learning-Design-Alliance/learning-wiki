Regularly audit ML models for fairness and proactively apply bias mitigation during model training¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

This strategy directs practitioners of predictive analytics in education to treat fairness as an ongoing operational concern rather than a one-time check. The article's Notes for Practice state that "Regular audits of machine learning models are crucial to identify biases and ensure equitable outcomes across diverse student populations" and that practitioners "should proactively apply bias mitigation techniques (e.g., reweighting, suppression, disparate impact remover) during model training." It also calls for transparency in preprocessing decisions such as outcome aggregation, since these influence both bias and accuracy.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Access to model performance broken down by key demographic subgroups (e.g., gender, age, disability, socioeconomic status)
  • Availability of bias mitigation techniques applicable during model training

Constraints¶

  • The article did not evaluate the long-term effects of these mitigation strategies in real-world educational settings

Target Learners¶

  • adult learners in virtual learning environments
  • higher education students in online courses

Target Learning Goals¶

  • equitable identification of at-risk students for timely intervention
