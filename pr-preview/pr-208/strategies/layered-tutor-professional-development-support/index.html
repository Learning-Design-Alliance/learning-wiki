Sustain tutor quality through layered professional development: live and asynchronous learning, observations, coaching, and weekly office hours¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The program sustains instructional quality through a multi-layered support system: teachers "participate in both asynchronous and live professional learning and are regularly observed through formal observations and instructional spot checks." A Professional Services Team, subject matter coaches, weekly drop-in office hours, and individual collaboration with senior managers provide ongoing development focused on elevating instructional practices.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Senior managers of instruction conducting formal observations and spot checks
  • A Professional Services Team with subject matter coaches
  • Weekly office hours for drop-in support
