Funder recommendations: evidence on importance plus tips for starting and sustaining initiatives¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The document is explicitly written as guidance for funders and intermediaries: "This document contains recommendations for funders and other intermediaries looking to support similar strategic learning work." It pairs "evidence on the importance of strategic learning work" with "concrete tips for starting and sustaining a successful initiative."
