Provide LLI interventionists with 3-day formal training including follow-up on-site coaching¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The summary recommends that LLI Interventionists receive formal 3-day LLI training from Heinemann Publishing. This consists of "2 days of in-person training, followed by one day of on-site coaching throughout the year", pairing initial training with distributed coaching support during implementation.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Access to Heinemann-provided training and an on-site coach during the implementation year

Constraints¶

  • Training is specific to the LLI program and its publisher

Target Learners¶

  • struggling readers in grades K-2 taught by trained interventionists

Target Learning Goals¶

  • faithful implementation of the LLI tutoring model

Related Strategies¶

  • Provide virtual and on-site professional learning on the digital platform and grade-specific curriculum training
  • Brief initial training with extensive follow-up coaching and on-site facilitation for whole-school program implementation
  • Layered implementation support for teachers adopting a new science curriculum: preservice training, follow-up training, and a science coach
  • Provide comprehensive professional development and ongoing coaching when implementing a structured literacy program
  • Implement World of Words with developer training, ongoing coaching, and structured text-set materials
  • Staff Passport to Literacy with trained paraprofessional interventionists receiving 8 hours of training over 2 days
  • Provide formal training, ongoing supervisory support, and colleague collaboration when introducing new child assessments
