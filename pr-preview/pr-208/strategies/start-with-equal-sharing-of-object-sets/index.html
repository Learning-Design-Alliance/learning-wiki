Begin fraction instruction with activities that involve dividing groups of objects equally¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

An implementable activity sequence in which students first divide sets of objects into equal shares. The presentation advises: "Begin with activities that involve dividing groups of objects equally," illustrated by a problem where four friends share 12 pretzels and each must get the exact number. Teachers then vary the number of sharers to require different partitioning.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Concrete or drawable objects students can count, draw, or distribute one by one

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • primary-grade students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • equal sharing
  • division into equal shares

Related Strategies¶

  • Introduce unit fractions through partitioning a single object among sharers
  • Use varied equal-partitioning problems to develop fraction comparison and equivalence
