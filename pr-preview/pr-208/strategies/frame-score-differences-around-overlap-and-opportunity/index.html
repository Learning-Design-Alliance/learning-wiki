Frame subgroup score discussions around subgroup overlap and access to opportunity¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The guide's second recommendation urges leaders to highlight that subgroup memberships overlap and that scores reflect lived educational opportunities shaped by systemic inequities. By "showcasing how membership in multiple subgroups overlaps and its influence on academic performance, leaders can better understand the complexities of disparities" and build consensus for targeted initiatives, while acknowledging that all assessments contain some degree of bias.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Requires the ability to analyze overlapping subgroup memberships, e.g., students who are both low-income and multilingual learners

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students in districts and schools monitoring subgroup achievement gaps

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Building shared, nuanced understanding of achievement disparities among educators, boards, and families

Related Strategies¶

  • Identify cross-subgroup themes and combine broad access-expanding policies with targeted subgroup-specific interventions
  • Examine data across subgroups and account for diversity within subgroup categories before interpreting score differences
