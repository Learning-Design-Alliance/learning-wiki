Invest in educator professional learning on experiential AI pedagogies using existing federal funding streams¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The brief recommends that federal policymakers invest in educator AI literacy training by leveraging existing funding, stating policymakers "should utilize Title II, Part A grants to fund educator training" alongside Teacher Quality Partnership and SEED grant programs. It also recommends expanding NSF investments in PK-12 age-appropriate AI literacy research and AI-focused career and skill-building opportunities for high school students, strengthening the AI workforce pipeline.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Professional development should offer differentiated pathways based on teachers' existing experience levels and subject-area contexts

Constraints¶

  • The brief notes research on AI literacy implementation and effectiveness remains in early stages, with limited data on the status and efficacy of AI curricular integrations

Target Learners¶

  • PK-12 educators and high school students

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Educator capacity to teach about and with AI; learner AI literacy through hands-on, innovative learning experiences

Related Strategies¶

  • Implement sustained educator development programs including needs assessment, coaching, communities of practice, and experiential learning
  • Invest in sustained, role-specific, discipline-specific professional learning and structured student AI literacy pathways across PK16
  • Invest in dedicated instructional technology leadership roles and role-specific professional learning for AI literacy
