Use a multi-stage screening tool (SSBD) to nominate and rank at-risk students for behavioral intervention¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The program and its studies identify participants through a standardized multi-stage screening procedure. Teachers "use a screening tool to nominate students and rate their behavior using a standardized scale and definition of antisocial behavior." In Walker et al. (2009), the SSBD was used to identify the child with the highest average ranking across Stage 2 adaptive and maladaptive behavior ratings in each classroom. The SSBD itself is "a nationally normed, multistage assessment approach" combining teacher rankings, rating scales, and observation.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Teachers complete standardized rating scales (Stage 2 of the SSBD) and observations to rank students by severity

Constraints¶

  • Designed for identifying children exhibiting the most severe behavioral concerns within each classroom at school entry or early grades

Target Learners¶

  • Kindergarten through third-grade students at risk for antisocial behavior

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Accurate identification of students needing early behavioral intervention
