Six formative practices for leveraging public-private partnerships to support family engagement¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report distills formative findings about the viability of leveraging public-private partnerships for family engagement into six practices: "allowing sufficient time when moving from planning to implementation; investing in the maintenance of strong partnerships; attending to the difficulties of engaging traditionally hard-to-reach families; emphasizing supports professionals need, especially teachers and schools, to foster family engagement; supporting quality implementation of evidence-based models; and sustaining a long-term vision for improvement in children's educational outcomes."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Public-private partnerships in support of family engagement, as the context in which the report presents these practices.

Constraints¶

  • The report notes the difficulties of engaging traditionally hard-to-reach families as an ongoing challenge to attend to.

Target Learners¶

  • Low-income immigrant children from birth through age 8 and their families

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Family engagement practice and children's educational success

Related Strategies¶

  • Design future cross-sector collaboration efforts with clear goals and sufficient time period and scope, and include teacher-level activities
