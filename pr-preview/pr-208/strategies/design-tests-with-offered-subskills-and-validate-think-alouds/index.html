Design RC and LC tests with the offered subskills and validate them with think-aloud protocols and interviews¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

As a follow-up direction, the article recommends designing reading and listening comprehension tests using the offered subskills, then observing students while they answer questions on the measured subskills. Test scores combined with students' think-aloud protocols and interviews are proposed as a way to further explain the dimensionality of the subskills and learners' performance on subskills common to both modalities.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Tests must be built on the taxonomy's offered subskills, and validation requires observing students and collecting think-aloud protocols and interviews alongside scores.

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • EFL/ESL test takers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Measuring and validating reading and listening subskill performance

Related Strategies¶

  • Use the unified subskills taxonomy to diagnose learners' strengths and weaknesses and to build self-assessment tools
