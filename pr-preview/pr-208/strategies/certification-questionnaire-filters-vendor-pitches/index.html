Require a brief certification questionnaire to filter vendor pitches¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Because vendors bombard decision makers with pitches that consume enormous time and lead leaders to disregard many of them, the report recommends requiring vendors to respond to a brief questionnaire asking which Product Certifications they have earned, including the Open Badge. This cuts down hours spent listening and responding to pitches and helps "elevate research-based products that are learner-centered to the top for meaningful conversation."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Vendors must respond to the questionnaire before receiving pitch time
  • Submissions should include the Open Badge confirming the certification has not expired

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • district and school edtech decision makers

Target Learning Goals¶

  • efficiently screening vendor products for quality before committing evaluation time

Related Strategies¶

  • Embed certification-status questions with evidence requirements in RFPs and RFAs
  • Offer templates and exemplars to streamline edtech review requests for vendors and staff
