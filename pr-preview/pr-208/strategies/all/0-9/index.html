  • 3-Source Rule - A corroboration strategy requiring students to confirm any claim found online against at least three independent sources before accepting or citing it.
  • 5-Minute Writing Conferences - Brief, structured one-on-one conferences in which students lead with a self-assessment and the teacher delivers targeted, rubric-anchored feedback on one strength and one growth area.
