Measure usability, usefulness, and utilization during development and early implementation¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The guide recommends timing 3Us measurement early: it states that understanding the three indicators "is critical for implementing organizations and their funders, especially during development and early implementation." Measuring during these phases lets teams refine a solution while changes are still inexpensive, before full scale-up locks in design decisions.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Access to implementing organizations and their funders during the development and early implementation phases
  • Measurement tools for the three indicators

Constraints¶

  • The recommendation is tied to the development and early implementation window; the guide does not describe later-phase measurement in the available text

Target Learners¶

  • grantees developing and scaling educational solutions

  • timing implementation measurement to inform solution refinement
