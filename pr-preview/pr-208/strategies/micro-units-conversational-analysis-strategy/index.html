Break conversation into micro-units to study the structure of interaction¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Goffman suggested studying conversational phenomena in "micro-units," which the essay lists as naturally bounded units, basic interaction units, concrete units of interaction, natural units of social organization, and members of a single natural class. He believed conversations need to be broken into small units before they are studied, and that similarities in the structure of these fragments matter more than the content of each individual message. He developed these concepts through repeated application to real-world conversations, not deductive logic.
