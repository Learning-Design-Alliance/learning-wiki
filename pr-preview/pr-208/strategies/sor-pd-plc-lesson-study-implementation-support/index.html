Support implementation with initial Science of Reading training plus PLC-based lesson study¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Implementation is supported by an initial Science of Reading and Implementation Professional Development Session followed by targeted support sessions in grade-level Professional Learning Communities, where teachers conduct lesson plan studies and observe model lessons. In the research, three professional development sessions were provided during the year, and "trainings emphasized using student data to inform instruction, small-group instruction for targeted phonics practice, and integrating the Take-Everywhere Literacy Packs™ into classroom routines."
