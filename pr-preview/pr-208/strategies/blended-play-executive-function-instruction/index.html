Blend guided and open-ended play to build executive function in pre-kindergarten¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The program's instructional approach blends guided and open-ended play with hands-on, interactive learning, targeting core cognitive skills linked to long-term academic and social outcomes. Instruction is designed to be ecologically authentic, allowing children to apply learned skills in meaningful, real-world contexts rather than in isolated drills.

  • Professional development/training for administrators, teachers, and instructional coaches, delivered in-person, virtually, or asynchronously

Target Learning Goals¶
