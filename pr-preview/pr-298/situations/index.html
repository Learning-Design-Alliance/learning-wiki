Design for a situation¶

What the wiki's principle and pattern pages say to change for particular learners, goals and settings. Each converted page ends its default design with a table, If the brief says… / Then change… / Basis; these pages collect those rows by the situation they name, so a learner and context analysis can start here rather than from the name of a principle.

3,705 rows from 224 pages. A row's basis is a claim, with its evidence marker, or the word untested. The tags are the controlled values of the wiki's evidence dimensions; a row that names its situation in words rather than a tag is listed under the tag its wording names ("one session only" under single-session), marked (from the wording).

Agents can ask the same question of the MCP server's situations tool, combining tags and words (for example novice learners, a self-paced course and a procedure).
