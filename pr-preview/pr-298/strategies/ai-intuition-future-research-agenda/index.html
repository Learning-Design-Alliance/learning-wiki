Future research agenda: longitudinal studies, evidence-based strategies, teacher professional development, and domain-specific frameworks for AI intuition¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article closes with a four-part research agenda for the experiential dimension of AI education: longitudinal impact studies of AI intuition on critical thinking; expansion of practical, classroom-based literature with "evidence-based instructional strategies for fostering intuitive 'gut feelings' alongside technical literacy"; specialized teacher professional development for inductive, exploratory learning environments; and domain-specific refinement of the Level 2 "Embodied-Technical Layer" across arts, humanities, and advanced sciences.
