Prioritize relevance, concision, and learner-interest alignment in scalable AI instructional video, selectively reintroducing human presence¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article's design recommendation for scalable instructional systems is that "designers may achieve greater impact by prioritizing relevance, concision, and alignment with learner interests, while selectively reintroducing human presence where social connection is pedagogically essential", rather than attempting to maximize human likeness in AI presenters. This follows from the finding that students accepted weaker social presence in exchange for higher personal relevance.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Instructor-supervised content generation and auditing of personalization pipelines; transparency to students about AI use

Constraints¶

  • The article notes an uncanniness threshold may exist beyond which personalization no longer compensates for discomfort, and that the tradeoff may shift as avatars become indistinguishable from humans

Target Learners¶

  • undergraduate online learners, including a large population of English Language Learners

Target Learning Goals¶

  • perceived relevance and engagement with course content tailored to learner interests

Related Strategies¶

  • Generate multiple concise LLM perspectives for learners to select from, and explore relevance-driven diversity aligned with learner interests
