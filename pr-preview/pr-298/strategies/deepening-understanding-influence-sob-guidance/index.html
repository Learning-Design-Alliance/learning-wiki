Use the Deepening understanding Influence to nurture online learners' sense of belonging¶

Strategy · All strategies Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

'Deepening understanding' is the third Influence in the article's adapted Community of Inquiry Framework, an overlap of the three Presences through which tutors can promote SoB. The article offers "guidance illustrated by examples" for each Influence, leading to particular suggestions concentrating on the promotion of a sense of belonging. Tutors apply this guidance when facilitating deepening understanding among online learners.
