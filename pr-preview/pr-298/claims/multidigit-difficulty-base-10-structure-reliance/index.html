S.H.G. van der Ven 2015¶

S.H.G. van der Ven, M. Straatemeier, B.R.J. Jansen, S. Klinkenberg, H.L.J. van der Maas. (2015). Learning multiplication: An integrated analysis of the multiplication ability of primary school children and the difficulty of single digit and multidigit multiplication problems. Learning and Individual Differences. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lindif.2015.08.013

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The article's exploratory analyses of multidigit problem difficulty suggest that "children rely on the base 10 structure of our number system." The authors present this as exploratory and suggestive rather than a confirmed test; no effect size is printed.

"Finally, exploratory analyses on the difficulty of multidigit problems suggest that children rely on the base 10 structure of our number system."
