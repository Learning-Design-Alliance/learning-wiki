Favero 2025¶

Applications of Generative AI to support teaching and learning in Higher Education, co-located with AIED 2025, Palermo, Italy. https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.06878 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2507.06878

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The paper attributes to Bećirović et al. the finding that students with better understanding of AI systems "show higher levels of academic self-efficacy". No effect size is printed; the paper draws on it to argue demystifying AI supports trust in students' own abilities.

"Students who have a better understanding of how AI systems work, including what they can and cannot do, show higher levels of academic self-efficacy [33]."
