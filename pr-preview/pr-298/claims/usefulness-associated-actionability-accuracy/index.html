Mendonça PC 2026¶

Mendonça PC, Quintal F, Figueiredo M and Mendonça F (2026) Perceived usefulness and intention to use large language model-generated feedback across three educational levels: a user-centred study in programming. Front. Educ. 11:1934069. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1934069

q3 · i? · associational · r1

Standardised OLS regression on student-level ratings (n = 144) with Johnson's relative weights; the model accounted for 76% of variance [R2 = 0.76, F(4, 139) = 108.58, p < 0.001]. By relative weight, actionability accounted for 32.6% and accuracy for 29.0% of explained variance; "The standardised coefficients of accuracy [β = 0.35, 95% CI (0.16, 0.50)] and actionability [β = 0.43, 95% CI (0.18, 0.80)] had bootstrap CIs that excluded zero".
