Moreno 2024¶

Moreno, M., Patino Melo, L., Grewal, K., Matin, N., Azher, S., & Harley, J.M. (2024). Analyzing Multimodal Data to Understand Medical Trainees' Regulation Strategies and Physiological Responses in High-Fidelity Medical Simulation Scenarios. Metacognition and Learning, 19, 1161–1213. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-024-09403-z

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Repeated measures ANOVA over ten coded simulations (N=29) comparing SRL/SSRL code percentages across the three segments; the omnibus test for Execution was F(2, 22) = 4.54, p = .01, and the pairwise contrasts quoted above were significant. No effect size is printed.

"With ‘Execution’ codes, there were differences between ‘Beginning’ and ‘Escalation’ (p < .0001, 95% CI [-3.66, -0.98]) and ‘Beginning’ and ‘Peak’ (p= 0.03, 95% CI [-5.43, -0.39])."
