Qualitative case analysis (§4.2) of refined versions of one signed multiplication problem personalized to the Led Zeppelin topic, comparing outputs of the three strategies in Table 5. The authors' summary of the trade-offs is interpretive; no quantitative effect size is printed.

"Centralized emphasizes mathematical preservation at the expense of contextual richness, Centralized with Planning emphasizes realism but may compromise pedagogical intent, while Decentralized emphasizes narrative coherence but increases sentence complexity."
