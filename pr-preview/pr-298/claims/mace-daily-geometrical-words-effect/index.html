Eveline M. Schoevers & Paul P. M. Leseman & Evelyn H. Kroesbergen 2019¶

Enriching Mathematics Education with Visual Arts: Effects on Elementary School Students' Ability in Geometry and Visual Arts. International Journal of Science and Mathematics Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10763-019-10018-z

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Multilevel analysis of geometrical vocabulary ratios in the GAT open questions. MACE lesson series participation explained class differences in tier 1 word growth; for tier 2 academic words neither condition explained differences (t(94) = 0.36, p = 0.72), and their proportion decreased at posttest in all conditions.

"With regard to the daily geometrical words (tier1), the difference could be explained by the participation in the MACE program (t(94) = 2.60,p < 0.05), but not by participation in the MACE PD program (t(94) =− 1.42,p = 0.16)."
