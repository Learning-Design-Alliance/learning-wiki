Kizilcec 2017¶

Kizilcec, R. F., Pérez-Sanagustín, M., & Maldonado, J. J. (2017). Self-regulated learning strategies predict learner behavior and goal attainment in Massive Open Online Courses. Computers & Education, 104, 18–33. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2016.10.001

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Session-level correlation analysis of log data from N = 2,949 learners with more than one recorded session, evaluating 6 strategies × 22 behavioral indicators (132 correlations; 18 significant and relatively large). "learners who reported using more goal setting, strategic planning, self-evaluation, and task strategies spent more time in absolute and relative terms on revisiting assessments".
