Wang Guanhua 2026¶

Wang Guanhua, Wang Wenna, Yang Daozhou, Ren Jifan. (2026). Generative AI, Cognitive Offloading, and Learner Agency in Higher Education: A Scoping Review. Behavioral Sciences. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16071150

q2 · i? · review · r3

Descriptive mapping of the mechanism-related construct theme family (116 of 123 included studies). The review states these constructs recur across the charted corpus and help organize why GenAI serves as scaffold for some learners and source of dependence for others.

"Among these, self-regulation, metacognition, critical thinking, self-efficacy, trust, feedback literacy, AI literacy, and cognitive offloading/reliance/dependence recur across the charted corpus."
