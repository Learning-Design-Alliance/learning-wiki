Evidence¶

Lixiang Yan 2026¶

Lixiang Yan, Yueqiao Jin, Xibin Han, Dragan Gasevic. (2026). Socially fluent AI decouples conversational signals from source identity in online interaction. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23426 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.23426

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Logistic models of AI-attribution (N = 1,282) showed judgments only weakly tracked dialogue cues: longer latency and greater conversationality decreased AI-attribution; "explanatory power was small (McFadden's pseudo-R² = 0.018)". Judgment-level models performed near chance (AUC = 0.527) versus truth models (pseudo-R² = 0.692).

"In the logistic model of AI-attribution (𝑁 = 1,282), longer mean response latencies were associated with a lower likelihood of judging a teammate as AI (𝛽 = −0.210, SE = 0.094, 𝑧 = −2.25, 𝑝 = .025, 95% CI[−0.394,−0.027])"
