Evidence¶

Neiburg 1997¶

Neiburg, Michael S. (1997). Following Native Language Acquisition. Paper presented at the Annual Meeting of the Japan Association for Language Teaching (23rd, Hamamatsu, Japan, October 11, 1997). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED417613

q1 · i? · theoretical · r1

The article reports, citing Sticht's paper "Listening and Reading," that written language development draws on oral language competence and that "there is a positive correlation between preschool and oral language skills and subsequent achievement in reading." No coefficient is printed, so no magnitude is reported.

"He shows that there is a positive correlation between preschool and oral language skills and subsequent achievement in reading."
