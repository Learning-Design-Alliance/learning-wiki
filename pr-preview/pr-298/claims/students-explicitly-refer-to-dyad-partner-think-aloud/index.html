Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09660-0

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Quantitative content analysis of think-aloud transcripts coded for references to the dyad partner (semantic domain 5). Mean references were 16.7 during provision and 10.9 during processing; only Robert made no references in either phase. The article argues such references form "evidence of feedback providers' awareness of the feedback recipient".

"eleven of the twelve students (all, except Robert) explicitly referred in their think-aloud utterances to the feedback provider and feedback recipient"
