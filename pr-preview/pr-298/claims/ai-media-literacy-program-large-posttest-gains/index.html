Demir 2025¶

Demir, R., & Akar, C. (2025). Transforming Clicks into Critical Thinking: An AI-Based Media Literacy Program for Children. https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-6786882/v1

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Between-group post-test comparison (Table 5) of the quasi-experimental pre-test–post-test control group study with 18 experimental and 18 control fourth-graders; independent samples t-tests were significant (e.g., Total p < .001) and Cohen's d values were "1.12 (Reading), 1.18 (Writing), and 1.31 (Total Literacy)".

"Cohen’s d values across subscales—1.12 (Reading), 1.18 (Writing), and 1.31 (Total Literacy)—indicate large effect sizes, thereby reinforcing the transformative capacity of AI-driven pedagogy (Cohen, 1988)."
