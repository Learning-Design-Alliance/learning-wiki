Evidence¶

van der Sande 2023¶

van der Sande, L., van Steensel, R., Fikrat-Wevers, S., & Arends, L. (2023). Effectiveness of Interventions that Foster Reading Motivation: a Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09719-3

q4 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Categorical moderator analysis on immediate post-tests comparing motivation-only programs (d = 0.41 motivation; d = 0.28 comprehension) with programs adding skills instruction (d = 0.39; d = 0.29); Q(1) = 0.01 for both outcomes.

"We found no effect of the combination of motivation interventions with skills instruction: programs that focused solely on motivation were equally effective in stimulating affirming motivations and reading comprehension as programs that combined this with, for instance, reading strategy instruction."
