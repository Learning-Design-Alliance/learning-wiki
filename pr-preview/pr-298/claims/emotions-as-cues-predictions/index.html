Evidence¶

Prinz-Weiß 2023¶

Prinz-Weiß, A., Lukosiute, L., Meyer, M., & Riedel, J. (2023). The role of achievement emotions for text comprehension and metacomprehension. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09331-w

q3 · i2 · associational · r2

Correlational analyses in the same field study (N = 358) showed all achievement emotions except boredom (marginally significant) significantly related to predictions, with correlations ranging from small to medium-to-large (e.g., negative emotions r = -.34).

"The positive emotions (i.e., enjoyment, hope, pride, and positive emotions overall) were positively correlated with predictions, and the negative emotions (i.e., anger, anxiety, shame, hopelessness, and negative emotions overall) were negatively correlated with predictions."
