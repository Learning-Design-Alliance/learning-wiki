Narrative review of ADHD comorbidity with learning disorders, citing Sciberras et al. (2014) and Willcutt et al. (2012). The review reports that "About 25– 40% of all patients with ADHD have major reading and writ- ing difﬁculties". Percentages only; no effect size printed.

"About 25– 40% of all patients with ADHD have major reading and writ- ing difﬁculties, and many show co-existing language disor- ders ( Sciberras et al., 2014; Willcutt et al., 2012 )."

The review reports, citing Hart et al. (2010) and Rapport et al. (1999), "a considerable overlap between ADHD and disor- ders of arithmetical skills". No prevalence figures or effect sizes are printed for this overlap.

"Similarly, there is a considerable overlap between ADHD and disor- ders of arithmetical skills ( Hart et al., 2010; Rapport et al., 1999 )."
