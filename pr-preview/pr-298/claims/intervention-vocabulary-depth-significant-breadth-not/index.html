Evidence¶

Proctor 2011¶

Proctor, C.P., Dalton, B., Uccelli, P., Biancarosa, G., Mo, E., Snow, C., & Neugebauer, S. (2011). Improving comprehension online: effects of deep vocabulary instruction with bilingual and monolingual fifth graders. Read Writ 24, 517–544. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11145-009-9218-2

q2 · i? · causal · r?

Researcher-developed outcome measures administered in the same 16-week quasi-experiment with 240 students. The article reports "significant effects on researcher-developed measures of vocabulary depth"; no effect size is printed.

"Similarly, significant effects on researcher-developed measures of vocabulary depth were detected, but not for a researcher-developed measure of breadth."

Proctor 2011 (2)¶

Proctor, C.P., Dalton, B., Uccelli, P., Biancarosa, G., Mo, E., Snow, C., & Neugebauer, S. (2011). Improving comprehension online: effects of deep vocabulary instruction with bilingual and monolingual fifth graders. Read Writ 24, 517–544. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11145-009-9218-2

q2 · i? · causal · r?

In the same quasi-experiment, the researcher-developed breadth measure showed no significant intervention effect; the article prints no effect size, so the null is reported without an equivalence claim.

"Similarly, significant effects on researcher-developed measures of vocabulary depth were detected, but not for a researcher-developed measure of breadth."
