Ioanna Taouki 2022¶

Ioanna Taouki, Marie Lallier, David Soto. (2022). The role of metacognition in monitoring performance and regulating learning in early readers. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-022-09292-0

Verification-check Spearman correlations in the Grade 1 sample (VA span N=55, lexical decision N=55, emotion recognition N=59; age and non-verbal IQ covariates) confirmed expected convergent validity: e.g., word speed correlated with VA span d' (r=0.947, p<0.001, BF10>100) and lexical decision d' (r=0.416, p=0.005), while emotion recognition d' did not (e.g., r=-0.018, p=0.893).

"a strong association was shown between participants’ type-1 task sensitivity in the reading-related experimental tasks and their performance in the standardised reading tests (all ps < 0.05)."
