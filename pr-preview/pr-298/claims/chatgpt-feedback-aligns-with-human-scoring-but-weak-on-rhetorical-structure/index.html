Evidence¶

Reza Farzi 2026¶

Reza Farzi. (2026). Human–AI Collaboration in Academic Writing Instruction: Calibrated Feedback Model for EAP. OLBI Journal. https://doi.org/10.18192/olbij.v15i1.7773

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Mixed-methods comparison in which 30 first-year EAP students each submitted two argumentative essays evaluated independently by the instructor and ChatGPT-3.5 across three calibration stages. The study reports "close alignment with human scoring" and weaker handling of rhetorical structure and contextual nuance; no effect size is printed.

"The findings suggest that while ChatGPT-3.5 consistently identified surface-level issues and demonstrated close alignment with human scoring, it was less effective in addressing rhetorical structure and contextual nuance."
