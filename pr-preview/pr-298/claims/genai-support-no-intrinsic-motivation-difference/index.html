q3 · i1 · causal · r2

ANOVA with Tukey's HSD on post-task Intrinsic Motivation Inventory responses from the randomised four-group lab experiment (114 respondents across CN, AI, HE, CL groups). All four IMI dimensions were non-significant, with small printed effects such as "η2=0.029" for Interest/Enjoyment.

"No signiﬁcant diﬀerence between the four groups was observed with re- gards to Interest/Enjoyment (F=1.087, p=0.358, η2=0.029), Perceived Competence (F=0.453, p=0.716,η2=0.012), Ef- fort/Importance (F=1.152, p=0.332, η2=0.030) and Pressure/T ension (F=0.546, p=0.652, η2=0.015)."
