Ren S (2026) College students’ metacognitive awareness of generative-AI reliance: an experimental study of decision confidence and attribution bias. Front. Psychol. 17:1926110. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1926110

Figure 2 analysis of AI-consistent final responses by advice correctness and task category: "reﬂection markedly reduced alignment with incorrect advice" while both groups showed similar high alignment with correct advice. Descriptive pattern; no test statistic is printed for this contrast.
