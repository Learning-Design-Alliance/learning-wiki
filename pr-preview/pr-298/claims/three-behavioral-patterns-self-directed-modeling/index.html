Evidence¶

Sungeun An 2025¶

Sungeun An, Jennifer Hammock, Ashok Goel. (2025). How Online Learners Engage in Self-Directed Modeling: A Behavioral Analysis. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-025-00506-5

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Behavioral clustering of activity sequences from 822 models created by 315 learners in VERA, grouped by sequence length and clustered via Levenshtein distance and agglomerative hierarchical clustering. The article reports "Type 1 (N = 382): Observation", Type 2 Construction (N=338, mean 16 actions), and Type 3 Exploration (N=69, mean 154.73 actions).

"Type 1 (N = 382): Observation. This pattern is characterized by frequent sequences of parameterization followed by simulation"
