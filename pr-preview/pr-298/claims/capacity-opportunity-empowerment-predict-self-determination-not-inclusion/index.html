Shogren 2007¶

Shogren, K.A., Wehmeyer, M.L, et al. (2007) Examining individual and ecological predictors of the self-determination of students with disabilities. Exceptional Children, 73(4), 488-509. https://hdl.handle.net/1808/10983

q2 · i? · associational · r?

SEM analysis of baseline data from the multistate longitudinal intervention-evaluation project tested individual and ecological predictors of self-reported self-determination. The article reports that "Capacity, opportunity, and transition empowerment predicted students' self-reported level of self-determination" while general education inclusion did not. No coefficients are printed in the available text.
