Abrami 2012¶

Abrami, P.C., Bernard, R.M., Bures, E.M., Borokhovski, E., Tamim, R.M. (2012). Interaction in Distance Education and Online Learning: Using Evidence and Theory to Improve Practice. In: Moller, L., Huett, J. (eds) The Next Generation of Distance Education. Springer, Boston, MA. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4614-1785-9_4

The chapter reports, citing Bernard et al. (2009), a meta-analysis of distance and online learning that "quantitatively verified the importance of three types of interaction". No effect sizes are printed in the available text.

"In a recent meta-analysis of distance and online learning, Bernard et al. (Distance Education 25(2):175–198, 2009) quantitatively verified the importance of three types of interaction: among students, between the instructor and students, and between students and course content."
