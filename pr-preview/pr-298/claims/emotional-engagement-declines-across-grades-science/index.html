Xin Xia, Robert Tai. (2026). Exploring the association of student perceptions of science instruction and emotional engagement: The variance between grade level. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09800-8

Descriptive analysis of survey data from 6,465 students in grades 3–12 across 25 schools, plotted by grade in Fig. 1. The article reports that "the mean scores range from 2.89 in grade 10 to 3.93 in grade three"; scores were above the grand mean (M = 3.34) before sixth grade and below it from sixth grade on.
