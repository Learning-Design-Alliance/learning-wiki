The review reports, citing Allam et al. (2024), a multi-national cross-sectional survey of 4,492 medical students in which "92.4% of students had not received formal AI training, and 87.1% exhibited a low level of AI knowledge" while 84.9% believed AI would revolutionize medicine and radiology.
