Evidence¶

Kulkarni 2015¶

Kulkarni, C., Koh Pang Wei, Le, H., Chia, D., Papadopoulos, K., Cheng, J., Koller, D., Klemmer, S. R. (2015). Peer and Self Assessment in Massive Online Classes. In: Design Thinking Research, Understanding Innovation. Springer, Cham. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-06823-7_9

q2 · i? · design · r3

Appendix analysis comparing unaggregated peer grades with staff grades in the online class. The paper reports "26.3 % of grades were within 5 % of staff grades, and 46.7 % within 10 %", versus median agreement of 42.0% and 65.5%, demonstrating the value of aggregating peer grades.

"26.3 % of grades were within 5 % of staff grades, and 46.7 % within 10 %. (Recall that the median agreement was 42.0 % and 65.5 %, respectively)."
