Evidence¶

Mesenhöller J and Böhme K 2026¶

Mesenhöller J and Böhme K (2026). Empowering teachers to use AI for differentiation: changes in teachers’ acceptance of AI-based technologies following participation in a short professional development training session. Front. Educ. 11:1875856. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1875856

q2 · i1 · causal · r1

Pilot pre-post evaluation of the INSIGHT training with paired-samples t-tests on the SAELKIS subscales (N = 100 teachers). The article reports "a significant increase in PU from pre- to post-test" with the printed statistics above; pre-test PU was M = 2.89 (SD = .49), post-test M = 3.02 (SD = .52).

"A paired-samples t-test revealed a significant increase in PU from pre- to post-test, t(99) = −3.24, p = .002, mean difference = −0.14, 95% CI [−0.22, −0.05], d = −0.32, 95% CI [−0.52, −0.12]."
