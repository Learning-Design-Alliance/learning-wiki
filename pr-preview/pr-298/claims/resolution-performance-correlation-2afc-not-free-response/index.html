Evidence¶

Vuorre 2021¶

Vuorre, M., & Metcalfe, J. (2021). Measures of relative metacognitive accuracy are confounded with task performance in tasks that permit guessing. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09257-1

q3 · i? · causal · r2

In the same 100-participant within-participant online experiment, Kendall's tau correlations between proportion correct and each resolution metric were computed separately by condition. 2AFC: tau = 0.28 (p < 0.001) for all three metrics; free response: da tau = 0.12 (p = 0.090), ROC gamma 0.12 (p = 0.090), pairs gamma 0.15 (p = 0.027).

"The correlation was significant for each of the three resolution metrics in the 2AFC condition. However, it was much smaller in the free response condition, where it was only significant for the concordant-discordant pairs based gamma (Table4)."

Vuorre 2021 (2)¶

Vuorre, M., & Metcalfe, J. (2021). Measures of relative metacognitive accuracy are confounded with task performance in tasks that permit guessing. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-020-09257-1

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Mixed effects regression models predicted each resolution metric from performance, condition (free response = 0, 2AFC = 1), and their interaction. The significant positive interaction terms indicate a stronger performance-resolution correlation in the 2AFC condition, supporting the guessing-confound hypothesis.

"This prediction was supported in all three models ( da: b = 1.28 (SE = 0.62), p = 0.042; gamma (pairs): b = 0.67 (SE = 0.24), p = 0.006; gamma (ROC): b = 0.61 (SE = 0.25), p = 0.018 )."
