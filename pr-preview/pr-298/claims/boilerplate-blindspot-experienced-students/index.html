Evidence¶

Liu 2026¶

Liu, D., Fan, G., & Pan, L. (2026). Tool, tutor, or crutch?: A grounded theory of cognitive scaffolding and offloading in AI-assisted programming education. International Journal of STEM Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-025-00592-w

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Interview, interaction-log, and Git-history analysis of experienced AI-section students, illustrated by top-performing student Mei whose scheduling-algorithm debugging traced to uncritically accepted CSV-parsing code. The authors write that "we label the Boilerplate Blindspot" for this pattern; in the human-pair contrast group, peer review even of trivial code was described as a safeguard.

"More experienced students were not immune to evalua - tion lapses; a different pattern emerged that we label the Boilerplate Blindspot."
