Evidence¶

Fan Chen 2026¶

Fan Chen, Gaowei Chen. (2026). How a technology-augmented inquiry approach affects teacher practice and student learning in disadvantaged science classrooms. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09788-1

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

One-way ANCOVA in the six-week quasi-experiment (final sample 94 students; EG n = 46 TAIA, CG n = 48 worksheet inquiry), controlling pre-test and ICT competency. The article reports "a significant effect on post-test scores" with ηp² = 0.102; EG Madj = 68.61 vs CG Madj = 56.75.

"After adjusting for these covariables, the inquiry approach had a significant effect on post-test scores [F(1, 90) = 10.25, p = 0.002, ηp² = 0.102]. Bonferroni post-hoc comparisons indicated that EG students [ Madj = 68.61, SE = 2.63] significantly outperformed their CG peers [Madj = 56.75, SE = 2.57]."
