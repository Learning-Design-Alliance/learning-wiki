Fan 2001¶

Fan, X., Chen, M. (2001). Parental Involvement and Students' Academic Achievement: A Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1009048817385

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

The article's meta-analysis of the quantitative literature on parental involvement and academic achievement reports "a small to moderate, and practically meaningful, relationship"; no pooled effect size value is printed in the available text.

"A meta-analysis was conducted to synthesize the quantitative literature about the relationship between parental involvement and students' academic achievement. The findings reveal a small to moderate, and practically meaningful, relationship between parental involvement and academic achievement."
