Evidence¶

Coogan P 2020¶

Coogan P, Schon K, Li S, Cozier Y, Bethea T, Rosenberg L. (2020). Experiences of racism and subjective cognitive function in African American women. Alzheimer's & Dementia: Diagnosis, Assessment & Disease Monitoring. https://doi.org/10.1002/dad2.12067

q3 · i2 · associational · r3

Multinomial logistic regression in the same BWHS analytic cohort of 17,323 women. Institutional racism was ascertained across six circumstances (job, housing, police, courts, school, medical care). The multivariable ORs for poor compared to good SCF "ranged from 1.31 (95% CI 1.15 to 1.48) among women who reported 1 to 2 domains of institutional racism to 2.66 (95% CI 2.24 to 3.15) among those who reported 5 to 6 domains". The printed effect size for the highest versus lowest category contrast is OR = 2.66 (95% CI 2.24 to 3.15).

"The multivariable ORs for poor compared to good SCF ranged from 1.31 (95% CI 1.15 to 1.48) among women who reported 1 to 2 domains of institutional racism to 2.66 (95% CI 2.24 to 3.15) among those who reported 5 to 6 domains (P trend < .0001)"
