Rachel E. Scherr and David Hammer 2007¶

Rachel E. Scherr and David Hammer. (2007). Student Behavior and Epistemological Framing: Examples from Collaborative Active-Learning Activities in Physics. Submitted to Cognition and Instruction. https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Student+Behavior+and+Epistemological+Framing

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Cross-group observational finding from the four-hour tutorial video corpus covering three groups and three tutorials. The authors state the recurrence of clusters across groups "is a finding in itself, probably reflecting a set of cultural norms shared by the participants."

"The fact that the behavioral clusters identified for one group appeared in others as well is a finding in itself, probably reflecting a set of cultural norms shared by the participants (almost all of whom are junior and senior university students)."
