Evidence¶

Brevik A 2026¶

Brevik A, Jerpseth H and Lafontan SR (2026). Large language models as grading assistants in public health education: a method-comparison study of essay-style exam assessment. Front. Educ. 11:1904452. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1904452

q3 · i? · design · r2

Repeated single-file grading by ChatGPT on five different days (Table 3 last row: alpha 0.625, 95% CI 0.445–0.735). The authors state this "is not particularly convincing" and that variation "seems higher for the middle grades than for high and low grades" (Figure 2).

"A Krippendorf's alpha of 0.63 for ChatGPT performing the same assessment on five different days is not particularly convincing, suggesting that the average or median of multiple assessments would be a prudent step in the real-world implementation of LLM-assisted grading of non-STEM university courses"
