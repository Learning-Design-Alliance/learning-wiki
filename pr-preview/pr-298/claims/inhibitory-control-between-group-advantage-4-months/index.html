Evidence¶

Kelly Quinn 2018¶

Kelly Quinn. (2018). Cognitive Effects of Social Media Use: A Case of Older Adults. Social Media + Society. https://doi.org/10.1177/2056305118787203

q2 · i2 · causal · r1

Mixed ANOVA comparing intervention and control groups across three assessment periods; reciprocal values were used for inhibitory scores because they violated homogeneity of variance. The article reports "η2 = .135" at 4 months and a significant main effect of time (η2 = .351 at 4 weeks; η2 = .525 at 4 months) for both groups.

"These differences were moderate at the 4-week assessment point (F1,32 = .4.071, p = .052, η2 = .113), but had a large effect at the 4-month assessment (F2,31 = 5.011, p = .032, η2 = .135)."
