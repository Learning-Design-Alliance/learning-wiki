Aydinbek C 2022¶

Aydinbek C. (2022). Contributions of cognitive theory to the problem of automatization of grammatical structures in teaching foreign language. Educational Research and Reviews. https://doi.org/10.5897/ERR2022.4230

q2 · i? · review · r2

In the Conclusion the review draws this as the second of three conclusions from research on automaticity, specifying that by repetition the authors mean practice in communicative tasks rather than traditional grammar exercises; no effect size is printed.

"a consistent environment is necessary for practice to be effective, because automatic processing will occur only after practice in a consistent environment"
