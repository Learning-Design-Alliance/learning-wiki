Danae Kamdar 2025¶

Danae Kamdar, Tiffany Leones, Amanda LaTasha Armstrong, Shelton Daal & Ximena Dominguez. (2025). Unlocking the Potential of Technology Innovations: A Vision for Supporting Multilingual Learners. Digital Promise. https://digitalpromise.dspacedirect.org/items/f37dc670-36a6-4f6e-a6c4-bf312be4fac0 doi:10.51388/20.500.12265/260

q1 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative open coding of seven interviews (October-December 2024) with public media producers, research institutions, edtech developers, and review organizations. Coding found audience focus most frequent, while "explicit design for MLLs" was less prominent than universal design.

"Notably, explicit design for MLLs—where language development or cultural representation shaped core design decisions—was less prominent than general universal design."
