Markus S. Feser & Paul L. Tschisgale. (2026). AI-based scoring systematically underestimates conceptual understanding of linguistically weak students' explanations in physics. Physics Education, Leibniz Institute for Science and Mathematics Education, Kiel. https://orcid.org/0000-0001-8503-0951

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Lower linguistic quality of physics explanations is associated with higher odds that AI-based scoring underestimates conceptual understanding, across all eleven examined AI-based scoring approaches — related
  • Expert-assigned conceptual understanding and linguistic quality scores are moderately correlated (r = .50) in the student explanation dataset — related
  • ML-based scoring approaches more often overestimated expert-assigned scores, whereas LLM-based approaches more often underestimated them — related
