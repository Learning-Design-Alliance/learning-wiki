Otis 1915¶

Otis, M. (1915). A Study of Association in Defectives. The Journal of Educational Psychology, Vol. VI, No. 5. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22A+Study+of+Association+in+Defectives%22+Otis

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Comparative analysis of association-test papers from 132 defectives and 202 normal children, grouped by mental versus chronological age. The author reports "Type IV is found more frequently among defectives than among normals"; no effect size is printed.

"Type IV is found more frequently among defectives than among normals. The curve for defectives is higher and descends to 0 at a later age; that is, 11 years."

Related Claims¶

  • Sound reactions (Type III) occur sporadically at any age and indicate abnormality of mind rather than a developmental level — related
