q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical argument in Section 3.2 of this conceptual paper. The five principles govern "knowledge base curation, human oversight authority, limitation to bounded verification, transparency and attribution, and security" and are presented as "non-negotiable boundary conditions rather than aspirational guidelines".

"Institutions considering AI involvement at the intermediate level must demonstrate that all five conditions are satisfied simultaneously; satisfying only four is insufficient."
