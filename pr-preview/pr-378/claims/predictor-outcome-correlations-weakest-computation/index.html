Evidence¶

Martin Rebecca B. 2014¶

Martin Rebecca B., Cirino Paul T., Sharp Carla, Barnes Marcia. (2014). Number and counting skills in kindergarten as predictors of grade 1 mathematical skills. Learning and Individual Differences. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lindif.2014.05.006

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Correlational analysis (Table 3) in the longitudinal sample showed predictor-outcome correlations "ranged from .23 to .60, with an overall median of .47", with symbolic number predictors strongest (median r = .56) and computation showing the weakest relations.

"These ranged from .23 to .60, with an overall median of .47 (median counting r = .44; median number r = .56; median domain general r = .47). In general, relations of these predictors were weakest for computation relative to other mathematical outcomes."
