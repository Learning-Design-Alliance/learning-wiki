Evidence¶

Grier 1919¶

Grier, N. M. (1919). The range of information in biology: III. Botany. Journal of Educational Psychology, 10(10), 509–516. doi:10.1037/h0073567

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Range-of-information test of 100 botanical words given to 97 first-term and 86 second-term pupils, with self-checked second-series marking. The author reports "a decided increase in the number of terms that can be defined" and a corresponding decrease in new terms; only percentages are printed, no effect size.

"Table I, A & B, shows that with maturity there is a decided increase in the number of terms that can be defined, (D), explained, (E), with a corresponding decrease in the number of new terms, (N)."
