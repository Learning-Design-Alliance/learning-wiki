Evidence¶

Alison Mackey 1999¶

Alison Mackey. (1999). INPUT, INTERACTION, AND SECOND LANGUAGE DEVELOPMENT: An Empirical Study of Question Formation in ESL. Studies in Second Language Acquisition, 21(4). https://doi.org/10.1017/S0272263199004027

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Pretest-posttest experiment with 34 adult ESL learners of varying L1 backgrounds assigned to four experimental groups and one control group taking part in task-based interaction. The article reports that "Results of this studysupport claims concerning a link between interaction and grammatical development"; no effect size is printed.

"The central question addressed by the current study was: Can conversational interaction facilitate second language development? The study employed a pretest-posttest design. Adult ESL learners ( N =34) of varying L1 backgrounds were divided into four experimental groups and one control group."
