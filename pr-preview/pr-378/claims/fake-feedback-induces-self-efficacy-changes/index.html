Related Claims¶

  • The false feedback manipulation successfully altered performance beliefs: the high-PFB group rated their performance higher relative to age peers than the low-PFB group — related
  • High-performance feedback increases self-efficacy more than low-performance feedback, with comparable effects across younger and older adults — possibly the same claim (merge candidate)

  • Changes in self-efficacy correlate with changes in visual discrimination sensitivity after regressing out objective ability and baseline self-efficacy — related
