Evidence¶

Bialystok Ellen 2011¶

Bialystok Ellen. (2011). Coordination of Executive Functions in Monolingual and Bilingual Children. Journal of Experimental Child Psychology. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3139691/

q3 · i2 · associational · r2

Controlled experiment comparing 32 monolingual and 31 bilingual 8-year-olds on a dual-modality classification task. The 3-way ANOVA on accuracy "indicated a significant effect of language group, F (1, 61) = 3.93, p = .05, d = 0.49, with higher scores for bilinguals".

"the dual-modality condition indicated a significant effect of language group, F (1, 61) = 3.93, p = .05, d = 0.49, with higher scores for bilinguals"

Bialystok Ellen 2011 (2)¶

Bialystok Ellen. (2011). Coordination of Executive Functions in Monolingual and Bilingual Children. Journal of Experimental Child Psychology. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3139691/

q3 · i2 · associational · r2

Follow-up analysis within the dual-modality condition of the same experiment. "Considering only the visual task, the higher accuracy for the bilinguals was substantial, F (1, 61) = 7.04, p < .01, d = 0.67", with a trend interaction (p = .06) limiting the group difference to the visual task.

"Considering only the visual task, the higher accuracy for the bilinguals was substantial, F (1, 61) = 7.04, p < .01, d = 0.67"

Related Claims¶
