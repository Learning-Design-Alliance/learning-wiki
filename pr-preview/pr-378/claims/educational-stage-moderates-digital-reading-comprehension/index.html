Evidence¶

Altamura 2023¶

Altamura, L., Vargas, C., & Salmerón, L. (2023). Do New Forms of Reading Pay Off? A Meta-Analysis on the Relationship Between Leisure Digital Reading Habits and Text Comprehension. Review of Educational Research, 95(1), 53–88. doi:10.3102/00346543231216463

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Multilevel meta-regression with robust variance estimation across the 39 effect sizes found educational stage significantly moderated the association; "the effect size for high school students was positive and higher (r = .085) than the effect size obtained in middle school students (r = -.025)". The relationship turns positive at later stages.

"educational stage was a significant moderator (p = .047; R2 = .57). We performed pairwise comparisons across the levels and two comparisons were significant: the difference between middle and high school students (p = .021) and between primary and university students (p = .030)."
