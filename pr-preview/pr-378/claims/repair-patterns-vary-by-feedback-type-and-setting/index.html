Evidence¶

Roy Lyster and Hirohide Mori 2006¶

Roy Lyster and Hirohide Mori. (2006). Interactional feedback and instructional counterbalance. Studies in Second Language Acquisition, 28(2), 269-300. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0272263106060128

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Observational analysis of immediate effects of the three feedback types on learner uptake and repair across the two immersion settings. The article reports "the largest proportion of repair resulting from prompts in French immersion and from recasts in Japanese immersion." No effect size is printed.

"distinctively varied student uptake and repair patterns in relation to feedback type, with the largest proportion of repair resulting from prompts in French immersion and from recasts in Japanese immersion"
