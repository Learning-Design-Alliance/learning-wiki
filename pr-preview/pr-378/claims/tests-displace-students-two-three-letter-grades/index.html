Uhl 1916¶

Uhl, W. L. (1916). Mentality tests for college freshmen. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/edu

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Quarter-by-quarter displacement analysis (Tables V and VI) comparing test ranks with first-semester English and mathematics grades for the 100 freshmen; the author gives the example of an A by one test corresponding to a C or D by another.

"the shiftings are so great in all the tests that from one-fifth to one-third are displaced as much as would be indicated in terms of university grades by two or three letters;"
