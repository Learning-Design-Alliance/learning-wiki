Evidence¶

Flunger 2024¶

Flunger, B., Verdonschot, A., Zitzmann, S., Hornstra, L., & van Gog, T. (2024). A Bayesian approach to students' perceptions of teachers' autonomy support. Learning and Instruction, 91, 101873. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2023.101873

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Bayesian Multilevel-CFAs comparing competing factor structures in a sample of 446 Dutch primary students from 22 mathematics classes. Model 1.2 with two L1 factors and one L2 factor fit best, so "individual support could be distinguished from class-directed support on L1, but not on L2."

"For all autonomy-supportive strategies, Model 1.2, specifying perceptions of individual and class-directed autonomy as two separate factors at L1, and one latent factor of class-average autonomy support at L2, had the best fit."
