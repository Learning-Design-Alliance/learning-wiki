Evidence¶

Frank N. Freeman 1922¶

Frank N. Freeman. (1922). Research Versus Propaganda in Visual Education. The Journal of Educational Psychology, Vol. XI, No. 5. https://psycnet.apa.org/journals/edu/13/5/

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Tentative psychological analysis, offered in advance of experimentation. Freeman illustrates with motion pictures, where the raw material of experience is given visually but significance is furnished by captions, and notes only crude meanings such as physical combat and slap-stick humor are conveyed visually.

"The meanings conveyed by concrete objects or their pictorial representation must be of a rather concrete, simple kind. Such representation is not suited to convey the more subtle abstract or general meanings. These meanings are conveyed by language."
