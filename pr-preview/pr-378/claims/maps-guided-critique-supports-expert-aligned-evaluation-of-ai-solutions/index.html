Evidence¶

Nikhil Sanjay Borse 2026¶

Nikhil Sanjay Borse, Amir Bralin, Sean Savage, N. Sanjay Rebello. (2026). Probing AI-generated physics solutions and preparing students to critique them. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12533 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2608.12533

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Exploratory qualitative comparison of 24 lab groups (15 Solution-condition, 9 Critique-condition) evaluating an o4-mini solution found Critique-condition responses "showed more frequent attention to the kinds of AI-solution weaknesses identified in the model analysis"; the authors frame it as exploratory.

"These exploratory results suggest that MAPS-based reflection may support more expert-aligned critique than independent problem solving alone."
