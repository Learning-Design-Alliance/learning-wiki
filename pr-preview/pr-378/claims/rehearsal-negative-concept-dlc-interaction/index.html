Evidence¶

Sijmkens 2024¶

Sijmkens, E., De Cock, M., & De Laet, T. (2023). Scaffolding Students' Use of Metacognitive Activities Using Discipline- and Topic-Specific Reflective Prompts. Metacognition and Learning, 18(3), 811–843. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-023-09363-w

q2 · i? · associational · r2

From hierarchical regression and mediation test results (N=272). The rehearsal and time/effort regulation variables were negatively correlated in the regression model. The authors suggest rehearsal-focused students may not benefit from topic-specific feedback in the reflection modules.

"This indicates that students who put more emphasis on rehearsal and memorising techniques while studying may have a less profound conceptual understanding of the exam problem studied. Similarly, the mediation tests revealed that students' prescore on the 'rehearsal' subscale is negatively correlated with their interaction with the DLC."
