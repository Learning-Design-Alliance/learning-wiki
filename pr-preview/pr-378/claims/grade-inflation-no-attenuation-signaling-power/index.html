Key Questions: How important are course grades as predictors of students' future success? 2025¶

Key Questions: How important are course grades as predictors of students' future success? (2025). UChicago Consortium / FutureEd. https://consortium.uchicago.edu

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The article reports, citing Pattison, Grodsky, and Muller (2013), that analysis of GPA data from four nationally representative longitudinal samples found "no statistically significant attenuation in the signaling power of grades" and some evidence of an increase. No effect sizes are printed in this article.

"Analyzes GPA data from four nationally representative, longitudinal samples and "do not observe any statistically significant attenuation in the signaling power of grades over this time period. In fact, we find some evidence of an increase in the signaling power of grades.""
