Sophia C. Weissgerber 2021¶

Sophia C. Weissgerber, Matthias Brunmair & Ralf Rummer. (2021). Null and Void? Errors in Meta-analysis on Perceptual Disfluency and Recommendations to Improve Meta-analytical Reproducibility. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09579-1

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

The commentary's re-analysis, using TRANSFER as the chosen outcome variable across Levels 2–4 of its framework, found the original results robust for TRANSFER but doubtful for RECALL and the moderator analysis.

"Applying reproducibility checks to TRANSFER performance as the chosen outcome variable, we found Xie's and colleagues' results to be (rather) robust. Yet, regarding RECALL performance and the moderator analysis, the identified problems raise doubts about the credibility of the reported results."
