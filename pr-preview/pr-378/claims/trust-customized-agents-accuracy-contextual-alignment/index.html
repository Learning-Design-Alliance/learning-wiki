Thematic analysis found clarity/operability codes more prevalent in customized groups (C1 and C3), while lack-of-contextual-anchoring codes were dominant in C4 (general-purpose). Students described customized agents as clearer, more professional, and better aligned with task requirements.

"Together with the quantitative evidence of enhanced accuracy, these qualitative findings suggest that perceived feedback quality may be associated with learners' trust in the agent."
