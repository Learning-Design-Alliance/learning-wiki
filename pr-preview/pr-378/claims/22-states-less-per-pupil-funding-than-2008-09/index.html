Krausen 2020¶

Krausen, K., Caparas, R., Ripma, T., & Willis, J. (2020). Strategic resource allocation for the 21st century: How state leaders can address the silent recession. WestEd. https://www.wested.org/resources/strategic-resource-allocation/

q2 · i? · review · r2

The report's review of state funding trends states that "22 states still provided less per-pupil K–12 education funding than in 2008–09" as of fiscal year 2017, citing Leachman (2019). Interviewed state leaders noted increases were often restoring programs rather than adding resources.

"Indeed, as of fiscal year 2017, 22 states still provided less per-pupil K–12 education funding than in 2008–09."
