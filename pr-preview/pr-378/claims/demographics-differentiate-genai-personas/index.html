Evidence¶

Afef Saihi and Vian Ahmed 2026¶

Afef Saihi and Vian Ahmed. (2026). Uncovering adoption personas for generative AI in higher education: a clustering-based segmentation approach. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00583-z

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Normalized frequency distributions of six profiling variables visualized via stacked bar charts (Fig. 5a-f) across the four clusters. The article concludes the variables "meaningfully differentiate user personas".

"These analyses demonstrate that demographic and contextual variables meaningfully differentiate user personas, revealing distinct patterns in how individuals across roles, disciplines, genders, age groups, and levels of technological proficiency engage with GenAI chatbots."

Afef Saihi and Vian Ahmed 2026 (2)¶

Afef Saihi and Vian Ahmed. (2026). Uncovering adoption personas for generative AI in higher education: a clustering-based segmentation approach. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00583-z

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Study Field distributions (Fig. 5d) across clusters. The article also reports Cluster 1 was male-dominated (88%) whereas Cluster 2 was predominantly female (89%), and Cluster 2 skewed younger (52% aged 18-24).

"Cluster 1 was overwhelmingly composed of engineering participants (88%), while Cluster 3, the most engaged persona, featured a balanced mix with a notable presence from business disciplines (55%)."
