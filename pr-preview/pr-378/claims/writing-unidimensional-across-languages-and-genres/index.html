Evidence¶

Kim Young-Suk Grace 2022¶

Kim Young-Suk Grace, Wolters Alissa, Mercado Janet, & Quinn Jamie. (2022). Crosslinguistic Transfer of Higher Order Cognitive Skills and Their Roles in Writing for English-Spanish Dual Language Learners. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000516

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Confirmatory factor analysis of 317 Grade 1–2 dual language learners' writing quality ratings (TEWL-3 narrative and WIAT-3/adapted opinion prompts in English and Spanish) compared nine models. The article states writing "was best described as unidimensional across the languages and genres"; Model 3 showed ΔnBIC = −8.74 versus Model 2 and the writing factor was reliable (ω = .77).

"In other words, higher order cognitive skills were defined by a bifactor structure with a higher order cognitive general factor, and English and Spanish specific factors whereas writing was best described as unidimensional across the languages and genres."

Related Claims¶
