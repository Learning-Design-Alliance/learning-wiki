Evidence¶

Hosseini 2026¶

Hosseini, H. (2026). The Pedagogy of AI Mistakes: Fostering Higher-Order Thinking. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05472 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2605.05472

q2 · i? · design · r2

Correlational analysis in the prior-knowledge survey of the 13-student course, comparing six adapted self-report AI-literacy questions (from AILQ and MAILS) with five objective multiple-choice AI competency questions. The authors report "a moderate negative correlation (r=−0.39) that is not statistically significant (p≈0.21)" and interpret it as possible overestimation.

"correlating self-reported AI literacy with objective competency, we find a moderate negative correlation (r=−0.39) that is not statistically significant (p≈0.21), but could suggest potential overestimation of AI abilities among students."
