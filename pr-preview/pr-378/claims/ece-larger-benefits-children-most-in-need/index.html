High-Quality Early Child Education is Associated With 2023¶

High-Quality Early Child Education is Associated With... (2023). Regional Educational Laboratory West, WestEd. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/edlabs/regions/west

q2 · i? · review · r?

The summary reports, citing Watts et al. (2023) on heterogeneity in public preschool impacts, that ECE "often yield larger benefits" for low-income children, children of color, and children in resource-poor environments. No effect sizes printed.

"Early Childhood Education programs often yield larger benefits for children who are most in need, such as children from low-income families, children of color, and those living in environments with fewer educational resources."
