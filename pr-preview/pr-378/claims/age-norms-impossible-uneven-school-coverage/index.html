Evidence¶

Pressey 1920¶

Pressey, S. L. (1920). Two Important Points with Regard to Age-Grade Tables. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=Two+Important+Points+with+Regard+to+Age-Grade+Tables

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Descriptive analysis of what fraction of each age would be covered by achievement testing given the two cities' age-grade distributions; in City C 63 per cent of eight-year-olds and 35 per cent of nine-year-olds would not take the examination at all. The author concludes general age norms are practically impossible under these conditions.

"A test given to the Junior High School of City C would not test more than 45 per cent of the children at any age. A similar test in City F would get scores from 62 per cent, of the thirteens, the highest per cent at any age."
