Spangenberg 2025¶

Spangenberg, E. D. (2025). Constructive alignment of a mathematics methodology module. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-025-09712-z

q2 · i? · design · r2

Experiment-phase survey of 42 fourth-year mathematics pre-service teachers on Blackboard; responses were inductively coded against the six derived learning outcomes and frequencies calculated. The 21st-century teaching methods outcome drew the largest response total, with "Knowledge of teaching methods (20)" the most frequent code.

"Integrate teaching methods for the 21st century into mathematics lessons; • Knowledge of teaching methods (20) • Lesson planning and presentation (10) • Technology integration (8)"
