Evidence¶

J. B. Miner 1910¶

J. B. Miner. (1910). The College Laggard. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22The+College+Laggard%22+Miner+Journal+of+Educational+Psychology

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Tabulation of the author's impressions from questionnaire letters for 86 laggard-list students, classifying causes as extrinsic (14), intellectual (15), or moral (57). The author notes doubtful cases were never classed in the moral group.

"in 15 cases to intellectual incapacity, and in 57 to moral rea-sons such as lack of purpose, laziness, and inability to resist social, fraternity and other temptations which interfered with work."

J. B. Miner 1910 (2)¶

J. B. Miner. (1910). The College Laggard. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22The+College+Laggard%22+Miner+Journal+of+Educational+Psychology

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Case-letter evidence for about a third of the 78 heard-from cases, including laggards with high school averages of 90 per cent and principals' testimonials to strong prior work, which the author treats as making intellectual incapacity implausible for them.

"I have at least 27 in-stances which indicate that the student has passed high school work creditably or showed reversal of form after leaving Minnesota."
