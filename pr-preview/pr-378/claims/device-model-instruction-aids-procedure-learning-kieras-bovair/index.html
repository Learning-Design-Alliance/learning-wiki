Evidence¶

Ellen P. Hall 1995¶

Ellen P. Hall, Sherrie P. Gott, Robert A. Pokorny. (1995). A Procedural Guide to Cognitive Task Analysis: The PARI Methodology. Armstrong Laboratory, Human Resources Directorate, Brooks AFB, Texas. AL/HR-TR-1995-0108. https://doi.org/10.21236/ada303654

q2 · i? · design · r2

The report attributes this to experiments by Kieras and Bovair (1984) in which one group learned control-panel operating procedures by rote while another received device topology and internal-structure instruction first; the report states the device-model group "learned the procedures faster, retained them more accurately." No effect sizes are printed.

"they learned the procedures faster, retained them more accurately, executed them faster, and simplified the inefficient procedures more often than the rote group."
