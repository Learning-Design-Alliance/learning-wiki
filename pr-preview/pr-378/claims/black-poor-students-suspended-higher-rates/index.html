Evidence¶

Nathan Barrett 2021¶

Nathan Barrett, Andrew McEachin, Jonathan Mills, Jon Valant. (2021). Disparities and discrimination in student discipline by race and family income. Journal of Human Resources, 56(3), 711-748. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/disparities-and-discrimination-in-student-discipline-by-race-and-family-income/

q3 · i? · causal · r?

The article opens by establishing the well-documented discipline disparity: "Black and poor students are suspended from US schools at higher rates than White and nonpoor students," while noting the causes of these disparities had not been established.

"Black and poor students are suspended from US schools at higher rates than White and nonpoor students. While the existence of these disparities has been clear, the causes of the disparities have not."

  • Students experiencing homelessness change schools at triple the rate of other students and are twice as likely to receive an out-of-school suspension — related
  • The authors interpret the same-offense discipline gap as suggesting possible implicit bias against Black students and students with disabilities — related
