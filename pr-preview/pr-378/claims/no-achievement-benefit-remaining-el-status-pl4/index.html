Faulkner-Bond 2026¶

Faulkner-Bond, M. (2026). Finding the sweet spot for English Learner reclassification: A study of California's criteria. WestEd. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED681359

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Analysis of subsequent SBAC math and ELA scores for students in grades 3-7 in spring 2021 or 2022 who scored at ELPAC overall PL 4, comparing those reclassified (ELP4 RFEP) with those not reclassified (ELP4 EL) by census day the following year, with standardized scores and confidence intervals. The report finds "minimal or no achievement differences 1 year later... after controlling for SES."

"Across all years and content areas, there were minimal or no achievement differences 1 year later between ELPAC overall PL 4 students after controlling for SES."
