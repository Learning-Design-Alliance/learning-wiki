Evidence¶

ap-enrollment¶

Mills, K., Coenraad, M., Ruiz, P., Burke, Q., & Weisgrau J. (2021, December). Computational thinking for an inclusive world: A resource for educators to learn and lead. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/138

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The report's current-state section, citing Code.org et al. (2020), reports that "In 2019, girls comprised only 29 percent of students that took the AP Computer Science exam." It adds that students of color comprised less than 25 percent even though they account for about 44% of the total student population, reflecting the lack of diversity in the CS workforce.

"In 2019, girls comprised only 29 percent of students that took the AP Computer Science exam. Additionally, students who identified as Black/African American, Native Hawaiian/Pacific Islander, Latinx, Alaska Native/Native American comprised less than 25 percent"
