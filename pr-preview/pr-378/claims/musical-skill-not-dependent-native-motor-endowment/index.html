Evidence¶

J. C. B 1916¶

J. C. B. (1916). Endowment. The Journal of Educational Psychology, 7(8), 498-499. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Educational_Psychology

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The editorial reports, citing Baerwald's 1916 study of motor imagery, that findings were "negative" on whether musical performance skill depends on "the degree of native motor endowment". No effect size is printed.

"On the more fundamental question whether the development of skill in musical performance is in any way dependent on the degree of native motor endowment, the author's findings are negative."
