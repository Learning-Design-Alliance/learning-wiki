Evidence¶

Stephan Vonschallen 2026¶

Stephan Vonschallen, Karim Kaufmann, Dominique Oberle, Friederike Eyssel, Theresa Schmiedel. (2026). Teachy Mini: Development and Preliminary Evaluation of a Knowledge-Based Generative Social Robot for Higher Education. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22345 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.22345

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Welch t-test on the Interest/Enjoyment subscale of the Intrinsic Motivation Inventory; the KBD group was "descriptively higher" but the difference "did not reach statistical significance".

"Although the KBD group (M = 5.30, SD = 1.27) reported descriptively higher enjoyment than the control group (M = 4.88, SD = 1.41), this difference did not reach statistical significance,t(21.76) = 0.762,p=.454,95%CI={−0.718,1.551}."

Stephan Vonschallen 2026 (2)¶

Stephan Vonschallen, Karim Kaufmann, Dominique Oberle, Friederike Eyssel, Theresa Schmiedel. (2026). Teachy Mini: Development and Preliminary Evaluation of a Knowledge-Based Generative Social Robot for Higher Education. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22345

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Welch t-test on the Perceived Competence subscale; the control group (M = 4.71, SD = 1.00) had descriptively higher perceived competence than the KBD group (M = 4.56, SD = 1.41), but "no significant difference was found".

"However, no significant difference was found between the KBD group and the control group,t(19.80) = 0.31,p= .762."
