What Works Clearinghouse 2007¶

What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. (2007). WWC Intervention Report: Waterford Early Reading Program™. July 16, 2007. http://www.pearsondigital.com/pdfs/werp/researchsummary-werp.pdf

q2 · i? · causal · r2

WWC re-analysis of the Hecht & Close (2002) quasi-experimental study (76 Kindergarten students in six Ohio schools, intervention matched to comparison). The WWC found none of the nine alphabetics effects statistically significant after clustering and multiple-comparison corrections, while the "average effect size across all nine outcomes" was large enough to be considered substantively important according to the WWC criteria.

"Hecht & Close (2002) reported statistically significant positive effects of the Waterford Early Reading Program™ for all nine outcomes. However, the WWC analysis found that none of these effects were statistically significant. The average effect size across all nine outcomes was large enough to be considered substantively important"
