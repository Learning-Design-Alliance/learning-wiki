de la Torre 2021¶

de la Torre, M., Freire, S., & Blanchard, A. (2021). English Learners in Chicago Public Schools: An exploration of the influence of pre-k and early grade years. Chicago, IL: University of Chicago Consortium on School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/English-learners-in-Chicago-Public-Schools-an-exploration-of-the-influence-of-pre-K

q2 · i? · associational · r?

The report compares ELs who "received language supports through their schools’ Bilingual Education Services" with students who refused bilingual services in kindergarten, finding higher attendance and long-run academic outcomes for service recipients. Observational; no effect size printed.
