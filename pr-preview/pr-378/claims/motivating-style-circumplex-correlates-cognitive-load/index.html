Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Extraneous cognitive load is negatively associated with autonomous motivation and positively with disengagement, while intrinsic load is negatively associated with motivation, engagement, and achievement — related
  • The demanding subarea of teacher control shows near-zero or ambiguous correlations with motivation, engagement, and achievement, unlike the domineering subarea — related
  • Teachers' load-reducing instructional strategies predict lower extraneous and intrinsic cognitive load and have direct positive associations with motivation and engagement — related
  • Teachers' load-reducing strategies correlate with lower cognitive load and higher motivation, engagement, and achievement, and their motivating styles with lower load and higher motivation and engagement (Evans et al., 2024) — related
  • Promoting autonomous motivation was linked to higher motivation, lower experienced cognitive load, and higher achievement, but causality cannot be interpreted — related
