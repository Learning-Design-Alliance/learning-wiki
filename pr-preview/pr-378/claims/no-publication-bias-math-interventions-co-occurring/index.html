Evidence¶

Ellinor Waaland 2026¶

Ellinor Waaland, Vibeke Rønneberg, Dieuwer ten Braak. (2026). Effects and Characteristics of Mathematics Interventions for Students with Co-Occurring Difficulties in Mathematics and Reading: A Systematic Review with Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10184-x

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Publication-bias assessment including contour-enhanced funnel plots, Egger's regression test with robust variance, and PET models. PET intercepts were non-significant in both models; the authors conclude "no substantial evidence of publication bias and small-study effects" and did not proceed to PEESE.

"Formally testing for funnel plot asymmetry and accounting for dependencies, Egger`s regression test produced non-significant findings both with (intercept: −3.8, SE = 1.62, p = 0.100, and slope: 13.3, SE = 4.63, p = 0.128) and without outliers (intercept: 0.29, SE = 0.425, p = 0.571, and slope: 0.81, SE = 1.21, p = 0.565), indicating no evidence of publication bias."
