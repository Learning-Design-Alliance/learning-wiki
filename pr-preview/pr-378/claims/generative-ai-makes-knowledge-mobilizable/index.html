q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

A theoretical argument in the section on what generative and agentic AI changes. The authors define the new condition: "generative AI can reorganize externally available knowledge into immediately usable explanations, plans, drafts, examples, and action sequences". This is a conceptual claim, not a tested result; the article offers no direct data for it here.
