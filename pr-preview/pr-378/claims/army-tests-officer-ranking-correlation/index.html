Evidence¶

Geyer 1921¶

Geyer, D. L. (1921). The Reliability of Rankings by Group Intelligence Tests. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.jstor.org/journal/jeduapsy

q2 · i3 · associational · r1

The article cites, from Yoakum and Yerkes's Army Mental Tests, the correspondence between Army Intelligence Test selection and officers' rankings, with printed correlations r = 0.536 (average) and 0.64 to 0.75 in seven of twelve companies.

"in twelve companies the average correlation between rankings by intelligence tests and rankings by officers on the basis of soldier value was 0.536, and in seven of the twelve companies it ranged from 0.64 to 0.75."
