Morales-Chicas Jessica 2017¶

Morales-Chicas Jessica, Graham Sandra. (2017). Latinos’ Changing Ethnic Group Representation From Elementary to Middle School: Perceived Belonging and Academic Achievement. Journal of Research on Adolescence. https://doi.org/10.1111/jora.12292

q3 · i2 · associational · r2

Descriptive correlation analysis (Table 1) across the analytic sample; the printed correlation between belonging and GPA is "r = .20", a modest positive association reported as descriptive, not as a tested causal link.

"School belonging and GPA were correlated (r = .20) and most of the predictors and covariates were modestly correlated with these outcomes in the expected direction."

Related Claims¶

  • Latino girls reported higher belonging and achievement than boys, and Latino subgroup and parent-education differences emerged in the sixth grade — related
  • School ethnic diversity moderates the representation-change association: declining same-ethnic representation predicts lower belonging and GPA only in less diverse middle schools — related
