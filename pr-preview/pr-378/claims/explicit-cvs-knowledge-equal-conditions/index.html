Evidence¶

Triona 2003¶

Triona, L. M., & Klahr, D. (2003). Point and Click or Grab and Heft: Comparing the Influence of Physical and Virtual Instructional Materials on Elementary School Students' Ability to Design Experiments. Cognition and Instruction, 21(2), 149–173. https://doi.org/10.1207/s1532690xci2102_02

q3 · i? · causal · r2

A 2 × 3 MANOVA on the proportion of trials with an unconfounded design plus an explicit mention of controlling other variables found "no interaction, F(2, 88) = 0.71, ns". Means rose from pretest M = .07 (SD = .17) to posttest M = .52 (SD = .42) and transfer M = .46 (SD = .39).
