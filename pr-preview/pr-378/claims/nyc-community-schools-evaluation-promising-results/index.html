Maier 2023¶

Maier, A., & Rivera-Rodriguez, A. (2023). State strategies for investing in community schools. Learning Policy Institute. https://doi.org/10.54300/612.402

q2 · i? · review · r2

The report cites an evaluation of the New York City initiative, the largest in the state, drawing on both state and local funding to support a network of over 420 community schools. It characterizes the findings as "promising results" across the three outcomes; no effect sizes are printed.

"An evaluation of the New York City initiative found promising results, including increased graduation rates, increased achievement in elementary and middle school mathematics, and reductions in chronic absenteeism."
