Suriá-Martínez R 2026¶

Suriá-Martínez R, García-Castillo F, López-Sánchez C and García del Castillo JA (2026). Between perceived competence and artificial intelligence: academic self-efficacy profiles in university students with motor disabilities. Front. Educ. 11:1956192. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1956192

q2 · i3 · associational · r1

One-way ANOVA comparing reported AI use across the three LPA profiles (N = 102), with Bonferroni post hoc comparisons significant among all three profiles (p < .05). The quote fragment "F(2, 99) = 27.84, p < .001, η2 = .36" grounds the large effect; means were 2.41, 3.56, and 4.68.

"Analysis of variance showed significant group differences in AI use, F(2, 99) = 27.84, p < .001, η2 = .36, representing a large effect size."
