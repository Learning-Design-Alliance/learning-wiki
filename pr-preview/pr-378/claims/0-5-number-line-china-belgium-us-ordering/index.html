Evidence¶

Torbeyns 2015¶

Torbeyns, J., Schneider, M., Xin, Z., & Siegler, R. S. (2015). Bridging the Gap: Fraction Understanding is Central to Mathematics Achievement in Students from Three Different Continents. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2014.03.002

q3 · i3 · associational · r1

The same Country × Grade ANOVA applied to the 0-5 number line estimation task in the 187-student study showed the full hypothesized ordering China > Belgium > U.S., with printed effect size partial eta² = .28; the Country × Grade interaction was again not significant.

"On this more difficult number line task, Chinese students estimated more accurately than Belgian students, who, in turn, estimated more accurately than U.S. students, PAE = 9%, 16% and 23%, F(2, 186) = 34.46, p < .01, partial eta² = .28."
