Evidence¶

Potel 2026¶

Potel, J. T., & Kumashiro, M. (2026). Artificial Intelligence as a Personal Coach: A Narrative Review of Benefits and Risks in Educational and Health Contexts. Behavioral Sciences, 16(8), 1431. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16081431

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review cites an RCT (Stasinaki et al.) where AI incorporation did not improve sustained weight loss, and an uncontrolled study (Daley et al.) where stress, anxiety, and depression reductions could not be specifically attributed to chatbot use.

"when used to counsel dieting children in a long-term randomized controlled trial, incorporating AI did not improve sustained weight loss, although there were improvements in other physical outcomes"
