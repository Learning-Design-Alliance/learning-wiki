q2 · i? · the article prints no effect size for this finding · qualitative · r3

Theme from caregiver focus groups and healthcare worker interviews in the qualitative study. Counselors reported the system "has brought us together with the caregivers so that they share their feelings with us."

"In addition, connecting directly with HCWs on a regular basis resulted in stronger relationships between HCWs and caregivers (TDF domain: environmental context and resources) through building trust and improved communication, resulting in greater satisfaction with the patient/provider relationship which is a key to delivery of quality health services."
