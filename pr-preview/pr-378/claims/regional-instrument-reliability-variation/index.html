Evidence¶

Könül Karimova 2026¶

Könül Karimova, Hsing-Fen Tu. (2026). Dimensionality, Reliability, and Validity of Academic Resilience Scales: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10207-7

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Exploratory moderator analysis of internal consistency by geographic region (Table 6). The review states Latin America was represented by a single estimate and Europe and the Middle East by three each, so these should not be interpreted as stable regional summaries.

"Reliability was highest in Latin America, α/ω = 0.98, 95% CI [0.98, 0.98], followed by the Middle East, α/ω = 0.91, 95% CI [0.56, 0.98], Asia, α/ω = 0.90, 95% CI [0.88, 0.92]"

Könül Karimova 2026 (2)¶

Könül Karimova, Hsing-Fen Tu. (2026). Dimensionality, Reliability, and Validity of Academic Resilience Scales: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10207-7

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Instrument-specific pooled reliability estimates from the systematic review (Table 7). The review cautions that instrument-specific estimates based on one or two effects should not be treated as stable evidence of comparative instrument quality.

"The ARS showed strong reliability, α/𝜔 = 0.90, 95% CI [0.87, 0.92], and the ABS showed acceptable-to-strong reliability, α/𝜔 = 0.82, 95% CI [0.79, 0.85]."
