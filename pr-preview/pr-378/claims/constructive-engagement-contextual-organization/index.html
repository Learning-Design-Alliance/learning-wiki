Chen 2026¶

Chen, Z., Li, H., Xiao, Z., Qu, H., & Xie, X. (2026). Informal learning emerges in everyday human–LLM interaction. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17643 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.17643

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Descriptive contrast across the six task settings comparing framed versus unframed conversations, reported in Results Section 2.2 with lifts of "+2.5 to +12.8 points" for constructive engagement; constructive participation also appeared without explicit framing.

"The cognitive-engagement lift ranged from +17.7 to +51.3 percentage points across settings, and the constructive-engagement lift ranged from +2.5 to +12.8 points."

Chen 2026 (2)¶

Chen, Z., Li, H., Xiao, Z., Qu, H., & Xie, X. (2026). Informal learning emerges in everyday human–LLM interaction. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17643. doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.17643

q2 · i1 · associational · r2

Grouped-binomial rate model using total user turns as denominator, from Results Section 2.2. Per-turn odds were higher in longer conversations ("OR=1.13" for 4–6 turns; "OR=1.06" for 7 or more).

"relative to conversations with 2–3 user turns, the per-turn odds of constructive engagement were 13% higher in conversations with 4–6 user turns (OR=1.13) and 6% higher in conversations with 7 or more user turns (OR=1.06; Supplementary Table B13)"
