q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Null-coverage finding from the RQ1 construct tally in Results. The review attributes the absence of self-regulation studies to underdeveloped cognitive and metacognitive scaffolding in current IVR environments, and suggests immersion may have been implicitly assumed rather than measured.

"No studies examined motivation, self-regulation, or immersion."
