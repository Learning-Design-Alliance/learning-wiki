q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The review reports, citing Fong et al. (2024), a scoping review finding that "belongingness was more strongly associated with academic achievement among marginalized students" relative to white students. The reviewed study's design is not described in this article beyond its scoping-review status.

"Fong and colleagues’ (2024) scoping review of research found that belongingness was more strongly associated with academic achievement among marginalized students, including racially marginalized students, when compared to white students."

  • Belongingness is among the factors that most strongly and reliably affect learners' academic motivation and achievement — a broader claim this one bears on

  • Students with a stronger sense of belonging tend to show more academic self-confidence, motivation, engagement and achievement (review reports Pedler et al.) — a broader claim this one bears on
  • Learning happens best when students feel a sense of belonging, while alienation and exclusion create cognitive barriers — related
  • Belonging mediates the relationship between racial/ethnic identity and both academic achievement and emotional symptoms — related

  • Sense of mathematics belonging predicts algebra learning, even after controlling for prior knowledge and background factors — related
