Evidence¶

Tetzlaff 2023¶

Tetzlaff, L., Edelsbrunner, P., Schmitterer, A., Hartmann, U., & Brod, G. (2023). Modeling Interactions Between Multivariate Learner Characteristics and Interventions: a Person-Centered Approach. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09830-5

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Latent profile analysis of the whole sample (N = 517) on four indicator variables; the four-profile model was selected on fit indices (VLMR significant up to four profiles, p = .02). The article reports "The poor readers (n = 78)" among the four profiles, with good readers n = 88, poor comprehenders n = 195, poor decoders n = 156.

"The four identified profiles can be labeled in accordance with the SVR (see Fig. 2). The poor readers (n = 78) are mainly characterized by their low performance in the reading comprehension task, but they also score well below the mean on all the other indicator variables."
