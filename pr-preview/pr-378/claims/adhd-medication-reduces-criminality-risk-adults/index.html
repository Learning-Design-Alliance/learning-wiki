Narrative review of pharmaco-epidemiological register analyses, reporting a Swedish register study of adults with ADHD attributed to Lichtenstein et al. (2012). The review states treatment "signiﬁcantly reduces the risk for criminality". Significance reported without a printed statistic or effect size.

"A large register-based Swedish study of adults with ADHD found that treatment with ADHD medication signiﬁcantly reduces the risk for criminality ( Lichtenstein et al., 2012 )."

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Pharmaco-epidemiological register studies indicate ADHD medication treatment reduces risk of injuries and emergency ward visits in children with ADHD
  • At least 15% of children with ADHD continue to meet full diagnostic criteria by age 25 and a further 50% meet criteria in partial remission, with European estimates of persistence up to 80%
  • A third systematic review concluded ADHD medication reduced symptoms and impairments but found limited and inconsistent evidence for long-term medication effects on social functioning, academic achievement, employment status, and psychiatric comorbidity — related
