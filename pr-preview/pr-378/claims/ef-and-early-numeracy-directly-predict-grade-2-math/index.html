Evidence¶

Mercader J 2018¶

Mercader J, Miranda A, Presentación MJ, Siegenthaler R and Rosel JF (2018) Contributions of Motivation, Early Numeracy Skills, and Executive Functioning to Mathematical Performance. A Longitudinal Study. Front. Psychol. 8:2375. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2017.02375

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Structural equation modeling of 180 children assessed at T1 (kindergarten) and T2 (second year of Primary Education); the final model fit the data (CFI = 0.960; RMSEA = 0.058) and "The explained variance of the model dependent variable (F4, Mathematic Performance) was R2 = 0.573."

"The latent variable Mathematic Performance (F4) receives significant effects only from the latent variables Executive Functioning (F2) and Early Numeracy Skills (F3), but not from Motivation (F1)."
