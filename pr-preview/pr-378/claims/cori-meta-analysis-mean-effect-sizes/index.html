q3 · i3 · theoretical · r3

Meta-analysis by Guthrie et al. (2007) summarizing 11 CORI studies with elementary and middle school students, each containing CORI and comparison groups. Mean effect sizes spanned "a low of 0.49 for students' self-efficacy to 1.20 for the intrinsic motivation composite", including 1.00 for teacher-rated engagement and 0.91 for standardized reading comprehension.

"They range from a low of 0.49 for students' self-efficacy to 1.20 for the intrinsic motivation composite. As can be seen, nearly all of the effect sizes are larger than those Lazowski and Hulleman (2016) reported in their summary of motivation interventions."
