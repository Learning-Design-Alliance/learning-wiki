Narang 2025¶

Narang, Unnati, Vishal Sachdev, and Ruichun Liu. (2025). When AI Wears Many Hats: The Role of Generative Artificial Intelligence in Marketing Education. Journal of Public Policy & Marketing. https://doi.org/10.1177/07439156251328237

q1 · i? · theoretical · r2

Authors' conceptual interpretation in the framework development section, not a tested result. The article states that "over-reliance on GAI can weaken teaching presence by reducing human interaction, potentially limiting mentorship opportunities," and similarly warns that over-reliance as a teammate risks diminishing peer-to-peer interaction and collaborative skills.

"However, over-reliance on GAI can weaken teaching presence by reducing human interaction, potentially limiting mentorship opportunities."
