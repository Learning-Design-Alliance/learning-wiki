Evidence¶

Rebecca Pape 2026¶

Rebecca Pape, Joachim Thomas. (2026). Exploring young learners’ prompted and unprompted SRL think-alouds. Learning and Instruction 102 (2026) 102294. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102294

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Between-subjects experiment with 33 German 6th graders (n = 17 prompted, n = 16 control) in a 30-min CBLE session with think-aloud protocols; Mann-Whitney U tests showed the prompted group "engaged in domain- general metacognitive SRL activities more frequently than students who were not prompted".

"students in the experimental group engaged in domain- general metacognitive SRL activities more frequently than students who were not prompted"
