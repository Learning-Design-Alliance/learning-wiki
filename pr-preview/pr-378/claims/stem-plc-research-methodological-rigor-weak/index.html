Fulton 2010¶

Fulton, K., Doerr, H., & Britton, T. (2010). STEM Teachers in Professional Learning Communities: A Knowledge Synthesis. National Commission on Teaching & America's Future / WestEd. https://www.nctaf.org/resources/events/STEMWingspreadConference.htm

q2 · i? · review · r2

The synthesis's own Standards of Evidence analysis: an intensive 4-8-hour PhD-level analyst inspection of each of the 30 Type 1 articles against 31 standards, summarized in the overall-rating table showing "adequate 0%" through "poor 54%".

Fulton 2010 (2)¶

Fulton, K., Doerr, H., & Britton, T. (2010). STEM Teachers in Professional Learning Communities: A Knowledge Synthesis. National Commission on Teaching & America's Future / WestEd. https://www.nctaf.org/resources/events/STEMWingspreadConference.htm

q2 · i? · review · r3

The synthesis's judgment in the How Well Do We Know It section, based on its methodological inspections. It found individual studies weak while concluding that collectively the research builds "a supportive case that STEM PLCs have effects valued by STEM educators and policymakers".

"Individual research studies were found to be weak during our analysis, but, collectively, research is building a supportive case that STEM PLCs have effects valued by STEM educators and policymakers."
