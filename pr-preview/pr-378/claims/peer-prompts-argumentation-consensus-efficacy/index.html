Carolyn P. Rosé 2017¶

Carolyn P. Rosé, Sten Ludvigsen, Nancy Law, Ulrike Cress & Gerry Stahl. (2017). Divisive or facilitative: The two faces of technology in CSCL. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning, 12:215–220. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-017-9265-7

The editorial's summary of Harney and Hogan's findings: the study explored summative outcomes (perceived consensus, perceived efficacy, discomfort within the group, team orientation) plus a process analysis coding argumentation style and complexity, and explored statistical connections between process and outcome variables. The editorial reports no effect sizes or test statistics.
