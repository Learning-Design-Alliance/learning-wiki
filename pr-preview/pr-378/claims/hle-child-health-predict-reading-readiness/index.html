Evidence¶

Dilnot 2017¶

Dilnot, J., Hamilton, L., Maughan, B. and Snowling, M. (2017). Child and Environmental Risk Factors Predicting Readiness for Learning in Children at High-Risk of Dyslexia. Development & Psychopathology, 29(1). http://dx.doi.org/10.1017/S0954579416000134

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Hierarchical regression (Model B) predicting reading readiness at school entry in the longitudinal sample, entering non-significant predictors dropped. The article reports "home literacy environment and child health accounted for 11% of the variance" and family-risk a further non-significant 2.8%.

"In the model predicting reading readiness, home literacy environment and child health accounted for 11% of the variance and family3risk for a further 2.8% which was not significant."
