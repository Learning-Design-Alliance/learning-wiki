Evidence¶

Janneke van de Pol 2020¶

Janneke van de Pol, et al. (2020). Mapping and Drawing to Improve Students’ and Teachers’ Monitoring and Regulation of Students’ Learning from Text: Current Findings and Future Directions. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09560-y

q2 · i? · review · r2

Study in which secondary teachers judged student comprehension with diagrams, names, and texts available and indicated which of 28 cues they used, also judging cue manifestations. The review reports that accurately judging cue manifestations of highly diagnostic cues improved monitoring accuracy, and that non-diagnostic student cues "was found to hamper teachers’ monitoring accuracy."

"Mere use of diagnostic cues did not promote teachers ’ absolute monitoring accuracy. That is, when teachers used highly diagnostic cues, their judgments were not more accurate."
