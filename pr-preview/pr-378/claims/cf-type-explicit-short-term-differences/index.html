Evidence¶

Jang 2020¶

Jang, Sung-Soo. (2020). The efficacy of different types of metalinguistic information in L2 written corrective feedback. English Teaching, 75(4), 33-56. https://doi.org/10.15858/engtea.75.4.202012.33

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Tukey pairwise comparisons in the error correction test results of the four-session experiment with 93 low-intermediate Korean EFL learners randomly assigned to four CF groups and a control. The study reports "large" effects, "d = 1.202 for DR; d = 1.156 for MS; d = 1.204 for MN", with the MC group not outperforming controls.

"Specifically, all the experimental groups except for the MC group performed better than the CO group on the immediate posttest. Effect sizes between the CO group and the experimental groups were large (d = 1.202 for DR; d = 1.156 for MS; d = 1.204 for MN)."

Jang 2020 (2)¶

Jang, Sung-Soo. (2020). The efficacy of different types of metalinguistic information in L2 written corrective feedback. English Teaching, 75(4), 33-56. https://doi.org/10.15858/engtea.75.4.202012.33

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Factorial repeated measures ANOVA on error correction test scores with time and group as factors, reported in the Results section. The omnibus "time Ⅹ group interaction" was significant, F(8, 176) = 5.243, p < .001, η2 = .192, alongside significant main effects for group and time.

"The ANOV A showed significant main effects for group, F(4, 88) = 4.974, p = .001, η2 = .184, significant main effects for time, F(2, 176) = 68.308, p < .001, η2 = .437, and a significant time Ⅹ group interaction, F(8, 176) = 5.243, p < .001, η2 = .192."
