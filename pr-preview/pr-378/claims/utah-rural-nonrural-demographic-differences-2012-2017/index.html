Evidence¶

Rice 2018¶

Rice, J., Huang, M., & Derby, K. (2018). Characteristics of rural and non-rural districts in Utah from 2012 to 2017. San Francisco, CA: REL West @ WestEd. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/edlabs/regions/west

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Descriptive analysis of Utah State Board of Education data for 18 rural and 23 non-rural districts, 2012-2017, with percentages weighted by district enrollment. The report states rural districts had "a higher proportion of economically disadvantaged students compared to non-rural districts" and a higher disability proportion.

"Each year, r ural districts had a higher proportion of economically disadvantaged students compared to non-rural districts (e.g., 17.1 percentage point difference in 2017) and a slightly higher proportion of students with one or more disabilities compared to non-rural districts (e.g., 2.2 percentage points by 2017)."
