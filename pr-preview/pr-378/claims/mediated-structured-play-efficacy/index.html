Evidence¶

Rosalyn A. Muir · Steven J. Howard · Lisa Kervin 2023¶

Rosalyn A. Muir · Steven J. Howard · Lisa Kervin. (2023). Interventions and Approaches Targeting Early Self-Regulation or Executive Functioning in Preschools: A Systematic Review. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09740-6

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Synthesis of 16 mediated structured play studies (average sample 99.25; average dosage 17.60 h). The review reports "75.00% (n = 12)" of interventions had significant acute effects, 26 of 41 evaluated effects (63.41%) were significant, and six interventions met high-efficacy criteria. Effects were often larger for WM and CF than for IC, global EF or SR.

"Statistically significant effects acutely after intervention were reported for 75.00% (n = 12) of the interventions."
