Evidence¶

Armitage 2023¶

Armitage, KL; Suddendorf, T; Bulley, A; Bastos, APM; Taylor, AH; Redshaw, J. (2023). Creativity and Flexibility in Young Children's Use of External Cognitive Strategies. Developmental Psychology, 59 (6), pp. 995-1005. https://doi.org/10.1037/dev0001562

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Overall finding of the preregistered study (N = 72, ages 3–8, Australia): children who adopted the demonstrated marking strategy at least once were given a transfer task, and the authors conclude the results "demonstrate young children’s remarkable flexibility in the transferral of external strategies."

"These results demonstrate young children’s remarkable flexibility in the transferral of external strategies across contexts and point to pronounced individual differences in the strategies children devise."
