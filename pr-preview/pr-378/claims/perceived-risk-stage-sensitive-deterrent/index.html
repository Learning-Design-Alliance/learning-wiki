Evidence¶

Liu Y 2026¶

Liu Y, Meng M and Zhang Y (2026) The competence paradox: negotiating ease, risk, and creative identity in text-to-image generative AI use among art and design students. Front. Psychol. 17:1858187. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1858187

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Structural model of the 400-student survey: PR→UB printed β = −0.384, SE = 0.168, p = 0.022; the same sentence reports the FC→UB null (β = −0.002, p = 0.980), so H4b was rejected.

"For downstream outcomes, PR signiﬁcantly and negatively predicted UB, supporting H7, whereas FC showed no signiﬁcant direct eﬀect on UB, leading to the rejection of H4b."

Liu Y 2026 (2)¶

Liu Y, Meng M and Zhang Y (2026) The competence paradox: negotiating ease, risk, and creative identity in text-to-image generative AI use among art and design students. Front. Psychol. 17:1858187. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1858187

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Thematic analysis of the 20 follow-up interviews distinguished evaluation-oriented risk (submission legitimacy, external judgment) from development-oriented risk (weakened originality, loss of foundational skills).

"the interviews suggested that students rarely described risk as a barrier to initial experimentation; instead, it became most salient when AI-assisted work moved from exploration to submission, critique, or formal evaluation."
