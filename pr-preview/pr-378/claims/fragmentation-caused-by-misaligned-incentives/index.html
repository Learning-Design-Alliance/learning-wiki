Ronnel B. King 2024¶

Ronnel B. King, Luke K. Fryer. (2024). Hybridizing Motivational Strains: How Integrative Models Are Crucial for Advancing Motivation Science. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09850-9

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical argument in the essay's 'What Causes the Problem of Fragmentation?' section, quoting Skinner (2023). The essay adds that 'One reason behind this is that of misaligned incentives': end-users benefit from integration but researchers bear its cost. No empirical data are offered.

"fragmentation in the field is not a problem for experts; it is actually caused by experts—an inadvertent byproduct of many specialists working in multiple theoretical perspectives."
