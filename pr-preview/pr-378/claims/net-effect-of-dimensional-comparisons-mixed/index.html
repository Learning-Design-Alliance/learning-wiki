Evidence¶

Jens Möller 2024¶

Jens Möller. (2024). Ten Years of Dimensional Comparison Theory: On the Development of a Theory from Educational Psychology. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09918-6

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports an experimental study by Pohlmann and Möller (2009) testing whether downward dimensional comparison effects exceed upward ones, demonstrating some overall benefit; Möller and Marsh (2013) accordingly stated the net effect of dimensional comparisons is positive.

"they found positive self-concept effects for downward dimensional comparisons that were larger than negative effects for upward dimensional comparisons in both self-perceived competence and satisfaction with test outcomes."

Jens Möller 2024 (2)¶

Jens Möller. (2024). Ten Years of Dimensional Comparison Theory: On the Development of a Theory from Educational Psychology. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09918-6

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports three experiments and two field studies by Müller-Kalthoff et al. (2017a, 2017b) in which "positive effects of downward and negative effects of upward dimensional comparisons cancelled each other out", contradicting the positive-net-effect claim.

"revealing that positive effects of downward and negative effects of upward dimensional comparisons cancelled each other out, so that net effects were more or less negligible."
