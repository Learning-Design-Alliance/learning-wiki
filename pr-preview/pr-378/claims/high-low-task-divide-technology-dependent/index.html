Laurence Dierickx, Fredrik Bjerknes, Andreas L. Opdahl, Carl-Gustav Lindén. (2026). A Taxonomy of LLM-Supported Tasks for Critical AI Literacy in Journalism. Postdigital Science and Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s42438-026-00690-0

q1 · i? · design · r1

Theoretical argument in the task-definition section. The paper contrasts the computational-journalism convention of distinguishing "high-level tasks" from "low-level tasks" with interview findings it cites that LLMs are used across the entire journalistic value chain, and concludes the divide is technology-dependent. No new data are presented for this claim.

"the high/low-task divide is not a fixed property of the tasks themselves but rather a relationship that is influenced by the available technologies at a given time"
