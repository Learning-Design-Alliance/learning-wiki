survey-1¶

Russell, Christina A. (2023, April). Centering wellbeing: Advancing social emotional learning for all. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/177

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Survey of Melbourne convening participants (23 responses, including all nine traveling working-group districts) administered by Policy Studies Associates in December 2022. Respondents reported "82 percent of leaders" planning changes and "66 percent" already implemented; no effect size is printed.

"In survey responses less than two months after the Melbourne convening, 82 percent of leaders reported that the district was planning to make concrete changes informed by their participation, and 66 percent reported the district had already implemented changes."
