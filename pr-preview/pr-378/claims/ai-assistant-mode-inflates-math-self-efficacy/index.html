Evidence¶

Esposito 2026¶

Esposito, N., Tricarico, A., Porzio, L., Aghazadeh Ardebili, A., & Stella, M. (2026). Math Education Digital Shadows for Investigating Learning with GenAI: Mathematics Performance, Anxiety, and Confidence in LLMs. arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27618 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2604.27618

q2 · i? · design · r2

Task 2 distributional analysis of psychometric scores by prompting mode. The article reports AMAS distributions "tend to cluster towards the lower bounds of the scale" for AI assistants, and the MSEAQ self-efficacy subscale shows a pronounced rightward skew while the anxiety subscale shows a reciprocal leftward skew.

"This divergence is particularly evident in the MSES distributions, where the AI-assistant peaks are markedly shifted to the right relative to the human-shadow data, indicating systematically higher self-efficacy."
