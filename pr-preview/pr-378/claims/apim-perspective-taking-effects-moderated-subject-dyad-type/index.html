Evidence¶

Bai 2026¶

Bai, H.; Chan, L.; Luo, A.; Kroesbergen, E.H.; Christie, S. (2026). Creativity in dialogues: How do children interact with parents vs. strangers for generating creative ideas? Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102286

q3 · i? · associational · r2

APIM multilevel regressions on three-level subject data (subjects nested within stimulus objects nested within dyads) showed an actor effect of Listen/understand on originality moderated by subject and dyad type; "children of the child-stranger dyads particularly benefitted from their own Listen/understand behaviors in generating more original ideas".

"There was also an actor effect, moderated by subject type and dyad type together. As in Fig. 2(a), while the effect was neglectable for other subjects, children of the child-stranger dyads particularly benefitted from their own Listen/understand behaviors in generating more original ideas."

Bai 2026 (2)¶

Bai, H.; Chan, L.; Luo, A.; Kroesbergen, E.H.; Christie, S. (2026). Creativity in dialogues: How do children interact with parents vs. strangers for generating creative ideas? Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2025.102286

q3 · i? · causal · r2

APIM estimation showed the Extend/revise partner effect on fluency was moderated by subject type and dyad type together; per Fig. 3(a), "the general trend was that this effect was positive for children (child-parent > child-stranger) but negative for adults (child-parent < child-stranger)".

Related Claims¶

  • Child-parent dyads manifested more cognitive perspective taking than child-stranger dyads, but no group difference was found in social-emotional perspective taking — related
  • Dyads showing more Listen/understand and Extend/revise behaviour produced more original ideas regardless of dyad type, while no social-emotional perspective-taking measure was significantly associated with originality — related
  • Child-parent dyads generated more ideas and more original ideas than child-stranger dyads on the AUT, with no significant difference in appropriateness — related
