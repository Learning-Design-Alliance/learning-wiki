q3 · i? · causal · r2

Regression discontinuity estimates from the large California district administrative data for on-track-to-graduate status. The author reports the estimates as "positive but imprecise," indicating wide confidence intervals; no effect size or test statistic is printed in the available text.

"Estimates for on-track status in 10th and 11th grades were positive but imprecise."
