Evidence¶

Esther Ziegler 2021¶

Esther Ziegler, Dragan Trninic, Manu Kapur. (2021). Micro productive failure and the acquisition of algebraic procedural knowledge. Instructional Science 49, 313–336. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09544-7

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Mixed-factorial ANCOVA on the 24-item isomorphic test (Cronbach's α = 0.89), with 41 PS-I and 40 I-PS sixth-graders and mathematics grade and reasoning ability as covariates; the article reports the condition main effect "F(1,72) = 4.35, p = 0.041, η2p = 0.06".

"There was a main effect of condition on students' isomorphic problem solving in favor of the PS-I condition after controlling for mathematics grade and reasoning ability, F(1,72) = 4.35, p = 0.041, η2p = 0.06"

Esther Ziegler 2021 (2)¶

Esther Ziegler, Dragan Trninic, Manu Kapur. (2021). Micro productive failure and the acquisition of algebraic procedural knowledge. Instructional Science 49, 313–336. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-021-09544-7

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Same ANCOVA with a time factor (one-day vs one-week posttest); the article reports no time effect and no condition × time interaction, calling it "a consistent advantage of the PS-I condition in the one-day and one-week delay."

"there was no main effect of time, F(1,72) = 0.25, p = 0.619, η2p = 0.00, and also no interaction of condition × time, F(1,72) = 0.13, p = 0.715, η2p = 0.00, indicating a consistent advantage of the PS-I condition in the one-day and one-week delay."
