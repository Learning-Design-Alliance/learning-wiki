DaisyQuest 2006¶

DaisyQuest. (2006). WWC Intervention Report: DaisyQuest. What Works Clearinghouse, U.S. Department of Education. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

WWC synthesis of four randomized controlled trials totaling 223 students. The report states DaisyQuest "was found to have positive effects on alphabetics skills" and computes an improvement index averaging +23 percentile points, with a range of -18 to +45 across individual findings.
