Evidence¶

Peng Wang 2024¶

Peng Wang, Lesya Ganushchak, Camille Welie, Roel van Steensel. (2024). The Dynamic Nature of Emotions in Language Learning Context: Theory, Method, and Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09946-2

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative review synthesis attributing this identification to Dewaele and colleagues (2023a). The review reports these emotions "significantly affect learners' language outcomes and their level of engagement", with enjoyment positive and boredom and anxiety negative; no effect sizes are printed.

"Dewaele and colleagues (2023a) identified anxiety, enjoyment, and boredom as three critical emotions in language learning. These emotions, which have been increasingly studied since Dewaele and MacIntyre (2014), significantly affect learners' language outcomes and their level of engagement"
