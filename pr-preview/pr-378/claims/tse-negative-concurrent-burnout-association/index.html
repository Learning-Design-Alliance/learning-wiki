Evidence¶

Kim 2020¶

Kim, Lisa E. and Burić, Irena. (2020). Teacher self-efficacy and burnout: Determining the directions of prediction through an autoregressive cross-lagged panel model. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000424

q3 · i3 · associational · r3

Latent within-time correlations from the final cross-lagged path models with covariates. The article reports the effect size r = -.43 for TSE with exhaustion at Time 1 and r = -.56 with disengagement at Time 1, all p < .001, supporting H8.

"TSE correlated negatively at each time point with exhaustion (rT1 = -.43, p < .001; rT2 = -.33, p < .001; and rT3 = -.38, p < .001) and with disengagement (rT1 = -.56, p < .001; rT2 = -.46, p < .001; and rT3 = -.46, p < .001)"
