Evidence¶

msi-1¶

Jackson, J., Starr, J., and Weaver, D. (2024, August). A Framework for Digital Equity. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/223

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The report states that MSIs, HBCUs, and TCUs are historically lower-resourced than other public and private institutions, and that students enrolled there are less likely to use learning technologies in their coursework. No effect size is printed.

"As a result, they have stark capability deficits in new hardware, software, and digital technologies and struggle to pay for reliable broadband connections."
