Evidence¶

Bingham 1916¶

Bingham, W. V. (1916). Some Norms of Dartmouth Freshmen. The Journal of Educational Psychology, VII(3), 129-142. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22Some+Norms+of+Dartmouth+Freshmen%22+Bingham+1916

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Suggestibility testing with progressive weights and three standard normal illusions, individually administered. For the size-weight illusion the freshman was told the blocks were equal, then asked how the smaller one felt. The result is a descriptive frequency with no effect size.

"This procedure, it will be noted, tends to select those who are negatively suggestible, those who suspect the experimenter is trying to fool them, when as a matter of fact, he is entirely frank and straightforward. Eight per cent, of the group were susceptible to this form of suggestion."
