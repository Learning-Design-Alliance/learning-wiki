Evidence¶

Chassell 1921¶

Chassell, C. F., & Chassell, L. M. (1921). A survey of the three first grades of the Horace Mann School by means of psychological tests and teachers' estimates, and a statistical evaluation of the methods employed. Part II. The Journal of Educational Psychology, 12(5), 243-252. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0068285

q2 · i3 · associational · r1

Correlational comparison of each measure type with the composite of all measures, based on 60, 30, and 60 cases respectively; the printed correlations with the composite are r = .89, r = .87, and r = .85, all large effects. The article cautions the measures "are not independent, but are already contained within the composite itself", inflating these correlations.

"any one of the three types of measures which were used in the survey, that is, an individual examination or two group tests or two teachers' estimates, would approximate the same results for classification and promotion as the composite itself."
