q2 · i? · review · r3

Thematic synthesis attributing this barrier to the reviewed studies, quoting Mohamed & Laher (2012). A Ghanaian study reported that packed classrooms made it troubling to teach using any method apart from the lecture method. No effect sizes are reported.

"due to the large class sizes of 40 to 80 children, providing extra attention to learners identified as weaker or struggling or individualized teaching was “an impossible task” (Mohamed & Laher, 2012)."
