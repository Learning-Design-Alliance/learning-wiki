Jordan Gutterman 2026¶

Jordan Gutterman, Ashish Gurung, Lee Branstetter, Kenneth Koedinger, and Vincent Aleven. (2026). Cross-Subject Predictive Validity for Learning Outcomes of Delayed Start Behavior. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25308 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.25308

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Session-level regression analysis (Table 2) on the 711-student iReady sample: total time on task showed positive effects of "+.11 SD" in math (p<.001) and +.09 SD in ELA (p=.001).

"There were also effects for outcomes in other session-level metrics: total time in the DLP (Math = +.11 SD, ELA = +.09 SD) and relative delayed start accounting for classroom-level effects (Math = -.13, ELA = -.15)."
