Evidence¶

Dave Neale & Deborah Pino-Pasternak 2016¶

Dave Neale & Deborah Pino-Pasternak. (2016). A Review of Reminiscing in Early Childhood Settings and Links to Sustained Shared Thinking. Educ Psychol Rev. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-016-9376-0

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review attributes this to Taumoepeau and Reese (2013), an intervention training mothers of 19-month-olds in high elaborative style with pre- and post-test measures of language and theory of mind. Peterson et al. (1999) and Reese and Newcombe (2007) reported improved vocabulary and narrative skills after training interventions.

"It appears that use of high parental elaborative style enabled children with poor language skills at pre-test to catch up with their more able peers in terms of both language and theory of mind over the course of t he study."
