Chen 2026¶

q2 · i1 · associational · r2

Covariate-adjusted within-setting models from Results Section 2.3 of the observational corpus, where scaffolded support appeared in 31.7% of assistant turns. Associations held within both explicitly and non-explicitly framed conversations (rate ratios 1.356–1.703, all p<.001).

"scaffolded-support presence was associated with higher constructive-turn counts, with Poisson count ratios ranging from 1.569 to 2.491, and with higher odds that a conversation contained at least one constructive user turn, with logistic odds ratios ranging from 1.437 to 2.140"

Related Claims¶

  • Constructive engagement is ecologically organized: amplified by explicit learning-oriented framing, higher in coding than writing, and more visible in sustained exchanges — related
  • Coding-oriented conversations show higher cognitive and constructive engagement than writing-oriented conversations across all three corpora — related
  • Contingent tutoring held delegation at the level of an answer-on-request assistant while delivering more artifacts, one decision at a time — related
  • Adjacent-turn support–engagement coupling is state- and form-dependent: explaining predicts constructive follow-up after all prior states, while feedback's positive association concentrates after prior passive turns — related
  • After-school setting offered richer activity diversity, dialogue, and peer interaction than regular classrooms — related
  • Using generative AI as a dialogic partner for brainstorming, feedback, and co-construction can support constructive and interactive learning — a broader claim this one bears on
  • A secondary ENA adding ICAP engagement codes showed proactive interactions linked constructive engagement with adequate reasoning, while reactive dialogues emphasised procedural pairings — related
  • Interactive dialogue with the AI tool, available only in some sites, promoted deeper engagement and student agency during revision — related
  • At UofT, GATs produced a reliable end-of-course increase in Constructive engagement, with no effects on Passive, Active engagement, or MSLQ elaboration — related
  • Support form differentiates the scaffolding–engagement association: feedback and explaining align most with constructive engagement, with a task-ecology crossover between instructing and modelling — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Trained students engage in more conversational turns with the GenAI tool — related
  • Learning-oriented engagement is recurrent but selective in everyday human–LLM conversations, with cognitive engagement in 31.9% and constructive engagement in 4.9% of user turns — related
