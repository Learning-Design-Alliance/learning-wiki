Evidence¶

Linnenbrink-Garcia 2018¶

Linnenbrink-Garcia, L., Wormington, S. V., Snyder, K. E., Riggsbee, J., Perez, T., Ben-Eliyahu, A., & Hill, N. E. (2018). Multiple Pathways to Success: An Examination of Integrative Motivational Profiles Among Upper Elementary and College Students. Journal of Educational Psychology, 110(7), 1026-1048. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000245

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Latent profile analysis of mastery, performance-approach, performance-avoidance goals, perceived competence, and task value among 158 fifth graders. A "four-profile solution best fit the data"; the four profiles were labeled Moderate-High All, Intrinsic and Confident, Average All, and Very High All.

"Fit indices indicated that a four-profile solution best fit the data (see Table 2). Based on the changes in the AIC, BIC, and adjusted BIC, both a four and five profile solution were reasonable to consider."
