Evidence¶

Grace E. Bird 1917¶

Grace E. Bird. (1917). Pupils' Estimates of Teachers. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0072302

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Comparison of frequency percentages across the three surveyed groups (139 high school girls, 253 high school boys, 150 normal school students). The article reports girls ranked kindness first, boys ranked fairness most appreciated, and normal school students valued sociability more than either high school group.

"With girls, kindness ranks first. Among boys fairness is the most appreciated. This sex difference is probably due to training and experience rather than original nature. Sociability is more appreciated by normal school students than by either high school girls or boys."

Related Claims¶

  • High school and normal school pupils rank kindness, fairness, sociability, sense of humor, good temper, ability to discipline, neatness, patience, adequate preparation, and ability to impart information as the ten most important teacher qualities. — related
  • Opinions of boys, girls, and normal school students about the best teacher qualities correlate highly (r = .80 between high school girls and boys; r = .88 between normal school girls and high school girls). — related
