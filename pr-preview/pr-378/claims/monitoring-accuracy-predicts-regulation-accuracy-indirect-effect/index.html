Evidence¶

Janneke van de Pol 2021¶

Janneke van de Pol, Selia N. van den Boom-Muilenburg, Tamara van Gog. (2021). Exploring the relations between teachers' cue-utilization, monitoring and regulation of students' text learning. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-021-09268-6

q3 · i? · causal · r2

The article's findings table records hypothesis H3.1 (monitoring accuracy predicting regulation accuracy) as supported "for relative and absolute accuracy", and records an indirect effect of diagram-only versus name+diagram on absolute accuracy; multilevel mediation models in Mplus with bootstrap confidence intervals tested these paths.

"H3.1 Monitoring accuracy—> r egulation accuracy Yes, for relative and absolute accuracy Indirect effects H3.2a Diagram-only > name-only No H3.2b Diagram-only > name + diagram Yes, for absolute accuracy"

Related Claims¶

  • Relative comprehension-based regulation accuracy is higher in the diagram-only condition than in the name-only condition (direct effect) — related
  • Teachers' monitoring accuracy is higher in the name+diagram condition than in the diagram-only condition, opposite to the blinding hypothesis — related
  • Teachers' monitoring accuracy does not differ significantly between the name-only condition and either diagram condition — related
  • Pre-structured diagrams improved metacomprehension accuracy while self-assessment cues showed no differences — related
  • Adding diagram self-assessment to diagram completion does not increase diagnostic cue utilization or metacomprehension accuracy beyond diagramming alone — related
  • Drawing after learning, both from scratch and with spatial scaffolds, improves absolute monitoring accuracy (calibration) compared with a time-matched mental reactivation control condition — related
  • Regulating GA proceeds via metacognitive monitoring of adequacy and accuracy plus control through outward- and inward-oriented strategies — related
  • Diagramming conditions show no significant differences from control in regulation measures or reading comprehension test scores for causal relations — related
  • Little or no relation between absolute and relative accuracy in metacomprehension research — related
  • Giving teachers access to students' diagrams improves teachers' regulation accuracy but not their monitoring accuracy — related
  • Monitoring activities and metacomprehension accuracy serially mediated the effect of in-between versus after-study explaining on conceptual learning (Experiment 1) — related
