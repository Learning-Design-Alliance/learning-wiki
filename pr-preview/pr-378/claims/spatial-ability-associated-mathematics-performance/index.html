Evidence¶

Kelsey E. Schenck 2024¶

Kelsey E. Schenck, Mitchell J. Nathan. (2024). Navigating Spatial Ability for Mathematics Education: a Review and Roadmap. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09935-5

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative review attributing this to Sorby and Panther (2020): PISA mathematics scores were significantly positively correlated with spatial cognition test scores. The review also cites early spatial scores predicting later mathematics reasoning. No effect size is printed.

"scores on the mathematics portion of the Program for International Student Assessment (PISA) are significantly positively correlated with scores on tests of spatial cognition (Sorby & Panther, 2020)."
