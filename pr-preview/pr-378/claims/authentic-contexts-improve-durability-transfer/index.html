Evidence¶

Innwoo Park and Michael J. Hannafin 1993¶

Innwoo Park and Michael J. Hannafin. (1993). Empirically-Based Guidelines for the Design of Interactive Multimedia. Educational Technology Research and Development, Vol. 41, No. 3, pp. 63-85. http://www.jstor.org/stable/30218388

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Brown, Collins, and Duguid (1989), that "learning in contexts that are 'ecologically valid' is often more rapid, durable, and transferable," because knowledge and context are inextricably tied. It cites the Cognition and Technology Group at Vanderbilt's mathematics vignettes as systems designed to situate reasoning in authentic problems. The design implication is to anchor knowledge in realistic contexts and settings.
