Evidence¶

Ariel 2018¶

Ariel, R., & Karpicke, J. D. (2018). Improving Self-Regulated Learning With a Retrieval Practice Intervention. Journal of Experimental Psychology: Applied. https://doi.org/10.1037/xap0000133

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Experiment 1 behavioral analysis of self-regulated choices. Group means differed, t(58) = 3.52, p < .01, d = 0.92; the instructed group's mean did not differ from 3, t(29) = 0.55, p = .58, while the control group's did, "t(29) = 8.09, p < .001, d = 1.48".

"The retrieval practice instruction group recalled translations approximately three times before dropping them from the learning task, whereas the control group dropped translations on average after one correct retrieval practice trial."

Ariel 2018 (2)¶

Ariel, R., & Karpicke, J. D. (2018). Improving Self-Regulated Learning With a Retrieval Practice Intervention. Journal of Experimental Psychology: Applied. https://doi.org/10.1037/xap0000133

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Experiment 1 analysis of the proportion of items reaching criteria of 0, 1, 2, or 3+ correct retrievals before being dropped. Instructions shifted the distribution toward "recalled 3 or more times during practice"; groups did not differ for items recalled twice (d = 0.06).

"the minimal retrieval practice instructions used in our intervention decreased the number of items that were never recalled, t(58) = 2.07, p < .05, d = 0.52 [0.02, 1.05], or recalled only once, t(58) = 2.15, p < .05, d = 0.56 [0.04, 1.07], while increasing the number of items that were recalled 3 or more times during practice, t(58) = 3.49, p < .01, d = 0.91 [0.37, 1.43]"

  • Socratic participants required nearly twice as many attempts to reach the 80% mastery threshold as Full participants (d = -2.01), and 58% of the exercises of Socratic participants who abandoned the dialogue mid-way never reached the threshold — related
  • Self-regulated multiple-choice students scored lower on final tests than experimenter-controlled groups but spent less time practicing, making their one-correct criterion comparatively time-efficient. — related
  • Mental effort did not explain the testing effect: retrieval practice effort did not predict delayed test scores — related
