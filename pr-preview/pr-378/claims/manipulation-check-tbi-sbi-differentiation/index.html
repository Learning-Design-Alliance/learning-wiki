Evidence¶

Yu S 2026¶

Yu S, Lin Y and Chen C (2026) Designing for AI literacy: psychological mechanisms and self-determination. Front. Psychol. 17:1937845. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1937845

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Two-item 7-point manipulation check administered immediately after the instructional videos in the randomized experiment confirmed the intended differentiation between the TBI and SBI conditions (both t(318) > 20, p < 0.001).

"The TBI group scored signiﬁcantly higher on “inspiring critical thinking” (M = 5.82, SD = 0.91) than the SBI group (M = 3.45, SD = 1.12), t(318) = 20.76, p < 0.001. Conversely, the SBI group scored signiﬁcantly higher on “teaching speciﬁc techniques” (M = 5.95, SD = 0.87) than the TBI group (M = 3.61, SD = 1.04), t(318) = 21.83, p < 0.001."
