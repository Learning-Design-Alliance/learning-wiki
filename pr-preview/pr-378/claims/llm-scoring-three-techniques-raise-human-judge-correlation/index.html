Evidence¶

Organisciak 2026¶

Organisciak, P., & Acar, S. (2026). Know when to trust: Making AI scoring more reliable for educational assessment. Behavior Research. https://doi.org/10.3758/s13428-026-03058-1

q3 · i3 · design · r2

Evaluation of three LLM scoring improvements on Alternative Uses Test responses from over 20,000 responses by more than 2,000 participants across multiple studies, reporting "improvements in correlation with human judges (from r = 0.781 to r = 0.823)" and reduced error.

"These methods, tested in divergent thinking response scoring, each show statistically significant positive results, with improvements in correlation with human judges (from r = 0.781 to r = 0.823) and reduction in error."
