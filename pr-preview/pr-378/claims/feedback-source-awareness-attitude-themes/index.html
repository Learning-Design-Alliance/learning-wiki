Supporting self-regulated learning through generative AI feedback in online higher education: the importance of student perceptions of the source of feedback. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education, 23(16). https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-026-00592-y

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Thematic analysis of open-ended responses from 17 treatment-group students (κ = .830), yielding four themes: Awareness-Stable Perception, Unaware-but Content-Focused, Unaware-but Open to Change, and Unaware-Would Reconsider if Aware.

"Among students who were aware of the feedback source, all (n = 5) stated that their attitude toward the feedback did not change, even though they knew it was GenAI-generated"
