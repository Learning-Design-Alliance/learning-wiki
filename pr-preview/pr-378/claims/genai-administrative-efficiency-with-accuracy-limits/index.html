Wei 2026¶

Wei, Y., and Perkins, M. (2026). Generative AI and student collaboration: a scoping review of group work processes, outcomes, and risks. International Journal for Educational Integrity. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40979-026-00217-x

q3 · i? · review · r2

The review reports Lin et al.'s observation, within a randomised controlled trial, that control group students showed "higher frequencies of repetitive execution, monitoring, and planning behaviors," attributed to absent technological support; AI-supported groups coordinated more efficiently.

"Lin et al. (2024) who observed that "control group students had higher frequencies of repetitive execution, monitoring, and planning behaviors," which they attribute to the absence of technological support, resulting in "unintentional repeated trials and error behaviors.""

Wei 2026 (2)¶

Wei, Y., and Perkins, M. (2026). Generative AI and student collaboration: a scoping review of group work processes, outcomes, and risks. International Journal for Educational Integrity. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40979-026-00217-x

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports Danahy et al.'s (2024) mixed-methods study of AI-supported versus self-selected group formation, in which students judged AI-analysed strengths and weaknesses "not entirely accurate"; the authors recommend human safeguarding after initial AI administrative work.

"Students reported that these strengths and weaknesses were not entirely accurate, with a tendency toward exaggeration and hallucination by GenAI."
