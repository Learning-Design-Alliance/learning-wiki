Evidence¶

Norman 1972¶

Norman, D. A.; And Others. (1972). Adaptive Training of Manual Control: 1. Comparison of Three Adaptive Variables and Two Logic Schemes. Life Sciences, Inc. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED059585 doi:10.1037/e426822004-001

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Experimental comparison of eight independent groups of eight subjects each under different training methods on a single-axis tracking task. The report states that "manual adaptation is slightly superior to automatic adaptation" while noting this could reflect differences in performance measurement levels.

"Principal results show that manual adaptation is slightly superior to automatic adaptation, although this could be a function of the difference in performance measurement levels."
