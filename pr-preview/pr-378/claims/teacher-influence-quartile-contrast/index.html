Sebring 1995¶

Sebring, P. A., Bryk, A. S., Luppescu, S., & Thum, Y. M. (1995). Charting Reform in Prairie School: Results of Student and Teacher Surveys. Consortium on Chicago School Research. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/charting-reform-prairie-school-results-student-and-teacher-surveys

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Descriptive results from the thirteen-item Teacher Influence scale covering classroom and school policy matters. In top quartile schools teachers "appear to be highly active in local governance and improvement efforts," with about a quarter claiming extensive influence and 55 percent at least moderate influence.

"Seventy percent of the teachers in these schools indicate they have minimal or limited influence (scores of 5 or lower). While they have some say over instructional matters, they have little influence over broader school policies, such as teaching assignments, the use of discretionary funds, and hiring a principal or teachers."
