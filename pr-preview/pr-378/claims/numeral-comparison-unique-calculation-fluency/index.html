Evidence¶

Cowan 2014¶

Cowan, R., & Powell, D. (2014). The Contributions of Domain-General and Numerical Factors to Third-Grade Arithmetic Skills and Mathematical Learning Disability. Journal of Educational Psychology, 106(1), 214-229. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0034097

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Fixed-order regression analyses for basic calculation fluency in 258 third graders. Numeral comparison uniquely accounted for 14% of variance when entered with quantity enumeration alone, and remained significant in all models; quantity enumeration was never significant.

"The single digit processing factors differed: numeral comparison was a significant unique predictor whatever other factors were in the model but quantity enumeration never was: Model C-1, numeral comparison (14%, t = 6.82, p < .001) and quantity enumeration (t = 1.84, p = .067)"
