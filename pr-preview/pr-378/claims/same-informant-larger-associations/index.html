Dicke 2026¶

Dicke, T., Ferber, K., Devine, E., Collie, R., Granziera, H., Parker, P., Guo, J., Marsh, H., et al. (2026). Association of Teacher Well-Being With Student Motivation and Achievement: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10216-6

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

Exploratory moderator analysis of data source (same-source vs. multi-source reporting), coded at the effect-size level. The review reports associations were "larger when teacher and student variables were reported by the same informant", with significance only for ill-being; no effect size for the moderation is printed.

"Associations were also larger when teacher and student variables were reported by the same informant, although this difference reached conventional significance only for teacher ill-being and approached significance for teacher well-being."
