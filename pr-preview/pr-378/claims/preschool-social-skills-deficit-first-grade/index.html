Ansari 2018¶

Ansari, A. (2018). The persistence of preschool effects from early childhood through adolescence. Journal of Educational Psychology, 110(7). https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000255

q2 · i0 · associational · r3

Matched-sample path models in the ECLS-K 1998 cohort using the teacher-reported social skills subscale (9 items on self-control and interpersonal skills) showed a small kindergarten deficit with printed effect size E.S. = 0.07 SDs, below d = 0.2, that had disappeared by third grade.
