Evidence¶

Hecht 2019¶

Hecht, C. A., Harackiewicz, J. M., Priniski, S. J., Canning, E. A., Tibbetts, Y., & Hyde, J. S. (2019). Promoting Persistence in the Biological and Medical Sciences: An Expectancy-Value Approach to Intervention. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000356

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Path models added biology course grade as a predictor of both persistence outcomes, controlling for direct intervention effects. The article reports the printed percentage increases and coefficients for both outcomes.

"Grade in the first course had a strong effect on both measures of persistence: a change of one standard deviation in grade corresponded, on average, to a 10.24% increase in the predicted probability of enrolling in the second course (z = 6.71, b = 0.73, p < .001) and an 8.44% increase in the predicted probability of enrolling in a biomedical major (z = 5.08, b = 0.61, p < .001"
