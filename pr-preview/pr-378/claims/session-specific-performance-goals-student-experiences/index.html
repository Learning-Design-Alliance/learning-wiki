Evidence from two studies on perceived teaching quality and emotional experiences. Learning and Instruction. Advanced online publication. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2021.101458

Learner Variables¶

  • Achievement Emotions — outcome: instruction changes it

Related Claims¶

  • Higher education teachers' self-efficacy beliefs at semester start positively predict students' end-of-course overall rating, learning, and joy, and negatively predict boredom — related
  • Achievement goal instructions show no main effects on achievement emotions in game-based learning — related
  • Within persons, state mastery goals positively predict enjoyment and negatively predict boredom and anger in the classroom — related
  • Between persons, trait mastery goals positively predict enjoyment and negatively predict anger and boredom; performance-approach goals predict pride; performance-avoidance goals predict anxiety and shame — related
  • In Study 2, teachers' general self-efficacy replicated its positive associations with students' session ratings, and general performance-approach and work-avoidance goals predicted perceived learning — related
  • Unexpected session-level associations: task goals positively predicted joy, relational goals negatively predicted learning and boredom, and learning goals negatively predicted overall rating and positively predicted boredom — related
