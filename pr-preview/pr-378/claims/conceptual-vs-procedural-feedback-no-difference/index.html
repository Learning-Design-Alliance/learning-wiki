Evidence¶

Fynn Töllner 2026¶

Fynn Töllner, Poldi Kuhl, Michael Besser. (2026). Feedback complexities interact with prior knowledge. Learning and Instruction 105 (2026) 102413. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2026.102413

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Linear contrast in the N = 190 multilevel RCT comparing the two elaborated feedback groups; both groups' standard errors overlapped noticeably. Conceptual feedback yielded the highest raw posttest mean (12.38) but was not statistically significantly better.

"Regarding the provision of different types of elaborated feedback, the test for linear contrasts revealed no significant differences between procedural and conceptual feedback t(3.46) = 2.75, p = .361, 95% CI [ 10.75, 3.53]."
