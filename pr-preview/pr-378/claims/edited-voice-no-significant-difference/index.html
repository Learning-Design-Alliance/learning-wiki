The same logistic regression models across P1 and P2 found edited voice prompts "did not significantly differ" from text (OR𝑃1 = 0.57, p= 0.17; OR𝑃2 = 0.72, p= 0.38); confidence intervals cross 1. The authors suggest students who edit voice prompts may be as likely to succeed as text users.
