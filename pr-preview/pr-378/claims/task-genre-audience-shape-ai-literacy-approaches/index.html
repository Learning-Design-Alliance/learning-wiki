Authors' interpretive discussion of the two cases: Zhimo voluntarily sought AI for linguistic accuracy in an argumentative essay for a scientific discourse community, while Asuka was guided to experiment with AI in a creative poetry remediation requiring cultural meaning negotiation.

"First, Zhimo’s and Asuka’s distinct approaches may be shaped by the differing rhetorical objectives, genres, and audiences associated with their specific writing tasks."
