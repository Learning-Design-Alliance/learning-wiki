Theoretical argument in the article's framing section, illustrated through the vignette of Maya's silence in a history discussion. The article argues this approach "may fail to capture other constructs" and obscures complexity, dynamics, and situated contextual features. No empirical evidence is offered; this is the authors' conceptual claim.
