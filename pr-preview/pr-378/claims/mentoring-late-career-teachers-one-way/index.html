The review's synthesis of the 12 studies characterises how mentoring of late-career teachers is framed: "a one-way relationship where only the mentee learns", not mutual growth for mentor and mentee.

"However, mentoring late-career teachers is typically seen as a one-way relationship where only the mentee learns, rather than as a shared learning experience in which both mentor and mentee grow together."
