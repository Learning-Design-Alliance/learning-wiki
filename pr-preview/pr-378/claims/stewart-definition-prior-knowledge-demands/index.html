Burgos 2021¶

Burgos, M., Bueno, S., Pérez, O., & Godino, J. D. (2021). Onto-semiotic complexity of the Definite Integral. Implications for teaching and learning Calculus. REDIMAT – Journal of Research in Mathematics Education, 10(1), 4-40. https://doi.org/10.17583/redimat.2021.6778

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

Onto-semiotic analysis (type d) of the general definition in Stewart's section 5.2, decomposed into elementary practices P0–P7 grouped into epistemic configurations EC1-DG and EC2-DG (Tables 6–7), identifying the objects and interpretation, representation and definition processes each practice puts at stake.

"The subject who reads and understands the definition of definite integral (Figure 5) must mobilize a system of prior knowledge, for which he/she needs to relate the different objects involved in the practices and recognizing the role that each one plays in the definition process."
