Kearney CA 2019¶

Kearney CA, Gonzálvez C, Graczyk PA and Fornander MJ (2019) Reconciling Contemporary Approaches to School Attendance and School Absenteeism: Toward Promotion and Nimble Response, Global Policy Review and Implementation, and Future Adaptability (Part 1). Front. Psychol. 10:2222. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.02222

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review's synthesis of meta-analytic and large-scale studies of the school refusal-truancy dichotomy, reporting that "broad, extensive overlap of internalizing and externalizing symptoms" undermines the internalizing-externalizing basis of the distinction. No effect sizes are printed.
