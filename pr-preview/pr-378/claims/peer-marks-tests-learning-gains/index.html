Outcome study in which 120 marketing students peer marked multiple-choice and short essay tests; "Learning gains from peer assessment were reported by 88% of participants, and an impact on the ability to self-assess was reported by 76%" — subjective self-report measures.

"Catterall (1995) had multiple-choice and short essay tests peer marked by 120 marketing students. Learning gains from peer assessment were reported by 88% of participants, and an impact on the ability to self-assess was reported by 76%."
