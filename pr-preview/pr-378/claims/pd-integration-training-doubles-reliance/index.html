Evidence¶

The Power of Digital Learning: Integrating Digital Content. The CEO Forum School Technology and Readiness Report 2000¶

The Power of Digital Learning: Integrating Digital Content. The CEO Forum School Technology and Readiness Report, Year Three. (2000). https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED447781

q2 · i? · theoretical · r2

The report cites survey statistics on teacher training and technology reliance in its professional development section. It reports that "48 per-cent say they rely on software and the Internet to a "very great" or "moderate" extent" after 11 or more hours of integration training, which it calls almost double the untrained percentage. No effect size or test is printed.

"Of teachers who received 11 or more hours of training on integrating digital content and tools in the previous year, 48 per-cent say they rely on software and the Internet to a "very great" or "moderate" extent."
