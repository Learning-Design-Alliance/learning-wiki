The guide's methodological explanation of the cross-sectional view states it shows "term-over-term changes in student outcomes using the full sample of students who tested at each term," while noting samples may differ between terms because some schools tested in one term but not the other.

"This approach allows us to show term-over-term changes in student outcomes using the full sample of students who tested at each term."
