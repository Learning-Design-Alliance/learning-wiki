Kennedy 2026¶

Kennedy, A. I., Mejía-Rodríguez, A. M., Strietholt, R. (2026). Civic Education Outcomes After Emergency Lockdowns: International Evidence from the COVID-19 Pandemic. Preprint submitted to Learning and Instruction. https://github.com/OxCGRT/covid-policy-dataset

q2 · i? · associational · r3

Separate regression model from the same fixed-effects analysis of ICCS 2016/2022 data. The article reports longer closures "are associated with greater declines in average civic knowledge", with a coefficient slightly more negative than the stringency one, though not significantly so.

"The relationship implies that an increase of school closure duration by one standard deviation (47 days, approximately 9 school weeks) would be associated with a 0.04SD larger decrease (or 4-points on the ICCS scale) in average civic knowledge scores for a country."
