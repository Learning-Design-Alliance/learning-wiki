Evidence¶

Detlef Urhahne · Lisette Wijnia 2023¶

Detlef Urhahne · Lisette Wijnia. (2023). Theories of Motivation in Education: an Integrative Framework. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09767-9

q3 · i2 · theoretical · r3

Meta-analysis of expectancies for success reported in the expectancy-value section. It found a "moderate association of expectancies for success" with current achievement r = .34 and future achievement r = .41.

"A meta-analysis by Pinquart and Ebeling (2020) found a moderate association of expectancies for success with both current (r = .34) and future academic achievement (r = .41)."
