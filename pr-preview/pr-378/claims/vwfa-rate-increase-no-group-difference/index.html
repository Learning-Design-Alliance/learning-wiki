Evidence¶

Christodoulou JA 2014¶

Christodoulou JA, Del Tufo SN, Lymberis J, Saxler PK, Ghosh SS, et al. (2014) Brain Bases of Reading Fluency in Typical Reading and Impaired Fluency in Dyslexia. PLoS ONE 9(7): e100552. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0100552

q3 · i? · causal · r1

An a priori 10 mm sphere ROI at canonical VWFA coordinates was examined across rates and groups. The ROI showed greater activation as a function of rate (p<.0001) with no group effect and no group x rate interaction (p = .41).

"the putative visual word form area (VWFA; left fusiform gyrus) region of interest exhibited greater activation as a function of rate ( p,.0001), but there was neither an effect of group nor a group x rate interaction ( p = .41)"

  • In-scanner sentence judgment accuracy declines with faster word presentation rates, and dyslexic readers' accuracy declines disproportionately across rates — related
  • Group differences in activation are more extensive when behavioral performance is equated across groups, indicating they are not a consequence of worse performance — related
