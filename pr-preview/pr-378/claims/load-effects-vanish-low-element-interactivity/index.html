Evidence¶

Ouhao Chen · Fred Paas · John Sweller 2023¶

Ouhao Chen · Fred Paas · John Sweller. (2023). A Cognitive Load Theory Approach to Defining and Measuring Task Complexity Through Element Interactivity. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09782-w

q3 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Sweller and Chandler (1994), four experiments on learning computer programs in which element interactivity was estimated by counting interacting elements for novices. Effects were obtained with large effect sizes for high interactivity material but "disappeared entirely" for low interactivity material.

"They found that the two extraneous cognitive load effects that they were investigating, the split-attention and the redundancy effects could be readily obtained with large effect sizes for information that had a high level of element interactivity associated with intrinsic cognitive load but disappeared entirely for information for which the element interactivity associated with intrinsic cognitive load was low."
