Evidence¶

Sung Yao-Ting 2017¶

Sung Yao-Ting, Yang Je-Ming, Lee Han-Yueh. (2017). The Effects of Mobile-Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning: Meta-Analysis and Critical Synthesis. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654317704307

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Random-effects moderator analysis of teaching methods. Table 4 reports LT g = 0.527 (k = 29), jigsaw g = 0.589 (k = 6), GI g = 0.562 (k = 7), TGT g = 0.442 (k = 4), STAD g = 0.162 (k = 2); QB = 1.232 was not significant. Combined structured versus ill-structured mCSCL also did not differ significantly.

"The mCSCL studies with the teaching methods of jigsaw produced the largest ES, followed by GI. The studies with teaching methods of TGT and STAD did not yield significant ESs."
