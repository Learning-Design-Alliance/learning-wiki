Xiaojuan Ke · Kristie J. Newton 2024¶

Xiaojuan Ke · Kristie J. Newton. (2024). English learners learn from worked example comparison in algebra. Instructional Science (2024) 52:831–858. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09668-6

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Qualitative coding of explanation responses into six categories (inter-rater reliability over 85%) shown in Table 6. The article concludes "the intervention materials improved the quality of English learners’ written explanations," with blank explanations reducing substantially in the treatment group.

"the intervention condition had a greater gain in terms of fully correct explanations. In addition, at the beginning of the intervention, many students did not know how to do mathematical reasoning in writing."
