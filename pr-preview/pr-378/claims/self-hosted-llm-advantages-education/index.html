Evidence¶

Alina Deriyeva 2025¶

Alina Deriyeva, Jesper Dannath, and Benjamin Paaßen. (2025). SCRIPT: Implementing an Intelligent Tutoring System for Programming in a German University Context. Artificial Intelligence in Education (Springer). https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-99261-2_16

q1 · i? · theoretical · r?

This is the authors' design argument (type e), not a tested result: the article offers reasons but no empirical measurement for the claimed advantages of self-hosting an LLama-70b model instead of "invoking any commercial LLM interface."

"we argue that such a setup is more suited to reduce the environmental impact of ITS operation (due to a smaller model size compared to very large LLMs), enhances scientific reproducibility (because we can exactly specify the model version we are using), and decreases dependency upon commercial LLM providers."

  • In one essay-feedback system, fine-tuned models produced no usable deep-level feedback output, so only directly prompted LLMs were evaluated; the authors suggest prompting may be more effective than supervised fine-tuning for this task — related
  • Under the fixed protocol, hosted LLMs produce the largest gain on Spanish thematic classification, with thematic macro F1 rising from 0.46 (TF-IDF) to roughly 0.82-0.86 for the two hosted LLMs — related
  • Both fine-tuned models show regression toward the mean in score-wise bias, and error rises monotonically above score 3.0, with high scores hardest to predict — related
  • A LoRA-adapted open-weight Gemma-3-27B model fine-tuned on 120 essays outperforms a fine-tuned GPT-3.5 baseline on TOEFL essay scoring across all reported metrics — related
