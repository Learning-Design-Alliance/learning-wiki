Evidence¶

Florence Lespiau 2019¶

Florence Lespiau, André Tricot. (2019). Using Primary Knowledge: an Efficient Way To Motivate Students and Promote the Learning of Formal Reasoning. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-019-09482-4

q3 · i? · causal · r?

In the two experiments with high school students (n=210), the training phase was compulsory or left to learners' choice, and the article reports that "primary knowledge favored the involvement and persistence of learners in the training phase regardless of their personal characteristics". No effect sizes are printed in the available text.

"In particular, primary knowledge favored the involvement and persistence of learners in the training phase regardless of their personal characteristics unlike secondary knowledge."
