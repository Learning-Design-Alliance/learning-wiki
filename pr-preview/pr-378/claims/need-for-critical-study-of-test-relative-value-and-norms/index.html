Lewis M. Terman and Clara Harbison Town 1917¶

Lewis M. Terman and Clara Harbison Town. (1917). Communications and Discussions, The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.jstor.org

q1 · i? · theoretical · r?

A symposium communication stating a research-agenda claim: after determining relative test value, the field must 'establish norms for these tests' and decide criteria such as correlation with child success, elemental versus complex tests, and whether the ideal scale should be eclectic. No data support it; it is an expert statement of need.

"One of the greatest needs at the present time in the field of psy chological diagnosis is an extensive experimental and critical study of the relative value for purposes of practical diagnosis of the larger number of individual tests which are now available."
