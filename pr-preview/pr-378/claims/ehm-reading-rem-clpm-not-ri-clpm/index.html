Evidence¶

Herbert W. Marsh 2022¶

Herbert W. Marsh, Reinhard Pekrun, & Oliver Lüdtke. (2022). Directional Ordering of Self-Concept, School Grades, and Standardized Tests Over Five Years: New Tripartite Models Juxtaposing Within- and Between-Person Perspectives. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09662-9

q1 · i? · associational · r2

The review reports, citing Ehm et al. (2019), that RI-CLPMs showed no statistically significant cross-paths from prior ASC to subsequent achievement or the reverse for young children's reading. For math (Ehm et al., 2021), CLPM and RI-CLPM results were similar and gave partial REM support.

"For reading constructs, Ehm et al. (2019) found support for REM with CLPMs but not RI-CLPMs. Indeed, their RI-CLPMs provided no support for either skill development or self-enhancement perspectives"

  • RI-CLPMs control unmeasured time-invariant covariates better than CLPMs, but neither controls unmeasured time-varying covariates well — related
  • Reanalyses of simulated and PALMA data provide converging support for the extended reciprocal effects model, including an effect of academic self-concept on achievement — related
  • The extended REM was robust to several alternative modeling specifications proposed by Sorjonen et al. — related

  • Time-reversed analysis of simulated data suggests a decreasing rather than increasing effect of self-concept on achievement, yielding inconsistent conclusions from the same data — related
