Detect suspected mental debility in children via a four-part test battery plus medical examination¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 review, 1 associational), q1–q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

This strategy operationalizes Major's recommendation that detection of mental debility be carried out through a short, structured battery: "the chief tests being (1) mathematical problems; (2) questions involving discrimination; (3) tests of school learning, and (4) various tests by the psychoanalytic method," followed by "an elaborate outline for medical examination." The review presents this as a procedure usable beyond specialists, since the method "can be applied by the capable parent and teacher."

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A capable parent or teacher administering the tests, plus a medical examination component using Major's outline

Constraints¶

Target Learners¶

  • school-age children suspected of mental debility

Target Learning Goals¶

  • early detection of mental debility to guide placement and instruction

Affordances¶

  • Major Three Class Feeble Mindedness Taxonomy

Claims¶

  • Quality of work rather than time consumed proved the most reliable measure of intelligence across the battery [+W]
  • The test battery showed striking parallelism between growth of intelligence and age in unretarded children, with leaps at grade transitions [+W]
  • Feeble-mindedness physically represents arrested development of the entire organism and is distinct from mental disease [+W]
  • Weak-minded children are capable of being taught if proper methods are employed, but the hope of improvement diminishes with age [+W]

Related Strategies¶

  • Establish a provisional classification period of roughly 45 to 90 days to detect and correct initial EL misclassifications
  • Increase efforts to detect and treat post-deployment mental disorders, since effects may be long-lasting
  • Teachers should periodically test all pupils in a wide variety of mental and physical traits to gain intimate knowledge of individual qualities and weaknesses
