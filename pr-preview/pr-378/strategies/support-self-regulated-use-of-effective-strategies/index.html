Support learners in monitoring and regulating their own learning using effective strategies¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The review closes by drawing practical implications for situations in which learners must manage their own study. It states that the research discussed "has important implications for the increasingly common situations in which learners must effectively monitor and regulate their own learning". The strategy is to design instruction and supports that steer learners toward spacing and retrieval practice while correcting the false beliefs and metacognitive illusions that drive underuse.
