Diversify AIED: use diverse technologies, address big problems, and reuse existing resources rather than reinventing content¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

As concrete revolutionary moves, the article recommends broadening beyond desktop computers to handhelds, wearables, robotics, and mobile sensors for new interaction styles; applying educational data mining and learner modelling to open activities needing just-in-time support and personalization; and building ILEs as shells over existing resources instead of developing decontextualized content, which is labour intensive.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Building on existing resources requires bridges to sibling communities such as the Learning Sciences.

Constraints¶

  • All but one of the reviewed papers used a desktop or laptop computer and targeted formal school settings, indicating how far current practice is from this recommendation.

Target Learners¶

  • classroom learners
  • informal learners
  • workplace learners

Target Learning Goals¶

  • collaboration
  • personalized learning
  • learning in authentic contexts

Related Strategies¶

  • Advance AI-motivation research through theory-driven, pedagogically explicit, longitudinal designs covering broader motivational constructs
  • Adopt unified KPIs and rigorously assess learner diversity, recommender fairness, and long-term engagement when evaluating ITS
  • Future AI-SRL research should broaden search strategies, include educators and younger learners, address motivation, and apply longitudinal designs
  • Use data mining in education research in a principled, goal-driven way informed by domain knowledge
