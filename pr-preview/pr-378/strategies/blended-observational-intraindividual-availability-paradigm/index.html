Model assets and threats to availability by blending inter-individual self-reports with process-oriented intraindividual online measures¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article closes by proposing a research paradigm for handling complexity captured in real-life circumstances. "Assets and threats to one's availability to learn can be modeled across an ecological system that blends observation of typical self-reported inter-individual differences with the process-oriented intraindividual study of SRL afforded by online measures." These online measures include think aloud protocols, learning analytics, field observation, and self-reports, allowing researchers to observe learners typically omitted from laboratory studies.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Researchers must combine inter-individual self-report measures with process-oriented intraindividual online measures across ecological layers

Constraints¶

  • The paradigm is a proposal; the article reports no empirical implementation of it

Target Learners¶

  • All learners, including those unavailable to learn who are typically unobserved in laboratory studies

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Studying how learners establish, maintain, and lose availability to learn in daily-life circumstances

Related Strategies¶

  • Study effort regulation in real-world, personally relevant contexts over longer timeframes, including via ecological momentary assessment
  • Adopt an integrative methodological toolkit using machine learning and computational models to capture SR's multi-level, temporal, and social dynamics
  • Use multimodal data and time-sensitive analyses to capture situational motivational processes
