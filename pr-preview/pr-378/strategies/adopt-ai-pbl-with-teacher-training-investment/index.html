Adopt AI-PBL frameworks with institutional and policy-level support and teacher training investment¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The study calls for institutional and policy-level adoption of AI-PBL frameworks, investments in teacher training, and further research to ensure scalability. The authors argue that AI-supported pedagogy is essential for equitable skill acquisition and preparing students for the world of work, particularly in under-resourced contexts where access to expert facilitators and robotics kits may be limited. The strategy positions AI tools as scalable, context-aware scaffolding embedded in inquiry-driven instruction.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Institutional and policy-level adoption of AI-PBL frameworks
  • Investments in teacher training

Constraints¶

  • Teacher preparedness and infrastructure gaps may hinder equitable implementation in resource-constrained areas

Target Learners¶

  • University students in STEM courses in developing-country contexts

Target Learning Goals¶

  • Computational thinking
  • Problem-solving capabilities for future workforce needs

Affordances¶

  • Ai Pbl More Capable Peers Zpd Framework

Related Strategies¶

  • Accrediting bodies and professional organizations should provide AI policy frameworks integrating academic integrity, data ethics, and equitable access, and programs should invest in faculty development
  • Implement AI-supported STEM instruction through sustained, system-level integration of one to two months or more rather than isolated short interventions
