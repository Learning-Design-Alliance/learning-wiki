Evidence¶

Allensworth 2026¶

Allensworth, E., Gordon, A. & Young, C. (2026). Combining early grade assessments to study literacy skills: Addressing the variability in tests taken across schools and students. Journal of Research on Educational Effectiveness, 19(1), 1-26. https://consortium.uchicago.edu/publications/combining-early-grade-assessments-to-study-literacy-skills doi:10.1080/19345747.2026.2636957

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Predictive analysis linking Chicago K-2 records to third grade MAP, PARCC/IAR, and literacy grades. Individual assessments were slightly less predictive than the latent scores; MAP scores were the most predictive.

"Most of the assessments, and the combined latent scores, are highly predictive of third grade assessment scores in pre-pandemic years, with correlations ranging from 0.67 (kindergarten latent score predicting third grade PARCC/IAR score) to 0.82 (second grade latent score predicting third grade MAP score)."
