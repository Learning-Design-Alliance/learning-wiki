Bakkum 2022¶

Bakkum, M. J. et al. (2022). Teaching resources for the European Open Platform for Prescribing Education (EurOP2E)—a nominal group technique study. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00141-y

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Discussion-section comparison with an earlier overview of digital educational resources for prescribing education; the authors conclude existing resources "are restricted to local universities" and that EurOP²E should also make existing resources openly available, not only create new ones.

"such resources are restricted to local universities and not known or available to the participants. This shows how little CPT educators currently collaborate and emphasizes that EurOP 2E should not only be about creating new resources but also about making existing ones openly available."
