Jessica T. DeCuir-Gunby 2026¶

Jessica T. DeCuir-Gunby, Paul A. Schutz, Saba L. Modaressi. (2026). Racializing Motivation: A Race-Reimaged Framework of Belonging, Emoting, and Achieving Within STEM Higher Education Contexts. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10220-w

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The review attributes to Rainey et al. (2018) the finding that "racially marginalized students are less likely to report a sense of belonging in STEM education", attributing part of the disparity to social exclusion and microaggressions in STEM learning environments.

"researchers have revealed that racially marginalized students are less likely to report a sense of belonging in STEM education (Rainey et al., 2018), in part due to their experiences with social exclusion and microaggressions within STEM learning environments"
