Evidence¶

Martine Baars & Lisette Wijnia & Anique de Bruin & Fred Paas 2020¶

Martine Baars & Lisette Wijnia & Anique de Bruin & Fred Paas. (2020). The Relation Between Students' Effort and Monitoring Judgments During Learning: A Meta-analysis. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09569-3

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

Supplementary meta-regression (footnote 1) of 26 studies found monitoring accuracy score was not a significant moderator (b = .007, z = 0.60, p = .551); the article reports this as a null moderation finding.

"We examined if absolute monitoring accuracy score (0 –100%) moderated the association between effort and monitoring in a sample of 26 studies.
