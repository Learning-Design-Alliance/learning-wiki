The review reports, citing Sitzmann (2011), a meta-analysis of 65 studies finding simulation conditions beat non-game conditions on skill-based knowledge, "the practical application of knowledge." No effect size is printed.

"Sitzmann’s (2011) meta-analysis of 65 studies on simulation games found that participants in simulation conditions had significantly higher skill-based knowl-edge outcomes (practical application of knowledge) than those in non-game conditions."
