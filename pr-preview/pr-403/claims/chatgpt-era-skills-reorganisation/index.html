q1 · i? · theoretical · r1

Expert opinion contribution arguing from an analogy to digitised article access that technology-driven skills reorganisation will permeate human life; the author states "The skills required in the world powered by ChatGPT and related technologies will be different." No data are offered.

"The skill of finding and having a collection of the relevant articles is no longer in demand, but the demand for another skill, the volume of the articles a student may be expected to comprehend, emerged instead."
