Evidence¶

Demetriou 2011¶

Demetriou, A., Spanoudis, G., & Mouyi, A. (2011). Educating the Developing Mind: Towards an Overarching Paradigm. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-011-9178-3

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports developmental findings on deductive reasoning, citing Moshman 1990, 1994 and Mouyi 2008. At this stage children grasp modus ponens but fail problems requiring differentiation of logical form from content; no effect size is printed.

"This awareness appears for the first time by about the age of 5 or 6 years, when inference becomes explicit but logic is implicit."

Demetriou 2011 (2)¶

Demetriou, A., Spanoudis, G., & Mouyi, A. (2011). Educating the Developing Mind: Towards an Overarching Paradigm. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-011-9178-3

q2 · i? · theoretical · r3

The review reports that modus tollens, requiring construction and comparison of alternative models, emerges at about ages 9-10, with fallacy handling at 11-12 and explicit metalogic in adolescence. No effect size is printed.

"At the end of this phase, at the age of about 9 –10 years, children can deal with modus tollens inferences"
