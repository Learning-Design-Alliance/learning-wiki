Alharbi 2026¶

Alharbi, W., Hamid, S., Abbas, S., & Salieva, Z. (2026). Ethical Generative AI Integration in English for Academic Purposes within Higher Education: A Mixed-Methods Study. Journal of University Teaching and Learning Practice. https://doi.org/10.53761/xwb6h668

q2 · i3 · causal · r1

Quantitative pre- and post-program testing in the convergent mixed-methods study, measured with original instruments including a Scenario Rubric, Artefact Rubric, and engagement index. The article reports "significant gains in ethical awareness (d = 0.93)" and gains in digital andragogical competence, with high inter-rater reliability supporting the ratings.
