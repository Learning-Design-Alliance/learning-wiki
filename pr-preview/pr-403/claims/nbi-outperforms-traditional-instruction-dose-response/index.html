Evidence¶

Kuo M 2019¶

Kuo M, Barnes M and Jordan C (2019) Do Experiences With Nature Promote Learning? Converging Evidence of a Cause-and-Effect Relationship. Front. Psychol. 10:305. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.00305

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports a randomized controlled trial of school garden-based instruction with waitlist controls, in which "students gained more knowledge than waitlist control peers taking traditional classes"; the review adds that the more garden-based instruction, the larger the gains. Attributed to Wells et al. (2015).

"In a randomized controlled trial of school garden-based instruction involving over 3,000 students, students gained more knowledge than waitlist control peers taking traditional classes"
