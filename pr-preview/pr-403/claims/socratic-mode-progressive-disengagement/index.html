Evidence¶

Alexandre Clin Deffarges 2026¶

Alexandre Clin Deffarges, Nataliya Kosmyna, and Pattie Maes. (2026). Socrates went Nuclear: Comparing Interaction Strategies for AI systems in a Learning Context using Brain Sensing. arXiv preprint arXiv:2609.00584. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00584 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2609.00584

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Behavioral and neurophysiological analysis of the Socratic condition during the training phase. Mode 2 participants exchanged far more messages overall (M=119.2, SD=50.2) than Mode 1 (M=37.3, SD=19.8), but the per-question message count declined after Q5 while EEG engagement fell to its lowest point at Q10. The authors state the two independent measures indicate participants who expected direct answers progressively abandoned the chatbot.

"This behavioral disengagement in Mode 2 converges with the EEG data: the Socratic mode's EEG engagement drops to its lowest point at Q10 (𝐸norm = 0.297, well below the 0.5 baseline)."
