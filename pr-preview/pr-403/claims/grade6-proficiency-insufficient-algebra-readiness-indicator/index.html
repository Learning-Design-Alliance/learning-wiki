Evidence¶

Chun-Wei Huang 2016¶

Chun-Wei Huang, Jason Snipes, Neal Finkelstein, WestEd. (2016). Using assessment data to guide math course placement of California middle school students. SREE Spring 2016 Conference. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED567123

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Cross-tabulation of grade 6 math CST scale scores against grade 8 algebra I CST proficiency for the 2,579-student analytic sample identified the scale score associated with at least a 50 percent probability of proficiency; "Students who scored 350 had only a 39 percent probability of achieving proficiency."

"Students who scored 350 had only a 39 percent probability of achieving proficiency. Raising the probability to 50 percent required a scale score of 367 or higher on the grade 6 math CST (0.27 standard deviation above the proficiency threshold)."
