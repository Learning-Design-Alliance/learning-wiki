Evidence¶

Blachman 1994¶

Blachman, B.A., Ball, E.W., Black, R.S. & Tangel, D.M. (1994). Kindergarten teachers develop phoneme awareness in low-income, inner-city classrooms. Read Writ 6, 1–18. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01027275

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Baseline comparison of the 84 treatment and 75 control children in inner-city schools in an urban district in upstate New York. The article reports the groups "did not differ" on the listed background and preliteracy measures, establishing pretest equivalence for the intervention comparison.

"Prior to the intervention, the 84 treatment children and 75 control children, who attended inner-city schools in an urban district in upstate New York, did not differ on age, sex, race, SES, PPVT-R score, phoneme segmentation, letter name knowledge, letter sound knowledge, or reading."
