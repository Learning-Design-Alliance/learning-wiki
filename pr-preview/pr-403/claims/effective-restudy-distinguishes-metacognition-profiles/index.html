Evidence¶

Mariëtte van Loon 2024¶

Mariëtte van Loon, Claudia M. Roebers. (2024). Development of metacognitive monitoring and control skills in elementary school: a latent profile approach. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-024-09400-2

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Authors' interpretation across the profile solutions: deficient profiles (e.g., at-risk and ineffective restudy profiles, with effective restudy means of 0.00) contrasted with high-metacognition profiles characterized by highly effective restudy selections. The authors note that in self-regulated learning settings such strategic decisions would most likely lead to improved learning scores.

"The most pronounced difference between the profiles seems to be the extent to which children made effective restudy selections. For children in profiles showing metacognitive deficiencies, most children decided to restudy very few not-learned items; often, they did not restudy anything at all."
