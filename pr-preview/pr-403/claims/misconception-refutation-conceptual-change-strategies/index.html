Evidence¶

Kendeou 2007¶

Kendeou, P., & van den Broek, P. (2007). The effects of prior knowledge and text structure on comprehension processes during reading of scientific texts. Memory & Cognition, 35(7), 1567-1577. https://doi.org/10.3758/bf03193491

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Think-aloud experiment with 86 University of Minnesota undergraduates (6 excluded), classified via the Force Concept Inventory into misconception and nonmisconception groups, reading refutation and nonrefutation physics texts. The interaction of prior knowledge and text structure on conceptual change strategies was significant (η² = .42), with post hoc differences only in the refutation text.

"This main effect was qualified by an interaction between prior knowledge and text structure [F(1,71) 50.8, p .0001, h2 .42]. Post hoc comparisons showed that the misconception group included more conceptual change strategies than did the nonmisconception group, but only during their reading of the refutation text"
