Evidence¶

Blair 2024¶

Blair, K. P., Banes, L. C., & Martin, L. (2024). Fostering noticing of classroom discussion features through analysis of contrasting cases. Instructional Science, 52:417–452. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-024-09661-z

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Study 1 exploratory result on the valence dimension of teacher-focused codes, explicitly reported as nominal and non-significant; equivalence was not tested.

"Nominally, there was an interaction within the teacher-focused codes, such that the number of codes noting the presence of a feature in the teacher’s behavior decreased while those noting absence of a feature or missed opportunity increased, though this interaction was not significant."
