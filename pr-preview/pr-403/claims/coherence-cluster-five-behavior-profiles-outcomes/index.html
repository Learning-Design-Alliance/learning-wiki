Evidence¶

Gautam Biswas 2016¶

Gautam Biswas, James R. Segedy, Kritya Bunchongchit. (2016). From Design to Implementation to Practice: a Learning by Teaching System: Betty's Brain. International Journal of Artificial Intelligence in Education. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0057-9

q2 · i? · associational · r1

In the classroom study applying coherence analysis (Segedy et al. 2015, described in this paper), clustering the 98 students' six CA measures revealed five profiles; "engaged and efficient" students significantly outperformed all other clusters on science gains. No effect sizes are printed.

"Cluster 5 students, who were characterized as engaged and efficient, had higher reading scores on skill tests and exhibited significantly higher science gains when compared to all other clusters."
