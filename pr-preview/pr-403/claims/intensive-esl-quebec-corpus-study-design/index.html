Evidence¶

Patsy M. Lightbown and Nina Spada 1990¶

Patsy M. Lightbown and Nina Spada. (1990). Focus-on-Form and Corrective Feedback in Communicative Language Teaching: Effects on Second Language Learning. Studies in Second Language Acquisition, Volume 12, Issue 4. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0272263100009517

q2 · i? · associational · r?

Descriptive account of the study's design as printed: oral English development of "approximately 100 second language learners (four intact classes)", native French speakers aged 10–12, after a 5-month intensive ESL course in grade 5 or 6 in Quebec. A large corpus of classroom observation data was also analyzed.

"The developing oral English of approximately 100 second language learners (four intact classes) was examined in this study. The learners were native speakers of French (aged 10–12 years) who had received a 5-month intensive ESL course in either grade 5 or grade 6 in elementary schools in Quebec."
