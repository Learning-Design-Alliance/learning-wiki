Subgroup latent profile analyses compared teachers with prior AI literacy experience (N = 186; three profiles) and without (N = 102; three profiles). The comparison showed "overestimation ... appeared only among teachers with prior AI literacy experience, whereas underestimation emerged in both groups"; the low–low profile (n = 30) was unique to the inexperienced group.

"When comparing the two subgroups, overestimation (high self-reports paired with lower performance) appeared only among teachers with prior AI literacy experience, whereas underestimation emerged in both groups"
