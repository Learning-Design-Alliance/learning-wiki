Mohamed A. Ahmed 2014¶

Learning Disabilities in Different Types of Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorders and its Relation to Cortical and Brainstem Function. J Neurol Res. 2014;4(1):22-30. https://doi.org/10.14740/jnr268w

q2 · i3 · associational · r2

Pearson correlations in the 40 ADHD patients between Myklebust learning disability scores and neurophysiologic parameters (Table 5): wave III r = -0.412 (P = 0.008), I-III interval r = -0.474 (P = 0.002), I-V r = -0.464 (P = 0.003), epileptiform activity r = -0.313 (P = 0.049).

"Regarding neurophysiologic parameters, learning disabilities had significant negative correlation to absolute latency of wave III, IV , V , interpeak latency between wave I-III and I-V interval."
