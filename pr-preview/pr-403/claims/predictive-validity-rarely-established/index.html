Laura A. Outhwaite 2024¶

Laura A. Outhwaite, Pirjo Aunio, Jaimie Ka Yu Leung, Jo Van Herwegen. (2024). Measuring Mathematical Skills in Early Childhood: a Systematic Review of the Psychometric Properties of Early Maths Assessments and Screeners. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09950-6

q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r2

Synthesis of predictive validity evidence for screeners against the correlation threshold ≥ .60 (Table 1), over follow-up periods of 10 weeks to 3 years. The review reports only the NSS screener met the threshold; all others were rated mixed (n = 1) or low (n = 7).

"Predictive validity was considered in nine screeners, over periods ranging from 10 weeks to 3 years. However, only one screener had acceptable predictive validity (NSS; Jordan et al., 2012)."
