Fostering inclusion in EEG measures of pediatric brain activity 2024¶

Fostering inclusion in EEG measures of pediatric brain activity. (2024). npj Science of Learning 9, 27. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00240-y

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review’s central argument, grounded in the authors’ experience with over 2000 pediatric EEGs: hair-based exclusion “constitutes unintentional systemic racism, by disproportionately excluding participants from certain racial and ethnic groups from participation and representation in neuroscience research.”

"This ultimately constitutes unintentional systemic racism, by disproportionately excluding participants from certain racial and ethnic groups from participation and representation in neuroscience research"

Fostering inclusion in EEG measures of pediatric brain activity 2024 (2)¶

Fostering inclusion in EEG measures of pediatric brain activity. (2024). npj Science of Learning 9, 27. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00240-y

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review states that “such exclusion leads to reductions in the diversity of the participants from which we acquire EEG data and limits the generalizability of research findings,” and that this may consequently misinform interventions aimed at reducing educational inequalities.

"Such exclusion leads to reductions in the diversity of the participants from which we acquire EEG data and limits the generalizability of research findings"
