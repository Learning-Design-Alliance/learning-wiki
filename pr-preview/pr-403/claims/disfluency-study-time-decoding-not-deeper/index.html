Tim Kühl & Alexander Eitel 2016¶

Tim Kühl & Alexander Eitel. (2016). Effects of disfluency on cognitive and metacognitive processes and outcomes. Metacognition and Learning, 11, 1–13. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-016-9154-x

q2 · i? · review · r2

Editorial synthesis citing Eitel and Kühl (2015) and Rummer et al. (2015, Exp. 1): disfluency lengthened study time but did not raise performance or mental effort. No effect size is printed.

"Although more disfluent materials increased study times (e.g., Eitel and Kühl 2015; Rummer et al. 2015, Exp. 1), overall performance or the mental effort invested studying the contents was not higher"
