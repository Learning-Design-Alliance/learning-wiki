Evidence¶

Vidal 2025¶

Vidal, V., Pretel, M. R., Capurro, L., Tassone, L. M., Moyano, M. D., Malacari, R. G., Brusco, L. I., Ballarini, F. M. & Forcato, C. (2025). Short naps improve subsequent learning in a high school setting. npj Science of Learning 10:15. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00307-4

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Pearson correlation analysis within the napping group (n = 26) between NREM frequency-band power densities (SWA 0.5–4 Hz, slow oscillation, delta) and multiple-choice performance. The article reports "no signiﬁcant correlation"; equivalence was not tested and no effect size for the null is printed.

"we did notﬁnd a signiﬁcant correlation between the SWA power density and performance in the testing session (r = −0.03, p = 0.876). Neither between slow oscillations nor delta power density"
