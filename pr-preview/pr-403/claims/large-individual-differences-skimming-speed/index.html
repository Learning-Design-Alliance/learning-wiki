Evidence¶

Whipple 1914¶

Whipple, G. M., & Curtis, J. N. (1914). Preliminary Investigation of Skimming in Reading. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://eric.ed.gov/?q=preliminary+investigation+of+skimming+in+reading

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Main experiments, in which six observers skimmed twelve selections from Mangold's Child Problems with per-word timing (Table 7). The article cites the extremes: "Gst skims at nearly three times the pace of Gou"; Gou showed no noticeable speed increase when directed to skim.

"Under instructions to skim there remain marked individual differences in the rate; thus, to cite extremes, Gst skims at nearly three times the pace of Gou"
