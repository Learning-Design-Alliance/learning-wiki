Evidence¶

Jing Teng 2025¶

Jing Teng, Xinuo Qiao, Kelong Lu, Tuo Liu, Xinyue Wang, Zhenni Gao, Tingting Yu & Ning Hao (2025). Neural mechanisms underpinning the association between visual arts education and creativity. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00388-1

q2 · i2 · associational · r2

Weighted partial correlations (FDR-corrected) between differentially activated brain regions and creativity task performance, computed separately in each matched group. The quote reports the product drawing stage correlation (r = 0.361 in the non-design group); parallel significant correlations held for originality (r = 0.353) and aesthetic scores (r = 0.418) in the non-design group only.

"during the product drawing stage, CH78 activation was significantly correlated with the product imagination score in the non -designer group (r = 0.361, pcorr = 0.040); No significant correlation was observed in the designer group ( r = 0.097, pcorr = 0.630)"
