Evidence¶

Na'ilah Suad Nasir 2008¶

Na'ilah Suad Nasir, Victoria Hand and Edd V. Taylor. (2008). Culture and Mathematics in School: Boundaries between "Cultural" and "Domain" Knowledge in the Mathematics Classroom and Beyond. Review of Research in Education, Vol. 32. https://doi.org/10.3102/0091732X07308962

q2 · i? · review · r2

Study of middle and high school basketball players (Nasir, 1996, 2000) who solved average and percentage problems in two formats: basketball-framed tasks and typical school math worksheet tasks. The article reports players "were better able to solve the problems in the context of basketball," with invented strategies on basketball problems and algorithms, often misapplied, on school problems. No effect size is printed.

"Overall, players were better able to solve the problems in the context of basketball, and they used quite different strategies across the two contexts."
