Evidence¶

Kostopoulos 2025¶

Kostopoulos, G., Gkamas, V., Rigou, M., & Kotsiantis, S. (2025). Agentic AI in Education: State of the Art and Future Directions. IEEE Access. https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2025.3620473

q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative review synthesis: the authors' evidence map (Table 3) consolidates studies into five agentic roles across HE, VET and K-12, aligning each role with evaluation domain, outcome metrics and technology readiness level. The review reports that "quasi-experimental and randomized controlled designs report measurable improvements" in these two areas.

"The strongest empirical grounding appears in formative tutoring and self-regulated learning (SRL) coaching, where quasi-experimental and randomized controlled designs report measurable improvements in feedback accuracy and strategy use."
