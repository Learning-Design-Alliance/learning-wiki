Evidence¶

Korochkina 2025¶

Korochkina, M. & Rastle, K. (2025). Morphology in children's books, and what it means for learning. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00313-6

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Corpus comparison of CYP-LEX age bands against SUBTLEX-UK (nine BBC channels over three years) and its child channel CBBC, using television language as a proxy for spoken language. The comparison showed a substantial number of book-only words, and "the majority of these 'new' words are morphologically complex" (e.g., 73% missing from SUBTLEX-UK at 7–9).

"these numbers indicate that children may encounter many words in books that are not present in their spoken language environment. Moreover, this analysis revealed that the majority of these 'new' words are morphologically complex."
