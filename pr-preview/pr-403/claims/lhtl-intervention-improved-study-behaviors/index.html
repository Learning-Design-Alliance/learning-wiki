Evidence¶

Barbara A. Oakley and Terrence J. Sejnowski 2019¶

Barbara A. Oakley and Terrence J. Sejnowski. (2019). What we learned from creating one of the world's most popular MOOCs. npj Science of Learning 4:7. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0046-0

q2 · i? · design · r1

The article cites a first study of LHTL's effectiveness, quoting its report that the experimental group changed study behaviors and that online study behavior verified questionnaire answers. No effect sizes are printed in the quoted passage.

"after the LHTL intervention, "… students of the experimental group reported cramming less, using more learning techniques, redoing exercises with answers covered, and spacing repetitions more often instead of massing."
