David Blazar 2015¶

David Blazar, Matthew A. Kraft. (2015). Teacher and Teaching Effects on Students' Academic Behaviors and Mindsets. Working Paper 41. Cambridge, MA: Mathematica Policy Research. https://www.mathematica.org/publications/teacher-and-teaching-effects-on-students-academic-behaviors-and-mindsets

q3 · i? · associational · r?

The paper's summary reports a dissociation between teacher effects on non-tested outcomes and on math test scores: "teachers who are effective at improving these outcomes often are not the same as those who raise math test scores." No correlation or effect size is printed on the page.
