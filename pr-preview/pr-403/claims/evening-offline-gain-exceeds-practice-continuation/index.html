Evidence¶

Charlène Truong 2023¶

Charlène Truong, Célia Ruffino, Jérémie Gaveau, Olivier White, Pauline M. Hilt and Charalambos Papaxanthis. (2023). Time of day and sleep effects on motor acquisition and consolidation. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-023-00176-9

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Extrapolation analysis in the main experiment: individual power-function fits were extended to two additional predicted trials (G8pred), and an independent-samples t-test showed the actual 24 h gain "was signiﬁcantly different from the T2_T3 pred gain", indicating offline learning beyond practice continuation.

"the T2_T3 gain of G8 pm was signiﬁcantly different from the T2_T3 pred gain ( t = 2.21, p = 0.04, d = 0.90;"
