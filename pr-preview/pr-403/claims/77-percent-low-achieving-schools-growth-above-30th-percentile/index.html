Andy Hegedus 2018¶

Andy Hegedus, Ed.D. (2018). Evaluating the Relationships between Poverty and School Performance. NWEA Research Report. https://www.nwea.org/research-data-galleries/poverty-and-school-performance

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Observational analysis of the bottom 5% of schools by spring reading achievement in the MAP Growth school sample. The article reports "77% of the lowest 5% achieving schools based on spring reading achievement grow their typical student better than the 30th percentile (about 0.5 standard deviations below the mean)," suggesting most schools labeled low-performing grow students at or better than slightly below average.

Related Claims¶

  • Achievement of the lowest-growth schools varies dramatically, and fall achievement accounts for about 80% of spring achievement versus 14% for growth — related
  • MAP Growth cut scores at the 30th percentile of national norms best identify Grade K-8 students in need of intensive intervention in reading and mathematics — related
  • Lowest-income schools show more variable growth than highest-income schools, with more schools both below the 50th and above the 82nd growth percentile — related
  • About 46% of the lowest 5% achieving schools show the same growth as the highest 5% achieving schools — related
  • Massachusetts students overall perform above the national average on screening assessments, with a median national percentile of 58 at EOY and 66 percent growing at average or above-average rates — related
