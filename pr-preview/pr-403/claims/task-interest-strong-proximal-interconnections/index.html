Fryer 2021¶

Fryer, L. K., Shum, A., Lee, A., & Lau, P. (2021). Mapping students' interest in a new domain: Connecting prior knowledge, interest and self-efficacy with interesting tasks and a lasting desire to reengage. Learning and Instruction. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2021.101493

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Longitudinal-SEM results for the 310-student course study. The article reports "Task interest was strongly connected, particularly with the interest in the subsequent two tasks", with large βs in the discussion; no single effect size is printed in the quoted sentence.

"Consistent with H3, Task interest was strongly connected, particularly with the interest in the subsequent two tasks (H3) and strongly connected to Course interest (see Figure 2; H4)."
