Evidence¶

Emely Hoch 2023¶

Emely Hoch, Yael Sidi, Rakefet Ackerman, Vincent Hoogerheide, Katharina Scheiter. (2023). Comparing Mental Effort, Difficulty, and Confidence Appraisals in Problem-Solving: A Metacognitive Perspective. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09779-5

q3 · i3 · causal · r2

Online experiment with 284 Prolific participants solving 17 CRT items, randomly assigned to goal-driven effort, data-driven effort, difficulty, or confidence appraisal groups. ANOVA on within-participant appraisal-response time correlations showed a "significant large effect of appraisal type" with η2=0.15, confidence weaker than all three load-related appraisals (all ps < 0.001).

"There was a significant large effect of appraisal type, F(3, 280) = 16.93, MSE = 0.09, p< 0.001, η2=0.15. Post hoc pairwise comparisons (t-tests with Bonferroni adjustment for p-values) showed that the correlation with response time was significantly weaker for confidence than for each of the other three appraisal types"
