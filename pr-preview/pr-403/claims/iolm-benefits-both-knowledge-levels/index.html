Evidence¶

Vania Dimitrova & Paul Brna 2016¶

Vania Dimitrova & Paul Brna. (2016). From Interactive Open Learner Modelling to Intelligent Mentoring: STyLE-OLM and Beyond. Int J Artif Intell Educ 26:332–349. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-015-0087-3

q2 · i? · qualitative · r1

Discourse analysis of interaction transcripts from the same LARFLAST user study coded reflection as learners rendering statements about beliefs, changing beliefs, and developing arguments to justify beliefs. The article reports the differential pattern for the two learner groups; no effect size is printed.

"The study showed that STyLE-OLM would be beneficial both for more knowledgeable and less knowledgeable learners - the former were engaged in reflective interactions about the domain, while the later were provoked to inspect their models and challenge the robustness of these models."
