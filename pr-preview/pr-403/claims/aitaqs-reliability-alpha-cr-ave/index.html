Evidence¶

Zhang 2026¶

Zhang, Z., Katayoun, M., Tan, L., & Wei, G. (2026). Development and Validation of a Multidimensional Scale for AI Tutoring Acceptance in Higher Education. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00431-9

q3 · i? · design · r2

Internal consistency analysis (Table 8) of the four AITAQS subscales. All α values "remain within the range of 0.721 to 0.745", indicating acceptable internal consistency per the 0.70 criterion cited.

"the α coefficients of the four dimensions of feedback quality, instructional effectiveness, interaction experience, and user trust all remain within the range of 0.721 to 0.745, and all are higher than the recommended critical values."

Zhang 2026 (2)¶

Zhang, Z., Katayoun, M., Tan, L., & Wei, G. (2026). Development and Validation of a Multidimensional Scale for AI Tutoring Acceptance in Higher Education. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00431-9

q3 · i? · design · r2

Convergent validity analysis (Table 9). All CR values "exceed 0.7"; AVE values below 0.5 are judged acceptable given the loadings, with the authors noting future item refinement may further improve AVE values.

"the CR values of all items exceed 0.7 and all factor loadings exceeded 0.5. Therefore, even if the AVE value is lower than 0.5, this item is still within the acceptable range"
