Evidence¶

Aakriti Kumar 2022¶

Aakriti Kumar, Aaron S. Benjamin, Andrew Heathcote and Mark Steyvers. (2022). Comparing models of learning and relearning in large-scale cognitive training data sets. npj Science of Learning 7:24. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-022-00142-x

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Retention analysis of the Lumosity data (Fig. 6) comparing first and second gameplays after retention intervals; in Lost in Migration, after a loss of nearly 100% of prior performance at 800 days, "a single gameplay restores performance to the same level". The article states relearning benefits of this magnitude over these timescales had not previously been documented.

"The dramatic finding here is that those losses are quickly offset by a very small amount of practice. This effect can be seen in the forgetting function for the second gameplay within the session, which is flat or nearly so."
