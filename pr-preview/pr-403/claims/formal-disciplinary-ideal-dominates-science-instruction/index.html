Evidence¶

J. C. B 1915¶

J. C. B. (1915). Editorial. The Journal of Educational Psychology, Volume VI, Number 10. Warwick & York. https://archive.org

q1 · i? · theoretical · r?

Editorial interpretation of why science teaching is misdirected, offered without data. The author states that "the observational study of nature has been decried and flouted as unscientific" and that little has been done to improve elementary science.

"the formal disciplinary idea of science as developing accuracy of method in thinking has come to be the lode star of science instruction."
