Conway 2025¶

Conway, C. M., Jenkins, H. E., Milne, A. E., Singh, S. & Wilson, B. (2025). Addressing the theory crisis in statistical learning research. npj Science of Learning 10, 68. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00359-6

q2 · i? · review · r3

Narrative review of comparative modality studies within this Perspective. The auditory advantage for serial presentation is described as consistent evidence supporting the theory that statistical learning exists across modalities with modality-specific constraints; the advantage vanishes with simultaneous visual presentation, and transfer across modalities is limited.

"performance in the auditory modality is typically superior to the visual modality for the serial presentation of materials"
