Evidence¶

Bidzan-Bluma Ilona 2018¶

Bidzan-Bluma Ilona, Lipowska Małgorzata. (2018). Physical Activity and Cognitive Functioning of Children: A Systematic Review. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph15040800

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review's synthesis of its 58 included studies states that interventions "are effective" with only two studies not confirming the positive influence, and that the relationship is "stronger, and the effect size is larger" for interventions. No numeric effect size is printed.

"Notably, almost all studies indicate that the interventions (of various kinds, e.g., a short physical activity break, aerobic exercise, or afterschool physical activity program) are effective (with the exception of two studies, which do not confirm this positive influence of physical activity on cognitive functioning [31,32]), which is implied by the fact that the relationship between the physical activity and cognitive functioning (independently of its dimension) is stronger, and the effect size is larger."
