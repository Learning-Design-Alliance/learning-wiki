Evidence¶

Ping Li and Hyeonjeong Jeong 2020¶

Ping Li and Hyeonjeong Jeong. (2020). The social brain of language: grounding second language learning in social interaction. npj Science of Learning 5:8. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-020-0068-7

q2 · i? · review · r1

The review attributes this to Lan et al. and Hsiao et al., who trained American students on Mandarin vocabulary through Second Life. Low achievers followed a fixed nearest-neighbor route while high achievers explored; roaming entropy, quantifying movement trajectory variability, was higher in better learners.

"the virtual learners needed only about half of the number of exposures to gain the same level of performance as learners through traditional associative learning"
