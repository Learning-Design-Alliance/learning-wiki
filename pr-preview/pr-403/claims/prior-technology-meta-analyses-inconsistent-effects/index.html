Evidence¶

Dorris 2024¶

Dorris, C., Winter, K., O'Hare, L., & Lwoga, E. T. (2024). A systematic review of mobile device use in the primary school classroom and impact on pupil literacy and numeracy attainment: a systematic review. Campbell Systematic Reviews, 20, e1417. https://doi.org/10.1002/cl2.1417

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

The review reports, citing Talan (2020), a meta-analysis of mobile learning across subjects and grades finding "an effect size of −0.015 for maths interventions". The review attributes this second-hand finding and concludes the findings reveal a lack of consistency.

"Talan (2020) undertook meta‐analysis of studies using mobile learning across all subjects and grade levels and found an effect size of −0.015 for maths interventions, representing the smallest impact across all subjects. The findings therefore reveal a lack of consistency."
