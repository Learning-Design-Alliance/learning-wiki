Evidence¶

Orna Heaysman 2026¶

Orna Heaysman, Bracha Kramarski. (2026). Authentic evidence from the 'SRL-AIDE' simulative environment on explicitness and duration of in-class SRL teaching practices. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-026-09787-2

q2 · i3 · causal · r2

Cohen's d comparisons of pre-post explicitness gains in the quasi-experimental video study. The SRL-AIDE group's MCS gain was "d = 2.08" versus KC "d = 1.03"; its KC gain (d = 1.03) exceeded the comparison group's d = 0.31.

"The results indicated a higher effect size for the difference in the MCS explicitness than for the KC explicitness [d = 2.08 and d = 1.03, respectively]. Moreover, both groups increased their scores significantly in the KC explicitness levels. Comparing the effect size of the increase between the two time points using Cohen's d formula indicated a higher effect size for the SRL-AIDE group than for the comparison group [d = 1.03 and d = 0.31, respectively"
