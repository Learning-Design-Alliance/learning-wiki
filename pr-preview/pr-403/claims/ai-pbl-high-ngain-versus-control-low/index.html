Ayanwale 2026¶

Ayanwale, M. A., & Omeh, C. B. (2026). AI-supported problem-based learning for enhancing computational thinking skills in STEM education. Computers in Human Behavior: Artificial Humans 7 (2026) 100263. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chbah.2026.100263

q2 · i2 · causal · r2

Supplementary normalized gain (N-Gain) analysis, computed with Hake's formula and interpreted with Hake's classification (g ≥ 0.7 = high), complementing the ANCOVA in the same quasi-experiment; independent samples t-tests confirmed "statistically significant (p < .001)" differences.

"Table 4 shows that students in the AI-PBL group achieved high N- Gain values (g = 0.73) in both computational thinking and academic achievement, indicating substantial learning improvement. In contrast, the control group recorded low N-Gain scores (g ≤ 0.20)."
