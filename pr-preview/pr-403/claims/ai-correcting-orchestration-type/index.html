Qualitative case analysis of Snorkl episodes. Errors arose mainly when the AI failed to recognize student graphs or produced feedback inconsistent with curriculum context; the article reports the type was observed 10 times among 46 episodes, with the teacher sometimes re-assigning scores by curriculum criteria.
