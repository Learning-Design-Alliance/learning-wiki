Evidence¶

Man Su 2025¶

Man Su, Belle Dang, Andy Nguyen & Tomohiro Nagashima. (2025). Choice-making in an adaptive learning system with motivational pedagogical agents. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00366-7

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Process mining (Disco tool, 100% of activities) of logged interaction pathways for the HPK and LPK groups, complementing the LSA. The LPK group's higher proportion of correct decisions, its early engagement with preparatory tasks, and reduced later willingness to take advanced tasks are reported as descriptive pathway frequencies.

"Interestingly, the LPK group demonstrated a higher proportion of CorrectDecision outcomes compared to the HPK group (f Low = 70.8%; f High = 56%)."
