Jingwei Yi, Yueqi Xie, Jiyan He, Rui Ye, Junming Huang, Bin Zhu, Sean Rintel, Yu Xie, Xing Xie, and Fangzhao Wu. (2026). Learning behavior accounts for background-related advantage in AI-assisted education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10101 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.10101

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Nested OLS models of exam score within the experimental group (background-only vs behavior-augmented) in the Python course; the article reports coefficients falling "from 2.235 to 1.552" and "from 2.753 to 1.881" with both adjusted associations non-significant.

"In Python, the university ranking coefficient decreases from 2.235 to 1.552 and the prior knowledge coefficient decreases from 2.753 to 1.881, and both behavior-adjusted learner profile associations become non-significant. Learning behavior accounts for 30.5% and 31.7% of the total associations, respectively."
