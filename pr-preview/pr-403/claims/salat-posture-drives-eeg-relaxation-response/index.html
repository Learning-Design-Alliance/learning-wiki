Husain et al. 2026¶

Husain, W., Ammar, A., Wusqa, U. T., Farooq, M., Tariq, Z., Pandi-Perumal, S. R., BaHammam, A. S., Trabelsi, K., Griffiths, M. D., Pakpour, A., & Jahrami, H. (2026). q2 · i? · review · r2

Narrative review synthesis of Salat EEG studies; the review reports, citing Doufesh et al. 2012, that alpha augmentation "was equivalent between actual prayer and mimicked conditions". No effect size printed.

"Alpha augmentation in parieto‐occipital regions during prostration was equivalent between actual prayer and mimicked conditions, indicating that posture rather than concurrent recitation principally drives EEG relaxation responses"
