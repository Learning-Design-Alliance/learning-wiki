(2026). “I didn't want physical education days to come”: Emotional experiences in physical education of prospective primary education teachers. Learning and Instruction 104 (2026) 102382. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2026.102382

q2 · i? · qualitative · r3

Authors' interpretation of the thematic comparison across the two educational stages in the narrative corpus, discussed as “an increasingly adverse emotional trajectory over time” shaped by pedagogical and interactional conditions.

"Participants’ narratives revealed a clear and progressive shift from predominantly positive emotions in CPE to more negative, intense, and frequent emotions in CSE, indicating an increasingly adverse emotional trajectory over time."
