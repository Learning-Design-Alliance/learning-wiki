In addition, brain activation was signiﬁcantly higher in the AF group than in the MF and NF groups in channel 11 (supramarginal gyrus)."

Related Claims¶

  • In the metacognitive feedback group, frontopolar activation correlates with metacognitive sensitivity and middle temporal gyrus activation correlates with retention — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Machine learning models using whole-module fNIRS features predict transfer scores in all feedback groups, with the frontopolar area a predictor regardless of feedback type — related
  • Metacognitive and affective chatbot feedback each produce higher retention scores than neutral feedback, with no significant difference between metacognitive and affective groups — related
  • Metacognitive chatbot feedback produces higher transfer scores than both affective and neutral feedback — related
  • Metacognitive chatbot feedback raises metacognitive sensitivity relative to affective and neutral feedback — related
  • SVM classification of fNIRS activation patterns distinguishes RD with 60–90% accuracy, with left MTG channels the most consistent discriminative features — related
  • Task and interpersonal interdependence interact on inter-brain synchrony in the right supramarginal gyrus — related
