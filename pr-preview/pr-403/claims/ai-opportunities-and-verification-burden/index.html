Hochberg N and Waitzman R 2026¶

Hochberg N and Waitzman R (2026). From information providers to learning designers: heutagogical teaching practices in AI-enhanced education. Front. Educ. 11:1905833. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2026.1905833

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Qualitative findings on perceived opportunities and challenges from teacher reports in the professional development study. Teachers described faster generation of alternatives and differentiation, but also emphasized unreliable or contextually inappropriate outputs and possible overreliance; the article concludes "AI was thus experienced as an expansion of design possibilities coupled with an increased burden of verification and professional responsibility."
