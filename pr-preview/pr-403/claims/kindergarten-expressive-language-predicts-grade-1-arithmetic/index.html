Evidence¶

Praet 2013¶

Praet, M., Titeca, D., Ceulemans, A., & Desoete, A. (2013). Language in the prediction of arithmetic in kindergarten and grade 1. Learning and Individual Differences (manuscript LAEIND-D-12-OO174R1). https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/LAEIND-D-12-OO174R1

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Longitudinal regression with grade 1 KRT-R as outcome for the same 63 children retested one year later; kindergarten arithmetic predicted 29.9% of grade 1 variance, and "productive language added 4.6% to the prediction".

"In addition productive language added 4.6% to the prediction when controlling for kindergarten arithmetic (F 2, 62) = 15.78, p < .001, R² = .345)."
