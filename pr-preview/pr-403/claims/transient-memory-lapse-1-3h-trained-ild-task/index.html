Evidence¶

Beverly A. Wright & Ruijing Ning 2024¶

Beverly A. Wright & Ruijing Ning. (2024). A transient memory lapse in humans 1–3h after training. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00272-4

q3 · i1 · causal · r1

Between-groups experiment: young adults trained on ILD discrimination and tested on ILD discrimination 30 min (n=20), 1–3 h (n=17), or 6–10 h (n=20) after training; LMER bout x group F(2,499) = 3.05, p = 0.048; pairwise comparisons printed small effect sizes "30 min vs. 1–3 h: t(499) = 2.17,p = 0.047, d = 0.30" and "6–10 h vs. 1–3 h: t(499) = 2.16, p = 0.047, d = 0.29".

"the magnitude of improvement was greater 30 min ( n = 20; improvement: 1.34 dB) and 6 –10 h ( n = 20; 1.34 dB) after training than 1 –3h ( n = 17; 0.88 dB) after training (pair-wise comparisons of the simple effects: 30 min vs. 1–3 h: t(499) = 2.17,p = 0.047, d = 0.30; 6–10 h vs. 1–3 h: t(499) = 2.16, p = 0.047, d = 0.29)"
