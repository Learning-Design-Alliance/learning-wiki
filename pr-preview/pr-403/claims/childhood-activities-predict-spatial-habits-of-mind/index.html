Emily Grossnickle Peterson 2020¶

Cognitive Research: Principles and Implications. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41235-020-00239-0

Multivariate regression Model 1 predicting spatial habits of mind (SHOMI) from childhood and adolescent activities with covariates (F6,298 = 11.67, R2 = .19); "spatial ac- tivities in childhood were a significant predictor" (β = .33) while adolescent activities were not (β = .08).

"revealed that spatial ac- tivities in childhood were a significant predictor ( b = .43, β = .33, SE = .08, p < .001), while adolescent spatial activities were not ( b = .08, β = .08, SE = .06, p = .16)."
