Renkl 2004¶

Renkl, A., Atkinson, R.K. & Große, C.S. (2004). How Fading Worked Solution Steps Works – A Cognitive Load Perspective. Instructional Science. https://doi.org/10.1023/B:TRUC.0000021815.74806.f6

q2 · i? · causal · r?

The authors' interpretation of Experiment 1 led to a hypothesis that "specificself-explanation activities are triggered byfaded steps". Experiment 2 tested this directly with thinking-aloud protocols collected during learner interaction with the environment.

"This suggested that specificself-explanation activities are triggered byfaded steps. The second experiment investigatedthis hypothesis directly by collecting andanalyzing thinking-aloud protocols generated bythe learners during their interaction with thelearning environment."
