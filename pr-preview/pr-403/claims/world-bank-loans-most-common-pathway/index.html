Evidence¶

Edwards Jr. 2023¶

Edwards Jr., D. Brent; Caravaca Hernández, Alejandro; Rappeport, Annie; et al. (2023). World Bank influence on policy formation in education: A systematic review of the literature. Review of Educational Research, Vol. 94, Núm. 4. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543231194725

q2 · i? · review · r2

Synthesis of the retained literature in the review's results section: the review reports that loans are the most common pathway and that top borrowers such as Brazil and India agreed to education-related loans valuing more than $8 billion during 1987-2012, citing Mundy & Verger (2015). The review attributes the scale figures to those cited studies.

"Given that the World Bank is, first and foremost, a bank, the pathway of loans is the most obvious and common form of influence. The scale of World Bank lending can be enormous."
