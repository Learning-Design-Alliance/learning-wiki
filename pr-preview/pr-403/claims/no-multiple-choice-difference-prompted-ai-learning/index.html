Evidence¶

Xiaoyu Hou 2026¶

Xiaoyu Hou, Bo Xiao, Hexu Liu and Shane Mueller. (2026). The Role of Instructional Guidance in Generative AI-Assisted Learning: Empirical Evidence from Construction Engineering Education. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05509 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.05509

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Same randomized controlled experiment (n = 95). Multiple-choice accuracy means were 0.63, 0.59, and 0.72 for control, unprompted, and prompted groups; the between-condition ANOVA on total MC score was "not statistically significant (F = 2.38, p = 0.099 )". Equivalence was not tested.

"For multiple-choice questions, the Prompted group achieves the highest mean score (6.45), followed by the NA (5.67) and Unprompted (5.27) groups. However, the ANOVA results indicate that this difference is not statistically significant (F = 2.38, p = 0.099 )."
