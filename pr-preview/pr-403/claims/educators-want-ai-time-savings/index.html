Evidence¶

anchor-time¶

Griffiths, R., Peters Hinton, V., Daal, S., Pattenhouse, M. (2026, July). K‑12 AI Infrastructure: Findings from educator and developer outreach. Digital Promise. https://doi.org/10.51388/20.500.12265/309

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Findings from the educator interviews on workload. A district staff member said "Time is always of the essence, and teacher capacity is a massive bottleneck," and a rural district association leader noted even two hours saved per week is highly valuable.

"When asked what they most want AI to do for them, nearly all educators focused on tasks that would free up time in existing workflows and provide new visibility into student and teacher needs and behavior."
