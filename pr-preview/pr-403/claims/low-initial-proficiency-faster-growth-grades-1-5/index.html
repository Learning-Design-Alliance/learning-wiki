Evidence¶

Scammacca Nancy 2020¶

Scammacca Nancy, Fall Anna-Mária, Capin Philip, Roberts Greg, Swanson Elizabeth (2020). Examining Factors Affecting Reading and Math Growth and Achievement Gaps in Grades 1–5: A Cohort-Sequential Longitudinal Approach. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000400

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Conditional latent growth curve models on 5,912 students from one large Texas district, tested at six time points across two years with Renaissance STAR assessments. The article reports that "students with lower scores at Time 1 grew at a faster rate over two school years than those with higher initial scores."

"Inverse relations were evident between math quartile at the beginning of Year 1 and math slope as well as reading quartile at the beginning of the Year 1 and reading slope, with the effect of initial status being larger in math than in reading. Standardized coefficients ranged from −.10 to −.33 for reading and −.60 to −.79 for math."
