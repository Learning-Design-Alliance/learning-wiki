Evidence¶

Brummelman 2024¶

Brummelman, E., van Atteveldt, N., Wolf, S. & Sierksma, J. (2024). Using social and behavioral science to address achievement inequality. npj Science of Learning, 9, 44. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00259-1

q2 · i? · review · r2

The editorial reports, citing Sierksma, two pre-registered experiments in which children overheard that peers were good or not so good at a task before helping them. The printed finding is that children "provided moreempowering help(e.g., hints)" to competent peers. No effect sizes are printed.

"Children provided moreempowering help(e.g., hints) to peers portrayed as competent and moredisempowering help (e.g., right answers) to peers portrayed as incompetent."
