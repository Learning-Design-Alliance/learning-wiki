Evidence¶

Kral 2018¶

Kral, T. R. A., Stodola, D. E., Birn, R. M., Mumford, J. A., Solis, E., Flook, L., Patsenko, E. G., Anderson, C. G., Steinkuehler, C. & Davidson, R. J. (2018). Neural correlates of video game empathy training in adolescents: a randomized trial. npj Science of Learning 3:13. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0029-6

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Regression of change in empathic accuracy-modulated RTPJ BOLD onto a game engagement score built from telemetry (first tries at emotion recognition and intensity ratings), controlling for total playtime; the analysis reported t(30) = 2.92, p = 0.01, b = 0.47. No standardized effect size was printed.

"Engagement with the training aspects of Crystals was associated with significantly higher increases in empathic accuracy-related RTPJ activation ( t(30) = 2.92, p = 0.01, b = 0.47, CI [0.14, 0.79]"

Kral 2018 (2)¶

Kral, T. R. A., Stodola, D. E., Birn, R. M., Mumford, J. A., Solis, E., Flook, L., Patsenko, E. G., Anderson, C. G., Steinkuehler, C. & Davidson, R. J. (2018). Neural correlates of video game empathy training in adolescents: a randomized trial. npj Science of Learning 3:13. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-018-0029-6

q3 · i? · causal · r2

In the same telemetry regression analyses, total playtime did not predict RTPJ activation change (t(31) = −0.21, p = 0.84) and "no relationship between game engagement and change in empathic accuracy" was found; non-significant taser use and quest completions also failed to predict RTPJ change.

"There was no relationship between game engagement and change in empathic accuracy controlling for total playtime ( t(30) = 1.02, p = 0.32, b = 0.06, CI [ −0.06, 0.18]"
