Shiza Khaqan 2024¶

Shiza Khaqan, Gisela Redondo-Sama. (2024). A systematic review of the role of dialogic leadership: Characterization and impacts. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2024.100618

Thematic synthesis of 27 peer-reviewed articles in this systematic review; the review reports, citing Padrós and Flecha (2014), that "equality between leaders and actors is a key characteristic that defines dialogic leadership". No effect size is printed.
