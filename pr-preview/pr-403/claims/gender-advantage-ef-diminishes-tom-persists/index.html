Evidence¶

Marcovitch S. 2014¶

Marcovitch S., O'Brien M., Calkins S. D., Leerkes E. M., Weaver J. M., Levine D. W. (2014). A Longitudinal Assessment of the Relation between Executive Function and Theory of Mind at 3, 4, and 5 Years. Cognitive Development. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cogdev.2014.07.001

q3 · i? · associational · r3

Control-variable analyses in the latent variable model of the 226-child sample. On EF, girls' advantage was β = −.16, p = .02 at age 3, marginal at 4 (B = −.48, p = .06), and not significant at 5 (B = −.27, p = .11).

"girls outperformed boys at age 3 (β = −.24, p = .01), and tests of indirect effects revealed that girls continued to outperform boys in ToM at age 4 (B = −.37, p = .02) and age 5 (B = −.29, p = .01)"
