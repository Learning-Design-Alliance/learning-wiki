Evidence¶

Rovers 2019¶

Rovers, S. F. E., Clarebout, G., Savelberg, H. H. C. M., de Bruin, A. B. H., & van Merriënboer, J. J. G. (2019). Granularity matters: comparing different ways of measuring self-regulated learning. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-019-09188-6

q2 · i? · review · r3

Review's summary statement following its synthesis of the ten fine-grained comparison studies; no pooled effect size is printed, so the finding is directional rather than quantified.

"Traces tend to have a higher predictive value in terms of achievement than self-reports."

Rovers 2019 (2)¶

Rovers, S. F. E., Clarebout, G., Savelberg, H. H. C. M., de Bruin, A. B. H., & van Merriënboer, J. J. G. (2019). Granularity matters: comparing different ways of measuring self-regulated learning. Metacognition and Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11409-019-09188-6

q2 · i? · review · r2

Review reports the follow-up analysis of Jamieson-Noel and Winne (2003): self-reported SRL intensity significantly predicted achievement (16% of variance) while trace-based measures did not; the review attributes this partly to traces being clustered into global scales.

"a notable exception to the general finding that traces have a higher predictive value for achievement than self-reports is the study by Jamieson-Noel and Winne ( 2003), in which traces of SRL intensity did not predict achievement, while self-report of SRL intensity did"

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Self-report measures of SRL capture perceptions of strategy use rather than implementation quality, and a combined trace-data approach may be best
  • Students are better calibrated in predicting achievement than in reporting specific study tactics (Winne & Jamieson-Noel 2002) — related
  • When SRL is measured at a fine grain size (specific strategies), self-reports show low calibration with behavioral measures — related
  • Self-reports of achievement goal orientations diverge markedly from traced goal orientations, which predict reading achievement while self-reports do not — related
