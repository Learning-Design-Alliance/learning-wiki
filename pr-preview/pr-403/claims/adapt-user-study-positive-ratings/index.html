Yanjie Zhang 2026¶

Yanjie Zhang, Jiajun Zhu, Minyu Wu, Huamin Qu, and Sicheng Song. (2026). AdaPT: Adaptive Lesson Plan Transformer for Cross-Regional and Differentiated Instruction. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17633 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.17633

q2 · i? · design · r2

Mixed-methods user study with 12 participants (7 male, 5 female; average 12.83 years of K-12 teaching experience) combining 7-point Likert questionnaires with post-study interviews. The article reports "mean ratings consistently above the neutral baseline of 4"; no effect sizes are printed.

"Overall,teachers evaluated the system positively across all dimensions, with mean ratings consistently above the neutral baseline of 4."
