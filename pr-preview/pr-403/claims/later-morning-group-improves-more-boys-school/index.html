Evidence¶

Winch 1911¶

Winch, W. H. (1911). Mental adaptation during the school day as measured by arithmetical reasoning.—Part I. The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://www.google.com/search?q=%22Mental+adaptation+during+the+school+day%22+Winch+1911

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Results of the first series in a London municipal boys' school (Standard III, about 40 boys), where a class split into two equal groups on preliminary tests worked the same problematic-arithmetic tests at 9.40 or 11.25 A.M. The article reports "a balance in favor of the later working group of about 5 per cent"; no standardized effect size is printed.

"Group A improved 6.3 per cent, on its preliminary record; Group B improved 11.4 per cent. There is, therefore, a balance in favor of the later working group of about 5 per cent."
