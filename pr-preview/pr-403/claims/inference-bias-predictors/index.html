For inference questions, higher self-reported general monitoring strategies (General Monitoring Strategies Checklist) predicted stronger overestimation, against the expectation that lower levels would overestimate more; even lower levels "still showed some overestimation."

"self-reported general monitoring strategies was related to stronger overestimations compared to lower levels of this learner characteristic, which, against our expecta- tion, still showed some overestimation"

Related Claims¶

  • For postdiction bias on factual questions, underestimation was prevalent for females, higher reading skill, and lower self-perceived topic knowledge and metacomprehension ability — related
  • For prediction judgments on factual questions, males and learners with lower WMC and lower topic knowledge, but higher self-perceived topic knowledge and self-perceived metacomprehension ability, showed stronger overestimation — related
  • Significant learner characteristics explained 17% and 14% of variance in prediction bias and 11% and 12% in postdiction bias, so characteristics were slightly more relevant for predictions — related
