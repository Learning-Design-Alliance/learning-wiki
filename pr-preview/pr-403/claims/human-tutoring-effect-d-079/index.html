Mario Brcic 2026¶

Mario Brcic, Stjepan Frljic. (2026). The Effortless Trap: Productive Struggle, AI, and the Illusion of Learning. Preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26181 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.26181

q2 · i2 · theoretical · r3

The article reports, citing VanLehn (2011) and Bloom (1984), that one-to-one tutoring's honest effect size is "about d≈0.79", large but short of two sigma. The article uses this to argue AI could make such scarce attention available at scale, stated as promise, not delivered fact.

"A good human tutor lifts an ordinary student well beyond the average classroom; the honest size of that effect is about d≈0.79 , large but short of the celebrated two sigma (VanLehn, 2011; Bloom, 1984)."
