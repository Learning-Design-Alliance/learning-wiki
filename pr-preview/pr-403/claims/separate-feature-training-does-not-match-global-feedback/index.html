Evidence¶

Behnam Karami & Caspar M. Schwiedrzik 2024¶

Behnam Karami & Caspar M. Schwiedrzik. (2024). Visual perceptual learning of feature conjunctions leverages non-linear mixed selectivity. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00226-w

q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Wilcoxon rank-sum tests comparing conjunction test performance across experiments after post-cued single-feature training (Experiment 3, n = 12, plus the trained groups), which required only one feature update per trial to mitigate load differences; the Experiment 1 vs. 3 contrast gave d = −0.7277.

"We found a statistically signi ﬁcant difference between Experiments 1 and 3 (Wilcoxon rank-sum test, Z =2 . 0 8 9 2,p = 0.0367 ,d = −0.7277), but not between Experiments 2 and 3 ( Z = 1.4608 ,p = 0.1441 ,d = −0.5767) in the conjunction test (Fig. 5a)"

Behnam Karami & Caspar M. Schwiedrzik 2024 (2)¶

Behnam Karami & Caspar M. Schwiedrzik. (2024). Visual perceptual learning of feature conjunctions leverages non-linear mixed selectivity. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00226-w

q3 · i? · causal · r2

Within Experiment 3, half of participants (n = 12 total) received 180 trials of brief conjunction exposure before the test; this "Brief additional training" showed no statistically significant effect (p = 1), addressing novelty of the conjunction task.
