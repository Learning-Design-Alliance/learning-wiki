Evidence¶

Imundo 2024¶

Imundo, M. N., Zung, I., Whatley, M. C., & Pan, S. C. (2024). When Two Learners Are Better Than One: Using Flashcards with a Partner Improves Metacognitive Accuracy. Metacognition and Learning. https://link.springer.com/journal/11409/articles

q3 · i? · causal · r3

Experiment 3's 2x2 mixed ANOVA on cued-recall performance revealed a significant Condition x Test Delay interaction (ηp2 = .02). Follow-up t-tests showed Individual learners scored higher at the immediate test (d = 0.31) but the conditions did not differ at the delayed test (d = 0.07).

"the Test Delay x Condition interaction was significant, F (1, 208) = 5.11, p = .02, ηp2 = .02. The pattern of the interaction suggested that the Individual condition’s rate of forgetting was greater than the Paired condition’s."
