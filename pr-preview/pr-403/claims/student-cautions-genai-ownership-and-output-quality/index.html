Evidence¶

Leman Figen Gül 2025¶

Leman Figen Gül, Burak Delikanlı, Oğulcan Üneşi, Ertuğrul Ömer Gül. (2025). Development and applications of Generative AI in architectural design studios. ITU A|Z • Vol 22 No 3 • November 2025 • 751-774. https://doi.org/10.58278/0.2025.109

q2 · i? · qualitative · r2

Thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews (N=6) in the third semester. The article reports participants' stated concerns qualitatively, including "the perceived imperfection or incomplete- ness of AI-generated outputs"; a related theme was a perceived loss of ownership when employing GenAI. No effect size is printed.

"Nevertheless, participants re- mained cautious about relying on GenAI throughout the entire creative process. Their main concerns were the perceived imperfection or incomplete- ness of AI-generated outputs, prompt- ing skepticism regarding the accuracy of some results"
