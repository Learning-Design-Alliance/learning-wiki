q3 · i? · quant-synthesis · r3

The review's multilevel meta-analysis of secondary behavioural outcomes reports negligible or non-significant effects on conduct problems and delinquency and a small, non-significant effect on substance use; no effect sizes for these outcomes are printed in the supplied text.

"However, the results for secondary behavioural outcomes were more limited: effects on conduct problems and delinquency were negligible or non‐significant, and the impact on substance use was small and not statistically significant."
