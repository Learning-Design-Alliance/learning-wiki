Blanchard 2015¶

Blanchard, E. G. (2015). Socio-Cultural Imbalances in AIED Research: Investigations, Implications and Opportunities. Int J Artif Intell Educ 25. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-014-0027-7

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Observational analysis of national affiliations of authors in the ITS and AIED conferences over 12 years (2002–2013), reported in Tables 4 and 5. The paper states "First authors and other authors are affiliated to US and WEIRD institutions to a very large extent", with Table 4 totals of 50% USA and 90% WEIRD for first authors.

"First authors and other authors are affiliated to US and WEIRD institutions to a very large extent. Latin American and Asian authors have extremely low representations"
