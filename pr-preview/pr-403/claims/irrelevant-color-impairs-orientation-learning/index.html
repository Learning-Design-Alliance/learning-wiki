Evidence¶

Behnam Karami & Caspar M. Schwiedrzik 2024¶

Behnam Karami & Caspar M. Schwiedrzik. (2024). Visual perceptual learning of feature conjunctions leverages non-linear mixed selectivity. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00226-w

q3 · i3 · causal · r1

Mixed ANOVAs comparing orientation learning indices (Experiment 4, n = 10, orientation trained with color irrelevant and maximally salient, versus trained groups). The Experiment main effect against Experiment 1 reached p = 0.008 with η2 = 0.2331; the Experiment×Block interaction η2 = 0.1891.

"We ﬁnd that orientation learning in the presence of task irrelevant color was smaller in Experiment 4 than in Experiments 1 (Experiment: F(1,27) = 8.2049,p = 0.008, η2 = 0.2331, Block: F(6,162) = 123.5437,p <1 0−15, η2 = 0.8353, Experiment × Block: F(6,162) = 6.2979,p < 10−5 , η2 = 0.1891)"

Discussion¶

Related Claims¶

  • Conjunction learning transfers partially to new spatial locations, with color showing larger transfer indices than orientation, indicating pure selectivity neurons also participate alongside mixed selectivity — related
  • Under global feedback, learning reflects significant non-linear mixing of color and orientation, while the linear mixing term does not differ between feedback regimes — related
  • Conjunction benefit of global feedback is not due to higher cognitive load of dual feature updates: post-cued single-feature training (Experiment 3) does not match global-feedback conjunction learning — related
  • Reaction times on the XOR task reveal a strategy difference: the feature-specific feedback group was slower on XOR relative to conjunction than the global-feedback group — related
