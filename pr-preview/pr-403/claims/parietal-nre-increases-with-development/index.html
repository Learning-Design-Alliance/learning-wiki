Evidence¶

Haist Frank 2015¶

Haist Frank, Wazny Jarnet H., Toomarian Elizabeth, Adamo Maha. (2015). Development of brain systems for nonsymbolic numerosity and the relationship to formal math academic achievement. Human brain mapping. https://doi.org/10.1002/hbm.22666

q2 · i? · associational · r2

Whole-brain linear regression of the voxelwise neural ratio effect on the natural logarithm of age across the 44-participant developmental sample, analyzed in the fMRI results section. Positive age effects included bilateral IPS, angular gyrus, IPL/SMG, and SPL, and were "generally more extensive in right hemisphere parietal cortex."

"Overall, the parietal lobe areas produced the most extensive age effects for the NRE, showing increasing ratio sensitivity with development. These positive effects were generally more extensive in right hemisphere parietal cortex."

Related Claims¶

  • A reliable positive relationship between parietal NRE and math achievement appears only in children, not adolescents or adults — related
  • In adults only, poorer math achievement is associated with greater hippocampal NRE sensitivity — related
  • In children, the BOLD numerical ratio effect correlates with individual differences in the behavioral ratio effect in right OTC, left superior parietal cortex, and bilateral IFG; no such correlation reaches significance in adults — related
  • Behavioral ratio sensitivity for reaction time increases with age, with adults showing greater RT ratio sensitivity than children — related
  • Left IPS connectivity with right IPS, superior parietal lobule, and fusiform gyrus increases over childhood — related
