Reupert 2023¶

Reupert, A; Sullivan, A; Tippett, N; White, S; Woodcock, S; Chen, L; Simons, M. (2023). An Exploration of the Experiences of Substitute Teachers: A Systematic Review. Review of Educational Research. https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543221149418

q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r?

The review's own mixed-methods research synthesis of peer-reviewed studies and dissertations on substitute teachers in primary/elementary, middle, and secondary/high schools reports that "their conditions and experiences are less than satisfactory, exacerbated by a lack of targeted support." No effect size is printed.

"The study found that, although substitute teachers are a heterogeneous group, their conditions and experiences are less than satisfactory, exacerbated by a lack of targeted support."
