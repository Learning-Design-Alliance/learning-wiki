q1 · i? · design · r2

Cross-case synthesis of four retrospective case studies scored by the CDI instructional team from archived capstone artifacts. The rule flagged at-floor P2/P3 pillars "behind otherwise strong P1 profiles" without blocking certification in Cases 1 and 2.

"The no-thin-pillar rule helped flag gaps in Cases 1 and 2, surfacing P2 or P3 pillars sitting at the floor behind otherwise strong P1 profiles; the rule was not the binding gate for any student in these two cases, but it identified the pillars in most need of remediation"
