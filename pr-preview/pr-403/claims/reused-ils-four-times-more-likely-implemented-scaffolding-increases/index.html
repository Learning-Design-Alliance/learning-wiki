Evidence¶

Ulrike Cress 2020¶

Ulrike Cress. (2020). The richness of CSCL environments. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-020-09335-1

q2 · i? · review · r2

The editorial reports, on the Rodríguez-Triana et al. large-scale Go-Lab study tracing about 40,000 ILSs, that "Scaffolding increased the implementation rate" and re-use was linked to about four times higher implementation likelihood. The editorial attributes both findings to the study.

"Re-used ILSs were about four times more likely to have been implemented than those created from scratch. Scaffolding increased the implementation rate."

Related Claims¶

  • ILS adoption rate increases across knowledge maturation stages, from 3.38% for individually created ILSs to 43.94% for published ILSs
  • ILS reuse and edition as a proxy for knowledge appropriation relates to about four times higher adoption (19.77% vs 4.51%) — possibly the same claim (merge candidate)
  • The higher the maturation level of an Inquiry Learning Space (create, share, co-create, publish), the higher its probability of being implemented in a classroom — related
  • Teachers who participated in ILSs with KAM practices were far more likely to be involved in an implemented ILS (42–53%) than the dataset average (16.27%) — related
  • Expert scaffolding is associated with ILS adoption, but the fading stage shows a comparatively low rate (8.27%) and was a non-significant predictor — related
