Likert-scale information-quantity ratings (1 = Not Enough Information to 5 = Too Much Information) aggregated across snippets and analyzed with Kruskal-Wallis H tests; the article reports mean ratings around 3.3–3.4 and "no significant difference in perceived information quantity across groups."

"A Kruskal-Wallis H test also showed no significant difference in perceived information quantity across groups,𝐻( 7)= 3.57, 𝑝=. 828, showing that students generally rated the information load similarly regardless of condition."
