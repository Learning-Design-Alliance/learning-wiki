Evidence¶

Walter R. Miles; W. S. Smiley 1915¶

Walter R. Miles; W. S. Smiley. (1915). Communications and Discussions, The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://books.google.com

q2 · i3 · associational · r?

Correlational study of school records for 106 pupils (53 girls and 53 boys), using both the Pearson coefficient and the method of quartiles. The printed effect size was r = +.71 between average elementary and average high school standing.

"The coefficient of correlation between the average elementary school stand- ing and the average high school standing was found to be +.71. (The similar coefficient in Dearborn's study was about +.80)."
