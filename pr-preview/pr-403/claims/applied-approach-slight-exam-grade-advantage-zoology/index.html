Evidence¶

Gilbert 1909¶

Gilbert, J. P. (1909). An experiment on methods of teaching zoology (preliminary report). The Journal of Educational Psychology. https://psycnet.apa.org/journal/edu

q2 · i? · causal · r1

Parallel-groups comparison in the Academy of the University of Illinois, first semester 1909-10: two beginners' zoology sections with the same teacher, texts, and examination. The applied-science Section B averaged "73.2%" versus 72.5% for Section A; papers were graded blind to student names.

"Section A made an average grade of 72.5% on the final examination, while Section B made an average grade of 73.2%."
