Evidence¶

Sofia Jusslin 2022¶

Sofia Jusslin, Kaisa Korpinen, Niina Lilja, Rose Martin, Johanna Lehtinen-Schnabel, Eeva Anttila. (2022). Embodied learning and teaching approaches in language education: A mixed studies review. Educational Research Review. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2022.100480

q2 · i? · review · r3

The review's own narrative synthesis across 41 studies (23 qualitative, 12 quantitative, six mixed methods), using an assimilated approach merging quantitative with qualitative findings. This is the review's synthesizing proposal, not a single pooled effect; no effect size is printed for it.

"The review proposes an understanding of embodied language learning that holds potentials to engage learners holistically, while simultaneously promoting language learning skills and adding emotional and motivational benefits to language learning."
