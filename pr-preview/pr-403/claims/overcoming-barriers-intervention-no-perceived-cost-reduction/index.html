Evidence¶

Lisi Wang 2023¶

Lisi Wang, Katherine Muenks, Veronica X. Yan. (2023). Interventions to Promote Retrieval Practice: Strategy Knowledge Predicts Intent, but Perceived Cost Predicts Usage. Journal of Educational Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/edu0000813

q3 · i0 · causal · r3

Null result from the randomized two-version comparison at T1: means were identical at 3.01 in both conditions, 'Cohen's d = 0.001'. The mixed ANOVA also found no main effect or interaction of perceived cost across time points.

"The overcoming barriers version ( M = 3.01, SD = 1.20) did not reduce perceived cost relative to the traditional version ( M = 3.01, SD = 1.14), t (384) = 0.01, p = .50 (one-sided), Cohen's d = 0.001"
