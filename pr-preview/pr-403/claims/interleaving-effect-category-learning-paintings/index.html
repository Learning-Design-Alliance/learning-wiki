Evidence¶

Onan 2022¶

Onan, E., Wiradhany, W., Biwer, F., Janssen, E. M., & de Bruin, A. B. H. (2022). Growing Out of the Experience: How Subjective Experiences of Effort and Learning Influence the Use of Interleaved Practice. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09692-3

q3 · i2 · causal · r3

Within-subjects experiment in which all 150 participants studied 12 artists' painting styles through both blocked and interleaved units before Test I. The paired t-test showed interleaved practice "resulted in better classification performance than blocked practice" with d = 0.76.

"A paired sample t-test revealed that interleaved practice (M = 5.95, SD = 3.55) resulted in better classification performance than blocked practice (M = 3.11, SD = 2.54) in Test I, t(149) = 9.35, p < 0.001, d = 0.76."

Onan 2022 (2)¶

Onan, E., Wiradhany, W., Biwer, F., Janssen, E. M., & de Bruin, A. B. H. (2022). Growing Out of the Experience: How Subjective Experiences of Effort and Learning Influence the Use of Interleaved Practice. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-022-09692-3

q3 · i2 · causal · r3

Test II followed study phase II, in which participants studied six new artists with the strategy they chose. The independent-samples t-test showed choosers of interleaved practice "outperformed those who chose blocked practice" with d = 0.61.

"an independent sample t-test further revealed that participants who chose interleaved practice to study novel materials (M = 12.85; SD = 4.36) outperformed those who chose blocked practice (M = 10.04, SD = 4.61) in Test-II, t(148) = -3.73, p < 0.001, d = 0.61."
