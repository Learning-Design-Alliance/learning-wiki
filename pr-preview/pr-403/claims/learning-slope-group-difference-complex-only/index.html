Evidence¶

Hehl 2026¶

Hehl, M., Toyonaga, T., Carson, R. E., Dupont, P., Van Laere, K., Swinnen, S. P. & Cuypers, K. (2026). No significant changes in synaptic density and gray matter volume following motor learning—a pilot study. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-026-00451-5

q3 · i? · causal · r1

Wilcoxon rank sum comparison of individual learning-progress slopes in the 22-participant pilot, significant for complex subtasks and not for the 1:1 subtask (p = 0.5190, W = 71). The article reports "median difference = −0.0267" with a 95% CI of −0.0447 to −0.0071.

"Training progress, as expressed using the linear slope of the progress test scores over time, was signiﬁcantly different between the simple and the complex training group for the complex subtasks ( p = 0.0041, W = 18, median difference = −0.0267, 95% CI = −0.0447 to −0.0071"
