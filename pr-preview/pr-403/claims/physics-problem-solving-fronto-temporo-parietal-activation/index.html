Evidence¶

Bartley 2019¶

Bartley, J. E., Riedel, M. C., Salo, T., Boeving, E. R., Bottenhorn, K. L., Bravo, E. I., Odean, R., Nazareth, A., Laird, R. W., Sutherland, M. T., Pruden, S. M., Brewe, E. & Laird, A. R. (2019). Brain activity links performance in science reasoning with conceptual approach. npj Science of Learning 4:20. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-019-0059-8

q3 · i? · associational · r2

Whole-brain GLM contrast of physics (FCI) versus control blocks in 107 undergraduates scanned after a semester of physics instruction, thresholded at P < 0.001 CDT and P < 0.05 FWE-corrected. The contrast "revealed activation across a fronto-temporo-parietal network" including V5/MT+ and RSC.

"Maps of FCI > Control blocks revealed activation across a fronto-temporo-parietal network, including the prefrontal cortex (PFC), left dorsal striatum, PPC, RSC, and dorsal posterior cingulate cortex, lateral occipitotemporal cortex (V5/MT +), and cerebellum"
