Xinfei Cen 2026¶

Xinfei Cen, Vincent Aleven, Kenneth R. Koedinger, Conrad Borchers, and Paulo F. Carvalho. (2026). Self-Efficacy and Favorability Shape Learning from Tutoring Systems and Paper Practice. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17470 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2606.17470

q2 · i? · causal · r2

Figure 3 visualizes the significant three-way interaction, splitting favorability into low, medium, and high levels (mean ± SD). The quoted contrast is the paper-panel pattern; the tutor panel showed the mirrored pattern for computer-based favorability. No effect size printed.

"Specifically, low baseline self-efficacy students who were less favor-able toward paper-based practice benefited more from paper-based instruction, compared to low baseline self-efficacy students who were more favorable toward paper-based practice."
