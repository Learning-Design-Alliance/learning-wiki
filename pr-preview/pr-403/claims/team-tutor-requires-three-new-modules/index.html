Evidence¶

Gilbert 2017¶

Gilbert, S. B., Slavina, A., Dorneich, M. C., Sinatra, A. M., Bonner, D., Johnston, J., Holub, J., MacAllister, A., & Winer, E. (2017). Creating a Team Tutor Using GIFT. Int J Artif Intell Educ. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40593-017-0151-2

q1 · i? · design · r2

Design analysis in the Background section, not an empirical test. The authors derive the module requirement from the literature on team training and ITS architecture, stating that "creating a team tutor requires at least three new modules" and noting "there will be a new individual learner model for each individual."

"As shown in Fig. 1 (bottom), creating a team tutor requires at least three new modules: an expert model for team skills, a pedagogical model that can decide when to give feedback to specific members vs. the whole team, and a team model to record the current team skills of the team."
