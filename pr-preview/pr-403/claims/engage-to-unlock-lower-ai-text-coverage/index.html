q3 · i2 · causal · r2

Paste telemetry and sequence matching (n≥8 words) across the three AI-assisted conditions (n=299) in the controlled experiment; the Standard Chatbot contrast yielded d=−0.47 and the Time-Matched Unlock contrast d=0.07. The article notes these aggregate comparisons do not establish how prompt count relates to text incorporation within participants.

"Planned Welch contrasts with Holm correction confirmed significantly lower AI text coverage under Engage-to-Unlock (27.32%) than Standard Chatbot (47.16%;𝑝Holm =. 002,𝑑=−0.47), while Engage-to-Unlock and Time-Matched Unlock did not significantly differ (24.51%;𝑝Holm =.617,𝑑=0.07)."
