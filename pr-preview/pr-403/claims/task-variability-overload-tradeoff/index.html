Slava Kalyuga 2010¶

Slava Kalyuga, Alexander Renkl, Fred Paas. (2010). Facilitating Flexible Problem Solving: A Cognitive Load Perspective. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-010-9132-9

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Authors' theoretical caveat within the CLT framework, discussing Paas and van Merriënboer's variability-of-worked-examples findings. No empirical test of this overload claim is reported in the paper.

"relating many components and levels of knowledge in working memory can, in some cases, also overload working memory, thus decreasing the efficiency of learning"
