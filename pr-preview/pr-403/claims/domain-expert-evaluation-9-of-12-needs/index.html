Ulla Hemminki-Reijonen 2025¶

Ulla Hemminki-Reijonen, Noha M. A. M. Hassan, Minna Huotilainen, Jaana-Maija Koivisto & Benjamin Ultan Cowley. (2025). Design of generative AI-powered pedagogy for virtual reality environments in higher education. npj Science of Learning. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00326-1

q2 · i? · design · r2

Qualitative deductive content analysis of feedback from five semi-structured interviews with eight domain experts who interacted with the text-based characters in CurreChat; the authors report the characters "were able to fulﬁll 9 out of the 12 desirable needs," with comments focused on AI-as-character and assisting-student-learning categories.

"In summary, the comments from domain experts indicated that the two pedagogical GAI characters, Tero and Madida, were able to fulﬁll 9 out of the 12 desirable needs."
