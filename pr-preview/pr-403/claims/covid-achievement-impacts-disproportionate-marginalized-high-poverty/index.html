NWEA Research brief. https://www.nwea.org/research/publication/learning-during-covid-19-an-update-on-student-achievement-and-growth-at-the-start-of-the-2021-22-school-year/

The brief reports subgroup patterns in fall 2021 MAP Growth achievement: declines were universal across student groups, with "disproportionately high impacts for historically marginalized students and students in high-poverty schools." No subgroup effect sizes are printed in the available text.
