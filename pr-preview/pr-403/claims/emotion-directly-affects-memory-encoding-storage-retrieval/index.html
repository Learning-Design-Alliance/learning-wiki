Evidence¶

Plass 2019¶

Plass, J. L., & Kalyuga, S. (2019). Four Ways of Considering Emotion in Cognitive Load Theory. Educational Psychology Review, 31, 339–359. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-019-09473-5

q1 · i? · theoretical · r3

Theoretical argument from the article's abstract, presented as the second perspective. It asserts that "encoding, storage, and retrieval of information are affected by emotion even before awareness of the material," citing mechanisms such as mood-dependent and mood-congruent processing.

"Another perspective shows that encoding, storage, and retrieval of information are affected by emotion even before awareness of the material, and that emotion may directly affect memory by broadening or narrowing cognitive resources, and by mechanisms such as mood-dependent and mood-congruent processing."
