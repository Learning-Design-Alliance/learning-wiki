q2 · i? · quant-synthesis · r1

WWC synthesis of two studies (Brandt et al., 2013; Martin et al., 2009) meeting group design standards with reservations, based on 3,096 students. The effectiveness rating is "Potentially positive effects" with improvement index "+2"; the report notes a positive improvement index does not necessarily mean the estimated effect is statistically significant.

Related Claims¶

  • Brandt et al. (2013) finds a statistically significant positive effect of eMINTS on seventh- and eighth-grade mathematics achievement — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Martin et al. (2009) finds indeterminate effects of eMINTS on both mathematics and literacy achievement in grades 4–5 — a narrower finding that bears on this claim
  • Core-Plus Mathematics has potentially positive effects on mathematics achievement for high school students, with an average improvement index of +15 percentile points — related
  • eMINTS shows no discernible effects on general literacy achievement — related
  • Both included studies report high-fidelity implementation of eMINTS — related
