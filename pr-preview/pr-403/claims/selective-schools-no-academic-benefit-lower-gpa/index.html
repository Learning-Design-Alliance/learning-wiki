Evidence¶

Allensworth 2017¶

Allensworth, E. M., Moore, P. T., Sartain, L., & de la Torre, M. (2017). The Educational Benefits of Attending Higher Performing Schools: Evidence From Chicago High Schools. Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis. https://doi.org/10.3102/0162373716672039

q2 · i? · associational · r3

IPW propensity-score analysis of Chicago Public Schools administrative records for four ninth-grade cohorts (ACT outcome n = 58,900). The unadjusted 7.40-point ACT advantage fell to .51 points, "not significantly different from zero"; GPA was .17 points lower for selective attendees.

"students who attend a selective school, on average, have 7.40 points higher 11th-grade ACT over students in other high schools; however, when we adjust for pretreatment differences among students the difference is .51 points and not significantly different from zero."
