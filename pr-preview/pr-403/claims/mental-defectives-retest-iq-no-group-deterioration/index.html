Evidence¶

Poull 1921¶

Poull, L. E. (1921). Constancy of the I.Q. in mental defectives according to the Stanford revision of Binet tests. Journal of Educational Psychology, 12(6), 323–324. https://doi.org/10.1037/h0071851

q2 · i? · associational · r1

Retest study of 126 unselected inmates of Children's Hospital, Randall's Island, excluding epileptics, with intervals from six months to three years. The article reports "The average change is an increase of + 1.28" and a standard deviation of 5.83; no effect size is printed.

"The average change is an increase of + 1.28. The middle 50 per cent lie between — 3.3 and -j- 4.8 variation. The Standard Deviation was found to be 5.83."
