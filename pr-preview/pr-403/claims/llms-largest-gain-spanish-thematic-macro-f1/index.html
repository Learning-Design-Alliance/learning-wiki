Evidence¶

Esteban U. Vega Barajas 2026¶

Esteban U. Vega Barajas. (2026). A Durability and Cross-Language Transfer Benchmark for a Validated Teaching-Feedback Classification Protocol. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11873 doi:10.48550/arxiv.2607.11873

q2 · i? · design · r2

Descriptive comparison across arms on the unified 233-comment Spanish held-out split (seed 20260616), all arms sharing the identical labeled subset, guide, and held-out set. The article reports the thematic macro F1 jump "from 0.46 for TF-IDF to about 0.68 for BETO and the encoder, then to roughly 0.82 to 0.86"; no significance is claimed.

"The largest single jump is in thematic macro F1: it rises from 0.46 for TF-IDF to about 0.68 for BETO and the encoder, then to roughly 0.82 to 0.86 for the two hosted LLMs."
