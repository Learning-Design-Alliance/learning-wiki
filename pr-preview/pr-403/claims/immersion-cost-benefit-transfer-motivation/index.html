Evidence¶

Alexander Skulmowski & Kate Man Xu 2022¶

Alexander Skulmowski & Kate Man Xu. (2022). Understanding Cognitive Load in Digital and Online Learning: a New Perspective on Extraneous Cognitive Load. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09624-7

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Makransky et al. (2019), that "immersive virtual reality did not significantly affect retention performance compared to written instruction, but increased transfer scores, enjoyment, and motivation". Further studies reportedly support stronger immersion effects on transfer than other measures.

"Makransky et al. (2019) found that immersive virtual reality did not significantly affect retention performance compared to written instruction, but increased transfer scores, enjoyment, and motivation."

Alexander Skulmowski & Kate Man Xu 2022 (2)¶

Alexander Skulmowski & Kate Man Xu. (2022). Understanding Cognitive Load in Digital and Online Learning: a New Perspective on Extraneous Cognitive Load. Educational Psychology Review. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09624-7

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports, citing Frederiksen et al. (2020) in surgical training, that "Highly immersive virtual reality training in a simulator led to a decrease in various learning process variables compared with less immersive learning settings", identifying immersion as a contributor to excessive extraneous load.

"Highly immersive virtual reality training in a simulator led to a decrease in various learning process variables compared with less immersive learning settings"
