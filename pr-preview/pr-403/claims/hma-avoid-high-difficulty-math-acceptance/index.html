Jie Liu 2025¶

Jie Liu, Zhifeng Li, Jiawang Yang, Hao He & Fang Cui. (2025). The neurocognitive mechanism underlying math avoidance among math anxious people. npj Science of Learning 10:50. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-025-00343-0

q3 · i? · associational · r2

GLME analysis of accept/reject choices in the fMRI approach-avoid conflict study of 61 participants (31 LMA, 30 HMA after exclusions). Post-hoc tests showed "for low task difﬁculty, the group effect was not signiﬁcant" but "HMA accepted signiﬁcant fewer math problem challenges" under high difficulty (odd ratio = 7.44, p = 0.003).

"Post-hoc comparison revealed that for low task difﬁculty, the group effect was not signiﬁcant (odd ratio =2 . 2 0 ,SE = 1.59, z = 1.10, p =0 . 2 7 2 ) , while for high task difﬁculty, HMA accepted signiﬁcant fewer math problem challenges (odd ratio = 7.44, SE = 4.95, z = 3.02, p = 0.003)."
