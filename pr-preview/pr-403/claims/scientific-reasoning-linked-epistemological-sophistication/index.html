Evidence¶

Ian Robottom and Sharifah Norhaidah 2008¶

Ian Robottom and Sharifah Norhaidah. (2008). Western science and Islamic learners: When disciplines and culture intersect. Journal of Research in International Education 7(2). https://doi.org/10.1177/1475240908091302

q2 · i? · associational · r2

From the same Malaysian survey, which administered Lawson's Classroom Test of Scientific Reasoning alongside the epistemological beliefs questionnaire. The article reports that "scientific reasoning levels are linked directly to levels of sophistication of epistemological belief scores."

"A valuable finding in this study is that scientific reasoning levels are linked directly to levels of sophistication of epistemological belief scores; that is, students able to operate at a higher level of scientific reasoning exhibited a more sophisticated epistemological belief system while students who displayed lower levels of reasoning exhibited naïve epistemological belief scores."
