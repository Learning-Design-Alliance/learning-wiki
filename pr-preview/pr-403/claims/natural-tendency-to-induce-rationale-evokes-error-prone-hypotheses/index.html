Evidence¶

Ard W. Lazonder 2009¶

Ard W. Lazonder, Pascal Wilhelm, Emiel van Lieburg. (2009). Unraveling the influence of domain knowledge during simulation-based inquiry learning. Instr Sci, 37, 437-451. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-008-9055-8

q1 · i? · causal · r2

Anecdotal observations gathered by the experimenters during the intermediate condition, reported as unsystematic: "Many participants thought the numerical score represented money or weight, and used this idea to formulate and test partially-speciﬁed hypotheses". The article states there was no systematic observation of this usage of inferential mechanisms.
