The article's challenge analysis (type e) argues VET students may already be disadvantaged through "lack of social capital, lower academic achievement in earlier years, or fewer post-secondary opportunities", and that gender disparities intersect with socioeconomic status in VET enrollment and outcomes.

"Without accounting for these underlying inequities, assessments like PISA-VET could reinforce negative percep tions of VET systems that serve disadvantaged populations, rath er than highlighting the need for structural reforms"
