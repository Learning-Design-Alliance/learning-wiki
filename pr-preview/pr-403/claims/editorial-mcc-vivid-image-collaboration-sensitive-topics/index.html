Carolyn Rosé · Sanna Järvelä 2022¶

Carolyn Rosé · Sanna Järvelä. (2022). Supporting perspective taking across chasms of thinking: Do real-time analytics hold the key?. International Journal of Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11412-022-09378-6

q2 · i? · review · r2

The review reports on the Aldemir et al. study of shared meaning-making in online intergroup discussions around sensitive topics, which brings the MCC and GCC models "into conversation with one another"; no effect sizes are printed in the editorial.

"MCC enabled them to highlight four very frequent patterns, which had some overlap with more general patterns operationalized through GCC, but offering a more vivid and comprehensive image of collaboration in this setting."
