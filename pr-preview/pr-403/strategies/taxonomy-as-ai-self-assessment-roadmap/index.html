Use the taxonomy as a self-assessment and strategic-planning roadmap for institutional AI adoption¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article recommends that educational institutions use the ten-domain taxonomy as a self-assessment tool: by mapping existing AI initiatives to the domains, leaders can identify gaps in their AI strategy and prioritize future investments. It also serves as a roadmap for strategic implementation—for example, a district struggling with resource allocation might focus on the Administrative Efficiency and Organizational Leadership domains. The article states institutions "can use this taxonomy as a self-assessment tool to evaluate their current AI implementation and identify areas for improvement."
