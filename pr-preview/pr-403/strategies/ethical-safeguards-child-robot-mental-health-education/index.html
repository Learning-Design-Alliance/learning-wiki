Ethical governance safeguards for robot-supported mental health education with children¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The article argues that deploying adaptive AI systems with children and emotionally sensitive content requires strict ethical governance. It states that 'ethical acceptability depends not only on "what works," but on how systems are designed, governed, and explained to stakeholders in context', including teachers, parents, and students. Specific safeguards include transparency about the experimental setting and use of external coaches to control robots in boundary-pushing scenarios.

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Strict ethical governance regarding data protection, transparency, and human oversight is necessary when deploying AI components in child-centred educational contexts

Constraints¶

  • Prior work raises concerns about data privacy, student over-surveillance, and children's privacy and data protection in classroom robotics
