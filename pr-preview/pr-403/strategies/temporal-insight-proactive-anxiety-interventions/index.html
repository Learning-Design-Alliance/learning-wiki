Use temporal insights from dynamic emotion data to design proactive interventions addressing anxiety before it undermines enjoyment¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The review recommends leveraging temporal relationships revealed by dynamic analyses to intervene before negative emotions escalate. It suggests interventions grounded in cognitive-behavioural theory, which emphasizes the interconnectedness of thoughts, feelings, and behaviours over time, "By helping students identify and challenge anxiety-provoking thoughts continuously in the learning process", and interventions based on self-determination theory and positive psychology that design learning environments consistently supporting students' psychological needs. This strategy depends on the VAR-style temporal insights described in the article.
