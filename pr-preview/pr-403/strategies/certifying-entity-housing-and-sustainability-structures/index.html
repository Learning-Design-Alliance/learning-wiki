House the certifying entity within structures and policies that promote alignment, buy-in, and sustained funding¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The report recommends couching certification within larger systems of structures and policies that promote coherence and sustainability. Options for housing a certifying entity include "institutions of higher education, nonprofit organizations, and state education agencies," chosen based on staff expertise, resources, and high-level institutional commitment. State and district accountability and reporting mechanisms may need revision to avoid implementation barriers, and because certification is a multiyear intensive process, schools need adequate and sustained funding.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • A potential certifying entity needs sufficient staff with the right expertise and resources, plus commitment at the highest institutional level to sustain a complex, multilayered process.

Constraints¶

  • Stable funding is needed so schools can fully focus on implementation and meeting the needs of students and families.

Target Learners¶

  • K-12 students in community schools

Target Learning Goals¶

  • sustainable implementation of the community schools strategy
