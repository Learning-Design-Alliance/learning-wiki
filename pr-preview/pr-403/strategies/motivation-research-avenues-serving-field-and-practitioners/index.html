Pursue new research avenues on motivation that serve both the field and practitioners¶

Strategy · All strategies
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The commentary's forward-looking recommendation is that the special issue's articles stimulate new research with dual value: work "that would have the potential to advance the field" and that would "also be useful to research professionals working day to day with children and adolescents". New avenues for research are the fourth enumerated element of the commentary, alongside construct integration, interpersonal relationships, and school or cultural contexts.

Design Implications¶

Context¶

Requirements¶

  • Research should connect motivational theory development to the day-to-day work of professionals with children and adolescents

Constraints¶

  • The available text states this as a hope for the special issue, not a tested recommendation

Target Learners¶

  • children and adolescents in school

Target Learning Goals¶

  • academic motivation

Related Strategies¶

  • Advance AI-motivation research through theory-driven, pedagogically explicit, longitudinal designs covering broader motivational constructs
