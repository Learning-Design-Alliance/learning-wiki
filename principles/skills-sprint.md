# Skills Sprint

## Description
Skills sprints are focused, real-world learning experiences for teams where participants acquire new skills by directly designing, developing, or delivering something for their organization. These sprints can enhance leadership, collaboration, cognitive flexibility, and problem-solving abilities through active learning.
