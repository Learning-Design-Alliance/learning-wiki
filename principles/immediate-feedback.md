# Immediate Feedback

## Description
Immediate feedback can improve a learner's confidence, self-awareness, and enthusiasm for learning, leading to increased motivation. Feedback provided at the time of learning helps improve understanding, address misconceptions, reinforce strategies, and support retention in long-term memory. Effective, immediate feedback is timely, ongoing, formative, actionable, specific to the learner, and reflects a positive tone.
