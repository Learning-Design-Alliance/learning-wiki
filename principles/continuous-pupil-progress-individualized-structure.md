# Structure curriculum for continuous pupil progress so each student advances at his own rate

> **Principle** · [All principles](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide's preface promotes individualized instruction through a curriculum structure rather than staffing. It states that "a continuous progress curriculum enables each student to progress at his own rate," fostering success that reinforces positive self-concept and personal, social, and occupational effectiveness. It clarifies that "This concept does not imply a one-to-one teaching ratio, but does offer a curriculum structure which allows for instruction prescribed to meet the needs of the individual" in large-group, small-group, or individual learning situations.
