# Peer Discussion

## Description
Learning in social contexts has been shown to have significant effects on comprehension of material and retention of new information. Having discussions with peers can cement learning for adults, increasing disciplinary literacy and strengthening understanding of new information, including numeracy skills. When learners discuss new concepts, they begin to think more critically, consider alternative perspectives, and create deeper meaning.
