# Self-monitoring

## Description
When adults monitor their comprehension, performance, and use of strategies during learning, they become more invested in their work, build their metacognition, and actively participate in the process. Helping learners self-assess their behavior aids in developing their self-regulation, monitoring comprehension during reading, and strategizing during math tasks. Self-monitoring also supports critical thinking and problem-solving skills.
