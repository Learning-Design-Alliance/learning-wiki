# Inquiry-based Learning

## Description
Inquiry-based learning centers around open-ended questions posed by instructors and/or learners, fostering a learner mindset. Instructors design learning experiences that encourage students to locate, analyze, and present relevant information independently or in small groups. This approach supports and encourages self-regulation, allowing learners to adapt their learning plans according to their needs and interests. Motivation may also increase as learners experience greater autonomy in their learning environment.
