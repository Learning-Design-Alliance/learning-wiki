# Creating Visual Representations

## Description
When learners process and express information visually, they activate more cognitive processes while problem-solving. Creating visible representations of knowledge helps learners fully immerse themselves in content creatively, gaining new insights and deepening understanding. Visual representations of data, such as graphs and charts, also support everyday and scientific numeracy skills. Learners can exhibit what they know in ways that respect their out-of-school literacies and connect to their existing knowledge, increasing motivation and retention of information in long-term memory.
