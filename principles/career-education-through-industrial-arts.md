# Deliver career education through industrial arts so all experiences prepare students for economic independence and the dignity of work

> **Principle** · [All principles](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide's governing principle is that career education should be embedded in existing curriculum disciplines rather than added as a separate subject. It states that "career education, a facet of total education, prepares the individual for a meaningful and productive life," and that industrial arts is the vehicle: "Career education through industrial arts is the primary goal of this Industrial Arts Curriculum Guide." Career concepts are incorporated into planned educational experiences while maintaining the curriculum disciplines as the structural framework.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Maintaining the curriculum disciplines as the structural framework
- Implementation independent of any organizational pattern
#### Constraints
- Designed within the Louisiana State Plan for Career Education context

### Target Learners
- Middle school students in grades 6 through 9 in Louisiana

### Target Learning Objectives
- Preparation for economic independence, personal fulfillment, and appreciation for the dignity of work
