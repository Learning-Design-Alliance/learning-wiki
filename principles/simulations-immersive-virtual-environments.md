# Simulations & Immersive Virtual Environments

## Description
Simulations and immersive virtual environments provide authentic learning experiences that engage multiple senses, fostering curiosity and deeper understanding. These technologies offer rich learning opportunities beyond geographical limitations, which can mitigate the effects of socioeconomic constraints, adverse experiences, and physical challenges. Immersion in such environments can enhance attention, working memory, facilitate the transfer to long-term memory, and support the development of a positive learner mindset.
