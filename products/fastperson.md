## Description
FastPerson is a multimodal video-to-video summarization system developed by Kazuki Kawamura that generates condensed lecture videos while preserving the original speaker's voice and allowing chapter-level switching between summary and full versions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **FastPerson multimodal video summarization system with chapter-level summary/full switching**: FastPerson is a video-to-video summarization system that analyzes both visual and audio streams of lecture videos to generate condensed videos, using voice cloning to maintain the original speaker's voice and preserve continuity with the original lecture. Its interface "allows learners to seamlessly switch between summary and full versions on a chapter-by-chapter basis", letting learners select viewing granularity according to their comprehension level. In evaluation it cut viewing time by 53% on average without a significant quiz-score difference. (Kazuki Kawamura (2025))
