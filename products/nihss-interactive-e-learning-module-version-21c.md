# NIHSS interactive e-learning module (version 21c)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A Creative Commons web-based interactive e-learning module for teaching previously trained healthcare professionals to score the National Institutes of Health Stroke Scale, developed for the NIHSS training intervention.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Highly interactive e-learning pattern combining content-skipping prevention, video-based scoring questions, and tailored feedback**: The article's module uses several learning mechanics per chapter: users are "prevented from skipping content, as they must click on each numbered button to discover the score"; they must "correctly score the NIHSS item" from subtitled video extracts; and "Feedback is provided for each question," with a clue on incorrect answers and reinforcement on correct answers. Branching logic generates the tailored feedback, and chapters may be taken in any order. (Suppan M et al. (2021))
- **NIHSS interactive e-learning module (version 21), freely available online**: A 16-chapter Articulate Storyline module teaching the NIHSS, accessible "on regular computers as well as on smartphones and tablets." It was the experimental learning material in this randomized trial and outperformed the didactic video on the 50-question quiz. The article states that "This e-learning module, along with its previously studied iteration, can be accessed freely on the internet," and its curated dataset is available on Mendeley Data. (Suppan M et al. (2021))
- **Interactive NIHSS e-learning module (184 slides, chapter-per-item structure)**: A self-paced e-learning module of "184 highly interactive slides" built in Storyline 3 and available at https://e-learning.nihss-study.ch, divided into chapters, one per NIHSS item. Each chapter has a scoring description, an illustration of patient testing, and a summary of scoring and pitfalls. Challenging questions open each chapter "prior to displaying key learning material to stimulate curiosity", wrong answers trigger cues or explanations, and completion unlocks a practice quiz. It was compared against the Lyden subtitled video in the RCT. (Koka A et al. (2020))
