# Three-group competency system for modeling competence: general scientific, common professional, and specific professional

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A competency framework proposed by E.O. Modlo for organizing electromechanics bachelor's modeling competence into general scientific, common professional, and specific professional groups.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Three-group competency system for modeling competence: general scientific, common professional, and specific professional**: Built from analysis of the Ukrainian branch standard, Ontario program standards, Human Resource Systems Group classifications, and the European VQTS II competence matrix, the article proposes its own competency system. It concludes that "система компетенцій бакалавра електромеханіки в моделюванні включає в себе три групи компетенцій: загальнонаукові" (general scientific: applied mathematics, ICT, fundamental sciences), common professional (applying different ways of representing models, critical thinking, solving professional problems with ICT, electric machines), and specific professional (modeling of electric power objects and electromechanical systems, analysis of processes in power equipment, decision-making on operating regimes, and application of steady-state and transient analysis for accident prevention). (E.O. Modlo (2015))

- [Experts rate the contributions of common professional and specific professional competencies to modeling competence as equal (35.3% each), exceeding general scientific (29.4%)](../claims/expert-contributions-three-competency-groups-electromechanics.md) [+M]

- [Within the general scientific group applied mathematics competencies lead (38.3%), and within the common professional group critical thinking leads (28.2%)](../claims/applied-mathematics-and-critical-thinking-lead-groups.md) [+W]
