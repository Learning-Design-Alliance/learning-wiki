# Viana tablet-PC video analysis application

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Viana is a free iOS tablet application for recording, tracking, analyzing, and visualizing motion in physics experiments, developed for educational video analysis.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Viana tablet-PC video analysis application as a digital experimental tool**: Viana is a video analysis application for tablet PCs used as the treatment's digital experimental tool. It "combines all of the single video analysis process steps on one and the same mobile device, from the recording of the moving object in an experiment to the analysis of the movement to the visualization of the relevant physical quantities". The application is available for iOS for free and supports tracking, strobe pictures, tables and diagrams, and manipulation of the coordinate system. (Sebastian Becker et al. (2019))

### Claims
- [Tablet-PC-supported video analysis reduces intervention-induced extraneous cognitive load compared with traditional experimental tools in regular physics lessons](../claims/video-analysis-reduces-extraneous-cognitive-load.md) [+M]
- [Tablet-PC video analysis improves conceptual understanding of uniform motion relative to a reference system, but not of velocity-as-rate or velocity-as-vector sub-concepts](../claims/video-analysis-improves-reference-system-understanding.md) [+M]
