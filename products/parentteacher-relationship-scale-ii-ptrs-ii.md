# Parent–Teacher Relationship Scale II (PTRS-II)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A parent- and teacher-report assessment developed to measure perceptions of the parent–teacher relationship, including communication, for use in family–school partnership research and evaluation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PTRS-II Communication-to-Other subscale as the congruent-communication measure**: The study measured each dyad member's perception of communication using the Communication-to-Other subscale of the Parent–Teacher Relationship Scale II, which is "composed of ﬁve items rated on a 5-point Likert scale." Parallel items comprise the parent and teacher scales, with items such as "I tell this teacher (parent) when I am pleased" and "I ask this teacher (parent) for suggestions." Internal consistency was reported as α = .92 for parents and α = .87 for teachers. Dyadic congruence scores were then derived from the two members' responses using a distance formula, with higher scores indicating less congruence. (S. Andrew Garbacz et al. (2015))
