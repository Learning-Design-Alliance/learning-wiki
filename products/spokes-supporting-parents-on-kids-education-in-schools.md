## Description
A manualised programme run by trained group leaders that teaches parents of young struggling readers strategies for supporting reading at home.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SPOKES programme: ten weekly group sessions teaching parents Pause Prompt Praise and whole-language reading support strategies**: SPOKES (Supporting Parents On Kids' Education in Schools) is "a series of ten weekly sessions to teach parents strategies for supporting their children’s reading", designed for parents of struggling readers in Year 1. Parents learn the Pause Prompt Praise approach — waiting five seconds before prompting, then praising specific reading behaviour — plus whole-language strategies such as discussing books and relating stories to everyday experience. Sessions include practical activities, group discussion, role play, homework, a library visit and a one-to-one home visit, delivered by pairs of trained group leaders following a manual. (Tracey et al. (2016))

### Claims
- [SPOKES showed no statistically significant effect on primary literacy outcomes (Letter ID, Word ID, Word Attack) at immediate post-test in a pupil-randomised efficacy trial](../claims/spokes-no-posttest-literacy-effect.md) [-M]
- [Boys whose parents took part in SPOKES showed positive follow-up effects of about three months' additional progress at six months and about seven months at twelve months on some reading measures](../claims/spokes-positive-followup-effects-boys.md) [+M]
