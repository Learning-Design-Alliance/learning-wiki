# Argument Evaluation Test (AET)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A two-part assessment developed by Keith E. Stanovich and colleagues that measures evaluation of argument quality while separating it from participants' prior beliefs.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Argument Evaluation Test (AET) with expert-based objective argument quality standard**: A two-part instrument in which participants first rate agreement with 23 target propositions on real social and political issues, then evaluate the strength of a fictitious character's rebuttals to critics on those same propositions, instructed to focus on rebuttal quality and ignore agreement with the belief. Objective argument quality is operationalized as the median of eight experts' judgments, with "The median correlation between the judgments of the eight experts was .74." Expert consistency exceeded that of any of 43 groups of 8 participants, whose mean median correlation was .28. (Keith E. Stanovich et al. (1997))
