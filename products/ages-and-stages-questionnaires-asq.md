# Ages and Stages Questionnaires (ASQ)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A parent-completed developmental screening system developed by Brookes Publishing for assessing young children's communication, gross motor, fine motor, problem-solving, and personal-social development.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Ages and Stages Questionnaire (ASQ) as a cross-cultural developmental assessment tool**: The ASQ is a developmental screening instrument used here as the primary outcome measure for displaced children. It "generally consists of tasks rather than test questions, rendering it easy to administer in cross-cultural settings." It is divided into five categories: gross motor, fine motor, communication, problem solving and personal-social, with children scored on a scale from 0 to 60. The authors selected it over NIH toolbox alternatives because it does not require a computer, measures a broad range of developmental outcomes, and is relatively brief and simple to execute. (Tobin et al. (2015))
