# Kingsbury pictorial primary scale, Pintner combined mental-educational survey test, and Pressey seventh-grade attainment scale

## Description
A set of early group-administered school assessments measuring primary-grade intelligence, class mental status and educational attainments, and seventh-grade subject attainments.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **New group tests presented at the 1920 Chicago meetings: Kingsbury pictorial primary scale, Pintner combined mental-educational survey test, and Pressey seventh-grade attainment scale**: The report describes three newly devised instruments. Kingsbury's group intelligence scale for the primary grades is "pictorial in character and involving no reading, writing, or complicated drawing" and yielded an average correlation of +0.69 with Stanford-Binet mental age of 1300 primary pupils. Pintner's combined mental-educational test, standardized on 4300 cases, affords a measure of class mental status and evaluates attainments in terms of ability to profit by instruction. Pressey presented a scale measuring seventh-grade attainments in history, reading vocabulary, English composition, and arithmetical reasoning, the composition part scored by children's judgments of compositions of known value. (Gates (1921))
