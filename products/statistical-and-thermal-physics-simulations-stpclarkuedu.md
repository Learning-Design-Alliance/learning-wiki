# Statistical and thermal physics simulations (stp.clarku.edu)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A collection maintained by Jan Tobochnik and colleagues of approximately forty modifiable open-source simulations for teaching statistical and thermal physics, accompanied by instructor-adaptable instructional materials and notes.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Approximately forty open-source simulations of statistical and thermal physics systems with modifiable instructional material**: The authors maintain a collection of simulations available from stp.clarku.edu: 'Approximately forty (open source) simulations of systems of interest in statistical and thermal physics are available from <stp.clarku.edu>.' The simulations come with related instructional material that can be easily modified by instructors, and the site also hosts notes by the authors on statistical and thermal physics incorporating simulations and algorithm discussions throughout the text. The simulations are also available as an EPAPS document. (Jan Tobochnik et al. (2007))
