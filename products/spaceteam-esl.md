## Description
Spaceteam ESL is a free mobile multiplayer language-learning game adapted for ESL oral-fluency practice by David Waddington and Walcir Cardoso.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Spaceteam ESL mobile game for fluency development**: Spaceteam ESL is a free mobile game for Android and iOS, adapted from the original Spaceteam game by David Waddington and Walcir Cardoso. In teams of two to four, players "must engage in real-time computer-mediated interaction with other players to navigate a spaceship", giving aloud time-sensitive orders from panels labelled with randomly generated noun-verb-adjective combinations based on vocabulary frequency bands. The study used it as a 15-minute in-class warm-up over six weeks. (Grimshaw et al. (2016))

### Claims
- [Playing Spaceteam ESL as a warm-up produced no significant difference in fluency improvement between treatment and control groups over time](../claims/spaceteam-esl-no-significant-group-difference-fluency.md) [+M]
- [Participant interviews attributed the treatment group's improvement to increased motivation from game play and reduced anxiety about speaking English](../claims/spaceteam-interviews-motivation-anxiety.md) [+W]
