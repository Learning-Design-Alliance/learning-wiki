# Campus Pack wiki

## Description
Campus Pack wiki is a VLE add-on that provides private group workspaces for collaboratively creating, editing, storing, and auditing shared learning documents.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Campus Pack wiki within the VLE for group collaboration on case-based learning records**: The Campus Pack wiki is a VLE add-on described as "Good for group collaboration tasks on same document" with auditable edits showing who contributed, easy addition of pages, and document upload. A wiki was set up for each CBL group within Blackboard, accessible only by group members and the facilitator, and used by some groups to record agreed learning outcomes, share flipchart photos, and build comprehensive revision notes. (Duncan C et al. (2017))
