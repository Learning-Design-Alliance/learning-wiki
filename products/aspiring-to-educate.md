# Aspiring to Educate

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A Pennsylvania state-supported programme that creates multiple pathways into teaching through preparation partnerships and financial supports for high school students, adults, paraprofessionals, and college students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Pennsylvania's Aspiring to Educate program: multiple pathways into teaching with financial supports and district-preparation partnerships**: Aspiring to Educate is Pennsylvania's Grow Your Own-style investment providing "multiple pathways to high school students, adults, paraprofessionals, and college students to become teachers" with financial supports for preparation. It includes a $500K investment in partnerships between districts and teacher preparation programs, and a "Youth Pathway for Aspiring Educators" giving high school juniors a dual-enrollment path into a partnering institution of higher education with upwards of 30 credits available. The brief rates the state green on GYO support, teacher academies/dual enrollment, and district-preparation partnerships. (The Education Trust (2022))
