## Description
Memrise is a web-based and mobile language-learning application with user-created courses, vocabulary presentation, and review activities, developed and operated by Memrise.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Memrise mobile application used as the asynchronous online component of a supplemental blended course**: Memrise is a free web-based and mobile application with community-created courses, used here to teach the first 400 TOEFL words. It "teaches words by first showing students meanings, concepts, pronunciations", followed by timed speed-review quizzes with immediate feedback marking right answers in green and wrong answers in red. The researchers created a course whose content ran parallel with classroom teaching, and learners accessed it via Telegram invitations and submitted screenshots as proof of study. (Saman Ebadi (2018))
