# Smart Kids technology

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Smart Kids technology is an elementary-education package described by Lytvynova that combines electronic textbooks, electronic educational game resources, and structured lesson activities for developing pupils' learning activity and competencies.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Smart Kids technology as a system of methods, forms, and electronic resources for elementary education**: Smart Kids technology is defined by the article as "a system of methods, forms, electronic educational game resources and electronic textbooks to provide education of elementary school pupil". E-textbooks and EEGR are its components, and the technology organizes lesson-stage use of these resources, relay competitions, quests, contests and crosswords, with a health-preserving time limit. The article positions it as a system of active target development of memory, attention, thought and perception of teaching data from the screen. (Lytvynova (2018))
