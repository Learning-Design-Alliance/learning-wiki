## Description
A German small-group preschool training programme developed by Krajewski, Nieding, and Schneider that builds quantity, counting, number, and numerical-relation knowledge through structured, playful sessions using concrete materials.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MZZ training program "Mengen, zählen, Zahlen" (Krajewski, Nieding, & Schneider, 2007)**: MZZ is a German training program promoting early awareness of quantities, numbers and relations between numbers. The article describes it as one that "systematically builds up conceptual knowledge from practising basic skills to instruction in numerical structures" and "offers a clear structure of the numerical space, allowing for effective problem solving strategies". Children use graspable materials that represent the structure of numbers, so the abstract structure is rendered "tangible" and "visible". The program comprises three focal areas corresponding to competence levels I through III, delivered playfully in small groups; the manual specifies 24 sessions of 30 minutes. (Marcus Hasselhorn et al. (2013))
