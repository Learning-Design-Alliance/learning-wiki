# Effective Lifelong Learning Inventory (ELLI)

## Description
A 72-item online self-report assessment developed by the Lifelong Learning Research Centre for measuring learners' dispositions across seven dimensions of lifelong learning and presenting their profiles visually.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Effective Lifelong Learning Inventory (ELLI) with spider-diagram feedback**: ELLI is a 72-item online self-report measuring dispositions in seven areas found to influence competence as an "intentional" learner: resilience, creativity, changing and learning, meaning-making, strategic awareness, critical curiosity, and learning relationships. In this project students took ELLI in the first and last weeks of the semester and received scores as spider diagrams, allowing holistic viewing of initial profiles and, with post-scores added, growth across all seven dimensions as a comparison between two concentric circles. (Shaffer et al. (2015))

### Claims
- [Under-prepared students in a paired reading-plus-content course model made statistically significant pre-to-post gains on six of seven ELLI lifelong learning dimensions, with Learning Relationships the exception](../claims/paired-course-significant-elli-gains-six-dimensions.md) [+M]
