## Description
An observation sheet developed by Androni Alexandra et al. for coding ten observable agentic-engagement behaviours in pre-clinical medical PBL tutor groups.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Agentic engagement observation sheet for pre-clinical medical PBL groups**: A compiled observation sheet listing the ten behaviours identified as embodying agentic engagement in pre-clinical medical education, each characterized as student-initiated or teacher-prompted and unilateral or transactional. The authors state: "We compiled an observation sheet of behaviours that we identified as embodying agentic engagement in the context of pre-clinical medical education." They envision it as a basis for measuring agentic engagement in higher education and for guiding educator training, going beyond the original self-reported scales by involving group observations. (Androni Alexandra et al. (2024))
