# Jackson's learning styles profiler

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A personality-based questionnaire developed at Queensland University that classifies learners into five styles for use in learner modelling and adaptive content and presentation selection.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Jackson's learning styles profiler as a learner-modelling framework with five learning styles**: The paper adopts Jackson's model, developed at Queensland University from research in the psychology of personality, as its learner-modelling basis. It states that "A key premise of Jackson's model is that cognitive strategies redirect Sensation Seeking to predict functional behaviors" and that the model "is based on the most recent researches in psychology of personality." The profiler classifies learners into five styles, each described with characteristic behaviors that the proposed tutor uses to select content and presentation method. (H. Movafegh Ghadirli et al. (2012))
