## Description
A personality-based questionnaire developed at Queensland University that classifies learners into five styles for use in learner modelling and adaptive content and presentation selection.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Jackson's learning styles profiler as a learner-modelling framework with five learning styles**: The paper adopts Jackson's model, developed at Queensland University from research in the psychology of personality, as its learner-modelling basis. It states that "A key premise of Jackson's model is that cognitive strategies redirect Sensation Seeking to predict functional behaviors" and that the model "is based on the most recent researches in psychology of personality." The profiler classifies learners into five styles, each described with characteristic behaviors that the proposed tutor uses to select content and presentation method. (H. Movafegh Ghadirli et al. (2012))
- **Jackson-model five learning styles as the basis for learner adaptation**: The article bases adaptation on five learning styles from the Jackson model: Sensation Seeking, Goal Oriented Achievers, Emotionally Intelligent Achievers, Conscientious Achievers, and Deep Learning Achievers. Each style is characterised behaviourally; for example, Sensation Seeking learners "would rather to explore their environment by themselves and also learn by test and error," while Deep Learning Achievers may learn well when aware of a note's value. The system recognises the learner's style and adapts content presentation accordingly. (Hossein Movafegh Ghadirli et al. (2012))
- **Jackson's learning styles profiler as the learner-modeling framework, with five learning styles**: The article adopts Jackson's learning styles profiler as the pedagogical basis for learner modeling, grounding it in the psychology of personality. The framework classifies learners into five styles printed in the article's Table 4: Sensation Seeking (SS), Goal Oriented Achievers (GOA), Emotionally Intelligent Achievers (EIA), Conscientious Achievers (CA) and Deep Learning Achievers (DLA). The authors state the profiler was adopted in order to model learning styles of the learners, and that a key premise is that cognitive strategies redirect Sensation Seeking to predict functional behaviors. (H. Movafegh Ghadirli et al. (2012))
