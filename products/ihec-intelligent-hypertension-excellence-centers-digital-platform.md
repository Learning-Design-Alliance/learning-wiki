# iHEC (Intelligent Hypertension Excellence Centers) digital platform

## Description
A digital hypertension diagnosis and management platform launched by the China Hypertension Alliance and led by the Shanghai Institute of Hypertension, providing digitized patient profiles, trajectory monitoring, clinical decision support, and chronic disease management tools.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **iHEC (Intelligent Hypertension Excellence Centers) digital platform used as an interactive residency learning environment**: The iHEC platform, launched by the China Hypertension Alliance and led by the Shanghai Institute of Hypertension, uses "Bluetooth, wireless remote networking, and wide-area network technologies" to support hypertension diagnosis and management. In this study it was repurposed as a teaching environment: residents managed real-time digitized patient profiles, performed dynamic blood pressure trajectory monitoring, used embedded clinical decision-support systems, and constructed chronic disease management plans, reviewing a minimum of 15 authentic digital hypertension cases per rotation (average 18.4 ± 2.6), with bi-weekly supervisor review and a 30-min weekly structured debrief. (Xie Dili et al. (2026))
