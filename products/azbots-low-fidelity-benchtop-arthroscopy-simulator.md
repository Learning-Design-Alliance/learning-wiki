# AZBOTS low-fidelity benchtop arthroscopy simulator

## Description
A Marui & Co., Ltd. simulator that provides surgical trainees with eight instrument-based arthroscopy task boxes, timed practice, auditory error alarms, and completion scoring.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **AZBOTS low-fidelity benchtop arthroscopy simulator with eight task boxes**: The AZBOTS simulator (Marui & Co., Ltd.) is "a low-fidelity device developed for training surgeons in various arthroscopic procedures". It contains 8 task boxes covering inspection, touching, loose-body removal, size estimation, cutting, pinch-and-connection, going over the line, and pinch-and-pulling, using actual surgical instruments. Training is guided by time limits and auditory error alarms, with completion scores displayed after each task. In the study, AZBOTS participants completed the 8 task boxes sequentially for 90 minutes, practicing inspection, instrument handling, and object manipulation. (Kutsuna Tatsuhiko et al. (2025))
