# Student Writing Assessment Literacy (SWAL) scale

## Description
A 25-item five-point Likert questionnaire developed by Xu et al. to measure L2 students' knowledge, beliefs, behaviors, and critical engagement concerning writing assessment and feedback.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Four-dimensional framework of student writing assessment literacy (knowledge, belief, behavior, critique)**: Student writing assessment literacy (SWAL) is conceptualized in the L2 writing context as a construct with four dimensions measured by the Xu et al. (2023) scale. The article states the scale was "incorporatingfourdimensions:knowledge,belief,behavior,andcritique." The knowledge dimension covers understanding of assessment methods, purposes and drawbacks; belief covers perceptions of how writing assessments affect skills, emotions and engagement; behavior covers strategies, self-reflection and feedback use; critique covers critical thinking and discussion about assessment results and feedback. In this study the scale (25 items, 5-point Likert) served as the continuous predictor of the three psychological factors. (Siyi Cao et al. (2025))
