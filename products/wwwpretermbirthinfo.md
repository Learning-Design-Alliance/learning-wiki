## Description
A freely available, non-commercial interactive e-learning resource developed by Johnson and colleagues that provides reusable learning objects on preterm birth for teachers and education professionals.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Interactive e-learning resource about preterm birth for education professionals (www.pretermbirth.info)**: A freely available, interactive e-learning resource (www.pretermbirth.info) comprising five discrete reusable learning objects (RLOs) covering what preterm birth is, educational, cognitive/motor, and behavioural/social/emotional outcomes, and how professionals can support affected children. RLOs combine "audio, video, animation, text, images, case studies and quizzes designed to engage the user in active learning and allowing individuals to set their own pace of learning". The resource represents about one hour of learning activity and is available non-commercially. (Johnson S et al. (2019))

### Claims
- [Teachers' median PB-KS scores rose from 13 pre-resource to 29 post-resource use in one uncontrolled pre-post study, a 2.6 SD pre-post difference](../claims/elearning-resource-increases-teacher-preterm-knowledge.md) [+M]
- [Teachers' self-reported confidence in supporting the learning of children born preterm rose significantly from before to after using the e-learning resource in one uncontrolled pre-post study](../claims/elearning-resource-increases-teacher-confidence.md) [+M]
- [Teachers evaluated the e-learning resource's utility very positively, with 97% reporting they would recommend it to others](../claims/elearning-resource-utility-rated-positively.md) [+M]
