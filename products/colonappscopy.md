## Description
A smartphone preparation-support app developed by Sewitch MJ et al. for patients preparing for screening colonoscopy, providing tailored instructions, reminders, alerts, and accessibility features.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **colonAPPscopy smartphone app for colonoscopy preparation**: colonAPPscopy is a smartphone app developed from the focus group findings to support patients preparing for colonoscopy. Its content areas cover colonoscopy description and purpose, food, liquid, and medication restrictions, preparation logistics, clear liquid diet and laxative instructions, and "what to expect postcolonoscopy including when to seek medical help". Features include check boxes or drop-down menus for simple data input, daily tailored reminders and alerts beginning 7 days before colonoscopy, disability considerations (resizable content, color contrast, bell notification images), and safety considerations such as red alerts on medications needing cessation. Beta testing for comprehension was conducted with 20 patients before French translation. (Sewitch MJ et al. (2019))
