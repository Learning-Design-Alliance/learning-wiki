# Six-domain AI skills and competencies framework for educators and educational leaders

## Description
A Digital Promise framework organizing AI literacy and readiness competencies for PK–12 educators and educational leaders across technical, analytical, interpersonal, ethical, adaptive, and implementation domains.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Six-dimension model of teachers' AI competence**: Based on a systematic literature review and qualitative interviews with 35 stakeholders, the article identifies six dimensions of AI competence for teachers: Theoretical Knowledge about AI, Legal Framework and Ethics, Implications of AI, Attitude toward AI, Teaching and Learning with AI, and Ongoing Professionalization. The article states that "AI competence in the context of education is a set of skills that enable teachers to ethically responsible develop, apply, and evaluate AI for learning and teaching processes." The model is holistic and multidimensional, distinguishing itself from frameworks that focus on a single construct. (Delcker et al. (2025))
- **DigCompEdu framework adapted for teachers' AI competency across six areas**: The article adapts the European Framework for the Digital Competence of Educators (DigCompEdu) as an educator-specific competency framework defining teachers' key competencies and proficiency levels. It organizes components "within six major areas": professional engagement, digital resources, teaching and learning, assessment, empowering learners, and facilitating learners' digital competency, and discusses how each applies to AI-driven tools such as recommendation engines, chatbots, and learning analytics dashboards. (Davy Tsz Kit Ng et al. (2023))
