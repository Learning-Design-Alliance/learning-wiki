# A-OSATS score

## Description
The A-OSATS score is an expert-consensus, 15-substep observational assessment instrument for rating minimally invasive linear-stapled, hand-sewn intestinal anastomosis performance in surgeons, residents, and medical students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **OSATS-based assessment scale for chest tube insertion with 10 key steps scored on a 5-point Likert scale**: An objective scoring instrument for chest tube insertion built on the OSATS framework and the Hutton et al. chest tube scoring system. An interdisciplinary surgeon team "identified 10 key steps of correct chest tube insertion", prioritising safety first and ergonomics and speed second. Each key step is scored 1 (worst) to 5 (best), giving a maximum of 50 and minimum of 10 points, and the scale can be applied by an on-site rater or by raters viewing recorded video. (Ober J et al. (2018))
- **Modified OSATS scoring tool for chest tube insertion (ten procedural steps, 5-point Likert subscales, 10–50 points)**: A modified Objective Structured Assessment of Technical Skills instrument for chest drain insertion, developed from key steps originally published by Hutton et al. and amended by trauma and general surgeons. "Ten representative procedural steps can be evaluated separately using a 5-Point Likert scale complemented by detailed formulation of performance levels", with substeps adding up to "a maximum total score of 50 points and a minimum score of 10 points". It is intended to standardize expert rating, depict skill levels, and allow individual feedback based on subscore assessment. (Friedrich Mirco et al. (2018))
