## Description

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Modified IMPROVE-based metacognitive instruction program for solving math word problems**: The intervention, titled "Metacognitive instruction for solving math word problems", was based on the IMPROVE program of Mevarech & Kramarski (1997) and the SOLVE IT! cognitive model for self-correcting strategies (Montague et al., 2008). The article describes IMPROVE as "a multidimensional teaching method" with three components: facilitation of metacognitive strategy acquisition, learning in collaborative groups with diverse previous knowledge, and provision of rich corrective information focusing on lower and higher order cognitive processes. It was implemented over one month in four experimental classes. (Eda Vula et al. (2017))
