## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The European Training Requirements (ETR) framework defines minimum standards for competency-based postgraduate specialist education**: The ETRs are European instruments developed under the UEMS that define common frameworks and minimum standards for postgraduate specialist education. They rest on the premise that "the quality of medical care is directly proportional to the quality of medical training" and are grounded in the CanMEDS framework, in which the physician integrates complementary roles. They are updated approximately every five years and set minimum requirements adaptable to national contexts. The 2025 Geriatric Medicine ETR is presented as a coherent, evidence-informed standard promoting harmonisation, mobility and comparability of training. (Romero Ortuño R et al. (2026))
