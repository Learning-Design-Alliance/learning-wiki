# Spanish version of the Academic Motivation Scale (AMS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A Spanish-language self-report assessment developed by Núñez Alonso et al. that measures intrinsic, identified, introjected, and external academic motivation, here validated for mathematics motivation among secondary students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Spanish Academic Motivation Scale used to measure autonomous and controlled motivation**: The measurement instrument used to assess students' motivation toward mathematics across the three waves: the Spanish version of the Academic Motivation Scale (Núñez Alonso et al., 2005). Autonomous motivation was scored from the intrinsic and identified subscales and controlled motivation from the introjected and external subscales, each subscale comprising four items preceded by the question 'Why do you study?'. The article reports that "Sum scores displayed a good internal consistency (α= [.84, .91])". Measurement invariance analyses supported reliable use of the scores across waves. (Santana‐Monagas et al. (2025))
