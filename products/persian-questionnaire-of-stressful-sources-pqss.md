# Persian Questionnaire of Stressful Sources (PQSS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The PQSS is a 29-item Persian-language questionnaire developed by Iranian researchers to measure sources of stress in clinical learning environments among nursing and midwifery students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Persian Questionnaire of Stressful Sources (PQSS) for clinical learning stressors**: The PQSS is a 29-item, four-point Likert questionnaire developed by Iranian researchers that "evaluates the sources of stress in clinical learning environment from nursing and midwifery students’ view point". It measures four dimensions: interpersonal communication (8 items), clinical practices (8 items), unpleasant emotions (7 items) and humiliating experiences (6 items), with total scores ranging from 29 to 116. In this study its reliability was confirmed with a Cronbach's alpha of 0.94, and it was administered alongside the Persian 14-item Cohen Perceived Stress Scale. (Rezaei Behrooz et al. (2020))
