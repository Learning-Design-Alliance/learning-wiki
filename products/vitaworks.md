# Vitaworks

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
Vitaworks is a mobile 3D anatomy application that provides reconstructed anatomical models with interactive rotation, scaling, sectioning, concealment, measurement, and segmentation features for medical education and surgical planning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Vitaworks 3D anatomy software for pheochromocytoma surgical education**: Vitaworks is a mobile-phone 3D anatomy application used in the experimental group. It provides "detailed 3D reconstructed images of the specific PCC and its contiguous anatomical structures, including adjacent organs and major blood vessels". Residents manipulated models through rotation, scaling, sectioning, and concealing, and used measurement and segmentation post-processing features to illustrate spatial relationships between tumor and surrounding tissues. (Wang Dong (2025))
