## Description
LearnLM is Google's Gemini-based language model co-trained to follow pedagogical instructions for interactive tutoring and learning applications.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Two-stage textbook augmentation: personalize the source text first, then transform it into multiple views and assessments**: The article's core framework holds that generative AI can transform a fixed, one-size-fits-all textbook into personalized, multi-representational learning material. As the authors state, "Our textbook transformation and augmentation follows a two step approach": a Text Personalization stage rewrites the material to match learner attributes (grade level via Flesch-Kincaid re-leveling, and one selected personal interest), and a Content Transformations stage creates multiple views (slides, audio-graphic lessons, mind maps, Immersive Text) of that rewritten text, all inheriting its personalization. The approach rests on the learning-science concepts of multiple representations (dual coding theory) and personalization, and treats formative assessment as a driver of self-regulated learning. (LearnLM Team (2025))
