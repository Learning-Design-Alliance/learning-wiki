# Accelerate your Teaching MOOC

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A free online professional-learning course providing teachers and motivated high-school learners with scenarios, videos, animations, and foundational physics content, hosted on the EU SchoolNet platform and sponsored by the European Commission.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Accelerate your Teaching MOOC on the EU SchoolNet platform**: A Massive Open Online Course sponsored by the European Commission, introduced to learners' program participants during the accelerator physics presentation. The report states the MOOC is "accessible for free on the EU SchoolNet platform" and that "Its content covers the foundations of particles, magnetism, forces, light, and electromagnetism." It provides teachers with learning scenarios, short movies, and animations similar to those used in the introduction. (Kétévi A. Assamagan et al. (2024))
