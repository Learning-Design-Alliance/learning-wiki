## Description
UNITSS is a Likert-scale self-report instrument developed by Douwes-van Ark et al. to assess university teachers' self-efficacy across six teaching domains.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **UNITSS: a six-domain taxonomy of university teachers' self‐efficacy beliefs**: The UNIversity Teaching Self‐Efficacy Scale (UNITSS) conceptualizes university teachers' self‐efficacy beliefs across six teaching domains: "creating a positive learning climate, instruction/design, differentiation, student engagement, assessment and professional development". It was built by adapting items from three prior instruments and adding items to cover each domain's conceptualization, with five to eight items per domain scored on a 5‐point Likert scale. The article uses it to profile teachers' efficacy levels and relate them to teacher characteristics. (Douwes‐van Ark et al. (2026))
