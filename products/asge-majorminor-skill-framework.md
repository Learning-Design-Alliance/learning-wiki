# ASGE major/minor skill framework

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A framework developed by the American Society for Gastrointestinal Endoscopy for classifying endoscopic procedures by training complexity and specifying competence and supervision requirements for major skills.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ASGE major/minor skill framework for classifying and training in new endoscopic technologies**: The ASGE framework distinguishes a "major skill", described as "a new technique or procedure that involves a high level of complexity, interpretative ability, and/or new type of technology" requiring formal supervised training, from minor extensions of established procedures acquirable through limited education such as didactic videos and short courses. Competence in a major skill comprises six components, from understanding indications and risks to providing a comprehensive pre- and postprocedural plan. The review applies the framework to classify procedures such as EUS, ERCP, ESD, and POEM as major skills. It stresses the classification is background-dependent: radiofrequency ablation may be minor for an experienced endoscopist but major for a novice. (Yang D. et al. (2020))
