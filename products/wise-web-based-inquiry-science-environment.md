## Description
WISE is a web-based science-inquiry authoring and learning platform developed by the University of California, Berkeley, providing teacher tools for creating, managing, and assessing inquiry projects and student tools for structured collaborative investigation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **WISE (Web-based Inquiry Science Environment) platform for designing and implementing science inquiry activities**: WISE is an online platform from the University of California at Berkeley developed along the lines of the knowledge integration approach. It "provides a teacher's portal, including possibilities for classroom management, student assessment, and creating or editing curriculum projects" and a student interface with an inquiry map structuring activities in steps. It supports inquiry steps such as exploring a simulation, brainstorming, constructing an argument, and reflection and self-assessments. Students work in pairs and all teamwork is stored in a database accessible to teachers and researchers for assessment. The authors used the WISE authoring environment to create a new global climate change curriculum project codesigned with science teachers and technology specialists. (Annelies Raes et al. (2013))
- **WISE (Web-based Inquiry Science Environment) used to deliver a macro-scripted history inquiry in teacher education**: WISE is an online platform for designing, developing, and implementing inquiry-based learning, described as well received by research and practitioner communities. In this study its navigation panel guided students through nine key inquiry activities, from studying historical context to writing a conclusion, with grayed-out buttons preventing access to an activity before completing previous ones. Students entered and stored notes alongside content for each step. (Voet et al. (2018))
