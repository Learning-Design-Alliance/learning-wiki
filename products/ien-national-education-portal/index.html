iEN national education portal¶

Description¶

iEN is a Saudi Ministry of Education education portal that provides students, teachers, principals, and parents with e-textbooks, lessons, revision materials, augmented-reality experiments, games, videos, and other digital resources.

Components¶

  • iEN national education portal with books, revisions, and additional resources sub-portals: The iEN portal is Saudi Arabia's national education portal, built by the Ministry of Education to integrate technology into the educational system. From its home page "users can log in and access all the benefits in the portal, it has three sub-portals: books and lessons, iEN revisions, and additional resources". It offers AR experiments, e-textbooks, learning games, video clips, and TV channels, and offers role-based accounts for students, teachers, principals, and parents. (AlNajdi (2022))
