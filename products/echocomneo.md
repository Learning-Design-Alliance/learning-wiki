## Description
EchoCom|Neo is a simulator for neonatal and infant echocardiography training that combines real 3D echocardiographic datasets, tracked transducer input, a manikin, and a 3D heart model to support image acquisition and congenital-heart-defect recognition.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **EchoCom|Neo, a neonatal and infant echocardiography simulator with real images**: EchoCom|Neo is the second simulator with real images dedicated to echocardiography training in neonates and infants. Like VNETS, it uses 3D echocardiographic datasets acquired by a matrix probe, "which are sliced into cut planes to present as 2D images", with cut planes derived from a tracking sensor in an electromagnetic field, and it displays real-time 2D images alongside a 3D heart model. The review states its efficacy has been tested and established and that it holds a large library of normal hearts and congenital heart diseases. (Singh Yogen et al. (2025))
- **EchoCom Neo echocardiography simulator for neonatal TTE training and assessment**: The EchoCom Neo echocardiography simulator (EchoCom GmbH, Nieheim, Germany) is one of only two neonatal TTE simulators the article says currently exist. It was used both for the standardised training session and for the 3- and 6-month evaluations, set to a normal heart scenario and placed on an open neonatal warmer. The authors note that "The TTE simulator selected for this study used images acquired from real TTE, providing greater reliability than the three‐dimension reconstructions found in other simulators". (Blanchetière Amélie et al. (2025))
