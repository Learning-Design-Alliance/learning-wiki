# QuVis suite of four interactive QKD simulations

## Description
A suite of four HTML5/JavaScript interactive quantum key-distribution simulations developed by the QuVis Quantum Mechanics Visualization Project for undergraduate quantum-mechanics teaching.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **QuVis suite of four interactive QKD simulations (BB84 photons, BB84 spin 1/2, two non-orthogonal states, entangled spin 1/2)**: The article introduces four HTML5/Javascript simulations developed as part of the QuVis Quantum Mechanics Visualization Project to teach the basic principles of quantum key distribution via three protocols, using "polarized single photons and spin 1/2 particles as physical realizations of qubits". They run on a wide range of devices including tablets and PCs, are freely available online or for download, and include challenges tabs with score counters and password-protected instructor solutions. Evaluation with students over three years refined the simulations and activities. (Antje Kohnle et al. (2016))
