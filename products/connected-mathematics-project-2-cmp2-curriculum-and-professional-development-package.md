# Connected Mathematics Project 2 (CMP2) curriculum and professional development package

## Description
Connected Mathematics Project 2 is a problem-centered grades 6–8 mathematics curriculum with student materials, teacher guides, and publisher-provided professional development developed by the Connected Mathematics Project at Michigan State University.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CMP2 curriculum and professional development package**: Connected Mathematics Project 2 is a problem-centered mathematics curriculum for grades 6–8 in which "students progress through a series of units made up of problem-centered investigations that address core mathematics topics." Students are introduced to a problem, work in groups to develop solution strategies, and justify their thinking by presenting solutions to the class, with practice problem sets in each investigation. The accompanying teacher PD is "intended to deepen teacher understanding of mathematics, strengthen their pedagogical knowledge, and develop their ability to use inquiry-based instructional strategies." In the study it was provided with all standard publisher PD and materials. (Martin et al. (2012))
