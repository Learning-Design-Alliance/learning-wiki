Description¶

i-Ready Classroom Mathematics is Curriculum Associates’ core K–8 mathematics curriculum integrating print and digital instructional materials, practice tools, hands-on resources, and professional learning supports.

Components¶

  • i-Ready Classroom Mathematics (iRCL): a K-8 core mathematics curriculum combining print and digital materials: iRCL is described as "a core K–8 math curriculum designed to build conceptual understanding, problem-solving skills, and student engagement." It combines print and digital materials including teacher guides, student workbooks, practice tools, and hands-on manipulables, and integrates professional learning supports for teachers implementing the curriculum. (i-Ready Classroom Mathematics (2024))
  • Curriculum Associates professional development for iRCL: on-site development days and the Online Educator Learning platform: Curriculum Associates offers "research-driven professional development for a variety of different user types," with training for new, practicing, and advanced users. On-site development days are up to six hours, with an annual number of sessions varying by implementation length, and an Online Educator Learning platform provides digital courses available at any time. (i-Ready Classroom Mathematics (2024))
  • Lessons grounded in NCTM Effective Mathematics Teaching Practices emphasizing discourse, reasoning, and real-world connections: iRCL lessons are designed around the Effective Mathematics Teaching Practices from the National Council of Teachers of Mathematics. The page states lessons "emphasize discourse, reasoning, and real-world connections," and the program is closely aligned with grade-level standards and designed to support all learners, including those needing additional scaffolding. (i-Ready Classroom Mathematics (2024))
