Custom GPTs¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 qualitative, 1 design), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Custom GPTs are OpenAI's user-configurable GPT applications that let educators and students create specialized assistants by setting instructions and supplying reference files.

Components¶

  • Customised GPTs system integrating GPT-4 with retrieval-augmented generation of the classroom dialogue analysis scheme: The authors built a customised GPTs application (OpenAI's "My GPTs") that codes classroom dialogue automatically. The system "integrated the GPT-4.0 API" and used "retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), we vectorized the classroom dialogue analysis scheme (CDAS) and fed these knowledge vectors into GPT-4", with a tuned prompt-engineering mechanism providing category definitions and inclusion/exclusion rules. It annotates dialogue turns autonomously under the same coding scheme used by human experts. (Long et al. (2024))

Claims¶

  • Students reported that continued interaction with a cGPT improved its adherence to instructions [+W]
  • A customised GPT-4 agreed with a human coder on over 90% of coded dialogue turns in both math and Chinese lessons [+M]
  • Human–GPT coding agreement varied sharply by code, with near-chance or negative Kappa for co-ordination codes despite near-perfect agreement on others [~M]
