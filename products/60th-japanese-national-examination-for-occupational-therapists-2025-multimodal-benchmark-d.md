# 60th Japanese National Examination for Occupational Therapists (2025) multimodal benchmark dataset

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A dataset of 199 official Japanese occupational-therapy licensing-exam questions, including text-only, visually based, and case-based items, released and used by Saita Kazuya et al. to benchmark multimodal LLMs.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **60th Japanese National Examination for Occupational Therapists (2025) as an LLM benchmark dataset**: The 60th JNEOT (February 2025) is a publicly available national licensing exam published by the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare of Japan, used here as a benchmark for multimodal LLMs. After excluding one officially withdrawn question, "a total of 199 questions were scored, including 177 text-only questions and 22 visually-based questions." Questions span basic sciences and OT-specific content, with 40 practical case-based questions and visual materials such as clinical photographs, CT, MRI and illustrations. (Saita Kazuya et al. (2026))
