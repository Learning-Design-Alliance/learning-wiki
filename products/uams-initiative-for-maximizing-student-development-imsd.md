# UAMS Initiative for Maximizing Student Development (IMSD)

## Description
A University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences programme that supports underrepresented biomedical Ph.D. trainees through peer mentoring, transition programming, competency-based portfolio management, role-model seminars, and active-learning coursework.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **UAMS IMSD program: a bundle of curricular and co-curricular support initiatives for UR biomedical Ph.D. trainees**: The UAMS Initiative for Maximizing Student Development is a NIH-NIGMS funded program to increase the pool of individuals from underrepresented groups earning biomedical science Ph.D.s and transitioning into research careers. Its stated activities are "1) a formal peer mentoring: 2) a five-week summer transition program; 3) competency-based academic portfolio management; 4) a seminar series highlighting minority role models, and 5) active learning-based biochemistry course." Across 10 years (2008–2018), UR trainees made up 21% of biomedical doctoral enrollment on average with a mean GPA of 3.25. (Williams Tremaine B. et al. (2021))
