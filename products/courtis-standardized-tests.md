# Courtis standardized tests

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A set of standardized arithmetic tests developed by Stuart A. Courtis for measuring performance in the four fundamental operations and simple reasoning against age- and grade-based standards.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Courtis standardized tests for measuring and interpreting results in the four fundamental operations and in simple reasoning**: The article identifies the tests of Mr. Courtis as the best available standardized tests for arithmetic, noting in a footnote that "They afford means of measuring and interpreting results in the four fundamental operations and in simple reasoning." The tests are presented as usable by class teachers without special psychological or statistical training, both for evaluating teaching methods and for determining the needs and progress of pupils relative to definite standards for boys and girls of the same age and grade. (Stone (1913))
