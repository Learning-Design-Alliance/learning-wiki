# Springboard Summer program model

## Description
Springboard Summer is an intensive 5-week summer literacy program for rising kindergartners through fourth graders aimed at reversing summer reading slide. It combines daily reading instruction built on a "5-component literacy block (read aloud, shared reading, word work, differentiated reading & literacy centers, and writing period)", weekly family workshops training parents to teach reading at home, initial home visits, teacher training and coaching supports, and "an incentive structure that awards learning tools to families in proportion to scholar reading gains." The evaluation studied districts implementing this model with high fidelity in 2018 and 2019.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Implemented with fidelity to the program model, including the three-pronged approach of student-facing, family-involvement, and teacher-capacity elements
#### Constraints
- Districts using assessments that do not readily measure incremental progress, such as i-Ready, were excluded from the evaluation sample

### Target Learners
- Rising kindergarten through fourth grade students, primarily from low-income families

### Target Learning Goals
- Reading on grade level by fourth grade
- Reversing summer reading slide
