Vocabulary Enrichment Full Programme (VEFP)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A Bolton Local Authority literacy catch-up programme for low-attaining Year 7 pupils that combines VEIP, Sounds-Write, and Literacy Plus, with VEIP as its principal component.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • The Vocabulary Enrichment Full Programme showed no statistically significant impact on Year 7 pupils' reading ability in a pupil-randomised controlled trial [+M]
  • Schools combined the three intervention components with considerable variation, and the evaluators concluded the programme would have benefited from a pilot stage [~M]
