# WriteSim TCExam

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A computer-based formative-assessment simulation developed by Shah Jatin et al. for novice researchers to practise scientific-writing structure and content placement through question items, immediate explanatory feedback, and second attempts.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Computer-aided formative assessment with immediate corrective feedback as the mechanism for writing simulation**: The article grounds WriteSim TCExam in computer-aided formative assessment: assessment aimed at improving learning rather than grading it, delivered through question-and-answer items with instantaneous feedback that clarifies concepts and provides positive reinforcement. The authors argue that the difficulty users experience in distinguishing ideal from less-than-ideal text structures simulates the challenges novice writers face on their first manuscripts, and that a second-chance mechanism encourages brainstorming. They cite the behavioral-science tradition of reinforcement through immediate feedback in computer-based instruction as precedent. (Shah Jatin et al. (2010))
