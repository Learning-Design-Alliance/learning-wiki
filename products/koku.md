## Description
An NHS-approved tablet-delivered falls-prevention programme co-developed with older adults and falls-prevention experts, combining progressive strength-and-balance exercises with eCoach guidance, safety information, reminders, progress tracking, and health-literacy games for older adults.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Keep-On-Keep-Up (KOKU) digital falls prevention programme**: KOKU is an NHS-approved, tablet-delivered digital programme offering "a progressive strength and balance programme with animated characters (eCoaches) providing instructions, guidance, safety information, automated reminders and in-app progress tracking", alongside health literacy games on nutrition, hydration and falls awareness. It was co-developed with older adults and falls prevention experts using behaviour change techniques and MRC complex-intervention guidance. In the trial it was used 20–30 minutes, three times a week for 12 weeks at home. (French Chloe et al. (2026))

# Keep-On-Keep-Up (KOKU) digital falls prevention programme

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
KOKU is an NHS-approved, tablet-delivered digital programme offering "a progressive strength and balance programme with animated characters (eCoaches) providing instructions, guidance, safety information, automated reminders and in-app progress tracking", alongside health literacy games on nutrition, hydration and falls awareness. It was co-developed with older adults and falls prevention experts using behaviour change techniques and MRC complex-intervention guidance. In the trial it was used 20–30 minutes, three times a week for 12 weeks at home.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A tablet device and initial training at baseline to familiarise participants with using the programme, with ad hoc technical support available
#### Constraints
- Several participants faced technical glitches and confusion regarding the dose of each exercise; the programme was felt by some to offer insufficient challenge for higher-functioning users and to feel relatable only to White Caucasian older adults

### Target Learners
- community-dwelling older adults aged 60 years and over

### Target Learning Goals
- strength and balance exercise capability
- health literacy around fall prevention, nutrition and hydration
