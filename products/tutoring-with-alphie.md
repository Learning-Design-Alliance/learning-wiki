## Description
Tutoring with Alphie is a computer-assisted paired-reading literacy programme developed for struggling readers, using paired pupil roles, adult monitoring, and software-guided fluency activities.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Tutoring with Alphie computer-assisted paired reading programme**: Tutoring with Alphie is a computer literacy tutoring programme in which pupils work in pairs at a computer, alternating as reader and coach, monitored by an adult tutor (a teaching assistant in all trial schools). Pupils are paired by an inbuilt reading assessment, follow two-week tutoring plans of animated fluency and expression activities, and coaches evaluate responses with software-provided instructional support; teams cannot advance until both pupils master a level. It was adapted from a US programme for younger struggling readers. (Centre for Effective Education (2015))

### Claims
