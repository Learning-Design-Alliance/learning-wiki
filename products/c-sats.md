## Description
C-SATS is a commercial platform that securely captures operating-room video and coordinates blinded reviewer assessment of surgical technical skill using the GEARS rubric.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **C-SATS crowd-sourced video assessment platform with GEARS rubric**: C-SATS is a commercial, Seattle-based platform that acquires secure operating-room video and performs blinded skill assessments using the Global Evaluative Assessment of Robotic Skills (GEARS) rubric, which "assesses 6 separate domains—depth perception, bimanual dexterity, efficiency, autonomy, force sensitivity, and robotic control" on a 5-point scale with performance anchors at 1, 3, and 5, averaged across evaluators. Its methodology of training non-expert medical personnel as reviewers has been validated in other settings. In this study it was used to score 70 RAC and RIHR videos from 48 surgeons. (Laverty Robert B. et al. (2025))
