SYMP Test¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The SYMP Test is a group-administered, timed paper-and-pencil assessment created by Brankaer et al. for measuring one- and two-digit symbolic numerical magnitude comparison fluency in primary-school children.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • The SYMP Test shows satisfactory test-retest reliability, with overall-score correlations between .72 and .86 across Grades 1–6 [+M]
  • SYMP Test performance correlates moderately and significantly with mathematics achievement in every grade of primary school, for both one-digit and two-digit comparison [+M]
  • Typically achieving children outperform children with mathematical learning difficulties on the SYMP one-digit subtest in Grades 1–5 and the two-digit subtest in Grades 2–5, but not in Grade 6 [+M]
