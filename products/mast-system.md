## Description
A two-year Mathematics and Science Teaching professional-development programme run by Loyola Marymount University's Center for Math and Science Teaching for in-service secondary mathematics and science teachers, combining workshops, coaching, observation, rubrics, feedback, and evaluation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MAST archive of Kepler light curves as a publicly accessible classroom data source**: The Mikulski Archive for Space Telescopes (MAST) is a publicly accessible web interface holding the light curves of transiting exoplanet systems detected by the Kepler satellite. The article shows how to query it — "in the ”Target Name” ﬁeld, enter the ID of the desired object (for example, Kepler-2)" — and then "read the ﬂux and detection time values" needed for calculations by moving the cursor, with corrected (PDCSAP) and uncorrected (SAP) flux curves displayed. (Ollé et al. (2022))

- [During the two-year MAST intervention, the seven Alliance schools' percentage of students at or above Proficient in CST mathematics rose far more than state or district trends](../claims/mast-cst-math-gains-exceed-state-district.md) [+M]
- [Student mathematics achievement normalized changes from the final intervention year to two years later were +0.15 (Advanced or Proficient) and -0.33 (below Basic) in the five schools that improved during MAST](../claims/mast-gains-sustained-post-intervention.md) [+M]
- [Schools whose teachers scored higher on MAST rubrics of adaptive-expert strategies showed larger student CST mathematics gains than low-rubric schools](../claims/mast-rubric-scores-track-student-gains.md) [+M]
