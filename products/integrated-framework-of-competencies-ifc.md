## Description
A Delphi-developed framework by Aleo Giuseppe et al. that organises transversal teamwork competencies for community-based interprofessional health and social care teams.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Integrated Framework of Competencies (IFC) for community-based interprofessional teamwork**: The IFC is the competency framework developed in the study's preliminary phase and refined through the e-Delphi rounds. It began as "a preliminary Integrated Framework of Competencies (IFC) was developed, consisting of 63 proposed core competencies divided into 5 areas" and was restructured after Round 2 into the final five-area structure covering teamwork, communication and conduct, shared vision, digital health, and care planning. It organises competencies by general thematic area, name, and brief description, structured to be articulated into learning outcomes using ECVET action verbs. (Aleo Giuseppe et al. (2026))
