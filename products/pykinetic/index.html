PyKinetic¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

PyKinetic is an Android mobile tutor developed by Fabic et al. for novice learners of Python programming through Parsons problems and menu-based self-explanation prompts.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Both experimental and control groups improved significantly on all questions and conceptual questions from pre- to post-test after using PyKinetic [+M]
  • Low prior knowledge students learned more from PyKinetic's Parsons problems than high prior knowledge students, especially in the experimental group with SE prompts [+M]
