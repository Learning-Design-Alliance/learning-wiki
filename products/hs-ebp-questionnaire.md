# HS-EBP questionnaire

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 60-item five-domain self-report questionnaire developed by Yeh et al. to assess evidence-based practice competence and factors affecting its improvement among health science professionals.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **50-item EBP questionnaire adapted and validated for Colombian physical therapists**: A 50-item screening questionnaire estimating attitudes, beliefs, knowledge, and skills regarding EBP, developed in the USA by Jette et al. and validated in Spanish by Guerra et al. The article states "This instrument has been adapted and was validated previously in Colombia by Flórez-López et al." Items are grouped into sub-dimensions covering attitudes and beliefs, interest and motivation, academic background and skills, use of and access to clinical practice guidelines, perceived barriers, and demographics, using a 0-4 ordinal scale, dichotomous responses, and three-option comprehension items. (Ramírez-Vélez R et al. (2015))
