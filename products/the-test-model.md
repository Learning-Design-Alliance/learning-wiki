# The TEST model

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A clinical teaching framework developed by Glick et al. that guides health-care educators to diagnose a learner's need, teach rapidly to it, and provide feedback during time-limited clinical encounters.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The TEST model (diagnose the learner, teach rapidly, provide feedback) as a framework for teaching health care disparities in time-limited clinical settings**: The Time-Effective Strategies for Teaching (TEST) model is an established clinical-teaching model the authors adapt to disparities education. It structures teaching in three steps: "diagnose the learner's instructional need, then teach rapidly to that need and provide feedback." The paper argues it is "straightforward, easy to use," applicable in both inpatient and outpatient settings, and responsive to time-limited environments, and illustrates each component with clinical case scenarios. (Glick SB et al. (2010))
