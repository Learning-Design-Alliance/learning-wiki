# Swift formal education curriculum materials

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
NASA's Swift mission formal-education curriculum package comprises standards-aligned classroom guides, units, posters, videos, and related materials for middle-school through early-college science learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Swift formal education curriculum materials (GEMS Invisible Universe guide, Gamma-ray Burst Educator's Guide, Newton's Laws poster set, Eyes Through Time curriculum)**: A set of NASA Product Review-approved, standards-aligned classroom materials linking Swift mission science to National Science Education Content Standards A, B, and D for grades 7-14. The products include the GEMS Invisible Universe guide for grades 6-8 (over 6500 copies sold), the Gamma-ray Burst Education Unit for grades 9-14 (more than 3000 distributed through educator workshops), the Newton's Laws poster set for grades 5-8 (more than 7000 sets distributed and downloaded more than 27,000 times in 2012), and the six-unit Eyes Through Time curriculum for grades 5-8 with accompanying videos. (Lynn Cominsky et al. (2014))
