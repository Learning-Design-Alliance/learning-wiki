# 30-item ICU nurses' postextubation swallowing-disorders knowledge, attitude, and practice questionnaire

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (8 for) · 3 studies (3 associational), `q2` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
A Delphi-developed and psychometrically tested questionnaire for measuring tertiary-hospital ICU nurses' knowledge, attitudes, and practices concerning postextubation swallowing disorders.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Questionnaire on ICU Nurses' Knowledge, Attitude, and Practice of Postextubation Swallowing Disorders in Patients with Tracheal Intubation**: A 30-item, three-dimension (knowledge, attitude, practice) questionnaire developed through an initial item pool, two rounds of Delphi expert correspondence, item analysis, and reliability testing. The article reports "Cronbach's alpha coefficient was 0.901, and the retest reliability was 0.990," with item content validity (I-CVI) ranging from 0.88 to 1.00 and S-CVI/UA of 0.90. It was used to survey 627 ICU nurses and includes a demographic section covering gender, age, nationality, education, title, job satisfaction, employment mode, and night shifts. (Sun Shanshan et al. (2024))
