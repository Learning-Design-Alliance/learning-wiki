# NC Child Treatment Program

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A North Carolina statewide programme that trains and consults licensed clinicians in trauma-focused assessment and TF-CBT, certifies treatment fidelity, and connects trained clinicians with children and families through a public roster.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **NC Child Treatment Program training and treatment platform with public clinician roster**: A statewide dissemination platform created to improve community mental health care for maltreated and traumatized children by combining clinical training with public health and implementation-science strategies. Critical design elements included recruiting licensed clinicians, providing training and consultation in trauma-specific assessment, TF-CBT and implementation principles, standardizing fidelity and outcome criteria for certification, and maintaining "a public roster to link children and families with trained clinicians". Graduates were invited to join the publicly accessible web-based TF-CBT clinician roster searchable by county. (Amaya-Jackson L et al. (2018))
