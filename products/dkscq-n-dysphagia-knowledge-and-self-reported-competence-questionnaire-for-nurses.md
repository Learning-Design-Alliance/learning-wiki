# DKSCQ-N (Dysphagia Knowledge and Self-reported Competence Questionnaire for Nurses)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A 21-item, 11-point questionnaire developed by Mubeen Raffa et al. to assess hospital nurses' dysphagia knowledge and self-reported care competence across six domains.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **DKSCQ-N: Dysphagia Knowledge and Self-reported Competence Questionnaire for Nurses**: The DKSCQ-N is a 21-item questionnaire rated on an 11-point scale (0–10) assessing nurses' dysphagia knowledge and self-reported competence. It covers six domains: anatomy and physiology, risk factors, signs and symptoms, complications, screening and management. The knowledge section uses a graded response format with correct responses and incorrect distractors rated separately, allowing assessment of partial knowledge and misconceptions. Domain scores are converted to percentages for standardisation from 0 to 100. (Mubeen Raffa et al. (2026))
