## Description
A 30-item multiple-choice English reading-comprehension achievement test developed by Mohammad Mahmoud Talal Mohaidat for ninth-grade EFL students and validated for measuring three levels of comprehension.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Researcher-built 30-item reading comprehension test covering three comprehension levels**: The study's measurement instrument is a multiple-choice reading achievement test constructed from the taught texts and the grade 9 Teacher Guide. Items were written "in accordance with the three reading comprehension levels (reading lines, reading between lines, reading behind the lines)". After jury validation and piloting, five items were dropped, leaving 30 items (10, 11 and 9 across the three levels), scored 0-30. Test-retest reliability over two months was r = 0.89. (Mohammad Mahmoud Talal Mohaidat (2018))
