# Gravitymancer

## Description
Gravitymancer is a student-facing tool developed by Reichart and collaborators for retrieving official LIGO/Virgo data products and modeling gravitational-wave events.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Gravitymancer tool for student modeling of official LIGO/Virgo gravitational-wave data products**: Gravitymancer is a tool the authors built for Module 5C that lets students work with real gravitational-wave detections. The article states: "We have built a tool that allows students to grab the official data products and model them themselves, measuring the masses and distance to merging objects, and more." Students use frequency-vs-time plots to constrain merging masses and strain-vs-time plots to constrain distance and phase, and they reproduce the GW 170817 optical counterpart discovery from images of about 40 galaxies. (Reichart (2024))
