A¶

  • A guide for states on evaluating ARRA-funded programs and other educational reforms - A jointly developed evaluation-planning guide from the American Institutes for Research and Mathematica Policy Research to help state education agencies evaluate ARRA-funded initiatives and other educational reforms.

  • A Toolkit to Support Evidence-Based Writing Instruction in Grades 2 Through 4 (Historical) - A historical toolkit providing evidence-based writing-instruction guidance, tip sheets, and professional-development supports for grades 2–4 teachers and school leaders.
  • A2i - A Learning Ovations-partnered system that uses online assessments, instructional recommendations, lesson-planning support, and professional development to help Pre-K through Grade 3 teachers individualize literacy instruction.

  • Absence Reports - Absence Reports is an attendance-focused intervention delivered by school districts to provide absence information and support improved student attendance.
  • Academic integrity indicator framework: 130 items organised under eight dimensions of academic integrity - A framework developed by Hamish Coates and colleagues that measures and reports institutional academic-assessment integrity across eight dimensions using 130 indicators.

  • Accelerate, TN ALL Corps, and Vetted Texas Tutor Corps - These are statewide intensive-tutoring programmes run by Louisiana, Tennessee, and Texas education agencies, respectively, with dedicated public funding, approved or supported providers, tutor training, instructional materials, and implementation assistance.
  • Accelerated Reader - Accelerated Reader™ is Renaissance's software-based K–12 reading practice and assessment system in which students read books, complete computerized quizzes, and receive progress data for teacher monitoring and goal setting.
  • Accelerated Study in Associate Programs (ASAP) - A three-year City University of New York programme that combines financial, academic, cohort-based, and personal supports to help community-college students complete associate degrees.

  • Achieve3000 - Achieve3000® is a supplemental online literacy platform developed and provided by Achieve3000 that delivers differentiated nonfiction reading and writing instruction for learners from preK through grade 12.
  • AdaPT - AdaPT is a Vue.js and TypeScript NestJS web application developed by Yanjie Zhang and colleagues that structures existing lesson plans, analyzes student learning profiles, and supports teacher-in-the-loop adaptation for differentiated K–12 instruction.
  • AdaptED Stories - A practitioner-facing web application developed by the AdaptED Stories research team for authoring, personalizing, and delivering Social Stories to autistic children, with practitioner review, reading-session supports, and analytics.
  • Adaptive Through-Year Assessment (ATYA) - NWEA's proposed through-year adaptive assessment system designed to measure both grade-level standards proficiency and academic growth while replacing an end-of-year state summative assessment.
  • Admiral Squad - Admiral Squad is a Middletown City Schools fellowship and recruitment initiative that supports, develops, and helps hire Black male educators in the district.
  • Adult Promise initiative - A Lumina Foundation multiyear state-grant program launched in 2017 to engage and support adults ages 25 to 64 in postsecondary education, with Mathematica serving as its learning and evaluation partner from 2017 to 2021.
  • AgentSchool - AgentSchool is an LLM-powered multi-agent simulation system for modeling, inspecting, and comparing student–teacher interactions and learning trajectories in simulated educational settings.
  • AI & Data Acumen Learning Outcomes Framework - A University of Arizona faculty, student, and staff task force framework that crosses seven AI-and-data knowledge dimensions with four cognitive proficiency levels to support higher-education curriculum development and assessment.
  • AI Ecological Education Policy Framework - A framework proposed by Chan for guiding institutional AI integration through pedagogical, governance, and operational dimensions.
  • AI-Enabled EdTech Adoption Cycle - A four-phase adoption framework described by Ruiz et al.
  • AiAWE - AiAWE is an open-source Django platform developed by John Maurice Gayed and collaborators for real-time and batch automated essay scoring, custom-rubric feedback, and formative writing support.
  • AICoFe - AICoFe is a human-centered AI collaborative feedback system developed by Alvaro Becerra and colleagues that supports peer, self-, and teacher assessment through customized rubrics, multimodal performance recording, dashboards, and mediated AI-generated feedback.
  • AICoFe Visualization Module - AICoFe's Visualization Module is a Dash-based learning-analytics dashboard system developed by Becerra et al.
  • Air Tutors - Air Tutors is a virtual high-dosage math tutoring programme run by Air Tutors that provides small-group online tutoring for upper-elementary and early-middle-school students.
  • AISSA - AISSA is a web-based platform developed by Alvaro Becerra et al.
  • AIvaluate - AIvaluate is a browser-based conversational assessment agent developed by Habeeb Yusuf et al.
  • ALAS dropout prevention program - ALAS is a multi-component dropout-prevention programme for middle- and high-school students that combines counselor mentoring, attendance and performance monitoring, resilience and self-control instruction, parent training, recognition activities, and community-service coordination.
  • Alder's high-touch, whole adult support approach - Alder's framework for proactively coordinating academic, financial, social, health, housing, and career supports for teacher-residency candidates facing barriers to completing graduate-level preparation.
  • ALF (Augmented Lectures Feedback System) - ALF is an augmented-reality classroom feedback system developed by Zarraonandia and colleagues that displays students' reported understanding and responses to teachers wearing AR glasses.
  • Algebra Toolkit - The Algebra Toolkit is a Regional Educational Laboratory Central at Mathematica professional development programme that supports middle and high school algebra educators through facilitated PLC sessions, preparation, classroom implementation, and school-leader guidance.
  • AlgoRAG - A retrieval-augmented generation software system developed by Sushan Adhikari for providing context-grounded assistance in theoretical computer science education.
  • Alharbi et al. (2026) Scenario Rubric, Artefact Rubric, and engagement index - A set of original instruments developed by Alharbi et al.
  • Alignment and coherence framework with six interrelated domains for system improvement - A Walrond et al.
  • Alliance for Resource Equity (ARE) diagnostic toolkit - The Alliance for Resource Equity diagnostic toolkit, developed by Ed Trust and Education Resource Strategies, organizes questions and guidance around ten dimensions of educational equity to help districts and advocates identify and address local resource and student-experience problems.
  • Alliance for Resource Equity (ARE) diagnostic toolkit organized around ten dimensions of equity - A toolkit developed by the Education Trust and Education Resource Strategies that helps district and school leaders diagnose inequities across ten dimensions of student experience and select guidebook actions for improvement.
  • Alumni Survey Roadmap - The authors’ nine-step framework guides organizations through co-designing, launching, interpreting, and acting on a first alumni survey.
  • America's Teacher Corps (ATC) - A proposed federal programme to recognize highly effective K–12 public school teachers and provide eligible teachers, especially those in high-poverty Title I schools, with recognition, salary supplements, and a portable credential.
  • American Board for Certification of Teacher Excellence (ABCTE) alternative teacher certification - A teacher-certification pathway, including the Passport to Teaching credential, developed and administered by the American Board for Certification of Teacher Excellence for alternative-route teacher candidates.
  • American Industrial Arts Student Association (AIASA) student industrial arts clubs - A national student-club programme organized by the American Industrial Arts Association to familiarize students with industrial and technical fields through tours, club activities, and leadership conferences.
  • Ames Philosophical Belief Inventory - A forced-choice questionnaire developed to measure education students' beliefs across five philosophical dimensions: Realism, Idealism, Pragmatism, Existentialism, and Phenomenology.
  • Amico dual-mode prototype (AmicoMio and AmicoTuo) - A Benedetti-developed AI-mediated educational interaction device, deployable as a digital agent or social robot, that provides either procedural task guidance or reflective dialogue under age-sensitive configuration and governance constraints.
  • Amira - Amira Learning's Amira is an AI-powered personal reading tutor that assesses oral reading fluency, provides individualized reading practice and intervention, and reports diagnostic information to educators and families.
  • Amplify Science - Amplify Science is a phenomena-based K–8 science curriculum developed and provided by Amplify Education, integrating hands-on investigation, literacy practices, modeling, and explanation to support three-dimensional science learning.
  • AMSTI - The Alabama Math, Science, and Technology Initiative is a state-run programme that combines summer teacher training, school-year coaching, and rotating instructional materials kits to support hands-on, inquiry-based mathematics and science instruction.
  • Annotated oral English corpus of 4,374 recordings from 486 Chinese university students across five diagnostic dimensions - A corpus created by Chai Rui containing 4,374 recorded oral-English performances from 486 Chinese university students, scored by trained evaluators across five diagnostic dimensions for model training and evaluation.
  • ANSYS Workbench - ANSYS Workbench is an engineering simulation platform made by Ansys, Inc., used to build and analyze finite-element models for applications such as heat transfer.
  • AntiSkillBench - AntiSkillBench is a benchmark dataset and evaluation suite created by Yongli Xiang et al.
  • ANVIL - ANVIL is an open-source generative software pipeline developed by Noviello et al.
  • ARC Core - ARC Core is a K–12 English and Spanish literacy curriculum developed by American Reading Company that organizes extensive reading, writing, research, and analysis into knowledge-building units integrating literacy with science, social studies, and literature.
  • Argus - Argus is a static-analysis triage tool developed by David Racovan et al.
  • Arizona linked education–child welfare student database - A study-created database linking longitudinal K–12 education records from the Arizona Department of Education with child records from the Arizona Department of Child Safety to identify and describe students in foster care.
  • Arthur book-based educational television program - Arthur is a PBS Kids educational television programme based on Marc Brown’s storybooks, produced and distributed for young children through PBS stations and online materials.
  • Arts for Learning (A4L) Lessons Project - A Young Audiences, Inc.
  • ASE-26 - ASE-26 is Mikael Gorsky's twenty-one-module undergraduate curriculum for teaching agentic software engineering to engineering students with foundational computer science and software-engineering knowledge.
  • ASEAN subregional EE curriculum prototypes for primary and secondary schools and pre-/in-service teacher training - A set of adaptable environmental-education curriculum prototypes for primary and secondary schools and teacher education, developed by UNESCO-UNEP's International Environmental Education Programme with Malaysia's Curriculum Development Centre and the University of the Philippines' ISMED.
  • Aspiring to Educate - A Pennsylvania state-supported programme that creates multiple pathways into teaching through preparation partnerships and financial supports for high school students, adults, paraprofessionals, and college students.
  • Assessment Integration Management (AIM) - Assessment Integration Management (AIM) is a web-based system for creating tests, uploading test models and item pools, and running large-scale computerized-based-exam simulations with configurable testing and student parameters.
  • ASSISTments - ASSISTments is a free web-based platform developed and operated by the ASSISTments Project for assigning mathematics practice, providing feedback, and reporting student-performance data to teachers.
  • ASTRA - ASTRA is an end-to-end air-traffic-control training simulator developed by Ethan Chew and colleagues that automates the simpilot role through speech recognition, response generation, and geospatial scenario visualization.
  • ASU CreateAI Chat platform - ASU CreateAI Chat is a secure, institutionally managed Arizona State University platform that provides a closed-system GPT-4o environment with retrieval-augmented generation for working with approved sources.
  • Asyncform (AF) dataset - A corpus of 863 AI-generated educational questions in English, Mathematics, and Science, labeled by K–12 teachers with Bloom Taxonomy levels and related subject, grade-level, objective, and question metadata; it was created and released by Michael Lawrence Castanares et al.
  • ATI-UP - ATI-UP is a preventive, school-wide, multi-tiered attendance intervention developed for school teams to promote attendance and reduce chronic absenteeism through problem-solving, family and community engagement, attendance promotion, and behavior motivation.
  • Attitude Scale towards Scientific Knowledge (SKS) - The SKS is a 25-item five-point Likert assessment developed by Ayvacı (2007) to measure attitudes toward scientific knowledge and views about its nature.
  • Attitudes Toward Trafficked Women and Sex Trafficking Scale (ATTS) - The ATTS is a modified 29-item attitude scale developed by Litam in collaboration with the original APPS developers to measure counselors' views of trafficked women and sex trafficking using person-first language.
  • Augustus F. Hawkins Center for Excellence grant program - A competitive federal grant programme administered by the U.S.
  • Aurora Institute's Seven Critical Elements of Instructional Practice - A framework developed by the Aurora Institute to guide competency-based instructional practice through learner agency, meaningful assessment, differentiated support, mastery-based progression, varied pathways and pacing, equity strategies, and transparent expectations.
  • AutoGen, MetaGPT, CrewAI, LangGraph, and Phidata - A set of software platforms developed by their respective makers for building, orchestrating, and deploying agentic AI systems, including multi-agent workflows and role-based delegation.
  • Automated Extended Essay Grading Software (AEEGS) - A web-based application developed by Olaoye DD et al.
