Products & Programmes¶

Named products and programmes others adopt: software and platforms, curricula, assessments and instruments, datasets, and branded programmes and initiatives, each with its own frameworks and the evidence about it. A design is one setting's.

A¶

  • A4L Lessons curriculum - An arts-integrated curriculum package for elementary students in grades 3–5, developed and delivered through the Arts for Learning program.
  • AACTE and Westat pilot survey of beginning teachers and their supervisors - A state-oriented survey instrument developed by the American Association of Colleges for Teacher Education and Westat to collect information about beginning teachers’ classroom experiences, performance, and needs for educator-preparation program improvement and cross-program comparisons.
  • Academic Parent-Teacher Teams (APTT) - Academic Parent-Teacher Teams (APTT) is a structured family-engagement programme, developed for implementation by schools and districts, in which teachers convene parents, share assessment data, and support learning activities at home.
  • ACEs Aware Initiative - A California statewide initiative led by the California Surgeon General and the Department of Health Care Services that promotes adverse childhood experiences screening, provider training, and trauma-informed systems of care.
  • AI-Enabled EdTech Adoption Cycle - A four-phase adoption framework described by Ruiz et al.
  • Air Tutors - Air Tutors is a virtual high-dosage math tutoring programme run by Air Tutors that provides small-group online tutoring for upper-elementary and early-middle-school students.
  • Alignment and coherence framework with six interrelated domains for system improvement - A Walrond et al.
  • Amplify Science - Amplify Science is a phenomena-based K–8 science curriculum developed and provided by Amplify Education, integrating hands-on investigation, literacy practices, modeling, and explanation to support three-dimensional science learning.
  • Arts for Learning (A4L) Lessons Project - A Young Audiences, Inc.
  • ASSISTments - ASSISTments is a free web-based platform developed and operated by the ASSISTments Project for assigning mathematics practice, providing feedback, and reporting student-performance data to teachers.
  • Aurora Institute's Seven Critical Elements of Instructional Practice - A framework developed by the Aurora Institute to guide competency-based instructional practice through learner agency, meaningful assessment, differentiated support, mastery-based progression, varied pathways and pacing, equity strategies, and transparent expectations.

B¶

  • Bachelor's Degree Attainment Index (Bachelor's DAI) and Direct Bachelor's DAI - These are UChicago Consortium measurement metrics estimating the proportion of a ninth-grade cohort that earns a bachelor's degree, distinguishing direct four-year pathways from pathways involving delayed entry or two-year colleges.
  • Blueprint for Statewide Pathways Scaling - A Babineau et al.

C¶

  • California Healthy Kids Survey (CHKS) - The California Department of Education's California Healthy Kids Survey, administered statewide with WestEd, is a K–12 survey system that measures student well-being, school conditions, and related behavioral-health outcomes.
  • California Practitioners' Guide for Educating English Learners with Disabilities - A California Department of Education practitioner guide providing research-based guidance on assessment, pre-referral supports, and special-education referral practices for English learners with disabilities.
  • California School Staff Survey (CSSS) - A California Department of Education staff survey, developed and administered with WestEd, that measures school staff perceptions of school climate conditions including instructional equity and respect for diversity.
  • California's Statewide System of Support (SSOS) - A California state-run system coordinated by the CCEE, CDE, and SBE that provides tiered technical assistance, tools, and resources to local educational agencies and schools.
  • Career-connected learning (CCL) framework - A Chicago Public Schools framework that coordinates college-preparatory and career-readiness activities across grades 6–12 through strengthened CTE and districtwide planning.
  • CCSR five-category framework of noncognitive factors - A research-synthesis framework developed by the University of Chicago Consortium on Chicago School Research (CCSR) that organises noncognitive factors into academic behaviors, academic perseverance, social skills, learning strategies, and academic mindsets.
  • CCSSO's implementation chain - A phased, multi-level implementation framework developed by the Council of Chief State School Officers (CCSSO) to guide coordinated adoption, implementation, and sustainability of educational initiatives.
  • Challenge Based Learning (CBL) Framework - A challenge-driven learning framework developed and maintained through Apple-supported Challenge Based Learning initiatives for educators and learners working on authentic real-world challenges.
  • Chicago High School Redesign Initiative (CHSRI) - A Chicago Public Schools high-school redesign initiative that created small, voluntary schools with supported autonomy and organizational and instructional reforms for urban students.
  • Children and Youth Behavioral Health Initiative (CYBHI) - A California statewide initiative that builds an upstream behavioral-health ecosystem for children and youth through screening, prevention, treatment capacity, awareness, and access to evidence-based care.
  • College Explorer - College Explorer is a data-visualization tool from NWEA Research that links MAP Growth scores with national benchmarks for colleges, universities, and majors to support post-secondary planning.
  • College match indicator - A measurement tool developed by the University of Chicago Consortium on School Research to gauge how students are progressing toward college enrollment by comparing their academic qualifications with college enrollment outcomes.
  • College Readiness Indicator Systems (CRIS) - A framework developed by the Annenberg Institute for School Reform and partners for organizing indicators of students' academic preparedness, academic tenacity, and college knowledge.
  • Colorado Educational Stability Grant Program (HB 18-1306) - A Colorado state grant program established through HB 18-1306 that funds districts and education providers to build community partnerships supporting school stability for highly mobile students.
  • Compass Care - Compass Care is a family-centered wraparound programme run by a Compass Care Team to support chronically absent students and their caregivers through attendance and engagement barriers.
  • Comprehensive Cross Unit (CCU) Assessments (Joy, Ruth, and Jackie Tests) - Assessment instruments developed by Dr.
  • Computational Thinking Pathways - A Digital Promise framework of system-wide K–12 computational-thinking learning progressions designed to support equitable participation through consistent, cumulative, and competency-based learning across classrooms and years.

D¶

  • Digital Promise AI Literacy Brief Series - A series of Digital Promise implementation briefs that provides audience-specific AI literacy recommendations, strategies, examples, and equity considerations for PK-12, postsecondary, workforce, and informal learning settings.
  • Digital Promise Micro-credential Platform - A competency-based micro-credential platform developed and operated by Digital Promise through which K–12 educators select credentials, submit implementation evidence, and receive expert-reviewed competency recognition.
  • Digital Promise's Competency-Based Assessment Framework - A framework created and maintained by Digital Promise to guide the design and accessibility review of competency-based micro-credentials on its platform.
  • Digital Promise's Inclusive Innovation model - An equity-centered research-and-development framework created by Digital Promise that places historically marginalized communities at the center of identifying, designing, developing, and implementing educational and workforce technologies.
  • Dual Enrollment Equity Pathway (DEEP) framework - A Community College Research Center framework that organizes four equity-oriented practice areas for designing and supporting dual-enrollment programs, especially for underserved high school students.

E¶

  • Early College - A whole-school dual-enrollment programme run through district-college partnerships that combines college-level coursework with academic, social, and transition supports for high-school students.
  • East Alabama Regional Cybersecurity Alliance (EARCA) - A regional alliance run by partner school districts, industry, and postsecondary organizations in Alabama Workforce Region 2 to expand rural cybersecurity education, workforce preparation, and career opportunities.
  • Elevate Math summer program - Elevate Math is a Silicon Valley Education Foundation summer mathematics support programme that combines a Common Core–aligned curriculum, blended instruction, Khan Academy practice, college-readiness activities, and professional development for its teachers and assistants.
  • ERWC 3.0 curriculum - A California State University-developed college-preparatory English language arts curriculum comprising 67 selectable modules for grade 11–12 courses, with integrated reading, writing, discussion, English Language Development, and Universal Design for Learning features.
  • Expository Reading and Writing Course (ERWC) - The Expository Reading and Writing Course is a college-preparatory English language arts curriculum developed by the California State University system for secondary students, with modules that integrate rhetorical reading, writing, revision, and language study.
  • Expository Reading and Writing Curriculum (ERWC) - A California State University-developed college-preparatory English language arts curriculum that uses rhetorical reading, discussion, and writing practices to prepare high-school students for college-level English.

F¶

  • Five considerations framework for engaging educational partners in school climate improvement - An Ojetunde et al.
  • Five EdTech Quality Indicators - A framework developed by 1EdTech, CAST, CoSN, Digital Promise, InnovateEDU, ISTE+ASCD, and SETDA for evaluating AI-enabled edtech against safety, evidence, inclusion, usability, and interoperability criteria.
  • Five-Component Framework for Systemic School Turnaround - TenSquare's framework guides boards, school leaders, and turnaround partners in transforming leadership, talent, teaching and learning, culture, and operations and finance in low-performing charter schools.
  • For Our Babies campaign - A U.S.
  • Foundations for Young Adult Success: A Developmental Framework - A conceptual framework developed by Nagaoka and colleagues at the University of Chicago Consortium on Chicago School Research that organizes the knowledge, skills, mindsets, strategies, and behaviors intended to support agency and success from childhood through young adulthood.
  • Framework for Leading Next Generation Science Standards Implementation - A leadership framework developed by Stiles et al.
  • Freshman OnTrack indicator - A research-based early-warning measurement developed and used by the University of Chicago Consortium and Chicago Public Schools to identify ninth-grade students at risk of not graduating.

H¶

  • Hero Elementary - Hero Elementary is a multiplatform K–2 science and literacy curriculum programme offering themed activity playlists for use in formal and informal learning settings.
  • How I Know project - WestEd's How I Know project framework provides five synergistic practices for school leaders guiding formative-assessment implementation and scaling.

I¶

  • Indicators-of-distress framework - A National Charter School Resource Center framework that organizes leading signs of charter-school decline across six domains to help leaders, boards, authorizers, and support organizations identify risks and target interventions.
  • Instruction Study - A Chicago Public Schools district-wide dataset created by Stevens et al.
  • Instructional coherence framework: four student-centered approaches share commitments to identity, agency, equity, and community - A framework developed by Browning et al.

K¶

  • K-12 Computer Science Framework - A framework developed by ACM, CSTA, Code.org, and collaborating experts that defines core practices and concepts for K-12 computer science education.
  • Kansas MTSS framework - A statewide multi-tiered system of support framework developed and implemented by the Kansas State Department of Education to coordinate academic and behavioral supports for K–12 students.
  • Kansas Multi-Tier System of Supports (Kansas MTSS) - A statewide framework run by the Kansas State Department of Education that organizes evidence-based academic and behavioral supports, assessment, data-based decision making, professional development, leadership, and implementation fidelity across Kansas public schools.

L¶

  • Learner Variability Project (LVP) - The Learner Variability Project, operated by Digital Promise, organizes research-based learner factors and instructional strategies into models intended for teachers and edtech developers serving diverse learners.
  • Liberatory Space framework for Black student thriving - A framework developed for Long Beach Unified School District's Black Community to guide the design of environments that promote Black students' belonging, identity affirmation, agency, and achievement.
  • Linked California child welfare-education dataset - A WestEd-created dataset linking CDE's California Longitudinal Pupil Achievement Data System with CDSS's Child Welfare Services Case Management System to study the educational experiences of more than 43,000 California students in foster care.

M¶

  • M-PLANS (Motivation - Planning Lessons to Activate eNgagement in Science) - M-PLANS is a multi-week professional-learning programme and toolkit developed by Harris et al.
  • M-PLANS reference toolkit and classroom-based quick tools - M-PLANS is a teacher-facing framework and toolkit that provides motivation-principle look-fors, planning questions, aligned activities, talk moves, and just-in-time classroom supports for middle-school science instruction.
  • Massachusetts DESE early literacy screening assessment dataset - A multi-year dataset of K–3 early literacy screener results collected and maintained by the Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education from participating grantee schools and districts.
  • Massachusetts DESE grantee early literacy screening assessment dataset - A dataset of K–3 literacy screening assessment scores collected by the Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education from participating grantee schools and districts to analyze reading-risk patterns and inform intervention and resource targeting.
  • Massachusetts DESE statewide K–3 early literacy screening dataset (2023/24) - A Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education dataset of K–3 early literacy screening results collected from participating schools and districts to identify students at risk of reading difficulty and guide early intervention.
  • Massachusetts early literacy screening assessment dataset linked with MCAS results - A Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education dataset of K–3 early literacy screening results linked to state MCAS results for longitudinal analysis.
  • Massachusetts early literacy screening assessment dataset, 2022/23 - A Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education dataset of K–3 early literacy screening scores collected from participating grantee schools and districts during the 2022/23 school year.
  • Massachusetts early literacy screening assessment longitudinal database - A longitudinal database built by WestEd for the Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education linking grantee K–3 early literacy universal-screening scores with SIMS, MCAS, ACCESS for ELLs, and school- and district-level data.
  • Massachusetts K–3 early literacy screening longitudinal dataset (2020/21–2023/24) - WestEd’s longitudinal dataset for the Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education links K–3 early-literacy screening results with SIMS demographics, MCAS, ACCESS for ELLs, and school- and district-level contextual data to monitor literacy performance and reading risk.
  • Mathematics Diagnostic Testing Project (MDTP) - MDTP is a mathematics diagnostic assessment project that provides topic-level mastery information for students and educators, including online results used to inform algebra placement decisions.
  • MDTP Algebra Readiness test - A 45-item multiple-choice mathematics diagnostic assessment developed by the Mathematics Diagnostic Testing Project to measure students' algebra readiness across seven topic areas.
  • Measure C and the Oakland Children's Initiative - County and city early-childhood and education funding initiatives administered or implemented by First 5 Alameda and Oakland Promise to expand early learning access, improve provider conditions, support school readiness, and promote college persistence.
  • Mellon Research Forum initiative on the value of a liberal arts education - A Mellon Foundation research initiative that supports and organizes scholarly inquiry into the multiple outcomes of a liberal arts education.
  • Mindsets mathematics program - Mindsets is a middle-school mathematics curriculum program built around collaborative, hands-on, challenge-based tasks aligned with the Standards for Mathematical Practice and supported by teacher professional development and implementation assistance.
  • Multi-Payer School-Linked Fee Schedule - A California optional programme administered through Carelon that enables enrolled local educational agencies and designated school-linked providers to obtain reimbursement from commercial and Medi-Cal managed care plans for student mental health and substance use disorder services.

N¶

  • National Education Longitudinal Survey (NELS) - A nationally representative longitudinal education dataset collected and maintained by the National Center for Education Statistics, tracking students from eighth grade through postsecondary outcomes.
  • NBA Math Hoops - NBA Math Hoops is a Learn Fresh programme developed in partnership with the NBA and WNBA that uses an interactive basketball board game, real player statistics, and supporting math lessons to teach elementary and middle-grade mathematics.
  • NCS capacity-building model - A whole-school improvement framework developed and implemented by the Network for College Success to build school staff capacity through professional learning networks, data use, coaching, and distributed leadership.
  • Nebraska's AQuESTT Dual State Accountability System - A state accountability framework operated by the Nebraska Department of Education alongside the federal ESSA accountability system to evaluate and guide Nebraska schools and districts.
  • Nevada MRI menu of more rigorous interventions for CSI schools - The Nevada Department of Education, with Region 15 Comprehensive Center support, developed this evidence-based menu to help LEAs and Comprehensive Support and Improvement schools identify and implement interventions addressing persistent low or stagnant academic performance.
  • Nevada's GRAD score - A machine-learning-generated student at-risk indicator run through Nevada's Infinite Campus system and used by the Nevada Department of Education in its school funding formula.
  • Next Generation School Accountability initiative - Utah's state school-accountability framework, administered by the Utah State Board of Education, that includes growth and equity indicators such as the Lowest Quartile measure.
  • NFTE entrepreneurship courses - A semester- or year-long entrepreneurship curriculum developed and delivered by the Network for Teaching Entrepreneurship (NFTE), culminating in student business plans, presentations, and competitions.
  • NFTE entrepreneurship education program - NFTE's entrepreneurship education program, developed and run by the Network for Teaching Entrepreneurship, uses project-based and experiential instruction to develop entrepreneurial-mindset skills alongside small-business-management knowledge.
  • NFTE Startup Summer - NFTE's Startup Summer is an intensive business-startup programme that supports selected high-school students from under-resourced communities through boot camp, coaching, funding, and a public pitch event.

O¶

  • Observation Protocol for Teachers of English Learners (OPTEL) - OPTEL is an observation protocol developed with the Region 15 Comprehensive Center for the California Department of Education to support standardized evaluation of English learners' English proficiency during academic learning and reclassification decisions.
  • Ohio's School Quality and Student Success (SQSS) Accountability System - Ohio's statewide accountability framework, administered by the Ohio Department of Education and Workforce, includes the Early Literacy Indicator to monitor K–3 literacy outcomes alongside other school-quality and student-success measures.
  • Opportunity Culture - Opportunity Culture is a school redesign and staffing framework developed by Public Impact that uses tiered teaching roles to expand the reach of excellent teachers and strengthen teacher leadership.

P¶

  • Positive Behavioral Interventions and Supports (PBIS) - PBIS is an evidence-based, multitiered framework used by schools to organize data, systems, and behavioral practices through universal, targeted, and individualized supports.
  • Post-secondary Attainment Index (PAI) - The University of Chicago Consortium on School Research's Post-secondary Attainment Index is a district-level indicator combining high-school graduation, college enrollment, and college-completion rates to estimate post-secondary attainment for CPS students.
  • Practice-driven data - A five-lesson, context-sensitive framework for using data in school improvement—prepare, focus, make meaning, strategize, and disrupt—developed through the University of Chicago’s partnership with Chicago Public Schools.
  • Principals Pursuing Excellence (PPE) - A two-year competency-based programme initiated by the New Mexico Public Education Department to develop and support turnaround principals, with trained mentors and leadership-focused training.
  • Program for Infant/Toddler Care (PITC) - Program for Infant/Toddler Care is a comprehensive training programme developed by WestEd in collaboration with the California Department of Education to support responsive, relationship-based care for infants and toddlers.
  • Project Cal-Well - Project Cal-Well is a California Department of Education initiative, funded through a SAMHSA grant, that helps local educational agencies build sustainable school- and community-based mental health systems.
  • Project LIFT - A public-private partnership run by Charlotte-Mecklenburg Schools and its philanthropic partners to implement Opportunity Culture staffing, professional development, and compensation structures in high-needs schools.

Q¶

  • Quality Teaching for English Learners (QTEL) - QTEL is a professional-development and instructional programme that supports educators in teaching rigorous academic content and academic English simultaneously to secondary English Learners.

R¶

  • RAMPUP (Reimagining and Amplifying Mathematics Participation, Understanding, and Practices) - A three-week summer bridge mathematics programme developed through WestEd’s National Research and Development Center to Improve Education for Secondary English Learners, serving rising ninth-grade students through modules on patterns, networks, and equivalence.
  • Readiness Framework (Appendix B) - A district-readiness framework developed by Zohal Shah et al.
  • Really Great Reading - Really Great Reading is a branded foundational-literacy curriculum developed by Really Great Reading for teaching early reading skills, including phonics and fluency, through classroom teachers, paraprofessionals, or tutors.
  • Reimagining Excellence blueprint - A seven-dimension framework from Reimagining Excellence for integrating equity, social-emotional well-being, and academic learning across K–12 in-person, remote, and hybrid programs.
  • Rocky Mountain Prep Family Advocacy Support Team (FAST) - Rocky Mountain Prep's Family Advocacy Support Team (FAST) is a referral-based family-support programme providing emergency basic-needs assistance and connections to longer-term resources for students' families.
  • RP-Observe observation tool - RP-Observe is a Rutgers University-developed school observation tool for assessing restorative-justice implementation and outcomes.
  • RULER - RULER is a social-emotional learning programme developed and run by the Yale Center for Emotional Intelligence that teaches recognizing, understanding, labeling, expressing, and regulating emotions.

S¶

  • SEAD integration framework (the three-legged stool) - A guide-based framework for integrating explicit social-emotional skill instruction, embedded academic practice, and relationship-rich environments into K–12 school improvement.
  • SEERNet - SEERNet is an Institute of Education Sciences-funded network that supports responsible, inclusive, theory-driven research using data from digital learning platforms through shared infrastructure, governance, datasets, and research-practice partnerships.
  • Seven categories of charter school core functions for organizing distress indicators - A charter-school authorizer toolkit framework that organizes longitudinal review of school distress indicators across leadership, governing board, operations, financial management, talent, culture, and instruction.
  • Seven co-designed Learning Transition Design Principles for LER technologies - A co-designed framework from the report authors for guiding and evaluating learning and employment record technologies serving HSE learners and workers.
  • Shaping Our Appalachian Region (SOAR) - A nonprofit regional revitalization programme that coordinates Appalachian individuals and organizations around shared values and goals, including technology, computer science education, and digital-economy workforce development.
  • Shared dataset combining the Anthropic Economic Index with Census BTOS employer adoption data - A dataset used by the brief series that combines Anthropic's task-level AI exposure index with U.S.
  • Six-domain AI skills and competencies framework for educators and educational leaders - A Digital Promise framework organizing AI literacy and readiness competencies for PK–12 educators and educational leaders across technical, analytical, interpersonal, ethical, adaptive, and implementation domains.
  • Six-domain AI skills and competencies framework for PK-12 learners - A Digital Promise framework that organizes PK-12 learner AI competencies across technical, practical, ethical, analytical, interpersonal, and adaptive domains.
  • Sobrato Early Academic Language (SEAL) model - A PreK–Grade 3 oral-language development model developed by the Sobrato Family Foundation for multilingual learners, including English learners with disabilities.
  • Social, Academic, and Emotional Behavior Risk Screener (SAEBRS) - SAEBRS is a universal screening assessment for students’ social, academic, and emotional behavior risk, used by schools and districts to identify needs and guide mental-health supports.
  • Special Education Leadership Academy (Massachusetts) - A WestEd Learning Innovations leadership-development programme, funded by the Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education, that supports experienced Massachusetts special education directors through sessions, follow-up meetings, and peer networking.
  • ST Math - ST Math is game-based instructional software for K–12 mathematics developed and provided by the MIND Research Institute, using visual-spatial puzzles to support mathematics learning.
  • Student Behavioral Health Incentive Program (SBHIP) - A California initiative that distributed Medi-Cal managed care plan funding to county offices of education and local educational agencies from 2022 through 2024 to expand school-affiliated behavioral health services for TK–12 students.

T¶

  • Teacher Assessment of College-going Climate measure - A school-level survey-based measure developed by Roderick et al.
  • TECTA: Tennessee Early Childhood Training Alliance - A Tennessee statewide programme that provides childcare providers and administrators with free orientation training, professional development, credential support, and tuition assistance through participating public higher-education institutions.
  • Ten Aspects of Community framework - A Forum for Youth Investment framework that categorizes community features and assets affecting youth across ten domains.
  • The Leandro sound basic education framework - A North Carolina constitutional education framework defining the components of a sound basic education and guiding state action, accountability, and resource recommendations for K–12 public education.
  • The Region 15 FACE Collaborative - A Region 15 Comprehensive Center initiative that convenes state education agency family and community engagement leaders across Arizona, California, Nevada, and Utah to share practices, learn from experts, and develop state-level projects.
  • Three-category framework for organizing school mental health and well-being supports - A National Charter School Resource Center framework that helps public charter-school leaders organize and assess school mental-health, well-being, and SEL supports across three categories.
  • Three-component framework for Portrait of a Graduate assessment systems - A framework developed by Kyle Dunbar et al.
  • To&Through Project - The To&Through Project at the UChicago Consortium on School Research tracks Chicago Public Schools students from high school through college and publishes the data in public online tools.
  • Tool #3: Assessing Schools for Indicators of Distress rating scales - Aimee Evan et al.'s rating-scale assessment helps charter school authorizers and schools evaluate organizational indicators of school distress using school-level evidence and reflection prompts.
  • Turnaround Leader Competencies - A Center on School Turnaround at WestEd competency framework identifying high-impact motives, habits, and behaviors for developing leaders responsible for rapid improvement in persistently low-performing schools.

U¶

  • UNESCO AI Competency Framework for School Students - A UNESCO framework that organizes 12 AI competencies for school students into four dimensions: human-centred mindset, ethics of AI, AI techniques and applications, and AI system design.
  • UNESCO AI Competency Framework for Students - A UNESCO curricular framework that organizes 12 AI competencies across four dimensions and three progression levels to guide educators in preparing school students for safe, meaningful, and responsible engagement with AI.
  • UNESCO AI Competency Framework for Teachers - A UNESCO framework specifying 15 AI-related teacher competencies across five dimensions to guide national competency frameworks and teacher training programmes.
  • UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence - A UNESCO global standard-setting framework that provides ethical principles and policy guidance for governments, educators, learners, and other stakeholders implementing and governing artificial intelligence.
  • University of Virginia Partnership for Leaders in Education turnaround competency model - A seven-competency, four-level proficiency framework developed by the University of Virginia Partnership for Leaders in Education to assess and develop school leaders in turnaround contexts.

V¶

  • Valor Collegiate Academy Compass Model - Valor Collegiate Academy's whole-school SEL and mental-health programme uses five interdependent domains, developmental badges, and weekly mentor-facilitated Compass Circles for students in Grades 5–12.
  • VITAL (Visibly Improve Teaching and Learning) professional learning service - WestEd's VITAL service builds K–12 teachers' capacity for collaborative inquiry through structured teaching-and-learning cycles, peer feedback, reflection, and analysis of student and teacher learning.

W¶

  • WestEd Center on School Turnaround Journeys Project - A WestEd Center on School Turnaround initiative that follows schools' turnaround efforts over time and shares implementation episodes, webinars, research, and practitioner dialogue.
  • WestEd MTSS coaching model - WestEd's framework provides school-based coaching to support implementation of academic and behavioral multi-tiered systems of support (MTSS).
  • Whole School, Whole Community, Whole Child (WSCC) model - A Centers for Disease Control and Prevention framework that organizes coordinated school health and education efforts across 10 components to support K–12 students' health, learning, and development.
  • Woodcock-Johnson Tests of Achievement—Applied Problems subtest - A standardized achievement subtest measuring applied mathematical problem-solving and early numeracy, developed and published as part of the Woodcock-Johnson assessment system by Riverside Insights.
  • Workforce AI literacy framework of six skill and competency domains - A workforce-oriented framework organizing AI literacy into six domains—Technical Knowledge, Practical Implementation, Analytical Skills, Interpersonal and Communication Skills, Ethical Evaluation, and Adaptive Skills—developed by Zachariah et al.
