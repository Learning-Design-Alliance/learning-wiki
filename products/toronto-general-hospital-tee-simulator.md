# Toronto General Hospital TEE simulator

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A free web-based transesophageal echocardiography simulator developed and maintained by the Interactive Perioperative Education Group at Toronto General Hospital, providing standard TEE images, a manipulable 3D heart model, and structured guidance.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Toronto General Hospital free web-based TEE simulator used as standardized pre-training self-learning material**: A free web-based TEE simulator developed by the Interactive Perioperative Education Group at Toronto General Hospital (http://pie.med.utoronto.ca/TEE/) was used by both trial groups in the shared foundational phase. "This platform provides standard TEE images, a manipulable 3D heart model, and structured textual guidance for identifying anatomical landmarks and clinical applications," with text prompts under each view explaining structure identification and diagnostic use. The article notes prior studies have successfully used and evaluated this tool for TEE teaching. (Ou Xinxu et al. (2026))
