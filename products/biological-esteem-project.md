## Description
An online BioQUEST collection of mathematical biology simulation tools and modules for exploring genetics, ecology, epidemiology, and related biological models.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Biological ESTEEM Project (Biological Excel Simulations and Tools in Exploratory, Experiential Mathematics)**: The Biological ESTEEM Project is an online collection of BioQUEST mathematical biology simulation tools and modules introduced in this article. The article names it among "Two projects that use this approach": "The Biological Excel Simulations and Tools in Exploratory, Experiential Mathematics (ESTEEM) Project (http://bioquest.org/esteem)". Its tools cited in the article include Deme (population genetics), deFinetti (fitness landscapes), the Luria–Delbrück fluctuation simulation, Two-Species Model (predator–prey and competition), and the Mark–Recapture module with five models and a salamander data set. (Jungck John R. et al. (2010))
