# Operation Outbreak experiential simulation

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
Operation Outbreak is a hybrid experiential simulation combining a mobile contact tracing application with a live, schoolwide event in which students experience and analyze the spread of a simulated pathogen. The review describes it as a simulation that "models disease transmission through the release of a virtual pathogen transmitted via Bluetooth-enabled mobile devices, allowing students to explore real-time, real-world patterns of contagion safely and repeatedly, without the risks of actual infection." It bridges digital and physical learning environments, requiring live social interaction to be actualized, and can be implemented as a schoolwide simulation or in smaller, class-based and modular formats.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Mobile devices with Bluetooth technology, device access, network availability, and multi-adult facilitation; the review notes dependence on local school policies such as cell phone bans.
#### Constraints
- Requires a live, in-person social interaction that cannot be duplicated exclusively online; scalability is constrained by logistical planning, time demands, technology access, and the need for teacher training and administrative support.

### Target Learners
- K-12 students
- middle and high school students

### Target Learning Goals
- epidemiology and disease transmission knowledge
- systems thinking
- public health literacy
- civic awareness and social responsibility
