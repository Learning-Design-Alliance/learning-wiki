# Problem Roulette

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A University of Michigan web-based study service that gives authenticated learners random within-topic access to locally authored past exam problems and records responses while providing correctness and peer-performance feedback.

### Claims
- [After adjusting for selection bias with beginning-of-term GPA, regular PR users show a grade point improvement of 0.22, significant above the random noise level of 0.04, with replication at 0.32 in Winter 2013.](../claims/gpa-adjusted-022-grade-boost-regular-pr-users.md) [+M]
- [Regular users of Problem Roulette in the Phys 240 electromagnetism course outperformed their complement by an average of 0.40 grade points (on a 4.0 scale), before adjusting for selection bias.](../claims/regular-pr-users-outperform-by-040-grade-points.md) [+M]
- [PR usage spiked immediately before scheduled exams, with activity peaking at 800 problems served per hour on exam day, and no formal course credit was attached to use.](../claims/pr-usage-spikes-before-exams.md) [+M]
