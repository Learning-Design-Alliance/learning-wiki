# Problem Roulette

## Description
A University of Michigan web-based study service that gives authenticated learners random within-topic access to locally authored past exam problems and records responses while providing correctness and peer-performance feedback.

### Claims
- [After adjusting for selection bias with beginning-of-term GPA, regular PR users show a grade point improvement of 0.22, significant above the random noise level of 0.04, with replication at 0.32 in Winter 2013.](../claims/gpa-adjusted-022-grade-boost-regular-pr-users.md) [+M]
