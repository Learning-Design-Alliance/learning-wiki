# Jefferson Scale of Empathy, Healthcare Professions Student version (JSE-HPS)

## Description
The JSE-HPS is a 20-item Likert-scale self-report assessment developed by the Jefferson Scale of Empathy authors for measuring cognitive empathy among healthcare professions students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Jefferson Scale of Empathy–Health Profession Students version (JSE-HPS) as the outcome measure**: The JSE-HPS is a 20-item self-report questionnaire used as the study's outcome measure, scored on a 7-point Likert-type scale with total scores ranging from 20 to 140, where higher scores indicate more empathy with patients. It has 10 reverse-scored negative items and three sub-scales: perspective-taking (10 items), compassionate care (8 items), and standing in patient's shoes (2 items). In this study the Persian version's validity was confirmed by 10 faculty members and reliability was Cronbach alpha 0.71. (Larti N et al. (2018))
