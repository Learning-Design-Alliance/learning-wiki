# Researcher-made 50-mark English achievement test

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 50-item objective English achievement test created by G. Singaravelu for pre- and post-testing in-service B.Ed. teachers' competency in methods of teaching English.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Researcher-made 50-mark English achievement test with test-retest reliability of 0.89**: The study's instrument is the investigator's self-made achievement test, prepared on the basis of a blue print and used for both pre-tests and post-tests of both groups. Items are objective type, one mark each, totalling "50 marks", testing methods of teaching English; sample items ask, for example, which strategy teaches spelling to high-school students. Validity was established by jury opinion and reliability by the test-retest method, yielding a calculated value of 0.89, which the author calls quite significant and reliable. (G. Singaravelu (2011))
