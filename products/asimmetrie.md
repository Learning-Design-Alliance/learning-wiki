## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Asimmetrie, INFN's monothematic magazine for high school pupils and teachers**: Asimmetrie is INFN's editorial product aimed mainly at High School pupils and teachers, written by INFN personnel and edited with the support of the Communication Office for clarity. The article describes it as having "monothematic issues, an engaging graphics, and a board composed of researchers", calling it "a unique editorial product". It doubles as an institutional-communication and dissemination tool: INFN's presence as publisher of Asimmetrie earned an invitation to the Turin Book Fair in 2018, where its stand has since drawn several thousand visitors per year. (G. Chiarelli (2022))
