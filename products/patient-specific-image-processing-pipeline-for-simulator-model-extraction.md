# Patient-specific image processing pipeline for simulator model extraction

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A dedicated image-processing tool that combines CT, MRI, and DSA data to rapidly generate annotated, patient-specific 3D models for neurosurgical planning and aneurysm-clipping simulation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Medical holoimaging pipeline infrastructure for generating modular AR/MR holoimages from clinical CT/MR imagesets**: A pipeline infrastructure that identifies, retrieves, and manipulates real patients' de-identified CT and MR imagesets for "rendering, packaging, transfer, and display of modular holoimages onto AR/MR headset devices and connected displays." It comprises a PACS query mechanism, voxel intensity-based segmentation, marching-cubes mesh conversion, mesh smoothing with MeshLab, compositing in Maya, and transfer/retrieval mechanisms. In its current implementation the pipeline required approximately 30 minutes for a typical head or chest CT imageset from patient selection to holoimage display. (Kobayashi L et al. (2018))
