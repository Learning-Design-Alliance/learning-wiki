# Three-minute acuity-sustain test of the eye's power to sustain clear seeing under lighting conditions

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A laboratory visual-acuity assessment developed by C. E. Ferree and colleagues to measure the eye's ability to sustain a specified level of clear seeing during prolonged reading under controlled lighting conditions.

### Claims
