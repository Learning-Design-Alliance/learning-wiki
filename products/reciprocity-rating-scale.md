# RECIPROCITY rating scale

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A writing-assessment rating scale devised by Van der Aalsvoort and Lidz (2002) for evaluating learners' responses to mediated interaction, comments, and written feedback across dynamic-assessment sessions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **RECIPROCITY rating scale for assessing writing within dynamic assessment.**: The RECIPROCITY rating scale, devised by Van der Aalsvoort and Lidz (2002), was used to assess writing assignments at each session. It "focuses on bidirectional interaction between mediator and learner" and "signifies the role of documentation which will be revealed by comments and written feedback." Ten scores were assigned for taught points repeated across sessions, since the scale focuses on removing errors during a course of study. (Erfani et al. (2015))
