# Achievement test for the unit of cell division and inheritance

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A 30-item author-developed achievement test for measuring eighth-grade students’ knowledge of cell division and inheritance, refined through pilot item analysis.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Achievement test for the unit of cell division and inheritance (30 items, KR-20 = .86).**: An achievement test developed by the author to determine students' current knowledge about the cell division and inheritance unit. It initially included 60 items developed based on the stated goals for the unit; following a pilot study and item analysis the number of items became thirty. The analysis showed that its KR-20 reliability coefficient was .86. (Balci et al. (2016))

### Claims
- [Scientific argumentation based learning significantly improves eighth-grade students' academic achievement in the unit of cell division and inheritance compared to traditional teaching.](../claims/argumentation-improves-cell-division-achievement.md) [+M]
