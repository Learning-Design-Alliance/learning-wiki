# Acuity Predictive interim assessment tool

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Acuity Predictive was an Indiana Department of Education statewide interim assessment tool that administered recurring mathematics and ELA tests and provided rapid student- and class-level performance reports, including predicted year-end proficiency.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **mCLASS and Acuity interim assessment programs**: The study evaluated two interim assessment programs administered multiple times per school year. In grades K-2, mCLASS diagnostic probes are conducted face-to-face; for reading and ELA, "the student performs language tasks while the teacher records characteristics of the work using a personal digital assistant (PDA)," while mCLASS mathematics uses paper and pencil with results entered by the teacher. CTB/McGraw-Hill's Acuity provides online assessments in reading and mathematics for grades 3-8, consisting of 30- to 35-item multiple-choice online tests aligned to Indiana standards. (Konstantopoulos et al. (2015))
