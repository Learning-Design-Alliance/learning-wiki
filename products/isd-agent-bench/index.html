ISD-Agent-Bench¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes

Description¶

ISD-Agent-Bench is a benchmark dataset and evaluation harness created by YoungHoon Jeon et al. for assessing LLM agents across instructional-systems-design scenarios using ADDIE-based rubrics and trajectory evaluation.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • React-ADDIE, integrating the ADDIE framework with ReAct-style reasoning, achieves the highest benchmark performance (86.49), outperforming pure theory-based agents and technique-only baselines [+M]
  • Theoretical quality of agent outputs strongly correlates with benchmark performance (r = 0.656) [+M]
  • Theoretical quality of both theory-based and non-theory agents remains stable across Easy, Medium, and Hard scenario difficulty levels [~W]
