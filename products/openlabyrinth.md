# OpenLabyrinth

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
OpenLabyrinth is a web-based virtual-patient authoring and delivery platform that enables branching clinical scenarios, dynamic group-specific pathways, and centralized logging of learner decisions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Web-based virtual patient delivery system (OpenLabyrinth) with central path logging for D-PBL**: D-PBL depended on a Web-based virtual patient platform; Figure 1 shows a decision-point page authored in "the authoring application OpenLabyrinth". The article states the delivery system was required to serve multiple PBL classrooms simultaneously, respond dynamically and independently to each group's choices, and record the paths groups took: "the paths taken by the individual groups were tracked and logged centrally". Central logging was intended to permit later analysis of group paths to understand the effectiveness of option steps and iteratively improve the cases. All 5 Life Protection module cases were built in this software in both linear and D-PBL versions. (Poulton T et al. (2014))
