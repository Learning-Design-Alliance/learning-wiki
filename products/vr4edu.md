## Description
A multi-platform smartphone mobile-VR application developed by Degli Innocenti et al. for teaching primary-school students to identify and characterize music genres through immersive virtual musical rooms.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **VR4EDU multi-platform mobile application for music genre learning**: VR4EDU is a multi-platform mobile application developed for music genre identification and learning in primary education. Students are "immersed in music performances of different genres (e.g., classical, country, jazz, and swing), navigating inside several musical rooms." The application uses mobile virtual reality on smartphones with natural interaction and immersion in virtual environments, and was tested with two primary school classes. (Degli Innocenti et al. (2019))
