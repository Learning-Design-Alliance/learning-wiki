# Electrical Installation Achievement Test (EIAT)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 30-item researcher-developed test measuring Senior Technical One students' achievement in electrical-installation testing and earthing, validated by subject experts and reliability-checked by Kuder–Richardson Formula 21.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Electrical Installation Achievement Test (EIAT), a 30-item researcher-developed instrument with reliability coefficient .88**: The Electrical Installation Achievement Test (EIAT) is the researcher-developed instrument used for both pre-test and post-test in the study. It "comprised 30 questions based on the concept of testing and earthing", was validated by three experts in the Faculty of Education, University of Uyo, and its "reliability coefficient of the instrument stood at .88", determined using Kuder-Richardson Formula 21. Each administration was scored to give 30 points. (Vincent E. Onweh et al. (2014))
