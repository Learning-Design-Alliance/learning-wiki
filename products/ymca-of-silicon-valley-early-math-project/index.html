YMCA of Silicon Valley early math project¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

An early-mathematics family-engagement programme developed and implemented by the YMCA of Silicon Valley and scaled to additional sites with support from the Heising-Simons Foundation.

Components¶

  • YMCA of Silicon Valley early math project scaled up in new sites: The YMCA of Silicon Valley (YMCA) is the second of two family engagement providers supported by the Heising-Simons Foundation to refine and scale up an early math project. The brief reports that the Foundation supported ROR and the YMCA "as they refined their early math projects and scaled them up in new sites." The scale-up effort was the subject of Mathematica's implementation study. (Jaimie Grazi et al. (2020))

Claims¶

  • The Heising-Simons Foundation funded family engagement providers to develop, test, and integrate research-based early math projects within existing family engagement programs [+W]
  • Mathematica conducted an implementation study of the early math scale-up effort during 2018 and 2019 [+W]
