# Polygence authentic learning dataset

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A large anonymized multimodal dataset of more than 100,000 hours of longitudinal one-on-one project-based tutoring interactions between PhD-level mentors and K–12 or pre-college students, collected and maintained by Polygence.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Polygence authentic learning dataset of over 100,000 hours of one-on-one tutoring interactions**: A large longitudinal dataset of one-on-one, project-based tutoring sessions between PhD-level tutors and students across more than 150 subjects, collected through the Polygence platform. The article states it "comprises more than 100,000 hours of one-on-one, longitudinal student–tutor interactions in multiple modalities," including meeting transcripts, shared documents, and chat. It was anonymized on internal servers and used for post-training and benchmarking LLMs for education. (Janos Perczel et al. (2025))
