## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Eight core service components framework for multidisciplinary alcohol care teams**: The article organizes ACT clinical competencies around eight core service components identified by NHS England and Improvement as essential roles of the ACT: the article states "Eight core clinical components have been identified as essential roles of the ACT". The components span case identification and brief advice, triage and comprehensive alcohol assessment, specialist nursing and medical care planning, management of medically-assisted alcohol withdrawal, psychosocial interventions, safe discharge and community referral, clinical leadership with dedicated time, and trust-wide education and training. The 72 competencies are mapped onto these components in Table 1, each phrased as what the ACT "possesses the specialist knowledge, skills and behaviours to" do. (Phillips Thomas et al. (2020))
