# Four researcher-developed assessment instruments for teacher knowledge, self-efficacy, self-report of use, and application to hypothetical cases

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A four-part assessment battery developed by Dille to measure teachers' autism-related knowledge, self-efficacy, reported instructional-method use, and application of methods to hypothetical autism cases.

### Claims

- [Teacher application of instructional methods did not differ significantly by autism severity level, and no treatment-by-severity interaction emerged at post-test](../claims/no-severity-difference-or-interaction-post-application.md) [+W]
- [At pretest, teachers' application scores were significantly lower for severe than mild/moderate hypothetical cases, with no group differences or interaction](../claims/pretest-severity-effect-application.md) [+W]
