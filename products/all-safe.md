## Description
ALL-SAFE is an open-source, low-bandwidth online laparoscopic-surgery training platform developed by surgeons from Africa and the United States in partnership with PAACS, combining case-based modules, expert videos, video submission and review, and a low-cost user-assembled box trainer.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ALL-SAFE cholecystectomy module: an open-source online platform with case-based learning and a user-assembled box trainer costing less than $10 USD**: ALL-SAFE is "an open-source, online laparoscopic surgery training platform designed for varied-resource settings," developed by surgeons from Africa and the US in partnership with PAACS. The cholecystectomy module pairs an online case-based learning exercise structured with Bloom's taxonomy with a low-cost, self-assembled box trainer and expert video tutorial. "The user-assembled cholecystectomy models (Fig. 1) and ALL-SAFE box trainer (Fig. 2) were designed to be cost-effective, using readily available materials costing less than $10 USD." Users practice in the simulator and upload videos for self, peer, and AI review; the platform hosts five procedural modules to date. (Kim Erin et al. (2025))
