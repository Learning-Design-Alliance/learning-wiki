## Description
NASA's Universe of Sound is a NASA public-engagement programme that creates and presents sonifications of astronomical datasets for public and accessible science communication.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **NASA's Universe of Sound astronomy sonifications (Galactic Center, Cassiopeia A, Chandra Deep Field South)**: NASA's Universe of Sound project provides bespoke audio representations of astronomical datasets for non-expert audiences, created with BLV representatives. The study evaluated three sonifications chosen "for their variation of instrumental vs. synthetic sounds and how they track the visual data": the Galactic Center (four pieces, orchestral instruments, left-to-right), Cassiopeia A (six pieces, string section, radial), and Chandra Deep Field South (one synthetic piece, bottom to top). Participants experienced them with accompanying videos and screen-reader-adaptable text descriptions. (Arcand KK et al. (2024))

### Claims
