## Description
A proposed mobile and web-based application by Alice Schmidt Hanbidge and colleagues that delivers interactive academic-integrity modules and assessments, tracks completion, and issues badges or e-certificates for postsecondary learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Foundations for Academic Success (FAS) mobile learning application for academic integrity**: The FAS tool is a proposed mobile application delivering academic integrity content through six interactive modules, built on the ProProfs web-based quiz service. It is designed to make information available at students' convenience and thereby acculturate them to university values before classes begin. The article states the tool will "track module completion, but also has capacity to recognize effort and completion by issuing badges and e-certificates". Once completed, the application will be freely accessible for adoption by interested institutions. (Alice Schmidt Hanbidge et al. (2017))
