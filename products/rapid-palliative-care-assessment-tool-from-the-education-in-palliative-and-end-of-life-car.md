# Rapid Palliative Care Assessment Tool from the Education in Palliative and End-of-life Care (EPEC) Project Curriculum

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A structured secondary-survey tool for rapidly assessing advance directives, symptom relief, caregivers, and decision-making capacity in palliative-care situations, developed for the EPEC Project curriculum.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Rapid Palliative Care Assessment — A Secondary Survey of ABCDs (EPEC)**: A Rapid Palliative Care Assessment Tool from the Education in Palliative and End-of-life Care (EPEC) Project Curriculum, structured as a secondary survey of ABCDs: A — advance directives (documents detailing wishes for life-sustaining measures); B — better (how to help the patient feel better and manage symptoms during acute resuscitation); C — caregivers (anyone at the bedside, in the waiting area, or reachable by phone, considering their needs); D — decision-making capacity (whether the patient can make their own care decisions). It was "included in each educational feedback session" of the simulation intervention. (Goldonowicz Jessica M. et al. (2018))
