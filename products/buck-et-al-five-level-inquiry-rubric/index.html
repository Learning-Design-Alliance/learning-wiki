Buck et al. five-level inquiry rubric¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A five-level rubric developed by Buck et al. for assessing the degree of student-generated inquiry in laboratory activities across six investigation characteristics.

Components¶

  • Buck et al. five-level inquiry rubric for classifying laboratory activities: A rubric defining "five levels of inquiry that range from conﬁrmation to authentic inquiry", based on the presence or absence of six characteristics of scientific investigations: problem/question, theory/background, procedures/design, results analysis, results communication, and conclusions. An activity's inquiry level is determined by how many characteristics are student-generated versus provided by the manual or instructor. The article uses the rubric to audit all eleven laboratory classes, target the three lowest-inquiry classes for revision, and verify that revisions raised inquiry proportions. (Grissom et al. (2015))

Claims¶

  • Rubric-guided revisions measurably shifted the inquiry-level composition of the three targeted laboratory classes toward higher levels [+M]
