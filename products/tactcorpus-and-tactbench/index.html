TACTCorpus and TACTBench¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Dongjie Yang et al.'s released corpus, diagnostic benchmark, annotations, and model weights support reproducible research on pedagogically adaptive next-turn generation for one-to-one ESL tutoring.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Taxonomy-guided post-training raises TACTutor's TACT_Overall on TACTBench from 0.629 (backbone) to 0.832, the highest among evaluated models [+M]
  • Post-training improves TACTutor's auxiliary guardrail flags on TACTBench: accept rate rises from 0.603 to 0.872 and leak-or-overhelp falls from 0.346 to 0.026 [+M]
