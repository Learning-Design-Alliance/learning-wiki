## Description
A trauma-team workflow framework and associated checklist developed for multidisciplinary teams to coordinate reception and resuscitation during simulated and clinical trauma scenarios.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Trauma Team Time-out Model as a standardising framework for team-based trauma reception and resuscitation**: The article introduces the Trauma Team Time-out Model as part of its lecture content on trauma team dynamics and human factors, and encourages participants to use the Trauma Team Time-out Checklist during simulated scenarios. It positions the model as defining the workflow and activities of a high-functioning trauma team, in contrast to the serial single-provider approach of ATLS and the WHO trauma checklist's safety framework, which the authors state "do not define the workflow and activities of a high‐functioning trauma team". (Fitzgerald MC et al. (2022))
