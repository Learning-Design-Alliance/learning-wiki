# From sheltered workshop to university

## Description
A three-year qualification programme developed and implemented by participating universities to prepare adults with intellectual disabilities and sheltered-workshop experience for inclusive educational-specialist roles through theory, practice placements, psychosocial support, and co-teaching.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Three-year qualification programme 'From sheltered workshop to university' for educational specialists with intellectual disabilities**: A conceptual and practice-oriented guideline describing a "structured three-year qualification program featuring modules on work and education, participation, educational practice, teaching methods, and assessment" that prepares adults with intellectual disabilities and prior sheltered-workshop experience for employment as educational specialists at universities. Graduates act as experts by experience, co-design and co-deliver teaching using biographical material, work in co-teaching tandems with academic staff, and function as change agents for inclusive institutional development. Several cohorts of approximately 6–8 participants have completed the qualification at different universities. (Felix Gorny et al. (2026))
