# Learn.Plan.Succeed.

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Learn.Plan.Succeed. is a district-run college- and career-readiness advising programme that uses Individualized Learning Plans, software, curricula, and advising frameworks to track students’ milestones and postsecondary pathways.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Learn.Plan.Succeed. (LPS) initiative with Individualized Learning Plans**: Through the district's Learn.Plan.Succeed. (LPS) initiative, students track their completion of college and career exploration and preparation milestones via an Individualized Learning Plan (ILP). The report states the district has established software, curricula, and advising frameworks to monitor and support LPS, making it a promising system for organizing college and career advising across students over time. LPS requires students to select one of six postsecondary pathways by grade 12 and provide evidence of their plan to graduate. (Illinois Workforce et al. (2024))

Through the district's Learn.Plan.Succeed. (LPS) initiative, students track their completion of college and career exploration and preparation milestones via an Individualized Learning Plan (ILP). - Software, curricula, and advising frameworks to monitor and support milestone completion
#### Constraints

### Target Learners
- Students in grades 6 through 12 in Chicago Public Schools

### Learning Goals
- Postsecondary pathway selection
- college and career exploration
