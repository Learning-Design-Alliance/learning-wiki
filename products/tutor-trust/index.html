Tutor Trust¶

Description¶

Tutor Trust is a Manchester charity programme that recruits and trains university students and recent graduates to provide affordable small-group and one-to-one tuition, especially in schools serving disadvantaged pupils.

Components¶

  • Tutor Trust affordable tuition model with seven-point tuition plan: The Tutor Trust is a Manchester-based charity providing affordable small group and one-to-one tuition by recruiting university students and recent graduates, enabling competitive rates, and predominantly supporting schools in challenging communities and pupils who are looked-after or eligible for free school meals. Tutors pass a two-stage recruitment process and receive a minimum of two-and-a-half days of structured training. "All tutors were introduced to a seven-point tuition plan to structure tuition sessions (introduction, remember, model, try, apply, secure, reflect) during their training, but encouraged to deviate from it as appropriate." (Emily Buchanan et al. (2015))
  • Tutor Trust affordable tuition programme: university-student tutors, selective recruitment, structured training and a seven-point tuition plan: The Tutor Trust is a Manchester-based charity providing "affordable small group and one- to-one tuition, predominantly to disadvantaged pupils in schools in challenging communities", using university students and recent graduates as tutors on a not-for-profit basis. It screens applicants rigorously, accepting around 30%, requires at least an 'A' at GCSE in the tutored subject, and delivers a minimum of two-and-a-half days of structured training covering safeguarding, lesson planning, behaviour management and subject-specific teaching. Tutors are introduced to a "seven- point tuition plan to structure tuition sessions (introduction, remember, model, try, apply, secure, reflect)" but encouraged to deviate as appropriate. Schools chose which pupils received tuition, with no prescribed selection criteria. (Buchanan et al. (2015))

Claims¶

  • Participating schools were positive about the tuition and keen to work with the Tutor Trust again, and staff saw a need for more affordable high-quality tuition [+W]
  • Schools perceived Tutor Trust tuition as most effective when tutors had strong pedagogical skills, subject knowledge, and could engage pupils and target sessions appropriately [+W]
