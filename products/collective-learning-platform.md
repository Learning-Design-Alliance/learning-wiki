## Description
An online collective-intelligence platform developed by researchers at the University of Zaragoza and Kampal Data Solutions that moderates networked participant interactions to generate collaborative solutions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Collective Learning online platform**: Collective Learning is an online collective intelligence tool developed by the Institute for Biocomputation and Physics of Complex Systems (BIFI) and Kampal Data Solutions, "designed to apply collective intelligence to certain problems or tasks with the aim of achieving high-quality solutions through a model of successive social interactions". It deploys actions such as edit, view, copy, permutation of users, modifying responses and response deletion across seven phases, with an AI acting as moderator selecting Top 10 answers. (Bautista Alcaine et al. (2024))

### Claims
- [Pre-service teachers' answer scores on cyberbullying identification, typology, confrontation and prevention questions increase significantly across the seven phases of a Collective Learning session](../claims/collective-learning-cyberbullying-score-increase.md) [+M]
