## Description
Net.Create is a network-visualization platform developed by Craig and colleagues that enables students to co-construct representations of historical events and relationships for collaborative reasoning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Net.Create network visualization platform for collaborative historical knowledge building**: Net.Create is a platform presented by Craig et al. that "affords the opportunity for students to co-construct a network representation of pieces of history". Students reason separately and together about what happened and why, layering their own interpretation over network structures that serve as scaffolding for joint reasoning. The editorial describes its use in history instruction on the time of Claudius, Agrippina, and Nero. (Sanna Järvelä · Carolyn Rosé (2021))

### Claims
- [Students' collaborative historical reasoning evolved over two days while co-constructing network representations with Net.Create, per the editorial's report of Craig et al.](../claims/netcreate-collaborative-historical-reasoning-evolved.md) [+M]
