## Description
Tarski's World is a computer-based learning environment developed by Barwise and Etchemendy for teaching and practicing first-order logic through visual, manipulable worlds.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Tarski's World computer-based learning environment for teaching first-order logic**: Tarski's World is a computer-based learning environment designed for teaching first-order logic (Barwise & Etchemendy, 1992), used in this study to teach the logical connective, conditional. In the experiment, instructions for 72 first- and second-year social science students were varied within it, including "a visual representation in which geometrical objects could be manipulated". It served as the delivery setting for the guidance and manipulation variables. (Tessa Eysink et al. (2002))
