## Description
The Dutch Medical Leadership framework version 1.0 is an open-access national competency framework defining medical-leadership competencies for Dutch physicians and medical educators, created and maintained by PML, the University of Twente, and the Royal Dutch Medical Association.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **DML framework version 1.0 as an open-access national knowledge artefact**: The 1.0 Dutch Medical Leadership framework is an open-access, evidence-based national competency framework launched in December 2015 by a consortium of PML, the University of Twente and the Royal Dutch Medical Association (KNMG). The article calls it "a canonical 'knowledge artefact' created by a community of practice", comprising a contemporary ML definition and 12 domains of four competencies each. Its final version and development process are published at https://osf.io/qknds/, and it was later translated into English via professional, topical-expert and backward translation. (Keijser Wouter A. et al. (2019))
