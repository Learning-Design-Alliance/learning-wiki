Retrospective academic and administrative records dataset of 922 postgraduate public health students (HIPH, Alexandria University, 2020–2024)

A retrospective institutional dataset of 922 postgraduate public health students' demographic, pre-enrollment, course-registration, withdrawal, and academic-performance records, collected by the High Institute of Public Health at Alexandria University for predicting final CGPA.
