## Description
Numbers in Nappies is an early-childhood flashcard intervention that uses repeated visual and auditory presentation of numbers, shapes, and colours for infants and toddlers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Image-brain stimulation rationale for infant flashcard learning**: The programme's theoretical basis, drawn from Doman and Doman (2005) and Shichida (1993), holds that the right brain or image brain, which the article says "is dominant between the ages of 0 and 3", allows immediate recall of information stored from flashcards, while the more logical left brain relies on repetition to absorb information. The intervention therefore presents number, shape and colour concepts in isolation and repetitively, aiming to strengthen synaptic connections during the first year of life, a period of exuberant synaptogenesis and overlapping sensitive periods.
