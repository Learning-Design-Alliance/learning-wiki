## Description
myPLACE Online is a platform of the Philadelphia Mayor's Commission on Literacy, described as "the first online cohort learning program for low-literate adults." It functions as "a single electronic registration that adult learners fill out to access services," and all 30 of the Commission's partner agencies use this registration in their enrollment processes and can access information for any learner in the system. It emerged from a $1 million demonstration project allocated by Mayor Michael Nutter in 2011 that scaled from the city budget, illustrating a municipally funded adult learning market.
