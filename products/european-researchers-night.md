# European Researchers' Night

## Description
A Europe-wide public-engagement programme in which researchers meet citizens through laboratory demonstrations, interactive activities, and adapted communication formats, organized through local participating institutions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Lessons-learnt pattern: direct contact with researchers and interactive activities improve public understanding of the researcher's role**: The paper distils ten years of European Researchers' Night implementation into a lessons-learnt table pairing observed public demands with adopted solutions. One recurring lesson is that "people understand better about the role of researchers when they have the opportunity to see what they do in their labs and what are the possible applications of their job for a 'better life'", with the adopted solution to "increase the direct contact between researchers and common people". Other paired lessons cover dedicated areas for kids and parents, improving researchers' communication skills with experts, and expanding interactive activities. (S. Arnone et al. (2016))
- **European Researchers' Night activity typology: nine macro areas of public science-engagement activities**: The ERN organizes about 400 activities per year divided into macro areas: hands-on activities (small interactive experiments with kids as main target), workshops, exhibitions, science shows, demonstrations, competitions, games and quizzes, informal talks with researchers, guided tours, and science trips. Each macro area has a distinct target audience and format, from "small interactive experiments designed and presented by researchers, requiring the participation of the general public, with kids as the main target" to visits of natural and historical sites accompanied by a scientist. The article uses this typology to describe the project's methodology for bringing researchers and the public together. (G. Mazzitelli et al. (2017))
- **Frascati Scienza European Researchers' Night project: twelve-year growth to 50,000 attendees and 50+ partners in 30 Italian cities**: The European Researchers' Night is a European Commission-funded project (Marie Skłodowska-Curie actions, Grant Agreement No. 722952) held every last Friday of September to bring researchers closer to the public and stimulate interest in science careers. Coordinated since 2006 by the volunteers' association Frascati Scienza, it grew from 4000 visitors at the INFN Frascati laboratory to "more than 50.000 attendees, a media plan with 30 millions contacts, about 400 activities offered and 1800 researchers actively involved" in the 2017 MADE IN SCIENCE edition, operating in over 30 Italian cities with more than 50 scientific partners. (G. Mazzitelli et al. (2017))
- **Frascati Scienza's diversified public-engagement event format repertoire for researcher-public encounters**: The NIGHT programme is built from a repertoire of about 400 events per year for the general public, based on science shows, hands-on experiments, demos, simulations, workshops and games. The article enumerates formats including Citizen Scientists interactive experiences, "GigaLabs, great science games designed especially for kids and teens with a high impact scenography", School of Science classes, research internships, guided tours, Street Scientists, Science Chats, Science Shows and interactive Science Exhibitions. (G. Mazzitelli et al. (2019))
