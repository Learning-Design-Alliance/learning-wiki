## Description
A Europe-wide public-engagement programme in which researchers meet citizens through laboratory demonstrations, interactive activities, and adapted communication formats, organized through local participating institutions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Lessons-learnt pattern: direct contact with researchers and interactive activities improve public understanding of the researcher's role**: The paper distils ten years of European Researchers' Night implementation into a lessons-learnt table pairing observed public demands with adopted solutions. One recurring lesson is that "people understand better about the role of researchers when they have the opportunity to see what they do in their labs and what are the possible applications of their job for a 'better life'", with the adopted solution to "increase the direct contact between researchers and common people". Other paired lessons cover dedicated areas for kids and parents, improving researchers' communication skills with experts, and expanding interactive activities. (S. Arnone et al. (2016))
