Roads to Success¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 6 claims (4 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Roads to Success is a school and career planning programme that supports students in exploring careers, understanding pathways to career success, and developing school and career plans.

Components¶

  • Roads to Success education and career planning program: Roads to Success is a school and career planning program evaluated over a 2007-2009 time frame in high-poverty rural schools. The report assesses "the impact of two years of exposure to the school and career planning program, Roads to Success, on eighth graders from high-poverty rural schools." It targets career planning knowledge, confidence, and engagement with school staff around career and school plans. (Duncan Chaplin et al. (2010))

Claims¶

  • Exploratory analyses suggest Roads to Success increased confidence in choosing careers and understanding career success requirements [+W]
  • Exploratory analyses suggest Roads to Success positively impacted eighth graders' talking to school staff about career and school plans [+W]
  • Estimated impacts of Roads to Success on student behaviors were mixed [~W]
  • Two years of Roads to Success exposure had no measurable effect on eighth graders' desire to learn job skills or their study and learning habits [-W]
  • Roads to Success was evaluated as part of an associated project running 2007-2009 [+W]
  • Funding cuts in 2008 ended the Roads to Success program and its evaluation in 2009 [+W]
