# Fifteen-item SDT-based survey instrument blending IMI and LCQ subscales on a four-point scale

## Description
A 15-item self-report assessment developed by Stieha et al. for undergraduate STEM students, combining modified Learning Climate Questionnaire and Intrinsic Motivation Inventory subscales to measure perceived autonomy support, competence, peer-relatedness, and effort.
