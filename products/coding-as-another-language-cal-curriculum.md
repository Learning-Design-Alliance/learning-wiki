## Description
A Bers-developed preschool and kindergarten coding-literacy curriculum combining unplugged and ScratchJr activities to develop coding, computational-thinking, creative-expression, and socio-emotional skills.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Coding as Another Language (CAL) framework: coding as a literacy acquired through five consecutive stages**: Coding as Another Language (CAL; Bers, 2019) is a pedagogical approach that "recognizes coding as a form of literacy—as a language like any other", bridging computer science powerful ideas with language arts via the ScratchJr block platform. It stems from three theoretical frameworks: the Curriculum Research Framework, Constructionism, and Positive Technological Development. Bers proposes "five consecutive coding stages"—Emergent, Coding and Decoding, Fluency, New Knowledge, and Purposefulness—corresponding to the accumulative consolidation of traditional literacy, with the Fluency stage marking the shift from learning to read/write/code to using these skills for learning. (Avia Ben-Ari et al. (2025))
