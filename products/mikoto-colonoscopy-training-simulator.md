# Mikoto colonoscopy training simulator

## Description
Mikoto is a commercially available colonoscopy-training simulator made by R ZERO Inc. that provides a three-dimensional colon model, realistic visual and haptic feedback, adjustable difficulty, and objective performance scoring.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Mikoto colonoscopy training simulator with self-directed 100-session curriculum**: The mikoto simulator (R ZERO Inc., Tottori, Japan), commercially available since 2023, replicates colonoscopy using "a three‐dimensional colon model derived from computed tomography data and a flexible silicone–resin structure designed to provide realistic visual and haptic feedback." It generates a composite performance score from insertion-related parameters (CIT, loop formation, abdominal pressure, landmark navigation), offers adjustable difficulty, and provides objective feedback. In the trial, trainees completed 100 self-directed sessions over about 2 weeks without continuous instructor supervision. (Self-Directed Mikoto Simulator Training for Colonoscopy in Gastroenterology Trainees: An I)
