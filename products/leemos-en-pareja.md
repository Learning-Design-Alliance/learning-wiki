## Description
A 12-week peer-tutoring reading programme for primary students, developed and implemented through teacher training, structured activity sheets, and family involvement.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The Leemos en pareja peer tutoring reading programme as a framework for developing Reading Self-Concept**: The Leemos en pareja programme is a peer tutoring reading intervention grounded on three conceptual bases: peer tutoring, reading competence and family involvement, with a teacher training process implementing peer tutoring. It runs for 12 weeks, with two 30-minute sessions each week, revolving around an Activity Sheet designed by teachers to guide the pairs' interaction through pre-reading, while-reading and post-reading activities. The article uses this framework to examine changes in Reading Self-Concept and to identify the processes responsible for the observed changes. (Flores et al. (2013))

### Claims
- [Participation in a 12-week peer tutoring reading programme is associated with statistically significant Reading Self-Concept gains in the intervention group, while a comparison group using the same Activity Sheets shows no significant change](../claims/peer-tutoring-reading-programme-rsc-gains-intervention-group.md) [+M]
