## Description
Inclusive Innovation is an equity-centered research and development process in which "district-community equity challenges are co-researched and innovative equity-centered solutions centering the needs of HSE students are co-designed in partnership with district and community stakeholders." Districts and communities work on Core Teams that co-construct an inclusive team culture enabling power-sharing and equitable decision-making. The process moves through five phases — Connect and Commit, Inquire and Investigate, Design and Develop, Implement and Iterate, and Sustain and Scale — and is anchored by six Core Tenets (Collective Leadership; Student Voice and Leadership; Context Expertise and Proximity; Continuity of Equity; Reimagine Progress; Build Capacity). The RCS project used this process to develop Socratic Circles OER.

- A district-community Core Team reflecting diverse role types (district leaders, teachers, parents, community members, students) with co-leads guiding research and design activities
#### Constraints

### Target Learners
- Historically and systematically excluded K-12 students

## Description
The Inclusive Innovation R&D model is an equity-centered education research and development framework organized around six core tenets that Digital Promise developed and refined over three years with over 30 school districts. Four practice tenets (Collective Ownership, Student Voice and Leadership, Context Expertise and Proximity, Continuity of Equity) describe how the R&D process is conducted, and two impact tenets (Reimagine Progress, Build Capacity) describe the outcomes it targets. The model is premised on the understanding that "practice informs product" so that solutions enabling historically and systematically excluded students to thrive must reflect inclusive practices. It defines historically and systematically excluded learners as those from low-income backgrounds, rural learners, learners with learning differences, and Black, Indigenous, and Latino/a learners.

#### Constraints

### Target Learners
- historically and systematically excluded learners including low-income, rural, learning-difference, Black, Indigenous, and Latino/a students
