# Digital Promise's Inclusive Innovation model

## Description
An equity-centered research-and-development framework created by Digital Promise that places historically marginalized communities at the center of identifying, designing, developing, and implementing educational and workforce technologies.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Inclusive Innovation model centering marginalized communities in design and development**: Digital Promise's Inclusive Innovation model holds that existing R&D models often fail historically marginalized populations, and that innovation must move beyond inviting marginalized voices to conversation. As the report states, "it places them at the center of design, development, and implementation." In this project the model was operationalized as co-design: frontline workers served as participant-advisors in three cohorts of bi-weekly video design sessions, with a Technical Work Group and LER pilots iterating on the resulting principles, followed by a broader survey for validation. (Inclusive Design Principles for Learning and Employment Records: Co-Designing for Equity)
- **Inclusive Innovation model: a phased process in which people closest to the challenge lead, participate in, and benefit from innovation**: The Inclusive Innovation model is a formalized co-design framework guiding the San Diego STEM Pathways Initiative. The report defines it as "a formalized process by which the people closest to the challenge lead, participate in, and benefit from innovation". Figure 3 presents phases from Readiness and Connect & Commit through Inquire & Investigate, Design & Develop, Implement & Iterate, and Sustain & Scale, each with associated artifacts such as a Community Charter, Solution Prototype, and Codified Solution. The Community Advisory Committee used the model to structure its collaboration, moving from establishing core values and root cause analysis to developing solution concepts. (Babineau et al. (2025))
- **The Inclusive Innovation model: an equity-centered R&D process with five phases that prioritizes the voices of those most impacted**: The report describes the Inclusive Innovation approach as "An equity-centered R&D model that engages communities—students, parents, families, and community leaders—in partnership with district leaders, educators, researchers, and developers to create novel solutions to complex educational challenges." It encompasses five phases: connect and commit, inquire and investigate, design and develop, implement and iterate, and sustain and scale. The model intentionally prioritizes the perspectives, experiences, and ideas of those most impacted by a challenge, and in this project it structured LEA Design Teams' work from problem identification through pilot launch. (Recruitment and Retention: Pilot Solutions Designed by Teachers of Color (Phase I))
- **Collaborative Innovation model**: Collaborative Innovation is Digital Promise's research and development model underpinning its regional pathways work: "a structured process where those most directly impacted by educational challenges lead, shape, and benefit from the solutions". As an R&D model, it engages communities and schools in the co-research and co-design of innovative approaches, centering the voices of students, families, educators, and community members alongside researchers and solution developers. The report claims the resulting solutions are more effective, relevant, and responsive, particularly for rural learners and historically underserved communities, and it grounded the co-design of district cybersecurity pathways in the national initiative. (Babineau et al. (2025))
