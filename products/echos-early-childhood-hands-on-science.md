# ECHOS: Early Childhood Hands-On Science

## Description
A preschool hands-on science curriculum developed by the Miami Science Museum, comprising guided science units, integration cards, and professional development for Head Start educators and support staff.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ECHOS curriculum: nine units of guided week-long science lessons with integration cards**: ECHOS: Early Childhood Hands-On Science is a comprehensive set of preschool science lessons developed at the Miami Science Museum, "sequenced to lead children toward a deeper understanding of science content and the use of science process skills." It consists of nine units, each with four guided week-long lessons introduced in 20-minute segments to small groups of 6–8 children, supplemented by 12 integration cards (i-Cards) per unit — 108 for the program — delivering open-ended explorations in language and literacy, mathematics, and creative arts, typically led by teacher assistants, volunteers, or parents while the teacher runs the core lesson. (Brown et al. (2013))
