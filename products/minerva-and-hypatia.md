## Description
Event-analysis programs developed by Rutherford Appleton Laboratory, Birmingham University, and the University of Athens that enable students to explore real ATLAS particle-collision data.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Minerva and Hypatia event-analysis programs for student exploration of real ATLAS collision data**: Minerva (Rutherford Appleton Laboratory and Birmingham University) and Hypatia (University of Athens) are analysis programs created so that high school students can analyze samples of actual ATLAS events. The article states that "several analysis programs were created for high school students to make it possible for them to analyze samples of actual ATLAS events, which were tested at worldwide masterclasses." The programs remain in use in International Masterclasses for analysis of the W and Z particles, the mediators of the weak force. (R. Michael Barnett (2023))
