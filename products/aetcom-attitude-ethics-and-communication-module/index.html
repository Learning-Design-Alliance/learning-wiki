AETCOM (Attitude, Ethics, and Communication) module¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A structured curriculum module for medical students that develops attitudes, ethics, communication skills, and sociocultural and medico-legal understanding, delivered within medical education programmes.

Components¶

  • AETCOM (Attitude, Ethics, and Communication) module for integrating communication skills into medical curricula: The AETCOM module is a structured curriculum module focusing on attitudes, ethics, and communication, cited by the review as an example of integrating communication skills into the teaching curriculum. The review reports that "This module has been well-received by medical students, with a significant majority of students reporting an improved understanding of sociocultural, ethical, and medico-legal aspects." Panel discussions and reflective exercises are noted as complementary andragogical approaches within communication skills teaching. (Zagalo Daniela Mariana et al. (2026))
