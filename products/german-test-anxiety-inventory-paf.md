## Description
A 20-item self-report questionnaire measuring worry, interference, lack of confidence, and arousal facets of test anxiety in students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **German Test Anxiety Inventory (PAF) four-facet model of test anxiety**: The PAF conceptualizes test anxiety as comprising three cognitive facets and one emotional facet. As the article states, "The PAF differentiates between worry, interference, and lack of confidence as cognitive facets and arousal as an emotional facet of test anxiety." Worry refers to concerns about a test and the consequences of failing; interference captures test-irrelevant thoughts experienced as distracting; lack of confidence refers to low confidence of performing well, similar to academic self-efficacy but specific to the test situation; arousal refers to bodily reactions such as a pounding heart. The 20-item questionnaire uses five items per subscale rated on a 4-point Likert scale. (Schillinger et al. (2021))
