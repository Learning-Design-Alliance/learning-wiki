# Future Ready Schools—Librarian Framework (FRS-LF)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A framework developed by the U.S. Department of Education's Office of Educational Technology and the American Institutes for Research to guide K–12 schools in using digital resources through pedagogy, collaboration, and systems and policies.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Future Ready Schools—Librarian Framework (FRS-LF) three meta-constructs of school librarianship**: The Future Ready Schools—Librarian Framework (FRS-LF), developed by the U.S. Department of Education's Office of Educational Technology and the American Institutes for Research, guides schools to "apply digital resources for effective learning outcomes." It organizes school librarianship into three meta-constructs: pedagogy (Use of Space and Time; Literacy; Curriculum, Instruction, and Assessment), collaboration (Community Partnerships; Collaborative Leadership; Personalized Professional Development), and systems and policies (Robust Infrastructure; Budget and Resources; Data and Privacy). The article uses it as the lens for surveying stakeholder alignment. (Felicia Warden · Andrew A. Tawfik (2026))
