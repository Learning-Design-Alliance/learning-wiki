## Description
CaseTrain is a web-based case-player software product developed at the University of Würzburg for delivering interactive case-based e-learning with embedded questions, media, and feedback.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CaseTrain web-based case player for case-based e-learning in WueCampus**: CaseTrain is a web-based case player developed at the University of Würzburg to enable case-based learning within blended-learning concepts; the article describes it as "einen webbasierten Fallplayer, der in die Lehrangebote der Plattform WueCampus" integrated. Each case opens with a clinical case introduction, followed by 6–12 self-test questions including image and video material, with feedback explaining correct and incorrect options after each answer. Cases could be restarted at will and counted as successfully completed from 60% correct answers. Fifteen ENT patient cases aligned with exam-relevant lecture topics were implemented. (Engert J et al. (2024))
