# Edpuzzle

## Description
Edpuzzle is a software platform developed and operated by Edpuzzle that lets educators embed questions, commentary, and quizzes in videos and track learners’ viewing and responses.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Edpuzzle interactive video-learning platform**: Edpuzzle is an interactive learning platform that turns passive video viewing into active learning by letting teachers weave questions, commentary, and engaging elements into videos, slides, and quizzes at precise moments. The article describes it as a tool that "transforms how educators deliver video-based instruction by turning passive viewing into active learning experiences", usable across grade levels and subjects, with viewing analytics, LMS integrations, and compatibility with flipped, distance, and face-to-face instruction.
