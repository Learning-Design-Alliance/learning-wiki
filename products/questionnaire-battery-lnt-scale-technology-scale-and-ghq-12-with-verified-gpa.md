# Questionnaire battery: LNT scale, technology scale, and GHQ-12 with verified GPA

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A three-part online questionnaire battery combining measures of lighting, noise and temperature, technology adequacy for online learning, psychological distress, sociodemographics, and verified academic performance, used by the study researchers with undergraduate medical students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Questionnaire battery: LNT scale, technology scale, and GHQ-12 with verified GPA**: The study used a three-part online self-administered questionnaire: a 9-item LNT scale covering "lighting, noise, and temperature", a 6-item technology scale measuring adequacy of technology for online classes, and the 12-item GHQ-12 for psychological distress, plus a 9-item proforma for sociodemographics and GPA. GPA was verified with the academic office. In this sample the measurement models showed good fit (e.g., LNT: CFI = 0.99, TLI = 0.98, SRMR = 0.03, RMSEA = 0.03). (Taleb Ola K. et al. (2023))
