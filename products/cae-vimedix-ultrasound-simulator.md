# CAE Vimedix ultrasound simulator

## Description
CAE Vimedix is a CAE Healthcare ultrasound-training simulator combining a probe, computer display, and anatomical phantom for practicing probe handling, image interpretation, diagnosis, and decision-making.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Vimedix Integrated Ultrasound Virtual Training System used as the simulation platform for ICU nurse training**: The Vimedix Integrated Ultrasound Virtual Training System (USA) served as the simulation platform for the experimental group's practical training. The ultrasound simulator "uses real-time dynamic imaging and custom designed human models to perform chest, abdominal, and pelvic scans from basic cases to complex cases", letting nurses examine heart, abdomen, and pelvic structures, practice hand-eye coordination and probe operation, and improve pathology and case recognition abilities. Nurses operated it "on the basis of simulated cases while reviewing and consolidating their theoretical knowledge". (Cheng G et al. (2025))
