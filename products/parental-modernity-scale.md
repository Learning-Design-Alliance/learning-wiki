## Description
A parent self-report instrument measuring traditional and progressive childrearing beliefs, developed from Schaefer's parental modernity construct and used to assess parenting orientations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Parental modernity construct: traditional versus progressive childrearing beliefs**: The parental modernity construct, proposed by Schaefer, captures "individual differences in parents' conceptions of children's inherent characteristics and the related beliefs about how to best approach parenting that stem from those conceptions". Traditional beliefs view children as needing directive parenting and adherence to authority, while progressive beliefs cherish children's curiosity, autonomy, and self-directed informal learning. This study measured both with the Parental Modernity Scale (8 progressive and 22 traditional items) and treated them as two separate factors predicting practices and outcomes. (Ren et al. (2024))
