## Description
The TAQE is Taiwan's traditional-Chinese professional licensing examination for audiologists, administered by the relevant national examination authority to assess qualification knowledge across six audiology subject areas.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **2024 Taiwan Audiologist Qualification Examination (TAQE) as a Chinese-language audiology benchmark**: The 2024 TAQE is a professional licensing examination for audiologists written in traditional Chinese, comprising "300 multiple-choice questions across 6 subject areas" with 50 questions per area. In this study the officially released questions and answer sheets were downloaded as a PDF, entered manually into the models' dialogue interfaces with a fixed prompt, and used to benchmark GPT-4-turbo and DeepSeek-R1 by overall accuracy, subject-specific scores, and question-type scores. (Mavragani A et al. (2025))
