# Gardner's Attitude/Motivation Test Battery (AMTB)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 25-item Likert-type questionnaire developed by Gardner for measuring learners' attitudes and motivation in language learning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Gardner's Attitude/Motivation Test Battery (AMTB) questionnaire as the motivation measure**: The study's motivation instrument, prepared by Gardner (2004), used as both pre-test and post-test. It "includes 25 items and in front of each item six alternatives" from Strongly disagree to Strongly agree, scored on a one-to-six Likert scale. Reliability was checked with Cronbach's Alpha, and the article reports "Cronbach's Alpha Coefficient for this measurement is 0.76", above its stated 0.7 threshold. Participants answered by underlining or circling alternatives as quickly as possible. (Asghar Salimi (2015))
