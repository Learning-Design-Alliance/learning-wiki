## Description
An author-developed two-part reading-and-writing performance test created for Alharbi (2015) to assess EFL college students' reading comprehension and argumentative writing before and after instruction.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Integrated Reading/Writing Test with two parts assessing reading and writing separately**: A performance reading/writing test used for pre-tests and post-tests to obtain gain scores. Part I asks participants to read two passages and write short answers to comprehension, analysis and synthesis questions; Part II requires an argument essay on a related topic. Part I scores assess reading skills separately while Part II assesses writing skills; the overall score indicates integrated reading/writing performance. Internal consistency was significant at .01 level (reading r = .876, writing r = .861 with total score), and Cronbach's alpha was 0.81. (Alharbi (2015))
