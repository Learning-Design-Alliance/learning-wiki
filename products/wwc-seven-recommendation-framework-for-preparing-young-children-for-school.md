# WWC seven-recommendation framework for preparing young children for school

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)

## Description
This practice guide organizes school-readiness instruction into seven recommendations spanning two social-behavioral domains (social-emotional skills, executive function), two mathematics domains (intentional math instruction, math conversations and language), and three literacy domains (vocabulary and language, letters and sounds, shared book reading). The panel "distilled recent contemporary research into seven easily comprehensible and practical recommendations for preschool educators to use to help prepare children for school." Each recommendation carries a WWC evidence level and implementation steps, examples, and obstacle advice.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Implementation informed by the studies supporting each recommendation plus panel expertise
- Professional development for teachers may be needed to implement practices or a new curriculum effectively
#### Constraints
- Evidence studies predate the COVID-19 pandemic; the panel judges the recommendations remain generalizable
- The guide should be used as a decision-making tool rather than a cookbook

### Target Learners
- Children in the year or two before kindergarten in preschool, prekindergarten, pre-K, Head Start, and formal-curriculum daycare

### Target Learning Objectives
- School readiness across social-emotional, executive function, mathematics, and literacy domains
