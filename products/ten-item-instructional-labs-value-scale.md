# Ten-item instructional labs value scale

## Description
A 10-item, five-point Likert self-report scale developed by LM Dana et al. to measure physics instructional lab instructors' perceived value of instructional labs, including agency, resources, compensation, staffing, and career satisfaction.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Ten-item instructional labs value scale with good internal consistency (Cronbach's alpha 0.828)**: The authors developed a 10-item scale to examine the perceived value of instructional labs by those working in the field, with items such as "Instructional labs are valued" and "I have agency to make choices about the instructional labs." All items were answered on a 5-point Likert scale from strongly disagree to strongly agree, with items 7 and 9 reverse coded. The scale showed "a Cronbach's alpha of 0.828, which corresponds to a good internal consistency between responses on items," and was used to compare faculty and staff respondents by role and to examine individual item distributions. (LM Dana et al. (2023))
