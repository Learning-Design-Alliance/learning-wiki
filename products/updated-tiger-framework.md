# updated TIGER framework

## Description
A role-based framework developed for nursing informatics education that organizes core competency areas across five nursing roles and maps them to biomedical and health informatics domains.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The updated TIGER framework organizes 43 core competency areas for nursing informatics across five nursing roles**: The updated TIGER framework retains the 2018 role-based structure covering Clinical Nursing, Nursing Management, Quality Management in Nursing, Coordination of Interprofessional Care, and IT Management in Nursing. It expands the original list of 24 core competency areas to 43, of which 18 remained unchanged, 16 were newly added, and 9 were renamed or adapted. The framework maps all areas onto the IMIA 2023 Biomedical and Health Informatics education domains. (Hübner Ursula H. et al. (2026))
