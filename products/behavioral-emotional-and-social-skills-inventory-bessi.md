# Behavioral, Emotional, and Social Skills Inventory (BESSI)

## Description
The BESSI is a self-report assessment developed by Yoon Hee Jun and colleagues for measuring 32 social, emotional, and behavioral skill facets organized into five domains.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **BESSI five-domain taxonomy of social, emotional, and behavioral skills**: The Behavioral, Emotional, and Social Skills Inventory (BESSI) organizes SEB skills using the Big Five taxonomy: it "captures 32 SEB skill facets nested within five broad SEB skill domains (social engagement skills, cooperation skills, self-management skills, emotional resilience skills, and innovation skills)." Each domain corresponds to a Big Five trait (e.g., Innovation to Openness, Self-Management to Conscientiousness). Validation work showed the 32 facets can be reliably assessed within a higher-order five-factor structure, and the present study sampled one representative facet per domain. (Yoon Hee Jun et al. (2024))
