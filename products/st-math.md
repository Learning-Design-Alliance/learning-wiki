# ST Math

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3` · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
ST Math is game-based instructional software for K–12 mathematics developed and provided by the MIND Research Institute, using visual-spatial puzzles to support mathematics learning.

# ST Math: a PreK-8 supplemental math program based on visual problem solving

## Description
ST Math is described as "a PreK-8 supplemental program built on the science of how the brain learns", inviting students to "explore, make sense of, and build confidence in math through visual problem solving". It is used by existing general ed teachers or math specialists during regular instructional time, on modern devices from desktops to tablets.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Designed for use by existing general ed teachers or math specialists during regular school instructional time, with no additional staff time required.
#### Constraints
- Smartphone screen size is too small for interactivity; any modern device and O/S from desktops to Chromebooks to tablets is required.

### Target Learners
- PreK-8 students

### Target Learning Goals
- Elementary and middle school mathematics achievement
