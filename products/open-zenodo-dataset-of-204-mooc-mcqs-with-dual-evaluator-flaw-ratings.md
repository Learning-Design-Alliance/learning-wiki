## Description
An openly released spreadsheet dataset containing evaluator ratings, consensus flaw classifications, quantitative features, and prevalence statistics for 204 multiple-choice questions sampled from 18 MOOCs, created by Costello et al. for MOOC designers and assessment researchers.
