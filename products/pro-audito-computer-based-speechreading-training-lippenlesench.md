# Pro audito computer-based speechreading training (lippenlesen.ch)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 3 studies (3 causal), `q3` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A freely accessible web-based speechreading training developed by pro audito Schweiz, providing video instruction, flashcards, and exercises to practise visual speech cues and audiovisual speech comprehension in noise.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Adaptive computerized speechreading training program for deaf children (seven space-themed games, 48 ten-minute sessions)**: A computerized intervention delivered within "a suite of seven space-themed computer games", designed to run across forty-eight 10-min sessions with adaptive algorithms that varied trial difficulty "based on the child's performance". The first 16 sessions trained video-to-picture matching of 103 trained words; sessions 17–48 added trials with orthographic stimuli focusing on mappings between visual speech patterns and letters and words. Sessions carried a narrative structure with a virtual reward collected in an online trophy cabinet, and built-in speechreading assessments occurred every eight sessions. (Pimperton et al. (2019))
