## Description
Beable is a K-12 digital platform for literacy recovery that "customizes content to the student's reading level, interests, and career goals." It is available in English and Spanish with translation support to over 100 languages, and integrates literacy acceleration, intervention response, English language acquisition, core content mastery, and test preparation, connecting literacy with career and essential skills development.
