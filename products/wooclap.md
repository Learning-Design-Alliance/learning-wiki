# Wooclap

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Wooclap is an interactive classroom-response platform made by Wooclap that enables synchronous learner responses, live result visualization, and assessment activities including Script Concordance Tests.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Wooclap platform with integrated Script Concordance Test template**: Wooclap is an interactive online platform used in this study to administer the SCT electronically and synchronously, letting students respond by mobile device, tablet, or computer with results displayed live on the teacher's screen. The article states: "Wooclap was selected due to its unique feature of having an integrated template for SCT (Script Concordance Test) within the system." It allowed tutors to collect, display, and compare student and expert answers on a single platform, providing automatic compilation of scores and immediate visualization of concordance levels between learners and experts. (Aboueisha Hadeel et al. (2025))
