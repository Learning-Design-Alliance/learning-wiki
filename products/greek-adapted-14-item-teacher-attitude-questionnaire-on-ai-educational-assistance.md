# Greek-adapted 14-item teacher-attitude questionnaire on AI educational assistance

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A 14-item five-point Likert questionnaire translated and adapted into Greek to measure secondary science teachers' perceptions of AI benefits and collaboration with AI in teaching, developed for the study by Dimitrios Gousopoulos.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Greek-adapted 14-item teacher-attitude questionnaire based on Nazaretsky et al. (2022)**: The study's measurement instrument is a digital questionnaire "based on the study by Nazaretsky et al. (2022) and translate d and adapted into Greek". It "consisted of 14 five-point Likert scale questions covering two thematic areas: (a) awareness of the benefits of Artificial Intelligence in an educational environment, and (b) collaboration with Artificial Intelligence to improve the teaching process". Responses were analyzed in R, administered before and after the training. (Dimitrios Gousopoulos (2025))
