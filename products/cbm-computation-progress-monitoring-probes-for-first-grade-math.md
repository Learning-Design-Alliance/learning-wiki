## Description
Curriculum-based measurement (CBM) Computation probes are brief standardized math tests used throughout the RTI process. Each probe consists of "25 math computation problems representing the year-long, first-grade math computation curriculum," with problem types constant across forms and facts and positions randomized. Students complete the probe in 2 minutes and are scored by number of correct digits, which the authors say is "a more sensitive index of student change" than whole-problem scoring.
