## Description
The CSL-LTS is an 18-item assessment used with undergraduate nursing students to measure teamwork and leadership knowledge transfer from simulation to clinical practice.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Three-level knowledge transfer interpretation framework for simulation-derived competencies**: The article proposes a three-level interpretation framework for scores on the CSL-LTS, categorising the degree of knowledge transfer from simulation to clinical practice as low, average or high. For each dimension separately, scores of 9–17 indicate low transfer, 18–27 average transfer and 28–36 high transfer; for the joint interpretation of both dimensions, the intervals are 18–34, 35–54 and 55–72 respectively. The article states that 'A score of 55 to 72 points indicates a high level of knowledge transfer from simulation to practice.' This framework operationalises the construct of transfer as a measurable continuum rather than a binary outcome. (González‐Moret et al. (2026))
