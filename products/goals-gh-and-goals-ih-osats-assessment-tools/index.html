GOALS-GH and GOALS-IH OSATS assessment tools¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

GOALS-GH and GOALS-IH are objective structured assessment tools for evaluating technical performance in laparoscopic inguinal and incisional hernia repair among surgical trainees.

Components¶

  • GOALS-GH and GOALS-IH OSATS assessment tools for laparoscopic hernia repair: The GOALS-GH and GOALS-IH are Objective Structured Assessment of Technical Skills (OSATS) tools designed specifically for TEP and TAPP laparoscopic inguinal hernia repair and incisional hernia repair respectively. The review reports "Both were shown to have high inter-rater reliabilities (0.96, 95% CI 0.74–0.99) as well as construct validity", with good correlation between GOALS-IH scores and the number of laparoscopic procedures performed. The review recommends future model developers employ these tools for formal evaluation and comparison. (Pelly T. et al. (2021))

Claims¶

  • The SAW model shows construct validity and good correlation (0.87) between simulated and operating-room laparoscopic ventral/incisional hernia repair performance [+M]
  • Simulation-based training with the McGill Laparoscopic Inguinal Hernia Simulator improved operating-room GOALS-GH scores by a mean of 3.4 points in a randomised trial [+M]
