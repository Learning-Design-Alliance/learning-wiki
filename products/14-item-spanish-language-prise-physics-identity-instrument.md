# 14-item Spanish-language PRiSE physics identity instrument

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A Spanish-language adaptation of the PRiSE physics identity survey developed by González-Peña et al. for assessing recognition, competence/performance, physics interest, and science interest among early-semester Mexican STEM students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Spanish-language adaptation of the PRiSE physics identity instrument for Mexican STEM students**: A Spanish-language adaptation of 28 physics identity items drawn from the PRiSE survey of the Harvard-Smithsonian Center for Astrophysics, rated on a seven-point anchored scale from 0 ("strongly disagree") to 6 ("strongly agree"). Items were translated independently by five bilingual STEM professors, refined through three focus groups for cultural and face validity, and reduced by factor analyses to a 14-item four-subconstruct final version measuring recognition, competence/performance, physics interest, and science interest, whose average yields a physics identity score. (O. I. González-Peña et al. (2024))
