# Danoosh Academy (Dayamoz) gamification platform

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A gamified digital learning platform managed by Daya Mobile (Dayamoz) that delivers sequenced content and challenges with points, leaderboards, scoreboards, badges, and multimodal questions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Danoosh Academy (Dayamoz) gamification platform with leaderboards, scoreboards, badges, and sequenced challenges**: The research team developed game elements on the Danoosh company platform, managed by Daya Mobile (Dayamoz), implemented in Python and accessible at https://app.dgamify.ir. The platform, suitable for computers and mobile devices, "features leaderboards, scoreboards, and badges"; students earn points for reading each section and view rankings relative to peers. Challenges use multiple question types (multiple choice, descriptive, audio, video, short answer), with scoring by difficulty level, badges for some questions, and gating so incomplete weeks block access to later content. (Behmanesh Fereshteh et al. (2025))
