## Description
Qookies is a theory-based serious game developed by Caroline Wermann and colleagues that teaches foundational quantum-technology concepts through interactive escape-room-style puzzles and short conceptual units.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Qookies serious game for quantum technology concepts**: Qookies is a theory-based serious game designed to make abstract quantum technology concepts accessible to learners without prior mathematics or physics knowledge. Learners "progress through the game by solving interactive educational puzzles" complemented by short conceptual units in simple language, framed as escape-room-based levels. It targets a heterogeneous audience including school students, university students, and the general public. (Caroline Wermann et al. (2026))
