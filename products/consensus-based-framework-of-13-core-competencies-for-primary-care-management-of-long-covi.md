# Consensus-based framework of 13 core competencies for primary care management of Long COVID

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A consensus-based framework developed by Geyer et al. for organizing competencies that primary care clinicians need to diagnose and manage Long COVID across the six ACGME domains.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Consensus-based framework of 13 core competencies for primary care management of Long COVID, organized within the six ACGME domains**: The article presents a competency framework developed through a modified Delphi needs assessment with surveys, focus groups, and matrix analysis across five stakeholder groups. The final set comprised 13 core competencies aligned with the six ACGME domains (Patient Care, Medical Knowledge, Systems-Based Practice, Interpersonal and Communication Skills, Practice-based Learning and Improvement, Professionalism). The authors state the study "establishes a consensus-based framework of competencies for PCPs managing Long COVID, reflecting the condition's evolving nature and the importance of continuous, relationship-centered care." (Geyer Rachel E. et al. (2026))
