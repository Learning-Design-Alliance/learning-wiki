# Prêt à Négocier (PàN)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A digital information-gap game created by Rueb et al. for intermediate French learners to practise synchronous oral interaction, vocabulary, and negotiation through timed buying-and-selling scenarios.

### Claims
- [Both experimental and control groups improved significantly in French vocabulary over time, with no time-versus-group interaction attributable to the digital game treatment](../claims/pan-no-time-group-interaction-vocabulary.md) [+M]
- [Individual differences produced highly variable vocabulary gains among PàN players, with some participants benefiting far more than others](../claims/pan-individual-differences-vocabulary-gains.md) [+W]
- [Between-group comparisons show no significant difference in pronunciation improvement between the PàN group and the paper-based gamified control group](../claims/pan-vs-paper-no-between-group-difference.md) [-W]
- [Fluency showed a trend toward significance for the treatment group between pre- and post-tests (p=.055), suggesting digital game pressure may push learners to speak faster](../claims/pan-fluency-trend-toward-significance.md) [~W]
