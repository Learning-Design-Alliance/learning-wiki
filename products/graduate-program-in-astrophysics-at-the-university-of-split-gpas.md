# Graduate Program in Astrophysics at the University of Split (GPAS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A graduate astrophysics programme run through a partnership between the University of Split and a non-governmental organisation, combining advanced education, research, and outreach in Croatia and South-Eastern Europe.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **GPAS model: a graduate program as the backbone of a sustainability strategy combining education, research and outreach**: The article proposes a program-sustainability model in which a graduate program is the backbone of a wider initiative whose strategy is 'a carefully designed combination of educational, research, and outreach programs'. The Graduate Program in Astrophysics at the University of Split (GPAS) operates through the synergy of a public university and a non-governmental organisation, and pursues objectives including attracting young people to science careers, promoting interdisciplinary connections, promoting top-level research in Croatia, and setting an example for other institutions in South-Eastern Europe. (Davor Krajnović (2006))
