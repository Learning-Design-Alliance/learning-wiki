# Talking Diabetes blended learning programme

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A blended professional-development programme developed for paediatric diabetes practitioners that combines web-based learning, face-to-face seminars, consultation reports, and trainer feedback to develop communication skills for behaviour-change conversations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The Talking Diabetes blended learning programme for paediatric diabetes practitioners**: The Talking Diabetes programme is a blended learning programme for paediatric diabetes practitioners that provides training in communication strategies and skills for behaviour change conversations. It combines web-based e-learning with face-to-face seminars: the article states the programme provides training in communication strategies and that practitioners work through e-learning modules of approximately 1.5 hours total duration, attend two face-to-face seminars approximately two weeks apart, record off-line learning activities online, and submit reports of three consultations after the second seminar with feedback from pre-assigned trainers. (McNamara Rachel et al. (2010))
