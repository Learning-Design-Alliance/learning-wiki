## Description
The American Association of Variable Star Observers (AAVSO) archival database is a freely disseminated collection of more than 33 million variable-star observations for scientific research and education.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **AAVSO archival database of over 33 million variable-star observations as a resource for student research**: The American Association of Variable Star Observers (AAVSO) is a non-profit society that since 1911 has collected, evaluated, archived and freely disseminated variable-star observations. Its website is described as a "gold mine" of public data containing "over 33 million measurements", made mostly by skilled amateur astronomers. The author's student projects use AAVSO archival visual and photoelectric photometry (PEP) data, with visual data uniquely spanning a century or more. (John R. Percy (2016))
