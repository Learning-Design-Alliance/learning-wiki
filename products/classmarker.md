## Description
ClassMarker is a web-based platform operated by ClassMarker for creating, administering, scoring, and managing online tests.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ClassMarker online testing platform used to deliver the computer-based achievement test**: The computer-assisted version of the achievement test was delivered via www.classmarker.com, which the article describes as a platform that "enables teachers, testers and researchers to prepare and administer online tests." Test-takers logged in with student IDs and passwords, could move back and forth or skip questions, control the allotted time on screen, see scores immediately upon completion, receive immediate feedback showing correct answers, and earn an on-screen certification at 70 points or higher. The system stored all responses for researchers. (Öz et al. (2018))
