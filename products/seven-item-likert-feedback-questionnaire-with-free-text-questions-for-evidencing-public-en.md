# Seven-item Likert feedback questionnaire with free-text questions for evidencing public engagement impact

## Description
A researcher-constructed seven-item Likert and free-text questionnaire used by McLaughlin et al. to measure sixth-form students' self-reported interest, engagement, motivation, and awareness after a science-outreach programme.

### Claims
