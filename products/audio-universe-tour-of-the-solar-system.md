# Audio Universe: Tour of the Solar System

## Description
A free multilingual, pre-rendered multisensory planetarium show created by Chris Harrison and collaborators to teach primary-age learners about the Solar System through coordinated narration, visuals, music, and sonification, including access for blind and visually impaired audiences.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Audio Universe: Tour of the Solar System — a pre-rendered, multi-sensory, BVI-accessible planetarium show**: A pre-rendered audio-visual planetarium show (full-dome and flat-screen, surround and stereo soundtracks) designed with BVI community members, BVI specialist teachers and pupils so it is "suitable for audiences with all sight levels by representing key concepts through sound". Its theme is a spacecraft tour with a "sonification machine" that turns light into sound; planets are represented by different musical instruments with pitches corresponding to their mass. It was released free in December 2021 in English, Italian and Spanish, with narration by a spaceship captain and a blind astronomer character providing role models for BVI viewers. (Chris Harrison et al. (2023))
