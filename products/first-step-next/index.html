First Step Next¶

Description¶

First Step Next is a Tier 2 early-intervention programme for pre-K through grade 2 children with elevated externalizing behavior, combining social-skills instruction, the Green-Card Game, home–school connections, and coach-to-teacher implementation support.

Components¶

  • First Step Next (FSN) Tier 2 early intervention program: First Step Next is a Tier 2 early intervention for pre-K through grade 2 children targeting social skills and academic enablers central to school success. "The program includes three major tasks: social skills instruction, the green-card game, and home-school connections." A trained coach delivers the first 5-7 days (coach phase), then transitions control to the teacher from day 11 onward. The program was evaluated in the reported RCT with 160 at-risk preschoolers. (Feil et al. (2020))
  • First Step Next early intervention program: First Step Next is a targeted early intervention for pre-K through Grade 2 students with moderately severe, primarily externalizing behavioral challenges, implemented by general education teachers in collaboration with a coach. "FSN contains the following elements: social skills (SS) instruction, green card game, and home-school connections; it is implemented across four implementation phases (i.e., preparation, coach, teacher, and maintenance phase)." The coach teaches seven super student skills one-to-one, the class-wide green card game provides non-verbal feedback and points on a fixed interval schedule, and parents sign daily notes and run reinforcement activities at home. (Andy J. Frey et al. (2023))

Claims¶

  • First Step Next significantly improves pro-social behavior in at-risk preschoolers, with Hedges' g effect sizes from .34 to .91 [+M]
  • First Step Next replication RCTs produced small to large effect sizes and statistically significant behavioral gains compared with control conditions [+M]
