# Professional competency framework for GPs in tertiary hospitals in China

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A four-domain, three-level competency framework developed by Wei Yun et al. for assessing and supporting the education, self-evaluation, quality improvement, and multisource feedback of general practitioners in Chinese tertiary hospitals.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Professional competency framework for GPs in tertiary hospitals in China (4 primary, 12 secondary, 54 tertiary indicators)**: A three-level professional competency framework for general practitioners in Chinese tertiary hospitals, developed via a modified Delphi process. It organises competency into four primary domains — medical services, teaching, research and prevention — with the final structure including "4 primary indicators, 12 secondary indicators and 54 tertiary indicators". It is intended for use in medical education, general practice research, quality improvement, GP self-evaluation and multisource feedback. (Wei Yun et al. (2025))
