School Attitude Assessment Survey-Revised (SAAS-R)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A 35-item, five-subscale Likert questionnaire developed by McCoach and Siegle for assessing high-school students' school attitudes, academic self-perceptions, goal valuation, and self-regulation.

Components¶

  • School Attitude Assessment Survey-Revised (SAAS-R) as the attitude outcome measure: The SAAS-R (McCoach & Siegle, 2002) served as the attitude measure in the study. The article describes it as "a 35-question assessment with five subscales, including students' academic self-perceptions, attitudes toward teachers, attitudes toward school, goal valuation and self-regulation." Students answer on a 6-point Likert scale, with scores of one to three suggesting negative attitudes and four to six suggesting positive attitudes. It was administered at the alternative school exit interview (pretest) and at the end of the school year (posttest). (Kelchner et al. (2017))
