# Thomas-Kilmann Conflict MODE Instrument (TKI)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A proprietary self-report assessment developed by CPP, Inc. that measures individual conflict-handling preferences across five conflict modes.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Thomas-Kilmann Conflict MODE instrument for measuring conflict handling styles**: The MODE instrument measures individual conflict handling styles by allocating respondents to the assertiveness and cooperativeness dimensions. "The MODE instrument consists of 30 paired items, which makes a total of 60 statements." Participants choose from each pair the statement that best describes how they usually behave in conflict situations, yielding scores on five modes. The study used it for both pre- and post-testing of experimental and control groups. (Abel Gitimu Waithaka et al. (2015))
