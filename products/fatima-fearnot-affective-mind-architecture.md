# FAtiMA (Fearnot AffecTIve Mind Architecture)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A cognitive-appraisal agent architecture for authoring synthetic agents with goals, emotions, beliefs, action tendencies, planning, and Theory-of-Mind models, used here in the MIXER intercultural-conflict learning environment.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **FAtiMA cognitive appraisal agent architecture with cultural Theory of Mind**: FAtiMA (Fearnot AffecTIve Mind Architecture) is a cognitive appraisal architecture in which events are assessed in relation to agent goals and generate an affective outcome, used in reactive action-tendency rules and a deliberative planning system generating coping behaviours based on hope and fear. For MIXER it was extended with a 1-level Theory of Mind consisting of a collection of Models of Others forming a recursive hierarchical tree, encoding equivalents to Baron-Cohen's Eye-Direction Detection and Shared-Attention Mechanism capabilities. Making the utility of goals depend on cultural values establishes a link between culture and behaviour, so agents' appraisal and action generation are culturally dependent. (Hall et al. (2014))
