# Speak & Improve

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
Speak & Improve is a free browser-based application developed by the University of Cambridge with Cambridge University Press & Assessment and ELiT that provides communicative English-speaking practice and automated CEFR-like proficiency and confidence feedback.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Speak & Improve web application for L2 English speaking practice with automated assessment**: Speak & Improve is a free, browser-based web application from the University of Cambridge with Cambridge University Press & Assessment and ELiT, letting learners practise English speaking on communicative tasks. Users "are able to practice their English speaking and improve their confidence on a wide range of communicative speaking tasks", receiving an auto-marker CEFR-like level and a confidence estimate after each part. Version 1 ran from December 2018 to September 2024, gathering 1.7 million users and over 18.4 million submitted answers. (Kate Knill et al. (2024))
