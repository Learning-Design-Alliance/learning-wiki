# Roebuck's analytic scoring rubric modified by Maftoon & Rabiee (2006)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An analytic writing assessment rubric scoring mechanics, vocabulary, grammar, and organization on five-point component scales, used by the researchers to assess advanced adult EFL learners' argumentative essays.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Roebuck's analytic scoring rubric modified by Maftoon & Rabiee (2006) as the writing assessment checklist**: The study's writing assessment instrument is Roebuck's analytic scoring rubric as modified by Maftoon & Rabiee (2006). It was chosen for its "simple separation of scores that has four components, mechanism, vocabulary, grammar, and organization", each scored 1-5 for a total of 20 marks per essay. All pre-tests, post-tests and treatment essays were scored with it and analyzed in SPSS. (Lily Mahnam; Dariush Nejadansari (2012))
