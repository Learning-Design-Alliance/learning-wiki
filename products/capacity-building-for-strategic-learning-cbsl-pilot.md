# Capacity Building for Strategic Learning (CBSL) pilot

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A Mathematica and Catalyst:Ed programme, supported by the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation, that built nine K–12 education grantees’ equity-centered strategic-learning capacity through project scoping, provider matching, cohort learning, and thought partnership.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Equity-centered strategic learning as an organizational capacity framework**: The partnership defines equity-centered strategic learning as the set of efforts that enables organizations to set goals contributing to increased educational equity for Black and Latino students and students experiencing poverty, establish hypotheses for reaching goals, build organizational culture, practices and systems to implement and test those hypotheses, and use information to improve their model on an ongoing basis. The framework holds that strong equity-centered strategic learning is demonstrated when equity is considered in both the organization's goal and its learning and decision-making approach. (Rabb et al. (2021))

## Description
The CBSL pilot was a partnership running from May 2020 to September 2021 in which "Mathematica and Catalyst:Ed partnered with the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation and a set of nine K–12 education grantees." Its purpose was to strengthen grantees' capacity for equity-centered strategic learning through scoped projects, provider matching, cohort learning activities, and thought partnership. It was prepared for the Gates Foundation and published by Mathematica in October 2021.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires a funder, an intermediary partner (Catalyst:Ed), a technical assistance provider, and a cohort of grantees willing to scope capacity-building projects.
#### Constraints
- Piloted with nine K–12 education grantees over roughly sixteen months (May 2020 to September 2021).

### Target Learners
- K–12 education grantee organizations

### Target Learning Goals
- Strengthening organizational capacity for equity-centered strategic learning
