# German-language 20-item multiple-choice single-select physics proficiency test for middle school based on adapted TIMSS items

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A German-language, 20-item paper-and-pencil multiple-choice physics proficiency test for eighth-grade students, developed by Feser et al. from adapted TIMSS science-assessment items to assess content and procedural knowledge.

### Claims
- [A 20-item German-language TIMSS-based multiple-choice test of middle-school physics proficiency shows sufficient person-separation reliability (0.78) and excellent item-separation reliability (0.97)](../claims/german-timss-physics-test-reliability.md) [+M]
- [The test's correlational validity is in line with expectations: moderate positive correlations with science interest (r = 0.45) and self-concept (r = 0.34), a low negative rank correlation with physics grades (ρ = -0.24), and a low positive rank correlation with cultural and economic capital (ρ = 0.22)](../claims/german-timss-physics-test-correlational-validity.md) [+M]
