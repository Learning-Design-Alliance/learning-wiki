## Description
MoodMap App is a prototype software application developed by Pammer-Schindler et al. for recording, contextualizing, and visualizing individual and collaborative workplace moods.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MoodMap App for self-tracked mood and team awareness**: The MoodMap App is a mood self-tracking prototype whose interface follows Russell's two-dimensional model of affect along valence and arousal, with mood captured by clicking a bi-dimensional mood map based on Itten's colour system. Users can attach free-text notes and context information; moods, notes and context are aggregated and visualized at individual and collaborative levels, including a Compare Me View and a Collaborative View showing others' current mood. It was tested in two field studies, in virtual team meetings and in call-center teams. (Pammer-Schindler et al. (2021))
