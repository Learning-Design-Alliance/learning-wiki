# Analytics Layers for Learning Design framework

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A framework that organizes analytics for learning-design decision-making into learning-experience, learning-design-element, and educator-community layers, developed for use by learning-design stakeholders.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Community analytics layer of the Analytics Layers for Learning Design framework**: The Analytics Layers for Learning Design framework proposes "three layers of data analytics to support and inform decision-making in learning design: learning experience (learning analytics), learning design elements (design analytics) and community of educators (community analytics)." This study's cross-case analysis of motivations, behavior, and contribution quality contributes to the community analytics layer, using data to inform stakeholders in online communities of teachers. (Gutiérrez-Páez et al. (2023))
