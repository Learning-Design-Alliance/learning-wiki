Written discrete-item grammar test of mostly explicit knowledge with three components and split-block test versions¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes

Description¶

A researcher-developed written English-grammar assessment by Jakub Bielak comprising receptive binary-choice and productive constrained constructed-response tasks, administered in parallel split-block versions to Polish senior high school learners.

Claims¶
