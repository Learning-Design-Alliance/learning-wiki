# Heidelberg AalPlus tutor qualification programme

## Description
A four-module, approximately 40-hour qualification programme run by Heidelberg Medical Faculty to prepare medical student tutors in didactics, group leadership, subject-specific teaching, and reflective practice.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Heidelberg AalPlus tutor qualification programme (four modules, about 40 teaching hours)**: A structured didactic qualification programme preparing medical student tutors at Heidelberg Medical Faculty since 2010. "This programme consists of four modules: 1. medical didactics and group leadership, 2. subject-specific training, 3. performance of tutorial courses as well as 4. collegial advice and reflection on the tutors' activities." It comprises about 40 teaching hours on seven training days, and completers receive a Heidelberg Didactic Certificate for Tutors. Tutors then guide small groups of 10 to 12 preclinical students and serve as OSCE examiners. (Homberg Angelika et al. (2019))
- **Four-module longitudinal tutor training curriculum totaling 200 curricular units culminating in a certificate**: A modular peer-tutor training curriculum developed on the Kern cycle and structured "in cooperation with the Department for Key Competencies and Higher Education". It comprises four modules totaling 200 curricular units: a basic course in medical teaching and leading groups (20 units), topic-specific preparation by the responsible departments (60 units), independent conduction of tutorials (84 units), and evaluation of the learning process through supervision, peer observation, and a three-part portfolio (36 units). Successful participation is recognized with the University of Heidelberg Teaching Certificate for Tutors, awarded in an official ceremony. (Fellmer-Drüg Erika et al. (2014))
