Thai computerized attention and working memory battery tests and Bond-Ladder mood scale¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A Thai-language computerized battery of attention and working-memory tasks, paired with a 16-item Bond-Ladder visual analogue mood scale, developed and used by the study authors for Grade 9 assessment.
