## Description
A 16-week programme of scripted gentle-yoga and mindfulness sessions designed for teachers and school personnel and delivered by a certified yoga instructor.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CALM: a yoga-based mindfulness intervention delivered in 20-minute sessions, 4 days per week, over 16 weeks**: CALM is a yoga-based professional development intervention for educators delivered in 20-minute sessions, 4 days per week, over 16 weeks in the school setting. The current study used data from the wait-list control condition of the broader trial evaluating CALM's efficacy, and did not itself evaluate the intervention. (Abenavoli et al. (2014))
