## Description
A 16-week programme of scripted gentle-yoga and mindfulness sessions designed for teachers and school personnel and delivered by a certified yoga instructor.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CALM: a yoga-based mindfulness intervention delivered in 20-minute sessions, 4 days per week, over 16 weeks**: CALM is a yoga-based professional development intervention for educators delivered in 20-minute sessions, 4 days per week, over 16 weeks in the school setting. The current study used data from the wait-list control condition of the broader trial evaluating CALM's efficacy, and did not itself evaluate the intervention. (Abenavoli et al. (2014))
- **Child Anxiety Learning Modules (CALM): a brief six-module CBT-based intervention delivered by school nurses**: CALM is a brief, individual, school-nurse-administered intervention consisting of six modules delivered during brief meetings over 8 weeks. It is based on CBT strategies and was developed and modified for nurse implementation with input from school nurses, national school mental health experts, National Association of School Nurse leadership, and parent and youth feedback. The article states "CALM included the core components of CBT for pediatric anxiety: psychoeducation, relaxation strategies, exposure, cognitive-restructuring, problem-solving, and relapse prevention." These components address the somatic, behavioral, and cognitive manifestations of anxiety. Training was one day, with optional consultation from a doctoral-level clinical psychologist. (Golda S. Ginsburg et al. (2019))
