## Description
A nine-month Ministry of Health and JICA programme combining a standardized curriculum, trained preceptors, and guideline-based management for Vietnamese new graduate nurses seeking national practice certification.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Standard clinical training program for new graduate nurses in Vietnam (standardized curriculum, trained preceptors, management guidelines)**: A nine-month national clinical training program developed by Vietnam's Ministry of Health with JICA, required for new graduate nurses to obtain a national nursing practicing certificate. "The clinical training program developed by the Project was composed of three elements": standardized curriculum-based training, support from trained preceptors, and systematic management per guidelines. The curriculum totals 1520 units (76 OJT, 1324 Off-JT, 120 self-learning) organized around five goal capacities and the Competency Standard's three domains, 25 competencies and 110 indicators. A five-day, 40-unit preceptor training course for experienced nurses accompanied it. (Horii Satoko et al. (2021))
