# LGBT-DOCSS scale (Polish and Spanish versions)

## Description
A validated 18-item self-administered Likert-scale instrument for assessing health professionals' clinical preparedness, attitudes, and knowledge when working with LGBT patients.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **LGBT-DOCSS scale (Polish and Spanish versions) as used in the HERE study**: The LGBT-DOCSS is a validated self-administered instrument for assessing clinical skills in working with LGBT patients, used here in newly validated Polish and Spanish versions. It is an "18-item instrument employs a 7-point Likert scale, with eight items reverse-coded" to score positive attitudes, knowledge and preparedness. Scores yield an overall score plus Clinical Preparedness, Attitudes and Knowledge subscales, with higher scores indicating greater preparedness, more positive attitudes and better knowledge. The Polish version showed a Cronbach's alpha of 0.789 and the Spanish version 0.769. (Karniej Piotr et al. (2025))
