# One-Minute Preceptor (OMP)

## Description
A structured five-microskill clinical teaching framework developed by Neher and colleagues for preceptors to guide learners' clinical reasoning and provide situation-specific feedback.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **DEFT: a four-step clinical teaching framework (Diagnosis, Evidence, Feedback, Teaching) enhancing the one-minute preceptor**: DEFT is a descriptive acronym — Diagnosis, Evidence, Feedback, Teaching, meaning skillful — that condenses the one-minute preceptor's five microskills into four steps and de-emphasizes the temporal 'one-minute' component. The article uses it as 'a potentially helpful reminder to improve the quality of interactions between learners and preceptors', illustrated with a pediatric respiratory infection vignette aligned to Bloom's taxonomy. (Savaria MC et al. (2022))
- **One-Minute Preceptor (OMP) five-stage microskills model of clinical teaching**: The OMP is a preceptor training model developed for clinicians to educate trainees within busy clinical environments without interfering with work. The article describes it as consisting of five stages: making a mutual commitment related to education, adjusting practical training by evaluating the preceptee's level of understanding, teaching general rules and sharing expertise, providing positive feedback on good behavior, and providing constructive feedback correcting mistakes. The PAR team used the OMP as the theoretical framework for the PEP, mapping its stages onto program development, work-standard drafting, six weeks of practical application, and mutual feedback at conferences. (Jeong Hye Won et al. (2021))
- **Table 1 clinical vignette illustrating the DEFT approach for a child with a respiratory illness**: Table 1 presents a sample preceptor-learner interaction about a 3-year-old hospitalized with suspected respiratory infection, mapping each DEFT step to revised Bloom's taxonomy objectives with printed preceptor prompts and learner responses. The article states it was included 'to illustrate the potential value of the OMP/DEFT skills-focused strategy'. (Savaria MC et al. (2022))
