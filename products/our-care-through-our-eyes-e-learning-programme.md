# Our Care Through Our Eyes e-learning programme

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A co-designed e-learning programme of three short packages that trains registered children's nurses to understand self-harm, communicate effectively with children and young people, and assess risk and manage safety; it was developed by children and young people, registered children's nurses, and academics.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **'Our Care Through Our Eyes' co-designed self-harm e-learning programme (three 15-minute RLO packages)**: A digital education intervention made of reusable learning objects — digital resources with a single learning objective engaging users in four learning components — co-designed by children and young people, registered children's nurses and academics. It consists of three short (15 min) e-learning packages covering understanding self-harm and care pathways, effective communication with CYP following self-harm admission, and assessing risk and managing safety. This study evaluated it in a new hospital setting with new outcome measures. (Singh‐Weldon J. et al. (2022))

### Claims
- [In one uncontrolled pre–post study, children's nurses' self-rated antipathy scores towards patients who self-harm, measured by overall SHAS scores, fell from 68.1 before to 62.8 after the 'Our Care Through Our Eyes' e-learning programme, with small decreases across most SHAS sub-scales](../claims/rlo-elearning-reduced-nurse-antipathy-self-harm.md) [+W]
- [Children's nurses reported that lack of confidence and knowledge were barriers to caring for CYP who self-harm, and valued the e-learning programme's communication tools and scenario videos](../claims/nurse-confidence-barriers-rlo-valued-tools.md) [+W]
