Waterford Early Reading Program™¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 6 claims (2 for, 4 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), q2 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Waterford Early Reading Program™ is a software-based K–2 reading curriculum developed and provided by Waterford.org, combining individualized computer sessions with literacy activities, multimedia content, and teacher management tools.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Alphabetics improvement-index findings for Waterford Early Reading Program™ were inconsistent across outcomes, ranging from –26 to +37 percentile points [~W]
  • The evidence base for Waterford Early Reading Program™ is thin: only one of 36 reviewed studies met WWC evidence standards, and extent of evidence was rated small for both domains [~W]
  • In the qualifying quasi-experimental study, at-risk Kindergarten students using Waterford Level One for 15-minute daily sessions were matched to comparison students and pretested in fall and posttested in spring [+W]
  • Waterford Early Reading Program™ showed no discernible effects on comprehension, with a small non-significant effect size on vocabulary [~W]
  • The WWC rated Waterford Early Reading Program™ as having potentially positive effects on alphabetics, based on a substantively important average effect size with no statistically significant individual outcomes [+W]
  • No individual alphabetics outcome reached statistical significance in the WWC analysis [~W]
