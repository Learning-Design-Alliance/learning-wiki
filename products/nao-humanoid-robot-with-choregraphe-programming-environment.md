# NAO humanoid robot with Choregraphe programming environment

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
SoftBank Robotics' NAO humanoid robot and Choregraphe visual programming environment enable developers to create multimodal behaviors using voice, vision, touch, and movement for educational and other applications.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **NAO humanoid robot with Choregraphe programming environment for embodied educational support**: The article cites the humanoid robot NAO, used to support students with autism spectrum disorders, as a notable advancement transferring Embodied AI principles into education. Its programming environment Choregraphe "relies on visual blocks and Python code, facilitates the creation of integrated multimodal behaviors (voice, vision, touch, movement)", yet the article reports it "still lacks flexibility in unpredictable scenarios", with improvisational and adaptive operation remaining in early stages. (Ronnie Videla et al. (2025))
- **Emma: a teachable NAO robot learning companion used in a 48-student math study**: Emma is a NAO robot used as a teachable agent in a study of 48 students between the ages of 12 and 14 (26 female, 22 male). Students spent 30 minutes teaching the robot to solve problems involving proportions, equations, and ratios using spoken language and a touch-screen interface on a Microsoft Surface. The study had three conditions (non-social; non-social with acoustic entrainment; social), with math pre-tests and post-tests for learning gains and a post-survey yielding a rapport score. (Amanda Buddemeyer et al. (2019))
