# SCALE-UP (Student-Centered Active Learning Environment with Upside-down Pedagogies)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
SCALE-UP is Robert Beichner's studio-style, technology-rich instructional framework, developed at North Carolina State University, that combines lecture and laboratory work with collaborative inquiry around small-group learning stations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SCALE-UP pedagogy: studio-like, technology-rich inquiry-based learning with merged lecture/lab and round learning stations**: SCALE-UP (Student Centered Active Learning Environment with Upside-down Pedagogies) is a model of instruction developed by Robert Beichner at North Carolina State University to improve conceptual understanding more effectively than separate lectures and seminars/labs. Its most salient feature is that "Inquiry-based learning in a studio-like technology-rich setting is the most salient feature of SCALE-UP model of instruction." Classes use round learning stations for small-group tasks, combine lecture and lab into single interwoven sessions with one shared grade, and typically enroll about 27 to 117 students with two instructors and teaching assistants facilitating team interactions. (Ganga P. Sharma et al. (2018))
