# Doubao AI (V3.8)

## Description
Doubao AI (V3.8), developed by ByteDance, is an AI platform providing intelligent question answering and agent-based simulated cases and feedback for educational and other user interactions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Doubao AI (V3.8) intelligent Q&A and AI agent modules supporting pre-class, in-class, and post-class phases of PBL-CBL teaching**: Doubao AI (V3.8, developed by ByteDance) served as the core AI tool throughout the AI-assisted PBL-CBL intervention. It "primarily employs two key modules: the intelligent Q&A module and the AI agent module," delivering text responses to theoretical questions, dynamic simulated PHN patient cases that "simulate symptom progression and changes in patient condition according to students' intervention measures," and feedback evaluating the rationality, safety, effectiveness, and individualization of student treatment plans. Students interacted via chat-based interaction combined with structured tasks across pre-class preparation, in-class simulation, and post-class consolidation phases. (Fang et al. (2026))
