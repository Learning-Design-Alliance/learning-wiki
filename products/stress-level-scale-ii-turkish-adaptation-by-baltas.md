# Stress Level Scale II (Turkish adaptation by Baltas)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 25-item self-report stress-level scale developed by Leighton and adapted into Turkish by Baltas for assessing stress in school-aged students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Stress Level Scale II (Leighton, 1989), Turkish adaptation by Baltas**: Stress Level Scale II, developed by Leighton (1989) and "adapted to Turkish by Baltas (1994)", was the sole measurement instrument for pretests and posttests. It includes 25 items and is scored with printed bands: "Scores between 27 and 29 are within normal range; scores between 30 and 35 are at the high boundary, and scores above 36 suggest high stress." The study administered it to children in 6th and 7th grade. (Köroğlu et al. (2016))
