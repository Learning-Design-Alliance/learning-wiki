# Investigative Science Learning Environment (ISLE) approach

## Description
A physics-education instructional framework developed for organizing authentic investigation through model-generating, testing, and application experiments, with guided questioning and scientific-abilities assessment.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ISLE (Investigative Science Learning Environment) as a guided-inquiry framework built on observational, testing and application experiments**: ISLE encourages students to engage in knowledge construction mirroring professional scientific practice: "generating explanations in groups, testing them through experiments, and refining them". It distinguishes "three types of experiments: observational, testing and application", in which students observe phenomena, falsify their own explanations via hypothetical-deductive reasoning, and apply knowledge to realistic problems. The article implements ISLE with iOLab devices in Italian high school labs, noting its emphasis on growth mindset, formative assessment and rubric-based assessment of scientific abilities. (E. Tufino et al. (2024))
- **The Investigative Science Learning Environment (ISLE) approach embodies the intentionality that learning physics should enhance well-being**: The article invokes Brookes, Etkina, and Planinšič's recommendation to clarify instructional intentions and align instruction with them. It quotes their intentionality that 'The way in which students learn physics should enhance their well being' and states this intentionality underlies the Investigative Science Learning Environment (ISLE) approach to teaching high-school and first-year university physics. The article applies this framing to justify embodied physics activities. (Carolyn Sealfon (2024))
