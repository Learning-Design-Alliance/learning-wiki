34-item Community of Inquiry Survey¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A validated survey developed by Swan et al. to measure teaching, social, and cognitive presence in Community of Inquiry-designed courses.

Components¶

  • 34-item Community of Inquiry Survey instrument: A validated 34-item survey instrument developed to assess the three CoI presences (teaching, social, and cognitive). Swan et al. (2008) collaboratively developed it and "tested its efficacy on a multi-institutional, multi-disciplinary sample of students taking CoI-designed courses," validating the three-dimension framework. In the present study it was administered electronically to MBA students, with additional items added to capture prior online experience and course delivery method. (Honig et al. (2021))
