## Description
PROMISE (Promoting Readiness of Minors in Supplemental Security Income) is described as "a federal initiative that aimed to improve the long-term self-sufficiency of youth ages 14-16 receiving Supplemental Security Income", a means-tested benefit for people with very low incomes. The evaluation covered 2013-2022 and was prepared for the Social Security Administration. This report provides "a more detailed picture of PROMISE youth's employment, education, training, and living arrangements" and benchmarks outcomes against similarly aged youth.
