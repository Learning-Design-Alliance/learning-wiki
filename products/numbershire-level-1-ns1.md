# NumberShire Level 1 (NS1)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A game-based Tier 2 mathematics intervention developed by Fien et al. for first-grade students with or at risk for mathematics difficulties, delivered through structured computer-based sessions covering whole-number concepts and skills.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **NumberShire Level 1 game-based Tier 2 mathematics intervention**: NS1 is a game-based, Tier 2 mathematics intervention for first-grade students with or at risk for mathematics difficulties, integrating explicit instruction, whole-number content, and an engaging gaming platform set in a Renaissance-style fairy tale village. "NS1 consists of 48 sessions, themed into 12 weeks of instruction (four sessions per week). Each session is designed to provide 15 minutes of instruction." Sessions comprise Warm-Up, Teaching Event, Assessment Event, and Wrap-Up activities with virtual representations, frequent practice opportunities, and academic feedback. (Hank Fien et al. (2016))
