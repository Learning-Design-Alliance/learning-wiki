## Description
Mentice VIST G5 is a virtual-reality endovascular intervention simulator made by Mentice for procedural skills training and metric-based performance assessment.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Mentice VIST G5 VR thrombectomy simulator curriculum with metric-based automated performance registration**: A VR task-training simulator used for endovascular thrombectomy skill training at three Norwegian stroke centers. The software includes nine endovascular stroke cases with middle cerebral artery occlusions and aortic arch types 1, 2, and 3, and "automatically registers metric-based data parameters" including procedural steps completed, handling errors, contrast volume, fluoroscopy time, and radiation dose. Participants completed a minimum of 30 training cases over five months; the median number performed was 38. (Olav Søvik et al. (2023))
