ChatGPT-FLGA learning system¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2–q3 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A ChatGPT-based learning system designed for flipped or online university courses to support learners' self-regulated learning, self-efficacy, motivation, and creative thinking.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Learning with a ChatGPT-based flipped learning guiding approach significantly improves students’ performance, self-efficacy, learning attitudes, intrinsic motivation and creative thinking in a courseware project [+M]
  • Teaching a ChatGPT agent improves students' self-regulated learning, specifically self-efficacy and use of cognitive strategies [+M]
