## Description
A free bilingual Ukrainian–German web-based rehabilitation education module developed by Medizinische Hochschule Hannover with orthopedic-technology partners for medical professionals, amputees, and relatives.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **RehAmpTT, a free bilingual e-learning module on rehabilitation after limb amputation**: RehAmpTT (Rehabilitation after AmputationTeaching Tool) is a web-based learning module developed by the Medizinische Hochschule Hannover with orthopedic-technology partners, aimed at medical professionals, amputees and their relatives. It offers a low-threshold, holistic introduction to all phases of rehabilitative care after arm or leg amputation and is available free of charge in Ukrainian and German at https://digitale-lehre-mhh.de. The article states the content "consists of a pathway, explanatory texts, images, video sequences and audio files on treatment, self-exercises and practical tips." (Egen C et al. (2025))
