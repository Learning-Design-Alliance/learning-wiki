# Programmatic assessment

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A centrally coordinated health-professions assessment framework that aggregates predominantly formative evidence, supports continuous feedback and remediation, and periodically informs summative progression decisions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Programmatic assessment as a centrally coordinated, feedback-centred assessment system**: The article describes programmatic assessment as an innovative approach that applies current assessment principles so that assessment maximizes learning while supporting robust progress decisions. It features centralized institutional coordination, emphasis on formative assessment, individual events that "are not intended for decision making for approval or failure purposes", portfolio-based aggregation of predominantly formative data, periodic review for summative decisions, remediation plans, and continuous student–mentor dialogue. Quality criteria include validity, reliability, acceptability and positive educational effects. (Flauzino Rodrigo Humberto et al. (2025))
