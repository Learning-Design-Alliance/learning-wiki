Transfer of Knowledge Test in Physics (TKTP)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A 50-item multiple-choice physics assessment developed by Akinbobola to measure senior secondary students' transfer of knowledge from heat energy to sound and light energy concepts.

Components¶

  • Transfer of Knowledge Test in Physics (TKTP), a 50-item multiple-choice transfer measure: The Transfer of Knowledge Test in Physics (TKTP) is a researcher-made instrument of 50 multiple-choice items on sound energy and light energy, each with four options and one correct answer, used to measure students' ability to extend what was learned about heat energy to new concepts. It was face and content validated by four physics teachers and trial tested with 50 subjects outside the main sample. The article reports that "TKTP has a reliability coefficient of 0.76" using Kuder-Richardson formula 21, with average difficulty and discrimination indices of 60.62 and 0.52. (Akinbobola (2015))
