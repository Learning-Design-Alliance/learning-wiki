ePSRL Management System¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A purpose-built electronic personalised self-regulated learning management system developed by Khiat and colleagues to support learners' planning, monitoring, appraisal, reflection, and learning analytics throughout the learning process.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Students who use the ePSRL Management System meet the course completion deadline at a significantly higher rate than non-users [+W]
  • Students who use the ePSRL Management System spend more time learning per month and complete the course in fewer months than non-users [+W]
  • Students who use the ePSRL Management System post more scaffold reflections and self-reflections per month than non-users [+W]
  • Using the ePSRL Management System increases postgraduate online students' perceived awareness, understanding and application of self-regulated learning [+W]
  • Using the ePSRL Management System shows no significant difference in mean assessment results or mean attempts per quiz between users and non-users [-W]
