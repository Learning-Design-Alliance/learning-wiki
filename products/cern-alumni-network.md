## Description
A CERN-run alumni and early-career networking programme providing an online platform, jobs board, employer showcases, and mentoring resources for current and former CERN-affiliated researchers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CERN Alumni Network as a mature model for HEPA-industry career networking**: The CERN Alumni Network grew out of CMS Collaboration networking events begun around 2013 and was later adopted by all CERN Experiments. It now has "over 7500 active members, 85% of whom are alumni or soon-to-be alumni" with an annual budget of 35-50K Swiss Franks, mostly for the alumni.cern platform. Its early-career support includes experience-sharing events, a jobs board, virtual company showrooms with 30-minute presentation and Q&A sessions, and a mentoring module with short videos of alumni industry-transition experiences. (Sudhir Malik et al. (2022))
