## Description
The Institute of Physics New Quantum Curriculum is a freely available online introductory quantum-mechanics curriculum of short articles, interactive simulations, activities, and problem sets for university students and instructors.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **IOP New Quantum Curriculum: free online learning and teaching materials for introductory quantum mechanics starting from two-level systems**: The Institute of Physics New Quantum Curriculum consists of "freely available online learning and teaching materials (quantumphysics.iop.org) for a first course in university quantum mechanics starting from two-level systems". The core content is around 80 short articles co-authored by leading experts, arranged in themes, with 17 interactive simulations with accompanying activities and problem sets. Materials can be used flexibly, from supplements to existing courses to a complete programme, and solutions to activities are available to instructors. (Kohnle A et al. (2013))
