# Walsh et al.'s fever management knowledge, attitudes, and practices questionnaires (FMK, FMA, FMP)

## Description
A structured self-administered assessment developed by Walsh et al. to measure pediatric nurses' fever-management knowledge, attitudes, and practices.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Walsh et al. fever management survey (FMK, FMA, FMP)**: A structured self-administered questionnaire developed by Walsh et al. in 2005, divided into three questionnaires: "fever management knowledge (FMK), fever management attitudes (FMA), and fever management practices (FMP)". It was the dominant measurement instrument in the educational-program studies the review analyzed: four of the programs applied or adapted it, while one study used a validated questionnaire created by its authors and another used an unvalidated questionnaire to evaluate knowledge. (Vicens-Blanes et al. (2021))
