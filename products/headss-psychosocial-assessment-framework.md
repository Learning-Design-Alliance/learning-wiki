# HEADSS psychosocial assessment framework

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A structured primary-care assessment framework developed for identifying young people's psychosocial needs across Home, Education and employment, Activities, Drugs, Sexuality, Suicide/depression and Safety, supporting clinical decision-making.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **HEADSS psychosocial assessment framework for young people in primary care**: The article cites the HEADSS assessment as an example of good practice used in primary care in Australia for assessing the psychosocial needs of young people and guiding clinical decision-making. It assesses "the domains of Home, Education and employment, Activities, Drugs, Sexuality, Suicide/depression and Safety". The authors suggest introducing such an assessment in UK general practice would require a shift in GPs' conceptualisation of risk and changes in primary care organisation. (Michail Maria (2016))
