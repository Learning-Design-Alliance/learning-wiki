# DECODE framework: Digital Health Competencies in Medical Education

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An international consensus framework developed by Car and colleagues that defines digital-health competencies and specific learning outcomes for medical students across telehealth, data science, and informatics.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **DECODE framework: digital health competencies in medical education**: DECODE is an international consensus framework developed by Car and colleagues via a Delphi study in medical education, cited by the review as a model for nursing. The review states: "The resulting Digital Health Competencies in Medical Education (DECODE) framework defines both competencies and specific learning outcomes for digital health in medical education, with digital health broadly inclusive of telehealth, data science, and informatics." It offers detail that current nursing frameworks lack. (Cummins MR et al. (2026))
