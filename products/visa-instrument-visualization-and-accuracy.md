# VisA instrument (Visualization and Accuracy)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A paper-and-pencil, group-administered assessment developed by Jacobse et al. that measures grade 5 students' metacognitive judgments and schematic visualization during mathematical word-problem solving.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **VisA instrument (Visualization and Accuracy) for group-administered on-line metacognition assessment**: The VisA instrument combines prediction judgments, problem visualizations, and postdiction judgments into one paper-and-pencil measure of metacognitive monitoring and regulation during word problem solving. "In the VisA instrument, four word problems are presented," each split into four booklet steps: confidence rating, sketching, solving, and postdiction. Scoring awards points for accurate judgments and for schematic versus pictorial sketches, yielding a maximum of 12 points; internal consistency was α=0.70. It can be administered to groups, unlike individual think-aloud protocols. (Jacobse et al. (2012))
