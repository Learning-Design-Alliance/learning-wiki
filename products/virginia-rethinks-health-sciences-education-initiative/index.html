Virginia RETHINKS Health Sciences Education initiative¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes

Description¶

A TAACCCT-funded initiative organized across Virginia's 23 community colleges to redesign health-sciences education, strengthen workforce connections, and improve college efficiencies.

Components¶

  • Virginia RETHINKS three-goal grant design: student outcomes, workforce connections, and college efficiencies: The Virginia RETHINKS Health Sciences Education initiative organized a $24 million TAACCCT grant across all 23 Virginia community colleges around three broad goals: improving student outcomes, building connections to the workforce, and improving college efficiencies. Strategies included redesigning curriculum in developmental education and health-related courses, in-person counseling and mentoring, and technology platforms for students, faculty, and staff. The report evaluates implementation of each strategy. (Margaret Sullivan et al. (2015))

Claims¶

  • Career coaches strengthened collaboration between colleges and the workforce development system, but sustaining these connections requires additional resources after the grant period [+W]
  • College-specific barriers related to resources, time, and local labor market conditions limited implementation of the statewide E-HLTH career studies certificate [~W]
  • Colleges adopted new technology platforms to increase efficiencies, but face challenges sustaining their use after the grant ends [~W]
