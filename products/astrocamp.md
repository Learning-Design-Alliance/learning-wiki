## Description
AstroCamp is a two-week residential astronomy and physics academic-excellence programme for pre-university students, organized by CAUP with the Paredes de Coura municipality and partner institutions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **AstroCamp residential excellence program for pre-university astronomy and physics students**: The AstroCamp is an academic excellence program in astronomy and physics organized by CAUP and the Paredes de Coura municipality for students in the last three years of pre-university education, roughly 15-18 years old. The article states it was "created in 2012" and now accepts applications from 42 eligible countries, with participation strictly by invitation after an application and interview phase. Its goals include promoting scientific knowledge in "a secluded and tranquil setting", stimulating curiosity, critical thinking, teamwork and group responsibility, and stimulating interactions among students with different backgrounds. The main academic activity consists of two courses, each with 15h of lectures and a 2h written exam, given by active researchers with a PhD. (C. J. A. P. Martins (2021))
