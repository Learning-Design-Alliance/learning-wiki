## Description
Corrective Reading is "a program for upper-elementary struggling readers that uses scripted, structured lessons focused on phonics, fluency, and comprehension". Explicit and systematic methods engage students in a rapid series of activities to build word-level skills, and oral reading of carefully constructed stories builds rate and fluency while countering word-guessing habits. It was evaluated in one qualifying study with 206 students.

# Corrective Reading: a scripted direct-instruction reading program with decoding and comprehension components across multiple levels

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (1 for, 3 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Corrective Reading is a commercial program designed to promote "reading accuracy (decoding), fluency, and comprehension skills of students in third grade or higher who are reading below their grade level." It uses "a direct instruction approach, a scripted presentation that uses a brisk pace, carefully chosen exercises and examples," with sequenced, scripted lessons delivered in 45-minute sessions four to five times weekly to small groups or whole classes. It has decoding and comprehension components with four levels each, ongoing mastery tests, and approximately seven hours of staff development plus recommended follow-up coaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- 45-minute lessons taught four to five times a week
- approximately seven hours of staff development focused on delivering direct instruction, with follow-up observations and coaching recommended
#### Constraints
- In the single study meeting WWC standards, only the word-level skills components were implemented, so the comprehension component was not evaluated

### Target Learners
- students in third grade or higher reading below grade level, including non-readers and struggling decoders

### Target Learning Goals
- decoding accuracy
- reading fluency
- reading comprehension
