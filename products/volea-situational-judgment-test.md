# voLeA situational judgment test

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A theory- and curriculum-anchored situational judgment test for assessing medical students' knowledge-based interpretation and evaluation of doctor–patient communication situations across structuring, content, and relationship levels.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Three-level model of clinical communicative competence: structure, content, and relationship**: The voLeA SJT is grounded in a competence model distinguishing three levels of communicative competence: goal-oriented structuring of a conversation (e.g., metacommunication techniques), effectively advancing the conversation on the content level (e.g., strategies to create common ground), and building a positive working relationship (e.g., empathic behaviour). The article states that "the model is based on the theoretical assumption that conversations are in principle to be analyzed on several levels, namely the formal-structural, the content-problem-related, and the level of interpersonal relationships", and that doctors must act competently at these levels simultaneously. These three levels are included as separate ratings in the SJT. (Schick K et al. (2020))
