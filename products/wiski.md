## Description
Wiski is an e-learning platform that uses Elo-based mastery estimates to recommend mathematics exercises and lets learners steer and visualise subsequent recommendations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Wiski e-learning platform with mastery-level steering slider and control-impact visualisation**: An extension of Wiski, an existing e-learning platform where middle and high school students solve multiple-choice maths questions. It estimates mastery level and exercise difficulty with an Elo rating system and recommends series of two exercises chosen so the modelled probability of a correct answer is closest to 0.7. After each series, students can steer subsequent recommendations with a 20-step slider that lowers or raises their Elo rating up to 10%, and optionally see a visualisation combining a fixed explanation, a description of mastery-level change, and a line chart. Think-aloud studies with 11 adolescents found the slider provided "intuitive and sufficient control" and that adolescents preferred the line chart over an animated bar chart. (Steering Recommendations and Visualising Its Impact: Effects on Adolescents' Trust in E-Le)
