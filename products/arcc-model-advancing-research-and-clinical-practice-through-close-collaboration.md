# ARCC model (Advancing Research and Clinical Practice through Close Collaboration)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A healthcare evidence-based-practice implementation framework that uses organizational readiness, trained mentors, and supportive strategies to build clinicians’ EBP competence and sustain practice change.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ARCC model (Advancing Research and Clinical Practice through Close Collaboration) as the study's guiding framework**: The study adopted the Advancing Research and Clinical Practice through Close Collaboration (ARCC) model as its organizing framework, used "to identify barriers, develop EBP mentoring systems, and implement supportive strategies for daily clinical practice." The model structured the three-phase research design: baseline assessment of nurse competencies and organizational readiness, an intervention phase combining simulation-based EBP training with structured mentorship and organizational support, and an endline evaluation-and-sustainability phase. In the discussion the authors report that "the ARCC model had a profound impact in a short time." (Akhter N et al. (2026))
