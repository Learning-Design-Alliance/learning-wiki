SEALS (Supporting Early Adolescent Learning and Social Success) teacher professional development program¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

SEALS is a year-long professional development programme for sixth-grade teachers that trains them to use proactive classroom-management and engagement strategies to foster positive peer culture and productive academic engagement.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Students with disabilities and academically gifted students report perceiving more peer encouragement of bullying than other students, but within SEALS intervention schools they report perceiving less than nondisabled peers [~W]
  • The SEALS intervention showed no significant effect on peer victimization subtype placement, but students in intervention schools showed less variability in involvement over time [~M]
