Ecree automated writing feedback tool¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 8 claims (4 for, 4 against) · 3 studies (1 causal, 1 review, 1 associational), q2 · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Ecree is an automated writing-feedback software tool used by teachers and students to support writing instruction and revision in classroom settings.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Teachers and students found Ecree easy to use and helpful for improving writing [+W]
  • Ecree likely improved the quality of students' writing, especially for students with less advanced writing skills at the start of the school year [+W]
  • Some students may be overwhelmed by Ecree's feedback about their writing [-W]
  • Teachers found Ecree and MI Write useful for their writing instruction and for helping students improve their writing [+W]
  • Teachers used Ecree less than intended, and some reported they did not have enough time to learn it and integrate it with instruction [-W]
  • Some students struggled to use Ecree's and MI Write's automated feedback [-W]
  • Teachers reported curriculum and technical integration challenges when implementing Ecree and MI Write [-W]
  • Regular and tailored support helped teachers integrate Ecree and MI Write into their teaching [+W]
