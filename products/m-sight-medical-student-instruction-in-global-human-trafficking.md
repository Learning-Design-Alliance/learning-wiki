# M-SIGHT (Medical Student Instruction in Global Human Trafficking)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
M-SIGHT is a University of Louisville curriculum package combining online learning, medical documentation, and standardized-patient simulation to prepare medical students to recognize and respond to human trafficking.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **M-SIGHT: a multi-modal human trafficking curriculum for third-year medical students**: M-SIGHT (Medical Student Instruction in Global Human Trafficking) is a curriculum developed in 2014 at the University of Louisville School of Medicine for third-year medical students. The article describes it as "a multi-modal instructional module, using online learning, medical documentation, and standardized patient (SP) -based simulation to prepare students to recognize victims and intervene on their behalf." Its goal is to prepare every graduating medical student with knowledge to identify trafficking victims, communication skills, and referral resources. Assessment uses written assessments and SP checklists developed by the authors. (Stoklosa Hanni et al. (2017))
