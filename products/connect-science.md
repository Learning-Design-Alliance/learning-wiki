Connect Science is a 31-lesson fourth-grade science curriculum that integrates NGSS practices, social and collaborative skills, and service learning for classroom implementation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Connect Science curriculum: 31 lessons integrating NGSS practices, social and collaborative skills, and service-learning**: Connect Science is a curricular approach designed to prepare teachers to use Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS) instructional practices, teach social and collaborative skills, and facilitate a service-learning experience in their science class. The program is described as "The sequence of 31 lessons are typically taught over 14 weeks or more"; the lessons "replace existing fourth grade science units and can be integrated with other content instruction." Teachers receive a manual including the CS lessons, a kit of science materials such as batteries, bulbs, fans, solar panels and motors, and trade books to accompany lessons. (Connect Science program description et al. (2022))
- **Adopt Connect Science without additional staffing, using existing classroom teachers trained through its professional development**: The program is designed to be delivered by regular classroom teachers rather than added personnel: the article states "No additional staffing requirements." (Connect Science program description et al. (2022))
