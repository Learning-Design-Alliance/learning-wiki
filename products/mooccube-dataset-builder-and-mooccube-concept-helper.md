# MOOCCube Dataset Builder and MOOCCube Concept Helper

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Two research toolkits released with the MOOCCube repository: one supports composable querying and dataset construction, while the other supports concept extraction and prerequisite-graph construction for education data mining and adaptive-learning research.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MOOCCube Dataset Builder and MOOCCube Concept Helper toolkits for constructing datasets and concept graphs**: The article releases two toolkits with the repository. It states "we provide researchers with two toolkits for an easy and quick start with our repository". The MOOCCube Dataset Builder packages 10 common operations for querying different data types, combinable for building more complex datasets; the MOOCCube Concept Helper lets researchers replicate the published concept extraction and prerequisite discovery methods to build fine-grained prerequisite concept graphs with a small number of annotations. (Jifan Yu et al. (2021))
