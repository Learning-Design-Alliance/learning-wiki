## Description
Lumen One Introduction to Statistics is an online courseware product designed to replace traditional textbooks in gateway statistics courses. Content is "provided online as short segments of text with extensive graphics and video interspersed with interactive exercises." It includes student-facing components (Readiness Checks, Study Plans, Learn It text, Apply It activities, Fresh Take videos) and instructor-facing supports (a Faculty Engagement Center, automated messages, time-sensitive intervention flags, question banks). The courseware embeds diverse identities and real-world statistics applications, and automatically executes 13 of 27 surveyed EBT practices.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructor assignment of core components (Study Plans, Self-Check quizzes) for most or all modules
- At least 20% of student grade coming from Lumen One for full fidelity
#### Constraints
- Fresh Take videos had lower assignment rates (53% of instructors); intended as optional supplementary resources

### Target Learners
- College students in introductory statistics, including those from historically marginalized groups

### Target Learning Goals
- Introductory statistics conceptual understanding and skills
