# RESHAPE antistigma training intervention

## Description
RESHAPE is a cofacilitated mental-health antistigma training programme developed and evaluated by Kohrt and colleagues, combining mhGAP-IG training with PhotoVoice recovery narratives, lived-experience facilitation, and aspirational clinician stories for primary care practitioners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **RESHAPE antistigma training intervention with PhotoVoice recovery narratives and aspirational figure cofacilitation**: RESHAPE is a stigma-reduction intervention in which people with lived experience of mental illness cofacilitate a 40-hour mhGAP-IG training for primary care practitioners. PWLE "present recovery testimonials through photographic narratives, using a technique known as PhotoVoice," with narratives of about 7 minutes covering life before treatment, the experience of treatment, and life after treatment, followed by question-and-answer sessions. Aspirational figures (PCPs previously trained in mental health care) present a myth-busting session and a clinician-perspective recovery story, and PWLE join structured and unstructured social activities with trainees. (Kohrt BA et al. (2021))
