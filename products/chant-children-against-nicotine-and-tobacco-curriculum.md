# CHANT (Children Against Nicotine and Tobacco) curriculum

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A five-session storybook-based preschool curriculum developed by Calabro et al. for teaching children aged 4–6 to identify and avoid secondhand and thirdhand tobacco-smoke exposure.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CHANT: a five-session storybook curriculum teaching preschoolers to identify and avoid secondhand and thirdhand smoke**: CHANT (Children Against Nicotine and Tobacco) is a classroom curriculum for children aged 4 to 6 about awareness and health consequences of exposures to SHS and THS. It comprises five 20-minute sessions delivered by researchers, built around an original storybook titled "Brady and Charlie's Brand New Day" with "individual corresponding workbooks, stickers, flash cards, Brady and Charlie dolls, bandanas, and certificates for graduation." Curriculum content is mapped to Texas pre-kindergarten and kindergarten educational standards across social-emotional, reading, mathematics, science, and health domains. (Calabro et al. (2016))
