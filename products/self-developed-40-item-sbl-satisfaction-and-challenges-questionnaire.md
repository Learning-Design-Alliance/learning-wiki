# Self-developed 40-item SBL satisfaction and challenges questionnaire

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A researcher-developed 40-item, 5-point Likert questionnaire with open-ended follow-up questions for assessing medical students’ satisfaction with and challenges in simulation-based learning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Self-developed 40-item SBL satisfaction and challenges questionnaire (5-point Likert, alpha 0.73)**: A self-developed questionnaire assessing medical students' satisfaction with and challenges of simulation based learning, consisting of "40 items related to satisfaction and challenges they faced during simulation teaching" on a 5-point Likert scale, with open-ended follow-up questions. Items covered knowledge, skill, environment, resources, and faculty, and were validated by expert reviewers with reliability alpha 0.73. (Agha Sajida et al. (2015))
