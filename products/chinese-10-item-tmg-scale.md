## Description
A 10-item Chinese-language self-report instrument measuring perseverance of effort, consistency of interests, and adaptability to situations in primary-school students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Triarchic model of grit (TMG)**: The TMG conceptualizes grit as "the tendency to show perseverance of effort, consistency of interests, as well as adaptability to situations when working on temporally distal goals". It extends the original two-factor grit model by adding adaptability to situations—one's tendency to modify goal-attainment pathways or unrealistic goal features based on socio-contextual considerations—to enhance the relevance of grit measurement in collectivist as well as individualist contexts. In this study the TMG's three dimensions were measured with the Chinese 10-item TMG Scale and tested as differential predictors of math self-efficacy and multidimensional math engagement. (Datu et al. (2025))
