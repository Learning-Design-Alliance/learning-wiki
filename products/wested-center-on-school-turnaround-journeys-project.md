# WestEd Center on School Turnaround Journeys Project

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A WestEd Center on School Turnaround initiative that follows schools' turnaround efforts over time and shares implementation episodes, webinars, research, and practitioner dialogue.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **WestEd Center on School Turnaround Journeys Project documenting school turnaround efforts in progress**: The Journeys Project of WestEd's national Center on School Turnaround documents turnaround efforts "from the early stages through implementation," following schools such as Amargosa Valley School. The team "chronicles issues in periodic episodes posted online, and produces webinars and shares relevant research in a blog," and "invites comments from practitioners, researchers, and the general public in order to engage in a dialogue about the turnaround process." It also produced a handbook on the state role in rural turnaround. (Rural Turnaround: Challenges et al. (2014))
