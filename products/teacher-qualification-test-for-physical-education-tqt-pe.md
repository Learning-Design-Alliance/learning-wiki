# Teacher Qualification Test for Physical Education (TQT-PE)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The TQT-PE is a standardized professional qualification examination administered under ÖSYM guidelines to assess prospective physical education and sports teachers’ field knowledge and preparedness for teaching.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Teacher Qualification Test for Physical Education (TQT-PE) as the outcome assessment**: The TQT-PE is the standardised professional qualification exam whose scores the study predicts. It is "a meticulously crafted assessment tool, informed by experts and academicians", covering topics from fundamentals of physical education and sports (13%, 11 questions) to anatomy and kinesiology, health and first aid, exercise physiology, training science, adaptive practices, and team sports (8%, six questions). Topic weightings are set under guidelines established by the Student Selection and Placement Centre (ÖSYM). (Anapalı Şenel et al. (2025))
