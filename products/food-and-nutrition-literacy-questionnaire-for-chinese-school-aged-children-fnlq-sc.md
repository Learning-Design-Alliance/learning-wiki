# Food and Nutrition Literacy Questionnaire for Chinese School-aged Children (FNLQ-SC)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A validated questionnaire developed for Chinese school-aged children in grades 3–9 to assess functional, interactive, and critical food and nutrition literacy.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Food and Nutrition Literacy Questionnaire for Chinese School-aged Children (FNLQ-SC) as primary outcome measure**: The FNLQ-SC is the primary outcome instrument, assessing cognition and skills "across functional, interactive, and critical nutrition literacy levels, focusing on food selection, preparation, and intake abilities." The article describes it as feasible and acceptable, typically completed in 20 minutes with an 85%+ completion rate and good internal consistency (Cronbach's α = 0.698). It was chosen because it is validated for Chinese school-aged children and aligns with the intervention's three-level approach. (Li Nana et al. (2026))
