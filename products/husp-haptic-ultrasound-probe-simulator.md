# HUSP (Haptic Ultrasound Probe) simulator

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A medical-training simulator developed by Sánchez-Poveda and colleagues that combines a sensor-equipped ultrasound-probe replica with desktop software to train ultrasound image identification, probe handling, and anatomical spatial orientation.
