# EDUCE modular curriculum

## Description
EDUCE is a modular curriculum of self-contained data-science learning bundles developed for integration into undergraduate life-sciences courses, with publicly accessible materials and instructor adaptations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Modular curriculum: self-contained data science bundles integrated into existing courses to overcome instructor capacity, prior knowledge, and relevance barriers**: EDUCE modules are "self-contained, adjustable bundles of learning materials" delivered as stand-alone classes, recurring instances such as data science Fridays, or consecutive integration supporting capstone projects. Each module consists of introduction, practice, application, and communication, with emphasis shifting toward application and communication in later modules as data complexity increases from pre-cleaned tidy data to raw, under-explored data sets. The article states "Because EDUCE is modular, it overcomes several of the largest barriers to data science education in the life sciences including instructor capacity, student prior knowledge, and relevance to discipline-specific problems". (Dill-McFarland KA et al. (2021))
