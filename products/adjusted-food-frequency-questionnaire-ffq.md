# Adjusted Food Frequency Questionnaire (FFQ)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An adapted questionnaire that measures children's reported consumption frequency across food categories and eating occasions, used by the study authors as a behavioral outcome measure.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Adjusted Food Frequency Questionnaire (FFQ) as secondary outcome measure for children's dietary habits**: The secondary outcome measure is an adjusted Food Frequency Questionnaire that "captures the daily frequency of food consumption across various categories" including grains and tubers, vegetables and fruits, seafood, meat and eggs, soy and dairy products, nuts, sugar-sweetened beverages, and breakfast and snacks frequency. It is used to capture behavioral manifestations of improved nutrition literacy alongside the FNLQ-SC at baseline and post-intervention. (Li Nana et al. (2026))
