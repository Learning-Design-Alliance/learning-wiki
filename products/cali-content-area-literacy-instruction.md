# CALI (Content-Area Literacy Instruction)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
CALI is an individualized K–4 content-area literacy curriculum developed by Dombek et al. that integrates science and social studies learning into the literacy block through leveled texts, graphic organizers, notebooks, research, discussion, and application activities.
