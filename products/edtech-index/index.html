EdTech Index¶

Description¶

Digital Promise's EdTech Index is a searchable directory of educational technology tools that hosts third-party validations concerning safety, effectiveness, usability, inclusivity, and interoperability.

Components¶

  • EdTech Index with third-party validations: The EdTech Index is a searchable directory of edtech tools that hosts each product's third-party validations. The guide states it is "a searchable directory of edtech tools that hosts each product's third-party validations," covering safety, evidence of effectiveness, usability, inclusivity, and interoperability. Examples include the 1EdTech Data Privacy Seal, iKeepSafe COPPA and FERPA certifications, Digital Promise ESSA Tier 3 and Tier 4 certifications, the ISTE Seal, CAST UDL certifications, and 1EdTech OneRoster and LTI Advantage interoperability certifications. Districts use these signals to narrow options before examining district-specific needs. (Digital Promise (2026))
