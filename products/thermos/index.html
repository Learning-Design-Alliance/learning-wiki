Thermos¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 5 claims (2 for, 2 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Thermos is a student-facing learning analytics dashboard developed to visualize motivation, engagement, group-work skills, and study progress while offering actionable self-regulation support and connection suggestions.

Components¶

  • Thermos learning analytics dashboard: The Thermos dashboard is a student-facing, enhanced visualization type LAD designed to support the appraisal and preparatory phases of self-regulation of study behaviour. It presents the Motivation and Engagement Wheel, the Group Work skill graph, and a Study Progress widget, combined with a feedback box that "provides actionable feedback, self-improvement exercises" under Reflect, Explore, and Get Skilled buttons and support suggestions under a Connect button. Students use it mostly of their own volition; no teacher or tutor can monitor student results. (de Vreugd et al. (2024))

Claims¶

  • Perceived impact of LAD use on study behaviour varied, with most participants reporting no impact [~W]
  • Students and tutors rated predetermined LAD constructs similarly in importance but differed in suggesting and ranking new constructs [~W]
  • The LAD did not support the preparatory phase because most participants did not use the actionable feedback despite perceiving it as potentially useful [-W]
  • The LAD was mostly perceived to support student appraisal of study behaviour by providing new insights or awareness [+W]
  • After design adjustments, the LAD achieved good–excellent usability (SUS M = 4.13, equal to 78.25%) [+W]
