## Description
Akyol et al.'s 10-week preschool programme for five-year-old children integrates Turkish, art, play, movement, drama, music, and science and mathematics activities through themed mosaic stages to increase children's involvement.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Mosaic Approach Based Education Programme for five-year-old children**: A 10-week education program developed by the authors to develop the involvement level of five-year-old children, applied "3 days per week, 4 hours per day" for a total of 120 hours. It included Turkish, art, play, movement, drama, music, and science and mathematics activities, organised into three themes (learning centres, activity types, play areas) with three education plans per theme, each theme embedding the three mosaic stages. It was reviewed by 7 academicians and an experienced preschool teacher and piloted in August 2016 before application. (Akyol et al. (2018))
