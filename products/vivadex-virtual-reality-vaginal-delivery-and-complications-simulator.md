# ViVaDeX (Virtual Reality Vaginal Delivery and Complications Simulator)

## Description
An immersive virtual-reality application for simulating normal vaginal delivery and third-stage labor management, developed by the Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology at Yong Loo Lin School of Medicine, NUS, with the Engineering Design and Innovation Centre.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ViVaDeX: an in-house immersive VR simulator for normal vaginal delivery using the Oculus Quest 2 head-mounted display**: ViVaDeX (Virtual Reality Vaginal Delivery and Complications Simulator) is a novel, internally developed immersive VR simulation of normal vaginal delivery "utilizing the Oculus® Quest 2 HMD", built by a collaboration between the Department of Obstetrics and Gynecology, Yong Loo Lin School of Medicine, NUS and the Engineering Design and Innovation Centre. In the trial it delivered a 1-hour simulation session as one arm of a crossover comparison against the PROMPT Flex mannequin. It was funded by internal undergraduate education funds. (Gosavi A et al. (2025))

### Claims
- [Medical students trained with the VR NVD simulator achieved significantly higher post-simulation knowledge scores than those trained with the mannequin simulator](../claims/vr-simulator-higher-post-simulation-knowledge-scores.md) [+M]
- [The mannequin simulator received significantly higher total feedback scores than the VR simulator, and higher scores in all six domains except knowledge](../claims/mannequin-higher-feedback-scores-than-vr.md) [-M]
