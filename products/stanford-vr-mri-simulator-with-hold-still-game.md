# Stanford VR MRI simulator with hold-still game

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A Stanford-developed standalone virtual-reality simulator for preparing adolescents for MRI procedures, combining an immersive MRI environment with an optional gamified stillness-training activity and real-time head-motion feedback.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Stanford VR MRI simulator with hold-still game (Quest 3)**: A standalone-headset (Quest 3) VR MRI simulator featuring "a panda avatar, a squeeze ball for interaction, and 6-DOF head tracking that aligns with a physical bed through passthrough capabilities". It includes an optional interactive hold-still game in which head deviation beyond 5° pauses a movie and beyond 10° makes it disappear, training stillness through real-time feedback. A virtual squeeze ball provides an opt-out mechanism simulating early scan termination. The same environment without game mechanics served as the Passive-VR condition, isolating the effect of gamification. (Yue Yang et al. (2025))

### Claims
