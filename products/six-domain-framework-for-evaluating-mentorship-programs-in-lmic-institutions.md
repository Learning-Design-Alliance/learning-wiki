# Six-domain framework for evaluating mentorship programs in LMIC institutions

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A context-sensitive evaluation framework developed by Chi et al. for assessing mentorship programs in resource-constrained academic and research institutions across six domains at individual and institutional levels.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Evaluate mentorship programs at the institutional level, not only the individual pair**: The article argues that mentorship evaluation should emphasize measurement at the program, department, or school level rather than only the individual mentor–mentee pair. It states: "we emphasize the role of measurement and evaluation at the institutional level. This perspective has been largely overlooked in the mentorship literature." Institutional-level data can identify gaps in support, direct resources, demonstrate return on investment, and help LMIC institutions optimize management of their mentoring resources. (Chi BH et al. (2019))
