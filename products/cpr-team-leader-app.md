## Description
CPR Team Leader is a tablet-based advanced-life-support algorithm cognitive-aid application made by Alphabet Medical GmbH for resuscitation team leaders and teams.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **LLM-based multi-agent ACLS team leadership simulation platform built on Claude 3.5 Sonnet**: A web-based simulation system in which four AI agents powered by Anthropic's Claude 3.5 Sonnet play role-specific ACLS team members (monitoring, defibrillation, medication administration, compressions, airway) while an ACLS-certified user acts as team leader. The article states "The simulation employed Anthropic’s Claude 3.5 Sonnet model to generate four AI agents, each with distinct roles within the ACLS team." Agents follow the AHA 2020 ACLS algorithm, use closed-loop communication, and are coordinated through a memory mapping framework and time-stamped action log; scenarios terminate at return of spontaneous circulation or algorithm completion. (Altuğ K et al. (2026))
