## Description
A scenario-based situational judgement test developed to assess integrity in prospective medical-school applicants using responses grounded in honesty-humility and cognitive-distortion categories.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Deductive SJT development guided by two theoretical models: honesty-humility (positively related to integrity) and cognitive distortions (negatively related)**: The article's deductive development approach grounds SJT response options in two established theoretical models. The honesty-humility (HH) subscale of the HEXACO personality inventory, which "has been demonstrated to be positively related to integrity", generated desirable responses, while cognitive distortions (CDs), "inaccurate thinking styles which may lead to antisocial behaviours", generated undesirable responses. Response options were assembled into seven categories across three HH facets (sincerity, fairness, modesty) and four CD categories (self-centredness, blaming others, minimising, assuming the worst). (de Leng W E et al. (2018))
