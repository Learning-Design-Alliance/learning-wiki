# FairytaleQA

## Description
FairytaleQA is a publicly accessible reading-comprehension dataset of 10,580 explicit and inferential questions paired with 278 child-friendly stories, developed for research and educational language applications.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **FairytaleQA dataset selected for the platform's initial implementation**: FairytaleQA comprises 10,580 questions — both explicit comprehension and implicit inferential questions — derived from 278 child-friendly stories, publicly accessible via the Hugging Face repository. The authors selected it because fairytales "provide a compelling context for young learners", its mix of explicit and implicit questions promotes critical thinking and deeper comprehension, it matches their English-language, text-based requirements, and it supports narrative understanding, moral reasoning, and language development. (Hassam Tahir et al. (2026))
