# Prior Research Table: a documentation tool for recording previous research on a solution before evaluation

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Prior Research Table is a tool designed to help organizations document previous research on their solution to inform future evaluations. It begins with a question about efforts organizations have made to understand the needs of the priority community they intend to serve, then provides instructions for populating the table. It was developed by Mathematica as part of a grantee support suite on evidence-based solutions in education.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Organizations using the tool should first consider efforts they have made to understand the needs of the priority community they intend to serve through their solution
#### Constraints
- Designed for organizations developing, refining, and scaling evidence-based solutions (programs, products, or practices) aimed at improving educational outcomes for students who are Black, Latino, and/or experiencing poverty

### Target Learners
- Education organizations and grantees developing evidence-based educational solutions

### Target Learning Goals
- Documenting prior research to inform future evaluations of educational solutions
