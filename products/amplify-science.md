## Description
Amplify Science is a phenomena-based K–8 science curriculum developed and provided by Amplify Education, integrating hands-on investigation, literacy practices, modeling, and explanation to support three-dimensional science learning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Amplify Science 1st grade curriculum: three phenomena-based units integrating science and literacy**: The Amplify Science 1st grade curriculum consists of "three phenomena-based instructional units" covering life science, physical science, and Earth and space science, each 22 lessons of 45 minutes taught over 6–8 weeks. Lessons engage students in doing, reading, writing, talking, and visualizing science, with hands-on investigations, model building, and explanation writing. The package includes a digital teacher's guide, science kit materials with literacy supports, student notebooks, and books for read-aloud, shared, and partner reading. (Harris et al. (2023))
