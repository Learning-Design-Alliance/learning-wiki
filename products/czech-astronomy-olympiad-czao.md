## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Czech Astronomy Olympiad: a multi-round, multi-age-category national astronomy competition**: The Czech Astronomy Olympiad (CzAO) is an extra-curricular competition for secondary-school and high-school students, founded in 2003 and organised by the Czech Astronomical Society. It uses "four age categories: AB (for the oldest students), CD, EF, and GH (for the youngest ones)", merging pairs of consecutive school grades because astronomy is not part of the standard Czech school curriculum. Each academic year proceeds through a supervised in-person school round with multiple-choice and computational problems, a take-home regional round including an observational exercise, and a countrywide final in Prague or Opava that includes data-analysis questions, night-sky observations, and planetarium orientation tasks. (Pavlík et al. (2024))
