## Description
PowerSchool's attendance-intervention platform integrates attendance data into dashboards and supports educator-to-family messaging to help schools monitor and improve student attendance.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PowerSchool Attendance Intervention platform for attendance monitoring and two-way family messaging**: PowerSchool Attendance Intervention is a school-family engagement tool that helps educators monitor daily and period-level attendance and chronic absenteeism data for each student. It supports two-way messaging with families via texts and email, gathers data from a school or district's administrative system to provide attendance dashboards, customized messaging templates, and options for administrators and teachers to communicate with families. Auto-translation features help reach parents from diverse backgrounds, and automatic population of names and real-time attendance data into templates reduces staff time burden.
