## Description
A serious-game software product developed by the LabForSIMS simulation center with four nursing institutes for practicing clinical-deterioration detection and SBAR-based interprofessional communication.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **LabForGames Warning serious game for nursing deterioration detection and communication**: LabForGames Warning is a serious game developed by the LabForSIMS simulation center with four nursing institutes, in which the player acts as a nurse moving through a 3-D environment, examining virtual patients, reading their files, and calling the physician using the SBAR method. The article states it "aims to improve nursing students' interprofessional communication behavior and their capabilities to detect patient clinical deterioration". Each scenario presents three consecutive steps of increasing severity to introduce early warning signs, and automatic feedback with scores is given at the end of each case. In the trial, two cases (postoperative hemorrhage and brain trauma) were played individually followed by instructor debriefing. (Blanié Antonia et al. (2020))
