Non-Technical Skills for Surgeons (NOTSS) framework¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A validated behavioral taxonomy and rating framework for assessing surgical leadership, situation awareness, decision-making, and communication/teamwork, used by surgical educators and trained raters.

Components¶

  • NOTSS as the predominant validated framework for assessing surgical leadership and non-technical skills: The Non-Technical Skills for Surgeons (NOTSS) framework is a behavioral taxonomy rating leadership, situation awareness, decision-making, and communication/teamwork, used in more than two-thirds of the reviewed studies either directly or as a derived system. The review states that "The predominance of NOTSS in particular underscores its acceptance as a reliable framework for evaluating leadership and decision-making in surgical teams." Frameworks such as NOTSS, NOTECHS, and CRM-based models supported standardized measurement and organized feedback, aiding interrater reliability and reproducibility of non-technical skills training. (Gulnaz Nadia et al. (2026))

Claims¶

  • Simulation-based training is associated with communication and teamwork gains of approximately 2–3 points and NOTSS domain means of 2.8–3.5 on 4-point scales [+M]

Related Products and Programmes¶
