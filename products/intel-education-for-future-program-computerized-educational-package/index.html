Intel Education for Future program computerized educational package¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A computerized mathematics curriculum package prepared by the Ministry of Education as part of the Intel Education for Future program for use with eighth-grade learners.

Components¶

  • Ministry of Education computerized educational package (Intel Education for Future program) for eighth-grade mathematics: The intervention artifact is a computerized educational package "selected from the packages prepared by ministry of education as part of Intel program ; education for future". It was externally arbitrated with an excellent evaluation, contained evaluation tools built by content analysis against a standards table, and was re-arbitrated by experts outside the educational administration before use in the experimental groups. (Dr. Raed Omar Salah (2016))

Claims¶

  • Computerized educational packages produced statistically significant higher mathematics achievement than traditional teaching in eighth-grade classes in Ma'an [+M]
