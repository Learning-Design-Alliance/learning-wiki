# Tutor-annotated student-tutor dialogue misconception dataset

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A dataset by Joshua Mitton et al. containing 922 online mathematics student–tutor dialogues with tutor-rated misconception likelihoods and 546 unique misconception labels, split by misconception label to evaluate generalization to unseen misconceptions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Tutor-annotated student-tutor dialogue misconception dataset (922 dialogues, 546 unique misconception labels)**: A new dataset collected from an online educational learning platform for the task of predicting the tutor-confirmed misconception from a multiple-choice maths question, the student's chosen answer, and the student-tutor dialogue. It contains "922 student-tutor conversations as data points" with "546" unique ground-truth misconception labels, so most misconceptions occur only once. Tutors rated misconception likelihood on a 0-100 scale, and only dialogues rated 75 or 100 were retained. The split is by unique misconception label (70/10/20) so that generalisation to unseen misconceptions is assessed. (Joshua Mitton et al. (2026))
