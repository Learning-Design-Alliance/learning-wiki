## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Entrustable professional activity (EPA) framework for teaching and assessing ward round practice**: The entrustable professional activity (EPA) concept, devised by Ten Cate in 2005, links all competences needed for a clinical task to that task so it can be entrusted to a trainee once sufficient competence is reached. The article defines EPAs as "tasks or responsibilities that can be entrusted to a trainee once sufficient, specific competence is reached to allow for unsupervised execution". It proposes an EPA "Conducting a ward round" built on the identified competence list, usable with competency frameworks such as ACGME or CanMEDS, and notes the existing EPA for internal medicine ward rounds can serve as a basis for other specialities. (Vietz E et al. (2019))
