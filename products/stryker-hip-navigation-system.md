## Description
A CT-integrated orthopedic surgical-navigation system made by Stryker that supports three-dimensional preoperative planning and intraoperative guidance for acetabular cup placement in total hip arthroplasty.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CT-based navigation (Stryker Hip Navigation System) as an educational tool for trainee cup placement**: CT-based navigation is a preoperative-planning and intraoperative-guidance system: CT data are transferred to the navigation system, three-dimensional planning determines cup size, angle, and 3D position, and the system guides acetabular reaming and cup placement. The article notes it "is reported to be an effective educational tool to help trainees master the technique in arthroplasty", and in this study a trainee with <20 prior THA cases used it to reach accuracy comparable to an expert surgeon after 20 cases. All preoperative planning was performed by the trainee and reflected in the system with no deviation. (Tachibana Tetsuya et al. (2025))
