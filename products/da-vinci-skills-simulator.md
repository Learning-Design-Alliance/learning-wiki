## Description
A computer-based virtual-reality simulator for practicing and assessing robotic-surgery console maneuvers and techniques, developed for robotic-surgery training.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Da Vinci Skills Simulator with the Wrist Articulation 1 task as a robotic skills training and assessment element**: The Da Vinci Skills Simulator is "a computer-based virtual-reality system that grants to practice both basic maneuvers and more advanced techniques for robotic surgeon aspirants"; the article reports it had "the highest score in face and content validity" among virtual-reality robotic simulators and that simulator skills seem transferable to clinical performance. The article used the simple Wrist Articulation 1 task with two practice rounds before a documented third attempt, capturing OS, TT, EM, P and GM metrics. (Szabó DI et al. (2024))
- **DVSS virtual reality training console with Mimic software and MScore metrics**: The DaVinci training console (DVSS) equipped with Mimic training software was used for virtual exercises during the intensive training week. The article states the software "uses comprehensive metrics and experienced surgeon data in the MScore" and that it "has been tested for face, construct and predicative validity," meaning it resembles the real-life situation, discriminates the ability tested for, and estimates future candidate performance. Virtual training was encouraged but not mandatory in part 1 due to availability at host institutes. (Rusch Peter et al. (2017))
