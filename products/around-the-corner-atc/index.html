Around the Corner (ATC)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A Success for All Foundation preschool and kindergarten curriculum programme that combines language and early-reading lessons with child-facing computer activities, videos, Home Links, and video-based teacher professional development.

Claims¶

  • ATC produced no statistically significant difference from business-as-usual controls on the confirmatory Word Attack measure over two years [+M]
  • ATC was implemented with fidelity in four of five program components in both implementation years, with little cross-site variation [+M]
