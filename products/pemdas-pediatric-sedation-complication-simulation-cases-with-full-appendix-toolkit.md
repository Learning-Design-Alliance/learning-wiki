# PEMDAS pediatric sedation complication simulation cases with full appendix toolkit

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A pediatric emergency procedural-sedation simulation curriculum package developed by Dupont et al., including complication cases, triage materials, critical-action checklist, equipment list, debriefing resources, evaluation form, and didactic slides.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PEMDAS pediatric sedation complication simulation cases with full appendix toolkit (cases, patient triage set, critical actions checklist, equipment list, debriefing materials, evaluation, didactic)**: Two simulation cases for learners in the pediatric emergency department: case 1, a 12-year-old with shoulder dislocation requiring propofol sedation who develops apnea and hypotension, and case 2, a forearm fracture under ketamine with laryngospasm, hypersalivation, and hypoxia. Learner actions include that participants "demonstrated how to take a presedation history, perform a presedation exam, assess American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) classification, prepare the room for sedation, perform a time-out, and appropriately dose and redose two common sedation medications." Appendices A-H supply the cases, three triage patients, a critical actions checklist, equipment list, X-ray images, PEARLS debriefing materials, an evaluation form, and a PowerPoint, so instructors "would have access to all potential resources needed to carry out the session in their own setting." (Dupont A et al. (2024))
