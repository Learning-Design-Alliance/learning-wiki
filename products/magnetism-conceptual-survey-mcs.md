## Description
A 30-item multiple-choice conceptual test developed by Jing Li and colleagues to assess introductory physics students' understanding of magnetism through Faraday's law.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Magnetism Conceptual Survey (MCS), a validated 30-item multiple-choice test on magnetism**: The Magnetism Conceptual Survey is a research-based 30-item multiple-choice test covering magnetism topics in introductory calculus- or algebra-based physics up to Faraday's law. The article reports a KR-20 of 0.83 for the combined sample, an average difficulty index of 0.46, an average item discrimination index of 0.33, and an average point biserial index of 0.42, with all items at or above the 0.2 point-biserial criterion. It was administered as a pre-test and post-test requiring a full class period (40-50 minutes). (Jing Li et al. (2009))
