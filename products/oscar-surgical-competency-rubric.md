# OSCAR surgical competency rubric

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A structured ophthalmic surgical-skills assessment rubric that combines procedure-specific steps with global competency indices and four-level descriptors for rating residents and guiding progression.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Surgical competency rubrics structured as procedure steps plus global indices with level descriptors**: The OSCAR pattern decomposes a surgical procedure into its essential steps and adds global indices that 'serve as markers of overall surgical competency beyond the specific surgical steps involved in PK.' Each step and index is described at four competency levels so evaluators can rate trainees and learners have 'specific targets for behavioral improvement.' The six global indices for PK are wound neutrality, tissue handling, microscope use, iris/lens protection, spatial awareness, and overall speed and fluidity. (Mira S et al. (2025))
- **ICO-OSCAR: phaco rubric — standardized 20-step assessment of ophthalmic surgical competence**: The International Council of Ophthalmology's Ophthalmology Surgical Competency Assessment Rubric-Phacoemulsification (ICO-OSCAR: phaco) is the standardized assessment tool this study used to grade novice surgeons' competence. The article describes it as "a standardized, internationally valid tool to teach and assess an ophthalmologist's competence" and states that "The rubric divided the surgery into 20 steps." Steps are scored 2–5 (novice, beginner, advanced beginner, competent), with supervisor-performed steps scored 0; total scores range 40–100. (Aadithreya Varman et al. (2024))
