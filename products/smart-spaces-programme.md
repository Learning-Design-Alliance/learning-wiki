## Description
A school-based GCSE science revision programme developed by O'Hare and colleagues that combines SMART Materials, SMART Training, and SMART Activities to deliver spaced lessons in biology, chemistry, and physics.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SMART Spaces programme (SMART Materials, SMART Training and SMART Activities)**: SMART Spaces (Spaced-learning for Memory And Retention Teaching) is a GCSE science revision programme for Year 9 and 10 pupils comprising SMART Materials (PowerPoint slides, manual and activity pack), SMART Training (a one-day training course with modelling, practice and feedback) and SMART Activities (materials for the ten-minute spaces, including juggling balls). Biology, chemistry and physics topics are taught in three 12-minute lessons with spaces between, repeated over three consecutive days. The evaluator estimated a cost of £10 per pupil in the first year of delivery. (Dr Liam O'Hare et al. (2017))
