# Project C.A.R.E.

## Description
A dementia-caregiver support programme that uses a family consultant to connect primary-care offices and families with community services through assessment, education, counseling, referrals, respite funding, and follow-up communication.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Project C.A.R.E. family consultant as a single point of entry linking physician offices to community dementia services**: Project C.A.R.E. provides dementia caregivers with a family consultant offering "in-home assessment, education, counseling, and funding up to $2,500 per year for caregiver respite services such as adult daycare and home aide visits". During training, physicians learned via case examples how the family consultant serves as a single point of entry for community services; offices received pocket cards and an electronic referral link. Post-training, consultants followed up with each office by phone and in person, and physicians received feedback on referral outcomes via a standardized form (regions B and Q) or consistent e-mailed feedback (region G). (Lathren Christine R et al. (2013))

### Claims
- [Physician-initiated referrals to community dementia services rose from almost none before the project to approximately 160 over two years post-training](../claims/physician-community-referrals-increased.md) [+M]
