## Description
EdNet is a large-scale dataset of TOEIC self-study student interactions released by Choi et al. for knowledge tracing, prediction, and learning-analytics research at four levels of action resolution.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **EdNet's four-level hierarchy of student-action abstraction (KT1–KT4)**: EdNet organizes student actions into four datasets of increasing resolution: KT1 holds question-response pairs, KT2 adds question-solving actions, KT3 adds learning activities such as reading expert commentaries and watching lectures, and KT4 provides the complete list of actions including purchasing. The authors write that "the resolution of each dataset increases in the given order, starting from question-response interaction sequences used by most deep knowledge tracing models to the complete list of user actions gathered by Santa." This lets researchers choose the abstraction level suited to their AIEd task. (Choi et al. (2020))
