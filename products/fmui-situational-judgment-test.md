## Description
The Faculty of Medicine, Universitas Indonesia makes and administers this situational judgment admission test to assess non-academic attributes of applicants for undergraduate medical school.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **FMUI situational judgment test assessing non-academic attributes for medical student selection**: A knowledge-based instruction situational judgment test developed at the Faculty of Medicine, Universitas Indonesia to assess non-academic attributes in admissions. Each scenario is "a short paragraph (approximately 50–100 words to reduce cognitive load)" followed by five to eight responses rated from very appropriate to very inappropriate; scoring rewards proximity to an expert consensus key, with a maximum possible score of 512. (Soemantri Diantha et al. (2022))

### Claims
