# Wilson et al. (2016) IRT and DKT model code and result-reproduction release

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An open-source Theano-based implementation of IRT variants and recurrent Deep Knowledge Tracing, with evaluation and reproduction instructions for comparing proficiency-estimation models on public student-response datasets.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Released code for IRT and DKT models and result reproduction (github.com/Knewton/edm2016)**: The article releases implementations of the compared models: "Code for the IRT and DKT models, as well as instructions for reproducing our results, can be found at github.com/Knewton/edm2016". This covers the 1PO IRT, HIRT, and TIRT variants and the RNN-based DKT implementation coded in Theano, together with the evaluation pipeline for online response prediction. It supports replication of the comparison on the two public datasets. (Kevin H. Wilson et al. (2016))
