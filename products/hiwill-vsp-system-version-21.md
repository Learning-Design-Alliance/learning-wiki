## Description

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **HIWILL VSP System (version 2.1) interactive 3D clinical simulation platform**: The HIWILL VSP System (version 2.1; Huawei Medical Technology) is an "interactive 3D clinical simulation platform designed for clinical thinking training" used in the trial's experimental arm. Each student completed "12 standardized VSP cases" representing common internal medicine scenarios, with "up to 3 attempts per case" and immediate feedback after each attempt. Its intelligent module scored consultation skills, diagnostic accuracy, treatment planning, and overall clinical reasoning. (Xu Liyuan et al. (2025))
