## Description
ThinkForward is a school-based coaching programme run by the ThinkForward organisation that provides at-risk secondary pupils with sustained one-to-one and group coaching, action planning, and employer mentoring to support transitions into further education, training, or employment.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ThinkForward four-step coaching process with GROW model and SMART targets**: The ThinkForward intervention places a trained, full-time coach in a school to work with pupils aged 14 onwards identified as most at risk of becoming NEET, through one-to-one and group sessions. "Coaches follow a four step process in order to carry out their work with pupils": identify participants via a bespoke NEET-risk scoring mechanism, understand needs using the Personal Development tracker and Apollo psychometric platform, design action plans using the GROW coaching model with SMART targets, and do and review through regular sessions. In late Year 10/early Year 11 pupils also undertake 6-8 business mentoring sessions with corporate partners covering CV writing and interview practice. (Sean Demack et al. (2016))
