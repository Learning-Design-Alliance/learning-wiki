# Elementary Algebra (Kaufmann & Schwitters, 9E Edition, 2009) e-book

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A digital edition of Jerome E. Kaufmann and Karen L. Schwitters's Elementary Algebra textbook, used as a mathematics learning resource for algebra students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Elementary Algebra e-book (Kaufmann & Schwitters, 9E Edition, 2009) as the experimental learning resource**: The experimental learning resource was the e-book version of "Elementary Algebra" by Jerome E. Kaufmann and Karen L. Schwitters, 9E Edition, published in 2009, used in place of conventional physical textbooks. Students accessed it digitally on "computer screens, laptops, and mobile wherever and whenever", and were given "mathematical assignments through the e-book". The article treats it as a digitally available, low-cost alternative to limited physical textbook stocks. (Mursalin (2019))

### Claims

- [Students taught Elementary Algebra with e-books showed significantly higher mathematical critical thinking gains than students taught with conventional textbooks](../claims/ebooks-higher-critical-thinking-gain-than-textbooks.md) [+M]
- [Posttest critical thinking scores averaged 11.64 in the e-book class versus 9.41 in the control class, with pretest means of 4.42 and 4.31](../claims/posttest-means-ebook-1164-vs-control-941.md) [+W]
- [Normalized gain in mathematical critical thinking was 0.83 in the e-book class versus 0.76 in the conventional-textbook class](../claims/n-gain-083-ebook-vs-076-control.md) [+W]
