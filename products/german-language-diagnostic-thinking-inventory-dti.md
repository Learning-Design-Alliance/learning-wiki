## Description
A German-language 41-item questionnaire developed for self-assessing medical students' perceived flexibility of thinking and knowledge structure in clinical reasoning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **German-language Diagnostic Thinking Inventory (DTI) for self-assessed clinical reasoning**: The DTI is a 41-item self-assessment questionnaire measuring "self-evaluated flexibility in thinking and structure of knowledge in memory independently of context". Each item presents a stem with a 6-point scale whose ends hold opposing statements, with the more advanced reasoning response randomly placed at either end. This study administered a German translation (courtesy of Dr. Stieger) in machine-readable form to 247 student volunteers from three medical schools in varying clinical semesters. (Gehlhar Kirsten et al. (2014))
