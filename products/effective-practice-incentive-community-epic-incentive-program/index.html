Effective Practice Incentive Community (EPIC) incentive program¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 associational), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A New Leaders for New Schools programme that offers incentives to educators in eligible schools showing significant student achievement gains and supports documenting and disseminating their practices.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Most principals in EPIC sites have positive attitudes toward EPIC and merit pay in general [+W]
  • Principals exhibit an incomplete understanding of how achievement growth is captured in EPIC performance measures [~W]
  • Principals prefer school-level performance and achievement growth over teacher-level performance alone or test-score levels for incentive awards [+W]
  • The EPIC evaluation baseline report compares student characteristics across the program's three partners and compares award-determination data with data on all students in the awarded schools [+W]
