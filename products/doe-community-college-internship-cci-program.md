# DOE Community College Internship (CCI) program

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A U.S. Department of Energy programme that places community college students in paid, flexible-term research internships at DOE national laboratories under the guidance of laboratory scientists and engineers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **DOE Community College Internships (CCI) program with flexible scheduling for CC students**: The CCI is an internship program specifically for community college students, who hone technical skills and participate in research projects at DOE national labs working closely with lab scientists and engineers. It offers 10-week terms over summer, fall, and spring, with a "ﬂex-schedule" adaptable to student availability during spring and fall, plus a stipend so students need not choose between a job and research. The article reports an average of 78 participants per year from 2014 to 2020 and recommends wider advertisement of the program. (Matt Bellis et al. (2022))
