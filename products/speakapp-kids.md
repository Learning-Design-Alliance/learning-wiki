# SpeakApp-Kids!

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
SpeakApp-Kids! is a prototype virtual-reality application developed by Sülter et al. for children to practise public speaking through repeated brief exposures to a virtual audience.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SpeakApp-Kids! VR prototype for public speaking practice**: The SpeakApp-Kids! prototype is a virtual reality application that embeds repeated brief exposures to a virtual audience for children practising public speaking. The article describes it as "a prototype of the Virtual Reality (VR) SpeakApp-Kids!" used across three public speaking practice sessions before an actual presentation. It was evaluated in children aged 9-12 years as a proof of concept, with the aim of reducing state anxiety during practice and during the actual presentation, and reducing general public speaking anxiety. (Sülter et al. (2022))

### Claims

- [The SpeakApp-Kids! condition showed a larger decrease in general public speaking anxiety from the first practice session until one week after the actual presentation compared to control.](../claims/vr-condition-larger-decrease-general-public-speaking-anxiety.md) [+M]
- [Pupils in the SpeakApp-Kids! condition reported lower state anxiety during the actual presentation than the control condition.](../claims/vr-condition-lower-state-anxiety-actual-presentation.md) [+M]
- [Repeated brief VR exposures reduced state anxiety during the first two practice sessions.](../claims/vr-practice-sessions-reduce-state-anxiety.md) [+M]
