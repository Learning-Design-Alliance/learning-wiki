## Description
Quantum Composer is a free cross-platform node-based drag-and-drop tool made by the authors for constructing, simulating, and visualizing one-dimensional quantum-physics models, with accompanying tutorials, references, and exercises.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **IBM Quantum Composer: graphical, browser-based environment for programming gate-based quantum computing**: IBM's Quantum Composer is the environment the article uses to implement the Quantum Penny Flip algorithm: programming is done graphically with gate symbols placed by drag-and-drop on qubit wires, the simulated state vector and measurement results are displayed immediately, and circuits can also be run on real quantum hardware. The article stresses that the environment "does not require any installation" and can be used without registration. (Rainer Müller et al. (2021))
- **Quantum Composer, a drag-and-drop 1D quantum simulation and visualization tool**: Quantum Composer is an interactive, flow-based programming tool developed at Aarhus University and available for free download, described as "a flexible and interactive tool that enables educators and students to build and simulate one-dimensional quantum systems through a 'drag-and-drop' visual programming and execution interface". Users connect nodes with interactive capabilities and use visualization nodes such as the State Comparison Plot, which can display real part, imaginary part, and probability density of wavefunctions, statically and in time evolution. Built simulations can be saved and loaded as flowscenes for guided exploration. (Shaeema Zaman Ahmed et al. (2021))
