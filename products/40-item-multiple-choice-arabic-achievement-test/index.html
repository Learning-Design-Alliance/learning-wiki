40-item multiple-choice Arabic achievement test¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A researcher-developed, validity- and reliability-checked 40-item four-option multiple-choice assessment for measuring second-grade Arabic listening, speaking, reading, and writing achievement.

Components¶

  • 40-item multiple-choice Arabic achievement test with verified validity and reliability: The researcher developed a 50-question multiple-choice test with four options, reduced by arbitrators to 40 questions. Difficulty indices ranged between (0.37 - 0.73) with a whole-test difficulty of 0.52, and discrimination indices ranged between (0.41 - 0.94) with a whole-test value of 0.58. Reliability was estimated with the Cooder Richardson equation (0.841) and a two-week test-retest Pearson correlation (0.89) on 25 students outside the sample. (Ribhi Khaleel Ahmad Hamdan (2017))
