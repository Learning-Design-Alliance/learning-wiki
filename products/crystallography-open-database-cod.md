# Crystallography Open Database (COD)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The COD project is an open-access database of computer-readable crystallographic information files that educators can use as source data for crystal-structure visualization and 3D-printed models.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Crystallography Open Database and related open-access CIF sources for education**: The Crystallography Open Database (COD, crystallography.net) is a large open-access database whose approximately 400,000 records are crystallographic information framework files (CIFs), the mandated, computer-readable format of the IUCr. The chapter notes "The approximately 400,000 records of this database are in the form of crystallographic information framework files (CIFs), which is the mandated format of the IUCr and computer readable." Related resources include the American Mineralogist Crystal Structure Database (AMCSD), whose entries are an integral part of the COD, and the Open-Access Crystallography project websites at PSU, which house some 9,000 freely downloadable CIFs with special relevance to college/university education plus a small depository of ready-made 3D print files and a COD mirror. (Peter Moeck et al. (2018))
