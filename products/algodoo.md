# Algodoo

## Description
Algodoo is free interactive physics-simulation software developed by Algoryx Simulation AB for creating and exploring physics scenes and visualising their variables.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Algodoo free interactive physics simulation program**: Algodoo is a free, small-download interactive physics simulation program "developed by considering the laws of physics", especially for mechanical physics. It supports adding objects in various geometric shapes, adjusting all kinds of physical properties of objects, plotting variable graphs relative to each other, visualising vector representations of variables, adding inclined planes at different angles, and adding forces of different sizes and directions. The article uses it to build six impulse and momentum applications for online lessons. (Atakan Çoban (2020))
- **Algodoo Brownian-motion animation environment (fluid of small disks plus a larger Brownian particle with Tracer and Show Plot tools)**: A 2D simulation built in the freeware Algodoo in which a fluid is modeled as many small randomly moving disks colliding elastically with a larger Brownian particle. The article specifies "a ﬂat rectangular region of 5.75.0 m2"-scale parameters (7.2×10^3 blue disks, velocities between 0.1 m/s and 5.5 m/s) and uses the Tracer tool to draw trajectories and the Show Plot tool to export x(t) and y(t) time series as CSV files at 60 points per second. The software "does not require speciﬁc knowledge about computer programming or training to realize tasks with the software, which allow easy learning by students". (Samir L. da Silva et al. (2015))
