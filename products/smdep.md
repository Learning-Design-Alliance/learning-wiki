# SMDEP: a free six-week residential science enrichment program for disadvantaged undergraduates at 12 sites

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
SMDEP is described as "a free, six-week residential science enrichment program offered to rising college sophomores and juniors of minority and socioeconomically disadvantaged background who are interested in attending medical or dental school." It operates at 12 national sites, three offering only the medical component and the others both medical and dental components. The program targets competitiveness of applicants to medical and dental schools.

## Design Implications

### Context

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Goals
