# Talking about sex after prostate cancer

## Description
A SCORM-based Moodle e-learning resource developed by McCaughan et al. to provide healthcare professionals with foundation-level training in sexual-wellbeing communication and support for prostate-cancer patients.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **'Talking about sex after prostate cancer' brief e-learning resource with interactive and reflective elements**: A brief e-learning resource built as a SCORM package on a Moodle platform, developed through a "systematic, iterative and theory-based approach" including an expert group, evidence reviews, qualitative synthesis, a 2-h workshop with 21 clinical nurse specialists, and usability testing. It consists of three sections: background, the EASSi framework, and integration of learning into practice. Interactive elements include "a 'virtual tutor', quiz questions, videos showing patient and partner interviews, conversation demonstrations and goal-setting activities". (McCaughan E.M. et al. (2021))

### Claims

- [Healthcare professionals rated the e-learning resource as useful, easy to use and relevant to practice, and about one-third reported applying it in clinical practice at 4 weeks](../claims/elearning-usefulness-usability-ratings.md) [+W]
- [Brief e-learning significantly improved healthcare professionals' self-perceived understanding of how prostate cancer and its treatment affect men's sexual wellbeing, maintained at 4 weeks](../claims/elearning-improved-sexual-wellbeing-understanding.md) [+W]
- [Self-reported agreement that sexual issues should be discussed with partners of men living with prostate cancer rose from baseline to immediately after e-learning and remained higher at 4 weeks in one uncontrolled pre-post study](../claims/elearning-partner-discussion-attitude.md) [+W]
- [Confidence in addressing sexual concerns rose from baseline to immediately after e-learning but did not change significantly by 4 weeks in one uncontrolled pre-post study](../claims/elearning-confidence-gain-not-maintained.md) [~W]
- [Among healthcare professionals working in prostate cancer care, self-rated perceived knowledge of the right language for discussing sexual concerns rose from baseline to immediately after e-learning and was still higher at 4 weeks in one uncontrolled pre-post study](../claims/elearning-language-improvement-maintained.md) [+W]
- [Total modified SABS scores did not change significantly after the e-learning, despite significant changes on individual items](../claims/elearning-total-sabs-no-significant-change.md) [~W]
