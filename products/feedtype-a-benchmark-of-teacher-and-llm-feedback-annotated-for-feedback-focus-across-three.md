# FEEDTYPE: a benchmark of teacher and LLM feedback annotated for feedback focus across three writing domains

## Description
A released corpus of paragraph-level teacher- and LLM-generated writing feedback with feedback-focus annotations, scores, and student revisions across composition, ESL, and fiction courses, created by Norah Almousa et al.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **FEEDTYPE: a benchmark of teacher and LLM feedback annotated for feedback focus across three writing domains**: FEEDTYPE is a released corpus of paragraph-level teacher and LLM-generated feedback on student essays, drawn from the SEFORA corpus across composition, ESL, and fiction courses, with feedback focus type annotations, scores, and revisions. The authors state it is the only corpus that "jointly provides scores, paragraph-level feedback, feedback focus type annotations, and revisions across all three domains." It contains 1,966 annotated paragraphs and feedback from six LLMs under three prompting strategies. (Norah Almousa et al. (2026))
