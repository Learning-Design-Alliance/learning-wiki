# Train(e)d

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Train(e)d is an on-demand virtual training platform created by the study authors that provides expert-led modules, case examples, interactive learning, and peer discussion for clinical evaluators assessing spinal muscular atrophy and other neuromuscular disorders.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Train(e)d on-demand virtual training platform for neuromuscular disorder assessments**: Train(e)d is a sustainable, on-demand virtual training platform created as a direct result of this study, available at https://learn.muscle.ca/. It "delivers accessible, up-to-date educational modules focused on SMA assessments" with expert-led instruction on outcome measure selection, administration, and interpretation, real-world case examples, and interactive components. It also includes a dedicated online community forum where clinical evaluators can connect with peer experts, ask questions, and exchange clinical experiences. (Osman Homira et al. (2026))
