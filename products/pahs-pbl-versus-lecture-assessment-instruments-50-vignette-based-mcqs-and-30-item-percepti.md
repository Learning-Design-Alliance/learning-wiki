# PAHS PBL-versus-lecture assessment instruments: 50 vignette-based MCQs and 30-item perception questionnaire

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A researcher-developed assessment battery from Adhikari Yadav Saroj et al. comprising vignette-based multiple-choice tests of basic-science knowledge and a forced-Likert questionnaire measuring medical students' perceptions of PBL and lectures.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PAHS PBL-versus-lecture assessment instruments: 50 vignette-based MCQs and a 30-item perception questionnaire**: Two instruments were developed for this study: a 50-item vignette-based MCQ test split evenly between PBL-taught and lecture-taught topics, with scores interpreted as "<60% = very low, 60-70% = low, 70-80% = moderate, 80-90% = high and 90-100% = very high", and a 30-question forced Likert perception questionnaire (1 = strongly agree to 4 = strongly disagree) with qualitative comment options. The perception scale showed internal consistency with coefficient alpha 0.893. Both were piloted on 15 randomly selected students for validity and feasibility. (Adhikari Yadav Saroj et al. (2022))
