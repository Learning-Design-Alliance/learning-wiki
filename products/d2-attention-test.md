## Description
A standardized paper-and-pencil assessment developed for individually or group-administered measurement of selective attention and concentration in children and adolescents.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **D2 attention test as the measure of selective attention and concentration**: The D2 attention test is a paper-and-pencil measure of selective attention and concentration used to assess the students' cognitive functioning. It "consists of 14 rows of 47 characters each, with a total of 658 elements", where subjects cross the d's with two dashes, 20 seconds per row. Scores include TR, TA, O, C, TOT, CON, TR+, TR- and VAR, and the article reports test–retest reliability up to 0.90. (González-Fernández Francisco Tomás; Delgado-García Gabriel; Coll Jesús Siquier; Silva Ana Filipa; Nobari Hadi; Clemente Filipe Manuel (2023))
