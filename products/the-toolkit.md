## Description
The Toolkit is a four-part framework created by its authors to help teachers plan, implement, and evaluate technology-based lessons.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Four-piece toolkit architecture linking planning, implementation, and evaluation**: The Toolkit is architected as four complementary pieces that map onto stages of instructional practice: an entry-point Technology Affordances Matrix, diagnostic Conceptual Frameworks, concrete Checklists, and an Evaluation Sample demonstrating application to real student work. As the document states, the four pieces "are designed to work together to guide teachers through the process of planning, implementing and evaluating technology-based lessons." Each tool serves a distinct stage: choosing a work form, diagnosing levels, specifying skills, and modeling assessment. (The Evaluation Toolkit: A Work-in-Progress (2002))

# Four-piece toolkit architecture linking planning, implementation, and evaluation

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The Toolkit is architected as four complementary pieces that map onto stages of instructional practice: an entry-point Technology Affordances Matrix, diagnostic Conceptual Frameworks, concrete Checklists, and an Evaluation Sample demonstrating application to real student work. As the document states, the four pieces "are designed to work together to guide teachers through the process of planning, implementing and evaluating technology-based lessons." Each tool serves a distinct stage: choosing a work form, diagnosing levels, specifying skills, and modeling assessment.

- The Evaluation Sample is worked out in detail only for live presentations in this work-in-progress version

- K-12 teachers and the students they assess
