# Kaligo digital notebook app

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Kaligo is a stylus-oriented digital handwriting workbook developed in the IntuiScript project that analyzes children's handwriting, provides automated feedback and adaptive exercises, and supports teacher monitoring and personalization.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Kaligo digital notebook app for stylus-oriented tablets**: Kaligo is a digital workbook developed in the IntuiScript project that gives children realtime extrinsic feedback (knowledge of results) each time they write a letter, digit or word. Basic feedback covers "shape, direction, order of the strokes and continuity", shown as a colored horseshoe gauge with a star when production is sufficiently like the model. Its AI analyzes spatiotemporal handwriting features, adapts pedagogical scenarios automatically, and stores productions in a dashboard teachers use to program personalized exercises. (Nathalie Bonneton-Botté et al. (2020))
