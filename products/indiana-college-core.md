# Indiana College Core

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Indiana Commission for Higher Education's Indiana College Core programme enables high school students to earn transferable college credits, supported by the My College Core interactive planning tool and counselor guidance.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Indiana College Core and My College Core planning tool for transferable college credits in high school**: The Indiana College Core "lets students earn up to 30 college credits while in high school at low or no cost via Advanced Placement, International Baccalaureate, or dual enrollment/dual credit courses, and credits are easily transferable across all of Indiana's public postsecondary institutions." An accompanying interactive planning tool, My College Core, helps students plan and track coursework toward the Core and serves as a centralized resource with financial aid and transfer information. Students can share plans with school counselors, who help schedule classes aligned with their goals. (Rebeca Shackleford et al. (2024))
