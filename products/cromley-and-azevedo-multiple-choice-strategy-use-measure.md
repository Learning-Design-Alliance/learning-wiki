# Cromley and Azevedo multiple-choice strategy-use measure

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A domain-general and domain-specific assessment developed by Cromley and Azevedo that measures readers’ accurate enactment of reading-comprehension strategies through objectively scored multiple-choice items.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Multiple-choice strategy use measure requiring readers to enact strategies while retaining group administration and simple scoring**: Cromley and Azevedo developed a domain-general and domain-specific multiple-choice strategy use measure as an alternative to self-report questionnaires. The method requires readers to enact a strategy and measures whether they perform it accurately by posing multiple-choice questions. It preserves the practical advantages of questionnaires, namely group administration and simple scoring, and was examined with three samples within a series of studies testing a model of reading comprehension. (Gonny Schellings et al. (2011))
