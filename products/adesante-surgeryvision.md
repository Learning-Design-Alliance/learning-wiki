## Description
Adesante SurgeryVision is a virtual-reality software environment for interactive temporal-bone anatomy and mastoidectomy training, developed by Adesante and used with a head-mounted display and controllers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **VR temporal bone anatomy training environment (Adesante SurgeryVision with HTC Vive Pro)**: A VR environment for anatomical mastoidectomy training built from a reconstructed 3D model of a temporal bone generated from a basic computed tomography scan. The article states that "Two controllers and a head-mounted display (HTC Vive Pro, HTC, New Taipei, Taiwan) were used to display and manipulate the model in a VR environment." The model can be viewed from all angles and cut with a cross-sectioning tool. In the study, the VRT group (n = 16) used this environment during 2-hour structured training sessions in place of anatomy books and a dissection guide. (Tapiala Jesse et al. (2023))
