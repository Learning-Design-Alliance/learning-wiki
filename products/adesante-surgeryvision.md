## Description
Adesante SurgeryVision is a virtual-reality software environment for interactive temporal-bone anatomy and mastoidectomy training, developed by Adesante and used with a head-mounted display and controllers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **VR temporal bone anatomy training environment (Adesante SurgeryVision with HTC Vive Pro)**: A VR environment for anatomical mastoidectomy training built from a reconstructed 3D model of a temporal bone generated from a basic computed tomography scan. The article states that "Two controllers and a head-mounted display (HTC Vive Pro, HTC, New Taipei, Taiwan) were used to display and manipulate the model in a VR environment." The model can be viewed from all angles and cut with a cross-sectioning tool. In the study, the VRT group (n = 16) used this environment during 2-hour structured training sessions in place of anatomy books and a dissection guide. (Tapiala Jesse et al. (2023))
- **Adesante SurgeryVision VR environment with head-mounted display for temporal bone anatomy evaluation**: A medical VR environment in which HRCT data are rendered as 3D volumes viewed in a head-mounted display (HTC Vive Pro) with hand-held controllers, created with the Adesante SurgeryVision™ software. The article states that "A head-mounted display (HTC Vive Pro, HTC, New Taipei, Taiwan) with a pair of controllers was used to visualize and manipulate the 3D models in the VR environment generated by the software". Participants could rotate the model, adjust image settings, and use preset windows for bone, soft tissue and translucent bone visualization. It was evaluated against PACS cross-sectional viewing in a simulated preoperative planning setting. (Timonen Tomi et al. (2022))
