# DIRECT (Determining and Interpreting Resistive Electric Circuit Concepts Test)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 29-item multiple-choice diagnostic test developed by Engelhardt et al. for assessing high-school and university students’ conceptual understanding and misconceptions about DC resistive electric circuits.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **DIRECT: a 29-item multiple-choice diagnostic test on DC resistive electric circuits for high school and university students**: DIRECT (Determining and Interpreting Resistive Electric Circuit Concepts Test) is a diagnostic instrument with two versions, each of 29 multiple-choice questions, built from a set of instructional objectives and with common misconceptions built into the distracters. The article states that "Common misconceptions were incorporated into the distracters of the test items" and that it was "designed for use with high school and college/university students." Version 1.1 increased answer choices to five and redrew bulb-in-socket diagrams after interviews showed confusion. (Engelhardt et al. (1995))
