# UK Obesity Care Competencies Working Group's 29 core obesity care competencies

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A five-domain framework of 29 obesity-care competencies developed by the UK Obesity Care Competencies Working Group for selecting and assessing competencies relevant to health professionals in specialist obesity services.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Table 1 list of 29 core obesity care competencies across five domains**: Table 1 prints the full set of 29 core competencies devised by the UK Obesity Care Competencies Working Group, organised under the five core domains. Items range from knowledge competencies (e.g. "Demonstrates knowledge of obesity epidemiology") to patient care competencies such as eliciting a comprehensive obesity-focused history, using evidence-based models of health behaviour change to assess readiness to change, and applying knowledge of nutrition, physical activity, behavioural, pharmacological and surgical treatments to personalised care plans. The list is the framework's operational content for training and assessment design. (Capehorn Matthew S. et al. (2022))
