## Description
RM-CC5 is a comprehensive, adaptive, blended digital core curriculum for Grade 5 mathematics developed by the nonprofit Reasoning Mind, intended to replace textbook materials. Students progress through missions aligned to 49 grade-level learning objectives (plus below- and above-grade objectives), solving problems at three difficulty levels with immediate feedback, and complete 136 "Smarter Solving" test-preparation activities; game-like features such as narratives, characters, and points motivate students. Teachers receive real-time dashboards with performance data and instructional recommendations, and implementation coordinators coach against an implementation rubric.

- Sufficient school technology resources and daily use as the core mathematics resource, with approximately 60 hours of required teacher professional development over two years
#### Constraints

### Target Learners
- Grade 5 elementary mathematics students

### Target Learning Goals
