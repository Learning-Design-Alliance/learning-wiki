# La Serena School for Data Science (LSSDS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q1`–`q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
AURA's intensive multidisciplinary data-science school in La Serena, Chile, where advanced undergraduate and early graduate students receive lectures, hands-on training, and faculty-mentored group projects using real astronomy and bioscience data.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **LSSDS student selection process with ranking criteria and a numerical bias-mitigating scoring system**: LSSDS receives between 150 and 300 applications for a limited number of ~30 positions, targeting students with diverse majors and huge cultural and language differences. The committee builds a ranking favouring early-career students and applicants with misconceptions about the topics, then applies corrective adjustments for gender parity, flat field distribution, and small-university representation. Because evaluation of motivation letters is "extremely sensitive to biases", recent editions use a numerical scoring system matching skills to possible expressions proposed by English and Spanish native speakers. (A. Bayo et al. (2024))
- **LSSDS program structure: roughly 34% lecture time, 23% hands-on lab time, and 43% project work time**: The LSSDS course pattern allocates time so that project work dominates: "roughly 34% of the time for lectures, 23% of the time for Hands-on / Lab sessions, and 43% of the time allocated to 'project' work". Lectures cover data science, statistics, machine learning, databases, image processing and NLP, with material as slides and Python notebooks. Hands-on sessions are Python notebooks with missing or erroneous cells; project work is carried out in groups of four on real faculty research projects and occupies the fully devoted second half of the school. (A. Bayo et al. (2024))

- [The LSSDS program shows high demand (oversubscription factor 5-8) and positive testimony from alumni and faculty.](../claims/lssds-oversubscription-factor-5-8-testimony.md) [+W]
