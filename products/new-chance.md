# New Chance: a two-phase education and employment program for young welfare mothers who dropped out of school

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
New Chance is a program for young welfare mothers who have dropped out of school, aiming to improve "both their employment potential and their parenting skills." Phase one provides adult basic education, GED preparation, and life skills training including parenting education; phase two provides occupational skills training, internships, and job placement assistance. Participants have access to free child care and a case manager throughout, and programs are small, serving no more than 40 participants at any time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants must be 16 to 22 years old, have first given birth as a teenager, be economically disadvantaged, lack a high school diploma or GED certificate, and not be pregnant at program entry
#### Constraints
- New Chance is no longer an active program, and no current developer or contact information is available
- Services are available for a period of up to 18 months and participation is voluntary

### Target Learners
- young welfare mothers aged 16-22 who dropped out of school and lack a high school diploma or GED certificate

### Target Learning Goals
- high school diploma or GED completion
- parenting and life skills
- occupational skills and employment readiness
