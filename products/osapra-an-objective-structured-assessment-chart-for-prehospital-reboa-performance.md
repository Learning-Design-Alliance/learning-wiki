## Description
A 12-item, five-point global-rating assessment chart developed by the study authors to evaluate physician-staffed prehospital personnel's technical performance of resuscitative endovascular balloon occlusion of the aorta (REBOA).

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **OSAPRA: an objective structured assessment chart for prehospital REBOA performance**: The OSAPRA is a global-rating-scale-based assessment chart developed for this study because no prehospital REBOA rating scale existed. It consists of "12 5-point categorical scores each anchored 0–4", for a total of 48 points, with an investigator-agreed cut score of 30 for adequate performance. Assessments were performed from video recordings independently by two observers, with a third observer resolving major discrepancies. (Brede Jostein Rødseth et al. (2019))
