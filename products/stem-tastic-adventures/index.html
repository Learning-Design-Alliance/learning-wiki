STEM-tastic Adventures¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A free mobile app developed through a National Science Foundation-funded co-design project with preschool teachers and families to provide children ages 3–5 with playful computational-thinking and STEM learning activities.

Components¶

  • STEM-tastic Adventures app provides young children ages 3-5 with playful CT and STEM learning opportunities: The STEM-tastic Adventures app is a free resource, available through the App Store and Google Play, that emerged from a National Science Foundation-funded co-design project with preschool teachers and families. Its "City Walk" game "provides opportunities for children to identify a sequence of directions for the robot" and "progressively presents more complex sequences and scaffolds how to identify errors (testing and debugging) with the support of visual and audio feedback."
