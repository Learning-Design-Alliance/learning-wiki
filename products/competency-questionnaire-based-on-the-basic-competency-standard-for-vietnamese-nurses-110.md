# Competency questionnaire based on the Basic Competency Standard for Vietnamese Nurses (110 indicators, 25 competencies)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A web-based self-administered questionnaire developed by Horii Satoko et al. to measure new graduate nurses’ self-rated clinical competencies across 25 competency areas in Vietnam.
