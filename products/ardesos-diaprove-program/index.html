ARDESOS-DIAPROVE program¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A 16-week classroom programme developed by the ARDESOS-DIAPROVE team that teaches critical thinking through problem-based and cooperative learning activities, contextualized problems, rubrics, and feedback.

Components¶

  • DIAPROVE: a best-explanation methodology for critical thinking instruction: DIAPROVE (DIAgnosis, PROgnosis and VErification) is the authors' instructional methodology that shifts the center of critical thinking from argumentation toward "the best explanation", treating efficacy as the defining feature of critical thinking. It reorganizes instruction around "three fundamental aspects: learning to look, learning to combine conditional and causal structures and, finally, learning to rule out explanations or hypotheses". It was added to the ARDESOS program without removing existing components and tested against the unmodified program. (Carlos Saiz (2016))
  • ARDESOS v2 critical thinking instruction program: ARDESOS (ARgumentation, DEcision, SOlving of problems in daily Situations) is a direct-teaching program for critical thinking skills built on Problem-Based Learning with daily personal and professional problems. It ran "over 16 weeks" for "a period of 55 -60 hours", in classes of 30-35 students working in groups of 4, organized in four activity blocks: argumentation, conditional and analogical reasoning, explanation and causality, and decision making. Rubrics guided each activity and weekly quantitative feedback was given 2-3 days after sessions. (Carlos Saiz (2016))

Claims¶

  • The ARDESOS-DIAPROVE intervention significantly improved critical thinking skills, with higher PENCRISAL medians after intervention in the total score and all five factors [+M]
  • A critical thinking instruction program (ARDESOS-DIAPROVE) improved students' metacognition, with significant pre-post gains in the metaknowledge total and most MAI dimensions [+M]
  • Students taught with the DIAPROVE methodology outperformed the ARDESOS-only group in deductive reasoning [+M]
  • The group-by-time interaction was significant only for inductive reasoning, where the DIAPROVE group improved more than the ARDESOS-only group [+M]
  • The ARDESOS program produced a significant pre-post improvement in overall critical thinking scores [+M]
