## Description
A six-domain patient-safety competency framework finalised and published by the Canadian Patient Safety Institute for guiding education, practice, and assessment of healthcare professionals.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The Canadian Safety Competencies Framework's six domains as the content scope for patient safety assessment**: The review adopts the Canadian Safety Competencies Framework, finalised by the Canadian Patient Safety Institute in 2008, as the content scope for selecting and analysing assessment tools. The framework "consists of six core competency domains: 1. Contributing to a culture of patient safety. 2. Working in teams for patient safety. 3. Communicating effectively for patient safety. 4. Managing safety risks. 5. Optimising human and environmental factors. 6. Recognising, responding to, and disclosing adverse events". Articles were included only if their assessment content covered these domains. (Okuyama Ayako et al. (2011))
