## Description
A self-administered 10-item questionnaire developed by the researchers to assess surgical nurses’ knowledge of negative-pressure wound therapy indications, device operation, patient care, and anomaly response.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Researcher-developed 10-item NPWT knowledge evaluation scale for surgical nurses**: A self-administered, semistructured two-part questionnaire developed by the researchers after literature review and consultation with three wound experts. The knowledge part contains 10 items covering "NPWT indications and effects, operation process of NPWT devices, patient assessment and care, and anomaly response", rated on a 3-point Likert-type scale with total scores from 10 to 30. The scale's content validity index was 0.83. (Tseng Yi-Ling et al. (2025))
