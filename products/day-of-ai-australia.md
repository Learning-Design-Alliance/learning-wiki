## Description
Day of AI Australia is an initiative providing free curriculum-aligned AI-literacy resources and school workshops for secondary students, including lessons on AI foundations, ethics, safety, misinformation, and careers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Day of AI Australia single-day workshop program (five lessons on AI foundations, ethics, safety, misinformation and careers)**: Day of AI Australia is an initiative providing free curriculum-aligned resources to schools on machine learning, AI ethics, and natural language processing. The evaluated intervention was delivered in a single-day format following a linear progression from pre-survey through Lessons 1–2 (Foundations & Ethics), then either Lessons 3–4 (Safety & Literacy) or Lesson 5 (Careers), then post-survey, with sequence varying by school scheduling. Lessons had students experiment with AI models, analyse media examples and biases, examine deepfake risks and personal data value, and evaluate AI-generated content. (Christian Bergh et al. (2026))
