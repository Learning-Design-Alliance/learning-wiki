# Kids in Transition to School (KITS) Program

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A manualized school-readiness intervention developed by Pears and colleagues that combines child groups and parent groups to support children with developmental disabilities or delays entering kindergarten.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **KITS Program: 24-session child school readiness group plus 8-session parent group delivered before and during the kindergarten transition**: The Kids in Transition to School Program is a short-term, intensive school readiness intervention with two components: "a 24-session school readiness group for the children (2 hours twice weekly in the school readiness phase, 16 sessions; 2 hours once weekly in the transition/maintenance phase, 8 sessions)" and "an 8-session parent group (2 hours every 2 weeks; 4 sessions in each intervention phase)". The manualized child curriculum covers early literacy, prosocial skills, and self-regulatory skills, delivered in classrooms with a 4:1 student-to-teacher ratio. Parents receive parallel content on literacy involvement, routines, and behavior management, with missed content delivered via home visits. (Pears et al. (2016))
- **Deliver school readiness programming during the summer before kindergarten to fill the summer services gap for high-risk children**: The article argues that the transition into school is an optimal time to intervene on school readiness, and that academic-year calendars create "a summer services gap during which the most vulnerable, highest-risk children may lose or fail to gain valuable skills". A short-term intensive summer program delivered just before kindergarten entry, followed by maintenance sessions in the first months of kindergarten, can augment early intervention services and boost literacy and self-regulation before school starts. The KITS trial provides evidence that such programming improves early literacy in children with developmental disabilities and behavioral difficulties. (Pears et al. (2016))
- **KITS school readiness intervention: 24-session child group plus parent group with clear, contingent feedback**: The Kids In Transition to School (KITS) program is a 4-month intervention for children entering kindergarten at high risk for poor school adjustment, consisting of "a 24-session school readiness group and an 8-session parent group". The manualized child curriculum covers self-regulatory, prosocial, and early literacy skills, taught through instruction, role-playing, and activity-based intervention. A key feature is "the provision of consistent, contingent feedback about children's performance" delivered by a graduate-level lead teacher and two assistant teachers for groups of 12–15 children, with a parallel parent curriculum on consistent behavior management. (McDermott et al. (2017))
