IKS-Instruct dataset¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A released multilingual dataset of 24,795 instruction-response pairs for fine-tuning and evaluating language models on Indian Knowledge Systems pedagogy, created and released by Shwetha Singaravelu and colleagues.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • IKS-Instruct fine-tuning is necessary for IKS pedagogical capability: a compact 7B fine-tune reaches a median judge score of 6.39, within 0.15 of a general-purpose reference, while the base model scores near zero on IKS-specific dimensions [+M]
  • Model-level judge quality is non-monotonic in dataset size: the 13,882-pair v1.1 yields the best model (6.39), while both expansion to ~20,000 pairs (6.33) and aggressive curation to 7,130 pairs (6.12) reduce quality [+M]
