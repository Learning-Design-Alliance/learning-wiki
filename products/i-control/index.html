I Control¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

I Control is a yearlong self-regulation curriculum developed for middle-school students with significant emotional and behavioral problems, combining scripted classroom lessons with the Brain Training Lab computerized executive-function games.

Components¶

  • I Control curriculum with Brain Training Lab for middle school students with significant behavior problems: I Control is a yearlong self-regulation curriculum for middle school students with significant behavior problems in self-contained and alternative settings. Per the article, it is "a yearlong program with four units of instruction in SR (i.e., introduction to EF, goal setting, emotion regulation, SPS) and computerized games to directly train EF skills." It comprises 46 scripted lessons of about 30 minutes delivered twice weekly, plus the Brain Training Lab, a set of computerized games giving students 20 to 30 minutes of weekly direct training of working memory, cognitive shift, and inhibitory control. Lessons combine direct instruction, teacher modeling, guided practice through role-plays and journaling, and independent practice, concluding with six booster lessons for generalization. (Stephen W. Smith et al. (2017))

Claims¶

  • I Control improved teacher-reported executive function behaviors, with the largest effects for students with the highest baseline deficits on the BRIEF Behavior Regulation Index [+M]
  • Students taught I Control reported better emotional control than controls regardless of pretest score, and teacher-reported gains appeared for students with the highest pretest deficits [+M]
  • Students taught I Control gained more curricular knowledge than controls on Units 1–3, with Unit 4 knowledge gains for students with lower pretest knowledge [+M]
