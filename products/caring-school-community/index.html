Caring School Community™¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (1 for, 1 mixed, 2 against) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A multiyear K–6 school improvement programme developed by the Developmental Studies Center that uses class meetings, cross-age buddies, family activities, and schoolwide community-building to promote prosocial behavior and shared values.

Components¶

  • Caring School Community™ program with four schoolwide components: Caring School Community™ (formerly the Child Development Project) is a multiyear school improvement program for all students in grades K–6 that aims to promote core values, prosocial behavior, and a schoolwide feeling of community. The report states "The program consists of four elements originally developed for the Child Development Project: class meeting lessons, cross-age “buddies” programs, “homeside” activities, and schoolwide community." Class meeting lessons use scripted sessions to teach values such as fairness, caring, and respect; buddies activities pair older and younger classes; homeside activities are conversational activities sent home to families; and schoolwide activities bui (What Works Clearinghouse (2007))
