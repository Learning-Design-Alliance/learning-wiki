## Description
Wailand's social growth scale is a standardized age-spanning assessment instrument for measuring social maturity and daily-life self-help and social abilities.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Wailand's social growth scale as the outcome measure**: Wailand's social growth scale is the instrument used to measure social maturity in this study. It "covers birth to older than 25 years" with age-specific questions to age 12 and common questions thereafter; it "has 117 items but 84 items were questioned" for these children aged 12 and younger. Items fall in eight categories including self-help general, eating, dressing, direction, occupation, communication, locomotion and socialization, and answers are rated 0, 0.5 and 1 to compute a social quotient. (Shakibaei et al. (2013))
