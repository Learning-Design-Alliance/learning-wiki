## Components

- **Check & Connect theory of action: student engagement as the primary mediator linking mentoring to school completion**: The article describes a theory of action in which trained mentors' persistent support improves students' behavioral, affective, and cognitive engagement, which improves interim academic outcomes (persistence, course performance, credit accrual, achievement) and ultimately on-time graduation. Engagement is described as "a primary mediator for promoting school completion" and as multidimensional, spanning behavioral, affective, and cognitive engagement. The framework draws on research on resiliency, systems theory, cognitive-behavioral theory, and intrinsic motivation, and is depicted in Figure 1 with some cyclical interrelationships. (Heppen et al. # Check & Connect: monitored mentoring dropout-prevention program

## Description
Check & Connect is a dropout-prevention intervention built on monitoring of school performance, mentoring, case management, and other supports. The Check component is designed to "continually monitor student performance and progress" while the Connect component involves program staff "giving individualized attention to students in partnership with other school staff, family members, and community service organizations." A monitor reviews attendance, behavior, and academic problems and intervenes when problems are identified. Developers report implementation cost of about $1,400 per student per year in secondary schools (2001-02 school year).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Monitors work with the same students across several years, following them to different schools as needed
- Program coordinators who supervise monitors can be teachers or school psychologists
#### Constraints
- Most recent cost information was not available at the time of the report

### Target Learners
- high school students with learning, behavioral, or emotional disabilities at risk of dropping out
