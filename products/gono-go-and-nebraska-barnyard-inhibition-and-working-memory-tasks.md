# Go/No-Go and Nebraska Barnyard inhibition and working-memory tasks

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A computerized assessment battery combining a fish/shark Go/No-Go inhibition task and the Nebraska Barnyard complex-span working-memory task for preschool children.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Go/No-Go and Nebraska Barnyard tasks measuring inhibition and working memory in preschoolers**: The Go/No-Go task, adapted from Simpson and Riggs, presents fish (Go, 75% of trials) and sharks (No-Go, 25%) with children pressing a button for fish and withholding for sharks; the percentage of correct No-Go responses indexes "inhibition of the prepotent, more frequent button press response." The Nebraska Barnyard is a computerized complex span task adapted from the Noisy Book in which children remember sequences of animal names and press corresponding colored boxes, with span scored as "the maximum span length (highest span where the subject was correct on two trials)." Both were standardized before entering multilevel models so their effects on costs were directly comparable. (Chevalier N. et al. (2012))
