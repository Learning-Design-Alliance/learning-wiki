# Palliative care communication skill assessment scale for healthcare staff

## Description
A 39-item, six-dimension online Likert self-report scale developed by Qiu Lanying et al. to assess communication skills and attitudes among oncology physicians and nurses in palliative-care settings.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Palliative care communication skill assessment scale for healthcare staff (39 items, six dimensions, ~7 minutes)**: A self-report instrument measuring healthcare staff communication skills in cancer palliative care, developed through literature analysis, an open-ended questionnaire, Delphi expert consultation, and pilot testing. The final version contains 39 items across six dimensions covering cognition, emotion, behavioral intention, verbal expression and skills, empathy, and use of appropriate communication methods. Items use a 5-point Likert format with positive scoring, and "The design of the tool emphasizes objectivity and ease of understanding, with an average completion time of approximately seven minutes." (Qiu Lanying et al. (2025))
