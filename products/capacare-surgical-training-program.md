## Description
A three-year surgical training pathway for associate clinicians and other eligible health professionals, organised by CapaCare with Sierra Leone's Ministry of Health and Sanitation and the United Nations Population Fund.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CapaCare surgical training program for associate clinicians in Sierra Leone**: The CapaCare program is a 3-year surgical training pathway for associate clinicians, run since 2011 by a Norwegian NGO with the Sierra Leone Ministry of Health and Sanitation and the United Nations Population Fund. Candidates need a diploma in community health plus at least 2 years of work experience as community health officers. The article states "ACs participate in a 3-year surgical training program organized since 2011 by CapaCare", including 1 year of internship, and the trial's AC operators came from this program. (Ashley T et al (2021))
