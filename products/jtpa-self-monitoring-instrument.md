# JTPA self-monitoring instrument

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A North Carolina State Department of Public Instruction instrument for reviewing Job Training Partnership Act program compliance across administrative, eligibility, training, work-experience, transition, and employment provisions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **JTPA self-monitoring instrument with 28 rated compliance indicators**: A rating instrument accompanying the guide on which each compliance item is scored 2 (meets program standards), 1 (does not meet), or 0 (not applicable). "The instrument lists the references, reviewlevels, procedures and indicators to be used in collectinginformation to determine compliance." Items span program administration, eligibility, pre-employment skills training, work experience, school-to-work transition, and tryout employment provisions. (North Carolina State Dept. of Public Instruction (1988))

# JTPA self-monitoring instrument with 28 rated compliance indicators

## Description
A rating instrument accompanying the guide on which each compliance item is scored 2 (meets program standards), 1 (does not meet), or 0 (not applicable). "The instrument lists the references, reviewlevels, procedures and indicators to be used in collectinginformation to determine compliance." Items span program administration, eligibility, pre-employment skills training, work experience, school-to-work transition, and tryout employment provisions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Reviewers apply the instrument's procedure tests, conduct interviews, observe classrooms, and review records such as project applications, counseling logs, and budgets
#### Constraints
- Indicators are tied to specific JTPA statutory and state references (e.g., Sec. 165, Sec. 203, SDA 84-20) and apply only to JTPA-funded programs

### Target Learners
- JTPA coordinators and self-monitoring team members in school districts

### Target Learning Goals
- Determining regulatory compliance of local JTPA programs
