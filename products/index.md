* [AI-Enabled EdTech Adoption Cycle](ai-enabled-edtech-adoption-cycle.md) - A four-phase adoption framework described by Ruiz et al.
* [Aurora Institute's Seven Critical Elements of Instructional Practice](aurora-institutes-seven-critical-elements-of-instructional-practice.md) - A framework developed by the Aurora Institute to guide competency-based instructional practice through learner agency, meaningful assessment, differentiated support, mastery-based progression, varied pathways and pacing, equity strategies, and transparent expectations.
* [Bachelor's Degree Attainment Index (Bachelor's DAI) and Direct Bachelor's DAI](bachelors-degree-attainment-index-bachelors-dai-and-direct-bachelors-dai.md) - These are UChicago Consortium measurement metrics estimating the proportion of a ninth-grade cohort that earns a bachelor's degree, distinguishing direct four-year pathways from pathways involving delayed entry or two-year colleges.
* [Career-connected learning (CCL) framework](career-connected-learning-ccl-framework.md) - A Chicago Public Schools framework that coordinates college-preparatory and career-readiness activities across grades 6–12 through strengthened CTE and districtwide planning.
* [CCSR five-category framework of noncognitive factors](ccsr-five-category-framework-of-noncognitive-factors.md) - A research-synthesis framework developed by the University of Chicago Consortium on Chicago School Research (CCSR) that organises noncognitive factors into academic behaviors, academic perseverance, social skills, learning strategies, and academic mindsets.
* [Challenge Based Learning (CBL) Framework](challenge-based-learning-cbl-framework.md) - A challenge-driven learning framework developed and maintained through Apple-supported Challenge Based Learning initiatives for educators and learners working on authentic real-world challenges.
* [Chicago High School Redesign Initiative (CHSRI)](chicago-high-school-redesign-initiative-chsri.md) - A Chicago Public Schools high-school redesign initiative that created small, voluntary schools with supported autonomy and organizational and instructional reforms for urban students.
* [College Explorer](college-explorer.md) - College Explorer is a data-visualization tool from NWEA Research that links MAP Growth scores with national benchmarks for colleges, universities, and majors to support post-secondary planning.
* [College match indicator](college-match-indicator.md) - A measurement tool developed by the University of Chicago Consortium on School Research to gauge how students are progressing toward college enrollment by comparing their academic qualifications with college enrollment outcomes.
* [College Readiness Indicator Systems (CRIS)](college-readiness-indicator-systems-cris.md) - A framework developed by the Annenberg Institute for School Reform and partners for organizing indicators of students' academic preparedness, academic tenacity, and college knowledge.
* [Computational Thinking Pathways](computational-thinking-pathways.md) - A Digital Promise framework of system-wide K–12 computational-thinking learning progressions designed to support equitable participation through consistent, cumulative, and competency-based learning across classrooms and years.
* [Digital Promise AI Literacy Brief Series](digital-promise-ai-literacy-brief-series.md) - A series of Digital Promise implementation briefs that provides audience-specific AI literacy recommendations, strategies, examples, and equity considerations for PK-12, postsecondary, workforce, and informal learning settings.
* [Digital Promise Micro-credential Platform](digital-promise-micro-credential-platform.md) - A competency-based micro-credential platform developed and operated by Digital Promise through which K–12 educators select credentials, submit implementation evidence, and receive expert-reviewed competency recognition.
* [Digital Promise's Competency-Based Assessment Framework](digital-promises-competency-based-assessment-framework.md) - A framework created and maintained by Digital Promise to guide the design and accessibility review of competency-based micro-credentials on its platform.
* [Digital Promise's Inclusive Innovation model](digital-promises-inclusive-innovation-model.md) - An equity-centered research-and-development framework created by Digital Promise that places historically marginalized communities at the center of identifying, designing, developing, and implementing educational and workforce technologies.
* [Early College](early-college.md) - A whole-school dual-enrollment programme run through district-college partnerships that combines college-level coursework with academic, social, and transition supports for high-school students.
* [Five EdTech Quality Indicators](five-edtech-quality-indicators.md) - A framework developed by 1EdTech, CAST, CoSN, Digital Promise, InnovateEDU, ISTE+ASCD, and SETDA for evaluating AI-enabled edtech against safety, evidence, inclusion, usability, and interoperability criteria.
* [Foundations for Young Adult Success: A Developmental Framework](foundations-for-young-adult-success-a-developmental-framework.md) - A conceptual framework developed by Nagaoka and colleagues at the University of Chicago Consortium on Chicago School Research that organizes the knowledge, skills, mindsets, strategies, and behaviors intended to support agency and success from childhood through young adulthood.
* [Freshman OnTrack indicator](freshman-ontrack-indicator.md) - A research-based early-warning measurement developed and used by the University of Chicago Consortium and Chicago Public Schools to identify ninth-grade students at risk of not graduating.
* [K-12 Computer Science Framework](k-12-computer-science-framework.md) - A framework developed by ACM, CSTA, Code.org, and collaborating experts that defines core practices and concepts for K-12 computer science education.
* [Mellon Research Forum initiative on the value of a liberal arts education](mellon-research-forum-initiative-on-the-value-of-a-liberal-arts-education.md) - A Mellon Foundation research initiative that supports and organizes scholarly inquiry into the multiple outcomes of a liberal arts education.
* [National Education Longitudinal Survey (NELS)](national-education-longitudinal-survey-nels.md) - A nationally representative longitudinal education dataset collected and maintained by the National Center for Education Statistics, tracking students from eighth grade through postsecondary outcomes.
* [NCS capacity-building model](ncs-capacity-building-model.md) - A whole-school improvement framework developed and implemented by the Network for College Success to build school staff capacity through professional learning networks, data use, coaching, and distributed leadership.
* [Post-secondary Attainment Index (PAI)](post-secondary-attainment-index-pai.md) - The University of Chicago Consortium on School Research's Post-secondary Attainment Index is a district-level indicator combining high-school graduation, college enrollment, and college-completion rates to estimate post-secondary attainment for CPS students.
* [Practice-driven data](practice-driven-data.md) - A five-lesson, context-sensitive framework for using data in school improvement—prepare, focus, make meaning, strategize, and disrupt—developed through the University of Chicago’s partnership with Chicago Public Schools.
* [Readiness Framework (Appendix B)](readiness-framework-appendix-b.md) - A district-readiness framework developed by Zohal Shah et al.
* [SEAD integration framework (the three-legged stool)](sead-integration-framework-the-three-legged-stool.md) - A guide-based framework for integrating explicit social-emotional skill instruction, embedded academic practice, and relationship-rich environments into K–12 school improvement.
* [SEERNet](seernet.md) - SEERNet is an Institute of Education Sciences-funded network that supports responsible, inclusive, theory-driven research using data from digital learning platforms through shared infrastructure, governance, datasets, and research-practice partnerships.
* [Shaping Our Appalachian Region (SOAR)](shaping-our-appalachian-region-soar.md) - A nonprofit regional revitalization programme that coordinates Appalachian individuals and organizations around shared values and goals, including technology, computer science education, and digital-economy workforce development.
* [Six-domain AI skills and competencies framework for educators and educational leaders](six-domain-ai-skills-and-competencies-framework-for-educators-and-educational-leaders.md) - A Digital Promise framework organizing AI literacy and readiness competencies for PK–12 educators and educational leaders across technical, analytical, interpersonal, ethical, adaptive, and implementation domains.
* [Six-domain AI skills and competencies framework for PK-12 learners](six-domain-ai-skills-and-competencies-framework-for-pk-12-learners.md) - A Digital Promise framework that organizes PK-12 learner AI competencies across technical, practical, ethical, analytical, interpersonal, and adaptive domains.
* [Teacher Assessment of College-going Climate measure](teacher-assessment-of-college-going-climate-measure.md) - A school-level survey-based measure developed by Roderick et al.
* [Three-component framework for Portrait of a Graduate assessment systems](three-component-framework-for-portrait-of-a-graduate-assessment-systems.md) - A framework developed by Kyle Dunbar et al.
* [To&Through Project](tothrough-project.md) - The To&Through Project at the UChicago Consortium on School Research tracks Chicago Public Schools students from high school through college and publishes the data in public online tools.
* [Woodcock-Johnson Tests of Achievement—Applied Problems subtest](woodcock-johnson-tests-of-achievementapplied-problems-subtest.md) - A standardized achievement subtest measuring applied mathematical problem-solving and early numeracy, developed and published as part of the Woodcock-Johnson assessment system by Riverside Insights.
