# Key Features examination and WHOBARS behavioral rating scale

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A paired patient-safety assessment consisting of a scenario-based Key Features examination for clinical reasoning and the WHOBARS observational rating scale for checklist-based surgical safety performance.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Key Features examination and WHOBARS checklist for assessing reasoning and performance in patient safety education**: The study used two instruments: a paper-based Key Features examination (15 questions, each with one scenario and 12 options from which students chose three; scoring range 0–45) to assess reasoning in managing patient safety incidents, validated by 15 experts; and the WHOBARS behavioral rating scale covering Sign-In, Time-Out, and Sign-Out, each with five items scored 1 (poor) to 7 (excellent), total range 15–105. "The psychometrics of the mentioned instrument were approved by the authors (Cronbach's Alpha 0.86, ICC = 0.81)" for WHOBARS. (Mehrdad M et al. (2025))
