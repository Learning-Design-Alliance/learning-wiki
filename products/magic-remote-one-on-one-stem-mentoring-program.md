# MAGIC remote one-on-one STEM mentoring program

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
MAGIC is a nonprofit programme that matches girls interested in STEM with women technology and computing professionals for remote one-to-one mentoring and project-based STEM learning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MAGIC remote one-on-one STEM mentoring program for teenaged girls**: MAGIC is a non-profit that acts as "a nationwide matching bridge between girls who are interested in STEM topics and women with successful technology and computing careers". Each pair is matched on mentee interests and maturity, schedules, mentor expertise, and mentor maturity; relationships officially last four to seven months and culminate in a final project presented at a partner-school meeting. Over five years it facilitated 16 mentors mentoring 23 girls from 12 schools across at least seven US states. (Khare et al. (2013))
