Description¶

CARE for Teachers (Cultivating Awareness and Resilience in Education) is a mindfulness-based professional-development programme developed and delivered for teachers, combining emotion-skills instruction, mindful awareness and stress-reduction practices, caring and listening practices, coaching, and home-practice materials.

Components¶

  • CARE for Teachers professional development program: CARE for Teachers (Cultivating Awareness and Resilience in Education) is a mindfulness-based professional development program for teachers combining "emotion skills instruction, mindful awareness and stress reduction practices and caring and listening practices". It was delivered as "30 hr over 5 in-person training days (6 hr each) between November and February", plus three one-on-one phone coaching calls during intersession breaks, a workbook, and audio recordings of practices for home practice. (Jennings et al. (2017))
  • CARE for Teachers logic model: synergistic program elements producing teacher and classroom outcomes: The CARE for Teachers logic model (Figure 1) maps program elements to hypothesized outcomes for K-5 teachers. The article states the "program elements of emotion skills instruction, mindful awareness and stress reduction practices and caring and listening practices are hypothesized to promote increases in adaptive emotion regulation, teaching efficacy and mindfulness and reductions in psychological and physical distress". The elements are hypothesized to act synergistically, such that "no one single program element is hypothesized to have a unique and direct impact on any one outcome". (Jennings et al. (2017))

Claims¶

  • CARE for Teachers improved teachers' adaptive emotion regulation, mindfulness, psychological distress, and time urgency in a cluster randomized trial [+M]
  • CARE for Teachers improved the emotional support domain of observed classroom interaction quality (CLASS) [+M]
