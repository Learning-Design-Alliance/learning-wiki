## Description
Concentrated Language Encounter (CLE) is a named language-teaching curriculum approach developed by its proponents and implemented by EFL teachers to build English communication, reading, and writing through immersive activity, modeling, and scaffolding.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Concentrated Language Encounter (CLE) instruction theory: immersion, scaffolding, and activity-based language learning**: The Concentrated Language Encounter approach holds that children learn language mainly through encounters with others in which they concentrate intensely on making themselves understood. The article identifies three principles: "an immersion program in which children are constantly involved in doing new and ever more challenging activities using language"; scaffolding, where the teacher models and supports what students need to accomplish; and activity-based learning in which students understand the meaning of what they read and write. The approach draws on the natural approach, genre-based approach, and communicative approach. (Promnont et al. (2015))
