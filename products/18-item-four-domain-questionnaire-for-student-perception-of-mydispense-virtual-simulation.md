# 18-item four-domain questionnaire for student perception of MyDispense virtual simulation

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An expert-reviewed, 5-point Likert questionnaire developed by Palanisamy et al. to assess Pharm.D. students' perceptions of exercise, instructor, technical, and communication aspects of MyDispense virtual simulation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Four-domain questionnaire structure covering exercise, instructor, technical, and communication perceptions**: The questionnaire "was structured with four domains, including exercise, instructor, technical, and communication", each with five items on a 5-point Likert scale. Three domains map onto course learning outcome domains of knowledge, cognitive, and skill; the instructor domain covers teaching strategies and instruction methods. The authors state the extra domains let academicians improve exercise quality and instructional approach through student feedback. (Palanisamy A et al. (2024))
