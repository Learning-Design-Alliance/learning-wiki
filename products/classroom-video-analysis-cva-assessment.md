## Description
A video-based assessment developed by Ramirez and colleagues that elicits and scores teachers' usable mathematical teaching knowledge through written analyses of authentic mathematics-instruction episodes.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Classroom Video Analysis (CVA) assessment of teachers' usable mathematical teaching knowledge**: The CVA is a video-based instrument that "elicits usable teaching knowledge by asking teachers to analyze teaching episodes shown in video clips of authentic mathematics instruction." Teachers' written responses are scored by trained raters and machine learning techniques according to four rubrics: analysis of the mathematics, analysis of student thinking, suggestions for improvement, and overall depth of interpretation, each scored 0–2. The study used a short three-clip version on variables, expressions, and equations, with internal consistency α = .82 for rater-assigned rubric scores. (Ramirez Gerardo et al. (2018))
