## Description
Alcoholic Alert is a web-based computer-tailored alcohol-reduction programme for Dutch adolescents, developed and evaluated by Drost and colleagues and incorporating the Watskeburt game.

### Claims
- [The Web-based computer-tailored alcohol prevention intervention was more costly and more effective than care as usual, with ICERs of €40 and €79 from the health care perspective and €62 and €144 from the societal perspective per incremental reduction of one glass of alcohol per week and one binge drinking occasion per 30 days, respectively.](../claims/alcoholic-alert-icer-base-case.md) [+M]
- [The intervention produced a mean incremental effect of 0.73 glasses per week reduction in alcohol use and 0.49 fewer binge drinking occasions per 30 days relative to control.](../claims/alcoholic-alert-incremental-effects.md) [+M]
