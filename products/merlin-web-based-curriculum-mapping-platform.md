# MERlin web-based curriculum mapping platform

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
MERlin is a protected web-based curriculum-mapping platform used by medical faculties to map courses and objectives against the NKLM competency hierarchy and conduct structured curriculum characterization.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MERlin web-based curriculum mapping platform for mapping surgical curricula against NKLM standards**: MERlin is a web-based mapping platform used by all eight faculties as a common tool to enter curriculum data into protected data spaces. The mapper is guided by a user interface displaying the mapping structure following the NKLM hierarchy of chapters, competencies, sub-competencies and objectives, with course characterization conducted at sub-competency level via pre-defined menu options covering competency level achieved, transparency in teaching, and completeness. The study states "The method of curriculum mapping itself represents another possibility to sensitizes lecturers for objective based teaching, and effectively increased the number of taught objectives in surgery." (Rothdiener M et al. (2020))
