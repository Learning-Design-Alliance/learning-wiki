## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ECoach web-based coaching platform with Exam Playbook for large gateway courses**: ECoach is a web-based coaching platform that gives students tips about how to approach their course, personalized performance feedback, and success tools. The article states "ECoach helps students learn by prioritizing the most important tasks for each week, tracks their progress on grades and study habits, and provides tailored strategies to help them improve their grade." Student-facing features include the exam playbook, grade calculator, and 'Name that scenario'; faculty-facing features include tailored messages, texts, and emails plus classroom survey and research tools. It is used in introductory statistics, chemistry, biology, and physics, and the report presents it as technology medi (Peters et al. (2022))
- **Exam Playbook: a scalable, self-administered online version of the Strategic Resource Use intervention**: The Exam Playbook is an online app adaptation of the previously validated Strategic Resource Use intervention, delivered through ECoach to 14 large introductory STEM and Economics classes. It prompts pre-exam self-reflection: "It prompted students to anticipate the format and demands of their upcoming exam, provided a comprehensive checklist of learning resources available in the class, and asked students to select which would be useful". Students then explain each choice and plan when, where, and how to use the resources, and receive a printable summary of their responses. (Chen et al. (2022))

## Description
ECoach is a web-based coaching platform that gives students tips about how to approach their course, personalized performance feedback, and success tools. The article states "ECoach helps students learn by prioritizing the most important tasks for each week, tracks their progress on grades and study habits, and provides tailored strategies to help them improve their grade." Student-facing features include the exam playbook, grade calculator, and 'Name that scenario'; faculty-facing features include tailored messages, texts, and emails plus classroom survey and research tools. It is used in introductory statistics, chemistry, biology, and physics, and the report presents it as technology mediating support for students' metacognitive and self-regulation practices.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Faculty-facing configuration, such as assigning grade cutoffs and choosing course resources listed in the exam playbook
#### Constraints
- The exam playbook was tested as a stand-alone support before being incorporated into ECoach; a correlational study found significant positive relationships between use of specific ECoach features and final course grade, which is not causal evidence

### Target Learners
- students in high-enrollment first-year introductory courses

### Target Learning Goals
- metacognition and self-regulated learning, including strategic resource use before exams
