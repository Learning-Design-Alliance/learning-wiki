# Talent Transfer Initiative

> **Evidence** · 9 claims (9 for) · 4 studies (4 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 4 report an effect size · 9 claims rest on one study

## Description
A U.S. Department of Education–supported programme that offered high-performing teachers financial incentives to transfer into hard-to-staff schools within participating districts.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Talent Transfer Initiative (TTI) transfer-incentive program**: The Talent Transfer Initiative is a transfer-incentive strategy piloted in seven school districts from 2007 to 2014, offering $20,000 per teacher to move high-performing teachers into hard-to-staff schools. The report describes it as "a transfer-incentive strategy, known as the Talent Transfer Initiative (TTI)," which is "intended to expand disadvantaged students' access to the most effective teachers." Districts used it to fill targeted vacancies with top-performing teachers. (Steven Glazerman et al. (2012))

## Description
The Talent Transfer Initiative was a teacher transfer incentive program evaluated between 2007 and 2014 for the U.S. Department of Education's Institute of Education Sciences and National Center for Education Evaluation. It offered high-performing teachers $20,000 to transfer into the hardest-to-staff schools in 10 school districts, and the evaluated article examines teachers' "behavioral responses" to this offer.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Offers a financial transfer incentive ($20,000) to teachers identified as high-performing
- Targets the hardest-to-staff schools within participating districts
#### Constraints
- Evaluated in 10 school districts across the country; scope limited to those settings

### Target Learners
- Students in the hardest-to-staff schools in the 10 participating districts

### Target Learning Goals
- Staffing low-performing schools with high-performing teachers
