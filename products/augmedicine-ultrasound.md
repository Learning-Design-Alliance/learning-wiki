## Description
An augmented-reality cardiac point-of-care ultrasound training application developed for Microsoft HoloLens 2, used with a mobile-phone probe and 3D-printed thorax phantom to support tutorial, practice, and evaluation for novice medical learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Augmedicine: Ultrasound XR application for cardiac POCUS training**: The 'Augmedicine: Ultrasound' application is an AR-based training tool developed for the Microsoft HoloLens 2 that "displays a 3D model of a beating human heart within a male human 3D model" with a mobile phone functioning as an ultrasound probe. It includes a life-size 3D-printed thorax phantom for haptic feedback and consists of three sequential phases: Tutorial, Training, and Evaluation. Face and content validity was established through stakeholder evaluations. (Mulder Tessa A. et al. (2026))
