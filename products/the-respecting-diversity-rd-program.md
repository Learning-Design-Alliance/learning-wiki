# The Respecting Diversity (RD) program

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A nine-lesson social-emotional learning curriculum developed by Katz et al. for elementary students that uses a multiple-intelligences framework to promote self-awareness, respect, and a positive classroom climate.

### Claims

- [Classroom climate significantly improved for treatment classrooms and decreased in control classrooms according to both teachers and students.](../claims/rd-program-improved-classroom-climate.md) [+M]
- [The Respecting Diversity program significantly increased students' self-respect, awareness of others, and respect for others, while control students decreased in these factors.](../claims/rd-program-increased-self-respect-awareness-of-others-and-respect-for-others.md) [+M]
- [Students in the treatment group decreased in self-awareness while control students increased, a finding the authors attribute to the self-consciousness measure used.](../claims/rd-program-self-awareness-decreased-in-treatment-group.md) [-M]
- [Treatment students significantly increased in prosocial behaviors and social responsibility, and were less excluded by peers, while control students decreased in these behaviors.](../claims/rd-program-improved-prosocial-behavior-and-social-responsibility.md) [+M]
