# Bahrain Defence Force Instrument for professionalism, communication, and collaboration (BDF/PCC)

## Description
A 39-item, five-point multisource feedback questionnaire developed by Strachan et al. to assess medical graduates' professionalism, communication, and collaboration through ratings from colleagues and coworkers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **BDF/PCC instrument: a 39-item multisource feedback questionnaire for professionalism, communication, and collaboration**: The Bahrain Defence Force Instrument for professionalism, communication, and collaboration (BDF/PCC) is a multisource feedback questionnaire built from prior instruments including PAR, MAAS-Global, the Calgary-Cambridge tool, SPRAT, AITCS, and specialist opinions. "It included 39 items, 15 of which measured professionalism, 13 of which to measured communication skills, and 11 of which measured collaboration." It uses a 5-point response scale from "among the worst" to "among the best", with an "unable to assess" option. (Strachan Kathryn et al. (2017))
