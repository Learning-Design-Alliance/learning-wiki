# Technical Assistance Reporting System (TARS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
NTTAC's Technical Assistance Reporting System is a database of provider-entered technical-assistance contact records, including recipients, dates, duration, format, topics, summaries, and next steps, used to monitor delivery and dosage.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Technical Assistance Reporting System (TARS) log database of TA contacts**: TARS is NTTAC's activity-logging system, "in operation since July 2014," which tracks "details about each TA contact, such as the recipient, provider, date, length, format of the contact, and the topics covered," plus a narrative summary and next steps. The evaluation downloaded all TARS contact entries through March 31, 2018, totaling 3926 entries across both cohorts, and recoded the 143 possible topics into 19 overarching themes for analysis. (Olson Jonathan R. et al. (2020))
