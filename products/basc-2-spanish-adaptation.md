## Description
A Spanish adaptation of the Behavior Assessment System for Children, a multidimensional self-report assessment of psychological adjustment and maladjustment in children.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **BASC-2 Spanish adaptation as outcome measure of psychological adjustment**: The Behavior Assessment System for Children, Second Edition (BASC-2), Spanish adaptation (González et al., 2004), is a "multidimensional test that measures several aspects of behavior and personality, including dimensions of psychological adjustment (adaptive) as well as maladjustment (clinical)". It yields four global dimensions and 12 subdimensions via self-report. In this study Cronbach's alpha was "greater than .80 for all the items analyzed". (Castillo-Gualda et al. (2023))
