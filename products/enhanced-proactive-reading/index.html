Enhanced Proactive Reading¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A 120-lesson small-group early literacy, language arts, and English language development curriculum for first-grade English language learners experiencing reading difficulties, developed for delivery in daily sessions by teachers.

Components¶

  • Enhanced Proactive Reading curriculum: 120 daily small-group lessons across five content strands: Enhanced Proactive Reading is a comprehensive, integrated reading, language arts, and English language development curriculum targeted to first-grade English language learners experiencing difficulty with conventional reading instruction. It "provides 120 daily lessons, each composed of 6–10 short activities, to be delivered throughout the school year", delivered to small groups of three to five students in approximately 50-minute sessions. Lessons focus on five content strands: phonemic awareness, letter knowledge, word recognition, connected text fluency, and comprehension strategies. Teachers model new content, use visual aids and gestures to clarify vocabulary, and monitor student respon (Enhanced Proactive Reading (2006))

Claims¶

  • In the Vaughn, Cirino, et al. (2006) trial, no reading measure reached statistical significance, but five of seven effect sizes were substantively important [+W]
  • Enhanced Proactive Reading shows no discernible effects on English language development [-W]
  • Enhanced Proactive Reading has potentially positive effects on reading achievement for first-grade English language learners at risk for reading problems [+W]
  • In the Vaughn, Mathes, et al. (2006) trial, Enhanced Proactive Reading students significantly outperformed comparison students on Word Attack and Passage Comprehension [+W]
