# ASRA-ESRA UGRA competency framework

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A regional anesthesia competency framework developed by the ASRA-ESRA joint committee, defining six UGRA training domains and milestone-based assessment expectations for anesthesiology residents and fellows.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ASRA-ESRA six-domain UGRA competency framework with milestone-based assessment tools**: The article describes the ASRA-ESRA joint committee's competency-based framework for UGRA training, in which core competencies "are encompassed into six domains: patient care, medical knowledge, system-based practice, practice-based learning and improvement, interpersonal and communication skills, and professionalism". Assessment tools include a validated multiple-choice sonoanatomy exam, UGRA-specific checklists and global rating scales, and the ACGME/American Board of Anesthesiology Milestone Project with five proficiency levels measured primarily by the level of supervision required. (Kim T. Edward (2019))
