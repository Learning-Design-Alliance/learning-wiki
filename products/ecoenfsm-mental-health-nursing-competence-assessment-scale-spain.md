## Description
A validated scale developed by Rivera Vicente for assessing mental health nursing competencies among residents in Spain's Specialized Health Training Program.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ECOEnfSM — Mental Health Nursing Competence Assessment Scale (Spain)**: The ECOEnfSM scale (Assessment competences of Mental Health Nurses in Specialized Health Training Programs, Rivera, 2021) is a validated tool developed to assess the competences of mental health nurses during their residency training program in Spain. The review reports that Rivera Vicente (2021), a reliability and validity study with 40 MHNs, "Validated the scale ECOEnFSM. to assess mental health nursing competences in Spain", covering assessment, nursing diagnosis, intervention, evaluation, research, clinical management, teaching and interpersonal relationships. The review lists it among notable tools targeting general and crosscutting competences. (Calvo Javier Sanz et al. (2026))
