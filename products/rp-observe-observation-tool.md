## Description
RP-Observe is a Rutgers University-developed school observation tool for assessing restorative-justice implementation and outcomes.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **RP-Observe observation tool for measuring restorative justice in schools**: RP-Observe is an observation tool that Rutgers University developed to measure RJ in schools, cited by experts among the very few instruments designed to measure RJ. The article reports that experts often created their own instruments, including fidelity instruments specific to the RJ model being implemented, and that some approach measurement through changes in school climate and relationships among students and staff. Interviewees agreed a resource should compile and disseminate existing evaluation tools and findings. (Hurley et al. (2015))
