Balanced 192-text evaluation dataset of EFL, professional, AI-generated, and hybrid texts¶

Description¶

A research dataset created by Mohammad Hadra et al. containing 192 balanced EFL, professional, AI-generated, and hybrid texts for evaluating authorship-detection and academic-integrity decisions.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Originality outperformed Turnitin in overall accuracy and macro-average recall on a balanced EFL-relevant dataset, but both showed limited consistency across classes [+W]
  • Both detectors performed poorly on hybrid human–AI texts, with Originality showing near-zero recall for the Hybrid class [+W]
  • Both detectors achieved substantially lower accuracy on scientific writing than on humanities texts [+W]
  • Detector accuracy declined significantly as text length increased, with both detectors performing best on short texts [+W]
  • Originality showed a borderline non-significant trend toward higher accuracy on professional than EFL student writing, while Turnitin showed no significant difference [+W]
  • A detector can achieve high aggregate accuracy while producing materially different over-detection rates across author groups [~W]
