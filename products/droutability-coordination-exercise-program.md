## Description
A school-based physical-activity programme developed by Tatsuo Yasumitsu that uses cross plates and colored, numbered disks to train elementary students' agility, orientation, and reaction speed.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Droutability coordination exercise program with cross plates and colored numbered disks**: The Droutability coordination exercise program, developed by Yasumitsu (2007), uses cross plates (24 cm × 24 cm) and colored disks 15 cm in diameter placed on half of a gymnasium floor. Students in two subgroups alternate 30-second activity bouts: they step quickly over a cross plate, then respond to a command such as a color or number by running to the matching disk, touching it with a foot, and returning to resume stepping. The experimental group completed "four sets of 30-second intervention activity in each intervention recess". (Tatsuo Yasumitsu et al. (2012))
