# Social, Academic, and Emotional Behavior Risk Screener (SAEBRS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
SAEBRS is a universal screening assessment for students’ social, academic, and emotional behavior risk, used by schools and districts to identify needs and guide mental-health supports.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SAEBRS multi-informant universal mental health screening in DNUSD**: The Social, Academic, and Emotional Behavior Risk Screener (SAEBRS) is a universal screening tool that DNUSD used with a "multi-informant approach," having "teachers and students complete" it, to monitor and respond to students' mental health needs. The brief reports that from fall 2020 through December 2023 the district conducted "nearly 11,000 student screenings," supported by Project Cal-Well's effort to build a culture of data-based decision-making about mental health supports and interventions. (WestEd (2024))
