# E-TRIALS eight data files

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A collection of eight ASSISTments E-TRIALS data files containing student interaction logs, problem and assignment records, prior and post-performance measures, assignment settings, and redo activity, released to researchers after preregistration and eligibility requirements.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Eight E-TRIALS data files released to researchers after OSF pre-registration**: After a study is deployed and the researcher pre-registers it at OSF.io, E-TRIALS releases eight data files covering student interactions, outcomes, and conditions. The article states that "researchers receive eight distinct data files, each containing specific insights into student interactions, learning outcomes, and experimental conditions." The files include action logs (click stream), problem logs with discrete and continuous scores, assignment logs, prior and same-skill prior/post logs, assignment settings, and redo logs. (Siedahmed et al. (2025))
