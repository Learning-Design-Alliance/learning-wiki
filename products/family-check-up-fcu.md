## Description
Family Check-Up is an ecological, school-based, family-centered intervention developed and delivered by trained providers using assessment, feedback, motivational interviewing, and tiered support to address family, behavioral, and academic needs.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Family Check-Up (FCU) adapted as a school-based, family-centered intervention for kindergarten**: The Family Check-Up is described as "an ecological, school-based, family-centered intervention" that "includes an initial interview and ecological assessment, followed by a feedback session", with motivational interviewing embedded throughout to promote motivation to change. The kindergarten adaptation delivered a home visit with videotaped assessment and a feedback session in the home, integrated reading and math assessments, teacher reports, and parent–child observations, and followed a tiered service delivery model offering universal, selected, and indicated support tailored to family needs and risk. (Garbacz et al. (2019))
