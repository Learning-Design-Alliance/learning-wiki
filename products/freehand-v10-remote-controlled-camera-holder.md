# FreeHand v1.0 remote-controlled camera holder

## Description
A robotic laparoscope-holding and joypad-control system developed for use in laparoscopic skills training, in which a human operator moves the camera under the trainee's direct instructions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **FreeHand v1.0 remote-controlled camera holder for laparoscopic skills training**: The FreeHand v1.0 is a robotic arm specifically designed for holding laparoscopes, capable of movement in 3 planes, controlled with a joypad by a human operator and moved only under the direct instructions of the operating participant. In this trial it served as the camera-holding intervention for novices training on the E-BLUS curriculum, compared against a human camera assistant. The article states it "requires minimal practice to gain competency" and that it eliminates tremor, camera rotation, and muscular fatigue. (Amin Mohammad S. A. et al. (2020))
