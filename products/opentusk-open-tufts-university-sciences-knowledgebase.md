# OpenTUSK (Open Tufts University Sciences Knowledgebase)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
OpenTUSK is a virtual-patient authoring and delivery system developed for creating interactive clinical cases with questions, free-text responses, multimedia, and preformulated feedback.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **OpenTUSK virtual patient system as prototyping tool**: OpenTUSK (Open Tufts University Sciences Knowledgebase) is the virtual patient system the authors chose, after consensus in a reference group of 10 experienced primary care teachers, as the prototyping tool for the model. The article notes: "This tool allows open-ended questions and free-text answers that may promote students’ own reflections" and also allows video clips and preformulated teacher comments. The case structure was visualized in VUE and written in a text document before being entered into OpenTUSK. (Helena Salminen et al. (2014))
