# Reperfused Human Cadaver Colonoscopy Simulator (RHCCS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A named colonoscopy training simulator developed by Rohr et al. that combines a prepared human cadaver colon with pulsatile reperfusion and bowel-cleansing procedures for procedural skills training.
