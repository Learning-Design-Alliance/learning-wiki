## Description
New Leaders runs a selective principal-residency and ongoing support programme that prepares and develops school leaders, particularly for high-need schools.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **New Leaders principal training program**: New Leaders is a principal preparation program founded in 2000 that has trained more than 1000 school leaders serving high-need schools. The article describes that "New Leaders supports aspiring principals through a structured residency program and ongoing training and support after they assume leadership positions in schools." Its selection process is described as "highly competitive, with substantial outreach to identify high quality applicants." Its mission is "to ensure high achievement for all children, especially children of color growing up in poverty." This report evaluates its impacts in Oakland Unified School District. (Kevin Booker (2014))
