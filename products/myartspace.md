## Description
Myartspace is a mobile phone and web service developed to support museum-based inquiry learning by enabling students to collect, transmit, and synthesise multimedia evidence across museum and classroom settings.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Myartspace mobile service for inquiry-led learning across museum and classroom**: Myartspace is a service using mobile phones and a website to support inquiry learning between classrooms and museums. Students collect photos, audio recordings, text comments, and objects via two-letter exhibit codes during a museum visit; content is automatically sent by GPRS to personal online stores. In post-visit lessons students create personal galleries synthesising collected evidence to answer inquiry questions set in a pre-visit lesson. (Vavoula et al. (2009))
