## Description
The TMPT Program is a preschool thinking-training programme that develops perspective-taking and social problem-solving through a five-dimension progression and teacher-led classroom activities.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Five-dimension perspective-taking progression from self to universal perspectives**: The TMPT Program organizes Thinking Training as a progression through five perspectives: the self ('I' point of view), the other (empathic perspective), a third person or object, the group (pluralist perspective), and finally universal perspectives evaluating conditions from five or more viewpoints. Each dimension is discussed for one month with 20 classroom activities built on ordinary materials such as stories, films, paintings, and discussion questions. The design assumes perspective-taking supports social problem-solving; the article states the aim is for children "to learn how to think, not what to think." (Gur et al. (2018))
