# Automated Extended Essay Grading Software (AEEGS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A web-based application developed by Olaoye DD et al. that uses contextual similarity to score senior-secondary Economics extended essay responses against examiner-supplied questions and marking schemes.

### Claims

- [AEEGS scores show high absolute agreement with 12 human expert raters' scores on mathematical Economics essay items (average-measures ICC = 0.863)](../claims/aeegs-high-agreement-human-raters-icc-0863.md) [+M]
- [The AEEGS software demonstrated internal consistency of Cronbach alpha 0.86 in trial testing and was validated for face and content validity by three software experts](../claims/aeegs-reliability-cronbach-086.md) [+M]
- [Each of the 12 human raters' scores correlates significantly with AEEGS scores, with coefficients ranging from 0.604 to 0.864](../claims/aeegs-pearson-correlations-range-0604-0864.md) [+W]
- [AEEGS and human raters produced similarly shaped score distributions, with most students' scores clustered around the mean in the 'good' band](../claims/aeegs-human-score-distributions-similar.md) [+W]
