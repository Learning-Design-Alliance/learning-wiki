# PlayINAF repository and AMACA documentation templates

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
AMACA's PlayINAF repository and associated templates provide reproducible activity documentation, workshop-accessibility guidance, and feedback/debriefing forms for science communicators, teachers, and other facilitators.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PlayINAF repository and AMACA documentation templates (Feedback Wheel, ICEBAGS, descriptive activity documents)**: AMACA releases all its activities as documented, reproducible resources. Each finalized activity is described in a template document with sections for title, brief description, needed material, preparation, development, and the underlying physical processes. "It is also uploaded on the INAF repository PlayINAF (play.inaf.it) to allow anyone to recreate the activity." Supporting tools include the Feedback Wheel, a debrief form collecting attendees' feelings, perceptions and criticisms, and ICEBAGS, a six-section template (Introduction, Conclusion, Explanations and voice, Body language, Accessibility, Group dynamics, Space arrangement) of best practices for running an accessible workshop, shared with HS students during training. (Rachele Toniolo et al. (2026))
