## Description
A California Commission on Teacher Credentialing resource and live-counselor initiative that helps prospective teachers navigate preparation pathways and access financial-aid information.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Roadmap to Teaching website and live career counselors for prospective California teachers**: The Roadmap to Teaching is a resource launched by the California Commission on Teacher Credentialing in August 2023 as part of a complete overhaul of resources for prospective teachers, resulting from the Collaborative's affordability communications strategy. It is accompanied by live career counselors who support prospective teachers in navigating the steps of becoming a teacher. As of March 2025, the report states the Roadmap "had received 123,376 hits and its live career counselors had served nearly 5,000 people," and practitioner testimonials describe it as helping candidates access financial aid information and choose residency pathways. (White et al. (2025))
