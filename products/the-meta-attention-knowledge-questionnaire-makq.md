# The Meta-Attention Knowledge Questionnaire (MAKQ)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
A self-report and open-ended questionnaire developed by Mulvihill et al. to measure early adolescents' self-perceived attentional control and declarative attention-regulation strategy knowledge.

### Claims

- [The MAKQ dimensions show acceptable internal consistency across both studies (alphas 0.77-0.93)](../claims/makq-reliability-alphas.md) [+W]
- [MAKQ dimensions correlate moderately with each other, with strategy knowledge only weakly related to the self-perception scales](../claims/makq-intercorrelations-self-strategy.md) [+W]
- [The MAKQ self-knowledge items show a stable two-factor structure: meta-attention control (15 items) and meta-attention monitoring (5 items)](../claims/makq-two-factor-self-knowledge-structure.md) [+W]
- [MAKQ strategy knowledge, but not the self-perception scales, converges with behavioural inhibitory control on the Go-No-Go task](../claims/makq-strategy-knowledge-inhibitory-control.md) [+W]
- [MAKQ dimensions show convergent validity with parent-rated attention and executive functioning (moderate-to-strong negative correlations)](../claims/makq-convergent-validity-parent-measures.md) [+W]
- [Cluster analysis across both studies identifies three meta-attention knowledge profiles, including an overconfident group with a self-knowledge/strategy mismatch](../claims/makq-three-meta-attention-profiles.md) [+W]
