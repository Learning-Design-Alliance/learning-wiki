## Description
The MRBQ is a 58-item, six-point Likert-scale questionnaire developed by Op ’t Eynde and colleagues to measure students’ beliefs about mathematics, themselves as mathematics learners, and their classroom social context.

As printed, it is "the experimental version of the Mathematics-Related Beliefs Questionnaire (MRBQ) containing 58 items that are scored on a 6 point Likert-scale, from 0 (I completely disagree) to 5 (I totally agree)." ## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Adjustment of scales in accordance with the components found, leaving out items that load on more than one factor or have a loading of less than .40
#### Constraints
- Beliefs about the social context were limited to one component, students' beliefs about their teacher's cognitive, motivational and affective behavior

### Target Learners
- junior high school students (age 14) in Flemish secondary education

### Target Learning Goals
- measuring students' mathematics-related belief systems
