## Description
The CPC Expansion Program is a school-based programme run by the Child-Parent Center model that provides continuous early education and family-support services from preschool through third grade for children in high-poverty communities.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CPC Expansion Program elements: collaborative leadership team, effective learning experiences, menu-based parent involvement, continuity and stability, aligned curriculum, and professional development system**: The CPC Expansion Program provides continuous education and family support services from Preschool to Third Grade to schools serving large percentages of low-income children. The program has the following elements: (1) collaborative leadership team of Head Teacher, Parent Resource Teacher, and School-Community Representative under the supervision of the Principal; (2) effective learning experiences (e.g., small classes, intensive learning opportunities); (3) menu-based system of parent involvement and engagement; (4) continuity and stability; (5) aligned curriculum; and (6) professional development system of coaching and on-line modules. (Erika Gaylor et al. (2015))
- **School-family-university collaboration model based on shared ownership, committed resources, and progress monitoring for improvement**: The article frames CPC-P3 as a school reform model grounded in a collaboration model with three major principles. In "shared ownership", partners have equal responsibility to plan, implement, manage, and improve the program rather than adopting an externally developed model unmodified. In "committed resources", each partner makes key investments of "time, financial capital, and physical space". "Progress monitoring for improvement" is described as ongoing formative evaluation essential for continuous improvement, using implementation-fidelity data shared among partners. (Reynolds Arthur J. et al. (2017))
- **CPC-P3 six core elements of the Midwest Child-Parent Center Expansion program**: Each CPC-P3 site implements a set of six core elements following program guidelines in the manual: a collaborative leadership team run by the Head Teacher with the principal; effective learning experiences through small classes and full-day preschool and kindergarten (classes limited to 17 in preschool and 25 in K-3 with assistants); an aligned curriculum updated annually; parent involvement and engagement via menu-based services; a professional development system with online modules and on-site follow-up; and continuity and stability of services from preschool to third grade. (Reynolds Arthur J. et al. (2017))
