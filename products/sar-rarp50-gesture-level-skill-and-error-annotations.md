# SAR-RARP50 gesture-level skill and error annotations

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A validated dataset annotation layer containing blinded gesture-level skill and error labels for robotic suturing videos, produced for training and evaluating automated surgical technical-skill assessment models.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SAR-RARP50 gesture-level skill and error annotations as ground truth for surgical AI evaluation**: The article produced a validated annotation layer on the open SAR-RARP50 dataset: 54 RARP videos (266 min) from 8 surgeons, blindly and independently annotated at gesture level with OSATS, M-GEARS and OCHRA, yielding 2507 annotated errors. The authors state this "benchmark evaluation of AI models sets a foundation and promising approach for future advancements in automated technical skill assessment." It serves as ground-truth data for training and testing vision-based deep learning models for skill and error prediction. (Sirajudeen N. et al. (2024))
