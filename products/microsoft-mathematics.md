## Description
Microsoft Mathematics is free mathematical software from Microsoft Corporation for entering and manipulating expressions, symbolic calculation, graphing, visualization, step-by-step problem solving, exploration, and answer checking.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Microsoft Mathematics software as a Calculus learning tool**: Microsoft Mathematics is free software made by Microsoft Corporation with a symbolic computing system that works based on mathematical expressions. The article describes that writing, calculating, and manipulating of mathematical expressions and graphical visualization of 2D, 3D and animation can be carried out with simple instructions, and that solving problems features step solutions as obtained when done manually. In the study it was used to support visualization, calculation, graphing, exploration, and answer validation in first-year Calculus lectures. (Rina Oktaviyanthi (2015))
