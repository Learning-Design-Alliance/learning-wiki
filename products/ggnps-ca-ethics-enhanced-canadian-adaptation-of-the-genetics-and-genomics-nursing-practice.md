# GGNPS-CA: ethics-enhanced Canadian Adaptation of the Genetics and Genomics Nursing Practice Survey

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A modified Genetics and Genomics Nursing Practice Survey developed to assess nurses' genomic knowledge, competence, confidence, attitudes, adoption-related factors, and ethical awareness.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **GGNPS-CA: ethics-enhanced Canadian Adaptation of the Genetics and Genomics Nursing Practice Survey**: The GGNPS-CA is a modified version of the Genetics and Genomics Nursing Practice Survey that "retains the original framework while incorporating an important new dimension: ethics". It covers attitudes, receptivity, confidence, genomic knowledge, social system, and adoption domains grounded in Rogers' Diffusion of Innovation theory, adding three direct and four indirect ethical questions and a 12-item Knowledge Score. This study translated it into Finnish, replaced one knowledge question, and administered it via REDCap; the Finnish version was back-translated and approved with the developers. (Laaksonen M et al. (2025))
