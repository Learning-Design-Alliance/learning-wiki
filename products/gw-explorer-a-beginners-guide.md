# GW Explorer: A Beginner's Guide

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A three-notebook Python and Jupyter curriculum for introducing high-school and early-undergraduate learners to gravitational-wave astrophysics, computational literacy, waveform simulation, and simplified Bayesian inference, created by Rachel Langgin and collaborators.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **GW Explorer: A Beginner's Guide — three-notebook computational curriculum with released GitHub materials**: GW Explorer is a curriculum of Python Jupyter notebooks that "weave together narrative explanations, interactive visualizations, guided questions, and executable code". Notebook 1 covers GW foundations with an embedded Python tutorial; Notebook 2 covers binary merger waveform simulation; Notebook 3 introduces "simplified Bayesian inference concepts". Materials are publicly released at https://github.com/rlanggin/GW_Explorer_A_Beginners_Guide and mirror at rachellanggin.com/tutorials. (Rachel Langgin et al. (2026))

### Claims

- [High school participants' coding confidence rose with the curriculum, with 67% reporting intermediate-level confidence after Notebooks 2 and 3 versus 33% beginner-level confidence after Notebook 1](../claims/gw-explorer-coding-confidence-gains-high-school.md) [+W]
- [An undergraduate self-guided pilot showed 94% correctly identified gravitational waves as spacetime perturbations and 83% reported increased coding confidence after completing the notebooks](../claims/gw-explorer-undergraduate-pilot-outcomes.md) [+W]
- [After an after-school GW Explorer workshop, 89% of post-survey high school respondents correctly identified gravitational waves as spacetime perturbations and 78% reported increased interest in astrophysics](../claims/gw-explorer-high-school-post-survey-gains.md) [+W]
- [Students in both pilots singled out interactive real-time waveform plotting and parameter exploration as the components most responsible for building understanding and computational comfort](../claims/gw-explorer-visualization-driven-learning.md) [+W]
