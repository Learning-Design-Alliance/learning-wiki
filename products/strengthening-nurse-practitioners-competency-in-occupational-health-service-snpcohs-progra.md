# Strengthening Nurse Practitioners’ Competency in Occupational Health Service (SNPCOHS) program

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A four-week, 10-hour blended e-learning programme developed for nurse practitioners in primary care units, combining ten occupational-health modules with videos, quizzes, case assignments, online discussion, peer sharing, and expert feedback.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SNPCOHS program: a 4-week, 10-hour e-learning intervention with 10 modules for NPs in primary care units**: The Strengthening Nurse Practitioners’ Competency in Occupational Health Service program is a 4-week, 10-hour blended e-learning intervention comprising "10 modules covering key topics such as pesticide fundamentals, occupational health laws, screening and referral, primary care, risk communication, and the role of nurses in occupational health services." Activities include self-paced e-learning with video clips, post-lesson quizzes, case-based assignments, and online discussions with peer-sharing and expert feedback. It was developed from a literature review, grounded in Bandura's self-efficacy theory, and pilot tested with 5 NPs in Phayao Province. (Napamon P et al. (2025))
