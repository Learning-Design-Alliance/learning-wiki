## Description
A validated 10-item assessor-rated scale adapted from Blackburn’s revised Cognitive Rating Scale to evaluate the therapeutic competence of newly trained professionals delivering cognitive-behavioural psychological support.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Cognitive First Aid Rating Scale (CFARs) for therapeutic competence of newly trained CBT-naive professionals**: The CFARs is a validated 10-item scale (Cronbach's Alpha 0.93), adapted from Blackburn's revised Cognitive Rating Scale, for rating the skills of newly trained "CBT-naïve" healthcare professionals delivering psychological support. Each item is scored zero (poor skills) to six (high skill); "A total score of >30 and three or above on any item is considered to reflect therapeutic competence." Items cover focus/structure, pacing, chunking/feedback, integration of CBT into the professional model, collaborative relationship, guided discovery, interpersonal effectiveness, eliciting model components, change techniques and closure. (Wileman V. et al. (2025))
