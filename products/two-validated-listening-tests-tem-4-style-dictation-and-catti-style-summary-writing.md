# Two validated listening tests: TEM-4-style dictation and CATTI-style summary writing

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A pair of researcher-designed listening assessments for undergraduate English and Translation and Interpreting majors, comprising a repeated-play dictation test for intensive listening and a single-play summary-writing test for selective listening.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Two validated listening tests: TEM-4-style dictation and CATTI-style summary writing**: The study designed two tests to assess L2 listening competence: a 162-word dictation passage about flash-mob played three times with 15 pause segments (following TEM-4 design), and a 2-minute-52-second mini-lecture about kite-surfing safety rules followed by a summary under 100 words played once (following CATTI design). Piloted with 25 students from a third class, "both tests were able to separate the highest 8 performers (i.e. top 33.33%) from the lowest 8 performers (for dictation, t=4.76, p.<0.001; for summary, t=7.49, p.<0.001)". Scoring by two independent assistants showed high inter-rater reliability (dictation r=0.952; summary r=0.967, both p<0.001). (Tongtong Zhang et al. (2017))
