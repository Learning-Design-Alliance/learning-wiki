# Newtonian Mechanics Quiz (NMQ) instrument

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 38-item multimodal online assessment instrument developed by Ashutosh Kumar Pathak et al. for measuring undergraduate students' conceptual understanding of Newtonian mechanics through multiple-choice, free-text, and law-selection items.
