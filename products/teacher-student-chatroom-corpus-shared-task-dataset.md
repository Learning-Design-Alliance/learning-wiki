# Teacher-Student Chatroom Corpus shared-task dataset

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A dataset released by Tack et al. from the Teacher-Student Chatroom Corpus, containing 7,047 ESL teacher–student chat contexts paired with reference teacher responses for dialogue-generation and evaluation research.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Teacher-Student Chatroom Corpus shared-task dataset of 7,047 extracted teacher-response samples with conflict-free train/test splitting**: The shared task's data resource, drawn from the Teacher-Student Chatroom Corpus (TSCC) of ESL teacher-student chatroom interactions. Samples consist of a dialogue context plus the teacher's reference response, capped at 100 tokens for licensing reasons. Extraction yielded "7, 047 data samples", which were split with a Hungarian-algorithm selection procedure to prevent test references appearing in training contexts, producing 3,052 training samples (2,747 training, 305 held-out) and 273 test samples. (Anaïs Tack et al. (2023))
