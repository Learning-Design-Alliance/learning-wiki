# Four-element framework for restorative practices implementation: adaptive, relational, structural, and technical

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The brief organizes restorative practices implementation around four interacting element sets. Adaptive elements are 'mindsets, paradigms, values, and belief systems, which lay the groundwork for any technical elements'; relational elements are intentional actions that build relationships and social capital; structural supports are infrastructure such as 'funding, people, time, discipline and other policies, and equity'; and technical elements are the concrete practices and toolkits. The guide treats the first three as precursors that make technical toolkits succeed.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Leaders and educators must attend to adaptive, relational, and structural elements alongside the technical elements for authentic, sustainable implementation
#### Constraints
- The brief states that aligning the elements is 'a journey, not a destination' and that results take years

### Target Learners
- K-12 students and school communities

### Learning Goals
- School climate and community transformation
- equitable whole-person outcomes
