## Description
C3-IoC is an AI-based online career-guidance system developed by Adán José-García et al. that profiles learners' technical and non-technical skills and matches them to IT job roles.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **C3-IoC career guidance system (three-module architecture)**: C3-IoC is an online AI-based career guidance system helping undergraduates and high school students explore IT job roles. As the article states, "The system consists of three modules: (1) a novel knowledge base covering the full range of skills, both technical and non-technical...; (2) a user skill profiling module in which students' technical skills are collected using a CV parser and non-technical skills are self-assessed through a questionnaire; and (3) a job role matching and visualisation module". It supports self-evaluation of skills and exploration of emerging IT jobs via a personalised job network visualisation. (Adán José-García et al. (2023))

### Claims
- [C3-IoC job role matching accuracy increases monotonically with the number of questionnaire questions answered, reaching about 80% accuracy with four questions and nearly 100% with 24](../claims/c3-ioc-matching-accuracy-increases-with-questions.md) [+M]
- [User trial participants rated the C3-IoC system positively for usability, with the Job roles map and Job roles overview the highest-rated components](../claims/c3-ioc-user-trial-usability-positive.md) [+M]
