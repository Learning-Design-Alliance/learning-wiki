## Description
Pearl AI's Second Opinion® is dental radiographic-analysis software that uses artificial intelligence to detect and visually annotate suspected abnormalities such as proximal carious lesions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Second Opinion® AI software for color-coded proximal caries visualization on bitewing radiographs**: Second Opinion® (Pearl AI) is an AI-based dental radiographic analysis software that analyzes radiographic images and provides visual annotations highlighting areas suspected of containing carious lesions. During training, students were presented with radiographs with AI-generated annotations including color-coded visual indicators designed to assist in identifying and localizing suspected proximal carious lesions. (Alkattan Rana et al. (2026))
