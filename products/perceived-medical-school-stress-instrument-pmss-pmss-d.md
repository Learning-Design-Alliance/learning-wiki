## Description
The PMSS is a 13-item Likert self-report questionnaire developed by Vitaliano et al. and adapted into German and other languages to measure perceived stressors among medical students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Perceived Medical School Stress Instrument (PMSS / PMSS-D), a 13-item questionnaire covering workload, competition, social isolation and financial worries**: The PMSS is the most widespread validated stress questionnaire designed for use in Medical School, introduced by Vitaliano et al. and adapted into German (PMSS-D) and other languages. It "addresses a wide range of possible stressors, including workload, competition, social isolation and financial worries"; each of the 13 items is answered on a five-point Likert scale and summed (range 13–65). The study used the German 13-item version at two time points. (Kötter T et al. (2017))
