# ILDE+ (Integrated Learning Design Environment Plus)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
ILDE+ is a customized Integrated Learning Design Environment platform that supports online communities of educators in creating, sharing, discussing, reviewing, and co-designing learning designs.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ILDE+ platform for online communities of pre-service teachers sharing learning designs**: ILDE+ is a customized version of the Integrated Learning Design Environment supporting an online community of pre-service teachers who explore, share, discuss, and co-create learning designs. It implements six main functionalities including an LD creation template with metadata, commenting, design duplication preserving authorship, co-design via shared authorship, and social features such as "a like button for the designs, counters of visualizations and comments, or follow button for the members of the community." The platform logs user actions for analytics. (Gutiérrez-Páez et al. (2023))
