# Spanish-language Spirituality and Spiritual Care Rating Scale (SSCRS-Sp)

## Description
A validated Spanish-language 17-item Likert self-report scale developed for nursing students to measure perceptions across spirituality, spiritual care, religiosity, and personalized care.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SSCRS-Sp: Spanish-language Spirituality and Spiritual Care Rating Scale for nursing students**: The SSCRS-Sp is the validated Spanish version for nursing students of McSherry et al.'s Spirituality and Spiritual Care Rating Scale, "consists of 17 items with a 5-point Likert scale response (0 = strongly disagree; 5 = strongly agree), grouped into four dimensions: Spirituality, Spiritual Care, Religiosity, and Personalized Care." It reports Cronbach's alpha = 0.72 in this sample and served as the pre/post outcome measure. It was developed from a literature review and data from 549 clinical nurses in England. (Domènech-Sorolla et al. (2025))
