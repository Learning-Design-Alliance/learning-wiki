# Spanish adaptation of the 13-item Student Satisfaction and Self-Confidence in Learning Scale (SCLS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
A Spanish-language adaptation of the National League for Nursing's 13-item Student Satisfaction and Self-Confidence in Learning Scale, validated by Farrés-Tarafa et al. for assessing nursing students' perceptions after clinical simulation.

### Claims

- [The Spanish SCLS shows high internal consistency, with Cronbach's alpha of .885 for the total questionnaire and above .81 for both dimensions](../claims/scls-spanish-high-internal-consistency.md) [+M]
- [Confirmatory factor analysis supports the two-dimensional structure of the Spanish SCLS with good fit indices](../claims/scls-spanish-cfa-two-dimensional-fit.md) [+M]
- [The Spanish SCLS is essentially unidimensional as well as bidimensional: ECV .864 and UniCo .965 indicate a strong dominant general factor](../claims/scls-spanish-essentially-unidimensional.md) [+M]
- [Nursing students rated satisfaction and self-confidence above 4 on the 5-point scale, with satisfaction (4.2) slightly higher than self-confidence (4.1)](../claims/scls-spanish-high-satisfaction-selfconfidence-scores.md) [+W]
- [The authors conclude that clinical simulation helps nursing students increase their confidence and satisfaction for facing real clinical scenarios](../claims/simulation-increases-confidence-satisfaction-authors-conclusion.md) [+W]
- [Total SCLS scores differed significantly by teaching shift and academic year, with morning-shift and second-year students scoring higher](../claims/scls-scores-shift-and-academic-year-differences.md) [+W]
- [No statistically significant differences in total SCLS score were found for age, sex, employment status, contract type, work shift, work experience, or healthcare experience](../claims/scls-scores-no-other-demographic-differences.md) [+W]
