# SSE scale

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A multidimensional self-report questionnaire measuring health-discipline students' satisfaction with simulation through debriefing and reflection, clinical reasoning, and clinical learning components.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Three-factor structure of simulation satisfaction (debriefing and reflection, clinical reasoning, clinical learning)**: The SSE scale conceptualises student satisfaction with simulation as a multidimensional construct rather than a single enjoyment rating. The article states the questionnaire "provides data about general student satisfaction with simulation experience and its three components: debriefing and reflection, clinical reasoning, and clinical learning." The CFA in this study confirmed this three-factor structure with high factor loadings (predominantly above 0.800) in both language groups. The authors also relate satisfaction to Kolb's experiential learning, Vygotsky's scaffolding, Bandura's social learning, and Piaget's constructivism, viewing satisfaction as an indicator of pedagogical soundness. (Polónyiová K. et al. (2025))
