Paas 9-point mental effort rating scale¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), q2–q3 · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A single-item nine-point subjective rating scale developed by Paas for measuring perceived mental effort invested in completing a learning or problem-solving task.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • The predictive value of cognitive load ratings for problem performance was mixed: mental effort predicted performance in Experiment 1, perceived difficulty in Experiment 2, but never both at once [+M]
  • The delayed-higher-than-immediate difference for cognitive load ratings replicates regardless of whether problem complexity increases or decreases across the sequence (Experiment 2) [+M]
  • A single delayed cognitive load rating after a problem sequence is significantly higher than the average of immediate ratings for both mental effort and perceived difficulty (Experiment 1) [+M]
  • Invested mental effort and perceived task difficulty are strongly correlated yet relate differently to relative task difficulty, indicating the two ratings measure different aspects of overall cognitive load [+M]
