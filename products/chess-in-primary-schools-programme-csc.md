# Chess in Primary Schools programme (CSC)

## Description
A 30-hour primary-school chess curriculum delivered during the school day by trained chess tutors, with participating class staff and optional chess clubs, run by Chess in Schools and Communities.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Chess in Primary Schools programme (CSC 30-hour curriculum intervention)**: A whole-school approach in which Year 5 children receive 30 hours of chess lessons delivered during the school day by a trained chess tutor following a standardised CSC curriculum, with an optional lunchtime or after-school chess club. Lessons use whole-class teaching with a demonstration board or whiteboard software, and children share chess sets to practise moves and play games in pairs. The programme was delivered by the charity Chess in Schools and Communities at a cost of approximately £1,900 per school for two classes, or £32 per pupil. (John Jerrim et al. (2016))
