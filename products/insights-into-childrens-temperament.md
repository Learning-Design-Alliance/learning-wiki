## Description
INSIGHTS is "an evidence-based intervention that helps families, educators and young children identify how young children's personalities and temperaments affect their behavior." It has three components: a six-lesson self-paced professional development program for educators with remote facilitated sessions, ten weekly 30-minute classroom sessions for children using mitten puppets, videos and activities, and six self-paced one-hour lessons for parents in facilitated groups of eight. No additional staffing is required.
