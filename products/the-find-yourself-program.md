# The "Find yourself" program

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Aydar M. Kalimullin and colleagues’ eight-lesson sensory-room intervention uses sensory equipment, relaxation activities, and peer group work to develop adolescents’ self-regulation, relaxation, cooperation, communication, and emotional comfort.
