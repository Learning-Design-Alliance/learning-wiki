## Description
3T: TimeToTalk is a patient agenda-setting device developed by McNamara and colleagues for shared agenda-setting in paediatric diabetes consultations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **3T: TimeToTalk shared agenda-setting device for clinical consultations**: 3T: TimeToTalk is a patient agenda-setting device used during clinical encounters to promote shared agenda-setting between practitioners, patients and carers. The article describes it as consisting of a rigid folder and an inserted paper agenda pad of tear-off sheets which can be completed in advance by patients and carers to record topics of importance to be raised within consultations. Practitioners may also complete a proforma recording general topics discussed at clinic visits, kept with patient notes to aid record keeping and communication between healthcare professionals, and refill pads are supplied to each clinic. (McNamara Rachel et al. (2010))
