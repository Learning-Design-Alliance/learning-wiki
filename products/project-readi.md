## Description
Project READI is a research-developed framework for teaching adolescent reading comprehension through evidence-based argumentation across multiple disciplinary sources and media.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Project READI science instructional modules with student notebooks, text sets, and scaffold templates**: The intervention materials were curricular modules assembled from the Strands 2 and 4 design work, provided to intervention teachers to ensure consistency across classrooms. "These were assembled into student notebooks and text sets ('readers')." The notebooks included descriptions of activities, instructions, and templates of worksheets that scaffolded reading, reasoning, and modeling activities; the design assumed teacher facilitation and mediation, supported by annotated teacher guides carrying guidance and tips from teachers involved in the iterative design process. Complete modules were made available at www.projectreadi.org. (Susan R. Goldman et al. (2019))
