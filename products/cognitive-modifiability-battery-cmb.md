## Description
A dynamic assessment and intervention instrument developed by Tzuriel for evaluating and supporting cognitive modifiability in young children, including children with complex learning difficulties.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **LPAD Set Variations B-8 to B-12 mediation tasks with the Raven's Coloured Progressive Matrices**: The study used two instruments from the Learning Potential Assessment Device. The LPAD Set Variations B-8 to B-12 consist of sets of six variations on five Raven's Coloured Progressive Matrices items and were selected because "they permit the examiner to access and to modify the area of abstract thinking, and also represent tasks that under normal circumstances are rarely offered to the low-functioning individual." The RCPM, a nonverbal problem-solving test with sets A, AB and B scored 0 to 36, served as the pre- and post-measure (sets A and AB, scores 0 to 24), with set B used for mediation. Both measure forming comparisons, reasoning by analogy and organising spatial perceptions. (Seabi (2012))
