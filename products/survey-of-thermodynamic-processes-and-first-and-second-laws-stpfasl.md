# Survey of Thermodynamic Processes and First and Second Laws (STPFaSL)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A 33-item multiple-choice conceptual survey developed by Benjamin Brown and colleagues to measure introductory physics students' understanding of thermodynamic processes and the first and second laws.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **STPFaSL: a 33-item conceptual multiple-choice survey of thermodynamic processes and the first and second laws**: The Survey of Thermodynamic Processes and First and Second Laws is a 33-item conceptual multiple-choice instrument for introductory physics courses, covering processes, systems, quantities and relations, PV diagrams, and the first and second laws. The article states that "the final version of the survey has 33 multiple-choice items" and that "Each question has one correct choice and four alternative or incorrect choices," with distractors built from documented student difficulties. It is designed as a low-stakes group-level measure of instructional effectiveness. (Benjamin Brown et al. (2021))
- **STPFaSL-Long survey instrument**: STPFaSL-Long is a research-validated conceptual multiple-choice survey on thermodynamic processes and the first and second laws for introductory physics courses. "The final version of the STPFaSL-Long survey instrument has 78 multiple-choice items including 22 true/false questions and each question has one correct choice and three incorrect choices." Items span six topic categories (Processes, Systems, Quantities & Relations, Representation, First Law, Second Law), use 19 contexts identical to the short version, and avoid embedding alternative conceptions in options. It is available via PhysPort. (Mary Jane Brundage et al. (2015))
- **STPFaSL-Long: a validated 78-item multiple-choice survey of introductory thermodynamics concepts**: The STPFaSL-Long is a “research-based, validated 78-item multiple-choice survey instrument” in which “each item focuses on one specific concept (e.g., change in internal energy in isothermal processes), greatly increasing its power to reveal specific student ideas.” Most items have four answer choices asking whether a thermodynamic variable is positive, negative, or zero during a specified process; 22 of 78 items are true/false. It was administered pre- and post-instruction in 12 courses at four institutions and used with think-aloud interviews. (Mary Jane Brundage et al. (1991))
- **STPFaSL-Long survey instrument**: The Survey of Thermodynamic Processes and First and Second Laws-Long is "a research-based survey instrument with 78 items at the level of introductory physics" used to investigate students' difficulties with entropy and the second law. Unlike the earlier STPFaSL-Short, each item is "tightly focused on a single concept involving a single thermodynamic variable," with answer options restricted mainly to increases/decreases/remains constant and not-enough-information. It was administered pre- and post-instruction in proctored in-person classes at four institutions, with 22 of 78 items in true/false format. (Brundage et al. (2024))
