# ATI TEAS and ATI Content Mastery Series RN Fundamentals exam

## Description
The ATI TEAS is an ATI admissions assessment of entry-level reading, mathematics, science, and English-language skills, while the ATI Content Mastery Series RN Fundamentals exam is an ATI nursing-content assessment aligned with the NCLEX-RN test plan.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ATI TEAS admission assessment and RN Content Mastery Series Fundamentals exam as predictor and outcome measures**: The ATI Test of Essential Academic Skills (TEAS) is a standardized admission test measuring "entry-level skills and abilities in Reading, Math, Science, and English and language usage" for health science programs. The ATI Content Mastery Series RN Fundamentals exam measures essential nursing practice concepts "with content specifications directly based on NCLEX-RN test plan" and is typically administered within the first year of nursing programs. The study used adjusted scores from both instruments across more than 100 000 students, treating TEAS content areas as predictors and Fundamentals scores as the indicator of early academic success. (Yoo H et al. (2025))
