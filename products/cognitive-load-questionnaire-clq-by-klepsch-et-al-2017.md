## Description

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Cognitive Load Questionnaire (CLQ) by Klepsch et al. (2017) for differentiated measurement of ICL, ECL, and GCL**: The cognitive load questionnaire (CLQ) is an eight-item self-report instrument with three scales: "A scale with two items asking for perceived ICL, a scale with three items asking for perceived ECL, and finally a scale of three items asking for perceived GCL." All items are rated on 7-point Likert-type scales. The article uses it across six studies to test whether intentional variations of one load type are reflected in the corresponding scale, finding prognostic validity to be good. (Melina Klepsch (2020))
