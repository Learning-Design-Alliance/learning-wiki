## Description
Number Rockets is a scripted Tier 2 small-group mathematics tutoring program for first-grade students struggling in math, delivered by tutors in supplemental 40-minute sessions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Number Rockets scripted small-group first-grade math tutoring program**: Number Rockets is "a scripted tutoring approach used in first grades as a Tier 2 intervention with students struggling in math." Tutors are retired teachers and substitute teachers working with 2-3 children at a time. Sessions run 40 minutes a day over 17 weeks, for 63 days total, supplementing students' ordinary math instruction. (Number Rockets (2020))
- **Number Rockets Tier 2 small-group grade 1 mathematics intervention**: Number Rockets is a supplemental small-group (two or three students) Tier 2 mathematics intervention for grade 1 students at risk for mathematics difficulties, meeting three or four times weekly from November to May. It "covers 17 topics, each divided into three to six lessons, not all of which are required," with mastery-based skipping of extra lessons; students averaged 45 lessons of about 40 minutes delivered by part-time interventionists using scripted lessons during regular school hours. (Rolfhus et al. (2013))

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tutors receive one day of training before beginning, plus two two-hour booster sessions afterwards
#### Constraints
- Designed as a Tier 2 supplement for first-grade students struggling in math, not a replacement for ordinary math instruction

### Target Learners
- first-grade students below the 38th percentile in math

### Target Learning Goals
- early mathematics concepts and procedures, including math facts
