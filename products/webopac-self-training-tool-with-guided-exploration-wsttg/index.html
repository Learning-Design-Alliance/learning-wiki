WebOPAC Self Training Tool with Guided Exploration (WSTTG)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A web-based self-training tool accompanied by a self-guided manual, developed for first-year university students to learn library-catalogue use and information-literacy skills.

Components¶

  • WSTTG instructional design grounded in Ausubel's Instructional Design Theory and Gagne's Nine Events of Instruction: The WebOPAC Self Training Tool with Guided Exploration (WSTTG) is a web-based self-training tool plus a self-guided manual for teaching library catalogue (WebOPAC) use. Its instructional design was "developed according to Ausebel’s Instructional Design Theory and Gagne‘s Nine Events of Instruction which consist of constructivist learning environment model and cognitive of multimedia learning model." The article states the strategy "is used to develop individual’s logical thinking skill and access the information literacy sk ills" aligned with ACRL Information Literacy Competency Standards and Bloom Taxonomy. It is the guided-exploration analogue of the otherwise identical non-guided WSTT tool. (Mohd Nasir Ismail et al. (2018))

Claims¶

  • Instructional method significantly affects information literacy skill, with WSTTG outperforming both WSTT and traditional instruction (F=13.409, p=0.000) [+M]
  • For both HLT and LLT students, the self-training methods (WSTTG and WSTT) each significantly outperformed traditional instruction, but did not differ significantly from each other [+M]
