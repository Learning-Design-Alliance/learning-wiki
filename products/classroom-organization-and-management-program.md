## Description

## Components

- **Classroom Organization and Management Program (COMP)**: The Classroom Organization and Management Program (COMP) is a manualized classroom management program that appeared in 7 of the 12 included studies (58%), making it the most frequently used intervention in the review. It involves teacher training in classroom management, with the teacher as implementer of the strategies targeting student problem behavior as the outcome. COMP studies produced a statistically significant effect size ES=.75 (p =.00). (Oliver et al. (2011))
