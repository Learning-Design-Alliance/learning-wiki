# MyBinder

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
MyBinder is a free, browser-accessible Project Jupyter service that builds and launches reproducible computational environments from repository specifications, developed and operated by the Binder community and its supporting organizations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MyBinder zero-install cloud provision of the Ubermag computational environment**: MyBinder is a free, publicly accessible Project Jupyter service used in the course for zero-install software provision: it scans a data repository, installs the required software with a Jupyter notebook server in a Docker container, and connects it to the user's browser. Students can "connect to a Binder session from their desktop, laptop or mobile device", which the authors say helps lower the usability barrier. They report most students carried out all of their simulation computation on the MyBinder service. (Martin Lonsky et al. (2024))
