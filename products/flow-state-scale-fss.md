## Description
A 36-item self-report scale developed to assess nine dimensions of flow, including challenge–skill balance, goals, feedback, concentration, control, and autotelic experience, for use after learning or training activities.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Flow State Scale (FSS) as a self-report measure of practitioner involvement in simulation training**: The Flow State Scale is a self-report test used in this study to gauge realism, involvement and stress management during simulation. The article describes it as follows: "The FSS consists of 36 items divided into 9 subscales, the short flow scale (SFSS), each representing the nine different dimensions of Flow", scored 1–5 (never to always) and administered after each scenario session. The nine dimensions include challenge-skill balance, clear goals, unambiguous feedback, concentration, sense of control, loss of self-consciousness, time transformation and autotelic experience. (Strozzi Mariachiara Martina et al. (2021))

### Claims
- [In a cohort of 43 practitioners, three Flow State Scale subscales — merging of action and awareness, unambiguous feedback, and loss of self-consciousness — had significantly higher self-reported scores at the second than the first high-fidelity simulation course in one uncontrolled pre-post study](../claims/hfs-improves-fss-subscales-delivery-room.md) [+M]
- [Most Flow State Scale measures showed no significant change between the two simulation courses: 29 of 36 items and 5 of 9 subscales did not differ significantly](../claims/most-fss-measures-unchanged.md) [+M]
