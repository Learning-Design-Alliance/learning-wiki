# AccuTouch Endoscopy Simulator

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A high-fidelity virtual bronchoscopy training simulator made by Immersion Medical, combining a flexible-scope interface, motion and resistance hardware, simulation software, and automated procedural performance metrics for healthcare trainees.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Accutouch high-fidelity virtual reality bronchoscopy simulator (CAE Healthcare)**: The training and assessment platform used in the study: "an Accutouch high-fidelity virtual reality simulator from CAE Healthcare®, Quebec, Canada." It consists of a proxy flexible bronchoscope, a robotic interface device, a computer with simulation software, and a monitor. The virtual patient responds to trainee instrumentation with coughing, and the simulator provides force-feedback which enhances realism. It supplies metrics including procedure time, wall collisions, time in red-out, and percent-segments-entered. (Bjerrum Anne Sofie et al. (2016))
