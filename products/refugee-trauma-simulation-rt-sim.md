## Description
A video-based virtual patient system developed for transcultural psychiatry training in which resident psychiatrists conduct a simulated clinical interview, examination, testing, and diagnostic-management assessment.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **RTSim: a Refugee Trauma Simulator with video-based interview, screening instruments, and dual feedback**: RTSim is a virtual patient system for training primary care professionals to manage traumatized refugee patients. Its prototype supports six interaction areas: medical interview, physical examination including mental status examination, screening instruments (HTQ, HSCL-25, PHQ-2, PHQ-9 and others), laboratory tests and imaging, additional data, and preliminary assessment with treatment plan. The interview uses video clips of a Bosnian actor recorded in Bosnian and subtitled in English, and after the encounter the learner receives feedback from the virtual refugee herself and from a virtual advisor (domain expert). (Ekblad Solvig et al. (2013))
