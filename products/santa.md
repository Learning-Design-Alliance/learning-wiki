## Description
Santa is a multi-platform mobile AI tutoring service for TOEIC preparation, developed and operated by Riiid.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Santa Claus quantum teleportation classroom worksheet**: A classroom worksheet (Appendix C) split into two sections: a motivation based on Santa's Christmas Eve deliveries, and a personalised task in which students choose their own present type and delivery region while keeping Santa Claus as the invariable element. Students then answer questions on key protocol steps and discuss open questions. The article states "The worksheet questions are intended to address naive conceptions that students have in relation to quantum physics". Its primary goal is for students to demonstrate their understanding of quantum teleportation. (Barry W. Fitzgerald et al. (2024))
