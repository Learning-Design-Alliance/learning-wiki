# IPPOG, the International Particle Physics Outreach Group

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An international collaboration of particle-physics scientists, educators, communication specialists, research experiments, laboratories, and countries that coordinates science-education and public-engagement programmes worldwide.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **IPPOG global outreach network for particle physics**: IPPOG is a global network of scientists, educators and communication specialists dedicated to informal science education and public engagement in particle physics. The article states it is a global network dedicated to "connecting students, educators, and the general public with the world of particle physics", serving as the "front line of the particle physics world with society". It evolved from EPPOG (founded 1997), was renamed IPPOG in 2011, and became a formal collaboration in 2016. By end of 2024 it comprised 42 members: 34 countries, 7 experiments and CERN, with DESY and GSI as associate members. (IPPOG Collaboration (2025))
- **IPPOG as a multi-role global outreach infrastructure for particle physics**: The article frames IPPOG as simultaneously a network of scientists, educators and communication specialists; an international collaboration (33 members: 26 countries, 6 experiments and CERN) based on a memorandum of understanding since 2016; an exchange forum and brainstorming platform with working groups; a key partner for promoting the scientific mission globally; and a strategic pillar fostering long-term sustainable support for fundamental research. Its activities aim to improve public understanding of fundamental research, spark interest among young people, and strengthen trust in science and evidence-based decision making. (Barbora Bruant Gulejova (on behalf of the IPPOG Collaboration) (2020))
