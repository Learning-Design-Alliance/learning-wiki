## Description
TeamSTEPPS is an evidence-based healthcare team-training programme developed and publicly released by the U.S. Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality with the Department of Defense.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **TeamSTEPPS three-phase model of change for implementing team training in clinical settings**: TeamSTEPPS is a publicly released, evidence-based team training programme based on teamwork theory and change theory, developed by the US Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality with the Department of Defense. It organises implementation into a recommended "model of change" with three phases: (1) assessment and planning, (2) training and implementation, and (3) sustainment. The programme aims to optimise five teamwork principles — team structure, communication, leadership, situation monitoring and mutual support — and in this study was implemented over 12 months on a surgical ward with monthly tool rollouts. (Ballangrud R et al. (2020))
