## Description
A bilingual HTML5/JavaScript/CSS interactive web application created by Lan Xi Zhu and colleagues for exploring planetary climate, radiative transfer, greenhouse effects, and habitability in high-school, college, and public-outreach contexts.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Radiative-equilibrium greenhouse framework underlying the Climate App**: The explanatory framework underlying the App combines the two-band approximation separating shortwave stellar from longwave planetary radiation, the Stefan-Boltzmann law, Kirchhoff's law of thermal radiation, radiative equilibrium of each climate component, and the two-stream approximation. The article states the App "is built upon atmospheric physics that have been known since the late 19th century", and derives single-layer and three-layer greenhouse models from it. (Lan Xi Zhu et al. (2021))

### Claims
- [With default parameters (A = 0.30, ϵLW = 0.78) the Beginner app's model yields Tatm = 242 K and Tsurf = 288 K, in good agreement with modern Earth, and a 33 °C greenhouse effect](../claims/beginner-single-layer-model-earth-default-temperatures.md) [+M]
