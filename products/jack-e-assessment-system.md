## Description
JACK is a web-based e-assessment and automated tutoring system developed and operated at the University of Duisburg-Essen for authoring, delivering, automatically evaluating, and providing feedback on exercises.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **JACK e-assessment system extended with R integration for statistics exercises**: JACK is a web-based e-assessment and automated tutoring system at the University of Duisburg-Essen whose exercises may contain "multiple choice, ﬁll-in, and drop-down elements". The paper's central extension connects JACK to the statistical language R via the RServe TCP/IP server, so that R is used not only for generating random exercise parameters and plots but also for evaluating student submissions and computing feedback. The authors report heavy use in large statistics lectures for both formative and summative assessment. (Nils Schwinning et al. (2018))
