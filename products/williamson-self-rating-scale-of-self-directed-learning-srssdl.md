# Williamson Self-Rating Scale of Self-Directed Learning (SRSSDL)

## Description
A 60-item five-point Likert self-report assessment developed by Williamson to measure self-directed learning across awareness, learning strategies, learning activities, evaluation, and interpersonal skills.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SRSSDL: Self-Rating Scale of Self-Directed Learning (60-item, five-area instrument)**: The SRSSDL is a sixty-item self-report instrument developed by Williamson in 2007 to assess readiness for self-directed learning, which this study used with slight demographic adaptations for the Pakistani context. It covers "awareness, learning strategies, learning activities, interpersonal skills and evaluation", twelve items each, rated on a five-point Likert scale from 1 (Never) to 5 (Always), with total scores from 60 to 300. The study reports reliability of Cronbach's alpha = 0.93 and CFA fit indices RMSEA = 0.031 and SRMR = 0.055. (Hassan KE et al. (2025))
