## Description
A five-level clinical competency assessment scale used to rate trainees' autonomy in performing procedures, from show-and-tell through independent expert performance.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Five-level Dreyfus scale used to rate surgical autonomy per procedure**: The survey measured trainees' current subjective autonomy for each of the 40 procedures on the 5-point Dreyfus scale, which "evaluates competency to perform a procedure according to five levels": Level 1 show and tell, Level 2 active help, Level 3 passive help, Level 4 supervision only, and Level 5 expert (learner performs procedures and manages complications independently). Instructors separately rated expected competency on a 5-point Likert scale, and both groups rated potential risk if a procedure is performed by a noncompetent surgeon. (Hori Kanta et al. (2024))
