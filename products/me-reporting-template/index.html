M&E Reporting Template¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A Mathematica reporting template that guides organizations, research partners, and funders in producing concise, accessible summaries of study approaches, findings, and next steps.

Components¶

  • Measurement and Evaluation Reporting Template for summarizing and sharing study findings: A reporting template from Mathematica that helps organizations, research partners, and funders summarize and share findings from a study. It "guides users in creating a short report" with a brief solution overview, study approach, three to five key findings, and next steps, in a format described as easy to read and accessible for a variety of audiences. (Kate Place et al. (2023))
