# On the Rise Academy

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A Detroit Public Schools Community District alternative teacher-certification and recruitment programme that prepares local community members and school support staff through coaching, observations, and supported school placements.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **On the Rise Academy community-recruitment alternative certification program**: On the Rise Academy, run by Detroit Public Schools Community District, "recruits new teacher candidates from the local community," with 75% of participants working in DPSCD as paraprofessionals, attendance agents, and academic interventionists. Candidates receive coaching cycles, observations, and intentional school placements under principals committed to their success. In 2021 about 90% of candidates were teachers of color, and DPSCD credits improvement in teacher vacancies partly to the Academy's pipeline of new teachers offsetting retirements and resignations. (Charlotte Pierce (2026))
