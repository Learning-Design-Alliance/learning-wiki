## Description
VolumetricOR is a SteamVR-compatible virtual-reality demonstrator developed by Queisner Moritz et al. that immerses surgical trainees in photogrammetric and volumetric recordings of operating-room workflows.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **VolumetricOR virtual reality demonstrator for surgical workflow training**: VolumetricOR is a virtual reality demonstrator that places users inside a photorealistic virtual operating room recorded at Charité – Universitätsmedizin Berlin, where they navigate sequences of a living kidney donation surgery. Its core is 'a technical workflow that consists in 3 elements': 'a photogrammetric reconstruction of an operating room', 'volumetric video sequences which are recorded directly in the operating room' and 'location-based metadata'. The prototype cost approximately 5000 US Dollars and the software and raw data sets are released under a CC BY-NC 4.0 license. (Queisner Moritz et al. (2022))
