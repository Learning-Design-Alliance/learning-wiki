# VolumetricOR virtual reality demonstrator

## Description
VolumetricOR is a SteamVR-compatible virtual-reality demonstrator developed by Queisner Moritz et al. that immerses surgical trainees in photogrammetric and volumetric recordings of operating-room workflows.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **VolumetricOR virtual reality demonstrator for surgical workflow training**: VolumetricOR is a virtual reality demonstrator that places users inside a photorealistic virtual operating room recorded at Charité – Universitätsmedizin Berlin, where they navigate sequences of a living kidney donation surgery. Its core is 'a technical workflow that consists in 3 elements': 'a photogrammetric reconstruction of an operating room', 'volumetric video sequences which are recorded directly in the operating room' and 'location-based metadata'. The prototype cost approximately 5000 US Dollars and the software and raw data sets are released under a CC BY-NC 4.0 license. (Queisner Moritz et al. (2022))
- **Embodied volumetric simulation shifts surgical learning from observation to participation**: The article proposes that combining volumetric video with virtual reality creates an embodied form of knowledge acquisition in which users 'cognitively and physically enter a virtual operating room with a sense of presence for the real clinical environment'. Interaction in real space and scale connects sensor and motor activity, shifting the learning and training experience 'from observation to participation' and 'from screen to space'. The framework targets acquisition of procedural and spatial knowledge before operating-room participation, and the authors state this 'embodied form of knowledge acquisition distinguishes VolumetricOR from other forms of digital simulation'. (Queisner Moritz et al. (2022))
