# HEALCARe health literacy and respectful compassionate care education programme

## Description
HEALCARe is a co-produced one-week undergraduate nursing education programme on health literacy, respectful and compassionate care, and customer care, developed by Tanzanian and European university lecturers with clinical practice teachers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **HEALCARe health literacy and respectful compassionate care education programme for undergraduate nursing students**: A co-produced package of modules on respectful and compassionate care, health literacy and customer care, developed by Tanzanian and European university lecturers with clinical practice teachers and delivered over one week. It was integrated into the harmonized bachelor curricula of three Tanzanian universities and taught via learning activities including role-plays, case studies and tools for systematic reflection, with twelve master trainers trained to cascade delivery. Its health literacy component produced significant pre-post gains, while the respectful and compassionate care component did not. (Kidayi Paulo L. et al. (2025))
