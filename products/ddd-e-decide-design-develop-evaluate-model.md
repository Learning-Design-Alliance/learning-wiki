# DDD-E (Decide, Design, Develop, Evaluate) model

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A four-phase multimedia learning-materials development framework proposed by Ivers and Baron that guides decisions, design, development, and evaluation, including expert validation and learner try-out.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **DDD-E four-phase development of multimedia learning materials**: The article describes developing the multimedia listening materials through "four phases: Decide, Design, Develop, Evaluate (DDD-E) proposed by Ivers and Baron (2010)". This is a reusable development cycle for producing interactive learning materials: decisions about the materials, their design, their development, and evaluation of the resulting product. In this study the cycle produced expert-validated materials that were then summatively evaluated for effectiveness in a quasi-experiment. (Andi Tenri Ampa (2015))
