## Description
A high-fidelity airway-management simulator made by Kyoto Kagaku that supports procedural training and records objective performance measures across graded difficult-airway scenarios.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Kyoto Kagaku MW11 difficult airway management simulator evaluation system**: The MW11 DAM simulator is a high-fidelity airway training and assessment device used as the study's measurement platform. It presents four graded scenarios — normal airway, locked jaw, rigid neck and micrognathia — and records success, intubation time, peak force on incisor and tongue, and Cormack Lehane grade. The authors note it "was developed at Waseda University in 2006 to serve as an active training device and as an advanced evaluation tool for surgical instruments and medical procedures." (Yau Sze-Yuen et al. (2021))
