# California Critical Thinking Disposition Inventory (CCTDI)

## Description
A 51-item, six-point Likert self-report inventory measuring dispositions toward critical thinking, adapted for Turkish use by Kökdemir (2003) and used with undergraduate teacher candidates.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **California Critical Thinking Disposition Inventory (CCTDI) as outcome measure**: The study used the California Critical Thinking Disposition Inventory, a six-point Likert measure with a score range of 51-306, adapted to Turkish by Kökdemir (2003) with 51 items and six subscales. The whole-scale internal consistency was .88 in the adaptation and a pilot with 152 education students found a Cronbach Alpha of .79 before use in this study. (Fettahlıoğlu et al. (2018))
