## Description
A North Carolina state-funded pre-kindergarten programme administered through qualifying public-school and community-based classrooms for eligible four-year-old children, operated by the state of North Carolina.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **More at Four: North Carolina's state-funded pre-K program for high-risk 4-year-olds**: More at Four (later NC pre-K) is the "state-funded pre-K program for high-risk 4-year-old children, with risk deﬁned as annual family income at or below 75% of the state median, limited English proﬁciency, disability, chronic illness, or develop- mental need." Funded classrooms must meet state standards for staff credentials, class size, teacher-child ratios, and licensing. About half of slots were in public school settings; the program reached up to 25% of all 4-year-olds by 2010. (Dodge et al. (2017))
