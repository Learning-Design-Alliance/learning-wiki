> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
MyDigitalHand is a ticketing platform that manages student help requests and teaching-staff interactions during office hours, as used in the studied CS2 course.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MyDigitalHand (MDH) office-hours ticketing system**: MyDigitalHand (MDH) is "a ticketing system for help requests" used in the studied CS2 course to manage office hours. Students fill out a form that places a request in the queue when a session is active; teaching staff select a student from the queue, invite them into the Zoom meeting, and close the ticket after resolving the interaction. The system supplied the request time, start time, cancel time, and resolved time data used to define wait time and interaction time in this study. (Z. Gao et al. (2024))
