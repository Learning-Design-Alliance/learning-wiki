# NEXUS/Physics publicly available modular materials

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A modular package of WikiBook content, homework and recitation problems, clicker discussion problems, and laboratories developed by the NEXUS/Physics project for interdisciplinary physics courses serving life-science majors.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **NEXUS/Physics publicly available modular materials (WikiBook, homework and recitation problems, clicker problems, laboratories)**: The project released "a significant body of modular materials" including a WikiBook, homework and group-work recitation problems emphasizing biological content and sense-making with no plug-and-chug problems, collections of clicker problems for class discussion, and a set of laboratories. "All of these materials are presently available at the NEXUS/Physics website, http://nexusphysics.umd.edu." The authors intend the materials to be used as modular pieces or supplements and to migrate to an open IPLS distribution environment. (E. F. Redish et al. (2014))
- **NEXUS/Physics interdisciplinary introductory physics course for life sciences**: NEXUS/Physics is an introductory physics class developed for biology and life-sciences (pre-health-care) students, with calculus, biology, and chemistry as prerequisites so physics can have authentic value for biology students on topics like chemical bonding, diffusion, and entropy. Its goals are to "create prototype open-source instructional materials that can be shared", focus on interdisciplinary coordination of biology, chemistry, physics, and math instruction, and "emphasize competency-based instruction, building general scientific skills". (Edward F. Redish (2015))
