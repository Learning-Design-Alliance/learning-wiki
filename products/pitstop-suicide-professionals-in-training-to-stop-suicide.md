# PITSTOP suicide (Professionals In Training to STOP suicide)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A multifaceted suicide-prevention Train-the-Trainer programme developed for psychiatric department staff and delivered by trained mental-health-care-worker trainers using interactive role play, personalized feedback, and protected e-learning modules.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PITSTOP suicide: a multifaceted e-learning supported Train-the-Trainer program for full psychiatric department staff**: PITSTOP suicide (Professionals In Training to STOP suicide) is a practical, multifaceted, small interactive group Train-the-Trainer program whose content reflects the Dutch multidisciplinary guideline on suicidal behavior (PGSB). Instruction uses role play with personalized feedback, with role plays tailored by trainers to the specific patient category trainees work with daily. The program is supported by e-learning modules to complement and prolong the effect of the training. (de Beurs Derek P et al. (2013))
