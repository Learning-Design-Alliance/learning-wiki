## Description
An interactive electronic geometry-optics teaching resource with Flash animations and simulations, developed by Záhorec and colleagues for higher-secondary physics learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Principles of Geometry Optics – interactive multimedia teaching material for secondary physics**: An electronic teaching material with flash animations and interactive simulations of optical phenomena, developed by the authors for teaching geometry optics at higher secondary level. Its "main attention was paid to visualisation of the presented physical concepts (notions) and relations" through "a big number of (flash) animations and interactive simulations". It was used hands-on by experimental group A and won first place in the Assistance materials for on-line education category at the E-learning in practice competition at ICETA 2007. (Záhorec et al. (2010))
