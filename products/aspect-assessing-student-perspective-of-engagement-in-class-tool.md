# ASPECT (Assessing Student Perspective of Engagement in Class Tool)

## Description
A 16-item Likert survey developed by Wiggins and colleagues to measure undergraduate students' perceived engagement in active-learning classrooms across group-activity value, personal effort, and instructor contribution.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ASPECT: a 16-item survey measuring student self-reported engagement in active-learning classrooms**: ASPECT (Assessing Student Perspective of Engagement in Class Tool) is a 16-item, six-point Likert survey measuring three constructs: value of group activity (9 items), personal effort (3 items), and instructor contribution (4 items). It was developed through a mixed-methods process of student interviews, focus groups, think-alouds, and factor analysis, and "takes students on average 6–7 minutes to complete," providing a rapid tool for assessing engagement in large active-learning classrooms. (Wiggins Benjamin L. et al. (2017))

### Claims
- [ASPECT's 16 items load onto three engagement factors (value of group activity, personal effort, instructor contribution) with Cronbach's alpha 0.78–0.91](../claims/aspect-sixteen-items-three-factors.md) [+M]
