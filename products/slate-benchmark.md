# SLATE benchmark

## Description
A benchmark developed by Jingzhuo Wu et al. containing standardized low-resource-language instructional units, assessable items, and matched near- and far-transfer test sets for evaluating AI-generated language-teaching slides through learner outcomes.

### Claims
- [Across 10 frontier slide-generation systems, pedagogical design (D2) correlates more strongly with learning gain (ρ=0.72) than content validity (D1, ρ=0.38)](../claims/slate-d2-pedagogical-design-gain-correlation.md) [+M]
- [Across all 10 evaluated systems, near-transfer accuracy (31–42%) consistently exceeds far-transfer accuracy (11–16%)](../claims/slate-near-far-transfer-gap.md) [+M]
