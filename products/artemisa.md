# Artemisa

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Artemisa is a chatbot developed through the ACACIA project to provide anonymous, accessible support for university students experiencing sexual harassment and to recruit volunteers promoting diversity and tolerance.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Artemisa chatbot for sexual harassment support at Peruvian university**: The European project ACACIA (Restrepo et al., 2019), funded in part by Erasmus+, features a chatbot named Artemisa designed to address sexual harassment and recruit volunteers to promote diversity and tolerance at the Peruvian National University of San Marcos. The review lists it as a cultural and inclusive AIED feature providing support to victims of sexual harassment, ensuring anonymity, accessibility, and responsiveness valuable in sensitive and emotionally challenging situations. Like other chatbots, it presents accessibility challenges for users. (Ana Mouta et al. (2023))
