## Description
Norway's central public portal for aggregated administrative data on higher-education students, programs, and assessment outcomes, accompanied here by openly released code for reproducing grade-distribution analyses.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **DBH public statistics portal with an openly released reproducible analysis workflow for describing grade distributions**: The Database for Statistics on Higher Education (DBH) is Norway's central repository of administrative data on students, programs, and assessment outcomes, "publicly accessible via the DBH public statistics portal". The article extracts aggregated grade counts (year × sex × grade) for bachelor degrees in pedagogy at one large university over ten years, and states that "all the analysis code is openly available, enabling researchers to readily extend the approach to other programs, institutions, disciplines, or national datasets". It serves as a template for future descriptive investigations using administrative education data. (Johansen M O et al. (2026))
