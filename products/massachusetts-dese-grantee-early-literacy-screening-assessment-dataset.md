# Massachusetts DESE grantee early literacy screening assessment dataset

## Description
A dataset of K–3 literacy screening assessment scores collected by the Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education from participating grantee schools and districts to analyze reading-risk patterns and inform intervention and resource targeting.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Massachusetts DESE literacy screening assessment data collection from grantee schools and districts**: Beginning in the 2020/21 school year, the Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education began collecting literacy screening assessment data from schools and districts participating in certain state grants, drawing on a list of state-approved commercial screener products. The analysis in this brief uses 'More than 4,000 students from the 2021/22 school year, including 28 districts and 63 schools,' with scores from DIBELS 8th Edition, FastBridge aReading, i-Ready Diagnostic, Lexia Rapid, MAP Growth, mClass, and Star Reading. The stated goal is analyzing performance patterns to inform improvement efforts and target resources. (Lemke et al. (2023))
