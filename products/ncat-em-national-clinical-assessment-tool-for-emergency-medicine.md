## Description
A standardized emergency-medicine clerkship assessment tool developed through national consensus, comprising clinical performance domains, entrustability ratings, global assessment, professionalism ratings, and required comments for use by supervising clinicians.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **NCAT-EM: National Clinical Assessment Tool for Emergency Medicine**: The NCAT-EM is a nationally standardized, specialty-specific assessment tool developed in 2016 via consensus to standardize medical student assessment during emergency medicine rotations. It consists of "six clinical performance domains rated on a four-point entrustability scale, a global assessment domain, a professionalism section, and mandatory free-text comments". It has begun replacing institution-specific tools across US EM rotations and informs clerkship grades, the MSPE, and SLOEs. (Amin Bushra Z. et al. (2025))
