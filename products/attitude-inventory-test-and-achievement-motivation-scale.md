## Description
A researcher-used five-point Likert questionnaire developed for Guido's study to measure engineering and technology students' attitudes toward and motivation for learning physics.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Attitude Inventory Test and Achievement Motivation Scale (5-point Likert instrument)**: The study's measurement instrument is a standardized Attitude Inventory Test and Achievement Motivation Scale administered as a survey questionnaire with "five degrees of intensity with weights of 5 being the highest and 1 being the smallest rating". Responses were coded on the 5-point Likert scale so that higher scores embodied more positive responses, and weighted means were interpreted verbally (e.g. 4.51-5.00 as Very True to Me). It was used to measure attitudes and motivations of the physics students. (Guido (2013))
