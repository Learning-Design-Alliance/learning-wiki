> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A proposed 76-unit AI-literacy curriculum organized across four pillars and adaptable for non-CS students, school learners, workforce learners, and civic audiences, developed by Sri Yash Tadimalla.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **AI Literacy for All adjustable curriculum (76 CS/KA units across four pillars)**: A foundational AI literacy curriculum presented as an alternative Knowledge Area in the CS2023 curriculum, totaling 76 units across the four pillars: Pillar 1 covers introduction to AI/ML, symbolic and connectionist knowledge representation, search/generative systems/RAG, and LLM mechanics; Pillar 2 covers interacting with LLMs, academic integrity, and prompt engineering for learning; Pillar 3 covers responsible use, security/privacy/ethics, and case studies; Pillar 4 covers public perception, future of work, AI policy, and AI for good. Tables II-V map topics and learning outcomes across seven educational contexts, with asterisks marking topics the authors have previously taught. (Sri Yash Tadimalla (2024))
