# COM-ON-Check Rating Scale

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A structured observer-rating instrument developed for simulated medical consultations to assess learners' delivery of difficult news and handling of patient emotions using SPIKES and NURSE components.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **COM-ON-Check observation framework grounded in the SPIKES six-step bad-news protocol with the NURSE emotion-handling model integrated**: The article structures its observer feedback tasks around a named assessment framework: the COM-ON-Check Rating Scale, which the article states is "theoretisch auf dem SPIKES-Modell zur Übermittlung schlechter Nachrichten aufgebaut". SPIKES comprises six steps (Setting, Perception, Invitation, Knowledge, Strategy and Summary) for structuring difficult conversations. The NURSE model of Back et al. is integrated into the observation tasks to guide handling of emotions: Naming, Understanding, Respecting, Supporting, Exploring. In this study these frameworks operationalize what peer observers watch for during simulated consultations and what feedback the practising student receives. (Gür Deniz et al. (2024))
