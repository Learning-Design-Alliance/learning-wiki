Children's Literacy Initiative (CLI) Model Classroom program¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A literacy professional-development and coaching programme run by the Children's Literacy Initiative that supports K–2 teachers and develops selected model teachers to mentor colleagues and host peer classroom visits.

Components¶

  • Children's Literacy Initiative Model Classroom program: three years of literacy PD, coaching, and model-teacher development for K-2 teachers: The CLI program "provides three years of professional development and coaching in literacy practices to all teachers in kindergarten through second grade and provides additional resources and coaching to one teacher per grade, in an effort to develop that teacher into a model teacher for the rest of the teachers in the grade." Year 1 delivered three summer institute days, three seminar days, and 50 hours of classroom-embedded coaching, plus literacy resources such as book collections and Message Time Plus materials; year 2 lowered dosage to three seminar days and 25 hours of coaching. Model Classroom teachers received extra coaching, a stipend, and additional resources, and opened their classrooms to peer visits. (Parkinson et al. (2015))

Claims¶

  • The CLI Model Classroom program significantly improves teachers' language and literacy practices (ES = 0.68, p<0.001) as measured by the ELLCO [+M]
  • The CLI Model Classroom program significantly improves teachers' classroom environment (ES = 0.52, p=0.003) as measured by the ELLCO [+M]
  • CLI significantly improves kindergarten letter-word reading in two consecutive cohorts (ES = 0.16 and 0.17) [+M]
  • CLI shows no significant impact on cohort 1 first-grade students' GRADE literacy achievement [~M]
