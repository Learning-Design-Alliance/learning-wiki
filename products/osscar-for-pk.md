## Description
OSSCAR for PK is an interactive open-source physics-learning application using guided notebook tasks, hidden solutions, and visualizations for student exploration and teacher demonstration.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **OSSCAR notebook structure: short guided introduction, hidden solutions, interactive visualization, legend**: Each OSSCAR application follows a fixed structure serving two use cases: independent student learning (A) and live teacher demonstration (B). It opens with a brief textual section listing educational goals, a link to background theory and guiding tasks, followed by the interactive visualization (figures plus controllers) and a bottom legend. Task solutions are hidden by default, a choice the authors say "encourages students to answer the questions themselves rather than read directly the solutions, promoting active learning". The short introduction is easy to skip for use case B. (Dou Du et al. (2022))
