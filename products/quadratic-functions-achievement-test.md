# Quadratic Functions achievement test

## Description
A four-item multiple-choice achievement test developed by Filiz Tuba Dikkartin Övez to measure tenth-grade students' learning of four quadratic-functions acquisitions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Quadratic Functions achievement test (four items, KR20 = 0.89)**: A multiple-choice achievement test developed for the study, with "four items, each of which took the form of a question to measure each acquisition in sub-learning field of quadratic functions". It was developed from a 16-question item pool, refined through expert content-validity review (minimum CVR 0.78 at α=0.05), pre-trialed with 197 eleventh-grade students, and finalized by selecting the item with the greatest discriminatory power (greater than .30) for each acquisition. Validity coefficient was reported as 0.89 (KR920). (Filiz Tuba Dikkartin Övez (2018))
