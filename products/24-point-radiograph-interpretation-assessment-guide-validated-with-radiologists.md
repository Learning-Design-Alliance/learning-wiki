# 24-point radiograph interpretation assessment guide validated with radiologists

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 24-point scoring guide for assessing physiotherapists' identification of pathological features on three lumbar-spondylosis plain-film radiographs, developed and validated by Bello Ajediran et al. with radiologist input.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **24-point radiograph interpretation assessment guide validated with radiologists (Cronbach's alpha 0.79)**: An assessment guide developed from an experienced independent radiologist's identification of pathological features on three plain film radiographs of lumbar spondylosis, listing "A total of 24 features were identified by the radiologist from three PFR with each consisting 8 features". Correct identification scores 1 point, wrong or non-identification 0, with total scores of 0–8, 9–16, and 17–24 interpreted as low, moderate, and high agreement. Test–retest reliability among seven experienced radiologists yielded "A good internal consistency of 0.79 Chrombach's alpha value". The guide served as the gold standard for scoring physiotherapists in the survey. (Bello Ajediran I et al. (2014))
