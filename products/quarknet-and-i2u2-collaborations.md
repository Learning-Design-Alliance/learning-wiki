## Description
Fermilab-led educational collaborations that provide high-school learners and teachers with online particle-physics data, masterclasses, e-Labs, and teacher-developed investigations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Online particle physics data and short investigations for high school students (QuarkNet and I2U2 collaborations)**: Through the QuarkNet and I2U2 collaborations, Fermilab provides online data from DZero, CDF, CMS and student cosmic ray detectors for use in data-driven activities, masterclasses and e-Labs. Short investigations written by physics teachers with physicist assistance include Calculate the Mass of the Top Quark based on DZero data, a histogram study of U.S. pennies, the Quark Workbench, and superconductivity activities on temperature dependence of resistance. (M. Bardeen (2011))
