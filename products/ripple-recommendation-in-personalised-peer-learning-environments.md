# RiPPLE (Recommendation in Personalised Peer-Learning Environments)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 2 of 3 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
RiPPLE is an adaptive higher-education peer-learning platform for creating, evaluating, revising, and practising with peer-generated learning resources.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **RiPPLE adaptive peer-learning platform**: RiPPLE (Recommendation in Personalised Peer-Learning Environments) is the platform in which the study was conducted. As printed, "RiPPLE (Recommendation in Personalised Peer-Learning Environments) is an adaptive learning platform that engages students in creating, evaluating, revising, and practising with peer-generated learning resources." It structures activity across creation, review, and practice stages; peer moderation ratings are aggregated into a submitted-work quality score on a 0-5 scale. Its instrumentation captured fine-grained interaction logs recording every state transition between drafting, reviewing feedback, AI-supported dialogue, revision, and submission, enabling all outcome measures in the study. (Omar Alsaiari et al. (2026))
- **RiPPLE learnersourcing platform with embedded feedback guide**: RiPPLE is a teaching and research platform developed at the University of Queensland in which students create learning resources (multiple-choice questions, multi-answer questions, worked examples, and open-ended "notes") and moderate peers' resources before release to a shared bank. In this study the platform housed the intervention: "The guide, housed on RiPPLE, popped up the first time students began to provide feedback" and remained accessible thereafter. It also supports large-scale experimental research designs such as RCTs. (George Gyamfi et al. (2024))
- **RiPPLE learning platform**: RiPPLE is a discipline-agnostic learning platform operating at the course level, utilised to facilitate a student-centred, interactive, and collaborative learning environment. The platform incorporates learnersourcing, learning analytics, and adaptive learning mechanisms. Students generate learning resources (MCQs, worked examples, flashcards, slides, notes) and review peers' resources, which upon approval become accessible to the entire class for educational purposes. (Lahza et al. (2025))
- **Multivariate Elo-based overlay learner model with an open learner model**: RiPPLE models learners with a multivariate Elo rating system that conducts a paired comparison between students and questions, using "independent parameters to model a student's proﬁciency level on each individual concept in the domain" — an overlaying learner model. Rating updates are proportional to the rating difference, and the authors cite the approach's simplicity and self-correcting behaviour. The model is externalized through an open learner model visualization (bar charts and line charts, with red/yellow/blue competency levels and a cohort-average line), placed close to recommendations so students understand the rationale behind them. (Hassan Khosravi et al. (2019))
- **RiPPLE platform for crowdsourced adaptive recommendation of learning activities**: RiPPLE (Recommendation in Personalised Peer-Learning Environments) is an adaptive learning system that "recommends personalized learning activities to students based on their knowledge state from a pool of crowdsourced learning activities" generated by educators and students. It supports LTI integration into LMSs such as Blackboard, Moodle, and Canvas or standalone use, provides an open learner model, filters and sorting for resource selection, collaborative-filtering recommendations, badges, student profiles, and downloadable course reports for research. It was piloted in an introductory relational-databases course with 453 students. (Hassan Khosravi et al. (2019))
- **RiPPLE learnersourcing platform with integrated LLM-based AI feedback**: RiPPLE is "an adaptive educational system that employs a constructivist epistemology-driven learnersourcing mechanism to engage students in socio-cognitive and socio-cultural-based learning through three main activities: content authoring, peer review, and drill and practice". Students create resources such as worked examples, notes and MCQs; a machine learning-enhanced peer review process refines them; and resources passing an AI-based consensus algorithm enter course repositories for reuse. The platform recently integrated LLM-generated AI feedback delivered before resources or peer feedback are submitted, which was the vehicle for this study's emotional enrichment intervention. (Omar Alsaiari et al. (2024))
- **RiPPLE adaptive educational platform for student co-creation of learning resources**: RiPPLE is an "adaptive educational platform designed to support student co-creation of learning resources". It operates through a continuous cycle of creation, evaluation, and practice: students author multiple choice questions, worked examples, and explanatory notes; resources undergo peer evaluation; and students complete adaptive practice sessions where algorithms model individual knowledge and recommend tailored resources. In this study RiPPLE served as the experimental environment for implementing and evaluating the three AI-generated feedback conditions, with quality control maintained through learning analytics and AI oversight. (Omar Alsaiari et al. (2025))
