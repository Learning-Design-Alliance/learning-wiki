# RiPPLE (Recommendation in Personalised Peer-Learning Environments)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
RiPPLE is an adaptive higher-education peer-learning platform for creating, evaluating, revising, and practising with peer-generated learning resources.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **RiPPLE adaptive peer-learning platform**: RiPPLE (Recommendation in Personalised Peer-Learning Environments) is the platform in which the study was conducted. As printed, "RiPPLE (Recommendation in Personalised Peer-Learning Environments) is an adaptive learning platform that engages students in creating, evaluating, revising, and practising with peer-generated learning resources." It structures activity across creation, review, and practice stages; peer moderation ratings are aggregated into a submitted-work quality score on a 0-5 scale. Its instrumentation captured fine-grained interaction logs recording every state transition between drafting, reviewing feedback, AI-supported dialogue, revision, and submission, enabling all outcome measures in the study. (Omar Alsaiari et al. (2026))
- **RiPPLE learnersourcing platform with embedded feedback guide**: RiPPLE is a teaching and research platform developed at the University of Queensland in which students create learning resources (multiple-choice questions, multi-answer questions, worked examples, and open-ended "notes") and moderate peers' resources before release to a shared bank. In this study the platform housed the intervention: "The guide, housed on RiPPLE, popped up the first time students began to provide feedback" and remained accessible thereafter. It also supports large-scale experimental research designs such as RCTs. (George Gyamfi et al. (2024))
