# The EM Model (Model of the Clinical Practice of Emergency Medicine)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The EM Model is an expert-consensus emergency-medicine content framework maintained for organising residency curricula and informing specialty board certification examinations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The EM Model (Model of the Clinical Practice of Emergency Medicine) as the de facto conference content foundation**: The EM Model is the expert-consensus specialty content framework the review recommends as the scaffold for conference curricula (Level 5, Grade D). It is "most commonly used as the de facto foundation of the conference curriculum in most residencies," is informed by areas most relevant to the emergency physician, and is refined every three years. During its creation, "hospital data from over 90 million ED visits were compared to its content and found to have 82% overlap, validating the content of the EM Model." It also informs board certification examinations, making familiarity with its topics important for residents. (Wood D. Brian et al. (2020))
