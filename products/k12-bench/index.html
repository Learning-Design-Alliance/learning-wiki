K12-Bench¶

Description¶

K12-Bench is a 23,640-item multi-select benchmark dataset created by Hao Liang et al. for evaluating large language models' structural understanding of K–12 curricula.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • OmniEdu-27B achieves the best overall curriculum-grounding performance among evaluated models, raising K12-Bench EM from 52.11% to 63.12% [+W]
  • Current LLMs lack robust curriculum cognition: even the best model reaches only 57.1% exact match on K12-Bench, with prerequisite and neighbor tasks hardest [+W]
