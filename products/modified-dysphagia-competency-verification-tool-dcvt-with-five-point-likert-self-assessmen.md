# Modified Dysphagia Competency Verification Tool (DCVT) with five-point Likert self-assessment

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A modified version of the ASHA-subcommittee Dysphagia Competency Verification Tool adapted by Smaoui et al. for graduate speech-language-therapy students to self-assess dysphagia clinical competency before and after clinical exposure.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Modified DCVT with a 5-point agree/disagree Likert scale adapted for second-language English speakers**: The Dysphagia Competency Verification Tool (DCVT), developed by an ASHA subcommittee to standardize evaluation of clinical competency in dysphagia, was modified for this study. The team changed the rating scale to a 5-point ordinal Likert format (strongly disagree to strongly agree) and lowered the reading level, stating this "allows for easier interpretation of these terms and subsequently a more accurate self-assessment of competency." Responses were dichotomized as Emergent (scores below 4) or Adequate (4 and above) for analysis. (Smaoui S et al. (2025))
