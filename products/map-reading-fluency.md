# MAP Reading Fluency

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 12 claims (12 for) · 7 studies (3 design, 1 causal, 1 review, 1 associational, 1 theoretical), `q1`–`q3` · 1 of 7 report an effect size · 12 claims rest on one study

## Description
MAP Reading Fluency is NWEA's online adaptive assessment for pre-K through grade 5 reading skills and oral reading fluency, with benchmark testing, progress monitoring, and an optional K–3 dyslexia screener in English and Spanish.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MAP Reading Fluency adaptive oral reading fluency assessment with dyslexia screener and progress monitoring**: MAP Reading Fluency is an online, adaptive assessment for pre-K to grade 5 readers measuring foundational skills, literal comprehension, language comprehension, and oral reading fluency, with automatic speech-scoring and group administration in one class period. It includes four benchmark tests, a 5- to 10-minute progress-monitoring tool, and an optional K-3 dyslexia screener, and is "Available in English and Spanish." The paper describes it as the entry point that places students into personalized coaching pathways. (MAP Reading Fluency with Coach Evidence Base (2024))
- **MAP Reading Fluency Adaptive Oral Reading default form routing students to Oral Reading Fluency or Foundational Skills tracks**: MAP Reading Fluency is "a multistage adaptive assessment" administered typically three times a year, presenting easier or more difficult tasks based on prior performance. Under the default Adaptive Oral Reading form, students are routed "to either the Oral Reading Fluency track if they are ready to read passages or to the Foundational Skills track if they are not"; teachers can also assign tracks directly. Foundational Skills items are scored dichotomously and calibrated with the Rasch model onto a scale with mean 500 and SD 10 using Winter 2020 estimates as reference. (Wei He (2022))
- **MAP Reading Fluency assessment as the vehicle of the theory of action**: MAP Reading Fluency is an NWEA assessment product that the theory of action organizes: the paper describes the hypothesized mechanisms of change and intermediate goals through which the product is intended to help all students read fluently with comprehension. The publication page tags it under Equity and Measurement & scaling topics, situating it as a measurement tool in early literacy. (Mary Ann Simpson (2021))
- **MAP Reading Fluency adaptive online early literacy assessment**: MAP Reading Fluency is "an adaptive online assessment that supports students on their path to reading comprehension by assessing and helping to improve both oral reading fluency and foundational reading skills" for pre-K to Grade 5. "The test adapts in real-time and presents easier or more difficult passages and items depending on student performance," and all spoken-response tasks are scored via automated speech recognition. Students typically take 20–30 minutes, and results include proficiency levels, an individualized literacy profile, and recommended next steps. (NWEA (2024))
- **Achievement and growth norms for Spanish MAP Reading Fluency Foundational Skills (grades K–3)**: A 2026 technical report presents "achievement and growth norms for Spanish MAP Reading Fluency Foundational Skills (grades K–3)", enabling educators to interpret early literacy performance "relative to national peer groups". The norms cover foundational skills in Spanish reading fluency for early grades and are listed under topics including item response theory, measurement and scaling, and test design. (NWEA Research (2026))
- **Stress Awareness field test measures for Spanish suprasegmental phonology**: Beginning Fall 2024, NWEA field tested three new Stress Awareness measures: Syllable Segmentation, Stressed Syllable, and Word Classification, all untimed. The guide states "Stress awareness is the strongest predictor of reading success in Spanish and is directly linked to word recognition (Levis, 2018)," categorizing it under suprasegmental phonology, measuring abilities that Phonological Awareness measures do not. (NWEA (2025))
- **MAP Reading Fluency theory of action: hypothesized mechanisms of change and intermediate goals toward fluent reading with comprehension**: The MAP Reading Fluency theory of action is a causal model, in white-paper form, that shows the hypothesized mechanisms of change and intermediate goals leading to the overarching goal of helping all students read fluently with comprehension. As the abstract states, it shows the hypothesized mechanisms of change and intermediate goals that culminate in the goal of helping all students read fluently with comprehension. It frames the assessment within an equity and measurement agenda. (Mary Ann Simpson (2021))
- **Spanish MAP Reading Fluency Foundational Skills assessment**: A Spanish-language early-grades reading assessment covering three domains: Phonological Awareness, Phonics & Word Recognition, and Language Comprehension. It is "administered as a multistage adaptive test in which students receive easier or more-difficult tasks based on previous tasks" and can be given in group settings. Items are scored dichotomously, each domain is scaled separately with the Rasch model, and scores are placed on a scale with mean 500 and SD 10, comparable across grades. (Wei He et al. (2026))

# Spanish MAP Reading Fluency Foundational Skills assessment

## Description
A Spanish-language early-grades reading assessment covering three domains: Phonological Awareness, Phonics & Word Recognition, and Language Comprehension. It is "administered as a multistage adaptive test in which students receive easier or more-difficult tasks based on previous tasks" and can be given in group settings. Items are scored dichotomously, each domain is scaled separately with the Rasch model, and scores are placed on a scale with mean 500 and SD 10, comparable across grades.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administered typically three times a year in fall, winter, and spring; adaptive Oral Reading is the default form routing students to the Oral Reading Fluency or Foundational Skills track
#### Constraints
- Spanish Foundational Skills measures focus predominantly on syllables rather than phonemes, reflecting Spanish's highly transparent orthography

### Target Learners
- Students in grades K-3 in biliteracy programs and emergent bilinguals

### Target Learning Goals
- Foundational Spanish reading skills: phonological awareness, phonics and word recognition, and language comprehension
