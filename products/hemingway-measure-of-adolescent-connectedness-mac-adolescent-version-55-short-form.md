# Hemingway: Measure of Adolescent Connectedness (MAC), Adolescent Version 5.5 short form

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 40-item Likert-type self-report assessment developed by Karcher for measuring adolescents’ connectedness to self, family, school, and friends across ten subscales.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Hemingway: Measure of Adolescent Connectedness (MAC), Adolescent Version 5.5 short form**: A 40-item self-report survey measuring "the degree to which adolescents care for and are involved in particular relationships and activities" across four adolescent worlds: Self, Family, School, and Friends. It has 10 subscales rated on a 5-point Likert-type scale from 1 (not at all true) to 5 (very true). This study administered it before and after the mentoring program and analyzed the six school-connectedness subscales; test-retest reliability ranges from .69 to .91 and subscale Cronbach alphas ranged from r = .60 to r = .94 in Karcher's validation. (Kraus et al. (2016))
