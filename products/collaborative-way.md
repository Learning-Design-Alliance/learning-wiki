# Collaborative Way

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A teamwork framework used by the Team Learning Foundation that structures collaboration around generous listening, direct communication, encouragement, commitment-keeping, appreciation, and a shared team mission.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Collaborative Way teaming model (Fickett and Fickett)**: The Collaborative Way is the teamwork model used by the Team Learning Foundation, drawn from Fickett and Fickett (2006). The article states: "The Team Learning Foundation uses Collaborative Way model for teamwork provided by Fickett and Fickett (2006)." The model encourages students to listen generously, speak straight, be encouraging, honor commitments, and appreciate others' contributions. Prior to departure, students create a team mission statement called the "up to", which the authors report provides focus, purpose, and the first opportunity for student dialogue and group cohesion. (Breitkreuz et al. (2022))

# Collaborative Way teaming model (Fickett and Fickett)

## Description
The Collaborative Way is the teamwork model used by the Team Learning Foundation, drawn from Fickett and Fickett (2006). The article states: "The Team Learning Foundation uses Collaborative Way model for teamwork provided by Fickett and Fickett (2006)." The model encourages students to listen generously, speak straight, be encouraging, honor commitments, and appreciate others' contributions. Prior to departure, students create a team mission statement called the "up to", which the authors report provides focus, purpose, and the first opportunity for student dialogue and group cohesion.
