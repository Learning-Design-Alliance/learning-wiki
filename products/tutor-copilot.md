## Description
Tutor CoPilot is a human-AI tutoring-support tool developed by Wang and colleagues that provides customizable, real-time expert-like guidance to tutors during K–12 mathematics sessions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Human-AI real-time expertise scaling: models of expert thinking embedded in human workflows complement human contextual knowledge**: The article's theory of change holds that many tutors lack the experience to respond effectively in the moment, producing low-quality responses like generic feedback or giving away the answer. "By offering real-time guidance tailored to the conversation and following high-quality strategies, Tutor CoPilot empowers tutors with the right language to develop the student's understanding." The approach combines complementary strengths: AI lacks educators' grounded, context-specific knowledge of curricula, assessment standards, and individual students, but can provide real-time guidance during instructional moments, while the human selects expert-curated strategies — without which LMs tend to default to repetitive strategies. (Rose E. Wang et al. (2025))
