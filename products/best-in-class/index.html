BEST in CLASS¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), q3 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A teacher-delivered, practice-based coaching and professional-development intervention run by its developers to improve teacher–child interactions and reduce problem behavior among preschool children at risk for emotional or behavioral disorders.

Components¶

  • BEST in CLASS prevention model: a teacher-delivered Tier 2 indicated preventive intervention for children at risk for EBD: BEST in CLASS is an indicated (Tier 2) prevention science model that "systematically identifies and targets young children who engage in elevated chronic problem behaviors" placing them at risk for EBD. Unlike other Tier 2 interventions, it is implemented by teachers during ongoing classroom instruction, giving them flexibility to use practices when identified children most need them. Theoretically it leverages teacher-child social transactions within the classroom ecology; in practice it combines professional development and practice-based coaching to promote positive teacher-child interactions, enhance engagement, increase learning opportunities, and decrease problem behaviors. (Conroy et al. (2018))
  • BEST in CLASS professional development package: workshop, manual, and 14-week practice-based coaching: The intervention's professional development consists of three core components: "a 1-day teacher training workshop, a teacher manual, and weekly practice-based coaching occurring over 14 weeks." The workshop orients teachers to the instructional practices (rules, precorrection, opportunities to respond, behavior-specific praise, corrective feedback, instructive feedback) using modeling, exemplar videos, and interactive activities. The manual contains seven modules, one per practice plus a concluding linking module. Coaching is cyclical—modeling/prompting, focused observation, reflection, facilitated instruction, and action planning—with 2 weeks per module and weekly individualized action plans. (Conroy et al. (2018))
  • BEST in CLASS professional development model (teacher workshop, teacher manual, and 14 weeks of practice-based coaching): BEST in CLASS is a targeted early childhood intervention whose professional development model trains teachers to use six practices (rules, precorrection, opportunities to respond, behavior-specific praise, corrective feedback, instructive feedback) intentionally with focal children at risk for EBD. The article states "BEST in CLASS includes the following three manualized professional development components for teachers: (a) BEST in CLASS teacher training workshop, (b) BEST in CLASS teacher manual, and (c) BEST in CLASS practice-based coaching." The 6-hr workshop builds initial knowledge, the seven-module manual extends it, and weekly coaching supports high-fidelity implementation over 14 weeks. (Maureen A. Conroy et al. (2019))
  • BEST in CLASS behavioral–transactional theoretical model of teacher practice change: The article grounds BEST in CLASS in behavioral and transactional theories, proposing that training teachers to embed targeted practices in reciprocal interactions with focal children reduces coercive exchanges and raises positive interactions, self-efficacy, and classroom quality. The article states the intervention is "Founded in behavioral (Skinner, 1953) and transactional (Sameroff, 1983) theories" and is "designed to increase teachers' intentional use of the BEST in CLASS practices during naturally occurring classroom activities with children who are demonstrating elevated rates of problem behavior". Through increased direct use of the practices, the model postulates negative interactions decrease, teachers' self-efficacy increases, and overall classroom atmosphere improves. (Maureen A. Conroy et al. (2019))

Claims¶

  • BEST in CLASS reduced the percentage of children in the clinical or borderline range on all three C-TRF problem-behavior subscales [+M]
  • Treatment reduced the percentage of children in the clinical or borderline range on the SSIS-RS Problem Behavior subscale (SRD = −.186) [+M]
  • BEST in CLASS significantly increased teachers' observed use of rules, precorrection, opportunities to respond, behavior-specific praise, corrective feedback, and instructive feedback with focal children [+M]
  • BEST in CLASS improved observed classroom quality on all three CLASS domains, with effect sizes .50 or larger [+M]
  • BEST in CLASS raised teachers' sense of self-efficacy on all TSES subscales, with effect sizes .50 or larger and the largest effect for Classroom Management [+M]
