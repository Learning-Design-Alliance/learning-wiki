# seven-item AR usability questionnaire

## Description
A researcher-developed seven-item Likert questionnaire for evaluating perceived ease of use, learning/procedural understanding, confidence/efficiency, comfort, and overall acceptance of AR smart glasses in simulated critical-care training.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Four-domain conceptual model of AR smart-glass usability in simulated procedural training**: The article organizes its seven-item questionnaire into four conceptual domains for AR-supported clinical simulation: "D1: ease of use (p1, p2); D2: learning / procedural understanding (p3); D3: confidence / efficiency (p4, p5); D4: comfort (p6)", with a separate global acceptance item (p7). A hierarchical visual model maps items to domains under a central overall-usability node. The domains were defined for descriptive and organizational purposes and follow conceptual elements of the system usability scale and the technology acceptance model. (Fontes Carlos Edmundo Rodrigues et al. (2026))
