## Description
A 56-item, five-point Likert self-report questionnaire adapted from the Life Attitude Profile and Positive Coping, Spirituality and Well-Being Scale to measure nursing students’ meaning of life, positive beliefs, and well-being across pre-test, post-test, and four-week follow-up administrations.
