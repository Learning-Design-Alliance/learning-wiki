# Chinese version of the 10-item Academic Grit Scale (AGS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A back-translated 10-item, five-point Likert self-report assessment developed by Lin Rongmao et al. to measure academic grit—determination, resilience, and focus—among Chinese primary-school students aged 9–14.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Chinese version of the 10-item Academic Grit Scale (AGS) for middle- and upper-grade primary school students**: The Chinese AGS is a back-translated, 10-item self-report measure of academic grit with 4 determination items, 4 resilience items, and 2 focus items, rated on a 5-point Likert scale from 1 (not at all like me) to 5 (very much like me). Translation followed "well-established back-translation procedures for the cross-cultural study", with expert content-validity review and teacher face-validity judgment. In this study it showed Cronbach's α = 0.88 and ω = 0.94. (Lin Rongmao et al. (2024))
