# M-PLANS (Motivation - Planning Lessons to Activate eNgagement in Science)

## Description
M-PLANS is a multi-week professional-learning programme and toolkit developed by Harris et al. to help middle-school science teachers provide motivationally supportive NGSS-based instruction.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **M-PLANS professional learning program and resource suite**: The Motivation - Planning Lessons to Activate eNgagement in Science (M-PLANS) program aims to equip science teachers to support students' motivation and engagement in NGSS-based instruction. It includes a multi-week professional learning approach, a comprehensive reference toolkit, and pocket-sized classroom-based quick tools for lesson planning and just-in-time instructional decisions. Resources were iteratively developed and tested in middle school science classrooms. (Harris et al. (2023))
