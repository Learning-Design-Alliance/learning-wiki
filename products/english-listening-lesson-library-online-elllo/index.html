English Listening Lesson Library Online (ELLLO)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

ELLLO is an online English-listening courseware and lesson library run by ELLLO.org, offering ESL learners audio lessons from varied speakers and countries with vocabulary, comprehension, and cloze exercises.

Components¶

  • ELLLO English listening courseware used as the online learning treatment: The study's experimental group learned listening through English Listening Lesson Library Online (ELLLO), a courseware the article describes as "designed for English Second Language learners" with "lots of listening exercises from various countries" on themes including world issues, described as interactive. The teacher selected two topics per class, and the same audio and matched question items (vocabulary, comprehension, and cloze texts) were used in the traditional classroom to avoid bias when comparing results. (Aminuddin Hassan et al. (2014))
