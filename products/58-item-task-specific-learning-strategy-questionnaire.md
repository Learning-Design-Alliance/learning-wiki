# 58-item task-specific learning strategy questionnaire

## Description
A researcher-constructed questionnaire measuring ninth-grade students’ self-reported cognitive and metacognitive activities while studying a specific history text, developed by Gonny Schellings.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **58-item task-specific learning strategy questionnaire scored on a three-point frequency scale**: A questionnaire constructed "straightforwardly on the basis of a taxonomy for coding think-aloud protocols", with all items referring to the specific history text read before completion and each of the taxonomy's categories tapped by one item. Items were scored on a three-point frequency scale ('almost never'=1, 'sometimes'=2, 'often'=3), a choice the article says may "reduce the variation in the choice of a reference point amongst students." The full instrument reached α=.87 (Study 1) and α=.79 (Study 2). (Gonny Schellings (2011))
