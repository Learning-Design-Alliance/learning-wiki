## Description
A commercial introductory biology textbook developed by its authors around Vision and Change and AP Biology recommendations, organized by core concepts and scales and published by Trunity for AP and college introductory biology learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Integrating Concepts in Biology (ICB) inquiry-focused textbook**: The ICB textbook (Campbell, Heyer, and Paradise, 2014) is an online, inquiry-focused introductory biology textbook that reverses the traditional content-centered design: "While traditional textbooks often place content at the center and include scientific practice in the margins, the ICB textbook reverses that approach." Each chapter is built on discussion and dissection of published figures and tables from the primary literature, connected to fundamental themes of biology, with embedded "Integrating Questions" answered as extended-response homework graded by humans. The authors liken resulting lectures to journal clubs, referred to as "inquiry-in-lecture." (Luckie Douglas B. et al. (2017))
