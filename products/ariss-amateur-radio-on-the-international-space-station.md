## Description
ARISS is an international education and outreach programme operated by amateur-radio, space, and volunteer partner organisations that enables students and the public to communicate with astronauts through scheduled ham-radio contacts.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ARISS: Amateur Radio on the International Space Station education and outreach program**: ARISS is a cooperation experiment for education and outreach that "enables students worldwide to engage directly with astronauts through a radio scientific experiment via ham radio". Operated through five regional offices with volunteer technical mentors, it hosts roughly 60-100 contacts involving about 15,000 to 100,000 students since November 2000. Its hardware includes an FM radio system on VHF (144-146 MHz) and UHF (435-438 MHz) bands in the Zarya module and the next-generation Interoperable Radio System (IORS) installed in the Columbus module in 2020, supported by a worldwide telebridge network of ground stations. (Barbosa D et al. (2026))
