## Description
ATR CALL BRIX is web-based TOEIC preparation courseware with integrated LMS materials, activity tracking, feedback, and monitoring functions, developed by ATR CALL.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ATR CALL BRIX web-based TOEIC courseware with LMS monitoring functions**: ATR CALL BRIX is a www-based courseware including a Learning Management System with e-learning materials designed to prepare students for the TOEIC Test, accessed by students on mobile devices before class. The LMS featured "Seven different functions", including study logs, feedback on achievement rates of student-set goals, records of frequency of use and time spent learning, continuous updates of average TOEIC task scores, evaluation of weak points with advice, and student rankings. (Ishikawa et al. (2015))
