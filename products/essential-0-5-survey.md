## Description
The Essential 0-5 Survey, created in partnership with Start Early, gathers teacher and parent perceptions to assess six organizational conditions in early-childhood education programs and provide leaders with actionable data.

## Description

They extend and complement existing measures of ECE quality constructs and are intended to give leaders and practitioners actionable data about organizational supports. The instrument has since been renamed The Essential 0-5 Survey.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires teacher and parent survey responses from ECE sites to compute scale scores
#### Constraints
- Measures organizational conditions rather than directly observing classroom practice

### Target Learners
- early childhood education practitioners and program leaders

### Target Learning Goals
- measuring and improving organizational conditions that support effective work with children and families
