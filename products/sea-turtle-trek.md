# Sea Turtle Trek

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 124-page environmental-education curriculum packet for grades 6–8, developed for Hammocks Beach State Park and providing hands-on sea-turtle and conservation activities aligned with North Carolina standards.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Sea Turtle Trek Environmental Education Learning Experience packet**: A 124-page educator's activity packet developed by a park superintendent for Hammocks Beach State Park, providing "environmental education through a series of hands-on activities geared to Hammocks Beach State Park." It targets grades 6-8, meets North Carolina standard course of study objectives, and includes an introduction to reptiles, a vocabulary list, scheduling worksheet, parental permission form, evaluation forms, and appendices with sea turtle fact sheets and conservation information. (Bland (1995))

## Description
A 124-page educator's activity packet developed by a park superintendent for Hammocks Beach State Park, providing "environmental education through a series of hands-on activities geared to Hammocks Beach State Park." It targets grades 6-8, meets North Carolina standard course of study objectives, and includes an introduction to reptiles, a vocabulary list, scheduling worksheet, parental permission form, evaluation forms, and appendices with sea turtle fact sheets and conservation information.

#### Constraints
- Designed to be reproduced for use in North Carolina classrooms; other uses require crediting the N.C. Division of Parks and Recreation

### Target Learners
- Middle-grade students (grades 6-8) in North Carolina

### Target Learning Goals
- Sea turtle life cycle and adaptations
- Taxonomy and dichotomous keys
- Endangered species and human impact
- Resource management
