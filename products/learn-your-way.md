# Learn Your Way

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Learn Your Way is an experimental AI-augmented textbook learning experience from the LearnLM Team that transforms textbook content into personalized text, slides, narration, audio lessons, mind maps, and embedded assessments.

### Claims
- [In a randomized controlled trial, students learning with Learn Your Way scored higher than Digital Reader students on both immediate and three-day retention assessments](../claims/learn-your-way-rct-higher-immediate-retention-scores.md) [+M]
- [Across all learning-experience survey measures, Learn Your Way was rated more positively than the Digital Reader, with several differences statistically significant](../claims/learn-your-way-survey-more-positive-ratings.md) [+M]
- [Students using Learn Your Way scored significantly higher on an immediate recall assessment than students using a digital textbook](../claims/learn-your-way-better-immediate-recall.md) [+M]
- [Learn Your Way students also scored significantly higher on a delayed long-term recall assessment 3-7 days after learning](../claims/learn-your-way-better-long-term-recall.md) [+M]
- [Learn Your Way students reported more positive learning experiences than textbook students, especially on perceived usefulness, test-taking comfort, and perceived effectiveness](../claims/learn-your-way-positive-learning-experience.md) [+M]
