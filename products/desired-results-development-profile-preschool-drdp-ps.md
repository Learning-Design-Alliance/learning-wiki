## Description
The DRDP-PS (2010) is "a teacher rating of children's skills that has been used in the district for over 10 years." Within the ELTM it serves as the existing assessment that the CPAA is intended to complement, together expanding information available to teachers and parents regarding children's progress.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Completed as a teacher rating of children's skills
#### Constraints
- Used in the Milpitas district context for over 10 years

### Target Learners
- preschool children ages 3 to 5

### Target Learning Goals
- children's developmental skills as rated by teachers
