# Swift public-facing web resources: GRB Skymap site, GRB Lotto game, and Epo's Chronicles weekly webcomic

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A suite of SSU-maintained public websites providing gamma-ray-burst and space-science information, an astronomy guessing game, and a multilingual educational webcomic with supporting definitions and multimedia links.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Swift public-facing web resources: GRB Skymap site, GRB Lotto game, and the Epo's Chronicles weekly webcomic**: A suite of websites maintained by SSU and integrated with the mission site, providing public access to Swift E/PO materials. The GRB Skymap site serves the scientifically attentive public with short writeups of every GRB seen by Swift and other satellites plus positional information and finding charts; the GRB Lotto game invites the public to guess the closest sky location to the next GRB; and Epo's Chronicles is a weekly webcomic, translated into French, Spanish and Italian, whose monthly readership doubled in the past year and is "now read by over 60,000 unique visitors each year." (Lynn Cominsky et al. (2014))
