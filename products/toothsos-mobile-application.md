## Description
ToothSOS is a free International Association of Dental Traumatology mobile application providing guideline-based dental-trauma algorithms for the public and practitioners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ToothSOS mobile application (IADT, 2018)**: The ToothSOS app is a free mobile service created by the International Association of Dental Traumatology and launched in 2018. Per the review, it offers "guideline-based trauma algorithms aimed at the general public, and also providing practitioners easy access to relevant guidelines". The review suggests GDPs may develop greater familiarity with guidelines by using the app, and cites a study reporting improved dental students' TDI knowledge from its patient-directed section. (Eddery R. (2026))
