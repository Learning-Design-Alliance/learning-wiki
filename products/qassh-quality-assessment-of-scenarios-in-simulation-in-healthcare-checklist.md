# QASSH — Quality Assessment of Scenarios in Simulation in Healthcare checklist

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A dichotomous checklist developed by Rivière et al. for assessing the educational quality of immersive, procedural, and simulated-participant healthcare simulation scenario scripts before deployment.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **QASSH — Quality Assessment of Scenarios in Simulation in Healthcare checklist**: QASSH is a dichotomous (present/absent) checklist for assessing the quality of healthcare simulation scenario scripts before deployment. It "consists of a global section applicable to all simulation modalities and one section that is specific to each of the simulation modalities", with 24 global items in four categories and 10 (immersive, procedural) or 11 (SP) specific items, for total scores of 34 or 35. It functions as three distinct instruments, one per modality. (Rivière E et al. (2025))
