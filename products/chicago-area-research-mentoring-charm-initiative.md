# Chicago Area Research Mentoring (CHARM) initiative

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A school-based research-mentoring initiative run by Vikram V. Dwarkadas that introduces 11th-grade students to astrophysics, research methods, and STEM careers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CHARM research-mentoring initiative teaching astrophysics to 11th-grade honors students**: The Chicago Area Research Mentoring (CHARM) initiative, in which the author taught astronomy roughly every 4 weeks to 17 11th-grade Physics Honors students at the UC Woodlawn charter school. Topics included stellar evolution, deaths of massive stars and supernovae, which students are generally not exposed to. The article states CHARM "aims to develop the student's critical thinking, introduce them to astrophysical research methods and techniques, and prepare them for a career in STEM." (Vikram V Dwarkadas (2021))
