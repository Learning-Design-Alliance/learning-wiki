Nuffield Early Language Intervention (NELI)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A structured oral-language intervention for four- to five-year-old children with relatively poor spoken language, developed at the University of York and marketed with I CAN.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • The 30-week Nuffield Early Language Intervention improved the language skills of children with low language ability, equivalent to about four months of additional progress [+M]
  • The 20-week version of NELI improved language skills by an estimated two months of progress, though the evidence is less secure than for the 30-week version [+M]
