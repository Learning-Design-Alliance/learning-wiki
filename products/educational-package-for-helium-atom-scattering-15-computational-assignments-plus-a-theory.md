# Educational package for helium atom scattering: 15 computational assignments plus a theory handbook

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A freely available Cambridge-developed curriculum package of computational helium-atom-scattering investigations, a theory handbook, and supporting documentation for undergraduate and postgraduate research students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Educational package for helium atom scattering: 15 computational assignments plus a theory handbook**: A freely available resource package for emerging research students in helium atom scattering, whose focus is 15 computational investigations into areas of relevance in surface physics, accompanied by a theory handbook. The article states the assignments are "designed in such a way to partially bridge the gap from typical British undergraduate teaching to research". The package also contains a readme manual and the article itself. (E.L. Arnold et al. (2020))
