## Description
Flowscene is a node-based visual authoring tool for building and exploring quantum simulations by connecting physical and mathematical concepts, developed by the article's authors.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Node-based flowscene design: physical and mathematical concepts as connectable nodes**: The article's organizing design framework distills physical and mathematical objects and concepts into intuitive, individual nodes whose user-defined interconnectivity defines the simulation's behavior. Nodes have titles, contextual content, inputs and outputs; connections visualize dependency relations between parts of the simulation. Scopes compartmentalize nodes into customizable layers of abstraction, and collapsed Scopes function like any other node, letting users build customized nodes of arbitrary complexity. (Shaeema Zaman Ahmed et al. (2020))
