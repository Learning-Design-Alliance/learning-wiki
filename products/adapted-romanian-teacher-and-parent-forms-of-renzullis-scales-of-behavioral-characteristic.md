# Adapted Romanian teacher and parent forms of Renzulli's Scales of Behavioral Characteristics of Superior Students

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A Romanian adaptation of Renzulli's behavioral-characteristics rating scales, completed by teachers and parents to assess primary pupils' potential giftedness across learning, mathematics and sciences, creativity, leadership, and motivation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Adapted Romanian teacher and parent forms of Renzulli's Scales for rating behavioral characteristics of superior students**: Potential giftedness was estimated with two adapted forms (for teachers and parents) of Renzulli's Scales of Behavioral Characteristics of Superior Students, in the Romanian adaptation by C. Cretu (1999). The teacher form has 51 items and the parent form 46 items on six-point Likert scales covering "learning, mathematics and sciences, creativity, leadership and motivation"; internal consistency ranged from .66 to .94 (teacher form) and .87 to .92 (parent form). (Nicoleta Laura Popa (2015))
