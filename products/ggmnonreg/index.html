GGMnonreg¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes

Description¶

GGMnonreg is an R package that implements non-regularized Gaussian graphical-modeling methods for low-dimensional data, developed by Donald Williams.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Non-regularized graphical model methods are specifically designed for low-dimensional data common in the social-behavioral sciences [+W]
  • Regularization-based graphical modeling has key drawbacks, including difficulty obtaining valid parameter uncertainty and inflated false positive rates [+W]
  • The frequentist graphical LASSO has difficulty estimating centrality indices and uncertainty in these measures in symptom networks [+W]
