The "Find yourself" program¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Aydar M. Kalimullin and colleagues’ eight-lesson sensory-room intervention uses sensory equipment, relaxation activities, and peer group work to develop adolescents’ self-regulation, relaxation, cooperation, communication, and emotional comfort.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • The \"Find yourself\" sensory-room program significantly reduced the proportion of adolescents with a low level of subjective control in the experimental group (φ = 2.59, significant at the 1% level) [+W]
  • After the program, 100% of the experimental group showed a medium level of behavior self-regulation, with a significant change on the low and medium levels (φ = 2.59) [+W]
