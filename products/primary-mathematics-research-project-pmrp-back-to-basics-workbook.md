# Primary Mathematics Research Project (PMRP) Back to Basics workbook

## Description
A Primary Mathematics Research Project workbook and accompanying teacher manual that use diagnostic strand placement, graded arithmetic materials, daily mental-mathematics practice, and scheduled review, assessment, enrichment, and remediation for South African primary learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Primary Mathematics Research Project (PMRP) Back to Basics workbook with diagnostic strand placement and daily mental maths**: The PM material set is a learner workbook and teacher manual designed to move learners beyond unit counting and build automaticity in the four arithmetic operations. Teachers administer a diagnostic test and "place them into strands according to their achievement on the diagnostic test", with graded levels (Cow, Lion, Elephant, Goat) covering Grade 3 to Grade 6 level work on the same content. Each day starts with a 10 minute mental maths exercise; Day 5 lessons prescribe review, assessment, enrichment and remediation. The learner book is written in directly and is not amenable to self-study. (Brahm Fleisch et al. (2011))
