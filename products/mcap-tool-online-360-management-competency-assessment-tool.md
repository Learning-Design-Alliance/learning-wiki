## Description
A web-based 360-degree multisource assessment instrument developed by the research team for measuring health service managers' management competencies using self-, supervisor-, colleague-, and supervisee ratings.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MCAP Tool: online 360° management competency assessment tool**: The MCAP Tool is an online evidence-based management competency assessment tool comprising a 360° assessment involving self, supervisor, colleague and supervisee raters. It was developed and validated in Victoria between 2012 and 2014, hosted on a web-based platform built by the research team, and administers the 79 behavioural items covering the six MCAP competencies on a 7-point Likert scale. The article reports it "is a reliable and valid measure of HSM managerial competency" and demonstrates its use for identifying individual behavioural items requiring improvement for targeted professional development. (Liang Zhanming et al. (2018))
