## Description
WPadV4 is a multilingual cloud-based adaptive content-management and collaboration platform developed by the V4+ Academic Research Consortium Integrating Databases, Robotics and Language Technologies for research and learning teams.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **WPadV4 adaptive content management tool from the V4+ACARDC project**: The article describes WPadV4, educational software produced within the V4+ Academic Research Consortium Integrating Databases, Robotics and Language Technologies (V4+ACARDC). The consortium created "a complex system of IT support consisting of the technological network infrastructure, educational software WPadV4 and a didactics methodology" with multi-lingual support on a cloud-based platform. The tool supported adaptive content management so that research data were "findable, accessible, interoperable" for all partners, and the platform "proved to be suitable to meet these needs and to perform smoothly and intuitively". (Maiia V. Marienko et al. (2020))
