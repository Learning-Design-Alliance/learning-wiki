WritingTutor¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 5 claims (1 for, 4 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A web-based conversational tutoring system developed by Wambsganss et al. that supports university students' persuasive essay writing through dialogic guidance, adaptive feedback, and learner-control features.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • WritingTutor with high learner control was rated easier to use than static tutoring (TG1 vs CG), but not after Bonferroni correction. [~W]
  • No significant difference in essay-assessment learning gains between TG1 and CG. [~W]
  • High vs low learner control (TG1 vs TG2) showed no significant differences after pair-wise comparison. [~W]
  • Qualitative analysis suggests CTS dialog-based interaction already provides a natural level of learner control. [+W]
  • TG1 rated learning enjoyment higher than CG (d = 0.49), not significant after Bonferroni correction. [~W]
