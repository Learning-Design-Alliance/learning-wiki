## Description
CareerWISE is an online career-development and interpersonal problem-solving learning programme for women STEM doctoral students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CareerWISE four-step interpersonal problem solving model**: The CareerWISE learning environment teaches a specific model for managing interpersonal problems, shown in Table 1, grounded in problem-solving processes and cognitive-behavioral interventions. The model comprises four steps: "Step 1: Assess the problem", "Step 2: Specify the outcome you want", "Step 3: Strategize", and "Step 4: Execute and evaluate", each with sub-steps such as listing the facts, understanding the context, setting a concrete outcome, weighing strategies, and cycling back to Step 1 if the outcome was not achieved. The environment focuses on developing skills such as considering others' perspectives, managing emotional responses, and appropriately identifying stakeholders needed to apply the model to interpersonal situations. (Jennifer M. Bekki et al. (2014))
