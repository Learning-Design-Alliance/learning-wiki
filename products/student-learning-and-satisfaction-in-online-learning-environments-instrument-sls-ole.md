# Student Learning and Satisfaction in Online Learning Environments Instrument (SLS-OLE)

## Description
A 34-item, seven-point Likert questionnaire developed for measuring learners' perceptions of course structure, interaction, engagement, instructor presence, satisfaction, and perceived learning in online learning environments.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SLS-OLE instrument for student satisfaction with online learning environments**: The Student Learning and Satisfaction in Online Learning Environments Instrument (SLS-OLE) is a 34-item questionnaire used in this study to measure nursing students' satisfaction with the online BLS course. It covers six domains — "course structure, learner interaction, student engagement, instructor presence, student satisfaction, perceived learning" — on a seven-point Likert scale. The researchers translated it into Persian, validated it with ten professors (CVI > 0.7, CVR > 0.62), and reported Cronbach's alpha values from 0.72 to 0.94 across domains. (Ranjbar Fataneh et al. (2024))
