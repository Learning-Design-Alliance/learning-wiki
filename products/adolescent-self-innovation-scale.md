# Adolescent Self-Innovation Scale

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A self-report scale measuring adolescents' self-perceived innovativeness across idea generation, idea dissemination, and idea implementation, adapted in this study for remote-learning contexts.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Adolescent innovativeness as a three-dimensional construct: cognitive, technological, and social innovation**: The article defines adolescent innovativeness as "the ability to engage in creative thinking, problem-solving, and social responsibility within the contexts of cognitive development, technological application, and social adaptation". It organizes the construct into three primary dimensions. Cognitive innovation includes "creative thinking, critical analysis, and cognitive flexibility". Technological innovation is adolescents' "capacity to comprehend and apply emerging technologies effectively". Social innovation highlights adolescents' ability to "creatively and responsibly address societal challenges", nurtured through civic education and service-learning. The study operationalizes self-innovativeness with a 9-item scale divided into idea generation, idea dissemination, and idea implementation. (Qi Chunlin (2025))
