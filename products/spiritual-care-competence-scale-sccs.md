## Description
A 27-item original or 22-item Chinese self-report Likert-scale instrument developed by Van Leeuwen et al. and adapted by Wei et al. to assess clinical nurses' spiritual-care competence across six dimensions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Spiritual Care Competence Scale (SCCS) and its Chinese version**: The SCCS, translated and revised by Van Leeuwen et al. (2009) from the Dutch version, evaluates the spiritual care competence of clinical nurses. The original has "six dimensions" — assessment and implementation, professional development and quality improvement, support, referral, spiritual attitude towards patients, and communication — with 27 items scored on a five-level Likert method (total 27–110). The Chinese version (Wei et al. 2017) has 22 items, total 22–110, Cronbach's α 0.974. (Kaiyue Wu et al. (2025))
