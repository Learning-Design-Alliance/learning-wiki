Probability Achievement Test (PAT)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A 15-question open-ended probability achievement test developed by Ömer Sinan Can for assessing pre-service elementary mathematics teachers' understanding of sample space, counting, permutations, combinations, and probability.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Pre-service teachers taught probability with the ABSL approach achieved significantly higher post-test scores than those taught with traditional methods [+M]
  • Both ABSL and traditionally taught groups showed statistically significant pre-test to post-test achievement gains in probability [+M]
