# Program of Critical Reading Instruction (PCRI)

## Description
A three-stage critical-reading instructional program based on Goatly (2000), designed to develop learners’ analysis, interpretation, inference, and ideological reading of texts.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Program of Critical Reading Instruction (PCRI), a three-stage critical reading program from Goatly (2000)**: PCRI is the instructional program implemented in the experimental group, drawn from Goatly (2000) and delivered over 18 course hours arranged as a three-hour course structure. The article describes it as consisting of three stages: "'Code Declination and the Description of the Text (What is meant in the text?)'", interpretation and inference of the text, and determining "'The Ideology behind the first and second stages and the Determination of the Explanations'". Instruction was conducted mostly through the program's critical reading activities. (Aysegul Karabay (2015))

### Claims
- [After critical reading instruction, the experimental group preferred reviewing over summarizing an ironical text while the control group did not](../claims/pcri-shifts-summarizing-to-reviewing.md) [+M]
- [After PCRI, most experimental-group teachers inferred the deeper ironic meaning of the text while most control-group teachers took it literally](../claims/pcri-deeper-meaning-ironical-text.md) [+M]
