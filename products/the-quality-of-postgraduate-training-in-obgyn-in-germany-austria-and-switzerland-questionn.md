# The quality of postgraduate training in OBGYN in Germany, Austria and Switzerland questionnaire

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 30-question web-based Likert-scale questionnaire designed by the OEGGG, DGGG and SGGG trainee networks and circulated by ETH Zurich to assess residents' perceptions of postgraduate OBGYN training quality.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Trinational web-based survey on the quality of postgraduate OBGYN training (30 questions, 422 residents)**: A web-based questionnaire entitled "The quality of postgraduate training in OBGYN in Germany, Austria and Switzerland", designed by the trainee networks of the OEGGG, DGGG and SGGG and circulated by ETH Zurich. It contained 30 questions covering demographics, workload, practical and surgical skills, mentoring and feedback culture, and training satisfaction, answered on 7-point Likert scales. After four reminders, 422 residents responded (97 Austrian, 209 German, 116 Swiss), with a response rate of up to 55%. (Meister Tara et al. (2023))
