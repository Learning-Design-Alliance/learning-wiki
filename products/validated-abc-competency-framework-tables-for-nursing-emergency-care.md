# Validated ABC competency framework tables for nursing emergency care

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A competency framework developed and expert-validated by Miranda Fernanda Berchelli Girão et al. for progressively assessing undergraduate nursing students' and nurses' airway, breathing/circulation, and hemodynamic emergency-care competencies.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Validated ABC competency framework tables across three progression levels (student pre-internship, intern, nurse)**: The artifact is a set of tables of competency frameworks for nursing care in airway, breathing/circulation and hemodynamic-state approaches in urgent situations, presented in figures for each ABC item. Each cell "reveals a competency framework to be demonstrated by the students within their level of progression", and frameworks are "present in greater number mainly at the initial levels of training", so "the level 3 (nurse) has consequently a higher level of empty cells". The progression levels were set by expert consensus based on the national curricular guidelines requiring a supervised internship in the last two semesters. (Miranda Fernanda Berchelli Girão et al. (2018))
