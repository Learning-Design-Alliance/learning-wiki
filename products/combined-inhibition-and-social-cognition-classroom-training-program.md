# Combined inhibition and social cognition classroom training program

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A classroom training programme for kindergarten and first-grade children that combines inhibition and executive-function activities with Theory of Mind and social-information-processing activities, delivered through 18 weekly sessions by a trained psychologist with teacher follow-up.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Combined inhibition and social cognition classroom training program (18 weekly sessions with metacognitive characters)**: A manualized classroom program of "eighteen 45-min sessions implemented in the classroom in the presence of the teacher", delivered once a week over 6 months by a trained psychologist. It trains four inhibition components (predominant response, interruption of an ongoing response, external distractors, impulsivity control) through three metacognitive characters — Mr. Stop, the Detective and the Statue — plus ToM and SIP activities of increasing difficulty, with weekly teacher follow-up activities and record books. (Honoré N et al. (2020))
