# Science-in-CTE intervention

## Description
A North Dakota agricultural-education intervention developed by CTE and science teacher partners that provides science-enhanced lesson plans, a seven-element pedagogical model, peer review, and intensive professional development.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Science-in-CTE seven-element pedagogical model and science-enhanced agricultural education lessons**: The Science-in-CTE intervention consists of a seven-element pedagogical model and 15 science-enhanced agricultural education lesson plans developed by agricultural education/science teacher partners during seven days of intensive professional development. Lessons were "developed by the agricultural education/science teacher partners and evaluated by their peers," and each experimental teacher had the opportunity to teach all 15 lessons. The model was adapted from the Math-in-CTE study's seven-element pedagogical model, and the follow-up study assessed whether teachers continued using both the model and the lessons after the pilot ended. (Wendi Mizer Stachler et al. (2013))
