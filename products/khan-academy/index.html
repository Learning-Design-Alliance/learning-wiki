Khan Academy¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Khan Academy is a free nonprofit online learning platform developed and operated by Khan Academy, offering instructional videos, practice problems, progress tracking, and motivational features across academic subjects.

Components¶

  • Khan Academy as a computer-based supplemental mathematics intervention: Khan Academy is a large, free open educational resource offering video-based lectures paired with problems for students to solve, with points and badges that "serve to provide positive reinforcement and encouragement." In this study it was used by seventh graders for a minimum of 30 minutes per class over four weeks as a self-paced, self-directed supplement, with students free to choose topics, simulating typical at-home use. The platform internally tracks metrics including minutes spent, points attained, and topics mastered. (Daniel P. Kelly et al. (2017))

Claims¶

  • Khan Academy use as supplemental mathematics instruction produced no statistically significant difference in post-assessment scores compared to a combined control group [+M]
  • Khan Academy internal metrics of topics mastered and points attained were positively correlated with student test scores, but minutes spent was not [+M]
