Pintner's Mental Survey¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A standardized group mental-ability test consisting of six tests for school children ages 6–16, developed by Rudolf Pintner and normed on more than 3,000 children with percentile norms.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Mental index ratings on the group test showed close correspondence over a two-year interval (r = .72, rank correlation .77) among 46 retested children [+W]
  • Against Stanford Binet criteria for 300 grade 3–6 children, the mental index correlated .47, the educational index .52, crude score with mental age .80, mental with educational index .62, and Difference with I.Q. .21 [+W]
