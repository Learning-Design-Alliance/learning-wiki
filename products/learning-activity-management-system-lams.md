## Description
Learning Activity Management System (LAMS) is a software platform for authoring, delivering, coordinating, and tracking collaborative learning activities, developed and maintained by LAMS Foundation and its community.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Learning Activity Management System (LAMS) platform for delivering IRAT, TRAT and AES in TBL closing sessions.**: The IRAT, TRAT and AES are delivered using an online platform (Learning Activity Management System; LAMS) which allows facilitators to track student progress in real-time, including identifying questions or concepts the cohort struggled with. Students have individual log-in details and answered the 10 IRAT questions independently on their personal devices, with a team leader chosen each week to enter the teams' 10 TRAT and AES responses. LAMS also allows students to submit 'burning questions' for the expert panel. (Drovandi Aaron et al. (2026))

### Claims
