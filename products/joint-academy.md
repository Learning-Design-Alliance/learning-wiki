# Joint Academy

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Joint Academy is a digital, individualized osteoarthritis self-management programme providing exercise, education, quizzes, and clinician communication, delivered by Joint Academy.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Joint Academy digital OA self-management program**: A "digital, structured and individualized treatment programme for people with hip or knee OA" delivering daily neuromuscular exercises adjusted for complexity and difficulty, weekly education themes of 1-5 lessons with quizzes on lesson material (70 lessons over 48 weeks), and "continuous access to and dialogue with a physiotherapist through an encrypted chat function, and/or telephone". Participants log exercise completion, rate difficulty, and answer quizzes correctly to complete lessons; outcomes follow the ICHOM standard set for hip and knee OA. (Dahlberg Leif E. et al. (2020))
