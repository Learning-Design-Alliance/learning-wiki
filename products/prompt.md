## Description
PROMPT (Practical Obstetric Multi-Professional Training) is a simulation-based obstetric-emergency training programme with train-the-trainer delivery and course materials for local multidisciplinary maternity teams.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PROMPT Train-the-Trainers (T3) programme and Course-in-a-box implementation package**: The intervention evaluated in THISTLE is the 2-day PROMPT Train the Trainers (T3) programme, which "includes a demonstration PROMPT course, a Train-The-Trainers (T3) day and the local use of the PROMPT Course-in-a-box (Second Edition)". Teams of four multi-professional clinicians (2 midwives, 1 obstetrician, 1 anaesthetist) per unit attend T3 at a simulation centre, then receive a Course-in-a-box containing a Trainers Manual, Course Manual and DVD with tutorials, checklists, algorithms and demonstration videos, plus telephone/email support, to run annual local one-day multi-professional courses covering emergencies such as eclampsia, post-partum haemorrhage and shoulder dystocia. (Lenguerrand Erik et al. (2017))
