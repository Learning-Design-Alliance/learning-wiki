# SARA (Student Advice Recommender Agent)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
SARA is a personalized learning-support and advising system developed to predict course success and direct undergraduate students to appropriate learning resources and activities.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SARA (Student Advice Recommender Agent) personalized learning support system.**: SARA is a learning support system that "provides personalized advice" and "directs the student to appropriate learning supports and resources by mining data from student academic history, personal history (including demographics), and current activity." It uses "a predictive model of student academic success in specific courses" and was introduced in the Introduction to Biology course alongside the MindTap platform. In this study, SARA served as the delivery vehicle for the persuasive social visualizations, and the persuasive intervention was placed into SARA's general advice-giving system to trigger reflection and inspire engagement in MindTap activities. (Orji et al. (2021))
