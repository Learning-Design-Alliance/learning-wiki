# Mentice VIST C vascular interventional simulator

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A high-fidelity vascular-intervention simulation system made by Mentice AB that reproduces catheter and guidewire manipulation in virtual patient anatomies for neuroangiography training and research.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **VIST LAB vascular interventional simulator setup for neuroangiography training and workload research**: The high-fidelity vascular intervention system trainer VIST C (Mentice AB) integrated in the VIST LAB, "a stationary unit that resembles a vascular intervention workspace including a patient silhouette and multiple screens". An insertion sheath detects pushing, pulling and rotation of wires and catheters via three sensors; catheter movements are transferred into a virtual patient anatomy displayed in real time. The article used it for ten diagnostic cerebral angiography cases per participant, with sessions limited to 2h to avoid fatigue. (Hinzmann Dominik et al. (2022))
- **Mentice VIST-C endovascular simulator with 3D virtual coronary anatomy overlay**: The Mentice VIST-C (Gothenburg, Sweden) is a portable, high-fidelity endovascular simulator used in this trial as the intervention platform. The article describes it as "a portable, high-fidelity endovascular simulator, with good construct and concurrent validity, used in catheter training", connected to a monitor, laptop running VIST-8 software, foot switches and a contrast syringe. Its distinctive studied feature is the ability to switch to 3D visualized virtual anatomy overlaying the coronary anatomy on the fluoroscopic image, alongside virtual manipulation of the patient table and C-arm. (Lee Kwan S. et al. (2022))
- **Mentice VIST-Lab high-fidelity coronary angiography simulator session**: The Mentice VIST-Lab CA simulator is a high-fidelity interface with a mannequin, two monitors, and joysticks, mimicking a modern catheterization laboratory; users can "switch between the angiographic view and a 3D view to better identify the take-off and location of the coronary arteries". In the trial, each SIM student received the same 30-min theoretical course as controls plus 15 min of individual simulator manipulation on a single case, in sessions of 8 to 10 students. (Fischer Q et al. (2018))
