# FliP-CoIn: a bilingual English-German multiple-choice and ranking-task concept inventory for flight physics

## Description
FliP-CoIn is a bilingual English-German formative concept inventory developed by Florian Genz for assessing students' conceptual understanding and misconceptions in flight physics and fluid dynamics.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **FliP-CoIn: a bilingual English-German multiple-choice and ranking-task concept inventory for flight physics**: FliP-CoIn is a research-based assessment instrument for improving fluid dynamics and flight physics learning and teaching. It uses "a combination of multiple-choice forced-select questions, and ranking tasks" to assess key flight physics concepts. It was developed simultaneously in English and German, which the author says inspired better items and wording that works in both learning cultures, and it is disseminated via PhysPort and a University of Cologne site. (Florian Genz (2025))
