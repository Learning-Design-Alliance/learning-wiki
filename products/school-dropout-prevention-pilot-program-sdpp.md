## Description
A five-year USAID-funded, multi-country dropout-prevention programme piloted in Cambodia, India, Tajikistan, and Timor-Leste that uses attendance, performance, and teacher-observation data to identify at-risk students and provide case-managed support.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **School Dropout Prevention Pilot Program (SDPP) Early Warning System intervention**: The SDPP is a five-year, multi-country program funded by USAID that pilots and evaluates dropout prevention interventions in Cambodia, India, Tajikistan, and Timor-Leste. Its core component is an Early Warning System that "uses student attendance and course performance data, along with teacher observations on other local factors associated with school-leaving, to identify students at highest risk of dropping out of school." Staff then monitor these students using a case management approach, providing additional support and outreach when necessary. (Murray et al. (2014))
