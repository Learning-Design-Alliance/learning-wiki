# Instructional Quality Assurance Model in Nursing Science

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A six-standard, 18-indicator framework developed for Thai higher-education nursing divisions to assure curriculum, instructional-support, instructor, teaching, assessment, and student/graduate quality.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **A systematic instructional quality assurance model for nursing science consisting of input, process and output across principles, concepts and practice**: The developed model is a systematic instructional quality assurance model for nursing science. As the article states, "The instructional quality assurance model in nursing science was systematic consisting of input, process and output. The operation was based on 3 domains: principles, concepts and practice." It comprises 6 standards and 18 indicators, with the curriculum standard holding 3 indicators, instructional support 4, nursing instructor 2, instruction 4, learning evaluation and assessment 3, and nursing student/graduate 2, plus 21 elements of guidelines. It aligns with ONESQA standards 1, 5 and 6, Nursing Council instructional elements and the Thailand Qualification Framework. (Haruthai Ajpru et al. (2008))
