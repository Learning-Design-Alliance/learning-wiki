# Wong and Law Emotional Intelligence Scale (WLEIS)

## Description
The WLEIS is a 16-item self-report assessment developed by Wong and Law for measuring four dimensions of emotional intelligence: self-emotional appraisal, others' emotional appraisal, regulation of emotion, and use of emotion.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Wong and Law Emotional Intelligence Scale (WLEIS) with GHQ-12 screening in a fresh-undergraduate census**: The study measured EI with the Wong and Law Emotional Intelligence Scale, "a widely used tool based on the ability model of EI" with four dimensions: self-emotional appraisal, others' emotional appraisal, regulation of emotion, and use of emotion. Respondents rated 16 statements on a 7-point scale, and those scoring 48 points or more (50%) were classified as above-average EI. Psychiatric morbidity was screened with the GHQ-12 using binary 0-0-1-1 scoring, a score of 3 or above indicating morbidity. The census approach surveyed fresh undergraduates from November 2023 to January 2024. (Falade Joshua et al. (2025))
