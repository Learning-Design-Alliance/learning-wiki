# Immersive Technology Evaluation Measure (ITEM)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
ITEM is a 40-item multidomain self-report questionnaire developed by its original authors to evaluate learner experience in immersive technology-enhanced learning environments across immersion, motivation and educational value, workload, usability, and reflective learning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Immersive Technology Evaluation Measure (ITEM) five-domain framework for user experience of immersive technology in medical education**: The ITEM is a validated instrument developed to capture the user experience of immersive technology in medical education, organized into five domains: "immersion, motivation, cognitive load, system usability, and debriefing." The domains were derived from quantitative and qualitative content analyses of survey responses plus an online consensus meeting with stakeholders including doctors, allied health educators, technicians, and simulation administrators. In this study, the five domains served as preconceived themes structuring deductive thematic analysis of medical students' interview narratives, yielding 5 themes, 10 subthemes, and 23 codes. (Chartash David et al. (2026))
