# Talk for Writing whole-school programme

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Talk for Writing is a whole-school primary writing programme developed and delivered through the Primary Writing Project professional-development package, with training, implementation requirements, shared writing practices, text frameworks, and formative assessment.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Talk for Writing whole-school programme and its Primary Writing Project non-negotiables**: Talk for Writing is a universal, whole-school approach to teaching primary writing, delivered within the Primary Writing Project professional development package. Schools commit to non-negotiable components; the article states "The main non- negotiables contained seven elements related to written text production", including adoption of the 3Is, daily shared writing, visible common practice such as story maps and boxing up, systematic word and sentence level teaching, a school-wide text framework, writing for audiences and purposes, and regular formative assessment. Each school appointed a literacy lead responsible for implementation, supported by leadership training days and whole-school conferences. (Julie Dockrell et al. (2015))
