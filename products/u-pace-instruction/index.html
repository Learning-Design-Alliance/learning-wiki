U-Pace instruction¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q4 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

U-Pace instruction is a named online instructional model developed by Fleming and colleagues that combines concept mastery with instructor-initiated, data-tailored assistance for supporting undergraduate course learning.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • U-Pace instruction produced greater learning on a proctored cumulative exam than concept mastery, amplified assistance, and face-to-face instruction in an RCT [+M]
  • Neither concept mastery alone nor amplified assistance alone produced greater learning than face-to-face instruction [+M]
