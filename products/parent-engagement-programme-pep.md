# Parent Engagement Programme (PEP)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A low-cost, school-based programme developed by Miller et al. that uses scheduled text messages to engage parents with pupils' tests, homework, and lesson content.

## Components

### Claims
- [Texting parents about tests, homework and lesson content produced a small positive effect on maths attainment (ES 0.067) in a cluster RCT](../claims/pep-texting-small-maths-attainment-gain.md) [+M]
- [Texting parents reduced pupil absenteeism (ES -0.054, about half a day) even though no texts concerned attendance](../claims/pep-texting-reduced-absenteeism.md) [+M]
- [The PEP texting intervention showed no evidence of an impact on science attainment](../claims/pep-no-science-attainment-impact.md) [-M]
