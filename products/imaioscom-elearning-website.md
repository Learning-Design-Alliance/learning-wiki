## Description
IMAIOS.com is a paid medical eLearning website operated by IMAIOS that provides dynamic, interactive anatomical images for learning and identifying sectional anatomy structures.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **IMAIOS.com eLearning website for learning sectional anatomy**: IMAIOS.com is a paid eLearning website providing dynamic (scrollable) and interactive (selectable labels) transverse CT images for learning sectional anatomy. Participants used it to identify specific anatomical structures in transverse CT images of the chest and abdomen. The article reports that "Groups A and B assessed the IMAIOS.com website with mean SUS scores always above 68, the threshold of usability." Static images of the scanned body functioned as cross-references alongside the dynamic CT images. (Fonseca Guilherme Reis Borges Coelho et al. (2026))
