## Description
CourseKata is a non-profit education R&D platform whose online textbooks are "fully instrumented to yield data relevant to students' interactions, learning, and thinking as they progress through the course". It collects trace data via the enGauge tool, over 1,500 embedded auto-scored formative assessments and coding activities, and embedded self-report surveys, storing de-identified data in a warehouse outside institutional LMSs.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivery through an LMS with teacher access to identified student data; de-identified data shared under rigorous privacy standards via the SafeInsights research hub.
#### Constraints
- End-of-chapter quizzes exist only through Chapter 8 and chapter summaries only through Chapter 9, so those features are set to 0 in later chapters.

### Target Learners
- introductory statistics students in colleges and high schools

### Target Learning Goals
- learning introductory statistics and data science with R programming
