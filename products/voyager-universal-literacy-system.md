# Voyager Universal Literacy System®

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 2 against) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Voyager Universal Literacy System® is a core reading curriculum developed for students through grade three, combining teacher-led individual, small-group, and whole-class literacy instruction with computer-based practice in phonological skills, comprehension, fluency, language development, and writing.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Voyager Universal Literacy System® core reading program**: A core reading program "designed to help students learn to read at or above grade level by the end of the third grade." It uses "individual reading instruction, higher-level comprehension activities, problem solving, and writing," plus computer-based practice in phonological skills, comprehension, fluency, language development, and writing, delivered in whole classroom, small group, and independent settings. First published in 2000, it has been used in 360 districts in 22 states, with almost 17,500 teachers and over 331,000 students. (Voyager Universal Literacy System® — WWC Intervention Report (2007))
