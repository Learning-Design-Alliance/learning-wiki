## Description
Thesis Writer is a domain-specific online learning environment with an academic-text editor, developed to support instruction, planning, and genre mastery in thesis and research-article writing.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Thesis Writer (TW): domain-specific online scaffolding environment with an academic-text editor**: Thesis Writer is described as "a domain-specific, online learning environment for the scaffolding of student academic writing, combined with an online editor optimized for producing academic text." It targets theses and research article writing using the IMRD scheme rather than the essay genre, and unlike automated essay scoring tools it focuses on "instruction, planning, and genre mastery" rather than feedback. It was evaluated with first-year business administration students writing research proposals. (Rapp et al. (2018))
