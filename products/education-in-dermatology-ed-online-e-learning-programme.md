# Education in Dermatology (ED) online E-learning programme

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A dermatologist-developed online programme run for GP residents, comprising 31 image-rich clinical cases with multiple-choice questions, answers, feedback, and links to further learning materials.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Moodle-based twelve-week e-learning dermatology module with weekly texts, videos, and discussion forums**: A twelve-week e-learning module in dermatology built on the open-source Moodle learning management system, run in parallel with face-to-face activities. As the article describes it, "A new text, video and online discussion forum, which addressed the same content as the face-to-face activities, was available each week." Content covered skin physical examination, lesion morphology terminology, and skin physiology and anatomy, developed from practice guidelines, textbooks, and primary journal articles. Students could also receive tutor feedback through discussion boards or direct messages. (Silva Cristiana Silveira et al. (2011))
