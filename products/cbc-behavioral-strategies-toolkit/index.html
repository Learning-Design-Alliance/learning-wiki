CBC Behavioral Strategies Toolkit¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A consultation-oriented behavioral intervention toolkit developed for Conjoint Behavioral Consultation that provides standardized, collaborative strategies and individualized implementation plans for addressing young students' disruptive behavior and promoting prosocial behavior.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • CBC significantly improves directly observed classroom behaviors, including on-task, off-task, appropriate social behaviors, and motor movements, in rural classrooms [+W]
  • CBC and behavioral plan implementation were delivered with high fidelity in the rural trial [+W]
  • The BASC School Problems effect is driven by significant improvements in Learning Problems and Attention Problems subscales favoring CBC [+W]
  • The teacher-parent relationship improves under CBC and partially mediates CBC effects on several student outcomes [+W]
  • CBC improves teacher-reported school problems among rural K-3 students with disruptive behaviors more than business as usual [+W]
