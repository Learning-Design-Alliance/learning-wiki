# 25-item Chemistry Achievement Test (CAT) on stoichiometry and mole concept

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A 25-item researcher-developed chemistry achievement test measuring Senior Secondary 2 students' understanding of stoichiometry and the mole concept, used as a pre-test and post-test in the study.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Problem Solving Achievement Test in Chemistry (PSATC)**: The PSATC is a 25-item multiple-choice test constructed as pretest and post-test to measure students' ability to solve numerical chemistry problems using the mole concept. Items were adapted from WAEC and NECO Senior School Certificate Examinations, rated against a Bloom's taxonomy table of specification, and field-tested on 25 SSIII students; items with difficulty index p between 0.32 and 0.72 were retained. Reliability via Kuder Richardson formula 21 was 0.92. (Bamidele et al. (2013))
