# Prototype online survey/rubric tool for eliciting instructor and researcher beliefs

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A public online survey and interactive rubric tool that dynamically updates response options to elicit instructor and researcher beliefs and focus collaboration on improving course components.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Prototype online survey/rubric tool for eliciting instructor and researcher beliefs to facilitate collaboration**: The authors "present a prototype survey tool for instructors intended to facilitate practitioner-researcher matches and successful collaborations." The tool's questions elicit "instructors and researchers respective beliefs about" quantifiable measures, behaviors, data collection, and which modular components instructors are open to having modified. "All questions have both a free response open field – to get rich qualitative feedback and personal responses – as well as a list of options which is dynamically updated using responses of instructors." It is available at a public URL and used with an interactive online rubric to focus discussion. (Joseph Jay Williams et al. (2015))
