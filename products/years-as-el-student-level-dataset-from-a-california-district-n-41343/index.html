Years-as-EL student-level dataset from a California district (N = 41,343)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), q2–q3 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A student-level California district dataset used to analyze English learners' years classified as EL, course taking, English proficiency, credit recovery, and graduation outcomes.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • High school course-taking differs substantially among English learners depending on years spent as an EL [+W]
  • Years-as-EL course-taking differences disappear once eighth-grade test scores are considered [+W]
  • The program's impact was estimated with a difference-in-differences-in-differences approach on student-level data from a large urban California district [+W]
  • Summer credit recovery shows suggestive evidence of positive effects on English proficiency for newcomer ELs [+W]
  • Summer credit recovery increased the number of math, English Language Arts, science, and social science classes taken by newcomer EL students [+W]
  • Effects of summer credit recovery on 4- and 5-year graduation rates for newcomer ELs were imprecisely estimated [+W]
