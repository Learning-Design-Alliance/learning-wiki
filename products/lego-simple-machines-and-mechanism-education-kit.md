## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Lego simple machines and mechanism education kit for early childhood engineering instruction**: The Lego simple machines and mechanism education kit is a physical construction set used as the core learning environment in this case study. The article states the kit "contains more than 200 bricks and elements that can be joined to create structures and mechanisms" and that its components "can be used to teach the principles and functioning of various types of simple machines such as wheels and axles, pulleys, levers and gears." It served as the medium for hands-on design-and-build activities in a summer camp for children aged 8-10. (Vishnu Agrawal (2017))
