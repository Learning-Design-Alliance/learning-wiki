Interaction Equivalency Theorem Indicator¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A Miyazoe et al. student inventory that measures learners’ forced-choice priorities among teacher, student, and content interaction across face-to-face, online, course, and subject contexts.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Students prioritize teacher interaction for face-to-face learning but content interaction for online learning [+W]
  • In a survey of 236 blended-learning students, learners readily ranked teacher, student and content interaction, which the authors read as supporting both theses of the Interaction Equivalency Theorem [+W]
