# Spanish version of the Fresno test of EBP competence

## Description
A Spanish-translated and validated open-ended performance assessment developed by Argimon-Pallàs et al. to measure novice health-care learners’ evidence-based-practice knowledge and skills through clinical scenarios, scored with standardised rubrics.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Spanish version of the Fresno test of EBP competence (score range 0-212)**: An open-ended performance instrument translated and validated into Spanish, including assessment of responsiveness. It presents two clinical scenarios and asks candidates to formulate focused questions, select study designs, name information resources, write a PubMed search strategy, appraise validity and applicability, and calculate indices such as number needed to treat. Short answers are scored with standardised rubrics; the maximum possible score is 212 points. (Argimon-Pallàs Josep M et al. (2011))
