Better Conversations with Aphasia (BCA)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A conversation-therapy intervention package using conversation analysis and video feedback, developed for speech and language therapists working with people with aphasia and their conversation partners.

Components¶

  • Better Conversations with Aphasia (BCA) e-learning resource: Better Conversations with Aphasia is a standard intervention package underpinned by conversation analysis for speech and language therapists delivering conversation therapy to people with aphasia and their conversation partners. The resource "also includes online self-study for SLTs delivering the intervention", using videos of BCA intervention sessions so SLTs reflect on facilitative neutral questions such as 'what is happening here?' when opening video-feedback discussions. Evaluations showed significant positive interactional change for both patients and partners. (Pilnick Alison et al. (2018))

Claims¶

  • CST interventions underpinned by conversation analysis show improved interactional outcomes in neurology, psychiatry and aphasia settings [+M]
