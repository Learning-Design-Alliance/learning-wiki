## Description
A curated resource listing ways to measure middle grades students' math outcomes in a "valid, reliable, and accessible way". It covers three domains: math skills and knowledge, math perceptions and beliefs, and math use beyond the classroom. Mathematica and a panel of researchers in math instruction and measurement developed it for Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation grantees improving middle grades math outcomes.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Measures listed are intended to assess middle grades students' math outcomes validly, reliably, and accessibly
#### Constraints
- Developed for Gates Foundation grantees working with middle grades students who are Black, Latino, and/or experiencing poverty

### Target Learners
- middle grades math students, including students who are Black, Latino, and/or experiencing poverty

### Target Learning Goals
- middle grades mathematics skills and knowledge
- math perceptions and beliefs
- math use beyond the classroom
