# AMT mobile planetarium programme

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A mobile planetarium outreach and astronomy-education programme run by the Africa Millimetre Telescope with NOVA, Radboud University, the University of Namibia, and the Rössing Foundation for schools in remote areas of Namibia.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **AMT mobile planetarium programme visiting remote Namibian schools**: The Africa Millimetre Telescope's mobile planetarium project aims to enhance astronomy outreach and education in Namibia by travelling to schools, including those in remote areas. In 2019 the AMT joined with NOVA, Radboud University, the University of Namibia and the Rössing Foundation and "spent a week travelling to schools in remote areas in northern and eastern Namibia". The project now sits under the AMT's Social Impact Plan. (Hannah Dalgleish et al. (2021))
