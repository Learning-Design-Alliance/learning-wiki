Remembering Rules and Regulation Picture Task (RRRP)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A brief, direct assessment developed by Day et al. for measuring children's working memory, attentional flexibility, and inhibitory control through ordered and rule-switching block-placement tasks.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Fall RRRP scores are significantly associated with working memory and attention measures, and its wait-for-go score with inhibitory-control ratings, in third graders [+M]
  • Fall RRRP predicted spring reading outcomes, with Part 1 predicting decoding and comprehension and Part 2 predicting vocabulary and gains in comprehension and vocabulary [+M]
