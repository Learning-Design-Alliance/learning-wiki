kar2ouche¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 qualitative), q2–q3 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

An electronic storyboarding tool that lets pupils construct visual representations of texts using characters, images, backgrounds, speech and thought bubbles, and captions.

Components¶

  • kar2ouche storyboarding tool for visualizing Macbeth: kar2ouche is "an electronic storyboarding tool, which allows pupils to construct their own visual representations of Macbeth in a series of frames". Pupils insert characters, pictures and backgrounds, adjust size, position and pose, and add speech bubbles (for Shakespeare's dialogue), thought bubbles (for their interpretation), and a caption box (for their reasons). It computerises traditional storyboarding to simplify frame construction and make Shakespeare more enjoyable, while supporting exam preparation. (Christian Greiffenhagen (2011))
  • Computer-based cartooning tool with posable characters, camera controls and visual storytelling prompts: The tool lets users build sequences of still shots from backgrounds, clipart and character artwork; characters can be "'posed' via one interaction mode which allows limbs in a skeleton to be dragged with the mouse, and 'warped' via another interaction mode which allows simple manipulation of vector control points". It provides camera point-of-view controls, a sequence bar with undo/redo and a full-screen slide show, and optional "visual storytelling prompts" offering ideas, character and camera help per story stage. No typing interface for captions is provided, deliberately emphasising purely visual communication. (M. Madden et al. (2007))

Claims¶

  • Pupils often neglected the caption box although they were aware of it, displaying a divergent orientation to the teacher's aims [~M] (pupils left kar2ouche's caption box empty)
  • Making rounds minimizes off-task behaviour because pupils monitor for the teacher's approach and adjust their behaviour [+M] (observed in kar2ouche lessons)
  • No preparation process (software cartooning, paper cartooning, or teacher's normal planning) produced clearly different written stories [~M]
