# Level-Points online examiner training platform

## Description
A custom-built online platform developed by Zhang Lina et al. for surgical-examiner calibration through sequential competency modules, point-based progression, real-time scoring-deviation feedback, and progress tracking.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **“Level-Points” online examiner training platform with level-based progression and point-reward system**: A custom-built online platform delivering examiner calibration training in three sequential phases: pre-assessment, level-based and point-reward training, and post-assessment. "The "Level-Points" training model integrates two complementary mechanisms": five competency-dimension modules are unlocked sequentially like game levels, and examiners earn points for scoring accuracy (e.g., 2 points for a deviation within 15 points of the reference score), with a minimum of 2 cumulative points required to unlock the next dimension. The platform provides real-time numerical and visual feedback on scoring deviations and tracks progress through a personalized dashboard. (Zhang Lina et al. (2026))
