## Description
RULER is a social-emotional learning programme developed and run by the Yale Center for Emotional Intelligence that teaches recognizing, understanding, labeling, expressing, and regulating emotions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **RULER social–emotional learning approach adopted districtwide in PUSD**: RULER is an SEL approach PUSD implemented as a primary objective to enhance student achievement. The name is "an acronym for Recognizing, Understanding, Labeling, Expressing, and Regulating," and it "focuses on developing these five key social–emotional intelligence skills." The district's initiative aims to establish a shared social–emotional vocabulary throughout all grades and schools within the district, positioning SEL as a Tier 1 universal support under Project Cal-Well. (Paradise Unified School District: Building School Mental Health Systems to Support Healing et al. (2024))
- **RULER approach: a systemic SEL approach based on emotional intelligence theory with a theory of change starting from adult training**: RULER (Recognizing, Understanding, Labeling, Expressing, Regulating) is described as "a systemic approach based on emotional intelligence theory" that "provides a series of tools to the entire educational community" to develop five emotion skills. Its "theory of change is primarily based on the training of adults", inclusion of RULER skills in school culture, integration of tools in pedagogy, and subsequent coursework adapted to students' development. The four tools named are the Charter, Mood Meter, Meta-Moment, and Blueprint. (Castillo-Gualda et al. (2023))
