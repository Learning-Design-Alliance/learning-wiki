## Description
A course-based near-peer mentorship programme in which trained undergraduate mentors provide psychosocial, instrumental, and academic support to first-year biology majors.

- **BIOME conceptual model of course-based near-peer mentorship: psychosocial, instrumental, and academic support driving academic habit complexity, performance, and retention**: The article presents a conceptual model (Figure 1) in which near-peer mentors provide three theoretically grounded support types: "psychosocial support, instrumental support, and academic support". The model posits that mentors who "make explicit effective disciplinary behaviors and practices" promote mentee adoption of new habits, increasing academic habit complexity, which in turn improves grades and short-term retention in the major.
