# Jacobs et al. (1981) paragraph rating scale modified by Hedgcock and Lefkowitz (1991)

## Description
A paragraph-writing assessment scale that rates content, organization, grammar, vocabulary, and mechanics across four performance bands, developed by Jacobs, Zinkgraf, Wormuth, Hartfiel, and Hughey and later modified by Hedgcock and Lefkowitz.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Jacobs et al. (1981) paragraph rating scale for assessing five writing components**: The study's assessment instrument is the paragraph rating scale first proposed by Jacobs, Zinkgraf, Wormuth, Hartfiel, and Hughey (1981) and modified by Hedgcock and Lefkowitz (1991). It "defines the following five component areas on s 0-100 point scale": content, organization, grammar, vocabulary, and mechanics of writing, with four bands per component (excellent to very good, good to average, fair to poor, very poor). Two trained assessors used it to score pretest and posttest paragraphs. (Mohammad Khatib (2015))
