# Walter's Cube

## Description
Walter's Cube is a Hungarian-developed virtual gallery platform hosted on Online Viewing Rooms that enables interactive 3D spaces with multimedia content and an integrated AI mediator for educational use.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Walter's Cube virtual gallery spaces with an integrated AI mediator for philosophy instruction**: Walter's Cube is a Hungarian-developed virtual gallery technology, deployed at Széchenyi István University on the Online Viewing Rooms platform, that lets visitors "virtually enter a gallery, walk around" as if physically present. In this study it hosted ten philosophy topics across ten interactive 3D rooms enriched with visual content, quotations, and explanatory texts. Its distinctive functionality over prior 3D/VR materials is that "students can interact with the AI mediator while virtually walking through the space, asking questions in writing or verbally and receiving accurate answers related to the material, assisting them in exam preparation." (Adél Vehrer (2025))

### Claims
- [In a virtual-gallery philosophy course with an AI mediator, 80% of participating first-year students achieved a good or excellent grade on the final test, significantly above an assumed 50% chance baseline](../claims/virtual-gallery-ai-mediator-80-percent-good-excellent-grades.md) [+W]
