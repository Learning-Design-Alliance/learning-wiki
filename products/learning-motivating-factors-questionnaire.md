## Description
A 19-item Likert self-report instrument developed from Law et al.'s seven-factor model to measure intrinsic and extrinsic learning-motivation factors in learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Law et al.'s seven-factor model of intrinsic and extrinsic learning motivating factors**: The study adopts the Learning Motivating Factors model of Law et al., which combines intrinsic and extrinsic motivation factors: "(1) an individual attitude and expectation factor", "(2) a challenging goals factor", "(3) a clear direction factor", "(4) a reward and recognition factor", "(5) a punishment factor", "(6) a social pressure and competition factor", and "(7) an efficacy factor". Intrinsic motivation encompasses individual attitude and expectation and challenging goals, whereas extrinsic motivation includes clear direction, reward and recognition, punishment, social pressure, and competition. The article operationalizes these with the 19-item Learning Motivating Factors Questionnaire on a 6-point Likert scale. (Dirzyte Aiste et al. (2021))
