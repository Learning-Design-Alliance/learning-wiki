## Description
LON-CAPA is an open-source course management and online assessment platform developed and maintained by the LON-CAPA community for delivering and tracking educational content and assignments.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **LON-CAPA open-source course management system used to deliver and track flip-JiTT warm-ups**: LON-CAPA (http://www.lon-capa.org) is an open-source course management platform with substantial physics content that the authors used to deliver warm-up questions and monitor what students did before class. It hosted the 4-6 question warm-ups, logged attempts, and allowed instructors to track participation, which yielded the reported 83.1% participation rate. The authors note the structure can be used on any course management system. (Nathaniel Lasry et al. (2012))
