## Description
A researcher-developed 20-item five-point Likert questionnaire by Mojtaba Ghassemi for measuring Iranian university EFL students' attitudes toward listening-comprehension learning, with demographic and open-comment sections.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **20-item five-point Likert attitude questionnaire on listening, validated by expert rating and pilot reliability testing**: The study's attitude instrument is a questionnaire in three parts: demographic information, 20 Likert-scale items (1 = strongly agree to 5 = strongly disagree) measuring attitudes toward listening, and an open-ended comment section. Items are categorized into positive-attitude and negative-attitude parts. Validity was checked by three PhD holders in TEFL rating suitability and clarity, and a pilot with 23 EFL students showed "the questionnaire was reliable (ά=0.74)". (Mojtaba Ghassemi (2013))
