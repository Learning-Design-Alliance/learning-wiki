# Nepal-adapted Principal/Head Teacher Instructional Management Rating Scale (PIMRS) questionnaire

## Description
A 33-item, 11-factor, five-point Likert questionnaire adapted for Nepal to measure instructional leadership practices of public secondary-school head teachers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **A Nepal-adapted PIMRS questionnaire with 11 factors and 33 items rated on a 5-point Likert scale**: The study constructed a new version of the Principal/Head Teacher Instructional Management Rating Scale for Nepal. Items were deselected, added (e.g., parent meetings and financial transparency), and reworded to fit Nepali schools, producing a questionnaire that "consists of 11 factors and 33 items" rated on a 5-point Likert scale from 1 = almost never to 5 = almost always. (Pande Suman et al. (2026))
