## Description
A two-volume secondary-school physics textbook by Ramón G. Loyarte and Enrique Loedel Palumbo, published by Ángel Estrada for Argentine national colleges and normal schools.

- **Tratado Elemental de Física (Loyarte and Loedel Palumbo, 1928/1932): a widely reprinted two-volume secondary-school physics textbook**: A two-volume secondary-school physics textbook by Ramón G. Loyarte and Enrique Loedel Palumbo, published by Ángel Estrada (volume one 1928, volume two 1932). The article presents it as the fruit of the collaboration between two graduates of the La Plata Physics Institute and reports that it "alca nzó un éxito editorial conside rable", remaining in print until at least 1964, by which time it had reached its tenth edition. (Gangui et al. (2022))
