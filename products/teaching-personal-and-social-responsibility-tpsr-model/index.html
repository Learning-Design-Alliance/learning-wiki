Teaching Personal and Social Responsibility (TPSR) model¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 7 claims (7 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Donald Hellison's curriculum model uses responsibility levels and structured physical-activity sessions to develop personal responsibility, social responsibility, self-direction, leadership, and transfer beyond the activity setting.

Components¶

  • Teaching Personal and Social Responsibility (TPSR) model with five responsibility levels: TPSR is a physical activity curriculum model for youth development that teaches responsibility through five levels: "respect for the rights and feelings of others, effort/participation, self-direction, helping others/leadership and transferring out of the sports hall". The model is implemented through strategies including relational time, awareness talk, physical activity plan, group meeting and self-reflection time, and in leadership development proceeds through stages from learning to take responsibility to transferring out of the sports hall. The study used TPSR as the basis of a 16-lesson leadership program because it includes a leadership/cooperative behavior level. (Filiz (2019))
  • Hellison's Teaching Personal and Social Responsibility (TPSR) model as the framework for social responsibility education through sport: The article grounds its intervention in the social responsibility education model created by D. Hellison, "often is referred to as TPSR – Teaching Physical and Social Responsibility", which aims to help young people, including social exclusion risk groups, develop personal and social skills and responsibilities in sport and in life. The model treats social responsibility as "a kind of moral obligation to respect yourself and others", holds that young people can learn to become responsible for themselves and others, and is realized through sports games. The article's educational program (Table 2) was prepared by the authors according to Hellison (2011), organizing content into the components respect and caring and helping. (Romualdas K. Malinauskas (2017))

Claims¶

  • A 16-lesson TPSR leadership program significantly improved personal and social responsibility scores of sports high school students compared with a control group [+M]
  • The TPSR leadership program significantly improved leadership behavior scores (initiation of structure and consideration) on the LBDQ compared with the control group [+M]
  • Students in the TPSR leadership program reported gains in self-confidence, taking responsibility, cooperation, and leading behaviors in post-program interviews [+W]
  • Students' definitions of leadership broadened over the course of the TPSR program, from helping peers to a more comprehensive view including empathy and reassurance [+W]
  • Overall social responsibility improves significantly after the education program in the experimental group but not in the control group [+M]
  • A social responsibility education program significantly improves the respect component among football sports school students [+M]
  • A social responsibility education program significantly improves the caring and helping component among football sports school students [+M]
