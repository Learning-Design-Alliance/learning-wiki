FUSION outcome and observation measurement suite¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A measurement suite used in the FUSION pilot to assess students’ conceptual understanding and procedural fluency and to rate instructional fidelity and quality through standardized and researcher-developed instruments.

Components¶

  • Outcome and observation measurement suite: proximal and distal math measures plus fidelity and instructional-quality observation instruments: The pilot measures conceptual understanding and procedural fluency with both proximal and distal outcomes: modified and unmodified EN-CBM subtests, SAT-10 problem solving and procedures subtests, and the researcher-developed ProFUSION assessment of FUSION content for at-risk conditions. Instruction is observed with the FUSION observation instrument, which measures implementation fidelity, and the RCMIS, which rates 11 features of instructional quality across learning environment, classroom management, and delivery of instruction on a 4-point scale. (Strand Cary et al. (2013))

Claims¶

  • PF students outperformed direct-instruction students on conceptual understanding and transfer without compromising procedural fluency [+W]
