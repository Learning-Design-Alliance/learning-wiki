# First Steps in the NICU

## Description
A free multiplatform visual-novel serious game developed collaboratively by neonatology and medical education contributors to simulate NICU observation, care, communication, and decision-making for students and the public.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Just-in-time preparatory boot camp: three 1-hour sessions immediately before a high-stakes clinical rotation**: A recurring monthly curriculum of three 1-hour sessions—two interactive lectures with case-based application and one high-fidelity simulation session—completed 1–2 months before a rotation. The article states it "is designed for just-in-time learning and hands-on practice, that is, immediately prior to starting a rotation," with small groups of two to five residents for individualized support. Sessions cover labs, vital signs, X-rays, nutrition calculations, respiratory management, and on-call problems. (Surcouf JW et al. (2018))
