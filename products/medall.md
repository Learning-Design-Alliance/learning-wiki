# MedAll

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
MedAll is a web-based streaming and videoconferencing platform with broadcast and breakout-room features used by trainers and trainees for virtual surgical-skills instruction.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MedAll web-based streaming platform with main stage and breakout rooms for virtual surgical skills teaching**: The virtual teaching format used the MedAll platform, in which trainers and trainees were logged in with a 'main stage' broadcast onto a large monitor and 'breakout rooms' corresponding to webcam broadcasts of individual stack systems. Trainers could re-play demonstration videos, highlight key steps, and move between breakout rooms to give individual advice and feedback, using two-way Bluetooth headphones for communication. (Das Bibek et al. (2024))
