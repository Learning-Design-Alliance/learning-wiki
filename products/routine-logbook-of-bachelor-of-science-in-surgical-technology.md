## Description
An Isfahan University of Medical Sciences logbook and checklist for directly observing, documenting, scoring, and giving limited feedback on undergraduate surgical technology students’ expected scrub-nurse skills across three surgical-procedure stages.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Routine Isfahan University logbook for surgical technology competency assessment**: The control-condition assessment instrument, the logbook of Bachelor of Science in surgical technology approved by Isfahan University of Medical Sciences. "The logbook checklist consisted of 13 expected skills", yielding minimum and maximum scores of 0 and 65 on a 5-point scale per skill. It has four sections: demographic information, competency evaluation across 13 skills (from preparing the room to end of surgery), three tables for the three test stages scored 1 to 13, and marking of incomplete skills with feedback to the student. The assessor directly observed performance throughout the surgical procedure. (Bagheri Sara et al. (2025))
