# TEACH e-Learning course

## Description
A five-hour, four-module online professional-learning course developed for pre-service and in-service early childhood educators to build knowledge and confidence for promoting physical activity and reducing sedentary behaviour.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **TEACH e-Learning course in physical activity and sedentary behaviour for early childhood educators**: The TEACH e-Learning course is a ~5 h online professional learning course for pre- and in-service early childhood educators, with expert-developed content generated via a Delphi process. The article states "the course comprised four modules developed for ECEs", covering introductory movement-behaviour content, the childcare environment and outdoor/risky play, practical strategies to promote physical activity and minimize sedentary time, and ECE-focused professional learning with resources and a video library. It was pilot tested with a pre-post survey design. (Bruijns BA et al. (2022))

### Claims
- [Both pre-service and in-service early childhood educators significantly increased their total physical activity and sedentary behaviour knowledge scores after completing the TEACH e-Learning course](../claims/teach-elearning-increases-ece-knowledge.md) [+M]
- [In one pre-post study with no comparison group, in-service ECEs' self-rated task and barrier self-efficacy rose significantly from pre- to post-course; pre-service ECEs' self-rated barrier self-efficacy changed significantly, while task self-efficacy did not significantly change](../claims/teach-elearning-self-efficacy-group-differences.md) [+M]
