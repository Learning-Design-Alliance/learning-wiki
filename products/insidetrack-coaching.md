# InsideTrack Coaching

## Description
InsideTrack© Coaching is a postsecondary student-support programme run by InsideTrack that provides proactive, personalized coaching through multiple communication channels to help students overcome academic and non-academic barriers to persistence and graduation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **uCoach® Technology Platform**: The uCoach® Technology Platform is one of the two primary components of InsideTrack© Coaching. It "enables InsideTrack© Coaching to deliver proactive, one-on-one coaching through multiple channels; send automated messages at predefined intervals; track student engagement and coach observations; and predict when to reach out to students on particular issues." Automated messages remind students about deadlines, opportunities, and resources, and the platform includes self-directed resources and can be integrated with institutional systems such as learning management systems. # InsideTrack© Coaching program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
InsideTrack© Coaching "provides proactive, personalized coaching to help students identify and overcome both academic and non-academic barriers to college persistence and graduation." It partners with universities to supply personnel and technology, delivering coaching through phone, video, email, text, and mobile apps. Coaches help students clarify goals, track deadlines, access resources, and build time management and study skills, focusing on students' lives outside school as well as academics. In the qualifying study, students generally worked with coaches over two semesters and met at least five times.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- InsideTrack© partners with universities to deliver its coaching program, supplying the personnel and technology
- Colleges provide information such as transcripts, course syllabi, or course performance data to customize coaches' interactions
#### Constraints
- The effects of InsideTrack© Coaching are not known for outcomes including college access and enrollment; college attendance; academic achievement; and labor market outcomes

### Target Learners
- college students including traditional undergraduates, first-generation, low income, minority, graduate, online, adult, and military-connected students

### Target Learning Goals
- college persistence
- degree completion
- self-advocacy and study skills
