# CCC product badges: Centering Students' Diverse Lived Experiences, Commitment to Continuous Improvement, and Faculty Agency and Connection

## Description
Three competency-based badges let vendors "demonstrate product alignment to student and faculty priorities". Digital Promise "recommended the development of three product badges, or competency-based applications vendors can complete to attempt to demonstrate product alignment". Each badge lists evidence requirements: CSDLE covers multi-device use, low-bandwidth functionality, in-app guides or helpdesk, English learner support, LMS integration, and design research with diverse community college students; FAC covers faculty content agency, device compatibility, LMS integration, user support, and faculty feedback; CCI covers learning sciences consultation, studies with community college students within two years, improvement driven by findings, customer support feedback channels, and explained updates.
