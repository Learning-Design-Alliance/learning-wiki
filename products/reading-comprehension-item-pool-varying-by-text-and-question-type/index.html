Reading comprehension item pool varying by text and question type¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A study-developed reading-comprehension assessment item pool for fourth-grade learners, with items systematically varied by text and question type to support multidimensional measurement.

Components¶

  • Curriculum-based assessment pool of 15 standardized informational texts with 10 comprehension questions each: The researchers developed a pool of 15 informational texts on different topics (e.g., American Indians, volcanoes, ants) of 280-320 words, each with 10 comprehension questions. The article states that "the LIX readability index was always between 25 and 30" to control difficulty, and that items were previously tested with a small sample of fourth graders to ensure relatively equal difficulty. Questions were worded so only one specific answer could be counted as correct. During each testing phase, every tutee read one randomly selected text per day across three consecutive days. (Leidig et al. (2018))
