# GRAN software complex (GRAN1, GRAN-2D, GRAN-3D)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A mathematical and physics learning software complex developed for school pupils in grades 6–11 and university students, supporting the construction and investigation of graphs, models, equations, inequalities, and computer-assisted problem solving.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **GRAN software complex (GRAN1, GRAN-2D, GRAN-3D) for mathematics and physics learning**: The GRAN complex (GRAN1, GRAN-2D, GRAN-3D), alongside DG and SAGE — named in the monograph as the predecessor of CoCalc — is listed among integrated pedagogical software tools. Citing Zhdan, the monograph reports the complex can be used in mathematics and partly physics lessons "від 6 -го до 11 -го класу включно" and in university disciplines from geometry to computational mathematics, with school mathematics and physics courses alone offering "понад 700 годин" where the programs apply. (Попель М. В (2018))
