Bayes Net Toolbox for Matlab¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Bayes Net Toolbox for Matlab is a software toolbox developed by Kevin Murphy for representing, manipulating, and performing inference with Bayesian networks.

Components¶

  • Bayes Net Toolbox for Matlab used to carry out graph transformations algorithmically: The article reports that all directed-acyclic-graph to moral-graph, triangulated-graph, and junction-tree transformations were done algorithmically with software rather than by hand: "All graph transformations in this paper were carried out using the Bayes Net Toolbox for Matlab (Murphy, 2001, 2007)." Directed acyclic graphs are represented as adjacency matrices, from which the moral graph, a triangulated graph, its cliques, and the junction tree can be readily obtained. (Rijmen (2010))
