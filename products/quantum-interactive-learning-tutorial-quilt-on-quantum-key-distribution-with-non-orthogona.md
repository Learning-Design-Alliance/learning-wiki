# Quantum Interactive Learning Tutorial (QuILT) on quantum key distribution with non-orthogonal photon polarization states

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A guided quantum-physics tutorial developed by DeVore and colleagues that uses quantum key distribution to connect formalism with conceptual understanding for upper-level undergraduate students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Quantum Interactive Learning Tutorial (QuILT) on quantum key distribution**: A research-based tutorial that helps upper-level undergraduates learn quantum mechanics through quantum key distribution with a simple two state system. It 'uses a guided approach in which various concepts build upon each other gradually', covering the B92 protocol with non-orthogonal photon polarization states and a second protocol using entangled spin-1/2 particles, with prediction phases, a checking simulation, and a table students must construct. (Seth DeVore et al. (2014))

### Claims
- [Upper-level undergraduate students show large pretest-to-posttest gains on quantum key distribution concepts after working through the QKD QuILT](../claims/qkd-quilt-pretest-posttest-gains.md) [+M]
