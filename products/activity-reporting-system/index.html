Activity Reporting System¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

An Excel-based case-management and activity-reporting system developed for coaching interventions, comprising daily logs, activity logs, and case-notes logs with data-validation controls.

Components¶

  • Activity Reporting System for coaching process documentation and fidelity monitoring: An Excel-based Activity Reporting System with three components — a Daily Log, an Activity Log, and a Case Notes Log — allowed the coach to "maintain electronic case notes and logs and to track the number, type, and duration of coaching contacts and activities." The system documented that 79% of the coach's available time went to intervention and provided "data validation controls to maintain the accuracy and integrity of the information collected." It served both intervention management and embedded formative and summative evaluation. (Zeller et al. (2013))

Claims¶

  • The graduation coach spent 79% of total available time (1029 hours) on intervention, with the Activity Reporting System accounting for over 83% of available time [+M]
