# MAP Growth retest dataset for evaluating E-M scoring accuracy

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A unique dataset built from archived RIT-score data on MAP Growth assessments in math and reading from students who had been retested within a short period of time. Across two testing terms, instances of students retested within one day were identified, yielding over 5,000 cases with disengagement (10%+ rapid guesses) on the first test and zero rapid guesses on the retest. This dataset permits evaluation of E-M score-adjustment accuracy, which is otherwise challenging.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students must have been retested within a short period, with one subgroup retested within one day.
#### Constraints
- Evaluation rests on retest cases where the retest showed zero rapid guesses, assuming the retest reflects fully engaged performance.

### Target Learners
- K-12 students taking MAP Growth math and reading assessments

### Target Learning Goals
- Accurate achievement measurement under test-taking disengagement
