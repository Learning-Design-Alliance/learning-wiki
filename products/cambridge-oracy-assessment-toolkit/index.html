Cambridge Oracy Assessment Toolkit¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 design), q1–q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A research-informed set of teacher-administered tasks and procedures developed by Cambridge researchers for assessing the spoken-language skills of students aged 11–12.

Components¶

  • Oracy Assessment Toolkit: three pre/post assessment tasks, six assessment-for-learning tasks, and a rating scheme: The Oracy Assessment Toolkit measures the skills set out in the Oracy Skills Framework. It includes three assessment tasks for beginning and end of Year 7: "a formal presentational speech; an instructional activity whereby one student enables another to complete a specific task; and a group discussion in which three students are asked to reach joint conclusions about a specific topic". A rating scheme lets teachers rate each relevant framework skill, build a skills profile per student, and track progress across the year. Six additional assessment-for-learning tasks can be used flexibly and adapted to a school's existing curriculum. (Maxwell et al. (2015))

Claims¶

  • The Cambridge Oracy Assessment Toolkit was developed and validated with children and teachers in English secondary schools [+M]
  • No comparable teacher-friendly instrument for assessing oracy existed before the Cambridge Oracy Assessment Toolkit [+W]
