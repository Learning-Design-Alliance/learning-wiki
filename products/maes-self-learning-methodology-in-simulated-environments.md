# MAES (Self-Learning Methodology in Simulated Environments)

## Description
MAES is a structured six-phase, facilitator-guided methodology for self-directed peer learning through realistic clinical simulation, developed for healthcare education.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MAES: Self-Learning Methodology in Simulated Environments**: MAES is a self-learning methodology for simulated clinical environments developed in 2013, built on problem-based learning, peer education, and clinical simulation in highly realistic environments. Its "structure of MAES is developed with 6 phases in a minimum of 2 sessions and the completion of homework by the student between sessions 1 and 2": establishing group identity, choosing a scenario, setting baseline learning objectives, designing the scenario at home, running the simulation, and a structured GAS debriefing. Students take an active role, reflecting in small working groups on their learning needs and seeking supporting evidence, guided by an expert facilitator. In this study MAES was applied for the first time to interprofessional CPR training with nursing and medical students. (Fenzi Giulio et al. (2024))
