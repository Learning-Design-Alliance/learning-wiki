# Self-developed virtual simulation teaching platform for laparoscopic surgical training

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A web-accessible 3D virtual simulation platform developed by the research team for medical undergraduates to practise laparoscopic procedures, instrument handling, and complication management with immediate corrective feedback.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Low-cost haptic laparoscopic training simulator with automatic performance assessment**: A computer-based low-fidelity laparoscopic simulator pairing two real standard laparoscopic instruments with Geomagic Touch force-feedback devices, a standard monitor showing a virtual 3D environment, and open-source software (CHAI3D, Open-GL) for haptic interaction. Trainees perform navigation tasks with laparoscopic grasping tools while avoiding 3D obstacles, and the system logs trajectories, moments, collisions and affine velocity for automatic assessment. The article states "Its cost has been evaluated at around $2500 for a single specimen, including hardware purchases, rapid fabrication, and software development." (De Witte Benjamin et al. (2021))
- **SIMISGEST-VR: a portable, low-cost, gesture-mediated 3D VR simulator for basic MIS psychomotor skills**: SIMISGEST-VR is a web-based 3D virtual reality simulator mediated by a Leap Motion Controller, built in Unity3D with two nonfunctional laparoscopic forceps, support devices reproducing the fulcrum effect, and a mounting pad with a 45-degree LMC tilt. It supports 6 MIST-VR-adapted tasks, records metrics (time, efficiency of movement, economy of diathermy, errors, final score), and provides concurrent, immediate, and terminal feedback via an integrated SQLite database. The article reports a total hardware cost of approximately US $200, against US $2000-US $100,000 for market VR simulators. (Alvarez-Lopez F et al. (2020))
