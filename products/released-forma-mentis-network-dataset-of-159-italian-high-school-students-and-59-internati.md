# Released forma mentis network dataset of 159 Italian high school students and 59 international STEM researchers

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A released dataset of free-association responses and valence ratings from Italian high school students and international STEM researchers, assembled by Massimo Stella and colleagues for studying group-level perceptions of educational and scientific concepts.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Forma mentis networks are knowledge graphs that combine conceptual associations and sentiment to reconstruct the mindset of individuals or groups**: Forma mentis (Latin for mindset) networks combine artificial intelligence, cognitive psychology and complex systems to map conceptual knowledge and affect. They "combine these elements in order to reconstruct the semantic frame", or meaning, and emotions attributed by individuals to concepts, yielding a networked model of their mindset. Behavioral FMNs build links from free associations collected through a simple form plus positive, negative and neutral sentiment ratings; cross-validation with independent affective norms found words surrounded by negative associates to correspond to anxiety-eliciting concepts. The study applied BFMNs to data from 159 Italian high school students and 59 international STEM researchers. (M. Stella et al. (2020))
