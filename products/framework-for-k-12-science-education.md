# Framework for K-12 Science Education

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A National Research Council framework that organizes K-12 science learning around eight scientific and engineering practices, including questioning, modeling, investigation, data analysis, explanation, argument from evidence, and communication.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Framework for K-12 Science Education 8 practices of science as organizing framework**: The paper adopts the 2012 Framework for K-12 Science Education by the National Research Council, USA as the conceptual framework for video analysis and modeling, aiming to capture students' interest and provide foundational knowledge. Students are mentored through 8 practices: ask question, use models, plan and carry out investigation, analyse and interpret data, use mathematical and computational thinking, construct explanations, argue from evidence and communicate information. The performance task and primer activities scaffold subsets of these practices. (Loo Kang Wee (2014))
