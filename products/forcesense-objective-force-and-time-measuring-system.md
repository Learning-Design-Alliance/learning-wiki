## Description
ForceSense, made by Medishield in Delft, is an instrumented surgical-simulation assessment system that measures force- and time-based performance parameters and records trials for feedback and learning-curve analysis.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ForceSense objective force and time measuring system for surgical simulation training**: The ForceSense system (Medishield, Delft) is an objective measuring system used to assess tissue handling during simulated surgical tasks. It "provided feedback regarding maximum force, mean force, max impulse, force volume and time", and in this study assessed robotic suturing trials on the da Vinci Si system, with all trials recorded and uploaded to an online database. Its five force-based parameters were used to distinguish experience groups and plot learning curves. (Rahimi A. Masie et al. (2023))
- **ForceSense instrumented box trainer for objective force, motion and time assessment of laparoscopic performance**: The LAPSTAR box trainer used in the study was equipped with the ForceSense measuring system, which "provides force-, motion- and time parameter outcomes used for objective assessment of laparoscopic performance". It combines a decoupled three degrees of freedom force sensor measuring force exerted on the task with registration of three degrees of freedom instrument movements and task completion time. Seven parameters derived from these signals served as the study's outcome measures. (Postema Roelf R. et al. (2020))
