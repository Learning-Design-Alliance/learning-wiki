## Description
The California framework organizes all K-12 social sciences programs around five goals: disciplinary understanding, skills, diversity, values, and social participation. It states that programs 'will be directed toward the achievement of civic competence' and that the central purpose is to 'develop responsible citizens who will preserve and continue to advance progress toward a just society.' Each goal is elaborated in a dedicated section with discussion and illustrative objectives.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Programs must be planned to address all five goals through selected studies of human experience in American and world societies
#### Constraints
- Implementation materials and courses of study are to be developed at the school district level; the framework itself is explicitly not a course of study

### Target Learners
- Kindergarten through grade twelve students in California public schools

### Target Learning Objectives
- Civic competence
- Interdisciplinary conceptual understanding
- Social sciences skills
- Valuing and social participation

# Grade-span structure of program goals with illustrative, non-sequential level objectives

> **Pattern** · [All patterns](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The framework organizes objectives across four grade spans—K-3, 4-6, 7-8, and 9-12—stating program goals for the whole span and illustrative level objectives for each. It explains that 'Illustrative level objectives are presented for each of the grade spans. These objectives are not comprehensive as to objectives that might be specified for any particular program goal, nor are they sequential. They are, however, responsive to the changing maturity of students.' Districts are responsible for further delineation into behavioral objectives.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Further delineation of objectives into behavioral statements must be the responsibility of each school district
#### Constraints
- Objectives are illustrative, not comprehensive or sequential; grade spans were selected because they 'make possible reasonable instructional statements'

### Target Learners
- Students in kindergarten through grade three, grades four through six, grades seven and eight, and grades nine through twelve

### Target Goals
- Developmentally appropriate social sciences understandings and skills
