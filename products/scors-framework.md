## Description
A multidimensional framework for assessing organisational readiness, support, resources, culture, and sustainability before implementing simulation-based education, used by health-education institutions and managers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Organisational readiness as a multidimensional determinant of simulation-based education success**: The article frames organisational readiness as an important yet under-examined determinant of SBE success, operationalised through the SCORS framework into four dimensions: defined need and support for change, readiness for culture change, time/personnel/resource readiness, and sustainable education development to embed culture. Readiness is treated as a psychological and structural perception that identifies factors contributing to readiness and provides management an opportunity to address the readiness gap. The framework assumes that successful implementation and sustained integration of SBE depend on adequate resource allocation, comprehensive faculty training and robust organisational support across all levels. (Naismith et al. (2026))
