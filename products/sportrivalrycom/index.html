SportRivalry.com¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

SportRivalry.com is a website hosting comic strips and animated cartoons about sports rivalry, fan behavior, and in-group bias for educational use.

Components¶

  • SportRivalry.com comic strips and cartoons for teaching about rivalry and fan behavior: The element is a set of comic strips and animated cartoons hosted on www.SportRivalry.com, "a website dedicated to teaching the public about the rivalry phenomenon." Two types exist: comics about historical rivalries detailing on-field and off-field occurrences and ending with a psychological term or theory, and Adventures with Sport Rivalry Man stories showing appropriate behavior toward rival fans. At the time of the study, thirty comics on college rivalries, two in Major League Baseball, two in the NFL, and five Sport Rivalry Man stories were available. (Cody T. Havard et al. (2018))

Claims¶

  • Exposure to rivalry comic strips and cartoons significantly influences fan rival perceptions overall (MANCOVA) [+W]
  • Students exposed to rivalry comics experienced less satisfaction when their favorite team beat the rival [+W]
  • Students exposed to rivalry comics rated rival fan behavior (Out-Group Sportsmanship) more negatively than controls [+W]
