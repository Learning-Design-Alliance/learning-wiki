# ABCDE VR application

## Description
An immersive Unity-based virtual-reality application developed for self-instructed practice of systematic ABCDE clinical observation by novice medical and nursing learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ABCDE VR application for self-instructed practice of systematic clinical observation**: A fully immersive, interactive VR application built in Unity for commercially available goggles and hand controllers, letting inexperienced users practice the ABCDE approach alone or in multiplayer. Its solutions were categorised into "15 core features like onboarding, instructions, quiz, and feedback", including a virtual patient with haptic pulse feedback, a documentation tablet, and a three-star scoreboard. It was used in two RCTs with 578 first-year medical and nurse students. (Berg H et al. (2023))
- **VirSam ABCDE immersive and interactive VR application for individual self-practice of systematic clinical observation**: A purpose-built VR application in which students practice the ABCDE approach on a virtual patient using virtual versions of real equipment (blood pressure gauge, oximeter, ear thermometer). Its main features are "Immersion" in a 3D virtual room with 360-degree vision and "Interaction" via virtual hands with haptic feedback (pulse and chest vibration), plus a documentation tablet, silent subtitled instruction video, and automatic scoreboard feedback covering order, completeness and correctness of observations. (Berg Helen (2020))
