# Pulsar Search Collaboratory (PSC)

## Description
A GBO-WVU programme that gives middle- and high-school students and teachers authentic pulsar-search research experiences using student-accessible Green Bank Telescope data, online training, mentoring, and related activities.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Pulsar Search Collaboratory program and database websites**: The PSC operates two websites: a program website hosting training materials and a discussion forum, and a database website providing access to pulsar diagnostic plots grouped into pointings. The article states the program "engages high school students, teachers, and undergraduate mentors in real-world research by searching for pulsars in data collected with the 100-m Green Bank Telescope". Students quantitatively grade plots through a graphical interface after passing certification tests, and each dataset is reviewed five times by five different students before removal from the student interface. (Blumer et al. (2019))

### Claims
- [The Pulsar Search Collaboratory shows positive effects on middle and high-school students' science identity, self-efficacy, and STEM intentions relative to a control group](../claims/psc-student-identity-self-efficacy-gains.md) [+M]
- [Undergraduate mentors in the PSC showed statistically significant increases in research knowledge, confidence, and collaboration-related skills](../claims/psc-undergrad-mentor-gains.md) [+M]
- [PSC I students discovered seven pulsars and detected 25 already known pulsars while analyzing program data](../claims/psc1-student-pulsar-discoveries.md) [+M]
