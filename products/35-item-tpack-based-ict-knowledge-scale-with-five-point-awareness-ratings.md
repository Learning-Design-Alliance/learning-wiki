# 35-item TPACK-based ICT knowledge scale with five-point awareness ratings

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 35-item Likert-scale instrument developed to measure in-service teachers' awareness and knowledge of ICT components across four technology-related TPACK dimensions for e-course design.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **35-item TPACK-based ICT knowledge scale with five-point awareness ratings (Cronbach's alpha 0.846)**: A knowledge scale measuring teachers' ICT knowledge based on TPACK (Koehler & Mishra, 2006), using only the four technology-related dimensions. It contains 35 items rated on a five-point Likert scale from 0 (Not aware of the components) to 4 (Highly knowledgeable about the components). Two computer-technology experts validated the Arabic version, a pilot with 22 teachers was conducted, and internal consistency calculated with Cronbach's coefficient alpha was 0.846. The scale served as pretest and posttest for both groups, analyzed with SPSS 17.0. (Qasem & Viswanathappa (2016))
