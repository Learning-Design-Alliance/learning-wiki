# T-SQsrl paired teacher and student SRL questionnaires

## Description
A paired teacher-and-student questionnaire instrument developed by Avivit Arvatz and colleagues to assess self-regulated-learning subprocesses and reflection through self-report and triangulated open-ended responses.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **T-SQsrl: paired teacher and student SRL questionnaires with a triangulated reflection assessment**: The T-SQsrl is an assessment tool comprising a teacher version (TQsrl, 31 closed-ended items plus an open-ended reflective question) and a student version (SQsrl, 15 closed-ended items), both adapted from the SRLMQ and structured on Zimmerman's cyclical SRL model. It evaluates SRL sub-processes including goal-setting, task-strategy, self-efficacy, help-seeking, elaboration, and reflection. The teacher version enables triangulated assessment by correlating self-assessed reflection with expert grading of an open-ended reflective question, while teachers provided the external assessment triangulating students' self-assessments. The article describes it as innovative in its focus on reflection skills and in allowing simultaneous assessment of teachers' and students' SRL within the same classroom or school. (Avivit Arvatz et al. (2025))
