# Mathematics Conceptual Understanding Test (MCUT)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The MCUT is an 18-item two-tier multiple-choice diagnostic test developed by Abdissa et al. to assess Grade 10 students' conceptual understanding of relations and functions, polynomial functions, and exponential and logarithmic functions.
