## Description
A nation-wide quantum-science outreach programme run by researchers, educators, communicators, and partner institutions across Italian cities through lectures, tours, workshops, games, contests, and exhibitions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Italian Quantum Weeks 2022: nation-wide coordinated quantum outreach initiative**: The Italian Quantum Weeks (IQWs) project organised several weeks from March 26 to May 31, 2022, dedicated to exploring the foundations and applications of quantum mechanics. More than 130 researchers, technicians, disseminators, communicators, and teachers from more than 40 institutions in 17 Italian cities were involved. Activities included lectures, laboratory tours, workshops, quantum game sessions, a creativity contest, and the interactive exhibition "Speaking the Unspeakable - Quantum Superposition". (S Faletic et al. (2023))
