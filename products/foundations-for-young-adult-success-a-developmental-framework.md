## Description
A conceptual framework developed by Nagaoka and colleagues at the University of Chicago Consortium on Chicago School Research that organizes the knowledge, skills, mindsets, strategies, and behaviors intended to support agency and success from childhood through young adulthood.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Integrated identity framework: young adult success as agency and motivation grounded in an integrated identity**: The article's central framework conceptualizes a well-prepared young adult as one with an integrated identity and the agency to use internal and external resources toward self-set goals. It defines success as "being poised to work toward and achieve the goals a person sets for herself," with goals emerging from values and purpose. The framework names key factors — knowledge and skills, awareness, mindsets, strategies, and behaviors and habits — that come together in an integrated identity enabling directed action across life domains. (Nagaoka et al. (2014))
