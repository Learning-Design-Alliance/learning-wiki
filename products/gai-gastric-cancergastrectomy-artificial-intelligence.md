# gAI (gastric cancer/gastrectomy artificial intelligence)

## Description
gAI is a deep-learning surgical video-overlay system developed by Hasegawa Takumi et al. that visualizes the pancreas and gastric surgical landmarks during laparoscopic gastrectomy to support surgeons' landmark recognition.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **gAI: an AI system visualizing pancreas and dimpling lines as real-time overlays during laparoscopic gastrectomy**: gAI (gastric cancer/gastrectomy artificial intelligence) is a deep-learning system that visualizes the pancreas and three dimpling line landmarks (DMP, DTP, DIP) as real-time overlays during laparoscopic gastrectomy, built on "the HyperSeg architecture (a nested U-Net) trained on 2493 annotated images from 50 laparoscopic gastrectomy videos". It encodes tacit knowledge of experienced surgeons to help prevent postoperative pancreatic fistula. In this study its outputs were displayed on anonymized video clips to evaluate landmark recognition by surgeons. (Hasegawa Takumi et al. (2026))

### Claims

- [gAI intervention produces subjective recognition changes in anatomical landmarks that are significantly less frequent among trainers than trainees in the infrapyloric area](../claims/gai-infrapyloric-recognition-change-expertise-gradient.md) [+M]
