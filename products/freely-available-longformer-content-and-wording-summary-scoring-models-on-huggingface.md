# Freely available Longformer Content and Wording summary scoring models on HuggingFace

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Two Longformer checkpoints, longformer-content-global and longformer-wording-global, released by Morris et al. for automated content and wording scoring of learner summaries in educational platforms and intelligent textbooks.

### Claims
- [Including the textbook source text in the model input improves automated summary scoring accuracy, with Longformer explaining 82% of Content and 70% of Wording score variance versus 67% and 41% for summary-only RoBERTa](../claims/longformer-source-text-outperforms-roberta-summary-scoring.md) [+M]
- [On professional OpenStax textbook summaries, matched summaries scored significantly higher than unmatched ones, with a larger effect for Content (d = 1.56) than Wording (d = 0.938)](../claims/matched-unmatched-summary-validity-large-effects.md) [+M]
