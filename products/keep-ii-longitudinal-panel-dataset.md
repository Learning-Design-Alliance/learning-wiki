## Description
The Korea Employment Education Panel (KEEP) II is a longitudinal dataset tracking sampled South Korean high-school students annually from 2016 onward, including later-wave measures of AI use, AI perceptions, and science-technology-society views.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **KEEP II longitudinal panel dataset (2016–2023) with 2,348 general high school cohorts**: The Korea Employment Education Panel (KEEP) II began in 2016 with 10,558 cohorts of 11th graders in South Korea, random-sampling high school students and tracking them annually. The study combined the 1st (2016), 2nd (2017) and 7th (2023) waves, restricted to general high schools, cleaned by listwise deletion, yielding n = 2,348 (male = 1,079; female = 1,269). The 7th wave, administered after the release of ChatGPT, added 15 items on AI use, AI perceptions, and STS views. (Gyeonggeon “Boaz” Lee (2025))
