# Body Interact™ Virtual Patient Simulator

## Description
Body Interact™ is a computer- and interactive-table-based virtual patient simulation platform developed for health-professions education, enabling learners to conduct simulated clinical encounters and make treatment decisions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Body Interact virtual patient simulation platform used for fully online pre-graduation clinical training**: Body Interact™ is a platform providing education training by means of "virtual patients built with artificial intelligence". Students interview the virtual patient, perform physical examination, order laboratory and imaging tests, administer medication and provide interventions; every medical decision influences the patient's outcome, and inappropriate decisions result in deterioration or death. The system produces a timeline report and performance metrics benchmarked to clinical guidelines, made available online to the tutor for evaluation. In this study 21 patient-based cases were delivered in two-hour tutored online sessions. (De Ponti R et al. (2020))
- **Body Interact virtual patient simulation software**: Body Interact (Coimbra, Portugal) is a screen-based, nonimmersive virtual patient simulation in which "the virtual patient shows the dynamic pathophysiological response to user decisions" and "Participants can act as real clinicians, ordering almost any tests or treatments as needed." In this study it was used for two scenarios (altered mental status and acute chest pain), with participants allotted 20 minutes per scenario to diagnose and treat, followed by an automatic summary of their decision-making. (Watari Takashi et al. (2020))
- **Body Interact clinical virtual simulator with debriefing tool**: Body Interact is the clinical virtual simulator used in the experimental condition. It "presents virtual patients backed up by a physiological algorithm that recreates a dynamic health condition that responds to user interventions". After a briefing, users interact through dialogues, physiological monitoring, observation and physical examination, and prescription of examinations and treatment; the case closes on successful resolution or elapsed time. A debriefing tool then presents the simulation report, simulation timeline, and performance report covering physical examination, diagnosis, and therapeutic activities, with the best evidence-based decisions and supporting scientific references. (José Miguel Padilha et al. (2019))
