## Description
A Catapult Learning supplemental-education programme that delivers AchieveLiteracy and AchieveMath curricula through structured, low-ratio small-group instruction in literacy and mathematics.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **AchieveLiteracy and AchieveMath curricula with explicit, research-based small-group instruction**: The Wraparound program delivers systematic, small-group instruction in literacy and math through the AchieveLiteracy and AchieveMath curricula, which the page describes as emphasizing "explicit, research-based strategies to improve students' skills and confidence." Instruction is delivered in a structured environment with a low student-to-teacher ratio, aiming to accelerate academic achievement while fostering a supportive learning atmosphere. (Catapult Learning's Wraparound Supplemental Educational Services (2024))
- **Wraparound support services: counseling and special education support alongside academic instruction**: Beyond academic instruction, the Wraparound program offers counseling services to address students' social and emotional needs and special education support to accommodate diverse learning requirements. Counselors must hold counseling certification registered in compliance with state law and/or an MSW, and all staff complete pre-service onboarding training plus ongoing professional development for each service type. (Catapult Learning's Wraparound Supplemental Educational Services (2024))
