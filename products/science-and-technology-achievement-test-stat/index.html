Science and Technology Achievement Test (STAT)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A 30-item multiple-choice science achievement test developed by Teke et al. to assess seventh-grade students’ understanding of human body systems.

Components¶

  • Science and Technology Achievement Test (STAT), a 30-item multiple-choice instrument: The STAT is a multiple-choice achievement test covering the 'Systems of the Human Body' unit (digestive system, urinary system, nervous system and sense organs). It was developed from an initial pool of 50 questions administered to 95 eighth-grade students for item analysis, after which "the validity and reliability of the items were ensured (Cronbach's = 0.831)" and the final 30-question test was created. It served as both pretest and post-test in the study. (Teke et al. (2015))
