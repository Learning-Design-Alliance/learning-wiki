# OPEQ (Opportunities for Equitable Access to High Quality Basic Education)

## Description
A five-year IRC-led education programme in the Democratic Republic of the Congo that integrates reading and mathematics instruction, safe and inclusive classroom practices, and cluster-based collaborative teacher professional development.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **OPEQ: integrated reading and math curriculum with Healing Classrooms techniques plus school-based collaborative professional development through Forums of Pedagogical Exchange**: OPEQ (Opportunities for Equitable Access to High Quality Basic Education) is a 5-year intervention in three DRC provinces aiming to improve teachers' motivation and performance and children's numeracy, literacy and socio-emotional wellbeing. It combines an innovative curriculum integrating reading and math lessons with IRC's Healing Classrooms techniques "to create safe and inclusive learning environments for all learners", and a school-based collaborative professional development system built on the DRC tradition of Forums of Pedagogical Exchange, delivered by Master Trainers, one per cluster of 2 to 6 schools. (Wolf et al. (2014))
