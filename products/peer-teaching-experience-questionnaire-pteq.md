# Peer Teaching Experience Questionnaire (PTEQ)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 14-item, five-point Likert self-report questionnaire originally developed for nursing education and adapted by Williams et al. for evaluating paramedic students' peer-teaching experiences.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Peer Teaching Experience Questionnaire (PTEQ), a 14-item Likert instrument adapted for paramedic students**: The PTEQ is a 14-statement questionnaire using a 5-point Likert response format, originally developed to evaluate peer learning of first year nursing students and adapted by McKenna and French (2011) for third year nursing students. This study modified it for paramedic students by changing "nursing" to "paramedics" in three items, with no other wording changes, and added an open-ended feedback question. It was completed voluntarily and anonymously by 41 peer teachers after their final tutorial. (Williams et al. (2014))
