# ACTFL Performance Descriptors and NCSSFL-ACTFL Can-Do Statements

## Description
A proficiency-assessment and performance-alignment framework developed by the American Council on the Teaching of Foreign Languages (ACTFL) and the National Council of State Supervisors for Languages (NCSSFL) for describing language competence and matching tasks to proficiency levels.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ACTFL Performance Descriptors and Can-Do Statements as external criteria for establishing competence level, task difficulty, and appropriateness**: The authors adopt the ACTFL Performance Descriptors (PD) and NCSSFL-ACTFL Can-Do Statements (CDS) as external criteria for establishing student competence level, task difficulty, and appropriateness. The PD document was used to "determine the mode of communication in which students engaged: interpretive, interpersonal or presentational" and to analyze domain descriptions at the advanced level. The CDSs were used to corroborate that the objective of the task was at the proficiency level or just one sub-level beyond the participant's competence level. (Burston et al. (2018))
