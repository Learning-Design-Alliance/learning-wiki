# DUS Prosthodontics question benchmark: 128 official exam questions (2012–2021) with expert case/knowledge classification

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A benchmark dataset assembled by Erdal Selda Gökçe et al. from official Turkish ÖSYM Dentistry Specialization Exam questions, with expert classifications of case-based and knowledge-based items for evaluating language models.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **DUS Prosthodontics question benchmark: 128 official exam questions (2012–2021) with expert case/knowledge classification**: A Turkish-language question set drawn from 13 Dentistry Specialization Exam periods (2012–2021) administered by ÖSYM, used to benchmark four LLMs. It contains "128 questions" in two expert-classified categories: "case-based" (n = 22) and "knowledge-based" (n = 106), including items with visuals such as radiographs and clinical photographs. Inter-rater agreement for classification was "a Cohen's kappa of 0.97", with one disagreement resolved by a third expert. (Erdal Selda Gökçe et al. (2026))
