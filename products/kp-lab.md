# KP-Lab toolset supporting four types of mediation, including the Visual Model Editor and analytic mirroring tools

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The project developed and tested tools for four types of mediation: epistemic mediation for creating and working with knowledge artefacts, pragmatic mediation for organising and coordinating knowledge-creation processes, collaborative mediation for building and managing networked communities, and reflective mediation. Specific tools include the CASS-Query tool using 3G mobile phones, Map-It for collaborative knowledge creation in real time discussions, Activity System Design Tools (ASDT), Semantic Multimedia Annotation (SMAT) for video-based material, and the Visual Model Editor (VME) for creating and editing diagrams with predefined or custom visual languages. Analytic mirroring tools such as the Visual Analyzer and Timeline-Based Analyser let users depict, explore and interpret electronic digital traces of their knowledge practices iteratively and collaboratively.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tools must make knowledge creation practices visible and support reflection upon and transformation of such practices
#### Constraints
- The analytic tools rest on the premise that analysis and reflection are active, productive processes geared towards development of shared understanding, not only optimisation of a given way of working

### Target Learners
- higher education students
- researchers
- workplace teams

### Target Learning Goals
- collaborative modelling
- reflection on knowledge practices
- annotation of knowledge artefacts
