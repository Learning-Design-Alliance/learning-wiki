## Description
A standardized six-condition balance and postural-stability screening assessment used by clinicians and researchers with athletes and other populations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Balance Error Scoring System (BESS) as a static postural stability measure for dancers**: BESS is a standardized, rapid, inexpensive screening test of postural stability used in this study to measure balance in young dancers. The article states "The BESS comprises 6 conditions: double-leg, single-leg, and tandem stances on firm and foam surfaces", with 20-second trials and errors scored for movements such as lifting hands off the iliac crests, opening the eyes, or stepping, stumbling, or falling. (Notarnicola Angela et al. (2014))
