> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A framework developed by Wlodkowski and Ginsberg for designing culturally responsive instruction through the motivational conditions of inclusion, attitude, meaning, and competence.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Motivational Framework for Culturally Responsive Teaching (MFCRT) with four motivational conditions**: The article presents the Motivational Framework for Culturally Responsive Teaching (MFCRT), developed by Wlodkowski and Ginsberg (1995), as a way to ensure relevance in education and promote intrinsic motivation. It is activated through four motivational conditions that work collectively and individually: inclusion (respect and connectedness), attitude (relevance and volition), meaning (engagement and challenge), and competence (effectiveness and authenticity). Each condition pairs criteria with an explanation of how learners experience increased intrinsic motivation, such as feeling safe and able to exhibit authenticity under inclusion. (Donald E Grant Jr. et al. (2020))
