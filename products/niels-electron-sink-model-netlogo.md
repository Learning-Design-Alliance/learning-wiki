# NIELS Electron Sink model (NetLogo)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A NetLogo multi-agent simulation developed by Pratim Sengupta and colleagues that models electric current through an electron-source/electron-sink representation for school physics learning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Representing flow-rate as fictive, transient accumulation events at the agent level**: A design pattern in which an aggregate, rate-based phenomenon is represented at the individual level as discrete, dynamical events of accumulation in a container, aligning with learners' intuitive sense of mechanism. The authors frame "electrical conduction in terms of a fictive representation of accumulation of charges inside the battery-positive", and deliberately appropriate the source-sink analogy at the microscopic level: "one of the battery terminals acts as the source, whereas another acts as the sink", unlike traditional source-sink models in which the battery is the source and the whole circuit the sink. This avoided previously reported traps of the source-sink mental model while letting students bootstrap knowledge about "building up" toward a formal understanding of rate. (Pratim Sengupta et al. (2016))
