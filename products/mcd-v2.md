## Description
MCD-v2 is a computerized adaptive cognitive diagnostic test developed by Vy Le et al. to report college introductory-mechanics students' mastery of 14 fine-grained learning objectives during instruction.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Mechanics cognitive diagnostic (MCD) — a cognitive diagnostic computerized adaptive testing assessment delivered on the LASSO platform**: The MCD is a cognitive diagnostic computerized adaptive testing assessment under development for introductory mechanics: "The MCD is a cognitive diagnostic (CD) computerized adaptive testing (CA T) assessment [10]." CDs assess which skills a student has or has not mastered, and CATs adapt item selection to students' proficiency and skill mastery profiles, making assessment individualized and more efficient. The MCD will provide instructors with student-level and course-level assessments to help tailor instruction, delivered online through the LASSO platform, which is free for instructors. This study contributes its student and evidence models and an initial item bank of 102 items from three RBAs. (Vy Le et al. (2025))
