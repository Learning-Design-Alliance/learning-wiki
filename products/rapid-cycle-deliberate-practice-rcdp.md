# Rapid-cycle deliberate practice (RCDP)

## Description
A simulation-based procedural-training framework used by clinical educators that structures repeated skill cycles with immediate targeted feedback and error correction until performance improves.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Rapid-cycle deliberate practice (RCDP) framework for simulation-based procedural training**: RCDP is a simulation-based teaching method that "breaks down professional skills into steps" and guides trainees through deliberate practice in a structured sequence while providing targeted feedback, developing muscle memory and strengthening weak areas until mastery. As originally described, RCDP "emphasizes iterative feedback and immediate error correction, contrasting with TTM that provide feedback only after all steps are completed". This study extends the framework to radiology procedural training, a novel application in a high-fidelity ex vivo setting; previously it was used mainly in low-frequency, high-risk, time-sensitive skills such as neonatal resuscitation and advanced life support. (Xie Lichun et al. (2025))
- **Rapid-cycle deliberate practice: simulation with in-scenario directive feedback and repeated cycles of increasing complexity until mastery**: RCDP is a simulation-based education model, created by Hunt and grounded in Ericsson's deliberate practice, in which "learners develop their actions repeatedly (deliberate practice) for a short period (rapid cycle) in the same clinical scenario until the desired skill is mastered". When one cycle's objectives are met, a new cycle begins with increased task complexity. It "modifies the debriefing from traditional post-simulation feedback to directive feedback within an event": on non-conformity the scenario pauses, the instructor gives brief corrective information, and the scenario restarts correctly. The study applied it to pediatric CRA team training. (Pereira RI (2024))
- **Rapid cycle deliberate practice: a simulation model combining repetitive practice, targeted feedback, mastery learning, and progressive difficulty**: RCDP is a simulation instructional model the article describes as combining "repetitive practice, targeted feedback, mastery learning, and progressive difficulty". Sessions consist of consecutive rounds of increasing difficulty; learners must master predefined learning objectives before progressing. When the facilitator identifies a performance gap, the scenario is paused, direct feedback is given, and the scenario is repeated, creating a feedback-replay loop until mastery is achieved. The article contrasts this with PSD, which emphasizes reflection and discussion after scenario completion. (Coelho Laila Pinto et al (2024))
