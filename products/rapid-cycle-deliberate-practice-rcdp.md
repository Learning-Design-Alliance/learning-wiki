## Description
A simulation-based procedural-training framework used by clinical educators that structures repeated skill cycles with immediate targeted feedback and error correction until performance improves.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Rapid-cycle deliberate practice (RCDP) framework for simulation-based procedural training**: RCDP is a simulation-based teaching method that "breaks down professional skills into steps" and guides trainees through deliberate practice in a structured sequence while providing targeted feedback, developing muscle memory and strengthening weak areas until mastery. As originally described, RCDP "emphasizes iterative feedback and immediate error correction, contrasting with TTM that provide feedback only after all steps are completed". This study extends the framework to radiology procedural training, a novel application in a high-fidelity ex vivo setting; previously it was used mainly in low-frequency, high-risk, time-sensitive skills such as neonatal resuscitation and advanced life support. (Xie Lichun et al. (2025))
