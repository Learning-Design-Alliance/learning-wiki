# Wilson Reading System

## Description
The Wilson Reading System is a structured literacy curriculum developed by Wilson Language Training for teaching decoding, spelling, fluency, vocabulary, and related reading skills to learners with word-level reading difficulties.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Daily 10-part lesson plan in three instructional blocks with built-in review**: The Wilson Reading System organizes each lesson into "a daily 10-part lesson plan that builds on interaction between the teacher and student," divided into three blocks: parts one through five emphasize word study, parts six through eight emphasize spelling, and parts nine and 10 emphasize fluency and comprehension. The program sequences word-structure instruction from basic word skills to more complex language structure including morphological principles, with reviews reinforcing previous lessons. Lessons run 45–90 minutes in reading classes, small groups, or tutorials. (What Works Clearinghouse (2007))

## Description
The Wilson Reading System® is "a supplemental reading and writing curriculum designed to promote reading accuracy (decoding) and spelling (encoding) skills for students with word-level deficits." It teaches phonemic awareness, alphabetic principles, word study, spelling, sight words, fluency, vocabulary, oral language, and comprehension, using activities such as hearing sounds, practicing with syllable and word cards, and reading aloud. Two vocabulary levels (A for younger or ESL students, B for older students) support use in grade 2 through adulthood.

## Design Implications

#### Constraints

### Target Learners
- students in grade 2 and above with word-level reading deficits
- ESL students (Level A vocabulary)
- adult learners

### Target Learning Goals

- spelling (encoding)

- word study and fluency
