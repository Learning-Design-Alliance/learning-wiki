# DOE Visiting Faculty Program (VFP)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A U.S. Department of Energy-sponsored programme that places faculty and student participants from underrepresented institutions with DOE national-laboratory scientists for collaborative summer research, professional development, and networking.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **DOE Visiting Faculty Program (VFP) for faculty and students from underrepresented institutions**: The VFP is a DOE-sponsored program aiming to increase the research competitiveness of faculty at institutions typically underrepresented in the research community. For 10 weeks during the summer, a faculty member and up to two graduate or undergraduate students collaborate with a scientist or engineer at a DOE national lab on a project of mutual interest; participants receive stipends, the host lab covers housing and travel, and teams can continue for up to three consecutive summers. The authors present it as a program that builds lasting relationships and exposes participants to "state-of-the-art technology and equipment at lab facilities" they likely lack at home institutions. (Matt Bellis et al. (2022))
