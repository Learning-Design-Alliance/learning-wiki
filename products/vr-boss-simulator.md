## Description
VR-BOSS is VitaSim's immersive virtual-reality training and automated performance-assessment simulator for orthopedic surgical procedures and intraoperative fluoroscopy interpretation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **VR-BOSS immersive virtual reality simulator for distal radius fracture fixation assessment**: The VR-BOSS simulator (VitaSim, Odense, Denmark) is a commercially available immersive VR system comprising a head-mounted display, two handheld controllers, and a connected laptop, immersing users in a virtual operating room with a simulated patient. It "offers objective, automated performance metrics, unlimited practice, and radiation-free training for intraoperative fluoroscopy". Its 32 metrics were derived from a global Delphi consensus study and scored automatically. (Jacobsen Mads Emil et al. (2026))

### Claims
- [A composite total simulator score on an expert-informed iVR simulator significantly discriminated between novice and experienced orthopedic surgeons after a single procedure (Cohen's d = 4.2)](../claims/ivr-total-score-discriminates-novices-experienced-drf.md) [+M]
