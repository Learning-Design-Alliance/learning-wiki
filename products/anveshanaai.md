## Description
AnveshanaAI is an application-based adaptive learning platform developed by Rakesh Thakur et al. for AI/ML education, combining question generation, personalized delivery, interactive practice, gamification, simulation, and learning analytics.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **AnveshanaAI platform for AI/ML education with gamified adaptive learning modes**: AnveshanaAI is an application-based learning platform for artificial intelligence that combines a personalized dashboard (streaks, levels, badges) with modes including Playground, Challenges, Simulator, Dashboard, and Community. Its architecture is modular, consisting of a Question Generation Pipeline, adaptive delivery engine, multimodal interaction layer, and analytics dashboard, with Docker-based sandboxes, vector databases, and Whisper-based ASR for viva interaction. The paper positions it against static-question-repository platforms, aiming to integrate "adaptivity, gamification, interactivity, and explainability to support next-generation AI education." (Rakesh Thakur et al. (2025))
