## Description
A Benedetti-developed AI-mediated educational interaction device, deployable as a digital agent or social robot, that provides either procedural task guidance or reflective dialogue under age-sensitive configuration and governance constraints.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Amico dual-mode prototype (AmicoMio and AmicoTuo) with age-sensitive configuration**: Amico is an AI-mediated interaction device, developed as a digital agent and where feasible as a social robot, for non-clinical educational contexts, particularly technical and vocational education. AmicoMio emphasizes "precision, step-by-step reasoning, explicit framing of tasks, and concrete suggestions for action," while AmicoTuo prioritizes "maieutic questioning, reformulation, and meaning -making" without a therapeutic stance. The prototype enforces transparency of system identity, bounded sessions, dependency-prevention prompts, and data minimization, and was configured by age band (6–18, 20–40, 40–65 years) via mode-specific instruction sets and fine-tuning of the supportive mode, with per-mode sampling parameters (temperature and top-p) tuned as part of the design surface. (Pier Paolo Benedetti (2026))
