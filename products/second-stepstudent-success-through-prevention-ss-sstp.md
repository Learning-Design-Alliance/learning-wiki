# Second Step–Student Success Through Prevention (SS-SSTP)

## Description
A Committee for Children middle-school SEL curriculum for grades 6–8 that teaches empathy, emotion regulation, communication, problem-solving, friendship, and bullying-prevention skills through structured lessons and teacher training.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Second Step–Student Success Through Prevention (SS-SSTP) middle school SEL curriculum**: SS-SSTP (Committee for Children, 2008) is a middle school SEL curriculum "designed for sixth- through eighth graders" covering "empathy, communication skills, problem-solving, bully prevention, friendship skills, harassment, emotion regulation, alcohol and drug prevention" across grades, with context increasing in complexity from one grade level to the next. Fifteen lessons are delivered in sixth grade and 13 lessons at each of the seventh- and eighth-grade levels, each taking one 50-min session or two 25-min sessions. Teachers received a 4-hr training covering the research rationale, lesson walkthroughs, and fidelity strategies. (Espelage et al. (2016))
- **Second Step curriculum: a classroom-based SEL and character development program for preschool through 8th grade**: Second Step is a comprehensive, classroom-based social-emotional learning and character development curriculum aimed at inculcating skills in "the areas of empathy, perspective taking, problem solving, self-control or self-regulation, and anger management or emotion regulation for preschool through 8th grade". It makes "extensive use of social learning theory" and "social information-processing models of children's social behavior". In this study, 19 schools implemented it for four consecutive school semesters while 16 schools did not. (Top et al. (2016))
