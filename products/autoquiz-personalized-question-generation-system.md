# AutoQuiz personalized question generation system

## Description
AutoQuiz is a personalized computer-aided question-generation and quiz-selection system developed for the IWiLL English learning platform by Yi-Ting Huang and colleagues.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **AutoQuiz personalized question generation system on the IWiLL platform**: The AutoQuiz Project is the article's implemented personalized computer-aided question generation system, deployed on "the IWiLL learning platform", which offered learners an online English reading and writing environment. It automatically generated "2,481 questions with difficulties (from one to six)" from 72 news stories collected from online news websites, and selected quizzes from fit, history-based, and challenging questions. Each test contained ten vocabulary questions, five grammar questions, and three reading comprehension questions, and proficiency levels corresponded to the six semesters of senior high school in Taiwan. (Yi-Ting Huang et al. (2014))
