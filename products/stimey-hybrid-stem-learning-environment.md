# STIMEY hybrid STEM learning environment

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
STIMEY is a European Horizon 2020 project learning environment that combines a social web platform, e-portfolio, serious games, entrepreneurial tools, digital learning facilities, and socially assistive robots for STEM learning among 10- to 18-year-olds.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **STIMEY hybrid STEM learning environment combining virtual and physical components**: The STIMEY environment is a hybrid learning environment for 10-18-year-olds developed in a European Horizon 2020 project across Belarus, Finland, Germany, Greece and Spain. The article states it "consists of components such as a social web platform, e-portfolio, serious games, entrepreneurial tools, a digital radio (virtual learning environment) as well as physical socially assistive robots". It connects stakeholders to increase students' interest and motivation in STEM education, innovations and careers, and the co-designed principles were employed in its development. (Mäkelä et al. (2022))
