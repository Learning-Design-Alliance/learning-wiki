Linguatorium¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Linguatorium is a web-based computer-assisted language-learning system that automatically generates and schedules vocabulary-learning activities under instructor curriculum control.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Lexical units brought to the 'fully learned' stage by the tutor scored significantly lower on the posttest than units students already knew before introduction [-W]
  • Supplemental computer-based spaced repetition activities nearly triple long-term vocabulary retention in EFL students compared with conventional instruction alone [+W]
  • Posttest scores increase monotonically across the tutoring algorithm's acquisition stages, supporting its stage criteria, though control-group items did not differ from items still in active acquisition [+W]
