Incredible Years Teacher Classroom Management (IY TCM) program¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 8 claims (8 for) · 4 studies (4 causal), q3 · 2 of 4 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A six-day universal teacher-training and coaching programme developed by The Incredible Years for elementary teachers, focused on teacher–student and teacher–parent relationships and effective classroom management.

Components¶

  • Incredible Years Teacher Classroom Management Program (IYT): IYT is a teacher training program within a series of parent, child, and teacher interventions designed to prevent and treat aggressive behavior and conduct problems in young children aged 3-8 years. Its approach includes "video-modeling, behavioral rehearsal of key skills through numerous role plays, classroom practice assignments, and teacher goal setting and self-monitoring." In the study it was delivered in five monthly full-day workshops led by two trained co-leaders with approximately 12-15 teachers per group, plus two brief classroom consultation visits and regular support emails. (Murray et al. (2014))

Claims¶

  • IY TCM intervention teachers were more likely to rate parents in the most adaptive involvement profiles at follow-up compared to control teachers [+M]
  • IY TCM improves elementary students' emotional regulation [+M]
  • IY TCM improves elementary students' social competence [+M]
  • IY TCM teachers implement higher rates of proactive classroom management strategies over time than control teachers [+M]
  • IYT produced significantly greater improvement in classroom Positive Climate than control over time in grades K-2 [+M]
  • IYT improved STAR Reading growth in first grade but not second grade [+M]
  • The IY TCM program reduced teacher-reported student emotional dysregulation compared to control classrooms (d = −0.14) [+M]
  • The IY TCM program increased teacher-reported prosocial behavior and overall social competence (d = 0.13 each) [+M]
