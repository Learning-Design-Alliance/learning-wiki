# 33-item Arabic multi-informant SEL questionnaire based on the CASEL five-skill model

## Description
An Arabic online questionnaire with parallel student, parent, and teacher forms that measures five CASEL social-emotional learning competencies in students in grades 3, 5, 7, and 9.
