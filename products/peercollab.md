# PeerCollab

## Description
PeerCollab is a web-based socio-technical platform developed by the authors for creating, facilitating, and participating in rapid learning communities among MOOC learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PeerCollab platform for MOOC learning communities**: PeerCollab is a web-based socio-technical platform (React front-end, Node.js back-end, mySQL database) that lets MOOC learners create, find and join "rapid communities" with forums, notice boards, profiles, likes, private messaging and leader-driven activities. The article states: "Our system provides 1) a domain for a group of online learners to create a community, 2) a practice for facilitation, and system 3) a community to learn together throughout the MOOC course they decide to take as a group." It was evaluated with a controlled experiment and a field study. (Together we learn better: leveraging communities of practice for MOOC learners)

### Claims
- [In a randomized between-subject experiment, MOOC learners using PeerCollab reported significantly higher sense of belonging on all six scale items than learners using the standard MOOC forum.](../claims/peercollab-higher-sense-of-belonging-than-forum.md) [+M]
- [In the 6-week field study, participants created five rapid communities generating 286 threads and averaging 2.4 hours of use per member, with engagement concentrated in the Creative Problem solving community (44% of discussion).](../claims/field-study-five-communities-uneven-engagement.md) [+M]
