# Protoplanet Express

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A first-person educational and science-outreach video game developed by Jorge Cuadra and collaborators that lets players explore protoplanetary discs rendered from hydrodynamical simulation data.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Protoplanet Express, a first-person video game built from peer-reviewed protoplanetary-disc simulations**: Protoplanet Express is a video game based on hydrodynamical simulations of protoplanetary discs, developed as an outreach product. The player pilots a spaceship with a cockpit, observation deck and chart room, and explores discs rendered from SPH gas and dust simulation data. The authors state: "We are developing 'Protoplanet Express', a video game based on hydrodynamical simulations of protoplanetary discs." It uses Unity with Visual Effect Graph and Blender, and renders roughly a million particles. (Jorge Cuadra et al. (2023))
