# DOPS-based eight-step BLS mannequin skill assessment

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A study-developed observational assessment instrument created by Kochhar et al. for certified BLS instructors to rate first-year MBBS students' performance on eight BLS mannequin-skill steps.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **DOPS-based eight-step BLS mannequin skill assessment with validity and reliability evidence**: A Directly Observed Procedural Skills (DOPS) scoring system for assessing BLS performance on mannequins, comprising eight checklist steps (scene safety, responsiveness, emergency activation, hand placement, compression rate/depth, chest recoil, airway opening, breath delivery) each rated sufficient, partially sufficient, or insufficient, yielding scores from 8 to 24 where lower scores indicated better skills. The authors report "Cronbach's alpha coefficient (0.812) was calculated to determine the internal consistency and reliability of the DOPS scoring system," and note studies found DOPS works well for undergraduate skills in laboratory settings on mannequins. (Kochhar S et al. (2024))
