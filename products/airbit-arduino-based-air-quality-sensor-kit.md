## Description
The air:bit is an Arduino-based air-quality sensor kit and accompanying guides developed for students to assemble, program, and use for environmental data collection.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **air:bit Arduino-based air quality sensor kit**: The air:bit is a small microcontroller-based data logger students solder, assemble and program to measure dust particles (PM2.5 and PM10 via the Nova SDS011), air temperature and humidity (DHT22), GPS location, time and date, writing CSV data to a memory card. A custom PCB with pre-defined pins fits on the Arduino UNO to simplify assembly, and the kit is enclosed in a laser-cut box with an external battery for portability. The second version's Nova SDS011 allows data comparable to official measurement stations, and the total component cost is listed at $67.50. Students build complete solutions of around 500 lines of code from example programs typically less than 100 lines each. (Bjørn Fjukstad et al. (2019))
