# Iowa Assessments

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Iowa Assessments are nationally normed, school-administered achievement tests measuring student performance in reading, mathematics, science, and related academic domains, developed and distributed by Riverside Insights.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Iowa Assessments obtained in a school setting as an ecologically valid measure of academic performance**: The Iowa Assessments are validated, annually administered achievement tests measuring reading, math, and science in Iowa public and private schools. The article uses school-administered Iowa Assessment scores precisely because psychological achievement tests lack ecological validity: "these measures are typically administered in a quiet testing space with a dedicated examiner. By contrast, most school activities occur while experiencing significant environmental distractions." The tests show composite reliability coefficients of 0.927–0.983 and strong associations with ACT scores. (Al‐Kaylani HM et al. (2022))
