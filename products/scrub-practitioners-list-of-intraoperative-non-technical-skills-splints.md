# Scrub Practitioner's List of Intraoperative Non-Technical Skills (SPLINTS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
SPLINTS is a behavioral rating system and handbook developed for training and assessing scrub practitioners' situation awareness, communication and teamwork, and task-management skills in operating-room settings.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SPLINTS taxonomy of scrub practitioners' intraoperative non-technical skills**: The Scrub Practitioner's List of Intraoperative Non-Technical Skills (SPLINTS) is "a behavioral rating system used as a structured tool for training and assessing the intraoperative non-technical skills of scrub practitioners". It comprises three categories — situation awareness, communication and teamwork, and task management — each with three key elements (e.g. gathering information, acting assertively, planning and preparing), and "provides examples of good and poor performance". The trial translated the SPLINTS handbook and built its questionnaire items and video scenarios from the taxonomy's classification elements. (Mohammadi Masoumeh et al. (2025))
