# MAC scale measuring use of the Comprehensive Aquatic Method

## Description
A 26-item Likert self-report scale developed by Castañón-Rubio et al. to measure aquatic educators' reported use of the Comprehensive Aquatic Method.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MAC scale (Castañón-Rubio et al., 2022) measuring use of the Comprehensive Aquatic Method**: A validated 26-item scale used with permission to measure educators' use of the Comprehensive Aquatic Method, with items rated on a Likert scale from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree). It comprises three subscales — way of teaching (10 items), game (9 items) and a third subscale of 10 items — with internal consistency of .93, .88 and .87 respectively and .91 for the global MAC. In this study it supplied the MAC scores used in the correlation and regression analyses. (Fonseca-Pinto R et al. (2024))
