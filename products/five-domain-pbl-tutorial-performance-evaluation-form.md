# Five-domain PBL tutorial performance evaluation form

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A tutor-completed assessment form developed by Pu Dan et al. to rate medical students' PBL tutorial performance across participation, preparation, communication skills, critical discussion, and teamwork.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Five-domain PBL tutorial performance evaluation form (participation, preparation, communication skills, critical discussion, teamwork)**: A tutor-completed evaluation form developed by the authors from literature review and their school's PBL objectives. Each of the five domains — "participation, preparation, communication skills, critical discussion, and teamwork" — is rated on five levels worth 20 scores, and students are evaluated by their tutors immediately after case learning. The form operationalises PBL performance in this study. (Pu Dan et al. (2019))
