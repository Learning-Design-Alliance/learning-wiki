## Description
Microsoft Teams is Microsoft's collaboration and communication platform for chat, video conferencing, document sharing, screen sharing, and collaborative work, including educational use.

## Components

- **Microsoft Teams as a distributed pair programming platform**: The study used Microsoft Teams as the primary platform enabling distributed pair programming, providing a shared editor, support for the roles of the driver and navigator and their role changing, a chat tool for communication, and remote code highlighting. Students used it to discuss, conduct video conference, share documents, co-edit or co-program, and share and see each other's screens outside of class hours. (Tsai Chia-Wen et al. (2023))
