# SEENET-MTP Network

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A regional higher-education and research network run by its governing committees and permanent Office at the University of Nis to connect institutions and support training and collaboration in mathematical and theoretical physics.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SEENET-MTP Network as regional infrastructure bridging high schools, higher education and research**: The Southeastern European Network in Mathematical and Theoretical Physics was agreed at the UNESCO-ROSTE-sponsored BW2003 workshop and links high schools, universities and research in the region. By 2009 "17 institutions from 8 c ountries" had joined, with 11 partner institutions worldwide and about 250 individual members. Its activities include PhD training programmes, a map of top regional research groups built from 2000-2009 publication records, 11 scientific meetings with about 750 participants, about 150 short-term scientific exchanges, 5 mid-term fellowships, joint regional physics contests and popular lectures, and it indirectly supports the Nis special physics class. (G.S. Djordjevic et al. (2011))
