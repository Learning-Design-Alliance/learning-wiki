# SDMA and SPS OSCE rating scales for shared decision-making attitudes in end-of-life care

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q1`–`q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Examiner- and standardized-patient-scored OSCE rating scales developed by Lin Mei-Hsiang et al. to assess nursing professionals’ empathic communication and shared decision-making attitudes and process skills in end-of-life care.
