## Description
The Holodeck is Burleson et al.'s envisioned networked experiential supercomputer integrating multimodal interaction, sensing, analytics, and human–agent collaboration for education, research, and innovation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MedicalHolodeck Anatomy Master VR module with plane transection tool for anatomy review**: The VR component of the intervention was the Anatomy Master module of MedicalHolodeck, run on an HTC VIVE Pro headset (1440x1600 resolution, 90Hz refresh rate) with dual controllers. The article states the software "provides VR anatomical models" and "offers the ability to transect anatomical models in any angle using a VR controller," a function described as similar to manipulating an ultrasound probe, and that it "allows users to easily take screenshots and save them for later review." Intervention participants captured screenshots of the interactive 3D model in place of the control group's electronic atlas. (Hu Kai-Chun et al. (2020))
