## Description
A biology-learning approach developed by the Biology Department of FMIPA UNNES that integrates environmental exploration, scientific work, constructivist learning, learning communities, edutainment, and authentic assessment in indoor and outdoor instruction.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Jelajah Alam Sekitar (JAS) approach with six defining characteristics**: JAS is a learning approach that "uses the student's nature environment, whether the physical, social, technology, or culture environment as the learning object in biology" whose phenomena are learned through scientific work. It is characterized by six components — exploration, constructivism, science process, learning community, edutainment, and authentic assessment — integrated into indoor and outdoor learning. Developed by the Biology Department of FMIPA UNNES since 2005, it spread to eight Indonesian provinces and has been studied from primary school to college. (S. Ngabekti et al. (2017))
