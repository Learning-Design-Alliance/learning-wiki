# Augustus F. Hawkins Center for Excellence grant program

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A competitive federal grant programme administered by the U.S. Department of Education that supports teacher-preparation programmes at HBCUs, minority-serving institutions, and tribal colleges and universities.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Augustus F. Hawkins Center for Excellence grant program for teacher preparation at HBCUs, minority-serving institutions, and tribal colleges**: The Hawkins Center for Excellence program "provides competitive grants to teacher preparation programs at historically Black colleges and universities (HBCUs), minority-serving institutions, and tribal colleges and universities." Congress appropriated $8 million for Hawkins grants in 2022, the first funding since the program's creation in 2008. Required grant uses include inclusive pedagogical practices and sustained, high-quality preservice clinical experiences. (The Education Trust (2022))
