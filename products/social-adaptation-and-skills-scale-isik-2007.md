# Social Adaptation and Skills Scale (Isik, 2007)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A 25-item parent- or teacher-completed Likert assessment for measuring social adaptation and social maladaptation in children aged 4–11.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Social Adaptation and Skills Scale (Isik, 2007) for children aged 4-11**: A 25-item scale completed by a child's mother, father or teacher, with a 17-item social adaptation subtest (communication, making friends, empathy, cooperation, tolerance, obeying rules) and an 8-item social maladaptation subtest (fighting, interrupting, harming the environment), both 3-point Likert type. The article reports factor analysis with a Kaiser-Meyer-Olkin value of 0.88 and Bartlett value of 2930.99 (p = 0.000 < 0.05), factor load values of 0.509 and above, and reliability of α = .93 for social adaptation and α = 0.83 for social maladaptation. (Hikmet Zelyurt (2018))
