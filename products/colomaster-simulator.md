## Description
COLOMASTER is a five-feature, anatomy-faithful dry-lab simulator design developed by Hasegawa et al. for surgical-resident training in membrane-layer dissection and anatomical recognition.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Five-feature dry-lab simulator design: bonded multilayer membranes, anatomical reproducibility, electrocautery-compatible materials, color-coded layers, and uniform kits**: The article enumerates COLOMASTER's design characteristics as a reusable template for anatomy-faithful dry-lab simulators: "(1) a multilayered membrane structure created using bonding technology, (2) high reproducibility of human anatomy, (3) materials that can be dissected using an electric scalpel, (4) visualization of differences in the structure of the anatomical layers using colored polyester fibers, and (5) uniform training kits." Peeling strength is controlled via per-layer adhesive strength so learners can dissect along the correct layer, enabling CME training. Colored fibers make layer structure visible, which the authors say makes it ideal for resident training. (Hasegawa H. et al. (2025))
