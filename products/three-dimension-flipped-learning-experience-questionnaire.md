# Three-dimension flipped learning experience questionnaire

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A 14-item self-report questionnaire measuring undergraduate students' flipped-learning experience across materials and session activities, lecturer's work, and comparisons with traditional classes.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Three-dimension structure of the flipped learning experience: materials and session activities, lecturer's work, and comparison with traditional classes**: The article proposes and empirically confirms a three-factor structure for students' flipped learning experience: (1) materials and session activities (items 1–5), (2) lecturer's work (items 6–8), and (3) comparison with traditional classes (items 9–14). The authors argue these factors 'represent key factors for analysing the experience of flipped learning environments among university students,' with pre-class materials enabling self-paced preparation, the lecturer shaping engagement, and comparison items evaluating FL's impact relative to traditional classes. (Urcola‐Pardo et al. (2024))
