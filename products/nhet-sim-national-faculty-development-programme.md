# NHET-Sim national faculty development programme

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A national Australian faculty-development programme that provides health and social care professionals with introductory skills and concepts for facilitating simulation-based education.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **NHET-Sim national faculty development programme and its reflective-prompt narrative dataset**: The National Health and Education Training Simulation (NHET-Sim) programme has since 2011 provided "thousands of health and social care professionals across Australia with the introductory skills and concepts necessary to facilitate SBE". Its introductory module includes an online reflective prompt — describe the most powerful learning experience you have had using simulation — from which the study extracted 327 narratives from 2624 consenting participants (52% of 5033 completers between October 2012 and January 2015). Contributors were predominantly nursing (56%) and medicine (24%), average age 44 years. (Bearman Margaret et al. (2019))
