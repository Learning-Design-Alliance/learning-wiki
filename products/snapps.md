## Description
SNAPPS is a six-step learner-centred clinical-teaching framework developed for outpatient education in which learners summarise, narrow, analyse, probe, plan, and pursue self-directed learning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SNAPPS as a learner-centered six-step case-presentation model for expressing clinical reasoning**: SNAPPS is a mnemonic guiding the learner to structure the case presentation into six steps: Summarize history and findings; Narrow the differential; Analyze the differential; Probe the preceptor about uncertainties; Plan management; and Select case-related issues for self-study. Unlike the OMP method, which gives instructions to teachers, SNAPPS provides them to learners, helping learners understand what is expected of them and take on a central role during the case presentation. The article positions it as both a learning and an assessment tool, allowing teachers to detect gaps in trainees' knowledge or reasoning. (Fagundes E. D. T. et al. (2020))
