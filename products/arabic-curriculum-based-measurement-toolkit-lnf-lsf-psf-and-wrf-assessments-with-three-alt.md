# Arabic curriculum-based measurement toolkit: LNF, LSF, PSF, and WRF assessments with three alternate forms each

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A researcher-developed Arabic curriculum-based measurement toolkit for assessing letter naming, letter sounds, phoneme segmentation, and word-reading fluency in Saudi kindergarten and grade 1 students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Arabic curriculum-based measurement toolkit: LNF, LSF, PSF, and WRF assessments with three alternate forms each**: A set of four researcher-developed Arabic CBM assessment tools for early literacy: Letter Naming Fluency, Letter Sound Fluency, Phoneme Segmentation Fluency, and Word Reading Fluency. "The materials used in this study consisted of four assessment tools, each with three alternate forms, developed by the researcher based on well-established English-language CBM tools such as easyCBM, FAST, and DIBELS." Each is a one-minute timed task; alternate-form reliability coefficients exceeded 0.92, and word lists drew on the Saudi elementary Arabic curriculum vocabulary. (Alqahtani SS (2025))
