Regional Educational Laboratory (REL) Program¶

Description¶

The Institute of Education Sciences' REL Program connects practitioners, researchers, and policymakers through regional research, technical assistance, and engagement activities.

Components¶

  • Research alliance model of practitioner-researcher engagement: The article describes the research alliance model as a way of engaging with practitioners throughout the research process, from study design through interpretation of findings. Under IES's 2012-17 REL program, alliances "connect practitioners, researchers, and policymakers around educational challenges in each region," carrying out regional research, technical assistance, and engagement projects. The article notes many researchers lack experience with this model, motivating the professional learning community's work. (No author line printed (2017))
