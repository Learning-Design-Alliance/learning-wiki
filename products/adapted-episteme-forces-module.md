# Adapted epiSTEMe Forces module

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A nine-lesson Forces and Movement curriculum package with teaching notes, student booklets, and presentation slides, adapted from epiSTEMe for the Chilean fourth-grade science context.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Adapted epiSTEMe Forces module with dialogic, argumentation-fostering lesson plans**: An adapted version of the epiSTEMe Forces module used as the intervention curriculum: nine 45-minute lessons on Forces and Movement, with 48-page teaching notes, student booklets and PowerPoint slides. "EpiSTEMe Forces lessons are based on carefully structured problem situations designed to activate students' wider experiences and to promote a scientific inquiring attitude in order to develop scientific concepts." Each lesson moved from a whole-class warm-up through small-group problem-solving, much of it hypothesis-testing (predict, test hands-on, discuss and agree on a conclusion), to whole-class plenaries. The adaptation redesigned five lessons for the Chilean curriculum. (Larrain et al. (2018))
