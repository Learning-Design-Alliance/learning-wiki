## Description
A yearlong professional-development program developed and run by BSCS Science Learning in which science teachers analyze lessons through summer institutes and ongoing study-group sessions.

## Components

- **STeLLA® cost structure**: Implementing STeLLA® costs approximately $1,750 per teacher for the yearlong program, covering the 2-week summer institute and eight monthly study group sessions. A 2-year leadership development program for personnel to become STeLLA® professional development leaders costs approximately $10,000 per leader but is not required. Schools and districts pay BSCS Science Learning for the program and are responsible for facilities, transportation, and videorecording costs; program fees cover teacher materials. (Science Teachers Learning from Lesson Analysis (STeLLA))
- **STeLLA® professional development program**: STeLLA® (Science Teachers Learning through Lesson Analysis) is a yearlong professional development program developed by BSCS Science Learning that aims to improve student science achievement by improving teachers' science content knowledge and their abilities to explain science concepts, identify the science concepts used in learning activities, and engage students in thinking about science. It combines a 2-week summer institute (60 hours) led by postsecondary science faculty with eight monthly school-based study group meetings (30 hours) in which teachers analyze videorecorded lessons. Teachers receive model lesson plans, a strategy guide, and lesson analysis protocols. (Science Teachers Learning from Lesson Analysis (STeLLA))

# STeLLA® professional development program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
STeLLA® (Science Teachers Learning through Lesson Analysis) is a yearlong professional development program developed by BSCS Science Learning that aims to improve student science achievement by improving teachers' science content knowledge and their abilities to explain science concepts, identify the science concepts used in learning activities, and engage students in thinking about science. It combines a 2-week summer institute (60 hours) led by postsecondary science faculty with eight monthly school-based study group meetings (30 hours) in which teachers analyze videorecorded lessons. Teachers receive model lesson plans, a strategy guide, and lesson analysis protocols.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers videorecord their own classroom instruction and meet in study groups with a STeLLA® professional development leader to analyze one another's lessons
#### Constraints
- Personnel who wish to become STeLLA® professional development leaders require an additional 1 to 2 years of training
- The leadership development component was not part of the study that contributes to this intervention report

### Target Learners
- Teachers who provide science instruction to students in kindergarten to grade 12

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving teachers' ability to explain science concepts to students
- Clearly identifying to students the science concepts used in student learning activities
- Engaging students in thinking about science
