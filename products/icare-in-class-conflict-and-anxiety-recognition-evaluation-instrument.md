## Description
A brief end-of-class survey instrument developed by Lei Bao and colleagues to assess students' experiences of cognitive conflict, associated anxiety, and reactions in inquiry-based learning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **iCARE: In-class Conflict and Anxiety Recognition Evaluation instrument**: iCARE is a quantitative survey instrument measuring two assessment constructs: "the context elements of the instruction and curriculum that trigger cognitive conflicts" and "students’ feelings and reactions in responding to their cognitive conflicts." It has four parts: contexts leading to discrepancies, feelings during conflict, an anxiety estimate scored on a 5-point Likert scale with a cutoff of 9 separating low and high anxiety, and reactions with an open-ended item. The words "anxiety" and "conflict" never appear in the instrument; items are descriptive of students' experiences. It can be completed in a few minutes at the end of class and is available as supplementary material. (Lei Bao et al. (2013))
