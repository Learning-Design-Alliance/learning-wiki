# NCSBN Clinical Judgment Measurement Model (NCJMM)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The NCJMM is a six-step clinical-judgment framework developed by the National Council of State Boards of Nursing (NCSBN) for structuring and assessing nursing students’ clinical judgment.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **NCSBN Clinical Judgment Measurement Model (NCJMM) as the structural framework for AI-generated vignette items**: The NCJMM, developed by NCSBN, "delineates a six-step clinical judgment process: recognizing cues, analyzing cues, prioritizing hypotheses, generating solutions, taking action, and evaluating outcomes." In VEGA, each of the six stages shaped item structure, with one item per stage and prompt instructions operationalized to reflect each step's cognitive demands. This alignment ensured item sets were clinically contextualized and pedagogically grounded in a recognized nursing framework. (Shin Sujin et al. (2026))
