# Every Circuit

## Description
Every Circuit is a web-based and mobile interactive circuit simulator developed by MuseMaze that lets learners construct circuits and observe electrical quantities and behavior.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Every Circuit web application for virtual circuit building**: Every Circuit (www.everycircuit.com/app) is a web-based application that the article used as the technology module in a high school physics class. It "enabled students to build circuits in a virtual setting", letting students construct series or parallel circuits by adding resistors, add a light bulb as an indicator of current flow, and see current, voltage, and resistance displayed in numerical values. The virtual light bulb showed whether a circuit was running properly, letting students correct their own mistakes. (Patrick Gryczka et al. (2016))
