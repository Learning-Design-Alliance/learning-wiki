# T-MED: the first large-scale multimodal teacher sentiment analysis dataset

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A multimodal dataset of 14,938 teacher-emotion instances from classroom-related text, audio, video, and instructional contexts, created by Zhiyi Duan et al. for teacher sentiment-recognition research.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **T-MED: the first large-scale multimodal teacher sentiment analysis dataset**: T-MED is a dataset of "14,938 instances of teacher emotional data from 250 real classrooms across 11 subjects ranging from K-12 to higher education, integrating multimodal text, audio, video, and instructional information", totalling over 17 hours from more than 200 teachers. It carries eight emotion labels — neutral, anger, joy, surprise, sadness, plus the teacher-specific patience, enthusiasm, and expectation. Labels were produced by a human-machine collaborative annotation framework combining pre-trained model pre-labeling, expert correction, fine-tuning, and a five-expert voting review requiring at least four experts to confirm each label. (Zhiyi Duan et al. (2025))
