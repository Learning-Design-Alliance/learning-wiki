## Description
Fair Grader 2000 is NISSIN's standardized non-contact 3D scanning and scoring system for objectively evaluating dental cavity-preparation morphology, cutting volume, and volume ratio against a reference model.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Fair Grader 2000 digital teaching practice evaluation system for objective post-hoc cavity preparation scoring**: Fair Grader 2000 (NISSIN, Japan) is a standardized digital evaluation system for dental cavity preparation. The article describes it as combining "scanning and 3D imaging technology", using a non-contact 3D scan of the tooth preparation to score cavity morphology, cutting volume and volume ratio against a standard model, with a maximum score of 100 points. Unlike real-time systems it cannot provide feedback during preparation, but it requires no special instruments, can be sent for assessment post-operation, and was used here for baseline and final scoring of faculty competence. (Zhang Yuejiao et al. (2025))
