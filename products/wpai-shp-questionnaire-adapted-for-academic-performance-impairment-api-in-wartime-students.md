# WPAI: SHP questionnaire adapted for academic performance impairment (API) in wartime students

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A university-student adaptation of the Work Productivity and Activity Impairment: Special Health Problems questionnaire that measures self-rated academic performance impairment and mental-health-related study absenteeism over a 14-day recall period.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **WPAI: SHP questionnaire adapted for academic performance impairment (API) in wartime students**: The study adapted version 2.0 of the Work Productivity and Activity Impairment: Special Health Problems questionnaire for university students. The authors state that "we replaced the phrase “work productivity” with “academic performance”" and related wording, and extended the recall timeframe from 7 to 14 days based on expert opinion. Answers to Q5 yield the API variable (0–10 self-rated academic performance impairment), and Q2–Q4 yield the HM absenteeism variable used as regression outcomes. (Pinchuk Irina et al. (2025))
