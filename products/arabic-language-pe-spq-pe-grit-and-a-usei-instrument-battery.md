# Arabic-language PE-SPQ, PE-Grit and A-USEI instrument battery

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An Arabic-language battery of validated self-report scales developed for measuring study processes, grit, and academic engagement among university physical-education and sport students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Arabic-language instrument battery: PE-SPQ, PE-Grit and A-USEI for PES students**: The study used three contextually appropriate Arabic scales: the 20-item Physical Education-Study Process Questionnaire (deep/surface × theory/practice tasks), the 16-item PE-Grit (physical and academic interest and effort), and the 15-item A-USEI (behavioral, cognitive, emotional engagement). Reliabilities were strong: "The McDonald internal consistency indices across all four PE-SPQ components ranged from 0.86 to 0.94", with CFA fit indices also reported for each scale. (Tannoubi Amayra et al. (2023))
