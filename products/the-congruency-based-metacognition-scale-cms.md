# The Congruency-based Metacognition Scale (CMS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A paired child–parent questionnaire developed by dos Santos Kawata et al. that measures metacognitive ability and self-judgment accuracy through perspective-taking congruency ratings.
