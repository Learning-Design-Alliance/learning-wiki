## Description
A University of Washington research-based introductory-physics curriculum of guided, worksheet-based small-group recitation tutorials for developing conceptual understanding, implemented with trained teaching assistants and undergraduate learning assistants.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Tutorials in Introductory Physics with Learning Assistants in weekly recitation sections**: Tutorials in Introductory Physics are research-based recitation materials from the University of Washington implemented in 50-minute weekly recitation sections, staffed by a graduate Teaching Assistant plus one or two undergraduate Learning Assistants who had previously performed well in the course. TAs and LAs are prepared in a weekly meeting where they review online pretest responses and work through the upcoming Tutorial in small groups just as students do. At CU the materials were implemented in converted basement laboratory bays with seven tables of four students. (Steven J. Pollock et al. (2008))

### Claims
- [All semesters using research-based Tutorials in recitation produced higher learning gains than any semester with traditional recitations](../claims/tutorials-beat-traditional-recitations.md) [+M]
