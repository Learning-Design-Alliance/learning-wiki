# Enhanced Proactive Reading curriculum: 120 daily small-group lessons across five content strands

## Description
Enhanced Proactive Reading is a comprehensive, integrated reading, language arts, and English language development curriculum targeted to first-grade English language learners experiencing difficulty with conventional reading instruction. It "provides 120 daily lessons, each composed of 6–10 short activities, to be delivered throughout the school year", delivered to small groups of three to five students in approximately 50-minute sessions. Lessons focus on five content strands: phonemic awareness, letter knowledge, word recognition, connected text fluency, and comprehension strategies. Teachers model new content, use visual aids and gestures to clarify vocabulary, and monitor student responses in fast-paced lessons with largely choral responding.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered by trained teachers (12 hours of professional development prior to implementation and six additional hours six weeks in, plus on-site coaching) as a supplement to core reading instruction
#### Constraints
- Implemented only with first-grade students; the developers do not indicate if it is applicable to all elementary English language learners
- No cost information is available for the program

### Target Learners
- First-grade English language learners at risk for reading problems (below the 25th percentile on Letter Word Identification)

### Target Learning Goals
- Phonemic awareness
- Letter knowledge and letter-sound correspondence
- Word recognition
- Connected text fluency
- Comprehension strategies
- English language and vocabulary development
