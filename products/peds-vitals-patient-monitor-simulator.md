# PEDS VITALS patient monitor simulator

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
PEDS VITALS is a computerized paediatric patient-monitor application that lets an instructor control and display an infant mannequin’s vital signs during simulation-based clinical training.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PEDS VITALS patient monitor simulator for live vital-sign display during infant simulation**: PEDS VITALS is a computerized paediatric patient monitor simulator application used in the case simulation clinic to display the low-fidelity infant mannequin's vital signs in real time. The article states it "was used to provide the instant display of vital signs of saturation level, heart rate and respiratory rate of the low fidelity mannequin throughout the session", controlled by the instructor from a tablet device, complemented by verbal narration of clinical signs such as coughing and SpO2 readings. (Kwan Connie Ching-Yin et al. (2025))
