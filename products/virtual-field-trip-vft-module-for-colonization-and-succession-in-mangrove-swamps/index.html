Virtual Field Trip (VFT) module for Colonization and Succession in Mangrove Swamps¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A researcher-developed interactive multimedia biology-learning programme that uses text, graphics, video, audio, photographs, communication features, and links to teach Form Four students about mangrove colonization, succession, and plant adaptations.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Students taught with a VFT module show significantly higher overall achievement gains than students taught with conventional methods for Colonization and Succession in Mangrove Ecosystems [+M]
  • The VFT group outperformed the conventional group on all three item types, while the conventional group improved only on objective items [+M]
  • VFT-taught students significantly outperformed conventionally taught students on structured items for Colonization and Succession in Mangrove Ecosystems [+W]
  • VFT-taught students significantly outperformed conventionally taught students on objective items for Colonization and Succession in Mangrove Ecosystems [+W]
  • A significant Group x Time interaction indicates the VFT group's overall achievement improved more from pre-test to post-test than the conventional group's [+W]
  • VFT-taught students significantly outperformed conventionally taught students on the essay item for Colonization and Succession in Mangrove Ecosystems [+W]
