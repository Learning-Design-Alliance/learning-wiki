## Description
A four-category institutional framework proposed by Zeng Aiying et al. for medical simulation-center infrastructure, faculty development, curricular integration, and quality assurance under competency-based medical education.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **A four-domain framework of 43 core competences for public health emergency response in medical students**: The article develops a competence framework for public health emergency response education of public health majors, built via a two-round Delphi process with 21 experts in Fujian province. It contains 43 statements grouped into four domains: "intellectual competences, practical competences, behavioral competences, personnel and motivation". The intellectual domain covers knowledge such as epidemiology and laws; the practical domain covers outbreak investigation, information management and epidemic judgment; the behavioral domain covers professionalism, comprehensive quality and attitudes; the motivation domain covers innovation, learning and professional identity. (Liu Fengqiong et al. (2025))
