## Description
A point-of-care ultrasound training and credentialling programme run by the Emergency Medicine Society of South Africa for emergency-care clinicians, combining a basic course, reflective practice, and written and practical competency examinations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **EMSSA four-stage international approach to PoCUS training and credentialling**: The Emergency Medicine Society of South Africa's PoCUS training and credentialling programme, established in 2008, follows "the international approach to PoCUS training, which comprises four stages: introduction, gaining experience, confirmation of competency, and skills maintenance". Credentialling involves attending a basic PoCUS course, reflective practice, and a written and practical exam, after which candidates are accredited to use basic PoCUS independently. EMSSA defines competency as knowing when to use an application, acquiring and optimising images, and correctly interpreting and integrating findings into patient care plans. The study used this framework as the benchmark for assessing training and competency. (Ferreira K et al. (2025))
