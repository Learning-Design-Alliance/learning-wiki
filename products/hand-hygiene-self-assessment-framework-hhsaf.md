# Hand Hygiene Self-Assessment Framework (HHSAF)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A World Health Organization framework and scoring instrument that helps healthcare facilities assess hand-hygiene implementation, identify improvement targets, and recognize leadership-level performance.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **HHSAF levels of hand hygiene implementation progress taxonomy**: The HHSAF assigns each healthcare facility to one of four levels of hand hygiene implementation progress based on its overall score out of 500: Inadequate (0–125), Basic (126–250), Intermediate (251–375) and Advanced (376–500), each with a printed definition of what the score implies about practices and promotion. An additional Leadership level applies to Advanced facilities meeting at least 12 of 20 leadership criteria with at least one criterion per category. The article's three case scenarios are scored against this taxonomy to train standardized completion. (Tartari E et al. (2019))

### Claims
- [Healthcare facilities that participated in both WHO HHSAF global surveys showed a significantly higher overall hand hygiene score in 2015 than in 2011](../claims/hhsaf-2015-score-increase-over-2011.md) [+M]
