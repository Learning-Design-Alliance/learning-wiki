## Description
PLAN A is a tabletop card-based serious game developed by Mohar Sebastian et al. for practicing airway-management decision-making and escalation reasoning among novice medical trainees.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PLAN A tabletop serious game for airway management decision-making**: PLAN A is a tabletop card-based serious game in which players manage "progressively complex airway scenarios using card-based instruments and strategies aligned with established algorithms." Each player holds instrument and strategy cards, draws patient cards describing cases, and combines interventions under predefined rules aligned with the Vortex Approach and ASA/Difficult Airway Society guidelines. Experience thresholds gate higher-level interventions, support actions allow peer assistance, and invalid combinations trigger penalties. A facilitator guide and standardized gameplay instructions are provided in supplementary materials. (Mohar Sebastian et al. (2026))
