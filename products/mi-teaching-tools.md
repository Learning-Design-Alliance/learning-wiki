## Description
A collection of online template-based authoring tools developed by Sazilah Salam and colleagues that lets lecturers create, edit, save, print, and export games, quizzes, activities, and interactive diagrams without programming.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Online MI Teaching Tools: template-based generators for interpersonal, visual-spatial and verbal-linguistic activities**: A collection of online teaching templates letting lecturers create materials without IT or programming knowledge. "Using the MI Teaching Tools, lecturers can create free games, quizzes, activities, interactive diagrams and applications based on the MI theory in a few seconds." Tools include a Group Maker for jigsaw collaborative activity, an Interactive Diagram Generator with mind map maker, and Quiz Maker and Word Game Maker (multiple-choice, match-up, interactive quiz, crossword). Templates can be organised, saved, printed, exported and edited later. (Sazilah Salam et al. (2012))
