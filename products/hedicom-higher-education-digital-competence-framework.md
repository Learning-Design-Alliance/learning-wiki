# HeDiCom (Higher Education Digital Competence) framework

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A framework developed by Tondeur and colleagues that defines digital competencies for higher-education teachers, including technical, pedagogical, and student-empowerment dimensions, to guide online and blended teaching.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **HeDiCom framework for higher education teachers' digital competence**: The HeDiCom (Higher Education Digital Competence) framework is a generic framework of digital competencies for teachers in higher education, developed by Tondeur and colleagues through a review of digital competence research and comparison of existing frameworks, expert meetings with policy makers, and validation with practitioners. The preface states it "provides guidance and clearer expectations of higher education teachers' digital competency which can guide innovation in practice beyond the GOT." It extends pedagogical and technical knowledge domains to include empowering students. (Sarah K. Howard (2023))
- **The HeDiCom framework organizes Higher Education teachers' digital competencies into four dimensions**: The HeDiCom framework is a digital competence framework designed specifically for Higher Education teachers, developed because existing frameworks such as DigCompEdu were created for compulsory education. The article states that "The final version of the Higher Education Digital Competence (HeDiCom) framework includes four dimensions, with two or three subdimensions (see Fig. 1)": teachers' digital practice, empowering students for a digital society, teachers' digital literacy, and teachers' professional learning. Each dimension is elaborated with named sub-dimensions and enumerated teacher competencies, and the dimensions are presented as interrelated rather than separable. (Jo Tondeur et al. (2023))
- **Teachers' digital practice dimension: designing, facilitating, and evaluating digital learning**: The first HeDiCom dimension covers competencies required for teachers' digital practice, grounded in the literature review finding that integrating ICT requires teachers to rethink educational designs, implement them, evaluate results, and potentially redesign. The article states "the three sub-dimensions are: (1) Designing and implementing, (2) Facilitating and monitoring, and (3) Evaluating and modifying." Competencies include designing digital learning consistent with conceptions of teaching and institutional vision, using ICT for formative and summative assessment, and using data from digital systems to evaluate and optimize learning designs. (Jo Tondeur et al. (2023))
