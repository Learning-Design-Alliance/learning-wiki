# HUMAn (Human is an Uncomplicated Model of Anatomy)

## Description
A simplified real-time mathematical physiology model developed by Laurent et al. for simulating trauma patients' vital-sign evolution and treatments in major-incident management training.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **HUMAn: a simplified physiological model combining a heart-lung-brain interaction model with anatomical functional blocks to simulate real-time trauma patient evolution**: HUMAn (Human is an Uncomplicated Model of Anatomy) is a simplified mathematical model of physiology that computes the real-time evolution of vital signs for trauma patients. Iterations led to a "heart-lung-brain interaction model coupled to functional blocks representing the main anatomical body parts"; these three organs were chosen because they are responsible for almost all the vital signs of the ABC evaluation performed by prehospital providers. Functional blocks divide the body into anatomical subsections, allowing traumas at different locations and localized treatments. (Laurent M et al. (2025))
