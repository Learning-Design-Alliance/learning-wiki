## Description
A World Health Organization framework for improving student health and well-being by integrating health into the curriculum, school ethos and environment, and family and community engagement.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Health Promoting Schools (HPS) framework: whole-school orientation toward health across three domains**: The WHO Health Promoting Schools framework advocates orienting the whole school system toward health, producing “a cultural shift toward the provision of a health enhancing environment.” The article states the framework “identifies the following three areas through which schools can improve health: embedding health within the curriculum; promotion of health and well‐being through systemic changes to the school ethos/environment; and external engagement with families and the community.” The study operationalises these three domains as a composite embeddedness measure scored 0–3, emphasising changing settings rather than individuals. (Long SJ et al. (2020))
