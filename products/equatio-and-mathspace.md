## Components

The report describes that "it includes features such as a Desmos graphing integration, handwriting recognition, language translation, and a screenshot reader with the ability to quickly and easily copy and paste math without losing formatting," plus Mathspace, "a digital space where learners can work freely with equations, shapes, and freehand drawings." # Equatio digital math tool with Mathspace

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
Equatio is Texthelp's advanced equation editor for digital mathematics environments. The report describes that "it includes features such as a Desmos graphing integration, handwriting recognition, language translation, and a screenshot reader with the ability to quickly and easily copy and paste math without losing formatting," plus Mathspace, "a digital space where learners can work freely with equations, shapes, and freehand drawings." The study examined how teachers use these features to support UDL principles in grades 4-12 mathematics, finding the tool versatile for both teacher authoring and student expression.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The report notes the power of such tools lies in how they are used; teachers needed professional learning to learn features beyond the basic equation editor
#### Constraints
- In some teachers' settings only students requiring assistive technology had access to Equatio, limiting classroom-wide use

### Target Learners
- Grade 4-12 mathematics students and their teachers, including students requiring assistive technologies

### Target Learning Goals
- Communicating mathematical symbolism, tables, graphs, and diagrams; creating, using, and connecting multiple mathematical representations

### Affordances
- [Udl Three Principles Framework Math](universal-design-for-learning-udl-framework.md)
