Estonian reflective-writing AI-feedback corpus of 2988 homework triplets from 283 students¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes

Description¶

A restricted corpus created by Andres Karjus et al. from first-year humanities coursework, containing 2,988 reflective-essay, AI-feedback, and student-appraisal triplets with human-validated annotations.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Students found AI feedback helpful and actionable more often than not across a semester of reflective writing [+W]
  • Student appraisals became more critical over the semester, with actionable-support themes falling from 67% to 43% [+W]
  • Most student essays remained at a descriptive reflection level despite repeated AI feedback [+W]
  • Expressed helpfulness of AI feedback showed no association with next essay reflection depth [+W]
