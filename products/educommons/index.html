eduCommons¶

Description¶

eduCommons was an open-source platform developed by the Center for Open and Sustainable Learning at Utah State University for publishing and sharing open educational resources and open courseware.

Components¶

  • eduCommons open source platform for sharing open educational resources: eduCommons was an open source software platform developed by the Center for Open and Sustainable Learning (COSL) at Utah State University for sharing open educational resources. The article states that "COSL was the home of eduCommons, an open source software platform for sharing open educational resources that powered one-third of the open courseware initiatives at universities around the world." COSL also organized the annual Open Education Conference from 2005 to 2007 and developed standards for OCW. (Curtis J. Bonk (2020))
