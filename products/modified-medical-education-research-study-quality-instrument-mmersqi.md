## Description
A modified study-quality assessment instrument used by medical education researchers to score the methodological quality of studies, including research on surgical training curricula.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The Modified Medical Education Research Study Quality Instrument (MMERSQI) for assessing study quality in medical education research.**: The MMERSQI is a tool for assessing the quality of studies in medical education research, used in this review with a minimum score of 23.5 and a maximum score of 100. The review reports that "The modified MERSQI scoring does not differentiate between descriptive statistics or simple inferential statistics" and that "Points are doubled when modelling is used." The review used this tool to assess the methodological quality of the included studies, finding overall low quality. (Kieslich Anna K. et al. (2026))
