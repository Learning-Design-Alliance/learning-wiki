MathsFlip¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

MathsFlip is an online mathematics learning environment built on Microsoft Office 365, comprising teacher-managed class sites and a topic-organised learning zone for primary pupils' learning inside and outside school.

Components¶

  • MathsFlip online learning environment (class site and learning zone on Microsoft Office 365): The MathsFlip programme's core element was an online learning environment, based on the Microsoft Office 365 platform, providing teachers and pupils with resources for learning mathematics outside the classroom. It consists of two main parts: "the class site and (2) the learning zone". The class site is a personalised page per class with resource areas and shared collaborative communication areas; the learning zone is a resource bank organised by topic, Year level and presentation type, including maths games and programmes such as Mangahigh and MyMaths. Teachers selected resources and shared them with pupils through class sites. (Rudd et al. (2017))

Claims¶

  • Ongoing technical and pedagogical support plus ample mathematics resources were the two elements teachers identified as key to successful implementation [+W]
  • The MathsFlip effect on KS2 maths for FSM-eligible pupils was slightly higher than for all pupils but not statistically significant [+W]
  • MathsFlip showed no statistically significant impact on KS2 maths achievement in a two-armed cluster RCT of 24 primary schools [-W]
  • Lesson observations indicated MathsFlip was delivered well, with class behaviour reflecting key components of a flipped learning approach [+W]
