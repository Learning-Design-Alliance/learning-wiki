# Quantum measurement multiple-choice question sequence (MQS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A validated 16-question multiple-choice assessment sequence on quantum measurement developed by Yangqiuting Li and colleagues from the Quantum Interactive Learning Tutorial and intended for peer-instruction use with clickers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Quantum measurement multiple-choice question sequence (MQS) for infinite-dimensional Hilbert space**: A validated, research-based sequence of 16 multiple-choice questions on quantum measurement in the context of wave functions in infinite-dimensional Hilbert space, developed from the learning objectives of the quantum measurement QuILT and redesigned for in-class peer instruction with clickers. "The quantum measurement MQS in its final iteration includes four sections (see Appendix A), with each section focusing on a broad learning objective discussed earlier": differentiating operator action from measurement, measurement postulates and probabilities, time development after measurement, and consecutive measurements. It was validated through faculty interviews and approximately 20 hours of think-aloud student interviews. (Yangqiuting Li et al. (2024))

### Claims
