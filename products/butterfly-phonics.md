## Description
Butterfly Phonics is a phonics-based reading intervention programme developed by Irina Tyk and delivered by trained practitioners to support struggling readers through structured whole-class and small-group instruction.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Butterfly Phonics intervention: phonics-based course-book programme with formal whole-class teaching, small groups of six to eight pupils, and comprehension-focused Advanced Butterfly Reader**: Butterfly Phonics aims to improve the reading of struggling pupils through phonics instruction delivered in a formal style where "pupils sit at desks in rows facing the teacher", who directs questions at random to check understanding. It is based on course books by Irina Tyk: The Butterfly Book (Tyk, 2007) for beginning and weakest readers and the Advanced Butterfly Reader for more skilled struggling readers. Lessons last about an hour, build and revisit literacy skills chapter by chapter, avoid worksheets in favour of an exercise book as a permanent record, and include teacher-read passages, class discussion of gist, corrected reading aloud, and writing using new vocabulary. A distinctive feature is continuing phonics-style attention to sound patterns of new vocabulary into the comprehension-based Advanced Reader lessons. (Christine Merrell (2015))

### Claims
