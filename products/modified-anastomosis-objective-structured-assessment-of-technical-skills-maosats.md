# Modified Anastomosis Objective Structured Assessment of Technical Skills (MAOSATS)

## Description
MAOSATS is a 65-point observational assessment form developed by Xia Jianfu et al. for rating surgeons' and surgical trainees' laparoscopic intestinal anastomosis technique, including stapler fluoroscopic skills.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MAOSATS: a modified anastomosis OSATS scale scoring four surgical steps, 13 sub-steps, and stapler fluoroscopic skills**: The Modified Anastomosis Objective Structured Assessment of Technical Skills (MAOSATS) is a 65-point assessment form developed for LIIA on this model. It includes "multiple scores for four surgical steps, 13 sub-steps, and fluoroscopic skills for a stapler", addressing the gap that existing OSATS/GOATS-style tools do not score specific surgical steps or advanced instrument use. Steps cover intestinal placement and disconnection, enterotomies, stapled enteroenterostomy, and running-suture closure. (Xia Jianfu et al. (2023))
