## Description
A 20-item Likert self-report assessment developed by Yildirim and Correia that measures nomophobia across four latent constructs and is used to classify its severity.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Nomophobia Questionnaire (NMP-Q), 20-item self-report scale with four latent constructs**: The NMP-Q is a self-report Likert scale validated by Yildirim and Correia that measures nomophobic behaviour (fear of separation from one's phone) via four latent constructs: not being able to communicate, losing connectedness, not being able to access information, and giving up convenience. Each dimension is rated on seven pointers from strongly disagree to strongly agree, yielding scores from 20 to 140 with banding for absent, mild, moderate, and severe nomophobia. In this study it was administered as a Google Forms e-Questionnaire and showed "an overall Cronbach’s alpha (and, McDonald’s omega) of α = .97 (ωt = .97)". (Essel Harry Barton et al. (2021))
