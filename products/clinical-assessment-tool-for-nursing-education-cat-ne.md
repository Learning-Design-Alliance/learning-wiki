# Clinical Assessment Tool for Nursing Education (CAT-NE)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The CAT-NE is a 13-item rubric-based clinical assessment tool proposed by Skúladottir and Svavarsdottir for evaluating nursing students' personal and professional performance factors.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Clinical Assessment Tool for Nursing Education (CAT-NE) with 13 rubric items**: The CAT-NE, proposed by Skúladottir and Svavarsdottir (2016), is the study instrument: an assessment tool "comprises of rubrics that list the criteria for the expected performance of students". It contains 13 items categorized as personal factors (6 items: enthusiasm, initiative, realistic confidence, competence with staff, competence with clients, caring) and professional factors (7 items including documentation, clinical competence, critical thinking, self-control). It was used to compare the two learning-approach groups. (AlKhaibary AA et al. (2021))
