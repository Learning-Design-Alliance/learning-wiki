## Description
BMAT Section 2 is the scientific-knowledge-and-applications component of the BioMedical Admissions Test, developed and administered by Cambridge Assessment Admissions Testing for medical-school selection.

## Components

- **Schools wishing to assess scientific knowledge independently of A-levels may find BMAT Section 2 useful, but should not expect it to add predictive value over prior attainment**: The article's practical recommendation is that institutions wanting a standardised measure of scientific knowledge that does not rely on prior educational attainment may use BMAT Section 2 ('scientific knowledge and applications'), the only section with a consistent pattern of predictive validity for written assessments across both studied institutions. The authors state: "Schools who wish to assess scientific knowledge, independently of A‐levels, may find Section 2 useful." However, this recommendation is bounded by the finding that Section 2 added little over A-level grades. (Davies DJ et al. (2022))
