## Description
A curriculum-oriented framework developed by Amy Lisa Graves for organizing undergraduate computational-physics research skills into fundamental, technical, and computational-physics-related tiers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Three-tier taxonomy of computational physics skills: fundamental, technical, and computational physics-related**: Drawing on the AAPT Undergraduate Curriculum Task Force, the article organizes computational physics skills into a hierarchy in which "Skills	are	broken	into	three	categories	that	build	upon	one	another:	fundamental,	technical,	and	computational	physics-related". The authors' own survey operationalizes this as categories B through G, spanning algorithmic thinking, basic coding, software engineering, scientific project and data management, fundamental scientific computing, and more advanced computational skills, alongside workplace skills such as finding information, collaborating, and project management. The taxonomy frames the paper's question of which skills belong in the curriculum versus in research. (Amy Lisa Graves (2019))
