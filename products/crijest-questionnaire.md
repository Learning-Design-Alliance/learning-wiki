## Description
The CRIJEST questionnaire is a self-report assessment instrument, adapted here through subconstructs measuring secondary students' interest in school science and technology and their school S&T self-concept.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CRIJEST questionnaire subconstructs for interest in school science and technology and school S&T self-concept**: The study used an adapted version of the general CRIJEST questionnaire, drawing on two six-item subconstructs: "interest in science and technology at school" (Cronbach's α = 0.89), with items such as "I look forward to upcoming activities in S&T", and "school S&T self-concept" (Cronbach's α = 0.82), with comparative items such as "Compared to all the other students, I consider myself (good) at S&T". The same questionnaire served as pre- and post-test for both groups, and gains were calculated as pre-post differences. (Patrice Potvin et al. (2017))
