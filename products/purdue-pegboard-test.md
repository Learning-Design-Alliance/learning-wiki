## Description
The Purdue Pegboard Test, made by Lafayette Instrument Company, is a standardized assessment of unilateral, bilateral, and assembly-based hand dexterity used in educational, clinical, and occupational settings.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Purdue Pegboard Test as an objective fine motor assessment for nursing student readiness**: The Purdue Pegboard Test (Lafayette Instruments, model 3202) assesses fine motor skills by emphasising the speed and manipulative ability of the hands and fingers. In this study four subtests were administered — right hand, left hand, both hands, and assembly — with one-handed subtests counting pins inserted in 30 s and the assembly subtest counting pins with washers and clamping rings over 1 min. The authors describe it as providing "an objective means of assessing a student's readiness for skill‐intensive nursing procedures" and use it as the hand-function measure correlated with nursing skill checklists. (Ela Yılmaz Coşkun et al. (2025))
