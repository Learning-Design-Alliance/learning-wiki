# Kahoot!

## Description
Kahoot! is a game-based classroom response and assessment platform made by Kahoot! ASA that lets instructors deliver interactive questions, collect learner responses, provide feedback, and review performance data.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Kahoot! smartphone clicker application used as the student response system in ESP classes**: Kahoot! is a smartphone clicker application that lets students join a game with a pin number, answer projected multiple-choice questions by tapping colourful figures on their phones, and see immediate right/wrong feedback and score rankings. It provides instructors with "the detailed report of the overall performance of the students for each week and test", including correct/incorrect percentages, Likert-style feedback, and per-student answer times. In this study it replaced dedicated clicker handsets for 10 weeks of ESP instruction. (Mehmet Asmalı (2018))
- **Kahoot® game-based learning platform with high-resolution image quizzes**: Kahoot® is a freely available online game-based learning platform that "enables professors to create trivia quizzes in any language and on any device", playable live in class with questions projected on a shared screen or viewed on students' own mobile devices. It provides time limits and scoring to create competition, and the article highlights its ability to display high-quality images or videos with great graphical resolution, which the authors used for histology morphology questions. (Felszeghy Szabolcs et al. (2019))
