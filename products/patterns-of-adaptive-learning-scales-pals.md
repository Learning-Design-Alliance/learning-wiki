# Patterns of Adaptive Learning Scales (PALS)

## Description
PALS is a Likert-type student and teacher survey instrument developed by Midgley et al. for measuring mastery and performance goals, classroom goal structures, achievement-related beliefs and strategies, and related perceptions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Patterns of Adaptive Learning Scales (PALS) instrument**: The PALS (Midgley et al. 2000) is a survey instrument measuring adaptive and maladaptive learning patterns related to mastery and performance goals. It uses "a five point Likert-type for all the scales", with student items anchored at 1 = "Not at all true," 3 = "Somewhat true," and 5 = "Very true". It includes student scales (personal achievement goal orientations, perceptions of teachers' goals, classroom goal structures, achievement-related beliefs and strategies, perceptions of parents and home life) and teacher scales (school goal structure, goal-related instruction approaches, personal teaching efficacy). (Christian Alberto Mendoza-Nápoles (2022))
