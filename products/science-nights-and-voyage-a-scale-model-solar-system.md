# Science Nights and Voyage - A Scale Model Solar System

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
National Air and Space Museum family and community science-outreach programmes that combine a collaborative evening event with a walk-through, scale-model solar-system installation, developed with teachers and community representatives.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Family and community-based space science outreach: Science Nights and the Voyage scale model solar system**: Two award-winning programs developed at the National Air and Space Museum under Dr. Jeff Goldstein, premised on the idea that "learning is a family experience". 'Science Nights' is an evening event where parents, teachers, students and siblings participate together at a museum. 'Voyage - A Scale Model Solar System' is "a nearly exact scale model of the solar system, on the 1:ten billion scale, stretching along a 600-meter walking path", letting visitors sense the immense distances and relative sizes of the solar system in memorable context. (Smith (2000))
