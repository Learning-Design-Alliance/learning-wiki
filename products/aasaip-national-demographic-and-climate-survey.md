# AAS/AIP national demographic and climate survey

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A survey initiative recommended by the American Astronomical Society and conducted with the AIP Statistical Research Center to collect standardized demographic and departmental-climate data for benchmarking diversity, equity, and inclusion progress.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **AAS/AIP national demographic and climate survey with coordinated standard metrics**: The paper recommends that departments "Participate in the recommended AAS/AIP national demographic and climate survey," with the AAS partnering "with the AIP Statistical Research Center to collect demographic and climate data." The survey addresses the paper's diagnosis that "the absence of a coordinated data collection effort with standard metrics has prevented departments from making meaningful comparisons" against their own prior progress and against peer departments. Departments would report progress on a platform provided by the AAS and on their websites. (AAS Task Force on Diversity et al. (2019))
