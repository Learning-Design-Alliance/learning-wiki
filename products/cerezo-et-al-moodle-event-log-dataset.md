# Cerezo et al. Moodle event-log dataset

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A preprocessed and anonymized dataset of Moodle event logs from 101 Spanish university undergraduates, released by Cerezo et al. for studying self-regulated learning processes through learning analytics.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Preprocessed Moodle event-log dataset from 101 undergraduates with 21,629 events split into Pass/Fail and per-unit sub-files**: The study's empirical dataset consists of interaction traces from an online course on the Moodle 2.0 platform. The authors report using "data from 101 undergraduate students (mean age=20.23; SD=1.01; 83% women) from a university in the North of Spain" whose course activity "after preprocessing led to 21,629 events". Preprocessing deleted duplicates and irrelevant records, selected 16 SRL-relevant actions, applied the five-label high-level coding, and split logs into Pass (73 cases, 15,637 events) and Fail (28 cases, 5,992 events) files plus eleven per-unit sub-files. (Cerezo et al. (2018))
