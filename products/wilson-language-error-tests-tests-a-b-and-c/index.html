Wilson Language Error Tests (Tests A, B, and C)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A standardized written-language assessment published by the World Book Company in which pupils identify and correct embedded errors in compositions, with forms and grade-based norms for grades III–XII.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • The three Wilson Language Error Tests (A, B, and C) approach equality across grades III to XII [+M]
  • The probable error of a score on the language error tests is 2.24 [+M]
