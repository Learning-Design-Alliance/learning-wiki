# Five Facet Mindfulness Questionnaire (FFMQ)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 39-item self-report assessment developed by Baer and colleagues that measures five facets of dispositional mindfulness, including observing, describing, acting with awareness, nonjudging, and nonreactivity.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Five Facet Mindfulness Questionnaire (FFMQ) as a measure of dispositional mindfulness in nursing students**: The FFMQ is a 39-item self-report questionnaire with 5-point Likert-type responses ranging from "1" (never or rarely true) to "5" (very often or always true), scoring in the range of 39–195. It measures five subscales: observing, describing, acting with awareness, nonjudging of inner experience, and nonreactivity to inner experience. In this study its internal reliability was α = 0.84 (subscales 0.71–0.87), and it was administered online via Microsoft Forms before mid-semester exams. (Mathkor Darin Mansor (2025))
