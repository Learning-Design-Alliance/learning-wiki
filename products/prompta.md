## Description
PROMPTa is a web application developed by Puro Vezolu et al. for managing iterative writing submissions and anonymous peer, mentor, and expert assessment against shared analytical rubrics.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PROMPTa web application for multifaceted alternative writing assessment**: PROMPTa (Performance Review of Manuscript Proficiency Tool for Assessment) is a web application developed for the study that serves as "a structured submission platform for multi-tiered assessment" relying on human-assessed rather than automated feedback. It prioritizes "the cyclical nature of writing over solely evaluating the final product", with anonymous peer, mentor, and expert review of drafts against shared analytical rubrics covering Content, Structure and Organization, Language and Vocabulary, and Overall Impact. Over eight weeks, three writing tasks (email, story, essay) each involved three drafts with three rounds of assessment. (Puro Vezolu et al. (2025))
