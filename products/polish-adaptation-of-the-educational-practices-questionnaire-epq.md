# Polish adaptation of the Educational Practices Questionnaire (EPQ)

## Description
A 16-item questionnaire adapted and validated by Zalewska and colleagues for Polish undergraduate nursing students to measure the presence and perceived importance of educational practices in high-fidelity simulation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Polish adaptation of the Educational Practices Questionnaire (EPQ) with EPQ-PO and EPQ-IO sections**: The Polish EPQ is a 16-item questionnaire measuring educational techniques used during high-fidelity simulation. As printed, the scale "consists of sixteen questions divided into the following subscales: Active Learning (items 1–10), Collaboration (items 11 and 12), Diverse Learning Methods (items 13 and 14), and High Expectations (items 15 and 16)". It has two parts: EPQ-PO on the presence of practices during classes and EPQ-IO on their importance to students. It was adapted via a six-stage WHO translation schema and validated with 361 nursing students. (Zalewska Katarzyna (2022))
