## Components

## Description
First Step to Success is "an early intervention program designed to help children who are at risk for developing aggressive or antisocial behavioral patterns." A trained behavior coach works with the student, class peers, teacher, and parents for approximately 50 to 60 hours over three months. It comprises three interconnected modules — screening, classroom intervention (CLASS), and parent training (HomeBase) — in which students earn points toward behavioral goals, peers reinforce appropriate behavior, and parents are coached weekly in six competencies such as communication, cooperation, and limit setting.
