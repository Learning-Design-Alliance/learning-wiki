# ARIN-561

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
ARIN-561 is an educational game developed by Leitner et al. that uses an escape-from-an-alien-planet scenario and game mechanics to teach high school learners core artificial-intelligence techniques.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ARIN-561 game teaching core AI techniques with AI Consumer/Operator/Developer role distinctions**: ARIN-561 is a game described by Leitner et al. for teaching Search, Bayesian Networks, Decision Trees, Clustering, and Linear Regression to high school learners, built around an escape-from-an-alien-planet narrative with AI concepts woven into game mechanics (e.g., applying breadth-first search to find a companion robot). The commentary also highlights the paper's role taxonomy: "they use AI Consumer to refer to someone who simply uses AI-based technologies, AI Operator to refer to one who applies AI techniques to solve problems, and AI Developer for people who implement and improve AI algorithms." (H. Chad Lane (2023))
