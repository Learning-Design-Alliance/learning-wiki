# Community schools framework: four pillars for a whole-child, whole-school, whole-community approach

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Oakes et al. community schools framework organizes school redesign around integrated supports, expanded learning, family and community engagement, and collaborative leadership to improve outcomes for students in underserved communities.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Community schools framework: four pillars for a whole-child, whole-school, whole-community approach**: The report describes community schools as an approach in which schools partner with local communities to provide high-quality teaching plus additional resources and supports that mitigate barriers poverty erects. It is explicitly "an approach to schooling based on evidence-based principles, rather than a 'program'" and is built on "four pillars—integrated health and social supports, expanded learning time and opportunities, strong family and community engagement, and collaborative leadership and practice" that meet community needs and use community assets. Together the pillars provide more than wraparound services, using a whole child, whole school, whole community redesign of schools. (Oakes et al. (2020))
