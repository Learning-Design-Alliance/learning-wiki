# Jasper Woodbury Problem Solving Series

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
The Jasper Woodbury Problem Solving Series is a set of anchored-instruction video adventures and open-ended mathematics problem-solving materials developed by the Cognition and Technology Group at Vanderbilt for upper-elementary and middle-school learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Jasper series: anchored-instruction video adventures with open-ended mathematical problems**: The Jasper series is a set of video adventures developed within the anchored instruction approach for fifth- and sixth-grade mathematics learning. An anchor story — saving a wounded eagle using Jasper's ultralight with "limited payload and fuel capacity" — requires students to perform many mathematical tasks, cooperating with teacher support and using tools such as maps. Preliminary evaluations were reported as very promising, and the problems stimulate interest and teach application conditions of the mathematical concepts. (Renkl et al. (1996))
