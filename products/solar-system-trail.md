# Solar System Trail

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A physical hiking-trail scale model of the Solar System with an accompanying multilingual educational website, developed as an astronomy teaching resource for Grade 7 students and camp participants.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Solar System Trail scale model with hiking trail, adopted as a Grade 7 teaching resource**: The Solar System Trail is a deliverable of the first four AstroCamps: "the second scale model of the Solar System, in the Iberian Peninsula" that is accurate both in sizes and distances of objects, and one of only sixteen in Europe as far as the authors know. A full hiking trail following the Sun, the planets up to Neptune (including the Moon) and Ceres was officially opened on 13 August 2016. The trail has recently been included among the teaching resources for Grade 7 students, and has an accompanying website due to be expanded to at least 10 different languages in early 2022. (C. J. A. P. Martins (2021))
