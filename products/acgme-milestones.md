# ACGME Milestones

## Description
ACGME Milestones are specialty-specific performance standards used by the Accreditation Council for Graduate Medical Education to assess residents' and fellows' progression toward clinical competence and autonomy.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Milestone achievement and mastery learning as the emerging standard for airway management proficiency**: The review describes a shift from process- and volume-based training toward outcome-based, performance-assessed standards. It explains that "Sometimes referred to as mastery learning, milestone achievements rely on demonstrated performance rather than time-based metrics," with five performance levels based on the Dreyfus model from novice to expert, and mastery (level 6) unlikely obtained during training. The framework organizes ACGME milestones for anesthesiology, emergency medicine, and surgical critical care, with increasing autonomy as milestones are met. (Grissom TE (2020))
