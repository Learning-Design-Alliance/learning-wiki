## Description
MExplore is a visual exploration system developed by Pang Xiao et al. for navigating Chinese-language medical texts through document-, paragraph-, entity-set-, and entity-level views to support illness-script construction and clinical reasoning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Multilevel metaphor framework for entity-centered exploration of medical text**: The article organizes MExplore's visual design around a 3-tiered spatial metaphor that maps unstructured medical text onto progressively finer levels of granularity: medical documents, medical paragraphs, medical entity sets, and individual medical entities. As the article states, "The system adopts a 3-tiered metaphor—cosmic space, star map, and planet cross-section", inspiring the MD space view, the MP star map, and the focused sectional view. The framework guides a top-down analytical workflow from macrolevel document retrieval to microlevel detail exploration while preserving global context, and is grounded in the goal of supporting illness script construction from real-world clinical texts. (Pang Xiao et al. (2026))
