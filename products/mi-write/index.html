MI Write¶

Description¶

MI Write is Measurement Incorporated's automated writing assessment and feedback software used by schools to support writing instruction and student revision.

Components¶

  • MI Write automated writing feedback tool: MI Write is an automated writing feedback tool used in classrooms. The article describes a study of the tool "which took place during the 2021–2022 school year," examining "teachers' and students' use of the tool during the study and the impact of MI Write on student and teacher outcomes." The tool is the focal artifact of the study and of the accompanying briefs for teachers, students and families, and school and district leaders. (Lindsay Fox (2023))

Claims¶

  • Some students struggled to use Ecree's and MI Write's automated feedback [-W]
  • Teachers reported curriculum and technical integration challenges when implementing Ecree and MI Write [-W]
  • Regular and tailored support helped teachers integrate Ecree and MI Write into their teaching [+W]
  • Teachers found Ecree and MI Write useful for their writing instruction and for helping students improve their writing [+W]
  • MI Write's automated feedback is most useful to students when it supplements, not replaces, teacher feedback [+M]
  • Teachers and students found Ecree easy to use and helpful for improving writing [+W]
