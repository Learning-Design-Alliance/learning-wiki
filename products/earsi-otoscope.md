## Description
Earsi Otoscope is a high-fidelity virtual-reality otoscopy simulator with three-dimensional instrument tracking, direct insertion-depth feedback, tutor monitoring, and automated performance recording, used by medical educators for otoscopy training and practical assessment.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **High-fidelity virtual reality otoscopy simulator (Earsi Otoscope) with instrument tracking, feedback, and performance recording**: This element is the high-fidelity otoscopy simulator used both for training and as the practical exam platform in the trial. The instrument's tip is tracked in three-dimensional space, and the simulator renders the ear canal, tympanic membrane and middle ear in virtual reality, displaying the image on a monitor built into the otoscope. It provides "direct feedback on the insertion depth of the instrument", lets the tutor follow what the learner sees on an external monitor, and records the percentage of the tympanic membrane examined — a feature the authors propose using to objectively evaluate performance in a practical examination. (Albrecht Tobias et al. (2024))
- **Earsi Otoscope virtual reality otoscopy simulator**: A commercially available virtual reality otoscopy simulator (Earsi Otoscope; VRMagic) equipped with a lifelike model ear and an otoscope handpiece. The article describes that "the handpiece allows the user to see, in real time, a detailed 3-dimensional model of the outer and middle ear when looking through the otoscope," with selectable normal anatomy and common pathologies. It includes an insertion-depth scale, virtual patients that moan when the otoscope is inserted too deeply, and a second screen letting the tutor see the same image as the student. The study validated its face, content, and construct validity for otologic training. (Albrecht T et al. (2021))
