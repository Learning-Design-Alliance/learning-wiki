# Achievement Test on Mole Concept (ATMC)

## Description
A 30-item multiple-choice chemistry achievement test on the mole concept, drafted from validated WASSCE questions and used with SS1 students in the Lamidi et al. study.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The researcher-designed mastery learning instructional material and the 30-item Achievement Test on Mole Concept (ATMC) used in the Nigerian quasi-experimental study.**: The instruments used for this study were an Achievement Test on Mole Concept (ATMC), a set of 30 multiple choice questions drafted from past WASSCE Chemistry question papers already validated by the West African Examinations Council, and a researcher-designed mastery learning instructional material. Both instruments were validated by three professional secondary school Chemistry teachers and three science educators in a university. (Bashirat T. Lamidi et al. (2015))

### Claims
- [Students taught the mole concept with mastery learning instructional strategy performed better (mean score 15.50) than controls taught conventionally (mean score 7.04) in a quasi-experimental Nigerian study.](../claims/mastery-learning-better-mole-concept-achievement-nigeria.md) [+M]
