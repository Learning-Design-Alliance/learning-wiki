# Hamburg Multiple Mini-Interview (MMI)

## Description
A seven-station, rater-scored admissions interview assessment developed for selecting undergraduate medical students by sampling psychosocial competencies across interpersonal situations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Hamburg MMI construct: psychosocial competencies sampled across stations via trait activation**: The Hamburg MMI defines "psychosocial competencies" as effective regulation of the self and interaction with others in critical interpersonal situations, operationalised through three focal domains: empathy, communication skills and self-regulation. Station content is selected so that situational cues activate these traits; following trait activation theory, each station score is interpreted as a mixture of domains rather than a single trait, and the overall score represents psychosocial competencies in situations requiring the three domains. Candidates rotate through seven stations with two raters each. (Knorr M et al. (2018))
