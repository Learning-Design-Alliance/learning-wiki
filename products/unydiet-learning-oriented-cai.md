# UNyDIET learning-oriented CAI

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
UNyDIET is a nutrition-assessment software system with embedded learning-oriented computer-assisted instruction for clinical students and health professionals, developed by García de Diego and colleagues.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **UNyDIET learning-oriented CAI for clinical nutritional assessment**: A learning-oriented computer assisted instruction embedded in the UNyDIET nutrition software, comprising the Medical History, Diagnoses and Quality of Life modules. It guides users through simulations of clinical cases, integrating validated questionnaires, clinical guidelines, cardiovascular risk charts, photographs of diseases and genetic risk scores so students can practice integral nutritional assessment. The authors state it 'constitutes an advanced method for students and health professionals to accomplish nutritional assessments combining theoretical and empirical issues'. (García de Diego Laura et al. (2015))
