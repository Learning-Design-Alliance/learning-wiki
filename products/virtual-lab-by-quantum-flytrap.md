# Virtual Lab by Quantum Flytrap

## Description
Virtual Lab by Quantum Flytrap is an online real-time optical-table simulation for exploring quantum measurement, interference, entanglement, cryptography, and communication through interactive experimental setups.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Quantum Flytrap: an online virtual environment for quantum experiments used to demonstrate superposition and Elitzur-Vaidman bomb testing**: Quantum Flytrap is described as "a virtual environment for quantum experiments online". The authors use it to demonstrate the misty-state probability result of Section 3 experimentally (Fig. 1) and to implement the Elitzur-Vaidman setup that "allows the detection of a super-sensitive explosive device without setting it off" (Fig. 2, 25% detection without explosion). It is hands-on and classroom-tested, and the resource at [13] is called "very engaging, entertaining and also very effective". (Dan-Adrian German et al. (2026))
