Vocabulary Achievement Test (VAT)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2–q3 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A 45-item multiple-choice vocabulary achievement assessment developed by Manal Mohamed Khodary for first-level university EFL students in Saudi Arabia, measuring knowledge of prescribed textbook lexical items.

Components¶

  • Researcher-developed 50-item Vocabulary Test (KR-20 = .93): A "Vocabulary Test" developed by the researcher to measure fourth-grade students' vocabulary achievement, built from a question pool of 85 items aligned with the Primary Education Turkish Curriculum. After expert review and item analysis (eliminating items with discrimination index below .30 and difficulty outside .10–.90), "the V ocabulary Test consisting of 50 items was prepared". Mean difficulty was .59 and KR-20 was .93. (Berker Bulut (2017))

Claims¶

  • University EFL students taught with VSSPlus significantly outperformed students taught with VSS on a post vocabulary achievement test [+M]
  • The VSSPlus experimental group improved significantly from pre-test to post-test on the vocabulary achievement test [+M]
  • Active listening training produced a significantly larger vocabulary gain than traditional listening practice in fourth-grade students over eight weeks [+M]
