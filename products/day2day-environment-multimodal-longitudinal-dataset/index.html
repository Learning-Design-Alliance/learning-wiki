Day2Day Environment multimodal longitudinal dataset¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (2 associational, 1 design), q2 · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A longitudinal dataset created by Falkenstein Kim et al. that combines neuroimaging with wearable, physiological, behavioral, cognitive, affective, and environmental data collected across 25 timepoints from 30 adults.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Time of day and days since the first scan are robust predictors of within-person variance in grey matter and total cortex volume [+W]
  • Time spent outdoors in the 24 hours before scanning is positively associated with grey matter volume in the right dorsolateral prefrontal cortex [+W]
  • Dictionary-based SEANCE features yielded 21 nominally significant within-person associations with wearable outcomes, but none survived Bonferroni or FDR correction [~W]
