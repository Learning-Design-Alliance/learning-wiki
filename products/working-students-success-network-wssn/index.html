Working Students Success Network (WSSN)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (1 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A comprehensive integrated-services strategy implemented by community colleges to support low-income students and students of color through financial, career, and other coordinated supports.

Components¶

  • Working Students Success Network comprehensive integrated-services strategy: The Working Students Success Network (WSSN) is described as "an innovative, comprehensive strategy for supporting low-income students and students of color," implemented in nineteen community colleges as an expansion of the Working Families Success Network. It "brings together and integrates access to a full range of services and supports" spanning financial knowledge, budgeting, financial-product choice, and career planning, aiming to put students "on a path to securing marketable postsecondary credentials and achieving economic success." Mathematica and DVP-Praxis documented implementation and analyzed service receipt relative to academic outcomes. (Margaret Sullivan et al. (2018))

Claims¶

  • Colleges may reach more students with less intensive group-delivered services, while one-on-one services can be customized to individual needs and goals [~W]
  • Receiving an intensive, one-on-one service may matter more than receiving services across multiple pillars [~W]
  • One-on-one service delivery may not be the only way to target services to individual student needs [+W]
