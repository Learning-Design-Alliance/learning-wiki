## Description
Merit Software Solutions is an integrated computer-based learning system providing individualized reading and language arts instruction for grades 5–8, developed and supplied by Merit Software.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Merit Software Solutions for middle school reading and language arts**: An integrated computer-based learning system with tutorial and content modules delivering individualized reading and language arts instruction for grades 5-8. The article describes it as "an integrated computer-based learning system with tutorial and content modules arranged to deliver, on demand, basic and intermediate individualized reading and language arts instruction for grades 5-8." Modules cover reading comprehension skill sets (main idea, sequence, inference, fact-opinion, vocabulary), critical thinking within content areas, and interactive grammar exercises, with a management component giving performance feedback to students and teachers. (Securro et al. (2010))
