# MESA-Web

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
MESA-Web is a browser-based cloud interface to the MESA stellar-evolution code, developed by Carl E. Fields and colleagues, that lets learners and researchers submit stellar-model parameters and receive computed movies and data files without local installation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Learner-supplied custom nuclear reaction rates for eight key reactions, added mid-2017**: Since mid-2017 learners can choose a specific nuclear reaction from a list of eight key reactions and upload their own custom reaction rate file. The article states this capability "expanded MESA-Web's impact to being a viable means of scientific investigation for the nuclear astrophysics community without significant prior experience with MESA". The eight reactions span H-, He- and C-burning and were chosen for their demonstrated impact on stellar properties. (Carl E. Fields et al. (2023))
- **Custom stopping conditions, added mid-2019, letting learners limit stellar quantities to target specific evolutionary epochs**: In mid-2019 MESA-Web added the option for learners to specify a limit to different stellar quantities as a condition for which the model will complete. The article reports this "provides learners an increase in throughput especially if investigating a particular evolutionary epoch". The stopping condition is set through the submission form, with iron core collapse as the default quantity. (Carl E. Fields et al. (2023))
