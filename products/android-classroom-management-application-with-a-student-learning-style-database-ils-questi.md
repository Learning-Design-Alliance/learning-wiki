# Android classroom-management application with a student learning-style database, ILS questionnaire, and adaptive strategy/media recommendations

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
An Android database-backed classroom-management application developed by Raditya Bayu Rahadian for teachers to record Felder–Silverman learning-style data and receive individual or group recommendations for instructional strategies and media.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Felder-Silverman learning style model with the Index of Learning Styles as the classification basis**: The article adopts the Felder-Silverman model as the theoretical basis for classifying students along the dimensions of "processing, perception, input, and understanding", which it links to the selection of strategies and learning media. The Index of Learning Styles questionnaire operationalizes the model and is described as having "gone through long and in-depth validation", being easy for students to use and interpret, and having been successfully implemented in prior research adapting electronic learning materials and strategies. The application stores each student's ILS results and uses the detected style type to generate recommendations. (Raditya Bayu Rahadian (2023))
