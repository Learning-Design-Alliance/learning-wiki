## Description
A twelve-item yes/no checklist developed for medical educators and item writers to detect technical item-writing flaws in AI-generated multiple-choice questions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Twelve-category taxonomy of quality issues in LLM-generated MCQs**: A twelve-category taxonomy of recurring defects the authors observed in LLM-generated multiple-choice questions used for data science retrieval practice. Categories span content errors and structural flaws, from 'question and options containing mistakes, hallucinations, question containing hints to answers, duplicated options, ambiguous answers, easy distractors, trivial knowledge' to double-concept questions and ambiguous wording. Each category is illustrated with an example MCQ generated from course materials injected into the prompt. (An et al. (2025))
