# MOSART HS-LS 29-item misconception-driven pre/post assessment

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 29-item MOSART High School Life Science multiple-choice assessment developed by the MOSART project to diagnose common misconceptions and measure pre/post learning gains among grades 9–12 life-science students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MOSART HS-LS 29-item misconception-driven pre/post assessment for high school life science**: A 29-item multiple-choice assessment developed by the MOSART HS-LS project from an original bank of 543 high school life science items, each targeting a single grade 9–12 NGSS disciplinary core idea across cell biology, ecology, genetics, and biological evolution. Each item contains one dominant common misconception as a distractor. The selected 29 items showed 'high unidimensionality (pretest eigenvalue of first factor = 6.34 all others <0.62, Cronbach alpha = 0.88)', difficulty ranging 0.25–0.84, and misconception strength ranging 0.3–0.9. It was administered as pretest and posttest to measure student learning gains. (Chen et al. (2020))
