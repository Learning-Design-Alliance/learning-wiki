## Description
A commercial virtual-reality hip-arthroscopy simulator made by Simbionix that combines a mannequin, haptic instrument controllers, and interactive virtual joint imagery for surgical skills training.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Simbionix Arthro Mentor VR hip arthroscopy simulator (diagnostics module)**: A commercial virtual reality arthroscopy simulator comprising "a computer with monitor, a mannequin, and two haptic feedback devices that provide tactile feedback to a pair of instruments via connecting motors". The mannequin has four predefined 5-mm portals, and the virtual joint image responds to the operator's camera movements. In this study its hip diagnostics module was used to test face validity: participants located 12 targets in the central compartment via three portals. (Bartlett Jonathan D. et al. (2018))
