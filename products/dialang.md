## Description
DIALANG is a free adaptive web-based diagnostic language assessment system that reports learners' proficiency in relation to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages and provides feedback and advice for progression.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **DIALANG web-based diagnostic assessment tool**: DIALANG is a free, adaptive diagnostic web-based assessment tool that "provides test-takers with scores related to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR)" and "provides feedback and advice on how to move towards the next CEFR level". The article uses its structure section as a diagnostic tool to identify learners' grammatical problems (modals and prepositions) as target structures for DA sessions. The article characterizes it as a non-dynamic test whose feedback is not attuned to learners' ZPD. (Ebadi (2016))

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Used here as a diagnostic instrument to highlight grammatical problems to be targeted in subsequent interactionist DA sessions
#### Constraints
- As a non-dynamic test, DIALANG does not provide feedback attuned to the learners' ZPD, per the article

### Target Learners
- L2 learners of English assessed for grammatical competence
