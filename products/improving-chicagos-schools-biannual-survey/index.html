Improving Chicago's Schools biannual survey¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes

Description¶

A biannual Consortium on Chicago School Research survey that collects students' reports on school experiences and educators' reports on instruction and working conditions across Chicago Public Schools.

Components¶

  • Improving Chicago's Schools biannual survey of students, teachers, and principals: The Improving Chicago's Schools survey is a biannual Consortium on Chicago School Research data collection in which students report on school experiences, attitudes, and activities, while teachers and principals report on instruction, professional development, and working conditions. In 2005 'more than 132,000 students, teachers, and principals across the Chicago public school system participated,' and the Consortium produced individualized confidential reports for schools meeting response-rate criteria, which show change over the ten years of survey administration. (Stuart Luppescu et al. (2005))

Claims¶

  • The 2005 CCSR survey drew over 130,000 participants and produced reports for 454 schools [+W]
  • School response rates at the reported school were 86.4 percent for students and about 90 percent for teachers [+W]
