## Description
Thinking Math! is a PreK-8 mathematics program "grounded in socio-constructivist principles" aiming to develop "conceptual understanding, mathematical fluency, problem-solving, and mathematical reasoning." It bundles lesson guides, a digital planning and progress-monitoring platform, manipulative materials, student logbooks, and an adaptive individualized practice app, plus professional development and coaching.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires teacher technology (projector, computer, internet) and, optionally, student devices for the individualized practice app; 1:1 devices are recommended from 1st grade onwards
#### Constraints
- The practice app does not support smartphones since the screen size is too small; printed practice and teaching-guide alternatives are available

### Target Learners
- PreK-8 students

### Target Learning Goals
- Conceptual understanding in mathematics
- Mathematical fluency
- Problem-solving
- Mathematical reasoning
