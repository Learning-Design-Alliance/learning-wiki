## Description
A ten-item questionnaire developed by Compeau and Higgins to measure learners’ confidence and perceived capability in using computer software.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Compeau and Higgins (1995) Computer Self-Efficacy Questionnaire measuring magnitude of self-efficacy for using packaged software.**: This study adopts the Compeau and Higgins (1995) Computer Self-Efficacy Questionnaire, which contains ten Yes/No questions and measures the magnitude of self-efficacy, providing data of students' abilities to use packaged software. If the answer is the former, students make a personal self-assessment of strength of self-confidence on a 1 to 10 scale. (Hsieh et al. (2012))
