# Publicly available quantum education landscape dataset and dashboard

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A publicly downloadable dataset and Streamlit dashboard created by Piña et al. containing aggregate course, program, and institutional data on quantum and QISE education opportunities in U.S. higher education.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Dataset of quantum-related courses and QISE programs across 1,456 US institutions**: A curated dataset built from university and college course catalogs between June and September 2024 by seven researchers over approximately 600 hours, recording title, description, course number, prerequisites, departments, and academic levels for courses containing "quantum". The sample covers 1,456 institutions and captures "8,456 total STEM and non-STEM courses containing 'quantum' and 89 distinct degree programs in QISE". Data were recorded in a standardized Qualtrics form and analyzed with Python. (A.R. Piña et al. (2025))
