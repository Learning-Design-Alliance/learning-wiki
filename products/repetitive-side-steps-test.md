## Description
A Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology (MECSST) agility assessment for children aged 6–11 in which participants side-step across marked lines for 20 seconds and receive a score based on lines passed.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Repetitive side steps test as a measure of children's agility**: The repetitive side steps test is the agility measure adopted by Japan's Ministry of Education (MECSST) for 6-11 year olds, a Japanese version of the side step test in the California Physical Performance Test. Students side step (not jump) from a center line to touch or cross lines on the right and left for 20 seconds, scoring one point per line passed; the test was administered twice at pretest and twice at posttest with the better score recorded. The article states it "is an accepted way to measure the agility aspect of coordination". (Tatsuo Yasumitsu et al. (2012))
