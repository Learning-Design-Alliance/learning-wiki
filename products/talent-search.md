# Talent Search program: federal TRIO services supporting high-school completion and college access for low-income, first-generation students

## Description
Talent Search is a federally funded TRIO program (under the Higher Education Act of 1965) that "aims to help low-income and first-generation college students ... complete high school and gain access to college" through academic support and college-access services. Services include test-taking and study-skills assistance, academic advising, tutoring, career development, college campus visits, and financial aid application assistance. It serves both middle and high school students, about 380,000 students a year through more than 400 projects, at an average federal cost of $376 per participant in fiscal year 2005.
