## Description
QUESTOURnament is a Moodle module made for organizing time-constrained academic challenges in which learners answer, submit, and assess questions while earning dynamically varying points and rankings.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **QUESTOURnament competitive e-learning tool with variable scoring system**: QUESTOURnament is a Moodle module that lets teachers organize contests of time-constrained intellectual challenges. The article states that "Students obtain points both by answering the proposed challenges and by submitting new questions and assessing the answers submitted by their classmates." Its variable scoring system increases linearly until the first correct answer, then decreases toward zero at the challenge's end, and the system permanently displays an updated five-top ranking. In this study it was used in four individual, non-anonymous contests during a Human Immunology semester. (Corell A et al. (2018))
