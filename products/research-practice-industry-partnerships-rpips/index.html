Research-Practice-Industry Partnerships (RPIPs)¶

Description¶

A partnership framework developed by the Creativity Labs at the University of California, Irvine, for coordinating practitioners, learning-science researchers, and edtech developers around authentic problems of practice and iterative feedback loops.

Components¶

  • Research-Practice-Industry Partnerships (RPIPs) integrating problems of practice, learning sciences, and edtech development: RPIPs, emerging from research at the Creativity Labs at UCI, build on participatory design and RPP foundations and bring industry into a partnership built around feedback loops. The report states that "Research-Practice-Industry Partnerships (RPIPs) bring together authentic problems of practice and the learning sciences together with the edtech development process." Each party offers a unique value: edtech developers scale products but typically lack practitioner feedback or learning-science foundations; researchers know methods for analyzing user feedback but few designs scale; practitioners have first-hand knowledge but lack time or avenues to share it. The model ensures evidence of effica (Practitioners at the Center: Catalyzing Research on Problems of Practice in Realistic Sett)
  • Research-Practice-Industry Partnership (RPIP) model with four pillars for involving educators in edtech development: The RPIP model extends the Research Practice Partnership framework by bringing in industry voices, offering researchers and educators an opportunity to apply their experiences to a real world product. "An RPIP consists of four distinct pillars: 1) professional learning for educators...; 2) collaborative research design; 3) feedback loops between educators, researchers, and developers; and 4) the resulting improved product and practice improvements." A neutral third party facilitates the relationship and maintains a balanced power dynamic between educators and developers. (Tarik Buli et al. (2025))

Claims¶

  • Educators perceived FH2T as strongly connected to Common Core Standards for Mathematical Content and uniquely integrating the Standards for Mathematical Practice [+W]
  • Educators who implemented FH2T through partner or group work perceived it promoted peer collaboration and mathematical discussion [+W]
  • Educators perceived FH2T positioned students as doers of mathematics with agency and mathematical decision making in a safe practice environment [+W]
  • Educators used FH2T's teacher console insights to modify instruction, and recommended the console share students' mathematical capabilities and content-area difficulties [+W]
