## Description
A dataset released by Leslie Ching Ow Tiong and colleagues containing 94 university examination records with answers, scores, grades, completion times and IP addresses for e-cheating detection research.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **7wiseup behaviour dataset of 94 student examination records**: A released dataset for e-cheating research. The article states: "We have designed a new dataset, namely, the 7wiseup behaviour dataset, which consists of 94 student records that were acquired from different end-of-term examinations at Pyeongtaek University in South Korea." Records were collected in Spring semester 2020 from an e-class LMS during mock, mid-term and final-term exams, and each record includes answers, per-question scores, grades, completion time and IP address. Training used mock-exam data split 80:20, with data augmentation adding 60 abnormal-behaviour samples. (Leslie Ching Ow Tiong et al. (2021))
