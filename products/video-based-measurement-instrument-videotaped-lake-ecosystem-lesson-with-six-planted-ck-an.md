# Video-based measurement instrument: videotaped lake-ecosystem lesson with six planted CK and PCK mistakes and an analysis booklet

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An assessment developed by Rosenkränzer et al. that measures student teachers' reflective content and pedagogical content knowledge by having them identify and document errors in a videotaped lake-ecosystem lesson.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Video-based measurement instrument: videotaped lake-ecosystem lesson with six planted CK and PCK mistakes and an analysis booklet**: To measure reflective CRK in a situational context, the authors developed "a video with an associated booklet" showing a lesson in which 6th graders learn about the ecosystem lake. The authors "installed six mistakes in the video vignette, three in each form of CRK", such as an incorrect definition of a system and a confusing map of labeled interactions. Student teachers analyzed the video and noted erroneous situations; notes were coded (0-6 score) with Krippendorff's alpha over .80. Expert review led to omitting multiple-choice options to avoid anticipations. (Rosenkränzer et al. (2016))
