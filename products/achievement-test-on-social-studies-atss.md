# Achievement test on social studies (ATSS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 30-item standardized Social Studies achievement test drawn from Nigeria's Universal Basic Education curriculum and administered to junior secondary school students by the study researchers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **25-item social studies achievement test for 'The Place We Live' unit (alpha = .82)**: A researcher-developed achievement test measuring objectives of the 4th-grade social studies unit "The Place We Live." Drafted with 65 multiple-choice items, reviewed by an expert panel, piloted with 196 fifth graders, and refined so that "The final form of the achievement test comprised 25 items and the alpha reliability coefficient of the test was estimated to be .82." Administered as pre-test, post-test, and retention test. (Korkmaz Toklucu et al. (2016))
