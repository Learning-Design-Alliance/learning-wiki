KiDZ – Kindergarten of the Future in Bavaria preschool model project¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A Bavarian preschool programme run as a model project that combines play-based early-childhood education with integrated literacy, mathematics, and science stimulation, team teaching with primary-school teachers, and specialized teacher training and materials.

Components¶

  • KiDZ – Kindergarten of the Future in Bavaria preschool model project: KiDZ was a Bavarian model project (main phase 2004-2009) providing children aged 3-6 with traditional preschool stimulation "combined with more academic and domain-specific stimulation and school preparation" in literacy, mathematics, and science. It was child-centered, served all children in mixed-age classes, integrated domain-specific stimulation into daily routines rather than planned hours, used team teaching with an elementary school teacher in each classroom, and included teacher training with hands-on materials such as the KiDZ-Handbuch. It was evaluated in a quasi-experimental longitudinal design across three preschool centers. (Lehrl et al. (2017))

Claims¶

  • Attendance at the KiDZ preschool intervention is positively associated with mathematical competencies at age 12 [+M]
  • The association between KiDZ attendance and age-12 mathematics is mediated by preschool process quality [+M]
