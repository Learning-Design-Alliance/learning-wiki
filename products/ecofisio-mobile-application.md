# ECOFISIO mobile application

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A mobile learning application for undergraduate physiotherapy students that provides shoulder palpation and ultrasound theory, anatomical illustrations, probe-placement images, ultrasound views, diagrams, and procedure videos.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ECOFISIO mobile application for shoulder palpation and ultrasound learning**: A mobile application with specific content for the shoulder region, containing for each structure a theoretical description, a drawing with anatomical description, an image with specific ultrasound probe placement, an ultrasound slice, a diagram of the ultrasound image, and a video of the manual palpation procedure. It was used as a supplement to traditional learning in the m-learning group during 20 self-study hours. (Fernández-Lao Carolina et al. (2016))
