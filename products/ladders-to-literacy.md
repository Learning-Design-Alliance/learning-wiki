# Ladders to Literacy: A Kindergarten Activity Book — supplemental print awareness, phonological awareness, and oral language curriculum

## Description
Ladders to Literacy is "a supplemental early literacy curriculum published in Ladders to Literacy: A Kindergarten Activity Book" targeting typically developing children, children with disabilities, and children at risk of reading failure. Activities are organized into three sections of about 20 activities each covering print awareness, phonological awareness, and oral language skills. Teachers use an observational checklist, and the manual includes lesson plans, assessment checklists, and home activities; the book costs $49.95.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers choose activities that fit existing classroom routines; professional development is available as an extra-cost one- or two-day on-site seminar.
#### Constraints
- The report covers only the Kindergarten version, not the preschool version of the curriculum.

### Target Learners
- kindergarten students, including typically developing children, children with disabilities, and children at risk of reading failure

### Target Learning Goals
