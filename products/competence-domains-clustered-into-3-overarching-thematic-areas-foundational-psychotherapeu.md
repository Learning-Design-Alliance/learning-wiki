# Competence Domains clustered into 3 overarching thematic areas: Foundational, Psychotherapeutic Process, and Reflexive

## Description
A framework by Weiher Gerald Marc et al. that organizes eight BCP competence domains into Foundational, Psychotherapeutic Process, and Reflexive clusters for psychotherapists and psychotherapists in training.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Competence Domains clustered into 3 overarching thematic areas: Foundational, Psychotherapeutic Process, and Reflexive**: The article organizes the 8 Competence Domains into 3 overarching thematic clusters. The Foundational Cluster contains Data Security Legal Issues Ethics and Safety, Technological Aspects, and Creation of Setting and Infrastructure, providing structural, technical, and legal conditions necessary for BCP. The Psychotherapeutic Process Cluster encompasses Therapeutic Relationship and Transfer of Communication and Methods. The Reflexive Cluster incorporates Self-Regulation Self-Confidence and Self-Care along with Individual and Cultural Diversity and Scientific Knowledge and Methods. (Weiher Gerald Marc et al. (2026))
