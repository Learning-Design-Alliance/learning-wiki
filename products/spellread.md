## Description
SpellRead™ is a structured literacy programme for struggling readers in grade 2 and above, delivered through systematic explicit auditory and visual reading instruction in small groups and supported by an instructor monitoring system.

## Description
SpellRead™, formerly SpellRead Phonological Auditory Training®, is a small-group literacy program for struggling readers in grades 2–12 distributed through PCI Education. It "consists of 105 lessons implemented in three distinct phases that interweave phonemics, phonetics, and instruction in language-based reading and writing". The program takes five to nine months and is delivered in small groups of five students with one instructor in 60–90 minute classes.
