## Description
A 44-item, seven-point Likert self-report questionnaire developed by Stubbé and colleagues to measure Dutch secondary students' creative self-concept across seven constructs.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Creative Self-Concept questionnaire (Stubbé et al., 2015) as a moderator measure for Dutch secondary writing research**: The study used a 44-item Creative Self-Concept questionnaire "designed and tested by Stubbé et al. (2015) for use in Dutch secondary education", with items on a 7-point Likert scale representing seven constructs: inquisitiveness, imaginativeness, focus on output, pride in own work, dare to be different, persistence, and collaborativeness. The compound average score (Cronbach's alpha ".92") was used to test whether instruction effects held irrespective of creativity (RQ3), and CSC moderated the transfer effect in panel 1. (Anouk ten Peze et al. (2024))
