# SBAR (Situation–Background–Assessment–Recommendation)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
SBAR is a standardized healthcare communication framework, originating in the U.S. Navy and adapted for clinical handover and patient-safety practice by healthcare organizations including Kaiser Permanente.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SBAR as a structured communication framework for clinical handover**: SBAR (situation, background, assessment, recommendation) is a structured communication tool comprising "the communication of the patient's current situation, the background and causes of the situation, the assessment of the current condition, and the reporter's recommendations for further treatment." The review describes it as reliable, validated, easily implemented in hospital practice, and enabling clear communication in a short time. It is the intervention core of the simulation programs reviewed, embedded in scenarios, role-play, and reports. (Yun Jungmi et al. (2023))
