## Description
HIPPY is a structured home-based early-childhood education and parental-involvement programme delivered through curriculum materials and regular home visits to support school readiness and later school success.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Systematic parental involvement programs in early childhood can support long-term school success for at-risk children**: The article argues that systematic parental involvement during the preschool years, such as the HIPPY model's structured home-based curriculum and weekly home visits, can build a foundation for long-term school success. The authors state that "systematic parental involvement can benefit children's school success and help at-risk children to overcome the barriers they encounter." This principle is grounded in the study's finding that HIPPY children outperformed matched comparison children on the majority of school performance measures through 9th grade. (Brown et al. (2014))
