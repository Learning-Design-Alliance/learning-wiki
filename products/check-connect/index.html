Check & Connect¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 5 claims (3 for, 2 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Check & Connect is a dropout-prevention programme developed and implemented through monitored student performance, individualized mentoring, case management, and coordinated support from school, family, and community partners.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Check & Connect shows no significant effect on completion by one year after expected graduation (supplemental fifth-year finding) [-W]
  • Check & Connect increases credits accrued (progressing in school) in one RCT [+W]
  • Check & Connect shows no discernible effect on on-time high school completion [-W]
  • Check & Connect has statistically significant positive effects on staying in school for high school students with disabilities [+W]
  • Check & Connect increases the percentage still enrolled at end of fourth year (supplemental finding) [+W]
