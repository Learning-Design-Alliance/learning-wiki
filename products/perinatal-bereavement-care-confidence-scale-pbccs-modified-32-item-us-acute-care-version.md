# Perinatal Bereavement Care Confidence Scale (PBCCS), modified 32-item U.S. acute-care version

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A modified self-report assessment instrument measuring hospital staff confidence in perinatal bereavement care across knowledge, skills, self-awareness, and organizational support, developed by the Garavito et al. study team from the Australian College of Midwives' PBCCS.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Perinatal Bereavement Care Confidence Scale (PBCCS), modified to 32 items for U.S. acute-care staff**: The PBCCS is a validated questionnaire developed by the Australian College of Midwives to measure factors affecting bereavement support provision; its internal consistency "ranges from 0.753 to 0.871". The study team adapted the original 43-item tool for U.S. acute-care hospital staff, removing 14 questions and adding three organizational-support items, producing a 32-item instrument with subscales for knowledge, skills, self-awareness, and organizational support, scored 1–5 per item with a total range of 32–160 and 80% (128) as sufficient confidence. (Garavito C et al. (2026))
