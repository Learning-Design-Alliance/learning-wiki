## Description
An open-source spoken-English practice chatbot developed by Li Siyan et al. that combines open-domain conversation with grammar correction and emotion-triggered empathetic feedback.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **EDEN: open-source open-domain empathetic spoken English-practice chatbot**: EDEN (Empathetic Dialogues for ENglish learning) is a "high-quality and robust dialogue system capable of empathetic and grammatical feedback" for spoken English practice. Each turn is analyzed for negative sentiment and prolonged pauses, triggering empathetic feedback; otherwise a grammar correction model and template-based feedback module operate, with a conversation model handling chit-chat and ChatGPT handling follow-up queries. It improves on the Siyan et al. (2024) architecture and is released with data, code, and model checkpoints. (Li Siyan et al. (2024))
