## Description
Uro-Island is a Windows-based electronic adventure game developed at the University Medical Center Freiburg for teaching medical students phase-contrast microscopic urinalysis through story-driven exploration, dialogue, quests, and diagnosis.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Uro-Island educational adventure game for phase contrast microscopic urinalysis**: Uro-Island is an electronic adventure game developed at the University Medical Center Freiburg "built on the open source Wintermute game engine" for Windows computers. The player controls a character stranded on an island who explores scenarios, dialogues with non-player characters, gathers items, and masters quests covering "exactly the same urine pathologies as described in the script" in increasing difficulty, with the goal of reaching a diagnosis to regain freedom. (Boeker Martin et al. (2013))
