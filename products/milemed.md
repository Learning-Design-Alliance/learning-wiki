## Description
MiLeMed is a smartphone microlearning app developed at the University of Bonn that supports obstetrics OSCE preparation through short lessons, visuals, questions, reflection prompts, and gamification.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MiLeMed mobile microlearning app for obstetrics OSCE preparation**: MiLeMed is a smartphone app developed at the University of Bonn that delivers short microlectures, original visuals, multiple-choice questions, open-ended reflection prompts, and gamification elements for obstetrics OSCE preparation. Content is aligned with the local obstetrics curriculum and national standards, and students are guided by "a structured 20-day study plan developed to guide students through the content in a sequential and time-efficient manner." The app was designed to be accessible and time-efficient without adding workload for instructors. (Michlmayr N. et al. (2025))
