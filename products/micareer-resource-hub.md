## Description
MiCareer Resource Hub is a Michigan Consortium for Addressing Rural Education Expansion and Retention partnership initiative that connects educator-preparation institutions and rural school districts to help prospective and experienced educators earn teaching credentials and classroom experience.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MiCareer Rural Credentialing Hub for rural Michigan educators**: The Michigan Consortium for Addressing Rural Education Expansion and Retention (MiCareer) Resource Hub is "an innovative partnership between educator preparation institutions and rural school districts that works to increase the number of qualified and credentialed educators in rural classrooms." It engages rural residents who are uncertified but have completed most coursework, hold temporary credentials, or are veteran teachers certified in another state, helping them earn credentials and experience at little to no cost. The report presents it as a response to rural barriers such as housing affordability and recruiting local talent. (Charlotte Pierce (2026))
