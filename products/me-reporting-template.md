# Measurement and Evaluation Reporting Template for summarizing and sharing study findings

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
A reporting template from Mathematica that helps organizations, research partners, and funders summarize and share findings from a study. It "guides users in creating a short report" with a brief solution overview, study approach, three to five key findings, and next steps, in a format described as easy to read and accessible for a variety of audiences.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Users complete a short report covering the solution overview, study approach, key findings, and next steps as structured by the template
#### Constraints
- Designed for reporting on studies of learning and education solutions, prepared for the Gates Foundation's math teaching and learning work

### Target Learners
- Organizations, research partners, and funders reporting on education studies

### Target Learning Goals
- Communicating study findings about learning solutions clearly and consistently
