## Description
The M&E Checklist Phase 2 is a practical guidance document from Mathematica that guides users through the second phase of an evidence-building process for education solutions. It is one of four checklist documents, each covering one evidence-building phase, and this portion focuses on refining a solution using lessons learned during implementation. The checklist was prepared for the Gates Foundation under a project on middle years math teaching and learning solutions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Users work through the checklist activities in collaboration with stakeholders from a priority community, as the checklist is designed to guide evidence building with those stakeholders.
#### Constraints
- The document covers only Phase 2 of the four-phase evidence-building process; other phases are covered by separate checklist documents.

### Target Learners
- Middle years math students in priority communities served by education solution developers

### Target Learning Goals
- Improving middle years math teaching and learning solutions through evidence-based refinement
