# Reusable didactic materials and calculation tools from the collective Eratosthenes replication

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The collective Eratosthenes replication project provides a spreadsheet with formulas, Stellarium-based procedures, and an electronic measurement form for groups and basic-education learners to replicate the Earth-circumference experiment.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Reusable didactic materials and calculation tools from the collective Eratosthenes replication (spreadsheet with formulas, Stellarium procedures, data form)**: The project produced didactic materials and calculation tools intended as a parameter for other groups, including activities in basic education. These include a spreadsheet with all data and relevant calculations of the 23 participants, publicly accessible with its formulas; the Stellarium-based procedure for locating the zero-reference point and distances; and the electronic form collecting shadow measurements, coordinates and impressions. The article states these "podem ser utilizados como parâmetro por outros grupos, inclusive para atividades na educação básica". (Karen Luz Burgoa Rosso et al. (2021))
