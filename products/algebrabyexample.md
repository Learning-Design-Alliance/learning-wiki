# AlgebraByExample

## Description
AlgebraByExample is a freely downloadable set of algebra worked-example and problem-solving assignments co-developed by researchers, teachers, and mathematics administrators in the SERP-MSAN partnership.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **AlgebraByExample worked-example assignments**: AlgebraByExample is a set of paper-and-pencil classroom assignments co-developed by researchers, teachers, and math administrators in the SERP-MSAN partnership. Each assignment contains 10-12 items isomorphic to textbook problems: "2-3 correct examples to explain, 2-3 incorrect examples to explain, and 5-6 problems to solve", with self-explanation prompts and highlighted error locations. Incorrect examples are clearly marked and errors highlighted, and materials are available free of charge for download on the project website. (Booth et al. (2015))
- **AlgebraByExample assignments**: AlgebraByExample is a set of 42 classroom assignments covering pre-Algebra and 8 major Algebra topics, developed iteratively to interleave worked examples with practice problems. Items are "grouped in pairs that include an 'example' item for students to study and explain and a 'your turn' item to solve." Examples present a fictional student's correct or incorrect work, followed by self-explanation questions targeting commonly misunderstood concepts. Students typically completed assignments in 10-20 minutes. (Paré-Blagoev et al. (2013))
