# MINTS-DR behavioural-marker checklist

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A peer-observer checklist for rating non-technical skills in multiprofessional simulation debriefings using behaviourally anchored four-point items and a not-applicable option.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Behavioural-marker checklist items anchored on a 4-point scale with two extreme performance descriptions**: Each MINTS-DR item pairs a short stem sentence with two endings representing "the two extremes of a good or poor performance", presented as anchors on a 4-point Likert scale with a not-applicable option. The authors chose four points over a dichotomous scale, which respondents perceived as too judgemental, and over a three-point scale, which risked uninformative middle ratings. The two intermediate levels were milder but still informative enough to trigger open discussion during debriefing. (Bracco Fabrizio et al. (2017))
