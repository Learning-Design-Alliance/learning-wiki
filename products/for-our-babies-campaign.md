# For Our Babies campaign

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A U.S. advocacy and public-engagement initiative that promotes coordinated prenatal, family-support, screening, well-baby, and infant/toddler-care services for children from pre-conception through age three.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **For Our Babies platform of supports organized around four pillars tracking development from pre-conception to age three**: The For Our Babies campaign outlines a comprehensive platform of services and supports for infants, toddlers, and their families. The article states it is "focused around four pillars that track babies' developmental trajectory from pre-conception to age three: prenatal care, paid leave; screening and follow-up services and well-baby care; and high-quality, affordable infant/toddler care." The initiative combines a book, multimedia website, public events, and outreach to families, educators, the press, and policymakers to spur local action and national policy change. (A Call to Action on Behalf of U.S. Babies (2014))
