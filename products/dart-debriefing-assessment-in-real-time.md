# DART (Debriefing Assessment in Real Time)

## Description
A real-time scoring instrument developed at CAPE that tallies instructor questions, instructor statements, and trainee responses during debriefings and calculates ratios intended to assess debriefer inclusivity and participant engagement.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **DART tool: a real-time quantitative debriefing assessment instrument based on cumulative tallies and ratios**: The DART (Debriefing Assessment in Real Time) is a scoring tool developed at CAPE that "scores observable sequential debriefing contributions in a cumulative fashion including Instructor Questions (IQ), Instructor Statements (IS) and Trainee Responses (TR)". It requires minimal training and yields two ratios, IQ:IS and TR:[IQ + IS], intended to estimate debriefer inclusivity and participant engagement. It was designed as a real-time objective alternative to subjective Likert-scale debriefing assessment tools. (Baliga Kaushik et al. (2022))
