Hallé SHINE on Manchester Saturday school programme¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (1 for, 2 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q4 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A SHINE Trust and Hallé Orchestra education-team programme providing music-themed supplementary literacy and numeracy lessons and cultural experiences for disadvantaged or underachieving Key Stage 2 pupils.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • The Hallé SHINE on Manchester Saturday school programme showed no evidence of impact on reading attainment across all three trial years [-M]
  • The programme showed no positive effects on secondary outcomes of maths attainment and attitudes to reading, maths, music, and school [-M]
  • Pupils who attended the Saturday school showed positive engagement, improved behaviour, confidence, and social skills per the process evaluation [+M]
