## Description
A dataset from the cluster-randomized evaluation and re-analysis of the Incredible Years Teacher Classroom Management programme, containing kindergarten-through-third-grade student and teacher measures of disruptive classroom behavior.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Incredible Years Teacher Classroom Management randomized controlled trial dataset (disruptive behavior re-analysis)**: A cluster-randomized trial evaluating the Incredible Years Teacher Classroom Management program, which focuses on effective classroom management practices for preschool and early elementary teachers. As the article states, "The RCT included 1817 students and 105 teachers in kindergarten to third grade from nine schools in a school district in the Midwestern part of the United States." Teachers were randomly assigned to IY TCM training or business as usual; the outcome re-analyzed here is student disruptive behavior measured with the TOCA-C subscale. The dataset serves as the step-by-step illustration of moderated GAMLSS in school-based intervention research. (Wiedermann et al. (2025))
