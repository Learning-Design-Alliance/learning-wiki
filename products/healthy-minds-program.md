## Description
A smartphone meditation and well-being training app developed and operated by the Center for Healthy Minds, organized around the Awareness, Connection, Insight, and Purpose model.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Awareness, Connection, Insight, and Purpose (ACIP) training-based model of well-being**: The ACIP model, proposed by Dahl and colleagues, holds that well-being rests on four trainable pillars: "awareness, connection, insight, and purpose (ACIP)". Awareness comprises attention and meta-awareness skills; connection emphasizes prosocial dispositions such as gratitude and kindness; insight targets clarity about the nature of one's identity and experience; and purpose aims to clarify values and apply them in daily life. The model claims these pillar skills "demonstrate plasticity" and that this plasticity "can be harnessed through intentional practice," specifically meditation training. The Healthy Minds Program app and this trial's hypotheses were constructed around this framework. (Hirshberg Matthew J. et al. (2022))
