## Description
A school-counselor consultation model developed by Warren et al. that combines REBT and social cognitive theory to address teachers' irrational and efficacy beliefs through cognitive, emotive, and behavioral strategies.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Rational emotive-social behavior consultation (RE-SBC) integrates REBT and social cognitive theory to address teachers' irrational and efficacy beliefs**: RE-SBC is a consultation model that combines rational emotive behavior therapy (REBT) principles with social cognitive theory (SCT) to help school counselors work with teachers. In REBC, school counselors help identify and challenge "irrational beliefs that impede teachers' classroom performance." The model targets irrational beliefs (e.g., self-downing, low frustration tolerance, demandingness) and efficacy beliefs through cognitive, emotive, and behavioral strategies. The article describes its application via face-to-face and asynchronous online group formats across eight weeks. (Jeffrey M. Warren et al. (2013))
