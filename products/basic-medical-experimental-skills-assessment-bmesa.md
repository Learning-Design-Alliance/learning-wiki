# Basic Medical Experimental Skills Assessment (BMESA)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A college-developed 100-point-per-objective assessment used by medical undergraduate programs to evaluate mastery of basic medical experimental principles, procedures, problem analysis, skills, research design, and scientific writing.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **BMESA: an eight-objective basic medical experimental skills assessment with 100 points per objective**: The basic medical experimental skills assessment (BMESA) is the outcome measure aligned with RFETM: 'The BMESA reflected students’ mastery of experimental principles, steps, problem analysis, and experimental skills, and evaluated the improvement of students’ innovative ability.' It has eight assessment objectives, from mastery of scientific research project design to mastery of paper writing, and 'The total score for each assessment objective was 100 points'. (Miao Chenggui et al. (2025))
