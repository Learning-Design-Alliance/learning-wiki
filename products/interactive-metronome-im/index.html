Interactive Metronome (IM)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

Interactive Metronome is a branded, computer-guided sensorimotor timing-training programme developed and delivered by Interactive Metronome, Inc., using metronome-synchronization exercises and millisecond performance feedback.

Components¶

  • Interactive Metronome (IM) synchronized metronome tapping intervention: A non-academic timing intervention in which participants wear a glove and clap "as close to the point in time when a reoccurring metronome beat is heard through headphones", receiving visual millisecond readouts and auditory feedback. Each participant completed "twelve 45 to 60 minute IM treatment sessions over about four weeks", with eight to twelve tasks per session using four to eight exercises, repetitions increasing from 30 to a maximum of 2000. Performance is scored as a mean millisecond deviation score, with lower scores indicating more accurate timing. (Gordon E. Taub (2012))

Claims¶

  • A synchronized metronome tapping intervention produced statistically significantly higher post-test Broad Reading scores than a non-treatment control in high school students [+M]
