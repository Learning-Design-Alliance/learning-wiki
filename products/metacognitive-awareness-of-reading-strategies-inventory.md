# Metacognitive Awareness of Reading Strategies Inventory

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
MARSI is a self-report assessment developed by Mokhtari and Reichard to measure learners' awareness and use of global, problem-solving, and support reading strategies.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MARSI-based questionnaire adapted to Vietnamese for measuring metacognitive reading strategy use**: A 30-item, 5-point Likert questionnaire adopted from the Metacognitive Awareness of Reading Strategies Inventory (MARSI) by Mokhtari and Richard (2002), administered in Vietnamese with items clustered into three categories: Global Reading Strategies (items 1–13), Problem-Solving Strategies (items 14–22), and Support Reading Strategies (items 23–30). In this study it showed high internal reliability (α = .87) and was scored using high (≥3.5), moderate (2.5–3.4), and low (≤2.4) usage levels. (Thao M. T. Nguyen et al. (2011))

## Description
A 30-item, 5-point Likert questionnaire adopted from the Metacognitive Awareness of Reading Strategies Inventory (MARSI) by Mokhtari and Richard (2002), administered in Vietnamese with items clustered into three categories: Global Reading Strategies (items 1–13), Problem-Solving Strategies (items 14–22), and Support Reading Strategies (items 23–30). ## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administered in the learners' first language and with items clustered by category to support understanding
#### Constraints
- Self-report instrument; the authors note some learners may report awareness of strategies without actually using them when reading

### Target Learners
- adolescent and adult EFL learners

### Target Learning Goals
- awareness and self-reported use of metacognitive reading strategies
