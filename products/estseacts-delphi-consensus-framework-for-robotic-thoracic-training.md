## Description
A European Society of Thoracic Surgeons and European Association of Cardiothoracic Surgery framework specifying simulation, supervised console practice, and proctored assessment for robotic thoracic surgery training.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ESTS/EACTS Delphi consensus framework for robotic thoracic training with phases aligned to a recognised learning curve**: The article describes a 2018 Delphi consensus statement by the European Society of Thoracic Surgeons (ESTS) and European Association of Cardiothoracic Surgery (EACTS) as a framework for robotic thoracic training. It states that "The robust framework defined training phases aligned to a recognised learning curve, advocated for mandatory dry-lab and simulation-based training, and advised supervised console exposure with formal proctored assessment prior to independent practice." The article notes this framework was mainly focused on consultant-level training rather than for resident doctors. (Jesani Hannah S. et al. (2026))
