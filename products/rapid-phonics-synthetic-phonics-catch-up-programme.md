## Description
Rapid Phonics is Pearson's structured synthetic-phonics intervention, developed from Sound Discovery by Marlynne Grant, for teaching grapheme–phoneme correspondences, blending, decoding, spelling, and reading fluency to struggling or beginner readers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Rapid Phonics synthetic phonics catch-up programme with Snappy Lesson format**: Rapid Phonics is a synthetic phonics intervention that "teaches the relationship of word sounds to their corresponding letter groups in a structured way", developed from Sound Discovery by Marlynne Grant. In this trial it was delivered as three half-hour lessons weekly in groups of four or fewer across six weeks in Year 6 and six weeks in Year 7, using interactive exercises, word games, posters, e-books, magnetic boards and letters. Lessons followed the Snappy Lesson format with recaps, phoneme synthesis, spelling rules such as the split digraph, aloud reading of illustrated books, and writing. It is published by Pearson's and is adaptable for whole classes, Year 2 strugglers, or older struggling readers. (King et al. (2015))
