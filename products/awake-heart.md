## Description
Awake Heart is a free educational virtual-reality anatomy application that teaches heart structures, functions, and related medical conditions through a narrated interactive 3D model.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Awake Heart VR anatomy tutorial**: Awake Heart is a free educational VR application used as the instructional treatment in the study, covering names of heart components, heart functions, and potential medical conditions such as tachycardia and heart murmurs. In it, "study participants explored a highly detailed, anatomically accurate 3D model of the human heart. The treatment combined guided narration with interactive elements that required participants to identify anatomical structures." The video group watched a screencast of the identical tutorial on a standard class projector, with no pausing or rewinding. (Kaplan-Rakowski et al. (2026))
