# SMART®

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
Start Making a Reader Today (SMART®) is a low-cost Oregon school-based programme that provides volunteer one-on-one reading tutoring and free books to K–2 students at risk of reading failure.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SMART® volunteer one-on-one reading tutoring program with book giveaways for at-risk K-2 readers**: Start Making a Reader Today® is a low-cost volunteer tutoring program for students in grades K-2 at risk of reading failure, operating in Oregon schools where at least 40% of students are eligible for free or reduced-price lunch. Volunteer tutors "read one-on-one with students twice a week for half an hour," typically with one volunteer working with two children. The program also gives each student two new books a month to encourage family reading, and overall program cost runs approximately $300 a year per child. (What Works Clearinghouse (2007))
- **SMART® four-strategy volunteer tutoring pattern: reading to, reading with, re-reading, and questioning**: The SMART® program structures minimal-training volunteer tutoring around four reading strategies: "reading to the child, reading with the child, re-reading with the child, and asking the child questions about what has been read." A volunteer handbook describes these strategies, and volunteers rely on their own judgment for any other needs. The pattern intentionally places minimal demands on volunteer tutors and classroom teachers, with training focusing as much on tutoring logistics as on reading instruction techniques. (What Works Clearinghouse (2007))
