## Description
A researcher-developed self-report scale measuring professional study, scientific research, academic ecological, and self-development and employment stress among medical master's students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Four-dimension classification of academic stress for medical master's students**: The study organizes academic stress for medical master's students into four dimensions measured by a 22-item scale adapted from the school stress model and prior postgraduate stress instruments. As printed, "the final scale includes four dimensions and 22 items": professional study stress, scientific research stress, academic ecological stress, and self-development and employment stress. The framework underpins the study's hypotheses about how different stress types relate differently to achievement, and the authors use it to distinguish 'challenging' from 'impeding' stressors. (Li Haiyang et al. (2026))
