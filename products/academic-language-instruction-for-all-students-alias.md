## Description
A 20-week classroom-based academic-vocabulary curriculum developed by Lesaux and colleagues for linguistically diverse sixth-grade students in mainstream ELA classrooms.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ALIAS academic vocabulary intervention design framework**: The Academic Language Instruction for All Students (ALIAS) program is a 20-week classroom-based vocabulary intervention organized around four design principles: text-based instruction in authentic contexts, focus on high-utility general academic words taught from multiple angles, building word-learning ability through morphological awareness, and integrating reading, writing, listening, and speaking opportunities. It features "9 two-week units, each consisting of a 9-day lesson cycle" using short informational texts from Time for Kids, targeting 70 high-utility academic words drawn largely from the Academic Word List. (Lesaux et al. (2012))
