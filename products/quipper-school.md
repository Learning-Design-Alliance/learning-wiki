# Quipper School

## Description
Quipper School is a web-based learning-management platform operated by Quipper that provides OER-based multimedia materials, class management, teacher–student communication, and blended-learning support.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Quipper School learning management system providing free OER-based learning materials**: Quipper School is a website facilitating technology-assisted learning, described as part of the implementation of the OER concept. It provides "free facilities and learning materials in the form of very various form of articles, animations, and video tutorials"; teachers create classes students join by code, interactions occur via links, chat and messages, and teachers can add additional material. In Indonesia it had more than 50,000 teachers and more than 250,000 students signed in by the end of 2015, with about 60 percent active monthly. (Sulisworo et al. (2017))
