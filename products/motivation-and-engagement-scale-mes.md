# Motivation and Engagement Scale (MES)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 44-item self-report assessment developed by Andrew J. Martin for measuring multidimensional student motivation and engagement across elementary, high-school, and university/college versions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The Motivation and Engagement Wheel integrates motivation and engagement into four higher-order adaptive and maladaptive factors**: The Motivation and Engagement Wheel (Martin, 2007) is a multidimensional framework integrating perspectives on student motivation and engagement. It comprises "the integrative four higher-order level of factors and 11 lower-level factors": adaptive motivation (self-belief, valuing, learning focus), adaptive engagement (planning, task management, persistence), maladaptive motivation (anxiety, failure avoidance, uncertain control), and maladaptive engagement (self-sabotage, disengagement). This study applies its 44-item MES instrument, for the first time, to Chinese primary school students. (Qi Xia et al. (2022))
