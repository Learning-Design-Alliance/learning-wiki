# ONTRAC: Online Neuroplasticity Targeted Remediation of Attention deficits in Children

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A 25-module online neuroplasticity-based cognitive training programme developed by Mishra et al. for children with ADHD, using adaptive attention exercises and gamified features.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ONTRAC: Online Neuroplasticity Targeted Remediation of Attention deficits in Children**: ONTRAC is an online neuroplasticity-based training program of "25 online cognitive exercise modules that either focused on attended-signal training or distractor-suppression training". Challenge is adaptively managed by staircase algorithms maintaining performance in the 75–85% range, with visual and auditory stimulus complexity progressing from simple to complex. Exercises are wrapped in a meta-game with avatars, ranks, rewards and feedback to promote compliance. (Mishra J et al. (2016))

### Claims
- [Training-driven improvements in distractor suppression, but not attended-signal accuracy, correlate with ADHD symptom improvement](../claims/distractor-suppression-correlates-adhd-improvement.md) [+W]
