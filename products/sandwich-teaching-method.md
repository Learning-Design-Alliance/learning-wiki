## Description
A clinical teaching model that alternates theoretical instruction with practical operation and student-led teacher–student and peer interaction, used especially in medical education.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Sandwich teaching method as alternating theory and practice with student-led interaction**: The article presents the sandwich teaching method as a clinical teaching model, originating from nineteenth-century British university sandwich courses and later introduced into medical classrooms, that "emphasizes the combination of theoretical teaching and practical operation." It positions students as the classroom主体 within a teacher-guided, student-led system, cultivating analysis and problem-solving through multiple teacher–student and student–student interaction links in which communication and cooperation are exercised. (Li L et al. (2026))
