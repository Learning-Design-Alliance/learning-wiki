## Description
Talk for Writing is a literacy curriculum approach developed by Pie Corbett and promoted for school-wide use to teach writing through imitation, innovation, and invention.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The 3Is framework: imitation, innovation and invention as a developmental scaffold for writing**: The 3Is framework is the distinctive pedagogical structure of Talk for Writing, in which pupils move from learning texts by heart (imitation), to adapting known stories to create their own versions (innovation), to composing original writing (invention). The article states that "The 3Is framework entails working from (1) Imitation, to (2) Innovation, and finally to (3) Invention in children’s writing" and regards it as a way of scaffolding the writing process, with responsibility shifting from teacher to pupil. The evaluators note the first two phases are unique to the approach, while invention is common later primary practice, and question whether the framework matches the original scaffolding concept's timescales. (Julie Dockrell et al. (2015))
