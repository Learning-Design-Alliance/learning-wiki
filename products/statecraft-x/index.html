Statecraft X¶

Description¶

Statecraft X is a multiplayer mobile governance simulation game in which students act as town governors and make value-laden decisions about wealth, citizenship, and citizen happiness.

Components¶

  • Statecraft X multiplayer mobile game on governance: Statecraft X is a multiplayer client-server mobile game, typically played on Apple iPhones, in which students take the role of town governors in the medieval fantasy kingdom of Velar. The article states: "Students are assigned the role of town governor in the game. When the game begins, each student is the governor of one town." Students expand their influence across towns, compete ideologically within factions, and manage embedded tensions between economic wealth and citizen happiness. A typical game session supports 20 concurrent players across four factions and lasts about three weeks, running continuously as a 24/7 game. (Chee et al. (2013))
