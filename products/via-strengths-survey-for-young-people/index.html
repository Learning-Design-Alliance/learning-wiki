VIA Strengths Survey for Young People¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A self-report assessment developed by the VIA Institute on Character for identifying character strengths in youth.

Components¶

  • Values in Action (VIA) Inventory of Character Strengths Survey for youth: The VIA Inventory is Peterson and Seligman's device for defining and categorizing character strengths and virtues, adapted for youth as the VIA Strengths Survey for Young People. In this study it was completed by each child with learning difficulties to establish individual strengths, and was combined with three class lectures on positive psychology as the intervention. The article notes that "adults on a global scale endorsed 24 character strengths" in prior international trials, and that positive psychology studies had not yet been adapted for children with LD. (Mohaned Ghazi Abed (2016))
