# Manchester Endoscopic Learning Tool (MELT) programme

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A structured, validated training pathway developed for novice endoscopic vein-harvesting practitioners to improve procedural performance and confidence.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Manchester Endoscopic Learning Tool (MELT) structured EVH training programme**: The MELT programme is the authors' structured, in-house EVH training pathway. The review reports that "Significant improvements in vein conduit quality were demonstrated alongside increased operator confidence when EVH was performed by practitioners trained using the Manchester Endoscopic Learning Tool (MELT) programme [77]", and states many centres have adopted this structured training pathway as standard EVH training. The review also argues an in-house structured, validated learning tool should minimise training costs for novice harvesters. (Krishnamoorthy Bhuvaneswari et al. (2016))
