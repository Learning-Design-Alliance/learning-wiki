# SHERPA

## Description
SHERPA is a multi-turn reinforcement-learning framework developed by Xu et al. for optimizing AI-teacher tutoring strategies against measured learning gains in simulated language-model student archetypes.

### Claims

- [Training a teacher LLM with SHERPA on diverse-preference student archetypes raises simulated student test accuracy across all seven archetypes, including unseen ones](../claims/sherpa-dp-raises-student-accuracy-all-archetypes.md) [+M]
- [SHERPA training transfers to the external MathTutorBench benchmark, raising the pedagogy average from 52.5% to 79.2% and beating PedagogicalRL on all four response-generation metrics](../claims/sherpa-transfers-mathtutorbench-pedagogy.md) [+M]
- [Adding gate-failure penalties based on predefined pedagogical criteria harms teaching performance and generalization, while training on student improvement alone allows broader capabilities to emerge](../claims/gate-penalties-harm-generalization-student-improvement-reward.md) [+M]

- [SHERPA-style teaching-specific training shows a side effect: mistake-location accuracy declines across all trained teachers, and most regress on Socratic questioning](../claims/teaching-training-side-effect-mistake-location-decline.md) [-W]
