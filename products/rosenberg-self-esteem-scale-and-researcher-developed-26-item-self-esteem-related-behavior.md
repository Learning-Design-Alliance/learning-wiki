# Rosenberg Self-Esteem Scale and researcher-developed 26-item self-esteem-related behavior questionnaire

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A self-administered assessment battery used by Alghamdi Saleh A. et al. to measure self-esteem and self-esteem-related behaviors among Saudi medical students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Rosenberg self-esteem scale and 26-item self-esteem-related behavior questionnaire with pilot reliability**: The study instrument combined demographic items, Rosenberg's 10-item self-esteem scale on a 4-point Likert scale, and a researcher-developed 26-item behavior questionnaire. In a pilot with 39 participants, the Rosenberg scale showed "a reliability result of 0.777 Cronbach Alpha", the behavior questionnaire 0.905, and the overall reliability 0.841, which the authors took as indicating the questionnaires were valid for use. (Alghamdi Saleh A. et al. (2023))
