## Description
A tutor-rated clinical performance assessment instrument for medical students that scores patient-centred consultation, history taking, physical examination, and problem solving and management.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **RICS (Rating Instrument of Clinical Consulting Skills) as tutor-rated secondary outcome measure**: The RICS is the trial's secondary outcome measure, a student performance assessment tool completed by clinical tutors. The article states that "the performance score by tutors gives a composite score and subscale scores of patient-centred approach, history taking, physical examination and problem solving and management". It was chosen because it was designed for the Patient Partnership Program, a similar teaching programme at the University of Tasmania, and its construct validity and psychometric properties have been reported. (Lai Michelle Mei Yee et al. (2014))
