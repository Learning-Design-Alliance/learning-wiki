# LEAP-OULAD leakage-controlled early-prediction benchmark

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A public benchmark dataset and evaluation codebase built from OULAD for leakage-controlled weekly early prediction of end-of-course learner outcomes using fixed machine-learning classifiers and multiple performance metrics.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **LEAP-OULAD leakage-controlled early-prediction benchmark with 32,593 instances across 22 course runs**: A public benchmark instantiation of LEAP on the Open University Learning Analytics Dataset, providing cutoff-specific datasets at weekly cutoffs t={7,...,56}, ten fixed-configuration scikit-learn classifiers, and ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, Brier and F1@0.5 metrics over five seeds. The article reports "32,593 instances from 22 course runs (7 modules, 4 presentations), with 15,385 successful (47.2%) and 17,208 unsuccessful (52.8%) outcomes". Code is released at the printed GitHub repository. (Ngoc Luyen Lê et al. (2026))
