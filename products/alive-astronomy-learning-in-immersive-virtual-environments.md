# ALIVE (Astronomy Learning in Immersive Virtual Environments)

## Description
ALIVE is a set of interactive astronomy visualization modules developed collaboratively by the Denver Museum of Nature & Science and Metropolitan State University of Denver for delivery in planetarium domes and classrooms.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ALIVE Uniview visualization modules and seasons curriculum quiz test bank**: ALIVE (Astronomy Learning in Immersive Virtual Environments) is a DMNS-MSUD collaboration developing interactive visualization modules in SCISS AB's Uniview software for seven astronomy topics, projected live by a navigator in both the Gates Planetarium dome and flat classroom screens. Learning was measured with weekly multiple-choice curriculum quizzes built from a test bank using popular alternative conceptions as distractors; the same five pre-instruction, nine contemporaneous, and five post-instruction seasons questions were used for every class, with "a reliability of 0.429, 0.590, and 0.429" (KR20) for the three question sets. (Ka Chun Yu et al. (2015))
