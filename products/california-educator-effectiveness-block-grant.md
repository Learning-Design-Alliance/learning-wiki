# California Educator Effectiveness Block Grant

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A California state grant programme that funds local educational agencies' professional learning and related educator-effectiveness, school-climate, social-emotional learning, mental-health, restorative-justice, and equity activities.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **California Educator Effectiveness Block Grant as a braiding hub for professional learning, school climate, and student well-being strategies**: An element of the braiding toolkit: the Educator Effectiveness Block Grant 'can be used to address areas of need related to professional learning, school climate, social-emotional learning, mental health, restorative justice, implicit bias, and discrimination and harassment prevention.' Table 1 lists fundable strategies, including 'professional learning, coaching, and mentoring opportunities for administrators and teachers, as well as paraprofessionals and classified staff,' covering coaching, instruction across subjects, reengagement and accelerated learning, trauma-informed practices, English Learner instruction, inclusive practices, and early childhood education. Six other funding sources can be braided with it. (Region 15 Comprehensive Center at WestEd (2022))
