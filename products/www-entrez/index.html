WWW Entrez¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 design), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

WWW Entrez is an NCBI-developed World Wide Web interface for searching and linking MEDLINE and public nucleotide and protein sequence databases.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Some users prefer a single-form Boolean query interface to the original Entrez interface [+W]
  • Browsing term lists (selection mode) helps searchers who do not know the exact query term [+W]
  • Internet-based Entrez use grew dramatically in 1994 while CD-ROM subscriptions plateaued [+W]
  • Post-deployment diagnostics attributed many remaining retrieval misses to query formulation, with bounded expansion recovering eleven of 24 original queries without lowering the relevance floor [~W]
