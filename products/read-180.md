# READ 180

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 10 claims (6 for, 2 mixed, 2 against) · 2 studies (1 quant-synthesis, 1 review), `q3` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 10 claims rest on one study

## Description
READ 180® is a branded blended-learning reading intervention for struggling adolescent readers, combining digital instruction, teacher-led small groups, independent reading, assessment, and professional development.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **READ 180® blended-learning reading intervention for struggling adolescent readers**: READ 180® is "a reading program designed for struggling readers who are reading 2 or more years below grade level," providing "blended learning instruction (i.e., combining digital media with traditional classroom instruction), student assessment, and teacher professional development." Sessions run 45–90 minutes and include whole-group instruction, three small-group rotations (adaptive computer application, teacher-led small group, independent reading), and whole-class wrap-up. The review evaluates it for students in grades 4–12. (READ 180® (2016))
- **READ 180 blended learning program for struggling readers in grades 4-12**: READ 180 is "a blended learning program designed for struggling readers who are reading 2 or more years below grade level." It combines online and direct instruction, student assessment, and teacher professional development, delivered in 45- to 90-minute sessions with whole-group instruction, three small-group rotations (adaptive computer application, teacher-led small group, independent reading), and whole-class wrap-up, for students in grades 4 through 12. (READ 180 evidence summary (2012))
- **READ 180® Student Application with six adaptive instructional zones**: The computerized READ 180® Student Application is one of three small-group rotations and "includes six components (called 'zones')": Explore (anchor videos with vocabulary activities), Reading (close reading of individualized texts at the student's instructional reading level), Language (vocabulary building and practice), Fluency (spelling and reading practice), Writing (argumentative, narrative, and informative essays), and Success (progressively more complex fluency and comprehension activities). "The goal of the READ 180® software is to continually adjust the level of instruction based on student performance." (READ 180® (2016))
