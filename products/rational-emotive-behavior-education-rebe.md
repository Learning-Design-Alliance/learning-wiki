## Description
A structured curriculum-based intervention derived from rational emotive behavior therapy that teaches learners to replace irrational beliefs with rational ones and develop more self-enhancing ways of thinking, feeling, and acting.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Rational emotive behavior education (REBE) as an outgrowth of REBT for reducing anxiety**: Rational emotive behavior education is described as "An outgrowth of rational emotive behavior therapy", which Albert Ellis first used in the mid-1950s to alter erroneous beliefs so they can be replaced by rational ones. REBE's central tenet is that a methodical, structured curriculum built on REBT concepts can enable people to take control of their lives by teaching them how to think, feel, and act in self-enhancing rather than self-defeating ways. It aims to change unfavorable beliefs related to value systems and replace them with true and sensible views, and it distinguishes self-esteem from self-acceptance. In this article it was operationalized as a 12-session intervention for teachers' computer anxiety. (Onah BI et al. (2023))
