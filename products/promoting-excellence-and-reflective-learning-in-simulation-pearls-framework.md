# Promoting Excellence and Reflective Learning in Simulation (PEARLS) framework

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Developed by Eppich and colleagues, PEARLS is a healthcare-simulation debriefing framework that combines learner self-assessment, focused discussion, and directive feedback.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PEARLS debriefing framework with three educational strategies (learner self-assessment, focused discussion, directive feedback)**: The article uses the Promoting Excellence and Reflective Learning in Simulation (PEARLS) framework to structure post-simulation debriefing. As the authors describe it, "Through PEARLS' three broad educational strategies (fostering learner self-assessment, facilitation of a focused discussion, and information provided via directive feedback) and its blended approach, debriefing was interactive and collaborative." Debriefing was conducted "immediately following both simulations in a safe environment" using Appendix F materials modeled after the PEARLS approach. In this publication PEARLS supplies the debrief structure for reviewing missed learning objectives and answering learner questions after each sedation complication case. (Dupont A et al. (2024))
