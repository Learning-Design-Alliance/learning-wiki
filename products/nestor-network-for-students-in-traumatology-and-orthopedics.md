# NESTOR (network for students in traumatology and orthopedics)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
NESTOR is a Blackboard-delivered e-learning module providing orthopedic examination videos, interactive radiology cases, audiovisual podcasts, and multiple-choice self-testing for students in traumatology and orthopedics.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **NESTOR e-learning module for traumatology and orthopedics**: NESTOR (network for students in traumatology and orthopedics) is an e-learning module provided through the Blackboard learning management system as a supplement to a problem-based block course. It contains four feature types as enumerated by the article: "Orthopedic examination videos", "Interactive radiology cases with X-rays, MRI- or CT-images and a patient history", "Audiovisual podcasts", and always-available "Multiple-choice questions" to enable self-testing. Its content contained no information not otherwise taught, and use was voluntary. (Back David A et al. (2014))

### Claims
