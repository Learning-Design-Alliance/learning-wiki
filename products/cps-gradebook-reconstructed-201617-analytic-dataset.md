## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CPS Gradebook electronic grading system and the reconstructed 2016-17 analytic dataset**: Gradebook was CPS's electronic grade tracking system, since replaced by the very similar ASPEN, that suggested default grading categories and weights teachers could alter and provided parent and student portals. The Consortium reconstructed five of 13 back-end tables into a single analytic file covering 17,056 students, 366 teachers, 86 schools, and 1,804 ninth-grade algebra and geometry sections. The report states it took "approximately six months to reconstruct and connect five of 13 different 'back end' Gradebook tables into a single file." (Diaz et al. (2021))
