## Description
ASPIRE is an authoring system and deployment environment developed for creating constraint-based intelligent tutors with domain models, student modelling, adaptive feedback, and problem selection.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ASPIRE constraint-based tutor authoring process applied to a procedural AR assembly task**: ASPIRE is an authoring system and deployment environment for constraint-based tutors. For MAT the authors followed the standard ASPIRE procedure with no changes to the authoring system: describing task characteristics (an 18-step procedural assembly task), composing a domain ontology of 14 concepts with properties and relationships, specifying problem/solution structures (with multiple correct solutions accepted where several configurations are valid), and generating a domain model of 275 constraints. ASPIRE-Tutor then performs student modelling, feedback and problem selection, matching every student action to constraints and providing progressively more detailed feedback as the student struggles. (Giles Westerfield et al. (2015))
- **The A-SPIRE teaching model: six iterative stages from theoretical understanding to practical creation of tailored health messages**: The A-SPIRE model is a structured pedagogical framework grounded in experiential learning and constructivist theory, comprising six iterative stages: Anchoring, Searching, Processing, Integrating, Realizing, and Evaluating. It aims to provide "a structured pathway from theoretical understanding to practical creation of tailored health messages." In the evaluated session, the stages moved students from context-setting through information gathering, mind-map organization, planning strategies for three demographic groups, producing a mask-design communication artifact, to multi-source (self, peer, teacher) evaluation. (Liu Minling et al. (2026))
