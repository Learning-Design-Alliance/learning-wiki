## Description
A researcher-designed 25-word vocabulary test by Ebubekir Bozavlı that assesses Turkish learners' recall of English word meanings with or without homophonous Turkish pronunciation cues.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Phonetic Association Method Vocabulary Test (25 English words with homophonous Turkish cues)**: A researcher-designed vocabulary test in which English words, mostly nouns and verbs, are listed with their English pronunciation and the Turkish meaning of that pronunciation. The article states it was "limited to 25 words in accordance with the design of the study", with high-frequency words selected from sources such as Time magazine and Daily News. The experimental group received the full test with homophone cues; the control group received a version containing only the English words. (Ebubekir Bozavlı (2017))
