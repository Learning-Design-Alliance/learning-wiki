## Description
A professionally produced planetarium educational show developed by ATLAS Collaboration members with production partners to introduce general audiences to dark matter and LHC research.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Phantom of the Universe planetarium show on dark matter and the LHC**: A planetarium show developed and produced by ATLAS members that explores dark matter "from the Big Bang to galaxies to underground searches to the LHC and ATLAS." The article reports it is in more than 750 planetariums in 74 countries, has been translated into 27 languages, and has been seen by more than 2 million people, winning an award for Outstanding and Innovative Production at an International Full Dome Festival. Production involved professionals including a Hollywood scriptwriter, producer, and Skywalker Sound, with consultation from experts at seven planetariums. (R. Michael Barnett (2023))
