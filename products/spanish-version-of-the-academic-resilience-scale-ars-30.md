# Spanish version of the Academic-Resilience Scale (ARS-30)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 30-item Spanish-language Likert questionnaire developed by Trigueros et al. to assess university students' academic resilience across perseverance, help-seeking adaptation and emotional response.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Spanish version of the Academic-Resilience Scale (ARS-30), a 30-item three-factor instrument for measuring academic resilience**: The Spanish ARS is an adaptation of Cassidy's academic-resilience scale, a questionnaire with 30 items answered on a 1-to-5 Likert scale following a scenario in which the student receives a bad grade and critical tutor feedback. It measures three factors: perseverance, reflection and adaptation of the search for help, and negative affect and emotional response. A high ARS score indicated greater academic resistance, and the full validated Spanish item text is printed in Appendix A. (Trigueros Rubén et al. (2020))
