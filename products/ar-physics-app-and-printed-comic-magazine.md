# AR-Physics app and printed comic magazine

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A smartphone-based augmented-reality physics learning resource developed by Lucia Helena Horta Oliveira that links a printed Newton's-laws comic magazine to animated GIFs and 3D figures.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **AR-Physics app and printed comic magazine with triggered GIFs and 3D figures for Newton's laws**: The central artifact is a printed comic-book magazine whose characters explain Newton's laws in simple language, combined with a mobile application (AR-Physics) that reads printed symbols on the pages and displays animated GIFs or 3D figures. The text states that "Através da tela do celular, o aluno poderá observar os exemplos em movimento , projetados na folha da revista", with figures directed to the studied context; the figure list shows twelve GIFs (e.g., 'homem na maca', 'foguete decolando', 'bala explodindo na parede') plus 3D Earth–Moon content triggered across the magazine's 17 pages. (Lucia Helena Horta Oliveira (2020))
