# AR-Physics app and printed comic magazine

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A smartphone-based augmented-reality physics learning resource developed by Lucia Helena Horta Oliveira that links a printed Newton's-laws comic magazine to animated GIFs and 3D figures.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **AR-Physics app and printed comic magazine with triggered GIFs and 3D figures for Newton's laws**: The central artifact is a printed comic-book magazine whose characters explain Newton's laws in simple language, combined with a mobile application (AR-Physics) that reads printed symbols on the pages and displays animated GIFs or 3D figures. The text states that "Através da tela do celular, o aluno poderá observar os exemplos em movimento , projetados na folha da revista", with figures directed to the studied context; the figure list shows twelve GIFs (e.g., 'homem na maca', 'foguete decolando', 'bala explodindo na parede') plus 3D Earth–Moon content triggered across the magazine's 17 pages. (Lucia Helena Horta Oliveira (2020))
- **AR Physics – Leis de Newton: an augmented-reality mobile app linked to a comic magazine whose targets trigger educational Gifs and 3D figures**: The app "Ar Phisycs - Leis de Newton" was developed exclusively for this research, built on the Unity platform with Vuforia plug-ins and JavaScript, reading printed targets in the comic magazine to trigger Gifs and 3D figures illustrating Newton's laws and related quantities (resultant force, weight, friction, normal force, Moon–Earth interaction). The authors state the app "guardará as informações (Gifs e 3D)", so it works without internet and was made available for free download on Google Play and at the author's blog, https://physics7.school.blog. Each law has dedicated Gifs, e.g. a person on an unfastened stretcher for the first law. (Ayler F. Horta Oliveira; Lucia Helena Horta Oliveira (2018))
