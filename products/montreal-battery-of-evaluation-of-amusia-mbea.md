# Montreal Battery of Evaluation of Amusia (MBEA)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The MBEA is a standardized battery developed by Isabelle Peretz and colleagues that assesses multiple components of music processing, including scale, contour, interval, rhythm, metric, and musical memory.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Montreal Battery of Evaluation of Amusia (MBEA) as a measure of musical aptitude**: The MBEA is "a standardized test that assesses multiple components of music processing", including scale, contour, interval, rhythm, metric, and music memory. In this study it was accessed online through Isabelle Peretz's Lab website and administered to 250 medical students in English, alongside demographic questions on gender, past musical experience, and linguistic preferences. (Chopra Jasneep Singh et al. (2025))
