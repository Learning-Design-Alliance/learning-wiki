# gradetools package

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An R package that automates repetitive administrative grading tasks while leaving pedagogical grading judgments, such as rubric design and evaluation of open-ended work, to human graders.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Automated grading workflow: systems that automate repetitive administrative grading tasks while preserving human pedagogical judgement**: The article defines automated grading workflows as "systems that automate all or most repetitive grading tasks so as to reduce the time and effort required for a grader to assess students' work". The framework distinguishes pedagogical tasks, which have "a direct impact on what students learn from their grade and their feedback", from administrative logistics tasks, and holds that automation should target the administrative ones. It is the organizing concept behind the gradetools package. (Federica Zoe Ricci et al. (2024))
