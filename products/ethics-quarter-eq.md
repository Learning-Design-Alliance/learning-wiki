## Description
A six-week, 12-quarter e-learning ethics intervention developed at the University of Turku for nurse managers, covering ethics knowledge, ethical influence, ethics research, and ethical problem solving.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Ethics Quarter: a five-dimension, 12-quarter e-learning intervention for nurse managers' ethical activity profile**: The Ethics Quarter (EQ) is an educational ethics e-learning intervention for nurse managers developed at the University of Turku under the CReDECI2 guideline. It has "five dimensions, 12 evidence-based educational quarters, including introduction and summary", covering developing own ethics knowledge, influencing ethical issues, conducting or implementing ethics research, identifying ethical problems, and solving ethical problems. NMs complete two quarters per week over six weeks, with exercises entailing self-reflection and development plans after every dimension. Its pedagogical basis is constructivism, treating NMs as active knowledge constructors who link new knowledge to prior experience. (Laukkanen L et al. (2022))
