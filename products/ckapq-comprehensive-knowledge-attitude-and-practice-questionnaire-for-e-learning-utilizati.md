# CKAPQ — Comprehensive Knowledge, Attitude, and Practice Questionnaire for e-learning utilization

## Description
A 16-item, five-construct, 5-point Likert questionnaire developed by Kumar Archana Prabu et al. to assess undergraduate medical and dental students' knowledge, attitudes, and practices regarding e-learning utilization.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CKAPQ — Comprehensive Knowledge, Attitude, and Practice Questionnaire for e-learning utilization**: The CKAPQ is a psychometrically validated questionnaire assessing multiple dimensions of e-learning adoption among undergraduate medical and dental students. Developed via Zhou's Mixed Methods Model with focus groups and expert content validation (Lawshe method), it began as 59 items across eight theoretical factors and was refined to 16 items across five constructs. The article states it "provides a new, standardized method to evaluate knowledge, attitudes, and practices related to E-learning among medical and dental students" and can serve as a benchmarking tool for institutional assessment and educational policy development. (Kumar Archana Prabu et al. (2025))
