# CTAT: Computational Thinking in AI Training Test

## Description
A validated multiple-choice assessment developed by Shile Zhang et al. for measuring higher-education CS and AI students’ computational-thinking concepts and practices in AI-training contexts.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CTAT: Computational Thinking in AI Training Test**: CTAT is a validated multiple-choice assessment of computational thinking in AI training contexts for higher education CS/AI students, developed using Evidence-Centered Design. The article states: "In this study, 34 multiple-choice items were developed across four scenarios: (1) Data, (2) Conditionals, (3) Loops, and (4) Testing and Debugging." After pilot testing one item was deleted, leaving 33 items used in the main study. Items use a notebook-style Python interface with schematic diagrams and visual symbols to reduce literacy and code-comprehension barriers, and combine AI model training tasks with text-based programming. (Shile Zhang et al. (2026))
