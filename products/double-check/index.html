Double Check¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Double Check is a school-based intervention developed by Bradshaw and colleagues that combines culturally responsive augmentations to Tier 1 SW-PBIS, CARES professional development, and adapted Classroom Check-Up coaching for teachers.

Components¶

  • Double Check CARES: five domains of culturally responsive practice: The Double Check professional development series maps onto five core domains of culturally responsive practice, the CARES domains: "Connection to the curriculum, Authentic relationships, Reflective thinking, Effective communication, and Sensitivity to students' culture". Each domain is taught through concrete classroom strategies rather than awareness-raising alone: linking lessons to students' cultural backgrounds, showing warmth and trust, reflective thinking and mindfulness to interrupt automatic biases, code-switching and non-judgmental communication, and framing culture-specific behaviors as differences rather than deficits. (Bradshaw et al. (2018))

Claims¶

  • Coached teachers were observed to use significantly more proactive behavior management and greater anticipation of and responsiveness to student problems than comparison teachers [+M]
  • Following school-wide Double Check professional development, all teachers reported improved culturally responsive teaching self-efficacy, multicultural efficacy, behavior management self-efficacy, and lower stress [+M]
  • Double Check coaching significantly reduced coached teachers' office discipline referrals of Black students relative to non-coached teachers, though this effect was sensitive to multiple-comparison adjustment [+M]
  • Students in classrooms of coached teachers showed more cooperation, less noncooperation, and fewer socially disruptive behaviors than students in comparison classrooms [+M]
