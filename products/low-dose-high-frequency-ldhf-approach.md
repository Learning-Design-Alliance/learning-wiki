# Low-dose, high-frequency (LDHF) approach

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A team-based maternal and newborn care in-service training approach that uses brief, structured, onsite, interactive activities spaced over time for health-facility providers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The LDHF approach: short, structured, onsite, interactive learning activities spaced over time to optimize learning**: The article defines the low-dose, high-frequency (LDHF) approach as an in-service learning model that delivers "information based on local needs during short, structured, onsite, interactive learning activities that involve the entire team and are spaced over time to optimize learning." It also involves brief ongoing onsite activities such as skills practice, team drills, games, and quality improvement activities to sustain learning and support clinical decision-making. The trial is designed to test LDHF plus mobile mentoring against traditional offsite group training. (Ugwa Emmanuel et al. (2018))
