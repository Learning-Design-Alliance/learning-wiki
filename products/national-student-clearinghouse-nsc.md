## Description
National Student Clearinghouse data are a nonprofit repository of postsecondary enrollment, completion, and related records voluntarily contributed by participating institutions and used for education research and reporting.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **National Student Clearinghouse (NSC) data as a source for two-year college outcomes**: NSC is a nonprofit central repository for college enrollment and completion data, with institutions serving 97 percent of college students voluntarily sharing enrollment data. The article describes its strengths as consistent, comprehensive, and national in coverage, including out-of-state and private institutions. Its limitations include limited program information, incomplete certificate and degree information, missing colleges and students, and possible matching errors, since "Colleges that provide records to NSC may opt to not share records for non-degree seeking students." (Jenny Nagaoka et al. (2020))
- **National Student Clearinghouse first-year persistence and retention snapshot report and downloadable data tables**: A National Student Clearinghouse snapshot report series tracking first-year persistence (continued enrollment at any institution) and retention (continued enrollment at the starting institution) for fall entering cohorts, with data current through fall 2016. The report defines its measures and cohort-selection rules and states that "Data tables for this snapshot may be downloaded from the NSC Research Center website" as an Excel file. It is sponsored in part by the Lumina Foundation. (First-Year Persistence et al. (2017))

## Description
NSC is a nonprofit central repository for college enrollment and completion data, with institutions serving 97 percent of college students voluntarily sharing enrollment data. The article describes its strengths as consistent, comprehensive, and national in coverage, including out-of-state and private institutions. Its limitations include limited program information, incomplete certificate and degree information, missing colleges and students, and possible matching errors, since "Colleges that provide records to NSC may opt to not share records for non-degree seeking students."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Institutional participation in voluntary NSC reporting and a data services agreement (CPS obtains NSC data for its graduates)
#### Constraints
- Credential name field often blank for two-year completers
- Some colleges, particularly for-profit two-year colleges, do not provide data
- Students may opt out under FERPA, and some colleges omit records for students without SSNs
- Matching is based on name and birthdate and can introduce a small amount of error

### Target Learners
- High school graduates whose postsecondary enrollment is tracked by districts and states

### Target Learning Goals
- Reporting college enrollment and credential completion rates for research, policy, and accountability
