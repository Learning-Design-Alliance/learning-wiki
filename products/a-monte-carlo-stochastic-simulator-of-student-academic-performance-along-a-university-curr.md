# A Monte Carlo stochastic simulator of student academic performance along a university curriculum

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A computer-based Monte Carlo simulator developed by Caro Huertas et al. to model university student passing, failing, and progression probabilities from historical data under institution-specific regulations.
