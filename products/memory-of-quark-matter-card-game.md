# Memory of Quark Matter card game

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A physics educational card game and accompanying deck-and-rules activity created by J. Csörgő et al. for introducing learners to elementary particles, quark–gluon plasma, and hadronization.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Memory of Quark Matter card game deck and rules**: A card game in which players detect particles from a face-up heap representing Quark Matter, the perfect fluid of quarks. Players first remove neutrinos without scoring, then search for lepton-antilepton pairs, and finally form baryons and mesons from remaining quarks and antiquarks until all quarks are used up. The deck uses colored quark cards, two-colored antiquark cards, and black-and-white lepton-pair cards. (J. Csörgő et al. (2012))
- **Four-level difficulty progression in a particle-physics card game**: The Quark Matter card game is played on four levels of increasing physics knowledge demand. On a beginner level players form hadrons without knowing hadron names, ensuring color neutrality. On an intermediate level players learn particle names using a table and must name each formed hadron. On an advanced level players race to collect and name combinations. On a layperson level the game becomes a Memory-style face-down matching game. (J. Csörgő et al. (2012))
