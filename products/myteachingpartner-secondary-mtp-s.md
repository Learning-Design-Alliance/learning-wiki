## Description
MyTeachingPartner–Secondary is a University of Virginia Center for Advanced Study of Teaching and Learning professional-development programme that combines a video library of effective teacher–student interactions with individualized web-based coaching for secondary teachers.

## Components

## Description
MTP-S is a professional development program aiming to increase student learning through improved teacher–student interactions. Teachers access "a video library featuring examples of high-quality interactions" and receive web-based coaching approximately twice per month. It uses the CLASS-S to define and observe effective teaching practices, and is available in a direct teacher support model and a coach support ('train-the-trainer') model.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers submit videotaped sessions of their own instruction to a trained consultant twice a month
- Consultants are master teachers trained in using the CLASS-S system
#### Constraints
- The study reviewed in this report used the direct teacher support model

### Target Learners
- middle and high school students in grades 6-12

### Target Learning Goals
- improved teacher-student interactions
- general academic achievement
