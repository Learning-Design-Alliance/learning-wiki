# BOPPPS six-step teaching model

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
BOPPPS is a goal-oriented, student-centered teaching framework organized around bridge-in, objectives, pre-assessment, participatory learning, post-assessment, and summary, used by educators to structure lessons and courses.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **BOPPPS framework as a structure for guiding purposeful observation in early clinical exposure**: The Bridge-in, Objectives, Pre-assessment, Participatory learning, Post-assessment, and Summary (BOPPPS) model is a structured, learner-centered instructional framework. In this study it was "not used merely as a general teaching format, but as a structure for guiding novice learners through a complex perioperative environment": Bridge-in and Objectives clarified focus before exposure, Pre-assessment identified baseline understanding, Participatory learning used checklist-guided observation, and Post-assessment and Summary supported reflection. (Zhu Xiaocheng et al. (2026))
- **BOPPPS model as a structured closed-loop instructional framework with six components organized across three phases**: The BOPPPS (Bridge-in, Objective, Preassessment, Participatory Learning, Postassessment, and Summary) model is a student-centered, closed-loop instructional framework introduced by Douglas Kerr in 1978. It structures lessons around six components spanning preclass preparation, in-class implementation, and postclass consolidation phases. In this study, the model was implemented through the Chaoxing Learning Platform to deliver blended disaster nursing education, with preassessment and postassessment scores providing timely feedback for adjusting teaching strategies. (Li P et al. (2026))
- **BOPPPS six-phase structure as organizational backbone for FC-CBL delivery**: The BOPPPS model provides a structured six-phase teaching sequence—Bridge-in, Objectives, Pre-test, Participatory Learning, Post-test, and Summary—that organizes flipped classroom and case-based learning components into a coherent cycle. The authors state it "serves as the structural backbone that organizes the FC and CBL components into a coherent learning sequence." Each phase contributes a distinct function: capturing interest, setting goals, diagnosing gaps, enabling active problem-solving, providing feedback, and consolidating knowledge. (Xiao Meixia et al. (2026))
- **The BOPPPS teaching model structures each course into six sequential components: Bridge-in, Objectives, Pre-assessment, Participatory Learning, Post-assessment, and Summary.**: The BOPPPS teaching model structures the instructional process into six sequential components: introduction, learning objectives, pre-assessment, participatory learning, post-assessment, and summary, standardizing and improving the effectiveness of teaching delivery. Throughout the implementation process, the BOPPPS teaching model was applied to each course to structure and enhance the teaching process among 36 peritoneal dialysis specialist nurses from 10 regions of Zhejiang Province. (He Peipei et al. (2026))
- **BOPPPS model: a six-component pedagogical framework emphasizing student engagement and timely feedback**: The BOPPPS model structures teaching into six sequential components: Bridge-in, Objective, Pre-assessment, Participatory Learning, Post-assessment, and Summary. The article describes it as "a pedagogical approach that emphasizes student engagement and timely feedback, aiming to systematically enhance teaching effectiveness." In this study, the six stages were distributed across pre-class, in-class, and post-class activities, with participatory learning as the central interactive phase. The model is grounded in constructivist and experiential learning theory, integrating pre-class preview, in-class interactive participation, and post-class review. (Shen Qi-ling et al. (2026))
- **BOPPPS model as a structured, constructivist instructional framework**: The article describes the BOPPPS model as "a structured, student-centered instructional framework comprising Bridge-in, Objective, Pre-assessment, Participatory Learning, Post-assessment, and Summary" that "promotes active learning and continuous feedback." Grounded in constructivist learning theory, it emphasizes learner engagement and scaffolding, and in this study was combined with case-based learning to contextualize theoretical knowledge within authentic clinical narratives. (Fei Yuda et al. (2026))
- **BOPPPS-based blended teaching model as a framework for Clinical Epidemiology instruction**: The BOPPPS model comprises six components — Bridge-in, Objectives, Pre-assessment, Participatory learning, Post-assessment, and Summary — implemented in a flipped-classroom blended format spanning before-class, in-class, and after-class stages. The article describes it as combining "the strengths of traditional face-to-face instruction with online learning, emphasizing a student-centered philosophy", integrating case-based and problem-based learning. In this study it structures the Clinical Epidemiology course whose cognitive outcomes were surveyed. (Wang Junqiang et al. (2026))
- **BOPPPS model: a six-stage, student-centered instructional design framework**: The BOPPPS model is a structured instructional design framework comprising six modular steps: "bridge-in, objective, pre-assessment, participatory learning, post-assessment, and summary". Originally proposed by the Center for Teaching and Academic Development at the University of British Columbia, it prioritizes student participation and feedback and integrates active learning strategies such as case discussions, small group work, and problem-solving activities. The article describes it as operationalizing constructivist principles by activating prior knowledge, engaging collaborative meaning-making, and consolidating understanding through iterative feedback. In this study it was implemented on the Rain Classroom platform in a nephrology clerkship. (Wang Yujia et al. (2026))
- **The BOPPPS six-stage instructional framework**: The BOPPPS model is a structured instructional framework of six sequential stages: Bridge-in, Objective, Pre-assessment, Participatory Learning, Post-assessment, and Summary, originally developed at the University of British Columbia. The article describes it as "Grounded in constructivist learning theory and interactive teaching strategies", providing "a comprehensive framework for achieving instructional goals", and states it emphasizes clearly defined learning objectives and diverse teaching methods while promoting student-centered participation and timely feedback. (Khalafi Ali et al. (2026))
- **BOPPPS instructional model: a six-phase, feedback-oriented framework for structured classroom teaching**: The BOPPPS model is a structured instructional framework comprising Bridge-in, Learning Objectives, Pre-Assessment, Participatory Learning, Post-Assessment, and Summary phases. The article describes it as "a structured framework designed to enhance classroom teaching through active engagement and real-time feedback", grounded in cognitive and constructivist principles, shifting focus from passive reception to participatory learning and allowing educators to adjust instruction dynamically based on incoming student input. In this study it supplies the in-class structure of the hybrid curriculum, centered on formative assessment enabling real-time monitoring of learning progress and immediate instructional adjustment. (Qiu Xili et al. (2026))
