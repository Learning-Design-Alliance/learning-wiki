# BOPPPS six-step teaching model

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
BOPPPS is a goal-oriented, student-centered teaching framework organized around bridge-in, objectives, pre-assessment, participatory learning, post-assessment, and summary, used by educators to structure lessons and courses.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **BOPPPS framework as a structure for guiding purposeful observation in early clinical exposure**: The Bridge-in, Objectives, Pre-assessment, Participatory learning, Post-assessment, and Summary (BOPPPS) model is a structured, learner-centered instructional framework. In this study it was "not used merely as a general teaching format, but as a structure for guiding novice learners through a complex perioperative environment": Bridge-in and Objectives clarified focus before exposure, Pre-assessment identified baseline understanding, Participatory learning used checklist-guided observation, and Post-assessment and Summary supported reflection. (Zhu Xiaocheng et al. (2026))
- **BOPPPS model as a structured closed-loop instructional framework with six components organized across three phases**: The BOPPPS (Bridge-in, Objective, Preassessment, Participatory Learning, Postassessment, and Summary) model is a student-centered, closed-loop instructional framework introduced by Douglas Kerr in 1978. It structures lessons around six components spanning preclass preparation, in-class implementation, and postclass consolidation phases. In this study, the model was implemented through the Chaoxing Learning Platform to deliver blended disaster nursing education, with preassessment and postassessment scores providing timely feedback for adjusting teaching strategies. (Li P et al. (2026))
