# First 3 Minutes pediatric resuscitation RCDP simulation curriculum

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A MedEdPORTAL curriculum by Songer et al. that provides a 30-minute in situ rapid-cycle deliberate-practice simulation for acute-care teams managing an unresponsive, pulseless infant.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **First 3 Minutes pediatric resuscitation RCDP simulation curriculum with eight peer-reviewed appendices**: A reusable MedEdPORTAL curriculum for a 30-minute in situ RCDP simulation in which participants resuscitate an unresponsive, pulseless infant. It includes eight appendices peer reviewed as integral parts of the publication: a facilitator guide, simulation scenario with critical action points, facilitator scripts and teaching points, visual aid with objectives, team role cards, pre- and postparticipation surveys with a CPR knowledge test, and a key take-home points sheet. Critical actions include checking responsiveness, calling for help, starting compressions, ventilating, and placing defibrillation pads while maintaining high-quality CPR. (Songer K et al. (2025))

### Claims
- [Knowledge of high-quality CPR principles improves from a mean 68% to 85% after the RCDP simulation, with improvement significant by role for advanced practice providers and resident/fellows but not reaching significance for nurses](../claims/rcdp-simulation-improves-cpr-knowledge-scores.md) [+M]
