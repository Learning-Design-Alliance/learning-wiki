# Modified Queen's Simulation Assessment Tool (mQSAT)

## Description
The mQSAT is a Likert-style instrument developed by the study authors to assess medical students' interprofessional simulation performance across primary assessment, diagnostic actions, therapeutic actions, communication, and global performance.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Modified Queen's Simulation Assessment Tool (mQSAT) for cross-modality simulation performance assessment**: The mQSAT quantified simulation performance in this study using 5 Likert-style scales ranging from inferior (1) to superior (5) performance, four rating primary assessment, diagnostic actions, therapeutic actions and communication, plus a global rating. Two unblinded authors marked all simulations; assessment simulations were additionally video-recorded and double-marked by two blinded assessors randomly allocated from the other study authors. (Medwell Lauren et al. (2026))
