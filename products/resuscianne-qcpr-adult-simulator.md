# ResusciAnne QCPR Adult simulator

## Description
ResusciAnne QCPR Adult is a Laerdal Medical feedback-equipped manikin simulator used to train and assess cardiopulmonary resuscitation performance.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ResusciAnne QCPR Adult feedback-equipped simulator on a customized portable cart**: The study used the ResusciAnne QCPR Adult simulator (Laerdal Medical, Norway) mounted on a customized cart designed to the organization's specifications. The setup "enhanced the portability of the device and facilitated implementation of the project in the workplace", allowing training in existing study, clinical, or common areas without a simulation center room or facilitators, with a simulation technician maintaining and operating the equipment. (Joyce Kelly Barreto Silva et al. (2025))

### Claims
- [Among fifth- and sixth-year medical students, overall CPR performance, compression depth, and compression rate were higher after practice with a feedback-equipped CPR simulator than at baseline practice without feedback, with change measured within one group from pre-test to post-test](../claims/feedback-cpr-simulator-improves-performance-baseline.md) [+M]
