# Anonymous online medical English course evaluation questionnaire

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A 14-item anonymous five-point Likert questionnaire developed by Lin Qiang et al. to measure physiotherapy students' perceptions of medical English course attractiveness, teaching effectiveness, workload, resources, and needed improvements.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Anonymous online student questionnaire for medical English course evaluation with reported reliability and validity statistics**: An anonymous online questionnaire distributed by cell phone one week after the medical English course, collecting basic information (grade, age, gender, entrance exam scores, learning characteristics, objectives, weekly study time) and 14 five-point Likert multiple-choice questions grouped into five topics: course attractiveness, teaching effectiveness, workload, curriculum resources, and curriculum improvement. The article reports an external reliability coefficient of 0.814, Cronbach's alpha of 0.762, and a KMO value of 0.832. (Lin Qiang et al. (2021))
