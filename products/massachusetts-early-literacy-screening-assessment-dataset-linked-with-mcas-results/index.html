Massachusetts early literacy screening assessment dataset linked with MCAS results¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 3 studies (3 associational), q2 · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education dataset of K–3 early literacy screening results linked to state MCAS results for longitudinal analysis.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Exploratory analysis found no observed differences in later MCAS performance between students just below and just above screening risk thresholds [~W]
  • Early screening benchmark status predicts later performance: most beginning-of-year at-risk students stay at risk, and meeting benchmarks by end of grade 1 is associated with much higher grade 3 MCAS odds [+W]
  • Equipercentile linking shows grade 3 screening benchmark cut scores identifying at-risk students cluster in the MCAS Partially Meeting Expectations performance level, with variation among assessments [+W]
  • Screening assessment benchmarks indicating significant risk shift up and down on the MCAS scale within and across grades, so students with the same skills may be classified differently at different times [~W]
