## Description
Fathom is dynamic statistics software developed under the direction of Bill Finzer for educational exploration, visualization, and analysis of data.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Fathom dynamic data software as a pedagogical tool for statistical concept development**: Fathom is dynamic statistics software developed by chief designer Bill Finzer in the mid-1990s, targeting secondary and higher education students. The article stresses that it "is a pedagogical tool designed for concept development (Finzer, 2001), rather than software designed for professional data analysis". Its design drew on research about how students learn statistical concepts, and its dynamic nature and friendly interface let students manipulate a statistical environment, produce graphs, and visualize multiple representations of data and observe the effects of their own actions. (Nanteni Ganesan et al. (2018))
