> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A European Space Agency competition in which secondary-school teams design, build, test, and launch a can-sized satellite while completing primary and student-defined secondary missions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ESA European CanSat competition as a project-based physics learning activity for secondary students**: The European CanSat competition asks student teams to build "a small satellite inside a soft drink can, launched by a rocket to an altitude of about 1000 m with an acceleration of 10g". Teams complete a primary mission (measuring pressure and temperature, transmitting to a ground station, landing safely) plus a team-designed secondary mission; ESA runs it to inspire young people toward science or engineering careers. (Giovanni Bindi (2017))
