# Dataset of 410 Northern California public non-charter high schools coded by physics access level

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A dataset compiled by David Marasco et al. containing demographic and physics-course access classifications for 410 qualifying public non-charter high schools in Northern California, including access to AP physics options.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Northern California AP STEM offerings dataset: SARC demographics plus UC Course List offerings for public non-charter schools over 200 students**: The study's dataset combines two printed sources: ethnic and socioeconomic data for each school for the 2016-17 school year drawn from the California Department of Education's School Accountability Report Cards, and course offerings determined "by looking at the University of California Course List search page," which provided uniform labels for high school classes. The sample was restricted to public, non-charter schools with more than 200 students in Northern California counties, covering eight AP STEM classes from Calculus AB through Physics-C E&M. (David Marasco et al. (2020))
