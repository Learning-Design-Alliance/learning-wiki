# SHORT debriefing assessment scale

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A brief scale for assessing healthcare simulation debriefers' attention to emotional release, context analysis, general-principle extraction, and transfer of learning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CDR-based debriefing framework emphasizing context analysis and contextualized transfer of learning**: The SHORT is grounded in the cognitive principles for debriefing of the CDR model and in principles of learning transfer. Its Analysis item "assesses how the debriefer considers the learners' performance during the case and explores their cognitive frames (contextualizing)", and its Transfer item assesses how the debriefer highlights overarching concepts pertinent to the present case (decontextualizing) and a future similar case (recontextualizing). The scale's development criteria also required "the need for an emotional phase, adequate context analysis and extraction of general principles from the situation, and systematic fostering of learning transfer". (Rivière Etienne et al. (2019))
