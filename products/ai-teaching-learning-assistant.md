# AI Teaching & Learning Assistant

## Description
An open-source Moodle plugin developed by Anna Ostrowska et al. that uses retrieval-augmented generation to provide grounded student tutoring and teacher-reviewed educational content generation.

### Claims
- [In an 18-participant user study, the system received a 4.00/5 overall recommendation, with hallucination resistance rated 4.44/5 and highest ratings for ease of starting a conversation (4.39/5) and response relevance and accuracy (4.06/5)](../claims/user-study-18-participants-recommendation-4-of-5.md) [+M]
