## Description
A framework for describing health information system adoption across availability, use, behavior changes, and outcome changes, developed for healthcare technology adoption analysis.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Clinical adoption meta-model (CAMM) framework for describing health information system adoption over time**: The clinical adoption meta-model (CAMM) is a framework developed to describe the adoption of health information systems over time. The article states that it "incorporates 4 dimensions: availability, use, behavior changes, and outcome changes." In this study, CAMM is used to interpret medical students' digital health attitudes and self-perceived competencies as behavior changes occurring in connection with the development of the Finnish national health care information system architecture across 2016, 2021, and 2022. (Paula Veikkolainen et al. (2025))

### Claims
