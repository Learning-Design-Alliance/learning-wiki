# TAME-ChatGPT survey instrument

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 25-item, seven-construct Likert-scale survey developed and validated by the study authors to assess university students' perceived determinants, attitudes, risks, anxiety, and adoption of ChatGPT for educational purposes.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **TAME-ChatGPT survey instrument (Technology Acceptance Model Edited to Assess ChatGPT Adoption)**: TAME-ChatGPT is a validated survey instrument based on the technology acceptance model designed to assess university students' attitudes towards and adoption of ChatGPT. It comprises 25 items across seven constructs — perceived usefulness, behavioral/cognitive factors, perceived risk of use, perceived ease of use, general perceived risks, anxiety, and attitude to technology/social influence — each scored on 5-point Likert items. The study administered it to 2240 students in five Arab countries and reported that confirmatory factor analysis indicated "the adequacy of the 'TAME-ChatGPT' constructs." (A multinational study on the factors influencing university students' attitudes and usage )
