## Description
OnLabs is a 3D game-like virtual biology laboratory developed by an interdisciplinary team at the Hellenic Open University for simulating microscopy experiments and practicing laboratory skills.

### Claims
- [Distance-learning students who chose to prepare with the OnLabs virtual lab, alongside a tutor-led Skype session, scored better on microscopy Pre-Tests in all three test-difficulty groups](../claims/onlabs-higher-pretest-baseline-all-groups.md) [+M]
- [The face-to-face tutorial equated Post-Test scores at low and medium difficulty, but With-OnLabs students scored higher in the high-difficulty group](../claims/onlabs-posttest-advantage-high-difficulty-only.md) [+M]
- [With-OnLabs students responded positively on an ARCS-based satisfaction questionnaire, with nearly all endorsing the Skype session and a majority wanting it before every lab exercise](../claims/onlabs-skype-satisfaction-arcs-positive.md) [+W]
