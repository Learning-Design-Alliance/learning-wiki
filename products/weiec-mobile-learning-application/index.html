WEIEC mobile learning application¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A mobile learning application developed by Mitra Yadiannur et al. that provides worked examples, evaluation questions, and essay exams for Indonesian high-school direct-current circuits.

Components¶

  • WEIEC mobile learning application for direct current circuits: WEIEC is a mobile learning application compiling worked examples for direct current circuits, intended for independent or classroom use. Its menus include materials, a worked example menu "containing 34 examples of problems, which are arranged based on the level of question and the solution", an evaluation menu with 10 short answer questions that block progression on incorrect answers, and an exam menu with 12 essay questions covering mathematical representation and circuit interpretation. (Mitra Yadiannur et al. (2017))

Claims¶

  • Expert and student judgements rate the WEIEC application feasible, with Aiken's V of 0.80 from experts and 0.81 from students [+M]
  • Using the mobile WEIEC application produced a significantly larger pretest-posttest gain in electric circuit interpretation ability than the control condition, with a reported 85.8% effective contribution [+M]
