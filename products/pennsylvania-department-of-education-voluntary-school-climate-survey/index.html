Pennsylvania Department of Education voluntary school climate survey¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 6 claims (5 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 associational), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A voluntary statewide school-climate survey developed and administered by the Pennsylvania Department of Education's Office for Safe Schools in partnership with REL Mid-Atlantic to measure school climate and identify schools needing support.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • No association found between 2021/22 school climate scores and the amount of virtual and hybrid instruction in 2020/21 [+W]
  • No evidence of a significant decline in school climate scores between 2018/19 and 2021/22 in the Pennsylvania sample [+W]
  • Students and teachers reported more positive school climate perceptions in 2020/21 during hybrid and remote learning than in 2018/19 and 2021/22 [+W]
  • School climate scores were steady across the years before COVID-19 in the Pennsylvania sample [+W]
  • Items in each of the three domains of the PDE elementary school climate survey measured the constructs they intended to measure [+W]
  • The safe and respectful school climate domain of the elementary student survey fell short of the established reliability threshold [-W]
