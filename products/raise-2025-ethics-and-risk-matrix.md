## Description
A supplementary fillable framework proposed by Allison (2026) for authors to identify, assess, and report ethical risks and mitigation strategies in educational AI studies.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **RAISE 2025 Ethics and Risk Matrix for surfacing under-reported AI risks**: The RAISE 2025 Ethics and Risk Matrix (Figure 2) is a supplementary fillable table complementing the core checklist. The article states "the RAISE 2025 Ethics and Risk Matrix invites authors to go further by critically reflecting on the diverse and often under-reported risks that can emerge when AI is deployed in educational contexts," including risks to learner agency, equity in access, data governance, and algorithmic transparency. Authors identify relevant risks by thematic category and describe potential impacts and mitigation strategies. "While its use is optional, we strongly recommend its inclusion, especially for studies involving generative or adaptive AI tools, complex student interactions, or deployment at scale." (Allison (2026))
