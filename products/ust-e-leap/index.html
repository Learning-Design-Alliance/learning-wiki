UST E-Leap¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 review), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

UST E-Leap is a web-based learning management software platform administered by the University of Santo Tomas for distributing course materials, supporting discussions and assessments, collecting submissions, and tracking scores.

Components¶

  • UST E-Leap learning management software for online writing instruction: UST E-Leap is a web-based course/learning management software (LMS) administered by the University of Santo Tomas that includes "announcement and discussion boards, class notes, traditional assessment tools like quizzes and examination, and score sheets or books." In this study it delivered ten writing lessons through PowerPoint and video presentations, with assignments submitted and evaluated online. (Topacio (2018))
