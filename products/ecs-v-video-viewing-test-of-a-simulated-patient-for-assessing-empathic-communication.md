# ECS-V (Video-viewing test of a simulated patient for assessing empathic communication)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A video-based assessment instrument developed by Shinohara Eriko et al. in which healthcare learners provide verbatim responses to a simulated patient and expert raters score empathic communication using the EUIP scale.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Video-viewing test of a simulated patient for assessing empathic communication (ECS-V)**: An assessment instrument in which participants watch an approximately 5-minute video of a simulated patient interviewed by a midwife and are asked what they would say to her verbatim; responses are rated on the EUIP 5-point empathy scale by expert raters. The article presents it as akin to a mock interview but "more standardised and easier to quantify", and as more convenient and cost effective than OSCEs, which require staff and time. Interrater agreement on 20% of responses was κ = 0.80. (Shinohara Eriko et al. (2022))
