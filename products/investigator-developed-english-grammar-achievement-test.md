# Investigator-developed English Grammar Achievement Test

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A researcher-developed pre-test and post-test measuring eighth-grade students' knowledge, understanding, and application of English grammar, validated by English-language subject experts and reported with reliability of 0.84.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Investigator-developed Mind Maps and Achievement Test (pre-test and post-test) for English grammar**: The study's measurement instrument is an achievement test in English grammar developed by the investigator, administered as pre-test and post-test alongside "Creation of mind map for selected English grammar" as an instructional tool. Reliability was established by the product moment correlation co-efficient, reported as "The reliability of the achievement test is 0.84". Validity was checked by subjecting the test to experts in English, whose suggestions led to item modifications. (D. Hallen (2015))
- **60-item grammar post-test combining a 30-blank cloze passage and 30 multiple-choice items**: The study's outcome measure was a 60-item achievement test of grammar with two parts: a cloze passage with 30 blanks followed by its 30 multiple-choice answers, and a 30-item multiple-choice grammar section. Students had 80 minutes to mark answers. Its reliability was checked by KR-21 in a pilot study and its validity by correlating with a Nelson test, yielding a reliability of 0.94 and a validity correlation of 0.41. (Minoo Alemi et al. (2011))
