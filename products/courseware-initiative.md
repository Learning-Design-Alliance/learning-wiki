## Description
A Gates Foundation initiative and theory-of-change framework that combines equity-centered digital courseware with instructors' evidence-based teaching practices to improve engagement and achievement, especially for students at risk of failing to earn course credit.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Courseware Initiative Theory of Change: courseware plus evidence-based teaching practices, not technology alone**: The Gates Foundation's theory of change for digitally enabled courseware posits that improved student engagement and achievement result from combining equity-centered courseware with instructor implementation of evidence-based teaching practices. The article states the theory "does not treat learning technology as a 'silver bullet'" and instead includes "instructor implementation practices grounded in empirical research on what constitutes effective instruction" as "a key part of the intervention to be scaled and tested." It targets students from groups historically at risk of failing to earn course credit. (Barbara Means et al. (2026))

## Description
The Gates Foundation's theory of change for digitally enabled courseware posits that improved student engagement and achievement result from combining equity-centered courseware with instructor implementation of evidence-based teaching practices. The article states the theory "does not treat learning technology as a 'silver bullet'" and instead includes "instructor implementation practices grounded in empirical research on what constitutes effective instruction" as "a key part of the intervention to be scaled and tested." It targets students from groups historically at risk of failing to earn course credit.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must implement evidence-based teaching practices alongside the courseware for the theory's predicted outcomes to materialize
#### Constraints
- Not a standalone technology intervention; effectiveness depends on instructor implementation quality

### Target Learners
- College students in gateway statistics courses, especially those from historically underrepresented backgrounds

### Target Learning Objectives
- Introductory statistics concepts and skills
- Course completion and credit attainment
