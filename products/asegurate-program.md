## Description
Asegúrate is a teacher-implemented secondary-school intervention developed by Del Rey and colleagues to reduce cyber-aggression, bullying, sexting, and abusive Internet and social-network use.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The 'Asegúrate' program is structured around three main pillars: normative social behavior, self-regulation skills, and adolescent ideas/beliefs**: The 'Asegúrate' program is a teacher-implemented intervention against cyberbullying and its associated risks. It is "structured around three main pillars": the theory of normative social behavior, self-regulation skills, and the ideas/beliefs held by adolescents. The normative pillar presents positive identification models and examines students' expectations; the self-regulation pillar includes reflective practice to enhance metacognitive skills; and the beliefs pillar uses a constructivist sequence of activities to identify, analyze, and reshape pre-existing ideas about virtual environments. (Del Rey et al. (2018))
