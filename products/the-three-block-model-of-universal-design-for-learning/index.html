The Three Block Model of Universal Design for Learning¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Jennifer Katz's three-block framework guides K–12 teachers in creating inclusive classrooms through social-emotional community building, inclusive differentiated instruction, and related implementation supports.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Implementing the Three Block Model of UDL significantly increases students' overall observed engaged behavior in inclusive K-12 classrooms [+M]
  • UDL treatment students were significantly more actively engaged while control students were significantly more passively engaged and spent more time not engaged [+M]
  • UDL treatment students interacted more frequently with peers and adults and spent less time not interacting [+M]
  • UDL instructional practices alone raised reported inclusivity and student autonomy but not classroom or school climate, with very small effect sizes on social variables [~M]
