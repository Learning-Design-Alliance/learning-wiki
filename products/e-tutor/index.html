E-Tutor¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A web-delivered intelligent language tutoring system developed by Trude Heift for university learners of German as a foreign language, providing curriculum-aligned activities, linguistically sophisticated answer processing, learner modeling, and instructor-accessible performance records.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Longitudinal learner data reveal individual differences in grammar acquisition not apparent in aggregate data, calling for fine-grained student models [+W]
  • Most learners (85%) prefer to work through error correction on their own, with proficiency moderating help use [+W]
