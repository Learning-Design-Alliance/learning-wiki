# Linked Swedish nationwide register dataset on school performance and family background (829,787 children born 1994–2004)

## Description
A dataset compiled by Okuyama et al. by linking Swedish national education, population, health, family, and labor-market registers to study ninth-grade school performance and family-background factors.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Umeå SIMSAM Lab linked total-population register data infrastructure**: The Umeå SIMSAM Lab is the Swedish data infrastructure through which the study's national registers were obtained. "The analyzed data include micro‐level medical and social information, and each individual is assigned a unique, fully anonymized, personal identification number (PIN) that links them to their parents and siblings across registers." Registers used include the Multi-Generational Register, Total Population Register, LISA, Medical Birth Register, National Patient Register, Prescribed Drug Register, and the Pupil Register, supporting whole-population linkage of health and educational records. (Bortes C et al. (2020))
