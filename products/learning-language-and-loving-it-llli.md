## Description
LLLI is a classroom-based language-intervention and teacher professional-development programme developed by Weitzman and Greenberg that trains educators to foster linguistically rich conversations with young children.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Learning Language and Loving It (LLLI) teacher professional development program**: LLLI (Weitzman & Greenberg, 2002) is a classroom-based language intervention delivered through teacher professional development. "The LLLI program provides professional development (PD) via workshops, manuals, and modest coaching designed to promote teachers' use of speciﬁc strategies to facilitate children's participation in linguistically rich conversations." Strategies include asking open-ended questions, following children's conversational lead, modeling advanced language forms, and recasting children's utterances across one-on-one, small-group, and whole-class interactions. In the RCT, 25 teachers implemented LLLI while 24 maintained business-as-usual practices. (Megan Johanson et al. (2016))
