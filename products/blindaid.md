## Description
BlindAid, developed by Orly Lahav and colleagues, is a haptic and spatialized-audio virtual-environment system for orientation-and-mobility training of people who are blind or have low vision.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **BlindAid virtual environment system with haptic and spatialized audio feedback**: BlindAid is a virtual environment system developed for O&M training that "allows interaction with different virtual components (structures and objects) via auditory and haptic feedback". It runs on a PC with a Phantom haptic device and stereo headphones; users control an avatar via the stylus, hear spatialized audio giving direction and distance of sound sources, and feel floors with different stiffness and texture. Nine Carroll Center spaces were modeled as virtual environments. (Orly Lahav et al. (2015))

### Claims
- [Experimental-group participants who practiced with BlindAid were more successful in 73% of real-space orientation tasks, especially perspective-taking and reverse tasks](../claims/blindaid-experimental-more-successful-orientation-tasks.md) [+M]
