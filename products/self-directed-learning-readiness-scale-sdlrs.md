# Self-Directed Learning Readiness Scale (SDLRS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 41-item self-report assessment developed by Fisher et al. to measure higher-education learners' self-directed learning readiness across self-management, desire for learning, and self-control.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Self-Directed Learning Readiness Scale (SDLRS) with three subscales**: The study measured self-directed learning with the SDLRS (Fisher et al., 2001), which "includes three subscales: self-management; related to learning process activities, desire for learning; associated with taking responsibility for learning, and self-control; related to control of the learning process". The scale has 41 items (13 self-management, 14 desire for learning, 14 self-control) and was administered three times. In this study the overall Cronbach alpha was 0.87, with subscale values of 0.90, 0.86, and 0.87. (Bagheri et al. (2013))
- **Fisher et al. 40-item Self-Directed Learning Readiness Scale (SDLRS) used to measure readiness in Nigerian nursing students**: The self-directed learning readiness scale (SDLRS) is a 40-item instrument measuring readiness across three domains: self-management (13 items), desire for learning (12 items), and self-control (15 items), answered on a 5-point Likert scale from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree). The study used it for pre- and post-test measurement, reversing four negatively phrased items. In this study the overall reliability coefficient was 0.945, with subscale coefficients of 0.756, 0.730, and 0.908. (Chikeme Paulina Chigwara et al. (2024))
- **Korean self-directed learning readiness scale (SDLRS) and group cohesiveness scale (GCS) instruments**: The study used two Likert-type instruments administered annually. The SDLRS was a Korean revision for college students with "a total of 23 items" across seven constructs including love of learning, openness towards learning, and self-perception, on a five-point scale. The GCS is "a seven-item, five-point, Likert-type scale" originally designed for psychiatric inpatients but suggested for any group activity involving interaction; it was translated into Korean and back-translated for this study, with Cronbach's alphas of .770 to .920 across years. (Kim S (2020))
