# Critical Reflection Competency in Delirium Care Scale (CRCDCS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 19-item, multidimensional Likert self-report scale developed by Magsoodi and colleagues to assess ICU nurses' analytical, ethical, and preventive reflective competence in delirium care.

### Claims

- [Exploratory factor analysis with 350 ICU nurses identified a three-factor structure (AECR, PCER, ROPR) explaining 57.34% of variance, with all 19 items loading strongly (0.633–0.853) and no substantial cross-loadings.](../claims/crcdc-efa-three-factor-structure.md) [+M]
- [Item generation for the CRCDCS combined a deductive systematic literature review (21 studies yielding 10 items) with inductive conventional content analysis of semi-structured interviews with 15 ICU nurses (yielding 20 items, 7 subcategories, and 3 principal categories).](../claims/crcdc-item-generation-deductive-inductive.md) [+M]
- [Exploratory Graph Analysis identified three distinct communities fully consistent with the theoretical three-factor model, supporting the structural validity and multidimensional nature of the CRCDCS.](../claims/crcdc-ega-network-structure.md) [+M]
- [Face validity assessment with 10 ICU nurses using the Impact Score formula led to removal of two items that demonstrated inconsistent interpretations of abstract concepts or partial conceptual overlap.](../claims/crcdc-face-validity-impact-score.md) [+M]
- [Content validity evaluation of the preliminary 30-item CRCDCS indicated CVR values from 0.40 to 1.00 with nine items below the 0.62 threshold removed, and I-CVI values from 0.80 to 1.00 for retained items.](../claims/crcdc-content-validity-cvr-icvi.md) [+M]
- [The CRCDCS demonstrated satisfactory internal consistency (α = 0.876; ω = 0.861) and test–retest reliability (ICC = 0.85, 95% CI: 0.82–0.91).](../claims/crcdc-reliability-alpha-omega-icc.md) [+M]
