Story Friends¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (2 causal), q3 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Story Friends is an automated Tier 2 preschool supplemental curriculum developed by Howard Goldstein and colleagues that delivers embedded vocabulary and story-comprehension instruction through prerecorded storybooks in small-group listening centers.

Components¶

  • Story Friends automated Tier-2 storybook intervention: Story Friends is "a small-group intervention in which vocabulary and question-answering lessons are embedded in a series of storybooks", delivered via prerecorded audio on mp3 players with headphones at classroom listening centers. The series includes 13 books (one introductory, nine instructional, three review) teaching 18 challenging Tier-2 words, 15 basic concept words, and 27 inferential story questions. An adult facilitator manages materials but provides no instruction. (Kelley et al. (2015))

Claims¶

  • Embedded vocabulary lessons in prerecorded storybooks produce large vocabulary learning gains relative to hearing the same stories without lessons [+M]
  • Embedded comprehension lessons did not improve story comprehension relative to story exposure alone [~M]
  • Story Friends treatment participants had greater vocabulary gains than comparison participants on unit tests, with large effect sizes [+M]
  • Story Friends produced no significant group effect on total story comprehension, but a significant group × time interaction emerged on inferential questions [+M]
  • The automated Story Friends intervention was implemented with high fidelity in preschool classrooms [+M]
