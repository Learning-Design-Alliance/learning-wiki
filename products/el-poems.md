## Description
El-Poems is a four-session web-based postural education programme for music students, delivered through the SHAD platform by trained physical-education and health educators.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **El-Poems four-session web-based postural education program delivered via the SHAD platform**: El-Poems is an e-learning postural education intervention for music students, delivered through the web-based Student Education Network (SHAD) at the Tehran Conservatory of Music. "The intervention program consists of four sessions, each one lasting for 90 min with a 1-week interval", implemented by a trained physical education instructor and a health educator. Content covers anatomy and physiology of the musculoskeletal system, proper postures for standing, sitting, lifting, and carrying instruments, upper limb position, stretching, and warming up before playing. (Akbari-Chehrehbargh Z (2022))
