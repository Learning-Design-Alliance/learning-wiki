# Modified Interprofessional Collaborator Assessment Rubric (ICAR)

## Description
A 17-item, six-domain, 9-point multisource assessment rubric developed to evaluate postgraduate residents' interprofessional collaboration competencies.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Modified Interprofessional Collaborator Assessment Rubric (ICAR) for multi-source feedback**: The modified ICAR is a 17-item rubric organized into six domains reflecting the CanMEDS Collaborator role, with items scored on a 9-point scale where "1 = well below expectations, 5 = meets expectations, and 9 = well above expectations". It was developed from interprofessional collaborator competency statements validated through typological analysis, a Delphi survey, and focus groups. In this study it was embedded in a multi-source feedback process in which physicians, nurses and allied health professionals rated post-graduate residents over four-week rotations, supplemented by qualitative comments. (Hayward Mark F et al. (2014))
