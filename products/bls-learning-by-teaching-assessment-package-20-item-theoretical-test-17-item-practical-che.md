# BLS learning-by-teaching assessment package: 20-item theoretical test, 17-item practical checklist, global rating scale and self-assessment

## Description
An assessment package developed by Veloso Sérgio Geraldo et al. for evaluating BLS theoretical knowledge, practical resuscitation performance, global performance, and learner self-assessment among medical students and community health professionals.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **BLS learning-by-teaching assessment package: 20-item theoretical test, 17-item practical checklist, global rating scale and self-assessment**: The study used a multi-instrument assessment package: a 20-item multiple-choice theoretical test on BLS developed by an ACLS-certified professor and reviewed by a second professor; a practical assessment station with a medium-fidelity mannequin, AED trainer and bag-mask measured by "a checklist consisting of 17 items followed by feedback"; a 9-item global rating scale completed by an observational teacher; and a 14-item student self-assessment on a Likert scale from 1 to 5. (Veloso Sérgio Geraldo et al. (2019))
