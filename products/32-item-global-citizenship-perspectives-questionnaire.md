## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **32-item global citizenship perspectives questionnaire adapted from Morais and Ogden (2011) and Roberts and Wilson (2016)**: A questionnaire 'adapted from the validated questionnaire from Morais and Ogden (2011) and Roberts and Wilson (2016)' consisting of 32 items with modifications such as country names and political policies. Items cover demographic features, social responsibility (GJD, AE, GIPR), global competence (SA, IC, GK), and global civic engagement (ICO, PV, GCA), all rated on a 5-point Likert scale from 1 (totally disagree) to 5 (totally agree). ## Description
A questionnaire 'adapted from the validated questionnaire from Morais and Ogden (2011) and Roberts and Wilson (2016)' consisting of 32 items with modifications such as country names and political policies. Items cover demographic features, social responsibility (GJD, AE, GIPR), global competence (SA, IC, GK), and global civic engagement (ICO, PV, GCA), all rated on a 5-point Likert scale from 1 (totally disagree) to 5 (totally agree).

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Pilot testing before distribution to allow adjustments; PLS-SEM analysis for validation
#### Constraints
- Some items required modification for the Vietnamese context, and several indicators were removed during validation

### Target Learners
- higher education students

### Target Learning Goals
- measuring student perspectives toward global citizenship
