# America's Teacher Corps (ATC)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A proposed federal programme to recognize highly effective K–12 public school teachers and provide eligible teachers, especially those in high-poverty Title I schools, with recognition, salary supplements, and a portable credential.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **America's Teacher Corps (ATC) program element**: America's Teacher Corps is a proposed federal program element for recognizing and rewarding teaching excellence. Membership would be open to "Highly effective K-12 public school teachers" documented through district or state evaluation systems complying with federal standards. Members would receive visible recognition, and those serving in high-poverty Title I schools would receive a salary supplement and a portable credential. The program is proposed, not implemented or evaluated, in this publication. (Steven Glazerman et al. (2010))
