## Description
An interactive astronomy education website created under a Space Telescope Science Institute education and public-outreach grant, using animations and simulations to teach students about the formation of the periodic-table elements.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Origin of the Elements interactive website for students**: An interactive website created in 2006 under a Space Telescope Science Institute E/PO grant to give students "a clear understanding of the formation of the elements in the periodic table." Animations and simulations were outlined to match the scientific concepts, accompanying text was written, and a graphics designer rendered the animations. It was built in Adobe Flash and is hosted at a web-docent.org URL. (Vikram V Dwarkadas (2021))
