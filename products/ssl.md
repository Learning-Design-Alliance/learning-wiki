# SSL

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
SSL is a structured early-literacy intervention programme whose developers provide lessons using modelling, guided practice, progress monitoring, and reteaching.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Model-lead-test lesson format with progress monitoring and re-teaching**: Each SSL lesson sequences four to six activities and follows a model-lead-test format: "The teacher first models the target skill. Students replicate the example and practice the skill with assistance from the teacher." Teachers then monitor student progress and re-teach skills to students who do not fully master them, and lessons can be repeated to support mastery. Each lesson begins with a set of nursery rhymes before the sequenced instructional activities. (What Works Clearinghouse (2007))
