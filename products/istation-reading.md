## Description
Istation Reading is, in the article's words, "a computer-adaptive testing and supplemental reading program for students from prekindergarten through Grade 8." Based on the science of reading, it assesses phonemic awareness, reading comprehension, listening comprehension, letter knowledge, alphabetic decoding, fluency, spelling, and vocabulary, adapting question difficulty to student responses. After assessment, students are routed into an online curriculum matched to their performance, and teachers receive reports identifying strengths and areas for improvement.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires students to complete the adaptive assessment, after which they are routed into the online curriculum based on performance.
#### Constraints
- The article describes it as supplemental: Istation recommends 30–40 minutes of supplemental curriculum use per week.

### Target Learners
- Students from prekindergarten through Grade 8, including struggling readers
