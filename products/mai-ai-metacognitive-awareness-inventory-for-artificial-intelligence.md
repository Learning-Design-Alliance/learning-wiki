# MAI-AI: Metacognitive Awareness Inventory for Artificial Intelligence

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 29-item AI-centered Likert self-report instrument developed by Pau Benazet i Montobbio et al. to measure students' metacognitive awareness of AI-assisted learning across six constructs.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MAI-AI: Metacognitive Awareness Inventory for Artificial Intelligence**: The MAI-AI is an AI-centered adaptation of the shortened Metacognitive Awareness Inventory and the MSLQ, used to assess students' metacognitive awareness of AI-assisted learning. As the article states, "the MAI-AI questionnaire consists of 29 questions (28 in post), divided into 6 constructs": Attitudes towards AI, AI Engagement, AI Literacy, Metacognitive Knowledge of Cognition, Metacognitive Regulation of Cognition, and Autonomy & Control. All questions use a five-point Likert scale with subscale averages as scores ranging from 1 to 5. It was administered at pre-test, post-test, and five-week follow-up. (Pau Benazet i Montobbio et al. (2026))
