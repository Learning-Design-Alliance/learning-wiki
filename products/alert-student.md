# ALERT STUDENT

## Description
ALERT STUDENT is an e-learning platform for creating, distributing, and studying flashcard-based learning objects with spaced-repetition sessions and test-enhanced learning activities.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ALERT STUDENT platform for learning objects, spaced repetition and test-enhanced learning**: ALERT STUDENT is an e-learning platform that "allows the creation and distribution of LOs named flashcards", which are "self-contained information chunks with related OEQs". Educators assemble flashcards into notebooks, the units in which spaced-repetition study sessions and quiz tasks are performed. The study mode presents flashcards with note taking, highlighting, and a study priority cue based on personal recall accuracy; the quiz mode presents open-ended questions as cues and has students grade recall accuracy on a 4-point likert scale, with flashcards graded 0 shown again at session end. (Taveira-Gomes T et al. (2015))
