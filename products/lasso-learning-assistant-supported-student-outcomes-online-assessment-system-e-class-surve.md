## Description
These are online physics assessment products associated with the PER community that administer, score, analyze, visualize, and support comparison of research-based assessment data.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Free online RBA platforms (LASSO, E-CLASS, PLIC, PhysPort DataExplorer) that automate administering, scoring, and analyzing assessments**: Free online platforms host and administer a wide array of physics RBAs, automating the administering, scoring, and analyzing process. The article states these platforms "can automate the scoring process altogether, providing instructors full student responses and scored responses", generate reports with visualizations and summary statistics, standardize data formats for comparison, and aggregate anonymized datasets to support large-scale, multi-institution PER investigations. (Ben Van Dusen et al. (2020))
