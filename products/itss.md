## Description
ITSS is a web-based intelligent tutoring system for grades K–8 that teaches students to recognize and use factual-text structures through animated modeling, self-paced practice, and immediate feedback.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Intelligent Tutoring for the Structure Strategy (ITSS): web-based text-structure tutoring program**: ITSS is a web-based reading comprehension program that "teaches students to use the organization of nonfiction texts to identify and remember important information." Students learn text structures including "comparison, problem and solution, cause and effect, sequence, and description" and use signaling words to identify main ideas and monitor comprehension. Students "work individually with an animated tutor that models strategies, provides practice and feedback," progressing at their own pace through lessons used as a partial substitute for regular language arts instruction for 30 to 45 minutes per week. (Evidence for ESSA (2021))

# ITSS: web-based intelligent tutoring system for the structure strategy

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (6 for, 2 mixed) · 6 studies (4 causal, 2 quant-synthesis), `q2`–`q3` · 5 of 6 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
ITSS is a supplemental web-based program for students in grades K-8 that teaches students to follow the logical structure of factual text and use text structure to improve understanding and recall. "ITSS highlights five main text structures that are used to (1) make comparisons; (2) present problems and solutions; (3) link causes and effects; (4) present sequences; and (5) describe things, people, creatures, places, or events." An animated Intelligent Tutor models each structure, and students practice with exercises providing immediate feedback, at their own pace, with roughly 12 lessons per text structure.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students access the program on a personal computer with earphones; teachers and administrators must complete professional development (a 2-day session for teachers, 1-day for administrators) plus 4 to 6 days of in-school coaching.
#### Constraints
- The evidence base covers grades 4, 5, and 7 in suburban and rural districts; effects are not known for other adolescent literacy outcome domains.

### Target Learners
- Students in grades K-8; studies included grades 4, 5, and 7 in suburban and rural districts

### Target Learning Goals
- Comprehension of factual (nonfiction) text using text structure
