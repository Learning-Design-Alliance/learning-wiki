## Description
Catch Up Numeracy is a structured one-to-one numeracy catch-up intervention developed for primary pupils struggling with numeracy and delivered by trained teaching assistants under school coordination.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Catch Up Numeracy one-to-one intervention with ten-component diagnostic targeting**: Catch Up Numeracy is a one-to-one intervention for primary pupils struggling with numeracy, delivered by trained teaching assistants in two 15-minute sessions per week. It "breaks numeracy down into ten components, assesses children's ability on each, and targets subsequent instruction so that the tutor always addresses the exact area of weakness." Components include counting verbally, counting objects, word problems and estimation. Delivery follows four stages: assessments for learning, identifying an appropriate focus, individual sessions (review and introduction, numeracy activity, link recording) and ongoing monitoring. TAs receive detailed session plans and three half-day training sessions. (National Foundation for Educational Research (NFER) (2014))
