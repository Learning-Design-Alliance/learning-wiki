## Description
Uceph is a digital orthodontic-training platform from Chengdu, China that provides scored landmarking practice and visual correction feedback on lateral radiographs for preclinical students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Uceph digital cephalometric training software with scored landmarking and visual error correction**: A digital cephalometric training platform (Uceph, Chengdu, China) used for both assessment and practice in preclinical orthodontic education. The software "included preloaded lateral radiographs with predefined “correct positions” for various landmark points" and assigned scores of 0–10 per landmark based on distance from the correct position. Its training mode visually indicates correct landmark positions with a circle, letting students adjust until close to the correct answer, and it generates total scores before and after training. (Lin Jiayu et al. (2025))
