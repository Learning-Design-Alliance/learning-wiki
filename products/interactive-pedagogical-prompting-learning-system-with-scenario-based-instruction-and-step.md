# Interactive pedagogical-prompting learning system with scenario-based instruction and step-by-step prompt builder

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), `q2` · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A JavaScript/React client-server learning system that uses scenario-based comics, a structured six-step prompt builder, GPT-4o validation, feedback, and sample solutions to train undergraduate CS students to write pedagogical prompts for AI help-seeking.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Pedagogical prompting: adapting the INSPIRE model and effective tutoring behaviours to steer GenAI as a Socratic writing tutor**: Pedagogical prompting is the article's method for instructing GenAI to behave in a pedagogically informed way: "Pedagogical prompting refers to a prompting technique used to instruct GenAI to respond to end-users (students) in a pedagogically informed manner, imitating effective tutor characteristics such as those outlined in the INSPIRE model". The INSPIRE model identifies seven tutor traits (Intelligent, Nurturant, Socratic, Progressive, Indirect, Reflective, Encouraging), originally from human tutoring. The study extends it to GenAI via expert prompting, constraints, few-shot and chain-of-thought prompting in planning/drafting, and metacognitive prompting in review, so EWA gives guiding questions rather than full drafts. (Febriantoro et al. (2025))
