## Description
A Chicago Public Schools framework that coordinates college-preparatory and career-readiness activities across grades 6–12 through strengthened CTE and districtwide planning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Career-connected learning (CCL) as a districtwide framework unifying college prep and career readiness**: The report describes CPS's vision and enactment of "career-connected learning" (CCL), a framework initiated in 2017, which aims to unify college preparatory and career readiness activities districtwide. The framework operates through two strategies: strengthening existing high school CTE programs and establishing a districtwide CCL framework for students from grades 6 through 12. Chicago is presented as one of many districts working to implement a modern CCL framework. (Illinois Workforce et al. (2024))
