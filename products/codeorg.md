- **Code.org 20-hour introduction course as the online algorithm visualization environment**: The OAV environment used in the study was provided by code.org, a non-profit organization launched in 2013 that "aims to teach computer programming and computer science through visualization" online. The researcher used its "20 hour introduction course", which students completed individually during course hours, and the environment "allows the instructor to observe his or her students' progress in real-time". It was chosen because it is online, free, and provides a user-friendly interface. (Fatih Saltan (2017))
