## Description
Code.org is a nonprofit-developed online platform that teaches computer science and programming through interactive visual activities and courses.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Code.org 20-hour introduction course as the online algorithm visualization environment**: The OAV environment used in the study was provided by code.org, a non-profit organization launched in 2013 that "aims to teach computer programming and computer science through visualization" online. The researcher used its "20 hour introduction course", which students completed individually during course hours, and the environment "allows the instructor to observe his or her students' progress in real-time". It was chosen because it is online, free, and provides a user-friendly interface. (Fatih Saltan (2017))
- **Code.org Computer Science Fundamentals Course B block-based curriculum**: Code.org is a block-based programming platform whose drag-and-drop interface, according to the article, reduces "the cognitive load associated with coding syntax and making programming more accessible". In this study, 20 third-grade students completed Course B from the Computer Science Fundamentals curriculum over 18 lessons in three weeks, covering algorithms, conditions, sequence, nested loops, and loops, mixing unplugged activities with puzzles. The platform offers game-based challenges, real-time program execution, teacher dashboards, lesson plans, and alignment with educational standards. (Wan Chong Choi et al. (2022))
