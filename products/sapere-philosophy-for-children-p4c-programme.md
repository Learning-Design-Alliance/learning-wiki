# SAPERE Philosophy for Children (P4C) programme

## Description
A school-based philosophical-enquiry programme run by SAPERE that trains and supports teachers to facilitate whole-class dialogues on philosophical issues.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SAPERE Philosophy for Children (P4C) programme with two-day Level 1 teacher training**: P4C as promoted by SAPERE is a whole-class approach in which pupils participate in group dialogues on philosophical issues prompted by a stimulus such as a story or video, based around concepts like truth, fairness or bullying. It is "a template to practise and organise a classroom session for philosophical enquiry" with no specified materials that must be used. Delivery in the trial involved two days of SAPERE Level 1 training for all staff, ongoing in-school support (nine support days available over two years), and cost £3,940 per school per year, roughly £16 per pupil for a school of 240 pupils. (Stephen Gorard et al. (2015))
