PSRBVBQ¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes

Description¶

The PSRBVBQ is a situational-judgment questionnaire developed by Bush and Harrison that uses bullying vignettes to assess perceived severity and teachers' likelihood of responding to bullies and victims.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • The three PSRBVBQ scales show good internal consistency, with Cronbach's alphas of .732 (perceived severity), .841 (response to bully) and .897 (response to victim) [+W]
  • The 18-item PSRBVBQ yields a balanced three-factor structure (perceived severity, likelihood of responding to the bully, likelihood of responding to the victim) in Romanian middle school teachers [+W]
  • Teachers' perceived severity of bullying predicts their likelihood of reacting to incidents of aggression [+W]
  • More teachers rate verbal aggression as serious (38.6%) than rate relational aggression as very serious (23.8%) [+W]
