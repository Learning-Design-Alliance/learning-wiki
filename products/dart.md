## Description
DART is a quantitative debriefing-assessment tool developed by Baliga Kaushik et al. for simulation faculty, debriefers, and peer coaches to record and compare conversational contributions during healthcare-simulation debriefings.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Quantitative tally-based assessment as a complement to subjective Likert-scale debriefing tools**: The article proposes a complementary quantitative approach to debriefing assessment, arguing that existing tools like DASH and OSAD are "relatively time consuming and use subjective scales" and are prone to response biases such as socially desirable or extreme responding. The DART replaces subjective scales with cumulative counts of questions, statements and trainee responses, avoiding response bias and reducing complexity, and is intended to provide a conversational substrate for peer feedback and coaching. (Baliga Kaushik et al. (2022))
