# Comprehensive Competency-Based Framework for RT education

## Description
A framework proposed by Skariah Tisha Ann et al. for organizing respiratory-therapy competencies by gap severity and integrating simulation-based training for students and newly employed respiratory therapists.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Comprehensive Competency-Based Framework for respiratory therapy with gap-tiered, simulation-integrated structure**: The article proposes a Comprehensive Competency-Based Framework for RT education that "ensures no competency is overlooked, making the framework all-inclusive and structured for progressive learning." It organizes competencies by gap severity (critical, high, medium, low from the survey's gap analysis), integrates simulation-based training strategies, and is intended to be globally adaptable and aligned with international accreditation. Fig. 2 illustrates the proposed framework linking training gaps, simulation use, and competency outcomes. (Skariah Tisha Ann et al. (2025))
