25-item multiple-choice achievement test for the unit 'electricity in our lives'¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A 25-item four-option multiple-choice achievement test developed for assessing 7th-grade students' knowledge of the electricity-in-our-lives unit, used as a pre-test, post-test, and retention test in the Aktas et al. study.

Components¶

  • 25-item multiple-choice achievement test for the unit 'electricity in our lives' (KR-20 = 0.797): A multiple-choice achievement test with four options per item, used as pre-test, post-test, and retention test in the study. An initial 30-question version was administered to 135 8th-grade students for item analysis; after computing item difficulty and discrimination indices, "the researchers decided to remove five questions from the achievement test". The finalized 25-question test reached a KR-20 reliability of 0.797, which the authors state means "the test served its purpose". (Aktas et al. (2016))
