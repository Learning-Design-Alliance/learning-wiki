## Description
A historical scholastic-ranking assessment formula devised by Cummins that weights pupils' A–D marks from school records to estimate and classify scholastic standing.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The Scholastic Quotient (S. Q.), a weighted-mark formula for ranking pupils' scholarship**: A formula devised by the author to rank pupils from high school record cards, weighting the counts of A, B, C and D marks as 3A+2B+C−D over A+B+C+D to yield a 'Scholastic Quotient'. The article states the quotient "has a range of from minus 1 to plus 3" and that the limits hold whether a pupil has one mark or a thousand and one. It was used to classify the upper quartile as 'Bright' and the lower quartile as 'Slow'. (Cummins (1917))
