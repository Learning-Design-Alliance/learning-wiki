> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
AccesSciencesDV is a collaborative accessible website and e-learning platform created by the project team to provide adapted academic science resources and accessibility practices for BVI students and their teachers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **AccesSciencesDV accessible website collecting adapted academic resources and good practices for BVI science education**: AccesSciencesDV (accessciencesdv.fr) is the article's project to nationalize and generalize its support approach. The article states "This accessible website will collect adapted resources in academic topics as: Mathematics, Biology, Physics" and provide the good practices for making science more accessible. Its collaborative e-learning platform gives teachers and BVI students access to methods for making science more accessible, addressing the documented scarcity of adapted materials and teacher training. (L. Petitdemange et al. (2025))
