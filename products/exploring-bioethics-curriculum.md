# Exploring Bioethics curriculum

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A life-sciences bioethics curriculum developed by the Educational Development Center for secondary students, using ethical-issue discussions and distinctions among scientific, legal, and ethical questions to develop moral reasoning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Exploring Bioethics curriculum as a model for distinguishing scientific, legal, and ethical questions**: The Exploring Bioethics curriculum (Educational Development Center, 2009), presented by Mildred Solomon, aims for students to "develop the ability to c ritically reason in the moral domain" and to participate in public policies about ethical issues in the life sciences. The report describes its approach of distinguishing between scientific, legal, and ethical questions as a model for curricula addressing socioscientific issues. An evaluation study presented by Jeanne Chowning found students motivated through discussion of ethical issues and engaging in critical thinking and analysis. (Peter Garik et al. (2014))
