## Description
GoSaveThem is an immersive virtual-reality application for practicing primary triage and decision-making in mass-casualty incident scenarios; its maker is not identified in the source.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **GoSaveThem VR mass casualty incident triage simulation**: GoSaveThem is a VR software with mass casualty incident scenarios, selected because it was "the only known available application adjusted to Swedish conditions." Participants use a fully immersive HTC VIVE head-mounted display to walk through a post-explosion subway station scene with 22 victim avatars, performing primary triage. Handheld controllers with vibrations simulate assessing a pulse, and breathing sounds play near an avatar's chest, enabling realistic respiratory rate assessments and actions like applying tourniquets and assigning triage colors. (Heldring Sara et al. (2025))
