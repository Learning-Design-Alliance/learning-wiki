## Description
A browser-based framework developed by Lockhart et al. for designing, generating, embedding, and customizing lightweight interactive educational simulations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The MicroSims framework positions educational simulations at the intersection of simplicity, accessibility, and AI generation**: MicroSims are defined as lightweight, standalone interactive simulations that run in standard web browsers for pedagogical applications, occupying the intersection of three characteristics: simplicity, accessibility, and AI generation. The framework combines "standardized design patterns that enable AI-assisted generation", iframe-based universal embedding with sandboxed security, and transparent, modifiable code supporting customization and pedagogical transparency. It spans design principles, technical architecture, metadata standards, and development workflows for AI-generated educational content. (Lockhart et al. (2025))
- **Open collection of over 100 example MicroSims demonstrating the framework across subject areas**: The MicroSims project maintains an open web collection of example simulations that serves as the framework's demonstration artifact. The paper states that "Acollectionofover100MicroSimexamplesdemonstratingtheseprinciplesacrossdiversesubject areas is available athttps://dmccreary.github.io/microsims/[7]." The collection spans diverse subject areas, validates the design framework through worked examples, and supports educator reuse and modification. (Lockhart et al. (2025))
