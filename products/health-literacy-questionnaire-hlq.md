# Health Literacy Questionnaire (HLQ)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The HLQ is a multidimensional self-report health-literacy assessment developed by Osborne and colleagues that measures nine health-literacy domains.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Health Literacy Questionnaire (HLQ) as a multidimensional 9-section measure of preservice teachers’ health literacy**: The HLQ is the primary instrument for measuring preservice teachers’ HL objectively, administered in week 1 and again at the conclusion of the 8-week unit, and reused as a pretest in the controlled trial. It is a multidimensional tool: "The selected HLQ assesses HL as a multidimensional concept categorized in 9 sections rather than producing a single score, hence offering a broad and valid understanding of a person’s HL capabilities." The 9 sections have been deemed valid for young adults and reliable, with composite reliability ranging from 0.8 to 0.9. Scores will be analyzed in SPSS using robust ANOVA with the Welch method. (Kealy-Ashby C et al. (2025))
