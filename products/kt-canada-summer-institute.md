# KT Canada Summer Institute

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An annual KT Canada training institute run by KT Canada that combines didactic and active learning, small-group project work, mentor exposure, and facilitated poster presentations for graduate trainees and postdoctoral fellows.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **KT Canada Summer Institute with small-group project work, facilitated poster sessions, and mentor exposure**: An annual event for graduate students focused on a different theme each year addressing one or more core KT competencies, such as developing KT interventions and targeting them to stakeholder groups. Institutes "include didactic and active learning with small group work focused on an assigned KT project and exposure to mentors" and facilitated poster sessions where trainees present research in progress. Three institutes were held with a total of 90 trainees (30 in 2009, 29 in 2010, 31 in 2011). (Straus Sharon E et al. (2011))
