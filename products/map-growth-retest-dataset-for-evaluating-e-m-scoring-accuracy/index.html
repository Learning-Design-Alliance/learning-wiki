MAP Growth retest dataset for evaluating E-M scoring accuracy¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (2 associational), q2–q3 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A research dataset assembled from archived NWEA MAP Growth mathematics and reading RIT-score records for students who completed closely timed retests, used to evaluate effort-moderated scoring accuracy.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • E-M scoring partially accounts for observed RIT score differences between disengaged first tests and engaged retests [+W]
  • Students who rapid-guess less on a MAP Growth retest tend to see score gains, while those who rapid-guess more tend to see declines [+M]
  • Seriously disengaged initial testers who stopped rapid-guessing on retest showed large RIT gains, averaging about 6 points in Reading and 12 in Math [+M]
  • Rapid guesses on MAP Growth reflect a lack of engagement rather than a lack of knowledge [+M]
  • Test-taking disengagement frequently differs between a MAP Growth initial test and a same-day retest [+M]
