Description¶

Buffalo Promise Neighborhood is a local Promise Neighborhoods initiative serving a high-poverty Buffalo community through coordinated education, early-childhood, and family-support programming.

Components¶

  • Buffalo Promise Neighborhood program element: The Buffalo Promise Neighborhood is a Promise Neighborhoods initiative site in Buffalo, New York, profiled by Mathematica Policy Research for the Promise Neighborhoods Institute at PolicyLink as part of the Promise Neighborhoods Case Studies (time frame 2014-2015). The profile describes it as serving "one of Buffalo's poorest communities," documenting the population and economic context in which the neighborhood's education, early childhood, and family-support programming operates. (Lara Hulsey (2015))
