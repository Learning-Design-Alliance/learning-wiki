## Description
A modular taxonomy created by Weihua Xiao et al. that organizes LLM-aided RTL generation, RTL verification, and hardware-security resources into topics, subtopics, and units for course and repository organization.

## Components

- **GUIDE taxonomy of GenAI digital design topics: LLM-aided RTL generation, RTL verification, and hardware security**: GUIDE organizes its repository into a modular taxonomy of topics, subtopics, and units. The article states "GUIDE has a modular structure (Table I) organized into topics, subtopics, and units." Its three topics are LLM-aided RTL generation (subtopics: generation from natural language, finetuned LLMs), LLM-aided RTL verification (subtopics: simulation-based and formal verification), and LLM-aided hardware security (subtopics: attacks and defenses). Table I lists representative units under each, such as AutoChip, VeriThoughts, Hybrid-NL2SVA, GHOST, and TrojanLoC. (Weihua Xiao et al. (2026))
