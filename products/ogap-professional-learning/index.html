OGAP Professional Learning¶

Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

OGAP Professional Learning is a mathematics professional-development programme run by the Ongoing Assessment Project (OGAP) for educators across developmental reasoning strands.

Components¶

  • OGAP strand-based professional learning structure: cohorts of about 24 educators, four onsite days, and two follow-up coaching days: OGAP professional learning is organized by Strand, each aligned to a developmental progression in mathematics: Additive Reasoning (K–2), Multiplicative Reasoning (3–5), Fractional Reasoning (3–6), and Proportional Reasoning (6–8). Each strand is delivered to educator cohorts, often around 24 participants, through four onsite professional learning days, with districts able to space sessions several weeks apart. Two onsite follow-up coaching days, often during PLC time, "support transfer into classroom practice." Multiple strands may run simultaneously under the same structure. (OGAP Math program description (2024))
