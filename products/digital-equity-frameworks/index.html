Digital Equity Frameworks¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A framework for planning and guiding digital-equity work in schools and districts through the principles of inclusive innovation, sustainability, and continuous improvement.

Components¶

  • Three guiding principles of the Digital Equity Frameworks: Inclusive Innovation, Sustainability, Continuous Improvement: The frameworks are animated by three guiding principles that form the belief system underlying domain actions. A belief in Inclusive Innovation holds that digital equity work must be done in collaboration with communities, including the most impacted, underserved, and underrepresented, grounding efforts in targeted universalism. Sustainability recognizes that digital equity efforts are society-building and mission critical, requiring long-term planning. Continuous Improvement accepts that schools and districts are always striving to achieve more equitable outcomes efficiently and effectively. (A Framework for Digital Equity)
