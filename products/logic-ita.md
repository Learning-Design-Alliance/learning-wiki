## Description
Logic-ITA is a web-based intelligent teaching assistant developed for supporting undergraduate students' formal-logic proof construction through practice, feedback, and teacher monitoring.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The Intelligent Teaching Assistant as an intermediary between teacher and students, with dual visibility of progress and mistakes**: The article frames the Logic-ITA as an intermediary between teacher and students that serves both parties at once: "on one hand, it provides students with an environment to practice formal proofs with feedback and on the other hand it allows teachers to monitor the class's progress and mistakes." This dual-visibility arrangement lets one system simultaneously support student practice and teacher oversight of a large class. (Kalina Yacef (2005))
