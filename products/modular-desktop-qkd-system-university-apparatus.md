# Modular desktop QKD system "University Apparatus"

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A LabVIEW- and FPGA-controlled desktop quantum-key-distribution apparatus developed by A.K. Fedorov et al. for undergraduate physics education, engineering training, and research.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Modular desktop QKD system "University Apparatus" for education and research**: The article presents a desktop QKD system consisting of Alice and Bob units, each with a row of 8 red add-on electronic cards with SMA connectors, a motherboard providing commutation with an NI board in an ordinary PC, and an optic table for fiber optic components. It "has a module structure and can be modiﬁed easily" and is proposed "specialized for student education purposes and scientiﬁc research". (A.K. Fedorov et al. (2017))
- **Released Arduino and Python control programs with full documentation on GitHub**: The workshop's experimental sequence is automated with Arduino microcontrollers and Python programs that synchronize the quantum and classical channels and generate each bit consistently. The article states: "The sequence was executed using Arduino and Python programs. These are fully documented and available on Github." The release is intended to facilitate educators in conducting the workshop, and the authors believe the simplified implementation "is an easily deployable and engaging QKD demonstration that can be realized by a wide range of educational institutions." (Adrian Nugraha Utama et al. (2020))
