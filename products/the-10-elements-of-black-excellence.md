# The 10 Elements of Black Excellence as an organizing design pattern

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The brief organizes the Center's foundation around 10 Elements of Black Excellence: Empowering Genius, Overcoming Systemic Barriers, Embracing Black Identity, Liberating Greatness, Elevating Intergenerational Wisdom, Promoting Advocacy and Awareness, Centering Health and Well-Being, Uplifting the Community, Encouraging Authenticity, and Cultivating Beauty and Joy. Each element emerged from recurring themes in community listening sessions and is elaborated with community quotes, a collective definition, Center aims, and practical approaches. The brief notes "These elements were identified and named based on the expressed envisioning of representative voices of LBUSD's Black Community" and that no element is ordered above another.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each element is discussed with direct community quotes, a collective definition, plausible Center aims, and practical approaches
#### Constraints
- Elements are not displayed in any particular order; each shares equal importance per the brief

### Target Learners
- Black students
- Black families and caregivers
- teachers
- community members

### Learning Goals
- Belonging, cultural pride, advocacy, well-being, and academic excellence
