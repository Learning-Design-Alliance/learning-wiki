## Description
IMPDET is an international online doctoral study programme in educational technology hosted in Finland and run for doctoral students, particularly from emerging and transitional countries.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **IMPDET online doctoral study program**: IMPDET (International Multidisciplinary PhD Studies in Educational Technology) is an international online doctoral study program hosted in Finland since 2004, originally designed for applicants from emerging and transitional countries. Its pedagogical components include independent research experience with two to four supervisors, a mixed-model teaching approach (online, campus, or mixed), and course work of 30 or 50 ECTS credits. The authors report that "A total of 93 students have been admitted to the program by end of 2021, 32 (34%) of whom graduated and 17 (18%) officially quit their studies between 2004 and 2021." (Jarkko Suhonen · Erkki Sutinen (2022))
