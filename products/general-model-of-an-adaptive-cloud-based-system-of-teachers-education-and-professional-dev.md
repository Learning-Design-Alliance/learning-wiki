# General model of an adaptive cloud-based system of teachers' education and professional development

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A prototype, content-invariant structural framework proposed by Maiia Marienko et al. for configuring corporate, public, communication, and scientific-information cloud services to support pre-service and in-service teacher education and professional development.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Adaptive cloud-based learning system for teacher education**: The article defines an adaptive cloud-based learning system as "the cloud-based system that has the property to be adjusted automatically by its parameters to the different individual characteristics and educational needs of the learning process participants". It lists key characteristics including virtualized or hybrid ICT infrastructure, personalization of services, openness to modification, accessibility, flexible evaluation algorithms, personalized assistance, real-time automatic adjustment, and systematic structure. Hybrid cloud solutions are presented as the platform for integrating adaptive learning with AR. (Maiia V. Marienko et al. (2020))
