# Multipurpose Unit Early Classroom Intervention Program (MUECIP)

## Description
MUECIP is a center-based 13-week early childhood intervention programme developed by the researchers to support the cognitive, psychomotor, language, personal-social, and self-care development of 4–5-year-old children at developmental risk.
