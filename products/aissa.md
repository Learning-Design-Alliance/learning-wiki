# AISSA

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
AISSA is a web-based platform developed by Alvaro Becerra et al. that extracts presentation-slide features and uses learning analytics and an LLM to generate rubric-based scores and actionable feedback for students, teachers, and administrators.
