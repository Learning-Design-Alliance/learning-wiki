# RAFT (Rules of the Road Adaptive Fleet Training) and RoR ship-driving simulation trainers

## Description
Two versions of a ship-driving simulation trainer developed for nautical education that present collision-risk scenarios and assess learners' COLREGs-based navigation reports and recommended actions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **RAFT and RoR ship-driving simulation trainers**: RAFT (Rules of the Road Adaptive Fleet Training) and RoR are two versions of a ship-driving simulation trainer for the nautical rules of the road, sharing identical instructional content, user interface, and scenario-generation framework. Students observe simulated navigation scenarios, identify the target vessel presenting collision risk, and complete a Captain's Report with target location, range, relative bearing, bearing drift, target angle, CPA measures, the applicable rule, and a recommended action. RAFT adapts difficulty continuously and gives immediate feedback; RoR uses three fixed difficulty levels and provides feedback only at the end of a quiz. (Amit Dutta (2026))
