# SimX fully immersive semi-automated VR trauma training scenario

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A commercially available immersive virtual-reality trauma-training scenario developed by SimX Inc. for practicing ATLS-based assessment and management with a virtual polytrauma patient on Meta Quest 2.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Fully immersive semi-automated VR trauma training scenario (SimX on Meta Quest 2)**: A commercially available, fully immersive semi-automated VR trauma training scenario (by SimX Inc.) run on a Meta Quest 2 headset and controllers, applying ATLS principles to a polytraumatized virtual patient. Participants had "45 min to complete the case or retry" and the scenario "could be repeated as often as desired". A study investigator moderated the scenario, giving prerecorded verbal responses and initiating automated physiological responses. (Birrenbach T et al. (2024))

### Claims
- [A fully immersive VR trauma simulation is feasible to deploy as an adjunct preparation for the ATLS course, with all completing participants achieving correct patient stabilisation.](../claims/vr-trauma-simulation-feasible-atls-adjunct.md) [+M]
- [The VR trauma simulation is well accepted by medical students, with good usability, moderate immersion, balanced workload, and high satisfaction and technology acceptance.](../claims/vr-trauma-simulation-well-accepted.md) [+M]
