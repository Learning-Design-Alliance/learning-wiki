# 4Rs+MTP

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
4Rs+MTP is an integrated elementary literacy, conflict-resolution, and social-emotional learning programme supplemented by MyTeachingPartner professional development and video-based teacher coaching.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MyTeachingPartner (MTP) video-based coaching program for 4Rs implementation**: MTP is a standardized, video-based coaching program supporting teachers using the 4Rs curriculum. Every two weeks teachers videotape their instruction; the coach edits the tape into short segments focused on a specific CLASS dimension of interaction, posts them with written "prompts" to a private website, and then meets the teacher for an in-person 30-minute conference to problem-solve and plan future lessons. Teachers complete 8 coaching cycles per year. (Downer et al. (2013))
- **4Rs (Reading, Writing, Respect, and Resolution) social-emotional and literacy curriculum**: The 4Rs program is an integrated SEL and literacy curriculum for grades 3-5 with two primary components: a comprehensive 7-unit, 21 lesson, literacy-based curriculum in conflict resolution and social-emotional learning, and 25 hours of teacher training plus ongoing coaching. Each unit begins with a read-aloud of a children's book, followed by "Book Talk" discussion, writing, and role-playing, then "Applied Learning" lessons taught weekly for 30-45 minutes throughout the academic year. (Downer et al. (2013))
