## Description
ArthroSim is a high-fidelity virtual-reality knee arthroscopy simulator with finite-element anatomical modeling and dual-device haptic feedback, made by TolTech Touch of Life Technologies.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ArthroSim high-fidelity VR knee arthroscopy simulator with haptic feedback and a standardized novice curriculum**: ArthroSim (TolTech Touch of Life Technologies) is a high-fidelity VR system the article describes as integrating "3 key features": high-resolution anatomical imaging from the Visible Human Project, real-time graphical adjustments responding to joint flexion, extension, and varus/valgus stress via finite element modeling, and high-resolution haptic feedback with two haptic devices allowing simultaneous camera and probe operation. In the study, ArthroSim served both as a 90-minute standardized novice training platform (camera navigation and probing exercises) and as the assessment device for all skill evaluation. (Kutsuna Tatsuhiko et al. (2025))
