# Self-administered Minimal Intervention Dentistry (MID) knowledge, skills, and attitudes instrument

## Description
A 17-item five-point Likert self-report instrument developed by de Moura Regina Cardoso et al. to assess dentists' knowledge, skills, and attitudes concerning the recognition, reduction, regeneration, and repair concepts of Minimal Intervention Dentistry.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **A self-administered instrument for assessing dentists' knowledge, skills, and attitudes towards Minimal Intervention Dentistry**: The article develops a self-administered instrument comprising 17 structured items on a five-point Likert scale assessing dentists' knowledge, skills, and attitudes related to MID, with total scores ranging from 17 to 85. Items are organized into thematic areas aligned with the four core MID concepts of recognition, reduction, regeneration, and repair. The instrument was reviewed by twelve MID experts, evaluated in two focus groups, and pre-tested with 30 dentists, achieving an overall Content Validity Coefficient of 0.93. (de Moura Regina Cardoso et al. (2026))
