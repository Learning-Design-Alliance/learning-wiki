## Description
MORE@Home is a researcher-developed mobile literacy app by James S. Kim and colleagues that provides personalized parent–child reading activities, discussion questions, gamification, and text-message support.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MORE@Home literacy app with personalized digital activities**: MORE@Home is a researcher-developed literacy app installed on parents' cell phones that paired each of 10 chosen print books with personalized reading activities. Activities were created at three levels of difficulty (low, medium, high) using children's MAP subtest scores, and included text-dependent and discussion questions, both literal and inferential, to stimulate parent–child conversation. Stage 2 added white hat gamification in which completed activities let students choose animals for a virtual zoo, plus text messaging supports for nonresponders. (James S. Kim et al. (2019))
