- **Augmented Reality Sandbox environment for distributed spatial sensemaking in group CSCL**: The editorial describes the Augmented Reality Sandbox, a novel environment for embodied CSCL experiences presented by Hod and Twersky. Unlike prior research that "has focused on AR Sandbox activity designs, outcome measures, and user's perceptions of different usability functions", this article probes deeply into the situated processes by which groups engage in CSCL activities, analyzing group interactions spanning material, social, and activity contexts.
