## Description
Amira Learning's Amira is an AI-powered personal reading tutor that assesses oral reading fluency, provides individualized reading practice and intervention, and reports diagnostic information to educators and families.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Amira AI reading tutor**: Amira is an AI tutor that uses 'advanced speech recognition and natural language processing technology' to listen to students read aloud, identify reading challenges, and deliver micro-interventions based on reading and neuroscience research. The team developed a logic model with research partner Instructure to describe its foundational research and theory of change, and has led three ESSA-aligned studies of the tool's impact on learning. (Mills et al. (2025))
- **Microintervention scaffolds delivered at word, phrase, and story levels during oral reading practice**: Coach delivers scaffolded support as responses to errors detected during oral reading, at three moments within a reading session: word level when a student is stuck on a word, phrase level when a student misreads words within a sentence, and story level as a wrap-up building comprehension at the end of the story. Each microintervention is described as a "scaffold" aligned to the Reading Rope, with dozens employed to build foundational reading skills in English and Spanish. (MAP Reading Fluency with Coach Evidence Base (2024))

# Amira — an AI-based personal reading tutor that assesses, tutors, and reports on oral reading

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Amira is "a personal artificial intelligent tutor program" that provides students with "an always available, infinitely patient, and highly-trained personal reading tutor". It assesses oral reading fluency, analyzes errors to pinpoint strengths and weaknesses, listens as a student reads aloud and intervenes when helpful, recommends appropriately challenging stories, and generates diagnostic reports for teachers and parents. It is browser-based and works on Chromebooks, PCs, Macs, iPads, and tablets without special software.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Devices should have at least 4 GB of RAM and a working microphone; headphones with a mic are encouraged in noisier classrooms.
- A current version of Chrome, Edge, Safari (required on iPads), or Firefox is recommended.
#### Constraints
- Amira operates as a closed, secure system that never accesses the open internet.

### Target Learners
- kindergarten and first grade students
- students in grades 1–4 who struggled with reading

### Target Learning Goals
- early reading outcomes
- oral reading fluency
