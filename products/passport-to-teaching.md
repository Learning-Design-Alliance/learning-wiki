# Passport to Teaching: ABCTE's examination-based certification approach for identifying competent teachers regardless of preparation route

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
The Passport to Teaching is an examination-based teacher certification approach devised by the American Board for Certification of Teacher Excellence (ABCTE). It "aims to identify people competent to teach, regardless of the individual’s method of preparation", offering an alternative to the varied state requirements regulating entry into teaching. This report evaluates it as part of Mathematica's five-year evaluation of ABCTE certification programs, gathering supervisor ratings of certified teachers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Candidates pass ABCTE examinations; certification is granted based on exam performance rather than a particular preparation route
#### Constraints
- Education researchers had not succeeded in isolating measures that cost-effectively and reliably predict teacher performance, the problem the approach addresses

### Target Learners
- Adults seeking entry into the teaching profession through an examination-based route

### Target Learning Goals
- Teacher certification and identification of people competent to teach
