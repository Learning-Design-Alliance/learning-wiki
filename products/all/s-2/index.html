  • Survey of Learning Behaviors - A self-report assessment used to measure students' self-regulation, including self-monitoring and knowledge-acquisition strategies, administered before and after instruction.
  • SUSSI questionnaire (Student Understanding of Science and Scientific Inquiry) - A dual-response Likert and open-ended questionnaire used to assess students' views of the nature of scientific knowledge and scientific inquiry across six aspects.
  • Sutkin's three-category framework of good clinical teachers (teacher, physician, human characteristics) - A framework developed by Sutkin et al.
  • SV-O questionnaire (Slovenian version of the Social Competence and Behaviour Evaluation questionnaire) - A Slovenian-normed 80-item teacher-rating instrument for evaluating young children's social competence, behavioural problems, emotional adaptation, peer and adult interaction, and general social adapt
  • SVLAT: Scientific Visualization Literacy Assessment Test - SVLAT is a standardized test developed by Patrick Phuoc Do et al.
  • Switch-on Reading - Switch-on Reading is a one-to-one, 10-week graded-book reading intervention developed for weak Year 7 readers and delivered by trained staff using a prescribed session protocol and running-record asse
  • Symbol Math task - A computer-based experimental assessment developed by Mielicki et al.
  • Symmetry Subjects Comprehension Test (SSCT) - A 28-question researcher-developed test created by Batdal Karaduman to measure undergraduate teacher candidates’ comprehension of symmetry concepts, administered as a pre-test and post-test.
  • SYMP Test - The SYMP Test is a group-administered, timed paper-and-pencil assessment created by Brankaer et al.
  • Symphony Math - Symphony Math is a web-browser and iPad technology intervention program that provides individualized mathematics practice for K–8 students, developed and distributed as the Symphony Math program.
  • Symptom Checklist-90 (SCL-90) - The SCL-90 is a 90-item multidimensional self-report instrument used to screen psychological symptoms across ten symptom dimensions, developed for clinical and research use.
  • Syntea - Syntea is IU International University of Applied Sciences' AI-based learning-assistant software for question answering, interactive tutoring, personalized feedback, progress tracking, and exam prepara
  • Synthesis Lab - Synthesis Lab is a web application that integrates with social annotation tools to help learners categorize peer annotations into conceptual building blocks and develop knowledge syntheses.
  • Systems Leadership for Middle Schools (SLMS) - A Modern Red Schoolhouse whole-school school-improvement programme that organizes middle-school staff into Action Teams and a Guiding Coalition, supported by facilitators, to build distributed leaders
  • Systems Review - Systems Review is an inquiry-based, systems-focused approach for teams working to improve a system, developed and facilitated by R15CC.
  • Systems Thinking Scale (20-item, Chinese validated version) - A culturally adapted 20-item self-report scale developed for assessing systems thinking among Chinese nursing personnel in clinical practice.
