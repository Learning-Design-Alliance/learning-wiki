# Test of Scientific Literacy Skills (TOSLS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
TOSLS is a 28-item multiple-choice assessment developed by Gormally and colleagues to measure scientific-literacy skills in undergraduate biology students using real-world contexts.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **TOSLS: a freely available 28-item multiple-choice test of scientific literacy skills for Gen Ed biology courses**: The Test of Scientific Literacy Skills is a multiple-choice assessment contextualized around real-world problems, designed to be quick to administer and score in large-enrollment courses. The article states that "The instrument consists of 28 multiple-choice questions that are contextualized around real-world problems," such as evaluating the reliability of an Internet site or determining what evidence would support a fitness product's effectiveness. It was developed through five semester-long pilot-and-revision cycles and is freely available with its answer key in the Supplemental Material. (Gormally C. et al. (2012))
- **Two-category, nine-skill taxonomy of measurable scientific literacy skills**: The TOSLS articulates scientific literacy as nine measurable skills in two categories: skills related to "recognizing and analyzing the use of methods of inquiry that lead to scientific knowledge" (identifying valid scientific arguments, evaluating source validity, evaluating use and misuse of scientific information, understanding research design) and skills related to organizing, analyzing, and interpreting quantitative data and scientific information (creating and reading graphs, quantitative problem solving, interpreting statistics, justifying inferences). Each skill is mapped to specific test questions and to documented student challenges and misconceptions. (Gormally C. et al. (2012))
