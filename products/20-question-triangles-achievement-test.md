# 20-question triangles achievement test

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A 20-item multiple-choice achievement test developed by Deniz Kaya et al. to measure eighth-grade students’ attainment of triangle-geometry objectives in pre-test and post-test settings.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **20-question triangles achievement test with KR-20 of .78**: The study's data collection tool was an "Achievement test about triangles" of 20 questions worth 5 points each, with a maximum score of 100. Content was drawn from the schoolbook and other references in line with the Mathematics Instruction Program's targets and behaviors; content and face validity drew on opinions of 3 mathematics teachers and 3 field educators. Piloted with 125 different 8th-grade students, its "KR-20 reliability coefficient of multiple-choice assessment instrument, which took its final form and have 20 questions was founded .78". (Deniz KAYA et al. (2013))
