# PhacoTrainer AI metric pipeline for cataract surgical video

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
A deep-learning video-analysis pipeline developed by Yeh Hsu-Hang et al. that detects surgical landmarks and computes instrument-motion and decentration metrics for assessing ophthalmology residents' cataract surgery technique.

### Claims

- [Pipeline-generated metrics strongly correlate with metrics computed from manually labeled positions on newly collected attending videos (Pearson r = 0.988, 0.957, 0.769)](../claims/phacotrainer-metric-validation-attending-videos.md) [+M]
- [AI-derived motion metrics (total path length, maximum velocity, covered area) for the pupil and phacoemulsification probe are significantly lower in attending than trainee cataract surgical videos](../claims/ai-motion-metrics-lower-in-attending-cataract-videos.md) [+M]
- [AI-generated metrics correlate negatively with overall OSACSS scores, including phacoemulsification decentration (r = −0.369) and eye decentration (r = −0.394)](../claims/ai-metrics-correlate-overall-osacss.md) [+M]
- [Attending surgeons demonstrate less phacoemulsification probe decentration and eye decentration than trainees, with no significant difference in zoom level change](../claims/ai-centration-metrics-differentiate-surgeon-experience.md) [+W]
- [Expert OSACSS ratings significantly differentiate attending from trainee cataract surgical videos (averages 91.3 vs 87.6)](../claims/osacss-ratings-differentiate-attending-trainee.md) [+W]
- [Tool- and landmark-specific AI metrics correlate significantly negatively with matched OSACSS subitems, with correlations ranging from −0.77 to −0.49](../claims/tool-specific-ai-metrics-correlate-osacss-subitems.md) [+W]
