## Description
SonoSim Ultrasound Training Solution is a computerized ultrasound simulator made by SonoSim that provides hands-on, didactic, and assessment modules using simulated patient ultrasound cases for healthcare trainees.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SonoSim Ultrasound Training Solution (ver. 2.8.1) computerized ultrasound simulator**: SonoSim is a computerized ultrasound simulator providing "integrated hands-on ultrasound training, didactic instruction, and assessment using real patient ultrasound cases." Through replication of the tactile experience of probe manipulation it is "designed to mimic the experience of scanning an actual patient," letting users practice image acquisition and interpretation in a risk-free environment. In this study residents used its training modules for one self-instructed hour per session, relying on software-provided instruction and feedback. (Silva Jack Philip et al. (2016))
