Individual digital study assistant (IDSA)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

An online higher-education student-support tool that integrates learner, campus, learning-management-system, and external-resource data to provide individualized study organization, goal-setting, and self-regulation recommendations without directly tutoring learning content.

Components¶

  • Individual digital study assistants (IDSA) as online student support tools that strengthen self-regulation, goal achievement, and study organization: The article defines an IDSA as "an efficient online student support tool that strengthens self-regulation skills, goal achievement, and study organization through suitable functionalities". Unlike pedagogical conversational agents, an IDSA does not directly assist in learning content; instead it provides individual study structuring, situation-specific guidance, and recommendations based on individual goals, interests, and competencies. It accesses information from student dialogs, campus and learning management systems, completed modules, and external resources such as OER platforms. (Karrenbauer et al. (2023))

Claims¶

  • Stakeholder and literature analysis yields seven IDSA requirement groups covering functionalities, contact options, data-based individuality, testing, marketing, data protection, and usability [+W]
  • In a three-month field test, exploration and learning-organization functionalities were the most used, while social and data-ethics functionalities were used least [+W]
  • Integrating the IDSA into an existing learning management system limited usability, as the platform predefined frontend structure and navigation [-W]
