# Arabic Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index (PSQI) and Arabic IPAQ Short Form

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Validated Modern Standard Arabic self-report instruments used to assess university students’ sleep quality and physical activity, respectively.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Arabic Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index and Arabic IPAQ Short Form as validated sleep and activity measures**: The study used the Arabic PSQI, a 19-item self-report measure yielding seven component scores summed to a global score of 0–21 where higher scores indicate poorer sleep, and the Arabic IPAQ-SF, seven items capturing walking, moderate, vigorous activity, and sitting time converted to MET-minutes per week. The authors state these instruments "enable parallel characterization of sleep and PA in large student samples". The Arabic PSQI translation showed Cronbach's α = 0.77 in its psychometric evaluation. (Alghadier Mshari (2026))
