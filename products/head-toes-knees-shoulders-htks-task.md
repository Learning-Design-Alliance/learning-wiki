# Head-Toes-Knees-Shoulders (HTKS) task

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A brief, game-like direct assessment developed for young children that measures behavioral self-regulation through rule reversal, attention, working-memory, and inhibitory-control tasks.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Head-Toes-Knees-Shoulders (HTKS) task for assessing early childhood behavioral self-regulation**: The HTKS is a direct assessment of behavioral self-regulation for children ages three to seven. "The HTKS is a short, game-like task where children are asked to 'do the opposite' in regards to a set of paired rules", tapping attention, working memory, and inhibition. Scores range from 0–40, with two points for correct responses and one for self-corrections. The article reports strong reliability (Cronbach's alpha .85–.94 across samples) and validity evidence, and notes growth in HTKS performance does not appear to be a function of practice effects. (Montroy Janelle J. et al. (2016))
