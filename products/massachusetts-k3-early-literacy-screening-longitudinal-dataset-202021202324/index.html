Massachusetts K–3 early literacy screening longitudinal dataset (2020/21–2023/24)

WestEd’s longitudinal dataset for the Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education links K–3 early-literacy screening results with SIMS demographics, MCAS, ACCESS for ELLs, and school- and district-level contextual data to monitor literacy performance and reading risk.
