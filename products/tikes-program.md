## Description
A program providing infant and early childhood mental health consultation to build consultees' capacity to support young children's mental health, implemented through the Center of Excellence framework.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **IECMHC theory of change (Center of Excellence framework)**: The TIKES program follows the IECMHC theory of change from the Center of Excellence for Infant and Early Childhood Mental Health Consultation. The article states the framework "posits the direct effects of an IECMHC program are realized by increasing consultees' capacity to promote infant and early childhood mental health," with indirect effects including program adjustments, reductions in disparities, and positive child and family impacts. Consultee and consultant characteristics (education, race, experience) shape engagement in consultation, which in turn shapes direct outcomes (consultee capacity) and indirect outcomes such as reduced child mental health problems and reduced expulsion. (Trigg et al. (2026))
