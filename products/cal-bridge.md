## Description
Cal-Bridge is a California partnership programme run by UC, CSU, and community-college faculty that recruits and mentors underrepresented physics and astronomy undergraduates toward PhD study.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Four-pillar support structure: financial support, mentoring, cohort building and professional development, and research experiences**: The article states that the design and implementation of Cal-Bridge reflect the National Academies report's priorities "by building the program on four pillars: financial support, mentoring, cohort building and professional development, and research experiences." Faculty at both the scholars' home institutions and the institutions where they hope to earn a PhD are active participants. Each pillar supplies a distinct resource: need-based scholarships, joint CSU-UC mentoring, monthly cohort workshops, and supervised summer and academic-year research. (Alexander L. Rudolph (2019))
- **Relationship-centered model of mentorship for historically underrepresented STEM students**: The article proposes a "relationship-centered model of mentorship" as critical to the success of young academics, especially those underrepresented in their field. It rests on three features: multiple mentors providing a network of support; mentorship education that includes "interactive, group elements and resources"; and continuous improvement supported by quantitative and qualitative assessments. The Cal-Bridge program instantiates it through dual faculty mentorship, biweekly meetings, a Mentorship Handbook, workshops, and a Peer Mentorship Program. (M. K. Rodriguez Wimberly et al. (2021))

### Claims
- [Of Cal-Bridge scholars who earned a BS while in the program, 27 of 33 (82%) had begun PhD programs in physics or astronomy by the article's report](../claims/cal-bridge-82-percent-bs-graduates-enter-phd-programs.md) [+W]
- [Scholars rate faculty mentorship the highest-quality program element (over 50% excellent, over 90% good or better) while the peer network was the lowest-rated element](../claims/faculty-mentorship-highest-rated-peer-network-lowest.md) [+M]
