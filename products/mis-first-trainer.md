# MIS First Trainer®

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A portable laparoscopic simulation-training platform that uses a smartphone, tablet, or laptop webcam with physical instruments and models for surgical-resident practice, distributed in Portugal through the Training4all Project.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MIS First Trainer® portable laparoscopic training platform and the Training4all distribution project**: The MIS First Trainer® (ACADEMIA FT, Portugal) is "a portable simulation platform to be used with a smartphone, a tablet or a laptop (with webcam)", distributed through the Training4all Project started in Portugal in 2017 so that "every resident in Portugal gets one laparoscopic training platform with a set of instruments when they start surgical residency". Each trainee in the course had an individual platform and model. Participants rated it "very adequate" for this kind of training (M = 4,72, SD = 0,457) and important or very important for their residency (51% and 36%). (Gonçalves Mário Rui et al. (2025))
