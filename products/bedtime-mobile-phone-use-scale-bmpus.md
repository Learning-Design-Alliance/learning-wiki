# Bedtime Mobile Phone Use Scale (BMPUS)

## Description
A six-item, five-point self-report scale developed by Ragupathi Darnisha et al. to measure undergraduate students' frequency of mobile-phone use at bedtime over the previous 30 days.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Bedtime Mobile Phone Use Scale (BMPUS), a 6-item self-report measure**: The BMPUS is a 6-item self-administered scale developed from Exelmans and Van den Bulck to measure bedtime mobile phone use over the last 30 days, with items scaled from 1 (never) to 5 (everyday) and total scores ranging 6–30. Sample item: "How frequently do you send text messages after lights out?". Content validity was established with I-CVI and S-CVI indices of 1, and "Cronbach alpha for the Bedtime Mobile Phone Use Scale (BMPUS) in the present study was high (α = 0.89)". (Ragupathi Darnisha et al. (2020))
