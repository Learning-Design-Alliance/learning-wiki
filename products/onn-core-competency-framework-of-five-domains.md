## Description
A five-domain competency framework for oncology nurse navigators covering care coordination, communication, education, professional role, and expert navigation skills, adapted for use in the Egyptian setting.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ONN core competency framework of five domains (care coordination, communication, education, professional role, expert navigation)**: The article adopts a competency framework in which the oncology nurse navigator role is organized into "5 key categories: care coordination, communication, education, professional role, and advanced skills unique to expert ONNs," drawing on previously published competency lists. These competencies require knowledge of cancer care and healthcare systems, communication and advocacy skills, and patient-centered attributes such as empathy and cultural sensitivity. In the study the framework structured the competency questionnaire and the domain-level correlation analysis with patient satisfaction. (Bassyouny HAA et al. (2026))
