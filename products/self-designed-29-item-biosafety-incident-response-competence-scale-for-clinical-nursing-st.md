# Self-designed 29-item biosafety incident response competence scale for clinical nursing staff

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 29-item, four-dimension, five-point Likert self-report scale developed by Wu Chao et al. to measure clinical nurses' competence in monitoring and warning, incident disposal, knowledge preparedness, and infection protection.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Self-designed 29-item biosafety incident response competence scale for clinical nursing staff (4 dimensions, 5-point Likert).**: A self-designed scale developed by the research team to assess clinical nursing staff's biosafety incident response competence. The article states "It consists of 29 items across 4 dimensions, using a 5-point Likert scale" with scores from 0 to 145, where high scores signify high proficiency. In this study "The Cronbach's alphas coefficient in this study was 0.977, and the values for each dimension ranged from 0.899 to 0.959." (Wu Chao et al. (2025))
