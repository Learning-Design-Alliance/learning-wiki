## Description
RATsApp is an open-source web-based application developed by Steffen Steinert and colleagues for delivering automated formative feedback, scaffolds, STEM competence estimation, and learning analytics dashboards.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **RATsApp lecturer dashboard with aggregated statistics and error categories for identifying misconceptions**: The lecturer dashboard displays aggregated student statistics and difficulties so lecturers can monitor performance on RATs and individual competencies. The article explains that lecturers "can see the most frequently chosen answer option. This can help them identify misconceptions their students may have and intervene." Tasks frequently answered incorrectly are shown in error categories: always answered incorrectly, often answered incorrectly (correct less than 40% of the time), and deceptive answer (the same wrong answer selected more than 30% of the time). The article frames this as supporting formative assessment and self-reflective adjustment of teaching methods. (Steffen Steinert et al. (2024))
