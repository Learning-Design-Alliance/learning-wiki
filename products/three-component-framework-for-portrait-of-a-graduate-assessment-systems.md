# Three-component framework for Portrait of a Graduate assessment systems

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A framework developed by Kyle Dunbar et al. for organizing district Portrait of a Graduate assessment systems around collecting, analyzing, and communicating evidence of student learning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Taxonomy of POG assessment strategies: state menu-based demonstration, experiential/performance assessment, embedded formative assessment, and competency-based progression**: The brief organizes how states measure POG competencies into distinct approaches. Colorado uses Graduation Guidelines under which "Local school boards select from a state-provided menu of assessment options"; Nevada and Virginia use experiential learning demonstrated through "portfolios and capstones or hands-on learning opportunities"; Michigan emphasizes formative assessments embedded in learning alongside summative assessments of deeper learning; and New Hampshire integrates POG assessment into competency-based education where students advance on mastery rather than time in class. (Terrell et al. (2025))
