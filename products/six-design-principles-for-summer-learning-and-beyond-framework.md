# Six Design Principles for Summer Learning and Beyond framework

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 review), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A framework developed by Summer Learning et al. for designing intellectually rigorous and equitable summer-learning settings through six interrelated principles addressing relationships, belonging, whole-child learning, knowledge, creative inquiry, and educator needs.
