## Description
A movement-writing and analysis system created by Rudolf Laban that represents bodily movement for recording, reading, teaching, and analyzing dance.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Labanotation as a cognitive, notation-based teaching method for folk dance**: The article presents Laban movement notation, created in 1928 by Rudolf Van Laban, as "a writing system in which all movements that can be made by the body are simply taken into account". Its vertical staff represents the human body from the dancer's point of view. The authors argue it grounds dance teaching in cognitive bases, supporting coding, analysis and synthesis, and serves as a record analogous to music notation for dance. (Dilek Cantekin Elyağutu et al. (2017))
