## Description
Pixton is an online comic-strip creation platform developed by Pixton that enables users to create, personalize, share, and download visual comic materials.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Pixton online comic-strip creation platform**: Pixton is an online platform for creating comic strips that the article describes as "an easy-to-use and intuitive tool that promotes collaborative work, creativity, and critical thinking". It offers feedback options, online sharing, and downloading of comic strips, and allows personalization without building from scratch. In the study, teachers in the experimental group designed supplementary grammar and vocabulary materials using Pixton exclusively over four months. (Cabrera et al. (2018))

### Claims
- [EFL students taught grammar and vocabulary with Pixton comic strips scored significantly higher on a post-test than control students taught without Pixton](../claims/pixton-comic-strips-improve-efl-grammar-vocabulary-posttest.md) [+M]
