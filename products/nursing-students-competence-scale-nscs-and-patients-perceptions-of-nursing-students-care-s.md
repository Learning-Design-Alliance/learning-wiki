# Nursing Students' Competence Scale (NSCS) and Patients' Perceptions of Nursing Students' Care Scale (PPNSCS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A matched pair of self-report scales measuring nursing students' perceived clinical competence and patients' perceptions of care received from those students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Matched student–patient dyad instruments: NSCS and PPNSCS used to compare perceived competence and received care**: The study pairs two validated instruments administered to matched student–patient dyads: the 43-item Nursing Students' Competence Scale with six subscales (total Cronbach's alpha 0.978) and the 28-item Patients' Perceptions of Nursing Students' Care Scale with four subscales, "scored on a 5-point Likert scale". The Turkish PPNSCS adaptation showed "good fit [RMSEA = 0.03, CFI = 0.99, Cronbach's alpha = 0.94]". Patients qualified only after at least eight hours of direct care from a participating student. (Ulupınar Fadime (2025))
