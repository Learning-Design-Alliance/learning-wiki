## Description
An open educational platform established by the European Association of Clinical Pharmacology and Therapeutics (EACPT) to facilitate international collaboration and sharing of online prescribing-education resources.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **EurOP²E — the European Open Platform for Prescribing Education**: EurOP²E is a platform that "seeks to improve and harmonize European clinical pharmacology and therapeutics (CPT) education by facilitating international collaboration and sharing problem-based, online, open educational resources." It was set up to address the EACPT Education Working Group's recommendation 6, that schools utilize more online learning resources and share them nationally and internationally. Depending on the theme, its function will vary from supporting international communication and collaboration to providing teach-the-teacher materials and initiating collaborative production of ready-to-use teaching materials. (Bakkum (2022))
