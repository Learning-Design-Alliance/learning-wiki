# TenSquare's Data-Driven School Improvement Process

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
A continuous-improvement cycle in which a turnaround partner first conducts a comprehensive school performance audit covering academic, financial, and operational areas, then develops a multi-year redesign plan prioritized by intensity of focus, identifies evidence-based interventions, monitors implementation and impact toward goals, adjusts implementation, and provides ongoing feedback. At Perry Street the process began with an audit "to identify the areas in need of improvement — from academic to financial to operational" and its findings "served as the basis for the creation of a multifaceted five-year redesign plan."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A comprehensive baseline audit of performance, processes, and resources to set improvement goals
- Ongoing progress-monitoring structures at board, leadership, and staff levels
#### Constraints
- The report presents this as one school's process; it states no framework should be a magic bullet and decisions should consider each school's needs and context

### Target Learners
- Charter school leaders, board members, and authorizers pursuing school turnaround

### Learning Goals
- School-wide organizational improvement and improved student achievement outcomes
