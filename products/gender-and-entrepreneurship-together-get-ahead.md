# Gender and Entrepreneurship Together (GET Ahead)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An ILO five-day participatory business-training programme that develops entrepreneurial and business-management skills among low-income women, including those with low literacy.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ILO GET Ahead business training program for women in enterprise**: The ILO's Gender and Entrepreneurship Together (GET Ahead) program is a five-day participatory business training course for low-income women. The article describes it as highlighting "essential entrepreneurial skills from a gender perspective, addressing the practical and strategic needs of low-income women in enterprise by strengthening their basic business and people management skills." It covers gender concepts alongside standard topics such as recordkeeping, marketing, costing and pricing, and business idea generation, using participatory and practical methods suitable for women with low literacy. In this study it was offered free with transport subsidies of approximately $6 per day, at an estimated provision cost of $US222 to $US333 per woman trained. (Diwan Faizan et al. (2014))
