## Description
EDAQino is an educational data-acquisition system developed by Gingl and colleagues that exposes sensing, signal conditioning, conversion, software processing, and actuation for teaching electronics and related living-system analogies.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Universal signal-chain principle linking modern devices, sensors and living things**: The article organizes the operation of modern electronic devices around a general signal chain: external signals are sensed, conditioned, converted by an ADC to integer numbers, processed by software, and can be reversed through a DAC and actuator to act on the real world. It argues that "the main principles can be surprisingly universal and similar in distant fields", extending the chain to living things — humans sense light, process information, and produce movements or sounds. The EDAQino system is designed so students can identify and exercise each element of this chain transparently. (Gingl Z et al. (2018))
