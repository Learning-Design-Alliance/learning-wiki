# CSTA/ISTE operational definition of computational thinking

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A framework developed by the Computer Science Teachers Association and International Society for Technology in Education that defines nine computational-thinking concepts and capabilities for integrating computational thinking across K–12 subjects.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CSTA/ISTE operational definition of computational thinking with nine core concepts and capabilities**: The Computer Science Teachers Association and International Society for Technology in Education developed "an operational definition of computational thinking that includes nine core CT concepts and capabilities" (Barr and Stephenson 2011). The nine ideas are data collection, data analysis, data representation, problem decomposition, abstraction, algorithms & procedures, automation, parallelization, and simulation. The article treats this framework as the starting point for embedding CT in K-12 core content areas and for professional development design. (Yadav et al. (2016))
