# Clinical Reasoning Indicators History Taking Scale (CRI-HT-S), Persian version

## Description
A Persian-language, five-point rater-scored assessment instrument developed for evaluating undergraduate medical students’ clinical reasoning during history-taking OSCE stations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CRI-HT-S: Clinical Reasoning Indicators History Taking Scale (Persian version) for assessing clinical reasoning in OSCEs**: The CRI-HT-S, "originally developed by Furstenberg et al.", assesses clinical reasoning indicators during history taking in OSCE exams. It is scored on a 5-point Likert scale from 1 (least aligned to criteria) to 5 (most aligned). The Persian version was translated, back-translated, and cross-culturally adapted; ten medical education specialists assessed content and face validity, and reliability was high with "an alpha coefficient of 0.91". (Delirrooyfard Ali et al. (2026))
