Chula Vista Promise Neighborhood¶

Description¶

A place-based Promise Neighborhood community programme serving the Castle Park neighborhood of Chula Vista, California, across early childhood, education, college and career readiness, child development, and family support.

Components¶

  • Chula Vista Promise Neighborhood (CVPromise): a place-based community program serving the Castle Park neighborhood: CVPromise is a Promise Neighborhood initiative serving the Castle Park neighborhood of Chula Vista, California. The profile describes it as serving "the neighborhood of Castle Park, a 33-census block area in the city of Chula Vista." The program is documented as part of the Promise Neighborhoods Case Studies project (2014-2015), prepared for the Promise Neighborhoods Institute at PolicyLink by Mathematica Policy Research, covering areas including early childhood, child development, education, college and career readiness, and family support. (Esposito et al. (2015))
