# Research Learning Communities (RLC) intervention

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A school-improvement professional-learning programme in which school-based Evidence Champions attend cross-school research workshops, develop and evaluate improvement strategies, and support colleagues' use of educational research.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Research Learning Communities intervention with Evidence Champions and cross-school workshops**: Research Learning Communities is a school-improvement intervention in which two Evidence Champion teachers per school attend four one-day workshops per year with peers from up to four other schools, examining research on an agreed focus such as literacy or growth mindsets. Champions then "develop, apply and evaluate school or key-stage wide improvement strategies using the learning from the workshops" and support colleagues. Workshops follow a cycle from identifying good practice and developing a research question through trialling approaches to analysing impact data, with year two workshops led by the Champions themselves. (Dr Jo Rose et al. (2017))

### Claims
