## Description
A cohort-based coaching and professional-development programme for early-stage biomedical PhD students, especially those from underrepresented groups, run by faculty coaches to support academic-career persistence.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Academy for Future Science Faculty coaching program**: The Academy for Future Science Faculty is "a novel coaching program aiming to positively influence biomedical science PhD students' persistence into academic careers, with a particular focus on encouraging and supporting URM students." Cohort 1 recruited 99 students entering PhD programs in Fall 2011, of whom 51 self-identified as URMs, subdivided into 10 coaching groups of 10 students each with faculty coaches from different institutions. The intervention combined annual 2- or 3-day in-person meetings in Chicago with between-meeting videoconferences and email, covering career planning, professional development, and diversity topics. Coaches received diversity training informed by social science theory. (Williams et al. (2017))

### Claims
- [Career coaching provided emotional support to URM biomedical PhD students, with coaches offering a caring, listening presence and peers offering mutual encouragement](../claims/coaching-emotional-support-urm-phd-students.md) [+M]
