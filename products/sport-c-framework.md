# SPORT-C framework

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A classroom instructional framework developed by Basoah et al. that embeds STEM learning objectives in sports-based case scenarios and guides students through systems-thinking problem solving.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The SPORT-C framework blends sports, case-based pedagogy, and systems thinking into STEM classroom activities**: The SPORT-C intervention is a framework in which "sports, a case -based pedagogy, and systems thinking learning are blended into an activity potentially applicable in all STEM courses". Students receive a real-world sports scenario incorporating the current unit's learning objectives and must use the unit's learnings together with a systems thinking problem-solving method to progress through the activity. The study tested a basketball scenario on probability and expected values. (Jeffrey Basoah et al. (2022))
