TextNow programme¶

Description¶

TextNow is a coached literacy programme for struggling readers that supports text choice, enjoyment, and comprehension through individualized reading activities.

Components¶

  • The 'choose, enjoy and understand' rubric underlying the TextNow programme: TextNow coaching is organised around three strands captured by the rubric that the programme's "role of the coach is to support the three Text Now core principals of 'choose, enjoy and understand'". 'Choosing texts' develops pupils' skills in selecting suitable, interesting reading materials and familiarity with environments where texts are found; 'enjoying texts' uses shared reading, ICT, audio books and related discussion; 'understanding texts' targets literal and inferential comprehension through techniques such as skimming and scanning, KWL, storyboards and reciprocal teaching. The report situates this as a model with strong common-sense appeal but inconclusive causal evidence. (Maxwell et al. (2014))

Claims¶

  • The TextNow programme produced no statistically significant difference in enjoyment of reading or motivation to read compared with the comparison group [~M]
