# eHealth Literacy Questionnaire (eHLQ)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The University of Copenhagen's eHealth Literacy Questionnaire is a seven-scale self-report instrument for measuring electronic health literacy across distinct capability areas.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **eHealth Literacy Questionnaire (eHLQ) measuring electronic health literacy across 7 scales**: The University of Copenhagen’s eHealth Literacy Questionnaire is used to measure preservice teachers’ eHL levels, addressing the growing importance of navigating online health information and misinformation. Like the HLQ, it is multidimensional: "This multidimensional tool assesses eHL across 7 scales and does not generate a single score but rather provides a broader understanding of eHL levels across different areas and scales." The same analysis process as the HLQ (robust ANOVA with the Welch method) will be applied. Enhancing eHL is described as a priority given preservice teachers’ expressed concerns about teaching HL in an increasingly digital world. (Kealy-Ashby C et al. (2025))
