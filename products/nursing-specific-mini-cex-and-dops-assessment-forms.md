## Description
Revised bedside-observation mini-CEX and DOPS forms for assessing novice nurses’ clinical skills and providing formative feedback through scaled criteria, self-evaluation, and evaluator comments.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Nursing-specific Mini-CEX: a seven-dimension workplace-based assessment tool for new nurses**: The article developed a customized Mini-Clinical Evaluation Exercise for nurses, adapting a physician-resident tool to nursing's caring role. It assesses "history taking, physical examination, intervention/therapeutic management, counseling skills, nursing professionalism, organization/efficiency, and overall competency" based on observed bedside practice. Evaluators rate each dimension on a nine-point scale, and the tool doubles as a formative feedback instrument. (Liu Yueh-Ping et al. (2019))
