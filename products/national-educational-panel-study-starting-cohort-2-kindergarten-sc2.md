## Description
A nationwide German longitudinal panel dataset following children from kindergarten through secondary school, administered and maintained by the National Educational Panel Study (NEPS).

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **NEPS Starting Cohort 2 (Kindergarten) longitudinal panel**: The National Educational Panel Study Starting Cohort 2 (Kindergarten, SC2) is a nationwide German longitudinal panel; the authors used it because it "comprised information on developing competencies from kindergarten to the different secondary school levels". Math tests were administered from the first grade onwards, and competencies were scaled with weighted maximum likelihood estimates on a joint metric so that score differences can be interpreted as development trajectories across measurement points. (Herrmann et al. (2025))
