# OGAP Professional Learning

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
OGAP Professional Learning is a mathematics professional-development programme run by the Ongoing Assessment Project (OGAP) for educators across developmental reasoning strands.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **OGAP strand-based professional learning structure: cohorts of about 24 educators, four onsite days, and two follow-up coaching days**: OGAP professional learning is organized by Strand, each aligned to a developmental progression in mathematics: Additive Reasoning (K–2), Multiplicative Reasoning (3–5), Fractional Reasoning (3–6), and Proportional Reasoning (6–8). Each strand is delivered to educator cohorts, often around 24 participants, through four onsite professional learning days, with districts able to space sessions several weeks apart. Two onsite follow-up coaching days, often during PLC time, "support transfer into classroom practice." Multiple strands may run simultaneously under the same structure. (OGAP Math program description (2024))

OGAP professional learning is organized by Strand, each aligned to a developmental progression in mathematics: Additive Reasoning (K–2), Multiplicative Reasoning (3–5), Fractional Reasoning (3–6), and Proportional Reasoning (6–8). Each strand is delivered to educator cohorts, often around 24 participants, through four onsite professional learning days, with districts able to space sessions several weeks apart. ## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cohorts of about 24 educators per OGAP Strand
- Four onsite professional learning days per strand
- Two onsite follow-up coaching days, often scheduled during PLC time
#### Constraints
- Districts may choose consecutive sessions or a split schedule, with flexibility to space the sessions several weeks apart

### Target Learners
- K–8 mathematics teachers participating in OGAP strands

### Learning Goals
- Transfer of professional learning into classroom practice
