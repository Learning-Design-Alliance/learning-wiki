# UD Old English Cairo treebank

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A publicly released Universal Dependencies treebank of 20 Old English Cairo sentences with syntactic, morphological, hyperlemma, root, and Modern English gloss annotations, created by Lauren Levine et al. for historical-linguistics research and teaching.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **UD Old English Cairo treebank with morphological, hyperlemma, root, and gloss annotations**: A publicly released sample treebank of 20 Old English UD Cairo sentences annotated for syntactic dependencies, morphological features, and historically relevant features. The authors "provide morphology, hyperlemma, and root annotations", with word-level Modern English glosses, and state it is "the first OE contribution to UD". Hyperlemmas align word forms and lemmas across historical periods, and roots link forms to reconstructed Indo-European origins. (Lauren Levine et al. (2025))
