## Description
CogTeach is a browser-based video-conferencing and online-learning platform developed by Songlin Xu and colleagues that integrates learner interaction, webcam-based gaze sensing, cognitive-state data collection, and real-time visual and auditory feedback.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CogTeach browser-based experimental platform for gaze collection, cognitive-state sensing, and real-time feedback**: CogTeach is the online education platform the authors built for this study, described as "a browser-based integral video conferencing system that simultaneously enables interaction, cognitive data collection and real-time visual and auditory feedback". It uses WebGazer webcam eye tracking with 9-point calibration, detects inattention via face-lost, records confusion via slide clicks, detects areas of interest from slide screenshots, and visualizes peer attention as a red bounding rectangle around the AoI voted by most Control-group participants every 5 seconds. Backends run in JavaScript and Python with Flask. (Songlin Xu et al. (2023))
