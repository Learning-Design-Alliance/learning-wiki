## Description
A National Public Health Management Centre of Tabriz University of Medical Sciences programme comprising 11 short management and public-health courses for district health managers, developed for and used by Iran's Ministry of Health.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **District health management fellowship training programme (11 short courses, Tabriz, Iran)**: A training programme developed by the National Public Health Management Centre of Tabriz University of Medical Sciences to build the managerial capacity of district health managers, based on an educational needs assessment by the Ministry of Health. It comprised 11 short courses (1–3 days each) covering management and leadership, managing the district, quality improvement, planning and evaluation, health information management, health resources management and economics, community participation, epidemiology, research in health systems, human resources and creativity, and rules and ethics. The article reports that "The programme developed was used by the Ministry of Health as an applicable package for training district health managers across the country". (Gholipour Kamal et al. (2018))
