# Teacher Ability Assessment Tools

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A teacher-appraisal instrument modified by Indonesia’s Ministry of National Education from the Georgia Department of Education tool, assessing seven abilities related to planning, implementing, engaging learners, concluding instruction, and evaluating learning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Seven-ability teacher performance construct used for teacher appraisal in Indonesian senior high schools**: The article operationalizes teacher performance as seven abilities measured by questionnaire and appraisal: formulating lesson plans (RPP), planning learning activities, choosing learning sources and media, applying learning approaches and strategies, encouraging and maintaining student involvement, ending learning effectively, and designing assessment methods and evaluation tools. It reports that these seven factors "possessed a significant effect on teacher performance", with loadings ranging from 0.46 to 0.73 in the measurement model. The construct derives from the Teacher Ability Assessment Tools modified from the Georgia Department of Education instrument by the Indonesian Ministry of National Education. (Pardosi J et al. (2021))
