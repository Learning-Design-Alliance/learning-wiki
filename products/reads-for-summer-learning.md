## Description
READS for Summer Learning is a school- and home-based summer literacy intervention developed to support elementary students' reading comprehension and independent reading engagement through matched books, guided lessons, family involvement, and summer nudges.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **READS summer literacy intervention (Traditional and Adaptive versions)**: READS is "a program designed to narrow income-based reading skill gaps," implemented for fourth-grade students and their teachers over the 2014-2015 school year and summer of 2015. Traditional READS gives students level- and interest-matched books with tri-folds leading through a reading routine (pre-reading activity and post-reading comprehension check), mailed back with prepaid postage, plus teacher training on six scripted lessons. Adaptive READS replaces top-down management with grade-level team adaptation based on school-specific data. (Quinn et al. (2016))
