Battery of twelve tests for originality with self-recording time device¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 7 claims (5 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A Chassell (1916) battery of twelve individually or group-administered tests for measuring and ranking originality in university students and adults, using oral and placard instructions and a self-recorded time device.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • The three laboratory-devised production tests (Economic Prophecies, Sheet Music Invention, Novel Situations) tied in average merit among the most effective tests. [+W]
  • A three-test subset (Analogues, Chain Puzzle, Economic Prophecies) differentiated the group nearly as well as the full twelve-test battery. [+W]
  • An inventor of international reputation ranked within the first half but showed no exceptional originality on the tests. [~W]
  • The Analogues test ranked first among the twelve originality tests in agreement with the group's amalgamated rank-order. [+W]
  • The Chain Puzzle best distinguished the top and bottom quarters of the group among all twelve tests. [+W]
  • The twelve tests differed in value as measures of originality, yet each appeared to test some phase of the trait. [+W]
  • Word-Building showed the lowest agreement with the amalgamated rank-order, attributed to prior anagram experience. [~W]
