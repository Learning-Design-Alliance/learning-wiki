## Description
A European Schoolnet framework and exemplar learning space that organizes active blended learning around six zones—investigate, create, exchange, develop, interact, and present—paired with digital technologies.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Brussels Future Classroom Lab with six learning zones for active blended learning**: The Future Classroom Lab (FCL), opened in January 2012 in Brussels within European Schoolnet, is an innovative active learning space consisting of six fundamental learning spaces called education zones. The article states their mission is to "actively connect, engage, and stimulate students in learning". Each zone pairs anticipated didactic uses with digital technologies: investigate (inquiry-based learning with probes, simulations), create (content creation, 3D printers), exchange (collaboration with interactive whiteboards), develop (informal individual learning), interact (peer discussion with e-voting), and present (presentation tools). (J Hanč et al. (2024))
