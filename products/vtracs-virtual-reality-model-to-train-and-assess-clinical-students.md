# VTRACS (Virtual reality model to TRain and Assess Clinical Students)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A standalone-headset virtual-reality clinical simulation platform developed by Komolafe et al. for training and assessing nursing, dentistry, medicine, and surgery students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **VTRACS: a haptic-limited VR simulation platform for training and assessing clinical students**: VTRACS (Virtual reality model to TRain and Assess Clinical Students) is a VR platform that delivered "immersive clinical scenarios through standalone VR headsets (Meta Quest 2)" across Nursing, Dentistry, Medicine, and Surgery. Simulations replicated scenarios such as "performing vital signs checks, abdominal examinations... and conducting tooth extraction." Participants interacted via hand-held controllers; haptic feedback "was limited to vibrations; no tactile sensations or physical resistance were simulated." Sessions lasted 20-25 minutes in a controlled laboratory with technical support. (Komolafe AO et al (2026))
