# Bewust eten, hoe doe je dat?

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A Dutch web-based computer-tailored nutrition education intervention developed by Springvloet and colleagues, offering basic and plus versions that target dietary knowledge, motivation, self-regulation, and selected environmental factors.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Web-based computer-tailored nutrition education intervention 'Bewust eten, hoe doe je dat?' with basic and plus versions**: The article describes a web-based computer-tailored nutrition education intervention called 'Bewust eten, hoe doe je dat?' ('Conscious eating, how do you do it?') consisting of four modules for fruit, vegetables, high-energy snacks and fat. Two versions were developed: "a basic version targeting knowledge, awareness, attitude, self-efficacy and volitional and self-regulation processes, and a plus version additionally addressing the home environment arrangement and the availability and price of healthy food products in supermarkets". It was built with TailorBuilder software and delivered via a website with personal log-in codes. (Springvloet Linda et al. (2014))
