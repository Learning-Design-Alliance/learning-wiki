## Description
A seven-dimension competency framework and self-assessment scale for evaluating registered nurses' clinical, research, leadership, interpersonal, ethical-legal, and professional-development capabilities.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Competency Framework for Registered Nurses in China as the theoretical framework for specialist-nurse competency evaluation**: A self-assessment scale that "comprehensively evaluates registered nurses' core competency and can also be used for other related assessments", comprising seven dimensions: critical thinking; scientific research; clinical nursing; leadership; interpersonal relationships; law and ethical practice; and professional development, education, and consulting ability. This study adopted it as the theoretical framework, combined with literature and semi-structured interviews with eight experts, to derive the incontinence-specific index system. (Tian Xiao Miao et al. (2026))
