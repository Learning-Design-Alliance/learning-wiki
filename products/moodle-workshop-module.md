# Moodle Workshop module

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Moodle's Workshop activity module supports the distribution, anonymous peer assessment, and teacher assessment of learner submissions using defined assessment criteria and grading schemes.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Moodle workshop module as the online peer assessment platform**: The experiment's observation and marking tool was the "workshop" module of the Moodle platform, into which anonymised digitalised exam papers were placed for peer and teacher marking with a defined marking scheme. The article reports that "This module is stable and presents a user-friendly environment", but identifies a weakness in its marking tool: the scale scheme was implemented as option buttons from "excellent" to "very poor", a method the authors say can lead to errors. (Bouzidi et al. (2009))
