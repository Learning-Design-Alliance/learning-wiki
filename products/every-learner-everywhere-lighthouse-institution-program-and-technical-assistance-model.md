# Every Learner Everywhere lighthouse institution program and technical assistance model

## Description
A Gates Foundation-funded network of 12 organizations working with 12 lighthouse two- and four-year colleges to implement adaptive courseware in gateway courses serving first-generation, low-income students and students of color. Institutions committed to cross-functional project teams, summit participation, a community of practice, and research participation, receiving subgrants of $25,000 or $50,000. Technical assistance combined on-campus workshops, five to eight distance coaching calls, and two to four convenings, plus Digital Promise support for impact study design and data collection.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Cross-functional project team including a department chair or dean, academic affairs representative, faculty, and instructional technology staff
- Commitment to redesign gateway courses and participate in data collection
#### Constraints
- Two of 12 institutions could not complete redesign in time for fall 2019 pilots due to a late project start

### Target Learners
- first-generation students
- low-income students
- students of color in gateway courses

### Learning Goals
- improved gateway course success rates and reduced equity gaps
