# STEM Smart

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
Stony Brook University's umbrella STEM outreach programme, comprising federally funded initiatives that provide mentoring, research and internship opportunities, career preparation, and tutoring across the STEM education pipeline.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **STEM Smart outreach programs and LSAMP at Stony Brook University**: STEM Smart is Stony Brook's umbrella outreach program including over 10 federally funded programs providing "mentors, internship and research opportunities, career preparation workshops, and tutoring services for students all along the STEM pipeline from middle school to Ph.D." LSAMP, one component, provides a four-year curriculum for underrepresented minority STEM majors plus a Bridge to the Doctorate with a full NSF fellowship. Participants show retention and completion rates substantially higher than the institution as a whole. (Mary Nguyen et al. (2012))
