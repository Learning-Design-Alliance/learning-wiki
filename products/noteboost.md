## Description
NoteBoost is an internally developed NLP-based web system by Bansal et al. that grades medical students' patient notes against checklist items and presents individualized feedback on documented findings and diagnoses.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **NoteBoost automated patient note grading system**: NoteBoost is an internally developed NLP-based software system that grades patient notes by finding "checklist items in a patient note and checks for phrases and paraphrases that are close matches to a checklist item." It generates individualized reports listing items found and not found in history and physical sections, shares case diagnosis and alternative diagnoses, and presents feedback through a web-based interface with color-coded visual cues. (S. Bansal et al. (2025))
