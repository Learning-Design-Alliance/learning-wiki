My environment and energy¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A web-assisted educational software package developed by Ercan et al. that teaches environmental and energy-saving content while incorporating emotional-intelligence and empathy activities for eighth-grade students.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Web-assisted instruction with emotional intelligence content produced higher post-test academic achievement, environmental attitudes and emotional intelligence than traditional lecture in 8th-grade energy-saving instruction [+M]
