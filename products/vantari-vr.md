## Description
Vantari VR is a commercial immersive virtual-reality training platform that provides interactive procedural simulations, including a chest-tube-insertion module, developed by Vantari VR in Sydney, Australia.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Vantari VR chest tube insertion simulation delivered on Oculus/Meta Quest 2**: A commercially available immersive VR module (Vantari VR, Sydney, Australia) delivered through an Oculus/Meta Quest 2 headset and hand controllers. It "places the learner in a fully immersive 3D operating room environment" requiring natural motor movements to sequentially complete 26 procedural steps with integrated instructions and real-time feedback from embedded metrics. Surgical experts verified alignment with ATLS guidelines and the online module. (Knobovitch Ryan M. BSc et al. (2025))
