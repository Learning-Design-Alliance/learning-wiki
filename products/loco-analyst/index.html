LOCO-Analyst¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

LOCO-Analyst is a semantic-technology-based learning analytics and feedback tool developed by Jelena Jovanovic for helping online-course teachers analyze learning-context data and adapt course content and structure.

Components¶

  • LOCO-Analyst feedback tool based on Semantic technologies: LOCO-Analyst, developed by Jelena Jovanovic, is "a generic feedback provisioning tool based on Semantic technologies and analysis of learning context data captured and integrated from online learning systems and tools". It enables teachers to rethink the content and structure of their courses and helps them customise the courses to students' needs. The commentary presents it as an example of ontological frameworks and tools developed for education. (Devedzic (2016))
