## Description
A four-dimension, three-level coding framework developed by Odden et al. for rating undergraduate students’ observable ownership efforts in programming, investigation, modeling and data analysis, and communication.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Shared epistemic agency framework with epistemic and regulative action dimensions**: Drawing on Damsa et al., the article identifies evidence of epistemic agency at the scale of a collaborative group. Shared epistemic agency describes the process by which groups work together to deliberately produce knowledge. The framework considers two main dimensions: epistemic actions that contribute to the development and refinement of a knowledge object, and regulative actions that organize the process of knowledge creation, from goal setting to coordination and social negotiation. (Ian Descamps et al. (2024))
