# MaiSurge model

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
MaiSurge is a relatively inexpensive physical laparoscopic surgical-training model designed to reproduce complex procedural steps with haptic resistance and electrocautery compatibility.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **A training model should be realistic, cost-effective, and support electrosurgery to enable continuous complex-procedure practice**: The article argues that training models for complex procedures must be realistic enough to cover all surgical steps, inexpensive enough for continuous use, and compatible with electrocautery, because existing commercial models are expensive, mostly nonconductive, or too simple. The MaiSurge model "allows for the use of electrocautery, provides haptic resistance, and is relatively inexpensive to produce." (Brechter Anna Maria et al. (2026))
