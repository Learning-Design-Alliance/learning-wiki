## Description
This element is a published data table of one-year teacher stability rates for Chicago Public Schools unit elementary schools across four fall snapshots (2003-2006). The document states it shows the "Percent of teachers who were still in their CPS schools in the fall of the year listed, compared to the previous fall." Each row lists a school with its four yearly stability percentages, covering hundreds of schools identified by CPS unit number and name.
