# Geographic Concepts Acquisition Test

## Description
A 30-item multiple-choice achievement test developed by AL-Edwan to measure eighth-grade students' acquisition of Natural Resources geography concepts before and after instruction.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Geographic Concepts Acquisition Test (30 multiple-choice items, KR-20 = 0.91)**: A researcher-built instrument measuring eighth-grade acquisition of the geographic concepts in the Natural Resources unit. It began as 34 four-alternative multiple-choice items, was revised after review by ten arbitrators, and "the test in its final form consists of (30) items of Multiple-Choice question" scored from 30 to zero. Reliability was estimated on a 31-student sample using Kuder Richardson KR-20, reaching 0.91, with difficulty coefficients from 0.39 to 0.88 and discrimination coefficients from 0.26 to 0.79. (AL-Edwan (2014))
