# WATASHI-KOKO PROGRAM

## Description
WATASHI-KOKO is a 30-minute video-based online e-learning programme developed with KODOMO-PEER to prepare elementary schoolteachers to recognize and support children of parents with mental illness.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **WATASHI-KOKO 30-minute video e-learning program for elementary schoolteachers**: The WATASHI-KOKO ("We are here") PROGRAM is a 30-min video-based e-learning program for elementary schoolteachers supporting children of parents with mental illness. It has three purposes: gaining basic knowledge, recognizing children in need of support, and gaining confidence. Content was co-created with KODOMO-PEER, "the largest self-help group for adult children of parents with mental illness in Japan", based on a childhood needs survey. Feasibility was high: 83 of 86 intervention participants were very or somewhat satisfied. (Kageyama Masako et al. (2023))
