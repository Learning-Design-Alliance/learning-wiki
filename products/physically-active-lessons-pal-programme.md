# Physically Active Lessons (PAL) programme

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A University of Bristol-developed primary-school programme that integrates short bouts of physical activity with literacy and numeracy instruction for UK pupils.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Cogni-Actif: a co-designed video-based physically active learning program with avatar Ratou**: Cogni-Actif is the PAL program developed through the co-design process: teachers described it as an "easy-to-use and fun way to promote learning through movement" that "combines physical and academic activities into 15-minute video clips". It comprises 24 sessions delivered over 8 weeks (three sessions per week) covering 32 listed academic lesson themes and 14 physical movements classified by intensity, guided by an avatar raccoon named Ratou. Teachers highlighted it as "an end-to-end solution that does not require any additional preparation from them". (Simard et al. (2023))

### Claims
- [The PAL pilot found no differences in literacy or numeracy attainment between intervention and control pupils at post-test](../claims/pal-pilot-no-attainment-differences.md) [+M]
- [Feasibility of implementing PAL in classrooms was mixed: initial enthusiasm faded as teacher workload accumulated](../claims/pal-feasibility-mixed-enthusiasm-faded.md) [~M]
- [Teachers perceive that combining movement with academic content consolidates learning, particularly for practice-friendly concepts such as multiplication tables](../claims/pal-movement-academic-consolidates-learning-perceived.md) [+M]
- [Teachers perceive that the PAL program improves student attention and focus immediately after sessions, an effect that faded over the course of the day](../claims/pal-temporary-attention-focus-benefit.md) [+M]
