MTP-M/S Plus hybrid teacher support system¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A hybrid professional-development supplement to the MyTeachingPartner mathematics and science curricula, developed to support pre-kindergarten teachers through online demonstration videos, teaching challenges with expert feedback, teaching tips, and in-person workshops.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Children in MTP-M/S Plus classrooms made significantly greater geometry and measurement gains across the pre-k year than children in business-as-usual classrooms (g = .52), while Basic-classroom children did not differ from either group [+M]
  • Basic and Plus teachers showed fairly high and statistically similar adherence to the MTP-M/S curricula [~M]
