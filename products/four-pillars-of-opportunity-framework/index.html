Four Pillars of Opportunity framework¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Cook-Harvey et al.'s framework organizes equitable ESSA implementation around access to higher-order learning, multiple equity measures, resource equity, and evidence-based interventions.

Components¶

  • Four Pillars of Opportunity framework for leveraging ESSA toward equity: The report organizes its analysis of ESSA around four pillars of opportunity: "(1) access to learning opportunities focused on higher-order thinking skills; (2) multiple measures of equity; (3) resource equity; and (4) evidence-based interventions." The framework is presented as a bridge from an inequitable "factory model" of schooling relying on rote thinking and memorization toward a 21st-century model offering deeper learning. Each pillar is treated in a dedicated section with policy provisions and state and district examples. (Cook-Harvey et al. (2016))
