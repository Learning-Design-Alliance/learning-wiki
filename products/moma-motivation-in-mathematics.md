# MoMa (Motivation in Mathematics)

## Description
A 90-minute classroom-based mathematics relevance and motivation intervention developed by Gaspard et al. for ninth-grade academic-track students in Germany, combining a psychoeducational presentation with individual relevance-reflection tasks.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MoMa (Motivation in Mathematics) 90-minute classroom-based relevance intervention**: MoMa is a 90-minute classroom-based relevance intervention for ninth-grade academic-track math students in Germany. "The 90-min intervention consists of a psychoeducational presentation for the whole class and relevance-inducing reflection tasks, on which students work individually." The presentation covers research on effort and self-concept in math achievement (targeting growth mindsets and inoculating against backfire effects for low-expectancy students) and examples of mathematics' utility across life domains; students then evaluate six interview quotations from young adults about the usefulness of mathematics for personal relevance. Materials were optimized from the efficacy trial (e.g., traffic-light cards as ice-breaker, take-home message slides, updated daily-life examples). (Gaspard et al. (2021))
