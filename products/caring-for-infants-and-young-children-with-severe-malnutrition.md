## Description
A free interactive scenario-based eLearning course developed by the University of Southampton and the International Malnutrition Task Force to train health professionals in assessing and managing severe acute malnutrition according to WHO guidelines.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **eLearning course 'Caring for infants and young children with severe malnutrition'**: A free, interactive, scenario-based eLearning course developed by the University of Southampton and the International Malnutrition Task Force, "designed around a hypothetical nutrition community in which children and their care are the focus for learning". It "has three modules, each taking about 2–3 hours to complete, and is based on WHO guidelines for the management of severe acute malnutrition". Since being made freely available in 2010 it has been taken by over 16 000 health professionals, trainees and educators in over 120 countries, and in 2018 it was re-implemented for access on mobile devices. (Choi Sunhea et al. (2020))
