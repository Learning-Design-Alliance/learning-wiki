## Description
A human-centred generative-AI digital assistant developed by the Open University to provide provenance-grounded, pedagogically configured learning support, feedback, quiz practice, and study guidance.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **GAIDA and XAIDA expert systems for instructional design**: Two systems designed and implemented under the Air Force AIDA project: 'Guided Approach to Instructional Design Advising (GAIDA) was based on Gagné's (1985) events of instruction and was aimed at extending the abilities of a novice instructional designer', and was 'later adopted for use in Air Force technical training'. XAIDA, based on Merrill's Transaction Theory, 'was aimed at replacing an instructional designer' and 'had success in automatically generating effective lessons for simple declarative and procedural learning tasks'. (Jonathan Michael Spector (2017))
- **AIDA-WP5: a self-consistent resource giving schools and public access to Virtual Observatory data**: AIDA-WP5 is the Work Package 5 resource of the Astronomical Infrastructure for Data Access project, developed within the European Virtual Observatory project. It is "a self-consistent resource offering a set of use cases that are interesting astronomical problems to be solved using free software tools and data". Its aim is to give access to VO data through professional-grade software tools modified to be appealing and easily usable by students, teachers and public, while maintaining the look and feel of professional astronomers' tools. All use-cases and software can be downloaded from the project site. (Freistetter et al. (2011))
