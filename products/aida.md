## Description
A human-centred generative-AI digital assistant developed by the Open University to provide provenance-grounded, pedagogically configured learning support, feedback, quiz practice, and study guidance.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **GAIDA and XAIDA expert systems for instructional design**: Two systems designed and implemented under the Air Force AIDA project: 'Guided Approach to Instructional Design Advising (GAIDA) was based on Gagné's (1985) events of instruction and was aimed at extending the abilities of a novice instructional designer', and was 'later adopted for use in Air Force technical training'. XAIDA, based on Merrill's Transaction Theory, 'was aimed at replacing an instructional designer' and 'had success in automatically generating effective lessons for simple declarative and procedural learning tasks'. (Jonathan Michael Spector (2017))

### Claims
- [In an exploratory randomised trial, the AIDA-supported group showed a higher learning gain than the control group, though the authors caution about the small volunteer sample](../claims/aida-experimental-group-higher-learning-gain.md) [+M]
