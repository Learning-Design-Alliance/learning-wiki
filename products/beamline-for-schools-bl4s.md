# Beamline for Schools (BL4S)

## Description
Beamline for Schools is CERN's annual international competition in which high-school teams propose particle-physics experiments and selected winners conduct them with professional support at an accelerator facility.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Beamline for Schools, CERN's international proposal competition for high school students**: BL4S is "an international competition for high school age students held each year by CERN." Teams propose experiments for a Proton Synchrotron beamline; CERN physicists review proposals and pick 1-2 winners, brought to CERN expenses paid to run their experiment with professional support. Since 2014, eleven teams have won, including one from the United States, and several U.S. teams carried out experiments at the Fermilab Test Beam Facility. (Arce-Larreta et al. (2021))
