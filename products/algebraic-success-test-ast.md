# Algebraic Success Test (AST)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 21-item researcher-developed algebra achievement test used to measure seventh-grade students’ understanding of algebraic expressions and operations, with reported item-analysis and KR-20 reliability evidence.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Algebraic Success Test (21 items, KR-20 = .84)**: A researcher-developed achievement test used as pretest, posttest, and persistency measure. From 30 initial questions, expert review removed seven and item analysis removed two with discrimination below .20, leaving 21 items with "the item difficulty degree changes between .50 and .92  and the discrimination index is between .34 and .63". Average test difficulty was .70 and the KR-20 reliability was .84. (Bal (2016))
