## Description
In a partnership among the University of Pennsylvania's Graduate School of Education, Digital Promise, and the University of Florida, PennGSE offers a Data Science Methods for Digital Learning Platforms certificate. The "17-week program, with modules facilitated by 14 experts in different areas of learning analytics, provides learners with hands-on experience" applying intermediate and advanced data science methods on real DLP datasets. Five cohorts run over three years, the first beginning February 2024, at no cost via IES Grant R305B230007.

### Context
#### Requirements
- Funding from IES (Grant R305B230007 to the University of Pennsylvania) enabling no-cost participation
#### Constraints
- Five cohorts of fellows over three years, with the first cohort beginning in February 2024

### Target Learners
- emerging scholars and future researchers in learning analytics
- diverse set of new researchers

### Target Learning Goals
- applying intermediate and advanced data science methods to digital learning platform datasets
- developing professional identity in equity-oriented data science
