# Linked Learning Alliance two-tiered certification system

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A certification framework run by the Linked Learning Alliance to evaluate and support secondary-school Linked Learning pathways combining academics, career technical education, work-based learning, and student support services.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Linked Learning Alliance two-tiered certification system for Linked Learning pathways in secondary schools**: The Linked Learning Alliance, a California-based nonprofit, supports a two-tiered certification process for Linked Learning pathways—combining rigorous academics, career technical education, work-based learning, and comprehensive student support services tied to the local community. Several hundred schools are supported through a process offering "multiple opportunities for collaboration among evaluators and pathway staff," an accessible platform for artifact collection and self-study, regular feedback on submitted materials, and codeveloped improvement goals after certification concludes. (Germain et al. (2025))
