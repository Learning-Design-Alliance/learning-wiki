## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Compass Project's four cultural values framework**: The article names four cultural values central to the Compass community: iterative problem-solving, collaboration, ownership, and supporting the whole person. Problem-solving is iterative because "we try a solution, observe the results, and use those observations to improve our solution." Collaboration and personal ownership are balanced by developing solutions "as a group while maintaining individual pride over our work." The framework is applied in dual contexts: the Compass classroom and the Compass organization, so a single set of values informs course structure and organizational decision-making alike. (Badr F. Albanna et al. (2012))
- **Compass Project program suite (summer program, fall and spring courses, mentoring)**: Compass is a student-run organization founded in 2006 at UC Berkeley providing a summer program, fall and spring seminar courses, a mentoring program, tutoring, undergraduate-oriented research colloquia, and leadership opportunities. Its "services exist as optional supplements to the required curriculum" and women, minority, and first-generation college students are especially encouraged to participate. Courses are co-taught by teams pairing veteran with new teachers, use student-driven small-group discussions with Modeling Discourse Management and Think-Pair-Share, and serve dual purposes of developing physics disciplinary skills and college-success behaviors. (Badr F. Albanna et al. (2012))
