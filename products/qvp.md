## Description
QVP is a standardized questionnaire developed by the study team to measure undergraduate nursing students' perceptions, acceptance, and user experience of virtual-patient simulation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **QVP design grounded in Technology Acceptance Model and Unified Theory of Acceptance and Use of Technology**: The QVP's design of standardized, comparable measures was "inspired by the Technology Acceptance Model [38] and the Unified Theory of Acceptance and Use of Technology [39] focusing on aspects such as user-friendliness, clarity of instructions and overall experiences using virtual simulation with virtual patient." These frameworks shaped the questionnaire's two themes and its mix of Likert-scale, slider-scale, and open-ended items. The article reports that students' generally positive perceptions align with this theoretical foundation. (Mårtensson S et al. (2026))
