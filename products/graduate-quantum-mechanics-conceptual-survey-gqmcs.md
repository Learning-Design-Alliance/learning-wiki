# Graduate Quantum Mechanics Conceptual Survey (GQMCS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A 24-question essay-style survey developed by L. D. Carr and colleagues to assess graduate students’ conceptual understanding of quantum mechanics.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Survey of graduate quantum mechanics concepts administered to 202 students at seven universities**: A written survey instrument probing fundamental quantum mechanics concepts, administered "at the beginning of a first-year, first-semester/quarter graduate quantum mechanics course". During design the author "consulted three faculty members at Pitt who had taught quantum mechanics" and "iterated the survey several times before coming up with the final version". It covers allowed wave functions, time-dependence of wave functions, probability of measuring energy, and expectation value of energy. (Chandralekha Singh (2016))

### Claims
- [Students scored higher on the GQMCS in reformed course years, with year 3 (most comprehensive textbook) highest](../claims/gqmcs-scores-improved-reformed-years.md) [+M]
- [GQMCS conceptual scores correlate strongly with final exam scores (Pearson correlation coefficient of 0.74) even though the final was 75% calculations](../claims/gqmcs-final-exam-correlation-0-74.md) [+M]
