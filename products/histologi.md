## Description
Histologi@ is a cloud-accessible virtual 3D microscopy slide box developed by the authors for undergraduate medical and dental education in cytology, histology, and microscopic anatomy.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Histologi@ virtual 3D slide box for microscopic anatomy**: Histologi@ is a virtual 3D slide box containing 197 virtual 3D slides digitized from the department's glass slide collection with a research-grade microscope, capturing x-, y-, and z-axis (focus plane) information. Students "can directly magnify the virtual slides and change the focus plane, as was previously common practice with analog microscopy". It is accessible via cloud on personal devices or laboratory workstations using the Biolucida Cloud Viewer, and all 3D virtual slides can be obtained for free from the authors. (Sternecker K et al. (2026))
