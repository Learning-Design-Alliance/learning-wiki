Reimagining Excellence blueprint¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A seven-dimension framework from Reimagining Excellence for integrating equity, social-emotional well-being, and academic learning across K–12 in-person, remote, and hybrid programs.

Components¶

  • Seven-dimension blueprint for integrating equity, well-being, and academics in learning programs: The blueprint organizes program quality into seven overarching dimensions — equity, responsive relationships, climate and culture, instruction, instructional materials, professional learning, and leadership — each with specific indicators of success. It states that "The blueprint has seven overarching dimensions of quality that apply to all programs, and each dimension has specific indicators of success." The dimensions build upon each other, beginning with equity, and are interconnected with common themes and practices throughout. It applies to the full range of program formats, using in-person, remote, and hybrid as its distinguishing terms. (Reimagining Excellence: A blueprint for integrating social et al. (2021))
