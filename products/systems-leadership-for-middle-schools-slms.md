## Description
A Modern Red Schoolhouse whole-school school-improvement programme that organizes middle-school staff into Action Teams and a Guiding Coalition, supported by facilitators, to build distributed leadership and organizational capacity.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Systems Leadership for Middle Schools (SLMS) intervention: six Action Teams, a Guiding Coalition, and three support dimensions**: SLMS is a whole-school intervention developed by Modern Red Schoolhouse that reorganizes a school's teachers and administrators onto six Action Teams, each headed by a teacher who also serves on the school's Guiding Coalition. Teams focus on family and community partnerships, curriculum and instruction, data analysis, technology, professional development, and school climate and culture, and are meant to replace standing and ad hoc committees. The program "is designed to provide support for middle school staff to effectively devise their own solutions to contextually specific problems" through three dimensions: structure, process, and technical knowledge, with MRSH facilitators providing on-site support during three-day visits. (Borman et al. (2013))
