## Description

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The NinU framework integrates four science-education learning goals with three dimensions of inclusive pedagogy to plan inclusive science lessons**: The NinU framework, developed by a German network for inclusive science education, provides a new perspective by "systematically combining four major learning goals in science education" (reasoning about relevant scientific issues, learning scientific content, doing science, learning about science) "with three dimensions of inclusive pedagogy" (acknowledging diversity, recognizing barriers, enabling participation). The two sets "need to be understood as interwoven", each subcategory of one linked to each subcategory of the other, with guiding questions in each category to support planning and reflection on lessons pursuing scientific literacy for all. (Lenzer et al. (2024))
