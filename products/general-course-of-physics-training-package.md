> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A set of general-physics laboratory workshop manuals and instructor guidelines developed by Kurbanbekova et al. for undergraduate students at pedagogical universities, combining step-by-step laboratory procedures with creative mini-investigation tasks.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Training package of manuals for the general physics laboratory course with mini-investigation tasks**: The authors developed training manuals, 'Workshop on general physics' and 'Guidelines for the implementation of tasks for students’ independent work under control in the course of general physics', as components of a General Course of Physics Training Package. The workshop is designed to help students be more aware of basic physical laws and acquire basic experimentation skills, with guidelines describing step-by-step actions in each lab. The guidelines for independent work carry the creative tasks for mini investigations that form experimental research competencies. The article reports the developed materials can be partially used within the traditional system of higher education. (Bakytzhan A. Kurbanbekova et al. (2016))
