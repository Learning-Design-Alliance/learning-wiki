## Description
A collection of readings, groupwork activities, homework problems, and assessment materials for teaching dimensional analysis in introductory life-science-major physics, made available through the Living Physics Portal.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Supplementary DA problems and the Living Physics Portal resource collection**: The article is accompanied by supplementary materials containing a groupwork lesson, a longer homework problem on DA (linking DA and stoichiometry), and an exam problem. A more extensive collection of readings and activities from the project on dimensional analysis is available via the Living Physics Portal, searchable under Making Meaning with Mathematics: Dimensional analysis. (Edward F. Redish (2020))
