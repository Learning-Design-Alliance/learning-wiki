Metacognitive Awareness Listening Questionnaire (MALQ)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The MALQ is a 21-item Likert-scale questionnaire adapted from Vandergrift et al. for measuring university English-language learners’ metacognitive awareness of listening strategies.

Components¶

  • Metacognitive Awareness Listening Questionnaire (MALQ) as strategy measurement instrument: The study measured listening strategies with the Metacognitive Awareness Listening Questionnaire, adapted from Vandergrift et al. (2006), described as quantitative data using a 6-point Likert scale chosen "so that students cannot hedge in answering the questionnaire." It contained 21 items about listening strategies excluding demographic data, and was interpreted using SPSS. The strategies it covers comprise planning and evaluation, directed attention, person knowledge, mental translation, and problem solving. (Aminuddin Hassan et al. (2014))

Claims¶

  • No significant difference in metacognitive awareness listening strategy use between online and traditional classroom learners [~W]
  • Online learners reported stronger directed attention but also more giving up when comprehension failed, compared with traditional classroom learners [~W]
  • Online learners reported more mental translation while listening than traditional classroom learners [~W]
