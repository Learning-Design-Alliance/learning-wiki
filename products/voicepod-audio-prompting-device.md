## Description
VoicePod is a portable digital recording-and-playback system made by Attainment Company Inc. for delivering reusable recorded prompts during instructional activities.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **VoicePod audio prompting device for naïve peer tutors**: The VoicePod (Attainment Company Inc., 2006) is "a portable digital recording and playback system that includes multiple, reusable cards." Each card holds a printed text prompt behind a plastic sleeve and an activation strip that plays a recording of up to 10 s when inserted into the pod. In this study eight cards per target word carried the word, definition, example sentences, and fill-in-the-blank items, giving naïve tutors audio models so they could provide correct modeling and feedback. The device was chosen because it was transportable, easily adapted, and cost efficient (about $60 each). (Mackiewicz et al. (2011))
