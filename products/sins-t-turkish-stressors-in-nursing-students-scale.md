## Description
A 41-item, four-factor Likert self-report scale developed and validated for measuring stress levels and stressor domains among Turkish undergraduate nursing students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SINS-T: Turkish Stressors in Nursing Students scale (41 items, four factors)**: The SINS-T is a 5-point Likert instrument (1 = not stressful at all to 5 = extremely stressful) with 41 items across four factors: Education (14 items), Relationships (13 items), Confidence (9 items), and Uncertainties (5 items). Scores range from 41 to 205, and "as the score increases, nursing students' stress levels also increase." In this study its Cronbach's α was 0.99, which the authors note may reflect item redundancy. (Ozdemir Nur Guven et al. (2025))
