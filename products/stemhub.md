## Description
STEMHub is a modular AI-enhanced personalised and adaptive learning platform developed by Nwogo et al. for context-aware tutoring, adaptive assessment, and bilingual English–Nigerian Pidgin content delivery in resource-constrained educational settings.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **AI-enhanced personalised and adaptive learning platform: layered architecture integrating fine-tuned Pidgin-aware LLM, RAG, bilingual translation engine, and rule-based adaptive assessment**: A modular, layered educational platform (presentation, application, AI services, data, and integration layers) delivering conversational AI, adaptive assessment, and multilingual content delivery. Its chatbot operates in Translate, Ask, and Ask/Translate modes, with web-based retrieval triggered "when the similarity score falls below a 60% threshold". RAG grounds LLM outputs in a vector database (Qdrant) to address static curricula and hallucination-prone generation, and the full implementation is publicly accessible as STEMHub. (Nwogo et al. (2026))
