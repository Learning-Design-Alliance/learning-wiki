## Description
ANet is a school-improvement programme run by Achievement Network that combines interim assessments, data tools, leader coaching, and peer-school networks to support data-based instruction in mathematics and English language arts.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ANet's data-based instructional program model: interim assessments, data tools, leader coaching, and peer networks organized around a data cycle**: ANet is a school-level intervention built on the belief that timely interim assessment data, leader support and structures, and teacher knowledge of how to help struggling students will make educators more effective at closing learning gaps. During the evaluation it had "four key components": quarterly standards-aligned interim assessments in math and ELA for Grades 3-8, data tools with individual student progress reports via the MyANet platform, coaching of school leaders, and a geographic network of peer schools sharing results and joint professional development. Its work is organized around a data cycle whose key leverage point is data review and action planning meetings, with coaches initially partnering with leaders who are then expected to take ownership. The logic model was later revised to add school structures as a key mediator and renamed the teaching and learning cycle. (Martin R. West et al. (2016))
