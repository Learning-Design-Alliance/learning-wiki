# Native Skywatchers indigenous star maps (Ojibwe and D(L)akota)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Collaboratively created Indigenous star-map artworks and astronomy teaching resources developed through the Native Skywatchers initiative for community members, educators, and astronomy learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Ojibwe and D(L)akota native star maps as astronomically accurate artworks communicating an indigenous perspective of the night sky**: Two native star maps created in 2012 are the foundation of the initiative's work: the Ojibwe Giizhiig Anung Masinaaigan, painted by the author and William Wilson based on Carl Gawboy's unpublished research, and the D(L)akota Makoċe Wiċaŋḣpi Wowapi, painted by the author with language expert Jim Rock based on Ron Goodman's published work. The article states the maps are "astronomically accurate and visual works of art created to communicate an indigenous perspective of the night sky." Both center on the north celestial pole and North Star, with seasonal stars arranged around the center and Greek constellations lightly painted for common ground. (Lee (2016))
