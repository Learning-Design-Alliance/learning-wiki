## Description
A digital training prototype for CAMHS crisis team clinicians that develops relational, culturally responsive crisis-care competencies through a Theory of Change linking training inputs, activities, process outputs, and intended impacts.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Theory of Change-based logic model linking crisis team competencies to desired outcomes**: The article integrates consensus findings into a Theory of Change informed logic model that "postulated how the desirable knowledge and skills related to both intermediate outputs and activities, and, ultimately the most valued outcomes." Outcomes were further categorised into 'activities', 'outputs' (process outcomes) and 'outcomes' (impacts) in accordance with the Theory of Change framework. This model serves as the pathway from training inputs to impact for the SCriPT digital training prototype. (Clau N et al. (2026))
