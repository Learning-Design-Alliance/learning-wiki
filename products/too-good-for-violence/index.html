Too Good for Violence¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A scripted K–8 school-based character-education curriculum developed by the Mendez Foundation, using role-playing, cooperative learning, group activities, and classroom discussions to promote character values and social-emotional skills.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • WWC improvement indices for Too Good for Violence are +18 percentile points for behavior and +16 for knowledge, attitudes, and values [+W]
  • Too Good for Violence shows a substantively important but not statistically significant effect on students' knowledge, attitudes, and values [~W]
  • Too Good for Violence produces statistically significant positive effects on elementary students' behavior 20 weeks after the program ends [+W]
