## Description
A four-year longitudinal dataset of social-emotional learning scores used to fit growth models for three constructs related to transitioning to secondary school. The article describes it as "four years of longitudinal SEL data from students in a large urban district with a sample size of about 3,000 students per timepoint." The dataset supports comparing a student's mean 6th-grade SEL score against growth between 6th and 8th grade as predictors of being off track academically in 9th grade.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Longitudinal SEL measures collected across four years
- Coverage of students in a large urban district across intermediate and early secondary grades
#### Constraints
- Sample size of about 3,000 students per timepoint as reported
- Drawn from a single large urban district, limiting generalizability beyond that setting

### Target Learners
- Students in grades 6-9 in a large urban district

### Target Learning Goals
- Modeling SEL growth trajectories
- Predicting 9th-grade academic off-track status
