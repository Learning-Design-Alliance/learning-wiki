REACH (Recognizing Educators Advancing Chicago's Students)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 32 claims (19 for, 9 mixed, 4 against) · 11 studies (9 associational, 1 design, 1 theoretical), q1–q3 · 0 of 11 report an effect size · 28 claims rest on one study

Description¶

REACH is Chicago Public Schools' teacher-evaluation programme combining classroom observations using a modified Danielson Framework rubric with student-growth measures and evaluator certification.

Components¶

  • REACH Students teacher evaluation system: REACH (Recognizing Educators Advancing Chicago's Students) is CPS's teacher evaluation system launched in 2012-13, replacing a 1970s-era checklist. It combines "multiple classroom observations" using a modified Danielson Framework rubric with student growth measures from Performance Tasks and value-added or expected gains scores. All administrators must be certified as evaluators through training modules and assessments. In its first year it applied with stakes to non-tenured teachers, generating over 36,000 observations. (Susan E. Sporte et al. (2013))
  • REACH (Recognizing Educators Advancing Chicago's Students) teacher evaluation system: REACH is Chicago Public Schools' teacher evaluation system, which "replaced the previous checklist system, which rated nearly all teachers as excellent or superior and failed to provide much useful feedback for improving teacher practice." It combines classroom observation with student growth measures, with growth accounting for up to 25 percent of a teacher's rating in 2012-13 and 2013-14. The brief measures teacher and administrator perceptions of the system across its first two years. (Jiang (2014))
  • REACH teacher evaluation system: REACH (Recognizing Educators Advancing Chicago Students) is Chicago Public Schools' teacher evaluation system launched in fall 2012. It replaced a checklist system with multiple observations rated on a detailed rubric, and "Final evaluation scores are based on a combination of these observations and student growth metrics" including performance tasks and value-added scores. Professional practice scores are combined with student growth scores into an overall REACH score ranging from 100 to 400, translated into ratings of Unsatisfactory, Developing, Proficient, or Excellent. (Sporte et al. (2016))
  • REACH Students teacher evaluation system combining professional practice and student growth measures: REACH (Recognizing Educators Advancing Chicago's Students) is CPS's teacher evaluation system instituted in fall 2012, replacing a 1970s-era checklist system. It "incorporates a professional practice score and up to two measures of student growth in a teacher's evaluation score." Professional practice is evaluated through four observations using the CPS Framework for Teaching, a modified version of the Danielson Framework, with 19 components across four domains. Student growth includes value-added scores and performance tasks. (Jiang et al. (2016))
