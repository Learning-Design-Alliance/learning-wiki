Intercultural Sensitivity Scale (ISS)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The Intercultural Sensitivity Scale is a five-point Likert self-report assessment developed by Chen and Starosta and adapted into Turkish by Bulduk, Tosun, and Ardıç to measure perceived intercultural sensitivity.

Components¶

  • Intercultural Sensitivity Scale (Chen & Starosta, 2000; Turkish adaptation by Bulduk, Tosun and Ardıç, 2011): A five-point Likert data collection tool used to measure students' intercultural sensitivity, developed by Chen and Starosta (2000) and adapted into Turkish by Bulduk, Tosun and Ardıç (2011). The article reports that in the adaptation study the Cronbach's Alpha internal consistency coefficient was 0.72, while in this study Cronbach's Alpha was found 0.86. Although the original scale comprises five dimensions, the Turkish adaptation's item distribution was inconsistent with the original, so the scale was used unidimensionally with 24 items scored from 24 to 120 points. (Tuncel (2019))
