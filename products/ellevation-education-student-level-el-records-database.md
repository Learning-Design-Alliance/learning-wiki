# Ellevation Education student-level EL records database (2016-17 to 2023-24, four states)

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
The study's data source is a database constructed in partnership with Ellevation Education, "an EL-centered education technology platform that facilitates administrative record keeping, EL program management, and tracking of EL students' progress toward English language proficiency." It contains individual-level records from the 2016-17 to 2023-24 academic years covering ELP scores, state assessments, demographics, grade levels, and EL reclassification status, merged with state-level reclassification policy documents. States remain anonymous at the partners' request.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Access is provided in partnership with Ellevation Education; districts in the database are anonymized.
#### Constraints
- Anonymized districts make it impossible to know their specific reclassification policy, so State B districts are assumed to follow the state-wide automatic reclassification approach.

### Target Learners
- English Learner (EL) students in districts across four states

### Target Learning Goals
- Tracking EL students' progress toward English language proficiency and reclassification outcomes
