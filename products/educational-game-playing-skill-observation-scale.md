# Educational Game Playing Skill Observation Scale

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A trained-observer video-based Likert scale developed by Acar Eyup et al. to assess pre-service coaches' and physical-education teachers' educational game-playing competencies across preparation, implementation, and evaluation phases.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Educational Game Playing Skill Observation Scale with preparation, game implementation, and evaluation sub-dimensions**: A standardised observation instrument used to measure pre-service educators' game playing competencies during video-recorded implementations. The scale "consists of three sub-dimensions: Preparation Phase (5 items), Game Phase (19 items), and Evaluation Phase (4 items)", each scored on a 4-point Likert scale with totals from 30 to 120. Internal consistency in this study was Cronbach's alpha = 0.92; inter-rater Cohen's kappa ranged 0.85-0.91 across sub-dimensions. Representative items map to organisational, tactical intervention, and reflective CCK. (Acar Eyup et al. (2026))
