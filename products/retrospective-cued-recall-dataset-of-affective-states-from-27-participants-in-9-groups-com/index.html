Retrospective cued-recall dataset of affective states from 27 participants in 9 groups completing the Weights Task¶

Description¶

A research dataset created by Sifatul Anindho et al. containing video-linked, timestamped retrospective cued-recall reports of seven affective states from 27 adults completing the Weights Task in groups.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Curious, optimistic, and confused are the most frequently reported affective states, and self-caught reports are more evenly distributed than probe-caught reports [+M]
