Shared Measures Construct Map¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A one-page construct-map instrument in a practical measurement guide that defines six dimensions, sub-constructs, and relationships for monitoring ELA and math curriculum-shift implementation.

Components¶

  • Shared Measures Construct Map instrument for ELA and math curriculum shifts: The instrument is a one-page construct map titled "Shared Measures Construct Map" within a practical guide for measurement and progress monitoring of ELA and math curriculum shifts. It prints six numbered dimensions, each with a construct statement and lettered sub-constructs, and a KEY that defines "(Sub-constructs)", marks "Unidirectional" and "Bidirectional" relations, and flags "Items specific to ELA and Math". It is designed so instructional leaders can monitor implementation progress across the system. (Assessing ELA and Math Curriculum Shifts: A Practical Guide for Measurement and Progress M)
