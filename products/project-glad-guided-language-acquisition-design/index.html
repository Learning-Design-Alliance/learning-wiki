Project GLAD (Guided Language Acquisition Design)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Project GLAD is a K–12 sheltered-instruction programme developed and delivered through structured teacher training and coaching to support English learners' academic English and grade-level content learning.

Components¶

  • Project GLAD: a multi-component K-12 sheltered instruction model of 35 strategies in four components: Project GLAD (Guided Language Acquisition Design) is described as "a multi-component K-12 instructional model designed to build academic English and grade-level content knowledge for students at varying levels of English language proficiency." Its "35 individual instructional strategies ... fall into four broad components": motivation strategies that set behavior expectations and build interest, input strategies presenting information in multiple formats, guided oral practice strategies scaffolding vocabulary through cooperative learning, and reading and writing strategies scaffolding academic literacy. The strategies intentionally build in differentiated instruction for varying English proficiency. (Theresa Deussen et al. (2014))

Claims¶

  • English learners showed marginally significant positive Year 1 effects on reading comprehension and vocabulary under Project GLAD [~M]
  • Project GLAD teacher training produced no statistically significant Year 1 improvement in fifth-grade reading comprehension or vocabulary for all students [-M]
