# Modified Fishman Augmented Feedback Observation Guide

## Description
A researcher-modified observation instrument based on Fishman's Augmented Feedback Observation Guide for trained observers to code the form, direction, timing, intent, and referents of instructional feedback.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Modified Fishman Augmented Feedback Observation Guide for coding clinical teaching feedback**: The study measured teacher feedback with "a slightly modified version of Augmented Feedback Observation Guide by Fishman", adding the subcategories auditory tactile, auditory visual, and no space. The tool codes feedback on Form, Direction, Time, Intent, General Referent and Specific Referent categories, and was originally designed for physical education motor-learning instruction but "was easily transferred for application with instruction of clinical skills". Validity was established through expert panel review and reliability showed 91.98% mean self-agreement, with 90% intra-observer agreement between investigators. (Andrews et al. (2011))
