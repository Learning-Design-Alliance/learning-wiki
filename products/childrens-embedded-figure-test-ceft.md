## Description
A paper-and-pencil assessment developed by Karp and Konstadt that measures children's field-dependence or field-independence through embedded-figure identification.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Children's Embedded Figure Test (CEFT) as the cognitive-style moderator measure**: The CEFT is a paper-and-pencil test "designed to determine cognitive style of a subject in the dimension of field-dependence or field-independence," normed for five to twelve year olds. Subjects locate an equilateral triangle and house shapes embedded within 25 larger pictures in 30 minutes, after a ten-minute practice trial, yielding quantitative scores from 0 to 25; scores of 0-11 indicate field-dependence and 12-25 field-independence. In this study it served as the moderator variable classifying the 248 preschoolers. (Kien Tsong Chau et al. (2018))
