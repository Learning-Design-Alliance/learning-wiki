PennGSE Data Science Methods for Digital Learning Platforms¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A 17-week certificate programme run by the University of Pennsylvania Graduate School of Education, Digital Promise, and the University of Florida that provides hands-on training in data-science methods using digital-learning-platform datasets.

Components¶

  • PennGSE Data Science Methods for Digital Learning Platforms certificate program: In a partnership among the University of Pennsylvania's Graduate School of Education, Digital Promise, and the University of Florida, PennGSE offers a Data Science Methods for Digital Learning Platforms certificate. The "17-week program, with modules facilitated by 14 experts in different areas of learning analytics, provides learners with hands-on experience" applying intermediate and advanced data science methods on real DLP datasets. Five cohorts run over three years, the first beginning February 2024, at no cost via IES Grant R305B230007. (Pautz Stephenson et al. (2023))
