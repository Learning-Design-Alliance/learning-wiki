# Twins Early Development Study (TEDS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A UK longitudinal twin cohort dataset following individuals born in 1994–1996 across childhood, adolescence, and early adulthood, maintained by the TEDS research team and available to researchers under its data-access policy.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Twins Early Development Study (TEDS) longitudinal cohort and STEM career choice measure**: TEDS is a UK longitudinal twin cohort born 1994–1996, representative of England and Wales in ethnicity and SES, with a 76.5% response rate by early adulthood. The study used waves at ~1.5 years, ~16, ~18–21 and ~21–24, randomly selecting one twin per pair. STEM career choice was self-reported degree/apprenticeship choices at age 21, classified via the HESA HECoS coding system and coded dichotomously. (Ferdinand et al. (2024))
