## Description
A 100-point analytic speaking-performance assessment developed by Kanatlar that scores grammar, vocabulary, fluency, intelligibility, and task achievement in oral interviews.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Kanatlar (2005) Speaking Grading Scale for oral performance assessment**: The study used the Speaking Grading Scale (SGS) developed by Kanatlar (2005) to score learners' oral interview performance. The scale "included five different sections to score such as grammar, vocabulary, fluency, intelligibility and task achievement," with a 100-point maximum: 30 points each for grammar and vocabulary, 20 for fluency, and 10 each for intelligibility and task achievement. Two trained research assistants scored pre- and post-test interviews, achieving acceptable reliability (Cronbach's alpha .80–.88). (Guzel et al. (2016))
