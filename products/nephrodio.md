## Description
NephrOdio is an open-access nephrology podcast series developed by the French College of Nephrology Professors (CUEN) to support medical students' preparation for nephrology clinical competencies and OSCEs.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **NephrOdio open-access nephrology OSCE preparation podcast series**: NephrOdio is an open-access academic podcast series developed by the French College of Nephrology Professors (CUEN), freely available on several platforms. Its second season, used as the trial intervention, dedicated "each of the 16 episodes... to one essential nephrological clinical competency for OSCE preparation", with "a median duration of 7 min" per episode. (Dupont V et al. (2025))
