# DEPTH online open-access educational resource

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
DEPTH is an evidence-informed resource developed by O’Brien and hosted on the National Forum’s National Resource Hub to guide health-science educators in selecting digital tools for soft- and hard-skill acquisition.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **DEPTH online open-access educational resource (OER) guiding digital tool selection for skill acquisition**: The study findings informed the development of an online, open-access educational resource named DEPTH. It is described as "an online, open-access, evidence-informed tool that guides educators in selecting appropriate digital tools for soft and hard skill acquisition", hosted on the National Forum’s National Resource Hub after free registration. It was developed to facilitate wider reach and impact of the study’s findings on technology-supported practical skill teaching. (O’Brien A (2023))
