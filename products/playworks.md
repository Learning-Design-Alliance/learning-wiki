# Playworks

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 11 claims (10 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (3 causal), `q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 11 claims rest on one study

## Description
Playworks is a school-based programme run by the Playworks nonprofit that places trained full-time coaches in schools to organize inclusive, active play during recess and throughout the school day.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Playworks program (full-time recess coaches in low-income schools)**: Playworks is an initiative of the Robert Wood Johnson Foundation that "places full-time coaches in low-income schools to provide opportunities for organized play during recess and throughout the school day." The evaluation covered implementation and impacts across 25 schools using random assignment, conducted by Mathematica and the John W. Gardner Center at Stanford University. (Martha Bleeker et al. (2012))
- **Playworks school-based recess program**: Playworks is a school-based program that guides students in organized activities during recess and makes improvements to the recess play yard. In the evaluation described here, it was delivered for one school year in schools randomly assigned to receive it, and its effects on boys' and girls' physical activity were measured with accelerometers and recess observations. The article describes it as one of the "School-based programs, such as Playworks" that might increase physical activity, especially for girls. (Martha Bleeker et al. (2015))
