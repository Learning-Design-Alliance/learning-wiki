# European Geriatric Medicine Specialty Exam (EGeMSE)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The EGeMSE is a UEMS-GMS examination developed with the British Geriatrics Society and the UK Royal Colleges to assess theoretical geriatric-medicine knowledge in doctors in training and qualified specialists.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **European Geriatric Medicine Specialty Exam (EGeMSE) as a pan-European knowledge benchmark**: The EGeMSE is a knowledge-based online examination organised by the UEMS-GMS in cooperation with the British Geriatrics Society and the Federation of Royal Colleges of Physicians of the UK. It "consists of two three-hour online papers in English, each comprising 100 best-of-five multiple-choice questions", and successful candidates receive the post-nominal title ESE (Geriatric Medicine). The 2025 ETR formally endorses it as a pan-European benchmark of theoretical knowledge supporting harmonised certification. The inaugural exam took place on April 23, 2025. (Romero Ortuño R et al. (2026))
