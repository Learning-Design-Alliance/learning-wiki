## Description
The Seestar S50 is a portable smart-telescope system made by ZWO that combines telescope imaging hardware with app control and image export for astronomical observation and analysis.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Seestar S50 portable smart telescope as an all-in-one classroom imaging instrument**: The Seestar S50 combines a small telescope, camera, alt-azimuth mount, focusing, app control, and image export in a portable system. The article lists the classroom working values used in its source materials: a 50 mm aperture, 250 mm focal length, native 1920 x 1080 images, a field of view of about 1.29 deg x 0.73 deg, and a working plate scale near 2.4-2.42 arcsec/pixel. It is the instrument around which all eight laboratories were developed. (Roger M. Hart (2026))
