# EDI program (Would you like to travel around the planet of emotions?)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A two-year school-based emotional-intelligence programme for 10–12-year-old children, developed in Spain and delivered by trained psychologists using experiential, psychodrama, cognitive-behavioral, discussion, storytelling, and related activities.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **EDI program: a two-year school-based emotional intelligence program for children aged 10–12 based on the Bar-On model**: The EDI program (Would you like to travel around the planet of emotions?) is a school-based EI program for children between 10 and 12 years old designed in Spain, structured as a journey guided by a comic character from Emotiko planet. It uses "experiential methodology with psychophysical, psychodrama, and systemic techniques, as well as some cognitive-behavioral techniques" including film forum, storytelling, drama, group discussion, case studies, relaxation, and music therapy. It was delivered over sixteen weeks per academic year during homeroom time by trained psychologists. (Cantero María-José et al. (2020))
