## Description
openBIS is an open-source electronic laboratory-notebook and research-data-management platform, developed for organizing experimental metadata and data with programmatic access.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **openBIS electronic lab notebook deployment in the electronics practicum with structured free-text fields and Python data access**: The university's open-source electronic lab notebook (openBIS) was introduced in the 4th-semester electronics practicum in spring 2022. Teams of two share a lab-book space pre-structured by a Python script into projects, experiments and experimental steps; each step carries three free-text fields for "Ziele des Experiments", "Experimentbeschreibung" and "Dokumentation der Resultate". Metadata and raw data are merged in one place during experimenting, and a supplied Python module loads data from the ELN into Jupyter with a single program line. (M. Krieger et al. (2022))
