MindUP PreK¶

Description¶

MindUP PreK is a mindfulness-based social-emotional learning programme developed by the Goldie Hawn Foundation for preschool children, supported by teacher training, community-of-practice meetings, coaching, and classroom materials.

Components¶

  • MindUP PreK mindfulness-based SEL program with four elements: curriculum, teacher training, community of practice, and coaching: MindUP PreK (The Goldie Hawn Foundation) is a mindfulness-based SEL program for preschoolers being tested in a cluster randomized trial. The program "consists of four main elements: the mindfulness-based SEL preschool classroom curriculum for students, the curriculum training for teachers, and two additional implementation supports for teachers—monthly community of practice meetings and coaching sessions." The curriculum comprises 15 lessons in three units (Mindful Me; Mindful Senses; Mindful Me in the World) plus a daily core practice. The community of practice consists of monthly hour-long small-group meetings, and coaching comprises monthly 30-minute one-on-one check-in calls adapted from the Practice Based Coaching framework. (Choles JR et al. (2022))
