# TestingWisely

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
TestingWisely is an online game developed to train medical learners and practitioners to estimate diagnostic probabilities and apply Bayesian updating through short case-based questions, immediate feedback, tips, scores, and peer comparisons.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **TestingWisely online diagnostic-reasoning game**: A publicly available online game (https://bird.testingwisely.com/) developed iteratively with clinician feedback to train estimation of post-test probability. The article describes that it "requires users to estimate probability of diagnosis based on 10 simplified case presentations", takes 5 minutes or less per game, and delivers "immediate feedback on each question ... using natural frequency methods with a score, tips, and comparisons to other users". It was the core of the 45-minute GBL intervention in the pilot RCT. (Morgan Daniel J. et al. (2024))
