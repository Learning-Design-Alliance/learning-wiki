## Description
SalsaJ is a free, open-source ImageJ-based astronomy image-analysis platform developed through the Hands-On Universe project for middle and high school and introductory college astronomy learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SalsaJ, a free ImageJ-based astronomy image-analysis platform for middle and high school classrooms**: SalsaJ is an open-source, free web platform software built on the ImageJ codebase, developed for the Hands-On Universe project and targeted at middle and high schools. It is described as "student-friendly and developed specifically for the HOU project", allowing students to "display, analyze, and explore professionally obtained astronomical images". The interface was modified from ImageJ to be more pupil-friendly, with colour icons and simplified menus, and it runs on Windows, Linux and Mac OS, handling 8-bit to 32-bit images and formats including DICOM, FITS and raw professional science data. (Doran et al. (2012))
