# The Colorado Classical Mechanics/Math Methods Instrument (CCMI)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
The CCMI is a nine-question open-ended assessment developed by CU Boulder researchers to measure sophomore-level physics students' learning of core classical mechanics and mathematical methods.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The Colorado Classical Mechanics/Math Methods Instrument (CCMI), a 9-question open-ended assessment for middle-division mechanics**: The CCMI is a research-based assessment instrument developed at CU Boulder to probe student learning in the first half of a two-semester classical mechanics / math methods sequence. The article states that "The CCMI is a 9-question open-ended test that focuses on topics taught in the first half of a two-semester classical mechanics sequence," built on faculty-consensus learning goals and observed student difficulties. It is designed to fit a standard 50-minute lecture period, with a shorter 15-20 minute pre-test containing three problems from the post-test. Questions cover skills such as solving common differential equations, finding equilibria on potential energy maps, and decomposing vectors in Cartesian and plane-polar coordinates. (Marcos D. Caballero et al. (2013))
