# CyberAGENTS

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A custom orchestrator-worker framework developed by Hornung et al. that coordinates Challenge, Buddy, Critic, and Reward agents to deliver adaptive cybersecurity learning challenges, support, feedback, and progression.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CyberAGENTS four-agent learning loop: Challenge, Buddy, Critic, and Reward agents**: CyberAGENTS decomposes the gamified learning loop into four specialized agents coordinated by a central orchestrator. The article states "the framework decomposes gameplay into four specialized agents: ChallengeAgent,BuddyAgent,CriticAgent, andRewardAgent," enacting the cycle of challenge, learner attempt, evaluation, and progression. Each agent maps to a gamified learning function: progressive challenges, adaptive support, structured feedback, and mastery-based progression, with the RewardAgent assigning XP across tiers from Apprentice to Legendary. (Hornung et al. (2026))
