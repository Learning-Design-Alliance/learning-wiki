# Maternal Fetal Triage Index (MFTI)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Maternal Fetal Triage Index is an AWHONN-developed acuity-assessment and prioritization tool for evaluating pregnant patients and guiding obstetric triage care decisions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Maternal Fetal Triage Index (MFTI) as acuity-assessment template**: The MFTI, a validated tool from AWHONN, serves as the template students use to assess acuity, assign priority, and determine a plan of care during the simulation's asynchronous preparation video. "The Maternal Fetal Triage Index (MFTI) tool was used as a template for this acuity assessment (Figure 1), as use of this validated tool improves the quality and efficiency in OB triage settings." Students receive guidance on using the MFTI for prioritization and care planning. (McDevitt K et al. (2025))
