# Expanding Your Horizons (EYH) conferences

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A one-day STEM outreach programme run through the Expanding Your Horizons Network, in which professional women lead hands-on activities for girls aged 12 to 18 to encourage STEM interest and persistence.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Expanding Your Horizons (EYH) conferences for girls aged 12 to 18**: Expanding Your Horizons conferences are one-day programs for girls aged 12 to 18 that "engage young girls in interesting and fun hands-on STEM activities." Each one-hour activity is designed and led by professional women in STEM fields. The format began in 1976 in California; the article reports about 900,000 girls have attended, with conferences now held annually in 85 cities in 37 US states and several other countries. The EYH Network helps new conference sites get started and provides technical assistance. (Abramzon et al. (2014))
