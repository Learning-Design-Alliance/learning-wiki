Ten Aspects of Community framework¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A Forum for Youth Investment framework that categorizes community features and assets affecting youth across ten domains.

Components¶

  • Ten Aspects of Community framework for organizing youth-supporting community assets: The Ten Aspects of Community, developed by the Forum for Youth Investment (Pittman et al., 2003, based on Pittman & Irby, 1996), is a taxonomy of community features that directly affect youth. The report states it "defines Ten Aspects of Community": Basic Services; Cultural and Artistic; Economic; Civic and Political; Education and Information; Health and Human Services; Individual Development; Physical; Social and Associational; and Spiritual and Religious. The needs assessment used this framework to organize and categorize the county's community assets and services data by aspect and by Supervisorial District. (Roadmap to the Future for Yolo County Children et al. (2024))
