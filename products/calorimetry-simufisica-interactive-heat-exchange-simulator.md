# Calorimetry – SimuFísica® interactive heat-exchange simulator

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An interactive heat-exchange and thermal-equilibrium physics simulator developed for the SimuFísica® platform and distributed online and as downloadable applications for secondary and introductory undergraduate learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Calorimetry – SimuFísica® interactive heat-exchange simulator**: The Calorimetry – SimuFísica® simulator is an interactive computational tool for teaching heat exchange, available on the freely accessible SimuFísica® platform online and as downloadable apps. It offers two modes, Heating and Thermal Equilibrium, real-time display of heat absorbed, sound effects, predefined examples, and a Graph button showing temperature and exchanged-heat evolution. The article describes it as enabling "dynamic and audiovisual exploration of heating and thermal equilibrium processes, making it suitable for both high school and introductory undergraduate Physics courses." (Marco P. M. de Souza et al. (2025))
