Learning and Teaching Geometry (LTG) professional development materials¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

The LTG materials are a teacher-professional-development curriculum developed by Seago and colleagues, including a Foundation Module and extension modules that use classroom video cases to develop teachers’ mathematical and pedagogical knowledge of geometric similarity.

Components¶

  • LTG Foundation Module: 30 hours of video-case-based professional development on geometric similarity: The Foundation Module is the central component of the Learning and Teaching Geometry PD materials, consisting of "10, 3-hour sessions that are intended to provide teachers with a thorough grounding in key mathematical and pedagogical issues related to similarity." Each session centers on unedited digital video clips of unstaged classrooms, chosen because they "typically represent a conceptual hurdle or portray some degree of mathematical confusion." The module follows a mathematical storyline moving from a transformational view of congruence and similarity through dilation properties to area and connections with slope and linearity. (Seago (2013))

Claims¶

  • Students of LTG treatment teachers gained 6.55 percentage points more than students of comparison teachers on a similarity assessment [+M]
