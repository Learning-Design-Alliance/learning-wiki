## Description
SEE+ is a computerized whole-class socio-emotional learning programme developed by Mayer and colleagues that uses animated social scenarios, reflection, prediction, and feedback to teach emotion recognition, mental-state inference, perspective taking, and empathy to primary-school pupils.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SEE+ computerized whole-class socio-emotional learning activity**: SEE+ (Socio-emotional engagement through observation) is a computerized, classroom-based learning activity in which children observe "four virtual characters' interactions in social scenarios involving social dilemmas or conflict" and infer their mental states. It comprises 30 activities of 12 minutes each, delivered three times weekly over 10 weeks during PSHE lesson time, built from eight archetypical activities derived from developmental paradigms (emotion recognition, Reading the Mind in the Eyes, action observation, Faux Pas, Cyberball, Director, Sandbox, Strange Stories) presented in increasing complexity. Activities have four phases interleaving observation, reflective questions, prediction and feedback animations. (Mayer Sveta et al. (2025))
