CILT — the Center for Innovative Learning Technologies¶

Description¶

An NSF-funded national center and multi-institutional partnership that sponsors and coordinates research and development on technology-enabled K–14 science, mathematics, engineering, and technology learning.

Components¶

  • CILT — the Center for Innovative Learning Technologies: CILT is an NSF-funded national center "designed to be a national resource for stimulating research and development of technology- enabled solutions to critical problems in K-14 science, math, engineering and technology learning." Launched at the end of 1997, it is a partnership among SRI International's Center for Technology in Learning, UC Berkeley, Vanderbilt University's Learning Technology Center, and the Concord Consortium, and it seeks to build a broader web of organizations, industries, schools, foundations, agencies, and labs devoted to producing, sharing, and using knowledge about learning technologies. (Roy D. Pea et al. (1999))
