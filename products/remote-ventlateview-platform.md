## Description
A cloud-based remote mechanical-ventilation visualization and automated recognition platform developed by the authors' team to identify patient–ventilator asynchrony from ventilator waveforms in real time.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Remote-VentlateView platform for automated identification of patient-ventilator asynchrony**: A remote mechanical ventilation visualization network system with an automatic recognition algorithm for patient-ventilator asynchrony, developed by the authors' team. The system "can accurately and quickly identify several types of PVA in real time and provide real-time feedback through the cloud platform", using rule-based decision logic on pressure-time, flow-time and volume-time waveforms (e.g., a flow-velocity deflection amplitude above 5 L/min and duration above 0.12 s flags an ineffective trigger). In this study the platform recognized all 8 validated asynchrony waveform sets, and the authors suggest automated assistance and simulation tools may help teach PVA identification skills. (Liu Shengjun et al. (2025))
