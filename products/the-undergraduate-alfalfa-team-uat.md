# The Undergraduate ALFALFA Team (UAT)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A long-term collaborative astronomy research programme run through the ALFALFA survey to embed faculty and undergraduates at primarily undergraduate institutions in mentored scientific research.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The Undergraduate ALFALFA Team model of long-term collaborative research for PUI faculty and students**: The UAT is a 12-year NSF-sponsored program embedding faculty and students at primarily undergraduate institutions in the ALFALFA extragalactic HI legacy survey. Its cornerstone is an annual workshop at Arecibo or Green Bank that "provides an overview of the UAT scientiﬁc work, as well as training in research methods, as it nurtures peer and mentor collaboration." Faculty mentors supervise undergraduates on team research projects during summers and the academic year, with on-site and remote observing extending participation. The model is designed so students join a long-term scientific project through their local faculty member rather than a short off-campus experience. (Koopmann et al. (2019))
