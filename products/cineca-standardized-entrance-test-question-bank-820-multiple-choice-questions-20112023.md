# CINECA standardized entrance test question bank (820 multiple-choice questions, 2011–2023)

## Description
An official CINECA question bank of 820 Italian multiple-choice entrance-test items in logical reasoning, general culture, biology, chemistry, physics, and mathematics for healthcare science degree applicants.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CINECA standardized entrance test question bank (820 multiple-choice questions, 2011–2023) used as an AI-chatbot accuracy benchmark**: The article's evaluation corpus: official CINECA tests from the past 13 years (2011–2023) obtained from freely available public repositories, providing "a total of 820 multiple-choice questions considered for the analysis". Each question has five options with one correct answer, covering logical reasoning and general culture, biology, chemistry, and physics and mathematics. Questions were manually reformatted and entered into the chatbots in Italian without translation. (Rossettini G et al. (2024))
