# Simulation Culture Organisational Readiness Survey (SCORS)

## Description
SCORS is a 38-item Likert-scale survey developed by Leighton et al. that assesses academic and professional staff perceptions of organisational readiness, resources, and capacity for integrating simulation-based education.
