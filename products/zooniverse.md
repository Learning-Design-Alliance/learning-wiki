## Description
Zooniverse is a web-based citizen-science platform operated by the Zooniverse team at the University of Oxford and partner institutions, hosting projects such as Disk Detective in which public participants classify or analyze research data.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Zooniverse and Disk Detective as citizen science entry points**: Zooniverse is a citizen science platform with many astronomy projects to choose from, positioned at the widest part of the funnel. Lesson plans provide a framework for canned use of the data, supporting educators just getting started. One project, Disk Detective, places the RA and Dec of each object with a link to SIMBAD on object pages, and used Zooniverse to find the few most highly interested and capable amateurs worldwide, who now join weekly science meetings. The author notes "Citizen Science at its best enables “hooks” for interested people to go further". (L. M. Rebull (2018))
