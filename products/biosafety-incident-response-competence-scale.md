## Description
A 29-item, four-dimension, five-point Likert self-report scale developed by Wu Chao et al. to assess clinical nursing staff's biosafety incident response competence.

- **Biosafety incident response competence is structured as four communities: monitoring and warning abilities, nursing disposal abilities, knowledge preparedness, and infection protection abilities.**: The article models nurses' biosafety incident response competence as a network of 29 items organized into four communities. As printed, "Biosafety incident response competence consists of four factors: biosafety event monitoring and warning abilities, biosafety incident nursing disposal abilities, biosafety knowledge preparedness, and biosafety infection protection abilities." The communities contain 9, 8, 7, and 5 nodes respectively, and bridge centrality quantifies links between them. (Wu Chao et al. (2025))
