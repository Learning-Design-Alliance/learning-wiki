## Description
The HCTSR is a rubric developed for rating the quality of critical thinking demonstrated in learners’ written or verbal work across educational programs and assessment contexts.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Holistic Critical Thinking Scoring Rubric (HCTSR) used to assess quality of critical thinking in written and verbal postgraduate work**: The HCTSR is "a rating measure to assess the quality of critical thinking displayed in a verbal presentation or written text in any educational program or assessment process." The article notes it requires a task where "the thinker is asked to explain their thinking process and not just to provide the conclusions," that its criteria help learners internalize characteristics of strong and weak critical thinking, and that it has demonstrated strong inter-rater reliability and construct validity across diverse educational settings. Two trained faculty raters scored essays and case-scenario presentations on a 1-4 scale, resolving discrepancies by consensus. (Singh Veena Kumari et al. (2025))
