## Description
A 15-item self-report scale measuring moral, benevolent, and authoritarian paternalistic leadership, based on Zheng Boxun's model.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Paternalistic leadership three-dimensional model: moral, benevolent, and authoritarian leadership**: The article adopts Zheng Boxun's three-dimensional model of paternalistic leadership, which comprises moral, benevolent, and authoritarian dimensions. "Moral leadership emphasizes strong moral cultivation and restraint in leaders"; "benevolent leadership involves leaders who regard their staff as relatives and show tolerance, love, help, care and encouragement"; and "authoritarian leadership emphasizes the use of power by leaders to control staff' behavior." This framework, rooted in strict discipline and dual aspects of affection and authority, structures the study's hypotheses and measurement approach. (Wang Dongdong et al. (2026))
