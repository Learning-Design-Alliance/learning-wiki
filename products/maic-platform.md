## Description
An online learning environment developed by the article's authors that combines course-preparation workflows, multi-agent classrooms, customizable classmate agents, and large-model learning-analytics tools for instructors and university students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MAIC platform with intelligent agents and learning analytics tools**: The MAIC platform is an implemented online learning environment offering course preparation workflows (Read and Plan stages), multi-agent classrooms, and analytics. The article states: 'This platform comes pre-equipped with a suite of intelligent agents and tools that support course analysis and the construction of new MAIC course examples.' It also integrates learning analytics tools powered by large models for accessing learning data, forecasting academic outcomes, and automating interviews and assessments. Four preset classmate agents (Class Clown, Deep Thinker, Note Taker, Inquisitive Mind) are provided, with user customization supported. (From MOOC to MAIC: Reshaping Online Teaching and Learning through LLM-driven Agents)
- **A collaborative version of MIT App Inventor for group programming**: The final full article of the issue presents and evaluates a collaborative extension of MIT App Inventor (MAI), "a popular block-based programming (BBP) learning environment designed to aid new programmers in making quick progress towards producing computer programs by removing the need for as much knowledge of programming language syntax." The study explores interactions afforded by a platform not originally designed to be collaborative, applying analytics to gain visibility into behavior patterns. It contrasts individual vs. collaborative work, heterogeneous vs. homogeneous disciplinary-background teams, and team self-efficacy. (Carolyn Rosé (2021))
- **MAI real-time collaboration platform for synchronous multi-user block-based programming**: The article extended MIT App Inventor with a real-time collaboration mode that "allows users to co-edit their blocks in the program", like a Google Document, while sharing components and notes. The current version added "a chat room for each group and an annotation function for each block" so collaborators can discuss changes in real time, making instant co-creation at a distance feasible in the block-based programming environment. (Ting-Chia Hsu et al. (2021))
