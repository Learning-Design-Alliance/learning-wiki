## Description
An online learning environment developed by the article's authors that combines course-preparation workflows, multi-agent classrooms, customizable classmate agents, and large-model learning-analytics tools for instructors and university students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MAIC platform with intelligent agents and learning analytics tools**: The MAIC platform is an implemented online learning environment offering course preparation workflows (Read and Plan stages), multi-agent classrooms, and analytics. The article states: 'This platform comes pre-equipped with a suite of intelligent agents and tools that support course analysis and the construction of new MAIC course examples.' It also integrates learning analytics tools powered by large models for accessing learning data, forecasting academic outcomes, and automating interviews and assessments. Four preset classmate agents (Class Clown, Deep Thinker, Note Taker, Inquisitive Mind) are provided, with user customization supported. (From MOOC to MAIC: Reshaping Online Teaching and Learning through LLM-driven Agents)
