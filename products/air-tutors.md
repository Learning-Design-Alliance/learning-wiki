# Air Tutors

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 8 claims (7 for, 1 mixed) · 3 studies (3 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 3 of 3 report an effect size · 8 claims rest on one study

## Description
Air Tutors is a virtual high-dosage math tutoring programme run by Air Tutors that provides small-group online tutoring for upper-elementary and early-middle-school students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Air Tutors virtual high-dosage math tutoring program**: Air Tutors is an online high-dosage math tutoring program for upper elementary and early middle school grades, delivered via "dynamic video conferencing and interactive whiteboards" at a ratio of four students per tutor. It offers 45-minute sessions four to five times per week over 10 weeks (30 intended hours), uses the i-Ready curriculum's scope and sequence, employs tutors with at least a bachelor's degree and 3 years of experience, and structures instruction within the gradual release of responsibility framework alongside an Ubuntu professional learning community. (Fong et al. (2025))
- **Air Tutors high-dosage math tutoring program**: Air Tutors is a high-dosage math tutoring program evaluated in two districts (Colorado and Texas). The implementation plan "called for students to attend 45-minute sessions 4–5 times per week for at least 10 weeks, totaling approximately 30 hours of tutoring", with adequate participation defined as at least 24 hours (80 percent of full dosage). This follow-up evaluation found no measurable long-term effects on statewide math assessments 1 year after tutoring concluded. (Fong et al. (2026))
- **Air Reading live virtual small-group tutoring program**: Air Reading is an assessment-driven, live virtual tutoring program for foundational reading from Kindergarten through 6th grade. "Small groups of up to four students are paired with the same tutor for 30-minute sessions four times each week," with one-on-one diagnostics informing group placement and bi-weekly progress assessments. Sessions are tracked on the program's proprietary platform, and the model is described as scalable across geographies and staffing capabilities. (Air Reading (2025))
- **Targeted Skills Instruction (TSI): live small-group virtual tutoring for K-12 reading and math**: Targeted Skills Instruction is "a live, small-group virtual tutoring program for K-12 reading and math, delivered in 30–45-minute sessions three or more times per week." In the studied implementation, students were assigned based on fall Exact Path Insights diagnostic scores at or below the 25th percentile and served in groups of 2-6 students in three 30-40 minute sessions per week across three 6-week cycles. Sessions follow a gradual release model with tutor support. (Targeted Skills Instruction Evidence Rating Review (2025))
