# National Plan for Science Degree (PLS)

## Description
An Italian Ministry-funded programme in which universities and schools collaborate on laboratory-based science orientation, teacher development, and recruitment into science degrees.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Laboratory as a method, not as a place: joint teacher-university planning makes students the main characters of learning**: The National Plan for Science Degree's methodological guideline treats the laboratory as a way of teaching rather than a room: "the main road consisted in considering laboratory as a method, not as a place." Under this pattern, "Students must have become the main character of learning", and joint planning by school teachers and university researchers is a mandatory step, pursued through orientation conceived as an action students do rather than a teaching path given to them. The Plan distinguishes laboratories that approach the discipline and develop vocations, self-assessment laboratories, and deepening laboratories for motivated and talented students. (Montalbano et al. (2012))
- **PLS strategy: orientation as the student's own action, joint school-university planning, and the laboratory as a method rather than a place**: The National Plan for Science Degree articulates guiding ideas the authors say proved effective in the 2005-2009 trials: "orientation does not conceive how a teaching path given to student, but as an action that the student is doing, from meaningful activities"; teachers are trained "by involving teachers in solving concrete problems" and through comparison with peers and experts; and student orientation and teacher training are pursued simultaneously through joint planning of laboratories. The article condenses this as "considering laboratory as a method, not as a place, students must become the main character of learning and joint planning by teachers and university is a mandatory step." (Vera MONTALBANO (2012))
- **National Plan for Science Degree (PLS) laboratory methodology for orienting students to science degrees**: The PLS is a large Italian ministry-funded plan in which more than 30 universities promote science degrees through teacher professional development and student orientation "essentially by means of laboratory activities". Its stated strategies are orienting by training, "Laboratory as a method not as a place", students becoming "the main character of learning", and joint planning by teachers and university. PLS laboratories engage groups of up to 15 students (better fewer) for at least 15 hours and can aim at approaching the discipline, self-assessment, or deepening for talented students. (Roberto Benedetti et al. (2012))
