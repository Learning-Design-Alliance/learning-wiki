# STEM benchmark dataset: 448 skills and 1,073,146 multimodal K-12 multiple-choice questions across all STEM subjects

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A benchmark dataset released by Jianhao Shen et al. containing over one million image-containing K–12 STEM multiple-choice questions with skill, subject, and grade metadata for evaluating neural models.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **STEM benchmark dataset: 448 skills and 1,073,146 multimodal K-12 multiple-choice questions across all STEM subjects**: STEM is a large-scale multimodal benchmark for testing neural models' STEM problem solving. It spans "science, technology, engineering, and math" with 448 skills and 1,073,146 multiple-choice questions from pre-K to grade 8, built from IXL Learning aligned with U.S. National Education and California Common Core standards. Each question is multimodal — "We exclude a question if both the question and its answers are text" — and includes skill, subject, and grade metadata supporting analysis at multiple granularities. Train, validation, and test splits are provided (644,797 / 214,272 / 214,077 questions). (Jianhao Shen et al. (2024))
