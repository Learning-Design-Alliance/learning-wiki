Stepping Stones to Literacy¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Stepping Stones to Literacy is a supplemental early-literacy curriculum developed and distributed by Sopris West for underachieving kindergarten and older preschool readers.

Components¶

  • Stepping Stones to Literacy supplemental curriculum: Stepping Stones to Literacy (SSL) is a supplemental early-literacy curriculum of "25 lessons, for a total of 9–15 hours of instructional time," delivered in 10- to 20-minute daily lessons in small groups or individually. It targets "listening, print conventions, phonological awareness, phonemic awareness, and serial processing/rapid naming" for kindergarten and older preschool students considered underachieving readers. The kit includes a lesson book with serial rapid automatic naming activities and instructional prompts in English and Spanish, distributed by Sopris West for $223.49. (What Works Clearinghouse (2007))

Claims¶

  • No studies meeting WWC standards addressed SSL effects on fluency, comprehension, or general reading achievement, and the extent of alphabetics evidence is small [~W]
  • In the Nelson, Benner, & Gonzalez (2005) RCT, SSL produced statistically significant positive effects on all five alphabetics outcomes [+M]
  • In the Nelson, Stage, Epstein, & Pierce (2005) RCT, SSL produced statistically significant positive effects on all four alphabetics outcomes [+M]
