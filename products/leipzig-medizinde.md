## Description
A locally operated student portal at Universität Leipzig that provides medical students and teaching staff with forums, news, and downloadable study resources for peer exchange and course communication.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Leipzig-Medizin.de local student portal with forum and file exchange**: Leipzig-Medizin.de is a local platform, established in 2003 and founded by a medical student, that hosts a forum plus news and downloads such as past exam papers, scripts and oral examination records. The article calls it "an integral part of studying medicine in Leipzig, with awareness reaching 95%". Students discuss study problems and past examination questions there, particularly in revision periods, benefiting from higher-years students' experience. Lecturers also register, observe where problems lie, and contact students overtly or covertly. (Hempel G et al. (2013))
