# National Agriscience Teacher Ambassador Academy (NATAA)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A five-day intensive professional-development programme run through the National FFA Organization to prepare secondary agriscience teachers to deliver inquiry-based instruction.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **National Agriscience Teacher Ambassador Academy (NATAA) professional development program**: The NATAA is the professional development program through which the study's teachers were prepared to deliver inquiry-based instruction. As the article describes it, "The NATAA is a five–day intensive professional development training on the inquiry–based instructional method." Teachers were selected by their state's FFA executive secretary and/or agricultural education specialist, and after the training they presented workshops at the National FFA Convention and NAAE conference. The article recommends expanding NATAA opportunities nationally. (Thoron et al. (2011))
