# Starch's 600-word dictionary-sampled spelling test in six comparable 100-word lists

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A standardized dictated spelling assessment developed by Edward L. Starch from a dictionary-based sample of 600 nontechnical English words for measuring spelling attainment in elementary pupils.
