## Description
A SwissMAP-developed upper-secondary cosmology and general-relativity curriculum package comprising an eight-chapter book, supplementary appendices, online exercises, and teacher answer keys for non-specialist students.

### Claims

- [A semester-long cosmology and GR course for non-specialist upper secondary students produces very large conceptual learning gains (overall d = 2.78)](../claims/gr-cosmology-course-large-conceptual-gains.md) [+M]
- [The course significantly increases curiosity state (d = 0.46) and shows a small, non-significant increase in self-concept (d = 0.26)](../claims/cosmology-course-curiosity-self-concept-gains.md) [+M]
- [For physics misconceptions, pre-test dominant wrong answers were overtaken by correct answers after the cosmology course, in one uncontrolled pre-post study](../claims/cosmology-course-reduces-misconceptions.md) [+W]
- [Conceptual learning scores rose from pre-test to post-test in one group with no comparison group; prior physics grade was associated with the gains, while class and gender were not, and no significant associations were found between the predictors and affective outcomes](../claims/predictors-cosmology-course-outcomes.md) [+W]
- [The course produces no significant change in interest and no change in perceived relevance of science](../claims/cosmology-course-no-interest-relevance-change.md) [-W]
