# Battery of twelve tests for originality with self-recording time device

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (5 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
A Chassell (1916) battery of twelve individually or group-administered tests for measuring and ranking originality in university students and adults, using oral and placard instructions and a self-recorded time device.
