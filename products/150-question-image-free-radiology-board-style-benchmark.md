# 150-question image-free radiology board-style benchmark

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A benchmark dataset of 150 expert-validated, image-free multiple-choice radiology board-style questions for evaluating or practicing radiology knowledge and reasoning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **150-question image-free radiology board-style benchmark validated by two academic radiologists**: The study assembled a benchmark of 150 multiple-choice questions "drawn from historical radiology board exams similar to those given by the Canadian Royal College and the American Board of Radiology", sourced from Board Vitals and CanadaQBank. Image-based questions were excluded, and each question was individually reviewed and validated by two board-certified radiologists (ultrasound, 20 years; abdominal radiology, 4 years), with inclusion requiring agreement of both reviewers. Questions were categorized by Bloom's Taxonomy cognitive level and by disease-focus area. (Boxiong Wei (2025))
