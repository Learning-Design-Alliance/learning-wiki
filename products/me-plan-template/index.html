Description¶

The Measurement and Evaluation (M&E) Plan Template is a roadmap described by Place et al. for organizations and funders to develop detailed evaluation plans with internal or external research partners.

Components¶

  • Measurement and Evaluation Plan Template as a roadmap for developing an M&E plan with a research partner: The Measurement and Evaluation (M&E) Plan Template is described as "a road map organizations and funders can use to develop a detailed measurement and evaluation plan with support from a research partner." The research partner may be internal evaluation staff, an external organization, a technical assistance provider, or a consultant. The template structures planning for evaluating solutions, including education solutions such as middle years math teaching and learning. (Kate Place et al. (2023))
