# New categorization scheme for CUE superposition-question responses

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A Table 2 coding scheme developed by Zwolak et al. for classifying undergraduate physics students’ answers to the CUE superposition question by what is superposed and whether the term “superposition” is used.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **New categorization scheme for CUE superposition-question responses (Table 2: A–D by what is superposed and term use, plus F/X/Z)**: A new categorization of student responses to the CUE superposition question, shown in Table 2, which the authors state "focuses primarily on what is being superposed and secondarily on whether the word “superposition” is used". It sorts relevant answers into clearly adding fields (A), clearly adding potentials (B), apparently adding charges (C), and ambiguous responses (D), each subdivided by whether the word “superposition” is used, plus irrelevant (F), no answer (X) and “I don't know” (Z). It was applied to 90 OSU and 64 CU tests and revealed differences and difficulties the original rubric obscured. (Justyna P. Zwolak et al. (2017))

### Claims
- [The CUE superposition-question rubric carries a potential bias toward traditional subject order and emphasis, obscuring differences between approaches](../claims/cue-rubric-bias-traditional-order.md) [+W]
