## Description
LES-IN-DIG is a tablet-based digital reading screening developed by Ebenbeck and colleagues for Levumi.de, comprising four Rasch-conform subtests measuring phonological awareness, vocabulary, flash reading, and sentence comprehension in learners with lower reading abilities.
