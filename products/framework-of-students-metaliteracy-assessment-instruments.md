# Framework of students metaliteracy assessment instruments

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A higher-education assessment framework developed by A. I. Kristiana and colleagues for evaluating students' metaliteracy across producing, incorporating, using, sharing, and collaborating indicators.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Metaliteracy assessment instrument framework with five indicators and sub-indicators**: The article presents "The framework of students metaliteracy assessment instruments" (Table 11) organised by five indicators: produce, incorporate, use, share, and collaborate. Each indicator has sub-indicators and a test framework specifying tasks such as identifying problem properties, developing generalizations, testing whether the obtained r-dynamic chromatic number is optimum, developing a poster or video to share results, and collaborating using IoT platforms. (A I Kristiana et al. (2022))
