# Positive Behavioral Interventions and Supports (PBIS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
PBIS is an evidence-based, multitiered framework used by schools to organize data, systems, and behavioral practices through universal, targeted, and individualized supports.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PBIS three-tiered prevention and intervention framework**: PBIS is described as "a three-tiered, comprehensive, evidence-based framework aimed at improving and integrating all the data, systems, and practices that shape student outcomes." It organizes supports into universal Tier 1 schoolwide expectations taught and positively reinforced for all students, targeted Tier 2 small-group social skills supports such as Check In–Check Out for roughly 5–15 percent of students, and intensive individualized Tier 3 supports based on functional behavior assessment for 3–5 percent of students. It is a framework supporting ongoing professional development and capacity building, not a curriculum or scripted program. (Gage et al. (2025))
- **School-wide Positive Behavioral Interventions and Supports (PBIS)**: PBIS is a universal, school-wide prevention strategy aimed at reducing disruptive behavior problems, described in the article as "a universal, school-wide prevention strategy that is currently implemented in over 7,500 schools". It operates as a whole-school prevention model whose implementation was tested in a group-randomized effectiveness trial in 37 elementary schools, where trained schools varied in how quickly they adopted the model. The article treats PBIS as an organizational as well as behavioral intervention, linking it to staff-reported school climate. (Bradshaw et al. (2009))
- **SWPBIS as a 3-tiered universal prevention framework altering the school's organizational context**: SWPBIS is a noncurricular universal prevention strategy that "aims to alter the school 's organizational context to implement enhanced procedures and systems to guide data-based decisions related to student behavior problems and academics." It applies behavioral, social learning, and organizational principles school-wide, establishes positively stated expectations taught to all students and staff, and implements "a 3-tiered prevention framework" in which selective and indicated programs complement universal components. In this trial it was delivered via team training, booster sessions, and monthly behavior-support coaching. (Catherine P. Bradshaw et al. (2012))
- **SWPBIS is a multitiered whole-school prevention framework aimed at altering the school environment**: The article describes Positive Behavioral Interventions and Supports as "a multitiered system o f supports framework aimed at altering the school environment through improved systems, data-based decision making, and implemention of evidence-based practices." It applies behavioral, social learning, and organizational principles across all school contexts to prevent disruptive behavior and enhance school climate. The model follows a three-tiered prevention model in which selective (Tier 2) and indicated (Tier 3) programs complement universal (Tier 1) school-wide components. Most of the more than 20,000 implementing schools focus on the universal elements due to resource needs. (Bradshaw et al. (2015))
