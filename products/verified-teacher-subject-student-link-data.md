# Verified teacher-subject-student link data

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A dataset of teacher-verified mathematics and English/language arts subject and student links used to assess the accuracy of administrative teacher–student linkage data and examine departmentalization.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Verified teacher-subject-student link data for examining value-added model assumptions**: A dataset of teacher-student links in which teachers of mathematics and/or English/language arts verified the subjects and students they taught. The article uses it as a benchmark against teacher-student links from original administrative data to detect departmentalization in grades 4-5, finding that "instruction is often departmentalized in these grades." It serves research on teacher effectiveness data quality. (Eric Isenberg et al. (2015))

### Claims
- [Instruction in upper elementary grades is often departmentalized: about one in six elementary school teachers in the original administrative data was linked to a subject he or she did not teach](../claims/one-in-six-elementary-teachers-linked-to-untaught-subject.md) [+M]
