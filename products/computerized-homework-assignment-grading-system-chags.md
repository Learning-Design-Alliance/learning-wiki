# Computerized Homework Assignment Grading System (CHAGS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
CHAGS is an online homework submission and automated grading system developed for large introductory physics courses by Toback and colleagues.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Computerized Homework Assignment Grading System (CHAGS) with closed-form-before-login requirement**: CHAGS automates homework grading in large courses. Students first solve textbook problems in closed form with pen and paper; then CHAGS presents "the same problems on screen, but with different numeric parameters," requiring students to type the new numeric answer within 20 minutes. The time restriction "emphasizes the utility of closed-form solutions and the need to do the work before logging on to the computer." Numerical answers foster unit and "reality" checks on physical magnitudes. (David Toback et al. (2004))
