## Description
The Open Universe portal and its Firmamento platform provide smartphone-friendly tools for searching, viewing, and analyzing astronomical survey data, developed through the Open Universe initiative and Firmamento at NYU Abu Dhabi.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Open Universe portal and Firmamento platform for citizen-science blazar research**: Open Universe is an initiative under COPUOS aiming to increase the availability and usability of space science data and to extend "the potential of scientific discovery to new participants in all parts of the world". Its blazar-specific tool Firmamento, developed at NYU Abu Dhabi and building on VOU-Blazar, hosts the association-search, sky-survey and SED tools the students used, and is smartphone-friendly. The students used these tools to find and verify blazar counterpart associations. (L. Fronte et al. (2022))
