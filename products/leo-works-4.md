## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **LEO Works 4 educational software for analysing satellite data**: LEO Works 4 is an educational software package developed by the European Space Agency for teaching and learning the basic steps of analysing satellite data. It is Java-based and therefore independent of the operating system, and it permits close to professional treatment of up-to-date satellite data. The article uses it as the core tool of the activity: students load multispectral Landsat datasets, adjust contrast with Interactive Stretching, build RGB and false-colour views, compute the NDVI with a dedicated tool, and perform band arithmetic for other spectral indices. (M. Nielbock (2017))
