## Description
PeriopSim is an iPad-based surgical simulation platform developed by Conquer Mobile with clinical surgeons and perioperative clinicians for practicing surgical instruments, procedures, and techniques.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PeriopSim iPad simulation platform for surgical instrument learning**: PeriopSim is an iPad-based surgical simulation platform developed by Conquer Mobile with clinical surgeons and perioperative clinicians, comprising two neurosurgery modules: PeriopSim Instrument Trainer and PeriopSim for Burr Hole Surgery. The article describes it as 'an iPad application to provide a convenient user-friendly platform to facilitate learning about surgical procedures, instruments and techniques.' The Instrument Trainer uses voice prompts and a simulated tool tray with HD simulations of real instruments; the burr hole module uses interactive video of a real procedure, with gamification scoring and timed challenges motivating practice. (Clarke David B. et al. (2016))
