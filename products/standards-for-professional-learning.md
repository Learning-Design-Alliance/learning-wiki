# Standards for Professional Learning as a seven-standard framework for results-oriented educator learning

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Learning Forward's Standards for Professional Learning, developed in 2011 with more than 40 other organizations, form the report's organizing framework. The seven standards are "Learning Communities, Leadership, Resources, Learning Designs, Data, Implementation, Outcomes," and the standards "outline the research-based conditions and elements essential for professional learning that lead to changes in educator practice and improvements in student results." The report maps each standard onto micro-credential practice with recommendations for states, districts, and issuers.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- The standards are a framework for planning, implementing, and evaluating professional learning, not a roadmap or how-to guide
#### Constraints
- The standards have been adopted or adapted in more than 35 states to shape professional learning policies and guidelines

### Target Learners
- teachers
- school and district leaders
- policymakers

### Target Learning Objectives
- results-oriented, outcome-focused professional learning systems
