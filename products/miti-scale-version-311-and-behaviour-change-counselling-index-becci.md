# MITI Scale version 3.1.1 and Behaviour Change Counselling Index (BECCI)

## Description
The MITI and BECCI are published rating instruments used to assess motivational-interviewing and behaviour-change-counselling delivery and practitioner competence.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MITI 3.1.1 and BECCI rating instruments used to assess treatment fidelity and clinician competency**: The study used two published instruments to quantify delivery of the psychological intervention. The MITI Scale version 3.1.1 measures Global Spirit, intended to capture "the overall demonstration of MI principles", and Global Empathy, plus behaviours such as reflections, questions, and MI-adherent counselling, with specified Beginning-proficiency and Competency thresholds. The BECCI comprises 11 items scored 0–4, with the mean used as the overall Practitioner Score, and was included to assess competence in eliciting patients' thoughts and cognitions, addressing the CBT element. (Magill Nicholas et al. (2018))
- **Motivational Interviewing Treatment Integrity (MITI) tool as a self-reflection instrument for students**: The MITI is a validated coding tool for motivational interviewing treatment integrity that the study repurposed from supervisor use to structured student self-assessment. The article notes "The MITI tool is the benchmark for assessing MI treatment integrity" and that it is useful "for providing targeted feedback on areas in which the interviewer is performing well, and how they can improve". It has global ratings for five MI key principles and six behaviour counts, and 10-minute interview segments yield comparable reliability to 20-minute segments. (M. Schoo et al. (2015))
