## Description
CS50-Duck is a subject-matter conversational agent developed for and deployed in Harvard's CS50 programming course through the CS50 platform and Visual Studio Code.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CS50-Duck conversational agent for Harvard's CS50 course**: CS50-Duck is a CA deployed in Harvard's CS50 course, operating within the CS50 platform and Visual Studio Code. The review reports it "strives for a 1:1 teacher-student ratio by providing a subject-matter expert bot", and that its deployment achieved positive outcomes such as providing continuous and individualized support, allowing human educators to focus on more complex pedagogical issues. It also integrates a heart-based feedback system as a gamification mechanic. (Barzanji et al. (2025))
