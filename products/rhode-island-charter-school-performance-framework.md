Schools must "demonstrate strong academic gains, measured through standardized assessments" and match or exceed sending district performance; governing boards are accountable for oversight and culture; schools must show long-term financial viability; and they are evaluated on mission fulfillment and service to diverse populations including English learners and students with disabilities.
