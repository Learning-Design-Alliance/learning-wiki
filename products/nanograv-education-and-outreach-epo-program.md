# NANOGrav Education and Outreach (EPO) program

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
NANOGrav's five-level education and public-outreach framework maps audiences and methods to escalating goals from inspiring the public to training researchers for participation in gravitational-wave astronomy.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **NANOGrav EPO pyramid: a five-level framework (inspire, inform, engage, educate, train) linking audiences and methods to escalating engagement goals**: The EPO pyramid is NANOGrav's organizing framework for education and public outreach, described as demonstrating "the importance of initiating broad and inclusive outreach efforts if one wants to ultimately build a well-trained and diverse scientiﬁc community." It has three dimensions: audiences ranging from the general public and media through K-8, high-school and undergraduate students, grad students and post-docs, to peers; methods such as social media, exhibits, SPOT presentations, pulsar searching, and special sessions; and a vertical progression of goals from inspiring a broad range of people to training students and junior researchers for direct involvement in NANOGrav. Pyramid level sizes reflect intended audience sizes, and several initiatives are designed to bridge external and internal audiences at the Engage level.
