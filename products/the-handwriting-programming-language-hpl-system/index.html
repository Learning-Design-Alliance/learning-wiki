The handwriting programming language (HPL) system¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The HPL system is a web-based computer-vision programming interface developed by Brender et al. that lets primary-school learners write program instructions by hand and execute the decoded commands through virtual or physical educational robots.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • HPL students learned as much as Scratch students, with equivalent improvement in both conditions [+W]
  • Over 80% of pre-service teachers were willing to use HPL to teach CS, irrespective of gender or prior experience [+W]
  • Pre-service teachers showed a slight preference for HPL over an equivalent paper-based TPL in direct comparison [+W]
  • Students perceived HPL as positively as Scratch across TAM dimensions [+W]
