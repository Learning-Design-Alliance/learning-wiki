# Nurses' Clinic Communication Competency Scale (NCCCS) and Chinese Caring Ability Inventory (CAI)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A paired online self-report assessment battery measuring nurses' clinical communication competency and caring ability, developed and administered by the study researchers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Nurses' Clinic Communication Competency Scale (NCCCS) and Caring Ability Inventory (CAI) as paired self-report measures**: The study operationalizes two self-report instruments administered online: the NCCCS, which "included six dimensions" covering team, basic language, basic non-language, emotional perception, emotional support, and difficult-clinical-scenes competency (58 items, Likert 5-point, range 58–290), and the Chinese CAI (37 items, Likert 7-point) with knowing, courage, and patience dimensions. In this sample Cronbach's alpha was 0.995 for NCCCS and 0.856 for CAI. The pair enables correlated assessment of caring and communication competence in practicing nurses. (Jing et al. (2025))
