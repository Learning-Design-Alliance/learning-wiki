# ABCDE immediate life support methodology

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A structured clinical framework for assessing and initially stabilising critically ill patients by sequentially evaluating airway, breathing, circulation, disability, and exposure.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ABCDE immediate life support methodology for structured assessment of the critical patient**: The article's training rests on immediate life support (SVI) organised as the A-B-C-D-E methodology: "A (airway/vía aérea) – B (breathing/respiración) – C (circulation/circulación) – D (disability/neurológico) – E (exposure/exposición)". Every critical patient should be treated following these stages "en estricto orden, y no iniciar ninguna etapa sin haber valorado la anterior". The AIPCAP checklist operationalises this framework into 7 stages (adding first look and additional information) for structured evaluation and feedback during simulated emergencies. (Cánovas Zaldúa Y. et al. (2020))
