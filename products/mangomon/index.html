Mangomon¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 5 claims (4 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 quant-synthesis), q2 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Mangomon is a CEFR-based role-playing gamification mobile application developed by a Thai software company with Thammasat University's Language Institute for out-of-class business English vocabulary learning.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Vocabulary performance shifted from B1–B2 CEFR levels at pre-test to B2–C1 at post-test after four weeks of Mangomon play [+W]
  • Thai undergraduates' business vocabulary test scores rose from pre-test to post-test after four weeks of out-of-class role-playing gamification with Mangomon, in a single-group study with no control group [+W]
  • IMI motivation ratings after four weeks of Mangomon play were moderate (all sub-scale means below 4.00), highest for effort/importance and lowest for perceived choice [+W]
  • Interest/enjoyment and perceived choice were the weakest motivation dimensions, suggesting Mangomon's RPG mechanics did not yet fully serve learner autonomy [-W]
  • In one mixed-methods study of university students, reported in a review, gamified vocabulary learning gave better test results, motivation and satisfaction than traditional instruction [+W]
