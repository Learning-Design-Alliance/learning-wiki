## Description
Zoom is a web-conferencing platform developed by Zoom Communications that enables real-time video, audio, chat, breakout-room, screen-sharing, polling, whiteboard, and recording functions for education and other forms of collaboration.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Zoom web conference system as a synchronous language-learning environment**: Zoom is a subscription-based web conferencing program that supports synchronous interaction between educators and students via webcam and microphone in real time. The article describes its educational activities: "communication-related activities include greeting others, classroom lectures, question and answers and group discussions in breakout rooms", plus material-sharing, polling, whiteboard practice, and recordable individual meetings. In this study it mediated the emergency shift to at-home EFL learning for 75 Saudi undergraduates during COVID-19 university closures. (Alfadda et al. (2021))
