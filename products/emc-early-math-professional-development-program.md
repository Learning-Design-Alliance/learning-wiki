## Description
A yearlong early-mathematics teacher professional-development programme run by the Early Math Collaborative in partnership with Chicago Public Schools, combining interactive learning labs, classroom coaching, and guided implementation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **EMC early math professional development program (learning labs, on-site coaching, guided classroom implementation)**: A yearlong in-service PD program delivered in partnership with Chicago Public Schools to approximately 80 Head Start, pre-kindergarten and kindergarten teachers annually. It includes three components: "Learning labs—early math instructors lead these yearlong, interactive learning sessions focusing on teachers' understanding of foundational mathematics"; on-site coaching in teachers' classrooms; and guided classroom implementation in which teachers practice mathematizing classroom experiences under coach guidance. (McCray et al. (2013))

### Claims
