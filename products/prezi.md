## Description
Prezi is a web-based presentation platform developed by Prezi Inc. for creating and delivering zoomable, non-linear visual presentations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Prezi web-based zoomable presentation tool as used for French reading instruction**: Prezi is a web-based presentation application that uses one canvas instead of sequential slides, letting users place text, images and video in frames and define paths between them. The article defines it as "a presentation that displays information in an optical map, in which text, video and graphics can be rendered dynamically to zoom in and out within content". In this study it was the instructional medium for the experimental group's French reading lessons, and the article credits it with supporting conceptual mapping and non-linear presentation of relationships between concepts. (Samer Al-Hammouri (2019))
