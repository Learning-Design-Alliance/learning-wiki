> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
The STEM-tastic Adventures app is a free resource, available through the App Store and Google Play, that emerged from a National Science Foundation-funded co-design project with preschool teachers and families. Its "City Walk" game "provides opportunities for children to identify a sequence of directions for the robot" and "progressively presents more complex sequences and scaffolds how to identify errors (testing and debugging) with the support of visual and audio feedback." The app complements hands-on unplugged activities linking CT to everyday routines.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Co-design iteration informed by teachers, families, and children; pairing with unplugged activities such as following direction sequences on a large map
#### Constraints
- Designed for young children ages 3-5 in preschool mathematics and science contexts

### Target Learners
- Young children ages 3-5 in preschool and home settings

### Target Learning Goals
- Algorithmic thinking, sequencing, and testing and debugging skills linked to early math and science learning
