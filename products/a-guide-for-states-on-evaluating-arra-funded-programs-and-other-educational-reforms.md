## Description
The artifact is a guide developed jointly by two research organizations for state education agencies. As the page states, "The American Institutes for Research and Mathematica developed this guide to help you consider evaluation issues likely to arise as you launch ARRA-funded initiatives and other educational reform activities." It is published jointly by AIR in Washington DC and Mathematica Policy Research in Princeton NJ, dated April 30, 2011, and tagged under Teacher and Principal Effectiveness.
