# AI-Enabled EdTech Adoption Cycle

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A four-phase adoption framework described by Ruiz et al. for guiding education systems through vision and planning, implementation, ongoing evaluation, and scaling and sustaining of AI-enabled edtech.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **AI-Enabled EdTech Adoption Cycle (vision and planning, implementation, ongoing evaluation, scaling and sustaining)**: Appendix 1 of the brief outlines a four-phase adoption cycle for AI-enabled edtech: vision and planning, implementation, ongoing evaluation, and scaling and sustaining, each with key challenges and recommendations. It states that "Planning for AI-enabled edtech requires districts to move beyond reactive purchasing decisions and establish strategic frameworks that align technology with clear learning objectives and pedagogical goals." AI-enabled tools require specialized evaluation criteria including algorithmic transparency, bias mitigation, and data privacy. (Ruiz et al. (2025))
