## Description
Andes2 is an intelligent tutoring system for supporting students solving introductory physics homework problems, including algebraic equation solving and validity checking.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Algebra subsystem for Andes2 physics tutoring system with four capabilities**: The article describes a subsystem that resolves algebraic questions for Andes2, an intelligent tutoring system for introductory physics homework problems. As printed in the abstract, it is "capable of 1) solving the systems of equations at the level of introductory physics problems, 2) checking the validity of equations the students enter". The author notes the capabilities "would be useful for any ITS which deals with problems involving complex systems of equations". (Joel A. Shapiro (2005))
