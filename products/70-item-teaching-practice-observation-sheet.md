# 70-item Teaching Practice Observation Sheet

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 70-item, three-point observation instrument adopted from Azmy and Mohamed (2006) for trained judges to assess preservice physical education teachers' planning, implementation, and evaluation skills from videotaped teaching performances.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **70-item Teaching Practice Observation Sheet with three teaching-skill dimensions**: The study's measurement instrument is a 70-item observation sheet adopted from Azmy & Mohamed (2006), rated on a three-point scale (1=Never; 2=Sometimes; 3= Always). "The teaching practice observation sheet consists of three main dimensions representing the three major teaching skills addressed by the researchers (see Appendix 1); including planning skills (16 items), implementation skills (46 items), and evaluation skills (8 items)." Cronbach alpha coefficients for the three dimensions were between .93 and .98, and inter-rater reliability via the Pearson Correlation Formula on videotaped performances by two lecturers was 0.97. (Haythem Abdel Mageed Mohamed et al. (2008))
