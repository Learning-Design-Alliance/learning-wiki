Virtual Walking the Pens¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A serious digital game that simulates swine-confinement management and embeds animal-health scenarios and workplace mathematics practice for agricultural education learners.

Components¶

  • Virtual Walking the Pens serious game: Virtual Walking the Pens is a serious digital game designed to assist swine producers in learning production and management practices through digital gaming. Students interact with a simulated virtual swine confinement operation, performing virtual barn walkthroughs in which they identify and treat unhealthy pigs; "the game allowed students to experience 10 swine disease scenarios within a virtual world". Basic workplace mathematical problems using addition, subtraction, fractions, ratios, and percentages were embedded in each scenario, such as completing a veterinarian report. (Bunch et al. (2014))

Claims¶

  • A serious digital game produced no statistically significant effect on secondary students' animal science competence, as measured by a swine health and management examination, compared with lecture and discussion [+M]
  • A serious digital game produced no statistically significant effect on secondary students' mathematics achievement, as measured by a standardized mathematics examination, compared with lecture and discussion [+M]
