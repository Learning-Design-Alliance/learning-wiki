# Questionmark's Perception (QMP)

## Description
Questionmark's Perception is an assessment-delivery and reporting platform made by Questionmark for administering online tests, formative evaluations, feedback, and performance analysis.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Questionmark's Perception (QMP) automated formative assessment platform with immediate feedback and reporting**: Questionmark's Perception (QMP) is a Computer Based Assessment (CBA) software platform used to deliver online reading comprehension modules with formative evaluations and automated immediate feedback. In this study, seven lessons were implemented as 11 online modules, each containing a paper/reading in the field of kinesiology and several related exercises. QMP also generates reports such as the Test Analysis Report, which shows diverse test statistics and reliability analysis, enabling the professor to monitor student performance. (Lazzeri et al. (2015))
