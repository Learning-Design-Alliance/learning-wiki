# Simodont haptic virtual reality dental trainer

## Description
Simodont is a commercial haptic virtual-reality dental training simulator made by Nissin Inc. that provides interactive manual-dexterity exercises, force feedback, automatic scoring, and learner performance feedback.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Simodont haptic virtual reality dental trainer with objective force feedback and automatic scoring**: The Simodont (Nissin Inc.) haptic 3D VR dental simulator provides "objective mechanical force feedback to the learner" alongside automatic scoring, level setting, interactive question forms, post-operation feedback, a Target in Tooth manual dexterity exercise, assessment of adjacent tooth, and a self-assessment tool. The institute purchased 42 virtual reality haptic stations. Cases include simple manual dexterity tasks, cariology cases, and indirect vision exercises; the teacher station can view up to eight trainers at once and project to the classroom screen. (Ranauta A et al. (2025))
- **SIMODONT mirror operation examination task with automated scoring of preparation accuracy and time**: The SIMODONT virtual reality dental trainer was used to examine mirror operation: each student prepared "a specified rectangle hole with rounded corners by a high-speed handpiece using indirect vision through a dental mirror" on the back of a red-pixel-colored cuboid within 10 min. The system automatically recorded total performance time, handpiece working time, completion degree of the hole, and edge damage (leeway bottom, leeway sides, container bottom, container sides), scoring work against standard criteria. (Chu Fengqing et al. (2023))
