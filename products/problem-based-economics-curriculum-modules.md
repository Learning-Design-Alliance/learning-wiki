# Problem Based Economics curriculum modules

## Description
A Buck Institute for Education curriculum package of case-based economics modules for high-school students, developed with university economists and expert teachers and supported by teacher guides, student materials, and professional development.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Problem Based Economics curriculum modules (Buck Institute for Education)**: A problem-based economics curriculum developed since 1995 by the Buck Institute for Education in partnership with university economists and expert teachers, designed to respond to NCEE standards and supported by teacher professional development. Each module is "set up around a case study that is well-suited to student-driven problem solving" with staggered learning of core concepts, and includes a teaching guide laying out the problem statement, objectives, standards, procedures, and do's and don'ts, plus collateral student materials and, where applicable, a DVD with video clips. Units last 4–15 instructional days and provide clear instructions for covering core content. (Finkelstein et al. (2011))

### Claims
