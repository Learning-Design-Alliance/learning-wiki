# Standardized Spatial Skills (SSK) test

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A standardized assessment developed by Lee and published by the Association of American Geographers to measure spatial and geospatial-technology skills in grades 7–12 students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Standardized Spatial Skills (SSK) test measuring geospatial skills in the context of geospatial technologies**: The SSK test by Lee (2007), published by the Association of American Geographers, measures geospatial skills such as geographic relief, spatial interactions, movements, and distributions/patterns of phenomena as surfaces. Lee and Bednarz (2009) gathered validity evidence including a Pearson correlation of 0.578 between SSK scores and geotechnology coursework completed, and reported reliability of Cronbach's alpha = 0.7034. In the present study, the geospatial skills section showed Cronbach's alpha = 0.845. The article states "The first 16 questions assessed spatial skills in the context of geospatial technologies (Lee, 2007)." (Hedley et al. (2013))
