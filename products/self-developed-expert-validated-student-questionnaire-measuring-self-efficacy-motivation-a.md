# Self-developed, expert-validated student questionnaire measuring self-efficacy, motivation, and academic anxiety

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 30-item Likert-scale student questionnaire developed by Aja Lucy et al. to measure self-efficacy, motivation, and anxiety/stress among Ugandan public secondary school students, with expert review and pilot reliability evidence.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Self-developed, expert-validated student questionnaire with Cronbach's alpha reliability of 0.842**: The instrument was "a self-developed questionnaire, and it was not previously published," with the full version included as supplementary material. Five specialists in educational administration and management evaluated the items for consistency, relevance, and clarity against the study objectives. A pilot study with 50 respondents was conducted, and "The investigation, utilizing Cronbach's alpha, produced a reliability index of 0.842 for the students' questionnaire," exceeding the authors' benchmark of α = 0.70. The questionnaire contained ten Likert-scale items for each of three constructs: self-efficacy, motivation, and anxiety/stress. (Aja Lucy et al. (2025))
