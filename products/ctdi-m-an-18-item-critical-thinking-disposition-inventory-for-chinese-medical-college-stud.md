# CTDI-M: an 18-item critical thinking disposition inventory for Chinese medical college students

## Description
An 18-item five-point Likert self-report inventory developed by Xiaoxia Wang et al. to measure critical-thinking disposition among Chinese medical college students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CTDI-M: an 18-item critical thinking disposition inventory for Chinese medical college students**: The CTDI-M is an 18-item, five-point Likert-type questionnaire measuring critical thinking disposition of Chinese medical college students, developed through open-ended, half-open, and closed-ended questionnaire stages from 264 preliminary items. The article reports it "demonstrated a reliable and valid instrument for clinical thinking disposition, which measured motivation and cognitive components." Items include seeking evidence, accepting different views, and avoiding the negative effect of mental set. (Xiaoxia Wang et al. (2015))

### Claims
- [An 18-item CTDI-M for Chinese medical college students shows a three-factor structure (open-mindedness, systematicity/analyticity, truth seeking) explaining 57.66% of variance](../claims/ctdi-m-three-factor-structure.md) [+M]
