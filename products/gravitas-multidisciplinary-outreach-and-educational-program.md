## Description
A multidisciplinary physics and philosophy-of-science outreach programme for high school students, coordinated by the Cagliari division of INFN with content developed by a committee of physicists and philosophers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Public validation dashboard with 243 passing checks across 25 areas, separating check types by evidential weight**: A public dashboard reports scientific verification of the simulation, currently "243/243 passing checks across 25 areas" citing 50 sources, covering orbital periods, Kepler relationships, conservation laws, convergence behavior, escape speed, exoplanet observables, habitable-zone quantities, rotation curves, and black-hole scaling relations. It separates 87 analytic, 109 integrated, 34 published, 12 approximation, and one empirical check so internal arithmetic tests are not presented as equivalent to agreement with observation. Each numeric tolerance has a stated justification and the suite can be rerun in the browser. (Carl Ziegler (2026))
- **Student measurement toolkit turning simulated motion into quantitative evidence**: Gravitas supplies task-specific instruments in both sandbox and guided modes: a ruler, protractor, and scale bar; a simulation-time stopwatch with automatic latching at successive periapsis passages; an object inspector reporting mass, radius, speed, orbital elements, and energies; velocity, acceleration, and source arrows; reference-frame controls; observing panels; specialized plots; and exports. "A ruler, protractor, stopwatch, plots, and event-aware measurements let students attach numbers to what they see." Table 2 maps each tool to the reasoning it supports. (Carl Ziegler (2026))
