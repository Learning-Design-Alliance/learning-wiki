# QISCIT (Quantum Information Science Concept Introductory Test)

## Description
QISCIT is a 31-item concept inventory with an integrated glossary for assessing introductory quantum information science understanding, developed by Kelley Durkin and colleagues and distributed through OSF.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **QISCIT: a 31-item validated multiple-choice concept inventory for introductory quantum information science with an integrated glossary**: QISCIT (Quantum Information Science Concept Introductory Test) is an openly shared assessment instrument whose "final version ... includes the glossary and the remaining 31 multiple-choice questions randomized for assessments." Each question addresses multiple key QIS concepts — quantum states, quantum measurement, qubits, entanglement, coherence and decoherence, quantum gates/computing, and quantum communication — plus subconcepts such as Bell states, specific gates, quantum circuits, and teleportation. It is distributed in PDF, Word, and Qualtrics formats via an OSF repository with concept mappings for each question. (Kelley Durkin et al. (2025))
