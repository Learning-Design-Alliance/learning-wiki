## Description
A competency-classification framework developed by Massaroli and colleagues for organizing healthcare-related infection prevention and control competencies for generalist and specialist nurses.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Three-tier classification of HCRI competencies: core, generic and specific**: The article organizes infection prevention and control competencies into three levels. It states that "Core competencies correspond to those common to all health professions", that generic ones are "common to a professional field of knowledge" and denote what the generalist nurse must have developed upon graduation, and that specific competencies are those inherent to the specialty, expected of a specialist nurse at the end of a specialization course. This taxonomy lets the article differentiate the roles of the generalist and the specialist nurse in infection control. (Massaroli Aline et al. (2019))
