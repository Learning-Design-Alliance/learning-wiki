# Davison et al. (2011) longitudinal bilingual language and literacy dataset

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A longitudinal dataset from 81 bilingual Puerto Rican-heritage Head Start children containing repeated English and Spanish language measures and reading outcomes, collected and analyzed by Davison et al.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Longitudinal bilingual language and literacy dataset from 81 Head Start children with growth curve analysis**: The study's dataset comprises 81 bilingual Puerto Rican-heritage children from low-income families in Central Pennsylvania, assessed in fall and spring of two Head Start years and at the end of first grade. Language measures included the TELD-3 and PPVT-III for English, the PLS-3 and TVIP for Spanish, and the WLPB-R letter-word identification and passage comprehension subtests for reading. Children were divided into Home English Communicators (n = 48) and School English Communicators (n = 33) based on mothers' reports of English exposure timing. Growth curve models were estimated using R-software 2.5.1 with measurement occasion as the time metric. (Davison et al. (2011))
