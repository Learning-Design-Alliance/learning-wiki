# Competency-based framework for disaster nursing education built on the disaster cycle

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A disaster-nursing education framework that organizes prevention, preparedness, response, and recovery competencies across progressive nursing-practice levels and operational domains.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ICN Core Competencies in Disaster Nursing Version 2.0: an eight-domain, three-level competency framework for disaster nursing**: The CCDN V2.0, published by the International Council of Nurses in 2019, is a competency framework for disaster nursing that the article describes as "a comprehensive framework outlining eight essential domains for disaster nursing: preparation and planning, communication, incident management, safety and security, assessment, intervention, recovery, and law and ethics". It stratifies competencies into three levels based on nurses' roles and responsibilities, with Level II nurses defined as any nurse who has achieved Level I competencies and is or aspires to be a designated disaster responder. The framework articulates 33 core competencies at Level II and serves as the basis from which the CCDNS-DRN was translated and validated. The article positions it as an aspirational benchmark guiding improvement of disaster nursing education worldwide. (Lai Jinjia et al. (2025))
