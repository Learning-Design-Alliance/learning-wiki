# SEAL: a Self-Evolving Adaptive Learning system

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
SEAL is a proposed AI-based learning platform that uses student interaction histories and knowledge profiles to recommend personalized study guides, reports, and learning support for pre-tertiary students and their teachers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SEAL: a Self-Evolving Adaptive Learning system for personalized pre-tertiary education at scale**: SEAL is an AI-based system proposed to address the trade-off between quality and scalability in general education, where large classes mean teachers could hardly tailor guidance to each student. It targets teachers and students and delivers features such as customised reports and tailor-made study guides. The system comprises a loosely coupled SEALaaS backend, an AI-based recommender that models student knowledge profiles, and feature-rich client ends for teaching and independent learning. (Junhua Liu et al. (2020))
