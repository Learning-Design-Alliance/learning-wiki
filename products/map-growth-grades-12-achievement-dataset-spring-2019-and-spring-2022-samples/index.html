MAP Growth grades 1–2 achievement dataset (spring 2019 and spring 2022 samples)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes

Description¶

A research dataset assembled by Kuhfeld et al. from NWEA MAP Growth reading and mathematics assessments for approximately 1.6 million first- and second-grade students in public schools, comparing spring 2019 and spring 2022 achievement.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • First- and second-grade spring 2022 achievement in reading and math was lower than spring 2019 prepandemic achievement [+W]
  • Second graders' 2021–22 fall–spring growth in math and reading paralleled prepandemic achievement gains [+W]
  • Achievement declines from 2019 to 2022 were smaller for Asian American and White first and second graders than for Hispanic, Black, and AIAN students [+W]
  • Pandemic achievement declines were uneven across racial/ethnic groups, with the largest declines still apparent for Hispanic, Black, and AIAN students in spring 2022 [+W]
