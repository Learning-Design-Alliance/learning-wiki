Levels of Use Interview¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A focused branching interview developed by Loucks (1977) that assigns overall and category-specific Levels of Use ratings, including Not Doing and No Information classifications.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • LoU Interview ratings correlated .65 with readers' ratings of written ethnographic protocols, which the author read as support for the interview's validity tempered by second-hand information [+W]
  • The LoU Interview shows high interrater reliability, and the rating procedure was streamlined over two years without loss of agreement [+W]
