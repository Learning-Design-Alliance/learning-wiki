## Description
An interactive animated video learning module for simulation-educator refresher training, developed by the LIFT Simulation Design Lab at the University of Utah with PRONTO International and UCSF input.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Super Divya: Origin Story — an interactive animated video module for simulation educator refresher training**: Super Divya: Origin Story is a virtual interactive video module built on a comic superhero protagonist named Divya, developed by the LIFT Simulation Design Lab at the University of Utah with PRONTO International and input from UCSF. Its learning objectives are "to recognize elements of positive simulation facilitation," to "recognize elements of a poor simulation facilitation," to introduce the characters, and to introduce the PRONTO pouch, the virtual toolkit of a simulation educator. Characters are modeled after Nurse Mentors in Bihar, with similar facility layouts, clothing, accents, and Hindi language to simulate known medical settings. It was distributed to NMS in May 2020 via a WhatsApp link and required internet connectivity. (Kalra Anika et al. (2022))
