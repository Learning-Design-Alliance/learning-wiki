## Description
iTalk-iSee is a participatory visual learning analytics tool developed by Hu, Wu, and Chen that codes and visualizes peer talk to support student reflection and regulation of discourse engagement.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **iTalk-iSee participatory visual learning analytical tool for productive peer talk**: The iTalk-iSee tool, by Hu, Wu, and Chen, is a participatory visual learning analytical tool couched in Bakhtinian dialogism that "makes talk processes visible to students", giving them "a position of agency in regulating their engagement". It follows a scaffold with "a three-step paradigm (code visualize reflect)", continuing a progression of analytics tools prompting teacher reflection on discourse practices. (Carolyn Rosé · Sanna Järvelä (2022))
