# 50-domain HI competency framework

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A health-informatics competency framework developed by Jidkov and colleagues for postgraduate medical trainees, derived from IMIA recommendations, curriculum analysis, and expert consultation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **A 50-domain HI competency framework developed from the IMIA recommendations and scoping review mapping**: The study developed a framework of 50 HI competency domains for postgraduate doctors by applying the revised 2010 IMIA recommendations to curricula identified in a scoping review, iteratively adding domains not covered (international developments, medical physics, teaching). The IMIA framework distinguishes 'IT Users' from 'Biomedical Health Informatics (BMHI) specialists' with introductory, intermediate and advanced skill levels. The article reports "A framework of 50 HI competency domains was developed using 21 curricula from a scoping review, curricular content analysis and expert consultation." Domains 5, 9 and 12 were present in all review documents, while health information science, chemoinformatics and nanoinformatics (domains 41–43) appeared in none. (Jidkov Lydia et al. (2019))
