# Professional learning questionnaire characterizing curriculum-based professional learning across districts and schools

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A questionnaire gathering information on professional learning in each district and its schools, grounded in a definition of curriculum-based professional learning (CBPL) as learning that supports implementation and sustained use of specific curriculum with integrity to the design while adapting to student needs. It includes four sections: basic district facts including built-in professional learning hours; types of professional learning structures such as PLCs and summer institutes with average monthly time; alignment with "11 CBPL features, including whether it is individualized or student focused"; and ratings of CBPL and HQIM implementation for each school. It is designed to identify best practices in districts where implementation is going well.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Requires district-level respondents who can report on professional learning structures, hours, and school-level ratings
#### Constraints
- The report notes professional learning resources and supports vary dramatically across districts and remain largely hidden to external actors

### Target Learners
- district administrators and school leaders supporting middle-grades mathematics teachers

### Learning Goals
- characterizing professional learning supports for HQIM implementation
