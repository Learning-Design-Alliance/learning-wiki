Washington, DC's Early Childhood Educator Pay Equity Fund (PEF)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 associational), q1–q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A Washington, DC initiative that provides funding to increase compensation for early childhood educators and reduce pay disparities with K–12 teachers.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • A benefit-cost analysis of DC's Early Childhood Educator Pay Equity Fund finds the initiative to address educator pay gaps has economic value [+W]
  • The PEF's total FY 2024 cost was $76 million, up from $54 million in FY 2023, reflecting larger educator payments and new facility administrative costs [+W]
  • Washington, DC's PEF sustained a positive social return on investment of 21 percent in FY 2024, similar to 23 percent in FY 2023 [+W]
  • Program costs rose under the PEF's facility payment model, but the size and economic value of benefits also increased [+W]
  • The PEF's total FY 2024 economic value of benefits was $93 million, up from $67 million in FY 2023, with benefits to families the largest share [+W]
