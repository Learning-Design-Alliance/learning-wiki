## Description
The EPT is an online mathematics diagnostic and self-learning assessment developed and implemented by the University of Hull for beginning undergraduate mathematics students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Electronic Preparatory Test (EPT) for beginning mathematics undergraduates**: The Electronic Preparatory Test is an online test for beginning undergraduates reading mathematics: "The EPT comprises a diagnostic element and a self-learning element." The diagnostic element probes background knowledge across five topics (algebra, numbers, geometry, functions, calculus) using varied question types including algebraic response and graph sketching; the self-learning element previews university material such as logic and sets. Delivery is platform-based: "The implementation of the EPT at Hull is based on Möbius Assessment (formerly MapleTA)." (Chongchitnan (2018))
