# National Astrophysics and Space Science Programme (NASSP)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A South African multi-institutional postgraduate training pipeline in astrophysics and space science, funded by the Department of Science and Innovation through the National Research Foundation and serving Honours and Master's students progressing toward PhD study.

## Components

- **National Astrophysics and Space Science Programme (NASSP) multi-institutional training pipeline**: NASSP is described as "a multi-institutional initiative funded by the Department of Science and Innovation through the National Research Foundation to train South African students in Astrophysics and Space Science at Honours and Master's levels and to provide a pipeline to PhD studies in these and related research areas". It is presented as an existing intervention promoting scientific development in Africa. (Ramaila (2022))
