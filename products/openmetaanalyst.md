## Description
OpenMeta[Analyst] is a free downloadable meta-analysis program that supports statistical synthesis, heterogeneity analysis, and exploration of heterogeneity sources.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **OpenMeta[Analyst] (OMA) as a free meta-analysis program scaffolding heterogeneity analysis instruction**: OpenMeta[Analyst] is described as "a free downloadable meta-analysis program" used in the course to teach synthesis and heterogeneity interpretation. Its setup wizard, supplemented in class with a decision algorithm, helps students judge between alternative analyses based on collected data; a built-in calculator supports computing or estimating measures not reported in commensurate formats. The article notes OMA facilitates post-hoc exploration of sources of heterogeneity. (Parrott J. Scott (2015))
