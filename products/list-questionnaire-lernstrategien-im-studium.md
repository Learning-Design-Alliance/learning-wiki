## Description
The LIST is a 96-item German online questionnaire used to assess cognitive, metacognitive, and resource-oriented learning strategies in students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **LIST questionnaire (Lernstrategien im Studium) for assessing cognitive, meta-cognitive, and resource-oriented learning strategies**: The LIST is a German questionnaire based on the Anglo-American Motivated Strategies of Learning questionnaire (MSLQ) used to assess medical students' learning strategies. It "classifies various learning activities into the following four domains–cognitive strategy use, meta-cognitive strategy use, intrinsic and extrinsic resource-oriented strategy uses", scored on a 1-to-5 Likert scale. In this study its 96 items covered thirteen subscales, was administered online via EvaSys at two time points, and showed Cronbach's alpha of 0.85 (cognitive), 0.68 (meta-cognitive), 0.76 (intrinsic), and 0.79 (extrinsic resource-oriented) scales. (Hogh Annemarie (2021))
