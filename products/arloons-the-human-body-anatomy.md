## Description
Arloon's augmented-reality anatomy application uses interactive 3D models and self-evaluation activities to teach human body systems, developed by Arloon.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Arloon's The Human Body Anatomy augmented reality application**: The application selected for the study was Arloon's "The human body anatomy", chosen from a shortlist against six criteria including AR features and 3D models, scientifically correct material compatible with students' mental capacity, ease of use, pleasant graphics, self-evaluation questions and tests, and highlighting the relationship between the respiratory and circulatory systems. It had no Greek version, so it was translated into Greek with the developer's permission over about two months. Its learning material closely resembled Greece's sixth-grade science textbooks. (Emmanuel Fokides et al. (2018))
