# Box 1 competency framework: fundamental and application-specific knowledge, skills and attitudes for US use

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A competency framework proposed by Whittaker et al. for physical therapists, specifying fundamental and application-specific ultrasound knowledge, skills, and attitudes needed for safe and effective practice.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Box 1 competency framework: fundamental and application-specific knowledge, skills and attitudes for US use**: Box 1 enumerates the competencies the article deems necessary for safe and efficacious US use, defined via the Canadian National Physiotherapy Advisory group's notion of an essential competency as 'the repertoire of measurable knowledge, skills and attitudes required by a physical therapist throughout their professional career'. It lists fundamental competencies (professional and ethical considerations, communication, basic anatomy and physiology, US basic physics, safety, terminology and instrumentation, image generation and optimisation, interpretation including artefact) plus parallel competency sets for RUSI, diagnostic, interventional and research US. The article recommends that all physical therapists using US meet the fundamental competencies followed by one application-specific set. (Whittaker Jackie L et al. (2019))
