# Dundee Ready Education Environment Measure (DREEM)

## Description
A 50-item standardized questionnaire developed at Dundee University Medical School with an international Delphi panel to assess learners’ perceptions of the medical educational environment across five subscales.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Validated French version of the Dundee Ready Education Environment Measure (DREEM)**: The DREEM is a 50-item instrument assessing students' perceptions of their educational environment, used here in its validated French version. The article states it "consists of 50 items rated on a 5-point Likert scale (0 = strongly disagree to 4 = strongly agree), with a maximum total score of 200", comprising five subscales (SPL, SPT, ASP, SPA, SSSP) with published interpretation bands. In this sample, "internal consistency of the DREEM was excellent (Cronbach's α = 0.91 for the total score)", with subscale alphas from 0.70 to 0.85. Nine negatively worded items were reverse scored before analysis. (Sridi Chayma et al. (2026))
