# 18-item engagement rating list for observing CL engagement

## Description
An 18-item, rater-administered observation instrument developed by Hijzen et al. to assess task-irrelevant, task-relevant, and socially oriented engagement during cooperative-learning sessions.

### Claims

- [Effective and ineffective cooperative learning teams differ significantly only in socially oriented engagement, with ineffective teams showing lower socially oriented engagement](../claims/cl-team-engagement-socially-oriented-difference.md) [+M]
- [In ineffective CL teams, belongingness goals are negatively related to socially oriented task engagement, and no other goal domain relates significantly to engagement](../claims/belongingness-goals-negative-social-engagement.md) [+W]
- [Goal profiles differ between effective and ineffective CL teams: mastery and social responsibility goals prevail in effective teams, entertainment, certificate and work avoidance goals in ineffective teams](../claims/cl-goal-profiles-effective-ineffective-teams.md) [+W]
