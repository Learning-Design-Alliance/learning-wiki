> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
An arts-integrated curriculum package for elementary students in grades 3–5, developed and delivered through the Arts for Learning program.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **How People Learn framework as the pedagogical foundation of the A4L Lessons curriculum**: The report identifies the "How People Learn" framework (Bransford, Brown, & Cocking, 1999) as "the foundation for the program's pedagogy and strategies for student engagement." Under this framework, the curriculum emphasizes teacher-guided, student-initiated activities, encourages students to think and learn independently, and provides tools and strategies to help students approach challenging schoolwork. The arts-integrated curricula are described as giving students opportunities to excel through activities tapping a wide variety of skill sets and learning styles. (Jonathan Nakamoto et al. (2015))
