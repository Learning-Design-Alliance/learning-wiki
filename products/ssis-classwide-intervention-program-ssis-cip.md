# SSIS Classwide Intervention Program (SSIS-CIP)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
SSIS-CIP is a 10-week universal classroom intervention within the Social Skills Improvement System, delivered by classroom teachers to teach primary-grade students social skills and support classroom engagement.
