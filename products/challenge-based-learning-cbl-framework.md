# Challenge Based Learning (CBL) Framework

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A challenge-driven learning framework developed and maintained through Apple-supported Challenge Based Learning initiatives for educators and learners working on authentic real-world challenges.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Challenge Based Learning framework: three phases with key design elements**: The paper describes Challenge Based Learning as connecting student projects to real-world challenges, divided into three interconnected phases: Engage, Investigate, and Act. It lists key elements including that everyone is a learner, learning moves beyond the classroom walls, challenges are learner-inspired and owned, there is space and freedom to fail, technology is authentically used, and learners continuously reflect. As printed: "The CBL Framework is divided into three interconnected phases – Engage, Investigate, and Act." (Barbara Pape et al. (2020))
- **Challenge-Based Learning is a framework for learning while solving real-world challenges through three phases: Engage, Investigate, and Act**: The presentation defines Challenge-Based Learning as "A framework for learning while solving real-world challenges" that "Takes participants through three phases: Engage, Investigate, and Act." It positions CBL as fostering global competence and employability skills such as leadership and collaboration, and as promoting teachers as designers and facilitators rather than transmitters of content. (April Williamson (2024))
