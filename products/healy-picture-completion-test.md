## Description
A standardized performance assessment in which children complete pictures and receive score-, time-, and age-level classifications, administered and scored using Pintner and Anderson procedures.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Healy Picture Completion Test (administered by Pintner and Anderson procedures)**: The Healy Picture Completion Test is a performance test in which the child completes pictures, scored by score and completion time, and classified into age levels such as Below 5, Age 8, and Adult. The article administered it following "the procedure and standards laid down by Pintner and Anderson," noting the test is relatively useless for adults, so no adult cases appear. Across 65 children it produced scores, times, and age-level classifications compared against Terman Binet IQs and ratings. (E. B. Skaggs (1920))
