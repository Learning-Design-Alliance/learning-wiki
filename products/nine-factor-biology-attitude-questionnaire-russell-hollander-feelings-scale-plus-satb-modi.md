# Nine-factor biology attitude questionnaire (Russell & Hollander feelings scale plus SATB-modified SATB)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 62-item online Likert questionnaire adapted by Almasri Firas et al. to assess undergraduate students’ attitudes toward biology across nine dimensions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Nine-factor biology attitude questionnaire (Russell & Hollander feelings scale plus SATS-modified SATB)**: A 62-item questionnaire combining a 14-item Feelings toward Biology section from Russell and Hollander (1975) with a modified Students’ Attitudes Towards science scale (SATB) from Glynn (2007), whose 8 sub-scales (48 questions) cover general interest, motivation, benefit and utility, career motivation, self-efficacy, self-determination, grade motivation, and assessment anxiety. The article used it as the dependent measure, administered online via Qualtrics on a five-point Likert scale, with anxiety items reverse-scored. The article reports "Internal consistency reliability for each factor was higher than 0.7". (Almasri Firas et al. (2021))
