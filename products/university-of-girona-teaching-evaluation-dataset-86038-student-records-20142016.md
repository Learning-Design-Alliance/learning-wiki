## Description
An anonymised dataset of online student teaching evaluations linked to course, teacher, and final-mark records, released with microdata and Stata analysis code by the University of Girona study authors via Dryad.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **University of Girona teaching-evaluation dataset (86,038 student records, 2014–2016) with released microdata and Stata code**: The study's empirical base is the universe of anonymous on-line teaching evaluations collected via Moodle three weeks before final exams at the University of Girona over three years. It comprises "86,038 complete students' records", information on 2,204 different course subjects and 1,832 evaluated teachers, with achievement (final marks 0–10, mean 6.98) linked from student records. The microdata and the Stata econometric code are openly available via the Dryad repository link the article prints. (Ayllón Sara et al. (2019))
