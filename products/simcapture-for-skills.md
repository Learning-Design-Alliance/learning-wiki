# SimCapture for Skills

## Description
SimCapture for Skills is Laerdal's mobile clinical skills-training platform integrating simulators, mobile evaluation checklists, peer-observed performance records, and administrative management for healthcare workplace training.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SimCapture for Skills mobile training system for clinical peer-to-peer SDL**: A mobile e-learning platform combining simulators (mobile skills trainer), evaluation apps on mobile phones, and an administrative backbone to manage training activities distributed at healthcare workplaces. The system, SimCapture for Skills from Laerdal, integrates checklists aligned with evidence-based practices for care tasks including CPR, tracheostomy care, and urinary catheterization. Educational coordinators created checklists following national and/or clinical guidelines, and completed checklists record peer-observed performance of care tasks. (Södersved Källestedt et al. (2025))
