Description¶

The Expository Reading and Writing Course is a curriculum and associated teacher professional-learning and coaching programme developed and implemented to support secondary students' expository reading and writing.

Components¶

  • ERWC professional learning and coaching supports for teachers: Beyond curriculum materials, the ERWC provides "four to six days of professional learning over the course of several months, professional learning communities among participating teachers, and at least four on-site coaching days for each teacher." These supports accompany implementation of the course across the school year. (Expository Reading et al. (2015))
  • Expository Reading and Writing Course (ERWC) for grade 12: The ERWC is a program for 12th graders designed to prepare them to enter the California State University and participating community colleges ready for credit-bearing English courses without remediation. It "replaces ordinary English classes for the whole twelfth grade" and provides curriculum materials, professional learning, professional learning communities, and on-site coaching days for each teacher. (Expository Reading et al. (2015))
  • ERWC instructional focus on discussion, critical thinking, oral language, genre writing, and rhetorical analysis: The ERWC's design centers English instruction on a set of interrelated practices: "discussions of text meaning, developing critical thinking skills, encouraging group discussions, developing oral language skills, developing writing skills in multiple genres, and analyzing the rhetorical features of texts and applying them in student writing." These foci operate across the whole twelfth-grade English course. (Expository Reading et al. (2015))
