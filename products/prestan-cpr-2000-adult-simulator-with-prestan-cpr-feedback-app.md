## Description
PRESTAN's CPR 2000 manikin and connected feedback app provide real-time visual and auditory guidance plus recorded compression and ventilation performance data for CPR training.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PRESTAN CPR 2000 adult simulator with real-time feedback app**: The PRESTAN CPR 2000 adult simulator is a CPR training manikin that "provides visual cues through light indicators on the thoracic side and auditory signals during compressions." It records compression rate and depth, ventilation volume, and pause duration via wireless connection to the PRESTAN CPR feedback app, with data exportable for quantitative analysis. It was purchased for U.S. dollars 400 and used as the single simulator in this study. (Piñeros-Alvarez JL et al. (2026))
