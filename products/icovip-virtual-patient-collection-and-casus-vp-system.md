# iCoViP virtual patient collection and CASUS VP system

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An open-access collection of 200 multilingual virtual patients created by the iCoViP project and implemented in the CASUS learning system for medical students’ clinical-reasoning training.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **iCoViP virtual patient collection and CASUS VP system for clinical reasoning training**: The iCoViP project created a collection of 200 open-access virtual patients representing the European patient population in sociodemographics, disease incidence, and key symptoms, built in the CASUS learning system. VPs include "quiz questions to help interpret findings, modeled patient-physician dialogs... an interactive concept map to visualize the CR process, and the formulation of a summary statement." The project involved over 20 clinicians and took two years to complete. (Fąferek J et al. (2025))
- **Publicly available collection of 75 virtual patients in German, English, and Polish for clinical reasoning training in the CASUS system**: The article describes a publicly available virtual patient collection hosted in the CASUS VP system at https://crt.casus.net/, consisting of "75 VPs in German, English, and Polish". Virtual patients are online cases used to train health profession students in clinical reasoning, including gathering information, formulating and prioritizing differential diagnoses, ordering tests, deciding on a final diagnosis and developing a treatment plan. During the pandemic the collection was adapted and partially translated into Spanish and Portuguese. (Hege I et al. (2020))
