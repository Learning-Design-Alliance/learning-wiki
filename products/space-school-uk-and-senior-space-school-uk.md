## Description
University of Leicester residential summer-school programmes for secondary students that combine space-science teaching, practical experiments, problem-solving, astronomy activities, and astronaut-training experiences.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Space School UK and Senior Space School UK residential summer schools**: Space School UK and Senior Space School UK are 'six - and eight -day fee -paying, non -selective, residential summer schools at the University of Leicester' for students aged 13–15 and 16–18 respectively. Students experience undergraduate-style lectures, small-group practical experiments and problem-solving workshops, plus scuba diving as astronaut-training experience, hands-on use of ground-based telescopes, and a night of camping to observe the Perseids. The programme is run by mentors who are all alumni, and its 30-year history underpins the alumni survey evaluated in this paper. (Robson et al. (2019))
