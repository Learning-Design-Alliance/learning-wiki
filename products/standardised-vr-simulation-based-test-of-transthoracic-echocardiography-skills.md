# Standardised VR simulation-based test of transthoracic echocardiography skills

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 50-point simulator-based assessment developed by Nielsen et al. for evaluating physicians' transthoracic echocardiography competencies across image acquisition, ejection-fraction estimation, diagnosis, and anatomy identification.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Standardised VR simulation-based test of transthoracic echocardiography skills (50-point test with two diagnostic cases and an anatomy quiz)**: A standardised assessment tool delivered on an ultrasound simulator: "The ultrasound simulator resembles an ultrasound machine with a mannequin torso, a touch screen and a sector probe." The test comprises an introductory normal-findings case, a case of acute myocardial infarction with 17 requested projections and ejection-fraction estimation, a case of mitral insufficiency in a 9-year-old boy, and an anatomical structures quiz, with a cumulative maximum score of 50 points. Expert panels in cardiology and simulation established content against international guidelines. (Nielsen Martine S. et al. (2022))
