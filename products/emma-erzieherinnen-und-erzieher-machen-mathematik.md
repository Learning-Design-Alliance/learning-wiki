# EmMa (Erzieherinnen und Erzieher machen Mathematik)

## Description
EmMa is a year-long continuous professional development programme run for in-service early childhood teachers to develop early-mathematics content and pedagogical-content knowledge through module-based instruction, classroom application, documentation, cooperation, and reflection.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **EmMa – a year-long competence-oriented continuous professional development course on early mathematics education**: EmMa (Erzieherinnen und Erzieher machen Mathematik) is a continuous professional development course for in-service early childhood teachers on early mathematics education. It comprises two introductory and four basic modules, lasts one year with a workload of approximately 100 hours, and combines presence phases (theoretical input, self-reflection, mathematical experiences) with practical phases in which teachers apply new knowledge and document children's work. Its design follows DZLM principles: competence-orientation, participant-orientation, case-relatedness, various instruction formats, stimulating cooperation and fostering (self-)reflection. (Julia Bruns et al. (2017))
