# MAP Growth math assessment data corpus

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A large corpus of NWEA MAP Growth mathematics assessment data, including standard and accessible test administrations, used to study item accessibility and quality for students with and without visual impairments.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MAP Growth math assessment data corpus for accessibility research**: The study draws on MAP Growth math assessment data from two populations: about 29.5 million students taking standard versions of the assessment and 48,845 students taking accessible versions. This large-scale corpus enabled the researchers to "identified high-quality items, those that measured achievement for both students with and without VI equally well, and low-quality items, which showed differences between the two groups of students." The corpus is the empirical foundation for the item-quality analysis and the machine-learning prediction work described in the brief. (Kang Xue (2022))
