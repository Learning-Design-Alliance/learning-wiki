# Maslach Burnout Inventory–Student Survey (MBI-SS), English version

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 15-item, seven-point Likert self-report assessment developed from the Maslach Burnout Inventory to measure student exhaustion, cynicism, and professional efficacy and classify each domain as low, moderate, or high.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **English version of the Maslach Burnout Inventory-Student Survey (MBI-SS)**: The English version of the Maslach Burnout Inventory-Student Survey (MBI-SS), a modified version of the Maslach Burnout Inventory-General Survey, is a self-administered questionnaire used in this study to assess burnout prevalence. Its 15 questions cover exhaustion (five items), cynicism (four items) and professional efficacy (six items) on a seven-point Likert scale from zero (never) to six (always), with domain scores categorised as high, moderate or low. It "has been validated and has demonstrated adequate reliability among students from Portugal, Spain, the Netherlands and China". (Aljadani Ahmed H. et al. (2021))
