# Meta-analytic evidence base of 14 studies on phonics instruction for students with intellectual disability

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), `q4` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A corpus of eight single-case and six group experimental or quasi-experimental studies comprising 297 participants, assembled to synthesize evidence on phonics instruction and decoding outcomes for students with intellectual disability.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Meta-analytic evidence base of 14 studies (eight single-case, six group) on phonics instruction for students with intellectual disability**: The evidence base synthesized by this meta-analysis consists of "Eight single-case experimental studies and six experimental or quasi-experimental group studies" that "met the inclusion criteria, encom- passing a total of 297 participants with intellectual disability". Effects were pooled with a random-effects model, and moderators including study design, outcome-measure type and interventionist type were examined. (Sermier Dessemontet et al. (2019))
