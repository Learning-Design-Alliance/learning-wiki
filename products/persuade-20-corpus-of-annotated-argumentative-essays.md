# PERSUADE 2.0 corpus of annotated argumentative essays

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A large-scale dataset of more than 25,000 U.S. student argumentative essays annotated for discourse elements, holistic scores, and component effectiveness ratings, created for training and evaluating automated essay-scoring models.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Critical thinking essay scoring dataset with six rubrics across three skills**: The dataset of Peczuh et al. contains "500 student essays from the public portion of the PERSUADE 2.0 corpus", argumentative writing samples from 6th-12th grade U.S. students on civic, scientific, and social topics. It defines six rubrics organized under three broader skills (Information Analysis, Argument Generation, Logical Reasoning), with two rubrics per skill, each assigning a 5-point label (0-4: Not Applicable, Below Emerging, Emerging, Expanding, Exemplifying). Because each essay is annotated under all six rubrics, the dataset allows essays and rubrics to be decoupled for cross-rubric study. (Nischal Ashok Kumar et al. (2026))
