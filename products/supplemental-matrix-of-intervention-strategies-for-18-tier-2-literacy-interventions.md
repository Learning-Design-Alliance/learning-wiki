# Supplemental matrix of intervention strategies for 18 Tier 2 literacy interventions

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
The study team developed a supplemental matrix (table 2) that "outlines essential implementation strategies used in the 18 interventions reviewed", organized by the seven MiMTSS key elements and discrete strategies such as structured curriculum, gradual skill-building, leveled materials, learning pathways, guided and independent practice, corrective and supportive feedback, embedded progress monitoring systems, assessment tools, peer support, and scaffolded support. It is intended to help CNMI PSS staff evaluate feasibility and inform selection of a Tier 2 literacy intervention, and to provide strategies for a homegrown intervention if branded options are not feasible.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Coding focused on recording the strategies specified in each program model rather than those implemented in the studies reviewed, because documents often lack critical implementation details
#### Constraints
- The individual strategies should not be considered evidence based since the ESSA evidence ratings were synthesized at the intervention level

### Target Learners
- Students in grades K–3 requiring supplemental Tier 2 literacy support

### Target Learning Goals
- Selection and implementation of evidence-informed Tier 2 literacy intervention strategies
