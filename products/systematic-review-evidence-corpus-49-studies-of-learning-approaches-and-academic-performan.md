## Description
A PRISMA-guided corpus of 49 empirical studies on learning approaches and academic performance among undergraduate medical and dental students, assembled by Marlen A. Roehe et al.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Systematic review evidence corpus: 49 studies of learning approaches and academic performance in medical and dental education (2000–2023)**: A PRISMA 2020-guided, PROSPERO-registered (CRD42023474280) systematic review corpus of empirical, peer-reviewed studies published 2000–2023 on learning approaches and academic performance in medical and dental education. "This systematic search yielded a total of 859 publications" across eight databases, screened down to "Records included in systematic review (n = 49)". The corpus spans 26 countries, with the UK most represented (16.3%), and yielded "470 co-occurrences were observed between teaching methods, learning approaches, and academic performance". Quantitative studies were quality-appraised with an adapted MERSQI and qualitative studies with QualSyst. (Marlen A. Roehe et al. (2024))
