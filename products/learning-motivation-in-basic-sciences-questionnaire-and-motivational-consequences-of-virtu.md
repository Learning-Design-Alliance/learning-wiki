# Learning Motivation in Basic Sciences Questionnaire and Motivational Consequences of Virtual CBL Sessions Questionnaire

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Two researcher-developed 0–5 Likert self-report questionnaires by Alizadeh et al. that measure medical students' motivation to learn basic sciences and perceived motivational consequences of virtual case-based learning sessions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Two researcher-developed motivation questionnaires validated via Lawshe's method and Cronbach's alpha**: Two questionnaires were developed through individual semi-structured interviews with six students and content analysis: a 12-item Learning Motivation in Basic Sciences Questionnaire and a 10-item Motivational Consequences of Virtual CBL Sessions Questionnaire, rated 0–5 from strongly disagree to strongly agree. "Cronbach's alpha for the learning motivation questionnaire and the motivational outcomes questionnaire of case-based learning sessions was calculated to be 0.92 and 0.89, respectively." Items map onto the three SDT needs. (Alizadeh M et al. (2024))
