# World Wide Data Day (W2D2)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
World Wide Data Day is a CERN-associated international school programme in which students analyze CMS or ATLAS dimuon events, combine class measurements into worldwide histograms, and discuss the results through videoconference.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **World Wide Data Day: a school-based masterclass-style measurement culminating in global videoconferences**: World Wide Data Day (W2D2) is a single day of videoconferences concluding student and teacher measurements done that day or in the weeks before, with 'a compact masterclass-style experience that can be done completely from school, without the need to visit a masterclass institution or even involve a physicist locally.' Teachers help students analyze CMS or ATLAS events for dimuon events, measure muon direction angles with a protractor, bin results into class histograms, and enter results into a Google spreadsheet displaying combined worldwide histograms, followed by the videoconference discussion. (K. Cecire et al. (2019))
