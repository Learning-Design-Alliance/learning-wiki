## Description
Responsive Classroom is a school-based social and emotional learning programme that organizes classroom practice around components including Morning Meeting, Rule Creation, Interactive Modeling, and Academic Choice.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Responsive Classroom core component indices: Morning Meeting, Rule Creation, Interactive Modeling, and Academic Choice**: The Responsive Classroom approach is a school-based social and emotional learning program whose implementation was indexed through four core components. The article states that "Indices of teachers' implementation of Morning Meeting, Rule Creation, Interactive Modeling, and Academic Choice were derived from a combination of teacher-reported surveys and classroom observations." These component indices served as predictors of teacher–student classroom interaction quality among 143 treatment teachers in third, fourth, and fifth grade classrooms within a randomized controlled trial. (Abry et al. (2017))
