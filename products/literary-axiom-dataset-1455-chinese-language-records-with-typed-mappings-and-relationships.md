## Description
Qiang Liu et al.'s dataset provides 1,455 Chinese-language literary-axiom records with typed mappings to literary works and typed relationships for literary scholarship and computational-creativity research.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Literary axiom dataset: 1,455 Chinese-language records with typed mappings and relationships**: A concrete implementation of the framework consisting of axioms_consolidated.json and relationships.json, with statements, descriptions, and notes primarily in Chinese. The abstract reports that "A concrete implementation contains 1,455 Chinese-language records, 1,464 mappings to 149 works, and 472 typed relationships." Records carry category (content/form), contextual-scope type, abstraction level, domain/sub-domain, era and region context lists, keywords, and literary examples; mappings use primary, secondary, and technique roles, and relationship links use five typed labels. (Qiang Liu et al. (2026))
