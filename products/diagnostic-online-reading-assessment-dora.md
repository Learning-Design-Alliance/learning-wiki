## Description
DORA is an online diagnostic reading assessment developed and provided by Let's Go Learn to measure learners' reading skills, including comprehension and vocabulary.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Diagnostic Online Reading Assessment (DORA) as pre/post reading outcome measure**: The Diagnostic Online Reading Assessment (DORA) is the online instrument used to measure comprehension and vocabulary gains in the study, administered to control and experimental groups in September and October and again in April. It "assesses comprehension with the usage of short passages," which aligned with the literature and short passages used during read-aloud. More information is available at www.letsgolearn.com. (Jennifer Kohart Marchessault (2013))
