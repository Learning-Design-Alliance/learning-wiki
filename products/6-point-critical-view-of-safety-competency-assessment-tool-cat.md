## Description
A three-criterion, 0–6 scoring instrument adapted from Sanford et al. for assessing the critical view of safety in laparoscopic cholecystectomy videos.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **6-point critical view of safety competency assessment tool (adapted from Sanford et al.)**: A 6-point competency assessment tool (CAT) for scoring the critical view of safety in laparoscopic cholecystectomy videos, adapted from Sanford et al. It comprises three criteria — "1. clearance of hepatocystic triangle, 2. cystic plate, and 3. two structures connect to the gallbladder" — each scored 0 to 2 for a maximum total of 6, where a higher score indicates superior surgical performance and a score of 5 or 6 indicates a favorable CVS. (Grüter Alexander A. J. et al. (2024))
