# Extreme Energy Events (EEE) project

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An INFN-run Italian particle-physics outreach and education programme in which high-school students assemble and maintain MRPC muon telescopes and analyse real cosmic-ray data through a distributed school-and-laboratory network.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Extreme Energy Events (EEE) muon telescope network involving students in assembly and data analysis**: The Extreme Energy Events project, described as "one of the most successful projects", is a large-area array based on multigap resistive plate chambers (MRPCs). Students are "directly involved in the assembly, maintenance and data analysis", with EEE stations located in high schools and at CERN or INFN laboratories, and additional participating schools without a telescope. The project is run by INFN in Italy as part of the outreach cosmic ray activity landscape. (IPPOG Collaboration (2025))
