## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Final PBLS assessment instrument with nine consensus items for laypersons**: A nine-item checklist for assessing laypersons' paediatric basic life support skills, produced by the Delphi panel. The items are Responsiveness, Call for help, Open airway, Check breathing, Rescue breaths, Compressions, Ventilations, Time factor and Use of AED. The article states that "Description wordings have been aligned to ease the use. Content has not been changed." The instrument is intended to determine when laypersons have acquired effective PBLS competencies. (Hasselager AB et al. (2018))
