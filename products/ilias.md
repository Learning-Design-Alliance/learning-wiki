## Description
ILIAS is an open-source learning management and collaboration platform developed and maintained by the ILIAS open-source community for delivering and managing digital learning content.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ILIAS open-source learning platform as the delivery infrastructure for RehAmpTT**: The learning content was implemented on the Integrated Learning, Information and Work Cooperation System (ILIAS), the open-source learning platform of the Bundesarbeitsgemeinschaft digitale Lehre. The article notes that "The open-source software ILIAS is released under the GNU General Public License (GPL), so that it can be provided with content free of charge and likewise used free of charge by the general public." Technical bilingual implementation was carried out by staff of the Peter L. Reichertz Institute for Medical Informatics at MHH. (Egen C et al. (2025))
