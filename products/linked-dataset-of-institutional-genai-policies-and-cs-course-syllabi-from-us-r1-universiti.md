# Linked dataset of institutional GenAI policies and CS course syllabi from U.S. R1 universities

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 qualitative), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A research dataset created by Amrita Ganguly et al. that links publicly available GenAI policy documents from U.S. R1 universities with coded computer-science course syllabi to study alignment between institutional and course-level guidance.
