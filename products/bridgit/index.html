Bridgit¶

Description¶

Bridgit is a school-counseling platform used to track students’ pre-matriculation tasks, tailor college-enrollment steps, and support counselor communication.

Components¶

  • Bridgit platform for tracking pre-matriculation tasks: Bridgit is "a schoolwide intervention used by school counselors to track students' progress toward college enrollment". Students create profiles identifying their chosen college and admissions status; the system then prioritizes pre-matriculation steps tailored to that college. Counselors monitor prioritized task lists and communicate via text messages directly in the platform, with phone calls for follow-up. It was evaluated in a cluster randomized trial of six intervention and six comparison schools in Tennessee and Missouri (3,281 students). (Summer Counseling (2018))

Claims¶

  • In the Castleman et al. (2012) RCT, summer counseling showed a substantively important positive effect on fall college enrollment despite non-significant test statistics [+W]
  • Summer counseling has mixed effects on college access and enrollment for recent high school graduates, with a domain-average improvement index of +5 percentile points [+W]
  • Summer counseling produces a small but statistically significant positive effect on credit accumulation and persistence (WWC rating: potentially positive effects) [+W]
  • Three of the five reviewed RCTs found no statistically significant effect of summer counseling on fall college enrollment (indeterminate effects) [-W]
