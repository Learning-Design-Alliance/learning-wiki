## Description
A supervised eight-session coping-strategy intervention programme developed by researchers for family carers of people with dementia, with a manual, individualised planning materials, and relaxation audio resources.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **START: a manualised eight-session coping-strategy intervention for family carers of people with dementia**: START (STrAtegies for RelaTives) "comprises eight manualised sessions delivered to individual carers aimed at supporting them to develop adaptive coping strategies". Sessions cover learning about dementia and carer stress, behavioural management, relaxation, legal and care planning and pleasant activities; carers keep a manual with an individualised plan and a relaxation audio resource. The article disseminated it via free online materials. (Lord Kathryn et al. (2017))
