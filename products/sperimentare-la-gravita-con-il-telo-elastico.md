# Sperimentare la gravità con il telo elastico

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An open-access bilingual curriculum resource of eight step-by-step rubber-sheet gravity and general-relativity activity cards for high-school learners, produced by Adriana Postiglione and collaborators.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Eight educational cards guiding step-by-step rubber-sheet GR activities, collected in an open-access eBook**: A set of eight educational cards, written in Italian and English and collected in the open-access eBook "Sperimentare la gravità con il telo elastico". "Overall we developed eight educational cards that guide teachers step by step in carrying out different activities focused on phenomena related to gravity, using the rubber sheet." Each card contains the topic, purpose, materials list, steps, and notes with images, videos and links. (Adriana Postiglione (2020))
