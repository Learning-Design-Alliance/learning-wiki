# C-loop proficiency criteria: a 23-point procedural checklist, a 5-point knot quality scale, and a competency table

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A laparoscopic suturing competency-assessment protocol that combines procedural implementation scoring, knot-quality ratings, and timed performance thresholds for medical-student training.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **C-loop proficiency criteria: a 23-point procedural checklist, a 5-point knot quality scale, and a competency table**: The protocol defines proficiency with three instruments: a previously validated modified 23-point procedural implementation checklist originally published by Munz et al., a 5-point knot quality scale by Muresan et al. assessing throws, tightness, edge opposition and tension, and a competency table setting goals. The protocol states a trainee demonstrates competency "upon finishing it in ≤01:15 (min:sec), attaining ≥18 points on the procedural implementation checklist, scoring ≥4 on the knot quality scale, and maintaining stitching ≤2 mm" from the pad's entry and exit points. (Hendrie JD et al. (2016))
