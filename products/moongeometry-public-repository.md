# moonGeometry public repository

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A public repository created by Hugo Caerols and colleagues containing sample Moon photographs, GeoGebra files, and spreadsheet resources for calculating curved distances and areas and exploring spherical geometry.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **moonGeometry public repository with sample images and GeoGebra files for curved distance and area calculations**: A public GitHub repository (https://github.com/raxlab/moonGeometry) released by the authors containing sample Moon images and GeoGebra files. The article states: "We have left sample images and GeoGebra ﬁles in a public repository that can be found at https://github.com/raxlab/moonGeometry". Using the code, a student gives three points in the photograph and, with a spreadsheet, computes the total curved area of interest; the geodesic formula (Eq. 7) can also be programmed in GeoGebra to compute distances efficiently. (Hugo Caerols et al. (2021))
