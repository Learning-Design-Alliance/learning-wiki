# Nanotechnology Reflection Survey

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 17-item five-point Likert survey created by Diefes-Dux and colleagues to measure undergraduate students' motivation for studying nanotechnology and plans to engage in nanotechnology-related activities.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Nanotechnology Reflection Survey instrument measuring motivation across 17 items**: The Nanotechnology Reflection Survey, created by Diefes-Dux and colleagues, was administered at the beginning and end of the fifteen-week semester to students in both NSNT courses. "Students' self-reported levels of motivation for studying nanotechnology was measured across 17 items", each on a five-point scale from strongly disagree (1) to strongly agree (5). Participation was voluntary and uncompensated, with hard-copy and electronic versions for campus-based and distance students respectively. (Kaabouch et al. (2017))
