## Description

Its assistant avatar Mojo integrates pre-programmed content from ELA curricula with generative AI (GPT-4o). ## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Integrated within existing high-quality instructional materials to maintain pedagogical fidelity
- a mediating layer of instructional coaching that translates raw data into pedagogically-driven classroom practice
#### Constraints
- Automated feedback is not always precisely differentiated for students' developmental level

### Target Learners
- fifth through eighth grade students
- multilingual learners
- students with IEPs

## Description
Coursemojo is an AI-powered teaching and learning platform for English Language Arts instruction. Its intelligent assistant, named Mojo, "works alongside classroom teachers to provide individualized support to every student while maintaining the collaborative, social nature of classroom learning." It integrates with existing curricula rather than replacing instruction. Implementation requires a device per student, pre-launch training, and no additional staffing.
