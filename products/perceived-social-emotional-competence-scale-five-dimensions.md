## Description
A five-dimension, 1–7 student self-report scale developed by Collie for assessing adolescents’ perceived competence in social-emotional skills.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **"For me it's Easy" scale for personal and socio-emotional skills**: A self-report evaluation tool of personal and socio-emotional skills for children and adolescents, built from the social skills checklist (Goldstein & McGinnis, 1997) and the emotional self-regulation questionnaire (Moilanen, 2007), with back translation of some items. The final version consists of 43 items "that include competencies from the simplest to the most complex", with several inverted items, and was used to assess the effect of the Social and Emotional Skills Promotion Program. (Tania Gaspar et al. (2018))
