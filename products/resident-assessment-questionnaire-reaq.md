## Description
The ReAQ is a 20-item questionnaire developed to assess resident physicians' performance across the six ACGME core competencies during standardized residency training.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Resident assessment questionnaire (ReAQ) measuring six ACGME core competencies**: The ReAQ is a resident performance instrument the article uses to measure training performance. It "consists of 20 items that assess the six core competencies identified by the Accreditation Council for Graduate Medical Education", administered to help assess resident performance during training. In this study it served as the performance measure on the 5-point Likert-type scale (Cronbach's α = 0.92, composite reliability 0.94, AVE 0.50 as printed in Table 1). (Deng Guangwei et al. (2019))
