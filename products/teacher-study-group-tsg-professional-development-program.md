## Description
A school-site professional-development programme developed by Russell Gersten and colleagues in which literacy personnel facilitate recurring teacher study-group sessions using a facilitator's guide, trained facilitators, and collaborative planning to improve vocabulary instruction.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Teacher Study Group (TSG) professional development program with facilitator's guide and five-phase session process**: The TSG intervention is a concentrated professional development effort to improve first grade teachers' vocabulary teaching practice and student vocabulary outcomes. It consists of 11 interactive twice-monthly school-site sessions of approximately 75 minutes, covering topics such as Words in Context, Selecting Words to Teach, and Student Friendly Definitions. Each session follows a five-phase recursive process: Debrief, Discuss the Focus Research Concept, Compare the Focus Research Concept with Practice, Plan Collaboratively, and Assignment. Facilitators use the TSG facilitator's guide providing a specific "game plan" and attend a 2-day training with ongoing support. (Russell Gersten et al. (2013))
