## Description
Regular Upward Bound is a federal pre-college program evaluated by Mathematica for the U.S. Department of Education. The evaluation measures its effects on "postsecondary enrollment and receipt of financial aid" and on "the completion of bachelor's or associate's degrees," with outcomes observed 7-9 years after participants' scheduled high school graduation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Operates with high school students whose postsecondary outcomes can be tracked via surveys, transcripts, National Student Clearinghouse records, and federal Student Financial Aid files
#### Constraints
- The evaluation found no detectable effects on enrollment, financial aid, or degree completion; only vocational certificate or license receipt increased

### Target Learners
- High school students targeted for college access support

### Target Learning Goals
- Postsecondary enrollment
- Financial aid application and receipt
- Postsecondary certificate, license, and degree completion
