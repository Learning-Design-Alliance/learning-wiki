# UNESCO AI Competency Framework for Teachers

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A UNESCO framework specifying 15 AI-related teacher competencies across five dimensions to guide national competency frameworks and teacher training programmes.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Three progression levels — Acquire, Deepen, Create — structure teacher AI competency development**: Within the framework, the 15 competencies are "categorized into three progression levels: Acquire, Deepen, Create." This progression axis describes how teachers advance from initial acquisition of AI-related competencies through deepening them to creating new applications and practices. It complements the five-dimension structure and is intended to inform the design of teacher training programmes and assessment parameters. (Miao et al. (2024))
