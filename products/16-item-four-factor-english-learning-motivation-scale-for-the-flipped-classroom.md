# 16-item four-factor English learning motivation scale for the flipped classroom

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A 16-item, five-point Likert questionnaire developed by Wang Zhonger et al. to measure Chinese college students' English-learning motivation across autonomous, instrumental, integrative, and intrinsic motivation factors in flipped-classroom contexts.
