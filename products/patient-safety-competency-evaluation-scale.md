# Patient Safety Competency Evaluation Scale

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 35-item, six-dimension Likert self-report scale developed by Wang et al. (2018) to assess patient safety competency among nursing populations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Patient Safety Competency Evaluation Scale (35-item, six dimensions)**: A 35-item self-report instrument developed by Wang et al. (2018) based on the Canadian Patient Safety Competency Framework, measuring "patient‐centred care, clinical practice, continuous quality improvement, evidence‐based nursing practice, patient safety culture, and safety risk management" on a five-point Likert scale (total 35–175). In this study Cronbach's α was 0.993. (Chen Daoming et al. (2026))
- **Patient safety competency, safety culture perception, and safety control measurement scales**: The study employed three validated scales: the patient safety competency scale (14 items, Cronbach's alpha 0.91) developed by Schnall et al. (2008) and revised by Lee et al. (2014); the perception of patient safety culture scale (35 items across seven subscales, alpha 0.93) modified for Korea by Lee (2015); and the safety control scale (7 items, alpha 0.84) developed by Anderson et al. (2004) and translated by Chung (2009). All used five-point Likert scoring. (Choi Jung-hyun (2024))
