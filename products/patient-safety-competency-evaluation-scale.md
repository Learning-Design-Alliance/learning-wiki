> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A 35-item, six-dimension Likert self-report scale developed by Wang et al. (2018) to assess patient safety competency among nursing populations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Patient Safety Competency Evaluation Scale (35-item, six dimensions)**: A 35-item self-report instrument developed by Wang et al. (2018) based on the Canadian Patient Safety Competency Framework, measuring "patient‐centred care, clinical practice, continuous quality improvement, evidence‐based nursing practice, patient safety culture, and safety risk management" on a five-point Likert scale (total 35–175). In this study Cronbach's α was 0.993. (Chen Daoming et al. (2026))
