# Rusty Flying Robots course

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An openly accessible masters-level practical course and teaching-material package from TU Berlin that teaches modern flight-stack mathematics and implementation using the Bitcraze Crazyflie 2.1 quadrotor.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Rusty Flying Robots course and openly accessible teaching material on a Crazyflie 2.1 quadrotor platform**: A 9 ECTS (270 h) masters-level practical course at TU Berlin built around a Bitcraze Crazyflie 2.1, a "commercially-off-the-shelf (CoTS) open-source and open-hardware quadrotor with two CoTS extensions (for optical flow and data recording)". The roughly 35 g robot is safe for humans and needs no special external equipment. Teaching material is openly accessible at https://imrclab.github.io/teaching/flying-robots, providing a blueprint the authors say can be readily deployed in other universities. (Wolfgang Hönig et al. (2026))
