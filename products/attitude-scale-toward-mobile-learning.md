## Description
A 45-item, four-factor Likert instrument developed by Demir and Akpınar to measure undergraduate students' attitudes toward mobile learning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Attitude Scale Toward Mobile Learning instrument**: A 5-point Likert-type attitude instrument developed by the researchers (Demir & Akpınar, 2016) to measure attitudes toward mobile learning. The final version consists of 45 items with four factors (satisfaction, effect to learning, motivation, usability) explaining 50.34% of total variance. The article reports "The Cronbach's alpha internal consistency coefficient of the scale was found .950", KMO of 0.93, and a significant Bartlett test (χ2=7820,10; p<,000). It was used for pre-test, post-test and follow-up measurement of both groups. (Kadir DEMIR (2018))
