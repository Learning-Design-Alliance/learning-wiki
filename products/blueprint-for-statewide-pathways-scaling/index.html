Blueprint for Statewide Pathways Scaling¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes

Description¶

A Babineau et al. framework for coordinating state and regional policy, labor-market assessment, K–12 pathway scale-up, postsecondary alignment, and regional partnerships to expand career-connected pathways.

Components¶

  • Blueprint for Statewide Pathways Scaling (five-component theory of action): The report's central framework is a theory of action for scaling career pathways statewide, consisting of "three critical layers": state-level actors, models, and policy/funding mechanisms; regional-level district cohorts, pathways design, and partnerships; and measurable outcomes across education and economic systems. It is operationalized through five complementary components—ACTIVATE (state and regional policy levers), ASSESS (employment market and skills opportunity), CREATE (K-12 pathways scale-up model), ALIGN (postsecondary and credentials landscape), and CONNECT (regional infrastructure)—each illustrated with Alabama Workforce Region 2 implementation. The framework emphasizes reciprocal state-regional interplay and strengthening existing relationships rather than creating new systems. (Babineau et al. (2025))
  • Three-tiered core-pilot-replication implementation model for scaling across contexts: To manage multiple contexts on a pathway to scale, the report proposes a three-tiered system classifying participating entities on a continuum from core to pilot to replication, indicating their readiness for involvement. Core entities are co-leaders and co-experts with decision-making power who test and refine a solution first; pilot entities test the alpha and contribute case studies and data; replication entities serve as beta test sites for production-ready solutions. The paper states this model supports development, rapid-cycle iteration, and testing in environments reflecting multiple contexts. (Angevine et al. (2019))
  • Five-pillar statewide blueprint: ACTIVATE, CONNECT, CREATE, ALIGN, ASSESS: The blueprint organizes pathway design into five pillars spanning state, region, school districts, postsecondary, and industry. Each pillar provides "Brief overview, Core factors, Top policies, conditions, systems, and practices that must be true, Key strategic questions." The pillars are ACTIVATE state and regional policy levers, CONNECT the regional infrastructure, CREATE K-12 pathways scale up models, ALIGN postsecondary and credentials landscape, and ASSESS state employment market and skills opportunity. The article says the blueprint "is designed as a starting point that can be used to define the foundation of a regional and/or statewide plan." (Scaling career pathways statewide: A blueprint for creating K-12 + postsecondary + industry pathways (2026))
  • Seven-dimension pathway quality standards spanning outcomes-driven practice, equity culture, industry themes, project-based learning, work-based learning, supports, and distributed leadership: The report proposes state quality standards for college and career pathways mirroring the seven dimensions on which certified Linked Learning pathways produced positive impacts. Standards include that "The progress of every student is the driving focus for evaluating pathway quality," a culture of high expectations with nondiscriminatory and inclusive practices, an industry-themed program of study offering dual credit, inquiry- and project-based learning that is "relevant, rigorous, outcome-focused, and collaborative in nature," a personalized continuum of work-based learning for every student, personalized academic and social-emotional supports, and shared responsibility among staff, leaders, and community partners. (Hoachlander (2020))
  • Entry, action and goal journey maps: the maps that codify success criteria for a learner's and a school system's pathway journey

Claims¶

  • Alabama's cybersecurity labor market shows sustained demand, with about 18,778 professionals employed and 8,373 new job openings posted in 2024 [+M]
  • The blueprint asserts that K-12 systems are well positioned to advance students into entry-level career credentials and employment [+W]
