## Description
The Teach For America (TFA) Investing in Innovation scale-up was a program that recruited and trained new corps members placed as teachers in high-poverty schools. The report evaluates first- and second-year corps members hired during the first two years of the scale-up period, comparing their students' reading and math achievement with that of students of other teachers "in the same high-poverty schools." The evaluation covered reading and math outcomes across elementary grade bands.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Corps members are recruited and trained through the TFA program before placement as teachers in high-poverty schools.
#### Constraints
- Findings apply to corps members hired in the first two years of the scale-up period in high-poverty schools.

### Target Learners
- Students in high-poverty schools, prekindergarten through grade 5

### Target Learning Goals
- Reading achievement
- Math achievement
