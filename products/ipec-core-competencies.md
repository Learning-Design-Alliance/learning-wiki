## Description
A competency framework developed by the Interprofessional Education Collaborative that organises interprofessional collaborative practice around Values and Ethics, Roles and Responsibilities, Communication, and Teams and Teamwork.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **IPEC framework of four core competencies for interprofessional collaboration**: The Interprofessional Education Collaborative (IPEC) framework identifies "four core competencies of interprofessional collaboration": Values and Ethics, Roles and Responsibilities, Communication, and Teams and Teamwork. The review states these competencies "enhance patient care, promote population health and reduce healthcare costs". The review used this framework deductively, mapping inductively derived themes and sub-themes from 88 included studies onto the four IPEC competencies to determine which competencies IPE develops. (A scoping review of interprofessional education in healthcare: evaluating competency devel)
