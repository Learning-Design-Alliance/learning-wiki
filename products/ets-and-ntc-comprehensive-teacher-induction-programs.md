# ETS and NTC comprehensive teacher induction programs

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3`–`q4` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
Comprehensive teacher-induction programmes developed by the Educational Testing Service and the New Teacher Center that provide beginning teachers with trained mentors, orientation, professional development, observation, and formative feedback.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Comprehensive Teacher Induction (CTI) programs by ETS and NTC**: The CTI programs featured intensive, structured, and sequenced support delivered by experienced, trained full-time mentors. Beginning teachers in the treatment group were assigned to a full-time mentor with a 12 to 1 ratio. Mentors received ongoing training and a curriculum of materials; mentees were provided weekly meetings with the mentor, monthly professional development sessions, opportunities to observe veteran teachers, and continuing evaluation of their practices. (Hong et al. (2013))

### Claims
- [Comprehensive induction significantly increased beginning teachers' receipt of induction support during the treatment years](../claims/comprehensive-induction-increased-support-receipt.md) [+M]
- [One year of comprehensive induction did not change beginning teachers' classroom practices in literacy lessons](../claims/induction-no-impact-classroom-practices.md) [-M]
- [Treatment teachers received significantly more mentors than control teachers in the CTI randomized trial](../claims/cti-treatment-more-mentors.md) [+M]
