Generative AI Student Feedback Literacy Scale (GenAI-SFLS)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A 39-item Chinese-language self-report scale developed by Cui et al. to measure Chinese college students' literacy in prompting, judging, enacting, emotionally engaging with, and ethically using feedback from generative AI.
