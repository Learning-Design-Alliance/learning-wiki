# UCI Student Performance dataset (two Portuguese secondary schools)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A UCI Machine Learning Repository dataset of demographic, social, family, and school-related attributes from students at two Portuguese secondary schools, with Mathematics and Portuguese subject records used for student-performance prediction.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Kaggle student performance datasets (mathematics and Portuguese language courses)**: Two datasets from Kaggle covering student performance in Mathematics and Portuguese language courses at two Portuguese secondary schools, collected through school reports and questionnaires. "The Mathematics dataset comprises 395 entries, and the Portuguese language dataset includes 649 entries, with each entry characterized by 33 attributes." Attributes include demographic details, social factors, and academic performance metrics, with grades G1, G2, and G3; G3 was selected as the prediction target. Preprocessing encoded textual attributes numerically and split data 70% training, 30% testing. (Yan Zhao et al. (2024))
