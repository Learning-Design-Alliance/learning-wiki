- **METRIC framework emphasizing bias detection, transparency, interpretability, and completeness for trustworthy AI in medical education**: The review cites the METRIC model as a framework emphasizing "bias detection, transparency, interpretability, and completeness" as essential elements of trustworthy AI products.
