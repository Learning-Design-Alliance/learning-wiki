## Description
ELSA Speak is an AI-powered English speaking-practice app developed by ELSA that uses speech recognition, dialogue activities, pronunciation feedback, and fluency reporting to support language learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **AI-based speaking learning app (ELSA) with multi-turn spoken dialogue, pronunciation checker and fluency reporting**: The study integrated an AI-based speaking learning app, ELSA (https://elsaspeak.com/), into both experimental conditions. The article introduces "a multi-turn spoken dialogue system (SDS), pronunciation checker, and fluency reporting" as its learning features and tasks. Students used it autonomously to check speaking fluency, intonation and pronunciation, and the post-test English speaking assessment was collected from students' log profiles in the app, which reported pronunciation, intonation, fluency and IELTS speaking predictor scores from 15 read sentences. (Darmawansah et al. (2025))
