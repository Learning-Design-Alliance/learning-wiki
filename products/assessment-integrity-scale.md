# Assessment Integrity Scale

## Description
A 10-item English-language Likert self-report scale developed by Ntumi Simon et al. to measure tertiary students' academic-dishonesty behaviours and tolerance in Ghanaian Colleges of Education.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Assessment Integrity Scale (researcher-developed, 10-item)**: A researcher-developed instrument adapted from an Academic Dishonesty Inventory, measuring "behavioral engagement in dishonest practices (e.g., copying during tests, unauthorized collaboration, plagiarism) and attitudinal tolerance toward such practices." It comprises 10 items on a 5-point Likert scale, with a Cronbach's alpha of 0.76 and McDonald's omega of 0.78. CFA supported a unidimensional structure with good fit (CFI = 0.95; RMSEA = 0.056). (Ntumi Simon et al. (2026))
