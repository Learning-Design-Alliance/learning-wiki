# AITAQS four-dimensional framework of AI tutoring acceptance quality

## Description
A learner-perceived self-report assessment framework for higher-education students that measures AI tutoring acceptance quality across feedback quality, instructional effectiveness, interaction experience, and user trust.

### Claims

- [Bifactor-ESEM shows the highest fit among CFA, ESEM, bifactor-CFA and bifactor-ESEM models, supporting AITAQS as one general factor plus four specific dimensions.](../claims/aitaqs-bifactor-esem-best-fit.md) [+M]
- [Principal component analysis of the AITAQS extracts four factors accounting for 65.421% of variance, with item loadings from 0.64 to 0.85 matching the four hypothesized dimensions.](../claims/aitaqs-four-factor-pca-structure.md) [+M]
- [AITAQS dimension reliabilities fall within 0.721–0.745 Cronbach's α, exceeding acceptability thresholds, with all CR values above 0.7 though some AVE values fall below 0.5.](../claims/aitaqs-reliability-alpha-cr-ave.md) [+W]
- [All AITAQS items load above 0.50 on their target factor with cross-loadings below 0.30, in the hypothesized assignment (X1–X3 Feedback Quality through X10–X12 User Trust).](../claims/aitaqs-item-loadings-and-cross-loadings.md) [+W]
- [The AITAQS item pool shows sampling adequacy for factor analysis, with KMO = 0.758 and a significant Bartlett test (χ² = 438.75, df = 66, p < 0.001).](../claims/aitaqs-kmo-bartlett-adequacy.md) [+W]
