# Self-Evaluated Expertise (SEE) score

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (4 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A class-level physics-identity assessment score developed by Michael F. J. Fox and colleagues that subtracts students' perceived expert responses from their own E-CLASS responses to measure self-recognition as an expert experimental physicist.

### Claims
- [Total SEE scores correlate only moderately with traditional E-CLASS YOU scores, indicating the two measures share only part of their information](../claims/see-you-total-scores-moderately-correlated.md) [+M]

- [All item-wise pre-to-post shifts in both SEE and YOU scores show negligible effect sizes (Cohen's d < 0.2), with conclusions resting on low uncertainty from large sample sizes](../claims/item-shifts-negligible-effect-sizes.md) [~M]
