# SACD Research Program

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The U.S. Department of Education's Social and Character Development Research Program framework organizes school-based programming around six goals, including violence prevention, social-emotional development, character education, tolerance, health promotion, and civic responsibility.

## Description
