Content-Focused Coaching¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Content-Focused Coaching is a literacy-focused professional-development and instructional-coaching programme in which trained district coaches support teachers through demonstrations, grade-level meetings, co-teaching, observation, and feedback.

Components¶

  • Content-Focused Coaching (CFC) professional development program: Content-Focused Coaching is a professional development approach in which district coaches work with all teachers through in-class demonstrations, weekly grade-level meetings, and individual monthly visits for co-teaching, observation, and feedback. Coaches receive extensive training in the literacy strategies and in coaching methods, with schools receiving "about 15 days per year for 2 years" of professional development and coaching. No additional staff or technology are needed. (Content-Focused Coaching (2023))
