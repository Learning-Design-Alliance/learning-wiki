## Description
Utah Compose is a web-based automated writing-evaluation system developed by Measurement Incorporated that provides writing prompts, automated scoring, instant revision feedback, peer review, tutorials, and graphic organizers for Grades 3–12 students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Utah Compose (UC), a statewide automated writing evaluation system with the PEG scoring engine and interpretable partially connected neural networks**: Utah Compose is a web-based AWE system developed by Measurement Incorporated, implemented statewide in Utah from Fall 2014, providing "unlimited access to pre-designed prompts aligned with different grade levels and writing genres" plus automated scores and instant revision feedback. It uses the PEG scoring engine built on "800 + linguistic variables" and trains models for six writing traits across three genres and five grade bands, with human-machine agreement reported at a quadratic weighted kappa of 0.835 (SD = 0.026). For interpretability, UC "employs neural networks with selectively connected hidden nodes" matching pre-classified linguistic feature groups so revision improves scores, plus peer review, tutorials, and graphic organizers. (Yue Huang et al. (2024))
