# OSSCAR Monte-Carlo Metropolis-Hastings notebook

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An OSSCAR interactive notebook that lets computational-physics and chemistry learners vary simulation parameters while viewing complementary visualizations of potential energy, probability distributions, and energy traces.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Monte-Carlo Metropolis-Hastings notebook exemplifying the Multiple Representation Principle**: An OSSCAR application illustrates Monte-Carlo sampling of the canonical distribution with the Metropolis–Hastings algorithm for a selectable potential. Students set starting coordinates, temperature and simulation parameters (maximum move size and total number of iterations) to investigate physical and numerical effects on efficiency and ergodicity. The article states: "This notebook is also an example of the Multiple Representation Principle, where complementary representations of related quantities are displayed to facilitate learning". It shows a 3D potential-energy surface (plotly), a probability histogram color map and an energy trace (matplotlib). (Dou Du et al. (2022))
