# Inventory of School Motivation (ISM)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A school-motivation measurement instrument developed by McInerney and used to assess motivation across cultural groups, with cross-cultural validation and measurement-invariance testing required for new populations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Inventory of School Motivation as a cross-culturally validated motivation measure**: The Inventory of School Motivation (ISM) is a motivation instrument McInerney developed and validated across cultural groups; the tribute's reference list cites work on its "Cross cultural model testing: Inventory of School Motivation" and its cross-cultural validation with Navajo and Anglo students, as well as measurement-invariance testing across Chinese and US college students. It served as a key measurement tool in his cross-cultural motivation research program. (Bembenutty et al. (2023))
