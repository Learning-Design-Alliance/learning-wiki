SET (Social and Emotional Training)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

SET is a Swedish universal social and emotional learning programme developed by Kimber and delivered by trained ordinary teachers in regular compulsory-school classrooms.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • The SET intervention's benefits per participant ($7,510) outweigh its costs ($540), yielding a benefit-cost ratio of 14:1 and a net present value of $6,970 [+M]
  • In the SET intervention, zero/light drug users in the treatment group decreased drug use over five years while zero/light control students increased, an effect size gain of Cohen's d = .64 [+M]
