# Space Scoop

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Space Scoop is a multilingual astronomy news service for children, operated through Universe Awareness with observatory partners, educational reviewers, and volunteer translators.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Space Scoop astronomy news service for children**: Space Scoop is an astronomy news service for children aged 8 and above in which 'The latest press releases from leading astronomical ground and space observatories are rewritten, so that they are understandable for children'. Items pass an internal educational review, are translated by volunteer translators into up to 33 languages, and are released to the public at the same time as the original scientific press release. (George Miley et al. (2015))
