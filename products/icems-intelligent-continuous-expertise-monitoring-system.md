# ICEMS (Intelligent Continuous Expertise Monitoring System)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A multifaceted deep-learning system developed by Yilmaz Recai et al. that integrates with surgical simulators to assess performance continuously and provide real-time instructional feedback and risk detection.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ICEMS: a multifaceted deep-learning system providing real-time surgical instruction and risk detection**: The Intelligent Continuous Expertise Monitoring System is described as "a multifaceted multi-algorithm deep learning system with a rapid-decision capability for real-time applications" designed to mimic human expert surgical instructors during simulation training. It comprises six long-short term memory network algorithms — one for objective skill assessment and five for risk detection and feedback — and "assesses surgical performance in 0.2-s intervals and provides real-time instruction and risk detection" on five metrics: bleeding risk, tissue injury risk, aspirator force, bipolar force, and using the two instruments together. In this trial it delivered real-time auditory instructions when a learner's score deviated more than one standard deviation from expert level for at least one second, plus post hoc 10-s error video clips. (Yilmaz Recai et al. (2024))

### Claims
