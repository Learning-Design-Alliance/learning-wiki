# Multicultural Personality Questionnaire (MPQ)

## Description
The MPQ is a self-report questionnaire developed by Van der Zee and Van Oudenhoven that measures emotional stability, flexibility, openmindedness, cultural empathy, and social initiative for assessing intercultural effectiveness.
