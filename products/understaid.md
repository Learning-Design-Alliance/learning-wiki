## Description
understAID is an ICT-based e-learning platform developed to support informal dementia caregivers through structured information, daily-care tools, personalization, and moderated social networking.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **understAID e-learning platform for informal dementia caregivers**: understAID is an ICT application accessible via mobile device or PC browser, designed to support informal caregivers of people with dementia. It contains "a Learning section with a database of contents organized in 5 modules with information about 15 different topics", each topic organized in four complexity levels from quick fixes to more details, plus a Daily Task section with calendar and reminders, a moderated Social Network section, and an interactive customization questionnaire that personalizes the information level shown. Topics were selected from questionnaires completed by 101 caregivers across Spain, Poland, and Denmark. (Núñez-Naveira L. et al. (2016))
