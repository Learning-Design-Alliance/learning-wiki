Edpuzzle¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Edpuzzle is a software platform developed and operated by Edpuzzle that lets educators embed questions, commentary, and quizzes in videos and track learners’ viewing and responses.

Components¶

  • Edpuzzle interactive video-learning platform: Edpuzzle is an interactive learning platform that turns passive video viewing into active learning by letting teachers weave questions, commentary, and engaging elements into videos, slides, and quizzes at precise moments. The article describes it as a tool that "transforms how educators deliver video-based instruction by turning passive viewing into active learning experiences", usable across grade levels and subjects, with viewing analytics, LMS integrations, and compatibility with flipped, distance, and face-to-face instruction. (Edpuzzle evidence summary (2025))

Claims¶

  • Edpuzzle Original math videos raise grades 3–5 mathematics achievement on the CAASPP (effect size +0.09) [+W]
  • Dosage relationship: more assigned Edpuzzle videos associated with larger gains [+W]
  • Edpuzzle qualifies for a Promising evidence rating based on one school-level randomized study [+W]
  • Edpuzzle Original math videos raise Renaissance Star Math scores and fall-to-spring growth (effect size +0.11) [+W]
