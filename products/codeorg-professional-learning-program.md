## Description
A year-long professional-development programme run by Code.org for high-school teachers implementing the Computer Science Principles curriculum, combining summer and academic-year workshops with facilitator development and ongoing online support.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Code.org Professional Learning Program for CS Principles teachers**: A year-long professional development program supporting teachers implementing the Code.org CS Principles curriculum. It coordinates three efforts — Regional Partners, Facilitator Development, and Professional Development Workshops — and "offers both in-person and online support for teachers before and during their first year teaching the Code.org curriculum." It begins with a five-day summer in-person workshop and continues with academic-year workshops run by trained facilitators, plus ongoing supports such as the Code.org Forum online professional learning community. (Richard S. Brown et al. (2019))

### Claims
- [Code.org's Professional Learning Program significantly increased AP Computer Science Principles test taking in treatment schools, with an average increase of almost 18 additional tests per school](../claims/codeorg-plp-increased-csp-ap-test-taking.md) [+M]
- [The program significantly increased the number of AP qualifying scores earned in Computer Science courses, including an average of 10.41 more Computer Science Principles qualifying scores per school](../claims/codeorg-plp-increased-csp-qualifying-scores.md) [+M]
