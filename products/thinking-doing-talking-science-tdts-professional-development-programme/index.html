Thinking, Doing, Talking Science (TDTS) professional development programme¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A primary-science teacher professional-development programme that trains teachers in conceptually challenging, practical and interactive teaching strategies, supported by planning time and classroom resources.

Components¶

  • Thinking, Doing, Talking Science (TDTS) professional development programme for primary science: TDTS is a primary school CPD programme in which two teachers per school receive five full-day training sessions across a year, designed to make science lessons more conceptually challenging, practical and interactive. It deliberately avoids off-the-shelf lesson plans, instead building a repertoire of strategies including a "dedicated discussion slot, the ‘Bright Ideas Time’" with prompts such as the Odd One Out, the PMI and the Big Question, plus Practical Prompts for Thinking demonstrations. Teachers also get in-school planning time and an equipment and resources package. (Pam Hanley et al. (2015))

Claims¶

  • TDTS improved Year 5 pupils' science attainment by around three additional months' progress (effect size +0.22) in a clustered RCT [+M]
  • TDTS pupils reported more positive attitudes towards science, science lessons and practical work than comparison pupils [+M]
