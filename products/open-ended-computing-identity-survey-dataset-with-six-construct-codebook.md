# Open-ended computing identity survey dataset with six-construct codebook

## Description
An IRB-approved dataset of 731 coded open-ended survey responses from 37 adults in computing-related fields, created by the authors to study computing identity, mentorship, challenges, and belonging.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Open-ended computing identity survey dataset with six-construct codebook (37 participants, 731 coded response lines)**: An IRB-approved online survey dataset of open-ended responses from 37 participants in computing-related fields (ages 20 to 76, M=45.4, SD=20.6; 13 women, 24 men), including 31 who reported mentorship support and 6 who did not. Prompts asked about pathways into computing, encouragement, challenges, pride, supporting others, mentorship, and inclusiveness. "In total, the final coded dataset included731response lines across 37 participants", coded binary for the six identity constructs with strong inter-rater agreement. (Understanding Computing Identity Development Through Mentorship and Epistemic Network Anal)
