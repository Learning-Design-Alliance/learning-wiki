## Description
MUDT-Tutor is an AI-assisted Uyghur grammar tutoring system developed by Jiaxin Zuo et al. that uses MUDT parsing and rule-based structural comparison to provide corrective and reinforcing feedback on learner sentence corrections.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MUDT-Tutor AI-assisted grammar tutoring system**: MUDT-Tutor is an interactive learning environment that translates MUDT-based syntactic analyses into interpretable pedagogical feedback. Learners correct erroneous Uyghur sentences; an integrated MUDT parser performs real-time analysis, and a rule engine compares the resulting dependency tree against a gold standard or predefined error patterns. The system verifies syntactic relations rather than relying on string matching, so it can reveal deep structural flaws such as an incorrect dependency between a postposition and its governor, and can explicitly identify the cop:zero relation in verbless sentences to provide positive reinforcement. (Jiaxin Zuo et al. (2025))
