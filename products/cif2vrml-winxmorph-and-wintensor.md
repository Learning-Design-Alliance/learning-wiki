# Cif2VRML, WinXMorph, and WinTensor

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Three free Microsoft Windows programs developed by Peter Moeck and colleagues for interactive crystallographic, materials-science, mineralogy, and condensed-matter-physics visualization and 3D-print-file creation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Three free Microsoft Windows programs (Cif2VRML, WinXMorph, WinTensor) for creating 3D print files of molecules, crystal morphologies, and tensor property representations**: Three separate Microsoft Windows executable programs were developed for interactive visualizations needed in crystallography, condensed matter physics, materials science, and mineralogy teaching: Cif2VRML (molecule and prospectively crystal structures from CIFs, one-click STL/VRML export), WinXMorph (crystal morphologies with growth sector coloring, simulated cuts, twin simulations), and WinTensor (longitudinal-effect tensor representation surfaces of anisotropic physical properties). The chapter states "Three separate Microsoft WindowsTM executable programs were developed over the years for each of the above-mentioned types of interactive visualizations" and that in 2014/2015 the latter two were extended with 3D print file export routines. All are completely free for non-commercial purposes. (Peter Moeck et al. (2018))
