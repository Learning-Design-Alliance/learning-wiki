## Description
The Cochlear Implant Surgery Assessment Tool (CISAT) is an 11-item, expert-rated performance assessment developed by Frendø et al. for evaluating otolaryngology residents' cochlear implant surgical competence from procedure videos in VR or cadaver settings.
