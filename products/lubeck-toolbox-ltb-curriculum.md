# Lübeck Toolbox (LTB) Curriculum

## Description
A video-based, goal-directed laparoscopic basic-skills simulation curriculum developed by the Lübeck surgical-training team for novice surgical residents to practise independently over a structured six-week period.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Lübeck Toolbox (LTB) laparoscopic training curriculum**: The LTB curriculum is a simulation-based minimally invasive surgery training programme consisting of "six related exercises: pack your luggage, weaving, Chinese jump rope, triangle cut, hammer cut, and suturing". It is performed in an LTB video box trainer with standard laparoscopic instruments, and learning progress is operationalized by the number of repetitions and duration per repetition needed to reach a defined training criterion. In this study it served as the 5-week training vehicle whose completion freed attentional resources for a concurrent auditory task. (Thomaschewski M et al. (2021))
- **Luebecker Toolbox standardized laparoscopy box-training setup with timed exercises**: The Luebecker Toolbox is a commercially available laparoscopy training box with an integrated camera connected to a monitor, four standardized modules allowing six different exercises, and associated online didactic videos. In this study three exercises were used — "Pack Your Luggage", "Chinese Jump Rope", and "Weaving" — training instrument handling, hand-eye coordination, and bimanual and crossing instrument use, with performance measured as time to complete each exercise. (Nees Lisa Katharina et al. (2021))
