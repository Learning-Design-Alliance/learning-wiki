## Description
A box-trainer simulator with a removable suturing sheet, made by Kyoto Kagaku Co., Ltd., for training and objectively assessing open or laparoscopic suturing skills.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **A-LAP mini simulator for objective open suturing skill assessment**: The A-LAP mini (Kyoto Kagaku Co., Ltd.) is a box-trainer simulator "originally designed for endoscopic surgery training" that was used under direct vision to evaluate open suturing. Each participant performed three single-ligature sutures scored on five items: "procedure time, outline area, aperture area, needle count with firmness, and leak pressure", each rated on a 5-point scale with a maximum total of 25 points. The removable suturing sheet allows evaluation comparable to laparoscopic suturing. (Onishi Shun et al. (2026))
