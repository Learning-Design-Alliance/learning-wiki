# Blackboard Collaborate

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A web-conferencing platform developed by Blackboard for synchronous and asynchronous teaching, communication, presentation sharing, and group collaboration.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Blackboard Collaborate web conferencing system with ten interface components**: Blackboard Collaborate (previously known as Elluminate Live) is defined in the study as "a web conferencing system which allows participants (teachers and students) to communicate synchronously and asynchronously, view presentations or videos, interact with other participants, and engage with resources in work groups". The article details ten components, including the Title Bar, Menu Bar, Audio and Video Panel, Participants Panel, Chat Panel, Collaboration Toolbar, and Content Area, and used it as the delivery platform for the experimental group's teaching methods course. (Hussein El-ghamry Mohammad Hussein (2016))
