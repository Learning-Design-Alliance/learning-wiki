# Problem-Solving Learning Portal (PSLP)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An online problem-solving environment developed by Pavlo D. Antonenko and colleagues that presents context-rich, ill-structured problems with staged tasks, categorized resources, time-costed resource access, and pathway tracking for learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Problem-Solving Learning Portal (PSLP) for multifaceted context-rich problems**: The PSLP is an online environment developed by the authors to present ill-structured story problems; its "shell can be populat ed with problem-scenarios in different content areas". Students see the problem statement, a task bar (qualitative analysis, relevant concepts, ongoing monitoring, solution, problem review, and a post-submission simulation), and a left menu of resources split into data, physical principles, diagrams, and advice. Accessing resources costs time from a 90-minute account, and students determine the sequence of tasks and resource access themselves, enabling pathway tracking. (Pavlo D. Antonenko et al. (2006))
