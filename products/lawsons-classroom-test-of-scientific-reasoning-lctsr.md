# Lawson's Classroom Test of Scientific Reasoning (LCTSR)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 10 claims (10 for) · 4 studies (3 associational, 1 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 2 of 4 report an effect size · 9 claims rest on one study

## Description
A classroom assessment instrument developed by Anton E. Lawson for measuring scientific reasoning patterns, including proportional, control-of-variables, probability, correlation, and hypothetico-deductive reasoning.

### Claims

- [Lawson scores correlate weakly with FCI and BEMA scores, while FCI and BEMA scores correlate strongly (r = 0.70 in the Chinese class)](../claims/lawson-content-score-correlations-small-fci-bema-large.md) [+W]
- [Traditional physics content training, even at a conceptually and computationally demanding level, does not by itself improve students' general scientific reasoning ability](../claims/traditional-content-training-no-reasoning-transfer.md) [+W]
- [Pre-instruction LCTSR scores correlate with normalized learning gains, most strongly for theoretical content (TUG-K r=0.59) and more weakly for descriptive content (DIRECT r=0.50)](../claims/lctsr-prescore-correlates-content-gains-theoretical-strongest.md) [+W]
- [In physics-based courses for non-science majors taught with active-engagement pedagogy, normalized gains were 0.38–0.42 on content tests but 0.06 on a scientific-reasoning test](../claims/reformed-pedagogy-content-gains-but-no-reasoning-gains.md) [+W]
- [In one class of 14 non-science majors, preliminary explicit instruction in scientific reasoning patterns (e.g., IAT statement construction) was followed by a larger average normalized LCTSR gain (68%) than in earlier courses without it (11%, n=42)](../claims/explicit-reasoning-instruction-preliminary-large-gains.md) [+W]
- [Non-STEM majors in conceptual physics and astronomy courses score significantly lower pre-instruction on the LCTSR than STEM majors](../claims/non-stem-lower-lctsr-pre-scores-than-stem.md) [+M]
- [A majority (56%) of non-STEM students are classified as transitional reasoners, while formal operational reasoners predominate among STEM majors](../claims/non-stem-majority-transitional-reasoners.md) [+W]
- [Scientific reasoning gains (LCTSR) are equal between flipped and nonflipped active classrooms and significant within both](../claims/lctsr-reasoning-gains-equal-flipped-nonflipped.md) [+W]
- [Non-science majors average an abysmal 38% on LCTSR questions testing hypothetico-deductive reasoning](../claims/non-majors-hypothetico-deductive-lctsr-38.md) [+W]
- [US and Chinese freshmen score almost identically on the Lawson scientific reasoning test (effect size 0.10, p = 0.201), despite large differences in physics content training](../claims/lawson-test-no-us-china-difference.md) [+W]
