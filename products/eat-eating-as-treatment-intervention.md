## Description
A manualised, dietitian-delivered nutrition and behaviour-change intervention developed for head and neck cancer patients undergoing radiotherapy, using motivational interviewing, collaborative goal-setting, nutrition assessment, and a written nutrition planner.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **EAT: Eating As Treatment intervention for head and neck cancer patients**: The EAT Intervention is a dietitian-delivered health behaviour change intervention designed to reduce malnutrition in head and neck cancer patients undergoing radiotherapy. Although EAT skills and principles are manualised, it is "not a linearly structured intervention" and is "based on motivational and behavioural principles" so it can be flexibly integrated into all consultations. It uses motivational interviewing to elicit and reinforce patient reasons for maintaining adequate nutrition, collaboratively identifies goals documented in a written nutrition planner, includes a standardised nutrition assessment, and features the "EAT to LIVE" conversation linking nutrition to radiotherapy outcomes. (Beck A.K. et al. (2020))
