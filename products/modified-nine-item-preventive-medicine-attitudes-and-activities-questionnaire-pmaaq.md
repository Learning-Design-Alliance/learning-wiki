# Modified nine-item Preventive Medicine Attitudes and Activities Questionnaire (PMAAQ)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A nine-item modified self-report questionnaire used to measure postgraduate health professional students' attitudes toward preventive healthcare and behavioural-change counselling through five-point Likert ratings.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Modified nine-item Preventive Medicine Attitudes and Activities Questionnaire (PMAAQ) for measuring preventive healthcare attitudes**: The study uses a modified version of the PMAAQ, an 85-item tool addressing physicians' health prevention behaviours and attitudes, as the attitude measure completed before and after the online MI course. The version used here "has been further modified to exclude four questions based on the results of reliability testing we conducted of the questionnaire, leaving nine items relating to preventive healthcare attitudes". Items cover beliefs such as the effectiveness of behavioural change counselling and clients' willingness to change, rated on a 5-point Likert scale from strongly agree to strongly disagree. (Oster Candice et al. (2020))
