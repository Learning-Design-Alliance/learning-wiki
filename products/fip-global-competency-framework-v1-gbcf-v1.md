## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **FIP Global Competency Framework v1 (GbCF v1) for foundation-level pharmacy practitioners**: The GbCF v1 is a competency framework developed by FIPEd in 2012 using evidence-based methodologies, designed as "a source document containing the core competencies expected of foundation level pharmacy practitioners" (pharmacists with less than 5 years practice experience). It comprises 100 behavioural statements (B1–B100) grouped under 20 competency domains and four broad clusters: pharmaceutical public health, pharmaceutical care, organisation and management, and professional and personal. It has been used to design pre-service curricula and country-specific in-service frameworks. This survey evaluated its relevance to practice in African countries. (Udoh Arit et al. (2018))
