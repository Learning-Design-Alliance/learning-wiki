Literacy and Morphemes programme¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A Worth et al. early-primary literacy curriculum that teaches morphemic spelling, sentence structure, composition, and comprehension through structured lessons and accompanying materials.

Claims¶

  • The Literacy and Morphemes programme had no significant impact on Year 2 pupils' literacy ability overall [+M]
