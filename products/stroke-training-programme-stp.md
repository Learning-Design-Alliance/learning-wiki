# Stroke Training Programme (STP)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A four-module online training programme developed by Temehy Basema et al. to improve physiotherapists' knowledge and confidence in long-term stroke care, including assessment, discharge planning, self-management and telerehabilitation.
