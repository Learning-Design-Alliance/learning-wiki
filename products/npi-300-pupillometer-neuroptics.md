# NPi-300 Pupillometer (NeurOptics)

## Description
A handheld digital pupillometer made by NeurOptics that measures quantitative pupillary-response variables such as pupil diameter and constriction and dilation velocities.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Digital pupillometer (NPi-300) as a repeatable, noninvasive physiological measure of simulation-induced anxiety**: The NPi-300 Pupillometer (NeurOptics) was used to measure pupillary response before and after simulated clinical scenarios in sixth-year medical students. The article positions pupillometry against questionnaire-based anxiety measurement, noting that "pupillometry allows for consecutive application as many times as deemed necessary, as it is a noninvasive technique with an extremely easy training curve for providers". It recorded maximum diameter, max/min difference, constriction velocity, and dilation velocity for both eyes. (Martín-Rodríguez Francisco et al (2025))
