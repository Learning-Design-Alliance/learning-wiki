## Description
A prototype educational game that introduces beginners to quantum concepts through progressively more sophisticated Tetris-like gameplay.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Graded three-level progression introducing quantum operations one concept at a time**: The Quantum Tetris prototype structures learning as three levels of increasing quantum sophistication. Level 1 uses quantum-inspired probabilistic circuits for shape, orientation, and color generation plus a two-tetromino superposition; Level 2 introduces quantum evolution via a 1° rotation about the X-axis at every game-loop update; Level 3 introduces the Many-Worlds interpretation with two parallel game boards. “Whenever the player clears a predetermined number of lines, the falling speed of the tetrominoes increases, indicating progression through sub-levels,” and completing sub-levels advances the player to the next level. (Deeptanshu Malu et al. (2026))
- **Quantum Tetris prototype: a downloadable Tetris variant whose block generation, orientation, and selection are governed by superposition, measurement, and unitary operations**: Quantum Tetris is a working prototype demonstrating the GOF concept, with source code adapted from the Tetromino Tetris clone by Al Sweigart and a downloadable version available online. In it, “several core Tetris mechanics are reimagined through the lens of quantum computing”: an unfavorable tetromino may be generated in superposition with a possibly favorable one, and the player uses the GOF to increase the chances of a favorable outcome. The board is divided into Quantum, Measurement, and Classical regions, with a Bloch sphere providing real-time visual feedback of the qubit state. (Deeptanshu Malu et al. (2026))
