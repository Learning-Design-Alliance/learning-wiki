# College Promise

## Description
A place-based postsecondary access and completion framework combining financial assistance, student supports, early messaging, and cross-sector collaboration, used by College Promise programmes and their partners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **College Promise as a comprehensive place-based access and completion model combining financial assistance, student support services, early messaging, and cross-sector collaboration**: The Guidebook frames College Promise as more than financial aid: "A strong program also provides students and their families with targeted student support services and early messaging about program benefits and requirements." Programs are collaborative, engaging education institutions, nonprofits, local government, and philanthropy, and increasingly serve part-time students, GED completers, and incumbent workers alongside recent high school graduates attending full time. More than 300 programs across 44 states are reported nationwide. The model aligns with California's Vision for Success goals of increasing completion and transfer and reducing equity gaps. (Rauner et al. (2019))
