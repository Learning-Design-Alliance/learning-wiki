## Description
Skills is a web-based assessment and treatment-tracking system used by treatment centers to assess functioning across eight domains and monitor treatment progress and mastery of learning objectives.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Skills assessment and treatment-tracking database**: Skills is the web-based assessment and treatment-tracking system whose retrospective records supplied the data for this study. The article states that "Skills includes a well-validated assessment that evaluates functioning across eight treatment domains and tracks ongoing treatment progress." It defines mastery of a learning objective as greater than 70% accuracy over at least two treatment sessions conducted across separate days, and operational data such as treatment hours were collected by participants' treatment centers. (Linstead E et al. (2017))
