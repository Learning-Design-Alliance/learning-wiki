## Description
An eight-week mindfulness-training programme developed by Jon Kabat-Zinn and implemented by qualified providers through formal meditation practice, mindful movement, and related exercises.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Mindfulness as a foundation for healthy teacher self-regulation: the MBSR logic model**: The article frames mindful awareness as "a foundation for healthy self -regulation" and models MBSR's effects through a logic model in which training builds mindful presence and mindful acceptance, which in turn improve teacher wellbeing, self-regulation ability and professional performance. Mindfulness is defined as the ability to pay purposeful, non-judgmental attention to the present moment, and mindful acceptance is distinguished from resistance or avoidance of negative emotions, which can prolong them. The mediation results are read as support for this pathway. (Rupprecht et al. (2017))
