# MINTS-DR (Multi-professional Inventory for Non-Technical Skills in the Delivery Room)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A set of profession-specific and global team behavioural-marker inventories for observing and rating non-technical skills during delivery-room simulation and debriefing, developed by Bracco Fabrizio et al.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MINTS-DR: multi-professional NTS behavioural-marker observation forms for delivery room simulation**: The MINTS-DR (Multi-professional Inventory for Non-Technical Skills in the Delivery Room) is a set of five NTS inventories, one for each professional category (anaesthetists, gynaecologists, nurses/midwives, assistants) plus a global team assessment tool. Each checklist item uses a stem sentence with two endings representing "the two extremes of a good or poor performance", anchored on a 4-point Likert scale with a not-applicable option. Forms were adapted from existing tools (ANTS, NOTSS, SPLINTS) and refined iteratively with practitioners to reference only observable behaviours. (Bracco Fabrizio et al. (2017))
