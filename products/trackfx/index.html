TrackFX¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

TrackFX is a touch-tablet game-based multiple-object-tracking assessment developed by Kimiko Ryokai and colleagues to measure selective visual attention and tracking ability in children as young as 30 months.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Children aged 30–58 months tracked on average 1 target among 2 distractors, with a slight trend for older children to reach higher thresholds [+M]
