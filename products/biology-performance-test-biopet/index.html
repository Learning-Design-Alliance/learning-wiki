Biology Performance Test (BIOPET)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A 30-item, five-option multiple-choice biology achievement test developed by the study authors for SSS I students, aligned to a table of specifications spanning six cognitive-domain levels and used as pre- and post-test.

Components¶

  • Biology Performance Test (BIOPET), a 30-item multiple-choice achievement test: BIOPET was the study's test instrument, "a 30 item multiple-choice objective test with five options each" drawn from past WAEC Senior Secondary Certificate Examination biology paper II questions. Its content was based on a table of specification covering the six levels of the cognitive domain of learning. It served as both pre-test and, rearranged, as post-test for all three groups. (Mudasiru Olalere YUSUF et al. (2010))

Claims¶
