## Description
A 30-item, five-point Likert self-report instrument developed by Harty and Beall and translated into Turkish by Serin to measure students' science curiosity across novelty, lack of clarity, complexity, and surprise or bafflement.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Science Curiosity Scale (Harty & Beall, 1984; Turkish translation by Serin, 2010)**: The Science Curiosity Scale is the quantitative instrument used to measure students' science curiosity before and after the experiment. It is a 5-point Likert scale consisting of 30 items, 8 of which are negative, based on four factors: novelty, lack of clarity, complexity of stimuli, and surprise/bafflement. The article reports the Cronbach's α reliability coefficient as 0.85 for the English version, 0.87 for the Turkish version, and 0.81 in this study's sample of 100 students. Scores range from 30 (lowest) to 150 (highest possible). (Karadeniz et al. (2020))
