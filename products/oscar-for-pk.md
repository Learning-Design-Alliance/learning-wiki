# OSCAR for PK

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A structured rubric for assessing ophthalmology residents’ competence in routine penetrating keratoplasty, developed by cornea specialists and approved by the Ophthalmology Foundation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **OSCAR for penetrating keratoplasty: a structured surgical competency assessment rubric**: The OSCAR for PK is an assessment tool that breaks the penetrating keratoplasty procedure into 11 essential steps and six global indices, with expected functioning described at the novice, beginner, advanced beginner, and competent levels. It was developed by a team of cornea specialists using previously published OSCARs as a template and refined through iterative expert feedback. The article states it was 'approved by the Ophthalmology Foundation, which promotes the use of the OSCARs for international ophthalmology education.' (Mira S et al. (2025))
