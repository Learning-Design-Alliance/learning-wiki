## Description
Entering Mentoring is a modular, facilitator-led curriculum developed by the University of Wisconsin–Madison team for training research mentors in core mentoring competencies.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Entering Mentoring research mentor training curriculum built on six core competencies**: The Entering Mentoring (EM) curriculum is an evidence-based research mentor training curriculum delivered as "a process-based forum wherein mentors learn core mentoring competencies, experiment with various mentoring strategies, and solve mentoring dilemmas within small peer groups." It is organized around six competencies, from maintaining effective communication to promoting professional career development, and its modular chapters pair learning objectives, activities, and detailed facilitator notes, with adapted curricula made freely available online. (Pfund Christine et al. (2015))
