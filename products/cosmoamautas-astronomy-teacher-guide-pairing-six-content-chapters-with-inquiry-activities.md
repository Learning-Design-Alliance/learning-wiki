# CosmoAmautas astronomy teacher guide pairing six content chapters with inquiry activities

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A Spanish astronomy teacher guide produced by El Equipo CosmoAmautas that combines six astronomy content chapters with six classroom inquiry activities for school teachers and their students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Chapter structure pairing expository cosmology content with practical classroom activities**: Each chapter of the book follows a recurring design: expository content on a cosmology topic followed by practical activities located at the end of the book and related to their respective chapters. The activities go beyond questions and include suggestions of experiments performable in class with very simple equipment. The authors describe the set as follows: "Também desenvolvemos uma série de atividades práticas, relacionadas aos seus respectivos capítulos, as quais ser encontradas no final do livro." The stated intent is to enrich lessons and open new opportunities. (Bento et al. (2021))
