## Description
The Virtual Temporal Bone Project is an interactive virtual temporal-bone dissection simulator developed for otorhinolaryngology residents and trainees, with 3D anatomical rendering, landmark warnings, and force feedback.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Virtual Temporal Bone Project simulator for temporal bone dissection training**: A North-American virtual temporal bone dissection simulator, developed in the United States at Ohio University and being validated, is used at several institutions including a Brazilian one. The review describes it as "a promising tool that provides structure recognition features and alarms when the trainee is close to anatomical landmarks" such as the facial nerve, dura-mater and semicircular canals, with 3D graphic rendering of temporal bone textures and force feedback controls. (Nogueira Júnior JF (2010))
