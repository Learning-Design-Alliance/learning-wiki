## Description
Seeds of STEM is an eight-unit integrated Pre-K STEM curriculum developed for Head Start classrooms by engineers, STEM pedagogy experts, psychologists, and classroom teachers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Seeds of STEM: an 8-unit integrated Pre-K STEM curriculum built around the engineering design process**: Seeds of STEM (SoS) is an 8-unit integrated STEM curriculum for Pre-K classrooms developed by engineers, STEM pedagogy experts, psychologists, and six Head Start classroom teachers, refined through feedback from approximately 20 teachers over a 10-week period. Each unit integrates science concepts, technology tinkering centers, mathematics activities, and engineering via the EDP: "children are taught the steps to solve design problems encountered by the curriculum's main character, Problem Panda." Units include formative and summative authentic embedded assessments. (Sibuma et al. (2018))
