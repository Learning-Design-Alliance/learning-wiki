Pro audito computer-based speechreading training (lippenlesen.ch)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 against) · 2 studies (2 causal), q3 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A freely accessible web-based speechreading training developed by pro audito Schweiz, providing video instruction, flashcards, and exercises to practise visual speech cues and audiovisual speech comprehension in noise.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Three weeks of computer-based speechreading training improves audiovisual speech-in-noise comprehension in middle-aged and older adults, with the effect limited to the audiovisual condition [+M]
  • Speechreading training does not significantly improve subjectively perceived everyday listening effort or hearing abilities [-W]
  • Speech-in-noise perception improves significantly after a short-term immersive auditory-cognitive training, but not after an audio-only training session [+W]
