# Five-dimension core competency framework for gastroenterology endoscopy nurses

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A Chinese clinical-practice framework organizing endoscopy-nurse competence into professional attitude, practice skills, theoretical knowledge, communication and coordination, and professional development for item development and assessment.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Five-dimension core competency framework for gastroenterology endoscopy nurses grounded in core competency theory and role theory**: The article constructs a competency framework for gastroenterology endoscopy nurses derived from core competency theory (knowledge, skills, and abilities, originally from business management) and role theory (patterned behaviors according to social expectations). The framework "comprises 5 dimensions: Professional Attitude, Professional Practice Skills, Professional Theoretical Knowledge, Communication and Coordination Abilities, and Professional Development", derived from the roles and functional requirements of endoscopy nurses. It served as the organizing structure for item generation and was confirmed by EFA in the validation surveys. (Xie G et al. (2025))
