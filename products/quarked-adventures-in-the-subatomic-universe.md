## Description
A University of Kansas multimedia science education project combining a website, animated videos, online games, lesson plans, and hands-on museum programs to teach youth about scale, matter, and particle physics.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Quarked! Adventures in the Subatomic Universe multimedia project**: The Quarked! project, created by physicists, designers, and museum educators at the University of Kansas, provides "an engaging and educational science-based experience for youth aged 7 and up, educators and the general public." It focuses on concepts of scale and matter, presenting subatomic particles as relatable characters in story-driven adventures. Components include the quarked.org website, three 3D animated videos, online Flash games, lesson plans, and two hands-on museum programs. (A. Bean (2011))
