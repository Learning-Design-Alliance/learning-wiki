## Description
Osmosis is a commercial web-based medical learning platform offering audiovisual videos, flashcards, clinical decision-making trees, concept-focused notes, embedded case-based quizzes, and administrator usage dashboards.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Osmosis multimedia learning platform with videos, flashcards, and self-assessment quizzes**: Osmosis is a web-based commercial multimedia learning platform featuring "videos using audiovisual picture mnemonics, flashcards, clinical decision‐making trees, and concept‐focused notes" along with "embedded case‐based multiple‐choice quiz questions for self‐assessment of learning." In this study, instructors compiled playlists of assigned videos with accompanying quizzes and flashcards, recommended before live sessions, and completion of assigned questions and flashcards counted for 2% participation credit. Usage was monitored via the platform's administrator dashboard. (Hoyt Garrik et al. (2024))
