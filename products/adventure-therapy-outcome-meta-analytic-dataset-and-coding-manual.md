# Adventure therapy outcome meta-analytic dataset and coding manual

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A meta-analytic dataset and coding manual assembled by Daniel J. Bowen and colleagues, containing coded pre–post outcome evidence from adventure therapy studies for psychological and behavioural functioning analyses.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Adventure therapy outcome meta-analytic dataset and coding manual (197 studies, 2,908 effect sizes, 206 samples)**: A systematically assembled corpus of adventure therapy outcome studies published between 1967 and 2012, identified via electronic databases, specific journals, websites and listservs, related meta-analyses, expert emails and bibliographies. It comprises "2,908 effect sizes from 206 unique samples within 197 studies", covering 17,728 unique participants, coded with a manual "available from http://www.daniel-bowen.com.au/meta-analysis" along with detailed breakdowns by 67 specific outcomes. The dataset underpins all effect size, publication bias and moderator analyses in the study. (Daniel J. Bowen et al. (2013))
