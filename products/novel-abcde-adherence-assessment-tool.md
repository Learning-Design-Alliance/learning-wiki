# Novel ABCDE adherence assessment tool

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A 24-item video-based assessment instrument developed by Linders et al. for rating neonatal healthcare providers’ adherence to the ABCDE algorithm during NALS simulation, using trichotomous scoring.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Novel ABCDE adherence assessment tool with 24 APLS-derived items and trichotomous scoring**: A purpose-built scoring instrument developed because no suitable tool existed: it incorporates "All 24 items of the ABCDE approach, as presented in the Dutch Advanced Pediatric Life Support (APLS) course manual", categorized under domains A–E. Items score 2, 1, or 0 points for adequate-and-timely, incomplete-or-out-of-sequence, and inadequate performance, with a maximum of 48 points, expressed as a percentage score. It demonstrated face and content validity and high intra-observer reliability. (Linders M et al. (2021))
