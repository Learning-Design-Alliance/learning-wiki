## Description
A Noosfera Education Support Foundation programme delivered with university astronomers in which secondary pupils learn astronomy research skills by investigating variable stars and may make officially recognised discoveries.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Discovery Project after-school astronomy research club for Key Stage 3 pupils**: The Discovery Project is an extra-curricular programme, originally developed in Moscow by the Noosfera Education Support Foundation, in which university astronomers teach school pupils the skills to discover new variable stars over a series of sessions. In the London pilot, clubs ran over ten weekly sessions at Hammersmith and UCL Academies for around ten 11-12 year olds each, with priority given to pupil premium students. Students could become the official discoverer of a new variable star and be listed in the International Variable Star Index database. The article states students were given "the chance to become the oﬃcial discoverer of a new variable star, and to be listed in the International Variable Star Index database". (Katy Chubb et al. (2017))
