# ESPE e-Learning website

## Description
A free, globally accessible ESPE–ISPAD e-learning platform run and maintained by the ESPE–ISPAD e-learning committee, providing pediatric endocrinology and diabetes chapters, clinical cases, multilingual resource-limited-setting courses, and accredited CME modules.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ESPE-ISPAD CME-accredited e-learning platform: 30 accredited credit hours across three module categories with multilingual resource-limited-setting courses**: The ESPE e-Learning website is a free, globally accessible online resource containing 80 chapters and more than 130 cases covering core and advanced courses in pediatric endocrinology and diabetes, including 16 chapters and 25 cases for health care professionals in resource-limited settings. Since August 2022, 30 credit hours of CME courses have been EACCME-accredited, organized in three categories with 10 courses each; each course takes approximately 1 to 1.5 hours and provides one European CME credit. The RLS module is available in five languages (English, French, Spanish, Swahili, and Chinese). Content is "closely aligned with published international consensus guidelines" and includes interactive chapters and case-based learning. (van der Zwan YG et al. (2026))

### Claims
- [Across all survey items, learners rated the CME modules highly, with median Likert scores of 4 and means above 4.0 for content quality, clarity, motivation, competence, and perceived educational value](../claims/espe-cme-high-likert-satisfaction.md) [+M]
- [Learners completing the ESPE CME e-learning modules came from all inhabited continents, with most practicing in Europe (59%) and 12% in resource-limited countries](../claims/espe-cme-global-participation-distribution.md) [+M]
