# 38-item neonatal resuscitation skills observational checklist and 25-question knowledge test

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A study-adapted assessment package combining an objective checklist for observing neonatal resuscitation performance on an anatomical model with a written knowledge test for midwives and nurses.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **38-item observational skills checklist with 25-question knowledge test for assessing neonatal resuscitation provider competence**: An assessment instrument package consisting of a 38-step objective observational checklist for skills and a 25-question knowledge test, adapted from prior validated tools including the AMDD Needs Assessment Toolkit. Experts "observed participants performing NR on an anatomical model and used an observational checklist (38-item skills tools) to rate their skill level". Each performed step or correct answer scored one point, with 80% or higher classified as good retention or good knowledge. (Sintayehu Y et al. (2020))
