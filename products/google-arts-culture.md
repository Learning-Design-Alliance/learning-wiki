# Google Arts & Culture

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Google Arts & Culture is Google's online cultural-heritage platform, providing digitised artworks and high-resolution gigapixel images from partner museums and galleries for exploration and educational use.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Google Arts & Culture gigapixel artworks for observational training in medical education**: Google Arts & Culture is described as "an online art museum that has formed partnerships with over 1,800 art galleries and museums in 150 countries around the world" to upload artworks scanned with Google's Gigapixel Camera. The article uses it for art-integrated medical education: zooming into Rembrandt's "The anatomy lesson of Dr. Nicholaes Tulp" reveals a white cord along the cadaver's ulnar aspect that a conventional textbook could not show in detail, later identified as likely a tendon of an accessory abductor digiti minimi. (Park Jung-Chul et al. (2021))
