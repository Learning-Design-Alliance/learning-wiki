## Description
A school-based social-emotional competencies intervention developed by CED and delivered by teachers and psychologists through structured classroom group sessions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ISC Integrated Social Competencies group-session program structure**: The ISC program is a research-action school intervention delivered by teachers and psychologists in the classroom, involving "the implementation of 22 group sessions, each school year". Each session follows the same global structure: an initial review activity and ice-breaking, an activity related with the session's content and objective, a cooperation activity, and a final reflection activity on the session and work until the next session. Activities used manuals, stories, music, games and group dynamics, adapted to each group's characteristics and needs. (Tania Gaspar et al. (2018))
