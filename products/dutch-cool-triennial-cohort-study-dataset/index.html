Dutch COOL triennial cohort study dataset¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A Dutch national longitudinal cohort dataset following elementary students with repeated teacher reports, student motivational measures, and nationally normed reading and mathematics achievement tests.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Student-teacher closeness, conflict and dependency, students' motivation, and academic achievement inform each other across the elementary grades [+W]

Related Products and Programmes¶

  • Early Childhood Longitudinal Study, Kindergarten Class of 1998-1999 (ECLS-K)
  • Anonymized Dutch student-monitoring test-score dataset covering 15% of primary schools, 2017 to 2020
  • Five-year longitudinal cohort dataset of 4,228 students assessed up to three times per year comparing ever-SPED and never-SPED growth
  • NWEA MAP Growth assessment and longitudinal achievement database
