# Fresno test, Berlin questionnaire, Taylor questionnaire, Scale of efficacy toward EBP, EBP competence questionnaire, and validated EBP knowledge instrument

## Description
A set of validated instruments used in health-professions education to assess evidence-based-practice knowledge, skills, competence, and attitudes, including pre-intervention and post-intervention measurement.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Validated EBP assessment instruments (Fresno test, Berlin questionnaire, EBP competence questionnaire, and related tools)**: The review identifies a set of validated instruments used to assess EBP learning outcomes across its included studies: the Fresno test, the Berlin questionnaire, the Taylor questionnaire, the Scale of efficacy toward EBP, the EBP competence questionnaire, and a validated instrument for assessment of EBP related knowledge. These tools measure EBP knowledge, skills, and attitudes and anchor the review's finding that "validated tools to assess knowledge and skill acquisition exist and have been widely used". (Kyriakoulis Konstantinos et al. (2016))
- **Fresno test as outcome measure for EBM competence**: The Fresno test, adapted for this study, is "a standardized and objective tool to measure one's competence on the application of EBM". It covers four domains — asking a clinical PICO question, searching strategy, critical appraisal skill and applying to clinic — and was administered for 30 minutes one week after each course ended to both groups. (Huang Hsien-Li et al. (2020))
