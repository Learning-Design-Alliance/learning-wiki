## Description
TC3Sim, also known as vMedic, is a 3D serious-game simulation developed for training US Army combat medics and combat lifesavers in tactical combat casualty care.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **TC3Sim combat medic training simulation integrated with GIFT**: TC3Sim (also known as vMedic) is a serious game used to train US Army combat medics and combat lifesavers on tactical combat casualty care tasks, including care under fire. It presents linearly structured scenarios with fixed event sequences while allowing learners to freely navigate 3D environments and choose how to administer casualty care. TC3Sim is integrated with the Generalized Intelligent Framework for Tutoring (GIFT), which managed sensors, delivered pre-tests, training materials, post-tests, and run-time motivational feedback triggered by affect detectors. A downloadable version has been made publicly available. (DeFalco et al. (2018))
