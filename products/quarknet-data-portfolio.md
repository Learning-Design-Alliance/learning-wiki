# QuarkNet Data Portfolio

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A developing collection of particle-physics data-interpretation and Standard Model learning activities for pre-university students and the public, developed through the QuarkNet program.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **QuarkNet Data Activities Portfolio (DAP)**: The QuarkNet Data Activities Portfolio is a compendium of classroom activities designed for teachers to use with their students, whose activities "range from skill-building (Level 0) to independent research (Level 4)". Activities are tied to NGSS, AP Physics and IB standards, searchable by topic such as Electricity & Magnetism, and linked to experiments on cosmic rays, the LHC and neutrinos. They were vetted by teachers at QuarkNet Center meetings and by teachers and students in the classroom. (Bardeen M G et al. (2022))
