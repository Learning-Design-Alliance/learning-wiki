# Micro Trainer

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A microsurgical simulator platform from Digital Surgicals Pte Ltd that supports self-guided suturing practice with immediate performance feedback and cloud-based progress tracking.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Microsurgical simulator platform with cloud-based immediate performance feedback and progress tracking**: The article describes a meditech startup's simulator platform, footnoted as Micro Trainer, Digital Surgicals Pte Ltd, Singapore. The "cloud‐based application that provides immediate performance feedback and tracks progress over time" supports self-guided training; Figure 2 shows a movable arm for practice in different positions and captured data to assess suturing symmetry. (Velásquez‐Plata D (2026))
- **Digital Surgicals computer-aided assessment of suture placement (MicroTrainer Platform)**: A computer program from Digital Surgicals scored the elastic practice strips, with an "algorithm is based on uniform and optimal suture density as well as alignment of the sutures with a maximum total score of 35 points". Participants sutured 4 mm-wide elastic strips under the microscope using the MicroTrainer Platform, completing five strips total. The article states the assessment method "has previously been validated and eliminates the possibility of bias arising from a non-blinded assessor". (Teo Wendy Z. W. et al. (2021))
- **MicroTrainer strip and software for objective microsurgical skill assessment**: A commercial silicone practice strip (DigitalSurgicalSkillsAcademy) with accompanying software/app used for home microsurgical practice. The article states its key benefit: "it provides objective assessment of accuracy of suture spacing, orientation and progress over time". The method is currently used in the RCSEd microsurgical skills course, and a test strip scored 29/35 in the authors' illustration. (Higgins GC et al. (2021))
