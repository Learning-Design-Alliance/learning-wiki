## Description
TeachTown: Basics is a TeachTown computer-assisted intervention programme for young children with autism that combines software-based discrete-trial instruction, teacher-delivered interpersonal activities, progress reporting, and mastery-based progression.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **TeachTown: Basics, a computer-assisted intervention combining discrete-trial software lessons with teacher-delivered interpersonal lessons**: TeachTown: Basics is a CAI for children developmentally aged 2-7 years that "includes off-computer interpersonal activities, automatic data collection and reporting, and a note system for communication with the child's team." Computer lessons use a discrete trial format grounded in ABA principles with prompting procedures such as fading and highlighting, animated reward games, and mastery-based progression through five levels across six content domains. Teachers are asked to have each child spend 20 minutes per day on the software, and lesson plans for the interpersonal lessons are included with the program. (Pellecchia M et al. (2016))
