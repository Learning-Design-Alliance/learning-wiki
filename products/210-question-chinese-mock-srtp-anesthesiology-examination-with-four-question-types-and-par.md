# 210-question Chinese mock SRTP anesthesiology examination with four question types and partial-credit case analysis

## Description
A Chinese-language 210-item mock anesthesiology completion examination developed by Wang Shuo et al. for anesthesiology residency trainees, aligned with Chinese SRTP examination standards and including selected-response and partial-credit case-analysis items.
