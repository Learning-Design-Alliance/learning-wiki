# KSAVE framework

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
The KSAVE framework is a 21st-century skills framework produced by the ATC21S project to organize knowledge, skills, attitudes, values, and ethics for curriculum and large-scale assessment.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **KSAVE framework of 21st century skills**: The KSAVE framework, produced in Phase 1 of the ATC21S project sponsored by Cisco, Intel, and Microsoft and led by the University of Melbourne, organizes skills for success in the 21st century by knowledge, skills, attitudes, values, and ethics. "The KSAVE framework uses 10 skills, grouped into four categories": ways of thinking, ways of working, tools for working, and living in the world. For each skill it provides knowledge requirements, guiding abilities and skills, and related behaviors and aptitudes. (Markle et al. (2013))
