# 47-point WFWB collection and transfusion skills checklist

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 47-point checklist developed by military and civilian clinical experts to assess safe warm fresh whole-blood collection and transfusion performance across knowledge, collection, and transfusion sections.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **47-point WFWB collection and transfusion skills checklist with three sequential sections**: A checklist of integral steps for WFWB collection and transfusion, created through literature review and iteratively revised by an expert panel of military and civilian physicians, nurses, and pre-hospital providers. "The checklist separated the procedure into three sequential sections: (1) a knowledge portion, (2) a collection portion, and (3) a transfusion portion." The maximum score is 47 points (4 for Section A, 17 for Section B, 26 for Section C). (From battlefield to community: Simulation-based education for walking blood bank whole blo)
