## Description
Rain Classroom is an e-learning platform co-developed by Tsinghua University and XuetangX that integrates PowerPoint and WeChat for synchronous participation, quizzes, feedback, and learning analytics.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Rain Classroom digital teaching platform for real-time behavioral data collection**: Rain Classroom is a WeChat-integrated mini program jointly developed by Tsinghua University and Xuetang Online, widely adopted across Chinese universities. It integrates with Microsoft PowerPoint and mobile devices, allowing instructors to "deliver quizzes, share learning resources, collect feedback, and monitor classroom dynamics during live sessions". In this study it automatically logged attendance, quiz completion, answer accuracy, and interactive engagement behaviors during obstetrics and gynecology sessions. (Sun Shiying et al. (2026))
