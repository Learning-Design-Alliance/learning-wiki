# Rain Classroom

## Description
Rain Classroom is an e-learning platform co-developed by Tsinghua University and XuetangX that integrates PowerPoint and WeChat for synchronous participation, quizzes, feedback, and learning analytics.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Rain Classroom digital teaching platform for real-time behavioral data collection**: Rain Classroom is a WeChat-integrated mini program jointly developed by Tsinghua University and Xuetang Online, widely adopted across Chinese universities. It integrates with Microsoft PowerPoint and mobile devices, allowing instructors to "deliver quizzes, share learning resources, collect feedback, and monitor classroom dynamics during live sessions". In this study it automatically logged attendance, quiz completion, answer accuracy, and interactive engagement behaviors during obstetrics and gynecology sessions. (Sun Shiying et al. (2026))
- **Rain Classroom educational technology platform**: Rain Classroom is an educational technology platform that "facilitates real-time interaction, provides immediate feedback to students, and allows for the integration of multimedia resources". In this study it was used to deliver the BOPPPS cycle's interactive phases: pre-class quizzes, real-time quizzes during sessions, bullet-screen comments for questions, post-assessment question sets, and post-class evaluations. It also logged the number and frequency of student responses, which served as an engagement measure. The article positions it within Technology-Enhanced Learning, where digital tools transform rather than merely supplement pedagogical interactions. (Wang Yujia et al. (2026))
- **Rain Classroom smart teaching platform used to deliver blended case-based instruction**: Rain Classroom is a smart teaching tool developed by Tsinghua University that "integrates seamlessly with PowerPoint and mobile devices to enable real-time interaction, in-class questioning, and personalized post-class review." In this study it let instructors embed interactive questions in slides, push them to students' smartphones, display aggregated answers to guide discussion, send personalized review materials after class, and monitor participation through backend analytics for real-time teaching adjustments. (Zhu P et al. (2025))
- **Rain Classroom platform for online delivery of clinical courses**: Rain Classroom is the online platform through which the online group's neurosurgery course was delivered, accessible on both mobile phones and computers and facilitating real-time interaction between students and teachers. The teacher uploaded the case-based PPT before class; during class interaction was facilitated through the platform's instant messaging function; after class the teacher posted answers to reflective questions and explained lesson content, and students could view a recorded replay of the lecture. The article notes such platforms were originally designed for a hybrid model integrating online and face-to-face teaching. (Shen Jun et al. (2025))
