- **Researcher-developed 35-item Listening Comprehension Test (KR-20 = .92)**: A "Listening Comprehension Test" developed by the researcher to measure students' listening comprehension achievement, prepared through expert consultation, a 50-item question pool covering 15 acquisitions, a 46-item trial form piloted with 218 students, and item analysis.
