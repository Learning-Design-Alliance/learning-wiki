# Endodontic Complexity Assessment Tool (E-CAT)

## Description
A digital clinical assessment tool developed for objectively classifying the difficulty of non-surgical endodontic cases using patient- and tooth-specific factors.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Four contemporary endodontic complexity grading systems (ECS, DPI, EndoApp, E-CAT)**: The article evaluates four systems for grading root canal treatment complexity: "the English Clinical Standards for Restorative Dentistry (ECS), the Dental Practicality Index (DPI), the Endo App (EA) and the Endodontic Complexity Assessment Tool (E‐CAT), the latter two tools utilising an electronic format for application." ECS uses three complexity levels for NHS commissioning; DPI sums domain scores (0, 1, 2 or 6 per domain) across patient history, structural integrity, endodontic status and periodontal condition; EA scores criteria 1–9 and maps totals to treating-clinician recommendations; E-CAT evaluates 19 complexity categories and 22 factors into three classes. The study applied all four to the same 201 case records. (Nour et al. (2025))
