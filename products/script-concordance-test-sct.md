## Description
The Script Concordance Test, developed by Charlin and colleagues, is a medical-education assessment in which examinees judge how new clinical information supports or undermines diagnostic or management hypotheses, with responses scored against an expert panel.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Script Concordance Test based on Script Theory for assessing clinical reasoning**: The Script Concordance Test (SCT), developed by Charlin and based on Script Theory, assesses clinical reasoning by presenting brief clinical scenarios and asking examinees to judge how new information supports or undermines a diagnostic or management hypothesis. "Responses are scored on a 5-point Likert scale, ranging from '−2' to '+2', reflecting the degree to which the new information supports or undermines the hypothesis." Scoring compares examinees against an expert panel's response range. The article evaluates training faculty to produce high-quality SCTs. (Gaillet Antoine et al. (2026))
