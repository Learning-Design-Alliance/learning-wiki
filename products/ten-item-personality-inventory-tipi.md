## Description
A brief self-report personality assessment measuring the Five-Factor Model traits with ten paired items rated on a Likert-type scale.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Five-Factor Model of personality traits as measured by the Ten-Item Personality Inventory**: The article adopts the Five-Factor Model, "an internationally well-established framework of personality traits," comprising extraversion, agreeableness, conscientiousness, emotional stability and openness to experience. Traits are measured at matriculation with the Italian adaptation of the Ten-Item Personality Inventory, a short-form questionnaire whose paired items respondents rate on a seven-point Likert-type scale; reverse-scored items are recoded and item pairs averaged per scale. The framework serves as the set of explanatory regressors for first-year GPA. (Corazzini Luca et al. (2021))
