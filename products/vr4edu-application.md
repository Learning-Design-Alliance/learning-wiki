## Description
VR4EDU is a mobile virtual-reality educational application developed for primary-school learning activities, including music-genre identification and learning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Technical and methodological frameworks for mobile VR digital didactics**: The article develops technical and methodological frameworks for enhancing music learning through mobile virtual reality in primary education. The frameworks leverage "natural interaction and immersion in virtual environments" enabled by mobile VR on smartphones. They were tested with an evaluation study on music genre identification and learning using the VR4EDU application with primary school children. (Degli Innocenti et al. (2019))
