## Description
A programme developed by the Astronomical Society of the Pacific that pairs school teachers with astronomers for recurring classroom visits and hands-on astronomy activities.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Project ASTRO teacher-astronomer partnership program**: Project ASTRO, developed in 1994 by the Astronomical Society of the Pacific, pairs a school teacher with an astronomer who commits to visiting the classroom at least four times during the school year. Its main focus is hands-on astronomy activities that put students in the position of acting like scientists. The article reports that each year about 1000 partnerships bring science through astronomy to roughly 100,000 students over 12 project sites across the USA. (Caroline Barban et al. (2005))
