# Labelled corpus of 4,217 discussion posts

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A dataset created by Gorgun et al. containing 4,217 discussion-forum posts from 111 students in fully online Canadian graduate courses, coded by cognitive engagement level for classifier training and testing.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Labelled corpus of 4,217 discussion posts from fully online graduate courses**: A dataset of discussion forum posts from fully online graduate-level courses in a Canadian college of education, spanning topics from language learning to educational psychology, educational technology, and educational policy. "The dataset included 4,217 posts that had been produced by 111 students." Posts were coded into four hierarchical cognitive engagement levels (social, active, constructive, interactive) with .91 inter-rater reliability, and courses varied in facilitation method (peer vs. instructor) and term length. The corpus was split 70/30 for training and testing classifiers. (Gorgun et al. (2022))

# Labelled corpus of 4,217 discussion posts from fully online graduate courses

## Description
A dataset of discussion forum posts from fully online graduate-level courses in a Canadian college of education, spanning topics from language learning to educational psychology, educational technology, and educational policy. "The dataset included 4,217 posts that had been produced by 111 students." Posts were coded into four hierarchical cognitive engagement levels (social, active, constructive, interactive) with .91 inter-rater reliability, and courses varied in facilitation method (peer vs. instructor) and term length. The corpus was split 70/30 for training and testing classifiers.

### Context
#### Requirements
- Data were de-identified and post content cannot be shared due to learner privacy; students could exclude posts by marking them private.
#### Constraints
- Collected from graduate-level courses in one college of education; demographic data were not collected and cannot be obtained retroactively.
