SCI-ML Survey¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A 41-item Likert-type survey developed by Duane E. Wallace and colleagues to assess ninth-grade students' scientific citizenship identity, STEM career and education interest, and attitudes toward mobile learning.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • The MobiLAP intervention significantly increased ninth-graders' attitudes and perceptions of self as citizen scientists (Ideas about Citizen Science subscale) [+M]
  • The MobiLAP intervention significantly increased ninth-graders' interest in STEM careers and education (Ideas about Careers subscale) [+M]
  • The MobiLAP intervention produced no significant interactions on attitudes toward learning science and technology or toward mobile learning [+M]
