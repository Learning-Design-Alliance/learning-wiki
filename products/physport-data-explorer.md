## Description
An online physics-education data-analysis tool developed through PhysPort that securely processes and visualizes concept-inventory results for instructors and provides statistical summaries and teaching recommendations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PhysPort Data Explorer for analyzing and interpreting concept inventory results.**: The PhysPort Data Explorer is an online tool that lets instructors "securely upload your students’ concept inventory results and visualize them in a variety of ways." It computes normalized gain and effect size, tracks results over time as courses change, offers "one-click statistics" that perform appropriate statistical analyses with teaching recommendations, and reports per-question and question-cluster performance. Downloadable reports support tenure and accreditation documents, and the system protects student and instructor identities. (Madsen et al. (2016))
- **PhysPort assessment resources: expert recommendations, guides to RBAs, and an assessment data explorer**: PhysPort is a website that supports physics faculty in implementing research-based teaching and assessment practices. It contains expert recommendations for using different RBAs including troubleshooting help, guides to many common RBAs with administration and analysis information, information about more than 50 RBAs, and an assessment data explorer which "automatically analyzes and visualizes results from a variety of RBAs" and lets faculty upload results for comparison to similar institutions and a national data set. (Adrian Madsen et al. (2013))
- **PhysPort Data Explorer and LASSO centralized assessment systems**: The article describes two other systems exemplifying aspects of the centralized model. The PhysPort Data Explorer is 'a tool for scoring, analyzing, and interpreting results of RBAs' but does not assist with administration or data collection, representing a halfway point between the default and fully centralized models. The LASSO system, like E-CLASS, 'both hosts the RBAs and manages data collection, in addition to facilitating scoring and analysis', and serves as a hub for multiple RBAs. (Bethany R. Wilcox et al. (2016))
