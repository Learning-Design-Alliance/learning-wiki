# CISS (computer-assisted intervention for safer sex)

## Description
CISS is a computer-assisted safer-sex counseling programme developed for delivery by trained service providers using target-group-specific DVD role-model materials and interactive tools.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CISS: a three-session computer-assisted motivational interviewing intervention for safer sex with target-group-specific DVD role-model materials**: CISS (computer-assisted intervention for safer sex) consists of "3 semistructured counseling sessions delivered by service providers" who work through video materials and interactive slide shows on a DVD. Sessions move from exploring emotional responses to safer-sex problems ("Who am I?"), through developing personal solutions with self-assessment tools, to a personalized risk reduction plan, using a motivational interviewing counseling style. Three DVD versions were tailored for MSM, heterosexual migrant women, and heterosexual migrant men. In the trial it was delivered after a 2-day staff training in 8 European HIV-care settings. (Nöstlinger C et al. (2016))

### Claims
- [A computer-assisted safer sex counseling intervention increases self-reported condom use at last intercourse among HIV-positive MSM at 3 months, but not significantly at 6 months](../claims/ciss-condom-use-last-intercourse-3-months.md) [+M]
- [The intervention effect on condom use is mediated mainly by increased self-efficacy to negotiate condom use and, to a lesser extent, favorable condom attitudes, but not by depressed mood](../claims/ciss-self-efficacy-attitudes-mediation.md) [+M]
