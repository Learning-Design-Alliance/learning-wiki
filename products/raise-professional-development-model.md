## Description
RAISE (Reading Apprenticeship Improving Student Engagement) is a year-long professional-development programme that trains secondary teachers in Reading Apprenticeship literacy, metacognitive inquiry, and collaborative-learning practices through face-to-face sessions and teacher-leader on-site support.
