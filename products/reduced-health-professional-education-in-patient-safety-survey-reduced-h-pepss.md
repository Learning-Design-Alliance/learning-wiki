# Reduced Health Professional Education in Patient Safety Survey (Reduced H-PEPSS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A 16-item validated self-report survey developed by Doyle et al. to measure health-profession learners’ confidence in learning sociocultural patient-safety competencies and clinical skills across classroom and clinical settings.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Reduced H-PEPSS: 16-item survey measuring six sociocultural patient safety competencies plus clinical skills, classroom and clinical settings**: The reduced Health Professional Education in Patient Safety Survey (H-PEPSS) is a validated questionnaire measuring learners' confidence in learning six sociocultural patient safety competencies based on the Canadian Patient Safety Institute's Safety Competencies Framework, plus a clinical skills dimension of four items. Respondents answer each item separately for classroom and clinical settings on a 5-point Likert scale; the modified stem reads 'I feel confident in what I am learning about…'. Confirmatory factor analysis in both samples achieved good fit (CFI >0.95, RMSEA <0.06). (Doyle Patricia et al. (2015))
