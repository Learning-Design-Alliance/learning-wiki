# SLOTE scale: Simulation-based Learning Outcomes in Teacher Education questionnaire

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 29-item, 7-point self-report questionnaire developed by Levin et al. to measure peer reflective learning, simulation-related hindrances, and communication outcomes after simulation-based learning workshops for preservice and in-service teachers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SLOTE scale: Simulation-based Learning Outcomes in Teacher Education questionnaire**: The SLOTE scale is a validated 29-item questionnaire with 7-point Likert responses (not at all to very much) measuring the extent to which an SBL workshop produced specific outcomes. "Overall, this scale consists of three factors: peer reflective learning (α = .86), simulation-related hindrances (α = .74), and communication skills (α = .95)." It was developed and validated in the authors' previous study and used here to collect data from 370 workshop participants via a Google Form. (Levin et al. (2025))
