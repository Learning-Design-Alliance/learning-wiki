# European Competence Framework for Quantum Technologies

## Description
A QTEdu CSA framework that organizes quantum-technology knowledge, skills, and competences across theoretical, practical, and application domains for mapping courses and defining workforce qualification profiles.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **European Competence Framework for Quantum Technologies (version 2.0) as the QCTF content backbone**: The CF for Quantum Technologies is a tool designed by the EU Quantum Flagship to serve as a common language for communicating skills in QIST, developed via a Delphi method according to industry needs and available in version 2.0. It assigns eight numbered topical domains: "covering Quantum Background (domains 1 and 2), Core Device Technologies (domains 3 and 4), and QT systems and applications (domains 5-8)." Within each domain, topics are numbered so a complete map of QIST knowledge can be generated. The QCTF builds on the CF as its backbone for content selection, and it can describe skills for jobs, course learning outcomes, and degree-program content. (Simon Goorney et al. (2024))
- **European Competence Framework for Quantum Technologies (CFQT) version 2.5 with nine Qualification Profiles and a proficiency triangle**: The CFQT is a European competence framework for quantum technologies, updated to version 2.5 in April 2024 using this study's interview findings. "Key competences identified in the interviews were used to formulate six proficiency levels for the three proficiency areas": Quantum concepts; QT hardware and software engineering; and QT applications and strategies. These form a proficiency triangle, and iterative refinement of eleven initial roles led to "the nine qualification profiles described in the framework version 2.5", from QT aware person to QT specialist and strategist, specifying QT-specific qualifications independently of the concrete technology. (Franziska Greinert et al. (2024))
