My English Coach (Spanish Edition)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A Ubisoft educational Nintendo DS game that teaches English vocabulary, grammar, phrase construction, and pronunciation through placement testing, mini-games, audio comparison, and reference tools.

Components¶

  • My English Coach (Spanish Edition) for Nintendo DS as a classroom English-learning game: My English Coach (Spanish Edition) is an educational game developed and published by Ubisoft, released in 2009 for the Nintendo DS. "The videogame teaches the basics of word use, grammar, and construction of phrases through mini -games, and evaluates your progress." It begins with a placement test, teaches pronunciation via voice recording and playback comparison to a native speaker, uses eight mini-games to reinforce lessons, and contains 10,000 English words plus a built-in Spanish-English dictionary and phrase book with 400 phrases. In the study it was played twenty minutes daily for four weeks by the experimental group. (Vélez-Agosto et al. (2018))

Claims¶

  • Sixth-grade students in a Puerto Rican public school who played My English Coach in class showed significant pre-post growth in English receptive vocabulary, but so did a same-school control class that did not play it, while an external control group did not [~W]
  • Students mastered significantly more game words each successive week of the four-week intervention [+W]
