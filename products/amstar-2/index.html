AMSTAR-2¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

AMSTAR-2 is a structured critical-appraisal instrument developed by Shea and colleagues for assessing the methodological quality and overall confidence of systematic reviews of healthcare and educational evidence.

Components¶

  • AMSTAR-2 critical-domain framework for rating the confidence of systematic reviews: AMSTAR-2 is the quality-assessment framework the article uses to rate systematic reviews by overall confidence. Confidence is determined by evaluating seven critical domains — preregistration, literature search, justification for excluding studies, risk of bias assessment, appropriateness of meta-analytical methods, risk of bias in synthesis of results, and publication bias. A lack of addressing one or multiple critical domains yields a low or critically low rating; with no critical flaws, non-critical weaknesses determine a high or moderate rating. The article applied it with two independent reviewers to all 19 reviews. (Wassenaar Thomas M. et al. (2020))
