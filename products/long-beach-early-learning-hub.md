## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Long Beach Early Learning Hub online enrollment system**: The Long Beach Early Learning Hub is an online enrollment system developed collaboratively and hosted by the Mayor's Fund for Long Beach. According to the report, it "helps families find child care options and financial assistance." It is part of a broader city infrastructure that includes the Long Beach Early Childhood Education Committee, a partnership created in 2003 that unites district, city, nonprofit, and health partners around a shared strategic plan, and annual outreach events and a citywide conference for early educators across the mixed delivery system. (Melnick et al. (2026))
