## Description
Breakout is an open platform developed by Dana Calacci and colleagues for creating and measuring synchronous video peer-learning groups in online courses.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Breakout data collection platform capturing speaker history and participation events without linguistic content**: The data collection platform interfaces with video-conferencing tools to record participants' actions during video chats. The article states that "the primary data collected by the platform is speaker history, excluding linguistic content of speech", determined from participants' audio output volume, with low-volume events filtered out. Because volumes are recorded per user, the platform can also identify simultaneous speaking events (overlap), and it collects participant join/leave events. Data is stored in a secure database and exposed via a real-time REST and websocket API. (Dana Calacci et al. (2016))
