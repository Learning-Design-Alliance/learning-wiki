# Robotic cardiac surgery WetLab

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
An anonymized dataset of annotated robotic cardiac surgery wet-lab simulation videos for automated action recognition and objective surgical-skill assessment, released by the article's authors through Synapse.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Robotic cardiac surgery WetLab open dataset of 435 annotated video recordings**: The article releases an anonymized video dataset of robotic cardiac surgery wet lab simulation recordings with temporal action labels. Recordings were captured at "30 frames per second (FPS) with a resolution of 1920 × 1080 pixels" from the da Vinci console, temporally annotated in BORIS for suturing and dissection, and skill-assessment recordings were selected from first attempts where TBS and GEARS scores differentiated novices from experts. The dataset is described as "available as open-source under the project name "Robotic cardiac surgery WetLab" at www.synapse.org". (Atroshchenko Gennady V. et al. (2025))
