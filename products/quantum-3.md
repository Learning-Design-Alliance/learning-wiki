## Description
Quantum 3 is a mobile Match 3 educational game developed by Özkan and colleagues that teaches introductory quantum chromodynamics through gameplay on Android and iOS devices.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Quantum 3, a Match 3 mobile game for learning QCD rules through intuitive play**: Quantum 3 is a phone game in the Match 3 genre that teaches basic QCD through play: the player matches quark tiles on a hexagonal grid to form hadrons, guided by the mascot Atom. As printed, "the gameplay would require learning simple QCD rules to progress," with color introduced first, then flavor, antiparticles and spin. Antiquark power-ups form mesons that clear rows, and dead cells limit overly plentiful matches. It is available on the Google Play store and the Apple Appstore. (Özkan et al. (2019))

### Claims
- [Playtesting showed Quantum 3 players learned baryon composition passively, predicting how to form baryons they had not previously seen](../claims/quantum3-passive-baryon-learning-playtests.md) [+W]
