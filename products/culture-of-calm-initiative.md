# Culture of Calm initiative: a multi-strategy school-climate program delivered through vendors and school staff in 47 Chicago high schools

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
The Culture of Calm initiative was a Chicago Public Schools safety program begun in late 2010 in 47 high schools, intended to improve school climate through "Leadership and staff commitment; Behavior frameworks; Staff development; Student development; Community engagement; and Performance management." Schools received funds for professional development, mentoring, case management, and curriculum, often delivered by vendor community organizations, with a designated coordinator overseeing efforts. Six pilot schools received more resources and central-office consulting support.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A designated CoC coordinator to oversee school and vendor efforts, and principal buy-in for implementation
#### Constraints
- CoC schools served neighborhoods with higher unemployment, poverty, and crime, and had slightly lower incoming test scores than comparison schools

### Target Learners
- high school students in Chicago Public Schools

### Target Learning Goals
- improved school climate and safety supporting student learning
