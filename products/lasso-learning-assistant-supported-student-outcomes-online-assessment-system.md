# LASSO (Learning Assistant Supported Student Outcomes) online assessment system

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
LASSO is a free web-based platform hosted by the Learning Assistant Alliance for administering, scoring, tracking, and reporting research-based STEM assessment instruments.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **LASSO online assessment tool**: LASSO (LA Supported Student Outcomes) is a free online assessment tool hosted on the LA Alliance website that lets STEM faculty administer Research-Based Assessment Instruments as online pre- and post-tests. Faculty provide course-level information, select assessments, and upload student lists; students receive unique links, and faculty can track participation, send reminders, and download responses plus summary reports of pre/post scores, normalized learning gains, and Cohen's d. As of Fall 2016 it hosted 15 online instruments across STEM disciplines, and this study used it to collect physics concept inventory data nationwide. (Jada-Simone S. White et al. (2016))
- **LASSO online assessment tool**: LASSO (LA Supported Student Outcomes) is a free online assessment tool hosted on the LA Alliance website. It "allows faculty (LA-using or not) to easily administer Research-Based Assessment Instruments as pre and post tests to their students online," tracks participation, sends reminder emails, and generates summary reports of pre/post score distributions, normalized learning gains (Hake score), and Cohen's d. As of Fall 2016 it hosted 15 instruments across STEM disciplines, and it supplied the data for this study. (Van Dusen et al. (2016))
