# QuanTime

## Description
QuanTime is a pre-packaged collection of interactive K-12 quantum-information-science activities created and managed by the National Q-12 Education Partnership for classroom use by educators without prior QIST-teaching experience.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **National Q-12 partnership and its QuanTime ready-to-use K-12 quantum activities**: The National Q-12 partnership promotes and supports K-12 quantum education by connecting interested educators and hosting a framework for K-12 quantum education usable to develop QIST curricula. It hosts QuanTime activities, "ready-to-use activities, that K-12 educators can use in their classes without having prior experience with teaching QIST concepts"; in 2024 there were 27 activities available, and teachers were encouraged to devote at least one class period around World Quantum Day on April 14. (Apekshya Ghimire et al. (2024))
- **QuanTime pre-packaged K-12 quantum activities**: QuanTime is a program created and managed under the National Q-12 Education Partnership, "designed as a pre-packaged, interactive resource that helps K-12 teachers introduce quantum concepts to their students in a fun and approachable manner," inspired by the Hour of Code. Activities target foundational topics such as wave-particle duality, electron transitions, entanglement, quantum art and polarization, and quantum games, and "include background materials and implementation guides to support teachers with limited prior experience in quantum physics." In this survey, 8 QuanTime activities were used by respondents. (Ghimire et al. (2024))

### Claims
