# T3 Turnaround Teacher Teams

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
T3 Turnaround Teacher Teams is a Teach Plus and Boston Public Schools school-turnaround programme that places trained teacher leaders and coaches in low-performing schools to support shared leadership, data use, and collaborative planning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **T3 Turnaround Teacher Teams shared-leadership model**: T3, developed by Teach Plus with Boston Public Schools, recruits experienced teachers with records of raising achievement into low-performing schools and trains them to use student data and facilitate teams in formal teacher-leader roles. “The T3 model requires at least 25 percent of a school’s teaching staff to be T3 teachers, to create a critical mass of teacher leaders,” with a T3 coach in each school and weekly teacher-facilitated common planning time. (Sarah Almy et al. (2012))
