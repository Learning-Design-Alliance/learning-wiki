# Bloom's Taxonomy-based PCP Literacy Test (BTPL)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An open educational resource developed by C. Srinivas et al. that combines Parallel Coordinate Plot literacy assessments across Bloom's taxonomy levels with training videos, datasets, and rubrics for use in data-visualization courses.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Bloom's Taxonomy-based PCP Literacy Test (BTPL), an open educational resource**: The BTPL is an open educational resource for teaching and assessing Parallel Coordinate Plot literacy, released with training videos, formative and summative assessment questions for each Bloom's taxonomy module, the original datasets from the intervention, and rubrics for scoring student-created PCPs. The article states it "was designed to improve understanding and proficiency with PCPs" and provides it for reproducibility and reuse by instructors in their own data visualization courses. (C. Srinivas et al. (2025))
