# Singapore Tracker Digital Library

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A community-created digital library distributed through Tracker's Shared Library and related web links, providing Creative Commons video-analysis, modelling lesson packages, and student Tracker files for physics teachers and learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Singapore Tracker Digital Library of creative commons lesson packages and student Tracker files**: Through their mentorship and lesson packages, the authors created creative commons attribution resources that form the Singapore Tracker Digital Library collection, downloadable through Tracker as a Shared Library and via the URL http://iwant2study.org/lookangejss/. Student Tracker files (*.TRZ) are also downloadable via Dropbox blog links for the benefit of all. (Loo Kang Wee (2014))
