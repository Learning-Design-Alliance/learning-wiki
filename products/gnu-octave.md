## Description
GNU Octave is an open-source numerical-computation and programming environment used by educators and learners to model, visualize, and simulate mathematical and engineering systems.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **GNU Octave simulation platform for demonstrating convolution, correlation and optical correlation**: The paper uses the open-source numerical computation platform GNU Octave to generate all simulated teaching materials: 2D sinusoidal gratings, their algebraic addition and product, their Fourier transforms, random screens and their cross-correlations, and simulated Vander Lugt filter recordings. The author states "Simulation platform such as GNU Octave can be utilized to demonstrate the principles" and recommends that correlation exercises "can be assigned to the students as mini projects which can be carried out using GNU Octave". The simulations complement the optical demonstration experiments. (Debesh Choudhury (2018))
