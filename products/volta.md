## Description
VOLTA is a web-based virtual teaching assistant developed by Saleheen et al. to support undergraduate electric-circuits laboratory instruction through digital materials, simulation assistance, hardware diagnostics, quizzes, and feedback.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Circuit Comparator: automated topological equivalence checking of student circuits**: Circuit Comparator is a VOLTA module, developed in Python, that verifies whether a student's simulated circuit (built in CircuitLab or Multisim) is topologically equivalent to a reference circuit stored in the VOLTA database by comparing netlists. The article states it "attempts to emulate the process used by a TA to check the circuit". It translates and sorts netlist components, then constructs a fundamental loop matrix based on Kirchhoff's Voltage Law to determine equivalence, outputting diagnostic information about missing element names or incorrect values when circuits differ. (Saleheen et al. (2018))
