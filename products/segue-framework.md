## Description
The SEGUE framework, developed by Gregory Makoul, is a structured 25-item checklist for assessing physician–patient communication across five encounter domains.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SEGUE framework as a structured checklist for teaching and assessing physician-patient communication**: The SEGUE framework, proposed by Gregory Makoul in 2001, is used in this study as the primary evaluation tool: "a structured checklist designed to assess physician–patient communication competence and consists of five domains encompassing 25 specific behavioral items". The five domains are Set the Stage, Elicit Information, Give Information, Understand the Patient's Perspective, and End the Encounter. Content items are scored if performed at least once, while Process items require correct performance throughout the encounter. A Chinese-adapted version introduced by China Medical University in 2006 was used. (Sun Ning et al. (2026))
