Reach Out and Read (ROR) early math project¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Reach Out and Read's early math project is a family-engagement programme developed and implemented by Reach Out and Read to support young children's early mathematics learning.

Components¶

  • Reach Out and Read early math project scaled up in new sites: Reach Out and Read (ROR) is one of two family engagement providers the Heising-Simons Foundation supported to refine its early math project and scale it up in new sites. The brief states the Foundation "has since supported two providers—Reach Out and Read (ROR) and the YMCA of Silicon Valley (YMCA)—as they refined their early math projects and scaled them up in new sites." Mathematica's implementation study examined this scale-up effort. (Jaimie Grazi et al. (2020))
