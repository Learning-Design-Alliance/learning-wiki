# Three-class trainee taxonomy for NIBS practitioner training: Technician, Clinician, Scientist

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A training-guideline framework that assigns NIBS practitioners to Technician, Clinician, or Scientist roles and specifies role-specific competencies and supervision responsibilities.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Three-class trainee taxonomy for NIBS practitioner training: Technician, Clinician, Scientist**: The guidelines organize NIBS training around three distinct trainee classes, each with its own core competencies. The Technician "applies NIBS to research participants or patients, monitors their wellbeing, and administers certain outcome assessments"; the Clinician establishes indications, prescribes protocols, and supervises Technicians; the Scientist designs the NIBS intervention protocol and may serve as PI. The article notes the roles can overlap, since a Clinician or Scientist may also directly deliver NIBS. (Fried P.J. et al. (2021))
