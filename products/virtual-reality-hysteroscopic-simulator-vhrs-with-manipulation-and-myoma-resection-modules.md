# Virtual Reality Hysteroscopic Simulator (VHRS) with Manipulation and Myoma Resection modules

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A virtual-reality hysteroscopic surgical simulator developed by Immersion Medical (CAE Montreal) that provides interactive modules for resectoscopic targeting, manipulation, and myoma resection practice.
