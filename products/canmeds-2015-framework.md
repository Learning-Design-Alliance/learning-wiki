## Description

## Components

- **CanMEDS 2015 framework as an organizing lens for patient-generated feedback content**: The CanMEDS 2015 framework defines physician competencies across seven roles. The article describes it as "comprised of seven roles: Scholar, Professional, Health Advocate, Communicator, Collaborator, Leader, and Medical Expert" and uses it deductively to categorize patient comments from low-rating reviews. It functions here as a coding taxonomy showing that lay patient complaints map onto formally defined physician competency domains, enabling frequency summaries such as Professional (59.7%) and Leader (45.3%). (Pulkki KH et al. (2025))
