# Ad hoc emotion questionnaire (Test 2)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Jesus Sanchez-Martin et al.'s researcher-developed questionnaire measures secondary students' self-reported emotions in Technology-learning situations using weighted emotion scores before and after an intervention.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Ad hoc emotion questionnaire (Test 2) scoring five emotions with a 100-point positive emotional score**: The authors built an ad hoc questionnaire in which students report which emotion they feel in given class situations (e.g. using the drill, building circuits), choosing among emotions such as Surprise, Happiness, Sadness, Wrath and Fear. Each emotion carries a specific score from 10 (Surprise) to 2 (Fear), and the global score gives a positive emotional score with a maximum of 100 points. The instrument was administered before and after the intervention and validated with Cronbach's alpha. (Jesus Sanchez-Martin et al. (2017))
