## Description
A programme run by the Astrophysics Research Institute at Liverpool John Moores University that gives schools guided access to Liverpool Telescope observations alongside astronomy content and learning activities.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **National Schools' Observatory: guided access to the Liverpool Telescope with online astronomy content and learning activities**: The NSO, established in 2004 and run by the Astrophysics Research Institute at Liverpool John Moores University, provides schools in the UK and Ireland with access to the 2-metre Liverpool Telescope on La Palma "through a guided observing system, using 10% of the LT's time". Its website contains astronomy-related content, news and learning activities, and since 2017 non-UK/Ireland schools and teachers can register for free access to the observing portal and resources. The LT features a broader range of instrumentation than FTN/FTS, including the SPRAT spectrograph. (Lewis (2018))
