## Description
A written-English grammar teaching framework based on Allen's sector analysis and a set of X-words, used with trained teachers to teach L2 learners sentence structure and verb construction.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **X-Word Grammar lesson design: fewer unleveled forms, linked related structures, writing-focused practice with discovery techniques and narrow correction symbols**: The article contrasts the design characteristics of X-Word Grammar lessons with traditional grammar lessons on three key issues. X-Word Grammar teaches "Substantially fewer forms that are unleveled", links related grammar forms together, focuses lessons on written language, uses "Discovery techniques including labelling of structures taught with correction symbols", bases accuracy practice on writing only, and gives feedback on narrow categories of error. Traditional grammar, by contrast, follows structural syllabi in leveled texts, disperses related forms across units, integrates lessons with spoken communicative tasks, uses pedagogic rule descriptions in a Presentation-Practice-Production sequence, and gives feedback on broad or narrow error categories. (Sue Livingston et al. (2018))
