# ASSISTments2017, NIPS34, Algebra2005, and Bridge2006 mathematics knowledge-tracing benchmarks

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A collection of public mathematics student-interaction datasets with question identifiers, knowledge-concept annotations, correctness labels, and varying temporal or response-duration metadata, reused to develop and evaluate knowledge-tracing models.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Four public mathematics knowledge-tracing benchmarks used to evaluate TASA**: The evaluation uses four public benchmarks: ASSISTments2017, NIPS34 (NeurIPS 2020 Education Challenge / Eedi), Algebra2005, and Bridge2006 (KDD Cup 2010). The article reports that "Assist2017 contains 1,708 students, 3,162 questions, 102 KCs, and 942,816 interactions," with NIPS34, Algebra2005, and Bridge2006 providing complementary scales up to 3,679,199 interactions. All provide question IDs, knowledge concept annotations, and correctness labels; only Assist2017 includes response duration. (Yang Wu et al. (2025))
