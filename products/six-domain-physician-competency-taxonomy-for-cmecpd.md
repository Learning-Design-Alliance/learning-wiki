# Six-domain physician competency taxonomy for CME/CPD

## Description
A Delphi- and factor-analysis-informed framework developed by Serbian medical education experts to organize physicians’ continuing professional-development competencies into six domains.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Six-domain physician competency taxonomy for CME/CPD developed by Delphi consensus**: The article develops a competency framework for physicians' continuing professional development through a three-round Delphi process with 51 experts from CME Centres at all five Serbian medical faculties, starting from an Irish CME-needs instrument. "All listed competencies were classified into six domains: management, clinical practice, communication, public health, essential, and clinical emergency." The classification triangulated exploratory factor analysis, correlation coefficients between competencies, and competency concepts, yielding 74 competencies with 75%-or-more expert agreement. The framework is intended to focus CME/CPD on learning outcomes and support self-assessment and program organization. (Mandic Ruzica Nikolic et al. (2024))
