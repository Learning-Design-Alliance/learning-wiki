# Online network survey for mapping friendship, prestige and collaboration ties

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
An online roster-based survey developed by Javier Pulgar et al. for measuring directed friendship, academic-prestige, and physics-collaboration ties among secondary students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Mid-semester network survey collecting nominations of strong peers, explanations, and self-reported interactions**: An online survey administered as a homework assignment in the middle of the 15-week lab course semester. It asked students to list peers "particularly strong in the course material" in each course, to "briefly explain why you chose this student", and to report peers with whom they had a meaningful interaction about course material that week, up to 15 peers per prompt with course rosters available. Response rates were at least 95% of enrolled students, and at least 90% of nominations and interactions were matched to the course roster. (Sundstrom et al. (2024))
