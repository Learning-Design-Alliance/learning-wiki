## Description
Bioblox2.5D is a Unity3D touch-controlled educational puzzle game developed by Frederic Fol Leymarie and colleagues for learning protein docking and molecular-shape concepts on mobile and web platforms.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Bioblox2.5D educational game for protein docking**: Bioblox2.5D is a touch-controlled educational puzzle game, built in Unity3D for mobile and web, in which players dock 2D slices of real 3D protein structures from the Protein Data Bank. It targets teenagers between 13 and 17 years of age and 'aims to improve learning about molecular shape complexity, the roles of charges in molecular docking and the scoring function'. It is freely available on Google Play for Android and playable on the web at the project's website, and is used as in-class support and in public engagement events. (Frederic Fol Leymarie et al. (2022))
