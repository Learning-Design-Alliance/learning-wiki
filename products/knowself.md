## Description
KnowSelf is a Windows activity-logging and reflection application developed as a prototype by Pammer-Schindler et al. to help knowledge workers review and improve their time management.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **KnowSelf activity logging and reflection prototype**: KnowSelf is a Windows activity-log application that "collects time-stamped PC activity data, linked to web or file resources, identifies idle times, and visualizes the fragmentation of worktime". It supports manual time labeling into user-meaningful categories, note-taking, visualizations supporting reflection-on-action, and proactive prompts triggered at predetermined times or by user behavior such as long idleness. It was positively evaluated in two field studies with senior IT and strategy consultants. (Pammer-Schindler et al. (2021))
