# Learning Experience Questionnaire (LEQ) with event-triggered PDA response protocol

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
A questionnaire and handheld-PDA response protocol developed by Lars-Erik Malmberg for repeated real-time measurement of primary pupils’ effort, competence beliefs, and autonomous and controlled motivation during learning tasks.
