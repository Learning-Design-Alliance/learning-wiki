Description¶

LearnLM is Google's Gemini-based language model co-trained to follow pedagogical instructions for interactive tutoring and learning applications.

Claims¶

  • Pedagogy experts prefer LearnLM over contemporaneous flagship models across comparative assessment categories [+M]
  • LearnLM receives the highest expert pedagogy-rubric marks across rubric categories, with the largest leads in active learning, metacognition, and curiosity [+M]
