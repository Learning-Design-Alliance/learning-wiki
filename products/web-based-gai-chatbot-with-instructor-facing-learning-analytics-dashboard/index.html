Web-based GAI chatbot with instructor-facing learning analytics dashboard¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (1 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A login-based web application built with OpenAI's Assistants API that provides course-specific Socratic tutoring and instructors with dashboards for analyzing student conversations and engagement.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Chatbot engagement was highly uneven: 8 of 15 voluntary users interacted continuously while 7 interacted superficially [~W]
  • Students primarily used the Socratic-tutor chatbot as a study aid aligned with existing study habits rather than for the intended Socratic reflection [~W]
  • Telegram-based chatbot use declined after initial novelty, with platform friction cited as a barrier [~W]
  • Students find reflective prompts accompanied by visual elements more actionable and easier to interpret than plain-text prompts [+W]
