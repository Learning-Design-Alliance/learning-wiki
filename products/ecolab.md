## Description
Ecolab is a software learning environment developed by Rosemary Luckin and collaborators for children to explore ecological relationships through interactive modelling and graduated support.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Ecolab software learning environment**: The Ecolab is a software environment with an underlying design metaphor of an Ecology Laboratory into which children aged 9-11 place organisms and explore food-web relationships. It operates in build and run modes, offers four phases of relationship complexity, and provides World, Web and Energy views. The system scaffolds learners through graded help and adjustable activity difficulty, and Ecolab II adds metacognitive scaffolding for planning and help-seeking skills. (Luckin (2003))
