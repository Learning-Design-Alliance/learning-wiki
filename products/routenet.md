# Route.Net

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
Route.Net is a modular extracurricular modern-physics curriculum and thesis pathway developed by Schultheiss et al. for secondary students, comprising interconnected downloadable handouts taught with teacher guidance and culminating in practical cosmic-ray measurements.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Route.Net modular steppingstone pathway to a thesis**: Route.Net organises extracurricular physics content as 'a grid of interconnecting downloadable hand.outs' taught under the guidance of a teacher, with modules of about two hours intensive study time. Steppingstones start from the second grade of secondary education and can be finished with a thesis of about eighty hours study time, including practical measurements. (Schultheiss et al. (2012))
