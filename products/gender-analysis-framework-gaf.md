> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A Jhpiego framework for systematically analyzing gender differences across access to assets, practice and participation, beliefs and perceptions, and institutions, laws and policies.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Gender Analysis Framework (GAF) as a lens for reviewing ICT-enabled learning for schoolgirls**: The review adopts the Gender Analysis Framework (GAF; Jhpiego, 2020) as its organizing lens, a "multidimensional framework that systematically examines how ICT-enabled learning influences schoolgirls". GAF comprises four interrelated dimensions: access to assets, practice and participation, belief and perception, and institutions, laws and policies. The review narrows GAF to primary and secondary education and uses it to code and synthesize the 30 included studies. (Liang et al. (2023))
