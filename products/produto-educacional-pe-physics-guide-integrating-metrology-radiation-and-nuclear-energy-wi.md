# Produto Educacional (PE): physics guide integrating metrology, radiation and nuclear energy with African history and science

## Description
A didactic-methodological high-school physics guide developed by Silva et al. that integrates metrology, radiation, nuclear energy, and African and Afro-Brazilian histories and scientific contributions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Produto Educacional: physics guide integrating metrology, radiation and nuclear energy with African history and science**: The Produto Educacional (PE) is a didactic-metodological guide for high school students presenting physics, metrology and radiation knowledge — "massa, peso, unidades de medidas, energia nuclear, radiações ionizantes e não ionizante" — tied to African history and science. It was applied in a state school in Rio de Janeiro between 2015 and 2016, aiming to demystify erroneous conceptions that African peoples would be primitive and to support implementation of Law 10.639/2003 with Modern and Contemporary Physics content. (Silva et al. (2018))
