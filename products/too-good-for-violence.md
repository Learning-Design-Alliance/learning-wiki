# Too Good for Violence: a scripted K-8 character education curriculum with role-playing, cooperative learning, and eight character values

## Description
Too Good for Violence is a school-based character education program for elementary (K-5) and middle school (6-8) students promoting character values, social-emotional skills, and healthy beliefs. "All lessons are scripted and engage students through role-playing and cooperative learning games, small group activities, and classr oom discussions." Eight character values are incorporated: caring, cooperation, courage, fairness, honesty, respect, responsibility, and self-discipline. Grade-level kits include scripted curricula, workbooks, puppets, visual materials, and parent materials.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Lessons are intended to be taught by trained teachers or Mendez Foundation instructors; the developer encourages attendance at on-site or regional training workshops.
#### Constraints
- The program may have changed since the studies were conducted; the WWC recommends asking the developer about the most current version and considering that student demographics and school context may affect outcomes.
