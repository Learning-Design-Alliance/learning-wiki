# Yiya Solutions

## Description
Yiya Solutions is an offline remote-learning platform that delivers interactive STEM courses through radio broadcasts, basic mobile phones, SMS, Robocalls, and USSD, developed and operated for rural learners in Uganda.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Yiya Solutions radio-and-phone STEM course platform**: Yiya Solutions is the offline, remote learning platform studied: "an offline, remote learning platform designed to bring interactive hands-on 15-week STEM education to participants in rural Uganda". It relies on basic keypad phones and radios; learners receive Robocall and SMS alerts, listen to ~30-minute radio broadcasts, and answer multiple-choice practice questions and weekly assessments in real time via free USSD menus. The 15-week curriculum follows nine Engineering Design Process steps culminating in a hands-on product (a solar-powered food dehydrator), a 16-question final exam, and a community exhibition. (Phenyo Phemelo Moletsane et al. (2026))
