# MadQCI — the Madrid Quantum Communication Infrastructure

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
MadQCI is a metropolitan quantum-communication test bed operated by its infrastructure consortium, providing a software-defined network interface for quantum key distribution and related experimentation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MadQCI — the Madrid Quantum Communication Infrastructure, largest European test bed for quantum communication technologies**: The Madrid Quantum Communication Infrastructure, MadQCI, "is currently the largest test bed for quantum communication technologies in Europe and uses the software-deﬁned approach to quantum networking for providing QKD." In the workshop, attendees could replace their implemented BB84 and E91 algorithms "with a call to the MadQCI network interface to retrieve real quantum distributed key". (Alberto Sebastián-Lombraña et al. (2023))
