## Description
IP-STAR is a two-hour in-situ, low-technology interprofessional pediatric cardiac-arrest simulation module developed and delivered by Kumar Manish et al. for medical and nursing learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **IP-STAR in-situ low-technology simulation module for acute pediatric cardiac arrest team training**: IP-STAR is a two-hour interprofessional simulation module using a low-technology PRESTAN® Infant Manikin coupled with enhanced environmental fidelity using hospital props such as standard pediatric ward equipment, hospital clothing, and crash cart trolleys. It was delivered in-situ within the clinical environment with simulation-safety practices including saline-filled vials labeled for simulation purposes only. The scenario, an 8-month-old infant in pulseless electrical activity with hypovolemic shock, was validated by five external experts with s-CVI 0.93 and referenced the six CIHC competency domains. (Kumar Manish et al. (2025))
