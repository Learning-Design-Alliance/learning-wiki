Kids in Transition to School (KITS) Program¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A manualized school-readiness intervention developed by Pears and colleagues that combines child groups and parent groups to support children with developmental disabilities or delays entering kindergarten.

Components¶

  • KITS Program: 24-session child school readiness group plus 8-session parent group delivered before and during the kindergarten transition: The Kids in Transition to School Program is a short-term, intensive school readiness intervention with two components: "a 24-session school readiness group for the children (2 hours twice weekly in the school readiness phase, 16 sessions; 2 hours once weekly in the transition/maintenance phase, 8 sessions)" and "an 8-session parent group (2 hours every 2 weeks; 4 sessions in each intervention phase)". The manualized child curriculum covers early literacy, prosocial skills, and self-regulatory skills, delivered in classrooms with a 4:1 student-to-teacher ratio. Parents receive parallel content on literacy involvement, routines, and behavior management, with missed content delivered via home visits. (Pears et al. (2016))

Claims¶

  • The KITS intervention has a significant indirect effect on kindergarten teacher-rated literacy through its effect on pre-kindergarten literacy skills (β = .09). [+M]
  • A greater percentage of KITS children than services-as-usual children moved into the low-risk category for reading difficulties on DIBELS and CAPT benchmarks over the summer. [+M]
