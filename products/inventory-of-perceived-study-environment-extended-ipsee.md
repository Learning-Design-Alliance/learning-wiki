# Inventory of Perceived Study Environment Extended (IPSEE)

## Description
A 56-item student questionnaire, with a parallel teacher version, developed by Karin D. Könings and colleagues to measure perceptions of learning environments and discrepancies between perceived and desired design across eight scales.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **IPSEE questionnaire measuring perceptions, desires, and their discrepancy across eight scales of a learning environment**: The Inventory of Perceived Study Environment Extended (IPSEE) is a 56-item Dutch questionnaire measuring students' perceptions of a learning environment and their desires for its design, with each item answered twice on six-point scales. The "absolute difference between the scores on question A and B expresses the perceived-desired discrepancy", with small discrepancies indicating good fit. It has eight scales (fascinating contents, productive learning, integration, student autonomy, interaction, differentiation, clarity of goals, personalization); a parallel teacher version (IPSEE-T) has acceptable internal consistencies (alphas 0.68–0.87 for perception items). (Karin D. Könings et al. (2010))
