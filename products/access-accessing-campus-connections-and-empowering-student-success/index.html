ACCESS (Accessing Campus Connections and Empowering Student Success)¶

Description¶

ACCESS is a two-semester cognitive-behavioral treatment and mentoring programme developed by Laura D. Eddy and colleagues for supporting college students with ADHD in time management, executive functioning, adaptive thinking, study skills, and daily functioning.

Components¶

  • ACCESS: a two-semester CBT and mentoring program for college students with ADHD: ACCESS (Accessing Campus Connections and Empowering Student Success) is a cognitive-behavioral treatment program delivered over two academic semesters for emerging adults with ADHD attending college. The first-semester active phase includes "eight weekly group sessions (90 minutes each)" covering ADHD psychoeducation, behavioral strategies, and adaptive thinking, plus up to 10 weekly 30-minute individual mentoring sessions supporting implementation. The second-semester maintenance phase fades support to one group session and up to six mentoring sessions. Groups of four to six students used a discussion-based format with handouts and guest speakers from campus support services. (Laura D. Eddy et al. (2021))

Claims¶

  • ACCESS produces significantly greater improvement in self-reported time management than delayed treatment among college students with ADHD [+M]
  • ACCESS produces a moderate improvement in self-reported daily functioning relative to delayed treatment among college students with ADHD [+M]
  • ACCESS does not improve GPA or credits earned relative to delayed treatment among college students with ADHD [-M]
