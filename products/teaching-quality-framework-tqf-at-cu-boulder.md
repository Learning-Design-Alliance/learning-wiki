# Teaching Quality Framework (TQF) at CU Boulder

## Description
A campus-wide University of Colorado Boulder framework that defines teaching as scholarly work and organizes departmentally interpreted, multi-measure evaluation around six teaching-quality categories.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Teaching Quality Framework at CU Boulder: scholarly, departmentally interpreted categories for assessing teaching**: The Teaching Quality Framework (TQF) is a campus-wide framework for using scholarly measures of teaching effectiveness that are disciplinary specific and provide faculty feedback to support improved teaching. "The framework defines teaching as a scholarly activity—like research—and assesses the core components of such scholarship," with six evaluation categories held constant across departments: clear goals, adequate preparation, appropriate methods, significant results, effective presentation, and reflective critique. Departments define the components' interpretation and relative weights locally, preserving disciplinary identity while giving the university a common framework. (Dennin M. et al. (2017))
