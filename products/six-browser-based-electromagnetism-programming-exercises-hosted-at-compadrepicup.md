# Six browser-based electromagnetism programming exercises hosted at ComPADRE/PICUP

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A publicly hosted set of six browser-based p5.js programming activities for introductory electromagnetism, developed for adoption in introductory physics courses by Orban et al.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **A set of six browser-based electromagnetism programming exercises hosted at compadre.org/PICUP**: The article presents six required programming activities for the second semester of introductory physics at OSU's Marion regional campus. The authors print the list: "(1) Particle Accelerator!, (2) Particle Accelerator (with potential!), (3) Repulsion between two point charges, (4) RC circuits, (5) Magnetic Force, and (6) Wave Interference." Each activity is designed to take about an hour, and all exercises and solutions are available at compadre.org/PICUP. (Orban et al. (2017))
