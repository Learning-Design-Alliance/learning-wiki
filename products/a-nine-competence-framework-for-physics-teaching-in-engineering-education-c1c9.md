# A nine-competence framework for physics teaching in engineering education (C1–C9)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A nine-domain framework developed by Vanessa Cruz Molina et al. for defining and assessing disciplinary, pedagogical, digital, social, and professional competences of university physics instructors teaching engineering students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **A nine-competence framework for physics teaching in engineering education (C1–C9) with physics-specific interpretations**: The article derives and operationally defines nine teaching competences for physics instruction in engineering programs, synthesized from international and regional frameworks (EU Key Competences, DigCompEdu, UNESCO ICT-CFT, Latin American adaptations). As printed, "nine competences were operationally defined: Technical mastery (C1); Methodological and digital integration (C2); Social interaction (C3); Assessment for learning (C4); Adaptability and innovation (C5); Communication (C6); Information management (C7); Digital ethics and safety (C8); and Professional reflection and resilience (C9)." Each is read through physics-education lenses such as modelling, experimentation, data analysis and problem-solving, and assessed along two axes: conceptual understanding and application in practice. (Vanessa Cruz Molina et al. (2025))
