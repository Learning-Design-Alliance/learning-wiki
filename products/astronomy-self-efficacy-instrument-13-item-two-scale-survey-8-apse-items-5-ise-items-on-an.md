# Astronomy self-efficacy instrument: 13-item two-scale survey (8 APSE items, 5 ISE items) on an 11-point Likert scale

## Description
A 13-item survey developed by Rachel Freed and colleagues to measure undergraduate and other learners' astronomy personal self-efficacy and instrumental self-efficacy for using remote robotic telescopes.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Astronomy self-efficacy instrument: 13-item two-scale survey (8 APSE items, 5 ISE items) on an 11-point Likert scale**: A domain-specific self-efficacy instrument for students, developed following Bandura's guide and modeled on the constructs of the STEBI, probing two aspects: Astronomy Personal Self-Efficacy (content understanding, 8 items) and Instrumental Self-Efficacy (using remote robotic telescopes, 5 items). Unlike the original STEBI instruments, it "employed an 11-point Likert scale of 0 ¼ Strongly Disagree to 10 ¼ Strongly Agree". Scale scores are formed by summing item scores, ranging 0 to 80 for APSE and 0 to 50 for ISE. It is intended as a precursor to investigating science identity in astronomy. (Rachel Freed et al. (2022))
