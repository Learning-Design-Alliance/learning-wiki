Eight-test ancient-history recall scale with weighted score cards¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

Description¶

An assessment developed by Sackett for measuring high-school and university learners’ recall of ancient-history facts through eight timed tests with weighted scoring.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • A 933-pupil survey of ancient-history recall provided the data for scaling question difficulty [+W]
  • Question difficulty was scored as percentage of correctness, assumed inversely related to difficulty [+W]
  • Grading answers required weighted partial credit because responses varied in degrees of rightness [+W]
  • The scale measures only the ability to recall definite facts promptly, a limited kind of historical ability [+W]
  • No question among the sixty had zero difficulty; the easiest averaged 7.83, 7.62, and 7.23 [+W]
  • Question E was answered by all 27 high-school pupils, establishing a zero-difficulty reference 2.191 units below the median [+W]
