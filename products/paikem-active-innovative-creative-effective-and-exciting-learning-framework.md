## Description
An Indonesian Ministry-endorsed instructional framework that organizes classroom learning around active student participation, innovation, creativity, effectiveness, and engaging learning experiences.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PAIKEM: active, innovative, creative, effective, and exciting learning framework**: PAIKEM is an Indonesian Ministry-endorsed instructional model whose name abbreviates "Active, Innovative, Creative, Effective, and Exciting Learning". Active means the teacher creates an atmosphere where "students feel free to actively ask and tell their ideas". The Ministry of National Education lists six benefits, including making students learn more thoroughly, developing children to become more critical and creative, and providing varied learning environments and experiences. Figure 1 presents the main components of PAIKEM, and the model was implemented as the experimental treatment in this study. (Priyono (2018))
