## Description
A computer-assisted wearable measurement and video-analysis system developed for objective assessment of musculoskeletal loads and work-related postures.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CUELA measurement system with synchronized video recording (WIDAAN software) for field assessment of trunk postures**: The Computer-Assisted Recording and Long-Term Analysis of Musculoskeletal Loads (CUELA) system is a personal, ambulatory measurement system for assessing trunk postures during real work. The article states: "Sensors on the thoracic and lumbar spine recorded data on locational and angular positions at a frequency of 50 Hz." Because no external connection was required and the equipment weighed 2.7 kg, nurses could move freely during their daily care routine. The specially developed WIDAAN software synchronized measurement data with video footage, allowing each trunk posture to be assigned to the corresponding work task via an animated computer figure. (Kozak Agnessa et al. (2017))
