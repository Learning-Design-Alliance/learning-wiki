Description¶

A questionnaire developed by Czerniewicz et al. to measure ICT access, ICT-mediated teaching and learning activities, and support satisfaction among students and academic staff at South African universities.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Staff at Structured Corporate institutions report markedly better resources and support than staff at Unstructured institutions, with teaching-venue adequacy the greatest disparity [+M]
  • Variety of ICT use shows a reversed pattern: students in Structured Corporate institutions report the most varied use, but staff variety is highest in the Unstructured Collegium institution [+M]
  • Staff individual ICT use frequency is higher in the Unstructured Collegium institution than in the Structured Corporate institutions, while student frequency is higher in the Structured types [+M]
  • The Unstructured Bureaucratic institutional type is the least enabling context for elearning, showing little evidence of either top-down or bottom-up change [+M]
