# Quantum Teaching Strategy

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A branded instructional framework that orchestrates classroom context and content through an engaging sequence of enrollment, experience, demonstration, reflection, repetition, and celebration, developed for adoption by teachers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Quantum Teaching context orchestration: atmosphere, foundation, environment, and lesson design**: The context element of Quantum Teaching assigns the teacher to orchestrate four aspects of the classroom learning setting. First, orchestrating the learning atmosphere creates intimacy, warmth, humor, and accountability with positive emotional bonds between teachers and students. Second, orchestrating a strong foundation commits teachers to learning behaviors aligned with positive values and lifelong learning. Third, orchestrating the learning environment makes it safe, comfortable, and fun. Fourth, orchestrating the lesson plan prepares students for success through strategies bridging teaching materials and students' prior knowledge. (Gunarhadi et al. (2014))

### Claims
- [Quantum Teaching Strategy produced significantly higher Bahasa Indonesia achievement than conventional teaching in inclusive sixth-grade classes](../claims/qts-higher-bahasa-indonesia-achievement.md) [+M]
- [Quantum Teaching Strategy produced significantly higher Science achievement than conventional teaching in inclusive sixth-grade classes](../claims/qts-higher-science-achievement.md) [+M]
- [The Quantum Teaching Strategy treatment significantly affected academic achievement in both subjects in ANCOVA models](../claims/qts-ancova-significant-both-subjects.md) [+M]
