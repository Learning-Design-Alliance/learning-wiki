Textbook thematic based character education for fourth-grade thematic learning¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A supplementary thematic-learning textbook developed by Wuryani et al. that embeds character-education values and daily-life illustrations for independent use by fourth-grade primary students.

Claims¶

  • Learning with a textbook thematic based character education produces higher thematic-learning outcomes than a conventional textbook for fourth-grade primary students [+M]
  • Authors report, without measuring character, that a character-education thematic textbook strengthens students' character [+W]
