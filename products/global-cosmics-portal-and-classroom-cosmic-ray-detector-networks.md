# Global Cosmics portal and classroom cosmic ray detector networks

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A DESY-hosted portal and network infrastructure supporting classroom cosmic-ray detector activities, data sharing, international comparison, and collaboration for students and teachers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Global Cosmics hands-on cosmic ray activity network**: The Global Cosmic group invites educators and students to hands-on sessions using setups suitable for tabletop deployment. Projects include building cosmic ray detectors with scintillator materials and photomultipliers, and open-source software for data acquisition and analysis, such as the Cosmic Piano project which introduces "both a hardware and data analysis experience". IPPOG serves as an exchange hub for these projects, with long-term school collaborations such as QuarkNet's International Muon Week, where paired schools collect, analyse and share data. (IPPOG Collaboration (2025))
