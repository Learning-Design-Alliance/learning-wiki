## Description
Reading Success Academy is a research-based small-group reading intervention programme delivered by schools to low-achieving secondary students through structured pre-, during-, and post-reading instruction.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Reading Success Academy small-group reading intervention**: The Reading Success Academy was an academic skills intervention program using research-based instructional practices for low-achieving students in small-group instructional sessions. Its core instructional components "consisted of a focus lesson, mini-lesson, and an instructional passage," delivered through pre-, during, and post-reading strategies. In this study it was implemented for ninth-graders pulled from social studies classes three times a week over an eight-week period in 2011-2012, integrating the social studies curriculum with reading strategies tailored to individual student reading data. (Burlison et al. (2014))
