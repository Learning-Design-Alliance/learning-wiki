# Five-goal evaluation framework for embedded computing units: effectiveness, usefulness, usability, equity, sustainability

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A framework proposed by Murali Mani et al. for evaluating computing-skill units embedded in non-computing discipline courses across effectiveness, usefulness, usability, equity, and sustainability.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Five-goal evaluation framework for embedded computing units: effectiveness, usefulness, usability, equity, sustainability**: The article defines five goals for evaluating an ounce of computing skills: effectiveness (the ounces help achieve student learning outcomes), usefulness (the computing skills are relevant and applicable to the discipline), usability (the students can easily use the ounces), equity (all students, regardless of background, benefit equally), and sustainability (the discipline instructor can sustainably integrate, update, and maintain the ounces over time). The framework adapts a K-12 computational thinking tool checklist, adding effectiveness and sustainability while dropping systemic adoption. (Murali Mani et al. (2025))
