# Sound Scouts

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
Sound Scouts is a tablet-based automated hearing-screening platform developed by Sound Scouts that uses game-like listening sub-tests to screen children for possible hearing loss and auditory-processing difficulties.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Sound Scouts game-based hearing and listening screening platform**: Sound Scouts is an automated hearing assessment software presented as a game on tablet devices, used in this study as the listening skills assessment. It comprises three sequential sub-tests — speech-in-quiet, tone-in-noise and speech-in-noise — with the speech-in-noise test establishing a speech reception threshold in noise and aiming to identify auditory processing deficits. Scores are compared to normative data and translated into pass-fail results: fail (0–70), borderline (71–78) and pass (79+). The article reports "a sensitivity of 85% to correctly identify any hearing loss in children increasing to 100% to identify an average hearing loss of worse than 30dBHL" and specificity of between 95–98%. (Tomlin D et al. (2025))
