# PROMPT (Practical Obstetric Multi-Professional Training)

## Description
PROMPT is an adaptable in-house multiprofessional obstetric-emergency training programme developed and delivered through local maternity-unit training teams following a Train the Trainers course.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PROMPT training package (Second edition) with Train the Trainers programme and local multiprofessional courses**: PROMPT (PRactical Obstetric Multi-Professional Training) is an 'in-house', adaptable multiprofessional training day covering management of core obstetric emergencies including postpartum haemorrhage, sepsis and shoulder dystocia as well as fetal monitoring in labour and team-working. In THISTLE it was delivered as the Second edition package with a 2 day Train the Trainers programme at the Scottish Clinical Simulation Centre, after which each unit's in-house training team ran local PROMPT courses aiming to train all maternity staff within 12 months. (Lenguerrand Erik et al. (2020))
