## Description
A direct-observation clinical procedural-skills assessment form used by teachers to rate learners performing procedures with real patients and provide immediate feedback.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **DOPS form for evaluating procedural skills with a 9-point rating scale**: DOPS (direct observation of procedural skills) is used in this study to evaluate the operational skills of community general practice clinics, with teachers directly observing students performing clinical technical operations on real patients and giving immediate feedback. The article states "The DOPS form includes 9 aspects: knowing indications and contraindications, informing patients and obtaining consent, being familiar with operational preparation, having a good aseptic concept, correct and standardized operating steps, accurate and proficient techniques, seeking assistance, post operative treatment, and overall performance". Each item uses a 9-point system: 1–3 for non-compliance, 4–6 for compliance, 7–9 for outstanding performance. (Wang Yuanmei et al. (2024))
