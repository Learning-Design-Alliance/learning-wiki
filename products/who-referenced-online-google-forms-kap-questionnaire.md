# WHO-referenced online Google Forms KAP questionnaire

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A self-administered Google Forms questionnaire developed by Neupane Harish Chandra et al. to assess healthcare professionals' and medical students' COVID-19 knowledge across four domains using 20 scored knowledge questions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **WHO-referenced online Google Forms KAP questionnaire with four COVID-19 knowledge domains**: A self-administered online questionnaire built in Google Forms, prepared "with reference from various current interim guidelines and information for healthcare personnel provided by WHO." It consisted of socio-demographic questions plus items across four knowledge domains — general information about COVID-19, pathogenesis and screening measures, management protocol, and infection prevention measures — with 20 knowledge questions scored by summing correct responses. Validity and readability were checked with ten randomly selected faculty members and pretested among ten randomly selected consultant physicians before distribution. (Neupane Harish Chandra et al. (2020))
