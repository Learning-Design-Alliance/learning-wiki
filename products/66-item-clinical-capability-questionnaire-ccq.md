# 66-item Clinical Capability Questionnaire (CCQ)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 66-item self-report questionnaire developed by Scicluna et al. to assess recent medical graduates’ perceived clinical capability and preparedness for hospital practice across clinical, procedural, operational, administrative, and preparation-for-practice domains.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **66-item Clinical Capability Questionnaire (CCQ) for graduate self-assessment**: A 66-item Clinical Capability Questionnaire constructed in two parts for this evaluation. Part 1 comprises "46 items listing a range of clinically-relevant tasks" rated from 'I did not try the skill' (1) to 'I tried the skill and I mastered it' (5), divided into clinical skills, procedural skills, operational management and administrative tasks subscales; Part 2 draws four subscales from the Preparation for Hospital Practice Questionnaire. Cronbach’s alpha coefficients "were from 0.82 to 0.85". (Scicluna Helen A et al. (2012))
