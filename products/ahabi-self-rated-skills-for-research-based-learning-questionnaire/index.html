AHABI (Self-rated skills for research-based learning) questionnaire¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A 20-item Likert-scale self-administered questionnaire developed by Cobos Alvarado et al. to measure undergraduate students' self-rated research skills for research-based learning.

Components¶

  • AHABI: a 20-item self-rated questionnaire for measuring undergraduate research skills: The AHABI (Self-rated skills for research-based learning) is a self-administered Likert-scale questionnaire measuring research skills in university students. It consists of 20 items rated from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree), developed because of a shortage of instruments for research skills: "The shortage of these instruments led to the creation of the scale of 'Self-rated skills for research-based learning' (AHABI)". It was validated with 150 basic-cycle engineering students and takes about 20 minutes to complete. (Cobos Alvarado et al. (2016))
