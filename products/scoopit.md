## Description
Scoop.it is an online content-curation platform developed by Scoop.it that lets users collect, annotate, organize, and share web resources through topic pages.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Scoop.it curation platform with master, group and subject topic pages for case-based learning**: Scoop.it is an online content curation platform that allows "online content relevant to a particular audience to be gathered in the same place", with commentary from the curator. In this implementation each of the 30 CBL groups had a group page with all members invited as co-curators, plus case master pages for the 6 cases and subject pages; staff reviewed and 're-scooped' useful content to whole-year topic pages. Activity was publicly viewable under the educational license. (Duncan C et al. (2017))
