# ALPHA (Athlete Learning Program about Health and Anti-Doping)

## Description
WADA's interactive e-learning anti-doping programme, translated into Chinese by CHINADA, comprising eight modules on anti-doping knowledge, ethical decision-making, doping-control procedures, and resisting pressure to dope.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **WADA's ALPHA e-learning anti-doping programme (Zh-hans version)**: ALPHA (Athlete Learning Program about Health and Anti-Doping) is WADA's interactive e-learning seminar, translated into Chinese by CHINADA in 2020. It consists of 8 modules covering the doping control process, whereabouts, therapeutic use exemptions, results management, medical and ethical reasons not to dope, practical help to stay clean, and dealing with pressure. Modules 7–8 use "an 'if–then' plan, scenario-based learning, and video testimonials from elite athletes". In this study it was delivered on a computer screen in a fixed module sequence taking approximately 80 min. (Deng Zhangyan et al. (2022))
