# ePA Coach

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (5 for, 1 mixed, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
ePA Coach is a web-based microlearning platform developed by Perotti et al. for older adults to learn electronic patient-record use through multimedia units, simulated practice, gamification, and conversational support.
