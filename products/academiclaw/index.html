AcademiClaw¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

AcademiClaw is an 80-task bilingual benchmark dataset with rubrics and evaluation code for assessing OpenClaw AI agents on complex academic workflows, created by its article authors.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Over a quarter of benchmark tasks (23 of 80) defeat all six frontier models, with over 22% showing capability boundaries where scores swing by up to 90 points [+W]
  • Even the best frontier model achieves only a 55% pass rate on AcademiClaw's 80 academic tasks [+W]
