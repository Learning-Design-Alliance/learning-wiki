## Description
Gephi is an open-source network analysis and visualization platform developed and maintained by the Gephi community and contributors.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Gephi network visualization tool for controversy mapping in classrooms**: Gephi is "an interactive visualization and exploration tool for all kinds of networks" used by students to render hyperlinked website corpora as network maps. It displays nodes as websites and edges as hyperlinks, showing "the relative centrality of a website in relation to a particular issue" through size (inbound links), position (edge quantity), and color attributes that update in real time as students rank, filter, and colorize nodes. (Solli et al. (2018))
