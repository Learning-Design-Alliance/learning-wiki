## Description
A basal reading textbook curriculum developed by Condelli et al. that adapts Wilson and Orton-Gillingham phonics methods for low-literate adult ESL learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Sam and Pat Volume I basal reader: systematic, sequential, multisensory literacy instruction adapted from Wilson and Orton-Gillingham systems for adult ESL literacy learners**: Sam and Pat Volume I (Hartel, Lowry, & Hendon, 2006) is a basal reader adapting Wilson and Orton-Gillingham methods to adult ESL literacy learners. It moves students "systematically and sequentially from simple to complex skills and materials", uses multisensory phoneme segmenting and blending (e.g., sound tapping), sound cards and controlled text, and continual cumulative review. The evaluated volume contains 22 multi-component lessons, with the vowel introduction sequence modified to maximize contrasts among short vowels that are challenging for English learners to discriminate. (Condelli et al. (2010))
