# Persian-validated measurement battery: Academic Adjustment Scale, Academic Hope Scale, and Student Academic Engagement Scale

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A Persian culturally adapted battery of self-report scales used to measure undergraduate students’ academic adjustment, academic hope, and academic engagement, administered by the study researchers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Persian-validated measurement battery: Academic Adjustment Scale, Academic Hope Scale, and Student Academic Engagement Scale**: The study administered three instruments: the nine-item Academic Adjustment Scale (Anderson et al. 2016) covering "Academic Lifestyle", "Academic Success", and "Academic Motivation"; the 27-item Academic Hope Scale (Khormaei and Kamari 2021) with subscales for gaining opportunities, life skills, and achieving competence; and the 40-item Student Academic Engagement Scale (Alonso-Tapia et al. 2023) with agency, behavioral, cognitive, and emotional subscales. Reliabilities were 0.73, 0.73, and 0.83 respectively, with CFA fit indices within acceptable thresholds. (The Mediating Role of Academic Engagement in the Relationship Between Academic Hope and Ac)
