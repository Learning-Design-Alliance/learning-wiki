# TNCSSE-NGSS Guidemaps

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An 18-poster standards-correlation framework developed by Larry Bowman and colleagues that maps Tennessee science standards to NGSS disciplinary core ideas for K–12 science educators.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Eighteen correlation posters (guidemaps) mapping Tennessee Science Standards to NGSS across kindergarten through high school**: A set of 18 posters created by STEM Fellow Larry Bowman showing the correspondence between the current Tennessee Science Education standards (Huffman, 2009) and the NGSS (Achieve, 2013). The article describes them as "a series of guidemaps betwee n the Tennessee Curriculum Standards for Science Education (TNCSSE) for grade levels kindergarten through high school and the corresponding NGSS." They were built as tools for educators doing curriculum development and revision and are accessible on the GK-12 Program Website. (Bowman et al. (2014))
