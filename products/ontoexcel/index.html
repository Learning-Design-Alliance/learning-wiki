OntoExcel¶

Description¶

OntoExcel is a Semantic Web-based pedagogical-agent component developed by Karalar et al. for representing prerequisite relations among ICT learning objectives and supporting learning-object assignment.

Components¶

  • Ontology-driven prerequisite objective analysis for meaningful learning in SWBL: The article grounds SWBL in cognitive theories of meaningful learning: prerequisite knowledge activated as a foundation for new knowledge, and learning promoted when new information relates to existing schemas. OntoExcel encodes classes, object properties (hasLO, hasPrerequest, isLoOf, hasObjectiveOf, isObjectiveOf) and inverse properties so the pedagogical agent can determine which objectives are prerequisites or become difficult unless prior objectives are learned. Objective analysis was conducted for each objective before learning objects were assigned. (Karalar et al. (2016))

Claims¶

  • Pre-service teachers who learned ICT spreadsheets via a Semantic Web based learning environment achieved significantly higher post-test scores than those receiving traditional teaching [+M]
