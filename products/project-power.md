## Description
Project POWER is a universal trauma-informed school-based program that teaches emotion regulation, coping and decision-making, communication, and psychoeducation to support behavioral, emotional, and academic functioning among adolescents.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Project POWER theory of change: improving self-regulatory capacities leads to improved emotional and behavioral functioning and, in turn, academic performance**: The trial is organized around an intervention theory of change (Figure 1) in which a universal trauma-informed program builds "underlying processes" — emotion regulation, coping/decision making, and self-efficacy — which are expected to improve "emotional and behavioral outcomes" such as internalizing symptoms and externalizing problems, and ultimately "education outcomes" including grades, test scores, and disciplinary actions. The program's intervention components are emotion regulation, problem solving, communication, and psychoeducation. Findings will be used to refine this model. (Mendelson et al. (2020))
