# Collaborative Logical Framework (CLF)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (2 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A web-based collaborative-learning support tool developed by Santos et al. that guides learners through individual, collaboration, and agreement stages and adapts grouping, moderation, and recommendations from interaction indicators.
