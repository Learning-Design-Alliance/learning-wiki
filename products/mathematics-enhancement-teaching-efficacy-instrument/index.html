Mathematics Enhancement Teaching Efficacy Instrument¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A validated self-report instrument developed by Jansen to measure mathematics teaching efficacy, personal mathematics efficacy, and personal teaching efficacy in agricultural education teachers.

Components¶

  • Mathematics Enhancement Teaching Efficacy Instrument (Jansen, 2007): The Mathematics Enhancement Teaching Efficacy Instrument, developed and validated in a doctoral dissertation at Oregon State University, measures three constructs: mathematics teaching efficacy, personal mathematics efficacy, and personal teaching efficacy. "The instrument utilizes a different rating scale for each construct" — personal mathematics efficacy (1 = not at all confident to 4 = very confident), mathematics teaching efficacy (1 = strongly disagree to 5 = strongly agree), and personal teaching efficacy (1 = nothing to 9 = a great deal of influence). Post-hoc reliabilities in this study were .93, .80, and .89 respectively. (Christopher T. Stripling et al. (2013))

Claims¶

  • Preservice agricultural teachers were efficacious in personal mathematics and personal teaching efficacy but only moderately efficacious in mathematics teaching efficacy throughout the course [+M]
