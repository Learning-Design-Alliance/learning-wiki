# ComCare questionnaire (ComCareP and ComCareD)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An eight-item Likert questionnaire developed by Gärtner et al. for simulated-patient and physician self-assessment of communication, interpersonal skills, and consultation satisfaction.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ComCare questionnaire: an eight-item instrument for rapid post-consultation assessment of communication and interpersonal skills**: ComCare is a newly developed short questionnaire combining three communication items with four interpersonal (CARE-derived) items plus one general satisfaction item, each rated on a five-point Likert scale. It exists in a simulated-patient version (ComCareP) and a physician-perspective self-assessment version (ComCareD). Its design purpose was "the need for quick evaluation by the simulated patients directly after the consultations" so formative feedback could be given between patient interviews. (Gärtner Julia et al. (2021))
