## Description
A publicly downloadable OSF-hosted study dataset containing item-level self-concept and comparison-related belief measures, intervention indicators, and class identifiers, released with Mplus analysis syntaxes by the study authors.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Publicly downloadable study dataset hosted on OSF**: The article states that the study data can be downloaded from the Open Science Framework at the printed link, accompanying the Mplus syntaxes for the latent change score models of self-concepts and comparison-related beliefs. The dataset file is named finaldataset.dat in the syntaxes and contains item-level variables for both measurement times, intervention dummy variables, and class identifiers. (Boosting Students' Academic Self-Concepts (2024))
