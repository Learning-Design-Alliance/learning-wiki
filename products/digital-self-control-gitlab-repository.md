## Description
The digital-self-control GitLab repository, released by Bodonhelyi et al., provides implementations and reproduction instructions for centralized and federated learner-state detection benchmarks, including environment, data-distribution, and hyperparameter details.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Published implementation and reproduction instructions for the federated learner-state detection benchmarks**: The authors released their implementation in a public GitLab repository (gitlab.lrz.de/hctl/digital-self-control) together with "instructions for reproducing our experiments", covering the experimental environment, data distribution, and hyperparameter tuning detailed in the article's appendix, to support reproducibility of the centralized and federated learner-state detection benchmarks.
