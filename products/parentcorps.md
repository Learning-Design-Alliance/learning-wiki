# ParentCorps

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
ParentCorps is a family-engagement and social-emotional learning program that equips early-childhood educators and families through structured professional development, materials, and facilitator support, including the ParentCorps Portal.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ParentCorps Portal**: The ParentCorps Portal is a technology component provided to program facilitators. "Facilitators receive access to the ParentCorps Portal, where they can connect with ParentCorps staff, access all program materials, and receive additional resources" to strengthen social-emotional learning and family engagement and enhance cultural and racial awareness in daily practice. (ParentCorps program description (2024))
- **ParentCorps three-component model: staff professional development, a 14-week parenting program, and a classroom SEL program**: ParentCorps operates through three coordinated components. First, professional development helps staff "build strong, culturally responsive relationships with families" and reflect on their beliefs about families while learning the science of early childhood development. Second, a group-based 14-week parenting program affirms families' experiences, builds family-to-family connections, and shares evidence-based parenting practices. Third, Friends School is a classroom program developing children's social-emotional skills. (ParentCorps program description (2024))

# ParentCorps program

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
ParentCorps, developed by New York University Langone Health's Center for Early Childhood Health and Development, "works with schools and parents/caregivers to help pre-kindergarten children develop foundational skills." It is available in multiple languages and "deeply rooted in racial equity," with three components: professional development for staff, a group-based 14-week parenting program, and Friends School, a classroom-based social-emotional learning program. It was evaluated in two randomized controlled trials in New York City.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Works with existing staff and educators at Head Start or public pre-K programs
- Professional development, training, responsive coaching and close support from experienced ParentCorps Educators
#### Constraints
- 

### Target Learners
- Pre-kindergarten children in high-poverty, predominantly Black and Latino school districts

### Target Learning Goals
- Social-emotional skills including communicating about thoughts and feelings, positive sense of self, and healthy relationships
- Early academic skills in reading, writing, and math
- Evidence-based parenting practices and family engagement
