## Description
BeachBoard is CSULB's customized D2L Brightspace learning management system, operated by the university for course materials, communication, assessments, assignments, recordings, and grades.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **BeachBoard (BB), CSULB's customized D2L Brightspace learning management system**: BeachBoard (BB) is the learning management system CSULB adopted in 2010, "developed and supported by the D2L (Desire 2 Learn) company as a customized version of their 'Brightspace' platform." It provides instructor-student communication, course material sharing, lecture recording, discussion forums, and design and management of assessments, assignments and grades. During the COVID-19 transition, CSULB advised instructors to mainly focus on learning BB (and Zoom) to convert courses online, and BB quizzes supported randomizing question order and parameter values and preventing return to previous questions. (Shadnaz Asgari et al. (2020))
