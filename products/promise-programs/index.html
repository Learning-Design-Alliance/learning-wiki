PROMISE programs¶

Description¶

PROMISE programs are federally supported state-level initiatives that coordinate services for youth with disabilities receiving SSI and their families to improve educational and employment outcomes.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • PROMISE's primary innovations over usual services were intensive case management, facilitating early work experiences, and a family focus [+W]
  • The PROMISE evaluation estimated program impacts on service receipt, education, employment, and related outcomes at 18 months after enrollment [+W]
  • The PROMISE interim report estimated impacts across seven outcome domains for youth and families [+W]
  • Many SSI youth received transition services without PROMISE, but there was substantial room for PROMISE to improve service use [+W]
  • PROMISE youth experienced worse education and employment outcomes than ACS youth with and without disabilities [-W]
