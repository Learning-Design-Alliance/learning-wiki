# Compass Care

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
Compass Care is a family-centered wraparound programme run by a Compass Care Team to support chronically absent students and their caregivers through attendance and engagement barriers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Six-phase program cycle from referral through post-program sustainability**: The article specifies a reusable program structure: although adaptable to school context, "Compass Care is consistently composed of six phases: referral, onboarding, engagement, action, transition, and sustainability." Referral identifies students missing at least 10 percent of school days; engagement builds rapport through nonjudgmental communication; action involves weekly student meetings and biweekly caregiver meetings with SMART goals; transition celebrates completion; sustainability adds check-ins for three months after the cycle ends. (Zimiles et al. (2025))
