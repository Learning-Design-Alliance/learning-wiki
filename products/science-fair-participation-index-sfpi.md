## Description
A quantitative index developed by Dejan Vinković for comparing Croatian counties' representation in national science competitions relative to their high-school populations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Science Fair Participation Index (SFPI) as a size-normalized measure of county competition participation**: The SFPI is the article's quantitative instrument for comparing counties: it is defined as the ratio between the actual number of students from a county who reached the national competition level and the expected number based on the county's total high-school population. The article states that this ratio between the actual and expected number of students is the Science Fair Participation Index, and that a balanced educational system would have SFPI close to one for all counties. Counties are also grouped into low, medium and high development categories (7 counties each), with group standard deviations shown as error bars. (Dejan Vinković (2010))
