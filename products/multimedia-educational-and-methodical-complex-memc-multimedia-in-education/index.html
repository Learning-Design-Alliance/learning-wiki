Multimedia educational and methodical complex (MEMC) “Multimedia in education”¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A multimedia educational and methodical complex developed for Informatics teacher-training courses to support classroom instruction and students’ independent study of multimedia tools.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • The multimedia-based methodology produced a significantly higher share of students reaching high and medium levels of the integrative cognitive independence indicator than traditional teaching [+W]
  • The multimedia tool was reported to motivate students and support positive attitudes toward the course [+W]
