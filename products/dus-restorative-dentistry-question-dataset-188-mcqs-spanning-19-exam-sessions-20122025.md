# DUS restorative dentistry question dataset: 188 MCQs spanning 19 exam sessions 2012–2025

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A benchmark dataset of 188 Turkish restorative-dentistry multiple-choice questions from 19 Turkish Dental Specialty Examination sessions, compiled and verified for dental education assessment and AI evaluation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **DUS restorative dentistry question dataset: 188 MCQs spanning 19 exam sessions 2012–2025**: A benchmark dataset of restorative dentistry multiple-choice questions compiled from all 19 Turkish Dental Specialty Examination (DUS) sessions between 2012 and 2025, obtained from the ÖSYM website. After excluding two annulled items, 188 questions were analyzed in their original Turkish format and cross-checked against official answer keys, with a randomly selected subset independently verified by a second researcher. Questions were categorized by exam year, content domain, question type (case-based/knowledge-based), and format type (image-based/text-based). (Haberal Merve (2026))
