# VLCAM (Virtual Learning Center for Asthma Management)

## Description
A computer-based interactive e-learning programme developed around the Korean Asthma Management Guideline for physicians-in-training, combining an e-book, self-assessment, virtual-patient problem-based learning, and an online reference room.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **VLCAM: a four-section e-learning program for the Korean asthma guideline**: The Virtual Learning Center for Asthma Management (VLCAM) is a computer-based interactive e-learning program built on the Korean Asthma Management Guideline. It "consists of four sections: asthma guideline e-book, self-assessment, problem-based learning with a virtual patient, and online reference room." The virtual patient section presents complaints as text with voice narration plus photographs, radiographs, laboratory findings and auscultation sounds, and users perform history-taking, diagnosis and management decisions. The reference room supplies guideline documents, management cards, action plans, and patient handouts. (Kang Sung-Yoon et al. (2018))
