## Description
The PEF is Washington, DC's public funding stream addressing pay disparities between child care and early education (CCEE) educators and public school teachers. Launched in 2022, it "delivered direct payments of $10,000 to $14,000 annually to more than 4,000 educators over its first two years." In FY 2024 it shifted to a facility payroll model aligned with a public school-based salary scale, and in FY 2023 it began providing access to subsidized health insurance.
