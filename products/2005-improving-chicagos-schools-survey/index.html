2005 Improving Chicago's Schools survey¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A spring 2005 survey administered across Chicago Public Schools by the Consortium on Chicago School Research to collect reports from students, teachers, and principals about school experiences, instruction, professional development, and working conditions.

Components¶

  • 2005 Improving Chicago's Schools survey of Chicago public school students, teachers, and principals: A system-wide survey administered in spring 2005 across the Chicago public school system, in which "nearly 130,000 students, teachers, and principals" participated. Students reported on "their school experiences, attitudes, and activities," while teachers and principals reported on classroom instruction, professional development experiences, and their working conditions. (Improving Chicago's Schools: Sample Elementary Summary 2005 (2005))
