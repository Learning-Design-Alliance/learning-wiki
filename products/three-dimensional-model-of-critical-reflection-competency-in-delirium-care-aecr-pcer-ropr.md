# Three-dimensional model of critical reflection competency in delirium care (AECR, PCER, ROPR)

## Description
A researcher-developed framework and 19-item self-report assessment model for evaluating ICU nurses' critical reflection competency in delirium care across analytical-evaluative, person-centered ethical, and risk-oriented preventive dimensions.
