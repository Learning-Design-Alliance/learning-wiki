# ECCT — Emotional and Cognitive Competence Test

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A patented admissions test developed by Vera María and Cortés José A. for an Ecuadorian technological institute, measuring verbal, numerical, and abstract reasoning alongside emotion regulation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ECCT — Emotional and Cognitive Competence Test**: The ECCT is a patented admission test (patented in 2015 by Cortés and Vera) used at an Ecuadorian technological institute that measures verbal aptitude, numerical aptitude and abstract reasoning via dichotomous scored items (maximum 90 points), plus emotion regulation through six scored scenarios (0–20) and a 15-item Likert self-questionnaire. The article proposes it as an admissions instrument for Ecuador, analogous to the SAT in the United States. Its CFA fit in this sample was χ2(48) = 124.298, RMSEA = 0.029, CFI = 0.98. (Vera María; Cortés José A (2021))
