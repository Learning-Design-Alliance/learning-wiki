Equity-centered, vendor-accountable edtech evaluation system model¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A proposed community-college edtech evaluation and procurement framework from Van Nostrand et al. that uses vendor disclosures, student-co-designed competency badges, and transparent directories to make vendors accountable to institutional and learner needs.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Community college students want to play a role in edtech evaluation and procurement decisions [+M]
  • Student assessors led the pilot assessment of the Centering Students' Diverse Lived Experiences badge, shifting evaluation power toward student voice [+M]
  • Community college students hold concrete quality criteria for edtech, including evidence of claims, accessibility, and peer and faculty connection [+W]
