Children's Progress Academic Assessment¶

Description¶

The CPAA is a computerized adaptive assessment developed for preschool settings to measure children's literacy and mathematical skills and supplement teacher ratings of developmental progress.

Components¶

  • Children's Progress Academic Assessment (CPAA): The CPAA is "a computerized, adaptive assessment" integrated into teachers' practice in preschool classrooms as a key ELTM feature. It "assesses literacy and mathematical skills" and is intended to complement the DRDP-PS teacher rating, expanding the information available to teachers and parents about children's progress. (Emily Moiduddin et al. (2014))
