# Spanish-translated MIA:STEP self-assessment questionnaire

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A Spanish-language self-report questionnaire adapted from SAMHSA's Motivational Interviewing Assessment: Supervisory Tools for Enhancing Proficiency (MIA:STEP) to measure prison professionals' perceived adherence to and competency in motivational interviewing.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Spanish-translated MIA:STEP self-assessment questionnaire**: A Spanish-translated version of the Motivational Interviewing Assessment: Supervisory Tools for Enhancing Proficiency (MIA:STEP) questionnaire, originally developed by SAMHSA, used as a self-report measure on a Likert scale of 1 to 7. It assesses four dimensions: adherence (frequency of applying MI techniques such as open questions, affirming and reflective listening), competency (perceived skill), and the incompatible versions of both, which capture "managerial or confrontational approaches, such as imposing solutions, excessive use of closed questions or direct persuasion". (Martín-Coca A (2025))
