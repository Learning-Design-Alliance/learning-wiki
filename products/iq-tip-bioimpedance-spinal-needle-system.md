# IQ-Tip bioimpedance spinal needle system

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
Injeq Plc's spinal-needle system detects cerebrospinal fluid through tip bioimpedance and provides real-time audio-visual feedback during lumbar puncture.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **IQ-Tip bioimpedance spinal needle system with real-time CSF detection**: The IQ-Tip system (Injeq Plc, Tampere, Finland) is a 90 mm spinal needle that senses tissue bioimpedance at the needle tip. It "provides real-time feedback from the needle tip when penetrating the lumbar tissues and informs the physician when the needle tip reaches CSF with an audio-visual alarm." In the study it was used by novice students on a lumbar phantom, requiring no additional devices such as ultrasound imaging. (Lilja Helmiina et al. (2024))

### Claims
- [Novice medical students using a bioimpedance needle achieve a higher overall lumbar puncture success rate on a lumbar phantom than those using a conventional spinal needle](../claims/bioimpedance-needle-higher-overall-lp-success-novices.md) [+M]
