# Jefferson Scale of Empathy – Student Version (JSPE-S)

## Description
A 20-item, 7-point Likert self-report instrument for measuring medical students' empathic orientation toward patients, developed by the Jefferson medical education research group.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Jefferson Scale of Empathy – Student Version (JSPE-S)**: The JSPE-S is the self-report instrument used by most included studies; the review describes it as measuring affective empathy and studying "the empathic relationship between the medical student and the patient". It consists of 20 items on a 7-point Likert scale, with a minimum of 20 and a maximum of 140 points, where a high score indicates a more empathic orientation. All cross-sectional studies except four, and all longitudinal studies except one, employed the JSPE-S. The review notes the scale focuses on thoughts and not actions, citing Sulzer. (Andersen Freja Allerelli et al. (2020))
