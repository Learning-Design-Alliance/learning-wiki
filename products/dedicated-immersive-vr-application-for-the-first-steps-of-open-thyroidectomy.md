# Dedicated immersive VR application for the first steps of open thyroidectomy

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
An OramaVR Inc. application commissioned by the Hellenic Society of Endocrine Glands Surgery that provides immersive, haptic-controller-based practice of the initial technical steps of open thyroidectomy on MetaQuest3.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Dedicated immersive VR application for the first steps of open thyroidectomy (MetaQuest3, OramaVR)**: A dedicated immersive VR training application commissioned by the Hellenic Society of Endocrine Glands Surgery and designed by OramaVR Inc., run on a MetaQuest3 head-mounted display with haptic controllers. It "illustrated specific steps in thyroidectomy including neck incision, dissection of the subcutaneous tissue, creation of sub-platysma flaps, middle line dissection, vagus nerve dissection and placement of a continuous intraoperative neuromonitoring (C-IONM) electrode on the vagus nerve". Residents had to complete the process at least once but could resume training as long as they wished. (Basios Anestis et al. (2025))
