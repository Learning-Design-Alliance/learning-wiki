# Spanish adaptation of the Community Service Attitudes Scale (CSAS)

## Description
A Spanish-language, 46-item, ten-subscale Likert questionnaire adapted from Shiarella et al.’s Community Service Attitudes Scale to assess undergraduate dental students’ attitudes toward community service.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Spanish adaptation of the Community Service Attitudes Scale (CSAS) for dental students**: The study used a web-based questionnaire adapted from the Community Service Attitudes Scale (CSAS) of Shiarella et al., translated into Spanish and reviewed by a bilingual native speaker. It retained "all ten subscales originally proposed for the CSAS", comprising 46 items on a five-point Likert scale, with empathy kept as a distinct subscale. A pilot with 24 speech therapy students showed excellent internal consistency (Cronbach's α = 0.929), and overall reliability in the main sample was 0.885 pre-test and 0.957 post-test. (Leiva Hernández Carolina et al. (2026))
