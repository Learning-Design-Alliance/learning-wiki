# Attitudes toward Mathematics Word Problem Inventory (ATMWPI), Students' Interest Inventory (SII), and Instructional Programme on Mathematics Word Problem (IPMW)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (5 for) · 3 studies (3 causal), `q2` · 1 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
Awofala's study-developed battery comprising an attitudes inventory, an interest-preference inventory, and parallel personalised and non-personalised mathematics word-problem instructional materials.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ATMWPI, SII and IPMW instruments used to measure attitudes, elicit preferences and deliver personalised versus non-personalised instruction**: The study used three evaluative instruments and one stimulus instrument: the Attitudes toward Mathematics Word Problem Inventory (ATMWPI), a 35-item, seven-subscale measure with Cronbach's alpha coefficients of 0.96, 0.97, 0.98, 0.98, 0.94, 0.87 and 0.90 and test-retest stability of 0.83; the Students' Interest Inventory (SII), a 20-item open-ended inventory used to determine selected student preferences; and the Instructional Programme on Mathematics Word Problem (IPMW) in two parallel versions. "The personalised version provided familiar, relevant problem contexts and was written by incorporating the most popular referents (places, foods, sports, etc.) from the SII", while the non-personalised version used standard textbook problems with minimal contextual information; both incorporated Polya's four-part strategy. (Awofala (2014))
