StructRAG¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), q1 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A Sun Y. et al. framework that converts engineering-diagram images into graphs, retrieves structural templates, and uses pattern-aware LLM reasoning to support interpretation, feedback, and automated grading.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Current AI tutoring systems misinterpret complex STEM engineering diagrams, omitting edges, misclassifying component connections, and flattening hierarchical topologies [+W]
  • StructRAG achieves 89.3% question-level accuracy and 93.0% edge-level F1 on 1,650 STEM diagram questions, outperforming OCR+CV, GPT-4 graph-only, direct-image GPT-4o, and ablated variants [+M]
