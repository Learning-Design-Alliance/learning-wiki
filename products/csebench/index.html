CSEBench¶

Description¶

CSEBench is a publicly released benchmark dataset of 624 expert-annotated multiple-choice English-proficiency items with human response data, developed by Shangchao Min et al. for evaluating performance against China’s Standards of English Language Ability.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Closed-source LLMs reach CSE Level 6 while most open-source baselines cluster at CSE Levels 3–4 on CSEBench [+M]
  • CSEBench items show adequate psychometric quality, with satisfactory difficulty, discrimination, and reliability for both diagnostic forms [+M]
  • Benchmark success on human-designed assessments may not establish that the underlying abilities are being measured [~W]
  • LLMs perform competently at comparing item difficulty but fall below random chance on comparing item discrimination [~W]
