# Knowledge with Standard Precautions Questionnaire (KSPQ), Attitude with Standard Precautions Scale (ASPS), and Compliance with Standard Precautions Scale (CSPS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A validated Chinese-context assessment battery developed or adapted for nursing students to measure standard-precautions knowledge, attitudes, compliance, and objective hand-hygiene effectiveness.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Validated Chinese-context instruments for measuring standard precautions knowledge, attitudes, compliance, and hand hygiene**: The study measured outcomes with the Knowledge with Standard Precautions Questionnaire (42 items, CVI = 0.92, α = 0.76 in this study), the Attitude with Standard Precautions Scale (34 items across 5 dimensions, CVI = 0.89, α = 0.84), and the Compliance with Standard Precautions Scale (20 items, α = 0.88 in the pilot), which was translated into Chinese through a bilingual forward-backward process authorized by the scale author. Handwashing effectiveness was measured objectively with the Standard Bacterial Colony Index via swab cultures, with qualified counts below 10 CFU/cm2 in general units. The authors state the study "used validated and reliable measures appropriate to the Chinese context, and used an objective outcome measure of behavior change." (Xiong Peng et al. (2017))
