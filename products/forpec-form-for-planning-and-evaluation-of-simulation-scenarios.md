# ForPEC (Form for Planning and Evaluation of Simulation Scenarios)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
ForPEC is a standardized form and companion operational manual developed by the authors for guiding health professionals in planning and evaluating healthcare simulation scenarios.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ForPEC Operational Manual version 1.2 with instructions and two example scenarios**: The Operational Manual is a companion document with instructions for completing ForPEC. The article states it "contains 43 pages and the 91 ForPEC items are presented with their respective descriptions and guidelines to guide the scenario elaboration." It includes two example scenarios: a technical scenario (chest tube) and a non-technical scenario (communicating bad news). Participants rated the manual 100% agreement on including all instructions and 96.43% on usefulness. (Kaneko Regina Mayumi Utiyama et al. (2022))
