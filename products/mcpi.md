## Description
An 8-item instrument for assessing the clinical learning environment and gathering free-text feedback from graduate-entry medical students on clinical placements, grounded in Experience-Based Learning in Communities-of-Practice theory.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Use the MCPI rather than the DREEM when assessing the graduate entry student learning experience in the clinical setting**: The authors recommend the MCPI, an 8-item instrument grounded in Experience-Based-Learning in Communities-of-Practice theory, for assessing the clinical learning environment of graduate entry medical students. It correlated strongly with the 50-item DREEM, was rated slightly more worthwhile, and its free-text comments provided "a rich description of the clinical learning environment". The recommendation rests on the instrument's theoretical grounding, brevity, and richer qualitative yield in this cohort. (Hyde Sarah et al. (2018))
