Arizona linked education–child welfare student database¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A study-created database linking longitudinal K–12 education records from the Arizona Department of Education with child records from the Arizona Department of Child Safety to identify and describe students in foster care.
