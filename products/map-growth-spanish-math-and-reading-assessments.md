# MAP Growth Spanish Math and Reading assessments

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
Computer adaptive Spanish-language assessments parallel to English MAP Growth, administered up to four times per school year in fall, winter, spring, and an optional summer window. "While the MAP Growth Spanish Math assessments use the same scale and norms as the English Math assessments, MAP Growth Spanish Reading is on its own scale and has independent norms", reflecting construct differences between English and Spanish literacy. Spanish Math tests cover grades K–12 using shared English blueprints; Spanish Reading covers K–8 with Spanish-specific content, targeted audio support in K–2, and grade-band splits to keep texts age-appropriate.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Items aligned to mathematics or language arts standards and mapped to grade-band instructional areas
- Blueprints with a maximum of 43 items per test event and minimum Aspects of Rigor category counts for Math
#### Constraints
- Spanish Reading item sets with extended passages currently include only field test items until a sufficient calibrated pool exists
- Spanish Math items are not separately field tested; they adopt the RIT values of their calibrated English counterparts

### Target Learners
- Students who speak Spanish as their first language or receive instruction in Spanish, grades K–12 (Math) and K–8 (Reading)

### Target Learning Goals
- Mathematical achievement and growth without the barrier of English-language fluency
- Spanish reading achievement and longitudinal growth in reading
