## Description
StudentBench is a public platform and associated open dataset developed by Curtis Northcutt et al. for large-scale evaluation of AI tutors using randomized studies with real students, expert ratings, and post-test learning gains.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **StudentBench platform and open dataset for evaluating AI tutoring by real student learning gains**: StudentBench is "a suite of AI teaching evaluations and a public platform that enables large-scale data collection with over 175,000 student–AI messages". It runs randomized pre/post learning-gain studies comparing AI tutors, human tutors, and control, and produces five leaderboards covering lesson planning, practice-problem creation, conversational pedagogy, cost, and engagement. Unlike prior LLM tutoring benchmarks judged by LLM evaluators or simulated students, it measures real students' unaided post-test gains after live tutoring. The de-identified data and reproduction code are open-sourced. (Curtis Northcutt et al. (2026))
