# Review corpus of 44 peer-reviewed articles comprising 62 embodied learning environments for graphing motion

## Description
A literature-review evidence corpus assembled by Carolien Duijzer et al. containing 44 peer-reviewed articles and coded descriptions of 62 embodied learning environments for teaching graphing of motion in mathematics and physics.
