# Work Competence Report (WCR)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A supervisor-completed instrument for assessing occupational-therapy students' fieldwork competence across professional knowledge and skills, clinical reasoning, ethics, interpersonal relationships, professional development, and work performance.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Work Competence Report (WCR) for summative assessment of fieldwork performance**: The Work Competence Report is a summative assessment instrument used by supervisors to evaluate students' fieldwork performance. It "comprises professional knowledge and skills, clinical reasoning, ethical behaviour, interpersonal relationships, professional development and work performance." It is completed twice per fieldwork block (mid-term and end-of-term) and conveyed to students through grades, written comments and face-to-face feedback. (de Beer Marianne (2015))
