# extended Essay-BR and Brazilian Portuguese Narrative Essays datasets

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A pair of Portuguese essay corpora containing ENEM-style and Brazilian public-school narrative essays with human rubric scores, released for research on automated essay scoring.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Portuguese essay corpora: extended Essay-BR and Brazilian Portuguese Narrative Essays datasets**: The study used two human-rated Portuguese essay corpora. The abstract states the corpus "consisted of the extended Essay-BR, which includes 6,563 essays in the style of the National High School Exam (ENEM), and the Brazilian Portuguese Narrative Essays, comprising 1,235 essays written by 5th to 9th grade students from public schools". Essay-BR essays were scored 0–200 per competency by two expert raters with a third resolving discrepancies; BPNE essays were scored I–V on four competencies under the Brasil na Escola rubric. Both datasets show significant imbalance in cohesion score distributions. (Bruno Alexandre Rosa et al. (2025))
