# Korbit dialogue-based intelligent tutoring system

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (6 for, 1 mixed) · 5 studies (5 causal), `q2` · 0 of 5 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
Korbit is a machine-learning, NLP, and reinforcement-learning platform developed to provide interactive, personalized tutoring through lecture videos, exercises, automated free-text solution checking, and adaptive interventions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Korbit: a large-scale dialogue-based intelligent tutoring system for data science topics**: Korbit is "a large-scale, open-domain, mixed-interface, dialogue-based ITS, which uses ML, NLP and reinforcement learning to provide interactive, personalized learning online", with thousands of students enrolled for topics related to data science, machine learning, and artificial intelligence. It tutors by alternating between short lecture videos and interactive problem-solving; incorrect solutions trigger an inner-loop system that selects among pedagogical interventions using an ensemble of ML models. (Kochmar et al. (2020))
- **Korbit's inner-loop finite-state machine architecture with reinforcement-learned intervention selection**: The Korbit ITS inner loop manages exercise interaction as a finite-state machine in which each pedagogical intervention is a separate state; 'each state operates independently of the rest of the system', and transitions between states are decided by a reinforcement learning model that continues to improve with data, while an outer loop selects content from a personalized curriculum. (Serban et al. (2020))
- **Korbit: a large-scale, open-domain, mixed-interface, dialogue-based ITS for STEM**: Korbit is 'a large-scale, open-domain, mixed-interface, dialogue-based ITS' using machine learning, natural language processing and reinforcement learning to provide interactive, personalized learning online. Launched with a machine learning course in 2019, it has enrolled over 7,000 students and teaches data science, machine learning and artificial intelligence topics. (Serban et al. (2020))
- **Mixed-interface lessons alternating short lecture videos with interactive dialogue-based exercises**: Korbit tutors students by 'alternating between short lecture videos and interactive problem-solving exercises', delivered through a mixed interface that 'includes videos, interactive dialogue-based exercises, question-answering, conceptual diagrams, mathematical exercises and gamification elements'. The outer loop decides which video or exercise comes next based on the personalized curriculum. (Serban et al. (2020))
- **Automated data-driven personalization: an ML/NLP approach to selecting pedagogical interventions from a student's zone of proximal development**: The article proposes an approach in which state-of-the-art machine learning and NLP techniques generate automated, data-driven personalized feedback — hints, Wikipedia-based explanations, and mathematical hints — that "takes the individual needs of students into account". Feedback is chosen by "an ensemble of machine learning models from the student's zone of proximal development (ZPD) based on their student profile and last solution attempt". The approach requires no expert intervention or hand-crafted rules and is designed to scale across domains; it was deployed in the Korbit ITS. (Kochmar et al. (2020))
- **Korbit learning platform (Platform B): AI tutor delivering personalized, active learning through problem-solving exercises**: Platform B is the Korbit online learning platform, powered by an AI tutor that "creates a personalized curriculum for every student" and teaches "through short lecture videos, interactive problem-solving exercises, mini-projects, and by providing personalized pedagogical interventions mimicking a human tutor." The tutor addresses each incorrect attempt or help request with one of a dozen pedagogical interventions tailored to student needs. In the study, its personalized curriculum and programming exercises were disabled to allow a fair comparison. (Comparative Study of Learning Outcomes for Online Learning Platforms)
- **Automate, standardize and simplify content creation so teachers can author ITS modules in hours**: Korbit was 'designed to easily scale to thousands of subjects, by automating, standardizing and simplifying the content creation process'; unlike other ITS, 'a teacher can develop new learning modules for Korbit in a matter of hours'. The design targets the high authoring cost that has limited ITS deployment in education and industry. (Serban et al. (2020))
- **Korbit: an AI-powered, dialogue-based intelligent tutoring system with ML, NLP and RL personalization**: Korbit is an AI-powered, large-scale, dialogue-based intelligent tutoring system whose "fully-automated system based on a suite of machine learning (ML), natural language processing (NLP), and reinforcement learning (RL) models" customizes the curriculum for each individual student and adapts content in real time to the student's proficiency and needs. To achieve deep personalization and interactivity, it "alternates between teaching via video lectures, Socratic tutoring, interactive problem-solving exercises, coding exercises and project-based learning". When a student's solution is incorrect, the system selects one of a dozen pedagogical interventions — hints, explanations, elaborations, mathematical hints, concept tree diagrams, multiple choice quiz answers — with the type and difficulty chosen by RL models based on the student's learning profile and previous solution attempts. (A New Era: Intelligent Tutoring Systems Will Transform Online Learning for Millions)
