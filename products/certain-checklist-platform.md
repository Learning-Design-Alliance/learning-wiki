## Description
The Checklist for Early Recognition and Treatment of Acute Illness (CERTAIN) is a standardized, evidence-based checklist platform developed for structuring critical-care patient presentation, evaluation, and management.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CERTAIN checklist platform for structured evaluation of critically ill patients**: The Checklist for Early Recognition and Treatment of Acute Illness (CERTAIN) is a standardized platform providing "a standardized approach and checklist of common, evidence-based best critical care practices." In this study it served as the platform for structured evaluation of critically ill cases during tele-education: learners presented patients using the CERTAIN Admission Checklist and Rounding Checklist, and providers evaluated ICU structure and processes by organ systems based on the platform. The authors note the evidence for the checklist's efficacy per se is limited, but its content is based on current critical care society practice guidelines. (Kovacevic Pedja et al. (2019))
