# 44-item IADT-based TDI knowledge and confidence questionnaire

## Description
A researcher-developed questionnaire assessing fourth-year dental students' knowledge and self-reported confidence in managing traumatic dental injuries across primary and permanent dentitions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **IADT-based TDI questionnaire validated by Delphi expert consultation**: A structured electronic questionnaire assessing TDI management was developed based on the 2020 IADT guidelines and validated through a Delphi expert consultation. It comprises demographics plus four sections: "Section I evaluated TDI clinical exposure and educational experiences", Section II knowledge competency (five items), Section III professional attitudes (six Likert-scale items), and Section IV clinical practice patterns (four items). It was administered via SoJump and WeChat with digital informed consent. (Yu Jian et al. (2025))
