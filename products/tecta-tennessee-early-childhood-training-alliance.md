## Description
A Tennessee statewide programme that provides childcare providers and administrators with free orientation training, professional development, credential support, and tuition assistance through participating public higher-education institutions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **TECTA: Tennessee Early Childhood Training Alliance**: TECTA is 'a statewide program that provides free training and academic tuition support to childcare providers and administrators throughout the state of Tennessee.' It offers a free 30-hour orientation with instruction in five areas — center-based child care, infant/toddler care, family child care, school-age child care, and administrator training — after which participants become eligible for tuition assistance for coursework at any public higher education institution in Tennessee. Nine state and community colleges currently offer TECTA programs, which support workers pursuing the CDA, associate degree, and bachelor's degree, and provide CDA candidates with tuition assistance, textbooks, professional development, and application support. (Jacobs et al. (2024))
