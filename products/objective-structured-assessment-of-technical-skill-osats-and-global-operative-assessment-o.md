# Objective Structured Assessment of Technical Skill (OSATS) and Global Operative Assessment of Laparoscopic Skills (GOALS)

## Description
OSATS and GOALS are validated global rating-scale assessments used by surgical educators and researchers to evaluate operative technical performance, including laparoscopic skills.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **OSATS and GOALS global rating scales for assessing operative performance in laparoscopic cholecystectomy**: The article uses two validated global rating scores as primary outcome measures: the Objective Structured Assessment of Technical Skill (OSATS) and the Global Operative Assessment of Laparoscopic Skills (GOALS). OSATS "was originally validated for direct observation in open surgery, and later validated in laparoscopic cholecystectomy", while GOALS "was developed and validated to assess the specific skilled required for laparoscopic surgery, including direct and delayed observation". GOALS domains include depth perception, bimanual dexterity, efficiency and tissue handling, with autonomy in direct observation. Both use continuous scales with minimum 1 and maximum 5 per category. (Humm Gemma; Mohan Helen Peter; Fleming Christina; Harries Rhiannon; Wood Christopher; Dawas Khaled; Stoyanov Danail; Lovat Laurence B (2022))
- **OSATS (Objective Structured Assessment of Technical Skills) as a multi-component rubric for assessing surgical technical skill from video**: OSATS is a structured scoring instrument for surgical technical skill consisting of six components: "Gentleness", "Time and motion", "Instrument handling", "Flow of operation", "Tissue exposure", and "Overall technical skill". Each component is "graded between 1 and 5, with a score of 1 being deficient/traumatic and 5 being flawless". Summing the components yields a total OSATS score for surgical performance. In this article, two blinded graders applied OSATS to video recordings of robotic gastroenterostomy to measure surgeon performance and learning curves. (Video Assessment of Surgical Performance in Robotic Gastroenterostomy During Pancreatoduod)
