# In situ specific IC checklist

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A 13-step dichotomous checklist developed by Tudor Car et al. to assess application-specific VR interaction proficiency independently of clinical reasoning in final-year medical students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **In situ specific IC checklist embedded in the STEP-VR emergency room simulation**: A checklist-based performance measure embedded within the VR emergency room scenario previously used for summative OSCE assessment. Participants receive standardized verbal instructions decomposed into discrete interaction steps (eg, "Pick up and attach SpO₂ clip"), each scored dichotomously as correctly completed within a calibrated time limit. The final instrument comprises 13 steps classified as object manipulation, spatial navigation, or UI activation, deliberately designed to be independent of clinical reasoning so that immersive competence is isolated from medical knowledge. (Tudor Car L et al. (2026))
