## Description
A 20-item National League for Nursing participant-perception questionnaire, translated and validated in Portuguese, that assesses the structure of healthcare simulation settings across five factors.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Simulation Design Scale (EDS) instrument for assessing simulation setting structure**: The Escala do Design da Simulação, developed by the National League for Nursing and translated and validated for Portuguese, is a participant-perception instrument used "to evaluate the structuring of HS settings". It is "made up by twenty items, distributed in five factors (objectives and information, support, problem resolution, feedback/reflexion, and realism)". In this study it was administered after the simulation training to all 91 participants, yielding an overall mean of 4.55 and Cronbach's alpha of 0.898. (dos Santos Emílio Carlos Alves et al. (2021))
