## Description
The BBKS is an 18-item self-report questionnaire developed by Bernal-Guerrero, Cárdenas-Gutiérrez, and Montoro-Fernández to measure compulsory-secondary students’ basic business knowledge in management, legal, and strategic domains.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Basic Business Knowledge Scale (BBKS), an 18-item self-report instrument with a three-value Likert response format**: The BBKS is an 18-item questionnaire measuring basic business knowledge of Compulsory Secondary Education students, organized in three subscales (KBM 7 items, LK 5 items, SK 6 items). To suit adolescents it "was decided to adopt a three-value Likert scale" scored 2 for worked and learned, 1 for worked but not learned, and 0 for not worked. Students completed it online via the Moodle platform during a 50-minute session, and the article provides Spanish and English versions as supporting files. (Bernal-Guerrero Antonio; Cárdenas-Gutiérrez Antonio Ramón; Montoro-Fernández Elisabet (2020))
