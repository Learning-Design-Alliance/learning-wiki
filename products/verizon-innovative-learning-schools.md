## Description
Verizon Innovative Learning Schools is a partnership between Verizon and Digital Promise, running since 2014, that now reaches over 560 Title I middle and high schools nationwide. Students and educators receive free devices and up to four years of data plans for off-campus access. The program focuses on developing educators' digital skills through extensive training and technology-driven STEM curriculum, and "each school is required to deploy a full-time learning coach who receives further training from Digital Promise and is dedicated to supporting educators in successful technology usage." Digital Promise supports schools in creating sustainability plans and has published a toolkit for all districts creating technology plans.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each participating school deploys a full-time learning coach trained by Digital Promise
#### Constraints
- The program serves select Title I middle and high schools and provides data plans for up to four years

### Target Learners
- Title I middle and high school students and educators

### Target Learning Goals

## Description
The Verizon Innovative Learning Schools program, a Verizon-Digital Promise partnership begun in 2014, has reached 626 schools, over 620,000 students, and nearly 32,000 teachers across 11 cohorts. It rests on two named pillars: "Always Available Access," providing students 24/7 connectivity and devices, and "Powerful Professional Learning," including live and on-demand learning, customized support, and job-embedded coaching. District leads reported devices and connectivity were the hook for joining, but coaches are described as the linchpin of the transformation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructional technology coaches hired for and supported by the program, since without the coach "the program does not work" per one district lead
#### Constraints
- Access to devices and internet alone is described as "far from enough" without coaching support

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in program-supported schools
- teachers and instructional technology coaches

### Target Learning Goals
- technology integration into instruction
- digital equity and inclusion
- STEM and digital skills
