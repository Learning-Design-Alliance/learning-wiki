# CitNetExplorer

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
CitNetExplorer is software developed for managing, analyzing, and visualizing large citation networks, including clustering and identification of core publications.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CitNetExplorer software for citation network analysis of academic performance literature**: CitNetExplorer is the software tool the authors used to analyze and visualize citation networks of scientific publications, downloading citation networks directly from Web of Science. It "makes it possible to manage citation networks which include millions of publications and related citations", and offers clustering, drilling down, and core-publication identification functions. The authors argue it "offers a more detailed analysis of the citation networks in comparison with other databases such as WOS or Scopus", since those databases do not provide an overview of connections between citations across a group of publications. In this study it supported analysis of 16,157 publications and identified 4660 core publications with four or more citations. (Martinez-Perez C. et al. (2020))
