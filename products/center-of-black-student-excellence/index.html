Center of Black Student Excellence¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A planned Long Beach Unified School District entity that provides tutoring, mentoring, cultural programming, wellness services, legal aid, career support, and advocacy for Black students and families.

Components¶

  • The Center of Black Student Excellence (CBSE): The CBSE is a planned district entity in LBUSD described as "a visionary establishment whose design is shaped by and for the Black Community where they can proudly discover and honor their identity, history, and cultural heritage, which fosters a sense of inclusion and confidence." It is more than a physical location: a commitment to nurturing belonging, pride, and agency among Black students and families. The Center will offer tutoring, mentoring, cultural programming, wellness services, legal aid, career support, and advocacy, and will act as a bridge between the district and the Black Community. (Powell (2024))
