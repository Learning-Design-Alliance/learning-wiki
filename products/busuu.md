## Description
busuu is a commercial mobile and web language-learning application developed by busuu for learners to practise foreign-language skills through structured lessons and interactive activities.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **busuu mobile language learning app integrated as an addition to language lessons**: busuu (https://www.busuu.com/enc/) is a mobile language learning application chosen for the project to combine computers and mobile devices. It was used by the experimental group as "an addition to their language lessons, which generally took place over 90 minutes twice a week" for eight weeks. Students received two-month premium access and reported the app was easy and simple to use and worked smoothly on their devices. (Kétyi (2015))

### Claims
- [Students using the busuu mobile app alongside lessons improved language test performance (+2.2%) while the control group declined (-3.1%), a statistically significant post-measurement difference (p=.013)](../claims/busuu-experimental-group-gains-control-declines.md) [+M]
- [Students found busuu easy and useful especially for vocabulary and writing, but 73.3% thought it helps without being the ultimate tool, and language learning apps were largely unknown to them](../claims/busuu-user-satisfaction-limited-help.md) [+M]
