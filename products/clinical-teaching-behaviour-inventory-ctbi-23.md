## Description
A 23-item, six-domain, five-point Likert student-rated assessment of preceptors' clinical teaching behaviours, developed for undergraduate nursing clinical-practicum settings.

### Claims

- [Preceptors of undergraduate nursing students in Islamabad were rated highest on guiding interprofessional communication (M=3.97) and lowest on showing concern and support (M=3.48) across six CTBI domains](../claims/preceptor-ctbi-domain-profile-islamabad.md) [+M]
