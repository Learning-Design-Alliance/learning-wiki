Edmodo¶

Description¶

Edmodo is a private educational micro-blogging and learning-management platform developed by Edmodo, Inc. for teachers, students, and parents to share assignments, quizzes, feedback, files, polls, calendars, and updates.

Components¶

  • Edmodo as a secure private micro-blogging learning platform for secondary EFL classrooms: Edmodo is a private micro-blogging service that "provides a free and secure learning platform" resembling Facebook but restricted so that only teachers create and manage accounts and only students with a group code can join. Teachers can post quizzes, assignments, feedback, grades, files, links, polls, calendars and SMS alerts, and parents can view homework and events. In this study it served as an additional online classroom community layered onto traditional EFL teaching for six weeks. (Fatimah Al-Kathiri (2014))

Claims¶

  • Saudi female secondary EFL students who used Edmodo alongside traditional teaching showed more positive EFL learning attitudes than a control group after six weeks [+M]
  • Experimental students' perceptions of Edmodo were highly positive, ranking pedagogical purposes highest and social interaction lowest [+M]
  • Students reported technical difficulties with Edmodo more than financial ones, with the small mobile screen and slow internet the top challenges [~M]
