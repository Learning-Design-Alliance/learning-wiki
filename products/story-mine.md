# STORY MINE

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
STORY MINE is a digital recovery-narrative module in the Minddistrict client portal that provides curated hopeful recovery stories as video podcasts with written summaries and audio narration.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **STORY MINE digital recovery narrative intervention**: STORY MINE is a transdiagnostic eHealth module within the Minddistrict client portal offering 12 purposively collected hopeful recovery narratives as video podcasts with written summaries and audio narrations. Narratives were curated for "a diversity of narrators and experiences, authenticity, hopeful content, and a recovery phase between intermediate and full recovery." Service users follow a four-step process of selecting, engaging, evaluating, and returning to the overview, deciding themselves when and how many narratives to consume. (Lases Mitch N et al. (2026))

# STORY MINE digital recovery narrative intervention

## Description
STORY MINE is a transdiagnostic eHealth module within the Minddistrict client portal offering 12 purposively collected hopeful recovery narratives as video podcasts with written summaries and audio narrations. Narratives were curated for "a diversity of narrators and experiences, authenticity, hopeful content, and a recovery phase between intermediate and full recovery." Service users follow a four-step process of selecting, engaging, evaluating, and returning to the overview, deciding themselves when and how many narratives to consume.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Sufficient understanding of the Dutch language; delivered as an unguided self-help module within an existing eHealth platform
#### Constraints
- Evaluated only as an unguided 12-week supplement to care as usual in a pilot feasibility trial; not tested for clinical efficacy

### Target Learners
- adult service users receiving outpatient specialized mental health care

### Target Learning Goals
- personal recovery
- hope
- mental health confidence
- reduced self-stigma
