Math 180¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Math 180 is Scholastic's targeted mathematics intervention programme combining adaptive student software with teacher-facilitated instruction for students striving to reach grade-level proficiency.

Components¶

  • Math 180 targeted math intervention program with adaptive student application and teacher-facilitated instruction: Math 180 is a targeted math intervention program that "builds algebra readiness in core or dedicated intervention classrooms, using research-based strategies for striving students." It combines an adaptive student application with teacher-facilitated instruction to accelerate students toward grade-level proficiency. Lessons target foundational computation and reasoning skills, including "operations with whole numbers, fractions, ratios, decimals, proportional reasoning, and functional thinking" to prepare students for on-grade-level coursework. (Math 180® Evidence Rating Summary (2017))

Claims¶

  • Math 180 matched comparison study: intervention-eligible students in grades 5-8 showed significantly greater NWEA MAP gains than matched comparison students (ES +0.23) [+W]
  • Math 180 randomized study: 6th graders receiving Math 180 showed greater growth on NWEA MAP mathematics than business-as-usual comparison students (ES +0.31) [+W]
