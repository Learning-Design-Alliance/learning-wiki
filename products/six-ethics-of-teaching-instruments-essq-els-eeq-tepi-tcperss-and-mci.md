# Six ethics-of-teaching instruments: ESSQ, ELS, EEQ, TEPI, TCPERSS, and MCI

> **Evidence** · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 review), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A review-synthesized set of six self-report or peer-assessment instruments developed by their respective instrument authors to measure teachers' ethical sensitivity, leadership, ethical evaluation, professional-ethics principles, compliance with ethics, and moral competence.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Six ethics-of-teaching instruments: ESSQ, ELS, EEQ, TEPI, TCPERSS, and MCI**: A set of 6 instruments assessing the ethics of teaching and related moral constructs, synthesized by this COSMIN-based systematic review: the Ethical Sensitivity Scale Questionnaire, Ethical Leadership Scale, Ethical Evaluation Questionnaire, Teaching-Profession Ethical Principles Inventory, Teachers' Compliance with Professional Ethics in Relations with Students Scale, and Moral Competency Inventory. The review reports that "The number of items ranged from 10 to 56, and the number of domains ranged from 1 to 7," and that most are "self-report Likert-type questionnaires, except for the TCPERSS scale, which incorporates peer assessment." Target populations were primarily teachers and faculty members in Iran, Türkiye, and the United States, totaling more than 3,000 participants. (Chaimo Kesinee (2026))
