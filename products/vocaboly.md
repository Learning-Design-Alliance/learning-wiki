## Description
Vocaboly is a multimedia computer-assisted vocabulary-learning software package, developed for learners and instructors to study, practise, test, and review vocabulary.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Vocaboly multimedia software for computer-assisted vocabulary instruction**: Vocaboly is the multimedia software the study used to teach vocabulary to the experimental groups. It "has several parts such as ‘Study’, ‘Test’ and ‘Game’", including word lists with meaning, difficulty level and phonetic symbol, pronunciation playback, spelling practice with corrective feedback, multiple-choice testing in two modes, and the ‘memory’ and ‘star war’ games. Sessions used prepared source files of ten words each, delivered over eight 15-minute sessions. (Nejati et al. (2018))
