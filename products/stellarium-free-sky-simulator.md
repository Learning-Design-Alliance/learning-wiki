## Description
Stellarium is free, open-source planetarium software developed by the Stellarium team that simulates the sky for educational and observational use.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Stellarium free sky-simulation software as a data-collection and visualization tool in astronomy teaching**: Stellarium is a free program that simulates the sky, downloadable at www.stellarium.org, used throughout the three sequences for locating celestial structures, visualizing historical and simulated observations, accelerating time to record Io's eclipse exits, changing observer location to Jupiter's surface, and reading stellar data such as spectral type, magnitude and distance. The authors state that "a utilização de simuladores de céu, como o Stellarium, torna-se uma ferramenta essencial" in classroom work, and that it is "uma ótima ferramenta alternativa" for identifying lunar features. (Oliveira et al. (2020))
