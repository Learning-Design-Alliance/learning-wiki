## Description
EXCALC is a REDUCE-embedded computer-algebra package for symbolic exterior differential geometry and tensor calculus, developed for scientific and educational use.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **EXCALC package for exterior differential-geometry calculus embedded in REDUCE**: EXCALC is a computer-algebra package "completely embedded in REDUCE", designed for easy use by those familiar with the calculus of modern differential geometry. It handles scalar-valued exterior forms, vectors, and non-scalar-valued (indexed) forms, with operations such as exterior multiplication, exterior differentiation, inner product, Lie derivative and Hodge duality, plus COFRAME/FRAME declarations for metric structures and the RIEMANNCONX command for connection 1-forms. The article uses it to compute connection and curvature forms (e.g. on S(2)) and to derive exact Einstein-equation solutions in class. (Florin A. Ghergu et al. (2000))
