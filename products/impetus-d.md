## Description
IMPETUS-D is an 11-module online screen-based simulation training programme developed by experts in simulation and healthcare-worker education to teach dementia-specific palliative and end-of-life care to nursing-home staff.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Functional task alignment and learner engagement as design concepts prioritised over fidelity in simulation training**: The article adopts two simulation-design concepts, citing prior work, that it considers "more valuable than 'fidelity'": functional task alignment, defined as "the simulator does what is required to meet the learning objective", and learner engagement, defined as "the extent to which the learner participates or 'buys-in' to in the activity to meet the learning objective." IMPETUS-D aimed to achieve both by developing narratives with provider- and user-perspective experts and by shooting videos and still images in local facilities with the voices of actual care staff. This framework organises how the modules were designed rather than reporting a tested result. (Tropea Joanne et al. (2019))
- **Trigger-narrative module design with threaded storylines, deliberate practice and recorded-response rehearsal**: The IMPETUS-D modules are built around 'trigger narratives' reflecting common residential care scenarios that raise opportunities or dilemmas for staff. Each module uses a brief 1-2 min video scenario with still images, text and guiding questions to encourage active learning. The article states that "the story line of the trigger narratives has been threaded across modules" and that "deliberate practice is encouraged by having different but somewhat overlapping tasks in different modules." An innovative communication feature asks learners to record a response to a family member's question, listen, reflect and revise it before hearing a good example response, with uploads shared anonymously on a discussion board to encourage collaborative learning. (Tropea Joanne et al. (2019))
