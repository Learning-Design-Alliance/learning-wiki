## Description
A Delphi-developed checklist created by Kocyigit et al. for expert-observed assessment of lay caregivers’ pediatric basic life-support performance before and after simulation training.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **16-step PBLS checklist aligned with 2021 ERC guidelines and developed through the Delphi method**: A standardized 16-step performance checklist used to assess caregiver PBLS skills before and after simulation training. It was developed through the Delphi method and aligned with the 2021 European Resuscitation Council PBLS Guidelines. Each item is scored as 10 (correct), 5 (partially correct), or 0 (incorrect or omitted), with performance independently assessed by two expert instructors through structured direct observation. (Kocyigit S et al. (2026))
