# Modified Knowledge and Attitudes Survey Regarding Pain (KAS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A Swedish-translated and context-adapted version of the Knowledge and Attitudes Survey Regarding Pain used to measure registered nurses' knowledge and attitudes about cancer pain management.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Modified Knowledge and Attitudes Survey Regarding Pain (KAS) as primary outcome measure**: The primary outcome is RNs' attitude and knowledge measured with a modified version of the KAS at baseline, four and twelve weeks. The original instrument has 40 items including false-true statements, multiple-choice questions and two case studies, with test-retest reliability r > 0.80 and internal consistency between 0.70 - 0.73. The authors removed two items considered context-specific to the instrument's country of origin and made a back translation into Swedish by an authorised translator, as the instrument had not been used in Sweden previously. (Borglin Gunilla et al. (2011))
