# Psychomotor Assessment Scale for Pre-schoolers

## Description
A preschool assessment instrument developed by Borrego-Balsalobre et al. for rating locomotion, positions, balance, limb coordination, and body-schema awareness.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Psychomotor profile framework for early childhood assessment**: The article organises preschool psychomotor assessment around a profile of dimensions drawn from the most frequently used assessment instruments. It states that "the following five areas or variables are the most frequently mentioned by the authors: locomotion, positions, balance, coordination, and knowledge of the body schema", operationalised in the Psychomotor Assessment Scale for Pre-schoolers as eight measurable dimensions (locomotion, positions, balance, leg, arm, and hand coordination, and body schema self- and other-awareness), each rated from low through normal to good. The framework is used longitudinally to track development and relate it to academic performance. (Borrego-Balsalobre F.J. et al. (2021))
