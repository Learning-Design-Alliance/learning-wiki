## Description
A Cambridge English assessment that measures learners’ English proficiency through standardized tasks and examiner ratings across vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation, discourse management, and interactive communication.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Cambridge Preliminary English Test (PET) speaking test as pre/post proficiency measure**: The study used "the Cambridge Preliminary English Test (PET) as a pre-post speaking test to measure freshmen university students' overall speaking proficiency". The test is conducted by two examiners with pairs of candidates in 10-12 minutes across four parts, and the interlocutor awards a global mark out of 25 on five criteria: Vocabulary, Grammar, Pronunciation, Discourse Management, and Interactive Communication. Recorded pre-test samples were rated by four raters, and inter-rater reliability was 0.85. (Mohammad Mohammadipour et al. (2015))
- **PET-based listening pre-test and post-test instrumentation for captioning studies**: The study used the listening section of the standardized Preliminary English Test (PET) as both pre-test and post-test, each consisting of 25 multiple-choice questions with a ceiling score of 25 released by Cambridge ESOL for intermediate learners. Learners listened to a conversation or monologue and answered questions. The PET reading and listening sections (60 items in total) were also used to select 45 homogeneous participants from an initial 58, excluding scores outside -1 SD to +1 SD. (Sorayya Behroozizad (2015))
