## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Persuasive Systems Design (PSD) model as a framework for analyzing Web-based health behavior change interventions**: The PSD model is a framework for designing and evaluating persuasive systems, conceptualized by Oinas-Kukkonen and Harjumaa. It builds on multiple theoretical constructs, such as goal-setting theory, elaboration likelihood model, and theory of reasoned action/planned behavior. The model presents four categories of persuasive system principles: primary task support, dialogue support, system credibility, and social support. In this review, the PSD model was applied as the framework for identifying various persuasive techniques incorporated into Web-based substance use interventions, with the authors considering it "the most sophisticated persuasive design and evaluation method available" for this context. (Lehto Tuomas MSc (2011))
