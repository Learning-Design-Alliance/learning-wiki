# Steps for Life

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Steps for Life is a school-based personal and social skills curriculum for elementary pupils, delivered by educators with family-supported continuation activities at home.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Letters to the Family: parental involvement component of 'Steps for Life'**: One of five curriculum components, the Letters to the Family "inform parents of the taught skills and provide them with simple guidelines and indicative ways of dialoguing, thus supporting the continuation of the child's learning at home". Families received the letters during lessons that required them, containing "guidelines concerning the continuation of learning of the taught skills". This parental involvement is one of the programme's three distinguishing design features alongside question-based teaching and diffusion in formal and hidden curriculum. (Kourmousi et al. (2018))
