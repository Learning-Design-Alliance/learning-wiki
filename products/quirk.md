# Quirk

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Quirk is an interactive, browser-based quantum-circuit simulator that enables learners to design and simulate quantum circuits through a visual interface.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Quirk circuit simulator chosen over Qiskit Composer as the CT tool for the introductory quantum hackathon**: Quirk is a circuit-based CT tool for quantum program simulation, compared by the authors against Qiskit Composer using heuristics for novice programming systems. On engagement, learner-appropriate abstractions, human-centered syntax, and minimal viscosity, the authors "conclude that Quirk is far more appropriate for an introductory course on quantum computing", though it appears more threatening at first and offers no easy way to execute circuits on real hardware. All circuit simulations in the hackathon were performed within Quirk. (Fahimeh Bayeh et al. (2026))
