## Description
The Multi-strategy test (MST) is an open-response, scenario-based assessment instrument developed by Schuster et al. to measure students' intended application of metacognitive skills and cognitive strategies.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Multi-strategy test (MST) vignette instrument for intended strategy application**: The MST is a scenario-based test presenting "a fictitious learning situation in which students are asked to describe their intended approach to process the given learning task" in an open-response format. Two versions (MST_E for an experimentation task, MST_T for a text-highlighting task) are coded with a category system awarding up to 9 points for metacognitive skills and 9 for cognitive strategy steps. Double-coding a subsample of N = 60 (about 25% of the sample) yielded good inter-rater reliability with an average Cohen's of .91. (Schuster et al. (2020))
