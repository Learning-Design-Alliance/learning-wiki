# Self-designed trauma orthopedic teaching satisfaction scale with five domains scored out of 20 points each (total 100 points)

## Description
A self-designed 100-point questionnaire by Xue Gang et al. for measuring clinical medical interns' satisfaction with trauma orthopedic teaching across five domains.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Self-designed trauma orthopedic teaching satisfaction scale with five domains scored out of 20 points each (total 100)**: The article describes a self-designed trauma orthopedic teaching satisfaction scale used to assess teaching satisfaction. It includes "five domains: 1.teaching effectiveness, 2.learning interest, 3.teaching content, 4.self-value, 5.humanistic competence and teamwork", with each domain assigned 20 points for a total score of 100 points. A total score of ≥ 90 was defined as very satisfied, 60–89 as basically satisfied, and < 60 as dissatisfied. The questionnaire demonstrated good internal consistency (Cronbach's α = 0.91). (Xue Gang et al. (2026))
