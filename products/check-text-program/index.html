Check Text program¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), q1–q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A Visual Basic Script text-analysis tool developed by C.E. Holdich and colleagues to provide teacher-relevant statistics on vocabulary, syntax, sentence construction, and punctuation in children's story writing.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Story length correlates with National Curriculum attainment level in children's Key Stage 2 narrative writing, with roughly 300 words needed for Level 4 and 400 or more for Level 5 [+M]
  • The ratio of mean sentence length to commas was the most useful computer test for discriminating maturity level in children's writing [+M]
  • Counting the connective 'and' separately from other basic connectives highlights immature writing and identifies a writer's stage [+M]
  • HARRY-assisted stories received more 'good' CHECK TEXT grammar and style reports than control stories in the case studies [+W]
