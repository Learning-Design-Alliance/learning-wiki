# GED, HiSET, and TASC

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
GED Testing Service, ETS, and Data Recognition Corporation provide these standardized high school equivalency assessments, which states use to award secondary-school equivalency credentials to individuals who did not graduate from high school.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **New Jersey's three high school equivalency exam options (GED, HiSET, TASC)**: Since 2014 the New Jersey Department of Education has offered three high school equivalency exams for nongraduates seeking credentials: the GED, the High School Equivalency Test (HiSET), and the Test Assessing Secondary Completion (TASC). The study analyzed takers of these exams using records of students who were grade 8 public school students between 2008/09 and 2013/14 and attempted at least one exam between March 2014 and December 2018. The exams serve as alternative credential pathways, and the state sets and revises their passing thresholds. (Jacob Hartog et al. (2021))

- [Under the new thresholds, two-thirds of GED takers and more than half of HiSET and TASC takers passed on the first attempt, and three-quarters of all takers ever passed each exam](../claims/nj-new-threshold-passing-rates.md) [+W]
- [Under New Jersey's original passing thresholds, first-attempt passing was more likely on the HiSET and TASC than on the GED after controlling for grade 8 test scores](../claims/nj-original-thresholds-hiset-tasc-easier-first-attempt.md) [+W]
