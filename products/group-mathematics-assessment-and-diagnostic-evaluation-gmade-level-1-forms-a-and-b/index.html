Group Mathematics Assessment and Diagnostic Evaluation (GMADE) Level 1, Forms A and B¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A norm-referenced standardized mathematics assessment for first-grade learners, developed and administered to measure concepts and communication, operations and computation, process and applications, and total mathematics achievement.

Components¶

  • GMADE Level 1 standardized mathematics assessment as outcome measure: The Group Mathematics Assessment and Diagnostic Evaluation (GMADE) Level 1, Forms A and B, a norm-referenced test, served as the outcome measure. "Student mathematics performance was measured at preintervention and postintervention using the standard scores" of the GMADE, with subtests Concepts and Communication, Operations and Computation, and Process and Applications combined into a Total Score. Form A was used at pretest and Form B at posttest to control for recall; the same trained researcher administered the test at both sites. (Lore et al. (2016))

Claims¶

  • First-grade students whose parents received numeracy training and delivered a home numeracy intervention made significantly larger GMADE Total Test gains than the control group [+M]
