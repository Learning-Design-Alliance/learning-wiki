# New Mexico Community Schools Act grant program

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A New Mexico Public Education Department-administered grant programme that funds community-school coordinators, planning, implementation, and technical assistance for participating public schools.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **New Mexico community schools grant program and funding structure**: New Mexico's Community Schools Act (2013, updated 2019) established a state grant program; legislative funding began in 2019 with an initial $2 million and totals $36.9 million allocated through 2025. For 2024-25, "90 schools either received funding for implementation grants or were designated to participate in the Accredit Community Schools pilot program." The program funds full-time community school coordinators, planning, and implementation, supplemented by federal Full-Service Community Schools grants totaling $7.5 million and a $1.8 million congressionally directed technical assistance award. (Germain et al. (2025))
