## Description
Bernard Schutz's textbook presents gravity from Galileo through general relativity using mainly intuition and heuristics, with optional mathematical boxes for learners who want the formal derivations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Gravity from the Ground Up (Schutz 2003) as a pre-calculus pathway from Galileo to general relativity**: Gravity from the Ground Up is the author's textbook, the product of nearly 20 years of work on how far into general relativity one can go relying mainly on intuition and heuristics. It teaches gravity "starting with Galileo and going all the way to general relativity – using only pre-calculus mathematics", reserving most mathematics for optional "boxes" that students may attempt or simply accept. Its pedagogical aim was to link new concepts with ideas students already learned at school, and it reorders presentation so Newtonian gravity is treated from the start as a special case of Einsteinian gravity. (Schutz (2020))
