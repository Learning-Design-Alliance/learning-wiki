# Measurement and Evaluation Checklist Phase 1: Design the Solution

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
The M&E Checklist is a resource that guides users through an evidence-building process to design, refine, and test a solution in collaboration with stakeholders from a priority community. This document covers Phase 1, whose goal is to develop a solution based on a well-defined theory of change for how that solution is expected to improve outcomes for students or teachers in a priority community. It was produced by Mathematica for the Gates Foundation under a project on middle years math teaching and learning solutions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Users engage in evidence-building activities in collaboration with stakeholders from a priority community
#### Constraints
- The checklist is one of four documents, each covering one evidence-building phase

### Target Learners
- students in a priority community
- teachers

### Target Learning Goals
- improving student or teacher outcomes through well-designed solutions
