## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Basler Consensus Statement as the dominant curricular orientation framework for communication teaching in D-A-CH**: The Basler Consensus Statement is a position paper from the German Society for Medical Education (GMA) from 2008 defining learning objectives for communicative and social competencies in medical studies. In the survey it served as the dominant orientation instrument for instructional content design: "Cited 16 times, the Basler Consensus Statement7 was by far the most frequently indicated learning objectives catalogue" among the 28 of 40 programmes referring to a superordinate model or catalogue. The authors interpret its dominance as evidence of the success of the GMA committee's long-standing work. (Härtl Anja et al. (2015))
