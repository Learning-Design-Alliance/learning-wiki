## Components

- **Derive computer algebra system as an instruction tool for the derivative concept**: Derive is a computer algebra system — software that performs numeric and symbolic operations and draws graphics — used in this study as the instruction tool for the experiment group. The authors selected it because it is easy to use and has features such as drawing graphs, solving equations, and taking derivatives and integrals. Students worked individually with it to re-solve examples and check their answers. (Kaplan et al. (2015))
