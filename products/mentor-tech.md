## Description
Mentor Tech is a Texas Tech University programme that supports underrepresented students through matched faculty mentoring, workshops, community connections, and scholarships.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Mentor Tech mentoring and support program at Texas Tech University**: Mentor Tech is a Texas Tech program for underrepresented students pairing them with faculty mentors matched on "ethnicity, gender, hobbies, and career and academic interests," with required weekly contact and twice-monthly in-person meetings. It runs about 60 workshops a year on academics, career, and personal development, connects students to community groups, and awards $500–$1,000 scholarships. Started with 46 students in 2002, it now serves more than 1,000 students (45 percent black) with a waiting list. (Nichols et al. (2016))
