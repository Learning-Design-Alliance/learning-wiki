# prEPAred mobile application

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A mobile application made by precisionED Ltd. for trainees and supervisors to record, compare, discuss, and store workplace-based assessments of Entrustable Professional Activities.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **prEPAred mobile application for near real-time workplace-based assessment of EPAs**: The prEPAred app (precisionED Ltd, Wollerau, Switzerland) is a mobile application that lets trainees and supervisors record workplace-based assessments of EPAs in daily clinical routine. "The trainee initiates the process by selecting the EPA performed", after which both parties independently rate task complexity and the recommended level of supervision; comparing the two assessments "a feedback conversation is stimulated which is optionally guided by the app using structured feedback instructions". Each datapoint is stored in the trainee's ePortfolio. In this study it was used to record 3936 assessments across 28 Swiss anesthesia residency programs. (Tessmann Tobias et al. (2024))

### Claims
- [Frequent app-based workplace-based assessments of EPAs were feasible at scale in Swiss anesthesia residency programs, with 3936 assessments recorded by 306 supervisors for 295 trainees over five months](../claims/app-based-epa-wba-feasible-at-scale-swiss-anesthesia.md) [+M]
- [App-based EPA assessments stimulated a feedback process in 57% of assessments, with a learning goal documented in 96% of those](../claims/app-epa-assessments-stimulate-feedback-learning-goals.md) [+M]
