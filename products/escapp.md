# Escapp

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Escapp is a web-based educational escape-room platform developed for managing enrollment, multimedia content, puzzle verification, hints, progress, timing, leaderboards, grading, attendance, and interaction data.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Escapp web platform for conducting remote educational escape rooms**: Escapp is the web platform through which the remote database escape room was conducted, accessible "from home, from school, or from elsewhere". It handled student enrollment (in pairs or alone), content and multimedia resource management, verification of puzzle solutions, progress monitoring, hint management, showing remaining time and the leaderboard, and handling student grading and attendance. It also unobtrusively recorded interaction and performance data used as an evaluation instrument. (Enrique Barra et al. (2023))
