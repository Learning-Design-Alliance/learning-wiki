# Environmental awareness knowledge test developed for the study

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A 50-item multiple-choice achievement test developed by Gökhan Baş to measure Turkish seventh graders' environmental-awareness knowledge aligned with the elementary science and technology curriculum.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **20-question pre/post environmental questionnaire for evaluating the service-learning activities**: The project evaluation used a questionnaire "formado por 20 preguntas relacionadas con distintas cuestiones medioambientales implicadas en los ODS", such as which packaging is recycled in the yellow container or which renewable energies are most used, plus questions assessing possible changes in student habits. It had to be completed before and after the ApS activities by both university students, as executors of the ApS, and secondary students, as final recipients of the service. (A-BASE-DE-PROS: una implementaci\'on pr\'actica de los Objetivos de Desarrollo Sostenible )

### Claims
- [Seventh graders taught environmental topics with a Multiple Intelligences strategy gained higher environmental awareness knowledge scores than peers taught by traditional methods](../claims/mi-strategy-higher-environmental-awareness-knowledge.md) [+M]
- [Participation in the A-BASE-DE-PROS service-learning project increased students' knowledge of the SDGs, which before participation was around 50%](../claims/aps-sdg-knowledge-increase-abase-de-pros.md) [+M]
- [The final questionnaire indicated changes in student habits and behaviours producing small savings of water and electricity, and high motivation with the activities](../claims/habit-change-and-motivation-abase-de-pros.md) [+M]
