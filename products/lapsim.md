## Description
LapSim, made by Surgical Science Ltd, is a virtual-reality laparoscopic simulator that supports procedural practice and assessment through automated performance metrics, including metrics aligned with the GOALS scale.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **LapSim laparoscopic simulator with GOALS-aligned metrics and a structured SBT program (1-hour orientation plus 4-hour simulator session with individualized instructor feedback)**: The LapSim (Surgical Science Ltd, Gothenburg, Sweden) laparoscopic simulator provides automated performance metrics grouped according to the Global Operative Assessment of Laparoscopic Skills (GOALS) rating scale. In the studied program, all enrolled residents completed a designed training program, "which consisted of a 1-hour orientation and a 4-hour simulator training session", followed by individualized posttraining feedback from one of three qualified surgeons and repeat performance on the simulator. Trainees performed pattern cutting (juniors) or laparoscopic appendectomy (seniors) on the LapSim, with scores recorded before and after feedback. (Tee Yu-San et al. (2024))
- **LapSim virtual simulation training system for laparoscopic skills practice**: The LapSim (Surgical Science Company, Sweden) is the VR simulator used in the STALS course's practice and assessment phases. The article states it "supports realistic three-dimensional rendering and physical deformation simulation of various organs and soft tissues" and "features highly interactive capabilities and multichannel feedback, allowing real-time simulation of surgical procedures". It generates scores based on parameters such as operation time, tissue tearing, and path length, and provides a risk-free learning environment with real-time error correction in the course's second stage. (Liang Yao et al. (2024))
- **LapSim virtual reality laparoscopy simulator with proficiency-based training protocol**: The trial used three identical non-haptic LapSim virtual reality simulators (software version 2016.1) from Surgical Science connected to a common server, with 27-inch height-adjustable monitors. Training covered "four basic skills tasks (Cutting, Grasping, Lifting and grasping, and Fine dissection)" and a procedural salpingectomy module, all with proficiency criteria covering parameters such as instrument time, path length, tissue damage, bleeding, and energy damage, which had to be passed simultaneously. (Anishan V et al. (2024))
