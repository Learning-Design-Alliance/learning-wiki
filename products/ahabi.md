## Description
AHABI is a self-report questionnaire developed by Cobos Alvarado et al. to assess undergraduate engineering students’ research skills in processing, managing, and developing scientific information.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Three-factor structure of self-rated research skills: process, managing and developing scientific information**: The validated AHABI organizes research skills into three correlated factors. Factor 1, "Process scientific information", covers organizing, analysing, reflecting on, summarizing and critically discussing scientific information; factor 2, "Managing scientific information", covers searching and managing scientific sources; factor 3, "Develop scientific information", covers producing new outputs such as abstracts, essays, keywords and oral communication of results. The article states the factors "indicate relationship and dependence between them", supporting the questionnaire's validity. (Cobos Alvarado et al. (2016))
