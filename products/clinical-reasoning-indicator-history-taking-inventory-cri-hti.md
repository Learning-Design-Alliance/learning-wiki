# Clinical Reasoning Indicator – History Taking Inventory (CRI-HTI)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
CRI-HTI is a five-point, eight-criterion clinical reasoning assessment instrument developed to rate medical students' history-taking performance from transcripts, including focusing questions, creating context, and securing information.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CRI-HTI: Clinical Reasoning Indicator – History Taking Inventory**: A validated five-point Likert-scale instrument measuring the clinical reasoning skills of medical students during history taking. The score "consists of eight criteria assigning to three fields of competence: \"Focusing questions\", \"Creating context\" and \"Securing information\"", with items ranging from taking the lead in the conversation to summarizing. In the trial, two blinded human raters scored chat transcripts with excellent agreement, and the same criteria were embedded into the ChatGPT feedback prompt. (Brügge Emilia et al. (2024))
