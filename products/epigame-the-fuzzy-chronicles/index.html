EPIGAME (The Fuzzy Chronicles)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 5 claims (2 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A digital puzzle game developed by Martinez-Garza et al. for teaching Newtonian kinematics through trajectory-planning challenges across 32 levels, with student actions recorded for analysis.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Playing EPIGAME produced a large, statistically significant pre-to-post gain in conceptual understanding of force and motion in 7th graders [+M]
  • Solomon four-group pilot found significant pre-post gains from EPIGAME play with no testing effects and no pretest-by-treatment interaction [+W]
  • Improvement in conceptually-laden in-game challenges over gameplay is strongly moderated by prior physics knowledge [~W]
  • Affinity propagation clustering of gameplay attempts yielded an interpretable six-cluster solution (part.6) [~W]
  • Students favoring fast iterative solution strategies achieved lower learning gains than students with slow, elaborate, or balanced play [~W]
