## Description
CATME is an online software suite for instructor-managed student team formation and teamwork support, maintained by the CATME project.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CATME team-maker tool for instructor-managed student team formation**: CATME is "a system of online tools designed to help instructors manage student teams effectively"; its team-maker sorts students into teams from survey responses and instructor-selected criteria. In this study, students completed a survey on identities, GPA, schedules, and goals, and CATME settings were assigned to create groups dissimilar in race, gender, generation in college, and GPA while keeping schedules similar. It served as the intervention mechanism for strategic group assignment in the lab sections. (Navlekar Anisha S. et al. (2025))
