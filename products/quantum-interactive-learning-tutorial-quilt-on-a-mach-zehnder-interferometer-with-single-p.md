# Quantum Interactive Learning Tutorial (QuILT) on a Mach-Zehnder Interferometer with single photons

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (4 for) · 3 studies (2 causal, 1 qualitative), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A research-based guided quantum-mechanics tutorial developed by Singh and colleagues that combines thought experiments, background material, and an interactive computer simulation for learning single-photon Mach–Zehnder interferometry.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Investigate student difficulties via think-aloud interviews and build a guided tutorial that addresses them, with prediction-observation simulation support**: The tutorial design pattern is: first investigate the difficulties students have with the relevant concepts, then build a tutorial that accounts for them. The QuILT "uses a guided approach to learning and accounts for the common student difficulties" and was developed so that students "can manipulate the MZI setup to predict and observe what happens at the detectors for different setups." Iterative refinement with faculty and student administration ensured clarity before class evaluation. (Chandralekha Singh et al. (2014))
- **Research-based MZI tutorial with thought experiments and an interactive simulation**: The research-based Mach-Zehnder Interferometer tutorial is the instructional element preceding the transfer questions. The article states that it "uses thought experiments and an interactive simulation" and helps students learn wave-particle duality of single photons, interference of a single photon with itself, the probabilistic nature of quantum measurements, and collapse of a quantum state upon measurement. Students manipulate the MZI setup in the interactive simulation to predict and observe detector outcomes for various polarizer configurations, learning how polarizer orientations affect interference via WPI considerations. (Maries et al. (2015))
- **Mach-Zehnder interferometer single-photon QuILT with warm-up, tutorial, checkpoints, and computer simulation**: The Quantum Interactive Learning Tutorial (QuILT) on a Mach-Zehnder interferometer with single photons targets upper-level quantum mechanics students. It includes a warm-up and tutorial, uses guided peer-discussion questions with student-voice dialogues, and checkpoints that help students "check their predictions in different situations and reconcile the differences between their predictions and what actually happens." Students use a computer simulation to manipulate the MZI setup, predict and observe detector outcomes, and reconcile discrepancies for setups with additional detectors and polarizers. It was developed through a cyclical, iterative process based on research on student difficulties and iterated with four faculty members and two Ph.D. students. (Emily Marshman et al. (2016))
