HOPS (Homework, Organization, and Planning Skills)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A brief individual school-based intervention developed by Langberg and colleagues to teach middle school students with ADHD organization, homework-recording, and planning/time-management skills, with parent involvement and reward-based skill tracking.

Components¶

  • HOPS intervention: a 16-session brief school-based organizational skills intervention: HOPS (Homework, Organization, and Planning Skills) is a 16-session individual intervention delivered by school mental health providers during the school day, with each meeting lasting 20 minutes or less. It covers three skill areas: "school materials organization and management, homework recording, and planning/time-management," supported by a point system with gift-card rewards and two parent meetings. In this trial it was implemented with an average adherence of 85.44% across sessions and providers. (Langberg et al. (2017))

Claims¶

  • Both HOPS and CHIEF produce significantly greater improvements than waitlist on parent ratings of homework problems and organizational skills in adolescents with ADHD, with large effect sizes [+M]
  • HOPS produces moderate effect-size improvements on teacher-rated organized actions and materials management, whereas CHIEF does not differ from waitlist on teacher ratings [+M]
  • HOPS produces significantly greater improvements than CHIEF in parent- and teacher-rated use of organized actions, but not on measures of homework problems [+M]
