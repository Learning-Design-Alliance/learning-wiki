# UHTRUST: Utrecht Hamburg Trainee Responsibility for Unfamiliar Situation Test

## Description
UHTRUST is a multilingual authentic performance assessment developed collaboratively by Utrecht and Hamburg to assess near-graduate medical students' readiness for clinical practice and entrustment-relevant competence in unfamiliar situations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **UHTRUST: Utrecht Hamburg Trainee Responsibility for Unfamiliar Situation Test**: UHTRUST is an authentic performance assessment placing near-graduates "in the position of beginning residents on a very busy day", developed in Utrecht in collaboration with Hamburg and validated in a separate study following Kane's argument-based approach. It has three phases: seeing five standardized patients with unusual medical problems, a three-hour information-gathering phase with seven realistic distracting tasks and supervisor contact, and a reporting phase. Candidates are independently assessed by two physicians, a nurse and five standardized patients plus one SP simulating a relative, using facets of competence, EPA entrustment ratings, PPEFs and the CARE questionnaire. (Wijnen-Meijer M et al. (2015))
