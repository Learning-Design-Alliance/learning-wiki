K-DTVP-2 and VMI-3R assessment instruments for visual perception and visual-motor integration¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 5 claims (5 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The K-DTVP-2 and VMI-3R are standardized instruments used to measure visual perception and visual-motor integration across developmental and adult populations.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • K-DTVP-2 appears more strongly affected by age than VMI-3R, but is also more sensitive to gender and disease [+W]
  • Age, gender, and presence of diseases significantly affect K-DTVP-2 score, while only age significantly affects VMI-3R score [+W]
  • K-DTVP-2 and VMI-3R scores are strongly positively correlated (r = 0.855) [+W]
  • K-DTVP-2 visual perception scores decline significantly with age in Korean adults, dropping significantly from the 50s compared to the 20s [+W]
  • VMI-3R scores decline significantly with age in Korean adults, dropping significantly from the 50s compared to the 20s [+W]
