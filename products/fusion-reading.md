# Fusion Reading

## Description
Fusion Reading is a two-year supplemental reading curriculum developed for middle-school struggling readers and students with reading disabilities, combining explicit word-level, comprehension, motivation, and assessment instruction.

### Claims
- [Middle school students with reading disabilities taught Fusion Reading significantly outperformed matched comparison students on the overall GRADE reading measure after Year 1 (Hedges's g = 1.66)](../claims/fusion-reading-grade-overall-gain-1-66.md) [+M]
- [In a single-group pre-/posttest study of 266 students with IEPs, Fusion Reading produced statistically significant gains on the TOSCRF (d = 0.55) and a mean grade-level gain of 0.92 after about 4 months](../claims/fusion-toscrf-single-group-gains.md) [+M]
- [In a large RCT (n = 871), Fusion Reading produced a small significant effect on word reading efficiency (Glass Δ = 0.11) but no significant differences on reading comprehension measures](../claims/fusion-striving-readers-rct-small-effects.md) [~M]
