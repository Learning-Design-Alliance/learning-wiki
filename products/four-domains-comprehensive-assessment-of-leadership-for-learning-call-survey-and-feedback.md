# Four Domains Comprehensive Assessment of Leadership for Learning (CALL) survey and feedback system

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q1` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A leadership-team survey, diagnostic, and feedback-reporting system developed for schools and districts using the Four Domains framework to identify leadership-practice needs and guide improvement planning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CALL survey and feedback system links diagnostic results to guide sections**: The Four Domains Comprehensive Assessment of Leadership for Learning (CALL) survey and feedback system is a leadership-team diagnostic organized by the framework's domain, practice, and item numbers. The guide states it "was initially developed for schools and districts that are using the Four Domains Comprehensive Assessment of Leadership for Learning (CALL) survey and feedback system," with survey reports organized so teams can locate the guide sections most relevant to their improvement plan. Teams not using CALL can instead use the descriptive labels of each practice section. (The Center for School Turnaround et al. (2025))
