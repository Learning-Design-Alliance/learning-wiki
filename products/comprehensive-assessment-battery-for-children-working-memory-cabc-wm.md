## Description
The CABC-WM is a computer-based, pirate-themed battery of 13 individually administered working-memory tasks for children, developed by Gray Shelley et al.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Comprehensive Assessment Battery for Children - Working Memory (CABC-WM): 13 computer-based tasks in a pirate-themed game**: The Comprehensive Assessment Battery for Children - Working Memory is a computer-based battery of 13 experimental working memory tasks presented "in a computer-based, pirate-themed game that took six to seven 2-hour sessions to complete over a period of approximately two weeks." Tasks span central executive (n-back auditory and visual, number updating), short-term phonological (digit span, digit span running, nonword repetition), short-term visuospatial (location span, location span running, visual span, visual span running), and binding (phonological, visuospatial, cross-modal) domains. A guide pirate delivered instructions and demonstrations, and children earned virtual rocks and gold coins for motivation. Most task reliabilities were moderate to high. (Gray Shelley et al. (2016))
