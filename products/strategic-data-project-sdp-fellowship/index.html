Strategic Data Project (SDP) Fellowship¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 3 studies (2 qualitative, 1 design), q1–q2 · 0 of 3 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Fellowship programmes run by the Strategic Data Project and Education Pioneers that place and support fellows in education agencies to strengthen data-use capacity and practices.

Components¶

  • SDP and EP fellowship programs as the intervention context for agency data-use change: The Strategic Data Project (SDP) and Education Pioneers (EP) fellowship programs are the intervention whose agency-level effects the Year 2 report evaluates. The document provides "background on the SDP and EP fellowship programs and the study's purpose and methodology" in the main report, while the companion profiles document each agency's partnership experience, including fellows' projects and lessons learned during the partnership. (Kristin Hallgren (2014))
  • Four-section structure for documenting agency data-use partnerships: Each profile follows a recurring four-part structure: background and context for data use; working with SDP/EP fellows; data analysis and reporting; and challenges encountered and lessons learned. The document states that "Each profile contains the following information" and lists these sections, giving a reusable template for describing how an education agency builds data-use capacity through an embedded fellowship. An organizational chart in each profile displays the path from the fellow's position to agency leadership. (Kristin Hallgren (2014))

Claims¶

  • SDP and EP partnerships enabled education agencies to develop new data-use practices that agencies continue to refine [+W]
  • SDP and EP programs aim to enhance education agencies' data capacity through support, training, and staff placement [+W]
  • SDP and EP partner agencies began working with the programs during the study's first year (2012–2013) [+W]
