# Canvas/Terracotta: LMS-embedded tool for randomized experiments within regular instruction

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Terracotta "integrates with the Canvas learning management system (LMS) and makes it possible to embed controlled experiments in Canvas assignments." It automates informed-consent or assent assignments, randomizes consenting students to conditions, collects data automatically, and produces de-identified standardized csv exports including clickstream data and end-of-unit assessment scores. The article uses it to illustrate a within-subjects 2x2 experiment manipulating context and detail in weekly practice question sets.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Teachers and researchers create original experimental content and deploy it within regular instructional practice in the LMS; a cohort of math teachers from Terracotta-integrated schools must be recruited
#### Constraints
- Participants must opt in via consent or assent assignments; outcomes rely on common unmodified end-of-unit assessments imported from Canvas

### Target Learners
- secondary math students in Canvas-based courses

### Target Learning Goals
- mathematics learning from word problems varying in context and detail

# Terracotta: a SEERNet platform enabling randomized research within the Canvas LMS

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q1`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Terracotta is described as "a platform that enables research within a popular LMS". It "works with the common Learning Management System Canvas": an instructor can create multiple forms of the same assignment (e.g., retrieval practice vs. restudy), and "Terracotta handles the logistics of randomizing the assignment of these two forms to the population of students" along with consent, de-identification, and exporting results. The paper uses Terracotta as the vehicle for both a published study and the hypothetical district improvement scenario.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Instructors must use the Canvas learning management system to create multiple forms of assignments
#### Constraints
- Other research methods beyond what Terracotta supports would likely be needed for summative evaluations of combined improvements across a district

### Target Learners
- High school students
- college students

### Target Learning Goals
- Comparing alternative instructional approaches within authentic courses
