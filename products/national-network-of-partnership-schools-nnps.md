## Description
A Johns Hopkins University network that supports schools and districts in developing and sustaining family and community partnership programmes through institutes, workshops, professional-learning series, and facilitation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **NNPS professional development: institutes, workshops, and quarterly series**: NNPS builds implementation capacity through a professional development infrastructure: it "conducts institutes and workshops that build leaders' abilities to strengthen and sustain their partnership programs." Partners also get access to two quarterly professional development series designed to build a professional community around partnerships and to learn about partnership research and evidence-based family engagement best practices. NNPS facilitators work with partners to support the development and implementation of strong programs throughout the schools they are guiding. (Attendance — National Network of Partnership Schools (NNPS) (2001))
- **NNPS framework of six types of involvement with an action team approach**: The NNPS model is organized around "a framework of six types of involvement and an action team approach." Rather than treating family engagement as isolated events, the framework structures partnership work into distinct types of involvement that a school-based team plans, schedules, and evaluates over the school year. The framework is operationalized through the Action Team for Partnerships, which writes a One-Year Action Plan connecting engagement activities to school improvement goals, and through NNPS-provided coaching, training, and planning materials that support implementation. (Attendance — National Network of Partnership Schools (NNPS) (2001))

# National Network of Partnership Schools (NNPS) program model

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
NNPS, established at Johns Hopkins University in 1996 by Dr. Joyce Epstein, guides schools, districts, and states to implement research-based partnership programs. Members "receive coaching, training, and planning materials to help them implement the program," and since its founding NNPS has partnered with over 5,000 schools and districts to improve family engagement. The model combines a framework of six types of involvement with an action team approach, and helps each team assess the quality and progress of its partnership work at the end of each school year.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each participating school forms an Action Team for Partnerships (ATP), a standing committee or work group, composed of the principal, parents, teachers, others such as counselors, community members and alumni, and students in high schools.

# NNPS framework of six types of involvement with an action team approach

> **Theory** · [All theories](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
The NNPS model is organized around "a framework of six types of involvement and an action team approach." Rather than treating family engagement as isolated events, the framework structures partnership work into distinct types of involvement that a school-based team plans, schedules, and evaluates over the school year. The framework is operationalized through the Action Team for Partnerships, which writes a One-Year Action Plan connecting engagement activities to school improvement goals, and through NNPS-provided coaching, training, and planning materials that support implementation.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- An Action Team for Partnerships that meets monthly, shares leadership, and conducts activities engaging all families in their children's education.
#### Constraints
- The framework is implemented through NNPS membership, which provides the coaching, training, and planning materials described.

### Target Learners
- All families in a school community, elementary through high school

### Target Learning Objectives
- Family and community engagement connected to student success and welcoming-climate goals
