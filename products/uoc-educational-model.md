## Description
The Universitat Oberta de Catalunya's framework for organizing online higher education through collaborative relationships, learner autonomy, human and technological support, digital resources, and continuous evaluation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **UOC educational model integrating collaborative relationships, student autonomy, three-role human support, and digital resources**: The article describes the UOC's educational model as "based on the integration of four fundamental elements": a horizontal collaborative student–teacher relationship, promotion of student autonomy and self-regulation, human support instantiated in consultant, tutor and technological support roles, and resources and content with active use of digital technologies. It is supported by evaluative flexibility through continuous evaluation tests and an environment favouring interactivity and cooperation. (Rivera-Vargas et al. (2021))
