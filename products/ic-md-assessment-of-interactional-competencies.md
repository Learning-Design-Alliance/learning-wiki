## Description
A standardized, video-based assessment used in medical admissions to rate interactional and socio-communicative competencies through emotional-availability scales and trained-rater judgments.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Emotional Availability (EA) as the construct underlying the IC-MD assessment of interactional competencies**: The article grounds the IC-MD in the empirically validated concept of Emotional Availability (EA), which "stems from attachment theory and has already been extended to social communication in a clinical context." EA "comprises communicative skills that enable the development of a stable relationship characterized by trust, is empirically based, and can be easily assessed in realistic contexts." The assessment manual operationalizes EA through four main scales — sensitivity, structuring, non-intrusiveness, and non-hostility — each consisting of five subscales, which jointly form the IC-MD score. (Schütte C et al. (2025))
