## Description
The 2025 MAP Growth Norms are a released set of national achievement and growth norms for U.S. public school students, derived from "a comprehensive dataset from 30% of all US public schools,makingthemoneofthelargestnormingsamplesevercreated". The release includes student and school achievement norms and percentiles for Mathematics and Reading (K–12), Language Usage (grades 2–11), and Science (grades 2–10), plus growth norms tables (Appendices A–C), built from Survey with Goals adaptive test events meeting reliability and growth-event eligibility criteria.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Eligible test events must meet reliability standards, including event duration of at least 6 minutes, RIT scores within scale bounds, and standard error of measure between 1.0 and 5.5
#### Constraints
- MAP Spanish Reading test events are excluded because they are on a different measurement scale and have separate user norms

### Target Learners
- U.S. public school students in grades K–12, including students using assistive technologies

### Target Learning Goals
- Evaluating student and school achievement and growth; individualizing instruction and goal setting
