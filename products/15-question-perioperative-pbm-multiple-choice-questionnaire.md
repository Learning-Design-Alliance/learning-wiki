# 15-question perioperative PBM multiple-choice questionnaire

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 15-question multiple-choice questionnaire developed and peer-reviewed by the perioperative patient blood management group at Royal Brisbane and Women’s Hospital to assess postgraduate doctors’ knowledge of PBM principles.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **15-question perioperative PBM multiple-choice questionnaire developed from NBA and NSQHS guidelines**: A 15-question multiple-choice questionnaire "formulated by members of the perioperative PBM group at RBWH using information attained from NBA's PBM guidelines and NSQHS Chapter 7: Blood and blood products". It was peer-reviewed by department staff not involved in development to remove ambiguous or misleading questions, then administered pre-intervention and 5 weeks post-intervention. The article notes no validated PBM-specific assessment tool previously existed. (Wyssusek K et al. (2021))
