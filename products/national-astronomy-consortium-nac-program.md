## Description
An NRAO undergraduate research programme that recruits underrepresented minority students from HBCUs and Hispanic-serving Institutions to gain authentic radio-astronomy research experience and preparation for graduate study or STEM careers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **National Astronomy Consortium (NAC) program for underrepresented minority undergraduates**: The NAC is an NRAO undergraduate research program "designed to provide research opportunities to underrepresented minority (URM) students." Students are recruited from an established network of Historically Black Colleges and Universities and Hispanic-serving Institutions. The article states the ngVLA will open more opportunities for traditionally underrepresented students to participate in cutting-edge research, increasing their chances of attending graduate school or beginning careers in radio astronomy. (Lyndele von Schill et al. (2018))
