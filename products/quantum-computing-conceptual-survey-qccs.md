# Quantum Computing Conceptual Survey (QCCS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 20-item closed-form conceptual assessment developed by Josephine C. Meyer et al. for measuring introductory quantum computing fundamentals across diverse course contexts.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Three-facet construct definition for quantum computing conceptual understanding: representations, properties, and evolution**: The QCCS operationalizes its construct as 'the ability to reason about the representations, properties, and evolution of single- and multi-qubit systems.' The three facets are 'conceptually interdependent': representations supply the formal language, evolution describes how properties change, and many items require their intersection, such as reading a circuit diagram to judge whether a gate produces entanglement. The authors present this as a preliminary operationalization, not a complete theory of knowledge in the domain. (Josephine C. Meyer et al. (2026))
