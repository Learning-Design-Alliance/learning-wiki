# TwinLife dataset

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A genetically informative longitudinal dataset of German twins and siblings, including harmonized academic-achievement information derived from photographed school reports, produced and distributed by the TwinLife research project.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **TwinLife dataset of German twins and siblings with photographed school reports**: TwinLife is a genetically informative, longitudinal study about the development of social inequality in Germany; its first wave supplied the twin and sibling grade data for this study. The article states that "The full TwinLife dataset and materials can be downloaded by the scientific community at http://dx.doi.org/10.4232/1.12665". Academic achievement was surveyed by photographing current school reports and encoding the information into a consistent, comparable format across school types and federal states. (Eifler Eike Friederike et al. (2019))
