## Description
Passport to Literacy is a semi-scripted small-group reading intervention programme for K–5 students reading below grade level, using explicit instruction in foundational reading, comprehension, and vocabulary skills.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Passport to Literacy lesson structure: paired Word Works and Read to Understand components with embedded assessment cycles**: Each Passport to Literacy lesson consists of two main components: "Word Works and Read to Understand." Word Works provides advanced word study (affixes, roots, multisyllabic word strategies), shrinking from 20 to 5 minutes after week six, while Read to Understand organizes before-, during-, and after-reading comprehension strategy instruction, expanding from 10 to 25 minutes. Lessons are organized into 12 ten-day adventures, with Day 5 mastery checks, Day 10 cumulative assessments, and biweekly oral reading fluency measures every 10th lesson to monitor progress and inform reteaching. (Jeanne Wanzek et al. (2016))

## Description
Passport to Literacy is a reading intervention for K-5 students reading below grade level. It "employs explicit instruction and strategies for reasoning in foundational reading skills such as decoding and word reading, as well as reading comprehension and vocabulary." "Semi-scripted lessons are used sequentially to help students gain missing foundational reading skills and build strategies for comprehending text." In the validating study it was delivered daily for 30 minutes in groups of 4-7 students throughout the school year.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Delivered by trained interventionists in small groups of 4-7 students
- Daily 30-minute sessions throughout the school year
#### Constraints
- Validated with fourth graders reading below grade level

### Target Learners
- K-5 students reading below grade level

### Target Learning Goals

- Vocabulary
