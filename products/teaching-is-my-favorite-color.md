## Description
Teaching is My Favorite Color (TiMFC) is "a teacher-designed and -led support and professional development community" for teachers of color across three southwestern Pennsylvania districts. It grew from one teacher of color in a school to "a support network of 50+ teachers across southwestern Pennsylvania." At its heart, TiMFC builds community and addresses the sense of isolation that teachers of color experience, providing a space to mentor each other, celebrate, share experiences openly, foster belonging, navigate bias, and discuss unspoken rules in schools.

# Teaching is My Favorite Color: a three-district regional support ecosystem for teachers of color

## Description
Teaching is My Favorite Color was developed by Avonworth, Hampton Township, and South Fayette Township school districts as "an initiative to create a holistic ecosystem of support for teachers of color across the Southwest Pittsburgh region." It had four vital elements: empowerment and amplification of voices, building a sense of belonging, professional development opportunities, and attracting and retaining diverse teachers. Activities included a social hour with a guest speaker and a convening with breakout sessions such as "Having an Affirming Classroom for Students of Color."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Combined resources and insights across districts; emphasis on support for new and early-career teachers of color
#### Constraints
- Designed for the greater Pittsburgh region where teachers of color are especially scarce

### Target Learners
- New and early-career teachers of color

### Learning Goals
- Belonging, professional growth, recruitment and retention of teachers of color
