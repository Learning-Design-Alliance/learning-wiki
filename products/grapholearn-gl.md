## Description
GraphoLearn is an adaptive digital reading game developed for young learners to practise phoneme discrimination, grapheme–phoneme correspondences, and early word decoding.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **GraphoLearn (GL) adaptive digital reading game**: GraphoLearn is a serious game for children aged 6–8 that trains phoneme discrimination and grapheme–phoneme correspondence. The player hears a speech sound and clicks the matching letter among alternatives, receiving immediate feedback; trials are short and fast-paced, and content is organized into three difficulty categories chosen by an adaptation mechanism based on previous responses. Engagement is supported by "a labyrinth-like fantasy world and an avatar", with rewards of virtual accessories for completing learning tasks. Difficulty adapts dynamically, with typical success rates of 80–90%. (Ronimus et al. (2019))
