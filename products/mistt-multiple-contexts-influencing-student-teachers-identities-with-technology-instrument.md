## Description
A semantic-differential survey instrument developed by Trevisan et al. to measure student-teachers’ perceived professional identities across personal, university, internship-school, and school-system contexts with and without digital tools.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MISTT instrument for exploring student-teachers' professional identities with and without digital tools**: The Multiple Contexts Influencing Student Teachers' Identities with Technology (MISTT) instrument is a survey tool designed to explore how student-teachers perceive their professional identities across four contexts — personal, university, internship school, and school system — with and without digital tools. It uses semantic differential items to capture these perceptions. The article introduces it as a new instrument for research on teacher identity and technology integration. (Trevisan et al. (2026))
