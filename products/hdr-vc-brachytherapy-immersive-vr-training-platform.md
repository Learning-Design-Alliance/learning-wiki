# HDR VC brachytherapy immersive VR training platform

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A Meta Quest 3 and edge-cloud-based virtual-reality platform developed by Ronghua Xu et al. for risk-free, repeatable procedural training in HDR vaginal cylinder brachytherapy.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **HDR VC brachytherapy immersive VR training platform with hands-free voice assistant**: A distributed edge-cloud system pairing a Meta Quest 3 VR frontend with a GPU-accelerated backend. The frontend renders "a high-fidelity digital twin of an HDR brachytherapy suite" with anatomically precise patient models, catheters, afterloaders, and applicators, and a three-tier voice interface (STT, TTS, diegetic virtual monitors) that removes controller dependency during procedures. The article positions it as risk-free, repeatable pre-training that complements physical HDR training. (Ronghua Xu et al. (2026))
- **Two-module VR simulator for HDR prostate brachytherapy built in Unreal Engine with augmented feedback features**: A virtual reality simulator with two modules, patient preparation and template guided needle insertion, built in Unreal Engine with assets modeled in Blender and run on a Meta Quest Pro head mounted display. In the needle module, "Catheters changed color on contact with anatomy: red in organs at risk (OARs) and green in prostate (tumor target)", and a visual steering guide operated as augmented feedback. Ultrasound displays were generated by virtual cameras and are view proxies rather than physics-based ultrasound. (Varlukhin et al. (2024))

### Claims
