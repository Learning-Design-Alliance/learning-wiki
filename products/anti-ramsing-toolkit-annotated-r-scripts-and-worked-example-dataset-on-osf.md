# Anti-RAMSing toolkit: annotated R scripts and worked example dataset on OSF

## Description
A publicly available toolkit by Bicakci et al. providing annotated R scripts and a worked dataset for structural mapping, bias-adjusted Bayesian meta-analysis, effect-size benchmarking, and moderator power analysis.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Anti-RAMSing toolkit: annotated R scripts and worked example dataset on OSF**: The authors developed "a comprehensive toolkit, implemented as fully annotated R scripts and accompanied by a worked example dataset, which is publicly available on the Open Science Framework." Its workflow is explicitly sequential and integrates four components: structural mapping, robust Bayesian meta-analysis and meta-regression (RoBMA-PSMA and RoBMA-Regression), effect-size benchmarking, and prospective power analysis. Once a domain-specific dataset is constructed with the shared coding framework, the toolkit can reproduce the core analytic procedures and extend them to other fields; the structural mapping stage itself depends on systematic qualitative coding and cannot be automated in R. (Bicakci et al. (2026))
