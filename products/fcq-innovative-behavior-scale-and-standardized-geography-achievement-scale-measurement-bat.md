# FCQ, Innovative Behavior Scale, and standardized geography achievement scale measurement battery

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A multi-section measurement battery combining an adapted Family Capital Questionnaire, a nine-item Innovative Behavior Scale, and a standardized geography achievement scale for high-school students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **FCQ, Innovative Behavior Scale, and standardized geography achievement scale used as the measurement battery**: The study used a four-section survey: demographics, a Family Capital Questionnaire adapted from the PISA 2018 Family Background Questionnaire covering parents' schooling, occupations and family assets, a nine-item Innovative Behavior Scale with idea generation, promotion and realization dimensions, and a standardized Academic Achievement in Geography Scale that maps six grade bands to points 1–6. Reliabilities were printed as "the Cronbach's alpha of the scale was 0.735" for FC, 0.901 for creativity, and 0.776 for achievement. (An Zhenni et al. (2023))
