Wayang Outpost / MathSpring multimedia mathematics tutoring system¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A multimedia intelligent tutoring system for middle- and high-school mathematics that provides worked examples, practice problems, adaptive difficulty, hints, affective learning companions, and teacher progress reporting.

Components¶

  • Wayang Outpost (MathSpring): intelligent tutoring system for middle-school mathematics combining cognitive apprenticeship, multimedia learning and multidimensional personalization: Wayang Outpost, later MathSpring, is an intelligent tutoring system for middle-school mathematics described in this special issue. It "combines principles of cognitive apprenticeship and multimedia learning to implement a range of coaching and scaffolding approaches helping students develop problem-solving skills." It adapts in both the outer loop (problem selection, informing students of their learning state) and the inner loop (instructional videos, elaborate feedback, step-by-step worked-out examples of similar problems). The system has been used in mathematics classrooms since 2003 and the accompanying paper presents extensive empirical evidence for its effectiveness. (Sergey Sosnovsky et al. (2014))

Claims¶

  • Students using Wayang Outpost earned higher state standardized test (MCAS) scores at year end than matched control students [+M]
  • In the 2012 study, classes using Wayang showed significantly larger MAP gains in patterns and algebra than control classes (d = 0.38) [+M]
