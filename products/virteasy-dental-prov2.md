# Virteasy Dental PRO/V2

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (1 causal, 1 design), `q2`–`q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
Virteasy Dental PRO/V2 is a commercial virtual-reality dental training simulator with haptic instrumentation, immersive or monitor-based display modes, and computerized performance assessment for dental skills training.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Virteasy Dental PRO/V2 VR haptic dental training simulator with force-feedback handpiece and immersive headset option**: The Virteasy Dental PRO/V2 is a high-fidelity dental training simulator combining VR headsets with haptic hardware: the haptic device "provides force feedback up to 7.9 N forces and with 6 degrees of freedom (DOF) positional sensing and 3 DOF force feedback". It supports fully immersive or semi-immersive (monitor) modes, a virtual magnification tool up to 20× approximating loupes or a microscope, a Polhemus Patriot tracker mimicking a dental mirror for indirect vision, and computerized assessment with qualitative feedback statements. In this article it hosted the case-specific endodontic microsurgery scenario. (Pasqualini et al. (2026))
- **VirTeaSy Dental VR haptic simulator with automated dexterity performance metrics**: The VirTeaSy Dental simulator (VirTeaSy Dental©, France) is a virtual reality haptic training system with an integrated touchscreen and 3D display showing teeth, endodontic instruments and dental tools. "Haptic tools, a dental mirror and a 3D mouse are positioned to enable an interactive and immersive simulation experience." After each performance it automatically records surgery time, drilling time, target progress, accuracy, target volume and outside volume, which the study used as manual dexterity metrics. (Usta Sıla Nur et al. (2025))
