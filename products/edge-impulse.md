## Description
Edge Impulse is a web-based TinyML development platform and associated educator toolkit, made by Edge Impulse, for building and deploying machine-learning applications on edge devices such as Arduino boards and Raspberry Pi systems.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Edge Impulse TinyML-enabled edge-device platform and Arduino education toolkit**: Edge Impulse is a development platform for machine learning on edge devices such as phones, tablets, and Arduino boards. The article states that "EdgeImpulse is working to take the complicated portions of a computer science (CS) idea and make it digestible to non-CS audiences through a simple online web interface". Edge Impulse designed an open-source TinyML-enabled Arduino education toolkit for engineering disciplines, and its educator program supplies hardware: "Educators can sign up for ten free kits for Edge Impulse's educator program to provide edge devices in engineering classrooms". The article's four case studies are implemented with this platform. (Andrew Schulz et al. (2023))
