# Seven-domain framework of health literacy competencies for health professionals

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A seven-domain framework developed by Mac Olivia et al. for organizing and measuring health-literacy competencies among practicing and trainee health professionals.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Validated set of 42 health literacy competencies (knowledge, attitudes, skills) plus 32 ranked practices for health professionals**: This study delivers a cross-culturally translated, Delphi-validated set of health literacy competencies for health professionals, organized as knowledge items (e.g., K2 "knows which kinds of words, phrases, or concepts may be jargon to patients"), attitude items (e.g., non-judgmental, empathic attitudes), and skill items (e.g., S13 teach-back, S21 analogies). The 42 retained items "collectively serve as a framework for guiding the development of curricula for enhancing health professionals' health literacy competencies," and the ranked 32 practices are translated into Traditional Chinese Mandarin. (Tsai Han-Yi et al. (2024))
