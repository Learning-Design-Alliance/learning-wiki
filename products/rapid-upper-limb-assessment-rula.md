## Description
RULA is a validated observational assessment scale developed by McAtamney and Corlett for scoring upper-limb posture and work-related musculoskeletal ergonomic risk, including in seated professional tasks.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **RULA (rapid upper limb assessment) score as an ergonomic-risk measure for robotic surgeons**: The rapid upper limb assessment scale is used in this study to estimate ergonomic risk and surgeon posture during robotic procedures. The article describes it as "a validated and dependable tool used to calculate a surgeon's work-related ergonomics during robotic procedures, where lower scores equate to better ergonomics." It primarily evaluates the upper body and suits seated individuals; scores of 1-2 are satisfactory, 3-4 low risk, 5-6 medium risk warranting early investigation and modification, and 7 high risk requiring immediate action. Joint angles measured in Kinovea are converted into the RULA score. (Elkhouly AG et al. (2025))
