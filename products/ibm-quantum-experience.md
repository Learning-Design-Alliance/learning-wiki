# IBM Quantum Experience

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
IBM Quantum Experience is IBM's online quantum-computing platform that lets learners and users design, execute, and analyze quantum circuits through graphical and Python-based interfaces.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **IBM Quantum Experience (Circuit Composer and Qiskit) as a remote instructional quantum laboratory**: The IBM Quantum Experience is a free online platform that lets students design and test real quantum circuits remotely. It offers two ways to build circuits: "the Circuit Composer, which is a graphical interface, and Qiskit, which is a Python package that can be used either locally or on the IBM website." Qubits are initialized to j0i by default, measurements occur only in the computational basis, and each experiment is repeated many times with outcomes counted as frequencies. The authors note the Circuit Composer is easy to learn but requires manual entry of each angle, while Qiskit for-loops eliminate that tedium. (Jed Brody et al. (2021))
