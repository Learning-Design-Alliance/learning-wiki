## Description
A reformed calculus-based introductory physics curriculum developed for teaching fundamental principles, microscopic models, coherence across physics domains, and computer modeling.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Matter & Interactions (M&I) curriculum for introductory electromagnetism**: The Matter & Interactions curriculum is a reformed calculus-based introductory physics curriculum that, per the article, "diﬀers from the traditional calculus-based curriculum in its emphasis on fundamental physical principles, microscopic models of matter, coherence in linking diﬀerent domains of physics, and computer modeling." For E&M it reorganizes the traditional topic sequence by increasing early emphasis on the vector field concept and on the effects of fields on matter at the microscopic level, introducing magnetic fields earlier and treating flux and Gauss's law later than traditional courses. The article evaluates it against traditional curricula using the BEMA across four universities. (Kohlmyer et al. (2009))

### Claims
