# Modified Mini-CEX scale with seven scoring items for health assessment teaching

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An unpublished seven-item, 9-point observational assessment scale adapted for evaluating undergraduate nursing students' health-assessment competence and providing teacher feedback.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Mini-CEX-based formative evaluation system with seven domains and a three-tier nine-point scale**: A standardized Mini-Clinical Evaluation Exercise formative assessment system encompassing "medical history collection, clinical examination, communication skills, humanistic care, diagnosis, therapy and overall clinical competence". Each 15–20 min assessment was immediately followed by form completion and a 15–20 min feedback session, using a three-tier nine-point Likert scale (1–3 below, 4–6 meets, 7–9 exceeds expectations) with a passing threshold of 6. Assessors completed standardized training with calibration sessions to minimize rater variability. (Zeng Jiahao et al. (2026))
