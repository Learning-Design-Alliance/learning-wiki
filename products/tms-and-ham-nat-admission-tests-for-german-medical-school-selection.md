# TMS and HAM-Nat admission tests for German medical school selection

## Description
The TMS and HAM-Nat are proctored German medical-school admission tests measuring cognitive aptitude and science knowledge for selection by participating medical faculties.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **HAM-Nat – Hamburg Assessment Test for Medicine, Natural Sciences Test**: The HAM-Nat is a subject-specific study aptitude test used in dental and medical admission at UKE. It is "a multiple-choice test at high school level with a focus on aspects of mathematics, physics, chemistry and biology relevant for medical studies". Piloted in 2006–2007 with freshmen, it entered dental selection in WiSe 2009/10, combined with GPA in the admission criterion, and applicants receive a subject list and practice versions for preparation. (Kothe Christian et al. (2013))
