## Description
SimConverse is a web-based AI simulated-patient platform made by Dymaxion Technologies Pty. Ltd. that supports scripted spoken clinical-history practice with automated rubrics, transcripts, and feedback summaries.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SimConverse AI simulated patient platform for surgical history-taking practice**: A commercial, web-based AISP platform (SimConverse, Dymaxion Technologies Pty. Ltd.) that "uses a model-agnostic approach", combining user speech-to-text, curated large language models and generative voice synthesis to create a simulated patient. Medical educators script the patient's history, laboratory results and personality, and the AISP is "constrained to stay within these designated settings". Built-in automated rubrics evaluate learner performance, and the platform outputs a full transcript and an AI-powered feedback summary. (Baraza W. et al. (2026))
- **Model-agnostic AISP architecture combining speech-to-text, curated LLMs and generative voice within educator-defined pedagogical constraints**: The article describes an explanatory architecture for AI simulated patients in which "SimConverse uses a model-agnostic approach and uses different AI models to create the simulated patient including user speech-to-text, curated large language models and realistic generative voice synthesis." Educator-authored pedagogical coding and case scripting define the patient's history, laboratory results and personality, and the AISP is constrained to stay within these designated settings during interactions. This framework lets educators design responsive platforms fulfilling specific curriculum requirements while allowing scenario complexity to increase as appropriate. (Baraza W. et al. (2026))
