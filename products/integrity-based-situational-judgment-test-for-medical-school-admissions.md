# Integrity-based situational judgment test for medical school admissions

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A situational-judgment test using written critical medical scenarios to assess non-cognitive abilities and personal qualities in applicants to medical school.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Four-phase procedure for developing SJT items for a medical admission test**: A four-phase development procedure for written SJT items used at Medical University Graz, drawing on lecturers/professors and advanced students. In phase 1, students with 4–6 semesters of study experience described critical medical situations as particularly appropriate or inappropriate, which an expert core team turned into test items using criteria including "the comprehensible context/the possible reference to basic statements of the bio-psycho-social model" and "no medical (pre)-knowledge is necessary for responding". Phases 2–4 cover professor evaluation, steering-committee ordering of answer options, and pre-testing with final revision. (Luschin-Ebengreuth M et al. (2015))
