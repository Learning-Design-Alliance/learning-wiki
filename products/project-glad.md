# Project GLAD

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Project GLAD is a professional-development and sheltered-instruction programme that trains and coaches teachers to implement its instructional strategies, typically through workshops, classroom demonstrations, planning, and follow-up support.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Project GLAD seven-day professional development sequence with demonstration and coaching**: The professional development element begins with "a two-day workshop, which provides an overview of the approach and an introduction to the instructional strategies." A month or two later, teachers attend a five-day demonstration in which a trainer teaches a compressed unit in a participating teacher's classroom each morning while other teachers observe and a second trainer explains the strategies; afternoons are spent planning teachers' own units with the trainers. Coaching amount is not standardized; study teachers received three days spread over the year. (Theresa Deussen et al. (2014))
