# LEARNBOT structured training program for robotic pancreatoduodenectomy

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
LEARNBOT is a pan-European multicenter surgical training programme run by E-MIPS to prepare experienced pancreatic surgeons for robotic pancreatoduodenectomy through simulator and biotissue practice, observation, video assessment, and proctoring.
