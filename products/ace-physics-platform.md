# ACE Physics platform

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
ACE Physics is an interactive web-based platform that delivers redesigned ACEQM quantum-mechanics tutorials with response-dependent guidance for undergraduate physics learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ACE Physics platform with dynamic guidance elements for out-of-class tutorials**: The ACE Physics website hosts interactive online versions of the ACEQM tutorials, redesigned so students work "on their computers, outside of class, without instructors present, and (possibly) alone". Tutorials intersperse "guidance elements based on student responses" — hints, follow-up questions, messages prompting students to compare disagreeing answers, and explicit correctness feedback — intended to model facilitator conversations, with guidance logic based only on multiple-choice and numerical responses. (Giaco Corsiglia et al. (2022))
