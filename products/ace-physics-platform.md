# ACE Physics platform

## Description
ACE Physics is an interactive web-based platform that delivers redesigned ACEQM quantum-mechanics tutorials with response-dependent guidance for undergraduate physics learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ACE Physics platform with dynamic guidance elements for out-of-class tutorials**: The ACE Physics website hosts interactive online versions of the ACEQM tutorials, redesigned so students work "on their computers, outside of class, without instructors present, and (possibly) alone". Tutorials intersperse "guidance elements based on student responses" — hints, follow-up questions, messages prompting students to compare disagreeing answers, and explicit correctness feedback — intended to model facilitator conversations, with guidance logic based only on multiple-choice and numerical responses. (Giaco Corsiglia et al. (2022))
- **AcePhysics: an online platform delivering pencil-and-paper QIS tutorials with computer-generated Socratic-style feedback**: AcePhysics is an online platform using computerized feedback to mimic the Socratic style of questioning students may receive from instructors or peers, allowing instructors to assign tutorials as a "plug-and-play" out-of-class task. Each tutorial is based on a pencil-and-paper tutorial developed by the group, adhering closely to its structure with adaptations for the virtual medium. The platform intentionally does not rely on AI, because available AI models could not replicate Socratic dialogue. Students receive real-time feedback on closed-form answers, and some problems offer optional hints. (Josephine C. Meyer et al. (2026))
