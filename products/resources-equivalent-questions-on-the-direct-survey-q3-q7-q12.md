## Description
A three-question diagnostic assessment subset from the DIRECT survey that uses contextually varied circuit-power questions mapped to shared cognitive resources for undergraduate physics learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Resources-equivalent questions on the DIRECT survey (Q3, Q7, Q12) with a choice-to-resource mapping**: The article identifies a set of resources-equivalent questions on the DIRECT test (Q3, Q7, Q12), defined as "a set of questions that should be answered with the same underlying physics principle but designed in different contexts". The three questions examine understanding of the power of direct current circuits, and their distracters relate to the same set of unproductive cognitive resources. Table 1 maps the four resources (S, R1, R2, Null) to the answer choices of each question. The DIRECT version used had 29 multiple-choice questions, each with 5 choices. (Tianlong Zu et al. (2017))
