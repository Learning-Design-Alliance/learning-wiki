# 2022 Taiwanese TCM licensing examination question bank

## Description
A 480-question Chinese Medicine Practitioner licensing-examination question bank released by Taiwan's Ministry of Examination, covering ten subjects across the examination's two stages for learners and AI benchmark users.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **2022 Taiwanese TCM licensing examination question bank used as a GPT-4 benchmark**: A publicly downloadable set of 480 multiple-choice questions from the first 2022 National Senior Professional and Technical Examinations for Chinese Medicine Practitioners, spanning 10 subjects across two stages, with 4 image-related questions excluded for analysis. Each domain contains 80 questions scored to 100, and "Only examinees obtaining average scores ≥60 pass the examination." The bank was characterized by cognitive level, depth of knowledge, question type, vignette style, and polarity. (Tseng Liang-Wei et al. (2025))

### Claims

- [The 2022 Taiwanese TCM licensing examination questions were predominantly HOTS (55.3%), SAMC format (92.2%), and negatively framed (62.2%)](../claims/tcm-2022-exam-question-characteristics-hots-samc-negative.md) [+M]
- [GPT-4 achieved 43.9% accuracy on the 2022 Taiwanese TCM licensing examination, below two human TCM resident doctors (70% and 78.4%) and below the 60% pass threshold](../claims/gpt4-43-9-accuracy-tcm-licensing-below-humans.md) [+W]
- [GPT-4 accuracy did not significantly correlate with most question characteristics, though TCM internal medicine, dermatology, and acupuncture showed significantly better performance](../claims/gpt4-accuracy-question-characteristics-no-significant-correlation.md) [+W]
- [Most GPT-4 errors on TCM examination questions stemmed from misunderstanding of TCM concepts (55.3%), not misreading questions](../claims/gpt4-tcm-errors-concept-misunderstanding-55-3.md) [+W]
- [Human review of GPT-4's explanations reduced overall accuracy from 43.9% to 40.3% and 39.1%, driven mainly by LOTS and recall questions](../claims/human-curation-lowered-gpt4-accuracy-lots-recall.md) [+W]
