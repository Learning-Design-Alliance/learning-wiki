## Description
Cog-Fun is an occupational-therapy intervention programme developed to support executive function, self-efficacy, self-directed learning, and everyday functioning in children with ADHD through child-centred goals and stop–plan–review strategies.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Cog-Fun intervention program for executive function and self-directed learning in children with ADHD**: Cog-Fun is an occupational therapy intervention, designed based on the Person-Environment-Occupation Model and the Model of Human Occupation, to enhance executive function and self-efficacy in children with ADHD. Its main properties include "setting child-centered goals, providing training in the three execution strategies of \"stop, plan, and review,\"" and aiding children to set their own strategies. In this study it ran as ten 60-minute sessions with parent consultation after each session. (Kim Mi Ji et al. (2020))
