# Caregiver-facilitated problem-solving based self-learning program (PBSP)

## Description
A five-module Chinese self-help and group-review curriculum developed for family carers of people with early psychosis, combining bibliotherapy, case scenarios, behavioural rehearsals, and trained peer facilitation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Caregiver-facilitated problem-solving based self-learning program (PBSP) for family carers of people with early psychosis**: The PBSP is a five-module Chinese self-help manual, adapted from an Australian bibliotherapy program, that carers work through over five months, combined with four 1.5-hour group review sessions facilitated by an experienced family carer trained in three full-day sessions. The article states "The PBSP consisted of five modules (M1–M5; see Table 1), concerning carers' self-care, seeking services/supports, psychosis care, managing illness impacts on patient and family." Modules teach the four-step ADAPT problem-solving method (Adopt, Define, Alternative, Predict, Try-out) through reading materials, case scenarios and behavioural rehearsals, at roughly 8–10 hours per module. (Chien Wai Tong et al. (2020))
