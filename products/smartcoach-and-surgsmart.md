## Description
SmartCoach and SurgSmart are AI-assisted surgical-coaching software systems that analyse laparoscopic simulation or procedural video to generate performance reports and feedback for surgeons and surgical trainees.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **AI-assisted coaching systems (SmartCoach and SurgSmart) providing automated feedback and surgical reports**: Among the AI technologies identified in the narrative synthesis, the review describes "integrated AI-assisted coaching systems (e.g., SmartCoach and SurgSmart)" that "provided automated feedback and surgical reports". SmartCoach uses an intelligent visualization system to generate a surgical report and pinpoint problems in the procedure, enabling debriefing and AI-enhanced feedback; SurgSmart analyzes laparoscopic cholecystectomy videos for surgical phases, CVS scores, disease severity, and critical actions to generate coaching reports. These systems appeared in randomized coaching trials included in the narrative synthesis. (Hanna Fouad et al (2026))
- **Digitization-plus-visualization framework: two prerequisites for applying AI to surgical education**: The article proposes that practical AI application in surgical education requires two things: sufficiently accurate algorithms that digitize surgery (recognizing surgical phases, disease severity, critical duct division actions, and CVS scores, which SurgSmart achieves at over 90% average accuracy), and a visual platform for coach-learner interaction. The authors state that "the successful development and implementation of the two key technologies, 'digitization' and 'visualization' of surgeries, have paved the way for the successful implementation of this AI-assisted SmartCoach" and that no comparable visualization platforms had been reported worldwide. (Wu Shangdi et al. (2024))
