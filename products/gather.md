## Description
Gather is a 2D virtual workspace platform made by Gather Presence that supports avatar-based synchronous interaction, spatial audio and video, embedded content, customizable environments, and breakout spaces.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Gather 2D Metaverse platform as the host for synchronous immersive flipped lessons**: Gather is a 2D Metaverse platform used to conduct the course's synchronous lessons in a gamified environment. The article states the course "was hosted on Gather" and describes features "such as avatars, spatial audio and video interactions, content embedding, and customizable environments," including breakout zones where all participants are visible but only those in the same private zone can communicate. It also served to share pre-class materials ahead of virtual meetings. (Alizadeh (2024))
