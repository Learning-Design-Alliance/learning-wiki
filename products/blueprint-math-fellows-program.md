# Blueprint Math Fellows program: daily in-school group math tutoring by AmeriCorps volunteers with a web-based learning platform

## Description
The Blueprint Math Fellows program is a middle school math tutoring intervention that "used AmeriCorps volunteers to provide middle school students with in-person and online group tutoring and leveraged a web-based math learning platform for supplemental instruction and practice". Tutoring took place "during the school day, five days per week in 45-minute sessions". The program was evaluated for math knowledge impacts and participant math perceptions as one of six reports on math tutoring programs.
