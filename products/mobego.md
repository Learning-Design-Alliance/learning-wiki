## Description
MoBeGo is a mobile self-monitoring app developed by Allison Bruhn and colleagues through an Institute of Education Sciences grant to support teacher–student behavior monitoring and data-based goal adaptation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MoBeGo self-monitoring app with automated data-based goal adaptation**: MoBeGo is a multicomponent mobile self-monitoring app with parallel teacher and student components in which both independently rate the student's behavior on a fixed interval schedule (e.g., every 5 min) on a 0–4 scale. The app calculates an individualized initial goal 10 percentage points above the baseline median, graphs teacher and student ratings, and its "data-based algorithm" notifies teachers and students when a goal should be increased or decreased. It was developed iteratively by the authors through an Institute of Education Sciences grant. (Allison Bruhn et al. (2022))
