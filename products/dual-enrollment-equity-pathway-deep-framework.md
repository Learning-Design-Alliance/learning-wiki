## Description
A Community College Research Center framework that organizes four equity-oriented practice areas for designing and supporting dual-enrollment programs, especially for underserved high school students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Dual Enrollment Equity Pathway (DEEP) framework of four equity-oriented practice areas**: The Dual Enrollment Equity Pathway (DEEP) framework, developed by the Community College Research Center, organizes what distinguishes equity-oriented dual enrollment programs from conventional ones. The brief states it "identifies four practice areas": "outreach to underserved students; alignment of dual enrollment coursework to postsecondary pathways; advising and exploration; and, most relevant here, high-quality instruction and academic support that responds quickly when students are struggling." The framework shifts focus from eligibility to supports in place, which the authors present as consistent with their findings. (Terrell et al. (2026))
