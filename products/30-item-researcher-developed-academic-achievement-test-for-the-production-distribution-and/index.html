30-item researcher-developed academic achievement test for the Production, Distribution and Consumption unit¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A KR-20-validated, researcher-developed 30-item achievement test measuring fifth-grade students' knowledge of the Production, Distribution and Consumption unit in social studies.

Components¶

  • 30-item researcher-developed academic achievement test (KR-20 = .83): A data collection tool built from a literature review of the 'Production, Distribution and Consumption' unit. A 40-question draft was reviewed by two social studies lecturers, one assessment-and-evaluation lecturer and two teachers; 4 questions were removed and 3 adjusted. After piloting with 213 students and item analysis, "30 items with a KR 20 reliability coefficient of .83 were included in the achievement test." Difficulty and discriminability indices were calculated for each item, and six unsuitable items were removed. (Fitnat Gürgil (2018))
