## Description
A 30-item, five-point Likert questionnaire adapted by Yun Heoncheol et al. from the eight-strategy motivational regulation scale to measure interest enhancement, goal orientation, behavioral reinforcement, and environmental control in medical students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Eight-strategy motivational regulation questionnaire (30 items, four constructs) adapted for medical students**: A survey instrument adapted from the eight-MRS questionnaire extended by Schwinger and colleagues, measuring motivational regulation in medical students. "The MRS questionnaire consists of 30 items with four constructs: interest enhancement, goal orientation, behavioral reinforcement, and environmental control." Each item uses a 5-point Likert scale from strongly disagree to strongly agree, with higher scores indicating more active MRS use. The article used it as the exogenous measure in the SEM. (Yun Heoncheol et al. (2021))
