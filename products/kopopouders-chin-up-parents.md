## Description
A Dutch eight-session facilitated online group parenting course for parents with mental illness, developed to strengthen parenting skills and children's protective factors.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **KopOpOuders: an anonymous facilitated 8-session online group parenting course for parents with mental illness**: KopOpOuders (Chin Up, Parents) is a preventative group course "delivered via the Internet to Dutch parents with psychiatric problems," aiming to promote children's well-being by strengthening children's protective factors via their parents. It consists of eight 90-minute weekly sessions in a secured chat room facilitated by one or two trained health promotion workers, with groups of at most six participants, structured home exercises between sessions, and a supporting public website with information, videos, a forum, and an email service. Participation is anonymous, which the authors consider important because participants may feel shame or fear losing custody. (van der Zanden Rianne AP et al. (2010))

### Claims
- [An 8-session online group course for parents with mental illness significantly improved parenting skills, with moderate effects on laxness (d = 0.52) and overreactivity (d = 0.48)](../claims/koppouders-improved-parenting-skills-pilot.md) [+M]
- [The online course significantly improved parental sense of competence, with a large effect on feelings of incompetence (d = 0.61) and a moderate effect on feelings of competence (d = 0.46)](../claims/koppouders-improved-parental-competence-pilot.md) [+M]
- [Course satisfaction was high (mean 7.8 on a 10-point scale), and 74% of parents considered the online intervention a better way to receive professional help than face-to-face](../claims/koppouders-high-course-satisfaction.md) [+M]
