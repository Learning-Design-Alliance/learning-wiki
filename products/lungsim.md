# lungsim

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Lungsim is a web-based interactive pulmonary physiology and ICU simulation program developed for medical students and pulmonary, critical-care, and anesthesiology trainees.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Interactive computer-based pulmonary gas exchange program (lungsim) with physiology and ICU simulator tabs**: A restored and enhanced web-based program built on the three-compartment lung model calculations of Riley, accepting inputs such as tidal volume, dead space, shunt fraction, and V/Q matching and outputting blood gases and oxygen delivery. New features include clickable variable definitions, pop-up alerts and red highlighting for out-of-range values, an equations tab, and mobile/desktop functionality. The program "can be accessed at https://hopkinstrials.org/pccm/lungsim.html" and includes an ICU tab simulating ventilated patients with asthma or ARDS. (Broderick B et al. (2025))
