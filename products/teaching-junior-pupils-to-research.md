# Teaching junior pupils to research

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A Sabirova et al. extracurricular programme for 7–11-year-old pupils that combines research-skills lessons, ICT-supported mini-research and creative projects, and seminars or defenses for monitoring and presenting research activity.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Extracurricular program 'Teaching junior pupils to research' with three subprograms**: During the experiment the authors developed and introduced into school practice a program of extracurricular activity of junior pupils called "Teaching junior pupils to research", intended for extracurricular work in elementary school and institutions of additional education. Its purpose is transformation of the intellectual-creative potentiality of children's personality through elaboration of research skills during self-growth. The program "included three relatively independent programs": lessons directed to formation of research skills; research practice using informational-communication technology as the leading, central subprogram with mini-researches and creative projects; and monitoring of learning and research activity through seminars, conferences and defenses of research works. (Sabirova et al. (2016))
