## Description
Quark Wars is a named outdoor particle-identification outreach game created by T. Csörgő and colleagues in which learners use particle cards and team play to practise elementary-particle terminology and identification.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Quark Wars outdoor particle-identification game**: Quark Wars is an adventure-style outdoor outreach game in which two opposing teams, each with an elected leader, wear particle war bonnets made of cards representing elementary particles. Teams win "by identifying all players of the opposing team by leading out loudly the combination of elementary particles on their particle war bonnets", or by identifying the opposing leader, who wears a Higgs boson or Omega baryon bonnet. Players may hide, run, rush, or band together in groups of 2-4, and identified players become silent observers. It was tested at a Hungarian summer camp. (T. Csörgő et al. (2016))
