## Description
A Programme for International Student Assessment 2018 dataset linking student-, school-, and country-level measures of intercultural education, teacher attitudes, and psychosocial outcomes across 58 countries, produced by the OECD.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PISA 2018 school-level measures of academic and cultural demands and resources**: The study operationalises school-level factors using PISA (2018) codebook indices. Academic demands comprise resource shortage (item code "SC017"), reflecting instruction hindered by staffing or resourcing issues, and hindered learning ("SC061"); the academic resource is student assistance ("SC052"). Cultural demands are immigrant socioeconomic disadvantage ("SC048Q03NA"), and cultural resources comprise cultural learning ("SC165"), shared cultural values ("SC166") and immigrant (remedial) language support ("SC150"). (Andrew J. Martin et al. (2025))
- **PISA 2018 secondary-analysis dataset and definition-driven operationalization of academic resilience**: The study uses the publicly available PISA 2018 dataset: "the sample consists of 606,191 students nested within 21,562 schools" from 79 countries. Resilience follows the definition-driven approach: "Students are classified as academically resilient if they are in the lowest quartile of SES in their countries but are able to achieve proficiency level 3 or above in all three PISA domains". Growth mindset was measured with one reverse-coded item; peer mindset is the school-level aggregate computed with a leave-one-out approach. (King et al. (2026))
