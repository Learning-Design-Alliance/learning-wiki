## Description
Maple is a computer algebra system developed and distributed by Maplesoft for symbolic and numeric mathematics, modeling, visualization, and interactive mathematical instruction.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Maple CAS as the chosen computer algebra system for physical-mathematical education**: The article selects Maple as the most appropriate system of computer mathematics for physical-mathematical education, citing cost, interface simplicity, and a programming language close to standard mathematical and imperative languages such as Pascal. It highlights "the excellent quality of 3D dynamical graphics" and "simple tools for creation of self-written libraries of procedures", and notes Maple versions 17-18 contain interactive 'Tutor' procedures in the 'Student' library displaying results in Maplet windows. Compared with MatLab and Mathematica, Maple is described as lower-cost with user-friendly interactive interface and interactive 3D and dynamic graphics. (Yurii Ignat'ev et al. (2014))
