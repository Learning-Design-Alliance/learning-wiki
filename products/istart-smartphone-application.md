# iSTART smartphone application

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A free iOS and Android application developed by informatics engineers and physicians to provide EUNACOM-style internal-medicine clinical vignettes with study-mode feedback and timed exam-training practice for Chilean medical interns.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **iSTART smartphone application delivering EUNACOM-style internal medicine clinical vignettes**: A free smartphone application for iOS and Android, devised by informatic engineers and physicians, delivering multiple-choice questions as brief clinical vignettes on internal medicine, built to resemble the Chilean national licensing exam. It had two modes: untimed "study mode" with instant feedback and brief explanations, and a "training mode" with a default 60-second time limit (user-adjustable to 30 or 90 s). The application required an internet connection to monitor adherence. (Martínez F et al. (2017))

### Claims
