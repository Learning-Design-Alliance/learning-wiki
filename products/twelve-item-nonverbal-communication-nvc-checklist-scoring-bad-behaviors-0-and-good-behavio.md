# Twelve-item nonverbal communication (NVC) checklist scoring bad behaviors 0 and good behaviors 1

## Description
A 12-item checklist developed by Park Kyung Hye and colleagues for trained raters to score nonverbal communication behaviors in recorded medical interviews and derive a summed NVC score.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Twelve-item NVC checklist scoring bad behaviors 0 and good behaviors 1**: The authors developed a nonverbal communication checklist by reviewing prior instruments and monitoring sample interview videos. It builds on "the checklist of Collins et al. [8], which consists of eight items" and adds two items from Ishikawa et al. plus two behaviors observed in sample videos, yielding "a checklist of twelve NVC items which can be categorized into two groups: bad and good behavior." Each item is scored 0 for bad and 1 for good, and the sum of the twelve items defines the NVC score. Two researchers used it to code students' recorded interviews. (Park Kyung Hye (2018))
