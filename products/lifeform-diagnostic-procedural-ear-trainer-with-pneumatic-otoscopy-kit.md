# Life/form® Diagnostic & Procedural Ear Trainer with Pneumatic Otoscopy Kit

## Description
A Life/form® mounted pediatric mannequin-ear task trainer developed at the University of Virginia for practicing and assessing pneumatic otoscopy technique, including speculum sealing and insufflation pressure.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Life/form Diagnostic & Procedural Ear Trainer with Pneumatic Otoscopy Kit task trainer**: The task trainer used in one intervention arm is the "Life/form® Diagnostic & Procedural Ear Trainer with Pneumatic Otoscopy Kit," developed at the University of Virginia. It "consists of a mounted pediatric mannequin head with a soft, detachable ear that has a simulated mobile tympanic membrane" and "can be configured to measure insufflation pressure on an attached manometer during pneumatic otoscopy." Training involved direct instruction followed by individual practice with goals of obtaining an adequate seal and using appropriate insufflation pressure. (Higgins Joyce Alanna et al. (2019))
