## Description
LumiNote is an instructor-facing Unity3D and Meta Quest 3 VR system developed by Danxuan Liang and colleagues that uses GPT-4o and Whisper to convert spoken teaching intent into reviewable spatial annotations, demonstrations, and language support for stage-lighting education.
