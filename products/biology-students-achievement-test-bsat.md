# Biology Students' Achievement Test (BSAT)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 60-item four-option multiple-choice biology achievement test developed by Sakiyo et al. for assessing Senior Secondary II students across six cognitive domains.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Biology Success Test (Pre-Test/Post-Test) instrument for measuring academic success in biology instruction.**: The researchers prepared a multiple-choice success test about biology for daily life, used as both pre-test and post-test to measure academic success. The test included three sections: fill in the blanks (10 items), matching (10 items), and short answer (10 items). Validity and reliability were checked with 45 students outside the main study sample. The test measured "the percentage of correct answers in the test and the percentage of the item known." (Sasikala et al. (2016))
