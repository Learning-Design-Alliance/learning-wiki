# Metacognitive Skills Inventory (MSI)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A 52-item six-point Likert self-report questionnaire adapted by Hong Wei Han et al. from Schraw and Dennison's Metacognitive Awareness Inventory to assess metacognitive knowledge and regulation in first-year medical students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Metacognitive Skills Inventory (MSI), a 52-item medical-context adaptation of the MAI**: The Metacognitive Skills Inventory is a 52-item six-point Likert-scale questionnaire adapted from Schraw and Dennison's Metacognitive Awareness Inventory for the medical context. The article states "There are a total of 52 six-point Likert-scale items in this inventory which were adapted according to the context of the medical field", covering knowledge of metacognition (declarative, procedural, conditional) and regulation (planning, monitoring, debugging, evaluation). It was administered in a 30-minute session and the authors suggest it may serve as a selection tool. (Hong Wei Han et al. (2015))
