# U.S. CMS PURSUE

## Description
U.S. CMS PURSUE is a paid research internship programme run by the U.S. CMS collaboration that provides MSI undergraduates with mentored particle-physics research, software training, lectures, and professional networking.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **U.S. CMS SPRINT/PURSUE undergraduate research internship program**: A 10-week in-person summer research internship for undergraduates from under-represented groups and minority-serving institutions, followed by a semester-long extension for a subset. The program "includes a 2-week cohort-based training experience hosted at Fermilab and remaining 8-weeks at one of the U.S. CMS institutions", with projects in physics analysis, software, computing or instrumentation on the CMS Experiment. In 2023, about 150 students applied, 20 were selected, and 6 continued into the semester; all interns earned credit in a new course (PHY 495) at Tougaloo College and received laptops with a common computing platform. (Santanu Banerjee et al. (2024))
