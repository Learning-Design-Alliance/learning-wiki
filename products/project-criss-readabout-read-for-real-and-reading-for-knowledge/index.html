Project CRISS®, ReadAbout, Read for Real, and Reading for Knowledge¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 7 claims (1 for, 6 against) · 4 studies (4 causal), q3 · 2 of 4 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A set of four named supplemental fifth-grade reading-comprehension curricula designed for use alongside schools' existing core instruction, evaluated by James-Burdumy et al. (2010).

Components¶

  • Four supplemental reading comprehension curricula evaluated in a school-randomized design: The element is the set of four fifth-grade supplemental reading comprehension curricula tested in the evaluation: Project CRISS, ReadAbout, Read for Real, and Reading for Knowledge. Schools were randomly assigned to use one of the four treatment curricula or to a control group, with teachers developing their own ways of fitting the assigned curriculum into daily schedules. The report states the curricula "were designed to supplement—not replace—the core curriculum being used by each teacher." (James-Burdumy et al. (2010))

Claims¶

  • Project CRISS, ReadAbout, and Read for Real showed no significant impacts on reading comprehension in the study's first year [-W]
  • Reading for Knowledge had a statistically significant negative impact on students' reading comprehension scores in the study's first year [-W]
  • A large randomized trial found no statistically significant effects of Project CRISS® on standardized grade 5 comprehension measures (James-Burdumy et al., 2009) [-W]
  • After one school year of implementation (first cohort), the curricula produced no statistically significant positive impacts, and four statistically significant negative impacts were observed. [-W]
  • The four supplemental reading comprehension curricula had no statistically significant impacts on students one year after the end of their implementation (Cohort 1 follow-up in sixth grade). [-W]
  • ReadAbout showed a positive, statistically significant impact (effect size 0.22) on social studies reading comprehension for second-cohort students taught by teachers with one prior year of experience with the curriculum. [+W]
  • Four supplemental reading comprehension curricula had no positive impact on fifth-grade students' test scores, with negative impacts in some cases [-W]
