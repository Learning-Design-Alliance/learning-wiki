# ePOCT+

## Description
ePOCT+ is a tablet-based, digital IMCI-informed clinical decision-support algorithm developed and implemented by the DYNAMIC project to guide outpatient pediatric assessment, diagnosis, testing, and management.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ePOCT+ digital IMCI-based clinical decision support algorithm for outpatient pediatric care**: ePOCT+ (electronic point of care tests plus) is a tablet-based digital clinical decision support algorithm implemented in 16 primary health centers in Rwanda as part of the DYNAMIC project. The article describes it as "a digital IMCI-based CDSA created to improve the quality of outpatient care for children under 15 years of age". Healthcare workers input anthropometric measures, vital signs, and symptom/sign presence following a logical consultation flow; the algorithm proposes diagnostic tests, then clinical diagnoses with management guidelines that can be accepted or rejected. All inputs are mandatory, with a limited 'not feasible' option, and it includes point-of-care devices such as pulse oximetry, hemoglobin and C-reactive protein measurement. (Karoui Haykel et al. (2025))
