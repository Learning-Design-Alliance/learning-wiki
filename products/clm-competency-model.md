## Description
A four-supra-competency framework for assessing and developing command, leadership and management effectiveness, developed by Young et al. for senior Royal Navy officer selection and development.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The CLM Competency Model of four supra-competencies (Conceptualise, Align, Interact, Create Success)**: The CLM Competency Model organizes command, leadership and management effectiveness into four supra-competencies: Conceptualise ("Get and share the picture of what needs to be achieved"), Align ("focus controllable activity"), Interact ("work with and through people") and Create Success ("habitually deliver success"). Each supra-competency is measured by clusters of OPQ personality and MQ motivation scales selected via Bartram's Great Eight Competencies mapping and reliability testing. The article validates the model against 481 senior RN officers' appraisal and promotion data. (M. Young et al. (2025))
