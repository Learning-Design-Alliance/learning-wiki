# Relativity Concept Inventory (RCI)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The RCI is a 24-question confidence-rated multiple-choice concept inventory developed by J. S. Aslanides and colleagues to assess undergraduate students' understanding of special-relativity concepts.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Relativity Concept Inventory: a 24-question confidence-rated concept inventory for special relativity**: The RCI is a concept inventory for special relativity developed because the literature lacked one. It tests nine concepts including time dilation, length contraction and relativity of simultaneity, using twenty-four multiple-choice questions each with a five-option confidence scale. The article states: "It consists of twenty-four multiple choice questions, with each having a conﬁdence scale." It was validated by expert feedback and benchmarked against homework and exam assessment, and is available from the Supplemental Appendix. (J. S. Aslanides et al. (2013))
