# Set of translated and adapted instruments (SRIS, SRQ-L-PJ, PCS, CS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A German medical-education assessment battery developed by Heckmann Markus B. et al. for measuring self-reflection, self-regulated learning, perceived competence, and clinical competence in final-year medical students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Set of translated and adapted instruments (SRIS, SRQ-L-PJ, PCS, CS) for assessing self-reflection, regulation, and competence in German final-year medical students**: A battery of German-translated and adapted questionnaires developed per the Artino et al. framework: the 20-item SRIS measuring self-reflection and insight, the SRQ-L-PJ for autonomous and controlled regulation, a 12-item perceived competence scale (PCS), and a 10-item competence scale (CS) based on NKLM 2.0 entrustable professional activities. The SRIS "showed a good internal reliability" (alpha 0.86–0.89), PCS reached alpha 0.91–0.92, and CS alpha 0.89–0.95, with EFA supporting the intended factor structures except for partial separation of SRQ-L-PJ subscales. (Heckmann Markus B. et al. (2025))
