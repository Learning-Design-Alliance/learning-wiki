Ciena Solutions Challenge¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Ciena's challenge-based learning initiative provides educators and students with global problem-solving projects, a resource library, facilitation guidance, a challenge builder, and related learning materials.

Components¶

  • Free CBL resource set: global competence resources and Ciena Solutions Challenge supports including a Facilitation Guide and Challenge Builder: The presentation catalogs free supports for implementing CBL: global competence resources such as "UN SDG Project Examples", "Topic-Specific Projects", "Student Global Competence Modules", and "Free Online Courses for Educators", plus Ciena Solutions Challenge resources including a "Resource Library", "Facilitation Guide", "Challenge Builder", and "Video Learning Series". (April Williamson (2024))
