# OPTIS (Observing Principles of Teaching In School)

## Description
A subject-independent classroom observation protocol developed by Mühlberg Theres et al. for trained observers to identify and rate applied teaching principles during school lessons.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **OPTIS observation protocol for identifying applied teaching principles during lessons**: OPTIS (Observing Principles of Teaching In School) is a subject-independent classroom observation protocol designed to help observers identify the applied teaching principles during class. It comprises 27 teaching principles with 39 observation categories organised into dimensions including cognitive activation, lesson design, contents and topics, classroom management, constructive support, and learning space. It uses social-form changes (individual work, partner work, group work, plenum, frontal teaching) to define observation intervals with a five-minute minimum. (Mühlberg Theres et al. (2026))
