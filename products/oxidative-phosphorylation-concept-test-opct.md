## Description
A 15-item multiple-choice diagnostic test developed by Jena (2015) to assess undergraduate students’ understanding of mitochondrial oxidative phosphorylation and identify related misconceptions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Oxidative Phosphorylation Concept Test (OPCT), a 15-item multiple-choice misconception test**: The OPCT is a fifteen-item multiple-choice misconception test on ATP synthesis and the chemiosmotic hypothesis, each item having "one correct answer and two powerful distracters" designed to diagnose misconception types. It was developed through a table of specifications, expert review, and primary, secondary, and final tryouts, with content validity ratio CVR = .68 and Cronbach Alpha reliability 0.76. The same test served as pretest (misconception test) and posttest for both groups, with a maximum of twenty minutes to respond. (Jena (2015))
