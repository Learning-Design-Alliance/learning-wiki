# CELP: a computer-assisted errorless learning-based memory training program

## Description
A touch-screen memory-training programme developed by Lee Grace et al. that combines errorless learning, spaced retrieval, vanishing cues, graded practice, and immediate feedback for older adults with early Alzheimer's disease.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CELP: a computer-assisted errorless learning-based memory training program for early Alzheimer's disease**: CELP is an individualized, touch-screen computer-delivered memory training program for Chinese older adults with early Alzheimer's disease, delivered by occupational therapists in 12 thirty-minute sessions over about six weeks. It combines errorless learning with spaced retrieval and vanishing cues, and its "content and structure" mirror the therapist-led version "except for the mode of delivery." Training uses culturally relevant daily-life content such as Dim Sum categorization and community-living scenarios, with immediate positive feedback and simple-to-complex gradation. (Lee Grace Y et al. (2013))
