Becoming Effective Learners Student Survey (BEL-S)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The BEL-S is a Rasch-based student self-report survey developed by Camille A. Farrington and colleagues to measure noncognitive factors and perceptions of classroom environments within specific courses.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Classroom environment has a small positive within-student relationship with course grades that is mediated by student noncognitive factors [+W]
  • Classroom environment is positively associated with students' self-reported motivation and other noncognitive factors within students [+W]
  • Student-by-classroom interaction accounts for a substantial share of variation in noncognitive factors, indicating context-specific malleability [+W]
  • Within-student differences in noncognitive factors are associated with differences in course grades across the same student's classes [+W]
