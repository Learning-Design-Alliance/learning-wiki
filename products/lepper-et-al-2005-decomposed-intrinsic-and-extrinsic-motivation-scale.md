# Lepper et al. (2005) Decomposed Intrinsic and Extrinsic Motivation Scale

## Description
A Likert-type student self-report scale developed by Lepper and colleagues that independently measures elementary and middle school students' intrinsic and extrinsic motivational orientations in classroom contexts.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Decomposed independent scales of intrinsic and extrinsic motivational orientation for elementary and middle school children**: A modification of Harter's (1980, 1981) forced-choice scale in which each of the original 18 items was decomposed into two separate items rated on 5-point Likert-type scales, yielding 17 intrinsic items (α = .90, one-factor) and 13 extrinsic items (α = .78, three factors: easy work, pleasing the teacher, dependence on the teacher). The authors state it "provides the first independent measure of extrinsic motivation for elementary and middle school-children". Test–retest reliability was r = .74 for both composites. (Lepper et al. (2005))
