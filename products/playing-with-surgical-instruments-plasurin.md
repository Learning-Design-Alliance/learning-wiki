# Playing with Surgical Instruments (PlaSurIn)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
An English computer-based serious game developed by Akbari and colleagues for operating-room novices to learn and assess the setup of basic surgical instruments.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The PlaSurIn serious game for training surgical instruments setup**: The 'Playing with Surgical Instruments (PlaSurIn)' game is an English computer-based serious game adapted from the Portuguese game 'Playing with Tweezers', developed for OR novices to set up basic surgical instruments on the Mayo stand or a back table at the commencement of minor general surgeries. It has a main menu with three modes: 'virtual mode' for viewing 35 instruments across six classifications, 'learning mode' for repeated practice with tips, and 'assessment mode' for a timed seven-minute setup with scores, remained time, and bonuses. Trainees must reach 70% correct instrument positions per attempt or repeat the game. (Akbari F et al. (2022))
