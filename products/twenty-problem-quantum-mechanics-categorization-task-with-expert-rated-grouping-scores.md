# Twenty-problem quantum-mechanics categorization task with expert-rated grouping scores

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A study-specific assessment developed by Shih-Yin Lin et al. in which students and faculty group quantum-mechanics problems by solution similarity, explain their groupings, and receive blind faculty ratings of categorization quality.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Categorization task as a probe of expertise-relevant knowledge organization**: The categorization task asks individuals to group physics problems based upon similarity of solution, revealing how their knowledge is organized and retrieved during problem solving. The article frames it as a window onto expertise: "Categorizing or grouping together problems based upon similarity of solution can give a glimpse of the “pattern” an individual sees in a problem while contemplating how to solve it", building on the classic Chi et al. study. In this article it is used with 20 quantum mechanics problems to compare faculty and student expertise. (Shih-Yin Lin et al. (2016))

### Claims
