Four-pillar competency rubric¶

Description¶

A rubric developed by Smith et al. for assessing manufacturing workforce readiness across digital and AI literacy, cyber-physical systems fluency, human-machine collaboration, and data-driven decision making.

Claims¶

  • The WRL rubric transferred unchanged across four industry sectors, with research- and industry-mentored capstones yielding broadly comparable pillar profiles [+W]
  • P2 pillar means rose from 1.3 to 2.7 between two successive unmatched teams on the same sponsor problem [+W]
  • The no-thin-pillar rule was diagnostically informative in three of four cases and the binding constraint in one [+W]
