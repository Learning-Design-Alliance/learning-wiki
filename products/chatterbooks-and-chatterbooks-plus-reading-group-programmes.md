## Description
Named extracurricular reading programmes run by The Reading Agency in which pupils read and discuss age-appropriate books in weekly small-group sessions, with Chatterbooks Plus incorporating the SPICE dialogic-reading sequence.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Chatterbooks and Chatterbooks Plus reading-group programmes with the SPICE dialogic reading sequence**: Chatterbooks is an extracurricular reading initiative by the Reading Agency aiming to "increase a child's motivation to read" through weekly small-group sessions where pupils read and discuss an age-appropriate book. Chatterbooks Plus replaces 15 minutes of each hour-long session with dialogic reading structured by the SPICE prompt sequence: Share a text, Prediction, Improve it, Cognitions, Emotions. In this trial both were delivered by trained graduates over nine weekly sessions to Year 7 pupils who had not reached a secure Level 4 in English. (Styles et al. (2014))

### Claims
- [Chatterbooks showed no statistically significant effect on reading ability among Year 7 pupils below Level 4, with a slightly negative point estimate](../claims/chatterbooks-no-impact-reading-ability.md) [+M]
- [Chatterbooks Plus showed no statistically significant effect on reading ability, with an effect size indistinguishable from zero](../claims/chatterbooks-plus-no-impact-reading-ability.md) [+M]
