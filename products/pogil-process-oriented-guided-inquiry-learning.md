# POGIL (Process Oriented Guided Inquiry Learning)

## Description
POGIL is a guided-inquiry instructional programme and activity design model developed and supported by The POGIL Project for collaborative content learning and process-skill development across disciplines.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **POGIL: a guided-inquiry pedagogy coupling process skills to content learning through a three-phase learning cycle**: POGIL is a pedagogy, rooted in chemistry but used across disciplines, that "provides opportunities for improving process skills during content learning through guided-inquiry activities". Its materials use a learning cycle of three phases: exploration of a model, concept invention, and application. Students take individual roles within small groups (manager, reporter, skeptic) to establish positive interdependence, and well-designed activities target one or two of seven process skills: communication, teamwork, management, information processing, critical thinking, problem solving, and self-assessment. (Walker L (2017))
