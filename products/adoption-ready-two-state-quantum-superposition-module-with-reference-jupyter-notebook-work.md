# Adoption-ready two-state quantum superposition module with reference Jupyter notebook, worksheet, and rubric

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A packaged 50-minute introductory quantum-mechanics lesson module developed by Boris Kiefer, combining a worksheet, concept checks, assessment rubric, and reference Jupyter notebook for teaching quantum superposition and measurement reasoning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Reference Jupyter simulator displaying the full Hamiltonian-to-sampling sequence in one interface**: A reference Jupyter notebook is the module's implementation component. Four sliders control the Hamiltonian parameters, and the output displays the Cartesian and spherical parameterizations, the Hamiltonian matrix, eigenvalues and eigenstates, computational-basis amplitudes and probabilities, and finite-shot sampling. The article states the interface is intentionally compact so the mathematical sequence from Hamiltonian to finite sampling remains visible during the activity, and the notebook is publicly available on GitHub. (Boris Kiefer (2026))
