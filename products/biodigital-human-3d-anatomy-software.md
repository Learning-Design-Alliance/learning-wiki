## Description
BioDigital Human is BioDigital's freeware interactive 3D anatomy visualization platform, offering manipulable anatomical models for education and self-study.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **BioDigital Human 3D anatomy software (version 1.0.4)**: BioDigital Human is the freeware 3D anatomy software used as one of the three learning modalities in the trial: "The software used was the Biodigital Human (version 1.0.4, www.biodigital.com), which was freeware and available in Windows Android and iOS." Students inspected structures using rotate, zoom in/out, dissect for removing structures, and isolate mode for segregating structures. The discussion highlights its isolation and 360° view of every anatomical structure, which was not feasible with dissection, as aiding spatial anatomy acquisition, and notes it can be freely used at home for self-study. (Sudha R et al. (2024))
