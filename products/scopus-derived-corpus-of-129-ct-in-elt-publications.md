# Scopus-derived corpus of 129 CT-in-ELT publications

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A Zenodo-released bibliographic dataset of 129 Scopus-indexed publications on critical thinking in English language teaching, with accompanying analysis code for bibliometric and thematic mapping.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Scopus-derived corpus of 129 CT-in-ELT publications analysed with VOSviewer, Biblioshiny and Microsoft Excel**: A bibliographic dataset of publications on critical thinking in English Language Teaching retrieved from the Scopus database and filtered through identification, screening and qualification stages to 129 eligible publications. As the article states, "Data was retrieved from the Scopus database, and 129 eligible publications were examined using VOSviewer, Biblioshiny (R 4.4.1), and Microsoft Excel." The corpus supports trend, country, journal, author, keyword and thematic analyses, and the extended data are released in a Zenodo repository with R source code on GitHub. (Sukmojati E et al. (2025))
