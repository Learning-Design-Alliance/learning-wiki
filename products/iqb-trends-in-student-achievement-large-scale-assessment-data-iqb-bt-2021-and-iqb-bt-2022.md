## Description
German large-scale educational assessment data produced by the Institute for Educational Quality Improvement (IQB), containing achievement scores and contextual questionnaires for representative student cohorts before and during the COVID-19 pandemic.

### Claims

- [In Grade 9, negative achievement trends emerged for non-SEN and SEN-L-R students in all domains, and SEN-L-R trends were always significantly more pronounced than for non-SEN peers](../claims/grade9-sen-l-r-steeper-negative-trends.md) [+W]
- [Grade 4 cohorts affected by COVID-19 restrictions showed significant negative trends in verbal achievement for non-SEN and SEN-L-R students in all three domains, and for SEN-L-S students only in orthography](../claims/grade4-negative-verbal-cohort-trends-sen-l.md) [+W]
- [During the pandemic, Grade 4 students with SEN-L experienced less favorable home learning environments and lower parental support than non-SEN students, while Grade 9 group differences were largely non-significant](../claims/grade4-home-learning-conditions-less-favorable-sen-l.md) [+W]
- [In Grade 9, fewer problems during distance learning were associated with better verbal achievement, summer school attendance was associated with lower reading and listening achievement, and learning-condition relations did not differ between student groups](../claims/grade9-distance-problems-summer-school-achievement.md) [+W]
