# 120-item schema-based cloze multiple choice item test (SBCMCIT)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A 120-item schema-based cloze multiple-choice assessment developed by Ebrahim Khodadady from the first four units of English for the Students of Medicine I to measure students' reading comprehension, vocabulary, and structure knowledge.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **120-item schema-based cloze multiple choice item test (SBCMCIT) developed on the course textbook**: A 120-item schema-based cloze multiple choice item test developed on the first four units of English for the Students of Medicine I. Its competitives were chosen from 360 of the 1892 semantic schemata parsed from the texts, giving it construct and content validity. Piloted on 16 general English students, it reached an Alpha reliability of .83 as pretest and .96 as posttest, and served as both pretest and posttest in the study. (Ebrahim Khodadady (2012))
