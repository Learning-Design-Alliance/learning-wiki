## Description
OSCRS is a VR-, AI-, and haptic-enabled dental clinical-reasoning training and assessment platform developed by Beijing Unidraw Virtual Reality Technology Institute Co., Ltd.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **OSCRS dental situational clinical thinking training system**: The Oral Scenario-Based Clinical Reasoning Training and Assessment System (OSCRS; Beijing Unidraw Virtual Reality Technology Institute Co., Ltd.) is a virtual simulation teaching assistance system employing VR, AI, and haptic feedback to simulate clinical scenarios with standardized patients having common oral pathologies such as pulpitis, periodontal disease, and dental defects. Its workflow comprises AI-driven voice interaction for history-taking with a hierarchical question database, haptic-enhanced oral examinations with force-feedback devices in a 3D oral cavity model, selection of evidence-based auxiliary tests with penalties for irrelevant choices, and diagnostic and treatment-planning modules. (Ba Ruikai et al. (2026))
