# UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A UNESCO global standard-setting framework that provides ethical principles and policy guidance for governments, educators, learners, and other stakeholders implementing and governing artificial intelligence.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence as a global standard instrument**: The Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence is UNESCO's first global standard-setting instrument on AI ethics, adopted in 2021 and applicable to all 194 UNESCO member states. The page describes it as grounded in "the protection of human rights and dignity", and notes it is available in many languages including English, French, Spanish, Chinese and Arabic. It functions as a norms-and-standards resource for governments, policy-makers, teachers and students. (UNESCO (2022))
