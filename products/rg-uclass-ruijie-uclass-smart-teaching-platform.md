# RG-UClass (Ruijie-UClass) smart teaching platform

## Description
RG-UClass is an interactive, multi-terminal teaching and learning platform developed by Ruijie that supports pre-class learning, in-class interaction, assessment, attendance, reporting, and teaching-data capture.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **RG-UClass smart teaching platform**: RG-UClass (Ruijie-UClass) is an interactive teaching system for university classroom instruction described as "a multi-terminal, full-process integrated tool" that establishes a closed-loop teaching workflow covering online pre-class learning and offline in-class interaction. Built-in functions include attendance tracking, real-time questioning, and case discussions, with automatic grading (objective-item accuracy over 98.7%), lesson reports, and whole-process teaching data capture supporting iterative teaching improvement. (Liu Sha et al. (2026))
