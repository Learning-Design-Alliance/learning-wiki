# Families and Schools Together (FAST) program

## Description
FAST is a multifamily group program aiming to "empower parents to improve their parenting skills, build positive relationships and social capital between families and schools." It has three stages: initial outreach, eight weekly multifamily group sessions of 10–12 families, and FASTWORKS monthly parent-led sessions for two years. Sessions include parent-led family activities (crafts, meals, singing, communication games), children's activity time with school/community staff, parent discussion groups, and 15 minutes of coached one-to-one "special play." Core components constitute 40 percent of implementation with structured local adaptations at 60 percent, intended to foster local buy-in and ownership.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Each FAST school creates its own trained team representing students' ethnic and linguistic backgrounds, including community professionals, school representatives, and parents
- Families completing at least six of eight weeks graduate in a ceremony hosted by the principal
#### Constraints
- FASTWORKS was not a focus of this evaluation
- None of the activities require parent literacy or mastery of English

### Target Learners
- Incoming kindergarten students and their families in persistently low-performing elementary schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Parenting skills
- Family-school relationships and social capital
- Children's well-being, engagement, and early academic learning
