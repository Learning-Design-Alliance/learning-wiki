# ALICE LEGO model v2.0

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A publicly documented educational LEGO model and construction-resource package developed around the ALICE particle-physics detector for hands-on outreach with learners from elementary school through high school.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ALICE LEGO model v2.0 with segmented instructions and 3D-CAD files for parallel building at different skill levels**: The v2.0 ALICE LEGO model has more than 18,000 parts and weighs 18 kg. Detailed instructions were developed for the full model and "splitintoconstructionsegmentsforparallelbuilding", complemented by information on the purpose of the various detector components and on the ALICE experiment in general. Instructions, the parts list and 3D-CAD files are publicly available. Because the segmented instructions "can be picked for diﬀerent skill levels", the model has been built with children from elementary school to high school. (Christian Klein-Bösing (2022))
