## Description
A behavioural marker assessment system developed by the University of Aberdeen for training and evaluating anaesthetists' non-technical skills through four categories, 15 elements, and calibrated observer ratings.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ANTStr: Turkish version of the Anaesthesiologists' Non-Technical Skills in Denmark Form**: ANTStr is the Turkish cross-cultural adaptation of the ANTSdk behavioural rating tool for non-technical skills, comprising 'four categories and 16 elements: situational awareness (four elements), decision-making (three elements), teamwork (four elements) and leadership (five elements)'. It uses a 5-point Likert scale plus a 7-point Global Rating Scale with space for verbal feedback, and is described as applicable to all healthcare team members in settings requiring teamwork. (Celik N et al. (2025))
