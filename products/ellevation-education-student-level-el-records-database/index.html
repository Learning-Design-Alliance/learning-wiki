Ellevation Education student-level EL records database¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A longitudinal database constructed with Ellevation Education containing individual English-learner proficiency, assessment, demographic, grade-level, and reclassification records from 2016–17 to 2023–24 across four states.

Components¶

  • Ellevation Education student-level EL records database (2016-17 to 2023-24, four states): The study's data source is a database constructed in partnership with Ellevation Education, "an EL-centered education technology platform that facilitates administrative record keeping, EL program management, and tracking of EL students' progress toward English language proficiency." It contains individual-level records from the 2016-17 to 2023-24 academic years covering ELP scores, state assessments, demographics, grade levels, and EL reclassification status, merged with state-level reclassification policy documents. States remain anonymous at the partners' request. (Camila Morales (2024))
