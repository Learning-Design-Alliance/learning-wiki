## Description
A Chicago Public Schools professional-development programme that recruits, prepares, and supports aspiring principals through intensified leadership knowledge, skills, and experience.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **LAUNCH principal development program (Leadership and Urban Network for Chicago)**: LAUNCH is "a professional development program created to accelerate, inten- sify, and deepen the knowledge, skills, and experience of principal candidates," designed to recruit, identify, prepare, and support aspiring principals in Chicago Public Schools. Begun in 1998, it had 160 completers by the time of the study, with 55 serving as principals. The report evaluates LAUNCH graduates' professional development activity, leadership ratings, and their schools' ITBS learning gains. (Penny Bender Sebring et al. (2004))
- **LAUNCH principal preparation program**: LAUNCH (Leadership and Urban Network for Chicago), begun in 1998, is a professional development program created to 'accelerate, intensify, and deepen the knowledge, skills, and experience of principal candidates' in Chicago Public Schools. It includes a four-week summer Leadership Academy at Northwestern University's Kellogg Graduate School of Management, a full-time yearlong internship with mentor principals covering both elementary and high school experience, monthly meetings with book studies and action research, and an ongoing Urban Network of retreats and workshops. CPS has invested $7,500,000 in LAUNCH since 1999. (Hart et al. (2006))
