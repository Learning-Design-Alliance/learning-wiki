# Self-Regulation of Learning Self-Report Scale (SRL-SRS), Brazilian-Portuguese translation

## Description
A 50-item questionnaire translated and psychometrically adapted by Lucieer Susanna M. et al. to assess planning, monitoring, evaluation, reflection, effort, and self-efficacy in self-regulated learning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Self-Regulation of Learning Self-Report Scale (SRL-SRS), translated to Brazilian-Portuguese**: The SRL-SRS is a 50-item self-report questionnaire measuring six self-regulated learning skills: "planning, monitoring, evaluation, reflection, effort, and self-efficacy", each on 4- or 5-point Likert scales. It was composed and validated in a Dutch study with adolescents, and for this study translated to Brazilian-Portuguese following Beaton et al. guidelines. In this sample the original six-factor model showed moderate fit (CFI .86) and an adjusted model showed good fit (CFI .902, CMIN/d.f. 1.8, RMSEA .045) with strong internal consistency across all four groups. (Lucieer Susanna M. et al. (2016))
