# Improving Chicago's Schools biannual survey and individualized confidential school reports

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
The Consortium on Chicago School Research's biannual survey collected responses from 'more than 132,000 students, teachers, and principals across the Chicago public school system' in spring 2005. The Consortium prepares individualized, confidential reports for every school meeting response-rate criteria; in 2005, 479 elementary and high schools qualified, and reports show how measures have changed over the ten years schools have participated. Reports are provided at the request of the principal to assist self-assessment and long-term planning.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- At least 42 percent of teachers or 50 percent of students must respond in order for a school to receive a report
#### Constraints
- The report is confidential and will not be distributed outside the school without the written permission of the principal; if only one group responds, only that group's measures are reported

### Target Learners
- Chicago Public Schools students, teachers, and principals

### Target Learning Goals
- school improvement planning
- self-assessment of learning climate and instructional quality
