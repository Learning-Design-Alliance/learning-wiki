# KVEC stack of over 18 free competency-based micro-credentials hosted on the Digital Promise Micro-credential Platform

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)

## Description
The Kentucky Valley Educational Cooperative, through a grant partnership with Digital Promise, created "a stack of over 18 micro-credentials focused primarily on the needs of students experiencing poverty, as well as special education populations." The credentials are free, competency-based professional learning opportunities for rural K-12 educators, hosted on the Digital Promise Micro-credential Platform and available to the public. Teachers leverage them to personalize continuing education credits and increase their rank, and schools adopt them whole-faculty or via team leads based on school data and student demographics.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- State acceptance of micro-credentials for educator rank advancement, which in Kentucky came through a regulation change facilitated by a partnership with the State Board of Education
#### Constraints
- Kentucky requires four days of seat time for professional development, making it difficult to equate micro-credentials focused on demonstrating content knowledge rather than time spent

### Target Learners
- Rural K-12 educators in southeastern Appalachian Kentucky

### Learning Goals
- Understanding the needs of students experiencing poverty
- Special education populations including hearing and visual impairment
- Competency-based continuing education credits
