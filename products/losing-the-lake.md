## Description
Losing the Lake is a web-based educational simulation game developed by E. Michael Nussbaum and colleagues for teaching middle school students and informal learners about Lake Mead, water conservation, and climate change.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Losing the Lake simulation game with five modules on Lake Mead, water conservation, and climate change**: Losing the Lake is a computer simulation/game exploring climate change effects on declining water levels in Lake Mead, designed with "five modules/activities to help break down the complex topic of climate change." Activities include background-knowledge multiple-choice questions with feedback, household and community conservation option-selection tasks with points, a climate-change tutorial with mini-simulations, and a lake-level prediction simulation based on Hadley Center 2-A1B climate model data. Playing takes approximately 20 minutes, and the game and a facilitator's manual are available on the web. (E. Michael Nussbaum et al. (2015))
