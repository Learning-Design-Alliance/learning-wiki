## Description
A one-day trauma-inquiry and response training programme developed by Read and colleagues for health care professionals, adaptable across health care settings and supplemented by a refresher session.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Learning How to Ask one-day training on trauma inquiry and response**: The 'Learning How to Ask' training is a one-day professional training developed by Read and colleagues on the inquiry of traumatic events and adequate response strategies, adaptable to different health care settings. It "includes eight 50-min units" covering trauma types and prevalence, effects on mental health, PTSD symptoms, barriers to inquiry, asking and responding rules, documentation, and community resources. Basic rules are "discussed and practiced in role-plays", and participants reflect on their current routine practice and barriers. A 1.5-hour refresher was conducted 3 months later, delivered by an experienced psychiatrist and/or graduated psychologist in groups of 5 to 16 professionals. (Lotzin Annett et al. (2019))
