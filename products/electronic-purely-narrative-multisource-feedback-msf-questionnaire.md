# Electronic purely narrative multisource feedback (MSF) questionnaire

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A SurveyXact-based narrative multisource-feedback assessment developed by representatives of the national scientific societies of internal-medicine specialties for postgraduate trainees, with supervisor-mediated feedback conversations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Electronic purely narrative MSF instrument for postgraduate internal medicine training**: An electronic, purely narrative multisource feedback questionnaire developed by representatives of the national scientific societies of the internal medicine specialties, transferred into SurveyXact. It contained "two open-ended questions within each domain" of communication, collaboration, management and professionalism, plus a request for advice on further improvement. Trainees chose their own respondents (advised approximately eight); a standard computer-generated summary report without interpretations was mailed to the supervisor, who arranged a feedback conversation. (Holm Ellen Astrid et al. (2021))
