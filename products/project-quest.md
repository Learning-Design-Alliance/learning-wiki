## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Project QUEST comprehensive support services program for occupational training completion**: Project QUEST is a community-based organization, started in 1992 in San Antonio, Texas, that "provides comprehensive support services to help participants complete occupational training programs at local community colleges and professional training institutes, pass certification exams, and obtain well-paying jobs in targeted sectors of the local economy." It targets adults 18 or older with a high school diploma or equivalent, at minimum 8th grade reading and 6th grade math levels on the TABE, available for full-time enrollment. Adaptations operate under other names, including VIDA, Capital IDEA, Project ARRIBA, and JobPath. ## Description
Project QUEST is a community-based organization, started in 1992 in San Antonio, Texas, that "provides comprehensive support services to help participants complete occupational training programs at local community colleges and professional training institutes, pass certification exams, and obtain well-paying jobs in targeted sectors of the local economy." It targets adults 18 or older with a high school diploma or equivalent, at minimum 8th grade reading and 6th grade math levels on the TABE, available for full-time enrollment. Adaptations operate under other names, including VIDA, Capital IDEA, Project ARRIBA, and JobPath.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Participants must be available to enroll full-time in an occupational training program and demonstrate minimum TABE reading and math levels
#### Constraints
- The effects of Project QUEST are not known for other outcomes within the topic area protocol, including technical skill proficiency and long-term employment

### Target Learners
- unemployed or underemployed adults meeting federal poverty guidelines or on public assistance who are interested in but not currently enrolled in college

### Target Learning Goals
- complete occupational training programs and pass certification exams
- obtain well-paying jobs in targeted sectors of the local economy
