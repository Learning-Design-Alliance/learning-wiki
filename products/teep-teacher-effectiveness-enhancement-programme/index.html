TEEP (Teacher Effectiveness Enhancement Programme)¶

Description¶

TEEP is a whole-school professional-development programme run by the Institute for Effective Education to improve secondary teachers' classroom practice and students' learning through a three-tier pedagogical framework.

Components¶

  • The TEEP three-tier pedagogical framework model: The TEEP model is a pedagogical framework represented graphically and "broken down into three tiers": a six-phase learning cycle (preparing for learning, agreeing learning outcomes, presenting new information, constructing meaning, demonstrating understanding, and reviewing); five underpinning elements (assessment for learning, thinking for learning, accelerated learning, collaborative learning, and effective use of ICT); and effective teacher and learner behaviours (interactive teaching, classroom management, variety of teaching approaches, and classroom climate). The whole-school training programme is structured around these tiers across five days, and the framework is described as providing "a comprehensive language and framework for understanding how to improve the effectiveness of both teachers and learners in secondary schools". (Institute for Effective Education (2016))
