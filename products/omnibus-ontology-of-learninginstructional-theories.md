# OMNIBUS ontology of learning/instructional theories

## Description
OMNIBUS is an ontology-based framework developed by Mizoguchi and colleagues for representing instructional theories as alternative decompositions of learning goals into sequenced sub-goals for instructional-system and scenario design.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **OMNIBUS: an ontology of learning/instructional theories as ways of goal decomposition**: OMNIBUS is an ontology of learning/instructional theories built with the Hozo editor, capturing theories "as a set of ways of decomposing a goal into a sequence of sub-goals". The article reports that "OMNIBUS has 99 ways of goal decomposition extracted from 11 theories/models", for example decomposing 'to help learners learn' differently under behaviorist versus constructivist approaches. It is described as the framework able to include any theory compliant with its specifications of theory and model. (Mizoguchi et al. (2015))
- **OMNIBUS ontology of learning, instruction and instructional design and the SMARTIES authoring system**: OMNIBUS is an ontology of learning, instruction and instructional design built over years by Riichiro Mizoguchi, Jacqueline Bourdeau and their students and associates. On top of it sits SMARTIES, described by the article as "the world-first authoring system that helps authors develop theory-compliant learning scenarios". The commentary cites both as among the best-known research efforts in educational ontologies. (Devedzic (2016))
