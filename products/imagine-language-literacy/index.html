Imagine Language & Literacy¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

Imagine Language & Literacy is Imagine Learning’s web-based adaptive supplemental literacy program for students from pre-kindergarten through sixth grade.

Components¶

  • Imagine Language & Literacy adaptive supplemental literacy program: Imagine Language & Literacy is an adaptive supplemental program for students in pre-kindergarten through sixth grade that "provides individualized instruction with exercises tailored to each user's needs, accelerating instruction levels based on the user's rate of progress". It targets vocabulary, comprehension, fluency, phonics and phonemic awareness, and incorporates art, music, sports and multi-disciplinary subject matter. It is web-based and compatible with PC, MAC, iPads, Kindle, and Nook. (Imagine Language et al. (2023))
