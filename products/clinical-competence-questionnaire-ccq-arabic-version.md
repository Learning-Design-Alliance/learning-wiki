# Clinical Competence Questionnaire (CCQ), Arabic version

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A 46-item Arabic self-report questionnaire developed by Mohamed Rasha A. et al. to measure registered nurses' perceived clinical competence across professional behaviors, general performance, core nursing skills, and advanced nursing skills.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Clinical Competence Questionnaire (CCQ), Arabic version, for self-assessing nurses' clinical competence**: The CCQ is a 46-item self-assessment instrument measuring perceived nurses' clinical competence across four domains: professional behaviors (16 competencies), general performance (12), core nursing skills (12), and advanced nursing skills (6), scored on a five-point Likert scale from incapacity to perform to theoretical concept fluency and practical performance under supervision, with total scores from 46 to 230. The study used a translated Arabic version whose "test-retest reliability of the Arabic version in this study was 0.910". Scores were categorized using modified Bloom's cutoff points (competent enough at 80–100%). (Mohamed Rasha A. et al. (2024))
- **Clinical Competency Questionnaire (CCQ) self-assessment instrument**: The CCQ is a self-report instrument measuring perceived clinical competency in baccalaureate nursing students across two categories: nursing professional behaviors and skill competencies. "Selected for high internal consistency and reliability, the CCQs used a 5-point Likert scale to measure self-reports of clinical competency from baccalaureate nursing students." The CCQ authors granted permission for modification to fit the study population, and it was administered in both pretest and posttest phases, taking approximately 10 minutes to complete. (Kiernan Llynne C. DNP et al. (2018))
