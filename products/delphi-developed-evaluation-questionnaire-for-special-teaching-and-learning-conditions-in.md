# Delphi-developed evaluation questionnaire for special teaching and learning conditions in dental propaedeutic courses

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A digitally administered student and instructor questionnaire developed by Uzun Deniz et al. to evaluate theoretical and practical knowledge transfer, course relevance, and learning-objective attainment in preclinical dental education.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Delphi-method evaluation instrument for special teaching and learning conditions in dental propaedeutic courses**: A multi-part evaluation questionnaire developed by the authors using a Delphi process, starting from questionnaires in similar publications and refined across 23 Delphi rounds, then pretested online with 5 informed students using the think-aloud method. The final instrument has 5 sections covering demographics, theoretical knowledge transfer, practical competence transfer, other items (including anatomy course relevance), and self-assessed NKLZ-based learning objectives. It was administered digitally via Sosci-Survey after repeat exams, with a measured internal consistency (Cronbach's alpha) of 0.95 for the student version and 0.79 for the instructor version. The authors suggest the "fakultätsübergreifende Einsatz" of these instruments could be helpful. (Uzun Deniz et al. (2023))
