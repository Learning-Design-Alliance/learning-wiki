# PÖÖ1 and PÖÖ2 performance measurement tools based on non-routine problems

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
Two researcher-developed assessments using non-routine mathematics problems and a four-stage scoring rubric to measure 9th-grade students’ mathematical performance and problem-solving approaches.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Performance Measurement Scales (PÖÖ1 and PÖÖ2) based on non-routine problems**: The study used two performance measurement tools, PÖÖ1 and PÖÖ2, consisting of non-routine problems administered before and after the 27-week application to measure 9th-grade students' mathematics performance. The pretest contained 3 questions probing verbal, algebraic and geometric skills; the posttest contained 4 problems designed to elicit knowledge of clusters, numbers, relations and functions from the 9th-grade curriculum. Solutions were scored with the authors' four-stage rubric, and the tools distinguished the experimental and control groups' outcomes. (Seval Deniz KILIÇ et al. (2018))
