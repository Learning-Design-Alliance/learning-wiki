# Cloud Bookshelf Management Platform

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A server-based platform established alongside the eBook by Hsiao Chih-Cheng et al. to collect, store, and analyze learners’ reading and testing histories for teacher monitoring and instructional adjustment.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Cloud Bookshelf Management Platform for monitoring learner reading and testing history**: A platform established alongside the eBook to centralize learner data for the teacher. It can "monitor complete records, statistical analysis of user reading and testing history", supports recording of offline reading history, and matches and cross-references reports so the administrator can grasp users' reading history. Student answers were sent to a server so the teacher could analyze class progress and adjust classes to problem areas. (Hsiao Chih-Cheng et al. (2016))
