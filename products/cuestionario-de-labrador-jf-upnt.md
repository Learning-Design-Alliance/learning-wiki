## Description
A Spanish-language self-report questionnaire developed by Labrador JF et al. to assess problematic use of new technologies across seven dimensions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Cuestionario de Labrador JF (UPNT) for detecting problematic use of new technologies**: A Spanish-language questionnaire developed by Labrador JF et al. for detecting problematic use of new technologies, used in this study as the addiction measure. The article states it "Consta de 26 preguntas que abordan 7 dimensiones" answered on a 4-point Likert scale (1 = never to 4 = always), with scores ranging from 26 to 104 where higher values indicate greater addiction. In this study a pilot with 30 students yielded Cronbach's alpha of 0,88, and responses were dichotomized at the 60th percentile (cut-off 1.71) to classify addiction. (Real Delor Raúl Emilio et al. (2025))
