# P-REFS (Physics Reasoning Evaluation Framework for Step-by-step Analysis)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
P-REFS is a 10-point stage-wise rubric developed by Nicy Scaria et al. for evaluating high-school and AP physics solutions across problem formulation, step-by-step reasoning, and final answers, with automated scoring by a validated LLM judge.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **P-REFS: a stage-wise rubric for evaluating physics reasoning step by step**: P-REFS (Physics Reasoning Evaluation Framework for Step-by-step Analysis) is a 10-point rubric that scores SLM physics solutions at distinct reasoning stages rather than by final answer alone. The article describes it as "a 10-point framework evaluating three components: Problem Formulation (0-2 pts) for interpretation and modeling, Step-by-step Solution (0-6 pts) encompassing conceptual planning, execution accuracy, and logical progression, and Final Answer (0-2 pts)". It was operationalized via an LLM-as-a-judge pipeline, with Gemini 2.5 Flash selected after expert validation against two independent physics-graduate reviewers on 550 question-response pairs per candidate judge. (Nicy Scaria et al. (2025))
