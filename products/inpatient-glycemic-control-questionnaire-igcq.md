# Inpatient Glycemic Control Questionnaire (IGCQ)

## Description
The IGCQ is a 16-item questionnaire developed by Horton William B. et al. to measure resident physicians’ comfort with, knowledge of, and perceived barriers to inpatient glycemic control.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **IGCQ: a 16-item preliminarily evaluated survey tool for resident physician perceptions and knowledge of inpatient glycemic control**: The Inpatient Glycemic Control Questionnaire (IGCQ) is a survey tool constructed from four previously published but unvalidated surveys, assessing resident physicians' "comfort with managing IGC, knowledge of inpatient glycemic target values, and perceived barriers to successful IGC". It was evaluated with Rasch Partial Credit Model and classical test theory analyses across four US institutions, with 246 of 438 (56.2%) eligible residents completing it. The final 16-item version contains a comfort scale (Questions 1–6), a knowledge scale (Questions 7–10), and a barriers scale (Questions 11–16). (Horton William B. et al. (2019))
