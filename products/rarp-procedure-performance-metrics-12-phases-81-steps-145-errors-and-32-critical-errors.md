# RARP procedure performance metrics: 12 phases, 81 steps, 145 errors and 32 critical errors

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A Delphi-developed observational assessment instrument for trained reviewers to score technical steps, errors, and critical errors in full-case videos of specified anterior transperitoneal nerve-sparing robot-assisted radical prostatectomy procedures.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **RARP procedure performance metrics: 12 phases, 81 steps, 145 errors and 32 critical errors agreed by Delphi consensus**: A structured observation instrument for scoring a full robot-assisted radical prostatectomy from video: the procedure is decomposed into phases and steps, each with associated errors and critical errors, scored binarily (occurrence) by trained blinded reviewers. The article reports the final instrument as 'the 12 phases and 81 steps did characterise a reference approach to RARP procedures by trainees at the start of their learning curve', with Table 1 totalling 81 steps, 145 errors and 32 critical errors after the Delphi meeting. It was used to score full case-videos of 12 very experienced and 12 novice surgeons. (Mottrie A et al. (2021))
