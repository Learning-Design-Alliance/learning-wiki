ChatTutor¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

ChatTutor is a course-grounded GenAI chatbot developed by Guido Makransky and colleagues that engages students in personalized teaching conversations, prompts self-explanation, and provides targeted feedback based on course materials.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • In Study 1, university students using ChatTutor scored significantly higher than teaching-as-usual students on a delayed conceptual knowledge test four weeks after the lesson, but did not differ significantly from ChatGPT students [+M]
  • In Study 1, ChatTutor students answered significantly more new follow-up conceptual knowledge items correctly than teaching-as-usual students, controlling for the testing effect [+M]
  • In Study 1, ChatTutor produced significantly higher trust, enjoyment, and behavioral intentions than ChatGPT, while students were blind to condition [+M]
