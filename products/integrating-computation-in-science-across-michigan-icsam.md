# Integrating Computation in Science Across Michigan (ICSAM)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An NSF-funded professional-development programme in which Michigan high school science teachers learn programming and computational modeling through a summer workshop and recurring facilitated workdays, run by the ICSAM project.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ICSAM professional development program for integrating computation in Michigan science classrooms**: Integrating Computation in Science Across Michigan (ICSAM) is "an NSF-funded project where teachers learn to program and to teach computational modeling to their students". It begins with a five-day summer workshop followed by periodic workdays every two months, and "more than 35 teachers have participated in the program" over the past two years. Workshop themes are equity in the classroom and confidence with computing. (Daniel P. Weller et al. (2021))
