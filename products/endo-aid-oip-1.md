## Description
ENDO-AID OIP-1 is Olympus's machine-learning computer-aided detection module that alerts endoscopists to suspicious colorectal lesions during live endoscopy.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ENDO-AID OIP-1 computer-aided detection module used during initial endoscopy training**: The ENDO-AID OIP-1 is an AI module added to an Olympus X1 series endoscope set that enhanced training for three of the six endoscopists in this study. The article states "The additional AI module that enhanced training in group A was ENDO-AID OIP-1" and that "The system is designed to aid endoscopists in detecting polyps." CADe "uses a complex algorithm created by machine learning, which alerts the endoscopist in real time when a suspicious lesion appears on the screen," highlighting suspicious areas in target mode. The article describes the equipment as safe for patients. (Orzeszko Z et al. (2025))
