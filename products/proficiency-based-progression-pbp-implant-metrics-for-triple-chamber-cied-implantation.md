# Proficiency-based progression (PBP) implant metrics for triple-chamber CIED implantation

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 theoretical), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
An expert-consensus set of binary, operationally defined intraoperative metrics for assessing novice and experienced implanters' performance during triple-chamber cardiac resynchronization therapy implantation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Expert-consensus 'skin-to-skin' CRT procedure metrics with unambiguous operational (yes/no) definitions**: A set of intraoperative performance metrics for a triple-chamber CIED implantation, developed by an international group of experienced implanters under a behavioural scientist using a validated scientific methodology and a modified Delphi consensus process. Each metric is built on 'unambiguous operational definitions (rather than descriptions)', specifying in detail how a step should or should not be done so it can be scored reliably as occurring or not (yes/no). Metrics cover compliance with recommended steps 'as well as errors to avoid', including critical errors, and are specific to lead type and target site. (Mascheroni Jorio et al. (2021))
