## Description
A regional Delphi-developed framework listing 40 core urological surgical procedures used to define and survey expected competency among urology trainees in Hokkaido.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Hokkaido 40-procedure core urological surgery list selected by three-round Delphi**: A list of 40 core urological surgical procedures distilled from the 121 procedures in the Japanese Urological Association board case-log book via a three-round Delphi process with 15 attending doctors. The authors write that they "selected ‘40 core urological surgical procedures’ out of the 121 procedures listed in a case‐log book." The 40 procedures were divided into 8 categories for analysis: transurethral, stone, pediatric, laparoscopic, open, reconstructive, robotic surgery, and others. The list anchors all four survey analyses. (Hori Kanta et al. (2024))
