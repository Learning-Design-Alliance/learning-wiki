# Schwander's vital sign simulator

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A free SourceForge-distributed medical simulation program that displays changing vital signs and cardiac rhythms and supports simulated defibrillation, cardioversion, pacing, and event logging.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Free-to-use vital sign simulator software displayed on a projector as a low-cost cardiac monitor substitute**: A free-to-use cardiac monitor program (Schwander's vital sign simulator, downloaded from SourceForge) was run on an available computer and displayed via projector to simulate a patient monitor. It showed "active changes in blood pressure, respiratory rate, heart rate, pulse oximetry, and variable rhythms on telemetry," simulated defibrillation, cardioversion, and pacing, and tracked events such as IV starts and CPR duration for later review. This replaced a costly proprietary simulator monitor. (Wang A et al. (2020))
