## Description
L2-Bench is a benchmark created by Ben Knight et al. for evaluating AI systems used in language education across specified competencies and performance dimensions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **L2-Bench operationalises the giving-feedback competency through six critical dimensions of effective feedback**: L2-Bench is an evaluation benchmark for AI systems in language education. For the giving-feedback competency, the authors operationalise the competency of "giving feedback" through a structured evaluation framework examining six critical dimensions: diagnostic accuracy, awareness of appropriacy, causes of error, prioritisation, guidance for improvement, and supporting self-regulation. Each dimension extends beyond simple error correction to encompass considerations essential to effective language teaching feedback. (Ben Knight et al. (2026))
