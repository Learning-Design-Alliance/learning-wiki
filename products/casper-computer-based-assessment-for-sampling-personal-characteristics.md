# Casper (Computer-Based Assessment for Sampling Personal Characteristics)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
Casper is an online situational judgement test developed and administered by Acuity Insights to assess personal and professional characteristics in admissions and selection.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Casper open-response situational judgement test as an admissions instrument**: Casper (Computer-Based Assessment for Sampling Personal Characteristics) is an online open-response situational judgement test assessing "personal and professional characteristics, skills, and behaviors that inform social intelligence and professionalism". It comprises 12 sections, each a video or word-based scenario followed by three open-ended questions with five minutes to answer, scored by trained raters and reported as a z-score. The study evaluated its incremental validity and diversity consequences in occupational therapy admissions. (Roduta Roberts Mary et al. (2024))
- **Casper: an online open-ended response format Situational Judgement Test assessing ten personal characteristics**: Casper is a digitally administered Situational Judgement Test, formerly known as Computer-based Assessment for Sampling Personal characteristics, which the article describes as focusing "on assessing inter-individual differences in ten personal characteristics including collaboration, communication, empathy, equity, ethics, motivation, problem solving, professionalism, resilience, and self-awareness". In this pilot it consisted of eight video and four text scenarios, each followed by three open-ended questions answered in text within a 5-minute limit, and it yields only one overall z-standardized score because the characteristics cannot be reliably discriminated within SJTs. (Knorr Mirjana et al. (2024))
