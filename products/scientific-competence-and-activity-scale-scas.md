# Scientific Competence and Activity Scale (SCAS)

## Description
A 29-item Likert self-report assessment developed by Koçak Cemal et al. for measuring scientific research competence and activity among medical faculty research assistants.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Scientific Competence and Activity Scale (SCAS) for medical faculty research assistants**: A 29-item, 5-point Likert self-report instrument measuring the scientific competence and activity of medical faculty research assistants, organized into four subdimensions: Competence (14 items), Literacy (8 items), Opportunities (4 items), and Barriers (3 items). Scores range from 29 to 145 with no reverse-scored items, and a cut-off of 92 separates those considered "competent and active" from those who are not. The article reports the mean total score in its sample as 91.23 ± 23.10 and states the scale "is valid, reliable" for use with research assistants in medical faculties. (KOÇAK Cemal et al. (2025))

### Claims
- [The 29-item SCAS shows high internal consistency (Cronbach's alpha 0.960) with all item-total correlations above 0.3](../claims/scas-high-internal-consistency-alpha-960.md) [+M]
- [A SCAS cut-off score of 92 distinguishes research assistants participating in at least one publication or project, with AUC 0.669](../claims/scas-cut-off-92-roc-auc-0669.md) [+M]
