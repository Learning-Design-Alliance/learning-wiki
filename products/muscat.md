# MUSCAT

## Description
MUSCAT is a self-report competency scale developed by Sakakida Chika and colleagues for assessing healthcare professionals’ multidisciplinary child-abuse-prevention competency from pregnancy through primary, secondary, and tertiary prevention.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Assess multidisciplinary competency for child abuse prevention with a scale spanning primary to tertiary prevention and the whole family**: The article argues that competency measurement for multidisciplinary child abuse prevention should cover all prevention stages and the entire family. It states the MUSCAT "is a measure that can comprehensively evaluate healthcare professionals' competency in relation to providing a multidisciplinary approach, from primary to tertiary prevention", and that targeting high-risk caregivers requires "consideration of the entire family, including babies, children, and fathers". This scope distinguishes it from existing collaboration scales. (Sakakida Chika et al. (2021))
