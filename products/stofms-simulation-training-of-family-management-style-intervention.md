# STOFMS (simulation training of family management style intervention)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
STOFMS is a multidisciplinary, family-centered epilepsy-management intervention developed and delivered by the study team through simulated post-discharge practice and Internet-based follow-up support for parents and caregivers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **STOFMS: simulation training of family management style intervention**: STOFMS is "a flexible and family-centered intervention that focuses on improving caregivers' perceptions of disease and problem-solving capability". It teaches family members to manage both the child's chronic condition and daily living, adding simulated post-discharge family management during hospitalization and Internet-based ongoing support. In the trial it was delivered across four sessions from admission to 6-month follow-up against a routine FMSF control. (Liu Hua-yan et al. (2024))

### Claims
- [STOFMS improved family management (FaMM) scores at 6 months after discharge compared with routine family management care](../claims/stofms-improves-famm-six-months.md) [+M]

- [STOFMS improved overall clinical efficacy at 6 months but not at 3 months after discharge](../claims/stofms-clinical-efficacy-six-months-only.md) [+M]
