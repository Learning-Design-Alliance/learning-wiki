# Quantum Mechanics Formalism and Postulates Survey (QMFPS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The QMFPS is a 34-item multiple-choice conceptual survey developed by Emily Marshman and colleagues to assess upper-level undergraduate and graduate students’ understanding of quantum-mechanics formalism and postulates.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Quantum Mechanics Formalism and Postulates Survey (QMFPS), a 34-item validated multiple-choice conceptual survey**: The QMFPS is a 34-item multiple-choice conceptual survey assessing students' understanding of the formalism and postulates of quantum mechanics, including Dirac notation, the Hilbert space, state vectors, Hermitian operators, compatible and incompatible observables, quantum measurement, time dependence, expectation values, and spin angular momenta. It fills a gap because previously developed QM surveys do not focus explicitly on these topics. The final version is available on PhysPort, and it can be completed in one 50-minute class period. (Emily Marshman et al. (2020))
