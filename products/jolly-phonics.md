## Description
Jolly Phonics is a branded synthetic-phonics curriculum with course books, audio recordings, and supplementary materials produced by Jolly Learning for teaching letter–sound correspondences, blending, segmentation, and early reading.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Jolly Phonics materials for adult EFL literacy intervention**: Jolly Phonics is a freely available course book with audios and supplementary material, originally "primarily designed for very young learners", used here as the treatment medium over 24 one-hour sessions. It targets five skills: letter sounds and digraphs, letter forms, blends, segmentation, and tricky words with irregular spelling. Sounds were taught in a non-alphabetical order so learners could combine sounds into words earlier. (Mahmoud Ibrahim (2018))
