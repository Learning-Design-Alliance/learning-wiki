## Description
A nursing-care framework developed to organize educational, informational, and management support for ICU nurses caring for brain-dead patients who are potential organ donors.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **IPSE (Improving Psychological Security and Empowerment) model for nursing care of potential organ donors**: The IPSE model is a nursing care model developed through qualitative studies to structure care of brain-dead patients who are candidates for organ donation. The article states it "covers all aspects of patient care, such as providing 'informational and educational support' and 'management support'". In this trial, both interventions' educational materials and gamification scenarios were organized around its dimensions: educational support (brain death definition and diagnostics), informational support (physiological monitoring of organ-eligible patients), and management support (legal aspects and family communication). (Hossein Meysam Pour et al. (2025))
