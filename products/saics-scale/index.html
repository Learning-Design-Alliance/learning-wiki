SAICS scale¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

The SAICS scale is a 32-item, eight-dimension self-report assessment developed by Thomas K. F. Chiu and colleagues to measure AI competency self-efficacy among secondary students.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Confirmatory factor analysis with 448 secondary students showed the 32-item SAICS measurement model has good fit and high validity and reliability [+M]
  • All eight SAICS dimensions showed high internal consistency (Cronbach's alpha ≥ 0.89), high factor loadings (0.76–0.96), and convergent validity (inter-dimension correlations r > 0.34) [+M]
