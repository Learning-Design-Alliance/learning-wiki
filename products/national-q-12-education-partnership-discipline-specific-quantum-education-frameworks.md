# National Q-12 Education Partnership discipline-specific quantum education frameworks

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A set of discipline-specific K–12 curriculum-integration frameworks developed by the National Q-12 Education Partnership, organized around nine key quantum concepts for teachers and curriculum developers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **National Q-12 Education Partnership discipline-specific frameworks structured around nine key quantum concepts**: The National Q-12 Education Partnership released frameworks for integrating quantum concepts into specific K-12 disciplines: middle school STEM and high school computer science, physics, chemistry, and mathematics. They provide "guidance about how discipline-specific learning goals could be met through teaching quantum concepts" and are "all structured around nine key concepts", from quantum information science through quantum sensors. The article presents these frameworks as a resource to help teachers see where quantum fits within their existing disciplinary curricula. (Holincheck et al. (2024))
