## Description
CONSULT e-learning is a free, self-paced four-module online training course created by Shepherd and colleagues for UK researchers and professionals learning about trials involving adults with impaired capacity to consent.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **E-learning program 'The STI-consultation'**: An individual, accredited, online CME-program created by the Dutch College of General Practitioners (NHG) with STI Aids Netherlands and the Academic Medical Centre, based on the NHG-guideline 'The STI-consultation' and the CanMEDS competency framework. "Completing the e-learning program 'The STI-consultation' takes about two hours" and it is freely available to all 12,500 Dutch GPs and GP-trainees, with participation voluntary and CME-credits awarded after full completion. (Bos-Bonnie Linda H. A. et al. (2017))
- **CONSULT: a longitudinal consultation communication curriculum for undergraduate medical education**: CONSULT is a consultation communication curriculum for different learner levels in undergraduate medical education, built on Kessler's 5 Cs model and designed for longitudinal implementation. "The curriculum was comprised of three parts: the didactic portion, consultation communication practice during high-fidelity simulations, and structured debriefing." Second-year students receive a 50-minute didactic with trigger videos and discussion; fourth-year students in the EM clerkship receive a 30-minute lecture, a 5 Cs pocket card, three weekly simulation sessions with recorded consultations reviewed by trained attending physicians, and structured debriefing feedback. (Carter Keme MD et al. (2015))
- **Kessler's 5 Cs of Consultation model for standardized emergency department consultations**: Kessler's 5 Cs of Consultation is a standardized-consultation model describing a formal consultation from the emergency department to hospital-based services. The article states the "5 Cs include Contact, Communicate, Core Question, Collaboration, and Closing the Loop, and offers specific action items for each component." The CONSULT curriculum teaches this model to medical students through didactics, trigger videos, simulation practice, and a pocket card, and measures adherence with a 13-component checklist and a Global Rating Scale. (Carter Keme MD et al. (2015))

### Claims
- [In a UK stakeholder survey (n=82), 40% of the 79 respondents who answered reported no previous training related to research involving adults lacking capacity to consent](../claims/survey-82-forty-percent-no-prior-training.md) [+M]
- [Survey respondents rated top tips (76%), case studies (72%), videos (56%), additional resources (55%), and forums/discussion groups (48%) as extremely useful learning formats](../claims/learning-preferences-top-tips-case-studies-videos.md) [+M]
- [Early e-learning evaluation (n=6) showed mean perceived knowledge about designing trials including adults lacking capacity rose from 7.2 to 8.8 on a 10-point scale](../claims/elearning-knowledge-score-7-2-to-8-8.md) [+M]
- [In one uncontrolled pre-post study, participants' scores rose from pre-test to post-test on 5 of 11 knowledge and attitude questions after completing the e-learning program 'The STI-consultation', with statistically significant changes](../claims/sti-elearning-knowledge-attitude-increase.md) [+M]
- [Knowledge and attitude changes persisted up to two years after completing the program, with no significant difference between recent and earlier completers](../claims/sti-elearning-lasting-effect-two-years.md) [+M]
- [In one uncontrolled pre-post study, second-year students' knowledge of the consultation process and comfort requesting consults rose from pre-test to post-test after a 5 Cs model didactic](../claims/consult-curriculum-second-year-knowledge-comfort.md) [+M]
