# PhET Photoelectric Effect interactive simulation

## Description
A free, downloadable interactive physics simulation made by the Physics Education Technology (PhET) Project at the University of Colorado Boulder that lets learners manipulate photoelectric-effect variables and observe resulting experimental representations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PhET Photoelectric Effect simulation as a direct representation with variable manipulation and frequency-energy graphing**: The PhET "Photoelectric Effect" simulation, accessible at phet.colorado.edu, served in this study as a "direct representation" of the photoelectric effect whose elements map directly onto the real phenomenon. Participants "were able to manipulate different variables and parameters such as light wavelength and intensity and observe their effects on the photoelectron emission" from a metal plate, guided by a structured lab worksheet including simple analytical calculations about energy and frequency. The simulation displays a photon beam, ejected electrons, and a graph of frequency versus energy illustrating the linear relationship once the work function is met. (Razan Hamed (2026))
