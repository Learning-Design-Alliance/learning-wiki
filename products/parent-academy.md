## Description
A University of Chicago parent-education programme providing English and mathematics classes plus family learning activities for parents and carers of primary-school children.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Parent Academy intervention: 12 fortnightly parent classes in English and mathematics plus a family field trip**: The Parent Academy was a parent education programme developed by the University of Chicago for parents of pupils in Years 3 to 6, comprising "12 Parent Academy classes, 6 on English and 6 on mathematics, delivered fortnightly by tutors with teaching qualifications and experience of teaching adults", plus an educational family trip. Sessions were skills-based, interactive, and each included a 'Fun Home Activity' for parents and children to complete together to enhance the home learning environment. The intervention was not manualised. (Fatima Husain et al. (2016))
