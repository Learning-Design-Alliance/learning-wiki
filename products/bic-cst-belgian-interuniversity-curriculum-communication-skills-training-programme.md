# BIC-CST (Belgian Interuniversity Curriculum communication skills training programme)

## Description
BIC-CST is a 40-hour Belgian interuniversity programme for medical residents that combines small-group communication-skills training, role play, immediate feedback, and stress-management training.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **BIC-CST: 40-h Belgian Interuniversity Curriculum communication skills training programme for residents**: The BIC-CST is a 40-h programme comprising 30 h of communication skills training and 10 h of stress management training, spread over 8 months in bimonthly small-group sessions of up to seven participants. It is described as "learner-centred, skills-focused, practice-oriented, and tailored to address a participant's individual needs". Seventeen hours focus on dyadic consultations, 10 h on triadic consultations with role play and immediate feedback, and a final 3-h session integrates communication and stress management skills. Trained residents attended an average of 25 h (s.d.=7.9). (Merckaert I et al. (2013))
