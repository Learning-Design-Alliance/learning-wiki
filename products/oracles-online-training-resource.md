# ORaClES online training resource

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
ORaClES is a self-guided online training resource developed by Oostendorp and colleagues to teach medical students the expert cues used to recognise imminent death in palliative-care patients.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ORaClES online training resource teaching the four most heavily weighted expert cues for recognising imminent death**: The ORaClES intervention is a newly developed online training resource designed to improve recognition of imminent death in palliative care patients. Its content derives from a prior judgement analysis identifying the clinical cues experts use, and it provides "a description of the four most important cues and, where possible, graphical information for ease of understanding". It is delivered via a purpose-built study website and "should take approximately 15 min to complete", and will be made freely available if proven effective. (Oostendorp L et al. (2019))
