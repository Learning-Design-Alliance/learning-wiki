Multiple-Text Strategy Inventory (MTSI)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 theoretical), q2 · 1 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A 15-item questionnaire developed by Ivar Bråten and colleagues to assess undergraduates' self-reported accumulation and cross-text elaboration strategies immediately after reading multiple texts.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Self-reported accumulation negatively predicts intertextual comprehension while cross-text elaboration positively predicts it, after controlling for prior knowledge [+M]
  • Accumulation and cross-text elaboration are uncorrelated and show opposite-sign correlations with intertextual comprehension [+W]
  • Self-reported multiple-text strategies did not predict intratextual comprehension, where prior knowledge was the unique predictor [~W]
  • External validity of the special-issue strategy measures was mixed: low-to-moderate comprehension correlations for Bräten and Strømsø and Magliano et al., indeterminate for Schellings and Winne and Muis [+W]
