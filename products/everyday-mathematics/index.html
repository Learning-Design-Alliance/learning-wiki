Everyday Mathematics®¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes

Description¶

Everyday Mathematics® is a comprehensive pre-K–6 mathematics curriculum developed by the University of Chicago School Mathematics Project, combining teacher guides, student materials, manipulatives, games, technology, and repeated exposure to mathematical content.

Components¶

  • Everyday Mathematics® core curriculum for grades pre-K–6: Everyday Mathematics® is "a comprehensive curriculum for students in grades pre-K–6 that exposes students to material over extended periods of time by repeatedly reinforcing material both within and across grade levels." The article states "The curriculum includes six key features": real-life problem solving, balanced instruction, multiple opportunities for basic skills practice, emphasis on communication, home/school partnerships, and use of technology. It is organized around Program Goals (the same at all grade levels, organized by content strand) refined through approximately 20–25 Grade-Level Goals per grade, with teacher guides, student materials, manipulatives, games, and online resour (Everyday Mathematics®)
  • Everyday Mathematics (EM) core elementary mathematics curriculum: EM is a prekindergarten-to-grade-6 core mathematics curriculum developed by the University of Chicago School Mathematics Project and published by Wright Group/McGraw-Hill. It emphasizes "six content strands" with learning targets per strand and grade, and "a strong emphasis on real-life problem solving, manipulatives, concept development, and targeted use of technology and parent participation". Materials include student materials, teacher manuals, assessment and practice guides, home links, a 2- to 3-day summer teacher workshop, and follow-up support from EM consultants; lessons follow a three-part format with differentiated instruction options. (Michael Vaden-Kiernan et al. (2015))
  • Multi-site cluster-randomized scale-up evaluation of Everyday Mathematics: A Goal 4 scale-up evaluation randomizing 48 elementary schools (K-5) in 7 districts across the country to EM or business-as-usual, blocked at the district level, with approximately 4,500 students and 1,200 teachers per year across two cohorts (grades K&3 and 1&4) over two school years. Outcomes are fall and spring GMADE assessments and the Student Motivation Form, with fidelity of implementation captured via classroom observations, interviews, and surveys. The study addresses overall impacts, subgroup variation, fidelity, and proximal-outcome moderators. (Michael Vaden-Kiernan et al. (2015))
  • Everyday Mathematics (EM) core elementary mathematics curriculum: Everyday Mathematics is a core mathematics curriculum for grades prekindergarten to six, developed by the University of Chicago School Mathematics Project and published by Wright Group/McGraw-Hill. It "emphasizes six strands of mathematics knowledge with learning targets identified for each strand by grade level" and includes student materials, teacher manuals, assessment and practice guides, and home links supporting parent involvement. The article evaluates it at scale in a cluster randomized trial. It is described as emphasizing "a constructivist philosophy, with a strong emphasis on real-life problem solving, manipulatives, concept development, and targeted use of technology and parent participation." (Vaden-Kiernan et al. (2015))

Claims¶

  • Everyday Mathematics® has potentially positive effects on mathematics achievement for primary students, with a substantively important but statistically non-significant effect size of 0.27 [+W]
  • Everyday Mathematics® showed positive effects on TAAS math subtests (Concepts, Operations, Problem Solving) with effect sizes of 0.25 to 0.31 that were not statistically significant after clustering correction [+W]
  • School-level assignment to Everyday Mathematics does not produce a statistically significant impact on elementary students' math achievement relative to business-as-usual control after one or two years of implementation at scale [+M]
