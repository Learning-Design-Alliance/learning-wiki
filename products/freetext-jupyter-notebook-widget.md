# FreeText Jupyter Notebook widget

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An IPyWidgets-based software package from Matelsky et al. for embedding natural-language short-answer questions and automated LLM feedback in Jupyter notebooks used for teaching and homework.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **FreeText Jupyter Notebook widget for embedding open-ended questions in notebooks**: A front-end built with IPyWidgets that lets instructors embed natural-language short-answer questions inside Jupyter notebooks used in computer science, data science, and statistics courses. The paper notes it is "designed to be easily integrated into lecture and homework notebooks, enabling instructors to easily enrich existing teaching materials." Students see a question with a text box, submit, and receive LLM feedback with seconds of latency. (Matelsky et al. (2023))
