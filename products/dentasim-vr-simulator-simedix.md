## Description
SiMedix's DentaSim is a virtual-reality dental training system that provides interactive practice and performance visualization for preclinical operative dentistry skills.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **DentaSim VR simulator for preclinical operative dentistry training**: The DentaSim VR simulator (SiMedix) is a virtual reality training system featuring "a movement recording system records the hand movements of the operator with 20 μm accuracy". It includes a head-mounted display and controller, and provides real-time graphical rendering of operator performance. Supplemental tools include transparency views of the tooth, magnification, and superimposition of the prepared cavity on an intact tooth. (Heidaridarani Mohammad Mehdi et al. (2025))
