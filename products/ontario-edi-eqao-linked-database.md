# Ontario EDI-EQAO linked database

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A province-wide longitudinal dataset created by Sinclair Jeanne et al. by linking Ontario's Early Development Instrument records with EQAO mathematics and reading assessments from kindergarten through Grade 10, with associated developmental, demographic, neighborhood, and school covariates.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **A multi-cohort Ontario EDI-EQAO linked database tracking achievement from kindergarten through grade 10**: This element is a province-wide, multi-level, multi-cohort database created by linking the Early Development Instrument (EDI) administered in senior kindergarten with EQAO math and reading assessments in grades 3, 6, 9, and 10. It contains "covariates for their development, demographics, affect, neighborhoods, and schools." Three versions were created with a "stepwise inner join merging strategy," and the fully linked dataset includes 86,778 students with all five datapoints and no missingness. (Sinclair Jeanne et al. (2023))
