# iVR application for femorotibial mobilization training on Meta Quest 2

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (3 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
A Meta Quest 2 immersive virtual-reality application developed by Trinidad-Fernández et al. for undergraduate physiotherapy students to learn and practise femorotibial mobilization.
