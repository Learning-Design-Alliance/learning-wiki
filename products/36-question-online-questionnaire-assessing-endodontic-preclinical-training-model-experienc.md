# 36-question online questionnaire assessing endodontic preclinical training model experiences and perceptions

## Description
A Turkish Google Forms self-report questionnaire developed by Özdemir et al. to collect dentistry learners' and educators' demographic information, experiences, perceived clinical contributions, and preferences regarding preclinical endodontic training models.
