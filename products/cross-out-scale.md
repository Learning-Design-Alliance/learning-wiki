## Description
A standardized school assessment developed by Pressey and normed for interpreting pupils' scores across grades and ages.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Grade and age norms for the Cross-out scale based on about 4,000 cases**: First norms for the final form of the scale: median scores for each of the four tests by grade (3 through 12) and by age (9 through 18), derived from surveys of three Indiana cities totalling "about 4,000 cases" across three revisions. Grade norms are accompanied by median ages so schools with different age-grade distributions can be compared fairly by adjusting scores by half the inter-age interval. (Pressey (1920))
