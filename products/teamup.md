## Description
TEAMUP is a commercial multiplayer 3D serious game made by The Barn for training and assessing team communication, coordination, leadership, self-organisation, and collaboration.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **TEAMUP multiplayer 3D team training and assessment game**: TEAMUP (© The Barn) is a commercial, multiplayer three-dimensional serious game built in the Unreal Development Kit engine in which "Four players need to self-organise, communicate, collaborate and arrange team communication, coordination and leadership in order to solve five levels of puzzles/team challenges". Players stranded on a deserted island must reach the other side to be rescued, with five puzzle levels (door, tile, maze, bridge, pillar) requiring alternating leadership. The game logs any kind of game data, communicates with a scoring, assessment and visualisation tool, and supports distributed play and video recording. (Mayer (2018))
