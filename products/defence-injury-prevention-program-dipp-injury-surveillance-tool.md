# Defence Injury Prevention Program (DIPP) injury surveillance tool

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A point-of-care injury-surveillance instrument used by Australian Defence Force training personnel to record injury type, location, activity, action, severity, and mechanism for injury-prevention monitoring.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Defence Injury Prevention Program (DIPP) injury surveillance tool for recruit training**: The DIPP was the injury data source for this study, an injury surveillance tool documenting injuries in the 28- and 80-day recruit training courses. It recorded "the type, location, activity during injury, action, severity and mechanism (cause)" at point-of-care facilities by medical staff, physiotherapists or physical training instructors. The article reports it documented up to 10 times as many injuries as the ADF's Defence Work Health and Safety incident reporting system. (Orr Robin M. et al. (2020))
