# NeoNatalie Live

## Description
NeoNatalie Live is a Laerdal neonatal resuscitation simulator and companion training application that provides progressive positive-pressure-ventilation scenarios, immediate feedback, and automated performance data collection.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **NeoNatalie Live neonatal simulator with automatic logging and app-based feedback**: NeoNatalie Live is a low-cost newborn simulator produced specifically to train competence in neonatal positive pressure ventilation. It simulates "newborns with differing initial lung compliance", uses real resuscitation data from 1237 newborns to guide heart rate response, and gives "immediate, targeted feedback" through a training application across 4 scenarios of increasing difficulty. Bluetooth technology allows automatic collection of training data in a Weblog, enabling the objective ventilation measures analyzed in this study. (Haynes Joanna C. et al. (2024))
- **NeoNatalie Live high-fidelity manikin with tablet app providing automated performance feedback**: NeoNatalie Live is a high-fidelity manikin used for daily neonatal resuscitation skill drills in this study. It has sensors that measure "head positioning for keeping the airway open, mask seal, air pressure given to the lungs, ventilation rate, and continuity of ventilation", simulates realistic lung and heart-rate development, and cries after sufficient adequate ventilation. A tablet app offers three case levels (Easy, Medium, Hard), gives immediate "well done" or improvement feedback, records participant IDs, and uploads results to a cloud database. (Gurung Rejina et al. (2019))

### Claims
