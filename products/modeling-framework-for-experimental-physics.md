# Modeling Framework for Experimental Physics

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A physics-education framework developed by H. Lewandowski and colleagues that guides students in modeling both physical systems and experimental measurement systems through iterative model construction and evaluation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Modeling Framework for Experimental Physics as a guide for student experimentation**: The course adopts the 'Modeling Framework for Experimental Physics' developed by H. Lewandowski and colleagues as a framework to guide students during experiments. The article states that it "clarifies the importance of modeling the experimental measurement system in addition to the physical system to be studied, and highlights the iterative aspect in modeling". Students are introduced to it in a tutorial at the beginning of the second semester, practice modeling electrical circuits and apparatus including identifying assumptions and limits of models, and its posters are displayed in each lab room for use during modeling practice. (M. Alemani (2024))
