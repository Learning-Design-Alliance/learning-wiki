## Description
MSWeight is a multimodal weight-management counseling curriculum for medical students, comprising web-based instruction, role-play, web-patient feedback, and enhanced clerkship training, developed by Ockene and colleagues.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MSWeight multi-modal weight management counseling curriculum for medical students**: MSWeight is a multi-modal education (MME) intervention implemented across medical school years 1–3, comprising "a 4-hour self-paced web course that is evidence-supported, competency-based and covers all 5As", a role-play exercise, a web-patient encounter with feedback, and an enhanced clerkship with preceptors trained in weight management counseling. Counseling focused on the 5As (Ask, Advise, Assess, Assist, Arrange) and patient-centeredness. Each component included written instructions, objectives, and discussion points to standardize delivery across schools. The curriculum was evaluated in an eight-school group-randomized trial against traditional education. (Ockene JK et al. (2021))
