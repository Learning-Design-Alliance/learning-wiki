## Description
Lapp is a React Native mobile app, React teacher web app, and AWS cloud backend developed by Ulloa et al. to support asynchronous laparoscopic-skills practice, video submission, and expert feedback.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **LAPP digital platform for remote and asynchronous laparoscopic simulation training**: LAPP is a digital platform created through the MITO project in 2019 to administer a validated advanced laparoscopic skills training program remotely, "possible through continuous feedback given in a remote and asynchronous manner". It has trained 369 trainees in 14 institutions across Latin America and stores 6729 videos of laparoscopic training exercises, including 6496 videos of 11 basic laparoscopic exercises. This study uses the platform's video database to train and test the AI assessment algorithm. (Belmar Francisca et al. (2022))
