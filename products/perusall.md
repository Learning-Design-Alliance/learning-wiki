## Description
Perusall is a social e-reader platform developed by G. King, B. Lukoff, E. Mazur, and K. Miller that enables collaborative annotation and threaded discussion of digital content, with integration into learning-management systems.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Perusall social e-reader platform for asynchronous social annotation in blended learning**: Perusall is a freely available digital platform, established in 2016 by G. King, B. Lukoff, E. Mazur, and K. Miller, classified as "a social e-reader" that allows "synchronous but mainly asynchronous and jointly active viewing, studying, and learning through individual annotations of any digital content or digital medium." Each annotation opens a conversation thread for discussion. The authors use it in over 10 STEM courses and note it can be integrated into LMS platforms like Canvas, BlackBoard, or Moodle. (Jozef Hanč et al. (2023))
- **Perusall as a combination of three digital technologies mapping onto assessment OF/FOR/AS learning**: The article's organizing account is that "Perusall, as a social e-reader, is a combination of three digital technologies": a social network linking participants with a thread per annotation, an annotation tool for highlighting text, images, video or podcast moments and commenting (including LaTeX, code, hashtags, and e-voting questions), and a data science report tool delivering automated reports and machine-learning grading of contribution quality on "three basic levels of quality 0, 1, 2." The article maps these onto Earl's assessment OF/FOR/AS learning: data analytics as assessment OF learning, social interaction as assessment FOR learning, and self-regulated annotation as assessment AS learning. (Jozef Hanč et al. (2023))
