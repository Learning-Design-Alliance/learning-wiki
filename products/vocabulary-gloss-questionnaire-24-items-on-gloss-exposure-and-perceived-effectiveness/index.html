Vocabulary gloss questionnaire (24 items on gloss exposure and perceived effectiveness)¶

Description¶

A Chinese-language 24-item questionnaire developed by Cheng et al. (2009) to measure learners' prior exposure to gloss types and perceived effectiveness for reading and vocabulary learning.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Learners hold positive attitudes toward glosses, but their perceptions of which gloss type works best do not match measured performance [+W]
