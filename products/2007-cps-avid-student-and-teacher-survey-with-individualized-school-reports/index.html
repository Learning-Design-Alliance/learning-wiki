2007 CPS AVID student and teacher survey with individualized school reports¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A CCSR-developed survey and school-reporting instrument used with Chicago Public Schools AVID students and teachers to measure program experiences and compare participating schools.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Students in high and highest rated AVID schools are overwhelmingly positive about the program's impact on them [+W]
  • Even students in lowest-rated AVID schools are fairly positive, though only half would recommend AVID to friends [+W]
  • Small gaps in AVID experiences by gender, achievement, and grade, with girls, lower achievers, and tenth graders more positive [+W]
  • AVID students in CPS report extremely high satisfaction with the program [+W]
