## Description
A seven-stage constructivist, inquiry-oriented instructional model developed by Eisenkraft for organizing science teaching through eliciting, engaging, exploring, explaining, elaborating, evaluating, and extending.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **7E learning model as a constructivist instructional sequence for science teaching**: The 7E learning model is a constructivist, inquiry-based instructional sequence for science teaching, described as an improved version of the 5E model developed by Eisenkraft. The article states its stages as "elicit, engage, explore, explain, elaborate, evaluate, and extend". In this study, multimedia materials (pictures, activity drawings, animations, simulations, video-films) were prepared for each stage of the model and used to teach the fifth-grade Matter's Change Unit. (Sarac et al. (2017))
