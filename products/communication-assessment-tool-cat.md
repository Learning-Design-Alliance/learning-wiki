# Communication Assessment Tool (CAT)

## Description
A 15-item, five-point patient questionnaire developed to assess clinicians' or medical students' communication and interpersonal skills from the patient perspective.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Communication Assessment Tool (CAT) used as a patient-rated measure of medical student communication**: The CAT is "a 15-item questionnaire that assesses communication skills from the patient perspective and has validity evidence to support its use," using a 1–5 rating scale across communication and interpersonal domains. The study omitted the final team-level item and scored students out of 70 points, dichotomizing into maximal (70) vs sub-maximal scores because of a ceiling effect. Its utility in medical students had not previously been studied. (Dubosh NM et al. (2020))

### Claims
