## Description
A school physics curriculum programme developed by Kaur et al. that teaches Einsteinian physics through models, analogies, thought experiments, and small-group activities, supported by professional development for teachers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Teaching Einsteinian physics through models, analogies and small-group classroom activities**: The Einstein-First program focuses on "developing simple, inexpensive activities that use analogies and models to teach the concepts of Einsteinian physics." All classes used small group activities working with models and analogies, and students were encouraged to participate in group discussions. The program introduces curved space first, then warped time through real and thought experiments with clocks, before introducing Newtonian and Euclidean approximations as useful tools. (Kaur et al. (2016))
