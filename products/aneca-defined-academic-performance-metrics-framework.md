# ANECA-defined academic performance metrics framework

## Description
A Spanish National Quality Evaluation and Accreditation Agency (ANECA) framework of subject-, semester-, and degree-level indicators for measuring university students’ academic progression, efficiency, success, and graduation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ANECA-defined academic performance metrics framework for Spanish university curricula**: A framework of institutional performance metrics defined in Spain by the National Quality Evaluation and Accreditation Agency (ANECA), which the simulator operationalizes. It spans subject- and semester-level rates — "the efficiency rate and the success rate" — and degree-level rates, including "the graduation rate measured as the percentage of students who finish as scheduled or taking an extra year" and a credit-based efficiency rate. The article uses this framework to structure the simulator's outputs for curriculum analysis. (Caro Huertas et al. (2014))

### Claims
