Description¶

A secured, low-cost JupyterHub sandbox and process-monitoring framework developed by Germán Arroyo et al. for connected Computer Graphics programming education.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Explicit API interception from a pilot cohort of 19 students yielded 1,880 events over about 57 active student-hours [+W]
  • Macro-level behavioral metrics flagged anomalies consistent with automated workflows and off-platform AI code synthesis [+W]
  • Rootless Podman isolation maintained containment with no observed privilege escalation or cross-identity contamination under classroom load [+W]
  • Class rhythm heatmaps revealed evening-peaked, deadline-driven temporal engagement patterns in the cohort [+W]
  • Session intensity and duration distributions flagged physically impossible persistence as severe statistical outliers [+W]
  • Cumulative engagement showed staircase growth with deadline-driven peaks of 348 events on April 4th and 205 events around April 9th [+W]
