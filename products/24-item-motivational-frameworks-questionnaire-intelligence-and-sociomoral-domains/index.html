24-item motivational frameworks questionnaire (intelligence and sociomoral domains)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 mixed) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A 24-item orally administered questionnaire developed by Gunderson et al. to measure children's implicit theories, goal orientations, success and failure attributions, and improvement-strategy generation across intelligence and sociomoral domains.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Parents' naturalistic process praise at child ages 14-38 months predicts children's incremental motivational frameworks at age 7-8 [~M] — children's frameworks at 7-8 years were measured with this questionnaire
  • Parents' person praise at 14-38 months was not associated with children's later entity frameworks [~M]

Related Products and Programmes¶

  • Mindset — the learner variable (implicit theories and related goals and attributions) the questionnaire measures
