# PSPA index from Chile's Agency of Quality Education

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 0–100 composite school-assessment index developed and administered by Chile's Agency of Quality Education to measure schools' promotion of physical activity and classify them as PSPA or non-PSPA.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PSPA index from Chile's Agency of Quality Education (0–100 scale, >70 classifies a school as promoting physical activity)**: The PSPA index is a school-level composite indicator produced by Chile's Agency of Quality Education that measures "the perception of schoolchildren and the Agency of Education Quality information according to the degree to which the school PSPA". It draws on the California Healthy Kids Survey, the Physical Education National Study (Chile), and administrative records of the Agency of Education Quality. Scores range between 0 and 100 points; a score > 70 classifies a school as PSPA and ≤ 70 as non-PSPA. In this study it was used to classify the 4,990 schools into PSPA (n = 710) and non-PSPA (n = 4,280) groups. (Delgado-Floody P et al. (2024))
