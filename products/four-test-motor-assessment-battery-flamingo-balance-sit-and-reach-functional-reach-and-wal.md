# Four-test motor assessment battery: Flamingo Balance, Sit and Reach, Functional Reach, and Wall Catch Coordination tests

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Akyol et al.'s battery combines four standardized child motor-performance tests to assess static and dynamic balance, flexibility, and coordination before and after training.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Four-test motor assessment battery: Flamingo Balance, Sit and Reach, Functional Reach, and Wall Catch Coordination tests**: The study evaluated motor outcomes with four printed instruments: the Flamingo Balance test for static balance (number of balance failures in 1 minute on a metal beam), the Sit and Reach test for hamstring and lower back flexibility, the Functional Reach test for dynamic balance (reached distance in centimetres, average of the last two of three trials), and the Wall Catch Coordination Test for coordination (successful one-hand throws at a 3-meter distance). All children were assessed before training and at the end of 16 weeks. (Betül Akyol et al. (2018))
