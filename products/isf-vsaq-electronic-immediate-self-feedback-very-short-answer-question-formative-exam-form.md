# ISF-VSAQ (electronic immediate self-feedback very short answer question formative exam format)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A named electronic short-answer formative assessment format developed at Phramongkutklao College of Medicine that combines student self-scoring against expected answers with immediate understanding-level feedback.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ISF-VSAQ: electronic immediate self-feedback very short answer question formative exam format**: The ISF-VSAQ is an electronic formative exam item format developed at Phramongkutklao College of Medicine in which each item has "two sections with four parts", comprising a clinical vignette with response space and "expected answers with scores and self-scoring choices" on a 0.00–1.00 scale in 0.25 intervals, plus self-feedback on understanding (CU, PU, NU levels). Students mark their own answers against expected responses and receive real-time scores, intended to provide consistent feedback where instructor capacity is limited (five pharmacology instructors for ~100 students). (Lertsakulbunlue Sethapong (2024))

### Claims
- [Student self-marking of ISF-VSAQ answers shows excellent inter-rater reliability with teacher ratings (rs = 0.87–0.98 per item; overall r = 0.97)](../claims/isf-vsaq-self-marking-teacher-reliability.md) [+M]
- [Generalizability decision study: one occasion of ISF-VSAQ requires about 25 items for acceptable reliability (Phi = 0.72), while two occasions with ten items suffice (Phi = 0.71)](../claims/gtheory-vsaq-items-occasions-dstudy.md) [+M]
- [Over 80% of students agreed ISF-VSAQs improve knowledge review, problem analysis, feedback quality, and electronic usability; perception questionnaire reliability was α = 0.90](../claims/isf-vsaq-positive-student-perceptions.md) [+M]
