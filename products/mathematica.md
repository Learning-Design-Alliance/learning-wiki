## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Mathematica computer algebra system as a CAMI tool for secondary mathematics**: Mathematica is the computer algebra system used to deliver the experimental instruction. The article describes it as software that "can do symbolic calculation besides numerical calculation to solve mathematical problems", with abilities to plot advanced graphs in cartesian, polar and cylindrical coordinates and to visualize. Its notebook (.nb) files can be saved in numerous formats, and the freely installable CDF Player lets users examine prepared structures and "interfere as far as allowed" without Mathematica installed. Manipulable structures such as slides intersect with dynamic geometry software features. (Mehmet Alper Ardıç; Tevfik İşleyen (2018))
