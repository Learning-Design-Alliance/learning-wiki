# Four-topic curriculum framework for TMS and tES training

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A competency-based framework from Fried et al. that organizes TMS and tES training for technicians, clinicians, and scientists into core knowledge, safety and ethics, basic skills, and advanced skills.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Four-topic curriculum framework for each NIBS technique: core knowledge, safety and ethics, basic skills, advanced skills**: For each NIBS technique (TMS and tES) the guidelines define curricula covering four general topics: Core knowledge; Safety and ethical concerns; Basic skills; and Advanced skills. Each area is subdivided into competencies, and Tables 1 and 2 note the minimum competencies required for each trainee role. Core knowledge provides systematic instruction in theoretical aspects, safety covers regulation-compliant human subjects practice, basic skills cover foundational techniques, and advanced skills cover specialized protocols that individual institutions may choose whether to require. (Fried P.J. et al. (2021))
