## Description
A nationwide survey of online charter school principals, described as the data source for "the first nationwide data and analysis of the operations and instructional approaches of online charter schools." It was completed by 127 principals of online charter schools across the country and was collected as part of research funded by the Walton Family Foundation. The survey underpins the brief's highlights of operational and instructional practices.

- Completion by principals of online charter schools, as the survey respondents are described as 127 principals of online charter schools across the country.

### Target Learners
- Students enrolled in online charter schools at elementary, middle, and high school grade levels
