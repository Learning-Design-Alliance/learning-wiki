# MALA (Modular Artificial Learning Assistance)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A GPT-4o-based modular chatbot architecture developed by Gabelmann et al. that routes exercise-solving requests among hint, explanation, feedback, and safety modules.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MALA: a modular agentic AI chatbot architecture for exercise-solving assistance**: MALA (Modular Artificial Learning Assistance) decomposes tutoring into a two-stage process of intent classification and task-specific execution. A central classifier routes each student message to one of four specialized modules: a hint module that handles requests where students ask for 'a starting point, the next step, or a nudge toward the solution', an explanation module constrained to short structured messages, a feedback module with a reasoning-then-evaluation-then-output workflow, and a fallback/safety module deflecting prompt-injection attempts. It was prototyped with GPT-4o for guiding students through problem-solving exercises and optionally generating new exercises. (Gabelmann et al. (2026))
