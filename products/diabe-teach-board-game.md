# Diabe-teach board game

## Description
A non-digital diabetes-learning board game developed by Mariam S et al. in which medical students connect keyword cards into chains and explain their reasoning for peer and instructor adjudication.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Diabe-teach board game (7×7 keyword-chain game with 49 cards)**: Diabe-teach is a non-digital educational board game for diabetes content. It uses “49 cards, each featuring a significant keyword selected by the instructor” shuffled into a 7×7 grid; players build chains of at least four connected keywords and must “explain each chain at the end of the round,” with consensus and instructor adjudication of validity. A 15-minute trial round precedes two 30-minute rounds, with cards reshuffled each round so the board arrangement “changes each game due to shuffling.” It was evaluated in an RCT against structured self-study. (Mariam S et al. (2025))

### Claims
- [Medical students who played the Diabe-teach board game scored higher on an immediate diabetes post-test than students in a self-study control group](../claims/diabe-teach-posttest-outperformed-self-study.md) [+M]
