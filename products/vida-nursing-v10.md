# VIDA-Nursing v1.0

## Description
VIDA-Nursing v1.0 is an immersive virtual-reality nursing simulator developed within the VIDA environment to let undergraduate nursing students and health workers practise adult vacuum blood collection procedures.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **VIDA-Nursing v1.0 immersive virtual reality simulator for vacuum blood collection in adults**: VIDA-Nursing v1.0 is an immersive virtual reality simulator teaching vacuum blood collection in adult patients, built on the VIDA (Virtual Interactive Distance-Learning on Anatomy) environment. It uses Unreal 4.18, Blender, GitHub and related tools, with a Leap Motion gesture sensor and Oculus Rift head-mounted display so users manipulate virtual objects with free hands and stereoscopic vision. Fifteen workers and fifteen students validated it on visual, interactive, movement simulation reality, teaching and user-friendliness aspects. (Souza-Junior Valtuir Duarte De et al. (2020))
