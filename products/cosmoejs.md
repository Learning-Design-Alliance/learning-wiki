# COSMOEJS

## Description
COSMOEJS is an open-source suite of user-friendly cosmological modeling programs with observational datasets, adjustable model parameters, real-time plots, and chi-squared goodness-of-fit calculations, developed by Jacob Moldenhauer and colleagues for physics outreach and undergraduate instruction.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Parameterized FLRW cosmological model with matter, dark energy, and curvature densities as adjustable parameters**: COSMOEJS assumes a big bang physical universe, a general-relativity mathematical model, and uniformly distributed spacetime, and numerically integrates an equation of motion for the expansion rate H(z) as a function of redshift (Eq. A1). The model's adjustable parameters are the Hubble constant H0, fractional densities Ωm, Ωb, Ωc, ΩΛ, and Ωk, and the dark-energy equation-of-state parameters w0 and wa, with w(z) = w0 + wa[z/(1+z)]. The article states "As the values of these parameters change, Eq. (A1) describes diﬀerent types of evolutions for the universe", and the curvature parameter distinguishes open, flat, and closed geometries. (Jacob Moldenhauer et al. (2018))
