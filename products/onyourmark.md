## Description
OnYourMark is an early literacy tutoring service providing "virtual, individualized, high dosage instruction focused on foundational early literacy skills". It is grounded in the science of reading, partners with schools as a complement to existing literacy instruction, and delivers a phonics, phonological awareness and fluency curriculum in 1:1 or 2:1 sessions embedded into the school day, four 20-minute sessions per week from September to May.
