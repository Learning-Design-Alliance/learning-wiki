## Description
Socrative is a cloud-based classroom response and assessment application developed by Socrative/Showbie that lets instructors deliver polls and quizzes, collect student responses, and display feedback or aggregate results.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Socrative mobile classroom application used for instant polling and weekly formative quizzes in medical English**: Socrative is a "free, cloud-based system" widely adopted in higher education, used here for anonymous instant polling of student expectations and weekly quizzes in multiple-choice, true-or-false, and short-answer formats. The instructor controlled item delivery (one by one), feedback timing, and shared anonymous scores and class results on screen to foster "classroom interaction by encouraging input from every student and sharing it instantly with the whole class." (Kim Kyong-Jee (2019))
