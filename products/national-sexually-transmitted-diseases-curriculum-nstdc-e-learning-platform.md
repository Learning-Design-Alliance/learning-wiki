# National Sexually Transmitted Diseases Curriculum (NSTDC) e-learning platform

## Description
A free University of Washington-developed and CDC-funded e-learning platform that provides interactive sexually transmitted infection diagnosis and management training through modular self-directed and quick-access modes, with an integrated question bank.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **National Sexually Transmitted Diseases Curriculum (NSTDC) e-learning platform with dual-functionality design**: The NSTDC is a free e-learning platform developed at the University of Washington with CDC funding that "provides free, interactive training on the diagnosis and management of STIs." It uses "a flexible, dual-functionality design and navigation system that provides access to the same information on the site via 2 different modalities": a modular, self-directed, longitudinal learning platform and quick access viewing of any site content. Modular content is augmented by an interactive board-review style Question Bank viewable through either modality, and content emphasizes congruity with CDC STD treatment guidelines. (Bauer KM et al. (2021))
