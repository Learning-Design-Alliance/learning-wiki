# Framework for Interactive Learning in Lectures (FILL+)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
FILL+ is a trained-coder classroom-observation protocol developed by Ibukunoluwa Bukola et al. to record and classify the timing and interactivity level of instructor and student activities in interactive lectures.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Framework for Interactive Learning in Lectures (FILL+) for classifying classroom activities by interactivity level**: FILL+ is a continuous, mutually exclusive classroom observation protocol that documents what students and instructors are doing over time and classifies each activity into three interactivity levels: "Interactive" (e.g., clicker questions, student discussion), "Vicarious interactive" (e.g., individual students asking a question), and "Non-interactive" (e.g., instructor lecturing). The authors chose it over COPUS because it "captures specific activities that typically occur in Peer Instruction classrooms," records the duration and chronological order of activities, and disentangles whole-class questions from individual-student responses. Codes include Feedback, Student Discussion, Student Thinking, Class Question, Student Question, Lecturer Question, Student Response, Lecturer Response, Lecturer Talk, and Administrative. (Ibukunoluwa Bukola et al. (2025))
