# Process Assessment of the Learner (PAL) Reading and Writing Lesson Sets 4 and 7

## Description
A scripted PAL curriculum package for written-language intervention that combines phoneme encoding, spelling, handwriting, and composition lessons for learners with writing difficulties.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **PAL Reading and Writing Lesson Sets 4 and 7 (Talking Letters, Spelling, Handwriting and Composition)**: A manualized written-language intervention comprising Lesson Sets 4 and 7 of the Process Assessment of the Learner, with three sections: "subword level — Talking Letters, word level— Spelling, and text level — Handwriting and Composition". Sessions included rapidly paced multi-sensory phoneme encoding, oral spelling of monosyllabic words with three cue types, 5-minute prompted compositions using a stated strategy poster and target words, and handwriting practice with numbered arrow cues. In this trial it was delivered in 24 sessions over 12 weeks, two 25-minute sessions weekly, in small groups of three to six students. (Hooper et al. (2013))
