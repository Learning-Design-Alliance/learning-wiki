## Description
The Lack of Attention Scale (LAS), developed by Al-Obeidi, is a teacher-completed Likert-type rating scale for measuring kindergarten children's lack of attention and concentration.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Lack of Attention Scale (LAS) for kindergarten children**: The LAS is Al-Obeidi's (1999) rating scale for kindergarten children measuring lack of attention, completed by teachers. It originally consisted of (40) items on a five-point Likert scale, and after referee review the modified responses became (Mostly, Sometimes and Rarely) and the scale was decreased to (38) items in its final version. Validity was approved through a half division yielding (0.93), which the article calls good. The scale was administered before and after the four-month experiment to both groups. (Bulquees Ismail Abdul Majid Daghistan (2016))
