# Validated self-administered questionnaire assessing warfarin–drug, warfarin–food, and counseling knowledge

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A validated self-administered questionnaire developed by Subih Maha et al. for assessing registered nurses' knowledge of warfarin–drug interactions, warfarin–food interactions, and patient counseling practices.
