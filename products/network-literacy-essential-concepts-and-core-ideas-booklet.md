# Network Literacy: Essential Concepts and Core Ideas booklet

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A free, multilingual educational booklet developed collaboratively by network-science researchers, educators, and NetSci High students and teachers to present essential network concepts to high-school graduates and the general public.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Network Literacy: Essential Concepts and Core Ideas booklet**: A free online booklet, published in March 2015 following the "Ocean Literacy" model, that "summarizes in layman's terms what every person who lives in the 21st century should know about networks by the time he/she graduates from high school." It was produced through a yearlong collaborative process including brainstorming sessions with researchers, educators, and NetSci High students and teachers, illustrated with professional visuals, and released at http://tinyurl.com/networkliteracy. As of May 2017 it had been translated into 18 languages entirely on a volunteer basis, indicating, per the authors, overwhelmingly positive worldwide reception. (Hiroki Sayama et al. (2017))
