## Description
An institutional teacher-education framework that integrates educational research, academic and disciplinary teaching, and school practice through interfaculty structures and dual-affiliated education-research chairs.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Professional School of Education model integrating research, teaching and school practice**: The article describes the Professional School of Education (PSE) as an institutional structure for teacher education in which "the main objective of a PSE is to offer a teacher education structure that integrates the fundamental elements of research – (academic) teaching – (school) practice." It is realized at Geneva's IUTE with discipline-based education research chairs dual-affiliated to their faculties, integrating initial and continuous teacher education, and awarding qualifications for secondary levels I and II simultaneously. The article presents it as one of the structural solutions with the highest chances of success, modeled on Stanford's PSE. (Müller (2018))
