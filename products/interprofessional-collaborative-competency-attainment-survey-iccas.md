## Description
The ICCAS is a 21-item retrospective pre-post survey developed by MacDonald et al. to measure health professions students' perceived interprofessional competencies across six domains.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Interprofessional Collaborative Competency Attainment Survey (ICCAS) as a retrospective pre-post measure**: The ICCAS, developed by MacDonald et al., is a 21-item survey measuring perceptions of interprofessional learning, with 20 items rated on a 5-point Likert scale covering six competencies: communication, collaboration, roles and responsibilities, patient-centred approach, conflict management, and team functioning. Each item is rated both before and after the learning activity, "enabling a comparison of students' perceived competencies over time". The article reports strong internal consistency (total Cronbach's alpha 0.919 pre-test, 0.890 post-test) and cites prior validation work reporting alpha coefficients from 0.92 to 0.96. (Gomwe Howard et al. (2026))
