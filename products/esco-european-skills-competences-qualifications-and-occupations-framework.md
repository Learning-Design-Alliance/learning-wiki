# ESCO (European Skills, Competences, Qualifications and Occupations) framework

## Description
A multilingual European classification framework maintained by the European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training (Cedefop) for mapping skills, competences, qualifications, and occupations across education and employment contexts.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ESCO (European Skills, Competences, Qualifications and Occupations) framework as competency mapping tool**: The ESCO framework, developed by the European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training, is the classification system the study adopted to structure and analyse the final transversal skills and competencies. The article states that "Through its standardised multilingual classification of skills and competencies, the ESCO framework ensures consistency, comparability and transferability across European educational and professional contexts." It categorises competencies into six main domains: self-management; social and communication; thinking; core; life; and physical and manual skills. It guided the preliminary identification of competencies and served as a reference throughout the e-Delphi process. (Aleo Giuseppe et al. (2026))
