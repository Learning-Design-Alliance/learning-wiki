# Strategic and Interactive Writing Instruction (SIWI)

## Description
A writing-instruction framework developed by Wolbers and colleagues that combines strategic, interactive, and linguistic/metalinguistic instruction for deaf and hard-of-hearing learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SIWI framework for writing instruction with deaf and hard of hearing students**: SIWI is a framework for writing instruction guided by three major principles: strategic instruction, interactive instruction, and linguistic competence and metalinguistic knowledge. The first two draw on evidence-based practices with hearing writers; the third addresses the specific language needs of d/hh children. Instruction uses guided, co-construction of authentic text with a language zone for repairing communication breakdowns and contrasting ASL and English. (Wolbers et al. (2018))

### Claims
- [SIWI improved discourse-level writing traits in both personal narrative and persuasive genres among grade 3-5 d/hh students](../claims/siwi-writing-trait-gains-elementary-dhh.md) [+M]
- [SIWI improved written language accuracy and complexity outcomes, especially for personal narrative writing](../claims/siwi-written-language-gains-elementary-dhh.md) [+M]
