# SIMPL (System for Improving and Measuring Procedure Learning)

## Description
SIMPL is a smartphone-based workplace assessment application used in U.S. general surgery residencies to record behaviorally anchored ratings of operative autonomy, performance, and case complexity.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SIMPL smartphone-based operative assessment application**: The System for Improving and Measuring Procedure Learning (SIMPL) is a workplace-based, smartphone-based operative assessment application used across US general surgery residencies. "Each SIMPL assessment consists of 3 behaviorally-anchored rating items": a 4-level Zwisch autonomy scale, a 5-level performance scale targeting "practice ready", and a 3-level case complexity item used for sorting. Assessments expire 72 hours after the procedure, and raters complete a 1-hour training session. (Sun Ting et al. (2024))
