# RoSTraC cloud

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A cloud-based learning platform developed for the RoSTraC training programme that provides robotic-surgery theory, instructions, video tutorials, progress uploads, video assessment, and trainee meetings.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **RoSTraC cloud-based learning platform for self-contained training**: The RoSTraC cloud is an interactive file-hosting platform (Google Drive and iCloud) through which "The cloud provides access to theoretical basics of RAS, instructions, and video tutorials of the RoSTraC exercises." Trainees upload learning progress for documentation and assessment, and trainee-created video lectures are added interactively. Regular video meetings complement the platform for sharing progress and analyzing procedure videos. It enables self-contained training at the trainee's own institution without traveling or permanent supervision. (Thomaschewski M et al. (2024))
