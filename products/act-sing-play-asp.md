# Act, Sing, Play (ASP)

## Description
A school-based arts workshop programme developed by Creative Futures for Year 2 pupils, offering weekly beginner strings, singing, or drama sessions delivered by trained freelance tutors.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Act, Sing, Play (ASP) three-arm arts workshop programme for Year 2 pupils**: ASP offered Year 2 pupils weekly 45-minute arts workshops within school hours: violin or cello lessons (ASP-strings), singing lessons loosely based on the Kodály Method (ASP-singing), or drama sessions as an active control (ASP-drama). Class groups of around 30 pupils were randomly split into three workshop groups of approximately 10 pupils each, with sessions running concurrently over 32 weeks. The programme was developed specifically for the trial by Creative Futures and delivered by freelance tutors given broad, non-prescriptive curricula. (Sarah Haywood et al. (2015))

### Claims
- [Music workshops (strings and singing combined) showed no greater impact on maths attainment than drama workshops in a large pupil-level RCT](../claims/asp-music-no-greater-maths-impact-than-drama.md) [+M]
- [Music workshops showed no greater impact on literacy attainment than drama workshops in the same trial](../claims/asp-music-no-greater-literacy-impact-than-drama.md) [+M]
