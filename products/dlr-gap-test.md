## Description
A computerized team-task assessment developed by DLR aviation psychologists that rates leadership, teamwork, communication, stress resistance, awareness, and adherence to procedures using trained-rater behavioural checklists.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Six-domain core competence framework for safety-critical professions (GAP-test domains)**: The article organises core competences for physicians and pilots into six domains assessed by the DLR GAP-test: leadership, teamwork, communication, stress resistance, awareness, and adherence to procedures. Each domain is defined with facets, e.g. awareness "is the ability to remain always cognizant of the surroundings" with facets situational awareness and workload management. The domains were rated by DLR aviation psychologists using empirically derived behaviour checklists on 6-point scales during a computerized team task simulating flight attendant duties. (Harendza Sigrid et al. (2019))
