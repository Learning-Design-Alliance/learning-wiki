# Updated 2002 UN education curricula for four space disciplines

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A multilingual United Nations curriculum package covering satellite meteorology and global climate, satellite communications, space and atmospheric science, and remote sensing and geographic information systems, produced for implementation by the UN-affiliated regional centres and beyond.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Updated 2002 UN education curricula for four space disciplines, available in the six official UN languages**: Curricula and course syllabuses updated at the 2001 Frascati Expert Meeting organized by the UN Office for Outer Space Affairs with the European Space Agency. The Meeting "updated the four education curricula and drew up course syllabuses that differ from most of those available in literature and on the World Wide Web." They were made available in 2002 in Arabic, Chinese, English, French, Russian and Spanish, on request, as United Nations documents, for implementation at the regional centres and beyond. (Hans J. Haubold (2002))
