Description¶

A Georgia Tech dataset of manually proofread LaTeX-converted student and reference solutions for undergraduate circuit-analysis homework, created by Liangliang Chen et al. to evaluate automated assessment of solution completeness, methods, answers, arithmetic, and units.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • GPT-4o frequently makes characteristic errors in homework assessment, including failing to recognize unit and numeric equivalences, marking complete solutions incomplete, and mishandling alternative methods and notations [+W]
  • GPT-4o shows more consistent performance than Llama 3 70B across topics and metrics, while Llama 3 70B falls below 60% on the unit metric for three advanced topics [+W]
  • Llama 3 70B outperforms GPT-4o on completeness and method metrics due to its leniency, but performs significantly worse on the unit metric [+W]
  • Sinusoidal steady-state analysis is the most challenging topic for both GPT-4o and Llama 3 70B in homework assessment [+W]
  • GPT-4o and Llama 3 70B significantly outperform GPT-3.5 Turbo across all five homework-assessment metrics in circuit analysis [+W]
