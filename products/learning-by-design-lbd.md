## Description
A project- and design-based middle-school science curriculum developed by the Learning by Design project, using iterative engineering challenges, collaborative activity structures, and supporting artifacts to teach scientific concepts and practices.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Learning by Design (LBD) project-based middle school science curriculum with iterative design challenges and embedded activity structures**: Learning by Design is a project- and design-based, iteratively structured middle school science curriculum merging Case-based Reasoning with Problem/Project-based learning, built around engineering design challenges such as building powered vehicles. Students make multiple passes through the same concepts in shifting contexts, supported by artifacts including whiteboards, Design Diaries, and Rules of Thumb as intermediate abstractions, and by activity structures such as Pin-up Sessions and Gallery Walks. The article reports it "promotes not only conceptual knowledge but also deep learning of scientific practices and explicit reflection on productive learning strategies at the same time", and has been evaluated with some two dozen teachers and 3500 students. (Camp (2010))
