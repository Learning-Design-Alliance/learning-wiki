## Description
Moodle is an open-source learning management system developed and maintained by Moodle Pty Ltd and its global community for delivering, organizing, communicating about, and assessing online learning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Moodle LMS platform for SR/MA course delivery**: The proposed course utilizes the Moodle learning management system (LMS) platform for distributing training materials and assignments, enabling communication and feedback. The article states Moodle is recognized for its effectiveness in collaborative online learning and assessments, and enables a clear overview of course structure, supports synchronous and asynchronous communication, and provides a centralized hub for assignment submission and evaluation. (El Ansari Walid et al. (2025))
- **MOODLE-based enrichment activity set for activity-based blended learning**: The experimental condition's instructional component: a MOODLE course management environment in which both groups completed shared activities (concept maps, ASSURE model application, presentations, videos, research papers, reflection), while the experimental group additionally completed enrichment activities such as "critical thinking in an online discussion", online quizzes, creating their own ASSURE model, presentations with Prezi, PresentationTube, PowToon and Emaze, videos with Camtasia and VideoStudio, and evaluating materials using rubrics. Resources and instructions were linked into MOODLE before the course started. (Ahmed Yousif Abdelraheem et al. (2015))
