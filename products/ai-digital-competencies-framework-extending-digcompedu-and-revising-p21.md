# AI digital competencies framework extending DigCompEdu and revising P21

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A framework proposed by Ng and colleagues that extends DigCompEdu and revises P21 to organize AI-related competencies for higher-education teachers' teaching, learning, and assessment practices.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Four-domain conceptual framework for teachers' AI competency**: The article proposes a new framework extending DigCompEdu, P21's framework, and Ng et al.'s four cognition domains model, organizing teachers' AI competency into four domains: "Teacher professional engagement", "Instructional design", "Content choices" across disciplines, and "Learning competencies". It frames AI competency as "a non-technical, critical and complex competency that young people need to learn to manipulate AI technologies ethically, safely and wisely", not merely technological skill. The framework is offered as a guide for instructional design and professional development in the post-pandemic context. (Davy Tsz Kit Ng et al. (2023))
