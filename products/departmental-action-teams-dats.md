# Departmental Action Teams (DATs)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Departmental Action Teams are department-scoped faculty collaborative teams, supported by departmental institutionalization, that work on shared educational issues and engage in scholarship of teaching and learning to promote local cultural change.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Departmental Action Teams (DATs) as a mechanism for local cultural change**: DATs consist of faculty working collaboratively on a shared issue of departmental interest. Like faculty learning communities, "DAT faculty have agency to choose the educational issue they will work on, and learning and community are considered central", but DATs differ in focusing on a common, shared issue within a single department. DATs embody the six core commitments and engage faculty in scholarship of teaching and learning; the authors see them as a mechanism for local cultural change that must be institutionalized through departmental support to be sustained. (Reinholz et al. (2014))
