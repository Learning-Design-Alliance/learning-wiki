# ETOILE and MIMIC multi-agent tutoring systems

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
ETOILE and MIMIC are multi-agent intelligent tutoring systems that coordinate pedagogical agents embodying different educational theories, developed by the article's authors and Baylor for instructional support.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Multi-agent tutoring systems ETOILE and MIMIC deploy several pedagogical agents embodying different educational theories**: The article describes two multi-agent systems. The ETOILE system consists of five teaching agents named Skinner, Bloom, Vygotsky, Piaget, and Papert, each taking a different approach "according to educational psychology principles," plus a coach agent that manages their roles and interactions with the learner. Baylor's MIMIC (Multiple Intelligent Mentors Instructing Collaboratively) system uses three pedagogical agents from three theoretical perspectives to teach instructional design to pre-service teachers, with each agent interacting with the learner and the other agents. (D. Sánchez-Guzmán et al. (2009))
