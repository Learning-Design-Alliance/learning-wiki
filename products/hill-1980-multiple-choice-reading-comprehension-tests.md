# Hill (1980) multiple-choice reading comprehension tests

## Description
A pair of multiple-choice English reading-comprehension tests developed by Hill (1980) for measuring learners' proficiency and achievement.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Hill (1980) multiple-choice reading comprehension tests as proficiency and achievement instruments**: The study used multiple-choice reading comprehension tests developed by Hill (1980). A 20-question proficiency test served to homogenize students, and eight multiple-choice reading comprehension passages with 40 selected questions served as pre-test and post-test. Reliability was .76 for the pre-test and .78 for the post-test via the K-R 21 formula, computed on a 40-student pilot sample. (Khansir et al. (2014))
