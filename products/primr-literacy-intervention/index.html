PRIMR literacy intervention¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 3 claims (2 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

Description¶

PRIMR is a structured bilingual early-grade literacy programme providing scripted lessons, aligned low-cost books and instructional aids, teacher training, and coaching support for Kenyan Grade 1 and 2 pupils.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • One year of PRIMR produced statistically significant gains in oral reading fluency in grade 1 formal and nonformal schools and grade 2 nonformal schools, in both English and Kiswahili, but not in grade 2 formal schools [+M]
  • PRIMR increased the percentage of pupils meeting the Ministry of Education's oral reading fluency benchmarks after one year [+M]
  • PRIMR's first-year effects on reading comprehension were positive but inconsistent across language, grade, and school type [~M]
