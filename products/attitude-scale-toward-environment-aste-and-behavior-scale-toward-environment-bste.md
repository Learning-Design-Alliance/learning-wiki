# Attitude Scale toward Environment (ASTE) and Behavior Scale toward Environment (BSTE)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
A pair of Likert-type self-report instruments developed by Atasoy and Ertürk and Yavetz and Pe'er, respectively, for measuring students' environmental attitudes and behaviors.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Attitude Scale toward Environment (ASTE) and Behavior Scale toward Environment (BSTE) measurement instruments**: The study measured outcomes with two Likert-type instruments. The ASTE consists of 25 items developed by Atasoy and Ertürk (2008), scored from Completely agree=5 to Completely disagree=1, with a lowest score of 25 and highest of 125. The BSTE is composed of 20 items developed by Yavetz and Pe'er (2006), adapted into Turkish, scored from Always=5 to Never=1, with a highest score of 100. Both were administered as pre-test, post-test and follow-up test. (Calis et al. (2020))
