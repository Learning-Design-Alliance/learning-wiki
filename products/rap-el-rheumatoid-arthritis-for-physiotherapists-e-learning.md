# RAP-eL (Rheumatoid Arthritis for Physiotherapists e-Learning)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
RAP-eL is an online modular e-learning package developed for physiotherapy students and practitioners to learn evidence-based rheumatoid arthritis management and related clinical skills.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **RAP-eL: an online e-learning package for physiotherapists on rheumatoid arthritis management within a chronic disease management framework**: RAP-eL (Rheumatoid Arthritis for Physiotherapists e-Learning) is a modular-based online e-learning package "designed for physiotherapists to increase their disease-related knowledge and clinical skills regarding the safe and effective management of rheumatoid arthritis". It delivers contemporary evidence for physiotherapy management of RA and integrates it with practice-relevant clinical skills, using real world clinical scenarios, problem-based and asynchronous learning, screening and diagnosis instruments, evidence summaries, clinical skills demonstrations and consumer narratives. In this study it served as the concrete operational example students discussed. (Gardner P et al. (2016))
