Modeling instruction¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A research-based, guided-inquiry science-teaching pedagogy in which students collaboratively develop, test, defend, and deploy models, supported by teacher facilitation and professional training.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Students taught with modeling instruction within the inverted curriculum scored highest on the ACT science subscore (M=24.3) compared with the inverted-curriculum-alone (M=23.7) and traditional (M=23.1) contexts [+M]
