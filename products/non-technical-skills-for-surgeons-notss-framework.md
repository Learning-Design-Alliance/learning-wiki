## Description
A validated behavioral taxonomy and rating framework for assessing surgical leadership, situation awareness, decision-making, and communication/teamwork, used by surgical educators and trained raters.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **NOTSS as the predominant validated framework for assessing surgical leadership and non-technical skills**: The Non-Technical Skills for Surgeons (NOTSS) framework is a behavioral taxonomy rating leadership, situation awareness, decision-making, and communication/teamwork, used in more than two-thirds of the reviewed studies either directly or as a derived system. The review states that "The predominance of NOTSS in particular underscores its acceptance as a reliable framework for evaluating leadership and decision-making in surgical teams." Frameworks such as NOTSS, NOTECHS, and CRM-based models supported standardized measurement and organized feedback, aiding interrater reliability and reproducibility of non-technical skills training. (Gulnaz Nadia et al. (2026))
- **NOTSS framework applied at the surgical team level with four nontechnical skill categories**: The Non-Technical Skills for Surgeons (NOTSS) behavioral rating system, here applied to the collective performance of the whole surgical team rather than an individual surgeon. The article states raters "assessed overall teams’ nontechnical skills along 12 items (behavioral elements) grouped in the following 4 categories: situational awareness, decision-making, communication and teamwork, and leadership." Each item uses a 4-point Likert scale from poor to good performance, and scores are averaged across raters per case. The framework serves as the ground-truth criterion against which the automated motion features were validated. (Harari Rayan Ebnali PhD et al. (2024))
- **Non-technical skills framework: social skills, cognitive skills and personal resource factors**: The review defines non-technical skills as "the cognitive and social abilities that complement a clinicians' technical ability", generally comprising decision making, leadership, team work, and situational awareness. It organises them into three categories: "social skills (leadership, communication and teamwork), cognitive skills (decision making and situational awareness), and personal resource factors", the latter covering coping with stressors and fatigue. Table 1 gives a description for each skill, and the review argues NTS deficits are a major cause of surgical error, motivating training and assessment across the framework's components. (Griffin Cora et al. (2019))
- **NOTSS: the non-technical skills for surgeons behavior rating system**: NOTSS is a behavior rating system proposed by Yule and colleagues for assessing surgeons' non-technical skills in the operating room. Based on surgical education programs at the University of Aberdeen, Scotland which "observe, rate and provide feedback" on desirable OR skills, it groups non-technical skills into four categories: situation awareness, decision-making, communication and teamwork, and leadership. A "Task Management" category was later dropped after further analysis and its elements incorporated into the other categories. (Tarkowski Radoslaw (2015))
