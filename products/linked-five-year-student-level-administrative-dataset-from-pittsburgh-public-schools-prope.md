## Description
The study team built a linked administrative dataset covering five academic years of student-level records from three agencies: Pittsburgh Public Schools, Propel Schools, and the Allegheny County Department of Human Services. The article states the team "collected and linked five academic years of student-level administrative data" from these entities, with each entity providing any available data on enrolled students for 2012/13–2016/17. This linked dataset is the evidentiary basis for the study's descriptive and predictive analyses of near-term academic risks.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Data-sharing agreements across school districts and the county human services agency enabling student-level record linkage
#### Constraints
- Coverage is limited to students enrolled in the participating local education agencies in 2015/16 or 2016/17 and to data those entities provided for 2012/13–2016/17

### Target Learners
- K-12 students enrolled in Pittsburgh Public Schools and Propel Schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Early identification of students at near-term academic risk
