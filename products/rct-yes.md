## Description
RCT-YES is a free, Institute of Education Sciences-funded software package that implements design-based methods for estimating impacts in randomized controlled trials and related experimental and non-experimental designs.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **RCT-YES software package for design-based impact estimation**: RCT-YES is a free software package funded by the Institute of Education Sciences that implements design-based methods for estimating impacts in randomized controlled trials. The report describes it as "a free, IES-funded software package that applies design-based methods to a range of experimental and non-experimental designs," and the study uses it to conduct all design-based re-analyses of the nine education RCTs. Its default settings encode a particular set of assumptions, including equal weighting of clusters and the finite-population framework. (Kautz et al. (2017))
