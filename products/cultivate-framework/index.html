Cultivate framework¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (1 associational, 1 design), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A classroom-learning-conditions and student-beliefs framework, supported by the Cultivate Survey and playbook, that the University of Chicago Consortium on School Research provides for school teams to interpret data and improve learning and well-being.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Students' survey reports of their school experiences predict later academic and well-being outcomes including attendance, grades, test scores, graduation, college enrollment, and well-being [+M]
  • Each individual learning condition predicts I am Valued gains, with Classroom Community the largest (15.0 middle grades, 16.9 high school percentile points) [+W]
  • All nine Cultivate learning condition measures show strong Rasch person reliability (0.82-0.87) in CPS administrations [+W]
  • Within-year improvements in overall learning conditions predict large gains in students' I am Valued beliefs [+W]
