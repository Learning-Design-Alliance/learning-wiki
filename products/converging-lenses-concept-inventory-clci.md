# Converging Lenses Concept Inventory (CLCI)

## Description
The CLCI is a 15-item multiple-choice conceptual assessment of converging-lens image formation developed by Wörner et al. for middle school physics learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Converging Lenses Concept Inventory (CLCI), a 15-item multiple-choice test of image formation by converging lenses for middle school**: The CLCI is a test instrument assessing middle school students' conceptual understanding of image formation by a converging lens, usable as formative or summative assessment. The authors state: "we present the development and validation of a test instrument in the field of optics, the Converging Lenses Concept Inventory (CLCI)". It began with 16 items; one item (Q2) was removed after quality checks, yielding a 15-item version scored with partial credit (0–2 points per item). (Wörner et al. (2022))
