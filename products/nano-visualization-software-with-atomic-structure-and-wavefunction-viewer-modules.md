# Nano-visualization software with atomic-structure and wavefunction-viewer modules

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A free MATLAB-based program developed by Lillian C. Oetting and colleagues that provides atomic-structure and wavefunction visualization modules for nanotechnology education.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Nano-visualization software with atomic-structure and wavefunction-viewer modules for high-school and undergraduate nanotechnology education**: A MATLAB-based nano-visualization program packaged with a graphical user interface, offering "a level of simplicity appealing to the general user". It has two independent modules: an atomic visualization module producing ball-and-stick schematics, and a wavefunction viewer module that plots atomic and molecular wavefunctions using the extended Hückel theory (EHT) method. The authors offer it "free of charge for research and educational purposes". (Lillian C. Oetting et al. (2013))
