# Six-construct instructor e-learning acceptance questionnaire

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A five-point Likert questionnaire measuring university instructors' e-learning acceptance across self-efficacy, educational quality, technological complexity, perceived usefulness, perceived ease of use, and behavioral intention.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Six-construct instructor e-learning acceptance questionnaire (299 respondents, Nigerian universities)**: A structured questionnaire measuring six constructs — self-efficacy, educational quality, technological complexity, perceived usefulness, perceived ease of use, and behavioral intention — using a five-point Likert scale with three indicators per construct adapted from prior studies (e.g., Mohammadi 2015; Davis 1986). It was administered to instructors of three private schools across three geopolitical zones in Nigeria; "299 respondents (90.60%) were included in this study". Factor loadings ranged from 0.74 to 0.92, AVE from 0.589 to 0.802, and Cronbach's alpha from 0.651 to 0.876. (Patience E. Idoga et al. (2022))
