## Description
A Canadian occupational-therapy competency framework defining professional domains, competencies, and indicators for education, fieldwork, and practice.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Six-domain competency codebook drawn from the Competencies for Occupational Therapists in Canada (2021) used to analyze fieldwork learning**: The study deductively coded interview transcripts using the domains, competencies, and indicators of the Competencies for Occupational Therapists in Canada, operationalized in a codebook of six domains: OT expertise, excellence in practice, communication and collaboration, engagement with the profession, culture, equity and justice, and professional responsibility. For example, excellence in practice "Views OTs as lifelong learners, regularly self-evaluating and cognizant of external modifiers." Two researchers coded and resolved discrepancies to consensus. (Gill Monique et al. (2024))
