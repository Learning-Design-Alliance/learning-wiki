Open Practices Disclosure form¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes

Description¶

A journal-run disclosure and badge framework that authors use to document public data, materials, and preregistered research plans for recognition of open scientific practices.

Components¶

  • Open Practices Disclosure form with badges for open data, open materials, and preregistered research plans: An Open Practices Disclosure form through which manuscripts can earn badges that "recognize open scientific practices: publicly available data, material, or preregistered research plans". Authors check the desired badge boxes and supply a permanent access path and confirmations for each badge; qualifying repositories include the Open Science Framework (OSF), Dataverse networks, and registries such as ClinicalTrials.gov. The form also permits an alternative note explaining circumstances where public sharing is not advisable. (Sarah Grünthal et al. (2024))
  • Open Practices Disclosure form for claiming open-science badges for data, materials, and preregistration: The Open Practices Disclosure is a manuscript form through which authors document open scientific practices. Articles are eligible to earn badges, and the form states that these "recognize open scientific practices: publicly available data, material, or preregistered research plans". Authors check boxes for desired badges and supply repository paths, confirmatory statements, registration details, and explanations of any changes or omissions. (Supplemental Material for Validation Processes and Reading Purpose: Is Validation Against )
  • Award open-science badges only for information deposited in permanent, public, open-access repositories: This principle sets the depository condition for open-practice recognition: the disclosure form instructs that "To qualify for a badge, you must provide a URL, doi, or other permanent path for accessing the specified information in a public, open-access repository" and that the information must be time-stamped, immutable, and permanent. It works by restricting badge eligibility to committed archival repositories. (Supplemental Material for Validation Processes and Reading Purpose: Is Validation Against )
