# Arabic versions of the PE-ARCS and PE-MS questionnaires

## Description
A bilingual-translated and Saudi-context-adapted questionnaire battery measuring autonomy, competence, relatedness, and motivational regulation among students learning physical education online.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Arabic versions of the PE-ARCS and PE-MS questionnaires for online PE learning perceptions**: The study used 12 items from the PE autonomy relatedness competence (PE-ARCS) scale and nine PE motivation (PE-MS) items from Sulz et al., translated into Arabic and adapted to the Saudi context through bilingual translation and back-translation. The authors report the Arabic versions "were found to be both reliable and valid for measuring PNS and motivations, respectively", with factor analyses supporting three components per scale and satisfactory internal reliability. Responses were given on a 5-point Likert scale. (Frikha Mohamed et al (2024))
