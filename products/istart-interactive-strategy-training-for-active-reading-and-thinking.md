## Description
An intelligent tutoring system developed by McNamara and colleagues that trains students in reading-comprehension self-explanation strategies through lessons, demonstrations, game-based practice, and automated natural-language feedback.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **iSTART: Intelligent tutoring system for self-explanation training**: iSTART (Interactive Strategy Training for Active Reading and Thinking) is an intelligent tutoring system designed to improve reading comprehension through self-explanation training. It teaches five strategies: "comprehension monitoring, paraphrasing, prediction, elaboration, and bridging" through video lessons, demonstration, and game-based practice. During practice, a natural language processing algorithm provides feedback on self-explanation quality. The game-based version includes identification games (e.g., Balloon Bust) and generative games (Map Conquest and Showdown). (McCarthy et al. (2017))
- **iSTART intelligent tutoring system trains reading comprehension through self-explanation and five comprehension strategies**: iSTART is an intelligent tutoring system designed to enhance students' reading comprehension skills through instruction on self-explanation and reading comprehension strategies. Within iSTART, "there are a total of five strategies: comprehension monitoring, predicting, paraphrasing, elaborating, and bridging", each explained in a five-minute video followed by a checkpoint quiz. The system includes instructional videos with animated agents, Coached Practice with scaffolded feedback, and a game-based practice interface with generative games (Coached Practice, Showdown, Map Conquest) and identification games. Self-explanations are scored 0 to 3 by an algorithm using Latent Semantic Analysis and word-based measures. (Weston-Sementelli et al. (2016))
