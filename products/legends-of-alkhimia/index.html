Legends of Alkhimia¶

Description¶

An educational multiplayer chemistry game and eight-session inquiry curriculum developed by Yam San Chee and colleagues for lower-secondary students.

Components¶

  • Legends of Alkhimia multiplayer game and associated curriculum: Legends of Alkhimia is an educational multiplayer game for 13- to 15-year-olds, an eight-session chemistry curriculum for lower secondary school science designed to foster learning of chemistry through inquiry. "7KH PXOWLSOD\HU JDPH VXSSRUWV IRXU FRQFXUUHQW SOD\HUV" on personal computers connected via a local area network, typically in a school computer laboratory. It embeds students in problem-solving challenges related to the use of chemistry in realistic contexts and requires individual laboratory work using an in-game virtual chemistry lab. As used in the study, "/HJHQGV RI $ONKLPLD FRPSULVHV VL[ OHYHOV RI JDPH SOD\", with levels building incrementally and a narrative arc providing coherence to the whole gameplay experience. (Yam San Chee et al. (2010))
