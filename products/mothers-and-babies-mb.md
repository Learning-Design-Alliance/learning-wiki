## Description
A six-week CBT-based perinatal depression prevention intervention delivered individually or in groups by paraprofessionals or mental-health professionals, with instructor and participant manuals.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Mothers and Babies (MB) group intervention**: Mothers and Babies is a CBT-based perinatal depression preventive intervention deliverable one-on-one or in groups. The group modality runs six weeks with sessions of 60–120 minutes covering "monitoring and scheduling pleasant activities," reframing harmful thought patterns, and developing support systems. An instructor manual provides a script and key points, and a participant manual contains activities for during and after sessions. (Diebold et al. (2020))
