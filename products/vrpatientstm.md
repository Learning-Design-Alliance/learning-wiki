# VRPatients™

## Description
VRPatients™ is a virtual-patient simulation platform for clinical training delivered through standalone VR headsets and developed by a multidisciplinary team of clinicians, communication specialists, and instructional designers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **VRPatients™ virtual patient simulation course on Meta Quest 3 with pre-briefing, guided familiarization and instructor-led debriefing**: A modular VR-based training programme built on the VRPatients™ platform and delivered through Meta Quest 3 headsets, developed by a multidisciplinary team of clinicians, communication specialists and instructional designers. Each block began with a “90-minute pre-briefing” covering objectives, clinical concepts and vocabulary, followed by role-play, 15 minutes of headset familiarization, a 15-minute interactive simulation, and a “60-minute instructor-led debriefing”. Two virtual patient scenarios were used: an elderly woman with acute breathing difficulties and a trauma case with a gunshot wound. (Dávidovics A et al. (2025))

### Claims
- [In a pilot VR virtual patient course, medical students’ self-reported communication confidence rose from 3.0 to 4.5 and decision-making confidence from 2.9 to 3.8 on matched pre-post questionnaires](../claims/vr-pilot-communication-decision-confidence-gains.md) [+M]
- [Perceived realism of virtual patient scenarios and comfort interacting in VR rose substantially from pre to post (realism 2.9 to 4.4; VR comfort 3.0 to 4.6)](../claims/vr-pilot-realism-comfort-gains.md) [+M]
