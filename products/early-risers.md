## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Four-component intervention model combining child skills, school support, parent skills, and family support**: Early Risers organizes its intervention as four coordinated components. The Child Skills component teaches skills that enhance children's emotional and behavioral self-regulation, positive peer relationships, and academic success. The Child School Support component identifies areas of difficulty in the classroom and creates individualized plans addressed during normal school activities. The Parent Skills component is delivered in family night group sessions teaching positive parent-child relations, effective discipline, and school involvement, while the Family Support component uses home visits to identify basic needs and health concerns and implement plans for healthy lifestyles. (Early Risers (2012))

# Early Risers: a multi-year prevention program for young children with early aggressive and disruptive behavior

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 5 claims (3 for, 2 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 5 claims rest on one study

## Description
Early Risers is a multi-year prevention program for elementary school children ages 6 to 12 with aggressive or otherwise disruptive behavior. It uses integrated child-, school-, and family-focused interventions aimed at altering the developmental trajectory of children with early onset aggressive behavior, generally including social skills groups, a summer school program, teacher consultation and mentoring, and parent training. A family advocate with a bachelor's degree coordinates the child- and family-focused components, consulting with classroom teachers during the regular school year. The total annual cost is approximately $1,500 to $2,500 per student.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A family advocate (someone with a bachelor's degree and experience working with children/parents) coordinates the child- and family-focused components
- Staff members are required to participate in a formal program of education and training prior to implementing each intervention component
#### Constraints
- The program is a fluid intervention with modifications and improvements made continuously based on research results

### Target Learners
- Elementary school children ages 6 to 12 with aggressive or otherwise disruptive behavior

### Target Learning Goals
- Emotional and behavioral self-regulation
- Positive peer relationships
- Academic success
