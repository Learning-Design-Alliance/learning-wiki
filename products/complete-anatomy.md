## Description
Complete Anatomy is 3D4Medical's commercially available mobile augmented-reality anatomy application for exploring interactive anatomical models on tablet devices.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Complete Anatomy mobile AR feature on iPad as evaluated by anatomy educators**: Complete Anatomy (CA) version 10.4 by 3D4Medical from Elsevier was chosen as "an example of a professional and commercially available AR application designed for learning human anatomy." Used on an Apple iPad 10th Gen 10.9″ purchased for $649AUD, it presented three AR models: a human skull with explode feature and infographic, a partially dissected body with beating heart, and a skeleton featuring primary vasculature. Key features included 'explode' which separates anatomical components, a 'beating heart' demonstrating cardiac function, and informatic displays. (Williams A et al. (2026))
- **Complete Anatomy 3D lumbar spine models with dynamic annotation for spinal surgery instruction**: The study used Complete Anatomy 3D software to generate anatomical models integrating 3D visualization with clinical anatomy education. The software "is equipped with a dynamic annotation system, layer-by-layer dissection features, 360-degree rotation capabilities, and cross-sectional 3D reconstruction". A lumbar spine model built from bone tissue, intervertebral discs, and spinal nerves illustrates vertebral body, disc, and nerve-root correspondence, supports MRI/CT learning via cross-sectional models, and animated videos show progression of lumbar spinal stenosis and herniated disc disease. (Li Wenbo et al. (2026))
