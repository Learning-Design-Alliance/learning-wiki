# Level Up!

## Description
Level Up! is a Moodle plug-in developed by Brickfield Education Labs that adds configurable points, levels, progress tracking, and leaderboards to online courses.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Level Up! Moodle plug-in configured as an endeavour-based points, levels and leaderboard system**: Level Up! is a Moodle plug-in through which "students can gain points by participating in the course via Moodle", with customisable rules, levels and points. In this study points were "endeavour-based and not merit-based", awarded for completing pre-lecture activities, tutorial questions, lab materials and assessed quizzes, with full points only before the associated session and half-points thereafter. Students see a block showing their level, points, progress and rank, plus an anonymous individual leaderboard and an optional team leaderboard by tutorial group. (Eilidh Jack et al. (2024))
