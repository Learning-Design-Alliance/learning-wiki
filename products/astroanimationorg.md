# AstroAnimation.org

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
AstroAnimation.org is an online collection created by the AstroAnimation program containing freely downloadable student animations of astronomy research for public outreach and educational use.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **AstroAnimation.org collection of forty-seven freely available student animations**: A released online artifact collecting the student films produced in the program. As the authors state, "As of early 2021, there are forty-seven animations covering a range of topics"; each film is typically about two minutes long and freely available for download under a licence allowing non-commercial use. The animations have been featured at science fiction conventions, art festivals, a STEAM festival, public outreach presentations, and in college and university science classes. (Arcadias et al. (2021))
