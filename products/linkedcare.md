## Description
Linkedcare is an AI-assisted cephalometric analysis platform with automatic landmark recognition that supports orthodontic learners in reviewing landmarks and obtaining measurement scores.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Linkedcare AI-assisted cephalometric platform with automatic landmark recognition**: An AI-assisted cephalometric analysis platform (Linkedcare, Shanghai, China) used by clinical training students to analyze skeletal, dental, and soft tissue indicators. "The system provided an initial set of automatically identified landmark positions, which students reviewed and adjusted based on their judgment." After submission it automatically generated specific scores for each case, enabling comparison of analysis accuracy across student grades. (Lin Jiayu et al. (2025))
