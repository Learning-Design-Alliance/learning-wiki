## Components

- **GPT-2 Output Detector Demo as an AI-text identification tool**: The GPT-2 Output Detector Demo is a language-analysis tool the article names as designed to analyse the language of written work and identify patterns or irregularities indicating chatbot authorship. In the authors' December 2022 trial, ten genuine student essays scored under 1% fake probability while ChatGPT essays on the same subjects scored close to 100%, and prompting changes only lowered this to around 97%. (Debby R. E. Cotton et al. (2024))
