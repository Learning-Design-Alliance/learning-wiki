## Description
A co-delivered domestic-violence and child-safeguarding training programme for general-practice teams, developed and delivered by paired healthcare and children's social-work professionals.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **RESPONDS pilot training on domestic violence and child safeguarding for general practice teams**: The RESPONDS training is a 2-hour safeguarding level 3 session delivered to whole general practice teams on their premises by two co-trainers, one healthcare professional and one local children's social work professional. Its content covered issues including "linking DVA and CS in practice" and "holding difficult conversations about DVA and speaking directly with children", plus responding to disclosure, the child protection referral process, working with other professionals, and record keeping. Teaching was interactive with discussion and reflection, built around a film of a GP consulting a depressed mother concerned about her teenage son, interspersed with expert narratives. Fidelity was supported by a train-the-trainer event. (Lewis Natalia V. et al. (2017))
