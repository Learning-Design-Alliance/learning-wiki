38-item engagement strategies survey instrument¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A 38-item self-report survey developed and used by Martin et al. to measure online higher-education students' perceived benefits of learner-to-learner, learner-to-instructor, and learner-to-content engagement strategies.
