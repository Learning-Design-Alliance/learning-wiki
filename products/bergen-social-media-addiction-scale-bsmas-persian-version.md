# Bergen Social Media Addiction Scale (BSMAS), Persian version

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An 18-item, six-component Likert-scale questionnaire measuring social-media addiction, using a Persian translation validated by the study authors for university-student research and screening.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Bergen Social Media Addiction Scale (BSMAS), Persian version, as the addiction measurement instrument**: The BSMAS is an 18-question questionnaire covering six addiction components, each measured by three questions, scored on a 5-point Likert scale from very rarely to very often, with a minimum score of 18 and a maximum of 90. The article used it as the main study instrument alongside a personal information form. The Persian version was psychometrically validated on 2676 students, and in this study its internal consistency was 0.88 by Cronbach's Alpha, described as "the reliability coefficient of the questionnaire for internal consistency was 0.88". (Azizi Seyyed Mohsen et al. (2019))
