# RAVE intervention

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 10 claims (6 for, 1 mixed, 3 against) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q2`–`q3` · 2 of 2 report an effect size · 9 claims rest on one study

## Description
A scripted academic-vocabulary curriculum developed by McKeown et al. that teaches middle-school students word meanings, contextual use, access fluency, and morphology through varied authentic contexts.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **RAVE instructional design targets five theoretical aspects of word knowledge**: The RAVE intervention teaches general academic words from the AWL through instruction providing multiple varied contexts, active processing, and Latin-root morphology. The authors designed activities around five key aspects: "polysemy around a core meaning", enriching semantic representations through patterns of use, building fluency of access, direct focus on integrating word meaning and context, and morphology. Introductions pair authentic contexts with a "friendly definition" built around a core meaning that unites various senses of the word. (McKeown et al. (2018))
