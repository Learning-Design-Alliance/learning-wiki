## Description
A 345-question Japanese pharmacist licensing examination administered by Japan and reused by researchers to benchmark generative AI performance on pharmacy knowledge and problem solving.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **107th Japanese National License Examination for Pharmacists as a benchmark for evaluating generative AI in pharmacy**: The 107th JNLEP is a 345-question, Japanese-language, multiple-choice pharmacist licensing examination (held February 2022) organized into 9 subject categories including physics, chemistry, biology, pharmacology, and practice, with a passing threshold of "an overall accuracy of at least 62.9%". The authors administered the complete question set in original order to each of 18 OC-LLMs, uploading images where permitted, and compared outputs with publicly available correct answers. They propose it as a reusable benchmark: evaluating future models on it "will enable their comparison with previous models". (Sato Hiroyasu et al. (2025))
