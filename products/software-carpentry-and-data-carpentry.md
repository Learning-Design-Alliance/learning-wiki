## Description
Open, customizable training curricula and instructor-training programmes developed and delivered under The Carpentries organization to teach foundational software development, data analysis, and related research practices.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Software Carpentry and Data Carpentry as foundational training resources**: Software Carpentry and Data Carpentry are two parts of The Carpentries organization that collaboratively build and teach basic concepts of software development and data analysis. "The materials that they develop and use are open, and can be customized for scie nce domains (e.g., HEP), or participant groups (e.g., undergraduates)." Their model offers training of trainers, after which graduated trainers can offer training under the SC/DC names, though anyone may use the materials without that branding. (Dario Berzano et al. (2018))
