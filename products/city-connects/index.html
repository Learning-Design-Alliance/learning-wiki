City Connects¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes

Description¶

City Connects is a systemic, tailored student-support programme run by City Connects that coordinates school and community resources to address elementary students' academic, social-emotional, health, and family needs.

Components¶

  • City Connects systemic student support intervention: City Connects is a theoretically-guided, evidence-based approach to addressing out-of-school barriers to learning in high-poverty urban elementary schools, implemented in 72 schools across four states. The intervention is "(a) systemic, optimizing resources and structures already present in the school" and "(b) tailored, identifying each and every student's strengths and needs in academic, social-emotional, physical and family domains." A full-time Masters'-trained Coordinator meets yearly with each classroom teacher to review every student, connects students with tailored community-based supports, tracks plans in a web-based tool, and monitors service quality using standardized fidelity tools. (O'Dwyer et al. (2016))
  • City Connects school-based student support intervention: City Connects is an evidence-based approach to school-based student support developed in 1999 that partners with community agencies to promote strengths and meet needs of students in high-poverty urban schools. A full-time School Site Coordinator in each school conducts a Whole Class Review with teachers, documenting each student's strengths and needs across "four domains (academic, social/emotional/behavioral, health, and family)", then connects students with a tailored set of prevention, intervention, and enrichment services tracked in a Web-based system. By 2014-15 it was implemented in 63 schools in Massachusetts, Ohio, and New York. (Mary Walsh et al. (2015))
  • City Connects theory of change: systematic student support builds beliefs and attitudes that produce lasting academic effects: The City Connects theory of change holds that implementing student support as a core school function, with a full-time Site Coordinator collaborating with every teacher to identify each student's strengths and needs across academic, social-emotional, health, and family domains, creates an environment where students' strengths and needs are addressed. The intervention is predicted to improve academic effort, behavior, and work habits, which in turn lead to improved academic achievement not just in elementary school but after students leave the program. The authors state that "effective student support interventions should lead to benefits that persist even after students have left the intervention, because they strengthen beliefs and attitudes that underlie achievement." The theory is depicted in Appendix B, Figure 1. (Walsh et al. (2015))
  • City Connects systematic student support intervention with Site Coordinator and Practice Manual: City Connects is a systematic student support intervention currently implemented in 63 schools in Massachusetts, Ohio, and New York. At its core is a full-time Site Coordinator, a Masters'-trained school counselor or social worker, who collaborates with every classroom teacher to identify strengths and needs across four domains, connects students to a tailored set of community-based support services and enrichment opportunities, documents and tracks the service plan for each student, and follows up to assure service delivery and assess effectiveness. The model is codified in a Practice Manual with accompanying training and tools, including a proprietary electronic database for tracking service referral and delivery. (Walsh et al. (2015))

Claims¶

  • City Connects participation is associated with a 0.15 SD advantage in grade 8 mathematics achievement [+M]
  • Comparative regression discontinuity finds positive City Connects effects on Grade 3 and 5 math and reading, with Cohen's effect sizes of approximately .20 to .28 [+M]
  • City Connects shows strong positive impacts on middle school MCAS ELA, Math, and GPA in almost every grade [+M]
