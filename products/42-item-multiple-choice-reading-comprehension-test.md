# 42-item multiple-choice reading comprehension test

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A researcher-developed Turkish-language reading-comprehension test created by Erkan Çer for assessing 10–12-year-old students' understanding of children's literature.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **42-item multiple-choice reading comprehension test (KR-20 = .91)**: A researcher-built multiple-choice reading comprehension test developed because existing tests' validity and reliability "were not verified for the Turkish language" and they did not reflect the study books' content. It began as 60 items, was reviewed by linguists, child literature experts and developmental experts, trialed with 161 students outside the research group, and reduced to 42 items after removing four items with item discrimination below .30. Reliability by KR-20 was .91. (Erkan Çer (2016))
