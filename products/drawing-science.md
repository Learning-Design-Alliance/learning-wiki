## Description
Drawing Science is a free, editable Portuguese- and English-language Natural Sciences board game created by M. A. M. Souza and colleagues in which teams draw and guess science concepts across disciplinary categories.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Drawing Science didactic board game with six scientific categories, inclusive mode, and free editable Portuguese and English versions**: Drawing Science is a didactic board game built on the structure of "Pictionary", adapted for Natural Sciences: teams advance pawns by having a designer draw a science word within 60 seconds while teammates guess. Six lettered categories cover Astronomy and Gravitation, Biology, Electromagnetism/Waves/Optics, Mechanics and Fluids, Chemistry, and Thermodynamics and Modern Physics, plus special houses such as "everyone play" and "choose category". The board measures 66 cm x 28.5 cm and cards 7.82 cm x 6.2 cm after printing. The complete game "in Portuguese and English versions is available for free and can be edited" at the released link, and a mime-only playing option is allowed if all players accept it. (M. A. M. Souza et al. (2021))
