## Description
TEA is a visual learning analytics tool developed by Josu Arruarte and colleagues for instructors to analyze item- and test-level quality in manually created assessments.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **TEA – a Visual Learning Analytics tool for analyzing the quality of test-based exercises**: TEA (test ariketen aztertzailea, "Test Exercises Examiner") is a teacher-oriented tool that uses Visual Learning Analytics to help instructors determine the appropriateness of test-based exercises. It analyzes performance at two granularity levels, each item and the whole test, using bar charts, circular charts and boxplots, and incorporates an alert mechanism highlighting suspicious tests or items. It can be used to supervise resource quality throughout a course or for retrospective analysis, and includes a guide describing each metric and plot for teachers who are not proficient in statistics. (Josu Arruarte et al. (2021))

### Claims
- [In a retrospective higher-education evaluation, instructors using TEA found low CIC values and unbalanced True Weights in three of the ten tests and marked them for further analysis](../claims/tea-retrospective-inconsistent-tests-three.md) [+M]
- [Boxplot visualizations enriched with correlation to final marks identified the same suspicious tests as the consistency metrics, and instructors modified two of them for the next academic year](../claims/tea-boxplot-correlation-identified-suspicious-tests.md) [+M]
- [Item-level visualizations of discrimination index and discrimination efficiency led instructors to identify troublesome items and replace or modify them](../claims/tea-item-level-troublesome-items-replaced.md) [+M]
