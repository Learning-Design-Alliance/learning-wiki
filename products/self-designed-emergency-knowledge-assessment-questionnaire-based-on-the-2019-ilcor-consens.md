# Self-designed emergency knowledge assessment questionnaire based on the 2019 ILCOR consensus

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

## Description
A researcher-developed questionnaire by Hu Yaojia et al. for assessing pediatric medical workers’ emergency knowledge and case-analysis ability using single-choice questions aligned with the 2019 ILCOR consensus.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Self-designed emergency knowledge assessment questionnaire based on the 2019 ILCOR consensus, with 13 single-choice knowledge questions and an 8-item case analysis**: The study used a self-designed questionnaire with 10 demographic questions and a first aid knowledge section based on the 2019 ILCOR consensus, containing 13 single-choice emergency knowledge questions and an emergency analysis case with 8 single-choice questions, scored out of 100 with a passing score of 60. Reliability testing showed "the internal consistency of the questionnaire was calculated to be 0.833 and the retest reliability was 0.899", with KMO = 0.889. Factor analysis identified factors of early identification, basic life support, and a multidimensional structure. (Hu Yaojia et al. (2022))
