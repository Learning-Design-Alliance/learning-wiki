# Teacher-reported Academic Performance Scores (APS) questionnaire based on Oliviero Bruni's Teacher school achievement form

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A six-item teacher-rated questionnaire used to assess primary students' reading, mathematics, executive ability, interest, and attention, developed by Zhang Yunting et al. from Oliviero Bruni's Teacher school achievement form.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Teacher-reported Academic Performance Scores questionnaire based on Oliviero Bruni's Teacher school achievement form**: The outcome instrument is a teacher-reported academic performance scores (APS) questionnaire "developed based on the Oliviero Bruni's Teacher school achievement form", composed of six items covering reading ability, reading comprehension, mathematics, executive ability, interest and attention. Components such as listening and speaking, reading, writing, and mathematical calculation and reasoning were rated by head teachers on 5-point scales and summed. Reliability was high: "the overall Cronbach α coefficient for this questionnaire was 0.962". (Zhang Yunting et al. (2015))
