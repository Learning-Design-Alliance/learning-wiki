# 20-item electronic BLS knowledge questionnaire

## Description
A researcher-developed electronic 20-item questionnaire assessing healthcare professionals' knowledge of basic life support procedures, airway management, defibrillation, and resuscitation quality control, scored from 0 to 100.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **20-item electronic BLS knowledge questionnaire developed with reference to 2020 AHA and 2021 ERC guidelines.**: The research team developed an electronic questionnaire with reference to the 2020 American Heart Association Guidelines and the 2021 European Resuscitation Council Guidelines for Basic Life Support. It comprised '20 knowledge items' covering core CPR procedures, airway management, defibrillation-related knowledge, and resuscitation quality-control principles. Each correct answer received 5 points, giving a total score between 0 and 100. Cronbach's alpha was 0.701, indicating acceptable internal consistency. (Pan Pan et al. (2026))
