Validated AI-TPACK scale for mathematics teacher education students¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A 24-item, six-variable, seven-point Likert self-report scale developed by Meijuan Xie and colleagues to assess mathematics teacher education students' AI-TPACK competence, self-efficacy, and teaching beliefs.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Chinese MTES show medium-level AI-TPACK overall, with AI-TK the lowest-scoring component and teaching beliefs the highest, indicating a basic, preliminary stage [+M]
