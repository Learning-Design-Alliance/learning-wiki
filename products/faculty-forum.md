## Description
Faculty Forum is an online database and bulletin-board platform developed by the partnership to provide teacher-reviewed curriculum web resources, lesson materials, and teaching-idea exchange for elementary educators.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Faculty Forum online database of teacher-reviewed curriculum web sites with lessons and a discussion board**: An online tool developed by the partnership that 'began as a database of unit-related web sites specific to the local elementary school curriculum,' with sites added by teachers who reviewed them or used them to enhance particular units, along with teaching tips. Two components were later added: a library of Technology Enhanced Lessons and an electronic bulletin board for teachers to discuss issues and share ideas, and coverage was extended beyond science to other elementary curriculum areas. (Milton W. Cole et al. (2001))
