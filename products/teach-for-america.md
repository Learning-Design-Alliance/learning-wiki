# Teach For America

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 17 claims (11 for, 6 mixed) · 6 studies (4 causal, 2 quant-synthesis), `q3` · 1 of 6 report an effect size · 17 claims rest on one study

## Description
Teach For America runs a nationwide alternate-route teacher-preparation and teacher-placement programme that recruits and prepares corps members to teach in low-income K–12 schools.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Teach For America alternate-route teacher preparation program**: Teach For America is a nation-wide alternate route teacher preparation program designed to address the shortage of effective teachers in high-poverty rural and urban K–12 public schools in the USA. It "selectively recruits both college undergraduates and graduates—many from top colleges—and professionals to teach in low‐income schools". Roughly 25% of recruits become corps members through a selection process including a writing activity, telephone interview, sample teaching lesson plan, group discussion, and in-person interview. Since 1990 TFA has placed approximately 40,000 corps members, and in 2012 recruited approximately 5% of the graduating classes of 135 colleges and universities. (Turner H et al. (2018))
- **Teach For America alternative certification program**: Teach For America (TFA) is a highly selective route to teacher certification that places non-traditionally trained teachers in high-need public schools, requiring a commitment of "at least 2 years of teaching". Recruits complete 5-7 weeks of summer training including practice teaching, then receive ongoing coaching from a manager of teacher leadership and development, regional learning teams, and subject- and grade-tailored toolkits during a 2-year commitment. Admission is highly selective; in 2015 TFA admitted 15% of applicants. (Teach For America (2016))
- **Teach For America (TFA) as a teacher-supply program for hard-to-staff subjects in high-poverty schools**: Teach For America is a program that supplies teachers for hard-to-staff subjects in high-poverty U.S. schools. The article describes it as "an important but controversial source of teachers for hard-to-staff subjects in high-poverty U.S. schools" and evaluates its secondary math teachers experimentally. The program requires only a two-year commitment from its teachers, a feature the authors explicitly address in their analysis. (Hanley S. Chiang et al. (2014))
- **TFA Theory of Change: a data-driven five-component program model from recruitment through alumni leadership**: The review distills TFA's Theory of Change from TFA's 2010–15 Business Plan. TFA "uses a data‐driven program model comprising (1) recruitment, (2) a rigorous selection process, (3) intensive pre‐service training for selected corps members, (4) two years of ongoing professional development for corps members, and (5) programming that fosters alumni leadership". Selection identifies candidates likely to succeed; training is guided by a Teaching as Leadership approach codifying a goal-oriented approach to teaching; ongoing mentoring provides regular feedback on teaching impact; alumni continue to receive professional development and leadership resources.
