Southwest English Learner Literacy (SWELL) Partnership¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 2 studies (2 design), q1–q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A partnership-run programme framework and logic model that guides the adaptation, testing, and scale-up of Write to Succeed professional learning to improve English learner literacy outcomes.

Components¶

  • SWELL partnership logic model linking teacher professional learning to English learner literacy outcomes: The SWELL logic model is a program theory that chains partnership activities (adapt, implement, and refine Write to Succeed; conduct optimization testing; conduct initial efficacy testing; statewide scale-up) through teacher-level outcomes to student-level outcomes. It states the partnership will "iteratively enhance and test the Write to Succeed professional learning program to help teachers improve their literacy instruction," so that English learner students ultimately experience positive impacts to English proficiency and literacy achievement. (Southwest English Learner Literacy (SWELL) Partnership (2023))

Claims¶

  • The SWELL logic model posits that participating teachers will increase self-efficacy in literacy instruction and in supporting English learner students [+W]
  • The SWELL logic model posits medium-term improvements in teachers' literacy instruction and ability to accommodate language learning and embed CLRI [+W]
  • The SWELL logic model posits long-term positive impacts on English learner English proficiency and on literacy achievement, including writing, for all students served [+W]
  • Teacher confidence in teaching literacy and English learner students increased over the pilot year, especially with a local coach [+W]
