## Description
A pedagogical framework associated with Scardamalia and Bereiter that organizes inquiry, dialogic discourse, and collaborative creation and refinement of community knowledge, with students sharing responsibility and teachers facilitating.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Knowledge Building as a social constructivist pedagogical model provides the discourse context in which curiosity chains emerge**: Knowledge Building (KB) is the pedagogical model underpinning the studied classrooms: it is "underpinned by social constructivism," emphasizing "inquiry and dialog-based classroom discourse" and "collaborative engagement and knowledge creation as a learning community." A distinguishing characteristic is "students’shared responsibility for learning and propelling the discourse forward," with teachers facilitating rather than leading. The article treats KB discourse as the ideal context in which antecedents, triggers, and manifestations of curiosity can be examined. (Ali et al. (2025))
