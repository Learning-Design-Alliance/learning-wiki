## Description
A school-based Transcendental Meditation programme delivered and supervised by certified TM teachers, with supported daily practice for pupils.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Quiet Time Transcendental Meditation school protocol (three-phase format)**: The Quiet Time protocol is a school-based Transcendental Meditation program delivered in a "sequential (3) phase format". In phase one, a certified TM teacher individually trains each participant over 4 days with 60-90 minute sessions covering the mind-body connection. In phase two, children meditate in class for 15 minutes twice daily, with weekly then biweekly 60-minute supervised group meetings (8 total). In phase three, each participant receives individual supervision to verify correct technique application. (Ruini et al. (2023))
