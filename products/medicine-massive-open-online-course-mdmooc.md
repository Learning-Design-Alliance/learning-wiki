# Medicine Massive Open Online Course (mdMOOC)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A university-produced online learning platform that delivers short medical video lessons and hosts related quizzes for learners’ pre-class preparation and post-class assessment.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Medicine Massive Open Online Course (mdMOOC) for pre-class radiology learning**: The mdMOOC is the pre-class learning platform used in the FC model, "produced by Anhui Medical University". Residents "acquired basic radiological knowledge of nervous system through the Medicine Massive Open Online Course (mdMOOC)", watching recordings of approximately 15–20 minutes at least one week before each in-class session. The same mdMOOC webpage hosted the post-class quizzes, whose results teachers reviewed to explain difficult problems, making it the carrier of both pre-class content and post-class assessment. (Hou Weishu et al. (2025))
