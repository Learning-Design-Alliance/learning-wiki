## Description
A validated multidimensional instrument developed by Jabar et al. for guiding online-instruction design and evaluating its effectiveness.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Use DLISt7 as a rubric to assist instructional design and as a measure to evaluate effectiveness of online instruction**: The article proposes that the validated DLISt7 instrument "can now be used as a rubric to assist the design of instruction and as a measure to evaluate the effectiveness of such instruction." It further proposes that DLISt7 be utilized "to enable the learning experienced by students to be systematically scalable to different levels of complexity," with teaching staff having flexibility in their pedagogical choices. Successively less facilitated guidance should be faded with each scaffolded task until students complete complex tasks independently. (Jabar et al. (2016))
