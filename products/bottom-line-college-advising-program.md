## Description
A branded, multi-year college-access and persistence advising program developed and operated by the nonprofit Bottom Line for low-income and first-generation students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Intensive one-on-one advising across the college application, transition, and first years of college**: The Bottom Line model pairs each student with an individualized advisor across sequential phases: application advising, financial aid support, college selection guidance, summer transition assistance, and continued campus support. Advisors interacted with students during high school, on average, 13 times during a 15-month period, entailing 10-15 hours of contact time, with support continuing at target colleges for up to six years. (What Works Clearinghouse et al. (2021))

## Description
The Bottom Line model pairs each student with an individualized advisor across sequential phases: application advising, financial aid support, college selection guidance, summer transition assistance, and continued campus support. Advisors interacted with students during high school, on average, 13 times during a 15-month period, entailing 10-15 hours of contact time, with support continuing at target colleges for up to six years.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Full-time advisors with college degrees who complete a first year learning the organization's curriculum and mastering advisor competencies; offices in served communities and at each target college.
#### Constraints
- Costs are nearly fully funded by private and corporate donors and foundations; students and families incur no costs.

### Target Learners
- High school juniors and seniors from low-income backgrounds transitioning to college

### Learning Goals
- Applying to and selecting high-quality, affordable colleges; persisting in college
