# Rising Stargirls

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
Rising Stargirls is an outreach programme that runs creative arts-based astronomy workshops for middle-school girls and provides educators with activity handbooks, supplements, translations, and webinar-based professional learning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Rising Stargirls program and its publicly available arts-based astronomy teaching handbooks and educator webinars**: Rising Stargirls is an outreach program founded in 2014 that offers "creative arts-based astronomy workshops for middle-school girls", integrating scientific discovery with arts-based activities such as free writing, visual art, and theater exercises. The program has publicly released The Rising Stargirls Teaching and Activity Handbook, a Zoom-adapted version, a Teaching Activity Supplement with five additional activities, and a Spanish-language translation; these free handbooks lead educators through over 20 hours of arts-based astronomy activities. Since 2019 the program has also hosted five two-hour educator webinars modeling how to incorporate its learner-centered activities, and participating educators received resources such as example workshop flyers, participant contracts, and consent and release forms. (Maya Silverman et al. (2024))
