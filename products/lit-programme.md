## Description
A literacy intervention programme that implements reciprocal teaching through staff training and structured lesson plans to develop reading comprehension and metacognitive strategy use.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Reciprocal teaching: four comprehension strategies in which pupils 'become the teacher'**: Reciprocal teaching is "an instructional procedure designed to teach students cognitive strategies that might lead to improved reading comprehension", in which teacher and pupils take turns leading discussion of a text. Pupils are taught four strategies — "summarising, clarifying, questioning, and predicting" — which force them to check understanding, identify problems, and make inferences. The LIT programme packages this approach with staff training and detailed lesson plans. (Claire Crawford (2014))
