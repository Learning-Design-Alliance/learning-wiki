Blueprint Math Fellows program¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A middle-school mathematics tutoring programme operated by Blueprint that uses AmeriCorps volunteers for daily in-person group tutoring supplemented by a web-based learning platform.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • Student math confidence and sense of belonging in tutoring increased modestly during the school year, with site-level variation [+W]
  • Exploratory analyses: students ending the year in larger tutoring groups showed the largest math score increases but reported weaker tutor relationships than peers in smaller groups [~W]
  • Most Blueprint students reported strong tutor relationships, but reported relationship strength varied across sites [+W]
  • Blueprint Math Fellows tutoring raised middle school students' math test scores by 0.12 standard deviations relative to nonparticipant peers [+W]
