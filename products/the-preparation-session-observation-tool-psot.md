# The Preparation Session Observation Tool (PSOT)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
PSOT is an observational protocol created by Amezcua for quantifying video-coded behaviors and interactions during weekly content-preparation sessions between faculty and undergraduate Learning Assistants.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The Preparation Session Observation Tool (PSOT), an observational instrument for LA-faculty weekly content preparation sessions**: PSOT is an observational protocol created to observe, characterize, and interpret LA-faculty behaviors during weekly content preparation sessions, providing a quantitative analysis of qualitative video data. It borrows its two-minute interval structure from COPUS and behavioral foci from RTOP. The text states: "The PSOT consists of 22 observational codes in two main categories (Faculty to LA and LA to Faculty). The researcher documents behaviors for every 2-min interval." It was designed for one-on-one settings at small institutions such as Chicago State University. (Amezcua (2018))
