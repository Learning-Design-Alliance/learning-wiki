# University simulation EPR

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A simulated electronic patient-record system created with an EPR system provider for academic institutions to teach healthcare students to use EPRs without access to live systems.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **University simulation EPR for delivering EPR education without live systems**: A simulated Electronic Patient Record created with an EPR system provider to give academic institutions access to EPR technology for teaching. The article states: "The researchers have been working with an EPR system provider to create a University simulation EPR [41] so that the education may be delivered through didactic, simulated, experiential and reflective pedagogy with and alongside the EPR." It addresses the barrier that implementing the learning outcomes into curricula requires EPR technology, which "is likely to be a barrier for many academic institutions." (Pontefract S. K. (2019))
