# DEL-based VR mHealth serious role-playing app

## Description
A Unity-built iOS and Android mobile health serious role-playing application developed by Sarvestan and colleagues for healthcare-professional training in cultural humility, inclusive communication, social determinants of health, bias awareness, empathy, and compassion.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **DEL-based VR mHealth serious role-playing app for health care professional training**: A mobile health (mHealth) app functioning as a serious role-playing game, developed on digital experiential learning concepts for iOS and Android devices. It features "2 virtual case scenarios": "Muhammad Ayoubi, a Syrian refugee with limited English proficiency" and "Ebony Williams, an African American pregnant woman with a history of opioid use disorder", each lasting about 30 minutes. Built with Unity and Reallusion tools, it embeds pre- and postsurveys within gameplay and distributes through the App Store and Google Play Store. (Sarvestan Javad; Aksoy Mehmet; Teaford Max; Patel Dixit Bharatkumar; Patel Mayank Bharatkumar; Wischgoll Thomas; Pei Yong; Hershberger Paul J (2025))
