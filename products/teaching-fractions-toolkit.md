# Teaching Fractions Toolkit

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
REL Midwest's Teaching Fractions Toolkit is a grade 6 mathematics professional-development and support package featuring teacher learning modules, reflection tools, formative-assessment probes, and resources for educational leaders.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Teaching Fractions Toolkit (TFT) providing grade 6 teacher PD modules, reflection tool, and formative assessment probes**: The TFT is a professional development and support package for grade 6 math teachers on evidence-based fraction instruction. It includes "six PD modules, a teacher reflection tool, and formative assessment probes", plus resources for administrators and math leaders such as "videos, infographics, checklists, and teacher PD facilitator guides". (REL Midwest (2023))
- **Fractions Resource Kit: a five-section binder of mathematical tasks, curriculum inquiry materials, lesson study templates, and refresher guidance**: A mailed binder providing mathematical and logistical support for each phase of the lesson study cycle, used by experimental groups without researcher facilitation. Section 1 has "Tasks to Solve and Discuss"; Section 2 is a "Curriculum Inquiry: Different Models of Fractions"; Section 3 supports choosing a lesson study focus (Path A linear measurement or Path B other fraction topics); Section 4 provides a research lesson template and observation/discussion protocols; Section 5 is a lesson study refresher. The four video-analysed groups used the binder as designed. (Catherine Lewis et al. (2014))
