# IC-JPA Lesson Plan and Task Card materials

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A published set of IC-JPA lesson-planning materials, checklists, student task cards, and academic-language supports for elementary mathematics instruction with English Language Learners.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **IC-JPA Lesson Plan and Task Card materials with The Arch of Collaborative Conversation-Based Instruction**: The treatment used published IC-JPA materials: the treatment group followed "The Arch of Collaborative Conversation-Based Instruction outline created by Mellom, Weber, and Gokee," and the teacher used "The IC-JPA Lesson Plan and Task Card checklist" to create activities aligned to the county pacing guide. Lesson plans include contextual and language goals, materials, questions to consider, lesson reflections, and follow-up activities. Student-friendly task cards with pictures were distributed so students could take ownership of tasks, and ETRC Academic Language cards supported language goals. (Hendy et al. (2020))
