# VR-SIMI (Virtual Reality-based Simulation Innlandet)

## Description
A group-based healthcare simulation programme developed and run by Innlandet Hospital Trust that uses 360° video, structured prebriefing and debriefing, and facilitator-led application to train non-technical skills.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **VR-SIMI: group-based 360°-video simulation programme with a five-stage protocol for training non-technical skills in healthcare professionals**: VR-SIMI (Virtual Reality-based Simulation Innlandet) is a programme developed at Innlandet Hospital Trust to provide healthcare professionals with training in non-technical skills. The protocol is "delivered in the form of group-based training sessions, consists of five stages: 1) clarification of objectives, 2) prebriefing, 3) visualizing a case through VR, 4) debriefing, and 5) application to practice", with a recommended group size of five to eight participants, led by a trained facilitator for about an hour. Cases are 360° videos developed by experienced clinicians. In this study, resident doctors attended three sessions with scenarios on compulsory treatment, initial assessment with a child and parents, and confronting a colleague. (Birkheim SL et al. (2024))
