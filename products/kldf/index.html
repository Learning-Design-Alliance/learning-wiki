Description¶

A KIPP programme that disseminates KIPP school-leadership preparation strategies to leaders and schools outside KIPP and to the broader principal-preparation field.

Components¶

  • KIPP Leadership Design Fellowship (KLDF) program disseminating school leadership development strategies outside KIPP: The third KIPP school leadership program examined in the evaluation, described as "the KIPP Leadership Design Fellowship (KLDF) program, which disseminates effective school leadership development strategies outside of KIPP". It extends KIPP's leadership preparation approaches to other schools and the broader principal preparation field. (Elias Walsh et al. (2022))

Claims¶

  • Former KIPP school leadership program participants reported satisfaction and application of program lessons in their own settings [+W]
