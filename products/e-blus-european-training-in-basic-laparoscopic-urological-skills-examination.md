# E-BLUS (European training in basic laparoscopic urological skills examination)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
E-BLUS is a validated standardized examination and curriculum developed and used by the European Association of Urology to assess and certify basic laparoscopic skills in urology residents.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **E-BLUS: European training in basic laparoscopic urological skills examination**: E-BLUS is a validated laparoscopic skills curriculum used by the European Association of Urology to certify Urology laparoscopists. The article states it "was developed to specifically evaluate the competence of basic laparoscopic skills in Urology residents and consists of four exercises: peg transfer (PT), cutting a circle (CC), needle guidance (NG), and laparoscopic suturing (LS)". In this study it served as the standardized task set, with time tolerance adjusted for novices and time, errors, and exercise failure recorded. (Uysal Daniel et al. (2020))
