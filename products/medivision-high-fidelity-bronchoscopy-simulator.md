# Medivision high-fidelity bronchoscopy simulator

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Medivision is a high-fidelity virtual and interactive bronchoscopy simulator made by Yangzhou Vision Technology Co., Ltd. for procedural-skills training, automated performance scoring, and feedback.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Medivision high-fidelity bronchoscopy simulator with automated scoring and feedback**: The Medivision simulator (Yangzhou Vision Technology Co., Ltd) is the high-fidelity bronchoscopy simulator used for training in this trial. It "provides high-fidelity virtual and interactive environments and can simulate both normal and difficult airways," though only the normal-airway model was used. It automatically records procedure time, assesses performance with an algorithm derived from the 5-point GRS standardized to 100, and gives brief feedback after each procedure including time, score, and identified deficiencies, which trainees use to refine the next performance. (Jiang Luyang et al. (2023))
