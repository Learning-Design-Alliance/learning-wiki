# Performance Assessment Resource Bank

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A CCSSO collaboration with the Stanford Center for Assessment, Learning, and Equity (SCALE) and the Stanford Center for Opportunity Policy in Education (SCOPE) that provides reviewed curriculum-embedded performance tasks, units, rubrics, scored student work, and educator training protocols.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Performance Assessment Resource Bank of reviewed curriculum-embedded tasks**: The Performance Assessment Resource Bank is a nationwide bank of curriculum-embedded performance tasks "developed by the Council for Chief State School Officers (CCSSO) in collaboration with the Stanford Center for Assessment, Learning, and Equity (SCALE) and the Stanford Center for Opportunity Policy in Education (SCOPE)". Tasks are "reviewed for quality by a team of assessment experts and, frequently, piloted and revised", and are presented with their embedded units, rubrics, and scored samples of student work. The bank includes "tools and protocols for training educators to develop, review, revise, and score tasks with consistency". (Darling-Hammond (2017))
