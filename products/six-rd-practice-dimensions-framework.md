# Six R&D practice dimensions (strategy, structure, intensity, spread, engagement, evidence) with a four-stage Nascent-to-Sustaining rubric

## Description
The report organizes school-system R&D practices into six dimensions—strategy, structure, intensity, spread, engagement, and evidence—each described on a developmental pathway. It states: "Within each dimension, we introduce four stages of practice: Nascent, Developing, Established, and Sustaining." The stages are explicitly "not intended to be evaluative or prescriptive" but a developmental lens for systems to assess current practice and identify growth. Each dimension page defines the progression, e.g., engagement moves from Informed through Consultative and Collaborative to Immersive co-design, and evidence from Informal Learning to Embedded data ownership.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- School systems use the rubric "to identify the practices your school system is implementing currently and opportunities for deepening your R&D practices"
#### Constraints

### Target Learners

### Target Learning Objectives
