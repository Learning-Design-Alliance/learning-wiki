# GenAI-SRL scale and GenAI-SRL-12 short form

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 3 claims (3 for) · 1 study (1 associational), `q2` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 3 claims rest on one study

## Description
A self-report assessment developed by Xiaoqi Wang et al. that measures six dimensions of GenAI-supported self-regulated learning in L2 writing, with full and abbreviated forms for learners experienced in GenAI-assisted writing.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **GenAI-SRL scale (34-item six-dimension instrument) and GenAI-SRL-12 short form**: A self-report questionnaire measuring GenAI-supported SRL in L2 writing on seven-point scales, with six subscales (cognitive, metacognitive, motivational, affective, social-behavioral, environmental regulation). Items were adapted from existing SRL writing scales, refined through interviews with 12 learners and expert content validation (I-CVI of 1.00 required), reducing a 50-item pool to 42 items and then 34 after EFA. The ACO-derived "12-item abbreviated form... retained the psychometric properties of the full-length instrument." (Xiaoqi Wang et al. (2026))

### Claims
