Automated Extended Essay Grading Software (AEEGS)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 4 claims (4 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A web-based application developed by Olaoye DD et al. that uses contextual similarity to score senior-secondary Economics extended essay responses against examiner-supplied questions and marking schemes.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • AEEGS scores show high absolute agreement with 12 human expert raters' scores on mathematical Economics essay items (average-measures ICC = 0.863) [+M]
  • The AEEGS software demonstrated internal consistency of Cronbach alpha 0.86 in trial testing and was validated for face and content validity by three software experts [+M]
  • Each of the 12 human raters' scores correlates significantly with AEEGS scores, with coefficients ranging from 0.604 to 0.864 [+W]
  • AEEGS and human raters produced similarly shaped score distributions, with most students' scores clustered around the mean in the 'good' band [+W]
