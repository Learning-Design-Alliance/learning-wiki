# National Diabetes Prevention Program (National DPP)

## Description
A CDC-led lifestyle-change programme implemented through CDC-recognized organizations and the Diabetes Prevention Recognition Program (DPRP) to help adults at high risk prevent or delay type 2 diabetes through approved curricula delivered in person, online, by distance learning, or in combination.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **National DPP four-mode delivery structure (in person, online, distance learning, combination) with DPRP quality assurance**: The National DPP is a CDC-led national implementation of the evidence-based DPP lifestyle change program allowing four delivery modes: in person, online (asynchronous self-paced sessions with coach interactions outside sessions), distance learning (synchronous call-in/videoconference), and combination. The DPRP sets quality standards, requires a CDC-approved curriculum, and collects biannual organization and participant data. (Cannon M. J. et al. (2022))
