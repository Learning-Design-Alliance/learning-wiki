# Missouri teacher equity plan

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Missouri's teacher equity plan is a state-run programme framework, developed and implemented by the Missouri Department of Elementary and Secondary Education, that uses shortage prediction to target recruitment and preparation in high-need areas.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Missouri shortage predictor model for the teacher workforce**: In 2014, as part of its teacher equity plan, Missouri developed a shortage predictor model that "identifies where the state is likely to see teacher shortages in the coming years," pinpointing specific content areas and geographic locations most likely to experience shortage. DESE targets districts identified by the model and supports their recruitment of potential teacher candidates at the high school level, while EPPs train those candidates in high-need content areas, ideally sending teachers back to their home districts. (Data Quality Campaign (2017))
