# Thinkspace

## Description
Thinkspace is an online problem-solving e-learning platform that delivers interactive scenario-based tasks and program-generated feedback, developed for instructional use by its creators.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Thinkspace online problem-solving intervention with four scenario-based budget tasks**: Thinkspace is an online problem-solving e-learning system used to deliver a stand-alone remediation during one 2-hour lab period, chosen to test efficacy in a large lecture course "where signiﬁcant instructor–student interaction is limited." Students role-play a snow-cone business owner and complete four tasks applying budget concepts in different contexts: a water-tank simulation with real-time feedback, a radiation-budget task using National Climate Data Center data, a bank-account task explicitly confronting the pattern-matching misconception, and an ill-structured atmospheric carbon task based on IPCC (2007) scenarios. Feedback comes from the program itself, via the Task 1 simulation and a Task 3 short-answer explanation of a contradictory example. (Collin Reichert et al. (2015))

### Claims
- [Students who completed the instructional intervention showed larger pretest-to-posttest gains in budget knowledge than lecture-only controls](../claims/intervention-larger-budget-knowledge-gains.md) [+M]
- [The experimental group outperformed controls on only one of five budget questions — identifying maximum stock levels from an inflow–outflow graph — with no significant advantage on the others](../claims/question-four-only-significant-advantage.md) [+M]
- [Reliance on the pattern-matching misconception dropped more in the experimental group than in the control group but persisted for the majority of students](../claims/pattern-matching-misconception-persists.md) [~M]
