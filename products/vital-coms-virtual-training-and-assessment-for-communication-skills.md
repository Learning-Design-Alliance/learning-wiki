# VITAL-COMS (Virtual Training and Assessment for Communication Skills)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
VITAL-COMS is a Unity 3D virtual patient simulation tool developed by Fiona Quigley and colleagues for rehearsing and assessing weight-related communication skills among health care professionals and allied health students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **VITAL-COMS virtual simulator for weight-related communication training**: VITAL-COMS (Virtual Training and Assessment for Communication Skills) is a Unity 3D simulation tool in which health care professionals verbally interact with virtual patient characters to rehearse sensitive weight-related conversations. It pairs educational videos on obesity science, communication, and weight stigma with two simulated patient scenarios. The virtual patient is operated in real time via a Wizard of Oz controller, so that "a human operator (FQ) manages the system's output to mimic intelligent responses that do not yet exist technologically," providing tailored verbal feedback and high-fidelity, emotionally resonant practice. (Fiona Quigley et al. (2025))
