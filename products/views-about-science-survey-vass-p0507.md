## Description
A Halloun-developed standardized 33-item survey that measures students’ expert-like and folk views about physics and science using a five-point Contrasting Alternatives response scale.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Views About Science Survey (VASS) P05.07 as an attitude measurement instrument**: The Views About Science Survey is a standardized, non-multiple-choice attitude instrument developed by Halloun to distinguish "expert" and "folk" views of physics. The P05.07 version "contains 33 items that measure students learning outcome before and after covering a certain content", rated on a 5-point Contrasting Alternatives scale in 40 minutes. This study administered it to 419 Ugandan students, computing percent expert-like responses pre- and post-test. (Kanyesigye ST et al. (2022))
