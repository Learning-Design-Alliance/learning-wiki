## Description
VA-MeCo is a video-based situational-judgement test for assessing medical communication competence across content advancement, conversational structure, and patient relationship dimensions, developed by Reiser et al.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **VA-MeCo SJT with five integrated automated feedback versions implemented in Moodle**: The VA-MeCo is a video-based situational judgement test assessing medical communication competence on three dimensions (advancing the conversation's content, providing structure, building relationship with the patient), with 117 items nested in eleven scenarios. For this study the authors developed five feedback versions: "We developed four versions of task-based (formative) feedback, differing in their enrichment strategies (i.e. reflection prompts or expert explanations) and the timing of the feedback (i.e. immediate feedback or post-test feedback)", plus one performance-based Summative Test Profile with sub-test scores and social comparison. Technical implementation used the open-source learning management system Moodle to ensure transferability. (Reiser et al. (2026))
