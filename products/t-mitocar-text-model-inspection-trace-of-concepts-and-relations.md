# T-MITOCAR (Text-Model Inspection Trace of Concepts and Relations)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
An automated computer-based assessment tool developed by Lehmann et al. that converts learners’ natural-language mental-model descriptions into concept-relation graphs and compares them with an expert reference model.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **T-MITOCAR as an automated model-based assessment tool for knowledge integration**: T-MITOCAR (Text-Model Inspection Trace of Concepts and Relations) is an automated computer-based diagnostic tool that uses natural language expressions as input data to generate graph-based representations of learners' mental models. It measures structural and semantic similarities of verbally externalized mental models compared to a reference (expert) model using measures such as GAMMA (structural density) and BSM (balanced semantic matching). The tool relies on "the dependence of syntax and semantics within natural language" and works with a comparably small amount of text (350 words+). (Lehmann et al. (2019))
- **T-MITOCAR computer-linguistic technology for modeling and comparing semantic knowledge structures of texts**: T-MITOCAR (Text-Model Inspection Trace of Concepts and Relations) is a computational linguistic heuristic technology "developed as a means of automatically analyzing, modeling, visualizing, and comparing the semantic knowledge structure of texts." It builds graphs of concepts linked by association strengths from propositional relations, grounded in mental model theory, and compares texts quantitatively via four structural measures (SUR, GRA, GAMMA, STRU) and three semantic measures (CONC, PROP, BSM) yielding similarity values between zero and one. The study used it to compare student texts with a reference model of the four source texts. (Inka Sara Hähnlein (2024))
