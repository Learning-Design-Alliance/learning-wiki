# Little Anne QCPR manikin with QCPR Training application

## Description
A Laerdal BLS training manikin and connected application that objectively records CPR performance metrics and provides real-time feedback for learner practice and assessment.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Little Anne QCPR manikin with QCPR Training application for objective BLS skill assessment and feedback**: Little Anne QCPR (Laerdal, Norway) is a training manikin with an internal feedback device that connects to an instructor's tablet PC or smartphone. The article states "Objective assessments were conducted using “Little Anne QCPR®” and “QCPR Training” application that can monitor in real time and save the results." It records chest compression rate, depth, recoil, flow fraction, and ventilation data, while instructors separately evaluate sequence and hand placement. (Kim Seo-Yoon et al. (2020))
