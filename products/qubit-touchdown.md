## Description
Qubit Touchdown is a two-player competitive tabletop game developed by Kristina Armbruster and colleagues to introduce high-school students to qubits, quantum gates, superposition, and measurement through football-themed play.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Qubit Touchdown's game mechanics map directly onto qubit states, quantum gates, and measurement on the Bloch sphere**: The game encodes quantum computing fundamentals: the six board positions correspond to the Bloch-sphere states 0, 1, +, −, i, and −i, with "the football is a qubit moving between these six states." Cards other than Measurement correspond to quantum gates, since "All quantum gates on a qubit rotate its state on the Bloch sphere by some angle around some axis," and the Measurement card and post-touchdown kickoffs model measurement yielding 0 or 1 with 50:50 probability for equatorial superposition states. (Kristina Armbruster et al. (2025))
