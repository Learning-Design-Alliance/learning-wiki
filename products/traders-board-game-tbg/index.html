Traders Board Game (TBG)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A physical entrepreneurship simulation board game developed by Prihadi et al. in which young players trade, invest, manage risk, and pursue profit through legal and illegal strategies and chance events.

Claims¶

  • Playing the TRADERS board game once a week for three weeks significantly improves resilience among university students compared to not playing [+M]
  • Frequency of playing TBG did not significantly affect self-esteem improvement over three weeks [~M]
