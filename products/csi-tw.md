## Description
A skill-based design-thinking competency measure for assessing Visualize, Discover, Prototype, and Evaluate process skills in higher-education learners and faculty.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Badding's four-stage recursive cognitive synthesis framework (Visualize, Discover, Prototype, Evaluate)**: The skill-based DT competency measure (CSI-TW) is based on Badding's four-stage framework designed for recursive cognitive synthesis in educational settings. The article explains that while commercial models such as IDEO and Stanford d.school conceptualise DT as an external sequential pipeline, Badding's framework "captures the recursive cognitive synthesis unique to higher education", with Visualize and Discover serving as internal mindset drivers that map onto IDEO's Empathize and Define stages, transitioning into the operational Prototype and Evaluate phases. (Liu Hsing-Yuan (2026))
