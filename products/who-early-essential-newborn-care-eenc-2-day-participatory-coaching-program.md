# WHO Early Essential Newborn Care (EENC) 2-day participatory coaching program

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A WHO-developed, on-site participatory coaching programme that trains healthcare workers in essential newborn care and neonatal resuscitation through demonstration, discussion, peer feedback, repeated practice, and checklist-based assessment.
