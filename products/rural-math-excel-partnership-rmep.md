# Rural Math Excel Partnership (RMEP)

## Description
RMEP is a rural Virginia education partnership programme that coordinates families, teachers, and communities through mathematics guidance, professional development, family events, STEM activities, technology access, and online resources to prepare students for advanced STEM study.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Rural Math Excel Partnership (RMEP) shared responsibility model**: The RMEP Project was a rural education intervention built on a model of shared responsibility among families, teachers, and communities to prepare students for advanced STEM studies. It included six core components: a gap analysis and Math Advanced Study (MAS) guide, professional development and coaching, Family Math Nights, a project website and social media presence, community-based STEM events, and technology access. The report describes it as "a model of shared responsibility among families, teachers, and communities in rural areas for student preparation for and success in advanced high school and postsecondary STEM studies." (Nagle et al. (2016))
