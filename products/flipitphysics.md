## Description
FlipItPhysics is a commercial suite of short, sequential multimedia physics pre-lecture modules developed by the Physics Education Research Group at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, with embedded questions, automatic feedback, and tutorials.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **FlipItPhysics multimedia learning modules (smartPhysics pre-lectures)**: On-line multimedia learning modules developed by the Physics Education Research Group at the University of Illinois at Urbana Champaign, commercially known as FlipItPhysics. These are "flash animations that are designed to introduce core concepts within a traditional physics course in relatively short presentations of approximately 15 min". Each pre-lecture includes narration with animations, is split into 1–2-min sections the student must view sequentially, and embeds two to three multiple choice questions with automatic feedback and short tutorials for incorrect answers. (James Christopher Moore (2018))

### Claims
- [Students completing multimedia learning modules before tutorials achieve significantly higher normalized learning gains on the CSEM than students preparing via textbook reading and lecture](../claims/mlm-preparation-higher-csem-normalized-gain.md) [+M]
