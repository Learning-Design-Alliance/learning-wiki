Aptitude Test for School Beginners¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 mixed) · 1 study (1 quant-synthesis), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A standardized, culture-free school-readiness assessment developed by South Africa's Human Sciences Research Council, comprising subtests of perception, spatial ability, reasoning, numerical ability, gestalt, coordination, memory, and verbal expression.

Components¶

  • Aptitude Test for School Beginners (HSRC standardized school-readiness test): The measuring instrument used in the experiment is "the Aptitude Test for School Beginners, a standardized culture-free school readiness test of the former Human Sciences Research Council, consisting of a battery of 8 sub-sections (Perception, Spatial, Reasoning, Numerical, Gestalt, Coordination, Memory and Verbal Expression)". It was administered as a pre-test to place participants and repeated as the post-test after the ten weeks; both tests were marked according to prescribed procedures and processed by a university statistics department, with Cohen's (1988) effect sizes used to determine practical significance. (Ona Janse van Rensburg (2015))

Claims¶

  • Within aptitude test subtests, verbal reasoning (d=0.704) and quantitative reasoning (d=0.643) best predict early program academic results, while abstract and verbal reasoning predict end-of-program outcomes [~W]
