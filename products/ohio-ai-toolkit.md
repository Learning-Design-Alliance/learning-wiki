## Description
The Ohio AI Toolkit is one of the seven reviewed state guidance documents, at 18,000 words. It guides policymakers, teachers, and parents in understanding and implementing AI in education, providing a step-by-step approach for policy development, an inventory of resources for each step, and a review of key AI governance guidelines. The report notes its "web-based design of the toolkit is particularly effective in curating external documents" through well-organized, expandable descriptions that let a reader build a reading list.
