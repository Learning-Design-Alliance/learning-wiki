> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
Vigyan Pratibha is an HBCSE, TIFR-directed programme that develops and implements science and mathematics learning units for grades 8–10 and builds teachers’ capacity to use them.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Vigyan Pratibha expert-developed learning units for grades 8–10 science and mathematics with teacher capacity-building, pivoted online during the pandemic**: Vigyan Pratibha, funded by the Department of Atomic Energy and directed by HBCSE, TIFR, centers on "a set of expert-developed learning units (LUs) that cover key concepts of science and mathematics" for grades 8–10, introduced to teachers through residential capacity-building workshops. "Some common student misconceptions that can hinder understanding are effectively addressed through the LUs." When the pandemic halted camps, the program "launched an online teacher professional development program through pedagogy discussion sessions." (V. Madhurima et al. (2021))
