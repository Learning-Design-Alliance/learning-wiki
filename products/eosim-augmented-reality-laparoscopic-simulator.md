# eoSim augmented reality laparoscopic simulator

## Description
eoSim is a commercial mobile augmented-reality laparoscopic simulator made by EoSurgical Ltd. that uses Surgtrac instrument tracking to provide task-performance metrics and feedback for surgical skills training.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **eoSim augmented reality laparoscopic simulator with instrument tracking**: The eoSim is a relatively low-cost (£699–£1298 at the time of writing), low-weight, mobile inanimate laparoscopic simulator by EoSurgical ltd. with an internally mounted high-definition camera and Surgtrac software that tracks instrument tips via coloured markings. The article states it "shows performance parameters after each task while working with real instruments", making it an augmented reality simulator providing parametric feedback independent of the task performed. In this study it recorded time, path length, off-screen percentage, speed, working area, acceleration and smoothness across three suturing tasks. (Leijte Erik et al. (2019))

### Claims
- [The eoSim differentiates expertise groups on the intracorporeal suturing task in time and distance, demonstrating construct validity for those parameters](../claims/eosim-task1-construct-validity-time-distance.md) [+M]
- [The upside-down needle transfer task differentiates expertise groups on time, distance, off-screen percentage and working area](../claims/eosim-task2-construct-validity.md) [+M]
- [Participants rated the eoSim positively on realism, didactic value and usability, supporting face and content validity, with no overall differences between expertise groups](../claims/eosim-face-content-validity-ratings.md) [+M]
