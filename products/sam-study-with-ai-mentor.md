## Description
SAM is a learning platform developed by the article authors that combines YouTube lecture viewing with transcript- and slide-grounded GPT-4o conversational mentoring for learners' content questions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SAM: an AI video-watching platform with a context-aware GPT-4o chat mentor**: SAM (Study with AI Mentor) is a video-watching platform where users view YouTube lectures and pose questions to an integrated AI mentor powered by GPT-4o. It integrates video transcripts, uploaded slides, and chat history as context, supports image uploads and LaTeX formula rendering, and automatically sends the current slide when the word 'slide' appears in a user's message. The authors state it 'enables users to view YouTube lectures while interacting with an AI mentor to pose questions related to the content.'
