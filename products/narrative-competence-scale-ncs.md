## Description
A 27-item, three-dimension self-report questionnaire developed by Wanzhen et al. to assess narrative medicine competence in clinical nurses.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Narrative Competence Scale (NCS) with three dimensions and high internal consistency**: The NCS, developed by Wanzhen et al, measures narrative medicine competence via a self-report questionnaire. It comprises "27 items distributed across three dimensions—attentional listening (9 items), reflective reproduction (6 items) and comprehension response (12 items)" rated on a seven-point Likert scale, with items 4 and 11 reverse-scored and total scores ranging from 27 to 189. In this study its Cronbach's α was 0.959. (Yang Chao et al. (2025))
