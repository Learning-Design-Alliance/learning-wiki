# California Healthy Kids Survey (CHKS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The California Department of Education's California Healthy Kids Survey, administered statewide with WestEd, is a K–12 survey system that measures student well-being, school conditions, and related behavioral-health outcomes.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **CHKS Mental Health Supports module and companion School Staff Survey Student Wellness Module**: For the comprehensive Project Cal-Well evaluation, UCSF "created a custom Mental Health Supports module for the California Healthy Kids Survey (CHKS)" to assess changes in students' mental health needs and access to support. In partnership with WestEd the module was expanded with trauma measures and is now part of the CHKS Behavioral Health Modules available to LEAs statewide. UCSF also developed a companion Student Wellness Module of the School Staff Survey to assess staff capacity to support student mental health over time. (Project Cal-Well: Building Sustainable School-Based Mental Health Systems (2024))
