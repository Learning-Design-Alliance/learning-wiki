# Test of mathematical representation ability with three indicators (visual, verbal, symbolic)

## Description
A researcher-developed test by Sari et al. for assessing eighth-grade students' visual, verbal, and symbolic mathematical representation ability, validated by six experts and reported with medium reliability (0.56).

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Test of mathematical representation ability with three indicators (visual, verbal, symbolic) and reliability coefficient 0.56**: The study's instrument was a test to measure students' mathematical representation ability, with a reliability coefficient of 0.56, which the article classifies as "the medium reliability level". Following Mudzakir (2006), it measured three indicators: visual representation (making the image of a real world situation to clarify the problem), verbal representation (answering using words or written text), and symbolic representation (solving problems involving arithmetic symbols). The same items were used as a 60-minute posttest in both classes. (Sari et al. (2018))
