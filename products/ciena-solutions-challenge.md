# Ciena Solutions Challenge: a global design challenge for student CBL projects addressing the UN Sustainable Development Goals

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The Ciena Solutions Challenge is "A global design challenge that invites educators and students to engage in CBL to design a solution for their local community that addresses the UN Sustainable Development Goals." The presentation showcases example student projects, including a Sustainable House Model (Brazil), a STEM Farm Project (Nigeria), and a Mystery Munch Nutri Bar Team (Zimbabwe).
