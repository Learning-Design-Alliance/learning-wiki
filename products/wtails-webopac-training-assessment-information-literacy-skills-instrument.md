# WTAILS — WebOPAC Training Assessment Information Literacy Skills instrument

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A one-hour multiple-choice assessment developed to measure first-year degree students' information-literacy skills against ACRL Standards One, Two, Three, and Five.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **WTAILS — WebOPAC Training Assessment Information Literacy Skills instrument**: WTAILS is the multiple-choice posttest instrument used in this study to measure students' information literacy skills. It was "designed to evaluate student learning in four content areas of th e ACRL Information Literacy  Competency Standards for Higher Education," measuring Standard One, Two, Three and Five; Standard Four was excluded because it is "not compatible with a multiple-choice item format." The test must be completed in an hour, with lower-order skills measured by most items and one-third measuring higher-order skills. Its Cronbach's alpha in this study was 0.782. (Mohd Nasir Ismail et al. (2018))
