Turkish-adapted SMTML questionnaire¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A 33-item Turkish adaptation of the Students' Motivation toward Mathematics Learning questionnaire, developed by Akçakın for students aged 12–15 and measuring six motivation dimensions separately.

Components¶

  • Turkish-adapted SMTML questionnaire (33 items, 6 dimensions): The Students' Motivation toward Mathematics Learning questionnaire, originally 35 items and 6 dimensions (self-efficacy, active learning strategies, value of science learning, performance goal, achievement goal, learning environment stimulation) developed by Tuan et al. (2005), was translated into Turkish by forward translation and adapted with 462 students. Explanatory factor analysis with varimax rotation confirmed the six-dimensional structure, with dimensions explaining "between 7.017 and 10.776" percent of variance and "Totally, all the dimensions were found to explain 54.903% of the motivation structure." Cronbach alpha ranged between 71 and 86; the final form has 33 items and dimension scores are evaluated one by one. (Akçakın (2018))
