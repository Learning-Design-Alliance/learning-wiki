# Quantum Katas

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
Quantum Katas is an open-source collection of self-paced quantum-computing and Q# programming exercises with simulator-based automated validation and feedback, developed for learners by Microsoft and contributors.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Quantum Katas as hands-on problem-solving and programming practice materials**: Quantum Katas is an open-source project of programming exercises for learning Q# and quantum computing, used in the course as supplementary materials for "hands-on problem solving and programming practice" alongside recommended reading. Students singled the Katas out as the course component contributing most to their learning. The assignment tasks in the course followed the structure of the Katas problems. (Mariia Mykhailova (2023))
