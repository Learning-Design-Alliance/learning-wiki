## Description
A UNESCO framework for evaluating whether generative-AI policies address ethical governance, inclusion, equity, access, gender, sustainability, and related responsible-use considerations in education.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **UNESCO's eight-component GenAI framework as an evaluation lens for higher education GenAI policy robustness**: UNESCO's eight-component GenAI framework is an evaluative framework whose components span ethical and governance principles as well as inclusion, equity, and sustainability considerations such as internet access, gender parity in AI, and environmental impact. The study uses it as "a lens for evaluating the robustness of the policies" of thirty highly ranked universities, scoring how fully each institution's GenAI policy covers the framework's elements. Coverage of the components varied widely across countries and institutions. (Adarkwah et al. (2026))
