# Researcher-developed Academic Achievement Test for the 'Resources of our Country' unit

## Description
A 50-question, Bloom-aligned mixed-format achievement test developed by Kenan Baş to measure sixth-grade students' learning in the Resources of our Country social studies unit.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Researcher-developed academic achievement test for the global-education unit**: A researcher-developed academic achievement test used as the quantitative data collection tool for pre-test, post-test and retention measurement. The article reports it was "a total of 52 questions" reduced to 35 after expert review, then item analysis eliminated 10 questions with discrimination below .30, leaving "a total of 25 multiple choice questions with four options" at moderate difficulty and high discrimination. Reliability was calculated as α=.82 after pre-application with 350 students in four Samsun secondary schools. (Egüz et al. (2019))
