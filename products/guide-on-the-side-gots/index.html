Guide on the Side (GotS)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

Guide on the Side is an open-source web-based tutorial authoring and delivery tool developed by the University of Arizona Libraries for guiding learners through interactive activities on live websites and databases.

Components¶

  • Guide on the Side (GotS) online tutorial tool: The Guide on the Side is a web-based learning tool developed by the University of Arizona Libraries that guides learners through navigating a live website or database "by presenting a series of linear steps and activities on the left -hand side of the screen." It was designed around active learning theory, letting students practice research skills in an authentic, real-time environment. It was released as an open source download in 2012 with a WYSIWYG administrative interface that removes the need for web programming skills. (Mery et al. (2014))

Claims¶

  • There is no statistically significant difference in post-test scores between students who complete the GotS tutorial and students who view an equivalent screencast [+M]
  • Undergraduates who complete either an interactive GotS tutorial or a screencast score significantly higher on a database-searching post-test than an uninstructed control group [+M]
