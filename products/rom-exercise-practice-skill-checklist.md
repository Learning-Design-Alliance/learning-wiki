## Description
A researcher-created, expert-validated 57-item observational checklist used by blinded observers to assess first-year nursing students’ range-of-motion exercise performance on a full-size human model.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ROM Exercise Practice Skill Checklist (57-item, expert-validated observational instrument)**: A researcher-created observational checklist used to score students' ROM exercise performance on a full-size human model. It "consist[ed] of 57 items" derived from the current literature and was reviewed by 8 experts from physiotherapy, fundamentals of nursing and internal medicine nursing using the Lawshe technique. The resulting "content validity index of the form was calculated as 0.97", and blinded observers used it to evaluate both groups in the trial. (Melikoğlu Seçil Erden et al. (2025))
