Strategic Data Use in Education¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

A Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation-supported initiative that ran from 2011 to 2015 and helped organizations and agencies build school-district capacity to generate and use data.

Components¶

  • Strategic Data Use in Education initiative evaluation (2011-2015): The brief draws its data and supporting examples from "work conducted as part of the evaluation of strategic data use initiatives." The associated project, Strategic Data Use in Education, ran from 2011 to 2015, was prepared for the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation, and supported organizations and agencies helping school districts build their capacity to generate and use data. It functions as the evidence base for the brief's four recommendations. (Cassie Pickens Jewell et al. (2014))
