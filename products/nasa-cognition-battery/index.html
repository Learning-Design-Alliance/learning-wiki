NASA Cognition battery¶

Description¶

The NASA Cognition battery is a NASA-developed cognitive assessment battery that measures multiple cognitive domains relevant to astronaut performance.

Components¶

  • NASA Cognition battery for multi-domain astronaut cognitive assessment: The NASA Cognition battery is a cognitive assessment tool for astronauts that assesses multiple cognitive domains relevant to astronaut performance. The review states it "represents a significant advancement in cognitive assessment by assessing multiple cognitive domains relevant to astronaut performance", improving on approaches that evaluate only single domains because astronauts' operationally relevant cognitive profile differs from that of clinical or general-population individuals. The review still judges scheduled periodic testing alone insufficient for exploration-class missions. (Dickerson Broderick L. et al. (2026))
