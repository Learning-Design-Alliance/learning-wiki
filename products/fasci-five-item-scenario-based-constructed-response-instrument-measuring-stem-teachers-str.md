# FASCI: five-item scenario-based, constructed-response instrument measuring STEM teachers' strategic knowledge on FA and SCI dimensions

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (1 for, 3 mixed) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
A five-item scenario-based constructed-response assessment developed by Talbot et al. to measure novice STEM teachers' strategic knowledge of flexible application and student-centered instruction.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **FASCI: a five-item scenario-based, constructed-response instrument measuring STEM teachers' strategic knowledge on FA and SCI dimensions**: The FASCI measures STEM teachers' strategic knowledge through open-ended scenario-based items that are not specific to a particular grade level, topic, or curriculum, and "require the respondent to construct an open-ended response." It scores two dimensions defined by construct maps: Flexible Application of instructional approaches and Student Centered Instruction, each with three qualitatively distinct levels (0–2). Trained raters score responses using scoring guides derived from the construct maps. The proposed use is evaluating the effects of a teacher education program on novice STEM teachers' strategic knowledge, supporting both norm-referenced and criterion-referenced decisions. (Talbot et al. (2014))
