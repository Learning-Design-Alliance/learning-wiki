# voLeA video-based Situational Judgement Test

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An online construct-based situational-judgement test developed by Schick et al. that uses 14 video vignettes and anchored response ratings to assess medical students’ clinical communicative competence.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **voLeA video-based Situational Judgement Test with 14 video-vignette tasks**: The voLeA test is a construct-based, online SJT for assessing clinical communicative competence. Video stimuli (approx. 45-60s excerpts from case history dialogues) serve as task stems, preceded by written background information; the video breaks off at a critical point and students assess several possible doctor statements against a predefined communication goal. The article states: "There are currently 14 tasks with corresponding video vignettes in which five alternative answers are anchored." Students rate each option's effectiveness, with the three competence levels included as separate ratings. (Schick K et al. (2020))
