# MyTeachingPartner-Math/Science (MTP-M/S) curricula

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 4 claims (3 for, 1 mixed) · 1 study (1 causal), `q3` · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 4 claims rest on one study

## Description
MyTeachingPartner-Math/Science is a pair of year-long pre-kindergarten mathematics and science curricula developed for classroom use, comprising structured inquiry activities with teacher supports for questioning, language modeling, differentiation, and extensions.
