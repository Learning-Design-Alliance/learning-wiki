Functional Writing Skills Test (FWST)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 1 claim (1 for) · 1 study (1 causal), q2 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A 100-point standardized writing-performance assessment developed by Salem for third-year business ESP students, covering reports and proposals, business emails, agendas and minutes, and CVs and resumes.

Components¶

  • Functional Writing Skills Test (FWST) for business ESP students: The FWST is a 100-point standardized instrument built for the study to assess the writing performance of business English students, with "four main sections; writing business reports & Proposals, writing business emails, writing agenda & minutes, and writing CVs & Resumes", 25 points each. It uses multiple-choice, open-ended and essay (free writing) questions and was designed to depend on web-based tools to match the flipped approach. The article reports Cronbach's alpha coefficients for the sections ranging from .558 to .714 (standardized-item alphas .968 to .981) and internal consistency coefficients supporting its validity. (Salem (2018))
