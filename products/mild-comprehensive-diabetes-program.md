## Description
A nurse-training and care-structuring programme for integrating motivational-interviewing-based lifestyle counselling into general-practice diabetes care, developed and evaluated by Jansink Renate et al.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MILD comprehensive diabetes program combining MI training with care-structuring and sustainability tools**: The MILD intervention is a comprehensive program for general practice nurses consisting of (a) MI-based lifestyle counselling training, (b) tools for structuring diabetes care such as agenda-setting training, a local diabetes protocol and a social map for lifestyle support, (c) record-keeping instruction to integrate lifestyle counselling into general practice, and (d) sustainability tools including an instruction chart reminder, regular telephone follow-ups, a helpdesk, and a follow-up meeting. The MI training comprised four half-day sessions (16 hours) with theory, group discussion, role playing and an individual assignment. (Jansink Renate et al. (2013))
