# AGILE-LV3 Tool

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An interactive online data-analysis tool developed by SSDC that enables learners and other users to search the public AGILE archive, generate and refine gamma-ray light curves, and compare spectral-energy-distribution results.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **AGILE-LV3 gamma-ray data analysis activity for students**: The AGILE-LV3 Tool is an interactive online tool developed at SSDC for gamma-ray AGILE satellite data analysis. In the hands-on activity, students in small groups searched the public AGILE archive for flaring sources, generated multi-year light-curves with "default time-bin parameters of about 1 month", identified the month of maximum flux, refined the analysis over shorter periods, and compared results in the SSDC SED Tool, reproducing published Astronomer's Telegram alerts. (Zinzi A. et al. (2020))
