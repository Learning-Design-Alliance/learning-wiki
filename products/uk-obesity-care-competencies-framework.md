# UK Obesity Care Competencies framework

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A 29-competency framework developed by the UK Obesity Care Competencies Working Group to guide multidisciplinary healthcare professionals' specialist obesity-care training and practice.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **UK obesity care competency framework organised into five focal areas for specialist obesity care**: The UK Obesity Care Competencies Working Group devised a framework of obesity care competencies for healthcare professionals involved in specialist obesity care (tiers 2–4 in the UK), adapted from the US OMEC competencies. As the article states, the group "developed a set of 29 competencies, divided into five focal areas, namely obesity knowledge, patient care and procedural skills, practice-based learning and improvement, professionalism and interpersonal communication skills, and systems-based practice". The framework is intended to be adaptable to nurses and allied health professionals as well as physicians, reflecting the UK multidisciplinary team approach. (Capehorn Matthew S. et al. (2022))
