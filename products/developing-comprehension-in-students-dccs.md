# Developing Comprehension in Students (DCCS)

## Description
A teacher professional-development and instructional programme that supports K–2 Latino English learners' phonemic awareness, phonics, vocabulary, comprehension, and oral-language development through nine structured literacy strategies.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **DCCS instructional strategies organized by constrained versus unconstrained literacy skills**: The DCCS program teaches a set of nine instructional strategies organized by whether they target constrained skills, which students can master over a relatively short time (Say It Move It, Blend as You Go, Word Card for phonemic awareness, phonics, and word recognition), or unconstrained skills, which continue to develop across time (Teaching Cognates, Vocabulary Text Talk, Frayer Model, Modified DRA, and oral and written Sentence Frames for vocabulary and comprehension). The program addresses both but provides much greater emphasis on unconstrained skills such as vocabulary and comprehension, based on persistent reading comprehension gaps for English learners. (Leslie M. Babinski et al. (2018))
