# 18-item Online Academic Emotions Scale

## Description
A validated self-report scale developed by Li Xingqiao and Ye Yinghua to measure positive-high, positive-low, negative-high, and negative-low academic emotions among university students in blended learning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Four-dimension taxonomy of online academic emotions by valence and arousal**: Building on Pekrun et al.'s definition of academic emotions as emotions "directly related to classroom instruction, coursework, and academic performance", the article categorises them "into four distinct dimensions based on valence (positive/negative) and arousal (high/low)": positive-high arousal (enjoyment, excitement), positive-low arousal (relaxation, calmness, contentment), negative-high arousal (anxiety, frustration), and negative-low arousal (exhaustion, boredom, hopelessness). An 18-item Online Academic Emotions Scale measures the four dimensions in blended learning. (Li Xingqiao; Ye Yinghua (2025))
