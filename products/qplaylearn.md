## Description
QPlayLearn is an online platform developed by the QPlayLearn project team that provides multi-level quantum science and technology education through games, videos, accessible explanations, mathematics, applications, and a quantum dictionary.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **QPlayLearn online platform for multi-level quantum science education**: QPlayLearn is an online platform offering multi-level quantum science and technologies education; its stated mission is "to provide multi -level education on quantum science and technologies to anyone, regardless of age and background". Inspired by the theory of multiple intelligences, it uses a three-step method — building intuition through games and videos, understanding concepts through accessible descriptions, and acquiring formal understanding through mathematics — organized in sections Play, Discover, Learn, Apply, and Imagine, with Quest as its quantum dictionary. The article presents it as a strategy for cultural change across social strata. (Quantum Games and Interactive Tools for Quantum Technologies Outreach and Education)
