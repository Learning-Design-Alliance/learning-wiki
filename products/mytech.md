## Description
MyTech is an NC State smartphone app that collects and visualizes accelerometer data and provides in-app help articles for introductory physics laboratory learning.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **NCSU MyTech smartphone app with accelerometer visualization and help articles**: MyTech is a mobile app developed at NC State for smartphone-based kinematic data collection in introductory physics labs. Its central feature is "a visualization of a mass and spring model of a one-dimensional accelerometer" that students explore in an initial lab, and a set of in-app help articles addressing common troubleshooting issues and guiding understanding of the device. Usage tracking suggests approximately 13% of students explored the help menu, the most popular article being "What are the accelerometer axes?" (Colleen L. Countryman et al. (2015))
