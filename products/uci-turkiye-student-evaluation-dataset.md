# UCI Turkiye Student Evaluation dataset

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A publicly available UCI Machine Learning Repository dataset of 5,820 student evaluations of courses and instructors across 13 courses at Gazi University in Ankara, Turkey, with 28 Likert-scale questions and course, instructor, attendance, repetition, and difficulty attributes.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **UCI Turkiye student evaluation dataset (5820 responses, 28 questions, 13 courses)**: A publicly available UCI repository dataset contributed by students of Gazi University in Ankara, Turkey, containing "a total of 5820 evaluation scores provided by the students in response to 28 specific questions (Q1 to Q28) related to course and instructor" on a 5-point Likert scale. It adds five attributes: instructor code, course code, number of repeats, attendance level, and perceived difficulty, captured across 13 courses. The authors use Q8-Q12 as target satisfaction aspects and Q1-Q7 and Q13-Q28 as predictors. (Farzana Afrin et al. (2020))
