## Description
Literacy First (formerly AmeriCorps for Community Engagement and Education, then A Community for Education) is an early literacy intervention providing trained tutors for students in kindergarten through grade 2, largely in the Austin, Texas area. It identifies "students reading below grade level each fall and provides tutoring across the school year." Tutors work with students daily in 30-minute sessions tailored to individual literacy/language needs, and 50% of tutors are bilingual, supporting both Spanish- and English-speaking students.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Tutors receive more than 70 hours of training in best practices and weekly visits from Literacy First experts and coaches
- One full-time supervisor per 20 full-time tutors across five campuses
#### Constraints
- Program is delivered in person; virtual training and professional support is available when needed

### Target Learners
- Students in kindergarten through grade 2 reading below grade level, largely economically disadvantaged, Hispanic, and limited English proficient students

### Target Learning Goals
- Early literacy and reading comprehension
