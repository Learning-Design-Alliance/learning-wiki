## Description
A CERN/ALICE outreach programme that offers public, student, and educator engagement with the ALICE experiment through in-person, Zoom-based virtual, and Instagram LIVE visits.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ALICE Visit Program with real, virtual, and Instagram LIVE modes**: The visit program offers three modes of engagement with the ALICE experiment: real visits, virtual visits via Zoom, and Instagram LIVE sessions. The article describes it as 'a comprehensive visit program, offering various modes of interaction, thus enabling visitors from different geographical locations and with diverse interests to experience ALICE in a manner that best suits them.' (Ragoni et al. (2024))

### Claims

- [ALICE Instagram LIVE sessions typically gather about 500 to 600 people per hour of live session](../claims/alice-instagram-live-500-600-people-per-hour.md) [+W]
