# 5CardsGame

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A card-based educational game for surgical technologist and other health-professions students, designed to integrate procedural knowledge and support clinical reasoning and retrieval.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Illness script theory and cognitive load theory as the theoretical basis for the 5CardsGame**: The article grounds the 5CardsGame in illness script theory, under which CIP promotes "the integration of knowledge and the consolidation of 'illness scripts'—mental frameworks that help learners organize and retrieve diagnostic information". Cognitive load theory informs the gamified presentation, on the premise that reducing cognitive overload through strategies such as gamification improves retention and engagement. The game aims to help students develop "surgical procedure scripts" synthesizing preoperative care, anatomy, surgical techniques, intraoperative care, and potential complications. (Sadati Leila et al. (2025))
