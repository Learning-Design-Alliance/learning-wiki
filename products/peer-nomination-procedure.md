# Peer nomination procedure

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A peer-report sociometric assessment developed by Moreno and adapted by Coie et al. in which children nominate liked and disliked peers to derive acceptance, rejection, social impact, social preference, and sociometric-status groups.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Peer nomination procedure for assessing children's sociometric status**: The peer nomination procedure, developed by Moreno and adapted by Coie et al. (1982), asks children to nominate up to three peers they like and three they dislike. Nominations are used to compute acceptance, rejection, social impact, and social preference, which in turn create the sociometric groups (popular, rejected, neglected, controversial, average). The review reports it is "a reliable and valid method for measuring children 's social position", used in 16 of the 25 reviewed studies, and recommends it remain part of future studies. (Femke van der Wilt et al. (2019))
