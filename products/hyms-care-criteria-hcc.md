# HYMS CARE criteria (HCC)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A four-theme taxonomy and curriculum-evaluation tool developed by Wright and Richmond Mynett for assessing resilience- and resourcefulness-building opportunities in undergraduate medical education.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The HYMS CARE criteria: a four-theme taxonomy of 31 resilience-building factors for curriculum evaluation**: The HYMS CARE criteria (HCC) are an itemised inventory of factors believed to influence resilience and resourcefulness among medical students, organised in three levels: "31 individual elements, 10 groups of elements and 4 overall themes". The four themes are internal resources, lifestyle factors, external resources (self-mediated) and external resources (agent-mediated), isolating forces from the individual's impact on their environment to the environment's impact on the individual. The acronym stands for Compassion to self and others, Adaptability, Resourcefulness and Emotional wellbeing. The authors state it is "not intended as a definitive exposition of resilience, rather a tool that can be used to navigate areas of interest and assess current curricula". (Wright Barry; Richmond Mynett Joseph (2019))
