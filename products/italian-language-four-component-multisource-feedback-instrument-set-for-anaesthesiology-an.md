# Italian-language four-component multisource feedback instrument set for anaesthesiology and intensive care residents

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An Italian-language online multisource feedback instrument set developed for anaesthesiology and intensive care residency assessment, combining medical-colleague, non-medical-coworker, self-assessment, and patient questionnaires.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **An Italian-language four-component multisource feedback instrument set (self, medical colleague, non-medical coworker, patient) for anaesthesiology and intensive care residents**: The article describes a new Italian language MSF system built by adapting items from Lockyer et al. within the Yamamoto framework, finalized into "the four typical components of multi-source feedback: medical colleague, non-medical coworker, self-assessment and patient questionnaires". Colleague and coworker questionnaires carry 17 items on a 5-point scale with an "unable to assess" option; the patient questionnaire has 12 items; questionnaires are released as online supplemental files. (Carenzo L et al. (2021))
