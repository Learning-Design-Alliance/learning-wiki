# The 33-exercise scientific method test in geography content

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (6 for) · 2 studies (2 design), `q2` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
A paper-and-pencil geography assessment by Herring containing 33 multiple-option exercises designed to measure pupils' ability to use elements of the scientific method rather than recall geography content.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Scientific Method Test scale of 33 problems administered to about 500 pupils in each of grades III–VIII**: The Scientific Method Test is a series of 33 scientific-thinking problems whose scale values are derived in this article. The data came from administering the test to about 500 pupils in each of grades III to VIII, with printed numbers tested of 362, 482, 518, 449, 500 and 498 across grades III to VIII. The final scale (Table X) orders the problems by difficulty, with problem 1 at 1.952 P.E. and problem 32 at 6.415 P.E. above zero, column C carrying values to the nearest tenth of a P.E. multiplied by 10. (Herring (1919))
