# Adapted four-domain EBM questionnaire (Hasabo et al.)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A four-domain self-report questionnaire adapted from Hasabo et al. and used by medical-education researchers to assess undergraduate medical students' evidence-based-medicine knowledge, competencies, attitudes, and information-seeking behaviours.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Adapted four-domain EBM questionnaire (Hasabo et al.) with good internal consistency for Palestinian medical students**: A survey instrument adopted from the previously published EBM questionnaire of Hasabo et al., comprising four domains: information-seeking behaviours, EBM competencies on a 5-point Likert scale (1 = poor, 5 = advanced), attitudes towards evidence-based practice (1 = strongly disagree to 5 = strongly agree), and knowledge of core EBM terminology on a 5-point scale. Domain items and response options were used without modification and analysed at item level. Internal consistency was good, with Cronbach's alpha "ranging between 0.864 and 0.920", comparable to the original study's 0.71–0.89. (Hamza K et al. (2026))
