# FourCe-PITO leadership framework

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A leadership-competency framework created and used by the Uniformed Services University of the Health Sciences for its military medical leadership education program, integrating Character, Competence, Context, and Communication across Personal, Interpersonal, Team, and Organizational levels.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **FourCe-PITO leadership framework for military medical leadership education**: The "FourCe-PITO" model was created by the Uniformed Services University of the Health Sciences for its military medical leadership program. The framework "integrated four core components: Character, Competence, Context, and Communication, with four levels of leadership interaction: Personal, Interpersonal, Team, and Organizational." It is an example of an institution developing and validating its own leadership framework tailored to program-specific needs. (Alhadithi Ahmed (2025))
