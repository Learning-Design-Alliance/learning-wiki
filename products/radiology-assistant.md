## Description
Radiology Assistant is a free, case-based radiology education platform developed by radiologist Robin Smithuis and operated as a non-profit organization for the Radiological Society of the Netherlands.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Radiology Assistant open-access radiology education platform**: The Radiology Assistant is "an internationally recognized educational platform providing up-to-date radiological knowledge for residents and practicing radiologists", available at https://radiologyassistant.nl. Developed by radiologist Robin Smithuis for the Radiological Society of the Netherlands, it "operates as a non-profit organization" that "freely provides high-quality, case-based radiology content" authored by specialists, focused on common clinical problems where imaging is key to patient management. In this study its materials anchored weekly blended seminars for medical imaging interns. (Zhang Tonghua et al. (2026))
