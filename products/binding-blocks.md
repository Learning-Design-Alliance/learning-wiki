## Description
Binding Blocks is a physical nuclear-physics chart that represents isotope mass excess and dominant decay modes with LEGO-like tower heights and colour-coded bricks for classroom and public outreach use.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Physical nuclide chart in which tower heights encode mass excess per nucleon and brick colours encode dominant decay mode**: This is the core representational design of the Binding Blocks chart: each isotope is a LEGO tower whose height carries a physical quantity and whose colour carries its decay behaviour. The article states that "The height of each tower gives the excess energy available in the nucleus for nuclear decay, ﬁssion, or fusion processes", while "The colours in the chart refer to the diﬀerent decay modes" — black for stable nuclei, light blue and red for beta decay, yellow for alpha decay, and others. Each brick layer corresponds to 25,000 GJ/kg, allowing everyday energy comparisons. (C. Aa. Diget et al. (2016))
