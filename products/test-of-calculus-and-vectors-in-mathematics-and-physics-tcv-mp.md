# Test of Calculus and Vectors in Mathematics and Physics (TCV-MP)

## Description
The TCV-MP is a multiple-choice assessment developed through the FisicaMente initiative to diagnose first-year Science and Engineering students’ use of derivatives, integrals, and vectors across mathematics and physics contexts.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Test of Calculus and Vectors in Mathematics and Physics (TCV-MP) with online learning modules**: The TCV-MP is a multiple-choice assessment tool developed in 2018 within the FisicaMente initiative to support first-year Science and Engineering students approaching Physics 1. "The test focuses on three mathematical tools - derivatives, integrals, and vectors", with parallel items in mathematics and mechanics contexts across words, graphs, formal language and numbers; from an initial pool of 78 items piloted with 70 students, 17 pairs of parallel items were selected. In the first administration "1252 students completed the test"; since 2018 it runs yearly (stabilizing at approximately 700 students per year) as a self-assessment tool for students and an information source for instructors, paired with Geogebra-book online learning modules. (J Guisasola et al. (2022))
