## Description
A free online corpus-search tool compiled and designed by Mark Davis that provides searchable English Wikipedia text, concordances, collocations, parts of speech, and synonyms for language learning and research.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Wikipedia Corpus (Mark Davis)**: The Wikipedia Corpus is a free online corpus tool compiled and designed by Mark Davis, containing "the full text of the English version of Wikipedia, 1.9 billion words in more than 4.4 million articles." It allows users to search by word, phrase, part of speech, and synonyms, find collocates, and view re-sortable concordance lines in KWIC format. The article uses it as the intervention tool in the experimental group's lexical learning activities. (Jing Shi (2015))

### Claims
