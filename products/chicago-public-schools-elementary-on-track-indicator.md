## Description
A Chicago Public Schools indicator system that classifies elementary students by GPA and attendance thresholds to identify academic and attendance support needs and monitor later educational outcomes, including its four-category Condensed EOT version.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Condensed Elementary On-Track (Condensed EOT) indicator**: The Condensed EOT is a simplified version of CPS's five-level Elementary On-Track indicator that places students in grades 3-8 into four categories using two thresholds: "At least a 3.0 GPA" and "At least 90% attendance." The four categories are On-Track, Academic Support, Attendance Support, and Intensive Support. The report uses it to connect elementary grades and attendance to later outcomes and offers it for identifying and matching students with interventions and monitoring progress over time. (Seeskin et al. (2022))

# Condensed Elementary On-Track (Condensed EOT) indicator

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)

## Description
The Condensed EOT is a simplified version of CPS's five-level Elementary On-Track indicator that places students in grades 3-8 into four categories using two thresholds: "At least a 3.0 GPA" and "At least 90% attendance." The four categories are On-Track, Academic Support, Attendance Support, and Intensive Support. The report uses it to connect elementary grades and attendance to later outcomes and offers it for identifying and matching students with interventions and monitoring progress over time.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students need end-of-year core GPA (math, reading, social studies, and science) and end-of-year attendance rates to determine placement
- Practitioners are recommended to use the framework at least quarterly to identify and intervene with students
#### Constraints
- Students who attend charter schools are not included in the analysis since course performance or GPA information for charters was unavailable
- The framework is best suited for conversations among educators and adults who work with educators, not directly with students themselves

### Target Learners
- Students in grades 3-8 in Chicago Public Schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Identifying students who need additional academic or attendance support
- Monitoring student progress and designing systems of student support
