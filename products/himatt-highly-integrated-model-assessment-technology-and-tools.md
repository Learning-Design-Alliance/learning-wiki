# HIMATT: highly integrated model assessment technology and tools

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
HIMATT is a suite of three integrated software tools that assesses skill and knowledge development by eliciting text and annotated concept maps and automatically comparing them with expert, prior, or peer representations.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **HIMATT: highly integrated model assessment technology and tools**: HIMATT is a suite of three integrated tools for assessing the development of skill and knowledge in challenging problem-solving domains. As the editors describe it, "HIMATT, elicits text and annotated concept maps, and then provides a set of automated analysis techniques to compare representations to a reference model (e.g., an expert model), to a prior representation by the problem solver, or to representations of other problem solvers." (Dirk Ifenthaler et al. (2009))
