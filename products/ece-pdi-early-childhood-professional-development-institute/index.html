ECE PDI (Early Childhood Professional Development Institute)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 2 studies (2 design), q2 · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 1 claim rests on one study

Description¶

A job-embedded professional-development programme for early-childhood leaders, supervisors, teachers, and coaches that combines learning labs, coaching, and reflective-practice groups to improve instructional leadership and teacher–child interaction quality.

Components¶

  • ECE PDI theory of change: aligning leader, supervisor, teacher, and coach learning cycles through three job-embedded PD contexts: The ECE PDI is a job-embedded professional development model in which "Through 3 core PD strategies or "contexts for learning" (i.e., learning Labs, coaching, and reflective practice groups [RPGs]), the ECE PDI supports ECE leaders' ability to provide organizational systems and cultures to support early learning teachers' instructional planning and implementation." Training labs build knowledge, coaching supports transfer of new knowledge into practice, and reflective practice groups build professional dispositions and a culture of reflection. The model aligns professional learning cycles of four stakeholders—center leaders, direct supervisors, teachers, and coaches—and incorporates six key frameworks, including the Five Essential Supports, CLASS, Teaching Strategies GOLD, and Motivational Interviewing micro-skills. (Whalen et al. (2016))

Claims¶

  • By the end of Year 3, more than 75% of centers implemented each ECE PDI component with fidelity, meeting the overall program fidelity benchmark [+M]
  • ECE PDI leader learning cycles supported the majority of center leaders to critically examine their leadership conceptions, with three design features catalytic for leader development [+M]
