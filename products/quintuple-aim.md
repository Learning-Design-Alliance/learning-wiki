# Quintuple Aim

## Description
A healthcare-improvement framework organized around patient experience, provider experience, cost reduction, population health, and health equity, adapted by Rees et al. for contextualizing digital-health and informatics competencies in Canadian health-professional education.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Quintuple Aim as an organizing framework for digital health and informatics competencies**: The article proposes the Quintuple Aim — a health care improvement framework — as a structure for identifying, prioritizing, and contextualizing digital health and informatics competencies in health professional education. The authors state that "Each dimension of the Quintuple Aim provides a critical lens for identifying, prioritizing, and contextualizing core competencies," spanning patient experience, provider experience, reduced cost, population health, and health equity. Competencies are argued to intersect across multiple aims rather than functioning in isolation, creating synergies that benefit individual and population-level outcomes. (Rees G et al. (2025))
