# Online homework and peer assessment platform developed by the authors

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
An author-developed web-based platform for submitting homework, distributing anonymized peer assessments, and delivering feedback in large introductory statistics courses.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **OHMS online homework management system enabling anonymized peer assessment and randomized experiments**: OHMS is the web-based homework platform the authors built for the study course. As the article describes it, "All homework responses and peer assessments were submitted through an online system, and submissions were anonymized before distribution." The platform collected free-response submissions, distributed each to three peers for anonymous scoring and comments, released feedback after grading periods, and enabled individual-level randomization, matched-pairs assignment, and server-log tracking of time spent on peer assessment. (Dennis L. Sun et al. (2014))
