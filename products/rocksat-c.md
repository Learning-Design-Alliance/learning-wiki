## Description
RockSat-C is a student spaceflight programme run by NASA’s Colorado Space Grant Consortium and partner universities, enabling undergraduate teams to design, test, and fly experiments on suborbital rockets.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Academic-year student spaceflight program structured around NASA-style design reviews and functional subteams**: The RockSat-C program organizes a student spaceflight mission around the academic year so it can be integrated into an undergraduate curriculum, with teams of roughly four to eight students under at least one faculty mentor. Teams develop and test their design over the fall, deliver three virtual presentations mirroring NASA mission reviews — the article notes these "help students maintain a steady work schedule and enable them to receive design feedback from the RockSat-C leadership" — and complete additional reviews through spring. At UD, students enroll in a research-for-credit course and work in five functional subteams (science, software, electrical, mechanical, management). (Goodwill et al. (2026))
