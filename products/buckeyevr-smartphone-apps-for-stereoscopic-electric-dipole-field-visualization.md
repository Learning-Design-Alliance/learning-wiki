# BuckeyeVR smartphone apps for stereoscopic electric dipole field visualization

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
BuckeyeVR is a set of Android stereoscopic-visualization applications developed by J. R. Smith and colleagues in Unity with the Google VR SDK to help physics students explore electric dipole fields using a smartphone and cardboard viewer.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Smartphone-based stereoscopic VR and WebGL visualizations of magnetic fields and forces**: The study developed Android smartphone VR applications, written "using Unity, a cross -platform game engine developed by Unity Technologies [18], and the Google VR SDK for Unity", displaying magnetic field vectors from moving charges and current-carrying wires as arrays of vectors. The same visualizations were exported as WebGL for browser use with mouse-drag rotation, and a static 2D image control group used textbook and screenshot images. Students viewed 5 instructional scenes and answered 7 in-simulation engagement questions. (Porter et al. (2019))

### Claims
