## Description
Yao.jl is an open-source Julia framework for quantum-information research and education that supports constructing, simulating, and visualizing quantum circuits and states.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Yao.jl, a Julia quantum computing framework, used to simulate entanglement experiments for teaching**: Yao.jl is "a very active and highly maintained quantum computing library in Julia" described by its authors as "an open source framework that aims to empower quantum information research with software tools." The article uses it to generate and simulate superposition, Bell, and GHZ states, with circuit diagrams via YaoPlots.jl, and notes maintainers added a requested plotting-with-barriers feature. (Shlomo Kashani et al. (2023))
