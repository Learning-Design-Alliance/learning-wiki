## Description
The American Association of Colleges of Nursing's competency and accreditation framework defining domains and entry-level competencies for baccalaureate nursing education, including informatics.

## Components

- **Maturity wave: informatics as a fully integrated core competency in the 2021 AACN Essentials**: In the maturity wave, informatics became a fully integrated competency domain for entry-level nursing practice. The review reports that "The 2021 AACN Essentials included informatics competencies in baccalaureate program accreditation standards", embedding NI within data ethics, health IT governance, interoperability, and digital advocacy, with Domain 8 charging all nurses with fluency in data-driven decision-making, health IT infrastructure, and emerging technologies.
