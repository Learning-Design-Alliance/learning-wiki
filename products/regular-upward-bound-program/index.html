Regular Upward Bound program¶

Description¶

A federally funded U.S. Department of Education pre-college programme that provides eligible high school students with services intended to prepare them for postsecondary education.

Components¶

Claims¶

  • This report provides the first estimates of Upward Bound's effects on postsecondary completion and updates earlier estimates for enrollment and financial aid [+W]
  • Regular Upward Bound produces a statistically significant increase in the likelihood of earning a postsecondary certificate or license from a vocational school [+W]
  • Regular Upward Bound shows no detectable effects on postsecondary enrollment, financial aid application or receipt, or bachelor's or associate's degree completion [-W]
  • Upward Bound impact estimates are accompanied by extensive subgroup and sensitivity analyses [+W]
