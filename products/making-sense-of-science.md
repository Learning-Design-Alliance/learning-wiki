# Making Sense of SCIENCE

## Description
Making Sense of SCIENCE is a content-focused professional-development programme for middle-school science teachers that combines science-content learning, analysis of student thinking, instructional strategies, and language-and-literacy activities.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Making Sense of SCIENCE professional development theory of action: cascade from PD features to teacher knowledge to instruction to student achievement**: The article describes a professional development logic model in which influences cascade "from features of the professional development to immediate impact on teacher knowledge, intermediate impacts on classroom instruction, and more distal effects on student achievement." The Making Sense of SCIENCE approach embodies this model by combining science content learning with analysis of student thinking and instructional strategies, plus language and literacy activities. The study tested the distal link of this cascade and found teacher knowledge gains but no significant student gains. (Heller (2012))
