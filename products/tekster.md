## Description
Tekster is a 16-lesson, strategy-focused writing instruction curriculum for Grades 4–6, developed by Bouwer and colleagues for Dutch general education classrooms and delivered through student workbooks, a teacher manual, teacher training, and modeling videos.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Tekster lesson format: mnemonic strategy steps taught through modeling, explicit instruction, and gradual release across a consistent lesson sequence**: Tekster lessons follow a generally consistent format lasting 45–60 minutes, in which a mnemonic acronym (an animal name whose letters spell the strategy steps) anchors instruction. Each lesson explicitly states its goal, introduces text-type characteristics through modeling or explicit instruction, names the strategy acronym, and moves through generating content in keywords to writing, with "gradual release of responsibility from 1a to 1c, 3a to 3c" as printed in the lesson format table. Later phases have students read, evaluate, and revise texts based on feedback. (Bouwer et al. (2018))
