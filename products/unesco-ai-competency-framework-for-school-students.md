# UNESCO AI Competency Framework for School Students

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A UNESCO framework that organizes 12 AI competencies for school students into four dimensions: human-centred mindset, ethics of AI, AI techniques and applications, and AI system design.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Four dimensions of student AI competency: human-centred mindset, ethics of AI, AI techniques and applications, AI system design**: The framework organizes its 12 competencies under four dimensions: "A human-centred mindset", "Ethics of AI", "AI techniques and applications", and "AI system design". These dimensions define the domains in which students are expected to develop AI-related competence, ranging from values and ethics to technical understanding and design capability. (Miao et al. (2024))
