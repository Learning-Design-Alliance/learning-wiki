## Description
MyET is LLabs Inc.'s computer-assisted pronunciation-training software, which analyzes recorded learner speech against model utterances and provides pronunciation, intonation, timing, intensity, and visual pitch-contour feedback.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MyET computer-assisted pronunciation training software with visual pitch-contour feedback**: MyET is a CAPT program designed in Taiwan by LLabs Inc. that "uses advanced audio and visual technology" to raise learners' awareness of their pronunciation problems. It provides automated speech recognition comparing a student's production with a native-speaker model, visual feedback such as contrasts between the learner's and a model teacher's pitch contours, and scores across pronunciation, intonation, timing, and intensity components. In this study it supplied the practice materials and feedback for two experimental groups over ten weeks. (Tsai Pi-hua (2015))
