## Description
The ACE-HE, developed by Graham et al., is a bilingual survey instrument measuring affective, behavioral, and cognitive engagement, institutional support, and personal-environment barriers among online and blended higher-education students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **ACE-HE survey instrument measuring ABC engagement and institutional support in online/blended higher education**: The ACE in Higher Education (ACE-HE) is a survey measuring indicators of affective, behavioral, and cognitive engagement (four items each) plus institutional support elements (nine affective, nine behavioral, six cognitive items) and eight external personal-environment barriers, on 6-point agreement scales. Items were "translated from English into Spanish (see Appendix A) and piloted for comprehension and clarity with students from the host university." ## Description
The ACE in Higher Education (ACE-HE) is a survey measuring indicators of affective, behavioral, and cognitive engagement (four items each) plus institutional support elements (nine affective, nine behavioral, six cognitive items) and eight external personal-environment barriers, on 6-point agreement scales. Items were "translated from English into Spanish (see Appendix A) and piloted for comprehension and clarity with students from the host university." ## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Administration to students with online course experience; stakeholder involvement in development, translation, and piloting as the authors describe
#### Constraints
- Validation was conducted only with the Spanish version at a single Colombian university; the authors state validation "should not be applied to the English version of the instrument considering the imperfect nature of translation"

### Target Learners
- undergraduate and graduate students in online and blended courses

### Target Learning Goals
