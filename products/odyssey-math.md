## Description
Odyssey® Math is a web-based mathematics program developed by Compass Learning® for grades K–8, combining a mathematics curriculum with formative assessments supporting differentiated, data-driven instruction. Based on assessment results, "the program generates an individualized sequence of mathematics topics and skills—a 'learning path.'" Teachers can manually modify learning paths, add or remove tasks, and use the program's test bank or imported assessments. It can serve as a standalone curriculum or a supplement, delivered via desktop, laptop, or mobile device.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Students typically begin with a diagnostic assessment aligned with local or state standards to generate the learning path
#### Constraints
- As of June 2016 the program is no longer available for purchase; its features were incorporated into Compass Learning Pathblazer® Math and Hybridge® Math, which the WWC treats as different programs outside the review's scope

### Target Learners
- students in grades K–8

### Target Learning Goals
- mathematics achievement
- differentiated and data-driven mathematics instruction
