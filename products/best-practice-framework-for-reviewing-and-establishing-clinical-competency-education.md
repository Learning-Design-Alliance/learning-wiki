# Best Practice Framework for reviewing and establishing clinical competency education

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A five-step framework developed by Han Sang Yun et al. for reviewing and building traditional medicine clinical competency-education systems through governance, graduate competencies, resources, assessment, and curriculum-gap analysis.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **A five-step Best Practice Framework for reviewing and establishing clinical competency education**: The article develops a Best Practice Framework (BPF) that structures the description of a clinical competency education system in five steps: "governance of the educational system, competency of the graduates, educational resources, assessment strategies and tools, and gaps in the curriculum". Each step yields a reviewable component of the system, from organizational governance through curriculum gaps. The authors state the framework "has not been reported previously" and offer it as a reference for countries and colleges building or upgrading traditional medicine clinical education. (Han Sang Yun et al. (2022))
