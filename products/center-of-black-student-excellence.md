# The Center of Black Student Excellence (CBSE)

## Description
The CBSE is a planned district entity in LBUSD described as "a visionary establishment whose design is shaped by and for the Black Community where they can proudly discover and honor their identity, history, and cultural heritage, which fosters a sense of inclusion and confidence." It is more than a physical location: a commitment to nurturing belonging, pride, and agency among Black students and families. The Center will offer tutoring, mentoring, cultural programming, wellness services, legal aid, career support, and advocacy, and will act as a bridge between the district and the Black Community.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Ongoing dialogue and input with the Black Community to refine its design and programmatic offerings
#### Constraints
- The Center is not tasked with reversing harm done to Black students and families; that responsibility lies with the school system, per the brief

### Target Learners
- Black students in LBUSD
- Black families and community members

### Learning Goals
- Identity, belonging, agency, academic and life outcomes
