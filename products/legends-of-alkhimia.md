- **Legends of Alkhimia multiplayer game and associated curriculum**: Legends of Alkhimia is an educational multiplayer game for 13- to 15-year-olds, an eight-session chemistry curriculum for lower secondary school science designed to foster learning of chemistry through inquiry. "7KH PXOWLSOD\HU JDPH VXSSRUWV IRXU FRQFXUUHQW SOD\HUV" on personal computers connected via a local area network, typically in a school computer laboratory. It embeds students in problem-solving challenges related to the use of chemistry in realistic contexts and requires individual laboratory work using an in-game virtual chemistry lab.
