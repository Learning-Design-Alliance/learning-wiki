## Description
Particle Builder is a physical board game made by an international group of physics teachers and distributed through CERN's Zenodo repository to teach upper-secondary learners about fundamental particles and the Standard Model.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Particle Builder physical board game, freely available on CERN's Zenodo repository**: A physical board game created in 2016 by an international group of physics teachers, in which students collect cards representing fundamental particles and use listed properties such as electric charge, spin, mass and mean lifetime to build target particle systems. The authors report that the physical version is "freely available on CERN's Zenodo repository" and has been viewed over 11,000 times and downloaded over 2000 times. (Lachlan McGinness et al. (2025))
