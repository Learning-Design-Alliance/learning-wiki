# User experience design tools for courseware: persona creation kit, journey maps, and ecosystem maps

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
WestEd's freely available suite of participant-data-informed tools helps postsecondary gateway-math courseware designers examine learner and faculty needs, experiences, and ecosystems.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **User experience design tools for courseware: persona creation kit, journey maps, and ecosystem maps**: The WestEd team developed freely available user experience design tools grounded in participant data for courseware designers and developers. These include "a modular, deck-based user persona creation kit" with eight student and faculty archetypes plus capacity, challenge, and design thought cards; eight journey maps visualizing a semester-long student experience with friction points and courseware opportunities; and two ecosystem maps illustrating the people, processes, features, and resources that positively or negatively impact success in math courses. (Higgin et al. (2025))
