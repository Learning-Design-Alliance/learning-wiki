Non-Language Mental Test¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 associational), q2 · 1 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A group intelligence test developed by Pintner and colleagues for school children from grade II upward, designed to assess mental ability while minimizing reading and language demands and to support fairer comparisons for non-English-speaking and deaf children.

Components¶

  • Pintner's standardized non-language performance test scale (15 tests, eight standardized so far): Rudolf Pintner reported progress in standardizing performance tests that are independent of language, intended for examining individuals with poor language endowment or limited English. The report states a scale of such tests "will be very serviceable in examining non-English-speaking individuals." The tests standardized at the time of the report were the Feature Profile, Manikin, Five Figure Board, Two Figure Board, Casuist Form Board, Seguin Form Board, Adaptation Board, and Healy's Five Block Frame Test. (Kelley (1915))

Claims¶

  • A Difference score comparing educational and mental index values diagnoses whether a child's attainment is commensurate with measured ability, with Differences between minus 8 and plus 8 occurring in about half of cases [+M]
  • Mental index ratings on the group test showed close correspondence over a two-year interval (r = .72, rank correlation .77) among 46 retested children [+M]
