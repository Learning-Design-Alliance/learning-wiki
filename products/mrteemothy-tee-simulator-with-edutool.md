# MrTEEmothy TEE simulator with EduTool

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 7 claims (7 for) · 2 studies (2 causal), `q3` · 0 of 2 report an effect size · 7 claims rest on one study

## Description
A medical simulation system combining a TEE-probe-sensing phantom, laptop software, and automated guidance for training standardized transesophageal echocardiography views, made by Medical Simulation Technologies in Krakow.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **MrTEEmothy TEE simulator with EduTool automated guidance for standardized TEE view training**: The MrTEEmothy simulator (Medical Simulation Technologies, Krakow) comprises a phantom, a laptop, and a generic TEE probe whose depth, rotation, bends and plane angle are sensed and logged continuously. Its "EduTool" deployed "well-established echocardiographic projections that adhere to the prevailing guidelines set by the ASE", providing automated guidance on requisite probe movements in a predetermined sequence and logging precision, time, and per-sensor motion time series for retrospective analysis. (Siniarski Aleksander et al. (2025))
