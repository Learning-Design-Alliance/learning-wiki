## Description
A web-based simulation tool released by Sánchez et al. for exploring vaccination and quarantining strategies in social networks and epidemic spreading.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Vax website for trying vaccination and quarantining strategies in social networks**: A web tool, at http://vax.herokuapp.com/, released alongside the outreach activities, where "strategies for vaccination and quarantining in social networks can be tried". The authors state it can do precisely what they intended to cover in the epidemic-spreading part of the workshop but never had time to reach. (Angel Sánchez et al. (2014))
