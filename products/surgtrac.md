## Description
SurgTrac is a tablet-based motion-tracking application repurposed with finger tags and a low-cost suturing simulator to assess open surgical technical skills through quantitative movement metrics.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **SurgTrac tablet-based finger-tracking setup with a low-cost suturing simulator for open surgical skills assessment**: An assessment setup in which SurgTrac software on a tablet tracks a red tag on the right index finger and a blue tag on the left index finger while a participant performs suturing tasks on a PediatrickBoxx simulator with an EduStitch pad. The software "recorded 30 frames per second for accurate constant motion tracking" and outputs time, distance, speed, acceleration, smoothness and handedness parameters. Originally developed for minimally invasive instrument tracking, it was repurposed for open surgery finger tracking. (Hillemans Vera et al. (2023))

### Claims
- [Finger tracking with SurgTrac shows construct validity for time, distance and smoothness across four basic open suturing tasks, discriminating novices from experts](../claims/surgtrac-finger-tracking-construct-validity-suturing.md) [+M]
