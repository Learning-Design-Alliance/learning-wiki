## Description
Google Trends is Google's web-based tool for exploring and visualizing relative search-interest data across terms, regions, and time.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Google Trends as a scientific literacy learning environment, with its chart-reading caveats**: Google Trends (GT) is the software environment around which the experimental class's investigative activities were structured. It plots charts from Google Search data for up to five user-inserted terms, using a relative and normalized interest score (RNIS) across time, regional and mean charts. The article stresses inherent limitations students must grasp: the time axis is blurred by a hidden technology-accessibility axis, points are discrete, and "a drop in the chart does not necessary means a decrease in the interest, but a drop in RNIS", since absolute interest may have grown if total search volume rose more. (Geovani Lopes Dias; Renato P. dos Santos (2017))
