## Description
An interactive 3D dental morphology education program by eHuman that provides models, multimodal anatomical views, and practical self-assessment quizzes for dental students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **3D Tooth Atlas interactive dental morphology software (eHuman, version 7.0.4)**: The 3D Tooth Atlas (eHuman, Fermont, CA, USA) version 7.0.4 is an interactive dental education software program the article describes as "designed primarily for dental morphology education". It contains an extensive database of dental anatomy, including supporting bone, for primary and secondary dentitions within 3D models, plus photographs, radiographs, and micro-CT scans viewable in vertical and horizontal cross-sections, with practical quizzes for self-assessment. In the study, intervention-year students were provided with CDs of the program. (Al-Thobity Ahmad M. et al. (2017))
