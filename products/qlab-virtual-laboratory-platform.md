# QLab virtual laboratory platform

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 2 claims (2 for) · 1 study (1 design), `q2` · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

## Description
QLab is a commercial 3-D virtual laboratory platform made by Anhui Qasky Quantum Technology Co. Ltd. for conducting simulated physics experiments such as Bell tests.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **QLab virtual laboratory platform with a Bell test experiment for undergraduate quantum entanglement learning**: QLab is a commercially available virtual laboratory platform from Anhui Qasky Quantum Technology Co. Ltd. that "simulates a 3-D physical laboratory environment equipped with various optical devices and instruments", letting undergraduates without experimental expertise conduct a Bell test. The virtual experimental setup consists of three units: generation of a quantum entangled state, distribution of entangled photons, and measurement of quantum correlations. A guidebook outlining principles and operational procedures was distributed in advance, and platform fidelity was ensured through repeated testing. (Hongbin Song (2025))
