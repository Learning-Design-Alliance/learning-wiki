## Description
A freely available best-practices-based eLearning programme developed by Thielmann and colleagues to teach physicians and practice assistants about vaccine cold-chain management.

### Claims
- [Vaccine storage knowledge scores rose from 5.6 to 9.8 of 11 correct answers among physicians and practice assistants after KeepCoool participation, in one single-group pre-post study with no comparison group](../claims/keepcoool-knowledge-score-increase.md) [+M]
- [Among general practices with cold chain breaches, refrigerator temperatures were measured from baseline to follow-up after KeepCoool participation; at follow-up, 17% of participating refrigerators continuously met the 2-8°C target range.](../claims/keepcoool-elearning-improved-vaccine-cold-chain.md) [+M]
