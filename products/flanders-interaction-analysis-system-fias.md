# Flanders interaction analysis system (FIAS)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A structured observational coding system that classifies teacher and pupil verbal behaviors during classroom interaction for analyzing teaching practice and interaction patterns.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Flanders interaction analysis system (FIAS) as a 10-category observational tool for quantifying teacher–student verbal interaction**: The FIAS is an observational coding system that classifies verbal behaviors of teachers and pupils during classroom interaction into 10 categories: seven teacher-talk categories (accepting feelings, praising, accepting/using ideas, asking questions, lecturing, giving direction, criticizing/justifying authority), two pupil-talk categories (response and initiation), and one silence/confusion category. The article states it is "an observational tool used to classify the verbal behaviors of teachers and pupils as they interact in the classroom". In this study, the FIAS was applied to videotaped OSTE feedback stations, with recordings made at 20–25 observations per minute over 15 minutes, and only 9 of the 10 categories (F0–F8) were analyzed after excluding standardized student responses. (Hsieh et al. (2019))
