# JAG four-domain framework for EUS training and certification

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A Joint Advisory Group (JAG) framework that specifies diagnostic EUS competencies, staged case-volume training, assessment requirements, certification, and postcertification mentorship for advanced endoscopy trainees.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **JAG four-domain framework for EUS training and certification with a three-stage case-volume syllabus**: A competency-based certification framework for diagnostic EUS organised into four domains: definition of competence (13 statements), acquisition of competence (10), assessment of competence (5) and postcertification mentorship (5), developed via a modified Delphi process with GRADE appraisal and 80% agreement thresholds. The panel proposes "a syllabus divided into three domains: (1) ‘early novice’ (0–75 cases), (2) ‘intermediate’ (76–150 cases) and (3) ‘advanced’ (151–250 cases)" with categories spanning background knowledge, scope handling, console use, anatomy, image interpretation and tissue acquisition, culminating in the complete EUS procedure. (El Menabawey Tareq et al. (2024))
