# Teamwork Evaluation of Non-Technical Skills (TENTS) instrument

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
TENTS is a validated observational assessment instrument for rating nontechnical teamwork behaviors such as communication, leadership, situation monitoring, and mutual support in surgical teams.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **TENTS-based observational measurement protocol with trained, blinded medical student observers**: The study measured safety behaviors in real operating rooms using the validated Teamwork Evaluation of Non-Technical Skills (TENTS) instrument, chosen over the TeamSTEPPS Team Performance Observation Tool for its focused evaluation of nontechnical teamwork dimensions. Seventeen medical student volunteers conducted real-time observations on a 0-3 scale; per the article, "The students received 1.5 hours of instruction on TeamSTEPPS patient safety behaviors and were trained to apply the validated TENTS instrument consistently." Students were blinded to surgeons' treatment status during postintervention observations and provided written justifications for extreme scores. (Mazur L et al. (2025))
