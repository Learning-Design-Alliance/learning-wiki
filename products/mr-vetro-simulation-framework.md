# Mr. Vetro simulation framework

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A student-facing, Agentsheets-based collaborative cardiovascular-system simulation developed by Gerry Stahl and colleagues for K–12 students to explore physiological parameters and complex-system causality.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Mr. Vetro simulation framework for collaborative exploration of the cardiovascular system**: Mr. Vetro is a student-facing simulation of the human body's cardiovascular system of lungs and heart, built on a substantial extension of the Agentsheets student-programmable simulation design environment. The editorial states that "the Mr. Vetro simulation framework allows students to explore the effects of different variations of physiological parameters within an interdependent complex system." Students collaboratively control the simulation, each simulating an individual organ or contextual parameter through mobile devices, in an approach the authors call collective simulations. (Gerry Stahl et al. (2010))
