18-item closed-ended survey with one open-ended item measuring critical thinking, engagement, and peer interaction¶

Description¶

A deNoyelles et al. (2015) researcher-developed student-perception survey for evaluating critical thinking, engagement, and peer interaction after word-cloud or linear discussion activities.

Components¶

  • Word cloud survey instrument measuring critical thinking, engagement, and peer interaction: The researchers developed an 18-item closed-ended survey with one open-ended item to measure student perceptions of critical thinking, engagement, and peer interaction after a word cloud or linear discussion activity. Items were generated from definitions and coding schemes employed in previous articles, and reliability was acceptable: Cronbach's alpha based on standardized items was .70 for the critical thinking scale and .84 for engagement. The instrument uses a 5-point Likert scale from agree to disagree. (deNoyelles et al. (2015))

Claims¶

  • Critical thinking was positively correlated with peer interaction in online discussions [+W]
  • A larger share of word cloud condition students reported that classmate interaction was promoted compared to linear condition students [+W]
