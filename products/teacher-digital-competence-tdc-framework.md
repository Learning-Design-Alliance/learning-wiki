## Description
Garry Falloon's Teacher Digital Competence framework organizes teachers' curriculum, personal-ethical, and personal-professional digital competencies to inform teacher-education curriculum and assessment design.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **The Teacher Digital Competence (TDC) framework integrates curriculum, personal-ethical and personal-professional competencies**: The TDC framework is a conceptual framework depicting broadly-based teacher digital competence as three integrated competency sets. It extends TPACK-aligned curriculum competencies (technical, technological, discipline/content, pedagogical/learning design) with personal-ethical pillars (digital citizenship, safety and data security, health and wellbeing, impacts on people, society and environment) and personal-professional pillars (information literacy, strategic professional networking, commitment to continuous professional learning). The article states the framework 'represents diagrammatically a broadly-based conceptualisation of teacher digital competence', with double-headed arrows indicating the interrelated nature of the pillars. It is intended to inform curriculum and programme design in teacher education rather than prescribe content. (Garry Falloon (2020))
- **Five-dimension framework of higher education teachers' digital competencies for post-GOT online and blended teaching**: The article organizes teachers' digital competencies around the four dimensions proposed by Esteve-Mon et al. (2020)—basic digital skills, the pedagogical application of digital technologies, the use of technology to support continuous professional development, and the ability to further the digital competencies of higher education students—extended with Villarroel and Bruna's (2017) transversal competencies. The authors state: "We organized our results around these five dimensions to converge the analysis of teachers' experiences with teachers' digital competency development needs for post-GOT online and blended teaching." The framework structures both the interview analysis and the professional development recommendations. (Carlos González et al. (2023))
