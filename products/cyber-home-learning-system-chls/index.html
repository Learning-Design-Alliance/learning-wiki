Cyber Home Learning System (CHLS)¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 2 claims (1 for, 1 against) · 1 study (1 causal), q3 · 0 of 1 report an effect size · 2 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A national open-access web-based K–12 learning system created and operated in South Korea to provide digitally mediated subject learning, including self-study and teacher-managed options.

Components¶

  • Cyber Home Learning System (CHLS) self-study option for Grade 9 English: The CHLS is a national open-access learning system implemented in South Korea in 2004 enabling K–12 learners with web access to study topics including English, created "to close the educational access divide, to reduce private tutoring expenses, and to improve the quality of public education." It offers a classroom option with cyber-teacher management and a self-study option where students select content by preference. In this study the self-study option comprised 12 units of 9 lessons each, with tasks structured as Motivation, Learning Instructions, Task 1–3, and Wrap-up, plus Need Help and Challenge buttons. (Ji Hye Shin et al. (2015))

Claims¶

  • CHLS self-study significantly benefited advanced-level students but not intermediate- or lower-level students [+M]
  • CHLS self-study produced no statistically significant overall improvement in English posttest performance compared with regular classroom instruction alone [-M]
