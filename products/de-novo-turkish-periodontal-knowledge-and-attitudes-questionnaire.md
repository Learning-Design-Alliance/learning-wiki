# De novo Turkish periodontal knowledge and attitudes questionnaire

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A researcher-developed, expert-reviewed and pilot-tested online questionnaire measuring periodontal-health knowledge, attitudes, and knowledge of periodontal–systemic disease relationships among physicians, residents, and dentists.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **De novo Turkish periodontal knowledge and attitudes questionnaire for physicians and dentists**: A self-administered online questionnaire (Google Forms) developed de novo for this study, consisting of 7 sociodemographic questions and 29 knowledge/attitude questions (26 multiple-choice, 3 multi-select) across five domains, with pre- and post-survey self-assessment items on a 0–10 scale. Items were adapted from established international instruments, content-validated by two periodontologists and one internal medicine specialist, and piloted with 20 participants. The 22 knowledge items showed "good overall reliability (α = 0.829)", with domain-specific alphas of 0.605–0.659. (Bozmaoğlu Beyza et al. (2026))
