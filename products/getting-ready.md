## Description
Getting Ready is a University of Nebraska–Lincoln program that trains early childhood educators in evidence-based family-partnership strategies and coaches them in collaborative goal-setting through home visits, conferences, and informal interactions.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Getting Ready family-partnership program for early childhood educators**: Getting Ready is a program developed at the University of Nebraska—Lincoln to increase school readiness among children five and younger in home visitation or center-based early childhood programs. It "trains educators in eight evidence-based strategies to enhance their partnerships with families" and provides coaching for meetings with families through home visits, conferences, and informal interactions in which educators and families "collaboratively set goals to position each other for success." (Getting Ready (2025))

## Description
Getting Ready is a program developed at the University of Nebraska—Lincoln to increase school readiness among children five and younger in home visitation or center-based early childhood programs. It "trains educators in eight evidence-based strategies to enhance their partnerships with families" and provides coaching for meetings with families through home visits, conferences, and informal interactions in which educators and families "collaboratively set goals to position each other for success."

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Educators need initial training plus ongoing coaching sessions to reflect on and support their approaches
#### Constraints
- Evidence ratings for Family Engagement and Academic Performance are Promising rather than Strong because the supporting sample was below 350 children

### Target Learners
- children ages five and younger who are educationally at risk in home visitation or center-based early childhood programs

### Target Learning Goals
- school readiness
- social-emotional learning
- family engagement
- academic performance
