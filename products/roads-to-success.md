## Description
Roads to Success is a school and career planning programme that supports students in exploring careers, understanding pathways to career success, and developing school and career plans.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Roads to Success education and career planning program**: Roads to Success is a school and career planning program evaluated over a 2007-2009 time frame in high-poverty rural schools. The report assesses "the impact of two years of exposure to the school and career planning program, Roads to Success, on eighth graders from high-poverty rural schools." It targets career planning knowledge, confidence, and engagement with school staff around career and school plans. (Duncan Chaplin et al. (2010))
