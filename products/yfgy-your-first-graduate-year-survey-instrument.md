# YFGY ('your first graduate year') survey instrument

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A culturally adapted survey instrument developed by Lai Wenyi et al. for Chinese medical postgraduate students, measuring demographic, academic-profile, postgraduate-transition, and self-assessment factors associated with academic performance.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **YFGY ('your first graduate year') survey instrument adapted for Chinese medical postgraduate students**: The YFGY instrument was "adapted from the widely utilized 'your first college year' survey" with cultural and pedagogical modifications for China's postgraduate system. After pilot testing with 50 postgraduate students, it comprised four domains: demographic information, undergraduate academic profile, postgraduate transition factors, and multidimensional self-assessment metrics. It was administered at 2020 postgraduate orientation and yielded 1,091 validated responses (96.3% validity rate), with Cronbach's α = 0.884 and KMO = 0.915. (Lai Wenyi et al. (2026))
