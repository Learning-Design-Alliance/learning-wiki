# Project SEARCH plus Autism Spectrum Disorder Supports (PS-ASD)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)

## Description
A full-school-year, employer-based transition-to-employment programme developed by Wehman and colleagues that embeds autistic youth with significant support needs in rotating internships, classroom instruction, job coaching, and interagency collaboration.

### Claims
- [PS-ASD participants acquired community-based competitive employment at far higher rates than high-school-services-as-usual controls (90.3% vs 5.9% at 3 months; 87.1% vs 11.1% at 12 months)](../claims/psasd-competitive-employment-rct.md) [+M]
- [Employment support intensity needs decreased significantly over 21 months for the treatment group while remaining unchanged for controls, but not when unemployed participants were excluded](../claims/psasd-support-intensity-decrease.md) [+M]
