## Description
A proposed RMIT-oriented online support system developed by Spichkova et al. that provides transnational-education orientation content, quizzes, and automated study and host-country assistance for international students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **TNE Online Support System with an online module and quiz plus an automated assistance module**: The proposed TNE online support system consists of two core parts: an online module on TNE-related topics with a corresponding quiz, and an automated assistance module. The module is designed to "ensure that students understand TNE-related potential problems, such as the importance of submission deadlines", while the assistance module provides advice on studying and living in Australia, tailored to a specific institution. The system is intended for onshore international students and offshore TNE students, including RMIT's Vietnam campuses. (Spichkova M et al. (2016))
