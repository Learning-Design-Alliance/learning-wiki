## Description
A multimedia remedial English-learning programme developed by Tupe for Marathi-medium primary students, using animated scenarios, audio, guided activities, feedback, and teacher facilitation.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Multimedia Scenario Based Learning Programme (MSBLP) for primary English**: MSBLP is a remedial multimedia programme in which learning scenarios are built from Gagne's nine events of instruction and scenario-based learning approaches, delivered with animation, graphics, music and sound. It teaches twenty-three concepts (e.g. Profession, Direction, Food) drawn from grade 1-3 Maharashtra textbooks, each with its own small scenario, a fairy anchor who states objectives in Marathi, guided activities, feedback, and teacher facilitation and scaffolding. It was trialled as a three-month remedial programme for grade-3 students weak on grade-2 competencies. (Tupe (2015))
