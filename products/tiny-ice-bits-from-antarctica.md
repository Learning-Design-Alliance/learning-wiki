## Description
A 10-part bilingual English-Spanish YouTube science-outreach series produced by PolarTREC educator Jocelyn Argueta, featuring short videos about travel, science, and life at the South Pole with basic assessments.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Tiny Ice bilingual short-video series on life at the South Pole**: Tiny Ice: Bits from Antarctica is a 10-part YouTube video series produced by PolarTREC educator Jocelyn Argueta after her pandemic-cancelled outreach events were translated into digital form. "The 10-part series, available in English and Spanish, highlights travel, science, and life at the South Pole", with each topic explained in 2-minute segments using photos, videos, and anecdotes. The videos have been collectively viewed 1,300 times, are aligned to Next Generation Science Standards and Polar Literacy Principles, and each video has an accompanying basic assessment. (The IceCube Collaboration (2021))
