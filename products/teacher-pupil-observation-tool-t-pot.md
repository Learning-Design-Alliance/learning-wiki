# Teacher-Pupil Observation Tool (T-POT)

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A structured real-time classroom observation instrument developed by Ford Tamsin et al. to frequency-count teacher behaviours and teacher–child interactions in primary classrooms.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Teacher-Pupil Observation Tool (T-POT) for direct classroom observation**: The T-POT is a structured real-time frequency count of defined teacher behaviours and types of teacher-child interaction, used in a sub-sample of 20 classrooms (25% of teachers) with observers blind to allocation. The article reports "Inter-rater reliability with two or three observers rating 21 primary school teachers was high (ICC = 0.78)". It codes nine teacher behaviours and seven child behaviours, including teacher negatives (e.g. reprimanding) and teacher positives (e.g. praise), and encourages comparing change between observations, particularly ratios of positive to negative behaviours. (Ford Tamsin et al. (2012))
