## Description
AICO_edu is a teacher self-report questionnaire developed by Delcker et al. for assessing perceived AI competence across six dimensions in pre-service and in-service vocational school teachers.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **AICO_edu questionnaire for teachers' perceived AI competence**: AICO_edu (AI Competence Educators) is a self-report questionnaire developed to assess teachers' perceived AI competence. "Each dimension was captured through six to eight items (TH: 8; LF: 6; IP: 8; AT: 8; TL: 8; PF: 7), which were answered on a five-point Likert scale." It focuses on perceived AI competence rather than general digital competence, and is intended for use by school leaders and educators in teacher training. (Delcker et al. (2025))
