# Tikichuela: Mathematics in My School

## Description
A bilingual Spanish–Guaraní preschool mathematics curriculum adapted from Big Math for Little Kids and delivered through interactive audio lessons with songs, stories, movement, games, teacher training, and in-class tutoring.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Tikichuela: Mathematics in My School — bilingual interactive audio preschool math program**: Tikichuela is a Paraguayan preschool math program adapted from the Big Math for Little Kids program, delivering the full preschool math curriculum through "108 audio CDs" of interactive lessons with songs, dances, dramatization of math stories, and games. Audio lessons run four days a week with a fifth review day; each class averages 60 minutes, including "playing the audio lesson for 30– 40 minutes". Key concepts are repeated in both Spanish and Guaraní, and teachers receive training and in-class tutoring in the methodology. (Naslund Hadley et al. (2014))

### Claims
- [The Tikichuela interactive audio math program raised preschool standardized math scores by 0.16 standard deviations in under five months](../claims/tikichuela-overall-016-sd-gain.md) [+M]
