## Description
The Washington state guidance document proposes a "Human AI Human" or "H - AI - H" model for integrating AI into K-12 education. The model emphasizes that AI should always be used as a tool to enhance human capabilities rather than as a replacement for human judgment and decision-making. The report quotes the state's framing: "Start with human inquiry, see what AI produces, and always close with human reflection, human edits, and human understanding of what was produced." The report notes this differs from other documents, which focus primarily on technical implementation aspects.
