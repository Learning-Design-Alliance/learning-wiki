# Whole-Person Education Guide

> **Product or Programme** · [All products and programmes](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A district-facing framework and facilitated planning guide for aligning whole-person education initiatives, developed for adoption by education systems.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Six-chapter alignment and coherence process from student understanding through strategic communication**: The guide's core reusable design is a six-chapter team process: "Chapter 1. Identify the Strengths, Needs, and Aspirations of Your Students; Chapter 2. Describe How These Strengths, Needs, and Aspirations Inform Your Vision for Serving the Whole Person in Your District; Chapter 3. Identify a Focus Area for Coherence; Chapter 4. Tailor the Implementation of Your Whole-Person Initiatives for Alignment With Your Focus Area; Chapter 5. Create an Action Plan; Chapter 6. Plan for Strategic Communication That Reinforces the Work." Each chapter follows a consistent structure of purpose, collaborative activities, hypothetical district responses, and reflection questions. (Walrond (2021))

# Six-chapter alignment and coherence process from student understanding through strategic communication

> **Design** · [All designs](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
The guide's core reusable design is a six-chapter team process: "Chapter 1. Identify the Strengths, Needs, and Aspirations of Your Students; Chapter 2. Describe How These Strengths, Needs, and Aspirations Inform Your Vision for Serving the Whole Person in Your District; Chapter 3. Identify a Focus Area for Coherence; Chapter 4. Tailor the Implementation of Your Whole-Person Initiatives for Alignment With Your Focus Area; Chapter 5. Create an Action Plan; Chapter 6. Plan for Strategic Communication That Reinforces the Work." Each chapter follows a consistent structure of purpose, collaborative activities, hypothetical district responses, and reflection questions.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- A cross-departmental team with defined facilitation must carry out the chapters collaboratively; the guide also directs teams to assess readiness before starting
#### Constraints
- The guide recommends choosing no more than one or two focus areas to start, and notes teams may work more quickly or more slowly than the guide indicates

### Target Learners
- K-12 students in the district
- families
- educators

### Learning Goals
- aligned and coherent whole-person initiatives that advance equitable outcomes
