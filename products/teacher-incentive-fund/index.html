Teacher Incentive Fund¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · 18 claims (5 for, 9 mixed, 4 against) · 7 studies (5 causal, 2 associational), q2–q3 · 0 of 7 report an effect size · 17 claims rest on one study

Description¶

A U.S. federal grant programme that supports performance-based compensation systems for teachers and principals in high-need schools.

Components¶

  • Teacher Incentive Fund (TIF) federal grant program supporting performance-based compensation systems: The Teacher Incentive Fund is a federal grant program that "provides federal grants to support performance-based compensation systems for teachers and principals in high-need schools." The 2010 cohort was evaluated nationally over multiple years, with this snapshot covering the third of four years of implementation in the 2013–2014 school year, including in-depth analysis in ten evaluation districts. (Alison Wellington et al. (2016))
  • Teacher Incentive Fund (TIF) grants supporting performance-based compensation systems: The Teacher Incentive Fund is a federal grant program established by Congress in 2006 that "provides grants to support performance-based compensation systems for teachers and principals in high-need schools." The grants pursue two stated goals: reforming compensation to reward educators for improving student achievement, and increasing the number of high-performing teachers in high-need schools and hard-to-staff subjects. This report examines the 2010 grant cohort's first year of implementation. (Max et al. (2014))
  • Teacher Incentive Fund (TIF) performance-based compensation grants for high-need schools: The Teacher Incentive Fund is a federal grant program established by Congress in 2006 that "provides grants to support performance-based compensation systems for teachers and principals in high-need schools." The evaluated intervention centers on performance bonuses tied to educator effectiveness and student achievement, implemented within districts' performance-based compensation systems over four grant years. (Hanley Chiang et al. (2017))
  • Teacher Incentive Fund (TIF) performance-based compensation program for teachers and principals in high-need schools: The Teacher Incentive Fund is a federal grant program that "provides grants to support performance-based compensation systems for teachers and principals in high-need schools." The 2010 grantee cohort was required to implement four program components for teachers, and the program uses performance measures and pay-for-performance bonuses whose communication to educators proved challenging in early implementation. (Jeffrey Max et al. (2014))
  • Teacher Incentive Fund (TIF) performance-based compensation program: The Teacher Incentive Fund is a federal grant program that "supports performance-based compensation systems in high-need schools." Its grant incentives "aim to raise student achievement by improving teacher effectiveness and workforce quality." The program required districts to implement four components, and Mathematica evaluated its implementation and bonus impacts. (Jeffrey Max et al. (2014))
