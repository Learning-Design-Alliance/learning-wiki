# Arizona linked education–child welfare student database

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · no claims cited

## Description
A unique database created for this study by matching statewide individual student education records from the Arizona Department of Education longitudinal data system with individual child records from the Arizona Department of Child Safety. It identified the education records of all K–12 students ages 5–17 with a foster care placement between August 1, 2012 and June 1, 2013, producing "the state's first-ever education snapshot of all K–12 students in foster care." The two systems previously lacked a common unique student identifier for students in both systems.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Collaboration and data sharing between the state education agency and the child welfare agency, which the report says the Arizona departments accomplished by collaborating to share data
#### Constraints
- The study population was restricted to students under age 18 at the start of the school year, which affects comparability with other statewide reports; district counts based on first school of enrollment are likely an undercount given high school mobility

### Target Learners
- K–12 students in foster care in Arizona public schools

### Target Learning Goals
- Identifying and tracking the academic vulnerability and education outcomes of students in foster care
