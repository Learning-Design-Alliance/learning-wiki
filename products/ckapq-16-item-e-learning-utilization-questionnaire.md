# CKAPQ (16-item e-learning utilization questionnaire)

## Description
A 16-item five-point Likert questionnaire developed by Kumar Archana Prabu et al. to measure purpose of internet use, technology confidence, perceived advantages and limitations of e-learning, and perceived educational impact among health-professions students.

## Components
<!-- A programme's own frameworks, tools, indicators and instruments, as its sources describe them -->
- **Five-factor model of e-learning utilization among health professions students**: The article proposes and validates a model in which e-learning utilization among undergraduate health professions students is explained by five latent constructs: purpose of using the internet (PUR), confidence/perceived ease of using technology (CON), perceived advantages of e-learning (PAE), perceived limitations of e-learning (PLE), and perceived educational impact (PEI). The SEM model showed factor loadings from 0.54 to 0.93 and construct correlations from −0.45 to 0.55, with PLE and PEI strongly positively correlated and both negatively related to the other constructs. The article frames this as distinct from TAM and UTAUT2, which it says "focus primarily on technological acceptance rather than learning effectiveness". (Kumar Archana Prabu et al. (2025))
