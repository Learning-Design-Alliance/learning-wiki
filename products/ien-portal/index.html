iEN portal¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes
Evidence · no claims cited

Description¶

The iEN portal is an educational platform that converts textbook experiment images into augmented-reality science experiments viewable on learners' smart devices.

Components¶

  • iEN AR experiments converting textbook experiment pictures into augmented reality: The iEN portal provides AR experiments that let students use smart devices to view augmented-reality versions of experiments printed in their textbooks. Per the article, students "could use their smart devices to transfer more than 100 experiments pictures provided in their textbooks to AR experiments". This is the AR capability the study situates QR codes within, linking printed textbook content to virtual content. (AlNajdi (2022))
