- **DCMP implementation costs and resource requirements**: The report provides a preliminary list of cost ingredients for implementing DCMP, based on information available as of December 2020. As reported in Rutschow et al. (2019), "the average DCMP start-up cost for each college was about $140,450 over two years", with ongoing costs of $19,340 per year per college or $132 per student. Cost ingredients include personnel training, facilities, curricula with a homework platform fee of approximately $40 per student, and start-up activities such as pathway alignment and transfer agreements. (Dana Center Mathematics Pathways)
- **Dana Center Mathematics Pathways (DCMP) intervention model**: DCMP is a developmental education intervention for community college students underprepared for college-level math. It offers three aligned math pathways—statistical reasoning, quantitative reasoning, and path to calculus—places students directly into college-level math courses with co-requisite supports, and uses "evidence-based curricula and pedagogy" with "integrated student supports". Curricula are contextualized around real-world problems and real datasets, using small group work, class discussions, and interactive lectures. (Dana Center Mathematics Pathways)

# Dana Center Mathematics Pathways (DCMP) intervention model

> **Element** · [All elements](index.md)
> **Evidence** · 6 claims (5 for, 1 mixed) · 6 studies (4 causal, 2 quant-synthesis), `q3`–`q4` · 5 of 6 report an effect size · 6 claims rest on one study

## Description
DCMP is a developmental education intervention for community college students underprepared for college-level math. It offers three aligned math pathways—statistical reasoning, quantitative reasoning, and path to calculus—places students directly into college-level math courses with co-requisite supports, and uses "evidence-based curricula and pedagogy" with "integrated student supports". Curricula are contextualized around real-world problems and real datasets, using small group work, class discussions, and interactive lectures.

## Design Implications

### Context
#### Requirements
- Math course sequences aligned to students' programs of study
- first-semester co-requisite model
- instruction using evidence-based curricula and pedagogy
- enhanced coordination between college faculty, advisors, and student support staff
- alignment of math pathways between two- and four-year colleges
#### Constraints
- The three studies meeting WWC standards investigated the original model with an accelerated developmental course in the first semester; the first-semester co-requisite model was adopted after those studies were conducted.

### Target Learners
- Community college students placed into developmental math, including Asian, Black, White, and Hispanic students in urban, suburban, and rural settings

### Target Learning Goals
- College-level mathematics proficiency aligned to programs of study
- timely progress toward a certificate or degree
- empowerment as mathematical learners
