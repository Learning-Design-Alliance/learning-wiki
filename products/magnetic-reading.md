## Description
Magnetic Reading is a print-based, supplemental reading program intended for 30-45 minutes a day, designed to support grade 3-5 students' comprehension skills as the comprehension strand within the daily reading block. It provides students with "culturally-relevant and high-interest grade-level texts" and includes a digital library of educator resources supporting whole-class and differentiated instruction, as well as scaffolds and protocols.
