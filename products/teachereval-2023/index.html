TeacherEval-2023¶

Product or Programme · All products and programmes

Description¶

A public corpus of 12,450 classroom-dialogue transcripts annotated with rubric-aligned teacher-performance scores and expert explanations across eight pedagogical dimensions, created by Li et al.

Components¶

  • TeacherEval-2023 dataset of 12,450 rubric-annotated classroom dialog transcripts: TeacherEval-2023 is a public corpus used as the primary evaluation dataset: it comprises "12,450 classroom dialog transcripts annotated with rubric-aligned teacher performance scores and expert explanations across eight pedagogical dimensions", covering mathematics, language arts, and science across multiple grade levels. The article splits it 70/15/15 into train/validation/test with stratification across subjects and grade levels, and projects all datasets onto a unified eight-dimension framework spanning learning objectives clarity through subject-specific practices. (Li et al. (2025))

Claims¶

  • The proposed framework outperforms fine-tuned BERT, Instructor-LM, GPT-4, and RAPT on TeacherEval-2023 across consistency, credibility, calibration, and fairness metrics [+M]
