## Description
CIRC is a reading and writing program for students in grades 2–6, developed in 1983 by Robert Slavin and Nancy Madden at Johns Hopkins University and distributed by the Success for All Foundation. It has "three principal elements: story-related activities, direct instruction in reading comprehension, and integrated language arts/writing." Daily 90-minute lessons have mixed-ability students work in pairs and cooperative teams of four to read to each other, predict story endings, summarize, clarify vocabulary, reread for fluency, and draft, revise, and edit one another's writing, with team-based rewards for motivation. A Spanish version exists for grades 2–5, and the program costs approximately $150 per student for training and materials.
