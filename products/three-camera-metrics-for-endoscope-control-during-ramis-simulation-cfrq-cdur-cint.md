# Three camera metrics for endoscope control during RAMIS simulation (CFrq, CDur, CInt)

## Description
A study-developed quantitative assessment set by Jarc et al. that uses endoscope kinematic and event data from the da Vinci Skills Simulator to measure camera-movement frequency, duration, and interval in robot-assisted minimally invasive surgery training.
